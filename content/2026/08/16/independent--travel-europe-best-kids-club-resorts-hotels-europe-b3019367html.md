It’s a truth universally acknowledged that a great kids’ club can transform a family holiday. As a travel editor who's spent the past three years exploring the world with a toddler in tow, I've come to appreciate just how much difference childcare can make. While my daughter has been busy building dens, or making boats out of sand on the beach, I’ve been able to enjoy a long lunch, a spa treatment or simply a quiet swim.

Today's best properties for parents and kids go far beyond face painting and soft play, with many offering age-specific programmes from babies through to teens (surf school anyone?) led by qualified childcare teams.

Whether you're looking for a once-in-a-lifetime luxury escape, a brilliant value beach resort or a family-friendly staycation, the best hotels now treat childcare as a central part of the experience rather than an afterthought.

From France and Croatia to Portugal and Greece, these are the resorts where children will be just as excited about the holiday as the adults; that allow everyone to return home feeling like they've actually had a proper break.

## The best hotels in Europe with kids’ clubs for 2026

- **Best for under threes:** Daios Cove hotel, Crete
- **Best for teens:** Le Dune Resort & Spa hotel, Sardinia
- **Best all-inclusive:** Club Med Palmiye hotel, Turkey
- **Best cheap hotel:** H10 Suites Lanzarote Gardens hotel, Lanzarote
- **Best resort hotel:** Falkensteiner Family Hotel Diadora, Croatia
- **Best in Greece:** The Peligoni Club, Zakynthos

## 1. OKU hotel Andalucía, Spain

![OKU Andalucia boasts aesthetic kids’ facilities in stunning surrounds](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/10/21/OKU-Andalucia.jpeg)

OKU Andalucía brings the brand's laidback luxury vibe to a low-rise property on Spain's southern coast, without forgetting its youngest guests. As well as spacious suites, multiple pools and direct beach access, the newly launched kids' club has been curated with the same aesthetic approach as the rest of the hotel, offering nature-inspired crafts, creative workshops and outdoor games in beautifully designed surroundings.

Families often get into a routine of spending the morning exploring the gardens or by one of the pools before children head off to make new friends. and parents can recharge horizontal with an excellent couple’s spa treatment.

**Address:** OKU Andalusia, s/n, 11315 La Alcaidesa, Cádiz, Spain

**Price:** From £278

## 2. Martinhal Sagres hotel, Portugal

![The hotel offers a baby concierge service and five age-specific clubs](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/29/14/44/Kids-Pool_Pool-Hangout.jpg)

Set on Portugal's wild Algarve coast, the village-style resort Martinhal Sagres is a benchmark for how to get family holidays absolutely right. Rather than treating childcare as an add-on, everything revolves around making holidays easier for parents; from its baby concierge service (where you can pre-book complimentary equipment such as baby baths, bottle sterilisers and potties) to five age-specific clubs welcoming children at every stage, including teenage years.

Days might involve beach Olympics, surfing lessons, science workshops or cookery classes; while babies are cared for by qualified staff in purpose-built crèches so you know they’re in good hands while you take some time out. And the best news? It delivers all the polish of a luxury resort without the eye-watering price tag of many Mediterranean competitors.

**Address:** R. Baiona la Real Apartado 54, 8650-908 Sagres, Portugal

**Price:** From £222

## 3. Daios Cove hotel, Crete

![Adults and kids are equally well catered for at Daios Cove](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/9/23/Daios-Cove-kids-club.jpeg)

Finding picturesque hotels that cater for babies can be surprisingly difficult, which is why Daios Cove – a terraced property built into a secluded hillside – remains such a standout and is recommended in parent WhatsApp groups time and time again. Its professionally run crèche welcomes children from just four months old – usually a sleepless time when you need even more of a break – with highly trained childcare staff and chic indoor and outdoor play spaces.

If you end up returning year after year, as tiny travellers grow, they move seamlessly into age-specific kids' clubs and organised activities for older children and teens.

Adults are equally well looked after, with a private sandy cove for soaking up some vitamin D and swimming in the sea, exceptional restaurants and one of Crete's finest spas providing ample opportunities to recharge.

**Address:** Βαθύ, Kritsa 721 00, Greece

**Price:** From £410

## 4. Le Dune Resort & Spa hotel, Sardinia

![Le Dune is home to one of Italy's largest children's villages](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/9/35/The-kids-club-at-Le-Dune.jpeg)

If you want maximum entertainment without the need to re-mortgage the house, then check into Le Dune. Spacious family rooms and generous half-board packages make it one of the Mediterranean's best-value family resorts.

Set on Sardinia's northern coast, the sprawling beachfront resort is also home to one of Italy's largest children's villages, with dedicated clubs for babies, children and teenagers alongside football, archery, swimming lessons, cooking classes and evening shows.

Younger guests can spend the day making holiday pals while you stretch out on the long sandy beach, retreat to the spa or enjoy dinner at one of the resort's impressive collection of restaurants.

**Address:** Loc, 07030 Li Junchi OT, Italy

**Price:** From £120

## 5. Parklane hotel, Cyprus

![The Explorer Kids Club is one of Europe's largest](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/05/07/10/06120757-a80eb9a3-d84b-4726-9cca-37960921ad9c.jpg)

Looking for a luxury family resort in Cyprus? Parklane is hard to beat. The Explorer Kids Club is one of Europe's largest, housed inside a whimsical two storey castle complete with sensory playrooms, climbing frames and dedicated sleep rooms for younger guests so naptime isn’t skipped (we all know how that turns out…).

Babies can be looked after from four months and there are sports, crafts and outdoor adventures for the older members of your tribe. Meanwhile, parents can slip off to the adult-only seawater pool for an Aperol or two, while the line-up of destination restaurants, such as Nammos and La Petite Maison, means you can get dressed up for dinner and have a proper date night out (with babysitters on hand).

**Address:** Giannou Kranidioti 11, Street, Lemesos 4534, Cyprus

**Price:** From £384

## 6. Club Med Palmiye hotel, Turkey

![This Club Med outpost benefits from a beautiful beachfront setting](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/9/55/The-kids-pool-at-Club-Med-Turkiye.jpeg)

Club Med pioneered the all-inclusive family holiday long before these types of resorts became fashionable. Today, Club Med Palmiye remains one of its finest properties, thanks to a beautiful beachfront setting on Turkey's Mediterranean coast, amazing sports facilities and one of the most comprehensive children's programmes in the world.

Almost everything is included, from sailing, tennis and waterskiing to Baby Club Med, Petit Club, Mini Club and exciting teen programmes, so you can get stuck in knowing that meals, activities and unlimited ice cream are already covered. Plus, the resort actively encourages families to try new experiences together, from learning circus skills to paddle boarding.

**Address:** Tatil Koyu Tekerlektepe Mevkii, JHP4+7G, 07980 Kemer/Antalya, Turkey

**Price:** From £366

## 7. Calcot & Spa hotel, Cotswolds, UK

![Calcot & Spa is one of the UK’s most family-friendly hotels](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/11/41/Calcot-Manor-Spa-kids-room.jpeg)

Hidden among 220 acres of dreamy Cotswolds countryside, cosy rural retreat Calcot & Spa proves you don't need to get on a flight for exceptional childcare. Families receive four hours complimentary each day at the Playbarn, a bright space packed with crafts, teddies, imaginative play toys and an enviable fancy dress collection.

While little ones are happily occupied, you can properly switch off in the award-winning spa, whether that's soaking in the fireside hot tub or lingering over a leisurely lunch with a glass of bubbles.

Thoughtful touches continue throughout the stay, from a daily kids' tea at 5pm – complete with baby buffet – to bibs, Doddl cutlery (no need to carry around sticky baby gear, hurrah!) and family-friendly rooms with bunk beds.

**Address:** Gloucestershire, Tetbury GL8 8YJ

**Price:** From £442 B&B

## 8. H10 Suites Lanzarote Gardens hotel, Lanzarote

![At Daisy Club, kids can enjoy games and themed activities tailored to their age](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/05/12/15/51/12_HLGPool10.jpg)

For families who want brilliant childcare without eye-watering prices, H10 Suites Lanzarote Gardens consistently punches above its weight. Just moments from the golden sands of Las Cucharas Beach, this cheerful and compact all-inclusive resort welcomes children at its Daisy Club to enjoy games, crafts and themed activities tailored to their age, alongside a baby club, playgrounds, splash pools and a packed schedule of mini discos and treasure hunts.

Spacious apartment-style accommodation makes life that bit easier when you’re bringing active younger children and all the paraphernalia you end up packing into your suitcase, while the all-inclusive packages take the stress out of budgeting for every drink and snack.

**Address:** Av. de las Islas Canarias, 13, 35508 Costa Teguise, Las Palmas, Spain

**Price:** From £204

## 9. AKI Family Resort Plose, Italy

![AKI Kids World keeps younger guests entertained from morning until evening](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/10/06/AKI-Family-Resort-PLOSE-kids-club.jpeg)

One of Europe’s most impressive alpine retreats, AKI Family Resort in South Tyrol proves that family holidays don't need a beach to make a splash. Water is woven throughout the experience, from baby-friendly sessions and paddling areas to pools designed for children to build confidence in the water through play.

Away from the pools, AKI Kids World keeps younger guests entertained from morning until evening with expert childcare, woodland adventures, climbing towers and even farm animals – a sure-fire hit even with shy youngsters – leaving parents free to explore the spectacular hiking trails of the surrounding Dolomites.

**Address:** Via Koja, Meluno, 20, 39042 Bressanone BZ, Italy

**Price:** From £1,199

## 10. Falkensteiner Family Hotel Diadora, Croatia

![Falkensteiner is situated on Croatia's Adriatic coast](<[https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/07/25/17/52/Falkensteiner-Schlosshotel-Velden_exterior-(c](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/07/25/17/52/Falkensteiner-Schlosshotel-Velden_exterior-(c))-FMTG.jpeg>)

Overlooking Croatia's Adriatic coast, Falkensteiner Family Hotel Diadora elevates the classic beach resort into something far more stylish. At the heart of this sizeable hotel is Falky Land, a colourful indoor-outdoor world where babies, toddlers and teens disappear into days filled with cookery classes, science experiments, sports and crafting.

Beyond the kids’ club there are family pools with splash zones and slides, adventure playgrounds and a Blue Flag beach just a short stroll away. Don’t miss out on a massage at the Acquapura Spa before getting the gang back together over a delicious dinner on the terrace as the sun sets over the sea.

**Address:** Falkensteiner Resort, Punta Skala, 23231, Zadar, Croatia

**Price:** From £252

## 11. Hotel Royal Évian Resort, France

![The professionally run Baby Club welcomes mini travellers from just four months](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/9/30/The-Baby-Club-at-Hotel-Royal-vian-Resort.jpeg)

Hotel Royal Évian is one of Europe's most elegant luxury resorts, yet despite its grand Belle Époque surroundings, it's a haven for families travelling with babies.

Set on the tranquil shores of Lake Geneva, the professionally run Baby Club welcomes mini travellers from just four months, with sensory play, music, crafts and gentle activities led by experienced carers, giving you the peace of mind to actually enjoy a massage, a round on the championship golf course, or just sit and admire the lake views with a good book in hand.

**Address:** 960 Av. du Léman, 74500 Évian-les-Bains, France

**Price:** From £379

## 12. Peligoni, Zakynthos

![Peligoni’s creches and kids' clubs cater for every age group](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/10/21/Peligoni-Club-kids-club.jpeg)

Part members' club, part boutique resort, Peligoni offers a different take on the traditional family holiday. Rather than staying in a main hotel, you’ll check in to one of around 60 villas, cottages and suites scattered across the hills, before spending your days at the stylish seaside club, where professionally run creches and kids' clubs cater for every age group.

Younger children can enjoy music sessions, treasure hunts, messy play and shaded outdoor adventures before napping in the peaceful sleep room, while older ones graduate to sailing, paddle boarding and watersports on the turquoise Ionian Sea. That’s your cue to join a yoga class, take to the waves yourself or just savour a long lunch of fresh seafood without a colouring book in sight.

**Address:** VPW7+XF Agios Nikolaos, Volimes, Ag. Nikolaos 290 91, Greece

**Price:** Membership and villa prices vary
