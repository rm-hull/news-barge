A dessert parlour that opened on the North Yorkshire coast is already proving a hit on social media thanks to its indulgent desserts.

Whether it's a traditional lemon top on the seafront or a towering sundae packed with choice toppings, J.Becketts of Saltburn is making waves just months into opening.

The dessert parlour, which opened in April this year, sits inside Saltburn's Victorian Station Buildings on Station Square, just a short walk from the pier and cliff lift.

At the heart of the parlour is its ice cream, with customers able to choose from classic scoops, traditional whippy ice cream and a range of sundaes made to order.

  The popular blueberry panna cotta sundae. 

*(Image: J Becketts of Saltburn)*

The dessert shop is particularly proud of its Saltburn lemon tops - the North Yorkshire seaside favourite combining vanilla whippy ice cream with a sharp lemon twist topping.

Visitors can choose from an extensive menu of sundaes inspired by flavours such as Biscoff, Kinder, mint chocolate, salted caramel, toffee fudge and white chocolate.

For those who decide there is too much to choose from, there is also the option to build a personalised sundae where you can choose your own scoops, toppings and sauces to create a mix-and-match that doesn't leave any of your favourite flavours out.

  Caramel latte sundae. 

*(Image: J Becketts of Saltburn)*

The ice cream counter offers a variety of flavours throughout the year, with dairy-free vanilla, strawberry and chocolate options always on offer.

Gluten-free cones also available for your ice creams.

The business has also built a following for its drinks menu, serving everything from americanos and cappuccinos to iced lattes and hot chocolates topped with cream and marshmallows.

One of the parlour's most popular recent additions has been its iced matcha latte, which can be customised with syrups including white chocolate, strawberry, cherry, blueberry, vanilla and raspberry.

  Fresh strawberries. 

*(Image: J Becketts of Saltburn)*

Customers looking for something more indulgent can opt for warm cookie dough topped with ice cream, plus there are a range of freshly blended milkshakes are available in flavours including Oreo, Kinder, Ferrero Rocher, Biscoff and Nutella.

Dogs are welcome inside and can even enjoy their own dog-friendly ice cream while their owners tuck into a lemon top or coffee.

  Doggy treats are always on offer for your furry friends. 

*(Image: J Becketts of Saltburn)*

Owners say the aim is to create a welcoming atmosphere for everyone, whether arriving with sandy feet after a beach walk, pushing a pram or meeting friends for a catch-up.

Open daily from 11am until 7pm, J. Becketts has quickly established itself as a year-round destination rather than somewhere reserved for sunny summer days.

With ice cream, coffee, matcha, milkshakes and desserts all under one roof, the business is hoping to prove there is room for a traditional seaside treat alongside more modern favourites.
