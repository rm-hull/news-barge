# Bruce Willis embraces daughter Tallulah at wedding in rare public appearance

- Bruce Willis has made a rare appearance at his daughter Tallulah’s wedding to musician Justin Acee in Idaho.
- Photos published in Vogue showed the 71-year-old actor embracing Tallulah, who described the interaction as a tender moment.
- Willis has largely remained out of the public eye since being diagnosed with frontotemporal dementia three years ago.
- Tallulah, 32, is the youngest of the actor's three daughters with his former wife, Demi Moore.
- His wife, Emma Heming Willis, recently discussed managing caregiver guilt while navigating her husband's ongoing health battle.
