Police are appealing for witnesses after a man suffered a suspected broken nose during an assault in Ripon.

The incident happened at around 4pm on Sunday 2nd August on Skellbank and involved an altercation between two men.

One of the men sustained a suspected broken nose as a result of the incident.

North Yorkshire Police is now asking anyone who witnessed the altercation or has information that could assist its investigation to come forward.

Information can be provided by emailing [Gareth.Skelton@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Gareth.Skelton@northyorkshire.police.uk) or by contacting North Yorkshire Police online.

*Anyone unable to use the online contact options can call police on 101.*

*Information can also be provided anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*

*Anyone contacting police should quote reference 12260148688.*
