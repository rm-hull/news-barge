# Iran ‘drafts plans to hit US bases in Europe’ should tensions escalate

- Iran has reportedly drafted plans to target US military assets in Europe, including in Bulgaria and Cyprus, should the conflict escalate.
- Iranian forces have also examined targeting subsea fibre-optic cables located in the Strait of Hormuz.
- US President Donald Trump stated there are no talks scheduled with Iran while claiming the Strait of Hormuz as New US territory.
- The United Arab Emirates has imposed an indefinite trade embargo on Iran after accusing Tehran of launching two ballistic missiles at the country.
- Iran has firmly rejected the UAE allegations, describing them as entirely baseless.
