# Stoke-on-Trent fire cause revealed after investigation

- An investigation concluded that a devastating grass fire in Sandyford Park, Stoke-on-Trent, which severely damaged four homes in the Goldenhill area, was accidentally caused by a disposable barbecue.
- High wind speeds and extremely dry conditions caused the blaze to spread rapidly, destroying roofs, blowing out windows, and forcing local residents to evacuate to a nearby community centre.
- Four firefighters were taken to hospital due to smoke inhalation and heat exhaustion, with three since discharged and one remaining in hospital for treatment.
- Following several wild blazes across Wales and the West Midlands, a temporary ban on disposable barbecues was announced to prevent further incidents during the ongoing hot weather.
- Station manager Liam Hickey highlighted the severe impact of disposable barbecues and urged the public to adhere to safety rules to protect communities and reduce pressure on emergency services.
