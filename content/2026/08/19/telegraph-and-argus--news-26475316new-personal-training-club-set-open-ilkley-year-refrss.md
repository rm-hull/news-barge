A NEW fitness club promising "a new standard of training" is set to open later this year.

The Old Armoury Training Club will launch in Ilkley in October, focusing on small group personal training, strength and conditioning, and expert coaching.

A spokesperson said: "If you’re looking to build strength, improve your fitness or simply train as part of a great community, The OA is your place."

The club is designed to help people reach their fitness goals in a supportive and motivating environment.

It aims to combine professional coaching with a strong sense of community.

The team behind the club have encouraged people to keep an eye on social media for further updates.

Their Instagram handle is @theoatrainingclub.
