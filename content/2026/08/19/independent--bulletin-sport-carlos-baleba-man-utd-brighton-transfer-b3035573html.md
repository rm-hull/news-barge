# Carlos Baleba: Manchester United make £65m move for Brighton midfielder

![Carlos Baleba could finally be on the move to Old Trafford](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/19/11/a7f20c945421eae985f967f14fbaaa57Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg3MjE3NjU0-2.83454727.jpg)

- Manchester United have submitted a £60m bid, which could rise to £65m with add-ons, for Brighton midfielder Carlos Baleba.
- Brighton are seeking a higher fee for the 22-year-old Cameroon international, but negotiations between the two clubs are progressing.
- United revived their interest in Baleba after missing out on alternative targets Mateus Fernandes, Elliot Anderson, and Atalanta's Ederson.
- An ankle injury currently affecting Baleba is not anticipated to prevent the transfer from going through.
- The club have already signed Youri Tielemans and Andrey Santos this summer, but need further midfield depth following Casemiro's departure and recent injuries.
