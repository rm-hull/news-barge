# Most Missouri waterways face fecal contamination risk, report finds

![Swimming or wading in water contaminated with fecal matter can lead to gastrointestinal and respiratory illnesses](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/19/15/09/iStock-482989003-3a516a2d.jpeg)

- A report by Environment America found potentially unsafe fecal contamination levels in 85 percent of Missouri's monitored recreational waterways in 2024 and 2025.
- Contamination was detected at 171 out of 202 testing locations, including high-risk sites like the Harry S. Truman Reservoir, Grand Glaize Creek and Sycamore Creek.
- Health experts warn that swimming or wading in contaminated water can lead to skin, eye, ear, respiratory and gastrointestinal infections.
- Primary causes of the bacterial pollution include stormwater runoff, sewage overflows, livestock manur and failing septic and wastewater systems.
- Across all 17 states evaluated in the study, 69 percent of tested waterway locations showed potentially unsafe contamination levels on at least one day.
