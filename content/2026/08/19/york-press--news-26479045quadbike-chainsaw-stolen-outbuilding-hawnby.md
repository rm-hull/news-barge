SIGHTINGS of suspicious vehicles are being sought following a burglary in a North Yorkshire village.

A quadbike and chainsaw were stolen from Hawnby, near Helmsley between July 28 and August 11.

Police said outbuildings were searched, and a Honda Foreman 450 quad bike (dark green) and a Husqvarna 435 chainsaw (bright orange with a grey handle) were taken.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We’re particularly appealing for information about any suspicious vehicles in the area around the time of the offence - in particular, any silver-coloured 4x4 vehicles seen late at night.

"If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us by emailing [adam.stubbs@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:adam.stubbs@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Please quote reference 12260155599.
