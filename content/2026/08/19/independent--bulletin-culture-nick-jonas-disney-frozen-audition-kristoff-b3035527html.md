# Nick Jonas revealed he ‘bombed’ audition for Disney’s Frozen

- Nick Jonas revealed on his Hey Jonas! podcast that he failed an audition for the role of Kristoff in Disney's hit animated film Frozen.
- The 33-year-old singer and actor explained that he bombed the audition after arriving without any sheet music.
- The role of Kristoff ultimately went to Glee star Jonathan Groff for the 2013 release, which made £885 million at the global box office.
- Jonas discussed the failed audition during an interview with Frozen stars Kristen Bell, Idina Menzel, and Josh Gad.
- Despite missing out on the lucrative franchise, Jonas admitted he loved the movie and playfully suggested he should have been cast instead.
