FIREFIGHTERS were called to a kitchen blaze on a Bradford street last night.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to Cape Street, near the city centre, at 8.16pm on Tuesday.

Three fire engines attended and extinguished what was described as a small kitchen fire.

No injuries were reported.

Images shared on social media showed a large response at the scene.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the cause of the fire was recorded as cooking.

## Statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said: "We were called to Cape Street at 8.16pm to reports of a fire.

"Three crews attended and extinguished a small kitchen fire.

"No reports of any injuries - the cause was recorded as cooking."
