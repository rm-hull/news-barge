Paramount+ customers can expect their bill to increase later this month.

If you pay for the popular streaming service, you could pay up to £2 more per month (£24 per year), with the current price for the standard plan being £7.99 and rising to £9.99.

Three, a phone provider which offers Paramount+ as part of some of its plans, says the price will increase from August 27.

Existing Three customers with Paramount+ included in their plan won’t need to do anything, and prices won’t increase for them until their inclusive period ends.

If you don’t have the streaming platform included in your Three plan, you can expect to see the price increase.

Customers are also able to cancel their Paramount+ subscription if they’d prefer.

## Paramount+'s new prices for UK customers

GB News has reportedly confirmed the new prices with Paramount+, with all available plans increasing in price before the end of this month.

The Basic with Ads plan, Paramount+'s cheapest option, will rise from £4.99 to £5.99, and Paramount+ already states that plans start from £5.99 on its website.

If viewers want to pay for the Standard plan, it will set them back £9.99 per month, instead of £7.99.

Premium Paramount+ customers will be charged £12.99 a month instead of £10.99.

Customers can expect an increase of up to £24 a year if they use the Standard or Premium plans.

Paramount+ customers can watch TV shows and films such as Yellowstone, Gladiator II, Dexter: Original Sin, and more.

Newsquest has approached Channel 5 for comment.
