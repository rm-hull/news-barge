Celtic will take a commanding 3-0 lead into Tuesday’s Champions League play-off second leg against Austrian champions LASK after a special night for new signing Camilo Duran.

The 24-year-old Colombian forward scored two stunning goals, after Benjamin Nygren had thumped Celtic ahead with a fine volley from the edge of the box, in a brilliant overall performance from Martin O’Neill’s men.

Despite the emphatic scoreline, visitors LASK created plenty of chances and twice had the ball in the net in the first half only to be denied by tight offside calls, so could still be a threat in Austria next week.

But this was a near-perfect night for the Scottish Premiership winners, who have lost their last five Champions League play-off ties, as the 74-year-old O’Neill delivered another memorable night on his Celtic return.

*Follow updates from Celtic v LASK in the Champions League play-offs, below*

## Champions League play-off second leg coming up on Tuesday

Celtic don’t have a match this weekend with the Premiership having a weekend off to help Scottish clubs progress in Europe.

LASK don’t play, either, so it’s full focus on Tuesday as LASK host Celtic looking to overturn their 3-0 defeat tonight.

## Camilo Duran: Celtic's new hero?

What a night for this man. His first two goals at Celtic Park were of the highest quality and have really announced this 24-year-old Colombian forward to the Celtic fans.

## Celtic in a wonderful position

Could Celtic’s heartbreak in the Champions League play-offs be coming to an end? They have lost their last five ties in qualifying but even a 2-0 defeat in Linz next week would see them through to the league phase for the third time in four years.

They’re in a great position.
