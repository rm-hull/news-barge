Comedy in the Kitchen is returning to Darley Street Market this November.

The comedy night, held in Bradford’s Darley Street Kitchen, will take place on Thursday 26 November, starting at 7pm.

Bradford Markets has confirmed the return of comedian Anthony J Brown.

A spokesman for Bradford Markets said: "We’re already looking forward to welcoming Anthony J Brown back to the Darley Street Market for our second comedy night, alongside another great line-up of comedians."

Councillor Ian Walker, executive member for neighbourhoods and communities at Bradford Council, added: "Following the success of the first Comedy in the Kitchen evening, I’m looking forward to the second event and would recommend booking early to make sure you get your tickets and don’t miss out."

Tickets start from £15 and are available from: [https://www.ticketsource.com/bradford-markets](https://www.ticketsource.com/bradford-markets)
