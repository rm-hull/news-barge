# Michelle Rodriguez among names to pay tribute to Hayden Panettiere

- American actress Hayden Panettiere has died at her home in Greenville, South Carolina, at the age of 36.
- Greenville County Coroner's Office stated an autopsy showed no signs of trauma, with the cause of death pending further investigation.
- Michelle Rodriguez paid tribute to Panettiere, praising her curious and joyous spirit while raising concerns over the recent deaths of young people.
- Ice Princess director Tim Fywell and actors Blake Lively and Alexa PenaVega honoured Panettiere alongside her late co-star Michelle Trachtenberg.
- Panettiere's father Skip described his daughter as an incredible light and a force of nature who brought immeasurable joy to millions.
