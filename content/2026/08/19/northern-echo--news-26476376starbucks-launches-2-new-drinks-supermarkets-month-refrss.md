Starbucks is launching two new chilled drinks across UK supermarkets throughout August.

The coffee chain has introduced Matcha Latte and Strawberry Matcha Latte to its ready-to-drink range.

Each drink has a recommended retail price of £2, though prices may vary by retailer.

The Matcha Latte blends milk with matcha, while the Strawberry Matcha Latte adds a fruity twist, as reported by creatorzine.com.

  Starbucks is launching two new drinks for customers to buy during their weekly shop at supermarkets 

*(Image: Jam Press/Starbucks)*

Both drinks are permanent additions to the Starbucks chilled coffee range, not limited-edition releases.

The Matcha Latte comes in green packaging, while the Strawberry Matcha Latte features a pink and green design.

Starbucks executive Nik Dhodi confirmed the new range had launched across several European, Middle Eastern and African markets.


Announcing the news on LinkedIn, he said: "Matcha is having a moment, and for good reason.

"Consumers are looking for new rituals, balanced energy and great taste.

"We’re thrilled to bring that to life with our new Starbucks RTD Matcha Latte and Matcha Latte Strawberry across several EMEA markets."

The seasonal favourite, priced at £2.20, is currently on shelves at Sainsbury’s.
