A County Durham shop is applying for a licence to sell alcohol, according to a public notice.

S&R Retailing Ltd has applied to Durham County Council for permission to sell alcohol for consumption off the premises at a shop at 3 Browns Buildings, Front Street, Dipton, near Stanley.

The council keeps a record of this application, which can be viewed at the Licensing Service, Annand House, John Street North, Meadowfield, Durham, DH7 8RS.

It is available for inspection by appointment on weekdays between 9.30am and 4.30pm.

A summary can also be viewed online at durham.gov.uk.

Members of the public can express their views on the application.

Any written representation must be made and received by the licensing authority no later than September 8.

The representations can be sent to Durham County Council, Licensing Service, PO Box 274, Stanley, Durham DH8 1HG or emailed to [licensing@durham.gov.uk](mailto:licensing@durham.gov.uk) .
