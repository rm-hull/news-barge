Police in Craven have issued an appeal following the theft of a farm trailer.

The 10ft by 5ft Graham Edwards 3.5 galvanised tipping trailer was taken from a farm in Plompton, south of Harrogate, in the early hours of Tuesday, August 11.

A spokesperson the Craven Neighbourhood Policing Team said: “A 10 foot x 5 foot 'Graham Edwards' 3.5 galvanised tipping trailer was stolen from a farm in Plompton HG5 postcode on Tuesday, August 11, at 1am.

“If you have any information about the theft or have seen this trailer contact the officer in the case PC 1864 Marshall at [Fiona.Marshall@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Fiona.Marshall@northyorkshire.police.uk) or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

“If you know the current whereabouts of this trailer or spot it on the roads, please contact North Yorkshire Police on 101

“Please quote reference 12260155673 when passing on information.”
