# Queen Camilla says King worked despite cancer diagnosis to ‘cope’

- Queen Camilla has revealed King Charles's defiant reaction upon learning of his cancer diagnosis.
- Speaking in a video to mark the 30th anniversary of Maggie's cancer charity, Camilla discussed the moment for the first time.
- King Charles chose to focus on his work to cope with the illness, telling his wife: “This is my way. I'm going to cope with it”.
- Queen Camilla admitted that keeping her husband's diagnosis a secret before the public announcement was challenging.
- She disclosed that she almost said something about the news earlier, but determined the timing was not right.
