# Sheffield Trees: some good people need a little help - together, we can do this...

![Sheffield Trees crowdfunder: The Yorkshire Post hopes donations can be found to help plant street trees in Sheffield as part of this small project by community groups in the city https://sponsor.treesforstreets.org/crowdfunder/lets-fill-bents-green-and-burngreave-with-trees (photo, PA)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjNmNjEyMGQ2LTk0N2YtNDM0OC05ZDg5LWEzNDE5OTg2NmFiNzowNWQyMTM0Ny00NjNlLTQ1NmItYWVkMi0yMTk1YzI2NmFiZGM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=70&enable=upscale)



## Friends of this newspaper in Sheffield who trusted The Yorkshire Post to partner with them in a ferocious campaign to protect the street trees in the Steel City from a city council that was hell-bent on destroying them, will recall the battle all too well.

It should be said that under the more honest and authentic stewardship of now-Chief Executive Kate Josephs, Sheffield City Council is a more listening local authority, better in tune with its residents.

And some of those residents, community groups and campaigners, are embarking upon a mission to bring more street trees to the city, between Burngreave and Bents Green. In relative terms, it is a modest ambition to plant 14 trees, seven in each suburb, yet this newspaper knows what it means to those committed to the job at hand.

A crowdfunding campaign has been set up, with £9,100 the target. Could it be that we all help them get there before the weekend arrives?
