A world-famous rockstar was spotted in a North Yorkshire market recently, browsing stalls and chatting with fans just hours before a sell-out show.

Alice Cooper was spotted enjoying a low-key outing on Saturday, August 15, ahead of Hollywood Vampires' appearance at Scarborough Open Air Theatre later that evening.

The 78-year-old American singer, famed for his theatrical shock-rock performances, spent time exploring Scarborough Market Hall without any obvious security presence, happily stopping to speak to fans.

  Alice Cooper posing with a fan "kissing his ring" in the market hall. 

*(Image: Lucy Webb)*

Photos shared online showed the musician posing for pictures during his visit.

One showed Cooper with a fan after he jokingly responded to a request to "kiss his ring".

The singer also called into the popular market stall, Bijou Creations, where he grabbed one of the shop's art tote bags.

  Alice Cooper paying a visit to Bijou Creations in the Scarborough Market Hall on Saturday, August 15. 

*(Image: Bijou Creations)*

Bijou Creations shared a picture of Cooper with one of their totes, writing: "Had a surprise customer in my shop today a gentleman by the name of Alice Cooper.

"He allowed me to take a picture of him modelling one of my Art Tote bags he had just purchased. He was lovely to talk to, very down to earth."

Based inside Scarborough Market Hall, the shop sells original artwork, prints, novelty gifts, accessories, kirigami pop-up cards and its distinctive art tote bags.

News of Cooper's visit quickly went viral, with shoppers and fans sharing photographs and stories after spotting the rock star around the market and elsewhere in the town.

  Hollywood Vampires at Scarborough Open Air Theatre on Saturday night (August 15).

Later that evening, Cooper joined Johnny Depp, Joe Perry and Tommy Henriksen at Scarborough Open Air Theatre as Hollywood Vampires performed to a sold-out crowd of around 8,000 people.

The Scarborough date formed part of the band's first UK tour in almost three years, with punk legends The Damned opening as special guests.

Before the dramatic stage outfits, rock anthems and packed-out venue, Cooper seemed happy to spend part of his day enjoying a browse around the market and a drink in a local pub.
