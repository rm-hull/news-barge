# Former supermodel says she was ‘locked away’ after abuse allegations

![Karen Mulder has broken her silence after more than 20 years away from the spotlight](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/02/11/8/53/PA-1226337.jpeg)

- Dutch supermodel Karen Mulder spoke out in her first interview in over 20 years, claiming she was “silenced” and “locked away” after making abuse allegations in 2001.
- During a 2001 French television appearance, Mulder accused high-profile modeling figures, European royalty, politicians, and police officers of rape.
- Following the 2001 interview, Mulder was placed in a private psychiatric clinic and forced to state that she made up the allegations.
- Mulder revealed to *Vogue**Germany* that she endured inhumane treatment in the fashion industry, hoping her fame would compel people to believe her.
- Although she never met Jeffrey Epstein personally, social media users now view Mulder as an early whistleblower amid the MeToo movement.
