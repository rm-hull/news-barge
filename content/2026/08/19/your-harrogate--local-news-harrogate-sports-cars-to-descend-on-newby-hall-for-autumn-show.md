Car enthusiasts are set to descend on Newby Hall next month as a popular motoring show returns to the historic estate.

Autumn Sports Cars in the Park will take place on Sunday 6th September, bringing together sports cars from clubs and individual owners across the UK.

Visitors can expect to see a wide range of prestigious marques, including Aston Martin, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Porsche and Lotus.

The event will also feature a selection of trade stands and market stalls, with owners still able to register their vehicles to exhibit at the show.

Gates will be open from 10am until 3pm.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/69e5ef9a68ecd.jpg)


Tickets are priced at £22 for adults and £16 for children aged four to 15, while under-fours can attend for free.

A family ticket for two adults and two children is priced at £65.

Entry to the show also includes access to Newby Hall's award-winning gardens, including its double herbaceous borders and newly restored Edwardian rock garden.

Visitors will also be able to enjoy the adventure playground and dollhouse and teddy bear exhibitions, with access to the house, miniature railway and river boat rides also included.

Stuart Gill, Commercial Director at Newby Hall, said:

“We look forward to the return of the show which is always popular with visitors who can enjoy a wide range of prestigious car marques in the unique setting of Newby’s magnificent parkland.

“The added attractions of our award-winning gardens and children’s activities make the event a great family day out for everyone.”



*For more information and to buy tickets for the show, visit: __www.newbyhall.com__*
