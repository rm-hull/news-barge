A North East wrestling star is headlining a family-friendly wrestling event in Teesside later this month.

The World Wide Wrestling League (W3L) will return to Queens Campus Sports Centre in Stockton on Friday, August 28, with local promotor Dropkick Championship Wrestling for an evening of action featuring six matches.

Leading the bill will be Hartlepool's Adam "Shreddy" Foster, who has made his mark on the world stage in recent years, with appearances for American wrestling organisations TNA Wrestling and All Elite Wrestling.

  Adam "Shreddy" Foster will headline a Stockton wrestling event later this month. 

*(Image: W3L Wrestling)*

Away from the ring, Shreddy is also a seasoned bodybuilder - winning the Mr Britain title and becoming a NABBA World Champion.

He is competing for the NABBA Universe Championship in November.

Shreddy will take on 300lb Canadian wrestler Tim Strange in a strength-versus-strength contest in Stockton, with the local favourite hoping to body slam his heavyweight opponent and take home the win.

The Teesside match will also feature a championship match presented by Dropkick Championship Wrestling, with reigning champion Martin Kirby defending his title against local wrestler Spawn.

Others confirmed for the show include Scottish fan favourite "Irn" Drew Marshall and Pakistan's Saqib Ali.

W3L, which has been touring the UK for more than 20 years, hold events suitable for families and wrestling fans of all ages.

Mike Musgrave, promoter for W3L, said the organisation was looking forward to returning to Stockton and giving the local wrestling scene a boost with the highly-anticipated event.

"We are excited to be presenting Shreddy to the Stockton fans at such an exciting time in his career," he said.

  Adam "Shreddy" Foster will headline a Stockton wrestling event later this month. 

*(Image: W3L Wrestling)*

The match will also be filmed for W3L Wrestling Showdown - the longest-running weekly wrestling broadcast on YouTube.

General admission tickets cost £12, while family tickets for four people are £44.

A limited number of £5 bench seats and ringside tickets are also available.

Doors open at 6.30pm, with the first bell scheduled for 7pm, and tickets can be purchased via the W3L Wrestling website.
