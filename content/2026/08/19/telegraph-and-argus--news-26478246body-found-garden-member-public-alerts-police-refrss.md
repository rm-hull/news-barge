A BODY has been found in the search for a missing Bradford district man.

West Yorkshire Police confirmed the discovery of a body is understood to be 57-year-old John - the force does not issue surnames for missing people.

Police say a call was received from a member of the public in Keighley who had found a man’s body in the garden of their property.

The force adds that the death is not being treated as suspicious.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for the force said: "A body has sadly been found in the search for John, 57, who was reported missing from home in Keighley.

"Shortly before 4.45pm yesterday, a call was received from a member of the public in Keighley who had found a man’s body in the garden of their property.

"The body is understood to be John, who was the subject of an earlier missing person appeal.

"His family have been informed.

"The death is not being treated as suspicious, and a file will be prepared for the coroner."
