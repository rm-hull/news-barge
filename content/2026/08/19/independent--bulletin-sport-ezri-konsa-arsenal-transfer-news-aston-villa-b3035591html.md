# Ezri Konsa: Arsenal closing in on deal to sign England defender from Aston Villa

![Ezri Konsa has been linked with a move to Arsenal](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/06/09/01KWV88K16XNCN9M615F3QY8Q3.jpg)

- Arsenal are growing increasingly confident of completing a deal to sign Aston Villa defender Ezri Konsa.
- The Gunners are seeking defensive reinforcements following injury setbacks to William Saliba and Jurrien Timber.
- Aston Villa value Konsa at £60m, though it remains unclear whether Arsenal will match the asking price or negotiate a lower fee.
- Konsa is reportedly keen on making the move to the North London club.
- The defender was a key member of Thomas Tuchel’s England squad which reached the World Cup semi-finals this summer.
