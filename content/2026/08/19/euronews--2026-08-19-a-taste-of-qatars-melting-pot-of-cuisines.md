## Qatar in Motion heads to some of Doha’s top culinary spots. We visit the Michelin-starred Alba where creativity and craftsmanship elevate fine dining. At Bib Gourmand-recognised Baron, innovative flavours and a relaxed dining experience reflect the diversity of Qatar’s modern food culture.

Qatar in Motion explores the evolution of Qatar’s culinary landscape - a food scene shaped by tradition, ambition, and the cultures that call the country home. From authentic Qatari flavours to global gastronomy, Qatar has become a melting pot of international cuisines. From the one-Michelin starred Alba at Raffles Doha to the Bib Gourmand restaurant, Baron at the Old Doha Port, we give you a taste of how Qatar’s dining scene has transformed from a growing appetite for international flavours to the emergence of a distinctive culinary identity of its own.
