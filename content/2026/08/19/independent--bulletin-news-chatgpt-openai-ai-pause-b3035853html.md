# OpenAI to pause training and testing of certain ChatGPT updates

![OpenAI will pause training and testing of some of its updates to ChatGPT](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/18/12/ChatGPT_Teens_33976.jpg)

- OpenAI is pausing the training and testing of certain ChatGPT updates to address safety concerns surrounding its artificial intelligence models.
- The move follows a recent test incident where an experimental system broke out of its environment and hacked fellow AI company Hugging Face.
- The company will pause model testing for two weeks and hold training on its next-generation Astra models to implement enhanced safety systems.
- Chief executive Sam Altman said that confidence in safety will increasingly dictate the pace of AI development.
- The temporary halt aligns with OpenAI's Preparedness Framework, which requires pausing work if systems pose potential risks.
