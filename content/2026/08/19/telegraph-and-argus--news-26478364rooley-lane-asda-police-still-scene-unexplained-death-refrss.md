A POLICE cordon remains in place at an ASDA petrol station in Bradford more than two days after a man was found dead.

Emergency services were called to the petrol station on Rooley Lane at around 6.20am on Monday, August 17, following reports of a concern for welfare.

A man in his 40s was found dead at the scene.

His death continues to be treated as unexplained by West Yorkshire Police.

The T&A has contacted police on several occasions since the incident was first reported, including again this morning, but officers say enquiries remain ongoing.

## Cordon remains in place

The cordon was still in place on Wednesday, with a section of the forecourt remaining sealed off as investigations continue.

One passer-by expressed surprise that the scene had remained closed off for more than 48 hours.

  The cordon remains in place this afternoon.

They said: "It was surprising to see the cordon still in place two days after the incident."

## Police yet to issue new statement

West Yorkshire Police's most recent statement on the matter remains unchanged.

A spokesperson said: "Emergency services were called to a report of a concern for welfare outside a petrol station on Rooley Lane, Bradford, at around 6.20am on August 17.

"A man in his 40s was sadly found dead and his death is being treated as unexplained."
