Police have issued a description of suspects after a chainsaw was stolen from a York.

It was one of a number of tools, also including a strimmer and hedge cutter, taken from a greenhouse in **Ousecliffe Gardens**.

It happened at about **4.45am** on**Monday 10 August**.

#### Ousecliffe Gardens, York

The one or possibly two male suspects are about 6ft tall, of stocky build and were wearing dark clothing.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re particularly appealing for information about the items that were stolen, any CCTV or video doorbell footage, or from anyone who saw the suspects.”
