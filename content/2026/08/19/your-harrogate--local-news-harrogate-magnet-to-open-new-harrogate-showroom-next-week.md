Kitchen retailer Magnet is set to open its new showroom in Harrogate town centre next week.

The company is relocating its Harrogate branch from Plantation House on Ripon Road to Albert Street.

Magnet has confirmed the new showroom will open on Tuesday 25th August.

The Ripon Road showroom is currently closed while the relocation takes place, although customers are still able to book virtual appointments.

Magnet's new showroom will offer kitchen displays, storage solutions and free design appointments, with customers able to meet designers to plan their kitchens.

The Harrogate team is headed up by Store Manager Michael Higgins, alongside kitchen designers Jonathan Warner and Robert Goldsbrough and Trade Sales representative Tom Newton.

Magnet has more than 150 showrooms across the UK, including other Yorkshire branches in Guiseley, Bingley and Skipton.
