POLICE are appealing for help to locate a wanted man from Leeds.

Jason Henriques, 35, is wanted on recall to prison and in connection with several offences in Bradford and Leeds, including stalking and assault.

Despite extensive enquiries, officers have so far been unable to trace him and are now asking the public for assistance.

He is described as being of medium build, around 5ft 4ins tall, and is believed to live in the LS9 area.

He also has links to East Leeds and Bradford.

Anyone with information about his whereabouts is asked to contact police in Leeds via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat, quoting reference 13260340168.

Information can also be given anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555111 or through their website.
