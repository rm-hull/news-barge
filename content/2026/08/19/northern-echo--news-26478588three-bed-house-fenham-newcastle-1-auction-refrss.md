This three-bedroom house could be yours for just £1, but it may need a bit of work.

The home is set to go to auction with no reserve and a starting bid price of £1.

The three-bedroom semi-detached house, in Fenham, Newcastle, could be snapped up for less than the price of an average loaf of bread.

   

*(Image: PATTINSON)*

   

*(Image: PATTINSON)*

The house has one bathroom, one reception room and a front and rear garden.

   

*(Image: PATTINSON)*

In need of renovation throughout, the house on Bruce Gardens could be a perfect investment opportunity or rung onto the property ladder for a buyer willing to take on a project.

   

*(Image: PATTINSON)*

Pictures of the property show it has fallen into disrepair but has kept a lot of original features, including its windows.

The property also offers on-street parking.
