A new Pepe’s Piri Piri restaurant has opened in Darlington, creating 15 jobs.

The chicken chain opened its at Cornmill Shopping Centre on this morning (Wednesday, August 19) offering takeaway, collection, and dine-in food.

Customers can choose from the brand’s flame-grilled chicken range, including whole, half, and quarter chickens, wings, and tender strips.

The menu also includes burgers, salads, Mexican-inspired burritos and nachos, as well as the Chick’n’Rice box which has proved popular on TikTok and Instagram.

The Darlington branch is owned by Ammar, an experienced hospitality operator who has multiple sites across the UK.

Matt Hart, Pepe’s Piri Piri’s head of franchising, said the opening takes the chain to more than 265 restaurants across the country.

He said: "It’s hard to believe that it was just back in 2007 that we opened the first Pepe’s Piri Piri in Watford, and the store in Darlington takes us to more than 265 stores across the UK.

"We’re proud to be able to offer exciting franchising opportunities that can turn someone’s dream of owning and running their own business into a reality, and we’re looking forward to the success that we know Ammar will have in Darlington.

"Of course, we’re also delighted to bring our delicious fresh cooking methods and range of tasty flavours to the people of Darlington who are in for a real treat."

Ammar added: "We are thrilled to be bringing Pepe’s to the Darlington area.

"The team are excited to fire up the grills and get cooking in this local community, which we’ll be proud to serve for what we’re sure will be many years to come."

To mark the opening, the restaurant is offering 20 per cent off all in-store and collection orders on today (Wednesday, August 19).

The new branch is located at Cornmill Shopping Centre, Darlington, DL1 1LS.
