# Swiss motorcyclist killed in Manx Grand Prix qualifying collision

![Mauro Poncini died in a qualifying crash](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/19/18/58/IMG_3549-696x462-copy.jpeg)

- Swiss motorcyclist Mauro Poncini has died at the age of 29 following a crash during a qualifying session for the Manx Grand Prix.
- Poncini, who was making his debut appearance on the Isle of Man Mountain Course, was involved in a two-rider collision at Cruickshanks on Tuesday.
- The second rider involved in the incident sustained minor injuries and was taken to hospital for treatment.
- Poncini is the 272nd participant to be killed on the Isle of Man circuit since 1911, following the death of British competitor Daniel Ingham in May.
- Racing on the island remains among the most dangerous in the world, with a number of other recent crashes resulting in injuries to competitors.
