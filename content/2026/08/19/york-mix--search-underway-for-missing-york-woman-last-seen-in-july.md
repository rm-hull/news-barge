Have you seen this woman?

She’s 27-year-old Jessica Ashworth, who was last seen in the Acomb area on Saturday 25 July.

Jessica, who is from the York area, is described as 5ft 4in, white and of a proportionate build with dark straight hair.

She has a tattoo of a love heart under her right eye and has other tattoos on her arm. She speaks with a Yorkshire accent.

Jessica has links to the Acomb and Huntington area of York.

If you see Jessica, please call 999 immediately. If you have any other information police ask you to contact them online.
