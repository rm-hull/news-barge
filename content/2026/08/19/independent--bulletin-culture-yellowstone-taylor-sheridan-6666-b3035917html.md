# The Yellowstone spin-off series that has been shelved

- Yellowstone creator Taylor Sheridan has confirmed that the planned *6666* spin-off has been shelved, saying he never intended to make the show.
- Despite Paramount announcing the series in 2021, Sheridan explained he would never fictionalise the real-life Four Sixes Ranch out of respect for its working cowboys and families.
- Sheridan purchased the active Texas ranch alongside a buyer group in 2021 following the death of its previous owner.
- While 6666 will not go ahead, the wider Yellowstone universe remains active, with the sequel series Marshals returning in October and Dutton Ranch renewed for a second series.
- Dutton Ranch star Ed Harris recently revealed he felt “misled” and underused during the first series, though he is set to return for series two.
