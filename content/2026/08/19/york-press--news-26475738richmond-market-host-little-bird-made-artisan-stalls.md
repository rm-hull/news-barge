## Handmade crafts and street food return to Richmond market this weekend

Budsie Crafts *(Image: Little Bird Artisan Markets)*

Handmade crafts and street food will take centre stage at Richmond’s historic market this weekend.

The event returns to Market Place on Sunday, August 23, and will run from 10am to 3pm.

Organised by Little Bird Made, the market will showcase stalls featuring jewellery, home decor, ceramics, natural skincare, and small batch preserves.

Visitors can also enjoy street food and artisan treats while exploring the historic market town.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "We are absolutely delighted to bring our artisan market to Richmond this Sunday.

"Our mission is all about celebrating independent Yorkshire producers and giving people a reason to spend a full day exploring this beautiful market town.

"Whether you’re hunting for unique handmade crafts, picking up delicious local treats, or heading out for a scenic riverside walk with the family and the dog in Richmond afterward, it’s a fantastic summer day for the whole family."

Dawn Outen of Crafty Dawn Outen said: "Little Bird Markets are run by a well-organised team and are a pleasure to work with.

"They ensure that each market is an enjoyable day out for everyone from traders to visitors, where you’re sure to find that original gift you’ll not find anywhere else."
