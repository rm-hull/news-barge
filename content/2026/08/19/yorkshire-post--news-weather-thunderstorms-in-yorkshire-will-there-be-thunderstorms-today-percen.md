# Thunderstorms in Yorkshire: Will there be thunderstorms today? Percentage chance for Leeds, York, Sheffield, Bradford, Doncaster, Hull and Ripon - and how to find out by postcode

## There is a likelihood of thunderstorms occurring across the UK today - could it reach your area?

We have looked into the percentage chance for some major Yorkshire cities - but you can also search by your own postcode.

According to the Met Office, a thunderstorm is a series of sudden electrical discharges from a tmospheric pressure.

When the atmosphere is unpredictable, it can cause thunderstorms; it is when warm air exists underneath much colder air.

In the UK, thunderstorms can take place at any time of the year but they are the most severe during summer months, especially following a period of extremely hot days, humidity and sunny weather.

At times, thunderstorms can result in thunder and lightning, which are associated with convective clouds and are often accompanied by heavy rain or hail.

Thunderstorms are formed by intense heating of the Earth’s surface and due to the unusually hot summer we have been experiencing this year, they are predicted to reach parts of the UK.

## **How to find out percentage for your town or city by postcode**

On the Net Weather website, there is a ‘Forecasts’ tab at the top, then a drop-down menu appears with a ‘Will it?’ option.

Click on that option and then select the ‘Will it thunder’ option. You can then type in your postcode or a town/city to find out the percentage chance your area will get thunderstorms.
