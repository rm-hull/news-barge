# Trump unveils latest White House construction project

- President Donald Trump led an impromptu press tour of the White House construction site, showcasing a new granite helipad on the South Lawn.
- Trump praised the durability of the California-quarried white granite while highlighting his background in real estate and construction.
- Helicopter manufacturer Sikorsky funded the helipad to prevent powerful next-generation Marine One aircraft from burning the South Lawn grass.
- Trump disclosed design details regarding the ongoing East Wing ballroom project, describing a drone-proof roof and fortified underground structures.
- The president criticized the National Trust for Historic Preservation for suing to halt the ballroom construction, alleging the lawsuit exposed sensitive security features.
