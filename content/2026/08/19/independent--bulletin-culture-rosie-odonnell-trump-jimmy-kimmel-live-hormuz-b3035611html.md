# ‘Makes no sense’: Rosie O’Donnell takes aim at Trump’s Hormuz claim

- Rosie O’Donnell mocked Donald Trump during her guest-hosting stint on *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* over his Truth Social post claiming the Strait of Hormuz as new US territory.
- The comedian showed a map shared by Trump circling the strait and labelled it nonsensical, noting that US forces remain unable to guarantee safe passage through the waterway.
- Iran’s deputy foreign minister rejected Trump's assertion as a delusion, stating the US could not seize the area by tweet or military force.
- O’Donnell sarcastically thanked Trump for renewing interest in her career and discussed her decision to move to Ireland following his second inauguration.
- The White House responded by linking to a previous post by Trump that questioned O’Donnell's gender and claimed she was obsessed with him.
