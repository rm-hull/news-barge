A man has been arrested after a van was set alight in an "arson attack” in Seaham.

Police and firefighters were called to Back North Terrace, behind the Aldi supermarket, on Friday (August 14) after a blaze broke out just before 9pm.

Shocking pictures from the scene showed the van engulfed in flames, with thick black smoke pouring from the vehicle.

County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service group manager Jon Singleton said two engines were sent to the fire, which spread to nearby properties and fencing.

On Wednesday (August 19), Durham Police confirmed a 60-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of arson.

He has since been released under investigation while enquiries are ongoing.
