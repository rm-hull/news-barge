A global engineering and environmental consultancy has been chosen to help shape how communities, housing, infrastructure and economic growth are delivered across the Tees Valley for decades to come.

Stantec will lead the development of a new spatial development strategy (SDS) for the region after being appointed by the Tees Valley Combined Authority (TVCA).

The SDS is one of the first to be commissioned in England and will set out a strategic, long-term framework for growth, infrastructure, and economic opportunity across Teesside, Darlington, and Hartlepool.

Ben Houchen, Tees Valley Mayor, said: "For too long, planning has stopped at council boundaries when our economy, our transport network and the businesses investing here simply don't.

"This gives us the opportunity to look at Teesside, Darlington and Hartlepool as a whole and plan properly for where new homes, jobs, infrastructure and investment need to go over the next 20 years.

"By working together with our five councils on one long-term plan, we can be much more proactive about unlocking land for development and making sure the roads, transport and infrastructure are there to support it.

"Stantec brings a huge amount of experience to this work and, importantly, already knows our region well.

"I'm looking forward to working with them to get this right and unlock even more investment across Teesside, Darlington and Hartlepool."

Stantec will lead a multidisciplinary team including communications consultancy Camargue, urban planning and design firm Prior + Partners, commercial property consultants Montagu Evans, and strategic planning experts Richard Wood Associates.

The team will bring together national expertise and local knowledge to deliver a strategy that addresses the region’s housing, economic, and environmental ambitions.

Nancy MacDonald, regional business leader for infrastructure at Stantec UK and Ireland, said: "The Tees Valley presents a distinctive set of opportunities and challenges, and this strategy will support the region’s economic transition.

"We’ll focus on accelerating key growth areas such as advanced manufacturing, energy, and emerging digital sectors.

"Our team understands the importance of this opportunity to shape the future of the region but also the need to bring local people with us on this journey.

"We want to make sure this plan takes advantage of new industries, connects its communities so they can really thrive, and is able to pair housing provision with aspirational economic development goals.

"We are all incredibly excited to demonstrate our people-focused approach to strategic planning and positive community development with this SDS."

Spatial development strategies provide a high-level, at least 20-year, vision for regions.

They guide local plans to ensure coordinated delivery of housing, infrastructure, economic growth, and environmental improvement.

Stantec’s appointment builds on decades of experience in the Tees Valley across transport, energy, water, and community development projects.

The firm’s Darlington office will support greater collaboration with regional parties throughout the project.

The SDS will identify priority areas for growth, strategic infrastructure needs, and opportunities for economic development across the Tees Valley.

It aims to help the region respond to current challenges while positioning itself for long-term success in areas such as advanced manufacturing, energy, and emerging digital sectors.
