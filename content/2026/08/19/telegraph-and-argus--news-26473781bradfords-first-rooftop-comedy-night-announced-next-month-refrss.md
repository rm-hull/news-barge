BRADFORD'S first rooftop comedy club is launching next month, promising a night of laughter.

The Glasshouse Comedy Club will take place at Cedar Court Hotel Bradford in its conservatory, with the opening night scheduled for September 18.

Steve N Allen, BAFTA-nominated comedian and star of The Mash Report, will headline the debut show.

Dom Dorgan, deputy hotel manager at Cedar Court Bradford, said: "We’re really excited to bring Glasshouse Comedy Club to Cedar Court Bradford for the first time.

"The Conservatory is such a unique space within the hotel, and with Steve N Allen headlining, it gives us a brilliant opportunity to create something a little different for Bradford."

The line-up also includes Edinburgh Festival favourite Kelly Rickard, Alex Mettrick, and Manchester comic Jack Wood.

The evening will be hosted by Bilal Rashid.

Chloe Hopkinson, events manager at Cedar Court Bradford, said: "I’m so excited for our first Glasshouse Comedy Club."
