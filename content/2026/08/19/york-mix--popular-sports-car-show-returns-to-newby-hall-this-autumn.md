Car enthusiasts are set to descend on Newby Hall & Gardens this September – as the Autumn Sports Cars in the Park show returns to the historic estate.

Taking place on **Sunday 6 September**, the popular event will bring together sports cars from clubs and individual owners across the UK.

Visitors can expect a wide range of prestigious marques including Aston Martin, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Porsche and Lotus, alongside trade stands and market stalls.

If you’d to exhibit your vehicle, you can register it here.

Tickets to the show include entry to Newby’s award-winning gardens featuring one of Europe’s largest double herbaceous borders and the newly restored Edwardian rock garden, as well as the popular adventure playground, dollshouse and teddy bear exhibitions.

Visitors can also access the house, miniature railway and river boat rides.

Stuart Gill, commercial director at Newby Hall, said: “We look forward to the return of the show which is always popular with visitors who can enjoy a wide range of prestigious car marques in the unique setting of Newby’s magnificent parkland.

“The added attractions of our award-winning gardens and children’s activities make the event a great family day out for everyone.”

Gates will open from 10am to 3pm. Tickets are Family £65 (2 adults + 2 children), Adults £22, Children £16 (4–15), and under‑4s free.

For more information and to buy tickets for the show, please visit the Newby Hall website.
