A hilltop home with panoramic views across North York Moors has hit the market for £720,000.

Far Shirby cottage, in the conservation village of Upleatham, is setting itself apart with a sprawling landscaped garden featuring a Japanese-inspired retreat, ornamental waterfall and an orchard.

The detached bungalow overlooks the Cleveland Hills towards the North York Moors National Park, but it is the extensive outdoor space surrounding the property that proves to be one of its most striking features.

  The property overlooks the Cleveland Hills towards the North York Moors. 

*(Image: Leapfrog Lettings and Sales)*

Accessed through a gated driveway, the property is nestled within extensive mature gardens that wrap around the home.

  The property is accessed through a gated driveway. 

*(Image: Leapfrog Lettings and Sales)*

Mature trees, colourful planting and a network of pathways wind through the grounds, which are said to attract wildlife including deer, hares, pheasants and a variety of bird species.

A timber summerhouse overlooks a stocked pond - while a greenhouse, potting shed and several garden buildings offer more space for keen gardeners.

The grounds are also equipped with an automated irrigation system designed to help keep the gardens in top condition.

  The most striking feature on the property is the Japanese-themed ornamental garden - complete with a pond, orchard and statues. 

*(Image: Leapfrog Lettings and Sales)*

The house itself offers more than 2,000 sq. ft of space - including four bedrooms, a bespoke Poggenpohl German kitchen, multiple reception rooms, a conservatory and dedicated home office spaces.

Large windows and glazed doors throughout the home maximise views across the surrounding countryside and lets plenty of natural light into the home.

  Views of the Cleveland Hills can be soaked up in the conservatory that sits at the back of the property. 

*(Image: Leapfrog Lettings and Sales)*

  The house is fully fitted with a bespoke Poggenpohl German kitchen. 

*(Image: Leapfrog Lettings and Sales)*

The principal bedroom suite features fitted furniture, a dressing area and an en-suite shower room, while three further double bedrooms are served by a spacious family bathroom complete with both a bath and walk-in shower.

The property also comes with dedicated gym and office areas, as well as a sun lounge and pantry adding plenty of extra space and storage options.

  The property has two bathrooms, including one en-suite attached to the master bedroom, and a family-sized bathroom. 

*(Image: Leapfrog Lettings and Sales)*

  The property also comes with a dedicated gym area. 

*(Image: Leapfrog Lettings and Sales)*

  The most striking feature on the property is the Japanese-themed ornamental garden - complete with a pond, orchard and statues. 

*(Image: Leapfrog Lettings and Sales)*

Located around three miles from the North Yorkshire coast between Guisborough and Saltburn, the property combines a rural setting with convenient access to Teesside and the wider region.
