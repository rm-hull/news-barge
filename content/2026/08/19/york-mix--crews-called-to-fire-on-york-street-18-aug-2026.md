Firefighters were called to a fire in York yesterday evening (Tuesday).

They went to the incident, in an undisclosed location in the city, at **5.16pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb crew attended a domestic street to investigate a report of a fire in the open.

“The crew extinguished a small fire in the open using backpack sprayers. Advice given to person on site.”
