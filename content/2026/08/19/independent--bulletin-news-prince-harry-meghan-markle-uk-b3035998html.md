# Harry and Meghan to relocate their family back to the UK

- The Duke and Duchess of Sussex are reportedly planning to relocate their family back to the UK from the US later this month.
- Harry and Meghan intend to live in a private, non-royal residence close to London, according to *The Daily Telegraph* .
- The couple's children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet, are set to start at a British school in September.
- King Charles was informed of the move on Sunday and is said to welcome the opportunity to see more of his family.
- There will be no change in the couple's status as private individuals following their return to the UK.
