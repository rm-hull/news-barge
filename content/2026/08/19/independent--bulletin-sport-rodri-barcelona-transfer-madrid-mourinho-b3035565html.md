# Rodri’s ‘doubts’ led to Real Madrid move collapsing, says Jose Mourinho

- Jose Mourinho has revealed that Rodri expressed doubts about joining Real Madrid, which ultimately led the midfielder to sign for Barcelona instead.
- Barcelona completed the deal to sign the World Cup-winning captain from Manchester City on Tuesday, with Rodri agreeing a contract until 2030.
- Mourinho stated that Real Madrid made an important offer, but Rodri's hesitation gave him doubts, leading the manager to cool his interest.
- The 30-year-old Spain midfielder sought a return to his homeland following a seven-year spell in the Premier League.
- Rodri is among the leading contenders for the Ballon d'Or after playing a pivotal role in Spain's World Cup triumph.
