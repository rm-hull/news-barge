# Who is Brian Hickerson? Hayden Panettiere’s on-off boyfriend was with star when she died

- Heroes and Nashville star Hayden Panettiere has died at the age of 36 at an apartment in South Carolina.
- An autopsy revealed no signs of trauma, with toxicology reports pending to determine the exact cause of death.
- Her boyfriend Brian Hickerson called 911 and handed over a bag of Panettiere's medication to police officers at the scene.
- The actor's father Skip Panettiere confirmed her death, while her ex-fiancé Wladimir Klitschko expressed deep sadness.
- Former co-stars including Masi Oka, Connie Britton, Viola Davis, and Tim Allen have paid tribute to the two-time Golden Globe nominee.
