### What will the weather be like in the North East today?

Today is set to turn fresher with heavy thundery showers.

#### 
  **Today:**

It will be a mostly cloudy day with scattered showers. Showers may turn heavy at times with a risk of hail and thunder. Light winds will freshen a little during the showers. Temperatures slightly below the seasonal average. Maximum temperature 20 °C.

#### 
  **Tonight:**

It will remain rather cloudy through the evening and overnight with further showers, some perhaps turning heavy and thundery, giving some surface water issues. A cool night to come. Minimum temperature 9 °C.
