# Man arrested for robbing NASCAR’s Greg Biffle’s home after deadly plane crash

- Terrell Williams, 44, was charged with a dozen felonies in North Carolina for burglarizing the home of late NASCAR driver Greg Biffle.
- The January burglary occurred weeks after Biffle, his wife, and their two children were killed in a fatal plane crash.
- Authorities allege Williams targeted the residence using news headlines and social media posts to determine when homes were unoccupied.
- Investigators recovered much of the stolen property, which included $30,000 in cash, handguns, coins, and NASCAR memorabilia.
- Williams was arrested in Georgia during a separate burglary investigation and faces extradition to North Carolina once his local case is resolved.
