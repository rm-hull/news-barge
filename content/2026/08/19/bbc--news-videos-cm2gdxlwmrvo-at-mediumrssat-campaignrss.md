# Watch: What to know about Trump's trusted aide Natalie Harp

President Donald Trump's right-hand woman, Natalie Harp, is in the spotlight after Georgia Senator Jon Ossoff - a Democrat and frequent Trump foil - name-checked her in a speech that has since gone viral.

Harp has become one of Trump's longest-serving and most loyal aides being trusted with typing out his Truth Social posts, among other top responsibilities.

The BBC's Bernd Debusmann explains who she is and how she came to hold such a prominent role in the president's inner circle.
