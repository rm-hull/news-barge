PUB-GOERS are in with a chance to bag £1,000 just for enjoying the Sunday match at their local.

The Sunday Social £10k Giveaway, sponsored by Sky, is running at selected Admiral Taverns pubs each Sunday from August 16 to September 20, including the Crown Hotel and Malt Kiln Inn in Bradford.

Fans simply need to be among the first 100 eligible adults through the door from 3pm to receive a raffle ticket.

If their pub is selected during a live reveal, a £1,000 cash prize will be drawn within 15 minutes of the final whistle.

Danny and Dawn O'Rourke were the most recent winners, taking home £1,000 at the Rhoden Inn in Oswaldtwistle.

On Bank Holiday Sunday, August 30, five pubs will be chosen to give away a total of £5,000.

Even if a pub isn’t selected for the main draw, one customer will still win a free drink through a separate raffle.
