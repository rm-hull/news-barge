One person was taken to hospital following a collision in Bradford.

The two-vehicle crash happened on Sunbridge Road just after 9pm last night (August 18).

Emergency services were called to the scene.

One person was taken to hospital while others were treated for minor injuries.

Yorkshire Ambulance Services said: "We received an emergency call at 9.11pm last night to report a road traffic collision on Sunbridge Road in Bradford.

"An ambulance and a team leader were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."

West Yorkshire Police said: "Police were called by the ambulance service to reports of two cars involved in a road traffic collision on Sunbridge Road, Bradford at around 9.20pm yesterday evening.

"Those involved have been treated for minor injuries."

Images shared on social media show a police van at the scene, with significant damage visible to both vehicles.
