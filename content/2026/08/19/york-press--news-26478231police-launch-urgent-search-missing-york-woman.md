Police have launched a search for a missing woman from York.

Jessica Ashworth, 27, was last seen in the Acomb area on Saturday (July 25).

She is described as white, 5ft 4 inches, and of a proportionate build with dark, straight hair.

  


Jessica, who is from the York area, has a tattoo of a love heart under her right eye and has other tattoos on her arm.

She speaks with a Yorkshire accent and has links to the Acomb and Huntington area of York, North Yorkshire Police said.

A spokesperson said: “If you see Jessica, please call 999 immediately.

“If you have any other information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online:

• Online: Visit our website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’.

• Live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“Please quote 12260151480 when sharing information.”
