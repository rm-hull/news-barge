A Teesside beer festival is returning this month with organisers promising a few extra surprises as they celebrate a major milestone.

Eaglescliffe Beer Festival returns to Eaglescliffe Community Centre for the next Bank Holiday - from Friday, August 28, to Sunday, August 30.

Organised by Stockton-based Three Brothers Brewing Company, the event will feature more than 45 beers and lagers, alongside at least 12 ciders, artisan spirits, wine and prosecco.

The festival will also mark the 10th anniversary of Three Brothers Brewing, with the company telling visitors to "watch this space for a few little extras" to celebrate the occasion.

  Three Brothers Brewing Company will bring Eaglescliffe Beer Festival back for 2026. 

*(Image: Three Brothers Brewery)*

Visitors can soak up live music on all three evenings while sampling beers from across the North East.

Attendees can also enjoy a selection of street food vendors throughout the weekend.

For those who aren't beer or ale drinkers, there are plenty of other options, including wine and prosecco, as well as a selection of artisan spirits.

Three Brothers have also confirmed that low-alcohol drinks will be available, as well as vegan and gluten-free options.

The festival will be supporting the Great North Air Ambulance Service for 2026, with 25 per cent of ticket revenue being donated to the charity.

The brewery also recently announced the first Hutton Rudby Beer Festival in October, and said both of the festivals this year will contribute to fundraising efforts for the charity.

The festival will run from noon until 11pm on both Friday and Saturday.

Events will conclude at 10pm on the Sunday, with the final day of the festival also set to feature a classic pub quiz at 3pm.

Three Brothers said further details for anniversary celebrations will be announced on their Facebook page ahead of the Bank Holiday festival.

Eventgoers should keep an eye out for vendor announcements.

The brewery is inviting local and national businesses to get involved with the festival through sponsorship opportunities across both of its events.

Tickets are £4 on the door.
