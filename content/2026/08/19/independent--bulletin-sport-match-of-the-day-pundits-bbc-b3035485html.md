# Who are Match of the Day’s new pundits as the Premier League returns?

![Kelly Cates, Mark Chapman and Gabby Logan will continue as presenters](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/01/15/13/Untitled-1.jpg)

- The BBC has announced four new pundits for *Match of the Day**,* including Thomas Frank, Ashley Young, Toby Alderweireld and Robbie Keane.
- The new additions join established experts such as Wayne Rooney and Alan Shearer, with Gabby Logan, Mark Chapman and Kelly Cates continuing as presenters.
- Saturday lunchtime programme *Football Focus* has been axed after 52 years on the air.
- BBC One will replace Football Focus with a new show called The Football Interview, presented by Kelly Somers.
- BBC Director of Sport Alex Kay-Jelski stated the updated line-up aims to put supporters firmly at the heart of the coverage.
