# Isack Hadjar: Who will replace Red Bull driver at the Dutch Grand Prix?

![Red Bull driver Isack Hadjar](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/19/09/01KYC73B677J33V21AKKG5QB93.jpg)

- French driver Isack Hadjar is set to miss the Dutch Grand Prix this weekend after suffering a wrist injury.
- Liam Lawson is expected to step up from Racing Bulls to replace Hadjar as Max Verstappen's teammate for the race.
- If Lawson moves up, reserve driver Yuki Tsunoda is in line to return to the Racing Bulls garage alongside Arvid Lindblad.
- The injury is a significant setback for 21-year-old Hadjar, who secured his sole F1 podium at Zandvoort last year.
- It remains uncertain whether Hadjar will recover in time to compete at the Italian Grand Prix in Monza in two weeks.
