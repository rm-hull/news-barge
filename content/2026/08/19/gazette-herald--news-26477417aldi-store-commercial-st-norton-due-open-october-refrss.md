A new supermarket is to open in a North Yorkshire town this autumn.

The Aldi store in Commercial Street, Norton, is due to open in October.

Highway works at the Aldi entrance are now complete and the temporary traffic lights removed.

Future activity includes topsoil importation, landscaping works, car park construction and store fit-out works.

Car park surfacing works are currently programmed through late August and September.

Store fit-out works are currently expected to be completed by early October.

A spokesperson for Aldi said: “Work is progressing well on our new store in Norton. We are currently fitting out the new store and developing the car park areas. Our aim is to open the store to customers in early October. All works on site have been completed in line with the planning conditions set by North Yorkshire Council.”

The Norton store is one of 15 the supermarket chain plans to open 16 new stores across the UK in the coming months.

The Germany-based retailer has more than 1,080 UK stores and is pushing ahead with continued expansion plans to grow its shop estate across the country.

The plans are part of long-term ambitions by Aldi to expand to 1,500 sites across the UK.

Jonathan Neale, managing director of national real estate at Aldi UK, said: “At Aldi, we’re committed to making high-quality, affordable food accessible to everyone, which is why we continue to invest in expanding our store network across the UK.

“Our £370 million investment in new stores this year will help us bring Aldi’s unbeatable value to even more communities, supporting local economies through our industry-leading pay for colleagues.
