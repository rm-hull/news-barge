Handcrafted treats and creative stalls will fill Malton’s Market Place this weekend as the town’s monthly artisan market returns.

Taking place on Saturday, August 22, the event is curated by Little Bird Made and promises a summer showcase of independent makers, food producers, and artists in the heart of Malton’s Georgian streets.

Shoppers can browse stalls featuring handmade crafts, jewellery, art, homewares, and seasonal goods, all part of the market’s signature 'Made In Malton' ethos.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "August is such a brilliant time to visit Malton, and we are delighted to be bringing our artisan market back to the heart of the town.

"Our markets are all about creating a warm, social atmosphere where people can catch up with friends, soak up the summer vibes, and discover incredible small businesses.

"From gorgeous handcrafted items to outstanding ‘Made In Malton’ food and drink, there really is something special for everyone to enjoy."

The market also offers a social community experience, with the surrounding town square providing outdoor seating and access to independent cafes.

For more information about future markets or to apply for a stall, email [info@littlebirdmade.co.uk](mailto:info@littlebirdmade.co.uk) or visit www.littlebirdmade.com.
