One person was rushed to hospital after a family was rescued when falling into difficulty at a popular North Craven beauty spot.

The Cave Rescue Organisation (CRO) was called out at 10.09pm on Tuesday to the Ingleton Waterfalls Trail to assist the Yorkshire Ambulance Service.

The family had encountered "medical issues, darkness and poor weather."

  The Cave Rescue Organisation rescued a family at Ingleton Waterfalls Trail 

*(Image: Cave Rescue Organisation)*

A spokesperson for the Cave Rescue Organisation said: "Yorkshire Ambulance Service (YAS) requested our help for a family group on the Ingleton Waterfalls Trail who were struggling with medical issues, darkness and poor weather.

"A small team quickly located them, guided other members in, and after assessments, helped the group back down to team vehicles for transfer to YAS ambulances at the roadhead.

"We’re wishing them all a safe and steady recovery."

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 10.01pm on Monday, August 17, to report a medical incident on the Ingleton Waterfalls Trail.

"Two ambulances and Mountain Rescue colleagues were dispatched to the scene, and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
