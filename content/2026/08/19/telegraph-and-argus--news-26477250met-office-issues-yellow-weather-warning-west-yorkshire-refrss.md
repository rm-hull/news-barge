Many areas in Yorkshire, including Bradford, have been issued with a yellow weather warning for thunderstorms.

The Met Office has released a yellow weather warning for many parts of the UK and it covers areas north of Wales, Manchester, Carlisle, and Newcastle.

Bradford and its surrounding areas, such as Ilkley, Otley, and Bingley, are also covered by the warning.

The Met Office has issued the warning from 7pm today (Wednesday, August 19) and it will be in place until 10am tomorrow (Thursday, August 20).


## Yellow weather warning for thunderstorms

The Met Office has said the heavy showers and thunderstorms are likely to lead to some travel disruption and flash flooding.

The Met Office said: "Heavy showers will develop this evening, becoming more frequent and widespread overnight.

"These are likely to bring 15 to 20 mm in an hour in some areas with as much as 30 to 50 mm falling in a period of six hours in some places.

"These accumulations may lead to a few places seeing some flooding.

"Alongside the rain, some showers are likely to be thundery, bringing the additional hazards of lightning and hail.

"The showers will ease from the northwest Thursday morning."

People should be prepared for driving conditions to be affected by spray, standing water, or hail, which can lead to longer journey times for buses and cars.

Some buildings and homes might flood, which will lead to some damage.


Delays to train services are possible and some areas might suffer a short-term loss of power.

Some buildings might be damaged by lightning strikes.

## How to prepare for thunderstorms

The Met Office has shared some advice on how to prepare for the thunderstorms.

If you are travelling during the warning, you should check road conditions and timetables for buses and trains.

If your house is at risk of flash flooding, you should prepare a flood plan and an emergency flood kit.

It is also a good idea to have torches and batteries, a mobile phone power pack, and other essential items ready in case of a power cut.
