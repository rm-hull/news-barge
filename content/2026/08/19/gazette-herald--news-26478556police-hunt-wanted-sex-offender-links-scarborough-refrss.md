*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

  Police are searching for a man with links to North Yorkshire who has breached his

  Registered Sex Offender notification requirements.

Extensive enquiries have been carried out to trace Zain Osman.

North Yorkshire Police believe he may be in the Leeds area, although he also has links to Scarborough and Newcastle.

  


  A force spokesperson said: “If you have seen Osman, or have any information about his whereabouts, please contact North Yorkshire Police on 101.

  

  “Alternatively, you can pass on information anonymously through independent charity Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or via their website.

  

  “If you have an immediate sighting of Osman, please call 999.

  

  “Please quote reference number 12260160145 when providing any information.”
