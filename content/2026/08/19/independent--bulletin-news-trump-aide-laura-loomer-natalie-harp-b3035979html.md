# MAGA activist claims White House staff are behind ‘nasty’ stories about Trump aide

- Conservative activist Laura Loomer accused White House staffers of leaking negative stories about President Donald Trump's executive assistant, Natalie Harp, due to professional jealousy.
- Loomer claimed internal leakers prioritizing their own ambitions over loyalty are behind recent media scrutiny regarding Harp's performance and close access to Trump.
- Harp, a 35-year-old former television presenter, manages Trump's Truth Social account and serves as a key administrative conduit for information to the president.
- The White House defended Harp, describing her as a beloved official and rejecting claims of dysfunction within the administration.
- Controversy surrounding Harp escalated after Democratic Senator Jon Ossoff faced backlash from conservative figures for mocking her inclusion on a presidential flight.
