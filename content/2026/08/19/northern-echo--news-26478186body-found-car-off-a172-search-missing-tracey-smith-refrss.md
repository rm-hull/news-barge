A missing woman has been found dead in her car down a ditch off a North Yorkshire A-road.

Tracey Smith, 57, left her home in the Stokesley area in a black Fiat Panda on Friday, July 24.

A major search was launched for Ms Smith and her car with pictures of the 10-plate Panda released by police.

On Tuesday (August 18) a black car was spotted in the in undergrowth down an embankment on the A172, at its junction with the A19 near the Cleveland Tontine near Stokesley.

A woman was found dead inside, and while formal identification is yet to take place, officers believe her to be Ms Smith.

The road was closed and is expected to remain closed for some time.
