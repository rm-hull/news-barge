An accountancy firm has been named one of York’s top three accountants for the tenth year running.

Mollan & Co, based in Stamford Bridge, received the Three Best Rated crystal award after a decade of consistent recognition.

The firm earned a near-perfect total inspection score of 116 out of 120 and holds an overall rating of 4.8 out of five.

Rob Mollan, managing director and owner of Mollan & Co, said: "We’re really proud to have been recognised by ThreeBestRated for the tenth year in a row.

"Winning an award once is great, but to receive it consistently for ten years is something the whole team can be incredibly proud of."

The award comes as Mollan & Co marks more than 40 years in business, having served clients across York, Yorkshire and the UK since 1985.

Mr Mollan added: "For me, it really comes down to the people we have here.

"We’ve got a fantastic team who care about our clients, know their businesses and always try to go that extra mile.

"The strength of the firm has always been the people in it, and this recognition is very much down to them.

"I’m hugely proud of the team we’ve built and what we’ve achieved together over the last ten years."

Mollan & Co supports individuals, contractors, landlords and small to medium-sized businesses.

Services include accounts preparation, bookkeeping, payroll, VAT, tax planning and business advice.

The firm has continued to develop its digital and cloud accounting services while maintaining personalised, one-to-one support.

Three Best Rated independently assesses businesses using a 50-point inspection.

Criteria include reviews, reputation, history, satisfaction, trust, pricing and overall trading standards.

Only the top three providers in each area receive the award.

Mollan & Co has been serving clients for more than four decades and is based in Stamford Bridge, near York.
