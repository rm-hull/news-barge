# Paul Scholes handed six-month driving ban after speeding offences

![Paul Scholes won 11 Premier League titles with Manchester United](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/04/14/17/efe5cce96170cd1dc65a7b67f87926fbY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzc2MjY3MzQ0-2.76954603.jpg)

- Former Manchester United midfielder Paul Scholes has received a six-month driving ban following two motoring convictions.
- The 51-year-old was caught driving a Range Rover at 37mph in a 30mph zone in Cleckheaton, West Yorkshire, in March.
- A separate incident in November involved a Mercedes registered to Scholes driving at 49mph in a 30mph zone, resulting in a prosecution for failing to respond to police letters.
- Scholes was convicted in his absence during private Single Justice Procedure hearings after failing to enter pleas to either charge.
- Along with the ban, he was handed six penalty points and ordered to pay fines, costs, and victim surcharges totalling £1,472.
