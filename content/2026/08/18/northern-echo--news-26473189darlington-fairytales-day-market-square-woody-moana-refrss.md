Darlington's Market Square is preparing for a Fairytales and Legends Day this week, with a variety of free activities for all ages.

The annual event, which will take place on Thursday, August 20, will run from 11am to 3pm, and promises a range of attractions.

Attendees are encouraged to dress as their favourite fairytale characters.

### 
  **What's on?**

A series of meet-and-greet sessions with well-loved characters such as Tinkerbell and Peter Pan, Moana and Snow White, and Jesse and Woody, are scheduled throughout the day.

These sessions will take place at various locations around the town, including Skinnergate, Queen Street Shopping Centre, Max Spielmann on Northgate, and Cornmill Shopping Centre.

The Market Square will host a meet-and-greet session before a show at 2.15pm.

The day will also include a free trail around the town centre, offering collectables for participants.

  The trail map for Thursday's event 

*(Image: Darlington Borough Council)*

Theatre enthusiasts can look forward to a live production in the Market Square, providing an additional layer of entertainment.

The event has already sparked excitement on social media, with one user writing: "The kids would love this."

Public facilities, including the Dolphin Centre, Market Hall, Cornmill Shopping Centre, and Darlington Library, will be open to attendees.

### 
  **How to get there**

Darlington town centre offers a variety of transport options for those planning to attend.

Cyclists can use the parking stands around the Market Square, while taxi services are available to drop visitors off directly at the square.

Regular train services to Darlington station and bus routes into the town centre provide additional travel options.

The Fairytales and Legends Day promises to be a captivating event, offering something for everyone and further details can be found on the Enjoy Darlington website.
