# Pete Hegseth’s Iowa trip raises questions about 2028 bid

- Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth joined other Trump administration figures in Iowa to campaign for vulnerable Republican candidates during the state fair.
- Hegseth's high-profile appearance sparked speculation that he may be testing a potential bid for the 2028 Republican presidential nomination.
- The Pentagon stated that Hegseth traveled on military aircraft as legally required, even though he was campaigning in a personal capacity.
- Top Republicans are flooding Iowa to protect key congressional seats after Senator Joni Ernst announced her retirement amid low approval ratings for Donald Trump.
- While promoting U.S. involvement in the Iran war during his trip, Hegseth faced criticism regarding poor living conditions reported on the USS Abraham Lincoln.
