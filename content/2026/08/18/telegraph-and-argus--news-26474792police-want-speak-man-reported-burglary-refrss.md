POLICE have released an image of a man they want to speak to as part of an investigation into an attempted burglary in West Yorkshire.

Officers were called to Cross Green Road, Dalton in Huddersfield, at around midnight on Saturday, July 25, following reports of an attempted burglary at a residential address.

West Yorkshire Police has now issued a photograph of a man, that they believe may be able to assist with their enquiries.

Anyone who recognises the man pictured, or who has information about the incident, is urged to contact police.

## Police statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Anyone who recognises the man pictured or has any information is asked to contact police on 101 or online at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat) referencing police log 13260423056.

"Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111."
