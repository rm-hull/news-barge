# UK vows to carry on supplying Ukraine with drones amid threat from Russia

- Ukrainian forces have reportedly deployed British-manufactured drones to conduct deep strikes against targets inside mainland Russia.
- Russia's embassy in London accused the UK of escalating the conflict and warned that Britain will face inevitable consequences for its involvement.
- Work and pensions minister Lilian Greenwood affirmed that the UK remains steadfast in supplying Ukraine with the military equipment it needs.
- Kyiv has significantly escalated long-range drone strikes targeting Russian military industries, energy facilities, and commercial infrastructure.
- British defence figures dismissed Moscow's threats as propaganda and expressed confidence in NATO's collective strength against aggression.
