There’s standstill traffic on the A64 north of York this morning (Tuesday).

Vehicles are queuing both ways at **Flaxton Moor**.

Roadworks by Yorkshire Water appear to be the problem.

#### Location of roadworks

Road users report a lane being closed causing standstill traffic.

The queues are stretching from Sandburn Hall to Barton le Willows.
