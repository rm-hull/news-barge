# Quirkiest Yorkshire staycations with availability this August

## Summer is passing fast, but for anyone feeling spontaneous, it’s not too late to book a Yorkshire staycation.

There are two weekends to go, and plenty of weekday getaways if you’re flexible.

From converted shipping containers to a tipi with hammocks, these are the quirkiest Yorkshire staycations – they still have availability this August.

![The Ginger Cow Company](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjc2ZWY0NDczLWQxNWItNDBjOS04MzUwLWE0YTdkNzZlNGM3ZjpjODhlMGY2Yy0yMDVlLTQxNDQtOGRmMC1lMGY3ZjVhODAxYWM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



This shepherd’s hut has a hot tub, private BBQ, and most importantly, three Highland cows and three goats waiting to be petted onsite. Availability is slim, but a gypsy caravan is also available at the same location.

**A rural shepherd’s hut with private field in Wass**

![Credit: Cherry Tub - Nicky Sutcliffe (shepherd's hut)](<[https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/18/11/46/Credit_-Cherry-Tub-Nicky-Sutcliffe-(shepherds-hut](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/18/11/46/Credit_-Cherry-Tub-Nicky-Sutcliffe-(shepherds-hut)).jpeg?trim=167,0,169,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65>)



Located between North York Moors National Park and the Howardian Hills, this shepherd’s hut is a rural option for an August getaway. Guests have exclusive use of a private field, plus access to 160 acres of private fields and woodland.

**A cozy cottage on Addingham Moorside**

![Louise Huddlestone](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjQyYTcxZTZkLTA2ZjAtNDk4MS1iMTU0LTU0MThiMDJiMGY1ZTplZGRkMDdmZC05ODQ1LTRhMzEtYmYyNS02MzFiY2NmOGFlMmE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



![Louise Huddlestone](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmE2N2RhOWE1LTQ1ZGQtNDk5NC05ZmVhLThjOGRmNzgwNTFmMzpiNDcxN2QzMS0zNDM4LTRkYWUtYjU3ZC1iMDA0MzM3NDAxMDY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Surrounded by thick heather moorland, this rustic cottage feels like an old-world film set – there’s even a ladder to climb upstairs. Guests can combine a stay with visits to Ilkley and Addingham, with river swimming and moor walks.

**A tipi in the Howardian Hills**

Only a short drive from Castle Howard, Roma Rose is a peaceful tipi staycation. For a countryside reset, there is currently availability throughout the next two weeks.

**A cozy shepherd’s hut outside Beverley**

Just outside of Beverley, this cozy shepherd’s hut still has availability this summer. Rattan furniture and a DVD player are a recipe for nostalgia on an overcast summer’s day.

**A shepherd’s hut with a fire pit in Langdale End**

This shepherd’s hut is another option in North York Moors National Park. Guests use wheelbarrows to transport their luggage, crossing a bridged stream to reach the hut; it seems like a whimsical last-minute stay.

**A converted shipping container in Stillington**

![Apple Crate - Nicky Sutcliffe](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjJmYTI5ZTUwLWZkOTAtNGNlYy1hNDBkLTU5YmUyOWRmOTlkZTozNTFjY2NiMC1hZDVlLTRhMzUtOTVkYS1kYWI2ZjY2OGE0NWU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



With bifold doors and rustic interiors, this former shipping container is a quirky place for a last-minute staycation. Previous guests have reported seeing barn owls, deer, and foxes. If availability becomes limited, the site also has a handmade shepherd’s hut.

**A duplex apartment with York Minster views**

If walking the Shambles sounds more appealing than camping near cows, this duplex apartment sits inside a former chapel in central York. Renovations have spotlighted original features; guests duck beneath a diagonal wooden beam between the bathroom and bedroom.
