# Jeanie Buss rips siblings’ attempt to sell off family Los Angeles Lakers stake

- Five Buss family siblings voted to sell their remaining 17.8 percent stake in the Los Angeles Lakers to prospective owners Bob Iger and Josh Kushner.
- Jeanie Buss's attorney demanded a retraction of the sale announcement, citing family trust regulations and a 2017 court ruling guaranteeing her lifetime control of the team.
- If the sale proceeds, Jeanie Buss would lose her position as team governor, which requires maintaining a 15% ownership threshold in the franchise.
- Iger and Kushner originally agreed to purchase a 65% controlling stake from Mark Walter for a record $12.5 billion, with the Buss shares raising their total stake to roughly 83%.
- The ongoing transaction and potential ownership change are subject to approval by the NBA Board of Governors in September.
