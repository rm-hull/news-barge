A police cordon is in place after two women were injured in a road traffic collision today.

The incident occurred on High Street in Queensbury at around 1.42pm this afternoon (August 18).

Images show a police cordon stretched across the road close to the junction with Mount Pleasant Street, between the High Street Cafe and the Ring O Bells pub.


Multiple police cars were present with officers diverting traffic away from the scene.

West Yorkshire Police were called to the scene by the ambulance service.

A spokesman for the force said: "Police were called by the ambulance service to reports of a road traffic collision on High Street, Queensbury, Bradford at around 1:42pm today (August 18).

"Two women aged 87 and 59 have been taken to hospital to be treated for their injuries."

A scene remains in place as enquiries remain ongoing.

When the T&A visited the scene, a white Nissan car could be seen within the cordon.

The wheels of the vehicle appeared to have been marked with pink chalk as part of the evidence-gathering process.
