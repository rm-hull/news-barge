Aldi has unveiled a fresh batch of Specialbuys for August, with bargain hunters able to pick up everything from a £40 garden gadget to student bedroom essentials from just £1.49.

The latest middle-aisle launches are arriving throughout the month, with the Garden Tidy range already in stores and the supermarket's new fitness and student collections following close behind.

As of August 17, shoppers can get their hands on the Garden Tidy range, while Aldi's Fitness Event has also launched in stores.

And from August 20, the supermarket will begin rolling out its huge Student Home, Office and Bedroom range, with a second wave landing on August 23.

As ever with Aldi Specialbuys, shoppers will need to be quick, with products available while stocks last.


## Taste of Asia range

Aldi's popular Taste of Asia range is already back on shelves, with prices starting at just 59p.

Among the new additions are Vietnamese Pho and Japanese Style Ramen Kits (£2.25), while fans can also pick up the returning Korean Style Kimchi (£1.99) and Chilli Crisp (£2.69).

Snack fans can get their hands on the returning Asian Hand Cooked Crisps (£1.15) in Crispy Duck and Teriyaki Beef flavours.

## Aldi's £39.99 garden gadget is back

Aldi's Garden Tidy range landed on August 13, giving shoppers the chance to prepare their gardens for the months ahead without spending a fortune.

The standout buy is the Manual Sweeper for £39.99, designed to make light work of fallen leaves, twigs and garden debris.

Aldi says the sweeper offers a saving of £60.01 compared with a similar Dunelm product, which is priced at £100.

  Aldi has a new range of August specialbuys 

*(Image: Aldi)*

Also worth a look is the Winter Greenhouse (£14.99), which can help protect plants from colder weather, while the Extendable Rake and Broom (£5.99) is designed to make clearing patios and awkward corners easier.

Other garden buys start from just £3.79.

## Student essentials land this week

  Aldi has a new range of August specialbuys, including student favoutites 

*(Image: Aldi)*

With university looming for thousands of students, Aldi's new Student Home, Office and Bedroom range could prove tempting for those trying to kit out a room on a tight budget.

The first products arrive in stores on Thursday, August 20, followed by another batch on Sunday, August 23.

One of the biggest bargains is the £69.99 Vacuum Cleaner Robot, which Aldi says offers a saving of 65% compared with a comparable Eufy model costing £199.

There's even a Hot Air Multistyler for £24.99, arriving on August 23, for anyone wanting to recreate a salon-style blow-dry on a student budget.
