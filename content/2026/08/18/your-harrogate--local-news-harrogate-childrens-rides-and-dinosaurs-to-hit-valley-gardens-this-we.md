Dinosaurs, children's rides and giant games are set to take over Valley Gardens in Harrogate this weekend.

The Valley Gardens Summer of Fun will run for three days from Friday 21st to Sunday 23rd August.

Organised by 2B Events, the event will be open from 10.30am until 5pm each day, with free entry.

Families will be able to meet dinosaurs and take on a selection of giant lawn games free of charge.

There will also be a giant dinosaur inflatable, children's rides, bungee trampolines and game stalls, although these activities will incur a charge.

Organisers said:

“The Harrogate Valley Gardens Summer of Fun is this weekend.

"We’ve got three days packed full of fun at this free entry event every day from 10.30am-5pm.”


A selection of food stalls will also be available throughout the weekend.
