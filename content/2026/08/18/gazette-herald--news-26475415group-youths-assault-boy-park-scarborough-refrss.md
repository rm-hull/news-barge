A group of youths allegedly assaulted a boy in North Yorkshire.

The incident is said top have happened on the Cinder Track by Manor Road Park in Scarborough at around 7.30pm on Monday (August 17).

North Yorkshire Police said: “We are looking for any witnesses or anybody who has information about the assault.

“We’re particularly appealing for a man who protected the victim and helped him get to safety to contact us.”

  


If you have any information that could assist the investigation, please email [daniel.farrington@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:daniel.farrington@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Please quote reference 12260160483 when providing information.
