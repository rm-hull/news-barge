A uniquely designed five-bedroom family home is on sale for £525,000 in Harrogate.

The property, in Sundew Heath, features more than 2,200 sq ft of living space on a large plot of land.

It has driveway parking, a detached studio/workshop, and beautifully established gardens that back onto open green space.

The home has been developed over the years to create a flexible living space suitable for modern family life.

The entrance hall has a ground floor cloakroom/WC and leads to a sitting room centred around a feature fireplace.

The heart of the home is a sequence of interconnected living spaces where the kitchen flows into the dining area, conservatory, and contemporary glass-roofed garden room.

The kitchen has a range of units, work surfaces, a range-style cooker, and breakfast bar.

The adjoining dining area opens into the conservatory and garden room, which has full-height glazing and a glazed roof with views across the gardens.

Completing the ground floor is a reception room that could be used as a fifth bedroom, snug, home office, or playroom, and a utility room for additional storage and laundry space.

On the first floor, there are four bedrooms, including a principal bedroom with an en-suite shower room.

The remaining bedrooms are served by a modern family bathroom.

Outside, the property has a driveway for off-street parking and a detached studio/workshop with electricity and underfloor heating.

The building is currently used as an artist’s studio with an adjoining gym.

The rear gardens are landscaped with mature trees, shrubs, and colourful planting, with various seating areas, terraces, and pathways.

The property also has owned solar panels.

Located in a cul-de-sac on the north-western side of Harrogate, the property is close to local amenities, schools, public transport links, and countryside walks.
