The best beauty advent calendars 2026 has to offer are landing earlier than ever, and this week saw Selfridges, the last of London’s big four department stores, finally reveal its festive offering. It means that, for the first time, I have the details of Harrods, Liberty, Selfridges and Harvey Nichols’ beauty advent calendars before September has even arrived.If you thought advent calendar season was still months away, think again: the big beauty retailers have brought forward their launches by several weeks, with many already opening waitlists, taking pre-orders or revealing their full contents.

Among the London stores, Cult Beauty, Space NK, John Lewis and Sephora have all shared details of their 2026 countdowns, but there are still plenty of other launches to come, including Next, which I'm expecting to hear more from soon.

Having reviewed beauty advent calendars for years (and bought them for more than a decade), I know there's more to judging one than simply counting the number of products or adding up their supposed value. I'll be looking at the brands featured, the full-size versus mini count, product selection (number of moisturisers, blushes and the like), value for money and, crucially, whether I'd actually want to open the doors myself.

I'll keep this guide updated as each new calendar lands, so consider it your running list of the best beauty advent calendars worth shopping this Christmas.
