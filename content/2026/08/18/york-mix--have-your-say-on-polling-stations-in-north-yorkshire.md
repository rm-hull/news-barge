The council wants residents in North Yorkshire to share their views on polling arrangements.

The consultation will consider around 800 polling districts and approximately 500 polling stations across North Yorkshire, with between 50 and 100 polling stations expected to be affected by the proposed changes.

Voters, particularly those who find it difficult to travel to or access their local polling station, are invited to have their say and help ensure polling arrangements remain suitable ahead of the full council elections in May 2027.

Find out more about the proposed changes and take part in the consultation at this council web link, here.

The outcome of the review will be used to prepare the revised Register of Electors, which is planned to be published on Tuesday 1 December 2026.

Where possible, people making representations should suggest alternative places that may be used as polling places.
