A North East eatery is seeking a premises licence, according to a public notice.

Blacks Corner Culture House Ltd has submitted an application to Sunderland City Council for a premises licence for its location at Keel Square, Sunderland.

The application outlines several proposed activities, including live music every day from 10am to midnight and recorded music from 9am to midnight.

It also seeks permission for the sale of alcohol, both for consumption on and off the premises, from 10am to midnight daily.

The licensing register is maintained by Sunderland City Council's Licensing Section at City Hall, Plater Way, Sunderland, and can be inspected by appointment.

Appointments can be made by emailing [licensing@sunderland.gov.uk](mailto:licensing@sunderland.gov.uk) .

Representations regarding the application must be made no later than August 26.

These must be made in writing and sent to the council or submitted via email.
