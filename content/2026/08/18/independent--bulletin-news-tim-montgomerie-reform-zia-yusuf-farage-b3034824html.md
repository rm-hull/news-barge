# Reform UK suspends senior figure after public criticism of Zia Yusuf

- Reform UK has suspended party board member and commentator Tim Montgomerie following his public criticism of home affairs spokesperson Zia Yusuf.
- Montgomerie accused Yusuf of failing to be a team player, freelancing on social media and behaving like a child toward party colleagues.
- The suspension comes after Montgomerie publicly condemned Yusuf for calling former Conservative defence secretary Ben Wallace a traitor.
- Speaking on a podcast, Montgomerie suggested Nigel Farage had fallen out of love with Yusuf and questioned whether Farage could bring him under control.
- It is understood Montgomerie was notified of his suspension prior to the broadcast of his latest critical podcast appearance.
