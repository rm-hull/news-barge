Police investigating a theft want to talk to the two teenagers on this CCTV image.

An e-bike was stolen from Nicholas Gardens in York between the hours of midnight and 5.30am on Sunday 21 June.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The e-bike was later sighted in the city centre and soon recovered.

#### Nicholas Gardens, York

“If you recognise the people pictured, please get in touch as they may have information that could assist our investigation.”
