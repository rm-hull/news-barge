Police have been called in after a fire in a play park.

Acomb firefighters put out the fire in an undisclosed location in **York** at**8.46pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “This involved a tyre which formed part of the play equipment.

“Backpack sprayer and bucket of water used to extinguish. Police informed of incident.”
