Drivers are facing delays in East Cleveland after two lorries broke down.

There are currently tailbacks along the A174, between Skelton and the Quarry Lane roundabout near Marske, after two lorries broke down on Apple Orchard Bank.

AA Traffic News is currently showing long tailbacks heading towards Skelton, as well as some delays on Marske Lane.

Cleveland Police said: "There are currently tailbacks on the A174 due to two broken-down lorries on Apple Orchard Bank on Marske Lane in Skelton.

"Please avoid the area where possible and take another route. Thank you for your patience."
