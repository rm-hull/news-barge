# Ricky Gervais says he’d marry longtime partner Jane Fallon for tax reasons

- Ricky Gervais has revealed he intends to marry his long-term partner Jane Fallon to avoid paying inheritance tax.
- The couple, both aged 65, have been together since 1982 and previously felt no need to wed after living together for four decades.
- Fallon revealed earlier this year that she was diagnosed with breast cancer following a routine mammogram and underwent two successful surgeries.
- In an interview with Saga magazine, Gervais stated he believes cancel culture has peaked and mainly impacted everyday workers rather than big-ticket comedians.
- The comedian also voiced support for assisted dying and confirmed he has no plans to retire from comedy.
