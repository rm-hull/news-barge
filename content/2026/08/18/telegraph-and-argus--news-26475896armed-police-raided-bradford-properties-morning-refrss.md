Armed police carried out raids at multiple properties in Bradford in the early hours of this morning (18 August).

The incidents took place in the BD9 area, including Westfield Drive, Nearcliffe Road, Jesmond Avenue and Hartman Place.

The addresses are all within a short distance of one another in a heavily-built-up residential area roughly half-way between Bradford Royal Informary and Lister Mills.

West Yorkshire Police confirmed the action was part of an ongoing operation.

A force spokesperson said: "In the early hours of the morning (18 August), armed police conducted warrants on Westfield Drive, Nearcliffe Road, Jesmond Avenue and Hartman Place in the BD9 area of Bradford as part of an ongoing operation."
