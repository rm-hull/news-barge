# Hayden Panettiere’s mother reacts to daughter’s death: ‘She lost her way’

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/3UPmA60X/poster.jpg?width=720)

- Lesley Vogel addressed the death of her daughter, Hayden Panettiere, in her first public comments to NBC News.
- Vogel criticized Brian Hickerson, stating her family had tried to remove him from Panettiere's life and that he was present when she died.
- Reflecting on Panettiere's career, Vogel highlighted the severe challenges faced by young people in the entertainment industry.
- Vogel expressed sorrow that Panettiere “lost her way,” wishing she had stayed true to herself despite her incredible attributes.
- She concluded by sharing her belief that Panettiere is now at peace with her brother Jansen, who died unexpectedly in 2023.
