A DELIVERY driver had a coat and dash camera stolen while on the job.

The theft took place on Quebec Street, Elland, on Friday, August 14, at around 11.30am while the driver was delivering furniture and had left the vehicle parked at the roadside.

Unknown suspects approached the vehicle, opened the door, and took the items before leaving in an unknown direction.

A spokesman for West Yorkshire Police said: "If you witnessed anything suspicious, have CCTV, doorbell footage, or dash cam recordings from the area at the relevant time, please report it to the police quoting the relevant incident reference number."

The force is reminding members of the public to always lock vehicles, even if only stepping away for a short time, and to remove or hide valuables from sight.

Police also advise parking in well-lit, busy areas.
