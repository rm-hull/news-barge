An "exceptional" four-bedroom detached home has been put on the market for £875,000.

The house, built around 1930, is on Newcastle Road in the Crossgate Moor area of Durham.

The property, called Fairway, is said to have undergone "meticulous modernisation" while retaining many of its original features.

  Newcastle Road, Crossgate Moor, Durham 

*(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

One of its most noteworthy features is the "stunning" gardens which rest on 0.3 acres of land, and are said to blend seamlessly into the open fields beyond.

Upon arrival at the property, an electric gate welcomes you, leading to a block paved driveway with parking space for multiple cars, complemented by an electric car charging point.

Bradley Hall, the estate agent marketing the property, said: "Stepping into the inviting hallway, you are greeted by an exquisite stain glass name plaque, while convenient storage space is ingeniously incorporated beneath the stairs.

  Newcastle Road, Crossgate Moor, Durham 

*(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

"Continuing from the hallway, you will find the open-plan kitchen/diner, featuring a bespoke kitchen design and a convenient built-in pantry cupboard.

"The kitchen showcases a range of high-quality appliances, including two Samsung induction hobs, a Samsung dishwasher, double AEG ovens, and a built-in microwave.

"Adjacent to the kitchen is the utility area, offering additional base units and accommodating a washing machine/dryer."

The dining area features a vaulted ceiling with Velux windows.

  Newcastle Road, Crossgate Moor, Durham 

*(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

Spanning the entire length of the house, the formal living room offers a dual aspect, with views of both the front and rear of the property.

The room also includes a gas fireplace with a marble and wood surround, along with built-in shelving nestled within the alcoves.

Through an original doorway adorned with stained glass windows, you can access the garden room.

  Newcastle Road, Crossgate Moor, Durham 

*(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

Completing the ground floor, you will discover a family room and a w/c.

On the first floor, you will find four generously proportioned bedrooms, with the master bedroom enjoying full-height built-in wardrobes and an en suite shower room.

The main bathroom has been recently renovated, featuring a walk-in shower, a bath, a w/c, a basin, and chrome hardware.

Underfloor heating has been added to this space.

  Newcastle Road, Crossgate Moor, Durham 

*(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

Additionally, the landing area offers a seating space, allowing you to relish views of the surrounding countryside.

The west-facing rear garden is said to exude tranquillity and seclusion, with mature shrubs and trees framing the property.

  Newcastle Road, Crossgate Moor, Durham 

*(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

The gardens have been landscaped and include paved seating areas and winding paths.

  Newcastle Road, Crossgate Moor, Durham 

*(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

The garage has been converted into a studio, complete with electricity and an outdoor water supply.

Furthermore, a separate garden office, also equipped with electricity, presents a versatile space for work or leisure.

Both the studio and garden office benefit from bifolding doors, merging the indoor and outdoor spaces.

The estate agent has said the home is "perfectly positioned" within convenient reach of the University Hospital of North Durham, Durham Johnston School, St Leonard's School, and several local primary schools.

Durham city centre is also said to be less than a mile away, with an array of shopping, recreational, and amenity options.
