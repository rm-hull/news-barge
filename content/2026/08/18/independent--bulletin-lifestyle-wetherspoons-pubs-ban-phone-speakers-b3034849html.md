# Wetherspoons bans people from using phone speakers in all 792 pubs

- JD Wetherspoon has banned customers from playing music or taking calls on loudspeaker across all 792 of its pubs.
- The chain asked visitors to switch their phones to silent or use earphones following an increasing number of complaints about noise.
- Wetherspoon chair Sir Tim Martin described the company's pubs as an oasis of tranquillity and contemplation.
- The decision comes amidst broader public debates over noise pollution, including campaigns targeting headphone dodgers on public transport.
- The policy announcement follows a recent profit warning from the pub chain due to slower sales growth and rising operational costs.
