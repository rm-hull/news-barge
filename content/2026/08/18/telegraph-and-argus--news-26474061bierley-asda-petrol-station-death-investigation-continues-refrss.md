POLICE enquiries are continuing today after a man was found dead outside a busy Bradford petrol station.

Emergency services were called to the ASDA petrol station on Rooley Lane at around 6.20am on Monday, August 17, following reports of a concern for welfare.

A man in his 40s was found dead at the scene. His death is currently being treated as unexplained.

This morning, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police confirmed enquiries were ongoing.

   

*(Image: T&A)*

In a statement released yesterday, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Emergency services were called to a report of a concern for welfare outside a petrol station on Rooley Lane, Bradford, at around 6.20am on August 17.

"A man in his 40s was sadly found dead and his death is being treated as unexplained."

A police presence remained in place at the petrol station throughout the day as officers continued their investigation into the man's death.

   

*(Image: T&A)*

Forensic specialists were seen carrying out examinations near the forecourt, where a police tent had been established.

   

*(Image: T&A)*

The petrol station was closed while enquiries were ongoing, with drivers unable to access the site or the neighbouring car wash.

Bierley and Holme Wood ward Councillor Matt Edwards (Greens) said: "Any news like this is deeply sad, and my thoughts are with the man's family and loved ones.

"I would urge anyone with information to contact West Yorkshire Police."
