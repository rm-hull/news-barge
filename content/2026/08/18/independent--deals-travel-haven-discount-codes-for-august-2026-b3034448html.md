## Helpful booking information

### £30 deposit

Haven offers low-deposit bookings from just £30, with flexible booking options to spread the cost of the remaining balance.

Always check the payment schedule before confirming your holiday, so you know when the remaining balance is due.

### Cleanliness guarantee

The cleanliness guarantee is designed to give you reassurance about the standard of their accommodation.

If your rental doesn't meet Haven's cleanliness standards when you arrive, you can report the issue to the on-site reception team so it can be addressed. If it can’t be rectified, you will receive a full refund for the cost of your holiday.

### Haven flexi plans

There are two Haven flexi plans (standard or premium), which enable you to amend and cancel your booking up to the day of arrival.

Depending on the plan and booking conditions, you may be able to make changes to your holiday, helping to reduce the risk of being locked into plans that later need to change. The exact benefits and restrictions vary, so check the terms when you book.

## Popular Haven holiday parks

### **Devon Cliffs**

Set on the Devon coast near Exmouth, Devon Cliffs is one of Haven's largest holiday parks. It's a great choice for families looking for a traditional seaside break, with a beach nearby and plenty of activities and entertainment on site.

### **Primrose Valley**

Primrose Valley is located on the Yorkshire coast, with direct access to the beach and the surrounding Yorkshire seaside resorts.

The park has a wide range of accommodation and activities, including an indoor and outdoor pool, inflatable arena and bungee trampoline, making it a popular choice during the school holidays.

### Weymouth Bay

Weymouth Bay’s Dorset location is an appealing option for families who want to combine a Haven holiday park with exploring the local area.

It’s located close to the popular Dorset seaside town of Weymouth, with easy access to a shingle beach and traditional seaside attractions. The site also has an adventure village, outdoor pool and lazy river, indoor pool and aerial high ropes.
