# DOJ plans record election monitor deployment for midterms

- The Department of Justice plans to deploy up to 1,000 election monitors for the upcoming midterms, marking the largest monitoring effort in the department's history.
- Harmeet Dhillon of the DOJ Civil Rights Division stated the monitors will focus on areas with language barriers, disability accessibility issues or “voter fraud issues.”
- DOJ Deputy Press Secretary Kiersten Pels confirmed the plan, though specific jurisdictions and final personnel counts remain unannounced.
- The deployment comes amid efforts by President Donald Trump to assert greater control over federal elections while continuing to promote unsubstantiated election fraud claims.
- Democratic lawmakers, led by Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer, are recruiting Senate staff as congressional observers to document potential voter interference.
