### Netflix looking for singles who have undergone dramatic weight loss for new dating series

![Netflix looking for singles who have undergone dramatic weight loss for new dating series](<[https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/11/22/2263930985.jpg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/11/22/2263930985.jpg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp) 83w>)

# Amid rising popularity of GLP-1s, Netflix is casting singles who’ve had dramatic weight loss

- Netflix has announced a new unscripted reality dating series titled Weight to Date.
- The documentary-style show will follow 10 to 12 singles navigating identity and romance after undergoing major body transformations and weight loss.
- Casting is currently open online for applicants who are legal U.S. residents aged 18 or older.
- The series expands Netflix's portfolio of reality dating shows, which includes hits like Love is Blind and Love on the Spectrum.
- The announcement arrives amid a significant surge in the popularity of GLP-1 weight-loss medications like Ozempic and Wegovy across the U.S.
