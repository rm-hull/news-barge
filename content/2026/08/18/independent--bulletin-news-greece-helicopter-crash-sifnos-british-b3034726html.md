# Helicopter crash in Greece kills British newlyweds and pilot

- A British couple on their honeymoon, Alexander Cromie and Marie Ebert, and a local pilot were killed in a helicopter crash on the Greek island of Sifnos.
- The aircraft spun out of control and burst into a fireball in the Tholos area on Monday evening, just minutes before its scheduled landing.
- Sifnos deputy mayor Manolis Fountoulakis stated the helicopter experienced a suspected mechanical failure in the air after residents reported a loud, strange noise.
- The UK Foreign Office confirmed it is in contact with local Greek authorities and stands ready to provide consular support to those affected.
- The tragedy comes shortly after two firefighters were killed when two helicopters collided while battling wildfires west of Athens earlier this month.
