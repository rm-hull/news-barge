POLICE have renewed their appeal to find a missing North Yorkshire woman who disappeared in July.

Tracey Smith, 57, left her home in her black Fiat Panda, carrying the registration WR10 ETK.

Officers say finding her vehicle remains a key line of enquiry that will help them piece together Tracey’s movements.

  


Tracey, from the Stokesley area, is described as white, 5ft 5 inches tall, of slim build with blonde, shoulder length hair.

She has a small mole above her hip.

  Tracey in her black Fiat Panda 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

It is unknown what she was wearing at the time of her disappearance on July 24.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said it is possible that Tracey’s car may be somewhere in less populated areas on the Cleveland-North Yorkshire border or the North York Moors/Cleveland Hills area.

They said: “Officers are appealing to the outdoor community, including walkers, runners, hikers, cyclists, climbers and anyone who visits the area to keep an eye out for Tracey’s car.

“There is a possibility that Tracey has travelled to London by other means, and that remains a line of enquiry.

“However, finding her car, which we believe is still in the local area, will help provide a more precise picture of her movements.”

  Police say a key line of enquiry into finding Tracey is the location of her car which she used when leaving her home in on July 24 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Inspector Ciaran Conlon of Hambleton & Richmond Neighbourhood Policing Team, said: “Extensive enquiries are ongoing to help find Tracey whose disappearance continues to be of great concern to us and her friends and family.

“Finding her black Fiat Panda is one of the key priorities in our search and I appeal to everyone who enjoys the local area to keep an eye out for it - from local beauty spots to the more remote areas of the national park.

“I also make a direct appeal to Tracey – if you see or hear this appeal, please get in touch with the police, your family or friends, and let us know that you are safe.

“Or, if you prefer, you can contact the charity, missing people on their free and confidential helpline 116 000, who can pass on a message to help put people’s minds at rest.”

If you have seen Tracey or her car, or know where she is now, please call 999.

If you have any other information that could help police find her, please call 101.

Please quote reference 12260154127 when passing on information.
