A striking modern home with sweeping views has come onto the market.

The contemporary detached house, in Alpine Way in Humbledon, is priced at £550,000 and occupies a generous third-of-an-acre plot near the top of Humbledon Hill.

Arranged over four floors, the property offers five bedrooms, four bathrooms and open-plan living areas designed with modern family life in mind.

   

*(Image: Peter Heron Residential Sales and Lettings)*

Christopher Stienlet, director of Paul Airey Estate Agents, said: “This is a truly unique home that combines bold architectural design with generous proportions, exceptional outdoor space and a highly desirable south-facing orientation.”

Mature trees and landscaped gardens surround the plot, giving the house a high degree of privacy while framing its far-reaching North East outlook.

At the heart of the home is the main living space, including a wood-burning stove for cosy evenings enjoying the view.

   

*(Image: Peter Heron Residential Sales and Lettings)*

Windows and bi-fold doors lead out onto a Juliet balcony, opening the room up to the sweeping vistas across Tyne and Wear and creating an easy flow between indoors and out.

Downstairs, the lower-ground floor has been designed as a leisure suite, opening directly onto the garden and offering flexible room for recreation or relaxation.

The kitchen is fitted with modern units, an integrated oven, gas hob and dishwasher, as well as an American-style fridge freezer.

   

*(Image: Peter Heron Residential Sales and Lettings)*

Outside, the landscaped gardens wrap around three sides of the home.

Formal terraces, decked seating areas and lawns create plenty of room for alfresco dining, entertaining or simply enjoying the south-facing setting.

There is also parking for several vehicles, along with two garages providing additional storage and practicality.
