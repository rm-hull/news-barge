# How Burger King is winning customers back with revamped Whopper

- Burger King achieved an 8.5 percent U.S. sales growth in the April to June quarter, outperforming competitors McDonald's and Wendy's.
- The sales surge follows an overhaul of the Whopper led by chef Amy Alarcon, who previously created Popeyes' viral chicken sandwich.
- Upgrades to the signature burger include a premium brioche bun, creamier mayonnaise and shape-preserving packaging.
- The successful redesign has helped Burger King reclaim the number two spot among America's largest fast-food burger chains.
- To keep up with growing consumer demand, Burger King announced plans to hire up to 60,000 U.S. workers.
