# Firefighters tackle blaze involving 50 tonnes of household waste at southeast London facility

- Around 100 firefighters and 15 fire engines are tackling a major blaze involving 50 tonnes of household waste at a facility in Deptford, southeast London.
- The London Fire Brigade was alerted shortly before 8 pm, deploying 32-metre and 64-metre ladders to pour water on the flames from above.
- Local residents have been advised to keep their windows and doors firmly shut due to the large volume of smoke produced by the incident.
- Rail services running into London Bridge station are being impacted while emergency services work to bring the fire under control.
- The cause of the fire on Landmann Way is currently unknown.
