A CRASH caused serious delays on a Bradford motorway last night.

The incident happened on M606 northbound between Junctions 1 and 2 at around 9.25pm on Monday.

Pictures on social media showed the stretch of motorway at a standstill following the collision.

Emergency services, including police and the ambulance service, scrambled to the scene.

An ambulance was dispatched but no one was taken to hospital.

The area reopened just before 10pm.

## What the emergency services said

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police attended a road traffic collision on the M606 North at around 9.25pm."

Yorkshire Ambulance Service confirmed that one ambulance was dispatched, but no one was taken to hospital.

All lanes reopened at approximately 9.56pm.
