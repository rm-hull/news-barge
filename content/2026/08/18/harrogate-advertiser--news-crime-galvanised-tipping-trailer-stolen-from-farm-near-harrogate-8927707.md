# Galvanised tipping trailer stolen from farm near Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a trailer was stolen from a farm near Harrogate.

The incident occurred on August 11 at approximately 1am. A police spokesperson said: “A 10 foot x 5 foot ‘Graham Edwards’ 3.5 galvanised tipping trailer was stolen from a farm in Plompton (HG5 postcode).

![This 10 foot x 5 foot "Graham Edwards" 3.5 galvanised tipping trailer was stolen on August 11 at 1am.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmY5OTY4YzVlLWRkYzEtNGZiMS04MmE4LWMyM2RlMGVjY2U1Mzo1ZjI5YTMzYi0xYTVkLTRhZTYtYjQ2OC02MDc3ZjBiMzQ2M2I=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



"If you have any information about the theft or have seen this trailer please contact the officer in the case PC 1864 Marshall on email - [email protected] or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

“If you know the current whereabouts of this trailer or spot it on the roads please contact North Yorkshire Police on 101

“Quote reference 12260155673 when passing on information.”
