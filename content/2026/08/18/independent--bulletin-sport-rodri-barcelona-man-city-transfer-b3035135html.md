# Rodri completes move to Barcelona from Manchester City in four-year deal

![The deal was finalised on Tuesday after Rodri successfully underwent a medical with the La Liga champions](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/18/18/2026-08-18T170405Z_76110461_UP1EM8I1BESO0_RTRMADP_3_SOCCER-SPAIN-BAR-RODRI.JPG)

- Rodri has completed a £65.4 million transfer from Manchester City to Barcelona.
- The midfielder has signed a contract until June 2030.
- The deal was finalised on Tuesday after successfully undergoing a medical with the La Liga champions.
- During a seven-year spell at Manchester City, Rodri won four Premier League titles and scored the decisive goal in the 2023 Champions League final.
- The transfer follows his performances for Spain this summer, where he was named player of the tournament in their World Cup triumph.
