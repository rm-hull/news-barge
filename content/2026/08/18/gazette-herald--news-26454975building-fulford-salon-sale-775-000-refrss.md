An eight-bedroom property in Fulford is up for sale for offers over £775,000.

The freehold building in Main Street is described by estate agents Ashtons as a mixed-use residential and commercial building with three separate apartments and a commercial unit.

Ashtons says it is an excellent opportunity for investors seeking to expand or diversify their portfolio.

The building has an EPC rating of D and is more than 3,000 sq ft in size.

There is off-street parking to the rear of the building, which the estate agent says is unusual for such a prominent position.

The ground floor apartment has two double bedrooms, a bathroom, a kitchen diner and a living room.

The first floor apartment has three double bedrooms, a bathroom, a kitchen diner and a separate living room.

The top floor apartment has two bedrooms and is accessed via the private rear car park.

The commercial unit is at the front of the building and is currently being used as a beauty salon, which has three treatment rooms.

The property is being sold as a going concern with tenants already in place.

The site is located in the highly sought-after area of Fulford, and is being offered with no onward chain and sold as a fully let going concern.

Ashtons added that this is a rare opportunity to acquire a substantial mixed-use investment in one of York's most desirable and well-connected locations.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact Ashtons on 01904 595164.
