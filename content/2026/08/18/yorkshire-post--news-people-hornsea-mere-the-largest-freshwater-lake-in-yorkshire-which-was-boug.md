# Hornsea Mere: The largest freshwater lake in Yorkshire which was bought for just £50

## As the largest freshwater lake in Yorkshire, Hornsea Mere is a popular destination for sailing, bird-watching and leisurely walks.

Yet fewer Yorkshire residents know that Hornsea Mere is part of the Wassand Hall estate, which was bought for £50 in the 16th century.

Hornsea Mere is open year-round, but this summer, visitors can also visit Wassand Hall.

The Constable family purchased Wassand Hall in 1529 and the hall remains a private residence.

![Hornsea Mere is Yorkshire's largest natural freshwater lake, located in East Yorkshire near the coastal town of Hornsea](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjc2MmE4NTg4LTcyNDQtNDBiMy05ZGJkLTcxYTAzOTJmMmU0NDo5YmQ5OTA3YS1iYWJiLTRmYjMtODhhYy1jODdiZDk0YWRmMzk=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



However, it opens to the public on select days throughout the year and the next available dates are August 28-30.

Visitors can tour its historic interiors including an ornate drawing room, library, and music room.

Each room is adorned with period furnishings, from porcelain dishware to antique furniture.

The family also displays a collection of 18th and 19th-century paintings.

For homemade cakes and refreshments, there is a tea room onsite.

Outside, there are walled gardens and shaded walks through wooded parklands.

Adult tickets cost £10, with the option of upgrading to all-day access for £15.

Discounted rates include reduced entry for children aged 11 to 15, and free admission for children under 10.

Visitors can start with breakfast at Hornsea Mere Cafe, before walking along the lakefront and spending a day at Wassand Hall.
