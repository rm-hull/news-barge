Police looking for a missing woman have released CCTV images of her leaving her home.

Tracey Smith, 57, from the Stokesley area, left her home in a black Fiat Panda with the registration number WR10 ETK.

Ms Smith was last seen on Friday, July 24.

   

*(Image: NORTH YORKSHIRE POLICE)*

Police believe her vehicle could be somewhere in the Cleveland/North Yorkshire border area, or possibly in the North York Moors or Cleveland Hills.

Inspector Ciaran Conlon of the Hambleton & Richmond Neighbourhood Policing Team said: "Extensive enquiries are ongoing to help find Tracey whose disappearance continues to be of great concern to us and her friends and family.

"Finding her black Fiat Panda is one of the key priorities in our search and I appeal to everyone who enjoys the local area to keep an eye out for it – from local beauty spots to the more remote areas of the national park."

   

*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Police are particularly asking members of the outdoor community – including walkers, runners, hikers, cyclists, and climbers – to look out for the vehicle in rural locations.

Inspector Conlon also addressed Ms Smith directly in his appeal.

He said: "I also make a direct appeal to Tracey – if you see or hear this appeal, please get in touch with the police, your family or friends, and let us know that you are safe.

"Or, if you prefer, you can contact the charity, missing people on their free and confidential helpline 116 000, who can pass on a message to help put people’s minds at rest."

There is a possibility Ms Smith may have travelled to London by other means, but police believe locating her car will help clarify her movements.

She is described as white, 5ft 5in tall, of slim build, with shoulder-length blonde hair and a small mole above her lip.

Anyone who has seen Ms Smith or her car, or knows where she might be, is urged to call 999.

Those with other information can call 101, quoting reference 12260154127.
