Dinosaurs descended on Northallerton on Sunday (August 16) as families enjoyed a prehistoric-themed day out.

Families turned out in force for the free Jurassic Northallerton on the High Street.

Young dinosaur enthusiasts had the chance to get up close to some prehistoric visitors while families enjoyed the lively atmosphere throughout the day.

There was a range of activities including children's games, giant Jenga, chess, a climbing wall and a bouncy castle.

Both adults and children queued up for photos with the dinosaurs roaming the streets, as local performers donned Jurassic-themed costumes.

Fans of Jurassic Park and Jurassic World had the chance to pose for photos with replica props from the film franchise.

Visitors could also bring home a memento from the event, with stalls selling a range of dinosaur-themed souvenirs including toys.
