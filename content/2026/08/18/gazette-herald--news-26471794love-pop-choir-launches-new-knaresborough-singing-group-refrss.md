A new women’s choir is launching in Knaresborough, promising an evening of music, laughter and connection.

Love Pop Choir will host a Prosecco launch night on Monday, September 7, from 7.30pm to 9pm at Forest Moor & Calcutt Village Hall.

The choir is open to women of all ages, and no singing experience is required.

Lauren Elliott, choir director, said: "Love Pop Choir is all about enjoying singing together, having fun and taking some 'me time' from our busy lives.

"It doesn't matter if you come alone or with friends, everyone gets on and sessions are very easy and relaxed."

There are no auditions, no solos, and no need to read music - all that’s needed is a willingness to join in and enjoy the music.

Attendees at the launch night will be offered a complimentary glass of Prosecco or orange juice.

Sessions take place every Monday during term time.
