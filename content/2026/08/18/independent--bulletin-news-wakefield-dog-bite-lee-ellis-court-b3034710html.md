# Manslaughter charge after man dies following dog attack

![The area near the old train line footpath off Blacker Lane in the Calder Grove area of Wakefield](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/17/13/01M07VZNCGSNVD7RXB2GWD86BC.jpg)

- Lee Ellis, 55, has been charged with manslaughter and owning a dog dangerously out of control following a fatal incident in Wakefield.
- A 55-year-old man died in hospital after an altercation involving a German Shepherd on an old railway footpath on Saturday.
- West Yorkshire Police confirmed the German Shepherd was seized and remains held in police kennels.
- Ellis, of Blacker Lane, Wakefield, was remanded into custody ahead of an appearance at Leeds Magistrates' Court on Tuesday.
- Specially trained officers are providing support to the family of the victim.
