# EastEnders shakeup as Nicola’s sisters Gemma and Jodie arrive with a bang

![Patricia Potter (Gemma), Jamie Bamber (Ned), Marian McLoughlin (Rose), Laura Doddington (Nicola), Anna Constable (Jodie) and Felix Uff (Ryder) who play the Hawkins family](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/31/12/01KYVVDKCVAM8PP5PJY22ZYGV9.jpg)

- *EastEnders* is welcoming the Hawkins family to Albert Square this week and they are already making an impression.
- Nicola Mitchell (Laura Doddington) was shocked to see sisters Gemma and Jodie make a dramatic entrance in Monday’s episode, closely followed by mother Rose.
- Gemma Hawkins is played by Patricia Potter, who is best known for her appearances on *Holby City* and*Doctors* .
- Marian McLoughlin, who plays Rose, is also known for her various roles on *Doctors*; she also had an appearance on* Hollyoaks*as Luke Morgan’s mother Sue in 2021 and played the recurring character DCI Chapman on* EastEnders* between 1991 and 1993.
- Jodie Hawkins is played by Anna Constable, who is known for her stage work and has starred in productions of *A Small Enclosed Room* and*Imposter 22.*
