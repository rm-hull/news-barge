THIEVES are reported to have stolen high-value gardening equipment from a York home.

The alleged burglary happened at around 4.45am on Monday (August 10) at a home in Ousecliffe Gardens.

It involved the theft of a STIHL-branded chainsaw, strimmer and hedge cutter from a greenhouse.

  


The suspects, North Yorkshire Police said, are approximately 6ft tall, of stocky build and were wearing dark clothing.

A force spokesperson said: “We’re particularly appealing for information about the items that were stolen, any CCTV or video doorbell footage, or from anyone who saw the suspects.

If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please email [lewis.appleby@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:lewis.appleby@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Please quote reference 12260154321 when providing information.
