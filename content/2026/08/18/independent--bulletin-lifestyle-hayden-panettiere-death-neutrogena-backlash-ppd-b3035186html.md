# Why Neutrogena is facing boycott calls after Hayden Panettiere’s death

- Neutrogena is facing widespread social media backlash and calls for a boycott following the death of actor Hayden Panettiere at age 36.
- In her memoir, Panettiere revealed that the skincare brand terminated her 10-year ambassador contract after she publicly discussed her postpartum depression diagnosis in 2015.
- Panettiere stated on Jay Shetty’s podcast in May that she was surprised when her representative told her that her remarks about PPD had broken her contract’s morality clause.
- Following Panettiere's death in South Carolina, fans and celebrities flooded Neutrogena's social media channels to condemn the brand's actions.
- Neutrogena has not publicly addressed Panettiere's death or responded to requests for comment regarding the resurfaced controversy.
