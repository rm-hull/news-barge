A couple from Ohio allegedly got drunk during a cruise and forced their way into a family's cabin where they assaulted a family and ended up punching a 9-year-old boy.

Gannon Jacox, 29, and his girlfriend Brooklyn Hicks, 28 were arrested on Saturday at Port Canaveral by Brevard County Sheriff's deputies, according to the *New York Post*. 

Jacox and Hicks were allegedly "arguing loudly after taking advantage of some adult beverages” when one of the victims asked them to be quiet, according to Brevard County Sheriff Wayne Ivey.

The couple allegedly became irate when they were asked to keep it quiet. Ivey said they "pushed their way into the victim's room" and that Jacox began fighting with two adults and their 9-year-old child.

Ivey said Jacox swung on everyone in the room, including the minor.

![Gannon Jacox, 29 and Brooklyn Hicks, 28, both cruise ship passengers, were arrested for allegedly assaulting three other travelers — including a 9-year-old child — after they were told to keep their voices down](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/18/16/07/Screenshot-2026-08-18-at-12-25-09-PM.png)

“As a fight ensued Jacox ends up hitting all 3 people (including the child) and Hicks was just swinging, hoping one would land,” Ivey wrote.

In his mugshot, Jacox appears to have an injury to his right eye. Ivey claims Jacox took a shot to the face during the scuffle.

“Jacox got a pretty gross injury to his eye, that was either caused by one of the victims or friendly fire from the Haymakers his girlfriend was throwing!” the sheriff wrote.

He noted in his report that none of the victims were "seriously injured in this ridiculous escapade."

Jacox was charged with burglary with battery and child abuse without great bodily harm. Hicks was charged with burglary with battery and battery-touch/strike.

Both were booked and placed in custody at Brevard County Jail.

Jacox's bond was set at $31,000. Hicks' bond is $6,000.

Ivey warned tourists to keep their cool on cruises.

“The moral of the story…if you can’t hold your liquor, then you definitely shouldn’t be out in public causing a scene like this and injuring random people including a child!!” he wrote. “And if that memo is not clear enough for you then you might want to stay your butt out of Brevard County!!”
