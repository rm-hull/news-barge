A sixth-floor flat offering panoramic views across York’s historic skyline has been listed for £695,000.

Located in the heart of the city, at Ryedale House, 58-60 Piccadilly, the flat has two double bedrooms, both with their own en-suite facilities.

One of the bedrooms is over 20 feet in length, and the second bedroom is described as suitable for guests or home working.

There is also a guest WC and a storage cupboard.

The main living area combines kitchen, dining, and reception space, featuring expansive windows that provide extensive views, including a clear sight of Clifford’s Tower.

According to details provided, the apartment is said to be flooded with natural light and finished in a contemporary style.

Parking is available, and residents have access to a concierge service and private locker storage.

The flat is leasehold, with the listing stating approximately 993 years are left on the lease, a ground rent set at £250 per year, and a service charge of £3,364 per year.

Energy Performance Certificate rating for the property is given as C.

Ryedale House’s city-centre position means that restaurants, cafes, shops, riverside walks, and historic attractions can be reached on foot.

The listing notes that properties with similar internal space and unbroken city views in York are in short supply.

The flat measures 1,009 square feet, according to the information provided. No mention is made of outside space or balconies.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact estate agents Ashtons on 01904 918018.
