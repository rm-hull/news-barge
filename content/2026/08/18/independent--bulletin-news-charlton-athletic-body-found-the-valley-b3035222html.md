# Investigation launched after body found at Charlton Athletic’s stadium

- Charlton Athletic have confirmed a man's body was discovered at their stadium, The Valley.
- Police were called to the venue following an incident in the early hours of Monday morning, where a male was pronounced dead.
- The club expressed their deepest sympathies to the individual's family and noted there is no suggestion of any wrongdoing by Charlton Athletic.
- Following the discovery, Charlton's under-21 fixture against Bolton was relocated to their Sparrows Lane training ground.
- The death remains under police investigation, and authorities have appealed for witnesses to come forward with information.
