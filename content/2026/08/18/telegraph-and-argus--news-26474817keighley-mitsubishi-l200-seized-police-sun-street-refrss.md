A stolen vehicle has been seized by police in Keighley.

Officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team found the Mitsubishi L200, parked on Sun Street, with its front number plate having fallen off.

West Yorkshire Police said: "A closer look revealed the vehicle was insecure, and on false plates.

Checks of the VIN soon revealed the Mitsubishi was an outstanding stolen vehicle from the Todmorden area."

The police have now recovered the vehicle.
