# What are the new A Court Of Thorns And Roses books called? Here’s what we know

- Author Sarah J Maas has unveiled the titles and cover artwork for the sixth and seventh books in her best-selling *A Court Of Thorns And Roses* series.
- The sixth instalment, titled *A Court Of Splintered Harmony* , will be published worldwide by Bloomsbury on 27 October.
- The seventh book, *A Court Of Forgotten Melody* , is scheduled for release on 12 January next year.
- The new releases will depict a major new storyline called *The Valkyrie Cycle* .
