# Three more earthquakes strike Spain’s Granada province

![A fallen tree branch lies atop a damaged vehicle on a street in the aftermath of an earthquake that struck southern Spain, in Granada, Spain](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/18/13/2026-08-18T120333Z_1123248543_RC2Z0NAN1P45_RTRMADP_3_SPAIN-QUAKE.JPG)

- Three earthquakes with magnitudes above 4 have struck Spain's southern Granada province, following a 5.0-magnitude tremor on Saturday.
- At least three people suffered light injuries during Tuesday's seismic activity, with one minor taken to hospital, according to Andalusia's regional top emergency official Antonio Sanz.
- The largest quake was a 4.7-magnitude tremor that had a surface-level epicentre in Gojar, 10 km south of Granada city, the country's National Geographic Institute (IGN) said.
- Authorities interrupted tourist access to parts of the historic Alhambra complex, while the provincial authority of Granada said it was shutting administrative buildings, museums and sporting facilities.
- Meanwhile, Granada's metro system said on X it was reducing the speed of its trains as a precautionary measure.
