AN E-BIKE was stolen from a house in a York suburb.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after an e-bike was stolen from a property in a York suburb.

The theft took place on Nicholas Gardens, off Hull Road, between midnight and 5.30am on Sunday, June 21.

Officers have released a CCTV image of two teenagers they would like to speak to in connection with the incident.

"The e-bike was later sighted in the city centre and soon recovered," said a spokesperson for the force.

Anyone who recognises the individuals or has information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on their website, live chat, or by calling 101.
