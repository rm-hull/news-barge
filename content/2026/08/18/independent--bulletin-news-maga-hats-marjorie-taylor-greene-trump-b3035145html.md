# Former Trump loyalist jokes about lack of MAGA hats being worn these days

- Former Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene publicly remarked on X that she rarely sees people wearing MAGA hats or Trump apparel anymore.
- Greene commented in response to the closure of a Trump Superstore in Knoxville, Tennessee, which shut down due to a severe drop in shoppers.
- Store owner William Hays stated that President Trump's popularity is dwindling as supporters struggle with high living costs and surging gas prices tied to the Iran war.
- A Reuters/Ipsos poll revealed that President Trump's job approval rating has fallen to 33 percent, with voters favoring Democrats on economic issues.
- The drop in political enthusiasm aligns with reports showing reduced audience viewership for several prominent right-wing social media influencers.
