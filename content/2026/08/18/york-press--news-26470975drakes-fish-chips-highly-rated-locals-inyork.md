## 'Special' fish and chip shop in York that offers a piece of the seaside in the city

This fish and chip shop is highly rated in York. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

If you are in York and craving an escape to the seaside, you might want to visit Drake’s Fish and Chips.

The award-winning fish and chip shop was established in 1984 and opened in York in 2010.

The fish and chip shop also has a location in Leeds, with a Knaresborough shop coming soon and can be enjoyed either as a takeaway or in the restaurant.

While you might not be able to get out of the city, you can enjoy a piece of seaside in the city centre restaurant.


## What is Drake’s Fish and Chips like?

Drake’s Fish and Chips’ website reads: “Our close-knit team is ready to provide you an outstanding dining experience, matching our traditional Yorkshire values of hearty food and great value for money with a 21st Century focus on top quality cooking and dedicated customer service.”

All of the restaurant’s fish has been caught around Iceland, Norway, and Finland, with the potatoes being grown in Yorkshire.

The fish is prepared carefully by the team to create “utter perfection”.

The takeaway menu has a range of classic dishes such as haddock, scampi, and pie served with chips.

The food spot also offers burgers, a few sides, and plenty of sauces from gravy and curry sauce to homemade tartare sauce.

If you sit in the restaurant, you can try some of the starters such as garlic mushrooms and prawn cocktail before enjoying your fish and chips.

To end your meal, you might want one of the desserts available, like the brown and blonde chocolate brownie, sticky toffee pudding or even a battered Mars bar.
