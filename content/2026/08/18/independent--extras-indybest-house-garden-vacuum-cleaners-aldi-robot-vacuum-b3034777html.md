In a plight to eradicate one of life’s most arduous chores, there are now robot vacuums to suit all budgets, with models ranging from feature-packed machines that cost upwards of £1,000 to options for less than £500, which have more basic features.

Aldi’s latest offering falls into the latter category, costing less than £70 while combining sweeping, vacuuming and mopping in one device.

The robot vacuum comes with a 400ml dustbin, a 250ml water tank, a HEPA filter that helps to capture particles as it cleans. It also has a side brush designed to prevent hair tangles, a big plus if you have long hair or pets.

Aldi says that sensors should help the robot avoid obstacles as it moves around your home, and that a fully charged battery provides around 1.5 hours of cleaning time. Once it needs more power, the automatic recharge function should send it back to its charging station. Plus, it can be controlled using either the included remote or an app.
