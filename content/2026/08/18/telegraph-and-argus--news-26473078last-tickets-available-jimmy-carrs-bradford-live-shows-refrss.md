LIMITED tickets remain for two upcoming performances by comedian Jimmy Carr.

The shows will take place at Bradford Live on Saturday, September 5, with a second performance added following demand.

A household name in the UK, Carr will be performing as part of his brand-new show, Laughs Funny.

His previous tour, Terribly Funny, was seen by more than 1.2 million people across 45 countries.

Bradford Live said: "If you like fast-paced, edgy one-liners then get ready to be happy.

"One of the world’s best-loved comedians, Jimmy Carr, is back on tour with a brand-new show.

"Jimmy tells jokes, and jokes are like magnets.

"Jokes attract some people, but they can also repel.

"Some people are repelled by Jimmy's dark brand of comedy.

"This show is not for them."
