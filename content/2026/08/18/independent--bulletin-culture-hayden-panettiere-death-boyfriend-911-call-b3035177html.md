# Police report reveals new details about Hayden Panettiere’s death

- Actor Hayden Panettiere, known for her roles in *Heroes*,* Nashville*and the* Scream* franchise, has died at age 36 in Greenville, South Carolina.
- Police in Greenville have released an incident report detailing the circumstances surrounding Panettiere's death at Judson Mill Lofts.
- Panettiere's boyfriend, Brian Hickerson, and his brother Zach were present at the apartment and placed the 911 call.
- Brian provided officers with a bag of Panettiere's medications, though the specific list of drugs was redacted in the public report.
- A responding officer noted that Zach was highly emotional during resuscitation efforts, while Brian did not show emotion until EMS declared Panettiere deceased.
