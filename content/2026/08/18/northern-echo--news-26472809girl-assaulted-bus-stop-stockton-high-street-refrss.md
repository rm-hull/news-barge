Police are trying to identify a man after a 12-year-old girl was assaulted at a bus stop.

The incident took place on Stockton High Street between 4pm and 4.40pm on May 27.

Cleveland Police said it has exhausted all other enquiries and is now appealing for the public’s help to identify the man in an image.

The force said he may be able to assist with the investigation.

The man in the image is described as a white male, around 5ft 7” tall, of stocky build and between 30 and 40 years old.

He is described as having mousey brown hair with dark blue eyes and was wearing a waterproof jacket which was navy or black.
