# Emergency services respond after multiple people in difficulty at Shoreham-by-Sea

![HM Coastguard deployed a helicopter alongside rescue teams from Shoreham, Littlehampton and Newhaven, as well as an RNLI lifeboat](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/18/16/35/lcimg-5a06d6d7-03f8-475a-9d8d-1f1fa41f2a19-copy-9e208826.jpeg)

- Emergency services were called to Shoreham Fort in West Sussex on Tuesday afternoon following reports of four people in difficulty in the water.
- HM Coastguard deployed a helicopter alongside rescue teams from Shoreham, Littlehampton and Newhaven, as well as an RNLI lifeboat.
- The South East Coast Ambulance Service dispatched multiple resources, including two air ambulances, with one road ambulance seen leaving under police escort.
- Sussex Police closed the A259 Brighton Road in both directions between New Road and Kingston Lane due to the ongoing incident.
- Authorities have urged the public to stay clear of the area while emergency responders remain at the scene.
