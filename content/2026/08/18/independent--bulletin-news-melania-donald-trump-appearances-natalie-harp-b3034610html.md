# Melania Trump has not been seen in public for nearly a month

- First Lady Melania Trump has not made a public appearance in nearly a month, with her last outing occurring on July 19 at the FIFA World Cup Final in New Jersey.
- She has recently missed several high-profile events attended by President Donald Trump, including Senator Lindsey Graham's funeral and the White House Correspondents' Dinner.
- Reports indicate Melania Trump has attended roughly half as many public events in 2026 compared to the same period during Trump's first term.
- In her absence, White House aide Natalie Harp has frequently traveled alongside President Trump to major international and domestic events.
- Harp, who helps manage Trump's Truth Social account, is considered by the president to be among his most loyal administration officials.
