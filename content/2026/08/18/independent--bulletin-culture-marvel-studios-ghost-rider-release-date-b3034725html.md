# Marvel Studios announces release date for Ghost Rider

- Marvel Studios has announced that *Ghost Rider* will be released on 28 July 2028.
- The film stars Ryan Gosling as the Spirit of Vengeance and is directed by Shawn Levy.
- *Ghost Rider* is among three Marvel movies being released in 2028.
- An as-yet untitled *X-Men* film will be released on 5 May and*Black Panther 3* is scheduled for 15 December.
- Levy and Gosling are also working together on *Star Wars: Starfighter* , a Lucasfilm title in which Gosling plays a pilot from “the wrong side of the galaxy”.
