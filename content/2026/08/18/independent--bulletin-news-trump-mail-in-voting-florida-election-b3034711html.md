# Trump votes by mail despite publicly calling it ‘scam’

- Donald Trump has voted by mail in Florida's Republican primary elections, despite repeatedly condemning mail-in voting as corrupt and fraudulent.
- The Trump administration has submitted an emergency request to the Supreme Court seeking to implement sweeping restrictions on mail-in ballots ahead of the midterm elections.
- White House spokeswoman Olivia Wales dismissed the controversy as a non-story, noting Trump primarily resides at the White House and supports mail-in voting exceptions for travel.
- A bipartisan group of 118 election officials and Democratic-led states warned the court that enforcing the executive order would cause chaos for voters.
- Florida is holding primary elections to select nominees for governor, congressional districts, and the Senate seat vacated by Marco Rubio.
