Police are appealing for information to help trace two missing Vietnamese men.

Dung, 28, and Chung, 43, are believed to be together and were last seen in Batley.

Dung last contacted his family in the early hours of August 15.

The police are urging anyone with information that could help locate the men to call 101, visit www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat, or contact the police and quote reference number 13260464916.
