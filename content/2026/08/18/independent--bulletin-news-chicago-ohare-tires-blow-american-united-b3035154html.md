# Chicago O’Hare has same incident happen twice with different airlines, hours apart

- Two separate commercial flights blew tires while landing at Chicago O’Hare International Airport on Monday.
- American Airlines Flight 386 from New York had two tires burst during landing, resulting in passengers being transported to the terminal by bus.
- Hours later, United Airlines Flight 739 from Omaha suffered a tire issue on landing, requiring passengers to evacuate using stairs.
- The Federal Aviation Administration is investigating both incidents, though officials have not indicated that the tire failures are connected.
- No injuries were reported in either incident, which follow two other tire-related aviation events in Chicago earlier this month.
