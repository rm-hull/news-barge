# Trump risks ridicule for using cheat card for White House event

- President Donald Trump used a staff-prepared cheat sheet during an Oval Office event honouring a teenage lifeguard who rescued a young boy.
- The card held by Trump contained photographs, biographical information of the meeting participants, and precise timing details for the schedule.
- Republicans and conservative commentators had previously launched severe criticism at Joe Biden for using similar note cards during his presidency.
- Trump previously mocked Biden for using generic staff notes, describing a 2021 press conference where Biden used a cheat sheet as sad and ridiculous.
- The incident comes as Trump faces ongoing scrutiny regarding his health and mental sharpness, with recent polls reflecting public doubts over his fitness for office.
