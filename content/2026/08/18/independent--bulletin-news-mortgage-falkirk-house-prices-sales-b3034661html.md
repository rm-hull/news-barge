# The UK town where it takes just 11 days to sell a home

![Falkirk, between Glasgow and Edinburgh in Scotland](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/18/00/17111802-9537b8d5-e05d-44e1-8103-f7e7fd5b9fbc.jpg)

- Property sales across Britain are progressing up to seven times faster in certain locations compared to others, according to Zoopla.
- While the nationwide average time to secure a buyer remained static at 42 days in the year to July, around half of local authority areas experienced longer transaction times.
- Melton in the East Midlands recorded the slowest sales process at an average of 76 days, whereas Falkirk in Scotland was the quickest at just 11 days.
- Zoopla attributed the regional delays to higher average mortgage rates, which have prompted prospective buyers to adopt a wait-and-see approach.
- Faster-selling markets are heavily concentrated in Scotland due to relatively affordable housing and a distinct sales system based on offers over valuation.
