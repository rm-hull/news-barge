# Who is MP Bayo Alaba and why has he been suspended?

![Bayo Alaba, MP for for Southend East & Rochford](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/17/15/55/Portrait.jpeg)

- Labour MP Bayo Alaba has been suspended from the party after referring himself for investigation regarding Covid business loans.
- The Southend East and Rochford MP allegedly attempted to wind up one of his businesses before settling a bounceback loan.
- Reports indicate that Mr Alaba, who was first elected to the House of Commons in 2024, is currently in the process of repaying both of the taxpayer-funded pandemic loans.
- Shadow cabinet office minister Mike Wood demanded transparency regarding whether ministers had to intervene to ensure debt repayment.
- The Labour Party confirmed that the whip suspension is a precautionary measure while an internal investigation takes place.
