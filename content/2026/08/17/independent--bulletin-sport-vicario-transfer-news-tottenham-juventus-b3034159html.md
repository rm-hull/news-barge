# Spurs goalkeeper Guglielmo Vicario set for loan move to Italian giants Juventus

![Guglielmo Vicario will sign for Juventus on a season-long loan](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/10/22/5a8d0cee35955fa6f7dee270cb6027d6Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzc4NTA5MzA3-2.83931055.jpg)

- Tottenham Hotspur have agreed a deal allowing goalkeeper Guglielmo Vicario to join Juventus on a season-long loan with an option to buy.
- The 29-year-old Italy international requested to leave Spurs earlier this summer to return to his homeland after three years in north London.
- Juventus accelerated talks for Vicario after potential moves for Aston Villa's Emiliano Martinez and Parma's Zion Suzuki fell through or stalled.
- Spurs manager Roberto De Zerbi sanctioned Vicario's departure following impressive performances from young goalkeeper Antonin Kinsky late last season.
- Vicario, who made 117 appearances for Spurs and helped them win the Europa League, could feature in Juventus's Serie A opener against Frosinone on Sunday.
