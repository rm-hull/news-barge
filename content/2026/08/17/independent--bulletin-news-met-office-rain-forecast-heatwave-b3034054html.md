# When it will finally rain after record-breaking UK drought

- Rain, showers, and potential thunderstorms are set to bring cooler and more changeable conditions to the UK following record-breaking summer heat and drought.
- A yellow heat health alert remains extended until Tuesday morning for London, the South East, and the South West.
- Firefighters across the UK have been tackling severe wildfires, with South Wales Fire and Rescue Service reporting unprecedented demand.
- Met Office meteorologists state that a shifting jet stream will allow low pressure to dominate, bringing much fresher weather by mid-week.
- A 50-year-old man has been charged with arson following a series of small fires started in Hackney, east London, on Saturday.
