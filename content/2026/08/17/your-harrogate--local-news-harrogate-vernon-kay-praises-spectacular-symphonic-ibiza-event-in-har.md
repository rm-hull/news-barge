TV and radio presenter Vernon Kay has praised the "absolutely spectacular" Symphonic Ibiza event held in Harrogate over the weekend.

The event took place at the Great Yorkshire Showground on Saturday (15th August), bringing together classic Ibiza and 90s dance anthems with a live orchestra.

Vernon was among the performers at the event, taking to the stage with a live DJ set.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a82acd1a65b9e298df.jpg)


Following the show, he shared his praise for the Harrogate crowd and the event with his 950,000 followers on Instagram.

He wrote:

“Harrogate Show ground! Ibiza Symphonic you were absolutely spectacular!

“What an evening of big 90’s house anthems!”


Symphonic Ibiza featured a 40-piece orchestra performing dance classics alongside DJs and live vocalists.

The line-up also included performances from Kym Sims, Heather Small, Rebecca Ferguson MBE and Alison Limerick.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a82ad61320b3d9609a.jpg)


Former Love Island star and DJ Alex Bowen opened the evening before Vernon and the Symphonic Ibiza performances.

The event saw thousands of music fans descend on the Great Yorkshire Showground for an evening of live music and classic dance anthems.

Saturday’s event also marked the return of major outdoor music to the Great Yorkshire Showground, with Symphonic Ibiza the first outdoor concert to be held at the venue since Sir Elton John performed there in 2012.
