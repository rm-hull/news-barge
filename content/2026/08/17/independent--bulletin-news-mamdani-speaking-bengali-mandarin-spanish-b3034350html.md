# Internet reacts to Mayor Mamdani speaking multiple languages to promote new campaign

- New York City mayor Zohran Mamdani went viral on social media after releasing videos of himself speaking Bengali, Mandarin, and Spanish to promote a free Broadway ticket campaign.
- Created in partnership with the non-profit Theatre Development Fund, the initiative offers 2,100 free tickets across seven Broadway productions to local high school students.
- The ticket lottery opened on August 13 and closed on August 17, with the final drawing scheduled for September 1.
- Prominent online figures and brands, including actress Rachel Zegler and language app Duolingo, commended Mamdani for his impressive linguistic talent and accurate pronunciation.
- Mamdani, who moved to New York at age seven, studied Mandarin in high school and frequently uses multiple languages such as Arabic, Hindi, and Urdu on the campaign trail.
