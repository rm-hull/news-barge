The winners of the 2026 Knaresborough Business Awards have been revealed.

Awards were presented across a range of categories, covering retail, hospitality, health and wellbeing, professional services and community organisations.

The annual awards, organised by Knaresborough Business Collective, celebrate the town's independent business community, with winners decided following a public vote.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a82a790b944c239895.jpg)


First launched in 2022, the awards have grown to feature 20 categories covering everything from hospitality and retail to professional services, customer service and community contribution.

This year's ceremony was held at Henshaws on Saturday (15th August), with Six Poor Folk taking the top prize of Knaresborough Business of the Year.

The full list of winners and highly commended businesses is:

- 
  **Favourite Female Led Business** – Winner: Market Town Deli; Highly Commended: Stomp
- 
  **Favourite Male Led Business** – Winner: The Barber Asylum; Highly Commended: Wild Style Tattoo
- 
  **Favourite Home Based Business** – Winner: The Knaresborough Bake Shed; Highly Commended: Sarah’s Childcare
- 
  **Favourite Healthcare & Wellbeing Business** – Winner: Chiropody By Louise; Highly Commended: Knaresborough Podiatry
- 
  **Favourite Hairdressers/Barbers** – Winner: Divine; Highly Commended: Kelly Teggin Hair & Beauty
- 
  **Favourite Beauty Clinic** – Winner: Boujee; Highly Commended: The Tanning Booth
- 
  **Favourite Retailer** – Winner: Harriets; Highly Commended: Twigi Boutique
- 
  **Favourite Shop Front** – Winner: Blind Jack’s; Highly Commended: The Crystal Buddha
- 
  **Favourite Professional Service Provider** – Winner: Greenwoods; Highly Commended: Newby James
- 
  **Favourite Attraction** – Winner: The Kingfisher Kiosk at Conyngham; Highly Commended: Knaresborough Exotic Rescue
- 
  **Favourite Coffee Shop** – Winner: Esme’s; Highly Commended: Nord
- 
  **Favourite Place To Eat** – Winner: So Bar & Eats; Highly Commended: Drakes
- 
  **Favourite Place To Drink** – Winner: Blind Jack’s; Highly Commended: The Half Moon
- 
  **Favourite Tradesman** – Winner: Brian King; Highly Commended: Sam Bolton
- 
  **Favourite Class/Workshop** – Winner: Painting Pots; Highly Commended: Freedom
- 
  **Favourite New Business** – Winner: Bonbon; Highly Commended: Sprout
- 
  **Best Customer Service** – Winner: The Farm Dairy; Highly Commended: Carol’s Bonnie Dogs
- 
  **Favourite Business Team** – Winner: Six Poor Folk; Highly Commended: Esme’s
- 
  **Inspiration Of The Year** – Winner: Knaresborough Lions; Highly Commended: Craft & Social
- 
  **Knaresborough Business Of The Year** – Winner: Six Poor Folk; Highly Commended: Freedom Performing Arts

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a82a78fe4e80cd6a26.jpg)


Organisers Knaresborough Business Collective congratulated all of the winners and those shortlisted.

They said:

"What an absolutely incredible night filled with excitement, love, laughter, fun, and an overwhelming love of Knaresborough.

"Seeing so many Knaresborough businesses come together to celebrate is just incredible, cheering each other on.

"We are so proud of Knaresborough and so lucky to be part of such a beautiful business community like no other."
