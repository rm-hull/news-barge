# Liverpool defence will take time to gel, admits Virgil van Dijk

- Virgil van Dijk has stated that developing an on-pitch understanding with Liverpool's new defensive signings, Jeremy Jacquet and Ronald Araujo, will naturally take time following a major summer overhaul of the central defence.
- The defensive restructuring follows the departure of Ibrahima Konate alongside persistent fitness struggles for Joe Gomez and Giovani Leoni, prompting Liverpool to secure £60 million signing Jacquet and Barcelona loanee Araujo.
- Both new defenders impressed during a 2-0 friendly win over Como on Sunday, where 21-year-old Jacquet scored the second goal on his debut after recovering from a long-term injury before making way for Araujo.
- Liverpool’s opening game of the season at Newcastle will mark the first competitive test under new head coach Andoni Iraola, who aims to re-establish a high-tempo tactical style following Arne Slot's two-year tenure.
- Van Dijk emphasised that while the squad is adapting to Iraola's pressing demands, Liverpool must remain compact and manage energy levels effectively across different phases of a match.
