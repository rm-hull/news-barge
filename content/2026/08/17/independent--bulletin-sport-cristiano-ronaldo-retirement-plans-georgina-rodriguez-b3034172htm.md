# ‘This is probably my last year in football,’ says Cristiano Ronaldo

- Cristiano Ronaldo has suggested that the current season will likely be his last in professional football as he aims to leave a spectacular legacy.
- The 41-year-old Portuguese forward has scored 950 goals for club and country and is aiming to reach 1,000 senior goals before hanging up his boots.
- Ronaldo revealed he has his post-football future mapped out, citing travel and padel among his favourite hobbies to fill the time.
- The legendary forward recently married his partner Georgina Rodríguez in Cascais following Portugal's World Cup campaign in North America.
- Ronaldo has rejoined Saudi Pro League champions Al-Nassr under new manager Ange Postecoglou, with his final matches potentially taking place in May.
