A HISTORIC agricultural show drew record crowds and entries as it returns for its 155th year.

The Rosedale Show, held in the village of Rosedale in the North York Moors National Park, took place on Saturday, August 15, with record-breaking entries.

A spokesperson for the show said: "The nationwide heat wave thankfully relented on Saturday 15 August for Show Day in Rosedale which went ahead on the Milburn Field in the village in balmy conditions.

"There was a record breaking 2,600 show entries in this year's Show catalogue and the large crowds of locals and visitors to the dale - thought to be around 5,000 - enjoyed the programme of agricultural, horticultural and industrial exhibits, along with a wide variety of arena events, trade stands, musical performances and food outlets.

"As ever, the Show could only go ahead thanks to the efforts of the organising committee over many months and to the many volunteers who turned out to help set up the arenas, then man the gates and run the programme of events on time and safely.

"Their vital contributions made Rosedale Show 2026 one of the best in its 155 year long history and 'the best little show in the county' according the flood of social media comments that followed."
