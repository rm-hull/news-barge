## Large quantity of LEGO reported stolen from North Yorkshire discount store

Officers would like to speak to the two men pictured after an alleged theft at B&M Bargains in Selby *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A large quantity of LEGO is reported to have been stolen from a popular discount store in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police have released images of two men officers would like to speak to after the alleged theft in B&M Bargains in Abbey Walk, Selby at 2pm on Friday (July 3).

  


  A large quantity of LEGO was reportedly stolen from B&M Bargains 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A force spokesperson said: “If you recognise the men pictured, please get in touch as they may have information that could assist our investigation.

Please email: Rhian Edmondson on [Rhian.Edmondson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Rhian.Edmondson@northyorkshire.police.uk) or visit our website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’.

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“Please quote reference 12260124898 when providing information.”
