EMERGENCY services were called to a North Yorkshire village today to rescue a person trapped inside a concrete tank.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service say three of its crews – Thirsk, Ripon, and Acomb – were called to Husthwaite at 12.57pm today (August 17) to help a person who had become trapped inside a concrete tank.
