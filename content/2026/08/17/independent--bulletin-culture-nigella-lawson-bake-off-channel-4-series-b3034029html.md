# New judge Nigella Lawson pictured with Bake Off co-stars

![Nigella Lawson (second right) with fellow judge Paul Hollywood (left) and hosts Alison Hammond and Noel Fielding ahead of the new series of The Great British Bake Off](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/16/14/01M050Z49P3RAYWK1P62BH8X4R.jpg)

- Nigella Lawson has been pictured for the first time alongside her *Great British Bake Off* co-stars ahead of the new Channel 4 series.
- The 66-year-old TV chef replaces Dame Prue Leith, who announced her departure from the judging panel in January after joining in 2017.
- Filming for the upcoming series has now concluded, with the show scheduled to air next month.
- Lawson expressed her excitement about joining the show, describing it as an institution of national treasure status.
- Channel 4 promised viewers that Lawson will bring her trademark warmth, wit, and passion for flavour to the iconic *Bake Off* tent.
