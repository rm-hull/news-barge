Every child learns differently. That’s why finding the right educational support makes all the difference. These independent schools, academic coaches and one-to-one tutors provide a tailored education that helps children thrive and be prepared for university and life beyond.

## Cultivate global ambition with an island education

![](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/17/9/16/King-William-College.jpeg)

King William’s College is a boarding and day school located in the Isle of Man, a self-governing British crown dependency with low crime, scenic beauty and strong community spirit. At secondary level, pupils begin their academic journey with (I)GCSEs before choosing one of three Sixth Form pathways: the globally respected IB Diploma Programme, IB Courses or the newly introduced blended A-Level pathway, all of which offer outstanding value.

With pupils from all parts of the globe, King William’s College nurtures confident, curious achievers who are well equipped to make their mark on the world, in whichever field they choose to excel.

**VisitKwc.imto find out more or arrange a private tour by emailing [admissions@kwc.im](mailto:admissions@kwc.im) or calling 01624 820110**

## **Prepare your child for a changing world**

![](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/17/9/40/Haberdashers-Elstree-Schools.jpeg)

Haberdashers’ Elstree Schools offer a unique model of education, which combines the academic strengths of single-sex teaching with the advantages of a shared, co-educational campus. Set across 100 acres in Elstree, Hertfordshire, Habs Boys and Habs Girls educate pupils from four to 18, nurturing intellectual curiosity, resilience and confidence. Named Independent School of the Year 2025, the schools were also recognised for Outstanding Educational Partnerships, owing to the institutions’ longstanding partnerships programme with six local state schools.

With a mission to be at the forefront of education, its students are all encouraged to engage with emerging ideas, from artificial intelligence and sustainability to entrepreneurship and digital innovation. Not just about strong academic outcomes, Habs is known for its pastoral care, broad co-curricular provision and continued investment in facilities.

**Learn more atHabselstree.org.uk**

## Give your child a head-start at the UK’s only STEM school

![](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/17/9/55/National-Mathematics-and-Science-College.jpeg)

The future belongs to innovators, problem-solvers and scientific thinkers. At the National Mathematics and Science College (NatMatSci), talented students with a passion for mathematics and science thrive through outstanding teaching, advanced laboratory experience, research opportunities and an award-winning STEM Communication Programme.

The purpose-built campus integrates maths, science, engineering, data and design into everyday learning, while luxury single-occupancy en suite boarding accommodation provides an exceptional home-from-home environment. Ranked among the UK’s top three schools for A-level results, NatMatSci prepares students for leading universities, with a one-in-five progression rate to Oxbridge and later STEM careers. Applications are now open for the school’s two-year GCSE programme launching in September 2027.

**Contact [admissions@natmatsci.ac.uk](mailto:admissions@natmatsci.ac.uk) or visitNatmatsci.ac.ukto join the virtual open event on 16 September**

## **Enrol your child at an excellent international school in Germany**

![](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/17/10/19/International-School-Bremen.jpeg)

For parents who wish for their children to receive an internationally minded education, International School Bremen (ISB) in Germany is a leading IB World School that educates students from early years through to diploma level. The school is home to 380 students from 48 different countries and creates a welcoming international community with a strong ethos of pastoral care.

ISB is fully accredited in the International Baccalaureate and NEASC frameworks and follows a rigorous and globally recognised curriculum. Students graduate with the IB diploma or ISB’s high school diploma, providing them with the skills and knowledge to thrive at universities worldwide and in their future careers. ISB promotes small class sizes and a broad co-curricular programme and welcomes day children as well as boarders. Those who board get their own room to nurture their independence.
