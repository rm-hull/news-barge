# Groundbreaking new kidney transplant process rolled out on NHS

- The NHS is rolling out a double matching blood process nationwide to reduce the risk of organ rejection in kidney transplant patients.
- Under the new system, scientists will align white blood cells as closely as possible in addition to matching traditional blood types.
- NHS Blood and Transplant estimates the initiative could prevent up to 100 kidney transplant rejections each year.
- The scheme is being implemented across the country following a successful pilot at London's Hammersmith Hospital and the Royal Free.
- The move aims to address antibody risks from blood transfusions and reduce the waiting list, which currently exceeds 7,300 people.
