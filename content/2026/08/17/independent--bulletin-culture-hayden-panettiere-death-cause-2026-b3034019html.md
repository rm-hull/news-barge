# Actor Hayden Panettiere dies aged 36

- Actor Hayden Panettiere, best known for her roles in *Heroes*,* Nashville*, and the* Scream* franchise, has died at the age of 36.
- Her father, Skip Panettiere, confirmed her death in a statement to ABC News, though no cause of death was announced.
- Panettiere rose to fame playing cheerleader Claire Bennet in *Heroes* and earned two Golden Globe nominations for her role as Juliette Barnes in*Nashville.* 
- She spoke candidly throughout her life about her personal struggles with addiction, postpartum depression, and trauma.
- Her death comes after the release of her memoir, *This Is Me: A Reckoning* , and three years after the death of her younger brother, Jansen.
