A WOMAN has been arrested following a police incident in York city centre.

Police were called to Fossgate in York city centre today after one woman was seemingly arrested by officers outside the Age UK shop.

One eyewitness reported seeing people crowding around the back of the van, with one woman sitting inside the vehicle speaking to police.
