**More than 8,000 South East Water customers have been affected by water supply issues across various parts of Kent.**

The firm said its staff were exhausted and "pushed to limits" as they attempted to restore water to areas including Herne Bay, Blean and Brabourne.

It said problems in the Wraik Hill area had been resolved but the storage tank remained "critically low", while in Benenden and surrounding areas about 400 properties had low pressure or no water after pipe bursts and high demand during the hot weather.

The firm apologised to customers and warned that some might not see water supplies return for a "couple of days" until demand drops and storage tank levels increase.

South East Water said issues were a result of prolonged hot weather, high demand, recent pipe bursts, and overnight power issues.

Mike Pickard, incident support manager at the water company, told BBC South East: "It's an ongoing challenge for us at the moment and we apologise sincerely to all affected customers."

He admitted the situation was "unacceptable" but said the firm was doing everything it could.

He added: "Our team is exhausted and being pushed to limits to make sure water is running."

## Staff receiving abuse

Jenny Rhodes, incident manager at South East Water, urged customers with water to only use it for essential reasons.

She added that the company was aware of a number of incidents of abuse directed at staff and contractors.

"Whilst we fully appreciate the frustrations due to the current disruption, we do not tolerate any abuse towards our staff," Rhodes said.

"Our team is working to restore supplies as quickly as possible and deliver water to customers.

"We fully acknowledge that this level of service is unacceptable, especially during hot weather."

Defra said the situation was "simply not good enough."

"South East Water needs to go further and faster to reduce leaks and follow their drought plans to ensure supplies are not disrupted in even the driest of weather," a spokesperson added.

"Their new leadership must now deliver a step change in performance, where customers' needs are prioritised and there is a stop to supply outages."

![South East Water customers collect water from a collection point](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1024/cpsprodpb/d585/live/375b3bc0-9a16-11f1-b0ec-399275ee28cb.jpg)

A number of bottled water stations have opened in the affected areas

In Blean, Canterbury, South East Water said a technical issue meant it was unable to pump water. It said it was working hard to fix the issue as quickly as possible.

Overnight power issues left a further 200 properties in the Wraik Hill area of Whitstable without full supply, but the firm said supplies were expected to be restored on Monday morning.

Meanwhile, it said customers in the Herne Bay area affected by water supply issues on Sunday had supplies restored overnight.

However, it said the area was experiencing further problems on Monday morning as the drinking water storage tank which supplied the town remained critically low.

"We are doing everything we can to try to recharge the levels in the storage tank that feeds the area to try and make sure we can restore supplies as soon as possible," it said.

At about 19:00 BST on Monday, South East Water said supplies had been restored for 800 properties in the Charing, Challock and Molash areas.

## 'Ongoing, changing picture'

One customer, Frazer Stodart, said: "I currently have eight people in the house and no water.

"I'm slightly annoyed after a large increase in bills - the least they should do is provide water."

David Ciechanski added: "It is getting a lot more common.

"We first had it a few months ago and there's now been one every three or four weeks across east Kent."

Pickard said: "We know we need to invest in certain areas and we are, but we can't do that overnight.

"I would love to say when all supplies will be back but it is an ongoing, changing picture."

South East Water said bottled water stations would be open until 22:00 at:

- The Weald Sports Centre in Cranbrook
- Challock Village Hall
- Sainsbury's at Altira Business Park in Herne Bay
- Kavanagh Cinema in Herne Bay
- Wincheap Park and Ride in Canterbury
- Sturry Road Park and Ride in Canterbury

They will reopen at 07:00 on Tuesday.
