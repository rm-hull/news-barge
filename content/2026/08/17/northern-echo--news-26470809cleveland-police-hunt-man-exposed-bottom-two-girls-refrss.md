Cleveland Police are appealing for information after a man exposed himself to two teenage girls.

The incident happened on Ingleby Way in Ingleby Barwick, Stockton, between 6pm and 8pm on Friday, July 24.

Cleveland Police said the man exposed his bottom to the girls as they were walking along the road.

There were several people in the park at the time, including a group of about five children.

The suspect is described as white, in his late teens or early 20s, with brown hair and wearing black shorts.

Inspector Simon Fetigan, from Stockton’s response team, said: "We understand this will be concerning to parents and the wider community, and we are doing all we can to locate the man.
