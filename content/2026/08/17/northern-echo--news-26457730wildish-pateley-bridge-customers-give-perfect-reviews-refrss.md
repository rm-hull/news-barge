A café and lifestyle store in a popular North Yorkshire location has earned a perfect rating from visitors, with customers travelling from as far as Australia praising its coffee.

Wildish, on the High Street in Pateley Bridge, currently holds a perfect five-star rating on Tripadvisor, with every review rating it "excellent".

Visitors regularly praise its speciality coffee, relaxed surroundings and friendly welcome, with some describing it as serving the "best coffee for miles".

One customer visiting from Australia wrote: "This is the best coffee for miles.

"We are from Australia, and this coffee is up there with the best at home.

"Thanks for a great flat white."

  Wildish, on the High Street in Pateley Bridge 

*(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

Another visitor, who also lives in Australia, went even further, saying: "This is the best coffee we have ever had.

"Even in London during our stay, nothing beats this place."

Wildish opened in July 2022 after its owners spent three years living and working in a remote part of the Yorkshire Dales.

They said the isolation had begun to take its toll, while their growing collection of bags was also taking over their home.

When the opportunity arose to take on a shop in Pateley Bridge, they decided to combine several of their passions under one roof.

The owners say: "We love bags, great coffee, beautiful things and awesome people.

"Opening the store has brought all those things together for us and more."

Wildish now combines a speciality coffee shop with a store selling British-made bags and other products.

Coffee is at the centre of many of its reviews.

One couple said they discovered Wildish on a Saturday and enjoyed their drinks so much that they returned the following day.

They wrote: "If you like coffee this place is for you.

"Amazing coffee, great service, friendly atmosphere.

"We will definitely be back."

Another reviewer described their decaf flat white as the "best" they had tried and praised the store for being dog friendly.

A local customer called Wildish a "firm fave", adding that "so much love and attention" went into making the coffee.

Visitors can also choose from different coffees and brewing styles.

One reviewer recommended the Workhorse pour-over, describing the range as "interesting and tasty but unpretentious".


Another customer said the choice of blends and single-origin coffees was enough to "bring you back for more".

The food has attracted praise too, with customers mentioning cakes, wraps and sweet treats.

One reviewer described the coffee as "divine", adding that the Syrian wraps were delicious and the cake "gets better with every bite".

Another visitor singled out the carrot cake, calling it the best they had eaten in some time.

Wildish has also developed a reputation for its atmosphere.

Visitors describe it as relaxed, stylish and welcoming, with several reviewers saying staff and locals are particularly friendly.

A family visiting from Cheyenne, Wyoming, said their lattes were "excellent" and praised both the staff and local customers for being welcoming.

Another customer who stopped while passing through Pateley Bridge said: "Lovely atmosphere and great tasting coffee.

"The lady serving was very friendly.

"We will come back again."

The store's retail side is another part of its appeal.

Visitors can browse local produce while having a drink, with reviews frequently mentioning its bags, clothing and other merchandise.

One customer warned that "the merchandise is very tempting", while another praised the business as "truly ethical and inspiring".

The store is also associated with the Wildish Club, described by one visitor as being "like Cubs for Adults" and aimed at people who enjoy spending time in nature.
