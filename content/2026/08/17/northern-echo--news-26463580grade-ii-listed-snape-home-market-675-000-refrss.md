Believed to date back to the 1600s, Springfield is a Grade II listed detached home originally constructed as a traditional cruck house and sympathetically renovated over recent years to create a beautifully appointed cottage.

The property’s entrance hall leads through to a characterful and spacious sitting room with exposed beams, original cruck timbers, and a multi-fuel stove set on a stone hearth.

Beyond this lies a versatile reception room currently used as a playroom but equally suited to being a snug or home office.

  Springfield is a stylish blend of period features and modern additions. 

*(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

To the rear is an oak-framed garden room, completed in 2023, which provides a spectacular living and dining space.

Featuring exposed oak framing, French doors, and large windows overlooking the lovely gardens, it is undoubtedly one of the property’s standout features.

Completing the accommodation is a cloakroom with exposed original stonework; a modern family shower room; two generous double bedrooms, both retaining character features including exposed beams, deep window sills and plantation shutters, and the kitchen.

  Springfield is a stylish blend of period features and modern additions. 

*(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

This is home to cream shaker-style units complemented by oak-effect work surfaces, a range of integrated AEG appliances including an induction hob, double oven and grill alongside a fridge/freezer and dishwasher, and a bespoke built-in seating area which not only provides a practical dining space but also additional storage.

Springfield is set in gardens that extend to around 0.6 acres and include a generous stone flagged patio, sweeping lawns, flower beds, mature trees, a wildlife pond and a number of seating areas, creating a lovely setting to enjoy throughout the seasons.

There is also a productive polytunnel and a chicken enclosure ideal for those looking to embrace country living.

  Springfield is a stylish blend of period features and modern additions. 

*(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

A range of outbuildings includes a recently completed detached stone-built double garage and workshop, a boiler room, log store, useful garden stores, a former brick-built piggery and a greenhouse (available by separate negotiation) plus there is off-road parking for up to three vehicles.

Subject to obtaining the necessary planning consents, these outbuildings could offer potential for conversion into ancillary accommodation or for other uses as required.
