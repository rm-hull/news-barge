Two people have been arrested after a Range Rover ploughed into a North East home.

The incident happened around 7pm on Howard Place in Tilery, Stockton on Sunday night (August 16).

Pictures shared on social media, seen by the Echo, show the Range Rover in the garden of a terraced home, with fencing knocked down.

The street was cordoned off with police at the scene. No injuries were reported.

An 18-year-old man and a 49-year-old woman have been arrested and remain in custody, Cleveland Police said.

Detective Constable Bethany Hunter added: "We understand that this incident may be concerning for the local community, however, officers will be patrolling in the local area to help provide reassurance to residents."

Enquiries are continuing and police are expected to remain in the area.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Stockton CID on 101, quoting reference number 164005.
