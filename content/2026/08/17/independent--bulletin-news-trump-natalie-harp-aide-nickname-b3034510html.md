# Trump’s ‘human printer’ gets new nickname

- Executive assistant Natalie Harp was one of three aides selected by President Donald Trump to join him during a secret plane switch in Turkey following the NATO summit.
- *New York Times* reporter Maggie Haberman described Harp as a “comfort blanket” for Trump, alongside long-serving aides Walt Nauta and Dan Scavino.
- Previously nicknamed Trump's “human printer,” Harp now manages the president's daily social media posts on Truth Social, often transcribing his dictated messages.
- Harp’s close relationship with the president has drawn public scrutiny, with her estranged brother calling it “unhealthy” and describing her as Trump’s “fan club.”
