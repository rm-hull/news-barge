President Donald Trump and two spokespersons lashed out Monday at Senator Jon Ossoff after he highlighted the 80-year-old Republican's relationship with a 35-year-old female aide.

Trump repeated his comparison of Ossoff to the iconic character created by the late comedian Paul Reubens when a reporter in the Oval Office asked about the Georgia Democrat's remarks, *The Hill* reported.

“You mean Pee-wee Herman?” Trump said.

White House communications director Steven Cheung got even more personal on social media, where he called Ossoff “Jon J***off” and “the biggest cuck loser in politics,” using a shortened form of the word cuckold, which is also a slang insult for a weak or submissive man.

“Jon should look deep into his soul and ask himself why he's a miserable person who hates this country. It's because he's a radical, extremist Dumocrat,” Cheung said, echoing his boss's favorite nickname for members of the opposing party.

Another White House spokesperson, Davis Ingle, also joined in, calling Ossoff a “cringeworthy, feminine theatre kid cosplaying as Barack Obama.”

“Nobody gives a s*** what this lightweight loser says,” Ingle added on social media.

Ossoff's Senate office referred *The Independent* to his re-election campaign, which didn't immediately respond to an inquiry.

Recent polls have put Ossoff ahead of Republican challenger Rep. Mike Collins by anywhere from four to 13 percentage points in a race that the Cook Political Report rates “Lean Democrat.”

Senate Republicans now hold a 53-47 majority over Democrats and two independents who caucus with them, and Ossoff's re-election is widely considered crucial to Democratic hopes of wresting control from the GOP.

During a Sunday campaign rally in Atlanta, Ossoff assailed Trump's war against Iran and said he “sleeps through his meetings, he golfs, and trades stocks.”

“He doesn’t want to do the job. He wants to build his ballroom and travel with Natalie on their apparently defenseless flying palace gifted by the Emir of Qatar,” Ossoff added.

The “Natalie” he mentioned is White House aide Natalie Harp, 35, who was nicknamed the “human printer” by colleagues on Trump's 2024 campaign staff because she carried a portable, battery-powered device to make printouts for Trump, who prefers reading information on paper, *The New York Times* reported shortly after Trump's victory.

Harp, a former far-right cable TV host, has also written devotional letters to Trump, including one that said, “You are all that matters to me,” according to the *NYT*.

Harp’s estranged brother, Preston Harp, told *The Daily Mail* in June that her relationship with Trump was “very unhealthy,” and said she likely saw him “as a kind of father figure because he embodies the doctrine of American exceptionalism.”

Last month, Harp was among three White House aides who flew with Trump and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth from Turkey to the U.K. on a military aircraft following a Nato summit, CNN reported last week.

Trump was snuck off Air Force One in a catering container in response to a reported Iranian assassination threat, as journalists and other administration officials, including Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, remained on the decoy flight.

Trump has claimed that “the plane that I flew on was at greater risk because that would be the plane I think that they would be more likely to go for,” even though he switched flights in secret.
