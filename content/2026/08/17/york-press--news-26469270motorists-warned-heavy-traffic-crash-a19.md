## Major road into York blocked following crash

Emergency services are in attendance at the site of a crash on the A19 into York *(Image: Newsquest)*

A major road into York has become blocked after a crash.

Emergency services have been called to the incident on the A19 close to Shipton by Beningbrough.

An update from AA Traffic said: "Road is blocked and slow traffic due to crash on A19 Main Street both ways from Newton Grange turn off to East Lane."

  


  The crash near Shipton by Beningbrough is causing extremely slow traffic on the A19 into York 

*(Image: AA Traffic)*

Motorists have been warned of extremely slow traffic.

A spokesperson from North Yorkshire Police said: "Please avoid the A19 by the Chapmans Lane, north of Shipton by Beningbrough, whilst we deal with a collision.

Please use alternative routes where possible while emergency services work at the scene."
