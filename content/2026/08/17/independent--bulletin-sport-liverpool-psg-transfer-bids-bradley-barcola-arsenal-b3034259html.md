# Bradley Barcola left out of PSG squad amid links with Liverpool and Arsenal

![Bradley Barcola did not make the PSG squad on Sunday (Adam Davy/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/11/17/ed72cb065f8a80ebaaf68966f7eb8c6bY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg2NTUxMjAy-2.85539640.jpg)

- Liverpool and Arsenal target Bradley Barcola was left out of PSG’s squad for their French Super Cup clash with Lens on Sunday.
- This follows on from Barcola being an unused substitute in PSG’s 2-1 Uefa Super Cup win over Aston Villa last Wednesday.
- PSG boss Luis Enrique refused to comment on Barcola’s future, stating he would “talk about the situation after the transfer window has passed”.
- Liverpool have been heavily linked with Barcola for a number of weeks, although PSG value the winger at £145 million.
- Arsenal have also shown interest in signing the left winger after missing out on Real Madrid’s Vinicius Jr.
