# Disney Cruise Line to launch 3 more ships as fleet expands

- Disney Cruise Line has announced plans to expand its fleet to 13 vessels by launching three new ships from 2029.
- The cruise line's fourth Wish class ship, *Disney Believe* , is scheduled to launch next year.
- *Disney Believe* will feature Moana as its Grand Hall icon, Tinker Bell on the bow, and Toy Story's Woody and Buzz Lightyear on the stern.
- The new class of ships will be sized between Disney's classic and modern vessels, enabling visits to new destinations.
- *Disney Wish* is set to depart from the UK for the first time next summer as part of the cruise line's ongoing expansion.
