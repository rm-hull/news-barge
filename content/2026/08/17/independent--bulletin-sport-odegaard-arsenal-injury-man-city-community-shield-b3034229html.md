# Arsenal captain Martin Odegaard opens up on battling back from injury woes

- Arsenal captain Martin Odegaard has revealed that the injury struggles he endured last season have helped him build resilience, using those difficult moments as motivation to return stronger for the new campaign.
- Odegaard scored his first goal for the club since December as Arsenal secured a dominant 3-0 victory over Manchester City in the Community Shield in Cardiff on Sunday, following their Premier League title triumph in May.
- The Norwegian captain praised £75 million summer signing Bruno Guimaraes, stating that an instant midfield connection has formed with the former Newcastle player, who he feels brings fight, composure, and exceptional quality to the team.
- The Community Shield defeat marked a difficult opening fixture for new Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca after replacing Pep Guardiola, with news also emerging that the club has accepted a £65.4 million offer from Barcelona for Rodri.
- Manchester City defender Ruben Dias acknowledged that his side's performance was not good enough, calling it part of an early-season transition that the squad must navigate through to improve.
