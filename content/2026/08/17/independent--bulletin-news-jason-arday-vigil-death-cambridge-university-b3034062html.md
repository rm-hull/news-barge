# Jason Arday fundraiser reaches £130k as Windrush campaigner speaks of ‘anger’

![Professor Jason Arday was found dead on Friday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/31/10/49/jason-arday.jpeg)

- Professor Jason Arday resigned from the University of Cambridge following plagiarism allegations and was found dead in Battersea on Friday.
- A GoFundMe fundraiser set up by Windrush campaigner Sir Patrick Vernon has raised £130,000 for the professor's family.
- Sir Patrick Vernon stated the outpouring of support shows the public still cares and feels angry about how the neurodiverse professor was hounded by the media.
- A vigil organised by Stand Up To Racism will be held on the steps of the National Gallery in Trafalgar Square on Monday evening.
- Campaigners stated the treatment of Prof Arday raises urgent questions regarding press regulation, structural racism, and the excessive scrutiny faced by minority professionals.
