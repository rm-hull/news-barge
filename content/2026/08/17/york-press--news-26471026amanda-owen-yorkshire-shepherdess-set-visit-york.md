The Yorkshire Shepherdess is set to visit York next month for the city's largest dog show.

Amanda Owen is set to judge the competition held at York Museum Gardens on Sunday (September 20) from 10am till 4pm.

The celebrity judge will choose the Handsomest Dog, Fanciest Female, Cutest Puppy and Best Trick.

  


Speaking about this, she said: "I’m so excited to be judging York Dog Show in York Museum Gardens.

"I always love to come and meet all the dogs and their owners and look forward to seeing them all on the day."

The event is organised in partnership with York Dog Park and promises something for everyone, from pet owners to families and dog enthusiasts.

A pop-up shopping village will offer treats, toys and accessories, while information stalls and talks from veterinary experts will provide visitors with insights and advice on caring for their pets.

Live demonstrations and activities will run throughout the day, including bale racing, training workshops and agility demonstrations.

Professional dog photographers will also be on hand to capture memories against the scenic backdrop of the gardens.

Jocelyn Palmer, senior events manager at York Museums Trust, said: "Dog owners of Yorkshire, this one is for you!

"We are so excited to be hosting our first ever dog show and bring together pet lovers, experts, businesses, trainers and photographers to celebrate our canine companions.

"With all the food, stalls and entertainment, it will be a full day affair for the whole family to enjoy and spend a day in the beautiful surroundings of the Museum Gardens with other likeminded people."

The Have-a-Go Dog Park offers a fun space for all, while a selection of food and drink stalls will keep everyone satisfied.

Tickets are priced at £5 for adults and £2 for children and are available to purchase online here.
