# Trump’s Iran deal set to end today as talks stall – what we know

- A 60-day deal signed between Iran and the US in June expires today with no breakthrough in permanent peace talks.
- Shipping traffic remains heavily disrupted despite the memorandum aimed at halting attacks and ending blockades around the Strait of Hormuz.
- Iranian Major General Amir Hatami threatened to break Donald Trump's legs after the American president suggested declaring the Strait a US territory.
- Major General Hatami declared that US forces have been driven out and are no longer permitted in the Gulf, the Sea of Oman, or the Strait of Hormuz.
- The Iranian military has offered a $30,000 reward for killing or capturing US soldiers, doubling the bounty if achieved by a woman.
