# Hayden Panettiere’s co-stars pay tribute after her death at 36: ‘She was a wonder’

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/njX7HIMt/poster.jpg?width=720)

- Actress Hayden Panettiere, best known for her starring roles in *Heroes*,* Nashville*and the* Scream* franchise, has died at age 36.
- Hollywood figures and former co-stars, including Viola Davis, Emma Roberts, Tim Allen and Kim Cattrall, paid tribute to Panettiere following the news of her passing.
- Davis hailed Panettiere as an “amazing talent” after her death, writing: "I wish the world had more time with you because I had a burning curiosity about who you were becoming.”
- Her *Heroes* co-star Sendhil Ramamurthy remembered Panettiere as a “kind, joyful, professional” actor, praising her generosity toward cast and crew.
