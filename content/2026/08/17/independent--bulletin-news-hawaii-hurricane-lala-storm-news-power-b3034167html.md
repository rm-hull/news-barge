# Hawaii hit by mass power cuts as Hurricane Lala sweeps in

- Hurricane Lala was downgraded to a tropical storm while over 180,000 households remained without power across Hawaii on Sunday afternoon.
- Approximately 183,123 customers were without power as of 3:30 p.m. HST, according to poweroutage.us.Outages in Oahu.
- At least one person died in a car-related accident, and state officials urged residents to stay indoors.
- A tropical storm warning remained in place for Kauai County, whilst warnings for Maui and Oahu were discontinued.
- Hawaii's airports have reopened, with weather projections indicating the storm should be clear of Hawaii's islands by 8am HST on Monday.
