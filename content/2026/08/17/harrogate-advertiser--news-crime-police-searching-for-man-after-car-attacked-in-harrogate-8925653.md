# Police searching for man after car attacked in Harrogate

## Police in Harrogate are asking for help to identify a man in a CCTV image.

Officers would like to speak to a man seen of CCTV after a second man was racially abused and had his car attacked as he drove along Ripon Road in Harrogate.

![Police in Harrogate have released an image of a man they would like to speak to following an attack on a vehicle](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjJkZTU0NmI5LTdiNTktNDI4YS1iZWRiLWMzNjBiZDYwNzQ3NDplMTY5N2Y4Mi02M2ZmLTQ2OWQtOGFkMC03NGM2YWY4MjczYmQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



A police spokesperson said: “We believe the man in the image will have information that could help our investigation.

“If you recognise him or can help identify him, please get in touch by: • Email to [email protected] • Online: Visit our website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’ - link below in the comments. • Live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm “If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.”

Quote reference 12260140563 when providing information.
