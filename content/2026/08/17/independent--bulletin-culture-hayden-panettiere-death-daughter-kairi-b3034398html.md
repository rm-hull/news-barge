# Hayden Panettiere: Police give update after actor found dead aged 36

- *Heroes* and*Nashville* actress Hayden Panettiere has died at the age of 36 at her home in Greenville, South Carolina.
- Greenville Police Department reported no preliminary signs of foul play or suspicious circumstances after responding to an emergency call from an acquaintance.
- Her father, Skip Panettiere, paid tribute to her as an incredible light who brought immeasurable joy, asking for privacy as the family processes the loss.
- Panettiere rose to fame as a child star before earning critical acclaim for roles in *Remember The Titans, A Bug's Life, Heroes, Nashville*, and the* Scream* franchise.
- The actress had recently published a memoir opening up about her struggles with addiction, mental health, and the death of her younger brother Jansen.
