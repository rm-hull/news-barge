### Johnny Depp in talks to return for the sixth Pirates Of The Caribbean film

![Johnny Depp in talks to return for the sixth Pirates Of The Caribbean film](<[https://static.independent.co.uk/2024/09/27/17/gettyimages-2174249882-594x594.jpg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp](https://static.independent.co.uk/2024/09/27/17/gettyimages-2174249882-594x594.jpg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp) 83w>)

# Johnny Depp could return as Jack Sparrow in next Pirates of the Caribbean film

- Producer Jerry Bruckheimer has confirmed that Johnny Depp is in talks to return for the sixth film in the *Pirates Of The Caribbean* franchise.
- Speaking at D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event, Bruckheimer told *Deadline* : “We’re talking to Johnny, we’re working on a screenplay, and hopefully we can get this done.”
- During his 2022 defamation trial against his former wife and The Rum Diary co-star Amber Heard, he claimed he would not return to the franchise.
- Co-star Orlando Bloom previously told ITV’s *This Morning* that he was keen to get the cast back together for a new film.
- The franchise has released five feature films to date, the last being *Dead Men Tell No Tales* in 2017.
