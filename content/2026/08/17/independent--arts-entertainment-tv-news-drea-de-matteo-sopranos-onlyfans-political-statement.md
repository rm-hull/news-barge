Drea de Matteo has revealed that her OnlyFans account earned her enough money to clear a $75,000 lien on her home within 75 minutes.

*The Sopranos* star, 54, said she turned to the platform in August 2023 after she lost acting work and mounting housing costs left her facing foreclosure. 

De Matteo had opposed Covid-19 vaccine mandates and lost her agent during the pandemic, while a mortgage forbearance she had taken out later left her receiving foreclosure notices.

She said she had originally planned to “use the OnlyFans platform to start a podcast because I was afraid that I’d be censored too much to talk about stuff just out there in the world”.

“So we were going to do the podcast there and so I set up the page and we were in a really bad spot. We knew that the foreclosure was around the corner,” she said.

“My house flooded, so I couldn’t sell the house quickly. It flooded right as I was getting news of the last foreclosure,” she told Jamie Kennedy on his *Hate to Break It to Ya* podcast.

She added then that her children told her to “show [her] feet on OnlyFans” before she eventually uploaded a photo of herself in a bikini on Instagram, asking fans to subscribe to her OnlyFans account for $15 a month.

“Within 75 minutes the lien was paid,” she said, adding that it had been a $75,000 lien.

“In some ways, the OnlyFans page was almost like a political statement on my part in a way. It was like, ‘F*** you. I don’t have to work.”

![Drea de Matteo has revealed that a bikini photograph posted to OnlyFans earned her enough money to clear a $75,000 lien on her home within 75 minutes](https://static.the-independent.com/2024/06/27/00/newFile.jpg)

Earlier in the episode, de Matteo addressed the misconception that her Emmy-winning role as Adriana La Cerva on the hit mafia drama had left her financially set for life.

“People think I’m made of f***ing money,” she said. “Everyone sees me coming, they’re like, ‘Here comes that rich b***h from* The Sopranos*.’ And it’s like, no, I’m not at all. In fact, you know, I live a pretty f***ing normal life. I had to do that [OnlyFans] page to get a lien off my house after foreclosure.”

She continued: “The OnlyFans thing, I always say, it’s Adriana’s fan page because people love her. I mean, I’ve done a lot of other TV shows, but that’s the TV show people really love. So, you want to see more Adriana, go there, and that’s where I am.”

In 2024, de Matteo said she had become an “outcast” in Hollywood due to her outspoken support for Donald Trump.

“Yes, there are a lot of quiet Trump supporters. I wonder how much these actors got paid to endorse Biden at this point. I am curious,” she told Fox News.

“I don’t really maneuver inside that industry. I never have. First of all, Sopranos, we were the outcast, even though we were critically acclaimed, we still were outcasts. So I’m still an outcast, here I am. They’re going to take me out into the woods and shoot me for not endorsing Biden,” she added.

After leaving *The Sopranos* in season five, de Matteo went on to play Joey Tribbiani’s sister Gina in the*Friends* spin-off Joey, and featured in series such as*Desperate Housewives* and*Sons of Anarchy*.
