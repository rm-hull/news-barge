# Elizabeth Olsen ‘welcomes first child with Robbie Arnett’

- Actor Elizabeth Olsen has reportedly welcomed her first child with her musician husband Robbie Arnett.
- The couple were photographed out in Los Angeles with the infant on Saturday 15 August, as reported by *Page Six* .
- Olsen and Arnett first met while on holiday in Mexico in 2017 and later married in a private ceremony.
- The Marvel star previously admitted in an interview with *The Times* that she was reluctant to allow any of her children to act professionally before they turn 18.
- “Being younger today is already so complicated. To amplify it by being a child actor and then amplify it by social media, I just think it’s a lot for a kid’s development... I think my sisters are on a totally different path than your average child actor,” she said.
