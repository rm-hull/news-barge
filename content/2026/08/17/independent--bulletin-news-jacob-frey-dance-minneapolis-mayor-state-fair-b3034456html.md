# Minneapolis mayor shows off dance moves at Black State Fair, leaving the internet divided

- Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey attended the annual Black Entrepreneur State Fair on Saturday 15 August.
- A video of Frey showing off his dance moves to Chingy's hit song “Right Thurr” went viral on social media.
- The mayor was taught how to perform the popular chicken head hip-hop dance move during the event.
- Online reactions were divided, with some critics labelling his performance as embarrassing and questioning his priorities.
- Other social media users defended and praised Frey, commending his confidence and describing him as cool.
