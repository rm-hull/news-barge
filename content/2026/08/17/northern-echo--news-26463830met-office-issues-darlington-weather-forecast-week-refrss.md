The Met Office has forecast rain for Darlington and County Durham this week.

  **Monday** will start off cloudy, with temperatures around 15C.

The chance of rain will increase to 20 per cent by 9am, with temperatures rising to 17C.

Light rain is expected from 3pm, with a 50 per cent chance of precipitation.

The rain will continue into the evening, with temperatures peaking at 21C.

  **Tuesday** will start off wet, with light rain expected throughout the early morning.

The chance of rain will decrease to 30 per cent by 9am, with temperatures around 19C.

The afternoon will see sunny intervals, with temperatures peaking at 23C by 3pm.

The evening will be partly cloudy, with temperatures dropping to 16C by midnight.

  **Wednesday** will start off partly cloudy, with temperatures around 14C.

Sunny intervals are expected throughout the day, with temperatures peaking at 20C by 1pm.

The evening will be partly cloudy, with temperatures dropping to 15C by 10pm.

  **Thursday** will start off partly cloudy, with temperatures around 13C.

Sunny intervals are expected throughout the day, with temperatures peaking at 19C by 1pm.

The evening will be partly cloudy, with temperatures dropping to 14C by 10pm.
