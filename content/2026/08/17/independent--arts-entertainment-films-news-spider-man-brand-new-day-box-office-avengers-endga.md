*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* has broken the $2 billion dollar mark at the global box office, becoming just the eighth film to reach that milestone.

The Marvel blockbuster, which stars Tom Holland as the web-slinging superhero, achieved the feat after being released three weekends ago. In terms of time taken to reach $2 billion, that places the film behind only 2019’s *Avengers: Endgame*, which took just 11 days to pull off a similar box office haul.

As *Variety* reported,*Spider-Man: Brand New Day’* s receipts are split between the $785 million the film grossed in North America and a further $1.23 billion overseas. It’s official total now stands at $2.022 billion.

The seven other films that have made over $2 billion at the box office are: 2009’s *Avatar*($2.9 billion), 2019’s* Avengers: Endgame*($2.7 billion), 2022’s* Avatar: The Way of Water*($2.3 billion), 1994’s* Titanic*($2.2 billion), 2015’s* Star Wars: The Force Awakens*($2.07 billion) and 2018’s* Avengers: Infinity War* ($2.03 billion).

The previous installment in the Spider-Man franchise, 2021’s *Spider-Man: No Way Home*, narrowly missed out on the landmark by finishing with a total box office of $1.9 billion.

![Tom Holland stars in ‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day’, just the eighth film in history to make more than $2 billion at the global box office](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/07/15/08/6597031-library-copy.jpeg)

As well as being a hit at the box office, *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* has also won over critics.

In a four-star review for *The Independent*, Clarisse Loughrey called the film “the best Spider-Man movie in more than 20 years.”

She added: “*Brand New Day* offers a maturation not only of its hero, but of the franchise’s entire vision, too. While returning screenwriters Chris McKenna and Erik Sommers provide a sense of continuity in the character, its director Destin Daniel Cretton (of*Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings* fame) and his frequent cinematographer Brett Pawlak have simultaneously allowed the series to feel completely revitalised.”

The film has also been noted for including an unusual post-credits scene.

Like many other films in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, the new Tom Holland flick features an extra scene once the credits have rolled but in a rare move, it doesn’t feature anyone at all.

***Warning – spoilers ahead for***Spider-Man: Brand New Day**

The post-credits scene shows the screen for a device used for tracking Spider-Man as it receives a new notification as to the superhero’s whereabouts. After scanning for the masked vigilante, it drops a pin on his location in outer space – teasing that we’ll next see Parker head away from Earth.
