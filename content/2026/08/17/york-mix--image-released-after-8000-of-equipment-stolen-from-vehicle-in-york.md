Police investigating a theft in York want to trace this person.

Items worth £8,000 were stolen from a vehicle on **Burton Stone Lane**.

It happened overnight between Tuesday, 7 July and Wednesday, 8 July.

#### Burton Stone Lane, York

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “As part of our ongoing enquiries, we are now in a position to release a CCTV image of a person we would like to speak to in connection with the incident.

“The footage has been provided by a third party and, as such, we have no control over how it was recorded or the quality of the images captured.

“The suspect has their face covered, and this is the best image we have been able to obtain. We appreciate that the image quality is distorted.

“However, we are hoping that someone may recognise the individual from their clothing, build, or overall appearance.

“The stolen equipment is valued at over £8,000, and any information, no matter how small it may seem, could help identify those responsible and bring them to justice.”

If you recognise the person in the image, or have any other information that can help police, you can contact them below.
