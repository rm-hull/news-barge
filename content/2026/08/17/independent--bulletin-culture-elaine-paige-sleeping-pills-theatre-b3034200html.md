# Elaine Paige opens up about her former sleeping pill addiction

- West End star Dame Elaine Paige has revealed she was addicted to sleeping pills for many years during her stage career.
- The 78-year-old explained that she managed to overcome her addiction during the pandemic and vows never to take them again.
- Paige originated iconic musical theatre roles, including Eva Perón in Evita and Grizabella in *Cats* .
- She was recently presented with a Special Award at the Oliviers, receiving high praise in a message from Queen Camilla.
- Upon accepting her award, Paige reflected on her early career struggles and encouraged the next generation of performers to persevere.
