A crash is causing problems near York this morning (Monday).

It’s on the **A19** north of Shipton by Beningbrough, close to the crossroads with Chapman’s Lane and Ambler’s Lane.

Emergency services are on the scene.

#### Crash location

A YorkMix Radio listener says the road is closed between Chapman’s Lane and East Lane Road. Police are turning traffic around with a fire engine and police vehicles blocking the road.

The AA reports: “Road blocked and slow traffic due to crash on A19 Main Street both ways from Newton Grange turn-off to East Lane.”

Traffic is slow in both directions. More as we get it.
