# Russian politician who called for ceasefire in Ukraine handed lengthy prison sentence

- Lev Shlosberg, deputy chairman of Russia’s liberal Yabloko party, has been sentenced to 11 years and one month in a penal colony after being convicted of discrediting the Russian armed forces and disseminating false information regarding the conflict in Ukraine.
- Addressing the court in Pskov during his final statement, Shlosberg asserted his innocence, denounced the trial as politically motivated, and delivered a scathing critique of the ongoing war while demanding an immediate ceasefire.
- The sentencing follows a recent ruling by Russia’s Supreme Court to exclude Yabloko—the country's sole registered political entity advocating for an end to the war—from contesting upcoming parliamentary elections.
- First detained in June last year after characterising the conflict as a game of bloody chess, Shlosberg faced cumulative charges over a Telegram repost under strict censorship legislation introduced in February 2022.
- Shlosberg's conviction reflects an intensifying state crackdown on anti-war figures, coming shortly after fellow Yabloko deputy leader Maxim Kruglov was sentenced to seven years in prison on similar charges.
