### Mrs Brown’s Boys Christmas specials cancelled as Brendan O’Carroll faces surgery for ‘unexpected medical issue’

![Mrs Brown’s Boys Christmas specials cancelled as Brendan O’Carroll faces surgery for ‘unexpected medical issue’](<[https://static.independent.co.uk/2022/12/24/16/newFile.jpg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp](https://static.independent.co.uk/2022/12/24/16/newFile.jpg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp) 83w>)

# Why filming for Mrs Brown’s Boys Christmas and New Year’s specials has been cancelled

- Filming for this year’s *Mrs Brown’s Boys* Christmas and New Year’s specials has been cancelled as creator Brendan O’Carroll requires “immediate surgery” for two jaw fractures.
- Writing on social media, the 70-year-old comedian said: “I am talking with the BBC about getting *Mrs. Brown’s Boys* back on the screen in 2027.”
- He added: “To say I am heartbroken is an absolute understatement. I would like to sincerely apologise to all our fans who were looking forward to seeing the shows and for any disappointment caused.”
- The *Mrs Brown’s Boys D’Panto* arena shows planned for later this year have also been postponed, though O’Carroll said he is hoping to reschedule them for 2027.
- *Mrs Brown’s Boys* sees O’Carroll play the bawdy Irish “mammy”, with some of his close friends and family in supporting roles.
