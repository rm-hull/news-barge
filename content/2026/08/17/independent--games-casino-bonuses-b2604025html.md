## **Types of casino bonus**

There are several types of casino bonus that users are likely to come across, either as part of casino welcome bonuses or as existing promotions. Common casino bonuses include:

**Welcome / Sign-up bonus:** This bonus is for new players on their first deposit. It is usually a percentage match (e.g. 100 per cent up to £100), meaning that the casino will match the amount of your first deposit as the bonus. 

**No deposit bonus:** A no deposit casino bonus can be claimed without making a deposit, and usually consists of free spins or a small credit. Winnings are subject to wagering requirements. No deposit free spins are rarely offered by online casinos, especially for casino sign up offers.

**Free spins:** These are another common and popular reward to use on online slots, and they often come as part of a welcome package or as a standalone promotion. Wins from free spins are usually paid as bonus credit and must be wagered before withdrawal.

**Reload bonus:** These are offered to existing players on subsequent deposits, usually weekly or monthly. Players typically need to opt in.

**Cashback bonus:** This bonus returns a percentage of net losses over a set period. Cashback can be paid as real cash or bonus credit, which of course makes a significant difference to value.

**Loyalty / VIP programme:** Points can be earned for regular wagers, and these are redeemable for bonus credit or other rewards. These vary significantly between operators.

## **How wagering requirements work**

Most casino promotions will come with wagering requirements. This requirement dictates the number of times bonus funds must be played through before they can be withdrawn.

For example, a £10 bonus at 10x wagering means that £100 in total bets are required before withdrawal is possible.

Since January 2026 the UKGC caps wagering at 10x for all UK-licensed operators. This is a significant improvement from the 30x–50x requirements that were previously common.

In addition, mixed-product bonuses (e.g. casino and sports bonuses in one offer) are no longer permitted under the new rules.
