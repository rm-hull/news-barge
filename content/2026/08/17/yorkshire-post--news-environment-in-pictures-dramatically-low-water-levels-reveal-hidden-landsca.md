Striking photographs have revealed the extent of the low water level at Baitings Reservoir in West Yorkshire.

The images show large expanses of the reservoir bed exposed, with the water retreating significantly from its usual shoreline.

Among the features revealed is an old stone bridge, while photographs taken from above show the remaining water winding through the otherwise exposed landscape.

People can also be seen walking across areas which would ordinarily be submerged, while discarded debris has become visible as the water has receded.

The scenes provide a stark contrast with a photograph of Baitings Reservoir at a much higher level, when water covers much of the landscape currently exposed.
