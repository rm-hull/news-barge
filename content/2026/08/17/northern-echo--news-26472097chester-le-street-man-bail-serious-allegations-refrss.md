A County Durham man arrested over allegations of serious offences including rape, child neglect and firearm offences has been released on bail.

The 30-year-old, from Chester-le-Street, was arrested on suspicion of rape, child neglect, possession of a firearm without a licence and possessing an offensive weapon in a public place.

He was arrested in Chester-le-Street on July 26 and had remained in police custody while enquiries were carried out.

Durham Police has now confirmed he has been released on bail while further enquiries continue.

"The 30-year-old, of Chester-le-Street, remains in custody today while enquiries are ongoing."

The force has confirmed in an update that the man has since been released on bail while further enquiries are carried out.
