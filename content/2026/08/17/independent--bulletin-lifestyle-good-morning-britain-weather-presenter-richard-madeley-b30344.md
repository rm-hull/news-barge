# Good Morning Britain weather presenter called out by Richard Madeley for running late

- Good Morning Britain presenter Richard Madeley called out meteorologist Laura Tobin when she missed the start of her segment on Monday.
- Madeley joked that they had a vanishing weather girl as co-host Ranvir Singh attempted to move to another item.
- Tobin quickly rushed back onto the set, explaining that she had just run to the bathroom.
- Asked by Madeley if she had lost her sense of timing, Tobin sheepishly noted that everyone now knew where she had been before presenting her weather report.
