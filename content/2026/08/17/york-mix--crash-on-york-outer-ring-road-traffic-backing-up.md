There’s been a second crash on a York main road this morning (Monday).

This one is on the **A64 York Outer Ring Road**.

It was on the **westbound carriageway** between**Grimston Bar** and the**Fulford Interchange**.

#### Crash location

Emergency services went to the scene and traffic on the westbound carriageway was very slow.

The crash has now been cleared.
