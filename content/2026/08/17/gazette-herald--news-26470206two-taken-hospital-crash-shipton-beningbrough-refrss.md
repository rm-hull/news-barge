TWO people have been rushed to hospital after a crash on a major road leading to York.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service (YAS) was called to the incident at the junction of Ambler's Lane and Chapman's Lane in Shipton by Beningbrough at 6.54am on Monday (August 17).

The road was closed as emergency services worked to make the scene safe.

  


A YAS spokesperson said: "Two ambulances and a team leader were dispatched to the scene and two patients were conveyed to hospital."

The road has since reopened.
