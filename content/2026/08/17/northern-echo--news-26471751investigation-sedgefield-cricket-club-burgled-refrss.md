An investigation has been launched after a County Durham cricket club was burgled overnight after multiple intruders broke into its building.

Police were called just after 11.35pm on Saturday, August 15, following reports that people had forced their way into Sedgefield Cricket Club on Station Road.

Officers attended on an immediate response, but the suspects had fled before they arrived.

Nothing is believed to have been stolen during the break-in. However, damage was caused to the building.
