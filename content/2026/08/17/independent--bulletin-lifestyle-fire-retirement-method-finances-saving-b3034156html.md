# How the FIRE method could help you retire early

![The FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) strategy is gaining popularity](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/14/8/51/iStock-2252664062.jpg)

- The FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) strategy is gaining popularity among younger generations facing rising retirement ages and cost-of-living challenges.
- To achieve FIRE, individuals typically multiply their expected annual retirement expenses by 25 to build a nest egg for a 4 per cent annual withdrawal rate.
- Personal finance expert Rajan Lakhani outlines three main approaches: Lean FIRE for minimalist living, Fat FIRE for higher spending, and Barista FIRE for semi-retirement.
- Adopters must make significant lifestyle sacrifices, saving 50 to 70 per cent of disposable income and investing through tax-efficient vehicles like ISAs.
- While the method offers early financial independence, experts caution against severe short-term sacrifices due to market fluctuations and health uncertainties.
