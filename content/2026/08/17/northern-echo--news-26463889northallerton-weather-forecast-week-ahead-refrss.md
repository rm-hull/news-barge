The Met Office has forecast a mix of sunshine and rain for Northallerton this week.

  **Monday** will start off cloudy, with temperatures around 15C.

Sunny intervals are expected from 8am, with temperatures rising to 22C by 1pm.

However, the afternoon will see a change in weather, with light rain expected from 6pm.

The chance of rain will increase to 60 per cent by 7pm, with temperatures dropping to 18C by midnight.

  **Tuesday** will start off wet, with light rain expected from midnight until 3am.

The chance of rain will decrease to 50 per cent by 4am, with the sky remaining cloudy.

Sunny intervals are expected from midday, with temperatures peaking at 23C by 3pm.

The evening will be partly cloudy, with temperatures dropping to 16C by midnight.

  **Wednesday** will start off partly cloudy, with temperatures around 14C.

Sunny intervals are expected from 10am, with temperatures rising to 20C by 1pm.

The sky will remain partly cloudy into the evening, with temperatures dropping to 15C by midnight.

  **Thursday** will start off partly cloudy, with temperatures around 12C.

Sunny intervals are expected from 7am, with temperatures rising to 19C by 4pm.

The sky will become partly cloudy again by 7pm, with temperatures dropping to 13C by midnight.

The weather is expected to remain changeable throughout the week, with a mix of sunshine and rain.
