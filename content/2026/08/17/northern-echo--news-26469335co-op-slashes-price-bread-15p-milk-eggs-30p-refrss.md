Co-op shoppers can grab three everyday essentials for just 75p in a huge one-day grocery deal.

The supermarket is slashing the price of bread, milk and eggs by up to 85% for customers ordering groceries through Uber Eats.

The offer runs for 24 hours only on Monday, August 17, with shoppers able to grab:

- 
    **Co-op White Toastie Loaf 800g - 15p** , down from 90p
- 
    **Co-op British Medium Free-Range Eggs 6s - 30p** , down from £2.10
- 
    **Co-op Fresh Semi-Skimmed Milk 4 Pints - 30p** , down from £2

  Co-op is slashng the price of eggs to 30p

That means shoppers can pick up all three staples for just 75p, although delivery and service fees are not included.

The deal is available through participating Co-op stores on Uber Eats, while stocks last.

Kate McCrae, Co-op Retail Director, said: “For 24 hours only, we're making it even easier for shoppers to pick up everyday essentials at an incredibly low price, with the added convenience of having them delivered straight to their door through Uber Eats.

"A loaf of bread, four pints of milk and a box of eggs for just 75p is an offer that speaks for itself.”

“We’re experts in rapid delivery here at Co-op and we’re proud to combine this expertise with the very best own-brand products and great-value offers, to keep customers stocked-up round the clock, whatever their needs.”
