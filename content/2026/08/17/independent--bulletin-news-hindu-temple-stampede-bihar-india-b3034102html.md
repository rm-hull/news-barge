# India temple stampede kills seven women after electricity pole collapses

- At least seven women have died in a crowd crush at the Ashok Dham Temple in Bihar, eastern India.
- Thousands of Hindu devotees had gathered at the temple on Monday to offer prayers during a holy month for Lord Shiva.
- The tragedy occurred when an electric pole collapsed, triggering rumours of electrocution and causing panic among the crowd.
- A sudden influx of worshippers arriving by train overwhelmed security and broke through barricades outside the entrance.
- Authorities and local police have launched an investigation into the exact cause of the incident and crowd control failures.
