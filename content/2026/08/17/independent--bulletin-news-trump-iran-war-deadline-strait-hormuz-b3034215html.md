# Trump issues new warning to Iran as 60-day ceasefire agreement expires

- President Donald Trump declared on Truth Social that Iran must not be allowed a nuclear weapon "in any way, shape or form".
- The statement comes as a 60-day ceasefire agreement signed between Iran and the US in June reached its expiration date with no permanent peace deal achieved.
- Tehran rejected the deadline, asserting it does not formulate policy under pressure or time limits and accusing the US of widespread violations.
- The original agreement sought to end US and Israeli strikes on Iran while addressing the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz.
- Shipping traffic remains severely restricted as both Iran and the US maintain their respective blockades.
