# Man talked about murdering ex for months before carrying out crime, say US police

![Matthew Almontes](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/16/21/19/Matthew-Almontes.jpeg)

- Connecticut police have arrested 41-year-old Matthew Almontes for the murder of his ex-girlfriend Tisha Marinoccio, who was stabbed to death in her New Haven apartment.
- Emergency services responded to a distress call from Marinoccio, who identified Almontes as her attacker before dying from her injuries in hospital.
- An apartment complex worker told investigators that Almontes had grown obsessed with Marinoccio and had spoken about stabbing her for six months prior to the attack.
- Security camera footage showed Almontes exiting the victim's apartment and discarding an object in a dumpster, where police later recovered the knife.
- Almontes faces charges of murder and tampering with evidence, remaining in custody on a $1.5 million bond ahead of a court appearance on 26 August.
