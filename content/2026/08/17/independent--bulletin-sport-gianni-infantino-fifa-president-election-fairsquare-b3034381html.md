# Gianni Infantino should be ineligible for Fifa re-election, claims human rights group

- Human rights group FairSquare has lodged a formal complaint arguing that Fifa president Gianni Infantino should be declared ineligible for another term, asserting that he has reached the maximum limit of three terms allowed under governing statutes.
- Although Infantino was first elected in 2016 following Sepp Blatter's resignation, he successfully argued in 2022 that his initial period in office merely completed an interrupted mandate and should not count towards his term limit.
- FairSquare director Nicholas McGeehan and Miguel Maduro, former head of Fifa's independent Governance and Review Committee, claimed that discounting Infantino's first term breaches Fifa rules and undermines fundamental governance reforms designed to restrict presidential power.
- The challenge arises ahead of the FIFA Congress on 18 March in Morocco, where Infantino is expected to seek re-election for the 2027-31 cycle despite opposition from the Scottish Football Association and requests for a conduct review from UEFA, Concacaf, and the Asian Football Confederation.
- Despite mounting scrutiny regarding a proposed $20-billion investment subsidiary linked to Fifa competitions, Infantino retains the support of both the South American and African football confederations.
