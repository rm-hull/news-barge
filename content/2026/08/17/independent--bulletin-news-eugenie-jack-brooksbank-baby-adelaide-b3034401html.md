# Princess Eugenie reveals baby daughter’s name and the reason behind it

- Princess Eugenie and her husband Jack Brooksbank have announced the name of their newborn daughter, Adelaide Elizabeth Annina Brooksbank.
- The baby girl was born on 3 August at a hospital in Lisbon, Portugal, and is the couple's third child.
- Eugenie shared that Adelaide is named after people they love and admire, including her late great-grandmother Queen Elizabeth II.
- Adelaide is 15th in line to the British throne, moving her great-uncle Prince Edward down to 16th place.
- She is the 15th great-grandchild of Queen Elizabeth II and the fifth grandchild of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor and Sarah Ferguson.
