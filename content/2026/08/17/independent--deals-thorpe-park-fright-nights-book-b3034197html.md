With summer temperatures still soaring, Halloween might be the last thing on your mind. But, if spooky season is your favourite time of the year, you might want to start thinking ahead, as tickets to Thorpe Park’s Fright Nights (one of the biggest events of the season) are cheaper when you book in advance.

Returning for 2026, this year’s event marks a pretty major milestone: 25 years of scary thrills. To celebrate, the Surrey theme park has promised a brand-new scare maze, alongside reimagined shows and scare zones – although, it’s keeping further details under wraps for now. There’s also the usual lineup of rides to tackle after dark, including Hyperia, the UK’s tallest rollercoaster, and Saw The Ride, the horror-movie-themed rollercoaster.

Tickets are already on sale, and booking online can save you up to £27, compared with the walk-up price. So if you’re planning a Halloween trip, it’s worth booking now, as popular dates are expected to sell out.

There are a few different ticket options available, depending on whether you want to tackle the scare mazes, too. Keep reading for everything you need to know.

## How much are Thorpe Park Fright Nights tickets?

The cheapest way to visit the theme park for Fright Nights is to book a standard one-day ticket online, with prices starting from £39. That’s £27 less than the £66 on-the-gate price, although it’s worth noting that scare maze entry isn’t included.

The ticket gives you access to the park from 10am to 9pm, including more than 25 rides and attractions, as well as the Fright Nights scare zones and shows.

If you want more than just the rides to get your adrenaline going, tickets that include entry to the scare mazes start from £71 online, compared with the £98 walk-up price.

For a full Halloween getaway, Thorpe Park is offering Fright Nights short breaks from £109pp, based on four people sharing. Packages include two-day park entry, an overnight stay at the Thorpe Shark Cabins or a nearby Surrey hotel, and buffet breakfast.
