# Seven dead as Russian Wildberries hub hit by huge Ukraine drone attack

- Ukraine has launched a major overnight air attack against Russia using more than 800 drones.
- A vast warehouse belonging to online retailer Wildberries in the Moscow region was completely destroyed in the strikes.
- Russian officials claimed 822 drones were destroyed overnight, while an 83-year-old man was killed in the Moscow region. Meanwhile, A Ukrainian missile strike killed six people and wounded another four, including a 14-year-old child, in Russia's border Belgorod region, acting governor Alexander Shuvayev said on his Telegram channel. In the early hours of Monday morning.
- At least three people were injured in Kyiv following Russian missile and drone strikes early on Sunday morning.
- President Volodymyr Zelensky revealed Ukraine also recently used flamingo missiles to strike a Russian rocket and space centre in Samara.
