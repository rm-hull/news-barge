A PASSENGER who fell from the back of a motorbike in North Yorkshire has been taken to York Hospital.

The incident in Braygate Street, Malton happened at 4.34pm on Saturday (August 15).

  


A road and air ambulance were sent to the scene, Yorkshire Ambulance Service.

A spokesperson said: “We received an emergency call at 4.34pm on August 15 to report an incident on Braygate Street where someone had fallen from the back of a motorcycle.

“An ambulance and an air ambulance were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed by road to York Hospital.”
