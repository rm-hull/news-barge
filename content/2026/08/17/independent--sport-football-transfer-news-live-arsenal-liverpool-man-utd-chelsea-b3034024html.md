## Man City set to sign Ayyoub Bouaddi after selling Rodri to Barcelona

Manchester City are now set to agree a fee for **Ayyoub Bouaddi** after agreeing to sell Rodri to Barcelona. 

Bouaddi, the 18-year-old wonderkid who impressed at the World Cup for Morocco, had been linked with a move next year but City have stepped up their advances.

His club, Lille, confirmed talks were ongoing on Friday but it would take at least £85m to sign the teenager.

City have already undergone major changes in midfield this summer, spending £116m on Elliot Anderson, while agreeing a deal to sell Tijjani Reijnders to Al Qadisah.

Enzo Maresca was asked about Bouaddi following Manchester City’s 3-0 defeat to Arsenal in the Community Shield on Sunday and said that when it comes to new signings the “sooner the better”.

"He's still a Lille player,” Maresca said. “But any new signing, any new player, for sure as soon as they arrive it's better. Because we're going to start the Premier League in one week.

“If they start to work with us as soon as possible, it's better if they can also understand the way we need to play."

## Tottenham's Guglielmo Vicario close to Juventus switch after Emi Martinez talks break down

Juventus have stepped up their interest in **Guglielmo Vicario** and are eager to finalise a deal quickly for the Tottenham goalkeeper.

Vicario has been in limbo all summer after he asked Spurs boss Roberto De Zerbi to leave and return to his homeland following three years in north London.

Juventus emerged as Vicario’s favoured option, but they first targeted a move for **Emi Martinez** and then Parma youngster**Zion Suzki**. However, after major developments across the weekend, the Serie A club have opened talks with Tottenham over the loan signing of Vicario.

De Zerbi gave the green light for Vicario to leave after Antonin Kinsky stepped up to guide Tottenham away from relegation with a string of fine displays in the final seven matches of last season.

## ‘An exciting moment’: Scotland confirm Sebastien Pocognoli as Steve Clarke replacement

Sebastien Pocognoli has been confirmed as the new Scotland head coach.

The former Union Saint-Gilloise and Monaco head coach has signed an initial two-year contract to succeed Steve Clarke.

Pocognoli, a former Belgium international, led Union to the 2024-25 Belgian title in his first season as a head coach.

The 39-year-old described his appointment as an “honour” and added on the Scottish Football Association website: “When I first heard of the interest, I was immediately excited about the opportunity.

“Scotland’s supporters are famous for their passion – most recently at the World Cup in the USA – and I have experienced it myself as a player for Belgium when we played in 2013.

“Along with my coaching staff, I will do everything I can to give them a team to be proud of and build on the success that has been achieved already by my predecessor.”

## Man City identify Rodri replacement after accepting £65m offer from Barcelona

Manchester City have accepted a bid of £65.4m from Barcelona for Rodri.

Spain’s World Cup-winning captain is set to leave the Etihad Stadium to join the LaLiga champions and leave City with the question of how to replace him.

Barcelona had two previous offers for Rodri refused with City deeming the first, of just £38.4m, not serious but their third bid matched City’s valuation.

Rodri, who had been reluctant to sign a new deal, has entered the last year of his contract and City risked losing him on a free transfer if he was not sold this summer.

Now they will have two weeks to find a replacement. They are interested in Ayyoub Bouaddi, with Lille manager Davide Ancelotti saying there have been talks about the Moroccan midfielder, and Enzo Fernandez.
