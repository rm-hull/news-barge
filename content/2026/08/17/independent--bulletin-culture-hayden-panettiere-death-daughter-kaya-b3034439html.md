# Hayden Panettiere opens up on relationship with daughter Kaya in one of her final interviews

- Actor Hayden Panettiere has died at the age of 36, as announced by her father Skip.
- Her cause of death has not yet been confirmed and is currently under investigation, according to her publicist.
- In one of her final interviews three months before her death, the star discussed her bond with her daughter, Kaya.
- Speaking on the Tamron Hall Show, the Heroes and Nashville star opened up about her postpartum depression and relinquishing custody of her child in 2018.
