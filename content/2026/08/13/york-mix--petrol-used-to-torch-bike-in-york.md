Firefighters were called to another suspected arson last night (Wednesday).

They went to the incident off **Water Lane** at**9.31pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from Acomb responded to reports of a fire in the open.

#### Water Lane, York

“This was found to be a fire consisting of a push bike and petrol can and was out on crews arrival.

“The cause is believed to be deliberate.”

There have been a series of deliberate fires in the Clifton Backies nature reserve next to Water Lane in recent weeks.

Firefighters tackled two on Sunday alone.
