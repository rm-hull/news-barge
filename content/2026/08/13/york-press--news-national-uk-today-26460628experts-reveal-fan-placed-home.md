## Experts reveal best place to put a fan and most people are getting it wrong

Here is exactly where you need to put a fan to cool your house. *(Image: Getty)*

Parts of the UK have been hitting temperatures of over 30 degrees throughout the summer, making it difficult to sleep and get through the day.

Many people have invested in fans to make it easier to cool down.

If you are struggling with the heat even with a fan running all day, it might mean you have placed it in the wrong place.

Experts have shared where you need to put your fan to make sure you are using it in the best way.

  If you put your fan in the wrong place, it will not work well. 

*(Image: Getty)*

## The best place to put your fan to cool your home

While most people will put a fan in the corner of a room to keep it out of the way, this prevents it from working as well.

Experts at Next Day Fans have said there are specific places you should put a fan to make it work better for you.

They said: "To allow fans to utilise the existing airflow of the room, they work best when placed near an open window or door."

A fan works by moving air around a room to help it carry heat away from you.

If you place a fan next to a wall in the corner of a room, it will block the air from getting into the fan and keep the room feeling hot.

You should be giving your fan at least 30 centimetres of space and it will make your house feel significantly cooler.

Putting the fan near a window or door can help get rid of the warm air while pushing cooler air around your room.

Finding the right spot for your fan can make a huge difference when trying to cool your house in the summer.


### 
  **How to cool down before bed to help you sleep at night**

While you want to keep cool during the day, the heat can make it incredibly difficult to get to sleep at night as well.

There are a few ways you can cool yourself down before going to bed such as using the pulse point method where you soak areas like your wrists and ankles in cool water.

You could also take a warm shower before bed to help cool your body down.

It is important to avoid taking a cold shower before bed, as this will cause your blood vessels to constrict and trap the heat in.
