## Three days of live jazz festival returns to Scarborough Spa next year

The Scarborough Jazz Festival, now in its 23rd year, will take place at Scarborough Spa next year 

Jazz fans are in for a treat as a much-loved music festival makes its return to the Yorkshire coast.

The Scarborough Jazz Festival, now in its 23rd year, will take place at Scarborough Spa from September 25 to 27, 2027, promising three days of live jazz across the traditional and contemporary spectrum.

Festival director Mark Gordon said: "The Scarborough Jazz Festival has become a much-loved event that brings together artists and audiences from across the country.

"We're delighted to welcome an incredible programme of performers to Scarborough Spa this September and look forward to celebrating the power of live Jazz in such a fantastic venue."

With a reputation for attracting top talent and inspiring collaborations, the festival is considered a highlight of the UK jazz calendar.

It also plays a key role in supporting live music and cultural tourism in the region.

Organisers are encouraging early booking due to strong demand.

Audiences can expect a packed line-up of concerts, with the full programme and tickets available through the Scarborough Spa website and box office.

Visitors are also encouraged to explore Scarborough’s coastal attractions, from its historic sites and scenic beaches to its vibrant hospitality scene.

Tickets can be purchased at www.scarboroughspa.co.uk or by calling 01723 376774.
