# Call for witnesses to alleged pub assault after 102-year-old dies

![Phillip Ormerod was allegedly assaulted at the Crow’s Nest pub in Cwmbran](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/13/12/33/crows-nest.jpeg)

- A 102-year-old man named Phillip Ormerod has died in hospital following an alleged assault at a pub in South Wales.
- Emergency services were called to the Crow's Nest pub in Cwmbran around 10.25pm on 1 August.
- A 56-year-old man from Llanelli has been arrested on suspicion of section 20 assault and remains under investigation.
- Senior investigating officer DCI Shelley Comley urged the public to refrain from online speculation while the investigation continues.
- Gwent Police are appealing for witnesses who were inside the pub between 10pm and 11pm on the night of the incident to come forward.
