# Ireland joins England and Wales in withdrawing support for Infantino’s re-election

![Gianni Infantino has been Fifa president since 2016 (Mike Egerton/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/10/9ecbb4d4da2921b8767300ea27f62c1bY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg2NDM5NjMz-2.76957128.jpg)

- The Football Association of Ireland (FAI) has officially withdrawn its endorsement for Gianni Infantino's Fifa re-election bid in March 2027.
- The decision follows actions by Uefa, England, and Wales after Infantino attempted to sell off shares in the World Cup to private investors.
- In a statement, the FAI cited significant concerns regarding governance, transparency, and a lack of meaningful consultation.
- Northern Ireland's IFA has expressed serious concerns over Infantino's leadership but is yet to formally withdraw its backing.
- Despite widespread European backlash, Infantino retains support from key allies including the African Football Confederation, Argentina, Mexico, and six Arab nations.
