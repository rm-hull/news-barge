Liverpool are hoping to swiftly add to the loan signing of Ronald Araujo by hashing out a deal for Paris Saint-Germain winger Bradley Barcola after reaching a reported agreement over personal terms. Barcola leads Liverpool’s list of transfer targets and the Reds are in talks with PSG over a fee, though the European champions are demanding £145m to sell the winger.

Arsenal have offered Myles Lewis-Skelly to multiple Premier League clubs, including rivals Chelsea and Manchester United, but both clubs have reportedly turned down the chance to sign the teenager, who is believed to want to fight for his place despite increased competition. As reported by *The Independent* last week, there was a chance Arsenal would reconsider their position on Lewis-Skelly after completing the signing of Bruno Guimaraes.

Meanwhile, Ferran Torres appears set to swap Barcelona for PSG, while Rodri looks set to leave Manchester City amid interest from Barcelona and Real Madrid. The World Cup winning captain is into the final year of his contract and favours a move to Barca – who are considering a second bid after an initial offer of £38m was rejected. City seem to be making preparations for that outcome as they close in on the signing of World Cup breakout star Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille.

## Everton striker charged by FA after placing 61 alleged bets on matches

The 20-year-old, who moved from the Netherlands to join the Toffees academy at the age of 13, is accused of placing 61 bets over a 16-month period.

## Chelsea sign Marc Cucurella replacement as full-back Pep Chavarria arrives on five-year deal

Pep Chavarria has joined Chelsea from Rayo Vallecano on a five-year contract for a fee understood by the Press Association to be £16.3 million plus £1.7m in add-ons.

An agreement was finalised last week for the 28-year-old to move to Stamford Bridge as the club’s squad overhaul continues under new manager Xabi Alonso.

The left-sided defender, who featured in last season’s Conference League final against Crystal Palace, is expected to battle with Jorrel Hato for the left full-back role following Marc Cucurella’s departure to Real Madrid.

## Youri Tielemans reveals he nearly joined Manchester United twice before

It was third time lucky for Youri Tielemans when he joined Manchester United last month.

As a deal to buy Brazil midfielder Ederson stalled, the Red Devils activated a £35m release clause to sign Tielemans from Aston Villa – but the move might have happened years ago for a player previously linked with United several times.

United reportedly considered Tielemans in the summer of 2019 as his loan spell at Leicester from Monaco ended but he instead joined the Foxes permanently. Tielemans was linked with United again in both 2022 and 2023.

Speaking at United’s pre-season training camp in County Kildare, the Belgium international said there had been direct talks in the past.

“There have been two times that I spoke with the club, but it didn’t (come) to anything, so I was just very calm about it,” Tielemans said.

Asked if he thought his opportunity to join United had passed, Tielemans added: “No, listen, I am very calm about it. When a move comes up, then if it needs to happen, it will happen.

“At the times that I have been linked with the club before, it did not happen, but it is what it is. I just keep working, keep doing my thing on the pitch and then it happens.”

## Atletico agree deal to sign Cristian Romero

Atletico Madrid have agreed a deal in principle with Tottenham to sign Cristian Romero, according to *Sky Sports.*

The package is in the region of £34.2m with add-ons, with a 15 percent sell-on clause set to be included.

Arsenal and Inter Milan have also been linked with the Argentinian.

## Amanda Staveley to explore buying London Stadium if West Ham takeover goes through

Amanda Staveley would explore purchasing the London Stadium if she is successful in buying a significant stake in West Ham, according to *The Times.* 

Mayor of London Sadiq Khan says he is open to “any discussions” on the matter, with West Ham currently tenants of the ground.

The stadium, built for the 2012 Olympics, could be available for as little as £50m given how much it is paid by the taxpayer.

## Lionel Messi ‘not sure’ if he will play much longer as he pays emotional tribute after father’s death

Lionel Messi has admitted he is “not sure” how much longer he will play football after the passing of his father.

Messi’s father, Jorge, died at the age of 68 last week after battling with a long illness.

Jorge was highly influential in Messi’s career, having also been his agent since the Argentinian was 14, overseeing his rise into arguably the greatest footballer of all time.

In an emotional tribute, Messi said he didn’t know how to “carry on” playing without his father, which could spell retirement for the 39-year-old.
