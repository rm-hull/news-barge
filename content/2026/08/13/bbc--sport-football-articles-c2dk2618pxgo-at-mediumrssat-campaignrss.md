**Manchester City** have rejected a £55m bid from**Barcelona** for Spain midfielder Rodri, with the Premier League club wanting more than £60m for the 30-year-old as talks between the two clubs continue. (Sky Sports), external

**Tottenham's** England defender Djed Spence, 26, will have a medical in Italy on Friday prior to completing a move to**Inter Milan** worth 30m euros (£25.6m), plus 5m euros in add-ons. (Gazzetta dello Sport - in Italian), external

**Arsenal** made a move for Spain defender Marc Pubill but were told by**Atletico Madrid** that the 23-year-old is not for sale. (AS - in Spanish), external 

**Hull City** are set to complete the signing of French midfielder Lucas Gourna-Douath, 23, from**Red Bull Salzburg** for 4m euros (£3.4m). (L'Equipe - in French), external 

**Hull** have submitted an offer worth more than £15m for**Everton's** English midfielder Tim Iroegbunam, 23. (Teamtalk), external

**Ipswich Town** are determined to sign German striker Derry Scherhant, 23, from**Freiburg** this summer. (Teamtalk), external

**Bayer Leverkusen** have knocked back an offer of 20m euros (£17m) from**Ipswich** for 27-year-old Argentina midfielder Exequiel Palacios. (Sky Sports Germany), external   

**Napoli** must decide on the future of Italy striker Lorenzo Lucca, 25, before opening talks with**Arsenal** over signing Brazil striker Gabriel Jesus, 29. (Corriere dello Sport - in Italian), external

**AC Milan** manager Ruben Amorim has told eight players - including England defender Fikayo Tomori, 28, and France forward Christopher Nkunku, 28 - that they are surplus to requirements at the Italian club. (Mail), external

Italian side **Genoa** are interested in signing Republic of Ireland striker Evan Ferguson, 21, on loan from**Brighton**. (Talksport), external
