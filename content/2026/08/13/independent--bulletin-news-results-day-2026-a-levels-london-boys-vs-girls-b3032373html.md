# A-level results day: Five key stats as thousands receive grades

1. The proportion of UK entries receiving top grades rose for the third consecutive year to **28.5** per cent, remaining higher than pre-pandemic figures.
2. A record **9.6** per cent of entries achieved an A* grade, the highest non-pandemic figure since the grade was introduced in 2010.
3. London achieved the highest proportion of top grades at **32.7** per cent, while north-east England and the East Midlands had the joint lowest at**23.1** per cent.
4. Boys outperformed girls in terms of top grades, with **28.9** per cent of boys’ entries awarded A or A* compared to**28** per cent for girls’.
5. Mathematics retained its status as the most popular A-level subject, while economics entered the top five subjects for the first time.
