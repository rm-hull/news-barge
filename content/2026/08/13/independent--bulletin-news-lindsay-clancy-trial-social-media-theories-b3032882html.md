# Lindsay Clancy murder trial sparks debate on social media

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/cPcVVQHn/poster.jpg?width=720)

- Lindsay Clancy, a 36-year-old former nurse from Duxbury, Massachusetts, is on trial for first-degree murder in the January 2023 strangulation deaths of her three young children.
- The high-profile trial has sparked fierce debate online, with observers divided between those calling for the maximum punishment and others pointing to Clancy’s previous efforts to seek mental health treatment.
- The case has ignited arguments on social media over whether the killings were a tragic result of severe psychiatric illness or a calculated act demanding maximum legal retribution.
- While jurors weigh medical records and testimony, social media has become a major forum for discussion, with viral videos offering everything from strong support for Clancy to alternative theories about the events of January 24, 2023.
- Some TikTok users have posted messages such as “You’ll never make me hate Lindsay Clancy” and “I wanna become a lawyer to defend women like Lindsay Clancy,” while others argue she could face life in prison for actions they believe were committed by her husband.
