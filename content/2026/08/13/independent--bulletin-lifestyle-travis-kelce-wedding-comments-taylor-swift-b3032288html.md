# Travis Kelce breaks silence on ‘crazy’ Madison Square Garden wedding to Taylor Swift

- Travis Kelce spoke publicly for the first time about his top-secret July 3 wedding to Taylor Swift when speaking to reporters Wednesday at training camp.
- “Best night of my life,” Kelce said of the wedding. “I appreciate everybody who came out and celebrated and had fun with us,” he added.
- Kelce said it was “a crazy night” and that the evening was “full of a lot of celebration,” before telling reporters he’s now turning his focus to “football, baby.”
- The high-profile ceremony was held at Madison Square Garden in New York City and was officiated by actor Adam Sandler. A star-studded guest list attended the celebration, which featured music from Paul McCartney, Stevie Nicks, and Bon Jovi.
- Kansas City Chiefs head coach Andy Reid defended Kelce against recent AI-generated rumors claiming the tight end was out of shape, confirming he is focused and ready for the football season.
