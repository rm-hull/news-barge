# Progressive becomes 2028 presidential frontrunner for first time on prediction market site

- Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez has emerged as the frontrunner to become the 2028 Democratic presidential nominee on prediction markets Polymarket and Kalshi.
- Conventional political polling still places former Vice President Kamala Harris in the top spot, followed by California Governor Gavin Newsom, with Ocasio-Cortez ranking fourth.
- Ocasio-Cortez has not formally announced a presidential run, stating that her primary focus is currently on helping Democrats win the upcoming midterm elections.
- A potential 2028 campaign by Ocasio-Cortez would test whether the Democratic Party shifts further left or tacks toward the center after Harris lost the 2024 election to Donald Trump.
- Democrats are currently projected to retake the House of Representatives in the midterms amid public concern over high gas prices and conflict in Iran.
