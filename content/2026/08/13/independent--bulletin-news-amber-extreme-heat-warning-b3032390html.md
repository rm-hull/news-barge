# UK braces for 37C highs with amber extreme heat warning in effect

- An amber weather warning for extreme heat is in place across large parts of England as temperatures are forecast to reach 37C on Thursday.
- The Met Office warned of potential health impacts, travel disruption, water safety incidents, and power outages during the heatwave.
- Dr Michael Gregory from NHS England advised the public to stay out of the sun between 11am and 3pm to avoid serious heat-related illnesses.
- Some rail operators have urged passengers to travel only if necessary on Thursday due to heat-related service disruptions.
- Nearly three-quarters of England and all of Wales are now suffering from drought conditions.
