POLICE are investigating the alleged theft of a bank card from York city centre.

The incident occurred in Station Road at around 1pm on Saturday (July 18).

  


North Yorkshire Police said: “It involved a male stealing a bank card and subsequently using it around central York.

“If you recognise the person pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.

“You can call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

“Please quote reference 12260139136 when passing on information.”
