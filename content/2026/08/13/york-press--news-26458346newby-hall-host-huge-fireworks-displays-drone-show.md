Crowds can expect a night of dazzling displays as Newby Hall hosts its Fireworks Championships next weekend.

The event will take place in the grounds of the historic stately home, with a wide variety of pyrotechnics on Saturday, August 22.

Three of the UK’s top fireworks companies will compete for the title, each delivering a 10-minute display set to music.

Stuart Gill, commercial director at Newby Hall, said: "Our annual fireworks championships feature stunning displays by some of the UK’s leading fireworks companies.

"It’s a popular evening of entertainment for all the family set in the beautiful parkland at Newby."

Visitors will be able to vote for their favourite display via SMS, and the night will end with a grand finale.

Gates open at 4pm, giving plenty of time for picnics before an 8.45pm drone show and fireworks starting at 9pm.

Camping is also available from 1pm, which costs £16 per adult, with under-16s free.

Advance online tickets are £30 for adults, £20 for children, and under-fives go free.

Newby Hall, one of the UK’s finest Adam houses, is internationally celebrated as one of the largest and most complete surviving Chippendale interiors, and award-winning gardens.

For more details and to buy tickets, visit: [https://www.newbyhall.com/event/fireworks-championships-2026/](https://www.newbyhall.com/event/fireworks-championships-2026/)
