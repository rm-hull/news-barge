Podcaster Vogue Williams has called out Red Hot Chili Peppers frontman Anthony Kiedis for previously dating a 19-year-old while he was 50.

The musician started dating Australian model Helena Vestergaard in 2013, and they split after being together for approximately two years.

Before that, Kiedis, now 63, had a relationship with Heather Christie, who was 22 years his junior. When the pair welcomed their son, Everly Bear, in 2007, Kiedis was 44 and his partner was 21.

In the latest episode of *My Therapist Ghosted Me*, Williams and her co-host Joanne McNally discussed age-gap relationships, with the former* I’m a Celebrity* campmate saying: “Do you know who else I find really disappointing, because I really like the Red Hot Chili Peppers? Anthony Kiedis.” 

Seemingly referring to his romance with Vestergaard, Williams continued: “He is like 54 or something, and he is going out with a 19-year-old. And it is like, ‘Dude, what are you talking about?’”

McNally replied: “I've seen this. I just accept now that most men, all men, are drawn to – I'm going to be very careful how I say this – significantly younger women. That's all I'm going to say. It is what it is and I don't think there's any getting away from it. We judge it but it's not going to change.”

“I would have to say that it is not all men,” Williams continued. “Because I do think it is a very specific thing.”

Addressing why younger women might decide to date older men, McNally added: “And of course, people like him [Anthony Kiedis] and rockstars like him can get away with it because no 19-year-old girl wants to be going out with some old lad who is driving a Toyota Corolla around town.

“They want to be going out with a millionaire rockstar who is going to treat them like a princess and they're going to have a gorgeous life. And he's cool because he's in the Red Hot Chili Peppers, do you know what I mean?”

*The Independent* has contacted Kiedis’s representative for comment. 

The rocker is currently dating podcaster Eileen Kelly, who is 33 years his junior. Kelly defended their romance in April via a *Vogue* essay titled*My Boyfriend is Double My Age*. 

“There is something to be said for a man who’s simply had more time to get his s*** together, and my much older boyfriend seems genuinely excited to be with me – not like he’s biding his time before he can swipe for someone better,” she wrote.

“Is it really so difficult to imagine that connection can exist across generations, and that two people of different ages can find something real in each other?” Kelly added.
