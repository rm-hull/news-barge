## This week’s poll

Consider me shocked and disgusted: because yes, yes they are. Forget the fact that Anne Hathaway is one of our most beloved and most talented stars; forget the fact that having a third baby at any age – let alone at 43 – is difficult and tiring and uncomfortable. When Anne smiled and posed for the cameras on the red carpet to promote her new film *The End of Oak Street* in LA, only one thing was on everybody’s mind: her baby bump outfit.

Beaming as she posed for the cameras, the *Devil Wears Prada 2* star wore a sleeveless baby blue crop top. From the back, it morphed into a long flowing cape-like garment with red lining, falling behind her onto the floor – and from the front, revealed her bump, openly and unabashedly. And she looked beautiful.

But you wouldn’t know that from the comments. Described variously as “ridiculous”, “tacky” and “bizarre”, some wrote it off scathingly as a “wardrobe malfunction”. Which just goes to show: you simply cannot win when you’re pregnant. Click here to see the outfit – and read more.
