Three people were taken to hospital after a car crashed with a van during a police chase in Seaham.

Officers were called to Lord Byron’s Walk in Seaham around 6pm on Wednesday (August 12), following reports that a VW Polo was driving erratically.

The car failed to stop for police and a short chase took place, Durham Police said.

The car crashed into a van travelling in the opposite direction in Lord Byron’s Walk, before hitting a tree.

The fire service, ambulance service and air ambulance also attended and called, and the road was closed for nine hours while investigations took place. It reopened at 3am.

The driver, a man in his 20s, suffered leg injuries.

His passenger, a woman in her 20s, suffered head injuries, and the driver of the van, a man in his 50s, suffered back injuries.

All three were taken to hospital for treatment.

The car driver and passenger remain in hospital today, and the van driver is now recovering at home.

An investigation is ongoing, and officers are appealing for anyone who might have seen the Polo leading up to the collision or have dashcam footage to get in touch.

If you have any information, call 101, quoting incident reference number 401 of August 12.
