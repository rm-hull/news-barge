A road in Bielby will close for proposed works, which are anticipated to be completed within three days.

Peters Field Farm Access Road will be closed to all through traffic starting on August 17, after East Riding of Yorkshire Council issued a temporary prohibition order.

The order allows the council to close the road to ensure the safety of the public during the completion of the works.

There will be no alternative route during the closure.

However, the road will remain open at all times to pedestrians, and for vehicular access to properties directly affected by the closure.

Emergency services will also have access.

The order will be in effect for a maximum of 18 months or until the works are completed, whichever comes first.

For further information, residents can contact Streetscene Services, Highways Maintenance on 01482 393939, quoting reference RJH.
