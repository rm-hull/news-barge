# Lewes train derailment: Full list of rail services disrupted

![Southern Rail has advised passengers not to travel](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/13/16/09/lewes-train-3-e8ff181e.jpeg)

- Emergency services are at the scene of a train derailment in Sussex between Haywards Heath and Lewes.
- Southern Rail has advised passengers not to travel.
- No trains can run between Haywards Heath and Lewes. Trains between **Brighton, Lewes and Eastbourne / Seaford** are mostly cancelled, the operator added.
- **Cooksbridge** and**Plumpton** stations will not be served by Southern services until further notice.
- Southern Rail also said that people travelling to **Gatwick Airport** should allow at least an extra 90 minutes for their journeys to ensure they make flight connections.
