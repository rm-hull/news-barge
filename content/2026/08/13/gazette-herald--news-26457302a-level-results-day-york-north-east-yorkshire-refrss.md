MAKE sure to follow along with The Press as our full A-Level results day coverage is set to take place today (August 13) and in print tomorrow.

Once again, A-Level students are set to find out their results as pupils across the country prepare to find out whether they’ve secured a place at their dream university, apprenticeship scheme, or if they’ll be entering the world of work.

There will be an eight-page souvenir picture supplement in Friday’s Press, packed with photos from the secondary schools and colleges across our publication area - alongside stories and pictures from the day in the main body of the paper.

  


- 
    **New cancer support group formed in York suburb**

    **Play telling the story behind much-loved Disney film coming to York**

We are not publishing lists of students’ results this year as over successive years the number of schools submitting them has sadly dwindled.

Meanwhile, you will also be able to follow our live coverage online, with similar coverage will be given to the GCSE results next week.

We will be publishing local schools’ results stories as we get them and also uploading pictures and local reaction from people across the city and county.

Visit The Press website and stay tuned throughout the day to see results from across the region.
