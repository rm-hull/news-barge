# Why Man Utd’s Youri Tielemans has apologised to fans of his former club

![The Belgian midfielder completed a £35m move from Villa to Manchester United earlier this summer](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/13/15/01KZVRW8BEBWFZ9ESMPXXVX75B.jpg)

- Youri Tielemans has apologised to Aston Villa supporters after claiming Manchester United were a level above his former club.
- The Belgian midfielder completed a £35m move from Villa to Manchester United earlier this summer.
- Tielemans faced criticism from fans after stating during a pre-season tour that United were among the clubs above Villa.
- In a statement on Instagram, he admitted his mistake and insisted he never intended to offend the club or its fans.
- The 29-year-old helped Villa win the Europa League earlier this year before making the move to Old Trafford.
