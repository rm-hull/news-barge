Knowings’ standout characters during her time on the sketch comedy show included news anchor Brenda Stone whose lucky pen had been stolen and Jessica the valley girl whose catchphrase was “whateverrr.”

Aside from All That, Knowings appeared in three episodes of Sesame Street alongside her twin brother Chris Knowings.

As a singer, she released her song “To the World” in 2020.
