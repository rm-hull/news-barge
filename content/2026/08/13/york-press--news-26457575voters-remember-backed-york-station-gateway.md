## Voters should remember who backed this scheme

Recently, a letter from Andy D'Agorne lamented the lack of progress with the cycle track at the station redevelopment debacle.

I would have thought it more appropriate to comment on the absence of bus shelters and electronic timetables at the station side.

I would like to thank The Press for explaining who was responsible for this idiotic development; as a matter of interest, would this be the same Andy D'Agorne who was in the Green Party mentioned in the same press exposé?

The Press has done us a big favour in showing who was responsible, particularly as local elections will soon be upon us.
