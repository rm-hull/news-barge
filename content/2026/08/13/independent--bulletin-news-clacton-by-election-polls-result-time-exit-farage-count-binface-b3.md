# Clacton by-election polls close: What time is the result – as Farage aims to retain seat

- Polling has closed in the Clacton by-election, with Reform UK leader Nigel Farage attempting to retain his parliamentary seat.
- The result of the contest, which is estimated to have cost taxpayers around £250,000, is expected to be announced on Friday morning.
- Farage triggered the by-election after resigning in protest over scrutiny into a £5 million gift from cryptocurrency tycoon Christopher Harborne.
- Main Westminster parties boycotted the election as a stunt, leaving Farage to face a record field of 33 other candidates.
- The parliamentary standards investigation into the donation was paused upon Farage's resignation but will resume if he wins the seat.
