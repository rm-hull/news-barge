The best mattress protectors aren't the most exciting thing you'll buy for your bedroom, but they could be one of the smartest. You'll probably never think about them until a child has an accident, someone spills a cup of tea or your morning coffee ends up in the wrong place and you find yourself praying you don’t need to fork out for a new mattress.

As a mum of four, I wouldn't be without one. Between potty-training accidents, the occasional bout of sickness and children who inevitably end up climbing into my bed during the night, our mattresses have seen just about everything. A mattress protector acts like an insurance policy, protecting against spills, sweat and everyday wear while helping to keep the mattress cleaner and in good condition for longer.

Considering a quality mattress can cost hundreds, or even thousands, of pounds, it's a small investment that could save you a much bigger expense down the line. To find the best mattress protectors, I tested a range of waterproof and quilted designs, comparing comfort, breathability, fit, durability and overall value for money to see which are genuinely worth buying.
