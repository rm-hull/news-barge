# Thunderstorm warning issued as temperatures exceed 36C

- A yellow thunderstorm warning has been issued across Northern Ireland, running until 6am on Friday.
- Temperatures in parts of the UK have exceeded 36C, with Charlwood near Gatwick Airport recording the highest temperature so far at 36.4C.
- The Met Office confirmed this is the ninth day in 2026 where UK temperatures have reached 35C or higher.
- Boomtown Festival attendees have been urged to stay safe and hydrated by Hampshire County Council as extreme heat hits the UK.
- More than 1,500 Arriva North London bus drivers are set to take industrial action on Friday over concerns regarding working conditions during hot weather, according to the Unite union.
