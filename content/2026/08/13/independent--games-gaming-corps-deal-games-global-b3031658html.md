Swedish gaming developer Gaming Corps has signed an iGaming aggregation deal with leading industry content supplier Games Global.

The deal marks the latest step in Gaming Corps’ commercial growth strategy, giving it access to one of the industry’s largest distribution networks.

The company will integrate its full suite of casino content onto the Games Global aggregation platform.

Games Global powers hundreds of online casino brands around the world, with a network that includes more than 30 in-house and partnered studios as well as a portfolio of over 1,400 proprietary games, reports *iGaming Business*. 

It will now add Gaming Corps’ portfolio of titles too, including the likes of Smash4Cash, Multiplier Games and Mine Games in addition to further online slots and table games.

“Having our entire portfolio available through Games Global is a significant achievement for Gaming Corps,” said company CEO Juha Kauppinen.

“It not only expands the global reach of our content but also demonstrates the confidence leading distribution partners place in the quality and diversity of our games.

“We're excited to begin this collaboration and see our titles reach new operators and players worldwide, further supporting our long-term strategy of growing with trusted partners in key regulated jurisdictions,” he explained.

Rupert Dix, commercial director at Games Global, added: “Gaming Corps has built a distinctive and innovative portfolio that is an excellent complement to our content offering.

“We're delighted to welcome them to the Games Global platform and look forward to introducing their games to our operator partners in regulated markets worldwide.”
