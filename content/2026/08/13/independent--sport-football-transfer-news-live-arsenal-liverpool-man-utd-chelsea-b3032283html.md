Chelsea have told Manchester City that they have until Friday to submit a bid for midfielder Enzo Fernandez. The Blues have told their Premier League rivals that they will need to sepnd £120m to sign the World Cup runner-up. City, meanwhile, remain in talks with Barcelona over Rodri, who is available for £60m, while they are also interested in the signing of World Cup breakout star Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille.

Mikel Arteta has said that Arsenal’s business is not done yet, with the Gunners receiving interest in Gabriel Martinelli from Galatasaray as well as Chelsea and Manchester United reportedly turning down the chance to sign Myles Lewis-Skelly. Arsenal have set their sights high and are interested in adding a marquee signing: they will have to play the long game in moves for Atletico Madrid’s Julian Alvarez and Paris Saint-Germain’s Bradley Barcola.

Barcola leads also Liverpool’s list of transfer targets and the Reds are in talks with PSG over a fee, though the European champions are demanding £145m to sell the winger.

*Follow all of the latest news, rumours and done deals with our live blog below* 

## Chelsea set Enzo Fernandez asking price

Chelsea will demand £120m from Manchester City for Enzo Fernandez.

The Blues have set their Premier League rivals until 5pm on Friday to submit their bid.

Enzo Maresca is an admirer of Fernandez, but relations between the clubs are frosty.

City had to pay Chelsea £17m in compensation after Maresca stepped down last season.

Chelsea subsequently said they felt “let down” by the Italian, who is now in charge of City.

## Newcastle in talks to sign Benfica right back Amar Dedic

Newcastle are in talks to sign Benfica right back Amar Dedic.

But the Bosnian is set to feature for Benfica in tonight’s Europa League play-off against Hearts, head coach Marco Silva confirmed.

Newcastle want to sign a full-back this window after Kieran Trippier’s exit and the injury to Tino Livramento limited their options.

## 'He's our player': Michael Carrick gives update on Marcus Rashford future

Michael Carrick has suggested that Marcus Rashford could feature for Manchester United this season and that he could still have a future at Old Trafford.

Rashford has returned to pre-season training at United following the World Cup but didn’t feature in the friendly against Leeds at Croke Park last night.

"He's our player. He's come back great, like any other player. I've known him a long time, he gives us something a bit different,” Carrick told ITV Sport.

"He's been in great spirits since he's been back, he's been back two or three days."

## Aston Villa linked with Aaron Wan-Bissaka and Joao Palhinha

Aston Villa impressed despite defeat to Paris Saint-Germain in the Super Cup last night, and Unai Emery’s side are still looking to strengthen in the transfer market.

Villa have held talks over signing right back **Aaron Wan-Bissaka** from West Ham, with a £25m fee discussed, according to the Athletic. 

Villa are also keen on signing **Joao Palhinha** on loan. The midfielder spent last season at Tottenham. Florian Plettenberg reports that Bayern Munich want to sell the 31-year-old. 

## Arsenal winger Gabriel Martinelli heading to Galatasaray?

Turkish clubs have been active in the transfer window and Arsenal winger **Gabriel Martinelli** is the subject of a £38.5m bid from Galatasaray. 

That’s according to journalist Florian Plettenberg, who reports that the 25-year-old Brazil winger is top of Galatasaray’s list of targets.

Arsenal have signed a winger in Christos Tzolis this summer, and they were then linked with Vinicius Jr.

## Mikel Arteta on Arsenal's transfer business: 'More we want to do'

Mikel Arteta has said that Arsenal still have targets in the transfer window, but stressed there are “very few” players who can take the Gunners to the next level.

The Gunners have been linked with both Atletico Madrid’s Julian Alvarez and Paris Saint-Germain’s Bradley Barcola as they bid to add a marquee signing to their attack.

"I think for the margins to improve, there are very few players in the market that are available to do that and bring that to the club,” Arteta said.

He added: "There is more that we want to do because every time we have an opportunity to improve the squad and bring more quality and depth to the squad, we need to do that.”

## Tottenham agree deal with Atletico Madrid for Cristian Romero

Atletico Madrid have agreed a deal in principle with Tottenham to sign **Cristian Romero**. 

The package is in the region of £34.2m with add-ons, with a 15 per cent sell-on clause set to be included, according to Sky Sports.

Inter Milan had also been linked with the Argentinian. But the Italian side are also set to a a deal to sign **Djed Spence**. 

## Myles Lewis-Skelly scores in Arsenal friendly with Como as transfer speculation continues

Myles Lewis-Skelly appeared to underline his commitment to Arsenal after setting aside transfer speculation to score in Wednesday’s 1-1 friendly draw with Italian club Como.

The 19-year-old has been linked with the Gunners’ Premier League rivals Chelsea and Manchester United amid uncertainty over his future.

Starting in midfield, he capitalised on an error from Como goalkeeper Jean Butez to slot the hosts ahead in the 33rd minute at Emirates Stadium.

Lewis-Skelly then made a heart sign to the crowd as he celebrated before tapping the club badge on his shirt on his way back to the centre circle.

## Arsenal in ‘pole position’ to sign Bundesliga striker, claims agent

Arsenal hold a "genuine" interest in Bayer Leverkusen forward Christian Kofane and talks have taken place between both clubs regarding the 20-year-old, according to the player's representative.

The Cameroon international featured 44 times for the Bundesliga outfit throughout last season, which included appearances in both legs of their Champions League last-16 fixture against Mikel Arteta’s team.

He ended the campaign with seven goals during his first year in top-flight football, having made the switch from Spanish Segunda Division outfit Albacete a year ago.

## Chelsea set Man City deadline for Enzo Fernandez

Chelsea have told Manchester City they have a deadline of 5pm this Friday to make an offer for Enzo Fernandez, who they value at £120m, according to *Sky Sports.*

It’s understood City are interested in the midfielder, with new boss Enzo Maresca looking to reunite with his former midfielder at the Etihad.
