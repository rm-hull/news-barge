# Man City midfielder set for Saudi Arabia move after Elliot Anderson arrival

- Manchester City are in negotiations with Saudi Pro League club Al Qadsiah over the potential £50m sale of midfielder Tijjani Reijnders.
- The Netherlands international joined City from AC Milan for £46.5m last summer, scoring seven goals in 50 appearances during his first season.
- Reijnders fell out of favour towards the end of the 2025/26 campaign, losing his starting place under Pep Guardiola.
- Al Qadsiah, managed by Brendan Rodgers, faced competing interest for Reijnders from Nottingham Forest.
- City are continuing a midfield overhaul under Enzo Maresca, having already signed Elliot Anderson from Forest for £116m.
