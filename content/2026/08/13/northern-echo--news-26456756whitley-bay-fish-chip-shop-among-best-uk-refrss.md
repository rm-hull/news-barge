Seaside towns and villages are a popular destination for day trippers throughout the summer.

But no trip to the seaside is complete without enjoying some traditional fish and chips while watching the sea.

Daniel Gray, who wrote the book Food of the Cods, has shared his top 25 fish and chip shops around the UK with the i Paper.

If you are ever visiting Whitley Bay, you will want to take a trip to Fisherman’s Bay to enjoy its award-winning fish and chips.


## Why is Fisherman’s Bay so popular?

On the fish and chip shop, the publication said: "Fisherman’s Bay presents customers with a dilemma.

"Sit inside and appreciate its elegant, calming restaurant?

"Or, take away and eat within sight of the rumbling North Sea, stabbing wooden forks into haddock so that it unleashes steam into the air?

"This Tyneside marvel also deserves praise for its creative vegan options."

Fisherman’s Bay is run by the Dhillon family, who have over 25 years of experience in the industry.

The fish and chip shop uses sustainable ingredients and promises to create the "freshest and most flavoursome dishes".

Whether you fancy cod bites, haddock, or some classic Whitby scampi, Fisherman’s Bay has it on the menu.

  You can take away your fish and chips to enjoy by the sea. 

*(Image: Tripadvisor)*

Of course, you can get each meal served with a portion of chips.

For those who are not lovers of fish, there are lots of alternative options, including halloumi fritters, sausages, burgers, and chicken goujons.

Fisherman’s Bay has all of the classic sides from curry sauce and gravy to mushy peas, as well as some of its own homemade sauces like chilli jam, hot sauce, and garlic mayo.

The shop offers gluten-free and vegan options, meaning everyone is catered for.

The fish and chip shop has lots of indoor and outdoor seating, but you can also take away your meal to head down to the coast.
