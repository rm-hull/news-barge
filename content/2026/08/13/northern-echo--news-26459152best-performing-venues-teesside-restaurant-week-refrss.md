A restaurant with “eye-catching” presentation and an award-winning Indian have been named as the winners of the latest Teesside Restaurant Week.

Noir D’or and Vadah Indian Restaurant, which are both located on the Riverside in Stockton, were the best performing venues of the most recent initiative, which kicked off on June 29.

  James Watson, who runs the week and hospitality business consultancy Over Ice, said it comes as both businesses benefit from the newly opened Stockton Waterfront urban park, __which now connects the High Street to the river.__

He said the opening of the park and bridge had “brought a welcome boost to local trade” and improved connectivity to both restaurants.

  Vadah, the winner of Teesside Restaurant Week 

*(Image: TEESSIDE RESTAURANT WEEK)*

  Noir D’or, which is described as an “upscale dining experience” with “bold flavours and eye-catching presentation”, __opened above Vadah at the start of last year__.

Meanwhile Vadah, which opened in 2014, has remained popular for more than a decade and is known for its “authentic flavours, traditional cooking techniques and carefully crafted dishes.”

“Teesside Restaurant Week is a powerful example of how a focused local initiative can generate real economic and social impact across the region,” James said.

  Noir D'or, the winner of Teesside Restaurant Week 

*(Image: TEESSIDE RESTAURANT WEEK)*

“It doesn’t just fill tables in restaurants, it strengthens the entire hospitality ecosystem, from independent venues to key regional suppliers like Country Valley Foods and Quality Foods.

“The result is a genuine domino effect that keeps money circulating locally, supports jobs, and raises the profile of Teesside as a vibrant food destination.

“At Over Ice Business Consultants, we see this as exactly the kind of community-led activity that drives sustainable regional growth and long-term business resilience.”

Teesside Restaurant Week takes place four times a year and allows diners to enjoy some of Teesside’s best restaurants for less.

A string of popular businesses regularly take part, from Tomahawk Steakhouse venues to the Impeccable Pig in Sedgefield and Il Mulino in Stokesley.

Details regarding the next restaurant week and the venues taking part will be announced in due course.
