# Adam Sandler’s daughter praised for ‘truly lovely’ performance in Netflix film

- Seventeen-year-old Sunny Sandler stars in her first major lead role in the Netflix coming-of-age dramedy *Don't Say Good Luck* .
- The film follows high school theater student Sophie as she lands a lead role in a musical while coping with her mother's recurring cancer diagnosis.
- Critics from major outlets praised Sandler's emotional performance and her onscreen dynamic with co-star Melanie Lynskey.
- Directed by Julia Hart, the project is Sandler's first major film without her family appearing onscreen, though her father Adam Sandler produced it.
- *Don't Say Good Luck* premieres Friday on Netflix.
