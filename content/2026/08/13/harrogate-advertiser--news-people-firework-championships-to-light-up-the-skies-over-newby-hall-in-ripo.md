# Firework Championships to light up the skies over Newby Hall in Ripon

## The skies above Newby Hall and Gardens will be alight with spectacular pyrotechnic displays this weekend when the estate hosts its annual Fireworks Championships.

Three of the UK’s leading fireworks companies will compete for the title this on Saturday, August 22.

![Firework Championships will light up the skies over Ripon’s Newby Hall this weekend.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjQ3NDM0ZDkyLTBmNWQtNDViMy04MzA2LTZiZDlkNzJjZmJiMzo1NjZjM2MwNC1jZDM2LTQ5Y2UtYjY5Mi0zY2QyNTEyNmQzODM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Each team will put on a 10‑minute display choreographed to music, with visitors voting for their favourite via SMS. The competition will conclude with a huge finale display, promising one of the most spectacular nights of the summer.

Stuart Gill, commercial director at Newby Hall, said: “Our annual fireworks championships feature stunning displays by some of the UK’s leading fireworks companies. It’s a popular evening of entertainment for all the family set in the beautiful parkland at Newby.”

Gates open at 4pm, giving visitors plenty of time to enjoy a picnic with friends and family before an 8.45pm drone show and the first fireworks at 9pm. Camping is available from 1pm for those wishing to make a weekend of it.

Advance online tickets are £30 for adults, £20 for children, with under‑5s free. Camping costs £16 per adult, with under‑16s free. For more details and to buy tickets, go to: www.newbyhall.com/event/fireworks-championships-2026.

Newby Hall is one of the UK’s finest Adam houses and is internationally celebrated as one of the largest and most complete surviving Chippendale interiors.

Its 25 acres of award-winning gardens include the newly restored Edwardian rock garden (one of the largest and most significant in the UK), one of Europe’s largest double herbaceous borders, an enchanted woodland walk, a miniature railway and a children’s adventure playground as well as permanent dolls house and teddy bear exhibitions.
