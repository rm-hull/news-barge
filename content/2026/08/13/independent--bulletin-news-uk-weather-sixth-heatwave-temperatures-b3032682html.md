# Will a sixth heatwave hit the UK this summer? Here’s the forecast

- Met Office forecasts indicate that the UK could see a sixth heatwave before summer is out.
- The country is currently on track for its hottest summer on record, with five heatwaves so far.
- On Thursday, a temperature of 38.1C was recorded at Kew Gardens in London – the highest of 2026.
- The current heatwave is due to end by the weekend, with temperatures dropping to 27C.
- However, the Met Office said that high pressure could return towards the end of August, potentially triggering another heatwave.
