The scorching heat will continue across the North East today, with temperatures expected to rise rapidly through the morning before reaching around 32C.

The Met Office says it will be a dry and largely sunny morning, although skies are expected to turn hazy during the afternoon as high cloud spills in from the north west.

Temperatures will climb quickly, reaching the mid-20s by 10am before peaking at 32C between 2pm and 5pm.

Winds will remain fairly light throughout the day, reaching up to 10mph early in the afternoon.

The Met Office says: “Sunny and very warm today but turning cooler from tomorrow (August 14).

“A dry and largely sunny morning but skies will turn hazy this afternoon as some high cloud begins to spill in from the northwest. Feeling hot, especially in the south. Maximum temperature 32°C.”

The warm conditions will continue into the evening, with temperatures remaining at 25C at 9pm.

It says: “Overnight cloud will continue to thicken with some rain arriving into the northwest by dawn. Minimum temperature 15°C.”
