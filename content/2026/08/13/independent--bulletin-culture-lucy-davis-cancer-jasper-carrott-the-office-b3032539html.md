# Lucy Davis’s father Jasper Carrott makes heartbreaking statement about her cancer diagnosis

- Actor Lucy Davis has revealed she was diagnosed with incurable Stage 4 breast cancer, which has metastasised to her spine, right hip, and ribs.
- The 53-year-old star of *The Office* stated she is determined to live her remaining life as fun as possible and wishes to continue acting.
- Her father, comedian Jasper Carrott, expressed immense pride in how she has handled her diagnosis and asked for privacy for the family.
- Industry peers including Ricky Gervais, Jenna Fischer, Samantha Morton, and Edgar Wright have publicly offered messages of love and support.
- Davis urged her followers to get any health concerns checked immediately, emphasizing the vital role humour has played in helping her cope.
