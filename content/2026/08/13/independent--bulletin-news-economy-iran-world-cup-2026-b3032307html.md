# World Cup and hot weather help UK economy weather Iran shocks

- UK economic growth slowed to 0.4 per cent in the second quarter of the year, down from 0.6 per cent in the first quarter.
- Monthly GDP grew by 0.3 per cent in June, buoyed by the service sector, warm weather, and the start of the football World Cup.
- The ongoing conflict stemming from Donald Trump's war on Iran has driven up energy costs and strained household finances.
- Chancellor John Healey and Prime Minister Andy Burnham pledged to maintain economic resilience and deliver growth across all UK postcodes.
- Business leaders and trade unions have urged the government to use the Autumn Budget to cut costs for firms and reform fiscal institutions.
