# Actor from Nickelodeon’s All That dead at age 46 after suffering medical emergency

![Knowings was hospitalized after an asthma attack, according to TMZ](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/13/18/52/GettyImages-1132364663.jpeg)

- Former Nickelodeon star Christy Knowings has died at the age of 46 following a severe asthma attack that left her with brain damage, according to *TMZ.*
- Knowings joined the cast of Nickelodeon's popular sketch comedy series *All That* in late 1997, alongside Kenan Thompson and Amanda Bynes.
- Her other Nickelodeon appearances included starring in the Rosie O’Donnell sketch comedy pilot *And Now This!* and the game show*Figure It Out.*
- Beyond her comedy roles, she appeared in three episodes of *Sesame Street* with her twin brother, Chris Knowings.
- In addition to acting, Knowings was a musical artist who released her song *To the World* in 2020.
