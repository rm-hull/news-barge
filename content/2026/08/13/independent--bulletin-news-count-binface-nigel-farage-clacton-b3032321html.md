# Voters go to polls as Count Binface challenges Nigel Farage in Clacton

- Polls have opened in the Clacton by-election as voters cast their ballots to decide Nigel Farage's future as an MP.
- The by-election was triggered after the Reform UK leader resigned following intense scrutiny over his financial affairs.
- A record 34 candidates are standing, though Labour, the Conservatives, Lib Dems and Greens are boycotting the vote.
- Satirical candidate Count Binface, whose policies include nationalising Adele and building “one affordable home”, has emerged as Mr Farage's primary rival in the contest.
- Voting will close at 10pm, with results expected on Friday morning.
