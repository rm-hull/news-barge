From historic streets and thriving theatres to a legendary nightlife scene and attractions within walking distance, one North East city has plenty to offer for competitive prices.

With its striking architecture, bustling restaurants and bars, and compact city centre, you can see why it is a favourite for weekend breaks.

Its affordability may be its biggest draw, with a new study naming the North East city the UK's cheapest city break destination.

Research by ticket marketplace SeatPick ranked Newcastle as the best-value city in the country for a budget-friendly weekend away, ahead of Liverpool in second place and Birmingham in third.

  The Quayside in Newcastle. 

*(Image: Tripadvisor)*

Researchers said Newcastle's appeal lies in its compact city centre, cultural attractions and vibrant nightlife, alongside average hotel prices of just £79 a night.

The city is home to venues including the Victorian-era Tyne Theatre & Opera House and Northern Stage, while areas such as Grey Street are known for their historic architecture, independent businesses, restaurants and bars.

Whether it's the buzzing nightlife or the unwavering support for its football club, the city is renowned for a distinctly Geordie welcome.

SeatPick also highlighted the city's compact layout - allowing visitors to move between hotels, attractions, restaurants and entertainment venues without relying heavily on taxis or public transport.

Gilad Zilberman, CEO at SeatPick, said: "Newcastle combines a vibrant nightlife with one of the UK's most affordable city-centre accommodation.

"With average hotel prices of just £79 per night, a walkable centre and plenty of bars and restaurants close to major venues, it's one of the best value cities for turning a concert into a weekend away."
