# Graham Norton opens up about Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s star-studded wedding

- Graham Norton has opened up about Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s star-studded wedding at Madison Square Garden, describing it as “lovely”.
- However, Norton hinted there were some details he would have omitted from his own nuptials.
- Norton told *The Chris Evans Breakfast Show: “* You saw things, you know, ‘Oh, that's a lovely touch,’ and then you saw things, like, ‘Oh, I wouldn't have done that’.”
- The high-profile July ceremony was officiated by actor Adam Sandler and featured musical performances from Sir Paul McCartney, Stevie Nicks, and Bon Jovi.
- Kelce called the wedding the “best night” of his life, adding: “I appreciate everybody who came out and celebrated and had fun with us.”
