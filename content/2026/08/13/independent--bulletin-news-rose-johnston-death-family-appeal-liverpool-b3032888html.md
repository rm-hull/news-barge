# Family of murdered Liverpool grandmother appeal for answers on 69th birthday

![Rose Johnston was killed in October last year](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/13/16/01KZWVF9T730Q6D5TYWJJDHYBG.jpg)

- The family of Rose Johnston, who was murdered at her home in Dovecot, Liverpool, have issued a public appeal for information on what would have been her 69th birthday.
- The grandmother was found dead with a blunt-force head injury on 4 October 2025, leaving her family desperate for answers and justice.
- A 37-year-old man who was arrested on suspicion of her murder in May remains on conditional bail.
- Merseyside Police believe answers lie within the local community and have urged anyone with information to come forward.
- Independent charity Crimestoppers is offering a reward of up to £20,000 for information leading to the arrest of those responsible.
