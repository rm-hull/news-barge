FIREFIGHTERS have been called to a luxury car on fire in a North Yorkshire village.

A Selby crew from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service attended a fire in High Street, Carlton, involving a Porsche Boxster at 11.16am on Thursday (August 13).

  


A spokesperson said: “The car, which was well alight, was extinguished by a foam hose reel.

“The cause was unknown.”
