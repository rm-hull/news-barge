# James May gives positive news about Jeremy Clarkson’s health

![Jeremy Clarkson, left, James May and their former Grand Tour co-presenter Richard Hammond](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/03/16/8/07/PA-77419072.jpg)

- James May has reassured fans that Jeremy Clarkson is “fine” and “over the hurdle” following his aggressive prostate cancer diagnosis.
- Clarkson revealed his health struggle in the fifth season of *Clarkson's Farm* and has since confirmed that he is officially in remission.
- The presenter urged men to get checked for prostate cancer, describing the testing process as a simple no-brainer.
- Clarkson also disclosed that he suffered a severe medical emergency after taking blood thinners without medical advice during his cancer treatment.
- Both May and Clarkson are set to make cameo appearances in the rebooted series of *The Grand Tour* , which launches on 4 September.
