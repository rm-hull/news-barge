# Lionel Richie blasts Usher and Chris Brown tour: ‘We were doing that 40 years ago’

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/BrQPYwiC/poster.jpg?width=720)

- Lionel Richie criticized Usher and Chris Brown's joint stadium tour during a recent concert, claiming co-headlining tours are not innovative.
- Richie joked that the only difference between his past tours with Earth, Wind & Fire and Usher and Brown's tour is that "we didn't go to jail."
- After the crowd reacted, Richie quickly apologized for referencing Brown's past legal troubles, which include serving jail time for assaulting Rihanna in 2009.
- Richie's recent performances follow several last-minute concert postponements and cancellations due to health concerns for both himself and Earth, Wind & Fire members.
- Usher and Chris Brown launched their co-headlining Raymond & Brown tour in June, with dates scheduled across major U.S. cities through December.
