# Burnham better suited than Starmer to ‘world of social media’: Cooper

- Health Secretary Yvette Cooper has suggested that Sir Keir Starmer's style of governing does not necessarily work in politics compared to new Prime Minister Andy Burnham.
- Ms Cooper praised Mr Burnham as being better suited for “a world of social media” and more personally invested in policy creation, including social care reform.
- She criticised Sir Keir’s management approach, saying that he had a lack of direct involvement in policy development.
- Sir Keir stepped down as Labour leader in July, pledging his full support to Mr Burnham, who subsequently appointed Ms Cooper to his cabinet.
- Mr Cooper reflected on the frustrations of frequent cabinet reshuffles while expressing enthusiasm for the opportunities within her new health portfolio.
