A County Durham company has applied to keep an extra vehicle and trailer at its operating centre, according to a public notice.

Maudesville Corp Ltd of Burnt House Bank, Pelton Fell Road, Chester-le-Street, has applied to keep an extra goods vehicle and trailer at its operating centre at Plot 3, O’Brien Business Park, Durham Road, Birtley.

Residents and businesses near the centre who believe their enjoyment of the land may be affected can make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner.

- 
    **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo (publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be foundhere.**

These must be sent to Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE within 21 days of the notice being published.

The notice was published on Wednesday, August 12.

They should include the reasons for the objection.

A copy of the representation should be sent to Maudesville Corp Ltd at the address provided in the notice.

Those wishing to object can also find a guide to making representations from the Traffic Commissioner’s Office.
