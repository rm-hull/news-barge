From skip-the-line tickets to iconic landmarks, to food tours, local excursions and hidden-gem activities, Viator gives you access to over 400,000 bookable experiences worldwide, so there's something to suit every kind of traveller.

With free cancellation on most bookings and 24/7 customer support, you can plan with total confidence, whatever your summer plans end up looking like.

Prize: £350 voucher worth of Viator experiences to put towards a weekend getaway or upcoming summer trip. Whether it's a staycation or a trip abroad, the prize can be used to book must-see attractions, tours and activities via Viator.
