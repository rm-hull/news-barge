A biker has been seriously hurt in a crash in Selby.

It happened on Flaxley Road at around 12.40pm yesterday.

A black Ford Fiesta travelling from Selby collided with a silver Honda motorbike coming the other way.

#### Flaxley Road, Selby

The rider is being treated for serious injuries in hospital.

Flaxley Road was closed for several hours while an investigation was carried out.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us.”
