Police are searching for a North East man in his 20s who has been missing for more than ten days.

Ernest, 25, was last seen in the Thorney Close Road area, near Farringdon, Sunderland, just before 11.30pm on Sunday, August 2.

He is described as 5ft 7in tall, of slim build, with mousey brown, short hair and a stubbled beard.

He was last seen wearing dark clothing, including cargo pants.

A spokesperson for Northumberland Police said: "Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate him, and officers are now requesting anyone with information that could assist to come forward."
