## The Impact of Modern Communication

While this trend may lead one to believe it’s the result of disenfranchisement, in fact, a unique form of ‘digital curiosity’ has emerged. Responsiveness is increasingly shaped by whether users can verify who is contacting them. Modern phone users now want to identify the person behind the phone number or username before deciding if it is wise to respond, a habit formed through a frequent __misuse of communication__. 

This habit is particularly entrenched amongst younger generations who view unsolicited and unidentified communication as an intrusion on their time and possible safety. But even older generations are shifting away from their previous sense of duty, choosing to take a more guarded approach to protect their privacy and evade spoofers, spammers and robocalls.

Geography has an impact on these behavioural patterns as well.
