"One of our most gifted students" is how St John's Catholic School headteacher Lisa Byron sums up maths whizz Imogen Chen.

Imogen not only finished her maths A-level in 2025 – a year early and earning a top A*grade – but today also secured an A* in further maths and an A in chemistry at the Bishop Auckland school.

She hopes her grades – and the A* extended project qualification she wrote on the implications of China’s emergence as a global superpower – will stand her in good stead to follow her dreams of joining the financial sector.

She said: "The teachers at St John’s are always here for me, even offering up their free time to make sure that I was able to reach my full potential.
