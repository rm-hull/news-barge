## York aerial gymnast set to join circus arts centre in London after top marks

Ava Thomas-Richards achieved three A*s in Fine Art, Theatre Studies and RE - earning her a place at the National Centre for Circus Arts in London*(Image: Newsquest)*

A TALENTED York student has taken a major step towards her dream career as a circus aerial gymnast after achieving top marks in her A-levels.

Ava Thomas-Richards is set to begin her studies at the National Centre for Circus Arts in London after receiving three A* grades.

The All Saints Catholic School student sailed through her A Levels in Fine Art, Theatre Studies and RE – praising her teachers for their help in her success.

  


She said: “I love theatre and everything creative.

“I have been performing with gymnastics since I was 15 – and love the combining the two on stage.

“My success today is testament to my teachers, in particular Mr Norton.

“Mr Norton really got me to where I wanted to be – he was absolutely brilliant.”

  Phil Norton celebrated with students as they achieved an "incredible" set of results 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Ava joined seven other students receiving A*s from All Saints’ art department this academic year.

Speaking about the cohort, headteacher Sharon Keelan-Beardsley said: “We could not be prouder of our students.

“These results are a wonderful reflection of their hard work, determination and ambition, supported by the dedication of our staff and the strong partnership between school and families.

“A significant number of students achieved A’s or A*across the board and several achieved three or more A* in all their chosen subjects including Hope Fomekong Zome, Owen Fryer, Monty Parmiter, Oliwia Rosciszewska, Florence Slezak and Ava Thomas-Richards.

“All Saints students leave us with far more than outstanding exam results; they take with them strong values, a commitment to making a positive difference and the ambition to succeed in whatever path they choose.

“We wish them every happiness and success for the future.”
