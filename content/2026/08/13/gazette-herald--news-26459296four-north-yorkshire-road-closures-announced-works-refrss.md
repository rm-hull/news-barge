North Yorkshire Council has announced four temporary road closures coming up.

Barney Lane, Flaxton, will be closed from August 18 to August 22 for disconnection works carried out by M Group Water on behalf of Yorkshire Water.

The maximum duration for this order is until November 18.

In Rillington, Low Moor Lane will close for four months for culvert repair works from September 1 to December 31.

The permitted period for this order is until September 18.

Heygate Bank, Rosedale Abbey, will be closed for seven months between August 31 and March 31 for the replacement of a cattle grid.

Access to the premises will be maintained throughout the closures.

A three-week closure will take place on the stretch from Nunnington to Ellerby Bridge for repairs to a bridge parapet for a temporary period of three weeks between September 1 and November 27.

The closures will be indicated by traffic signs, with alternative routes provided where appropriate.

For further information about each closure, visit North Yorkshire Council's roadworks map at [https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/roadworks-map.](https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/roadworks-map.)

Enquiries regarding the closure in Flaxton can be directed to M Group Water on behalf of Yorkshire Water on 0345 124 2424.

Enquiries regarding closures in Rillington, Rosedale Abbey and Nunnington can be directed to the North Yorkshire Council Customer Service Centre on 0300 131 2 131.
