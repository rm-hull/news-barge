# New allegation laid against double murder-accused Nick Reiner

- Nick Reiner, 32, has been indicted by a grand jury on murder charges over the deaths of his parents, actor-director Rob Reiner and Michele Singer Reiner, with a new special circumstance allegation of lying in wait.
- Rob Reiner, 78, and Michele Singer Reiner, 70, were found dead from multiple sharp force injuries at their Los Angeles home on 14 December.
- Nick Reiner pleaded not guilty on Wednesday and remains held without bail ahead of a pretrial hearing scheduled for 15 September.
- The Los Angeles County District Attorney is evaluating whether to seek the death penalty, taking into account all aggravating and mitigating circumstances.
- The grand jury indictment replaces initial charges filed on 16 December, allowing prosecutors to bypass California’s preliminary hearing process to expedite a trial.
