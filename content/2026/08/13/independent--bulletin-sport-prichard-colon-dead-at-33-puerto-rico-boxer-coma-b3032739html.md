# Prichard Colon: Boxer who spent 221 days in a coma after life-changing fight dies aged 33

- Former boxer Prichard Colon has passed away at the age of 33, 11 years after suffering a catastrophic brain injury during a controversial fight.
- Colon's father, Richard, confirmed his son's death in a Facebook post on Thursday, thanking supporters for their years of love and prayers.
- The Puerto Rican fighter sustained a subdural haematoma after receiving repeated illegal punches to the back of his head during a 2015 bout against Terrel Williams, subsequently falling into a 221-day coma.
- Following his emergence from the coma, Colon remained in a vegetative state and required computer assistance to communicate, receiving full-time care from his family until his death.
- Prior to his tragic 2015 contest, Colon was an undefeated professional prospect who held a record of 16 wins, including 13 knockouts.
