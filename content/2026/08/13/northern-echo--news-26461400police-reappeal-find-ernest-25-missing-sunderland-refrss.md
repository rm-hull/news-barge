Police are continuing to search for a man who has been missing for more than ten days.

Ernest, who is 25, was last seen in the Thorney Close Road area of Sunderland, near Farringdon, shortly before 11.30pm on Sunday, August 2.

He is described as 5ft 7in tall, of slim build, with mousey brown, short hair and a beard and was last seen wearing dark clothing, including cargo pants.

Northumbria Police said: "Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate him, and officers are now requesting anyone with information that could assist to come forward."

Anyone with information is asked to contact police, quoting reference number 26000416584.
