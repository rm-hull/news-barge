# What time do A-Level results come out? When you can collect your grades

- A-Level results are being released on Thursday 13 August for students in England, Wales and Northern Ireland; Scottish students have already received their SQA results separately, on 4 August.
- Results are released at **8am** online (students can log into their school or college’s online portal to see their grades) and by email for institutions that send digital copies.
- Those collecting their results from their school or college in person should check the institution’s opening times and take photo ID to collect a printed slip with their results.
- The Ucas hub updated around **8.15am** , confirming whether students had met their university offer.
- An overall picture of the national results will be resulted at **9.30am** .
