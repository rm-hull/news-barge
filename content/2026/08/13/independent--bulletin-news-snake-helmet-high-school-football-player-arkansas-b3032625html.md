# Venomous snake found in student’s helmet after he felt unusual movement in headgear

- A high school football player at Maumelle High School in Arkansas narrowly avoided injury after discovering a two-foot-long venomous cottonmouth snake concealed inside his helmet after warm-ups.
- The pit viper refused to leave the headgear on its own and was eventually removed by animal control officers who submerged the helmet in water and extracted the reptile using tongs.
- Animal services director Chris Davis noted that the student had worn the helmet for nearly an hour and was remarkably lucky not to have been bitten by the deadly snake.
- Officials believe the snake was likely seeking a dark place to digest a recent meal when it slithered into the helmet while the equipment was resting on the ground.
- The cottonmouth was safely released into a nearby wooded area, prompting the team's head coach to instruct all players to thoroughly inspect their athletic gear before future practices.
