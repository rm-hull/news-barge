# A-level results day: Where is the Earl of Wessex going to university?

- James, Earl of Wessex, is understood to have secured a place at the Royal Agricultural University in Cirencester after receiving his A-level results.
- The 18-year-old son of the Duke and Duchess of Edinburgh studied at Radley College in Oxfordshire, though his specific grades remain undisclosed.
- He is set to begin studying rural land and property management this autumn.
- His university entry coincides with a rise in UK A-level top grades, with 28.5 per cent of entries receiving an A or A* grade this year.
- Lady Louise Windsor, his elder sister, graduated from the University of St Andrews earlier this summer and plans to take a gap year.
