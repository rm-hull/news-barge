Christmas has come early for Terry’s White Chocolate Orange fans as the much-loved chocolate treat makes its return to supermarket shelves after four years.

The fan-favourite Terry’s White Chocolate Orange is back in stores from today, August 12, giving shoppers the chance to get their hands on the popular festive-style treat months before Christmas.

And there’s an added incentive for chocolate lovers who want to stock up.


Co-op has become the first retailer to stock the returning White Chocolate Orange, with the 145g treat launching at £2 as part of an introductory offer, down from its usual £2.50 price.

The chocolate orange is available across Co-op stores nationwide from today.

The return comes after years of demand from shoppers who have been calling for the white chocolate version to make a comeback.

Fans have been sharing their excitement online, with the relaunch announcement prompting a flurry of comments from shoppers eager to get their hands on one.

For anyone who can't wait until the Christmas chocolate aisle appears, the return means the white chocolate and orange combination can now be enjoyed much earlier than usual.

Jacob Calvert, Confectionery Buyer at Co-op, said: "We know shoppers have been eagerly waiting for the return of Terry's White Chocolate Orange, so we're excited to be the first shop this year to sell this fan favourite treat.

"If you’re after that white chocolate orange fix, and not up for waiting until Christmas, shoppers can pop to Co-op stores nationwide now and get their hands on it here first."

The Terry’s White Chocolate Orange 145g costs £2 at Co-op, with the 50p saving available as part of the launch promotion.
