A FIRST solo exhibition will go on display this September thanks to a bursary honouring a respected West Yorkshire artist.

Jenny Bratt was selected for the Joan Day Bursary Award by the South Square Centre in Thornton, Bradford.

The award has supported the creation of new work for her debut solo show, Here, Somehow, which opens at the gallery in September.

Alice Withers, director at South Square, said: "We’re delighted to honour Joan Day’s memory through this bursary.

"As an artist and educator, Joan was passionate about nurturing artistic development, and it feels particularly meaningful to support Jenny at this important stage in her practice.

"We’re very grateful to Joan’s family for enabling us to continue her legacy in this way – and, of course, congratulations to Jenny on this award.

"Jenny joins a fantastic list of Joan Day Bursary recipients who have exhibited with us over the years, and we’re thrilled to continue that tradition with her first solo exhibition.

"We hope audiences will come along in September to experience Here, Somehow and discover Jenny’s work for themselves."

  A painting by Jenny Bratt depicting everyday life in Armley, Leeds 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The exhibition features paintings inspired by Armley, Leeds.

It explores the people, places, and quiet moments that shape everyday life, capturing the beauty in overlooked domestic scenes.

The Joan Day Bursary Award was established in memory of Joan Day, a talented painter who lived and worked in West Yorkshire for much of her life.

The award supports emerging artists who use paint as a prominent feature of their practice and gives them the opportunity to exhibit new and recent work at South Square Centre.

Ms Bratt said: "I’m truly honoured to have been awarded the Joan Day Bursary Award by the team at South Square Centre.

"Painting has always been deeply personal to me and my way of processing the complexities of everyday life.

"Having started my artist journey in Bradford, the chance to exhibit at South Square is also a great opportunity to return with new work to what feels like my formative artistic homeland."

The exhibition opens at South Square Centre on Friday, September 4, from 6pm to 9pm, and runs until November 1.
