Durham County Council decided a number of planning applications this week.

Decisions covered changes to a former café and offices, school play equipment and garage demolition.

Here are four plans given the green light this week (August 24-28):

### Education use proposed for Station Road premises

Durham County Council approved a change of use at Shop 2, 3-6 Station Road, Consett, DH8 5RL.

The application covered the change from a ground-floor café and upper-floor offices to an education provision.

Plans were approved on August 28.

### Defibrillator and bleed kit planned for Chester-le-Street

An application for the installation of a combined defibrillator and bleed kit cabinet was approved by Durham County Council.

The cabinet will be attached to the external wall of Nationwide, 6 Front Street, Chester-le-Street.

The decision was issued on August 26.


### Timber playground equipment approved at Aycliffe school

Durham County Council approved plans to install timber playground equipment at Aycliffe Village Primary School, North Terrace, Aycliffe Village, Newton Aycliffe.

The decision was issued on August 28.

### Demolition of 10 garages in Spennymoor

Permission has been granted for the demolition of 10 garages at the garage block on St Davids Close, Spennymoor.

The application was a prior notification submission for demolition.

Durham County Council’s decision, issued on August 26, stated that prior approval was required and granted.
