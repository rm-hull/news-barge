A weather warning is still in force across North Yorkshire.

The Met Office has issued a Yellow level alert valid until 23:59 (11:59pm) tonight (Sunday 30th August)

As a thunderstorm passed over York there was lightning, very loud thunder and torrential rain which briefly overwhelmed some drains, flooding streets.

And part of Tower Street in York is closed after being damaged during the storm.

Around Malton and Pickering a number of homes have lost power. Northern Powergrid is aware of the issues and is trying to restore supply.

In York, Scarcroft Road and Scott Street drains failed to cope with the amount of rain and there was minor flooding briefly.

Many people said it was one of the most powerful storms they had seen in a long while with intense rain and cracks of thunder that made windows rattle.

The Met Office says: “Whilst some areas will miss the heaviest showers, where they do occur 10-15 mm of rain is possible in less than an hour, with 30-40 mm possible over several hours.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/eb5edd04-f027-4852-b012-abb260a44d67-1000x1333.jpg)

Even though the weather warning expires at midnight, forecasters say that heavy rain will continue to track across North and East Yorkshire overnight.

Bank Holiday Monday will see a bit of sunshine as well as the odd shower.
