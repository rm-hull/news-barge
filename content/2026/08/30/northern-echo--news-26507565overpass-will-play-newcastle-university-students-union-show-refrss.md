Rising indie band Overpass have told fans they are “so excited” to perform in Newcastle after playing to a huge crowd at Leeds Festival.

The Birmingham band took to the stage at the festival on Saturday afternoon, performing in front of a packed crowd as they continue to build momentum across the UK.

Now, the group are heading to Newcastle for an intimate show at Newcastle University Students’ Union on Thursday, 29th October.

The gig is already completely sold out, with demand for tickets highlighting the band's growing popularity.

Fans who missed out on tickets can still join the waiting list in the hope that more become available ahead of the show.

Overpass have a strong connection with the North East, having previously played The Cluny in Newcastle and Independent Sunderland.

They also held a sold-out HMV signing in the region in May to celebrate their new album, giving them another opportunity to meet their growing North East fanbase.

The band have previously spoken about their love for playing in the region, with North East crowds earning a reputation among the group as some of their favourite audiences.

“We absolutely love coming up to the North East – the crowds are always incredible. There’s just something different about playing up there. Newcastle and Sunderland always bring such a brilliant energy, and we genuinely can’t wait to be back,” the band said.

Overpass have been making a name for themselves with their infectious indie-rock sound and energetic live performances, and their appearance at Leeds Festival has given their growing fanbase another chance to see them live.

Following their huge festival performance, the band are now looking ahead to their Newcastle show and have told fans they are “so excited” to return to the city.

For those who managed to get tickets, the wait is now on until October 29.

And for anyone who missed out, the waiting list could provide one final chance to see Overpass up close when they arrive in Newcastle this autumn.
