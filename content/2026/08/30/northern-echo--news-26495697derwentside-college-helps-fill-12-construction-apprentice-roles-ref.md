A partnership in the North East is already helping young people take their first steps into one of the region’s most in-demand industries, after a successful recruitment drive led to a wave of new apprenticeship opportunities.

Derwentside College and Hewitson Limited joined forces to fill eight groundworker apprenticeship roles, but after the process began, the company was so impressed with the candidates that it decided to take on 12 instead.

Nicola Raine, assistant principal for business development and technical education at Derwentside College, said: "We're proud to have led the recruitment process on behalf of Hewitson Limited and to have connected 12 talented young people with career opportunities in a sector where skills are in high demand.

"This partnership demonstrates the positive impact that employers and colleges can achieve together."

Derwentside College designed and delivered a bespoke recruitment campaign after Hewitson identified a need for groundworker apprentices to support its ongoing growth.

The college managed the entire process, from promoting the roles to coordinating a recruitment day for candidates from across the North East.

The initial plan was to fill eight positions, but the calibre of applicants led Hewitson to create four additional roles.

All 12 apprentices will now begin a Level 2 Groundworker Apprenticeship.

Paul Mathison, director of construction at Hewitson Limited, said: "Supporting young people into the construction industry is incredibly important to us.

"We want to give individuals the opportunity to build rewarding careers, develop valuable skills, and realise their potential.

"The recruitment process delivered by Derwentside College introduced us to some outstanding candidates, and we're looking forward to supporting them throughout their apprenticeship journey and beyond."

Hewitson Limited has already provided the apprentices with preparatory training and development activities to help them transition into the workplace.

The collaboration highlights the value of employer-education partnerships in addressing skills shortages and opening up career pathways for young people.

By combining Hewitson's investment in future talent with Derwentside College’s expertise in large-scale recruitment, the scheme is helping to strengthen the region’s construction workforce.

The success of the programme also shows how focused recruitment and high-quality apprenticeships can drive economic growth.

It supports the development of future skills and helps build a sustainable talent pipeline for industries facing workforce pressures.

As the 12 apprentices begin their careers, the partnership serves as a "powerful example" of how targeted collaboration can create lasting opportunities for young people while meeting the needs of employers.
