The Yorkshire Dales is a hugely popular area in the UK that is loved by hikers thanks to its many walking trails.

If you are looking for a Yorkshire Dales walk that combines quaint pubs with “impressive” views, you might want to try the Buckden Pike Circular walk.

The walk is 15km long and is described as being quite hard but will offer you rewarding sights along the way.

The walk will take around five and a half to six hours to complete but can be broken up with a stop in the village of Starbotton.


## What is the Buckden Pike Circular walk like?

The trail starts in Buckden and will take you along Buckden Rack past the gravel pit and through Rakes Wood.

You will then walk up to the top of Buckden Pike, which features a summit seat to allow hikers to rest for a bit and enjoy the views.

You will see a memorial cross to commemorate a Wellington Bomber crash that happened in 1942.

The memorial features a bronze fox head to remember the lone survivor who followed a fox’s footprints into the valley to find help.

During the walk, you might want to stop off at Starbotton where you can visit the Fox & Hounds Inn.

At the pub, you can enjoy a range of cask ales from breweries around the Yorkshire Dales.

The pub also offers home-cooked food such as fish and chips, pies, and burgers, as well as a lunch menu with sandwiches for those wanting something a little lighter.

You will end the walk back in Buckden, where you can book a room at the Buck Inn or stay at one of the campsites in the area.


## What do hikers say about the Buckden Pike Circular walk?

The trail has a rating of 4.6 out of 5 on AllTrails, with 129 hikers leaving reviews about their experience.

One person wrote: “Lovely walk- glad I read the comments about the bogs at the top.

“Gaiters and good boots and it’s no problem.

“Lots of livestock so dogs need to be well trained- but stunning views and easy to follow.”

Another person said: “Fabulous ascent up a decent track.

"Slightly boggy but ok.

“Coming off Buckden Pike was very boggy but actually a really good laugh.

“Last part returning along the river from Starbottom was very easy going.”
