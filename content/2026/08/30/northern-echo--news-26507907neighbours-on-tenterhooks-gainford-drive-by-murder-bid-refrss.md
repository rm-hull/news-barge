Neighbours have spoken of their fear after a man was shot in "drive-by murder bid" on their usually quiet street.

Residents say typically "nothing really happens" on Balmer Hill in Gainford, but on Saturday (August 29) night 999 crews swarmed the street on blue lights after a drive-by shooting.

Two gunshots were fired at a detached house on the cul-de-sac - now at the centre of a cordon and being scoured by forensic officers - smashing the front window and leaving a hole in the door.

  CSI officers at the scene on Balmer Hill, Gainford. 

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

A man, 46, was left with birdshot injuries to his abdomen. He remains in a critical but stable condition in hospital.

Two people, a man aged 20 and a 16-year-old boy, were arrested on suspicion of attempted murder and remain in custody. The teenager was also arrested on suspicion of theft, failing to stop and driving without a licence.

Neighbours, who did not want to be named, told The Northern Echo they heard two loud 'pops' around 8.10pm.

  CSI officers at the scene on Balmer Hill, Gainford. 

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

"I was just sat watching the TV," they said. "They were talking about Newcastle on Sky Sports News, then we heard like a pop.

"At first I thought it was a car backfiring. We jumped.

"And then there was another pop, and it was a case of 'What the heck was that?'

"Then the next thing we saw was a red soft-top car just go down the road."

  CSI officers at the scene on Balmer Hill, Gainford. 

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

"After the pops, I looked along and we saw all the glass outside, and the front windows were gone.

"It is something you expect to see in movies or on television programmes.

  CSI officers at the scene on Balmer Hill, Gainford. 

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

"You don't expect to see it in a little village, in your own of street.

"Especially what's going on in Middlesbrough at the moment, you think 'what is it?'. I know my anxiety levels have gone up.

"Everybody's on tenterhooks, but the police have been brilliant and it does give you reasurance to see them all here."

They described seeing armed officers arrive with rifles as the street became "lit up" with blue lights.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

"We saw about eight police cars all with flashing lights and it was chaos," another neighbour said.

Mr Jones, a resident of more than 20 years, added: “Nothing ever happens here.

“A couple of weeks ago some milk was pinched from a doorstep, and there was hell on."

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Police officers were seen at the cordon surrounding the house on Sunday (August 30) and CSI teams in white overalls were working inside and outside the home, filling large brown evidence bags.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A cordon could be in place until Tuesday, residents say they have been told, as police continue to examine the scene.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "An investigation has been launched after a man was shot in Gainford last night.

"Emergency services were called to an address in Balmer Hill just after 8.10pm following reports that suspects had driven to a house and fired at it from their car before making off.

"A 46-year-old man suffered birdshot injuries to his abdomen and was taken to hospital. His condition has been described as critical but stable.

"No one else at the address was injured and officers remain at the scene today.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

"A 20-year-old man and a 16-year-old boy have been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder.

"The boy has also been arrested on suspicion of theft, failing to stop and driving without a licence. They both remain in custody."

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson added: "We were called to an incident at a private address in Gainford, Darlington at 8.10pm on Saturday, August 29.
