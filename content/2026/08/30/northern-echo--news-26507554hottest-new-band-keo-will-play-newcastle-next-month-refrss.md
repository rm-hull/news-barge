Hottest new band KEO have told fans they “can’t wait” to kick off their intimate album launch tour next month in Newcastle.

The rising group, performed at Leeds Festival on Saturday afternoon, are gearing up for the release of their highly anticipated debut album, Put A Smile On For Me.

The album is due to be released on Friday 25th September, with KEO set to celebrate its arrival with a run of intimate live shows including a date in Newcastle.

  KEO performing at Leeds Festival 2026 

*(Image: LIAM RUSSELL)*

Speaking to fans at Leeds Festival, the band revealed just how excited they are to launch the album and take it on the road.

Their upcoming Newcastle show follows the success of their first headline tour, which proved hugely popular with fans and sold out within minutes.

The intimate dates are expected to give fans the chance to see KEO up close and personal as they perform tracks from the new record.

Put A Smile On For Me marks the latest chapter for the band, building on the momentum generated by their previous releases and their growing reputation as one of the UK's hottest new acts.

Tickets for the show were available exclusively through Newcastle record store Reflex, but have now sold-out.

And after their appearance at Leeds Festival, KEO will be hoping to carry that festival momentum straight into the album's release and their intimate tour.

Their date in Newcastle promises to be an unmissable chance to see KEO at an exciting point in their career before the venues get bigger.
