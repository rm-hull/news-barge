**Alfie Joey gives parents and grandparents some much needed summer holiday inspiration!**

It is that time of the summer holidays when children start the dreaded chant: “I’m bored… I’m bored!”

There was a brilliant TV programme when I was younger called ‘Why Don’t You?’

It ran from the mid-1970s to the mid-1990s, and its concept was like so many great ideas, unbelievably simple. The young presenters responded to letters from viewers suggesting fun things to do – from arts and crafts to days out and creative adventures. It doesn’t get simpler than that.

The full title, as heard in the opening sequence, was: “Why Don’t You Just Switch Off Your Television Set and Go and Do Something Less Boring Instead?”

So, if you are hearing the “I’m bored” mantra from the youngsters in your life, here are five ‘Why Don’t You?’ ideas right here in our brilliant North East.

  And I’ve specifically tried to find things that are **FREE**. My great pal at BBC Newcastle, Gilly Hope (still presenting, Saturday and Sunday breakfast), used to have a fab feature called ‘No Money Fun’, so I’m unashamedly stealing the idea and putting it to very good use!

  **Why don’t you** …explore Durham Cathedral?

Durham Cathedral has family activities running through the summer, with arts, crafts and of course plenty to explore and discover. Entry to the cathedral itself is free, though a donation is encouraged’. History on our doorstep.

Add Palace Green, the cathedral precincts and a stroll along the riverside and you’ve got the makings of a whole free family day in Durham.

  **Why don’t you**…. go to South Shields?

A brand-new exhibition, Mignard: The Power of Posing, opens at South Shields Museum & Art Gallery this weekend – and entry is free.

The star attraction is Pierre Mignard’s magnificent The Marquise de Seignelay, featuring a French noblewoman and her children dressed as figures from classical mythology.

Her son appears as the Greek hero Achilles. If your youngsters have enjoyed the current blockbuster The Odyssey, this may be the bait to expand their cultural and classical knowledge even further! (This suggestion was really to impress my old Classics lecturer, Fr. Cornforth, now parish priest in St. Mary’s Newton Aycliffe!! Morning Fr. Tony!)

  **Why don’t you...** simply get outside?

This is to specifically pay homage to the spirit of TV’s ‘Why Don’t You? ‘. The North East is full of brilliant free places to adventure. Pack some sandwiches, leave the devices at home for an hour and go for a walk, or discover somewhere near you where you’ve never been before.

So, come on gang – go out and do something less boring instead!

  **Why don't you...** Get your free ticket to Hopetown Darlington that covers entry to North Road Station Museum, the Goods Shed, Clocktower Café, Darlington Locomotive Works Viewing Gallery.

  **Why don't you...** visit Seven Stories National Centre For Children’s Books, Ouseburn, Newcastle. Perfect for young bookworms and writers.
