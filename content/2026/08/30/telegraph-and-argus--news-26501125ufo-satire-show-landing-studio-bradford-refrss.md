A sci-fi satire show arrives in Bradford this autumn.

Comedian Rosie Holt will bring her latest show, The Illegal Aliens Have Landed!, to The Studio on October 16.

Holt’s MP alter ego faces an unusual crisis when UFOs arrive in the UK.

Ms Holt started her satirical videos, Woman Who..., during the 2020 COVID-19 lockdown.

The performance lasts 80 minutes.

Tickets are priced at £23.

Bradford Theatres' season preview also lists Holt among the comedy performers scheduled for King's Hall.
