A North Yorkshire pizza delivery company has gone into liquidation owing more than £30,000.

Chico’s Harrogate Ltd was formed in 2019 and was based at 4 Commercial Street, Harrogate. Companies House lists its sole director as Fuat Gundogan.

The now-closed business entered liquidation on July 9 and Qimzen Advisory of Birmingham subsequently became the administrator.

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs says the business had no assets available for distribution to creditors.

There were no preferential creditors- those who are paid first- but HMRC was a secondary preference creditor – those who are paid next- and was owed £11,308.

The report said there were unsecured creditors who were owed £21,565 in total. They were made up of seven trade and expense creditors owed a total of £11,065, plus banks and financial institutions owed £10,500.

This meant that Chico’s Harrogate Ltd had outstanding debts of £32,873. With the business having issued and shared up capital of £100, this meant the business had an outstanding deficit of £32,973 owed.
