The Malton and Norton Theatre Group has announced a new pantomime production of Robin Hood next year.

Ahead of the show, the production team, which has seen a complete change in crew, is inviting locals to a 'getting to know you' day at Kirkham Henry Studios on Sunday, September 6, at 4.30pm.

This event will allow people to meet the crew and participate in a read and sing-through of the script.

Auditions for principal roles will be held on Sunday, September 13, at the studio from 4.30pm.
