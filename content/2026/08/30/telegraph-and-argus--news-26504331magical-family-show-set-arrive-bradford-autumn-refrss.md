A TOURING family theatre show described as magical is set to stop in Bradford this October.

The Magic Stick, created by Ri Ri’s Dance Academy, will be performed at Bradford Arts Centre on October 28 as part of a wider northern tour.

Aimed at children aged six to 12 and their families, the show combines Indian dance, storytelling, music, and stage magic.

Rushi Munshi, producer, said: "The Magic Stick is our biggest theatre tour to date, and we're incredibly excited to share this story with families across the North.

"At its heart, this production is about showing children that kindness is powerful, magical and something we can all choose every single day.

"We hope audiences leave inspired, smiling and ready to spread a little more kindness in their own communities."

The story follows eight-year-old Diya, who is gifted a magic stick that helps her see acts of kindness come to life.

Guided by Mr Talking Tree, Diya journeys through the Utsav (Kindness) Festival, meeting colourful characters along the way.

The 45-minute show will be followed by a live Q&A.

Tickets are available through each venue’s box office or by visiting ririsdanceacademy.com.
