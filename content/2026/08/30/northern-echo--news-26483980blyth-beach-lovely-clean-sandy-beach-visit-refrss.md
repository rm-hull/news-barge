If you want to enjoy the last few sunny days England has left before autumn, you might be thinking of going on a last-minute trip to the beach.

Luckily, the North East has plenty of beautiful beaches to choose from.

Blyth beach has been described as “lovely” and “clean” by visitors and is a popular place for families.

The sandy beach is backed by vibrant beach huts and a promenade that is perfect for a stroll along the coastline.


## What is Blyth beach like?

The golden sandy beach runs for miles and is ideal for a long walk where you might spot some dolphins and seals if you are lucky.

The beach has sand dunes along the back, which is a designated area of Special Nature Conservation, making it great for those wanting to see some wildlife.

You can either take a paddle in the sea or get involved in some surfing at the surf school on the beach.

There is also a zone for watersports like jet skiing and yachting.

After the beach, you might want to take a trip to the famous Coastline for some of the best fish and chips in the UK.

The fish and chip shop not only offers a range of different fish options but also has pizzas, chicken and daily specials.

Coastline also has loads of gelato flavours to choose from to finish off your trip to Blyth.
