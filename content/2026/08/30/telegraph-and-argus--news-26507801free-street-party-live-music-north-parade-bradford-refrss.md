Bradford’s North Parade Street Party returned today (August 30) running from 12pm to 9pm.

It promises nine hours of live entertainment and street food with plenty of pubs to nip into should the weather turn.

The T&A popped down to have a walk round at about 2pm this afternoon.

  The annual North Parade street party. 

*(Image: T&A)*

## 'A free music festival in Bradford city centre'

James Brunskill, owner of Boar and Fable and part of the organising team, previously told the T&A: "A free music festival in Bradford city centre – who else is doing that?"

He explained that the event is completely and proudly independently run.

Now in its 12th year, the event has become a popular fixture of the August Bank Holiday, and is said to draw thousands of visitors to Bradford each year.

  The annual North Parade street party. 

*(Image: T&A)*

As photographs illustrate, it was not overly busy when we visited, but that is likely due to the poor weather and it still being very early in the day.

A lot of punters appeared to be keeping warm and dry inside the venue locations - but there's plenty of outdoor seating if preferred.

We're sure things will liven up as the afternoon progresses!

  The annual North Parade street party. 

*(Image: T&A)*

This year’s line-up includes Bradford band The Fourth Act, Natural Rhythm, the Richard Harris Experience, Rubber Kazoo, Austin Haddock and Boki.

Food is available from vendors including Rumshackalack, Grade 2 sports bar, Harvana Lounge and L&G Bar and Lounge.

Mr Brunskill said the event "offers something different" and hopes people will still come out to enjoy the festivities despite the "dreadful weather" expected for the weekend.

He said the event should "avoid the worst of it."

  The annual North Parade street party. 

*(Image: T&A)*

David Jared, who is up visiting family this weekend, told the T&A: "My family were coming along so I fancied coming along too to see what it's all about.

"What beautiful architecture on this street. It's a shame the sun isn't shining, but there's plenty of places to go inside and nice beers.

"I think it's great to have a free event like this for a city, championing independent businesses and musicians."

  The annual North Parade street party. 

*(Image: T&A)*

A Bradford resident said: "I think it's important to host these community events and get everyone talking, supporting local businesses.

"Less and less people are visiting the high street, city centres, so we need to give them a reason to come out - and this is it!"

Entry is free and the street party is open to all ages.

Let's hope the rain holds off!
