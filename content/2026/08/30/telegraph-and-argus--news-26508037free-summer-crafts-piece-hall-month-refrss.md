Children can get creative this month with free craft sessions.

The drop-in workshops are being held at The Piece Hall, in Halifax, every Tuesday and Thursday from 11am to 2pm throughout the summer holidays until September 16, supported by a team of volunteers.

"From 11-2, our wonderful team of volunteers will be on hand to help you create some exciting summer crafts."

Families are invited to look out for the Art Cart in the courtyard or visit the Learning Studio on the top floor.
