Leeds Festival is in full swing, with thousands of music fans heading to Bramham Park for one of the biggest weekends of the year.

And while plenty of festival-goers will be watching their favourite acts on stage, some of the artists appearing at Leeds this weekend will be back in the North East soon.

From rising indie bands to some of the biggest names on the bill, there are plenty of chances to see Leeds Festival favourites in a much smaller venue.

Here are some of the acts you can catch in the North East after Leeds Festival:

## ARKAYLA – Sunderland Independent – September 25

Manchester indie favourites ARKAYLA are heading to Sunderland just weeks after opening Leeds Festival's main stage on Thursday.

They'll play Independent in Sunderland on September 25.

## KEO – Newcastle Boiler Shop – September 22

North East favourites KEO are also set for an intimate album show at Newcastle's Boiler Shop on September 22.

The band are performing at Leeds on Saturday, making their Newcastle date a perfect chance to see them again in a more intimate setting.

## Jamie Webster – Newcastle NX – September 19

Liverpool singer-songwriter Jamie Webster brings his hugely popular live show to Newcastle's NX on September 19.

He's one of the weekend's big names and is known for his lively crowds.

## Unpeople – O2 City Hall Newcastle – October 4

Rockers Unpeople will head to Newcastle's O2 City Hall on October 4.

The band are another Leeds act offering fans the chance to swap the festival fields for a proper North East venue.

## Locky – Newcastle Digital – October 9

Leeds performer Locky is also heading north, with a Newcastle Digital show on October 9.

He appears at Leeds Festival on Saturday.

## Gurriers – Newcastle University Students' Union – October 15

Irish post-punk outfit Gurriers will play Newcastle University Students' Union on October 15.

Their intense live show should translate perfectly from the festival circuit to the smaller Newcastle venue.

## Overpass – Newcastle University Students' Union – October 29

Birmingham indie band Overpass are another Leeds favourite heading to Newcastle.

They'll play Newcastle University Students' Union on October 29 after their Leeds Festival appearance.

## Skye Newman – O2 City Hall Newcastle – November 10

Skye Newman, who performs at Leeds on Saturday, will return to the North East for a show at O2 City Hall Newcastle on November 10.

## East End Dubs – NX Newcastle – November 13

Electronic music fans can catch East End Dubs at NX Newcastle on November 13.

The DJ and producer appears on the Leeds Festival Saturday line-up.

## Westside Cowboy – Newcastle University Students' Union – November 25

Rising indie stars Westside Cowboy will be back in Newcastle on November 25 for a show at Newcastle University Students' Union.

They appear at Leeds on Friday.

## The North – Newcastle Think Tank – November 26

The North will also return to Newcastle later this year, with a show at Think Tank on November 26.

## Dylan John Thomas – Newcastle NX – December 4

Scottish singer-songwriter Dylan John Thomas heads to Newcastle's NX on December 4.

It's another chance for festival fans to catch the artist away from the huge crowds at Bramham Park.

## Holly Humberstone – Newcastle NX – January 23, 2027

And if you don't mind waiting until the new year, Holly Humberstone will bring her show to Newcastle's NX on January 23, 2027.

She is part of Friday's Leeds Festival line-up and will be one of the more anticipated North East shows of the winter.

## The Lilacs – Newcastle Digital – March 5, 2027

And finally, The Lilacs will return to Newcastle for a show at Digital on March 5, 2027.

So if you've discovered a new favourite at Leeds Festival this weekend or missed out on tickets, there are plenty of opportunities to see them again and this time, you won't have to battle through a muddy field to get to the front.
