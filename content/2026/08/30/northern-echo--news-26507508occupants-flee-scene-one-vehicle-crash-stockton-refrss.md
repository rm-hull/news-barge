Police are trying to track down those inside a vehicle that crashed on a roundabout in Teesside then fled.

Emergency services were called to Durham Road in Stockton around 12.35am this morning (Sunday, August 30), after a single-vehicle crash.

Cleveland Police say that the occupants of the vehicle had fled the scene before officers arrived.

The vehicle was seized, and officers are appealing for witnesses.

A Cleveland Police spokesperson said: "The occupant(s) had left the scene before police arrival, and the car was seized.

"Anyone with information is urged to contact us on 101, quoting reference number 173877."
