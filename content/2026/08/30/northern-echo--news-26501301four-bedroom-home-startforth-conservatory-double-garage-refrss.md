FROM a wealth of immaculately presented accommodation that combines original Victorian features with a contemporary aesthetic to grounds that extend to approximately 1.8 acres and a location from where its elevated position means it enjoys spectacular and far-reaching views over Barnard Castle and the countryside beyond, East House has plenty to appeal to buyers who are in the market for a home that stands out from the crowd.

The property’s front door opens into an entrance porch with original tiled floor, and from there into the reception hall with period details including coving to the ceiling plus both a picture and dado rail.

Accessed off the hall are East House’s three principal reception rooms: the sitting room with a sash window framing views over Barnard Castle, and with wooden flooring and an open grate fireplace; the drawing room which shares the same views as the sitting room, and the living/dining room.

A generous double space, this has a bay window with window seat, ornate coving and an open fireplace with a carved surround and an inset cast-iron stove in the living room, alongside which is the dining area with glazed double doors that open into a substantial conservatory.

One of many highlights of East House, this light-filled space with tiled floor is large enough to accommodate both a substantial dining area and a seating area, which benefit from the beautiful view over the garden and patio area as well as a wonderful flow between the indoor and outdoor areas.

A door from the conservatory – and another from the hall – leads into the kitchen, which is home to a range of fitted units incorporating rolled edges, a fireplace with an inset AGA, integrated appliances and a hidden walk-in pantry.

Completing the ground floor are a cloakroom and a useful utility room that’s home to a range of matching units incorporating a gas hob and electric oven.

Upstairs are a family shower room, a separate WC and four bedrooms.

These include the dual-aspect master bedroom with a cast-iron feature fireplace with marble surround, coving to the ceiling and an open doorway to a luxurious en-suite bathroom.

There is also a guest bedroom with stunning views and an en-suite bathroom.

If, like Oliver Twist, you’re looking for more, then more you shall have in the form of a detached former coach house.

A versatile addition to East House, this offers the purchaser the ability to accommodate family or guests or to generate a rental income should they so wish and subject to obtaining any necessary permissions.

The coach house’s ground floor accommodation comprises an entrance hall with doors to the sitting room and dining room, which has an open archway leading to the fitted kitchen.

Upstairs are a modern family bathroom and two bedrooms.

East House is set within extensive grounds that are home to beautifully maintained mature gardens, laid mainly to lawn with planted borders, mature trees, a large pond, a summerhouse and greenhouse and a vegetable patch, with a driveway providing off-street parking for several vehicles as well as access to a double garage.

There is also a timber-built single stable and approximately one acre of grassland, divided into similar-sized fenced grass paddocks.

Directly to the rear of the property is a large patio area with both paved and gravelled areas, a sunken garden converted from a swimming pool, planted borders, walled boundaries and two useful outbuildings.
