A charity is inviting the public to help shape its future community work.

Children in Distress, the organisation behind the charity shop in Kirkbymoorside, is introducing a new series of Community Connections drop-in events to hear directly from local residents.

The drop-ins will take place on the first Wednesday of each month from 10am to 1pm, starting in September and running through November.

Tracy Russell, manager of the Kirkbymoorside shop, said: "I've worked in our Kirkbymoorside shop for the past four and a half years, but the shop has been part of the town for much longer than that.

"Over the years, we've got to know lots of local people and it's lovely being part of the community.

"We'd really like to hear from more people about Kirkbymoorside – what you like about the town, what you think it needs and what you'd like to see more of.

"So, if you're passing, come in for a chat.

"Whether you're a regular customer or have never been in before, we'd love to meet you and hear what you think."

The charity hopes that the informal events will help it better understand local priorities, explore new collaborations and identify opportunities for future partnerships.

Children in Distress has a long history of supporting children, young people, and communities in both Romania and the UK.

The charity has recently funded projects for the Romanian diaspora in Hull and now wants to explore how it can make a positive impact in other Yorkshire communities, starting with Kirkbymoorside.

The charity shop is already a valued part of the community, with many residents volunteering their time.

Volunteering has helped give young people skills for employment and everyone, including retired volunteers, has built friendships and enjoyed the opportunity to give something back.

Children in Distress is keen to hear a broad range of views from the community.

The feedback gathered during the drop-in sessions will help shape the charity's approach to engagement in the area, which could include partnerships with other organisations, new volunteering roles, or identifying funding opportunities.

The drop-ins will take place on September 2, October 7, and November 4 at the charity shop.

More information about the charity is available at childrenindistress.org.
