A housing association grant is helping to bring power to the people in a North Yorkshire village.

Previously the village green Christmas tree at Hackforth, near Bedale, has been lit up using solar and battery powered decorations, but results have often left villagers in the dark.

Now a £500 Broadacres grant, through its Community and Sustainability Fund, will help pay for a mains power connection to boost community events at Christmas and throughout the year.

North Yorkshire Parish Councillor Graeme Dodd said the reliable power source will help efforts to combat isolation.

“The tree is generously donated by a local family but previously the lights have proved a bit of a disaster,” said Cllr Dodd.

“With a reliable power source, people can organise a grand switch on and carols round the tree and then socialise back at the village hall or the local pub.”

The village is mentioned in the Domesday Book in 1086 and Cllr Dodd is the fourth generation of his family linked with the village hall.

He said the power source will also be useful for other community events including the annual village fete, barbeques and national celebrations.

“These are important events for bringing the community together and also for raising funds to support the village hall and local charities,” said Cllr Dodd.
