A County Durham café run by the same family for more than a century has closed its doors for the final time.

Valentes Café, on Church Street in Seaham, served its last customers on Saturday (August 29) bringing an end to 106 years of family trading in the town.

The business was founded in 1920 by Frank and Carmela Valente and passed down through the generations to their granddaughters Sue Duggan and Maria Blain.

Speaking on the café’s final day, she said: “We are all very sad.

"It is the end of an era.

“The customers were all very sad, but they were laughing and saying, ‘Don’t worry, we will all just come to your house instead of the shop’, so I think we will get some knocks on the door.”

  Valentes was opened in 1920. 

*(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

Valentes had become a familiar part of Church Street over its 106-year history, welcoming generations for breakfasts, meals and coffee.

Sue added: “I would really like to thank my staff who have all been lovely.

“I would like to thank all the customers who are more like family really.”

  Customers flocked to the site to have one last lunch.

The Church Street premises was placed on the market before the café’s closure, and Sue said a buyer ws found within two weeks.

Plans for the site have not yet been made public.

While the building is expected to begin a new chapter under different ownership, Saturday marked the end of Valentes’ long family connection with Seaham.
