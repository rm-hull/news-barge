Beer lovers are set to raise a glass for the return of a popular festival.

The Leeds International Beer Festival will take place at Kirkstall Abbey from September 3 to 6, celebrating its 14th year with UK and international craft beers, street food, and live music.

Organisers said: "For the latest news and updates about the Leeds International Beer Festival, including brewery announcements, dog friendly sessions and the ever-popular Family Day please visit leedsbeer.com or follow us on Instagram and on Facebook."
