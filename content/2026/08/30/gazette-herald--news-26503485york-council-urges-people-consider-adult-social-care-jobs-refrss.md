A fresh call has been issued to have people consider a career in adult social care.

City of York Council is encouraging people to explore opportunities in adult social care, whether they’re considering a career change, starting out in the workforce, or already working in care.

The roles support residents to stay well, independent, and connected to their communities.

Councillor Lucy Steels-Walshaw, Executive Member for Health, Wellbeing and Adult Social Care at City of York Council, said: "Every day, our teams support people across York to stay independent, feel connected to their communities, and live the lives they want.

"It’s meaningful work, and many colleagues tell us that supporting people in this way is one of the most rewarding careers they’ve had."

The council is looking for people from a wide range of backgrounds, skills, and experiences to join the service.

Councillor Steels-Walshaw added: "York is looking for people who care about others and want to make a positive difference.

"Whether you’ve worked in support services for years, are thinking about a change in direction, or are just starting out in employment, there’s a range of career opportunities that will help you develop new skills, grow in confidence and offer great job satisfaction."
