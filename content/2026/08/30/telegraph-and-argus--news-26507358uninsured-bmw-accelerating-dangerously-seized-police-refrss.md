A driver has been reported to court after being stopped by police in Bradford.

Officers from West Yorkshire Police said the BMW was seen accelerating hard and repeatedly closing the gap with the car in front during patrols in the Bradford City area.

The driver was reported for driving without insurance and without due care and attention.

A police spokesperson said: "During some patrols of West Yorkshire Police - Bradford City area we sighted this BMW on Leeds Road driving in a manner that caught our attention, hanging back to leave a gap before accelerating hard to catch up with the car in front.

"We saw this happen three times before deciding enough was enough.

"On speaking with the driver, the matter of insurance reared its head.

"After going around the proverbial houses for a while, no legitimate insurance policy could be identified.

  "Driver reported to court for no insurance and driving without due care and attention, and vehicle seized."
