Police received reports of shots being fired on Smithies Lane in Barnsley

**A man has been arrested on suspicion of firearm offences after a gun was fired in Barnsley.** 

South Yorkshire Police was called to Smithies Lane at about 22:05 BST on Saturday to reports of gunshots, with a man allegedly threatened during the incident.

No injuries were reported, with a 39-year-old man later arrested in Harrogate, North Yorkshire. He currently remains in custody, the force said.

Urging anyone with information to come forward, Det Insp Joanne Chambers said: "When a gun is fired the consequences can be devastating.

"Thankfully no one was injured as a result of this incident, but next time this might not be the case."

She added: "Our communities should not be put at risk because of the reckless actions of those willing to pull a trigger and place innocent lives in potential danger."
