A TASTE of Sri Lanka and the Philippines has arrived at a local market.

Shipley Market has welcomed two new food traders to its growing list of international cuisine offerings: Savoury Ceylon and Marilou Armada.

Savoury Ceylon, a family-run business operated by a husband-and-wife team originally from Sri Lanka, will serve authentic street food every Friday and Saturday from 9am to 4pm.

Diyuni Wisidagamage Don from Savoury Ceylon said: "We're thrilled to bring the flavours of Sri Lanka to Shipley.

"Food is a big part of our culture and we're passionate about sharing our authentic recipes.

"Whether someone is already familiar with Sri Lankan cuisine or trying it for the first time, we look forward to welcoming them to our stall and introducing them to some of our favourite dishes."

  Savoury Ceylon 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Their menu includes kottu roti, a popular street food made from chopped flatbread, vegetables and spices, as well as Sri Lankan hoppers—bowl-shaped pancakes made from fermented rice flour and coconut milk—and coconut roti served with sambal or curry.

The stall also offers crispy savoury rolls.

Marilou Armada will be serving Filipino specialities every Saturday from 9am to 4pm.

Ms Armada said: "I'm excited to join the fantastic community of traders at Shipley Market and share our Filipino food with shoppers.

"Filipino cuisine is all about bringing people together over great food, and I hope visitors will enjoy discovering something different, whether they're picking up freshly baked breads or a traditional noodle dish."

Her stall features freshly baked sweet and savoury Filipino breads, along with tasty noodle dishes.

Councillor Ian Walker, Bradford Council’s Executive Member for Neighbourhoods and Communities, said: "We're delighted to welcome these two new traders to Shipley Market and to see even more international flavours being added to our vibrant market offer.

"Savoury Ceylon and Marilou Armada join a fantastic community of traders at Shipley Market, giving customers even more reasons to visit, shop locally and discover something new while supporting local independent businesses."

The market already boasts a wide variety of stalls selling fresh produce, meats, fish, baked goods and household essentials, as well as crafts, gifts and international produce.

With the addition of Sri Lankan and Filipino cuisine, visitors now have even more reason to explore the market and support independent businesses.

More information about Bradford Markets and upcoming events can be found at www.bradfordmarkets.com.
