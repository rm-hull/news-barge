A machete teen, killer and drug dealer caught via a police drone are among those locked up in August.

These six people have been taken off the streets for their crimes.

Find out more about each below.

### Machete teenager

A teenager has been locked up after he was caught with a machete when a car rolled during a police chase in Darlington.

Rio Jamison was the passenger in a black Kia Sportage which was used to ram into a Nissan Qashqai parked outside a woman's home.

The woman’s car was pushed into her house in Darlington and she blamed the incident on her grandson’s £5,000 drug debt, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Charlie Thompson, prosecuting, said the Kia drove away before police spotted the vehicle and a chase ensued which ended on Tornado Way when the driver lost control of the vehicle.

“It is worth pointing out that the defendant was not the driver,” he said. “The chase has been dangerous with the Sportage driving well in excess of the speed limit before coming to an end when the car flipped over and rolled through a hedge."

  Rio Jamison 

*(Image: Durham Constabulary)*

Jamison, of Kildale Court, North Ormesby, Middlesbrough, pleaded guilty to possession of a bladed article on January 6 this year and theft and battery from December 28 last year.

Chris Baker, mitigating, said his client has been held on remand since his arrest on January 6.

Recorder Ayesha Smart sentenced Jamison to a total of ten months in a young offenders’ institute for all offences.

She said: “You are 18-years of age and for someone so young you have already a substantial criminal record – ten convictions for 22 offences.

“They include robbery, burglary and assaults upon emergency workers.”

### Violent killer

A killer who ‘butchered’ her disabled victim when she stabbed her almost 50 times has been detained in a high-security mental hospital.

Patricia Hewitt attacked Angela Lloyd with a knife and scissors while demanding she hand over money to her just hours after being given a suspended prison sentence.

And part of the horrific attack was recorded when the 56-year-old managed to call her care provider shouting for help as she was 'being stabbed to death'.

The court heard Hewitt did not know Miss Lloyd before the brutal attack.

Teesside Crown Court heard how the 42-year-old was acting erratically following her appearance at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court for assault on August 13, last year.

Hewitt was reported to police after she was stealing wine from Morrisons Daily and trying to enter other people’s homes but was not arrested.

Consultant forensic psychiatrists Dr Lois Carey and Dr James Stoddart both recommend that Hewitt is treated in Rampton Hospital due to her mental health diagnosis and continued risk to the public.

  Patricia Hewitt 

*(Image: Durham Constabulary)*

Hewitt, of Keats close, Stanley, pleaded guilty to manslaughter by diminished responsibility after being declared fit to plead to the charge.

Rod Hunt, mitigating, said: “Whatever her mental frailties over the months, the defendant has wanted to apologise for her wrongdoing.

“She has insight into the enormity of her actions and she is profoundly affected by it.

“She is not fit to be detained anywhere other than in a very secure hospital setting.”

Judge Jonathan Carroll passed a hospital order which will result in Hewitt being held in Rampton Hospital indefinitely.

### 
  **Drug dealer**

A drug dealer was caught in the act by a police drone in a North East park.

Junior Lavin was spotted dealing drugs in Albert Park, Middlesbrough by Cleveland Police’s Proactive Team and Neighbourhood Team on Monday, February 16.

The 19-year-old, of Pemberton Crescent, Middlesbrough, arrived on an electric bike and attempted to escape when confronted by plain-clothed officers.

After a brief chase, Lavin was arrested.

  Junior Lavin 

*(Image: Cleveland Police)*

An electric bike and more than 30 deals of crack cocaine were recovered at the scene.

At Teesside Crown Court on Monday, August 3, Lavin was sentenced to two years in prison, suspended for two years, for possession with intent to supply crack cocaine, possession of criminal property, and driving without a licence or insurance.

### Darlington pervert

A Darlington man plied a girl with vodka before offering her money to let him take off her underwear.

Teesside Crown Court heard how 36-year-old Michael Mason pleaded guilty to one charge of trying to incite sexual activity with a child but denied three further counts of sexual activity with a girl.

Phillip Morley, prosecuting, said the defendant changed his pleas to guilty before he was due to stand trial but after the victim had given her pre-recorded evidence and been cross examined by defence counsel.

He said: “Aggravating features include the time and place of the offences and the fact the defendant was under the influence of alcohol and drugs at the time.”

  Michael Mason 

*(Image: Durham Constabulary)*

Mason, of no fixed abode, carried out all four offences in Darlington on one night several years ago.

“You targeted a child who was vulnerable and who you knew was,” he said. “To facilitate the offence, you got her to drink vodka when she was a young teenager.

“No doubt, you were hoping to lower the resistance they would have otherwise put up.”

Mason was also made subject to a sexual harm prevention order for five years and ordered to sign on the sex offenders’ register for ten years.

### Crack cocaine

A crack cocaine dealer offering "fat rocks, fast drops" has been locked up after his drugs line burner phone was traced to him.

Jason Walker sent out a series of messages offering the Class A drug for sale but also used the same burner phone to message his partner.

When police searched the 34-year-old’s home, they recovered digital scales, ziplock bags, two further mobile phones and £300 in cash.

Tabitha Buck, prosecuting, said: “The drug line was investigated, and it was sending out bulk messages regarding the sale of crack cocaine.

“There were messages ‘fat rocks, fast drops’ and ‘new wash, get on it’ and ‘fat juicy rocks that will blow off your socks’.

“It transpired that it was being used to send messages to this defendant’s partner and messages from the dealer phone were being sent to her of a romantic nature.”

  Jason Walker 

*(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Walker, of Myrtle Grove, Thornaby, pleaded guilty to being concerned in the supply of crack cocaine between November 19 last year and January 5 this year on the day he was due to go on trial.

He said: "It was a shared phone with a number of people using it to deal.

“You were an active user of it and you were identified by mixing messages on the trading of drugs with messages to your partner.”

### Child rapist

A North East child rapist who failed to declare his crimes to his new partner was exposed when police spotted a "love bite" on his neck.

Mark Hubbard was made subject of a sexual risk order (SRO) as he continues to pose a danger to members of the public, Teesside Crown Court heard.

The 32-year-old was sentenced to 12 years in custody in 2014 for raping a child under 16 and convicted again in 2016 for engaging in sexual activity with a child.

Albany Kidd, prosecuting, said Hubbard was made subject of the SRO as police remained concerned about the likelihood of him re-offending after his release from custody.

She said the defendant had to inform any person he was starting a relationship about his previous convictions and alert the authorities about the relationship.

The woman told police that she believed the defendant was called Mark Wilson and she was ‘disgusted’ when she learned of his previous convictions.

  Mark Hubbard 

*(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Hubbard, of The Crescent, Middlesbrough, pleaded guilty to breaching his SRO on July 5 this year.
