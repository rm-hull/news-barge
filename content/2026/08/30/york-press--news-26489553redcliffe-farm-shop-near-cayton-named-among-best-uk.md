## 'Perfect' farm shop easy to reach from York named one of the best in the UK

Redcliffe Farm Shop is the perfect place for grocery shopping. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

A farm shop that is easy to reach from York has been named one of the best in the UK.

The country lifestyle experts at Welligogs have released a list of the best farm shops around the UK with Redcliffe Farm Shop making it onto the list alongside huge names like the Diddly Squat Farm Shop.

Each farm shop was judged based on its Tripadvisor ratings, Google review scores, and Instagram following.

The farm shop, which can be found just outside of Cayton close to the Yorkshire coast, has a café and offers lots of fresh produce.


## What is the Redcliffe Farm Shop like?

The Redcliffe Farm Shop sells meat and eggs from its own farm as well as produce from other local farmers.

The bakery in the shop offers a range of cakes, savoury items, and pies to pick up.

You can also find fresh fruit and vegetables as well as artisan products from local suppliers such as honey, coffee, beers, and gin.

After walking around the farm shop, you can take a break in the café that serves a range of breakfast options from sandwiches to full English breakfasts.

There are also lunch choices such as homemade quiche, lasagna, soup, pork belly, and pies for those wanting something a bit more substantial.

On Sundays, the farm shop’s café offers a traditional roast with Yorkshire puddings, potatoes, and seasonal vegetables and your choice of meat or a vegetarian wellington.


Tess Annan, manager at Welligogs, said: “Farm shops are such an important part of the British countryside experience because they give visitors the opportunity to experience a destination beyond its traditional tourist attractions.

“Whether you're picking up locally produced food, stopping for lunch or simply browsing what a farm has to offer, visiting a farm shop can be a lovely way to slow down and experience the character of the surrounding countryside.

“Diddly Squat is obviously one of the country's most recognisable farm shops, but what's particularly interesting about this ranking is that there are so many brilliant alternatives across the UK.”
