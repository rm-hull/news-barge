Fontaines D.C. closed Saturday night’s Leeds Festival with a huge set in front of a packed crowd as speculation continues to grow that the band could be heading to Newcastle for a major stadium show next summer.

The Irish rockers took to the stage at the festival as they continue to enjoy a huge rise in popularity, with thousands of fans turning out to see them perform some of their biggest tracks.

Their Leeds set included fan favourites including Boys in the Better Land, Jackie Down the Line, Favourite, I Love You and Starburster.

  Fantaines D.C. at Leeds Festival 2026 

*(Image: LIAM RUSSELL)*

The performance comes just weeks after a potential Newcastle date for the band was 'leaked' by a music insider.

Music insider Gavin Davis claimed earlier this month that St James' Park could be among the stadiums Fontaines D.C. visit as part of a 2027 tour.

He suggested the Newcastle United ground could be included on the band's plans, with the possibility of further dates being added.

And there could now be another reason for fans to keep an eye on Newcastle.

  Fantaines D.C. at Leeds Festival 2026 

*(Image: LIAM RUSSELL)*

Fontaines D.C. have announced a new UK and Ireland tour for November, but the dates currently announced do not include a North East show.

That means fans in Newcastle and the surrounding area will not get the chance to see the band on the upcoming arena tour, potentially leaving the door open for a much bigger North East date further down the line.

  Fantaines D.C. at Leeds Festival 2026 

*(Image: LIAM RUSSELL)*

The band have previously played Newcastle, including a sold-out Exhibition Park date, while their popularity has continued to soar following the release of their acclaimed albums.

Their latest era has seen them become one of the biggest names in alternative rock, with major festival appearances and increasingly large headline shows.

A concert at St James' Park would represent another significant step up for the group.

The stadium has recently had North Shields legend Sam Fender selling out two shows there in 2023 before returning for three packed concerts in June 2025.

Kings of Leon and Ed Sheeran have also previously played at the home of Newcastle United.

For now, there is no official confirmation that Fontaines D.C. will play St James' Park in 2027.
