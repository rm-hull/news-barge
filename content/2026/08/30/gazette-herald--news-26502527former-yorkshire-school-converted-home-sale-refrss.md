A former school converted into a home has hit the market in a Yorkshire village.

The three-bedroom former Methodist school is for sale in Tockwith for £650,000.

Carter Jonas, which is marketing the property, said it is a “striking” Victorian home which has been “comprehensively improved by the current owners”.

The owners have created a “stylish and ready-to-move-into home with real architectural character”, the estate agent said.

Inside, an entrance hall opens into a central dining area.

The kitchen sits to the rear of the property with a breakfast bar and quality fittings.

Upstairs are the three double bedrooms.

The main bedroom has an en-suite toilet, with the main house bathroom serving the other two bedrooms.

Outside is a landscaped rear garden, gated access and off-street parking.

A Carter Jonas spokesperson said the property is “positioned within the centre of this highly regarded village” and offers a “superb lifestyle and commuter location”.
