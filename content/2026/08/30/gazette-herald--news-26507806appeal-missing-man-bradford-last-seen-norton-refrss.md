  **Update: In a statement this evening (Sunday, August 30), North Yorkshire Police said the missing 31-year-old from Bradford has been found safe and well.**

A MAN from West Yorkshire last seen in the Ryedale area of North Yorkshire has gone missing, prompting an urgent appeal from police.

The man has recently been staying in Scrayingham, a village a few miles north-east of Stamford Bridge and was last seen in Welham Road, Norton at 8.25am on Friday, August 28.

North Yorkshire Police describe him as white, around 6 feet tall, of medium build, with green eyes, short light brown hair, and a well-kept short beard.

  


He was last seen wearing a black hoodie, grey trousers, black shoes, and carrying a black backpack.

A police spokesperson said: "Any immediate sightings of the man should be reported by dialling 999 so we can quickly ensure he is safe."

Other information can be reported by calling 101 and quoting reference number 12260168826.
