A four-star historical drama exploring the untold story of Anne Boleyn’s mother is heading to the Yorkshire Dales as part of a regional tour following its acclaimed premiere earlier this year.

Anne Boleyn’s Mother, written and performed by Heather Leech, is the final instalment in a trilogy of one-woman plays about tragic mothers.

The play opens at Leyburn Arts Centre on September 5.

Keith Leech, co-director of Gleowit Productions, said: "We are really excited to be touring the last in the trilogy of one woman plays about tragic mothers, written and performed by Heather Leech in early September."

The drama delves into the emotional world of Lady Elizabeth Boleyn during the rise and fall of her daughter Anne, from her marriage to Henry VIII to her execution.

It explores the heavy personal cost of ambition and court intrigue, and the pain of losing two children to the political schemes of the Tudor court.

The play premiered in Hastings in June to a full house and received a four-star review.
