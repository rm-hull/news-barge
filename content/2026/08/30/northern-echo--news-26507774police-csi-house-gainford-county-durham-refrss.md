Crime scene investigators are scouring a house on a quiet County Durham street after a suspected shooting saw one person taken to hospital.

The front window of a house on Balmer Hill in Gainford was left shattered and the front door appeare to have been shot at in the incident on Saturday (August 29) night.

Emergency services including the air ambulance were called around 8.10pm, and one person was taken to hospital.

The nature of their injuries and condition are not currently known.

  Police and CSI are at the scene of a house on Balmer Hill, Gainford, while a police cordon remains in place on Sunday (August 30) 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

White-suited forensic officers continued to work at the scene this afternoon (Sunday, August 30).

They were seen going in and out of the detached house which remained at the centre of a cordon.

Residents said the entire street was shut off after the incident as police flooded the area. Officers continue to stand guard at the cordon today.

  Police and CSI are at the scene of a house on Balmer Hill, Gainford, while a police cordon remains in place on Sunday (August 30)

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) has confirmed it dispatched an ambulance crew, duty officer and clinical team leader after being called at 8.10pm.

The Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS) also attended the incident.
