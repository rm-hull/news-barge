Runners taking part in the Middlesbrough 10K paid an emotional tribute to two Cleveland Police officers killed in the devastating A66 crash.

PC Matthew Blades, 37, and PC Tom Clough, 38, were remembered before the start of the race on Sunday, August 30, as thousands of runners gathered in Middlesbrough.

  Middlesbrough 10K 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Ahead of the event, the race announcer read out the names of both officers, prompting applause from runners and spectators as they paid their respects.

The tribute came just a day after Middlesbrough FC supporters also remembered PC Blades and PC Clough before the club's Championship fixture against West Bromwich Albion at the Riverside Stadium.

Similar tributes were also seen at the Darlington FC game against Macclesfield on Saturday.

  PC Matthew Blades 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

The two officers died in the line of duty after their marked police vehicle was struck by a Volkswagen Passat travelling the wrong way along the A66 at South Bank in the early hours of Saturday, August 22.

PC Blades and PC Clough had been travelling in a marked Armed Response Vehicle when the collision happened at around 3.39am.

  PC Tom Clough 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

Five people travelling in the Passat also died. They were Michael Robert Cahill, 23, Theo Rae, 17, Cole Robert Worthy, 17, Jakub Matusiak, 23, and Makai Saddington, 18.

Tributes have continued across Teesside in the days since the tragedy.

Hartlepool United supporters observed a two-minute silence for the officers before their game against Eastleigh on Friday night, while two police hats were placed on the pitch in their memory.

A fundraising appeal launched by the Cleveland Police Federation for the families of PC Blades and PC Clough has also raised more than £1.1million.

The investigation into the circumstances surrounding the crash remains ongoing.
