A main road in York is closed after being damaged during the storm this evening (Sunday).

The westbound carriageway of Tower Street is shut.

The damage is to the road surface at the pedestrian crossing where Tower Street meets Fishergate and Paragon Street.

During the torrential downpour, water apparently surged through the drains underneath the road and the manhole cover, and lifted the entire surface of the road.

The Tarmac is now buckled and a section has lifted by a few inches.

Police and fire crews were on site this evening. City of York Council highways workers were called out to assess the damage.

There was a discussion of bringing in ramps to cover the damage, but the road has to be checked that it’s still safe to carry traffic.

Traffic is being diverted down Piccadilly.

***YorkMix*** was told the closure could last for some time.
