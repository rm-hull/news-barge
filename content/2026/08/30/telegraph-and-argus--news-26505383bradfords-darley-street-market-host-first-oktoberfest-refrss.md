A TASTE of Bavaria is coming to West Yorkshire this September.

The first-ever Oktoberfest at Darley Street Market will take place at Darley Street Market’s Courtyard from Friday, September 18 to Sunday, September 20, featuring traditional German beer, food and live entertainment.

Councillor Ian Walker, executive member for neighbourhoods and communities at Bradford Council, said: "We’re excited to be bringing a taste of Bavaria to Darley Street Market at our first ever Oktoberfest.

"The event promises fantastic beers, delicious food and plenty of entertainment.

"Whether you're a real ale enthusiast, a fan of authentic German beer, or simply looking for a fantastic time with friends and family, it’s sure to be a great weekend.

"We’re looking forward to welcoming visitors from across the district and beyond to experience the unique atmosphere of this special celebration."

The event will run from 11am to 9pm on Friday and Saturday, and from 11am to 5pm on Sunday.

Entry is free.

Bradford CAMRA and Northern Monk will host a selection of beers, including six authentic Munich Oktoberfest brews.

These include Augustiner, Hacker-Pschorr, Hofbrau, Lowenbrau, Spaten and Paulaner.

There will also be four guest German beers and four Northern Monk brews, including a specially brewed Oktoberfest beer.

Ricky Holden of Bradford CAMRA said: "Bradford CAMRA are looking forward to bringing the genuine Oktoberfest beers to Bradford once again.

"Many places seem to have an 'Oktoberfest' event but few if any stock all the six Munich beers the festival traditionally should feature.

"The combination of that and the excellent venue in the heart of Bradford should ensure everyone has a great time.

"The hard to find, traditional beers are great tasting and good value, they are actually cheaper in Bradford than at the Munich event."

Taplins Butchers will serve foot-long bratwurst sausages and slow-cooked smoked pork shoulder with sauerkraut from 12pm to 5pm on Friday and Saturday, and from 12pm to 4pm on Sunday.

Entertainment will include live performances by the Yorkshire Oompah Band, who will play traditional Bavarian folk music, drinking songs and oompah-style covers of modern pop hits.

Their performances are scheduled for Friday and Saturday at 3pm and 6pm.

The Courtyard will be decorated with bunting and music will play throughout the weekend.

All beers will be priced at £6 per pint (or bottle for Augustiner), with third and half-pint options available.
