The Met Office has issued a yellow warning for heavy showers and thunderstorms in York and North Yorkshire.

The UK weather agency issued the warning just before 9am this morning (Sunday, August 30) and said it runs from 11am until midnight, covering almost all of England and Wales apart from parts of Greater London and the South East.

A spokesperson for the Met Office said: "Showers, heavy and thundery, will reach southwest England and Wales on Sunday morning, before spreading and developing more widely during the afternoon.

"Whilst some areas will miss the heaviest showers, where they do occur 10-15 mm of rain is possible in less than an hour, with 30-40 mm possible over several hours, these larger totals most likely for parts of north and northwest England and southwest Scotland.

"Hail, frequent lightning and, in southern parts of England and Wales, wind gusts of 35-45 mph will be additional hazards.

"Heaviest showers and thunderstorms will become more isolated later in the evening."

  


The Met Office has warned that driving conditions could be affected by spray, standing water, and hail, leading to longer journey times by car and bus.

Delays to train services are also possible.

The agency added that there is a chance of some damage to buildings and structures from lightning strikes, and some short-term loss of power and other services is likely.
