Three drivers are now serving bans for between 20 and 40 months after they were among defendants sentenced recently at Scarborough Magistrates Court.

Martin Harvey, 39, of St Leonards Crescent, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to driving with a cocaine derivative in his body in Scarborough.

He was given a 12-month community order with six months’ mental health treatment, 20 days’ rehabilitative activities and banned from driving for 40 months. He must pay £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

Eliza Carthy, 50, of Mount Pleasant East, Robin Hoods Bay, pleaded guilty to drink driving in Scarborough.

She was banned from driving for three years, fined £120 and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £48 statutory surcharge.

Moya Brown, 50, of Valley Road, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to stealing a bicycle worth £3,500 in Scarborough.

She was given a 12-month community order with 25 days’ rehabilitative activities and ordered to pay £1,800 compensation.

Michael Lovell, 48, of Holme Hill, Scarborough pleaded guilty to harassing a woman.

He was jailed for 16 weeks and ordered to pay a £154 statutory surcharge. He was made subject to a five-year restraining order to protect the woman.

Brett Taylor, 44, of Candler Street, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to failure to provide a specimen of breath when suspected of drink driving.

He was given a six-month community order with 10 days’ rehabilitative activities and must wear an alcohol abstinence tag for 80 days.

He was banned from driving for 20 months and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.
