This East Cleveland coffee spot with stunning sea views and sweet treats has attracted thousands of visitors who dub it a 'hidden gem'.

Whether you're tackling the Cleveland Way, enjoying a stroll along the beach or simply looking for a coffee with a view, Blooms Coffee Box in Skinningrove is proving that the best things often come in the smallest packages.

Blooms has built a loyal following since opening on the seafront in December 2025, serving barista coffee, iced drinks and homemade sweet treats from its compact shipping container-style café overlooking the coast.

Sitting within Skinningrove Beach's Car Park, the business offers uninterrupted sea views to enjoy alongside a morning coffee, making it an increasingly popular stop for walkers and dog owners.

  Coffees and sweet treats - with a particular knack for a biscoff iced-latte. 

*(Image: Blooms Coffee Box)*

While the box may be small, the menu is impressive - with freshly brewed coffees, iced drinks and seasonal specials featuring on the menu.

A particular favourite for staff and customer is the classic flat white, with the owner saying: "The flat white was actually the drink that started my love for coffee, so it will always have a special place at Blooms."

The café's Biscoff-inspired offerings have also become a particular favourite with customers - particularly the iced latte.

Made with layers of espresso, creamy milk and rich Biscoff sauce before being topped with whipped cream and a generous sprinkle of Biscoff crumb, it's easy to see why it has become a bestseller for those with a sweet tooth.

  A cookie sandwich with a view. 

*(Image: Blooms Coffee Box)*

The Cookie Ice Cream Sandwich has also been flying out - featuring two freshly baked cookies filled with vanilla ice cream, chocolate sauce and a swirl of chocolate topping.

The café welcomes four-legged visitors along for coffee stops, and even offers treats made just for the dogs out on a walk along the Skinningrove coastline.

  Four-legged friends are catered for too.

Before settling permanently on the Skinningrove seafront, Blooms kept themselves busy at events across the North East - attending Christmas markets throughout East Cleveland, Stokesley and North Ormesby, as well as Redcar's Christmas lights switch-on.

Now firmly established on the seaside, the business has become something of a hidden gem for those seeking quality coffee, homemade treats and one of the best views in East Cleveland.

As Blooms puts it: "Worth the drive. Worth the view. Worth every sip."
