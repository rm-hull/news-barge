A scenic circular walk in Ripon has been named among the best Sunday walks for the Bank Holiday.

Fountains Abbey & Studley Royal Walk, a 9km route, took second place in a list compiled by GO Outdoors, which follows the River Skell and winds through parkland into the landscaped water gardens of Studley Royal.

The waterscape, complete with stone bridges, lakes, and historic follies, frames the ruins of the UNESCO World Heritage Site, Fountains Abbey.

Experts from GO Outdoors analysed mapping data and reviews to create the top 10 list of the best August Bank Holiday walks in the UK, all close to a Sunday lunch spot.

The Fountains Abbey & Studley Royal Walk secured a "Sunday Stroll Score" of 9.68 out of 10, reflecting its overall appeal.

The route concludes in Ripon at The Royal Oak, an 18th-century coaching inn located in the city centre.

The pub, which received a review score of 4.4 out of 5, is praised for its food, with one reviewer describing its offering as "the best Sunday lunch out we have had in a long time."

The list was topped by Padley Gorge Circular in Grindleford, which received a score of 9.87 out of 10.
