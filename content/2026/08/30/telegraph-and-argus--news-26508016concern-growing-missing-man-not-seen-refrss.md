A man has been missing for two weeks.

Karl, 62, from Kippax, was last seen on August 15 and his disappearance has prompted growing concern from police and family members.

Officers have carried out searches in Kippax, Allerton Bywater, and Ledston since he was reported missing on August 18.

Detective Inspector Kirsty Walsh said: "We are growing increasingly concerned for Karl as time passes, as are his family.

"His disappearance is out of character as is his lack of contact with relatives.

  Can you help find Karl?

"A significant number of enquires remain ongoing to locate him and we are very much still focussing our searches in the Kippax and Castleford areas.

"I would ask anyone in Kippax, Allerton Bywater and in Ledston who perhaps has a property with outbuildings to check them in case Karl has been using them to stay or sleep."

The last confirmed sighting of Karl was at the Tesco store in Allerton Bywater, where he was seen shopping.

He was wearing a grey Santa Cruz t-shirt at the time, but a matching shirt was later found at his home, suggesting he may have changed clothes after the sighting.

Karl is described as 5ft 4ins tall and of medium build.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Leeds CID on 101 or via the West Yorkshire Police LiveChat website, quoting log 1356 of August 18.
