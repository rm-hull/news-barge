Psychic Sally is set to appear at St George's Hall on September 10.

The popular medium, known for her television appearances and best-selling books, will bring her live show to Bradford.

Sally, whose real name is Sally Morgan, said: "My tour has become a way of life for me, as I’m getting older my abilities as a medium are stronger than ever, so to be able to pass messages on to the audiences around the country is a huge privilege, as well as a pleasure.

"I call every message validated a ‘wonder moment’.

"So take your seats, relax, be open minded and ready to come forward if you think the message is for you."

Tickets are priced at £30.50.

She has previously given readings to figures including Princess Diana, Katie Price and George Michael.
