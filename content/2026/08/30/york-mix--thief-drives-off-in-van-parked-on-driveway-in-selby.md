North Yorkshire Police are appealing for any witnesses to a stolen Ford Transit van from the Selby area.

Stolen from a victim’s driveway between 24th of August 2026 and 25th, from 22:50-07:00hrs. Ford Transit van in white with grey decals. Suspect headed towards the Long Mann Hills Road area.

If you have any information that could assist the investigation, please contact the police online in the first instance.
