A new dog-friendly event is set to debut this September.

Paws in the Park will take place at Aire Park on September 12, offering a full day of activities for dogs and their owners from 11am to 4pm.

The event will include agility runs, light-hearted competitions, and plenty of opportunities for pups to show off their skills and charm.

Stalls run by Real Markets will offer a range of dog treats, accessories, and services for visitors to browse.

"Whether you’re entering your dog into the competitions or simply soaking up the atmosphere, Paws in the Park promises a lively day out for the whole family."

Competitions will run throughout the day, with categories including Loveliest Lady, Fabulous Fella, Best Family, Best Trickster, Cutest Puppy, Golden Oldie, and Best in Show.

Dogs Trust will also be present to talk to visitors about their work across the UK and how people can support their Sponsor a Dog programme and Lucky Pups Lotto.

"We’re delighted to welcome Dogs Trust to this year’s Paws in the Park event. "Their team will be on hand throughout the day to chat with visitors about the incredible work they do to help dogs across the UK, both locally and nationally."

There will be free play agility sessions between competitions, giving every dog a chance to enjoy the course.

"After your dog has tackled the agility course, it’s time for their moment in the spotlight at the Doggy Day Winner’s Podium. Whether they’ve raced into first place, secured a proud third, or simply given it their all, every pup gets the chance to strike a pawse."
