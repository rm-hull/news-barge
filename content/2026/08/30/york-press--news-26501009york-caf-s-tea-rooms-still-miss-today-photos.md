## 7 'lost' York cafés and tea rooms we still miss today

Charlie' Bistro in Tanner Row in 1977. *(Image: The Press)*

York has lost more than a few places for coffee and cake over the years.

As The Press reports on the latest moves to open new venues around the city - Eddie Copley-Farnell has applied to licence a former Boots pharmacy in Clifton as part of its conversion into The Curious Coffee Company café - we look back on lost cafes that we still miss today.

Older readers may still remember Charlie's Bistro in Tanner Row from the 1970s which later became other haunts like the 71 Club and Ziggy’s. Or Flanagan's in Goodramgate, which The Press photo shows in 1988/9, was more to your taste.

  Flanagan's in Goodramgate, pictured here in 1988/9.


One of the best known cafe hotspot was perhaps Little Bettys on Stonegate which had been part of York’s shopping scene since the 1960s until it closed in 2021, ending decades of afternoon teas, cakes and catch-ups.

  Principalis in Low Petergate in 1996 which went on to become Cafe Veranda.


Café Concerto on High Petergate was another favourite. The music-themed café served customers for 27 years before closing in 2020, leaving a gap in one of York’s most historic thoroughfares.

  Bond's Bistro in New Street, pictured in 1995, went on to become Dusk.


Goodramgate lost Café Luca in 2022 after 12 years, while Chloe's of York said goodbye to Colliergate in 2023 following a decade in business.

For many residents, these were more than cafés: they were reliable meeting places, somewhere for a pot of tea, a toasted teacake and a rest after a day in town.

  Bistro 52 in Walmgate, pictured in 1994, is now home to Thai favourite Khao San Road. 

*(Image: The Press)*

Our collection of photos, most of which were taken in the 1990s, features Café Ella in Walmgate and Bistro 52, also in Walmgate which is now home to Khao San Road, as well as Cleopatra in The Stonebow, Principalis in Low Petergate, and Bond's Bistro in New Street which is home Dusk.
