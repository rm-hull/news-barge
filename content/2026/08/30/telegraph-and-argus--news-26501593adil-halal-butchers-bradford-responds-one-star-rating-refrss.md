A BRADFORD butchers slapped with a one-star food hygiene rating has admitted he was "out of the country" at the time of the inspection.

Adil Halal Butchers Ltd on Spencer Road in Great Horton was given a one-star rating - with "major improvement necessary" in the management of food safety - following an inspection by Bradford Council’s environmental health food safety team on June 16.

The business has since contacted the T&A to address the rating.

The owner said the boiler had broken down whilst he was out of the country leaving the premises "without an adequate supply of hot water".

He added that the butchers "should not have continued operating under those circumstances".

## Hygiene and safety taken 'extremely seriously'

A spokesperson for Adil Halal Butchers said: "We take food hygiene and the safety of our customers extremely seriously and would like to provide some context regarding the recent inspection and our food hygiene rating.

"At the time of the inspection, I was out of the country and unfortunately our boiler had broken down, resulting in the premises temporarily being without an adequate supply of hot water.

"The business should not have continued operating under those circumstances.

"As the owner, I fully accept that stronger procedures should have been in place to ensure that, in my absence, the business would stop operating until an issue of this nature had been rectified."

## What did the inspection find?

The inspection report highlighted a number of issues, including missing hand-washing materials, inadequate pest control, and damaged equipment and infrastructure.

Flies were found in the raw meat production area, and there was damage to the left side of the chest-freezer handle and floor mats.

While hygienic food handling was rated as "generally satisfactory," cleanliness and the condition of the facilities were rated as "improvement necessary."

## 'Rather than make excuses'

The owner added that following the inspection, Bradford Council issued Hygiene Improvement Notices, including one for the hot water supply.

The spokesperson said: "We took the council's concerns seriously and immediate action was taken.

"The hot-water issue has been addressed and our food-safety management and HACCP procedures have been reviewed and strengthened.

"Staff have also been given further instruction regarding their responsibilities and when food operations must cease if essential hygiene facilities are unavailable.

"We were extremely disappointed to receive a one-star food hygiene rating.

"However, rather than make excuses, our priority has been to address the matters raised by Environmental Health and ensure the necessary improvements are put in place.

"We are committed to working constructively with Bradford Council and maintaining the standards expected of our business.

"We would like to reassure our customers that we have taken the inspection findings seriously and have acted to address the issues identified."

  **It's important to note that other businesses that operate on the site are separate to Adil Halal Butchers and this rating does not reflect their own food hygiene standards of rating.**
