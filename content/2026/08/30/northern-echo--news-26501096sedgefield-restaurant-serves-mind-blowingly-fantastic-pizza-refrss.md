An Italian restaurant in County Durham praised by diners for its “mind-blowingly fantastic pizza” and “phenomenal service” has added to its growing list of top TripAdvisor reviews.

Julia & Sofia’s, on Church View in Sedgefield, now has 143 “excellent” reviews on TripAdvisor — the highest rating available to contributors on the travel and review platform.

The restaurant serves Mediterranean-inspired dishes including pizza, pasta, seafood and Italian favourites, and has earned a strong reputation among both regular customers and visitors to County Durham.

One recent TripAdvisor reviewer, who visited while holidaying in the area, praised the restaurant’s welcome and its handling of dietary requirements.


They said: “On holiday in the area for a few days and decided to try for evening meal.

“The kerb look is excellent. Looks very inviting. The inside is just as good.

“We found the staff, atmosphere, and food excellent. One of the party had a couple of dietary requirements and these were dealt with.

“Recommend this to anyone in the area. Unfortunately we live 180 miles away so will be a little while before we can visit again.”

## ‘Mind-blowingly fantastic pizza’

Another diner described their meal as “perfect”, writing: “Mind-blowingly fantastic pizza, phenomenal service and really nice atmosphere.

“Couldn’t find anything to mark down. This place is a bit of a gem. Will look forward to my next visit. Perfect!”

Another customer praised the choice available and said they planned to return.

They wrote: “Great atmosphere, extensive menu, high quality food, sufficiently varied wine list, staff very pleasant, will definitely recommend and return.”

The latest comments build on earlier reviews which saw Julia & Sofia’s described as serving “the most authentic Italian pizza” and as being among the best Italian restaurants in County Durham.

One previous contributor said: “We went for our wedding anniversary. Service is cheerful and professional, no pressure. Food is stunning and gently perfectly cooked. Great wine list.

“Must be one of the best Italians in County Durham. A big thank you.”

Another called the venue a “gem of an Italian”, while a regular praised the restaurant’s attentive service and said: “We have always come to Julia and Sofia’s and it never fails.”

The restaurant is located on Church View in Sedgefield, close to the historic town centre and its village green.
