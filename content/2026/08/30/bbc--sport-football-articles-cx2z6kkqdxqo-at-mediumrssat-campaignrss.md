Fernandes hat-trick helps Man Utd recover to beat Ipswich

## Manchester United

**Senne Lammens:** Stood firm and came out on top as Enciso charged at him. Out of his area to control and lay off a pass to Shaw at one point.**8Diogo Dalot:** So close to a rare goal with a curling left-footed effort. Defensively solid.**7Harry Maguire:** Reliable presence at the heart of the home defence, then got lucky with the deflection, otherwise his 'goal' might have been ruled out.**8Lisandro Martinez:** Experience vital on a day when the home side might have buckled. Mistake for Ipswich's late goal.**6Luke Shaw:** A difficult day for the England full-back. Struggled to deal with Fatawu.**5Kobbie Mainoo:** Restored to the starting line-up and good as ever on the ball. But United's midfield struggled to cope with Ipswich running at them.**6Youri Tielemans:** Still to find form in a Man Utd shirt. Went down too easily in the penalty area. Not secure defensively.**5Bryan Mbeumo:** Held his wide position and threatened with crosses and short passes. Unlucky Rashford's cross was deflected as he hit the bar from close range. Got his goal at the end.**6Bruno Fernandes:** Hat-trick hero. Sensational performance from the captain. Thunderous equaliser, kept his nerve with the penalty after two pre-season failures and calm third. Almost scored an outrageous fourth from halfway.**10Marcus Rashford:** First league start for Man Utd since December 2024. Got on the ball often enough and ran at the visitors' defence. Almost created a goal for Mbeumo.**7Matheus Cunha:** Began through the middle again and alternated with Fernandes as the furthest player forward. Decent.**7Subs:Leny Yoro (on 72, Dalot): 6Noussair Mazraoui (on 72, Shaw): 6Benjamin Sesko (on 79, Cunha): 6Patrick Dorgu (on 79, Rashford): 7Andrey Santos (on 79, Mainoo): 6Subs not used: Darlow, Zirkzee, Heaven, Lacey**

## Ipswich

**Kjell Scherpen:** Superb save to deny Dalot the opener and crucial touch to deny Mbeumo an easy finish. No chance with the equaliser.**8Dara O'Shea:** Robust defensive display and did well, until Fernandes took control.**6Issa Diop:** Lot of scrambling defence for the former West Ham man but coped OK for the most part and talked the Ipswich rearguard through the game.**6Jacob Greaves:** Composed on the ball and held his position, not getting drawn out when Cunha dropped deep. Probably lacked a bit of class at this level.**6Leif Davis:** Superb pass inside Dalot to release Maeda created one good chance for the visitors. Precise execution for his goal and might have had another.**8Marcelino Nunez:** Busy, in front of defence, wants to get on ball and slot in between centre-halves. Disastrous slip at just the wrong time let Man Utd in for crucial equaliser.**6Sasa Lukic:** Excellent addition to Ipswich ranks. Had the game nous to halt the home side's momentum, even at the expense of a yellow card.**7Abdul Fatawu:** Skinned Shaw close to the touchline and then had the awareness to pick out a team-mate as Ipswich took the lead. Booked for a poor tackle on Mbeumo but overall really good.**7Julio Ensico:** Held off Shaw and Mbeumo on length-of-field run but no reward as effort lacked power. A real threat.**8Daizan Maeda:** Good early raid down the left was early sign of Ipswich intentions. Shot from angle of area threatened home goal.**7Emersonn:** Tried use his size to pin Maguire and Martinez back and relished the physical tussle with the England man.**6Subs:Chuba Akpom (on 46, Emersonn): 7Exequiel Palacios (on 62, Nunez): 6Darnell Furlong (on 72, Maeda): 6Abdoul Ouattara (on 73, Lukic): 5Kasey McAteer (on 83, Fatawu): 5Subs not used: Walton, Kipre, Egeli, Clarke**
