## New play inspired by infamous canoe man story coming to Scarborough

Phil Corbitt *(Image: Supplied)*

A new play inspired by the canoe man story is set to hit the stage this autumn.

All at Sea, by Alison Carr, will be performed at the Stephen Joseph Theatre (SJT) in Scarborough from October 16 to 31 following its premiere run at Newcastle’s Live Theatre.

The play tells the story of Colin and Deborah, a couple drowning in debt who hatch a scheme to fake Colin’s death in a canoeing accident to claim insurance money.

Phil Corbitt and Sarah Belcher will star in the two-hander, directed by Maria Crocker.

Ms Crocker said: "I am so excited to welcome Phil Corbitt and Sarah Belcher to the All at Sea team.

"This sharp and witty play requires two expert actors to bring this extraordinary couple to life, and this casting has delivered just that."

The creative team includes Hannah Sibai as designer, Roma Yagnik as sound designer, Catja Hamilton as lighting designer, and Ziad Jabero as composer.

Presented by Live Theatre and SJT in association with Laurels, the production is supported by Newcastle-based architects SPACE.

All at Sea will run in Newcastle from September 24 to October 10 before moving to Scarborough.

Tickets for the SJT dates are available via the box office on 01723 370541 or at www.sjt.uk.com.
