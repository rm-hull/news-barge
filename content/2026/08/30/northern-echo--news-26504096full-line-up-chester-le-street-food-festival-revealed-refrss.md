Chester-le-Street's food and drink festival will return for its fifth year next month with its biggest-ever line-up ever promised and new food-and-drink masterclasses.

Chester-le-EATS will take over Banks Homes Riverside, the home of Durham Cricket, on Saturday, September 26 and Sunday, September 27.

Visitors will be able to attend from 11am until 5pm on both days, with no advance booking required for the main festival.

Organisers say this year’s event will feature more traders than ever before, plus a dedicated Family Zone, children’s activities and a programme of performers arranged with Proper Mint Concerts.

Ythan Sale, events manager at Durham Cricket, said: “It’s difficult to believe Chester-le-EATS is five years old.

“What started as an idea to bring great food, drink, music and people together at Durham Cricket has grown beyond anything we could have imagined, and the support from traders, performers and thousands of visitors over those five years has been incredible.

“For our fifth birthday, we didn’t simply want to do the same festival again. We wanted to celebrate how far we’ve come while making this our biggest year yet with the most traders, new experiences, a fantastic entertainment programme and for the first time, our Masterclasses.”

## Food and drink traders

The festival will bring together street-food businesses, independent food and drink producers, artisan traders, sweet treats and gifts.

Hot-food vendors confirmed for the weekend include Chicken Ting, Flankman Tacos, Geordie Banger Co, Grandpa’s Fish Bar, Redheads Mac ‘N’ Cheese, Spud Gun Loaded Fries and Burgers, The Big Banh and The Loaded Dawg.

For dessert, visitors will be able to choose from The Dessert Hub, The Hedgehog Bakery, The Little Poffertje Pancake Shack, Le Mini Macaron and The Sunrise Patisserie. Bass Notes Coffee will also be on site.

Artisan food, drink and gift traders include Great British Cheese Company, Sawley Kitchen, Clarence Spirits, Prohibition Distillery, WL Distillery, Fat Rabbit Crafts and Pevs Prints.

## New masterclasses announced

For the first time, festival-goers can also book one of three paid masterclasses, each priced at £35.

They are:

- Flight! — a beer and chicken-wing experience hosted by Hen & Hops.
- Uncorked! — a wine-tasting event led by Daniel Jonberger, owner and sommelier at Durham’s Crush Wine Bar.
- Distilled — a gin, cheese and charcuterie experience hosted by the Ginologists from Tin of Sardines.
- 
    The masterclasses require separate tickets, but festival entry remains free for adults and children.
