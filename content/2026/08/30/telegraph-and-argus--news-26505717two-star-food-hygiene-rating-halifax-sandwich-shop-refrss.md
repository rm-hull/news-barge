A TAKEAWAY needs improvement where its food hygiene is concerned, according to a council inspection.

Noors Slice and Delights Ltd in Halifax has received good reviews from customers who are enjoying food the staff are serving up.

But improvements are necessary where food hygiene is concerned, say Calderdale Council.

The council awarded the takeaway and sandwich shop at Ovenden Road, Halifax, two stars on July 16, 2026.

## Positive reviews

Reviews on the takeaway’s social media show customers enjoying their food.

The comments include “these slices are a dream”, “first time trying these beautiful desserts, I’ll be back for sure”, “the flavours melt on your tongue” and “delicious and fresh.”

Another praises the quality of delivery the shop provides.

## Improvements to be made for food hygiene

However, in terms of food hygiene and safety, inspectors found improvements were needed in terms of hygienic food handling and the cleanliness and condition of facilities and building.

But there are indications the shop is on the right track, found the inspectors, with confidence in management adjudged generally satisfactory, meaning there are systems or checks in place to ensure that food sold or served is safe to eat, evidence that staff know about food safety, and the food safety officer has confidence that standards will be maintained in future.

The shop has been contacted by the Local Democracy Reporting Service for comment.

According to the Food Standards Agency, food hygiene ratings are scores from 0 to 5 awarded by local authority food safety officers after inspecting food businesses, with the aim of giving the public clear information about hygiene standards so they can make informed choices about where to eat or buy food.

Ratings are published for the public to see.
