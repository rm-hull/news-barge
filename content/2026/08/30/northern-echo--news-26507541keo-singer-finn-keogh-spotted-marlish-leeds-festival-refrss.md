There was a little bit of the North East on the Leeds Festival main stage on Saturday - thanks to KEO frontman Finn Keogh.

The singer was spotted drinking a can of Marlish water during KEO’s performance on the festival’s main stage.

Marlish is a North East-based water brand, making its appearance in Finn’s hand as the band performed to a huge crowd at Bramham Park.

  KEO at Leeds Festival 2026 

*(Image: LIAM RUSSELL)*

Finn and his KEO bandmates are currently enjoying a huge rise in popularity, with the Leeds Festival appearance coming just weeks before the release of their highly anticipated debut album, Put A Smile On For Me.

The album is due to be released on Friday, September 25, with the band set to follow it with an intimate UK tour, including a date in Newcastle.

KEO have already developed a strong following in the North East, making the Marlish moment a lovely connection between the band's growing fanbase and the region.

While Finn certainly wasn't making a grand statement about his choice of water, eagle-eyed festivalgoers spotted the North East brand as he took a well-earned drink during the performance.

So while KEO may have been in Yorkshire for Leeds Festival, there was at least a small taste of home on the main stage.
