A MAIN road over a river in the centre of York is to face closures due to survey works.

City of York Council said that Tower Street will be closed in phases between its junctions with The Castle (Eye of York) and Paragon Street.

The closures will be in place from midnight until 5am each day from Monday, September 7, to Saturday, September 12.

  



This is to allow for survey works to be carried out safely.

An alternative route for diverted vehicles will be signed via the York Inner Ring Road during the works period.

Traffic signs and barriers will indicate the extent of the prohibition.

The council said it is envisaged that the road will only be closed in one direction at a time.
