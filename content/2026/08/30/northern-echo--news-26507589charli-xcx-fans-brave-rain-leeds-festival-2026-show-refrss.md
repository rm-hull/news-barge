Rain poured down on Bramham Park as thousands of music fans gathered for Leeds Festival 2026, but it did little to dampen the excitement for Charli XCX.

The pop superstar was one of the weekend's biggest draws, with anticipation building from the moment the main arena opened at midday.

Despite the wet conditions, fans could be seen running towards the barrier to secure the best possible spot for Charli's performance despite knowing they had around eight hours to wait.

  Charli XCX at Leeds Festival 2026 

*(Image: LIAM RUSSELL)*

By the time the singer took to the stage, those who had spent much of the day at the front had endured hours of rain, but the mood remained remarkably upbeat.

There were smiles across the crowd as fans waited patiently for one of the weekend's most anticipated performances.

And when Charli finally appeared, the wait proved to be worth it.

Her set delivered a hit-packed run through her catalogue, opening with Rock Music before moving into Apple, Wink Wink and Card Declined.

She also performed fan favourites including Girl, so confusing, Pink Diamond, Club classics, 365, 360, Von Dutch and Guess.

The rain may have made for a very wet day at Bramham Park, but for the Charli XCX fans who had raced to the front of the crowd eight hours earlier, nothing was going to stop them from getting their moment.

And judging by the smiles in the crowd, they certainly weren't regretting it.
