A much-loved cat is on the mend after being diagnosed with a serious illness.

Coco, also known as the 'Keighley train station cat', became known around the area as a frequent visitor to the railway station, as well as a number of local businesses.

He could even be seen visiting Keighley College and Asda.

However, his absence from familiar haunts prompted concern from the community, leading to a heart-breaking discovery.

In July, Coco was diagnosed with pancreatitis and Feline Infectious Peritonitis (FIP), a disease caused by a mutation of feline coronavirus.

## Coco has 'reacted well' to treatment

Andrea Green, who works at Office Furniture Outlet on Dalton Lane, has been visited by Coco for several years.

She said: "He’s recovering really well.

"For the first few weeks, he was very lethargic and his belly was absolutely massive.

"But he’s reacted well to the medication and his stomach has got smaller."

Coco began treatment in July thanks to a GoFundMe page set up to cover his veterinary bills.

He is now in week eight of a 12-week treatment plan.

Ms Green said Coco visits the vet every Thursday for tests and scans.

## Remains 'the most adventurous cat ever'

Ms Green said: "Coco is the most adventurous cat ever.

"My day at work is so boring without him."

Whether the Keighley cat will return to the streets is unclear.

Ms Green said: "Everybody misses him but we don’t know when he will be back out on the street."

## The story behind the 'train station cat'

Ms Green said that when Coco started visiting her business, she did not think he had a home.

She said: "He used to come and sit in the office every day.

"We'd give him treats and he became part of the furniture company.

"We called him our company mascot and we actually called him Dave."

Staff thought Coco was a stray for more than a year, until he was scanned for a microchip and it was discovered he belonged to a resident.

He became increasingly well known around Keighley as he began to visit more areas.

Ms Green said: "He visits us, he visits the train station, he visits the college, he's even been to Asda."
