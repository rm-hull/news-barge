North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said its Acomb crew was called to Peter Hill Court in Clifton at 4.49am today (Sunday, August 30).

A service spokesperson said: “A fire in a garden was seen from a house further up the road.

“On arrival the crews found this to be a wheelie bin and a wheelbarrow of rubbish on fire.

“Crews used one hose reel jet to extinguish the fire.”

The spokesperson said they believed the fire was deliberately started and said police have been informed.
