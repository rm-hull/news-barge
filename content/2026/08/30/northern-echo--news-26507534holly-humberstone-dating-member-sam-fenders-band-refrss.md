Holly Humberstone brought her emotional indie-pop anthems to Leeds Festival on Friday with the singer having a rather special connection to the North East.

The BRITs Rising Star winner took to The Gallery at Bramham Park as part of this year's huge Leeds Festival line-up.

And away from the stage, Holly has a particularly strong link to Newcastle through her relationship with Joe, a musician who plays in Sam Fender's band.

Holly has previously spoken openly about their relationship, describing Joe as a musician she met at festivals while the pair were both working in the music industry.

  Holly Humberstone 

*(Image: Adam Kennedy)*

The couple have been together for several years, with Holly's relationship with Joe also inspiring some of the songs on her latest album Cruel World, including Red Chevy and Die Happy.

But on Friday, the focus was firmly on Holly as she delivered a set packed with fan favourites.

She opened with The Walls Are Way Too Thin, before launching into Cruel World, Die Happy and Falling Asleep at the Wheel.

The singer also treated fans to an unreleased track, Too Cute to Be Lonely, alongside White Noise, Red Chevy and Scarlett.

Holly's Leeds appearance comes ahead of a return to the North East, with the singer set to play NX Newcastle on January 23, 2027.

That means fans who caught her at Leeds now have another chance to see the singer this time in Newcastle, and considerably closer to her boyfriend's North East stomping ground.

Holly has previously collaborated with Sam Fender, joining him for an acoustic version of his huge hit Seventeen Going Under.

So while Leeds Festival may have been her latest stop, it won't be long before Holly Humberstone is back in the North East.
