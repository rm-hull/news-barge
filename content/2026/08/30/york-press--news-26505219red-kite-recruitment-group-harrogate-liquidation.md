A North Yorkshire hospitality and food company has gone into liquidation owing almost £300,000.

Harrogate-based Red Kite Recruitment Group was formed in 2009 but entered voluntary liquidation on July 10.

Companies House lists its directors as David and Jenna Nicholson.

Leeds-based Clough Corporate Solutions have been appointed liquidators.

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs says the business, which was registered at Tattersall House, East Parade, Harrogate, had assets of £6000 available to preferential creditors- those who are paid first.

The preferential creditors were four employees owed £3054, leaving £2946 available to secondary preferential creditors- those who are paid next.

The secondary preferential creditors consisted of HMRC, which was owed £30,886, leaving a deficiency of £27,940.

In addition, there was a debt to HSBC bank of £116,704 secured by a floating charge.

A floating charge is a flexible security interest or lien that a lender holds over a company's changing or fluctuating assets, such as inventory or accounts receivable.

This gave a deficiency of £144,644 after floating charges were considered.

The liquidator’s report said there were no assets available for unsecured creditors.

Altogether, there were unsecured claims totalling £151,207.

These comprised of four employees owed £67,243 between them, HMRC owed a further £1,816, a bank owed £29,369, a finance agent owed £7,695, the local authority owed £4,810, an accountant owed £1,800 and £1,530 owed in fines.

This gave an overall estimated deficit of £295,851.

With the business having ordinary shares and called up capital of £100, this gave a total outstanding deficiency of £295,951 owed.
