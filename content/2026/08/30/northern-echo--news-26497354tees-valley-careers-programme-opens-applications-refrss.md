Hundreds of young people across Tees Valley who are not in education, employment or training are being offered a fresh opportunity to get their careers back on track as a successful support programme returns for a second year.

The Youth Guarantee Trailblazer has reopened for applications, targeting 18 to 21-year-olds across the Tees Valley who are currently not in education, employment, or training (NEET).

Following a pilot year that drew interest from more than 1,700 young people, the scheme promises personalised support and practical pathways into work, training, or further study.

Tees Valley Mayor Ben Houchen said: "The first year of the programme showed what can be achieved when we back our young people with the right support at the right time.

  Ceinen Stark was one of eight trainees who were given paid work opportunities by charitable leisure trust Tees Active as part of the Tees Valley Youth Guarantee Trailblazer scheme. 

*(Image: Tom Banks)*

"We have huge projects delivering jobs now and we want all our young people to access these opportunities.

"We've already seen so many find opportunities with businesses across Teesside, Darlington and Hartlepool, gaining the skills, confidence and experience needed to succeed.

"That's why we're delighted to open applications again and help even more young people access the support, training and work opportunities available through the programme."

Each participant is guaranteed a meeting with a dedicated key worker, who will help identify individual needs and connect them with relevant support and opportunities.

This could include tailored careers advice, job search assistance, and training to develop workplace skills such as communication and confidence building.

Where appropriate, the programme also offers paid work placements of up to five months, with wages fully funded.

The Youth Guarantee Trailblazer is led by the Tees Valley Combined Authority and delivered through Stockton-on-Tees Borough Council and the region’s other local authorities.

The UK Government funds the scheme.

Cllr Lisa Evans, TVCA cabinet member for employment and skills, said: "The success of the first year has highlighted the difference this programme can make.

"Young people who may have faced barriers to employment have been supported into training, work placements and lasting opportunities, while employers have been able to access enthusiastic local talent.

"The Youth Guarantee Trailblazer is about ensuring every young person has somewhere to turn for advice, guidance and practical support.

"Whether it's help building confidence, finding training or securing valuable workplace experience, we're committed to making sure young people across Tees Valley can access the opportunities they deserve.

"I'd encourage both young people and employers to get involved."

The programme also offers extensive support for employers.

The Trailblazer team works to match candidates with suitable placements, and a key worker remains involved throughout to ensure the young person receives ongoing support and development.

Wages for placements are fully subsidised for up to 25 hours a week.

Training grants may also be available to help participants gain additional qualifications and skills.

In-work support includes developing training plans, accessing further education or professional development, and conducting progress reviews.

Past success stories from the scheme include young people securing roles at companies such as Hightech in Darlington, Axiom Events in Middlesbrough, and Alice House Hospice in Hartlepool.

To be eligible, applicants must be aged 18 to 21, not currently in education, employment, or training, and living in the Tees Valley area, which covers Darlington, Hartlepool, Middlesbrough, Redcar & Cleveland, and Stockton-on-Tees.

Employers interested in offering placements through the Youth Guarantee Trailblazer can register their interest via the Tees Valley Combined Authority website.

Eligible young people can also sign up online to begin the application process and connect with a key worker.
