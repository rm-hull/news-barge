A yellow weather warning for thunderstorms is in place for large parts of the North East later today (August 30).

The Met Office has put the warning in place for Darlington, Durham, Tyne and Wear, Northumberland and Teesside from 11 am until midnight.

According to the Met Office: "Showers will become widespread, heavy and thundery later Sunday morning, lasting through the afternoon and into Sunday evening.

"While some areas may miss the heaviest showers, where they do occur, 10-15mm of rain is possible in an hour or so, with 25-35mm possible in several hours. Lightning and hail will be additional hazards.

"Heaviest of the showers will ease from the west through the evening."

## What should people expect?

- There is a good chance driving conditions will be affected by spray, standing water and/or hail, leading to longer journey times by car and bus
- Delays to train services are possible
- Some short-term loss of power and other services is possible
- Some flooding of a few homes and businesses is possible, leading to some damage to buildings or structures

## Advice from the Met Office

According to the Met Office: "Give yourself the best chance of avoiding delays by checking road conditions if driving, or bus and train timetables, amending your travel plans if necessary.

"If you find yourself outside and hear thunder, protect yourself by finding a safe enclosed shelter (such as a car). Do not shelter under or near trees, or other structures which may be struck by lightning. If you are on an elevated area, move to lower ground.

"People cope better with power cuts when they have prepared for them in advance.
