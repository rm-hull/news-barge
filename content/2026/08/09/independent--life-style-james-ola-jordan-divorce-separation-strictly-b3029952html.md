Former *Strictly Come Dancing***stars James and Ola Jordan have spoken out about the “difficult personal challenges” that led to their split.

It was announced on Saturday that the couple, who have been married since 2003 and share a six-year-old daughter named Ella, are “taking some time apart”.

Their manager, Vickie White, said that the pair “began a trial separation some time ago”.

The Jordans have now shared a joint statement, posted across both of their Instagram pages.

“We have always tried to be open and honest with the people who have supported us over the years and we feel it’s only right that this update comes from us,” they said.

“At the moment we’re taking some time apart as we work through very difficult personal challenges.”

![Ola and James Jordan at Royal Ascot in 2018](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/08/8/46/GettyImages-1052601912.jpg)

The statement said that James “moved out of the family home some time ago”, and explained that their separation “wasn’t an easy decision”.

James, 48, and Ola, 43, are professional ballroom dancers and choreographers best known for their roles on *Strictly.*

The pair became household names after joining the show in 2006.

James featured on the show until 2013, partnering with stars such as Pamela Stephenson, Alex Jones, Denise Van Outen and Vanessa Feltz.

Ola competed on *Strictly* until 2015, where she danced with celebrities including DJ Spoony, Sid Owen, Steve Backshall and Iwan Thomas.

The Polish-born dancer also won the Glitterball trophy in 2009, partnered with TV personality Chris Hollins.

James and Ola conceived Ella through IVF, and she was born in 2020 following a widely publicised three-year fertility struggle.

Their statement continued: “Our absolute priority, now and always, is our beautiful daughter Ella. She’s at the centre of everything we do.

“We are committed to co-parenting her with all the love, care and stability she deserves.”

It also discussed James’ mental wellbeing, saying he “hasn’t been in a good place for quite some time”.

“I’ve been struggling more than I’ve let people see and I’m taking the time I need to focus on getting myself to a healthier place, both for myself and for Ella,” it added.

The statement continued: “Neither of us is in a good place right now.

“We’re both doing our best to navigate one of the hardest chapters of our lives and we ask that people remember there are real people and real emotions behind every headline, every comment and every post.

“We don’t know exactly what the future holds. For now, we’re taking things one day at a time, doing what we both believe is best for ourselves, our family, and most importantly Ella.”

The message concluded by thanking their followers, fans and supporters for “the love, kindness and concern” that they have shown.

The couple also asked for “kindness, empathy and a little privacy as we work through this as a family”.
