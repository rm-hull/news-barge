Europe’s biggest heatwave – But why aren’t people wearing sunscreen?

Misleading claims suggesting that sunscreen causes rather than protects against skin cancer are circulating online as Europe experiences record-breaking temperatures.

Influencers and podcasters are promoting the idea that sunscreen contains “toxic” chemicals and that its increased use correlates with rising skin cancer rates.

Experts, including cosmetic chemists and dermatologists, refute these claims, saying that there is no evidence that sunscreen increases the risk of skin cancer.

They say that the rise in skin cancer rates is attributed to factors such as an ageing population, improved detection methods, and incorrect application of sunscreen, not the product itself.

Sunscreens in both the UK and US are subject to stringent safety and efficacy regulations.
