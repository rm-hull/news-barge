# Burnham in ‘listening mode’ as he launches month-long tour of UK

- Andy Burnham is embarking on a national tour throughout August to engage with the public on the cost of living crisis and gather input for his 10-year plan.
- The prime minister aims to be in “listening mode” to understand how the government can improve people's lives and address everyday financial pressures.
- Mr Burnham has already introduced measures such as cutting VAT on energy bills, reducing business rates for venues, and capping bus fares at £2.
- He is expected to unveil further cost-of-living measures to provide “breathing space” for families.
- Opposition parties, including the Conservatives and Reform UK, have challenged Burnham on the funding for his pledges and criticised his announcements as “tinkering”.
