A casualty needed hospital treatment after their foot was impaled by a rake.

Emergency services were called to the incident at **12.02am this morning**(Sunday) at** Church Fenton**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from Tadcaster were requested by ambulance to cut down the length of a rake which had impaled the foot of a casualty prior to them being able to transport to hospital.

“A reciprocating saw was used.”
