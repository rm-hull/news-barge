A quad bike has been stolen from a farm in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information about the theft in Lebberston, near Scarborough, overnight on Saturday, June 27

The force on Sunday (August 9) issued a photograph of the red Honda Foreman 400 that was stolen, urging anyone who sees it to get in touch.

“If you can help our appeal, please email [steven.holloway@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:steven.holloway@northyorkshire.police.uk),” a police spokesperson said.

“If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

“Please quote reference 12260144570 when passing on information.”
