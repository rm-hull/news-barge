The Royal Navy has reported a significant increase in Russian naval activity near UK waters, saying that it spent 21 days in July monitoring Moscow's vessels.

Naval operations to track Russian ships surged by 25 per cent in July compared to 2025, with warships repeatedly traversing the Dover Strait and North Sea.

Offshore patrol vessels HMS Tyne, HMS Mersey, and HMS Severn were deployed to monitor Russian frigates Neustrashimy and Admiral Grigorovich, which escort “shadow fleet” tankers.

Russia uses its ageing shadow fleet to evade international sanctions on its oil exports, helping to fund its war in Ukraine.

The Ministry of Defence said that Russia is using up “valuable operational capacity” to constantly guard cargo.
