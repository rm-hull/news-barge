# Why Jannik Sinner has withdrawn from Cincinnati Open

- Jannik Sinner has withdrawn from next week's Cincinnati Open due to a right knee injury.
- His absence from the tournament casts doubt on his participation in the upcoming US Open.
- Sinner has not competed since winning his fifth Grand Slam title at Wimbledon earlier this summer, having also missed the Canadian Open.
- He stated that his focus is now on recovering to be fit for the US Open, which begins on 30 August.
- Fellow player Carlos Alcaraz also pulled out of the Cincinnati Open as a precaution due to a wrist injury.
