The A19 south of York is closed by a crash this evening.

It’s between Deighton and Crockey Hill. The road is shut in both directions.

The AA reports: “Road closed and queueing traffic due to crash on A19 both ways from Naburn Lane to Howden Lane.”

#### Crash location

The collision happened at about 4.30pm.

Emergency services are at the scene. The Yorkshire Air Ambulance has also gone to the incident.

There are reports that a car and a motorbike were in collision.
