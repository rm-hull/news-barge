The summer is a great time to get out with your family and visit the Yorkshire coast.

After spending a day on the beach, you might want to spend a few hours in the pub enjoying a pint or two.

At the Ship Inn in Sewerby, you can spend some time soaking up the sun in the pub’s beer garden that overlooks the sea.

The pub is a great spot to stop off at before heading home after a day exploring the seaside village.


## What is the Ship Inn in Sewerby like?

The Ship Inn is described as "the perfect place to soak up the sun" thanks to its huge beer garden packed with tables and picnic benches overlooking the sea .

The pub offers a range of award-winning cask ales, spirits, craft beers, and wines for those wanting a drink to enjoy while watching the waves.

After visiting the pub, the Yorkshireman said: "The Ship Inn, Sewerby, boasts the most fantastic beer garden on the Yorkshire coast, with unrivalled views of the waves crashing into the North Sea.

"Located beside the breathtaking Sewerby Hall and Gardens, and overlooking both Flamborough Head and Bridlington Bay, it’s a must-visit for anyone heading to the coast this summer."

As well as drinks , you can get a range of pub classic dishes from burgers to fish and chips as well as a Sunday carvery.
