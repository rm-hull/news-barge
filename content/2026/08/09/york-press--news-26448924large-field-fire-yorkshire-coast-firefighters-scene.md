Firefighters and farmers have joined forces to battle a large field fire in North Yorkshire.

Six fire crews from across North and East Yorkshire are on the scene in Hunmanby, near Filey, on Sunday afternoon (August 9).

*(Image: North Yorkshire Weather Updates)*

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said it was first called to the fire at a stubble field shortly before 12.50pm.

“Crews are currently working to get the fire under control. The fire is now surrounded,” a service spokesperson said.

Photographs on social media show smoke rising from the field with fire spreading to several bale stacks.

The incident comes after firefighters and farmers worked to extinguish a large field fire in Duggleby, near Malton, on Saturday night.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said that fire measured around 150m x 100m and was to a stubble and standing crop field.

Farm machinery was used to make fire breaks at the blaze in Duggleby which was then extinguished by the firefighters, the service said.
