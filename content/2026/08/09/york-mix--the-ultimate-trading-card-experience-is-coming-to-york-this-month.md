Calling all trading card fans, players, and collectors!

Get ready as THAT! Collector Show is coming to York with their Trading Card Convention – a day packed with everything you love about trading cards and collectibles.

THAT! Collector Show York will take place at York St John University Sports Park on **Sunday 16 August**.

THAT! Collector Show (T!CS) are dedicated to bringing ‘the ultimate trading card experience’, with the chance to buy, sell, and trade cards with a huge selection of trusted vendors.

At T!CS you’ll be able to discover hidden gems and complete your collection, meet with fellow fans and traders, and explore top brands such as Pokemon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Disney’s Lorcana, One Piece Card Game, and many more.

As well as trading tables, there will also be a marketplace of unique and creative goods with custom artwork and handmade crafts, toys, plushies, and collectibles, and free raffles throughout the day with prizes to be won.

The event is family-friendly and welcome to everyone, from casual collectors to hardcore fans.

Tickets start from £4.80 and are available via Eventbrite.

And why not make a whole day of it by visiting the newly opened Rocket’s Hideout on Micklegate that specialises in Pokemon trading card games – as well as other games including Warhammer, Magic The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh, and Dungeons & Dragons.

Happy trading!
