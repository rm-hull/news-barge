Police have been called in after another two deliberate fires were started in York.

Fire crews were called to an incident on **Tennyson Avenue** at**9.55pm last night** (Saturday).

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “York and Acomb fire appliances responded to a fire in a rear garden which had spread to a fence.

**Tennyson Avenue, York**

“Fire extinguished by crews using a hose reel with Police informed and cause determined as deliberate.”

Then, at **4.23am this morning**(Sunday), York firefighters were called to a tree on fire on ** Bishopthorpe Road**.

The spokesperson said: “Crews extinguished the fire using two knapsack sprayers. Police were informed as the cause of the fire was believed deliberate.”
