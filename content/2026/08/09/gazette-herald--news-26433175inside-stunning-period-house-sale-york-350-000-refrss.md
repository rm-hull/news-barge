A stunning period home full of character has hit the market in York.

The two-bedroom terrace house is for sale in Hartoft Street for £350,000.

Ashtons, which is marketing the property, said it is a “beautifully renovated period terrace house” that has been “thoughtfully redesigned to combine heritage character with contemporary style”.

  The two-bedroom period home for sale in Hartoft Street, York, for £350,000 

*(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

The current owners purchased the property six years ago and transformed it “from top to bottom to create an exceptional home”, the estate agent said.

Inside, an entrance hallway leads into an open plan living and dining space.

The lounge, meanwhile, features a cast iron fireplace and stove.

Upstairs, two double bedrooms and a family bathroom are on the first floor.

The bathroom features a freestanding bath with a shower, “creating a luxurious space to relax and unwind”, Ashtons said.

Outside, the home has a rear courtyard garden offering storage and a place for outside seating.

“This is a rare opportunity to acquire a turnkey period home finished to an exceptional standard in one of York's most desirable residential locations,” an Ashtons spokesperson said.
