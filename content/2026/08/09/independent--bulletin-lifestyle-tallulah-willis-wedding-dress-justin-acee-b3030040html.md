# Bruce Willis and Demi Moore’s daughter marries in dress that took 712 hours to make

- Tallulah Willis, the youngest daughter of Bruce Willis and Demi Moore, married her fiancé Justin Acee in an intimate ceremony held in Sun Valley, Idaho.
- The bride wore a custom Balenciaga bridal gown designed by creative director Pierpaolo Piccioli, one of his first bridal creations for the fashion house.
- The silk satin strapless column dress, featuring a draped train skirt and bow, took 712 hours to create, including 405 hours for construction and alterations, according to *Vogue.*
- Her mother, Demi Moore, and sisters, Rumer and Scout, were present during Tallulah's final dress fitting at the Chateau Marmont.
- Tallulah, an artist and writer, announced her engagement to Acee in December, with the couple having dated since 2023.
