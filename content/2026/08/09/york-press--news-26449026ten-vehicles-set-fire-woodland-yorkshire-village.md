Police have launched an investigation after 10 vehicles were set on fire in a North Yorkshire woodland area.

Firefighters were called to the scene in the village of Brotherton, south of Sherburn in Elmet, shortly after 2pm on Sunday (August 9).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said three crews were called to the incident in Old Great Brotherton Road.

The fire service said the crews extinguished the fire and the incident was left with police.

In a statement, the fire service said: “A crew from Selby responded to reports of a fire in the open.

“Crews requested an extra two appliances from Selby and Castleford to assist with 10 vehicles on fire in woodland.

“Crews extinguished the fire and the incident was left with the police.”
