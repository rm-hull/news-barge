# ‘Trump has lost this war’: Democrat senator blasts president over Iran conflict

- Connecticut Sen. Chris Murphy stated that the president has "lost this war" with Iran and will likely be forced into a "bad" deal to end the conflict.
- *The Wall Street Journal* reported that the president is privately considering abandoning his goal of destroying Iran's nuclear material, potentially accepting a deal that only reopens the Strait of Hormuz.
- Murphy expressed his willingness to support even a "terrible bad deal" to end the war and reopen the Strait, citing the ongoing economic pain and the administration's inability to secure better terms.
- Restrictions in the Strait of Hormuz, a critical shipping route, have caused significant increases in oil and gas prices, impacting American consumers and the global economy.
- Despite the president's claims of military success, the U.S. has been unable to guarantee safe passage through the Strait, with incidents like a tanker explosion highlighting continued dangers.
