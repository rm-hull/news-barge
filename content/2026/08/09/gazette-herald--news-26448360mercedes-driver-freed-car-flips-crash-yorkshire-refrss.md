A driver has been freed after their car flipped following a crash in North Yorkshire.

Firefighters were called to the collision in the village of Scrayingham, north of Stamford Bridge, at about 11.10pm on Saturday (August 8).

A Mercedes overturned after the crash in Lowfield Lane, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

The fire service said the driver was unharmed but had been left “trapped” by their seatbelt.

Firefighters released the seatbelt which allowed the driver to exit the vehicle, the service said.
