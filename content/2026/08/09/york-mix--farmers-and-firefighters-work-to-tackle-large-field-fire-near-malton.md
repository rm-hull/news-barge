Farmers and firefighters teamed up to tackle a large field fire in Ryedale last night (Saturday).

Crews from Malton, Pickering, Filey and Driffield, and the Kirkbymoorsides Argocat, went to the fire at **Duggleby** south of Malton at**7.52pm**.

The fire measured about 150m by 100m.

**Duggleby, Malton**

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Farm machinery was used to cut fire breaks around the fire.

“Crews used hose reel jets, knapsack sprayers and beaters to extinguish the fire and put a wet line in to prevent re-ignition of the fire.”
