# ‘Unmissable drama’ promised as EastEnders matriarch departs soap

- Kellie Bright has announced her departure from the BBC soap *EastEnders* after 13 years, saying that it is "now the right time" to say goodbye.
- Bright, 50, has portrayed the character Linda Carter since December 2013, becoming a central figure as an owner of the Queen Vic pub.
- Her character has been involved in numerous significant storylines, including sexual assault, murder, infidelity, and struggles with alcoholism.
- Bright made the decision over a year ago but agreed to extend her stay to participate in an "exciting storyline" planned by the producers.
- Executive producer Ben Wadey praised Bright's contribution and promising an "unmissable drama" for Linda Carter's departure.
