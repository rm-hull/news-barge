A Land Rover Defender overturned in a crash last night (Saturday).

Emergency services were called to the incident on **Horsefair, Boroughbridge** at**10.44pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews from Ripon and Harrogate responded to an overturned Land Rover Defender.

#### Horsefair, Boroughbridge

“Both occupants were out on arrival with the driver unharmed and the passenger requiring a precautionary check.

“Fire crew made the affected vehicle and a number of parked vehicles which it had clipped, safe.”
