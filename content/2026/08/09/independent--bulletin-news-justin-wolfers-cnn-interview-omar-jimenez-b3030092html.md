# Economist accidentally swallows fly during live CNN interview

- Economist Justin Wolfers accidentally swallowed a fly during a live CNN interview while discussing the U.S. economy.
- The incident occurred as Wolfers, a University of Michigan professor, was analyzing labor market data with anchor Omar Jimenez.
- After a brief struggle to speak, Wolfers humorously acknowledged the mishap on air, joking that "Aussies love eating flies!"
- He then used the unexpected event as a metaphor to discuss Federal Reserve monetary policy and the clarity of economic communication.
- Wolfers later posted about the incident on X, confirming the fly's demise and humorously stating he was "no longer a vegetarian."
