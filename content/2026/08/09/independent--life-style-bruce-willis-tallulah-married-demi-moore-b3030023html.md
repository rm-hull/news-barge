Tallulah Willis, the youngest daughter of Bruce Willis and Demi Moore, has married her fiancé Justin Acee in an intimate ceremony in Sun Valley, Idaho.

Tallulah wore a custom dress made by Balenciaga creative director Pierpaolo Piccioli. According to *Vogue*, the dress marked one of the first bridal gowns that Piccioli crafted in his role for the fashion house.

The magazine attended Tallulah’s final dress fitting the week before the wedding in a suite at the Chateau Marmont, where her mother and sisters, Rumer and Scout, were both present.

The bride told the publication that she and her husband “made a decision at the very beginning of our relationship that we were going to choose each other every day.”

She continued: “My biggest hope [for the dress] was that [it would be] something magical, but magical in a way that was unexpected, kind of like me.”

The mother-of-the-bride, *Ghost* actor Demi Moore, told the publication that Piccioli spent four hours with them during their first meeting discussing the dress.

“The day after his first couture collection for Balenciaga, he was with us for four hours,” she said. “And I think he would have stayed there playing all day.”

The strapless column dress is crafted from silk satin and features a draped train skirt and bow at the back.

*Vogue* reports that the dress took 712 hours of work, including 405 hours of construction and atelier alterations. An additional nine hours were dedicated to the veil. 

Tallulah, an artist and writer, announced her engagement to Acee in December, sharing an Instagram post that included a photo of the couple holding hands over a rose-petal pathway with her engagement ring visible. She captioned the post: “Everyday @justinacee.” The pair have been been dating since 2023.

Bruce and Demi are also parents to Rumer Willis, 37, and Scout Willis, 35, while the *Die Hard* actor shares two younger daughters with wife Emma Heming Willis: Mabel, 14, and Evelyn, 12.

Back in June 2024, Tallulah shared pictures of her and Accee spending time with her dad, who lives with frontotemporal dementia — which affects a person’s speech, personality and behavior.

Bruce receives 24-hour care and lives separately from his wife and their two young daughters.

The actor’s wife has continued to share updates about her husband’s health. In June, she said that he remembers key details about his life, including the identity of his family members around him.
