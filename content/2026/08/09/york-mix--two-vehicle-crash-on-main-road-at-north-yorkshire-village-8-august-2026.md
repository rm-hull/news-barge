Emergency services were called to a two-vehicle crash yesterday evening (Saturday).

It happened on the **A168 at Sowerby** at**5.30pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews from Richmond, Knaresborough and Ripon responded to a report of a two-vehicle road traffic collision.

**A168 at Sowerby**

“All persons were out on arrival of the crew. Crews made the vehicle safe and left the incident in the hands of police.”
