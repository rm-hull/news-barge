President Donald Trump posted AI-generated images on Truth Social depicting a White House “Droneport” as a military installation atop the planned ballroom.

He claimed the “DronePort” would provide national security for Washington, D.C. and protect future presidents.

This renewed emphasis on the project's security features follows a federal appeals court ruling that construction must cease until congressional approval is obtained.

The court's decision marks the second legal setback for the project, which Trump has frequently championed, often veering off-topic during speeches to discuss it.

Trump criticized the court's ruling, asserting it jeopardizes the safety of White House occupants by preventing the installation of crucial anti-drone defenses.
