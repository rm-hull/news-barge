# Yorkshire Belle: Passenger injured and medical emergency triggered by drone crashing into pleasure cruiser in Bridlington

## A passenger was injured and another suffered a medical emergency after a drone crashed into the vintage pleasure cruiser Yorkshire Belle as she sailed out of Bridlington Harbour.

Some 50 passengers were crowded on the deck of the Belle on Saturday when the machine hit a mast stay and fell to the deck, hitting a man on the forehead.

The noise of the sudden incident apparently triggered an epileptic fit in a passenger nearby, who was later taken to hospital by ambulance.

"(It) crashed into one of our mast stays and fell down and hit a gentleman on the head and caused quite a serious gash on his head – there was blood pouring out.

He said: “I don’t think he was taking picture of us, it was the sailing coble festival. We were sailing past at the time.”

He said police have reported the incident to the UK Civil Aviation Authority (CAA).

In a Facebook post, Yorkshire Belle asked operators to maintain a minimum distance of 50 metres and never fly directly towards the boat.

Last Thursday West Midlands Fire Service urged recreational drone users not to fly above their crews or emergency incidents as they were interfering with efforts to tackle wildfires.

The BBC reported that in one recent incident, firefighters were being directed to a hotspot by one of their own pilots using a drone as a marker, but mistakenly gathered under a drone operated by a member of the public instead.

According to the UK Civil Aviation Authority (CAA), there are approximately 720,000 active registered drone flyers and operators.

One commentator on social media described what happened as a “very rare” and “unfortunate” incident. Humberside Police have been approached for comment.

Yorkshire Belle, which was built in 1947, is the last surviving traditional pleasure boat of her kind on the Yorkshire coast, offering scenic summer trips toward Flamborough Head and Bempton Cliffs.
