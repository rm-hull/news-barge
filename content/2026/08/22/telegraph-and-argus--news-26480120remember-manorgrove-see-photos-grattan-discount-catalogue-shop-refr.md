IN 1986 Grattan’s third discount catalogue shop, Manorgrove, opened in St James Street, selling a range of goods, such as fashion, bedding, jewellery and hi-fi.

Here’s a look back at the store - and at Grattan’s Bradford HQ.

  Queuing for bargains at the opening of the Manorgrove store on James Street in March 1986


  In 1993 Grattan sold off most of its Manorgrove chain, keeping just two flagship stores - at Westfield opposite its Ingleby Road HQ and at Centre Point, Leeds


  Bargains galore...The Manorgrove shop in Laisterdyke pictured in 1984


  These shoppers had queued for over 13 hours to grab first day bargains


  Manorgrove was open Monday to Saturday, selling merchandise at low prices
