An off-road bike has been seized, after officers responded to reports from residents about a rider repeatedly travelling up and down a street in the area.

The incident was dealt with by officers from the Leeds District Off Road Bike Team, who attended following information provided by members of the public.

## Document check reveals offences

After locating the bike, officers carried out checks on the vehicle and its documentation.

The checks identified offences relating to the bike's use, leading officers to take enforcement action.

The bike was subsequently seized under Section 165 powers.

## Police praise residents for reporting incidents

Leeds District Off Road Bike Team said the seizure highlights the importance of information provided by local residents.

Officers said reports from the public help them identify areas experiencing problems with illegal or anti-social riding, allowing patrols to be directed where they are most needed.

## Public urged to continue reporting concerns

Police are encouraging residents to continue reporting issues involving off-road bikes and anti-social vehicle use.

They say information from local communities plays a key role in helping officers tackle nuisance riding and take action against those breaking the law.
