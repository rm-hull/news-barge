# Watchdog launches probe after fatal crash involving police car

![Police said they were working to identify the victims and contact their families](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/22/12/01M0MMTX9KBNH72N3FAHA57QT2.jpg)

- Multiple people have died following a collision involving a police vehicle and a Volkswagen Passat in Teesside.
- The fatal crash occurred at around 3.39am on Saturday on the A66 in South Bank, near Middlesbrough, after a police car launched a chase.
- North East Ambulance said that despite paramedics’ best efforts, a “number of people” had died at the scene.
- The Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) confirmed it has launched an investigation into the fatal crash.
- Officers are working to identify the victims and contact their families.
