Two convertible cars and a motorbike with a L plate have been involved in a road rage incident close to York city centre, police have revealed.

The incident happened during the afternoon rush hour on Thursday at the junction of Bishopthorpe Road and Nunnery Lane.

According to police the two convertibles were both Audis and one of them was white.

The other vehicle involved was a Lexmoto motorcycle with an L plate attached.

North Yorkshire Police are appealing to anyone with information about the road rage incident at 5.15pm on Thursday or who witnessed it to contact PC Clair Bailey-Lane by emailing [Clair.Bailey-Lane@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:Clair.Bailey-Lane@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, you can visit North Yorkshire Police’s website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’ or contact the force through "Live Chat" via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

A third option is to call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website
