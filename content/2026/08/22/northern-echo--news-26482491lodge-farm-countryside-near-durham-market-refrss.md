A four-bedroom countryside home with panoramic views, landscaped gardens, and its own paddock has come onto the market near Durham for £799,995.

Lodge Farm is set within an exclusive semi-rural hamlet and occupies around 2.5 acres, including an approximately one-acre paddock.

The detached home has four bedrooms, three bathrooms, and two reception rooms, with a spacious garden designed for entertaining, a double carport, and a converted 17th-century byre.

  Lodge Farm, Durham 

*(Image: Bradley Hall)*

The property is within the catchment area for Durham Johnston School and is a short drive from Durham city centre.

At the heart of the home is an open-plan kitchen and dining area with bespoke wall and base units, marble worktops, and integrated appliances.

Bi-fold doors open from the room onto the gardens, creating a connection between the indoor living space and the countryside outside.

The lounge also has bi-fold doors, as well as a log-burning stove.

  Lodge Farm, Durham 

*(Image: Bradley Hall)*

A utility room, cloakroom, and fourth bedroom, currently used as a home office, complete the ground floor.

Upstairs are three further double bedrooms, all of which have fitted wardrobes and countryside views.

  Lodge Farm, Durham 

*(Image: Bradley Hall)*

The master bedroom has an en-suite shower room, while the family bathroom includes both a bath and a separate shower enclosure.

  Lodge Farm, Durham 

*(Image: Bradley Hall)*

Outside, landscaped gardens include several seating and entertaining areas, a sunken seating area, outdoor kitchen and cooking space, and a private putting green.

The property also has a substantial block-paved driveway providing off-road parking and a detached double carport with electric roller doors.

  Lodge Farm, Durham

One of the home’s most distinctive features is a converted 17th-century byre, which is fully insulated and has electricity.

It is currently used as a games and entertainment room, with a mezzanine office above, though it could also be used as a gym, studio, annexe, creative workspace, or for a home business.
