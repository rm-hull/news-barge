The Met Office is predicting a dry and bright weekend across York, with temperatures reaching 20°C.

Saturday will start cool and cloudy, with temperatures around 11°C at 6am, dropping to 9°C by midday.

The chance of rain remains low at less than 5 per cent, with light winds of around 4 to 5 mph.

Sunny intervals will develop from midday, with temperatures climbing to 16°C by 5pm.

The evening will remain bright, with temperatures reaching 18°C by 8pm.

Winds will stay gentle at around 3 to 5 mph, with the chance of rain staying below 5 per cent.

Later in the night, temperatures will fall to around 12 to 14°C, with partly cloudy conditions becoming clearer.

The risk of rain will remain very low, briefly reaching 10 per cent before dropping below 5 per cent again.

Sunday is expected to be warmer and sunnier, with virtually no indication of rain.

Clear skies overnight will allow temperatures to fall to 8°C by 6am.

The morning will see plenty of sunshine, with temperatures rising rapidly to 15°C by 10am and 17°C by 11am.

Sunny intervals will develop, with winds remaining around just 2 mph and a less than 5 per cent chance of rain.

The afternoon will be warm and settled, with temperatures reaching 20°C by 2pm and staying close to 20°C through 6pm.

The probability of rain will remain below 5 per cent, accompanied by exceptionally light 2 to 3 mph winds.

The evening will remain mild, with temperatures falling to 16°C by 9pm.

Conditions will become partly cloudy during the evening, with winds increasing slightly but remaining light at around 3 to 4 mph.

The chance of rain will remain below 5 per cent.

Late at night, skies will become clear again around 10pm, with temperatures near 14°C.

Rain will remain below 5 per cent, with winds around 3 to 4 mph.
