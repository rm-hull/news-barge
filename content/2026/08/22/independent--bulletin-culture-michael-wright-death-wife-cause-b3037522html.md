# Actor Michael Wright’s wife announces death of ‘beloved’ husband

![Michael Wright, right, with fellow ‘The Five Heartbeats’ actors, from left, Tico Wells, Leon Robinson, and Robert Townsend in 2011](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/22/11/55/GettyImages-117478134.jpeg)

- Actor Michael Wright, best known for playing Eddie King Jr in the 1991 musical drama *The Five Heartbeats* , has died at the age of 70.
- His family confirmed he died in Los Angeles on Wednesday after suffering heart failure coupled with complications from Marchiafava-Bignami disease, a rare, progressive neurological disorder.
- His wife, Susan, paid tribute to her “beloved” husband on Friday, describing him as “an extraordinary artist whose work touched generations.”
- Wright made his feature film debut in 1979's *The Wanderers* and went on to win the Volpi Cup for Best Actor for his performance in*Streamers* in 1983.
- His extensive screen credits also included the sci-fi franchise *V,* HBO's prison drama*Oz, Sugar Hill*, and the series* Black Lightning.*
