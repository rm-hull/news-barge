# Dolly Parton says she neglected her health while caring for late husband

- Country music icon Dolly Parton has provided a health update, revealing she neglected her own health while caring for her late husband.
- The 80-year-old singer recently pulled out of an appearance at her Dollywood theme park due to dizziness and dehydration.
- In an interview with *People* , Parton addressed online speculation surrounding her condition, saying that while she had gone through “hard times”, social media had magnified her health issues.
- The update follows the cancellation of her Las Vegas residency in May 2025 due to ongoing, “treatable” health challenges.
- Parton earlier reassured fans that she is responding well to medication and treatment while continuing to work as she heals.
