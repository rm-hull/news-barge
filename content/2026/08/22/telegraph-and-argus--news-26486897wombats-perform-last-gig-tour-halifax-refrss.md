A top-quality indie band in a scenic, iconic venue: what more could you ask for on a Friday night.

Liverpool's The Wombats have been creating hits for two decades and bringing them to life on the road.

The three-piece chose their first-ever Halifax show as the perfect way to bring the curtain down on sixth album Oh! The Ocean.

The Piece Hall crowd loved jumping to a selection of banging tunes, leaving a joyous atmosphere to enjoy.

## Circa Waves a good opener

In support was self proclaimed Wombats fans Circa Waves.

The fellow Liverpudlians produced a good compliment to the headliners.

  Tunes like *Movies*,* Fire That Burns*and, of course,* T-shirt Weather* were frankly brilliant.

  Circa Waves played before the headliners 

*(Image: CUFFE & TAYLOR)*

Lead singer Kieran Shudall was very complimentary of Halifax - jokingly saying he prefers it over San Francisco (the title of one of the band's songs).

Shudall added: “I've never been to Halifax before but it is sick as f***.”

## Kicking start to The Wombats

  A nice movement into the main act was the band saying tune *La Bateau* was named after a club in Liverpool where they used to dance to The Wombats.

Speaking of the trio - made up of vocalist Matthew Murphy, bassist Tord Øverland Knudsen and drummer Dan Haggis - they didn't half open up their show with a bang.

  A sold-out Piece Hall crowd 

*(Image: CUFFE & TAYLOR)*

  *Moving to New York* and*Techno Fan* went down a treat with everyone in the historic hall.

  *Kill the Director* was the next bopping banger before the supremely talented Mr Wombat made an appearance during*Ready for the High*.

As expected in a 19-song set, there were peaks and troughs - a lot more of the former, may I add.

## Murph is one funny guy

What really came across was frontman Murph's fun personality.

He would often offer antidotes on the mic before beginning another track.

The 42-year-old told the sold-out crowd: “This is our first time here and what a beautiful venue.”

  Murph is one funny guy 

*(Image: CUFFE & TAYLOR)*

## Wonderful ending

I'm a sucker for an acoustic solo to bring down the mood before firing it right back up again.

  This was in the form of the perfect *Lethal Combination* followed by*Blood On the Hospital Floor* and*Lemon to a Knife Fight*.

  The final four songs were fantastic - *Tokyo (Vampires & Wolves)* and*Turn* before they ended with a storming two-song encore of*Greek Tragedy* and the iconic*Let’s Dance To Joy Division.*
