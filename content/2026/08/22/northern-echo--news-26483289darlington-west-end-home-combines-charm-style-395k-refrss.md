Set in Darlington’s West End – on one of the area’s most characterful streets – is this spacious property that has been sympathetically renovated by the current owners to an exceptionally high standard to create a beautifully presented home that effortlessly combines timeless period charm with the comforts of modern living.

The property’s entrance vestibule opens into an impressive reception hall via a door (and side panels) featuring stained glass panels.

Off the hall is the elegant lounge with a double-glazed sash bay window overlooking the front elevation and a feature fireplace housing a log-burning stove that creates a warm and inviting focal point for the room.

  This home in Darlington's West End has much to appeal to buyers who want period character in a property designed for modern family living. 

*(Image: Anne Cordey)*

To the rear of the property is the dining room – also with a log-burning stove – which enjoys direct access to the garden via French doors.

Completing the ground floor is a pantry providing additional storage and space for white goods, and the contemporary kitchen, which is flooded with natural light thanks to Velux windows and is home to a range of quality fitted units complemented by contrasting work surfaces which sit seamlessly alongside original built-in cupboards that add a touch of character.

The first floor is home to a luxurious family bathroom with a freestanding bath plus three generously proportioned bedrooms.

  This home in Darlington's West End has much to appeal to buyers who want period character in a property designed for modern family living. 

*(Image: Anne Cordey)*

Upstairs again, the entire second floor has been given over to the impressive principal bedroom suite, which is made up of a spacious bedroom and a recently refitted contemporary shower room.

Externally, the property has a front courtyard with mature trees and established shrubs providing privacy, plus a fully enclosed garden to the rear with paved seating areas and a charming snug, all of which create the perfect space for relaxing and entertaining during the warmer months.
