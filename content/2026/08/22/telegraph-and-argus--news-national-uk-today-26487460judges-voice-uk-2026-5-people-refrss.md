The Voice UK is finally back on our screens after a two-year gap, and fans can expect a few new aspects to the show.

One of those is a new coach in former Destiny's Child member Kelly Rowland, alongside a change to the blind auditions.

The Rewind button will give each coach a chance to change their mind about a contestant who has left the stage without a chair turn.

The coach can then press the Rewind button and invite the act back to join their team.


Emma Willis is also back presenting, though it remains unclear if she will return as host for the future, as she is now one of three presenters on Strictly Come Dancing.

## Who are the coaches on The Voice UK 2026?

Kelly Rowland joins Sir Tom Jones, will.i.am, Tom Fletcher and Danny Jones among the coaches.

Rowland was one of the members of Destiny's Child and has had an award-winning solo career outside of that.

Sir Tom Jones is a Welsh singer whose career began with a string of top 10 hits in the 1960s, and he has since toured regularly.

Some of his hits include 'It's Not Unusual', 'What's New Pussycat?' and the theme song for the James Bond film Thunderball.

Meanwhile, will.i.am is an American rapper, singer, songwriter, and record producer who is known for his work with the Black Eyed Peas.


Finally, Tom Fletcher and Danny Jones are members of the band McFly, and share a double chair together.

It was announced in June that Kelly would be replacing singer LeAnn Rimes as coach on the show.
