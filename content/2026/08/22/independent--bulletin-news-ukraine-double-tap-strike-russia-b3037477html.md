# Ukraine retaliates for Russia’s ‘double-tap’ strike on shopping mall

- Ukrainian drones have targeted a Russian oil refinery and an online retailer warehouse, injuring several people in the Samara region.
- The strikes follow a ‘double-tap’ Russian attack on a shopping centre in central Ukraine that killed 16 people and wounded more than 130 others.
- Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky earlier condemned the shopping centre attack as a “terrorist act” and promised a firm response.
- At least nine people, including two children, remain missing and are believed to be trapped under the rubble of the shopping centre.
- Subsequent overnight Russian drone and missile strikes killed two further people in Kyiv and Zaporizhzhia.
