AN inspiring floral festival is set to brighten up the city with blooms, creativity, and community spirit.

The Sheffield Flower Festival will take place on Sunday in Neepsend, spanning two nearby venues: The Mowbray on Mowbray Street and Victoria on Rowland Street.

The Mowbray will host a flower and makers market featuring Yorkshire-grown blooms and nature-inspired crafts.

Visitors can enjoy coffee, cakes, and savoury treats in the main room or on the rooftop.

Victoria will open its Clubhouse Social at 9am, offering cocktails and a special Fire Kitchen menu throughout the day.

Highlights include a talk by Gardener’s World presenter Rekha Mistry on growing flowers in small spaces, and an Ikebana demonstration by master Junko Popham.

In The Courtyard, guests can enjoy seasonal food, botanical cocktails, a plant sale, and family-friendly activities.

Advance tickets are £2, or £3 on the door.
