Househunters looking to buy a spacious family home that also offers the potential for more might find that this property in a hamlet just outside the village of Tantobie has everything they want and more.

Its ground-floor accommodation comprises an entrance hall with a cloakroom off it, a spacious living room with double French doors that open onto a paved patio area and then into the gardens beyond, a utility room and an open-plan kitchen diner that flows seamlessly into the adjoining family room, forming the heart of the home and creating an environment perfect for everyday life and gatherings alike.

The dining area, with a feature period-style fireplace and ample room for a large table, is sandwiched between the modern kitchen that’s home to a wealth of fitted units and a range-style cooker, and the living area, with a fireplace housing a stove.

  At the heart of this village home is an open-plan kitchen diner with adjoining family room 

*(Image: youngsRPS)*

The first floor is home to a stylish family bathroom and four well-proportioned bedrooms, which include the principal bedroom with an en-suite shower room, while upstairs again is a WC, a substantial storage area and a generous bedroom that could be utilised as a principal suite, guest accommodation, a teenager’s retreat or a home office.

The house is complemented by generous wrap-around gardens with patio seating areas, lawns, mature planting, a pond and a storage shed. There is also ample parking.

An added bonus is a detached outbuilding located to the rear, which benefits from planning permission for conversion into a self-contained annexe suitable for multigenerational living or potential use as an income-generating space. A further detached outbuilding, currently used as a home gym, offers a versatile space suitable for a variety of uses.
