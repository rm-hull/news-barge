# Major store reveals opening date in Harrogate for new customers facility after relocation

## A major UK retailer has unveiled details about its new store showroom in Harrogate as the opening date approaches.

Magnet Kitchens said it is to launch a showroom at 5 Albert Street on Tuesday, August 25.

The new showroom will feature kitchen displays, storage solutions and free design appointments with customers able to meet designers to plan their kitchens.

![The Mayor of Harrogate, Councillor Viv Poskitt, who will cut the ribbon to officially open Magnet’s new Harrogate store.  (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmVmMWFmNGFkLTBmNDEtNDI0ZC1hZTg5LWE2MmEwMjNiYTk2MToyODI3Y2EwMS1mYjI2LTRiOTEtYWM5Ni1kMzVmNzdkMjY4OWE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



On hand at the launch ceremony will be The Mayor of Harrogate, Councillor Viv Poskitt, who will cut the ribbon to officially open Magnet’s new Harrogate store.

Sophie Rose, CEO of Magnet, and James Cheetham, Store Operations Director, will join the Mayor at the ceremony along with Magnet colleagues from the Harrogate store.

Magnet has more than 150 showrooms across the UK, including branches in Guiseley, Bingley and Skipton.

Its Ripon Road showroom is closed while the relocation takes place.
