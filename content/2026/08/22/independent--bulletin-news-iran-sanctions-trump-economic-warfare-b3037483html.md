# Trump claims Iran ‘not ready’ for deal after Tehran calls for end to war

- US President Donald Trump has claimed Iran is not ready “to make the right deal”.
- It comes after he threatened Tehran with unprecedented “Economic Warfare and Isolation”.
- Iran accused the US of "economic terrorism" following US Treasury plans to introduce the toughest sanctions in history against the nation.
- Iranian foreign ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei said that that the upcoming US secondary sanctions find no foundation in international law.
- Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has called for an end to the war with the US, saying that “we are in a position of power and dignity”.
