Johnny Depp will be in Halifax tonight.

The legendary actor is set to take to the Piece Hall stage with his band Hollywood Vampires.

The rock supergroup is made up of him, Alice Cooper, and former Aerosmith man Joe Perry.

Fans from across the region are expected to gather for the performance at the sold-out show.


## UK tour for Hollywood Vampires

Hollywood Vampires kicked off their summer run at The O2 in London on August 12.

They then headlined further UK shows, including a gig at Scarborough's Open Air Theatre.

In January, the band said: “After almost three years away, we’re finally coming back to tour the UK and Europe.

“ We’re looking forward to incredible crowds, massive energy and more unforgettable nights.

“See you all soon!”

## Who are the Hollywood Vampires?

Formed in 2015, Hollywood Vampires bonded over a shared love of their favourite songs and a “desire to celebrate their ‘dead, drunk friends’ by playing the music of rock’s fallen heroes”.

Named after Cooper’s 1970s drinking club, which included the likes of John Lennon, Keith Moon and Mickey Dolenz, Hollywood Vampires “quickly earned a reputation for blistering live shows - a riotous mix of original anthems and tributes to the legends who inspired them”.

Their sets are a “raucous celebration of rock’s greatest icons, performed alongside their own powerhouse material”.

Hollywood Vampires’ shows feature classics from The Who, Led Zeppelin, David Bowie, Motörhead, The Doors, AC/DC and more, alongside hits from Cooper and Aerosmith.

There is a documentary in the works called “Unleashed Spirits – The Rise of The Hollywood Vampires”.

## The Damned also set to perform

Special guests in Halifax tonight will be true punk and Goth-Rock icons The Damned.

With 12 studio albums and several UK chart singles under their belts, the legends mark their 50th anniversary with a new tour and album.

## Johnny Depp in Halifax

Although best known for his acclaimed acting career, Depp has long been a passionate musician and has earned praise for his work with Hollywood Vampires.

The band, which blends hard rock energy with a celebration of music's most influential figures, has built a loyal following through its dynamic live shows and star-studded line-up.

Tonight's show is expected to deliver a memorable evening of entertainment, combining powerful musicianship, classic rock anthems and the unmistakable appeal of one of Hollywood's biggest names.

For many fans, it is a rare opportunity to witness Depp in a completely different spotlight.
