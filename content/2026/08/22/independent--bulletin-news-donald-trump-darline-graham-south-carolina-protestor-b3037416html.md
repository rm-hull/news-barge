# Trump unfazed as protester interrupts rally for Darline Graham

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/Dx1cdHX1/poster.jpg?width=720)

- President Donald Trump's campaign rally in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, was briefly interrupted by a protester wearing a shirt that read "Kill Your Local Pedophile."
- Trump brushed off the disruption, joking that the protester's mother was voting for him, before the individual was escorted out by security.
- The event served as a campaign pitch for Senator Darline Graham ahead of South Carolina's Republican primary runoff against Representative Ralph Norman.
- Trump defended Graham after she recently admitted during a debate that she was "not that informed" on national security, praising her focus on affordability instead.
- Trump also highlighted a Supreme Court ruling that permits construction to continue on a $400 million White House ballroom.
