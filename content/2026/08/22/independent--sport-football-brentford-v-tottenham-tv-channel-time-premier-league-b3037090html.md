Tottenham Hotspur have caught the attention of the rest of the Premier League with their lavish outlay this summer and travel across London to Brentford to kick off their campaign.

Spurs will field some new faces, including Marcos Senesi, Jan Paul van Hecke, Andy Robertson, Sandro Tonali and Mateus Fernandes, while their spending is rocketing towards £400m after agreeing a deal to sign Savinho for £85m and the Brazilian’s Man City teammate Omar Marmoush is also a target.

The Bees are settled under Keith Andrews and could kick on again this season after a fine ninth place finish last term.

The evening kick-off on Saturday brings plenty of intrigue with this London derby ready to deliver entertainment during the opening weekend.

Here’s everything you need to know about the match and how to follow:

## When is Brentford v Tottenham?

Brentford host Tottenham at the Gtech Community Stadium on Saturday 22 August, with kick-off at 5:30pm BST.

## How can I watch it?

Viewers in the UK can watch the match on Sky Sports Main Event and Sky Sports Premier League. If you’re not a Sky customer, you can grab a NOWTV Day Pass here to watch without a subscription. Coverage starts at 5:30pm.

## Team news

Sepp van den Berg and Antoni Milambo are out, but otherwise Andrews has a fully fit squad.

Jaidon Anthony, Callum Wilson and international Mamadou Sangare could all make their debuts.

Micky van de Ven and Pedro Porro are out, while Pape Matar Sarr (hamstring) will not be risked either.

New signings Marcos Senesi, Jan Paul van Hecke and Andrew Robertson could all make their debuts.

Xavi Simons (ACL), Wilson Odobert (knee), Dejan Kulusevski (knee) and Mohammed Kudus (thigh) are all out, while Dominic Solanke, Mateus Fernandes and James Maddison are all in contention.
