North Yorkshire style will take centre stage at the UK's largest retail buying show this September.

Businesses from the county are preparing to exhibit at Autumn Fair, which runs from September 6 to 9 at the NEC Birmingham and showcases the latest trends in home, gift, and fashion.

Matthew Mein, event director of Autumn Fair, said: "Yorkshire has a long-standing reputation for quality manufacturing, craftsmanship, and independent enterprise.

"Autumn Fair provides the ideal platform for these businesses to showcase their products to retailers looking for something new and distinctive."

Ripon-based Twenty Birds will present its modern geometric card and giftware collections, which have expanded beyond their original bird motif to include flowers, cats, dogs, and ferrets.

Founder and designer Monica Gabb will unveil new collections at the show, including Nose to Toes, featuring embossed and foiled cat and dog designs, and a playful new Baked Goods range of die-cut cards inspired by eclairs, baguettes, and iced buns.

Walton & Co, of Boroughbridge, a heritage brand dating back to 1785, will also be in attendance.

The company designs table linens, kitchen textiles, rugs, and soft furnishings, blending contemporary style with long-standing craftsmanship.

Its in-house team creates seasonal collections by translating emerging trends in colour, fashion, and interiors into seasonal collections for the home.
