A restaurant in Craster has been described as having “excellent food” and is in a “great location” overlooking the sea.

If you are looking for somewhere to escape to this summer, you might want to try the scenic village of Craster.

Craster is home to the award-winning seafood restaurant, the Jolly Fisherman.

The Jolly Fisherman is found in a historic pub with “stone flagged floors, low beamed ceilings and comfortable seating”.


## What is the Jolly Fisherman in Craster like?

The Jolly Fisherman’s website says: “Combining stunning sea views with delicious home cooking and beautifully kept ales.

“This is a tastefully refurbished pub with tradition and character at its heart.”

While having a meal at the restaurant, you will be able to enjoy one of “Northumberland’s most coveted eateries with panoramic views of Dunstanburgh Castle”.

There is also a beer garden where you can relax with a pint and listen to the waves crashing against the coastline.

  The restaurant offers incredible views of the sea. 

*(Image: Tripadvisor)*

The restaurant offers a huge range of wines, beers and spirits, as well as lots of soft drinks for those who are looking for a non-alcoholic option.

The restaurant’s lunch menu has plenty of sandwiches to choose from, including The Jolly’s Famous Crab Sandwich and a poached salmon option with chive crème fraîche.

The evening menu features starters ranging from Lindisfarne oysters and a Craster Kipper Scotch egg to Craster smoked salmon mousse.

There are lots of seafood mains, like sea bream and the Jolly’s fish pie, as well as non-fish choices.


The Jolly Fisherman’s Sunday roast features duck-fat roast potatoes, gravy, carrots, parsnips and a Yorkshire pudding, and you can choose from beef, lamb, pork and a fillet of salmon.
