Here are the latest planning applications submitted to North Yorkshire Council for Pickering, Ebberston, Fadmoor, Sinnington, and Hovingham.

Two separate applications have been submitted to convert agricultural buildings into homes.

The first, at Red Oak House on Outgang Road in Pickering, proposes the creation of two dwellings with associated gardens and parking.

The second, at Lingy Plantation on Malton Cote Road in Ebberston, also seeks to convert an existing agricultural building into two homes, including building works and infrastructure.

Ref. No: ZE26/00546/GPAGB | Received: Tue 23 Jun 2026 | Validated: Tue 23 Jun 2026 | Status: Unknown

The Bay Horse Inn in Pickering has received approval for the installation of illuminated and non-illuminated signage.

The application was submitted on June 5 and validated on the same day.

Sinnington Methodist Church has been granted permission to install an accessible ramp.

The work will involve removing a section of the boundary wall, replacing the porch door, and altering a kitchen window.

Ref. No: ZE26/00467/FUL | Received: Mon 01 Jun 2026 | Validated: Wed 17 Jun 2026 | Status: Approved

Low Hagg House in Fadmoor has been granted permission to add an orangery to the rear of the property.

Ref. No: ZE26/00214/HOUSE | Received: Thu 12 Mar 2026 | Validated: Thu 19 Mar 2026 | Status: Approved

Hiblings Farm on Malton Road in Pickering has received approval for use as a four-wheel drive vehicle testing centre and driving experience course.

The application includes a driver training school, a 30-space car park, a toilet block, and landscaping.

Some elements of the development are retrospective.

Ref. No: ZE26/00042/MFUL | Received: Thu 22 Jan 2026 | Validated: Tue 24 Feb 2026 | Status: Approved

Plans have been approved to create ten in-channel woody structures, known as leaky dams, and one bankside floodplain barrier.

The work will take place east of Wath Beck in Hovingham.
