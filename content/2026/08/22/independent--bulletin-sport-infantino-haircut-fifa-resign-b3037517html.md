# Infantino brushes off calls to resign with haircut joke

![Gianni Infantino was confronted by a reporter in the Dominican Republic](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/10/01KZ79CR1DNVBXJ9PQQJWCYZ0B.jpg)

- Fifa president Gianni Infantino brushed off calls to resign by making a joke about a reporter's haircut when confronted over an abandoned World Cup sell-off scheme.
- Infantino was challenged in the Dominican Republic after three continental confederations accused him of “deception” regarding efforts to introduce private investment.
- When a journalist – who is bald, like Infantino – asked why he had not resigned, Infantino replied: ”You have a nice haircut. Thank you so much.”
- When pressed further by the journalist, Infantino said: “I’ll see you at the hairdressers.”
- Uefa has threatened a boycott of Fifa competitions, saying that it has lost confidence in Infantino's leadership.
