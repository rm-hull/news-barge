Police are investigating a road rage incident at a busy York junction.

It happened at the junction of Bishopthorpe Road and Nunnery Lane on Monday 20 July at 5.15pm.

The incident involved a Lexmoto motorbike with an L plate on it and two convertible Audi cars, one of which was white.
