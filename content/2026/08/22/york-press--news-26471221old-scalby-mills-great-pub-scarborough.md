During the summer, many families will flock to Scarborough to enjoy the beach and traditional seaside activities.

While visiting the popular seaside resort, you might be looking for somewhere to have a meal with your family.

Having been open for over 20 years, you could try out the Old Scalby Mills pub.

The pub is right next to the Scalby Beck and offers breathtaking views of the surrounding countryside and sea.


## What is the Old Scalby Mills like?

The pub is in an old, converted watermill that was built in the 17th century.

The pub’s website says: "During the off-peak months, there is much satisfaction to be had from sitting in the lounge at the Old Scalby Mills, Scarborough and staring through the huge picture window which overlooks the whole of the bay as far as the castle.

"The stormier the weather, the cosier you feel watching the waves hit the sea wall while you tuck into one of the decent platefuls of food or simply nursing a pint of North Bay Ale, made solely for the pub by Wold Top Brewery."

Old Scalby Mills sources all of its meat locally from Nockels, the longest-established butcher in Scarborough, with all of its meals being cooked to order.

At the Scarborough pub, you can find many pub classics from haddock and chips and steak pie to chicken tikka masala and even a giant Yorkshire Pudding filled with roast beef, peas, chips, and gravy.


If you want something a little lighter, the Old Scalby Mills also offers baguettes and paninis served with a side salad.
