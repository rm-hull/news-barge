Looking for a case for your brand-new Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold? You've come to the right place. The Pixel 11 Pro Fold is one of Google's new handsets. It was announced in August 2026 alongside the standard Pixel 11, the 11 Pro, and the 11 Pro XL. Those are all pretty good (we enjoyed testing them out), but the 11 Pro Fold stands out for being the only foldable option of the bunch.

The foldable phones market is looking increasingly competitive right now. In his Pixel 11 Pro Fold review, our editor-at-large Lance crowned it "the best folding Pixel yet"... although for him it wasn't quite up there with Samsung's finest.

If you've decided the Pixel 11 Pro Fold is the right foldable for you — perhaps you snagged a preorder deal? — then you'll want to get a case sorted. The phone is a slightly more niche choice, which means there aren't tons of case options to choose from, but I was pleasantly surprised to find most of my favorite case brands have at least one model suitable for the Pixel 11 Pro Fold. You'll find my top picks in the US and in the UK below.
