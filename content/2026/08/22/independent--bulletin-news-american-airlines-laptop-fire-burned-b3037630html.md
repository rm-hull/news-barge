# Plane passenger treated for burns after laptop catches on fire during flight

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/22/21/2026-08-18T130006Z_1600962746_RC2M0NAZ9QQ7_RTRMADP_3_AMERICAN-AIRLINE-CABINS.JPG)

- American Airlines Flight 2398 landed safely at Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport on Friday after a laptop battery caught fire in flight.
- Flight attendants successfully contained the burning device using a thermal containment bag aboard the Airbus A321, which was carrying about 133 people from Atlanta.
- The flight's pilot reported that four to five passengers suffered burns to their hands during the incident.
- Emergency medical crews met the plane upon landing, where one patient was treated for injuries and released.
- The Federal Aviation Administration warned that lithium-ion batteries can undergo thermal runaway without warning, causing dozens of aircraft fires annually.
