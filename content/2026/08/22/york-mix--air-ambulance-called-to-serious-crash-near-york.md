There’s been a serious crash near York this morning (Saturday).

Emergency services were called to the incident on **Wheldrake Lane** near**Elvington** shortly after 11am.

The Yorkshire Air Ambulance also went to the scene.

#### Map location

Reports say a motorbike and a cyclist are involved.

The road was closed. A ***YorkMix*** listener said people trying to travel to Elvington from Crockey Hill were being turned round.
