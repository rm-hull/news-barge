A million-pound Edwardian house boasting a range of features has gone up for sale.

Shadwell Towers, located on Western Terrace in East Boldon, was originally constructed in 1901 and has undergone extensive renovations in its half-acre south-facing plot.

Shadwell Towers was purchased by its current owners in 2024 and has since been further remodelled and refurbished, with every aspect considered.

Inside, the house features a bespoke Newcastle Kitchen, Karndean flooring throughout the ground floor, and a remodelled principal living space.

   

*(Image: Sanderson Young)*

The property offers six double bedrooms, four bathrooms, and five reception rooms over three floors.

The principal bedroom has a bay window seating area and an ensuite shower room.

One of the ground floor spaces was transformed into a luxurious ensuite bathroom featuring a freestanding copper bath and matching copper basin.

   

*(Image: Sanderson Young)*

In terms of leisure facilities, the house boasts a cinema room, a snug/whiskey room, a steam room, and a home gym.

The cinema room includes a projector and drinks fridge.

   

*(Image: Sanderson Young)*

The snug/whiskey room comes with a concealed secret doorway that leads to the cinema room.

The lower ground floor also has a spacious boot room, a laundry room, a separate WC, and additional storage.

   

*(Image: Sanderson Young)*

The open-plan kitchen, dining, and family room is equipped with underfloor heating, bespoke fitted seating, and bi-folding doors opening onto the raised entertaining terrace.

The Newcastle Kitchen includes high-quality cabinetry, granite work surfaces, integrated appliances, and a range cooker.

The property is also covered by an enhanced CCTV system.

Shadwell Towers has landscaped entertaining gardens, including a children's play area, a summerhouse, a covered hot tub beneath a pergola, and numerous seating areas suitable for outdoor gatherings.

   

*(Image: Sanderson Young)*

The house also has solar panels and an EV Tesla charging point.

The driveway provides parking for multiple vehicles and access to the integral double garage.

Shadwell Towers is ideally positioned within walking distance of the shops, cafés, and amenities of East Boldon village.

Boldon Golf Club and excellent transport links to Sunderland and Newcastle are also nearby.

The property has fibre broadband and an upgraded heating system with a new boiler.

It's easy to see why the house is considered one of East Boldon's premier family homes.
