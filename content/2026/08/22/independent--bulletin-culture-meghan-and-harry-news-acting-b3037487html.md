# Meghan ‘considers Netflix acting role’ ahead of return to UK with Harry

- The Duchess of Sussex is reportedly in preliminary discussions to return to acting with a potential role in Guy Ritchie’s Netflix action-comedy series, *The Gentlemen.* 
- The speculation comes alongside news that she and the Duke of Sussex are preparing to relocate to the UK later this month with their children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet, who have been enrolled in British schools.
- The potential appearance in a prospective third season of the show would mark her first major acting role since marrying into the royal family, following her previous fame as Rachel Zane in the US drama *Suits.* 
- Meghan recently made a brief return to screen acting by appearing as herself in a cameo for the upcoming comedy film *Close Personal Friends* , starring Lily Collins and Brie Larson.
- Leading entertainment industry figures have said that directors and producers would undoubtedly be keen to meet with the duchess if she decides to officially resume her acting career.
