A County Durham charity that helps adults with learning disabilities develop digital skills has been shortlisted for a national award recognising organisations tackling digital exclusion.

Coundon-based Journey Enterprises has been named a finalist in the Accessible Services category of the Digital Poverty Alliance’s Digital Inclusion Awards.

Elspeth McPherson, chief executive officer of Journey Enterprises, said: "This is tremendous news and is based on the work we have reported to the Alliance both on our website and the skills we are teaching Clients within our sessional IT delivery and specialist grant-aided programmes.

"Our focus is both on developing adults with intellectual disabilities’ digital skills and how to stay safe online."

Recent projects include the Access Able programme funded by the Forbes Charitable Foundation, I Can be Canny supported by the Santander Foundation, Working Journeys funded by the National Lottery Community Fund and Henry Smith Charity, and Women’s Journeys with support from the Smallwood Trust.

The Accessible Services category recognises organisations that prioritise making digital platforms and tools user-friendly for everyone.

Other finalists in the same category include Studio 24 and NHS Cheshire and Merseyside.

Elizabeth Anderson, CEO of the Digital Poverty Alliance, said: "Digital inclusion is shaped by the decisions organisations make every day, from how services are designed to where devices go and who receives the support needed to build confidence online.

"This year’s nominees show what those decisions can achieve when they are backed by purpose, collaboration and a clear understanding of the communities they are intended to serve."

The awards, open exclusively to signatories of the Digital Poverty Alliance’s Charter for Digital Inclusion, honour practical action to end digital poverty.

Awards span five areas: Device Donation, Digital Skills Development, Accessible Services, Raising Awareness, and Partnerships for Impact.

Winners will be announced at the End Digital Poverty Gala Reception on Thursday, September 10, at the Royal Watercolour Society Gallery in London.

The event takes place during End Digital Poverty Week and in the DPA’s 25th anniversary year.

The reception will bring together organisations and leaders from across the digital inclusion community, placing this year’s nominees at the centre of an evening that recognises what meaningful action on digital inclusion looks like.
