The NHS is urging the public to start booking flu vaccination appointments ahead of the winter season.

In a bid to help ease pressure on the health system, the NHS has begun preparing for winter earlier than ever by opening its booking system.

It means that eligible people, like children and pregnant people, will be able to get flu jabs from as early as September.

Over the summer, the NHS treated a record number of patients following the five back-to-back heatwaves.


It is now urging the public to help ease the winter pressure and book flu jabs.

## Who can get a flu vaccine in September?

In England, children and pregnant people can now book their flu jabs via the online booking portal.

While NHS Scotland and NHS Wales will be contacting those eligible for the flu vaccine from late August.

People do need to wait for an invitation to book and can find appointments on the NHS website, NHS App, or by calling 119.

## Who should have the flu vaccine?

The flu vaccine is offered to people who are at a higher risk of getting seriously ill from the flu, according to the NHS.


You are eligible for the vaccine if you are:

- Aged 65 or over (including those who will be 65 by 31 March 2027)
- Aged 18 to 64 and have certain long-term health conditions
- Pregnant
- Living in a care home
- The main carer for an older or disabled person, or receive a carer's allowance
- Living with someone who has a weakened immune system
- Experiencing homelessness or staying in a homeless hostel or night shelter
- Frontline health and social care workers who can also get a flu vaccine through their employer

## Can you still get the flu vaccine if you're not eligible on the NHS?

Although you can get the flu jab for free if you are eligible, if you are not, you can pay for a private appointment.

Boots has already opened its booking system for flu appointments, which cost £21.95 and can be booked via its website.

Dr Julie Yates, UK Health Security Agency’s Deputy Director for Immunisation Programmes, said, "Whilst we’re still in summer, illnesses like flu and COVID-19 are probably the last thing on your mind, but the seasons will change quickly and with the national booking system now open, I’d urge everyone who is eligible to plan ahead and book their vaccinations as soon as possible.

"Booking early means you’ll be vaccinated in good time, before these viruses start circulating widely, giving you the best possible protection through the autumn and winter months.

"I strongly encourage everyone eligible to take up the offer and get vaccinated."
