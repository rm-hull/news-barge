Following on from 2025/26, *Match of the Day* will continue to be fronted by a trio of presenters in Mark Chapman, Kelly Cates and Gabby Logan for the Premier League 2026/27.

Beyond the presenters, there has also been a shake-up in the punditry line-up, with Dion Dublin, Martin Keown, Nedum Onuoha, Theo Walcott, Shay Given and Stephen Warnock all departing. But don't worry, the BBC already have their replacements lined up, with former Tottenham players Toby Alderweireld and Robbie Keane joining the team, alongside Thomas Frank, who was Spurs' manager at the beginning of last season. Ashley Young has also joined following his retirement.

MOTD is a godsend for footy fans who don't have access to live TV broadcasts from the likes of Sky Sports and TNT Sports. It neatly rounds up all the highlights of the day's Premier League action every Saturday night, with a sister show, *Match of the Day 2*, providing the same service on Sundays. Don't forget about the Champions League version of Match of the Day which joined the collection back in 2024.

### How to watch Match of the Day in the UK for free

 ![UK flag](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/B9XNGCRYNHKJzN5zVfTMje.jpg) 


*Match of the Day* is free-to-air on**BBC One** in the UK. It goes out live on Saturday nights roughly at 10:20pm BST, with*Match of the Day 2* following on the same channel around the same time the next day.

There is also a continued addition to the BBC's football highlights portfolio this season, with *Match of the Day: Champions League* streaming every Wednesday following a matchday.

If you miss an episode, or simply prefer to watch it on a portable device, don't worry. Every *Match of the Day* episode is also available on**BBC iPlayer**.

Just keep in mind that each episode is only available to stream until the next episode airs, or for seven days after broadcast, whichever comes first.
