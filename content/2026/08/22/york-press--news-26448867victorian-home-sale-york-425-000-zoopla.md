An “immaculate” Victorian home has hit the market in York.

The three-bedroom townhouse is for sale in Fountayne Street, off Wigginton Road, for £425,000.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said it has been renovated to a “very high standard for modern living, yet retains much of its original charm and character”.

  Inside the three-bedroom house for sale in Fountayne Street, off Wigginton Road in York, for £425,000 

*(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

A front courtyard with mosaic tiles leads into the home’s tiled reception hall.

To the front of the home is a living room with large bay windows.
