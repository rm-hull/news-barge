# Trump insists his real approval numbers are the best they’ve ever been

- The president criticized media coverage of his job performance, attributing low approval ratings to "fake news" and "corrupt" polls.
- He claimed his "REAL Polling Numbers" are the best ever, despite providing no evidence to support these assertions.
- Recent polls from AP/NORC, Quinnipiac, and Fox News consistently show his approval rating around one-third to 39 percent.
- The significant decline in his approval is largely linked to the ongoing war with Iran and rising gas prices, with only two in ten Americans supporting the conflict.
- His dipping popularity, including a 10-point drop among Republicans, poses a serious challenge for the Republican Party in upcoming midterm elections.
