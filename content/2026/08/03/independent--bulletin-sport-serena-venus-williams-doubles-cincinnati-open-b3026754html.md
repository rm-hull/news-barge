# Legendary tennis pair to play in doubles at Cincinnati Open later this month

- Serena and Venus Williams are set to resume their doubles partnership for the first time in four years at the Cincinnati Open.
- The sisters were granted a wildcard for the WTA 1000 event, which will mark their debut as a doubles team in Cincinnati.
- Together, they have won 14 major doubles titles and secured three Olympic gold medals throughout their careers.
- Their last doubles appearance was at the 2022 US Open, coinciding with Serena's final tournament before her recent return to tennis.
- Venus also received a singles wildcard for the tournament, which runs from 11 to 23 August and could serve as a preparation for Serena's potential return to the US Open.
