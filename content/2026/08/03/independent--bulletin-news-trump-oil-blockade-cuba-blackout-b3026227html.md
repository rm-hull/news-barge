# Trump’s oil blockade looms over Cuba as another nationwide blackout hits

- Cuba's national electricity grid collapsed late Sunday, plunging the island of approximately 10 million people into darkness.
- The state-owned national electric utility, Unión Eléctrica de Cuba, announced the total disconnection of the electrical system on social media.
- This outage follows three previous nationwide blackouts that occurred in July, indicating a pattern of frequent power failures.
- The recurring blackouts are largely attributed to a US-imposed oil blockade that has severely crippled Cuba's already aging energy infrastructure.
- The oil blockade was imposed by Donald Trump after Washington deposed Venezuelan president Nicolas Maduro, leading to Venezuela and Mexico halting fuel shipments to the island.
