# Chelsea seal Jordan Henderson transfer after signing Danny Welbeck

- Jordan Henderson, the 36-year-old England international, has completed a move to Chelsea on a free transfer, signing a two-year deal with the club.
- His transfer follows the mutual termination of his contract with Brentford and marks Chelsea's eighth first-team acquisition this summer.
- Henderson expressed significant enthusiasm for joining Chelsea, citing the club's stature, his admiration for manager Xabi Alonso, and the ownership's ambition for success.
- This signing indicates a shift in Chelsea's transfer strategy, balancing their previous youth-focused approach with the addition of experienced players, having also signed Danny Welbeck over the weekend.
- Henderson brings extensive experience, having spent 12 years at Liverpool, where he captained the team to Premier League and Champions League titles, before brief stints at Al-Ettifaq and Ajax.
