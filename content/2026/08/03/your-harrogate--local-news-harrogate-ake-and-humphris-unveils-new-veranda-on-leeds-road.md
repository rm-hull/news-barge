                                    Ake & Humphris has officially opened its brand new veranda at its Leeds Road shop in Harrogate.

The new outdoor wine terrace was launched during a ribbon-cutting ceremony on Friday (31st July), with Harrogate Mayor Viv Poskitt carrying out the official opening.

Customers joined the celebrations from 6pm as the independent wine merchant marked the latest addition to its Leeds Road store, creating a new outdoor space for visitors to enjoy throughout the summer.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a70594c257f4fc6a6e.jpg)


Sharing the news on social media, a spokesperson for the business said:

"We're celebrating the official launch of our new veranda at our Leeds Road branch. It's smooth sailing from here into the summer!

"Thanks to Harrogate Mayor for cutting the big red ribbon and to everyone who came to join us! We love you guys!"


*The new veranda is now open, with customers able to reserve a table by contacting the Leeds Road branch on 01423 566009.*
