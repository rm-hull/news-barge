Rupert Lowe, a former Reform UK MP who now leads his own party, Restore Britain, has extended an offer of cooperation to Nigel Farage and Reform UK, despite previous public disagreements.

Lowe proposed that the two parties unite to combat a 'looming left-wing alliance,' suggesting that local cooperation in Norfolk County Council demonstrates the potential for a national partnership.

The conditions for this alliance include Reform UK adopting Restore Britain's policies, such as a ban on the burqa and Halal meat, and a referendum on the death penalty.

Additionally, Lowe stipulated that Nigel Farage must commit to excluding any former Conservative ministers, including figures like Robert Jenrick and Suella Braverman, from a future Cabinet.

Nigel Farage responded with a lukewarm statement, acknowledging the need for broad support but implying that only Reform UK possesses the capacity to bring about significant change.
