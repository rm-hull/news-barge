Police are investigating another two fires in York.

Firefighters were called to an incident off **Stray Road** in Tang Hall at**8.24pm last night**.

There’s a play area and recreation ground there.

#### Stray Road, York

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “York’s appliance was mobilised to reports of a fire that had been abandoned.

“Crews extinguished using knapsack sprayers. The incident was left in hands of police.”

Then at **8.40pm**, firefighters went to a fire at** Leeside Play Area** in Dringhouses.

The spokesperson said: “Acomb’s crew responded to a small fire that had been deliberately started in a park.

**Leeside Play Area**, York

“The fire had been extinguished prior to crews arrival. The incident was passed over to police.”
