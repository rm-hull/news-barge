Wingstop UK, operated by Lemon Pepper Holdings, reported a significant 73% surge in revenues to £216.4 million last year, primarily driven by the opening of new restaurants.

The hospitality chain has ambitious expansion plans for the current year, aiming to open up to 30 new sites across England, Scotland, Wales and Ireland.

Wingstop UK is poised to reach a major milestone with the opening of its 100th UK restaurant in Inverness later this month, having first launched in the UK in 2018.

Backed by private equity firm Sixth Street, the company also revealed an agreement to expand its operations into Poland, signalling its broader international growth strategy.

Chris Sherriff, CEO of Wingstop UKI, attributed the record results to the hard work of his team and customer loyalty, noting that trading continues to be encouraging despite wider hospitality sector challenges.
