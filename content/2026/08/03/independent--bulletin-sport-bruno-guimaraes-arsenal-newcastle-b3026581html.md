Arsenal's initial offer for Newcastle captain Bruno Guimaraes has been rejected by the Magpies, despite direct communication being established between the two Premier League clubs.

A significant disparity in valuation exists for the Brazil international midfielder, who joined Newcastle for £40 million in January 2022.

Despite the rejection of their opening bid, Arsenal is unlikely to be deterred, as indications suggest Guimaraes is keen to swap St James’ Park for the Emirates Stadium this summer.

Newcastle, having already lost Anthony Gordon and Sandro Tonali this summer, are reluctant to see another first-choice star depart and will only sanction a transfer if their financial terms are fully met.

Newcastle finished 12th in the Premier League last season and cannot offer Guimaraes European football, while Arsenal are looking to bolster their squad for a title defence.
