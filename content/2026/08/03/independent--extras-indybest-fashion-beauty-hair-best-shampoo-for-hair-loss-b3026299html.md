A shampoo for hair loss can't necessarily reverse all types of thinning like a magic wand, but the right formula can support a healthier scalp and help tackle underlying issues that may be contributing to excess shedding.

As certified trichologist Izabella Bordignon explains, ”Hair loss is far more complex than simply choosing the right shampoo. There are many different causes of hair loss, including genetics, hormonal changes, nutritional deficiencies and scalp conditions." Then again, shampoo is still one of the hair products you’ll use most frequently. So, as much as tackling hair loss often requires more than a change of products, choosing the right shampoo and conditioner duo for your concerns will support a healthier scalp environment and complement your wider treatment plan.

To help narrow down the options, I asked Bordignon to recommend the formulas she looks to as a professional – from pharmacy favourites to salon brands. These are the ones she suggested.

## Meet the expert

- **Izabella Bordignon** is a certified trichologist, cosmetologist and London salon founder with more than eight years experience working in the field. She specialises in hair thinning, scalp pain, inflammation and itchiness, dandruff and dry scalps, and hair texture changes.

Nizoral’s anti-dandruff shampoo earned a full five stars from Bordignon, who describes the formula as “particularly beneficial for individuals with seborrhoeic dermatitis, itching or persistent flaking alongside increased shedding.”

She tells me, “Nizoral stands out because ketoconazole has been investigated not only for dandruff control but also for its potential supportive role in androgenetic alopecia.

“While it shouldn't replace established hair-loss treatments such as minoxidil or finasteride, research suggests ketoconazole may help reduce scalp inflammation and create a healthier follicular environment.”

### Key specifications:

- **Size:** 60ml
- **Active ingredient:** Ketoconazole to kill fungus and relieve inflammation
- **Usage:** Twice a week for two to four weeks initially, then once every one to two weeks once dandruff has eased

While caveating that “it isn't a treatment for androgenetic alopecia,” she finds that “improving scalp health can help minimise inflammation-related shedding and support overall hair quality.”

“Polytar isn't marketed as a hair-loss shampoo, but it deserves recognition for its ability to manage inflammatory scalp conditions such as psoriasis and severe seborrhoeic dermatitis,” Bordignon claims.

This is because, she elaborates, “Persistent inflammation can contribute to increased hair shedding, and controlling these scalp disorders is often the first step in restoring a healthier environment for the hair follicle.”

She lists its benefits as reductions in scaling, itching and irritation and dubs the formula “particularly valuable for individuals whose hair loss is secondary to chronic scalp disease rather than androgenetic alopecia.”

### Key specifications:

- **Size:** 150ml
- **Active ingredients:** 4% anti-inflammatory coal tar solution to remove dead skin and slow down hyper-rapid skin cell production (which causes dandruff)
- **Usage:** Once or twice a week for a maximum of four weeks, leaving the product in contact with the scalp for no longer than five-minutes

Kérastase’s genesis nourishing shampoo is Bordignon’s pick for reducing the *appearance* of thinning. How does it work, exactly? “Ingredients such as ginger root and edelweiss stem cells are combined with conditioning agents that improve fibre resilience and leave hair feeling stronger and smoother,” she details, adding that this formula is “particularly suitable for chemically treated or heat-damaged hair prone to snapping.”

Not to be mistaken for a shampoo that can bring back lost hair, Bordignon stresses that “one of the most common misconceptions is confusing hair breakage with hair loss.” So before you check out, she cautions that this shampoo is “specifically formulated to strengthen weakened hair fibres, reducing breakage during washing and styling.” In essence, it improves the state of what’s there, versus helping ressurect patches where strands have been lost.

### Key specifications:

- **Size:** 250ml
- **Active ingredients:** Protective ginger root, antioxidant edelweiss, amino acids (arginine), caffeine
- **Usage:** Daily or two to three times per week, rinse and repeating with each wash
