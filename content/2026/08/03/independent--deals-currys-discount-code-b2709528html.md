**Delivery and installation**

Currys delivery options vary depending on whether you’re buying a small item or a large appliance.

- Small items – Free standard delivery orders more than £40, for orders less than £40 there is a £3.99 delivery charge and £5.99 for next-day shipping.

Installation services are available for most large products, such as ovens, aerials and TVs. Fees vary depending on the item, for example, washing machine installation starts from £30 and TVs from £45.

## Helpful FAQs

### Is there a Currys student discount?

Yes, Currys offers exclusive discounts to students. These aren’t sitewide; instead, Currys provides discounts on selected items or brands. For example, students can currently get 10 per cent off Dyson floorcare.

### Can I stack multiple Currys voucher codes?

Currys doesn’t usually allow promo code stacking. However, occasionally you can find voucher codes for extra savings on sale items.

### Does Currys offer NHS discounts?

NHS staff and keyworkers can find exclusive discounts on Currys’s dedicated deals page. To access the savings, simply verify your working status on the website.

### Does Currys provide free delivery?

If you spend more than £40 on small items, you’ll automatically qualify for free delivery. For larger items or orders less than £40, you’ll need to pay delivery fees.
