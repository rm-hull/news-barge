## Helpful FAQs

### When are the best English Heritage deals?

Here’s when you can expect the biggest English Heritage savings:

- Summer holidays – family-focused deals
- Spring (March to May) – pre-summer promotions
- Black Friday and Christmas – gift membership offers

If you sign up by direct debit, you can occasionally get three months free.

### Is English Heritage free?

Some smaller sites are free to visit, but most major attractions require paid entry or membership. If you visit up to three within 12 months, it may work out cheaper to invest in a membership for unlimited entry.

### How many places can you visit?

An English Heritage membership gives you access to more than 400 sites across England, including landmarks such as Aldborough Roman site, Hadrian’s Wall and Merrivale prehistoric settlement.

### Do children go free?

Yes, up to six children (under 18 years old) per adult English Heritage member can enter for free.

### Is there an English Heritage student discount?

Yes, students with a valid NUS card can get discounted English Heritage memberships for £44 a year.

### Does English Heritage accept an NHS discount?

Yes, NHS and healthcare workers can get 25 per cent off annual and gift memberships with a valid Health Service Discounts account.

## Why join English Heritage?

There are more than 400 historic English Heritage sites across England, including castles, abbeys, Roman ruins and stately homes.

With a membership, you can explore:

- iconic castles and fortresses
- Roman and prehistoric landmarks
- historic gardens and estates

Your English Heritage membership also helps fund the conservation of sites spanning 6,000 years of history.
