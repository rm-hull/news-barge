The body of Michael Henry, 19, has been recovered (Northumbria Police)

The body of Michael Henry, a 19-year-old kayaker missing since his boat capsized off Northumbria, has been recovered.

Mr Henry, from the Rothbury area, was found in the water off the coast of Blyth on 26 July.

He had been missing since 7 July when his kayak, carrying two men, overturned near Boulmer.

The second man involved in the incident was rescued by the coastguard after reaching a buoy.

Mr Henry's family previously released a statement on 15 July, describing him as a much-loved son, brother, and keen sportsman, and thanked everyone for their support.
