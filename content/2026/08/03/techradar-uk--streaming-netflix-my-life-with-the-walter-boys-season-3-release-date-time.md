Netflix is on a generational roll with its cozy dramas in 2026. In the last few weeks alone, we've had *Heartstopper Forever*,* Sweet Magnolias s*eason 5, and* Ransom Canyon* season 2.

Now, it's *My Life with the Walter Boys* season 3's time to shine. If you can't remember what happened at the end of season 2, don't panic. We can expect the action to pick up after George's medical emergency, following the open love confession between Jackie and Cole, and Alex finding out.

At this point, we don't actually know if George will survive or not. Regardless, when does *My Life with the Walter Boys* season 3 arrive on the streamer?

## What time can I watch My Life with the Walter Boys season 3 on Netflix?

![My Life With the Walter Boys: Season 3 | Official Trailer | Netflix - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/_nneiDtbdbk/maxresdefault.jpg) 

*My Life with the Walter Boys* season 3 drops on**Thursday, August 6**.

As for exact time, it should be the standard 12am PT / 3am ET release time that practically every other Netflix TV Original has.

For fans who don't live in North America, here's when you'll need to tune in for season 3:

- **US**– 12am PT / 3am ET
- **Canada**– 12am PT / 3am ET
- **UK**– 8am BST
- **India**– 12:30pm IST
- **Singapore**– 3pm SGT
- **Australia**– 6pm AEST
- **New Zealand**– 8pm NZDT
