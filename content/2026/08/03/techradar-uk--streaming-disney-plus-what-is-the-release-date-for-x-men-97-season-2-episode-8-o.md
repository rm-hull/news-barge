Ready to say goodbye to *X-Men '97* season 2 yet? I hope not, because you've got two more episodes to enjoy of the Marvel TV show before its latest season ends.

The first of those episodes, which is this season's eighth and penultimate chapter, will make its debut very soon, too. If you want to know when it'll premiere on one of the world's best streaming services, then you'll want to warm up that scrolling finger and read on. So, hop to it!

## What time can I watch X-Men '97 season 2 episode 8?

 ![Psylocke, Jubilee, Cable, and Angel in X-Men 97 season 2 episode 7](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ckZsz5pgNAEXbq9KxffqFY.jpg) 


Season 2 episode 8 will drop on **Wednesday, August 5 at 12am PT / 3am ET**.

That's the case in the US and Canada, anyway, but fans in other countries shouldn't worry about having to wait longer to watch the Marvel Phase 6 TV series' next entry.

Indeed, it'll also drop on August 5 everywhere else, so read on to find out when *X-Men '97*'s forthcoming installment will be released on Disney+ where you are:

- **US**— Wednesday, August 5 at 12am PT / 3am ET
- **Canada**— Wednesday, August 5 at 12am PT / 3am ET
- **UK**— Wednesday, August 5 at 8am BST
- **India**— Wednesday, August 5 at 12:30pm IST
- **Singapore**— Wednesday, August 5 at 3pm SGT
- **Australia**— Wednesday, August 5 at 5pm AEST
- **New Zealand**— Wednesday, August 5 at 7pm NZST

## When does the final episode of X-Men '97 season 2 come out?

 ![Nightcrawler and Rogue in X-Men 97 season 2 episode 7](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/TFDn6NiVvgqBbw2ztMx8PY.jpg) 


As I mentioned earlier, only one episode remains of this X-Men animated show's sophomore season after this week's chapter.

No matter where you're based, you'll be able to stream the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) adjacent program's next episode on Wednesday, August 12. Make sure you pencil it into your calendar, then, so you don't miss what I expect to be an explosive season 2 finale.
