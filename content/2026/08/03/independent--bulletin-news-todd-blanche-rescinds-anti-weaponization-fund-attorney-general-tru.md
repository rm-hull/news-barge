# The $1.8 billion concession that could save Trump’s AG pick

![The Senate Judiciary Committee is set to vote on Blanche’s confirmation on Tuesday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/01/04/12/Capitol_Riot_Anniversary_Police_57800.jpg)

- Todd Blanche, the president's nominee for attorney general, officially rescinded the controversial $1.8 billion "anti-weaponization fund" on Sunday.
- The fund, created in May as part of a settlement with the IRS, was intended to compensate individuals claiming to be victims of federal government weaponization, including those involved in the January 6 Capitol riot.
- Blanche's decision to rescind the fund was made to secure the votes of Republican Senators Thom Tillis and John Cornyn, who had been blocking his confirmation.
- The Senate Judiciary Committee is scheduled to vote on Blanche's confirmation as Attorney General on Tuesday.
- Donald Trump had defended the need for the fund as recently as Friday, stating that victims of government abuse should be compensated.
