Harcourt Gardens care home on Harcourt Road in Harrogate is hosting a 1950s-themed community food festival on Saturday, 8th August, starting at 2:30 pm.

The event welcomes local residents, neighbours, and families for an afternoon focused on food and community connection.

## Event Details and Activities

The festival features decorations created by the residents, including traditional 1950s outfits, recycled papier-mâché lanterns, and handmade bunting. The afternoon schedule includes:

- **International Street Food:** A selection of global dishes prepared by the kitchen team, inspired by the residents’ cultural backgrounds and favourite recipes.
- **Entertainment:** A two-hour interactive reptile show.
- **Games:** A tombola, a raffle, and a hook-a-duck stall.

The event aims to bring different generations together, allowing residents to share their favourite recipes and traditions with the wider local community.
