Police investigating a burglary in Selby have issued this CCTV image.

It happened at **City Electrical Factors** on**Prospect Way** at**12.06pm** on**Thursday 16 July**.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Numerous electrical products were stolen from the warehouse section of the store by multiple suspects.

#### City Electrical Factors, Selby

“One of the men we need to speak to is on the attached CCTV image.

“We are seeking information to help identify the man and any further relevant information about the individual or possible associates.”
