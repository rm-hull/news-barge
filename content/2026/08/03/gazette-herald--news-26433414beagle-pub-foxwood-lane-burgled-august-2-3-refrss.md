BURGLARS have struck at a popular pub in a York suburb overnight.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after The Beagle pub in Foxwood Lane was burgled last night (Sunday 2).

It happened between 11.45pm last night (August 2) and 8.30am this morning (August 3).

“We need to hear from anyone who recalls any suspicious activity or vehicles near the pub overnight, including a white Mercedes van,” said a spokesperson for the force.

If you can help in any way, please email [tom.birkinshaw@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:tom.birkinshaw@northyorkshire.police.uk) or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or by making an online report.
