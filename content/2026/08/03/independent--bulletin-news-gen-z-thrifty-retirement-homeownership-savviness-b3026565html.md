The View goes after Karoline Leavitt for saying Gen Z are lazy

Gen Z is adopting a thrifty mindset, with 62% of those aged 18-29 avoiding weekend plans to prevent financial regret and preferring quieter, cheaper 'soft socializing'.

This generation demonstrates an intentional approach to finances, with 68% believing that going out on weekends is not worth the cost.

Gen Z workers aged 24-28 are the most likely age group to be on track for a comfortable retirement, even surpassing baby boomers.

Despite high housing costs, 67% of Gen Z consider homeownership an important life goal, and almost 75% have started saving for a down payment, prioritizing career development first.

Experts attribute Gen Z's financial savviness to their adaptation to economic conditions, viewing money as a tool for freedom, and leveraging online resources for financial education.
