TWO vehicles have crashed on a key East Yorkshire road.

Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to the A164 Beverley Road in Watton at 11.42am yesterday (Sunday, August 2), south of Driffield.

  



The road is a principle route from Beverley to Bridlington.

A service spokesperson said: "All occupants managed to self-release prior to the crew's arrival.

Crews made one vehicle safe by disconnecting the battery.

"The other vehicle was safe to drive away.

"Soil was used to cover fuel which spilled onto the road surface."
