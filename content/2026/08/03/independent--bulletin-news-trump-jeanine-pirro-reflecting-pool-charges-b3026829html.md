# Trump says Jeanine Pirro ‘folded like an umbrella’ in Reflecting Pool case

- President Donald Trump expressed disappointment in top federal prosecutor Jeanine Pirro after she moved to drop charges against a former Olympian accused of vandalizing the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool.
- Pirro's office filed to dismiss criminal charges against David Hearn after the administration admitted the damage was due to "flawed installation by the contractor," not vandalism.
- Trump reportedly considered firing Pirro, accusing her of "choking" and blaming the judge, despite his administration's failure to produce evidence against Hearn.
- The court filing revealed that the recently applied blue sealant started to peel "almost immediately" after renovations, and the Department of Interior provided "less than fulsome information" initially.
- The administration spent nearly $14.7 million on the pool renovation, bypassing competitive bidding for a no-bid contract, and Trump continued to insist on vandalism despite evidence pointing to a "rushed and botched installation."
