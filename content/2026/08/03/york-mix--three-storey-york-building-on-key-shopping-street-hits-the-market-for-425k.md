A three-storey building on Stonegate in York is on the market for £425,000.

Numbers 9 and 9A Stonegate is a “freehold retail and ground rent investment” according to Savills which is marketing the property.

The ground floor retail unit is currently let to Sweaty Betty on a ten-year lease from June 2014. The shop remains now on a ‘holding over’ lease at £47.5K per year.

#### 9 Stonegate, York

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/9-and-9a-stonegate-york-savills-1.webp)

In its property details, Savills says: “The property comprises a three storey building arranged as ground floor and basement retail accommodation.

“The upper parts are sold off on long lease (999 years from 01.01.2007) and operated as Airbnb accommodation.”

Both 9 and 9A Stonegate are Grade II* listed. They date from the mid 1700s, with the front remodelled in the early 1800s.
