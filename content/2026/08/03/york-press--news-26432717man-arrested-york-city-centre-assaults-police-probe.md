A man has been arrested after police said people were allegedly assaulted by the same person in York city centre.

North Yorkshire Police said the reported assaults happened between Wednesday, July 22, and Saturday, July 25 , but “we believe there could be more [alleged] victims”.

All the incidents are said to have happened between 9pm and 3am within the city walls, according to the force.

A police spokesperson said the “suspect” was dressed in black clothing, aged between 35-45 years old, and was carrying a “distinguishable food delivery backpack while riding a black bicycle with one headlight on the handlebar”.

They urged anyone with information about the alleged incidents to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101 or Crimestoppers anonymously 0800 555 111 quoting reference 12260142809.

Police said a man from York has arrested in connection with the investigation and released on conditional bail.
