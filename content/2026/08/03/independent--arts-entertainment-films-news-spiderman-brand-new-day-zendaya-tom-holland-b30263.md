Zendaya and Tom Holland could cap off their phenomenal 2026 by becoming the most profitable box office actors in a single year in the history of Hollywood.

The married couple are the current king and queen of the movie industry, helped by their appearances in two films that are dominating the global box office: Christopher Nolan’s *The Odyssey* and Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) sequel*Spider-Man**: Brand New Day*.

In Nolan’s adaptation of the Homer poem, Holland plays Telemachus, the son of Matt Damon’s Odysseus, with Zendaya playing Athena, who serves as a spirit guide for Odysseus on his long journey home to Ithaca after the Trojan War. Holland plays Peter Parker in *Brand New Day*(he is into his 10th year playing the superhero), with Zendaya portraying his love interest, MJ.

*The Odyssey* has exceeded high expectations by amassing $911.4m (£676.9m) in just over two weeks since its release. Meanwhile,*Brand New Day* – Holland’s fourth solo outing as the web-slinger – has made a staggering $927m (£688.5m) in its opening weekend alone. Its combined takings over the past three days provided Hollywood with its most successful weekend in history domestically, making $434m (£322.3m) overall.

![Tom Holland and Zendaya star in two of the year’s biggest films](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/03/11/45/Screenshot-2026-08-03-at-12-16-05.png)

*The Odyssey* has become a cultural event that millions want to experience in IMAX, considering it’s the first film in history to be fully shot in the format. A new camera was specially created to ensure Nolan could shoot the quiet scenes of dialogue, having been unable to do so with his previous films because the camera in question was too loud.

Meanwhile, not only is *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* the latest instalment in an MCU franchise that’s been typically well-reviewed to date, but it’s the last Marvel film before* Avengers: Doomsday*, the blockbuster-in-waiting bringing back all the old familiars – including former Iron Man actor Robert Downey Jr, who’ll play the film’s primary antagonist, Doctor Doom. It’ll also mark the arrival of key figures from the X-Men franchise in the* MCU* for the first time.

But these films’ inclusion of Zendaya, 29, and Holland, 30, should not be overlooked as a reason for their success. They’ve been a force at red carpet premieres: Zendaya going viral for her fashion choices, and Holland’s loving reactions to his wife being perfect for viral content.

![Zendaya in ‘The Odyssey’, with Matt Damon](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/07/13/12/01KXD0WJX18656RTVJ0RD73Q34.jpg)

The pair became close friends while filming *Spider-Man: Homecoming* in 2016, and started a relationship soon afterwards, keeping it private until they went Instagram official in 2021. In June 2026, Holland confirmed they were married in a private ceremony.

While both actors currently have a box office total of $1,838.4bn (£1,365.4bn) with *The Odyssey* and*Spider-Man Brand New Day*, those films have legs and will continue to rake in serious dollars at the box office. But on top of this, Zendaya has* The Drama* to her name – one of studio A24’s most successful films to date – which made $132.5m (£98.4m) earlier this year.

In December, she’ll extend her profits for the year with Denis Villeneuve’s *Dune: Part Three*, which has the same IMAX pull as Nolan’s* The Odyssey.* Like Nolan’s film, it’s feasible it could make something in the region of $700m to $800m (£520m to 594.2m), depending on the reviews.

![Tom Holland in ‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day’](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/07/30/7/54/Screenshot-2026-07-30-at-08-08-58.png)

As for Holland, there’s every chance he could show up in *Avengers: Doomsday*– although he’s currently unconfirmed. This year seems to hint at an end – or at least a temporary pause – to superhero fatigue, and * Doomsday*, like previous Avengers films,* Infinity War*and* Endgame*, will most likely smash past $1bn (£742.8m) at the box office. 

Of course, there’s a small possibility that Zendaya could even show up in *Doomsday*, playing MJ, which would see her overall profits rock past her husband’s by year’s end.

The current victor for most box office profit in a single year is Samuel L Jackson, who amassed $6.4bn ($4,754.4bn) in 2019, helped by roles in *Captain Marvel*,* Avengers: Endgame*,* Spider-Man: Far from Home*and* Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker*.

It’s worth noting that some of these were cameo appearances – he was barely in *Endgame* beyond a three-second cameo and, in the case of*The Rise of Skywalker*, his voice could be briefly heard. Shaving off his* Rise of Skywalker* takings, it brings Jackson’s overall amount to $2.5bn (£1,857bn).

![Samuel L Jackson currently holds record for most profitable actor in single year](https://static.the-independent.com/2025/06/05/17/43/GettyImages-2202387968.jpeg)

Holland himself is currently in second place with $4.367bn (£3,250.7bn), thanks to a 2019 run that included *Avengers: Endgame* and *Spider-Man: Far from Home*, with Scarlett Johansson in third place with $4.024bn (£2.989bn). 

With the continued successes of *The Odyssey* and*Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, and the forthcoming release of* Dune: Part 3*, it seems Zendaya could give Jackson a run for his money.
