A DOG walker was treated in hospital after attempting to break up a dog fight.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information about a dog walker who was bitten by another dog in Brayton, near Selby, last month.

It happened at around 9.14pm on Sunday, July 19, on the field at the Community Centre on Foxhill Lane.

“The victim was bitten by the dog whilst he attempted to separate a fight between this and his own dog. He received injured to his arm and leg which required hospital treatment,” said a spokesperson for the force.
