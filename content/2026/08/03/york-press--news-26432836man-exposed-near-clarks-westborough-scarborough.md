A MAN has reportedly exposed himself near Clarks in a coastal North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information to help with an ongoing exposure investigation in Scarborough town centre that took place last month.

It happened at around 3.30pm on Friday, June 5 in Westborough, near the Clarks shoe shop.
