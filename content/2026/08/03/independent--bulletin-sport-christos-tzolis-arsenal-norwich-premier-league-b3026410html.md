# Christos Tzolis insists he’s ready to shine for Arsenal after Norwich struggles

- New Arsenal signing Christos Tzolis has confidently declared that the reigning Premier League champions are fully prepared to defend their title, and insists that the pressure now rests squarely on their rivals.
- The Greece international, who joined the Gunners in a £34 million move from Club Brugge earlier this summer, marked his debut by scoring in Arsenal's 4-1 victory over Girona on Saturday.
- Tzolis, currently the club's sole outfield acquisition of the summer, replaces Leandro Trossard and believes his past experiences, including setbacks at Norwich, have prepared him for a successful return to the Premier League.
- He highlighted the squad's collective hunger and clear mindset, stating that every player is ready to compete at the highest level for all possible titles, not just the Premier League.
- Despite facing early season challenges such as key defender William Saliba's injury and fitness doubts over Declan Rice and Bukayo Saka, Tzolis remains unequivocal about Arsenal's readiness to defend their crown.
