Kyiv has attacked about a dozen Wildberries sites since 18 July in a bid to disrupt the operations of the company, a linchpin of the consumer economy that Kyiv says helps bolster Vladimir Putin’s war machine.

## Drone fragments fell in Romanian territory near Ukraine border

Fragments of a drone shot down by Ukraine near its Danube river border fell on Romanian national territory on a farm field near the village of Periprava without causing any damage, Romania's Defence Ministry said on Sunday.

The drone had previously spent 20 minutes in Romanian air space before flying into Ukraine, the ministry said without specifying its origin. Romanian F-16 fighter jets shot down three Russian drones breaching national airspace over three consecutive days in late July.

## What is Wildberries?

In recent weeks, Ukraine has been turning its attention to attacking large warehouses owned by Wildberries.

Wildberries is Russia's largest online marketplace. It has been called the country's equivalent of Amazon, as its business model resembles a mix of Amazon Marketplace, eBay, and Temu.

Founded in 2004 by Tatyana Kim, who is now Russia's richest woman, the company has since become the dominant e-commerce platform in Russia and several neighbouring countries.

Hosting millions of third-party sellers, the platform sells almost everything, from electronics to groceries. The marketplace features clothes, books, cosmetics, toys, appliances, household items, sports gear and much more. There’s an “E-Pharmacy” page and a travel section for booking plane tickets or hotels.

It operates a vast network of warehouses, sorting centres, and logistics hubs across Russia.

In 2021, Kim acquired a small bank and turned it into what now is Wildberries Bank, but that institution has since come under sanctions by the U.K. and the European Union.
