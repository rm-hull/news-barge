# The staggering reason Starbucks’ CEO earns more than every rival

- Starbucks CEO Brian Niccol earned $31 million in 2025, making him the highest-paid fast-food or fast-casual restaurant chief in the United States.
- Niccol's compensation, which included $96 million for four months in 2024 largely to offset forfeited Chipotle shares, has drawn criticism, notably from Senator Bernie Sanders, due to the significant disparity with typical Starbucks worker wages.
- He was brought in to revitalize Starbucks, which was struggling with declining sales, a falling stock price, unionization efforts, and operational challenges under his predecessor, Laxman Narasimhan.
- Niccol has a proven track record of successful turnarounds at Taco Bell and Chipotle, where he implemented innovative strategies and significantly boosted company performance.
- Under Niccol's leadership, Starbucks' stock has risen over 23% year-to-date, and revenue has remained positive for five consecutive quarters, leading some experts and the company to believe his high compensation is justified by the results.
