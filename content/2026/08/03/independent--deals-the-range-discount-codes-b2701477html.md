## Buyer FAQS

**How do I redeem my The Range discount code?**

To redeem your The Range promo code, click the basket icon in the main menu. You will see a dedicated box labelled “Gift/Discount code” under your order total – here is where you can paste your code. Click apply to see your amended order total.

### Does The Range offer free delivery?

Delivery is free on orders over £59, however, you will need to check the product page for the "eligible for free delivery" badge. For orders under £59, delivery fees start from £4.99, up to £24.99 for large to extra-large items.

### What is the returns policy at The Range?

The Range has a 28-day returns policy, with marketplace items returned directly to the seller. Smaller items can be returned to the store, unless stated otherwise on the delivery note.
