A MAIN rail line is shut this evening (Monday) due to an incident.

Northern services between Driffield and Scarborough are currently not running.

Emergency services, including the air ambulance are at the scene.

Affected stations include Scarborough, Seamer, Filey, Hunmanby, Bempton, Bridlington, Driffield, Beverley, Cottingham, Hull, Brough, Goole, Doncaster, Meadowhall, Sheffield, Thorne North and Selby.
