Manchester United are considering a shock move for Arsenal’s Myles Lewis-Skelly as Michael Carrick targets versatile options to bolster his squad. Newcastle’s Lewis Hall is also a target this transfer window, *The Independent* understands, with Eddie Howe’s departure perhaps hinting at a shift in strategy at the club that could see the left-back on the market.

A move for Lewis-Skelly would be more ambitious, but could Arsenal need to sell? The Premier League champions have made progress on a major deal for Vinicius Jr, though will have to pay a significant fee to capture the Brazilian from Real Madrid – particularly if Bruno Guimaraes is signed as well. Christian Norgaard is one fringe figure set for the exit after a single season at Arsenal; Everton is his next destination, it seems.

Elsewhere, Chelsea have announced the surprise signing of Danny Welbeck, with Jordan Henderson soon to follow, and Mohamed Salah appears to have a new club, finally, after leaving Liverpool on a free.

*Follow all of the latest news, rumours and done deals with our live blog below:*

## PSG progress in pursuit of Zion Suzuki

Paris Saint-Germain are progressing in talks to sign Japan international Zion Suzuki, per *Sky in Italy.*

The Champions League holders have made an offer worth an initial £23.9m plus add-ons for the Parma goalkeeper.

## Real Madrid hail Uefa for protecting football after World Cup sell-off plans abandoned

Real Madrid have praised Uefa for helping protect football “at a decisive moment” after Fifa abandoned plans to sell stakes in a World Cup company to private investors.

“Real Madrid values very positively the decision taken by Fifa to abandon the proposal to partially sell the commercial rights of its competitions,” read a statement from the 15-time European champions.

“Our club considers that it is good news for football, for its institutions and, above all, for the millions of fans of our beloved sport.

“Real Madrid would like to express its gratitude to Uefa, the confederations and federations, and all the institutions that firmly and responsibly opposed this project. Their unity and sense of duty have protected football at a decisive moment.”

## Arsenal make breakthrough in pursuit of Vinicius Junior

Arsenal have made progress in negotiations that could yet secure the sensational transfer of Vinicius Junior, with the outline of a personal deal agreed.

There is still a considerable way to go for any deal to happen, and the preference of both Real Madrid and the player is to stay at the Bernabeu.

Present realities may yet see Vinicius end up at Arsenal, however, as he only has a year left on his contract. The situation is said to now be entering the decisive period, and it is possible that the 26-year-old’s future is decided this week.

## QPR set to sign Glenn Kamara

Glen Kamara is set to join Queens Park Rangers, according to *Fabrizio Romano.*

The Finnish midfielder returns to England from Rennes, having previously played for Leeds.

The agreement is a loan with an option to buy, with the deal at the paperwork stage.

## Spurs youngster in talks with Lincoln

Lincoln City are in talks to sign Mason Melia on loan from Tottenham, according to *Sky Sports*.

The 18-year-old forward has left Roberto De Zerbi's camp during their pre-season tour of Australia and New Zealand, after playing 25 minutes off the bench during their 2-1 win against Sydney FC.

## DONE DEAL! Juventus sign Randal Kolo Muani from PSG

Juventus have completed the signing of Randal Kolo Muani from Paris Saint-Germain.

He joins on a permanent deal for a fixed fee of €38m, which could rise to €50m.

## Mikel Arteta provides update on Arsenal transfer plans after Girona friendly

Mikel Arteta has declared Arsenal’s ambition to make moves in the transfer market over the next few weeks.

"We expect to have movements in the next few weeks, obviously," said Arteta after his side's 4-1 pre-season win over Girona.

"We want to get better like anybody else, and you can see the transfer market and our opponents... We won't sit still and we are very ambitious with what we are doing.

"The margins are very small. Because we want to get better and the level is going to increase. We need to increase the competition internally. We need to make sure we identify the things that we don't have in the team."

## Juventus turn to Gugleilmo Vicario after ending Emi Martinez pursuit

Juventus have ended their pursuit of Aston Villa goalkeeper Emi Martinez, according to *Fabrizio Romano*, following a reported gap in valuation.

It means the club are now exploring a loan deal for out-of-favour Tottenham stopper Guglielmo Vicario, per *Sky in Italy*.

Juventus had also explored the conditions of a deal for Parma goalkeeper Zion Suzuki but face competition from Paris Saint-Germain and costs could be prohibitive.

Speaking of Juve and PSG dealings, Randal Kolo Muani has landed in Italy ahead of his loan to the Old Lady. Busy times in Turin!

## DONE DEAL! Juventus sign Kerim Alajbegovic

One of Europe’s top talents is bound for Juventus. Kerim Alajbegovic was strongly linked with Chelsea earlier in the transfer window but has opted for Turin, with the Serie A club striking a deal with Bayer Leverkusen for the 18-year-old.
