                                    Harrogate's historic Turkish Baths has been shortlisted for three national honours in the Good Spa Guide Awards 2026.

The spa has been nominated for Best Spa Team, Best Spa for Wellness and Best Spa in the North-East.

The Good Spa Guide Awards celebrate excellence across the UK's spa industry, recognising venues that deliver outstanding facilities, treatments and customer experiences.

Voting is now open for the Best Spa in the North-East category, with members of the public able to support Turkish Baths Harrogate until 31 August.

The winners of the Good Spa Guide Awards 2026 will be announced later this year.
