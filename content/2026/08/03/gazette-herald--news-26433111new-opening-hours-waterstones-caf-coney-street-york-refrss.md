A CAFÉ in a much-loved bookshop in York has announced its brand-new opening hours.

The popular spot within the city’s Waterstones branch, in Coney Street, has extended its opening hours, giving customers more time to unwind, meet friends or take a break from browsing the shelves.

  


The new café opening hours are 9am till 5.30pm Monday to Saturday, and 10.30am till 4pm on Sunday.

  The move aims to allow visitors of the Coney Street café more time to unwind 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

A company spokesperson said on social media: “We’re pleased to let you know that our café is now open a little longer, giving you even more time to enjoy your favourite drink and a slice of cake.

“Whether you’re meeting a friend or taking a break with a good book. We would love to see you.”

Bookshop opening hours remain unchanged.
