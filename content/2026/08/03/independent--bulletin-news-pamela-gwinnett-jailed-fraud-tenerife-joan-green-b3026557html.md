Pamela Gwinnett being arrested at Manchester Airport after her extradition from Spain (PA)

Pamela Gwinnett, 63, has been handed an additional two months in prison for breaching her bail after spending 16 months on the run in Tenerife.

Gwinnett fled to Spain in April 2025, hours after her application to vary bail terms was refused, and was apprehended in July before being extradited.

She was previously convicted in her absence last October and sentenced to six years for theft and fraud, having conned vulnerable 89-year-old Joan Green out of approximately £300,000.

Gwinnett systematically plundered Mrs Green's life savings, controlling her finances and isolating her, using the money to fund an extravagant lifestyle.

Judge Michael Maher dismissed Gwinnett's apology, stating she treated the court process and Mrs Green with "utter contempt," and she has been ordered to repay the stolen funds.
