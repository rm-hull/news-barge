# Trump teases new ‘punishment’ for Canada over wildfire smoke

- Donald Trump stated he would soon reveal "punishment" for Canada regarding wildfires that have caused smoke to blanket parts of the U.S.
- Trump accused Canada of mismanaging its forests and vowed the country would be "held responsible" for the smoke, suggesting additional tariffs as a potential consequence.
- He characterized Canada's actions as "Willful Negligence," claiming the U.S. is being "unnecessarily invaded by filthy, polluted and unhealthy air."
- Experts and Senator Bernie Sanders have countered Trump's claims, attributing the increase in wildfires to climate change rather than Canadian mismanagement.
- The U.S. is also experiencing its own wildfires in Washington state, with smoke from these blazes expected to affect air quality in the Pacific Northwest and California.
