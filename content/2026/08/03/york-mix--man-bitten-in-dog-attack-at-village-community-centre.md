A man needed hospital treatment after being bitten by a dog.

It happened on the field at the **Community Centre** on**Foxhill Lane**,** Brayton**,** Selby**, at about** 9.14pm**on** Sunday 19 July**.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The victim was bitten by the dog whilst he attempted to separate a fight between this and his own dog.
