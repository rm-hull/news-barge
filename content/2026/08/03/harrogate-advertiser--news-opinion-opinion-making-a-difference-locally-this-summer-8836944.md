# OPINION: Making a difference locally this summer

## Summer is a time when communities come together, through local events, activities, volunteering or simply spending time with friends and neighbours.

Across the Harrogate district, local charities and community organisations work every day to support residents, strengthen connections and make a positive difference.

From helping people access vital services and tackling loneliness, to supporting health and wellbeing, young people, older residents and those facing challenges, local organisations are at the heart of our communities. Many rely on the generosity, time and support of local people to continue their important work.

There are many ways to support causes close to home this summer. Attending a community event, volunteering, donating unwanted items, or visiting a charity shop can all help raise awareness and make a difference. Volunteering opportunities with local charities and community organisations can be found through HADCA’s volunteering directory, making it easier to find a role that matches your interests and skills.

Many local charities and community groups are also running activities, events and opportunities for residents to get involved. The HADCA guide to summer activities brings together a range of free and low-cost events activities, helping residents discover local organisations and connect with their community.

Shopping locally is another way to give back. Charity shops offer the chance to find unique pre-loved treasures while raising funds for important causes. Residents can support charities by visiting local shops run by organisations including Saint Michael’s Hospice, Martin House Hospice Care, Yorkshire Cancer Research, and many more. Or check out Time Together’s pre-loved clothing online shop on Vinted, which helps reduce waste and keep support within the local charity sector.

Supporting local also means discovering the charity-run attractions, gardens and cafés that make the Harrogate district special. These welcoming spaces provide opportunities to enjoy a day out while supporting local causes. Horticap in Harrogate offers beautiful plants, gifts and refreshments; the Kiosk at Conyngham Hall in Knaresborough provides outdoor activities for children for a small donation; and Ripon Walled Garden provides a beautiful setting to relax and enjoy food and drink.

Across the district, community venues, cafés, groups and charities provide welcoming spaces for residents to connect, take part in activities and support one another. Exploring what is happening locally is a great way to discover new places, meet people and help community organisations thrive.

Another simple way to support local good causes is through The Local Lotto. Residents can choose a cause that matters to them while having the chance to win cash prizes, including a £25,000 jackpot. With more than 100 local community organisations signed up, players can support organisations making a difference across the Harrogate District. For every £1 ticket, 60p goes directly to local good causes.

Whether it’s joining a fundraiser, attending an event, donating, volunteering, shopping locally, visiting a charity attraction or playing The Local Lotto, every action helps build a kinder, more connected Harrogate district.

This summer, celebrate and support the people, places and organisations that make our communities special.
