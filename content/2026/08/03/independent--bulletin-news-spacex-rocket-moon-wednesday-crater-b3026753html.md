# Abandoned SpaceX rocket set to crash into the Moon on Wednesday

- An abandoned SpaceX rocket is scheduled to crash into the Moon on Wednesday, around 2:28 AM ET.
- The spacecraft will impact the lunar surface at 5,400 mph with the force of three tons of TNT, creating a crater approximately 27 meters across and 5 meters deep.
- Scientists are eager to study the resulting dust and debris to understand the Moon's composition, with other spacecraft like Nasa's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter observing the event.
- The collision highlights the growing issue of space debris and marks the second accidental rocket crash on the Moon, following a Chinese spacecraft in 2022.
- This rare, observable impact provides valuable data for future lunar missions, helping scientists understand lunar dust behavior and potential risks to astronauts.
