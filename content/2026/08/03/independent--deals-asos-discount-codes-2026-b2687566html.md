## More ways to save at ASOS this month

### New customers save 20 per cent

If you’re a new ASOS customer, you can instantly take advantage of a first-order discount. Ordering through the app gets you a larger discount of 20 per cent, however, if you prefer shopping directly on the website, you can still enjoy 15 per cent off.

The discount excludes sale items, but it’s a great way to save money of popular brands such as Adidas, & Other Stories, Mango, Topshop and more.

### Exclusive app discount codes and flash sales

App codes are a regular occurrence at ASOS. Flash codes appear multiple times a week and usually last for 24 hours, however, sometimes they are only active for a couple of hours in the evening.

The app also comes with more benefits than exclusive promotions. Setting up an account means you can save your favourite items for later, create your own inspiration boards and check out quickly using your phone’s wallet.

### Member perks with ASOS World

ASOS World is the retailer’s loyalty scheme, which offers customers extra benefits, such as an exclusive 20 per cent birthday discount. You will need to register for an ASOS account to join the scheme, and benefits depend on your tier level. Here’s a breakdown of the ASOS World tiers:

- Stylist (upon registration) – early access to promotions, digital stylist and 20 per cent birthday discount.
- Curator (upgraded at £100 spend) – all of the stylist benefits, plus early access to new launches.
- Icon (upgraded at £450 spend) – all of the curator benefits, plus early access to sales and member-only sales.
- A-lister (upgraded at £750 spend) – all of the icon benefits, plus priority ‘back in stock’ alerts and event invites.

The spend that upgrades your tier level is refreshed annually.

### Waive delivery with ASOS Premier

If you’re a frequent ASOS shopper, an ASOS Premier membership is well worth considering. It’s priced at £9.95 per year, and members can take advantage of:

- Free unlimited next-day delivery on orders over £15
- Free standard delivery on orders under £15

With standard shipping costing £4.50 on orders of less than £40, and next-day delivery fees starting from £5.95, you can quickly make your money back on a membership.

### 10 per cent off with the ASOS student discount

Students can upgrade their new-term wardrobes for less, as ASOS offers 10 per cent off full-priced items with its student discount. All you need to do is verify your student status with SheerID or Unidays and you can save on every order.

## ASOS FAQs

### Where can I apply my ASOS discount code?

At checkout, you will see the delivery address section. Above it, not yet expanded, is a 'promo/student code' section that you will need to click to reveal. This is where you can paste your code to apply your discount.

### Does ASOS offer free delivery?

Yes, either with a premier account or on orders of more than £40 (that's a saving of £4.50).

### Can I return my order for free at ASOS?

Items must be returned within 28 day, but there are two ways to get free returns at ASOS. Firstly, if you purchased directly through ASOS brand partners or, secondly, if you returned your order within 14 days and paid for delivery. You can read the full returns policy on the ASOS app or website.
