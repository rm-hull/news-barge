## Best ways to save at DFDS in August

Here are some tips from our deals experts to use before searching for a DFDS voucher code:

- **Route-specific special offers:** Many DFDS promotions apply only to certain routes or destinations, so be sure to factor this in when planning your trip.
- **Know when to book:** By selecting off-peak times (such as avoiding school holidays), booking in advance or travelling on weekdays, you could save up to 50 per cent on your booking.
- **Use the DFDS cheap fare finder:** Enter your route and travel dates and let the fare finder do the hard work. It'll find you the best deals, so you can find more flexible savings.
- **Look for partner discounts:** If you’re an AA, Angling Trust or English Heritage member, you are eligible for exclusive DFDS partner discounts. For example, English Heritage members can save up to 33 per cent on ferries, which is significantly higher than the average 15 per cent discount offered to all customers.
- **Book directly with DFDS:** Save an additional 10 per cent on your booking if you find a lower price elsewhere. The DFDS best price guarantee can be claimed within 24 hours of making your booking.

### DFDS student and over-60s discounts

DFDS offers a 20 per cent discount on selected routes for students aged 18 to 27, young people aged 18 to 25 and seniors over 60. For example, right now there’s 20 per cent off sailings from Newhaven to Dieppe. To take advantage of these savings, simply verify your eligibility and present a valid ID at the port.

### Multi-trip package deals

DFDS multi-trip packages are ideal for those who travel frequently. Here are the different packages available:

- **Bronze:** From £69 per car (one way) and includes three to four return trips or six to nine single trips. With no extras included, it’s ideal for occasional travellers looking for basic savings.
- **Silver:** From £67 per car (one way) and includes five to eight return trips or ten to 17 single trips. You’ll get transferable tickets and a pet travel discount. This option is great for flexible, pet-friendly travel.
- **Gold:** From £65 per car (one way) and includes nine to 11 return trips or 18 to 23 single trips. This also includes transferable tickets, pet travel discount and priority boarding – perfect for frequent travellers who want speed and convenience.
- **Platinum:** From £63 per car (one way) and includes 12+ return trips or 24+ single trips. This comes with all the perks, such as transferable tickets, pet discount, priority boarding and premium lounge access. This is the best value for regular DFDS travellers.

## What to know before booking

### Cancellation Policy

DFDS cancellation policies vary depending on your ticket type and route, so we highly recommend double-checking before you book. For example, for Dover to Calais/Dunkirk routes, economy tickets are non-refundable, whereas premium tickets can be cancelled up to 48 hours before departure.

All refund requests must be made in writing within three months of the cancellation date.

### Information for families

If you’re travelling with children, you’re in luck. Most DFDS routes offer child-friendly entertainment, such as treasure hunts, face-painting and play areas with mascots like Jack the pirate . Plus, children under three travel and dine free of charge, without needing their own seat or bed. Don’t forget to check out the DFDS offers page for regular family-specific discounts.

### DFDS pet policy

Your furry friends are also welcome on board DFDS crossings. As part of the DEFRA Pet Travel Scheme (PETS), travelling abroad with dogs and other pets is made straightforward and stress-free.

### Booking as a group

Large groups can save with DFDS’s group vehicle saver offer and get 10 per cent off when booking 10 or more cars or bikes on routes such as Newcastle to Amsterdam and Dover to France. Extra benefits include paying a 25 per cent deposit upon booking, with the remaining balance due eight weeks before departure.

## DFDS FAQs

### Where can I apply my DFDS discount code?

Visit DFDS and book as you normally would. The information you need to fill in and extras to select from will vary depending on the service you choose. Once you reach 'Booking and passenger details', search for the 'Redeem a voucher/campaign code' box. Paste the code and click redeem to save instantly.

### Is there a DFDS loyalty programme?

Officially, no. However, the multi-trip packages function just like a loyalty programme. The more you travel with DFDS, the more benefits you will save. Make sure to read this section under 'Ways to save at DFDS' for more information.

**Does DFDS have an NHS discount?**

NHS staff and other keyworkers can access DFDS discounts through the Blue Light Card scheme. All you need to do is verify your eligibility through the Blue Light Card portal to access your savings.
