Donald Trump shared a fabricated image on Truth Social depicting himself alongside George Washington and Abraham Lincoln, appearing to sign a map of the United States.

The post, made without comment, was celebrated by his supporters as showcasing 'three American legends.'

Critics on social media mocked the image, with some replacing Washington and Lincoln with figures like Jeffrey Epstein or depicting Trump as a clown.

This image is part of a series of recent posts where Trump has compared himself to Elvis Presley and claimed to be aging 'in reverse.'

Over the weekend, Trump made approximately 40 posts, largely consisting of fake images and content designed to boost his ego, with some assistance from White House aides.
