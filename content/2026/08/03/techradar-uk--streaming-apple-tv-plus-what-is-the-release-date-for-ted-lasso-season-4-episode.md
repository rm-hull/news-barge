It's nearly time to continue the AFC Richmond journey. Three years after we thought *Ted Lasso* had ended, the hugely popular Apple TV original is returning for its fourth season — and, this time, the titular soccer coach is set to manage the club's newly founded women's team.

So, will Ted be able to work his magic once more and make AFC Richmond Women a force to be reckoned with? And who among the show's supporting cast is back for *Ted Lasso* season 4?

I can't answer the former, but you'll want to read our***Ted Lasso* season 4 guide for more on the latter. Alternatively, you could just read on to find out when the series' next episode will air — so, if that's all you're here for, scroll on!

## What time can I watch Ted Lasso season 4 episode 1?

![Ted Lasso — Season 4 Official Trailer (Together) | Apple TV - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/GACU1N9hg5o/maxresdefault.jpg) 

*Ted Lasso* season 4 episode 1 is expected to make its Apple TV debut on**Wednesday, August 5 at 12am PT / 3am ET**.

'Expected' is the keyword here, too. You see, in recent times, the first episode of numerous other new and returning Apple TV series has actually come out in the US and Canada on Tuesday evenings. If *Ted Lasso 4* follows suit, its first episode could drop on Tuesday, August 4 at 6pm PT / 9PM ET.

However, the *Ted Lasso* season 4 review embargo is scheduled to lift at 12am PT on August 5. It would be odd for this season's first chapter to air before critics have published their impressions of it. So, unless Apple unexpectedly changes the aforementioned embargo details, I'm going to play it safe and say that this season's opening number will come out on August 5.

If that is the case, here's when season 4 episode 1, titled 'Home', will premiere in other countries:
