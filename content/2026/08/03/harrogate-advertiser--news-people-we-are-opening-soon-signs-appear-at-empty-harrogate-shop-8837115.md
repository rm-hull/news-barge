# 'We are opening soon' signs appear at empty Harrogate shop

## Food lovers in Harrogate have a new takeaway shop to look forward to.

The signs are up at 7 Station Parade between Husk Beer Emporium and Bar on the left and Mama Doreen's Emporium on the right.

Located at what formerly the home of Stations News newsagent, Holy Shnitzel is festooned over the shop frontage of the empty unit with the words “we are opening soon”.

The new takeaway’s branding also includes a photograph of what looks like either a pastrami sandwich or a breaded schnitzel sandwich, both icons of New York City and American Jewish cuisine.
