A York pub was burgled overnight.

The Beagle on Foxwood Lane was targeted between 11.45pm on Sunday and 8.30am on Monday (2-3 August).

Police are appealing for witnesses and relevant CCTV, doorbell and dashcam footage.

#### Pub location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We need to hear form anyone who recalls any suspicious activity or vehicles near the pub overnight, including a white Mercedes van.”
