In The Room: Why Burnham’s education reform isn’t all it seems

Education Secretary Lucy Powell stated that schools are constrained by an "academic straitjacket," hindering children's potential.

She announced a review into the adequacy of teaching, aiming to reverse reforms implemented by Michael Gove.

Ms Powell attributed the one million young people not in education, employment, or training (NEET) to Mr Gove's legacy.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham also prioritises tackling NEET figures, proposing new technical education routes for 14-year-olds and apprenticeship bursaries.

The National Grid is launching a £5 million fund to help 5,000 young people enter the workforce over the next five years, focusing on practical training for the energy, construction, and infrastructure sectors.
