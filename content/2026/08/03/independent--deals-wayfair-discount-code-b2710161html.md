## FAQs

### Does Wayfair offer free delivery?

Wayfair doesn’t offer standard free delivery on all orders, as delivery costs depend on the size and type of items you’re buying. However, the retailer does occasionally run free delivery promotions, or include free delivery on selected products or minimum spends.

### Does Wayfair run Black Friday sales?

Yes, Wayfair takes part in Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales, with discounts of up to 40 per cent site-wide. In previous years, there has been reductions on sofas, beds, storage and garden furniture, making it one of the best times of year to shop for larger home purchases.

### Can I use more than one Wayfair discount code?

Wayfair only allows one discount code per order. If you have multiple codes, you’ll need to choose the one that gives you the best saving. You can often stack promo codes with flash sale offers, but remember to check the T&Cs beforehand.
