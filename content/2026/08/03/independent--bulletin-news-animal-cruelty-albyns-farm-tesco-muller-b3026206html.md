Undercover footage reveals animal cruelty at award-winning dairy farm

Undercover footage exposed workers at Albyns Farm, an award-winning Crown Estate dairy, committing severe acts of animal cruelty against cows.

The video, filmed by activist Joey Carbstrong, depicted staff kicking, punching, and slapping cows, prodding them with sharp goads, twisting their tails, and one instance of a worker stubbing a cigarette on an animal's face.

Albyns Farm, which supplies Tesco and dairy giant Muller, was suspended by both companies immediately after the revelations of the abuse.

An animal welfare expert who analysed the footage identified over 600 instances where workers inflicted avoidable pain and distress upon the animals.

The farm's owners, Curtismill Holsteins, dismissed several employees and initiated an independent investigation, pledging to prevent any recurrence of such behaviour.
