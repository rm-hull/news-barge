Septuagenarian vegetable grower Gerald Stratford unexpectedly became an internet sensation five years ago during lockdown after a social media post about his potato harvest went viral.

Initially unfamiliar with social media, he now boasts over 714,000 followers and has featured in TV shows, British Vogue, and campaigns for luxury brands.

Now 77, Stratford is passionate about inspiring future generations to grow their own food.

He has partnered with HelloFresh to launch The Curious Kitchen, a free immersive family experience at the Natural History Museum in London, aimed at teaching children about food's journey from seed to plate.

Stratford provides practical advice on easy-to-grow vegetables for young gardeners, including radishes, potatoes, tomatoes, cucumbers, and carrots, highlighting the importance of quick results to maintain children's interest.
