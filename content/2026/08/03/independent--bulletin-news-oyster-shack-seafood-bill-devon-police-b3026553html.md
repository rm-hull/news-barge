Family-run restaurant issues warning after diners leave without paying £600 bill

The Oyster Shack, a family-run restaurant in Bigbury, Devon, reported that a group of six diners left without paying a bill of almost £600 on 25 July.

The party, comprising three men and three women, ordered several high-value items, including four £66 lobsters and a £120 fruits de mer platter.

Restaurant owner Kieron Vanstone stated that attempts to contact the group via a booking phone number were unsuccessful, and Devon and Cornwall Police are currently investigating the alleged 'dine and dash' incident.

The restaurant issued a public warning to other businesses, emphasising the significant financial loss and emotional distress such incidents cause for small independent establishments and their staff.

This event highlights a growing national trend, with UK Hospitality reporting that a third of operators have experienced 'dine and dash' incidents, and police data indicating only 3 per cent of over 2,100 reported cases resulted in charges over the past two years.
