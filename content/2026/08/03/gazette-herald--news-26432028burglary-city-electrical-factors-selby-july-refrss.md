MULTIPLE burglars have struck inside a specialist electrical shop in North Yorkshire.

The county’s police force is appealing for witnesses and information following a burglary at City Electrical Factors on Prospect Way in Selby last month.

It happened at 12.06pm on Thursday, July 16, and involved multiple people.

“Numerous electrical products were stolen from the warehouse section of the store by multiple suspects,” said a spokesperson for the force.

They added that one of the men that officers need to speak to is on the attached CCTV image and they are seeking information to help identify the man and “any further relevant information about the individual or possible associates.”

  If you have any information, please contact **[thomas.leah1@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:thomas.leah1@northyorkshire.police.uk)**

  using email, live chat, through the website, or by calling 101.
