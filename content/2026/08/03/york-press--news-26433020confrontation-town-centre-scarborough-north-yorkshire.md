THREE people have targeted a woman in the centre of a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a confrontation that took place in the centre of Scarborough last Wednesday (July 29).

It started in Cross Street in Scarborough at 5.40pm, initially outside Boyes before it continued up to Heron Foods in Newborough.
