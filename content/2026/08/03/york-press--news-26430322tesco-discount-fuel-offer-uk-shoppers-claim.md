Tesco is offering shoppers a discount at its filling stations this summer.

Customers who spend £60 or more in-store will receive a coupon for 5 per cent off fuel, which could mean a saving of up to 8p per litre.

The coupon can be used at Tesco petrol stations across the UK on petrol or diesel, and the offer is valid until Sunday, August 23.

The promotion is valid for in-store purchases made until Sunday, August 16.

A Tesco spokesperson said: "Tesco operates more than 500 filling stations across the UK and offering a discount on the price of fuel is just one way in which Tesco is helping its customers this summer.

"The supermarket recently launched Freebie Thursdays, giving away millions of coupons for free products to Clubcard members, alongside extending its Kids Eat Free offer at The Café at Tesco."

To redeem the offer, customers must present a printed coupon at the kiosk.

It cannot be used at pay at pump locations, unmanned stations, Tesco Express sites that sell Esso fuel, or in Northern Ireland and the Isle of Man.

A minimum of £1 must be spent on fuel to use the coupon, with a maximum discount of £10.

The offer comes alongside other summer promotions, including Freebie Thursdays, which gives Clubcard members free product coupons, and extending its Kids Eat Free offer at The Café at Tesco.

Earlier this year, Tesco expanded its Aldi Price Match to more than 2,000 Tesco Express stores.
