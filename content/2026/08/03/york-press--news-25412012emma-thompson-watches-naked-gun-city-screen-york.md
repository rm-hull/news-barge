It is one year since a movie superstar and certified ‘lovey’ visited York to watch a film with friends at the cinema.

Oscar winning actress Dame Emma Thompson visited City Screen last August to catch a screening of the Naked Gun film with friends.

City Screen manager Cath Sharpe said at the time: “Emma came in this afternoon to catch a screening of the new Naked Gun film, and the staff were very excited to meet her.

“A few of them had really nice chats with her – she gave Olivia a hug and held her hand.”

Whilst staff members were keen to keep their conversations private, they said that she shared her current projects, offered advice to aspiring filmmakers, and was very sweet.

Cath added that she was a ‘certified lovey’ and that she enjoyed a glass of prosecco in the cinema’s café with local friends.
