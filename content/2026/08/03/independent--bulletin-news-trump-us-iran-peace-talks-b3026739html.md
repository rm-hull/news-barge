# Trump makes fresh Iran jibe after Tehran denies peace talks

- Donald Trump accused Iran of being "unbelievably duplicitous" for denying that talks aimed at ending the conflict were taking place.
- Trump claimed Iran had "begged" for negotiations and that discussions were ongoing to reach a "solution" to the current stand-off, despite Tehran's denials.
- He dismissed Iran's assertions of controlling the strategic Strait of Hormuz as "their usual blather," stating the US Navy maintains full control.
- These remarks followed Trump's decision to halt a significant US military attack to allow for diplomatic efforts, which Iran subsequently denied were occurring.
- Iran's foreign ministry spokesperson stated the Strait of Hormuz would not revert to its pre-conflict status and denied any talks about reopening the waterway, though confirming discussions with Oman.
