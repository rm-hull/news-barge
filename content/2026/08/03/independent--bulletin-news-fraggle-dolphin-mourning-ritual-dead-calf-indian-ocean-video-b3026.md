Grieving mother dolphin carries dead calf at sea for days

A grieving mother dolphin named Fraggle carried her dead calf through the Indian Ocean for six days.

Drone footage captured by Geographe Marine Research, an Australian conservation organisation, showed Fraggle with her newborn, which was only two weeks old.

Fraggle had previously experienced the loss of four other calves.

Other dolphins were observed joining Fraggle during her period of mourning.

Researchers have documented similar grief-like behaviour in dolphins and whales, where they carry their deceased offspring.
