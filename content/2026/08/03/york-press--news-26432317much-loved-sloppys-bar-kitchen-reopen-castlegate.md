## Top York burger restaurant announces new site for August re-opening

One of the very meaty burgers *(Image: Pic supplied)*

  **A top-rated York burger bar and kitchen has revealed the location of its new city centre site.**

Sloppy’s Bar and Kitchen closed its doors in the Acomb Wood Shopping Centre in Woodthorpe just over a month ago in preparation for the move.

It will now re-open later this month in Castlegate, where Clucking Oinks used to be.

Partners Molly McGrath and Leon Sledmore co-founded the restaurant in November 2022 after several years in a kiosk in the city known as Sloppy Burger, which became extremely popular.

They also operated from a couple of other venues before settling next door to the top-rated Italian restaurant, attracted by its warm and welcoming staff and lovely food.

However, the couple had always a sought a return to the city centre and its greater footfall.

Molly told the Press: “We are currently redecorating and refurbishing the restaurant. We are hoping to open later in August but we don’t have a date yet.”

Molly says the location is perfect and central to get more footfall.

“We are excited to be back in the city centre. They are good friends at Clucking Oinks. It (the venue) was an opportunity we could not miss.”

Announcing their departure from the venue, whilst retaining their smaller operation at Spark, the operators of Clucking Oinks said a restaurant location wasn’t “viable” for them due to the “climate situation of hospitality in the UK.”

  Partners Molly McGrath and Leon Sledmore outside the Woodthorpe restaurant, which is now closed. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Clucking Oinks has since announced it is selling from its new food truck at the Fox in Holgate Road from Wednesday to Sunday over the summer.

Molly said: “We just hope the city centre will be the progression we need to push the business forward.”

The new location will have “similar vibes” and a “similar menu” to the former restaurant in Woodthorpe.

Molly continued: “It will have a more restaurant style but with a relaxed atmosphere.”

There will be the famous burgers and wings, but more small plates and cocktails, she added.

Sloppy’s is among the most popular restaurants in York, with Tripadvisor awarding it 4.9 stars out of five, based on 214 reviews, ranking it 13th out of 888 restaurants in York.

The latest Tripadvisor review gave five stars and said: “ Amazing friendly service and something a bit different local to us.”

It added: “Quick service, lovely friendly staff. American twist food, served hot and flavoursome. Quirky, great vibe with indie music. Great choice of cocktails.”

Another review also gave five stars and said: “Best place I’ve eaten”, adding: “Best burgers in York and bread buns and home made chips wings are good also.”

Google save 4.8 stars out of five based on 135 reviews.
