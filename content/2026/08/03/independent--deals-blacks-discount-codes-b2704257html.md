## Ways to save on your Blacks order this month

### 10 per cent offer with the Blacks student discount

If you’re in sixth form, college, university or an apprenticeship, you can get 10 per cent on select products. Just verify your identity via Unidays or Student Beans and you’ll receive a unique discount code to use at checkout. However, this discount can’t be used in conjunction with any other deals.

**Blacks’ NHS and Blue Light discounts**

NHS staff can access a 10 per cent discount (online only) by registering with Health Service Discounts. If you have a Blue Light Card, which covers NHS, emergency services, armed forces, teachers, and social care staff, you can also get a 15 per cent discount on full-price items in-store and online.

Verification requires a valid work email or photo ID. The discount will typically exclude sale items, gift cards, and certain premium brands.

### Exclusive discounts for Scouts, Guides, Duke of Edinburgh’s Award and more

Blacks also offers discounts to a range of groups, including Scouts, Guides, participants of the Duke of Edinburgh’s Award, NUS and more. How you can access this discount depends on which group you’re a part of, so head to Blacks’ website for the full list of partners and details of how to sign up.
