### Agoda members save up to 25 per cent

To access the best deals throughout the year, you may want to sign up to Agoda’s VIP loyalty programme. This is free to join – all you need to do is make two or more bookings in two years and you’ll automatically qualify for the programme. After that, the idea is simple: the more bookings you make, the higher your status will rise and the higher the discounts you’ll unlock.

Memberships are divided into four tiers, each with its own perks. Here’s what to expect:

- **Member:** Entry-level status doesn’t give you any additional discounts, but you will gain access to AgodaCash, which you can use to get money off future bookings.
- **VIP Silver:** Once you’ve made two bookings, you’ll find discounts of up to 12 per cent on selected listings in your account.
- **VIP Gold:** If you make five bookings within 24 months, you’ll be upgraded to VIP Gold status and enjoy 18 per cent off selected destinations.
- **VIP Platinum:** After 10 bookings, you’ll enter VIP Platinum status, where you’ll be given up to 25 per cent off hotels and accommodation around the world.
