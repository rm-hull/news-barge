Slingsby Gooseberry Gin has returned to the Harrogate brand's collection following months of requests from customers.

The flavour had previously been withdrawn from the range but is now available again alongside Slingsby's Blackberry Gin and Marmalade Gin, which have also returned after an extended period out of stock.

Made using Yorkshire gooseberries, the gin combines citrus notes with the tang of gooseberries and a fruity finish.

The Harrogate-based gin producer says many of the botanicals used across its range are sourced locally.

Chris Williams, Director of Slingsby Gin, said:

“One of the questions we've been asked most has been, 'When is Gooseberry Gin coming back?' Seeing how much people have missed it made bringing it back an easy decision.

“We're incredibly grateful for the continued support of the Slingsby community, and we're pleased to welcome Gooseberry back alongside Blackberry and Marmalade.

“It's wonderful to have these classics back in the Slingsby collection for long-time fans and those discovering them for the first time.”


The return means Slingsby's full collection is now available through its online shop, including its London Dry Gin, Yorkshire Rhubarb Gin and Slingsby Vodka.
