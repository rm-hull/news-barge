# Travis Kelce discusses wedding to Taylor Swift

- Travis Kelce has publicly referred to Taylor Swift as his wife for the first time since their wedding at Madison Square Garden.
- Speaking to reporters at his Kansas City Chiefs’ training camp in Missouri on Wednesday, Kelce said that he missed the Knicks play-off in June as he was stuck at a camp.
- The 36-year-old NFL player described their wedding on 3 July as the “best night” of his life.
- No photographs have been released from the nuptials, which Kelce said were “full of celebrations”.
- He added: “I appreciate everybody who came out and celebrated and, you know, had fun with us.”
