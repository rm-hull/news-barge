British cyclist Finlay Tarling has died at the age of 19 after a crash during stage eight of the Volta a Portugal.

The race was neutralised after Tarling was involved in a “serious accident” on the 166.8km route from Melgaco to Fafe.

“The organisation of the 87th Volta a Portugal em Bicicleta Jogos Santa Casa deeply regrets to announce the passing of British cyclist Finlay Tarling, from the NSN Development Team, following a serious accident that occurred during the 8th stage of the race,” race organisers confirmed in a statement.

“At this moment of profound dismay, the organisation of the Volta a Portugal and the Portuguese Cycling Federation express their most heartfelt condolences to the family of Finlay Tarling, to his teammates, to the NSN Development Team, and to all his friends and loved ones.”

Tarling was riding for the NSN Development Team having signed a two-year deal ahead of the 2025 season.

In a statement, NSN said: "We are heartbroken to announce the passing of Finlay Tarling at the Volta a Portugal today.

"Fin was a much loved member of our team but most importantly, he was a son, a brother, and a friend to so many. He will be deeply missed.

"Our deepest condolences go out to Fin's parents Michael and Dawn, his brother Josh, and everyone who was lucky enough to call Fin a friend."

A highly-rated talent, particularly as a time triallst, Tarling won three bronze medals at the 2024 UCI Junior Track Cycling World Championships.

His elder brother Josh rides for Netcompany Ineos and is a world champion in the points race on the track.
