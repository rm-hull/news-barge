# Trump says deployment aboard USS Lincoln has not gone on ‘nearly long enough’

- President Donald Trump on Friday angrily snapped back at reporters who asked him to weigh in on reports that morale aboard USS *Abraham**Lincoln* is so poor that some sailors have attempted to jump overboard to escape the ship after nine months at sea.
- “No, they’re not,” he said. “That ship is moving right now, or very shortly, and it's being replaced with another very similar ship,” he added.
- The reporter followed up by asking if the president was worried the deployment had gone on too long. “No, no, no, not nearly long enough,” he answered before walking away.
- Family members and reported text messages detailed deteriorating conditions on the aircraft carrier, including a lack of fresh food and sailors jumping overboard due to extreme distress.
- Navy officials and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth denied claims of widespread mental health crises or supply failures, stating mission-critical supplies remain available. White House Deputy Press Secretary Anna Kelly defended Trump's support for service members while criticizing former President Joe Biden's military policies.
