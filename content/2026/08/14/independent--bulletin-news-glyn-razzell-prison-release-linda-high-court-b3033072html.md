# Murderer who killed estranged wife cannot be released from prison, High Court rules

![Glyn Razzell was jailed for life with a minimum of almost 16 years in 2003 for the murder of estranged wife Linda Razzell](https://static.independent.co.uk/s3fs-public/thumbnails/image/2019/02/23/12/pa-1814212.jpg)

- Wife killer Glyn Razzell will remain in prison after the High Court overturned a Parole Board decision to release him on licence.
- Mrs Justice Cheema-Grubb quashed the decision, ruling that Razzell's risk to the public had not diminished sufficiently to justify release.
- Razzell was jailed for life in 2003 for the 2002 murder of his estranged wife Linda, whose body has never been found.
- The Ministry of Justice and Mrs Razzell's family urged the court to block his release over safety fears and the risk of never finding her remains.
- The killer continues to deny the murder and refuses to reveal the location of his wife's body.
