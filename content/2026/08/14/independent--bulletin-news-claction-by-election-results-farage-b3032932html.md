# Nigel Farage avoids count after winning Clacton by-election

![Count Binface arrives for the Clacton by-election at Clacton Leisure Centre in Essex](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/14/06/01KZYTQRNG74FAMS122AFD3NBB.jpg)

- Nigel Farage has won the Clacton by-election, retaining the seat for Reform UK on a turnout of 44.37 per cent.
- The Reform UK leader skipped the official count, claiming Essex Police advised him to stay away due to a credible security threat.
- Essex Police and local election officials said no candidates were advised to stay away and robust security was in place.
- The by-election was triggered by Farage resigning amid a parliamentary standards investigation into an undeclared £5 million donation from a cryptocurrency tycoon.
- Opponents noted that the standards investigation will resume now he has won, potentially exposing him to further sanctions or another recall petition.
