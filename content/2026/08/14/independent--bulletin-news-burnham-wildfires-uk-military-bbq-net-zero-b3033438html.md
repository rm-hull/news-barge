# How Burnham is planning to protect Britain from continued wildfires

- Andy Burnham has pledged additional military personnel and aerial firefighting support to help tackle devastating wildfires across Britain.
- The government has reaffirmed its commitment to Labour's 2050 net zero target, emphasising that extreme heatwaves show climate change is an immediate reality.
- A ban on disposable barbecues has come into force to prevent further blazes during unprecedented heat and drought conditions.
- Plans have been recommitted to construct nine new reservoirs and adapt public infrastructure, including schools and hospitals, for extreme temperatures.
- Recent wildfires in regions such as the West Midlands have destroyed homes, displaced residents, and resulted in dozens of hospitalisations.
