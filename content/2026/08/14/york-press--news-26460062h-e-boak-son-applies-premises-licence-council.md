A farm in Burythorpe has applied for a licence to sell alcohol 24 hours a day.

H&E Boak & Son, Park Farm, has submitted an application to North Yorkshire Council under the Licensing Act 2003.

If approved, the shop will be able to sell alcohol for consumption off the premises between midnight and 11.59pm.

The application is open for public comment until September 4.

People can submit their views in writing to North Yorkshire Council’s Licensing Services at Ryedale House, Old Malton Road, Malton, YO17 7HH, or via email to [licensing.east@northyorks.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing.east@northyorks.gov.uk.)

The application record and register are available to view by appointment during regular office hours at the above address or online.

The notice warns that making a false statement in connection with this application is a criminal offence, carrying an unlimited fine upon summary conviction.
