Mount Etna is erupting again in Sicily, attracting tourists despite concerns over safety at the active volcano.

The eruption has sent molten lava down the slopes of the volcano, while rising plumes have disrupted air travel in the region.

Italian authorities have closed Catania Airport because of the volcanic activity.

Despite the risks and warnings from local guides, tourists continue to visit the area to witness the eruption.

Mount Etna is the tallest active volcano in Europe and remains a major attraction for visitors to Sicily.
