# American Pie actor opens up on her staggering OnlyFans earnings

![Shannon Elizabeth shot to fame thanks to her role in American Pie](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/14/11/01KZBMCVZ30RXWJPC5QRJ50JKC.jpg)

- American Pie star Shannon Elizabeth revealed to *Variety* that she earned $1 million (£740,000) within just nine days of joining OnlyFans in April 2026.
- The 52-year-old actor, who offers subscribers behind-the-scenes photos and direct chatting, aims to help broaden the platform beyond adult entertainment.
- Elizabeth joins a growing list of mainstream celebrities using the site, alongside figures such as rapper Cardi B and British singers Lily Allen and Kate Nash.
- Harry Potter actor Jessie Cave also revealed she made more from one year on OnlyFans posting hair-brushing videos than from her entire acting career.
- Cave explained she turned to the platform to create non-sexual content out of financial desperation following industry rejections and high childcare costs.
