# EuroMillions results: Winning lottery and Thunderball numbers for Friday 14 August

- Tonight’s EuroMillions draw has offered players the chance to win a jackpot of £24 million.
- To win the prize, a player had to get five main numbers plus two lucky stars.
- In the UK, every EuroMillions entry automatically includes a unique "UK Millionaire Maker" code, entering you into an additional draw with a guaranteed £1 million winner in every draw.
- The winning Thunderball numbers were **4**,** 15**,** 25**,** 27**and** 32**. The Thunderball was** 11.**
- The winning EuroMillions numbers were **5**,** 29**,** 39**,** 48**and** 49**. The lucky stars were** 4**and** 8.**
