# ‘Jumped upwards’: Passenger describes moment train derailed near Lewes

- Two people were seriously injured and 18 others hurt when a train derailed between Haywards Heath and Lewes in Sussex on Thursday.
- An investigation is currently underway to determine the root cause of the derailment.
- Passenger Rob Bradley was travelling with his six-year-old daughter.
- He described how his carriage “jumped upwards” and skidded along the tracks.
- He recalled seeing the back of the train open, with “smoke and dust ... pouring into the carriage” after it came to a halt.
