# Netanyahu pressured by White House to condemn Israeli settlers in West Bank

- White House officials are urging Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to publicly condemn a settler-led siege on the West Bank village of Qusra.
- The U.S. diplomatic intervention escalated after authorities discovered a Palestinian-American's residence was among those targeted by settlers.
- Settlers have blockaded homes in Qusra for nearly a week, cutting off utilities and leaving around 15 Palestinians confined without electricity or running water.
- U.S. Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee publicly criticized the settlers surrounding a Palestinian residence, labeling them 'Israeli terrorists.'
- Netanyahu faces political risks in condemning the siege due to his reliance on right-wing settler support in an upcoming election.
