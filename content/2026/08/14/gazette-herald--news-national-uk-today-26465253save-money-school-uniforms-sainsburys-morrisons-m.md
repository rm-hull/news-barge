## How to save more money on back to school buys

Websites such as Topcashback have deals where you can get money back on your buys, and often a sign up bonus if you aren't already a member. There are currently deals with Sainsbury's and Morrisons - sign up using this link and then search for the retailers to see their current cashback offers.

Deals expert Ben Smye from HotUKDeals said parents should not panic if they missed the traditional July sales: “My advice to parents is this: don’t stress about having missed the ‘perfect’ buying window.”

He added that there were still deals to be found in August, while buying closer to the start of term could help parents avoid children outgrowing clothes before they have even been worn.

## Second-hand uniform could save parents £75

For families looking to cut costs even further, Gumtree data suggests buying pre-loved could make a significant difference.

Its figures show that a school shirt, trousers, shoes and bag could cost around £33 second-hand, compared with approximately £108 using a basket of sampled high-street prices from M&S, Next and Clarks.

That represents a potential saving of around £75.

## Could waiting until term starts save more?

Parents may be tempted to wait for end-of-season reductions, and there is some evidence that prices can fall as retailers clear stock.

Gumtree's earlier analysis found the average advertised price of a school coat was £18 in April, rising to £28 during the peak July shopping period.

However, waiting until the final days before term starts comes with a risk: sizes and colours can become harder to find.

Smye said parents should therefore consider buying essential basics now while keeping an eye out for further reductions later in August.

Gumtree CEO Nav Dhillon said: “Back-to-school shopping lands at an expensive point in the year, when many families are already paying for childcare, activities and days out during the summer holidays.”

He added: “Our data suggests parents could find a basic basket of uniform essentials and a school bag for around £33 secondhand, potentially saving approximately £75 compared with the equivalent sampled high-street basket.”

## Five ways to save on school uniform

  **1. Check the school rules first**

  Find out which items need school branding and which can be bought from supermarkets.

  **2. Shop around**

  Compare supermarket prices with high-street retailers before buying everything in one place.

  **3. Look for second-hand uniform**

  Check school uniform shops, Vinted, eBay, Facebook Marketplace and local selling groups.

  **4. Buy bundles**

  Multipacks and bundles can bring the cost per item down, particularly for shirts, polo shirts and trousers.

  **5. Check before you buy**

  If buying second-hand, ask for measurements rather than relying on the age printed on the label and check high-wear areas such as knees, collars, cuffs and shoe soles.

For families trying to keep costs under control, the biggest saving may not come from finding one spectacular deal, but from mixing discounted supermarket basics with carefully chosen second-hand branded items.
