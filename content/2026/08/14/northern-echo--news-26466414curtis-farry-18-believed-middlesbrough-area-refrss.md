An 18-year-old wanted in connection with a string of burglaries and assaults is 'believed to be in the Middlesbrough area' police have said.

Cleveland Police want to speak with Curtis Farry regarding these incidents, which happened between May and August.

Officers believe Farry is still in the Middlesbrough area.

Police are appealing for anyone who knows his current whereabouts - or for Farry himself - to get in touch with them.

Anyone with information is urged to contact the police on 101, quoting reference number 157945.

Information can also be reported anonymously to Crimestoppers via their website or by calling 0800 555 111.
