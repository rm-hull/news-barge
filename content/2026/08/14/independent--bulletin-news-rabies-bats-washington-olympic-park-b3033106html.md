# Rabies warning for park visitors after bat swoops at multiple people

![Little brown bats are among the species of bat commonly found in Washington state](https://static.independent.co.uk/2024/12/17/18/bats-little-brown_pepperell_troy-gipps-6691.jpg)

- The National Park Service has warned visitors at Olympic National Park in Washington state after a bat swooped at multiple parkgoers and became caught in a woman's hair.
- The incident occurred near the Staircase Rapids Bridge, with the bat also targeting several other people in the area.
- Health officials have urged anyone who may have come into contact with bats in the park to seek immediate medical attention, even if they were not bitten.
- Less than one per cent of bats carry rabies, but infected bats remain the leading cause of human rabies deaths in the United States.
- Rabies is fatal once symptoms appear, but prompt treatment with post-exposure vaccines can prevent the infection from developing.
