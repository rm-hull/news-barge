# JD Vance attempts to clarify ‘goal number one’ of Iran war ahead of midterms

- Vice President JD Vance declared that keeping energy prices low for Americans is the Trump administration's top goal during the conflict with Iran.
- The administration's secondary objective is preventing Iran from obtaining a nuclear weapon, despite previous assertions that its enrichment program was destroyed.
- Gas prices in the US have risen to a national average of $4.07 a gallon following strikes on Iran and the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz.
- Rising inflation and energy costs pose a significant political risk for Republicans ahead of the November midterm elections.
- While the administration maintains a naval blockade on the Strait of Hormuz, Vance has privately opposed the war and voiced concerns against deploying U.S. ground troops.
