# Fears raised about condition of train track before Lewes derailment

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/HtShPEwt/poster.jpg?width=720)

- Lewes MP James MacCleary says that concerns had been raised about the local train track a week before a Southern Railway train derailed.
- Twenty people were injured, two seriously, in the incident between between Lewes and Haywards Heath in East Sussex on Thursday.
- Emergency services declared a major incident after three carriages left the track.
- Mr MacCleary said that there had been an earlier meeting with Greater Thameslink railway to discuss the bumpy track, which was “possibly a result of the incredibly hot weather”.
- An investigation is underway to determine the root cause of the derailment on the London Victoria to Eastbourne service.
