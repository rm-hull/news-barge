# Dozens of homes destroyed in massive West Midlands fires

- Huge fires have spread across the West Midlands and the surrounding regions, destroying dozens of homes and prompting widespread evacuations.
- A major wildfire in Stourbridge caused extensive damage to grassland and properties, resulting in three firefighters and a child being hospitalised.
- Further blazes in Worcestershire, Staffordshire, and Warwickshire caused power cuts, property damage, and severe traffic delays along the M6.
- Fire chiefs have warned that emergency service operations in affected areas will continue for days, urging residents to stay away.
- Prime Minister Andy Burnham and Housing Secretary Angela Rayner praised the emergency services for tackling the blazes in exceptionally difficult conditions.
