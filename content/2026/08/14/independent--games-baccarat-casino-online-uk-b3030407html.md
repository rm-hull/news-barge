## **How to play baccarat online**

Here’s a brief guide for beginners on how to play baccarat.

The aim of the game is to bet on whichever hand – Player or Banker – that you think will finish closest to a total of 9 (or bet on a Tie).

If the first two cards total 8 or 9, that is a ‘natural’ and the round ends immediately. Otherwise a third card may be drawn automatically under fixed rules – you never make that decision yourself.

Card values are as follows:

- Aces = 1
- 2-9 = face value
- 10s and picture cards = 0

If a total goes above 9, only the last digit counts (e.g. a 7 and an 8 equals 15, which scores 5).
