# Firefighters tackle huge blaze at hotel near Wembley Stadium – what we know as 150 people evacuated

- Around 150 guests and staff were evacuated from a 13-storey Holiday Inn on Empire Way in Wembley after a fire broke out on Friday afternoon.
- Six fire engines and around 40 firefighters attended the scene, with no injuries reported and the cause of the fire under investigation.
- Major events at nearby venues, including a concert by The Weeknd at Wembley Stadium, are not expected to be disrupted by the incident.
- The blaze occurred as London emergency services operate under severe pressure during the UK's fifth heatwave of the year.
- Emergency services across the country are facing unprecedented demand, with extreme heat causing major wildfires elsewhere including a 40-acre blaze in Stourbridge.
