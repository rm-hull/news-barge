A drilling company is applying for a new new goods vehicle operator’s licence in the North East, according to a public notice.

United Drilling Limited based at 16 North Close, Elwick, Hartlepool, wants to use Dovecote Farm as an operating centre for one goods vehicle.

No trailers are included in the application.

Nearby landowners and occupiers who feel their enjoyment of their property could be impacted have the right to voice their objections.

These must be made in writing to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE.

The reasons for the objection should be clearly stated.

A 21-day period from the date of the notice, August 13, has been given for representations to be made.

At the same time, a copy of the representation should also be sent to United Drilling Limited at their North Close address.

For those unfamiliar with the process of making representations, a guide is available on the government website.
