Firefighters battled a suspected arson attack for around eight hours last night (August 13) at Eston Hills.

Emergency services were called at around 12.55pm, following reports of smoke and flames coming from the hills.

Fire crews from Billingham, Coulby Newham and Grangetown attended the incident to bring the flames under control.

The firefighters battled the fire for around eight hours before leaving at around 9.05pm.

Cleveland Fire Brigade believe the cause of the fire to be deliberate.
