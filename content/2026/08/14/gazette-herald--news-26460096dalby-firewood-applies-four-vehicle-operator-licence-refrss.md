Dalby Firewood Limited is seeking a licence to operate four goods vehicles from Middle House Farm, Low Marishes.

The applicant is inviting written representations from owners or occupiers of land and buildings that may be affected.

The application is open to feedback from August 12 to September 2.

Submissions should be sent to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE, within 21 days.

A copy should also be sent to the applicant at Middle House Farm, Low Marishes, Malton, YO17 6RH.

A guide to making representations is available at www.gov.uk/object-hgv-operator-licence.
