# Florida woman arrested for murder wearing ‘force to be reckoned with’ shirt

- Florida murder suspect Isabelle Rose Johnson was arrested following a 45-day multi-state manhunt across Florida, Georgia, and Alabama.
- Johnson was wanted for the murder of 43-year-old Jason Coulthart, whose body was found in mid-June in a concrete-covered shallow grave with a ziptie around his neck.
- Authorities captured the 38-year-old without incident near Cottondale, Florida, while she was wearing a T-shirt that read force to be reckoned with.
- Five other individuals have been arrested for accessory to murder after the fact for allegedly helping conceal Johnson during the search.
- Court records indicate a witness told investigators Johnson targeted Coulthart because he was irritating her and threatening to interfere with her child custody.
