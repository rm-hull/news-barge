Police are investigating after a video showed a driver doing ‘donuts’ in a coastal car park.

It happened on the evening of Friday 7 August at Filey Country Car Park, and involved a red Audi driving “in an anti-social manner”.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We understand that footage has emerged on Facebook and has attracted attention online.

“We’re particularly appealing for any witnesses to the incident, who could provide valuable information.”

The video was posted on the Filey Moan Facebook page with the message: “Went for a lovely sunset walk with the kids last night and had to watch this idiot ripping field up on Filey Brigg.”
