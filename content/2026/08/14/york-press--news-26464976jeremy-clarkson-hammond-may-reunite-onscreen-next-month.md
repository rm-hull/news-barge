Jeremy Clarkson, Richard Hammond and James May will reunite onscreen next month.

According to The Sun, the hosts will join YouTubers Thomas Holland and James Engelsman as well as social media star Francis Bourgeois in series seven.

Having presented Top Gear together for over a decade, the famed trio departed the BBC show back in 2015, moving on to Prime Video's The Grand Tour.

The petrol enthusiasts are expected to help ensure the show’s reboot gets off to a smooth and welcoming start, according to co-creator and TV producer Andy Wilman.

Mr Wilman, who is also executive producer of Clarkson's Farm, told The Sun that the guys will do cameos as they "pass that baton on" in the series which debuts on Friday, September 4.

The TV producer told 3Bs radio at the Buckingham Literary Festival: "We’ve got three guys coming soon with a new Grand Tour and we’ve got behind them.

"We’re really supporting them. Our guys are in the shows doing cameos, so we’re wishing them well because we do want to pass that baton on."

This will be the first time in almost two years the Top Gear originals, who went their own ways professionally, will be seen together since September 2024.

That was when The Grand Tour: One For The Road was released on Prime Video, marking an end to over two decades of Mr Clarkson, Mr May and Mr Hammond working together.
