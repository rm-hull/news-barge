A NORTH Yorkshire car dealer who defrauded customers out of large sums of money has been jailed following a long-running police investigation.

Andrew Mearns, 57, of Colwyn Place in Llandudno, was sentenced to two and a half years in prison at Sheffield Crown Court today (August 14, 2026) after pleading guilty to 14 counts of fraud.

The fraud took place while Mearns was a director at Gmund Cars Limited in Knaresborough - a high-value car dealership which specialised in the sale and modification of luxury vehicles, particularly Porsches.

  


Between December 2006 and July 2019, Mearns agreed to sell vehicles on behalf of clients on a commission, however he sold the cars and kept the proceeds for himself, leaving clients out of pocket.

Mearns' crimes came to light after 14 separate reports were made between October 2017 and July 2019 - with police investigating 'extensively' for several years.

Police Staff Investigator Corrina Graham-Merrett from North Yorkshire Police said: “Mearns lied to his customers time and time again. Thankfully, his offending was brought to a stop, and we have worked tirelessly to secure evidence against him over many years.

“The financial and emotional impact on his victims has been nothing short of devastating, and will no doubt continue for them whilst civil cases are pursued to have vehicles repatriated. It is right that he now faces the consequences for his actions.

“This case delivers a firm warning: fraud will be met with zero tolerance in our communities. We will pursue those who exploit honest trust by ensuring that we hold them to account.”

Anyone who believes they have been a victim of fraud is encouraged to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101 or through the force’s website or by contacting Action Fraud at actionfraud.police.uk or by calling 0300 123 2040.
