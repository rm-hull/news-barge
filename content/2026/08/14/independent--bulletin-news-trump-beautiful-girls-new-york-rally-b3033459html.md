- Trump took credit for declining crime rates in the capital, attributing the drop to his administration's deployment of the National Guard.
- The event was attended by officials including Attorney General Todd Blanche and FBI Director Kash Patel, as well as actor Kelsey Grammer.
- FBI Director Kash Patel praised Trump's leadership at the rally, crediting him for crime reduction trends that began under the previous administration.
