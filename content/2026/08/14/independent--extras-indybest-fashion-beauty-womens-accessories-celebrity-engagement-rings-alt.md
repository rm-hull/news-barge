From oversized diamonds to vintage-inspired settings, celebrity engagement rings have long been a source of inspiration for jewellery lovers. Whether a classic solitaire or vintage-inspired cut, they often set the tone for the jewellery trends that follow. While many celebrity rings are custom-made and come with eye-watering price tags, you can still recreate the look with similar styles, including lab grown diamonds, that feel just as special.

The Diamond Store has a wide selection of engagement rings inspired by some of the biggest jewellery trends, from timeless diamond solitaires to more distinctive cuts and settings. I’ve rounded up some of the celebrity engagement rings everyone is talking about, along with similar styles to shop if you love the look.

Zendaya and Tom Holland’s romance has captured the attention of fans for years, and recent rumours that the couple may have tied the knot have only added to the excitement. Zendaya’s engagement ring sparked plenty of conversation thanks to its elegant design. An east-west ring, this style places the diamond horizontally across the finger, putting a twist on a classic solitaire.

If you’re searching for a similar look, this east west elongated cushion cut lab diamond solitaire from The Diamond Store has a modern touch. It features a 3 carat elongated cushion-cut lab-grown diamond set horizontally across the band. Made from recycled 18 carat yellow gold with a white gold basket, it combines a contemporary setting with a classic diamond shape.

Dua Lipa’s engagement ring highlights the growing popularity of chunkier, statement-making designs. The singer’s bold cigar band style proves that engagement rings can come in all shapes and sizes. After tying the knot in Italy, her distinctive ring remains a talking point for it’s unique style.

For a chunkier take on the trend, this 18 carat yellow gold ring combines a bold silhouette with a classic oval diamond. It has a 1 carat lab-grown oval-cut diamond and a fluted gold band.

With her recent New York city wedding still firmly under wraps, one thing fans can still continue to gush over is the star’s engagement ring. A vintage piece of jewellery, designed by her now husband Travis Kelce, the ring features an antique-style feel that sets it apart from more traditional diamond designs

There are plenty of vintage designs to choose from at The Diamond Store, but this 18 carat yellow gold diamond ring offers a timeless alternative to Taylor Swift’s vintage-inspired style. A marquise cut is one of the most eye-catching diamond shapes, and Selena Gomez’s ring put it firmly in the spotlight when she announced her engagement to music producer Benny Blanco in 2024. The elongated silhouette creates the illusion of a larger stone, while offering an alternative to more traditional round cuts.

Featuring a 3 carat marquise-cut diamond, this platinum ring from captures the elegant, elongated silhouette that has made the shape increasingly popular. A hidden halo and pavé diamond band add extra detail, giving the classic cut a more contemporary finish.

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/05/13/08/Sapphire-2-3ct-And-Lab-Diamond-1ct-Cluster-Ring-in-18K-Gold.png)

The Princess of Wales’s engagement ring is one of the most recognisable royal jewellery pieces, featuring a deep blue sapphire surrounded by a halo of diamonds. Originally worn by Princess Diana, this ring has helped keep coloured gemstone engagement rings firmly in the spotlight.

If this is a ring style you’ve been eyeing up, this sapphire and lab diamond cluster ring is a similar design. The 18 carat yellow gold ring features an oval-cut blue sapphire centre stone surrounded by 1 carat of lab-grown diamonds for added sparkle. Set with a claw setting, the design combines a classic silhouette with a diamond halo for a statement finish.
