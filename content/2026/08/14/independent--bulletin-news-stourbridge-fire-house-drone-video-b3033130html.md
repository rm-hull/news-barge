# Drone footage shows Stourbridge houses gutted by fire

- A drone video shows the damage after more than 100 firefighters tackled a major blaze near Stourbridge Golf Course on Thursday 13 August.
- The fire is believed to have started in nearby grassland before spreading and setting six homes alight.
- West Midlands Fire Service described the extensive devastation as one of the most significant incidents it has ever dealt with.
- Three firefighters and a child were taken to hospital for treatment following the fire.
- A further six members of the public were treated for smoke inhalation.
