A member of staff at a North Yorkshire fast food outlet has been assaulted and verbally abused, police said.

Officers are appealing for information about the incident at the Subway sandwich shop in Westborough, Scarborough, at about 2.10pm on Wednesday (August 12).

North Yorkshire Police said it involved a man and woman who had been together but entered the premises separately when the incident happened.

The force is urging anyone with information about the incident to come forward.

“We’re particularly appealing for any eyewitnesses to come forward and speak with the police,” a police spokesperson said.

Anyone with information should email [joel.varey@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joel.varey@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101 quoting reference 12260156252.
