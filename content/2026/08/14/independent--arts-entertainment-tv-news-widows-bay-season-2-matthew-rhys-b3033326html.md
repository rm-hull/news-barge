The stars of Apple TV+’s newest hit series, *Widow’s Bay*, have revealed their hopes for the show’s highly anticipated second season.

The word-of-mouth comedy thriller, which has been nominated for 19 Emmys this year, stars Matthew Rhys as the mayor of a fictional New England seaport town, who seeks to boost tourism despite knowing the island may be cursed.

Throughout the show’s debut season, Rhys’s Tom Loftis experiences supernatural occurrences, suggesting that the town is indeed haunted. Still, he tries to keep the eeriness at bay in an attempt to market the cash-strapped town as the next Martha’s Vineyard. By the end of the season, however, Loftis acknowledges that there’s something truly wrong with the island.

While the show was renewed in June, the creator-showrunner Katie Dippold remains tight-lipped about her plans for the next installment.

But the show’s stars, including Rhys, Dale Dickey and Katie O’Flynn, have freely divulged their preferred theories for *Widow’s Bay*’s future. 

![‘Widow’s Bay’ earned 19 Emmy nominations](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/14/17/21/Widows-Bay.jpeg)

![Matthew Rhys achieved a rare feat, earning Lead Acting Emmys for both ‘Widow’s Bay’ and ‘The Beast in Me’](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/14/17/17/Widows-Bay.jpeg)

“It’s the ultimate setup, I think, for a Season 2,” Rhys told *Deadline*. “Massive cliffhanger. I’m just talking personally now. A father who now knows the folklore is true. Therefore, if he suddenly leaves the island, his son will die. He realizes he has the Scooby-Doo mission. It’s like, OK, how do we figure this out? What breaks the curse?”

Dale Dickey, who plays Loftis’s inept receptionist Rosemary, recalled a recent conversation with co-star Jeff Hiller, who portrays Dale, the town hall clerk.

“I was talking to Jeff Hiller,” she said. “I would love to do a boat scene where Rosemary and Dale are out searching for the sea hag to get some answers. I thought that would be fun. And then we tip over and there’s only one life jacket. Who’s going to live?”

British actor Kate O’Flynn, who stars as Loftis’s trusty executive assistant Patricia, added: “Next season, I don’t know, but I would love to play a British ancestor of Patricia or something… I like the idea of wigging up and doing a bit of period drama. Why not?”

![Jeff Hiller and Kate O'Flynn star in 'Widow's Bay' as part of the municipal team](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/14/18/02/Widows-Bay.jpeg)

*Widow’s Bay* trails behind HBO Max’s smash-hit comedy*Hacks* in the comedy categories for the 2026 Emmys. 

The former collected 19 nominations, including Outstanding Comedy Series and acting nods for Rhys, O’Flynn, Dickey, Stephen Root, Betty Gilpin and Hamish Linklater. The latter landed 24 nominations.

Rhys achieved a rare feat, earning two Lead Actor Emmy nominations for *Widow’s Bay* and for Netflix’s limited drama series*The Beast in Me.* 

The Welsh star recently admitted that money is the driving force behind his role selection. “Like what pays most wins. Very simple. Three children and a mortgage, I’ll get after it. I have no shame. I’ll whore myself to the best of them,” he humorously told *Variety*. 

“In fairness, *Widow’s Bay* is a script I’d never seen the like of,” Rhys added in a more serious tone. “I was desperate to do that.”

*Widow’s Bay* season one is available to stream on Apple TV+.
