A York beauty studio is in the running for two national awards after being shortlisted at the UK Hair & Beauty Awards.

Libs Beauty Studio, located in Boroughbridge Road, has been named a finalist in the Best New Salon and Best Beauty Salon categories for the 2026 awards.

Libby Reynolds-Harding, owner of Libs Beauty Studio, said: "To be named a finalist in not just one, but two national award categories is something I never imagined when I opened Libs Beauty Studio.

"It’s an incredible honour and a proud moment for both myself and everyone who has supported the salon."

The winners will be announced at a ceremony at Manchester’s AO Arena on August 15, which celebrates excellence across the UK beauty industry.

The salon, which offers a range of treatments including nails, lashes, brows, and beauty therapy, has gained praise for its high standards and welcoming atmosphere.
