# RFK Jr reveals Trump’s plane is filled with boxes of his favorite fast food

- Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. revealed on a podcast that President Donald Trump routinely fills his airplane with boxes of McDonald's fast food.
- Kennedy noted that Trump's diet seems at odds with the administration's “Make America Healthy Again” agenda targeting ultra-processed foods.
- Despite leading the food policy reform efforts at HHS, Kennedy stated he is “not a scold” and does not lecture Trump on his eating habits.
- Kennedy previously criticized the diet offered on Trump's aircraft, calling the selection of fast food “poison” and “kind of inedible.”
- The Department of Health and Human Services is pursuing new federal regulations to target ultra-processed foods and address chronic health issues.
