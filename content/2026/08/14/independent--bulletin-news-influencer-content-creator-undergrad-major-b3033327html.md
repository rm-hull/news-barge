# Universities are betting big on content creator majors but critics have concerns

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/04/09/8/46/university-campus-stock.jpeg)

- Arizona State University has launched a new bachelor's degree in content creation through its Walter Cronkite School of Journalism and Mass Communication.
- Multiple universities across the United States are introducing content creation majors and minors to remain competitive as overall college enrollment shrinks.
- Industry analysts project U.S. social media creator earnings will top $20 billion this year, though competition remains intense and individual earnings vary widely.
- Supporters and content creators note that these programs teach transferable skills in marketing, media production, and brand negotiation.
- Critics and experts question the financial return on investment for such specialized degrees given high college tuition costs.
