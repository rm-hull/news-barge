# Eight dead and 230,000 evacuated as flooding hits Japan

![Local residents walk in a flooded area after heavy rain in Oamishirasato, Chiba Prefecture, Japan](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/14/11/2026-08-14T042645Z_459577053_RC23YMAFGHM3_RTRMADP_3_ASIA-WEATHER-JAPAN.JPG)

- At least eight people have died following record rainfall and severe flooding in Chiba prefecture, east of Tokyo.
- Parts of Chiba recorded over 360mm of rain in 24 hours, prompting the Japan Meteorological Agency to issue its highest heavy rainfall warning.
- Transport networks were severely disrupted, leaving around 7,000 travellers stranded overnight at Narita international airport before services began resuming on Friday.
- Nearly 230,000 residents were advised to evacuate as floodwaters inundated more than 80 homes and caused widespread power outages across the region.
- Japanese military personnel were deployed to assist with disaster response operations following what officials described as an unprecedented weather event.
