A CAR has been damaged in a supermarket car park in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a car was damaged in a supermarket car park in Harrogate.

The incident happened between 8.30am and 7pm last Wednesday (August 5) in the Sainsbury’s car park on Railway Road.

"A silver estate car was scratched along the near side and both wing mirrors were bent and damaged," said a spokesperson.
