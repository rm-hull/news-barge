Born by the sea, Tianjin in northern China offers a wide array of summer experiences. This time, we set sail at dawn to catch the first light of day. Step aboard the Kiev aircraft carrier and relive the glory days of this steel giant. Venture into the National Maritime Museum of China for an immersive exploration of the ocean’s hidden wonders. At Dongjiang Bay Beach, ride the waves on a jet ski and embrace the thrill of summer. And no visit to Tianjin is complete without sampling its diverse local delicacies.
