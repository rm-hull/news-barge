# Ted Lasso season four premiere makes Apple TV history

- The first episode of the fourth season of *Ted Lasso* has become the most-watched premiere in the history of Apple TV.
- As reported by *Deadline* , Nielsen data reveals the episode recorded 296.6 million viewing minutes in the United States on 4 and 5 August.
- The new storyline follows Jason Sudeikis's character as he returns to coach a women's football team.
- Although a major hit with viewers, the show has divided critics, with *The Independent* awarding it a two-star review.
- Sudeikis is joined by returning cast members, including Hannah Waddingham and Brett Goldstein, alongside several new additions.
