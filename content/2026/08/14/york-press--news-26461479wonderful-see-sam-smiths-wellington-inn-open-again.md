**'Wonderful to see Sam Smith's Wellington Inn open'**

IT’S wonderful to see the Wellington Inn thriving again.

The Welly has always held a special place in my heart after 15 years of cricket there. Playing at the Retreat was idyllic – welcoming teams from all over and sharing great moments with great characters.

Every home match, and plenty of away ones, ended back at the Welly, where the camaraderie carried on long into the evening.

They were truly halcyon days.

Wishing the new tenants every success.

Here’s hoping the old habit of pushing out hard-working managers is gone for good, and that everyone running the pub is treated fairly.

  *John Jones,*

  *Russet House,*

  *Birch Close,*

  *York*
