The best blenders are versatile kitchen appliances, whether you’re making smoothies and soups, blending nut butter or tackling everyday chopping and food prep. If counter space is limited, they’re a useful all-rounder, helping you cover a whole range of tasks without needing a cupboard full of different gadgets.

The term “blender” can cover quite a few different types of kitchen appliance, each suited to slightly different jobs. You’ll find hand blenders that are ideal for blitzing soup directly in the pan or in a jug. Then there are jug blenders, as well as food processors, which tend to offer even more options for chopping, slicing and preparing food.

When choosing the right blender for you, start with space. What will fit in your kitchen? Look at the real estate space a blender will take up – remember to take into account all those attachments. Many blenders are designed to slot into cupboards, and some offer lots of different attachments.

Think about what you’ll use your blender for most. If you regularly tackle lots of chopping and grating, a food processor-style model with a powerful motor and multiple attachments may be the best choice. For soups, a hand blender lets you blitz straight in the pan and often comes with useful extras such as a chopper and whisk. Jug blenders, meanwhile, are ideal for smoothies, soups, nut butter and curry pastes, although they can be bulky to store.
