A man wanted in connection with assaults and controlling and coercive behaviour is 'likely to be' in the County Durham area, police have said.

Sam Walsh, 49, from Colburn, in North Yorkshire, is wanted by police officers in connection with the serious offences.

North Yorkshire Police believe Walsh is likely to be in Easington Colliery in County Durham.
