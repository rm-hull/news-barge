Military vehicles ranging from historic cars and trucks to armoured machines are set to roll into North Yorkshire as a popular show returns to a major aviation attraction.

The Military Vehicle Show will take place on Sunday, August 30, at the Yorkshire Air Museum in Elvington, near York.

It will feature privately owned vehicles on display across the museum grounds, along with re-enactors, militaria stalls, and displays.

Jerry Ibbotson, spokesman for the Yorkshire Air Museum, said: "The Military Vehicle Show perfectly complements our collection of aircraft and has always been hugely popular with visitors.

"They will be able to experience some incredible military hardware up-close.

"It’s a great way to see August out with a bang."

The event will showcase a diverse mix of vehicles, from historic military cars and trucks to fully armoured machines.

Entry is included with standard museum admission and no special tickets are required.

The Yorkshire Air Museum will be open from 10am, giving visitors a full day to explore both the vehicle displays and the museum’s extensive collection of aircraft and exhibits.
