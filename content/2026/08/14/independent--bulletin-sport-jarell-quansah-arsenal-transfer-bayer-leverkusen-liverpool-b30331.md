# Arsenal ‘in talks’ to sign former Liverpool defender

![Quansah has been linked with a move to Arsenal (Nick Potts/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/10/02/e426d03a90db048218e5386ba8378046Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzgzNzI4NDAw-2.85200629.jpg)

- Arsenal are reportedly in talks to sign former Liverpool defender Jarell Quansah from Bayer Leverkusen.
- Quansah, 23, left Anfield last summer to move to Germany and performed well as he earned a spot in Thomas Tuchel’s England squad for the World Cup.
- Quansah endured a difficult tournament after being asked to play at right-back, and was sent off in England’s last-16 win over Mexico.
- However, Mikel Arteta is understood to be in favour of the move as Quansah can offer cover at centre-back and right-back.
- The Gunners are aiming to bolster their backline ahead of the new Premier League season amid injury concerns over William Saliba and Jurrien Timber.
