AN urgent search has launched to find a missing woman from North Yorkshire.

Tracey Smith, 57, was last seen on Friday (July 24).

North Yorkshire Police say it now believes there is a possibility that Tracey may have travelled to the London area and has extended its search.

  


She is described as 5ft 5in tall, of slim build with brown shoulder length hair and has a small mole above her lip.

It is not known what Tracey, from the Stokesley area, was wearing when she was last seen.

She may be travelling in her car, a black Fiat Panda, with the registration number WR10 ETK.

A police spokesperson said: “We are appealing to anyone who may have seen Tracey or her vehicle to get in touch as a matter of urgency.

“If you have seen Tracey or her car, or know where she is now, please call us on 999.

“If you have any other information that could help us find her, please call us on 101.

“Please quote reference 12260154127 when passing on information.”
