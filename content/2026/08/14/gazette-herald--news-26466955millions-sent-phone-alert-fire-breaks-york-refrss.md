Millions of people across York and North Yorkshire have been sent a wildfire emergency phone alert warning against 'any activity that could start a fire'.

The government sent that alert to people across the country this evening (Friday, August 14), warning against setting light to anything that could spark a wildfire, including barbecues, firepits, and fireworks.

It comes after a fire broke out on the outskirts of York that spread for 1km.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said its crews from Acomb, Malton, and York crews were called to Kirk Balk Lane, Claxton at 2.15pm today (August 14) after a wildfire was seen.

“On arrival was found to be a wildfire measuring approximately 1000mx500m. Crews used two hose reel jets to extinguish fire,” said a spokesperson for the service.

The incident was left in the hands of the farmer.
