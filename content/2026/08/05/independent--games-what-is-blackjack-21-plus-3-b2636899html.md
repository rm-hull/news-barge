**How does the 21 + 3 side bet work?**

So what does 21 + 3 mean in blackjack?

The 21 + 3 side bet is based on the first two cards dealt to the player and the dealer’s upcard. Together, these three cards form the basis of the side bet.

Players bet on whether or not these three cards form a poker-style hand, such as a flush, straight, three-of-a-kind, or straight flush.

The outcome of the side bet differs from your main blackjack hand and can win even if the player loses their primary blackjack hand.

**Winning Hands in 21 + 3**

Below, we have provided a list of the possible winning combinations in the 21 + 3 side bet:

**Flush:** All three cards are of the same suit.

**Straight:** All three cards are in consecutive rank, regardless of suit.

**Three of a Kind:** All three cards are of the same rank.

**Straight Flush:** All three cards are both consecutive in rank and of the same suit.
