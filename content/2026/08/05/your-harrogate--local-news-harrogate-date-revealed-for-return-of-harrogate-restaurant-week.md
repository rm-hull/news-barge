                                    Harrogate Restaurant Week is set to return next month, bringing a variety of deals and offers to local diners.

Organised by Harrogate BID, the next Restaurant Week will take place from Monday 14th to Sunday 20th September.

The popular food and drink event will once again see restaurants, cafés, pubs and bars across the Harrogate area offer special set-price menus, giving diners the chance to enjoy meals at discounted prices while supporting local hospitality businesses.

As in previous editions of the event, participating venues will be able to offer deals at a range of price points, with menus expected to be available for £10, £15, £20, £25 and £30.

The offers are designed to encourage people to try somewhere new or revisit one of their favourite eateries.

Jason Maxwell, CEO of Harrogate BID, said:

"We're delighted to be bringing Harrogate Restaurant Week back for the second time this year.

"Harrogate is home to an incredible range of restaurants, and Restaurant Week is a fantastic opportunity for residents and visitors to discover somewhere new or revisit an old favourite and save some money on their meal.

"By encouraging more people to dine out locally, we're supporting our hospitality sector and helping to keep spending within the local economy. We hope people will get behind the campaign and help make September's Restaurant Week another great success."
