                                    Victoria Shopping Centre in Harrogate is inviting families to enjoy a series of free summer activities throughout August.

Following last weekend's dinosaur storytelling event, three more themed sessions are set to take place on the shopping centre's piazza between 11am and 2pm on Saturdays.

This Saturday (8th August), visitors can take part in a "Saturday of Sport", with children invited to get active through games including tig and ball games. There will also be opportunities to win prizes during the session.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a72e0d82d90c624fd2.jpg)


On Saturday 15th August, families can enjoy a "Saturday of Science" with a free make-and-take galaxy slime workshop. Children will be able to choose the colour of their slime, add glitter and take their creations home.

The summer programme concludes on Saturday 22nd August with a "Saturday of Song", featuring live performances from local musicians on the piazza.

The live music session aims to provide a relaxed atmosphere for visitors as they enjoy chilled acoustic performances during the afternoon.

*All of the Family Fun Fiesta events are free to attend, with no booking required.*
