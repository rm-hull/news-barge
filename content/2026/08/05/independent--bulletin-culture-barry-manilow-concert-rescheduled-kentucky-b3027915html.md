Pop icon Barry Manilow cancelled his concert in Lexington, Kentucky, on Tuesday night just hours before he was due to perform, citing "unforeseen circumstances".

It marks the second time the “Copacabana” singer has had to postpone the Lexington show, having previously moved the date due to treatment for lung cancer.

Manilow revealed his diagnosis in December last year, crediting his doctor with detecting it early during an MRI scan.

Despite preparing for his arena tour dates, his surgeon previously advised against him resuming the postponed concerts.

His next scheduled concert is tonight (August 5) in Cincinnati, Ohio, though it remains unclear whether the concert will take place.
