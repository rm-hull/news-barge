A boy had to go to hospital after being attacked in a York street.

It happened on **Sunday**(2 August) at about** midday**on** Moss Street**, close to its junction with** Caesar Court**.

The boy, aged 13, was “strangled, pushed and kicked” during the assault.

#### Incident location

He was taken to hospital for treatment for injuries to his throat and neck.

The attacker is described as:

- a man with tanned skin
- aged in his 40s
- about 5ft 10in tall
- of average build
- with an outgrow buzz-cut hairstyle
- and dark stubble.

He was wearing a red top and dark shorts at the time.

Anyone with information about what happened is asked to contact police.

A man has been arrested in connection with the incident and been released on bail.
