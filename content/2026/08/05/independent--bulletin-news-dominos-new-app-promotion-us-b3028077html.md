Domino's has launched a new promotion offering customers a $5 discount on orders placed via its redesigned app and website.

The initiative, which commenced on Tuesday and runs until 30 August, aims to boost engagement with the updated digital platforms.

This move comes amidst slow growth for Domino's, with US retail sales increasing by 1.9 per cent, half of the previous year's growth.

The broader pizza industry is facing declining popularity, largely due to increased competition from diverse fast-food options made accessible by delivery services.

Competitors such as Pizza Hut and Papa John's have also encountered significant challenges, leading to sales or potential sales of their businesses.
