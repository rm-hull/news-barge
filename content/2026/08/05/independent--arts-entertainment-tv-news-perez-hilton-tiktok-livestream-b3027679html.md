Celebrity blogger Perez Hilton has been rushed to hospital after his TikTok followers reported a concerning livestream.

Worried viewers reportedly saw Hilton, 48, self-harm in a video streamed on the social media app on Tuesday (2 August), following which emergency services swiftly arrived at his house in Miami.

He was “safely recovered” and transported by Miami-Dade Fire Rescue to a local hospital, where he is receiving medical attention.

![Perez Hilton has been rushed to hospital after concerning TikTok followers](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/05/7/10/Screenshot-2026-08-05-at-08-12-32.png)

Without naming Hilton, the Miami-Dade sheriff’s office released a statement saying they received reports of a man “livestreaming acts of self-harm on social media”, and confirmed he was at home alone.

Addressing their handling of the situation, they said they had “tactically disengaged”, adding: “In many incidents involving a person experiencing a mental health crisis or actively harming themselves, deputies prioritise de-escalation by creating time, distance, and opportunities for communication.

“Unless there is an immediate threat to others, slowing the situation and utilising crisis intervention techniques can reduce the likelihood of a suicide-by-cop encounter and minimise the risk of injury to the individual, deputies and the public.”

The former *Celebrity Big Brother* contestant’s managers released a joint statement to*TMZ*, confirming they were “aware of the concerning content circulating online”.

Dante Rusciolelli and Rebekah Kochan said they had “not been able to make direct contact with him, despite our ongoing efforts to reach him”, and that their “primary concern” was Hilton’s health and well-being, “as well as the well-being of his family”.

![In 2020, Perez Hilton expressed regret for the nature of his 2000s blog posts](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/05/7/16/Screenshot-2026-08-05-at-08-18-12.png)

They continued: “Until we have confirmed information, we will not speculate or comment further. We appreciate everyone’s concern and ask that his privacy be respected during this time.”

*The Independent* has contacted Hilton’s reps for additional comment.

Hilton – real name Mario Armando Lavandeira Jr – is known for his eponymous gossip blog, which, in the 2000s, caused controversy for its no-holds-barred posts as well as its practice of outing celebrities who were keeping their sexuality private.

He himself expressed regret for his actions in the book *TMI: My Life in Scandal*, which was published in 2020. “I regret that I thought it was OK to out celebrities,” Hilton wrote. “That is something I no longer believe.”

![Perez Hilton was a contestant on 'Celebrity Big Brother' in 2015](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/05/7/51/Screenshot-2026-08-05-at-08-15-31.png)

Hilton has three children, born via surrogacy. He appeared on *Celebrity Big Brother* in 2015 and became the seventh housemate to be evicted, two days before the final.
