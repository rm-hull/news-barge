Two people have been left injured after being bitten by an “out-of-control” dog in North Yorkshire, police said.

Officers used a taser and the dog was safely contained after the incident in East Ayton, near Scarborough, at 3pm on Tuesday (August 4).

North Yorkshire Police said two people sustained minor injuries from the dog during the incident.

The dog was taken to secure kennels and is “fit and well with no injuries”, the force.

A police spokesperson said there is “not believed to be any ongoing risk to the wider public at this time”.

The dog’s owner is helping with the police investigation, they added.

Police are asking anyone who witnessed the incident or with information about it to phone 101 quoting reference 12260150174.
