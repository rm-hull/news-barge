## Top sporting commentator set to host night at popular York restaurant

The Ivy York *(Image: JAK SPEDDING)*

RACING fans are invited to an evening of dining and conversation at one of York's top restaurants.

The Ivy St Helen's Square will host a special event with ITV Racing presenter Adele Mulrennan on Wednesday, August 12, as part of The Ivy Collection's Summer of Sport.

Held in the restaurant’s Etty Room, the evening will feature a three-course meal and a live Q&A with Adele, who will share racecourse stories and insights into her broadcasting career.

A spokesman for The Ivy said: "This August, The Ivy St Helen's Square, York is inviting racing fans to an exclusive evening with ITV Racing presenter Adele Mulrennan, as part of The Ivy Collection's Summer of Sport.

  Adele Mulrennan 

*(Image: Provided)*

"Speaking with Former Editor-in-Chief of OK! Magazine, Lisa Byrne, Adele will share behind-the-scenes stories from the racecourse, insights into her career at the heart of the action, and her thoughts ahead of this year's highly anticipated Ebor Festival."

The event comes just ahead of the festival - which takes place from August 19 to August 22 and is one of the highlights of the city’s racing calendar.

Tickets are priced at £60 per person and include dinner and a welcome drink.
