A shopping trolley has been used to damage the front window of a York charity shop.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing to find man who witnessed the incident outside Age UK in Front Street, Acomb, on Tuesday, July 28, at about 5.15pm.

The force said a man saw the incident and approached shop staff giving only the name of “Max”.

“We are appealing for 'Max' to make contact with us, in order to help us with the investigation,” a force spokesperson said.

“Of course, if anyone can also help us, please make contact.”

They urged anyone with information to phone 101 contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 quote reference 12260145289.
