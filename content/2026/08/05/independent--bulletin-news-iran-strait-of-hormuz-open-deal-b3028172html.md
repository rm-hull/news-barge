# Iran and Oman reach an understanding to reopen Strait of Hormuz

- Iran and Oman have reached an understanding on the geographic coordinates for a shipping route through the Strait of Hormuz, with a joint announcement pending if certain third parties do not interfere.
- The proposed deal would grant Tehran control over ships entering the Gulf via the Strait of Hormuz, representing a significant concession to Iran.
- Donald Trump stated that the Strait of Hormuz would be "open very soon" following "very good discussions" with Iran, but also threatened severe repercussions if a deal was not reached.
- Iran indicated that an agreement with Oman could be delayed as long as the United States continues to threaten Iran, casting doubt on immediate progress.
- The Strait of Hormuz is a critical waterway for global oil shipments, and its closure would significantly impact global energy prices and inflation.
