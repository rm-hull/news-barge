Kansas Mom Recovering After Suffering Multiple Spider Bites That Nearly Cost Her Life

Kansas mother Britagne Miller developed severe health complications following a suspected spider bite she sustained at Cheney Lake on May 28.

Days after the bite, a painful, swollen lesion on her calf led to a hospital visit where doctors discovered a severe blood clotting issue requiring emergency surgery.

Post-surgery, Miller's condition worsened with staph and bacterial infections, and she was diagnosed with end-stage liver failure, with doctors initially giving her weeks to live.

Despite the critical prognosis, Miller fought through her complications and was discharged from the hospital on July 11 to continue her recovery at home.

Miller's next health goal is to qualify for a liver transplant, while her family faces financial struggles due to her husband losing his job, prompting a GoFundMe campaign.
