# Oscar winner praises Prince William’s ‘hands-on’ climate work

- Oscar-winning actor Cate Blanchett has praised the Prince of Wales for his "extraordinary" leadership in tackling the climate crisis.
- Blanchett, who collaborated with Prince William on the Earthshot Prize Council, noted he "inherited" his passion from King Charles and is very "hands-on" in his commitment.
- She also discussed her role as co-founder of RSL Media, a not-for-profit technology company that allows creatives to register their consent for AI companies to use their work.
- Blanchett highlighted the polarisation surrounding AI and the critical need for consent in its development and use.
- The actor also commented on the post-MeToo political climate, stating that while society is at an "exciting point," further progress is still required.
