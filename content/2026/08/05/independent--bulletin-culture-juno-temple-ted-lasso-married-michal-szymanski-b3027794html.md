# Juno Temple reveals she’s married boyfriend she met on Ted Lasso set

- Juno Temple, known for her role in Ted Lasso, has confirmed her marriage to Michal Szymanski.
- The couple met during the filming of the second season of the Apple TV+ series.
- Szymanski was working as a driver for Temple's co-star, Hannah Waddingham, at the time.
- Waddingham played a role in their initial connection, facilitating their first date.
- Temple publicly confirmed her relationship with Szymanski in 2022, attending various events together.
