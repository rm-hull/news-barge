# Whoopi Goldberg jokes paycheck is best thing about co-hosting The View

- Whoopi Goldberg revealed on Keke Palmer’s podcast that her favourite aspect of co-hosting ABC’s The View is “the check”.
- The EGOT winner, who has moderated the show since 2007, joked about the substantial salary she receives.
- Reports indicate Goldberg is the highest-paid panellist, earning an estimated $6 to $8 million annually.
- She expressed gratitude for the financial and career stability the role provides, highlighting its importance for supporting her family.
- Goldberg previously stated in November 2024 that she works to afford her living and provide for her daughter and grandchildren.
