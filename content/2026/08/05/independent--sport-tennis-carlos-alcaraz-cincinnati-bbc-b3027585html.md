Carlos Alcaraz’s comeback has been delayed after the Spaniard withdrew from the Cincinnati Open due to an “ongoing” wrist injury.

The seven-time grand slam champion has been sidelined since suffering the problem during the Barcelona Open, missing the defence of his French Open title and Wimbledon.

In the lead-up to the tournament, Alcaraz posted videos of himself making a tentative return to the court.

But on Tuesday, organisers announced on X that the 2025 champion would be missing the tournament: “Carlos Alcaraz has withdrawn from the Cincinnati Open.

“Wishing all the best to our 2025 champion in his recovery process. Can’t wait to welcome you next year!”

Tournament director Bob Moran said in a statement provided to the BBC the 23-year-old was suffering from an “ongoing wrist injury”.

“We know Carlos is doing everything he can to get back to playing tournaments as soon as possible,” he said.

“We wish him the best with his recovery and look forward to welcoming him back to Cincinnati in the future.”
