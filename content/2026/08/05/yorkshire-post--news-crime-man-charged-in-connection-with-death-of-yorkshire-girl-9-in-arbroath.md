# Man charged in connection with death of Yorkshire girl, 9, in Arbroath

## A 35-year-old man has been charged in connection with the death of a nine-year-old child in Arbroath.

The girl – from West Yorkshire – was found seriously injured just after midnight on Monday morning at the Elliot industrial estate in the Angus town and taken to Ninewells Hospital in Dundee, where she later died.

Police are treating the death as suspicious and the white British man is due to appear at Forfar Sheriff Court on Wednesday (Aug 5).

Officers say they are not looking for anyone else in connection with the girl’s death.

Detective inspector Mike Ness of Police Scotland’s major investigation team said: “Our thoughts remain with those affected by these events.

![Police at the Elliot Industrial Estate in Arbroath where a nine-year-old girl was found seriously injured and later died after being taken to Ninewells Hospital in Dundee.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/05/7/04/PA-85480608.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



“A police presence will remain in the area while our inquiries continue.

“Anyone with any concerns, or information, should approach these officers or contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 0008 of Monday August 3 2026.”

The girl is believed to have been camping in the area with her family, who are from West Yorkshire and police say are being supported by specialist officers.

It is understood West Yorkshire Police are supporting Police Scotland’s probe.
