Costco has introduced buttermilk biscuits to its bakery section, sold in 10-count packages for approximately $6.99.

The new biscuits have sparked debate among shoppers, with many criticizing them as dry and suggesting they are easily made at home.

Despite the criticism, some customers expressed enthusiasm for the new bakery item, particularly for making dishes like biscuits and gravy.

This addition follows other recent product changes at Costco, including the introduction of Australian Tim Tams in bulk.

Earlier this year, Costco also altered its popular Kirkland Signature bagel offering, now selling single bags of eight bagels for $4.99, addressing previous customer complaints about package size.
