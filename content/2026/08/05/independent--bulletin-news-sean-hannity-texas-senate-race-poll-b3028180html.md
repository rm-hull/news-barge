Sean Hannity says he 'doesn't believe' Fox News's own poll showing James Talerico beating Ken Paxton

Fox News host Sean Hannity dismissed a recent poll from his own network showing progressive Democrat James Talarico leading Republican Ken Paxton in Texas's Senate race.

During a debate with Bill de Blasio, Hannity expressed disbelief at the poll results, highlighting Talarico's progressive views on gender inclusivity, including his past statement that “God is nonbinary.”

The Fox News poll, conducted on July 30, indicated Talarico held a 3-point lead over Paxton among registered Texas voters.

De Blasio countered Hannity, suggesting voters are more concerned with economic issues affecting their families than Talarico's progressive ideology.

Further findings from the Fox News poll revealed 63 percent of moderate voters favored Talarico, and nearly 60 percent believed Paxton lacked the personal character suitable for a senator.
