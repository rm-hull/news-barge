# Marjorie Taylor Greene launches new side hustle after leaving Congress

- Marjorie Taylor Greene, a lawmaker who left Congress last year, has joined Cameo, a platform where individuals can pay for personalized videos from public figures.
- She charges $149 for personalized video messages and $5,000 for business-related content.
- Greene's decision to join Cameo mirrors that of her friend and former colleague George Santos, who earned over $174,000 on the platform after his expulsion from Congress.
- Other political figures, such as Matt Gaetz and Sarah Palin, have also utilized Cameo as a significant source of income.
- Greene has used her Cameo videos to make critical remarks about the current president, including joking that a dog would make a better president.
