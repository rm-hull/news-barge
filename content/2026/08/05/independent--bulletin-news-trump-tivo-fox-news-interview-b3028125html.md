Donald Trump expressed his fondness for the digital recording service TiVo during a recent interview with Fox News anchor Trace Gallagher in Los Angeles.

During the discussion, Trump veered from talking about tariffs to praising Fox News and stating he often “tapes” shows, adding, “I love TiVo or its equivalent”.

The anchor, Trace Gallagher, interjected to clarify that the modern equivalent is a “DVR”, as TiVo's physical hardware sales are set to end in 2025 after the service was discontinued last year.

Elsewhere in the interview, Trump claimed the US was “taking out hundreds of billions of dollars” from Venezuela following a military operation to extract its president, Nicolas Maduro.

He also asserted that Venezuela was “a very happy country”, despite a recent devastating earthquake that resulted in over 6,000 fatalities.
