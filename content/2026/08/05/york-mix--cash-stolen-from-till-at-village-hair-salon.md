Police have released this image of a man they would like to speak to in connection with a burglary in Cawood near Selby.

It happened on **Rythergate** on**Monday 3 August,** and involved cash being stolen from the till of a hairdressers.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured, as he have information that could assist our investigation.”
