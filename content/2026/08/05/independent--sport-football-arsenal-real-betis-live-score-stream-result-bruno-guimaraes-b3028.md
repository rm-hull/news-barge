Arsenal face Real Betis in their latest pre-season friendly as they continue preparations for a Premier League title defence on a day where they moved much closer to sealing the signing of Bruno Guimaraes.

The Gunners take on their Spanish opponents – who finished fifth in LaLiga last season and reached the quarter-finals of the Europa League – at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin, hoping to build on a 4-1 thrashing of Girona last time out, where Gabriel Jesus, Kai Havertz, Max Dowman and new signing Christos Tzolis all scored

Mikel Arteta’s side received a boost earlier today when Brazil international Guimaraes was given permission to travel to Arsenal for a medical ahead of a £75m move from Newcastle United, after both clubs finally agreed a fee. The 28-year-old midfielder looks set to officially join in the next few days.

Arsenal are also continuing their pursuit of Guimaraes’ international team-mate Vinicius Jr, although Real Madrid are said to have offered fresh terms to the Brazilian after his return to training on Tuesday, but Arteta’s immediate focus is getting minutes into his players’ legs ahead of the Community Shield against Manchester City in two weeks’ time, with Betis offering a fascinating test this evening.

## LAST TIME OUT: Arsenal 4-1 Girona

It was a solid day’s work for Arsenal at the weekend as they beat Girona 4-1 to kickstart their pre-season campaign in earnest.

Summer signing Christos Tzolis, bought from Club Brugge for £34m, was on target at the Estadi Municipal, as were Kai Havertz and Gabriel Jesus, but wonderkid Max Dowman was the star as he claimed the assist for the opening goal of the game before getting on the scoresheet with a fine effort himself.

Girona, who were relegated from LaLiga last season, did reduce the deficit early in the second half with Arnau Martinez’s thumping header, but that proved to be nothing more than a consolation.

## Arsenal team news - unchanged from Girona win

Here’s the Arsenal line-up this evening. Mikel Arteta is opting for an unchanged starting XI from the 4-1 win over Girona.

It means that new signing Christos Tzolis, who scored in that game, starts once more, while youngsters Ethan Nwaneri and Max Dowman are given a chance to shine in midfield.

Arteta took Piero Hincapie, Kai Havertz and Viktor Gyokeres off after 30 minutes against Girona as they build up fitness, so it will be interesting to see if they’re given a longer leash tonight.

Ben White also came through 60 minutes unscathed on his injury return, so will he go for a full 90 in Dublin?

There are some interesting names on the bench, most notably Brazil duo Gabriel and Gabriel Martinelli - in the squad for the first time this pre-season after returning from their post-World Cup break.

## Arsenal agree cut-price fee with Newcastle for Bruno Guimaraes

The biggest news of the day from an Arsenal perspective is that Bruno Guimaraes looks set to become a Gunner.

Guimaraes has received permission to travel to Arsenal and complete his £75m move from Newcastle United. The 28-year-old midfielder will undergo a medical at the Premier League champions within the coming days after both clubs agreed a fee.

Brazil international Guimaraes told Newcastle earlier this summer that he wanted to join Arsenal, and a deal has quickly been agreed this week after the Premier League champions entered formal talks with the Magpies.
