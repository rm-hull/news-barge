  *UPDATE: North Yorkshire Police said in a statement this morning (Wednesday, August 5) that a 20-year-old man has now been arrested.*

A 20-year-old man is wanted by police in connection with an alleged burglary in York.

North Yorkshire Police said the man is believed to still be in the city and is known to have links to the Clifton area.

  


Officers from the force are appealing for information to help locate him and said extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate him.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Anyone who sees him is advised to call 999 immediately.

"Other information can be provided online via the force’s website or by emailing Justyna Krupinska at [justyna.krupinska@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:justyna.krupinska@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

"Alternatively, please contact police by dialling 101."
