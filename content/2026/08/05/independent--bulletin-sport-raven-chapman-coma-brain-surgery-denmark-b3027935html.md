British boxer Raven Chapman underwent emergency surgery in Denmark after collapsing following a sparring session on Friday, July 24.

She was diagnosed with a brain haematoma and cerebral oedema, leading to a procedure to stop bleeding and stabilise swelling, after which she was placed in a medically induced coma.

Chapman is now off sedatives and is awaiting medically supervised repatriation to a UK hospital for what is expected to be a lengthy rehabilitation period.

Her management company, Vote Boxing, confirmed the incident and her current condition, expressing hope for her recovery.

A GoFundMe page has been established to support her recovery, and boxing promoter Frank Warren has publicly offered his support.
