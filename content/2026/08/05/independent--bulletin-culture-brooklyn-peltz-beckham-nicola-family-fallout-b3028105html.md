# Brooklyn Beckham celebrates anniversary with wife Nicola

- Brooklyn Peltz Beckham celebrated his anniversary with his wife, Nicola, calling her his “absolute princess” and “best friend” in an Instagram post.
- The couple, who married in April 2022 and renewed their vows in August 2025, exchanged heartfelt tributes on social media.
- Their anniversary comes amidst an ongoing estrangement from Brooklyn's family, Sir David and Lady Victoria Beckham.
- Brooklyn previously accused his parents of attempting to “sabotage” his relationship with Nicola and prioritising “Brand Beckham” over family.
- Victoria Beckham recently addressed the feud, saying she and David “love our children so much”, while the Beckhams' public attempts at reconciliation have been ignored by Brooklyn.
