Alan Brazil told he had 'a couple of weeks' to live before liver transplant

Alan Brazil returned to TalkSport on Wednesday for the first time since undergoing a life-saving operation.

The radio presenter had been absent from the airwaves for several weeks while recovering from his surgery.

Brazil praised the "amazing" staff for their care, adding: “It’s not everyone’s cup of tea, the NHS, but there are too many bodies down there that need looking after.”

Speaking to co-host Gabriel Agbonlahor, Brazil revealed that he nearly got into a fight with a patient in hospital.

“You get some real idiots in there, you know, patients... They think it's their own hospital. You know, ‘nurse, nurse, nurse’ ... I nearly had a fight. I was gonna knock someone out,” he said.
