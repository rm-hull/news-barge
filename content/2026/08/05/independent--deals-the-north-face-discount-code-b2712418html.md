**XPLR Pass members save 20 per cent**

The North Face has its own membership programme – the XPLR Pass. It pays to sign up, whether you’re a regular shopper with the brand or at just looking for a one-off purchase. Some of the exclusive discounts you can expect include:

- 10 per cent off your next order above £100
- 20 per cent off on your birthday every year (for orders more than £100)
- Free standard delivery on every order

Along with these savings perks, members can also enjoy exclusive access to events and opportunities to join one-of-a-kind experiences with The North Face athletes.

**Explore North Face Renewed**

If you enjoy a spot of sustainable shopping and saving at the same time, it’s worth taking a look at The North Face Renewed. Launched in 2018, the programme was established to help reduce the amount of clothing waste going to landfill.

It works like this: The North Face takes any damaged, returned or defective clothing and repairs them so they’re good as new. Then, the item is listed at up to 50 per cent off the original retail price.

Availability depends on the type of items it receives, so it’s not always guaranteed that it’ll have what you want or need. Saying that, we’ve always seen hundreds of items for sale so far, ranging from insulated parkers to performance leggings.

**Extra promo codes with the Pro Programme**

Outdoor professionals, fitness instructors and guides may be able to qualify for the Pro Programme, which offers exclusive discounted pricing on technical gear and apparel.

Applications are assessed on an individual basis and you must have qualifying documentation to be considered. If you are accepted, you’ll receive bespoke The North Face discount codes to your inbox that you can use at checkout.

**Get free delivery on your order**

Standard delivery starts from £5 for The North Face, but there are some simple ways to avoid this cost. The easiest way to waive the standard delivery fee is to become an XPLR member. ## Helpful information

**Returns**

If you’re unhappy with your purchase, or it's not quite what you were expecting, you can return your items free of charge within 30 days of delivery.

To start the process, you’ll need to log in to your account and go to your order history. From here, you can initiate the returns process.

**Repairs**

If you’ve got a favourite jacket or pair of trousers that need mending, you can send them to The North Face to be repaired for a small charge.

Simply drop off your damaged items at a participating store and a staff member will help you understand if the item can be restored and how much it will cost. It can be an affordable alternative to buying something new from The North Face.
