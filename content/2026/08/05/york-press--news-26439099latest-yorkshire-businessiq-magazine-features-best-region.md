## Read the latest Yorkshire BUSINESSiQ for the stories driving the region forward

The front cover of the July Issue. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

  The newest edition of **Yorkshire BUSINESSiQ** is now available, bringing together a collection of inspiring stories that celebrate the businesses, organisations and individuals making a significant impact across York and North Yorkshire.

  Leading this month's edition is a feature on **Ramsdens Solicitors**, exploring how the Yorkshire-founded firm has successfully combined more than 150 years of tradition with a modern, innovative approach to growth.

  The magazine also highlights the success of **Treske**, the Thirsk-based furniture maker whose craftsmanship has earned recognition worldwide, and** Duttons for Buttons**, the family-run business celebrating 120 years of supporting everyone from local creatives to major film and television productions.

  Readers can also delve into the work of **York Archaeology**, whose efforts to preserve and share the region's rich heritage continue to attract visitors and contribute to the local economy. Alongside these features, the latest issue includes the highly anticipated** York and North Yorkshire Top 100 Businesses Report**, produced in partnership with York Business School, showcasing the strength, resilience and entrepreneurial spirit that defines the region's business community.
