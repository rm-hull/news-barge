President Donald Trump’s preferred candidate to take on a Democrat for a competitive Michigan U.S. House seat is likely to lose against a GOP opponent who dropped out of the race last month.

Amir Hassan, whom Trump endorsed on Truth Social this week, was trailing by more than 17 percentage points behind Thomas J. Smith in Michigan’s 8th congressional district primary Wednesday morning.

With roughly 99 percent of votes counted, according to the Associated Press and early returns from *The Detroit News*, it’s likely Trump will see his candidate lose to Smith. 

Smith dropped out of the race in Michigan last month but remained on the ballot. Smith endorsed fellow Republican Al Lemmo, who had roughly 16 percent of the vote as of Wednesday morning. It’s unclear why Smith initially suspended his campaign.

Hassan’s loss is just the latest example of a Trump-endorsed candidate failing to resonate with voters. However, the president’s endorsements are typically considered strong signs of success, and he often brags about his wins.

The winner of Michigan’s 8th congressional district race will face the incumbent Democrat, Rep. Kristen McDonald Rivet, in November.

The district, which includes the city of Flint, is expected to be one of the most competitive midterm races. Trump won the district in the 2024 election at the same time as McDonald Rivet took the House seat.

Smith issued a statement Wednesday morning, obtained by *The Detroit News*, saying he was “humbled and honored that Republicans have chosen me to take the fight to Kristen McDonald Rivet.”

"I look forward to working with Speaker Johnson and the entire Republican team to expand our House majority and advance President Trump’s America First agenda to lower costs for working families and make America affordable again," Smith said.

*The Independent* has asked Smith’s campaign for comment. 

Smith’s statement appears to indicate he is willing to accept the result of the primaries and take on McDonald Rivet, despite declaring in a Facebook video posted on July 16 that he was “putting my full support behind Al Lemmo.”

![Thomas J Smith, a Republican running in Michigan's 8th congressional district, was polling far ahead of his GOP opponents – despite dropping out of the race last month](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/05/13/04/745961443_122112897225361673_6052085966692264703_n.jpg)

Smith, a former General Motors employee and current entrepreneur, grew up in Wisconsin but moved to Flint at a young age.

On his campaign website, Smith promotes traditional conservative values such as limited government, protected personal freedoms and securing the U.S. border.

Meanwhile, Trump’s preferred candidate, Hassan, 40, grew up in Flint and spent years in the military and law enforcement. On his campaign website, he says he is a former Navy member who worked as security at prison camps in Guantanamo Bay.

His campaign website emphasizes his Christian beliefs, his loyalty to Israel and desire to reinforce Trump’s America First policies.
