Trump says US and Iran are "in talks" while the Strait of Hormuz is set to open tomorrow

Conservative commentator Bill O’Reilly stated he is "haunted" by a warning he gave President Trump earlier this year about the ongoing Iran conflict.

O'Reilly privately advised President Trump in February that engaging with Iran was the "biggest gamble" of his presidency, characterizing the Iranians as irrational and unlikely to surrender.

The commentator's remarks were made on NewsNation’s On Balance with Leland Vittert, where he also discussed President Trump's low approval ratings, including a 35% approval in a Reuters/Ipsos poll.

President Trump's public statements on Iran have been inconsistent, fluctuating from threatening to "hit very hard" if the Strait of Hormuz remained closed to announcing positive negotiations and calling the regime "unbelievably duplicitous."

Further concerns about the administration's strategy emerged when a U.S. Central Command intelligence officer reportedly sought "creative and unconventional ways to pressure and punish Iran."
