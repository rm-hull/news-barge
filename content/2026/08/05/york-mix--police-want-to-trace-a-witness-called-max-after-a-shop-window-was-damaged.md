A York shop window has been damaged, possibly by someone using a shopping trolley, say police.

It happened at around 5.15pm on Tuesday 28 July, with damage to the front window of Age UK on Front Street, Acomb.

A man has since approached staff to say he witnessed what happened, but only left a name of ‘Max’

Police are now appealing for ‘Max’ to make contact, in order to help with the investigation.

Of course, if anyone else can also help, please make contact too, they say.
