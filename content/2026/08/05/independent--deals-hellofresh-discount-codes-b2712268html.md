### 50 per cent off first orders, plus free desserts

HelloFresh is slicing 50 per cent off the price of your first box. And if you have a sweet tooth, it gets even better. You’ll also be able to add a free dessert to your box every week for the first three months of your subscription.

**Up to 55 per cent off with the student discount**

If you are a student, you can claim 55 per cent off your first HelloFresh box, and a free GU dessert every week for three months. Plus, you’ll get 20 per cent off your next seven boxes. Signing up activates a recurring weekly subscription, which you can pause or cancel at any time. Just make sure to cancel five days before the next delivery.

How to make the most of your student discount at HelloFresh:

- Sign up via Unidays or Student Beans using your university email or student ID to generate your code.
- Plan all eight boxes within the nine-week window to maintain full discount benefits.
- Pause or cancel after you’ve benefited – there’s no obligation beyond your chosen period.

**Keyworkers get 50 per cent off at HelloFresh, plus 20 per cent off four boxes**

HelloFresh also offers a Blue Light Discount for NHS staff, emergency services, social care professionals, teachers, military personnel, and charity workers. To qualify, you will need to go through a verification process via GoCertify, which can involve uploading your ID and other personal details.

Once an account has been verified, the discount will be applied automatically. For keyworkers, this is 50 per cent off the first box and 20 per cent off the following four boxes. Plus, complimentary desserts each week for three months.
