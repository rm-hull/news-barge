Firefighters were called to an incident by the river in York last night (Tuesday).

They went to **Water End** at**8.28pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb crews were mobilised to a fire sighted near some trees.

#### Water End, York

“The crew investigated and confirmed three wooden pallets had been set alight. The crew used a hose reel.”
