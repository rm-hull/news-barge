# Google Assistant is being replaced – here’s what to know

![The full transition is expected to take several weeks](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/03/05/9/37/GettyImages-2261784766.jpeg)

- Google is discontinuing its Assistant app on Android devices, replacing it with Gemini, its next-generation AI-powered assistant.
- Users are being notified that access to Google Assistant will begin to be removed from 4 September, with the full transition expected to take several weeks.
- Once the change is implemented, functions like long-pressing the power button or saying 'Hey Google' will activate Gemini instead of the former Assistant.
- This transition will also affect other devices paired with Android phones, such as smartwatches and earphones.
- Gemini is designed to perform the same tasks as Assistant but leverages more advanced AI technology, allowing for more natural user interaction.
