**Harrogate Town have signed Worthing striker Brad Dolaghan for an undisclosed fee on a three-year deal.**

The 21-year-old scored 23 goals in 40 league games in 2025-26 as Worthing won promotion to the National League.

He could make his debut for Simon Weaver's men in Saturday's season-opener against Solihull Moors.
