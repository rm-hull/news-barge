London Luton passengers travelling to the airport this August are being offered free bus travel.

The “Fly and Ride” initiative allows adult passengers to travel free on Arriva and Stagecoach services by presenting a valid printed or digital boarding pass for Luton flights.

The scheme aims to encourage passengers from across Bedfordshire, Hertfordshire and Buckinghamshire to leave their cars at home ahead of Luton airport’s predicted busiest summer on record.

Fully funded by the airport, the initiative complements the Department for Transport's free summer bus travel scheme for children aged 5-15.

To qualify, passengers must have a return journey booked before 31 August, and the offer does not include National Express or Greenline bus services. The free travel is not available on buses departing Luton airport.
