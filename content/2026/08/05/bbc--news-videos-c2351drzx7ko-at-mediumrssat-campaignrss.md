# Watch: Moment passenger train crashes into trailer carrying straw bales

CCTV footage shows a passenger train in Poland crashing into a trailer loaded with bales of straw.

Polish railway authorities said the driver was intoxicated when he stopped his trailer in the middle of railway tracks as the barriers were closing and warning lights flashed.

No injuries were reported, but officials said the driver had endangered the lives of 500 people.
