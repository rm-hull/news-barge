**Best ways to save at Harvey Nichols**

Whether you're a regular shopper or it's your first time browsing, there are plenty of clever ways to save at Harvey Nichols. From loyalty rewards and student discounts to exclusive newsletter perks and seasonal sales, here’s how to make the most of your money when shopping at Harvey Nichols.

### Rewards membership benefits

Signing up for the Harvey Nichols Rewards membership is one of the easiest ways to start saving. It’s free to join and allows you to earn points every time you shop at Harvey Nichols, both in-store and online.

Over time, the points you accumulate can be used against future purchases. The program operates in a five-tiered system: the more points you earn, the higher your tier and the better your benefits. No matter what point level you’re at, every member receives an early access pass to sales, an annual birthday treat, an anniversary gift and a complimentary hot drink when shopping in-store.

Here’s a quick breakdown on how the benefits differ from level to level:

- **Level 1**(up to £499) – You’ll receive one point for every £1 spent and can choose one additional benefit.
- **Level 2**(£500 - £1,999) – Get two points each time you spend £1 and select two benefits of your choice.
- **Level 3**(£2,000 - £4,999) – Gain three points when you spend £1, plus a complimentary glass of wine or champagne when you shop in-store. You can also choose three additional benefits.
- **Level 4**(£5,000 - £9,999) – Stack up four points for every £1 you spend online or in store, and enjoy a complimentary glass of wine or champagne. Then, choose four additional benefits to enjoy.
- **Level 5**(£10,000+) – The highest tier in the rewards programme, you get five points for every £1 you spend and a free glass of wine or champagne when you shop in-store. Plus, choose five additional perks to enjoy.

The benefits that you can choose change over time, but here are some examples of what we’ve seen in the past:

- Free delivery on every order for members on levels two to five
- 10 per cent off selected bars and restaurants
- Save 15 per cent on children’s wear
- 15 per cent off fashion for members on levels three to five
- Free luxury lunch for four people
- Get 10 per cent off beauty and grooming services

### First-order discount

When you sign up for your free membership account, you’ll be asked if you want to join the newsletter. Doing so will land you with a voucher for 10 per cent off your first online order. If you don’t want to receive the marketing emails, you can opt out once you’ve received your code.

### Harvey Nichols student discount

Harvey Nichols partners with Unidays to bring students a number of exclusive online offers. To unlock the exclusive deals, you’ll need to verify your educational status using your university email address.

Once you do so, you can enjoy 10 per cent off fashion and beauty every time you shop. You’ll also find several rolling offers available for a limited time only. In the past, this has included an additional 10 per cent off fashion, free gifts with selected purchases and 10 per cent off six or more bottles of wine.

### Friend referrals

The Harvey Nichols referral program is a win-win for both you and your friends. Simply refer a friend or family member to shop with Harvey Nichols for the first time, and they will get 15 per cent off their order. Once they’ve made a qualifying purchase, you’ll also receive a voucher for £30 off your next shop too.

To get started, register with your name and email to generate your unique referral link. Share it with your friends to start stacking up vouchers.

### Seasonal sales

Some of the best discounts you’ll find from Harvey Nichols aren’t always through promo codes. They can come from its seasonal sales, which offer up to 50 per cent off luxury fashion, accessories and beauty products.

Sales are available all year round, but typically take place during key retail events, including mid-season sales, bank holidays and Black Friday weekend.

### Free delivery

Harvey Nichols offers free standard delivery on orders over £300, saving you the usual £7.50 delivery charge.

If you’re not planning on spending quite so much, you can still avoid delivery fees by opting for the click and collect service, which is free on all orders, regardless of spend. Simply choose to pick up your order from any Harvey Nichols location at checkout.
