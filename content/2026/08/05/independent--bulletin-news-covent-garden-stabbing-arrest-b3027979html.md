A woman has been arrested after four people were stabbed in Covent Garden, central London.

The Metropolitan Police and London Ambulance Service responded to reports of a stabbing on Endell Street at 12:29pm on Wednesday.

Four people were treated at the scene by paramedics and subsequently taken to a major trauma centre.

The Metropolitan Police said four men, aged 34, 39, 42, and 52 were found with stab wounds; no information was immediately released about the seriousness of their injuries.

London's Air Ambulance was dispatched and landed in Trafalgar Square to assist with the incident.
