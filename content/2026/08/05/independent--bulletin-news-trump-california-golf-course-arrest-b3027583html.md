Armed man arrested days before Trump's Rancho Palos Verdes visit

Jeanine John Taele, 38, was arrested Sunday at Trump National Golf Club in Rancho Palos Verdes after being observed taking photos and videos, appearing to monitor security-planning activities.

Deputies found hollow-point ammunition in Taele's pocket and a loaded firearm in his vehicle at the golf club.

A subsequent search of Taele's home uncovered additional firearms, body armor, high-capacity magazines and notebooks containing "concerning statements."

Taele was charged with multiple felonies, including possession of a large-capacity magazine and a short-barreled rifle and a count of second-degree robbery, along with a misdemeanor for carrying a concealed firearm.

The arrest occurred days before Donald Trump’s scheduled visit to the golf club for a Republican National Committee fundraiser.
