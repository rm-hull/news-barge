Brooklyn Peltz Beckham has shared a post marking his anniversary with wife Nicola, calling her his “absolute princess” and vowing to “always protect” her.

The couple married in April 2022 and renewed their vows in a New York ceremony in August 2025.

They are currently estranged from Peltz Beckham’s family after he claimed that they prioritised ‘Brand Beckham’ over family and attempted to sabotage his relationship with his wife.

On Instagram, Peltz Beckham shared a photo of himself and his wife with their arms around each other, each holding a glass of wine.

Another photo showed the couple sharing a tight embrace.

“Happy anniversary Nicola. I can’t tell you how much I love you, you are my best friend and I love you more than life itself xx,” the 27-year-old wrote.

“I am so lucky to be able to call you my wife, you are my absolute princess, and I promise to always protect you.”

Nicola, 31, shared her own tribute to her husband and wrote: “Happy anniversary my love. You make life so beautiful. Thank you for being the most amazing husband I could ever dream of.”

The couple wed in a lavish ceremony at the Peltz family’s sprawling estate in Palm Beach, Florida.

They renewed their vows in August 2025 in Westchester, New York. The Beckham family did not attend this ceremony.

In his six-page statement, Peltz Beckham accused his parents, Sir David Beckham and Lady Victoria Beckham, of “trying endlessly to ruin my relationship since before my wedding, and it hasn’t stopped”.

He also alleged that his wife had been “disrespected” by his family, that his parents controlled narratives in the press about their family and put “Brand Beckham” first.

He said that he had no desire to reconcile with his parents and was distancing himself from the family.

Recently, Lady Beckham addressed the feud for the first time in an interview with *The Wall Street Journal*, and said she and Sir David “love our children so much” and have “always tried to be the best parents”.

The Beckhams have tried several times to extend a public olive branch to their son, mainly on social media, but he has ignored them.
