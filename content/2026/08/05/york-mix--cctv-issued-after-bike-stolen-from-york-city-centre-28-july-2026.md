Police have issued this picture of a man they want to talk to over a bicycle theft.

A bike was stolen from outside the Ask restaurant at the Assembly Rooms on Tuesday, 28 July.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’ve issued this CCTV of a male that we would like to speak to, following a theft of a bicycle in York.

#### Theft location

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
