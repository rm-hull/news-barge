# Los Angeles beaches under a stay-out-of-the-water warning during tense heatwave

- Los Angeles County health officials issued warnings for several popular beaches due to high bacteria levels, advising beachgoers to avoid ocean entry.
- The affected locations include areas around Venice Pier, Santa Monica Pier, Mother's Beach in Marina del Rey, and Inner Cabrillo Beach in San Pedro.
- Recent water samples revealed bacteria levels exceeding California health standards, posing an increased risk of illness for those entering the water.
- The warnings coincide with hot weather expected to attract large crowds to local beaches, though an earlier warning for Wilshire Boulevard storm drain at Santa Monica Beach has been lifted.
- Officials recommend checking current advisories via the Los Angeles County Department of Public Health's hotline or website before swimming.
