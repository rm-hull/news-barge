Items have been stolen from a shop in York city centre.

North Yorkshire Police has issued a photograph of a woman officers want to speak to about the theft at TGJones in Coney Street on Tuesday, July 14.

The force on Wednesday (August 5) issued the photograph, saying the woman pictured may have information about the theft of “various items”.

“Please contact us if you recognise the female pictured on CCTV, as she may have information that will assist our investigation,” a police spokesperson said.

They urged anyone with information to phone 101 or Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 quoting reference 12260136362.
