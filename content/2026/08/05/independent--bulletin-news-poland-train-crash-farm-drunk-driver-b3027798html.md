Passenger train smashes through trailer after ‘intoxicated’ tractor driver stops on tracks

A passenger train collided with a farm trailer in Poland on Monday, 3 August, after a tractor driver stopped on a level crossing.

CCTV footage released by Polish State Railways (PKP) captured the tractor, hauling straw bales, on the railway as barriers closed and warning lights flashed.

The tractor driver was found to be intoxicated, according to officials.

The train crashed through the trailers, which were still on the tracks.

No injuries were reported, but the railway stated that the driver endangered the lives of approximately 500 people.
