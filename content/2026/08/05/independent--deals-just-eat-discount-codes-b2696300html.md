## Ways to save on Just Eat takeaways

### 10 per cent off with a Just Eat StampCard

If you’re a loyal customer, a Just Eat StampCard – a loyalty scheme that turns your takeaways into discounts – is a must. Here’s how it works:

- Every time you place an order with the same restaurant, you’ll collect a stamp that’s worth 10 per cent of your order.
- Once you’ve collected five stamps (i.e. placed five orders), Just Eat turns your saved-up total into a personal discount voucher.
- The voucher is valid for three months and can be used at the same restaurant where you earned it.

So, for example, if your order costs you £45, you’ll receive £4.50. On your sixth order, you can use the collated funds towards your total.

### Save 20 per cent on Cheeky Tuesdays

Cheeky Tuesdays is a weekly deal offering 20 per cent off orders over £15. You can take advantage of this discount every week and it applies to most local eateries and fast food chains, including McDonald’s, KFC and Burger King.

To get the Cheeky Tuesday Just Eat discount, simply head to the app or website on a Tuesday and look out for places marked with ‘Offers’ or ‘With discount’ when browsing. Once you’ve picked your meal and reached the £15 minimum spend, the 20 per cent off will be applied automatically at checkout.

## Just Eat FAQs

### How much can I save with a Just Eat discount code?

Typical savings range from five to 25 per cent. However, many will give you a fixed amount off, such as £5 off on qualifying orders, often with a minimum order value requirement.

### Are there Just Eat coupons for free delivery?

You can often find free delivery codes, which sometimes include grocery orders. Some local restaurant offers may include delivery with orders over a specified value, too.

### Can I use multiple discount codes on one Just Eat order?

Just Eat allows only one voucher or discount code per order, so make sure to check which one will offer you the most money off.

Launched in 2001, Just Eat is one of the oldest food delivery services in the country, and it is still going strong. “What I love about Just Eat is that it has a far bigger number of local eateries when compared to Uber Eats and Deliveroo,” said *The Independent*’s tech writer Alex Lee. “Just Eat also lets restaurants waive delivery charges, take part in stamp card programmes and hold their own exclusive discounts, making it a prime joint to find tasty food on the cheap.” 

Just Eat’s price promise has you covered if you notice a price difference. If you pay more with Just Eat than you would when ordering directly from the restaurant’s own website or app, they’ll refund you twice the difference in the form of a Just Eat voucher.
