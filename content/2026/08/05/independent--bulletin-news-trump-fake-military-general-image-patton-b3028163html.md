The rendering pictured Trump, 80, alongside generals George S. Patton and Douglas MacArthur, two of the country’s most famous combat commanders and figures of World War II, in front of a fiery battleground.

Despite the image, Trump never served in the armed forces, having received five deferrals from the Vietnam War draft.

One of these deferrals was for bone spurs, which the daughters of the diagnosing podiatrist suggested was a favor to Trump's father.

Trump has previously faced criticism for his lack of military service and once considered awarding himself the Congressional Medal of Honor for a non-combat visit to an Iraqi air base.
