Harrogate Town AFC have signed the 2025/26 National League South top scorer, Brad Dolaghan.

The 21-year-old striker makes the permanent move from Worthing, signing a three-year contract with Town.



Brad Dolaghan said:I’m delighted to be here and really excited to get going, the project is a really exciting one and one I want to be a part of.” Dolaghan stated after signing.

To get Worthing up was the goal and we did that so I’m really proud of that and looking to bring that momentum into this year.






Head of Player Recruitment Lloyd Kerry added:We’re delighted to have him here, he’s a young, exciting centre forward who knows where the back of the net is.

He’s a natural finisher, gets in great positions and has great movement, and at 21 he’s only going to get better.


Having come through the ranks at Worthing, seven goals in his first ten games tempted West Ham into offering the forward a first professional contract.

A first loan spell at Aldershot brought further success and an FA Trophy winners medal.

Returning to Worthing ahead of the 2025/26 season, Dolaghan picked up right where he left off as his 23 goals saw him crowned National League South top goalscorer.

Brad will wear the number 23 shirt at Town, subject to FA and National League approval.
