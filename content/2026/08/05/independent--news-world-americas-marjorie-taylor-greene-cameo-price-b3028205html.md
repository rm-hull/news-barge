Marjorie Taylor Greene, the firebrand lawmaker who quit Congress last year after falling out with President Donald Trump, has a new side hustle.

Greene, 52, announced Tuesday she had joined Cameo, the website where people or businesses can pay celebrities and other recognizable peoplefor personalized videos. 

“I am excited to let you know I am now on Cameo,” Greene said in a TikTok video. “So if you have a special message for a friend or a loved one, I would love to deliver it for you – or any other message for anyone.”

For just $149, people can ask Greene to make a video saying whatever they want. For another $5,000, businesses can book a personalized video from her.

Greene’s decision to join the site mirrors a move by her friend and former colleague, George Santos, who made more than $174,000 on Cameo after he was federally indicted on wire fraud and money laundering, among other charges, and expelled from Congress.

Making personalized videos on Cameo has become a lucrative side gig for famous people, especially former politicians or political figures with notorious reputations.

Matt Gaetz, a former congressman and ally to Greene, also joined Cameo after resigning from Congress in 2024 and began charging $250 for personalized videos. His profile indicates he’s made at least 64 videos, netting an estimated $16,000.

Former Alaska Governor Sarah Palin reported making $211,529 from Cameo videos in 2021 – more than her salary as governor.

Since leaving Capitol Hill last year, Greene has remained politically active, mostly by criticizing Trump. She’s done some light campaigning for her former colleague and fellow Trump adversary, Thomas Massie, and appeared on TV news to share her qualms about the president. Greene has also teamed up with other former Trump allies, such as podcast host Tucker Carlson, to form an anti-Trump conservative movement.

Greene’s critiques of Trump are creeping into her new side hustle, too.

![Greene, once a staunch Trump ally, has become a vocal Trump critic, accusing the president of abandoning his ‘America First’ policy – now she’s sharing those criticisms on Cameo](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/04/19/20/2202780440.jpg)

In one birthday message where Greene wished a woman a happy 30th birthday, the former congresswoman joked that a dog would make a better president than Trump.

“I also heard that you have a cute little dog named Pinot and I heard that Pinot is just the greatest patriot which I absolutely love,” Greene said in the video.

She added: “And I also heard that Pinot might make a better president than our current one. And as I think about that, I would say that’s probably true. Definitely true.”

In another video, where Greene congratulated the winner of a fantasy football league, she commends the loser for “losing all the way down.”

“That’s extremely impressive. It reminds me of someone else’s poll numbers who lives at 1600 Pennsylvania Avenue,” Greene said.
