Angela Lloyd was stabbed to death by Patricia Hewitt (Durham Constabulary)

Patricia Hewitt, 42, who suffers from schizophrenia and has a history of drug abuse and criminal convictions, was given a hospital order after admitting manslaughter on the grounds of diminished responsibility for the death of Angela Lloyd.

Hewitt inflicted 47 stab wounds on Angela Lloyd, 56, a disabled wheelchair user, at her sheltered accommodation bungalow in Newton Aycliffe, County Durham, on 13 August last year.

The fatal attack occurred just hours after Hewitt received a suspended prison sentence for assault and mere minutes after police community support officers had spoken to her regarding her welfare.

Ms Lloyd made desperate calls to a Care Connect alarm service during the attack, screaming for help and stating she was being stabbed, with Hewitt reportedly shouting 'where’s your money'.

Ms Lloyd's family expressed profound disappointment with the hospital order, arguing there should be a 'penal element' and raising serious concerns about the 'actions or inaction of all the organisations that failed so badly to protect Angela'.
