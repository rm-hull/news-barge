- **Secretlab has revealed new skins for its Pokémon Titan Evo collection**
- **Three new skins inspired by Grass-, Fire-, and Water-Type Pokémon will be included in the collection**
- **The designs highlight fan-favorite Pokémon Venusaur, Charizard, and Blastoise**

Following the launch of its Secretlab Pokémon Collection for its Titan Evo gaming chair, Secretlab is expanding the collaboration with three new *Pokémon*-inspired skins.

The Secretlab SKINS Pokémon Pixel Art Collection celebrates Grass, Fire, and Water-Type Pokémon, including the fan-favorite First Partner Evolutions: Venusaur, Charizard, and Blastoise.

Each design is tailored to each Pokémon, with stunning green, orange, and blue gradients that fade from light to dark along the backrest. Patterned panels along the side feature more pixel art themed after Grass, Fire, and Water-Type Pokémon, like Arcanine, Bulbasaur, Vaporeon, and many more.

 ![Secretlab](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/CEmpx79sscB7YV5VUstADf.jpg) 


"The Secretlab SKINS Pokémon Pixel Art Fire-Type Edition sports a scorching hot look, just like Charizard's Flamethrower," reads one description. "Charmander, Charmeleon, Arcanine, Rapidash, Flareon, and more round out the fiery company."

The designs rendered in adorable pixel art for Venusaur, Charizard, and Blastoise are featured in the middle of the chair, showcasing your chosen Pokémon.

Currently, the Pokémon Titan Evo collection includes the Pokémon Pikachu Edition, the Pokémon Eevee Edition, and the Pokémon Gengar Edition.

"We wanted this chair collection to speak to the inner child in all of us, while still being elevated with craftmanship that would appeal to our now grown-up selves," said Secretlab senior product graphic designer Irene Tee of the chair designs.
