Chelsea’s pre-season preparations continue today in Hong Kong as they face Juventus in a friendly. Xabi Alonso side have already played two matches with both sending up surprising results as the the new manager looks to get to grips with his new squad.

The first game was a 6-4 victory over Western Sydney Wanderers before Tottenham defeated Chelsea 2-1 thanks to a Richarlison winner in extra time.

Alonso knows there is more work to be done before the season begins in three weeks saying “we are at the stage of pre-season, so not where we want to be,” at a press conference on Tuesday. Meanwhile, Jordan Henderson and Danny Welbeck are recent arrivals to the club as is winger Geovany Quenda who, along with Welbeck, has joined the squad on their tour of Australia and Asia and could feature against Juve. Yet, Alonso knows there is work to be done

The Serie A side have enjoyed a decent pre-season with a draw against Basel quickly followed up by wins over Stanrd Liege and OGC Nice but can they beat the Blues this afternoon?

## Alonso makes changes

Understandably, as this is pre-season, Xabi Alonso has made a bunch of changes to the Chelsea starting XI that lost to Spurs last time out.

The main headlines are that Robert Sanchez starts in goal, Cole Palmer misses out entirely, Mykhailo Mudryk is on the bench and Danny Welbeck is handed his first start for the club since joining from Brighton.

## Gary Cahill on Welbeck and Henderson signings

Chelsea have punted for experience in the transfer window with the signings of Danny Welbeck and Jordan Henderson.

Former Blues defender Gary Cahill had his say on both players: “They are top lads and ultra professional.

“They get the best out of themselves which is why they are still going strong at the age they are.

“I think they will bring their quality and experience. That experience will show through on the pitch, in certain moments in games, or at the training ground and in training sessions. They’ve been there and done it at the top level for years.”

## A Chelsea legend

Moises Caicedo has an aim to become a ‘Chelsea legend’.

He’s signed a contract extension with the club and explained the reasons behind his decision.

“I wanted to keep playing for the club,” he said, “The club wanted to renew the contract and I said yes because I want to become a legend here.

“That is why I renewed my contract and for all of us, it’s important.”

## Moises Caicedo thanks fans in Hong Kong

Moises Caicedo has been speaking about the Hong Kong leg of Chelsea’s Australia and Asia tour and he praised the fans for coming out to support the team.

“The people here have been great,” the midfielder said. “We feel like we are at home.

“Everyone here has been very kind. It’s my first time here in Hong Kong and I feel it’s like a home to me already. So for that, I want to say thank you to the fans.

“The way they support us with the time difference is incredible, it is not easy for them to watch in the Premier League.

“So we really appreciate them, because we feel and realise that the supporters here give everything for us.”

## Chelsea's statement on Mudryk ban being overturned

Chelsea released a statement on Mudryk’s ban being lifted and they are ‘pleased’ that the matter has been concluded.

“From the outset, this has been an extremely difficult period for Misha, both professionally and personally, and we are pleased that the matter has now reached a conclusion which allows him to return to football with immediate effect,” Chelsea said in a statement.

“We have always supported Misha throughout this process while fully respecting the integrity of the anti-doping system and the legal process.

“We welcome the acknowledgement that advances in the applicable scientific reporting standards for more reliable testing mean that, had Misha's sample been analysed under today's technical requirements, it would not have been reported as a positive test and no anti-doping rule violation would have resulted.”

## Chelsea’s Mykhailo Mudryk cleared for immediate return to football after resolving doping ban

Chelsea FC winger Mykhailo Mudryk has been cleared to make an immediate return to action after the Court of Arbitration for Sport (Cas) partially upheld an appeal against his four-year ban for doping.
