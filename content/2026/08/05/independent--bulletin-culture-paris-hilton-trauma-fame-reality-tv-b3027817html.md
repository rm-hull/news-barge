# Paris Hilton on the ‘trauma’ of being young and famous

- Paris Hilton described her experience of early fame as "traumatising", leading to trust issues and learning to discern people's intentions.
- The 45-year-old socialite and reality TV star, known for shows like *The Simple Life*, reflected on the intense scrutiny her life faced.
- Hilton previously alleged abuse at a Utah boarding school, stating this experience prepared her for the challenges of Hollywood.
- She also spoke about the "harassment" she endured from the media as a young woman, noting that such treatment would not be accepted today.
- Hilton, who is now married with two children, revealed that her "dumb blonde" persona was a protective "armour".
