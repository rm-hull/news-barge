Noel Edmonds responds after lobstergate divides fans

Noel Edmonds has addressed the controversy surrounding his decision to release two tank lobsters into the sea off the Isle of Man.

The television presenter and his wife, Liz Davies, stated they felt 'uncomfortable' seeing the crustaceans in a tank.

They released the lobsters into 'nice clear water' at Peel Breakwater.

While animal rights charity Peta praised their 'compassion', many online condemned the act, raising concerns about potential harm to native populations through disease transfer and genetic mixing.

Edmonds later posted a video on Tuesday (4 August) wearing a red boiler suit, simply stating he was 'going to bed'.
