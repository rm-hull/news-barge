Nine-year-old Minnie Merriman, from West Yorkshire, has been identified as the girl who died after being found seriously injured at the Elliot industrial estate in Arbroath.

Minnie was discovered shortly after midnight on Monday and subsequently passed away at Ninewells Hospital in Dundee.

Her family released a statement via Police Scotland, describing her as their 'beloved, absolutely incredible, beautiful and brave Minnie Moo', and requested privacy during this difficult time.

A 35-year-old man has been charged in connection with her death and is scheduled to appear at Forfar Sheriff Court; police are treating the death as suspicious but are not seeking any other suspects.

Police Scotland's major investigation team continues its inquiries at the scene, with a police presence remaining, and the local community, including Councillor Lois Speed, has expressed shock and heartbreak over the incident.
