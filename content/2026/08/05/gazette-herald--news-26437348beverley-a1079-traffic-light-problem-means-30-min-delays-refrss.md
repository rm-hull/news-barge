TRAFFIC problems are blocking a major road between York and Hull this morning (Wednesday, August 5).

The AA Traffic News is reporting that the A1079 is blocked in the East Yorkshire village of Bishop Burton, outside Beverley, between Finchcroft Lane and Finkle Street.

East Yorkshire Motor Service said the delays are due to faulty traffic lights.

⚠️ X46 Service Update



If you're travelling on the X46 today, please allow an extra 20–30 minutes for your journey.


This is due to faulty temporary traffic lights in Bishop Burton.


We're sorry for the delay and thank you for your patience. You can track your bus in real time… pic.twitter.com/NJDYD71pic— East Yorkshire (@EYBuses) August 5, 2026


Traffic is running very slowly from the A1079 roundabout to the east of the village at the junction for Beverley Racecourse and the B1248 for Malton
