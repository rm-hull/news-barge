## Good to know

### Free delivery

To qualify for Charlotte Tilbury free delivery, orders have to be more than £49 or you must be a silver or rose gold loyalty member. For orders less than this, there is a £2.95 standard delivery fee, which takes two to five working days.

Express delivery is available on orders placed before 10:30pm, taking one to two working days. This is £5.95 or free on all orders above £185, and for rose gold loyalty members.

### Returns policy

Charlotte Tilbury's returns policy allows unused or “lightly used” products to be returned for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Your refund should be issued within 14 working days back onto your original payment method.

You can start the returns process in your account or via the returns page by entering your email address and order number.
