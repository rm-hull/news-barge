Ski Yodl: British holiday firm plunges in liquidation as holidays are cancelled

UK travel provider Ski Yodl Ltd has entered liquidation, leading to the cancellation of all its holiday bookings after more than eight years of operation.

The Norwich-based company, established in March 2018, specialised in selling ski holidays and accommodation, including chalets, apartments and hotels across the European Alps.

As of 22 July, Ski Yodl Ltd is no longer protected by the Association of British Travel Agents (ABTA), which confirmed the voluntary winding-up order and the appointment of a liquidator.

While no ABTA-protected package holidays were booked at the time of liquidation, customers with accommodation-only holidays may be affected by the closure.

Customers who paid by credit or debit card are advised to contact their card issuer for assistance with obtaining a refund, while those who paid by bank transfer must register a claim with the appointed liquidator, RCM Advisory Limited.
