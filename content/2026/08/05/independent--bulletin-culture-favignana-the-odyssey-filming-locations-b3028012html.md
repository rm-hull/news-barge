# How The Odyssey is set to transform Italy’s Favignana island forever

- Favignana, a small Italian island, is hoping to significantly boost its tourism by capitalising on its prominent role as Ithaca in Sir Christopher Nolan's recent blockbuster film, *The Odyssey*, which achieved substantial global box office success.
- Local authorities, in collaboration with Universal Pictures and Sicily's culture department, are planning to establish a permanent exhibition this October at a former tuna-processing plant, designed to showcase the film's production process, costumes, and equipment to attract visitors.
- The film's production injected an estimated €1 million to €2 million into the local economy, and the island is now experiencing increased interest from international investors for high-end tourism developments, including the redevelopment of a former holiday village into a five-star resort.
- Island businesses are drawing parallels with the success of the *Inspector Montalbano* television series, which significantly boosted tourism in south-eastern Sicily, and are optimistic that*The Odyssey* will similarly enhance western Sicily's appeal to American visitors.
- Despite the anticipated increase in visitors, Favignana is proactively addressing concerns about overtourism by implementing measures such as installing mooring buoys to protect marine areas and considering regulations like vehicle permits tied to minimum-stay requirements to encourage longer visits and extend the tourist season.
