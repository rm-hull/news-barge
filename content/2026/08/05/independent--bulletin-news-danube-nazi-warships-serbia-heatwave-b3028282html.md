Secret Nazi ships emerge from riverbed as the Danube runs dry

Sunken Nazi warships from World War II have emerged from the Danube riverbed in Serbia due to plummeting water levels.

The vessels, estimated to be up to 200, were deliberately scuttled by German forces as they retreated from the Soviets towards the end of the war.

Water levels in the Danube dropped to their lowest in 30 years in several European countries, including Serbia and Romania, last Thursday (30 July).

This record-low water level is a direct consequence of Europe's scorching heatwave.

The low water levels are raising concerns about a potential energy crisis, disrupting cooling systems and reducing the capacity of hydroelectric plants, with Serbia's main plant operating at only 20%.
