  *By Tom Henry, director at Hethertons Solicitors*

When you think about economic success, many people may focus on the big brands we all use every day.

However, when you dig into the official figures, the real powerhouse of the UK’s economy are small businesses.

For example, SMEs accounted for £2.8 trillion of GDP in 2025, which equates to 51 per cent of the total turnover of private businesses.

York is an incubator for small businesses and has seen a number of success stories launched from our beautiful city.

It is, therefore, only right that we celebrate their success, which The Press Business Awards has done year in, year out.

At Hethertons Solicitors, we take great pride and pleasure in sponsoring the Small Business of the Year category once again, so we can share in the excitement of this prestigious ceremony.

It ties into our passion for supporting the businesses that have built York and that rely on our team’s legal expertise to thrive.

We have a close connection to companies across the region, which gives us a deeper understanding of the key commercial considerations that power growth.

From budding entrepreneurs to established business owners, we support people as well as their businesses with a practical, flexible and friendly approach.

On behalf of everyone at Hethertons Solicitors, I would like to wish entrants the best of luck and we look forward to seeing you at the awards night later this year.
