Children across Yorkshire are being invited to nominate an adult in their life to be covered in slime live on CBBC.

Saturday Mash-Up! is currently casting for the ninth series of the popular Saturday morning programme, which returns to screens in September.

Producers are looking for children aged between eight and 12 to take part in the show’s Slime Sealed Delivered feature.

The segment gives youngsters the chance to nominate an adult in their life who they think “really deserves” to be slimed live on television.

That could include a parent or carer, with successful applicants getting the opportunity to appear on Saturday Mash-Up! and deliver the messy punishment themselves.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a799c5606ce14bd187.jpg)


The programme is encouraging families from across Yorkshire to apply ahead of the new series.

*Children wishing to make a nomination can apply through the CBBC website:**[https://www.bbc.co.uk/cbbc/joinin/saturday-mash-up-nominate-an-adult-to-be-slimed*](https://www.bbc.co.uk/cbbc/joinin/saturday-mash-up-nominate-an-adult-to-be-slimed*)
