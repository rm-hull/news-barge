# New solar corona instrument faces crucial test during total eclipse

- Italian scientists have developed the Circular Slit Spectrometer (CISS) in a bid to transform the study of the solar corona, the outer layer of the Sun’s atmosphere.
- The instrument will undergo a crucial test during the total solar eclipse in Spain on 12 August.
- Principal investigator Federico Landini of the Astrophysical Observatory of Turin said that unlike conventional linear spectrometers, the new prototype "has a circular slit and can capture the spectrum of the entire corona at a certain distance from the centre of the Sun in a single photograph”.
- A successful test could lead to the new optical principle being used in future space missions.
- UVCS, the last major ultraviolet spectrometer, operated on the European Space Agency and NASA's SOHO spacecraft from 1996 to 2013.
