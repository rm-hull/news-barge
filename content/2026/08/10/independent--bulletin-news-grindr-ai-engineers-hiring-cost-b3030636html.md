# Dating app CEO financially breaks down what AI has done for company

- Grindr CEO George Arison stated that artificial intelligence has increased the company's engineering output by up to 3.5 times without significantly growing its staff.
- Arison estimated that achieving the same technical output without AI would have required hiring approximately 200 additional engineers at an annual cost of $60 million.
- The dating app company measured its increased productivity by tracking the amount of code shipped by its engineering team.
- Arison noted that using AI allows Grindr's human engineers to focus on areas that benefit most from human creativity and judgment.
- Arison is one of the few corporate executives to provide specific figures on the headcount and financial savings generated by AI integration.
