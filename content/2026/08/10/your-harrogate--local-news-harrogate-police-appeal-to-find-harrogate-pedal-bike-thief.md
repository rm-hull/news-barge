Police have released an image of a man they would like to speak to following the theft of a pedal bike in Harrogate.

The bike was stolen from Market Place at around 3.20pm on Monday 20th July.

North Yorkshire Police is now appealing for help to identify the man pictured as part of its investigation.

A spokesperson said:

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please make contact, quoting case reference 12260138531.”


Anyone with information can email PC 1113 Joe Simms at [joe.simms@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joe.simms@northyorkshire.police.uk) or contact North Yorkshire Police online.

Live chat is also available through the force’s website from Monday to Friday between 9am and 5pm.

Alternatively, call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260138531.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
