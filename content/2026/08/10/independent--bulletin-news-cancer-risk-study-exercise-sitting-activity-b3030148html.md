# Small decline in daily activity can increase risk of cancer, study finds

![Researchers say even a slight decrease in daily activity can contribute to an increased cancer risk](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/19/23/09142942-94b25ac7-7e70-4dba-af66-d35a89eb5e8b.jpg)

- New research indicates that even a slight reduction in daily vigorous exercise can elevate cancer risk by 15 per cent.
- Experts warn that replacing physical activity with sedentary habits, such as sitting or watching TV, directly contributes to this increased risk.
- The study, published in BMC Medicine, analysed lifestyle data from 59,218 UK Biobank participants over an eight-year period, recording 2,385 cancer cases.
- Findings showed that increased movement correlated with a lower cancer risk, with replacing 15 minutes of sedentary time with movement linked to a 2 per cent reduced risk.
- Conversely, swapping 30 minutes of movement for sedentary behaviour or sleep was associated with an 8 per cent and 7 per cent increased cancer risk, respectively.
