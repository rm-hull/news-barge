# Passengers delayed overnight after child refuses seatbelt on airplane

![A child refused to sit down and do up their seatbelt (stock image)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/23/09/01KY6RQQPPN7XCMZBPNZREXNDT.jpg)

- A Porter Airlines flight from Victoria to Toronto was cancelled after a young child refused to sit and fasten their seatbelt.
- The aircraft returned to the gate, but delays in removing the family and refiling paperwork caused the flight to miss the airport runway closure deadline.
- Passengers were forced to stay overnight in British Columbia before the flight eventually departed the following afternoon.
- Witnesses stated the child appeared frightened rather than misbehaved, repeatedly hiding under the seat and sliding out of the seatbelt.
- Canadian aviation regulations strictly require all passengers, including young children, to be safely buckled in during taxiing, take-off, and landing.
