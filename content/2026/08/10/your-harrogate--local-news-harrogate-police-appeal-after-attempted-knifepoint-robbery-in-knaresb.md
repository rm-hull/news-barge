Police are appealing for witnesses after a man was threatened with a knife during an attempted robbery in Knaresborough.

The incident happened at around 10.30pm on Friday (7th August) on Wetherby Road, near The Lido.

North Yorkshire Police said the victim was approached by three unknown men before being threatened with a knife.

The victim bravely challenged the suspects and sustained minor injuries in the process.

He later attended hospital for minor injuries following the incident.

The suspect fled the scene empty-handed.

A police spokesperson said:

"We’re particularly appealing for information about CCTV which may have captured the incident, or any eye-witnesses."


*Anyone with information is asked to email Elizabeth Parry at [elizabeth.parry@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:elizabeth.parry@northyorkshire.police.uk), contact North Yorkshire Police online or via live chat, or call 101 if unable to use those options.*

*Information can also be provided anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*

*Anyone getting in touch should quote reference 12260153117.*
