## Arsenal's Vinicius alternatives

Arsenal failed to convince Vinicius Jr to move to the Premier League but are moving on and have a couple of alternate options in mind.

The first is Julian Alvarez from Atletico Madrid.

Reports claim that Alvarez has already agreed personal terms with the Gunners but Atletico boss Diego Simeone seemed less sure.

He explained that the Spanish club have plans to keep improving Alvarez’s talents which would lean towards him sticking around for a while.

"The situation is very clear," Simeone told reporters.

"The club took a decision which Miguel Angel explained well. We are very happy with Julian.

"From the sporting side of things, we will help him keep growing and improving so that he can give us what he has given us over these two years at the club so far, which was a mountain of things."

## Cristian Romero eyes up shock Arsenal move

Tottenham captain Cristian Romero wants to the leave the club this summer and has his sights set on Arsenal though it is unclear whether the Gunners have the same desire for a switch.

Arsenal are aware of Romero’s preference and reportedly made an approach to Spurs last Thursday.

"He has two more years on his contract with Tottenham and a very high release clause," Victor Romero, Cristian’s father said.

"The English club isn't going to let him go for free; it's between $50-70m [£37.8m-£53m]. For me, they'll want to recover what they invested."

Romero has has interest from Atletico and Inter Milan with Tottenham preferring to do a deal with either of the European clubs.

## Six goals in two games: How much of a concern is Arsenal’s defence?

Arsenal suffered a second consecutive pre-season defeat ahead of the start of their Premier League title defence as clinical Borussia Dortmund won the Emirates Cup with a 3-2 victory in north London.

New signing Bruno Guimaraes was unveiled to the Arsenal fans before kick-off and there was a party atmosphere as the Gunners returned to the Emirates for the first time since lifting the Premier League title in May.

## Julian Alvarez not for sale

Diego Simeone has confirmed Atletico Madrid’s stance on Julian Alvarez amid interest from Arsenal: he is not for sale.

"The situation is very clear," the Argentine told a press conference.

"The club took a decision which Miguel Angel explained well. We are very happy with Julian.

"From the sporting side of things, we will help him keep growing and improving so that he can give us what he has given us over these two years at the club so far, which was a mountain of things."

## Arsenal outgoings - could Zubimendi be on the move?

With Arsenal splashing £75m on Bruno Guimaraes, Mikel Arteta may be required to sanction some sales.

That has led *Mundo Deportivo* to report that Martin Zubimendi, who only joined Arsenal last year, could leave the club, but only if a potential suitor matches their £77m asking price.

Meanwhile, West Ham are exploring a move for Reiss Nelson who has entered the final year of his contract, as per *Sky Sports.*

## Another Chelsea-Strasbourg loan on the horizon

Filip Jorgensen is set to move to Strasbourg on loan, according to *Sky Sports*, becoming the latest Chelsea player to make that jump to the Alsace sister club.

## Aston Villa in talks for Palhinha - but face competition from Newcastle?

Aston Villa transfer chief Damian Viadagany has confirmed the club are in talks to sign Joao Palhinha from Bayern Munich.

He told *Bild*: “We are in talks, but there is currently no agreement. 

"If something should happen, it is always on the basis of the great respect we feel for FC Bayern.

"We are friendly clubs; our relationship with Max Eberl (Bayern's director of sport), Christoph Freund (Bayern sporting director) and all other responsible persons is excellent.

"Therefore, I cannot and do not want to comment on any further details.”

This comes amid reports that Newcastle are also keen on the Portuguese midfielder, who was on loan at Tottenham last term, joining Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg on the list of potential Bruno Guimaraes replacements.
