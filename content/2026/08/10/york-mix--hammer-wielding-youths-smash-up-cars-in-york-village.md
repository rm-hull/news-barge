Police are investigating a wrecking spree targeting cars in a York village.

It happened on Friday night (7 August) in New Earswick.

The criminal damage happened in Hawthorn Terrace North, said resident Mal Bell.

#### Hawthorn Terrace North, New Earswick

Villagers reported being woken by car alarms going off in the early hours.

Mal posted on Facebook: “Several vehicles were damaged apparently by two youths wearing balaclavas on a moped with a hammer.”

He told ***YorkMix*** it happened at around 4am. “The police have told me that ten vehicles were involved.”

Another villager reported: “They almost caused an accident on the link road overtaking dangerously on a quad bike and moped.

“The passenger looked like he was videoing it on a mobile. All wearing balaclavas.”

According to another resident, on Saturday there were “several lads out and about… wearing black balaclavas, riding e bikes at speed, spread all over the road, doing wheelies.”
