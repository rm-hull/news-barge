# A Christmas Story star Tedde Moore dies, aged 79

![Tedde Moore who starred as Miss Shields in A Christmas Story](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/10/43/tedde-moore-actress-best-known-136981018-copy.jpeg)

- Canadian actor Tedde Moore, best known for playing schoolteacher Miss Shields in the 1983 film *A Christmas Story* , has died aged 79.
- Moore passed away on Wednesday in Huntsville, Ontario, surrounded by her family.
- The actor, who was diagnosed with multiple sclerosis in 2009, earned a Genie Award nomination for her performance in the beloved holiday movie.
- Over a decades-long career, her screen credits also included *The Terry Fox Story*,* Goosebumps*,* The Good Witch*, and* Murdoch Mysteries* .
- She is survived by four children, including the Grammy-winning music producer Noah "40" Shebib.
