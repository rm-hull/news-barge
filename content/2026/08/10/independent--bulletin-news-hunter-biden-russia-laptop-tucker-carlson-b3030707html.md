# Tucker Carlson says Israeli figures ‘connected to’ Netanyahu pushed Hunter Biden laptop story

- Conservative podcaster Tucker Carlson claimed Israeli figures falsely told his team that Hunter Biden's laptop contained explicit images of children.
- During an interview with Hunter Biden, Carlson dismissed conspiracy theories regarding the laptop, confirming it did not contain explicit images of minors.
- Carlson, who once used the laptop story to criticize the Biden family while serving as a Fox News anchor, put conspiracy theories about the laptop’s contents to rest in a wide-ranging interview with the former president’s son – specifically the false allegations that the laptop contained abusive images of children.
- “Can I just say one thing though, this is what I experienced during that time - the allegation that there was abusive minors proven on the laptop – that came from the Israelis,” Carlson told Biden. The “Tucker Carlson Show” host went further and claimed a man “connected to [Isreali Prime Minister] Netanyahu” had given a producer on his former Fox News show the information directly.
- Although the laptop contained nude photos of Hunter Biden and adult women, false claims about abusive images of children originated from online conspiracy theories. The Israeli government has been contacted for comment regarding Carlson's claims.
