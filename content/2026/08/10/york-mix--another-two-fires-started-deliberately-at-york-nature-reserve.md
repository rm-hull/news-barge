Firefighters tackled two deliberate fires at a York nature reserve last night (Sunday).

They were called to **Clifton Backies** off**Water Lane** at**8.51pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb crews were mobilised to a reports of a fire in the open.

#### **Clifton Backies**, York

After inspection crews confirmed this was two separate areas of fire both approx 5 sq metres.

“The cause is believed to be deliberate. Crews extinguished using knapsack sprayers and beaters.”
