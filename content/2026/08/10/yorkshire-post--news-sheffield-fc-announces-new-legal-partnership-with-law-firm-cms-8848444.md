# Sheffield FC announces new legal partnership with law firm CMS

## Sheffield FC has announced a new exclusive legal partnership with CMS, bringing together two organisations with deep roots in Sheffield.

CMS will serve as Sheffield FC’s exclusive legal partner, having spent 35 years operating in the city.

Sheffield FC is widely thought to be the world’s first football club, and was first founded in 1857.

In March, Reverend and the Makers frontmn John McClure was named as the new chairman of the club, after performing arts firm IAV Holdings Limited bought a stake in it.

![Bit like everybody else: Jon McClure in the stands at the Coach and Horses in Dronfield, home ground of the world's oldest football club, Sheffield FC.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjUzYTNlZjY0LWFkZWQtNGQwYS1iYTBlLTQwOGU3OTc3YzA4YjozMTg2ZDc5Ny02NDJjLTQ4NmItYWM3MS0xMTBjNDlmZjJmOGY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Speaking on the partnership with CMS, Mr McClure said: “We’re delighted to welcome CMS as our legal partner and would like to thank them for their support at a significant time for the football club.

“CMS shares our values, our ambition and our commitment to this city, and we’re looking forward to building what we believe will be a hugely positive relationship for both organisations.

“Over the first few months of our stewardship, we’ve spent time listening, learning and putting the right foundations in place for the future. Our ambition is not to change what makes Sheffield FC special, but to ensure that the club becomes financially sustainable. Strong commercial partnerships are essential to achieving that goal and turning our ambition off the pitch into success on it.”
