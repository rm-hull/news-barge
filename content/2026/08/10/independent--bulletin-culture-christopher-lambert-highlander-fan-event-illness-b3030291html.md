# Highlander’s Christopher Lambert pulls out of fan event due to illness

- Highlander actor Christopher Lambert was taken to hospital on Saturday after collapsing at Steel City Con in Pennsylvania.
- His representative explained that the 69-year-old French actor fell ill due to low blood sugar after failing to eat much during the day.
- Event organisers initially hoped Lambert would return on Sunday, but later confirmed he was unable to attend the remainder of the convention.
- Fans expressed concern and wished the actor a speedy recovery in messages posted across social media.
- The medical emergency follows a previous incident in March 2025, when Lambert cancelled an appearance in Sweden after suffering a back injury.
