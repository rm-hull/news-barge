# Britney Spears issues Botox warning after botched procedure: ‘You can’t trust anyone!’

![BRITNEY SPEARS-CONDUCCIÓN IMPRUDENTE](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/04/14/BRITNEY_SPEARS-CONDUCCI%C3%93N_IMPRUDENTE_16813.jpg)

- Britney Spears has disclosed a negative experience with Botox injections, revealing that an injector applied too much to her left eye, causing it to droop.
- The musician, 44, shared details of the botched procedure in an Instagram video, captioning it with a warning: 'You can’t trust anyone!!!'.
- Spears explained that her eyelid remained drooped for four weeks before it began to return to its normal state.
- She cautioned her fans to 'be careful' with Botox and other cosmetic treatments, warning that doctors 'can really f*** your eyes up' and 'try to change your face'.
- The singer urged individuals to be protective of their bodies, emphasising that 'it’s yours and you own it'.
