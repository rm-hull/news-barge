This is Zach Bowes, and he’s wanted by police.

The 29-year-old is wanted for recall to prison after breaching the terms of his prison licence.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Officers have carried out extensive enquiries to find him and are now appealing to members of the public to report sightings of him.

“They have also warned anyone who is harbouring Bowes may be committing a criminal offence.”

Police ask anyone who has an immediate sighting of him or knows where he is now to call them on 999.

If you have any other information, you’re asked to ring 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference 12260151505.
