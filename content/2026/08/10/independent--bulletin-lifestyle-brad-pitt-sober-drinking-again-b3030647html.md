# Oscar-winning actor admits he’s ‘off the wagon’ after seven years of sobriety

- Brad Pitt revealed he has resumed drinking in moderation after remaining completely sober for seven years.
- Speaking to *Esquire* , the 62-year-old actor stated he now drinks in a more restrained manner and must remain professional about his alcohol intake. “No, I got—I was sober for seven years. And then I got back off the wagon.”
- Pitt originally stopped drinking in 2016 following his highly publicized split from Angelina Jolie.
- The Oscar winner previously credited Alcoholics Anonymous meetings and fellow actor Bradley Cooper with helping him achieve sobriety.
- In the same interview, Pitt disclosed that he once experienced suicidal thoughts while navigating painful family difficulties.
