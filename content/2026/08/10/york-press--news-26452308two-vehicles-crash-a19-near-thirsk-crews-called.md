Two vehicles have crashed on a main road in North Yorkshire.

Firefighters and police were called to the scene on the A19 at Bridforth near Thirsk shortly before 11.10am on Monday (August 10).

Everyone was out of the vehicles when the fire crew arrived, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

A service spokesperson said the crew made the scene safe then moved one of the vehicles to a safe location.

“The incident was left with the police and recovery company,” they said.
