# Cruise ship comes to aid of stranded boat after Zuckerberg yacht ‘fails to respond’

![Mark Zuckerberg is the owner of Launchpad, but was not on board at the time](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/30/13/2026-07-29T023335Z_1586007670_RC2KAMASR3WO_RTRMADP_3_USA-CHINA-AI-META.JPG)

- Mark Zuckerberg's $300m mega yacht Launchpad reportedly failed to respond to a Coast Guard request to assist a stranded boat in Alaska.
- The cruise ship Wilderness Legacy came to the aid of the 21-foot skiff, which had run out of fuel near Farragut Bay on 1 August, according to the *Alaska Beacon**.*
- A passenger on the cruise ship claimed that Launchpad was closer to the stranded vessel and that passengers booed upon hearing it did not respond.
- A spokesperson for Zuckerberg confirmed that neither the Meta CEO nor his family were on board the 387ft vessel during the incident.
- The spokesperson said that the yacht crew was operating on a different radio channel and that assistance was already underway by the time they noticed the broadcast.
