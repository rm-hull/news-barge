# Russia in fresh move to overturn international athletics ban amid Ukraine war

![Boris Yaryshevskiy labelled the sanctions 'unprecedented'](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/13/2026-07-29T124109Z_1059727649_RC2PJMAHEAOB_RTRMADP_3_ATHLETICS-RUSSIA.JPG)

- Russian Athletics has submitted a fresh legal appeal to the Court of Arbitration for Sport (Cas) in Lausanne, challenging the ongoing sanctions imposed by World Athletics following the 2022 invasion of Ukraine.
- The appeal seeks to overturn the complete ban on Russian and Belarusian athletes and restore the federation's organisational rights, which currently prevent its involvement in World Athletics decision-making and international development programmes.
- This legal action follows a prior appeal filed on 9 July, which directly contested the World Athletics Council's decision on 3 July to extend the total exclusion of Russian and Belarusian competitors from global events.
- Boris Yaryshevskiy, the chief executive director of Russian Athletics, described the restrictions as “unprecedented”, stating that they hold back the development of Russian athletics and severely hinder young athletes.
- World Athletics remains one of the most stringent international sports governing bodies regarding Russia, maintaining an absolute ban while some other sports allow limited participation under a neutral banner.
