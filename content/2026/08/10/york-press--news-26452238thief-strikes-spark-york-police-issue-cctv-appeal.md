A thief has struck at a popular venue in York.

North Yorkshire Police has issued a photograph of a person officers want to speak to after the theft at Spark in Piccadilly at 5.30pm on Thursday, June 25.

The force on Monday (August 10) issued the photograph, saying the person pictured may have information about the incident.

Anyone who recognises the person is asked to phone 101 or email [Dom.Tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Dom.Tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting reference 12260118564.
