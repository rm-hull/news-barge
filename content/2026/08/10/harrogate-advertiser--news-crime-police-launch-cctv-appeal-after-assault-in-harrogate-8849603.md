# Police launch CCTV appeal after assault in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police have released a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to, following a assault in Harrogate.

The incident occurred at approximately 5:10pm on June 28 on Knaresborough Road in Harrogate.

![Police have released this still image, taken from CCTV footage of person they would like to speak to, following a assault on Knaresborough Road, Harrogate.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjY2MGQwNTQwLTU3ODMtNGNkNi1hZTc1LTU1MWJlZjAyNzFmMTo0ZDQyYWNiNy02NWY3LTQ2YzUtYTI2My0yMzRkYTA2ZTM1NGI=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



A police spokesperson said: “It involved a female assaulting another person, striking them on the shoulder and throwing a metal drink bottle at them.

“Email: [email protected] if you recognise the person pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.

“Alternatively, you can call North Yorkshire Police on 101, or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

“Quote reference 12260118491 when passing on information.”
