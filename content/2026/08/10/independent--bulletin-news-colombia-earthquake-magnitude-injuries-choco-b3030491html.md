# Multiple people injured as 7.4-magnitude earthquake strikes Colombia

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/R9pbSuv1/poster.jpg?width=720)

- A 7.4-magnitude earthquake has struck the Pacific province of Choco in western Colombia at a depth of 79 km.
- The epicentre was located near San Jose del Palmar, with tremors felt as far away as Bogota and neighbouring Venezuela.
- Multiple injuries and significant structural damage to buildings have been reported in the province's capital, Quibdo.
- Governor Nubia Carolina Cordoba-Curi warned of potential aftershocks as emergency officials assess the full impact.
- Juan Diego Patino, the governor of neighboring Risaralda province, said that Pereira, the regional capital, suffered "serious damage to some buildings", with many facades destroyed.
