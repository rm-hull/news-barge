Car enthusiasts are in for a treat as prized performance vehicles gear up to gather in North Yorkshire this weekend.

The free-to-attend Supercar Day will take place at The Motorist, New Lennerton Lane, Sherburn in Elmet, on Saturday, August 15.

Visitors can expect to see a range of luxury cars such as Ferraris, Lamborghinis, McLarens, Porsches, Aston Martins and Audi R8s.

  Performance cars at a previous event at The Motorist. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The day is a must-attend fixture in the calendar for petrolheads and will give people the chance to get up close with the cars, hear the distinct sound of V10s and V12s, take photographs and speak to the owners.

The Motorist hosts a variety of crowd-pulling events throughout the year including car and bike meets.

Arvelo, a luxury driving travel specialist, will also be attending this weekend's event.

As well as the vehicles on show, people will also be able to enjoy The Motorist's facilities such as the Drivers Lounge, which offers speciality coffee, breakfast and lunch.

Gates for the event will open at 9am, and people are recommended to arrive early.

Dogs are welcome at the event.

  A spokesperson for the venue said: “Whether you’re arriving in a supercar or simply coming to admire them, Supercar Day is the perfect way to spend a Saturday morning.

  

  "Supercar Day is free to attend, with no booking required. Gates open from 9am, so arrive early, enjoy the atmosphere and experience one of Yorkshire’s best supercar events.

  

  "If you own a supercar or represent a supercar club, we’d love to have you join us for another unforgettable Supercar Day at The Motorist."
