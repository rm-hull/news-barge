Face it, we all have paperwork that we need to destroy. Whether it’s because you’ve made some incriminating “mistakes” in political office, you need to rustle up some packing material to send a parcel via a low-cost courier service, or it’s just taking up space and you need to ensure you can recycle-bin it securely, the best way to rid yourself of problem paper is, of course, by buying yourself one of the UK’s best paper shredders. Or fire; but that can bring its own problems.

Available in three distinct and largely self-explanatory flavours, Strip Cut (strips), Cross Cut (confetti), and Micro Cut (basically granules), each type comes offering security on one of seven levels, from P-1 and P-2 for innocuous day-to-day waste and general non-sensitive stuff, respectively, to P-3 and P-4 for personal correspondence and bank statements etc, respectively, all the way up to P-6 and P-7 for legal documents and government/military/classified bumf, also respectively.

So, for home use and the sake of your bank account, you’ll probably have no need to buy above P-4. Which is what we have here, the very best of the first four Ps paper shredders for destroying docs at home. Let’s tear it up…

At more than £100, the Powershred LX70 from Fellowes isn’t the cheapest shredder around, but this P-4 powerhouse can reduce a stated 11 sheets of A4 simultaneously to 4 x 40mm nothingness at around three metres per minute, over its five-minute duty cycle.

Now, while this a relatively low runtime, it does cool-down in just half an hour, so you’ll be back in business in next to no time.

![Best shredder IndyBest review Fellowes Powershred LX70](<[https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/10/02/Best-shredder-IndyBest-review-Fellowes-Powershred-LX70.png?quality=75&width=608&auto=webp](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/10/02/Best-shredder-IndyBest-review-Fellowes-Powershred-LX70.png?quality=75&width=608&auto=webp) 608w>)

Featuring an 18l bin, there’s no continuous auto-feed and I found the 11 sheets per pass to be a bit on the optimistic side, but able to handle paperclips, staples and credit cards too, this is a highly efficient paper shredder.
