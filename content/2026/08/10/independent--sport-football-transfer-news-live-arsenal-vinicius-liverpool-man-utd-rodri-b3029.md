Arsenal have moved on from their chase of Vinicius Jr after he signed a contract extension with Real Madrid and the Gunners have now reignited their interest in Atletico Madrid’s Julian Alvarez, while PSG’s Bradley Barcola is also on their list.

Meanwhile, Rodri looks set to leave Manchester City amid interest from Barcelona and Real Madrid. The World Cup winning captain is into the final year of his contract and favours a move to Barca – who are considering a second bid after an initial offer of £38m was rejected.

Liverpool, meanwhile, also want to sign Barcola from Paris Saint-Germain and have lined up his teammate Ibrahim Mbaye in what could be a double swoop for Andoni Iraola’s side, while the Reds also look to have secured much-needed defender help with ex-Barcelona captain Ronald Araujo set for a loan deal with an option to buy.

## Palace getting busy

As well as potentially signing Dwight McNeil in a swap deal, Crystal Palace are interested in Monaco midfielder Lamine Camara and Torino winger Alieu Njie, according to *Sky Sports*.

## Youssef Chermiti stretchered off amid Galatasaray interest

News emerged before Rangers’ clash with Hibernian on Sunday that Galatasaray had seen a bid rejected for striker Youssef Chermiti, with the Gers unwilling to sell.

However, the Turkish side’s pursuit of Chermiti may now be over after he was stretchered off in that game.

## Man Utd working over deal for EFL Apprentice of the Year

Manchester United are working on a deal to sign Leicester City starlet Louis Page, according to *BBC Sport*.

Page, an 18-year-old midfielder, was named the EFL Apprentice of the Year last term and is an England U20 international.

## Hull announce seventh signing of summer

Sweden wing-back Elliot Stroud has joined Hull from Mjallby for an undisclosed fee on a four-year contract.

## Shea Charles set for Fulham medical

Southampton midfielder Shea Charles is set to undergo a medical at Fulham after the clubs agreed a deal worth up to £30m, according to the *Press Association*.

The 22-year-old is expected to sign a five-year contract at Craven Cottage and become the most expensive Northern Irish footballer.

Fulham will pay an initial fee of £26m, while the transfer includes a further £4m in performance-related add-ons.

## Ipswich bid for Exequiel Palacios

Ipswich Town have sent in a bid for Exequiel Palacios, according to *Fabrizio Romano.*

The Argentinian, who is among their key targets, is also being pursued by four other clubs in the Premier League and Bundesliga.

## Another big name set for Turkey move

Mohamed Salah’s switch to Trabzonspor may have knocked over the first domino of big names making moves to Turkey this summer.

That’s because now, Fenerbahce are set to finalise a deal to sign Belgium striker Romelu Lukaku from Napoli, as per *Gazzetta dello Sport.*

Chelsea will receive a sell-on fee.

## Another Vinicius alternative?

Arsenal are back exploring the market for a winger after their move for Vinicius Jr didn’t come to fruition.

And according to the *Mirror*, Juventus star Kenan Yildiz is being eyed up.

## The problem Liverpool must address after Andoni Iraola’s losing Anfield bow draws Arne Slot parallels

When Anfield had last hosted Liverpool, as Mohamed Salah and Andy Robertson bade farewell and their teammates embarked on their final lap of honour, Arne Slot had remained sat in the dugout. It proved the last sighting of him in Liverpool. Some 11 weeks later, Slot’s successor was a visible presence on the edge of the technical area, Andoni Iraola’s constant clapping encouraging his players at the start of a new era. It began with echoes of the old: a 3-2 defeat to Monaco, with a lead lost at Anfield, with a goal conceded to a set-piece, had some similarities with Slot’s unravelling, even if the stakes were lower.

Iraola may hope the parallels with last season end when the competitive football starts. As it was, Liverpool outlined why they require Ronald Araujo and how their recruitment is unfinished.

## Bruno Guimaraes ready to be a 'warrior' for Arsenal

"I feel amazing,” Bruno Guimaraes said after signing for Arsenal.

“ I'm glad for the opportunity. Since the first time I spoke to [Andrea] Berta and Mikel [Arteta], I was excited. I just felt inside of me that I need a new challenge in my life and I think to be an Arsenal player is going to be an exciting challenge in my life.

"I'm so excited to join you. I will give you my best, I promise. I'm your warrior, I'll never give up. Hopefully we can create many, many good memories together."
