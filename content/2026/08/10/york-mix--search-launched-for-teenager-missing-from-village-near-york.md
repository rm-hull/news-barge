This is Sophia, who’s 15 and from Huby north of York.

She’s gone missing and police say they are “becoming increasingly concerned for her safety”.

They’re appealing for people to be on the lookout for her.

Sophia was last seen at home at around 4.30pm on Friday (7 August).

She’s described as

- around 4ft 6in tall
- of slim build
- with black hair
- and may be wearing long hair extensions.

When she was last seen she was wearing blue jeans and an animal print halter-neck top.

Sophia also has a tattoo of a turtle on her left arm.

If you have seen Sophia or know where she is now, police urge you to call 999. If you have any other information that could help them find her please call 101 and select option 1 to speak to the control room.
