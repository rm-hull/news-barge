# Police desperately searching for missing girls Angelica and Verity

![Angelica, left, and Verity have gone missing in Warwickshire](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/8/38/Missing-girls.jpeg)

- Police are desperately searching for two girls who were reported missing on Saturday.
- Angelica, 11, and Verity, 12, were last spotted around 4am on Sunday in the Newbold area of Rugby.
- Officers are “growing increasingly concerned for their welfare”, Rugby Police said.
- The girls, who are believed to be together, have links to both Rugby and Cornwall.
- Anyone who sees them or has information as to their whereabouts is asked to call 999 immediately, quoting incident number 123 of 8 August.
