# UK minister pledges to build ‘more ambitious’ relationship with EU

- The UK’s top European Union negotiator Hamish Falconer has pledged to build a “deeper” and “more ambitious” relationship with the EU, with a particular focus on services, security and tech.
- The government maintains it remains committed to its manifesto red lines, which rule out rejoining the single market or customs union, as well as restoring free movement.
- Former Conservative MP Nick Boles has reportedly presented a new proposal for Britain to rejoin the EU single market.
- The proposal suggests extending the protection of the UK's armed forces and nuclear deterrent across Europe in exchange for access to the free-trade zone.
- The plan, reportedly seen by top government officials, advocates creating a European confederation that links single market access with Nato defence commitments.
