Postcode Lottery is a subscription-based lottery which raises money for charities.

Players not only are in the chance to win a cash prize but also help donate to good causes.

The chance of winning any prize is better than one in four, according to the Postcode Lottery.

But the chance of winning their biggest prizes, including Postcode Millions, Millionaire Street and their £250,000 prize is one in 250,000.

The chance of winning a £1,000 prize is one in 2,000.

In comparison, the odds of winning the jackpot in the Lotto is about one in 22 million and the Euromillions odds are about one in 139 million.

Each ticket that is purchased in the Postcode Lottery is allocated a ticket number made up of the buyer's full postcode and a unique number.

Every person that buys a ticket in the same postcode will be allocated a unique number and if that postcode gets lucky every player on that street wins £1,000.

Only postcodes that are entered for £12.50 a month are able to win, the more people on a street that enter the more likely they are to take home £1,000.
