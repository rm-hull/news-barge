# Trump in new security scare as fighter jets scramble over New Jersey golf club

- F-16 fighter jets were scrambled to escort two civilian aircraft out of restricted airspace near President Donald Trump's golf club in Bedminster, New Jersey.
- A third aircraft also breached the temporary flight restrictions on Sunday but complied before fighter jets needed to intervene.
- The airspace breaches delayed Donald Trump's return journey to Washington, D.C., by around 13 minutes.
- Following the incident, defense officials reminded pilots to check notices to airmen before every flight.
- The event comes amid heightened security around Trump following a series of recent threats and high-profile security incidents.
