# Greenland on high alert after Trump-aligned oil company brings unapproved drilling equipment

- Greenland's government issued a strong warning to Texas-based Greenland Energy for shipping drilling equipment ashore without valid approval.
- Greenland Energy aims to invest $60 million in exploratory drilling in Jameson Land, believing the basin contains up to $1 trillion in crude oil.
- Territorial authorities confirmed that the company's previous landing permit had expired and warned that all future logistics require prior clearance.
- The incident comes amid President Donald Trump's continued push to gain control of Greenland for U.S. national security purposes.
- Greenland Energy stated its goals are unrelated to American annexation and hopes to receive final approvals to begin drilling in October.
