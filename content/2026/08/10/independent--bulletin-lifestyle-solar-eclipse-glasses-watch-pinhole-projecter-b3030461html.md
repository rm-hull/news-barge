# How to watch the solar eclipse if you don’t have official glasses

You should never look at the sun directly – but there are other ways to watch the solar eclipse on August 12

- **Make a pinhole viewer:** Cut a 1-2-inch hole in a piece of card, stick aluminum foil over it and use a pin to poke a hole in the foil. Place a second piece of card on the ground and hold your pinhole viewer above to project the sun’s image onto it.
- **Use a colander** : Hold it up to the sun and look at its shadow for a makeshift pinhole viewer.
- **Look between the leaves:** The shadow cast by the gaps between foliage can also act as natural pinhole projector.
- **Use a solar telescope:** You can check with your local observatory if they’re running an eclipse viewing event. It’s not safe to use a regular telescope.
- **Watch the livestream:** The Royal Observatory will be livestreaming the solar eclipse.
