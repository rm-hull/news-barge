# Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez opens up about personal decision in candid video

- New York Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez revealed she is freezing her eggs, sharing a live video on Instagram where she administered a hormone injection.
- She stated her decision to go public was to educate women about their bodies and reproductive choices, noting a systemic lack of education on these topics.
- Ocasio-Cortez framed her choice as a way to "take our power back" in a political environment she believes is dedicated to politicizing women's bodies and restricting reproductive rights.
- She highlighted the double standards faced by female politicians regarding family planning and age, contrasting it with the experiences of men in public life.
- The announcement received varied reactions on social media, with some praising her transparency and bravery, while others criticized her decision in light of her advocacy for abortion rights.
