Police are searching for a North Yorkshire man wanted on recall to prison.

Zach Bowes, 29, from Filey, has been recalled to jail after breaching the terms of his prison licence, North Yorkshire Police said.

The force said officers have “carried out extensive enquiries to find him and are now appealing to members of the public to report sightings of him”.

It also warned that “anyone who is harbouring Bowes may be committing a criminal offence”.

Police are urging anyone sees Bowes or knows where he is to phone 999.

Those with any other information that could help find him are asked to dial 101.

Information can be passed anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Quote reference 12260151505 when providing information.
