# Olivia Attwood opens up about ‘unexpected’ Pete Wicks romance

- Olivia Attwood has spoken candidly about her unexpected romance with her Kiss FM co-host Pete Wicks.
- The former Love Island contestant separated from footballer Bradley Dack earlier this year before confirming her relationship with Wicks in June.
- Attwood revealed that her relationship with former TOWIE star Wicks quickly moved from platonic to romantic, describing him as compassionate and patient.
- She dismissed advice from others to remain single, stating it would be pointless to date other people when she already shares a strong connection with Wicks.
- The presenter also reflected on family plans, stating she is at peace with whether having children forms part of her future.
