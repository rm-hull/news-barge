### Average American pays $2,400 in taxes to fund Trump’s deportation push, analysis finds

# Staggering amount each American is estimated to have paid in taxes for Trump’s deportation push

- An analysis by the Economic Policy Institute reveals that President Donald Trump's $268.9 billion deportation effort costs average American taxpayers nearly $2,400 each.
- The institute stated the diverted funds could instead support essential public services, such as hiring teachers, expanding Medicaid, and funding public housing.
- In response, the Department of Homeland Security defended the policy, arguing that incentives for self-deportation ultimately save taxpayers money.
- Immigration and Customs Enforcement has deported more than 350,000 people in fiscal year 2026, with plans to deport one million individuals annually over the next two years.
- The deportation campaign is funded through the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, standard congressional appropriations, and reallocated federal money.
