Celebrated chef Steph Moon has teamed up with Yorkshire’s finest producers to create a brilliant banquet that reflects eight decades of food trends and says Happy Birthday to us!

Join the Yorkshire Life team and some familiar faces as we take over the Food Hall at Fodder, Harrogate, on Friday, September 25, for a unique banquet that celebrates Yorkshire Life’s milestone 80th year and the county’s fabulous food heritage.

  It promises to be a fabulous night celebrating the best of Yorkshire, so do join us. It’s £90 per ticket including a glass of wine per person - **click here to book.**

Kathryn Armstrong,

Editor, Yorkshire Life

  Steph Moon


  **A YORKSHIRE BANQUET**

1970s: Yorkshire Cham. A nod to the most fashionable drink of the 1970s, the iconic, perry Babycham. Toast us with Erishskroy Westow’s English sparkling white wine.

  **Canapés**

1950s: Potted Whitby Crab – a favourite in the decade that the Great Yorkshire Showground was purchased.

1980s: Shepherds Purse Mini Parcel – the decade this iconic Thirsk-based cheese company was first established by Judy Bell MBE.

2000s: Fodder Wagyu and Sheffield Forge Cheese Burger – the era of the gourmet burger arrives.

  **Starter**

1960s: Fanny Craddock’s Prawn Cocktail – a retro staple that stands the test of time in British cuisine. Prawns, baby gem lettuce, Marie Rose sauce, YO Bakehouse brown bread and Longley Farm butter.

  **Main Course**

1990s: Taste Tradition Yorkshire Beef & Herb-Fed Chicken – the traditional British roast became a sharing platter staple, a mix and match of meats with colourful, playful veg. It couldn’t be a food celebration without Yorkshire pudding and Fodder’s are benchmark.

Add to the platter beef fat potatoes, Denholme honey carrots, Fountains Gold cauliflower cheese, Longley Farm butter braised leeks, beef- dripping onions and ‘aaah’ gravy.

  **Dessert**

1940s: Chef Richard’s Very Yorkshire Sticky Toffee Pudding. A dessert created post-World War Two, when indulgent, comforting desserts were popular.

Filey Bay Whisky, Farrah’s Harrogate Toffee, Ian Taylor free-range eggs, Longley Farm butter, Dales Dairies cream and Yorvale Ice Cream.

  **To round it off**

Coffee Brothers Coffee and Yorkshire Treats.

PLUS: All guests will receive a foodie goodie bag, which includes a Fodder discount voucher and the event will feature in the pages of Yorkshire Life.
