# Rod Stewart a grandfather for the sixth time after daughter Ruby has a son

- Rod Stewart's daughter, Ruby, has given birth to her second child, a son named Levi Rhodes Kalick, making the singer a grandfather for the sixth time.
- Ruby announced the birth on Instagram, sharing photos of her newborn and his older brother, Otis, meeting for the first time.
- Levi Rhodes Kalick was born on 8 April 2026, weighing 9lbs, and his name signifies “my heart”.
- Rod Stewart's ex-wife Rachel Hunter and current wife Penny Lancaster both offered congratulations on Ruby's social media post.
- The article details Rod Stewart's extensive family, including his eight children from five mothers and his other grandchildren.
