# Salt Path star says press tour will be ‘interesting’ amid controversy

- Actor Jason Isaacs has addressed the controversy surrounding *The Salt Path* for the first time ahead of the film's US release on 4 September.
- An *Observer* investigation alleged that author Raynor Winn lied about key aspects of her life story in the best-selling 2018 memoir on which the film is based.
- Raynor Winn has strongly adenied the allegations, calling the investigation grotesquely unfair and maintaining that the memoir is a true story.
- Jason Isaacs, who stars alongside Gillian Anderson, confirmed the cast will still do press for the film, calling it an interesting experience.
- The movie was a hit in the UK earning £7.6m, though publication of Winn's next book has been delayed to January 2028 following the scandal.
