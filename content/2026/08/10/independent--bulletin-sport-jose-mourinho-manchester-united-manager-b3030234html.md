# Jose Mourinho turned down chance to succeed Sir Alex Ferguson at Manchester United

- Jose Mourinho has revealed in a new Netflix documentary that he signed a contract to become Manchester United manager in 2013, succeeding Sir Alex Ferguson.
- Despite the agreement, Mourinho tearfully reneged on the deal, explaining to Ferguson that he had given his word to Chelsea and felt unable to break it.
- Mourinho chose to return to Chelsea for a second spell, stating he made the decision 'with his heart' due to his strong affection for the club.
- Sir Alex Ferguson confirmed the phone call, expressing his disappointment at the time but understanding Mourinho's commitment to Chelsea.
- Mourinho eventually managed Manchester United from 2016, but his 2013 decision meant he missed the immediate opportunity to take over from Ferguson, a period during which the club struggled to maintain its previous dominance.
