# Anne Hathaway diplomatically addresses her box office flop of the year

- Anne Hathaway has enjoyed a landmark year at the box office in 2026, starring in two massive global hits, *The Odyssey* and*The Devil Wears Prada 2.* 
- *The Odyssey* passed $1.1 billion globally to become Sir Christopher Nolan’s highest-grossing film, while*The Devil Wears Prada 2* achieved over $691 million.
- However, Hathaway’s third 2026 release, the musical drama *Mother Mary* , failed at the box office, grossing just $3 million against its $20 million budget.
- She diplomatically acknowledged her mixed year in a red carpet interview, saying: “Two movies have done unbelievably well and one movie… was very well respected.”
- The actor is currently promoting her next film, sci-fi thriller *The End of Oak Street* co-starring Ewan McGregor, which arrives in cinemas on 14 August.
