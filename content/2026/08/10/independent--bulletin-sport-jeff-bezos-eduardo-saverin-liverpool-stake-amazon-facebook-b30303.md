# Jeff Bezos and Facebook co-founder set to acquire stake in Liverpool

![Bezos is part of a consortium that is set to acquire a stake in Liverpool](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/11/2026-07-23T100045Z_749033495_RC20SDADGAEY_RTRMADP_3_AMAZON-COM-PRIMEVIDEO.JPG)

- Amazon founder Jeff Bezos is reportedly part of an investment consortium that is nearing a deal to purchase a minority stake of roughly one-third in Premier League football club Liverpool.
- According to Sky News, the syndicate is being led by British-Indian millionaire Amit Bhatia, the former co-owner of Queens Park Rangers, and also includes Facebook co-founder Eduardo Saverin.
- Liverpool owners Fenway Sports Group confirmed last month that they had been approached regarding a strategic minority investment, with an official announcement expected in the coming days.
- Liverpool Football Club is currently valued at around £4.5 billion, representing a substantial increase since Fenway Sports Group acquired the team for approximately £300 million in 2010.
- Bezos, who is the world’s fourth-richest man with an estimated fortune of $257 billion, previously explored buying American NFL franchises before joining this consortium.
