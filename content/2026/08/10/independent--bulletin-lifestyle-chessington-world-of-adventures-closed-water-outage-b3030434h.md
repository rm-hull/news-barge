# Why Chessington World of Adventures has been forced to close – everything we know

![Chessington World of Adventures has been forced to close for the day](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/13/2026-08-10T120515Z_618992516_RC2NVMAZEUE4_RTRMADP_3_BRITAIN-WATER.JPG)

- Chessington World of Adventures has closed for the day during the summer holidays due to a local water outage.
- Customers have been told they can return for free on “selected dates” within nine months under the Return Promise. Returning requires customers to pre-book a reservation ticket.
- A statement from the park on its website read: “We apologise for the inconvenience and thank you for your understanding**.”**
- Thames Water reported a burst water main on Leatherhead Road which appeared to be behind the water outage, according to a statement issued on the water company’s website.
- It comes as parts of England face ongoing drought conditions and hosepipe bans.
