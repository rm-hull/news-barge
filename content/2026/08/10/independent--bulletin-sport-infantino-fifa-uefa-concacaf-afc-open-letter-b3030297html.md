# Infantino slammed in open letter signed by three confederations as football’s civil war takes fresh turn

![Infantino is under mounting pressure](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/10/01KZGSCJNDZ1BJF08C24CZ91N4.jpg)

- Uefa, Concacaf, and the Asian Football Confederation (AFC) have accused Fifa president Gianni Infantino of “deception” and called for his resignation.
- The three governing bodies, representing over half of Fifa's members, have held preliminary talks about creating their own competitions if they decide to boycott Fifa events.
- In an open letter, confederation leaders demanded an independent review into Infantino's attempt to sell a stake in the World Cup to private investors.
- The signatories criticised Infantino for a profound “failure of judgment” and accused him of attempting to limit scrutiny by avoiding proper consultation.
- Fifa claimed over the weekend that there is a concerted effort to undermine the organisation and its president, who still enjoys support from the African Football Confederation.
