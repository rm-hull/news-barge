# Nigel Farage to face 33 rival candidates in the Clacton by-election

- Nigel Farage is set to face an unprecedented 33 rival candidates in the Clacton by-election, which he initiated in protest against scrutiny of his financial affairs.
- The main Westminster parties, including Labour, the Conservatives, and the Liberal Democrats, have opted to boycott the by-election, dismissing it as a “stunt”.
- Tendring District Council confirmed a total of 34 candidates will stand in the 13 August by-election, believed to be a record for any parliamentary contest.
- Prominent challengers to Farage include satirical candidate Count Binface and actor-turned-politician Laurence Fox.
- Farage's vision of a “people versus the establishment” showdown has resulted in a diverse field, including three candidates from the Official Monster Raving Loony Party and numerous independents.
