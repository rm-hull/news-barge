A man was hurt after being threatened at knifepoint in Knaresborough.

It happened at about 10.30pm on Friday (7 August) near The Lido on Wetherby Road.

The victim was approached by three unknown men and threatened with a knife.

#### Incident location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The victim bravely challenged the suspects and sustained minor injuries in the process, he later attended hospital for minor injuries following the incident. The suspect fled the scene empty-handed.

“We’re particularly appealing for information about CCTV which may have captured the incident, or any eye-witnesses.”
