Three strangers threatened a man with a knife, police have warned.

North Yorkshire Police is now appealing for information following the attempted robbery in Knaresborough.

The county's force said the incident happened in Wetherby Road around 10.30pm on Friday, August 7, close to The Lido.

Police said a man was approached by three unknown men and was threatened with a knife.

  



A force spokesperson said: "The victim challenged the suspects and sustained minor injuries in the process.

"He later attended hospital for minor injuries following the incident.

"The suspect fled the scene empty-handed."

North Yorkshire Police said it is appealing for information including CCTV recordings which may have captured the incident, or for any eyewitnesses to come forward.

The spokesperson said: "If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance:

"Email Elizabeth Parry at [elizabeth.parry@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:elizabeth.parry@northyorkshire.police.uk), or call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

"If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

"Please quote reference 12260153117 when providing information."
