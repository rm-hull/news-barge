# Jeremy Clarkson on the impact UK heatwave has had on Diddly Squat farm

- TV presenter Jeremy Clarkson has warned that the UK heatwave is damaging harvests at his 1,000-acre Diddly Squat farm in Oxfordshire.
- Temperatures across the country could reach 36C or higher, with the Met Office considering issuing extreme heat warnings.
- Clarkson recently stated that making a profit from growing food has become impossible, leading him to leave 200 acres of farmland bare to prevent losses.
- The presenter continues to engage with agricultural policy, having previously campaigned against planning restrictions and proposed inheritance tax changes for farmers.
- Clarkson recently revealed he underwent successful treatment for an aggressive form of cancer caught at an early stage.
