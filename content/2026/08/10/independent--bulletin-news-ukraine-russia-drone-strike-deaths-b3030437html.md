# Period of mourning declared in Russian region as Ukrainian drone strike kills 13

- A Ukrainian drone attack has struck targets up to 680 miles inside Russia, hitting industrial infrastructure and an oil refinery in the Tatarstan region.
- Russian authorities reported that at least 13 people, including one child, were killed and over 75 others injured during the strikes.
- Ukraine confirmed targeting the Taneco oil refinery, while a period of mourning was declared by local authorities in Nizhnekamsk.
- Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky claimed North Korea is preparing to deploy up to 50,000 troops to bolster Russia's military operations.
- UK Defence Secretary Wes Streeting urged Western allies to enhance Ukraine's air defences to protect against Russian attacks over the winter.
