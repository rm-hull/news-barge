# Five reasons why Ebola outbreak has been hard to contain as WHO makes new revelation

![Confirmed cases are on the rise at an alarming rate](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/05/10/2026-07-31T085509Z_1633840064_RC2ODLA7HHKL_RTRMADP_3_HEALTH-EBOLA.JPG)

- WHO regional director for Africa Dr Mohamed Yakub Janabi has explained that genetic sequencing revealed that the Ebola outbreak in eastern Congo actually began in February, months before its official declaration in mid-May.
- Confirmed cases have reached 4,200 with over 1,900 deaths, making it the fastest-killing Ebola outbreak on record.
- Initial response efforts were delayed after early cases were misdiagnosed as malaria or typhoid and tested for a different Ebola strain.
- The outbreak is caused by the rare Bundibugyo strain of the virus, for which there are currently no approved vaccines or treatments.
- Containment efforts are severely disrupted by rebel conflict, health worker strikes, gear shortages, and widespread misinformation.
