# Verizon service restored after vandals caused mass mobile and internet outage

![Verizon service was restored after an outage Sunday caused by vandals who cut fiber cables](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/15/26/GettyImages-2287531505.jpg)

- Verizon users in Southern California endured an outage Sunday after vandals cut multiple fiber cables, prompting an investigation involving law enforcement.
- More than 12,000 outage reports were submitted to the Downdetector tracking website during the peak of the incident on Sunday afternoon.
- “Vandals cut multiple fiber cables, causing a service interruption for some mobile and home internet customers in Southern California earlier Sunday," a Verizon spokesperson said. “Acts of network theft and vandalism represent a direct threat to the safety and security of our communities.”
- Mobile and home internet services were down for several hours but were restored by 7:30 p.m.
- Specific details about the exact locations of the damaged cables, the total number of impacted customers, and the specific law enforcement agencies involved have not yet been released.
