# Where to go swimming in London – five pools where you can escape the heat

With temperatures potentially reaching up to 36C this week, here are five pools you can escape to...

1. **LondonFields Lido** in Hackney features a 50m outdoor pool with a sundeck and sunbathing area.
2. **Brockwell Park Lido** in Herne Hill provides a 50m outdoor pool – next to a Art Deco building – and a poolside pizzeria.
3. **Hampstead HeathSwimmingPonds** offer a natural bathing experience with three separate ponds.
4. **The London Aquatics Centre** in Stratford, designed by Zaha Hadid, was where athletes competed during the 2012 Olympics.
5.  **Finchley Lido Leisure Centre** caters to families with wave machines and water cannons.
