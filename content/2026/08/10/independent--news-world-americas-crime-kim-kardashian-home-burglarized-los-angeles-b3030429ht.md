A man was arrested on suspicion of burglarizing one of Kim Kardashian’s Los Angeles homes — and was seen trying to drive away in one of her cars, according to a report.

Authorities were called to Kardashian’s home in Hidden Hills, an upscale gated community in Los Angeles, just before 4 p.m. Sunday, the *Los Angeles Times* reported. 

A man, who authorities did not publicly name, was seen loading some of Kardashian’s belongings into a vehicle on the property, Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Deputy Tracy Koerner said.

He was then seen trying to drive away from the property in one of her vehicles, NBC News reported.

When authorities arrived at the home, they found the man inside. He was taken into custody without incident.

The suspect was booked into LA County Jail on suspicion of burglary, Koerner said.

No one was home at the time of the incident. A law enforcement source told the *Times* that Kardashian and her children are living elsewhere as the property undergoes renovations.

Despite the work, some of Kardashian’s cars were still at the home. It was not immediately clear what else the burglar attempted to steal.

Kardashian and her ex-husband, rapper Kanye West, bought the massive, multimillion-dollar home in 2014.

The 15,667-square-foot home has eight bedrooms and 10 bathrooms, as well as a home theater, game room with a wet bar, a library and a gym, according to the report.

Kardashian owns several adjacent properties in the affluent gated community.
