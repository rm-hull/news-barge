# Former Thai politician apologises for shooting official in money row

![Rescue workers perform CPR on Thongchai Yenprasert, president of the Nonthaburi Provincial Administrative Organization](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/08/2026-08-10T071948Z_168623363_RC2JVMAXH7EB_RTRMADP_3_THAILAND-SHOOTING.JPG)

- A former Thai MP has claimed responsibility for a shooting at a local government office in Nonthaburi province.
- Two people, a senior provincial official and his driver, were seriously injured in the attack outside the provincial office's building.
- The alleged shooter, former politician Chalong Reawraeng, has been arrested by police.
- His alleged target was Thongchai Yenprasert, president of the Nonthaburi Provincial Administrative Organisation.
- Reawraeng apologised to the family and friends of Mr Yenprasert in a press conference from the police station after he was arrested, calling him a “good friend” but saying he was angry with him for money-related issues.
