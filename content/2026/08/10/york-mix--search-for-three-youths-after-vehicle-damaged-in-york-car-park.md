Police want to track down a group of youths after a vehicle was damaged in a York café car park.

It was scratched while parked near the Starbucks branch in the Foss Islands Retail Park at about 2pm on Friday 31 July.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A group of youths may have been in the area around the time, three boys aged between 12 and 15, all wearing tracksuit type clothing were on bikes.

#### Incident location

“We’re particularly appealing for information if anyone has seen the youths or witnessed any part of this incident.”
