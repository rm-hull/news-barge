Fire crews put out another fire started deliberately in York overnight.

They were called to the incident near to **Fieldside Place**– which backs onto the St Nicks Nature Reserve – at** 6.38pm**. 

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The Acomb crew attended a small fire consisting of paper and wood and used a knapsack sprayer to extinguish the fire.

#### Fieldside Place, York

“The cause of the fire is believed to be deliberate.”
