# Trump unveils new vaccine recommendations for children in ‘gold standard’ executive order

![Trump signs an executive order calling for more research and flexibility on vaccines](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/19/25/GettyImages-2289375867.jpg)

- President Donald Trump has signed an executive order titled Gold Standard Childhood Vaccine Recommendations that alters federal guidance for childhood vaccines and urges states to reconsider school immunization requirements.
- The new guidance categorizes childhood vaccines into three distinct tiers, reducing universally recommended immunizations to 11 core vaccines while removing COVID-19, influenza, and Hepatitis B from general recommendation.
- Trump called for breaking up the combined Measles, Mumps, and Rubella (MMR) vaccine into three separate single-dose shots, despite single-dose MMR components not currently being available on the market.
- During his announcement, Trump regurgitated discredited theories linking childhood vaccines to autism spectrum disorder, ignoring extensive scientific research that has debunked any connection.
- The executive order notes that parents may still choose to administer non-core vaccines to their children with insurance coverage, while formally advising states to review and potentially update their immunization laws.
