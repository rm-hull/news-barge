A THIEF has hit a North Yorkshire hairdressers.

The burglary happened in Rythergate in Cawood at 4am on Monday (August 3).

It involved cash being stolen from a till.

  


A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: “Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured, as he have information that could assist our investigation.”

Please visit the force’s website or call 101 if you have any information, quoting reference 12260148996.
