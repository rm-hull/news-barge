A £20,000 reward is being offered for information leading to the whereabouts of Jordan Asije, 22, following the fatal stabbing of Finbar Sullivan, 21, on London's Primrose Hill.

Finbar Sullivan was killed during a fight at the lookout on 7 April, having gone to the park to try out a new camera he received for his birthday.

Metropolitan Police detectives have directly appealed to Jordan Asije to 'do the right thing' and hand himself in, though they do not believe he poses a threat to the public.

Anyone knowingly assisting Mr Asije to evade arrest or harbouring him may be committing a criminal offence and can expect to be investigated.

Three men – Oliuwadamilola Ogunyankinnu, Alexis Bidace, and Ernest Boateng – have already been charged with Mr Sullivan's murder and have pleaded not guilty, while a fourth man, Khalid Abdulqadir, faces a grievous bodily harm charge.
