## Ways to save on National Trust memberships

### Up to 25 per cent off with the senior concession

National Trust has a 25 per cent concession for senior members aged 60 or over who have been members for at least three consecutive years. That is £24 or more in annual savings with an individual senior membership for £72 or £120.60 for a joint senior membership.

Senior memberships cannot be completed online. To redeem this discount, please call the National Trust at 0344 800 1895.

### Free guest pass with direct debit memberships

Select annual direct debit as your payment method when getting a new joint, family, individual or young person membership to qualify for a free guest pass. The guest pass is valid at select locations and the member who claimed the benefit must be present. Make sure to check if the National Trust place you want to visit accepts guest passes and whether or not there are any pre-booking requirements.

### Young person membership discount

Individual memberships normally cost £96 a year, however, National Trust offers 50 per cent off the young person membership. Anyone aged 18-25 years old can purchase a young person membership for £48.

### Free parking at every National Trust car park

Another perk of a National Trust membership is free parking. This is available for everyone, aside from junior members under 17, and can save you between £3 and £5 on average per visit. Either scan your National Trust membership card or use the JustPark app to validate your parking.

### First order discount code

Sign up for the National Trust newsletter and get 10 per cent off your first online shop order of more than £50. The offer applies to the National Trust online store, which stocks homeware, garden décor, outdoor gear, food and more.

You will also stay up to date on all the National Trust sales, important updates and upcoming events when you register for the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time if the communication is no longer relevant to you.

## National Trust membership options

Not sure which membership to choose? Here's a quick breakdown:

- Individual membership: For solo explorers, starting from £96 annually
- Young person membership: For 18-25 year olds, this option is just £48 per year – significantly less than an individual membership
- Junior membership: For just £12 a year, the junior membership gives under-18s unlimited days, all at a pocket-money price
- Joint membership: For two adults living at the same address, from £160.80
- Family membership: For two adults and children up to 17 years of age, starting from £168.60 (or £103.80 for one-parent families)
- Lifetime membership: One-off payment for unlimited visits – ideal for dedicated National Trust lovers

For those making a flying visit to the UK, the explorer pass is ideal. It offers a short-term membership for four, eight or 14 days, with prices starting from £36.

### Member benefits

## FAQs

### How can I redeem my National Trust discount code?

If you have a National Trust discount code, add your items to the basket and head to the checkout. On the right-hand side of the page, look for the “Apply promotional code” link just above your order total. Click to open the box, paste in your code and apply it to see the savings reflected instantly.

### Can I get free delivery at the National Trust online shop?

Yes, the National Trust offers free standard delivery on online orders over £50, saving you £4.95. Delivery costs will be automatically removed once your basket reaches the minimum spend.

### How can I visit National Trust places for free?

New members who sign up via annual direct debit receive one free guest pass, which can be used to bring someone along for free. You can also visit selected National Trust sites during heritage open days throughout the year, when entry is free for everyone.

### Is there an 18-25 National Trust membership discount?

Yes, there is an 18-25 National Trust membership discount, which offers a reduced annual price compared to standard adult membership. It’s designed to make it more affordable for younger adults to explore National Trust places across the UK.

### Can I get a £10 gift card with my National Trust membership?

No, this promotion is no longer available. As a charity, the National Trust has replaced the £10 gift card offer with a free guest pass for new members who join via annual direct debit.
