WEST Yorkshire Police is appealing to the public for help to trace a man in connection with a recall to prison.

Officers want to trace 37-year-old Luke Linley, from Leeds, who is wanted by the domestic abuse integrated offender management team from the city force for breaching the conditions of his licence.

Despite extensive enquiries, police said they have been unable to locate Linley and are urging anyone with information to come forward.

  


A spokesman for West Yorkshire Police said: "We are renewing an appeal for information to help trace a man from Leeds who is wanted on recall to prison."

Anyone who has information regarding this person is asked to call police on 101, or report online via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat.

Information can also be passed to independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555111.
