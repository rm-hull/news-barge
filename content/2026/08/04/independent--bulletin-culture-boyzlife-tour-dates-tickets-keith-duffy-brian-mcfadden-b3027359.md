Boyzlife, the musical duo comprising Boyzone's Keith Duffy and former Westlife member Brian McFadden, have announced an extensive 12-date UK tour for May 2027.

The tour is scheduled to begin in St Albans on 6 May and will conclude in Northampton on 23 May.

Audiences can anticipate a blend of classic hits from their respective boyband careers, Boyzone and Westlife, alongside original material from Boyzlife.

Duffy and McFadden expressed their excitement for the upcoming shows, promising nights filled with music, laughter, and potential special guests.

Tickets for the tour will be available for purchase from Friday 7 August at 10 am via Ticketmaster.
