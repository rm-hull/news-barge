DRIVERS are being warned of a crash along a main road in East Yorkshire.

The A165 Bridlington Road is closed in both directions, between the Brandesburton roundabout and the Catfoss Lane junction, Humberside Police have confirmed.

  


A force spokesperson has advised motorists to find alternative routes where possible.
