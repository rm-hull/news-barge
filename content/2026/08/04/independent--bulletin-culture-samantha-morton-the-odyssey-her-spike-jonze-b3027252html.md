# Samantha Morton still ‘hurt’ after being replaced on 2013 Oscar-winning film

- Samantha Morton has spoken about her surprise and sadness at being replaced by Scarlett Johansson as the voice of Samantha in Spike Jonze’s 2013 film, Her.
- Morton, who had already recorded all her lines for the role, expressed that she wished for another chance to re-record with an American accent.
- She is currently earning rave reviews for her role as the Greek sorceress Circe in Christopher Nolan’s new epic movie, *The Odyssey*.
- Nolan praised Morton's performance in *The Odyssey*, comparing the crew's reaction to her work to that of Heath Ledger during the filming of* The Dark Knight*.
- The director recounted an instance where the crew gave Morton a round of applause after a take, highlighting her exceptional depth and impact on set.
