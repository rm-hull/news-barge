A detached four-bedroom house in Haxby has been put on the market for £750,000.

The York Road property was built in 2023, and the estate agent has described it as a "stunning executive home" perfect for families.

It is located close to Haxby’s amenities and provides easy access to the A64, so could be a good option for commuters.

The house has an energy performance certificate (EPC) rating of B, making it cheaper to heat and run.

The ground floor has an entrance hall, sitting room, open-plan living/dining kitchen with bi-fold doors that open out to the garden, utility room, study, one bedroom and a shower room.

The kitchen/living/dining room is the biggest space in the house, measuring 33 feet 10 inches by 16 feet 2 inches.

There is a garage to the side of the house and a large garden.

On the first floor is the principal bedroom with a dressing room and en-suite shower room, two further double bedrooms, and a family bathroom with a freestanding bath and walk-in shower.

The estate agent, William H Brown, said internal viewing is "highly recommended to fully appreciate the space and quality of the house".

There are also several bus stops within walking distance, including at Ryedale Court, Haxby Memorial Hall and Haxby WMC.

Haxby has shops, cafes and takeaways and is located just off the A1237, providing great links to the city centre.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact William H Brown on 01904 409468.
