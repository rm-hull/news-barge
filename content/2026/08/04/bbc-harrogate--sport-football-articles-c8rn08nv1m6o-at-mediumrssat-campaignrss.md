**Harrogate Town have signed Halifax Town midfielder Josh Hmami for an undisclosed fee.**

The 26-year-old scored 12 goals in 42 National League appearances for the Shaymen last season after joining from King's Lynn Town.

He could make his debut for the Sulpherites at home to Solihull Moors in their National League opener on Saturday.

"He's an ambitious lad and fits the profile we are looking for in that position, we hope he can bring goals like he did last season," head of player recruitment Lloyd Kerry told the club website., external

Harrogate have not confirmed the length of Hmami's contract.
