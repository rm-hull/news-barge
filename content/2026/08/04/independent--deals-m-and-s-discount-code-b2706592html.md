## Ways to save at M&S in 2026

From the popular rewards scheme to everyday discount codes, there are plenty of ways to save at M&S. Here are the easiest ways to update your wardrobe or treat yourself to a foodie haul at M&S for less.

**10 per cent off with the The Sparks reward scheme**

M&S’s customer reward scheme has gained popularity in recent years. Sparks, as it is known, gives customers the chance to shop personalised offers across all of its departments, including food, home and fashion.

Sparks offers can include:

- £5 off at an M&S Foodhall when you spend £40 upon signing up
- 10 per cent off fashion, beauty and home online or in store upon signing up
- Preferential rates on travel money for all currencies online or in-store
- A free gift during your birthday month – anything from prosecco to viral cookies
- A complimentary hot drink for every six purchases you make

Over time, if you scan your Sparks card every time you shop, you’ll be rewarded with different offers that will appear in your account. Simply activate them online or in the app and scan the bar code in store.

**Up to 50 per cent off in M&S seasonal sales**

Whether it’s a dedicated sale or the year-round clearance section, you’ll find thousands of discounted items on the M&S website. Savings span clothing, beauty and home, and often highlight top brands, including Nobody’s Child, Hobbs, and Mamas & Papas.

M&S is also partial to a bundle deal on clothing. Right now, you can enjoy £20 off when you buy two Charles Tyrwhitt items or get three T-shirts for £22 (selected items only).

**Shop smarter with the M&S app**

If you’re a Sparks member, downloading the M&S app makes it easier to track your discounts and receipts, as well as making it quicker than ever to shop online. M&S also rewards loyal app-users with exclusive discounts from time to time, as well as early access to sales.

**Free delivery on select M&S orders**

With a little forward planning, you can get free delivery on most of your online orders at M&S. Delivery policies vary depending on what you’re buying – here’s a quick lowdown of what to expect:

- Store collection is free for all orders
- Fashion, home and beauty orders over £60 automatically qualify for free delivery (£3.99 for orders less than this)
- Flower, hamper and wine orders come with complimentary nominated and next-day delivery

**Exclusive discounts for parents and babies**

In 2025, M&S launched its first-ever baby club, otherwise known as The Parent Hood. Parents can sign up for free, but will need to be a member of the Sparks loyalty scheme first.

Once you’re a member of The Parent Hood, you’ll get 10 per cent off babygrows for the first two years of your child’s life, as well as personalised monthly rewards. These can be anything from discounts on a particular brand to free items to help you out that month.

You can also join the weekly parent and baby morning at your local M&S cafe, and claim a free piece of cake with every hot drink purchase.

## M&S FAQs

### How do I get a discount on my first order at Marks & Spencer?

To get a discount on your first order at Marks & Spencer, you’ll need to join the free Sparks rewards scheme. New members typically receive 10 per cent off their first home, fashion or beauty purchase.

While this isn’t issued as a traditional Marks & Spencer promo code, the discount is automatically applied once you’re signed in to your Sparks account. You can also stack this with sale prices where available.

### Does Marks & Spencer offer a Blue Light Card discount?

M&S doesn’t currently accept Blue Light Cards. If you’re looking to save, your best options are checking here for a valid M&S discount code, shopping the clearance section online or signing up to Sparks for personalised offers and rewards.

### Can I get a student discount at Marks & Spencer?

There isn’t a permanent student discount at M&S, but it does occasionally run limited-time student offers throughout the year. These are usually promoted as exclusive deals rather than a standard Marks & Spencer promo code, so it’s worth keeping an eye on seasonal campaigns and email promotions. The most recent has included 25 per cent off selected food items.
