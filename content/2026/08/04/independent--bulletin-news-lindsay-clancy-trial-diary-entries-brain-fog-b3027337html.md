# Lindsay Clancy detailed her desperation and ‘brain fog’ in diary entries before killing children

![A page from a notebook kept by Lindsay Clancy in which she documents her medications and feelings during Clancy's murder trial at Plymouth Superior Court in Plymouth, Mass., Monday, Aug. 3, 2026](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/04/15/00/AP26215699739704.jpg)

- The murder trial of Massachusetts mother Lindsay Clancy, who is accused of killing her three young children in 2023, resumed this week with jurors hearing emotional diary entries detailing her deteriorating mental health.
- Clancy's journals, read aloud in court, chronicled overwhelming anxiety, crushing guilt, and desperate pleas for help, with entries from late 2022 expressing a desire for a mental break from motherhood and struggles with insomnia.
- Her defense argues she was a devoted mother whose grip on reality was shattered by severe mental illness, including undiagnosed bipolar disorder complicated by postpartum psychosis, which they claim was exacerbated by a series of prescribed medications.
- Prosecutors contend Clancy intentionally killed her children—5-year-old Cora, 3-year-old Dawson, and 8-month-old Callan, who died three days after being hospitalized—and should be held criminally responsible for her actions.
- The trial will determine if Clancy is guilty, facing life in prison without parole, or not guilty by reason of lack of criminal responsibility, which would result in her commitment to a state psychiatric hospital.
