# Democrats just seized Republicans’ biggest political advantage, new poll finds

- A new Reuters/Ipsos poll reveals that Americans trust Democrats more than Republicans to manage the economy for the first time in nearly a decade.
- The survey indicates 37% of registered voters chose Democrats for economic management, while 36% backed Republicans.
- Donald Trump's approval rating has slipped to 35%, a two-point drop from the previous month and just one point above his lowest recorded approval.
- The shift in economic trust is linked to rising living costs, including a significant increase in gasoline prices following U.S.-Israeli strikes on Iran.
- Democrats also lead on the generic congressional ballot, with 42% support compared to 37% for Republicans, suggesting economic issues could be pivotal in the upcoming midterm elections.
