Stockton woman killed in Idaho In-N-Out shooting

A mass shooting in Twin Falls, Idaho, left three people dead and seven others injured, with two victims remaining in critical condition.

One of the deceased victims was 23-year-old Ashley Garibay, an In-N-Out Burger employee, who was working her first job and loved her role.

The suspect, 24-year-old Chad Williams, died from a self-inflicted gunshot wound after attacking victims both on and off the In-N-Out property.

A private citizen, Jordan Salinas, and an armed off-duty Idaho State Police trooper confronted the shooter, actions credited with saving lives.

A second victim was identified to The Associated Press as Christopher Claunch, known for helping people achieve sobriety, and the In-N-Out location had opened only weeks before the incident.
