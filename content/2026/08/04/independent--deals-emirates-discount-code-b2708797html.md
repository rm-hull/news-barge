## How to save on Emirates flights

### Emirates Skywards perks

Emirates Skywards is the airline’s free loyalty programme, enabling you to earn miles on every flight and redeem them for discounts, upgrades and reward flights.

Frequent travellers can unlock extra perks with higher tier levels, such as bonus miles, lounge access and additional baggage allowances, helping to reduce the overall cost of travel.

Families can also combine their miles in a My Family account, allowing up to eight members to contribute to a shared balance. Younger travellers can join Skysurfers – a dedicated loyalty programme for children aged 2–17.

### Get up to 10 per cent off with the Emirates student discount

Students aged 16-31 can get up to 10 per cent off economy fares and 5 per cent off business-class flights with Emirates.

You’ll also benefit from extra baggage allowance and flexible booking options, making this one of the most valuable Emirates discounts available.

### Half-price Emirates flight deals

Emirates regularly offers limited-time promotions on flights to destinations worldwide, with savings of up to 50 per cent when using Skywards miles or booking during sales events.

If your travel dates are flexible, checking the deals page is one of the best ways to secure a lower fare.

### Get cheap flights with Emirates best fare finder

The Emirates “best fare finder” tool lets you compare flight prices across an entire month, helping you spot the cheapest days to travel.

Booking on less-popular days or outside peak seasons can significantly reduce your flight costs.

### Book early to secure lower fares

As our travel team discovered, booking in advance is one of the easiest ways to save on Emirates flights. Long-haul trips are often cheapest when booked around six months ahead, while short-haul flights can be cheaper around three to five months before departure.

## Emirates FAQs

### Where can I redeem my Emirates discount code?

You can apply your discount code when you search for your flights. Under the departure, arrival and passenger information, you will find a “use promotional code” section. Click there to reveal a box where you can enter your code.

### Is there an NHS discount at Emirates?

Unfortunately, Emirates doesn't offer an NHS discount, however, you can still take advantage of the many other discounts. As soon as any NHS promotions become available, we'll update this page.

### What is the baggage policy at Emirates?

Baggage allowances depend on your fare type and destination. Economy passengers usually receive 20-35kg, while business- and first-class customers get 40-50kg.

### When can you check in for an Emirates flight?

You can check in for your Emirates flight online 48 hours before, up until 90 minutes before departure.

### Do Emirates discount codes work in 2026?

Emirates discount codes for flights are rare, but occasional promotions and partner offers are available so if you see one, snap it up quickly. Most savings come from student discounts, reward programmes and seasonal deals.

### When is the cheapest time to book Emirates flights?

The cheapest fares are usually available when booking in advance or during airline sales. Travelling midweek and avoiding peak holiday periods can also help reduce costs.

### Can you use Emirates promo codes on flights?

Yes, when available, Emirates promo codes can be applied during the flight search process. However, most discounts are automatically applied through offers or loyalty programmes.
