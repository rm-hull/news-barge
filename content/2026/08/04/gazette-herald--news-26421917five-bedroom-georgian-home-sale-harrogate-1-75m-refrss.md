A Georgian home in Harrogate with a cinema room and gym has been put up for sale.

The five-bedroom semi-detached house in Swan Road is on the market for offers over £1,750,000.

According to the property listing, the house has been "restored and renovated to an incredible standard throughout", retaining some of its original Georgian features.

The spacious accommodation is arranged over four floors and features an open-plan dining kitchen with a glazed atrium, a family room, a utility room, and a cloakroom.

To the front of the ground floor is a sitting room with a bay window overlooking the front gardens, and a room currently used as a home gym.

The lower ground floor is a cinema room with a large bay window and a wine store.

On the first floor is a principal bedroom with built-in wardrobes and an en suite shower room, two further double bedrooms with fitted wardrobes, and a house bathroom.

The second floor consists of two more double bedrooms with fitted wardrobes and a contemporary bathroom with a separate shower cubicle.

Outside, the property is accessed via electrically operated entrance gates leading to a shared driveway with ample parking.

A detached double garage with an electrically operated door provides further secure parking.

The gardens lie to the front and rear of the property, featuring a raised lawn, a walled paved courtyard directly off the kitchen, and landscaped gardens with box hedging and colourful borders.

The house has an EPC rating of E and is chain-free.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact estate agents Carter Jonas on 01423 578436.
