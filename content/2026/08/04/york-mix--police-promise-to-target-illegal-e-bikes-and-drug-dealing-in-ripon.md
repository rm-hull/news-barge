The Neighbourhood Policing Sergeant for Ripon has had a meeting with the Mayor of Ripon, Cllr Andrew Williams, following concerns raised by residents about illegal e-bike use, suspected drug dealing, and anti-social behaviour.

North Yorkshire Police say they are actively dealing with reported issues and working closely with partner agencies to identify offenders, disrupt criminality, and address the concerns affecting our communities. The action includes:

**Increased patrolsTargeted enforcement activityPartnership working to tackle crime and ASB**

A spokesperson said: “We understand the impact these issues have on residents, businesses, and visitors, and we want the community to know that these concerns are being taken seriously.

“You can help too. Please continue to report incidents, anti-social behaviour, and any suspicious activity to North Yorkshire Police via 101, online, or 999 in an emergency.

“The information you provide helps us build intelligence, target offenders, and take positive action.”
