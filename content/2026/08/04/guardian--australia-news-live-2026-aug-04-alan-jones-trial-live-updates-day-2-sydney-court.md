Broadcaster allegedly paid complainant $110,000 over course of a decade, court hears

He alleged that a letter from Jones’s company suggested he was paid $110,000 over the course of almost a decade.

The court heard the payments began in August 2003 at $800 a fortnight but increased twice, and were $1,000 a fortnight by March 2004. He stopped receiving the payment in October 2012, J told the court. At that point, it was $1,200 a fortnight, he alleged.

The payments ended after he was contacted by Jones’s accountant, who said the payments were going to stop, J told the court.

J said that by that point he didn’t have much contact with Jones:

I wasn’t doing as well. I wasn’t competing and I was injured. Every now and again I would get a text message saying ‘I love you’. Or he would call intermittently.
