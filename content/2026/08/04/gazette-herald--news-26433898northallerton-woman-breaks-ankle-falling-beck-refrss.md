A WOMAN has been rescued after falling into a North Yorkshire beck.

  



North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Stokesley was called to Chantry Road in Romanby, around 5.50pm last night (Monday, August 3).

The woman suffered a broken ankle, the service said.

A service spokesperson said: "Crews assisted Yorkshire Ambulance Service at the scene.

"The injured person was removed from a ditch then taken to hospital."
