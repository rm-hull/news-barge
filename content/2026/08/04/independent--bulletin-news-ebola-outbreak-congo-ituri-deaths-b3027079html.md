# Deadly Ebola outbreak in eastern Congo kills over 1,700

- More than 1,700 people have died in eastern Congo in the fastest-growing Ebola outbreak, with 3,802 cases and 1,707 fatalities confirmed as of Tuesday.
- The director-general of Africa CDC expressed uncertainty about when the outbreak might peak, noting that 80 per cent of new infections are now stemming from community spread.
- The response to the outbreak linked to the Bundibugyo virus, for which no approved vaccines or treatments exist, has been severely hampered by factors such as community mistrust, violence, and delays in contact tracing.
- Healthcare workers have faced immense challenges, with staff in Mongbwalu issuing an ultimatum over unpaid wages, threatening to escalate action if demands were not met within 24 hours.
- The current outbreak has claimed more lives at a quicker pace than any previous one, while a study testing two potential Ebola treatments has recently commenced in Ituri.
