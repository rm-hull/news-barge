Exmoor National Park was officially designated in 1954 (PA)

Exmoor National Park has launched a new pilot scheme, “Pay with Exmoor Care”, to reward tourists for environmentally positive actions.

Until 30 September, visitors can receive a 20 per cent discount at Exmoor National Park Centres by demonstrating actions such as litter picking or using public transport.

Other eligible actions include recording wildlife and sharing positive messages about caring for the National Park on social media.

The scheme aims to encourage responsible and regenerative tourism, increase awareness of environmental care, and boost footfall in National Park Centres during the summer.

Inspired by Copenhagen's CopenPay scheme, this initiative is the first of its kind in a UK National Park, with potential for expansion in 2027.
