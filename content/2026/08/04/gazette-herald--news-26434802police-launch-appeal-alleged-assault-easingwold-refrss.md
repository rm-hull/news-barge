NORTH Yorkshire Police are investigating an alleged assault in a North Yorkshire town.

The incident occurred outside the Galtres Centre, in Market Place, Easingwold on Sunday (July 14) at around 12.30am.

  


Officers have released a series of CCTV images of a person they would like to speak to in connection with the alleged assault.

A police spokesperson said: “If you have any information, please contact the officer in the case PC Tyson 55 by calling 101, using reference 12260132001 or by emailing [dom.tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:dom.tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk.)”
