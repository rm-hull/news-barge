The Scottish Greens have urged Education Secretary Mairi McAllan to overhaul the "antiquated" exam system, following the release of almost 150,000 pupils' results.

The party is calling for the implementation of recommendations from a 2023 expert review conducted by Professor Louise Hayward.

Professor Hayward's review proposed reducing the number of exams, shifting focus towards coursework and continuous assessment, and abolishing exams for S4 pupils.

These changes were previously rejected by the former education secretary, Jenny Gilruth, but the Scottish Greens have offered their support to push them through.

Green education spokesperson Laura Moodie argued that continuous assessment would more accurately reflect a young person's knowledge and abilities, creating a qualifications system fit for the 21st century.
