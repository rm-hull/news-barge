                                    FatFace has announced it will close its Ripon store next week.

The clothing retailer's branch at Marketgate on Marshall Way will close its doors on Tuesday 11th August.

FatFace has traded from Marketgate in Ripon since 2009, when the retail park opened.

A FatFace spokesperson said:

"We’re focusing on supporting our store colleagues at this time and, where possible, looking at redeployment opportunities.

"We’d like to thank all customers who have shopped with us for their support over the years.”


The retailer became part of the Next Group in 2023 and has around 180 stores nationwide, including in Harrogate.

No reason has been given for the closure.
