Police have tonight issued this image of a York man they are searching for.

He’s Findlay Drinkhill, 20, and he’s wanted in connection with a burglary that occurred in the York area last month.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Drinkhill is believed to still be in the city and has connections to the Clifton area of York.

“Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Drinkhill and we are now appealing for your help to find him.”

If you have an immediate sighting of him, police ask you to call 999.
