Arsenal have opened talks over Newcastle midfielder Bruno Guimaraes, though an initial bid has been knocked back with the two clubs now set to negotiate over a fee. Their pursuit of Vinicius Jr continues, too, although Real Madrid are said to have offered fresh terms to the Brazilian after his return to training this week.

In order to facilitate two huge deals - both would need fees near to or in excess of £100m - Arsenal may need to sell. Christian Norgaard is set for the exit after a single season at the Emirates with Everton is his next destination, it seems. Myles Lewis-Skelly could also be an eye-raising sale with Manchester United exploring their options to bring the defender in as Michael Carrick looks to bolster his squad.

United’s aim is for a left-back with Lewis Hall also in the mix as Newcastle struggle to hold on to their stars in a summer of rebuilding. One man who could be on his way out of Old Trafford is Marcus Rashford, with Fenerbahce a surprise suitor for the England international.

## Vinicius Jr resumes talks with Real Madrid after return to training

Vinicius Jr has returned to Real Madrid after his World Cup exertions and resumed talks with the club over a new contract amid Arsenal’s interest in the Brazilian. ESPN believe, though, that the player still wants improved terms on an initial offer made by Real.

## Manchester United consider intriguing move for Arsenal defender

Manchester United are investigating whether a deal for Arsenal's Myles Lewis-Skelly is possible, as they also press on with attempting to sign Lewis Hall from Newcastle United.

The Old Trafford hierarchy have long-standing interest in the Lewis-Skelly and are aiming to see whether Arsenal would do business, given the English champions’ plan to spend big over the next few weeks.

There was the possibility of a move last season, when Lewis-Skelly found himself out of the team for long stretches of the campaign, but a late-season revival has transformed his profile. The youngster started the Champions League final against Paris Saint-Germain, and offered crucial energy in the late burst to clinch the title.

## Arsenal summer signing Christos Tzolis throws down gauntlet to Premier League title rivals

New Arsenal signing Christos Tzolis has boldly declared the reigning Premier League champions are fully prepared to defend their crown, insisting that the "pressure" now rests squarely on their rivals to prevent them from securing back-to-back titles.

The Greece international, who joined the Gunners in a £34 million move from Club Brugge, marked his debut with a goal in Saturday’s 4-1 victory over Girona.

"Of course. To defend a title, you don’t have to think about it. It’s more difficult for the other teams because the pressure is there, not on the team who won the title," he stated.

## Tottenham teenager Mikey Moore set for another loan as Bundesliga club lead race for highly-rated star

Tottenham’s highly-rated young attacker Mikey Moore is poised for a loan move away from the club, with German side Koln currently leading the race for his signature.

The decision comes despite the 18-year-old’s promising pre-season performances under manager Roberto De Zerbi, as all parties agree another temporary spell elsewhere would best serve the versatile forward’s development.

Moore enjoyed a standout season at Rangers last term, where he netted seven goals and was crowned PFA Scotland’s Young Player of the Year, overcoming a challenging start in Glasgow.

## Newcastle sign goalkeeper Lukas Hornicek from Braga as post-Eddie Howe era begins

Newcastle United have announced the signing of Czech international goalkeeper Lukas Hornicek from Portuguese club Braga on a five-year deal.

The Premier League side confirmed the arrival of the 24-year-old on Monday for a reported fee of €30.6m (£26.2m).

## Arsenal have opening bid for Newcastle captain Bruno Guimaraes rejected

Arsenal’s initial offer for Newcastle captain Bruno Guimaraes has been knocked back by the Magpies, the Press Association understands.

Speculation surrounding the Brazil international’s potential move to the Gunners has intensified, with direct lines of communication now established between the two Premier League clubs.

A significant disparity in valuation is believed to exist for the midfielder, who has netted 31 goals in 195 appearances for Newcastle since his £40 million transfer from Lyon in January 2022.

## Jordan Henderson joins Chelsea on free transfer after leaving Brentford

Jordan Henderson has joined Chelsea on a two-year deal after his contract with Brentford was mutually terminated.

The free transfer follows confirmation last week that the England international was set to depart the Bees amid interest from their Premier League rivals.

At 36, Henderson becomes Chelsea’s eighth first-team acquisition since manager Xabi Alonso took the helm, marking him as the most senior player bought by the club’s owners, BlueCo, since their 2022 takeover. Speaking to Chelsea’s official website, Henderson expressed his enthusiasm for the new challenge.

## Vozinha lands in Chile to complete Colo Colo move

Vozinha, the 40-year-old goalkeeper who made history with Cape Verde at the recently concluded World Cup, arrived in Chile on Sunday to join Colo Colo after a series of twists and turns that put his move in doubt.

“I’m very happy to be here. And I want to thank the fans for the patience they’ve shown while waiting for me,” the Cape Verde star told reporters in Chile. “See you at the Monumental Stadium.”
