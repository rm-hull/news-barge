You don’t need to let your old appliances go to waste when shopping with AO. Instead, you can trade in your old model for money that you can use towards something shiny and new at AO.

### Price match guarantee

AO offers a price match guarantee, which means that if you see your product cheaper with another retailer before buying, you can call and get your AO product cost matched. If you’ve already purchased it and notice it drop on AO’s own site or at another afterwards, you don’t have to miss out. AO will refund you the difference (if it meets its criteria), as long as you raise a price match request within seven days of receiving your new product.

### Check out the AO outlet at eBay

The AO outlet on eBay is another way to save on your home essentials. It’s packed with ex-display items and returns that are in working condition and can’t be resold through the main website. This means heavily discounted products compared to the RRP. There’s everything from the latest TVs to fridge-freezers, coffee machines, toasters, blenders and more.

Although you’re shopping through eBay, you will still receive direct AO customer care (available Monday to Friday), a one-year warranty and seven day delivery.

### Delivery and returns policy

Next-day delivery is available seven days a week on kitchen and home large appliances, which is handy if you’re in need of an urgent replacement. Shipping costs vary depending on the delivery method and product size, however, you can see this via the product page. Five Star loyalty members receive free delivery on all products.

AO has a 30-day returns policy for unused items, however, it’s worth noting that there is a collection fee. Large items, such as washing machines, TV and ovens, have a collection fee of up to £49.99 and smaller items of up to £29.99.

### AO services

If you need help with installation or removal of old items, AO offers a range of services to do it for you. Installation for all large home appliances, such as washing machines and hobs, and TV setup is available at an additional cost, which you can add on via the product page. It also provides a disconnection service of old appliances when you opt for an installation.

You can also arrange responsible collection of any old appliances from £30, even if you didn’t buy them through AO. If you are purchasing an appliance through AO, you can opt for the remove and recycle service on the product page, from £25.

### AO care

You can have peace of mind with AO care, too, which gives you additional protection for your home appliances. You'll enjoy immediate cover for accidental damage, breakdown protection, unlimited call-outs, access to the AO care portal, and lots more.
