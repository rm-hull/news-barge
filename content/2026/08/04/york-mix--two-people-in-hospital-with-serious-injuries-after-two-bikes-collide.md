Police are appealing for information and dashcam footage following a serious road traffic collision near Westow.

It happened on Mook Lane at around 10.35am on Sunday 2 August, and involved a yellow Yamaha motorcycle and a white Honda motorcycle.

Following the collision, both motorcyclists were airlifted to hospital with serious injuries.

Police say: “We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
