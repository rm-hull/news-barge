A man has been arrested on suspicion of murder following a fatal shooting at a traveller site near Cricklade, Wiltshire.

The victim has been identified by police as 36-year-old Jimmy Ward, who was declared dead at the scene on Sunday morning.

The 35-year-old suspect was arrested on Tuesday, bringing the total number of arrests in connection with the incident to five.

Four other individuals – two men aged 53 and 29, a 74-year-old man, and a 42-year-old woman – have been arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender.

All five people are currently in police custody for questioning as the investigation continues, with a significant police presence remaining at the site.
