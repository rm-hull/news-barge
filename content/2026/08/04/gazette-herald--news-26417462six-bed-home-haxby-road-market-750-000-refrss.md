A six-bed end-terrace house in York with off-street parking, garage, and three en suites is for sale at £750,000.

Situated a short distance from York city centre, in Haxby Road, the freehold period property is arranged over three floors and has three reception rooms.

According to those listing the home, there is an exceptional amount of versatile living accommodation and potential for various uses, including possible adaptation for an outstanding family residence.

Ground floor features include a fitted kitchen, utility room, a shower room, and an additional room currently used as a hair salon, which could be used as a home office or hobby space.

There is also an integral garage and off-street parking.

The first floor contains three double bedrooms, each with an en-suite bathroom.

The bedrooms could potentially be used for bed and breakfast accommodation, according to the listing information.

On the second floor, there are three further double bedrooms and a family bathroom.

Outside, the house has a rear garden, described by those marketing the property as a suitable area for relaxing or entertaining outdoors.

Those listing the property say it is a rare opportunity due to its location near York city centre and access to amenities, schools, and transport links.

The property is described by estate agents, Williams H Brown, as "bursting with character and potential".

The house is offered as freehold and is located on a road described as highly sought-after.

Early viewing is advised by those handling the sale to appreciate the scale and potential of the accommodation.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact William H Brown on 01904 918535.
