*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A thief has stolen a high-value folding bike from York city centre.

The incident happened outside ASK in Blake Street on July 28.

North Yorkshire Police are urging those with more information to come forward.

  


Officers have shared a CCTV image of a man that police would like to speak in relation to the incident.

To get in touch, please email [ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Please quote reference 12260144855 when providing information.
