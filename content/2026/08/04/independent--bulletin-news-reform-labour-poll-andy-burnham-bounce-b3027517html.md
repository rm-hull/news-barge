Reform UK has regained its lead over Labour in a new YouGov poll, with Reform securing 23 per cent of the vote share compared to Labour's 22 per cent.

This latest poll suggests the 'Burnham bounce', which saw Labour's popularity increase after Andy Burnham became prime minister, may be short-lived.

Last week, a similar YouGov survey had both Reform and Labour tied at 22 per cent, indicating a shift back in Reform's favour.

Dr Patrick English, YouGov's head of elections, noted that while Andy Burnham has positive favourability ratings, the public is not yet convinced the new Labour government is addressing concerns like the cost of living and immigration.

The poll results come amidst recent challenges for Nigel Farage, including a parliamentary sleaze probe over a £5m gift and his current campaign to win back his parliamentary seat in the Clacton by-election.
