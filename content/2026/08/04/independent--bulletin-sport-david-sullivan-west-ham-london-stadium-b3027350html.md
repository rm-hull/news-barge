The recommendation follows allegations of historic sexual misconduct against Sullivan, which were reported by a joint investigation from BBC Panorama and The Times.

London Stadium cited the 'potential for demonstrations and anti-social behaviour by some spectators' as the reason for their advice.

Sullivan has categorically denied the allegations, describing them as 'factually incorrect and entirely false, decades-old allegations concerning my personal life'.

The Football Association has also banned Sullivan from having contact with West Ham’s women and youth teams for three years due to safeguarding concerns, which he refers to as a 'negotiated agreement'.
