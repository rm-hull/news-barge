A man in his 50s has been found dead at his home in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is assisting the Coroner’s Office in locating the next of kin of David Teasdale, 56, who was sadly found dead at his home in Gatesgarth Close, Scarborough.

Mr Teasdale was found on Monday (August 3).

  


There are no suspicious circumstances to his death.

If you have any information that could help the Coroner to locate his family members, please email [coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.)
