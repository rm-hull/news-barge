# Nicolas Jackson transfer latest as Tottenham linked with move for Chelsea striker

![Nicolas Jackson has been linked with Chelsea (Rafal Oleksiewicz/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/08/11/04/bba7f13b8707b352e09a3ca8fb208adaY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzU0OTQxNzE2-2.80417388.jpg)

- Tottenham have been linked with a move to sign Chelsea striker Nicolas Jackson as Roberto De Zerbi looks to strengthen his attacking options.
- However, Tottenham sources have distanced themselves from a move for the 25-year-old, as things stand.
- Jackson is believed to be valued at around £65 million by Chelsea after spending last season on loan at Bayern Munich as Harry Kane’s understudy.
- Jackson has made 65 Premier League appearances for Chelsea since joining the club in 2023 but could be moved on this summer.
- Tottenham are believed to be keen on different attacking options, including Liverpool’s Cody Gakpo and Manchester City winger Savinho.
