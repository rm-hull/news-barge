THIEVES have targeted a farm in a York village overnight.

North Yorkshire Police said 15 Guinea Fowl were stolen from a farm in Plainville Lane, Wigginton between 9.30pm on Tuesday, July 28 and 8.30am the following morning.

  



North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and urging anyone who may have seen suspicious activity to come forward.

This includes anyone who has been offered any guinea fowl.

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC 1037 Jake Reed at [jake.reed@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:jake.reed@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, call North Yorkshire Police on 101 quoting reference 12260145535.
