# Famous faces gather to pay their respects to Oscar-winner Glen Hansard

![Crowds gathered outside the funeral of Glen Hansard at St Patrick's Cathedral in Dublin](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/04/14/01KZ6DTKGK5ATNBF3GRCT5GYNP.jpg)

- Oscar-winning musician Glen Hansard's funeral was held today at St Patrick’s Cathedral in Dublin, attended by stars from music, film, and politics.
- Hundreds of fans gathered outside the cathedral, watching the service on large screens, while many had queued for hours to attend.
- Notable attendees included musicians Hozier, Bono, Declan O’Rourke, Jerry Fish, and Imelda May, as well as actors Steve Coogan and Chris O’Dowd, and former Sinn Féin president Gerry Adams.
- Hansard, frontman of The Frames, died last week at 56 in a motorcycle crash on the outskirts of Dublin, shortly after a traditional music session.
- He was celebrated for his Oscar-winning song *Falling Slowly*, his role in* The Commitments*, and his annual Christmas Eve charity busk for the homeless.
