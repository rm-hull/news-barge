The Sephora beauty advent calendar 2026 has joined this season’s Christmas countdowns as the retailer reveals exclusively to *The Independent* the first details of its treasure trove of products. 

For now, these titbits are limited – to keep the festive spirit alive! – but I can disclose that this year’s 25-day haul will exceed more than £1,000 in value. This figure is on par with a handful of other beauty advent calendars, such as Harvey Nichols, but in my years reviewing these cosmetics round-ups, I’ve seen Sephora prove each and every pound of its calendar’s worth. In 2025, I even concluded that the advent was “the one I [was] most excited about.”

I’m not the only one, it would seem, as Sephora’s offering (previously known as the Sephora favourites advent calnedar) has sold out four years in a row. With that in mind, it’s important to stay abreast of all the important dates, from the waitlist launch – today, 5 August – to pre-order announcements and, finally, the live sale day. Here’s everything I know right now, from the number of full-sizes to the nine products hidden within the retailer’s first look image.
