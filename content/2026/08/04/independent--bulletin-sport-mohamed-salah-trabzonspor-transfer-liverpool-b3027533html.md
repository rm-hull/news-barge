# Mohamed Salah close to joining Turkish club on free transfer

- Turkish Super Lig club Trabzonspor has announced it has begun transfer negotiations with Mohamed Salah.
- Salah is available on a free transfer after departing Liverpool at the end of last season.
- Trabzonspor chairman Ertugrul Dogan expressed hopes of finalising the deal this week, with Salah expected for medical checks on Wednesday and a signing on Thursday.
- Salah spent nine years at Liverpool, scoring 257 goals and winning multiple trophies, including two Premier League titles and one Champions League.
- Trabzonspor finished third in the Super Lig last season, securing a spot in the Europa League playoff round.
