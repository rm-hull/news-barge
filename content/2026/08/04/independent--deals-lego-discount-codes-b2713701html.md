**Ways to save on your Lego order**

### Exclusive benefits for Lego Insiders

Signing up for a Lego Insiders membership enables you to earn points every time you shop at an official UK Lego store. Plus, you’ll get early members-only access to new set releases, exclusive prices and rewards such as Legoland tickets. Here’s how it works:

- **Points:** You will collect Insiders points for every pound spent on Lego products, with your balance automatically updated in your online account.
- **Redeem rewards:** Exchange your points for rewards, such as Lego discount codes, exclusive sets, digital downloads or entries into special sweepstakes.
- **Extra ways to earn:** You can also earn points by completing activities and challenges, such as registering Lego sets you own, following Lego on social media or taking part in surveys.
- **Member perks:** Insiders get early access to new sets, birthday rewards and exclusive gifts-with-purchase promotions.

## Lego FAQs

### Is there an NHS discount at Lego?

No, there’s not currently a discount for NHS or Blue Light card members. The best way to save is by becoming a Lego Insiders member.

### How do I get a Lego Insiders discount code?

To unlock exclusive discounts, simply sign up for a free Lego Insiders membership. Once you’ve done that, you can start building up points and exchange them for discount codes.

### Can I get a discount on my first order at Lego?

No, Lego doesn’t offer a specific first-order discount.

### Can I get replacement Lego parts and pieces?

Pieces of Lego inevitably go missing every once in a while, but fortunately, Lego has a solution – its piece replacement service enables you to request missing blocks, free of charge. Similarly, you’ll be able to request a replacement for any broken pieces. You can also get creative with existing Lego builds by buying extra pieces, from bricks to minifigure parts, so you can make the set your own.
