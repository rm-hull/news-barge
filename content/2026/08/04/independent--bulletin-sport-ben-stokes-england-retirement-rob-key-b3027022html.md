# Ben Stokes responds to England retirement U-turn speculation

![Stokes insisted his international career is over](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/03/14/01KWM2KVMNK8E7QCZXT7W005BE.jpg)

- Ben Stokes has ruled out the possibility of performing a retirement U-turn and returning to play for England in the future.
- Stokes announced his retirement from international cricket in June, but England’s managing director Rob Key admitted last week that he would not be surprised if the all-rounder came back to play in next year’s Ashes.
- However, speaking for the For the Love of Cricket podcast, Stokes was asked whether he was certain that he would not come back to play for England and replied: “Definitely”.
- Stokes has performed a retirement U-turn before, as he stepped away from one-day cricket in 2022 before returning to play in the World Cup the following year.
- Yet he remains adamant that his time as an international cricketer is up, as Joe Root prepares to lead the Test team for England’s series against Pakistan later this month.
