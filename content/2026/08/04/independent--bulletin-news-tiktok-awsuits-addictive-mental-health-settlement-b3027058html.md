# TikTok settles test cases that could decide thousands more lawsuits

![TikTok-Children-Safety](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/11/TikTok-Children-Safety_55961.jpg)

- TikTok has agreed to settle three major lawsuits brought by young users who accused the platform of being deliberately designed to be addictive and causing serious mental health problems.
- The confidential settlements, confirmed by the plaintiffs' attorney, mark a significant development in the ongoing legal battle against social media companies regarding harm to children and teenagers.
- These three cases were selected as "bellwether" trials, test cases intended to help assess how juries might respond before thousands of similar lawsuits move forward.
- The lawsuits alleged that TikTok's platform contributed to self-harm, anxiety, depression, addiction, and eating disorders among the young plaintiffs.
- While TikTok has resolved these specific claims, similar lawsuits against Meta, Google's YouTube, and Snap's Snapchat remain on track for trial, with proceedings scheduled to begin in October.
