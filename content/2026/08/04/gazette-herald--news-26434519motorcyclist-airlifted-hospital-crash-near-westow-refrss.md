TWO motorcyclists were airlifted to hospital following a serious crash near a North Yorkshire village at the weekend.

The incident happened on Mook Lane, near Westow, at around 10.35am on Sunday, August, 2 and involved a yellow Yamaha motorcycle and a white Honda motorcycle.

Following the collision, both motorcyclists were airlifted to hospital with serious injuries.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us.

"If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please email: Joseph Schramm on [Joseph.Schramm@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Joseph.Schramm@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

"If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

"Please quote reference 12260148423 when passing on information."
