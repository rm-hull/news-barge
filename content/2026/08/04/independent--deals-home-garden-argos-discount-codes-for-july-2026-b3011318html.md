## Ways to save at Argos

**Sign up for the Argos emails**

Right now, Argos is offering £5 off when you spend £40 if you sign up to its marketing emails. All you need to do is look out for the email banner at the bottom of the homepage, click on the link and enter your details. Argos will email your exclusive code to use at checkout.

### Shop the Argos clearance section

The Argos clearance section is one of the easiest ways to cut the cost of your shop. You'll find reduced prices across all departments, including furniture, homeware, toys, electricals, fitness equipment and seasonal products, with discounts often reaching 50 per cent or more. Stock changes regularly, so it's worth checking back if you're looking for a bargain.

### Collect Nectar points

Argos is part of the Nectar rewards programme, enabling shoppers to collect Nectar points on eligible purchases and spend them on future purchases. If you're already collecting points through Sainsbury's or other Nectar partners, shopping at Argos is an easy way to boost your balance.

### Use click and collect

If you live near an Argos store, you can take advantage of free click and collect on most items. Some stores offer immediate collection, while others will offer next-day collection or after a certain time.

You can check if the item is available for collection by adding your postcode to the relevant box on the product page.

### Look out for bundle deals

Bundle deals are almost always available on specific departments, such as toys and gaming. We regularly see two for £20 and two for £30 on toys and two for £25 on games. Each department has a dedicated offers section where you can locate any live bundle deals.

### Wait for seasonal sale events

Timing larger purchases around Argos's biggest seasonal events is one of the easiest ways to save. The retailer regularly launches limited-time offers across its most popular categories throughout the year, making it worth planning ahead for costly buys.

- **Christmas toys:** Argos is a popular destination for festive shopping, with discounts on bestselling toys, Lego sets, gaming consoles, dolls and gifts in the run-up to Christmas. Black Friday is often the cheapest time to place your Argos order ahead of Christmas, as toys and gifting can often be reduced by as much as 60 per cent.
- **Back to school:** Save up to 20 per cent on everything from laptops and tablets to school stationery, uniform, lunch boxes, backpacks and desks during the “back to school” period. You'll also find offers on office furniture and study essentials for students.
- **Garden furniture:** Spring and summer are the best times to shop for garden furniture, with deals offering savings of up to 50 per cent on rattan dining sets, loungers, parasols, outdoor storage and patio accessories. End-of-season clearance sales can offer even bigger savings, with up to 70 per cent off.
- **Barbecues and outdoor dining:** Argos often reduces the price of gas and charcoal barbecues, pizza ovens, outdoor dining sets and garden entertaining accessories in the run-up to spring and summer, although end-of-season clearance deals can often surpass these.
- **Valentine's gifts:** Whether you're shopping for jewellery, fragrances, beauty gifts, smartwatches or small home appliances, Argos regularly features Valentine's Day offers on thoughtful gifts for every budget.

## Argos FAQs

### Does Argos offer free delivery?

Argos offers free delivery on small orders of more than £100. For orders costing less than this amount, delivery fees start from £4.95, with larger items having a delivery cost of £9.95.

**Does Argos offer a first order discount?**

You can get £5 off your first order (as long as it’s more than £40) if you sign up to Argos’s marketing emails. Look out for the registration link on the Argos homepage, enter your details and wait for your discount code to come through.

### Is there an Argos student discount?

Unfortunately, there isn’t an Argos student discount right now. However, you can still take advantage of the other offers on this page to save up to 20 per cent on your order. We keep a close eye on student partners (such as those with Student Beans and Unidays), so we’ll update this page if any deals appear.

### Does Argos offer NHS or keyworker discounts?

NHS and healthcare workers can enjoy 3 per cent cashback or a 4 per cent gift card via Health Service Discounts.

### When is the Argos sale?

Argos runs sales year-round but they are rarely site-wide. There’s almost always an Argos discount code available for specific brands or departments, such as 10 per cent off garden furniture costing more than £100; 20 per cent off selected TVs; or 30 per cent off toys.

Many promotions don’t require a voucher code but it’s worth checking the T&Cs if you plan on combining the offer with a code, as it may be excluded.
