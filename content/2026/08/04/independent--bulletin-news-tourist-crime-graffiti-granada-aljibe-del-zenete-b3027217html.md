A tourist from the Basque Country has been charged with a crime against cultural heritage after allegedly spray-painting a historic building in Granada.

The individual is accused of graffitiing the word “Paula” in pink and blue on the Aljibe del Zenete, a 16th-century Mudéjar-style water cistern.

The Aljibe del Zenete is located in the historic Albaicín neighbourhood, which has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1994.

Granada's local police identified the perpetrator, who now faces a potential fine ranging from €3,000 to €30,050.

This incident follows a similar case in 2024 where a British tourist faced a hefty fine for carving initials into a wall at Pompeii, another UNESCO site.
