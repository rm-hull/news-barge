# WhatsApp users report being mysteriously blocked from app

![WhatsApp users have reported being mysteriously removed or blocked](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/01/27/16/39/GettyImages-2186498023.jpeg)

- Many WhatsApp users have reported being mysteriously removed or blocked from the messaging app without explanation.
- Affected users receive a notification stating their account activity is being reviewed for compliance with Terms of Service, with a result expected within 24 hours.
- WhatsApp acknowledges that it sometimes incorrectly bans accounts but states these measures are necessary to prevent abuse, spam, scams, or safety risks.
- Reasons for bans can include using unofficial app versions or “scraping” (automated data extraction), with temporary bans potentially becoming permanent if behaviour continues.
- A WhatsApp spokesperson confirmed they are working to restore wrongly suspended accounts “as quickly as possible' while emphasising the importance of rules for user security.
