In a city full of beavers, Buc-ee's takes issue with mini mart's logo

Buc-ee's is suing Beaver's Mini Mart in Beavercreek, Ohio, alleging its beaver logo and red branding are too similar to Buc-ee's, potentially confusing customers.

The owner of Beaver's Mini Mart, Vik Boparai, stated his store has used its logo for a decade and that other local businesses also feature beavers in their branding.

Buc-ee's seeks a court order to halt the alleged infringement, along with damages, profits and attorney fees from the small business.

A GoFundMe campaign has been initiated to help Beaver's Mini Mart cover its legal expenses, framing the lawsuit as a large corporation targeting a small, local establishment.

This lawsuit comes shortly after comedian John Oliver criticized Buc-ee's on his show, accusing the chain of "outright bullying" and filing "frivolous" trademark lawsuits against smaller entities.
