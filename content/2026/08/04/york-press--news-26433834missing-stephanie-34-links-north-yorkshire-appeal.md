POLICE are appealing for help to locate a missing woman who has links with North Yorkshire.

Northumbria Police said Stephanie, 34, was reported missing on July 23 and was last seen on July 16 near the Royal Victoria Infirmary in Newcastle city centre.

  


The force describes her as being white, 5ft 5in tall, of slim build, with long dark brown hair.

A spokesperson for Northumbria Police said: "Since being reported missing to us we've carried out extensive enquiries to locate her including a review of available CCTV and door-to-door enquiries."

Stephanie has links to South Shields, Gateshead and the wider Newcastle area, as well as Durham, the Cleveland, Police force area, Hartlepool, Darlington and North Yorkshire.

Anyone with information should contact Northumbria Police quoting reference number: NP-20260723-0309.
