North Yorkshire Police are investigating the theft of 15 Guinea Fowl from a farm on Plainville Lane Wigginton.

The theft happened between the 28th July at 9:30pm and the following morning.

Did you see or hear anything, have you been offered any Guinea Fowl?

If you have any information that could assist the nvestigation, please contact online in the first instance:

• Email: PC 1037 Jake Reed on [email protected]

• Online: Visit our website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’

• Live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm

If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Please quote reference 12260145535 when providing information.
