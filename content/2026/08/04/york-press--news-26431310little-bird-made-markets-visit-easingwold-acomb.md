## Summer markets promise artisan food and crafts in Easingwold and Acomb

Summer markets are set to bring a taste of Yorkshire to Easingwold and Acomb this month *(Image: Little Bird Artisan Markets)*

Summer markets are set to bring a taste of Yorkshire to two locations this month.

Artisan market organiser Little Bird Made will host events in Easingwold and Acomb on Saturday, August 8, celebrating the creativity and flavours of the region with a wide range of independent traders.

The Easingwold market will feature handmade crafts, home decor, bespoke creations, and freshly baked goods, while Acomb’s Flourish & Feast market in Front Street will focus on food, drink, and floral displays.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "Our markets are all about celebrating the incredible passion of local independent traders and bringing communities together.

"Whether you are hunting for unique homeware in Easingwold or grabbing a bite to eat with your dog in Acomb, Saturday 8 August is shaping up to be a fantastic day out for everyone."

Stallholder Jane Vafeas, of JBG Handmade, said she is looking forward to welcoming both regular and new customers.

Craig Holmes from BakeMi said the Acomb market offers a great way to connect with the community and introduce traditional Portuguese baking to new audiences.

Visitors can expect a relaxed, dog-friendly day out filled with unique finds.

Details on future markets and stall bookings are available at www.littlebirdmade.com.
