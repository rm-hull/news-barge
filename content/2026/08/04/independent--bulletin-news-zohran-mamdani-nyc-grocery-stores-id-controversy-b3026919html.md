# Mamdani hits back as MAGA turns his grocery store plan into a new culture war

![City officials, including Mamdani, clarified that no ID, residency, or income verification will be required to shop at the stores](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/08/15/52/GettyImages-2247836301.jpg)

- New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced plans for five city-owned grocery stores offering 30% off essential items, set to open in 2027.
- Conservative figures and commentators criticized the plan, falsely claiming shoppers would need an ID, drawing comparisons to voter ID laws.
- City officials, including Mamdani, clarified that no ID, residency, or income verification will be required to shop at the stores.
- The misunderstanding originated from a comment by a city official about a 'library-card-esque system' to prevent hoarding, which was later clarified as a voluntary savings card.
- The controversy highlighted a broader debate contrasting the alleged grocery store ID requirement with opposition to voter ID laws, such as those proposed in the Trump administration's SAVE America Act.
