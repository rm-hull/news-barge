### When is the best time to book cheap Qatar Airways flights?

You could save up to 10 per cent by booking a last-minute Qatar Airways flight. However, travel writer Amelia Neath recently found out that the “magic” booking day is Sunday, with fares typically around 22 per cent cheaper than booking on a Friday.

That said, while Sunday may be the cheapest day to book, it’s often one of the most expensive days to travel. For international trips, Fridays tend to offer the best value for departure, coming in at roughly 14 per cent cheaper on average.

### How do I become a Qatar Airways privilege member?

### How can I get free Qatar Airways wifi?

You can enjoy complimentary high-speed Starlink wifi on B777 or A350 aircrafts to more than 56 countries. If your flight doesn’t have Starlink, you can purchase on-board wifi and save 20 per cent by pre-purchasing before you fly.
