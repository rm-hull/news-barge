If you’re looking to save on summer holiday days out, we’ve found the latest Warwick Castle discount codes stretch your budget. As one of the UK’s most loved historical attractions, with nearly 1,000 years of rich history to its name, there are plenty of fun activities for the whole family.

## Best ways to save on Warwick Castle tickets

### Book in advance to save on tickets

Plan ahead for weekends and holidays and get up to £17 off adult tickets at Warwick Castle. Single-day tickets start from £22 when purchased in advance online. If you wait to pay for your tickets at the door, it will set you back upwards of £39, so it's well worth taking a few minutes to book tickets prior to your visit.

Entry to Warwick Castle includes the spectacular live shows and exhilarating attractions, which provide a fascinating overview of what life at the fortress looked like. You'll also get a rainy day promise included, which means that, should the weather not be in your favour, you can reschedule at no cost.

### Short break deals from just £40

Warwick Castle is also the perfect place for a short break stay, with fantastic deals starting from just £40 per person, when you pair your visit to the castle with stays in the Woodland Lodges, Medieval Glamping Tents, Tower Suites, or the newly-opened Warwick Castle Hotel.

When you return to your hotel after a fun-filled day out, you’ll enjoy even more action-packed entertainment before getting cosy in one of the many room styles. Plus, breakfast and parking are free of charge with all overnight stays.

### Students save up to 38 per cent

Students over 16 years old can enjoy cheap Warwick Castle tickets all year round, with full access to on-site attractions, including the historic towers, ramparts, live shows like The Falconer’s Quest and more. Optional upgrades, such as the castle dungeon experience, are available to pre-book separately.

Student Beans members get £10 off the standard online ticket price, paying only £16 instead with a student discount. Additionally, Totum provides a student discount for Warwick Castle, with tickets starting at £22, which is 15 per cent cheaper than the regular ticket price.

### Exclusive member days

Warwick Castle’s 2026 member days are exclusive events offering special access to the castle for Blue Light Card and Defence Discount Service members. These are exclusive days for members and their families to experience the castle, grounds and all of its attractions without the crowds. To take advantage, tickets must be pre-booked and a valid ID must be presented on arrival.

Not only do members get exclusive entry, but there is also the opportunity to book an overnight stay at the Warwick Castle hotel at a 20 per cent discounted rate, starting from £159 for a family of four. It’s important to note that accommodation is separate from castle admission, which must be booked in advance.

### English Heritage members save 20 per cent

If you’re an English Heritage member visiting Warwick Castle, you can save 20 per cent on tickets by booking through the link on the English Heritage website and entering the discount code provided. Remember to bring your membership card when you visit to show it at the gate.

**Rainy day promise**

If you are booking in advance, the Warwick Castle weather guarantee gives you added peace of mind. If your experience is impacted by bad weather, Warwick Castle will offer you a free ticket to come back again. Check out the rainy day promise page to see all eligible days and selected return days.

**Save all year with a Warwick Castle annual pass**

If it’s just Warwick Castle you want to visit, there is also a single-attraction annual pass available, which excludes the rest of the Merlin attractions. With the Warwick Castle annual pass, you get unlimited access to the castle on more than 330 days a year, including live shows, attractions and the castle grounds for £49 per person per year.

## Family and group ticket discounts

**Parent and toddler tickets**

If visiting with a child aged five or under, you can take advantage of the parent and toddler ticket rate, starting at £21. There is plenty for little ones to enjoy on site, including the Zog playland, the Horrible Histories maze, birds of prey displays and more than 60 acres of beautiful grounds and gardens to run around.

**Book as a group and save 30 per cent**

Another smart way to save on castle entry is to club together and book as a group. If there are 10 or more people in your party, you can pre-book from just £18 per person. This includes entry to the castle, the grounds and all attractions on site.
