                                    One of Harrogate's best-loved agricultural shows returns this weekend as the 174th Ripley Show takes place in the grounds of Ripley Castle Park.

With livestock competitions, family entertainment, food, shopping and arena displays, hundreds of visitors are expected for another packed day celebrating rural life.

Chris Smith, chairman of Ripley Show, said:

“We’ve got all sorts going on and lots to look at - the cattle, sheep, red bull BMXing in the Main Ring, lots of bits for the kids to do and lots of trade stands.

“There’s always something to see, it’s a good little show to come to.”


Here's everything you need to know before you go…

**When is Ripley Show?**

Ripley Show takes place on Sunday (9th August).

The show is held annually on the second Sunday in August and celebrates farming, food, rural life and the local community.

**Where is it?**

The show is held in the grounds of Ripley Castle Park, near Harrogate.

The venue is well signposted on the day and free parking is available for visitors.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a71892a58f29b16031.jpg)


**What time does it start?**

The showground opens from 8:30am, with visitors expected from 9am.

Visitors are encouraged to arrive early to make the most of the day's attractions and competitions.

**What's on?**

There will be plenty happening throughout the day, including:

- Livestock competitions featuring cattle, sheep, horses, ponies and heavy horses.
- Red Bull BMX displays in the Main Ring.
- Dog agility, companion dog show and terrier racing.
- Vintage tractors and machinery.
- Trade stands and craft exhibitors.
- Children's classes and family activities.
- Baking, horticulture, handicrafts, photography, farm produce, wine and honey competitions.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a71892a59453aeb4ab.jpg)


**Can you enter on the day?**

Yes. While many competition entries closed ahead of the event, several activities can still be entered on show day.

These include the companion dog show, dog agility, terrier racing and the fancy dress competitions for mounted and unmounted entrants.

**Can I bring my dog?**

Yes. Dogs are welcome, provided they are kept on a lead at all times and owners clean up after them.

Dogs can also take part in the companion dog show, gun dog retrieve and terrier racing.

Advance tickets are available until midnight on Friday 7th August, with discounted prices available.

Tickets can also be purchased online or at the gate on show day, with both cash and card accepted.

Children under five go free and parking is free.
