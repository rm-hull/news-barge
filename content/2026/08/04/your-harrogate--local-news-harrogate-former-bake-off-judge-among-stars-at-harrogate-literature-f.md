                                    A former Great British Bake Off judge is among the big names announced for this year's Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

The annual festival returns from 11th to 25th October, bringing bestselling authors, broadcasters, politicians and entertainers to venues across Harrogate.

And former Bake Off judge, broadcaster and restaurateur Dame Prue Leith is among this year's headline acts.

She will discuss her latest book, 'Being Old…and Learning to Love It!', sharing her thoughts on ageing well, staying curious and finding joy in later life.

Acclaimed novelist Kate Mosse will also appear, discussing her new book Autumn, described as a celebration of the changing seasons that combines memoir with nature writing.

Sharon Canavar, Chief Executive of Harrogate International Festivals, said:

"We are delighted to announce some of the headliners for the Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival 2026.

"This year's diverse programme brings together some fabulous writers, with more big announcements still to come!"


Broadcaster and journalist Sophie Raworth will be the special guest at this year's Yorkshire Life Literary Lunch on 22nd October, where she will talk about her memoir Running on Air.

Comedian, presenter and author Julian Clary will introduce his latest murder mystery, The Midmorning Murders, while Booker Prize-winning novelist Sir Ben Okri will discuss his new book Waking the Warriors.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a71ac81f27432064bc.jpg)


International bestselling author Heather Morris, whose novel *The Tattooist of Auschwitz* became a global phenomenon, is also on the programme and will speak about her latest novel,*The Piano Teacher of Montparnasse*.

Political analyst Sir John Curtice will also appear, offering his insights into Britain's evolving political landscape.

Rachel Tunnicliffe, Senior Partner at Raworths Solicitors, said:

"We are proud to be the sponsors of this world class Festival which brings the power of stories and the joy of storytelling to Harrogate.

"Once again there is a whole host of big names and bestselling authors coming to our town, and we can't wait for what promises to be a memorable Festival this autumn."


The festival has previously been described as one of the UK's leading literary festivals and attracts thousands of visitors to Harrogate each year.

Events will take place at The Crown Hotel and Cedar Court Hotel from 11th to 25th October.

Tickets go on sale to Friends of the Festival on Wednesday 5th August, before general sale opens on Wednesday 19th August.

Visitors can book via the Harrogate International Festivals **website** or by calling the box office on 01423 562303.
