The US Army's stockpiles of crucial surface-to-surface missiles, such as ATACMS and Precision Strike Missiles, have been significantly depleted during a five-month conflict with Iran.

Sources indicate that "virtually all" of these specific weapons have been used, raising concerns about military readiness for future engagements and the ability to deter adversaries.

Other key munitions, including Patriot interceptors and Tomahawk cruise missiles, have also seen substantial depletion, with reports suggesting significant percentages expended.

Trump asserted that the US has "far more munitions than anyone in the world" and that defence companies are increasing production at record levels.

However, analysts warn that despite increased production, current supplies may be insufficient for a prolonged conflict, with the drawdown reflecting a strategy to avoid riskier attack methods.
