Harris, who portrayed the character in all nine seasons, told The Times that the entire sitcom is framed as a story told by an older Ted, meaning Barney's depiction was coloured by the subjective nature of the story.

He said that Barney was “the devil on Ted’s shoulder” and his on-screen behaviours were not necessarily “certified, or even true”.

Harris reprised his role as Barney in two episodes of the 2023 spin-off series How I Met Your Father.

How I Met Your Mother was nominated for 28 Emmy Awards over the course of its run.
