Allan Nascimento (UFC)

MMA fighter Allan Nascimento died at 34 years old on Monday morning from an apparent heart attack in his sleep.

The UFC announced his passing and extended condolences to his family, friends, and teammates during this difficult time.

Nascimento had been with the UFC since 2021, achieving a 4-2 record in the Octagon and a career record of 22-7, with 16 wins by submission.

His training gym, Chute Boxe in Brazil, paid tribute to him as a "warrior, friend, dedicated competitor and extraordinary human being."

Fellow fighter Charles Oliveira also honored Nascimento on social media, referring to him as a "brother" and expressing gratitude for their shared experiences.
