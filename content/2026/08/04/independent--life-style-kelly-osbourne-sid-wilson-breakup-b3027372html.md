Kelly Osbourne has shared a series of scathing social media posts about child support and co-parenting as rumors suggest that she has split from Slipknot guitarist Sid Wilson.

On Monday, the 41-year-old television personality posted several messages on her Instagram stories that appeared to be directed at Wilson, with whom she shares a three-year-old son.

In the posts, Osbourne demanded child support and got candid about the difficulties of co-parenting.

“Strange how the loudest defense of the guilty is rarely innocence… It’s distraction,” she wrote in a now-deleted post, as reported by *People*.

“When the mask slips, they don’t face the mirror they throw it at someone else. I also heard that when you break a mirror you get 7 years bad luck! Or is that just karma? This is why I always take accountability! As they say, some are sicker than others.”

![Kelly Osbourne shared a series of cryptic Instagram stories about co-parenting and child support without naming Sid Wilson](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/04/16/03/xxxx.jpg)

Osbourne then shared a quote with Lewis Capaldi's “Love the Hell Out of You” playing in the background.

“My son’s little heart once beat right beside mine. So trust me when I say… I will protect it at all costs,” she wrote.

She followed the post with another personal message about parenting.

“Wake the f***up take some responsibility I will not take your s*** anymore! I’m done,” Osbourne wrote. “I want to be happy and I have a beautiful son to raise. Leave me alone. Let me heal. I can’t protect you from yourself anymore.”

![‘My job is to protect my kids. Not your feelings. Not your expectations,’ Kelly Osbourne shared on her Instagram Stories](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/04/16/40/Screenshot-2026-08-04-at-12-35-53.png)

For another post, soundtracked to Everybody Loves An Outlaw’s “I See Red”, Osbourne shared a quote about how she chooses to protect her child “over anyone.”

“My job is to protect my kids. Not your feelings. Not your expectations. Not judgements I didn’t ask for,” the quote read. “My responsibility is to protect their wellbeing. Their nervous systems. Their emotional safety.”

“I will choose them every single time. Over anyone. Over everything,” the quote continued.

Osbourne continued: “Also, while I’m at it, can I have my dogs back and all my possessions. Oh yeah and maybe some child support?”

*The Independent* has contacted representatives for Wilson and Osbourne for comment.

The pair began dating in 2022 and welcomed their son Sidney later that year, before getting engaged in July 2025— the same month Osbourne’s legendary rocker father Ozzy died aged 76.

![Slipknot guitarist Sid Wilson and Kelly Osbourne reportedly called off their engagement in March 2026](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/04/16/16/xxxxx.jpg)

They reportedly called off the engagement seven months later in March 2026. At the time, *People* spoke to a source who claimed that Kelly “has been in a challenging headspace for several reasons. It’s been very emotional for her. She’s had a lot going on.”

Osbourne’s cryptic posts follow reports suggesting that Wilson had been removed from Slipknot after 28 years with the group.

On Friday, *TMZ* published a story, citing a source close to the band, who claimed that the DJ and keyboardist, 49, had been “permanently” let go.

Within hours of the report, 54-year-old Jim Root — who joined the band in 1999 — shared a cryptic message on his Instagram Story, seemingly in reference to the rumors.

“Don’t believe everything you read. Stop. Think. Take a breath,” he urged. “Sit with it for a while. Maybe. Just maybe even wait until there’s more information.”
