                                    Miriam Margolyes and Richard Ayoade are among the big names set to perform as Harrogate Comedy Festival returns this autumn.

Now in its 17th year, the festival will bring another packed programme of stand-up, improv, storytelling and family entertainment to venues across the town.

This year's line-up also features Harry Enfield, Alexei Sayle, Maisie Adam, Jason Byrne, Reece Shearsmith, Seann Walsh, Tez Ilyas, Paul Merton and Suki Webster.

The festival opens with A Star Wars Improv Show: The One-Man Made-Up Movie Parody before audiences can enjoy the Olivier Award-winning Showstopper! The Improvised Musical.

Harrogate Comedy Festival programmer Marcus Romer said:

"Harrogate Comedy Festival has become one of the highlights of our year, attracting some of the country's finest comedians alongside exciting new voices.

"We're delighted to once again bring such a varied programme to Harrogate, offering something for everyone - whether you're a lifelong comedy fan or simply looking for a great night out."


The popular Hyena Lounge Comedy Club also returns, showcasing a selection of comedians from across the UK circuit.

Yorkshire comedian Maisie Adam will bring her new tour Whatsherface to Harrogate before hosting Frank's Fund Comedy Gala.

Actress and broadcaster Miriam Margolyes will appear at Harrogate Convention Centre with her live show Full English, while Richard Ayoade is also among the headline acts appearing during the festival.

Families are catered for too, with Olaf Falafel bringing his Stupidest Super Stupid Show to the programme.

Other performers include Laura Smyth, Ed Patrick, Rachel Mars, Scummy Mummies, The 4 Johns and Daniel Kitson.
