                                    Police have issued CCTV images of a man following a shop theft in Harrogate.

It happened on the 28th June at Sophie Allport on James Street and involved the theft of crockery items.

A police spokesperson said:

“Please contact us if you recognise the male pictured on CCTV, as he may have information that will assist our investigation.


People with information should email PC1488 Bell on [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

*Please quote reference 12260122336 when providing information.*
