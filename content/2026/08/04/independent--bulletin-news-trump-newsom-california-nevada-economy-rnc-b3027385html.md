### Trump returns to California with Gavin Newsom squarely in his sights

![Trump returns to California with Gavin Newsom squarely in his sights]([https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/15/19/09/GettyImages-2195134171.jpeg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/15/19/09/GettyImages-2195134171.jpeg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp) 83w)

President Donald Trump is making a rare visit to California and Nevada this week to contrast his policies with Democrats on the economy, border security, and crime, aiming to strengthen Republican support for the upcoming November elections.

Trump is struggling with voter confidence on the economy, as an AP-NORC poll shows only 32% approval for his economic handling, with 69% of Americans describing the economy as 'poor' and citing high stress from the cost of essentials.

In California, Trump will attend a Republican National Committee fundraising dinner and is anticipated to criticize Democratic Governor Gavin Newsom, a prominent political rival and potential 2028 presidential candidate, continuing their history of public disagreements.

During his Nevada stop, Trump will discuss the economy, while state Democrats are actively campaigning to associate him with Governor Joe Lombardo and rising costs, highlighting local issues such as housing shortages and increasing energy demands.

Trump's efforts to promote his income tax cuts and a manufacturing renaissance are challenged by high interest rates, elevated gasoline prices, ongoing tariffs, and a national housing shortage, often causing his economic speeches to shift focus to political adversaries.
