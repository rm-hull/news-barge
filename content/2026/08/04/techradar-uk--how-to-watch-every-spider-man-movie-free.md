### Are there any other Spider-Man movies?

*Spider-Man*(1977),* Spider-Man Strikes Back*(1978) and* Spider-Man: The Dragon's Challenge*(1981) formed a trilogy starring Nicholas Hammond as the titular hero, and* Spider-Man* (1978) is a Japanese adaptation led by Shinji Todō.

*Spider-Man: Brand New Day*(2026) isn't yet available via home media, while* Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse* is set for release in June 2027.

There have also been numerous *Spider-Man* spinoffs, such as the lukewarmly received *Venom* trilogy, the *Spider-Ham: Caught in a Ham*(2019) and* The Spider Within: A Spider-Verse Story*(2023) shorts, and the critically and commercially disastrous* Morbius*(2022), * Madame Web*(2024) and* Kraven the Hunter* (2024).

Spider-Man has also appeared in *Captain America: Civil War*(2016),* Avengers: Infinity War*(2018), and* Avengers: Endgame* (2019).
