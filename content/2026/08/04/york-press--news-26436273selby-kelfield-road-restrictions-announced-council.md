Search the Public Notice PortalTemporary speed and traffic restrictions have been announced for Camblesforth Road, Selby, and Main Street, Kelfield.

A 30 and 40 mph speed limit will be enforced in Camblesforth Road from today (August 4) to 12 for Yorkshire Water works, with the order extending until September 4 if needed.

The restriction is due to Yorkshire Water installing apparatus.

Residents with queries should contact Yorkshire Water on 03451 242424.

Meanwhile, Main Street in Kelfield will close to vehicles from August 17 to 21, except for premises access, due to Northern Powergrid installing a new connection.

The closure order could last until October 17 if necessary, with alternative routes signposted.

The restriction is due to Northern Powergrid works to install a new connection.

Residents with queries should contact OCU Utilities Services Limited on 0113 3227545.
