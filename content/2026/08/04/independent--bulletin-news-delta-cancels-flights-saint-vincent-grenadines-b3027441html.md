Delta Air Lines is permanently discontinuing its service to Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, with the final flight from Atlanta to Argyle International Airport scheduled for September 5.

The route was initially expected to be a seasonal pause, resuming on December 19, but the airline confirmed its permanent removal from the schedule on May 23.

Delta stated it routinely evaluates factors such as customer demand, seasonality, and operating costs when making network decisions but did not provide a specific reason for this cancellation.

Passengers with bookings after September 5 will be contacted directly by Delta to arrange alternate travel options.

Travelers can still reach Saint Vincent and the Grenadines via other airlines, including American Airlines, JetBlue and Caribbean Airlines, which offer various routes from the U.S.
