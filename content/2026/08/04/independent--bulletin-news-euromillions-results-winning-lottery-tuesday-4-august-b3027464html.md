# EuroMillions results: Winning lottery and Thunderball numbers for Tuesday 4 August

- Tonight’s EuroMillions draw has offered players the chance to win a jackpot of £84million
- To win the prize, a player had to get five main numbers plus two lucky stars.
- In the UK, every EuroMillions entry automatically includes a unique "UK Millionaire Maker" code, entering you into an additional draw with a guaranteed £1 million winner in every draw.
- The winning EuroMillions numbers were **25, 30, 34, 46** and**50**. The Lucky Stars were** 1**and** 12**.
- The winning Thunderball numbers were **15, 24, 30, 35** and**37**. The Thunderball was** 8**.
