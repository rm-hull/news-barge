Swinney Bridge in Masham will be closed from Monday 10 August for 5 days.

This closure is to allow the council to undertake ground investigation works to inform the design of a replacement bridge.

Due to the nature of the work pedestrian access will not be maintained.

See diversion details at: [https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/…/roads-and…/roadworks-map](https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/…/roads-and…/roadworks-map)
