Thousands of fans turned out as British pop icon Holly Johnson rolled back the years at Scarborough Open Air Theatre last night.

The Frankie Goes To Hollywood frontman delivered a memorable performance, blending hits from his band days and solo career - as can be seen in these 32 photos from Holly Johnson's hit-filled Scarborough show.

Our reviewer Dave Lawrence said it was a fitting end to another great evening of music in Scarborough as "Johnson’s voice displayed no evidence of wear or tear since Frankie’s breakthrough performance on The Tube 43 years ago, which thrust the band into the nation’s consciousness".
