# Hospitals evacuated after magnitude 7.7 earthquake strikes Indonesia

- At least 20 people have been killed after a 7.7-magnitude earthquake struck off the Flores region in eastern Indonesia.
- The shallow undersea quake occurred at 5:58am local time at a depth of 10km, triggering several aftershocks and widespread damage.
- Rescuers in the port town of Maumere reported that six people were injured and two remained trapped under rubble as buildings collapsed.
- Hospitals were forced to evacuate patients to outdoor areas before an initial tsunami alert was safely lifted.
- Indonesia experiences frequent seismic and volcanic activity due to its location on the Pacific Ring of Fire.
