EMERGENCY services were called after a person got stuck in a lift in North Yorkshire.

Firefighters in Scarborough responded to the call and helped the individual at 6.22pm on Friday evening, using lift keys to safely release them.

  **Recommended reads**:

A spokesperson said: "Advice was given to the responsible person present."

  The call-out was one of a series of incidents attended by North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service overnight including **reports of smoke,** controlled garden fires and false alarms.
