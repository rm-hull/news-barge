A cannabis crop worth £118,000 has been uncovered on a terraced Middlesbrough street.

Officers found the grow - which was home to roughly 118 plants inside - on Benedict Street, in North Ormesby, on Friday (August 14).

Pictures taken from inside the property show a hoard of cannabis plants planted in black plant pots and underneath hanging lighting bars.

  A cannabis farm was found on Benedict Street in North Ormesby

The cannabis plants have since been removed and the farm dismantled.
