# Investigation launched after orca seen with suspected gunshot wounds

- Spanish officials have launched a formal investigation after Toñi, the matriarch of a critically endangered Iberian orca pod, was injured by suspected shotgun fire.
- Multiple projectiles remain lodged in the whale's dorsal fin, creating a severe risk of infection for the matriarch.
- Spanish authorities are coordinating with Portuguese officials as the injuries were first detected after the pod travelled from waters near Faro.
- The endangered Iberian orca population numbers just 30 to 40 whales and already struggles against food scarcity, pollution, and dense shipping traffic.
- Tensions between sailors and the pod have escalated over vessel damage, though scientists stress the orcas' interactions are social play rather than aggressive attacks.
