Drivers are being warned of possible delays as temporary road restrictions will be in place across the York area from Monday.

The measures have been put in place in different parts of the city to allow for telecoms works.

  **Recommended reads:**

Pottery Lane in Strensall will be affected from 8am on Monday, August 17 to 6pm on Wednesday August 19, while Kent Street in York will also have temporary traffic restrictions from 8am on Monday, August 17 to 6pm on September 25.

Farmers Way in Copmanthorpe will be affected from 9.30am on August 24 to 3.30pm on August 26.

Moor Lane in Bishopthorpe will also be affected from 8.30am on Monday, August 24 until 4pm on Wednesday, August 26.
