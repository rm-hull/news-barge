A road closure in Hillam has been extended due to essential works.

It is expected that Betteras Hill Road will remain shut for Yorkshire Water’s mains replacement until October 19, 2026.

The closure began on May 18, 2026.

Signs will show when the road is closed, and a local detour will be signed on site where appropriate.

For more information, residents can contact Yorkshire Water at 0345 124 2424.

For updates on all roadworks, visit North Yorkshire Council’s dedicated map at northyorks.gov.uk/roadworks-map.
