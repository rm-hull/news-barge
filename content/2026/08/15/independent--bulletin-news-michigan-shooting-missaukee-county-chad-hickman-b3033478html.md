# Six dead, including suspect, in rural Michigan shooting

- Five people were killed, and a suspect died following a series of violent incidents across two homes and a wooded area in Missaukee County, Michigan.
- Michigan State Police initially responded to a shooting at a home, where they discovered three people dead and one person wounded.
- A fourth victim was found dead at a second home as law enforcement searched for the suspect, identified as 39-year-old Chad Hickman.
- Authorities later found Hickman's vehicle near Whitlock Lake, where officers located his body along with a fifth deceased victim.
- Police are conducting a thorough investigation into the deaths, and victim identities are being withheld until family members are notified.
