# Jason Arday sent voice note to friend hours before his death

- Former University of Cambridge professor Jason Arday has died after being found unresponsive at a property in Battersea, with police treating the death as unexpected but not suspicious.
- Hours before his death, Prof Arday sent a voice note to a friend saying he felt he “couldn't go on” following intense media scrutiny over allegations regarding his academic background.
- Sir Simon Baron-Cohen, director of the Autism Research Centre at Cambridge, described Prof Arday as a “vulnerable autistic man” who was struggling with the loss of his reputation.
- Prof Arday, who became Cambridge's youngest Black professor in 2023, had previously been cleared of wrongdoing by Liverpool John Moores University following plagiarism allegations.
- Prominent figures, including National Education Union leader Daniel Kebede, stated Prof Arday was subjected to a brutal, politically motivated campaign targeting diversity, equity and inclusion policies.
