A stunning home has hit the market in a York street that provided the backdrop for a hit television drama.

The four-bedroom property is for sale in Claremount Terrace, off Gillygate, for £775,000.

Cameras rolled in the Victorian street in 2024 for the Channel 4 crime drama Patience, which is set in York.

  Filming for detective drama Patience in Claremont Terrace in April 2024 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Film crews were in Claremount Terrace in April 2024 to shoot the first season of the detective show.

The first series of Patience premiered last year and was the biggest drama of the year for Channel 4 – averaging 4.2 million viewers per episode – and the second season was the channel’s most-watched series so far in 2026.

Patience’s third season was announced earlier this year, with actors seen filming in York this spring for the new series.

Buyers now have the chance to own one of the properties in a street that featured on the show.

The end-terrace period property for sale in Claremount Terrace has been “thoughtfully extended and comprehensively upgraded to create a stylish and versatile family residence of the highest quality”, according to Carter Jonas, which is marketing the home.

  The four-bedroom property for sale in Claremount Terrace, York, for £775,000 

*(Image: Carter Jonas/Zoopla)*

Inside, an entrance hall leads to the living room at the front of the home and to the open-plan kitchen and family room.

The kitchen and family room forms part of the extension and is an “exceptional space designed for everyday living and entertaining”, Carter Jonas said.

A separate home office is also on the ground floor, as well as a shower room and toilet.

Upstairs, two bedrooms and the family bathroom are on the first floor.

Another two bedrooms are on the top floor “with excellent views across York”, Carter Jonas said.

Outside, the property has an enclosed landscaped garden to the rear. The home also has a garage space.
