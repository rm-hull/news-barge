New Zealand conclude their preparations for the massive Test series against South Africa as they take on the Bulls at altitude in Pretoria.

Dave Rennie’s side are two-from-two so far on tour after following a scratchy showing against the Stormers with an altogether more comprehensive win over the Sharks. This, though, could well be their toughest encounter yet, with plenty of experience in the ranks for the Bulls – perennial United Rugby Championship challengers.

A shift to the Highveld and Loftus Versfeld will provide another challenge for Rennie and his squad to deal with as the All Blacks boss looks to narrow down his selection for the Springboks. Richie Mo’unga has arrived on tour to bolster the playmaking options but is not yet involved – who will put their hand up for Test involvement with the first clash in Johannesburg just a week away?

*Follow all of the latest from the clash in Pretoria with our live blog below:*

## A handy trio of returnees

The Bulls have been boosted ahead of the URC season by all of Curwin Bosch, Thaakir Abrahams and Hanro Liebenberg returning to South Africa following strong stints in Europe. Bosch has never quite deliivered on the huge potential he showed at the start of his career but was well liked by Brive, where he spent the last two seasons, while the hot-stepping Abrahams showcased some of his strike-running ability at Munster

.The capture of Liebenberg feels a real coup – such a good leader, Leicester were reluctant to part with a player who had been on England’s radar. But the 30-year-old has a young, growing family, and made the difficult decision that returning home would be best for him and wife Danielle.

## Test spots up for grabs?

I *think* the New Zealand team for the first Test will look pretty similar to the side that began against the Sharks on Tuesday, but there might be a few spots still up for grabs. The second row, for one, feels competitive, with both Fabian Holland and Patrick Tuipulotu pushing to displace Sam Darry and Josh Lord – a pairing that went well in Durban.

There’s still Ardie Savea to come into the back row mix, and striking the right balance there will be key. Is Tupou Vaa’i a certain starter on the blindside? Or does Dave Rennie consider the different profile that a Simon Parker or Wallace Sititi might offer? Plenty to consider.

## Team news – New Zealand

Codie Taylor returns from injury to captain New Zealand in a boost for Dave Rennie with a week until the Springboks series. Beauden Barrett is stationed at full-back with Josh Jacomb installed at 10, while Anton Lienert-Brown and Rieko Ioane unite in an experienced centre partnership.

Six of those involved also featured against the Stormers on Tuesday, including both scrum halves. Richie Mo’unga joined up with the squad on Friday but is not involved.

## Team news – Bulls

Former Leicester flanker Hanro Liebenberg makes his return to Bulls colours as part of a strong back row also containing 31-cap Springbok Marcell Coetzee. Veteran Willie le Roux forms an exciting playmaking partnership with Curwin Bosch, while Thaakir Abrahams is another recent returnee to South Africa after a couple of strong years with Munster.

In a late boost for the side, Elrigh Louw and Johan Grobbelaar have beeen released from Springboks camp and will feature off the bench.

## Adjusting to altitude

New Zealand zipped up to Johannesburg after facing the Sharks on Tuesday, settling into Sandton ahead of next week’s first Test against the Springboks. Have they had enough time to adjust to the altitude? Loftus Versfeld isn’t quite at quite the same altitude as Ellis Park, but it is still up on the Highveld.

## Dave Rennie expecting toughest New Zealand test so far

All Blacks boss Dave Rennie is expecting the Bulls to provide the toughest challenge so far to his side.

“The Bulls have consistently been South Africa’s best franchise side, making the United Rugby Championship finals in four of the last five years. Loftus Versfeld is one of the most formidable rugby grounds in the world, so we are aware of the challenge in front of us and excited at what lies ahead,” Rennie said.

“This is also our final hit out before the first Test match against the Springboks, so it is an important game for us. There is healthy competition for Test spots so this is another opportunity for players to put their hands up for selection.”

## Richie Mo’unga returns to New Zealand squad to leave All Blacks selection dilemma

Fly half Richie Mo’unga has been recalled to the New Zealand squad to leave head coach Dave Rennie with a major selection dilemma for the four-Test series against South Africa.

Mo’unga has not appeared for the All Blacks since starting the 2023 Rugby World Cup final defeat to the Springboks, with the playmaker subsequently moving to Japan and thus ineligible for international selection.

The 32-year-old has returned to New Zealand with Canterbury in recent weeks, though, and has now been summoned to the touring squad after centre Billy Proctor was ruled out with a shoulder injury.
