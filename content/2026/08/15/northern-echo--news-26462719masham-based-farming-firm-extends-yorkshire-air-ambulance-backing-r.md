A partnership that has raised more than £150,000 for Yorkshire Air Ambulance has been extended for a further three years.

I’Anson Bros Ltd, one of the charity’s longest-standing principal partners, renewed its commitment to Yorkshire Air Ambulance at its recent Farmers’ Open Day, held at the company's new Dalton feed mill near Thirsk.

Kate I’Anson Mackay, financial controller at I’Anson Bros Ltd, said: "Yorkshire Air Ambulance has been a charity close to our hearts for many years.

"The rural community is at the centre of everything we do, and we know just how important the charity's service is to rural communities across Yorkshire.

  Yorkshire Air Ambulance will continue to benefit from a longstanding partnership with animal feed manufacturer I’Anson Bros Ltd after the company renewed its principal partnership for a further three years. 

*(Image: Supplied)*

"Renewing our principal partnership for another three years was an easy decision.

"We're incredibly proud of everything we've achieved together over the last eight years and look forward to continuing to support Yorkshire Air Ambulance as it delivers lifesaving care across the region."

Around 600 farmers, customers, and supporters attended the open day, which included guided tours of the new facility, demonstrations, exhibitor stands, and refreshments.

Yorkshire Air Ambulance joined the event with a merchandise stand and members of its fundraising team.

  I’Anson Bros Ltd has extended its Yorkshire Air Ambulance partnership for three years after helping raise more than £150,000 for the charity. 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The charity’s helicopter also made a special appearance before being called away to an emergency, offering a timely reminder of the vital service it provides.

The new Dalton mill, the first feed mill of its kind to open in the UK in around 30 years, represents a major investment in British agriculture and the company’s long-term growth.

I’Anson Bros Ltd supplies animal feed to farm, equine, and lifestyle customers across the UK and exports to more than 40 countries.

The partnership between I’Anson Bros Ltd and Yorkshire Air Ambulance began in 2017, when the company pledged to donate the savings generated from switching to energy-efficient LED lighting at its Masham mill.

Since then, more than £150,000 has been donated to support the charity’s work across the region.

The company’s support has funded essential equipment such as night vision goggles, which help crews operate safely in low-light conditions by identifying hazards and assessing safe landing sites.

Staff at I’Anson Bros Ltd have also taken part in fundraisers including Total Warrior to raise additional money and awareness for the charity.

Helen Berriman, corporate fundraising manager at Yorkshire Air Ambulance, said: "I’Anson Bros Ltd has been an exceptional supporter of Yorkshire Air Ambulance for almost a decade.

"Their continued commitment has helped fund vital equipment, strengthen our service, and enable us to continue delivering advanced pre-hospital critical care across Yorkshire.

"As a business with deep roots in the rural community, they understand just how important our service is to people living and working in rural areas.

"We are incredibly grateful that they have chosen to renew their principal partnership for a further three years, and we look forward to building on everything we've achieved together."

Yorkshire Air Ambulance receives no day-to-day government or NHS funding and relies entirely on donations from individuals, businesses, and communities.
