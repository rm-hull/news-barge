Drivers are being warned about potential delays to their journeys in York as roadworks take place.

A temporary traffic restriction will be in place on Gladstone Street, York, for water main works from 9.30am on Monday, August 17.

The works will end at 3.30pm.

Similar restrictions will be put in place for Mill Lane, Heworth, from Monday, August 24, at 8am, to Friday, August 28, at 5pm.

Landing Lane, York, will also have water mains works from 8am on Monday, August 24, to 5pm Monday, October 5, with restrictions in place for traffic.

Ashville Street, York, will have works from 9.30am on Wednesday, August 26, to 4pm Friday, August 28.
