# First look at The Bluey Movie debuts new style

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/cbyCctfw/poster.jpg?width=720)

- Disney has announced that The Bluey Movie will be released in cinemas worldwide on 6 August 2027, before streaming on Disney+.
- A first-look trailer and poster for the feature-length adaptation were unveiled at Disney's D23 fan event.
- Creator Joe Brumm will write and direct the film, which is produced by Ludo Studio in collaboration with BBC Studios.
- Original series voice actors Melanie Zanetti and David McCormack will reprise their roles as Chilli and Bandit.
- The plot centres on an action-packed day where a major surprise alters everything for the Heeler family.
