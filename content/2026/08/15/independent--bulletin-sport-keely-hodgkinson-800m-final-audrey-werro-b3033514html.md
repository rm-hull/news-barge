# Keely Hodgkinson misses out on third consecutive European 800m title

![Keely Hodgkinson had to settle for silver in Birmingham](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/15/00/a22ad19a4f498e78b69ee5aff58950c9Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg2ODM1MTcw-2.85566187.jpg)

- Keely Hodgkinson missed out on a third consecutive European 800 metres title after finishing second behind Switzerland's Audrey Werro in Birmingham.
- Werro claimed gold in a championship record time of 1:54.81, ahead of Hodgkinson's 1:55.01, with Femke Broeders-Bol taking bronze.
- The Swiss athlete won the final despite uncertainty over her fitness following a fall and reinstatement to the final following an appeal.
- Zharnel Hughes secured silver for Great Britain in the men's 200m final after being beaten by Germany's Owen Ansah.
- Hughes' medal took Great Britain's total tally to 10 medals at the European Championships.
