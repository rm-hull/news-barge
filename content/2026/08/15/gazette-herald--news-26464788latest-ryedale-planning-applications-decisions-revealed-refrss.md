Here are the latest planning applications submitted to North Yorkshire Council for Ryedale.

A temporary Christmas marquee at Castle Howard has been approved.

The structure will be sited on The Stray, near the Swiss Cottage Roundabout, and will be accompanied by associated plant.

The application was validated on June 8. Ref. No: ZE26/00487/FUL | Received: Mon 08 Jun 2026 | Validated: Mon 08 Jun 2026 | Status: Approved

A first-floor extension to the cricket pavilion on the Strensall to Sheriff Hutton Road has been approved.

The plans include external alterations and the installation of photovoltaic panels.

The application was validated on June 8. Ref. No: ZE26/00405/FUL | Received: Thu 07 May 2026 | Validated: Mon 08 Jun 2026 | Status: Approved

Permission has been granted to demolish a derelict bothy and erect a replacement building on land near Oswaldkirk.

The application was validated on April 30. Ref. No: ZE26/00379/FUL | Received: Thu 30 Apr 2026 | Validated: Thu 30 Apr 2026 | Status: Approved

The discharge of condition 17 attached to a previous planning approval at Pickering Showground has been decided.

Condition 17 of application ZE24/00421/73AM, dated February 6, 2025, has now been decided. Ref. No: ZE25/01101/DISCON | Received: Thu 02 Oct 2025 | Validated: Thu 02 Oct 2025 | Status: Decided
