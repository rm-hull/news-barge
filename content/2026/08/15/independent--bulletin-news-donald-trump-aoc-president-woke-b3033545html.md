# Trump thinks AOC is preparing for 2028 presidential race

- President Trump claims Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez is attempting to distance herself from past progressive stances on sanctuary cities and cash bail to prepare for a 2028 presidential run.
- AOC clarified that her main focus remains on winning a Democratic majority in the upcoming midterm elections rather than planning a presidential campaign.
- Prediction markets like Kalshi and Polymarket recently named AOC a top choice for the 2028 Democratic nomination, though traditional polls still rank her behind Kamala Harris and Gavin Newsom.
- Trump's claims linking cash bail reform and sanctuary cities to rising crime rates are inaccurate and contradicted by studies showing no connection between bail reform and increased violent crime.
- AOC has previously defended sanctuary cities, maintaining that they protect citizens from illegal search and seizure by federal immigration agents.
