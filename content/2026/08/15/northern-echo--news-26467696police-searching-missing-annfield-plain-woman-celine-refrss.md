Police have found a missing County Durham woman.

Celine was reported missing from her home in Annfield Plain, having last been seen at 7.10pm on Friday (August 14).

Durham Police said they were increasingly concerned for the welfare of the 61-year-old.

In a new update, Durham Police confirmed she has been found.

A spokesperson said: "Thank you to everyone who shared our appeal earlier to find Celine, from Annfield Plain.

"We are happy to let you know she has been found safe and well."
