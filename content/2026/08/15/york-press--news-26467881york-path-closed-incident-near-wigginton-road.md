## Busy York path closed after police incident near city centre

A police cordon has been put up by the entrance to the foot and cycle path off Crichton Avenue, York. *(Image: Newsquest)*

A POLICE incident has closed off a usually busy footpath and cycle route near York city centre.

  A police van is guarding the scene by the entrance to the footpath and cycle path on Wigginton Road near the junction with Crichton Avenue. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

A cordon has been put up today, Saturday, August 15, to prevent people from accessing Foss Islands Path near the junction of Crichton Avenue and Wigginton Road.

  A gate leading to the Foss Islands Path is sealed off. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Officers are currently at the scene guarding the cordoned off area while a police vehicle is parked up at the junction with Dennings Mews, a small residential street.

  A police van is parked up by the junction with Dennings Mews as officers guard the scene. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Police officers said they were unable to comment when approached by The Press at the scene.

  A police van is parked up as officers guard the scene of the unexplained incident near the Crichton Avenue/ Wigginton Road junction. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The footpath would usually take allow people to pass under a bridge beneath Wigginton Road.
