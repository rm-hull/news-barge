# Egg recall upgraded to the most serious level as FDA warns Americans to check ‘best by’ date

![The recall applies to 19 million eggs](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/15/19/51/GettyImages-103646957.jpg)

- The US Food and Drug Administration has upgraded a recall affecting 19 million eggs to a Class I, warning that salmonella contamination presents a risk of serious health consequences or death.
- Nearly 100 people have fallen ill and 26 have been hospitalized in the latest salmonella outbreak, although no deaths have been reported to date.
- The affected eggs were distributed across several states including Texas, Oklahoma, and Louisiana under brand names such as Kroger, Simple Truth, Brookshire's, Cal-Maine Sunups, and Country Morning.
- Health authorities strongly advise consumers who have purchased the recalled eggs to discard them immediately or return them to stores for a full refund. They will have sell-by or best-by dates between July 20, 2026 and August 17, 2026.
- The egg recall comes alongside a major US cyclosporiasis outbreak linked to contaminated lettuce, though Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. claims that the health crises remain under control.
