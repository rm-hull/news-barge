This North East beach has a fascinating industrial past, golden sands and dramatic cliff views - but many drive straight past it without knowing.

Tucked between Saltburn and Staithes lies Skinningrove's Cattersty Sands - a quiet stretch of coastline that remains one of the North East's best kept secrets

Just a short walk from Skinningrove village, the beach is a favourite among locals seeking a quieter alternative to some of the area's busier seaside destinations.

Sheltered by the surrounding coastline, it creates a peaceful setting for families, walkers and anyone looking to escape the hustle and bustle.

  Skinningrove 

*(Image: RAY SIMPSON/CAMERA CLUB)*

But Skinningrove stands out for more than just natural beauty, as stretched along the shoreline lies the remains of a historic jetty that once played a vital role in the ironstone industry across Teesside.

The village was at the heart of an industrial boom that transformed the region in the 19th Century.

What began as a small fishing and farming community changed dramatically after ironstone workings opened in 1848.

By 1880, the coastal jetty had been constructed to allow ships to transport the area's valuable iron ore to major industrial centres including nearby Middlesbrough.

Mining continued until 1958, with iron production remaining a key part of the village's identity well into the 1970s.

Today, the beach offers a striking contrast between the area's industrial past and the rugged coastal scenery that now attracts visitors and keen photographers.

Cattersty Sands also sits alongside the Cleveland Way National Trail, making it a popular stop-off for hikers exploring the rest of the dramatic North East coastline.

  The Charm Bracelet above Skinningrove; artwork on the Cleveland Way 

*(Image: Oliver Sherratt/CAMERA CLUB)*

Visitors looking to learn more about the area's history and the story of the ironstone minors can head to the nearby Land of Iron museum, on the site of Cleveland's first ironstone mine.

For those wanting to make a day of it, the village is home to several cafes and chippy's so you can still get that classic British seaside experience.

With its combination of unspoilt beach, rich history and spectacular coastal scenery, Skinningrove's Cattersty Sands remains one of the North East coast's most underrated destinations.
