Two paranormal and spiritual events described as the first of their kind for the North East are set to take place at a hotel in Darlington.

Hosted at Hallgarth Hotel in Coatham Mundeville, the events promise to bring a mix of witchcraft, mediumship, and haunted objects to the region.

They are scheduled for October 30 and October 31.

The first event, Dine with the Devil, will take place on October 30 and offers an evening of mediumship, witchcraft, and haunted objects.

Guests will be able to get up close to objects believed to be haunted while enjoying spiritual stories and connections.

The following night will host a Halloween special focused on the darker side of the paranormal.

Attendees can expect talks on witchcraft and mediumship, real-life paranormal experiences, and more cursed objects.

A paranormal investigation will also be offered as an optional extra.

A spokesman for the organisers said: "We are bringing two of the biggest weekends in spirituality witchcraft and paranormal to hit the north east! "If you love the darker side of the paranormal, this is the one for you."

Looking ahead, a much larger event is planned for 2027.

The Paranormal and Spiritual Festival will take place from September 10 to 12 and is described as one of the biggest of its kind.

Across three days, guests will experience mediumship, witchcraft, spiritual readers and healers, and paranormal investigations, as well as talks from guest speakers.

The festival will also feature a hypnotist, sound therapy, animal psychics, a haunted museum, and UFO specialists.

Workshops, past life regression, and real animal healing are also planned.

VIP tickets are available and include an evening meal each night.
