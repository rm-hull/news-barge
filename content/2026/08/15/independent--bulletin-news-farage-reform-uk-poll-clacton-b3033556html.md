# Polling experts predict Reform UK could soon fall to third place

- Polling experts predict Reform UK could soon fall to third place behind Labour and the Conservatives as its national support continues to decline.
- Leading pollsters say Nigel Farage is driving the party's downfall after previously propelling its rise, with Reform's brand heavily tied to his personal standing.
- Farage faces a parliamentary investigation over an unregistered £5 million gift from a donor, which could potentially trigger a second by-election in Clacton.
- Reform UK is losing voters back to both the Conservatives and Labour.
- The Conservatives have stabilised under Kemi Badenoch, posing further political trouble for Reform UK if the Tory recovery continues.
