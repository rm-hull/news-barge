# Buildings and cars damaged as earthquake hits southern Spain

- A 5.0-magnitude earthquake struck the Granada metropolitan area in southern Spain in the early hours of the morning.
- The regional government reported damage to houses and vehicles, though no injuries have been recorded.
- Andalusian emergency services successfully rescued several people from damaged buildings following the quake.
- Authorities declared a regional emergency in response to the seismic event.
- Antonio Sanz Cabello, vice-president of the Andalusian regional government, urged residents to stay calm and keep away from building facades.
