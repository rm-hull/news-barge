## Multinational coffee chain to open at North Yorkshire retail park

Starbucks is coming to Selby's Three Lakes Retail Park *(Image: Getty Images)</i>*

A multi-national chain is set to open a new shop in a North Yorkshire retail park.

Starbucks is coming to Selby Three Lakes Retail Park on Thursday (September 3).

The new coffee shop is set to open at 6am at the site off Bawtry Road.

  


  Tim Hortons in Selby closed on May 16 

*(Image: Google Maps)*

It will replace the former Tim Hortons restaurant, which closed to customers on Saturday (May 16).

Starbucks began hiring for the store in spring and still has an opening for a barista position.

The Press has approached Starbucks for comment.
