If you’re looking for a home that’s genuinely different, Skerne Studios, a gated development of just five individually designed homes near Darlington town centre, is certainly that.

Number 4 Skerne Studios – the property on the market – is a truly distinctive home that combines character, space and fabulous contemporary living.

While it may not be the largest of homes, this property is perfect for a single person or couple who want space to stretch out in and enjoy.

  Cap: Number 4 Skerne Studios has been beautifully designed around open-plan living 

*(Image: Carvers)*

At its centre is a light, bright and spacious open-plan living area with a vaulted ceiling with exposed beams, beautiful wood flooring that flows throughout and a double-height feature fireplace that provides an unmissable focal point.

To one end of this space is the living room, with double doors that open onto a balcony overlooking the River Skerne, creating a wonderful place to sit and enjoy the peaceful surroundings.

To the other end is the kitchen, thoughtfully designed to complement the living space.

  Cap: Number 4 Skerne Studios has been beautifully designed around open-plan living 

*(Image: Carvers)*

It is centred around a substantial central island with breakfast bar seating and is home to an excellent range of quality fitted units and integrated appliances, making it equally suited to everyday living and entertaining.

Behind the kitchen is a bedroom with an en-suite shower room, whilst a mezzanine level provides space for a second bedroom that enjoys views across the living area below thanks to glass balustrades.

This unique space has a WC and a Juliet balcony that enhances the feeling of light and space.

  Cap: Number 4 Skerne Studios has been beautifully designed around open-plan living 

*(Image: Carvers)*

Outside, the development enjoys attractive communal gardens, with Number 4 having the benefit of two allocated parking spaces accessed via a gated entrance.
