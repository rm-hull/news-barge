Manchester United face a reunion with former manager Ruben Amorim as they take on AC Milan in their final pre-season friendly before the start of the new Premier League season.

Amorim was sacked at the start of the year after a wretched spell at Old Trafford having arrived with considerable fanfare. The Portuguese coach is settling into Milan after being appointed by the Serie A club, though is already facing familiar issues as he looks to embed his favoured three-at-the-back formation.

Michael Carrick has done strong work since replacing Amorim as Manchester United manager, and will hope his squad can build more confidence and cohesion before the campaign proper begins. Carrick is eager to do more business in the transfer market but his selection here may be instructive with just a week to go until United’s opening game against Hull.

*Follow all of the latest from the pre-season friendly in Wroclaw with our live blog below* 

## Man Utd 2-2 AC Milan

62 mins: Here comes Marcus Rashford! His first Manchester United appearance since December 2024. He is back as a substitute for the first time this pre-season. Rashford is one of six changes.

ON: Rashford, Mbeumo, Mainoo, Lacey, Dalot, Martinez

## Man Utd 2-2 AC Milan

60 mins: This should be a second yellow for Pervis Estupinan after pulling down Dorgu when he was through on goal. Estupinan was booked for doing exactly that on Amad in the first half, but he somehow gets away with it!

Estupinan is then taken off.

## GOAL! Man Utd 2-2 AC Milan (Cisse 58')

But Milan are level again! A lovely switch of play to Chukwueze opens up the space. He guides a ball across the face of goal and the 19-year-old forward Alphadjo Cisse arrives at the back post to equalise. There’s a suspicion of offside. Either way, it was a tight. But United were cut open there.

## GOAL! Man Utd 2-1 AC Milan (DORGU 51')

Dorgu restores United’s lead. Guess what? It’s another defensive howler!

Once again, it is awful defending from Milan. Terracciano plays the ball across the face of goal and straight to Dorgu.

Dorgu says thanks very much and calmly finishes under the goalkeeper.

Another nightmare from Amorim’s new side.
