# Hit movie musical to make its West End debut in 2027

- The hit movie musical *The Greatest Showman* will make its West End debut at London's Theatre Royal Drury Lane in 2027.
- The stage adaptation follows a completely sold-out premiere run at the Bristol Hippodrome in March.
- Oliver Tompsett will star as PT Barnum alongside Samantha Barks as Charity Barnum in the production directed by Casey Nicholaw.
- EGOT-winning songwriters Benj Pasek and Justin Paul have created new stage-exclusive tracks to complement the original film score.
- The production marks the first title to be adapted for the stage by the Disney Theatrical Group.
