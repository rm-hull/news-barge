# Former Strictly contestant unveiled as new host of It Takes Two

- Ellie Taylor has been announced as the new host of the Strictly Come Dancing spin-off series *It Takes Two* .
- Taylor replaces outgoing presenters Fleur East and Janette Manrara as part of a wider revamp of the show.
- The announcement was made on 15 August during Emma Willis's BBC Radio 2 show.
- Taylor revealed the refreshed format will include candid group interviews and greater viewer involvement.
- The main Strictly Come Dancing series returns in September with new presenting trio Emma Willis, Josh Widdicombe and Johannes Radebe.
