Two branches fell from a tree onto telephone wires in Ryedale.

The alarm was raised on Friday evening and a North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service crew from Pickering responded to the call at 8.48pm.

  **Recommended reads:**

A spokesperson said that fire fighters used GP lines to safely remove the branches.

  The incident happened just a day after **Emilie North, 21, from Haxby, YOrk, had a lucky escape after a large branch fell on her Fiat 500** while she was driving down Haxby Road on Thursday night (August 13).

She said that she believes the branch fell after it was hit by a passing lorry, which she watched smack into an over-hanging tree on other side of the road from her car.
