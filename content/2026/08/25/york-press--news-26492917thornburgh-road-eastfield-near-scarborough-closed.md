## Road connecting North Yorkshire town to the A64 closed after crash

A road in North Yorkshire has been closed after a crash *(Image: Staff)*

A ROAD connecting a coastal North Yorkshire town to the A64 has been closed after a crash.

North Yorkshire Police have warned the public that Thornburgh Road in Eastfield, near Scarborough, is closed from the junction of Cayton Low Road and Cayton Approach.
