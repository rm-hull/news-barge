Aldi's Harrogate supermarket is set to reopen this week following a temporary closure for refurbishment.

The Oak Beck Road store will welcome customers back at 8am on Thursday (27th August) after closing last week.

Improvements are expected to include additional shopping space, energy-efficient fridge doors and enhancements to areas including fresh food, bakery and health and beauty.

The work forms part of Aldi’s £300million investment programme to upgrade and extend stores across the UK.

The Harrogate branch opened in 2016 and is currently Aldi’s only supermarket in the town.

Its nearest store is on Swallowtail Way in Knaresborough.
