ALDI has closed its Harrogate store for a revamp.

The store on Oak Beck Road closed on Saturday but is due back open on Thursday at 8am.

The upgrade forms part of a £300 million programme from the German giant to revamp 25 UK stores this summer.

The planned changes will vary by location but will focus on creating a more spacious shopping environment.

Improvements will include energy-efficient fridge doors, the use of natural refrigerants to reduce carbon emissions, and upgraded sections for bakery, health and beauty, and fresh food.

The Press approached Aldi for comment on its Harrogate improvements but was only told the store will be back open on Thursday.

But a sign at the store, which opened in 2016, said: “This store will close temporarily while we work behind the scenes to create a new and exciting shopping experience for you.

“We’re making changes to give you an even better place to shop and we think you’re going to love the new look.”

Last month, the Aldi in Fulford Road re-opened after a refurbishment.
