## Much-loved brand returns to North Yorkshire with 'biggest' sale so far

 *(Image: Le Creuset)*

A POPULAR cookware company Le Creuset says it will host its ‘biggest sale ever’ in North Yorkshire next month.

The ‘Factory to Table’ event is set for the Great Yorkshire Events Centre in Harrogate from September 25-27 from 9am to 6pm.

This follows the success of last year's event, which was the biggest in the UK at the time and the first held in the north of England - bringing thousands who queued for hours with promises of discounts of up to 50 per cent on a range of cookware.

The event promises 'more cast iron than ever before', including rare and limited-edition pieces not typically available in UK stores or outlets.

The sale will include limited-edition colours, rare shapes and seasonal designs, including pumpkin and petal casseroles.

  Le Creuset cookware and kitchenware. 

*(Image: David Cotsworth)*

The sale will run from 9.45am to 7pm on Friday, 9am to 6pm on Saturday, and 9am to 5.30pm on Sunday - with last entry 45 minutes before closing each day.

Tickets are required and will be released in advance at [https://feverup.com/m/720912,](https://feverup.com/m/720912,) with a waitlist available for early access to presale tickets.
