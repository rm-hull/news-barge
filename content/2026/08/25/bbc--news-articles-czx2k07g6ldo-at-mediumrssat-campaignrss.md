**Dolly Parton, the country music superstar from the Smoky Mountains of Tennessee, rose from humble beginnings to become one of the most beloved entertainers in the world.** 

![Dolly Parton, aged 5, with her family 1951 in a black and white image. She stands in a sleeveless white dress while adults holding children stand out of focus behind her.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/e256/live/a4dff130-4896-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

Dolly Parton, bottom right, aged five

Over a career spanning more than 60 years, she sold more than 100 million records, wrote more than 3,000 songs and won 11 Grammy Awards, including a lifetime achievement honour.

Yet she has always described herself first and foremost as a songwriter. "It's my way of expressing myself. It's my therapy," she once said.

![Dolly Parton stands in a long sleeved patterned dress in small room with flowered wallpaper and window curtains. Above her on the wall is a slightly crooked portrait of a man. Her family, adults and children, are also crowded into the frame of the black and white image family Christmas in 1960.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/13ae/live/751c28b0-4896-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

Dolly Parton grew up in poverty in a one-room cabin on the banks of the Little Pigeon River in Tennessee's Smoky Mountains, the fourth of 12 children.

![Dolly Parton, age 14, with short hair and a slight smile, in a black and white high school portrait., She wears a dark shirt and pendant necklace.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/5e12/live/1a357a90-4897-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

Dolly Parton, aged 14, at Sevier County High School in 1960

She performed on local radio and television as a child and recorded her first single at 13.

![Country singer Dolly Parton poses for a portrait in 1965 in Nashville, Tennessee. In the black and white image, she has a massive beehive updo and a wide smile. A light-coloured shirt is slung over one shoulder.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1056/cpsprodpb/9d97/live/7e6ff060-4de9-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

Dolly Parton pictured in 1965

After moving to Nashville the day after graduating from Sevier County High School, Dolly Parton scored her first solo hit with Dumb Blonde in 1966.

![Dolly Parton and her collaborator Porter Wagoner perform onstage in circa 1967. She has a blond bob and a patterned dress and sings into the microphone with Porter, who plays guitar and is in a flashy suit and tie.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/79fc/live/6b0b60e0-4899-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

Country singer Dolly Parton and her collaborator Porter Wagoner perform onstage in circa 1967.

In 1967, country star Porter Wagoner invited Parton to join The Porter Wagoner Show, giving her a regular role on his nationally syndicated television programme as well as a place in his touring stage show.

Her breakthrough came with the album Hello, I'm Dolly.

![Dolly Parton photographed at the Grand Ole Opry. She wears a suit with bell sleeves and smiles as she stands by a microphone](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1920/cpsprodpb/f537/live/786c9220-4dfa-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

Parton performing at the Grand Ole Opry

Dolly Parton became a member of the Grand Ole Opry on January 4, 1969.

She has described the Opry as the "church of my heart" and said performing there was a lifelong dream.

![Dolly Parton performing live onstage at the UK Country Music Festival, playing  a banjo. She wears her hair big and blond and sits before a microphone in a plae pink bedazzled shirt and pants.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/2b68/live/78bad9a0-489e-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

In one remarkable 1973 writing session, Parton penned two of her signature hits – Jolene and I Will Always Love You.

Having written thousands of songs across her decades-long career, Parton said songwriting was her greatest form of self-expression.

![Dolly Parton poses for a portrait session dressed as a playboy bunny, 1978 in Los Angeles, California. She has a wide smile and holds her bowtie in both hands.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/a683/live/266a81b0-4e15-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

Parton dressed as a playboy bunny in 1978

![Dolly Parton poses for a portrait session in 1978. She holds a microphone against a black backdrop and wears a bright yellow flowing outfit.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1920/cpsprodpb/7d7d/live/32894990-488e-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

Known for her larger-than-life image, Dolly Parton combined rhinestones, wit and charm to sustain a career that crossed from country to mainstream pop.

![Parton performs on stage in 1978, in a pale shawl and before a big crowd.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/b45e/live/5ed10580-48a0-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

She headlined festivals and became a global star with albums such as Here You Come Again.

![Kenny Rogers, in a white suit, and and Dolly Parton in a dark fitted suit top over a white bustier, sing together](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/fea4/live/e9a68790-4d30-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

Kenny Rogers performing with Parton in New York around 1985

Parton also became known for memorable collaborations, notably with Kenny Rogers, whose duet Islands in the Stream became one of the defining hits of the 1980s.

![Jane Fonda, Lily Tomlin, Dolly Parton, Dabney Coleman in a promotional image for 9 to 5.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1056/cpsprodpb/90b1/live/8dc674c0-4890-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

Workplace comedy film 9 to 5 starred Jane Fonda, Lily Tomlin, Dolly Parton and Dabney Coleman

While Dolly Parton is best known for her decades-spanning music career, her work as an actor has also left a lasting impression on film audiences.

Parton's breakout screen role came in the 1980 workplace comedy 9 to 5, where she starred alongside Jane Fonda, Lily Tomlin and Dabney Coleman.

Her portrayal of Doralee Rhodes combined comic timing with warmth, helping to redefine expectations of performers crossing over from music into film.

![Dolly Parton in a bright red flouncy dress and the cast in costume of the Best Little Whore House in Texas](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1920/cpsprodpb/e5d9/live/dddf0910-4893-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

Parton also starred in 1982's Best Little Whore House in Texas

Parton starred alongside Burt Reynolds in the film

She followed this with roles in films such as The Best Little Whorehouse in Texas, alongside Burt Reynolds, and Steel Magnolias.

The latter featured an ensemble cast including Julia Roberts and Sally Field alongside Sylvester Stallone.

![Shirley Maclaine, Dolly Parton, Olympia Dukakis, Sally Field, Julia Roberts and Daryl Hannah smile in a group photo, They all wear black.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1056/cpsprodpb/59a2/live/03b0aac0-4896-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

Steel Magnolias starred Parton, Shirley Maclaine, Olympia Dukakis, Sally Field, Julia Roberts and Daryl Hannah

![Dolly Parton in 1984 wears a fitted plaid shirt and jeans and a cowboy hat. She smiles widely at the camera as she tilts her head to the side.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2048/cpsprodpb/192d/live/2cf7f680-4e0f-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

In these performances, Parton demonstrated a balance of humour and emotional depth, often anchoring scenes with a distinctly understated presence.

![Dolly Parton  waves from a big yellow construction truck at the ground breaking ceremony for her Dollywood theme park, September 1985](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/8b5b/live/6f122740-4d27-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

Parton opened her Dollywood theme park in 1985

In 1986 Dolly Parton opened Dollywood, a theme park in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee, near her birthplace.

![Dolly Parton , in a red skirt suit, speaks into a microphone as she sits on a Ferris wheel chair.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/2a87/live/0b456b20-4d2a-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

It became a major tourist attraction - one of the most visited regional theme parks in the USA.

The park's success helped fund Parton's charitable foundation and literacy programme.

![Dolly Parton speaks on a stage in the background, while in the foreground a large book with the words "Dolly Parton, Imaginary Library" sits open](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1280/cpsprodpb/8c2e/live/489fa2f0-4d2b-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

Through her Imagination Library, founded in honour of her father who could not read, Dolly Parton has given away more than 100 million free books to children around the world.

![Parton reads her book "Coat of Many Colors," to children as she makes it the 100 millionth book that Imagination Library. She wears reading glasses and a beige and brown shirt. Behind her is a pile of books and large red chairs.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1920/cpsprodpb/ae50/live/3fc59850-4d2c-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

Parton reading her book Coat of Many Colours to children in 2018

![Parton kneels as she speaks to two young children at the launch of her Imagination Library book project in 2007](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2048/cpsprodpb/374b/live/e8f32c60-4d4c-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

Her own book, Coat of Many Colours, was the 100 millionth book that programme donated to the US Library of Congress collection.

A council in Scotland announced it would dedicate a day to Dolly Parton every year to mark the impact of her reading scheme.

![Dolly Parton talks and laughs with Hank Williams Jr on stage, while two stage crews work around them. She wears a bright red shirt with tassels.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/f534/live/89f0a6b0-4d48-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

Parton personally donated millions to medical research and disaster relief.

The singer also led several major disaster-relief efforts, most notably creating the My People Fund after the 2016 Great Smoky Mountains wildfires which distributed about $10m to families who lost homes.

She lined up an all-star list of performers for a three-hour telethon to raise money.

In 2024, she pledged $2m for Hurricane Helene relief in East Tennessee and Appalachia, combining a personal donation with matching support from her businesses and foundation.

![Dolly Parton at the Glastonbury Festival wearing her hair long and down. She wears a white bedazzled fitted top and pants](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/d4f5/live/0f2dd070-4d4c-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

Parton performed in the "legends slot" at Glastonbury in 2014

![Dolly Parton plays a white guitar on stage a the Glastonbury Festival](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/b3e4/live/f09ffa40-4d4e-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

![Dolly Parton performs with Richie Sambora from Bon Jovi at the Glastonbury Festival](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/7795/live/fbc38a40-4d4e-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

Parton's 2014 Glastonbury Festival appearance quickly became one of the defining "Legends" slot performances in the event's history.

Drawing one of the festival's biggest-ever daytime crowds - estimated at more than 180,000 - she turned the Pyramid Stage into a mass singalong, with renditions of Jolene, 9 to 5 and I Will Always Love You prompting scenes of near-universal audience participation.

![Dolly Parton sits on stage at  Opry show. The backdrop is blue and red with a large screen saying it celebrates her 50 years at the Opry. A small band is on stage with her.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/5e46/live/3e1fcac0-4de0-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

In 2019, Parton celebrated 50 years as an Opry member with a major televised special called Dolly Parton: 50 Years at the Opry.

The event featured performances and tributes from other country artists.

Her decades-spanning relationship with the Opry helped cement its status as both a guardian of country music tradition and a stage for modern stars.

![Dolly Parton in Christmas on the Square. She wears white and sits on a kitchen counter, and another actor stands beside her.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1920/cpsprodpb/e406/live/dd91f620-4d57-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

Parton gradually began to step away from major touring, saying she no longer wanted to spend long stretches on the road.

Instead, she focused on selective live shows, new recording projects and screen appearances including Dolly Parton's Heartstrings, Mountain Magic and Christmas on the Square for Netflix.

![Dolly Parton wears a blue top and a matching face mask as a doctor in a white coat and purple latex gloves prepares to inject her shoulder.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/d8be/live/f7c62110-4d52-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

Dolly Parton encouraged her fans to get the Covid vaccine during the pandemic

In 2021, Parton publicly received a Covid-19 vaccine at Vanderbilt University Medical Center, using the moment to encourage others to do the same.

She sang an adapted version of her hit Jolene, singing: "Vaccine, vaccine, vaccine, vaccine, I'm begging of you, please don't hesitate."

![Dolly Parton speaks on stage during the 37th Annual Rock and Roll Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony. She is a a glittery stripped silver and black outfit and raises both her hands in the air.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/0449/live/1c029610-4d50-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

Parton performing at the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony in 2022

![Dolly Parton performs during the 37th Annual Rock and Roll Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1920/cpsprodpb/2c00/live/4aff16f0-4d50-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

![Inductee US singer-songwriter Dolly Parton performs during the 37th Annual Rock and Roll Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony at the Microsoft Theatre on November 5, 2022, in Los Angeles, California.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/9db7/live/5251efe0-4d50-11f1-bd52-e755d604ece4.jpg)

Parton's 2022 induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame marked a characteristically self-aware turn.

Having initially said she had not "earned" the honour, she later embraced it with humour - declaring "I'm a rock star now" - before performing her new song Rockin', written especially for the ceremony, a symbolic step toward her later rock album Rockstar.

![Dolly Parton performs with the Dallas Cowboys cheerleaders during halftime in the game between the Cowboys and the Washington Commanders at AT&T Stadium.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1920/cpsprodpb/a4b8/live/4ebebaf0-4de8-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

In 2023, Parton delivered another headline-grabbing live moment when she performed at the Dallas Cowboys' Thanksgiving halftime show, appearing in a rhinestone-studded Dallas Cowboys Cheerleaders outfit.

![Dolly Parton speaks during a news conference for her show Dolly: An Original Musical, Tuesday, Jan. 28, 2025, in Nashville, .](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1920/cpsprodpb/9a47/live/7e902500-4d59-11f1-ac78-2112837ce2aa.jpg)

More recently, Parton continued to balance personal loss with new creative work.

Following the death of her husband Carl Dean in 2025, she released If You Hadn't Been There in tribute, while also continuing development of Dolly: A True Original Musical, the stage production based on her life and career.

![Dolly Parton gives a speech after Ozzy Osbourne won the Lifetime Achievement Award during The BRIT Awards 2026 at Co-op Live on February 28, 2026 in Manchester, England.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1920/cpsprodpb/139a/live/5b2d1dd0-4e11-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

In early 2026, Parton gave a pre-recorded tribute speech for Ozzy Osbourne at the BRIT Awards in Manchester, where Ozzy received a posthumous Lifetime Achievement Award.

Her speech was short but was one of the emotional highlights of the ceremony.

![A 1978 photo of Dolly Parton onstage in Madison Wisconsin.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/0a80/live/ea4ed9d0-4e12-11f1-b55d-0f258dce1735.jpg)

Parton had been due to return to the stage for a short Las Vegas residency, but announced this month that she was cancelling the run because of health issues, telling fans she needed "a little more time to heal".

She will remain a cultural icon remembered for her generosity and humour, as well as her extraordinary songwriting - the craft at the heart of a catalogue that has shaped modern country music and crossed generations.
