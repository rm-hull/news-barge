A Harrogate jeweller has revealed the UK's most popular proposal locations and launched a competition for engaged couples.

Beaverbrooks on James Street has created a ‘Proposal Map of the UK’ using Instagram hashtag data.

The Lake District topped the rankings with 4.7million hashtags, followed by the London Eye and Tower Bridge.

York Minster was placed 12th, while other locations on the list include the Peak District, Isle of Skye, Stonehenge and Edinburgh Castle.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a8d8d635d9be18f13d.jpg)


To mark the launch, Beaverbrooks is inviting couples across Harrogate to share the story of where they got engaged.

One couple will win up to £1,000 towards their wedding jewellery.

Clare Watson, Branch Manager at Beaverbrooks Harrogate, said:

“A proposal is one of life’s most important milestones, and finding the perfect setting can make the moment even more memorable.

“We created our proposal map to inspire anyone thinking of asking the big question, helping them discover some of the UK’s most beautiful places to get engaged.

“But we also know that every proposal is unique, and the most meaningful locations are often those with a personal connection.”


The top proposal spots in the UK, by hashtag data, are:

1. Lake District – 4.7M
2. London Eye – 3.5M
3. Tower Bridge – 3M
4. Peak District – 2.5M
5. Isle of Skye – 1.1M
6. Stonehenge – 1M
7. Edinburgh Castle – 791k
8. Glen Coe – 689K
9. Brecon Beacons – 514K
10. Giants Causeway – 376K
11. Arthur’s Seat – 317K
12. York Minster – 233K
13. St Michael’s Mount – 165K
14. White Cliffs of Dover – 79.5K
15. Royal Crescent – 54.3K

*Couples have until Friday 25th September to enter through the Beaverbrooks website**here**.*
