Police are investigating after a car was "deliberately" rammed in Batley.

Officers were called to the vicinity of Howley Walk and Broomsdale Road in Soothill just after 3.30am this morning and found a woman injured.

West Yorkshire Police said: "At 3.32am today, police received reports of a disturbance in the street in the vicinity of Howley Walk and Broomsdale Road in Soothill, Batley.

"Officers attended and established a woman, in her fifties, had been injured when a car was deliberately rammed into a black Nissan Qashqai.

"She was taken to hospital for treatment to injuries that were not considered life threatening."

The suspect vehicle drove off, and the occupant of the rammed car left the scene.

A scene was in place but has since concluded.

Forensic examinations have been carried out, and enquiries are ongoing.

Anyone with information is asked to contact police on 101, quoting crime reference 13260483988.
