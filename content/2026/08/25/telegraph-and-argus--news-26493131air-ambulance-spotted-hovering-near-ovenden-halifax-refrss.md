An air ambulance was spotted in a village yesterday afternoon.

Emergency services attended Ovenden in Halifax on Monday.

The force says this was following a report of a concern for safety incident.

A man was taken to hospital with injuries, not believed to be life-threatening.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "This was a concern for safety incident where a man was taken to hospital with injuries that were not life threatening."

On Tuesday, a spokesperson for the Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 4.40pm yesterday (August 24) to report an incident near Ovenden Wood Road in Halifax.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including an ambulance and the air ambulance, and one patient was conveyed to hospital by road."
