A man has died following a crash on a main road in North Yorkshire.

The incident happened on the A1246, at the junction with Holyrood Lane near Selby, at around 3.10pm on Sunday (August 23).

The man was riding a motorcycle at the time and was pronounced deceased at the scene.

  


North Yorkshire Police said: “His family are being supported by specially trained officers, and have requested privacy at this difficult time.

“Officers want to hear from anyone who witnessed a Honda CBR 1000 motorcycle prior to the collision, or who may have relevant dashcam, CCTV or other footage from the area.

“Anyone with information that may assist the investigation is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police.

“Please email [sciu@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:sciu@northyorkshire.police.uk) - quoting the reference 12260164723 - or call 101.”
