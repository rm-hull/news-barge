A crew was called to a North Yorkshire property yesterday evening (Monday) following reports of a smell of smoke.

The Malton crew attended the property in Wold Street in Norton at 9.28pm.

They found a small grill fire, which they extinguished using a hose reel jet and breathing apparatus.
