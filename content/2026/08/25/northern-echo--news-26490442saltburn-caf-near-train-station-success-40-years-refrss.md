Whether you're after a full breakfast before a day at the beach, a coffee while waiting for a train, or cocktails and live music as the sun goes down, this East Cleveland café has it all.

Located in the heart of Saltburn at Station Square, Signals has been serving locals and visitors since 1986, earning a reputation for good food, quality coffee and a welcoming atmosphere.

The dog-friendly café and bar sits just moments from the famous North Yorkshire coastline and railway station, making it a popular stop-off for commuters, day-trippers and residents throughout the year.


By day, Signals operates as a bright and modern café serving breakfast, brunch and lunch.

The menu focuses on homemade dishes and locally sourced ingredients, with customers able to choose from everything from full breakfasts and smoothie bowls to loaded hash browns, bao buns and homemade soup.

Those looking for something heartier can tuck into bespoke burgers, classic fish and chips, toasted sandwiches, and jacket potatoes.

Coffee and cake lovers are also well catered for, as Signals serves its own Runway Coffee as well as a selection of homemade baked treats - which can be enjoyed inside the café or collected from the venue's Side Hatch for those on the move.

  Signals Saltburn have plenty of sweet treats on offer to go alongside their bespoke coffee. 

*(Image: Tees Valley Combined Authority)*

Thanks to its location overlooking the station, it's also an ideal spot to sit by the window with a coffee and watch the world go by.

On Friday and Saturday evenings, Signals transforms from a bustling café into a cosy bar where customers can enjoy cocktails, specially selected wines and live local music.

The relaxed atmosphere and regular entertainment make it a popular destination for locals and visitors on a night out in the town centre.

The venue prides itself on creating a welcoming environment built around good food, good drinks and good company.

The staff at Signals described it as a place for "great vibes, good tunes and a feel-good spirit".

Nearly four decades after opening, Signals remains a well-established part of Saltburn's hospitality scene, offering everything from morning coffees and brunches to evening drinks and live music under one roof.
