Kitchen retailer Magnet has officially opened its new showroom in Harrogate town centre.

The store opened its doors on Albert Street today (Tuesday 25th August), following the relocation of Magnet’s Harrogate branch from Plantation House on Ripon Road.

Mayor of Harrogate Councillor Viv Poskitt attended the official opening alongside members of the showroom team.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a8d4caf49aa92604b7.jpg)


The new store features a range of kitchen displays and storage solutions, with free design appointments available for customers planning a new kitchen.

The Harrogate team is led by Store Manager Michael Higgins, alongside kitchen designers Jonathan Warner and Robert Goldsbrough and Trade Sales representative Tom Newton.

*Magnet has more than 150 showrooms across the UK, including other Yorkshire branches in Guiseley, Bingley and Skipton.*
