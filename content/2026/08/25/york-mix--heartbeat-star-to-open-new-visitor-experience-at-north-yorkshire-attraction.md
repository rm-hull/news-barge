TV star to open new exhibition based on beloved North Yorkshire police drama.

The family attraction World of James Herriot in Thirsk will open a unique *Heartbeat Exhibition* on**Saturday 29 August**.

The new exhibition will be unveiled David Lonsdale, best known for playing David Stockwell in the ITV period police drama series *Heartbeat*.

*Heartbeat* is based upon the *Constable* series of novels written by Nicholas Rhea, the pen-name of former policeman Peter Walker, and produced by Yorkshire Television (now merged with ITV). 

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Heartbeat-Exhibition-at-World-of-James-Herriot-1000x561.png)

The series is set in the North York Moors during the 1960s, with plots centred around the fictional village of Aidensfield, portrayed by Goathland in the TV series. It ran for 18 years and is still one of the most popular repeat shows, aired on ITV3 several times a day.

World of James Herriot managing director Ian Ashton said: “I’m delighted that The World of James Herriot has been entrusted with the artefacts of the late Peter N Walker (Nicholas Rhea).

“The opportunity for the World of James Herriot to become the home for his TV series legacy, books and memorabilia is a welcome addition and complements the story of Alf Wight (James Herriot) whose *All Creatures Great and Small* BBC TV series was a forerunner to*Heartbeat* and of similar TV genre, both watched by millions.”

Nicholas Rhea’s daughter Sarah Walker added: “Alf Wight (or James Herriot) was our local vet and asked Dad, who’d had a few crime books published, for advice on an idea for some stories about life as a country vet. Dad had recently had his own idea for stories about the life of a country bobby rejected with the words: “There’s no call for Yorkshire humour” so passed this sage wisdom on to Alf! Alf tried anyway – and the rest is history!

“Herriot’s success inspired Dad to not give up and finally in 1979 *Constable on the Hill* was published. We are thrilled as a family to be working with The World of James Herriot to preserve my dad’s legacy alongside the great work they have already done for James Herriot.”

The new *Heartbeat Exhibition* has been installed adjacent to the film-showreel area of the World of James Herriot close to its gardens and replica TV studios.

Ian Ashton continued: “We are extremely grateful to Mrs Rhoda Walker, widow of the late author, to Sarah, and the rest of the family of Nicholas Rhea (Peter N Walker) for allowing us to organise the new exhibition, to Jason Bullard who has helped with various artefacts including the iconic period police motorcycles and to Lee Jones for the many photographs for the exhibition.

“It all fits perfectly with James Herriot as we continue to enjoy demand for screen tourism visits to North Yorkshire’s Herriot Country in and around the Yorkshire Dales, and of course, Heartbeat Country, which includes Whitby, Goathland and the North York Moors.

“We will give Heartbeat fans a permanent, secure location where their memories can be shared alongside All Creatures Great and Small fans, many of whom love both TV series.”
