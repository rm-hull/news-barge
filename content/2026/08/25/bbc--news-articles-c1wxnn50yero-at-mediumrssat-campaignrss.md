![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: "We will always love you Dolly."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/c68c/live/6b94a390-a0c9-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

The death of country music legend and philanthropist Dolly Parton at aged 80 dominates Wednesday's papers. "We will always love you Dolly" says the Daily Star as the paper reports the singer had been struggling with poor health.

![The headline on the front page of the Sun reads: "We will always love you, Dolly."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/6d27/live/890d0ac0-a0c9-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

The Sun follows with the same headline, saying music fans worldwide are in mourning over the music icon's death. It also quotes US President Donald Trump's pledge to lower the US flag across the country to honour Parton, calling her death "a true loss for millions".

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: "We will always love you, Dolly."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/904e/live/905c6140-a0c9-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

The Daily Mirror pays tribute to the "country icon" with a picture of Parton in one of her signature glittering outfits.

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: "Dolly Parton, queen of country, dies at 80."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1536/cpsprodpb/83b9/live/03912d80-a0ca-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

The Independent calls Parton the "queen of country". It also quotes the statement from her team, who remembered her as "a rhinestone life that shone bright enough for the world to see".

![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: "Back review on electoral reform, MPs tell Burnham."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/784/cpsprodpb/4e3e/live/a7b73b30-a0c9-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

The Guardian features Parton in her own words, saying: "I always wanted to be a star. I just seemed natural to me." Elsewhere, the paper's other top story says Prime Minister Andy Burnham will face a test of his support for electoral reform in his first week in parliament with almost 100 Labour MPs supporting a bid for a new national commission.

![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: "Boycott fears over Burnham's Israel plan."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/809/cpsprodpb/c954/live/96cf8890-a0c9-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

The Times says the PM is "poised to announce 'robust' sanctions" on Israeli settlements in the West Bank despite warnings that they could lead to a "defacto boycott" of the country. The paper adds that Burnham wants to strengthen the UK's approach in response to Israel's plans to open tenders to build 1,200 settler homes.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: "Birmingham set to be first 20mph city."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/1cd9/live/9effa220-a0c9-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

Birmingham is set to become England's first 20mph city by lowering speed limits on the majority of its roads, according to the Daily Telegraph. If introduced, it would make 20mph the standard on all local and residential roads. The paper adds the council wants all routes apart from A-roads to become 20mph zones to ensure that "cars no longer dominate street life".

![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: "Future of UK state pension triple lock in doubt - with older population growing faster than workers."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/831/cpsprodpb/a540/live/c265d400-a0c9-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

The i Paper leads with the future of the UK state pension being called into question due to a "public finance time bomb" which raises the prospect of higher taxes for the ageing population.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mail reads: "Burnham warned of impending Russian UK 'attack'."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/810/cpsprodpb/caed/live/cce42350-a0c9-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

The PM has been warned that the UK faces Russian retaliation over is support for Ukraine, says the Daily Mail. The paper reports that security experts have told the prime minister to be prepared for a "direct response" from Moscow.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Express reads: "Britain must prepare for Putin's threat."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/c758/live/d2e96da0-a0c9-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

"Britain must prepare for Putin's threat" echoes the Daily Express.

![The headline on the front page of the Financial Times reads: "Beijing threatens to strike back if US sanctions on Iran hit China's interest."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1056/cpsprodpb/c66d/live/dad6d8e0-a0c9-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

The Financial Times reports that China has warned the US it will retaliate if Donald Trump's latest sanctions against Iran are expanded to include Chinese companies.

![The headline on the front page of the Metro reads: "Switching to vapes better than puffing."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/828/cpsprodpb/d676/live/e1f2c350-a0c9-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

Finally, Metro says a review has shown that vaping is a "viable option" for people who want to stop smoking. Scientists say while e-cigarettes are "unlikely to be risk-free" they are still less harmful than traditional cigarettes.
