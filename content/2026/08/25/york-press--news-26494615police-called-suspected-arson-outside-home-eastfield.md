Police are investigating an alleged arson outside a home in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police said an unknown offender set fire to a fence at the rear of a home in High Garth, Eastfield, before fleeing the area, between 9am and 9.15am on Tuesday (August 25).

A force spokesperson said: “If you have any information that could assist our investigation, you can pass it via email paul.o'[neill@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:neill@northyorkshire.police.uk.)”

  


Alternatively, you can call police on 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Please quote reference 12260165955 when providing information.
