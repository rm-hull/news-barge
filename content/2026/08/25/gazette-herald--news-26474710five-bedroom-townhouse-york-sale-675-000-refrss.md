A five-bedroom house with three reception rooms is for sale in York.

The semi-detached house in Feversham Crescent has a guide price of £675,000.

It is described by the estate agent Churchills Estate Agents as an "elegant end townhouse, in a premium residential street".

The three-storey house is said to have retained many of its original and period features, while also having some modern updates.

The house has five double bedrooms, three bathrooms and three reception rooms.

It also has a 16-foot lounge with a bay window and a large feature fireplace, and a separate family/dining room.

Some of its period features include ceiling roses, picture rails, fireplaces, and coving.

The modern fitted kitchen comes with a double eye-level oven, an electric hob, and other fitted appliances.

The kitchen opens to a breakfast room, utility room, and a downstairs WC/cloakroom.

On the first floor, there is a galleried landing with a glazed door that opens to a rear sun terrace.

The house also has a large main bedroom, a second double bedroom, and a four-piece house bathroom on the first floor.

The second floor has a three-piece shower room and three more double bedrooms.

The house also has a traditional front forecourt and a paved rear courtyard garden with a garage door and the potential for off-street parking.

The house is conveniently located close to York’s city walls, as well as schools, York Hospital, and restaurants.

The EPC rating for the house is D, and it has a council tax band of E.

The estate agents have said that viewing the house is essential to appreciate the calibre on offer.

For more information or to arrange a viewing, contact Churchills Estate Agents on 01904 918709.
