A popular North East pub has reopened with a fresh new look, including upgraded sports facilities and revamped outdoor space.

The Rudds Arms in Marton, welcomed customers back on Saturday (August 22), following a six-figure revamp by pub operator Greene King.

The renovation has transformed both the inside and outside of the Middlesbrough pub, with upgraded facilities, refreshed décor and more space for welcoming customers.

The outdoor space has had a refresh just in time for the summer Bank Holiday weekend - with a newly decorated pergola, complemented by new festoon lighting, planters and garden furniture.

Inside, sports fans will have full access to a dedicated darts zone featuring two dartboards, as well as upgraded screens and sound systems that have been installed to maximise live sport coverage on Sky Sports and TNT Sports.

The expansion has also arrived in perfect time for the return of Premier League football.

   

*(Image: Greene King)*

The inside of the pub has had a general refresh throughout - with new lighting, updated furniture and décor.

A newly created function room means the pub will be hosting celebrations for larger gatherings such as birthday parties, cooperate events and family celebrations.

Rob Watson, general manager of The Rudds Arms, said: "The Rudds Arms is back and better than ever, and we're delighted to be welcoming our lovely locals and new customers through the doors to see our brand-new look.

"The pub has received a full refurbishment to give everyone added comfort, top quality sports and entertainment and fantastic spaces for groups of all sizes to come together and unwind, both in and outdoors.

"The team and I pride ourselves on delivering a brilliant pub experience, and we can't wait to showcase all we have to offer."

The investment is the latest refurbishment project carried out by Greene King as it continues to upgrade venues across the country.
