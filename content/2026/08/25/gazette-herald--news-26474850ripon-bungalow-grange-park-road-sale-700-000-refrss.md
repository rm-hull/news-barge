A four-bedroom bungalow with a summer house and large garden space has been placed on the market for £700,000.

The property, situated on Grange Park Road, Ripon, is said to be "immaculate" according to estate agents Linley & Simpson and is set on a third of an acre.

There is a driveway with space for several cars, as well as a carport and a garage.

Inside the house is more than 2,030 square feet of space spread over two storeys.

The ground floor has an entrance leading into a central hall, which provides access to the kitchen diner, reception rooms, house bathroom, and two double bedrooms.

The sitting room is described by the estate agent as generous and comfortable, and there is also a garden room with views over the garden.

The dining room sits alongside the kitchen, which the estate agent says provides excellent scope for family cooking and day-to-day living.

The ground floor also has two bedrooms, one of which has an en-suite shower room, along with a family bathroom.

Upstairs, there are two further bedrooms and a shower room.

The garden has a patio area for outdoor dining and a pond.

There is also a summer house with power that can be used for a home office or gym.

The estate agent has said the house is in a quiet location close to amenities.

Ripon is a cathedral city that is home to a number of independent shops, restaurants, and cafes, as well as a racecourse and golf course.

The estate agent has said it has superb transport links to Harrogate, Thirsk, and the A1(M) and A19.

It is also within the catchment area of a number of schools, including Ripon Grammar School.
