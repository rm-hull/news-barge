There’s a large fire service response to a blaze in an industrial building this morning (Tuesday).

They were called to reports of smoke coming from the building in **Masham**, north of Ripon, at** 5.50am**.

Crews from Bedale, Leyburn, Ripon, Thirsk, Harrogate and Northallerton have been sent to the incident.

#### Masham

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews have located an electrical fire in a grain store.

“Crews currently have six breathing apparatus, one hose reel jet, two foam extinguishers and a 45mm jet in use.

“This incident is ongoing at the time of publishing.”
