A 45-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of rape after police launched an appeal to trace him.

Officers had released an image of the man earlier this week as they sought to speak to him in connection with the reported rape of a woman.

The incident was reported to have taken place on Thursday, July 30, 2026.

He was located and arrested this morning (August 25) and is in police custody.

A Cleveland Police spokesperson said: "A 45-year-old man, who was circulated earlier this week as wanted, has now been located and arrested on suspicion of rape.

"He was arrested this morning, Tuesday 25 August, and taken to police custody for questioning.
