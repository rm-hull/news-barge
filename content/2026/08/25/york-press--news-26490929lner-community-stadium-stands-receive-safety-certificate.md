A safety certificate has been issued for regulated stands at York’s LNER Community Stadium.

City of York Council said the certificate covers regulated stands at the stadium on Kathryn Avenue, Huntington, York, YO32 9AF.

The notice was issued under the Safety at Sports Grounds Act 1975, as amended by the Fire Safety and Safety at Places of Sport Act 1987.

To arrange an inspection, call 01904 552422.
