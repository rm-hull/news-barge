Emergency services were called to a serious crash in York last night (Monday).

They went to the incident on **College Road, Copmanthorpe** at**10.06pm.**

One person was trapped in the wreckage of their car.

#### College Road, Copmanthorpe

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/college-road-copmanthorpe-york-gsv.webp)

Tadcaster and Huntington firefighters responded to the incident.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews used casualty long board, cutters and spreaders, reciprocating saw, sharps protection kit, lighting and stabilisation equipment to extricate the casualty.

“The casualty was left in the hands of paramedics on scene and taken to hospital via road ambulance. The car was left in the hands of police for recovery.”

No other vehicle was involved.
