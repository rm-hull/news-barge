Two suspected burglars have been arrested after police spent almost two hours searching a vast area of countryside.

North Yorkshire Police were called to a property in the village, near Richmond, at about 8.30pm on Monday, August 24, following reports of a burglary in progress.

Officers found signs of damage at the scene and learned two suspects had fled the area in a Vauxhall Insignia.

Shortly afterwards, police were told of a collision involving a Vauxhall and a stone pillar near Middleton Tyas.

The car’s occupants had abandoned it and run off, prompting officers to launch a large-scale foot search with canine support across fields and rural tracks.

Just before 11pm, the search ended with the arrest of a man and a woman, both in their 30s.

The man was held on suspicion of burglary and possessing illegal drugs, while the woman was arrested on suspicion of burglary and possession of a bladed article.

Both remain in custody as enquiries continue.
