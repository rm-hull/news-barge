There is no such thing as a stupid idea in *Stuart Fails to Save the Universe* — and I'm starting to think that there's no such thing as a boring idea, either.

As the gang leave behind their tribal oasis from episode 5 — which, let's face it, they ruined — we're back in a familiar suburban setting this week. The difference? Everyone eats plants and grass as if they were a Happy Meal at McDonald's.

No fast food favoritism here, but you get the idea. So, when does *Stuart Fails to Save the Universe* episode 6 arrive on HBO Max?

## What time can I watch Stuart Fails to Save the Universe episode 6 on HBO Max?

![Stuart Fails to Save the Universe | Episode 6 Preview | HBO Max - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/4ZUgp8TvaOY/maxresdefault.jpg)

Internationally, you're looking out for these timings:

- **US**  – Thursday, August 27 at 6pm PT / 9pm ET
- **Canada** – Thursday, August 27 at 6pm PT / 9pm ET
