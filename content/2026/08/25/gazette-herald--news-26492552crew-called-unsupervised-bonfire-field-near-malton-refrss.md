A Malton Crew was called to a fire sighted in the open at 11.19pm last night (Monday).

On arrival of the crew at Main Street, Burythorpe, they found it to be a bonfire measuring approximately 3m x 3m, with no responsible person at the scene.

The crew used a hose reel jet to extinguish the fire.
