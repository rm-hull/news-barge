A community choir entertained crowds with a selection of popular songs at a Harrogate pub’s summer garden party.

All Together Now Community Choir opened the entertainment at the Empress on the Stray on Saturday.

Led by Musical Director Chris Kemp, the choir performed songs including Neil Diamond’s *Beautiful Noise*, Cher’s* Believe*and ABBA’s* Voulez-Vous*.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a8d977b4b270ee5736.jpg)


Members of the choir’s Harrogate branch were joined by singers from Skipton, Baildon and Bingley for the outdoor performance.

A spokesperson for All Together Now said:

“The sun shone, the drinks flowed and our audience outside the pub hugely enjoyed our setlist of crowd-pleasers.

“The wonderful Harrogate branch of the choir were out in force and were joined by members of the Skipton, Baildon and Bingley branches.”


*All Together Now describes itself as a 'relaxed, friendly community choir' which welcomes new members.*
