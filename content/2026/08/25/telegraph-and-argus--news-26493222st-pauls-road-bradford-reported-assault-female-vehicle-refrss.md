Police were called to the scene of a suspicious incident in Bradford.

West Yorkshire Police have confirmed they are investigating an assault on a female in a vehicle on St Paul's Road in Manningham on Monday evening.

This followed a call-out to the scene at 6.47pm following reports of a suspicious incident.

Enquiries remain ongoing.
