Summer isn’t over yet – as Picturehouse Outdoor Cinema returns with crowd-pleasing favourites.

Back for the second time this summer, it promises a ‘proper night out’ rather than just a film on a screen.

The screenings will take place at dusk in the surroundings of York Museum Gardens, with audiences having the opportunity to enjoy the film with drinks and snacks under the stars.

Taking place over three evenings, **Friday 11 to Sunday 13 September**, it includes something for everyone – from classic favourites* Grease*and* Jurassic Park*to a special screening of* The Shining* with an intro from the Evolution of Horror, the UK’s biggest horror and film history podcast.

Doors will open to the event space at 6:30pm; the film will commence at dusk – or as soon as it gets dark.

Blankets, cushions and small picnic chairs are allowed, and a limited number of deck chairs will be supplied and offered on a first come-first served basis. A limited number of blankets and rain ponchos will also be available for purchase.

There will be a bar onsite providing alcoholic and soft drinks, and cinema snacks including popcorn, nachos, and hot dogs – and each outdoor ticket purchased directly from Picturehouse includes a £7 credit voucher towards food and drink.

Dogs may be brought to the event, provided that they are kept on a lead and under adult control at all times.
