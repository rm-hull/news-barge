Three women are wanted after a 'high-value theft' from Darlington's M&S store.

Pictures of the women have been released by Durham Police after items were stolen from the M&S Food Hall in West Park on July 19.

CCTV images show two grey-haired women - one wearing an all-blue jumper and trousers with white socks and navy sliders, and the other in a red jacket with a white t-shirt.

They also show a brown-haired woman wearing a dark top and a River Island-branded bag.

A Darlington police spokesperson said: "If you have any information, contact PC Nowicz by emailing [Jakub.nowicz2@durham.police.uk](mailto:Jakub.nowicz2@durham.police.uk) or calling 101 with extension 209782."
