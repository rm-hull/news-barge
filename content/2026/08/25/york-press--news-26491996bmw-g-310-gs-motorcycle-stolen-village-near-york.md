THIEVES have targeted a North Yorkshire village and made off with a multi-purpose motorcycle.

  



The county's police force said the theft happened in Cowstail Lane, Tockwith, between 10pm on Saturday, August 22 and 2.30am on Sunday, August 23.

A multicoloured BMW G 310 GS motorcycle was stolen from a driveway.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said it is believed the motorcycle was removed in another vehicle.

The spokesperson added that anyone who witnessed anything suspicious, or has CCTV, doorbell camera or dashcam footage, should contact police on 101 quoting incident number NYP-23082026-0072.
