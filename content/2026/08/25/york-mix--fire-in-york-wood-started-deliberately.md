Police have been called in following a fire in York today (Tuesday).

Fire crews were called to the incident in **Acomb Wood** at**2.20pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb responded to a fire to some woodland close to a residential area.

#### Acomb Wood

“The fire measured 4 by 4 metres and was extinguished using a hose reel jet.

“The cause of the fire is believed deliberate.”

North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service routinely informs police of all fires thought to have been started deliberately.
