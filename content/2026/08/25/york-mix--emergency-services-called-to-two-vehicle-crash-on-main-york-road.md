Emergency services were called to a crash on a main York road yesterday evening (Monday).

It happened on **Hull Road** at**5.30pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from York responded to a two vehicle road traffic collision.

#### Hull Road, York

“All persons were out the vehicles prior to fire service arrival.

“Crews made the vehicles safe and left the incident with the police on scene.”
