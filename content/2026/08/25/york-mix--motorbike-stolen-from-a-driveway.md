North Yorkshire Police officers are appealing for information following the theft of a motorcycle from Tockwith.

Between 10:00pm on Saturday 22 August and 02:30am on Sunday 23 August, a multicoloured **BMW G 310 GS** motorcycle was stolen from a driveway on Cowstail Lane.

It is believed the motorcycle was removed in another vehicle, possibly a van or similar vehicle.

If you witnessed anything suspicious in the area during these times, or have any CCTV, doorbell camera or dashcam footage which may assist, please contact North Yorkshire Police on 101 quoting incident number **NYP-23082026-0072**.
