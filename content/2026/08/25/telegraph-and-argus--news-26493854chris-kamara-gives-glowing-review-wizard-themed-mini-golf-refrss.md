BANTAMS legend Chris Kamara has described a magical day out with his grandchildren at a wizard-themed mini golf course.

He visited The Hole In Wand, located in Leeds city centre, and later shared his experience in a glowing review.

Mr Kamara said: "This isn’t just a mini golf course – it’s a little magical world tucked right into the heart of the city.

"The moment we stepped inside, the kids’ eyes were wider than mine when I first saw VAR."

The venue, part of The Potions Cauldron experience at St John’s Centre, features a fantasy-themed course with glowing potions, broomstick challenges, and immersive storytelling.

Mr Kamara said: "The staff made the kids feel like proper little wizards."

Describing one of the highlights, he said: "The Castle of Ice is a proper showstopper.

"Then came the Dragon Tunnel, where the kids insisted I go first ‘in case the dragon wakes up.’

"Cheers, lads."

After completing the course, Mr Kamara and his grandchildren explored the wizard emporium, The Potions Cauldron, filled with bubbling bottles and magical gifts.

He said: "This place is fun, affordable, and brilliantly run.

"Seeing my grandkids completely lose themselves in the magic – that’s unbelievable, Jeff."
