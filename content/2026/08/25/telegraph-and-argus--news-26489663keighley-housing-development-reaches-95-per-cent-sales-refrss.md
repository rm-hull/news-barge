A NEW housing development has reached a major sales milestone.

Avant Homes West Yorkshire has announced that 95 per cent of properties at its £33 million Odette’s Point development in Keighley have now been sold.

Located on Shann Lane, the site comprises 124 three- and four-bedroom homes across 10 energy-efficient designs.

Caroline Spray, regional sales manager at Avant Homes West Yorkshire, said: "Our Odette’s Point development has delivered a new thriving community in Keighley, with our homes proving hugely popular with buyers looking to move to the area.

"Surrounded by picturesque countryside, and with easy access to both Leeds and Bradford for commuters, Keighley is a fantastic place to live.

"We don’t anticipate the final homes will be available for long, so we encourage any interested buyers to visit the development and speak with our sales team to learn more about the final homes at Odette’s Point."

The few remaining homes are priced from £235,000 for the three-bedroom-plus Salbury, offering flexibility as a live/work area.

The largest available property is the four-bedroom detached Horbury, priced at £369,995.

Avant Homes is offering a range of incentives on selected plots.

These include part exchange, a five per cent deposit contribution, and the My Move Made Easy scheme, where the company helps buyers sell their existing home.

Ms Spray said the Horbury model is a prime example of the company’s approach to "practically designed, energy efficient homes."

On the ground floor, the Horbury offers a large living room, WC, and a versatile snug that can be used as a study, playroom, or music room.

The rear of the property features an open-plan kitchen and family dining space with French doors leading to the garden, and a utility room with external access and additional storage.

Upstairs, the main bedroom includes an en-suite shower room.

A large double bedroom and two single bedrooms share a family bathroom, and the landing offers further storage space.

The property also includes a detached garage.
