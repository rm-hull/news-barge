Harrogate Town have launched five and ten-game Flexi Tickets for the 2026/27 season.

The packages allow supporters to choose which home matches they attend at the Marcy Fitness Stadium without selecting the fixtures in advance.

Both seated and standing options are available, with fans able to save up to 20 per cent compared with purchasing individual match tickets.

Prices for five matches start at £94 for adults, £72 for concessions and £25 for under-14s.

Ten-game packages start at £182 for adults, £140 for concessions and £48 for under-14s.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/67208cf1c5114.jpg)


The Flexi Tickets operate as online memberships, with supporters downloading their chosen match tickets subject to availability. Seats are not reserved for those purchasing seated packages.

Concession prices are available to supporters aged 65 and over, Blue Light Card holders, Armed Forces members and full-time students.

Under-14s must be accompanied by an adult at all matches.

*Flexi Tickets can be purchased through Harrogate Town’s**ticketing website** or from the club shop on Commercial Street.*
