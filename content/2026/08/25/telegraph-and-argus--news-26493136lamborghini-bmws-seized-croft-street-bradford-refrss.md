A purple Lamborghini and two BMWs were seized in Bradford for the 'epitome of anti-social vehicle use'.

West Yorkshire Police saw the three cars on Croft Street, Bradford on Monday night.

The officers were dealing with a matter nearby when they noticed the behaviour.

Working with neighbourhood policing colleagues, police say they caught up with all three vehicles.

The drivers were reported to Bradford Council for breaching district-wide vehicular nuisance Public Space Protection Order.

## What police have said

A spokesperson for the force said: "Whilst dealing with a matter nearby, we couldn't help but notice the behaviour of the drivers of these BMW's and Lamborghini on Croft Street; it was the epitome of anti-social vehicle use.

"Alongside our Neighbourhood Policing colleagues we were able to catch up with all three vehicles, resulting in them being seized under S.59 Police Reform Act.

"All three drivers were reported to for breaching the District-wide vehicular nuisance Public Space Protection Order."
