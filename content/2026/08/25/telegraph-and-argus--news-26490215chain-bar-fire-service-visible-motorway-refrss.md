Emergency services were called to Chain Bar roundabout at 10.09am on Monday morning following a crash.

Two fire crews attended the scene to assist West Yorkshire Police officers.

They supported police with moving a vehicle off the road to a place of safety.

On Monday, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called to Chain Bar roundabout at 10.09 this morning to reports of an RTC.

"Two crews attended to assist police with moving a vehicle off the road to a place of safety."
