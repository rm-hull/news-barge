Harrogate BID's street rangers have spruced up one of the gateways to the town centre.

The team spent several hours at One Arch on Friday afternoon (21st August), cutting back overgrown areas and carrying out a general tidy-up.

The street rangers regularly carry out cleaning, maintenance and improvement work across Harrogate town centre.

A spokesperson for Harrogate BID said:

“We spent a few hours at One Arch cutting back the overgrown areas and giving the area a general tidy.

“Helping to keep the gateways to the town looking tidy, welcoming and well cared for.”
