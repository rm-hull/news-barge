## Take a look inside this £650,000 detached family home near York

A four-bedroom home in Wigginton, York, is on sale for £650,000 *(Image: Ashtons)*

A four-bedroom detached home in York is on the market for £650,000.

The property in Manor Garth, Wigginton, has been described by estate agents Ashtons as "substantial" and offering generous and versatile accommodation.

It has a welcoming entrance hall that leads into a large kitchen diner featuring a wooden kitchen, integrated appliances, and a large central island with a gas hob.

The spacious living room at the front of the house features a bay window and a cast iron fireplace.

Both the kitchen and living room open into a large tiled conservatory that overlooks the garden.

Upstairs, there are four bedrooms, including two particularly "generous" rooms.

The principal bedroom has an ensuite shower room, while the remaining bedrooms are served by a family bathroom featuring a bath and separate shower.

The property has a private, hedged rear garden with a lawn and paved patio adjoining the conservatory.

The front of the house has a gated driveway providing ample parking and leading to a double garage.

The property is located on the outskirts of the village of Wigginton, which has a range of amenities and provides access to York.

The surrounding countryside offers a setting for walking and outdoor pursuits.

According to the estate agents, this property is a substantial family home, offering excellent flexibility and an appealing village-edge position, and early viewing is advised to appreciate the space and setting on offer.

The property has a council tax band of E.
