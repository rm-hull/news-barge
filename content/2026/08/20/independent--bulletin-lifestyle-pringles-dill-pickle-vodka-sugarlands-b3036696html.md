# New Pringles dill pickle vodka sparks heated debate as fans compare it to popular shot

![Spirits maker Sugarlands Distilling released the vodka last month](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/20/20/55/GettyImages-2249030380.jpg)

- Sugarlands Distilling has released Pringles Dill Pickle Potato Vodka, a 60-proof clear liquor inspired by the popular chip flavor, featuring sharp vinegar, dill, and salty tasting notes.
- Retailing at $21.99 for a 750 mL bottle with a 30 percent alcohol by volume content, the brand recommends using the unique spirit in martinis, picklebacks, or other savory cocktails.
- The unexpected product collaboration sparked divided reactions across social media platforms, with some users expressing skepticism while pickle enthusiasts welcomed the drink and shared creative serving ideas.
- The official Dill Pickle Pringles flavor originally debuted in 2022 and was briefly discontinued before returning as a permanent product offering in 2024 following high consumer demand.
- This novelty spirit represents Pringles' latest venture into the alcohol space, following a previous crossover with Miller Lite to produce beer-flavored potato chips.
