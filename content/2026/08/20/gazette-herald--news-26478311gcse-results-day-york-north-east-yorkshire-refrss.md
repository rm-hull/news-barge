  **MAKE sure to follow along with The Press as our full GCSEs results day coverage is set to take place today (August 20) and in print tomorrow.**

Once again, GCSE students are set to find out their results as pupils across the country prepare to find out whether they’ve secured a place to sit their a-levels, if they’re on their dream apprenticeship scheme, or whether they’ll be exploring a new route entirely.

There will be an eight-page souvenir picture supplement in Friday’s Press, packed with photos from the schools across our publication area - alongside stories and pictures from the day in the main body of the paper.

We are not publishing lists of students’ results this year as over successive years the number of schools submitting them has sadly dwindled.

We will be publishing local schools’ results stories as we get them and also uploading pictures and local reaction from people across the city and county.

Visit The Press website for our live blog and stay tuned throughout the day to see results from across the region.
