# Chicago snubbed as Democrats reveal three finalists for massive 2028 convention

- Boston, Denver, and Philadelphia are the final three shortlisted cities to host the 2028 Democratic National Convention.
- Democratic National Committee Chairman Ken Martin announced that party leaders will make a final site selection later this year for the event scheduled for August 7-10, 2028.
- Atlanta and Chicago were eliminated after party assessments of fundraising capacity and logistics in all candidate cities.
- Republicans have already chosen Houston for their 2028 convention as both parties prepare for an open presidential primary at the end of President Donald Trump's term.
- The finalist cities offer crucial access to labor unions and fundraising resources as Democrats navigate a party financial deficit ahead of upcoming midterm elections.
