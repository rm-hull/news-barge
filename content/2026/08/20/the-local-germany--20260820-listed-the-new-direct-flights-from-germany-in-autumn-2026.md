As the summer holiday season enters its final stretch, airlines are revealing where travellers based in Germany will be able to fly during the autumn and winter months.

Already Lufthansa, Discover Airlines, Eurowings and Condor have announced new services from German airports, ranging from new short-haul links to Spain and Morocco to long-haul flights to Malaysia, Abu Dhabi and China.

More announcements are expected in the coming months as airlines finalise their winter schedules.

But the routes confirmed so far suggest residents in Germany will have a broader choice of destinations, whether planning autumn city breaks, arctic adventures or winter escapes to warmer climates.

Germany's two biggest aviation hubs, Frankfurt and Munich, are seeing some of the most significant additions.

Lufthansa will launch direct flights between Frankfurt and **Kuala Lumpur** in Malaysia on October 25th. The route is currently scheduled to operate five times per week.

Condor is also adding several notable long-haul destinations from Frankfurt. Some of these are entirely new additions to the airline's network, while others will be newly available during the winter season. Destinations include:

* Abu Dhabi (UAE)
* Cairo (Egypt)
* Tel Aviv (Israel)
* Sanya (China)
* La Réunion via Mauritius

Discover Airlines is adding two new Moroccan destinations for winter 2026/27. From late October, the carrier will fly twice weekly from Frankfurt to **Agadir**, operating on Mondays and Fridays.

**Munich**

Meanwhile, Munich will gain a weekly Sunday connection to **Fès**, which Discover says will be the only direct link between the Bavarian capital and the historic Moroccan city.

Looking further north, Munich will also add a seasonal route to **Ivalo** in Finnish Lapland. The service is scheduled to operate once a week from mid-December until April 2027, targeting demand from holidaymakers seeking snow sports and the Northern Lights.

**Berlin and Hamburg**

Berlin is set to gain several new options.

Condor is due to launch a new **Abu Dhabi** connection from the German capital.

From November 2nd, Eurowings will begin flying four times a week to **Rome** and three times a week to**Bologna**.

A new winter route to **Kuusamo** in Finnish Lapland is also being introduced, while flights to**Sarajevo** in Bosnia and Herzegovina will start from December 20th with two weekly services.

Eurowings is also introducing flights to **Jerez de la Frontera** in southern Spain from both Berlin and Hamburg from the start of the winter timetable.

**Nuremberg**

EasyJet will launch new services from Nuremberg to **Manchester** from November 2nd, followed by routes to**London Gatwick** on November 19th and**London Luton** on November 23rd, improving links between northern Bavaria and the UK.

Travellers in western Germany are also set to benefit from several additions.

From late October, Eurowings plans to launch new services from Cologne/Bonn to **Klagenfurt** in Austria and**Pristina** in Kosovo, while Hanover will gain a new winter route to**Alicante** in Spain as well as a connection to**Cairo** in Egypt starting in November.

Stuttgart is also joining the list of airports with direct access to the **Egyptian capital**, thanks to a twice-weekly Eurowings service.
