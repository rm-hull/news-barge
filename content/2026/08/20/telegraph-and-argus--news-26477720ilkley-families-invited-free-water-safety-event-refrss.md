A DAY by the water can quickly turn into tragedy.

That’s the message behind a free water safety event taking place at East Holmes Field in Ilkley on Thursday, August 27, from 3pm to 4.30pm.

The event is designed to promote water safety through interactive demonstrations and family-friendly activities, and is organised by Bradford Council ward officers.

Councillor Ian Walker, executive member for neighbourhoods and communities at Bradford Council, said: "It has been an exceptionally hot and dry summer, and people have been drawn to areas like the riverside at Ilkley to cool off.

"It's important that everyone understands the risks associated with open water.

"This event is an opportunity to learn practical and potentially life-saving advice while enjoying a free afternoon of informative family-friendly activities."

Firefighters from West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service will demonstrate specialist water rescue equipment and carry out in-water demonstrations, offering advice on what to do if someone gets into difficulty and how to assist others safely.

The event will also feature information about the Float to Live and Phone, Float Throw campaigns.

West Yorkshire Police will be in attendance to engage with the public and reinforce the event’s key safety messages.

Chris Burke, assistant district commander for Ilkley at West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, said: "On warm summer days when people are enjoying themselves, it’s easy to be caught off guard and a fun day out can quickly turn to tragedy.

"Anyone regardless of their age or swimming ability can get into difficulty in water, but by learning about water safety they can also help to save a life.

"Through our demonstrations and engagement activities, we hope to give people the knowledge and confidence to stay safe and know what to do to help themselves and others in an emergency."

Sgt Laycock said: "We encourage residents and visitors to come along, speak with officers and learn how everyone can play a part in staying safe around open water."

The event is free and open to all ages.
