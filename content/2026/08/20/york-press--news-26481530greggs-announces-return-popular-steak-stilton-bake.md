Greggs has announced the return of a popular menu item after years of speculation.

The Steak and Stilton Bake, which has been the subject of numerous customer requests nationwide, will now take pride of place on the company's autumn menu.

The new bake, which includes diced beef in gravy, blue Stilton cheese, and grilled onions, will be wrapped in Greggs' signature flaky puff pastry.

The item will be available in stores across the country from Thursday, September 3, and will headline Greggs' autumn menu alongside a variety of hot drinks, lunches, and sweet treats.


## What's on Greggs' new autumn menu

Greggs' autumn menu will also feature two new buffalo-inspired offerings.

The Buffalo Chicken Baguette contains buffalo-style chicken mayonnaise, mature Cheddar cheese, and red onion in a freshly baked baguette.

Meanwhile, the Buffalo Chicken Pizza Slice offers the same bold flavours on a focaccia-style base.

It combines chicken in a pizza-style sauce, mature Cheddar cheese, and pepperoni between slices of white sourdough-style bread, topped with béchamel sauce and grated mixed cheese.

Returning for 2026 is the fan-favourite Pumpkin Spice Latte and Iced Pumpkin Spice Latte, both with a new salted caramel drizzle.

These beverages, made with freshly ground Fairtrade coffee and pumpkin spice flavouring, return just in time for autumn.

Greggs is also expanding its range of chilled drinks with three new offerings.

The Iced Caramel Chocolate combines Fairtrade cocoa powder, chilled semi-skimmed milk, and a salted caramel flavour drizzle.

The Iced Mocha blends freshly ground Fairtrade coffee with cocoa and milk over ice.


The classic Iced Chocolate is another addition to the menu.

Greggs' autumn menu will also include a variety of sweet treats.

The new Bonfire Toffee Muffin contains a burnt toffee-flavoured filling and a chocolate and salted caramel flavour icing.

The Pumpkin Spice Doughnut also makes a return.

All new and returning autumn menu items will be available in Greggs shops nationwide from Thursday 3rd September and via Click+Collect through the Greggs App.

App users can access a range of rewards, including a free item after every nine qualifying purchases in selected categories.
