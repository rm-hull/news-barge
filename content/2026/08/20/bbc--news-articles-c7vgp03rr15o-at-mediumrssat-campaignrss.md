![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: "Harry's coming home."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/df8c/live/05950540-9c13-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

News that the Duke and Duchess of Sussex plan to move back to the UK is prominent across Thursday's papers with several printing late editions to fit it in. "Harry's coming home" says the Daily Telegraph as it reports the couple intends to relocate from the US later this month with their children Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet enrolled to start at a British school in September.

![The headline on the front page of the Sun reads: "Harry and Meg coming home."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/6933/live/0e2fc780-9c13-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

"Harry and Meg coming home" echoes the Sun, "six years after Megxit". The paper says the "shock return" will be for "an extended period" and the family have already found a new home in the UK.

![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: "Royal return: Harry and Meghan are moving back."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/809/cpsprodpb/98c0/live/5b5ac910-9c27-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

"Royal return" is the Times's headline as it says Prince Harry and Meghan's new home in the UK will be a private, non-royal residence thought to be outside of London.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mail reads: "Ministers forced to tell asylum seekers: don't rape women."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/810/cpsprodpb/91ed/live/b25efaf0-9c28-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

The Daily Mail calls Harry and Meghan's return "a royal sensation" as it pictures the couple holding their daughter Princess Lilibet. Elsewhere, the paper says a Home Office guidebook handed out to asylum seekers includes a warning that having sex with someone without their consent "is a serious crime in the UK". The document sets out the "rules and expectations" of living in the UK and is part of an education campaign for those looking to stay in the country.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Express reads: "Don't rape or beat up women when in Britain."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/6ef7/live/2cc621d0-9c13-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

The Daily Express says there is "outcry" over the guidebook having to explicitly give asylum seekers advice on how to behave towards women.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: "Day trip tragedy."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/f0a6/live/45917bb0-9c13-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

The Daily Mirror has more details of the three members of a family who died at Shoreham-by-Sea, West Sussex. The paper says the father who drowned with his partner and teenage daughter was a refugee who fled Palestine a decade ago. The family's younger daughter is still in critical condition in hospital.

![The headline on the front page of the Metro reads: "Refugee family who drowned in seaside tragedy."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/828/cpsprodpb/2185/live/3f50af00-9c13-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

Metro says the family is among many victims in a "summer of swim deaths". At least 35 people have drowned so far during record-breaking heatwaves according to data by the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine.

![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: "Working in Starmer's No 10 was unbearable, says ex-chief of staff."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/790/cpsprodpb/1d0d/live/4eb1c1a0-9c13-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

Sir Keir Starmer's former chief of staff revealed how "unbearable" it was working in No 10, saying she feared she was being recorded by colleagues, the Guardian reports. Sue Gray, who the paper says was "the most powerful adviser" in Downing Street before resigning in 2024, criticised the working environment under Sir Keir on a podcast.

![The headline on the front page of the Financial Times reads: "Moderna shares double after success for custom-made skin cancer vaccine."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1056/cpsprodpb/6a40/live/a6e0ad50-9c13-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

The Financial Times leads with US vaccine-maker Moderna's shares more than doubling in value after it reported successful trial results for an experimental skin cancer therapy. Jointly developed with Merck, the therapy would be "unprecedented" in cancer treatment, with custom-made vaccines for specific tumours. Analysts are expecting US regulatory approval next year.

![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: "New skin cancer vaccine can stop disease and offers hope for era of personalised jabs."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/831/cpsprodpb/4c68/live/af617ef0-9c13-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

The i Paper's top story also covers the landmark vaccine trial, which found patients with high-risk melanoma remained free of cancer for longer after surgery.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: Draculoo](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/06e5/live/b6f6b810-9c13-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

The Daily Star splashes with the story of a woman who found a bat in her toilet after it flew in the bathroom for a drink of water. "Draculoo" is the headline.
