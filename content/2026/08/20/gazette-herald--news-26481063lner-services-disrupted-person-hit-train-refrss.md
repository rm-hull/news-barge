TRAIN services to and from York have been disrupted by a major incident.

Disruption is expected between London and Stevenage, affecting LNER services between London Kings Cross, York, Harrogate and Leeds, until 4pm today.

It comes after National Rail announced a person was hit by a train between London and Stevenage.

  


A spokesperson said: “Whilst the emergency services carry out their work, all lines between these stations are closed.

“Trains may be cancelled, delayed by up to 60 minutes, revised or diverted.

“Major disruption is expected until 4pm.”
