# Trevor Nelson shares brain tumour diagnosis after stepping back from BBC Radio 2 show

![BBC radio presenter Trevor Nelson](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/26/10/01KVTSD848SNAHSJP9YKDYKQ3S.jpg)

- BBC radio presenter Trevor Nelson has revealed he was diagnosed with a brain tumour after taking a break from work earlier this year.
- The 62-year-old broadcaster shared that his world was turned upside down by the unexpected diagnosis.
- Nelson underwent a very serious surgery shortly after the discovery, which he confirmed went incredibly well.
- The DJ has spent the past month resting at home, recovering, and undergoing post-operative treatment.
- He explained that he stepped away from his broadcasting duties because he could not continue presenting while dealing with the diagnosis.
