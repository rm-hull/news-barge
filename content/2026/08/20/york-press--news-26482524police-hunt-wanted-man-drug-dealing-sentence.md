POLICE are hunting for a man wanted for breaching the terms of his prison release licence.

Charlie O’Neill, 27, had been released partway through a sentence for drug dealing.

Extensive enquiries have been carried out by North Yorkshire Police to find O’Neill, from Northallerton, who has links to Thirsk, Leeming Bar, Darlington and Hornby.

  


The force spokesperson: “Officers have warned that anyone harbouring him, could also be committing a criminal offence.

“If you have seen him or know where he is now, please call us on 999.

“If you have any other information, please call us on 101.

“If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111or online via their website.

“Please quote reference 12250219151 when passing on information.”
