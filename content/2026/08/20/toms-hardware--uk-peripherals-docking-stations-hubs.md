A 'grand piano style' Blu-ray drive and USB dock has got tech fans excited over on Kickstarter.

![Best Thunderbolt and USB-C Docks for Laptops](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/6JgTGGa4HkMDp5H7eAyZb7-320-80.jpg)

These are the best Thunderbolt and USB-C docks for expanding your laptop's port options.

![Thunderbolt 5 Docking Station Testing](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/VEupXC8GJaLkgkeDN7MGNc-320-80.jpg)

All three Thunderbolt 5 docks offer similar performance, but one really stands out with its features.

![Cooler Master MasterHUB](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/5PFx4Zs8vvY2rNXZVvR5eY-320-80.jpg)

Cooler Master's MasterHUB is a modular customizable macropad that's perhaps a little too ambitious. Its modularity is nicely implemented, but it's limited by its software and lack of plugins.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/gyiqjESLi9tJFhgubAx2p8-320-80.jpg)

Wokyis is already selling its M5 dock that turns your Mac Mini into a mini Macintosh. But it plans to add G7 NES-themed docks, as well, with up to 80Gbps of throughput and larger 7-inch screens.

![Thunderbolt 5 Docks](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/dC6AFmoVkDY9FeFZYRcybQ-320-80.jpg)

We’ve tested the top Thunderbolt 5 docks on the market, combining six into one battle for the crown.

![Wokyis M5 Retro Dock Station](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/aYmmLmvbySWgxxRr9PvcXT-320-80.png)

The Wokyis M5 Retro Dock Station will add USB ports, memory card readers, and an external SSD enclosure to your Mac mini while giving you a small '80s Macintosh on your desk.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/bQNLUgH43vbyXDN7SrPojM-320-80.jpg)

The first photos of the DockFrame, a modular USB-C hub built around the Framework-compatible Expansion Card standard, were released over the weekend.

![Sonnet Echo 13 Thunderbolt 5 SSD Dock](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/FdfAvJ89yVrwkrXAvcLpQ5-320-80.jpg)

Sonnet will be the first to ship a Thunderbolt 5 dock with a built-in PCIe 4.0 SSD, which delivers 6,100 MB/s transfers and offers many connectivity options.

![J5Create Thunderbolt 5 graphics and storage dock](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/bPFb3AJ4yvWmj6CJkT487N-320-80.jpg)

J5Create’s upcoming Thunderbolt 5 dock has an MXM graphics card plus a slot for 2280-sized SSDs.
