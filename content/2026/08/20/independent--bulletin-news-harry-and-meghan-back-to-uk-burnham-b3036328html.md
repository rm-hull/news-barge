# Harry and Meghan’s UK security funding ‘a private matter’, says Burnham

- Andy Burnham stated that security arrangements for the Duke and Duchess of Sussex upon their return to the UK are a private matter.
- The King reportedly learned of Harry and Meghan's planned move back to Britain just three days before it was publicly announced.
- The couple will return to the UK later this month to live in a non-royal residence outside London.
- Prince Harry remains involved in an ongoing legal dispute with the Home Office over his UK security protection since stepping down as a working royal in 2020.
- A review by the Risk Management Board regarding the couple's security requirements was still pending ahead of their visit.
