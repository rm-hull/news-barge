Thousands of music fans descended on the Great Yorkshire Showground for Harrogate's first-ever Symphonic Ibiza event last weekend.

The outdoor concert brought Ibiza classics to the Showground with a 40-piece orchestra, live vocalists and DJs.

Presenter Vernon Kay performed a DJ set at the event, which also featured performances from M People's Heather Small, Rebecca Ferguson MBE and Alison Limerick.

Crowds enjoyed an evening of dance anthems and live orchestral music as the Showground was transformed into a large-scale outdoor concert venue.

The event marked a new addition to Harrogate's summer events calendar and followed the Great Yorkshire Show, which welcomed 150,000 visitors to the same site in July.

Symphonic Ibiza combines iconic Ibiza and dance tracks with a full orchestra, DJs and live performers.

The event has since received plenty of praise from those who attended, with Vernon Kay describing the Harrogate show as "spectacular" on social media.

Organisers have also been met with calls from visitors for Symphonic Ibiza to return to Harrogate in the future.
