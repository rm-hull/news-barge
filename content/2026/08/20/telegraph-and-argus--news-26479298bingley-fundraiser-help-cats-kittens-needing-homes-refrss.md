A FUNDRAISER is set to take place in support of cats and kittens in need of new homes.

Organised by Allerton Cat Rescue, the tea, coffee and cake afternoon will be held at Little House Community Centre on Market Street, Bingley, on Saturday, August 22, from 12pm to 3pm.

Admission is £5 and includes refreshments, with all proceeds going toward the care of rescued cats.

A spokesman for Allerton Cat Rescue said: "EVERY PENNY HELPS OUR CATS & KITTENS!"

"Thank you for helping us change lives one paw at a time.

"Every donation makes a difference."

The event will also feature stalls, a raffle and cakes for sale.

Those unable to attend can donate via PayPal to [acrfundraising@hotmail.com](mailto:acrfundraising@hotmail.com) or through the charity’s bank account.
