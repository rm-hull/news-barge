YEAR 11 pupils at Norton College have been congratulated on their exam results.

Executive Headteacher, Domenica Wilkinson, said: "“On behalf of everyone at Norton College, I would like to extend sincere congratulations to all our students receiving their results today. We are so proud of the hard work, resilience and determination shown by our Year 11 students.

"Attainment across subjects has increased once again, and it was wonderful to see so many smiling faces this morning as our students arrived to collect their results. Your hard work, determination and commitment have truly paid off — well done to every one of you."

Head of School, Samuel Stones, added: "We are incredibly proud of our Class of ’26 and wish all our students every success and happiness as they take their next steps, whether that’s continuing their studies, starting an apprenticeship, entering employment or embarking on a new adventure. Congratulations everyone — you’ve done us proud."
