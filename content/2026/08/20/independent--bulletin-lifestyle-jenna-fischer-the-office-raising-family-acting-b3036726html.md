# Jenna Fischer reveals why she walked away from acting after ‘The Office’ success

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/TAMu4WiK/poster.jpg?width=720)

- Jenna Fischer revealed she stepped away from leading television roles after finding 65 to 70-hour work weeks incompatible with raising her two children.
- The decision came after the 2019 cancellation of her ABC comedy *Splitting Up Together* , which prompted her to reconsider her career goals.
- Fischer transitioned to podcasting by launching *Office Ladies* with former co-star Angela Kinsey to maintain a steady income with a flexible schedule.
- The shift allowed the actor to prioritize her family and be present for key milestones like school drop-offs, teacher conferences, and concerts.
- While stepping back from full-time TV commitments, Fischer continues to take on select supporting roles, including the 2024 Mean Girls film.
