A road in Kilnwick Percy is set for partial closure due to safety concerns.

Millington Lane will be closed between Kilnwick Percy Hall Access Road and Rose Cottages from September 14, 2026 at 8am until September 16, 2026 at 6pm.

The closure is due to the road's narrow width, which makes it difficult to maintain safety for the public.

The works are expected to be completed within three days, but the order can remain valid for up to 18 months if necessary.

Traffic affected by the closure will be diverted via Millington Lane, Fishpond Hill, Kilnwick Percy Hill, Cattle Hill, Coldwold, Cobdale Lane, Cobdale, Pasture Lane, Millington Pastures, Wood Gate, Swineridge Lane, Church Lane, Martin Lane, and Main Street, covering Huggate, Nunburnholme, Warter, Millington, and Wolds Weighton Ward.

The diversion route will be clearly signposted during the closure.

While the road will be closed to all through traffic, access will still be granted for residents, emergency vehicles, and pedestrians.

The East Riding of Yorkshire Council has announced the closure under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, ensuring the works are carried out safely.

The works are set to be completed within three days, but the order allows for an 18-month period if additional time is needed.

For further details, residents can contact the council's Highways Customer Care at 01482 393939, referencing CB.
