A MAN and a woman have been charged following the discovery of six people inside a lorry in Yorkshire.

The group was found inside the vehicle in Anderson Road in Goole.

  


Florin Anghel, 27-years-old who identifies as Italian and Tita Istrate, 24-years-old who identifies as Romanian have been charged with doing an act to facilitate the commission of a breach of UK immigrational law by a non-UK national.

Both have been remanded into custody to appear at Hull Magistrates Court this morning (Thursday, 20 August 2026).

It comes after Humberside Police officers attended this incident in Goole where six people were detained under the immigration act on Tuesday (August 18).
