Whether it’s a scattering of crisps and a dusting of biscuit crumbs or layers of pet hair and ground-in dirt, car interiors never seem to stay pristine for long. Fortunately, the best car vacuum cleaners mean you don’t have to lug an upright vacuum down the driveway every time you want to do a speedy clean-up.

From awkward gaps between seats to cup holders and nooks and crannies in the centre console, there’s a lot to contend with when vacuuming the car, which is why some vacuum cleaners are designed specifically to tackle these hard to reach areas. Compact cordless models are ideal for tackling crumbs clean up while corded units provide deeper cleans and can assist with removing stubborn upholstery stains.

To help you find the right tool for the job, I turned my own car into a testing ground. Thanks to a football-mad teenager with a snacking habit and a golden retriever who loves a muddy puddle, I had plenty of grime to work with.

My top pick is the Miele duoflex HX1 carcare cordless vacuum cleaner (£389, Johnlewis.com), thanks to its powerful suction and impressive runtime. For a deeper clean of upholstery and even car windows, I’d recommend the Hoover HS5 spot wash and Dry. Keep scrolling for models that impressed me, across a range of budgets.
