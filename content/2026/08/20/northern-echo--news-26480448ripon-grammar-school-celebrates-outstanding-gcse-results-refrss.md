Four Ripon Grammar School boys were on cloud nine as they celebrated achieving ten or more top grades in a stunning set of GCSE results.

The North Yorkshire school recorded one of its highest proportions of top grades in recent years, with almost one in five grades awarded being the very highest grade 9.

The results also included 485 grades at 9-8, equivalent to the top A* grades under the former GCSE grading system, with many exceptional performances among the cohort of 128 students collecting their results.

  Four Ripon Grammar School pupils celebrated achieving 10 or more grade 9 GCSEs as the North Yorkshire school recorded one of its strongest sets of results in recent years, with nearly one in five grades awarded at the highest level. 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The four 16-year-old top performers are Angus Millar, from Nidderdale, and Tom Charlton, Aiden Jolly and Joel Buckley, all from Ripon.

National fell-running champion and boarding student Angus achieved ten grade 9s alongside two grade 7s.

Tom, who aims to study astrophysics at university, and Aiden, who hopes to become an engineer, gained ten grade 9s and a grade 8, while aspiring doctor Joel achieved ten grade 9s and a grade 7.

Overall, 18.9 per cent of all grades awarded at RGS were grade 9s, with students securing a total of 246 grade 9s.

  Ripon Grammar School recorded one of its strongest sets of results in recent years, with nearly one in five grades awarded at the highest level 

*(Image: Supplied)*

A strong 58.4 per cent of all grades were at grades 9-7, while 92.5 per cent were at grades 9-5.

Headmaster Jonathan Webb said: “My congratulations go to all our students on these excellent GCSE results, which are the culmination of five years of hard work, commitment and academic endeavour.
