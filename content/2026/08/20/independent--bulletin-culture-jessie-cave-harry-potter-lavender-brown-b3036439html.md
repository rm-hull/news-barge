# Harry Potter star Jessie Cave says OnlyFans has made her ‘more confident as a woman’

- Jessie Cave, best known for playing Lavender Brown in the *Harry Potter* franchise, said making content for OnlyFans “really has saved me” during a time when she was struggling for work and money.
- The 39-year-old actor caters to a non-sexual niche by uploading “hair content” and previously revealed she made more money on the platform in one year than during her entire acting career.
- Speaking on ITV's *This Morning* , Cave said: “I took a chance and I thought I’d make a little bit of money to pay back the money I lost during one of the Edinburgh Festivals I’ve done – because it’s extortionate now – and I’ve just kept going.”
- She added: “It’s been a fun little hobby on the side, and it’s really opened me up and definitely made me more confident as a woman.”
- Cave, a mother-of-four, said that one of the reasons she struggles to find and fund opportunities for work is because “it costs so much for childcare”.
