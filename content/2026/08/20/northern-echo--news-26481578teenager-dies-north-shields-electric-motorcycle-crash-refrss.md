A teenager has died following a collision involving an electric motorcycle and a van.

The incident occurred in the Meadow Well area of North Shields, on Waterville Road at about 5.25pm yesterday (Wednesday, August 19).

The rider, 18-year-old McKenzie Ratcliffe from North Shields, was pronounced dead a short time later, despite the efforts of paramedics.

Sergeant Craig Bartle of Northumbria Police's roads policing unit said: "Our thoughts are firmly with McKenzie’s family and friends and we will offer them all of the support we can at this devastating time.

"We are carrying out a full investigation into the circumstances of the collision and have already undertaken several enquiries."

Police said that for reasons yet to be established, the electric motorcycle collided head-on with a green Mercedes Sprinter van.

The driver of the van, a man in his 60s, remained at the scene and is assisting officers with their enquiries.

Sgt Bartle said: "I would ask anyone with information which could assist us to get in touch."
