Police have closed a stretch of road east of York tonight (Thursday).

The A1079 is closed in both directions close to the turn-off to Barmby Moor.

There are police cars and officers stationed near to Main Street and The Green, Barmby Moor.

#### Crash location

Traffic is being directed through the village and round the obstruction. There are no major delays.
