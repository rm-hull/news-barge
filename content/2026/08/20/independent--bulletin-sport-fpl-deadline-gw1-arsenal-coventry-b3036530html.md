# FPL deadline: When does my team need to be locked in for GW1?

![Mikel Arteta's Arsenal will play in the opening game of the season](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/20/17/2026-08-20T135901Z_823873574_RC2YBLAQZ2WL_RTRMADP_3_SOCCER-ENGLAND-ARS-COV.JPG)

- The 2026-27 Premier League season will get underway on Friday night when defending champions Arsenal will face newly promoted Coventry City at the Emirates Stadium.
- The game is due to kick-off at 8pm BST, with Arsenal hoping to make a winning start to their title defence.
- Fantasy Premier League managers up and down the country will also be rushing to ensure they have their teams ready ahead of the deadline.
- With Friday’s match due to start at 8pm, the FPL deadline for Gameweek 1 is **6.30pm BST on 21 August.**
- It looks set to be an intriguing opening weekend, with Manchester United facing Hull City on Saturday, while Liverpool travel to Newcastle on Sunday.
