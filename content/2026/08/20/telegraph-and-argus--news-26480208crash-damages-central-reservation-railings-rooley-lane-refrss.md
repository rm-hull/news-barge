A CAR collided with a barrier on a busy Bradford road, causing damage to railings on a central reservation.

The incident happened on Rooley Lane close to Staithgate Roundabout at around 8:45 am this morning.

One vehicle also sustained damage, with its rear bumper appearing to have been affected.

An eyewitness who drove past shortly after the incident said the car was left facing lengthways across the road, with its rear bumper lodged in the railings.

  Damage to the central reservation 

*(Image: T&A)*

They believed the vehicle had spun before coming to a stop.

The car was later manoeuvred free from the barrier.

Despite the incident, traffic in the area appears to be flowing as normal.

West Yorkshire Police has been contacted for comment.
