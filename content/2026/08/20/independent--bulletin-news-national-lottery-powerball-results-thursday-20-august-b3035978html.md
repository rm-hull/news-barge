# National Lottery results: Winning Powerball numbers for Thursday 20 August

![The Powerball lottery game recently expanded to the UK](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/21/05/Powerball_UK_Sales_37222.jpg)

- Powerball players in the UK had the chance to win a £24 million jackpot in Thursday’s draw.
- The winning Powerball numbers were 10, 21, 58, 61, and 64, with Powerball number 17. The Power Play number is 2.
- The UK prize, payable over 30 years, represents an annuity calculated after tax at the highest possible rate.
- Powerball draws take place in the early morning every Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday.
- Even if you don’t match all five numbers and the Powerball, you could still land a prize up to £1,000,000.
