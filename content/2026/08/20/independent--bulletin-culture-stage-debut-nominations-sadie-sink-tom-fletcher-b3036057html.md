# Stranger Things star among high-profile nominees for Stage Debut Awards

- *Stranger Things* star Sadie Sink and McFly musician Tom Fletcher are among high-profile figures nominated for the Stage Debut Awards.
- Sink is shortlisted for best West End debut performer for her role in *Romeo and Juliet* , alongside her co-star Noah Jupe and actor Brendan Gleeson.
- Ralph Fiennes has earned a nomination for best director following his directorial debut with Shakespeare's classic comedy *As You Like It* .
- Tom Fletcher received a nod for best creative West End debut for his music and lyrics on *Paddington The Musical,* which also saw Arti Shah nominated.
- The winners across all categories will be announced at a ceremony hosted in London on 27 September.
