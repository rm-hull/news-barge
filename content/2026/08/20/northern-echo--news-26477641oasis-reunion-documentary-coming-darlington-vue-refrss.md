Music fans will be able to relive one of the biggest reunion tours in recent history when a new Oasis documentary arrives on the big screen in Darlington next month.

Oasis Live ’25, a documentary capturing the band’s long-awaited reunion and record-breaking world tour, will be shown exclusively in cinemas from September 9 to 17, including screenings at Vue Darlington.

The film features never-before-seen footage and the first joint interviews with Noel and Liam in over 20 years.

Stuart Dunn, general manager at Vue Darlington, said: "After decades of waiting, last year Oasis fans got the reunion they were hoping for.

"Now audiences can relive the iconic comeback on the big screen, with unprecedented access, behind-the-scenes footage and a joint interview – two decades in the making.

"What better way to enjoy this fly-on-the-wall documentary than from the comfort of Vue, offering the ultimate seat, screen and sound experience."

Don’t Look Back in Anger documents the band’s 41-date world tour, which included 17 UK shows and spanned five continents.

The film explores the "profound emotional impact" of the tour and what the band’s music means to audiences and generations worldwide.

Tickets for the Oasis documentary are available now.

For more information or to book tickets, visit www.myvue.com.
