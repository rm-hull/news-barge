Preparing for exams presents a challenge for most students, but one Teesside student had to balance revision with his flourishing ice hockey career.

Ben Elliott, who attended Our Lady & St Bede Catholic Academy in Stockton, had to make time for his studies between trips to Leeds four times a week to train and compete with the Leeds Knights ice hockey team.

Competing in the semi-professional National Ice Hockey League, Ben’s talent on the ice means he’s called on to play for both the Knights’ junior and senior teams.

Ben first began playing ice hockey more than ten years ago, and since developing into a strong skater, he has competed across both the UK and internationally.

Today, he has done exceedingly well in his GCSEs – achieving all Grade 4 or above, including Grade 6 in English Literature.

While taking some time to weigh up the options for his next step, Ben’s long-term goal is to compete in the European ice hockey leagues and to live and play in Sweden, Germany, Finland or the Czech Republic, where ice hockey enjoys a strong following.
