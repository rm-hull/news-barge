# Margaret Qualley walks movie premiere carpet in barefoot Chanel sandals

- Margaret Qualley attended the London premiere of her upcoming film, *The Dog Stars* , walking the white carpet in barefoot Chanel sandals that featured only heels, leaving her toes exposed.
- The 31-year-old actress paired the eye-catching footwear with a black fringed Chanel V-neck dress.
- The post-apocalyptic action film follows a pilot trying to survive alongside a group of survivors after a virus wipes out most of humanity.
- Co-star Jacob Elordi joined Qualley on the white carpet wearing a navy pinstripe suit and black-rimmed sunglasses.
- The appearance follows Qualley's recent separation from her husband of five years, Jack Antonoff, last month.
