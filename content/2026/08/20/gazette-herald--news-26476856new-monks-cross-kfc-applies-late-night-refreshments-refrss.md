A new KFC branch in York is seeking a late-night licence to serve until 5am.

Marsdens Caterers of Sheffield Limited, which plans to open the branch at Monks Cross Drive, Huntington, York, has applied to City of York Council for the licence.

The company wants to offer "late night refreshment" from 11pm to 5am every day, with the premises open daily from 9am to 5am.

The application is open to feedback until September 8.

Any objections or representations regarding the application can be made to City of York Council Licensing Services in writing.

Those interested can contact the licensing section at Hazel Court, Eco Depot, James Street, York, YO10 3DS, or email [licensing@york.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing@york.gov.uk.)

The application can also be viewed by prior arrangement with the council's licensing section by calling 01904 552422.

It is an offence to knowingly or recklessly make a false statement in connection with this application, with a maximum fine of an unlimited amount upon summary conviction.
