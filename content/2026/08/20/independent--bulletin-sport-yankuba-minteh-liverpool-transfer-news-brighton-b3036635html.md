# Liverpool have £50m offer for Mohamed Salah replacement rejected

- Brighton have rejected a £50m bid from Liverpool for Gambian winger Yankuba Minteh.
- Liverpool have targeted Minteh as a potential long-term successor to star forward Mohamed Salah.
- Minteh is currently out for around eight weeks with an injury, though he is reportedly open to making the move to Anfield.
- The Merseyside club are actively exploring alternative wide targets after missing out on Yan Diomande and encountering a £145m price tag for PSG's Bradley Barcola.
- Liverpool have already signed winger Victor Munoz this summer, but are reserving the majority of their transfer budget to secure another wide forward.
