Rangers battle FK Jablonec in the Conference League with the goal of reaching the league phase.

Rangers have enjoyed a boost on the eve of this fixture with their 11th signing of the summer in Kosta Nedeljkovic.

They enter the crunch tie against the Czech side after a 5-1 win over St Mirren on Sunday, with James Penrice scoring on his debut.

The big win snapped a four-game winless start for manager Derek McInnes, who underlined a change in atmosphere in training this week: “Even coming in on Monday morning after the win against St Mirren, everybody was a bit brighter and had a bit more of a spring in their step. I want a winning dressing room, I want a winning club and getting a win was important for us.”

Here’s everything you need to know about the tie:

## **When is Rangers v FK Jablonec?** 

The play-off first leg kicks off at 7.45pm on Thursday 20 August.

## **How can I watch Rangers v FK Jablonec?** 

Rangers v FK Jablonec will be exclusively live on RangersTV for fans in the UK and Ireland, with the game available worldwide with the exception of Czechia and Slovakia.

Individual games can be bought for £10.99, but fans can acquire a two-match bundle for the entire two-leg tie for £16.99.

Fans must purchase their game passes at least 90 minutes before kick-off for each match, with sales going offline 90 minutes before kick-off, with the club maintaining this measure will help provide a reliable viewing experience. More information can be found here.

## **What is the Rangers team news?** 

Kosta Nedeljkovic signed for the club on Wednesday and could go straight into the squad.

Though the initial squad list was submitted last week, Uefa regulations mean two changes can be made before Wednesday’s deadline, and McInnes confirmed that another summer arrival James Penrice will be one of those and go into the squad.

While McInnes added that Nedeljkovic could also feature too: “He has been training and playing with Villa throughout pre-season. We have looked at his minutes and training schedule, and he has been active.”

Tuur Rommens is out for several weeks, as are Youssef Chermiti and Jose Cifuentes. While Ben Godfrey is close to featuring, but this tie could come too soon, with the second leg expected to be a more realistic target.

Fatigue saw Daisuke Yokota replaced at half-time on Sunday against St Mirren, but he should have recovered for this game.

While John Souttar is expected to miss out and could leave the club in the coming days.
