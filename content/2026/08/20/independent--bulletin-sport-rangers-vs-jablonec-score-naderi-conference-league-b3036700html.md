# Rangers secure nervy win over little-known Czech side in Conference League play-off clash

![Ryan Naderi scored the only goal of the game](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/20/22/01M0GC6Q0DEAQT9KNPVDJVS1B8.jpg)

- Ryan Naderi scored the only goal of the game as Rangers beat Czech side Jablonec 1-0 in the first leg of the Conference League play-off clash at Ibrox on Thursday.
- Rangers endured a difficult first half and were booed off by their own fans at the break after failing to find a breakthrough.
- Naderi finally gave the fans something to cheer about when he opened the scoring in the 50th minute.
- However, Rangers had to play the final minutes of the game with 10 men after substitute Cammy Devlin was sent off for a high tackle.
- Rangers managed to hang on to take a slim advantage into next week’s second leg.
