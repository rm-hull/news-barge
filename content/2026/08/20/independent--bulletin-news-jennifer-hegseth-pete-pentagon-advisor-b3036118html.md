# Pete Hegseth’s wife sparks controversy with outsized Pentagon role: report

- Jennifer Hegseth, the wife of Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, has reportedly emerged as her husband's most influential advisor at the Pentagon.
- Sources indicate she encouraged efforts to restrict press access and revoke credentials for Pentagon reporters who wrote negative stories about her husband.
- Beyond press policies, Jennifer Hegseth has conducted detailed job interviews for prospective staffers and attended high-level defense meetings.
- Her inclusion in sensitive discussions, including a Signal chat on Yemen attack plans and a meeting with British officials, has drawn sharp criticism from lawmakers.
- While critics and Democrats have called her level of access highly unusual, Pentagon officials defended her involvement as a vital asset to the department.
