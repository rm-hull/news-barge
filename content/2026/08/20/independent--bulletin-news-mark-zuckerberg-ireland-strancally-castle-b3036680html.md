# Mark Zuckerberg to spend more time in Ireland after purchasing 200-year-old castle

- Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg and his wife Priscilla Chan have purchased Strancally Castle, a 19th-century Gothic-style estate in southeast Ireland.
- Strancally Castle was built almost 200 years ago on land overlooking the River Blackwater and underwent extensive renovation in 2003.
- The property is located 125 miles from Meta's European headquarters and is estimated to be worth between €20 million and €30 million.
- A spokesperson confirmed the purchase, saying that Zuckerberg and his family look forward to spending time in Ireland, where Meta employs around 1,500 people.
- The statement read: “Mark and his family are excited to continue caring for this historic home and look forward to spending time in Ireland, where Meta maintains its international headquarters.”
