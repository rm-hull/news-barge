# ‘Friends’ star reveals what role she actually wanted to play on the hit sitcom

- Lisa Kudrow revealed she originally asked *Friends* producers if she could play Rachel Green instead of Phoebe Buffay.
- Kudrow believed she could bring humor to the role of Rachel by portraying her as a Jewish American Princess.
- Show bosses rejected the request because casting Phoebe was particularly difficult and they did not see Kudrow as the romantic lead.
- The role of Rachel ultimately went to Jennifer Aniston, while Kudrow played Phoebe for the sitcom's full 10-season run from 1994 to 2004.
- Kudrow noted that portraying Phoebe required significant effort due to their personality differences, though she eventually absorbed some of the character's lightheartedness.
