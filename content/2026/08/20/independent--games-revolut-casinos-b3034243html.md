## **What is Revolut, and how does it work on casinos?**

Revolut is a popular digital bank that is licensed to operate in the UK (but is based in Lithuania).

It offers a Visa/Mastercard debit card rather than a dedicated e-wallet product like PayPal or Skrill.

While customers can use the credit card product at Revolut, only the debit card works on casinos.

Revolut credit cards are blocked under the 2020 UK credit card gambling ban, which is the same as any other UK-issued credit card.
