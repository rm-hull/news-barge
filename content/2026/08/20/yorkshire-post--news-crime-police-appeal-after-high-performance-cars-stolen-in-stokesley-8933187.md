# Police appeal after high-performance cars stolen in Stokesley

## Police have launched an urgent appeal after three high-performance cars were stolen overnight from a North Yorkshire market town.

Police have launched an urgent appeal after three high-performance cars were stolen overnight in Stokesley.

Thieves forced entry to a property in the North Yorkshire market town on August 19/20 and stole a red Jaguar F-type, a grey BMW M5 and a dark grey Porsche Macan S, along with a 1978 Yamaha TY80 Trials motorbike.

Detectives say the vehicles may now be displaying different number plates to those in the photographs below.

West Yorkshire Police said in a statement: “We are appealing to anyone who has seen the vehicles, knows where they are now or has any other information that could assist our investigation to get in touch.“

If you see them or know where they are now, please call 999.”
