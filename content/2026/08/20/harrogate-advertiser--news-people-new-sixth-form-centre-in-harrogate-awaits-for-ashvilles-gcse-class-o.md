# New Sixth Form Centre in Harrogate awaits for Ashville’s GCSE Class of 2026 celebrating top grades

## Ashville has praised pupils from its GCSE Class of 2026 after it achieved another “excellent” set of grades, complementing the success of its A Level high-performers.

Rhiannon Wilkinson, Head of Ashville, said: “Our whole school community is thrilled to congratulate our Year 11 cohort on their fantastic GCSE results today.

![](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmYwMmFmZmZhLTRlZmYtNDY3ZC1hYzhjLThjZTg1MjhmM2Y5YzowNmQzOTEwYi1lMjcyLTRlNGMtYTE0MS0yZjJiZGRmMzE0N2Q=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



“Every success has been well-deserved and we are so proud of what these pupils have achieved both in and out of the classroom.

“They can now focus on the future and the global opportunities that lie ahead.”

![Ashville in Harrogate has praised pupils from its GCSE Class of 2026 after it achieved another “excellent” set of grades. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjQyZjA1N2ZiLTAxMmEtNDFkNi1hZTNmLWY2YWYxY2IyZGNlNTo1YTZmNzNmMy00NWQzLTQzMzktODA2My0wMDk3MjNkZTUzYzY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



“We look forward to welcoming pupils to Six @ Ashville, our new-look Sixth Form Centre with the unique and reimagined experience it offers.

“It is tailored to open up varied pathways to young people to ensure they are exam ready, university ready, career ready, and life ready for a rapidly changing world.”

Among the many pupil success stories are:

Ciara Nixon, who achieved GCSE academic success alongside her drama and sports performances which saw her crowned Ashville’s Young Actor of the Year, playing a lead role in the school’s flagship musical Sister Act, while representing the school on the sports field in hockey and netball.

Natalie Wong, whose musical talents saw her play alongside Dr Blanc Chun-Pong Wan, an Ashville pupil from 2002 to 2008 and world-renowned classical pianist, in a Masterclass when he returned to the school in July after being honoured with a British Empire Medal by King Charles this year for his contribution to UK Higher Education and Music Culture.

There was also an across-the-board success for a group who won academic scholarships ahead of their GCSE years as a result of great work and progress at Ashville in Years 7 - 9.

Each of the four – Bella Precious, Oliver McAllister, Max Sochart and Lily Ross – progressed to achieve excellent results.

Lily combined her passion for Art and Netball, while Max blended his interests in Maths and Engineering while representing the school at Basketball, to achieve their first-class grades.

“Success is never a solo journey; this is testament to the teamwork that exists between pupils, staff and parents here,” said Mrs Wilkinson.

"Everyone at Ashville would like to extend our heartfelt thanks to our first-rate teaching and support staff.”
