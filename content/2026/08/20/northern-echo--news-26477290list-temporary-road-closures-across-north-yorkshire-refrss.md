A series of temporary road closures in North Yorkshire have been outlined in a public notice.

North Yorkshire Council has announced the temporary closures, which are due to various works, ranging from water mains renewal to bridge maintenance.

Deighton Lane in Little Smeaton will be closed from August 17 to August 21 due to works from Salutation Cottage Farm to opposite Danby Dale.

The Bynt Group, which is handling the works, has stated that the maximum duration of this order is until November 17.

In Barton, The Green will be closed from its junction at Kettle End to the junction with Wells Lane for footbridge repair works, from August 17 to September 25.

East Hauxwell Village Roads will be closed from August 17 to September 11 due to Yorkshire Water works to renew the water mains.

Middle Road in Redmire will be closed from September 1 to September 7 for the installation of approximately 60 metres of duct and road crossing.

East Harlsey to Somerset House Farm will be closed from September 11 to October 30 for bridge maintenance works.

Low Lane in Hutton Sessay will be closed from September 3 to October 9.

Moor Lane in Islebeck will be closed from September 3 to September 16 due to works on the junction of Islebeck Lane and Moor Lane.

The Aysgarth to Thornton Rust road will be closed from August 31 to September 18 for strengthening and drainage works at Thornton Gill Bridge.

Albemarle Drive in Catterick Garrison will be closed from September 1 to September 9 for works to excavate and locate cable, carry out disconnection/alteration.

They can also be viewed online at www.northyorks.gov.uk/roadworks-map or www.northyorks.gov.uk/traffic-regulation-orders.

Anyone wishing to object to the proposed order should send their grounds for objection in writing to North Yorkshire Council, Traffic Management, County Hall, Racecourse Lane, Northallerton, DL7 8AD, by email to [traffic.management@northyorks.gov.uk](mailto:traffic.management@northyorks.gov.uk), or via the website link above by September 4.
