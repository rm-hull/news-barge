A new on-demand laundry service is bringing doorstep dry cleaning and washing to customers through a familiar delivery app.

Just Eat has launched a partnership with Laundryheap to offer doorstep laundry collection and delivery through its platform.

The service began in London on Tuesday, August 18, and will roll out to Birmingham, Manchester, and Edinburgh over the next six months.


## Just Eat's new laundry service

Caroline Bates, grocery and new verticals director at Just Eat UK, said: "Summer often means busy diaries and busier wardrobes.

"Whether you've cycled into work on the hottest days of the year, have a week's worth of shirts waiting to be washed, or need your wedding outfit professionally cleaned before the next celebration, we're making it even easier to get everyday jobs done.

"Our partnership with Laundryheap is another step in our ambition to become the UK's go-to convenience platform.

"Customers already turn to Just Eat for dinner, groceries and everyday essentials and now they can book laundry collections through the same app, giving them one less thing to think about."

  The delivery app announced it has teamed up with UK-based Laundryheap 

*(Image: LAUNDRYHEAP/Kennedy News)*

Laundryheap, which operates in more than 60 cities across 167 countries, offers customers a 24-hour turnaround for dry cleaning, washing, and folding.

Its app is used by 400,000 customers, who book for their clothes and bedding to be picked up, cleaned and returned within 24 hours.

Deyan Dimitrov, founder and CEO at Laundryheap, said: "Our goal at Laundryheap is simple – to make busy lives easier.

"We want to give people more time to do the things they love by taking one of life’s most tedious chores off their hands.

"That’s why we are absolutely thrilled to partner with Just Eat on its mission to redefine everyday convenience.

"Now Brits will be able to order dinner and get their dry cleaning done all in the very same app, delivered to their door.

"Laundry has quite literally never been easier."


Just Eat said the new service is designed to support customers during the summer months, when hot weather and busy schedules can make keeping up with laundry more difficult.

Laundryheap services start from £1.85 per item for dry cleaning.

Customers are advised to pack laundry in disposable bags, though bags will be provided if needed at pick-up.

Collection is promised within two hours of ordering.

Just Eat said collection and delivery are free, but standard service fees will apply to each order.
