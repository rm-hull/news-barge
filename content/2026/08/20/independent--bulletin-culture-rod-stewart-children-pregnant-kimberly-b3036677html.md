# Rod Stewart to become a grandfather for the seventh time

- Musician Rod Stewart is set to become a grandfather for the seventh time after his daughter Kimberly announced her pregnancy.
- Kimberly Stewart, 47, revealed she is expecting her third child, having previously welcomed a daughter in 2011 and a son in July 2025.
- The 81-year-old singer currently has eight children by five mothers and six grandchildren.
- The news comes after Stewart announced a four-week break from performing to recover from a coronary stent procedure.
- The medical procedure forced the singer to cut short the remainder of his scheduled tour dates across the US and Mexico.
