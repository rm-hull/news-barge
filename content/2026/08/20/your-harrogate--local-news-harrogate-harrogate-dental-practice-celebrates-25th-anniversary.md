A Harrogate dental practice is celebrating 25 years in business.

The Raglan Suite has marked its 25th anniversary, having grown from a small two-surgery practice to three times its original size.

The team celebrated the milestone with a video montage featuring photographs, memories and staff members past and present who have helped shape the practice over the last quarter of a century.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a86bcd43ea5adfff83.jpg)


The Raglan Suite said its patients have remained at the heart of the practice throughout its growth.

A spokesperson said:

“Reflecting over the last 25 years we are filled with gratitude to our wonderful patients for trusting us with your smiles.

“We would like to extend a huge thank you to our referring dentists for your continued trust and partnership over the years.

“We truly appreciate your support in allowing us to care for your patients.”


The practice has also paid tribute to current and former members of its team.

It said:

“Everyone that has walked through those doors has played a part in our story.

“Every birthday celebrated together, every success shared, the laughter, the odd tear and the many, many social occasions!

“Thank you all for making these last 25 years so special.”
