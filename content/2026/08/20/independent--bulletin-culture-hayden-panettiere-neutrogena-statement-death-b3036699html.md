# Neutrogena issues statement to address Hayden Panettiere backlash

- Neutrogena has issued a public statement following the death of actor Hayden Panettiere, who died on Sunday at age 36.
- The skincare brand faced backlash after Panettiere's past comments resurfaced, suggesting she was fired from her role as a brand ambassador for speaking out about postpartum depression.
- Neutrogena acknowledged that the company “made her feel unsupported during a very difficult time”.
- The company pledged to make a significant investment in a long-standing community health partner to support women's health causes.
- Neutrogena extended condolences to Panettiere's family and noted that it delayed speaking out, out of respect for her loved ones.
