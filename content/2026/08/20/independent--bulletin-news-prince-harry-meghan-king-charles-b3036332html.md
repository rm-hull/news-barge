# MAGA piles on Harry and Meghan as couple reportedly prepares to leave America

![Allies of President Donald Trump mocked the couple on social media following reports suggesting their relocation is partly due to disillusionment with the White House](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/04/9/35/714652121_18041404841800310_1242387415731909405_n-copy.jpeg)

- Prince Harry and Meghan Markle are reportedly returning to the United Kingdom for an extended period after six years of living in the United States.
- Allies of President Donald Trump mocked the couple on social media following reports suggesting their relocation is partly due to disillusionment with the White House.
- Sources say the move is also intended to let Prince Harry spend more time with his father, King Charles III.
- King Charles reportedly welcomed spending time with his son and grandchildren, but confirmed there will be no change to their status as non-working royals.
- The decision comes after a history of public friction between Prince Harry and President Trump over military service remarks and the royal's US visa.
