After months of hard work and lots of waiting, GCSE students are set to find out their exam results.

Students will find out their results today (August 20), weeks after taking their final exams.

This will mean that everyone who took their GCSEs, including Maths, English, Science and their optional courses, will find out their results.

But do teachers find out results before them? Here’s what we know.


## Do teachers see GCSE results before students on results day?

It is likely your teachers will find out the results earlier than students, but the time they receive them can vary.

Teachers don’t receive the results directly, as it is usually the exams officer who will receive the results.

## How to collect GCSE results

Students will not find out their results from their teachers – instead, students must find out when they are released at 8am.

They can go to their school to receive their results - teachers will let them know when they can collect the results.

## Can I resit my GCSEs or A Levels?


BBC Bitesize shared: "Some students will receive their results via the Education Record app this year, with 95,000 students in Manchester and the West Midlands taking part in a pilot scheme ahead of a national rollout.

"If you are part of this launch, then your grades will be delivered directly to your phone from 11:00 BST on results day."

## What happens if you can't pick up your GCSE and A-levels from school?

If you can't pick up your GCSE exam results from school on Thursday, you will need to contact your school, which can either inform students of the results via post or email.
