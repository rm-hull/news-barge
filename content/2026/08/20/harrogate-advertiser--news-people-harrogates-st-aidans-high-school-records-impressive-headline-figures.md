# Harrogate's St Aidan's High School records 'impressive headline figures and individual success stories'

## Students, staff and families at St Aidan's Church of England High School are celebrating an exceptional set of GCSE results, with many of the school's headline measures reaching the highest levels ever recorded.

The outstanding outcomes reflect the dedication, resilience and ambition of the Class of 2026 and mark a significant milestone in the school's history.

![A delighted student at St Aidan's Church of England High School in Harrogate celebrates her GCSE grades.  (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjJhODgyZDM1LThjYTgtNGUzZC05ODlhLWFhOWE3NmY1YmU2Mzo4ZmVlNTJlYy04NDQ0LTRjZTMtOGVhNS0xMzc5OTZkYWMzZDI=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Across a range of key performance indicators, students have achieved record-breaking results, cementing St Aidan's reputation for academic excellence and ensuring that more young people than ever are progressing to ambitious next steps.

Alongside these impressive headline figures, there were countless individual success stories.

![Siân Dover, Headteacher of St Aidan's Church of England High School in Harrogate, with pupils on GSCE exam results day. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjNjMjA2OThkLTE4NTctNDZhOS05NTg0LTcxMWViNzYyNzAwMjo5ZTM1NWI2OC1hOGMyLTQ1N2EtODYyNC1kNjdhMDM4OWU1MzM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Students achieved remarkable outcomes across a wide range of subjects, with many securing multiple Grade 9s and 8s, significantly exceeding expectations and opening up exciting opportunities for further study and future careers.

Nearly 90% of all students have achieved a grade 4 or above in both English and maths with students celebrating gaining a grade 7 (the old grade A) or above in every subject.

Some students have overcome real adversity to achieve grades that could never have been thought possible without the care, nurture and support of such a dedicated team of staff.

Siân Dover, Headteacher of St Aidan's Church of England High School, said: "We are absolutely thrilled with these results and immensely proud of every one of our students.

![Exceptionally good results - Siân Dover, Headteacher of St Aidan's Church of England High School in Harrogate, said: "We are immensely proud of every one of our students. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjIxZGNiZmFiLWIyYzYtNGUwZC1hYzZiLWJmNjVmNjUwM2Q2NDo4NjEzYTk3Zi05NGFlLTRjODYtYTZjZC1hY2QyNDQzZTliZmE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



"This year's outcomes are among the strongest in the history of St Aidan's and represent the best results the school has ever achieved.

"These exceptional outcomes are the result of years of hard work, determination and resilience from our students, supported by dedicated staff and families who have encouraged them every step of the way.

"We are particularly delighted that so many students have not only achieved excellent grades but surpassed their own expectations.”

Jane Goodwin, Chief Executive of Yorkshire Causeway Schools Trust, said: "These results are a tremendous achievement for St Aidan's Church of England High School and reflect the ambition, commitment and excellence that are evident throughout the school community.”

Karen Baker, Chair of Governors, said: "Governors are delighted to celebrate this exceptional year of achievement.”
