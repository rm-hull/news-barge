A major motoring event is set to return this autumn, promising a day out for car enthusiasts and families alike.

Newby Hall & Gardens will host the Autumn Sports Cars in the Park show on Sunday, September 6, bringing together a vast array of sports cars from across the UK.

Visitors can expect to see vehicles from renowned marques including Aston Martin, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Porsche and Lotus, alongside trade stands and market stalls.

Stuart Gill, commercial director at Newby Hall, said: "We look forward to the return of the show, which is always popular with visitors who can enjoy a wide range of prestigious car marques in the unique setting of Newby’s magnificent parkland.

"The added attractions of our award-winning gardens and children’s activities make the event a great family day out for everyone."

Exhibitors can register their vehicles at www.carsinthepark.org.uk with gates opening from 10am to 3pm.

Tickets are priced at £65 for a family (two adults and two children), £22 for adults, £16 for children aged four to 15, and children under four go free.

Entry includes access to Newby’s gardens, adventure playground, dollshouse and teddy bear exhibitions, as well as the house, miniature railway, and river boat rides.
