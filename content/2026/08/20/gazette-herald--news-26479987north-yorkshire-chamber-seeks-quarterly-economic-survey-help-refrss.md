**Businesses across York and North Yorkshire are being urged to share their views and experiences to support important research about the region's economy.**

West & North Yorkshire Chamber of Commerce wants to hear how the region’s businesses are is faring as it looks to complete its next Quarterly Economic Survey for 2026.

The results are shared with the likes of the Treasury, the Bank of England, the Office for Budget Responsibility, West Yorkshire Combined Authority and both regional and national media.

Mark Casci, head of policy and representation at the chamber, said: "We need businesses like yours to get involved and take part in our crucial research, in order to obtain valuable information and data about key issues affecting the business community in our region.

"The QES acts as a way for businesses to feed back to the West & North Yorkshire Chamber of Commerce, so that we can assist with instigating positive change for the members that we represent.

  The chamber surveys businesses every quarter.

"This survey provides a snapshot of local and regional economic trends to outline any key areas where your business might be struggling or thriving at present. The survey also monitors business confidence and supports the economic forecasting of key organisations like the Bank of England.

"By taking part in this survey, you will enable us to present a clear overview of the region to the economic policy makers in the capital. Help us to help you!"
