If you are planning a trip to the Yorkshire Dales any time soon, there are so many tiny villages and towns that should be on your must-visit list.

Pateley Bridge is packed with cute shops and traditional Yorkshire streets while also being surrounded by "stunning scenery."

Pateley Bridge is not only a charming town to visit, but it is also home to the oldest sweet shop in England.

If you have a sweet tooth or need some fuel for a long hike, you might want to stop at Pateley Bridge for some old-fashioned sweets.


## What is Pateley Bridge like?

Pateley Bridge has been described as "charming" and a place where "you can experience all the charms of rural life."

The town is known for its historic buildings and history that dates back centuries.

For those who love shopping, Pateley Bridge has an award-winning high street packed with independent shops.

The Oldest Sweet Shop is a must-visit in the town and has shelves lined with old sweets to choose from, including boiled sweets, toffee, and luxury chocolates.


If you love flowers, you can stop off at the Sticks ‘N’ Stones Florist where you can pick up a bouquet or a hand-crafted gift to take home.

There is also the Paper People Books and Games where you can find your next read and the Fewston Farm Shop and Café for any locally produced food.

The town is also home to the "unique" Nidderdale Museum where you can see what rural life was like over the past 200 years.

The museum features different rooms such as a Victorian Parlour, General Store, and School Room with lots of old objects to see.

Reviewers on Tripadvisor say the museum is "definitely worth visiting" and is "crammed with objects."

  The museum is also popular with visitors.

For food, you can visit the Crown Inn, which offers a classic pub menu with dishes such as scampi and seabass to steak and ale pie.

The pub also offers a Sunday roast and has rooms for you to stay in if you would like to extend your stay in the area.

You might prefer The Willow where you can enjoy breakfast, a relaxing afternoon tea or a special evening meal.

You could also stop off at Cocoa Joe’s where you can find some of the best hot chocolate in England.
