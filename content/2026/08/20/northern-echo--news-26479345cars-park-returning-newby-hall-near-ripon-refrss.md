A North Yorkshire stately hall is preparing to welcome motoring enthusiasts as one of the region's most popular motoring events returns.

The Autumn Sports Cars in the Park event will take place at Newby Hall and Gardens, near Ripon, on Sunday, September 6.

Stuart Gill, commercial director at Newby Hall, said: "We look forward to the return of the show which is always popular with visitors who can enjoy a wide range of prestigious car marques in the unique setting of Newby’s magnificent parkland.

"The added attractions of our award-winning gardens and children’s activities make the event a great family day out for everyone."

The event brings together sports cars from across the UK, including models from Aston Martin, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Porsche, and Lotus.

Visitors will also be able to browse trade stands and market stalls.

Exhibitors can register their vehicles at www.carsinthepark.org.uk.

Gates will be open from 10am to 3pm.

Tickets are priced at £65 for a family (two adults and two children), £22 for adults, and £16 for children aged 4 to 15.

Entry is free for children under four.

Admission includes access to Newby Hall’s gardens, the adventure playground, doll’s house and teddy bear exhibitions, and the house itself.

Visitors can also enjoy the miniature railway and river boat rides.

Newby Hall is renowned for its Adam architecture and Chippendale interiors.
