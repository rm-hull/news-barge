# Trump warned people to evacuate Tehran as ICE made Iranian deportations a ‘priority’

- Newly disclosed emails show U.S. immigration officials worked directly with Iranian representatives to deport over 100 Iranian nationals between 2025 and 2026.
- Iranian officials requested amendments to deportation passenger lists and expedited removal processes, which ICE officials frequently accommodated.
- Deporting Iranian nationals remained a priority for the Trump administration even during a 12-day military conflict in June 2025 involving the U.S., Israel, and Iran.
- The records detail ICE facilitating meetings between Iranian representatives and detainees, sparking a lawsuit alleging asylum confidentiality violations.
- The Department of Homeland Security denied sharing confidential asylum records, despite reports that asylum seekers were deported against their will.
