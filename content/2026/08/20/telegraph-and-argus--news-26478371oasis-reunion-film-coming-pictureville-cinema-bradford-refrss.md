A CINEMATIC celebration of music legends is coming to the big screen.

Pictureville Cinema will host a series of IMAX screenings this September, including the Oasis documentary Don’t Look Back In Anger.

The Oasis film will run from September 9 to 12 and on September 15.

It follows Liam and Noel Gallagher on their Oasis Live ’25 reunion tour, featuring backstage footage and rehearsals.

Pictureville Cinema said: "Don’t Look Back In Anger charts Liam and Noel Gallagher’s triumphant reunion tour.

"The unique perspective includes rehearsal, backstage, and onstage access as well as the first joint interviews with Noel and Liam in over 20 years."

Queen Budapest, a newly restored 4K film of Queen’s legendary 1986 concert, will be screened from October 7 to mark its 40th anniversary.

The cinema will also show Raiders of the Lost Ark in IMAX from September 11 to 15.

Other highlights include Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey, which runs on 19–25, 29–31 August, and 5–6, 10, 13, 16 September, and the 30th anniversary 4K restoration of Trainspotting on August 21 and 22.

IMAX ticket prices are £12.50 for adults, £10.50 for concessions, and £8.60 for children.

Special screenings cost £20 for adults, £18 for concessions, and £10.50 for children.
