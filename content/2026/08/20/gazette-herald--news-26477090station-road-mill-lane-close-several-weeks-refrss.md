Two North Yorkshire roads will be closed to traffic for several weeks.

Station Road, Yedingham, will be shut for a temporary period of three weeks between August 24, 2026 and October 30, 2026 for scheme works.

Mill Lane, Church Houses, will be closed between August 24, 2026 and October 2, 2026 for water main and multiple service connection works.

Access to premises will be maintained during both closures.

Traffic signs will indicate the closure periods, and local alternative routes will be signed on site where appropriate.

The maximum duration for the Mill Lane order is until October 30, 2026.

The Station Road closure is being managed by North Yorkshire Council.

The Mill Lane closure is being managed by United Infrastructure.

Enquiries about the Station Road closure should be directed to North Yorkshire Council's customer service centre on 0300 131 2 131.

For Mill Lane, contact United Infrastructure on 07493 983062.

Further details are available at: northyorks.gov.uk/roadworks-map.
