# Southern Peru rocked by 6.7-magnitude earthquake

![Snow covered Andes Mountains in Peru](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/03/25/15/25134300-d95cea40-f31a-472d-82cc-cdcce7296cb8.jpg)

- A powerful 6.7-magnitude earthquake has struck Peru's southern Andes Mountains.
- The quake was centred 38 kilometres northwest of Upahuacho in the Ayacucho region and was felt across several southern areas, including Ica and Arequipa.
- Peru experiences frequent seismic activity due to its position along the Pacific Ring of Fire.
- The incident follows recent destructive earthquakes in Colombia and Venezuela.
- The region previously suffered a magnitude 7.9 earthquake in 2007, which killed nearly 600 people.
