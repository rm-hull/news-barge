A new Greggs menu is rolling out very soon across 200 chains in the North East - including the return of a popular pastry.

Fans have been speculating online about when we can expect the 2026 autumn menu - and now, the sausage roll chain has confirmed it's coming on September 3.

Greggs, founded in Newcastle in 1939, is launching 11 new menu items, including the return of the steak and stilton bake.

The new menu also includes a line-up of other autumn-themed flavours and returning classics.

All the fresh and returning products will be available at 200 Greggs shops across the North East and beyond, as well as via the click and collect option on the Greggs app.
