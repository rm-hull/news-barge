A major energy project in the Netherlands is being supported by the rapid work of a Yorkshire engineering firm.

Goole-based Salko UK completed critical maintenance on gas turbine exhaust systems at the Maxima Centrale site in just 37 days, working in partnership with ENGIE.

A 13-strong team from Salko UK delivered repairs across three exhaust gas housings and diffusers, helping to restore the unit for optimal efficiency and performance to prepare the site for operation at the end of the year.

Sim Sharphouse, operations manager at Salko UK, said: "We’re proud to have successfully delivered this vital project in just 37 days.

"Our team’s expertise ensured all units were repaired to the highest standard, supporting the project as it prepares for operation."

Salko UK was selected for the project due to its extensive experience in gas turbine exhaust systems, including comprehensive repairs and liner replacements.

Mr Sharphouse added: "Projects like this showcase the depth of our mechanical capabilities and the value of a highly skilled workforce.

"By working closely with ENGIE, we have been able to deliver tailored solutions that meet bespoke standards, demonstrating our commitment to safety, quality and timely delivery.

"We look forward to continuing to support ENGIE in driving forward the transition to cleaner, more efficient energy systems as this site prepares for operation."

The Maxima Centrale site is expected to come online at the end of the year.

Salko UK provides mechanical and electrical services across the energy, commercial, industrial and mining sectors.

The firm manages projects from design and supply through to fully managed turnkey solutions.

With a national client base, Salko UK prides itself on an agile approach, working directly with customers to deliver safe, high-quality outcomes.

The company also supports clients in the construction, mining, industrial, and nuclear sectors.
