# Harrogate school at the heart of community celebrates 'every achievement and every student'

## Harrogate’s ‘most welcoming’ school is celebrating the achievements of its students as they receive their GCSE results today.

As a proudly inclusive and ambitious school, Harrogate High School remains committed to ensuring that every young person, regardless of background or starting point, is supported to succeed.

This year's outcomes reflect sustained progress, continued improvements across a number of subject areas and the positive culture that has underpinned the school's recent improvement journey.

Alongside academic success, students at Harrogate High School have benefited from a wide range of enrichment and aspiration-raising opportunities, including participation in The Brilliant Club's Scholars Programme, which has helped develop confidence, critical thinking and preparedness for future study.

![Harrogate High School students receiving their GCSE exam results. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjg1NjkyNmUyLTE5NjQtNDNiZC04NDNlLTU0NmFhYzliN2ZkNjowZjQzZWQ1MC1mM2Q0LTQxMmMtYWIwYy00MDg2OGNlM2VmZjg=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The school also continues to place a strong emphasis on careers education and guidance, ensuring that all students move on to meaningful destinations, including further education, employment and apprenticeships, equipped with the skills and

confidence needed to flourish in adult life.

Harrogate High School’s Headteacher Sukhraj Gill said: "We are enormously proud of every student receiving results today.

"These outcomes reflect much more than grades.

(Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjgwNTY1ZmZiLTE3NWMtNDg0NS05YmU0LWI0MTM3Njc3NTliYTo1NDU2NTE5ZS1hODIxLTRjNmQtYmYxMC1lNDk2ZmI4ZmI2ZGM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



"They represent resilience, ambition and personal growth.

"As an inclusive school at the heart of our community, we are committed to ensuring that every young person feels valued, belongs, and has the opportunity to succeed.

"Whether our students are progressing to further education, apprenticeships or employment, we celebrate every achievement and every destination, and we look forward to seeing what they accomplish next."
