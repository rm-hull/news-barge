So you’ve got the grades, now you’re off to university. But before you stock up on Pukka Pads, reMarkable is giving students a reason to go digital, with the brand slashing prices on its ever-popular digital notebooks.

Designed to feel like putting pen to paper, reMarkable’s tablets let you scribble down lecture notes, annotate documents and convert your handwritten text into digital text for easy organisation.

Right now, reMarkable has slashed £100 off some of its digital notebook bundles. With the offer running for the next two weeks, you can save on the reMarkable paper pro and smaller paper pro move. Kicking off today (20 August) the deal will end on 1 September, so you’ve got time to grab a bargain before Fresher’s Week.

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/12/16/16/43/ReMarkable-paper-pro-move.png)

You can currently save £100 on this bundle, bringing the price down to just £399. With this bundle, you get the pocket-sized digital notebook, the brand’s premium marker stylus and a standard folio, giving you everything you need to start scribbling straight out of the box.

In his review of the paper pro move, IndyBest tester Thomas Deehan dubbed the device as the best digital notebook for writers. “The paper pro move is effectively a shrunk-down paper pro with all the features intact,” he said. “You have an E Ink-style display that’s capable of producing colour, and a backlight that makes scribbling down notes after dark an easy process.”

If you’d prefer something more luxe, you can get the same bundle but with a leather ‘folio premium’ rather than a regular ‘folio standard’ with a discount of £100, dropping the price to just £429.

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/12/16/16/25/ReMarkable-paper-pro.png)

If you want a bit more space to write, you can also save £100 on the larger reMarkable paper pro bundle. You get the 11.8in digital notebook, the marker premium stylus and a standard folio. The paper pro has a much larger colour display and adjustable reading light.

In his review of the paper pro, IndyBest tester Thomas Deehan named it the best premium digital notebook. “This larger tablet doubles down on everything that made its predecessor great, by boasting a larger 11.8in display that offers more room to scribble down your thoughts, alongside a colour display,” he said, while also praising the two-week battery life.

If you want an upgraded case, the paper pro bundle with the marker premium and folio premium has also been slashed in price, from £729 to £629. As with the paper pro move deal above, you get the same digital notebook and stylus, as well as reMarkable’s more premium leather case.

Finally, there’s a deal for anyone who wants to ditch their laptop entirely – not just their paper notepads. The paper pro bundle with the marker premium and type folio has been slashed from £769 to £669. The type folio case includes a physical keyboard for the paper pro, letting you type directly on the tablet when you’ve had enough of writing by hand.
