# Palestinian doctor beaten with sticks and stones amid rise in settler violence in West Bank

- Palestinian healthcare workers in the West Bank are facing an increasing wave of attacks and detentions, according to the charity Medical Aid for Palestinians.
- The World Health Organisation reported a fourfold increase in recorded attacks on healthcare in the region, rising from eight in June to 37 in July.
- Recent violent incidents include a nurse being shot and partially paralysed, a doctor beaten by settlers, and an ambulance attacked in Hebron.
- Obstetrician Dr Dima Mohammed Amin Barakat was detained by Israeli forces after publicly speaking out about the arrest of a colleague.
- Human Rights Watch and Medical Aid for Palestinians have called for urgent international action and targeted sanctions over state-backed settler violence.
