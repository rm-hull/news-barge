# Teen who died with parents in beach incident ‘will be greatly missed’, says school

- A 14-year-old British-Palestinian girl, Sara El-Khawas, and her parents died after getting into difficulty in the sea at Shoreham Fort, East Sussex.
- A fourth family member, a young girl, remains in hospital in a critical condition following the major emergency rescue operation on Tuesday.
- The Ellen Wilkinson School for Girls in Ealing paid tribute to Sara, describing her as a sweet and well-liked student who will be greatly missed.
- The London-based family were British citizens of Palestinian origin visiting the popular south coast location when the tragedy occurred.
- Andy Burnham issued a warning about the risks of open water swimming during heatwaves, reminding the public that cold water can cause sudden shock.
