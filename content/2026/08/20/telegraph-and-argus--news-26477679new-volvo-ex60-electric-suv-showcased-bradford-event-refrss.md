DRIVERS are being invited to test drive the latest fully electric SUV at an exclusive event.

Ray Chapman Motors Bradford, an approved Volvo retailer on Canal Road, is showcasing the new Volvo EX60 at a preview event running from Friday, August 21 to Sunday, August 23.

Duncan Chapman, managing director at Ray Chapman Motors Bradford, said: "We're delighted to welcome local drivers for an exclusive first look at the new Volvo EX60 here in Bradford.

"This weekend-long event is designed to be much more than a standard test drive, giving customers the chance to experience the technology that makes the EX60 so special."

The Volvo EX60 SUV recently won the title of the UK's most exciting upcoming new car at the What Car? Awards, just a day after its global debut.

The P12 AWD Electric model offers the longest driving range of any Volvo to date, reaching up to 503 miles on a single charge.

It also features ultra-fast charging from 10 to 80 per cent in as little as 16 minutes.

The event will include personalised test drives with a Volvo specialist, allowing visitors to experience features such as the 360-degree camera, intelligent parking sensors and driver assistance technologies.
