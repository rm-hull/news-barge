# What Max Verstappen committing his future to Red Bull means for F1

![Four-time F1 world champion Max Verstappen](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/20/09/01M0F0JPK8RV51P2DR0K1Z8ENC.jpg)

- Max Verstappen has ended speculation surrounding his Formula 1 future by signing a new long-term deal with Red Bull until 2030.
- The four-time world champion had previously been linked with potential switches to Mercedes and McLaren amid dissatisfaction with F1's new regulations.
- Verstappen described Red Bull as a second family and expressed his desire to remain with the team where he has spent his entire career.
- The commitment comes despite a difficult 2026 season so far, with the Dutchman currently sitting sixth in the drivers' standings.
- Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies hailed retaining the best driver on the grid as fantastic news for the team and motorsport as a whole.
