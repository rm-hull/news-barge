# Home Office booklet tells asylum seekers that rape is illegal in UK

- The Home Office has published a nine-page document entitled “Understanding behaviours and expectations in the UK” to inform asylum seekers about UK laws, gender equality, and acceptable public behaviour.
- The document advises that rape, domestic abuse, and sexual activity with minors are “serious crimes” which carry severe penalties, including imprisonment, rejection of asylum claims, and removal from the UK.
- Andy Burnham has defended the guidance, with the Prime Minister emphasising that UK laws and societal norms must be made explicitly clear to everyone living in the country.
- School standards minister Georgia Gould highlighted the importance of asylum seekers understanding British laws while cautioning against making sweeping generalisations about migrants.
- Opposition figures criticised the initiative, with shadow home secretary Chris Philp arguing that foreign offenders should be deported rather than taught how to behave.
