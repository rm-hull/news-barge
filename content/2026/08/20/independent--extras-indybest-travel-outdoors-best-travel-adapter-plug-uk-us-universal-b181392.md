Whether you’re planning summer holidays, skiing trips or weekend getaways abroad, the best travel adapters are essential bits of kit. From those with built-in USB ports to travel adapters designed for ultra-fast charging, I’ve tracked down the ones that are worthy of a spot in your luggage.

All adapters should come with the CE mark (you can find out more about travel adapter safety criteria at the end of this article) but, when it comes to watts, my advice is to avoid getting too obsessed with the numbers. The majority of factors that affect how effectively your gadgets will power up have nothing to do with the adapter. For example, up to 40 per cent of a power bank’s capacity can be lost to heat and conversion, plus the charging speed of any item (such as a laptop or tablet) is affected by its battery – mainly how old it is, and what condition it’s in.

Quality travel adapters are also designed to adjust their charging power depending on the voltage used by the destination in question (as a rule, between 220-240V in most European countries, and 120V in North America, for example).

With all that in mind, keep scrolling for my pick of the best travel adapters to pack in 2026.
