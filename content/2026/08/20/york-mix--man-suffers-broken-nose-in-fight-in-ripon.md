A man was hurt in a fight in Ripon.

It happened at 4pm on Sunday 2 August on Skellbank.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It involved an altercation between two men.

#### Skellbank, Ripon

“As a result of the incident, one of the men sustained a suspected broken nose.

“We’re appealing for information from anyone who witnessed the incident to come forward with information.”
