# Celtic confirms death of fan who attended Champions League qualifier

![Celtic Park, Glasgow](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/20/18/01M0G1EN4YGDKCYGMQX12KRN9H.jpg)

- A 65-year-old Celtic supporter has died in hospital after taking ill during Wednesday's Champions League qualifier against LASK at Celtic Park.
- Emergency services attended the scene at 9.05 pm and conveyed the man to Glasgow Royal Infirmary, where he died a short time later.
- Police Scotland confirmed that there are no suspicious circumstances surrounding the fan's death.
- Celtic Football Club expressed their deepest condolences to the supporter's family and thanked emergency services and fellow fans for their assistance.
- On the pitch, Celtic secured a 3-0 first-leg victory over LASK thanks to a goal from Benjamin Nygren and a brace from Camilo Duran.
