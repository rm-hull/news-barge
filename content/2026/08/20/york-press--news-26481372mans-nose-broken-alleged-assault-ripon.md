A MAN sustained a broken nose after an alleged assault in North Yorkshire.

Two men had the altercation at around 4pm on Sunday (August 2) in Skellbank, Ripon.

North Yorkshire Police said it was appealing for information from anyone who witnessed the incident to come forward with information.

  


A spokesperson said: “If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance by emailing [Gareth.Skelton@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:Gareth.Skelton@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

“Please quote reference 12260148688 when providing information.”
