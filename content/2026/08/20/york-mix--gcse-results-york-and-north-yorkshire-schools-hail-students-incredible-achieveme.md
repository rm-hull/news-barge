Thousands of students have been getting their GCSE results today (Thursday).

North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for education, learning and skills, Cllr Annabel Wilkinson, said: “Today is a special day for thousands of young people across the county, and I would like to offer my congratulations to every student receiving their GCSE and equivalent results.

“These results represent years of dedication, determination, and resilience, often met with challenges along the way.

City of York Council’s executive member for education Cllr Bob Webb, said: “Congratulations to all the young people receiving their GCSE and Key Stage 4 results today.

“Reaching this point reflects years of hard work, commitment and determination, and I would also like to thank the teachers, school staff, parents, carers and families who have supported students throughout their journey.

“Today is a significant milestone and a moment to celebrate each young person’s achievements. Whether you are moving on to sixth form, college, an apprenticeship, training or employment, these results mark the beginning of your next chapter.”

Here’s just some of the stories from our schools

## Huntington School, York

Matt Smith, headteacher of Huntington Schoool in York, said:

“Today is about celebrating the incredible achievements of our Year 11 students in their GCSEs.

“They have shown tremendous character, dedication and resilience – and their results show how they have lived out our school values of respect, honesty and kindness.

“In particular, the overall attainment of this year group comes out as our highest since the pandemic, which is an incredible achievement.

“The buzz in the results hall was really lovely this morning – it’s a proud moment for the teachers of Year 11 to see students reaping the rewards of their hard work and endeavour.

“Ultimately, it is our aim that students leave Huntington ready to move onto the ‘next step’.

This set of results confirms that our young people are well-positioned to take advantage of the opportunities that the future will bring. 

“For the vast majority, that means entry to our successful Sixth Form, whilst others will take a different route; regardless of their pathway, we extend our best wishes to everyone.”

## Ripon Grammar School

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/gcse-results-2026-ripon-grammar-school-1.webp)

Four Ripon Grammar School boys were on cloud nine as they celebrated achieving ten top grades or more in a stunning set of GCSE results.

The North Yorkshire school recorded one of its highest proportions of top grades in recent years, with almost one in five grades awarded being the very highest grade 9.

The results also included 485 grades at 9-8, equivalent to the top A* grades under the former GCSE grading system, with many exceptional performances among the cohort of 128 students collecting their results.

The four 16-year-old top performers are Angus Millar, from Nidderdale, and Tom Charlton, Aiden Jolly and Joel Buckley, all from Ripon.

National fell running champion and boarding student Angus achieved ten grade 9s alongside two grade 7s.

Tom, who aims to study astrophysics at university, and Aiden, who hopes to become an engineer, gained ten grade 9s and a grade 8, while aspiring doctor Joel achieved ten grade 9s and a grade 7.

Overall, 18.9% of all grades awarded at RGS were grade 9s with students securing a total of 246 grade 9s.

A strong 58.4% of all grades were at grades 9-7, while 92.5% were at grades 9-5.

Headmaster Jonathan Webb said: “My congratulations go to all our students on these excellent GCSE results, which are the culmination of five years of hard work, commitment and academic endeavour.

“We are particularly proud of the exceptional number of grade 9s achieved this year. The four students who achieved ten grade 9s each have produced outstanding performances.

## Boroughbridge High School
