A BRADFORD street will temporarily close for water works.

Back Southbrook Terrace will be shut to vehicles from 7am on September 1 to 7pm on September 11.

The closure stretches from the rear of No.10 to the rear of No.13.

The works, authorised by the City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, will see a temporary prohibition of traffic order in place.

Bradford council has confirmed that vehicles directly involved in the works will be exempt from the order.

Pedestrian access will remain open throughout the duration of the works.

No alternative routes for vehicles affected by the closure will be available.

The council has also stated that while the closure is scheduled to end on September 11, it might extend up to 18 months if necessary.

The notice was issued by the Director of Legal and Governance, Legal, and Democratic Services at the City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council.
