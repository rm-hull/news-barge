# Secret son of Belgian royal officially named a prince after 26 years

- Former car salesman Clément Vandenkerckhove has officially been named a Belgian prince after being recognised by his biological father, Prince Laurent.
- The 26-year-old is the secret child of Prince Laurent, brother of King Philippe, and Flemish singer Iris Vandenkerckhove, known as Wendy Van Wanten.
- A DNA test confirmed a 99.5 per cent match between Prince Laurent and Mr Vandenkerckhove, culminating in a private family ceremony six months ago.
- Although entitled to use a royal title and a share of his father's inheritance, he will not receive a royal allowance or join the line of succession.
- Mr Vandenkerckhove said that building a relationship with his father matters most to him, and he remains proud to keep his mother's surname.
