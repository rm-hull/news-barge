The traction engine rally is held next to the Abbey all weekend, 1st- 2nd July.

Performing in the main arena will be the Stannage International Stunt Show, featuring the flaming high fall show, exploding coffin, motorbike stunts, car jumps and trick riding.

As well as live acts, Scarborough Fair Stage Show will be showcasing a variety of family shows with a fantastic group of singers, dancers and entertainers.

There will be displays of traction engines, fairground organs, vintage cars/commercials, classic motorcycles, military vehicles, miniature steam engines, stationary engines and vintage tractors.
