Arsenal take their pre-season campaign in front of fans for the first time with a trip to Catalonia as they take on LaLiga outfit Girona.

The Gunners eased past MK Dons in a 3-0 behind-closed-doors win a few days ago, with Mikel Arteta hoping to go two for two as preparations continue for their Premier League title defence.

Fans have been whipped up into a frenzy by the Vinicius Jr transfer saga but all eyes will be on Christos Tzolis, the new arrival from Club Brugge who is set to get his first official run-out.

A number of World Cup stars have also rejoined the squad so could be in line to feature for the first time since the end of last term, with the last sight of Arsenal being their Champions League final shootout defeat to Paris Saint-Germain.

*Here’s everything you need to know about the clash.*

## When is Girona vs Arsenal?

Arsenal’s pre-season friendly with Girona kicks off at 7pm BST on Saturday 1 August at Estadi Montilivi.

## How can I watch it?

Fans can watch the clash on Arsenal.com by purchasing the pre-season pass, which costs £14.99 - or £9.99 if purchased before 9am on the day of the match. This will also allow you to stream the club’s remaining pre-season fixtures against Real Betis, Borussia Dortmund and Como.

## Team news

Arsenal are already lumbered with defensive injury problems after Ben White and Jurrien Timber left the camp nursing fresh woes, joining William Saliba who is out for a number of months with a back issue picked up at the World Cup. Piero Hincapie, Kai Havertz and Viktor Gyokeres have all joined up with the squad after the World Cup so could be in contention.

Girona, meanwhile, welcomed back Morocco midfielder Azzedine Ounahi from World Cup duty who could feature in a midfield against one-time Manchester United flop Donny van de Beek.

**Girona XI:** Krapyvytsov; A. Martinez, Lopez, Yaakobishvili, Moreno; Beltran, Ounahi; Camara, Misehouy, Gil; Vanat.

**Arsenal XI:** Kepa; Mosquera, Salmon, Calafiori, Hincapie; Lewis-Skelly, Norgaard; Dowman, Nwaneri, Tzolis; Jesus.
