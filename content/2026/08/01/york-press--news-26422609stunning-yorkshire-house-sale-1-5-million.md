## Inside stunning Yorkshire home in ‘idyllic rural setting’ for sale for £1.5 million

The home for sale near Everingham for £1.5 million *(Image: Ginger Cow)*

A stunning East Yorkshire home in an “idyllic rural setting” has hit the market – for £1.5 million.

The four-bedroom home near Everingham previously housed the Ginger Cow Company, which also offered holiday home shepherd huts and an event space at the 8.3-acre site.

  Inside the home for sale near Everingham for £1.5 million 

*(Image: Ginger Cow)*

A Ginger Cow spokesperson said its owners were moving on from the company after “eight wonderful years” and looking to hand over the property.

“We’re ready to embrace a new chapter in our lives and can’t wait to see what the next owners will do to make it their own,” they said.

“We’ve loved the peace and freedom that comes with living somewhere so beautiful – the long summer evenings enjoying a BBQ, surrounded by huge array of wildlife and amazing sunrises and sunsets”

The detached house blends modern living with traditional features, according to Savills, which is marketing the property.

Inside, the home has a spacious kitchen, open-plan dining and living area, and sitting rooms.

The four bedrooms offer views of the surrounding countryside and gardens, Savills said.

Outside, the estate agent said the property is “exceptionally well-equipped” for “equestrian enthusiasts”.

  A shepherd's hut which is included in the £1.5 million property sale near Everingham 

*(Image: Ginger Cow)*

Also included in the sale are a former tearoom used as a private event venue and the shepherd huts.

A Savills spokesperson said the properties are “surrounded by beautifully maintained gardens, bounded by a woodland planted with a huge array of seasonal bulbs”.

They added that the “idyllic rural setting is one mile from the village of Everingham on quiet country lane and only a short drive to the nearby market town of Pocklington”.
