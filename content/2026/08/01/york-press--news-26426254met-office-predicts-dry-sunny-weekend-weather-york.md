THE Met Office has forecast a sunny weekend for York, with temperatures reaching 24°C.

Saturday morning will start at 13°C, dropping to 10°C by late morning.

Clouds will clear for sunny intervals, with a low chance of rain, and a light northwesterly breeze will be present.

By the afternoon, temperatures will rise to 20°C, with mostly sunny skies and occasional clouds.

The chance of rain will peak at 30 per cent around 5pm.

Winds will increase slightly, but conditions will remain comfortable, while UV levels are expected to be high, so sunscreen is advisable.

The evening will remain warm, with temperatures around 21°C, cooling to 12°C by late evening.

Sunny intervals will continue before clear skies overnight, and dry conditions will persist, with winds easing to around 5 mph.

Sunday morning will start cool, with temperatures between 8°C and 10°C, rising quickly to 20°C by late morning.

Bright sunshine is expected throughout the morning, with a rain risk below 5 per cent.

Winds will be almost calm, creating a peaceful start to the day.

The afternoon will be warm and pleasant, with temperatures reaching 24°C, and the day will be predominantly sunny.

The chance of rain will remain below 5 per cent, and light winds will make it feel comfortably warm.

The evening will see temperatures gradually fall from 23°C to 15°C.

Sunny intervals will continue before becoming partly cloudy later at night.

Rain will remain unlikely, with winds staying light, ensuring a pleasant end to the weekend.
