## Analysis: Hamas agreement 'calling Israel's bluff', say experts

Chatham House expert and associate fellow in the Middle East and North Africa Programme, Dr Julie Norman told The Independent that Hama’s agreement “calls Israel’s bluff”.

“Hamas’s reported agreement to Trump’s disarmament plan essentially calls Israel’s bluff: for the past nine months, Hamas’s refusal to disarm has been held up as the primary obstacle to unlocking other elements of the ceasefire plan, including the entry of the technocratic National Committee for the Administration of Gaza (NCAG) and the International Security Force,” she said.

“Moreover, it has precluded any discussion of Israeli withdrawal from Gaza, with Israel’s control of the Strip only increasing over the same period.

“The announcement can shift that dynamic. In reality, it’s unlikely things will change on the ground anytime soon.

“The last thing Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu wants going into October’s elections is US pressure to withdraw from Gaza, and it’s likely he will foot-drag any further negotiations as much as possible.

“Slow-walking the process also suits Hamas. It’s a pragmatic move by new Hamas leader Khalil Al-Hayya that keeps the group in the conversation and improves their positioning with the Board of Peace. But they know that actual decommissioning will not be immediate.

“Indeed, both actors will likely double down on sequencing priorities that have stymied previous attempts to reach agreements in Gaza: Israel will demand full Hamas disarmament before withdrawal, while Hamas will demand Israeli withdrawal before decommissioning.

“The question is if the Board of Peace in general, and Trump in particular, are willing to put the pressure on both parties to move past the current stalemate.”
