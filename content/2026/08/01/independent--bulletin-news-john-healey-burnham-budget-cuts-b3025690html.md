# Healey warns ministers of spending cuts to fund Burnham’s new pledges

- Chancellor John Healey has informed cabinet ministers that no new funding is available for Prime Minister Andy Burnham's policy pledges, requiring departments to reprioritise existing budgets.
- This directive comes ahead of Healey's first Budget on 28 October, which he states will be founded on "fiscal discipline".
- Burnham's government has made several costly announcements, including VAT cuts on electricity bills and reduced business rates for pubs, alongside plans for radical devolution and expanded technical training.
- Healey faces significant challenges in funding these initiatives and increased defence spending, particularly given warnings that the Iran war will further strain public finances.
- Experts suggest Healey may need to consider tax rises or cuts to welfare and pensions, potentially conflicting with Labour's manifesto pledges, to meet fiscal rules amidst economic pressures.
