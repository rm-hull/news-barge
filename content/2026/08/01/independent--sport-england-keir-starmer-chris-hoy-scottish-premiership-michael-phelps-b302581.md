The Commonwealth Games comes to a close in Glasgow.

The final round will be played at the AIG Women’s Open while the opening weekend of the Scottish Premiership continues along with The Hundred.

Here is where you can watch the major sporting events taking place on Sunday.

## Final action in Glasgow

![Action concludes in the track cycling (Andrew Milligan/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/01/15/d26339127839d3db6fe108102ccd3a69Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1NjcxNjEy-2.85466125.jpg)

**Track cycling** headlines the final day of the**Commonwealth Games** in Glasgow, with events including the women’s keirin.

Medals are handed out in **netball**, with England going for bronze after their semi-final loss to New Zealand, while the action wraps up in** judo**and** bowls**.

Coverage begins on **TNT Sports 2** at 0830, while the closing ceremony will be shown from 1930.

## Winner crowned in Women’s Open

![Charley Hull will hope to be in contention (Peter Byrne/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/01/15/7ee66089cc096f0f686662064660ae37Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1Njc1OTA2-2.85466403.jpg)

The final round takes place in the **AIG Women’s Open** at Royal Lytham and St Annes.

Thailand’s **Jeeno Thitikul** and **Ryu Hae-ran ** of South Korea look set to be in contention, with the latter bidding for a third straight major crown.

**Charley Hull** and**Lottie Woad** are expected to lead the home hopes, with coverage starting at midday on **Sky Sports Golf** and 1330 on **Sky Sports Main Event**.

## What else is happening on Sunday?

**Hibernian** face** Motherwell**in the** Scottish Premiership**on** Sky Sports Football**at 1630, while** Kilmarnock**visit** St Johnstone** at 1400.

Action in **The Hundred** sees**Trent Rockets** take on **Sunrisers Leeds** on**Sky Sports Cricket** at 1045 (**Sky Sports Main Event** from 1100) for the women’s fixture, with the men following at 1400 on**Sky Sports Cricket**.** Manchester Super Giants**face** MI London **with the women’s game on** BBC Two England**from 1415 and the men on** Sky Sports Cricket**from 1730 and** BBC Two England** at 1800.

In tennis, the finals of the **DC Open** in Washington will be shown on**Sky Sports Tennis** from 1930 and**Sky Sports Main Event** from 2100, while the**Masters 1000** events in Montreal and Toronto kick off on**Sky Sports Tennis** at 1600.

The **Shanghai Masters** snooker final will be on**TNT Sports 3** from 0700.

## On this day in history

![Andy Murray waved goodbye to tennis at the Olympics in 2024 (Martin Rickett/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/01/15/a789f5388ffb1fe2a3f12dec10b16fe5Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1Njc3MDY0-2.77027348.jpg)

**1992**: Snooker star Ronnie O’Sullivan’s record run of 38 consecutive match wins in ranking tournaments was ended by Sean Storey.

**2012**: Cyclist Sir Chris Hoy claimed his fifth Olympic gold medal as he, Philip Hindes and Jason Kenny won the men’s team sprint, while American swimmer Michael Phelps secured his 16th gold medal by winning the men’s 200m individual medley.

**2024**: Prime Minister Sir Keir Starmer led the tributes to Andy Murray after his tennis career ended with defeat in the quarter-finals of the Olympic men’s doubles alongside Dan Evans.
