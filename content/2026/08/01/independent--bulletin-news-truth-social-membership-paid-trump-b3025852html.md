Truth Social, Donald Trump's social media platform, is launching a service offering Wall Street traders exclusive early access to his posts, sparking concerns over market manipulation.

The Truth API service, set to launch Saturday, aims to provide high-frequency traders with market-moving information from Trump's online pronouncements ahead of the general public at a reported $100,000 price tag.

Critics, including Irene Aldridge of Able Alpha Trading and Joe Saluzzi of Themis Trading, condemn the service as unethical, potentially disadvantaging retail investors and likening it to insider trading.

Donald Trump's posts frequently influence financial markets, with past examples including threats on tariffs and praise for companies like Palantir and Intel causing significant stock fluctuations.

The service could substantially increase revenue for Trump Media, which has reported significant losses, and has drawn criticism from political figures like Senator Elizabeth Warren, who has called for investigations.
