The pilot successfully ejected from the aircraft and was transported to a local medical facility in stable condition with non-life-threatening injuries.

The incident has been classified as a "Class A mishap," the most severe category, indicating significant damage or aircraft destruction.

A witness described seeing the jet slow down, the pilot eject, and the aircraft "blow up" upon impact near another plane.

F-35s are considered the safest and most capable fighter jets in the military's fleet, with fatal crashes being rare and pilots often ejecting successfully in previous incidents.
