TICKETS to a popular festive tradition go on sale next week.

The tickets will be available for A Dickens of a Christmas at Castle Howard, Father Christmas in the Castle, Santa’s Grotto in the Jellycat Shop, A Christmas Carol Theatrical Afternoon Tea, Christmas Afternoon Tea and Wreath Making.

Gold member tickets go on sale from 10am on Monday, August 3, followed by silver members on Thursday and bronze on Friday.

The following Monday, August 10, mailing list tickets go on sale followed by general tickets on Tuesday, August 11.

Meanwhile, Castle Howard's dedicated Jellycat shop in its Stable Courtyard, will remain open on varying days and times until the new year.

The shop will remain open seven days per week until Sunday, September 6, it will then revert to Thursdays to Sunday 11am-4pm from September,7 until Sunday, October 25

During the October half term holiday it will be open seven days a week and then Thursdays to Sundays, 11am to 4pm from November 2 to 10.

It will then open for the Christmas season from November 11 until January 3 for the full seven days.

For more information go to www.castlehoward.co.uk.
