Whitby swing bridge will be closed to traffic on both Saturday and Sunday, 1st and 2nd August, this weekend.

It will be closed between 10:30 am to 4pm to improve pedestrian safety during the busy school holidays.

See future closures at [https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/whitbyswingbridge](https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/whitbyswingbridge)
