A North Carolina woman who investigators believe was abducted from her home was found dead, and a registered sex offender has been charged with her kidnapping and murder.

Robin M. Doyle, 58, of Hickory, was reported missing after a family member last saw her on Monday. Authorities believe she was taken from her home the next day after her father, with whom she lived, woke up to find her missing, her car still in the driveway and the back door left open, according to WSOC.

Doyle’s body was found on Friday afternoon in a wooded area in northwestern Catawba County, the Catawba County Sheriff’s Office said. The agency said it was not releasing Doyle’s cause of death as of Friday afternoon, while the manner of death is suspected to be homicide.

Investigators identified Leroy Allen Loper, 54, a registered sex offender, as a person of interest in Doyle’s disappearance. Loper was arrested Wednesday in Surry County, about 60 miles from Doyle’s home, on unrelated sex offender registry violations. Doyle was not found at the location where Loper was taken into custody, officials said.

Following the discovery of Doyle’s body, Loper was charged with felony murder, first-degree kidnapping and concealment of death in connection with her abduction and death, the sheriff’s office announced on Friday night. Loper was previously employed with the same Propst Superette convenience store as Doyle, Lt. Dane Hoyle of the Catawba County Police Department, told*The Independent.*

Evidence collected during the investigation, including a bra found near her home and zip ties in her yard, suggests Doyle was taken against her will. Surveillance footage and witness reports also showed a red vehicle speeding away from Doyle’s neighborhood, leading authorities to Loper’s 2015 red Kia Optima. Similar zip ties were later found inside the vehicle, investigators said.

Loper has previous felony convictions for first-degree kidnapping and second-degree forcible sexual offense, according to the sheriff’s office. He remains in custody at the Catawba County Detention Facility without bond and is scheduled to appear in Catawba County District Court on Monday.

Catawba County Sheriff Don Brown expressed the agency’s condolences to Doyle’s family following the charges.

"We are deeply saddened for Robin Doyle's family as they continue to endure an unimaginable loss,” Brown said in a statement Friday night. “While today's charges mark a significant step in this investigation, they cannot undo the pain this family has suffered. The acts in this case represent pure evil, and those responsible will be held fully accountable under the law. This investigation remains active, and we will continue pursuing every fact until justice is fully served."

Investigators are continuing to look into Loper’s movements around the time of Doyle’s disappearance. Anyone who saw him or his car between July 27 and July 29 is asked to contact the sheriff's office. Loper may have traveled through both Catawba and Surry counties during that time, officials said.
