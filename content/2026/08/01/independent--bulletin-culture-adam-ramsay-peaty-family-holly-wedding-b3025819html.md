# Adam Ramsay-Peaty makes fresh admission about his estranged family

- Adam Ramsay-Peaty has stated he wishes to reconcile with his estranged mother, Caroline, but believes forgiveness takes time and requires him to feel they have "improved as people".
- The Olympic swimmer reportedly banned his mother from his wedding to Holly Ramsay, daughter of Gordon Ramsay, last year following a dispute over Holly's hen do.
- Ramsay-Peaty's parents were not in attendance at his December wedding at Bath Abbey, after which he changed his name to Adam Ramsay-Peaty, and he and Holly are now expecting their first child.
- His aunt, Louise Williams, publicly criticised Holly Ramsay for not inviting Caroline to the hen do, describing the move as "divisive and hurtful".
- Gordon Ramsay previously called the feud "upsetting", stating that he and his wife welcomed Peaty and respected Adam and Holly's wishes for his parents not to attend the wedding.
