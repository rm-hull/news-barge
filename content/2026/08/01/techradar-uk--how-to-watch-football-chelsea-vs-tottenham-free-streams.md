Today's **Chelsea vs Tottenham live streams** sees two London rivals clash outside of the English capital for the first time, as the Premier League duo meet at Sydney's Accor Stadium in their latest pre-season friendly.

The Blues beat Western Sydney Wanderers 6-4 at Accor Stadium in the opening fixture of their summer tour, with striker Joao Pedro scoring a nine-minute hat-trick as new manager Xabi Alonso made a winning start to his reign. Dastan Satpayev, Dario Essugo and Jamie Gittens also found the net as Alonso named a youthful starting XI, with many of Chelsea's stars still on holiday after representing their countries at the World Cup. The Spaniard will hope this clash with Tottenham is less chaotic than his first game in charge, as the Blues play their final match of the Australia leg of their tour before flying to Hong Kong to face Juventus on Wednesday.

Tottenham are a little further ahead in their pre-season program, having beaten MK Dons, Auckland FC and Sydney FC already this summer - the latter victory coming via a penalty shoot-out at Sydney's Allianz Stadium on Wednesday. Spurs are also without several first-team players on their tour Down Under, but new midfielders Sandro Tonali and Mateus Fernandes - who signed this summer for a combined fee that could rise to £185m - have both featured. Fernandes was left out of the squad against Sydney due to a minor calf issue and manager Roberto De Zerbi suggested he will be rested again here. After this, Spurs fly home to the UK before taking on Getafe in a behind-closed-doors friendly at their training ground next Saturday.
