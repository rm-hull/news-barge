# Prince William and Kate Middleton make rare outing with all three kids

- The Prince and Princess of Wales, accompanied by their children Princess Charlotte, Prince Louis, and Prince George, made a public appearance at the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow.
- The family attended a netball semi-final at the Hydro arena and subsequently met gold medal-winning para powerlifter Mark Swan and gymnast Reuben Ward.
- They also visited the Sir Chris Hoy Velodrome, where they met Sir Chris Hoy and watched some of the cycling action.
- Their visit took place on the penultimate day of the Games, which Glasgow agreed to host after the Australian state of Victoria withdrew due to escalating costs.
- The Princess of Wales is a patron of SportsAid, a charity supporting young athletes, and the family was set to meet athletes whose careers have been empowered by the organisation.
