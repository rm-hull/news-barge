The Met Office has forecast a mix of sunshine and cloud for Malton this weekend, with temperatures reaching up to 24C.

Saturday morning will start off partly cloudy, with temperatures dropping from 13C to around 10C before climbing again.

The sun is expected to break through by late morning, with a very low chance of rain.

Winds will be light, making for a fresh start to the day.

In the afternoon, temperatures will rise steadily to around 20C.

The sun will be out for most of the afternoon, although some cloud cover is expected in the mid to late afternoon.

There is a slight chance of a brief shower, but most places will remain dry.

Winds will pick up slightly, but conditions will remain comfortable.

UV levels are expected to be moderate to high, so sunscreen is recommended for those spending extended time outdoors.

The evening will be warm and pleasant, with temperatures holding around 20C before gradually cooling to 12C overnight.

Sunny intervals will return before the skies clear overnight.

Dry conditions are expected to continue, with winds easing, making for a calm night.

Sunday morning will start off cool but bright, with temperatures rising from 8C to 19C.

The sun is expected to shine throughout the morning, with virtually no chance of rain, and winds will be very light, creating calm conditions.

In the afternoon, temperatures will reach 24C, with the sun out for most of the day.

The chance of rain remains low, and winds will remain light, gradually increasing by early evening.

The evening will see temperatures gradually fall from 21C to around 14C overnight.

The skies will be mostly clear early in the evening before becoming partly cloudy later at night.

The weekend will round off with light winds, making for a calm and settled end to the weekend.
