![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Telegraph reads: "Healey: I will not allow supermarkets to rip you off."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/5f24/live/30eb95e0-8dec-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.jpg)

There's a mix of stories on Sunday's front pages. Writing in the Sunday Telegraph, Chancellor John Healey warns supermarkets he will not allow them to take the public "for a ride" because of the Middle East crisis pushing up cost of living. He vowed to crack down on "price-gouging", saying regulators have the the power to "clamp down on it if it happens". Elsewhere, a smiling Prince Louis is pictured wearing a Welsh cycling hat while watching the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Glasgow.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Mirror reads: "I'll help families in need."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/3fe7/live/46ed2160-8dec-11f1-b8ee-9b3c26ad07bb.jpg)

The Sunday Mirror follows with a pledge from the PM saying that helping families to ease the cost of living remains his top priority.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Express reads: "Fears families will be hit by £76,000 tax on death."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/e4a5/live/42395d50-8dec-11f1-b2ab-0dd01740f9f6.jpg)

But the Sunday Express says there are fears that families could be hit with a "tax on death" to pay for the PM's social care reforms. The paper says an analysis shows that families could be hit with a £76,000 bill should the levy go ahead, with Andy Burnham yet to directly rule it out.

![The headline on the front page of the Observer reads: "Own goal."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1024/cpsprodpb/5412/live/4bc7f140-8df3-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.png)

A photo of Fifa president Gianni Infantino dominates the Observer's front page as it reports on the fallout from his failed plan to sell stakes in the World Cup. "Own goal" is the headline.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Times reads: "Farage struck deal to return as leader long before election."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1024/cpsprodpb/0e27/live/3f847250-8df3-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.png)

The Sunday Times reports that Nigel Farage struck a secret deal to return as Reform UK leader months before the 2024 general election. The paper says Farage negotiated the pact in March and April of the year - undermining his claim that he had no intention of returning to politics until June 2024. The paper notes the date of Farage's return to politics has been raised amid questions over whether he needed to declare a £5m gift to Parliament. But it adds that Reform sources say the deal had been drafted for if, not when, Farage became leader.

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: "Spain calls EU 'selfish' as migrant crisis deepens."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1536/cpsprodpb/a8f3/live/fdd143d0-8df5-11f1-b2ab-0dd01740f9f6.jpg)

The Independent continues its coverage of the border crisis at the Spanish enclave of Ceuta. Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez has accused EU countries of being "selfish" in their response after more than 60,000 people crossed the border from Morocco on Thursday.

![The headline on the front page of the Mail on Sunday reads: "A migrant gasping for life. A Spanish enclave gripped by fear. If only the no-borders utopians were with me now."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/810/cpsprodpb/eba6/live/4e82a8a0-8dec-11f1-b2ab-0dd01740f9f6.jpg)

The Mail on Sunday follows with a photo of a Spanish solider checking on two migrants collapsed on the beach in Ceuta.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: "Henry killer's mum moved over attack fears."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/2e0d/live/9289f8f0-8dec-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.jpg)

The Daily Star shifts its focus to the mother of Henry Nowak's killer, who has been moved to a different wing in jail after fears of being attacked by other inmates. Kiran Kaur was jailed last month for three years for removing the murder weapon from the scene of the crime in Southampton.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday People reads: "Who else did my monster pal kill?"](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/75bc/live/566fecd0-8dec-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.jpg)

The Sunday People leads with a former friend of double murderer Allan Grimson, who fears he may have committed other crimes. Earlier this week, Grimson was found guilty of sexually assaulting four males and convicted of 13 offences committed in the 1990s.

![The headline on the front page of the Sun on Sunday reads: "TV Giant dumped me over OnlyFans."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/d8cb/live/5bf8db30-8dec-11f1-b2ab-0dd01740f9f6.jpg)

The Sun on Sunday features an interview with a model who says she was dumped by body builder Jamie Bigg, also known as Gladiator Giant, because of her OnlyFans career.
