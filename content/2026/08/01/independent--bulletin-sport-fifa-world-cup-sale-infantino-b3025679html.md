# Infantino abandons plan to sell World Cup profits after backlash

- Fifa has scrapped its controversial plan to sell stakes in the World Cup and other tournaments to private investors.
- The decision came after widespread backlash and threats of a boycott from major footballing bodies.
- Uefa, European football's governing body, confirmed it would boycott Fifa competitions if the plan was approved.
- The Asian Football Confederation and Concacaf, representing North and Central America, also backed Uefa’s stance.
- Fifa President Gianni Infantino stated the project created divisions and would not proceed, aiming to unite stakeholders in the future.
