Stockeld Park, outside Wetherby, attempted to secure a place in the record books by gathering more than 600 flat cap-wearing guests on Saturday.

The ambitious world record attempt marked Yorkshire Day at the estate and theme park, which also featured a packed programme of Yorkshire-themed entertainment.

Yorkshire Evening Post photographer Steve Riding visited Stockeld Park to capture its Yorkshire Day celebrations and world record attempt.
