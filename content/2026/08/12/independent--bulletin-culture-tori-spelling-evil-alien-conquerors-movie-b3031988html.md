### Tori Spelling names her worst filming experience caused by director who ‘hated’ her

![Tori Spelling names her worst filming experience caused by director who ‘hated’ her](<[https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/12/13/15/Tori-Spelling.jpeg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/12/13/15/Tori-Spelling.jpeg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp) 83w>)

# Tori Spelling says Evil Alien Conquerors was her worst movie experience

- Tori Spelling has described her role in the 2003 sci-fi film *Evil Alien Conquerors* as her worst on-set experience.
- Speaking on Stephen Baldwin's *One Bad Movie* podcast, the actor claimed director Chris Matheson constantly criticised her performance after every take.
- Spelling had hoped the minor uncredited role would be her big comedic break following her success on Beverly Hills, 90210.
- The actor alleged that the director singled her out in front of the entire cast and crew, leaving her feeling extremely distressed.
- To cope with the constant notes and perceived hostility, Spelling revealed she coped by turning the situation into an internal comedy routine. *The Independent* has contacted Matheson’s representative for comment.
