Liverpool are hoping to swiftly add to the loan signing of Ronald Araujo by hashing out a deal for Paris Saint-Germain winger Bradley Barcola after reaching a reported agreement over personal terms. Barcola leads Liverpool’s list of transfer targets and the Reds are in talks with PSG over a fee, though the European champions are demanding £145m to sell the winger.

Arsenal have offered Myles Lewis-Skelly to multiple Premier League clubs, including rivals Chelsea and Manchester United, but both clubs have reportedly turned down the chance to sign the teenager, who is believed to want to fight for his place despite increased competition. As reported by *The Independent* last week, there was a chance Arsenal would reconsider their position on Lewis-Skelly after completing the signing of Bruno Guimaraes.

Meanwhile, Ferran Torres appears set to swap Barcelona for PSG, while Rodri looks set to leave Manchester City amid interest from Barcelona and Real Madrid. The World Cup winning captain is into the final year of his contract and favours a move to Barca – who are considering a second bid after an initial offer of £38m was rejected. City seem to be making preparations for that outcome as they close in on the signing of World Cup breakout star Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille.

## Luis Enrique refuses to be drawn on Bradley Barcola's future

Paris Saint-Germain manager Luis Enrique has refused to comment on Bradley Barcola’s potential summer switch to Liverpool.

The 23-year-old, who scored three goals for France at the World Cup, is back with the PSG squad for Wednesday’s Super Cup showdown against Europa League winners Aston Villa in Salzburg.

And when quizzed about Barcola’s future, Luis Enrique said: “I don’t know and I don’t want to speak about any player individually.

“Because it’s always dangerous when you are asking something positive or negative about a player. I stay focused on my team.”

Barcola only returned to Paris last Monday, along with PSG’s other French and Spanish players who were at the World Cup, but could still feature against Villa.

## Liverpool play waiting game over Bradley Barcola

Latest update in the Bradley Barcola saga: GiveMeSport’s Ben Jacobs reports that if PSG sign one or more new attackers this window, Liverpool expect them to be willing to sell the Frenchman for under €170m (£145m).

Jacobs reports the Reds are yet to put in a formal bid as they wait out in hope of a better price, while Arsenal have yet to make formal contact over the striker despite their interest.

## Memphis Depay in spat with Corinthians over contract

Former Manchester United and Barcelona striker Memphis Depay has accused Corinthians of “unacceptable behaviour” after the club decided not to renew his contract.

The Netherlands international, who joined Corinthians on a two-year deal in 2024, said the Brazilian club had withdrawn a previously agreed contract extension.

He wrote on social media: “This renewal was explicitly agreed upon by the president as well as the sporting, legal and financial department. Some people decided however to breach this engagement.

“I didn't want this situation and always respected the club throughout the process, but now I am forced to react strongly in order to preserve my interests.

“In the upcoming days I will speak publicly but rest assured that I will not leave this unacceptable behaviour unsanctioned.”

A club statement said that “taking on a new commitment of this magnitude would not be compatible with the financial balance we need to preserve to ensure the institution's sustainability”, while admitting it was a “difficult decision”.

## Chelsea complete Chavarria signing

Speaking of Pep Chavarria... that signing has been confirmed this morning, with the left-back joining from Rayo Vallecano on a five-year deal.

He told the club: “I’m so excited to get started. This is a dream for me as Chelsea are one of the biggest clubs in the world. It is a big opportunity but I am prepared and will work hard to help the team achieve success.

“There are a lot of people who have helped me get to this moment and I have to thank my family. They have always supported me and today is only possible because of them.”

## Monaco bid rejected for Man Utd target

Pete O'Rourke reports that Monaco have rejected a €50m offer from Crystal Palace for Lamine Camara.

The Senegal international is also a target for Man Utd and Chelsea.

The 22-year-old, who joined Monaco in 2024, would add extra depth to the United midfield as the hierarchy look for reinforcements in that area after the departure of Casemiro.

## Chelsea medical for Pep Chavarria

Spanish left-back Pep Chavarria is set to join Chelsea, with the club finally reaching an agreement with La Liga side Rayo Vallecano.

An initial £16.2m deal plus £1.7m in add-ons has been agreed, with the 28-year-old set for his medical.

Chelsea were keen for a left-sided defender to replace Marc Cucurella, with Chavarria their ninth signing of the summer.

## Chelsea confirm Filip Jorgensen loan deal

Chelsea have confirmed the season-long loan of keeper Filip Jorgensen to sister club Strasbourg, in yet another move between the two sides.

The Dane’s opportunities have been limited in his two years at Stamford Bridge.

A club statement read: “Jorgensen was a key member of the Chelsea side that lifted the UEFA Conference League in May 2025, starting all but one game in the competition. The Denmark international became a Club World Cup winner later that summer.

“Jorgensen featured 12 times last season across four different competitions, playing his part as we reached the semi-finals of both domestic cups.

“We look forward to supporting Filip throughout his time in France.”

## Ferran Torres set to leave Barca for PSG

Spain star Ferran Torres is set to swap Barcelona for PSG, according to Fabrizio Romano.

He reports talks are in ‘final stages’ and that the winger is not scheduled to turn up for Barca training, with a deal expected to be finalised after PSG’s Super Cup meeting with Aston Villa tonight.

## Chelsea and Man Utd turn down chance to sign Myles Lewis-Skelly

*The Telegraph* has reported both Chelsea and Man Utd have turned down moves for Arsenal teenager Myles Lewis-Skelly, who faces increased competition both in defence and midfield. 

Lewis-Skelly is reportedly keen to fight for his place, while the Gunners value him in the region of £45m, which the Blues and Utd are not prepared to pay.
