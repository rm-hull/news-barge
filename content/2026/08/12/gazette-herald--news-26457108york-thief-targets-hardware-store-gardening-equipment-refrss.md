POLICE are appealing for information after gardening equipment was stolen in York from a hardware store.

North Yorkshire Police said the theft took place at 1.30pm on June 21 at the shop in Gillygate, close to the city centre.

Officers have released a CCTV image of a person they wish to speak to, saying they may have information which could help the force's investigation.

A force spokesperson said: "Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing [scott.nixon@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:scott.nixon@northyorkshire.police.uk), visiting our website and selecting 'Tell us about', or using live chat on the website."

The crime reference is 12260115113 and should be provided when contacting police.
