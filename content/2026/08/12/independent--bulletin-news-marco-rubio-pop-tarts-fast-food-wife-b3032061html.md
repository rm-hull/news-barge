# Marco Rubio reveals favorite junk food as ‘great American innovation’

- Secretary of State Marco Rubio defended his love for Pop-Tarts and fast food during an appearance on the *Katie Miller Pod* alongside his wife, Jeanette.
- The lighthearted interview occurred as reports surfaced that Rubio and other Cabinet members served as decoys on Air Force One while President Donald Trump returned from Turkey on another plane.
- Rubio refrained from discussing diplomatic efforts regarding the war with Iran or the Air Force One decoy incident during his appearance.
- During the podcast, Rubio lightly mocked Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s strict diet, describing his need to travel with sauerkraut as unsustainable.
- The podcast appearance reflects a broader trend of individuals close to Trump attempting to build influence within conservative media.
