# Great Britain lead medal table at European Athletics Championships 2026

- Great Britain lead the medal table at the European Athletics Championships 2026 after securing two golds and two silvers across the opening two days.
- Romell Glave took gold and Jeremiah Azu claimed silver for Great Britain in a men's 100m.
- Amy Hunt earned GB's first gold of the championships by winning the women's 100m in 11 seconds flat, with defending champion Dina Asher-Smith finishing fifth.
- Norway's Jakob Ingebrigtsen won the 5,000m, securing a record-equalling seventh European gold.
- Yemi Mary John led the British 4x400m mixed relay team to a silver medal, finishing behind gold-medallists Norway.
