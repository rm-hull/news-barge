# Julie Andrews breaks hearts by confirming she has turned down Princess Diaries 3

- Julie Andrews has confirmed she will not return for *The Princess Diaries 3* , stating that she believes she is retired.
- The 90-year-old actor revealed it was hard to say no after being asked throughout last year, but felt the project would flourish without her.
- Director Adele Lim is set to helm the third installment of the franchise, which returns to the fictional kingdom of Genovia.
- Anne Hathaway will reprise her role as Mia, alongside returning cast members Robert Schwartzman and Chris Pine.
- Production was delayed to allow Hathaway to film *The Devil Wears Prada 2* , but she confirmed the intention is to make the sequel next.
