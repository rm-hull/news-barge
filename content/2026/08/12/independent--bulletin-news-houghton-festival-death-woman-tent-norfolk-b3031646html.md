# Woman dies in tent at Houghton techno festival in Norfolk

![A general view of Houghton Festival](https://static.independent.co.uk/s3fs-public/thumbnails/image/2017/08/17/12/houghton-main-pavilion.jpg)

- Police are investigating the death of a woman who was found unconscious in a tent on the final day of Houghton Festival in Norfolk.
- An on-site medical team attempted to save the woman, but she was pronounced dead at the scene on Sunday.
- Norfolk Constabulary are treating the death as unexplained while awaiting post-mortem results, though officers do not believe there are any suspicious circumstances.
- Organisers of the 24-hour music festival expressed deep sadness over the tragedy and confirmed they are assisting police with their enquiries.
- The annual techno event takes place in the 1,000-acre grounds of Houghton Hall in north Norfolk, attracting around 10,000 attendees.
