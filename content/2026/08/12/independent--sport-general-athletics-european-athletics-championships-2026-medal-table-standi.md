Great Britain lead the medal table at the European Athletics Championships 2026 after a thrilling first two days of competition, with sprinters Romell Glave and Jeremiah Azu picking up gold and silver respectively in the men’s 100m to take the national tally to two golds and two silvers.

Their success followed world 200m silver medallist Amy Hunt winning GB’s first gold of the championships as she surged to victory in the 100m on Monday night in 11 seconds flat, having been seventh of the eight-athlete final after the first 10m due to a typical slower start. But she stormed back to overhaul Poland’s Ewa Swoboda and Belgium’s Delphine Nkansa, while defending champion Dina Asher-Smith finished fifth.

Italy and the Netherlands are close behind GB on four medals apiece while Norway took two golds on the opening night, with Jakob Ingebrigtsen claiming a brilliant 5,000m gold after nearly a year out of competition.

The Norwegian great was sidelined with an Achilles injury which required surgery in January and only returned to full training six weeks ago, but stunned his rivals in the final 100m - after appearing completely boxed in - to win a record-equalling seventh European gold.

The Norwegian 4x400m mixed relay team added a second gold later that evening, while Britain’s team were led home to silver by Yemi Mary John.
