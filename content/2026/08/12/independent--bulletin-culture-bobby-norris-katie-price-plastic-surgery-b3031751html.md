# Towie star says he’s ‘male Katie Price’ as he opens up on surgeries

- Former *The Only Way Is Essex* star Bobby Norris revealed on*Good Morning Britain* that he has undergone eight or nine plastic surgeries.
- The 38-year-old said he has been compared to Katie Price over his procedures, a comparison that he welcomes.
- Norris describing both himself and Price as "very open and honest".
- He said that he is neither embarrassed nor ashamed of his cosmetic work.
- Norris also said that it is important for those with a public profile to be “really transparent”.
