A VILLAGE pub has been named one of the best in the UK in a national listing of standout spots.

The Goodmanham Arms, located just outside Market Weighton in East Yorkshire, earned a spot in The Telegraph’s list of the 500 Best Pubs in England, which highlights venues for their charming character, welcoming staff, rich history and award-winning beer.

A spokesperson for the pub said: "To our family, the staff and the pub industry - this inspires us.

"We are incredibly grateful for the support and recognition - non of this would be possible without the amazing customers that come in and keep us going. Thank you to everyone who made this possible by supporting us."

Staff also thanked The Telegraph for featuring them in its 2026 Best Pubs in the UK guide.

In the review, it was described as an 'atmospheric East Riding local' with one reviewer saying the following: "One of The Goodmanham Arms’ regulars is standing at the bar, unusually well-attired. Where’s he off to, a series of employees and fellow customers wonder?

"The first day of Beverley Races, of course, but not before a few at this delightful pub in the heart of the East Riding. On a Wednesday lunchtime, this atmospheric, trinket-strewn pub is quickly filling up with couples eating lunch and regulars dropping in for a pint.

"The most sought-after seats appear to be those by the fire in the room to the left of the bar – but there are no bad spots in this delightful pub."

In a review from 2019, former York Press reporter Stephen Lewis added that upon opening the door on the quirky and rustic local, he 'fell in love' with the 'this absolutely brilliant, magical little pub'.

  The pub's letter from the Telegraph 

*(Image: Provided)*

The pub is among several from North and East Yorkshire to be included in this year’s expanded list, which now also features establishments from Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

In East Yorkshire, The White Horse Inn in Beverley, The Old Ship Inn in Bridlington, and The Old Star in Kilham also made the cut.

In the North riding, pubs like Birch Hall Inn in Beck Hole, Tan Hill Inn near Richmond and Hales Bar in Harrogate were recognised, along with York’s historic Blue Bell on Fossgate and The Swan in Bishopgate.
