# All the best gigs, events, and comedy from across the Harrogate district in August-September

## If you are planning to go out in the Harrogate district this weekend or in the next month, here are the shows, gigs and comedy not to miss:

**Until Sept 6:** Aesthetica Art Prize 20: Defining the Future of Contemporary Part II: Perception at Mercer Gallery, Harrogate.

**Until Sept 19:** Thank You, Sorry, and Congratulations exhibition by Thomas James Butler at RedHouse Gallery, 15 Cheltenham Mount, Harrogate.

**Until September:** From the archive: Paintings by Joash Woodrow at 108 Fine Art gallery, 16 Cold Bath Road, Harrogate.
