# London bus driver shot dead outside pub named by Met Police

![A crime scene was in place on Tuesday with officers in attendance (Jonathan Brady/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/11/17/48dffc330d4303fc68d6b1edfa3ec0a2Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg2NTUxMTI4-2.85538856.jpg)

- Auguste-Marie Bile-aka Amoa, a 25-year-old bus driver, has been named by the Metropolitan Police as the victim of a fatal shooting in south London.
- The attack occurred on Monday night outside The Landor pub in Willington Road, Clapham, where witnesses reported hearing multiple gunshots.
- A 22-year-old man who later attended hospital with a gunshot wound has been arrested on suspicion of murder and remains under police guard.
- Detective Chief Inspector Claire Guiver is leading the investigation and has urged anyone with information or footage of the incident to come forward.
- Friends paid tribute to Mr Bile-aka Amoa, describing the hard-working bus driver as a "cool guy" who was likely in the wrong place at the wrong time.
