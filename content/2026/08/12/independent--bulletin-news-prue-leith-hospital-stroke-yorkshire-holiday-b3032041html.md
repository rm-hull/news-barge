# Prue Leith hospitalised after falling unwell on holiday

![Dame Prue Leith was recently rushed to hospital (Andrew Matthews/ PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/12/00/5dcfaa9ae85c660a3f420eacf8ecf0a1Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg2MTg3OTAw-2.85204907.jpg)

- Dame Prue Leith was rushed to Harrogate Hospital by ambulance whilst on holiday in Yorkshire following concerns that she was suffering from a stroke.
- The 86-year-old broadcaster, known for her role on The Great British Bake Off, experienced a severe migraine with a “rare” symptom that left her unable to speak coherently.
- Her husband, John Playfair, contacted their doctor after her speech became an incomprehensible jumble, prompting the GP to call an ambulance despite Dame Prue attempting to explain it was a migraine.
- She spent 10 hours in hospital undergoing tests, including a CT scan, before doctors confirmed that she had not suffered a stroke.
- Writing in her column for *The Oldie* magazine, Dame Prue commended NHS staff for being professional and thorough, expressing her gratitude for their cautious approach.
