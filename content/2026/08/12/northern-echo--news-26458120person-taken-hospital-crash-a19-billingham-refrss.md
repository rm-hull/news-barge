One person has been taken to hospital after crash on A19 in the North East.

Emergency services were called to the A19/A689 exit slip road near Billingham shortly after 6pm on Monday, August 10.

A rapid response paramedic and an ambulance crew attended alongside police, and one patient was taken to North Tees Hospital for further treatment.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We were called to a road traffic collision on the A19 A689 exit slip road near Billingham shortly after 6pm on August 10.

"We dispatched a rapid response paramedic and an ambulance crew, who took one patient to North Tees Hospital."

Cleveland Police failed to provide a statement.
