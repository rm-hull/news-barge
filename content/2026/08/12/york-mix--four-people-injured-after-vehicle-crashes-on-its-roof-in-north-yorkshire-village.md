Four people were taken to hospital after a crash in North Yorkshire last night (Tuesday).

Emergency services were called to the incident in **Foxholes**, between Malton and Filey, at** 11.46pm**.

Firefighters from Sherburn and Scarborough responded to the incident.

#### Foxholes, North Yorkshire

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Four casualties involved in the incident were out on arrival of fire crews.

“Fire crews provided immediate emergency care alongside paramedics. Crews also assisted police with making the scene safe.

“The casualties were taken to hospital via road ambulance.”
