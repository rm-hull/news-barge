# Farm at center of cyclospora outbreak went years without FDA inspection

- A Mexican farm linked to a massive U.S. cyclosporiasis outbreak had not been inspected by the FDA in seven years, according to CBS.
- Contaminated lettuce from the Guanajuato facility has been linked to two deaths and thousands of parasitic infection cases.
- The FDA conducts roughly 1,000 foreign facility inspections annually, falling far short of the target of nearly 20,000 per year.
- Staffing cuts under the Trump administration contributed to a historic low in foreign food safety inspections.
- The FDA defended its oversight of imported food, stating that it relies on a comprehensive, risk-based system beyond on-site inspections.
