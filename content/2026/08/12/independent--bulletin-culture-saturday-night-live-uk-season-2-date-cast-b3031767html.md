# Saturday Night Live UK: Hit series will return for season 2 next month

- Sky has announced that Saturday Night Live UK will return for its second season on Saturday, 12 September.
- The upcoming run will consist of an initial six episodes, with further shows planned to follow in early 2027.
- All 11 inaugural cast members are set to return alongside a new lineup of celebrity hosts and musical guests.
- A ticket ballot for fans wishing to attend the live studio recordings opens at 10am on Friday, 14 August.
- Despite a dip in TV ratings after its premiere, the show achieved massive social media success, gathering over 86 million views across Instagram and TikTok.
