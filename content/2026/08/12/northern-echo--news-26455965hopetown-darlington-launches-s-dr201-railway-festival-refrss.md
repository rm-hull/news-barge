A month-long programme of steam experiences, exhibitions and heritage events is set to bring Darlington’s railway history back into the spotlight as Hopetown launches celebrations marking S&DR201.

Hopetown Darlington has announced a full schedule of events running from Saturday, September 5 to Sunday, September 27, marking 201 years since the opening of the Stockton & Darlington Railway.

The original railway carried hundreds of passengers between Shildon, Darlington, and Stockton on September 27, 1825, ushering in a new era of public rail transport.

*(Image: Hopetown Darlington)*

Councillor Libby McCollom, cabinet member for local services, said: "The success of S&DR200 demonstrated just how important Darlington's railway story remains, not only to our region but to the wider world.

"Through S&DR201, we're building on that incredible legacy and continuing to celebrate the innovation, ambition, and pioneering spirit that began right here more than 200 years ago.

"Hopetown Darlington is now a must-visit destination for anyone interested in railway heritage, offering unique experiences that bring our history to life for new generations.

"From iconic locomotives to family-friendly events, S&DR201 ensures the story of the railway that changed the world continues to inspire residents and visitors alike."

  Replica Locomotion 1 on the new heritage line 

*(Image: Hopetown Darlington)*

The month-long festival begins on Saturday, September 5 with the opening of Memory of a Journey: Final Station.

  This exhibition features thousands of wooden peg dolls decorated during last year's S&DR200 celebrations and displayed together in the Exhibition Hall.

  This unique installation brings together thousands of individual creations into one remarkable display, celebrating the communities, journeys and shared experiences that rail travel has brought to ordinary people.

Throughout September, visitors will be able to experience the sights, sounds and atmosphere of the steam age through a range of heritage activities, including steam train rides and special events.

The programme culminates in the S&DR201 Steam Festival on September 26 and 27, when the attraction, including Darlington Locomotive Works and the 1861 Shed, will be open for a spectacular weekend of steam-powered celebration.

The Darlington Locomotive Works and the historic 1861 Shed will be open to the public as part of the festival.

Highlights include journeys behind Replica Locomotion No.1, offering visitors a rare opportunity to experience a recreation of the locomotive that hauled the world's first public passenger railway journey in 1825.

On September 27, the anniversary of the Stockton & Darlington Railway’s opening, the 60163 Tornado will run on Hopetown’s Heritage Railway.

Built in Darlington and recognised as one of Britain's most iconic steam locomotives, Tornado represents the continuing legacy of railway engineering excellence that began with the Stockton & Darlington Railway more than 200 years ago.

Richard Starrs, Stockton & Darlington Railway project manager at Hopetown Darlington, said: "We're incredibly proud of the programme we've developed for S&DR201.

"Building on the momentum of the bicentenary celebrations, we've created a series of experiences that give visitors the chance to get closer than ever to the people, locomotives and stories that shaped railway history.

"From rides behind Replica Locomotion No.1 and Tornado to exhibition and steam-powered demonstrations, there's something for everyone to enjoy.
