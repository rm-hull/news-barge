Leeds United and Manchester United continue their preparations for the new Premier League season as old foes renew their rivalry at Croke Park in Dublin.

This will be another chapter in a storied history between the pair as they collide at the home of Irish sport just 10 days before the start of their league campaigns. Michael Carrick’s side impressed in a 1-1 draw against Paris Saint-Germain at the weekend as Youri Tielemans made his bow for the club, though injury worries over Mason Mount may underline the need for more midfield reinforcements.

Leeds have had a relatively eye-catching summer with James Trafford, Tarik Muharemovic and Harry Wilson all high-profile additions. Wins over Liverpool and RB Leipzig have shown encouraging signs and Daniel Farke will be keen to keep the momentum rolling ahead of their Premier League opener against Nottingham Forest next Saturday.

*Follow all of the latest from the pre-season friendly in Dublin with our live blog below:*

## Leeds team news

James Trafford is set to make his Leeds debut after missing their win over Leipzig, a match which saw Wilfred Gnonto star off the bench. The Italian could be rewarded with a start as a result.

**Predicted Leeds XI:** Trafford; Muharemovic, Rodon, Bijol; Bogle, Longstaff, Ampadu, Justin; Wilson, Calvert-Lewin, Gnonto.

## Man United team news

Marcus Rashford is back with the Man United squad as Michael Carrick looks to reintegrate the winger back into the fold.

Kobbie Mainoo may also play his first minutes of pre-season, while Benjamin Sesko is available after missing the entirety of pre-season so far with injury.

Senne Lammens and Matheus Cunha both missed the PSG game through illness and are also back in contention to feature, but Mason Mount will not feature after picking up a knock in that match.

**Predicted Man United XI:** Lammens; Dalot, Heaven, Maguire, Shaw; Santos, Tielemans, Fernandes; Amad, Mbeumo, Dorgu.

## Is Man United vs Leeds on TV?

Manchester United’s clash with Leeds kicks off at 7:30pm BST on Wednesday 12 August at Croke Park in Dublin, Ireland.

## How can I watch it?

The match will be shown free-to-air on ITV1, while also being available to subscribers on MUTV. Fans can stream the match on ITVX.
