# Trump says he made plane switch on Secret Service instructions

- President Donald Trump has confirmed he switched aircraft following a potential security threat detected by the Secret Service after leaving a NATO summit in Turkey.
- Trump said that he followed the instructions of military and security personnel to travel on a different plane.
- Reports indicated the threat may have originated from Iran.
- Trump said he did not enquire about the details because he receives a lot of threats.
- Trump was reportedly smuggled off Air Force One in a catering cart and transported onto a military aircraft upon departing Ankara on 8 July.
