# Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez quietly ends engagement to long-time partner

- New York Congresswoman Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez has quietly split from her fiancé Riley Roberts, according to reports.
- The couple reportedly ended their engagement around 18 months ago, with Ocasio-Cortez stopping wearing her engagement ring.
- The revelation follows the 36-year-old politician announcing on Instagram that she has decided to freeze her eggs.
- Ocasio-Cortez and Roberts first met at Boston University at 19 years old and confirmed their engagement in May 2022.
- The Democrat is currently documenting her egg-freezing journey and side effects on social media during the House recess.
