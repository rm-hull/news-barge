Police have arrested a woman after items were stolen from vehicles in three York streets.

The 26-year-old woman was detained yesterday (Tuesday) on suspicion of entering three vehicles in the Acomb area.

She has since been released on conditional bail while enquiries continue.

#### Kingsway West, York

The thefts all took place overnight on Sunday (9 August).

They occurred on Kingsway West, Oldman Court and Ascot Way. Several items, including bank cards, were taken.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are appealing for any residents to check their CCTV/doorbell cameras to see if anything has been captured or if anybody has any information that could assist our investigation to contact us.”
