A blaze on County Durham fields has been declared as a wildfire.

Emergency services were called to the rear of Scargill Drive in Tudhoe just before 4.30pm today (August 12) after reports of a field fire.

Five fire engines were sent to the scene of the incident, with pictures firefighters dousing the flames of a charred combine harvester in a field as smoke pours in the background.

County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service’s on-duty group manager John Singleton confirmed the incident has now been classed as a wildfire.

He said that one person had been treated by ambulance crews for smoke inhalation but that there was no need for any properties or people to be evacuated.

   

*(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

Mr Singleton could not confirm the cause just yet but said it is not believed to be malicious.

Crews are now dampening down the scene.

Mr Singleton said: “This was a fire to the rear of Scargill Drive in Tudhoe. The time of call was 4.18pm and we sent fire trucks to reports of a field fire.

   

*(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

“We [also] sent a black water carrier, an off road vehicle and an officer. We are classing it as a wildfire. Crews are now dampening down.”

Mr Singleton said that, with temperatures hitting 30 degrees across the region, it had been a busy day for the fire service.

He pointed people to the fire service website for tips and tricks on prevention of fires.
