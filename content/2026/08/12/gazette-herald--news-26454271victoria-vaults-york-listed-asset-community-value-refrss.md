A FORMER music venue in York with a national reputation has been given protected status in a bid to retain it for the community.

City of York Council bosses approved a nomination from the York branch of the Campaign for Real Ale (CAMRA) to list The Victoria Vaults in Nunnery Lane as an Asset of Community Value (ACV) in a meeting on Tuesday, August 11.

The move comes after the same status was removed from the venue at the end of last year.

ACV status ensures land and buildings which benefit local communities are “not disposed of” without the local community being given an opportunity to bid for them when the owner wishes to sell them.

  


The Victoria Vaults has hosted Bez from The Happy Mondays, Blondie drummer Clem Burke, goth discos, poetry readings and several other rock, pop and reggae acts.

A previous ACV application in October 2024 went before a tribunal and the case was withdrawn, and a second nomination of January 2026 was withdrawn by its applicant, papers to yesterday's meeting said.

Neil Love, representing CAMRA, told the meeting the test of an ACV nomination is whether it’s realistic to think there could be a qualifying community use within the next five years.

He said: “We contend that there is an overwhelming case for listing The Victoria Vaults as an ACV.

“We provided evidence of interest of credible parties and a determination amongst local residents to preserve a much-valued asset.

“It clearly passes the test that there has been current or recent non-ancillary use furthering social wellbeing or social interest which expressly includes cultural interests.”

Nabeel Haneef for the owners asked the meeting why the nomination needed to be relisted following the decision to delist in November 2025.

He also told the meeting that no leasehold offers had been made for the premises and added that no offers were received for the year before the current owners bought it and put it on the market.

A written representation received from the owners said “the freehold was openly marketed from late June 2023 until its sale in November 2024.

“The ground-floor leasehold has been marketed by Barry Crux & Co at £25,000 per annual since August 2025 and is available today.”

Chris Sherrington, co-founder of York Music Venue Network, established in 2019, told the meeting The Victoria Vaults was a dedicated grassroots music venue supporting local and touring artists with a national reputation and hosted over 125 cultural events each year.

He told the meeting he’d “personally spoken with five operators interested in returning The Victoria Vaults to a venue under viable terms” and others “have expressed serious interest in purchasing the building or supporting a community ownership route as well.”

In summarising the decision to approve the council’s recommendation to list The Victoria Vaults as an ACV, Katie Lomas, the council’s executive member for finance, performance and major projects said it had been a challenging and drawn-out process for everyone involved.

She told the meeting: “It’s important to acknowledge that there was a previous application which I approved on the basis of the information available at the time, which was later challenged, then the listing was removed, and that was the conclusion of the process at that point in time.

“This however is a fresh application, and I am looking at it as a fresh application, with all of the background information, all of the written representations, all of the public participants’ speeches in mind.

“I note that there has been further interest shown in operating the venue and that suggests that there is a future realistic prospect of use.

“I’m here to consider whether community use in the next five years is realistic, and I think with five operators interested and two people interested enough to put in writing their interest for consideration today I am persuaded that there is reasonable prospect of community use within the next five years.

“In saying that, I also appreciate that the current owners bought the property without the intention to operate it as a venue, but while the original application for listing had already been submitted.

“I absolutely accept it will be difficult for the current owners to hear this.”
