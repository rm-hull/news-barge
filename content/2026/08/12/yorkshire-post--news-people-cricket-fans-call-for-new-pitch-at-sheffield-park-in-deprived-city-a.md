# Cricket fans call for new pitch at Sheffield park in deprived city area

## Sports fans have asked for better cricket facilities at a Sheffield park in a deprived city area set to benefit from £20m in improvements.

A petition, signed so far by 189 people, calls for Sheffield City Council to install a “public-access, non-turf (mat/synthetic) cricket pitch in Parson Cross Park to provide accessible sports facilities for the local community”.

The petition states: “Parson Cross Park is a fantastic green space but it currently lacks dedicated facilities for cricket.

“There is a growing, passionate interest in cricket across Sheffield, yet local teams, families and young people in the area have to travel across the city to find proper playing surfaces.”

![Parson Cross Park](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjA1YjAwOTg0LTE4NDYtNGRhYS1iMWNiLTY2MDk4N2VmNDE3NTplOTdkM2E0Zi00MDVhLTQ0OWMtYTgxYy1mNjNiNGE5NmJmOTc=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The petition argues that installing a durable, low-maintenance mat cricket pitch would provide free, accessible sports facilities for all age groups and support grassroots sports.

It would also enhance community cohesion by bringing together diverse groups with a shared love of the sport.

The government has awarded £20m Pride in Place funding to Parson Cross and Foxhill to support community projects until 2036.

A local neighbourhood board will decide how the money is spent. A council survey running until the end of August will give the board a picture of what matters most to local people.

To find it online, go to [https://haveyoursay.sheffield.gov.uk/pride-in-place-parson-cross-and-foxhill](https://haveyoursay.sheffield.gov.uk/pride-in-place-parson-cross-and-foxhill)

The park has already benefited from a refurbished pavilion, new football and rugby pitch perimeter fencing, a illuminated artificial grass Cruyff Court for multi-sport use and extensive tree planting.

The cricket petition, started by Aneesh John Varkey, runs until August 16.
