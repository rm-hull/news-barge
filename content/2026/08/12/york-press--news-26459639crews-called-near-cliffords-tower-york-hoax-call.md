A “hoax call” resulted in two fire crews being called out to trees near a major York attraction.

The crews, from York and Acomb, were called to Tower Street near Clifford’s Tower at about 12.30pm on Wednesday (August 12).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said it followed a report of a “tree on fire with youths in the tree after climbing it”.

But a service spokesperson said: “After a thorough search of the area no fire and no persons in a tree were found. This was believed to be a hoax call.”
