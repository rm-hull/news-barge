# What time is the solar eclipse in New York? When to catch a glimpse from East Coast

- While the path of the total solar eclipse is confined to the Arctic and Europe, a partial eclipse will be visible in North America.
- Parts of the US and Canada will get to witness the celestial experience as a sliver of Moon passes in front of the Sun.
- The eclipse is expected to peak on the US East Coast at around 1:54 p.m. ET, according to the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, and end at around 2:30 p.m.
- **New York City** will begin to see the partial eclipse at 1:07 p.m. and it will peak at 1:54 p.m. before ending at 2:38 p.m., according to a list by Nasa.
- Peak time in other major cities: **Anchorage, Alaska** at 8:21 a.m. local time;**Bangor, Maine** at 1:53 p.m.;**Boston** at 1:55 p.m.;**Detroit** at 1:36 p.m.;**Fairbanks, Alaska** at 8:27 a.m.;**Juneau, Alaska** at 8:24 a.m.;**Philadelphia** at 1:53 p.m.;**Portland, Maine** at 1:53 p.m.; and**Washington, D.C.** at 1:53 p.m.
