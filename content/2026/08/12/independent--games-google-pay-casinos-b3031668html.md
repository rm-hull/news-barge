## **What is a Google Pay casino?**

A Google Pay casino is simply a UK-licensed online casino that lets you deposit (and often withdraw) using the Google Pay digital wallet on your Android phone, tablet or Chrome browser.

When using casinos that accept Google Pay, you simply link a debit card or bank account to Google Pay once, then pay in a couple of taps without re-entering card details each time.

Of course, when using Google Pay, players still get the same games, casino bonuses and UKGC protections as any other deposit method.
