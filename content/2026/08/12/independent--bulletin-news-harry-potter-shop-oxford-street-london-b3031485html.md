# UK’s biggest Harry Potter shop to open in London this autumn

- London is set to welcome the UK's largest Harry Potter shop on Oxford Street, opening on 4 November.
- The flagship store spans over 21,000 square feet across two floors within The Ribbon development.
- Inspired by Diagon Alley, the venue is the second official Harry Potter retail location in the UK after King's Cross Station.
- Immersive features include a full-sized Knight Bus, a Ukrainian Ironbelly Dragon and a Leaky Cauldron pub serving Butterbeer.
- Warner Bros has released concept artwork giving fans a first preview of the destination ahead of its autumn opening.
