A VEHICLE has ended up on its roof after a crash in a North Yorkshire village.

The county's fire and rescue service said crews from Scarborough and Sherburn were called to Foxholes around 11.45pm yesterday (Tuesday, August 11).

  


A service spokesperson said four people injured in the crash were taken to hospital.

The spokesperson added: "The casualties were out of the vehicle at the arrival of fire crews.

"Firefighters provided emergency care alongside paramedics.

"Crews also assisted police with making the scene safe."

Foxholes is on the route of the B1249, an important regional road linking the Yorkshire Wolds with the A64 corridor.
