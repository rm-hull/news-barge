# Rihanna shows her children Barbados street named after her in rare video

- Rihanna has shared a rare video and photographs of her children visiting her childhood home in Barbados.
- The 38-year-old singer revisited Westbury New Road, the street where she grew up.
- The street has since been renamed Rihanna Drive in honour of the star.
- She reflected on the visit on social media, writing that it was “trippy” how life works.
- The businesswoman rarely posts pictures or makes public appearances with her children, whom she shares with rapper A$AP Rocky.
