# Stunning solar eclipse photos as millions of people are treated to rare celestial display

![The moon completely covers the sun during a total solar eclipse as seen from Arija, Spain](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/12/19/2026-08-12T182906Z_965323106_RC26XMA1MD7A_RTRMADP_3_SOLAR-ECLIPSE-SPAIN.JPG)

- Photos show the stunning solar eclipse that plunged some parts of the globe into darkness on Wednesday.
- Millions of people across Europe gathered under clear skies to witness the continent's first total solar eclipse in 27 years, with optimal viewing locations situated across Spain, Iceland, and Greenland.
- The period of totality, during which the moon completely obscures the sun's light, was projected to last under 2.5 minutes, reaching its maximum duration off the western coast of Iceland before shrinking as it moved over Portugal and Spain.
- The celestial alignment creates a striking ring of fire around the lunar surface, causing daylight to dim dramatically and triggering unusual environmental effects, such as confusing birds into singing. This event marks the beginning of the so-called Iberian Eclipse Trio for the region, which last saw a total solar eclipse in 1912 and is set to experience another total eclipse in August 2027 followed by an annular eclipse in January 2028.
- Scientists and U.S. space agency NASA utilised the rare phenomenon for atmospheric research, deploying high-altitude research balloons and flying a WB-57 aircraft from Iceland to track the moon's shadow.
