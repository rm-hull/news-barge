# Is there a solar eclipse next year? Here’s what to know and how to see it

- Travellers gathered in Spain, Iceland, Greenland and Portugal to view a total solar eclipse, with partial views visible across the UK and parts of North America.
- A rare total solar eclipse, dubbed the eclipse of the century, is set to occur on 2 August 2027.
- The path of totality in 2027 will pass through southern Spain, Gibraltar, North Africa, the Middle East and parts of East Africa.
- Luxor in Egypt is expected to see the longest duration of totality, lasting approximately six minutes and 22 seconds.
- Space agencies have advised spectators to follow safety guidelines and wear specialised protective glasses during the event.
