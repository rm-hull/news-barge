# Trump’s Iran approval plunges 24 points among voters

- CNN data analyst Harry Enten revealed voter approval for President Donald Trump's handling of Iran dropped 24 percentage points over two years, falling from 53 percent to 29 percent.
- Democrats have overtaken Republicans on voter trust regarding wars and terrorism, swinging from an 18-point Republican lead in January 2025 to a two-point Democratic advantage.
- Polls show that only 27 percent of all voters and 41 percent of Republicans believe the United States will ultimately achieve a favorable agreement with Iran.
- White House spokesperson David Ingle responded to the polling, stating Trump bases national security decisions on American safety rather than fluid opinion polls.
- The ongoing war against Iran, launched over five months ago without congressional approval, has resulted in 18 U.S. military deaths and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz.
