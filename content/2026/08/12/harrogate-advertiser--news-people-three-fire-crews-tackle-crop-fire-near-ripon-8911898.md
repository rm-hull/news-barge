# Three fire crews tackle crop fire near Ripon

## Firefighters from three stations joined together to tackle a crop fire near Ripon on Tuesday afternoon.

The call for assistance was received at 2.39pm.

![Three fire crews were called to a 300sqm crop fire near Ripon on Tuesday](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjgwZmM2M2ViLTliYWUtNDNlNS04ZmI4LTAyMzllNDE3NDMyNTphNjAwMzNhYi05ODQ2LTQ4NTItYTY5ZS1mZTJiYzkwMzdkYzE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Crews from Ripon, Harrogate and Northallerton responded to a report of a field of standing crops on fire at Givendale.

On arrival the crew found a fire covering approximately 300m x 300m.

Crews fought the fire while farm machinery was used to create fire breaks.
