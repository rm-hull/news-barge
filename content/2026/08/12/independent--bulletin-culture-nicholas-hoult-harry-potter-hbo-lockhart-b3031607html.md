# Nicholas Hoult to join cast of Harry Potter HBO show in major role

- Actor Nicholas Hoult is set to play Gilderoy Lockhart in the second season of HBO's upcoming Harry Potter television adaptation.
- The role of Lockhart was previously portrayed by Kenneth Branagh in the 2002 film adaptation of *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets* .
- The first season of the decade-long project is scheduled to premiere on 25 December 2026 as a faithful adaptation of J.K. Rowling's books.
- Hoult joins a wide cast featuring John Lithgow as Dumbledore, Paapa Essiedu as Snape, and newcomers Dominic McLaughlin, Arabella Stanton, and Alastair Stout as the lead trio.
- The television series will feature several differences from the original films, including character ages that align more closely with the books.
