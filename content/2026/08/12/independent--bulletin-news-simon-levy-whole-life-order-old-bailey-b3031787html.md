# Simon Levy jailed for life for murdering two women and raping a third

- Serial sex attacker Simon Levy, 40, has been jailed for life at the Old Bailey for the murders of Carmenza Valencia-Trujillo and Sheryl Wilkins, as well as the rape of a third woman.
- Levy’s prolific sexual offending spanned the period between 2018 and 2025.
- The Metropolitan Police, British Transport Police and Crown Prosecution Service admitted to failings that left Levy on bail despite his escalating attacks.
- Systemic failings included delayed identification parades, errors in opposing bail by prosecutors and a failure to issue arrest warrants when Levy missed court appearances.
- The Independent Office for Police Conduct has served misconduct notices on two Metropolitan Police officers, while Scotland Yard urged any other survivors to come forward.
