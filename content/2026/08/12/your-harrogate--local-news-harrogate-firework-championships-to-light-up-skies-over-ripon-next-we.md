The skies above Newby Hall will be filled with spectacular displays when its annual Fireworks Championships return later this month.

The event will take place on Saturday 22nd August, bringing together three of the UK's leading fireworks companies to compete for the title.

Each company will deliver a 10-minute display choreographed to music, with visitors able to vote for their favourite via SMS.

The competition will then conclude with a huge finale display to round off the evening.

Stuart Gill, Commercial Director at Newby Hall, said:

“Our annual fireworks championships feature stunning displays by some of the UK’s leading fireworks companies. It’s a popular evening of entertainment for all the family set in the beautiful parkland at Newby.”


Gates will open at 4pm, allowing visitors to enjoy a picnic in the grounds before the evening's entertainment begins.

A drone show will take place at 8.45pm, followed by the first fireworks display at 9pm.

Camping will also be available from 1pm for visitors looking to make a weekend of the event.

Advance tickets are priced at £30 for adults and £20 for children, with under-fives admitted free. Camping costs £16 per adult, with under-16s able to camp for free.
