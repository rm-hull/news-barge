# Who is Josh Kushner? The 41-year-old billionaire buying the Los Angeles Lakers

- American billionaire Josh Kushner and former Disney executive Bob Iger are acquiring a controlling stake in the Los Angeles Lakers for a record-breaking $12.5 billion.
- Kushner, 41, is the founder of Thrive Capital, co-founder of Oscar Health, and a former minority owner of the Memphis Grizzlies. He’s married to supermodel Karlie Kloss, with whom he shares three kids.
- He is the younger brother of Jared Kushner, who is married to President Trump’s daughter, Ivanka Trump.
- The Lakers' acquisition coincides with controversy surrounding Kushner's role in a collapsed $4.2 billion plan to buy a stake in FIFA competitions.
- UEFA sent a letter to Kushner warning of potential legal action and demanding the preservation of all documents related to the FIFA deal.
