Two vehicles have crashed on a main road in North Yorkshire.

Firefighters were called to the crash on the A169 in Pickering shortly after 9.55am on Wednesday (August 12).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said its crew “administered immediate emergency care”.

The crew also isolated the vehicles, one of which was electric, and made the scene safe, the fire service said.
