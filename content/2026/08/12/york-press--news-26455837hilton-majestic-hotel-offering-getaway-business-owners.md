A Harrogate hotel is offering business owners a chance to win a much-needed break.

The ‘Holiday from your Business’ package, launched by DoubleTree by Hilton Majestic Hotel & Spa, aims to help senior leaders step away from daily pressures.

It includes a two-night stay in a Deluxe bedroom, tapas in Fredricks Bar & Lounge, one hour of spa access each day, access to meeting rooms, and an executive sleep recovery kit.

New research from Cairn Hotel Group, which operates the Majestic, found that 56 per cent of UK senior decision makers feel constantly "on" and have little time to step back from the day-to-day to think strategically.

Clare Hughes, group director of sales at Cairn Hotel Group, said: "The first half of 2026 has been really turbulent for businesses, and we know that when things get challenging, time to pause, think and focus on wellbeing can quickly fall down the priority list.

"Our research shows that many senior decision makers feel constantly 'on', but it also shows that leaders understand the value of stepping away from the day-to-day.

"Over a quarter told us that taking time away from daily operations significantly improves their decision-making and business performance.

"That is exactly why we have launched Holiday from your Business.

"We want to give senior leaders the chance to take a much-needed break from the everyday pressures of running a business, while creating space to reset and think clearly."

Members of the public can also nominate a business owner.

Nominations can be submitted via DoubleTree by Hilton Majestic Hotel & Spa, along with a short explanation of why the nominee is deserving.

In addition to the individual package, the Harrogate hotel has launched a new team meeting and events package, offering businesses a Mediterranean-style lunch, outdoor breakout spaces, wellbeing options, and discounted spa treatments.

More details are available at [https://experiencethemajestic.com/business-break-competition/](https://experiencethemajestic.com/business-break-competition/)
