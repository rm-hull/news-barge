One person had to be cut from the wreckage of their vehicle after a crash near York this afternoon (Wednesday).

Emergency services were called to the incident on **Sutton Road, Wigginton** at**3.27pm**.

Firefighters from York and Huntington went to the two-vehicle collision.

#### Crash location

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews extricated the occupant of the vehicle using e-draulic cutting equipment and left them in the hands of the ambulance.

“Crews then monitored one of the vehicles which was electric for a suitable length of time.”
