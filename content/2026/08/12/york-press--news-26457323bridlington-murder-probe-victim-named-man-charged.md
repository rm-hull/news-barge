A MAN who died following an alleged "stabbing" in Bridlington has been named by his family as a suspect in his 20s has been charged in connection with his death.

Humberside Police said 25-year-old Ibrahim Yesildeniz died in hospital after receiving treatment for life-threatening injuries.

Police stated on Wednesday morning that Mr Yesildeniz "died following an altercation".

A force spokesperson said: "Ibrahim's family are being supported by specially trained officers and have asked for privacy."

Humberside Police said it was called to First Avenue in Bridlington at around 5.20am on Monday, August 10, after reports of a suspected "stabbing".

The force spokesperson said Mehmet Gundez, 24, from Bridlington, who identifies himself as Turkish, has been charged with murder in connection with Ibrahim's death, and has been remanded into custody to appear at Hull Magistrates' Court this morning (August 12).
