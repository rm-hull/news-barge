# Dick Cheney’s doctor says Americans have been ‘kept in the dark’ about Trump’s health

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/5QWXLmlC/poster.jpg?width=720)

- Dr. Jonathan Reiner, the long-time cardiologist for former Vice President Dick Cheney, published an essay in *The New York Times* questioning President Donald Trump's physical health and fitness for office.
- Reiner raised medical questions regarding Trump's hand discoloration, acute leg swelling and instances where the president appeared to fall asleep during public events.
- The White House dismissed the concerns, with spokesman Davis Ingle calling the speculation political and asserting that Trump remains sharp and energetic.
- Trump's physician, Dr. Sean Barbabella, maintained that the president is fully fit to serve, attributing symptoms to routine conditions such as chronic venous insufficiency and daily aspirin use.
- A *Washington Post* /ABC News/Ipsos poll found that 55 percent of U.S. adults believe Trump is not in good enough physical health to serve effectively as president.
