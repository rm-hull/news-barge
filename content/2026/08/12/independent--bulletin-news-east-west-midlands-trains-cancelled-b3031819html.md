# Midlands train services cut amid extreme heat

![East Midlands Railway has advised passengers to only travel if essential on Thursday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/20/8/46/IMG_3494.jpeg)

- East Midlands Railway and West Midlands Railway have reduced or suspended several rail services due to soaring temperatures on Wednesday and Thursday.
- The Met Office has issued an amber weather warning for extreme heat across parts of England, with temperatures predicted to peak at up to 38C on Thursday.
- East Midlands Railway has advised passengers to only travel if essential on Thursday and strongly recommended making necessary journeys before midday when temperatures are cooler.
- Specific route changes include replacing trains with buses on parts of West Midlands Railway and cancelling several East Midlands Railway services around Nottingham from midday.
- Passengers who experience delays of 15 minutes or more can claim compensation under the Delay Repay scheme.
