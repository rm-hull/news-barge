A man who died following an altercation outside an address on First Avenue in Bridlington earlier this week has today been named by his family as 25-year-old Ibrahim Yesildeniz

Humberside Police were called to the address at around 5.20am on Monday (10 August), following reports of a stabbing, where Ibrahim was taken for treatment to life threatening injuries, and later died in hospital.

His family are being supported by officers, and they have asked for privacy at this incredibly difficult time.

Mehmet Gundez, 24-years-old, of Bridlington, who identifies himself as Turkish, has been charged with murder in connection with Ibrahim’s death, and has been remanded into custody.
