The world-famous Dreamboys are set to bring their latest high-energy stage show to a North East venue.

The all-new show will take place at Billingham’s Forum Theatre on September 10, and promises an evening of "heart-pounding choreography", feel-good anthems, and "cheeky entertainment".

Founded in 1987, Dreamboys are hailed as the most famous and successful male strip show in the UK history.

A spokesman for Dreamboys said: "From the moment the music starts to the final encore, the night promises a rollercoaster of laughter, awe, and excitement."

The show is designed to be inclusive and welcomes audiences from all walks of life aged 18–100.

Whether it’s a hen night, birthday celebration, date night or just a fun evening out, Dreamboys promises "from the moment the music starts to the final encore, the night promises a rollercoaster of laughter, awe, and excitement".

Dreamboys also celebrates the diversity of its audience.

The spokesman said: "The shows welcome everyone from all communities, whether a girls night out, gay stag do, hen night, birthday, date night or just because."

With more than three decades of experience touring the UK, Dreamboys continues to set the standard for live male entertainment.
