# McDonald’s teams up with Red Bull for its first-ever energy drink - and fans can customize it

![McDonald's is offering a brand new energy drink in partnership with Red Bull](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/11/20/49/McDonalds-is-offering-a-brand-new-energy-drink-in-partnership-with-Red-Bull.jpeg)

- McDonald's has partnered with Red Bull for its first-ever energy drink, the Red Bull Dragonberry Energizer, which will hit menus in the US starting August 17.
- The new drink blends Red Bull or Red Bull Zero with blue raspberry syrup and freeze-dried dragonfruit inclusions.
- McDonald's is also introducing a Vanilla Swirl option that adds vanilla flavor and a cold foam cap to select sodas.
- The expanded menu features additional reduced-sugar options, including Dr Pepper Zero Sugar and Sprite Zero Sugar.
- The beverage lineup expansion comes as the fast-food giant seeks to attract younger customers and boost sales alongside value menu efforts.
