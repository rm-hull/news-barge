# ICE agent points gun at US citizen accused of trying to run them over

- An Immigration and Customs Enforcement agent drew a firearm on driver Carolina Molina during an encounter in Falls Church, Virginia, after she shouted profanities at federal officers.
- Video footage shows law enforcement vehicles blocking Molina's car before a masked agent confronted her at gunpoint, accusing her of following the officers and attempting to run them over.
- Molina, a licensed counselor and U.S. citizen, denied attempting to hit the agents and asserted that her First Amendment rights protected her verbal comments.
- Molina explained she turned her vehicle around to prevent ICE agents from following her toward an immigration attorney's office where clients might be present.
- The Department of Homeland Security accused Molina of weaponizing her vehicle to aid fleeing undocumented immigrants and stated she could face criminal prosecution.
