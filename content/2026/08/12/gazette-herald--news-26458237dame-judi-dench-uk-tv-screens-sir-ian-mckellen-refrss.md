A HEART-WARMING TV special returns to screens this month (August 2026) with an iconic meeting between two of Britain's most loved actors - one of whom was born in York.

Tea with Judi Dench sees Dame Judi Dench welcome her great friend Sir Ian McKellen to her home for an hour-long conversation.

The pair will discuss their lives on and off stage and reflect on long careers that have led them from Shakespeare to the worldwide fame of the Lord of the Rings and James Bond movies.

Dame Judi Dench said last month: "I am absolutely delighted to be returning for a second film, and even more pleased to be sharing the experience with the wonderful Sir Ian McKellen.

"The response to the first film was deeply touching, and it's a great privilege to continue these conversations and capture moments of great friendship."

The premiere episode, which went on air last year, featured Sir Kenneth Branagh.

Dame Judi and Sir Ian are said to explore topics including fame, failure, and the importance of never taking either too seriously.

Phil Edgar-Jones OBE, executive director of unscripted originals at Sky, said: "Tea with Dame Judi is fast becoming one of our most treasured and delightful treats on Sky Arts.

"If you join Judi Dench as she reflects on life, art, culture, and all manner of topics with the peerless Sir Ian McKellen, you’re sure to be amused, moved and enlightened in equal measure."

Nancy Strang, co-founder and creative director of producers Mindhouse, said: "I’m completely thrilled that Sky have commissioned us to make another Tea with Judi Dench.

"Judi has a unique combination of warmth, intelligence and mischievousness which appeals to audiences across generations.

"To be invited in to spend a day with her and the incomparable Ian McKellen at her home, sharing their memories of their lives, careers and lost loves is a complete privilege.

"I’m sure we’ll make another magical slice of television together."

Tea with Judi Dench airs in August on Sky Arts, Freeview and streaming service NOW.
