# Ann Widdecombe’s funeral taking place today at Catholic abbey where she worshipped

- A private requiem mass is taking place at Buckfast Abbey in Devon for former Tory minister Ann Widdecombe.
- Miss Widdecombe died aged 78 on 8 July following a hammer attack at her home on Dartmoor.
- Joshua Kerry, 28, from South Yorkshire, was charged with her murder on 20 July.
- Buckfast Abbey described the former MP as a dedicated public servant of profound faith, while her family asked for the initial service to be kept private.
- A wider public memorial service for the politician and television personality will be held later this year.
