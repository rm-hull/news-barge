# Jenna Ortega says she didn’t look after herself as a child star

- Actor Jenna Ortega has spoken about the extreme lengths she went to as a child star, including going all day without food or water on set to avoid being in anyone's way.
- In an interview with *Esquire*, the 23-year-old* Wednesday* star admitted that failing to look after herself as a young actor was perhaps her biggest mistake.
- Ortega revealed her acting career was sparked at seven years old when her parents initially laughed at her ambitions, driving her stubborn determination to succeed.
- She also addressed negative stereotypes about child stars, acknowledging that "a lot of them are true".
- Ortega is set to star in Taika Waititi’s upcoming film adaptation of *Klara and the Sun* and will reprise her role in the third season of Netflix's*Wednesday* .
