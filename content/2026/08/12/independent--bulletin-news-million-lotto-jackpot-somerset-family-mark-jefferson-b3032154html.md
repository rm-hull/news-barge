# Somerset family almost lost £1m winnings after ignoring National Lottery’s calls

![Mark Jefferson, who played with his wife Naomi and four children, said it was only by chance that he reluctantly answered a call from the National Lottery after nine days turning down their attempts to reach him](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/12/15/26/Ilminster-Syndicate-Lotto-5-B-6.jpg)

- A Somerset family have claimed a £1 million Lotto jackpot after ignoring calls and emails from the National Lottery for nine days, believing they were spam.
- Builder Mark Jefferson, from Ilminster, set up the syndicate with his wife Naomi and their four adult children less than a year ago to celebrate his birthday.
- The family matched five main numbers and the Bonus Ball in the draw on 24 June, using winning numbers chosen by daughter Alexandra.
- Mr Jefferson admitted he was in tears when he finally answered the phone and discovered his family had won the life-changing prize.
- The jackpot will be shared among the family, who plan to buy a convertible, purchase a caravan on the south coast, and go on a holiday abroad.
