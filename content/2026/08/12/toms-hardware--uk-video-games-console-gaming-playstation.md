No console required, but you do need a PlayStation Plus Premium subscription.

![GTA V physical disc in PlayStation 5 console](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ehooWGrgPpZW2GDyPXyGp3-320-80.jpg)

Sony confirms that its stance has not changed on ending the production of physical game discs for titles released after January 2028.

![PlayStation 5 Pro](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/tg95hzQEgMNoJTJZHb5oXU-320-80.png)

The PS5 emulation scene is showing extremely impressive progress, with 3D titles now being playable at around 30 FPS just two weeks after the first 3D titles even became bootable.

![a silver Sony PlayStation 3 being photographed](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/fhjBsHVJ7Tw3Tio7tgKUsP-320-80.png)

This could help ensure that PS3 games remain playable even if all PS3 consoles no longer exist.

![PlayStation 5](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/jbshKzu9MSCWjQajF582PT-320-80.jpg)

Milestone after milestone.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/XjcgsYTLxWGb9B88CKrTVc-320-80.jpg)

A Change.org petition urging Sony to keep making physical PlayStation games has passed 172,000 signatures.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/cVv7pajuCXkp7bEa7u9RiU-320-80.jpg)

Meanwhile, the largest petition against the disc cutoff sat beyond 74,000 signatures on the morning of July 4th, closing in on its 75,000 goal.

![RPCS3](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/UgivybYyXt5eaaDwdthgT5-320-80.png)

Sony is shutting down PSN for PS3 consoles next year, so preservationists are being asked to wake up from their slumber to archive everything before it's too late.

![A photograph of PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5 games in their cases.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/XjcgsYTLxWGb9B88CKrTVc-320-80.jpg)

While Nintendo remains a holdout, this announcement essentially sounds the death knell for physical media in cutting-edge gaming.

![PlayStation leasing program in the UK](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/MjcZuEfPQrcKamX4D4pHuc-320-80.png)

The movies will be removed by September 1, 2026.
