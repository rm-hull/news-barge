Just eight homes remain for sale in the current phase of a popular new housing development.

The Barratt Homes site at Mortimer Park in Driffield has attracted strong demand, with the next phase set to launch later this summer.

Located just a 20-minute walk from Driffield town centre, the development offers a mix of two, three, and four-bedroom homes starting from £220,000.

Paul Hogan, sales director at Barratt Homes Yorkshire East, said: "We’re proud of the community forming at Mortimer Park and are looking forward to getting started on the next phase of new, energy-efficient homes.

"We urge prospective buyers in the area, or looking to move to the area, to visit Mortimer Park, tour the available homes and speak to our dedicated sales team about offers and upcoming homes."

The development is described as ideal for first-time buyers, families, and professionals who commute to York, Beverley, or Hull.

Barratt Homes offers a range of schemes to support buyers, including a Part Exchange Guarantee for existing homeowners.

The scheme makes Barratt Homes the guaranteed buyer of the customer’s current property, eliminating estate agent fees and avoiding the hassle of being in a property chain.

More details are available on the Barratt Mortimer Park website.
