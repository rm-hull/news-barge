Camping is made ultimately more comfortable with the addition of a few home comforts. Whether it’s a warm blanket from home for chilly evenings or a portable kettle packed for your morning coffee, small extras can make a big difference. And if you’re someone who likes to stay connected while being at one with nature, Lidl is launching a new gadget just in time for festival camping season.

The new outdoor solar light and power bank with loudspeaker combines an LED light, FM radio, wireless speaker and power bank all in one compact device, helping you keep your campsite lit, devices charged and the music playing. And we all know space is in short supply for camping trips, so this device is a practical way to add some extra convenience without taking up much space in your bag.

## When is Lidl’s solar light and power bank available?

The outdoor solar light and power bank with loudspeaker will be available in Lidl stores from Thursday 13 August. As ever, Lidl’s middle aisle buys tend to be popular, so we expect this will sell out fast.

## How much is Lidl’s solar light and power bank?

The power bank will cost £14.99. It will be joined by a selection of other camping essentials as part of Lidl’s latest outdoor range, including the Crivit 4 Person Family Tent (£59.99), Crivit Self-Inflating Camping Mat / Air Cell Camping Mat With Pillow (£12.99) and Crivit Lightweight Sleeping Bag (£11.99), among other camping essentials.
