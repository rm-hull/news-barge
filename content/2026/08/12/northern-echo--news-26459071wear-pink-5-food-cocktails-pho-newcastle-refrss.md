A restaurant group is inviting everyone to add a splash of colour and a dash of heat to their midweek plans.

Pho, which has a restaurant in Newcastle, has launched Pink Wednesdays across the UK for August, offering selected menu items for just £5 to anyone who dines in wearing pink.

  Wear pink to Pho on Wednesdays to unlock the £5 menu every Wednesday in August 

*(Image: Pho)*

The promotion celebrates the introduction of the world’s first pink sriracha sauce, made in partnership with Thai condiment brand SABZU and available exclusively at Pho locations throughout the month.

Libby Andrews, marketing director at Pho, said: "Not only does the new SABZU Pink Sriracha look amazing, it is delicious and so fun for summer."

"We are really excited to be the only place in the UK to serve it for the month of August."

  SABZU’s new pink sriracha sauce 

*(Image: Pho)*

Pho describes the sauce as a “reinvention of the global hot sauce,” made with white Thai chillies and coloured naturally with hibiscus.

The pink sauce inspired the launch of Pink Wednesdays.

Diners wearing pink can enjoy a spicy pink margarita - infused with dragonfruit and Thai chillies - as well as select dishes including crispy spring rolls, chicken wings and summer rolls, all for just £5 each.

  Spicy Pink Margaritas 

*(Image: Pho)*

The offer is available every Wednesday throughout August at all Pho locations.

Dan Pathomvanich, CEO of Bamboomart, said: "We are committed to bringing the authentic taste of Thailand to consumers around the world.

"Our partnership with Pho marks an exciting milestone for SABZU as we introduce the world's first-ever Pink Sriracha sauce exclusively across the UK."

   

*(Image: Pho)*

"We believe Pink Sriracha perfectly captures today's appetite for bold, playful flavours while staying true to authentic Thai ingredients, and we are proud to bring this exclusive collaboration to life with one of the UK's most loved Vietnamese restaurant brands."

Pho has over 50 restaurants across the UK.

The Pink Sriracha sauce is available to try in-restaurant or via Deliveroo until the end of August.
