# Chick-fil-A adds chicken and waffles sandwiches to its menu - and two new drinks

![Chick-fil-A is adding new Chicken & Waffles Sandwiches to its menu](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/12/15/50/Chick-fil-A-Chicken-Waffles-Sandwiches-2.jpg)

- Chick-fil-A has unveiled several new menu items, including a breakfast-inspired sandwich that will be available all day.
- The fast-food giant is set to add Chicken & Waffles Sandwiches to its menu on August 24, it announced in a news release on Wednesday.
- The sandwich will be available as a breakfast item during standard breakfast hours, and a larger entrée size will be sold from 10:30 a.m. to close.
- Chick-fil-A is also adding two new S’mores-inspired drinks to its lineup, the S’mores Milkshake and S’mores Frosted Coffee.
- The items are available as part of the chain’s limited-time fall lineup as it continues yearlong “Newstalgia”-inspired celebrations, bringing customers reimagined, familiar favorites with comforting flavors and unexpected new twists.
