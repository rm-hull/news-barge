A "vape" sparked a fire in the back of a recycling wagon in Newton Aycliffe.

Firefighters were called to the town on Tuesday (August 11) after smoke started pouring from the pile of rubbish in a recycling wagon.

Durham County Council said the fire is believed to have been caused by a vape - and that it had to tip the contents of the wagon onto a patch of grass.

The fire service then put out the flames.

Thankfully, no-one was hurt and the site was later cleared of rubbish - but a warning has now been issued to not put batteries or vapes in with general rubbish and recycling.
