A revamped play park and games court have reopened after a North East redevelopment aimed at giving children and young people a "modern, inclusive place to play, exercise and socialise".

Located on Deepdale Street in Hetton, the project was designed and delivered by playground specialist Streetscape.

The updated park now features new and refurbished equipment, including swings, climbing equipment, and a slide, alongside a refurbished ball game court.

  Centre, Joanne Gordon, development and regeneration director at Gentoo 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Joanne Gordon, development and regeneration director at Gentoo, said: "We are delighted to see the transformation of Deepdale Street Play Park completed.

"The park has been completely revitalised, creating a welcoming, accessible and inclusive space for local children and families to enjoy for years to come.

"This investment demonstrates our commitment to improving neighbourhoods alongside delivering much-needed affordable homes.

  Hetton’s revamped play park and games court on Deepdale Street has reopened following a redevelopment by Streetscape 

*(Image: Supplied)*

"We hope the new facilities will encourage more outdoor activity, play and social interaction for residents of all ages and is enjoyed by customers at Eskdene Rise and the further Hetton community for years to come.”

The park sits next to Gentoo’s Eskdene Rise development, which has delivered 86 new affordable homes in partnership with Adderstone Living.

The opening was marked with a small launch attended by Gentoo colleagues, Streetscape staff, councillors, and families from the area, who enjoyed free ice cream and refreshments.

Cllr Andrew Ramsey, portfolio holder for corporate services and local government efficiency at Sunderland City Council, said: “It was a great pleasure to attend the opening of Deepdale Street Park.

Hetton town councillor Joanne Lloyd, who attended the launch, said: "It was fantastic to attend the re-opening of Deepdale Street Park and even better to see so many local children and families enjoying the new play area really showed just how much the park means to local families."
