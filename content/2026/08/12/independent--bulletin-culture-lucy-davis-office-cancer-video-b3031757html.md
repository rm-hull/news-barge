# Terminally ill Lucy Davis rings bell symbolising end of cancer treatment

- Actor Lucy Davis has revealed she has been diagnosed with incurable breast cancer.
- The 53-year-old star of BBC sitcom *The Office* shared a video of herself ringing an end-of-treatment bell.
- Davis said that her cancer was first diagnosed 18 months ago and has since metastasised to her bones, including her spine, right hip, and ribs.
- She explained that it is now too late for chemotherapy following her diagnosis.
- The actor added that she is focusing on living the remainder of her life in as fun a way as she possibly can.
