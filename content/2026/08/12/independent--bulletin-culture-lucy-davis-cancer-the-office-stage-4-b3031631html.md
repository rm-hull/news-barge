# The Office star Lucy Davis praised by charities for sharing incurable cancer news

- Actress Lucy Davis, best known for playing Dawn Tinsley in The Office, has announced she has been diagnosed with incurable stage four breast cancer.
- The 53-year-old revealed on Instagram that she was diagnosed 18 months ago and the cancer has metastasised to her spine, right hip and ribs.
- Davis explained that it is too late for chemotherapy and she is focusing on living out her remaining time as enjoyably as possible.
- Charity Breast Cancer Now praised Davis for her openness in raising awareness about metastatic breast cancer across the UK.
- The charity emphasised the critical need for increased research funding, better treatments and access to life-extending drugs like Enhertu.
