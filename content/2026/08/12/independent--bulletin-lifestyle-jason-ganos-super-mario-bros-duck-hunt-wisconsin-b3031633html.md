# Rare hoard of ‘pristine’ Super Mario Bros game cartridges discovered

- Nintendo collector Jason Ganos has discovered a rare hoard of 97 “pristine” Super Mario Bros./Duck Hunt copies after a pop culture shop owner in Wisconsin revealed he possessed a long-forgotten box cartridges in storage.
- Manufactured in 1993, the cartridges represent a previously unknown variant of the game and mark the final recorded production date for the title.
- Five of the cartridges achieved a perfect score from Professional Sports Authenticator video games.
- A majority of the discovered titles will go up for sale in an online auction running until 9 September.
- Ganos and shop owner Kevin Braun are retaining some of the high-rated copies and plan to host a charity auction following the commercial sale.
