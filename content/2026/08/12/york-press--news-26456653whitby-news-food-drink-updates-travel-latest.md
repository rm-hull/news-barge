## Whitby news and updates on historic North Yorkshire seaside town

Whitby is known for its clifftop abbey and quaint, historic streets *(Image: Provided)*

Whitby is a picturesque seaside town on the North Yorkshire coast in England.

Known for its quaint harbour and dramatic clifftop abbey – which inspired parts of Bram Stoker’s Dracula - the town supports more a million visitors each year.

Independent businesses line its historic cobbled streets, which welcome the famous biannual Whitby Goth Weekend, among other festivals.

### Travel

The town is an approximate hour’s drive from York and can be visited by train and bus.

It is commonly taken via the A64, followed by the A169.

Once in Whitby, road users are often stopped by the Whitby Swing Bridge, in Bridge Street.

  Whitby Swing Bridge is set to close to cars for next five weekends 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

The 75-foot swing bridge, made in 1909, carries vehicles and pedestrians across the River Esk between Whitby’s east and west sides.

It is set to close at schedule times during the school summer holidays to protect pedestrian safety along the narrow walkway, and the local economy – with buses exempt from the order.

### 
  **Award-winning food and drink**

The town hosts multiple award-winning restaurants, cafes and bistros.

Among these are some prized fish and chip shops, including the Magpie Café, which was named one of the best in the region by The Telegraph in recent weeks.

Whitby’s pub scene is also something to behold.
