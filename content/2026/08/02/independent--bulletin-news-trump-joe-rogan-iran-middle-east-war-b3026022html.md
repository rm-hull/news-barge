Podcaster Joe Rogan stated there is "no clear way" to end the ongoing Iran conflict, which he labeled an "unprovoked attack" by the United States, arguing it makes America less safe.

Rogan's comments were made during a Friday episode of his show featuring investigative journalist Annie Jacobsen, who described the five-month-long war as "terrible" and "unnecessary," highlighting a fundamental warping of the military's purpose.

Both Rogan and Jacobsen lamented that media coverage has prioritized the financial cost over the human toll, noting 18 U.S. service members killed and hundreds wounded, alongside 3,636 deaths in Iran by March.

President Donald Trump has defended the conflict as essential to prevent Iran from developing nuclear weapons, asserting that the American death toll remains relatively low compared to previous conflicts.

Despite endorsing Donald Trump before the 2024 election, Rogan has publicly criticized the administration on various issues, including immigration and the Epstein files, aligning with a recent AP-NORC survey indicating nearly two-thirds of Americans believe the conflict has not been worth fighting.
