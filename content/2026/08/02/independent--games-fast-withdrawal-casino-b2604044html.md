## Why Might a Casino Withdrawal be Delayed?

Even at a fast payout casino, some withdrawals can get held up for various reasons. However, any hold-ups tend to be caused by routine checks or human error, rather than technical problems, and can easily be avoided.

Common reasons for payment delays include:

**Account verification (KYC):** UK-licensed casinos must verify a player’s identity before processing withdrawals. To avoid any delays, complete the KYC steps during sign-up.

**Anti-Money Laundering:** All licensed casinos are required to follow strict anti-money laundering regulations. This may require checks on the source of funds or account activity. 

**Payment method alterations:** Many casinos require withdrawals to be made using the same method as the deposit, which can slow down the process if an alternative is selected. 

**Active bonus wagering:** Any outstanding wagering requirements must be completed before funds can be released. 

**Timing of the request:** Some online casinos don’t process withdrawal requests at certain times of the day, or outside of usual business hours. 

**Large withdrawal amounts:** Higher-value payouts are often subject to additional internal checks.
