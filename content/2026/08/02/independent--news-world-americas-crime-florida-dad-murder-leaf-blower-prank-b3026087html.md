A Florida father has been indicted on a murder charge after authorities said he used a leaf blower on his infant son in what turned out to be a fatal prank.

James Westervelt III, 38, of Marion County, was indicted on first-degree felony murder, according to records filed by prosecutors on July 30. The filing noted that additional charges were pending.

Deputies responded to a home in August 2024 for a report of an unresponsive 6-month-old infant, *WESH 2***reported. The child — Westervelt’s son — was transported to a hospital and put on life support, but he died a few days later.

The boy’s death was determined to have been caused by shaken baby syndrome.

Westervelt allegedly told investigators that he used a leaf blower on his young child because “he thought it was funny,” *WCJB* reported. 

![James Westervelt, a 38-year-old Florida man, was indicted on a murder charge in connection with the death of his infant son, whom officials say he had used a leaf blower on as a prank](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/02/18/36/Bazaart_2D96913F-E625-436B-8F26-60A62CEA1AC4.jpeg)

Deputies also reportedly found video showing Westervelt using the yard tool on the infant’s face when the child was even younger, according to the outlet.

*The Independent* has contacted prosecutors, an attorney representing Westervelt and the Marion County Sheriff’s Office for comment.

Marion County is located in North Central Florida and encompasses the city of Ocala.
