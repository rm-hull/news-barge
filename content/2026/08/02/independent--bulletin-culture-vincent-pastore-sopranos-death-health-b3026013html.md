Vincent Pastore, the actor best known for his role as Salvatore ‘Big Pussy’ Bonpensiero in The Sopranos, has died at the age of 80.

He was reportedly found dead on Saturday in his Bronx, New York home by a neighbour, having not been seen or heard from for three days.

An investigation has been launched into the circumstances surrounding his death.

Pastore's agent, Bob McGowen, confirmed the passing, describing him as "such a great guy" who would help anyone.

Beyond The Sopranos, Pastore had a career playing Italian-American gangsters, with notable appearances in films such as Goodfellas and Gotti, and his final role was in Yellowjackets.
