A serious crash has closed a road in North Yorkshire today (Sunday).

Mook Lane is shut near Westow, between York and Malton.

North Yorkshire Police said at 11.36am: “Emergency services are currently dealing with a serious road traffic collision on Mook Lane near Westow.

#### Crash location

“The road is closed in both directions between Four Lane Ends and High Road and is expected to remain closed for a considerable time while emergency services work at the scene.

“Motorists are advised to avoid the area and use alternative routes where possible.”

Both air ambulances responded to the incident.

It is the same area where a motorbike rider died in a collision in May.
