# ‘Spider-Man’ has record-breaking opening day at the box office

- *Spider-Man*-*: Brand New Day* achieved the biggest opening day ever for a domestic release, earning $168 million on Friday across North America.
- This record-breaking performance surpassed the previous opening day record held by 2019's Avengers: Endgame, which earned $157 million.
- The film is projected to gross between $325 million and $358 million by Sunday, potentially becoming one of the largest North American opening weekends in box office history.
- The movie depicts Peter Parker embracing life as a full-time Spider-Man in a world that no longer remembers his identity, forcing him to rebuild his life and confront new threats.
- Other films like *The Odyssey* and*Toy Story 5* also performed at the box office, ranking second and third, respectively on Friday.
