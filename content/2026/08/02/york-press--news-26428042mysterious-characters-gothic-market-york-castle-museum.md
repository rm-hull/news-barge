A mysterious character is bringing their alternative market back to York.

The character Black Phillip, who docks a white face mask, black bowler hat and dark suit, will hold the “night(mare) market” at York Castle Museum from 7pm to 11pm on Saturday, October 24.

It follows the success of Phillip’s previous markets in York, most recently held at the Impossible bar in St Helen’s Square in April.

A unique ranger of traders will be at the York Castle Museum market selling gothic themed produce, which have previously ranged from witchcraft items and taxidermy to décor and drawings.

Phillip said: “I am hosting another fabulous (or terrible, depending on how you look at it) alternative market, in the Kirkgate street, inside the York Castle Museum.

“It was a hoot last year, with 37 people officially recorded as being scared, so this year I want to scare 38. It's on the 24th of October, between 7pm and 11pm and you MUST have a ticket to get in.”

  *Tickets for the market are £5 and available from**Eventbrite.*
