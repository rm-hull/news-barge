Prince Louis watches Commonwealth Games alongside family

Prince Louis garnered significant attention during his first official visit to Scotland, joining William and Kate, Princess Charlotte, and Prince George at the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow.

The young prince was seen wearing a Wales bucket hat while attending a netball semi-final at the Hydro arena with his family.

The royal party met gold medal-winning athletes and the Commonwealth Games mascot, Finnie, before visiting the Sir Chris Hoy Velodrome to watch cycling.

The Glasgow Games' opening ceremony, which featured the King and Queen emerging from a Tardis, was held indoors following financial restructuring after Australia's Victoria withdrew as host.

William and Kate are also scheduled to meet athletes supported by the charity SportsAid and participants in the Team England Futures project during their visit.
