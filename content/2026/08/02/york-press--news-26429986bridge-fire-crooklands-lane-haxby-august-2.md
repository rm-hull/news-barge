A WOODEN bridge has been deliberately set on fire in a York town.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said its crew from Huntington were called to a wooden bridge on fire in Crooklands Lane, Haxby at 8.14am this morning (August 2).

  


- 
    **Fire near York city centre car park - cause believed to be deliberate**
- 
    **Air ambulance called to crash in North Yorkshire road“** Crews extinguished the fire using a knapsack sprayer, and cordoned off the bridge,” said a spokesperson for the service.

They added that the cause was deliberate and police were informed.
