An “immaculate” home has hit the market in “one of York’s most desirable locations”.

The three-bedroom Victorian townhouse is for sale in Scott Street, off Scarcroft Road, for £495,000.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said it is a “charming and sympathetically updated period home”.

  Inside the three-bedroom home for sale in Scott Street, York, for £495,000 

*(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

An entrance hall leads to an open plan lounge and dining room with a log burning stove.

French doors let to a courtyard garden where an outhouse has been converted into an office space.

The modern kitchen is beyond the kitchen, as well as a separate utility room and a toilet.

Two double bedrooms and the main bathroom are on the first floor.

Another double bedroom is on the second floor “with plenty of room due to the rear dormer”, Hudson Moody said.

A spokesperson for the estate agent said the property is in “one of York's most desirable locations, a stones throw from 'Bishy road', the city centre and train station”.
