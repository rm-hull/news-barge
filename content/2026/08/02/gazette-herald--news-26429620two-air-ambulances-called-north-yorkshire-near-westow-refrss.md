BOTH air ambulances have been called to a serious crash in North Yorkshire.

Two Air Ambulances from Yorkshire Air Ambulance have responded to a serious crash in Mook Lane near Westow, south west of Malton, at around 11am this morning (August 2).

A spokesperson for the police said: "The road is closed in both directions between Four Lane Ends and High Road and is expected to remain closed for a considerable time while emergency services work at the scene.

"Motorists are advised to avoid the area and use alternative routes where possible."

Both helicopters left the scene shortly after 11.35am and are currently passing over York, seemingly towards Leeds.

We have contacted Yorkshire Ambulance Service for comment.
