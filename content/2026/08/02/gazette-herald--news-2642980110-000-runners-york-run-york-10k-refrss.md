  **THOUSANDS of runners took to the street in York city centre today as the annual Run For All 10K took place.**

More than 10,000 people attended the race at Knavesmire today (August 2) with everyone taking a total of 15 minutes to cross the line after the race started just after 9.30am.

  Participants took on a scenic tour of the city, passing notable landmarks such as Clifford's Tower, **York Minster**, and the medieval walls - all while raising money for a good cause and hoping to get a personal best on the flat track.
