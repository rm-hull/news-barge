BREAKING: Capital One SUSPENDS Trump’s 300+ Bank Accounts Due To Money Laundering Activity |U.S News

Capital One Financial has formally linked its decision to close over 300 Trump Organizationbank accounts to anti-money laundering (AML) concerns, a significant disclosure made on Friday.

This marks the first time a bank has publicly connected AML concerns to Donald Trump's business, asserting the closures were due to "months of analysis" by its AML team.

The disclosure came as Capital One sought to dismiss a lawsuit filed by the Trump Organization and Eric Trump in March 2025, which alleges they were illegally "debanked" for political or religious reasons.

Capital One maintained that the Trump Organization's allegations of political pretext are "misguided" and that the "transaction patterns identified" align with federal banking guidance.

Donald Trump has intensified pressure on banks regarding alleged discriminatory debanking practices, signing an executive order in August 2025 and filing a separate lawsuit against JPMorgan Chase on similar grounds.
