Stephen Baldwin, known for his role in the 1995 thriller The Usual Suspects, became a born-again Christian in 2001, which significantly altered his acting career trajectory.

He expressed no regrets about prioritizing faith-based projects over mainstream fame, stating he never aspired to be a major star like Tom Cruise.

Baldwin has dedicated decades to ministry, including leading skateboarding youth outreach and conducting evangelistic tours across numerous churches.

He recently criticized the movie industry, claiming he was fired from the 1998 film The Object of My Affection because he was deemed “funnier than Jennifer Aniston.”

Baldwin has previously attributed his feeling of disconnection from the entertainment industry to clashes over his support for Donald Trump in past presidential elections.
