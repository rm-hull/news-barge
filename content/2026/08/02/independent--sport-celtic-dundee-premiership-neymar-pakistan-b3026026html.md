Reigning William Hill Premiership champions Celtic begin their defence of the title when Dundee head for Glasgow.

Martin O’Neill’s men return to action at Celtic Park a little less than three months after clinching their latest crown in a dramatic final-day showdown with Hearts.

Elsewhere, the West Indies go head to head with Pakistan in the second Test in Port of Spain, and there is Hundred action from Cardiff as Welsh Fire and Southern Brave meet in both the women’s and men’s competitions.

Here’s what is happening in the sporting world on Monday – and where you can catch the action in the UK.

## The Bhoys are back in town

![Martin O’Neill will lead Celtic into Premiership battle with Dundee on Monday evening (Andrew Milligan/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/02/15/e02f1d254d5e90f1e1f29d73e32a7ab7Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1NzU4NzI4-2.84640983.jpg)

**Celtic** will attempt to strike an early blow in their Old Firm rivalry with**Rangers** when they entertain**Dundee** in their opening fixture of the new**Premiership** campaign.

The men from Ibrox could only draw 1-1 at **Dundee United** on Friday evening and O’Neill’s side will have a chance to establish a two-point advantage during the season’s first weekend.

Dundee proved something of a thorn in Celtic’s side last season, winning the first league clash between the sides 2-0 at Dens Park in October before going down 1-0 in Glasgow in December and 2-1 at home in April either side of a 2-1 Scottish FA Cup defeat in which they led 1-0 heading into the last minute of normal time.

All the action is on Sky Sports Football and Sky Sports Main Event from 6.30pm.

## What else is happening on Monday?

![Joe Root’s Welsh Fire are in men’s Hundred action against Southern Brace in Cardiff on Monday (Adam Davy/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/02/15/d46bc848f48c31d8c6ab3665939ddca2Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1NzU5MDMy-2.85386679.jpg)

It is day two at the **Queen’s Park Oval** in**Port of Spain, Trinidad** as the**West Indies** attempt to build upon their**first Test** victory over**Pakistan** in**Tarouba**. Coverage begins on TNT Sports 2 at 2.30pm.

In the **Women’s Hundred**,** Welsh Fire**meet** Southern Brave**at** Sophia Gardens** on Sky Sports Cricket from 2.45pm and Sky Sports Main Event from 3pm. Their male counterparts take centre stage on Sky Sports Cricket at 6pm.

Sky Sports Tennis has action from the **Canadian Open** at 4pm with Sky Sports Main Event taking over at 10.15pm, while stage three of the**Tour de France Women** is on TNT Sports 1 at 2.30pm.
