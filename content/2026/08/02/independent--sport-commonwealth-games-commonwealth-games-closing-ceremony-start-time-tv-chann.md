Eleven days of multi-sport competition come to an end on Sunday when the closing ceremony of the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Glasgow takes place.

As it stands, Australia lead the medal table with 104 medals and 48 goals, followed by Canada and England.

The closing ceremony will also feature a handover to Ahmedabad, the Indian city which is set to host the 2030 Games.

**Here’s everything you need to know.When is the closing ceremony?**

The closing ceremony starts at 9pm BST on Sunday 2 August at the Hydro in Glasgow.

**How can I watch it?**

Viewers in the UK can watch the opening ceremony on TNT Sports 2, with coverage starting at 7:30pm BST. It can also be streamed on HBO Max.

**Who will be performing?**

A number of artists will take to the stage to provide the musical entertainment, headlined by Scottish rock band Simple Minds.

BEMZ, Cammy Barnes and Sandi Thom will also perform, as will Australian singer Delta Goodrem – for her third appearance at a Commonwealth Games ceremony after appearing at the 2006 (Melbourne) and 2018 (Gold Coast) opening ceremonies.

There will also be a formal handover to the 2030 hosts, Ahmedabad, featuring performances by Shankar Mahadevan and Rashmika Mandanna.
