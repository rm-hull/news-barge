If you are looking for something to get the whole family involved in this summer, you might want to try the Wensleydale Experience.

Found in the beautiful Yorkshire Dales, the Wensleydale Experience offers tours of its farm and more hands-on activities.

If you book a farm experience, you will be able to get close to many of the farm animals, including the herd of Highland cows.

While walking around the farm, you will also be able to enjoy views of the surrounding countryside.


## What can I do at the Wensleydale Experience?

You can book a two- to three-hour farm experience that "offers a genuine insight into life working on a Yorkshire Dales farm."

You will get to feed many of the animals, help gather sheep, and explore the estate while learning more about how farming works.

The experience might be the perfect way to spend a day with your family if they love animals.

There are also tours available where you can relax and enjoy a walk around the farm to meet each of the animals with children.

You will also be given some time to help feed some of the animals, and children can learn more about where food comes from.

Tours are informal with plenty of opportunities to ask questions and take things at a slower pace.


The immersive tours and farm days can be booked through the Wensleydale Experience website.

### 
  **What do families think of the Wensleydale Experience?**

One reviewer on Tripadvisor said: "We booked a working farm afternoon experience on this working farm.

"It was absolutely fantastic and cannot recommend it enough.

"Bridgette runs the farm with her husband; she is super friendly and welcoming and caters for all ages.

"We had the opportunity to feed all of the animals and explore the farm and finished off the experience with a cream tea."

Other people have extended their visit by staying in one of the farm’s yurts or other rooms and said they had an "amazing weekend."
