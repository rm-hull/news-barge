Boy George has announced he has parted ways with his record label manager, Tony Pontius, after Pontius refused to be involved with the singer's new pro-Israel track.

The Culture Club singer released a song titled We Will Dance Again, which defends Israel's military actions in Palestine and disputes claims of genocide in Gaza.

Boy George said that his decision to “stand up for [his] Jewish friends” has led to him losing people from his life, including his manager, whom he described as “gutless”.

The controversial song's lyrics dismiss the idea of genocide, stating, “You say genocide, I say war,” and appear to criticise the pro-Palestine movement.

The singer previously withdrew from his role as King Herod in Jesus Christ Superstar due to the controversy and explained he was inspired to write the song after meeting families of victims from the Nova music festival attack.
