FIVE appliances are dealing with a fire at a North Yorkshire beauty spot.

Crews are dealing with the incident within Wykeham forest with the fire measuring 20 x 20 metres, consisting of trees, bushes and undergrowth.

An Argocat from Kirkbymoorside and a Water Bower from Tadcaster are among the equipment at the scene.
