## About Beerwulf

Beerwulf is an online retailer, specialising in home draught beer systems. Based in Amsterdam and backed by the Heineken Group, Beerwulf stocks beer taps, including the Blade and BeerTender, as well as compatible kegs from top-selling brands and craft breweries.

Originally launched in 2017, Beerwulf operates in ten countries across Europe. As part of the evolving business strategy, the SUB taps and 2l kegs will be discontinued soon and focus will be shifted to larger-capacity kegs.

### Where can I apply my Beerwulf discount code?

### Is there a student discount at Beerwulf?

Yes. Students and those under the age of 26 qualify for an extra 10 per cent off Heineken Experience merch or £25 off Blade starter packs at Beerwulf. Graduate students get the same merch discount, but five per cent off Blade starter packs.

### Does Beerwulf have a key worker discount?

Yes, Beerwulf offers £25 off Blade starter packs and 10 per cent off Heineken experience merch for key workers.

### What is the keg return process at Beerwulf?

Kegs can be returned within 30 days as long as they are unopened and in their original condition. Products in unsellable condition may result in a partial refund.
