## What are the three new Morrisons chocolate bars?

You can choose from Jaffa Orange Milk Chocolate, Peanut Butter and Jelly Milk Chocolate, or Custard Cream Milk Chocolate.

The custard cream-inspired chocolate is filled with a vanilla cream and biscuit crunch layer.

If you are a big fan of chocolate orange flavours, the Jaffa Orange bar has a zesty orange jelly layer through the middle.

Those who love a peanut butter and jam sandwich might want to try the creamy, nutty, and fruity jelly-layered chocolate bar.

The Peanut Butter and Jelly Milk Chocolate might not be around for too long, as it does have a limited-edition sticker on it so you might want to pick it up soon.

All three of the chocolate bars cost £2 for a 165g bar and are branded as luxury chocolate.

The chocolate bars can either be ordered from the Morrisons website or bought in-store.
