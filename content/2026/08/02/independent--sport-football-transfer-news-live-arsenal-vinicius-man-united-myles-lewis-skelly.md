Manchester United are considering a shock move for Arsenal’s Myles Lewis-Skelly as Michael Carrick targets versatile options to bolster his squad. Newcastle’s Lewis Hall is also a target this transfer window, *The Independent* understands, with Eddie Howe’s departure perhaps hinting at a shift in strategy at the club that could see the left-back on the market.

A move for Lewis-Skelly would be more ambitious, but could Arsenal need to sell? The Premier League champions have made progress on a major deal for Vinicius Jr, though will have to pay a significant fee to capture the Brazilian from Real Madrid – particularly if Bruno Guimaraes is signed as well. Christian Norgaard is one fringe figure set for the exit after a single season at Arsenal; Everton is his next destination, it seems.

Elsewhere, Chelsea have announced the surprise signing of Danny Welbeck, with Jordan Henderson soon to follow, and Mohamed Salah appears to have a new club, finally, after leaving Liverpool on a free.

## The flaw in Chelsea’s new transfer plan to sign older players

Chelsea are having a strange summer – few would have anticipated Danny Welbeck and Jordan Henderson featuring on their list of transfer targets. **Lawrence Ostlere** explores why the pair have been purchased.

## Ajax and Real Betis ignite interest in Ireland striker Troy Parrott

Ajax and Real Betis have made contact with AZ Alkmaar about Troy Parrott, in a deal that could go beyond £25m. All of Fulham, Como and PSV Eindhoven are meanwhile surveying how it develops, with a view to potentially trying to hijack any deal.

Parrott has had another sensational season with AZ, with one of many peaks the five goals against Portugal and Hungary in November's international break, that took Ireland into a World Cup play-off.

That has seen a series of clubs take interest in his progress, with the five above - from four different countries - currently the most keen. Parrott himself is open to another transfer abroad, given how beneficial the move to the Netherlands has been for his career.

## Enzo Maresca chasing familiar face for Manchester City

*The Sun* report new Manchester City boss Enzo Maresca wants to be reunited with Chelsea winger Pedro Neto, and also claim Maresca’s old club Chelsea are in the race for Atalanta centre-back Giorgio Scalvini along with Newcastle and Tottenham.

## Mohamed Salah set for new club

Former Liverpool forward Mohamed Salah is close to joining Trabzonspor, according to Foot Mercato, despite verbally agreeing to sign for fellow Turkish club Besiktas earlier in the summer transfer window.

## Everton target Arsenal midfielder as Idrissa Gueye replacement

Everton are closing in on the signing of Arsenal’s Christian Norgaard as David Moyes is on course to secure a replacement for the departed Idrissa Gueye.

The Merseyside club are in talks with the Premier League champions about a transfer of around £7m for the midfielder.

## Arsenal make breakthrough in pursuit of Vinicius Junior

Arsenal have made progress in negotiations that could yet secure the sensational transfer of Vinicius Junior, with the outline of a personal deal agreed.

There is still a considerable way to go for any deal to happen, and the preference of both Real Madrid and the player is to stay at the Bernabeu.

Present realities may yet see Vinicius end up at Arsenal, however, as he only has a year left on his contract. The situation is said to now be entering the decisive period, and it is possible that the 26-year-old’s future is decided this week.

## Manchester United also chasing Lewis Hall

United are meanwhile hopeful of a deal for Hall. He is open to the prospect amid a tumultuous summer for Newcastle United, as the owners assess strategy to build a new team.

Though it would still require a lot to actually get a deal done.
