At least three people have been killed and 21 injured after a woman brought a homemade bomb near a central Moscow restaurant, according to Russian officials.

The explosion, which occurred at around 7:55pm local time (5:55pm BST), occurred near an upscale Italian restaurant located in one of Moscow's seven Stalin-era skyscrapers at Kudrinkskaya Square, police said.

Russia's Anti-Terrorism Committee said that an unidentified woman had tried to get into the restaurant but was stopped by a security guard, Russian news agencies reported.

Among the dead were the security guard, the unnamed woman, and another visitor, the committee added.

The Kommersant daily newspaper cited sources as saying that the bomb had been intended to maim and kill guests who were enjoying themselves on the restaurant's summer terrace outside.

The restaurant's website said the venue had been closed on Saturday for a private event, but it is unclear who was present.

There is not yet any suggestion that the explosion is linked to the war in Ukraine.

It came after Kyiv struck three Russian oil refineries nearly 1,600 kilometres beyond enemy lines, Volodymyr Zelensky said on Saturday.

## Ukraine says it struck oil refinery and air base in Russia's Saratov region

## Russia fired a record number of missiles at Ukraine in July

Russia fired a record 376 missiles at Ukraine in July, according to an AFP analysis of Ukrainian air force data.

According to reports published by Kyiv’s air force, Russia has launched more than twice the number of missiles at Ukraine in July than in June.

A significant number of these attacks targeted Kyiv, which saw some of the deadliest strikes.

Russia also fired 4,956 long-range drones at Ukraine during the month, down 14 per cent on June.

## Ukraine says it struck Russian container ship in Black Sea

Ukraine struck a sanctioned Russian-flagged container ship in the Black Sea, President Volodymyr Zelensky said on X.

The vessel, Yanina, has a capacity of more than 100,000 metric tonnes and was targeted during Ukrainian operations in the Black Sea and the Sea of Azov using domestically produced "middle-strike" weapons, Zelensky said.

"Our targets are consistently defined facilities that sustain the war effort," he wrote, adding: "Grateful to Ukrainian warriors who are bringing the war back to Russia and responding to its attacks against life."

Russia's state nuclear corporation Rosatom confirmed the strike, saying the ship was hit by two Ukrainian naval drones in international waters about 130 nautical miles from the Russian port of Novorossiysk.

Rosatom chief Alexey Likhachev said all 17 crew members were rescued.
