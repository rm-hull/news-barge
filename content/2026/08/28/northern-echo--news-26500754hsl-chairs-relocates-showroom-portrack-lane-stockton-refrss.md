A furniture company known for its comfy chairs, sofas and beds has moved to a new showroom in Teesside after outgrowing its old store.

HSL Chairs has relocated its Stockton showroom to Portrack Retail Park, in a major upgrade for customers looking to browse HSL's large range of supportive furniture, designed to improve comfort and wellbeing.

With more than 55 years of experience, the family-run business has been investing in its showroom network to create a better experience for customers and strengthen ties with local communities.

The new Teesside showroom will feature HSL's full range of chairs, sofas and beds - alongside consultation areas where customers can take their time choosing the right product with advice from specialist staff.

The larger showroom will give customers more space to explore, try products and receive expert guidance before buying.

HSL's range includes a series of rise and recline chairs, recliner sofas, adjustable beds and other supportive furniture.

  HSL specialise in recliner chairs and comfortable furniture for people struggling with mobility. 

*(Image: HSL)*

Customers will also benefit from a complimentary seating assessment to help them find furniture suited to their individual needs.

HSL designs its products to specifically help people experiencing a range of issues such as back pain or reduced mobility, with plenty of options for people simply looking for furniture that offers greater comfort and support in everyday life.

Ben Waters, chief executive of HSL Chairs, said: "We're delighted to welcome customers to our brand-new Stockton-on-Tees showroom at Portrack Retail Park.

"This move represents an exciting step forward for HSL and allows us to showcase our furniture in a beautiful new environment that reflects the quality, comfort and service we're known for.

"At HSL, we're passionate about helping people live more comfortably and independently at home, and this new showroom gives customers the opportunity to experience our Clever Comfort approach and receive expert guidance tailored to their individual needs."
