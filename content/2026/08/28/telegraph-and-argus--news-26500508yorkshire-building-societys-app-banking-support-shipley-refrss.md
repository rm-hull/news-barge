Free digital support sessions are being offered to help people access online and app-based banking in Shipley.

The Yorkshire Building Society branch in Well Croft will host the events every Tuesday from 1pm to 4.30pm, starting September 1, 2026.

Adam Wardell, customer consultant at the branch, said: "We’re running these digital-focused events to support our members in feeling more confident about using online or app-based banking to manage their accounts with us.

"We understand that navigating the internet can be challenging for some, so we hope these free, in-person sessions will give people the reassurance and skills they need to use online services alongside the support they receive in our branches."

Sessions will cover setting up online accounts and addressing concerns about managing money digitally.
