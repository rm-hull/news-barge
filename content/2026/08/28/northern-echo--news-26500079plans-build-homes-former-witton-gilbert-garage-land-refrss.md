Plans have been submitted to build three new homes on the empty site of a former garage in a County Durham village.

The application concerns a former petrol station, motor garage and house on land off Front Street in Witton Gilbert.

Applicant Red Line Boundary Ltd has applied to Durham County Council for permission to build two two-bedroom houses and one three-bedroom house on the site of a former garage.

Two properties would face Front Street, while the third would be built at the rear of the site and accessed via a shared driveway.


Each home would have its own garden and off-street parking.

The application lists 10 parking spaces in total, including two spaces in garages.

The scheme would use brick, render, tiled roofs and timber windows and doors, while fences up to two metres high are proposed around private gardens.

Durham County Council will decide on the application in the coming weeks.
