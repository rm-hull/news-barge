People across Teesside and County Durham are being warned of thunderstorms, heavy rain and possible hail after the Met Office issued a yellow weather warning.

The warning is in place from 12pm until 9pm today (August 28), covering Darlington, County Durham, Hartlepool, Middlesbrough, Redcar and Cleveland, and Stockton-on-Tees.

According to the Met Office, while many areas are likely to avoid the worst of the weather, heavy showers and thunderstorms are expected to progress into the afternoon and evening.

Forecasters say the storms will initially hit North West England and much of Wales before spreading into parts of central and eastern England later in the day.

  Yellow thunderstorm warnings issued by the Met Office for parts of the North East tonight. 

*(Image: Canva/Met Office)*

Some areas could be hit by torrential downpours, bringing up to 20mm of rain in a short space of time, and as much as 40mm in places hit by repeated showers.

As well as torrential rain, the Met Office has warned that some areas could also see lightning strikes and hail.

The warning has been given a medium likelihood and a low impact rating, but people across the North East are being urged to remain alert.

Heavy downpours could potentially lead to localised flooding and travel disruption.

Conditions are expected to gradually settle later in the evening, with the yellow thunderstorm warning lifting from 9pm.

After a muggy start to the day, warm conditions are expected to continue across the North East as thunderstorms develop later this afternoon.
