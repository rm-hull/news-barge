## Coffee-fuelled morning rave with live DJ coming to Harrogate

The Rant & Ramble Coffee Rave will take place at Fodder Café on the Great Yorkshire Showground *(Image: Yorkshire Agricultural Society)*

A coffee-fuelled morning rave is set to bring music, movement and connection to Harrogate.

The Rant & Ramble Coffee Rave will take place at Fodder Café on the Great Yorkshire Showground on Friday, October 2, starting with a 7am walk before the dancing begins at 7.30am.

The 30-minute dance session will feature a live DJ and percussion from Romi Foxx.

Leesa Collinge, co-founder of Rant & Ramble, said: "You honestly have to experience a Rant & Ramble Coffee Rave to understand how it makes you feel.

"There’s something pretty special about standing in a room first thing in the morning with hundreds of people dancing, singing, laughing and completely letting go.

"For that hour, nobody is thinking about work, the washing, their to-do list or whatever else life is throwing at them."

Guests can refuel afterwards with breakfast from 8am, with coffee supplied by Huddersfield-based Coffee Brothers.

Vanessa Pitt, manager at Fodder, said: "We are so excited to be hosting a morning coffee rave at Fodder.

"We saw how Harrogate got behind the Symphonic Ibiza concert in the Main Ring this summer so we know there’s an appetite for great music, and we’ve seen how popular and vibrant Rant & Ramble’s Coffee Raves are."
