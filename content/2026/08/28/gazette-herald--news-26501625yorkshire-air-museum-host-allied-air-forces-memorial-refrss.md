A major memorial event is being planned in Yorkshire to honour the service and sacrifice of Allied air forces.

Taking place at the Yorkshire Air Museum in Elvington, Allied Air Forces Memorial Day will be held on Sunday, September 6.

The museum’s largest memorial event, it pays tribute to men and women from Commonwealth and Allied nations who have served in air forces past and present.

A spokesman for the museum said: "It is the Museum’s major ceremonial event, paying tribute to the service of men and women from Commonwealth & Allied Nations.

"We are honouring the bravery, service and sacrifice of members of allied air forces past and present."

This year, official representatives from the embassies of France, Belgium, Poland, Canada, Australia and New Zealand are expected to attend.

Group Captain John ‘Geordie’ Lawson, Officer Commanding RAF Leeming, will represent the Chief of the UK Air Staff.

He will take part in the "Turning of The Page" ceremony in the museum’s Book of Remembrance, to the inscription honouring the crew of Nimrod XV230, lost in Kandahar, Afghanistan on Saturday 2nd September 2006, with the loss of 14 personnel on board.

A statement from the RAF said: "Group Captain Lawson is honoured to accept the invitation and is very happy to participate in this important occasion.

"Thank you once again for including RAF Leeming in what will be a poignant and meaningful commemoration.

"We look forward to joining you in paying tribute and remembering those being honoured."

The Lord Lieutenant of North Yorkshire, Mrs Johanna Ropner, who represents HM The King, will attend the parade and service.

She will be joined by the Sheriff of York, Mr Geoff Cossins, who will also take part in the readings during the ceremony.

An aerial tribute is planned in the form of a mid-afternoon flypast by a fighter aircraft from the Battle of Britain Memorial Flight, subject to weather and serviceability conditions.

The event is open to the public and will include a service at 12pm in the museum’s main hangar beneath the Halifax bomber, Friday the 13th.

The service will be led by Revd Charles “Taff” Morgan MBE RAF (Rtd), the museum’s honorary chaplain.

Standard museum admission applies, and no special tickets are required.

The Yorkshire Air Museum stands on the former RAF Elvington, a World War Two heavy bomber base, and serves as a living memorial to Allied air forces.
