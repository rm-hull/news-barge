Real ale enthusiasts are looking forward to wetting their whistle at a long-running annual regional beer festival next week.

The Durham Beer Festival returns for its 44th edition on Thursday, September 3, staged at its now regular home, at the city rugby club’s Hollow Drift ground, off Green Lane.

As with the previous six festivals, the rugby clubhouse will again host the event, staged by the Durham branch of the Campaign for Real Ale (Camra).

Anyone planning to attend is advised that in a change from past events, it will run from Thursday to Sunday (September 3 to 6), rather than the previous regular time frame of Wednesday to Saturday.

Branch chair, Paul Dobson, explained the change, saying: “We decided that being open on a Sunday would suit more workers than Wednesday, and it gives the beers an extra day to settle down.”

  Paul Dobson, Durham branch Camra chair, left, with former city Mayor Lesley Mavin, centre, and her deputy, Trish Sutcliffe, at the start of the 2023 beer festival 

*(Image: The Northern Echo)*

He also asked potential attendees to note that the first pints will be pulled from noon on the opening day, rather than the previous starting time of 4pm.

Mr Dobson said although the full “menu” is still being finalised, the current plan is to have 27 real ales and six ciders and perries available.

  Line up of beers at previous festival in Durham 

*(Image: The Northern Echo)*

Waxing lyrical about the ales that will be on offer, he said: “We'll have two beers from the new-ish Fox Cub brewery of Spennymoor, and from the revitalised Castle Eden brewery, which recently celebrated its 200th anniversary.

“Others are from Norwich, Nottingham, Derbyshire, and Peterborough, alongside local favourites Maxim, Hill Island, Durham, Yard of Ale, George Samuel, Consett, Three Brother, Hopper House, Full Circle, and, oh, ciders from all over!”

The revised opening times are: Thursday to Saturday, September 3 to 5, from noon until 10.30pm, but on the final day, Sunday September 6, it will be 1 to 6pm.

  Logo for 44th Durham Beer Festival 

*(Image: Durham Camra)*

Entry is free to Camra members, on production of a valid membership card, and £5 to non-members.

On arrival at the venue there will be an entrance desk where payment can be made for entry to the festival.

Upon payment, paper wristbands, in different colours each day, will be issued.

Festival-goers will be given a free glass, an oversized half pint or pint glass, with clear markings for the half, third and full pint levels.

The glass can be kept or returned, but this year there will be no refund.

On entering the main festival hall the beer, cider and perry will be to your left, while the club's bar, which will be open for business, will be on the right where other drinks may be purchased as well as food, which can be collected from the hatch opposite the Camra bar.

There is a balcony in the main festival area as well as an exit to the left which offers access outside, but a plastic ‘glass’ will be needed for anyone choosing to drink outside.

Beer tokens are used for all purchases from the festival bars, one per half pint of all drinks up to and including 5.9% ABV, one third of a pint for drinks of 6.0% ABV or above.

Further beer tokens may be bought from the entrance desk.

Tokens are valid for any day of the festival and refunds will be given for unused tokens, providing they are not damaged.

Further details are available on Durham Camra social media channels and its website.
