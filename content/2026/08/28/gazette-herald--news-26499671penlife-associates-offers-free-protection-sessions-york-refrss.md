A day of complimentary Personal Protection Consultations is being offered to help people prepare for life's unexpected events.

York-based PenLife Associates will host the complimentary consultations on September 17, 2026, to help residents, families, and business owners review existing cover and identify any gaps in their protection plans.

With no obligation to take up any services, attendees will be able to gain a clearer understanding of what protection they already have and whether it continues to meet their needs.

Niall McSharry, protection adviser at PenLife Associates, said: "Protection is one of those things that's easy to put off.

"Life gets busy, and it's not always the most exciting item on the to-do list.

"But having the right plans in place can make a huge difference if the unexpected happens.

"These consultations are simply a chance to ask questions, review what you already have and leave feeling a little more organised and a little more confident about the future."

The sessions will take place at PenLife’s office at York Business Park on Opus Avenue and are open to anyone looking to learn more about life cover, critical illness cover, income protection, or business protection.

They will also be able to find out if there are areas that may need updating or adjusting.

PenLife Associates is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

Spaces for the complimentary sessions are limited, and early booking is advised.

To reserve a place, call 01904 661140 or email [enquiries@pen-life.co.uk.](mailto:enquiries@pen-life.co.uk.)

The company has advised that the consultations are for information only and do not constitute financial advice.

It also noted that protection policies typically hold no cash value at any time, and cover will cease at the end of the term.
