A man who failed to tell police who was driving his car when it was allegedly speeding through a village on the A64 today has six penalty points on his licence.

Ashley James William Gannon, 21, was not in court when his case was heard and did not respond to a summons ordering him to attend court.

But he must now pay a total court bill of £1,044.

North Yorkshire Police allegedly clocked his Ford Fiesta Zetec doing 48mph through the 30mph zone at Potter Brompton between Malton and Scarborough on August 7 last year.

Harrogate magistrates heard officers sent him an official form which he had to complete by law giving details of the car’s driver at the time. But he did not identify the driver.

Magistrates convicted Gannon, of Johnston Road, Poole, in his absence of failure to give details of a driver to police and put six penalty points on his licence.

They also fined him £660 and ordered him to pay a £264 statutory surcharge and £120 prosecution costs.

A charge of speeding against him was withdrawn. Speeding drivers get three to six penalty points on their licence, depending on the circumstances. Those convicted of not telling police who the driver was automatically get six penalty points.
