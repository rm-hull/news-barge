A new shop opened in York today – with queues stretching down the street.

Miniso opened its York store at 12pm today (Friday 28 August) at 40 Coney Street.

And there was a free goody bag packed with treats and surprises worth £25 for the first 200 customers who spent £5 or more instore – and to say people turned up is an understatement, with the queue for the opening going down the entire street.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/miniso-york-28.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/miniso-york-27.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/miniso-york-26.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/miniso-york-25.webp)

Miniso specialises in household and consumer goods including cosmetics, stationery, toys, and kitchenware.

The Chinese company is known for its cute products – from Hello Kitty merch, to Disney treasures, plushies, lifestyle items, snacks, plus new collabs with franchises such as Star Wars.

This is Miniso’s third Yorkshire store, along with Meadowhall and Leeds.

“And now we’re right in the centre of the beautiful city of York,” said Leighton Skinner, the regional area manager at Miniso.

“The queue is absolutely massive, the people of York have definitely come out in their thousands today!

“I’ve been up and down the queue talking to people, and they’re really excited to come in. We’re something a bit different, we’re a bit more Gen Z.

“But looking down the street, we’ve got some similar retailers so I think we fit in really well here.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/miniso-york-3.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/miniso-york-18.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/miniso-york-7.webp)

The brand opened its first store in Guangzhou, China in 2013, near its factory. Its first store in the UK, in Ealing, London, opened six years later. The brand now has more than 8,000 shops trading in 110 countries around the world.

Miniso is described on its website as ‘a design-led lifestyle store for all the family, offering fun high-quality homeware, tech, accessories, toys and so much more.’
