## Popular York sculpture trail to end with final exhibition at York Minster

The Watchers of York sculptures will gather at York Minster's Chapter House for a final exhibition <i>(Image: Alex Dyett)

A popular city-wide art trail at York Minster is coming to an end.

The Watchers of York sculptures will gather at York Minster's Chapter House for a final exhibition from September 3 to 13, offering visitors a last chance to see them all together.

The Watchers, inspired by medieval stone carvings, have been on display throughout York's streets, green spaces, riversides, and landmarks, providing a unique perspective on the city.

The sculptures have been part of a summer trail organised by Make It York in collaboration with York Minster, celebrating creativity, storytelling, and community spirit.

The trail will officially end on Monday, August 31, and following the final gathering, the sculptures will move to their permanent homes.

This will also be the last opportunity for people to bid on and own one of the Watchers sculptures.

Bidding closes on Thursday, September 10, with proceeds supporting charitable causes.

Throughout the summer, the Watchers have encouraged residents and visitors to explore York from a fresh perspective, with free trail maps available from the Visit York Visitor Information Centre.

The final gathering will mark the end of the trail, offering a fitting finale to the summer-long celebration of art and community in York.
