LEEDS station is gearing up for a record-breaking number of passengers over the August bank holiday weekend.

Northern says travel numbers are expected to surpass last year’s figure of more than 113,200, driven in part by crowds heading to Leeds Festival.

Alex Hornby, commercial and customer director at Northern, said: "We’re prepared for a busy bank holiday weekend and will be working hard to keep services running smoothly so we can get thousands of customers where they need to go.

"We are also deploying extra staff to provide support and assistance to anyone who needs it."

Staff at Leeds station are now wearing yellow vests to make them easier to spot for anyone who needs support, directions or advice.

David Dubaj-Downs, Network Rail’s head of customer and station transformation, said: "Leeds station is ready to welcome significant numbers of passengers this weekend, with additional resources in place and teams on hand to help passengers with their journeys and keep people moving safely.

"Passengers should look out for our station colleagues wearing their new bright yellow tabards, making it easier to find a helping hand, ask for travel information or get assistance when they need it."

Despite most Northern services running as planned, trains are expected to be busier than usual.
