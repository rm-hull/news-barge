A teenager who started work at a driving school 17 years ago now leads a key department at the company.

Tom Hixon joined Bill Plant Driving School in 2009 at the age of 16, working part-time while studying for a sports coaching BTEC.

Based in Ripon, the company is now nationally recognised with more than 1,100 instructors across the UK.

Mr Hixon said: "I did that, loved it, had a spring in my step.

"I worked part-time all the way up to July 2010 and thought I would take my summer holidays, however, Roy asked if I wanted a job.

"The business needed staff, I was offered a decent salary, and it made sense."

His early tasks included removing stickers and dual controls from used instructor vehicles.

A turning point came when Roy Danby, operations director at the time, asked Mr Hixon to answer calls, describing it as his "first integration for being in the nucleus of the business and was a bit of a baptism of fire".

He went on to join the booking team before helping to launch a dedicated phone line for instructor support — a service that has since grown into a full department.

Mr Hixon said: "We have to understand what instructors need — provide reassurance, guidance, be that caring ear at the end of the phone.

"In my mind, it’s about being that chirpy person at the end of the phone who cares and wants to understand — this is a partnership, we need to work together."

Bill Plant Driving School has won National Driving School of the Year five times, with all instructors teaching in Volkswagen tuition vehicles.

Mr Hixon’s journey has seen him manage the bookings team, lead instructor support, and now head up PDI success.

The company’s headquarters remain in Ripon, at Canalside House.
