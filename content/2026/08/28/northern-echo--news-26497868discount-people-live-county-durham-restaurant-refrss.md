A restaurant is offering County Durham customers a discount just for living in the local area.

Notch, in Durham city centre, said the offer is available from Sunday to Friday for customers visiting for food, drinks or a catch-up with friends.

The venue announced the deal in a social-media video, telling residents: "This one's for you."

It added that people living in a DH postcode can receive 10 per cent off their bill at the restaurant from Sunday to Friday.

## Who can get the Notch discount?

The discount is aimed at people whose home address falls within the DH postcode area, which covers Durham and parts of County Durham.

Customers are likely to need to show proof of their address when paying, such as a driving licence, bank card-linked digital wallet or another form of identification showing a DH postcode.

These areas include: Durham city, Chester-le-Street, Consett, Stanley, Houghton-le-Spring and surrounding villages

Notch has not set out the full terms of the offer in its social-media announcement, so diners are advised to check with the restaurant before booking or ordering.
