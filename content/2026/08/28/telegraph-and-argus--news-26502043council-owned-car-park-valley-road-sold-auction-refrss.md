A BRADFORD Council-owned car park that generates £2,300 a year income will be sold at auction next month.

The 340-square-metre car park on Valley Road, next to the Premier Store and opposite Shipley Fire Station, currently generates £2,300 a year rent, and is on a 20 year lease that ends in 2031.

Bradford Council has now listed the site for auction, with a guide price of £35,000.

It will go under the hammer at an online auction of BTG Eddisons on September 29.

It is one of many assets Bradford Council has recently listed for sale after Government granted “exceptional financial support” to balance its books.

That support allows the council to use the sale of assets to fund day to day services such as social care.

The auction listing describes the lot as a “Freehold commercial investment comprising an income-generating car park for use by the adjacent retail premises.

"The plot is sold subject to a tenancy agreement with a passing rent of £2,300 per annum, with a term 20 year term from March 2011 to February 2031.”
