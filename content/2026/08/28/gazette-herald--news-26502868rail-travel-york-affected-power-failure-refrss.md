RAIL passengers travelling through York today (Friday, August 28) can expect delays following the failure of the electricity supply.

National Rail, the umbrella service for train operators, said the outage happened between Alnmouth and Newcastle earlier today.

Lines have reopened, but National Rail said the following routes are affected:

- CrossCountry services between Edinburgh and Plymouth / Penzance
- LNER between Aberdeen and Kings Cross, between Edinburgh and Newcastle / Doncaster / Kings Cross, and Berwick-upon-Tweed and Kings Cross

Disruption is expected until 1pm today, according to National Rail.

  


  
    ⚠️ Due to damage to the overhead wires in the Alnmouth for Alnwick area, fewer trains are able to run on all lines.

    

    ❗ TransPennine Express services are currently unable to run between Edinburgh and Newcastle, in both directions.

The advice from train operators from National Rail is:

#### 
  **CrossCountry customer advice:**

Tickets will be accepted at no extra cost on the following routes:

- Avanti West Coast services between Edinburgh / Glasgow Central and Birmingham New Street
- LNER services between Aberdeen / Edinburgh and Doncaster
- Northern services between Carlisle and Newcastle, and also between Preston and York
- ScotRail services between Edinburgh and Glasgow Central / Glasgow Queen Street
- TransPennine Express services between Edinburgh / Glasgow Central and Leeds /Manchester Piccadilly

#### 
  **LNER customer advice:**

A reduced service will run between Edinburgh and London Kings Cross. You can defer travel until later today or until tomorrow, 29 August

Your ticket will be accepted at no extra cost on the following operators:

- Avanti West Coast services between Glasgow Central / Edinburgh and Manchester / London Euston
- CrossCountry between Edinburgh / Newcastle and York / Leeds / Doncaster / Sheffield
- East Midlands Railway between Sheffield / Leicester and London St Pancras International
- Great Northern and Thameslink between Peterborough and Stevenage / London Kings Cross
- Northern via any reasonable route
- ScotRail between Edinburgh and Glasgow Central
- TransPennine Express between Newcastle / York and Leeds / Manchester

Rail replacement buses have been requested to run to help you complete your journey. You will be updated once any transport has been confirmed.

Please see the alternative route map for more details.

#### 
  **Northern customer advice:**

Rail replacement transport has been confirmed to run between Chathill / Morpeth and Newcastle to help you with your journey. This will be operated by Northstar.

If the train you have a ticket for is cancelled, you can travel on any Northern, TransPennine Express, East Midlands Railway or LNER service within 2 hours before or after your cancelled service was due to depart.

#### 
  **TransPennine Express customer advice:**

You may use your ticket on the following services at no extra cost:

- CrossCountry services between Edinburgh and Leeds - *please note these services are also affected*
- LNER between Edinburgh and Newcastle - *please note these services are also affected*
- Northern between Carlisle and Newcastle
- TransPennine Express between Edinburgh and Carlisle

National Rail said passengers can check their journeys using the National Rail Enquiries real-time journey planner.
