Darlington families looking for things to do over the August Bank Holiday weekend can enjoy free town-centre entertainment, outdoor walks and low-cost days out nearby.

The main event is the Darlington Bank Holiday Weekender in Market Square, where visitors can browse food and drink traders across the four-day weekend.

## 
  **Darlington Bank Holiday Weekender**

The free-to-enter event runs from Friday, August 28, to Bank Holiday Monday, August 31.

Opening times:

- Friday, August 28: noon to 9pm
- Saturday, August 29: noon to 9pm
- Sunday, August 30: noon to 9pm
- Monday, August 31: noon to 5pm

Visitors will be able to choose from a range of food and drink stalls, with traders including wood-fired pizza, loaded fries, Brazilian açaí bowls and homemade cakes.

Food and drink must be paid for, but there is no admission charge to attend.

## 
  **Free events in Darlington**

The Bank Holiday Weekender is not the only option for people planning a day in Darlington.

A programme of events listed by Darlington Borough Council includes Absolutely ABBA on the Terrace on Sunday, August 30.

People interested in attending should check with the organiser before travelling, as the council listing does not confirm whether the event is free or ticketed.

Families can also enjoy a dinosaur-themed day in the Market Square on Thursday, August 27, immediately before the Bank Holiday weekend.

Dinosaur Day runs from 11am until 3pm and includes free live shows, fossil-dig activities and arts and crafts.


## 
  **Free outdoor days out**

For a no-cost family outing, there are several places to explore around Darlington:

- 
    **South Park** : Take a walk around the lake, use the playground or pack a picnic for one of the town’s best-known green spaces.
- 
    **The Skerne Trail** : Walk or cycle beside the River Skerne and through Darlington’s parks.
- 
    **Broken Scar** : Explore the riverside and woodland paths west of the town centre, with picnic space and views across the River Tees.

  **Preston Park, Eaglescliffe**: While admission applies to the museum, visitors can enjoy the outdoor parkland, riverside paths and play areas for free.

## 
  **Low-cost Raby Castle day out**

Raby Castle’s Harvest Food and Gift Market takes place from Saturday, August 29, until Monday, August 31, between 10am and 4pm.

The event will include local produce, food stalls, gifts and live music. Entry costs £2 when booked in advance or £5 on the day, while children aged under 15 can attend free.

Families travelling by bus may also be able to keep costs down.

Children aged under 15 can travel free on buses throughout August under the national offer, while passengers aged up to 21 can travel across Tees Valley and into County Durham for £1.
