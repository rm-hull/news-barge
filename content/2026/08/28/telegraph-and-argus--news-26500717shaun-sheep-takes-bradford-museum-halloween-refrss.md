SHAUN the Sheep is bringing spooky fun to Bradford this half term.

The National Science and Media Museum will host the half-term takeover from October 26 to November 1, inspired by the new Sky Original film Shaun the Sheep: The Beast of Mossy Bottom.

The museum’s Mossy Bottom Laboratory will be transformed into a week-long family experience filled with Halloween-themed science shows, stop-motion animation workshops, and Aardman-inspired activities, all led by the museum’s team of Explainers.

Alice Browne said: "Families can expect themed live science shows, Shaun the Sheep-themed photo opportunities, and stop-motion animation workshops inspired by Aardman’s beloved filmmaking techniques."

On Halloween, the museum will offer an expanded programme including a silent disco, family tours featuring horror film props and Victorian spirit photography, and other themed activities.

Throughout the week, visitors can also enjoy talks on special effects makeup and view an original set from the 2015 Shaun the Sheep Movie.

Pictureville Cinema will screen Aardman films during half term, including the new Shaun the Sheep film, with Kids’ Club tickets starting at £3.

The museum has also partnered with community groups for an “Ewesclusive” screening event on September 19.
