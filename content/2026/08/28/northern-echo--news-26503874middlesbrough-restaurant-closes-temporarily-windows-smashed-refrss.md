A Middlesbrough restaurant was forced to close ahead of Bank Holiday weekend after a "drunken" vandalism.

The Waggon, on Bedford Street, claimed that an individual smashed three of its windows during the night between Wednesday (August 26) and Thursday (August 27).

The food and drink spot said it would remain closed throughout Thursday while staff cleaned up the damage and made the venue safe for customers to return ahead of the Bank Holiday weekend.

In the post on social media, the owners wrote: "Sorry we are closed today due to a drunken idiot who decides to play let’s break windows. "

The independent Middlesbrough bar and kitchen said the damage was an unwelcome financial blow at a time when hospitality businesses are already facing rising costs.

They added: "As a small, independent bar and kitchen on Bedford Street trying to keep our heads above water with ever-increasing costs in hospitality, with energy and stock, this is another financial pressure we didn't need."

The business said it had spent months transforming the Teesside venue, making it more comfortable and welcoming for customers and rebranding it from its previous name, The Chairman.

The owners added: "For someone we don't know to go on the rampage last night and put three of our big windows in - it's an expense we don't need right now."

Despite the setback, staff began the clean-up operation on Thursday and said they were aiming to reopen at midday on Friday (August 28).

In an updated post around lunchtime today, The Waggon added: "We are back open - not a great look but you have to get on with it!

"Serving food and drinks all day. Saturday we have live music with Andy Brewster from 3.30pm. Join us for an ice cold pint."

The damaged windows are now boarded up.

The restaurant has a busy Bank Holiday weekend schedule planned, including live music, karaoke and Sunday lunch service.

The Northern Echo approached The Waggon, but the owners did not wish to comment.

Cleveland Police are investigating the incident.
