A dad died in hospital of a blunt head injury six days after a street attack, an inquest has heard.

Anthony Armstrong, known as Tony, died at James Cook Hospital in Middlesbrough on July 30 this year, after an incident on St Paul's Road in Hartlepool on July 24.

Mark James, 37, of St Paul’s Road has since appeared in court charged with manslaughter.

At an inquest was opened at Teesside Coroner's Court on Thursday (August 27) and heard that the 57-year-old's provisional cause of death was a blunt head injury.

Area coroner Paul Appleton said: "Police were called to an unknown male on St Paul's Road in Hartlepool on July 24 this year.

"It was initially reported at the time that he had hit his head at least twice on the floor and he was taken to James Cook Hospital.

"Since then, a police investigation has taken place and revealed that Tony had been pushed, causing him to fall forward.

"Tony remained at James Cook Hospital and had surgery. But, despite this, he deteriorated and died on July 30.

"Cleveland Police and the CPS have written to the coroner's office, making clear that a man, Mark James, will appear in court on September 1, linked with this incident.

The area coroner adjourned the inquest until after court proceedings had taken place.

Following Mr Armstrong's death, his family paid tribute to him.

In a statement, they said: “Tony lived a life that was not without challenges. Despite those struggles, he was a deeply caring man who loved his family and had a kind heart.

“Family meant a great deal to Tony. He was a father to Rachel and Amy, and a much-loved brother, cousin and uncle.

“In the final weeks of his life, Dad was helping to care for his cousin Darren, who was facing significant health difficulties of his own. He also cared deeply for Darren’s daughters, Kirsty and Chloe, and for his niece and nephew, Jessie and James.
