A waterfall in County Durham has been named one of the best hidden gems in the north of England.

The popularity of staycations has been increasing over the years, with 78% of UK adults planning at least one domestic holiday for the next 12 months, with the north of England coming out as the most popular destination.

Car rental experts SIXT have identified the best-kept secrets in Northern England that are worth visiting, with each place being judged on things like weather, social coverage, accessibility, and reviews.

The Cauldron Snout Waterfall came in third place on the list after scoring highly in the number of Instagram posts and its distance from the nearest car park.


## What is Cauldron Snout Waterfall like?

The waterfall can be found on the River Tees on the border between Cumbria and County Durham and can easily be reached from the Cow Green Reservoir car park.

There is an easy walking trail you can follow from the reservoir to the waterfall that takes around two hours to complete.

The area is usually very quiet, with only a few sheep to see, making it a peaceful place for a family trip.

The waterfall is one of England’s longest, as it is a 180-metre-long cascade along the river.
