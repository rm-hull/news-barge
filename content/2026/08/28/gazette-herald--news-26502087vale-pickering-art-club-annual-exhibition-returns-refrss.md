The Vale of Pickering Art Club's annual exhibition is returning next month.

The event will take place from Friday, September 4, to Monday, September 7, at the Memorial Hall in Potters Hill, Pickering.

The exhibition will be open from 10am to 5pm each day, offering free entry.

Visitors can expect a varied display of artworks, including watercolours, pastels, oils, and ceramics.

With more than 85 members, the club boasts a mix of full-time artists and amateur enthusiasts.

An exclusive preview evening is set for Thursday, September 3, from 7pm to 8.30pm, where Town Mayor Cllr Emma Dineen will officially open the event.

Refreshments will be available for purchase throughout the exhibition, and a raffle will be held, featuring original artwork donated by professional artists.

More information is available on the club's website: [https://www.pickeringartclub.co.uk/](https://www.pickeringartclub.co.uk/)
