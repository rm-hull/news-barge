A Yorkshire walking trail has been named as one of Britain’s favourite pub walks.

New research from Regatta has revealed the UK’s favourite hikes that have a pub on the way.

The Burgess Bridge and Nidd Gorge loop that starts from Knaresborough Viaduct made it onto the list with an overall rating of 4.8.

Knaresborough is also easy to reach from York by train, so you do not need to worry about finding a space to park your car.

  The walk will take you along the River Nidd. 

*(Image: Getty)*

## What is the Knaresborough walk like?

The “lovely” circular walk will take you through Knaresborough’s surrounding countryside.

The Nidd Gorge is loved by hikers with grassy paths and cycle tracks if you fancy completing the trail by bike.

The walk takes you along the River Nidd allowing you to enjoy the views and wildlife in the area.

You will end up back in Knaresborough, which is packed with places to relax after your walk including tea rooms and pubs.

You might want to visit the historic pub, The World’s End, where you can enjoy a pint and some classic pub food after working up an appetite on the walk.

You can also get a pizza from the North Yorks Pizza House in the pub’s garden.

Tom Blain, a spokesperson for Regatta, said: “A great walk is even better when there's a welcoming pub waiting at the end.

“Whether you're exploring the Peak District, the Welsh countryside or coastal routes, these hikes offer the perfect combination of fresh air, beautiful scenery and somewhere to relax afterwards.

“Better still, they're all easily reached by train, making it simple to enjoy a day out without needing the car.”
