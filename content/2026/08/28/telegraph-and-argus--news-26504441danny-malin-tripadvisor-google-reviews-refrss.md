A YouTube star has spoken out in defence of restaurants facing poor online reviews.

The food reviewer and YouTuber, Danny Malin, who formerly hosted Rate My Takeaway, now runs Danny v Food.

In his latest video, Danny, who is from Leeds, reviewed a takeaway described in TripAdvisor and Google reviews as the worst in Blackpool.

Danny said: “To be honest, this place has had some really, really bad reviews.”

“But sometimes they get it wrong and they have an off day.”

After trying the food, Danny defended the business and other poorly- reviewed restaurants.

He said: “I think these guys have been hard done by.”

“All I know is that what I’ve got in front of me is not a true reflection of what’s been said online.”

### Thoughts on zero-star food hygiene ratings

He previously worked as a food hygiene consultant and has criticised the system for zero-star hygiene ratings.

Danny said: “What really does my head in is we've got all these places that have got a zero-hygiene rating or one hygiene rating.”

“Years ago, it was done by a local authority. They would come out, they would assess the full business.

“Now, it's all turned into a money game because you can now request it, but you have to pay.”
