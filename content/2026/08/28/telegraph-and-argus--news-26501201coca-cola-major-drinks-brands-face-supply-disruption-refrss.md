DRINKS companies could face supply disruption due to strike action by workers at a West Yorkshire haulage firm, according to union bosses.

The industrial dispute centres on Lockwood Haulage which delivers glass bottles used by major brands including Coca-Cola, Budweiser, Stella, and Heineken.

Around 40 workers based in Knottingley and South Kirby will begin an all-out strike on September 2, after rejecting a one per cent pay offer.

Sharon Graham, general secretary of Unite the Union, said: "The fact that workers have not had a pay rise in nearly three years is an absolute insult and to be now offered a measly one per cent is a further kick in the teeth.

"Moving away from yearly increases to a bonus scheme is simply another way to shortchange our members at Lockwood.

"Unite will not allow this unacceptable attack on pay and our members have our full support in their industrial action."

The workers, who say they have not received a pay increase since October 2023, were previously employed by GXO and transferred to Lockwood in March 2025 under a TUPE agreement.

Unite claimed the strike will severely affect Lockwood’s customer Ardagh Glass, which manufactures bottles for the drinks industry.
