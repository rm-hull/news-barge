SEVERAL police horses were seen in Bradford city centre this morning - raising concerns amongst passer-byers.

Three mounted officers were spotted on Ivegate at around 11am on Friday.

A police car was also seen parked on the side of the road.

One passer-by alerted the Telegraph & Argus that there may be a possible ongoing incident.

However this is not the case, it is actually a regular sight to see them in city centres across the UK.

## Police horses

Mounted officers patrol busy shopping streets, public events and nightlife hotspots, providing a visible police presence that can help deter crime and reassure the public.

One of the main advantages of police horses is their height.

Mounted officers can see over large crowds and spot potential incidents more easily than officers on foot.

  Police horses could be seen in Ivegate this morning 

*(Image: Mike Simmonds)*

Their presence often encourages people to behave responsibly, while the horses themselves are specially trained to remain calm in noisy and unpredictable environments.

Many members of the public, especially children, are drawn to police horses and often stop to speak with officers.

This can help build positive relationships between communities and local police forces.

Despite advances in technology and modern policing methods, mounted units continue to play an important role in city centre policing.
