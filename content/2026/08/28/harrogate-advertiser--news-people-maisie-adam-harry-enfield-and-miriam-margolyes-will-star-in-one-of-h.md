# Maisie Adam, Harry Enfield, and Miriam Margolyes will star in one of Harrogate's highlights of the year

## The countdown is on for the feast of fun that is Harrogate Comedy Festival.

The 2026 edition of this hugely popular event will run from September 30 to November 1 with an outstanding programme of stand-up, improv, storytelling and family entertainment.

In this year's line-up are Reece Shearsmith, Richard Ayoade, Harry Enfield, Laura Smyth, Rachel Mars, Alexei Sayle, Scummy Mummies and Paul Merton and Suki Webster’s Improv Show.

Organised from, and based at, Harrogate Theatre, the festival spreads out at times to the Royal Hall and Harrogate Convention Centre.

"Harrogate Comedy Festival has become one of the highlights of our year, attracting some of the country's finest comedians alongside exciting new voices,” said Marcus Romer, Harrogate Comedy Festival programmer.

"We're delighted to once again bring such a varied programme to Harrogate, offering something for everyone.”

Now in its 17th year, Harrogate Comedy Festival 2026 will open with the hilarious A Star Wars Improv Show: The One-Man Made-Up Movie Parody, before audiences enjoy the Olivier Award-winning Showstopper! The Improvised Musical.

This year's programme sees Harrogate’s very own Maisie Adam return with her brand-new tour Whatsherface, before hosting Frank's Fund Comedy Gala.

Beloved actress and raconteur Miriam Margolyes will bring her live show Full English to Harrogate Convention Centre, while audiences can also enjoy high-energy stand-up from Jason Byrne, Seann Walsh and Tez Ilyas.

Harrogate Theatre is grateful for the support of Harrogate Comedy Festival's primary partner Vet Dentist.

Tickets are on sale now, with many shows selling quickly.
