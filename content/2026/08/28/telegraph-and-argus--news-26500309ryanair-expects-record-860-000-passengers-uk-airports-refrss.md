RYANAIR expects to carry a record number of passengers from UK airports this bank holiday weekend.

The airline forecasts 860,000 passengers will travel through its 22 UK airports during the August Bank Holiday weekend.

Jade Kirwan, director of communications at Ryanair, said: "Over this Summer Bank Holiday weekend, a record 860,000 Ryanair passengers will pass through our 22 UK airports, reflecting the strong demand for Ryanair's unbeatable low fares, unrivalled route network, and industry-leading reliability.

"As families, friends, and holidaymakers make the most of the long weekend, we look forward to welcoming them on board and delivering our busiest Bank Holiday weekend of the year across the UK."
