A man has denied two serious assault charges and other counts relating to the carrying of a bottle of ammonia in various parts of Spennymoor on the same day.

Brooklyn Knighton appeared at Durham Crown Court for a plea hearing, on Wednesday (August 20), via video link from HMP Doncaster, where he has been detained on remand.

The 20-year-old defendant denied two counts of causing grievous bodily harm to another man with intent, both on July 27, this year.

He also pleaded not guilty to three charges of having an offensive weapon in public, in each case a bottle of ammonia, also in July 27.

Those alleged offences were in Dundas Street and Baff Street, in Spennymoor, and on the A689, near the town.

Asked by Judge Richard Clews what the defendant’s case was, his counsel, Michael Forrest, said that it did not happen, it was not him and he has been wrongly identified.

A trial timetable was agreed in which the defendant must submit his signed defence statement to the court, setting out his full response to the allegations, by October 14.

The trial of up to three days was set for the court, starting on January 6.

Knighton, of St Andrews Road, Spennymoor, was remanded to remain in custody until the trial.
