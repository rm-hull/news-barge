BRADFORD cinema-goers have a packed Saturday of films, family entertainment and special screenings.

The listings cover Bradford Odeon and The Light, Bradford, with a wide selection of new releases, returning favourites and event cinema.

At Bradford Odeon, Spider-Man: Brand New Day is showing at 10.10am, 1.30pm, 2.30pm, 4.50pm, 5.50pm, 8.10pm, 9.10pm and 11.25pm.

The 12A film follows Peter Parker as he fights crime full-time in a world that does not remember him. It runs for two hours and 25 minutes. Adult tickets are from £12.50, while child tickets are from £7.11.

The Odyssey, rated 15, screens at 12.10pm, 3.50pm and 7.30pm. Christopher Nolan’s mythic action epic was shot across the world using brand new IMAX film technology.

The runtime is two hours and 53 minutes, with adult tickets from £12.50.

The Dog Stars is scheduled for 12pm, 2.50pm, 5.40pm, 8.30pm and 11.15pm. The 15-rated, one-hour-58-minute film is set after a virus wipes out humanity, with survivors facing roaming scavengers called Reapers. Adult tickets are from £12.50.

One Night Only, a 15-rated film lasting one hour and 42 minutes, screens at 12.50pm, 3.20pm, 5.50pm, 8.20pm and 11.10pm.

Its story asks what would happen if one messy night became the best thing that ever happened to someone. Adult tickets are from £12.50. The listing also says: “Win a Weekend Abroad!” and “Book Tickets by 30 August”.

Family options include The Super Mario Galaxy Movie at 9.50am. The PG animated film follows the world of Super Mario Bros.

Adult tickets are from £1. The Pout-Pout Fish screens at 10.30am and 11.45am, with adult tickets from £12.50 and child tickets from £7.11. Coyote vs. ACME is at 11.30am and 2.10pm, priced from £12.50 for adults and £7.11 for children.

Toy Story 5 is at 10.40am and 1.15pm, with adult tickets from £12.50 and child tickets from £7.11.

Minions & Monsters screens at 10.15am, with adult tickets from £12.50 and child tickets from £7.11. Moana is at 11.15am and 2pm, with the same adult and child prices. Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie screens at 10am and 12.15pm, also priced from £12.50 for adults and £7.11 for children.

Other Odeon screenings include Rise of the Footsoldier: Retribution at 6.50pm, 9.30pm and 11.30pm; Insidious: Out of the Further at 3.50pm, 6.20pm, 7.20pm, 8.50pm, 9.50pm and 11.20pm; and The End of Oak Street at 4.45pm. Each has adult tickets from £12.50.

The End of Oak Street also has child tickets from £7.11.

Spa Weekend is at 4.40pm, while Mutiny screens at 7.10pm and 9.40pm. Both are priced from £12.50 for adults.

Awarapan 2 is showing at 10.20am, 11.20am, 2.40pm, 5pm, 6pm, 8.15pm, 9.15pm and 10.50pm, with adult tickets from £12.50.

The CBeebies Musical: The Great Ice Cream Hunt is at 10.10am, with adult tickets from £1. GHOST: 2 Big To Rig screens at 7.50pm, with adult tickets from £15. Harry Potter & the Philosopher's Stone 25th Ann is at 1.40pm and 4.50pm, priced from £12.50 for adults and £7.11 for children.

La La Land (10th Anniversary Re-Release) screens at 2pm, with adult tickets from £10. The Fast and the Furious (25th Anniversary) is at 5pm and 10.40pm, also from £10.

Toxic: A Fairy Tale For Grown-ups (Hindi) screens at 1pm, 2.45pm and 8.40pm, while the Kannada version is at 10.45am. Adult tickets for both are from £12.50.

At The Light, Bradford, Spider-Man: Brand New Day is listed at 11.15am, 1.15pm, 2.30pm, 4.30pm and 7.45pm.

The Dog Stars is at 12pm, 2.50pm, 5.40pm and 8.30pm, while The Odyssey is at 3.30pm and 7.15pm. Ticket prices are not provided in the supplied listings.

The Light also lists One Night Only at 5.30pm and 8pm; Coyote vs. Acme at 9.45am, 12.20pm and 3pm; Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie at 11am; Minions & Monsters at 9.45am, 1.15pm and 3.20pm; and Toy Story 5 at 10.45am. No ticket prices are provided for these screenings.

Moana is at 12.30pm, while Tony screens at 8.20pm. Insidious: Out of the Further is at 5.45pm and 8.15pm, with the earlier screening captioned.

The End of Oak Street is at 5.50pm. Kiki's Delivery Service (Dubbed) is at 10am, alongside Wallace & Gromit: A Grand Day Out, also at 10am, marked Rewind and Toddler Time respectively. Ticket prices are not provided in the listings.
