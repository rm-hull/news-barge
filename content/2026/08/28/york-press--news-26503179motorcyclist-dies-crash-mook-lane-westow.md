## Motorcyclist dies after serious crash in North Yorkshire

A man was airlifted to hospital following the crash *(Image: PA)*

A MOTORCYCLIST has died following a serious crash in North Yorkshire.

The man was airlifted to hospital following the crash at 10.35am on Sunday, August 2 at Mook Street, near Westow.

Police have now confirmed that he has died after a short period in hospice care.

A force spokesman said: "This is an update following an earlier police appeal about a crash involving two men on motorbikes which happened on Mook Lane, near Westow, at around 10.35am on Sunday, August 2.

"The second rider remains in hospital with serious injuries.

  


"We urge anyone who may have seen either a yellow Yamaha motorcycle or a white Honda motorcycle in the area around this time and has not yet spoken to police, to make contact with us on the details below.

"If you can help, please email: Ruby Rutter on ruby.rutter@northyorkshire .police.uk or call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

"Please quote reference 12260148423 when passing on information."
