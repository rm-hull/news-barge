  **A YELLOW warning has been issued for thunderstorms and heavy rain over York today - with the forecast set to remain 'unsettled' over the bank holiday weekend.**

The Met Office has issued a warning until 9pm today (August 28).

A spokesperson said: "While many areas will miss the heaviest downpours, heavy showers and thunderstorms are expected to develop during Friday afternoon and evening.

“The focus of showers and thunderstorms over will be northern England and much of Wales during the early afternoon before extending over central and eastern England through the afternoon.

“Thunderstorms will bring 10-20 mm of rain in a short period in a few places with 30-40 mm possible should anywhere see repeated downpours.

“Lightning and hail will be additional hazards in a few locations. Heavy showers and thunderstorms will gradually ease during Friday evening.”

Today (August 28) will see showers developing more widely through the day, often heavy with a risk of thunder in places, but temperatures will remain around normal with a maximum of 21 degrees.

Tomorrow (August 29) will see showers ease off with late evening sunshine and mild weather, with Saturday (August 30) starting dying then rain will become widespread for the rest of the day.

From Sunday (August 31) to Tuesday (September 1), the unsettled weather looks set to continue with outbreaks of rain and showers – which the Met Office says will be “occasionally heavy with the risk of thunder”
