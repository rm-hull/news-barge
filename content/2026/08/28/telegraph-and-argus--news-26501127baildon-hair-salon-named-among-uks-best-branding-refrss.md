A HAIR salon has been named among the best in the UK for business branding.

One&One Hair Studio, based on Westgate in Baildon, placed 14th nationally for Best Business Branding in the 2026 UK Hair and Beauty Awards.

The salon also earned a spot in the Top 50 for both Best Salon Team and Best Apprentice.

One&One Hair Studio said: "We honestly couldn't be prouder of this achievement.

"To place 14th in the whole of the UK for Best Business Branding is something we never expected, especially alongside being recognised in the Top 50 for both Best Salon Team and Best Apprentice.

"There is so much work, time and passion that goes on behind the scenes to build a business and a team that you're proud of, so to have that recognised on a national level means so much to us.

"We're incredibly grateful to our amazing team, our clients and everyone who continues to support us.

"These awards are a reflection of so many people who have helped us get to where we are today."

The UK Hair and Beauty Awards recognise excellence across the industry, offering professionals the chance to have their work independently judged and celebrated on a national stage.

This year’s ceremony was held at the AO Arena in Manchester and brought together businesses and professionals from across the country.

One&One was recognised in three highly competitive categories, with the salon's 14th-place position for Best Business Branding being a particular highlight.

The studio offers a full range of services including cutting and styling, colouring, specialist treatments, hair extensions and mesh integration systems.

The salon prides itself on its personalised approach, relaxed atmosphere and experienced, qualified team.

Following its success at the awards, the team says it is focused on further growth and development.

The studio is looking forward to continuing to grow the business, develop its team and build on what has already been an incredible year.
