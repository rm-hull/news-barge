Four people have been arrested after a man suffered facial fractures in an incident involving a weapon.

Armed officers were called to Roseberry View in Thornaby around 9.15pm on Thursday (August 27) following a report of an altercation.

Four people - a 17-year-old boy, two men aged 39 and 43, and a 30-year-old woman - were arrested on suspicion of affray.

The adult men were also arrested on suspicion of wounding.

A 25-year-old man suffered facial fractures and was taken to hospital for treatment. He has since been released.
