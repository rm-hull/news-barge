A CAR has crashed into a wall in a suburb of York.

  


North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Tadcaster was called at 5.33pm yesterday to Cemetery Road in Fishergate.


A service spokesperson said all of the car's occupants were out on arrival and the scene was made safe.

Cemetery Road is a key through road between Foss Islands Road, Lawrence Street and Fulford.

Local residents reported a slight hold up to traffic in the area around 10pm yesterday as the vehicle was recovered.
