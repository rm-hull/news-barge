A new cafe is set to open in Clifton, York.

It was reported earlier this year that plans were lodged to transform the empty unit at 86 Clifton into The Curious Coffee Company café.

Those plans have now been approved as of this week, Wednesday 26 August.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/empty-former-boots-pharmacy-clifton-york.webp)

This will be the third location for The Curious Coffee Company, which already has cafés in Haxby and Easingwold.

The building at 86 Clifton was previously home to Boots, which closed in 2024 as part of the company’s plans to cut the amount of its high street pharmacies.

Two years on, the doors are set to reopen once again.

The Curious Coffee Company wrote on social media that they are “excited to grow the business, create new local jobs & bring a taste of Curious Coffee (and our brownies) one step closer to the City of York.

“We’ve got some big plans & ideas for events, but first, we need to transform the space.”

The new Clifton cafe will be open seven days a week, and will be serving all-day brunch, freshly crafted hot and cold sandwiches, tasty treats like French Toast, and their own Curious Brownies, which are handmade at their production bakery near Easingwold.

They will, of course, also be serving their own Curious Coffee, as well as a wider drinks menu including smoothies and milkshakes.

The Curious Coffee Company is owned and managed by Eddie and Jenny Copley-Farnell, and the first Curious Coffee shop opened just off The Green in Easingwold in 2013.
