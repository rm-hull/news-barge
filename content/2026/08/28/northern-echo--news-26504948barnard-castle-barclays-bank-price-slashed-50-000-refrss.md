A former Barclays branch in Barnard Castle has had its price slashed by £50,000.

The former bank on Barnard Castle's Market Place is now being marketed for £450,000 — down from its original £500,000 guide price — almost two years after Barclays closed the town branch.

Barclays closed the Barnard Castle site in September 2024, earlier than its previously announced January 2025 closure date.

The bank said footfall had fallen, with just 28 customers regularly using the branch for in-person banking services.

The closure led to public meetings and calls to retain face-to-face banking provision in the town.

A banking hub was announced in January 2025 and opened temporarily at The Witham Arts Centre in March that year.

The prominent two-storey stone building, opposite the Butter Market, includes the former banking hall, ancillary rooms and stores on the ground floor, plus a former first-floor apartment with its own rear access.

It also has a large gated car park at the rear, while free public parking is available in the Market Place and nearby Galgate.

## Sale or lease

The 6,215 sq ft building is being marketed by Connect Property North East with vacant possession.

On top of the revised freehold asking price of £450,000, the property is available to lease on a new full repairing and insuring agreement.

The agent is seeking a minimum five-year term at an annual rent of £55,000, excluding other costs.
