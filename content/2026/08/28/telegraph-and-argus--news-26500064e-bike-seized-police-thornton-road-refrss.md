A rider has been reported after being stopped on an e-bike in Bradford.

West Yorkshire Police said the rider was seen travelling along Thornton Road on a bike that did not meet the legal requirements for an electrically assisted pedal cycle (EAPC).

The rider also held no insurance, driving licence or vehicle registration documents.

The vehicle was promptly seized under S.165A Road Traffic Act 1988 with the rider reported for the relevant offences.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Seen riding on Thornton Road West Yorkshire Police - Bradford City for a significant distance without pedalling, with motor power being provided well in excess of the 6 km/h throttle-assist limit applicable to EAPCs.

"The vehicle therefore did not meet the requirements of an EAPC and was being used as a motor vehicle. The rider held no insurance, driving licence or vehicle registration documents.

"Vehicle seized under S.165A Road Traffic Act 1988 and rider reported for the relevant offences."
