A popular North East food reviewer insists that Billingham is home to the very best Chinese takeaways in the region.

Kyle Gibson, known as Kyle V Food and regularly shares reviews of restaurants, takeaways and food venues with thousands of followers on social media, recently returned to Lucky 88 Chinese Takeaway in Billingham to put its menu to the test.

In a video shared online, the viral man described the venue as “one of my favourite Chinese takeaways in Teesside".

Kyle, who boasts over 236,000 TikTok followers, also said that the ribs were "spot on" and claimed the "satay sauce was honestly the best."

He recommends the prawn toast, chicken skewer, and mixed sweet chilli with prawn, chicken and beef, which he dubs as "very, very nice".


Kyle also tried the roast duck in "sweet sauce" and dubbed it "absolutely perplexing".

He was also delighted to get a fortune cookie at the end.

One commenter wrote: "Round corner from me, will have to give it a try".

Another said: "You should try it with plum sauce with crispy chicken with chicken fried rice my favourite."

  A close up of Kyle trying prawn toast at Lucky 88. 

*(Image: Kyle-v-food on Instagram)*

Lucky 88 operates from The Stoney Oak on High Grange Avenue in Billingham and offers both collection and delivery services.

The takeaway serves a broad range of Chinese dishes, from familiar favourites such as sweet and sour chicken and chow mien to crispy duck, fried rice dishes and sizzling stir-fries.

According to the business, its menu has been designed to bring together a wide range of authentic Chinese flavours, with chefs preparing everything from traditional takeaway staples to the venue’s own speciality dishes.

The takeaway says customer service and speedy delivery are key parts of its offering.
