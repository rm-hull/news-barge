Passengers travelling through Newcastle International Airport can now enjoy a brand new seating area.

450 extra seats have been added to the departure lounge as part of a major investment programme aimed at improving the customer experience.

The additional seating has been created through extensions to Burger King and Tap + Brew, giving travellers more space to relax, dine and enjoy views across the airfield from the terminal’s floor-to-ceiling windows.

The latest developments are part of almost £60 million invested in airport facilities over the last few years, with a focus on enhancing capacity and delivering what the airport describes as a “world-class passenger experience”.

  The upgraded departure lounge at Newcastle International Airport offers extra seating, improved passenger facilities and views of aircraft movements on the airfield. 

*(Image: Newcastle International Airport)*

Alongside the new seating areas, an expanded security search area is now in operation during peak periods.

Airport bosses say the development is helping to ensure 99 per cent of passengers continue to pass through security in less than 10 minutes.

The airport’s international baggage hall has also been enlarged, with five baggage belts set to operate this summer to help speed up arrivals and improve passenger journeys.

The announcement was welcomed by travellers, with many praising the airport’s ongoing improvements.

  Passengers enjoy the airport's expanded departure lounge, featuring additional seating and panoramic floor-to-ceiling windows offering views across the airfield.
