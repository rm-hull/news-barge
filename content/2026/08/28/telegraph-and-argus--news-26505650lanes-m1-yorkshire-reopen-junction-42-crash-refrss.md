  *Traffic is now running normally on the southbound M1 between junctions 42 and 41 after a crash closed two lanes earlier this morning (Friday, August 28).*

The smash has closed two lanes of the West Yorkshire motorway this morning (Friday, August 28).

The lane closures are on the four-lane southbound M1 from junction 42 for Lofthouse, near Leeds, to junction 41 for the A650 and Carrgate.

Images from traffic cameras at junction 42 taken at 7.52am this morning show the extent of the delays.
