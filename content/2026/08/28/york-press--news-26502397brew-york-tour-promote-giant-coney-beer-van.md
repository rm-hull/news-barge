## Top York brewery on tour with beer van and new stronger ale

Friendly service from the van, plus spin the wheel. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A York brewery is taking its latest creation on tour in a beer van.

Brew York, which recently celebrated its tenth anniversary, is hitting the road to promote Giant Coney, a new 7.5 per cent double IPA.

The tour will visit several Brew York venues using the brewery’s customised Citroën Type H van, which features up to 12 beer lines and has previously appeared at events in London, Norwich, and Bristol.

Wayne Smith, co-founder and managing director at Brew York, said: "Although we love for people to come to our venues for the Brew York experience, we know it’s not always possible.

"Having our own beer van is a great marketing tool and means we can serve our product on our terms – meaning we look after the condition of the beer, cleanliness of the serving system and of course, pulling the final pint too."

The tour is part of the launch for Giant Coney, a stronger version of Brew York’s popular Coney hazy IPA.

  The Brew York beer van 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Mr Smith said: "It’s ideal for short-term solutions where we can control the beer condition.

"In this instance, it’s ideal to travel to some of our venues to launch our new beer – Giant Coney.

"The van will be acting as a showcase extension of our bar."

The original Coney IPA is named after both Coney Street in York and New York’s Coney Island, with the word "coney" also being an old English term for rabbit.

Lee Grabham, co-founder and production director, explained the reasoning behind a higher-strength version.

Mr Grabham said: "Standard Coney is a successful line for us in several supermarkets; however, research was showing that consumer habits were shifting slightly – meaning single cans of higher-strength beer was becoming more popular.

"We worked with our contacts to develop Giant Coney to meet this demand."

  A can of Giant Coney 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Giant Coney has an ABV of 7.5 per cent, compared to the original’s 4.5 per cent.

Mr Grabham said: "We’re a business so of course volume sales are of important, but we’re advocates of quality over quantity – and stronger products such as Giant Coney is a great example of that."

The new double IPA is brewed with Citra, Mosaic, and Simcoe hops.

It will be available on keg and in 440ml cans.

The beer van began visiting Brew York locations with Walmgate on Wednesday night, Osbaldwick last night; Towers Tap Room, Carlton on Friday 28th, 5pm to 8pm; Brew+ Goole Market Hall on Saturday 29th, 1pm to 6pm; and Horse & Farrier, Otley on Sunday 30th, 1pm to 6pm.
