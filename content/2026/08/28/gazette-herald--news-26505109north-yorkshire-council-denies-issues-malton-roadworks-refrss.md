NORTH Yorkshire Council has said current roadworks in a market town are not causing any 'issues'.

In a response to Cllr Keane Duncan, the authority said 'traffic is moving freely with no issues observed' despite the closure of Railway Street in Malton.

  The road is expected to be shut to traffic for two days while a new zebra crossing is installed.

  The one way system on Norton Road has been temporarily lifted while the work is carried out, however the closure has led to long queues in both Malton and Norton.

He said their response had been that every effort to communicate all the works on Railway Street was made.

They said clear diversion signage is in place and 'traffic is moving freely with no issues observed.'

The statement added: "A planned evening closure to Water Lane car park is likely to take place on Thursday 3rd and Friday 4th September, between 18:00 and 23:00

"This closure is due to an unavoidable change to the electrical connection point

"There have been delays to the scheme due to the Malton concrete plant being unable to provide the required material

"The works are being carried out in phased sections to maintain the safe flow of both vehicular traffic and pedestrians

  "The total cost of the scheme is not yet available The area is challenging for pedestrians and the crossing will positively improve access and safety, particularly for the elderly and people with disabilities."
