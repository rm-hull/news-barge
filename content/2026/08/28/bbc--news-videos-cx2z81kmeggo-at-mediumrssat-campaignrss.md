# Videos show people survive floodwaters on balcony in Nepal

A video has captured the moment a group of people stranded on a balcony in Nepal survived as powerful floodwaters rush around them.

It's one of several clips BBC Verify has located along the Trishuli River after a deadly cascade of water, mud and other debris flooded the area on Wednesday, as Merlyn Thomas reports.
