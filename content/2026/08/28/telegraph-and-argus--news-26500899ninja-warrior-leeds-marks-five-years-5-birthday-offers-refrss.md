NINJA Warrior UK Leeds is marking its fifth birthday with a weekend of £5 sessions and VIP meal deals.

The celebration takes place on Saturday, September 12, and Sunday, September 13, offering families and thrill-seekers the chance to enjoy an hour of action-packed fun for just £5.

Stephen Wilson, CEO of Oxygen Activeplay, which operates Ninja Warrior UK Leeds, said: "It’s Ninja Warrior UK Leeds’ 5th birthday, and all ninjas, big and small, are invited to join the party and help us celebrate this special milestone.

"All weekend long, we’re offering £5 sessions and £5 VIP meal deals, so taking on the challenge is more accessible than ever."

The indoor adventure park features the iconic Warped Wall, Flying Squirrel and Spider Wall, plus a giant inflatable course.

Visitors can also enjoy a £5 VIP meal deal that includes a kids’ meal, slushie, and ice cream or fruit bag.

Two-for-one tickets are available every Tuesday during term time, and two-hour toddler sessions run Monday to Friday from 10am to 12pm for just £9, including a hot drink for parents.

For more information or to book, visit ninjawarrioruk.co.uk/leeds.

Updates can also be found on Instagram and Facebook.
