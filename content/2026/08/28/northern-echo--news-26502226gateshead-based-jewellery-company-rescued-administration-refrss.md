A jewellery company based in the North East has been sold after it entered administration, saving all jobs at risk.

Roxoa Group Ltd, the parent company of online brands QP Jewellers, Ruby & Oscar, and Gold Boutique, entered administration on August 13 following financial difficulties linked to an internal efficiency project that led to overspending.

The business, which specialises in fine gemstone and diamond jewellery, was sold in a pre-pack deal on the same day to Roxoa Holdings Limited.

Iain Nairn, joint administrator, said: "This was a highly credible, well-run business facing short-term challenges as it looked to improve trading efficiency.

"We are delighted to have secured fresh investment from experienced investors at just the right time.

"This will allow the company to continue to serve its customers, maintain continuity under its trusted brands and preserve a valued workforce in Gateshead."

The overspend left the company reliant on external finance, and the cost of borrowing became unsustainable.

Two investors acquired the trade and assets of all four companies through the newly formed Roxoa Holdings Limited.

Bail & Stone Ltd, another online jewellery brand under the group, remains unaffected by the administration.

Support on valuations came from Cerberus Receivables Management and The Advisory Group Ltd, while Clark Mairs LLP acted as solicitors for the buyers.

The company’s Gateshead headquarters will continue to operate under the new ownership, and all 17 jobs have been retained as part of the deal.
