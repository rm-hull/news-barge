A MAN in his 70s has died after a crash on the outskirts of York.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage after a fatal crash that took place on York Road, approaching Sutton-on-the-Forest, earlier this month.

It happened at approximately 3.15pm on August 12 and involved a red Vauxhall Astra and a silver Audi.

  


-

- 
    **Roads near Yorkshire landmark closed after crash - emergency services at scene**

The road was closed for several hours whilst police investigated the scene and recovered the cars.

“Sadly, following a period of time in hospital for treatment to minor injuries, the driver of the red Astra, a man in his 70s, has passed away,” said a spokesperson for the force.

They added that the driver of the Audi has assisted officers with their enquiries.

If you have any information that could assist the investigation including any footage or if you witnessed the incident, please contact TC 1142 UPTON on [Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk), use the website or live chat, or call 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Please quote reference 12260156472 when providing information.
