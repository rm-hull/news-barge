## Ukrainians gather at York Minster to mark 35 years of independence

York marked Ukrainian Independence Day with a public show of solidarity and support at York Minster *(Image: Office of Rachael Maskell MP)*

York marked Ukrainian Independence Day with a public show of solidarity and support.

Ukrainians in York gathered outside York Minster on Monday, August 24, where they handed out sunflowers, sang songs, and shared their hopes for the future.

The city walls were illuminated in yellow and blue that evening to mark the 35th anniversary of Ukraine’s independence.

Rachael Maskell, MP for York Central, said: "It was poignant to stand with York’s Ukrainian community on the 35th anniversary of their independence."

Rebecca Russell of York City of Sanctuary added: "This gathering reminds us that the war continues and we must work towards peace."

Maryana Mukha from the York Ukrainian Society said: "The Ukrainian nation remains strong and resolute thanks to all the unwavering support."

Councillor Claire Douglas, leader of City of York Council, said: "This event highlighted the importance of coming together, especially given the number of Ukrainians displaced since Russia’s invasion of Ukraine in 2022."
