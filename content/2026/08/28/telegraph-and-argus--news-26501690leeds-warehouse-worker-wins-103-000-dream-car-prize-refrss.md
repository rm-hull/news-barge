A WAREHOUSE worker has finally landed a life-changing win after years of playing a dream car competition.

Kieran Chandler, 36, from Leeds, won a £53,000 Land Rover Discovery Sport D200 Metropolitan and £50,000 in cash through BOTB after entering the competition since 2018.

He said: "It’s amazing and I’m very happy.

"I’ve never won anything this big before.

"I chose a family sized car because I like going on day trips with all the family."

He learned of his win when BOTB presenter Nicole Jeffery arrived at his home to deliver the surprise.

She then led him down the road to where the car was parked.

Ms Jeffery said: "Kieran’s reaction was brilliant because you could see that he was genuinely stunned when he first saw the car.

"He’s been playing since 2018 and has come close to winning before, so to finally be able to turn up and tell him that he’d won not only a fantastic Land Rover but £50,000 in cash was a really special moment."

Mr Chandler now plans to use the money to treat his family.

He said: "The money is definitely life-changing.

"I will be treating my family in some way or another, like a holiday."
