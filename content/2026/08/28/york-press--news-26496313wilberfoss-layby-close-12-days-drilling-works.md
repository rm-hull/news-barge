A layby in Wilberfoss is expected to be closed for 12 days for directional drilling and service connection works.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has announced the temporary closure of the layby in front of Eastfield House.

The closure is set to begin on September 21, 2026 at 8am and will last until October 2, 2026 at 6pm.

The closure, between both junctions on the A1079 Hull Road, is necessary for directional drilling approximately 350 metres in the carriageway, along with service connection works.

The council has stated that the road closure is essential to ensure the safety of the public during the works.

Although the order for closure is valid for 18 months, the works are expected to be completed in 12 days.

During the closure, an alternative route will be available.

The diversion will be via A1079 Hull Road, Newton on Derwent CP, Wilberfoss CP, and Pocklington Provincial Ward, with signs posted at the time of closure.

The road will be closed to all through traffic, but access will be maintained for residents, emergency vehicles, and pedestrians.

For further information, contact the council's Highways Customer Care at 01482 393939, reference CB.
