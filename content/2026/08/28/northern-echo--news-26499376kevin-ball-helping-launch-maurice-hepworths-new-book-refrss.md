A former footballer is launching a new book with the support of a Sunderland legend.

Maurice Hepworth, who played for Darlington during a loan spell from Sunderland in 1975, will launch Add To You – Lessons from a Life of Football, Family and Faith at St George’s Church in Harraton, Washington, on September 13.

Sunderland legend Kevin Ball will join him as a special guest speaker.

Mr Hepworth said: "Kevin is absolutely perfect to help launch the book.

"He is a superb leader and a hugely inspirational human being, and his career at Sunderland speaks for itself.

"He knows what it means to lead, to overcome challenges and to inspire people around him.

"I know the evening is going to be very special, with so many inspiring moments to recall and share.

"For me, having Kevin there makes the launch even more meaningful, and I’m really looking forward to welcoming everyone along and sharing the story behind the book."

The book is described as a "searingly honest" account of a life shaped by football, family, faith and adversity.

It includes reflections on Mr Hepworth’s work in leadership and his guidance of young players at top clubs, including a mention of England and former Sunderland goalkeeper Jordan Pickford.

The event will run from 7pm to 9pm and will include discussions about the book, stories from Mr Hepworth’s career, and the chance for attendees to meet both him and Mr Ball.

Mr Hepworth hopes the launch will resonate with people from across the region.

He said: "This is much more than a book launch.

"It’s an opportunity to share experiences, memories and lessons learned throughout life.

"If people leave feeling encouraged, inspired or challenged to look at life differently, then the book will have achieved exactly what I hoped it would."
