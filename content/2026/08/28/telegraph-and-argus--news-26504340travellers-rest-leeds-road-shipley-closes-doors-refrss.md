A BRADFORD district pub appears to have closed its doors.

Travellers Rest, located on Leeds Road in Shipley, announced its closure on social media, thanking customers and reflecting on a decade of memories.

In a Facebook post, the team said: "And after a stressful couple of days our little pub is empty – sad day.

"Thank you to our friends who helped us with the clear out.

"And now the next chapter begins.

  The Travellers Rest, on Leeds Road, Shipley


"Thank you to our amazing Travs family for supporting us through the 10 years we have been here.

"Love you all."

## 'The jungle drums of Shipley'

Staff said saying goodbye was difficult as the pub prepared to close.

The post continued: "So, as you probably know, our lovely little pub will be closing next week.

"To be honest, the jungle drums of Shipley have had us closing for the last 10 years.

"Anyway, it has been so lovely to see so many faces who have come to say goodbye to us – saying see you later has broken me today, so over the next few days I will be a complete mess."
