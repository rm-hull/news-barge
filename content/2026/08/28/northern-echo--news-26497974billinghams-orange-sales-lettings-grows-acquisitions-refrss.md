An award-winning sales and lettings agency is expanding across the North East after a string of acquisitions helped grow its workforce and widen its presence across the region.

Orange Sales and Lettings, owned by David Lamb, has purchased a premises on Belasis Avenue in Billingham, as well as an office in Sunderland.

So far, Mr Lamb has acquired six lettings agencies across the North East, with more recent purchases in Hartlepool and Sunderland.

  David Lamb, presenting at a meeting of property developers 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Mr Lamb said: "People didn’t know I worked at the steelworks and on the rigs, as, if you’ve got a large portfolio of properties, people just assume you’ve come from money.

"I’m just a lad from Billingham and I’m not very good at looking back and appreciating what I’ve done, but when you’re talking, you’re reflecting and it’s a bit like journaling – it helps you mentally."

The acquisitions have grown his team to 12 agents, including four members from one of the newly acquired businesses.

Orange Sales and Lettings, based in Billingham, also contracts a network of tradespeople for property repairs and refurbishments.

Mr Lamb’s journey into property began after a career in the steel and offshore industries, investing earnings into real estate.

He said his interest in property began at an early age, inspired by his father’s work.

Mr Lamb said: "It had always been an interest – my Dad had a painting and decorating business and I used to go on building sites with him, and as a kid I would stop and stare at houses asking questions like 'how do the windows get put in?' and 'what’s that layer between the bricks?'

"My Mam used to think it was so bizarre that I was so interested in how houses were built – I think it’s just in my blood and I don’t think I was ever going to do anything else."

He left school with just "three or four" GCSEs and dropped out of a college business course, but said he wants young people to know that exam results are not the only path to success.

Mr Lamb credits his time at the steelworks for motivating him to invest in property.

He said: "I worked with 40 blokes all saying, 'I wish I’d bought property when I was younger,' so I decided that’s what I’d do."

Mr Lamb said: "No other lettings agency owner has got tens of properties themselves and while there’s some brilliant agencies out there, who we can learn a lot from, they don’t understand what landlords go through, because they are not going through it themselves.

"We have experienced strong organic growth as a result – because I think people buy into the fact that they can see me developing buildings and buying houses.

"I want to be the biggest and the best in the region – and we may look at other regions further down the line."

David and the Orange team were recently named Property Manager of the Year at the Blue Bricks Magazine Property Awards.
