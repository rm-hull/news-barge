THE Met Office has forecast a mix of rain and sunshine for Bradford this weekend.

Saturday will start off partly cloudy, with temperatures around 16C.

Sunny intervals are expected from 12pm, but the sky will become overcast by 2pm.

Light rain is expected from 4pm, with temperatures peaking at 18C.

The rain will become heavier by 9pm, with a 70 per cent chance of precipitation.

The rain will ease off by 11pm, with light showers continuing.

Sunday will start off cloudy, with temperatures around 16C.

Sunny intervals are expected from 6am, with temperatures gradually rising to 20C by 3pm.

Light showers are expected from 7pm, with a 60 per cent chance of precipitation.

The rain will continue into the evening, with temperatures dropping to 16C by midnight.
