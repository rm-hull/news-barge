A CARE home hosted a dog show to mark National Dog Day.

The Manor House Roundhay in Leeds, part of Anchor, held its Paws & Applause event to raise funds for Dogs Trust Leeds and bring together residents, families and members of the community for a day of canine fun.

Guests enjoyed games, treats and the opportunity to meet a variety of dogs, with residents taking part in judging categories such as Prettiest Pup and Waggiest Tail.

Sharna Neal, home manager at The Manor House Roundhay, said: "Days like this mean so much to us at The Manor House Roundhay.

"The event created so many smiles and memorable moments for our residents.

"It was lovely to see them enjoying time with the dogs and sharing their own memories, and knowing we were doing it all for such a great cause made the day even more special."

Many residents shared fond memories of their own pets.
