# Yorkshire weather: Hour by hour weather forecast for York, Leeds, Sheffield, Bradford and Hull ahead of bank holiday weekend

## Amid thunderstorm warnings, here is an hourly breakdown of the weather forecast for Yorkshire ahead of the bank holiday weekend.

While many areas will be missing the heaviest downpours, heavy showers and thunderstorms are predicted to develop this afternoon and evening (August 28) across the region.

The focus of showers and thunderstorms will be occurring across northern England and most of Wales during the early afternoon before extending over central and eastern England through the afternoon.

Thunderstorms will bring 10-20mm of rain in a short period in a few places with 30-40mm possible should anywhere see repeated downpours.
