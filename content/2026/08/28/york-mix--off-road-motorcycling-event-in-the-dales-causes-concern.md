**There are proposals to reduce the impact of an off-road motorcycling event, which attracts bikers from across the globe, in the Yorkshire Dales.**

Approximately 200 riders take part in the Scott Trial, which spans 130km (81 miles) and attracts some of the best trail bike riders in the world.

Oganisers had initially wanted either a five-year extension to their planning permission terms, or consent for the trial to continue permanently. However, that has changed as they want temporary consent to keep the event going.

The Yorkshire Dales National Park Authority has asked for a conservation management plan alongside any future proposal for the trial to be permanent.

The Scott Trial, which has been run by Richmond Motor Club since 1991, and which is held on ancient woodland, will celebrate its 100th event this October.

It also runs close to numerous archaeological features and scheduled monuments.

Historic England wants “micro-rerouting,” to avoid sensitive features including structural remains, earthworks and unstable spoil heaps.
