A new beer festival starting today (Fri) is raising a glass to Yorkshire’s brewing talent.

The event, called Waggonfest, is taking place at the Waggon and Horses pub on Lawrence Street in York, running through the bank holiday weekend.

The festival features beers from Brew York, Trig Brewery, Daleside Brewery, Ilkley Brewery, Rudgate Brewery and Rooster's Brewing Co, plus a special barrel of Bass.

Karen Crombie, landlady at the Waggon and Horses, said: "We are all excited for our first beer festival over the Bank Holiday weekend.

"It is great to support and showcase the excellent local breweries across Yorkshire and with the range of styles on offer there is sure to be a beer for everyone."

Waggonfest also includes a barbecue and live music on Sunday, featuring performances from Josh Gandy and Alistair Griffin, who has two top twenty hit singles and a top twenty album.

The pub is also taking part in York CAMRA's Ale Trail, which concludes on Monday, offering visitors a final chance to collect stamps.
