A man in his 60s has been taken to hospital after a crash on a County Durham road.

Emergency services were called to the one-vehicle crash on Newhouse Road, Esh Winning, around 4.25pm on Thursday (August 27).

Pictures shared by Northern Perspectives show several police and ambulance crews parked at the scene.

  Emergency servcies at the scene on Newhouse Road, Esh Winning. 

*(Image: NORTHERN PERSPECTIVES)*

A man in his 60s received medical attention and was taken to the Freeman Hospital for treatment, Durham Police confirmed.

The extent of his injuries and current condition have not been confirmed.

The North East Ambulance Service has been contacted for comment.
