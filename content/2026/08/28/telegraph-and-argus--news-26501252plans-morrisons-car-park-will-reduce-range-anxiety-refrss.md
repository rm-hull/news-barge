A NEW EV charging facility at a Morrisons supermarket will help reduce motorists’ “range anxiety.”

A planning application to create EV charging bays and a car wash at the Enterprise 5 retail park, anchored by Morrisons, has been approved by Bradford Council.

Submitted by the Motor Fuel Group, the plans are for the creation of an EV charging zone with EV canopy, the installation of jet wash bays, and the creation of air/vac bays in existing car park space.

The facility will create six EV charging bays, but will lead to the loss of 42 existing parking spaces.

The application details why the introduction of charging bays in this part of Bradford is so important.

It says: “The latest figures (as of 1 April) shows that there were 92,141 public electric vehicle charging devices installed in the UK, an increase of just 4,345 since January.

“At the current pace, just 116,196 would be installed by 2030, with the target of 300,000 not being met until 30 years later.

"To meet the target in time, 13,263 chargers would need to be installed each quarter between now and 2030 - approximately a 650 per cent increase over build rate.

“At present, it is understood that the uneven spread of EV charging facilities significantly hinders the take-up of electric vehicles, due to potential servicing constraints.

"En-route charging for longer journeys is deemed crucial to support the switch to EV and reduce concerns about charge 'range anxiety'.

“The proposal to install new EV charging points at the Morrisons site should be considered positively.”

The application was approved by planning officers this month.
