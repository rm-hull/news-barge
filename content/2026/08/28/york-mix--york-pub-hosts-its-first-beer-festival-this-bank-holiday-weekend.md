Cheers! There’s a new beer festival in town.

The Waggon and Horses on Lawrence Street have launched their inaugural beer fest ‘Waggonfest’ **today, Friday 28 August**.

The beer festival will continue over the bank holiday weekend, pairing good local beer with a barbecue as well as live performances on Sunday.

Waggonfest will be serving a selection of beers from Brew York, Trig Brewery, Daleside Brewery, Ilkley Brewery, Rudgate Brewery, and Rooster’s Brewing Co. From further afield, there will also be a much sought after barrel of Bass beer being served.

Music on Sunday will include local talents Alistair Griffin and Josh Gandy.

The pub is also on York CAMRA’s Ale Trail, which concludes on Monday, offering the perfect opportunity to collect last minute stamps.

Speaking about Waggonfest, landlady Karen Crombie said:

“We are all excited for our first beer festival over the Bank Holiday weekend. When I took over last year I knew I wanted to do something different and it is great that the first day of our festival has arrived.

“It is great to support and showcase the excellent local breweries across Yorkshire and with the range of styles on offer there is sure to be a beer for everyone!”
