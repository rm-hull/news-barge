**Harrogate MP Tom Gordon has announced a dual fundraising initiative for St Michael’s Hospice, combining an upcoming parliamentary coffee morning with his fourth consecutive appearance at the Great North Run.**

The MP for Harrogate and Knaresborough will host the latest instalment of his “Talk with Tom” series on **Friday 4 September at 10:00 AM**. The event will focus explicitly on palliative care, offering constituents a chance to discuss Gordon’s ongoing work in Westminster to secure increased funding for end-of-life care and local hospice providers.

Following the coffee morning, Gordon will lace up his running shoes on **Sunday 14 September** to tackle the Great North Run. Having successfully completed the half-marathon for St Michael’s Hospice in 2023, 2024, and 2025—raising more than £1,000 to date—the MP has set a fresh fundraising target of £500 for his 2026 campaign.


**Tom Gordon said:**

St Michael’s Hospice provides invaluable, compassionate care to families across our community during some of their most difficult moments.

Securing better funding for palliative care is a major priority for me in Parliament, and I’m proud to support their vital work both in Westminster and on the running track.



![Tom Gordon Saint Michaels Hospice](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/tom-gordon-hospice.webp)


The palliative care coffee morning is free to attend, though constituents are asked to register in advance.

- **To register for the coffee morning:** Sign up via the official attendance form.
- **To support the half-marathon campaign:** Donations can be made directly through Tom Gordon’s JustGiving Page.
