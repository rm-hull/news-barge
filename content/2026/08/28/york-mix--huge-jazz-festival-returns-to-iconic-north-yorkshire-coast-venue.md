Experience a spectacular celebration of live jazz this September – as the Scarborough Jazz Festival returns to the iconic Scarborough Spa.

Music lovers from across Yorkshire and beyond can look forward to three days of world-class performances, unforgettable talent, and vibrant musical energy.

Now in its 23rd year, the Scarborough Jazz Festival will once again bring together an exceptional line-up of acclaimed musicians, emerging artists, and jazz enthusiasts, from **Friday 25 to Sunday 27 September**.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Scarborough-Spa-1000x540.png)

From traditional swing and big band classics to contemporary jazz, blues-infused performances and innovative new sounds, the festival offers something for every music fan.

Festival organisers are encouraging both dedicated jazz followers and newcomers alike to attend this year’s event, which promises to showcase the diversity, creativity and enduring appeal of jazz music.

“The Scarborough Jazz Festival has become a much-loved event that brings together artists and audiences from across the country,” said festival director, Mark Gordon.

“We’re delighted to welcome an incredible programme of performers to Scarborough Spa this September and look forward to celebrating the power of live Jazz in such a fantastic venue.”
