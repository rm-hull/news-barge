## York bin collections this bank holiday weekend and what tip visitors should know

York recycling collections *(Image: City of York Council)*

York Council has confirmed what is happening with bin collections this bank holiday weekend.

Collections of household rubbish and recycling will be unaffected by the bank holiday on Monday, August 31.

Homes which would normally have their waste collected on Mondays will have it taken away as normal.

A council spokesperson said anyone planning a trip to York’s Hazel Court or Towthorpe tips should be aware that they are likely to be busier than usual.

People planning on going to Hazel Court, in James Street, should also remember to book an appointment prior to arriving.

Slots of 10 minutes can be booked on the council’s website here.

  York recycling boxes 

*(Image: City of York Council)*

Online bookings require an address, details of the waste being disposed of, the vehicle being used to transport it and of any pre-arranged permits such as for commercial waste.

People planning on visiting the Towthorpe tip, in Towthorpe Moor Lane, are encouraged to come at quieter times such as early in the morning or later in the afternoon.

Hazel Court and Towthorpe are open from 8.30am to 6pm, with the last entry 15 minutes before closing.

Both sites are closed on Wednesdays.
