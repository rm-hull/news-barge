TIPS across the Bradford district will shut on Monday.

Bradford Council has confirmed its five sites will be closed for the bank holiday.

They will reopen on Tuesday.

The affected centres are located at Bowling Back Lane, Keighley, Dowley Gap, Midland Road, and Dealburn Road.

A spokesperson for Bradford Council said: "Please be aware that all 5 of our Household Waste Recycling Centres will be closed on Bank Holiday Monday 31 August and will reopen on Tuesday 1 September.

"We apologise for any inconvenience."
