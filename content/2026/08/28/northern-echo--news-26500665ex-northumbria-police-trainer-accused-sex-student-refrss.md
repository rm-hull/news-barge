A former police trainer is accused of having inappropriate relationships with student officers and breaching Covid lockdown rules by taking one home for sex.

Former Northumbria PC Peter Miller is accused of abusing his position as a student trainer between September 2020 and January 2021.

It is alleged her had sex with student officers, made inappropriate comments, and sent messages over social media to pursue inappropriate contact.

It is also said he breached Covid lockdown rules in December 2020 by having sex with a student officer at his home, breaking the tier three rules in place at the time.

Those rules stated: "No person may participate in a gathering in the tier three area which consists of two or more people and takes place in a private dwelling or in any indoor space."

Former PC Miller is alleged to have breached the Standards of Professional Behaviour covering Authority, Respect and Courtesy, Discreditable Conduct and Orders and Instructions.

The allegations, if proven, are said to amount to gross misconduct and could justify dismissal.

The two-day hearing will start on September 3.

It will take place at the Old Magistrates Court at Houghton Police Station, Dairy Lane, Houghton Le Spring, DH4 5BL.
