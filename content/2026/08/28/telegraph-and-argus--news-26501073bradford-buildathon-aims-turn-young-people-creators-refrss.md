YOUNG people are being given the tools to become tech creators, not just consumers.

Bradford Buildathon 2026, taking place on Sunday, August 30, aims to close the youth employment gap by equipping attendees with digital skills through hands-on app development.

Using Lovable, a no-code AI platform, participants will build apps without needing any prior coding experience.

Nire Adetimehin, product marketing manager and organiser of the event, said: "You do not need a four-year software engineering degree to build products.

"Bradford has a large young population full of creative and ambitious young people.

"Buildathon is developing their tech skills and giving them access to AI tools, so they can move from being mere consumers of digital products to creators who lead innovation."

Attendees will work in teams during an intensive app-building sprint and present their ideas to a panel of tech professionals.

Judges include Nelson Uzenabor, AI consultant at Multiverse, Oluwatimilehin Oni, test manager at the NHS, and Mr Adetimehin.

The event is free, open to all, and designed to help young people pursue careers in technology.

Each participant will leave with a finished app, new skills in prompt engineering, a certificate of participation, and an expanded professional network.
