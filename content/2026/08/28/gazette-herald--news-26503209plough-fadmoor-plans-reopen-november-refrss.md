A VILLAGE pub made famous by a tv show with comedian Jon Richardson is planning to re-open in November.

The timing is revealed in an August Supporter and Investor update published on the pub's website earlier this week.

The update details how The Plough at Fadmoor, near Kirkbymoorside, is undergoing a transformation in preparation for reopening as a 'wet bar' serving drinks and snacks.

The project, involving builders, joiners, plumbers, and electricians, has seen significant progress in the past month.

Most of the windows have been installed, making the building watertight.

This has allowed for insulation and fire-boarding to be added to the ceiling of the bar.

Beams are being put back into the bar, while work continues on the walls, bar construction, and floor tiling.

Renovations are also ongoing in the new toilet block and the cellar to ensure they are ready for opening.

The pub's renovation is being carried out on a limited budget, the update continued.

The initial focus is to get the bar area ready for reopening in November, with additional funds to be raised later for further refurbishment.

Future plans include renovating other areas of the building, creating a fully functioning kitchen, developing the garden space, and completing work on the exterior of the building and outbuildings.

Updates on the progress and the exact opening day will be communicated to the public, the update explained.

The update also notes the pub's renovation has also attracted the attention of Channel 4, with filming ongoing for the second series of 'Our Yorkshire Pub Rescue with Jon Richardson.'

The filming timeline has been extended due to unforeseen complexities in the build process.

The film company aims to deliver all edited footage for four episodes to Channel 4 towards the end of the year.

The screening date is yet to be confirmed.

The pub's renovation project has been supported by community events such as the second Beer Festival held on July 10 and 11.

The festival was a success, raising £13,000, all of which will go towards the pub's renovation.

The event was supported by sponsors and volunteers.

Another successful fundraising event was the Pedal! Pie! Pint! @ The Plough cycle sportive held on May 9, which raised £5,000.

The event saw 200 cyclists participate despite challenging weather conditions.

Due to positive feedback, the event is likely to be held again next year, possibly later in May for better weather.

The Plough also supported the Kirkbymoorside 10K held on May 3, providing a BBQ and music for spectators in Fadmoor.

The pub's annual members meeting is scheduled for Thursday, September 24, at 7.30pm to 9.30pm at Fadmoor Village Hall.

Details about the meeting and the process for joining the committee have already been shared with investors, the update badded.
