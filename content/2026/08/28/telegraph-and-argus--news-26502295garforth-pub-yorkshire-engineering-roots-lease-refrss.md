A PUB with a story rooted in Yorkshire’s engineering past has been put up for lease.

The Podger in Garforth, built by Tetley Brewery in the 1950s, is being marketed by leisure property specialists Fleurets.

Its name is a nod to the "podger spanner," a tool used in steelwork and construction.

Simon Hall, director and head of agency north at Fleurets, said: "While the business is currently operated as a managed house by Mitchells & Butlers under the 'Sizzling' brand, we believe there is great opportunity for a hands-on tenant operator to build on the existing trade and further develop sales through a more independent approach."

The pub features an open-plan bar and lounge with seating for 96, a pool room, a well-fitted catering kitchen and three-bedroom private accommodation.

Outside, there is parking for around 35 cars, a front patio and a fenced rear garden with space for 14 picnic benches.

The leasehold is being offered on a tied lease at a guide rent of £40,000 per year.

Viewings are strictly by appointment through the Fleurets North Office.

For more information or to arrange a viewing, contact Fleurets at 0113 234 0304 or email [northwest@fleurets.com.](mailto:northwest@fleurets.com.)
