People are being invited to find out about a new wave of community projects launching to shape the future of Scarborough.

The work falls under the Pride in Place scheme and will be discussed at a public event taking place at The Street on Friday, September 11, featuring presentation sessions at 5.30pm, 6.15pm, and 7pm.

Each session will offer an overview of the programme, the board’s role, public engagement so far, and updates on projects entering the delivery phase.

Heath Samples, Chair of the Scarborough Neighbourhood Board, said: "Pride in Place is a long-term programme for Scarborough and it is important that people can see what is happening, understand how decisions are being made and continue to have opportunities to get involved.

"This event is a chance to hear where we are now, find out more about the projects beginning to move forward and, importantly, ask questions directly of the people involved."

Residents will have the opportunity to speak with board members and project representatives, ask questions, and learn more about ways to get involved.

Attendance is free, but advance booking is required.

Presentations will be recorded and made available online for those unable to attend.

To book a place, visit scarborough-pride-in-place.commonplace.is
