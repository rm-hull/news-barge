A major event at a Middlesbrough nightclub tonight has been cancelled at short notice.

The Prohibition Bar, on Corporation Road, announced that Tom Zanetti's 'Up Close & Personal' set, due to take place on Friday (August 28), will no longer go ahead.

In a statement posted on social media, the venue said the cancellation was due to last-minute "scheduling issues."

Prohibition Bar said: "With regret, we would like to inform you that our Tom Zanetti event planned for tomorrow evening has had to be cancelled.

"Due to scheduling issues and after in-depth discussions with Tom's management yesterday, we were unable to reach a conclusion that would guarantee his appearance at Prohibition at the time originally arranged.

"As a result, the only option available was to cancel this event."

  Prohibition Bar, Middlesbrough, has announced that the Zanetti event is cancelled, and that staff will be given the night off. 

*(Image: The Prohibition Bar)*

"We are devo-ed," commented one.

The venue confirmed all Ticket Source customers had been refunded and said it would be closed for the night in light of the cancellation to give staff a night off.

Zanetti, originally from Leeds but now living primarily in Marbella, is back in the UK for a series of appearances and performances.

Earlier this week, he attended the premiere of Rise of the Footsoldier: Retribution in Leicester Square, London.

Following the cancellation of the Middlesbrough event, the DJ and music producer is now scheduled to perform at Harlow Town Show in Essex tonight.

The cancellation comes just weeks after Prohibition warned customers attending the event that anyone caught taking drugs on the premises would face an immediate ban and could be reported to police.

No further details have been given about the scheduling issues, and there is currently no indication that the event will be rescheduled.

The Northern Echo has reached out to Tom Zanetti's team for comment.
