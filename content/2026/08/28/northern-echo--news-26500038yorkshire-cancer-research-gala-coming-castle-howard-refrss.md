A gala dinner is set to return to Castle Howard in 2027 in support of cancer research.

The event, titled A Night Like No Other, is organised by Yorkshire Cancer Research and will take place in March 2027.

It follows the success of the charity’s inaugural gala, held during its centenary year.

The event is hosted by the Hon Nicholas and Victoria Howard, who have opened their home to raise funds for research across Yorkshire.

Mr Howard, owner of Castle Howard, said: "Cancer has touched our family, as it has so many others, and it means a great deal to us to support the charity's pioneering work."

The evening will include a reception beneath Castle Howard’s iconic dome, followed by dinner in the Long Gallery, entertainment, and a fundraising auction.

Victoria Howard, who was diagnosed with breast cancer in 2020 and later discovered she carries a BRCA gene variant, said: "I'm hugely supportive of cancer research in all its guises and I'm proud to support Yorkshire's cancer charity.

"While my husband and I have both benefitted from the enormous strides being made in cancer treatment, the ultimate goal must be the prevention of this terrible disease happening in the first place."

Briony Harford, regional director for Yorkshire and the North East at Bonhams, will serve as auctioneer.

She said: "Yorkshire has absolutely stolen my heart.

"It’s fundamental that we fund research that can best support people with cancer across this incredible region."

The evening will feature a five-course Michelin-star tasting menu by chef Andrew Pern of the Star Inn at Harome.

Charlotte Lloyd-Webber, artistic director of CLW Event Design, will return to design the event’s interiors.

Neil Preston, director of fundraising and supporter experience at Yorkshire Cancer Research, said: "By attending, guests will play a vital role in helping to create a future where more cancers are detected early."
