A Grammy-nominated Nashville singer-songwriter will return to County Durham very soon.

Jeremy McComb will perform at Steam Machine Brewing Company in Newton Aycliffe on Monday, September 14, after selling out the venue during his first appearance there in 2025.

The Idaho-born country artist's career has seen him earn a Grammy nomination and share stages with some of the biggest names in country music.

McComb was so impressed by the intimate atmosphere at the brewery that he personally added it to a list of places he wanted to revisit.

His return show will once again feature support from multi-award-winning country singer Gary Quinn.

The performance forms part of a mini-UK tour and will be McComb’s second visit to Britain this year, having headlined the 10th anniversary of the Buckle & Boots Country Festival near Manchester in May.

It is expected to be his final UK appearance until at least 2028.

  Jeremy McComb at Steam Machine Brewing Company in 2025. 

*(Image: Art of Noise Photography)*

A-100-strong crowd packed into the Newton Aycliffe brewery for his previous performance, which sold out and drew praise from both fans and the musician himself.

Speaking about his return, the Nashville-based artist said: “I think it’s one of those places that it’s kind of built for that situation; it felt like it was really intimate.”

He added: "Everybody’s close up, everybody had a great time, and you could hear amazing in there, and even though it wasn’t necessarily set up as a listening room per se, it certainly acts as one.”

Gulen Smith, co-owner of Steam Machine Brewing Company, said: “It’s not every day a Nashville singer and songwriter turns up to play at a local brewery in Newton Aycliffe; his last show sold out here and it was a brilliant night.”

He continued: “Opportunities to see an artist like Jeremy this close to home don’t come around very often.”

Tickets start from £10 and are available through Ticketpass.
