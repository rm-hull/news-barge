Middlesbrough Football Club are inviting supporters to join a tribute to two Cleveland Police officers who tragically lost their lives in the A66 crash last weekend.

Before kick-off at Saturday’s Championship fixture against West Brom at the Riverside Stadium, fans will be asked to pay their respects to PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough.

In a statement online, Middlesbrough FC said it wanted to honour the memory of both men and recognise their dedication and service to the community.

The club said: “Immediately before kick-off tomorrow, Middlesbrough Football Club are asking supporters to join them in paying respects to PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough, the two Cleveland Police officers who tragically lost their lives in the line of duty last weekend.”

The deaths of PC Blades and PC Clough have had a profound impact across the region, with tributes continuing to be paid by colleagues, emergency services, local organisations and members of the public.

Cleveland Police has described the loss of the officers as devastating, while hundreds of people have shared messages of condolence and support with their families, friends, and fellow officers.

The officers were among seven people that died when their marked police vehicle was involved in a crash with a Volkswagen Passat in South Bank, Middlesbrough, at 3.39am on Saturday, August 22.

Police have arrested 17 people after the A66 crash, which followed a police pursuit, as part of investigations into alleged organised crime.

Some 200 extra officers have been deployed to help Cleveland Police.

  Police have not ruled out a link between the crash and a __fatal house fire a mile away from the site, which claimed the lives of a seven-year-old girl and her aunt on Wednesday.__

The match between Middlesbrough and West Brom kicks off at 12.30pm.
