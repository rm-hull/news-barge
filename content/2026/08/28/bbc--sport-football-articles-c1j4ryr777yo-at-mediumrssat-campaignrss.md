Manchester City beat Crystal Palace 4-1 at Selhurst Park on Friday

## Crystal Palace

**Dean Henderson:** No chance for Manchester City's goals but demonstrated his quality with a fine save early on from Anderson, then others from Semenyo and Cherki.**7Jaydee Convot:** Did nothing wrong but ultimately Palace had no answer to Cherki.**6Chris Richards:** Good battle with Haaland, but only one player was winning that one. Should have scored with a first-half header.**6Takehiro Tomiyasu:** Solid as usual and will bring experience to the Palace ranks.**6Anan Khalaili:** No lack of effort or energy but short of end product.**6Daichi Kamada:** Busy in midfield as always but struggled to exert influence.**5Adam Wharton:** Class act. Composure, vision and a superb range of passing. At the heart of Palace's good things, although like others in the Palace ranks was eventually over-run.**7Tyrick Mitchell:** Tenacious and quick but missed a big chance to equalise with a header in the first half.**6Eddie Nketiah:** Lively early on, forcing a save from Donnarumma, but faded and wasted a crucial opportunity early in the second half.**5Yeremy Pino:** Can be a mixed bag but offers real threat, as he showed with his splendid free-kick that went down as a Donnarumma own goal.**6Jorgen Strand Larsen:** Worked hard but lost the physical battle with Dias. Had one first-half chance but failed to convert. Disappointing.**5Subs:Guessand (on 63, Strand Larsen), Gozo (on 68, Pino), McNeil (on 68, Nketiah), Disasi (on Tomiyasu 74) Mingueza (on 74, Khalaili)Subs not used:** Benitez, Munoz, Lerma, Hughes

## Manchester City

**Gianluigi Donnarumma:** Hardly overworked but made a crucial early save from Nketiah as Palace threatened.**6Abdukodir Khusanov:** Showed his versatility at right-back. Lightning pace.**6Ruben Dias:** Rock solid and untroubled. Brushed aside any challenges.**7Josko Gvardiol:** Figured at left-back but was always willing to lend his weight to City's attacking forays.**6Nico O'Reilly:** Elegant and showed lots of classy touches in a role further forward.**7Elliot Anderson:** Always demanding the ball and ready to put his body on the line in the physical exchanges. Settling in.**7Marc Guehi:** Warm welcome on his return to Selhurst Park, playing in central defence as opposed to his previous role in midfield.**6Phil Foden:** Missed a big first-half chance when he should have scored but played a part in Cherki's first goal. Lively at times.**6Rayan Cherki:** Glorious touches and dribbling skills, capped with a touch of genius and arrogance for his goals.**8Antoine Semenyo:** Provided the inviting cross for Haaland's first goal. Always a threat.**7Erling Haaland:** Off the mark with a trademark header and a powerhouse finish. Scored in all six of his Premier League appearances at Selhurst Park.**7Subs:Kovacic (on 75, Gvardiol), McAidoo (on 82 Cherki), Bouaddi (on Anderson 82), Reis (on 89, Khusanov), Braithwaite (on 89, O'Reilly)**

Subs not used: Ruli, Ait-Nouri, Echeverri, Lewis
