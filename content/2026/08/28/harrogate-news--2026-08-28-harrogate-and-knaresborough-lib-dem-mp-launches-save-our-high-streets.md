The MP for Harrogate and Knaresborough has launched a “Save Our High Streets” plan as new analysis reveals the severe hit delivered to local jobs and businesses.

According to official figures from the Office for National Statistics (ONS), North Yorkshire saw 1,400 businesses close their doors in the first half of 2026. This led to an overall net reduction in the number of local firms by 85, as the number of business closures overtook the number of new businesses opening.

This comes after North Yorkshire lost 15 arts, entertainment, and recreation venues between 2019 and 2024 under the previous Conservative Government. These venues are considered particularly vital to Harrogate’s tourism sector. MP Tom Gordon stated that early reports of businesses considering closure are extremely concerning and argued that the Government must take urgent action to implement the Liberal Democrats’ plan to support local businesses.

### The “Save Our High Streets” Proposals

The newly launched plan includes several key economic interventions:

- **Hospitality Tax Relief:** An emergency 5% VAT cut on hospitality, accommodation, and attractions to boost local pubs, restaurants, and entertainment venues while saving consumers money.
- **Tax Overhaul:** An overhaul of the current business rates system, replacing it with a Commercial Landowner Levy.
- **Employment Protections:** Abandoning the planned National Insurance Contribution (NIC) hike.
- **High Street Enforcement:** Increasing local powers to tackle illicit vape shops.

### Rising Local Unemployment Numbers

Tom Gordon stated that high streets, and in particular the hospitality sector, are essential for local employment. The call to action arrives as 7,540 local people are currently looking for a job, representing an increase of 300 compared to last year. The number of unemployed young people has also reached 1,315—an increase of 115 compared to a year ago.

On top of the raft of national policies, the MP has launched a local survey to better understand the specific challenges and opportunities currently facing local businesses.



**Tom Gordon MP said:**

In North Yorkshire we’ve seen an unacceptable rise in job losses and business difficulties, following decades of neglect and mismanagement by consecutive Labour and the Conservatives governments.

Our high streets are dying but it’s not too late to save them. The Government must stop dragging its feet and implement the Liberal Democrats’ plan to bring the hustle and bustle back to our town centres, restore the high streets as the heart of our communities and make them a safe place for everyone to enjoy.

We need North Yorkshire Council to step up their game too and do more to support local businesses.
