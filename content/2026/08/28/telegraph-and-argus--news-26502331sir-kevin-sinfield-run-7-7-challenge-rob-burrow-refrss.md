SIR Kevin Sinfield will run as part of his 7 in 7 challenge to raise funds and awareness for motor neurone disease (MND).

The challenge begins in Hull on September 27 and finishes at the Super League Grand Final in Manchester on October 3.

It is dedicated to the memory of Sinfield’s friend and former Leeds Rhinos teammate Rob Burrow.

Sir Kevin said: "Seven years ago this December, my friend and Leeds Rhinos teammate Rob Burrow CBE was diagnosed with motor neurone disease.

"In that moment, as anyone who has been affected by MND will tell you, life changed in an instant for him and his loved ones.

"While Rob and his incredible family bravely came to terms with his diagnosis, his friends, teammates, colleagues and the wider Rugby League community were determined to make a difference."

The 7 in 7 challenges began in December 2020.

Inspired by the number seven shirt worn by Burrow, the runs aim to raise funds and awareness for MND charities across the UK.

Since then, Sir Kevin and his team have covered more than 1,000 miles, meeting MND patients and their families across the UK and Ireland.

He said: "We’ve laughed and we’ve cried.

"We’ve been inspired by everyone we’ve met.

"Every card, message, hug and handshake has fuelled us to do more and go even further in our fight against MND."

Despite their efforts, the impact of the disease remains stark.

Sir Kevin said: "Tragically, since we started these challenges in 2020, more than 15,000 people have died from MND.

"15,000 more have been diagnosed.

"These are shocking numbers, which is why, this year, we’ll run again – in memory of Rob and for everyone affected by this devastating disease."

The public are encouraged to get involved.

Supporters can cheer on the team along the route, make a donation, or take part by starting their own 7 in 7 challenge.

Sir Kevin said: "Every action and every donation – however big or small – matters and makes a huge difference."
