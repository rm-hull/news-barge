If you’ve been outside, to the gym, or on a basketball court, there’s a good chance you’ve seen someone wearing Reeboks. The brand has had its fair share of ups and downs, but is riding a wave of increased interest driven by smart business moves like a refocus on sports and lifestyle, alongside a number of high-profile collaborations like the Reebok G-Unit G6 50 Cent.

Founded in 1958, Reebok has changed hands and gone through multiple iterations. Most recently in 2022, Reebok was sold by Adidas to Authentic Brands Group for over two billion dollars. Since then, it’s reinvigorated its sports lineup, hired Shaquel O’ Neal as president of Reebok basketball, and signed tons of professional athletes across basketball, golf, and tennis to the brand.

But what about the shoes? They’re solid. As someone who used to test training and running shoes, the Reebok Floatzig and Reebok Nano X series are excellent performers for the price—I still wear my Nano X4s anytime I’m strength training. Other standouts include the more lifestyle oriented Classic and Club lineups.

But given how often you should replace sneakers (hint: often every six months), you should always look for a deal when shopping. That’s why I rounded up some of the best Reebok coupons and Reebok promo codes below, to help you save time and money.

## Use this Reebok Promo Code for up to $30 off

The more you spend on Reebok’s site, the more potential savings you can get when you shop its Buy More, Save More section. This collection has over 1,000 pieces of shoes and apparel, including popular styles like the Club and Nano series, so you can probably find something fitting. You get $10 off orders of $100 or more, $20 off $150 orders, and $30 off orders of $200 or more using the Reebok discount code **BMSM** at checkout.

## 15% Reebok Discount Code for New Customers

Anyone making a new account at Reebok online or the Reebok app can get 15% off their first purchase using the Reebok discount code **MOVE15**. This Reebok promo code works after you sign up for a new account and verify your email address, and is applied automatically at checkout if you are using the Reebok app. Standard exclusions to select collaboration items and limited-edition sneaker releases apply, so double check your order before purchase to make sure you qualify for the Reebok coupon code deal.

## Make the Most of Reebok Authentic Membership With More Discounts

This is the best Reebok deal for anyone who shops the brand frequently. With an Authentics Membership ($5 a month), you get 10% off all Reebok products and free shipping on all orders. You also get access to early product drops, sale alerts, and birthday rewards. This perk is applied automatically when logged in using Shop Pay at checkout. An Authentics membership also gives you discounts at other participating brands like Champion, Billabong, Hunter, Ted Baker, and more, so it’s worth considering, especially if you shop multiple brands. This Reebok discount is for online purchases only.

## Stack Reebok Coupons with Clearance and Seasonal Sales for up to 50% More Off

It’s easy to stack savings with Reebok if you know where to look. Items in season sales like Back to School or just the regular Reebok clearance section can be discounted by as much as 65%. Add extra discounts (and free shipping) from an Authentics Membership, plus Bundle & Save offers, and you can drop the price of your checkout cart dramatically, without needing a Reebok discount code.

## Reebok Discount Codes for Students, Educators, Military, and First Responders

Students, educators (active and retired), military, veterans, first responders, government workers, healthcare professionals, and nurses can get 30% off purchases as part of the Reebok heroes discount. All groups must verify their status via ID.me at checkout to receive a single-use Reebok discount code.

## Fix a Reebok Discount Code That’s Not Working

There are times when a Reebok discount code might not work like you thought it would. Make sure your items aren’t excluded from site-wide Reebok promo codes (some sneakers, new releases, and third-party collabs are). Also make sure you’ve entered the Reebok coupon code correctly, confirmed its terms of use and active window, and met any spending requirements.
