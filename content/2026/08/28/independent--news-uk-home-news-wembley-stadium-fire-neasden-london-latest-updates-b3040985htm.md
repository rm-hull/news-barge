Huge amounts of smoke can be seen near Wembley Stadium after a large fire broke out on an industrial estate, forcing a nearby Amazon warehouse to evacuate.

London Fire Brigade (LFB) said 20 fire engines and around 125 firefighters are on the scene in Hannah Close in Neasden.

Residents in the immediate area are told to close doors and windows and stay inside where possible, due to the large amount of smoke. People have also been urged to avoid the area as crews respond to the blaze.

Tube services on the Metropolitan line are fully suspended, and there are “severe delays” on the Jubilee line between Willesden Green and Wembley Park. Rail services into London Marylebone have also been disrupted.

The cause of the fire is not yet known.

Dramatic pictures show huge columns of smoke billowing into the sky over west London.

LFB said there were more than 70 calls reporting the fire at 11.34am. Crews from Wembley, Park Royal, Willesden and surrounding fire stations rushed to the scene.

TfL said tickets will be accepted on London Buses, DLR, Elizabeth line, Mildmay line, c2c and Thameslink services.

## Huge fire breaks out near Wembley stadium

Around 125 firefighters are responding to the scene of a large fire in Neasden this afternoon.

London Fire Brigade (LFB) said around 20 fire engines are responding to the blaze, which was first reported at around 11:34am.

We’ll be bringing you all the latest in this blog.

## Metropolitan line fully suspended as crews respond to blaze

The Metropolitan line has been fully suspended as fire crews respond to the blaze.

Transport for London (TfL) said ticket acceptance is in place on London Buses, DLR, Elizabeth line, Mildmay line, c2c and Thameslink services.

There are also “severe delays” on the Jubilee line, which is part suspended between Willesden Green and Wembley Park.

## Fire brigade receives more than 70 calls over blaze

London Fire Brigade has received more than 70 calls in relation to the blaze, it said.

It urged people to avoid the area as crews respond.

## Amazon warehouse evacuated

An Amazon warehouse located near to the scene of the fire has reportedly been evacuated.

The warehouse is not related to the industrial unit involved, the Press Association understands.

## Area roughly the size of two football pitches alight

An industrial unit roughly equivalent to the size of two football pitches is currently alight, fire services have said.

In an updated statement, LFB added that the blaze has broken out at a mixed waste facility.

“The fire is located at a mixed waste facility, and an industrial unit approximately 100m by 150m is currently alight,” it said.

## Residents urged to stay inside and close windows

Residents have been urged to stay indoors and close any doors and windows as firefighters tackle a massive blaze in Neasden.

“Residents in the immediate area should close doors and windows, remaining inside where possible, due to the large amount of smoke being produced,” London Fire Brigade said in a statement.

## Rail services into London Marylebone also impacted by fire

Rail services into London Marylebone are also facing disruption as a result of the ongoing fire.

“A fire on a property near the railway between London Marylebone and Harrow-on-the-Hill means that all lines towards Aylesbury are disrupted,” a statement on the National Rail website reads.

“Additionally there is a fire on the railway in the London Marylebone area. As a result of this, all lines are blocked.

“Trains may be cancelled, delayed by up to 40 minutes or revised.

“Disruption is expected until 15:30.”
