## Hidden ghosts to haunt York railway museum in spooky half-term trail

The National Railway Museum in York has teamed up with York Ghost Merchants to hide 30 hand-crafted ghost figures in its Station Hall *(Image: Trustees of the Science Museum Group)*

Hidden ghosts will haunt the halls of a museum this autumn as part of a new half-term trail.

The National Railway Museum in York has teamed up with York Ghost Merchants to hide 30 hand-crafted ghost figures in its Station Hall, each linked to a haunting tale from railway history.

The trail is part of the museum’s wider 'Echoes on the Line' programme, which runs during October half term.

Sarah Price, head of public programming at the National Railway Museum, said: "York is said to be one of the most haunted cities in the world, and part of that reputation is thanks to the ghostly tales about the railways.

"The beautiful Victorian style and stories associated with York Ghost Merchants fit so well with the style and stories of Station Hall, with its opulent Victorian carriages and secret dark corners – it seemed like the perfect collaboration.

"We can’t wait for visitors to spot the ghosts and discover some of our most haunting railway stories."

Visitors can also enjoy live storytelling and a Victorian-themed craft activity, creating keepsakes inspired by memory, travel, and communication.

The Ghosts of Station Hall trail and surrounding activities will run daily from October 24 to November 1, between 10am and 5pm.
