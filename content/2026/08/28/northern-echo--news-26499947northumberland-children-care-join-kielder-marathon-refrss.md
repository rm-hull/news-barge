Children in care are being given the chance to take part in a major running event in Northumberland.

Northumberland County Council is helping them participate in the Altra Kielder Marathon junior races, thanks to support from the event’s headline sponsor and its own long-standing partnership with the organisers.

The council will fund six places for children in care to take part in the junior races at Kielder Marathon, which takes place on October 3 and 4.

Altra, the event's headline sponsor, has donated running shoes and clothing worth around £1,500 to ensure the children are fully equipped to enjoy the day.

Allison Curbishley, event director and former international athlete, said: "We put a lot of effort into making sure that the junior races at the Altra Kielder Marathon have a big event atmosphere.

"It’s always great to see young people making the most of the occasion and enjoying their running.

"So it’s brilliant that Northumberland County Council is going to help even more kids have that experience and we’re delighted that Altra has stepped up to kit them out.

"I had a great time meeting the young people and look forward to welcoming them to stunning Kielder in a couple of months."

The marathon, described as ‘Britain’s most beautiful,’ follows a scenic route around Kielder Water and forms part of a two-day festival that includes a half marathon, 10K, gravel duathlon, and junior runs.

The event is organised by Events of the North in partnership with Northumbrian Water, Forestry England, and Northumberland County Council.

Councillor Wendy Pattison, cabinet member for stronger communities at Northumberland County Council, said: "We are delighted to be sponsoring the Kielder Marathon once again.

"This partnership is about more than a race; it’s about opening up opportunities for young people in our county who may not otherwise get the chance to take part in iconic sporting events like this.

"By funding junior run places for children in Northumberland, we are showing our commitment to grassroots sport, health and wellbeing, and stronger communities across the county."

For more information about the Altra Kielder Marathon events, and to book race places, visit www.kieldermarathon.com.
