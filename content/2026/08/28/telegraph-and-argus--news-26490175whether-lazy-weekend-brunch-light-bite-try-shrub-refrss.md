GENERAL Manager Howard Blagbrough tells the Telegraph & Argus about The Shrub in Brighouse

Q: What is the appeal of The Shrub?

A: Whether it’s a lazy weekend brunch, a fizz-fuelled bottomless with the girls, or a light-bite sat on our terrace relaxing the day away, The Shrub has all your needs covered.

Its brunch menu will leave you looking forward to your next food adventure, while our extensive drinks list will keep that thirst quenched. Be sure to look out for our themed events that will have you dancing the night away.

Q: For how long has The Shrub been open and can you describe it?

A: The Shrub has been established for around three years. We like to say that it is a bit of a Tardis, from a smallish entrance to a sitting area and an enclosed terrace, which is very popular. On a sunny day, with blue skies, you could imagine you are in the Mediterranean.

The terrace is also an ideal venue for a private party and can accommodate up to 30 people. This has proved popular for occasions such as baby showers, especially on a Sunday afternoon, where this can be an exclusive event.

Q: What food and drink do you serve?

A: We serve brunch up until 3pm each day, serving traditional dishes, including your traditional breakfast, vegetarian breakfast and eggs royal. One of our popular dishes is Turkish eggs - sauteed peppers and onions tossed through a rich Mediterranean sauce topped with two poached eggs, toasted bread and finished with tzatziki.

From noon we serve flatbreads - these are very popular with most people. Our most popular dish is salt and pepper chicken flatbread, followed by steak and cheese flatbread.

From 3pm we serve small plates and likewise the salt and pepper buttermilk chicken goes down well together with pork belly and mozzarella sticks

We offer a very speciality afternoon tea from £29.95 or try our special deal on a Thursday, two for £45.

Our bottomless brunch price is £39.50 and includes two small plates and a variety of prosecco-based cocktails, beers, wines and ciders.

Our dessert menu includes favourites such as sticky toffee pudding and cheesecake of the day.

On a Friday we promote two for £14 cocktails, with our most popular cocktail being the Pornstar Martini.

If you like coffee, you have come to the right place. We pride ourselves in serving on of the best coffees in the area. We also serve matcha, and at the moment the blueberry matcha is our fastest selling iced drink.

Q: What have customers said about The Shrub?

A: The Shrub and has some excellent reviews on Google, not only about the food but also the excellent service. We have a brilliant team and have developed into an establishment where people will return and also recommend to friends, so we are now seeing regular familiar faces.

Our staff are extremely friendly and very attentive and this is very much a key when deciding to book a bottomless brunch.

We are a friendly establishment and will try to accommodate everyone; we are dog friendly as well.

Q: When are you open?

A: We are open seven days a week, Monday to Thursday from 9am until 3pm, and on Friday and Saturday from 9am until 9pm and on Sunday from 9am until 6pm.

The Shrub is ideally located near to the Brighouse train station and five minutes from the town centre. We voluntarily contribute to the Business Improvement District which works to market the town and boost local trade. We work with them and often promote offers.
