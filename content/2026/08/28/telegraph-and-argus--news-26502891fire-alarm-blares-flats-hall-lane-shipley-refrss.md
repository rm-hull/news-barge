A large emergency service presence attended a block of flats following reports of a fire alarm sounding.

The incident happened on Hall Lane in Shipley just after 8.30pm on August 27, prompting a response from West Yorkshire Fire Service.

A spokesman for the service said: "We were called to reports of a fire alarm sounding on Hall Lane, Shipley, at 8.41pm.

"On arrival, crews found no signs of fire and the incident was confirmed as a false alarm."

Images and videos on social media show a fire engine parked on the street.

Crews can be seen entering the building and breaking a door down inside.
