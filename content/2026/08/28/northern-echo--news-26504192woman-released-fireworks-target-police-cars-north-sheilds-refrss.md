A woman has been released on bail following reports of disorder at what was believed to be a vigil.

The incident took place on Saturday, August 22, in the Waterville Road area of North Shields and is believed to be linked to a memorial for a teenager.

Police said the vigil was organised without the involvement of the teenager’s family.

Northumbria Police said: "It was reported that fireworks were being set off and targeted at police vehicles, with small fires also started.

"Thankfully, no-one was injured in the incident, and the fires were extinguished a short time later."

A woman in her 40s was arrested on suspicion of a public order offence.

Now, it is understood she has since been released on bail.

Officers have urged people to ensure future memorial events are carried out in a safe and respectful manner.

Northumbria Police said: "We would ask that anyone looking to hold memorial events in any of our communities does so respectfully and with consideration for other members of the public at all times.

"They are not a means to commit crime or take part in disorder."

Inquiries into the incident are ongoing and anyone with information is asked to contact Northumbria Police.
