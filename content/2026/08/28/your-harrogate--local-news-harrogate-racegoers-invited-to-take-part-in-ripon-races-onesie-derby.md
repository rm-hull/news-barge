Racegoers are being invited to wear their best animal onesies for a special race at Ripon Racecourse this Bank Holiday Monday.

The Onesie Derby will form part of the Curtains Up at the Races Family Day on Monday 31st August.

Participants will race along the final furlong in their animal onesies, with Lightwater Valley tickets and other prizes available.

Entry is free and remains open through the Ripon Racecourse website.

The event will also feature seven horse races and a full programme of family entertainment.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a7eb58793dcdc8396a.jpg)


Activities will include Punch and Judy and magic shows, circus entertainment, fire juggling, circus skills and a costume station.

There will also be a bouncy castle, balloon modelling and face painting.

Gates will open at 11.30am, with the first race beginning at 1.55pm and the final race scheduled for 5.20pm.

Children under 18 can attend free of charge when accompanied by an adult.

*Advance tickets are available through the Ripon Racecourse**website**.*
