There’s an update following an earlier appeal about a serious crash involving two men on motorcycles.

It happened on Mook Lane, near Westow, at around 10.35am on Sunday 2 August 2026.

Both were airlifted to hospital with serious injuries.

Sadly, North Yorkshire Police has confirmed that one of the riders has passed away after a short period in hospice care.

The second rider remains in hospital with serious injuries.

Officers are asking if anyone saw either a yellow Yamaha or a white Honda motorcycle kin the area at the time to contact them.
