The Aldwark Arms, a traditional pub in an idyllic village setting near York, has been sold.

Currently closed, but decorated in a modern, contemporary style, the pub is extremely well maintained throughout. The ground floor comprises an extensive trade area which is split into smaller drinking and dining areas, centred around a central bar.

The business occupies a central position in an affluent village setting near York and the location benefits from excellent access to the A1(M) and A19 north-south carriageways and the A59 east-west.

Extending to around 0.65 acres, the site features a large car park and a welcoming trade garden. There is also a substantial owner’s flat situated on the first floor and the property also has planning permission for an extension to the existing pub and the construction of a separate accommodation block.

FRP Advisory instructed Christie & Co to advise the company and sell the assets. Simon Dalingwater of Adair Paxton advised the purchasers.

David Cash, Regional Director – Pubs & Restaurants for Christit & Co, commented, “We had lots of interest in the pub and multiple offers, which is reassuring but not surprising given the quality of the asset and its location.

“This resulted in a best and final offers process and the pub going under offer within weeks of launching to the market, as well as a swift timeline between the deal being agreed and completion.”

The Aldwark Arms was sold at an undisclosed sum, off a freehold asking price of £450,000.
