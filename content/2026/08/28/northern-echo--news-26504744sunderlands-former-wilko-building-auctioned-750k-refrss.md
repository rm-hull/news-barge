A huge former Wilko site is going under the hammer in a city centre.

The old building in Sunderland, one of the chain’s largest stores in the North East, will be auctioned with a guide price of £750,000.

The branch closed back in autumn of 2023 after the retailer entered administration, with around 70 jobs lost at the site.

The property has a long retail history, previously associated with the Binns department store, which expanded along Fawcett Street in the early 20th century.

In 1913, Binns acquired 33 Fawcett Street, having already taken on numbers 35, 40 and 42.

Spanning 110,154 sq ft and arranged over six floors, the property will be sold with vacant possession at the Acuitus commercial property auction on September 17.

The building includes a large retail unit with street frontage and sales space across the ground, first, second, and third floors, as well as ancillary areas in the basement and on the fourth floor.

Alex Auterac of Acuitus said: "We expect strong interest from a broad range of investors."

Located in Sunderland city centre, the site is close to key amenities including the railway station, the Bridges Shopping Centre, Mowbray Park and two university campuses serving more than 30,000 students.

The auction will begin at 1pm and will be broadcast live on the Acuitus website.
