TWO drivers who were racing in an Audi and BMW have been convicted after killing a mum and her four-year-old daughter.

Shortly after 8.30am on 16 January 2023, Hardeep Bhachu lost control of the white Audi TT he was driving and hit 27-year-old Justyna Hulboj and her daughter, Lena Czepczor, before colliding with the wall of the Vertu Jaguar Leeds car dealership on Scott Hall Road, Leeds.

The mother and daughter both died at the scene.

Bhachu was taken to hospital due to the injuries he suffered in the collision and was later arrested.

## Pair had been racing

Thanks to dashcam and CCTV footage provided by members of the public and Bhachu’s own dashcam, police identified that he had been racing a black BMW 135i immediately before the collision.

Jasskamal Riyat, the driver of the BMW, was arrested on the afternoon of 16 January 2023.

  Jasskamal Riyat 

*(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

Bhachu’s dashcam captures a conversation between Bhachu and Riyat as they stopped at traffic lights with Bhachu boasting that his Audi had 560 horsepower and that he’d only been at quarter throttle.

## Both men guilty

Bhachu, 29, of Grant Row, Adel, pleaded guilty in June 2024 to causing the deaths of Justyna and Lena.

Jasskamal Riyat, 37, of Buckstone Avenue, Moortown, was found guilty of the same offence following a trial at Leeds Crown Court.

## 'My whole world collapsed in one second'

The fiancé of Justyna and father of Lena said: "Before 16 January 2023, my life was a fulfilment of my deepest dreams, I had everything that was most important in life. I had a good job, a wonderful fiancée, Justyna and a wonderful daughter, Lena.

"Unfortunately, on the morning of January 16, when I said goodbye to my family with the words “I love you”, I didn't know that it would be the last time I saw them.

"When I was informed about their deaths, my whole world collapsed in one second.

"Everyday I feel a huge void after their departure, and I imagine what it would be like if they were still alive. We had so many plans and dreams and in one moment it all ended.

"I know that I will have to live with the burden of losing the two most important people in my life for the rest of my life.

"I would like to live normally again without all this, but I know that it is no longer possible, no one will bring back the lives of my fiancée and my daughter. This nightmare will last for the rest of my life.

"My dream was to have a wonderful family, and I must say that it came true for a short while, but that was taken away from me on the 16th of January.

"Unfortunately, all that remains are wonderful memories and a constant fight for the next day, which is not easy."

  Justyna and Lena

## What the police said

Detective Inspector Paul Conroy, of West Yorkshire Police’s Major Collision Enquiry Team, said: "This was an absolutely horrifying collision that I know even shocked experienced collision investigators.

"An innocent mother and daughter who were simply making their way to school never made it there and tragically never made it home because of the selfish, reckless actions of these two men."

Mr Conroy added: "Bhachu and Riyat didn’t know each other.

"They were two men showing off their fast cars on a busy dual carriageway.

"We have released a video which shows the moments leading up to this collision.

"It is upsetting to watch but I hope that it might make people think about their own driving and encourage conversations with friends and family.

"Ultimately, Bhachu thought he could drive at those speeds in his high-powered car, but he couldn’t, and his loss of control took two lives and ruined many more.

"Riyat witnessed this collision but instead of stopping to check if those involved were okay, he left the scene.

"He denied his involvement but was convicted thanks to the extensive footage showing his speeding vehicle in the moments leading up to the collision.

"I would like to take this opportunity to thank the many people who came forward to provide dashcam and CCTV footage which helped us to demonstrate the dangerous nature of both Bhachu and Riyat’s driving that morning."
