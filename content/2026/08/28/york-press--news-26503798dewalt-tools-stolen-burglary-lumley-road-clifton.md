BURGLARS have struck and stolen tools in a York suburb earlier this week.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a garage was burgled in Clifton in the early hours of yesterday morning.

The incident took place in Lumley Road at around 1.47am on August 27, 2026, when someone broke into the garage and stole a number of DeWalt screwdrivers.

"We are appealing to anyone who may have witnessed any suspicious activity in the area around the time of the offence, or who may have relevant CCTV, doorbell, dashcam or other footage, to come forward," said a spokesperson for the force.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101 or by making a report on the North Yorkshire Police website.
