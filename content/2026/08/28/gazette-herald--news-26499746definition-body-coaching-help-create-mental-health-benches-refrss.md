A personal training studio has raised thousands for suicide prevention benches.

Definition Body Coaching, based in Nether Poppleton, has raised £10,000 in just 18 months to help fund four suicide prevention benches across North Yorkshire.

The benches are part of a nationwide project launched by former Tottenham Hotspur footballer Micky Hazard and his sister Michelle, who founded the charity Legend on the Bench in memory of Michelle’s son Jay, who died by suicide in 2019 at the age of 23.

Each bench is fitted with a QR code linking to mental health resources and support services, offering help any time of day.

Mr Hazard said: "Someone is always listening, that is the message we want to send out."

Definition Body Coaching partnered with the charity in spring 2025 to bring the benches to North Yorkshire.

The first two were installed in December 2025 at Chesney Park in York and Valley Gardens in Harrogate, with Mr Hazard and former UEFA Cup-winning captain Graham Roberts attending the unveiling.

A third bench was installed last month at South Bay Harbour in Scarborough, and a fourth will soon be unveiled in Whitby.

Justin Shepherd, owner of Definition Body Coaching, said: "These benches have been a brilliant fundraiser for us that all our studio clients have been a huge part of.

"We have had multiple prize draws and auctions to get to the brilliant amount raised.

"It has been very inspiring to see these benches arrive in North Yorkshire.

"Our message is that people matter.

"These benches create awareness of mental health, which is so vital for all of us.

"It would be great to see even more benches installed in North Yorkshire across 2026 and beyond."

Definition Body Coaching opened in 2014 and is run by Mr Shepherd alongside trainers Carl Ward and Mike Gray.
