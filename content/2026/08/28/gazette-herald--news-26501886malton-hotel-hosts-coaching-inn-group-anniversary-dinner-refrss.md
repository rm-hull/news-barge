A hotel group has marked 30 years in business with a special anniversary event.

The Coaching Inn Group, the UK’s number one Large Hotel Group of the Year as voted for by Which?, hosted a celebratory dinner on August 6 at The Talbot Hotel in Malton.

The event honoured some of its most loyal guests, including those who have stayed at multiple locations, supported the group since its early days, or celebrated significant milestones at one of its inns.

Adam Charity, managing director at The Coaching Inn Group, said: "As we continue to celebrate 30 years of The Coaching Inn Group, it was incredibly rewarding to mark this milestone with the people who have been such an important part of our journey.

"Hearing the stories, memories and special moments our guests have shared with us was very special, and this dinner was our way of saying thank you for their loyalty to the group."

Guests enjoyed a limited-edition Signature Beef Wellington created for the 30th anniversary.
