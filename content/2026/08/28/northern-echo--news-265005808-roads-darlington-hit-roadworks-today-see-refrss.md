Drivers in Darlington face eight sets of road restrictions, including temporary traffic signals and a lane closure.

Here are where they are:

### A68 Woodland Road

Telecoms work has caused temporary traffic signals on A68 Woodland Road in both directions between Hollyhurst Road and Bracken Road since August 18.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 15 at 11.59pm.

### Beechwood Avenue

Telecoms work has restricted Beechwood Avenue in both directions between B6280 Coniscliffe Road and Southend Avenue since August 17.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until August 28 at 4pm.


### A167

A lane has been closed in both directions on the A167 between B6444 St Andrews Way and A1(M) J59 (Aycliffe) because of gas main work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 1 at 4pm.

### Neasham Road

Construction has led to temporary traffic signals on Neasham Road in both directions at Burma Road since August 10.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until August 30 at 11pm.

### Auckland Avenue

One lane traffic is in operation on Auckland Avenue in both directions between Brinkburn Road and Auckland Oval because of construction.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 22 at 11pm.

### St Alban's Green

One lane traffic is in operation on St Alban's Green in both directions at Winchester Way because of electricity work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 9 at 11pm.

### Edinburgh Drive

Temporary traffic signals are in operation on Edinburgh Drive in both directions between A67 Coniscliffe Road and Teesdale Avenue because of gas main work.

Drivers should expect delays. The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 9 at 4pm.

### Faverdale

Temporary traffic signals are in operation on Faverdale in both directions after A68 West Auckland Road because of construction.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 6 at 11pm.

The information lists the works as being in Darlington on Friday, August 28. The listed restriction levels range from low to moderate, with Edinburgh Drive marked moderate.
