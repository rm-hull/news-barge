Greggs has opened its new shop today in Northallerton, creating 16 new jobs for the area.

The shop at Unit 1, North Moor Road will stock freshly prepared Greggs favourites,.

They include the retailer’s iconic Sausage Roll, the ever-popular Steak Bake, delicious vegan alternatives such as the Vegan Roll (Pork Free), and the all-new Chicken Roll, now available for customers to enjoy nationwide. Customers can also grab a range of over ice drinks including the brand-new Iced Blueberry Matcha.

A new line up of pastas and salads has joined the menu just in time for summer, including a Prawn Layered Pasta Salad, and Chicken Caesar Salad with Egg. Customers can also take advantage of Greggs’ deals throughout the day, including its 3-part Big Deal – a drink, a side and a main – from just £5.25.

Those looking for a bite to eat in the evening can enjoy a range of hot food from crispy Southern Fried Chicken Goujons to Hot Cinnamon Yum Yums, and even a host of tasty pizza slices including the Chicken Tandoori, BBQ Chicken & Bacon and Veggie Feast.

Customers looking to skip the queues can get their goodies delivered straight to their door with Just Eat and UberEATS, or alternatively grab a bite on the go through Click + Collect via the Greggs App.

The Greggs App gives customers access to a variety of perks and tasty rewards. For every 9 purchases in different categories – from hot drinks, to sandwiches, app users can get one completely free.

New users of the Greggs App also receive a free hot drink or iced drink of their choice upon registration, as well as a free sweet treat to celebrate their birthday each year.

The Northallerton shop opens with a modern new look and comfortable indoor and outdoor seating. The opening hours are 6am – 6pm daily.

Shop Manager Donna Mason said: “We can’t wait to welcome both Greggs fans and new customers to our brand-new shop. We’ve got an amazing range of iconic savouries, Fairtrade hot drinks, hot food and healthier choices available for takeaway, sit-in or Click + Collect.”

Gillian Long, Retail Operations Director for Greggs, said: “Our new shop in Northallerton has brought 16 new jobs to the local area, as well as providing customers with a modern, convenient new place to experience their Greggs favourites.”

Any further jobs created at the Northallerton shop will be posted on [https://careers.greggs.co.uk/.](https://careers.greggs.co.uk/.)
