Football fans tonight paused to pay their respects to the two police officers killed in the horror A66 crash.

PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough were killed in the early hours of Saturday, August 22, when their police car was struck by a VW Passat going the wrong way on the A66 in South Bank.

Hartlepool United's Victoria Park fell silent before tonight's tie against Eastleigh in memory of the officers, including dad-of-two PC Blades, who lived in the town and coached youth teams.

  A two-minute's silence was held for PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough before Hartlepool's game against Eastleigh tonight. 

*(Image: MARK FLETCHER)*

The officer's family joined the players in the centre circle as the stands fell silent just before kick-off.

Two hats had been left on the pitch marking the lives of each officer.

Hartlepool football clubs previously paid tribute to PC Blades.

FC Hartlepool said: "As coach of our under-ten reds, a team that was made even more special by having his eldest son playing alongside the boys, Matty was so much more than a coach.

"He was a role model, a mentor, a friend, and someone the children looked up to and admired.

"Matty gave his time, energy and heart to the team.

"He encouraged the boys, believed in them, supported them and helped them grow - not only as footballers but as young people.

"His kindness, patience, and infectious personality made him loved by all who had the privilege of knowing him.

"The under-ten reds have lost a coach, but our club has lost a truly special person and a valued member of the FC Hartlepool family.

"Our deepest thoughts and condolences are with Matty’s family, friends, and everyone who knew and loved him - especially his wife and two boys whom he adored."

Flags were flown at half-mast earlier in the week at both FC Hartlepool and Seaton Carew FC.

Seaton Carew FC said: "Matty and his oldest son are much-loved members of our club and our U10 Lions football family."

"Matty had only recently joined our club as a coach and was preparing for his first season with our brand-new U7 Seahawks team.

  Police officers at the scene of a fatal crash involving a police car and a Volkswagen Passat on the A66 in South Bank, near Middlesbrough (Owen Humphreys/PA)


"He was looking forward to being part of the team and, most importantly, to coaching his own son, who is going to be playing for the Seahawks this season.

"Our thoughts and deepest condolences are with Matty’s wife, his two boys and all of his family and friends at this incredibly difficult and heartbreaking time."

A fundraiser for the officers, created by the Cleveland Police Federation, has raised more tha £1m to be split equally between the two officers’ families.

  PC Tom Clough, 38, and PC Matthew Blades, 37 (L-R). 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

Five people travelling in the Passat - Michael Robert Cahill, 23, Theo Rae, 17, Cole Robert Worthy, 17, Jakub Matusiak, 23, and Makai Saddington, 18. - also died at the scene. Four of them had previous criminal convictions.

Officers have been working '20-hour days' with over 200 extra police drafted in on Teesside following the devastating A66 crash and the Grangetown house blaze that saw two lives lost.

Police now say the house fire and the A66 incident are 'not being linked'.

In an emotional tribute, PC Blades’ wife described him as an adored son, brother, uncle and son in law, whose loss had left a gap that could never be filled.

She said: "No words will ever feel like the right words to honour our Matt - my beloved husband, my best friend, and the most devoted father to our two young boys.

"He was an adored son and brother, a perfect son-in-law, and the most fun uncle. He was loved beyond words by all his family and friends, and his loss has left a space that can never be filled.

"He gave his life in the line of duty, protecting and serving others with courage, dedication and selflessness.

"The pain of losing him is beyond words, and our lives will never be the same. Neither will the love he gave us ever fade.

"Our children will grow up knowing what an incredible father he was and will always be, and I will carry his love, his memory and everything we shared with me always.

"He may have left this world committed to the duty he believed in, but his greatest legacy will always be the love he gave to his family.
