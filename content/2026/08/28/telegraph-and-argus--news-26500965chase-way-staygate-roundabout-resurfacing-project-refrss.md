A resurfacing project in Bradford has been completed a day ahead of schedule following three night-time closures.

Chase Way is located by the Staygate roundabout - where motorists can access the M606 from.

It's a small commercial road home to the Eurocam Technology Park, sitting near Mayo Avenue and Parkway.

A Bradford Council spokesperson posted on social media and said: "Chase Way resurfacing complete ahead of schedule.

"Delivered over three night-time closures, the resurfacing of Chase Way was completed a day earlier than planned thanks to the hard work, planning and collaboration of everyone involved.

"Working alongside National Highways, Heidelberg Materials and Jointline, Bradford Council teams completed the scheme while keeping disruption to a minimum and maintaining access for local businesses."
