A NEW horror bookshop is set to open its doors in a historic York building overlooking a graveyard – right in time for Halloween!

Horrifically Good Books is set to open its doors in Grade I listed 60 Goodramgate in York city centre at the end of October.

The shop – which is created by the team behind Criminally Good Books in Colliergate - will be dedicated to all things spooky, scary, and otherwise horrific.

Owner Isla Coole explained that she had originally looked at the historic Goodramgate shop for her original bookstore, Criminally Good Books, in 2023, but was unable to take on the building as it was undergoing restoration work at the time.

Once it returned to the market, she said that she was ‘blown away’ by how perfect the location would be for the horror bookshop that she had been planning for several years.

She said: “We have been looking for the perfect location for HGB for two years. The building is perfect for a horror bookstore as its graveyard adjacent next to Holy Trinity.

  Isla Coole of Criminally Good Books in Colliergate


“We have a series of opening events planned, which we will be announcing in the coming weeks and we will be hosting horror events instore and in venues around York.

“Retail is over two floors and we have a top floor community event space for writing workshops and other events.”

The shop will feature a variety of books including gothic and classic horror, folk horror, historic horror, translated work, young adult’s horror, and works by small press and indie publishers among many other staples of the genre.

Isla added: “Crime was always my first love, but there is a real appetite for horror with an increase of 21 per cent of horror sales in the UK last year alone.

  The graveyard from a nearby shop

“We sell a lot of horror crime in the current shop so it was a natural progression without diluting the CGBs brand to open a purely horror store.”

The shop’s first horror event will be held in partnership with Criminally Good Books at Merchant Adventurers Hall on October 23 and will feature illustrator Dom&Ink – who will be doing a window installation at the shop - and popular Doctor Who writer and young adult author Juno Dawson.

Information on recruitment to be announced shortly, with updates set to be shared to @horrificallygoodbooks on Instagram.
