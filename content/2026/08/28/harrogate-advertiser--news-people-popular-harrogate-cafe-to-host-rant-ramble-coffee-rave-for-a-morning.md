# Popular Harrogate cafe to host Rant & Ramble Coffee Rave for a morning filled with music, movement and connection

## An award-winning cafe and food hall will be the only venue in Harrogate to host a new feel-good sensation sweeping Britain – morning coffee raves.

Rant & Ramble Coffee Raves have been bringing hundreds of people together across the UK, creating mornings filled with music, movement and connection.

Standing in a room first thing in the morning with hundreds of people dancing, singing, laughing and completely letting go has already brought hundreds of people together across the UK, creating mornings filled with music, movement and connection.

![Rant & Ramble Coffee Rave - "There’s something pretty special about standing in a room first thing in the morning with hundreds of people dancing, singing, laughing and completely letting go." (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjE2MTAwZWVhLTYwYjUtNGMwOC1hMzNjLTY1MzE2NzhkMTlhNToxYTlmYTk2MC1hOGExLTQ1YTItYTE2MC0zZmQ0OWY5NmI2OWE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Rant & Ramble co-founder Leesa Collinge is confident the same thing will happen at Fodder when the event takes place on Friday, October 2, from 7am to 8am.

“You honestly have to experience a Rant & Ramble Coffee Rave to understand how it makes you feel,” said Leesa. .

"They walk in tired and walk out absolutely buzzing.

"For that hour, nobody is thinking about work, the washing, their to do list or whatever else life is throwing at them.

"They’re just there, completely in the moment.

The Harrogate Rant & Ramble Coffee Rave will kick off at 7am with a walk around the Great Yorkshire Showground, led by Leesa Collinge, before everyone heads into Fodder Café for a dance from 7:30am to 8am.

Participants can expect a live DJ, incredible percussion from Romi Foxx, big tunes, hands in the air moments, coffee and a room full of people choosing to start their day feeling great.

After the rave, Fodder will be serving special breakfast dishes from 8am, giving everyone the chance to stay, refuel and keep the morning going, with coffee provided by Huddersfield-based Coffee Brothers who are the main coffee suppliers at Fodder.

Vanessa Pitt, Fodder Manager, said: “We are so excited to be hosting a morning coffee rave at Fodder.

"We saw how Harrogate got behind the Symphonic Ibiza concert in the Main Ring, so we know there’s an appetite for great music.”
