A York rock band is set to launch their new album with an upcoming concert.

Progressive rock band One Iota will return to the Joseph Rowntree Theatre on Saturday, September 26, to launch their new album, Chapter and Verse.

The band’s show will include cinematic projections, a light show, and concert audio from Camel Events.

Adam Dawson, lead vocals, said: "We've created a show that surrounds the audience with music, visuals and emotion, bringing these songs to life on a scale we've never attempted before."

The double album, mixed by producer Dave Creffield, who has worked with the Kaiser Chiefs and Embrace, explores themes of "memory, loss, hope, and redemption".

The band, led by Mr Dawson, features Andrew Bowen on lead guitar, Dave Clements on bass, Dave Soulsby on keyboards, Phil Everard on drums, Chuck Walton on pedal steel guitar, and Andrew Prudames on saxophone.

Doors open at 6.30pm, and tickets are available online.
