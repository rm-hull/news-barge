Sherburn Aero Club has cancelled tomorrow’s annual family event, Saturday 29th August, due to the severe weather.

A spokesperson said, “It is not a decision we have taken lightly and know a lot of people will be disappointed but safety of the public and aircraft operations is paramount. However, we will be back in 2027”.

There was on the agenda:

Aeroplane & helicopter flights

Aerobatic display

Fairground

Live music from York Mix Radio

Market stalls

Fantastic food vendors

Go Karts

Paintballing

Historic emergency vehicles
