A woman on a hot air balloon which landed in the middle of a Bishop Auckland housing estate has spoken of her momentary panic as the balloon started heading towards the homes.

The Virgin-branded balloon landed in a ditch just off Marsh Way on the Bishops Park housing development around 8.30pm on Saturday (August 22).

Bemused residents rushed outside to see what was happening as the balloon landed outside their homes.

  Hot air balloon. 

*(Image: RACHEL SCOTT)*

Virgin Balloon Flights has since confirmed the unusual landing location was "intentionally selected" by the pilot who completed a safe descent.

Naomi Martin, 29, from Consett, who was on the balloon with mum Sharon as a 50th birthday treat, said: "Usually they would land in a field but they need to be granted access.

  Naomi Martin and her mum, Sharon Boyle. 

*(Image: NAOMI MARTIN)*

"A lot of the fields we went over didn't have access and then there started to be loads of power lines so we couldn't land.

"We all started wondering where we were going to land and then we started going towards the houses and we thought, 'oh no.'

  A picture Naomi took on the hot air balloon. 

*(Image: NAOMI MARTIN)*

"With hot air balloons, you have to go where the wind takes you.

"The pilot did such a good job of landing; I don't know how he did it in that small space.

   

*(Image: NAOMI MARTIN)*

"He was obviously trying to stay calm but as we were getting closer to the houses we started to think, 'Where are we actually going to land?'.

"We were all a bit nervous, but once we landed it was fine.

"It was a shock for us when the fire engine and policeman turned up.

"But the pilot did a very good job and I think it made everyone's night."

  Hot air balloon. 

*(Image: RACHELL SCOTT)*

Naomi, a stay-at-home mum, was on board with sixteen other passengers, including her mum, a mental health nurse.

She said staff followed the hot air balloon in a van and picked them up after landing.

A Virgin Balloon Flights spokesperson said: "The pilot carried out a routine landing in line with our established safety procedures, and everyone on board landed safely.

"As is standard with hot-air balloon flights, the precise landing location cannot be predetermined, as it depends on the wind and ground conditions at the time.

"Throughout the flight, the pilot continually assessed the conditions, intentionally selected that site as an appropriate landing location and completed a safe, controlled landing.

  Hot air balloon. 

*(Image: HOLLIE SMITH)*

"The passengers then continued with the usual post-flight experience, including the traditional balloon pack-away, certificate presentation and a celebratory glass of prosecco."

“As is standard practice, the basket and balloon were collected by our ground crew and transported from the landing site by trailer."

“We would also like to thank the local residents who kindly assisted our crew at the landing site.”

Pictures shared previously showed a large crowd gather as the balloon was packed away.

Hollie Smith, who lives nearby, previously told how she rang emergency services, fearing something had happened with the balloon.

She said: "We saw it coming towards the estate.
