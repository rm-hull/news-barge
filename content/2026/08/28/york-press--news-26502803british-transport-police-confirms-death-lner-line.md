POLICE have issued an update following a major incident which affected train services earlier this week.

British Transport Police was called to Hatfield railway station at 11.35am on Wednesday (26 August) following reports of a casualty on the tracks.

The incident affected trains running from London and Peterborough, which disrupted LNER services between London Kings Cross and York, for several hours with services cancelled and delayed while emergency services worked to make the scene safe.

  **Recommended reads:**

A spokesman for British Transport Police said: "Officers were called to Hatfield railway station at 11.35am on Wednesday (26 August) following reports of a casualty on the tracks.

"Paramedics also attended, however sadly a person was pronounced dead at the scene. The incident is not being treated as suspicious, and a file will be prepared for the coroner."

Passengers travelling to York and North Yorkshire were among those advised to check their journeys, with delays, cancellations, and short-notice alterations reported.
