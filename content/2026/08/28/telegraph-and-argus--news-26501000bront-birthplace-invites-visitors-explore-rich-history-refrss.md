THE Brontë Birthplace will open its doors for a national festival.

Located in Thornton, Bradford, the historic site forms part of the Brontë story, and will take part in Heritage Open Days, England’s largest community-led celebration of history and culture.

An exhibition at the birthplace will delve into the building’s past, tracing its journey through a butcher’s shop, visitor attraction, café, and a community-owned heritage site.

Thomas Haigh, vice chair and marketing and audience development lead at the Brontë Birthplace, said: "While the Brontë Birthplace will always be significant for being the first home of Charlotte, Emily, Anne, and Branwell, we know it enjoyed a rich history after their departure.

"We want to share this 200-year story with our visitors as well as showcase the challenges, discoveries, and community effort behind the restoration and how this remarkable building continues to evolve and serve a new generation of visitors."

The exhibition will feature photographs, plans, memories, and other artefacts that reveal the many lives of the building since the Brontë family lived there.

A free walking tour of Thornton will also be offered as part of the Heritage Open Days programme.

Led by local historian and Brontë family expert Paul Crossley, the two-hour tour will begin at the birthplace and take visitors to key sites including the Brontë Bell Chapel ruins, Kipping House, South Square Centre, and the village’s historic snickets and ginnels.

The tour concludes at the Brontë Birthplace, where visitors can enjoy the café, browse the gift shop, or book a house tour.

Heritage Open Days takes place every September and gives people the opportunity to explore lesser-known historical sites and try new experiences, all for free.

The Brontë Birthplace exhibition will be open from 11am to 4pm on Sunday, September 13, with Mr Haigh available to answer questions from visitors.

The walking tour will take place on Saturday, September 19, starting at 11am.

Both events are free to attend.

More information is available at brontebirthplace.com.
