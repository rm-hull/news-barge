A Ryedale pub has reopened its doors following a major investment.

The Bay Horse Inn in Pickering has relaunched with a fresh look following a £400,000 refurbishment by pub company Punch Pubs & Co.

Newlyweds Gareth and Jamie Furness, who previously owned a pub in Liverpool, have taken over the venue through Punch's Management Partnership scheme.

Mr Furness said: "When we heard a pub in Pickering may be available under Punch’s Management Partnership scheme, our instincts were to go for it, and we set off on a road trip to visit the pub.

  



"As soon as we stepped inside, we both just looked at each other and said yes, it just felt right for us.

"We have always had a fondness of North Yorkshire and have been many times on holiday in our touring caravan, mostly to Whitby.

"We appreciated all the traditional features of the building as well as some slightly more modern additions."

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

The pub underwent a sympathetic refurbishment over the summer, maintaining its historical character while introducing a fresher, more vibrant feel.

Mr Furness said the improvements are already paying off.

He said: "We are seeing more locals returning to the pub, faces that haven’t been seen here for many years and we are seeing them on a regular basis, whether that be for food, drinks or to enjoy the beer garden.

"They all comment on how amazing the pub now looks and feels and what great value the food is.

  The beer garden 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

"The refurb has really put the Bay Horse Inn back on the map and firmly at the heart of the Pickering community."

Tony Iles, Operations Manager at Punch Pubs & Co., welcomed the couple’s arrival.

Mr Iles said: "This is a wonderful pub in Pickering and I’m delighted Gareth and Jamie have taken over.

"They have experience in running a community focussed pub and I’m confident they’ll put their unique stamp on the place."

  The exterior of The Bay Horse in Pickering 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

The Furnesses plan to make the Bay Horse Inn a true community hub.

Jamie said: "Week by week as we find our feet we are introducing new things.

"We have already brought Friday night karaoke back and it has been a massive hit with the locals, and the pub has never been so busy.

"We are introducing the Great British Quiz, a league pool team, league darts team.

"Our aim is to be a community hub where we offer something for everyone including knit and natter groups, cake and sip classes and more."
