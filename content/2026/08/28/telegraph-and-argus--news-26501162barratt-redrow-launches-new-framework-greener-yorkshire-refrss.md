A NEW placemaking framework is setting the standard for healthier, greener neighbourhoods.

Barratt Redrow, the UK’s largest housebuilder, has launched the Great Places framework to guide the future design of its developments, including those in West Yorkshire.

The framework aims to improve the quality of life for residents by focusing on nature-rich environments, safe streets, and walkable neighbourhoods.

Joe Stenson, group head of urban design at Barratt Redrow, said: "Customers are telling us very clearly, they want more than just a home.

"They want places that feel safe, connected, and close to nature, with streets, green spaces and local facilities that support everyday life.

"Using our unified standard, we’re embedding this customer expectation directly into the design and delivery process of our new communities, alongside nationwide planning and design guidance."

Great Places embeds the government backed Building for a Healthy Life guidance into one group-wide design framework.

The company delivers more than 17,000 homes each year across England, Scotland, and Wales, and the framework is expected to benefit hundreds of thousands of future residents.

Eight key outcomes guide the design of each development: natural connections, getting around, facilities and stewardship, housing affordability, memorable character, healthy streets, green and blue infrastructure, and well-defined streets and spaces.
