# Man in his 70s dies in hospital after North Yorkshire two-car crash

## A man in his 70s has died after being taken to hospital following a two-car crash on a North Yorkshire road.

The crash happened on the B1363 York Road, approaching Sutton-on-the-Forest, at around 3.15pm on Wednesday (Aug 12).

A red Vauxhall Astra and a silver Audi were involved.

![B1363, York Road, approaching Sutton-on-the-Forest](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/28/16/10/B1363-York-Road-approaching-Sutton-on-the-Forest.png?trim=0,60,0,60&crop=&width=640&quality=65)



The road was closed for a number of hours while police investigated the scene and the vehicles were recovered.

The driver of the red Astra, a man in his 70s, was taken to hospital with injuries which were initially described as minor.

North Yorkshire Police has now confirmed that, following a period of time in hospital receiving treatment, the man has died.

The driver of the Audi has assisted officers with their enquiries.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: “Police are appealing for anyone who witnessed the crash, saw either vehicle beforehand or has relevant dashcam footage to come forward.

“Anyone with information can contact North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260156472.”
