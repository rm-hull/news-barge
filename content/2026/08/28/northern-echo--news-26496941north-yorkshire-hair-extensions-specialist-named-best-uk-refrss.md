A North Yorkshire hair extensions specialist has been named the best in the country after just one year of trading from her farm studio.

Kylie Hammond, 39, who runs FAKE at Crakehall, took first place nationally in the Hair Extensions Specialist of the Year category at the UK Hair and Beauty Awards.

The awards were presented by television personality Jake Quickenden at the AO Arena in Manchester on August 15.

  Kylie Hammond at the hair and beauty awards 

*(Image: PR)*

Entries were submitted anonymously and assessed by three independent judges, each with at least ten years' experience in the industry.

Kylie was ranked first in the national top 100 for her category, with the organisers saying the 2026 awards attracted thousands of entries.

The accolade comes after more than 20 years in the hair industry for Kylie, including 13 years in stylist and management roles at London salons specialising in hair extensions and hair loss.

  Kylie in her studio in North Yorkshire 

*(Image: PR)*

Her former clients included film and television actors, professional models and content creators.

She returned to North Yorkshire two years ago before opening her own studio at Crakehall last year.

There, she has developed her own style of tailor-made hair extensions, with each set created by hand to the client's specification.

  Kylie with Jake Quickenden at the awards 

*(Image: PR)*

Her colour-matching and fitting technique is designed to make the extensions virtually undetectable.

Kylie said: "Winning this after only a year of trading feels like an incredible achievement, but I've been working towards it a lot longer than that."

She added: "I spent years in some of the best hair extensions salons in London, and after developing my own style of hair extensions, we decided we wanted to come back to North Yorkshire with the goal of building the number 1 hair extensions salon in the UK.

"Being crowned the UK's number 1 hair extensions specialist is just the start for us, but it's an incredible reward for the work we've put in over the last 12 months of trading."

FAKE specialises in premium hair extensions, with hair sourced from Eastern Europe.

Each set is made by hand in the salon and tailored to the individual client.
