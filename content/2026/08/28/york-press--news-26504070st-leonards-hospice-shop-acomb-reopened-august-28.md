## WATCH - Popular York charity shop reopens after major renovation project

Customers admiring the book section *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

A YORK charity shop which has served a suburb for 35 years has undergone a modern and fresh makeover.

The St Leonard’s Hospice shop in Acomb reopened this morning (Friday, August 28) after closing its doors for a renovation nine weeks ago.

The popular store, which has been part of the suburb’s high street since the 1990s and was the charity’s first shop, has seen its old store completely revamped into something new with a whole new refreshed layout.

As the ribbon was cut, around twenty people gathered outside the shop with the team saying that visitors had been trying the door since the team arrived over an hour earlier.

The first donations were dropped off at the door before the shop officially opened, with a sale made just five minutes after the public were let inside to explore the new look shop.

Sarah Baldock, retail area manager at St Leonard’s Hospice, said that the changes were intended to help shoppers and staff alike, with changes to the donation process and to modernise the shop itself.


She said: “The Acomb shop is our oldest shop, so it's been in need of a bit of TLC for a while now.

"We've also made a lot of operational changes recently – we opened our new warehouse in Clifton Moor, which has been really successful, but we’ve outgrown our donation centre in Acomb and that’s closed now.

“What we wanted is to ensure that people could still donate in Acomb, because we’ve been here for a very long time now and it's a such a key part of our supporter base, so as well as all the stuff that people will see on the shop floor, we've made a lot of changes behind the scenes as well.

  Sarah Baldock, retail area manager at St Leonard’s Hospice 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

“We’ve got a better environment, so we can process those donations quicker. We are just so lucky to be supported by the people of the Acomb and North Yorkshire - a lot of him we've had lived experience with the hospice - and that's why they bring their donations to us.

“It’s really important to have somewhere where people can bring their stuff in Acomb, even with us like relocating our warehouse, so hopefully that will make things easier for donors.”

The shop is the fifth to adopt St Leonard’s new retail style - following refurbishments in Selby, Malton, Pocklington, and Colliergate in York.

  A section of women's clothing close to entrance 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Upgrades include digital screens at the till and in the window, improved storage and sorting areas, and a modern look throughout.

Sarah added: “Customers are going to really like the changes we’ve made in the shop, so we’ve freshened things up and made them a lot more modern.

“We’ve changed the layout of our shop, but we haven't lost any selling space. We've still got as much stock out as ever, so we've got a great selection of clothing, toys, and bric-a-brac.

  The new and improved bric-a-brac section 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

“We’re really fortunate here with the donations that the stock turns over really quickly, so we have lots of regulars – some who pop in every day, some every week – so it’s always worth coming to see what we’ve got.”

Acomb St Leonard’s will be open Monday to Saturday from 9am to 4.30pm, though it will remain closed on the bank holiday on Monday, August 31.
