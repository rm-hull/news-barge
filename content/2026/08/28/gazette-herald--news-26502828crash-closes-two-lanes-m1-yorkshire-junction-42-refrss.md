A CRASH has closed two lanes of a major Yorkshire motorway this morning (Friday, August 28).

The lane closures are on the four-lane southbound M1 from junction 42 for Lofthouse to junction 41 for the A650 and Carrgate.

Images from traffic cameras at junction 42 taken at 7.52am this morning show the extent of the delays.

The Press will bring any more information about the detail of the road closure as we receive it.
