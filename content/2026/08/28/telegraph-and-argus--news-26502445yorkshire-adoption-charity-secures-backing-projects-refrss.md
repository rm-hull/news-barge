NEW funding will help deliver fresh support for adopted children and their families.

Yorkshire-based children’s charity and voluntary adoption agency Adoption Matters will be involved in two new projects backed by Adoption England’s Voluntary Adoption Agency Early Support Innovation Fund.

Susy White, chief executive of Adoption Matters, said: "We are thrilled to be involved in two of these exciting and innovative projects.

"This funding recognises the value of providing support early, before families reach crisis point, and creates an opportunity to develop services that truly reflect the needs of adopted children, young people and their families."

The North West project, led alongside Caritas Care and Regional Adoption Agency partners across the North West, will support adopted pre-teens and teenagers aged 11 to 16 with therapeutic group sessions, creative and music-based activities, specialist trauma-informed support, and resources for parents, carers and schools.

Adoption Matters is a key partner in the Yorkshire Consortium’s “Village” project, designed to build peer-led support communities for adoptive families.

The aim is to reduce isolation, strengthen family resilience and provide access to both peer and professional support.

These projects are part of a broader national strategy to deliver earlier, more consistent adoption support through closer collaboration between voluntary and regional agencies.
