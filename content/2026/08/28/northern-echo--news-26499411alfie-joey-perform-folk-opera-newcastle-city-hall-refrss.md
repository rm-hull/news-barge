Former BBC Radio Newcastle presenter Alfie Joey has joined the cast of a new folk opera.

Titled No More Sleeping, the musical will be performed at Newcastle City Hall on Wednesday, September 23, at 7.15pm.

Described as “Alice in Wonderland meets The Wizard of Oz,” the show is set in the North East and follows a teenage girl coping with the loss of her grandparents.

Her life changes when she receives Marra, a Multifunctioning Autonomous Robotic Resource Assistant that leads her on a journey through the region’s industrial and cultural past.

Mr Joey said: "I’m a big fan of Richard O’Neill and I do a lot of work with Seven Stories as a writer and illustrator, and I love going into schools, so this combines all three.

"I have been on stage all my life, but I have never performed on a stage like this.

"This is a big moment for them to perform on stage and will be an experience these young people will remember forever."

The production is a collaboration between pupils from Bishop Chadwick Catholic Education Trust and writer Richard O’Neill MBE, musician Mike McGrother, illustrator Michelle Russell, and Seven Stories – The National Centre for Children’s Books.

Brendan Tapping, CEO of Bishop Chadwick Catholic Education Trust, said: "We are delighted to once more be working with pupils right across our Trust to produce a spectacular performance.

"At its heart, No More Sleeping is a story of resilience, transformation, and community.
