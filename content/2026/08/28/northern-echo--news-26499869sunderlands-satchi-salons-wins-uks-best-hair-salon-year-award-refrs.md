A North East hair salon has been crowned the best in the UK.

Satchi Salons, based at Everlast Gym on Timber Beach Road in Sunderland, won the title of Best Hair Salon of the Year at the UK Hair and Beauty Awards, held in Manchester.

The salon also placed in several other categories, including third place in the Best Trainee category for junior Bobby Parks, fourth in Best Colour Salon, and seventh for Best Business Branding.

Scott Atchison, who founded the salon 29 years ago, said: "To get national first place for Best Hair Salon of the Year is such an honour and testament to the work of all of our team.

"We have spent a great deal of time and effort bringing together the best people who have such a strong work ethic and want to do the very best for our loyal clients.

"To have that commitment and passion recognised on a national stage is absolutely incredible."

He said he was particularly proud of the recognition achieved by the younger members of the team.

Mr Atchison said: "It makes it even more special, seeing our younger team members receiving national recognition too.

"Investing in young people and giving them opportunities has become a huge part of what Satchi stands for.

"Seeing Bobby, Chloe and our junior team recognised nationally makes me every bit as proud as winning the main salon award."

The Satchi Junior Team also placed 11th for Best Salon Team and 13th for Best New Salon.

Chloe Carney took 10th place in the Trainee of the Year category.

Despite nearly three decades in the industry, Mr Atchison said he is always looking for new opportunities and has recently launched his own line of hair care products for both salon professionals and clients.
