Part of Millington Lane, Kilnwick Percy, will close to through traffic for up to three days from September 14, 2026.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has made an order preventing vehicles from using the section between Kilnwick Percy Hall Access Road and Rose Cottages.

The closure will begin at 8am on September 14, 2026 and is scheduled to end at 6pm on September 16, 2026.

The council said the road is too narrow to maintain minimum widths while works are carried out.

Works will be completed in a manner intended to ensure public safety.

The road will be closed to all through traffic, but access will remain available for residents, emergency vehicles and pedestrians.

Traffic affected by the order will be directed along Millington Lane, Fishpond Hill, Kilnwick Percy Hill, Cattle Hill, Coldwold, Cobdale Lane, Cobdale, Pasture Lane, Millington Pastures, Wood Gate, Swineridge Lane, Church Lane, Martin Lane and Main Street.

The diversion passes through Huggate, Nunburnholme, Warter and Millington. It is within the Wolds Weighton Ward.

Signs will be placed along the diversion route when the closure starts.

The order will come into force on September 14, 2026 and remain in force for no more than 18 months, or until the works are finished, whichever is earlier.

Further information is available from Streetscene Services, Highways Maintenance, on (01482) 393939.
