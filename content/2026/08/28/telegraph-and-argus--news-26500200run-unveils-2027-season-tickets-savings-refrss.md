RUNNERS are being offered a new way to plan ahead and save money on their 2027 race calendar.

Jane Tomlinson’s Run For All has launched season-ticket packages for 2027, giving runners the chance to secure entry to multiple events at a discounted rate.

Tristan Batley-Kyle, operations director at Run For All, said: "We're delighted to launch our 2027 season ticket packages, giving runners an easy and affordable way to plan their year of events in advance while making valuable savings on entry fees."

The season tickets cover a range of distances and include flagship events such as the Rob Burrow Leeds Marathon, Altra Yorkshire Marathon, and Leeds 10K.

Runners who complete all events included in their season ticket will also receive a special third medal in addition to their race day medals.

The packages offer discounted entry to events in Leeds, York, Sheffield, and Lincoln.
