Celebrate the sights, sounds, and skills of the Middle Ages this bank holiday – as Barley Hall host a Medieval Weekend.

Taking place at the city centre attraction from **Saturday 29 to Monday 31 August**, experts will bring this era of history to life with talks and live demonstrations of armour, weaponry, and combat.

Re-enactors from Three Swords will be joining the Barley Hall team to talk about the conflicts that were taking place in the Middle Ages, including the infamous 30-year-long War of the Roses between the House of Lancaster and the House of York.

Visitors can see – and maybe even handle – some of the armour and weapons that medieval warriors would have worn, before the warriors open the fechtbücher (a medieval combat guide) to demonstrate both armed and unarmed combat.

“We are hugely excited to be embracing this turbulent part of English history for our Medieval Weekend at Barley Hall,” said Richard Jones, event manager for York Archaeology which owns the rebuilt medieval town-house in Coffee Yard.

“The external courtyard and grandeur of the Great Hall provide superb locations for this kind of display, whatever weather the bank holiday weekend throws at us.”

The event runs each day from 10am to 5pm (last admissions at 4.30pm), and is included with admission to Barley Hall.

Admission prices are £9.50 for adults, £5.50 for children and £7.75 for students with family tickets available from £22.75.

The main presentations take place at 11.30am and 2.00pm and guided tours of Barley Hall at 1.00pm and 3.00pm. The Three Swords team will be in the Great Hall throughout the day.

Barley Hall is located in Coffee Yard, which is accessed via a snickleway from either Stonegate (under the Ye Olde Starre Inn sign) or Grape Lane.
