FORMER footballers are heading to the ring for a charity boxing night in support of good causes.

Leroy Lita, who played for Middlesbrough, and Nile Ranger are among 14 ex-professional footballers confirmed for A Night to Remember III, which takes place on October 22 at the Grosvenor House Hotel on Park Lane in London.

Organised by Pro Project Promotions, the event aims to raise £500,000 for charity, building on the £201,500 already raised across its first two events.

Graham Stack, former Arsenal goalkeeper and founder of Pro Project Promotions, said: "This is our biggest and most emotional line-up yet.

"Every one of these lads has a story worth telling—some have played at the very top of the game, others have had to rebuild their whole lives, and all of them are stepping back into a ring for people who need it more than they do.

"We've raised over £201,500 for charity in our first two events and we want the Grosvenor on 22nd October to be our biggest night yet."

A Night to Remember III will feature seven three-round contests between former footballers, all broadcast live on Sports.com.

Among those taking part is Ranger, the former Newcastle United striker whose career was derailed by off-field issues that led to a prison sentence for online banking fraud.

He now sees the event as part of his ongoing journey to rebuild both his career and reputation.

Also stepping into the ring is Jay Emmanuel-Thomas, a former Arsenal prospect who was jailed in 2023 for his involvement in a £600,000 cannabis smuggling plot.

His participation is seen as one of the series' most powerful redemption stories.

Lita, who spent part of his career with Middlesbrough, returns to the ring after competing in the event’s first edition.

Other names on the card include Gary Madine, Harlee Dean, Simon Cox, Lee Cook, Anthony Gardner, David Norris, Martin Woods, Jamie Sendles-White, Sebastien Bassong, Matthew Briggs, Armand Traore, and Emmanuel-Thomas.

Pro Project Promotions was founded to give retired athletes a new sense of purpose through competition and charity, supporting both their physical fitness and mental wellbeing.
