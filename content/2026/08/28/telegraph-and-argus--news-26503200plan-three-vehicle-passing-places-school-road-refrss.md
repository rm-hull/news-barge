A Calderdale school is looking to build in some passing places into the tricky single-track route to the school.

The William Henry Smith Foundation has applied to Calderdale Council seeking permission to make the changes at the William Henry Smith School at Rastrick, near Brighouse.

They are looking to improve the junction of Boothroyd Lane and Lower Edge Road, which will be widened, and incorporate three passing points along the single track route to the school.

The William Henry Smith School and Sixth Form is a specialist day and residential school for boys aged five to 19 with social, emotional and mental health needs, all of whom have an Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP).

Supporting statements with the application say the proposals aim to address highway safety issues.

The existing site access is only only single carriageway.

Creating the passing places will involve relocating and reconstructing sections of dry stone walling to enable the works, say the papers.

Walls will be carefully dismantled and reconstructed in revised positions using existing materials wherever possible, ensuring that the traditional character of the landscape is maintained, they add.

The proposal has been developed in response to operational and highway safety issues associated with the existing access serving the school, say the statements – “safe and suitable” is the aim, they say.

Given the specialist nature of the school and the varied range of vehicles accessing the site, including staff vehicles, visitor traffic, school transport, servicing vehicles and emergency access requirements, the existing access configuration has led to significant highway safety issues, say the statements.

The works are modest but will being benefit, they argue.

The site is in the Green Belt, note the papers, but harm to it will be very limited.

The application, number 26/00622/FUL, and supporting papers can be viewed on the council’s Planning Portal.
