A SOLD-OUT crowd packed The Piece Hall for Turnstile’s only UK headline show of the year.

The American hardcore band performed as part of the TK Maxx presents Live at The Piece Hall summer concert series in Halifax.

The show sold out within minutes of tickets going on sale.

Turnstile opened with Birds, Real Thing, and Endless, delivering a setlist spanning their 16-year career, including tracks from Nonstop Feeling, Time and Space, Glow On, and Never Enough.

The band paid tribute to Dolly Parton by playing Islands In The Stream before taking the stage.

Fans enjoyed favourites such as I Don’t Wanna Be Blind, Mystery, and Look Out For Me, as well as a cover of The Stone Roses’ I Wanna Be Adored.

The night ended with a four-song encore featuring Holiday, Blackout, Never Enough, and TLC.

The summer concert series concludes tonight, with a headline performance by Bastille.
