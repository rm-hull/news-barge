A mountain biker was rescued after falling from a height in woodland.

Mountain rescue crewd were called by paramedics at about 7.25pm on Monday (August 24) to help the injured rider in Errington Woods near New Marske.

Cleveland Mountain Rescue Team's Land Rovers were sent to the woods with one sent as close to the biker as possible.

They were treated by North East Ambulance Service and immobilised in a vacuum mattress before being wheeled on a stretcher to the Land Rover to be driven back to the waiting ambulance.

He was taken to James Cook Hospital for treatment having suffered facial injuries.

Cleveland Mountain Rescue Team said: "We wish the casualty a speedy recovery."

A total of 16 team members were involved in the rescue, which lasted just short of three hours.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: “We received a call at 6.21pm on Monday to reports of a person injured following a biking accident in Errington Wood in New Marske.

"We dispatched an ambulance crew and a clinical team leader to the scene, and received support from our colleagues at Cleveland Police and the mountain rescue team. One patient was taken to James Cook hospital for further treatment.”
