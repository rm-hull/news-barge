# Protected snail threatens Donald Trump’s other luxury ballroom plan

![President Donald Trump's proposed 12,000-square-foot ballroom at his Doonbeg golf resort in Ireland faces construction delays after a formal appeal](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/02/15/aba710630d7095e23728237c5babe8d1Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1NzY1MjMz-2.72014179.jpg)

- President Donald Trump's proposed 12,000-square-foot ballroom at his Doonbeg golf resort in Ireland faces construction delays after a formal appeal.
- Resident Liam Madden filed the appeal, claiming planning documents omitted proposed helicopter landing pads and failed to assess their environmental impact.
- The appeal argues that helicopter activity could harm a protected local species of narrow-mouthed whorl snail in the surrounding marshy grasslands.
- Madden previously succeeded alongside environmental groups in blocking the Trump Organization's 2016 plans to build a rock sea wall at the resort.
- Although many local Doonbeg residents favor the ballroom for its economic benefits, Madden asked Ireland's planning appeals board to reject the project.
