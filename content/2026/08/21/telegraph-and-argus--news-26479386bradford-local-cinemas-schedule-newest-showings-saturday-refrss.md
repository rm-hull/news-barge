BRADFORD cinema-goers have a packed choice this Saturday, with superhero spectacle, family favourites, horror, comedy and prestige drama all on the big screen at ODEON Bradford and The Light Bradford.

The standout release is Spider-Man: Brand New Day, which has the widest range of screenings across both venues. At ODEON, the 12A film is showing from 10am until 11.10pm, including multiple afternoon and evening options. The Light has six screenings, starting at 11.45am and finishing with a 7.45pm performance.

  Christopher Nolan’s *The Odyssey* is also available at both cinemas. ODEON is screening the 15-rated, two-hour-53-minute epic at 12.20pm, 5.50pm and 8.10pm, while The Light offers showings at 3.30pm and 7.15pm.

## 
  **Family films**

  Families have plenty of early options, with *Toy Story 5*,* Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie*,* Minions & Monsters*,* Moana*and* Coyote vs. ACME* all playing across the city.

ODEON has the broadest choice for younger viewers:

- 
    *The Sheep Detectives* is showing at 10am as part of an ODEON Kids screening, with adult tickets from £1.
- 
    *Toy Story 5* is on at 10.15am, 12.30pm and 3pm.
- 
    *Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie* has screenings at 10am, 11am and 12.20pm.
- 
    *Minions & Monsters* is available at 10.10am and 11.20am.
- 
    *Moana* is showing at 10.15am, 11.30am and 1.50pm.
- 
    *Coyote vs. ACME* runs at 10.10am, 12.40pm, 3.10pm and 5.40pm.

  At The Light, *Coyote vs. ACME* is screening at 10am, 12.30pm and 3pm, while*Toy Story 5* starts at 10.15am.*Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie* is on at 9.50am and 12.15pm, with*Minions & Monsters* at 10.40am and 1pm. There is also a 10.40am Toddler Time showing of*Pinkfong Super Rescue 2*.

## 
  **Evening choices**

  Horror fans can see *Insidious: Out of the Further* throughout the day at ODEON, with performances from 1pm to 11pm. The Light is showing it at 2.40pm, 6.15pm and 8.45pm.

  For something lighter, comedy *Spa Weekend* is on at ODEON from 12.50pm through to 10.50pm, while The Light has screenings at 12.45pm and 6pm.

  The Light also has two more distinctive evening choices: food-world drama *Tony* at 3.15pm and 8.30pm, plus a 30th-anniversary screening of Danny Boyle’s*Trainspotting* at 8.30pm.

  Those after a later night can choose ODEON’s 11.15pm showing of action thriller *Mutiny*, the 11.10pm* Spider-Man*screening, or late performances of* Awarapan 2*,* The End of Oak Street*and* Insidious: Out of the Further*.

## 
  **Other films at ODEON**

ODEON is also showing a wide range of titles not listed at The Light, including:

- 
    *The End of Oak Street* at 1pm, 3.40pm, 6.10pm, 8.40pm and 11.10pm.
- 
    *Mutiny* at 1.20pm, 3.50pm, 6.20pm, 8.50pm and 11.15pm.
- 
    *Awarapan 2* across the afternoon and evening, beginning at 11.10am.
- 
    *Batwara 1947* at 2.20pm.
- 
    *Bethlehem Kudumba Unit* at 5.30pm.
- 
    *Judaa* at 3.15pm.
- 
    *Momo and the Time Thieves* at 10.30am.
- 
    *Bing & Friends: Birthday Celebration* at 10.20am, with adult tickets from £1.

Standard adult tickets at ODEON start from £12.50 for the listed screenings, while child prices are advertised from £7.11 on eligible films. Showtimes and availability can change, so filmgoers should check their chosen cinema’s booking page before travelling.
