FRAUDULENT bank notes were used to buy a laptop in a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a top of the range laptop was bought using fake bank notes in Knaresborough.

The county police say the incident happened when a person used social media to negotiate for an Apple MacBook and handed over £340 in counterfeit notes.

“The male in the attached shot may be able to assist us with our enquiries, and we would like to speak with him,” said a spokesperson for the force.

They added that this scam – where a suspect will negotiate a price then pay using fake notes – is not the only incident “but is a different location” and urged people to be careful.

If you have any information, please contact PC 662 ABEL on [samantha.abel@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:samantha.abel@northyorkshire.police.uk), use the NYP website or live chat, or call them on 101. If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.
