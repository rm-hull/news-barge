# EuroMillions results: Winning lottery and Thunderball numbers for Friday 21 August

- Tonight’s EuroMillions draw offered players the chance to win a jackpot of £42 million.
- To win the prize, a player had to get five main numbers plus two lucky stars.
- In the UK, every EuroMillions entry automatically includes a unique "UK Millionaire Maker" code, entering you into an additional draw with a guaranteed £1 million winner in every draw.
- The winning Thunderball numbers were **8**,** 9**,** 16**,** 37**and** 38**. The Thunderball was** 6.**
- The winning EuroMillions numbers were **10**,** 14**,** 15**,** 19**and** 45**. The lucky stars were** 4**and** 12.**The Millionaire Maker code was** VSCL 41380**
