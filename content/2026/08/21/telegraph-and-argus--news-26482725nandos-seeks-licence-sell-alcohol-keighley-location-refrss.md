NANDO'S has applied for a new licence at a Keighley retail park.

The company is seeking permission to sell alcohol for consumption on and off the premises at its new outlet in Alexandra Mills Retail Park, Gresley Road.

The licence would allow sales from 10am to 12am, Monday to Sunday.

Additionally, the application includes permission for late-night refreshments from 11pm to 12am, Monday to Sunday.

The application was filed with the City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council.

Interested parties, including responsible authorities and residents, can view the full application at the Licensing Authority's offices at Shearbridge Depot, Shearbridge Road, Bradford, or online at bradfordlicensing.org.uk.

Written representations can be made to the Licensing Authority via post or email to [licensing@bradford.gov.uk](mailto:licensing@bradford.gov.uk), until September 7.

Submitting a false statement in connection with a licence application is an offence, carrying an unlimited fine on summary conviction.

The application is being handled by John Gaunt & Partners, Solicitors.
