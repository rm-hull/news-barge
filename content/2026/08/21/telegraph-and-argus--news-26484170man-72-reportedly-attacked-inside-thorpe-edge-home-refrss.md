POLICE are hunting "three or four people wearing dark clothing and carrying weapons" after a man was reportedly attacked in his Bradford home.

The incident happened on Abbotside Close in the Thorpe Edge area at around 10.50pm on Thursday night.

West Yorkshire Police said a 72-year-old man suffered cuts to his arms and a facial injury.

## The scene

Footage shared on social media appeared to show a large emergency service presence in the area following the incident.

  The scene this morning 

*(Image: T&A)*

A police cordon was in place, with up to four police vehicles and two Yorkshire Ambulance Service vehicles visible.

## Police appeal

Officers from Bradford CID are now investigating and are urging anyone who witnessed the attack, has CCTV, dashcam or doorbell footage, or has any information that could assist enquiries to come forward.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Detectives are appealing for information and footage after a man was attacked at a house in Bradford.

"The incident occurred at an address in Abbotside Close in the Thorpe Edge area at about 10.50pm yesterday (Thursday).

  The scene this morning 

*(Image: T&A)*

"A 72-year-old man suffered cuts to his arms and a facial injury.

"Enquiries suggest three or four people wearing dark clothing and carrying weapons were seen near the property at the time of the incident and detectives from Bradford CID would be keen to speak with anyone who can assist this investigation.

"If you can help then please contact the team via www.westyorkshirepolice.co.uk/LiveChat

"The crime reference is 13260476539.

"Information can also be reported anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555111 or via their website."

Yorkshire Ambulance Service has been approached for comment.
