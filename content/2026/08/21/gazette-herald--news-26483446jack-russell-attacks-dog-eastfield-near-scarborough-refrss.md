A BLACK and white Jack Russell has attacked another dog in a coastal North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a dog was attacked by another dog in Eastfield, near Scarborough earlier this month.

The incident happened on Herdborough Road at around 10am on Tuesday, August 4.

"The aggressive dog is described as a black and white Jack Russell which got loose from its owners and attacked another dog which was going for a walk with it's owner at the time," said a spokesperson for the force.

Anyone with information or CCTV footage is asked to call 101, quoting incident number 12260149959, or email [dom.tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:dom.tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk.)
