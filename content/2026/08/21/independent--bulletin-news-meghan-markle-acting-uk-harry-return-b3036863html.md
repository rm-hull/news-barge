# Meghan ‘offered acting role’ as Sussexes prepare for UK return

- Meghan Markle could be set to resume her acting career as she and Prince Harry prepare to return to the UK before the end of the month.
- The couple are relocating to a non-royal residence outside London after six years in the United States and have enrolled their children, Archie and Lilibet, in British schools.
- Sources close to the Duchess of Sussex have not denied reports suggesting she has been offered an acting role in an upcoming project in the UK.
- Addressing questions about protection costs, Andy Burnham said security arrangements are a matter for Harry and Meghan while wishing them well with their move.
- The news arrives as Prince Harry is due to find out on Friday the financial costs of his dismissed legal claim against the publisher of the Daily Mail.
