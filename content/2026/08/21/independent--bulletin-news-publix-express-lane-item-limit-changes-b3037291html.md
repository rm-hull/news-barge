# Supermarket chain changes express lane rules to cut wait times

- Florida-based supermarket chain Publix is updating its express lane policy to reduce checkout wait times across its stores.
- Store associates will now have greater flexibility in managing customer flow instead of strictly enforcing a 10-item limit.
- Publix has not yet detailed specific guidelines for how express lanes will operate without explicit item limits.
- The policy transition is occurring gradually, with some locations removing item limits from signs while others retain legacy signage.
- Headquartered in Lakeland, Florida, Publix operates more than 1,400 supermarkets across eight southeastern states.
