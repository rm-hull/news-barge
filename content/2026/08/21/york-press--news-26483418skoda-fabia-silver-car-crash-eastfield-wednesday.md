POLICE are hunting for a silver car that fled the scene after a crash.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for witnesses after a crash involving a silver vehicle that failed to stop at the scene and a blue Skoda Fabia.

The incident happened at around 6.45am on Wednesday (August 19) at the junction of Cayton Low Road and Holme Hill in Eastfield.

"We are appealing to anyone who witnessed the incident, saw either vehicle in the area before the collision, or has relevant CCTV, dashcam, or doorbell camera footage, to get in touch," said a spokesperson for the force.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Josh Hocken by email at [Josh.Hocken@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Josh.Hocken@northyorkshire.police.uk), through the North Yorkshire Police website, or by calling 101.

Please quote reference 12260161507 when providing information.
