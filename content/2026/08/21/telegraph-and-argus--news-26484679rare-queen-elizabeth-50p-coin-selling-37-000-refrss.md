A rare 50p coin featuring Queen Elizabeth II is being sold on eBay.

The item is listed on the online marketplace for over 74,000 times its face value and features the Battle of Hastings on one side.

With Queen Elizabeth II on the back, the coin dates back to 2016.

The description of the item reads: "The 2016 Battle of Hastings 50p coin is a rare collectable piece minted in Great Britain during the reign of Queen Elizabeth II.
