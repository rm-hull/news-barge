The final Grand Tour of the year is on the horizon as the peloton gathers in Monaco for the start of the Vuelta a Espana.

Defending champion Jonas Vingegaard will not take to the start line after winning the Giro d’Italia this spring before crashing and breaking his collarbone at the Tour de France last month.

But his great rival Tadej Pogacar - seemingly not content with the Dane completing the trio of grand tour victories before him - is the headline name as he returns to the Vuelta for the first time in seven years, after he finished third, winning three stages and the best young rider’s classification, in 2019.

The two-time world champion is, as always, the overwhelming favourite, even after his exploits winning a record-equalling fifth Tour de France this summer, while his UAE Team Emirates-XRG outfit have enough quality to complete a podium sweep, with Joao Almeida and Jay Vine in tow as super-domestiques.

Away from the general classification battle British starlet Matthew Brennan has been selected for his first grand tour, while the evenly-matched Wout van Aert and Mads Pedersen are likely to do battle for the points jersey.

*Here’s everything you need to know to watch the race.*

## When is the Vuelta a Espana 2026?

The Vuelta a Espana runs for 21 stages plus two rest days from 22 August to 13 September 2026, beginning in Monaco and finishing in Granada.

## How can I watch it?

Viewers in the UK can watch the Vuelta a Espana on TNT Sports and streaming service HBO Max. Free-to-air highlights will be available on DMAX.
