AN INVESTIGATION is underway after a person has stolen from a gambling machine inside a pub in Whitby.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a person stole from a gambling machine in Whitby last month.

It happened in the George Hotel at around 3.30pm on Saturday 25 July, and involved money being stolen from a gambling machine.
