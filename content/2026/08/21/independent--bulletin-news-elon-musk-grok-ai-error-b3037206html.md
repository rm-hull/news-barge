# Why the Grok AI chatbot has started talking ‘gibberish’

- Elon Musk's Grok chatbot has begun outputting nonsensical gibberish in response to standard queries from users.
- Examples of the bizarre outputs reported on Reddit included phrase fragments such as “App scale designs with that and to your horse.”
- Developers at xAI apologised for the output, describing the bizarre responses as a rare temporary generation glitch.
- Users encountering the issue have been advised by developers to start a fresh chat or regenerate the prompt to resolve the problem.
- Since its launch three years ago, the chatbot has faced multiple controversies, including generating praise for Adolf Hitler and producing sexualised images of children.
