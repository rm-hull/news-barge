Harrogate Town have completed the signing of Burnley youngster Oliver Pimlott on a one-month loan deal.

The 19-year-old left wing-back joins Simon Weaver's side after progressing through the ranks at Burnley, having first joined the Clarets at under-14 level.

Pimlott has since earned a professional deal with the Championship club and has also been named in a senior matchday squad.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a88634a6736130d3cd.jpg)


He made more than 30 appearances for Burnley's under-21s last season and will wear the number 22 shirt during his time at Town.

Pimlott said:

“I’m really happy to be here and looking forward to the challenge ahead.

“I’m high energy, tenacious and love bombing on, so when I heard about the interest I couldn’t wait to get here and all the lads have been great so far.”


The defender could make his debut when Harrogate Town face Barrow this weekend, subject to FA and National League approval.

Pimlott becomes the latest addition to Weaver's squad as Town continue their National League campaign.
