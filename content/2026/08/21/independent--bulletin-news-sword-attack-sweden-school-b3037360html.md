# Sword-wielding teen arrested after fatal attack at school in Sweden

![Shots were fired during the arrest but it was not clear whether the suspect had been injured](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/21/21/2026-08-21T161240Z_293887159_RC233NALEODU_RTRMADP_3_SWEDEN-POLICE-FAGERSTA.JPG)

- An 18-year-old has been arrested in a sword attack at the Brinell School in Fagersta, central Sweden, that left one person dead and two others seriously injured.
- Police found one person dead as they rushed to the school on Friday where between 400 and 500 students were attending classes.
- Police Chief Tommy Alriksson said investigators currently believe the suspect, who was wearing a helmet during the attack, acted alone, though the motive remains unknown as authorities begin reviewing security camera footage.
- Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson shared his condolences for those affected by the tragedy and said that police are working to determine what led to the violence.
- In the aftermath of the attack, local authorities established a crisis center in a nearby sports hall, set up roadblocks, and temporarily locked down several neighboring schools.
