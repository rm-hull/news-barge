Jet2 has launched a range of Yorkshire-exclusive travel discounts while reaffirming its commitment to Leeds Bradford Airport with its biggest ever programme from the airport.

The airline and tour operator announced that customers can now claim £100 off holiday bookings and 10% off flight bookings from Leeds Bradford Airport through a limited-time promotion.

Holidaymakers can receive £100 off bookings using the code YORKSHIRE100, while flight-only customers can save 10% with the code YORKSHIRE10.

The company said the offer reflects continued demand for flights and package holidays from Yorkshire's largest airport and highlights its long-term investment in the region.

Jet2 first began operating from Leeds Bradford Airport in 2003 with two aircraft. Since then, it has grown to become one of the airport's biggest operators and employs thousands of people across Yorkshire.

The company is currently operating its largest ever schedule from Leeds Bradford, with 65 destinations on sale. It is also planning to launch six new destinations next year: Hurghada and Sharm el Sheikh in Egypt, Tunisia, Preveza in Greece, Santorini and Tivat in Montenegro.

For Summer 2027, Jet2 expects to have 15 aircraft based at Leeds Bradford Airport.

Steve Heapy, chief executive of Jet2, said: "We have been proudly operating from Leeds Bradford Airport since 2003, when we launched with just two aircraft, and since then we have grown to become Yorkshire's leading airline and tour operator.

"Our longstanding commitment to the region has never been stronger and we want to say thank you to our loyal customers and independent travel agent partners by launching these brand-new offers today."

He added: "Customers deserve the best experience and choice when it comes to their well-deserved holidays and that is what we look forward to continuing to provide from Leeds Bradford for many years to come."

Additional discounts are also available for single-parent families and solo travellers. Single-parent families can save £100 using the code YORKSHIRE100SPF, while solo travellers can claim £50 off bookings with YORKSHIRE50SOLO.

The offers apply to departures up to and including March 31, 2028, and must be booked by August 31, 2026.

Customers can book directly with Jet2 or through an independent travel agent.
