# What we know about the ex-car salesman who’s become a Belgian prince

![Clément Vandenkerckhove (R) with his father Prince Laurent of Belgium](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/20/20/37/b941b6e0-9c7e-11f1-9653-11ace0f67d4d-jpg.jpg)

- A 26-year-old former car salesman has been officially recognised as a Belgian prince after a DNA test confirmed he is the son of Prince Laurent.
- Clément Vandenkerckhove was born in August 2000 following a relationship between Prince Laurent and Flemish singer Iris Vandenkerckhove.
- The official recognition grants Vandenkerckhove the right to a royal title and inheritance, though he will not receive an allowance or enter the line of succession.
- Vandenkerckhove learned his father's identity at the age of 16 but was sworn to secrecy, which he stated caused him severe anxiety and panic attacks.
- He emphasised that building a relationship with his biological father is his main priority and remains hesitant about adopting the royal surname.
