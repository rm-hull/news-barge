# Earth, Wind & Fire drummer facing ‘two major surgeries’ after medical emergency

![John Paris fell ill on 7 August and has been in hospital ever since](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/21/11/23/John_Paris_2009-03-21.jpeg)

- Earth, Wind & Fire drummer John Paris has woken from a medically induced coma following a medical emergency earlier this month, according to a statement from the band.
- The 58-year-old was placed in critical care after falling ill on 7 August, leading the band to cancel a performance in San Francisco.
- Medical staff reported that Paris's blood pressure and breathing are now stable, the band said.
- Paris remains in the intensive care unit as doctors prepare him for “two major surgeries” in the coming days.
- A GoFundMe campaign aiming to raise $90,000 (£65,884) has been set up to support his family during his recovery.
