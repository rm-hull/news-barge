# Island from where Sir Francis Drake set sail could be yours for £2m

![The battery on Drake's Island](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/21/11/22/Drakes_Island_Battery_-_geograph-org-uk_-_5600429.jpeg)

- Plymouth’s historic Drake’s Island has been put up for sale with offers invited in excess of £2 million.
- The 6.4-acre military island features planning permission for a 43-bed hotel conversion and over 30,000 sqft of historic buildings.
- Estate agent Carter Jonas is selling the site on behalf of receivers for Plymouth Sound Properties Ltd, which bought it for £6 million in 2019.
- The island possesses a rich maritime history, having served as the starting point for Sir Francis Drake’s voyage to circumnavigate the globe in 1577, as well as voyages by Captain Cook and Charles Darwin.
- Prior to its sale, the landmark generated £590,000 in gross income in 2023 through guided tours, filming, and events.
