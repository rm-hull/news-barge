THREE women have been arrested after a cross county 'theft'.

North Yorkshire Police says it has arrested three women have been arrested on suspicion of theft after an incident at Superdrug in Northallerton yesterday afternoon (August 21).

The incident took place on the High Street, Northallerton when two women reportedly filled their bags with cosmetics and left the shop without paying.

"They were believed to have got into a black Toyota Aygo, which was then driven away by a third person," said a spokesperson for the force.

Officers tracked the car to St James Retail Park in Knaresborough, where they arrested three women from the North East aged 51, 38, and 31.

They have been released on conditional bail while the investigation continues.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Retail crime remains a priority for North Yorkshire Police because of the impact it has on local businesses and our communities. It’s not just about stolen goods.

"Incidents like these can have a lasting effect on shop workers and their families.

"No one should feel scared, threatened or intimidated when they’re simply turning up to work and earning a living.

"This result shows what can be achieved when retailers and police work closely together, sharing information quickly and taking a proactive approach to tackling crime."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on their website, quoting reference 12260161913.
