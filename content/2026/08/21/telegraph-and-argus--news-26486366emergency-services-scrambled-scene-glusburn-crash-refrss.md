Emergency services were scrambled to the scene of a two-car crash at rush hour today.

The crash happened on Hartley Street in Glusburn at 4.52pm today (Friday), prompting a response from fire crews.

A spokesman for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "Harrogate crews were mobilised to a road traffic collision involving two vehicles.

"All persons were out on arrival so no persons were trapped but an electric vehicle is involved so crews are currently making the vehicle and scene safe."

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "Ambulance resources responded to a collision on Hartley Street in Glusburn which happened just before 5pm on Friday (21 August).

"No-one involved in the incident required conveyance to hospital."
