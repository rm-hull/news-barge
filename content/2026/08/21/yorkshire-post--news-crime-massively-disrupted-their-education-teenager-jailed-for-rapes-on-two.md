# "Massively disrupted their education": Teenager jailed for rapes on two girls

## A teenager has been sentenced for raping two girls in 2023 and 2024.

The crimes were first reported to police in April 2024 and investigated by detectives before Sam Brewster, who was a juvenile when the offences took place, was charged and later convicted of numerous offences during a trial in August 2025.

However, Brewster, from the Redcar and Cleveland area, launched an appeal against these convictions.

The appeal was heard before Teesside Crown Court in March 2026.

![The crimes were first reported to police in April 2024 and investigated by detectives before Sam Brewster, who was a juvenile when the offences took place, was charged and later convicted of numerous offences during a trial in August 2025.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmJmM2E2YTkxLTVlOWQtNDc5Ny1iOTA4LTEzM2RiMjY0ZWM4ZTpjMjI0NjgyNC03ZGQ3LTQyMWItYmEwMy1hNGE4ZjdmNmFlODc=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



During the appeal, the girls had to go through the trauma of providing evidence to the court all over again, with two rapes upheld by the court.

On August 19, a year after he was first convicted, Brewster, now 18, was finally sentenced to three years in prison for his crimes.

Detective Constable Jack McPhillips said: “Both of the victims in this case were extremely brave in coming forward and giving evidence. They had the trauma of having to go through this difficult process twice over in court, in addition to multiple last-minute adjournments and having to relive their nightmare again and again.

“This has caused great distress in the girls’ lives and massively disrupted their education. I hope today’s sentence will bring some closure for the girls, so that they can now begin to rebuild their lives.

“I would like to thank the support workers from Arch Teesside who were incredibly helpful and supportive throughout the investigation and subsequent trials.”
