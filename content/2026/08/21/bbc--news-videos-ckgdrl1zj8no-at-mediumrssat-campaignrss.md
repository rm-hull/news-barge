# Watch: Emotional survivors describe how terror attacks changed their lives

The UK's first annual national day for those impacted by terrorism was marked today in a ceremony that took place in London.

Survivors of various attacks spoke of how their lives changed forever, including Paul Price, a survivor of the Manchester Arena attack, that claimed the life of his wife Elaine.

The Prime Minister Andy Burnham also spoke at the ceremony, he told survivors he had a 'deep admiration' for them.

The room also observed a two-minute silence and there were multiple musical interludes.
