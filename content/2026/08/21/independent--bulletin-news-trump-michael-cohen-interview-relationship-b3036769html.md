# ‘They weaponized you’: Trump speaks to Michael Cohen for first time in eight years

- Donald Trump and his former personal attorney Michael Cohen reconnected in a newly released WABC radio interview, addressing their tumultuous past.
- “You and I have obviously traveled a pretty rocky road together,” Cohen told Trump, who called in over the phone.
- Trump expressed sympathy for Cohen, claiming officials "weaponized" him and praising him for recanting previous statements.
- Their relationship originally collapsed in 2019 when Cohen called Trump a "con man" and later testified against him in a New York hush-money trial.
- Cohen stated that he and Trump rekindled their relationship over the summer following a "shared experience of betrayal."
