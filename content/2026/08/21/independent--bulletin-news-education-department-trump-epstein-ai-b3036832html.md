# Trump admin’s AI image accidentally has everyone asking the same Epstein question

![A Department of Education promotional image drew online mockery after social media users said two people featured in it resembled Jeffrey Epstein and Hillary Clinton](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/20/18/58/HQGbGa5XMAA04u9.jpeg)

- The Department of Education sparked viral online mockery after posting an AI-generated Mario Kart-style promotional graphic on social media.
- The graphic was intended to depict Donald Trump alongside Education Secretary Linda McMahon and Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy.
- Social media users and Democratic lawmakers widely noted that the figures alongside Trump closely resembled Hillary Clinton and Jeffrey Epstein.
- The Grok Imagine-generated image was posted to promote the Freedom 250 Grand Prix event scheduled for the National Mall.
- The Education Department has been contacted for comment regarding the controversial social media post.
