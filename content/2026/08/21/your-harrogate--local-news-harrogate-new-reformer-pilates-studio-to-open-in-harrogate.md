A new Reformer Pilates studio is set to open in Harrogate next month.

BAYSE will open its new Harrogate studio on Prospect Place in September,.

The fitness brand is currently preparing the new studio ahead of its opening and has begun inviting people to register their interest.

To mark the launch, BAYSE is offering customers their first class at the new Harrogate studio for free.

Announcing the opening on social media, BAYSE said:

“Harrogate… it’s nearly your turn. The countdown is officially on.

“This September, we’re opening the doors to BAYSE Harrogate and we can’t wait for you to see what we’re creating behind the scenes.”


The company added:

“Never tried Reformer before? This is the perfect time to start because your first class is completely FREE.”


An exact opening date is yet to be announced.
