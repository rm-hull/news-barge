# North Yorkshire Police issue CCTV appeal after tools stolen from vehicle in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police have issued a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to following a theft from a vehicle in Harrogate.

The incident occurred on Thursday, August 13 at 1:00am, on Nydd Vale Terrace, Harrogate and involved the theft of tools.

![Police have issued this CCTV image of a male that they would like to speak to, following a theft from a vehicle in Harrogate.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmI4YWY3OTZiLWVkYjAtNDRhOC1iMDlhLWQ0ZThmZTliOGZiZjoxZTA2ZGQ0Yi03N2JlLTQ3NmUtYjIxOS0zZjI4NDdhMjZjNjc=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



A police spokesperson said: “If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance:

• Online: Visit our website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’

• Live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

“Quote reference 12260158560 when providing information.”
