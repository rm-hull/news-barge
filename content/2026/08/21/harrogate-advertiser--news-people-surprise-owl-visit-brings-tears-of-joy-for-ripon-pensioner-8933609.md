# Surprise owl visit brings tears of joy for Ripon pensioner

## A Ripon pensioner was left in tears of joy after a care worker arranged a surprise visit from a real owl.

‘Mrs C’, who has asked to be referred to by that name, is supported by Radfield Home Care Harrogate, Wetherby and North Yorkshire and has a longstanding love of owls, with pictures and ornaments of the birds filling her home.

![Sian and Mrs C with Luna the owl](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/21/8/58/1000105808.jpeg?trim=446,0,354,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)



Care professional Sian noticed her passion for the birds during her visits and decided to arrange something extra special - the chance to meet a real owl.

With the help of Amanda from Harrogate Bird of Prey and Creature Cave, Luna the owl arrived at Mrs C’s home for a surprise visit.

The unexpected guest brought plenty of smiles and happy tears as Mrs C got the chance to see and meet Luna up close.

Sian said: “Mrs C absolutely loves owls. She has pictures and ornaments of them all around her home, so it’s something you quickly notice when you spend time with her.

“I thought how lovely it would be if we could somehow arrange for her to actually meet one.

“Amanda was kind enough to make that happen and seeing Mrs C’s reaction when Luna arrived was just wonderful.

“There were smiles and happy tears all round. It was such a special moment and one I think we’ll all remember for a long time.”

Liz, a Field Care Supervisor at Radfield Home Care, was also present for the visit and said the surprise demonstrated the relationships that can develop between care professionals and the people they support.

She said: “Sian had taken the time to really get to know Mrs C and recognise something that would mean a lot to her. That’s such an important part of good home care.

“It isn’t only about the practical support someone needs. We want people to continue enjoying the things they love and to have moments and experiences that bring genuine happiness.”

Photographs were taken during the visit, with printed copies given to Mrs C to treasure and share with her family.

The Radfield team also thanked Amanda from Harrogate Bird of Prey and Creature Cave for giving her time and helping to make the surprise possible.

The local Radfield team provides care and support to older people across Harrogate, Wetherby and surrounding areas, helping people to remain independent and enjoy life in their own homes.

For Mrs C, however, one recent home visit was about something a little more unusual – and a lot more memorable.
