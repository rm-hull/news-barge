The **Arsenal vs Coventry live stream** serves up a momentous fixture a quarter of a century in the making. Taking place 25 years and 94 days since the Sky Blues were relegated from the top flight in 2001 – the longest such gap in Premier League history – it's an occasion to savour, even though the schedulers have been far from kind.

Arsenal at the Emirates stands to be the toughest test Coventry will face all season. Mikel Arteta's side had a formidable home record last term, with 16 wins, two defeats and a draw, and the relief of finally fulfilling their Premier League title ambitions combined with the summer signings of Christos Tzolis and Bruno Guimaraes, have had a transformative effect.

Their performance in the Community Shield last weekend suggested that they've added silk to their steel. Man City were their closest challengers for the title last season, and although they're now in the early stages of a new regime, blowing them away in the manner that the Gunners did was quite a statement. While Tzolis was their standout performer, Bruno not only slotted in seamlessly for Declan Rice, but brought the best out of Martin Odegaard too.

For fans of a certain vintage, Coventry's return to the big time brings back sun-kissed memories of cult heroes like Mustapha Hadji and Youssef Chippo, and new signings Gustavo Hamer and Taiwo Awoniyi certainly have the mercurial talent to follow in those footsteps.
