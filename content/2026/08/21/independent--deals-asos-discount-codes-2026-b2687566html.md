## More ways to save at ASOS this month

### New customers save 20 per cent

If you’re a new ASOS customer, check the latest new-customer ASOS discount code before placing your order. Ordering through the app gets you a larger discount of 20 per cent, however, if you prefer shopping directly on the website, you can still enjoy 15 per cent off.

New-customer promotions usually come with exclusions, so check the terms before applying your code. In particular, sale items and selected brands may not qualify.

### Exclusive app discount codes and flash sales

Downloading the ASOS app can be another way to find exclusive promotions and limited-time discount codes. These offers can be short-lived, so if you regularly shop at ASOS, it’s worth enabling notifications and checking the app when you're ready to buy.

The app also comes with more benefits than exclusive promotions. Setting up an account means you can save your favourite items for later, create your own inspiration boards and check out quickly using your phone’s wallet.

### Member perks with ASOS World

ASOS World is the retailer’s loyalty scheme, offering members additional benefits as they move up the tiers. Members can also receive a 20 per cent birthday discount, while higher tiers unlock additional perks, such as early access to selected launches and sales.

You will need an ASOS account to access the programme and its benefits.

### Waive delivery with ASOS Premier

If you’re a frequent ASOS shopper, an ASOS Premier membership is well worth considering.

It costs £9.95 a year and gives members free standard delivery with no minimum spend, plus free next-day and nominated-day delivery when they spend at least £15 after discounts.

If you place several ASOS orders throughout the year, this can work out cheaper than paying for delivery each time.

### 10 per cent off with the ASOS student discount

Students can upgrade their new-term wardrobes for less, as ASOS offers 10 per cent off full-priced items with its student discount. All you need to do is verify your student status with SheerID or Unidays and you can save on every order.

### Up to 70 per cent off in the outlet

The ASOS outlet is where you can save up to 70 per cent on end-of-season stock, final sizes and end-of-line products.

It covers clothing, shoes, accessories, beauty and more, including ASOS Design and hundreds of other brands. If you’re not bothered about having the newest-season colour or style, the outlet can be one of the easiest ways to cut the price of an ASOS order without needing a promo code.

## ASOS FAQs

### Where can I apply my ASOS discount code?

At checkout, you will see the delivery address section. Above it, not yet expanded, is a 'promo/student code' section that you will need to click to reveal. This is where you can paste your code to apply your discount.

### Does ASOS offer free delivery?

Yes, either with a premier account or on orders of more than £40 (that's a saving of £4.50).

### Can I return my order for free at ASOS?

Items must be returned within 28 day, but there are two ways to get free returns at ASOS. Firstly, if you purchased directly through ASOS brand partners or, secondly, if you returned your order within 14 days and paid for delivery. You can read the full returns policy on the ASOS app or website.
