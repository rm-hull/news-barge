IK Collections on Westgate in Bradford city centre welcomed two leading Pakistani music artists during a busy weekend of culture and fashion.

International folk singer Naeem Hazarvi visited the store on Saturday to collect a bespoke outfit ahead of his concert appearance. The tailored piece was created especially for the performer, who was part of the Leeds Road Festival line-up alongside headliner Abrar Ul-Haq.

Abrar Ul-Haq, widely known for his Bhangra music, also visited IK Collections and requested an outfit inspired by the style worn by His Majesty King Charles III. The striking royal-themed design was made in advance following more than three months of planning and preparation.

The singer was said to be highly impressed by the Royal Harmony ballroom, while the visits marked another memorable occasion for IK Collections, which is based at 164 Westgate in Bradford city centre.
