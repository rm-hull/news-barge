FANCY a taste of autumn - grab yourself a pie.

I haven't eaten an oven-bake supermarket-bought pie in a long time - so thought I would give them a try.

Boxes of four are very affordable, so I wasn't expecting the Earth to move, but I hoped for a reasonable depth of flavour. So which ones would I happily buy again and eat with mashed potato or chips for my tea? I tried eight supermarket brands, as well as Birds Eye.

Here's my verdict:

*4 Rich Chicken & Veg Pies by Asda, 568g (4x142g), £2.25

The pastry on top puffed up nicely in the oven and the shortcrust bottom was sufficiently firm. Inside it was well filled, with decent-sized pieces of chicken, and enough small pieces of carrot and peas to pass muster. So far, so good, but the taste let it down - it was bland and reminded me of the budget pies my housemates and I used to eat as a student.

Pros: pasty, contents.

Cons: taste.

Mark: 2/5

*Hearty Food Company, Tesco, 4 Chicken & Veg Pies, 568g, £3.49

These pies looked smaller, but were deeper than some others. The shortcrust pastry was quite heavy and a bit chewy, the puff pastry top was nice and light. While the pie had an acceptable amount of chicken, peas and carrot within the gravy - which was okay - it didn’t have much, if any, flavour. More costly than others with the same box weight - in my opinion, not worth the extra.

Pros: contents.

Cons: taste.

Mark: 2/5

*Sainsbury’s 4 Chicken & Vegetable Pies, 568g, £1.99

These looked lighter in colour than the others, which was less appetising. The pastry base was a bit chewy in texture, but it was a reasonably well-packed pie with a good amount of tender chicken which I could actually taste within the gravy - which was just the right thickness - along with the peas and chopped carrot. Not a bad pie and a very good price for four.

Pros: filling, taste, price.

Cons: consistency (pastry base).

Mark: 3/5

*Crestwood 4 Chicken & Vegetable Pies, Aldi, 568g, £1.99

Like Tesco, these were smaller in width but deeper. The pastry was light. Inside, and the pieces of chicken were a good size, tasty and tender. I found the dark gravy rich and tasty - the best gravy of the selection - but was disappointed to find peas but no pieces of carrot. This was a tasty, well-rounded pie, but unfortunately I was unable to find any evidence of carrot, which is listed on the ingredients. My husband also ate a pie, and his did have carrots, so, at that price, I’ll forgive them the production error and award it four.

Pros: consistency, taste.

Cons: contents, price.

Mark: 4/5

*Iceland 4 Chicken & Vegetable Pies (bought at The Range), 664g (4x166g), £2.50

This pie was bigger than the others, hence the higher price, and had a simple decorative pattern on top, which was a nice touch. The pastry top and bottom were light with a good texture and taste, but the contents did not live up to that. The gravy wasn’t bad but the amount of chicken pieces and vegetables was quite sparse - maybe that’s why this pie lacked flavour.

Pros: size, appearance.

Cons: contents, taste.

Mark: 2/5

*Morrisons Chicken and Vegetable Pies, 468g, £2.13

A good shape and colour - an appetising golden brown on top. The pastry had a light consistency and kept its shape while slicing into it, which was a bonus. There was a decent amount of peas and carrot - especially carrot, which good-sized pieces, which I could taste. There wasn’t much chicken, however, and what there was tasteless, and the gravy quite watery. As a whole the pie lacked flavour.

Pros: size, appearance.

Cons: content (chicken); taste

Mark: 2/5

*Birds Eye Chicken and Vegetable Pies (Farm Foods), 620g, £3.49

A good-sized pie, its neatly-shaped pastry covering was light and buttery – a good start, I thought. I also liked the dark gravy which surrounded the peas and carrots, but there was a shortage of the key player – chicken: there wasn’t much to get your teeth into. The taste came from the gravy. A bit more costly than some, but bigger pies.

Pros: size, taste (pastry) .

Cons: contents, taste.

Mark: 2/5

*Chef Select 4 Chicken & Vegetable Pies, Lidl, 568g, £1.99

This puffed up nicely, to a rich brown on top. The pastry base and top had a good texture and taste, while the chicken was tender and tasty within a light gravy. There were sufficient pieces of peas and carrots. All in all, a decent enough pie at a very affordable price.

Pros: consistency, taste, price.

Cons: none.

Mark: 3/5

*Pies bought July and August 2026
