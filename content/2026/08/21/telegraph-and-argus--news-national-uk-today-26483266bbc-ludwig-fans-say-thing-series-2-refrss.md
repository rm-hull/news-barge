Ludwig fans are all saying the same thing as the second series started airing last night (August 20).

From missing detectives to multiple murders and high-ranking cover-ups, the new episodes promise plenty of drama for BBC viewers.

In the second series, master puzzle-setter John ‘Ludwig’ Taylor (David Mitchell) is now a crime scene consultant, working alongside DCI Russell Carter (Dipo Ola) on ‘impossible’ crimes for the Cambridge Police Authority.

But with his identical twin brother still missing, John and his sister-in-law Lucy (Anna Maxwell Martin) won't stop – there’s a puzzle to solve and a husband and father to bring home.


## Ludwig series 2 sees fans saying the same thing as the first episode airs

The new series of Ludwig has been well received so far, with fans replying to David Mitchell’s X post, which shows a sneak peek of the episodes.

One said: “And it's as good as ever.”

Someone else put: “Excellent! I was hoping you were doing another season.”

Another said the cast is "fabulous", adding: “It was brilliant (as usual, and expected), and so great to see my local theatre feature both outside and inside too".

## Where is Ludwig series 2 filmed?

Cambridge features in the series, with David Mitchell sharing that he loves the English city.

He said: "I went to university there, and I love it.

"You want people to enjoy slipping into this world like a pair of slippers, but with a dead body in them.

"Cambridge is obviously a great candidate for that.

"Inspector Morse is one of my favourite shows, but that was set in Oxford.

"That's where people want to escape to in this kind of program.

"I think you've got to have a beautiful setting to juxtapose with the nature of the crimes."
