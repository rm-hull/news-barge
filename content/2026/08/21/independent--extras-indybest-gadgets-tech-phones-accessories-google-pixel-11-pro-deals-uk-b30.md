Google’s latest Pixel phones have officially launched. After debuting last week at one of the most unusual Made by Google events ever – with the tech giant essentially turning its annual launch into a celebrity variety show – the Pixel 11 series is finally available to buy.

There are four new smartphones available – the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL and Pixel 11 Pro Fold, boasting upgraded cameras, brighter displays and a whole heap of new Gemini-powered features.

As is tradition with any new smartphone launch, there are already plenty of Pixel 11 deals floating around. Google itself is offering boosted trade-in values and freebies until 1 September, and mobile carriers have their own deals on the Pixel 11 family.

## Google Pixel 11 price

There are four different Pixel 11 devices to choose from: the regular Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, larger Pixel 11 Pro XL and the book-style Pixel 11 Pro Fold.

The Google Pixel 11 starts at £879 for the 256GB model, the Pixel 11 Pro starts at £1,079, the Pixel 11 Pro X with the larger 6.8in display starts at £1,279 and the Google Pixel 11 Fold starts at £1,799. All four phones launched on 20 August.

You can get the regular Pixel 11 in frost, hibiscus, pistachio and obsidian. The Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL are available in canyon, olive, fog and matte obsidian. The Pixel 11 Pro Fold come in just two colours – olive and matte obsidian.

If you’re looking for a discount on the latest Pixel phones, you can find some trade-in deals when you buy one of the phones directly from the Google Store. Until 1 September, you can get up to £850 off the Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL or Pixel 11 Pro Fold when trading in an eligible smartphone – that includes a £400 trade-in bonus.

The trade-in bonus is a little smaller for the regular Pixel 11. It comes with up to £650 in trade-in. That maximum value includes a £200 trade-in bonus. The maximum £850 or £650 saving applies to a 1TB iPhone 16 Pro in good working condition. The exact amount depends on the model and condition of your old phone.

If you want Google’s newest Pixel Watch 5 smartwatch, you can get £80 off when you buy one with a Pixel 11 smartphone. You can save £50 on the 41mm wifi model, £60 on the 45mm wifi model, £70 on the 41mm LTE model and £80 on the 45mm LTE version

Lastly, the Google Pixel 11 Pro, Pro XL and Pro Fold come with six months of Google AI Pro out of the box, as well as three months of YouTube Premium. The regular Pixel 11 only comes with three months of YouTube Premium.

## The best Google Pixel 11 carrier deals in the UK

All the major carriers have dropped their best deals on the Google Pixel 11 series of phones, complete with perks and trade-in bonuses. You can spread the cost out over several months, while also getting a new contract and a shiny new phone.

As always, the cheapest way to buy a new phone is to secure it on 0 per cent finance with Google directly and spreading the cost over 24 months. You can then buy a cheap sim-only plan from a piggyback network.

You can get up to £290 when trading in an eligible handset for the Pixel 11, up to £470 for the Pixel 11 Pro and up to £600 for either the Pixel 11 Pro XL or Pixel 11 Pro Fold. You also get three months of YouTube Premium, and the Pro models come with six months of Google AI Pro.
