Shoppers can buy chocolates, including Maltesers, M&Ms and Galaxy Counters, for just 1p from Co-op for a limited time.

The major supermarket chain is running a promotion through Just Eat, giving new customers the chance to add a bag of chocolate to their grocery order for just 1p.

Co-op's quick commerce trading director, Gary Kisby, said: "We’re delighted to give new customers an extra reason to try Co-op on Just Eat.

"The 1p sweet treats offer combines fantastic value with quick and convenient grocery delivery from our local stores straight to customers’ doors."


## Full list of chocolates available for 1p through new Co-op offer

The new Co-op offer includes:

- Maltesers Milk Chocolate Bag (93g)
- M&M’s Crispy Pouch (107g)
- Revels Milk Chocolate Bag (101g)
- Galaxy Counters Milk Chocolate Bag (112g)
- Milky Way Magic Stars Milk Chocolate Bag (100g)

Each product usually costs £2.35 through the Just Eat delivery service.

The offer is only available to customers who have never ordered from Co-op via Just Eat before.

It is limited to one unit per order, per product, and will run from Friday (August 21) to Sunday (August 23), while stocks last.


Delivery and service charges still apply, and product availability may vary between stores.

The supermarket has partnered with Just Eat since 2023 and now offers delivery from more than 1,700 stores via the platform.
