Cyclists can get their bikes checked and repaired for free when a community bike fix returns to Harrogate this weekend.

Changing the Landscape CIC will be back in Valley Gardens from 10am until 12pm on Sunday (23rd August), following a previous event at the park last month.

The community session will offer bike safety checks, tune-ups and basic repairs, with advice also available on maintenance and servicing.

The previous Valley Gardens event was held as part of Cycling UK's Big Bike Revival initiative, aimed at helping people keep their bikes safe and roadworthy while encouraging more people to cycle.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a75938ab9c57e59d21.jpg)


North Yorkshire Police will also be supporting Sunday's event by offering bike ID registrations to members of the community.

Changing the Landscape CIC is also running a series of 'Learn to Fix Your Bike' workshops for people who want to become more confident carrying out their own maintenance.

The next workshop will take place at Chain Lane Community Hub in Knaresborough on Tuesday (25th August) from 6.30pm until 8pm.

Sunday's community bike fix runs from 10am until 12pm at Valley Gardens in Harrogate.
