A new indoor and outdoor children's play area has opened at Otley Garden Centre.

Grass Hoppers welcomed its first families this week after Yorkshire Garden Centres expanded the play brand to a third location.

The new attraction, on Pool Road, follows existing Grass Hoppers sites at Tong and Tingley.

It features a secure indoor play area as well as an outdoor space overlooking Otley Chevin, allowing families to visit throughout the year.

There is also a dedicated pre-school area, known as Dotty's, for younger children.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a87ef43e8e41ee85bf.jpg)


The attraction has its own Woody's Kitchen, serving hot and cold food for children and adults.

Ahead of the opening, local families were given the opportunity to win places at an exclusive pre-launch event and become some of the first to try the new facilities.

James Pretty, General Manager of Grass Hoppers Otley, said:

“Grass Hoppers has always been about making memories through play, no matter the weather, and this sits at the heart of the expansion.

“Alongside the indoor play and Dotty’s pre-school space, we have also brought Woody’s Kitchen to Otley, serving up a much-loved menu of hot and cold food for adults and little ones - so your family days out are as fun as possible.”
