A ROAD in Bradford is set to close for gas works.

Norman Avenue will be shut from outside No 4 to its junction with Norman Lane from 8am on August 24, until 11.55pm on September 4.

The closure is due to work being carried out on or near the road.

Vehicles involved in the work will be allowed access, and alternative routes for other vehicles will include an unaffected section of Norman Avenue, Norman Crescent, and Acre Avenue.

Pedestrian access will be maintained at all times.

The City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council issued the notice under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 - Section 14.
