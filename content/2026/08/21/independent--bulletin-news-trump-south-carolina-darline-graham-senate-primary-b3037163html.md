# Trump rides to rescue of handpicked Senate candidate days before make-or-break runoff

- President Donald Trump will travel to South Carolina to rally support for Sen. Darline Graham ahead of Tuesday's Republican Senate primary runoff.
- Darline Graham, appointed interim senator after her brother, Sen. Lindsey Graham, died, faces Rep. Ralph Norman for the GOP nomination.
- The Myrtle Beach event marks a rare midterm campaign appearance for Trump, underscoring how important he sees securing the nomination for his endorsed candidate.
- Darline Graham has positioned herself as a political newcomer committed to advancing Trump's agenda, focusing on border security and household economic issues.
- The runoff outcome will determine the Republican candidate for November's midterm elections in a key conservative state as control of Congress remains at stake.
