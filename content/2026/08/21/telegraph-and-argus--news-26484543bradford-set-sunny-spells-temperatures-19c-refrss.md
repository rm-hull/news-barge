THE Met Office has forecast a mix of sunshine and clouds for Bradford this weekend.

Saturday will start off partly cloudy, with temperatures around 11C.

Sunny intervals are expected from 12pm, with temperatures rising to 14C by 4pm.

The sky will become cloudy again at 6pm, with temperatures reaching 17C by 7pm.

The evening will see sunny intervals, with temperatures gradually dropping to 11C by 11pm.

Sunday will start off clear, with temperatures around 9C.

The day will be mostly sunny, with temperatures rising to 19C by 3pm.

The evening will remain clear, with temperatures dropping to 12C by 11pm.

Throughout the weekend, the chance of rain remains low, with 10 per cent or less expected at any given time.

Wind speeds will remain light, ranging from 2mph to 7mph on Saturday, and 2mph to 5mph on Sunday.
