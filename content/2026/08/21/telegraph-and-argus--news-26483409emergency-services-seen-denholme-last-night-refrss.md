ONE person was taken to hospital following a crash in the Bradford district late last night.

Emergency services were called to Halifax Road in Denholme at around 10.50pm on Thursday after reports of a collision involving two vehicles.

One person was taken to hospital for treatment.

Their injuries are not believed to be life-threatening.

Images shared on social media showed a large emergency services presence at the scene.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service has been contacted for comment.

## Statement in full

On Friday, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Collision involving two cars in Halifax Road, Denholme reported at 10.50pm yesterday.

"One person taken to hospital.

"Injuries not believed to be life-threatening."
