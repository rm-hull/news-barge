BRADFORD is set to come alive with the sound of classic ELO hits.

The ELO Experience, a multi-award-winning tribute to Jeff Lynne and the Electric Light Orchestra, will perform at Bradford Live on Thursday, September 10, as part of their 20th anniversary year tour.

The show promises a night of ELO’s greatest hits, along with a few deeper cuts from their albums.

A spokesperson for the band said: "This milestone tour marks two decades of performances honouring one of Britain’s most influential musical legacies."

Formed in 2006, The ELO Experience has grown into the UK’s leading tribute to Jeff Lynne and the Electric Light Orchestra.

The group has earned multiple awards and sold out theatres across the country.

Their 20-year milestone tour celebrates the lasting appeal of ELO’s music.

Fans can expect to hear timeless classics such as Mr Blue Sky, Livin’ Thing, Don’t Bring Me Down, Telephone Line, and Evil Woman.

A spokesperson for the tour said the show will take audiences "on a musical journey through some of the greatest music ever written and put onto vinyl."

Tickets are available now at bradfordlive.co.uk
