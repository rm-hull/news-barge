# Five-year-old boy detained by ICE days before starting kindergarten

- Federal immigration agents detained five-year-old Liam Tadeo and his father, Victor Martinez Nieto, during an August 16 traffic stop in Austin, Texas.
- The father and son are currently held at the South Texas Family Residential Center in Dilley, Texas, pending removal.
- Democratic Representative Greg Casar called for the immediate release of Liam Tadeo and the closure of the Dilley detention facility.
- The Department of Homeland Security stated that Martinez Nieto reentered the U.S. illegally after accepting voluntary departure earlier in the year.
- The arrest comes amid a surge in ICE enforcement under the Trump administration, which recorded over 43,000 arrests in July alone.
