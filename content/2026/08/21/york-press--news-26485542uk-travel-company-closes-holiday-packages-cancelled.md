## All Golf Villa Rentals holiday packages cancelled - are refunds available?

ABTA explained that Golf Villa Rentals Ltd, based in East Sussex, sold package holidays which included accommodation, travel (excluding flights) and other services.

With the company having now closed, all holiday packages have been cancelled, but refunds are available.


ABTA said: "If you booked a package holiday through Golf Villa Rentals Ltd, financial protection for those holidays was provided by financial failure insurance.

"Customers with bookings should visit the Evolution Insurance Company Ltd website for further information and advice on how to make a claim."

The Golf Villa Rentals website is still active (at the time of writing), but the company's Instagram and Facebook pages have been shut down, while the X account hasn't been used since 2019.


Golf Villa Rentals has been contacted for comment.
