A racket sports centre could be created in a west Leeds community if plans are agreed with the city council.

Jobs will be created if plans go ahead for the creation of a padel centre at Carr Crofts Drive in Armley.

Leeds City Council has been asked to approve planning permission for the scheme, which includes solar panels and a cafe and bar area.

A design report by Power Padel Group Ltd said there was a need for provision for the fast-growing sport in the area.

It said: “The building is currently empty and is ready to be re-purposed into a facility that will provide an employment opportunity to the local area.

“The new facility will be a fantastic addition to the area providing Leeds residents with an excellent facility to enjoy this fast growing and increasingly popular sport.”

  Carr Crofts Drive, Armley. Google Image. 

*(Image: Google)*

The report said 12 jobs were expected to be created at the former warehouse, around one-and-a-half miles from Armley gyratory and the city centre.

It said: “Its last use was as a shop fitting manufacturing, storage and distribution facility.”

The report said the site was close to public transport links and bike parking spaces were already in place.

It said: “Secure bicycle parking for eight persons is located near to the building main entrance for anyone wishing to travel to site via bicycle.”
