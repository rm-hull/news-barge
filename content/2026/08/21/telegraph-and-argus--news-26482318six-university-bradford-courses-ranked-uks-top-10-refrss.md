A university has seen six of its courses ranked among the best in the UK.

The University of Bradford was recognised in the National Student Survey (NSS), which gathers feedback from final-year students and highlights teaching quality and academic support.

The survey placed six of the university’s courses in the UK’s top 10 and nine in the top 20, with Chemical, Process and Energy Engineering ranked first in the country, Bioengineering and BSc (Hons) Public Health and Population Studies ranked second, mechanical engineering sixth, and artificial intelligence seventh.

Claire Pryke, associate director of outreach, recruitment and admissions at the University of Bradford, said: "For prospective students, choosing Bradford through Clearing means joining a university where student satisfaction continues to grow, courses are nationally recognised, and graduates gain the skills needed to succeed in industry.

"Combined with strong links to employers, award-winning facilities and a supportive campus community, the latest NSS results demonstrate why the University of Bradford is an excellent choice for students looking to secure a place through Clearing this year."

Courses still available through Clearing include accounting and finance, adult nursing, biomed, clinical sciences, computer science, criminology, engineering, law, psychology, social work, and Public Health and Population Studies.

Clearing 2026 runs until 9 October.
