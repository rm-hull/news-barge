# Mystery surrounds human remains found in London

![Police tape near to the scene, where a member of the public found human remains in woodland in Feltham](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/21/16/01M0JD85PS0V6755BK7D23DEVF.jpg)

- Metropolitan Police have launched a murder investigation following the discovery of human remains in woodland near Padstow Walk, Feltham.
- The remains are believed to belong to a woman aged between 25 and 45, whose identity is yet to be confirmed.
- Officers are currently unsure how long the remains had been at the West London location prior to their discovery on 12 August.
- A post-mortem examination took place on 17 August, prompting the formal opening of the murder probe.
- Detective Chief Inspector Wayne Jolley has appealed for anyone with relevant information to contact the police while a crime scene remains in place.
