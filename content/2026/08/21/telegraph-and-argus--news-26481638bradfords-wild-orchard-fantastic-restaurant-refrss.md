A “hidden” restaurant in the heart of Yorkshire has been described as “absolutely fantastic”.

If you are looking for somewhere special to enjoy a meal in Bradford, you might want to try the Wild Orchard.

The restaurant can be found at the Walton View Hotel and offers “exceptional food and a great atmosphere”.

Visitors can enjoy their meal in the art deco-styled restaurant, which is packed with beautiful plants to create an outdoor feel.


## What is the Wild Orchard like?

The Wild Orchard is open for brunch, dinner and afternoon tea, and even offers a Sunday roast.

The brunch menu offers dishes ranging from full breakfasts to toast topped with things like scrambled eggs and avocado, as well as lunch options like the Orchard fish and chips and seared wild seabass.

For those who love sweet treats, the menu also has plenty of dishes like pancakes, French toast and cheesecake.

The evening menu features starters like chicken gyoza and grilled lobster, and mains ranging from the Salt Marsh burger to surf and turf.

For the Sunday roast menu, you can choose from roast butter-baked chicken, beef and mint-roasted lamb, all served with Yorkshire puddings, oven-roasted potatoes and seasonal vegetables.

The restaurant also offers a party menu if you want to celebrate a special event at the Wild Orchard.


Guests are able to choose from a set menu of the restaurant’s best dishes, with three courses costing £45 per person.
