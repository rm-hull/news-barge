Are you ready for *Lanterns*' next episode? You better be, because it'll arrive on a streaming platform near you very soon.

With *Lanterns* episode 1 ending in shocking fashion with the death of a major character, I suspect you're dying (no pun intended) to know who's responsible for that individual's demise.

So, will the DC Universe (DCU) TV show's next chapter, entitled 'Trust Fall', reveal who it is? You'll have to tune in to find out. Read on to learn when *Lanterns*' second entry will drop where you live.

## When does episode 2 of Lanterns come out?

![Lanterns | Weeks Ahead Trailer | HBO Max - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/4trSu7ji0oU/maxresdefault.jpg) 

The second episode of HBO's Green Lantern TV show will premiere on **Sunday, August 23 at 6pm PT / 9pm** ET in the US and Canada.

As for viewers in other nations, here's when the DCU Chapter One TV series' next installment will air in your neck of the woods:

- **US** — Sunday, August 23 at 6pm PT / 9pm ET
- **Canada** — Sunday, August 23 at 6pm PT / 9pm ET
- **UK** — Monday, August 24 at 2am BST
- **India** — Monday, August 24 at 6:30am IST
- **Singapore** — Monday, August 24 at 9am SGT
- **Australia** — Monday, August 24 at 11am AEST
- **New Zealand** — Monday, August 24 at 1pm NZST

## Where can I watch Lanterns episode 2?

## How many episodes of Lanterns are there?

 ![John Stewart on the phone and looking into a mirror while holding up a Green Lantern suit in Lanterns episode 2](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Gjxhkc7NJ4zWo6ao2E8Yxg.jpg) 


There will be eight episodes of *Lanterns* in total this season. That means, after this week's episode comes out, we'll be a quarter of the way through the show's debut season.

But I'm getting off-track. With new chapters launching every Sunday in the Americas and every Monday on other continents, these are the dates that new episodes will be released on:

- ***Lanterns*episode 1** — out now
