## Delays of more than 60 minutes after multi-vehicle crash on A1

There are long delays on the A1. *(Image: Contributed)*

ALL vehicles have been held on a major North Yorkshire road this lunchtime after a crash.

All lanes of the A1(M) southbound near Wetherby were held following a crash involving multiple vehicles.
