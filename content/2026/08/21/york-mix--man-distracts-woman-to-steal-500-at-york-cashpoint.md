Do you recognise this man?

Police investigating a scam and theft at a York cash machine want to talk to him.

It happened last month as a woman was using the ATM at the **Halifax** on**Parliament Street**.

#### Halifax bank location

The man told her the machine wasn’t working as it had failed to pay out his cash.

As he was distracting her he pressed the button requesting £500 without her knowledge.

When the money appeared he convinced the woman it was his from his previous transaction, and she handed it over.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The deception has only been noticed at a later date when the victim has realised £500 has gone out of her account.”

If you can identify the man or have information that might help police, you can contact them below.
