*Strictly Come Dancing*’s Vito Coppola has revealed he experienced a “crippling” and “potentially life-threatening” eating disorder in his early career.

While promoting his new food memoir *Love, Vito*, the Italian professional dancer said that he struggled with body image while competing in dance competitions, where his physical appearance was repeatedly judged.

Coppola said that he was bulimic for a period, explaining: “I did it for a few months but felt very ashamed. It was a betrayal of my values – I was wasting food. I felt not clean and I didn’t have much love for myself. I felt guilty because my family were so worried about me but they didn’t know what was really going on. They didn’t know I had a problem.”

He said that his disorder reached its peak when he collapsed during a dance competition. “I fainted during the jive because I had no energy,” he told *The Sun*. “You push yourself but being strong means stepping away from a situation that is toxic and is damaging you. I’ve never gone back.”

!['Strictly Come Dancing' star Vito Coppola](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/21/7/41/549066.jpeg)

Coppola has been a professional dancer on *Strictly* since 2022, where he’s been paired with pop star and radio presenter Fleur East,*Coronation Street*’s Ellie Leach,* Miranda* star Sarah Hadland and model Ellie Goldstein.

He will return to the dancefloor for *Strictly*’s 24th series, which launches in September. And that’s despite a number of professional dancers being axed from the show. Karen Hauer, Gorka Marquez, Nadiya Bychkova, Luba Mushtuk and Michelle Tsiakkas are all confirmed to not be returning for* Strictly*.

Despite backlash from some fans, former *Strictly* professional James Jordan this week suggested the cull was “needed”, insisting that the show is merely “getting rid of dead wood”.

The axing of several dancers comes alongside the departure of long-time presenters Claudia Winkleman and Tess Daly, with Emma Willis, Josh Widdicombe and Johannes Radebe now signed up to replace them.

![Ellie Goldstein and Vito Coppola perform on ‘Strictly’ in 2025](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/03/08/15/1277f6acd522b55618cb02bb91bcd0eeY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzcyOTQyMjI3-2.81964089.jpg)

Last week it was also announced that former contestant Ellie Taylor will succeed the outgoing Fleur East and Janette Manrara as the host of *Strictly*’s spin-off show* It Takes Two*. She explained on Emma Willis’s BBC Radio 2 show that she was “thrilled” to re-join the* Strictly* universe, and described some of the changes being made to the show once it returns.

She said *It Takes Two* will still be “the show that everyone knows and loves but refreshed for 2026”, adding: “We’re gonna be talking to couples but also groups of celeb [contestants] and groups of pros away from their dance partners, for slightly more candid conversation.”

Speaking to *The Independent* this week, professional dancer Amy Dowden addressed the many changes to the show and cast exits, explaining: “Obviously, I know for a lot of them it was their own decision and it was time for them to take on new challenges. But once you’re part of the*Strictly* family, you’re always part of the family.”
