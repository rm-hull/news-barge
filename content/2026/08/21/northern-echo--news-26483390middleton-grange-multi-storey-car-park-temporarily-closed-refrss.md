A multi-storey car park at a North East shopping centre has been closed until further notice due to 'maintenance issues' at the site.

Hartlepool Borough Council said the car park at the Middleton Grange Shopping Centre has been temporarily closed today (August 21).

The council is yet to give a timeframe for when it will reopen, but has said that further updates will be provided when possible.

In a statement, a council spokesperson said: "Due to a maintenance issue, the multi-storey car park at Middleton Grange Shopping Centre is temporarily closed until further notice.

"We apologise for any inconvenience this causes, and a further update will be provided as soon as possible."
