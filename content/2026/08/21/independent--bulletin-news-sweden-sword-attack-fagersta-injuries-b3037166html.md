# Suspect in custody after sword attack at school in Sweden

![Fagersta in Västmanland, Sweden](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/21/13/36/iStock-1366655381.jpeg)

- Several people have been injured in a sword attack at a high school in Fagersta, central Sweden.
- Swedish police responded to reports of ongoing deadly violence at the school on Friday.
- One suspect has been taken into custody following the incident.
- Officers remain at the scene and have urged the public to respect police roadblocks and instructions.
- Justice minister Gunnar Strommer confirmed the government is in close contact with police as they investigate the attack.
