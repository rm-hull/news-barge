# Jack Draper withdraws from US Open as injury nightmare continues

![Draper will not play at Flushing Meadows](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/21/17/01M0JHTNDM3KJGHTBK2KJSJADG.jpg)

- Jack Draper has withdrawn from the US Open due to a persistent bone bruising injury to his dominant left arm.
- The decision means the former world No 4 will not feature in a single grand slam tournament throughout 2026.
- Draper previously missed the French Open and Wimbledon before suffering back-to-back losses in Montreal and Cincinnati.
- The 2024 US Open semi-finalist has decided he requires additional time away from the court to recover fully.
- His withdrawal follows Jannik Sinner pulling out with a knee issue, while Carlos Alcaraz is set to return from a wrist injury.
