A classic rock tribute show due to take place at a Darlington theatre has been cancelled after low ticket sales meant it was no longer “financially viable” to go ahead.

The Whitesnake and Journey Story was due to perform at The Majestic Theatre on August 28.

The band, which performs songs made famous by Journey and Whitesnake, said it had made the decision with “great regret and disappointment”.

In a statement, it said: “Despite a great deal of promotion, hard work and enthusiasm from everyone involved, unfortunately ticket sales have not reached a level that makes it financially viable for us to go ahead with the performance.

“This has been a huge disappointment for us, as we were genuinely looking forward to bringing the show to Darlington and sharing what we love doing with you.”

The group said it was sorry to people who had bought tickets or had been planning to attend.

It added: “We know that many of you have supported us, promoted the event and were excited to come along, and we are truly sorry to let you down.

“We would like to thank everyone who bought tickets, shared our posts, supported the show and helped us spread the word. Your support genuinely means a great deal to us.”

The Whitesnake and Journey Story is a five-piece tribute act performing classic rock songs including Don’t Stop Believin’, Here I Go Again, Separate Ways and Is This Love.

The Majestic Theatre said venues were facing difficult ticket sales, urging people to book in advance rather than waiting until the day of a performance.

A spokesperson said: “Ticket sales are probably the worst they have been for venues, and our little theatre is really feeling it now.

“Please don’t rely to purchase your tickets on the door as the production companies need to feel assured that their costs are covered by ticket sales earlier than the date they have booked.”
