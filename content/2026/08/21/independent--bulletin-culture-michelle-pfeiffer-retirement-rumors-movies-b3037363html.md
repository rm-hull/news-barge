# Michelle Pfeiffer hits back at retirement rumors: ‘It’s the most ridiculous thing’

- Actor Michelle Pfeiffer denied rumors that she is retiring from film, clarifying that previous comments she made were taken out of context.
- Speaking to Jimmy Fallon on *The Tonight Show* , Pfeiffer stated her previous remark about not wanting to star in another movie was meant in jest.
- The 68-year-old actor explained that she was emphasizing her love for ensemble casts rather than announcing an end to her movie career.
- Pfeiffer highlighted her recent enjoyment of working on ensemble projects, including the television series *Margo’s Got Money Troubles* and*The Madison* .
- She is currently nominated for an Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Comedy Series for her role in *Margo’s Got Money Troubles* .
