# Sky News face Ofcom investigation over Nigel Farage claims

- Ofcom has launched a formal investigation into Sky News following a complaint made by Reform UK on behalf of Nigel Farage.
- The media watchdog will examine claims of unfair treatment and unwarranted infringement of Mr Farage's privacy.
- Mr Farage accused the broadcaster of harassing his family after reporters approached a property linked to him amid questions over his financial affairs.
- Reform UK alleged in its complaint that the Sky News report portrayed Mr Farage unfairly and omitted critical context.
- Sky News defended its actions as routine journalism regarding a matter of public interest and denied hounding or interacting with Mr Farage's daughter.
