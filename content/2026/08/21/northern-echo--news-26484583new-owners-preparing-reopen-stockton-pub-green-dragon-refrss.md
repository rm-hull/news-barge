A North East pub described as a “hidden gem” which has been closed for more than three years is set to welcome punters again after new owners announced plans to bring it back to life.

The Green Dragon, on Finkle Street in Stockton town centre, closed in May 2023 after nearly seven years under its previous management.

Now, new owners say they are preparing the venue for its return, promising a traditional pub where local people, regulars and new faces can come together.

In a post shared online, they said: “Something exciting is happening in the heart of Stockton and we’re absolutely delighted to finally share it with you.

“We are so pleased to be taking on The Green Dragon and cannot wait to welcome you through our doors.”

The owners said they wanted to create a “proper local” where people could enjoy a drink, catch up with friends and feel at home.

They said: “We’re passionate about making our pub one of the best traditional pubs. We’ll make sure we excel with good company and creating somewhere that feels like a proper local, somewhere you can walk in, have a drink, meet friends, make new ones and feel right at home.

“There’s lots happening behind the scenes as we get everything ready, and we’re putting our heart and soul into making sure The Green Dragon is perfect and a place we can be proud of.”

Regulars voiced their upset when the pub’s closure was announced in 2023, with one customer describing it as their favourite pub and another saying major events in their life since 1990 had been marked at the venue.

The new owners have not yet revealed an opening date, but said customers would not have to wait “forever” for its return.

They added: “Make sure you like and follow the page because we’ll be sharing updates, sneak peeks and, of course, the big opening date as soon as we’re ready to announce it.

“The Green Dragon. Traditional pub. Proper welcome. Stockton on Tees. Watch this space, the dragon is waking up.”
