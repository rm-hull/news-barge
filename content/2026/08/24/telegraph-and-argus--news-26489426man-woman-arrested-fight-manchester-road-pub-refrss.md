POLICE have confirmed they made two arrests following a reported fight in a Bradford pub on Saturday night.

The force were called to a venue on Manchester Road at around 10.30pm to a report of ongoing fighting involving several men and women.

## Woman injured

Officers attended the scene at which a woman in her 40s had sustained facial injuries.

## Man and woman arrested

Following enquiries, a man, aged 57, was arrested on suspicion of affray and a woman, aged 26, was arrested on suspicion of section 18 assault.

Both have been released on police bail pending continuing investigations, the force have said.

## Police appeal

Anyone who saw or has information about the incident who officers have not spoken with is asked to contact Bradford Police on 101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat referencing police crime number 13260480173.

Information can also be given anonymously to the independent Crimestoppers charity on 0800 555 111.
