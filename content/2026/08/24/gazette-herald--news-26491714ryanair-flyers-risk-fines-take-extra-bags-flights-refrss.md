It can be quite hard to keep up with all of the different rules for airlines when travelling, especially when they can change dramatically between each company.

It is easy to be caught out when it comes to luggage allowance for both hand luggage and checked-in bags.

Ryanair has a long list of rules that travellers must follow to help keep its fares as low as they are.

The travel company has a very strict policy when it comes to hand luggage, so you must be careful to avoid being forced to pay extra for your bags.


## Ryanair passengers told to be careful with hand luggage

Crossbody bags are hugely popular for travellers, as it is easy to keep all of your important documents in one place close to you.

However, for many people, a crossbody bag might not be big enough to carry everything you want to take on board.

On the Ryanair website, it says: “All fares include one small personal bag (40 x 30 x 20 cm) that must fit under the seat.

"Additional options include Priority boarding with a second 10kg cabin bag, checked bags of 10kg, 20kg (up to 3), or 23kg (1 allowed).

“Infants have no cabin allowance but may bring a 5kg baby bag and two baby equipment items free.”

Ryanair has no specific policy about crossbody bags, but you should be careful if you also have another bag with you, like a backpack or handbag.

Ryanair can fine passengers for extra bags, which can cost between £40 and £51.

To avoid being charged extra, you should always make sure you have enough space in your cabin bag to fit your crossbody bag inside.


Many people have been debating this topic on Reddit in the r/Ryanair thread and how they avoid being charged for their crossbody bags.

One user wrote: “I always have a small cross body bag along with my carry-on, but I hide it under my coat when going through the gate check.”

Another user wrote: “Technically they will charge you for it if they see it.

“However, you can put it in your other bag at the gate and take it out later, or hide it under your coat.”
