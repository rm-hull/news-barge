The hugely famous actor was at The Piece Hall with Alice Cooper and the rest of the Hollywood Vampires, which also consists of Joe Perry and Tommy Henriksen.

The band delivered a raucous celebration of rock’s greatest icons, alongside their own hits.

And the Halifax crowd clearly made an impression on Cooper, who told fans: “Thank you Halifax! This is our last show in the UK and you’ve been the best audience!”

Opening with Hollywood Vampires’ I Want My Now and Dead Drunk Friends, the band delivered a one hour and 45-minute set with highlights including Aerosmith’s Livin’ On The Edge, David Bowie’s Heroes, AC/DC’s Highway To Hell and Run-DMC’s Walk This Way.

A medley of Cooper’s School’s Out For Summer and Pink Floyd’s Another Brick In The Wall saw huge balloons sent into the crowd, before Cooper promptly burst them as they returned to the stage.

Following an earlier tribute to guitar legend Jeff Beck, the Vampires closed the night with Beck’s Hi Ho Silver Lining, joined by the crowd in a huge sing-along.

Special guests The Damned kicked off the night with five decades of groundbreaking music as part of their 50th anniversary celebrations.

Live at The Piece Hall continues tonight with Public Service Broadcasting.

Turnstile will perform on Wednesday before the final gig of 2026’s summer shows, by Bastille, on Friday night.
