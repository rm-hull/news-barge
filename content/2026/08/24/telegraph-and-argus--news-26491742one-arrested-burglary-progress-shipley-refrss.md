A woman was arrested after police responded to reports of a burglary in the Bradford district.

West Yorkshire Police were called to Fountain Way, Shipley, this afternoon.

Images on social media show several police cars and multiple officers attending the scene.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police were called to Fountain Way, Shipley at 4.19pm to a report of a burglary in progress.

"Officers attended and a woman was arrested in connection with the report.

"Enquiries remain ongoing."
