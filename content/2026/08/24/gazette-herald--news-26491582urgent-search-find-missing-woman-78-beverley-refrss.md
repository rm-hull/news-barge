  **UPDATE: The woman has since been found.**

POLICE have launched an urgent search for a missing woman from Yorkshire.

The woman, 78, from Beverley, was last seen in the Flemingate area at around 11.30am on Monday (August 24).

  


She may be wearing a white bodywarmer, pink cardigan, and a black shirt, with black slip-on shoes.

Humberside Police said: “If you have seen Sylvia or know where she may be, please contact us on 101 quoting log 283 of 24 August 2026.”
