Andy Burnham has arrived in Kyiv to honour Ukraine’s Independence Day, just hours after at least three people were killed in a Russian attack on the outskirts of Kharkiv.

It is the first overseas visit for the British prime minister, more than a month after entering Downing Street, and it comes as Kyiv and Moscow continued to exchange devastating attacks overnight.

He is set to announce that Britain has agreed to allow European missile manufacturer MBDA to release classified information on UK-made components used in the SCALP long-range cruise missile, according to his office.

"Ukraine's security is our security," Burnham said in a statement, adding that Britain would stand by Ukraine "for as long as it takes".

In Kharkiv, emergency services have rescued and evacuated eight people, some of whom were injured, governor Oleh Syniehubov said, adding the search was ongoing to identify any other possible casualties.

Meanwhile, two people were killed and two more injured in a Ukrainian drone strike on the Russian border region of Belgorod, local authorities said.

Ukrainian also hit another online retailer, setting fire to Ozon's logistics hub in Makhachkala in the Russian republic of Dagestan.

## Ukraine not ready 'to simply surrender' to Russia, Zalensky says

Ukraine is not ready “to simply surrender” to Russia at any price, Volodymyr Zelensky said in a defiant speech marking Independence Day celebrations.

Standing in a traditional Ukrainian embroidered shirt in front of the golden-domed St Michael's monastery in Kyiv where burned-out Russian tanks are on show, the president reiterated that his country will not surrender territory.

He said Russia's demand - repeatedly rejected by Ukraine - that Kyiv cede the remaining part of the eastern Donbas area that is not in Russian hands after 4-1/2 years of war would not be enough to stop the war.

"We will not let them (Russians) seize the initiative, deceive Europe and the entire world into thinking that we simply need to give up Donbas and everything will be over soon," Zelensky added.

Paying tribute to the people and soldiers defending Ukraine in the war, he again urged allies to bring Russia to the negotiating table and hit it with more sanctions.

## Two people killed in Ukrainian drone attack on Russia, authorities say

Two people have been killed in a Ukrainian drone strike on the Russian border region of Belgorod.

Local authorities said two others were also injured in the attack earlier today.

In the past week, Ukraine has been targeting Russian logistic depots in the region.

## EU unveils further £5.2 billion in aid for Ukrainian defence

The European Commission has unveiled a further £5.2 billion in aid for Ukraine - for new defence systems, such as air defence, missiles, ammunition and radars to repel the worsening Russian attacks.

This latest aid package comes on top of £13.6 billion of previously approved procurement plans, of which £7 billion had already been disbursed, the EU said.

"As Russia intensifies its attacks, we are stepping up to help Ukraine protect its people and defend its skies. Europe stands with Ukraine and we will deliver what it needs, when it needs it," said European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen in a statement.

## Why is the SCALP missile so important for Ukraine?

The SCALP missile is the French-manufactured version of the prized British Storm Shadow.

It is a long-range, air-launched cruise missile, designed to hit high‑value fixed or fortified targets with precision and low observability.

It has a strike capability of over 155 miles , which means that it would potentially allow Ukraine to attack targets deeper into Russian territory.

This will be a massive boost to Ukraine in its efforts to target military sites in Russia.

Downing Street said the decision will allow France and Ukraine to go ahead with assembly of the missile on Ukrainian territory.

It follows efforts by the two countries to expand local production of long-range weapons, so it does not have to rely entirely on deliveries of complete missiles from abroad.

Britain was the first country to provide Ukraine with Western long-range strike missiles when it supplied Storm Shadow back in 2023.

## Minister says Ukrainian elections are ‘internal matter for the people of Ukraine’

A UK government minister has insisted whether to call an election on Volodymyr Zelensky premiership is an “internal matter for the people of Ukraine”.

Zelensky has faced pressure to call an election in recent weeks after sacked defence minister Mykhailo Fedorov called for a contest.

Defence minister Luke Pollard told Sky News: “When to have an election is an internal matter for the people of Ukraine, the government of Ukraine.

## Germany's Wadephul to urge Rubio to take more active role on Ukraine

German foreign minister Johann Wadephul plans to speak to his US counterpart, Marco Rubio, by phone today to urge him to become more personally involved in diplomatic efforts related to the war in Ukraine, he told radio broadcaster Deutschlandfunk.

Wadephul, who was recently in Ukraine, told the broadcaster that US diplomacy had so far been handled largely by presidential envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff.

Negotiations between Russia and the US to bring about an end to the conflict in Ukraine have largely stalled amid the war in Iran, and the Kremlin said last week that it had no information yet on a possible next visit by Kushner and Witkoff.

Russian deputy foreign minister Sergei Ryabkov had said in an interview on Friday that Russia was ready to listen to Rubio's new ideas and continue dialogue with Washington.

"I may be able to encourage him (Rubio) myself to do more there," the German minister said, adding that Berlin was pursuing other channels to help restart negotiations.

## Ukraine hit Russian fighter aircraft and two vessels in Black Sea

The Ukrainian military struck a Russian MIG-29 fighter aircraft and two Russian vessels in the Black Sea overnight, Ukraine's drone forces commander Robert Brovdi said on Monday.

Brovdi said on Telegram that one of the vessels attacked was a tanker, and the other was a bulk carrier.

## Attack on another Russian logistics hub tanks company’s shares

Shares in Ozon fell by as much as 12 per cent on the Moscow Exchange on news of the attack, while shares in its major shareholder, private equity firm AFK Sistema, fell by almost 13 per cent.

Ozon has become Russia's second e-commerce giant, after rival Wildberries, to be targeted in Ukrainian drone attacks. Work at a logistics centre in the Samara region was halted after the first Ukrainian strike on the company's facilities on 22 August.
