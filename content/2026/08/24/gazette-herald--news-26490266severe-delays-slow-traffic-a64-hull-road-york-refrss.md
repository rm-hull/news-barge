MOTORISTS have been warned of severe traffic delays on a major road into York.

Wait times of up to 20 minutes have been reported on the A64 Hull Road.

  


The AA said: “Severe delays of 20 minutes and delays easing on A64 Eastbound between A1237 and Sandy Lane. Average speed ten mph.”
