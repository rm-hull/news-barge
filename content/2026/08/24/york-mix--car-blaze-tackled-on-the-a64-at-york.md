A car burst into flames on the York outer ring road today (Monday).

York, Acomb and Tadcaster firefighters were sent to the incident on the dual carriageway of the **A64** at**Fulford** at**9.46am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews extinguished the fire using one hose reel jet, one thermal imagining camera and breathing apparatus for comfort wearing.”
