A CYCLIST suffered a fractured ankle after being knocked off their bike in a "fail-to-stop" crash in York.

North Yorkshire Police said it happened at about 11.40am on Friday, August 21 in Haxby, in York Road near the junction with Holly Tree Lane.

A spokesperson for the force said a "dark blue" vehicle was involved which did not stop at the scene.

  



North Yorkshire Police is appealing for witnesses and information to help identify the driver.

A force spokesperson said: "We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the collision, saw a dark blue vehicle in the area at the time, or has dashcam footage that could assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing PC 1111 Cooper at [sophie.cooper@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:sophie.cooper@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, the live chat service on the North Yorkshire Police website is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

Those unable to use these options can call 101.

People who wish to remain anonymous can contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Please quote reference 12260163323 when providing information.
