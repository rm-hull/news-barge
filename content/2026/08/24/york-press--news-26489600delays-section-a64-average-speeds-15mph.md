MOTORISTS are facing delays on a main road in North Yorkshire.

The A64 is very busy this morning (Monday) with traffic currently queuing westbound at the Malton bypass.

The AA said: "There are delays of four minutes and delays increasing on A64 Westbound between A169 (Old Malton roundabout) and Barnygate Lane. Average speed is 15 mph."
