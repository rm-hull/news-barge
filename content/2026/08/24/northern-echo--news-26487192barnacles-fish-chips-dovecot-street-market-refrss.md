A popular Stockton chippy could be yours for just £50,000.

Barnacle’s Fish & Chips, located on Dovecot Street, has been a staple in Stockton-on-Tees for over 30 years and is now on the market for £50,000.

The fish and chip shop, which includes both a restaurant and a takeaway, has been under the same ownership for over 12 years and is now looking for a new owner.

The 1,920.63 sq ft property is held on a lease at £22,500 per year.

The business is fully managed by long-standing staff members who wish to stay on.

The kitchen is fully equipped with all needed to service both a restaurant and takeaway fish shop.

This includes a newer Martin Edwards range, which was purchased a few years ago, and a second older range that could be used with some maintenance.
