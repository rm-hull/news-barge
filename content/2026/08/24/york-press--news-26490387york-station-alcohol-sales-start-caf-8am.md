## Booze sales could start at 8am in York Station under new plans

Alcohol could be sold from a York Station café earlier in the day if an application is approved *(Image: Pic supplied)*

ALCOHOL could be sold from a York Station vendor earlier under newly-lodged plans.

An application to extend the hours when Café Local, between the station’s platforms five and nine, would see alcohol sales start from 8am and stop at midnight every day.

It would see the café permitted to sell alcohol two hours earlier than its current licensed hours which begin at 10am.

The vendor would continue to open from 5am to half past midnight daily if York Council approves its application.
