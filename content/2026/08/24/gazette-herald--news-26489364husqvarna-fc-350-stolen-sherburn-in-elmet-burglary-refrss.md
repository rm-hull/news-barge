POLICE are appealing for information after a burglary in a North Yorkshire town in which an off-road motorcycle was stolen.

The incident happened at around 11pm on Tuesday, 18 August 2026, in New Lane, Sherburn-in-Elmet, where a summer house was entered and a yellow Husqvarna FC 350cc off-road dirt bike was taken.

  


Officers from North Yorkshire Police are asking for help to locate the stolen motorcycle and are urging residents and motorists in the area to check any CCTV, doorbell, or dashcam footage that could assist the investigation.

Anyone with information is asked to email [lucy.godfrey@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:lucy.godfrey@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, people can call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and ask for PC 67 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or through their website.

When providing information, please quote reference 12260161563.
