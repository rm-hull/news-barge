The Federal Agency for Civic Education and the Berlin State Agency for Civic Education presented the online tool, featuring the leading candidates Steffen Krach (SPD), Werner Graf (Alliance 90/The Greens), Elif Eralp (The Left), and Kristin Brinker (AfD), as well as Lukas Krieger for the CDU. After evaluating 38 statements, the Wahl-O-Mat is designed to show which party and which platform a user’s views align with. The website went live on Monday, just under four weeks before the election.
