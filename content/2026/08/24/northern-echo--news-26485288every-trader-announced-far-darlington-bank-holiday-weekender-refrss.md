From loaded hot dogs to wood-fired pizzas and indulgent desserts, there’s something for every appetite at Darlington Market’s Bank Holiday Weekender.

The four-day event will take over Market Square with street food, drinks, shopping and live entertainment, and organisers have been steadily revealing the traders set to attend.

Here are 11 food and drink vendors you will be able to find across the weekend.

  Darlington Market Bank Holiday Weekender Poster 

*(Image: Darlington Market Facebook)*

  **1.      Chicken Ting**

Fried chicken fans will want to make a beeline for Chicken Ting, which will be serving up crispy, flavour-packed chicken throughout the event.

  **2.      Geordie Bangers**

Expect loaded hot dogs and plenty of classic comfort food from Geordie Bangers, offering what organisers describe as the perfect Bank Holiday scran.

  One of the many food options at this years event 

*(Image: Darlington Market on Facebook)*

  **3.      Only Food Not Horses**

Known for wood-fired pizzas and loaded fries, this trader promises hearty street food with plenty of flavour.

  **4.      Spud Monkey**

Sometimes simple is best. Spud Monkey will be bringing piping hot baked potatoes with a choice of fillings, alongside refreshing drinks.

  **5.      Lobo Acai Spot**

  Lobo Acai Spot 

*(Image: Lobo Acai Spot)*

For something a little different, visitors can sample authentic Brazilian acai, offering a fresh and fruity alternative to some of the heavier options on the menu.

  **6.      Good Grounds Coffee Cart**

  **7.      Simply Good Food**

Burgers, hot dogs and loaded pulled pork buns are all on the menu from Simply Good Food

  **8.      Mrs Puddington’s**

Those with a sweet tooth can enjoy indulgent hot desserts, while refreshing iced lemonade will also be available.

  **9.      Not a Lie Delicacies**

Homemade cakes, cake pops and other sweet treats will be available from Not A Lie Delicacies

  **10. ABV Events**

No Bank Holiday event would be complete without a bar, and ABV Events will be keeping the drinks flowing across the weekend.

  **11. Molly’s Pet pantry**

Not strictly one for the humans, but dog owners will be pleased to know Molly’s pet Pantry will be offering a range of products and treats for four-legged visitors.

  **When is the Darlington Bank Holiday Weekender?**

The event is taking place in Darlington Market Square across four days:

- Friday, August 28 from 12pm to 9pm

- Saturday, August 29 from 12pm to 9pm

- Sunday, August 30 from 12pm to 9pm

- Monday, August 31 from 12pm to 5pm

Organisers say more traders and entertainment announcements are still to come, with live performances and bars joining the food vendors throughout the weekend.
