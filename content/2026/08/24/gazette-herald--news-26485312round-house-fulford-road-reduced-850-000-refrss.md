A UNIQUELY designed three-bedroom home in a prime York location has seen its price slashed,

The Round House, a newly built home with a circular feature room located on the corner of Fulford Road and Grange Garth, has seen its price reduced to £895,000.

As reported by The Press in June, it was originally on the market for £950,000, but saw the price reduced on Thursday (August 20).

According to the listing, the newly built property combines a "high specification contemporary finish" with the "period charm of its surroundings".

A spokesperson for the estate agent said the house features “elegant panelling, high ceilings and carefully considered architectural detailing throughout”, adding: “This is a rare opportunity to acquire a truly unique property.”

A curved reception room on the ground floor provides space for living and dining, with a separate kitchen with newly fitted appliances including a range oven, fridge, freezer and dishwasher.

The first floor contains the main bathroom and a double bedroom, both of which have en-suite bathrooms, and the top floor contains another bedroom with a Juliet balcony.

Outside, the property has a courtyard garden, with planters and hedging privacy from Fulford Road.

A single garage is located to the rear and is accessed via Grange Garth.
