Back by popular demand Pannal Cricket Gala will be on Sun 30 August 2026

It will feature a crazy colour run with lots of obstacles, mud bath and water along with the air filled with plumes of colour that will be projected through cannons!

Wear a white t shirt and expect to finish the race looking colourful

It’s set to be crazy, fun and muddyOpen to all age groups will be running all day.

For all you dog friendly enthusiasts, there is dog show so make sure you bring your dog down.

And back with a vengeance is the famous Pannal Cricket Dunking Stall – if you hit the target consider the Pannal Cricket chairman and coaches dunked in water – who’s up for the challenge?

Also running throughout the day is a cricket competition who can score the most runs.

Lots going on for the kids – bouncy castle, stalls tombola and a raffle.

Not forgetting the adults, they have a new bar serving Pimms, fizz and lagers. Tea and cake stalls, a famous Pannal cricket book stall selling a fantastic selection of pre loved books along with pizza oven and BBQ.
