Suspected burglars have been pictured as police continue to investigate a break-in at a County Durham cricket club.

A group of intruders forced their way into Sedgefield Cricket Club on Station Road shortly after 11.35pm on Saturday, August 15.

Officers attended on an immediate response, but the suspects had fled before police arrived.

Nothing is believed to have been stolen during the break-in, although damage was caused to the building.

Police are now reviewing CCTV footage and speaking to potential witnesses as the investigation remains ongoing.

The Northern Echo has obtained CCTV images from Sedgefield Cricket Club.

A spokesperson said: "We are sorry to share with you all that the cricket club was broken into Saturday evening.

"To the four who caused damage to the club house and escaped with nothing what was the point? We have handed over clear CCTV footage to the police. It makes for interesting viewing.

"A huge thanks to Durham constabulary for their timely response and also the CSI units diligence in gathering further evidence from the break-in."

Anyone with information which could help officers is asked to contact police on 101, quoting crime reference number CRI00701197.

Information can also be submitted online through Durham Police
