A new health food retailer has opened its doors, bringing fresh choice to shoppers in North Yorkshire.

Grape Tree is the latest addition to the Market Cross Shopping Centre in Selby.

It offers a wide selection of natural ingredients and wholefoods, including nuts, dried fruit, seeds, grains, and products for gluten-free, vegan, and high-protein diets.

  


Speaking about this, a representative for Grape Tree said: "We're delighted to be opening our new Grape Tree store at Market Cross and look forward to becoming part of the local community.

  The store offers a wide selection of products packed with natural ingredients and wholefoods 

*(Image: Market Cross Shopping Centre)*

They added: "Our aim is to make healthy eating more accessible by offering quality health foods, natural ingredients and everyday essentials at great value prices.

"We can't wait to welcome customers through our doors and introduce them to everything Grape Tree has to offer."

The arrival of Grape Tree means Market Cross is now fully let, with every unit occupied.

James Shepherd, managing director of Dransfield Properties, which owns and manages the centre, said: "We’re delighted to welcome Grape Tree to Market Cross.

"The store brings a fantastic range of healthy produce, giving our customers even more choice.

"Every unit at Market Cross is now occupied, which is a strong endorsement of the centre and its position at the heart of Selby.

"It also demonstrates continued confidence in the town centre and its future as a thriving destination for businesses, shoppers and the wider community."

Grape Tree opened its first store in 2013 and has since grown into a national health food retailer with more than 100 stores.
