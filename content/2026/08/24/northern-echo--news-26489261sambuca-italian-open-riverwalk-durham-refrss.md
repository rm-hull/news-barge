An Italian restaurant chain is preparing to open in Durham.

Sambuca will move into the former Thai River unit at the Riverwalk.

The Thai venue, previously known as Thai Central, moved to The Riverwalk in 2017, but closed last June.

In a Facebook post, Sambuca confirmed the site will open soon.

The post read: “Opening soon. Riverside’s new Italian fusion destination. Great food, beautiful atmosphere, memorable experiences. The wait is almost over.”

Sambuca has locations across the North East and offers a range of Italian food, including pizza, pasta and risotto.
