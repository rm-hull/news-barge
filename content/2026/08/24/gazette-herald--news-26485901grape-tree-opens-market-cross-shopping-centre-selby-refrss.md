A new health food shop has opened in Selby.

Grape Tree, the latest addition to the Market Cross shopping centre, offers an extensive range of wholefood products including nuts, dried fruit, seeds and grains.

The store also stocks gluten-free, vegan, and high-protein options.

A spokesperson for Grape Tree said: "We're delighted to be opening our new Grape Tree store at Market Cross and look forward to becoming part of the local community.

"Our aim is to make healthy eating more accessible by offering quality health foods, natural ingredients and everyday essentials at great value prices.

"We can't wait to welcome customers through our doors and introduce them to everything Grape Tree has to offer."

Staff will be on hand to recommend products and help shoppers make the most of the store’s regular offers.

The opening of Grape Tree means all retail units at Market Cross are now fully let.

James Shepherd, managing director of Dransfield Properties, which owns and manages the centre, said: "We’re delighted to welcome Grape Tree to Market Cross.

"The store brings a fantastic range of healthy produce, giving our customers even more choice.

"Every unit at Market Cross is now occupied, which is a strong endorsement of the centre and its position at the heart of Selby.

"It also demonstrates continued confidence in the town centre and its future as a thriving destination for businesses, shoppers and the wider community."
