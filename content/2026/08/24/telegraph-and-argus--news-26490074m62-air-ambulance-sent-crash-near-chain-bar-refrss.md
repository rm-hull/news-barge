Emergency services were called to a crash on the M62 on Saturday evening.

The collision took place near Chain Bar at 6.48pm, the Yorkshire Ambulance Service have confirmed.

They added that nobody involved was taken to hospital.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 6.48pm on Saturday, August 22 to report a road traffic collision on the M62 near Chain Bar.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including two ambulances and the air ambulance, but no patients were conveyed to hospital."
