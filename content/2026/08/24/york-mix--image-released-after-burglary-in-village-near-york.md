Police investigating a burglary want to trace this woman.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cctv-main-street-escrick-4-july-2026-full.webp)

It happened at about 3pm on Saturday 4 July on Main Street, Escrick.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “While nothing was taken from the property, officers are keen to identify and speak to the woman pictured as part of their ongoing enquiries.

“Do you recognise her? If so, please get in touch. We are also asking the woman herself to contact police.”

If you can help police, you can contact them on the numbers below.
