A 15-year-old boy named Luca has been reported missing from Bingley.

He is described as a Kurdish male of small build with short black hair.

  Missing Luca 

*(Image: WYP)*

He was last seen wearing a black T-shirt, tracksuit bottoms, and black Crocs.

Luca is known to have links to Middlesborough, Stockton-on-Tees, and Hartlepool.

Anyone with information or sightings is asked to call 101, quoting log reference 1845 23/08/26.
