The winners of this year's Knaresborough FEVA window competition have been revealed.

Businesses and organisations across the town turned their windows pink as part of the annual Festival of Entertainment and Visual Arts, which ran from 14th to 23rd August.

Knitting Pretty was crowned this year's winner following a closely fought competition.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a8be50700c6134f937.jpg)


Painting Pots took second place, with Yorkshire Eyewear finishing third and Drake's Fisheries fourth.

A spokesperson for Knaresborough Chamber said:

"A big thank you to the Knaresborough Town Criers for judging this year's competition, and to everyone who helped bring so much colour and community spirit to the town. Congratulations to all our winners!"


FEVA came to an end yesterday (Sunday) following ten days of live music, visual arts, performances and other events across Knaresborough.
