Harrogate Town AFC have signed Oliver Pimlott, from Burnley, on a one-month loan deal.

The 19-year-old left wing back joined the Clarets at U14 level and has since earned a professional deal with the Lancashire club, as well as being named in their senior matchday squad.


**Oliver Pimlott said:**

I’m really happy to be here and looking forward to the challenge ahead.

I’m high energy, tenacious and love bombing on, so when I heard about the interest I couldn’t wait to get here and all the lads have been great so far.


Pimlott, who made over 30 appearances for The Clarets’ U21s last season was available for the weekend’s clash with Barrow, subject to FA and National League approval.

Oliver will wear the number 22 shirt at Town.
