Police are investigating the alleged assault of a police officer in York.

The incident occurred at around 6.20pm on Saturday (August 22) in Scarcroft Hill, shortly after the end of the Ebor Festival at York Racecourse.

North Yorkshire Police said: “During an incident in which officers were assisting with the arrest of another individual, a police officer was pushed, causing them to stumble and fall to the ground."

  


A force spokesperson added: “We believe the man shown in the image may have information that could assist our investigation and are asking him, or anyone who recognises him, to get in touch.

“Please email [Aimee.Turnbull@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Aimee.Turnbull@northyorkshire.police.uk) if you can help.

“Alternatively, you can call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

“Please quote reference number 12260164304 when providing any information.”
