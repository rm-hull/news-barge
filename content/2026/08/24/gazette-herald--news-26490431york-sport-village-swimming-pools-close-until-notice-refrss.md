TWO swimming pools in York have closed.

York Sport Village, in Heslington, has closed its main pool and training pool until “further notice”.

The move came after the sports centre reported a “technical issue” on Sunday (August 23).

  


It cancelled all lessons this morning.

A spokesperson said: “We apologise for any inconvenience caused.”
