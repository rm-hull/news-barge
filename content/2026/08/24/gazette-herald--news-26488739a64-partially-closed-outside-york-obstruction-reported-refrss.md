A MAJOR road in York has been partially blocked this morning (Monday, August 24) after reports of an "obstruction" in a carriageway.

The A64 eastbound has been affected from the A659 junction with Tadcaster to the Bilbrough junction.


There are reports of slow traffic in the area.

The Press will bring more information on this story as we receive it.
