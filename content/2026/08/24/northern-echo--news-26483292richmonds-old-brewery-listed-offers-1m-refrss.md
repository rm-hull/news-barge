Dating from the early 19th century, The Old Brewery is an attractive Grade II-listed home on Richmond’s green – just a short walk from the town centre.

Behind its sandstone exterior, the property is far more substantial than it appears, with features including period fireplaces, sash windows, a wrought-iron balcony and exposed beams adding an abundance of character to the beautifully presented interior.

The front door opens, as you would expect, into a hall, off which are a sitting room, a games room equally suited to being used as a home office or boot room, a snug and a large formal dining room with a feature fireplace.

  The Old Brewery and Annexe offer buyers the potential to create a home and business or the perfect home for multigenerational living 

*(Image: FINE & COUNTRY)*

Also on the ground floor is a cloakroom, a double bedroom with en-suite shower room and the kitchen. Well equipped with integrated appliances, an island unit with a breakfast bar and contemporary grey cabinetry, this opens into the breakfast room. There is also a substantial cellar accessed from the snug.

Upstairs, off the landing, are a stylish family bathroom and five double bedrooms, three with en suites.

  The Old Brewery and Annexe offer buyers the potential to create a home and business or the perfect home for multigenerational living 

*(Image: FINE & COUNTRY)*

Then there’s The Annexe, a separate property with its own entrance.

Briefly, its accommodation comprises an entrance hall and three bedrooms – two with en-suite shower rooms and the other with an en-suite bathroom – on the ground floor. One of the bedrooms also has patio doors opening into a private seating area.

The first floor is home to a well-appointed kitchen, a generous family bathroom and a dual-aspect open-plan living room with vaulted ceilings and exposed beams, as well as ample space for a dining area. A staircase leads from here up to a mezzanine level ideal for use as a home office, which also benefits from additional storage and a cloakroom.

  The Old Brewery and Annexe offer buyers the potential to create a home and business or the perfect home for multigenerational living 

*(Image: FINE & COUNTRY)*

Both properties enjoy low-maintenance enclosed courtyard gardens with planted raised beds and stone-paved patio seating areas. These gardens could easily be combined to create a larger unified outdoor space if desired.

The Old Brewery also has a timber shed with power.

Both The Old Brewery and The Annexe are currently run as successful holiday lets but could also be used as a home for multigenerational living or as a home and a holiday let.
