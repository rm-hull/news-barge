A colour obstacle course and dog show will feature when Pannal Cricket Gala returns this weekend.

The family event will be held on Sunday (30th August), with activities and entertainment running throughout the day.

A new crazy colour run will see participants tackle obstacles, water and a mud bath as colourful powder is projected from cannons.

The activity is open to all age groups, with participants encouraged to wear white T-shirts which will become covered in colour during the course.

A dog show will also be held for visitors bringing their four-legged friends.

The club’s popular dunking stall will return, giving visitors the chance to send Pannal Cricket Club’s chairman and coaches into the water by hitting a target.

A cricket competition will challenge participants to score as many runs as possible, while children’s entertainment will include a bouncy castle, stalls, a tombola and a raffle.

Food and drink will be available from a pizza oven, barbecue, tea and cake stalls and a new bar serving Pimm’s, fizz and lager.

The club’s book stall will also sell a selection of pre-loved titles throughout the gala.
