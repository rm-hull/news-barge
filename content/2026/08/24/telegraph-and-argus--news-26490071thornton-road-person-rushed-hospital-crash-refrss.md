A person was taken to hospital following a crash in Bradford yesterday.

An emergency call was received following the collision on Thornton Road at 5pm on Sunday.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 5pm yesterday (23 August) to report a road traffic collision on Thornton Road in Bradford.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including an ambulance and a rapid response vehicle, and one patient was conveyed to hospital."

West Yorkshire Police have been contacted for a comment.
