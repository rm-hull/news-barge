POLICE are appealing for help to trace the owners of horses found in Bradford.

The animals are at P&B Food Ltd on Newhall Way, off Rooley Lane, and are currently safe, the force said.

West Yorkshire Police issued the following statement.

  One of the loose horses 

*(Image: WYP)*

  One of the loose horses 

*(Image: WYP)*

A spokesperson said: "A shout out to the owners of these horses.

"If you believe these horses belong to you, or you know who the owner may be, please make contact and arrange for them to be collected promptly.

"The horses are currently safe, however we are asking the owner(s) to please retrieve them as soon as possible."
