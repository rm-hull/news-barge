## What is the launch time for Ted Lasso season 4 episode 4?

 ![AFC Richmond Women's team standing in their changing room in Ted Lasso season 4 episode 4](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/KYvFao22etaPaw79Mh7CBT.jpg) 


Episode 4 of *Ted Lasso*'s fourth season will be released in the US and Canada at**6pm PT / 9pm ET on Tuesday, August 25**.

As I've explained in other articles like this one, Apple had publicly indicated that new chapters would drop on one of the best streaming services worldwide every Wednesday. However, this season's first three entries have aired in North and South America on Tuesday evenings, so this week's installment is all but certain to do likewise.

Where the rest of the world is concerned, *Ted Lasso* season 4 episode 4 will arrive on Wednesday, August 26. Check out the list below to see what time it'll come out where you live:

- **US** — Tuesday, August 25 at 6pm PT / 9pm ET
- **Canada** — Tuesday, August 25 at 6pm PT / 9pm ET
- **UK** — Wednesday, August 26 at 2am BST
- **India** — Wednesday, August 26 at 6:30am IST
- **Singapore** — Wednesday, August 26 at 9am SGT
- **Australia** — Wednesday, August 26 at 11am AEST
- **New Zealand** — Wednesday, August 26 at 1pm NZST
