*Wonka 2* is pressing ahead, Warner Bros has claimed after the first film’s co-writer claimed it had been “delayed indefinitely.”

*Wonka* starred Hollywood phenom Timothée Chalamet as a young version of Roald Dahl’s magical chocolate factory owner and was a huge hit at the box office, earning over $634 million worldwide against a $125 million budget.

However, according to writer Simon Farnaby, the fate of its revival is down to its star, Chalamet, who is one of the most in-demand actors in Hollywood.

“[Chalamet] wants to do one, but he’s not sure about timing or what he wants to do next,” Farnaby told *Variety***in a new interview, adding that* Wonka 2* was “delayed indefinitely.”

However, Warner Bros has now hit back at Farnaby’s suggestion, with a spokesperson telling *The Independent*: “Reports suggesting a* Wonka 2* delay are entirely false. The Wonka creative team is hard at work on the next chapter". 

![‘Wonka' lead Timothée Chalamet has reportedly stalled plans for a sequel as he figures out his next move in Hollywood](https://static.the-independent.com/2023/12/04/11/rev-1-WON-D23-00044r_High_Res_JPEG.jpeg)

Farnaby later followed up with his own clarification, writing on X: “Hi all, just wanted to clear up some rumours about *Wonka 2* after my*Variety* interview. My quotes were taken little out of context by some people. I 100% did not say*Wonka 2* wasn’t happening. A sequel was always the plan. Everyone is working really hard on it. 

“WB are committed. There is a great script by Paul and I. Timmy wants to do it (like I said!) it’s just about working out schedules and making sure it’s as fantastic as we know it can be - and these things take a little time. Very sorry for any confusion! Good day sirs.”

Farnaby co-wrote the first film’s script with his longtime collaborator Paul King — the writer and director behind the Paddington film franchise.

*Wonka* served as a prequel to 1971’s *Willy Wonka & the Chocolate Factory*— starring Gene Wilder as the chocolatier — which is itself based on Dahl’s classic children’s novel* Charlie and the Chocolate Factory.*

The latest film follows “a young, ambitious Willy Wonka as he arrives in a greedy city determined to open his own chocolate shop, using pure imagination to beat a corrupt local chocolate cartel,” according to an official description.

The supporting cast included Keegan-Michael Key, Matt Lucas, Sally Hawkins, Rowan Atkinson, Olivia Colman and Hugh Grant.

Following the box office success of *Wonka* and*Dune: Part Two*, Chalamet, 30, appears to have set his sights on more serious subject matter, earning widespread acclaim — and an Oscar nomination for Best Actor — for his portrayal of Bob Dylan in* A Complete Unknown*. 

He then starred in Josh Safdie’s *Marty Supreme*, which earned him another Academy Award nomination, and he is next set to reprise his role as Paul Atreides in Denis Villeneuve’s* Dune: Part Three*.

Chalamet made his feelings clear when accepting the Screen Actors Guild (now the Actor Awards) award for Best Actor in 2025.

“I know we’re in a subjective business, but the truth is, I’m really in pursuit of greatness,” he said. “I know people don’t usually talk like this, but I want to be one of the greats.”
