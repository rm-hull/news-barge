# Ransom hackers breached top financial firms with simple phone calls: report

- Ransom-seeking hackers have targeted dozens of prominent U.S. financial institutions and businesses over the past month, employing phone calls and sophisticated fake websites.
- Operating under aliases such as Redact, Pink, Falcon, and Helix, these cybercriminals impersonate company help desks to trick employees into revealing passwords and multi-factor authentication codes.
- Major private equity firms like Blackstone, Bridgewater Associates, and KKR, along with hedge funds and other companies including Uber and Zillow, were among the targets.
- Experts highlight that this low-tech social engineering tactic, despite advanced security measures, remains remarkably effective in breaching corporate defenses.
- The hackers strategically target industries with sensitive data, believing these firms are more likely to pay ransoms to prevent data exposure, with some unnamed companies already having done so.
