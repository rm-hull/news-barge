The *Jurassic World* franchise is on the hunt for a new director as Gareth Edwards has stepped away from the next instalment over creative differences. 

Edwards took the helm on last year’s *Jurassic World: Rebirth*, which saw Scarlett Johansson, Mahershala Ali and Jonathan Bailey (and his tiny glasses) star as new characters on a mission to extract DNA from three living dinosaurs. 

The film was largely a success, with global takings of more than $800m against an estimated production budget of $225m – but it’s now been confirmed that Edwards will not be back for more.

Universal Pictures appeared to confirm the news of Edwards’ exit in a statement, telling *Entertainment Weekly*: “We had an incredible experience working with Gareth on* Jurassic World Rebirth*.” However, according to the publication, Edwards left the franchise over “creative differences” despite initially being expected to return for a follow-up. 

*The Independent* has contacted Edwards’ representative for comment. 

The director had previously shared his hopes for *Jurassic World*, saying* Rebirth*felt like the beginning of a new trilogy “in a way”. He told* EW*: “I’m not sure what [Universal’s] plans are, but it felt like the beginning of a brand-new chapter in this franchise.”

Edwards, a former visual-effects artist, made his feature directorial debut with 2010’s *Monsters*– a low-budget sci-fi, horror film – before taking the helm on the 2014 remake of* Godzilla*and* Rogue One: A Star Wars Story* in 2016. 

Plot details for the *Rebirth* sequel are yet to be confirmed, but Johansson, Ali and Bailey are all expected to return, along with screenwriter David Koepp, a frequent Steven Spielberg collaborator who previously penned the scripts for *Disclosure Day*,* Spider-Man*and* War Of The Worlds*. Shooting is slated to begin next year. 

The film will be the eighth instalment in the *Jurassic* franchise, which kicked off with Spielberg’s classic 1993 adventure film*Jurassic Park*. The Oscar-winning director then returned with the 1997 follow-up* The Lost World*, before Joe Johnston took over to finish the initial trilogy with 2001’s* Jurassic World III*. 

Universal then launched its first spinoff trilogy with 2015’s *Jurassic World*, starring Chris Pratt and Bryce Dallas Howard – and that time around, each film managed to bring in more than $1billion at the box office.
