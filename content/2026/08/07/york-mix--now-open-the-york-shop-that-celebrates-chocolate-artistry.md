If you love chocolate, or know someone who does, we have some good news…

A new shop has opened that’s all about taking the confectionery to the next level.

**Savante** is an innovative new retailer that is “redefining gifting through its unique concept of chocolate artistry”.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/savante-chocolate-york-designer-outlet-opening-4.webp)

It’s just opened at York Designer Outlet.

Savante has two distinct collections. The Form Series features everyday objects sculpted in chocolate with remarkable precision, from tools and car-inspired designs to coffee-themed creations and more.

With over 50 designs available, the aim is to help shoppers find gifts that feel truly personal.

Then there’s the **Flavour Series**, a range of luxury truffles, pralines, dragées and filled bars, “hand-finished in Monza, Italy by artisan chocolatiers dedicated to perfecting flavour and craftsmanship”. 

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/savante-chocolate-york-designer-outlet-opening-2.webp)

Centre manager at Designer Outlet York Paul Tyler said: “We’re delighted to welcome Savante to Designer Outlet York.

“The brand brings something genuinely unique to our retail mix, combining creativity, craftsmanship and luxury gifting in a way that we know will resonate with our guests.

“Whether you’re searching for a special gift or simply looking to discover something different, Savante offers an experience that is as memorable as the products themselves.”

Ed Starr from Savante, said: “We are thrilled to bring the concept of ‘Chocolate Artistry’ to Designer Outlet York.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/savante-chocolate-york-designer-outlet-opening-5.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/savante-chocolate-york-designer-outlet-opening-3.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/savante-chocolate-york-designer-outlet-opening-1.webp)

“At Savante, we believe that gifting should go beyond the ordinary to offer a truly intentional, memorable experience.

“Whether it’s a meticulously detailed chocolate sculpture from our Form Series designed to surprise and spark conversation, or a stunning box of hand-finished truffles and pralines from our Flavour Series crafted to elevate the everyday, our goal is to redefine what a gift can be.

“We want to help people move away from generic presents and give something exceptional, memorable, and crafted to absolute perfection.”

Visitors can now discover Savante’s distinctive collection of chocolate artistry near the south entrance at Designer Outlet York.
