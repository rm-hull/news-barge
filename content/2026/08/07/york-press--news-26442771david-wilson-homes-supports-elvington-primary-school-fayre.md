A housebuilder is helping bring events to life at a school's summer fayre in York.

David Wilson Homes sponsored stalls at Elvington Church of England Primary School’s recent summer fayre, held near its Bramble Wood development in Wheldrake, as part of its wider outreach programme in the area.

Paul Hogan, sales director at David Wilson Homes Yorkshire East, said: "It is a pleasure to be able to support local schools and help to bring more events such as this to the community.

"We hope all those who attended had a fantastic day."

The event, organised by the school’s PTA, included a disco, stalls and other activities for pupils and their families.

Kara Palmer, PTA spokesperson, said: "We are incredibly grateful to David Wilson Homes for supporting our Summer Fayre.

"Its sponsorship made a real difference and will help us to continue to provide opportunities and resources that benefit all of the pupils at the school."
