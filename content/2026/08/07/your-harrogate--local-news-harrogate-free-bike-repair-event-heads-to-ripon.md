                                    Cyclists in Ripon can get their bikes checked and repaired for free this weekend.

Changing the Landscape CIC is bringing its community cycle fix to Ripon Market Place tomorrow (Saturday 8th August), following a successful event in Harrogate’s Valley Gardens last month.

The team will be on hand from 10am until 1:30pm, offering free bike fixes, servicing and advice for cyclists.

The event forms part of Cycling UK’s national Big Bike Revival campaign, which aims to encourage more people to get cycling and help existing cyclists keep their bikes safe and roadworthy.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a35377b1f01e.jpg)


The previous Harrogate event offered free safety checks, tune-ups, basic repairs and maintenance advice.

The Ripon session is free to attend.
