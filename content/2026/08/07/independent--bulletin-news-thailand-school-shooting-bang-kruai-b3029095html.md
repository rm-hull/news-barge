# At least seven dead in Thai school shooting

- At least seven people, including a teacher and pupils, have died following a school shooting near Bangkok in Thailand.
- The attack occurred around 10am local time on Friday at Debsirin Nonthaburi School in Bang Kruai district.
- At least 15 people were injured during the incident, with two currently remaining in a critical condition.
- Police confirmed that the suspected gunman, reportedly a ninth-grade student, died by suicide at the scene.
- The incident marks Thailand's second major school shooting this year, raising concerns despite the country's strict gun laws.
