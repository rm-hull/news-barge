# Chilling discovery as snow melts away in Nepal’s Rolwaling Valley

- Human remains, believed to be five climbers missing since an avalanche last November, have been located in Nepal's Rolwaling Valley.
- The bodies were exposed at Yalung Ri base camp this week as seasonal snow melted, nearly nine months after the disaster.
- Local residents reported the discovery on Thursday, prompting a police team to attempt recovery on Friday.
- Poor weather conditions prevented the immediate retrieval of the remains, which are yet to be formally identified.
- The five are thought to be among seven climbers, including foreign nationals and Nepali guides, who were swept away in the 3 November avalanche, with two bodies recovered shortly after.
