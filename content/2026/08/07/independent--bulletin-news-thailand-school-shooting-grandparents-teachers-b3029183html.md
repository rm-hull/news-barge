# Thailand shooting: Boy, 14, kills his own grandparents and schoolteachers

- A 14-year-old student killed his grandparents before attacking Debsirin Nonthaburi School in Thailand on Friday morning.
- The attack, which began around 10am local time, resulted in the deaths of five people at the school, including teachers.
- The suspected shooter died by suicide, bringing the total death toll to eight, including his grandparents.
- At least 15 people were injured in the incident, with two remaining in critical condition.
- This marks the second major school shooting in Thailand this year, a country with strict gun laws but a high rate of private gun ownership.
