# Major retailer recalls children’s bottle over choking risk

![Home Bargains has issued a product recall for the TJ Morris Toy Story Water Bottle.](https://static.independent.co.uk/2021/06/03/14/iStock-1311985593.jpg)

- Home Bargains has issued a product recall for the TJ Morris Toy Story Water Bottle.
- The recall is due to a choking hazard, as the bottle's spout can detach from the lid.
- A detached spout creates a small part that could obstruct a child's airway.
- The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.
- Customers are advised to stop using the bottle immediately and return it to Home Bargains for a full refund.
