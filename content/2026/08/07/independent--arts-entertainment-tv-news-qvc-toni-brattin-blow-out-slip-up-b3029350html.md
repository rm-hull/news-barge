A QVC host’s on-air slip-up has sent the internet into hysterics.

Toni Brattin, the founder of Toni Brattin Wigs, appeared Wednesday in a baby blue dress on the QVC shopping channel to promote her “Salon Select Wig.”

Showing off the back of the wig, she highlighted its “texture and wave pattern.”

“Again, it looks like you went and got a b** at your,” Brattin said before slowly correcting herself. “Blowout … at your salon.”

“Oh my god,” she whispered to herself, adding through laughs, “and it looks beautiful.” Her co-host, Terri Conn, quickly took over as Brattin, still back to the camera, continued quietly laughing before slowly sinking down behind the table.

![Toni Brattin appeared on the QVC channel to promote her Toni Brattin wigs](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/07/13/57/Toni-Brattin.png)

The clip then cut to another model standing in a green top, smiling and zhuzhing the wig.

Conn later shared the now-viral clip on Instagram, writing alongside a crying laughing emoji: “The show must go on. Love you, Toni Brattin.”

The official QVC Instagram account commented on the post, praising Brattin for “handling this like a pro. Is there an award for Best Live Blooper Recovery??”

The footage has since been shared on X and viewed more than 11 million times, with thousands of comments expressing amusement at Brattin’s blunder.

“Caught me off guard and I’m still laughing,” one person wrote, while a second declared, “You know it’s a wild moment when even the people on live TV don’t know how to react.”

“She never turned around ever again,” a third quipped, as someone else applauded Brattin’s “freeze right after the wrong word landed” as “pure live TV gold.”

“This is the best thing that happened today,” another chimed in.

![Brattin has been in the beauty and wig business for over 30 years](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/07/13/35/Toni-Brattin.jpeg)

Brattin has been in the beauty and wig business for over 30 years. She regularly appears on the QVC channel to host her dedicated “Toni Brattin Hair Fabulous” segments.

Speaking to *TMZ* about the hilarious moment, Brattin explained her brief pause, admitting she initially thought producers would switch cameras. 

She said that after the broadcast was over, everyone in the studio had a good laugh. Brattin added that QVC approached her the next morning, reassuring her and thanking her for her professionalism.

Instead of shying away from her gaffe, Brattin is leaning into it, recently sharing an Instagram video of Jimmy Fallon’s reaction on*The Tonight Show.*

After playing the clip, the late-night host mimicked Brattin, facing away from the camera. “By the way, the wigs are totally sold out,” Fallon joked. “Note to all the married men out there, the wig is only available on your birthday.”

“Jimmy Fallon makes it even funnier,” Brattin wrote.
