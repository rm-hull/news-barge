The BBC series Monarch of the Glen is being revived by Channel 5, 21 years after its original run concluded.

The reboot will feature a new female lead, Isla Campbell, played by Julia Brown, a lawyer who returns to the Scottish Highlands to find she has inherited a £1m debt.

The cast also includes Bridgerton actor Chris Fulton and Downton Abbey’s Phyllis Logan, alongside Ewan Miller, Rishi Nair, John Gordon Sinclair and Gordon Kennedy.

The new series is produced by PBS Masterpiece and Ecosse Films, with Jeremy Brock as creator and writer, alongside Stephen Greenhorn, and directed by Tom Vaughan.

Monarch of the Glen is loosely based on Compton Mackenzie’s Highland Novels, which are set in the 1930s and 1940s.
