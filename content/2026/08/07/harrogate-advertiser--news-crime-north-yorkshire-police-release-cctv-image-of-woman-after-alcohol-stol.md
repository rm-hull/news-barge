# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image of woman after alcohol stolen from supermarket in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police has released a CCTV image of a woman they would like to speak to following a theft at a supermarket in Harrogate.

The incident happened on July 6 at Sainsbury’s, on Cold Bath Road, and involved the theft of alcohol.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Please contact us if you recognise the female pictured in the CCTV image as she may have information that will assist our investigation.”
