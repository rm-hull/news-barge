# Cameron Norrie stages stunning comeback to beat Alex De Minaur in Montreal

- Cameron Norrie defeated third seed Alex De Minaur in the third round of the National Bank Open in Montreal.
- Norrie staged a remarkable comeback, saving a match point against De Minaur.
- The British number two secured victory with a final score of 5-7 7-6 (5) 6-1.
- After losing the first set and being 5-2 down in the second, Norrie fought back to win the tie-break and force a decider.
- Norrie dominated the third set, breaking De Minaur three times to seal the win in two hours and 53 minutes, continuing his strong form.
