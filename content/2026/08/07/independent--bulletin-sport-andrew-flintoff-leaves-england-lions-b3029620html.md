# What next for Andrew Flintoff after leaving England coaching role?

![Andrew Flintoff had been England Lions head coach since 2024](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/02/11/22e6579a007607f1fc64eeadaa1e7b43Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzgwNDgwNDc3-2.80463767.jpg)

- Andrew Flintoff has stepped down as head coach of the England Lions after almost two years in the role.
- The former England all-rounder was highly regarded for his work in developing the country's brightest young cricketing talents.
- Flintoff is leaving to take up a new coaching position with the Sydney Thunder in Australia's Big Bash League this winter.
- His departure comes shortly after Rob Key, the ECB's managing director, praised his coaching and tipped him as a potential future England head coach.
- Mike Yardy, a former Sussex and England all-rounder, will temporarily take charge for an upcoming Professional County Club Select XI match.
