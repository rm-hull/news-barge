# The 5 most expensive goalkeepers ever as James Trafford seals £40m Leeds move

England’s James Trafford has signed for Leeds in a £40m deal – but who are the most expensive goalkeepers ever?

![James Trafford has moved to Leeds United](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/10/28/13/c80cc04aaf3188f4123e12d51a4c1a5aY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzYxNzQ0Njgz-2.81547436.jpg)

- **Kepa Arrizabalaga** remains the most expensive goalkeeper at £72m, moving from Athletic Bilbao to Chelsea in 2018, winning the Europa League and Champions League before joining Arsenal in July 2025 after loan spells.
- **Alisson Becker,** signed by Liverpool from Roma for £67m in 2018, has been crucial to the Reds' success, securing two Premier League titles, a Champions League, two Carabao Cups, and an FA Cup.
- **Andre Onana** joined Manchester United from Inter Milan for £43.8m in July 2023 but faced pressure due to errors, leading to a second successive loan spell with Trabzonspor.
- **James Trafford** has become the fourth-most expensive goalkeeper, moving from Manchester City to Leeds for a reported £40m plus bonuses, signing a five-year contract.
- **Ederson**, who joined Manchester City for £35m in 2017, won 18 major honours including six Premier League titles and a Champions League, before his departure for Fenerbahce.
