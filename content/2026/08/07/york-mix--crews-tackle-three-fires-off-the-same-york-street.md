York crews were called to three fires in less than 20 minutes off the same street.

The calls were to **Water Lane** between**4.08pm** and**4.25pm**.

Water Lane backs on to the Clifton Backies nature reserve.

#### Water Lane, York

Firefighters were first called to a fire in the open. On arrival they found this was out and used a backpack sprayer to damp down the area.

They then discovered a further fire nearby consisting of wood and tree branches, and used two further backpack sprayers to extinguish.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A further crew attended a separate area of Water Lane following a report of a further fire in the open. Crew confirmed a fire involving approximately 200 sq metres of grassland.

“One hose reel and beaters used to extinguish. Police also attended the area.”
