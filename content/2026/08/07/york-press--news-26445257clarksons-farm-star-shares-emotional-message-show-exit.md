It has been one year since Clarkson's Farm star Harriet Cowan shared an emotional message after her exit from the Prime Video show.

The 25-year-old farmhand from Derbyshire was a new addition to the cast in the fourth season, which was released on Prime Video across May and June in 2025.

She temporarily stood in for long-time fan favourite Kaleb Cooper as he toured, helping Jeremy Clarkson at the farm in Chadlington, Oxfordshire.

Ms Cowan left midway through the series once her help was no longer needed, but became popular with viewers despite her short time on screen.

Farmers across the UK took part in Farm24 last August 7, and the Derbyshire-born star officially backed last year's campaign.

The event, also known as "24 Hours in Farming," is an annual event which showcases the work of British farmers, with the hashtag #Farm24 used on social media to share a day's worth of their farming activities, from 5am to 5am the next day.

In a video series with the official clothing sponsor Beaumont & Bear last August, Ms Cowan discussed the future of farming and how close-knit the community is.

"Farm24 is showcasing to the general public, over a 24-hour period, what a farmer gets up to," she said.

"The Government should invest more time and money into farming because we need it. It's a bit of a dying industry otherwise."

"Going forward, I hope that the next generation of farmers will be recognised more by the public and the Government, especially to put British farming on the map and for people to be aware that we are here."

"Being part of the community is probably the most rewarding part for me. You have your neighbours, and you're all in tune with each other.

"You can talk, and if you're having problems, they'll all be there to help you."
