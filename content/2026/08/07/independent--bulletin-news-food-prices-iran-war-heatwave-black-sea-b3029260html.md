# Why global food prices have reached their highest level in over three years

- Global food prices reached their highest level in over three years in July, as reported by the UN Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO).
- The surge was attributed to a combination of adverse weather conditions and escalating conflicts in the Gulf and Black Sea regions.
- The FAO's chief economist warned of renewed food inflation, citing ongoing conflicts in Iran and Ukraine, alongside the El Niño weather phenomenon, as creating a "perfect storm" of increased costs and reduced crop yields.
- Wheat prices jumped by 5.8 per cent amid concerns over Black Sea export disruptions and heat damage to crops, with vegetable oil and sugar prices also seeing increases.
- The UK public had previously been warned of a 10 per cent rise in food costs this year due to the Iran conflict.
