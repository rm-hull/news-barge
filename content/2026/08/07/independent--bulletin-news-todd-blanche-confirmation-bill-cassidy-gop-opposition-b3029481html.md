# GOP senator says he’ll support Todd Blanche’s confirmation after Republican opposition

- Todd Blanche’s nomination for U.S. Attorney General has seen mixed support from Republican senators, with Lisa Murkowski and Susan Collins opposing his confirmation, making his approval dependent on a narrow 50-vote majority in the 99-member Senate.
- Sen. Murkowski cited a lack of confidence that Blanche would curb the administration's "worst impulses” and expressed concerns over the handling of Jeffrey Epstein files and a proposed $1.8 billion fund for January 6 protesters, which she believes was only halted due to the pending nomination.
- Sen. Collins stated her opposition stems from Blanche's actions that she believes eroded the Department of Justice's independence, specifically mentioning his support for the $1.8 billion fund and the DOJ's move to indict Senators Mark Kelly and Elissa Slotkin.
- Despite previous concerns about the Justice Department, Republican Sen. Bill Cassidy has now declared his support for Blanche, arguing he is the best available choice for the role under the current administration.
- Democratic Sen. John Fetterman confirmed he will vote against Blanche's confirmation, aligning with the expected opposition from all 45 Democratic senators, further tightening the confirmation vote.
