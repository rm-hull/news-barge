# UK set for fifth heatwave of the summer – here’s what to know

- More parts of England could face drought conditions next week as a fifth heatwave is anticipated, with temperatures potentially reaching 36C in some areas.
- The Environment Agency reported less than 10mm of rainfall for seven consecutive weeks, suggesting several regions may move into or towards drought.
- Around half of England and the entirety of Wales are already experiencing drought following their driest July on record.
- The UK Health Security Agency has issued heat health alerts across most of England, while a second consecutive summer drought is deemed "exceptionally serious."
- South West Water has implemented a hosepipe ban, restricting water use for nearly 26 million people across central and southern England and Wales.
