## New chocolate shop opens doors in York

The Savante store opened on August 5 *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A new chocolate gifting experience has arrived in York, promising craftsmanship and creativity in every bite.

Savante, a premium chocolate retailer specialising in "chocolate artistry," has opened its latest store at Designer Outlet York, offering visitors a fresh take on luxury gifting with chocolates designed to impress both visually and flavourfully.

Paul Tyler, centre manager at Designer Outlet York, said: "We're delighted to welcome Savante to Designer Outlet York.

"The brand brings something genuinely unique to our retail mix, combining creativity, craftsmanship and luxury gifting in a way that we know will resonate with our guests.

Savante says its concept is centred around elevating chocolate into an art form, with collections that surprise and delight through imaginative design and sophisticated taste.

The Form Series features everyday objects sculpted in chocolate with striking realism.

  Some of the products in the store 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

The Flavour Series showcases a selection of luxury truffles, pralines, dragées and filled bars, hand-finished in Monza, Italy by artisan chocolatiers.

These are presented in elegant keepsake boxes, offering a contemporary twist on traditional chocolate gifts.

All of Savante’s chocolates are made by small artisanal producers, with a focus on quality, originality and attention to detail.

Ed Starr of Savante said: "We are thrilled to bring the concept of 'Chocolate Artistry' to Designer Outlet York.

  Some of the range 

*(Image: Facebook)*

"At Savante, we believe that gifting should go beyond the ordinary to offer a truly intentional, memorable experience.

"Whether it’s a meticulously detailed chocolate sculpture from our Form Series designed to surprise and spark conversation, or a stunning box of hand-finished truffles and pralines from our Flavour Series crafted to elevate the everyday, our goal is to redefine what a gift can be.

  Some of the range 

*(Image: Facebook)*

"We want to help people move away from generic presents and give something exceptional, memorable, and crafted to absolute perfection."

The arrival of Savante adds to the outlet’s expanding selection of brands, enhancing its mix across fashion, lifestyle, food and gifting.

The new store is located near the south entrance of Designer Outlet York.
