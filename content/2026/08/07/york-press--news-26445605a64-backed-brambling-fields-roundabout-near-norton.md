TRAFFIC is building up on a main road in North Yorkshire due to temporary traffic lights.

Vehicles are said to be backed up to Brambling Fields, near Norton, eastbound due to the lights at Rillington.

The A64 is also closed at Barton Hill due to a serious accident.
