Police are appealing for witnesses after a teenager was assaulted during a fight in a cemetery.

It happened at **Tadcaster Cemetery** on**Leeds Road** on**Saturday 25 July** some time between**6pm and 7pm**.

North Yorkshire Police say it “involved an altercation between a group of teenagers and the 13-year-old victim”.

#### Tadcaster Cemetery

A force spokesperson said: “We’re particularly appealing for information from any dog walkers in the area at the time who may have witnessed the assault, or had any interaction with the group.”
