Search the Public Notice PortalA bar and events space in York could be created in a neighbouring building under a new licence application.

The Ragged Trousered Philanthropists Ltd wants to extend its Premises Licence at Angel on the Green, Bishopthorpe Road, to include No. 6 Bishopthorpe Road.

The premises would act as a bar and event space, offering art workshops, coffee mornings, talks, film screenings, and private hire for functions.

Customers will have the option of ordering drinks with or without food, with most expected to be seated at tables.

The existing terms of the licence will remain unchanged.
