# In temporary win for Trump, judge pauses order to hand over financial secrets to the BBC

![Pro-Trump supporters storm the U.S. Capitol following a rally with Trump on January 6, 2021 in Washington, DC](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/7/16/GettyImages-1230454306.jpeg)

- Donald Trump's legal team secured an emergency suspension of a judge's ruling that required him to disclose financial documents in his $10 billion defamation lawsuit against the BBC.
- The temporary halt allows Trump to amend his complaint, aiming to narrow his claim and limit the information he must disclose.
- Trump intends to focus his lawsuit solely on reputational injury, moving away from initial allegations of broader harm to his brand and businesses.
- The original court order mandated a "substantial production" of paperwork by August 6 from the Donald J Trump Revocable Trust, which holds his business interests.
- The BBC, which opposed the motion to stay, is seeking to have the case dismissed, citing concerns about a "chilling effect" on reporting and questioning the Florida court's jurisdiction.
