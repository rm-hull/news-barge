                                    Police are appealing for witnesses and information following a burglary at Harrogate Hospital.

It happened over a four day period between Friday 31st July and Monday 3rd August.

The theft is believed to have occurred during the times of 10pm and 2am, and involved electric cables being stolen.

A police spokesperson said:

“We believe that the suspects have removed cable from various parts of the hospital and may have used a vehicle to transport the stolen items.

“We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have seen anyone acting suspiciously in the hospital grounds or any suspicious vehicles in the area during this time.”


If you have any information that could assist the investigation, please email: PC 10 on [000010@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:000010@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

*Please quote reference 12260147509 when providing information.*
