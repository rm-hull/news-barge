Tom Holland calls Zendaya his wife publicly for first time

Hollywood couple Tom Holland and Zendaya reportedly hosted a secret, £500,000 wedding at a sprawling Surrey estate.

The event, held on Tuesday, 4 August, at the five-star Beaverbrook hotel, reportedly enforced a phone ban for guests, while a source told People the wedding had a “natural theme”.

A source told The Sun that the pair “went to great lengths to stop any information or pictures getting out”, adding: “So neither guests nor staff were allowed their mobiles.”

It follows Holland confirming the two had gotten hitched in an interview with Esquire back in June.

The couple, who met on the set of Spider-Man: Homecoming a decade ago, are known for their efforts to keep their relationship private.
