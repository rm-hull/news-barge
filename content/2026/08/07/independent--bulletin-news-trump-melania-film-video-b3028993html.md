# Trump boasts Melania film is ‘number one movie of the year’ but fails to back up claim

- Donald Trump claimed that *Melania: Twenty Days to History* is the 'number one movie of the year' during a speech in Las Vegas.
- He made this assertion without providing any evidence to support his claim.
- The film, released in January, focuses on the 20 days leading up to the US president's 2025 inauguration.
- *Melania: Twenty Days to History* earned $7m in its opening weekend.
- As of August 2026, the highest-grossing film of the year globally is*Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, which has made over $1bn.
