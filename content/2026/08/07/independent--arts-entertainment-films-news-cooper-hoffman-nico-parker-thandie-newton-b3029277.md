Cooper Hoffman, son of the late Philip Seymour Hoffman, has confirmed his relationship with fellow actor Nico Parker, daughter of Thandiwe Newton.

Both 23-year-old Hoffman and 21-year-old Parker are second-generation performers, with their acclaimed parents well-known in the industry.

The couple, who starred together in the 2024 film *Poetic License*, were previously photographed with their arms around each other at the Toronto International Film Festival last September.

Speaking to the Hollywood Reporter, Hoffman said: “We didn’t officially start dating until a few weeks after filming, when she came to New York to meet my mom (costume designer Mimi O’Donnell), and I asked her to be my girlfriend. It was very sweet.”

The couple met on the set of the film, which is the directorial debut of Maude Apatow, who is the daughter of director Judd Apatow and actress Leslie Mann.

Parker began her career with the 2019 film *Dumbo* and has since appeared in*Bridget Jones: Mad About The Boy* and*How To Train Your Dragon*.

Hoffman made his film debut in *Licorice Pizza* in 2021 and had a lead role in the 2025 adaptation of Stephen King’s*The Long Walk*.

The actor remembered his father in an interview with GQ, saying: “He’s my favorite actor, but he’s also my dad.”

“As much as I would love him to be here and talk to him about acting, I also would be terrified to have him see my stuff and judge my stuff.

“Not that he would judge it, because he was a very empathetic person, and he would probably — hopefully — hold my hand through all of it.”
