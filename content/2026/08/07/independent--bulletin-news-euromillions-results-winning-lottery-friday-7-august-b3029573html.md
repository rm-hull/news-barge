### National Lottery Set For Life results on Thursday 6 August

![National Lottery Set For Life results on Thursday 6 August]([https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/08/28/21/28214209-3a3ce6bc-ae70-4564-842d-ed07319a172e.jpg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/08/28/21/28214209-3a3ce6bc-ae70-4564-842d-ed07319a172e.jpg?quality=75&width=83&auto=webp) 83w)

Tonight’s EuroMillions draw has offered players the chance to win a jackpot of £94 million.

To win the prize, a player had to get five main numbers plus two lucky stars.

In the UK, every EuroMillions entry automatically includes a unique "UK Millionaire Maker" code, entering you into an additional draw with a guaranteed £1 million winner in every draw.

The winning EuroMillions numbers were **26, 29, 35, 38**,and** 47**. The Lucky Stars were** 1 **and** 2**. The Millionaire Maker code was TNXR 81195.

The winning Thunderball numbers were **4, 18, 28, 30** and**34**. The Thunderball was** 11**.
