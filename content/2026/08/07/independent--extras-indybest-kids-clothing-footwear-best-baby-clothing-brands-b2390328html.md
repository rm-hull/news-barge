As the parent of a lively four-year-old with another little one due to arrive soon, I’ve become far more selective about where I shop for children’s clothes than I ever imagined. I’ve learnt the hard way that the cutest outfit in the world isn’t much use if it shrinks after one wash, loses its shape after a trip to the park or gets rejected because my child suddenly decides it’s “too itchy” or “not dinosaur enough”.

The best kids’ clothing brands make garments that still look good enough to hand down to younger siblings after countless nursery days, muddy adventures and washes. I also love finding brands that understand children at every stage, from practical baby basics to fun prints and dress-up-worthy outfits that little ones genuinely want to wear.

The number of online retailers in 2026 means there’s more choice than ever, whether you’re after affordable essentials, sustainable staples, beautifully made independent labels or investment pieces for special occasions. Keep reading for my roundup of the best places to buy clothes for your babies and children.

## Best high street brands

### JoJo Maman Bébé

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/29/10/51/Jojo-Maman-Bebe.jpeg)

JoJo Maman Bébé remains a dependable destination for babies and younger children, with collections covering everything from practical basics to occasionwear. Alongside clothing you'll find shoes, swimwear, maternity pieces, toys and nursery essentials, making it particularly useful for growing families.

### Next

Whether you're shopping for newborn essentials, school-ready outfits or party clothes, Next has almost every category covered. Its extensive range includes shoes, coats, denim, sleepwear and licensed collections, alongside a growing selection of third-party brands available through its online platform.

### The Little White Company

Classic styling and premium fabrics define The Little White Company's childrenswear collection. Beautiful sleepsuits, pyjamas, knitwear and occasion pieces sit alongside toys, bedding and gifts, making it a popular choice for baby showers and first birthdays.

### H&M

Affordable, trend-led and easy to mix and match, H&M continues to be a favourite for stocking up on everyday essentials. Parents can browse everything from leggings and jeans to character clothing, outerwear, shoes and accessories, often at wallet-friendly prices.

### Boden

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/29/10/56/Boden.jpeg)

Known for colourful prints and exceptional quality, Boden creates childrenswear that's equal parts practical and fun. The range includes dresses, knitwear, coats, swimwear and everyday separates that are designed to stay looking good wash after wash.

### Mamas & Papas

While many parents first discover Mamas & Papas through pushchairs and nursery furniture, its baby clothing deserves attention too. Expect soft cotton sleepsuits, coordinated outfits, occasionwear and accessories that make lovely keepsakes or gifts for new arrivals.

### Mori

Mori has become synonymous with luxuriously soft baby essentials thanks to its signature fabric blends. Its range extends well beyond sleepsuits to include daywear, pyjamas, towels, blankets, toys and feeding accessories, making it especially useful for newborn shopping lists.

### M&S

M&S remains one of the best places to buy reliable everyday childrenswear. Multipacks, school essentials, adaptive clothing, occasionwear and trend-led seasonal pieces all sit alongside excellent-value basics that stand up well to regular wear and washing.

### Newbie

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/29/10/10/Newbie.jpeg)

Inspired by Scandinavian simplicity, Newbie offers timeless collections in soft colours and delicate prints. From newborn babygrows and knitted cardigans to dresses, outerwear and matching family outfits, it's a lovely option for parents drawn to classic styling.

### Lindex

Lindex combines practicality with fun, producing colourful childrenswear that covers everyday basics, denim, pyjamas, outerwear and accessories. Many collections also feature coordinated family pieces, making it easy to create subtle matching looks.

### Zara Kids

Perfect for fashion-conscious families, Zara Kids offers trend-driven collections that mirror adult styles in miniature. Alongside everyday clothing you'll find smart tailoring, occasionwear, footwear and accessories that feel contemporary without losing practicality.

### George at Asda

George continues to impress with affordable staples that punch above their price point. It's particularly good for multipacks, school essentials, pyjamas, coats and seasonal updates that won't stretch the family budget.

## Best independent brands

### Kite

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/29/10/52/Kite-Clothing.jpeg)

If your family loves colour and playful prints, Kite is a brilliant place to start. The brand specialises in organic cotton clothing for babies and children, with everything from everyday leggings and dresses to knitwear, raincoats and cosy layers. The fabrics are soft enough for sensitive skin but sturdy enough to survive endless washes, making them ideal for active little ones.

### Polarn O. Pyret

Swedish favourite Polarn O. Pyret has built a loyal following thanks to its famously durable designs. The collections are packed with practical outerwear, striped staples, organic cotton basics and adventure-ready pieces that are designed to be handed down between siblings. It's a great choice if longevity is high on your priority list.

### Dotty Dungarees

As the name suggests, Dotty Dungarees is the destination for brilliantly designed dungarees, but there's plenty more besides this. Expect soft jersey tops, dresses, shorts and matching accessories in cheerful colours and timeless prints. Clever adjustable details mean many pieces can be worn for longer as your child grows.

### Claude & Co

Known for its effortlessly cool aesthetic, Claude & Co offers modern, gender-neutral clothing in muted tones and contemporary prints. Think oversized sweatshirts, relaxed co-ords, organic basics and stylish accessories that work just as well for everyday play as family outings.

### Sleepy Doe

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/29/10/44/Sleepy-Doe.jpeg)

Bedtime gets an upgrade with Sleepy Doe's beautifully made sleepwear. The collection includes organic cotton pyjamas, sleepsuits and matching family sets featuring hand-drawn prints and calming colour palettes. It's also a lovely place to shop for thoughtful baby gifts.

### Mutha.hood

Famous for empowering slogans and bold graphics, Mutha.hood's children's collection brings the same positive energy to little wardrobes. You'll find organic cotton T-shirts, sweatshirts and babygrows with playful designs that make an easy statement without sacrificing comfort.

### Toby Tiger

Bright colours, appliqué details and imaginative prints have long been Toby Tiger's signature. From rainbow leggings and dinosaur tops to cosy pyjamas and practical everyday outfits, the brand creates fun clothing that children genuinely enjoy wearing while parents appreciate the organic fabrics and durable construction.

### Mini Rodini

Mini Rodini continues to stand out with quirky illustrations and premium-quality fabrics. The Swedish label mixes fashion-forward design with practical childrenswear, offering everything from outerwear and knitwear to swimwear and accessories that are built for adventures and made with sustainability in mind.

### Frugi

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/29/10/27/Frugi.jpeg)

Bursting with cheerful colours and playful patterns, Frugi remains a favourite for babies and young children. The range includes everyday essentials, waterproofs, pyjamas, dresses and reversible pieces, with thoughtful details like reinforced knees and comfortable waistbands making them especially practical for busy families.

### Organic Zoo

Minimalist yet instantly recognisable, Organic Zoo specialises in beautifully soft baby and toddler essentials in understated colours and timeless prints. Its collections feature bodysuits, leggings, rompers, knitwear and accessories that are ideal for creating a capsule wardrobe you'll happily reuse for future siblings.

### Konges Sløjd

This Danish label blends Scandinavian style with whimsical details, producing elegant collections for babies and children. Alongside clothing you'll find swimwear, outerwear, shoes, toys and nursery accessories, making it a one-stop shop for parents who love coordinated, understated design.

### Bobo Choses

One of the most creative children's brands around, Bobo Choses is loved for its artistic prints and bold colour combinations. Its collections span clothing, knitwear, swimwear and accessories, offering distinctive pieces that stand out from typical high street styles.

### Little Green Radicals

Parents looking for ethically made everyday essentials should keep Little Green Radicals on their radar. The brand focuses on Fairtrade and organic cotton clothing, producing everything from baby sleepsuits and dresses to waterproofs and pyjamas in vibrant, child-friendly prints.

## Best luxury brands

### Binibamba

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/29/10/16/Binibamba.jpeg)

Binibamba has expanded far beyond its iconic sheepskin buggy accessories into a beautifully curated collection of clothing and cold-weather essentials. Think cosy booties, snowsuits, mittens and premium layering pieces, all crafted from exceptionally soft natural materials with the brand's signature focus on quality and comfort.

The brand is also known for its thoughtful collaborations, teaming up with like-minded names to create special collections. Past partnerships have included Bobo Choses, while one of the most recent – and one of my personal favourites – is its collaboration with Tommee Tippee. The collection includes beautifully designed baby essentials, including dummies (£9.99, Tommeetippee.com), bibs (£7.99, Tommeetippee.com) and glass bottles (£19.99, Tommeetippee.com), combining Binibamba's signature aesthetic with Tommee Tippee's trusted expertise. My favourite piece, though, has to be the snuggler (£120, Tommeetippee.com) – a super-soft comforter that's as stylish as it is soothing for little ones.

### Rachel Riley

Rachel Riley specialises in timeless childrenswear with a distinctly traditional feel. Smocked dresses, embroidered rompers, tailored separates and occasion outfits make it a popular destination for weddings, christenings and family celebrations.

### Trotters

Combining British heritage styling with practical everyday wear, Trotters offers premium clothing, shoes and accessories for babies and older children alike. The collections include everything from Liberty-print dresses to smart coats and polished footwear, all with an emphasis on quality craftsmanship.
