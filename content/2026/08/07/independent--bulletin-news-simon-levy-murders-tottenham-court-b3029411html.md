Simon Levy, a 40-year-old serial sex offender from Tottenham, north London, has been found guilty of murdering two women and raping a third.

He was convicted at the Old Bailey of murdering Carmenza Valencia-Trujillo, 53, in March last year, and Sheryl Wilkins, 39, five months later in August.

Levy was also found guilty of two counts of rape, assault occasioning grievous bodily harm with intent, and non-fatal suffocation over a brutal sex attack in January 2025, which the victim survived.

The prosecutor, Tom Little KC, told jurors the crimes were a series of attacks committed by a 'predatory serial sexual attacker in a similar way'.

Levy was already a registered sex offender, having been jailed in 2021 for sexually assaulting two women in 2018, and was recently convicted of 11 further counts of sexual assault in a separate trial.
