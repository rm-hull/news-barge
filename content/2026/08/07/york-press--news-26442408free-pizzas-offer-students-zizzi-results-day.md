A popular pizza chain is giving away free pizzas to students on results day.

Zizzi, which has restaurants in York and Harrogate, is giving away more than 3,700 free classic pizzas to students on results day.

The first 30 through the door will be able to claim the offer by showing their 2026 exam results and purchasing a soft drink.

Corinne Prior, brand and customer experience director at Zizzi, said: "Results Day is such a big milestone and whatever the outcome, it's a moment worth celebrating.

"After the excitement of National Lasagne Day, we wanted to keep the celebrations going by treating students to one of our Classic Pizzas."

The offer is available on A Level results day, Thursday, August 13, 2026, and GCSE results day, Thursday, August 20, 2026.

Students receiving their Leaving Certificate in the Republic of Ireland can claim the offer on Friday, August 21, 2026.

Pizza options include Classic Margherita, Classic Pepperoni Campagna, Classic Pollo & Pesto Fresca, Classic Pinoli, and Vegan Margherita, with non-gluten alternatives also available.

If the free pizzas run out, students can still enjoy 50 per cent off a Classic Pizza until 5pm when they purchase a soft drink.

Guests can book a table online at www.zizzi.co.uk/occasions/results-day.
