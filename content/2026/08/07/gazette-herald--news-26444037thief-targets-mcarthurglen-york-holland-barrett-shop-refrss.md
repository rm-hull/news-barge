A THIEF has targeted a health and wellness shop in a major York retail complex.

North Yorkshire Police said vitamins were stolen from Holland and Barrett in McArthurGlen York Designer Outlet on Tuesday, June 30.

  


The force issued a CCTV image of man it would like to speak to, saying he may have information that will assist its officers' investigation.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "Please contact us if you recognise that male pictured on CCTV.

"You can email PC1488 Bell at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

"Online, you can visit our website, select 'Tell us about', then, choose 'An existing case or report', or live chat via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

"If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

"Please quote reference 12260127984 when providing information."
