A leading accountancy firm has strengthened its team with a new appointment.

Clive Owen, which has an office in York, has welcomed Nathan Allan as consulting manager.

His arrival is part of the firm's strategy to expand its consulting services in response to growing demand for financial, operational, and strategic advice.

Mr Allan said: "I am delighted to be joining Clive Owen at such an exciting time for our Consulting offering.

"The firm has built an excellent reputation for delivering trusted advice, and it is clear there is significant ambition to grow the consulting offering even further."

Clive Owen’s Consulting team supports businesses with finance system and ERP implementations, outsourced Finance Director and board-level support, business process transformation and enhanced reporting and financial insight.

Brad Haywood, Consulting Associate Partner at Clive Owen, said: "Nathan's appointment reflects our ambition to continue evolving the way we support our clients."
