# Michael Portillo brings live shows to Yorkshire – here's how to see him in Doncaster, Harrogate and York

![Michael Portillo is heading to Yorkshire](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/07/22/59/Michael-Portillo-is-heading-to-Yorkshire.jpeg?trim=0,105,0,104&crop=&width=640&quality=70&enable=upscale)



## Former Cabinet minister turned TV traveller Michael Portillo is heading to Yorkshire as part of a major UK tour – with shows in Doncaster, Harrogate and York.

Michael Portillo is swapping the world’s great railway journeys for the stage as he brings his stories of politics, television and travel to Yorkshire.

The former Secretary of State and much-loved TV broadcaster will bring his *A Life of Two Halves* 2026 Tour to**The Dome, Doncaster, on Friday, October 9, 2026**, followed by** Harrogate Convention Centre on Wednesday, November 4, 2026**.

He returns to Yorkshire in the spring when **The Michael Portillo Show** arrives at the**Grand Opera House, York, on Sunday, March 7, 2027**.

Tickets are priced from **£34.99 to £67.99**, with the show lasting around one hour 50 minutes, including a 20-minute interval. It has an age recommendation of 14+.

The tour travels across England and Scotland, with stops including Liverpool, Edinburgh, Birmingham, Nottingham, Oxford, Portsmouth, Exeter, Salford and London as well as the three Yorkshire appearances.

Billed as *A Life of Two Halves* for the 2026 dates, the show promises funny, insightful and poignant stories from Portillo’s remarkably varied career.

The son of a Spanish refugee, Portillo rose to become one of Britain’s best-known politicians, serving in Margaret Thatcher’s government before famously losing his Enfield Southgate seat at the 1997 General Election.

His career subsequently took a very different track.

He became a familiar television face, including appearances alongside Diane Abbott and Andrew Neil, before winning a new legion of fans travelling by rail across Britain and around the world.

The live show will see Portillo reflect on his father’s experiences during the Spanish Civil War, his own political career and his memories of Margaret Thatcher.

He will also reveal stories from his move into television and take audiences behind the scenes of his railway adventures across countries including Spain, the United States and India.

Whether your interest is politics, history, travel or those famous colourful jackets, the show promises to cover both halves of an extraordinary public life.
