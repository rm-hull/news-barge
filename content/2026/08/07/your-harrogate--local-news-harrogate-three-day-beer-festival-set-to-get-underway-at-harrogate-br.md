                                    A three-day beer festival featuring guest breweries, live music and street food is set to get underway in Harrogate today (Friday 7th August).

Harrogate Brewing Co. will host the third Hookstone Hooley at its Hookstone Chase taproom until Sunday (9th August).

Three guest breweries – Brass Castle Brewery, Three Brothers Brewing Co and Elvington Brewery – will each have their own pop-up bar during the festival.

The event gets underway from 5pm today, with food from Loaf n Loaded and Casa Lasagna before live music from five-piece band The Reply at 7.30pm.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a759b21912c5e4ecb6.jpg)


Saturday will be split into afternoon and evening sessions, with Jay's Pizza Project and Blake's Bites serving food.

Singer-songwriter Sam Crane will perform at 2.30pm, while Undergroove UK will take to the stage at 7.30pm. DJ Phil will also perform during both sessions.

The festival concludes with a Family Day from 2pm until 8pm on Sunday, featuring games, face painting and food alongside a performance from acoustic duo Ally & Joe at 4.30pm.

Adult ticket holders will receive a festival beer glass to use during their session and take home afterwards.

*Full weekend and individual session tickets are available, with children permitted at the Saturday daytime and Sunday sessions. Under-fives can attend for free. Find out more  here.*
