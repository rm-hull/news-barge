A NORTH Yorkshire independent cinema is set to star on the big screen itself after it was transformed into a 'nightclub' for the evening.

The Palace cinema in Malton became Roxies on Wednesday evening while the filming took place.

A spokesperson for The Palace said: "We were approached by a location scout for a production filming in the area.

"They were looking for a nightclub entrance and thought our doorway and stairs were ideal.

"The art department transformed the front sensitively and quickly, filmed on Wednesday night and then put it back to normal the next day

"It was an exciting experience for the staff and it’s amazing to think we might be able to show a film on our screens that we feature in."
