                                    Harrogate's Health and Healing Festival is set to return next month for a weekend dedicated to holistic living and wellbeing.

The event will take place at The Pavilions at the Great Yorkshire Showground on Saturday 12th and Sunday 13th September, running from 10am until 5pm on both days.

The festival brings together therapists, practitioners, mediums and retailers, with visitors able to explore a range of treatments and products connected to health, healing and wellbeing.

There will also be a programme of free talks, workshops and demonstrations covering topics linked to the mind, body and spirit.

Organisers said last year’s Harrogate festival was a “fabulous weekend”, with 2026 marking the event’s fourth year at the venue.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a75b6a6030e3044e95.jpg)


Visitors will be able to try treatments, browse products, attend demonstrations and find out more about different approaches to wellbeing.

Tickets cost £6 for adults or £5 for concessions, which includes people aged 67 and over, students and NHS staff with a valid card.

Weekend passes are priced at £11 for adults and £9 for concessions, while accompanied children under 15 can attend for free.

*Tickets can be purchased in advance  here or visitors can pay on the door.*
