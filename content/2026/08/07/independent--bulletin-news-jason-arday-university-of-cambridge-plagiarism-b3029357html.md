Cambridge University confirmed it will continue its investigation into the circumstances surrounding Professor Arday's appointment and tenure, despite his immediate resignation.

The university stated that the findings from its ongoing investigation will feed into a review of the process for appointing senior academic roles and its research misconduct policy.

Senior academics at Cambridge are reportedly calling for an inquiry into the university's appointment processes, while Liverpool John Moores University had previously cleared Professor Arday of wrongdoing.

Academics have voiced concerns that this case could be exploited by those critical of diversity and equality initiatives, potentially discouraging other black scholars from entering higher education.
