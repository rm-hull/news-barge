There are delays on a stretch of the M6 in the West Midlands after a lorry caught fire, National Highways has said.

The incident is affecting the motorway northbound between junctions four and six in Birmingham.

Three out of four lanes are closed, causing 90-minute delays in the area at its peak.

National Highways West Midlands said in their latest post online: “Lanes 1 & 2 (of 4) remain closed on the #M6 north between J4A #M42 junction & J5 #SuttonColdfield following a fire resulting in damage to the road. Lane 3 is now open.”

West Midlands Fire Service continue to work on scene, they added.

There were no casualties or injuries.

Motorists are advised to follow National Highways West Midlands for traffic updates.
