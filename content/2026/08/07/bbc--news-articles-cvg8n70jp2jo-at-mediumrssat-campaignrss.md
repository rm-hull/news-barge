**NHS Tayside is investigating an alleged data breach into the medical records of a  nine-year-old girl at the centre of a murder probe in Arbroath.**

Minnie Merriman, from West Yorkshire, died at Ninewells Hospital in Dundee after being found seriously injured at an industrial estate in the town.

A 35-year-old man, who was known to the girl, has appeared in court charged with her murder.

NHS Tayside said it was "investigating the circumstances" of the alleged breach that happened in a clinical area where staff access patient information.

A spokesperson added : "As a matter of governance, any data protection breach would be recorded and investigated by NHS Tayside and, where appropriate, reported to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

"It would not be appropriate for us to comment further on individual staffing matters."

Officers had been called to the Elliot Industrial Estate in Arbroath shortly after midnight on Monday.

Police said they were not looking for anyone else in connection with Minnie's death and the other members of the family - who were camping in the area - are being supported by specialist officers.

Christopher Rice from Wakefield, West Yorkshire, made no plea during an appearance in private at Forfar Sheriff Court on Wednesday and was remanded in custody.

In a statement Minnie's family said: "We are devastated with the loss of our beloved, absolutely incredible, beautiful and brave Minnie Moo.

"Our family asks that our privacy is respected at this extremely difficult time."
