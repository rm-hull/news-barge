A THIEF has targeted a shop in a popular North Yorkshire venue and made off with a pair of designer sunglasses.

North Yorkshire Police said the theft happened on Wednesday, July 1 at The Motorist in New Lennerton Lane, Sherburn-in-Elmet.

  



The force released a CCTV image of a man it said it wants to speak, saying he may have information which could help its investigation.

In an appeal to the public, a force spokesperson said: "Please contact us if you recognise the male pictured.

"Email PC1488 Bell via [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk) or visit the North Yorkshire Police website and select ‘Tell us about’ before choosing ‘An existing case or report’.

"Please quote reference 12260137413 when providing information."
