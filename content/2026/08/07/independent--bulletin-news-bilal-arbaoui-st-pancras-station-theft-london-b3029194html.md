CCTV footage of thief stealing luggage worth £175,000 outside St Pancras Station

Bilal Arbaoui, 32, was sentenced to one year and 10 months in jail for stealing a gold suitcase valued at £175,000 from a woman outside St Pancras Station in London.

The theft occurred on Saturday, April 11, when Arbaoui distracted the woman, who had just arrived from Paris on the Eurostar, by claiming her trousers were dirty and offering a tissue.

The stolen suitcase contained high-value items including designer bags, watches, and jewelry, causing significant distress to the victim.

British Transport Police used extensive CCTV footage to identify and track down Arbaoui, who initially denied involvement but later pleaded guilty to theft.

Following his sentencing at Inner London Crown Court, Arbaoui, from north-west London, was also ordered to pay a £187 surcharge.
