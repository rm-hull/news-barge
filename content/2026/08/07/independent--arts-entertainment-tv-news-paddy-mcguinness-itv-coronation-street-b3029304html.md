Paddy McGuinness is best known for his stand-up career, hosting dating show *Take Me Out* and presenting on BBC Radio 2 – but the comedian has reminded fans of one of his lesser-mentioned jobs: a stint on the*Coronation Street* cobbles. 

The 52-year-old has this week been hosting *This Morning* alongside Josie Gibson and on Wednesday (5 August), the pair were joined by soap expert Sharon Marshall for a peek at what’s coming up in*Corrie*,* Emmerdale*and* EastEnders*. 

After a montage of clips, McGuinness said he recognised *Coronation Street*’s Sally Metcalfe, adding: "Yeah, that was my era of* Corrie*."

"It was – when you were in there as a survivalist,” Marshall responded, which prompted a confused reaction from Gibson and an explanation from the comedian.

"I was in *Corrie*,” he said. “I did a week in* Corrie*. I played a survivalist called Dougie and I just did a week in the woods.”

![McGuinness in ‘Corrie’](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/07/10/20/0_Screenshot-2026-08-05-at-132111-copy-5e6caad1.jpeg)

McGuinness appeared as Dougie Ryan in six episodes back in 2015. The character was introduced when a group of series regulars led by Tyrone Dobbs (Alan Halsall) ventured out of Weatherfield for a camping trip.

The eccentric Dougie made quite the impression on his new pals, helping them put up their tent, before barbecuing a skinned rabbit and leading a foraging expedition.

McGuinness is far from the only unexpected face to pop up in the soap. In 2019, Lorraine Kelly hilariously played herself – in a storyline that saw Gail Platt (Helen Worth) accidentally shoot with a crossbow – while Sir Ian McKellen stopped by the Rovers Return back in 2005, making a handful of appearances as a novelist, Mel Hutchwright.

McGuinness left fans stunned earlier this year when he revealed a new physique after embarking on a weight loss journey – with some even accusing him of using artificial intelligence (AI) to alter his before photo.

He told *Men’s Health UK*: “A few people went, ‘Clearly you’ve AI’d the first picture’.

“‘F***ing hell, you’re supposed to AI the after picture, not the before!’ I thought, ‘Jesus, that’s how bad I looked’.”

McGuiness had taken on the 75 Hard challenge, which involves a gruelling daily regime with two 45-minute workouts, consuming 4.5 litres of water, following a chosen diet, abstaining from alcohol and reading 10 pages of a non-fiction book.

The *Phoenix Nights* star decided to overindulge before starting the challenge, explaining: “I thought, ‘I’m going to have a real blowout, hit rock bottom’.

“I don’t recommend it. Just for me personally, I thought, ‘I’ll see if I can get myself out of it’.”
