York Vegan Christmas Festival is set to bring a taste of plant-based living to the city.

The event, which is being run by Vegan Events UK, will take place at the York RI Sports Centre on Sunday, December 13.

It promises to offer a "one-stop destination" for visitors to explore a variety of vegan products, support small family-run businesses, and sample a range of food.

Vegan Events UK has over a decade of experience working with traders across the country.

The festivals aim to showcase ethical and sustainable vegan products, ranging from food and cakes to fashion.

The York Vegan Christmas Festival is one of several events planned in Yorkshire, including the Wakefield Vegan Festival this autumn.

Businesses interested in participating can book a stall at www.veganeventsuk.co.uk/trade.

The organisers are keen to welcome a diverse range of stallholders, whether they are new or established.

Sponsorship opportunities are also available, starting at £80 per event.

Sponsors will receive three posts on Vegan Events UK’s Facebook page and three posts on the Facebook event.

They will also receive three posts on Instagram, along with a logo and description on the Vegan Events website.

There are also sampling opportunities, where businesses can provide samples, discounts, or special offers for the festival’s VIP goody bags.

The festival will feature speakers, demonstrations, and workshops.

These sessions offer a platform for experts in vegan cooking, nutrition, sustainability, and wellness to share their knowledge.

There is no charge for the sessions, but all content must be vegan.

Those interested in hosting a talk, demonstration, or workshop can apply via the Vegan Events UK website.

The festival offers a chance for businesses and organisations to join a community of like-minded individuals and be part of a marketplace that makes a difference.

For more information, visit the Vegan Events UK website.
