Tesco customers could bag themselves £50 worth of Clubcard points by taking part in a new shopping challenge.

The Clubcard Challenge launched on August 3 and runs until September 13, giving shoppers the chance to earn up to 5,000 bonus Clubcard points by completing a series of shopping tasks.

Clubcard holders can take part by selecting 10 personalised challenges through the Tesco Grocery & Clubcard app, either in store or online.

If all 10 are completed, shoppers will earn the full 5,000 points, equivalent to £50 to spend in store.

Points earned through the scheme can be converted into Tesco vouchers or doubled in value when redeemed with one of the supermarket’s 100-plus reward partners, including Cineworld, Hotels.com, Disney+ and RAC.

Not all Clubcard members are eligible; selected shoppers will be notified via the Tesco Grocery & Clubcard app.

In addition to the points challenge, customers also have the chance to win prizes, including an all-inclusive four-star holiday to the Mediterranean.

To enter the prize draw, shoppers must spend at least £25 per transaction in store or online between August 3 and September 1.

Each qualifying purchase counts as a separate entry, increasing the chances of winning.

There are 40 all-inclusive holidays up for grabs and the winner will also receive £500 cash and free travel insurance for the trip.
