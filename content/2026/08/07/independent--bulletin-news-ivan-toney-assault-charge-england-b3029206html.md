England's Ivan Toney has been charged with assault (PA)

England footballer Ivan Toney has been charged with assault causing actual bodily harm (ABH) by the Metropolitan Police.

The charge, issued on Thursday 31 July, relates to an incident at a nightclub on Wardour Street in central London on 6 December last year.

Toney, who recently transferred from Brentford to Saudi Pro League club Al-Ahli, is scheduled to appear at Westminster Magistrates’ Court on Thursday 24 September.

The Sun newspaper previously reported Toney's arrest on 8 December following an alleged headbutt.

A spokesperson for Toney stated that he is shocked by the charge but looks forward to clearing his name in court.
