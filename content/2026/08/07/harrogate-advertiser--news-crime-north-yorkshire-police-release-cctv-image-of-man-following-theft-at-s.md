# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image of man following theft at supermarket in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police has released a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to following a theft at a supermarket in Harrogate.

The incident happened on June 25 and involved a theft at Sainsbury’s on Cold Bath Road.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Please contact us if you recognise the male pictured in the CCTV image as he may have information that will assist our investigation.”
