A road in York is closed after a crash tonight (Friday).

Holgate Road is shut both ways after a collision.

It’s taped off near to the Crystal Palace pub and the junction with Cambridge Street.

#### Crash location

Police and ambulance crews are at the scene.

It appears that an Uber and a silver grey car have collided. Both cars have sustained significant damage to the front.

We have no word on any casualties as yet.
