Both Hoffman, 23, and Parker, 21, are second-generation actors, following in their acclaimed parents' footsteps.

The couple met on the set of the 2024 film Poetic License, which is the directorial debut of Maude Apatow.

Hoffman revealed to the Hollywood Reporter that they officially started dating a few weeks after filming, when Parker visited New York to meet his mother, Mimi O'Donnell.

Parker's career began with the 2019 film Dumbo, while Hoffman made his debut in Licorice Pizza in 2021.
