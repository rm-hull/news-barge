**York Station Gateway £34m overspend sparks criticism**

IT never ceases to amaze me when I read about various construction projects going over budget.

The York Station Gateway scheme is, we are told, £34 million in the red.

Some water companies have debts in the millions and HS2 has an overspend of billions of pounds.

How does this happen and keep happening?

If I spend beyond my means, I get a scathing letter from the bank and I sort it out PDQ.

I now take the view that whenever costing for a new project is announced, the thing to do is multiply the amount by five and add on a few million to be on the safe side.

And it probably still won't be enough.

  *Nigel Cummings,*

  *Charlton Street,*

  *York*
