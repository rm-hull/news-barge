Rodri looks set to leave Manchester City amid interest from Barcelona and Real Madrid. The World Cup winning captain is into the final year of his contract and favours a move to Barca who are in negotiations over a €45m fee.

Arsenal were dealt a blow in one of their summer transfer pursuits as Vinicius Jr has signed a contract extension with Real Madrid. The Gunners have moved on from targeting the winger and have reignited their interest in Atletico Madrid’s Julian Alvarez while PSG’s Bradley Barcola is also on their list.

In more positive news, Bruno Guimaraes has completed a medical at Arsenal ahead of his £75m move from Newcastle United. The 28-year-old midfielder is now expected to be announced as a new Gooner on Friday ahead of Arsenal’s pre-season fixtures this weekend. The deal represents a key piece of business of Mikel Arteta ahead of Arsenal’s title defence.

Liverpool, meanwhile, also want to sign Barcola from Paris Saint-Germain and have also lined up his teammate Ibrahim Mbaye in what could be a double swoop for Andoni Iraola’s side. The European champions may be willing to let them go especially following Maghnes Akliouche signing from Monaco.

Elsewhere, Manchester United have added to their ranks by bringing in a teenage midfielder and they are exploring their options at left-back with Lewis Hall reportedly now set on a move to Old Trafford, but Newcastle are determined to stop the exodus and insist he is not for sale.

## Man Utd 'learn Lewis Hall decision'

Newcastle are determined to keep Lewis Hall this summer, even if Manchester United are interested in a £60m transfer.

The 21-year-old Hall has emerged as Manchester United’s No 1 target for left back but Newcastle do not want to sell another first-team player this summer, with Bruno Guimaraes set to follow Sandro Tonali and Anthony Gordon out of the club.

That’s according to*The Athletic*, who report that Newcastle are insisting that Hall is not for sale.

## What next for Arsenal in the market?

Mikel Arteta called for Arsenal to be “ambitious” in the transfer window and agreeing a fee for Bruno Guimaraes certainly ticks that box.

The fee of £75m is big, but the Gunners have done well to come away from Newcastle having spent much less than Manchester City did in signing Elliot Anderson (£116m), or indeed Tottenham Hotspur for signing Tonali (£100m). Of course, Guimaraes is slightly older in profile.

After missing out on the signing of Morgan Rogers to Chelsea, Arsenal still want to sign an attacker who can instantly improve the first team this summer and a defender to act as cover for the injured William Saliba.

Arsenal’s blockbuster pursuit of Real Madrid superstar Vinicius Jr is over so they need to go back to the drawing board.

Names being batted around this morning are Julian Alvarez, Nico Williams and Bradley Barcola.

## Liverpool eye Ibrahim Mbaye but still want Bradley Barcola

Liverpool remain interested in signing Bradley Barcola this summer as the Reds bid to talk Paris Saint-Germain down from their £140m asking price.

But Liverpool could have received a boost by the news that PSG are set to sign Monaco’s France international winger Maghnes Akliouche in a £42.8m deal.

Could PSG already be lining up Barcola’s replacement in the 24-year-old Akliouche?

Meanwhile, Liverpool’s links to Ibrahim Mbaye appeared to suggest a shift away from key target Barcola.

The two players could cost in excess of £200m combined, with Andoni Iraola looking to upgrade the right wing area after Mohamed Salah’s exit.

## Manchester United make surprise midfield signing

Moving on from Arsenal, Manchester United have made a third midfield signing of the summer and this is a surprising one.

Needing to replace Casemiro, United have already brought in Andrey Santos and Youri Tielemans but now they’ve added 18-year-old Cristian Orozco from Fortaleza.

It’s a signing for the future, for sure, with the Colombia U17 international having only played five times in the top-flight last season.

The fee paid was rumoured to be around £850,000 and Orozco will go into the academy for the time being.

## Would Williams work for Arsenal?

We’re back with The Sun who don’t have any details but are instead speculating whether Arsenal could turn their attention to Nico Williams.

The Gunners have shown interest in the Spanish winger before and he is thought to have a release clause of around £77m in his contract.

That seems more in line with Arsenal’s spending habits which makes him a realistic alternative after the Gunners missed out on Vinicius Jr.

## Barcola on Arsenal's radar

More alternatives are creeping out for Arsenal now that their pursuit of Vinicius Jr is over.

The Sun believe that Maghnes Akliouche’s arrival at PSG, on a €50m move, means the French champions will be more willing to sell players.

That could have a knock on effect for the future of Bradley Barcola.

Liverpool are said to be in pole position to sign the winger but Arsenal have previously been interested in his services though they remain a way off in terms of valuations.

PSG rate him at around £130m and Arsenal are nowhere as high in their own valuations.
