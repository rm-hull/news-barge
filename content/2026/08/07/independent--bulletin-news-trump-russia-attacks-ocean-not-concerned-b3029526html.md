# Trump shares the surprising geographical reason Russia doesn’t worry him

- Donald Trump dismissed a question regarding Russia's increased attacks on civilian targets in Ukraine.
- He stated that the United States is “separated by an ocean” from the conflict.
- Trump expressed concern over the reported 25,000 young people being killed monthly in the Russia-Ukraine conflict.
- He indicated that the U.S. is “working on” the situation and believes “progress” is being made.
- New U.S. intelligence assessments have reportedly warned that Russian president Vladimir Putin could test Nato’s unity with a limited attack on an alliance member within the next few years.
