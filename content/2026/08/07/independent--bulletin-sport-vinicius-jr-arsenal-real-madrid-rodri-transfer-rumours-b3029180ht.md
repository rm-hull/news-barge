# Five transfer stories to look out for today after Arsenal miss out on Vinicius Jr

![Vinicius Jr is staying at Real Madrid (Bradley Collyer/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/04/24/15/c6eda9eb6900b99aac5cc31a569f9f32Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzc3MTI2NzE1-2.83836236.jpg)

- Manchester City's captain Rodri is expected to leave and is favouring a move to Barcelona over Real Madrid, with negotiations underway for a fee reportedly exceeding €45m.
- Arsenal's pursuit of Vinicius Jr ended as he signed a contract extension with Real Madrid, leading the Gunners to reignite interest in Atletico Madrid's Julian Alvarez and consider PSG's Bradley Barcola as alternative attacking targets.
- Liverpool are reportedly keen on a double swoop for Paris Saint-Germain's Bradley Barcola and Ibrahim Mbaye, with PSG's recent signing of Maghnes Akliouche potentially paving the way for Barcola's departure.
- Arsenal have agreed a £75m deal to sign Bruno Guimaraes from Newcastle United, with the midfielder completing his medical ahead of an imminent announcement as a key signing for their title defence.
- Manchester United have bolstered their midfield by signing 18-year-old Cristian Orozco from Fortaleza and are exploring left-back options, with Lewis Hall linked despite Newcastle's reluctance to sell.
