Visitors to Millennium Square can explore the wonders of the universe this summer when a state-of-the-art mobile planetarium lands in Leeds.

Planetarium Go UK arrives on the square next month, promising immersive astronomy and science-based experiences which use the latest technology to capture the stunning far reaches of space.

Employing high-resolution digital projections all housed within a portable dome structure, the attraction will screen a programme of films for visitors of all ages.

From September 4-26, 2026, Planetarium Go UK will host the screenings every day between 11am and 6pm Sunday to Friday and 10am to 8pm on Saturdays.

Featuring 3-2-1 Lift Off (recommended 6+), From Earth to Universe (recommended 10+), Beyond the Sun (recommended 5+) and Voyager: The Never-Ending Story (recommended 5+), the programme aims to offer an accessible way for families, school groups and community audiences to engage with science and space exploration.



José Jiménez, CEO of Planetarium Go UK said:Our objective is clear: to bring science to places where it has not reached before.

We want children, young people and families to enjoy an immersive experience that sparks scientific curiosity and brings the universe closer to everyday life, regardless of where they live.



The planetarium is part of the programme of summer events which have taken place on Millennium Square this year, including another hugely successful Summer Series of concerts and performances which attracted audiences of thousands.


![Stars to shine on Millennium Square for out-of-this-world attraction](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/leeds-mill-space.webp)




Councillor Salma Arif, Leeds City Council’s executive member for economy, said:Millennium Square continues to host an exciting and diverse programme of events and activities at the heart of the city centre each and every year and this summer has been no exception.

Giving families the opportunity to explore the fascinating wonders of space and astronomy will be an incredible experience and will hopefully help to inspire the next generation of young scientists in Leeds.



Tickets for Planetarium Go UK are priced from £8 and full programme information are now available at www.millsqleeds.com, by phone on 0113 376 0318 (Mon–Sat, 10am–4pm), or in person at the Carriageworks Theatre (open two hours before any performances at the Carriageworks Theatre).
