                                    Knaresborough Town AFC has announced the biggest sponsorship agreement in the club’s history.

Ashleigh Signs has become the club’s largest-ever sponsor, with the company’s logo set to feature on the front of the First Team shirts throughout the 2026/27 season.

Club Co-Managing Directors Neil Reaney and Rob Northfield welcomed Ashleigh Signs owner Tom Sadler to The Myrings Stadium to mark the new partnership.

Tom said:

“We're delighted to support the club and Neil and Rob's vision.

"Their passion, respect and hard work mirror the values of Ashleigh Signs.”


The agreement comes as Knaresborough Town continues to build commercial partnerships with businesses in the local area.

Neil and Rob are now inviting other local businesses to get involved with the club and become part of its plans for the future.
