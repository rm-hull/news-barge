Hotel Victoria in Robin Hood's Bay launches afternoon tea – with a twist

A NORTH Yorkshire hotel is serving up a coastal twist on afternoon tea – offering a chance to enjoy this very British tradition while taking in one of the best coastal views around.

The Bay Explorer Afternoon Tea is a limited-edition experience available at Hotel Victoria in Robin Hood’s Bay as part of Afternoon Tea week, which runs from August 10 to 16.

The Bay Explorer Afternoon Tea includes a leisurely journey aboard a charming Monaco Golf Buggy, transporting guests from the hotel into the heart of the historic fishing village.

From there, visitors have an hour to explore Robin Hood’s Bay, with its narrow, cobbled streets, maritime heritage and independent shops.

On returning to the hotel, guests can enjoy a traditional afternoon tea with a distinctly Yorkshire flavour, served in the AA Rosette Osborne’s Restaurant which benefits from panoramic sea views.

Ryan Osborne, executive chef at Hotel Victoria, said: "We’ve created an afternoon tea that is inspired by the Yorkshire coast."

The menu features a delicious selection of freshly prepared finger sandwiches, savouries, homemade cakes and warm fruit scones served with strawberry preserve and Chantilly cream, accompanied by a choice of tea or coffee.

Highlights include honey roast Yorkshire ham with beer chutney, oak-smoked salmon with cream cheese and cucumber, and the Robin Hood’s Bay Fisherman’s Treat – dressed Whitby crab served with sourdough bruschetta, saffron aioli, pickled cucumber and bay bronze fennel.

Savoury treats also include a goat’s cheese and beetroot tartlet with pumpkin seed pesto, alongside chicken liver parfait and house chutney on ginger loaf.

For dessert, guests can enjoy the Smuggler’s Sweet – a dark chocolate and sailor rum truffle – as well as Victoria sponge and a raspberry red velvet cupcake finished with cinnamon butter icing.

Ryan added: "The Robin Hood’s Bay Fisherman’s Treat and the Smuggler’s Sweet reflect the village’s history and pay tribute to the fishing and smuggling heritage.

"Together with traditional afternoon tea favourites, these distinctive touches create an experience that reflects the unique character of Robin Hood’s Bay."

The Bay Explorer Afternoon Tea is priced from £25 per person and includes the complimentary Monaco Golf Buggy trip, subject to availability.

Advance booking is required. For more information or to make a booking, guests can visit the Hotel Victoria website at [https://victoriarhb.com/.](https://victoriarhb.com/.)

Hotel Victoria is part of the Loxley Collection, which also includes the White Hart Hotel in Lincoln and the Polurrian on the Lizard in Cornwall.

The hotel is located on Station Road in Robin Hood’s Bay, near Whitby, and offers additional afternoon tea options starting from £30 per person.
