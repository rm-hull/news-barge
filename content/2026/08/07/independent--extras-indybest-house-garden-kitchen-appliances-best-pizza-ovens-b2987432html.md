One of the most enjoyable parts of summer is being able to eat outdoors. And there’s no better way to cook alfresco than with the best pizza ovens. They’re one of the most popular pieces of outdoor cooking kit, promising restaurant-quality results at home.

The best pizza ovens are designed to reach higher temperatures than conventional ovens. This ensures that pizzas cook quickly, while still producing a crispy base and bubbling toppings that’s hard to replicate in a standard oven.

Whether you’re a casual cook who enjoys the odd pizza night or a serious home chef looking for the authentic Neapolitan finish, the right oven can make all the difference. From indoor pizza ovens to larger, more permanent setups, there’s a wide range of options to suit all spaces, budgets and fuel preferences.

Luke Buckmaster, founder of Doughboys Pizza explains the rise in popularity: “At-home pizza ovens are part of a wider shift towards people wanting restaurant-quality food from the comfort of their own home. Pizza is the perfect meal for that – customisable, relatively quick and a very sociable meal people often love to share together. It’s not just about convenience, it’s about the experience and creative freedom you get.”

So with that in mind, I’ve hunted down the very best pizza ovens for indoors and outdoors, putting them through their paces, focusing on performance, usability and versatility.

The Gozney arc lite is an absolute joy to use and the best pizza oven for outdoor cooking that I’ve tried. It’s a gas pizza oven with a lateral flame, which means it rolls across the top of the oven and distributes heat more evenly to produce a flame-finished pizza. Performance wise, it excels and I found it heated up and cooked pizza quickly, with a 12in Neapolitan ready in around two minutes.

![best pizza oven indybest review Gozney arc lite]([https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/02/8/06/Gozney-arc-lite.png?quality=75&width=608&auto=webp](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/02/8/06/Gozney-arc-lite.png?quality=75&width=608&auto=webp) 608w)

You’ll need to rotate the pizza, but not having a door on the front means it’s easy to monitor and the design of the flame means you only need to turn it once. The pizza came out seriously good, the cheese was bubbling and the crust was crispy on the outside but fluffy on the inside. It also held its temperature well, so I was able to do multiple pizzas in a row without having to wait an age for it to heat back up to full temperature.

The compact pizza oven looks even smaller than it is, thanks to the sleek domed design. And I say small, but it does have the capacity to cook a 12in pizza, which is plenty for home cooks.

There are no real major downsides to this pizza oven. There’s no temperature gauge so you will need to invest in an external thermometer if you want more accurate results, but Gozney also sells a range of accessories, so you can get everything in a bundle when you order. If you don’t have the room for a large pizza oven, but you still want a premium product then this is one of the best practical pizza ovens for everyday use.
