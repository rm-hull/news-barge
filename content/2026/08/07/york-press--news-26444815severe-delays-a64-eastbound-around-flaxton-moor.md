## Severe delays as traffic crawls at 15mph major road through North Yorkshire

Motorists queuing *(Image: Contributed)*

SEVERE delays are being reported on a main road in North Yorkshire with average speeds of just 15mph.

The AA is reporting issues on the A64 this morning (Friday) around Flaxton Moor.

The organisation said: "Severe delays of 12 minutes and delays increasing on A64 Eastbound in North Yorkshire. Average speed of traffic is 15 mph."

It comes after motorists also experienced delays on the A64 from the Hopgrove Roundabout to Scotchman Lane earlier this week on Wednesday.

The road is typically congested during this time of year with the school holidays in full force.
