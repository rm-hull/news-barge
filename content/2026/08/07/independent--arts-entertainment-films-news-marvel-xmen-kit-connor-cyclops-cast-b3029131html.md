The forthcoming X-Men film – the first in Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) history – is causing a casting frenzy in Hollywood, with many actors meeting with the studio to lobby for roles.

Following the return of former X-Men cast members in upcoming film *Avengers: Doomsday*, a brand new generation of stars will play the mutant characters in a standalone film, directed by Jake Schreier.

Schreier and Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige are believed to be meeting with a variety of contenders for each role, with *Deadline* claiming that Kit Connor is the top choice to play Cyclops, the role played by James Marsden in three films from 2000 to 2006. 

![Kit Connor is reportedly top choice for Cyclops in new 'X-Men' film](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/07/7/05/Screenshot-2026-08-07-at-08-33-16.png)

Marsden’s Cyclops also had a cameo appearance in prequel *X-Men: First Class*(2011) – with the younger version of his character played by Tye Sheridan – and will reprise the role in* Doomsday*, which is out in December.

The new report arrives days after it was also claimed that *Ready or Not* star Samara Weaving would take on the role of Emma Frost, a character previously played by January Jones in*X-Men: First Class*(2011).* The Independent* has contacted Connor and Weaving for comment.

Connor, 22, is best known for Netflix teen series *Heartstopper*, in which he plays a schoolboy who develops romantic feelings for his friend. He appeared in the series opposite Joe Locke, who is also a Marvel star thanks to a role in Disney+ series* Agatha All Along*.* Heartstopper* ran for three seasons and recently concluded with a film.

Elsewhere, Connor, who was born in Croydon, London, has had roles in *Rocketman*, playing a teen Elton John, animated fantasy film* The Wild Robot *and the violent thriller* Warfare*, based on Ray Mendoza's experiences during the Iraq War as a US Navy SEAL.

He also played Romeo in a contemporary adaptation of William Shakespeare’s *Romeo & Juliet* on Broadway with Rachel Zegler.

Meanwhile, Weaving, 34, is an Australian star who shot to fame on soap *Home and Away* and is best known for playing the lead character in horror franchise *Ready or Not*.

Plans to fold the X-Men into the MCU have been in the works ever since 21st Century Fox merged with Disney in 2019, in a deal worth $71.3bn (£53.9bn).

Fox previously owned the rights to the characters, meaning the X-Men and associated characters, including Wolverine and Deadpool, were unable to appear in the MCU, an umbrella of superhero films that was launched with *Iron Man* in 2008.

The first key film marking the crossover was*Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness* (2022), which featured a cameo from Patrick Stewart’s Charles Xavier. Following this,* Deadpool & Wolverine *was released in 2024 and a surprise X-Men cameo has reared its head in blockbuster* Spider-Man**: Brand New Day*, which is in cinemas now. (No spoilers here.)

![James Marsden played Cyclops in original ‘X-Men’ trilogy](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/07/7/48/Screenshot-2026-08-07-at-08-32-15.png)

Next, Marsden and a number of his X-Men co-stars, including Rebecca Romijn (Mystique), Alan Cumming (Nightcrawler) and Kelsey Grammer (Beast) will fight along Thor (Chris Hemsworth), Ant-Man (Paul Rudd), Yelena Belova (Florence Pugh) and Mr Fantastic (Pedro Pascal) in *Avengers: Doomsday*.
