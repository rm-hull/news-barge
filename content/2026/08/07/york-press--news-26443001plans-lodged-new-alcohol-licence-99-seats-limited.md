### What is the Public Notice Portal?

The Public Notice Portal carries statutory public notices published in local newspapers and is the fastest and most effective way of finding out what is happening in YOUR neighbourhood.

Search the Public Notice PortalA venue in York is seeking permission to sell alcohol for on- and off-premises consumption.

99 Seats Limited, based on Haxby Road, applied on July 27 for a premises licence at Unit 10 Ryedale Court, Haxby.

The licence would allow the sale of alcohol for both on-site and off-site consumption from 11am to 11pm.

The City's Licensing Team has confirmed that the application can be viewed by setting up a prior appointment.

The documents are housed at the Hazel Court EcoDepot on James Street, available between 8.30am and 5pm from Monday to Friday.

The last date for residents or responsible authorities to express their views on this application is August 24.

Representations can be made in writing to the City of York Council Licensing Team at Hazel Court EcoDepot, James Street, York, YOIO 3DS.

Emails can be sent to [licensing@york.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing@york.gov.uk.)

The City of York Council Licensing Team can be reached at 01904 552422.

Under the Licensing Act 2003, it is an offence to knowingly or recklessly make a false statement in connection with a licence application, and offenders can be fined an unlimited amount.
