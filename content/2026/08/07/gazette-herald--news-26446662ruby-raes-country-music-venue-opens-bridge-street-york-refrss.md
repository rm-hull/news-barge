Yeehaw! A new country music venue was buzzing when it opened its doors in York on Thursday.

Ruby Rae’s Country Bar opened in the former Jalou nightclub on Bridge Street.

It adds to the recently opened Silver Spur a few doors away in Bridge Street, which form part of a thriving UK country and western scene.

Ruby Rae’s began in Newcastle and York is the company’s second venue.

Co-founder Dean Saunders chose the city as he has always loved it, he was a regular at Jalou, and when the former church became available, he could not refuse.

  Business partners Dean Saunders and Dan Cooke 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Dean says he has been working on developing the York site since February, which will be inspired by the world-famous Ryman stage in Nashville.

The venue will open Thursday to Sunday.

  More of the interior 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

In the renovations of the former church, all the floors have been changed. Much LVT flooring has been used, which Dean says is great for line dancing.

A new bar has been created, there is a revamped stage and DJ booth.

  The theme is Nashville 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Furthermore, to add character there are plates reflecting the region, ‘dollar walls’, flags, lots of vinyls and ‘lots of neon signs everywhere’ to create a Nashville look.

Dean said of the first night: “We were blown away by the support from everyone on the opening night in York.

“It was great to see hundreds of people come through the doors on a Thursday to enjoy some amazing country music!

  A most impressive interior. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

“Chuck Walton, Bryan Watson and Tom Humphrey performed all night with a special guest appearance from Twinnie, who is coming back tonight.”

Business partner Dan Cooke said: “After such a long month getting it ready, it was amazing to see the venue finally open with everyone enjoying themselves inside and we are already excited for the rest of the weekend.”

The pair reflected: “But it was a huge success and everyone seemed to really enjoy themselves. There were some amazing people who really loved their country music.”

  Plenty of neon on display at Ruby Rae's 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Already the venue is receiving rave reviews online, with 28 five stars reviews received by 4pm Friday.

One said: “Opening night! What a venue, great music and atmosphere!”

Another said: “If you like country music then this is the place for you.”

And one added: "Absolutely amazing! Fantastic atmosphere, great authentic singers & huge venue. Can’t WAIT to be back.”
