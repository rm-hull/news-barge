**Katseye vocalist Sophia Laforteza has announced she will temporarily step away from the popular girl group over mental health concerns.**

The departure comes less than six months after fellow bandmate Manon Bannerman said she would go on hiatus from the ensemble, known for its global hits Gnarly, Pinky Up and Gabriela.

Laforteza "will be temporarily unable to participate in upcoming promotional activities", HYBE x Geffen Records said in a statement.

"We understand how much EYEKONS were looking forward to seeing Sophia on stage, but it is imperative that her mental health and wellness are prioritized," the label said of the Filipino-American superstar.

Yoonchae, Megan, Lara Raj, Manon, Sophia, and Daniela of Katseye attend the 2026 Grammy Awards in Los Angeles.

The label said Laforteza has been receiving "the utmost support" and consulting medical professionals, and has been advised to dedicate time to "extended rest and ongoing care to ensure a full recovery".

"While Sophia herself is eager to perform alongside her members, the health, safety, and long-term well-being of our artists will always remain our highest priority," the statement said, noting that her condition will be reassessed in September.

In a separate statement, Laforteza wrote on Instagram: "Making this decision wasn't easy, but I'm learning that health has to come first. I'm realizing that if I don't take care of my mind and body now, I won't be able to keep doing what I love the most for so long."

She added: "It means more to me than I could ever say. I promise I'm going to work as hard as I can to get through this, heal properly, and come back stronger."

The girl group formed with six members in 2023 and was nominated for two Grammy Awards last year. It now will continue events with four members.

The group has previously said its members have received multiple online death threats since they made their debut.

In February, the label announced that Katseye member Bannerman would take a temporary break to focus on her health and wellbeing, as well. She has not returned but their label has said the door will always be open.

The 23-year-old Swiss singer told fans she was "healthy" and "taking care of myself".

The group said the decision had been made following "open and thoughtful conversations together".
