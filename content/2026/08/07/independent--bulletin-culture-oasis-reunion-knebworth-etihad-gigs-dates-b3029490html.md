# Are Oasis about to announce 2027 tour? Knebworth licence sparks fresh rumours

- SJM Concerts has secured an events licence for Knebworth House, permitting events for up to 125,000 people with performances until 3am, intensifying speculation about another Oasis reunion.
- This development coincides with the upcoming 30th anniversary of Oasis's historic Knebworth concerts, where the band played to 250,000 people across two nights in 1996.
- Reports suggest Noel and Liam Gallagher are planning a six-night residency at Knebworth, which would set a new record for the venue, alongside a 12-date run at Manchester City’s Etihad Stadium.
- A source indicated that the Gallagher brothers had always intended for their recent reunion tour to have a second run, with August being a potential timeframe for new announcements.
- The brothers previously mended a 15-year rift, announcing a reunion in 2024 for a summer 2025 tour, which sold out rapidly despite controversial dynamic pricing.
