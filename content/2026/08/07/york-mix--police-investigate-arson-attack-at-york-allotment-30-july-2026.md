Police are investigating a criminal arson attack at a York allotment.

It happened at Fulford Cross Allotments just before 5.47pm on Thursday 30 July.

North Yorkshire Police say “unknown persons attended this location and set fire to a shed which has also caused damage to some plantation”.

#### Fulford Cross Allotments, York

Fire crews from Thirsk, Harrogate and Malton went to the incident and put out the blaze, which measured 15m by 15m.

Anyone who can help police with their enquiries is asked to contact investigating officer PC41 Dave Ellison on 101.
