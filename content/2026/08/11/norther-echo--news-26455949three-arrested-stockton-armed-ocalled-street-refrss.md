A teenager has been arrested after armed officers were spotted on a residential street in Stockton following reports of a person in possession of a knife.

Cleveland Police were called to Piper Knowle Road in Stockton at about noon today (11 August) following reports a person was potentially in possession of a knife.

Armed officers attended the scene and arrested a 17-year-old boy on suspicion of possession of a knife and possession of a Class C drug.

Two women, aged 37 and 41, were both arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender.

All three remain in police custody at this time.
