More than 900 Junior Soldiers celebrated a major milestone in their Army careers as they passed out from the Army Foundation College (AFC) Harrogate yesterday (6 August), marking the completion of their initial training before moving on to regiments and corps across the British Army.

The parade brought together families, friends and staff to recognise the achievements of young soldiers who have spent months developing their military skills, education and confidence.

For many, the occasion represented far more than the end of their training. It was the culmination of personal journeys shaped by determination, resilience and ambition.

Among those celebrating was Junior Soldier Hayden Marley from Middlesbrough, who overcame challenges during his school years before finding support and opportunity at AFC Harrogate.


**Hayden said the college helped him develop both academically and personally, adding:**

I didn’t really receive much support at school, but since coming to AFC, I’ve had a lot of guidance.

A lot of people think it’s just about combat roles, but if you properly research you can go into so many careers that will help set you up for a life outside of the military.



As he prepared to join the Royal Armoured Corps, Hayden encouraged others to consider the opportunities available through Army service.

For Junior Soldier Harrison Woods, graduation marked the successful completion of a journey that included overcoming personal tragedy after his Mum passed away and adapting to the demands of military life.

Harrison, who is now joining the Princess of Wales’s Royal Regiment, reflected on the significance of the day, saying: “It’s been really hard, but I know my dad is so proud of where I am today and graduating the college. I’m also really proud of myself.”

He added that some of his highlights at Harrogate included adventurous training and the College’s visit to Normandy, which gives Junior Soldiers an opportunity to learn about the sacrifices of previous generations.

Family pride was also on display for Junior Soldier Olly Shipton, who graduated before beginning his career in the Royal Artillery.


Supported by his older brother, Louis, a serving Royal Artillery soldier and former AFC Harrogate student, Olly described the occasion as a special moment for the whole family.


**Olly said:**

I’m really happy and ready for what’s next. I’ve got my parents and my big brother on the day. They are super proud and happy to be there for me.
