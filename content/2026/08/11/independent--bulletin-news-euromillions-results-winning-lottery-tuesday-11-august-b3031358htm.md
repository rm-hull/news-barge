# EuroMillions results: Winning lottery and Thunderball numbers for Tuesday 11 August

- Tonight’s EuroMillions draw has offered players the chance to win a jackpot of £14 million.
- To win the prize, a player had to get five main numbers plus two lucky stars.
- In the UK, every EuroMillions entry automatically includes a unique "UK Millionaire Maker" code, entering you into an additional draw with a guaranteed £1 million winner in every draw.
- The winning Thunderball numbers were **3, 10, 20, 25** and**28**. The Thunderball was** 7.**
- The winning EuroMillions numbers were **3, 11, 17, 46** and**48**. The Lucky Stars were** 1**and** 2** .
