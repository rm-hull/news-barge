Six fire engines have been called to a blaze at a North East van dealership.

Tyne and Wear Fire and Rescue said they were called to the Sherwood Van Centre in Washington at 5.53pm this evening (Tuesday, August 11).

A spokesperson confirmed that six fire engines are currently (7pm) at the scene.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Police officers have also been called to the dealership.

Pictures show thick clouds of black smoke coming from the building while a cordon has been put in place.

A large crowd has gathered as firefighters attempt to bring the flames under control.
