# Who is Jayme Franklin, the woman who went viral at Trump’s vaccine announcement?

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/exflbZRt/poster.jpg?width=720)

- President Donald Trump signed an executive order calling for revamped federal guidance on childhood vaccine recommendations to promote spacing out shots.
- Social media attention focused heavily on Jayme Franklin, a former Trump staffer standing behind him in the Oval Office who repeatedly nodded during the announcement.
- Online commentators mocked Franklin's constant nodding and posture, nicknaming her “bobble head” and questioning her presence at the signing.
- Franklin, the CEO of conservative lifestyle brand *The Conservateur* , stated she was honored to attend the event to support parental freedom in vaccine decisions.
- White House spokesman Kush Desai stated Franklin represented parents whose vaccine concerns have been ignored, defending the administration's policy.
