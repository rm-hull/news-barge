The Sharks host New Zealand in Durban as the All Blacks continue to gear up for four Tests against South Africa on the Rugby’s Greatest Rivalry tour.

Dave Rennie’s side opened their trip with a scratchy victory over the Stormers, and there is an entirely fresh look to their side as the head coach looks at his options ahead of next Saturday’s first Test. Rennie will be hoping to see greater fluency in phase play from his remodelled squad against another talented team – and one that includes one of New Zealand’s own.

The ageless Ma’a Nonu won 103 All Blacks caps and two World Cups during his international career, and is still going strong at 44. The veteran centre is set to feature off the bench for a Sharks side that incudes top talent Vusi Moyo at fly half, and will be hoping to impress against their illustrious opposition at Kings Park.
