# New wildfires rage near Athens as latest heatwave hits

- Around 200 firefighters are tackling new wildfire outbreaks southeast of Athens following a deadly blaze in the Attica region.
- At least five firefighters have died battling recent blazes in Greece, where over 30,000 hectares of land have been scorched so far this year.
- Sparks from the electricity grid have reportedly caused up to 75 per cent of the burned land in Greece this year, prompting a nationwide investigation into wind farm infrastructure.
- Authorities have declared category 4 threat warnings across multiple regions, including Greater Athens and Crete, as temperatures are forecast to reach 35C.
- Severe understaffing and underfunding resulting from a decade of austerity measures continue to hinder fire prevention and mitigation efforts in Greece.
