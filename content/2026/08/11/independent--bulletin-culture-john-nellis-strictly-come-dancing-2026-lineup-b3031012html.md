# Who are the confirmed Strictly Come Dancing 2026 contestants?

- The BBC's *Strictly Come Dancing* has continued to announce its celebrity line-up for the 2026 series, with several notable figures confirmed.
- Confirmed contestants include *EastEnders* star**Lacey Turner**,* Love Island* 's**Dani Dyer**, Australian singer** Delta Goodrem**, celebrity hairstylist** Chris Appleton**, reality star** Cach Mercer**, Paralympian** Dame Sarah Storey**, TV presenter** Will Best**and actor** Jaime Winstone** .
- Recent additions to the cast include *House of the Dragon* actor**Bethany Antonia**, former footballer** Shaun Wright-Phillips**, “The Dog Father”** Graeme Hall**,* Emmerdale* star**Laurence Robb**, Olympian** Tabby Stoecker**and football content creator** John Nellis** .
- The show will also introduce five new professional dancers and new presenters **Emma Willis**,** Josh Widdicombe**and** Johannes Radebe** .
- Among those rumoured to be joining the competition are former football referee Mark Clattenburg and former *X Factor* contestant Jake Quickenden.
