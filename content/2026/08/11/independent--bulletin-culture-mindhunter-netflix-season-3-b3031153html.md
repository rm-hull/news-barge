# Netflix CEO shares the series he wished had come back for another season

- Netflix co-CEO Ted Sarandos claims he wanted a third season of *Mindhunter* , contradicting previous assumptions that the streaming platform cancelled the show.
- Sarandos said that creator David Fincher was too busy directing the Netflix film *Mank* to produce another season at the time.
- Fincher had previously attributed the end of the show to high production costs relative to its viewership numbers.
- Actor Holt McCallany previously hinted that Fincher had considered resurrecting the series, though its return remains uncertain.
- Fincher maintains a working relationship with Netflix, with his next film set for release on the service in November.
