The first Pateley Club Beer Festival will take place from 21-23 August, marking another step in the club’s exciting progression as a local community space, offering new and exciting events to members and visitors alike.

The club has recently come through a significant period of change, including amendments to its license, which attracted some local objections, before ultimately being approved.

Since then, the committee and volunteers have been working hard to promote The Pateley Club as a welcoming community hub, offering live music, bingo, private functions, local club meetings and events that bring people together. The Beer Festival is the latest example of that commitment.

Members and Visitors will be able to enjoy 12 carefully selected draught craft beers and ciders over the three-day event, celebrating some of the very best breweries and cider makers from Yorkshire and beyond.

Tickets are £10 per person and include:

- Entry for all three days of the festival and exclusive access to the upstairs Beer Festival bar
- A commemorative Pateley Club beer glass to keep
- A free pint of any craft beer or cider on offer
- A specially made, locally by Kendalls Butchers, sausage hot off the BBQ

The club has said that they hope the festival will become an annual event for Pateley Bridge and provide another reason for both locals and visitors to support one of the town’s longest-standing social clubs.

We would be extremely grateful if you could help us spread the word by featuring the event in your newspaper or online listings.

The festival takes place:

Pateley Club Beer Festival

21st-23rd August

12:00pm – 8:00pm daily

42 High Street, Pateley Bridge, HG3 5JU
