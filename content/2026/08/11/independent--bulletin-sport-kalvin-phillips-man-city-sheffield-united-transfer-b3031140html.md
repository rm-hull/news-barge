# Man City outcast Kalvin Phillips to be sent out on loan for fourth time

- Kalvin Phillips is set to rejoin Sheffield United on loan from Manchester City, marking his fourth loan spell away from the Etihad Stadium despite having two years remaining on his contract.
- The 30-year-old England international midfielder previously joined Sheffield United on loan last season, but was restricted to just three appearances following a red card in a Steel City derby and a subsequent knee injury.
- Sheffield United manager Chris Wilder has moved to bring Phillips back to reinforce his midfield options as the club faces the potential loss of Gustavo Hamer to Coventry City.
- Since completing a £42.5m transfer from Leeds United in 2022, Phillips has struggled for game time at Manchester City, making just two Premier League starts and six starts in all competitions.
- Despite Manchester City appointing Enzo Maresca as manager and facing uncertainty over Rodri's future amid interest from Barcelona, Phillips remains out of first-team contention.
