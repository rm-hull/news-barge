# James Buckley reveals he’s stopped drinking due to rare health diagnosis

- *Inbetweeners* actor James Buckley has revealed he was forced to make major lifestyle changes after being diagnosed with a genetic health condition.
- The 38-year-old was told by doctors that high levels of Lipoprotein(a) in his blood put him at a high risk of a cardiovascular incident.
- Buckley has given up alcohol and takeaway meals following the diagnosis, with his wife Clair supporting his decision.
- The actor expressed concern that he may have passed the inherited condition on to his two sons, Harrison and Jude.
- Buckley is reportedly set to reunite with his original co-stars for an upcoming Netflix reboot of The *Inbetweeners* .
