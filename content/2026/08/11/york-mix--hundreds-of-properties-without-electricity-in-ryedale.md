A power cut is affecting a large number of properties in the Ryedale area.

Malton, Norton and villages south of the A64 are all affected.

Meanwhile there are power cuts in the villages south of Harrogate town centre too.

Northern Powergrid says it is working on the problem and expects to have the supply restored by the afternoon.

You can check progress of work here [https://www.northernpowergrid.com/power-cuts-map](https://www.northernpowergrid.com/power-cuts-map)
