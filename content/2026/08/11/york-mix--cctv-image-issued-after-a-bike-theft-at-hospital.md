Police are appealing for assistance identifying a man who may be able to help with enquires relating to the theft of a bike.

The grey *Scott Strike Eride 930* bicycle was stolen from a bike shelter near the main entrance of Malton and Norton District Hospital.

It happened between 5:50 am and 12:05 pm, on Tuesday 30th June. An offender cut through the locks securing bike to the shelter and cycled away onto Hospital Road.
