# Thousands of homes and businesses without water as new heatwave looms

- Burst water pipes have left thousands of homes and businesses across London and Essex without supplies or facing low pressure as a fifth heatwave approaches.
- Thames Water confirmed 14 postcode areas are affected, with the *BBC* estimating 10,000 properties are impacted.
- A rare amber weather alert has been issued for the whole of England, with temperatures potentially climbing to 38C this week.
- The Met Office indicates this summer is on track to become the UK’s warmest since records began.
- Only average conditions over the remainder of August are required for the month to surpass the milestone set just last year.
