# Why Grey’s Anatomy star almost quit acting before securing break-out role

- Actor Jeffrey Dean Morgan revealed he considered quitting acting in his mid-30s after his former manager dropped him for having aged out.
- Morgan went on to secure breakout roles in *Grey's Anatomy*,* Supernatural*, and* Weeds* despite his age.
- His portrayal of heart transplant patient Denny Duquette in *Grey's Anatomy* catapulted him to fame with 30 million viewers tuning in weekly.
- Morgan later landed his best-known role as Negan in AMC's hit series *The Walking Dead,* as well as parts in*Watchmen* and*The Boys* .
- The actor is currently promoting Amazon Prime Video's new teen romance series, *Sterling Point* , in which he plays a complicated father figure.
