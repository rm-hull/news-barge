# How Adam Ramsay-Peaty fared in the European Championships 100m breaststroke final

- Adam Ramsay-Peaty finished fifth in the men’s 100m breaststroke final at the European Aquatics Championships in Paris.
- The 16-time European champion touched the wall in 59.10 seconds, missing out on a podium spot.
- Reigning Olympic champion Nicolo Martinenghi won gold for Italy with a time of 58.58 seconds.
- Silver was claimed by Ivan Kozhakin in 58.68 seconds, while Italy’s Simone Cerasuolo took bronze in 58.90 seconds.
- Fellow Briton Filip Nowacki placed sixth in 59.11 seconds, just behind Ramsay-Peaty, while Ireland’s Jack Kelly finished seventh.
