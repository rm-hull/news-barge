# Native birds to be vaccinated against H5N1 bird flu in Australia

- Australia is set to begin inoculating at-risk native bird populations against the lethal H5N1 strain of avian influenza over the coming days and weeks.
- Agriculture Minister Julie Collins confirmed that the vaccination drive will initially focus on vulnerable priority species held in captivity.
- The announcement follows Australia's first reported mass death among seabirds, after the virus spread from migratory birds to local native species.
- Roughly 45 per cent of Australia's 850 bird species are endemic, leaving much of the country's unique wildlife acutely at risk.
- The initiative follows a similar programme launched by neighbouring New Zealand in July to protect 300 key breeding birds from endangered species.
