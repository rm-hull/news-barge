# The actor tipped to become the next James Bond – according to Richard Osman

- Presenter Richard Osman has revealed that Scottish actor Jack Lowden is being strongly considered to play the next James Bond.
- The 36-year-old *Slow Horses* actor is reportedly in the running alongside previous frontrunners such as Jacob Elordi and Callum Turner.
- Casting director Nina Gold is overseeing the search for 007, with an official announcement expected by the end of the year.
- *Dune* director Denis Villeneuve is set to helm the upcoming Bond film, which Amazon MGM Studios is targeting for a 2028 release.
- Producers are taking a methodical approach to finding Daniel Craig's successor to ensure an exciting new direction for the franchise.
