You don’t have to have long, blonde curly locks and a penchant for breaking and entering to know what makes for the best dining chair. But with so many designs to choose from, finding one that perfectly suits your style and feels comfortable to sit in can be quite the mission.

Fortunately, I’ve tried and tested a wide range of dining chairs from online homeware brands such as Next, Loaf, Dusk and Grain & Frame to bring you our edit of the very best. I’ve selected a range of materials and colours including wood, leather, faux leather and plush velvet, plus they look eye-catching set against one of the best dining tables.

But before you click to buy the dining chair that catches your eye, it’s important to consider the practicalities and your setup. Do you need chairs for a formal dining room or are you in search for something that will fit a casual open-plan area, for example? Also think about who will be sitting on the chairs – if you’ve got kids, then chairs that are easy to clean are crucial.

Dining chairs are usually sold in pairs of two, which allows you to spread the cost if needed, or mix and match styles. Your table will indicate how many chairs you need, but measure carefully before you buy to ensure the chairs will neatly fit underneath the tabletop. For a standard 4-6 seater table, you might want to opt for two carver chairs at either sides of the table and four standard dining chairs without arms at the sides. If you have an extendable table and don’t have the budget to buy the extras in one go, check that the chairs you buy are staple designs and will be available throughout the year as opposed to end of line stock.

In my edit of the best dining chairs, I’ve included designs to suit both modern and traditional tables. I’ve tested each chair in my own open-plan kitchen/dining area and highlighted the best options to help you buy the most eye-catching designs.
