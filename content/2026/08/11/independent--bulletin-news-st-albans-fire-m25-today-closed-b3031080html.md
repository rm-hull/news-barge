# Hour-long delays for drivers as fire closes section of M25

![Locals are being urged to keep their windows and doors closed](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/11/10/31/0_m25-55234-m1-j6a-j21a-copy-55a8831a.jpeg)

- A large industrial estate fire in St Albans has forced the closure of the M25 motorway between junctions 21A and 22.
- The closure and thick smoke have caused traffic delays of around 60 minutes in both directions.
- Hertfordshire Fire and Rescue Service and Network Rail are managing the scene, advising local residents to keep doors and windows closed.
- The blaze comes as firefighters across the UK battle widespread wildfires during an ongoing drought, including in South Wales and the New Forest.
- The Met Office has issued an amber heat warning for Thursday, with temperatures expected to reach up to 38C in parts of England.
