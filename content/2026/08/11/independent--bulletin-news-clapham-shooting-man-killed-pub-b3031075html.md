# Murder investigation launched after man killed outside London pub

![The man was pronounced dead at the scene](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/07/08/11/0deabcdd362bc9d748abfeff4ee76853Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzUyMDU3OTI5-2.63820731.jpg)

- A murder investigation has been launched after a 25-year-old man was shot dead outside a pub in Clapham, south London, on Monday night.
- Paramedics arrived at the scene on Willington Road within three minutes, but the victim was pronounced dead.
- A 22-year-old man later presented at a hospital with a gunshot wound and has been arrested on suspicion of murder.
- The Metropolitan Police have set up a forensics tent outside the pub and increased local patrols as enquiries into the incident continue.
- Detective Chief Superintendent Emma Bond expressed condolences to the victim's family and urged any witnesses to contact police immediately.
