Police have released CCTV footage of person following an assault on Knaresborough Road in Harrogate.

It happened at around 5:10pm on 28th June and involved a female assaulting another person.

The suspect reportedly struck the victim on the shoulder and threw a metal drink bottle at them.

Anyone who recognises the person in the CCTV image or has further information is asked to email [usma.amireddy@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:usma.amireddy@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, you can call North Yorkshire Police on 101, or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

*Please quote reference 12260118491 when passing on information.*
