Harrogate Theatre is preparing to welcome some of the biggest names in comedy as Harrogate Comedy Festival returns this autumn with another outstanding programme of stand-up, improv, storytelling and family entertainment.

Now a firm favourite in the comedy calendar, the festival continues to grow year on year, attracting comedy fans from across Yorkshire and beyond to experience a brilliant mix of household names, much-loved performers and exciting talent.

This year’s line-up includes  

__Richard Ayoade__,__Harry Enfield__,__Alexei Sayle__,__Maisie Adam__,__Miriam Margolyes__,__Jason Byrne__,__Reece Shearsmith__and many more.
The festival opens with the hilarious  

__A Star Wars Improv Show: The One-Man Made-Up Movie Parody__, before audiences can enjoy the Olivier Award-winning__Showstopper! The Improvised Musical__. The ever-popular__Hyena Lounge Comedy Club__also returns as part of the festival, showcasing some of the very best comedians on the UK circuit.
This year’s programme sees Yorkshire favourite Maisie Adam return with her brand-new tour  

__Whatsherface__, before hosting__Frank’s Fund Comedy Gala__. Beloved actress and raconteur Miriam Margolyes brings her live show__Full English__to Harrogate Convention Centre, while audiences can also enjoy high-energy stand-up from__Jason Byrne__,__Seann Walsh__and__Tez Ilyas__. Families can look forward to__Olaf Falafel’s Stupidest Super Stupid Show__!
This year’s festival welcomes the brilliantly funny comedy legends  

__Paul Merton and Suki Webster__and audiences can also enjoy the sharp observations of__Laura Smyth__, the quick wit of__Ed Patrick__and__Rachel Mars__, as well as the hilarious__Scummy Mummies__,__The 4 Johns__and__Daniel Kitson__.
**Harrogate Comedy Festival programmer Marcus Romer said:** Harrogate Comedy Festival has become one of the highlights of our year, attracting some of the country’s finest comedians alongside exciting new voices.We’re incredibly proud to bring together such a diverse mix of established favourites and rising stars, creating a programme that celebrates the very best of live comedy.We’re delighted to once again bring such a varied programme to Harrogate, offering something for everyone – whether you’re a lifelong comedy fan or simply looking for a great night out.We can’t wait to welcome audiences back for another fantastic festival, filled with unforgettable performances, plenty of laughter and the unique atmosphere that makes Harrogate Comedy Festival such a special event.

Now in its 17th year, Harrogate Comedy Festival has built a reputation for bringing nationally acclaimed acts to North Yorkshire, while providing audiences with opportunities to discover rising stars before they become household names.

Harrogate Theatre is incredibly grateful for the support of Harrogate Comedy Festival’s primary partner Vet Dentist.

Tickets for all performances are on sale now, with many shows expected to sell quickly.
