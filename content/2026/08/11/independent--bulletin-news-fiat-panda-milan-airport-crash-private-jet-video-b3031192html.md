# Watch: Car collides with luxury jet as plane prepares for takeoff

- A Fiat Panda operated by an airport handling company crashed into a luxury business jet at Milan Linate Airport in Italy.
- The car cut across the aircraft's path and ploughed into its nose as it taxied towards the runway on Monday morning.
- No physical injuries were reported as a result of the collision.
- Airport operations have been fully restored and flights are operating as scheduled following the incident.
- An investigation into the circumstances surrounding the crash is currently underway.
