A four-bedroom townhouse located near Pinewoods and Valley Gardens is up for sale at £750,000.

This family home, in Beech Close, Harrogate, offers more than 1,900 sq ft of living space across three floors.

The property features an open-plan living kitchen with under-floor heating, a utility room, a large sitting room, a cloakroom, and three bedrooms, each with an en-suite bathroom.

There is also a guest WC.

One of the bedrooms was previously two separate bedrooms and could be easily reconfigured to create a fourth bedroom if needed.

The house is equipped with double glazing, a modern central heating system, and solar panels, according to the estate agent.

The open-plan living kitchen, which includes a range of stylish wall and base units with quartz worktops and an island with a breakfast bar, has doors leading to the garden.

The kitchen is fitted with an induction hob, double oven and microwave, fridge/freezer, and dishwasher.

There is also a utility room with fitted units, a worktop with a sink, an integrated washing machine, and a point for a tumble dryer.

The ground-floor double bedroom has en-suite facilities and opens onto a courtyard.

The first floor has a double bedroom with a Juliet balcony and fitted wardrobes, and another double bedroom that was previously two separate rooms.

Both of these bedrooms have en-suite facilities.

Outside, the property benefits from off-road parking on a resin drive, an integral garage, and a landscaped garden with paved sitting areas and well-stocked borders.

The garage has an electric vehicle charge point.

The house is part of a modern development and is within walking distance of Harrogate town centre, with local schools and shops nearby.

The estate agent, Verity Frearson, describes this as an "impressive home in a sought-after location".
