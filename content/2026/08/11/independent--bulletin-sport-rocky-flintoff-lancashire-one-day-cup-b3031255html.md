# Andrew Flintoff’s son Rocky smashes sensational first Lancashire century

- Andrew Flintoff’s son, Rocky, scored his first century for Lancashire during their One-Day Cup clash against Somerset on Tuesday.
- Rocky, 18, brought up his maiden hundred for his county off just 65 balls as Lancashire posted a huge total of 443-6.
- Rocky hit eight fours and four sixes on his way to his hundred before he was eventually dismissed for 123 off 76 balls.
- He shared a 214-partnership with former England international Keaton Jennings as Lancashire racked up an imposing score.
- Rocky’s knock comes just days after his father announced he was stepping down from his role as England Lions head coach.
