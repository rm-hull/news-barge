*Emmerdale* actor Christopher Coghill has recounted an incident where a viewer struck him over the head with a handbag after being appalled with his on-screen antics. 

Currently playing Kev Barton on the ITV soap, Coghill portrays his character as "a sympathetic psychopath, if you will". He explained that he holds no concerns about playing villains, having previously portrayed paedophile Tony King in *EastEnders* in 2008.

He said: "You’ve got to give people a bit of credit. When I started in *EastEnders*, there were people who were like, ‘You know, there are people out there who really can’t tell the difference between what they see on telly and real life, and because of the character you’re playing, you’re going to get a lot of shit, and you might need police protection’.

"And then you go out, and people go, ‘Oh, you’re brilliant in *EastEnders*’, and that was kind of it.

"I did get hit over the head with a handbag in Selfridges, but apart from that…"

His *Emmerdale* co-star William Ash, who plays Caleb Miligan, said he has had the same experience, adding: “People are genuinely generally really nice. You don’t get any grief, really. I did have one woman at the at the petrol station going ‘oh, you’re right horrible to Moira’.”

![Chris Coghill, who plays Kev Barton in Emmerdale, describes his character as ‘a sympathetic psychopath, if you will’, adding he is not concerned about being cast as a villain following his experience playing paedophile Tony King in EastEnders in 2008](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/11/7/48/EMBARGO_EMM_KEV_BACK.avif)

The pair, who have previously acted together in the 2003 drama *Burn It* and played brothers in both the 2006 one-off comedy*Magnolia* and a 2016 episode of*Moving On*, are currently caught up in an* Emmerdale* storyline involving an attack on Billy Fletcher (Jay Kontzle) at the depot.

Kev is the guilty party and Caleb has the assault on CCTV, but has been covering it up so he can frame his arch-nemesis, Joe Tate (Ned Porteous).

Cohgill said of the footage: “I hold my hands up, it does look bad – I am bent over him with a crowbar and he’s in a pool of blood.”

However, it may not be the bargaining tool that Caleb thinks it is.

Ash said: “I think [Caleb and wife Ruby, played by Beth Cordingly] just don’t know how to handle him, like most people in the village. It’s like he’s such a loose cannon. You just don’t know what you’re going to get from him.”

![Kev Townsend returned to Emmerdale earlier this year](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/05/28/15/47/Screenshot-2026-05-28-at-16-56-55.png)

Coghill is also adamant he does not want his character to have any more singing scenes, after his character serenaded his estranged husband Robert (Ryan Hawley) with ‘If You’re Not The One’ by Daniel Bedingfield and then tried to express his feelings to long-lost son Lewis (Bradley Riches) with a version of Boyzone’s ‘Father And Son’.

Coghill said: “Do you know what, [producer] Laura Shaw did say to me the other week, do you want to do ‘All I Want For Christmas Is You?’ And I said, if you keep me till Christmas 2029, I’ll do it then. That’s how my contract negotiations are going. But no, it’s excruciating. Horrible.”

“If I could sing, I wouldn’t be an actor. If I could sing, I would have been in a band in the 1990s.”
