# College athletes targeted in sextortion schemes prompts FBI to step in

![The FBI and the NCAA announced a joint initiative to protect college athletes from digital threats and online sexual exploitation](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/07/20/NCAA_Tournament_Expansion_Basketball_22693.jpg)

- The FBI and the NCAA announced a joint initiative to protect college athletes from digital threats and online sexual exploitation.
- Expanding social media presence due to Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL) deals has increased student-athletes' exposure to cybercriminals.
- Federal authorities warned of risks including phishing schemes, stolen private images, severe sextortion campaigns, and account takeovers.
- The partnership will focus primarily on educational outreach to raise threat awareness and promote digital safety among student-athletes.
- FBI and NCAA officials emphasized the pressure athletes face to maintain clean public images and pledged support and justice for victims.
