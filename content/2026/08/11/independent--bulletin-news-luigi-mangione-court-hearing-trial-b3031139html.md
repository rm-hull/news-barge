# Luigi Mangione’s high-profile murder case nears its final showdown

![A supporter wears a T-shirt as people gather outside the Criminal Courts Building in Manhattan ahead of a trial of Luigi Mangione](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/11/14/2026-08-11T121609Z_92290165_RC2CWMAZ80BH_RTRMADP_3_UNITEDHEALTH-CEO-MANGIONE.JPG)

- Luigi Mangione returns to court for a hearing ahead of his New York state trial for the December 2024 fatal shooting of UnitedHealthcare CEO Brian Thompson.
- State Supreme Court Justice Gregory Carro will finalize trial logistics during the hearing, including media access and jury selection procedures.
- Mangione has pleaded not guilty to state charges of murder, weapons offenses, and forgery, as well as separate federal stalking charges.
- Prosecutors possess extensive evidence against Mangione, including surveillance footage, DNA matches, a firearm, and a notebook outlining animosity toward insurance companies.
- Mangione's state murder trial is set to begin next month, while his separate federal trial is scheduled for January.
