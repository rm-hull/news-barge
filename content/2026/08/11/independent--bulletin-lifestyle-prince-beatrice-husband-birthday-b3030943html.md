# Princess Beatrice’s husband praises ‘phenomenal, beautiful wife’

- Princess Beatrice, the niece of King Charles, celebrated her 38th birthday over the weekend.
- Her husband, Edoardo Mapelli Mozzi, shared a collection of photos on Instagram alongside a heartfelt message addressed to his wife, signed from himself and their children.
- “Happy Birthday to my phenomenal, beautiful wife. We love so much. You make our world,” he wrote.
- The birthday comes shortly after Beatrice's sister, Princess Eugenie, gave birth to her third child, a baby girl, in Lisbon on 3 August.
- Princess Eugenie and her husband, Jack Brooksbank, are yet to decide on a name for their daughter, who is the fifth grandchild of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor and Sarah Ferguson.
