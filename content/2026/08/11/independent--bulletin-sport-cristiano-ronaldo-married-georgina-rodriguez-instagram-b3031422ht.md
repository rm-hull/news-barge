# Cristiano Ronaldo reveals major milestone in candid Instagram post

- Cristiano Ronaldo has married his long-term partner Georgina Rodriguez.
- The pair wed in a civil ceremony in Cascais, Portugal, around 20 miles west of Lisbon, the Portuguese capital.
- The service was attended by their five children, four days before Ronaldo begins the 2026-27 Saudi Pro League season with Al Nassr.
- The couple announced the marriage on Instagram by sharing a photograph of their rings captioned with their initials and a heart.
- Ronaldo and Rodriguez have been together since 2016 after meeting in Madrid, before publicly confirming their relationship in 2017.
