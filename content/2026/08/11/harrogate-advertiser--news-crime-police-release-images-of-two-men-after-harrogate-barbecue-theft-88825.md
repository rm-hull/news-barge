# Police release images of two men after Harrogate barbecue theft

## North Yorkshire Police has released the images of two men they would like to speak to following a theft in the Harrogate area.

It happened on Dene Park at 9am on Saturday, July 11, and involved a stainless steel barbeque being stolen from a property.

A police spokesperson said: “It is believed that the suspects arrived and left the area in an Iveco van with a yellow bonnet.

“If you recognise the men pictured, please get in touch as they may have information that could assist our investigation.

“Contact us online in the first instance:

• Online: Visit our website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’.

• Live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“Quote reference 12260131399 when providing information.”
