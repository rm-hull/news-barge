A POWER cut is affecting a number of properties in North Yorkshire town including two supermarkets.

Around 100 properties have lost electricity including the Lidl store in Welham Road, Norton and Asda in Norton Road.

Derwent swimming pool in Church Street is also closed.

A number of villages are also impacted.

Northern Powergrid said the electricity should be restored by 11.30am.
