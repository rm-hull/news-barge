Drivers and bus passengers in Darlington are being warned of temporary disruption as traffic lights are installed for work connected to a new housing development.

The lights will be in place on Rotary Way, Faverdale, from today (Tuesday, August 11), until Tuesday, August 25, Darlington Borough Council has said.

The work will also mean the closure of the 5676 Argos inbound bus stop on Rotary Way for the duration of the works.

Passengers who normally use the stop are being asked to use the stop on Faverdale North, opposite the Aldi Distribution Centre instead.

The council has not said whether the work is expected to cause significant disruption, but drivers using Rotary Way are being advised to allow extra time for their journeys.
