# Why men will be mimicking monkeys at this year’s Badminton World Champs

- Organisers of the 2026 Badminton World Championships in Delhi have hired three men trained to mimic langur calls to scare away monkeys from the venue.
- The decision follows embarrassing disruptions during the India Open earlier this year, when monkeys and pigeons entered court and practice areas.
- Using real langurs is prohibited under India's Wildlife Protection Act, making vocal mimicry a legal alternative previously used at the G20 Summit.
- The Indira Gandhi Indoor Stadium has also undergone major physical upgrades, including bird-repelling sound devices, sealed windows, improved lighting, and double doors.
- Former world champion PV Sindhu praised the measures, highlighting the dedicated effort to ensure India is ready to host the prestigious tournament.
