# Unclaimed Powerball jackpot rises to nearly $1 billion

- The Powerball jackpot has risen to an estimated US$975 million after no ticket matched all six winning numbers in Monday's draw.
- The current prize is the largest lottery jackpot of the year so far, increasing by $70 million ahead of Wednesday's draw.
- A sole jackpot winner opting for a lump-sum payout would receive an estimated $422.3 million before tax deductions.
- The game has recently been made available to players in the United Kingdom following its launch in July.
- The odds of matching all six numbers to win the Powerball grand prize stand at one in 292,201,338.
