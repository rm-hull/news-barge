Polcie are appealing for information about an attempted robbery that occurred in Knaresborough.

- At approximately 10.30pm on Friday 7 August, 2026
- Wetherby Road, Knaresborough, near The Lido,
- Involved a man being approached by three unknown men and being threatened with a knife

The victim challenged the suspects and sustained minor injuries in the process, he later attended hospital for those injuries. The suspects fled the scene empty-handed.

Police are particularly appealing for information about CCTV which may have captured the incident, or any eyewitnesses.

If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance: Elizabeth Parry on [elizabeth.parry@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:elizabeth.parry@northyorkshire.police.uk)

Please quote reference 12260153117 when providing information.
