VIDEO footage has been discovered that suggests someone may have been a potential victim of indecent exposure on a riverbank near York.

People who have walked their dogs along Tadcaster riverbank at any point in July are being urged to contact police.

The footage was uncovered during a separate investigation and suggests that a woman or girl walking a dog along Tadcaster riverbank in July may have been a victim, without realising it, said a North Yorkshire Police spokesperson.

The appeal is specifically directed at those who have walked along the path between Tadcaster Bridge and Tadcaster viaduct.

A police spokesperson said: "It is really important that we trace the victim and any other witnesses who may be able to assist our investigation.

"If you have used the footpath in the past two months or if you have witnessed indecent exposure or sexual acts, but have not reported them to the police, please come forward as soon as possible."

A man has been arrested in connection with the incident and remains under investigation.

He is due to appear in court in relation to other matters.

Indecent exposure is a serious crime, and the police urge anyone who experiences it to report it by calling 999 or, if the incident has passed, via their website or by calling 101.

Anyone with information can contact the police by emailing [lucy.wise@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:lucy.wise@northyorkshire.police.uk), using the 'Tell us about' section on their website, or calling 101, quoting reference number 12260116388.

Information can also be reported anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.
