Rosario Dawson has spoken out about her filmed cameo in *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* that didn’t make the final cut. 

The *Star Wars: Ahsoka* actor, 47, was originally expected to appear in the latest Marvel blockbuster as her*Daredevil* character Claire Temple/Night Nurse. But her scene was ultimately “cut out,” she revealed last month at GalaxyCon Raleigh 2026.

“I told my mom, and she was like, ‘That sucks. Now I can’t post the pictures,’ and I was like, ‘Right, mom, me being cut out of the movie that has like the largest viewership of a trailer ever, the issue is you can’t post your BTS photos,’” Dawson joked, according to *The Wrap*.

“When I tell you *my* post was already ready with my BTS, with like my grandson wearing all his Spider-Man stuff because he loves Spider-Man — then I got the email done,” she explained. “So yeah. I’m a reject of*Spider-Man.*”

Dawson starred as Claire Temple, a crucial medical ally and romantic interest to Charlie Cox’s superhero Matt Murdoch/Daredevil, in the three-season superhero drama *Daredevil*, which aired from 2015 to 2018. Daredevil and Spider-Man famously exist in the same live-action universe, with Cox even making a cameo in* Spider-Man: No Way Home* (2021).

![Zendaya and Tom Holland reprise their roles as MJ and Peter Parker in ‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day’](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/07/28/12/28/Spider-Man_-Brand-New-Day-Image-6-Only-In-Cinemas-July-29.jpeg)

![Rosario Dawson filmed a cameo scene for 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' that ultimately got cut due to time constraints](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/11/12/43/Rosario-Dawson.jpeg)

“I would love — I still, because everybody’s in it, it still means that my character is somewhere in that same universe, and I really love that,” Dawson said. “So I hope I get to be Claire again.”

The deleted *Brand New Day* scene showed Claire helping Tom Holland’s Peter Parker/Spider-Man into the hospital after his fight against Scorpion (Michael Mando), according to*The Wrap*. 

Screenwriters Chris McKenna and Erik Sommers previously confirmed to the outlet that Dawson’s scene was likely removed due to runtime constraints.

“Rosario Dawson is great. I wasn’t there for those discussions. But it was great. If anything, you have a movie that is two-and-a-half hours long, and it’s almost an embarrassment of riches with so many great characters and so many great scenes,” McKenna said.

![‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day’ is out now in theaters](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/02/16/Film_Review_-_Spider-Man__Brand_New_Day_41746.jpg)

“The scene they did was a good scene; I think the only reason it’s not in the final cut was just it was probably cut for time because it was very good,” Sommers added.

*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* marks Holland’s fourth solo outing as the web-slinging superhero, alongside Zendaya. The new film, which also stars Sadie Sink, Jon Bernthal and Jacob Batalon, is both a critical and massive box-office success, having earned $1.6 million globally in its second week. 

“This is the best Spider-Man movie in more than 20 years,” *The Independent*’s Clarisse Loughrey boldly proclaimed in a four-star review.
