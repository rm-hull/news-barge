A four-bedroom house in Osbaldwick has been put on the market with an asking price of more than £750,000.

The property, called The Bungalow & Paddock, in Bad Bargain Lane, is a link-detached house that comes with a two-acre paddock.

The house, which estate agent Churchills said is "uniquely located within York's ring road", also has a detached studio and brick outbuildings.

It is described as a lifestyle property that would suit a range of buyers.

The house has an entrance hallway, an 18ft dual-aspect kitchen/dining room, a lounge with a bay window, a utility room/WC, a ground-floor double bedroom, a four-piece house bathroom, a first-floor landing, and three further double bedrooms.

The detached 19ft external studio has power, lighting, and bi-folding doors.

The garden area includes a driveway with lots of parking, a small orchard, and a two-acre grass paddock with mature trees and timber storage sheds.

There is also an external studio with power, lighting, and bi-folding doors.

Churchills said an overage payment will be applicable in the event of any future development of the paddock.

The entrance hallway has an entrance door, a double-glazed window to the front, stairs with a spindle balustrade to the first floor, a double radiator, real wood flooring, power points, and a door to the rear courtyard.

The kitchen/dining room has a dining area with double-glazed French doors to the front, tiled flooring, power points, and an opening to the kitchen, which has French doors to the side.

It also includes wooden Shaker kitchen units with a stainless steel sink with mixer tap, a window to the front, space and plumbing for appliances, tiled flooring, and power points.

The house has an EPC rating of E and is in council tax band E.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact Churchills on 01904 409429.
