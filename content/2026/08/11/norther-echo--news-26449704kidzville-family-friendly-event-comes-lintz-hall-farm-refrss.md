A County Durham attraction famed for its Halloween scares is expanding its family-friendly offering with a one-day event packed with live entertainment, interactive activities and spooky fun for children.

Kidzville will take place on October 29, at Lintz Hall Farm, the home of the popular Psycho Path and Family Fright Fest.

The event is part of a new collaboration with Let’s Party Events.

  County Durham attraction Lintz Hall Farm, home to the popular Psycho Path and Family Fright Fest events, is launching a new family-focused Halloween experience this autumn. 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Olivia Shaftoe, director of Let’s Party Events, said: "Let’s Party Events has more a decade of experience in the events industry, specialising in family entertainment.

"With Lintz Hall Farm having a fantastic track record and great location, we believe we have found the perfect partner to launch Kidzville this October.

"Planning and preparation is well underway and we can’t wait to show everyone what we’ve been working on."

Kidzville is designed for children and families and will run from 11am to 4pm.

The day will include five stage shows featuring musical performances, a K-pop tribute, and the Techno Ravers sensory experience, which remixes nursery rhymes and party classics with techno beats.

Activities will also include creative stations, face painting, fairground rides, and street food.

Each child will receive an LED glow stick and can meet costumed characters around the site.

They’ll also have access to the Creative Cove activity zone and the Cosy Cauldron Lair.

Fancy dress is encouraged, with prizes for the best costumes.

Nigel Holliday, co-director of Psycho Path and Family Fright Fest, said: "We are always trying to up the offer at Lintz Hall Farm and we’re delighted to be partnering with Let’s Party Events on this very special and unique day.

"It’s a great option for parents who want to keep their youngsters entertained during half term and we know it will be something nobody wants to miss."

Anyone interested can sign up for pre-sale at [https://wp3.fatsomasites.com/kidzville/contact/](https://wp3.fatsomasites.com/kidzville/contact/) which closes at midnight on August 11.
