# Colombia earthquake death toll rises to 164 with more casualties expected

- At least 164 people have been killed and hundreds injured after a 7.4-magnitude earthquake struck western Colombia.
- The tremor hit near San José del Palmar on Monday morning, causing widespread building collapses, including a major hospital in Cali.
- Newly sworn-in President Abelardo De La Espriella declared a state of emergency to expedite recovery funding as rescue efforts continue for trapped residents.
- The UK Foreign Office advised against all but essential travel to parts of Colombia following the closure of six regional airports.
- Authorities expect the death toll to rise, with the highest number of casualties currently reported in the western province of Risaralda.
