British Transport Police officers, investigating an assault on a train travelling from London to Scotland via York, are appealing for witnesses.

At around 11.20am on Sunday 2 August, a woman was punched several times and kicked by a male.

Several people came to the woman’s aid as the male fled to another part of the train.

The incident happened on a London King’s Cross to York and Aberdeen service, whilst the train was travelling between Newark Northgate and Doncaster.

The suspect is described as white, around 15-20 years-old, of slim build with short brown hair. He was wearing a light blue baseball cap, black jacket, white t-shirt with black writing on the front and black shorts.

Anyone who witnessed the incident should come forward and speak to British Transport Police.

Anyone with information is asked to text BTP on 61016 or call 0800 40 50 40, quoting reference 230 of 2 August.

Information can be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
