*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Police are appealing for information following an incident of criminal damage in a popular supermarket car park.

The incident took place on Saturday, June 4, at around 4.50pm at Tesco in Thirsk, where damage was caused to a vehicle.

Officers believe that the individual pictured in the CCTV image may have information that could help with the investigation.

  


Anyone who recognises the person or has information that could assist the investigation is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police.

People are encouraged to use the force’s online services in the first instance, including the 'Tell us about' form on their website.

Those unable to use the online services can call 101, quoting reference number 12260125800.
