A shop in York city centre was hit by flooding last night (Monday).

Firefighters from York were called to the incident on **Goodramgate** at**8.15pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “York crews assisted a business with flooding into the shop caused by faulty drainage from flat above.

#### Goodramgate, York

“Crew isolated leak and water supply and gave advice to the tenants.”
