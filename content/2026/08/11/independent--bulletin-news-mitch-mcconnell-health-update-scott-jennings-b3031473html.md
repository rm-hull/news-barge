# CNN pundit reveals how Mitch McConnell is doing after rehab

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/gTyNwdCp/poster.jpg?width=720)

- CNN contributor Scott Jennings reported that Senator Mitch McConnell is recovering at home following his release from a rehabilitation facility.
- Jennings stated that McConnell is doing better after a fall in early June, adding that the senator remains intensely private about his personal healthcare.
- McConnell confirmed he is undergoing an intensive physical therapy regimen at home while continuing to manage Senate business with his staff.
- The senator aims to return to Capitol Hill when the Senate reconvenes from its August recess on September 14.
- McConnell, Kentucky's longest-serving senator, is not seeking reelection and will retire at the conclusion of his term in January.
