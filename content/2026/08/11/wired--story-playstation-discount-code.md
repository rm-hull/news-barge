## Unlock 80% Off the Latest PS5 Games

The simplest way to save money on PlayStation games right now is by shopping from the Summer Sale. It ends on August 12, but if you can shop now, titles are up to 75% off right now. That includes best sellers like Baldur’s Gate 3 (down to $49), Ghost of Yotei (down to $50), or Forza 5, which 50 percent off at just $30.

The deepest discounts are in the “Games Under 20” section, where some older titles are down to as little as just $6. So, if there are some older games you’ve been meaning to catch up on, now’s the time to buy. Additionally, if you’re a PlayStation Plus member, you save an additional 5% off purchases, which can really bring the prices down even further—no PlayStation discount code needed.

## How to Redeem a $100 PlayStation Discount Code

You probably didn’t realize PlayStation had its own Visa Credit Card, but if you’re a hardcore fan, it can offer some really impressive perks. If you sign up and spend $500 within the first 60 days, you immediately get $100 back in the form of a statement credit applied directly to your account.

The PlayStation Visa Credit Card has no annual fees and comes with a variety of gamer perks. These include a 12-month membership to PlayStation Plus if you spend at least $6,000 within a calendar year and 5% back in points when you shop at the PlayStation Store or Sony Store. There’s also some more standard perks such as pay-per-use roadside assistance and emergency card replacement. Plus, you can save $100 with this credit card without needing a PlayStation promo code.

## The PlayStation Store Coupon Membership Explained

Speaking of becoming a member, that’s another easy way to get PS5 game discounts without a PlayStation discount coupon. There are three tiers to choose from, starting at $11 per month for the “Essential” tier. Being subscribed to this tier (or any of the higher ones) gets you access to loads of freebies, such as free exclusive add-ons, in-game items, or content packs for some of your favorite games. Check out all the available exclusive content to see what you’re in for.

Being subscribed to PlayStation Plus also grants you access to exclusive discounts on games, which are new each month. PlayStation Plus members can receive up to an additional 30% off on both PS5 or PS4 titles with these PlayStation deals . There’s a lot more content here than you probably even realize.

## The Best Ways to Turn PlayStation Gift Cards Into Real Savings

As I mentioned above, finding discounts on a PS5 isn’t easy. But one way to save on a new console is by using a PlayStation gift card. You can save up to $100 on PlayStation by applying your accumulated gift card balance toward one of these items—so long as you are buying through an official channel.

Unfortunately, PlayStation Store gift cards can only be used on digital products at PlayStation Store. Physical products from PlayStation Direct and merchandise from PlayStation Gear can’t be purchased with a gift card.

## Refurbished PlayStation Deals: Save Up to $100

One of the only ways to actually get a deal on a PlayStation is by buying a certified model. You don’t want to just go and buy through any retailer though, as some have different certifications and define the term “refurbished” differently. Fortunately, Sony has its certified refurbished program to get you PS5 discounts that you won’t regret buying later. These undergo a rigorous recertification process to ensure that they function as well as a brand new PlayStation. In other words, they should work like they’re new.

This includes up to $100 off consoles, such as the PlayStation 5 Digital Edition or the PlayStation 5 Slim. Sony also has refurbished DualShock controllers for sale.
