President Donald Trump was secretly transported away from Turkey in an extraordinary plan triggered by an assassination threat from Iran, according to a report.

Air Force One, a catering truck and a secondary military plane were all part of an elaborate “deception operation” to safeguard the commander-in-chief when he departed last month’s Nato summit in Ankara, *The Washington Post* has revealed. 

Journalists and several White House personnel believed they were traveling with Trump on Air Force Once for his return journey to Washington, D.C, following the annual gathering of alliance leaders. Instead, *The Post* reports, Trump was smuggled out of the presidential plane and put on board another aircraft .

The newspaper based its account on reviewed documentation, a U.S. official aware of the operation and another individual familiar with the president’s travel arrangements.

Trump had originally arrived in Turkey on a newly-retrofitted red, white and navy blue jet presented as a gift from Qatar. Prior to leaving, however, he announced he would complete the first leg of his journey home on an older, baby-blue Air Force One aircraft.

![The ‘deception operation’ to remove President Trump was implemented after a threat against his life was made by Iran](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/11/7/18/Trump-1ac31e95.jpeg)

At the time, the president explained that the luxury aircraft was departing Ankara early so that military personnel stationed in eastern England could view the updated Air Force One.

The president was filmed by television crews boarding the older jumbo jet in Ankara before it set off for the Mildenhall Royal Air Force base in Suffolk.

But shortly after stepping onto the legacy Air Force One, Trump was quietly transferred to a smaller Air Force C-32A plane. He was moved using a catering truck usually employed for loading inflight meals, before flying to Mildenhall in the smaller aircraft.

The White House did not directly address enquiries about the clandestine operation to transfer Trump onto a third plane or the scheme designed to make it look as though he remained aboard the baby-blue Air Force One bound for Mildenhall.

“As the president has said recently, there are many enemies of America who have their sights on him, and we use every tool at our disposal to address those threats,” White House Communication Director Steven Cheung said in a statement.

![The catering truck used to transport President Trump to the smaller plane in which he secretly left Turkey is seen parked next to the legacy Air Force One at Ankara Esenboga Airport on July 8](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/11/7/46/Trump-plane-0aabef4f.jpeg)

The Secret Service declined to comment while the Air Force, which operates the executive transport fleet, directed enquiries to the White House.

During Trump’s stay in Turkey, the U.S. military carried out significant strikes inside Iran following attacks on commercial shipping in the surrounding region.

Intelligence officials had previously warned of a possible attack targeting the president or his aircraft, as reported last month by *The New York Times* and CBS News. Those concerns prompted heightened security measures and the decision not to fly the newly-gifted Qatari jet for the initial leg of Trump’s return from Ankara.

The switch raised doubts concerning the safety of the new plane, which the president later noted would undergo further security enhancements.

On Monday, Cheung insisted that the Qatari plane remained “a state-of-the-art aircraft that has been fitted with high-level security protocols that ensure the safety of the president and his staff.”

The revelations regarding Trump’s covert transfer to a third plane highlight the severity with which U.S. security officials view potential threats against him amid conflict with Iran.

Paul Eckloff, a former Secret Service agent, remarked that such a tactic could indicate specific intelligence or extreme caution amid regional instability involving conflicts in Iran and Ukraine.

“I would not say unprecedented, but it is an unusual occurrence,” Eckloff said.

To leave the aircraft unnoticed, Trump and a small group of aides boarded the hydraulic catering vehicle, which had been raised to a door on the far side of Air Force One, according to the *Post*.

The traveling press corps, unaware of the manoeuvre, was asked to pull down its window blinds prior to departure.

Upon landing at Mildenhall, photographers captured Trump disembarking the primary presidential plane, indicating he had re-boarded Air Force One away from the reporters.

![Trump speaking at the Nato summit in Ankara](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/13/15/42/GettyImages-2285207438.jpeg)

Connecticut Democratic Sen. Richard Blumenthal demanded a congressional inquiry into the matter.

“It is unprecedented and it’s surreal,” he stated on CNN Monday, describing the event as “downright scary.”

Following the short stop at the British airbase, Trump boarded the newly-gifted Air Force One jet to complete his journey to Washington.

Speaking to reporters on board, Trump was asked directly whether there were credible Iranian threats against Air Force One.

“Well, I have a threat all the time. I’m number one on their list before you,” he replied.

He then told the accompanying press: “But if I go, you go.”
