# Trump warns FIFA it faces financial ruin if officials push Infantino out

![Infantino (L), delivers remarks alongside (L-R) U.S. President Donald Trump, Secretary of State Marco Rubio and FIFA Senior Advisor for Global Strategy and Governance Carlos Cordeiro, during a meeting of the White House Task Force on the FIFA World Cup 2026](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/31/9/31/GettyImages-2247183562.jpeg)

- President Donald Trump warned FIFA that replacing Gianni Infantino as president would be a terrible mistake that would harm the organization's future profitability.
- Infantino faces mounting pressure to step down following a controversial proposal to sell a 20 percent stake in FIFA tournaments to private investors.
- Football governing bodies UEFA, AFC, and Concacaf accused Infantino of deception and called for an independent review into his commercial scheme.
- Multiple confederations have held preliminary talks about organizing independent competitions to insulate themselves from the ongoing FIFA crisis.
- Several member associations, including those from England and Wales, have officially withdrawn their support for Infantino's 2027 re-election bid.
