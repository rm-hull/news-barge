# Experts discuss whether Ted Lasso would be a good coach in real life

- Apple TV sports comedy-drama *Ted Lasso* has returned for a fourth season, with the fictional manager taking on the challenge of coaching a second-division women's football team.
- Ted's empathetic approach reflects autonomy-supportive coaching, a real-world model where managers give athletes ownership, clear communication, and strong relational support.
- Research involving thousands of participants indicates that autonomy-supportive coaching improves athlete motivation, wellbeing, and competition performance.
- Sports psychology shows that high support and high expectations are compatible, requiring coaches to balance empathy and trust with accountability and clear standards.
- The character's success demonstrates how building a culture of trust through small, consistent interactions can effectively transform team dynamics and overall performance.
