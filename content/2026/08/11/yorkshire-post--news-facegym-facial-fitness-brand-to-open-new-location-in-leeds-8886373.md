# Facegym: 'Facial fitness' brand to open new location in Leeds

## Victoria Leeds has announced that ‘facial fitness’ brand Facegym has signed to take up a space in the Victoria Gate shopping centre, marking the brand’s second standalone studio in the UK outside of London.

The 1,772 sq ft studio will offer clients non-invasive facial workouts, combining massage techniques and skincare designed to “lift, sculpt and strengthen the skin”.

Angelo Castello, CEO at Facegym, said: "We're incredibly excited to bring Facegym to Leeds and to open in the beautiful Victoria Gate.

"The city has a thriving beauty, wellness, and retail scene, making it the perfect location for our next studio.
