Soap star Christopher Coghill has revealed he was once hit over the head with a handbag by a viewer.

It came during his time on EastEnders when he played paedophile Tony King, and a viewer was appalled at his on-screen antics in 2008.

The actor, who now plays Kev Barton in Emmerdale, describes his character as "a sympathetic psychopath, if you will," adding he is not concerned about being cast as a villain after playing Tony King.


## Emmerdale's Christopher Coghill recalls being hit over the head with a handbag by soap fan

He said: "You’ve got to give people a bit of credit.

"When I started in EastEnders, there were people who were like, ‘You know, there are people out there who really can’t tell the difference between what they see on telly and real life, and because of the character you’re playing, you’re going to get a lot of s***, and you might need police protection.'

"And then you go out, and people go, ‘Oh, you’re brilliant in EastEnders’, and that was kind of it.

"I did get hit over the head with a handbag in Selfridges, but apart from that…"

His Emmerdale co-star William Ash, who plays Caleb Miligan, said he has had a similar experience, adding: "People are genuinely generally really nice.

"You don’t get any grief, really.

"I did have one woman at the petrol station going ‘oh, you’re right horrible to Moira’."

## The saddest plots in Emmerdale


The pair, who have previously acted together in the 2003 drama Burn It and played brothers in both the 2006 one-off comedy Magnolia and a 2016 episode of Moving On, are currently caught up in an Emmerdale storyline involving an attack on Billy Fletcher (Jay Kontzle) at the depot.

Kev is the guilty party, and Caleb has the assault on CCTV, but has been covering it up so he can frame his arch-nemesis, Joe Tate (Ned Porteous).

Coghill said of the footage: "I hold my hands up, it does look bad – I am bent over him with a crowbar and he’s in a pool of blood."

However, it may not be the bargaining tool that Caleb thinks it is.

Ash said: "I think [Caleb and wife Ruby, played by Beth Cordingly] just don’t know how to handle him, like most people in the village.

"It’s like he’s such a loose cannon.

"You just don’t know what you’re going to get from him."


## Christopher Coghill hopes Kev Barton has no more singing scenes

Coghill is also adamant he doesn't want his character to have any more singing scenes.

It comes after his character Kev serenaded his estranged husband Robert (Ryan Hawley) with If You’re Not The One by Daniel Bedingfield and then tried to express his feelings to long-lost son Lewis (Bradley Riches) with a version of Boyzone’s Father And Son.

Coghill said: "Do you know what, [producer] Laura Shaw did say to me the other week, do you want to do All I Want For Christmas Is You?

"And I said, if you keep me till Christmas 2029, I’ll do it then.

"That’s how my contract negotiations are going.
