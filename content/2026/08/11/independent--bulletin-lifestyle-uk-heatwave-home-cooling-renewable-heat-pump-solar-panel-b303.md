# How renewable energy can help keep your home cool in a heatwave

![How to stay cool at home with renewable technology, according to experts](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/11/10/01KZQPH3B2C38NJNS6K2QDBH7M.jpg)

- Sustainability expert Simon Bones reports a dramatic surge in homeowner interest in renewable cooling solutions during summer heatwaves.
- Air-to-water heat pumps integrated with underfloor heating can provide mild cooling by circulating cooler water through heating circuits.
- Pairing solar panels with home batteries allows homeowners to generate and store electricity, significantly lowering the cost and carbon footprint of active cooling.
- Proper loft insulation is highlighted as an affordable, high-impact solution to prevent extreme attic heat from penetrating living spaces.
- High-performance triple glazing reduces solar heat gain through windows in the summer while providing superior insulation during the winter.
