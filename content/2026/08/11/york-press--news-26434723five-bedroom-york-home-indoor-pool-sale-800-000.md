## Take a look inside the £800,000 York home with indoor pool and separate cottage

A five-bedroom detached house with an indoor swimming pool and an additional cottage is on sale in York for £800,000 *(Image: Ashtons)*

A five-bedroom detached house with an indoor swimming pool and an additional three-bedroom cottage is for sale in York.

The property, at Grimston Bar, is listed for offers over £800,000 and includes 4,836 square feet of internal space across the main house and separate cottage.

According to the listing, the main house features four reception rooms with solid wooden floors.

Two rooms are located at the front, with one currently used as a games room and another as a living room.

A drawing room has exposed beams, a log burner, and direct access to a private garden.

The kitchen is described as country-style and positioned to overlook the surrounding countryside.

A dining room sits centrally within the house, and a utility room plus a separate WC are situated beside the kitchen.

An indoor swimming pool suite is present and heated via solar panels and a heat pump.

The suite also contains a sunroom, shower, changing facilities, and another private garden.

Five bedrooms are situated on the first floor of the main residence.

The primary bedroom includes a vaulted ceiling, dressing room, en-suite bathroom, and views across the rear garden and nearby fields.

A guest suite is also located on this floor, featuring an en-suite bathroom and access to both the front and side aspects of the home.

A detached three-bedroom cottage stands within the grounds.

The cottage is described as suitable for use as a holiday let, guest accommodation, or workspace.

It offers a downstairs shower room and WC, log burner, as well as an upstairs bathroom.

Outside, the property consists of two private gardens, a driveway, and a double garage with an attached workshop.

The listing states the location offers easy access to York, the Park and Ride, A64 ring road, and local amenities, and has an EPC rating of D.
