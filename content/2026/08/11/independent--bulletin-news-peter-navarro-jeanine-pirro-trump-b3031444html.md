# ‘Never mention her’: Trump advisor erupts over Jeanine Pirro

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/mZrx7oPe/poster.jpg?width=720)

- Trade adviser Peter Navarro criticized U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro for refusing to fire prosecutor John Crabb, who was involved in Navarro's contempt of Congress prosecution.
- Pirro faces mounting pressure and a potential firing from President Donald Trump after dropping charges against an ex-Olympian accused of damaging the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool.
- Court documents revealed the Reflecting Pool damage was likely caused by a poor installation process during renovations rather than intentional vandalism.
- Trump publicly berated Pirro over her decision, claiming she made a terrible mistake and “folded like an umbrella.”
- Navarro's anger stems from his four-month prison sentence for defying a congressional subpoena related to the January 6 investigation.
