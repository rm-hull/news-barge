Emergency services were called to a collision in Selby yesterday evening (Monday).

A delivery van crashed on **Flaxley Road** at**5.10pm**.

Selby firefighters went to the incident. A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The van had flipped over and the driver was out of vehicle on arrival of crews.

#### Flaxley Road, Selby

“The driver was treated by paramedics at the scene for a minor head injury. Crews made vehicle safe and assisted with first aid.”
