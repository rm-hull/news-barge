**A forest school set up by singer Charlotte Church on a former pig farm might have to close because of environmental fears.**

The Awen Project opened in 2024 without planning permission in Wenvoe, Vale of Glamorgan.

The singer, who appeared on series one of Celebrity Traitors, applied for retrospective permission **in October 2025**, but that was refused.

The council raised concerns over the noise, impact on residents, and dangers to pedestrians because of insufficient parking. It also said the project failed to show how it would "conserve and enhance green infrastructure".

Church refused to comment, but has appealed to the Welsh government's Planning and Environment Decisions Wales (PEDW).

The Awen Project aims to give children an environment "where they feel championed to be themselves and follow their passions as active participants in a democratic community".

The school is free and accepts donations.

It was originally based in Cwm George and Casehill Woods, near Dinas Powys, with permission from the Woodland Trust.

But, in 2023, the charity revoked the license agreement because of "multiple breaches" of the permission granted which "posed health and safety concerns" for "visitors and wildlife".

In response, the project posted on social media that it had given its "best efforts to salvage" the partnership.

A council spokesperson said the application was refused because of "concerns regarding the sustainability" of the site's location and the "potential impact on neighbouring residents was another consideration".

There was also "insufficient supporting evidence to satisfy concerns around the potential impact of the proposal on biodiversity and the natural environment", the council said.

PEDW confirmed it had received the appeal, but said no date has been fixed for the hearing.
