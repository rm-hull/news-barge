# How many public appearances has Melania Trump made this year?

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/fzIpWxhs/poster.jpg?width=720)

- First Lady Melania Trump has made only 38 public appearances in 2026, according to an analysis of her schedule by *The Daily Beast* .
- Her most recent public engagement was on 19 July, when she attended the World Cup final alongside President Donald Trump.
- She was noticeably absent from several key events in July, including Senator Lindsey Graham's funeral and the rescheduled White House Correspondents' Dinner.
- Trump has delivered public remarks just 29 times since her husband returned to office, significantly fewer than her predecessors Michelle Obama and Jill Biden.
- Approximately 80 per cent of her appearances this year have taken place in Washington, DC, following a brief surge in activity to promote her Amazon documentary.
