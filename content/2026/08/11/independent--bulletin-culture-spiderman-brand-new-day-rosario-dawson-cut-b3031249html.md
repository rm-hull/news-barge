### Rosario Dawson breaks silence on getting cut from Spider-Man: Brand New Day

# Rosario Dawson tells of disappointment at being cut from Spider-Man: Brand New Day

- Rosario Dawson revealed her filmed cameo as her Daredevil character, Claire Temple, was cut from *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* .
- The deleted scene featured Claire assisting Peter Parker into hospital following a fight against Scorpion.
- Screenwriters Chris McKenna and Erik Sommers confirmed the scene was likely removed due to runtime constraints.
- According to The Wrap, she joked: “I told my mom, and she was like, ‘That sucks. Now I can’t post the pictures,’ and I was like, ‘Right, mom, me being cut out of the movie that has like the largest viewership of a trailer ever, the issue is you can’t post your BTS photos.”
- Dawson originally played the character in the live-action series *Daredevil* from 2015 to 2018.
