A child was showered with glass after a balaclava-clad suspect threw a brick through a house window.

The downstairs window of the house on Jameson Road in Hartlepool was smashed by the brick on Friday (August 7), around 3.45pm.

The same suspect then slashed a tyre and threw paint over a campervan parked outside the house.

Cleveland Police are now seeking information about the incident.

Any witnesses or those with footage are urged to call 101, quoting reference SE26156885.
