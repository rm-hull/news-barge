# Tottenham boss Roberto De Zerbi gives exciting transfer update

- Tottenham Hotspur head coach Roberto De Zerbi has emphasised that rebuilding the squad's spirit, soul and mentality will be crucial to restoring the club's fortunes following successive 17th-placed Premier League finishes.
- Having guided the club to safety on the final day of last season after taking charge in March, De Zerbi stressed that summer recruitment alone is insufficient to compete with the top teams in the world.
- Spurs have already completed several key summer signings, including Sandro Tonali, Mateus Fernandes, and Andy Robertson, but De Zerbi gave an exciting update to the *Men in Blazers* YouTube channel as he confirmed the club’s transfer activity is “not finished yet”.
- The squad received a boost with defender Micky van de Ven signing a new long-term contract, although the future of fellow centre-back Cristian Romero remains uncertain amid rumours linking him with a move to Arsenal.
- Tottenham are set to open their upcoming Premier League campaign against Brentford on 22 August, with De Zerbi calling for his players to show character and commitment that supporters can readily identify.
