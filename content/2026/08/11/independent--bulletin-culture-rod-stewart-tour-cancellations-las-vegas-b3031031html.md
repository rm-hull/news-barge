# Rod Stewart cancels US gigs ahead of six-night Vegas residency

- Rod Stewart has cancelled two US tour dates in Ohio following an unforeseen but minor medical procedure.
- The 81-year-old singer postponed a concert in Cincinnati and cancelled his performance in Cleveland after doctors advised him to rest.
- Venue statements confirmed that Stewart is expected to make a prompt recovery and aims to return to the stage soon.
- The announcements follow previous show disruptions, including a June cancellation due to laryngitis and an incident where he nearly fainted on stage.
- Stewart is still scheduled to begin a six-night residency in Las Vegas later this month.
