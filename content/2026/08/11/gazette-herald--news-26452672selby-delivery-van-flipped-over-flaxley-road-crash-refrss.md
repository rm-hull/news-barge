A DELIVERY van has 'flipped over' in a crash on a main road in a North Yorkshire town.

The county's fire and rescue service said crews from Selby were called to Flaxley Road in the town around 5.10pm yesterday (Monday, August 10).


A service spokesperson said: "The van had flipped over, and the driver was out of the vehicle on arrival of crews.

"The driver was treated by paramedics at the scene for a minor head injury.

"Crews made vehicle safe and assisted with first aid."
