# Beloved YouTube star Ms Rachel to release debut record – here’s what we know

- YouTube star and music educator Ms Rachel and her husband, Aron Accurso, are releasing a debut 23-track album titled *I'm So Happy* on 25 September.
- Co-produced by Accurso alongside Grammy winner Scott Riesett, the album features a number of Ms Rachel’s fan favourites and childhood classics.
- Ms Rachel, whose full name is Rachel Griffin Accurso, said: "Music is such a wonderful way for people to bond, for parents to bond. I know from the minute my kids were born, we’re singing to them.”
- She added: "We just want the world to feel like it’s helpful to all children and the musical worlds as well."
- Tracks such as “I’m So Happy”, “Icky Sticky Bubble Gum”, “Hop Little Bunnies” and “I Could Be Your Friend” will feature on the album.
