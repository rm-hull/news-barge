This image has been released by North Yorkshire Police after an incident in Harrogate.

It follows an assault on Knaresborough Road in the town at about 5.10pm on 28 June.

Police say a female struck another person on the shoulder and threw a metal drink bottle at them.

#### Knaresborough Road, Harrogate

Officers want to hear from you if you recognise the person pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist their investigation.
