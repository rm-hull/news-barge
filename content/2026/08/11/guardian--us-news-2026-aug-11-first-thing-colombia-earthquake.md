Good morning. More than 130 people died after a powerful earthquake struck western Colombia, collapsing and severely damaging scores of buildings in main cities, including Cali and Pereira.

The US and Colombian geological surveys reported that a 7.4-magnitude earthquake struck at 7.34am local time on Monday, with its epicentre in the western department of Chocó. It was felt in neighbouring Venezuela, still reeling from a deadly double earthquake on 24 June. The quake killed at least 132 people and more than 570 were injured as of Monday afternoon, according to the Colombian Association of Capital Cities, which coordinates cities across the country.

- 
  **How has the government responded?** In a national address, Colombia’s president, Abelardo de la Espriella, said: “The national government has all of its capabilities deployed to protect lives, assist affected communities and deliver aid wherever it is needed. The first priority is rescuing the people trapped under the rubble. Colombia has a president whose heart aches for his people and who will do everything in his power to help them in the most difficult moments.”

## **Trump reportedly hid in catering container to board secret flightfrom Turkey amid Iran threat**

![A catering truck is parked near Air Force One](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/1689d23cc9c76b118263c1cdf1b005a6d7d5e279/624_0_7200_5760/master/7200.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Donald Trump left Turkey on a secret military flight to Britain last month when the White House said he was flying aboard Air Force One, in an extraordinary ruse – prompted by an Iranian assassination threat – that involved hiding the president in an airport catering container, according to reports.

The operation took place when Trump was in Ankara for a Nato summit with world leaders, the Washington Post reported. The president reportedly boarded the old Air Force One jumbo jet in view of television cameras, and was secretly shuttled minutes later to a smaller plane via an airport catering truck, typically used to load meals and other supplies preflight, a US official told the Post.

A US official told the Post that a credible threat to Trump involving Iran set the “deception operation” in motion. A similar operation took place in 2000, when Bill Clinton used an unmarked executive jet to fly into Pakistan while sending his formal Air Force One as a decoy. The Guardian has contacted the White House and the US defense department for comment.

## **Trump signs order attempting to override CDC vaccine schedule and break up MMR shots**

![President Donald Trump holds a signed executive order about vaccines.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/62964542da0ee36649985274d1979736f793bd8f/525_0_5230_4187/master/5230.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Donald Trump has signed an executive order with plans for sweeping changes to the childhood vaccination schedule that attempts to override the authority of the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). The CDC is the federal agency tasked with recommending vaccines, not the White House.

Both Trump and Robert F Kennedy, the health secretary, made repeated mentions of autism throughout the signing, emphasizing a scientifically unproven link between vaccines and the condition. Dozens of studies show no link between the two.

- 
  **What does the order seek to change?** The order would require that the measles, mumps and rubella vaccine be broken into three separate shots, once it becomes available in the US. It would recommend that all shots be given at separate medical visits, a frequent point Trump has made despite the safety, ease and lower costs of giving vaccines together.

## **Bernie Sanders calls on Silicon Valley to ‘pause AI development’ in interest of humanity**

![Bernie Sanders at podium.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/a0a85c92c66d043112ebdf2fc0fb7dde3a5b1fe7/431_0_4321_3456/master/4321.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Senator Bernie Sanders has called on Meta, OpenAI and Anthropic executives to halt their development of artificial intelligence, saying the US Senate would implement regulation if the companies continued deploying AI at their current pace. Sanders said the capabilities of these AI models had reached a critical risk threshold.

The Vermont senator cited recent reports that AI was used to develop new viruses, warning if this technology fell into the “wrong hands” it might “lead to new bioweapons that result in the deaths of tens of millions of people”. All three companies have disclosed their models hacked other companies’ servers, a sign that the firms have lost control, Sanders said.

- 
  **What has Mark Zuckerberg said about AI this week?** Sanders’s call comes as the Meta CEO, Mark Zuckerberg, presented a utopian vision of AI in a 6,000-word essay amid Silicon Valley debate over government regulation. Central to Zuckerberg’s post was an argument for free-to-download, open-weight AI models – such as the kind Meta develops – and a lack of government regulation.
- 
  **What has a new study suggested the impact of AImight beon carbon output?** AI-driven productivity gains will enable more planet-heating pollution from fossil fuels than they avoid by boosting energy from renewables, a study has found.
