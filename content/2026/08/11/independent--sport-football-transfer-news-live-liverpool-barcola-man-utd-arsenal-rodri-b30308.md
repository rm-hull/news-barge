Liverpool are hoping to swiftly add to the loan signing of Ronald Araujo by hashing out a deal for Paris Saint-Germain winger Bradley Barcola after reaching a reported agreement over personal terms. Barcola leads Liverpool’s list of transfer targets and the Reds are in talks with PSG over a fee, though the European champions are demanding £145m to sell the winger.

Arsenal’s busy transfer window looks set to continue as they move on from their disappointment in not signing Vinicius Jr. They are hunting alternatives in the forward line with Julian Alvarez and the aforementioned Barcola on their list but defensive reinforcements may yet come in the form of Cristian Romero. The Tottenham captain is rumoured to leave Spurs this summer and is eyeing up a switch to their closest rivals.

Meanwhile, Rodri looks set to leave Manchester City amid interest from Barcelona and Real Madrid. The World Cup winning captain is into the final year of his contract and favours a move to Barca – who are considering a second bid after an initial offer of £38m was rejected. City seem to be making preparations for that outcome as they close in on the signing of World Cup breakout star Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille.

Finally, Manchester United are still searching for a left-back with Newcastle’s Lewis Hall seemingly off the table they instead turned their interest on the likes of Antonee Robinson, Jorge Salinas and Joaquin Seys.

## Tottenham and Barcelona make Harry Kane enquiries

Barcelona and Tottenham have both made recent enquiries to Harry Kane over a summer transfer, according to *Teamtalk*.

However, *The Times* say the England captain is likely to extend his contract at Bayern Munich until 2029.

## Arsenal exits?

Arsenal have welcomed Bruno Guimaraes through the door but that big arrival could spark the exit a few names.

Guimaraes’ signing means **Martin Zubimendi**, who only joined last summer and had an impressive debut season, may see his minutes threatened, and* Football.London* say Real Madrid want to take the Spaniard off their hands.

Meanwhile, Guimaraes may have to quickly say goodbye to a couple of his Brazilian countryman, who could be Italy-bound.

*Gazzetta dello Sport* says**Gabriel Jesus** is open to a move to Napoli, while the*Mirror* report that talks have opened with Roma over a switch for**Gabriel Martinelli**.

West Ham are also in talks with Arsenal to sign **Reiss Nelson**, the 26-year-old English winger who spent the past two seasons on loan at Brentford and Fulham, according to* The Standard.*

## Man City close in on Rodri replacement?

Manchester City are closing in on the signing of Ayoubb Bouaddi from Lille, according to the *Guardian.*

The arrival of Morocco’s World Cup breakout star is expected to soften the blow of Rodri’s expected exit, with Barcelona increasingly confident of signing the midfielder. Ferran Torres’ move to PSG will finance the transfer.

Lille value Bouaddi at £85m.

## Micky van de Ven pens new long-term Tottenham deal

Tottenham defender Micky van de Ven has expressed enthusiasm over the club’s current trajectory following his decision to sign a new contract.

Reportedly securing a five-year extension, he was delighted to pledge his long-term future to the side, where head coach Roberto De Zerbi views him as a pivotal figure to build his team around.

“It’s a special moment to sign a new deal and a proud moment for me and my family,” Van de Ven said.

“I’ve always loved Spurs, from the first day I stepped in here. I love the club, I love the fans and I’ve developed really well here.

“It’s clear the direction the club wants to go in and I want to be a part of it. I’m excited about what’s to come.”

## Liverpool agree terms with Bradley Barcola

Liverpool have been given the “green light” from Bradley Barcola on personal terms regarding a potential move from PSG, according to *Fabrizio Romano.*

It acts as a step forward in Liverpool’s pursuit of the Frenchman as the clubs remain in talks over a fee.

However, there is still quite the gap between valuations with PSG wanting £145m.

## Liverpool given boost in defence as Barcelona star Ronald Araujo joins on loan

The deal gives Liverpool defensive reinforcements and an option to purchase the Uruguay international for a pre-agreed price of £47m.

Liverpool will not pay a loan fee and will cover the centre-back’s salary for the length of his time at Anfield, but Araujo’s eagerness to join them was such that he took a pay cut.

The 27-year-old, who passed a medical on Merseyside on Monday, is available straight away and will join in team training later this week.
