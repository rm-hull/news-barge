# Child’s body found at Essex home after social services report

![A man and a woman have been charged with preventing lawful burial, Essex Police said](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/04/10/13/27145204-a9c0739f-46f8-4d01-b559-0452833f2555.jpg)

- The body of a child was discovered at an address in Southend after social services raised welfare concerns with Essex Police.
- Sophie Dean, 31, and James Thorpe, 32, have been charged with preventing a lawful and decent burial of a dead body.
- James Thorpe faces an additional charge of obstructing a police constable in the execution of their duty.
- Both defendants appeared at Southend-on-Sea Magistrates' Court and were remanded in custody until their next appearance at Basildon Crown Court on 7 September.
- Detectives from the Crime and Public Protection Command are continuing their investigation and appealing for anyone with information to come forward.
