# Trump targets Democrats in escalating National Guard showdown

![President Donald Trump fumes at corporate donors who supported Center for American Progress](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/11/05/2026-08-10T194035Z_955614705_RC2VVMAP70Q8_RTRMADP_3_USA-TRUMP-VACCINES.JPG)

- President Donald Trump attacked corporate and foreign donors of the Center for American Progress after the think tank published a report criticizing his federal law enforcement surges.
- The Center for American Progress report found that deploying the National Guard to Washington, D.C., and other cities cost $1.7 billion but had no impact on crime rates.
- Trump labeled the findings a Radical Left SCAM on Truth Social and threatened legal action against the organization and its financial supporters.
- CAP representative Chandler Hall noted on C-SPAN that violent crime reductions in Washington predated the federal intervention, making the costly deployments unnecessary.
- Trump administration border czar Tom Homan defended the law enforcement surges and urged cities like Chicago to adopt similar federal measures.
