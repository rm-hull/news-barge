Motorists are being warned of increasing delays along a major road into York.

Heavy traffic has been reported from the Hopgrove Roundabout to Whinny Lane off the A64, with further delays up to Flaxton Moor.

According to reports, average speeds are 15mph.

  


Delays are up to eight minutes.

There are no other reported incidents on the road.
