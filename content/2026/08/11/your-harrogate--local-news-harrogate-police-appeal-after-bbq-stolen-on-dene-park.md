Police have released images of two men following a theft in the Harrogate area.

It happened on Dene Park at 9am on Saturday 11th July, and involved a stainless steel barbecue being stolen from a property.

It is believed that the suspects arrived and left the area in an Iveco van with a yellow bonnet.

A police spokesperson said:

"If you recognise the men pictured, please get in touch as they may have information that could assist our investigation."


Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police online, via live chat or by emailing Alastair Graham-Merrett at [Alastair.Graham-Merrett@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:Alastair.Graham-Merrett@northyorkshire.police.uk.) Alternatively, call 101.

*Please quote reference 12260131399 when providing information.*
