# Rosie O’Donnell was ‘nervous’ filming intimate scene with friend Cynthia Nixon

- Rosie O’Donnell has reflected on filming an intimate scene with long-time friend Cynthia Nixon for the third season of *And Just Like That* .
- Speaking on Amy Poehler’s *Good Hang* podcast, O’Donnell admitted she was horrifyingly nervous during the post-coital scene.
- Nixon described the filming as a little strange but praised O’Donnell for portraying her character with tenderness and excitement.
- O’Donnell revealed she accepted the guest role immediately after Nixon phoned her, despite having done only one other sex scene in her career.
- Highlighting their long-standing bond, O’Donnell lauded Nixon as one of the best actresses who actively stands up against injustice.
