## Barcelona increase Rodri offer

Barcelona are set to return with an improved offer for Rodri.

Manchester City rejected an opening bid of €45m (£38.7m) for their Ballon d’Or winner.

The 30-year-old is out of contract next summer and Real Madrid are also interested.

City have already spent £116m on a midfielder this summer, paying a record sum for a British player to buy Elliot Anderson from Nottingham Forest.

They are also interested in Morocco international Ayyoub Bouaddi but Lens could seek a package of around €100m for the teenager.

## Turki Alalshikh’s takeover of Derby County falls through

Saudi government official and boxing powerbroker Turki Alalshikh has abandoned his proposed takeover of Derby County.

Derby were in discussions with Alalshikh’s Lion Sport over the purchase of controlling interest in the Championship outfit, but talks will no longer proceed with the club now “off the market”.

The acquisition process had been sanctioned by both the Independent Football Regulator (IFR) and the EFL, notification of which Derby received last week.

But in a stark change of course, Derby confirmed on Monday: “Today the club received notice that Lion Sport no longer wanted to continue with the purchase.

“For the benefit of any doubt, the club is now off the market and will continue to operate under the loyal, committed and unwavering support of Clowes Developments.”

## Andy Robertson determined for Spurs not to flirt with relegation again

New Tottenham signing Andy Robertson is determined to help take Tottenham away from the drop zone that has taunted them for the last two seasons.

“Obviously, mentality is a big thing in football,” he said, speaking to SPURSPLAY.

“Since I’ve been at this club…the lads are so determined. They’ve got a real strong mentality.

“I think they all had to come together, especially in the last part (of last season) when the new manager came in.

“I think they’ve carried that into the summer, a determination to not get the club in that position again.

“It’s up to the new lads to come in and help with that. Hopefully I can be a big part of that and try and help bring a winning mentality here, just help drive the boys forward. Hopefully we can do that, then we’ll have a good season.”
