# Delta Goodrem says she is ‘honoured’ to be part of Strictly 2026

- Australian singer Delta Goodrem is a confirmed contestant on the 2026 series of *Strictly Come Dancing* .
- The 41-year-old admitted she has no prior experience with any of the dance routines featured on the BBC show.
- Speaking on This Morning to presenters Rochelle Humes and Andi Peters, Goodrem expressed that she felt honoured to join the line-up.
- She revealed that she is about to learn all the dances from scratch ahead of her autumn debut.
- The Eurovision star described her feelings about taking part in the competition as both terrified and excited.
