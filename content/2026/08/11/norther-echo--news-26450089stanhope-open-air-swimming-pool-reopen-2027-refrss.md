An outdoor swimming pool in County Durham could open next year following a renewed appeal for support.

Stanhope Open Air Swimming Pool has been closed since 2023 following an electrical failure.

A fundraiser was launched to bring the pool back into use earlier this year.

   

*(Image: STANHOPE SWIMMING POOL)*

The charity behind the pool has issued a fresh plea for help from businesses and volunteers.

Caroline Ford, chair of the pool’s board of trustees, said: "We are getting a really good response from the community, which is fabulous.

"We've had quite a lot of tradespeople offering their expertise free of charge, which has been great.

"But we need to get the big works done, like the electrics, and then we can start looking at the smaller bits.

To help with the fundraising effort, a group of swimmers from the Wear Valley are taking on a sponsored challenge.

They plan to swim the equivalent distance from Wolsingham to the pool—a total of 10 miles, or 804 lengths—in just two hours.

Steve Lumb, from the pool’s fundraising group, said: "The pool was built in 1974 with support from the local rural parish council, local businesses and tradespeople and also with a lot of support and fundraising from locals and also the multitude of caravan sites in the valley.

"It has been run as a charity ever since and over the years has hosted a variety of events.

"Unfortunately, it has been closed for over two years due to funding issues for repairs.

"Our first target is to raise £50,000, which should allow us to open the pool by 2027.

"Our longer term aims are to refurbish the changing rooms, open a café, have a sauna/hot tub.

"We have had a wealth of support in terms of tradespeople offering their services and funding."

   

*(Image: STANHOPE SWIMMING POOL)*
