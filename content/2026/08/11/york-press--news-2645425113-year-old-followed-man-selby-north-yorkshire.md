A 13-YEAR-OLD was followed by a man acting 'suspiciously' in a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after an incident that took place in Doncaster Road, Selby last month.

It happened at 3.30pm on Monday, July 20 and 'involved a man following the 13 year-old victim and acting suspiciously'.
