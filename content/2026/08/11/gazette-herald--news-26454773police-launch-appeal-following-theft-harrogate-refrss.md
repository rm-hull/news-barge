POLICE are searching for two men in connection with an alleged theft in North Yorkshire.

The incident involved the 'theft' of a stainless steel barbecue from Dene Park, Harrogate at 9am on Saturday (July 11).

It is believed that the suspects arrived and left the area in an Iveco van with a yellow bonnet.

  


If you recognise the men pictured, please contact North Yorkshire Police online or by emailing [alastair.graham-merrett@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:alastair.graham-merrett@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, you can call 101, quoting reference 12260131399 when providing information.
