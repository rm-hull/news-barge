Investigations are under way after a North Yorkshire building fire.

Crews from Ripon, Boroughbridge and Harrogate responded to reports of a building on fire in Ripon shortly after 5pm on Sunday (August 30).

  


A service spokesperson said: “On arrival crews found a building measuring approximately 20m by 10m well alight.

“Crews used four breathing apparatus and two hose reel jets to extinguish.

“A fire investigation is to be conducted in daylight hours.”
