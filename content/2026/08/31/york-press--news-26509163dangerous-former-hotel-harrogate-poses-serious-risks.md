## Police warning after children enter 'dangerous' abandoned North Yorkshire hotel

The abandoned Kimberley Hotel in Harrogate following the fire in 2023 *(Image: Harrogate Fire Station)*

Two children gained access to a dangerous abandoned building, prompting a police warning to parents.

Officers were called to the former Kimberley Hotel in Kings Road in Harrogate, where the pair had gained entry on Saturday (August 31).

Both children were safe, but North Yorkshire Police say the building poses serious hazards, including asbestos and large holes in the floor.

  


A force spokesperson said: "Unfortunately, this is not an isolated incident, and we are currently receiving reports of children gaining entry to the building on a regular basis.

"While exploring abandoned buildings may seem exciting, these locations can contain hidden hazards that pose a real threat to safety.

"What may appear to be harmless fun can quickly result in serious injury or exposure to dangerous substances."

  The fire completely damaged the hotel's annex. Police say the building poses serious risks to those entering, including asbestos and large holes in the floor 

*(Image: Harrogate Fire Station)*

In 2023, emergency services were called to the former hotel after a fire, which was treated as arson.

The spokesperson said the fire caused 100 per cent damage to the annex measuring 10 metres by 10 metres, and caused 5 per cent smoke damage to the main building.

The force has urged parents and carers to talk to their children about the risks of entering such sites.

North Yorkshire Police added: "We would ask parents and carers to speak with their children about the risks of entering vacant or derelict buildings and to ensure they know where they are spending their time during the school holidays."
