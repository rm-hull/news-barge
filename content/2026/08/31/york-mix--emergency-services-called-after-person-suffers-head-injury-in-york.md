Emergency services were called to an incident in Acomb, York, this afternoon (Monday).

Fire crews went to an address on **Kingsthorpe** at**12.39pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “York’s crew responded to reports of a casualty that had fallen within their property and had sustained a possible head injury.

#### **Kingsthorpe, York**

“Crews gained entry to the premises using an entry kit and crew power to move the casualty into a comfortable position.

“The incident was then handed over to a first responder on scene.”
