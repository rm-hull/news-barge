£15,000 for North Shields! Live Well with Cancer is a finalist in the Certas Energy Community Bloom Fund 📢

Run by and for people affected by cancer, this charity offers free wellness workshops, 1-to-1 care and support groups. Winning £15k will help finish renovating their hub into a comfort-filled safe haven where local families can connect and heal.

Voting is fast, free and open now! Vote before 8th September to bring £15k home to North Shields.
