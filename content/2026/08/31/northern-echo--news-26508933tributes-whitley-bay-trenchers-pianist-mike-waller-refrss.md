Tributes have been paid to much-loved Whitley Bay pianist Mike Waller, who was “part of the heart and soul” of the town’s Trenchers fish and chip restaurant.

Mr Waller, a familiar figure to generations of diners, regularly performed beneath the restaurant’s iconic dome and helped create an atmosphere that became central to the experience for thousands of visitors.

HIs death was announced by family on August 22, after a battle with prostate cancer.

Trenchers chief executive Kyle Mackings said staff had been deeply saddened by the news of his death.

He said: “Everyone at Trenchers was deeply saddened to hear of Mike’s passing.

“He wasn’t simply our pianist. Mike was part of the heart and soul of Trenchers and someone who helped create so many wonderful memories for our customers over the years.”

  “He touched countless lives through his music and will be remembered with enormous affection by everyone who had the privilege of knowing him," Trenchers boss Kyle Mackings said. 

*(Image: GOFFY MEDIA)*

Mr Waller was widely respected as a musician, composer and former music teacher and worked with 'Fog on the Tyne' hitmakers Lindisfarne throughout his career, writing the charity single 'Try Giving Everything' in 1985

Ray Jackson from Lindisfarne added: "So sorry to hear of Mike’s passing. We made some recordings together, and he had a great talent.

"He was instrumental in writing the Geordie contribution to famine in Africa called “Try Giving Everything” featuring Brian Johnson, John Miles Alan Hull and myself along with local TV celebrities like Mike Neville to name but a few. A good man."


Mr Mackings of Trenchers said it had been “heartwarming” to see the strength of feeling expressed in reaction to his death.

He said: “The response tells you everything you need to know about Mike and the impact he had on people.

“So many customers felt as though he was a friend, even if they only saw him while dining with us.

“That feeling of familiarity and friendship became part of the atmosphere at Trenchers.

“Mike wasn’t just playing the piano in the background.

“He was helping to create memories and special moments for families and friends celebrating birthdays, anniversaries and other occasions with us.

“Nothing was ever a problem to him and it genuinely gave him a thrill to add his own magical musical touch to our guests’ celebrations.

“He made every session with us feel like a real occasion, and that is a real skill.
