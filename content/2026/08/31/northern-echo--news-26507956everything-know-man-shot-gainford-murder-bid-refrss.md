A man has been left in a critical condition after being shot on a 'quiet' County Durham street in an "drive-by murder bid".

Emergency services were called to Balmer Hill in Gainford at 8.10pm on Saturday (August 29) after gunshots were fired at the home.

A 46-year-old man is in hospital in a 'critical, but stable' condition with birdshot injuries to his abdomen.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A 20-year-old man and a 16-year-old boy were arrested on suspicion of attempted murder, and both remain in custody.

The boy has also been arrested on suspicion of theft, failing to stop and driving without a licence, police said.

One resident described the scene as "chaos" as a fleet of police arrived on blues and twos.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A cordon remained in place on Sunday (August 30), with neighbours saying they had been told police could remain until Tuesday.

CSI teams in white overalls were working inside and outside the home, filling large brown evidence bags.

Pictures show holes in the door where shots were fired, with the front window smashed through and shards of glass strewn across the ground on the driveway.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

## What neighbours said

"I was just sat watching the TV," they said. "hey were talking about Newcastle on Sky Sports News, then we heard like a pop.

"At first I thought it was a car backfiring. We jumped.

"And then there was another pop, and it was a case of 'What the heck was that?'

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

"Then the next thing we saw was a red soft-top car go down the road.

"After the pops, I looked along towards next door and we saw all the glass that was outside, and the front windows were gone.

"It is something you see in movies or television.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

"You don't expect to see it in a little village, in your own of street."

Another neighbour, Jenny, who has lived on the estate since 1973, said: “It’s a very quiet estate usually.

“You never get anything like this.”

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

## What emergency services said

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "An investigation has been launched after a man was shot in Gainford last night.

"Emergency services were called to an address in Balmer Hill just after 8.10pm following reports that suspects had driven to a house and fired at it from their car before making off.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

"A 46-year-old man suffered birdshot injuries to his abdomen and was taken to hospital. His condition has been described as critical but stable.

"No one else at the address was injured and officers remain at the scene today.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson added: "We were called to an incident at a private address in Gainford, Darlington at 8.10pm on Saturday, August 29.

"We dispatched one ambulance crew, one duty officer, one clinical team leader and were supported by Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS).

"One person was taken to hospital by road."

An investigation is ongoing and anyone with information is asked to call 101 quoting incident reference 479 of August 29.
