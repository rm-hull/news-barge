## Motorists warned of long delays up to 20 minutes on main Yorkshire roads

Motorists have been warned of heavy traffic and long delays on roads following the end of Leeds Festival this year *(Image: Newsquest)*

MOTORISTS are being warned of long delays following the end of Leeds Festival.

The tailbacks are particularly affecting Bramham Crossroads roundabout, the nearby A64 York Road and the A1 leading to Bramham in West Yorkshire.

Delays of up to 19 minutes, which are increasing, have been reported on the A1 Northbound in Wetherby.

  


The average speed is 20mph.

  The traffic is particularly affecting Bramham Crossroads roundabout, the nearby A64 York Road and the A1 leading to Bramham in West Yorkshire, along with roads leading to Wetherby and Tadcaster 

*(Image: AA Traffic)*

Heavy traffic on the A1 is also affecting the Wattle Skye Roundabout and areas around Boston Spa and the A64 close to Tadcaster.

AA Traffic said: “Long delays due to Leeds Festival, with closures at times until around 3pm today on A64 between Shadwell, then Scarcroft to Thorner on local roads and on the A64 York Road eastbound from Kiddal Lane End to the A1(M) J44 (York / Tadcaster).”
