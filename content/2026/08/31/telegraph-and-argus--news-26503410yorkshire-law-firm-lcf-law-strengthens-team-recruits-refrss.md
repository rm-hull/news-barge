A LAW firm has welcomed five new recruits as it continues to grow its team and expand its services.

LCF Law, which has offices in Leeds, Bradford, Harrogate and Ilkley, has made the appointments across its Leeds and Bradford branches.

In Leeds, Jenna Leach joins the corporate team as a legal executive, with Matthew Griffiths joining the personal law division as a paralegal, and Hannah O'Reilly joining the commercial litigation team, also as a paralegal.

In Bradford, solicitor Emily Bailey joins the firm’s specialist conveyancing arm, LCF Residential, while Anouska Seedheeyan has been appointed as a paralegal in the real estate division.

Ragan Montgomery, managing partner at LCF Law, said: "We’re very pleased to welcome Emily, Jenna, Matthew, Anouska and Hannah into our team at such pivotal moments in their careers.

"Both Emily and Jenna have several years’ experience behind them and are now stepping up to new roles in their preferred fields, which is always exciting."

Emily Bailey qualified as a solicitor earlier this year after more than four years as a paralegal and trainee solicitor at a Leeds practice.

Jenna Leach previously worked as a paralegal at two other Yorkshire firms before joining LCF Law.

All three new paralegals hold law degrees and have completed either the Legal Practice Course (LPC) or the Solicitors Qualifying Examination (SQE).

They will now begin two years of qualifying work experience, rotating through four six-month placements in different departments.

Ms Montgomery said: "In addition, the paralegals will play an important role in our firm, providing essential casework support, conducting legal research and preparing legal documents alongside their experienced colleagues and partners to streamline operations and client communications."

Since the start of the year, LCF Law has recruited 26 new team members, bringing its total headcount to 149.

Earlier this year, the firm was included in The Sunday Times Best Places to Work 2026 list in the medium-sized business category, one of only a handful of UK law firms to be recognised twice.

Ms Montgomery said: "Our solicitor training contracts are hugely popular and dozens of successful solicitors have completed our programme, which offers comprehensive, hands-on experience across our divisions including corporate, disputes, personal and real estate law.

"All this provides the foundations for young people to become fully qualified, confident and commercially minded solicitors."

LCF Law is a full-service law firm operating regionally, nationally, and internationally.
