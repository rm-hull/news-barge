ZAYN Malik has been named one of the UK's top artists for "sexiest" songs in a new playlist study.

The research, which analysed more than 12,000 Spotify playlists themed around intimacy and romance, identified Malik as the third most popular UK artist for bedroom playlists.

Formerly of One Direction, the Bradford-born singer appears 23rd overall in the global ranking of most-featured artists.

His track PILLOWTALK was the most popular, appearing 186 times across the playlists.

A spokesperson for AdultWork, the company behind the study, said: "It’s always interesting to see how much of a role music plays in our lives… including even soundtracking our bedroom habits and sex lives."

Other Yorkshire talent also made the list, with Sheffield’s Arctic Monkeys earning 11th place overall and ranking as the highest British act.

Their track I Wanna Be Yours appeared on 266 playlists, while Do I Wanna Know was featured 195 times.

Zayn’s other tracks to appear in the analysis included Sweat, TiO, and I Don’t Wanna Live Forever.

The spokesperson said: "While The Weeknd reigns supreme as the go-to artist for intimate time, the sheer variety of artists in the top 200 shows just how unique we all are in our tastes and preferences, which is always worth celebrating."
