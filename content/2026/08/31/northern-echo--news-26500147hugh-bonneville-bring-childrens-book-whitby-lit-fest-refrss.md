Whitby Lit Fest has announced a surprise headline appearance by an actor best known for performances in Downton Abbey and Paddington.

Hugh Bonneville will join the 2026 festival as a last-minute addition, taking part in a family event at Whitby Pavilion on November 21.

He will introduce his latest children’s book, Rory Sparkes and the Spy Who Loved Tea, as part of the four-day literary programme.

Lois Kirtlan, chair of Whitby Lit Fest, said: "We were already incredibly proud of the programme we had assembled for Whitby this November, so to be able to announce Hugh Bonneville as a surprise addition just as tickets go on sale is fantastic.

"Hugh is loved by audiences of all ages, from Downton Abbey to Paddington, and we think families are going to have enormous fun discovering another side to him as the creator of Rory Sparkes.

"The whole programme reflects what we want Whitby Lit Fest to be – big-name writers alongside exciting new voices, events inspired by Whitby and the Yorkshire coast, and everything from crime and history to politics, poetry, food, film and children’s books.

"We would encourage people to take a look at the full programme and book early, particularly for some of our headline events and smaller-capacity venues."

Mr Bonneville’s appearance will take place at 1pm on Saturday, November 21, and promises to be a lively session filled with storytelling, secret codes, and a dash of humour.

The actor will discuss the inspiration behind his book and answer questions from young readers and their families.

His event adds to an already packed line-up that spans genres including crime, fiction, history, poetry, politics, food, and film.

Other headline guests include John Boyne, author of The Boy in the Striped Pyjamas, with his new novel The Weight of Angels, and Ann Cleeves, who is celebrating the publication of her 40th novel.

Also appearing are Chocolat author Joanne Harris, Hairy Biker Si King, crime writers Elly Griffiths and Mark Billingham, and Grammy Award-winning singer-songwriter Corinne Bailey Rae.

Ms Bailey Rae will host a special family event celebrating her picture book Put Your Records On, inspired by her much-loved hit song.

The festival will open on November 19 with a seaside-themed mystery from bestselling author Jess Kidd, and will continue with four days of talks, performances, workshops, and family activities.

Events will be held at venues across Whitby, including the Pavilion, Eighteen91, Flowergate Hall, Whitby Museum, Whitby Library, the Coliseum, and Jet Black Jewel.

Tickets are priced at £11 for adults and £5 for children.

The full festival programme and booking information are available through the Whitby Lit Fest website.
