Fans of All Creatures Great and Small have been welcomed back to Darrowby as the first pictures from the seventh series of the hit Channel 5 drama are revealed.

The much-loved programme will return to screens in September, taking viewers back to the Yorkshire Dales and the world of James Herriot.

Channel 5 has shared a first glimpse of the new series, telling fans: “Welcome back to Darrowby. Your favourite faces return, alongside some new arrivals.”

But while millions of viewers have become familiar with Darrowby during the show's six previous series, they will not find it marked on a map.

The town is fictional, although the Yorkshire village seen on screen is very real.

Grassington, in the Yorkshire Dales, is transformed into Darrowby for Channel 5's adaptation of James Herriot's stories.

Its historic stone buildings, traditional shopfronts and market square have helped make it one of the most recognisable locations in the programme.

Several genuine Grassington businesses are given a 1930s and 1940s makeover whenever filming takes place.

The Devonshire becomes the exterior of The Drovers Arms, while The Stripey Badger bookshop has been transformed into G F Endleby's grocers.

Walker's Bakery has appeared as Darrowby Ironmongers, while other shops around the centre of the village have also been given fictional names and period façades.


Even Skeldale House – home to the veterinary practice at the centre of the stories – can be found in Grassington, although the exterior used for filming is a private house.

Grassington itself proudly celebrates its connection with the series and describes itself as the home of the fictional market town of Darrowby.

The village has been used since the current version of All Creatures Great and Small first arrived on Channel 5 in 2020.

Series seven will move the story into 1946, with the Second World War now behind the characters and Skeldale House once again bustling with familiar faces.

Nicholas Ralph returns as James Herriot alongside Rachel Shenton as Helen Herriot, Samuel West as Siegfried Farnon, Callum Woodhouse as Tristan Farnon and Anna Madeley as Mrs Hall.

The new series will also shake things up with the arrival of another vet, Granville Bennett, played by Caolan McCarthy.

Channel 5 says James will become excited by advances in veterinary medicine, only for the new arrival to threaten the status quo in the Dales.

Six new episodes are due to air, followed by another Christmas special, with an eighth series already commissioned.

Grassington is not the only Yorkshire location used for All Creatures Great and Small.

Scenes have previously been filmed across the region at locations including Broughton Hall, Ripley Castle, the Green Dragon Inn at Hardraw, Arncliffe, Kettlewell and the North Yorkshire Moors Railway.

However, it is Grassington that viewers are most likely to recognise as the heart of Darrowby.

Away from the cameras, visitors will find the modern-day village rather than the period town seen on television, but many of the buildings remain instantly recognisable.
