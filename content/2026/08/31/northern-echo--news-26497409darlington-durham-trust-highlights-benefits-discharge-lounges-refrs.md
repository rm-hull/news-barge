A hospital trust in County Durham and Darlington has used a month-long campaign to highlight the vital role discharge lounges play in helping patients leave hospital safely.

County Durham and Darlington NHS Foundation Trust (CDDFT) held its first 'Love Your Lounge Month' throughout August.

The campaign focused on discharge lounges at Darlington Memorial Hospital and University Hospital of North Durham.

Love Your Lounge Month aimed to raise awareness among patients, families and staff about the purpose of discharge lounges, how they support safe and timely discharges, and what patients can expect during their final hours in hospital.

Julie Form, outpatients and clinical site manager, said: “Leaving hospital is an important part of a patient’s recovery, and we want that experience to feel safe, supported and as smooth as possible.”

“Our Discharge Lounges help patients feel one step closer to home while they wait for final arrangements. They are calm and welcoming spaces where patients continue to be cared for, supported and kept informed.”

“Love Your Lounge Month has been about raising awareness of these spaces, helping patients and families know what to expect, and supporting colleagues to use the lounges consistently as part of safe and timely discharge planning.”

Discharge lounges provide a comfortable environment for patients while they wait for medication, transport, discharge information, collection by family or friends, or transfer to a community hospital or next place of care.

Throughout the month, the Trust shared patient information, staff resources, videos, posters, and patient and family experience stories.

The campaign also encouraged patients and families to think ahead about discharge arrangements where possible, including how they will get home, who may be able to collect them, whether they have suitable clothing and footwear, and whether they need support when they arrive home.

The Trust is continuing to encourage colleagues to think “discharge lounge early” as part of discharge planning, and to support patients and families to feel informed and reassured when they are ready to leave hospital.

The trust is also exploring opportunities for volunteers to support the lounges and help create a friendly and reassuring environment for patients.

More information about leaving hospital and what to expect in the discharge lounge is available on the trust’s website at [https://www.cddft.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/your-care-hospital/while-youre-us/leaving-hospital.](https://www.cddft.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/your-care-hospital/while-youre-us/leaving-hospital.)
