Eight North East businesses have been fined more than £500,000 after being found employing illegal workers.

Businesses in Newcastle, County Durham, Sunderland and Middlesbrough are among those named in the latest Home Office figures covering the period from January 1 to March 31, 2026.

Penalties ranging from £40,000 to £135,000 were handed to the eight businesses, totalling £510,000.

The largest penalty in the latest North East list was issued to Jana Shawarma Ltd, linked to Shawarma & Grill House on Gallowgate in Newcastle, which was fined £135,000.

Mr Bubbles Mini Valet Centre NE Ltd, linked to Mr Bubbles on Condercum Road in Newcastle, was handed a £90,000 penalty.

Fence Houses Hand Car Wash, on Morton Crescent in Fence Houses, Houghton-le-Spring, appears on the list under Sunderland Hand Car Wash Ltd with a penalty of £80,000.

Other businesses named include Hollathan’s in Chester-le-Street, Pizza Hot in Ferryhill, Style Barbers in Birtley, Tarantinos in Sunderland and Ali Barber in Middlesbrough.

A quarterly Home Office report identifies employers that have received civil penalties for illegal working.

The Government says employers must check that people they employ have the legal right to work in the UK.

Businesses can receive a penalty if they employ someone without the right to work and cannot demonstrate that the correct right-to-work checks were carried out.

Those who knowingly employ somebody they know, or have reasonable cause to believe, does not have the right to work in the UK can face an unlimited fine and up to five years in prison.
