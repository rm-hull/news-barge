BASSLINE meets classical in a groundbreaking concert coming to Bradford this autumn.

Bassline Symphony, described as the UK’s first full symphonic bassline event, will take place at Bradford Live on Saturday, November 28.

Created by Bradford-born producer Tanya Vital and produced by Vital Culture UK Ltd, the show brings together the Opera North Orchestra with leading UK bassline artists including TS7, DJ Q, and Jamie Duggan.

A spokesperson said: "This is not a novelty crossover.

"It is a statement about cultural recognition, Northern music heritage and which sounds are given major-stage treatment."

The event follows a sold-out debut at St George’s Hall in 2025.

Bassline Symphony blends 4x4 bassline, also known as 'Niche music', with orchestral arrangements by Katie Chatburn.

The genre, which originated in Yorkshire’s working-class communities in the early 2000s, has shaped the sound of clubs and artists across the North and beyond.
