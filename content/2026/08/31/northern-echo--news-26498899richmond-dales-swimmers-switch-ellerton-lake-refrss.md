A North Yorkshire swimming club has found a way to tackle rising pool hire costs and continue offering sessions throughout the summer.

Richmond Dales Amateur Swimming Club has switched to open water sessions at Ellerton Lake, with members quickly embracing the change.

Club chair Liz Byrne said: “We were a bit apprehensive about how people would respond but it has been fantastic.

“On the first night 30 turned up and it was lovely to see the mix which included para swimmers, triathletes, club swimmers and seniors.”

  Richmond Dales swimmers enjoy an open water session at Ellerton Lake 

*(Image: Supplied)*

As well as working out far cheaper than hiring a pool, the lake also offers a wider body of water which means different levels can train together.

The club received a £350 grant from Broadacres Housing Association, via its Community and Sustainability Fund, to trial the first sessions and they have gone down so well they aim to continue through to October before returning to a pool for winter.

“The grant enabled us to launch an inclusive competitive swimming training programme at the Lake and it has proved very worthwhile,” said Liz.

“We were able to send a team to the recent National Open Water Championships at Sale Water Park which was a great achievement considering this is our first year.”

Safety is key and swimmers have buoys and there is always a safety kayak on patrol.

Normally, participants would wear a wetsuit, but the weather has been so hot over the summer that some have opted for trunks or costume.

To find out more about the Fund, including how to apply, visit www.broadacres.org.uk.

Anyone interested in trying out open swimming can contact the club by emailing [admin@rdasc.org.uk](mailto:admin@rdasc.org.uk) or for more information visit www.rdasc.org.uk
