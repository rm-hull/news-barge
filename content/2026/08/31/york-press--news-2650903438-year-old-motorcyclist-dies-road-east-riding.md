A MOTORCYCLIST has died following an accident on a road in the East Riding of Yorkshire.

The 38-year-old was riding a dark coloured Suzuki motorcycle when it left the road and collided with a fence.

Humberside police said the single-vehicle road traffic collision occurred in Fieldend Lane in Estronwick near Burton Pidsea just after 3pm on Friday, August 28.

A spokesperson for Humberside Police said: "It is reported a dark coloured Suzuki motorcycle was travelling on Fieldend Lane towards Burton Pidsea when it left the carriageway and collided with a boundary fence.

"Emergency services attended, but sadly the rider, a 38-year-old man, was pronounced deceased at the scene."

  


The man's family has been informed and is being supported by specially trained officers.

"Enquiries remain ongoing to determine the full circumstances around the collision, and we would ask anyone who may have witnessed the incident, has any information, including dashcam footage leading up to the collision, to please contact us on our non-emergency number 101 quoting log 296 of 28 August."
