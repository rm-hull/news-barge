A historic Bradford district country house has been put on the market for £1.85 million.

Harden Hall, located in Harden near Bingley, is a Grade II* listed property believed to date back to the 17th century.

It is being sold for the first time since 1943 and sits in more than 11 acres of gardens, woodland and land.

Dacre, Son & Hartley’s Bingley office is handling the sale.

Steven Potts, who heads up the office, said: "Harden Hall is a particularly interesting property because of its Grade II* listed status, historic character and the scale of the grounds.

"The house has retained many of its original architectural features and comes with a substantial amount of land, woodland and two stone barns.

"It is also within easy reach of Bingley, Bradford and Leeds, which adds to its appeal as an impressive country property close to the region’s major centres."

The stone-built house has nine bedrooms and five reception rooms.

Period features include double-chamfered mullioned windows, exposed beams, moulded cornicing, high skirting boards and an inglenook-style fireplace.

An electric gate opens on to the property’s large driveway.

The approximately 11.32-acre (4.57-hectare) grounds include formal lawns, flower beds, a paved patio, mature trees and woodland.

The sale includes two large detached stone barns with potential for several uses, subject to any necessary planning consent.

The grounds also include paddocks, offering scope for buyers seeking equestrian facilities.

The location offers easy access to the countryside and nearby towns.

Harden Hall is offered for sale by private treaty on a freehold basis.

The guide price is £1.85 million.

For more information, contact Dacre, Son & Hartley’s Bingley office by calling 01274 560 421 or visiting www.dacres.co.uk.

Ryecroft, a hamlet in Harden, is a conservation area with a history dating back centuries.
