The search is on to find Darlington and County Durham's favourite bakery, as The Northern Echo reveals the top ten shortlist for its Best Bakery 2026 competition.

Readers have been nominating their favourite bakers across the region

From long-established family bakers to independent artisan businesses and popular town-centre shops, the competition has once again highlighted the range of bakeries serving Darlington and County Durham with freshly baked bread, pastries, pies, cakes and sweet treats.

We can now reveal the ten finalists competing to be crowned The Northern Echo's Best Bakery 2026.

  **The finalists are:**

- Bakerman – 9 Clark’s Yard, Darlington, DL3 7QH
- Bank Top Bakery – 127 Victoria Road, Darlington, DL1 5JJ
- Gregory’s – 105 Newgate Street, Bishop Auckland, DL14 7EW
- Longstaff’s Family Bakers – 91 Newgate Street, Bishop Auckland, DL14 7EW
- Number Eight Bakehouse – 21 Post House Wynd, Darlington, DL3 7LP
- RISE Artisan Bakehouse & Cafe – 13 Horsemarket, Barnard Castle, DL12 8LY
- Shell’s Village Bakery – 1 Main Street, Ferryhill, DL17 8LA
- The Hedgehog Bakery – 209 Newgate Street, Bishop Auckland, DL14 7EL
- The Leas Bakery – Thompson Street East, Darlington, DL1 3BA
- Thomas the Baker – 33 High Row, Darlington, DL3 7QW

Shell's Village Bakery in Ferryhill will be hoping to do the double and win the title for the second time, having beaten the competition last year.

   

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Northern Echo Editor Gavin Foster said: "Bakeries remain an important part of high streets and communities across the region.

"That's why we want to champion them and show off the best of the bunch."

This year's shortlist includes four finalists from Darlington town centre, three from Bishop Auckland, as well as bakeries in Barnard Castle and Ferryhill.

You can vote using coupons printed in The Northern Echo, with readers invited to choose their favourite from the ten finalists.

Post your coupon to The Northern Echo Best Bakery 2026, LOCALLY House, 1 Dalston Road, Carlisle, CA2 5NP.
