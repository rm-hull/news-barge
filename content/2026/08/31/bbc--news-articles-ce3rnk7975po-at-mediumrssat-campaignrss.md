Three people were attacked at a property in Lisnahull Road on Saturday

**A 16-year-old boy has been charged with the murder of a man in Dungannon, County Tyrone, and the attempted murder of two other people in the same incident.**

The body of the dead man, who was 61, was found alongside his injured eight-year-old daughter at a house on Lisnahull Road on Saturday morning,

The man's 48-year-old wife sustained non-life threatening injuries during the attack.

The teenage boy, who has also been charged with making threats to kill, is due to appear before Omagh Magistrates' Court on Tuesday.
