MICROSOFT Outlook appears to have experienced a possible outage.

Over 5000 users reported problems on Downdetector on Monday evening, with most saying they are having trouble receiving messages.

  **Microsoft’s status checker**, however, says that all products are operational at this time.

The terms "Microsoft Outlook down" and "Microsoft outage" are also trending on social media.

## Are Microsoft Teams, Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Copilot also down?

According to Downdetector, both Teams and 365 are experiencing issues for some user – albeit not at the same level as Outlook currently.

It is difficult to ascertain the extent, if any, of a Microsoft Copilot outage.
