A "decade of memories" is being celebrated by a business in York.

Nomination York, located in Low Petergate, has marked 10 years in the heart of the city.

The store was opened ten years ago by Ken Brown and Heather Wilson Brown, with Mr Brown playing an important role in helping to establish and grow the brand in the UK.

Valentina Ruiu, who works at the store, said: "From the very beginning, we built the business around a clear vision, a strong work ethic, a real passion for the brand and a strong focus on customer service.

"These foundations have remained at the heart of everything we do, and I continue to bring the same principles to my team, making sure that these standards are upheld and lived every day.

"I would like to believe that this is what our customers perceive when they visit the store, and that it is one of the reasons why they continue to support us day after day."

The business faced significant challenges during the pandemic, including three separate closures and ongoing financial commitments despite the loss of sales.

Ms Ruiu said: "One of the biggest challenges we faced was, of course, Covid.

"The store had to close three separate times, and it was an incredibly difficult and uncertain period for everyone."

To adapt, Mr Brown set up an online store, www.yorkcharms.co.uk, helping them stay connected with customers and keep the business moving during the closures.

Despite global uncertainty and economic challenges, the store has continued to grow and develop in York.
