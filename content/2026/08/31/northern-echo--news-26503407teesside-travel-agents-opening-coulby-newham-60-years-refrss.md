A travel agent that has been helping North East families make memories for more than 60 years is opening a new Middlesbrough branch next month.

Dawson & Sanderson, which has operated across the North of England since 1962, will move into M Parkway shopping centre, Coulby Newham, in September.

The new store will occupy the former All Things Good unit in the shopping centre - which moved out of the lot earlier this year.

The new Coulby Newham branch will further expand Dawson & Sanderson's footprint across the North East, where it has built a reputation as one of the region's longest - running independent travel agencies.

  M Parkway will welcome Dawson & Sanderson travel agents to the shopping centre next month. 

*(Image: M Parkway Shopping Centre)*

While Dawson & Sanderson already operates branches in Middlesbrough town centre and Stockton, the Coulby Newham store will expand its reach across Teesside.

Founded more than 60 years ago, Dawson & Sanderson specialises in package holidays and works with airlines, hotels, tour operators and local guides around the world.

The travel agent's network is largely concentrated across the North East, with additional branches in Carlisle, Hull and Doncaster.

The company has built a reputation as one of the North of England's leading travel agencies and has received a number of industry awards, including the Feefo Platinum Trusted Service Award.

M Parkway Shopping Centre is home to a range of retailers and services, including Superdrug, Greggs, Boyes, Peacocks and Ramsdens.

The arrival of Dawson & Sanderson is expected to boost the centre's retail offering ahead of the autumn while quickly filling a vacant unit.

An exact opening date has yet to be confirmed, but posters suggest the new branch is expected to welcome its first customers in September.
