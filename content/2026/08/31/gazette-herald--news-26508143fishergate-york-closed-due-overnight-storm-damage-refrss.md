A MAIN road in York is closed today as part of its surface has lifted following a storm.

  

  City of York Council shut a part of the Fishergate Gyratory following storm damage last night (Sunday, August 30).

  

  The road has lifted across a large part of its width at a pedestrian crossing on the corner of Fishergate where it meets Paragon Street and has been cordoned off.

  


  The eastbound A1036 Tower Street away from the city centre is closed this morning (Monday, August 31) after last night's storm 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*


This morning, the eastbound A1036 Tower Street coming out of York city centre was closed after the junction with Piccadilly and Fishergate with traffic turning right into Lead Mill Lane, opposite The Postern Gate pub.

City of York Council is advising vehicles travelling east from Tower Street to divert via Piccadilly and Walmgate Bar.

  A spokesperson for the council said it will provide an update following an inspection of the road.

  

  The Press has contacted City of York Council.
