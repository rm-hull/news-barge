The Bay Horse in Middridge has become a popular destination for pub food in County Durham, drawing diners from Newton Aycliffe, Darlington, Bishop Auckland and beyond with generous portions, classic dishes and a traditional village-pub setting.

Located on The Green in Middridge, close to Newton Aycliffe, the pub serves a wide-ranging menu of familiar favourites including fish and chips, steaks, pies, burgers, pasta dishes and Sunday lunches.

It is also known for rotating guest real ales and has gluten-free choices on its menu.

## What is on the menu?

The Bay Horse offers a mix of pub classics, grill dishes and more informal meals, with its menu including whole Atlantic cod with chunky chips, homemade corned beef pie, a 16oz rump steak, mixed grill, burgers and pasta.

The venue also caters for diners with dietary requirements.

A gluten-free review site says staff are knowledgeable about allergies, with separate fryers used for gluten-free food, while its menu flags gluten-free options.

Its location in Middridge makes it a convenient food stop for visitors exploring the area around Newton Aycliffe and the wider south Durham countryside.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

## What TripAdvisor reviewers say

TripAdvisor reviewers have regularly praised the pub’s welcome, food and value, although, as with any review platform, comments reflect individual customer experiences.

One visitor wrote: “We had an amazing stop here. Lovely lunch and drinks. It was busy but the staff remained calm and helpful. Could have stayed all afternoon.”

Another reviewer said: “The food was top quality and the best we have tasted for a long time. We will certainly be back when next in the area.”

A further customer described the venue as “fabulous”, adding: “Food and ambience once inside are fabulous. Try it out, you won’t be disappointed.”

The pub has also been praised for its portions and staff.

A more recent TripAdvisor listing includes a review from a Tuesday visit which described the food as locally sourced and said portions were “more than adequate”.

## Planning a visit

The Bay Horse is at The Green, Middridge, Newton Aycliffe, DL5 7JD.

Its listed opening hours are noon to 11pm from Monday to Friday, 11am to 11pm on Saturdays and 11am to 10pm on Sundays, although visitors should check directly before travelling as hours and food service times may change.
