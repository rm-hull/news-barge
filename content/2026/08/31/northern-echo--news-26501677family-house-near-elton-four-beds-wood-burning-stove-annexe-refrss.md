DESIGNED for effortless single-level living, Zarina House is a beautifully presented barn conversion that offers the perfect balance of rustic charm and contemporary luxury.

Its setting in the peaceful rural hamlet of Coatham Stob, close to the village of Elton, on a plot that extends to around 1.41 acres and enjoys far-reaching views over the surrounding countryside, is the cherry on the top.

The property’s approximately 2,143 sq. ft of accommodation comprises a porch which opens into a generous utility room and from there into the stunning open-plan kitchen, dining and family room with a vaulted ceiling and bifold doors that seamlessly connect the interior with the outdoor entertaining space.

The stylish high-spec kitchen includes quality cabinetry, new integrated appliances, resin worktops and a substantial central island, creating a space that functions as the heart of this home.

Another of Zarina House’s highlights is the light-filled and spacious lounge, accessed from the kitchen and home to an exposed brick feature fireplace housing a wood-burning stove plus bifold doors that open into the garden.

A rear inner hall leads from the kitchen right down to the other end of the property, providing access to the remaining accommodation.

This encompasses a family shower room, family bathroom and four well-proportioned bedrooms which include the principal suite with an en-suite shower room, and one bedroom currently used as a dressing room but which offers excellent flexibility to be utilised as a guest bedroom, nursery or home office.

A significant feature of the property is a detached annexe comprising an open-plan lounge and dining room, a double bedroom and an en-suite shower room.

This is ideal for multigenerational living, guest accommodation or for use as a home office.

Zarina House is approached via remote-controlled electric gates, with a new resin driveway leading to an extensive gravelled parking area and double timber carport which has an electric vehicle charging point and a useful gym/store room.

The landscaped grounds include expansive lawns and an outdoor entertaining area featuring a fully equipped garden kitchen complete with pizza oven.

Further enhancements to the property include solar panels, a Tesla Powerwall home battery, a new bio-septic tank, smart controlled heating and security systems and high-speed broadband that serves the house, annexe and gardens.
