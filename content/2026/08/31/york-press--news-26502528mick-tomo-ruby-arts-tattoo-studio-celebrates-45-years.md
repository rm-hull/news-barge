## York tattoo artist celebrates remarkable 45 years in industry

Mick tattooing Rick Witter of Shed Seven *(Image: Provided)*

A YORK tattoo artist is celebrating a remarkable 45 years in the industry.

Mick Tomo, who owns Ruby Arts Tattoo Studio in Blossom Street, has built an international reputation from his studio in York.

He first began tattooing at the age of 14, practicing on himself before his dad, impressed by his son's talent, dad became his second client.

After this, Mick began an apprenticeship to refine his craft and managed to turn his teenage hobby into a full-time career - opening his first studio in Green Lane in Acomb, York by the age of 18.

Mick said: "When I started tattooing 45 years ago, I never imagined I’d still be doing what I love every day.

"Tattooing has been my life, and I’m incredibly grateful to all the clients who have trusted me over the years. Their support has made this milestone possible."

  Mick tattooing as a teen 

*(Image: Provided)*

His career has taken him around the world, working in different countries and witnessing dramatic changes in the tattoo industry.

For the past 35 years, he has worked from his current studio in York, building a reputation that reaches far beyond the city.

Mick has worked in nearly every style of tattooing and has tattooed thousands of people from all walks of life, including well-known figures such as Rick Witter, lead singer of York band Shed Seven.

  Mick tattooing 

*(Image: Provided)*

He is especially proud of the relationships he has built with his clients over the decades, many of whom have become close friends.

Mick has also tattooed multiple generations within families, with children and grandchildren following in the footsteps of their relatives.

His wife, Liza, said: "Watching Mick’s dedication over the years has been incredible.

  Mick on the cover of Tattoo Master 

*(Image: Provided)*

"Even after 45 years, he still puts his heart into every tattoo. We’re so proud to celebrate this amazing milestone and thankful to everyone who has supported him throughout his career."

His contribution to tattooing has been recognised with more than 150 awards, including a recent Lifetime Achievement Award.

He continues to work as a tattoo artist.
