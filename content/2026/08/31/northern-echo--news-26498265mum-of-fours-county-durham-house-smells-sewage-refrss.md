A County Durham mum has hit out at her landlord, saying an uncapped pipe leaves her home stinking of sewage every time neighbours flush the loo.

Jess Partington, 34, who has lived in an end-terrace on Lyne Road in Middlestone Moor, Spennymoor, for the last two years, first noticed an 'odd smell' coming from the loft.

After complaining to housing association Livin Housing, she found the smell was coming from an exposed and uncapped pipe in the loft.

But Jess says the issue has got "ten times worse" over time, and claims it has led to damp and mould.

  The exposed pipe in the loft 

*(Image: JESS PARTINGTON)*

Livin said it is "aware" of the situation and is working with the mum-of-four to solve the issues.

"At first, I noticed a smell, and it smelt odd, and then it intensified," Jess said.

"When my neighbours flush the toilet, wash their hands or go in the shower, the water runs down the pipes, and then the smell seems to come up through this pipe in the loft.

  Jessica Partington, who has issues with her house in Middlestone Moor 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"I've also noticed damp and mould in my kitchen and other parts of the house because of the issue."

The 34-year-old says the issue hasn't been resolved despite contacting Livin numerous times.

  Mould on the walls inside the house 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Scaffolding was put up around her home eight weeks ago, but she says no work has been done.

"Every time we get in touch, they say they won't work in the loft," she added.

  Jessica Partington 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"They once came round and took a pipe from the roof, which I understand would have improved the smell, and there was no explanation why that was done.

"This is the fourth time that they've put up scaffolding."

Jess now worries that in the build-up towards winter and Christmas this year, issues could intensify for her and her four children, aged three, seven, nine, and 15.

"It's absolutely awful that we've been left in this way," Jess said.

  Silicone bridging the gaps on the brickwork 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"One of my children has serious asthma, and the smell and damp are causing them all sorts of problems.

"We just don't know where to turn to."

She says other issues at the property include asbestos in the loft, uneven brickwork, mould in the kitchen and bedroom, uneven roof tiles, and brickwork plugged up with silicone.

The issue hasn't just impacted Jess, but is also having a knock-on effect for her neighbour, Kris Lindsay, 50, who owns his house.

  Jessica Partington, who has issues with her house in Middlestone Moor and neighbour Kris Lindsay 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

After coming home one day, Kris, who has lived there ten years, was told contractors had been on his roof and broken roof tiles.

  Jessica Partington, who has issues with her house in Middlestone Moor and neighbour Kris Lindsay 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The 50-year-old says it took an "almighty battle" to get the problem resolved.

"It's taken so long to sort these issues. I'm frustrated for Jess and her kids. Instead of Livin focusing on new properties, they should spend money on solving issues.

"People shouldn't be living in these conditions.

"Before Jess moved in, the property was empty for ten months; why couldn't they have made the changes needed then?"

The pair have now urged Livin to " do the right thing and put the issues right".

Paul Thompson, head of property services at Livin, said. “We are aware of Miss Partington’s circumstances and are addressing these through our repairs and damp and mould procedures.

"Progressing these works has depended on agreeing suitable arrangements for access, and we have continued to work with Miss Partington to enable the outstanding repairs to be completed as quickly as possible.

"We take all reports about the condition of our homes seriously, and our approach is always to work directly with the customer to resolve any matters.”
