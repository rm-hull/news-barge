A North Yorkshire hair extensions specialist has been crowned the best in the UK in her first year of trading in North Yorkshire, marking a major achievement in a career spanning more than two decades.

Kylie Hammond, 39, who runs FAKE salon at Crakehall near Bedale, took first place in the Hair Extensions Specialist of the Year category at the UK Hair and Beauty Awards.

The ceremony was held at the AO Arena in Manchester on Saturday, August 15, and presented by television personality Jake Quickenden.

  Kylie Hammond with host Jake Quickenden at the UK Hair and Beauty Awards, AO Arena, Manchester, 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Ms Hammond said: "Winning this after only a year of trading feels like an incredible achievement, but I've been working towards it a lot longer than that.

"I spent years in some of the best hair extensions salons in London, and after developing my own style of hair extensions, we decided we wanted to come back to North Yorkshire with the goal of building the number 1 hair extensions salon in the UK.

"Being crowned the UK's number 1 hair extensions specialist is just the start for us, but it's an incredible reward for the work we've put in over the last 12 months of trading."

  FAKE salon owner Kylie Hammond at her salon 

*(Image: Supplied)*

With more than 20 years of industry experience, Ms Hammond spent 13 years in London salons working with clients including film and television actors, professional models, and content creators.

She returned to North Yorkshire two years ago and opened her own studio at Crakehall last year.

Since then, she has developed her own style of tailor-made hair extensions, each set created by hand in the salon and designed to be virtually undetectable through a specialist colour-matching and fitting process.

FAKE salon specialises in premium extensions using hair sourced from Eastern Europe and crafted to each client’s specifications.

The UK Hair and Beauty Awards received thousands of entries from across the country, all submitted anonymously and judged by a panel of three independent experts, each with at least 10 years’ experience in the industry.

Ms Hammond was ranked first in the national top 100 for her category.

The organisers said the 2026 awards attracted thousands of entries nationally.
