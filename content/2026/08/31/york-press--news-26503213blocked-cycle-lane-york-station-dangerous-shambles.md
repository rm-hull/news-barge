## Blocked cycle lane to York Railway Station called 'dangerous shambles'

New cycle lane in Queen Street. Photo by Andy D'Agorne

  **Blocked cycle lane to station is 'dangerous shambles'**

CYCLING to York Railway Station from Blossom Street junction is now more dangerous than before, with a newly painted cycle lane completed but filled with signs saying 'cycle lane closed' and to 'use main carriageway'!

I am including with this letter photos I took at this cycle path on Wednesday August 26th.

  New cycle lane in Queen Street. Photo by Andy D'Agorne


Before work started in 2023 there was already a cycle lane as far as the top of the demolished Queen Street Bridge, now replaced with multiple bus stops but no bike lanes!

  New cycle lane in Queen Street. Photo by Andy D'Agorne


Its unbelievable that former chair of York Cycle Campaign Cllr Ravilious would now be responsible for such a dangerous shambles, which we are told could continue with 'temporary diversions' until 2028!

  *Andy D'Agorne,*

  *York Green Party*

  **Charge higher parking fees for large vehicles**

ON average cars are larger than they used to be.

So they take up more space including when parking.

In York our space is limited so it would be good if we followed the example of Bath and some other towns and cities and charged higher parking fees for large vehicles.

  *Rose Berl,*

  *De Little Court,*

  *Vine Street,*

  *York*

  **Why are we not extracting our own oil and gas?**

THE next time the Prime Minister gives a TV broadcast could he please explain what benefit we gain by not extracting gas and oil from our own amenities off shore, instead of importing same at greater cost from foreign countries.

It seems to be turning logic on its head to satisfy the spurious claims of a vociferous net zero minority.
