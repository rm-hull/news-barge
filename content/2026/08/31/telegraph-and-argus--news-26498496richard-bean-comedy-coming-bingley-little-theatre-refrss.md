A comedy about ageing, family, and the messiness of home life is taking centre stage in Bingley this autumn.

Richard Bean’s play To Have and To Hold is being performed by Bingley Little Theatre at the Bingley Arts Centre from Tuesday, September 15, to Saturday, September 19.

The production opens the theatre’s new season.

The play follows the story of elderly Yorkshire couple Jack and Flo Kirk.

Andrew Long, who plays Jack, said: "There is strong comedy and some emotional conflict.

"Playing Jack, I have some moving speeches where I recount stories of my life as a policeman, providing contrast to the crack-fire of the gags."

The play is directed by Kevin Moore, who recently directed Educating Rita at Keighley Playhouse.

The cast, including Gilly Rogers and Deborah Mouat, has strong connections to Keighley Playhouse.

The production follows the success of Bean’s One Man, Two Guvnors, which made its amateur Airedale debut at Keighley Playhouse in 2017.

Performances of To Have and To Hold will begin with evening shows each day, with a Saturday matinee.

Tickets are available from www.bingleyartscentre.co.uk, by calling 01274 567983, or in person at the box office, which is open Monday to Friday from 10am to 1pm.
