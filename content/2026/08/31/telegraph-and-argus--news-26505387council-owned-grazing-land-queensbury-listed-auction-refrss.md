A PLOT of Council-owned grazing land in Queensbury will be sold at auction later this month.

The 1.74 acre site off Deanstones Lane will have a guide price of £30,000 when it goes under the hammer at the BTG Eddisons online auction on September 28.

The site is leased as grazing land for £209 a year, while garages at the top of the land generate £560 a year for the Council.

The site, next to Shibden Head Primary School, is one of many the Council is selling off in a bid to become more financially sustainable.

The authority needs to raise at least £150m from the sale of properties as part of a package of "extraordinary financial support" from Government.

At the same auction the Council is selling a small car park on Valley Road in Shipley.

The listing for the Queensbury site says: “The plot predominantly comprises grazing land with six garages at its northern boundary.

“The garages are all sold subject to tenancies which, in total, details an annual rent of £560.88, whilst the land is occupied via a grazing licence detailing a £209 annual licence fee.”

It points out that a number of public footpaths cross the site.
