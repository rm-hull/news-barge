A NEW winter service plan could see roads gritted more efficiently thanks to road surface temperature sensors.

Bradford Council's executive committee will consider the plan on Tuesday, September 8, which aims to improve road safety in icy and snowy conditions through the use of advanced technology.

A key element of the proposal is the use of data from road surface temperature sensors installed across the district last year.

Councillor Andrew Judson, Bradford Council’s executive member for economy, regeneration, and planning, said: "The network of road surface technology across the Bradford district is the largest in West Yorkshire.

"This reflects the council’s commitment to monitoring and treating roads across the district as effectively as possible, using new technology to deploy crews and resources more efficiently throughout the winter season."

By integrating sensor data with specialist weather forecasts and traditional thermal mapping, the council hopes to better identify areas most at risk of ice formation and deliver more targeted treatments.

This approach is expected to reduce unnecessary gritting, lower salt usage, and improve the efficiency of winter operations without compromising road safety.

Bradford’s varied landscape creates particular challenges for winter maintenance, with around half of the district’s population living at or above 500 feet.

Road surface temperatures in higher areas like Queensbury can be two to three degrees lower than in lower-lying parts of the district, making localised, data-driven decision-making crucial.

The new technology would support the council’s existing winter maintenance programme, which covers more than 800 miles of roads.

The executive committee has been advised to approve the Winter Service Plan 2026/27, enabling the updated system to be implemented ahead of the next winter season.

The plan aims to ensure that roads remain safe and communities stay connected during periods of severe weather, using technology to improve both effectiveness and sustainability.
