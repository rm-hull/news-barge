THE Music of The Jersey Boys returns to Silsden with a lively Christmas tribute.

Following four back-to-back sell-out performances, the show is returning to Silsden Town Hall for its fifth year, with two performances on Saturday, December 12.

Audiences can sing along to Frankie Valli and the Four Seasons hits, including Beggin’, Working My Way Back to You, Big Girls Don’t Cry and Oh What a Night.

Impeccably costumed performers faithfully recreate the tracklist of the multi-award-winning Broadway musical, while the all-singing, all-dancing production promises tapping feet and shuffling in the aisles.

The matinee begins at 2:00pm, followed by the evening show at 8:00pm. Doors open 45 minutes before this show begins.

Both performances will be held at Silsden Town Hall, Kirkgate, Silsden, Keighley, West Yorkshire, BD20 0AJ.
