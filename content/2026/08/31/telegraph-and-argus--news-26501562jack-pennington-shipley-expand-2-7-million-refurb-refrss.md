A family-owned Bradford district business is set to modernise and expand its historic site with the help of a £1.4 million funding boost.

Shipley-based Jack Pennington Ltd has secured £1.4 million from Barclays towards a £2.7 million refurbishment and expansion project at its Hird Street premises.

Mark Levenston, group chief executive officer at Jack Pennington Ltd, said: "As a family business, our roots in Shipley mean everything to us.

"This building has been at the heart of our operations and our identity, and investing in it ensures we can continue to serve our customers while staying true to where we’ve come from."

  Construction is underway on a new building at Jack Pennington Ltd in Shipley


## Firm going since 1974

The refurbishment will increase the company's operational capacity while preserving its long-standing connection to the area.

Founded in 1974 by Mr Levenston’s wife’s grandfather, Jack Pennington, the company has grown from a small start-up into a well-established industrial distributor serving sectors across the UK.

It remains family-owned and continues to operate near its original base in Shipley.

  An overhead view of the Shipley industrial site which includes Jack Pennington Ltd


## When will the work be complete?

Mr Levenston said: "The project will cost a total of £2.7 million and we’re funding quite a lot of it ourselves.

"The building work is well underway.

"We started work in March and we’re aiming for it to be finished in January.

  Construction is underway on a new building at Jack Pennington Ltd in Shipley


"We’ve been in the area for quite a long time.

"We employ 50 people in Shipley.

"We wanted to stay where we are so we could invest in local people."

  Inside Jack Pennington Ltd


## £1.4 million funding from Barclays

The site, which reflects more than a century of industrial heritage, is home to three businesses: Jack Pennington Ltd, Ron Hague Ltd, and Channel Socket UK.

Barclays has supported Jack Pennington Ltd since its founding in 1974.

Noah Richardson, relationship director at Barclays, said: "Jack Pennington Ltd is a fantastic example of a resilient Yorkshire family business that has evolved over decades while staying true to its roots.

"We are proud to have supported the company since its earliest days in 1974, and this £1.4 million investment reflects our continued commitment to helping established regional businesses grow.

"Their decision to reinvest in their historic Shipley premises, while planning for future growth, highlights both the strength of the business and the importance of its local identity.

"We are pleased to support the team as they enhance and expand their site, and we look forward to continuing our long-standing relationship as the business moves into its next phase."

Mr Levenston said the latest funding "marks an exciting next step for the business."
