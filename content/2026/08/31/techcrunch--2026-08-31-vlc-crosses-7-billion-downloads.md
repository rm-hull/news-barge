In our world of expensive streaming platforms, the very free and offline VLC media player, developed by the non-profit VideoLAN, has crossed 7 billion downloads across all platforms.

Jean-Baptiste Kempf, the president and lead developer of VLC, sent this update in an email. This milestone comes almost 18 months after the app reached the 6 billion download mark in January 2025.

Kempf mentioned that the company recently ported VLC to Amazon’s new Vega OS for TVs. The company said it is also working on supporting all other platforms and codecs and that VLC is preparing for the VLC 4 release, but it is taking time in development.

Last year, VideoLAN had teased AI-powered translation and subtitles using local models, but it is not clear if those features were ever released.
