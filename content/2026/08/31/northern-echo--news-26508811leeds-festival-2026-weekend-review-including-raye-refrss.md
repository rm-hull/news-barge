If you followed the forecast over the last week, yellow weather warnings and thunderstorms were predicted across the country over the August Bank Holiday weekend.

Thankfully, except the occasional downpour, Leeds Festival largely got away unscathed.

That was until Sunday evening, when a heavy thunderstorm hit Bramham Park during the break before headliner Raye arrived.

  Leeds Festival 2026 - weekend 

*(Image: Adam Kennedy)*

Despite this, the event’s hardened punters, many who had been waiting in front of the main stage all day, donned their ponchos and rain macks as the anticipation built for soulful superstar Raye.

And if you asked those at the front if it was worth the wait, the answer would have been an unequivocal yes.

Raye’s performance was not your typical festival set. Joined onstage by a gargantuan orchestra, the chart-topping artist arrived on stage looking like a million dollars.

  Leeds Festival 2026 - weekend 

*(Image: Adam Kennedy)*

Dressed in red, just like the stage set and flanked by her backing singers and band, the soulful songstress enchanted the Bramham Park crowd with her beautiful voice, radiating personality and arsenal of hits.

Raye provided the sunshine and brought her own thunder by way of her voice on what was one of the highlights of the whole weekend at Leeds Festival.

On Sunday afternoon, the main stage, now known as The Grid, was certainly the place to be at Leeds Festival.

Little Mix star JADE revelled in front of the huge crowd positioned in front of the stage and rejoiced at being back on home turf in the North, as she closed out her 2026 festival season.

Whilst US pop sensation Sombr drew a massive crowd late in the afternoon. The artist knew just how to work the audience as light rain descended upon Bramham Park.

And even ventured off stage into the pit to prove to the fans that he wasn’t afraid of getting his hands dirty, so to speak, as the rain poured down midway through his set.

  Leeds Festival 2026 - weekend 

*(Image: Adam Kennedy)*

The beauty of an event like Leeds Festival is witnessing many of the artists who are on their way up. The likes of Adela, Slayyyter and Skye Newman showcased the kinds of performances which could well see them headlining the main stage at Leeds in years to come.

These acts are also attracting a younger and new generation of music fans to the event. Skye Newman, in particular, brought a lot of young fans to the event, many of whom camped out in front of the stage at The Gallery on Saturday afternoon.

Over the course of the weekend, it would be hard not to notice the contingent of bands who were following in the footsteps of their famous parents.

With the likes of Moses Martin (son of Chris Martin and Gwyneth Paltrow) performing with his band People I’ve Met, Violet Grohl (daughter of Foo Fighters legend Dave Grohl), Gene Gallagher (son of Liam Gallagher) and his band Villanelle and Cruz Beckham (son of David and Victoria Beckham) all making appearances on The Canopy stage.

As the sun sets on another year at Bramham Park, we are already looking forward to next year’s event.
