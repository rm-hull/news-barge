A home in North Yorkshire has been struck by lightning in a thunderstorm that swept across Yorkshire last night.

Acomb and Huntington crews from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service were called to the home in Easingwold at 6.57pm on Sunday (August 30).

  


A service spokesperson said: “Crews used a thermal imaging camera to check for any hotspots in the loft space.

“Advice was given to the responsible person present.”
