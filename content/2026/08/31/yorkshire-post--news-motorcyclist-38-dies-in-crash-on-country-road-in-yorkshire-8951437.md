# Motorcyclist, 38, dies in crash on country road in Yorkshire

## A motorcyclist died after crashing into a fence on a country road in East Yorkshire.

The 38-year-old was riding a Suzuki on Fieldend Lane towards Burton Pidsea, just after 3pm on Friday, when it left the carriageway and hit a boundary fence.

His family is being supported by specially trained officers.

They’ve appealed for witnesses, and anyone with dashcam footage leading up to the collision, to call on the non-emergency number 101.
