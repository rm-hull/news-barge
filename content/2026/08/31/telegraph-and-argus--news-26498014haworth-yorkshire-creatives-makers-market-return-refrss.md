A grassroots market championing Yorkshire creatives is set to return to Haworth.

The Haworth & Yorkshire Creatives Makers Market will be held at The Old School Room, Church Street, on September 19 and 20 from 10am to 4pm.

The free event will showcase the work of 20 local artists.

The market is hosted by Rosalia Ferrara, founder of Haworth & Yorkshire Creatives, and Brontë Bitch, the Haworth brand inspired by the rebellious spirit of the Brontë sisters.

Ms Ferrara said: “I wanted to create another opportunity for talented local artists who don't always have a local platform to demonstrate their work.

“Visitors can meet the artists, learn about their creative process, and experience something far more engaging than simply browsing stalls.”

The dedicated artist space will feature Carolyn Edwards Art, KBJ Fine Art, Susie Jackson Art, Withens Art by Philippa, and guest Sleepy Hedgehog Designs.

Beyond the artist stage, visitors can explore a selection of independent Yorkshire makers.

This month, Delusional Doodles, Felts by Hannah, and Threads of Opal will make their Haworth debut, while Rob's Wood Turning returns after a successful first appearance.

Regular vendors include crochet plushies by Stitched with Love, Pawfect Pet designs & pet accessories and Can Can Creatives sea glass & fused art.

The Old School Room is located opposite the Brontë Parsonage Museum.
