A new boxing gym has opened in the North East, offering everything from fitness-focused sessions to serious ring craft.

Backed by local businessman David Cook - owner of wealth management company ‘Northern Spire’ - the Gateshead venture is the brainchild of coach and fighter Kieran Gorman.

He has been coaching Mr Cook since he took up the sport.

Mr Cook believes that Kieran Gorman Boxing (KGB) “can become a real asset for Gateshead and the wider area”.

He said: “I’ve always believed that businesses have a role to play in supporting the communities they operate in, and this is a project I’m particularly passionate about.

“Boxing teaches discipline, resilience, confidence and respect, and Kieran has a real passion for helping people develop those qualities.”

  Kieran Gorman, left, with backer David Cook at the new KGB Gym in Gateshead 

*(Image: NORTHERN SPIRE)*

Before opening KGB, Mr Gorman worked as a self-employed Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) coach.

He will continue coaching at KGB, alongside three other dedicated coaches.

More than 30 people have already joined the gym, which has a diverse membership.

Mr Cook has praised the “amazing cross section of members”, which includes ex-footballers, engineers, a paratrooper, company director, and a law student.

Speaking on the membership, Mr Cook said: “A lot of people come along without any intention of fighting a proper bout in a ring, it’s just about getting fit for them – we have strength and conditioning coaching available as well as boxing and Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) coaching.”

Both Mr Cook and Mr Gorman take part in competitive boxing - although Mr Cook will be fighting his last match on September 19.

“It’ll be my last fight as I’m getting on a bit now – I have shoes older than some of the lads joining the gym,” he said.

Speaking about Mr Cook’s assistance, Mr Gorman said: “I’m so grateful to David for his help and support in setting up KGB gym.

“He’s helped financially but also given me the confidence to go ahead and set my own place up.

“Establishing your own gym is a huge challenge, but having David’s support and experience behind me has made a massive difference.

“I want KGB to be somewhere that everyone feels welcome, whether they’re completely new to boxing, looking to get fit, or want to take their skills further.
