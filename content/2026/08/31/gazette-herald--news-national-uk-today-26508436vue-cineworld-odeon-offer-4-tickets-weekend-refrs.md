Film lovers can get cinema tickets at a reduced price across all major cinema chains for one weekend in September.

As Vue, Odeon, Cineworld and Showcase Cinemas celebrate National Cinema Weekend, customers will benefit from £4 cinema tickets.

The promotion applies to all films currently available at the cinemas, with some exceptions at Showcase Cinemas, and will run for two days – Saturday, September 5 and Sunday, September 6.


## Vue, Odeon, Cineworld and Showcase Cinemas offering £4 cinema tickets this weekend

Vue is offering customers £4 tickets for all films and seats across the weekend.

If you’re off to see a film at an Odeon cinema, you can expect to pay £4 for tickets, including IMAX, iSense, Dolby Cinema and XL screens.

Cineworld cinemas will offer the £4 ticket price for all films in most formats, with IMAX being £6 per ticket.

At Showcase Cinemas, film fans can see most films for £4 but the offer cannot be used when watching/attending:

- Shaun the Sheep: The Movie
- The Lord Of The Rings Motion Picture Trilogy: Extended Edition
- The Hunger Games Marathon
- Dirty Dancing Popcorn & Prosecco Party


## What films are currently showing at UK cinemas?

At the moment, there are several films you can watch at your local cinema.

These include:

- Spider-Man: Brand New Day
- The Odyssey
- Minions & Monsters
- Toy Story 5
- One Night Only
- The Invite

## Ray Winstone says British cinema has lost ‘a lot of our culture’

The news comes days after Ray Winstone said British cinema is losing “a lot of our culture” because the industry is caught up with the American way of filmmaking.

Winstone, 69, is best known for starring in 1979’s Scum, 1997’s Nil by Mouth and Jonathan Glazer’s 2000 film Sexy Beast.


The actor, who has worked in the industry for decades, said there is a need to make more projects about Britain and avoid getting caught up in the way other countries make film and TV.
