WE can now announce the top 10 best bakeries in the Bradford district.

From freshly baked bread and sourdough to pies, pasties, sandwiches, celebration cakes and sweet treats, there is no shortage of fantastic bakeries across the area.

Today, the Telegraph & Argus is revealing the finalists for its Best Bakery 2026 competition.

## 10 best bakeries in Bradford

These are as follows:

- Cupcakes By Sami - 766 Manchester Road, Bradford, BD5 7QP
- G & J Pitt Family Bakers - 55 Swain House Road, Bradford, BD2 1JN
- Hermano Bakery - 15 Waverley Terrace, Bradford, BD7 3HZ
- House of Bread - 253 Bradford Road, Frizinghall, Shipley, BD18 3AB
- Marshall’s Bakehouse - 13 High Street, Queensbury, Bradford, BD13 2PE
- Melvyn Davis Bakery - 32A Bingley Road, Shipley, BD18 4RU
- Miss Butterfinger’s - 26 The Green, Idle, Bradford, BD10 9PX
- Moore Bakery - Unit 6 Moore Industrial Park, Parry Lane, Bradford, BD4 8FB
- The Idle Bakery & Cafe - 12–14 Bradford Road, Idle, Bradford, BD10 9PP
- The Village Bakery - 14 Highgate, Heaton, Bradford, BD9 4BB

## Last year's winner

G&J Pitt Family Bakers will be looking to make it a hat-trick - having won in the previous two years.

For four decades, the team has been making anything from classic Cornish style pasties and ham salad sandwiches to teacakes and bread.

Geoff Pitt worked at the bakery for a long time before being offered the opportunity to buy the business when the previous owner, Jack, retired.

Geoff and his wife Janet have owned the shop since 1985 and they are now joined by son and business partner, Stephen Pitt.

## How to vote

Voting slips will then be printed in the Telegraph & Argus from Monday, August 31, until Saturday, September 12.

During that period, we will profile each of the finalists and readers will be able to back the business they believe deserves to be crowned the Telegraph & Argus Best Bakery 2026.

Telegraph & Argus editor Nigel Burton said: “The Best Bakery competition is a brilliant opportunity to shine a light on some of the amazing local businesses across the Bradford district.

“It is no secret that we have some fantastic bakeries serving up everything from fresh bread and cakes to pies, pasties and sweet treats.

“That’s why we are launching our Best Bakery 2026 competition. We want to celebrate the best of the best."
