# Man injured after BMW allegedly driven at him following street assault

## A man in his 30s was taken to hospital after being assaulted before a BMW was allegedly driven at him in Keighley.

The incident happened at the junction of Cavendish Street and Lawkholme Lane shortly after 8pm on Sunday (Aug 30).

Police said the victim, a man in his 30s, was assaulted in the street by another man.

The man then got into a BMW and drove it at the victim, and hit him with the car.

![The junction of Cavendish Street and Lawkholme Lane](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/31/12/25/junction-of-Cavendish-Street-and-Lawkholme-Lane.png?trim=0,139,0,139&crop=&width=640&quality=65)



The victim suffered a leg injury and was taken to hospital for treatment. His injury is not being treated as life-threatening.

Police have launched an appeal for any witnesses or anyone with information to come forward.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Multiple enquiries remain ongoing into the incident and anyone who can assist the investigation is asked to contact Bradford CID on 101 or online at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat)

“Those getting in touch should reference police crime number 13260495112.

“Information can also be given anonymously to the independent Crimestoppers charity on 0800 555 111.”
