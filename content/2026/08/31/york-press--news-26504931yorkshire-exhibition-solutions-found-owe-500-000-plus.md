A North Yorkshire business has gone into liquidation owing more than £500,000.

Yorkshire Exhibition Solutions of 23 Victoria Avenue, Harrogate, entered voluntary liquidation, following a vote to wind up the business.

Leeds-based Clough Corporate Solutions were subsequently appointed liquidator.

Companies House lists Robert James Wilcock and Jemma Louise Wilcock as the company's only directors.

A report known as a statement of affairs says the business had assets available for preferential creditors- those who are paid first- of £23,243.

The eight-year-old business had one such creditor, namely HMRC, which was owed £411,305, leaving a deficiency of £390,062.

In addition, when it came to unsecured creditors- those who are paid last- a total of £187, 724 was owed.

HMRC was owed a further £152,259, Clydesdale Bank was owed £24,817, an accountant was owed £6,192 and Virgin Money was owed £4166.

This gave an estimated debt or deficiency with regards to creditors of £577,486.

With the business having issued capital of £110, this left an overall deficit or debt of £577,591 owed.
