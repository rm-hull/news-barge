AN Italian entrepreneur behind popular venues in Bradford and Leeds is preparing to open a third branch.

Simo Alrababa, owner of La Caverna in Bradford's Sunbridge Wells and Brigante in Leeds city centre, is aiming to open his latest venture on Brook Street in Ilkley next month.

The new venue, which will also be called Brigante, will combine an authentic Italian pizzeria, café and bar with a community-focused upstairs space offering activities including yoga and running groups.

## Expanding a growing Yorkshire business

Mr Alrababa said work on the new restaurant is nearly complete, with the team now waiting for a specialist pizza oven to arrive from Italy before opening the doors.

  A wider view of the new Brigante site on Brook Street, Ilkley, which is currently being prepared ahead of its planned September opening. 

*(Image: Brigante)*

He said: "We finished the building and everything is built up...now we're waiting for the oven to come from Italy and waiting for the stock and cleaning up.

"We're thinking maybe the middle of September."

The restaurant will occupy premises at 26 Brook Street, opposite Piccolino in Ilkley.

While customers can expect traditional wood-fired pizzas, Mr Alrababa said the menu will also showcase dishes from Italy's Emilia-Romagna region, something he believes is largely missing from restaurants across Yorkshire.

  The interior of Brigante, the new Italian restaurant and community space set to open on Brook Street in Ilkley next month. 

*(Image: Brigante)*

## Inspired by family roots

Mr Alrababa said his passion for hospitality comes from his father, who owned a successful pizzeria in the Modena region of Italy.

He said: "I was in the pizzeria with my dad, so obviously passion was brought to me...I want to continue what he did in Italy."

Mr Alrababa's father ran a restaurant in the small town of Casinalbo, near Modena.

"His hard work and everything, I want to bring that back again," Mr Alrababa said.

"We were working with him when we were little."

## Bringing a taste of Emilia-Romagna to Yorkshire

Mr Alrababa believes many Italian restaurants in the UK focus on food from Naples, Rome or southern regions of the country, while the cuisine of Emilia-Romagna often goes overlooked.

"There is not much food from Emilia-Romagna in the UK," he said.

"Nobody does what we do from where we are from."

He hopes the Ilkley venue will introduce customers to traditional dishes inspired by the area around Modena while maintaining a strong focus on fresh, homemade food.

"What I see is when you do homemade food, everything fresh and with your passion, it works," he said.

## More than just a restaurant

  The new Brigante restaurant on Brook Street in Ilkley, where owner Simo Alrababa hopes to open a wood-fired pizzeria, café and community space in September. 

*(Image: Brigante)*

Alongside the pizzeria and bar, Mr Alrababa wants the venue to become a hub for local residents.

Plans include yoga sessions, activities for children, a running club and a daytime café aimed at bringing people together.

He said Ilkley had appealed to him for several years and that he had been searching for the right premises before finally securing the Brook Street site.

Mr Alrababa added: "I've been looking for three years and finally I found a place."

## Looking to the future

Despite the challenges facing the hospitality industry, Mr Alrababa said he remains optimistic and believes success comes from putting passion into a business.

"If you have to fail, you fail, but at least if you fail when you put your heart into it, that is what matters," he said.

"I'm there all the time. In the morning I'm in the café, in the evening I go to the pizzeria."

And while customers may come for the wood-fired pizzas and homemade tiramisu, Mr Alrababa hopes they leave with a sense of community too.

"We always liked Ilkley," he said. "I'm excited to bring something homemade and fresh there."
