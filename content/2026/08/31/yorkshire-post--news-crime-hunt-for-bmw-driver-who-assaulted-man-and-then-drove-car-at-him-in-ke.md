# Hunt for BMW driver who assaulted man and then drove car at him in Keighley

## A man was injured by a BMW which was driven into him in Keighley on Sunday night.

The victim who is in his 30s, was assaulted in the street, by another man just after 8pm on Sunday night.

His assailant then got into a BMW and drove it at him.

Officers at Bradford CID want to speak to witnesses or anyone with footage of the incident at the junction of Cavendish Street and Lawkholme Lane.

![Just after 8pm a man in his 30s was assaulted in the street by another man who then got into a BMW car and drove it at him.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjkyYTM0ZmUzLWRhZDUtNDI3Yi05NTkxLWU4MGVhMWY4YTNmZTpmYjljM2M3NC0wMTJkLTQwNzctYWEzYi1kMGNlMzAyYTJlZTY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The victim was struck by the car and suffered a leg injury. He was taken to hospital for treatment to the injury, which is not being treated as life threatening.

West Yorkshire Police said “multiple inquiries” are ongoing into the incident.
