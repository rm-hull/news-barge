Plans for six assisted living homes, described as a modest contribution to meeting a major housing shortfall, are being considered by Stockton Council.

Action4Care Ltd has applied for planning permission to build the six homes on Darlington Back Lane, Stockton, next to its own Springwood care home which caters for adults with learning difficulties. It runs six care homes in the North-east offering their residents “the chance to live in residential areas to help them to assimilate with society before living normal independent lives”.

“The proposed development is one step further into living in the community by offering selected adults the chance to live independent lives in small one-bedroom dwelling units but with an element of care when required,” says Eric Telford from the applicant’s agent Teldord Planning Associates in a planning, design and access statement sent to the council.

“The residents will be aged between 18-65 and while they will receive individual care packages where required, none of those residents have significant physical defects or receive nursing needs for the elderly.”

The plan proposes a car park providing five more spaces and four more cycle spaces.

The plans say there is a “clear and evidenced local need for housing-with-care/assisted living” in the Stockton area.

The statement adds: “With a net need of 444 units to 2032 and only 181 existing placements, the borough has a substantial and evidenced shortfall.

“The proposed scheme of six units will make a modest but positive contribution to meeting that identified need.”

It also says the site is in an sustainable urban location and that council guidance encourages such accommodation and policy supports specialist housing for older people or people needing an element of care.
