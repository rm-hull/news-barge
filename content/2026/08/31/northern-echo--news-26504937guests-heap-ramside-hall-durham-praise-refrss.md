A luxury County Durham spa has been praised for its "first-class" facilities.

Ramside Hall has an almost perfect rating of 4.3 stars from more than 3,400 Google reviews, and is home to a luxury spa, four restaurants, a hotel and an entertainment centre.

The spa features five pools, a spa garden with an infinity pool and a fully-equipped gym.

Guests can also enjoy a range of treatments, including massages and facials, in one of the 14 treatment rooms.

The lavish destination was originally a grand country house, home to the Pemberton family.

In 1963, the estate was converted into a hotel and in 1996, an 18-hole championship golf course was opened onsite.

In 2015, a major expansion introduced a second championship course, a new wing with 47 premier rooms, and Ramside Spa.

The hotel has a variety of unique hotel rooms on offer, including treehouse lodges, a farmhouse, a celebration suite and more.

Ramside Hall also receives praise for its dining experience.

It is home to four restaurants - The Rib Room, Fusion, Charcoa and The Pin Sports Bar.

The Rib Room offers steaks, fresh seafood, and a hand-picked wine list.

Fusion boasts bold Asian flavours, fresh ingredients, and dishes made for the table.

Charcoa is where you'll find a carvery and pub classics with wood-fired flavours, prime cuts, and small plates made for sharing.

The Pin Sports Bar makes for more casual dining with loaded burgers, stone‑baked pizzas, wings, sharable platters and more.
