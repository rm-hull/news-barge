## York-born Oscar-winning actress and charity patron has 'pride of place' in photobook

York Against Cancer's patron, Dame Judi Dench, takes "pride of place" on the cover of photobook "Yorkshire's Finest" - created by photographer David Charles (file image featured here) *(Image: Robert Wilson / PA Wire)*

A PHOTOBOOK of Yorkshire celebrities is raising funds for a cancer charity, and includes images of its patron, a York-born icon of stage and screen.

Yorkshire’s Finest, a decade-long project by photographer David Charles, features portraits of well-known figures including Dame Judi Dench, Sean Bean and Gaynor Faye.

Every penny raised from sales will go to York Against Cancer.

  Andy Knowles, CEO of York Against Cancer with photographer David Charles. Every penny of the proceeds of sales from 

*(Image: York Against Cancer)*

Andy Knowles, CEO of the charity, said: "I'm immensely proud that our patron, Dame Judi, takes pride of place on the cover and centre pages of ‘Yorkshire's Finest’.

"This isn't just a collection of photographs and words, it is a decade of dedication from the remarkably talented and generous David Charles, who has captured so many of Yorkshire's best-loved faces with real warmth and skill.

"That he's chosen to donate every penny of the proceeds to York Against Cancer is an extraordinary act of kindness, and on behalf of all the local people we support, I want to say a heartfelt thank you.

"Every copy sold makes a genuine difference.

"So please buy one if you can, you'll own a beautiful piece of Yorkshire heritage and help us continue our vital work.

"It's the easiest good deed you'll do all year."

The book is available to pre-order online from [https://yorkagainstcancer.enthuse.com/yorkshiresfinest.](https://yorkagainstcancer.enthuse.com/yorkshiresfinest.)

York Against Cancer is an independent charity caring for cancer patients and their families in York and parts of North and East Yorkshire.

Since starting in 1987, the charity has raised over £20 million to support people in York and across the region.

David Charles said: "Having gone through my own battles with cancer, I know firsthand the challenges and emotional toll it can take, not just on the individual, but on their families as well.

"I was inspired to create a unique photobook to raise funds and awareness for York Against Cancer after learning how they do so much for the Yorkshire community."

Tickets have already sold-out for a launch event on Monday, September 28 from 6pm to 8pm at The Leveson Centre in Kathyrn Avenue.
