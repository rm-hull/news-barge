Police are investigating reports that a car was driven at a man in a Bradford district town centre.

Police were called on Sunday night to reports of an incident involving an assault and collision which happened at the junction of Lawkholme Lane and Cavendish Street in Keighley.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for witnesses and footage after a car was driven at a man in Keighley.

"Officers at Bradford CID would like to speak to an anyone who saw or has footage of the incident which took place at the junction of Cavendish Street and Lawkholme Lane on Sunday night (August 30).

"It occurred at just after 8pm when the victim, a man in his 30s, was assaulted in the street by a man who then got into a BMW car and drove it at him.

"The victim was struck by the car and suffered a leg injury. He was taken to hospital for treatment to the injury which is not being treated as life threatening.

"Multiple enquiries remain ongoing into the incident and anyone who can assist the investigation is asked to contact Bradford CID on 101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat

"Those getting in touch should reference police crime number 13260495112.

"Information can also be given anonymously to the independent Crimestoppers charity on 0800 555 111."
