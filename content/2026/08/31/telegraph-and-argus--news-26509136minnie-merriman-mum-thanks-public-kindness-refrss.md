A mother has spoken of her heartbreak and gratitude following the death of her nine-year-old daughter.

Minnie Merriman, from Wakefield, West Yorkshire, died in hospital in Dundee after being found seriously injured in Arbroath, Scotland, on August 3.

Her father, Christopher Rice, is accused of murdering his daughter and is currently remanded in custody ahead of a trial.

Minnie’s mother, Laurie Merriman, has publicly thanked the people of Arbroath for their “incredible support.”

In a Facebook post, she said: "I want to thank you wholeheartedly for the incredible love and support you have shown me and my beautiful Minnie during the worst time of my life.

"The humanity and kindness carries me and will be with me every step of the way."

Hundreds of people gathered at Victoria Park in Arbroath last week to pay their respects.

A private ambulance carrying Minnie’s body back to England was accompanied by a motorcycle escort to the park. Local people laid flowers, poems and toys beside the private ambulance.

A piper led a procession accompanied by Minnie’s family.

Ms Merriman said: "To every person who came out for Minnie's journey home, every biker, neighbour and stranger who stood with us—thank you.

"To the football club for your support and the Arbroath shirts for Minnie's little brothers, the family who gave us a place to stay when we needed it most, and for the giant chocolate gifts that brought a smile to the boys' little faces.

"I will never forget your kindness."

She also expressed gratitude to those who donated and helped raise money in Minnie’s name.

A minutes' silence was held in her memory before Arbroath FC’s match against Inverness Caledonian Thistle on August 8.

Emergency services were called to the Elliot Industrial Estate in Arbroath shortly after midnight on August 3 following reports that a child had been found injured.

Minnie was taken to Ninewells Hospital along with four other people, but later died as a result of her injuries.
