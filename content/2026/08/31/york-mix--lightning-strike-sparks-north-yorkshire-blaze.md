This was the scene last night after a lightning strike set two barns on fire.

Crews from Malton, Kirkbymoorside, Scarborough and Tadcaster’s water bowser went to the incident at **Kirby Misperton** at**8.32pm last night** (Sunday).

Straw was stored in one of the barns, while the other was empty.

#### Kirby Misperton

Max Downing, who took the picture of the blaze above, said he saw the lightning strike which started the fire.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A dam was set up to cool three propane cylinders.

“Crews monitored the temperature using a thermal imaging camera. Four hose reel jets were used to extinguish the fire.

“Fire crews remain in scene at this incident. A fire investigation is to be conducted in daylight hours.”
