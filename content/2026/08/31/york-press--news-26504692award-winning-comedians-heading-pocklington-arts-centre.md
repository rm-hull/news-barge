Stand-up comedians from the UK and international circuits will be performing in Pocklington later this year.

The event will see four comedians take to the stage at Pocklington Arts Centre, with the organisers promising "something for everyone".

The line-up includes seasoned performer Dan Evans, known for his international gigs and contributions to shows featuring Milton Jones and Frankie Boyle.

Mr Evans is praised for his "original and inventive" style by The Times.

Liverpool's Steve Gribbin, a satirical comedian with a guitar, will also perform.

His comedy songs and off-the-wall observations have made him popular in the comedy circuit.

The Scotsman describes him as a "highly refreshing and original comic."

Catherine Young, winner of the Hilarity Bites New Act of the Year award in 2019, will bring her light-hearted and mischievous humour to the stage.

Storyteller Carl Jones will complete the line-up, known for his quick-witted audience interaction.

Mr Jones is also an award-winning podcast host and a contributor to Radio 4’s comedy shows.

The bar will open an hour before the event, with the auditorium opening 30 minutes before.
