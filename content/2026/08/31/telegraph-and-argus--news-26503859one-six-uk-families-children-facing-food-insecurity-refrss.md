ONE in six UK households with children are experiencing food insecurity, according to new figures from The Food Foundation.

Feedo Needo, a food bank charity operating in Bradford, Birmingham, London, and Manchester, said the return to school is putting additional pressure on already stretched families.

For many parents, back-to-school costs such as uniforms and transport are making it even harder to afford regular, healthy meals.

Gracefilled Okeke, project manager at Feedo Needo Birmingham, said: "While September is an exciting time for many children, we know that for some families it can also be one of the most financially demanding periods of the year.

"Alongside everyday household expenses, parents are often facing the added costs associated with going back to school."

The Food Foundation’s research shows 12.2 per cent of UK households face food insecurity, rising to 16.7 per cent among those with children.

Nearly four in ten households said it is harder to afford food than a year ago, and almost one in ten have used food support services in the past six months.

Feedo Needo is encouraging greater awareness of the challenges faced by families and the important role played by charities, volunteers, and community partners in supporting those in need.
