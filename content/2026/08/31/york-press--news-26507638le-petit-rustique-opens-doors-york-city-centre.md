## New French wine bar and bistro opens its doors in York city centre

Le Petit Rustique in Castlegate officially opens its doors at 10am today (Monday, August 31) *(Image: Contributed)*

A NEW French bistro and wine bar opens its doors in York today (Monday, August 31).

Le Petit Rustique will open at 10am in Castlegate.

The venue is the "sister site" of the long-established Rustique restaurant next door, which has been part of York’s dining scene since 2004.

Laurent Forestier, a director of Le Petit Rustique, said: "Food will be served from 11am, with the daytime menu available until 5pm, featuring French café and bistro favourites including croques, buckwheat crêpes, homemade tartes, omelettes, baguettes, salads and petits plats.

"From 11am to 10pm, guests can also enjoy our Assiette sharing platters - From Land, From Sea, Fromagerie and From Garden - alongside a selection of nibbles, perfect for sharing over a bottle of wine.

"Wine is very much at the heart of Le Petit Rustique.

"Our carefully selected list has a strong French influence, with wines available by the glass and bottle, alongside Champagne, Crémant, sparkling, rosé and orange wine.

"Whether it’s a lunchtime glass, an afternoon drink or a bottle shared with an Assiette in the evening, the aim is simple - relaxed food, good wine and a little taste of France in the heart of York."

As reported by The Press during the works phase to prepare the site, a spokesperson for the new business said: "Nestled next door to Rustique, Le Petit Rustique embraces the relaxed spirit of the traditional French bistro and wine bar.

"It offers a cosy setting for leisurely lunches, glasses of wine with friends, and relaxed evenings of sharing food and conversation.

"Whether you're joining us for a quick bite, an apéritif, or a leisurely evening, you'll find the same passion for French hospitality that has defined Rustique for over twenty years, now in a more intimate bistro setting."

"Bienvenue chez Le Petit Rustique."

  The new premises is next door to "big sister" Rustique - a staple of the York dining scene for more than 20 years 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

Le Petit Rustique takes over the site formerly occupied by Pairings, one of the city’s most popular wine bars and delis, which closed its doors in January after 10 years.

Le Petit Rustique opens Monday 31 August at 10am, with food served from 11am.
