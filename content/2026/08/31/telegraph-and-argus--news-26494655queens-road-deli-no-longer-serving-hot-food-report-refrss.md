A BRADFORD district deli that received a one-star food hygiene rating following an inspection has stopped serving food.

Queen’s Road Deli in Keighley, which also operates as a newsagents, was inspected by an environmental health officer on January 14 earlier this year.

The shop, whose food operator is Hooria Kly LTD, was found to have multiple food safety breaches.

## No wash basin in the kitchen

The report found that there was no sink in the kitchen.

Inspectors urged staff to install one and ensure that it was connected to the drainage system.

The report said: "Also provide hot and cold running water, soap and hand drying."

## Follow-up visit found 'three bins of refuse'

A follow-up visit on February 25 revealed further issues, including no schedule for cleaning down surfaces, cluttered storage, and that the gas meter had run out and was not connected to the hot water.

The inspection also identified three bins full of waste.

The report said: "You must ensure that this commercial waste is removed."

A wash basin had been obtained and was set to be installed at the time of the re-visit.

## Aftermath of the inspection

When the newspaper contacted Queen's Road Deli, a spokesperson told us they no longer serve hot food.

They said: "We stopped serving food a couple of months ago, after the report."

The shop now operates as a newsagents only, the spokesperson added.
