Police are investigating after an overturned car was found abandoned in bushes.

Officers were called to Bayton Lane in Horsforth after receiving multiple reports about the vehicle, which appeared to have been abandoned at the scene.

No reports of a collision were received.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police have received several calls regarding an abandoned, overturned car on Bayton Lane in Horsforth.

"No reports of a collision were received, and recovery has been arranged for the car.

"Enquiries remain ongoing."
