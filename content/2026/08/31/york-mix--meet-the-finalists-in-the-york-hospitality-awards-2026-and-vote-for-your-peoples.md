The York Hospitality Awards are back for 2026 – and you can have your say in your hospitality hero!

Hospitality Association York (HAY) launched the awards in 2024 to celebrate those who go above and beyond to give a warm welcome to visitors.

And now they’re back for 2026, and you can again play a part – by voting for your favourite in the **People’s Choice Award**, sponsored by ***YorkMix***.

Below are 2026’s People’s Choice Award finalists – you can vote at the end.

The People’s Choice award acknowledges team members who have made an exemplary contribution to their business or the wider industry or community.

The closing date for voting is **Friday 4 September**.

## Margaret Rus

**Margaret Rus** – Hilton York

Margaret Rus is described as the “beating heart” of Hilton York, becoming the trusted person colleagues turn to for support, advice and guidance. Over the past year, she has helped steer the hotel through a major refurbishment and significant leadership changes, while maintaining **team engagement scores of more than 90% across all areas**. A passionate champion of colleague wellbeing, Margaret has introduced initiatives including financial wellbeing sessions and suicide prevention workshops, while also building strong community partnerships with schools, colleges, Jobcentre Plus and Springboard to create opportunities and inspire the next generation of hospitality talent. Her combination of professional expertise, compassion and genuine commitment to people has helped create a supportive, inclusive culture where colleagues feel valued and proud to work.

## Poppy Frear

**Poppy Frear** – Middletons Hotel

Poppy Frear, Food & Beverage Supervisor at Middletons Hotel, is described as a positive, personable and hugely dedicated member of the team who consistently goes above and beyond for guests and colleagues. Passionate about developing others, she plays a key role in coaching and mentoring team members while also investing in her own development through a **Level 3 Hospitality Apprenticeship**. Poppy is constantly looking for ways to improve the guest experience, from introducing new service standards to finding ways to improve communication and personalise the guest journey. Her warmth, resilience and determination shine through even under pressure, with colleagues describing her as someone who always leads by example, tackles challenges with optimism and puts the needs of guests and her team first.

## Valentina Semeraro

**Valentina Semeraro** – Radisson York

Valentina Semeraro has spent more than eight years at the hotel, progressing from F&B Assistant to Meeting & Events Manager and becoming a valued member of the HOD team. A true advocate for hospitality, she is known for her **“Yes I Can”** approach, going above and beyond to make every event a success and always finding a solution for guests. Over the past 12 months, she has led a project to grow the hotel’s social events business, successfully securing ** more than £120,000 in new business**, while also achieving the company’s highest Office health check score of ** 97%**. Alongside her busy role, Valentina volunteers as the hotel’s Mental Health First Aider, reflecting her genuine care for colleagues and her commitment to supporting others. Her positivity, dedication and willingness to go the extra mile make her a true hospitality hero.

## Brogan & Catherine

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Peoples-Choice-Award-The-Milner-York-Brogan-and-Catherine-MOVED-FROM-WARM-WELCOME.jpeg)

**Brogan Newton & Catherine Gent** – The Milner York

Brogan Newton and Catherine Gent have become quite the double act at The Milner York, bringing warmth, personality and plenty of energy to the hotel’s Reception team. Both have been recognised as **Employee of the Month** over the past 12 months, but it’s their everyday impact that truly sets them apart. They instinctively support one another, look out for colleagues and go above and beyond to make every guest feel welcome, from recognising returning visitors to helping first-time guests discover the best of York. Their genuine care, infectious friendship and ability to make people feel at ease ensure that every guest receives a warm Yorkshire welcome from the moment they arrive. As two of the first faces guests see, Brogan and Catherine are not just representing The Milner York — they’re brilliant ambassadors for the city too.
