# Motorcyclist, 38, dies after crashing into fence on East Yorkshire road

## A 38-year-old motorcyclist has died after his bike left the road and crashed into a boundary fence in East Yorkshire.

The crash happened on Fieldend Lane in Estronwick, near Burton Pidsea, shortly after 3pm on Friday (Aug 28).

Police said a dark-coloured Suzuki motorcycle was travelling along Fieldend Lane towards Burton Pidsea when it left the road and crashed into a boundary fence.

![Fieldend Lane in Estronwick, near Burton Pidsea](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/31/13/12/Fieldend-Lane-in-Estronwick-near-Burton-Pidsea.png?trim=0,63,0,63&crop=&width=640&quality=65)



Emergency services attended, but the 38-year-old rider was pronounced dead at the scene.

Police have asked that any witnesses, or anyone with information, would come forward.

A spokesperson for Humberside Police said: “The man's family has been informed and is being supported by specially trained officers.

“Enquiries remain ongoing to determine the full circumstances around the collision, and we would ask anyone who may have witnessed the incident, has any information, including dashcam footage leading up to the collision, to please contact us on our non-emergency number 101 quoting log 296 of 28 August.”
