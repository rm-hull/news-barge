CLOSURES are planned for a major road outside York while resurfacing work is carried out.

City of York Council said the A1079 Hull Road will close across five areas from 8pm on Monday, September 7 and 6am on Saturday, September 19.

The council said the closures are to ensure the works can be carried out safely and it expects the road will only be closed between 8pm and 6am on each day of the works period.

  An advanced warning sign on the A1079 before Bore Tree Baulk ahead of the road closure 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

In a planning notice the council said the five closures on the A1079 will be:

1. Between the junction with Bore Tree Baulk (close to Grimston Bar roundabout and Grimston Court residential care home) and the loop road at Kexby Bridge
2. Between the junction with York Road outside Dunnington and the junction with the layby which contains The Scrap Box fish and chip shop
3. Between the junction with Common Lane in Dunnington and the junction with Common Road in Dunnington
4. Between the junctions of Common Road in Dunnington and Hagg Lane in Dunnington
5. Between the junction with Dauby Lane (for Elvington just after the sign for Kexby) and a point 130 metres closer to Kexby

  An advanced warning sign on the A1079 at Kexby ahead of the road closure 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

The five junctions connect the villages of Dunnington, Elvington and Kexby to the A1079 where the resurfacing works are taking place.

The A1079 is a key route between York and the Yorkshire Wolds and Kingston-upon-Hull, and is one of a limited number of main roads crossing the River Derwent.

Traffic signs and barriers have already gone up on the A1079 warning motorists of the proposed closures.

An alternative route for diverted vehicles will be signed during the works period via the A1079, A614, A63, A19 and A64, the council said.

Access for residents and emergency vehicles will be maintained throughout the works period.

Traffic signs and barriers will mark the restriction.
