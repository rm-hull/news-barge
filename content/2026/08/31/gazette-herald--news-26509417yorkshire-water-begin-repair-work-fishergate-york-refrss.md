A section of York's inner ring road which was damaged in Sunday’s storm is set to remain closed for "a few days" as repair work is carried out.

A team from Yorkshire Water started work at the Fishergate Gyratory, at the junction of Fishergate with Paragon Street, shortly before 3pm on Monday (August 31).

  


Yorkshire Water began removing the lifted road surface using a concrete saw at around 3.15pm.

  Yorkshire Water arrived at the scene with a digger shortly before 3pm on Monday (August 31) 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

After placing barriers along the pedestrian crossing, a representative for Yorkshire Water said: “We will be cutting this section out, ready for another team tomorrow who will begin placing fresh tarmac on the road.”

  City of York Council closed a section of Fishergate after a section had uplifted after the storm on Sunday (August 30) 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

City of York Council said the road would remain closed "for a few days" and vehicles heading eastbound on Tower Street would be diverted via Piccadilly and Walmgate Bar.

  Traffic is heavy along streets to Walmgate Bar 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

A council statement said: "The road will need to be closed for a few days while we work with Yorkshire Water to understand the cause, then get it fixed.

"For now, all traffic (including buses) will be diverted."

  A barrier was created by Yorkshire Water for the team to safely begin cutting the road with a concrete saw 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Motorists turning on to Piccadilly, next to The Postern Gate Wetherspoons pub, from Tower Street, are currently being diverted to Lead Mill Lane.

This is causing heavy traffic, which is building along George Street, Margaret Street and Walmgate.
