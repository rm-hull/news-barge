The York road closed by storm damage will stay shut for a few more days yet.

As ***YorkMix*** reported on Sunday night, the torrential rain led to water surging from the drains on Tower Street.

That lifted the road surface at the pedestrian crossing where Tower Street meets Fishergate and Paragon Street.

#### Damage location

As a result the Fishergate gyratory was closed.

On Monday afternoon, City of York Council has issued an update.

It said: “The road will need to be closed for a few days while we work with Yorkshire Water to understand the cause, then get it fixed.

“For now, all traffic (including buses) remains diverted.”

The diversion is via Piccadilly and Walmgate Bar. That has led to queuing on Walmgate, which is likely to be worse in the coming days after the Bank Holiday.
