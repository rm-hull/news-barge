A TREE has blocked a major train line in and out of York.

The tree blocked a section of railway between Doncaster and Retford, affecting LNER trains between York and London Kings Cross.

Other services affected include Hull Trains between Hull and London Kings Cross.

  


Disruption, National Rail have said, is expected until the end of the day.

A spokesperson said: “Trains may be delayed by up to 30 minutes or revised.”

Customers can use their ticket on the following:

- CrossCountry between Edinburgh / Newcastle and York, Leeds / Doncaster, Sheffield
- Grand Central between York and London Kings Cross
- Hull Trains between Doncaster and London Kings Cross
- TransPennine Express between Manchester, Leeds and York/Newcastle.
