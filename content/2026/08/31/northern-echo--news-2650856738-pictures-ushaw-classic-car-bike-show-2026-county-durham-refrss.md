Hundreds of classic cars and motorbikes descended on the grounds of Ushaw for a major motoring show over the Bank Holiday weekend.

The second Ushaw Classic Car & Bike Show of 2026 was held at Ushaw Historic House, Chapels & Gardens, near Durham, on Sunday (August 30).

   Ushaw Classic Car & Bike Show 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

More than 600 vintage, classic and modern classic cars and bikes were displayed across the historic grounds during the event, which ran from 10am until 4pm.

The show brought together motoring enthusiasts and families for a day celebrating vehicles from across the decades, all against the backdrop of the historic Ushaw estate.

   Ushaw Classic Car & Bike Show 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

As well as the impressive collection of cars and motorcycles, visitors could enjoy live music, children's activities and other family-friendly entertainment.

Local street food traders, bars and coffee stalls were also available throughout the day, while a selection of outdoor market and trade stalls were set up on Bounds Field.

   Ushaw Classic Car & Bike Show 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Entry was included with general admission to Ushaw and for annual members.

The event was supported by headline sponsor Workwear Express and sponsor 121 Business Links.
