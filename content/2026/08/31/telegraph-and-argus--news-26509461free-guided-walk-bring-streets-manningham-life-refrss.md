A free guided walk will bring history to life on the streets of Manningham.

Led by a guide in costume as industrialist Samuel Lister, the circular tour will highlight notable buildings and people from the area's past and present.

It will also touch on future plans for the neighbourhood.

Organiser Peter Nias said: "Whether you are a current or former resident of the area or an interested Bradfordian, a visitor or a student, you are very welcome to join in."

This marks the 46th Manningham guided walk and forms part of the National Heritage Open Days 2026 programme.

The walk starts at 2pm on Sunday, September 13, outside Manningham Mills on Lilycroft Road, opposite the police station, and lasts around one hour and 45 minutes.

Free on-street parking is available on Patent Street.
