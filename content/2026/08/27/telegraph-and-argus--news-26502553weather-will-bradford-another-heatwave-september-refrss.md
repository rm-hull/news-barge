THE MET Office has issued a warning for thunderstorms and heavy downpours in Bradford.

Weather forecasters said the district should be braced for bad weather on Saturday.

The district faces thunderstorms and heavy showers from Saturday afternoon.

A spokesman said: "Saturday will see any early brightness replaced by cloudy skies and outbreaks of rain in the afternoon."

The maximum temperature will be 18C - a far cry from two weeks ago when the mercury soar to 30C.

Conditions should improve marginally on Sunday when the district will still experience the occasional shower but the temperature will peak at 20C.

The medium-term forecast doesn't hold out much hope for a return to consistently high temperatures and dry weather conditions.

Bradford is expected to see cloudy conditions and heavy showers for the first week of September.

The Met Office said: "Atlantic weather systems may continue to dominate conditions into the first week of September, so further periods of wet and windy or showery weather look most likely, with short-lived drier and sunnier spells in between. The north and west may be wetter than the south and east. Temperatures are most likely to be near or slightly above normal."

But summer may not have gone forever.

The Met Office went on: "By the second week of September there are signs of a possible change. High pressure could begin building again across continental Europe, and this may push warmer air towards the UK, with temperatures ending up further above the September average."
