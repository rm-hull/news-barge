Residents on a Bishop Auckland street were left “shocked” when a hot air balloon landed outside their homes.

The Virgin-branded balloon landed in a ditch just off Marsh Way on the Bishops Park housing development around 8.30pm on Saturday (August 22).

Hollie Smith, who lives near where the balloon landed, thought it was an optical illusion at first.

She said: "We saw it coming towards the estate.

"It started to come down and then it went back up again so we thought it was an optical illusion and it was actually far away.

"But then it just kept going down and down and down.

"I went to where it had landed and people were running out in their pyjamas with their dogs."


Images on social media show the balloon landing near houses in Bishop Auckland and then deflating with a large crowd surrounding it.

Hollie, 20, decided to ring emergency services.

"The operator thought it was a prank call," she said. "He said he'd never had something like this in his life.

"He had to speak to his manager to see who would have to deal with it."

Rachel Scott, who lives in Bishops Park, added: "It was obviously a shock but exciting at the same time.

"We went outside to have a look because everyone was coming out of their houses.

"All the kids were having a look, and there were cars coming down who must have seen it."

It is not yet known why the balloon landed in the middle of the estate. Virgin did not respond to the Echo's request for comment. The UK's Air Accidents Investigation Branch said it had not been notified of any incident.

It is understood the basket of the hot air balloon was eventually loaded onto a tow truck and taken away.

Hollie added that residents helped wrap up the balloon: "It was nice to see everyone coming together and helping each other."
