A NEW festival is set to celebrate the best of food and drink.

The first-ever Calderdale Festival of Food will run from September 7 to 20, with events taking place across towns and villages in the borough.

Organised by Visit Calderdale with support from the Calderdale Food Network, the two-week festival will include markets, guided tours, tasting sessions and special offers.

Councillor Keith Ingle, Calderdale Council’s cabinet member for economic regeneration, said: "The Calderdale Festival of Food offers people the opportunity to discover a new favourite venue or explore hidden gems in their local area, with special events, offers and exclusive tours.

"It’s also a chance to support local businesses, including our local markets, and experience the creativity and passion at the heart of the borough’s thriving food and drink scene.

"We look forward to welcoming residents and visitors alike to explore, taste and celebrate all that Calderdale has to offer."

Highlights from the packed programme include artisan markets, fine dining evenings, family farm tours and a harvest festival.

There will be foraging experiences and camping experiences at Old Chamber Farm in Hebden Bridge, offering scenic views of the Calder Valley.

The festival supports the Calderdale Food Network’s new Food Strategy, which promotes a stronger and more sustainable local food economy.
