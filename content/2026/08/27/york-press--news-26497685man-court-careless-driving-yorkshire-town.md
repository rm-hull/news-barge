A MAN has admitted causing serious injury by careless driving on a main road in a North Yorkshire town.

Alec Newman was banned from driving after pleading guilty to the offence at York Crown Court on Wednesday (August 26).

The charge relates to an incident in Scott Road, Selby, on November 28, 2025, which left a man seriously injured.

Newman, 68, of The Ruddings, Selby, admitted to leaving the man seriously injured by driving his Mazda on the road, near Selby Leisure Centre, without due care and attention.

  Scott Road in Selby, where the incident took place 

*(Image: Google)*

The Recorder of York, Judge Sean Morris, ordered a report to be prepared before Newman is sentenced at the same court on Thursday, October 8.

Newman stood in the dock wearing a dark suit and spoke only to confirm his name and enter the guilty plea.

Judge Morris told Newman: “You have been very wise to accept your culpability in this way and that will be reflected in the sentence.”

The judge said he could not say what the sentence would be as it may be decided by another judge.

He banned Newman from driving after the court heard Newman had not driven to York Crown Court for the brief hearing.
