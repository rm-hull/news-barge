Police have issued this picture of a wanted man from York.

He is Anthony Jackson, 39. He’s been recalled to prison after he breached the terms of his licence.

Jackson is believed to still be in the city.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Jackson and we are now appealing for your help to find him.

“If you have an immediate sighting of him, please call 999.

“If you have any other information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online.”
