## The Press announces its nominated charity for York Community Carol Concert

York's Steve Cassidy performed a Christmas medley with choir singers, including children from *(Image: Anna Mehta Photography)*

Age UK York has been announced as the nominated charity for this year's York Community Carol Concert, which returns to York Barbican on Sunday, December 13.

The much-loved festive event, now in its 68th year, will bring together families and communities from across York for an afternoon of Christmas carols, seasonal music and festive entertainment, while raising money for local causes.

Organisers have confirmed that this year's concert will support both the Lord Mayor and Sheriff of York's Christmas Cheer Fund and Age UK York.

The annual concert has become a long-standing tradition in the city's Christmas calendar, welcoming hundreds of people to York Barbican each December.

This year's line-up will feature performances from York RI Golden Rail Band, Huntington School Big Sing Choir, Copmanthorpe Primary School Choir, soloist Alexandra Mather and regular performer Steve Cassidy.

Musical director Mike Pratt will lead the community singing, while Rev Andrew Foster and Adam Tomlinson will host the event.

Age UK York, a local independent charity supporting older people across the city, said it was delighted to have been selected as this year's charity partner.

Simon Holmes, chief executive of Age UK York, said: "We are absolutely delighted and incredibly grateful to The York Press for choosing Age UK York as the charity partner for this year's Community Carol Concert.

"It is a wonderful opportunity to bring people together in the spirit of Christmas while raising awareness and vital funds to support older people across York.

  Simon Holmes, the chief executive officer of Age UK York 

*(Image: Age UK York)*

"As a local independent charity here in York, the support of our local community means so much to us, and being chosen by The York Press is a real honour.

"We look forward to being part of what promises to be a very special afternoon and would like to thank everyone involved in making the Community Carol Concert possible.

"We look forward to seeing you there for a great event."

The concert traces its roots back to 1958 and remains one of York's most popular festive community events.

Last year's event raised thousands of pounds for local charities, demonstrating the continued generosity of residents and supporters across the region.

As well as providing a traditional start to the Christmas season, the event offers an opportunity for local organisations to showcase their work and connect with residents while raising funds for important causes.

Organisers are encouraging people of all ages to attend and join in with the community singing.

*(Image: Age UK York)*

Ben Hardy, editor of The Press and the Gazette & Herald, said: "The Community Carol Concert has been bringing people together for generations and remains one of the highlights of York's festive calendar.

"We are delighted that Age UK York will be our nominated charity this year.

"The work the charity carries out every day to support older people across our city is invaluable, and we hope this partnership will help raise both awareness and vital funds.

"We look forward to welcoming readers, supporters and families from across the region to what promises to be another fantastic community event."

York's Annual Community Carol Concert will take place at York Barbican on Sunday, December 13, at 2pm.

Tickets are on sale through York Barbican, with proceeds helping to support Age UK York and The Lord Mayor and Sheriff of York's Christmas Cheer Fund.
