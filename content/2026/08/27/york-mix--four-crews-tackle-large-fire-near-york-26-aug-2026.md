Firefighters tackled a large field blaze near York last night (Wednesday).

They were called to **Low Moor Lane, Hessay** at**5.02pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb crew responded to reports of a fire in a stubble field.

#### Low Moor Lane, Hessay

“On arrival the crew confirmed the fire was 100m by 300m and requested support from a further two appliances and an Argocat.

“The Argocat was from Settle and the other two appliances were Huntington and Harrogate. The cause of the fire is believed to be accidental.”
