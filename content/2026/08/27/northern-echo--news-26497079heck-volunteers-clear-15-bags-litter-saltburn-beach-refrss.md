Volunteers gathered along the North Yorkshire coast last week to join a community beach clean-up organised by a Yorkshire food company.

Family-run sausage and burger producers, HECK!, brought together staff and local volunteers on Saltburn beach on Wednesday, August 19, to help clear litter from the coastline and protect one of the North East's most popular seaside destinations.

The clean-up was hailed a huge success, with more than 15 large bags filled with litter, plastics and other rubbish picked up from the beach by volunteers.

  Volunteers clearing up litter from Saltburn beach. 

*(Image: HECK!)*

The Saltburn meet marks the fourth HECK! beach clean-up, and forms part of its wider litter-picking initiative to help keep local beauty spots clean and support local communities.

Locals of all ages got involved, with children helping collect waste from the beach while also spending time outdoors with family and friends and enjoying the views.

After the clean-up, HECK! thanked volunteers with battered sausage and chips - sourced by the company and cooked by local favourite, The Seaview Restaurant.

  Volunteers waiting for sausage and chips after the clean-up. 

*(Image: HECK!)*

  Volunteers were rewarded with sausage and chips, provided by Heck and cooked up by the Seaview Restaurant. 

*(Image: HECK!)*

Becky Keeble, from HECK!, said: "We believe small actions can make a big difference.

"Our beaches are such a vital part of the Yorkshire community, and we're proud to play a part in keeping them safe, clean and beautiful for everyone to enjoy."

HECK!'s wider environmental programme includes reducing packaging waste, sourcing ingredients responsibly, using renewable energy and supporting community projects across the North East and Yorkshire.

Saltburn beach attracts thousands of visitors throughout the year, with volunteers and community groups regularly organising litter picks to help protect wildlife and keep the coastline looking its best.

For more than a century, families have flocked to the Victorian seaside town to enjoy its pier and seafront - making community efforts to keep the beach clean all the more important to preserve it for future generations.
