14 sets of roadworks are affecting Darlington roads on Thursday, August 27.

Drivers should expect temporary traffic signals, lane restrictions and give-and-take traffic at locations across the town.

The works involve construction, telecoms, electricity, gas and emergency repair activity.

## A167 Grange Road

Construction at Southend Avenue is causing temporary traffic signals on the A167 Grange Road in both directions.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until August 27 at 11pm. Delays are expected.

## A68 Woodland Road

Telecoms work is causing temporary traffic signals on the A68 Woodland Road in both directions, between Hollyhurst Road and Bracken Road.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 15 at 11.59pm.


## Beechwood Avenue

Telecoms work is affecting Beechwood Avenue in both directions, between the B6280 Coniscliffe Road and Southend Avenue.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until August 28 at 4pm.

## A68 Bondgate

Emergency repairs and gas main work are causing temporary traffic signals on the A68 Bondgate in both directions at Four Riggs.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until August 27 at 11pm.

## A167 near Aycliffe

Gas main work has closed a lane on the A167 in both directions, between the B6444 St Andrews Way and the A1(M) junction 59.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 1 at 4pm.

## Neasham Road

Construction at Burma Road is causing temporary traffic signals on Neasham Road in both directions.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until August 30 at 11pm.

## Auckland Avenue

One lane traffic is in operation on Auckland Avenue in both directions, between Brinkburn Road and Auckland Oval, because of construction.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 22 at 11pm.

## Haughton Road

Electricity work is causing temporary traffic signals on Haughton Road in both directions, between Haughton Green and Mcmullen Road.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until August 27 at 11pm.

## Salters Lane South

Electricity work is causing temporary traffic signals on Salters Lane South in both directions, between Beadnell Close and Haughton Road.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until August 27 at 11pm.

## St Alban’s Green

One lane traffic is in operation on St Alban’s Green in both directions at Winchester Way because of electricity work.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 9 at 11pm.

## Edinburgh Drive

Gas main work is causing temporary traffic signals on Edinburgh Drive in both directions, between the A67 Coniscliffe Road and Teesdale Avenue.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 9 at 4pm. Delays are expected.

## Ely Close

One lane traffic is in operation on Ely Close in both directions at Exeter Drive because of electricity work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until August 27 at 11pm.

## A67 Coniscliffe Road

Gas main work has affected the A67 Coniscliffe Road in both directions, between Coniscliffe Mews and Edinburgh Drive.

The restrictions ended on August 26.

## Faverdale

Construction is causing temporary traffic signals on Faverdale in both directions after A68 West Auckland Road.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 6 at 11pm.

Drivers are advised to allow extra time where delays are expected, particularly on the A167 Grange Road and Edinburgh Drive.

The listed restrictions vary from temporary signals and lane closures to give-and-take traffic, and their scheduled end dates range from August 27 to September 22.
