A family concert celebrating beloved animated classics will entertain audiences of all ages.

The Princess Proms will take place at St George’s Hall on September 13, with live music and family entertainment.

The programme will feature songs from films including Frozen, Moana, Beauty and the Beast, Aladdin, The Little Mermaid, The Lion King and The Wizard of Oz.

The line-up will also include hits by Taylor Swift and Coldplay.

A spokesperson for the show said: "We’re absolutely enchanted to have you join us.

"Creating magical memories, sharing stunning musical moments, and spreading joy is at the very heart of what we do."

The cast includes performers who have appeared in West End shows, on ITV and at royal events.

The spokesperson said: "The stars of our show have performed on the West End, ITV, for Royalty, and more, but their dreams have truly come true touring this concert and performing for you."

Neither The Enchanted Voices nor its events are affiliated with the films or composers listed.

Tickets cost between £22 and £43.
