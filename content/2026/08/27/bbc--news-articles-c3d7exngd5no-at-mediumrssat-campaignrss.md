Grand Theft Auto 6 is one of the most anticipated video games ever

**After more than 18 months since the last official glimpse of Grand Theft Auto 6, developer Rockstar has shown fans a 26-minute preview of the upcoming blockbuster video game.**

Its "extended look" debuted on Netflix, six hours before going live on YouTube, and was the first chance players have had to see detailed footage of the title in action.

The montage, with no commentary or explanatory captions, showed off improved graphics, detailed environments and a selection of car-chases and shoot-outs from the sequel.

The broadcast followed a week of unauthorised gameplay leaks, which the developer said it had been "heartbreaking" to see shared online.

After it got its chance to show the version of GTA 6 it wanted the world to see, here are some things we learned, and some things we still don't know.

## Two's company

![A screenshot from GTA 6 shows main characters Lucia and Jason standing back-to-back against a palm tree-lined sunset. She wears tinted glasses, her long dark hair styled in side-parted cornrows. She wears a tan suede jacket over a string bikini top. He also wears tinted shades and a white shirt with an oversized collar and floral pattern.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/bc9d/live/861576b0-9d89-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

Grand Theft Auto 6 follows the story of couple Lucia and Jason

The extended look spent a long time showcasing the relationship between protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Camino, the couple at the centre of GTA 6's "Bonnie and Clyde" story.

In a preview published on website IGN, external, the developers said the characters' story - and the way it unfolds - can be affected by the player's actions, although they can "opt out" of developing the pair's bond if they want.

The Netflix preview didn't show this playing out in detail, but did show the ability to change control between the two characters at different points.

In one segment, Jason was seen lowering Lucia from a window as they escaped from a drug bust in a block of apartments.

A similar switching system was used in Grand Theft Auto 5, which had three playable main characters.

## 'The best-looking game'

![Two characters from GTA 6 gesture at the viewer. One, wearing a heavy gold watch, pulls back his top lip to reveal teeth covered in gold. The man next to him wears three thick, gold chains, one with a large round medallion attached. Both are heavily tattooed. The image is convincingly lifelike despite being computer generated](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/68fe/live/0161f050-a25d-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

Fans said the detailed characters in the game were "mind-blowing" to see

Praise for GTA 6's graphics was more or less universal, with characters displaying photorealistic faces and lifelike movements.

Fans were also impressed by the detailed locations on show, which included the interiors of apartments, rooftop bars and sleazy underground clubs.

The preview also showcased the various environments of Leonida, the fictional, Florida-inspired US state where GTA 6 is set.

Within Leonida, GTA 6 players can explore Vice City, an area inspired by Miami, which appeared in the hit 2002 game named after the city.

The range of different body shapes and sizes among background characters - something rarely seen in video games - was also highlighted by many players watching online.

Journalist Ryan McCaffrey said GTA 6 "might be the best-looking game I've ever seen".

The impressive visual fidelity has been attributed to the amount of time Rockstar has spent working on the game, and its budget, which is thought to be among the most expensive in video game history.

And according to reports, GTA 6's map is significantly larger than the playable area in Rockstar's previous games.

YouTuber TGG, who says he was invited to Rockstar North's headquarters in Edinburgh to see the game, says the developer told him the map was twice the size of GTA 5's map.

The company hasn't confirmed this officially.

## It's still Grand Theft Auto

![Cal Hampton, a character from GTA 6, stands in front of an alligator-themed crazy golf course. He holds a putter over one shoulder, and pulls a tongue-out face at the viewer. His loud, green, floral shirt stands out against the browns of the golf course. Behind him, a companion can be seen lining up an attempted putt, while a large alligator statue looms over the course.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/8f19/live/7aac83d0-9d8a-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

Rockstar's games are renowed for their attention to detail and vast worlds packed with activities

While GTA 6 appears to break new ground in terms of visuals and scale, it is still, at its heart, a Grand Theft Auto game.

During the preview, viewers saw the armed heists, car chases and criminal underworld scenes that have become a trademark of the series.

Satirical swipes at US culture, darkly comic interactions between characters and a huge range of optional side activities were all present once again.

And Rockstar appears to have expanded on features it introduced in its last major release, 2018 hit Red Dead Redemption 2, such as an "honour" system that affects the player's reputation among other characters.

Fans of the series, which is estimated to have sold half a billion copies since it launched in 1997, will have been thrilled to see it.

## No news on GTA 6 Online

![A male character from GTA 6 in an open-collared shirt with slicked-back hair and sunglasses leans, arms folded, against the controls of a yacht as it coasts down a waterway flanked by skyscrapers. A road bridge opens in the distance as orange dawn light reflects of the glass fronts of the tall buildings. The computer-generated image is convincingly lifelike.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/ce4f/live/a3eacca0-a147-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

The game is set amid the underworld of Leonida, a fictional US state inspired by Florida

Rockstar had already stated that GTA 6 will be a "single-player experience" on launch, leaving fans to assume a follow-up to the incredibly successful online element of its previous games would come later.

The Netflix preview was dedicated to showing off the story and gameplay, and was not necessarily expected to highlight any new multiplayer features.

Although the launch of the game on 19 November is widely expected to be the biggest of all time, the long-term success of GTA 6 will be judged on how well Rockstar converts players to any new online mode.

GTA online, which also launched after the release of GTA 5, remains one of the most-played games in the world, and is thought to have generated billions of dollars for Rockstar.

The previous game in the series, GTA 5, also broke records when it came out in 2013 and has sold more than 200 million copies since.

Two official trailers for the sixth game have almost 500 million combined views on YouTube, and the Netflix "extended look" on Thursday is being marketed as an event by the streaming service.

Next month the company is due to appear at an employment tribunal in Scotland to defend itself against claims it illegally sacked a group of ex-employees for trying to form a union.

It strongly denies the accusations, and says the workers were fired on the grounds of gross misconduct.
