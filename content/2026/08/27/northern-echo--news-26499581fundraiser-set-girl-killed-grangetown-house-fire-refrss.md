A fundraiser has been launched to support the family of a seven-year-old girl who died in a house fire, that also killed her auntie.

Valentina Foster, seven, and her aunt Natalie McDonald, 34, have been named locally as the two people killed in the blaze at a house on Birchington Avenue in Grangetown.

Emergency services were called to the property at 5.23am on Wednesday, August 26.

  Valentina Foster, seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, have been named locally as the two victims who died following a house fire in Grangetown. 

*(Image: Provided)*

Two men, aged 23 and 31, have since been arrested on suspicion of murder and remain in police custody.

Natasha Taylor, has set up a fundraiser for her friend stating it is 'to help her for losing her husband and daughter, a funeral and a holiday for her and her children'.

  It has already raised over £1,000 at the time of writing. You can donate to the fundraiser **HERE.**

The appeal comes as heartbroken tributes continue to be shared for the two victims.

Natalie's mother, Lisa Kirton, described her daughter as "a beautiful, bubbly person who everyone knew".

She said: "She had a heart of gold who would give anyone anything. She left behind a daughter with no parents."

  Natalie McDonald and Valentina Foster have been named locally as the victims of the Grangetown house fire. 

*(Image: Provided)*

Lisa also described Valentina as "one of the family".

"She had been staying at the house for the school summer holidays," she said. "She was a cute little kid. I can't believe it, seven years old - it's just a disgrace."

Valentina's grandmother also paid tribute online, writing: "Life is so cruel, my beautiful granddaughter Valentina. Your dad will be waiting for you."

Lisa said Natalie's 15-year-old daughter and a friend also escaped the house after the friend "kicked out a window" to "save their lives".

A 48-year-old woman remains in a critical condition at the Royal Victoria Infirmary in Newcastle.

Three other people suffered injuries believed to be non-life-threatening.

  Emergency services at the scene of a major residential fire on Birchington Avenue, in Grangetown, Middlesbrough. 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

Cleveland Fire Brigade sent six fire engines and the Incident Command Unit to the scene. The fire was confirmed extinguished at 1.30pm.

The blaze is being jointly investigated by Cleveland Police and the fire service.

Redcar and Cleveland Local Policing Commander Superintendent Emily Harrison said officers were working to establish "the circumstances of exactly what has happened".

She said: "As we continue to investigate this morning's tragic incident, we continue to support the families of those who have sadly died as a result.

"Our thoughts remain with those families and with everyone involved.

"Two men, aged 23 and 31, remain in police custody at this time and they are being questioned.

"We continue to work to establish the circumstances of exactly what has happened, but we are keeping an open mind and cannot rule out any links to wider organised crime at this time.

"We will have a high visibility presence in the Grangetown area and encourage anyone who has concerns, or who may have information, to approach our officers or alternatively call us on 101."

  Emergency services at the scene of a major residential fire on Birchington Avenue, in Grangetown, Middlesbrough 

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

The fire happened around two miles from the A66 crash which killed five young men and two police officers on Saturday, August 22.

Police have said they cannot rule out a link between the incidents.

The Mirror's Jeremy Armstrong, reporting from the scene, said it was understood that a family member of one of the house fire victims knew one of the five occupants of the Passat who died in the A66 crash.

Police said enquiries remain ongoing and anyone with information or concerns is asked to contact Cleveland Police on 101, quoting reference number 170941.
