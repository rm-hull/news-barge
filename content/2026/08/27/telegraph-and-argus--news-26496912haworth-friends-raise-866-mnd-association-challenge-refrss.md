A THREE-LEGGED charity challenge has raised £866.94 for the Motor Neurone Disease (MND) Association.

The event took place in Haworth, West Yorkshire, and involved ten friends—who met through the Voices of Yorkshire choir—who climbed the village’s steep, cobbled Main Street while tied together by the ankle.

The challenge began at the Old Hall Inn and saw five pairs visiting every pub and bar along the route to collect donations and raise awareness.

Siân Sargant, event organiser and owner of Siân Alison Designs in Haworth, said: "Main Street is challenging enough on two legs, so attempting it tied to a good friend certainly tested our coordination.

"We formed such a brilliant bond through singing together, and we wanted to use that connection to do something lighthearted that brought people together for a vital cause."

The team received warm support from the community, with people sharing personal stories of how MND had touched their lives.
