A MAN has been arrested and four people taken to hospital following a crash on a main road in North Yorkshire.

  The incident happened at 8:30am this morning (Thursday) on the B1249 at the top of Staxton Hill between Fordon Lane and North Cotes Road.

  

  A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said it involved four vehicles, including an orange Kia Ceed, a blue Ford Focus, a dark grey Ford Ranger and an orange Ford Ranger.

  

  "Four occupants were taken to hospital for minor injuries following the collision, where they are currently receiving treatment.

"A man has since been arrested following the incident."

    "The road was closed for a number of hours while we investigated the scene and to allow the vehicles involved to be recovered.

    

    "We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who witnessed the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage.

    

    "We are also appealing for any information regarding the orange Ford Ranger and any sightings of this particular vehicle prior to the collision, it is believed to have been heading north from Foxholes towards Scarborough."

    

    If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please email Alyssa Upton on [Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

    

    If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

    

    Please quote reference 12260167471 when providing information.
