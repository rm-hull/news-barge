Police are hunting a wanted man who officers believe is in York.

Christopher Norton, 45, is wanted on recall to prison after breaching the terms of his licence, North Yorkshire Police said.

The force said Norton is believed to be in the York area but also has connections to Selby.

A police spokesperson said “extensive enquiries are ongoing” to find Norton.

They urged anyone who sees Norton to call 999 and those with information about his whereabouts to dial 101 or report it via North Yorkshire Police’s website.

Information can be passed anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260163789 when passing on information to police or to Crimestoppers.
