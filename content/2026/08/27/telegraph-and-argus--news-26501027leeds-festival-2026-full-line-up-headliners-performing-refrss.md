Leeds Festival 2026 is starting tonight (Thursday, August 27) with people arriving at the campsite to get ready.

There will also be a few performances to keep people entertained before the start of the weekend.

Over Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, there will be a huge variety of acts taking to the stage, so you will want to prepare in advance and make a list of who you want to see.

To help you out, here are all of the headliners performing at Leeds Festival 2026.


## Headliners at Leeds Festival 2026

### Friday, August 28

The English drum and bass duo, Chase & Status, will be taking to the stage on Friday and are likely to be playing some of their most popular songs, including Blind Faith, End Credits, and Baddadan.

The pair has won a range of awards including a Brit in 2024 for Producer of the Year and multiple awards from the Drum & Bass Awards over the last few years.

  Florence and the Machine 

*(Image: PA Wire)*

Florence + the Machine are also performing on Friday and will be bringing some indie rock tracks to the stage with some newer songs sprinkled throughout the set.

The Dog Days Are Over singer has spent the past few months performing all across the world for the Everybody Scream tour alongside appearances at many festivals this summer.


### Saturday, August 29

Fontaines D.C., an Irish rock band fronted by Grian Chatten, will be putting on a show on Saturday, ahead of the release of their fifth album Dopamine Chamber.

After the success of their fourth album Romance that featured tracks like Starburster, Favourite, and Bug, the group won a Brit Award for Best International Group in 2025.

  Charlie xcx 

*(Image: PA)*

Charli XCX is the other Saturday headliner and is sure to bring a lot of energy with her tracks Good Ones, Speed Drive, and Von Dutch.

Brat was the only album people were talking about when it was released in the summer of 2024, with the singer going on to release two more albums in 2026, including the soundtrack for the 2026 Wuthering Heights film.


### Sunday, August 30

To close out the weekend, Dave will be performing with fans excited to hear his viral hit Raindance live.

Dave has said he has been influenced by a range of artists including Drake and Kendrick Lamar and has won many awards throughout his career including the Mercury Prize.

  Raye 

*(Image: Yui Mok/PA)*

Fans of Raye will be lining up to see her perform on Sunday, hoping to hear songs like Where Is My Husband!, the Oscar-winning Tears, and Escapism.

Raye currently holds the record for the most Brit Awards won in a single year after receiving six in 2024.
