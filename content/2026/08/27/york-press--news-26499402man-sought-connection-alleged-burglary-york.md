Police investigating an alleged burglary in York have issued a photograph of a man officers want to speak to.

The reported burglary allegedly happened in Acorn Way, Woodthorpe, late in the evening of Friday, July 31.

North Yorkshire Police issued the photograph of the man on Thursday (August 27), saying he may have information to help the investigation.

Anyone who recognises the man is asked to email PC Ben Robinson-Brockhill on [ben.robinson-brockhill@northyorkshirepolice.uk](mailto:ben.robinson-brockhill@northyorkshirepolice.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online using its website.

Quote reference 12260148018 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
