## Step back in time as medieval warriors take over historic York attraction

York's Barley Hall is hosting a medieval-themed weekend with immersive activities and displays this bank holiday weekend *(Image: Supplied)*

A medieval-themed weekend promises to transport visitors back in time with immersive activities and displays at a historic York site.

Taking place at Barley Hall during the August bank holiday from August 29 to 31, the event will shine a spotlight on life in medieval England, with a particular focus on the wider world during the era when the hall was home to goldsmith and former mayor William Snawsell.

Visitors will be able to explore the conflicts of the Middle Ages, including the 30-year-long War of the Roses, with help from historical re-enactors Three Swords.

Richard Jones, event manager for York Archaeology, said: "We are hugely excited to be embracing this turbulent part of English history for our Medieval Weekend at Barley Hall.

"The external courtyard and grandeur of the Great Hall provide superb locations for this kind of display, whatever weather the bank holiday weekend throws at us."

The re-enactors will demonstrate authentic armour, weapons and combat techniques, offering a hands-on experience of medieval warfare.

Barley Hall is a reconstructed medieval townhouse located in Coffee Yard, accessible via Stonegate or Grape Lane.

The event will run daily from 10am to 5pm, with last admissions - which are included with entry to the hall - at 4.30pm.
