TRIBUTES have been paid to a much-loved Bradford mum who died at the young age of 36.

Becky Duke, who lived on Halifax Road, passed away on Saturday from Sepsis after emergency surgery for a perforated bowel and ulcer.

Her death left family and friends devastated.

A fundraiser to help them give her the "send off she deserves" has hit over £3,200.

## 'Everyone knew her'

Family friend Georgia Conway, who set it up, said: "Everyone is really shocked.

"They don’t know what caused it (the perforated bowel)."

"Becky was very quirky and everyone knew her.

"You always knew she was there.

"She was the loudest person in the room.

Georgia added: "She was also obsessed with Michael Jackson.

"Becky was his number one fan.

"She also loved horror films."

## Over £3,200 raised for funeral

Becky, a former Tong High School student, was engaged to Damien Godward and had a 16-year-old daughter, Ella.

Ms Conway said: "We are raising money to help the family, her partner Damian and her daughter Ella to give Becky the send-off she deserves.

"All donations will be very appreciated."

Donations can be made at gofundme.com/f/giving-becky-duke-the-send-off-she-deserves.

## What happened?

The gofundme page reads: "Becky was taken into hospital on Friday just after dinner time.

"Upon arrival at the hospital they realised she was very unwell, Becky was suffering from a perforated bowel and ulcer.

"Very quickly it was decided she was going for emergency surgery as she had a severe infection.

"Becky was in theatre for quite some time into the night.

"Early hours in the morning the family was told we needed to make our way to the hospital as quick as we could.

"On arrival at the hospital we were sat down and told Becky had the surgery and it was quite difficult and she had sepsis.

"They told us the next 48 hours was critical.

"Becky was brought to ICU and was not in a good way.

"We as a family sat for a short time and reminisced about our pasts and made it known to Becky we were all there by her side.

"We were then told the machines at this point were keeping her alive as her body was too weak to fight."

It adds that she passed peacefully at 7:14am on Saturday.
