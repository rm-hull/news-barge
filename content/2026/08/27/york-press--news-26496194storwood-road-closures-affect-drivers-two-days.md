Roads in Storwood will close for works expected to take two days from September 1.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has made an order closing Postern, Gatehead and Narrow Lane in Storwood to through traffic.

The closure will allow proposed works to be carried out while helping ensure public safety.

Vehicles will not be able to use the roads as a through route during the closure.

The alternative route for affected traffic is via Postern Lane, Langrickgate Lane, Ballhall Lane and Hagg Lane.

The closure will apply to through traffic only, but will remain open to pedestrians and to vehicles accessing properties directly affected by the closure.

Emergency services will also retain access.

The order starts on September 1, 2026 and will remain in force for up to 18 months, or until the works are complete, whichever comes first.

This means the closure could continue beyond the anticipated two-day period if the work is not finished.

Drivers should follow the alternative route where necessary and plan their journeys accordingly.

Further information is available from Streetscene Services, Highways Maintenance, on (01482) 393939.

The council's reference for the notice is RJH.
