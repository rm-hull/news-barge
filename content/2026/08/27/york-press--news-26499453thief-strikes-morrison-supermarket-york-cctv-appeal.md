*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A thief has struck at a York supermarket.

North Yorkshire Police has issued a photograph of a man officers want to speak to about the theft at Morrisons in Foss Islands Road on Saturday, July 25.

The force on Thursday (August 27) issued the photograph, saying the man pictured may have information to help its investigation.

Anyone who recognises the man is asked to email PC Ben Robinson-Brockhill on [ben.robinson-brockhill@northyorkshirepolice.uk](mailto:ben.robinson-brockhill@northyorkshirepolice.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.

Quote reference 12260143935 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
