Fans of Pitbull will be able to party to the rapper’s biggest hits at a themed daytime event coming to the North East this Bank Holiday weekend.

The Pitbull Universe Party is taking place at Motel Mexicana on Mosley Steet, Newcastle on Sunday, August 30, with organisers promising an afternoon dedicated to the star’s music and collaborations.

Running from 2pm until 7pm, the over-18s event will feature DJs playing Pitbull tracks alongside songs by artists including Sean Paul, Flo Rida, Shakira, Enrique Iglesias, Jason Derulo, Bad Bunny, and J Balvin.

The event has been described as a "full afternoon dedicated to the Pitbull universe featuring the hits, collabs and the ones you forgot about until the chorus comes on".

Organisers are also encouraging partygoers to embrace the theme by wearing bald caps inspired by the singer’s trademark look.

Food and drinks will be available throughout the event, while VIP packages can be booked for groups.
