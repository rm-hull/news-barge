# Watch: Police chase suspects after drone found near Doncaster prison

## Three men have been arrested after a police pursuit near a Doncaster prison led to the discovery of a drone carrying suspected contraband.

An officer carrying out a proactive patrol near HMP Lindholme in Doncaster spotted a black Volvo S60 parked on a dirt track at around 10.15pm on Tuesday (Aug 25).

Police have been working alongside the prison service to increase patrols outside prisons in a bid to identify offences and prevent contraband from entering the facilities.

The Volvo moved off onto the A614 and failed to stop for the officer, prompting a police pursuit.

During the pursuit, items were seen being thrown from the window of the Volvo and the vehicle was also reported to have been speeding.

Three police vehicles became involved in the pursuit before the Volvo was brought to a safe stop on the Great Yorkshire Way.

Three men inside the vehicle, aged 36, 34 and 34, were arrested.

![During the pursuit, items were seen being thrown from the window of the Volvo and the vehicle was also reported to have been speeding](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/27/17/25/Doncaster_drone_arrest_still_1.jpeg?trim=661,0,539,0&width=640&quality=65)



A 36-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of failure to stop, going equipped, theft from a shop and attempting to convey a prohibited article into a prison.

One of the 34-year-old men was arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving, failure to stop, criminal damage, driving with excess drugs and attempting to convey a prohibited article into a prison.

The second 34-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of aggravated burglary, burglary, failure to stop, dangerous driving and attempting to convey a prohibited article into a prison.

Officers then searched the area where the Volvo had initially been spotted and recovered a drone with a camouflaged parcel attached.

Police said a closer examination of the parcel found suspected Class A and Class B drugs, a knife, e-cigarettes and tobacco.

The parcel was recovered as enquiries continued.

All three men have since been bailed pending further enquiries.
