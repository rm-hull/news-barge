North East residents could be in for a pleasant Bank Holiday weekend as highs of 20C are expected.

While there remains a chance of an odd shower, the latest forecast suggests sunshine will become increasingly prominent, culminating in the warmest weather arriving on Sunday.

This means plans for a day outdoors, a garden party, or other outdoor adventures can go ahead.

According to the Met Office, Friday appears set to get the weekend underway on a mild note, with temperatures expected to range between 15C and 18C.

There is a 50 per cent chance of rain and light winds averaging around 8mph, meaning those heading out may want to keep a waterproof close to hand.

Conditions begins to improve on Saturday, with temperatures forecast to reach up to 19C, while the chance of rain falls to 40 per cent.

On Bank Holiday Sunday, forecasters expect temperatures to reach as high as 20C, making it the warmest day of the weekend.

The chance of rain drops again to 30 per cent, while light winds of around 7mph are expected to help create pleasant late-summer conditions.

On Monday, the day is expected to start out glum. There is a 60 per cent chance of rain, with a mix of cloud and sun expected throughout the day,

For many people, it could provide one last opportunity to make the most of the outdoors before September arrives and attention turns back to schools, work, and the arrival of autumn.

The forecast represents a welcome improvement after recent unsettled conditions and suggests any showers that do develop are unlikely to dominate the weekend.

  **Bank Holiday weather breakdown:Friday:** Between 15C and 18C, 50% chance of rain

  **Saturday:** Between 15C and 19C, 40% chance of rain

  **Sunday**: Between 14C and 20C, 30% chance of rain

  **Monday**: Between 13C and 19C, 60% chance of rain.
