Passengers are reminded that testing ahead of digital signalling being introduced on the East Coast Main Line will affect journeys this weekend.

Engineering work will affect journeys south of Peterborough towards London between 22.00 on Saturday 29 August and 08.00 on Monday 31 August, with rail replacement bus services in operation for some journeys.

The work forms the final stages of testing ahead of the £1.4 billion East Coast Digital Programme’s introduction of digital in-cab signalling – replacing traditional lineside “traffic light” signals with real‑time information sent directly into the driver’s cab.

Over the long term, Network Rail says it will deliver more reliable, greener and safer journeys.

Digital signalling has been successfully introduced on the Northern City Line in London and will be introduced on the East Coast Main Line between Welwyn and Hitchin this summer.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/e10151226e984a7d9b004d852380f6e0-e1785944354526.jpeg)

Passengers are urged to check their train operator’s website and social media for the latest travel advice. For North and East Yorkshire that’s LNER, Grand Central and Hull Trains.

Lift refurbishment at London St Pancras means there is currently no step-free access to or from the Thameslink platforms at London St Pancras. Click here for further information and alternative routes for those requiring step-free access.

Full travel details:

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/grand-central-class-180-diesel-hydraulic-multiple-unit-rail-accident-investigation-branch.webp)

**Grand Central:** (Thirsk, York, Northallerton) 

Saturday – The 17.32 Sunderland to London King’s Cross will not run.

Sunday – **No Grand Central service will operate.**

Monday – The 07.48 London King’s Cross to Sunderland will not run. The following trains will operate additionally:

- 16.56 Bradford Interchange to London King’s Cross
- 21.15 London King’s Cross to York

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/1280x800-hull-trains-paragon-exterior-x2-1000x625.jpg)

**Hull Trains:** (Selby and Howden) 

Unaffected by engineering work on Saturday.

Sunday – A reduced service will run to/from London St Pancras. As a result, trains will not call at Beverley/Cottingham/Doncaster/Retford/Grantham or Stevenage.

Monday – The 07.18 service from London King’s Cross to Hull will depart later at 07.25 and will arrive in Hull at 10.22.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/LNER_Azuma_at_Bedford_3-1000x623.webp)

**LNER:** (York, Northallerton, Harrogate) 

A revised timetable will operate, with trains starting/terminating at Peterborough. A bus service will run between Peterborough and Bedford, for connections into/out of London St Pancras. This will also impact late Saturday evening, and early Monday morning.

On **Sunday** only, a limited LNER service will also operate between Newcastle, Northallerton, York and Bedford, via Doncaster, also connecting with Thameslink services to/from London St Pancras. 

The 08.26 and 12.27 departures from Newcastle, calling at Durham, Darlington, Northallerton, York and Doncaster will head direct to Bedford, returning from Bedford to Newcastle at 12.49 and 16.57.

Customers travelling to/from stations on the LNER route in Scotland, the North East, North and West Yorkshire to/from central London on this date are advised to avoid travel via Sheffield and Chesterfield, as CrossCountry and East Midlands Railway services on this route will be extremely busy.
