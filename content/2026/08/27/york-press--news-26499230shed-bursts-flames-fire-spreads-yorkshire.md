A shed has burst into flames after a fire spread from a hedge and fence in North Yorkshire.

Firefighters were called to the scene in Stockwell Lane, Knaresborough, at 9.05pm on Wednesday (August 26).

The fire was initially contained to a hedge and fence but spread to a garden shed and more fences and hedges along a ginnel, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

A service spokesperson said two fire crews were called to the scene and extinguished the blaze.

They said the cause of the fire is “undetermined at this time”.
