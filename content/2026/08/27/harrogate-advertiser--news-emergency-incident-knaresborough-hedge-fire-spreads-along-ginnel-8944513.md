# Knaresborough hedge fire spreads along ginnel

## Crews from Knaresborough Fire Station were called to attend a fire on Stockwell Lane at 9.05pm on Wednesday evening (August 26) .

Firefighters responded to reports of a hedge and fence on fire that spread to a garden shed and further fences and hedges along a ginnel.

![Fire crews were called to assist shortly after 9pm on Wednesday evening.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmQyMDZiNTQ3LTQ4ODktNDhlNS04ZGE0LWQyMWRhNDEyZTA4YjpjZDUxY2ZhOS1mMGZhLTQ1MzMtYmM1MS03ODgxYzRkNjU1MzA=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Crews extinguished the fire using hose reel jets.

The cause of the fire is undetermined at this time.
