An e-bike has been seized and will be crushed following a collision in the Bradford as police promise to crack down on what they describe as an epidemic of 'balaclava wearing nuisance'.

The incident occurred when two e-bikes approached officers from the T1 Shipley Neighbourhood Policing Team (NPT) while on patrol.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police spoke of their commitment "to stopping this epidemic of balaclava wearing nuisance in our community."

They added: "These are not road legal along with the likes of 'Revvi' bikes which we are seeing an increase in and amongst our communities being purchased."

  The seized bike 

*(Image: WYP)*

Officers from T1 Shipley NPT were on patrol in West Royd when two e-bikes came down towards them.

The spokesperson said: "Panicking they tried to turn but crashed into each other, both falling to the floor with one rider unable to recover their bike in time."

They urge residents to report any information anonymously through Crimestoppers online or via 0800 555111
