A reportedly stolen Lamborghini was seized after being spotted by patrolling officers.

The vehicle, a green Lamborghini Huracán, was recovered at Ravensthorpe Retail Park on Tuesday.

It was found by PCs Shilling and Coe from the Dewsbury and Mirfield Neighbourhood Policing Team, who were responding to reports of nuisance vehicles in the area.

West Yorkshire Police said: "While carrying out enquiries at Ravensthorpe Retail Park, officers located a Lamborghini Huracán which was identified as being reported stolen.

  The offending Lamborghini Huracán was recovered by officers 

*(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

"The vehicle was subsequently seized and recovered by police."

West Yorkshire Police said they will continue to act on information provided by the public and carry out patrols to address vehicle crime and anti-social behaviour.

Reports can be made by calling 101 or via westyorkshire.police.uk.
