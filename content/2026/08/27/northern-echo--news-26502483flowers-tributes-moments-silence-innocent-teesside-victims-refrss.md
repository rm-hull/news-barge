Tributes have been paid to an innocent child and her aunt who died in a house fire, as Teesside also fell silent to remember two police officers killed in the line of duty.

Flowers, toys and heartbreaking messages have been left outside the Grangetown home where Valentina Foster, seven, and her aunt Natalie McDonald, 34, died on Wednesday (August 26) morning.

  Valentina Foster, seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, have been named locally as the two victims who died following a house fire in Grangetown. 

*(Image: Provided)*

Police have not ruled out the blaze, which police are treating as murder, being linked to wider organised crime, including the horror smash on the A66 which killed seven.

Among the floral tributes is a photograph of the pair alongside bouquets and toys.

One message reads: "RIP Natalie and Valentina you will be missed."

  Flowers left at the scene of a house fire on Birchington Avenue where two people were killed 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

  Flowers at the scene on Birchington Avenue, Grangetown, near Middlesbrough, where Valentina Foster, aged seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, died following a fire at an end-of-terrace property on Wednesday morning. 

*(Image: OWEN HUMPHRIES)*

Natalie's mother, Lisa Kirton, told the Times her daughter was "a beautiful, bubbly person who everyone knew".

She said: "She had a heart of gold who would give anyone anything. She left behind a daughter with no parents.

Lisa described Valentina as "one of the family", saying she had been staying at the house for the school summer holidays.

  Flowers left at the scene of a house fire on Birchington Avenue where two people were killed 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

She said: "She was a cute little kid. I can't believe it, seven years old - it's just a disgrace."

A Care Bear and teddy bear have also been left at the scene, alongside a note which says: "Now she dances with butterflies Valentina."

Other messages read: "Valentina what a lovely young girl, so sad, RIP."Another says: "RIP Valentina, gone too soon, always missed."

Tributes to Natalie include: "RIP Natalie, life and soul of the party!"

  Flowers at the scene on Birchington Avenue, Grangetown, near Middlesbrough, where Valentina Foster, aged seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, died following a fire at an end-of-terrace property on Wednesday morning. 

*(Image: OWEN HUMPHRIES)*

Another reads: "Nat, will always be in my thoughts you were the party girl going to be sadly missed RIP."

Valentina's grandmother also paid tribute online, writing: "Life is so cruel, my beautiful granddaughter Valentina. Your dad will be waiting for you."

  Natalie McDonald and Valentina Foster have been named locally as the victims of the Grangetown house fire. 

*(Image: Provided)*

As tributes were laid for Natalie and Valentina, Teesside also paused to remember PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough, who died in the A66 crash on Saturday (August 22).

A minute's silence was held at Cleveland Police headquarters and at the scene of the fire on Birchington Avenue.

  At 11am police officers held a minutes silence for the two officers killed on the A66 while guarding the scene of a house fire on Birchington Avenue where two people were killed. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

One tribute at the police station reads: "Tommy, Bladesy, You will be remembered as two of the very best. I am proud of you both and you will be forever missed. I don't have the words to describe the loss we all feel. Genuine, real-life heroes. - M."

Another says: "PC Matthew Blades, PC Tom Clough, you will be missed, never forgotten. Rest in peace."

  PC Matthew Blades (left) and PC Tom Clough (right) 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE/CARMEL COLLEGE)*

A further message reads: "There are no words to convey the sadness we all feel at the tragic loss of Tom and Matt. We are all thinking about you and their families and the weight of grief you are carrying. POLIT"

Flowers and messages also continue to be left by the A66.

  Floral tributes by the A66 for PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Huge sums raised for the families affected by the tragedies.

The Cleveland Police Federation fundraiser says the officers "paid the ultimate sacrifice in the line of duty" and left behind "heartbroken loved ones".
