### Travel disruptions

Most of the roads across the region look clear this morning.

The A66 between Stockton and Elton on approach to and from Darlington is currently showing as heavier traffic than usual. This is due to road works and a reduced average speech check area of 40mph.

### Train disruptions

There are currently no rail disruptions at this time.

### What will the weather be like in the North East today?

Met Office weather today is expecting that the unsettled weather will be continuing.

#### 
  **Today:**

Overnight heavy rain will clear eastern areas early, with isolated showers elsewhere. Showers developing more widely through the day, often heavy with a risk of thunder in places. Temperatures around normal. Maximum temperature 21 °C.

#### 
  **Tonight:**

Showers easing off with some late evening sunshine possible. Clear spells overnight, along with light winds, could see some cooler temperatures in places. Minimum temperature 11 °C.
