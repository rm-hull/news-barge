TRIBUTES have poured in after the "heart-breaking" loss of a Bradford mum, who died aged 36.

Becky Duke, who lived on Halifax Road in Bradford, died from sepsis on Saturday after emergency surgery for a perforated bowel and ulcer.

  **Kerchella James** said: "My best friend in this whole world.

"I’m going to be so lost without you, my girl."

  **Tanya Godward** said: "My beautiful daughter-in-law. I will miss you."

## Readers pay tribute

People across the community shared memories of Becky and messages of support for her family.

  **Tara Flaherty** said: "Not a bad bone in her body.

"Fly high, Becky Duke."

  **Saghir Ahmed** said: "RIP. Condolences to family and friends.

"My heart goes out to everyone affected by this tragic event."

  **Karree Bell** said: “My mum passed away literally a month ago for the same thing. It’s heart-breaking.”

  **Eileen Fearon** said: “So tragic. Deepest condolences to her family and friends. Rest in peace young woman.”

  **Emma Dunne** said: “Sending prayers of condolences to all her family and friends at this sad time.”

  **June Kay** said: “My deepest condolences to the family and friends. RIP fly high with the angels.”

## Fundraiser now on over £3,400

Ms Duke, a former Tong High School student, was engaged to Damien Godward and had a 16-year-old daughter, Ella.

Family friend Georgia Conway, who launched a fundraiser to help cover funeral costs, said: "Everyone is really shocked.

"Becky was very quirky and everyone knew her.

"You always knew she was there.

"She was the loudest person in the room."

The fundraiser for Ms Duke’s funeral has raised more than £3,400.

Ms Conway said: "We are raising money to help the family, her partner Damian and her daughter Ella to give Becky the send-off she deserves."

Donations to support the family can be made at gofundme.com/f/giving-becky-duke-the-send-off-she-deserves.

Friends and others who knew Becky have also shared their grief online.

  **Jodie Tait** said: "Rip my beautiful, until we meet again."

  **Kirsty M Jardin** said: "Rest in eternal peace, Becky.

"My heart is with your loved ones."

  **Damian Prendergast** said: "Stuck with me since first reading it, poor lass, had her whole life ahead of her.

"Thoughts with her young one, family and friends."

Family friend Georgia Conway said the family did not know what had caused the perforated bowel.
