# Popular British fashion band picks Harrogate as location for its first store in North of England

## The owner of a major British fashion brand loved by celebrities such as Alison Hammond and Davina McCall says opening in Harrogate is a dream come true.

Until now, fashion store Scamp & Dude has only been available online at scampanddude.com and in bricks and mortar at a select few locations in the south including Marlow, Battersea Power Station and Brighton.

Now the community-focused, mood-boosting, vibrant fashion brand is to open its first shop in the North of England– in Harrogate.

Jo Tutchener Sharp OBE, founder and CEO of Scamp & Dued, said: “We’ve wanted a store in Harrogate for such a long time.

"It’s taken us over three years to find the perfect spot, so it’s long overdue, and celebrating our 10th anniversary this year makes the moment even more special.

"Harrogate is a real gem, and it feels only right that this becomes our permanent home in the North.

"The store will bring the very best of our product and experience.

"We cannot wait to welcome our northern community through the doors."

Scamp & Dude, which specialises in womenswear and kids' clothing designed for comfort and confidence in sizes 6 to 22, is scheduled to open a 2,100 sq ft shop at the corner of 2-3 James Street this October.

Formerly occupied by Castle Fine Art, the heart of the new store will be Scamp & Dude’s signature Bolt Room, a large private fitting-room experience.

It’s a place where the Scamp & Dude community can book the space in advance to try on the latest collections together in a relaxed and fun environment, with complimentary refreshments.

Scamp & Dude stores are known for hosting a year-round programme of local events, workshops, and brand activation.

A certified B Corp brand, Scamp & Dude prides itself in being a brand powered by purpose.

Its #SuperScarfMission has seen more than 93,000 Super Scarves donated to women starting chemotherapy, with one donated for every scarf sold.

It has also donated more than 10,000 Superhero Sleep Buddies to children who have lost a parent or are seriously ill themselves.
