# Huddersfield colleges' push for 3G football pitch as final plans submitted

## Plans for a 3G football pitch at a Huddersfield College are forging ahead, despite initial fears of “light pollution”.

Shelley College on Huddersfield Road was granted planning permission to build a realistic, all-seasons pitch with 4.5 metres of fencing and floodlights in 2022.

At the time, neighbours said they were “broadly supportive” although concerns were raised about the level of noise and light pollution the pitch may cause.

During the 2022 consultation period, one neighbour wrote: “I am broadly supportive of the proposal, but because my house is closest to the development, I would like three things to be considered as part of the application, including light pollution.

![Shelley College](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjFlN2I5YWZjLWJiNWEtNDQzYS04NDA3LWYxMmRhNzI2ZTliNDplOTg2MWQyYy1jNGQwLTQ5MDQtYjIyYy1iYmVmMWZjZjNlNmY=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



“The floodlights may affect my house because our bedroom windows face the college.”

The developers, Sports Labs Ltd, were given three years to respond with how they would abide by conditions given by the council, which were submitted earlier this week (August 20).

The evidence submitted by the developer indicates their intention to use specialist floodlight equipment, including a “master” control. Other areas addressed also included noise, where a noise impact assessment found that the pitch’s potential noise levels “were in accordance with the requirements of the Kirklees Council”.

Kirklees Council still needs to review the proposal, but if it is approved, Shelley College will be one step closer to a brand-new pitch for its students.
