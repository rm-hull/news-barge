Clothes have been stolen from a shop in North Yorkshire.

The incident happened at Boyes in Queen Street, Scarborough, on Wednesday, August 5.

North Yorkshire Police has issued images of three people, including a topless man, who officers want to speak to about the theft.

“Please contact us if you recognise the people pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation,” a police spokesperson said.

Anyone with information is asked to email [ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260150889 when passing on information to police or to Crimestoppers.
