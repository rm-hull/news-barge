Police believe a man wanted on recall to prison is in York.

Anthony Jackson, 39, from York, has been recalled to prison after breaching the terms of his licence, North Yorkshire Police said.

The force said Jackson “is believed to still be in the city of York”.

“Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Jackson and we are now appealing for your help to find him,” a force spokesperson said in an appeal to the public.

They urged anyone who sees Jackson to phone 999 and those with information about his whereabouts to phone 101 or report it via North Yorkshire Police’s website.

Information can be passed anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260163754 when passing on information to police or to Crimestoppers.
