Do you know this man?

Police investigating a burglary in York are trying to trace him.

It happened on **Acorn Way** in the late evening of**Friday, 31 July.** 

#### Acorn Way, York

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cctv-burglary-acorn-way-york-31-july-2026-full-693x1500.webp)

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Police are looking to identify the male in the attached image as part of the investigation.

“If anyone knows who this male is, please contact us.”
