## Main road in North Yorkshire to be shut 'for some time' after crash

 *(Image: Dave Cox)*

A MAIN road in North Yorkshire is shut following a crash.

Police have said the B1249 at Staxton Hill is expected to be for some time.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "We are currently dealing with a road traffic collision on the B1249 at Staxton Hill."
