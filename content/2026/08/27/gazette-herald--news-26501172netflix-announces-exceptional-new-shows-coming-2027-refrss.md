Netflix has unveiled a fresh slate of shows, including new comedies from the creators of Friday Night Dinner and Derry Girls.

The announcement is part of what the streaming giant describes as its “most varied range of commissions to date,” with new drama, entertainment, and unscripted formats also in the pipeline.

One of the headline comedies is Jig, a six-part mockumentary set in the world of competitive Irish dancing.

The series comes from Lisa McGee, the writer and creator behind Channel 4’s hit show Derry Girls, and will be shot in Northern Ireland next year.


## New comedy shows confirmed for Netflix

Another new comedy has been commissioned from Robert Popper, the creator of Friday Night Dinner.

The untitled family sitcom will feature an all-star cast including Robert Webb, Timothy Spall, Alexandra Roach, Denise Welch, and Loo Brealey.

Anne Mensah, vice president of UK content at Netflix, said: "In the amazingly competitive UK landscape, it is not good enough to be just ok – you need to swing for exceptional.

"Across drama, comedy, entertainment, live, and factual programming, our slate underscores the risks we're happy to take and the extraordinary UK creativity we're proud to champion."

Beyond comedy, the new commissions include The Unheard, a drama starring Kate Winslet and Maxine Peake about two sisters forced to go on the run after an act of vengeance.

The series comes from writer Jack Thorne and director Mounia Akl.

In entertainment, Beat Luke Littler will see 20 amateur darts players compete against the world’s top-ranked player, with a top prize of £501,000.


The live event will be hosted by Gary Lineker with analysis from Richard Osman.

Other additions to the upcoming slate include How To Kill Your Family, a darkly comedic drama based on Bella Mackie’s bestselling novel, and Outside, a new spin-off from the Sidemen’s hit series Inside.

Ms Mensah said: "Bring on 2027, we can't wait."
