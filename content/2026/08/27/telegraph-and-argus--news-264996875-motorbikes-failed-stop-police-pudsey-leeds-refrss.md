POLICE are appealing for information after five motorbikes failed to stop for officers during a patrol in Pudsey.

The incident happened on Tuesday during Operation Bradebank patrols by West Yorkshire Police’s Leeds West team.

The bikes were later seen being driven recklessly in Bramley and pulled out of a junction into the path of a police vehicle.

A Sur-ron type vehicle was left at the scene and recovered by the force.

Officers are asking the public to report information via the West Yorkshire Police website or Crimestoppers.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "The nominals using these vehicles are not wearing the proper protective equipment and we need your help to tell us as much about those using them and where they are being stored via WYP online reporting or anonymously via Crimestoppers."
