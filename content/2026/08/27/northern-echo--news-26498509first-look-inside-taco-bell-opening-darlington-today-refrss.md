Here is an exclusive first look inside a brand new fast-food spot launching in Darlington today.

Iconic American brand Taco Bell has come to Darlington, with a brand new restaurant opening at the former KFC site on North Road.

The fast-food giant is welcoming customers from Thursday, August 27, as it brings its popular menu to the town.

  The new Taco Bell on Darlington North Retail Park 

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

The new restaurant will serve up Taco Bell favourites including the Crunchwrap Supreme, Crunchy Taco Supreme and Volcano Burrito.

And to mark the opening, customers are being challenged to crack the 'Bell Code'.

QR codes have been hidden across Darlington, with treasure hunters able to scan them to unlock rewards.

  The new Taco Bell on Darlington North Retail Park 

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

There is even a chance to win free tacos for a year.

Lucy Dee, Marketing Director of Taco Bell UK and Ireland, said: "At Taco Bell, we live for pushing boundaries with bold flavours, category-defining innovation, and brand experiences people don't forget.

"We can't wait to open our doors and connect with the people of Darlington."

  The new Taco Bell on Darlington North Retail Park 

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

The Darlington restaurant will be operated by QFM Group, which manages 28 Taco Bell restaurants across the UK.

Kalham Overson, Brand Operation Leader for Taco Bell at QFM Group, said: "We are delighted to be opening our new Taco Bell restaurant on Albert Road in Darlington.

"This exciting new location allows us to bring the Taco Bell experience to even more guests across Darlington and the surrounding communities, while giving our loyal fans a fantastic new place to enjoy our menu.

"Becoming part of the local community matters enormously to us, and we look forward to building strong local partnerships and creating valuable employment opportunities for people in the area."

  The new Taco Bell on Darlington North Retail Park 

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

The opening comes as Taco Bell continues its expansion across the UK, with more than 150 locations now operating nationwide.

Fans can also sign up to Taco Bell Rewards through the Taco Bell UK app, with new members receiving a free Crunchy Taco.

The rewards scheme also offers points that can be exchanged for free menu items, early access to exclusive offers and new releases, plus the chance to order ahead and skip the queue.
