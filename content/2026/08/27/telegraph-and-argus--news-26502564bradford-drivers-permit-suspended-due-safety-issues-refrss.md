A private hire driver has had their permit suspended after police found safety issues with the vehicle.

Officers in Bradford stopped the vehicle and found a faulty bonnet and a cracked windscreen.

The driver was reported for the offences.

Their permit has also been suspended until further notice.

West Yorkshire Police said: "Whilst patrolling we noticed the bonnet on this Toyota private hire vehicle was unusually positioned.

"On stopping it we established that the bonnet did not actually latch down properly at all.

"We then also noticed the significant crack in the windscreen.

"The driver was reported for the offences and also reported to the relevant local authority licencing team, with private hire permit suspended until the issues are rectified."
