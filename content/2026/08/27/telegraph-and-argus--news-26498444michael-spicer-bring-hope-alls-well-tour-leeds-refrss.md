COMEDIAN and viral sensation Michael Spicer is hitting the road with a brand-new UK tour.

He will stop in Leeds this autumn as part of a run of shows that also includes the Edinburgh Festival Fringe and a closing date at London’s Leicester Square Theatre.

Best known for his viral hit The Room Next Door, Mr Spicer will perform at Leeds City Varieties on Wednesday, October 21.

Mr Spicer said: "Last time I was on tour the shows were packed out.

"Mainly because they were post-lockdown and people just wanted to go out.

"To be honest, they’d have been happy if I was just spinning plates and making dogs jump through hoops.

"However, I’m thrilled to be on the road again to try and make sense of the chaos of the modern world, or at the very least distract you from it for ninety minutes."

Tickets are available now from www.mickperrin.com/tours/michael-spicer-hope-alls-well.
