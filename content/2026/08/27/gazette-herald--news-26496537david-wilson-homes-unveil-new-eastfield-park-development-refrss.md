A housebuilder is inviting buyers to a special preview event for its latest development.

David Wilson Homes is set to unveil Eastfield Park, a new scheme in Dunnington, and will host a preview evening at its nearby Bramble Wood development in Wheldrake.

The event will take place on Thursday, August 27, from 3pm to 7pm.

Eastfield Park will feature 44 homes, with a mix of one, two, three, and four-bedroom properties, and is scheduled to be complete from next summer.

Paul Hogan, sales director at David Wilson Homes Yorkshire East, said: "We’re encouraging anyone looking to buy a home in North Yorkshire to attend our preview event on the 27th August.

"With just 44 homes being built at Eastfield Park, we’re expecting a high level of interest.

"To avoid missing out, we’d urge prospective buyers to attend the preview event and speak to our dedicated sales team about the upcoming homes and what you will need to do to purchase your new David Wilson home."

The development is described as offering a blend of semi-rural living with easy access to York city centre, making it suitable for commuters and families alike.

The event will give buyers the chance to learn more about the homes available.
