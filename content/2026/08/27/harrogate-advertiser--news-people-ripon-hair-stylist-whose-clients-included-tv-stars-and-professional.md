# Ripon hair stylist whose clients included TV stars and professional models wins top UK award

## A Ripon stylist has said it feels like it’s “just the start” after being named the UK's best hair extensions specialist.

The owner of FAKE, which operates by appointment from Crakehall, near Bedale, Kylie Hammond, 39, triumphed over thousands of entries to win the title at the UK Hair and Beauty Awards.

The salon specialises in premium hair extensions, with hair sourced from Eastern Europe and each set made by hand in the salon to the client's own specification.

"Winning this after only a year of trading feels like an incredible achievement,” said Kylie.

![Kylie Hammond of FAKE salon near Bedale celebrates winning Hair Extensions Specialist of the Year at the UK Hair and Beauty Awards at the AO Arena in Manchester. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmUzOTdhMjZlLTI4YWYtNDNkOC1hOGY1LWJmNjczNzI1MWY4NToyNzZkYmU3Ni0zNGRiLTRmZDMtOWMyNS1iMTEwMTZjMzE4YWM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



"But I've been working towards it a lot longer than that.

"It's an incredible reward for the work we've put in over the last 12 months of trading."

As both a businesswoman and a stylist, Kylie sees it all as just another step to where she wants to be in her career.

After gaining vital experience over 13 memorable years in London where her clients included film and TV stars and professional models, Kylie returned to North Yorkshire two years ago and opened her own studio at Crakehall last year.

![The talented North Yorkshire based hair stylist Kylie Hammond triumphed over thousands of entries to win at the UK Hair and Beauty Awards. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjdhNjIwYWNhLTUwMWItNDU4Yi04ODRjLWY0ZDllNjI5NThhYzoyYWZiMWFiMC1lNWU3LTQ5N2ItYTQ4Yi1mNWNkMjRiZWI3NmY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The move freed her to develop her own style of tailor-made hair extensions there.

Each set is created by hand in the salon, with a colour-matching and fitting technique designed to make them virtually undetectable.

"I spent years in some of the best hair extensions salons in London,” she said.

"After developing my own style of hair extensions, I decided to come back to North Yorkshire with the goal of building the number one hair extensions salon in the UK.

"But doing that is just the start for us, not the end.”

The UK Hair and Beauty Awards is not a public vote.

All the entries are judged anonymously via a panel of independent industry judges.

The 2026 awards were hosted at the AO Arena in Manchester by TV personality Jake Quickenden.

The new business is currently being fitted out and is due to open in November.
