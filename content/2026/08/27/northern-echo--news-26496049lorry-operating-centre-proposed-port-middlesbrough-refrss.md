A haulage company has applied for permission to operate a small fleet of lorries from a port site, with nearby landowners being invited to have their say on the proposal.

Davies & Sons Transport Limited, based in Billingham, has requested a Goods Vehicle Operator's Licence to use A V Dawson Transport Limited at the Port of Middlesbrough as an operating centre for two goods vehicles and two trailers.

The site is located on Depot Road in the TS2 1LE area.

The announcement was made on August 26, and the public has been given 21 days from this date to respond.

The Traffic Commissioner must receive written representations from local landowners or occupiers who think the plan will affect their use or enjoyment of their land.

These representations must explain the reasons for any objections.

The address to send these to is Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE.

Anyone submitting representations must also send a copy to Davies & Sons Transport Limited at 6 Plantations, Wynyard, Billingham, Stockton, TS22 5SN.

A guide on how to make representations is available on the government's website.
