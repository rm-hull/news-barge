Police have released a picture of a man who has gone missing from Middlesbrough.

Jake Tytler, 34, from Stainton, was last seen at around 7.50pm on Tuesday, August 25, at Tees Barrage.

Jake has since been reported missing, with concerns raised for his welfare.

He was last seen wearing a blue top and shorts.

Police officers are appealing for anyone who may have seen Jake, or who has information about where he may be, to come forward as soon as possible.

Anyone with information is asked to contact police on 101, quoting incident number SE26170747.
