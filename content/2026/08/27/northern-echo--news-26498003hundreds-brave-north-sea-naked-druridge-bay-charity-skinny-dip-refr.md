Hundreds of people are expected to bare all for a sunrise skinny dip.

Organisers have announced that the North East Skinny Dip will take place at Druridge Bay on September 20, to raise £25,000 for mental health charity Tyneside and Northumberland Mind.

So far, 800 people have already pledged to take part in the event, raising funds to support mental health services across the region.

   

*(Image: Peter Rushton)*

   

*(Image: Davey Poremba)*

Jax Higginson, event organiser, said: "North East Skinny Dip is an established and popular annual mass skinny dip which takes place on Druridge Bay in Northumberland.

"It is a celebration of life, of nature, and of our own, unique, physical bodies.

"It's about taking a risk and embracing a moment of pure joy and freedom."

Mr Higginson says the popular event provides a rare opportunity to step into our vulnerability, confront shame and "smash the beauty myth".

Participants are asked to pledge £20 to take part, with all proceeds going directly to the charity.

Since 2012, organisers say the event has raised more than £200,000.

Registration is required, and tickets can be booked at www.northeastskinnydip.co.uk.
