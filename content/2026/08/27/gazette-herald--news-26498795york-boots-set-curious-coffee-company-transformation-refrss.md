PLANS to transform an empty York shop have taken a step forward.

An application to licence a former Boots pharmacy in Clifton as part of its conversion into The Curious Coffee Company café has been lodged with City of York Council.

Eddie Copley-Farnell’s application stated they wanted to turn the empty unit opposite Clifton Green into a coffee shop and eatery serving alcohol alongside food.

It comes after a planning application to convert the pharmacy, which closed in February 2024, into the new venue was approved on Wednesday, August 26.

The Curious Coffee Company café planned for Clifton would seat up to 40 people.

The company already has branches in Haxby and Easingwold.

Plans approved by the council on Wednesday are set to see new hanging and facia signs installed as part of the conversion.

It would open from 7.30am to 5pm on Monday and Tuesday, until 10pm from Wednesday to Friday, from 8.30am to 11pm on Saturday and from 9am to 9pm on Sunday and bank holidays.

  An impression showing how The Curious Coffee Company café, in Clifton, York, could look 

*(Image: The Curious Coffee Company)*

The licensing application is open for public comments until Wednesday, September 23.

Plans stated no other potential tenants had come forward in the more than two years since the pharmacy closed and the old Boots hanging sign had been targeted by vandals.

The application stated: “This shop sits within a collection of local shops, a pub and convenience stores in the area also there are several commercial units nearby with staff.

“They all tend to use the Clifton Green grouping of shops and the public house like a local village area.

“The Curious Coffee Company already has coffee shops in Haxby and Easingwold, they have become destinations of choice for the locals in those areas.”

The closure of the Boots in February 2024 came as part of the company’s plans to cut the amount of its high street pharmacies.
