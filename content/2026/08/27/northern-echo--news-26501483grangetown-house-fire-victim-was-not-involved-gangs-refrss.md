The brother of a woman killed in a horrific house fire alongside her seven-year-old niece has slammed the 'senseless' actions of 'some terrible people in the world'.

It comes as Middlesbrough MP Andy McDonald said gangs and the drug trade appeared to "clearly feature" in incidents including a crash that killed seven on the A66 and the fire that killed Valentina Foster, seven, and her aunt Natalie McDonald, 34, on Wednesday.

Two men, aged 23 and 31, have since been arrested on suspicion of murder and remain in police custody after the blaze on Birchington Avenue in Grangetown.

  Natalie McDonald and Valentina Foster have been named locally as the victims of the Grangetown house fire. 

*(Image: Provided)*

Natalie's devastated brother, Josh Kirton, said: "My sister was not involved with anything to do with gangs, or the (crash on the) A66.

“That’s what makes this so senseless.

“People think they can do whatever they like.

“There’s some terrible people in the world and now my sister and Valentina have gone.”

  Valentina Foster, seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, have been named locally as the two victims who died following a house fire in Grangetown. 

*(Image: Provided)*

Labour MP for Middlesbrough and Thornaby East, Andy McDonald, said gangs and the drug trade are “clearly featuring” in a spate of incidents which have "devastated" Teesside communities.

Speaking to BBC Radio 4, Mr McDonald said: “It does look, to all intents and purposes, as though there is a causal link with organised crime.

"But whatever it is, this is a series of appalling incidents. It’s absolutely devastated our community, and it’s imperative that our people feel safe and secure in their homes and in their neighbourhoods.”

Asked whether gangs are involved, he said: “It’s hard for me to be particular, but I think gangs are clearly featuring in this, as is the drugs trade, but I’ll leave Cleveland Police to be more particular about that because they’re across it.”

  Emergency services at the scene of a major residential fire on Birchington Avenue, in Grangetown, Middlesbrough. 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

Asked whether he understands that people in the area no longer feel like they can report crimes for fear of retribution, he said: “That’s an observation that many people make, and and I understand that.”

He added that Cleveland Police has been “underfunded for many, many years” and that the funding formula “leaves us under-resourced”.

McDonald continued: “We do suffer those metropolitan levels of crime, and yet comparatively, our funding looks much more like a rural area, and it simply cannot continue like that. We’ve got to put resources where the demand is most high, and that is Cleveland, and that’s the message to ministers.”

  Emergency services at the scene of a major residential fire on Birchington Avenue, in Grangetown, Middlesbrough 

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

He said: "The only thing I can say this morning is there is an investigation underway. It will be the fullest possible investigation. The authorities will have all of the support that they need.

“We do need to understand the extent to which they may be connected, but they may not be."

Floral tributes have been left at the scene for the two fire victims in Grangetown.

  Flowers at the scene on Birchington Avenue, Grangetown, near Middlesbrough, where Valentina Foster, aged seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, died following a fire at an end-of-terrace property on Wednesday morning. 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Some tributes read: "Nat, will always be in my thoughts you were the party girl going to be sadly missed RIP."

Another said: "Valentina, what a lovely young girl so sad RIP. Fly high little angel."

  PC Blades and PC Clough 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

This incident comes after a crash on the A66 on Saturday (August 22) which killed two police officers - PCs Matthew Blades and Tom Clough - alongside five others travelling in a Volkswagen Passat on the wrong side of the road.

  Those individuals have been named as **Cole Robert Worthy, 17, Theo Rae, 17, Makai Saddington, 18, Michael Robert Cahill, 23, and Jacob Matusiak, 23.**

Police previously said that the five young men in the Passat may have had links to “serious and organised criminal activity”.

  From left to right: Makai Saddington, 18, Theo Rae, 17, Cole Robert Worthy, 17, Jakub Matusiak, 23, Michael Robert Cahill, 23. 

*(Image: SOCIAL MEDIA)*

Inquests into the deaths of all seven who died were opened on Thursday (August 27) at Teesside Coroner's Court by area coroner for Teesside and Hartlepool, Paul Appleton.

A 39-year-old man is receiving treatment after he was assaulted with a bladed weapon in Eston, some two miles away from a fatal crash on the A66 in Middlesborough on Saturday which killed seven people.

Cleveland Police said they were called to an address on Sedgemoor Road at around midday on Thursday.

No arrests have been made and inquiries are ongoing, the force added.

Police have not ruled out a link between the two incidents.
