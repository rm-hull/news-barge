MARKS & Spencer is offering Sparks customers £10 back on fashion purchases of £50 or more this bank holiday weekend.

From Thursday, August 27 to Monday, August 31, Sparks members can earn the reward in their Sparks wallet by shopping in-store or online at M&S.

The offer includes womenswear, menswear, kidswear, lingerie, shoes, accessories, and jewellery.

Sharry Cramond, marketing and Sparks director, said: "This bank holiday we're giving millions of Sparks customers the opportunity to earn £10 when they shop with us.

"Whether it's school uniforms for the new term, everyday essentials or a new-season wardrobe refresh, we know many customers are already planning these purchases.

"This is our way of making those shops even more rewarding and giving customers something back."

Shoppers can reach the £50 spend across multiple transactions during the promotional period.

New Sparks members can also take advantage of the offer by signing up during the weekend.
