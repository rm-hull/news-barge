RYANAIR has revealed its top three travel hotspots for British holidaymakers.

The airline named Bergerac in France, Porto in Portugal, and Pula in Croatia as the most popular destinations for summer 2026, offering travellers a mix of culture, coastline, and city escapes.

Flights to Bergerac are available from several UK airports, including Bristol, Bournemouth, East Midlands, Edinburgh, Liverpool, and London Stansted.

Porto can be reached from Belfast, Birmingham, Bristol, Edinburgh, Leeds Bradford, Liverpool, London Stansted, and Manchester, while Pula is served from London Stansted.

Jade Kirwan, director of communications at Ryanair, said: "We’re pleased to reveal Ryanair’s top three destinations from the UK this summer – Bergerac, Porto and Pula.

"So, if you haven’t gotten away yet this summer, why not take inspiration from your fellow UK holidaymakers and book a Ryanair flight to one of our top destinations today."

Ryanair’s full Summer 2026 schedule, featuring more than 600 routes from the UK, is now available to book via the airline’s app and website.

The carrier is encouraging travellers to make the most of the remaining summer season with affordable flights across Europe and beyond.

Bookings can be made directly through Ryanair’s official platforms.
