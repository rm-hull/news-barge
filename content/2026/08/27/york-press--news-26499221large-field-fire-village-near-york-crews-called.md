Emergency services have tackled a large field fire in a village near York.

Four fire crews were called to the scene in a stubble field off Low Moor Lane in Hessay shortly after 5pm on Wednesday (August 26).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the blaze measured around 100m x 300m.

A service spokesperson said its crew from Acomb arrived on the scene at about 5pm and called for two more fire appliances as well as an Argocat off road vehicle. Firefighters from Harrogate were also called to the scene, they said.

The spokesperson said the cause of the fire is believed to be accidental.
