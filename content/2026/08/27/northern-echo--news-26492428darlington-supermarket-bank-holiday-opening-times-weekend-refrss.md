Darlington shoppers planning a last-minute barbecue, family get-together or Bank Holiday food are being urged to check opening times before heading out this weekend.

Several of the town’s largest supermarkets will operate different hours over the August Bank Holiday, meaning customers could face earlier closing times than usual.

With thousands expected to visit stores across Morton Park, Yarm Road, North Road and Victoria Road over the long weekend, retailers are warning that opening hours may vary between branches.

Here’s what we know so far about supermarket opening times in Darlington…

### 
  **Tesco**

Tesco customers are being advised to check individual store opening hours, as times will vary depending on location and size.

Larger stores and large Express Stores are expected to be open from 8am to 6pm, with smaller express stores maintaining their normal hours.

Locations include:

- Morton Park

- Woodland Road

- Bondgate

### 
  **Aldi**

Aldi has confirmed stores in England will generally open between 8am and 8pm, although shoppers have been urged to check their local store.

Locations Include:

- Yarm Road Retail Park

- Faverdale East Business Park

   

*(Image: Ian West/PA Wire)*

### 
  **Morrisons**

Morrisons is expecting its larger stores to trade from 7am until 8pm, with Morrisons Daily stores being open their usual 6am – 10pm.

Locations Include:

- North Road

### 
  **Asda**

Most larger Asda branches are expected to open between 8am and 10pm during the bank holiday period.

Locations Include:

- Winbush Way

- Neasham Road

### 
  **Sainsbury’s**

Sainsburys has said its larger stores will operate from 7am until 10pm, with smaller stores sticking to their usual opening times.

Locations Include:

- Victoria Road

- Quacker Lane

### 
  **Lidl**

Lidl has indicated that many stores in England will trade between 8am and 10pm on Bank Holiday Monday.

Locations Include:

- Yarm Road

### 
  **Iceland**

Iceland stores often operate reduced hours on bank holidays, with many opening either 8am to 7pm or 9am to 6pm.

Locations Include:

- Yarm Road

- North Road

Although supermarkets have released guidance for the Bank Holiday weekend, individual branches can change opening times at short notice.

Motorists heading to Morton Park, Yarm Road or Victoria Road are also likely to encounter busier car parks than normal as shoppers stock up for the final long weekend of the summer.

The safest option is to check your chosen store before making the journey.
