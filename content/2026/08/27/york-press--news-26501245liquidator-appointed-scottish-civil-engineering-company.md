A liquidator has been appointed to a UK civil engineer.

The move follows court action by HMRC against the company.

TM Civil Engineering Limited was the subject of a petition presented to Falkirk Sheriff Court by His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs.

It called for the company to be wound up and for the court to appoint a liquidator.

The June action also invited interested parties to contact the court within eight days.

Now, Aver Chartered Accountants, has been appointed liquidator “following a deemed consent procedure”.

The nature of the company’s business was described as "construction of civil engineering projects".

The most recent accounts for the company, from 2020, show tax and social security owed £164,989, set against £123,173 the year before.

The liquidator said: “I can confirm the company is in liquidation. No further information can be disclosed at this time regarding the circumstances or redundancy position.”
