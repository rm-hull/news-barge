# Tiny but award-winning theatre company founded by Harrogate writer to revive 'much loved' hit

## A small Harrogate village theatre company which has punched well above its weight for nearly 30 years is to bring back one of its biggest hits shortly.

Renowned for touring village halls and rural locations usually overlooked by most theatre companies, Badapple Theatre was founded in 1998 by Kate Bramley, a successful comedy writer from Harrogate who is the group’s artistic director.

The tiny but award-winning company, which is currently the Associate Company of York Theatre Royal, is back on the road for 30 dates – ten of them in rural venues in North Yorkshire.

Back to the Land Girls will see Badapple Theatre revive a play by Kate, based on her 2010 interviews with former Land Girls from Yorkshire.

One of its best-loved classic shows, Back to the Land Girls is the comic and poignant journey of Buff and Biddy who arrive in the countryside not knowing one end of a cow from the other.

With songs by SONY Award-winning songwriter and BBC Radio 4 host Jez Lowe, the show returns after a gap of 15 years but its story remains as relevant as ever.

Kate Bramley said: “Back to the Land Girls is such a hopeful story.

"It was such a privilege to speak with these women and it's a great joy to share these tall tales once more."

Running from September 9 to October 25, Badapple Theatre will bring the show to Samwaies Hall at Wath near Ripon on September 11.
