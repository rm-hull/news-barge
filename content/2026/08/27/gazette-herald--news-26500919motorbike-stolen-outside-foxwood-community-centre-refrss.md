A motorbike has been stolen from outside a York community centre.

The incident happened outside the Foxwood Community Centre between 5.30pm and 6.45pm on Tuesday (August 25), North Yorkshire Police said.

A silver Honda 125cc motorbike was taken from the premises.

North Yorkshire Police is urging anyone with information about the theft to email Bethany Broadhead on [Bethany.Broadhead@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Bethany.Broadhead@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.

Quote reference 12260166383 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
