A WOMAN has been left angered after receiving a parking charge when her card payment appeared to go through at a town centre car park.

Liz McNeil visited the Euro Car Parks car park just off Skipton’s High Street on May 26.

Ms McNeil said: “I have an issue with Euro Car Parks in Skipton.

“I parked in the car park behind the former Rackhams store."

Ms McNeil claimed she tried to pay the £2.20 parking fee by card and received a “payment accepted” message on the machine.

She said she then received a reversal receipt showing that the fee had been debited and that the transaction was “not a parking ticket”.

Ms McNeil then went shopping.

## What followed?

A £60 Parking Charge Notice followed, which rose to £100 when she did not pay it within 14 days.

Ms McNeil said: “I paid £2.20 to park but they said I had not paid.”

The 79-year-old says she has spent months challenging the charge, but her appeal was rejected.

She said: “I sent them evidence but my appeal was rejected.”

## 'I think this is outrageous'

Ms McNeil said she was afraid when the company sent a collection notice.

She said: “I tried to pay, put my money in and received a ‘payment accepted’ message.

“I think this is outrageous this has happened on the local high street.”

Ms McNeil said: "I’m appealing as a matter of principle and I want to alert the town and warn other people."

Ms McNeil said she had evidence that the machine screen showed she had paid.

Euro Car Parks has been contacted for comment, but the parking firm did not reply.
