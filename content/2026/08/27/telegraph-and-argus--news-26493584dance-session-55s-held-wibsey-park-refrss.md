A DANCE session for over 55s will be held at a Bradford arts venue on Friday.

Dance On will be holding drop in taster sessions at The Beacon in Wibsey Park between 10.30am and 11.30am on August 28.

Everyone, from seasoned dancers to newcomers, are welcome at the event, which will be led by professional dance artists and coordinated by Yorkshire Dance.

  If you have questions about accessing this workshop, email **[engagement@bradfordculture.co.uk](mailto:engagement@bradfordculture.co.uk)**

Those taking part can do as much or as little as you like, and can be seated or standing. The classes are all adaptable to different mobility levels.

There are 20 spaces in each workshop (first come, first served), no booking required.
