AN 11-year-old boy has been seriously injured after falling off an e-scooter on a major Bradford road.

The incident happened at around 8pm on Wednesday on Barkerend Road near the junction with Hendford Drive.

The boy was riding downhill towards Bradford city centre when he came off the scooter, the force said.

He was taken to hospital with serious injuries that are not believed to be life-threatening.

Police added that he was the only rider on the scooter but was travelling with two other males who were also on an e-scooter.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for the force: "Police are appealing for witnesses and footage after a boy was seriously injured when he fell off an e-scooter in Bradford.

"It happened at about 8.10pm yesterday (Wednesday) in Barkerend Road.

"An 11-year-old boy was travelling downhill towards Bradford city centre when he came off the scooter, close to the Hendford Drive junction.

"He was taken to hospital with serious injuries, which are not believed to be life-threatening.

"The boy was the sole rider of the scooter, but was travelling with two other males who were also on an e-scooter.

"The Roads Policing Unit is keen to speak with anyone who witnessed the incident, or who saw what happened in the time leading up to it.

"They are also keen to hear from anyone who saw, or who has footage of the scooter being taken away after the incident.

"If you can assist with this investigation then please contact the Roads Policing Unit via www.westyorkshire.police.co.uk/LiveChat, quoting log reference 1656 of 26 August."

On Thursday, a spokesperson for the Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 8.10pm last night (August 26) to report a person who had fallen from a scooter on Barkerend Road in Bradford.

"An ambulance and a rapid response vehicle were dispatched to the scene and the patient was conveyed to hospital."
