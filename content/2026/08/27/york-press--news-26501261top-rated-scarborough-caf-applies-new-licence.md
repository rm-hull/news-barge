## Top rated café applies for new licence in North Yorkshire town

Walkers Of Bar Street, Scarborough. Courtesy Numminen, LDRS.

ONE of the top-rated cafés in a popular North Yorkshire resort has applied for a new licence from the council.

One of Scarborough’s most popular coffee spots has applied for permission to place tables and chairs outside the business at 14, Bar Street in the town centre.

Walkers of Bar Street is seeking a new pavement licence from North Yorkshire Council. If Andrew Walker’s application is approved, two tables and six chairs could be sited on the popular thoroughfare.

  Walkers Of Bar Street, Scarbroough. 14 Bar Street. Courtesy Numminen, LDRS.


On TripAdvisor, the coffee shop is rated as the sixth best café in Scarborough and has an overall rating of 4.9 out of five, based on customer reviews.

One customer left a review stating: “If we’re looking for coffee when visiting Scarborough, we always go to Walkers on Bar Street. It’s always bustling with people and spotlessly clean.”

Another customer described the business as “a credit to the owner, the resort, and the hospitality industry”.

The deadline for members of the public to comment on the pavement seating plans is Friday, September 4.

Anyone wishing to make representations regarding the application should give notice in writing to: North Yorkshire Council, Licensing Services, Town Hall, St Nicholas Street, Scarborough, YO11 2HG.

  Bar Street In Scarborough'S Town Centre. Courtesy Anttoni James Numminen, LDRS.


The authority can also be contacted by email at [licensing.east@northyorks.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing.east@northyorks.gov.uk.)

The council’s website states: “It is an offence knowingly or recklessly to make a false statement in connection with an application punishable on summary conviction by an unlimited fine.”
