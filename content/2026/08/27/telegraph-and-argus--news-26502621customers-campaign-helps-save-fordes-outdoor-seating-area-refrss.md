A WAVE of public support has helped Ilkley restaurant Forde secure permission to keep its controversial outdoor seating area.

The venue announced on social media today that Bradford Council has agreed to allow the seating area to remain, following a campaign that attracted widespread backing from customers, local residents and businesses.

Earlier this month, the restaurant revealed it faced losing the seating area and warned the decision could have significant implications for the business. The announcement prompted thousands of supportive messages online, with customers offering advice, contacting politicians, sharing posts on social media and visiting the restaurant in person to show their backing.

In a message to supporters, Forde said it had expected customers to be "angry and disappointed" when it announced the situation, but instead received an overwhelming response.

The business said: "You inboxed us with technical and legal advice, came into the restaurant to cheer us on in person and shake our hands, tagged MPs, caught the attention of the papers and radio and started hashtags to keep the support loud on social media.

"We will never ever be able to thank you sufficiently for all of your support, in all of its forms, but we can tell you that it worked."

The restaurant added: "You shouted so loud for us that the council heard us. We have been allowed to keep the seating area."

While the business will be required to pay additional costs to retain the seating area, it said permission has been granted for it to remain in its current form, subject to affordability.

To thank supporters, Forde is inviting customers to enjoy a complimentary drink throughout September in the atrium seating area.

The business said the response from customers had been extraordinary and pledged to continue operating in Ilkley for as long as possible.

It added: "Providing the cost of the atrium seating isn't too high, we will now be staying put and will keep trying as long as we're still standing.

"Thank you from the bottom of our hearts. We love you all."
