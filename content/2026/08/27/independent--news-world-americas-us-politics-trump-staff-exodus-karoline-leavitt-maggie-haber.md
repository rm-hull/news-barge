The mood inside the White House has reportedly taken a dive and numerous staffers could be heading towards the exit sometime soon, following Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt’s impending departure.

“He is not in a great place with his staff, according to everyone we talked to,” *New York Times* correspondent Maggie Haberman, co-author of a recent book on Trump, told MS Now on Thursday. “He is lashing out a lot, which tends to happen when things are not going well.”

“Most people have decided that they want to go do something else,” she added.

Reports have trickled out about tension inside the administration.

There has reportedly been grumbling inside the White House about Natalie Harp, an aide known for her intense devotion to the president.

![Numerous Trump staff members are considering leaving the administration this midterm season, according to Trump whisperer Maggie Haberman](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/01/13/2026-07-31T173757Z_311877760_RC23PMAPK364_RTRMADP_3_USA-TRUMP.JPG)

“She is not beloved by a lot of her colleagues,” Haberman told CNN last week.

The president allegedly recently confronted Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth at Camp David about how the Iran war is causing a shortage of U.S. munitions that could limit American options to end the conflict.

Trump allegedly expressed that he felt misled about the dwindling weapons stockpile, saying that he thought the issue “had been fixed,” two people familiar with the matter told *The Washington Post*.

The report prompted the president to lash out on social media against “treasonous” leakers.

“Defense companies are building the largest number of plants and factories in our country’s history,” the president wrote on Truth Social earlier this month about the “100 percent fake” news story. “The ‘leakers’ of these treasonous statements are being hunted down. Long term jail sentences will be sought!”

The White House was also reportedly unhappy that FBI Director Kash Patel was seen partying with members of the victorious U.S. Olympic hockey team earlier this year and openly discussed firing him.

The White House and Patel have denied such accounts, and the FBI director launched a defamation suit against *The Atlantic* over its reporting about Patel’s alleged troubles in office. 

![Multiple high-profile officials have left the Cabinet this year, including Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/23/20/01K4M5QXHT5BWZ08VNKT5Y8NC1.jpg)

Before Leavitt’s exit, expected at the end of this month, there were a few high-profile staff changes.

In March, Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem was ousted, in the wake of scrutiny into department spending and mass outrage after immigration agents fatally shot two protesters in Minnesota.

The following month, Attorney General Pam Bondi was fired, following chaos surrounding the DOJ’s handling of the Epstein files.

Over the fall, Trump had publicly called on Bondi to be more aggressive about prosecuting his enemies.
