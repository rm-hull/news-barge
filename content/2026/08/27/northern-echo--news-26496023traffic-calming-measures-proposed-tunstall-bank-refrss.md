Traffic calming measures aimed at reducing vehicle speeds are set to be introduced in a North East city.

Sunderland City Council has announced its intention to install these measures on Tunstall Bank in an effort to control traffic speeds.

The current speed limit on this stretch of road is 20mph.

The proposed traffic calming measures include one speed table and two speed cushions, which are raised sections of the road designed to help control how fast vehicles drive.

The speed table will be the full width of the road, 20 metres in length, with a height of 100mm.

The location of the speed table is set to be 6.6 metres west of the extended property boundary of 6 and 7 Skipsea View.

The two speed cushions will each be 1.8 metres wide, 2 metres long, and have a height of 75mm.

The eastbound speed cushion is planned to be positioned 2 metres west of the extended property boundary of 14 and 15 Skipsea View.

The westbound speed cushion is planned to be located 28.2 metres east of the same boundary.

The plans, along with a statement explaining the reasons for the proposed road humps, can be viewed at City Hall, Plater Way, Sunderland until the end of the consultation period.

Electronic copies of the documents are also available upon request from Sunderland City Council.

Residents and other interested parties have until September 17 to submit their objections to the proposed road humps.

These should be made in writing to E Waugh, Assistant Director of Law and Governance, City Hall, Plater Way, Sunderland, or via email.

All objections must state the grounds on which they are made and should quote reference PB/83439 in all correspondence.
