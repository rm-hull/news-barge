A highly-rated Ryedale pub has hit out at “vexatious lies” made against the business on a leading reviews website.

The Ham and Cheese at Scagglethorpe, near Malton, has posted about the matter on the pub’s Facebook site concerning a Tripadvisor post made earlier in the month.

However, landlady Claire Marsh has since posted on the pub’s site that she and her staff have been “overwhelmed” by support from the public concerning the “particularly hurtful and completely untrue review.”

Claire told The Press she had reported the matter to Tripadvisor, asking for the review to be removed. She would also report it to the police.

Claire, who has been running the highly-regarded pub for 12 years, said: “I was absolutely devastated. We are not that kind of pub.”

However, it appears that Tripadvisor, which awarded the Ham and Cheese a Traveller’s Choice Award for 2025, may have removed the post, as The Press could not find it today (Thursday).

Either way, Claire reports “exceptional support” with people ringing and calling in to show their backing for the Ham and Cheese.

The landlady continued: “It’s absolutely ludicrous that people can put lies online. It’s vexatious.

“Tripadvisor is an outpouring for some people’s emotion. If somebody has a real issue, if they discuss it at the time, we can look to sort it out. We take reviews extremely seriously. If someone has a problem, we want to make sure that never happens again. We want everybody who walks through the door to be happy.”

The reviews websites show the Ham and Cheese to be one of the most popular pubs or restaurants in Ryedale.

Tripadvisor awards the Ham and Cheese 4.6 stars out of five, based on 563 reviews, ranking it fifth out of 83 restaurants in Malton.

Google awards 4.7 stars out of five, based on 918 reviews.

The latest Tripadvisor review awarded five stars and said: “Lovely pub and great food.”

It continued: “Just recently moved to the area and am having lots of work done on the house. The kitchen was out of action for a month and so I ate at this pub often. Food is always lovely and reasonably priced. Staff are very friendly and the pub is dog friendly. I tend to go before 3pm as it does get busier after that and they allow children in the pub too.”

The latest Google review also awarded five stars.

It said: “The family room was immaculate, spotless and spacious. Also, the beds were extremely comfortable with nice pillows. The restaurant service was excellent and was very well presented as staff were very reliable and fast for service and I would highly recommend staying as it is very enjoyable. Also the breakfast was amazing, highly recommend the Spanish eggs and cappuccino as it was spectacular.”
