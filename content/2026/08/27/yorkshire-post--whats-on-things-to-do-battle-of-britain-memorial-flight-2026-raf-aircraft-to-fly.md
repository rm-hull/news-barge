# Battle of Britain Memorial Flight 2026: RAF aircraft to fly over Yorkshire museum as part of memorial event next month

## The Battle of Britain Memorial Flight aircraft is set to fly over the Yorkshire Air Museum as part of its largest memorial event.

The Yorkshire Air Museum (YAM) is hosting its largest memorial event in September honouring the service and sacrifice of members of allied air forces past and present.

Allied Air Forces Memorial Day will take place on Sunday, September 6, 2026, which will feature a colourful Parade, with 18 Standards (ceremonial flags) being presented.

It is the Museum’s major ceremonial event, paying tribute to the service of men and women from the Commonwealth and Allied Nations.

![Last year's memorial event at Yorkshire Air Museum. (Pic credit: Yorkshire Air Museum)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjU1MTMyNTA5LTA3YTUtNGQ1Ny05OWM3LTYxM2E2M2E0ZTk4Yjo3NDYwN2E3Yi1jN2ZhLTQzNTgtYTZkYS01ODAxZDE2MzNhOWQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The venue, at Elvington near York, will be welcoming official representatives from the Embassies of France, Belgium, Poland, Canada, Australia and New Zealand this year.

The chief of the UK Air Staff will be officially represented by group captain John ‘Geordie’ Lawson, officer commanding RAF Leeming.

He will conduct a poignant Turning of The Page in YAM’s Book of Remembrance, to the inscription honouring the crew of Nimrod XV230, lost in Kandahar, Afghanistan on Saturday, September 2, 2006, with the loss of 14 personnel on board.

The RAF made a statement, confirming his attendance: “Group Captain Lawson is honoured to accept the invitation and is very happy to participate in this important occasion.

“Thank you once again for including RAF Leeming in what will be a poignant and meaningful commemoration.

“We look forward to joining you in paying tribute and remembering those being honoured.”

The Lord Lieutenant of North Yorkshire, Mrs Johanna Ropner, who represents HM The King is attending the Parade and Commemoration Service and, along with the Sheriff of York, Mr Geoff Cossins, will give a reading during the ceremony.

An aerial tribute will take the form of a flypast by a fighter from the Battle of Britain Memorial Flight, expected mid-afternoon, subject to weather and serviceability conditions, which will provide a spectacular conclusion to the day’s proceedings.

The event is open to the public, with a service being held at 12pm in the Museum’s main hangar, under the nose of the Halifax bomber, Friday the 13th. It will be led by Rev Charles ‘Taff’ Morgan MBE RAF (Rtd) YAM Honorary Chaplain.

No special tickets are required.
