A hedgehog has died after being kicked by a group of youths.

The incident occurred on Wheelands Avenue, Sharlston, around 6pm last night (August 26).

Six youths were seen to be kicking the animal around.

West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers are investigating an incident that occurred on Wheelands Avenue, Sharlston, between 1800 - 1815, where a group of approximately 6 youths were seen to kick a hedgehog about.

"The animal was taken to a local rescue but unfortunately died of its injuries."

If anybody has any knowledge of who may have been involved in the incident please call 101 and quote 13260483304.
