# Police make arrest after driver crashes, runs away and 'throws cans into bush'

## A man has been arrested after a car was driven on the wrong side of the road and crashed into another vehicle in Yorkshire.

Humberside Police said the driver reportedly then fled the scene, while three people were seen to get out of the car and throw cans into a bush before running away.

The force said it was called following reports of a crash on Marton Lane in Beverley, near to the Tesco, at around 10.40pm on Wednesday (Aug 26).

No one was hurt in the crash.

An 18-year-old man, who was later identified as the driver of the car, has been arrested.

![The car crashed on Morton Lane in Beverley and the driver ran towards the Tesco car park](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/27/9/11/Crash-Beverley.png?trim=0,67,0,66&width=640&quality=65)



A statement from Humberside Police said: “Following the collision, the driver reportedly fled from the scene towards Tesco car park and three people were seen to exit the vehicle and throw what were thought to be cans into a bush.

“Officers were deployed, and after initial enquiries, an 18-year-old man was subsequently identified as the driver of the vehicle and was arrested on suspicion of drink driving.

“He remains in police custody assisting with ongoing enquiries at this time.”
