A County Durham robber who stole a man's car has been jailed for more than four years.

Josh Hyde and Jordan Fluen were accused of jointly stealing the Ford Mondeo from the owner, in Blackhall Colliery, on Monday March 2.

Both appeared at Durham Crown Court, on April 1, when Hyde pleaded not guilty and was remanded in custody.

The case was adjourned for a three-day trial due to start at Newcastle Crown Court, last week.

But 23-year-old Hyde, formerly of Peterlee, now said to be of no fixed abode, changed his plea and admitted the robbery on the scheduled first day of the trial, on August 17.

He appeared back before the court the following day when he received a four-and-a-half-year prison sentence, with a £228 victim surcharge.

His co-accused, 24-year-old Fluen, of Throston Grange Lane, Hartlepool, who did not plead to the robbery, admitted handling stolen goods, the car.

He will be sentenced next month following preparation of background reports on him by the Probation Service.

Fluen was bailed to return for sentence at the court on Monday September 28.
