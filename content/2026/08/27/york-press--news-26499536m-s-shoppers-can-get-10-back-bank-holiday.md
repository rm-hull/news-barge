M&S is giving millions of Sparks members the chance to earn £10 back this Bank Holiday weekend – and it could be particularly useful for families still racing to finish their back-to-school shopping.

From Thursday, August 27 to Monday, August 31, Sparks members who spend £50 or more on fashion at M&S will receive £10 in their Sparks Wallet.

The offer comes at one of the busiest shopping points of the year, with parents stocking up on school uniforms and shoppers beginning to switch their wardrobes from summer clothes to autumn staples.

And if you're already planning to spend £50 at M&S, the offer could effectively mean getting £10 back to spend on your next shop.


## How to get the £10 M&S Sparks reward

The offer is available to Sparks members who spend at least £50 on fashion between August 27 and August 31.

The £10 reward will be paid directly into the customer's Sparks Wallet.

That means shoppers don't need to buy a particular product or hunt down a special discount – the offer applies when the £50 fashion spending threshold is reached.

M&S has 25 million Sparks members, meaning a huge number of shoppers could potentially take advantage.

The timing could also be handy for parents still completing their school shopping.

Recent data shows UK shoppers spend an average of £116.95 on school uniform and supplies each year, with spending peaking around the August bank holiday weekend.

Schoolwear was also responsible for six of M&S's top 10 bestselling products during the same period last year.

  


## What to buy with the £50 spend

The reward applies to M&S fashion, meaning shoppers could use the opportunity to pick up school essentials as well as pieces for their autumn wardrobe.

For those who have already bought the children's uniform, the timing could be ideal for stocking up on new-season clothes.

M&S says almost three-quarters of UK adults buy new clothing for the season ahead, while 53 per cent start thinking about updating their wardrobes before summer has even ended.

And shoppers are already looking towards autumn, with jumpers, footwear, coats and layering pieces proving popular.
