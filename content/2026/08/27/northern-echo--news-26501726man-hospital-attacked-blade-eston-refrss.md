Armed police and an air ambulance were called after a person was attacked with a blade.

Emergency services were seen on Sedgemoor Road in the Bankfields area of Eston on Thursday afternoon (August 27), with a large armed police presence at the scene.

A spokesperson for Cleveland Police said: "A 39-year-old man is receiving treatment following an assault involving a bladed weapon in an address on Sedgemoor Road in Eston around midday today (Thursday, August 27).

"No arrests have been made at this time, however, enquiries are ongoing into the incident."

A North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) spokesperson said: "We were called to a person injured at 12 noon on August 27.

"We dispatched a clinical team leader and an ambulance crew who took one patient to hospital. We called on Great North Air Ambulance for support at the scene."

The extent of the injuries have not yet been confirmed.

Videos shared on social media later in the afternoon showed armed police and dog handlers in the area during investigations.
