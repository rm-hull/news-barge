Have you seen this man?

He’s Christopher Norton, 45, and he’s wanted on recall to prison after he breached the terms of his licence.

North Yorkshire Police believe Norton’s in the York area but he also has connections to Selby.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Norton and we are now appealing for your help to find him.

“If you have an immediate sighting of him, please call 999.

“If you have any other information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online.”
