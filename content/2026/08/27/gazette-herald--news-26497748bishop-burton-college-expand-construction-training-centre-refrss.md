A college is set to expand its construction training facilities following a major funding boost.

Bishop Burton College has secured £2.502 million from the Department for Education’s Construction Skills Capacity Fund to extend its Technical and Skills Construction and Engineering Centre, creating nearly 100 additional training places.

The investment will fund new workshops and support the introduction of courses in painting and decorating, as well as interior and exterior wall insulation.

Danny Metters, CEO and principal of Bishop Burton College, said: "This is fantastic news for Bishop Burton College, our learners, employers and the wider region.

"The investment recognises the important role colleges play in developing the skilled workforce needed to support economic growth and deliver the homes, infrastructure and retrofit projects of the future."

The expansion will accommodate 93 more learners and help address construction skills shortages across the region.

Existing facilities are already at full capacity due to rising demand for construction courses.

Mr Metters said: "Introducing new programmes in Painting and Decorating and Interior and Exterior Wall Insulation will ensure our curriculum continues to reflect the needs of employers and the evolving construction industry."

Construction of the project is expected to begin following completion of the project's mobilisation phase.
