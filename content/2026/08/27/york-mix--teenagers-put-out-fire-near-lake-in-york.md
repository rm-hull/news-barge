Firefighters were called to an incident close to a York lake last night (Wednesday).

They went to **Rawcliffe Lake** off**Greystoke Road**, at** 8.30pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb crews responded to investigate a report of a small fire by the lake.

#### Incident location

“A small group of teenagers had extinguished the fire prior to arrival of the fire service. Crew gave advice.”
