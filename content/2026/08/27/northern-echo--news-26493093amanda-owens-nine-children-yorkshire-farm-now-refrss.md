When Our Yorkshire Farm first arrived on Channel 5, viewers watched Amanda and Clive Owen raising nine children in one of the most remote corners of the Yorkshire Dales.

Raven, Reuben, Miles, Edith, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy became familiar faces as cameras documented everyday life at the family's 2,000-acre Ravenseat Farm in Upper Swaledale.

But much has changed since the programme first aired in 2018.

Some of Amanda and Clive's eldest children have now left school and developed careers of their own, while others continue to help with farming and the family's ambitious restoration of neighbouring farmhouse Anty John's.

The family has also returned to television in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, with a fourth series due to continue their story.

So what are Amanda and Clive Owen's nine children doing now?

## Raven Owen

Raven is Amanda and Clive's eldest child and has followed a very different career from the traditional farming life viewers saw at Ravenseat.

She studied Biomedical Science at York St John University, graduating with first-class honours in 2022 before going on to complete a master's degree in the same subject.

Raven's professional profile says her research has included work relating to oncology and improving chemotherapy drugs.

She has since moved into scientific work and is listed by her management as working as an assistant scientist, while a more recent professional profile describes her work in analytical chemistry and chemical residue analysis.

Despite building a career away from Ravenseat, Raven has not left farming life completely behind.

She still returns to the family's North Yorkshire farm and helps out when needed, while continuing to appear in Our Farm Next Door.

Viewers saw Raven return home during the third series in 2026 to join the rest of the family for youngest sister Nancy's ninth birthday.

## Reuben Owen

Reuben has arguably become the most prominent of the Owen children on television.

After viewers watched his love of machinery develop during Our Yorkshire Farm, he went on to build his own groundworks and heavy plant machinery business.

That eventually resulted in a television career of his own.

Reuben first appeared alongside his father in Beyond the Yorkshire Farm: Reuben and Clive before becoming the focus of Channel 5's Reuben Owen: Life in the Dales.

The programme follows Reuben as he grows his machinery and contracting business alongside friends and members of his family.

A third series aired on Channel 5 in 2026, with episodes continuing through June.

His brothers have also become involved, with Miles appearing alongside him and Sidney joining Reuben for farming and machinery jobs.

Reuben continues to appear in the family's Our Farm Next Door programme as well, meaning he now features across both the Channel 5 and Channel 4/More4 sides of the Owen family's television story.

## Miles Owen

Miles, the third eldest of the nine children, has remained particularly close to the farming world in which he was raised.

He is now described as a full-time farmer, splitting his time between work at Ravenseat and helping Reuben with his groundworks and digger business.

Miles is particularly interested in sheep and cattle farming but has ambitions to broaden his experience into arable agriculture.

His longer-term aim could eventually see him take on more responsibility at the family's farm or establish a farming operation of his own.

He has also become increasingly visible on television.

Alongside appearances in Our Farm Next Door, Miles has joined Reuben on Life in the Dales, combining farming work with his interest in diggers and machinery.

## Edith Owen

Edith was still a young child when viewers first became familiar with the Owen family, but she has now grown into one of the older siblings.

Unlike Raven, Reuben and Miles, considerably less has been made public about Edith's plans away from television.

She nevertheless remains part of the family's current TV story and has continued to appear in Our Farm Next Door alongside her parents and siblings.


That programme has given viewers an opportunity to see just how much the younger Owens have changed since the early days of Our Yorkshire Farm.

Production company Wise Owl Films recently highlighted that transformation itself, remarking on how much the Owen children had grown since filming on Our Farm Next Door began in 2023.

## Violet Owen

Violet is another of the Owen children who has largely remained outside the individual celebrity spotlight.

She grew up on screen helping around Ravenseat and remains involved in the family's television appearances.

Violet is among the children credited throughout Our Farm Next Door, which continues to show the siblings taking part in farm life and the restoration of Anty John's.

Amanda has spoken about wanting her younger children to retain the freedom and independence that comes with growing up in the Dales, describing how they explore the countryside around their home rather than spending their lives indoors.

## Sidney Owen

Sidney, often known as Sid, has increasingly emerged as another of the family's practical hands.

Viewers have seen him working alongside his older brothers and father, including tackling jobs connected to the Anty John's renovation.

During series three of Our Farm Next Door, Sid and Amanda inspected a collapsed bridge on the track leading to the farmhouse.

He later joined Reuben and Clive as they worked on repairing it.

Sid also appeared alongside Reuben and Miles during the 2026 series of Reuben Owen: Life in the Dales, including during sheep shearing at the family farm.

Like his older brothers, he appears to have inherited plenty of the family's enthusiasm for machinery and practical farm work.

## Annas Owen

Annas remains one of the younger Owen children living the rural life viewers first came to know through Our Yorkshire Farm.

Her recent television appearances have increasingly shown her taking responsibility for animals herself.

In series three of Our Farm Next Door, Annas and sister Clemmie were shown hoping to buy their first sheep.

Later in the series, Clive gave Annas, Clemmie and Nancy a lesson in working with the family's sheepdog Nellie – continuing the tradition of the Owen children learning farming skills from an early age.

## Clementine Owen

Clementine, generally known as Clemmie, has also continued to feature prominently among the younger members of the family.

Like Annas, she was shown attempting to negotiate the purchase of her first sheep during the latest series.

She has also joined Amanda on some of the historical adventures surrounding Anty John's.

One episode saw Clemmie and Nancy accompany their mother and a local historian to a church connected with a former resident of the farmhouse, as Amanda continued researching the property's past.

## Nancy Owen

Nancy is the youngest of Amanda and Clive's nine children and was only very young when Our Yorkshire Farm first put the family into millions of homes.

She turned nine during events shown in series three of Our Farm Next Door, with Raven returning to Ravenseat to join the family celebrations.

Nancy has continued to provide some of the programme's lighter moments too.

When new doors were being fitted at Anty John's, she had an important question for the adults – why there wasn't a cat flap.

She has also joined her sisters in learning to work the family's sheepdog and exploring the countryside around Ravenseat.
