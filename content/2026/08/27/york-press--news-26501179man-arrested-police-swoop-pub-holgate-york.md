A man has been arrested after police swooped on a pub in York.

Three police vehicles – one van, one unmarked Audi and a dogs unit vehicle – were parked outside the Fox in Holgate Road at 11pm on Wednesday (August 26).

North Yorkshire Police said its officers were called to the pub following reports of an “intoxicated man who was refusing to leave”.

  Police outside the Fox pub in Holgate Road, York, on Wednesday night (August 26) 

*(Image: Anttoni James Numminen)*

An eyewitness said police were in the pub’s beer garden and the area outside the premises for around 30 minutes.

Police said he a man in his 30s has been arrested on suspicion of breach of the peace.

A police spokesperson said further investigation found the man was also allegedly in breach of his bail conditions.

“He has since been charged and remanded in custody to appear before the courts today,” they said on Thursday.
