The family of a County Durham dad killed in a crash have said they are "truly heartbroken".

Ryan Scarth, from Sherburn Village, died on the A1(M) near Durham after the car he was driving was involved in a one-car crash at around 2.20am on Saturday, August 22.

The 31-year-old sustained fatal injuries and was sadly pronounced dead at the scene.

In a tribute, his family said: "The loss of Ryan has truly broken us as a family.

“Ryan was a son, daddy, brother, uncle, and partner. He lived for his two boys, and his memory will live on through them.

“Ryan has not only left a huge void in our lives but in his friends and the local community too.

“He could walk into a room with his cheeky smile and light it up.

"We are truly heartbroken.

"We will love you forever and always.”

His family are being supported by specialist officers.

Officers would like to speak to any witnesses, or those who may have dashcam footage of the collision.

If you can help, please contact Durham Constabulary’s Serious Collision Investigation Unit by email at [SCIU@durham.police.uk](mailto:SCIU@durham.police.uk) quoting incident number 41 of August 22.
