A FINALIST from Britain’s Got Talent is set to perform in Saltaire next month as part of the annual Saltaire Festival.

Amy Lou Smith will take the stage at Caroline Street Social Club on Friday, September 18.

She finished seventh in the 2023 edition of Britain’s Got Talent and was praised by judges for her powerful performances.

Judge Bruno Tonioli remarked: "The richness, the depth of your tone it is like smoky velvet [as] it’s unique and very very special", while Alesha Dixon added: "I hope this is the beginning of an amazing journey for you".

Amy Lou was 30 weeks pregnant during her audition, and her daughter Aida was born just hours before the episode aired.

At 42, the West Midlands singer was working part-time as a receptionist and performing in pubs on weekends before her rise to national attention.
