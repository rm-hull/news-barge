The gaming tournament is back bigger and better in a brand new venue.

Following a sold-out debut event earlier this year, Mario Kart Live returns to York on **Friday 4 September** with a larger venue, a bigger screen setup, and an upgraded live music experience.

Taking place at the Creative Centre at York St John University, the event combines classic Nintendo racing with a live band performing the soundtrack and sound effects in real time.

The game audio will be muted so the band can play a score spanning funk, jazz, dance, and metal, synced directly to the action on screen.

The move to the Creative Centre means an increased venue capacity, a bigger screen layout, and fully accessible seating to accommodate the demand after racer tickets for the inaugural show sold out quickly.

Attendees can choose to compete on stage for prizes or enjoy the atmosphere as spectators.

Fancy dress is strongly encouraged, and there’s a prize awarded for the tournament champion.

On scaling up the event, organiser Ryan Willis said, “The reaction to our first event was overwhelming, and moving to the Creative Centre lets us bring in a bigger screen, welcome more fans, and make the whole night fully accessible.

“It’s all about light-hearted silliness, brilliant live music, and bringing people together for a night of nostalgic fun.”

Tickets are on sale now here, at £16 for Racers and £12 for Spectators.
