A MAN has died following a crash in the Bradford district.

A 65-year-old motorcyclist passed away in hospital after suffering serious injuries following a collision on Hebden Bridge Road in Oxenhope on Sunday.

The man was riding a silver Suzuki SV1000 K3 towards Hill House Edge Lane at about 4.44pm when the vehicle struck a kerb and collided with a wall.

The Major Collision Enquiry Team has taken over the investigation and is appealing for anyone who witnessed the collision, or who has footage that may assist to contact them.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for the force: "Police are renewing their appeal for witnesses and footage to an incident in Oxenhope after the motorcyclist involved died in hospital.

"The 65-year-old man passed away on Tuesday after suffering serious injuries in the incident, which occurred on Hebden Bridge Road at about 4.44pm on Sunday, 23 August.

"He was riding a silver Suzuki SV1000 K3 motorbike along the road towards Hill House Edge Lane when it struck a kerb before colliding with a wall.

"The Major Collision Enquiry Team has now taken over the investigation and is appealing for anyone who witnessed the collision, or who has footage that may assist to contact them via www.westyorkshire.police.co.uk/LiveChat, quoting log reference 1130 of 23 August."
