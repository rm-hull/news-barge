A temporary speed limit will be enforced for roadworks in Bishop Wilton.

Drivers along Garrowby Hill and South Wold will face a 40mph speed limit starting September 14.

This change comes to enable the proposed works to be carried out and completed.

The council has stated that the reduced speed limit is necessary to ensure public safety while the work is carried out.

The project is expected to take 12 days, but the speed limit order could remain in place for up to 18 months, depending on the progress of the work.

For further information, residents can contact Streetscene Services, Highways Maintenance on 01482 393939.

The reference for this project is CB.
