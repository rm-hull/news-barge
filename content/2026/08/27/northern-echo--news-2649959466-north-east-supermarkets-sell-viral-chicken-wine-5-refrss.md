Asda shoppers across the North East can pick up a bottle of viral 'chicken wine' for £5 during the Bank Holiday weekend.

La Vieille Ferme Rosé — fondly known as 'chicken wine' because of the hen and rooster featured on its label — has been reduced from £8.95 to £5.

The offer is running until September 1, giving shoppers a chance to stock up before the end of summer.

The French rosé, whose name translates as 'The Old Farm', has become a social-media favourite because of its distinctive farmyard label.

A standard 75cl bottle is usually priced between £8 and £9, and the wine is known for fruity aromas with strawberry and raspberry notes.

## North East stores included

Asda has stores in the North East in areas including in Ashington, Benwell, Billingham, Bishop Auckland, Blyth, Chester-le-Street, Cramlington, Darlington, Durham, Gateshead, Hartlepool, Hexham, Middlesbrough, Newcastle, Peterlee, Redcar, Seaham, South Shields, Spennymoor, Stanley, Stockton, Sunderland, Thornaby, Wallsend and Washington.

The offer is available in-store, although shoppers are advised to check stock at their local Asda before travelling.

## Aldi also reduces rival 'dupe' rosé

The Asda promotion comes as Aldi has reduced the price of its Le Grand Poulet Rosé Magnum, a 1.5-litre wine often compared to La Vieille Ferme.

The 'duplicate' bottle has been cut from £11.99 to £9.99 from Thursday, August 27, until September 2, while stocks last.

Customers are limited to three bottles each.
