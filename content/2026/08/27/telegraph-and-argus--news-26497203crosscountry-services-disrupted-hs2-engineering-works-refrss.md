RAIL improvement works are set to cause disruption for passengers over the bank holiday weekend.

CrossCountry services will be affected by engineering works taking place from Saturday, August 29, to Monday, August 31, with journeys through Birmingham New Street facing delays and changes due to HS2-related work.

The line between Birmingham New Street and Coleshill Parkway will be closed to allow for engineers to install part of a new viaduct over the railway near Water Orton, part of ongoing work to accommodate the future high-speed rail line.

Mark Anderson, CrossCountry's Customer & Commercial Director, said: "This essential engineering work will help keep the railway resilient and ready for the future, ensuring passengers can continue to enjoy smooth and reliable journeys.

"From Saturday 29 until Monday 31 August, journeys through Birmingham New Street will take longer, with some journeys also requiring rail replacement bus services."

CrossCountry has advised passengers to check for updates and allow extra time for travel.

Mr Anderson said: "We'd like to thank customers for their patience while this work takes place.

"I'd ask customers to leave more time for their journeys and make sure they don't bring too much luggage – trolleys, in particular, won't be allowed on board CrossCountry services."
