French Connection is opening a new outlet store in the North East this autumn.

The contemporary fashion brand will join the North East’s largest outlet shopping destination, Dalton Park, Seaham, further expanding its range of premium womenswear and wardrobe staples.

The announcement comes as other businesses leave the park, including café eighteen38, which closed this month, and mini golf course The Hole In Wand, which will vacate the site in September.

   

*(Image: Dalton Park)*

Richard Kaye, centre manager at Dalton Park, said: "French Connection is a name so many of our shoppers will know and love, so we’re incredibly excited to be welcoming the brand to Dalton Park this autumn.

"Our shoppers will have even more choice when refreshing their wardrobes with quality fashion at great outlet prices.

"French Connection will bring a fantastic collection of elevated womenswear to Dalton Park, from versatile everyday pieces to capsule wardrobe staples that can be styled throughout the season and beyond."

To celebrate the launch, Dalton Park is offering shoppers the chance to win a £500 French Connection gift voucher, allowing one lucky winner to enjoy a shopping spree when the store opens.

The competition can be found on Dalton Park's official Facebook page.

Simon Donoghue, managing director at French Connection, said: "We are delighted to bring French Connection to Dalton Park Outlet and offer customers a new premium shopping destination.

"Dalton Park provides an excellent opportunity to connect with both existing and new customers, and we're excited to join its strong line-up of premium brands."
