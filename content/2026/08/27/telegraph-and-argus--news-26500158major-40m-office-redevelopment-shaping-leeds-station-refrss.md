A MAJOR office redevelopment is taking shape in the heart of Leeds.

Network Rail is leading a £40 million transformation of Princes Exchange, an eight-storey building beside Leeds station, to create a new rail industry hub that will bring together track and train colleagues under one roof.

Funded by the Department for Transport and Network Rail, the project is being delivered in partnership with Platform4 and contractor Willmott Dixon.

Andrew Banks, finance director at Network Rail, said: "The redevelopment of Princes Exchange will breathe new life into what is already an iconic building in a prime city location.

"We’re proud to be providing upgraded space for Network Rail and train operator colleagues to support them to deliver great service for rail passengers in the north, while opening up this iconic property to the wider market."

The 108,576 sq ft building will also offer five floors of commercial office space, available for letting from mid-2027, while Network Rail and its industry partners will occupy the remaining three floors.

Improvements will include a refurbished main entrance, external façade upgrades, public realm enhancements, and a dedicated rail hub entrance with new staff welfare facilities.

The scheme also features basement-level amenities such as cycle storage and changing rooms.

The project aims to deliver broader social value through apprenticeships, work experience placements, educational activities and support for community projects.

Plans to reuse furniture and materials sustainably are expected to save around 80 tonnes of carbon and result in £10,000 worth of equipment donations to charities.

Andrew Ferguson, interim chief executive of Platform4, said: "Working to maximise the value of rail land for both the railway and for wider public benefit is exactly what Platform4 was set up to do.

"At Princes Exchange, we’ve worked with Network Rail and our other partners not only to deliver landmark office space for colleagues and businesses but also to unlock critical land, enabling rail growth in Leeds."

Network Rail acquired the vacant building in 2024, and the redevelopment is scheduled for completion in June 2027.

Facing the station’s Aire Street entrance, Princes Exchange will also benefit from improved public spaces.
