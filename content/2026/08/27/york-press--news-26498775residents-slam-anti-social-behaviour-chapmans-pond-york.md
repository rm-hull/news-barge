## 'Such a shame' - residents fight back against damage and litter at much-loved pond

Ward councillor Stephen Fenton *(Image: Provided)*

CONCERNS have been raised after anti-social behaviour and overnight camping has caused a York beauty spot to be ‘trashed’.

Residents have shared their upset over Chapman’s Pond in Woodthorpe after it has become blighted by litter, ashes, and human waste from people misusing the site.

One resident, who wanted to remain anonymous, shared his concerns about the anti-social behaviour and litter at the site - telling The Press that he believed that many of the issues at the pond were due to local children.

He added that he suffers from anxiety and finds it difficult to communicate with strangers, with the pond serving as a ‘little haven’ for him to get out of the house – making the situation all the more upsetting.

The resident said: “It's such a shame that there isn’t enough resources for kids in the area, but why destroy a beautiful place? Such a shame.

"There were discarded carrier bags from when they brought food etc in and a bin at exit, it beggars’ belief that they just can't be bothered to clean up.

  A discarded sock at Chapman's pond. 

*(Image: Provided)*

"More worrying is the fires, the young don't understand how dangerous these can be. 20 feet from houses and bone-dry grass and trees. It's seriously worrying.

"I’m just afraid kids will keep at this, because it’s clean when they come back. There are currently 3 tents set up with a makeshift washing line and they are using an inflatable boat to fish. It’s out of hand.”

The resident praised the local councillors, especially Cllr Stephen Fenton, over their handling of the issues at the pond, with the team stepping up after volunteers stopped managing the site ‘due to abusive and threatening behaviour from some of the youths on site and a lack of Police support’.

  Litter and human excrement was found at Chapman's Pond 

*(Image: Provided)*

Cllr Fenton added that there had been a number of people coming to the spot and staying overnight to fish - which he believed was adding to the waste in the area.

He said: “It’s a really difficult issue – the pond is well-used and well-loved by the community here. Residents are really concerned about the state of the pond right now.

“We’ve had a lot of issues with fishing as there’s a number of trophy fish in the pond and people are camping overnight to fish. There are no toilet facilities on site, so that causes issues.

“Bit by bit, the site is being demolished and that’s just really sad. People mentioned setting up a committee like Rowntree Park, but the pond doesn’t lend itself to that approach – our priority is to try and stabilise things here.”

  Litter in the water at Chapman's Pond 

*(Image: Provided)*

He added that there is however ‘some glimmers of hope’ with volunteer litter pickers starting to visit the site more regularly to keep it clean, something which has reportedly had ‘some affect

Cllr Fenton said: “I’m pleased there’s now signs of hope. The majority of people who fish in the pond are responsible, but a minority ruin it for everyone else.

“We’re hoping to keep the momentum up, but it’s sad that it’s taken so long. I’ve tried to work constructively with council officers, so it’s important we keep the pressure on.”
