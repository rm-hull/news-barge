Plans for a major new housing development on the edge of York are moving forward.

Barratt Redrow has announced proposals for roughly 350 homes on land east of Metcalfe Lane, south of Bad Bargain Lane, and north of Murton Way, forming the next phase of York’s ST7 strategic housing allocation.

The site sits next to a northern parcel where up to 380 homes have already been approved.

A spokesperson for Barratt Redrow said: "York needs more homes, including affordable homes, and these proposals offer an opportunity to deliver around 350 new homes as part of a sustainable and well-connected new neighbourhood.

"The plans would provide 30% affordable housing alongside generous green space, play areas, walking and cycling routes, and sustainable drainage features.

"We are now keen to hear from local people and would encourage residents to take the opportunity to review the proposals and share their views."

The scheme includes a mix of housing for first-time buyers, families, downsizers, older residents, and self-build plots.

Thirty per cent of the homes would be designated as affordable, available through both affordable rent and shared ownership.

Plans also include green spaces, play areas, trim trails, and walking routes throughout the site.

New cycling and walking links would connect the development to the approved scheme to the north and its planned community hub.

Sustainable drainage features are planned to manage surface water and support biodiversity.

Developers would provide contributions towards local infrastructure, including education, healthcare and highways.

Residents can view the plans and submit feedback at www.barratt-redrow-murton-way.co.uk.

Barratt Redrow intends to submit a detailed planning application to City of York Council following the consultation.

Public feedback will be considered as part of the final plans.
