# Great British Menu star Stephanie Moon to join Wakefield's Blacker Hall Farm

## Wakefield food destination, Blacker Hall Farm, has announced that it has appointed award-winning chef and Great British Menu star, Stephanie Moon, as chef consultant.

The fifth-generation family-run business said the move comes as it continues develop its “farm-to-fork” restaurant offering.

Ms Moon’s remit will focus on the firm’s Barn Kitchen restaurant, as well as supporting Blacker Hall Farm’s event catering and food-to-go offerings.

Having previously worked with the Blacker Hall Farm team on a project basis, Ms Moon brings more than three decades of experience in professional kitchens, including The Dorchester in London, Rudding Park Hotel in Harrogate, and five-star hotels in the USA, Germany and Switzerland.

![Great British Menu star, Stephanie Moon](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjQ3MDY2MTQyLTE0MWEtNDIzMy04ZWVlLTI5N2ExYTA4N2MyYzoyN2VhMWYyOC03ZmE1LTQ1YzctOGU3Mi1iZDdkNTk4MWEyM2M=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



As well as staring on BBC Two’s The Great British Menu, Ms Moon has featured on BBC One’s Chef’s Programme, and Channel 5’s Street Market Chefs.

Speaking of the appointment, Ms Moon said: “I've always believed that good food starts with understanding your ingredients and respecting the people who produce them. Blacker Hall Farm has that knowledge throughout the business, from the farm and butchery to the kitchen.

“It's a pleasure to be working with Blacker Hall Farm again to support a team that shares those values.”

Edward Garthwaite, owner and managing director of Blacker Hall Farm, said: “Stephanie brings a depth of knowledge that reflects how we approach food at Blacker Hall Farm.

"Working alongside our talented restaurant team, she’ll continue to develop menus that make the most of produce from our farm and trusted local suppliers. Our close relationship between the farm, butchery, bakery and The Barn Kitchen means we can create dishes using fresh, seasonal ingredients with complete confidence in their quality and provenance.”
