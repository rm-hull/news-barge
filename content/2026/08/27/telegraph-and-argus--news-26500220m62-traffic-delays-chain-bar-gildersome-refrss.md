Motorists are experiencing delays on the eastbound M62 between junction 26 at Chain Bar and junction 27 for the M621 at Gildersome.

Traffic is averaging around 30 mph on the stretch, with delays of approximately two minutes reported.

The disruption began at 10.07am.

Drivers travelling towards Leeds are advised to allow extra time for their journey and check for the latest conditions before setting off.

One source told the T&A they had sighted debris on this road.
