**Fulford Cemetery volunteers thanked for improvements**

I WANT to congratulate all the lovely volunteers, who have transformed Fulford Cemetery through the year.

Earlier in the year it was massively overgrown and I have seen bit by bit over the year they have been making it look a lot better cared for.

So, thank you to all of those wonderful people!

When I went in June for Father's Day all around my parents grave was a mess and I spent time trying to clear around it as best I could so I could at least see my parent's gravestone.

Then after we left Fulford I went on to Huntington Cemetery to put flowers on my late friend's grave for her birthday – what a difference!

Huntington Cemetery was in an absolutely brilliant condition! After seeing how bad Fulford was at that same time was very upsetting for me re: my parents' grave and me having to weed to even see their gravestone!

When I went to Fulford in July I could see improvements, but still not round my parents' grave area. However, having been again today, it was in a much better condition, so just wanted to say a big thank you to those volunteers, it means a lot to us oldies who still go and pay our respects!

  *Ann Forshaw,*

  *Fourth Avenue,*

  *York*

  **Think twice before overdoing it this Bank Holiday**

FOR many of us, the upcoming Bank Holiday weekend is set to be a busy social occasion.

We'll be spending time with friends and family, whether that be at home, at a pub, or at any number of festivals and events across the country.

But when the weekend is longer, and there's no early start the next morning, it’s all too easy to drink more than we usually would.

Drinkaware’s latest research shows that a record 91per cent of UK drinkers are taking steps to moderate their alcohol consumption, but that we’re finding these positive everyday habits particularly hard to stick to during holidays and social occasions.

Just over half of us (56 per cent) drink more when on an evening out with friends, and an increasing proportion of us are now binge drinking weekly (that means drinking eight or more units for men, and six or more for women, in one drinking session).

Recognising this doesn’t mean we have to miss out on the fun. As we look ahead to our Bank Holiday weekend, taking extra care when drinking alcohol can help us stay safe and in control.

Planning ahead, whether that's opting for no- and low-alcohol alternatives, keeping track of units, and alternating alcoholic and soft drinks, can help us enjoy the occasion while maintaining a balanced relationship with alcohol.
