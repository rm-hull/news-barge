There have been two reports of anti-social behaviour within the last three weeks on Millfield Road, Chapel Haddlesey.

The reports involved approximately 18 young people on both occasions walking through the village, causing damage to property, being loud, and using offensive language.

It is believed that some of those involved may have been drunk.

The group was described as being aged between 16 and 18 years old, consisting of approximately 10 males and 8 females.

Local Police have a message for parents.

“We would kindly ask parents and guardians to speak with their children regarding their behaviour and the impact that this can have on residents and the local community, in an effort to help prevent any further incidents.

“Any available CCTV footage will be reviewed to determine whether any of the young people involved can be identified.”

Anyone with information relating to these incidents is asked to contact **North Yorkshire Police on 101**, quoting** reference number 12260166576**.
