THE UK's first dedicated dog bus service was forced to cancel a day's operation after vandals attempted to hot-wire it.

Billy's Bus, which transports dogs for walks and adventures while also providing employment and training opportunities through a community-focused social enterprise, revealed the damage in a post on social media.

In a message to supporters, a Billy's Bus spokesperson said the bus had been targeted by people who appeared to have tried to steal it overnight on Tuesday into Wednesday.

They wrote: "How anyone thought they could hot wire a massive bright yellow bus, covered with giant paw prints and get away with it.... Well, just genius."

Every day Billy’s Bus picks up around 15 passengers waiting at stops across areas like Ilkley, Skipton, Otley, Shipley and Guiseley.

  The inside of Billy's Bus 

*(Image: Laura Mohan)*

They are then taken to founder Laura Mohan's very own secure “Bark Park”, which is situated in two “massive” fields at the top of the Cow and Calf in Ilkley, where the dogs enjoy a variety of activities.

Overall, it is a minimum of three hours of fun, exercise and socialisation.

The vandalism left the organisation unable to collect its regular Wednesday dogs, resulting in the cancellation of services and a costly repair bill.

Billy's Bus said the incident had wider consequences than simply damaging a vehicle. The organisation provides opportunities for volunteers, adults with learning difficulties and young adults needing support, training and paid employment.

Billy said: "This yellow bus packed full of beautiful dogs provides volunteers, adults with learning difficulties and young adults who need support and direction in life the opportunity to find support, training, paid work, a brilliant job and a meaningful life."

As a result of the damage, staff and volunteers were unable to work and the organisation lost income through cancelled bookings.

They added: "We've lost a lot of money through cancelling service. We have a big garage bill to pay for. Our team and volunteers haven't been able to work."

The organisation thanked APW Cars for helping get the vehicle repaired and back on the road.

Despite the setback, Billy's Bus said services had resumed today.

"We are back on the road today and there will be extra beeps, smiles and waves as we roll through the towns in our beautiful bus packed full of fluffy love," they added.
