A PLACEMENT year has helped shape the career ambitions of a future solicitor.

Malika Hussain, 18, has chosen the University of Bradford’s four-year LLB (Hons) Law degree, starting in September 2026, because of its third-year professional placement.

She believes the hands-on experience will be a crucial step towards her goal of working as a solicitor in family law.

Ms Hussain said: "I looked at law courses at a few universities, but I couldn’t find one with a placement year.

"Bradford was the only one that offered a placement year, which will help to provide me with extra experience."

She added: "All employers are wanting experience now.

"You have to look at ways in the future that you will be able to get ahead of people when you come to look for a job.

"It will be great to go to university.

"It is a fresh start for me, a chance to meet different people.

"The University of Bradford is close to home.

"I wanted to study close to my family."

Her interest in law began at the age of eight, following her parents’ separation and a subsequent legal battle over custody of her and her siblings.

She is now encouraging others to consider applying for the University of Bradford through Clearing for this September.

Ms Hussain said: "Clearing should be talked about more.

"People should be encouraged to do it.

"I would say to them to just go for it.

"There are so many courses available through Clearing.

"It is definitely not a last resort.

"It gives you a wider perspective on what you might do."

Her views are echoed by Komal Iqbal, 22, a recent LLB Law graduate from the University of Bradford.

She completed a one-year placement with the university’s Students’ Union and believes it has improved her job prospects.

Ms Iqbal said: "Placements are so important.

"It changed me as a person.

"I grew so much even during that year.

"I was helping people.

"I was always speaking to people, it was really interesting.

"Doing the placements at Bradford give you an advantage.

"If you already have experience, the law firms don’t have to invest in your education."

She added: "The academics at the law school at Bradford really support you.

"The course itself gives you a lot of work experience.

"You meet real-life clients during your studies."

Clearing for 2026 is open until October 9.
