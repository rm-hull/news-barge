Fridges and freezers are once again being accepted at a household waste recycling centre.

North Tyneside Council recently paused collections due to a nationwide issue but has now resumed services at its site on the A193 just off the A19.

North Tyneside Council said: "If you’ve been trying to get rid of a fridge or freezer lately, you’ll have seen the notice on our website saying we’d had to pause taking them in due to a national issue.

"Good news though - we’re now able to take fridges and freezers again at the Household Waste Recycling Centre, and bulky waste bookings are back open for these items too.

They thanked everyone who kept hold of their appliances, after no rise in fly-tipping was reported while things were on pause.

"Our teams have cleared the backlog, and we’ve not seen any rise in fly‑tipping, which we really appreciate."

The Household Waste Recycling Centre was also temporarily closed following a fire, and as a precaution, the site closed for a short period.

The council added that any appointments missed due to the closure will still be honoured and residents won't need a new permit.
