There were queues inside Fenwick York this afternoon – as York City FC striker Ollie Pearce met with football fans.

He was there for the grand opening of the brand new city-centre YCFC pop-up store in the ground floor of Fenwick York, Coppergate.

Ollie met, chatted, and signed autographs from 3pm to 4pm this afternoon (Thursday 27 August) with the many eager City fans.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ycfc-fenwick-ollie-pearce-4.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ycfc-fenwick-ollie-pearce-2.webp)

The new pop-up shop features a variety of YCFC apparel, such as jerseys, t-shirts, bucket hats, bags, socks, mugs, and more.

It also has the new tee and hoodie collection that’s in collaboration with the York Revolution Baseball Team based in York, Pennsylvania.

Prices for the merch ranges from £4 to £55.

The opening of the pop-up shop marks another milestone for the club.

It’s been an impactful year already for York City, who were crowned champions of the National League this year and appointed Scott Lindsey as new head coach.

Earlier this year, Ollie Pearce was presented with ‘player of the season’ at the National League end of season awards, after receiving the golden boot for his 34 league goals.
