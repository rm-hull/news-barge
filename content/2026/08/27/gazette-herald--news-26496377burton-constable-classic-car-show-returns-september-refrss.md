A classic car show is set to return this autumn following record crowds earlier this year.

Burton Constable Hall, East Yorkshire, will host the popular event on Sunday, September 6, following the success of its May Bank Holiday Classic Car Show.

The May event was the most successful to date and drew large crowds to the historic venue.

Alasdair Hutson, chief executive of the Burton Constable Foundation, said: "The response to our Classic Car Show over the May Bank Holiday was incredible.

"It was our most successful car show to date, and it was brilliant to see so many people enjoying the remarkable collection of vehicles and the atmosphere throughout the day.

"We’re delighted to be welcoming the show back this September.

"There’s something about classic cars that brings generations together, whether you’re a lifelong enthusiast or simply enjoy seeing these wonderful vehicles up close.

"We’re looking forward to another memorable day and to welcoming back familiar faces, as well as those joining us for the first time."

Organised in partnership with the East Yorkshire Thoroughbred Car Club, which has held its rally at Burton Constable since 2005, the show will feature a wide array of classic cars and motorcycles spanning several decades.

The Lotus Owners Club will also be in attendance with a special display.

The event will run from 10am to 3pm and is included with regular admission to the hall.

Visitors can also enjoy food and drink stalls, refreshments from the Stables Kitchen, and Have A Go Archery.
