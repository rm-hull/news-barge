AN historic Knaresborough pub named after one of the town’s iconic figures re-opens on Friday (August 28) after a major revamp.

The Mother Shipton Inn at Low Bridge has been closed for around a month for the renovations, which follow a change of ownership.

The pub’s new general manager Matthew Chapman has described the makeover as a ‘transformation.’

He told the Press: “We are all set to go and everything will be right on the opening day.”

  


  A magnificent fireplace with portrait of Mother Shipton. 

*(Image: Facebook)*

The 17th Century pub in a Grade II-listed building is set to open for lunch from 12-noon until 3.30pm. It will then re-open at 7pm for a launch party with live singer and free buffet.

Matthew said of the renovations: “The whole building has been painted inside and outside, new carpets have been put down, and beer equipment and the kitchen have been serviced.”

“We also made sure the original pictures still gave the feeling of an old country pub.”

  The interior of the Mother Shipton Inn. 

*(Image: Facebook)*

Outside in the beer garden is a new pagoda and seating, plus new signage.

The food offering will be improved, with better service and more locally-sourced items.

  A magnificent fireplace. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Since the revamp and takeover was announced several months ago, Matthew, who previously managed The Stansfield Arms in Apperley Bridge, near Bradford, reports an ‘outstanding’ response from locals keen to hear of the changes and to see the pub back open. Some 600 people now follow the pub on Facebook, from a totally new site.

Matthew added: “People have been looking in and asking about what has changed. We have had outstanding support from locals and we are excited about opening.”
