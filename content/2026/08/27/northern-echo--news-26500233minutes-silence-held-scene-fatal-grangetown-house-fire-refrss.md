A minute's silence has been held at the scene of a devastating house fire which killed a seven-year-old girl and her aunt.

People gathered at the scene on Birchington Avenue in Grangetown at 11am today (Thursday, August 27) to remember Valentina Foster, seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34.

Police officers lowered their caps as those at the scene stood in silence for the victims.

  A one-minute silence has been held at the scene of a devastating Grangetown house fire which killed a seven-year-old girl and her aunt. 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Two men, aged 23 and 31, have been arrested on suspicion of murder.

A 48-year-old woman remains in a critical condition at the Royal Victoria Infirmary in Newcastle, while three other people suffered injuries believed to be non-life-threatening.

Both remain in police custody while enquiries continue.

  Valentina Foster, seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, have been named locally as the two victims who died following a house fire in Grangetown. 

*(Image: Provided)*

The incident is now being jointly investigated by Cleveland Police and the fire service.

Redcar and Cleveland Local Policing Commander Supt Emily Harrison said the force was working to "establish the circumstances of exactly what has happened".

She said: "We will have a high visibility presence in the Grangetown area and encourage anyone who has concerns, or who may have information, to approach our officers."

   

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

The families of those involved have been informed and are being supported by specialist officers.

A fundraiser has also been launched by Natasha Taylor to support her friend following the deaths. She said it was 'to help her for losing her husband and daughter, a funeral and a holiday for her and her children'. It had raised more than £1,000 at the time of writing. The fire happened less than a mile from the site of the A66 crash which killed seven people, including Cleveland Police officers PC Tom Clough and PC Matthew Blades, last weekend.

The five people in the Volkswagen Passat have been named as Cole Robert Worthy, 17, Theo Rae, 17, Makai Saddington, 18, Michael Robert Cahill, 23, and Jacob Matusiak, 23.

The Passat, understood to have been using cloned plates, was travelling the wrong way along the A66 before crashing head-on into a marked police armed response vehicle at about 3.39am on Saturday, August 22.

  Scene of the crash on the A66 

*(Image: NORTH NEWS)*

Police have previously said the five men in the Passat may have had links to "serious and organised criminal activity".

Seventeen people have so far been arrested in connection with the events leading up to the crash. Two people have been charged.

The arrests relate to a range of alleged offences, including suspected organised crime activity, firearms offences, drugs offences, criminal damage, assisting an offender and threats to kill.

A fundraiser for the families of PCs Clough and Blades has now reached £1 million.

The property suffered extensive damage, including a collapsed front bay window, smashed windows and a damaged front door.

The Independent Office for Police Conduct is continuing to investigate.

Meanwhile, Cleveland Police are also investigating a separate crash into a garden wall at a home on Steele Crescent, South Bank this morning (August 27).

The damage was discovered at 12.30am, just a short distance from the scene of the fatal house fire.
