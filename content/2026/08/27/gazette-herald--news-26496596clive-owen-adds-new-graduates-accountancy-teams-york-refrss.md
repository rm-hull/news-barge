An accountancy firm has welcomed seven new graduates to its teams across the North of England.

Clive Owen announced the latest cohort of trainees will join its offices, including its York site, as part of its long-term strategy to develop future leaders through structured professional development, mentoring, and hands-on experience.

Jonathan Doyle, partner and qualified person responsible for training at Clive Owen, said: "Investing in talented people has always been fundamental to our success.

"We are delighted to welcome our latest graduates, each of whom brings enthusiasm, ambition, and fresh perspectives to the firm.

"We look forward to supporting each of our new starters as they progress through their professional qualifications and become the next generation of advisers at Clive Owen."

The firm is nationally recognised for its commitment to workplace learning and development, holding Platinum Member status with The 5 per cent Club, which promotes earn-and-learn opportunities.
