A clean-up campaign has helped remove more than 15 large bin bags of litter, plastics and other waste from the Yorkshire coast.

Food brand HECK! organised the beach clean at Saltburn-by-the-Sea on August 19, bringing together staff and members of the public.

The event is part of the company's ongoing litter-picking programme and marks its fourth organised beach clean.

Becky Keeble, of HECK!, said: "We believe small actions can make a big difference.

"Our beaches are such a vital part of the Yorkshire community, and we’re proud to play a part in keeping them safe, clean and beautiful for everyone to enjoy."

Children also took part in the clean-up, making it a family-friendly community effort.

Volunteers were treated to battered sausage and chips, provided by HECK! and cooked by The Seaview Restaurant, as thanks for their hard work.

HECK!’s broader environmental aims include cutting packaging waste, using renewable energy and supporting community projects across the region.
