A man who fled the country after taking part in the violent riots that swept through Middlesbrough in summer 2024 has been jailed.

Jamie Latham, 38, was sentenced to 33 months in prison on Wednesday (August 26) for attacking police officers during the violent disorder in Middlesbrough.

Cleveland Police said Latham led a march along Linthorpe Road before turning violent against police on the front line - kicking and punching officers.

Following the riots, Latham fled the country and is believed to have travelled through several European countries while attempting to evade justice.

However, in June 2026, investigating officers received intelligence suggesting Latham was planning his return to the UK.

When he later arrived on a flight from Alicante into Newcastle Airport, officers were waiting to arrest Latham as soon as the plane landed.

Latham was charged with violent disorder and ordered to appear before the courts.

Assistant Chief Constable Dave Sutherland said: "The level of violence faced by officers here on Teesside in the summer of 2024 was unprecedented.

"Our officers remained at the forefront of responding to this disorder, and despite such violence, put themselves on the line to protect the public.

"Assaulting police officers is completely unacceptable, and we will never tolerate assaults on our officers and staff who are simply doing their job; tackling criminals and keeping people safe.

"Jamie Latham fled the country in an attempt to escape justice, but we won't forget what happened that day to our communities, who were subjected to frightening abuse, criminal damage and violence.

"Two years later, we remain committed to seeking justice."

Police said investigations into the 2024 Middlesbrough riots continues, with officers still seeking to identify and prosecute those involved.
