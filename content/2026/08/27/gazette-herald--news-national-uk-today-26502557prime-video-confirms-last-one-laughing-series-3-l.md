Amazon Prime Video’s Last One Laughing is returning for a third series and the cast has been confirmed today (August 27).

The hilarious show sees comedians attempt to make others laugh without laughing themselves when stuck in a room together.

They’ll perform on stage and attempt to crack their fellow comedians, with yellow cards given to all participants on their first laughs and a red card for any second laughs, booting them from the game.

Comedian Jimmy Carr, who has recently been linked with a Top Gear role, returns to front the show for a third series, alongside Roisin Conaty.


## Last One Laughing series 3 lineup confirmed by Amazon Prime Video

Here are the celebrities you can expect to see in the next series of Last One Laughing:

- Harry Hill
- Mel Giedroyc
- Katherine Ryan
- Lee Mack
- Jon Richardson
- Katherine Parkinson
- Michelle de Swarte
- Richard Ayoade
- Morgana Robinson
- Josh Jones

Among the third series' lineup are returning comedians Mel Giedroyc and Richard Ayoade who will do everything they can to be crowned the Last One Laughing after missing out in previous series.

Amazon Prime Video shared the news on its official Instagram account, with fans rushing to the comments to share their excitement and predictions for which comedians they think will get further in the competition.


One viewer commented: “I think Harry Hills got this!!!”

Someone else said: “Lee mack will be brilliant”.

This person said: “Money on Richard cause Bob is not there this time😌🤣”.

Another suggested who would be out first: “Katherine Parkinson out first i bet 😂”.

One fan complimented the lineup: “Fantastic line up, can’t wait for this one”.

It is not yet known when the new series will be available to watch.

## Amazon Prime Video confirms Last One Laughing Halloween special for 2026

The news comes after the streaming platform confirmed fans of the show would get a Halloween special this year.

Announcing the news earlier this month, Prime Video said: "The rules?

"Make everyone else laugh, don’t laugh yourself, and... Whatever happens? Don’t scream."

The first three episodes will be released from October 23, while episode four will be released on October 30, the day before Halloween.
