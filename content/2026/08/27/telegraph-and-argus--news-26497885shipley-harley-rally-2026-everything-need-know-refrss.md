HUNDREDS of Harley-Davidson riders will hit the roads across the Bradford district this weekend.

The popular Shipley Harley Rally will take place over the Bank Holiday weekend, with events running from August 28 to August 31, 2026, at Baildon Rugby Club.

The rally’s main ride-out will take place on the Sunday.

## What time is the Shipley Harley Rally ride out?

A spokesperson for the Shipley Neighbourhood Policing Team said: "The ride out will commence at 1pm, with bikes assembling from 10am onwards.

  Riders set off on the Shipley Harley Rally ride-out in 2024 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

"This event is the highlight of the Shipley Harley Rally, held at Baildon Rugby Club from August 28 to August 31, 2026.

"Between 300 and 400 motorcycles are expected to join the ride out, and if the weather cooperates, it promises to be a fantastic conclusion to the Bank Holiday weekend."

The rally is believed to be the UK's largest Harley-Davidson rally.

  Riders in the Shipley Harley Rally ride-out in 2024 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## Event's history

From Harley Davidson’s famous Road Kings to Sportsers, there are always plenty of iconic motorcycles to spot.

Last year, hundreds of Harley-Davidson bikes roared in the 46th rally.

Large crowds saw the leather-donned riders revving their engines as they set off from Northgate roundabout.

From there, they rode through Baildon, Guiseley, Otley and Ilkley.

The UK-based The Wrecking Crew is a Harley-Davidson club established in 1969, known for organising events like the Shipley Harley Rally.

  Lee Boyes at the 2025 Shipley Harley Rally 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## Success last year

Lee Boyes, of The Wrecking Crew, said 2025 was one of the best turnouts they have ever had.

"I have been here every year since it started," he told the Telegraph & Argus.

"It has definitely grown over the years. You always see some new faces.”

  Shipley Harley Rally riders and spectators gather for the annual rally in Baildon 

*(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

Mike Ruff, of Bradford, talked about how the event is great for the district.

He said: "All the village gets involved which is brilliant.

"It will also help the local businesses going into the winter too.”

## And two years ago

The riders also entertained thousands of spectators at the 45th rally in 2024.

One of those was Lyndon Fieldhouse who said: "It is a really good atmosphere.

"We have been coming for the last five years. It brings everybody together.

"We go all over Europe; Belgium, France and Ireland for the meetings."

John Barrams, of Apperley Bridge, who had attended for 25 years, added: "I usually ride solo but it is always nice to see so many bikes in one place."

## Road closures in Baildon

The police also warned residents that road closures will be in place.

The Shipley NPT spokesperson added: "Please be aware that road closures will be in place for the event which has previously been well organised with dedicated stewards and traffic marshals."

Live music will also play at Baildon’s bars throughout the day and evening.
