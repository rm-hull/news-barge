A soft play venue in Middlesbrough has applied for a licence to sell alcohol and host live music, recorded music and late-night refreshment until 1am seven days a week.

Funky Monkey in Middlesbrough has applied to Middlesbrough Council for the grant/variation of a premises licence.

The application was submitted by Michelle Taggart and, if approved, would see the venue located on Albert Road serve alcohol for consumption on the premises only.

The venue has also applied for permission to provide regulated entertainment, which includes both live and recorded music, and late-night refreshment.

All these activities are proposed to take place from 11am to 1am, seven days a week.

The application was submitted under the Licensing Act 2003, which governs the sale and supply of alcohol, the provision of regulated entertainment, and the serving of late-night refreshments.

Residents and businesses in the area have the right to make representations about the application.

These must be made in writing to the licensing team at Middlesbrough Council, either by post to PO Box 505, Civic Centre, Middlesbrough, TS1 9FZ, or by email to [licensing@middlesbrough.gov.uk](mailto:licensing@middlesbrough.gov.uk) .

Representations must be received no later than September 24.
