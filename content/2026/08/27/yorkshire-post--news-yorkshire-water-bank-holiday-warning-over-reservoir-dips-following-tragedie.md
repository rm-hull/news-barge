# Yorkshire Water: Bank holiday warning over reservoir dips following tragedies

## People are being warned to not to take a dip in any Yorkshire Water reservoir over the Bank Holiday Weekend.

It follows a series of drownings in Yorkshire during this year’s exceptionally warm conditions.

Reco Puttock, 13, died after swimming in a reservoir near Halifax on May 25.

Only a few days later Palwasha Akbar, from Bradford, was playing in the River Wharfe at Burnsall in the Yorkshire Dales on May 31, the day before her 14th birthday, when she got into difficulty.

![Alastair Harvey, lead countryside and woodland advisor at Yorkshire Water, said: “Sadly, we’ve seen incidents this year in Yorkshire where people have lost their lives in reservoirs and other open water."](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjE5ZGQxN2IzLWE2MDMtNGVlYy04MzA1LTI5OTZjNjYxMTM0ZTo3YWE4ZTRlZC04NTNmLTQxNzQtODYzNy00Y2E2N2U2MWZiYzk=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The body of Abdi Zubeir, 13, was recovered on August 5 from the sea off Hornsea.

Yorkshire Water said while looking inviting, reservoirs can pose a risk to life through cold water shock, sudden drops, hidden currents and equipment under water.

Alastair Harvey, from Yorkshire Water, advised sticking to the footpath and not to be tempted to take a dip in the shallows.
