A BIKE was stolen from outside a York city centre gallery in the dead of night

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a theft which took place on Sunday, August 16, at around 12.30am outside the Lucy Pittaway gallery.

"The image attached is a similar bike but without the top frame storage," said a spokesperson for the force.

Officers are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the theft, saw suspicious activity in the area, or may have any footage of the incident.

They added that they would like to speak to anyone who has been offered a bicycle matching the description of the stolen bike or who has information that could help the investigation.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk), reporting it online, using the live chat, or calling 101.
