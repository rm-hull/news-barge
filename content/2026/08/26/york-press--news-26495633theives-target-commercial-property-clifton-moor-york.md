THIEVES have targeted a commercial property in York.

  


North Yorkshire Police said the burglary took place at 4pm on Friday, August 14 from the hallway of Acorn Commercial Finance in Clifton Moor Business Village.

A force spokesperson said in a statement on Tuesday, August 25: "A white male youth, about 12 years old, with short brown hair entered the hallway of the business, stole an ignition key of a vehicle parked outside and cycled away.

"A 40-year-old white male was seen nearby on a bicycle and left with the youth."

Police are appealing for information from anyone who heard or saw anything at the time, or CCTV footage to contact PC 1222 David Fulford at [david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting reference 12260158328.
