If you want to enjoy the last bit of summer, you might be planning a quick trip to the seaside.

Spending a day at a beach building sandcastles and paddling in the sea is an easy way to keep the whole family happy.

Easington beach is incredibly easy to reach by car from Durham for a day trip away from the city.

After enjoying the beach for a bit, it is also worth exploring the village of Easington for a few hours.


## What is Easington beach like?

Easington is a small beach on the Durham Heritage Coast and can be found between Sunderland and Hartlepool.

The beach is made up of a mixture of sand and shingle and is backed by cliffs and sand dunes.

It is often quite quiet but is popular with both fossil hunters and sea anglers.

The beach is described as being “unspoilt” and is known for “its peaceful atmosphere and natural charm”.

Along the beach, you can enjoy views of the rocky coastline and the North Sea.

For those who want to be a bit more active rather than lying on the beach all day, you can find the England Coastal Path nearby, with other walking trails in the area.


Behind the beach, you can visit the Easington Collier Local Nature Reserve to spot some wildlife.

It is also completely dog-friendly, so no member of the family will be left behind.

The area is only a 27-minute drive away from Durham via the A181 and B1283, with there being a car park fairly close to Easington beach.
