Drivers face 13 sets of road restrictions as telecoms, utility and construction works continue across the area.

Here are all the roadworks you need to know about on Wednesday, August 26:

### A68 Woodland Road

Temporary traffic signals are in operation on A68 Woodland Road in both directions between Hollyhurst Road and Bracken Road.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 15 at 11.59pm.

The restrictions are due to telecoms work. The notice has been recorded as low impact.
