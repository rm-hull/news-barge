A woodland witch trail, zombie laser tag and a pumpkin patch are among the family-friendly highlights coming to a Halloween Adventure attraction in Yorkshire.

Running throughout October at Stockeld Park, the event features immersive storytelling, interactive experiences, and spooky seasonal fun.

Central to the Halloween Adventure is the Woodland Witches experience, which transforms the Enchanted Forest into a magical realm filled with mischievous witches and hands-on challenges.

Peter Grant, owner and chief fun officer at Stockeld Park, said: "Halloween is always one of our favourite times of year.

"This year’s event combines everything families love about Stockeld with some fantastic new experiences."

Children can collect stamps as they explore the forest and visit themed locations such as the Willow of Whispering Wishes, the Menagerie of Mismatched Monsters, and the Pantry of Poisonous Potions.

Families can also enjoy a pumpkin patch filled with home-grown varieties, tractor-trailer rides, and encounters with live-action scarecrows.

Other attractions include seasonal photo spots, refreshments, and the Mummy Maze, where children help a forgetful mummy find her missing children.

Zombie Laser Tag is available as an optional extra.

For those seeking an evening experience, Pumpkin Nights will be held on October 17 and 24, featuring live folk music, roaming entertainment, and a fire-eating show.
