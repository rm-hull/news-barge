A care home in Old Malton is opening its doors for a day of traditional fun and fundraising.

Manor Park Care Home will host its ‘Fun at the Fete’ event on Saturday, September 5, from 2pm to 4pm in Town Street, with all proceeds going to the Fitzwilliam Ward at Malton Community Hospital.

Laura Clegg, home manager, said: "We’re really looking forward to opening our doors and welcoming people from Old Malton and the surrounding area for what we hope will be a lovely afternoon.

"Our residents really enjoy events which bring together families, friends and people from the wider community, and there is always such a great atmosphere when the home is full of visitors.

"We also wanted the afternoon to have a wider purpose, so we’re delighted to be raising funds for Fitzwilliam Ward at Malton Community Hospital.

"It is an important local service and one that means a great deal to many families in our area.

"We hope lots of people will come along, enjoy some traditional fun and homemade treats, meet our residents and team, and help us support a wonderful local cause at the same time."

No booking is required, and all are welcome.
