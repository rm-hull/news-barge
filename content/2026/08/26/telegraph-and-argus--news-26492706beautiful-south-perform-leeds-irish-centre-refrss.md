Fans of The Beautiful South can enjoy a night of nostalgia with a tribute show in Leeds.

The Very Beautiful South will perform at Leeds Irish Centre on Saturday, October 24, promising a night of nostalgia and classic hits.

The nine-piece band will take audiences on a journey through The Beautiful South’s back catalogue, including 90s favourites such as Perfect 10, Rotterdam, Don’t Marry Her, and A Little Time.

Andy, who performs as Paul Heaton in the tribute band, said: "We can’t wait to get back to the legendary Irish Centre.

"It’s got to be one of the country’s best-run venues of its kind, and is absolutely perfect for this type of show.

"The audience raised the roof last year."

Very Beautiful South return to Leeds for the third year running, bringing their authentic sound and energy to the city once more.

Andy added: "Unlike some of Paul Heaton’s previous gigs, we can’t afford to put any money behind the bar – but with tickets at less than £20 each, the audience can afford to buy us a drink."

The evening will also feature support from local duo Key Of G.

Tickets can be booked by calling the venue on 0113 248 0887 or online at www.WeGotTickets.com/VBS.
