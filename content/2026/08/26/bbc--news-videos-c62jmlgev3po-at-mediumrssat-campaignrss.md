# Video shows scale of flash flood hitting Nepal-Tibet border

Video shows the moment a deadly flash flood hit the Nepal-Tibet border.

At least 18 people have been killed - among the preliminary list of the missing are more than 100 people from India and 12 from the UK, according to its tourist board.

This video has been edited by the BBC with white flashes, due to the distressing content.
