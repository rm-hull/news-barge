AN insurance firm has relocated to larger offices as part of its ongoing growth and expansion.

Coversure Keighley has moved from Low Street to 54 North Street in Keighley, providing the business with a more modern space to better serve its growing customer base.

The new location offers improved facilities while maintaining the company’s focus on delivering personal, locally focused service.

A spokesperson for Coversure Keighley said: "Moving from Low Street to our new North Street premises is a significant milestone for our business.

"The expanded office provides us with the space and facilities needed to support our growing customer base while continuing to deliver the friendly, local service that our clients value.

"We are delighted to remain in the heart of Keighley and look forward to welcoming both existing and new customers through our doors."

The company hosted an open day on August 10, 2026, inviting customers, businesses, and community members to visit the new office and meet the team.

Coversure Keighley offers insurance advice and customised solutions for individuals, families, and businesses across West Yorkshire and beyond.

The office move consolidates professional services in one location, creating a convenient hub for clients seeking reliable insurance support.
