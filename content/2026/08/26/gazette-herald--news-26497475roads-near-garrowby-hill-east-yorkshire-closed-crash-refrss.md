ROADS close to an East Yorkshire landmark are currently closed after a crash.

Humberside Police has advised motorists to avoid the area between between Garrowby Hill and Fridaythorpe near Pocklington after reports of a crash around midday today (August 26).

“Road closures are in place from Garrowby Street to York Road near Fridaythorpe to allow emergency services to work safely,” said a spokesperson for the force.

They added that motorists are advised to avoid the area and find alternative routes where possible.
