A MAN has been charged with conspiracy to supply heroin and crack cocaine on a Bradford estate.

Lewis Abbott, 19, of no fixed abode, faces two counts of conspiracy to supply Class A drugs and possession of criminal property.

The alleged offences took place in the Holme Wood area of Bradford.

## What police have said

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "A man has been charged in connection with drug offending in the Holme Wood area.

"Lewis Abbott, aged 19, of no fixed abode, has been charged with two counts of conspiracy to supply Class A drugs (heroin and crack cocaine) and possession of criminal property.

"He was remanded into custody to appear at Bradford Magistrates’ Court today (August 26).

"Operation Sweetgill is a team dedicated to tackling organised crime and drugs supply within the Holme Wood estate and continues to work as part of the Holme Wood Community Partnership to make the area a safer place for all."
