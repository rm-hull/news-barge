Detectives have released an image of a man they want to speak to in connection with an alleged sexual assault on a train in North Yorkshire.

British Transport Police said a woman was reportedly sexually assaulted by a man sitting behind her on a service from Leeds to Skipton at about 8.40am on Friday, July 24.

  **Recommended reads:**

  Detectives want to speak to the man pictured, as they believe he may have information that could assist their investigation.

  

  Anyone who recognises him is asked to text BTP on 61016 or call Crimestoppers on 0800 40 50 40, quoting reference 255 of July 24.
