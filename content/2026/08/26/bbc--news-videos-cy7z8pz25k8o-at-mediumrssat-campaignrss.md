# How Dolly Parton became a fashion icon

The late Dolly Parton was known for more than just her music, acting and philanthropy. Her signature looks on and off stage made her one of entertainment's most recognisable stars.

Culture correspondent Noor Nanji takes a look at the country legend's larger-than-life style, its influences, and how Parton used it to advance her career.
