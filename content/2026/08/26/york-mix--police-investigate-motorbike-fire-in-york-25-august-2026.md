Police are investigating a motorbike fire in York.

Firefighters were called to the incident in the **Acomb area** at**7.17pm last night** (Tuesday).

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb attended following multiple reports of a fire in the open close to woodland.

“On arrival, crew confirmed this to be an abandoned motorcycle.

“Crews extinguished using two backpack sprayers. Police informed and attended. The incident was left with police.”

The fire came just hours after a deliberate fire in Acomb Wood.

Firefighters were called to that incident at 2.20pm. Again, police were called in.
