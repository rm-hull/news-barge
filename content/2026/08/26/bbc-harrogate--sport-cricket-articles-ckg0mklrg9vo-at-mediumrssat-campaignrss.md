**The Blaze became the first side to secure a top-three finish in this Women's One-Day Cup and a guarantee of a place in the knockout stage.**

The Nottingham-based side beat Warwickshire by 70 runs at Edgbaston to stretch their lead over second-placed Hampshire to five points on a day when Somerset, Surrey and holders Lancashire also triumphed.

Orla Prendergast (69), Georgia Elwiss (57) and Kathryn Bryce (45) were the mainstays as **The Blaze** piled up another sizable score of 282-7, England fast-bowler Issy Wong taking 5-58.

**Warwickshire** looked well-placed at 148-2 in the 30th over, largely thanks to skipper Katie George (89) and Brooke Halliday (52), but they slumped to 212 all out in 43.3 overs.

That was thanks to the efforts of Lucy Higham (4-41) and pace ace Charley Phillips (8-2-17-1) as The Blaze secured a 10th win in 12 games, while the fourth-placed Bears remain very much in the hunt for a top-four finish.

Kate Cross took a season's best 4-33 to keep **Lancashire** in the hunt with a four-wicket win over**Hampshire** at Southampton.

After bowling out their hosts for 223, Emma Lamb then hit 68 out of her side's 224-6 as Lancashire resumed where they left off before the break for The Hundred with a third-straight win.

Earlier, Abi Norgove hit an unbeaten 70 for Hampshire, while Freya Kemp warmed up for England's three-game series against Ireland next week with an explosive 61 from 54 balls.

Hampshire could also have guaranteed a knockout spot had they won, but they were kept waiting by a three-wicket win for **Somerset** over Durham at Taunton.

Former England Under-19 seamer Ellie Anderson took 5-40 as Durham posted 248-9, of which Emma Marlow made 71, supported by Eleanor Larosa (44) and Grace Thompson (41).

Charlie Dean (76) and Niamh Holland (70) then shared a stand of 104 as the hosts won with 32 balls to spare.

**Surrey** captain Kira Chathli's unbeaten half-century helped her side to a rather nervy two-wicket win - their sixth in 12 games - over**Yorkshire** at Ampleforth College.

Skipper Lauren Winfield-Hill made 48 as the hosts slumped from 52-0 to be bowled out for 171 in 33 overs, South African overseas all-rounder Sune Luus taking 3-33 with her leg-spin.

Surrey then slipped from 106-3 to 148-8 before Chathli kept her cool in North Yorkshire to see her side home with an innings of 66 from 75 balls.

Yorkshire, who were without Jess Jonassen with a knee injury, have now lost eight of their 12 matches and are all but out of the running for a knockout place.
