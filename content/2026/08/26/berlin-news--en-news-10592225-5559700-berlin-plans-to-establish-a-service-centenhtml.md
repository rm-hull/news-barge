Conferences, major sporting events, the Carnival of Cultures, and the Lollapalooza Festival attract visitors from Germany and abroad. In 2025, for example, around 13.7 million people came to the capital to attend approximately 87,000 events. These events create jobs and boost the retail, restaurant, and hotel industries, as the Department of Economic Affairs argues.
