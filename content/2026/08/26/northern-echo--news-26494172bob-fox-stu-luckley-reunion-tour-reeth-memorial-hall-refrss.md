A popular 70s folk duo will perform an "unmissable" show at Reeth Memorial Hall.

Bob Fox and Stu Luckley will perform on Saturday, September 5, performing songs and tunes from their first two albums.

The duo, who began playing together in the late 1970s, released their debut album Nowt So Good'll Pass in 1978, which won Melody Maker's 'Folk Album Of The Year' award.

Bob and Stu reunited for the album's 30th anniversary, performing at select venues and finishing at The Sage in Gateshead.

After performing at The Davy Lamp Folk Club's 50th Anniversary concert in 2024, they agreed to a special reunion concert at the annual Morpeth Gathering in April 2025.

Tickets for the concert sold out quickly, prompting the duo to embark on a select series of reunion concerts in 2026.

Tickets for the show are £20 in advance and £22 on the door, with under-16s at half price.

Tickets can be purchased at Reeth Post Office, by contacting Glen Steward on 01748 884989, or emailing [memorialhall@swaledale.org.](mailto:memorialhall@swaledale.org.)

Proceeds will go to the hall funds, with doors opening at 7.30pm for an 8pm start.
