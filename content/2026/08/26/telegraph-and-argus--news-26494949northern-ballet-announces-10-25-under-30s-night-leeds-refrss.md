AN exclusive ballet performance for under-30s is coming to Leeds this September.

Northern Ballet is hosting the event as part of its Three Short Ballets programme, taking place at the Stanley & Audrey Burton Theatre.

The under-30s night will be held on Tuesday, September 22, and offers discounted tickets at just £10.25, which includes a free drink and programme.

The triple bill features the world premiere of Grandes Études by Tiler Peck, the company premiere of Five Tangos by Hans van Manen, and the return of Fools by Mthuthuzeli November.

Three Short Ballets runs from September 18 to 26.

Tickets are available at northernballet.com/three-short-ballets.
