## **The importance of a feed audit** 

Feed management is a core piece of online business success, as it will influence whether your products show up on Google, how accurate information is when it populates ads or Google Shopping, and more.

Despite this, many companies have a number of both simple and complex issues with their product data that prevents them from showing up across Google and affiliated networks. The end result? A hit to conversions, sales and the customer experience.

A feed audit can help you identify issues that impact overall feed performance, from minor mistakes such as extra spaces in titles to major optimisation problems. Even better: an audit takes little time, meaning you can do it well in advance of the holidays.
