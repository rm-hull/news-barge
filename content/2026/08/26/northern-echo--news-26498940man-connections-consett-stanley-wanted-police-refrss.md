A man with connections to two County Durham towns is wanted by police.

Durham Police are appealing for information to help trace Alan Elliott, who is wanted on recall to prison.

The 38-year-old has links to Consett and Stanley, police say.

A Consett Police spokesperson said in a post on Facebook: "Have you seen Alan Elliott?

"The 38-year-old is wanted on recall to prison.

"If you have seen him or know where he is, please don’t comment here.

"Instead, call us on 101, or contact us online."

Anyone who has seen him or knows where he is should call the police on 101 or report information online at durham.police.uk.
