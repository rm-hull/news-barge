This boarded-up home is filled with broken furniture, heaps of mail, and needs some serious DIY, but it is one of the best house bargains you will find.

The three-bedroom property is one of the cheapest North East homes currently on the market.

Located on North Road West in Wingate, County Durham, the mid-terrace property is being auctioned by Auction House, with bids starting from just £10,000.

The house needs a little work, with photos showing piles of mail, boarded-up windows, a grimy bath, broken furniture, and signs of general disrepair throughout.

   

*(Image: Auction House)*

Despite its current condition, the property offers “versatile accommodation” and is seen as a promising investment opportunity for those willing to take on a renovation project - you just need to have the eye for it.

Inside the 'doer upper', there is a spacious lounge, a kitchen and dining area, a family bathroom, and three bedrooms.

   

*(Image: Auction House)*

Despite the broken furniture and a few dead garden weeds, the kitchen has enough space to return to life as a family kitchen, leading onto a separate dining space big enough for a family table.

Although pictures show it has gathered some substantial dust, the family bathroom has a spacious tub.

   

*(Image: Auction House)*

The bedrooms are currently in a state of disrepair, with collapsed ceiling sections and significant clutter.

A courtyard at the rear of the property also offers potential for outdoor improvements, despite some overgrown weeds

The property heads to auction on September 14.
