A person was rescued following a house fire.

Emergency services were called to a property in Northowram, near Halifax, just before 10.30am on Wednesday morning.

Four fire crews and an aerial ladder platform were deployed.

### What West Yorkshire Fire Service said

On Wednesday, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire Service said: "We were called at 10.26am this morning to reports of a house fire in Northowram.

"Four crews and an aerial ladder platform have been in attendance.

"We rescued one person from the property and passed them to the care of Yorkshire Ambulance Service.

"Firefighters used two large jets, two hose reel jets, four breathing apparatus and the aerial to extinguish the fire.

"No cause recorded as yet."
