Police are appealing for information after a woman was allegedly bitten in the face by a dog in North Yorkshire.

The incident happened on August 24 at about 11.40am on Wykeham Street in Scarborough.

The victim was walking when a dog, without an owner, jumped on her and bit her face, said a spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police.

The animal then reportedly ran off towards Manor Road.

The woman needed numerous stitches for her injuries, which are expected to leave permanent scars.

The victim described the dog is described as a husky or malamute type, possibly a puppy, with a black head, mostly brown body, and wearing a black collar.

North Yorkshire Police are appealing for information, particularly if anyone has seen this dog without an owner before or knows who the owner is.

They are also asking for any witnesses or anyone with CCTV footage of the incident to come forward.

Anyone with information is urged to contact North Yorkshire Police online by emailing [josh.nolan@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:josh.nolan@northyorkshire.police.uk), visiting the website and selecting ‘Tell us about’, then ‘An existing case or report’, or via live chat, available Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

Alternatively, information can be given by calling North Yorkshire Police on 101 or anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

When providing information, please quote reference 12260165486.
