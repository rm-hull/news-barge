A North East doctor is set to face a medical tribunal over allegations of “dishonesty” and making "untrue" medical records.

Dr Yakuba Benjamin - whose area of practice is Stockton - is accused of carrying out consultations with three patients, during which untrue notes were made in the patient’s records.

It is further alleged that Dr Benjamin’s conduct was dishonest.
