A UK-based jewellery company and its four online brands have fallen into administration after nearly 20 years in business.

Roxoa, founded in 2007 and based in the UK, operates four online jewellery brands:

- QP Jewellers
- Gold Boutique
- Ruby & Oscar
- Bail & Stone

The online retailers specialise in gold, platinum, sterling silver, gemstones, and diamonds.


Each brand has built a strong reputation, with customers praising both the quality of the jewellery and the delivery experience.

## UK jewellery company enters administration with 4 online brands at risk of shutting down

Despite positive customer feedback, Roxoa and its four online jewellery brands have fallen into administration after nearly 20 years in business.

Iain Nairn and Sean Williams from Leonard Curtis were appointed joint administrators on August 20, according to The Gazette.


All five websites - Roxoa, QP Jewellers, Gold Boutique, Ruby & Oscar, and Bail & Stone - are still operational (at the time of writing).

Roxoa has been contacted for comment.
