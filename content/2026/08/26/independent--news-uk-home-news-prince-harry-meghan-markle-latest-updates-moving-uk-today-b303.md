The Duke and Duchess of Sussex have reportedly landed in the UK after it was announced they were moving their family from California back to Britain.

Prince Harry, Meghan Markle and their two children landed at Birmingham airport on Wednesday lunchtime after flying into the country privately from California, *The Telegraph* has reported. 

The BBC said the couple opted for a private over a commercial flight.

It comes a week after it was announced Harry and Meghan would return to the UK, where they are to live in a non-royal residence outside of London, having already enrolled Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet in British schools. The new term is expected to start next week.

The Sussexes are said to be settling in the UK for an “extended period” of time, and it’s believed the family will live in a private property in the Cotswolds area.

*The Independent* has contacted a representative of the Sussexes for comment.

Harry and Meghan, who are non-working royals, quit the monarchy six years ago, moving to the US, from where they criticised both members of the royal family and the institution.

## Return comes six years after controversial move to US
