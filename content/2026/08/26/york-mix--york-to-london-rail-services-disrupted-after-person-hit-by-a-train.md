There’s major disruption on the East Coast Main Line this afternoon after a person was hit by a train near Stevenage.

National Rail said: “A person has been hit by a train between between Potters Bar and Welwyn Garden City.

“Whilst the emergency services carry out their work, all lines between these stations are closed.

“As a result, trains between London and Stevenage will be cancelled, delayed by up to 60 minutes or revised.”

Major disruption is expected until 2.30pm.

## Routes affected

Grand Central between London Kings Cross and Bradford Interchange / Sunderland

Great Northern between London Kings Cross and Letchworth Garden City / Royston / Cambridge / Ely / Peterborough / Kings Lynn, and also between Moorgate and Welwyn Garden City

Hull Trains between London Kings Cross and Beverley / Hull

LNER between London Kings Cross and Lincoln / Hull / Leeds / Bradford Forster Square / Skipton / Harrogate / York / Middlesborough/ Newcastle / Edinburgh / Aberdeen
