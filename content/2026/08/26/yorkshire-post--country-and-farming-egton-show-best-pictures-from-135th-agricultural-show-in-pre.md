The 135th Egton Show took place on Wednesday (Aug 26) in the pretty village in the North York Moors National Park.

As ever, there was a wide range of animal attractions for all the family, from rabbits and cows to sheep and horses, as well as trade stalls, a fairground and plenty of refreshments.

The Egton Show is run by a dedicated team of voluntary committee members.

We sent photographer James Hardisty along to capture the best of the action.
