The Feversham Cricket League Top Four Play-off final between Gillamoor and High Farndale will take place on Duncombe Park (Helmsley) C.C's ground on Sunday, August 30, starting at 1.30pm.

There will be 24 overs a side, and the maximum number of overs per bowler is 4.

As well as the 53-year-old trophy, the Feversham Village Knock-out Cup, to be presented to the winning team captain, there will also be a player of the match award.

From 1981 onwards, Gillamoor have won it eight times, and High Farndale have won it 4 times.

Countess of Feversham Cup this season: Highest score 62 Callum Buckley (Westerdale).

Best Bowling 5 for 8 Mike Thompson (Westerdale).

Supplementary Cup this season: Highest score 36 not out (retired and able to return as per the rules) Charlie Parker (Glaisdale).

Best Bowling 4 for 11, Liam Calvert (Glaisdale).
