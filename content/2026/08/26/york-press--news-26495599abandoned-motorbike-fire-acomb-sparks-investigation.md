POLICE are investigating a fire which sparked close to woodland in a York suburb.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to an abandoned motorcycle which was on fire in Acomb around 7.15pm yesterday (Tuesday, August 25).

  


A service spokesperson said multiple calls had been received about the incident.

The spokesperson added: "Crews extinguished using two backpack sprayers.

"The police were informed and attended.

"The incident was left with the police."
