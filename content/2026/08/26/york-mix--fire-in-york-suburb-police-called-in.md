Fire crews were called to an incident in a York suburb last night.

Acomb firefighters responded to a fire in the open in the **Boroughbridge Road area** at**6.40pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The fire was out on arrival of fire crews.

#### Boroughbridge Road, York

“Crew confirmed a small fire had occurred in scrubland, affecting an area of approximately one square metre. Police informed.”
