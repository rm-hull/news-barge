Drivers are being warned of delays in part of York as temporary traffic lights are installed.

Electricity works are on the A19 Shipton Road in both directions at Galtres Grove.

The lights will be in place until 11pm on August 31.

  **Recommended reads:**

Temporary traffic signals are also in place on Thanet Road both ways between St James Place and Foxwood Lane due to electricity works.

Delays are expected during the works which are due to end at 5pm on Friday, August 28.
