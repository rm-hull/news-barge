A stunning Grade II listed townhouse in the heart of Teesside is proving that period properties don't have to choose between character and luxury.

Known as Deneholme, the four-bedroom mid-terrace home on Norton High Street has been transformed in recent years.

  Deneholme - a 4-bed terraced house in the heart of Norton high street. 

*(Image: Michael Pool)*

Bold botanical wallpapers, carefully chosen furnishings and premium finishes are among the standout features, giving the home the feel of a luxury townhouse straight from an interiors magazine.

Blending original period features with a bold, contemporary interior, the home offers a rare combination that is likely to generate plenty of interest from prospective buyers.

  The interior of the house boasts modern upgrades with luxury décor. 

*(Image: Michael Pool)*

  The living space balances luxury style with a cosy environment. 

*(Image: Michael Pool)*

The current owners have embraced colour and texture throughout, using bespoke feature walls and carefully selected décor to give each room its own identity while maintaining a sense of luxury from floor to ceiling.

Cast iron-style radiators, original fireplaces, a large staircase window and engineered oak flooring complement contemporary additions, striking a balance between old and new.

  Fireplaces throughout the house help add to the balance between traditional and modern. 

*(Image: Michael Pool)*

  The bedrooms have been thoughtfully decorated with botanical décor. 

*(Image: Michael Pool)*

At the heart of the property is an open-plan kitchen and dining area designed for both family life and entertaining.

Finished with quartz worktops and a range of integrated NEFF appliances, including an oven, induction hob, microwave and dishwasher, the kitchen is ready for its next owners to move straight in and make themselves at home.

  The kitchen keeps a traditional, country theme, while being fitted with state-of-the-art technology. 

*(Image: Michael Pool)*

A central island, separate wine fridge and built-in ceiling speakers create the perfect setting for entertaining guests, while a log burner provides a cosy focal point for quieter evenings spent with family during the winter months.

The ground floor also features a walk-in pantry, utility room and WC, with similarly thoughtful design touches throughout.

  The townhouse benefits from a utility room - that has also been carefully decorated with a botanical theme. 

*(Image: Michael Pool)*

On the first floor, two of the bedrooms feature original fireplaces and fitted wardrobes.

Plus, a luxurious family bathroom fitted with Lusso products includes a freestanding bath, walk-in shower and contemporary vanity unit.

  The bedrooms have been thoughtfully decorated with botanical décor. 

*(Image: Michael Pool)*

Perfect for those working from home, the dedicated Harry Potter-style office under the stairs leads up to the top floor.

Upstairs, there is a spacious bedroom is complemented by Velux windows, built-in ceiling speakers, an en-suite and a separate dressing room.

  A dedicated office makes the house a perfect space for those working from home. 

*(Image: Michael Pool)*

  The top floor features a bedroom with it's own en-suite and dressing area.

  The botanicals extend to the courtyard of the house too, with a beautifully maintained, quiet and enclosed garden area.

Outside, the enclosed rear garden offers a private retreat away from the bustle of the High Street, with a lawn, patio area and fitted lighting.

Blending historic architecture with boutique hotel-inspired interiors, Deneholme offers a rare opportunity to own a luxury townhouse in one of Stockton's most sought-after locations.
