## 'Renovator's dream' six-bed Victoria townhouse to go under the hammer

The outside of the property *(Image: Provided)*

A GRADE II listed York townhouse described as a 'renovator's dream home' is set to go to auction next week.

The Victorian era property, which is located at 56 Monkgate, is spread across five floors and has six bedrooms, three bathrooms, and a cellar.

The ground floor of the 1840 home features a front-facing living room with high ceilings, a fireplace, and a large window that allows plenty of natural light to fill the room, and a kitchen - located at the rear and described as an "excellent size" for catering to a large family.

The lower ground floor, which is accessible via stairs at the end of the hallway, was previously used as a bedroom and has its own bathroom.

The cellar also has a window that provides a good amount of light.

Floors one and two both have a two bedrooms and a bathroom, and the top level of the townhouse has the final sixth bedroom, along with a large space that could be used for an office or children’s playroom.

  The kitchen of the property 

*(Image: Provided)*

There is a private courtyard outside the property, which is currently paved but could be transformed into a garden space or used for additional storage.

The property is located just outside the historic city walls of York, providing easy access to many amenities and attractions, including York Minster, the Shambles, and the Jorvik Viking Centre.

Auction manager Romilly Woodman-Wilcock said: "Positioned just outside York’s historic city walls, this Grade II listed townhouse offers spacious accommodation arranged over several floors.

  One of the rooms in the property 

*(Image: Provided)*

"The property needs improvement throughout, giving a buyer the opportunity to update and personalise it.

"Subject to the necessary permissions, there may also be potential to explore alternative uses, such as student accommodation or a holiday let."

The University of York is approximately a 30-minute walk or a 10-minute drive away, with developers saying the house would be 'ideal for conversion into student accommodation' - with the right permissions.

  The outside of the property 

*(Image: Provided)*

The guide price for 56 Monkgate is £400,000, plus fees.

The online auction will be run by Auction House Hull & East Yorkshire, opening on Tuesday, September 1 at 1pm and closing on September 2 at 11am on [https://online.auctionhouse.co.uk/lot/details/8903a8d1-b3ca-4d06-82ae-84a73f1b6318](https://online.auctionhouse.co.uk/lot/details/8903a8d1-b3ca-4d06-82ae-84a73f1b6318)
