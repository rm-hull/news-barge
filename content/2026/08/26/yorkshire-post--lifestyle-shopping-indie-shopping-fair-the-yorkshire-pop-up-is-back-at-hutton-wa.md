# Indie shopping fair: The Yorkshire Pop-Up is back at Hutton Wandesley Estate near York

## The Yorkshire Pop-Up is back at the Stables and Walled Garden at Hutton Wandesley Estate in the Vale of York.

Twice a year, in April and September, this lovely converted Victorian stable block and garden become a lifestyle shopping fair for independent brands from across the UK showcasing fashion and accessories, home and garden, jewellery, beauty, textiles, vintage finds and reclamation pieces.

Independent brands taking part include Sasha’s own company Sash in the Garden (she has a wonderful Instagram account @sashinthegarden to help people create and get the most out of their garden, no matter the size).

Next month’s Yorkshire Pop Up opens with a charity evening fashion show on Wednesday, September 23 (tickets £25). With shopping 3-8pm.

![The lovely Hutton Wandesley Estate which hosts the Yorkshire Pop Up shopping event over two days.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmI4MzE4OWNlLTZmZmUtNGFiYS04M2I4LTFiYzljMjExMmQxZDphYzM0ODdkNS0xNWFkLTQ3NjYtOWNkNy04OGM2MDA0Y2M5NDk=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The following day, Thursday, September 24, the Yorkshire Pop Up is open 9am-4pm (entry £10) and there will be a cafe serving breakfast, lunch and cakes, with the Walled Garden open for all visitors to enjoy.

“Hopefully, the weather will be fine, and the event will offer visitors the perfect opportunity to see part of this beautiful estate for themselves, while raising money for a wonderful charity,” says Sasha.

The event is in aid of Martin House, and is sponsored by Raymond James.

Hutton Wandesley Stables has other events too, including many different workshops throughout the year, with upcoming ones for gardening, willow weaving, oil painting, calligraphy, social media and more.
