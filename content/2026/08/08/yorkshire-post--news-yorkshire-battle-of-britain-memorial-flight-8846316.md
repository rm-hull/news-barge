# Where and when Battle of Britain Memorial Flight aircraft to fly over Yorkshire this weekend

## An aircraft from the Battle of Britain Memorial flight will fly over Yorkshire this weekend.

An aircraft from the Battle of Britain Memorial Flight (BBMF) is scheduled to fly over parts of Yorkshire this weekend.

The Flight operates five Spitfires, two Hurricanes, a Lancaster, a C47 Dakota, and two Chipmunk aircraft (primarily for training).

On Saturday 8 August, the aircraft, the type of which depends on availability, is scheduled to fly over Doncaster between 1.40pm and 2.10pm.

![A Spitfire Mk Vb from the Battle of Britain Memorial Flight flies over Biggin Hill Airfield on 20 August 2010 in London, England.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/08/0/30/GettyImages-103501046.jpg?trim=0,114,0,114&width=640&quality=65)



On Sunday 9 August, the aircraft is expected to fly over Doncaster between 12pm and 12.30pm, Elvington Airfield between 12.05pm and 12.35pm, Hessle between 2.45pm and 3.15pm and Withernsea between 3.00pm and 3.30pm.

The Battle of Britain Memorial Flight (BBMF) operates from RAF Coningsby in Lincolnshire. The Royal Air Force write: “The mission of the RAF BBMF is to maintain the priceless artefacts of our national heritage in airworthy condition in order to commemorate those who have fallen in the service of this country. The RAF BBMF also serves to promote the modern day Air Force and to inspire the future generations.”

The Battle of Britain Memorial Flight will display at a number airshows and events this summer. In addition to the full displays, the BBMF also conducts up to 900 flypasts each year to make the most of the flying hours and transits to and from display locations.

The display season runs from May to September each year, with pre-season work-up flying for the crews taking place during April, culminating in Public Display Approval being sought for each pilot from the Air Officer Commanding No 1 Group.
