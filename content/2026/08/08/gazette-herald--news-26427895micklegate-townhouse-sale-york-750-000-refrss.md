A “stylish” townhouse has hit the market in York city centre for £750,000.

The four-bedroom home, dating back to the 17th century, is for sale in Micklegate.

It offers accommodation over four floors while combining “character with the convenience of city-centre living”, according to Hudson Moody which is marketing the property.

  From above: the four-bedroom townhouse for sale in Micklegate, York, for £750,000 

*(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

The estate agent said the home has been refurbished but retains its “charm and original period features”.

Inside, a grand tiled entrance hall leads to the sweeping staircase and offers access to the lower ground floor, which could be used as a “fantastic snug or cinema room”, Hudson Moody said.

On the first floor is a living room and the kitchen dining room with granite worktops and brass fittings.

A master bedroom, with an en-suite shower room, and the main bathroom are also on the first floor.

Three more bedrooms are on the second floor, along with a shower room.
