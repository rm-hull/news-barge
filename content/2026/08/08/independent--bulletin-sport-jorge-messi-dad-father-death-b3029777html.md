# ‘Deep sorrow’ as Lionel Messi’s father Jorge dies

- Jorge Messi, the father of Lionel Messi, has died at the age of 68 in Rosario, Argentina, following a long illness.
- He passed away at a medical clinic, having spent his final months alternating between the clinic and his home.
- Jorge Messi was a crucial pillar of support for his son's career, acting as his representative for several years from his early days at Barcelona.
- Lionel Messi had previously spoken about needing his father's approval after every match.
- Newell’s Old Boys, Lionel Messi’s first side, posted a tribute to Jorge, saying that they felt “deep sorrow and grief” at his death.
