“Budding golfers” have hit golf balls through several windows of a home in North Yorkshire, police said.

Officers are appealing for information about the incident in Finkle Street, Selby.

North Yorkshire Police said “some ‘budding golfers’ shot golf balls” through the home’s windows between 6.30pm on Tuesday, July 28 and Wednesday, July 29.

The force urges anyone with information about the incident to email [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Crimestoppers can be contact anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260145225 when passing on information.
