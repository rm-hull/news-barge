# Clacton locals feel disrespected in ‘Farage-vs-Binface’ race

- The Clacton by-election, triggered by Reform UK leader Nigel Farage's resignation in July, features a record 34 candidates.
- However, many local residents feel their concerns are not being taken seriously, with the election often framed as a contest between Mr Farage and satirical candidate Count Binface.
- Neither of the major political parties are fielding candidates, leading to a sense of disillusionment among some voters who feel they lack genuine choice.
- Residents highlight local issues such as deprivation in areas like Jaywick and homelessness, expressing a desire for an MP who will address these community needs.
- While some residents remain supportive of Mr Farage, others are sceptical of his ability to deliver on promises, with some regular voters considering abstaining due to a lack of resonating candidates.
