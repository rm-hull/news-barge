Amanda Knox defends comedy show after criticism from Meredith Kercher’s family

Amanda Knox's controversial comedy show, Cartwheel, premiered at the Edinburgh Fringe Festival on Friday night despite calls for its cancellation.

Knox was falsely imprisoned for the 2007 murder of Meredith Kercher in Italy and was later cleared by the country’s Supreme Court in 2015.

Kercher's sister, Stephanie, had urged the show's removal, claiming it “normalises and trivialises violence against women”, and a petition was signed by over 6,000 people.

Audience members on opening night described Cartwheel as “respectful”, “powerful”, and “genuinely funny”, noting its focus on storytelling rather than traditional stand-up comedy.

Knox said that the show is about raising a daughter and honouring Kercher's memory.
