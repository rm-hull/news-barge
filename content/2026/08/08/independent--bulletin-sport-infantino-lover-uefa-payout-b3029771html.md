# Infantino denies secret six-figure payment to alleged lover

![Gianni Infantino was Uefa general secretary before taking the Fifa presidency](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/08/09/bfdd8d97db2686181b9ad34fc16b995dY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg2MjYxODQw-2.76934849.jpg)

- Gianni Infantino has denied claims that Uefa paid a six-figure sum to an alleged lover during his tenure at European football's governing body.
- An investigation by *The Telegraph* reported that Uefa made the payment to a woman who allegedly had a relationship with Infantino, who is married with four children, while he was Uefa’s general secretary between 2009 and 2016.
- Uefa said that a “departure payment” was made to the woman, saying that it was in line with existing regulations at the time, which have since been tightened.
- A Fifa spokesperson disputed the allegations, telling *The Telegraph* that they are “categorically untrue” and “defamatory”, and denying any inappropriate conduct by Infantino.
- These claims add to the pressure on Infantino, who recently faced controversy over a plan to sell stakes in the World Cup and is up for re-election as Fifa president in March 2027.
