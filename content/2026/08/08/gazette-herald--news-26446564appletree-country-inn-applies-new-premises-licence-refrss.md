Search the Public Notice PortalA country pub in Sinnington could get a new premises licence under new plans.

Monty’s Pubs Limited has applied for a new premises licence at The Appletree Country Inn, Marton.

If granted, the licence would allow alcohol to be served from 11am until 11pm from Sunday to Wednesday, and until midnight from Thursday to Saturday.

It would also allow customers to take alcohol off the premises during these times.

Additionally, the pub would be permitted to play background recorded music and provide late-night refreshments between 11pm and midnight on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays.

The application is open for public comments until August 25.

Anyone wishing to make a representation must submit it in writing to North Yorkshire Council, Licensing Services, Town Hall, St Nicholas Street, Scarborough, YO11 2HG.

Representations can also be sent by email to [licensing.east@northyorks.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing.east@northyorks.gov.uk.)

It is a criminal offence to knowingly or recklessly make a false statement in connection with an application.

Those found guilty of doing so may be fined up to £5,000.

The full application is available for public inspection at North Yorkshire Council’s licensing office during normal working hours.
