A man in possession of a knife who allegedly made threats to harm himself and others in a North Yorkshire town has been detained in hospital, police said.

The man was arrested after police were called to an address in the Langthorpe area of Boroughbridge on Monday, August 3.

North Yorkshire Police said the man was arrested “in connection with a number of offences” and taken into custody.

He was assessed by a mental health professional and detained under the Mental Health Act then transferred to hospital to receive further treatment and support, the force said.

A police spokesperson said its investigation into the incident remains ongoing but “there is no wider threat to the public, and officers would like to thank local residents for their patience and understanding during the police activity in the area”.

“The man was in possession of a knife and making threats to harm himself and others,” they said. “However, he was in the property on his own at the time of police attendance.

“Thank you to residents who gave their cooperation during this incident so we could safely resolve it."
