Arsenal have moved on from their chase of Vinicius Jr after he signed a contract extension with Real Madrid and the Gunners have now reignited their interest in Atletico Madrid’s Julian Alvarez while PSG’s Bradley Barcola is also on their list.

But Bruno Guimaraes has finally officially completed his £75m move from Newcastle United with the deal for the 28-year-old midfielder representing a key piece of business of Mikel Arteta ahead of Arsenal’s title defence.

Rodri looks set to leave Manchester City amid interest from Barcelona and Real Madrid. The World Cup winning captain is into the final year of his contract and favours a move to Barca – though an initial offer of £38m has been rejected.

Liverpool, meanwhile, also want to sign Barcola from Paris Saint-Germain and have lined up his teammate Ibrahim Mbaye in what could be a double swoop for Andoni Iraola’s side. The European champions may be willing to let them go, especially following Maghnes Akliouche signing from Monaco, while the Reds also look to have secured much-needed defender help with ex-Barcelona captain Ronald Araujo set for a loan deal, with an option to buy, at Anfield.

Elsewhere, Manchester United have added to their ranks by bringing in a teenage midfielder and they are exploring their options at left-back with Lewis Hall reportedly now set on a move to Old Trafford, but Newcastle are determined to stop the exodus and insist he is not for sale.

*Follow all of the latest news, rumours and done deals with our live blog below:*

## Guimaraes 'hopeful we can achieve our dreams' at Arsenal

In his first official interview since joining Arsenal, Bruno Guimaraes has said he is hopeful “we can achieve our dreams” at the Gunners.

Speaking to Arsenal’s club media channels following his £75m move from Newcastle, Guimaraes said: “I feel good, I feel amazing. I’m glad for the opportunity.

“Since the first time I spoke to (Andrea) Berta and Mikel (Arteta) I was excited. I just felt inside of me that I need a new challenge in my life and I think to be an Arsenal player is going to be an exciting challenge in my life.

“I think after playing two World Cups, I’m at the point of my life, I think I need a challenge like this.

“I want to win trophies, I want to make history and I think I’m in the right place to do it. I’m so excited to start.

“We have tough games to play this season so the squad is going to be very, very important and I’m hopeful we can achieve our dreams.”

## DONE DEAL! Bruno Guimaraes joins Arsenal for £75m

It’s finally official. Bruno Guimaraes has joined Arsenal in a £75m deal from Newcastle.

The 28-year-old midfielder joins on a four-year deal and the contract includes a further one-year option.

Guimaraes arrived at Newcastle in January 2022 and leaves the club after making 195 appearances, scoring 31 goals along the way.

## Van de Ven nears Spurs extension

Micky Van de Ven is close to agreeing a new long-term contract with Tottenham Hotspur, according to *Sky Sports.*

Van de Ven’s current contract runs until 2029 and is entering his fourth season at the club.

## DONE DEAL! Another goalkeeper leaves Man United

Bayindir isn’t the only goalkeeper leaving Old Trafford, with Radek Vitek having completed his switch to Championship side Middlesbrough.

United have managed to secure a pretty hefty fee of up to £14m from a second-tier side and have also inserted a buy-back clause into the deal, along with matching rights and a 35 per cent sell-on clause to profit from any potential future sale.

The 22-year-old impressed on loan at Bristol City in the Championship last season.

## Manchester United continue squad overhaul by offloading goalkeeper

Manchester United have agreed to loan goalkeeper Altay Bayindir to Celta Vigo for the season.

The Spanish club have an option to buy the Turkey international, reportedly for a fee of £3.5m, which would give United a high sell-on clause on top of that.

## Newcastle sporting director admits Bruno Guimaraes wanted to leave

Ross Wilson, Newcastle’s sporting director, has revealed that Bruno Guimaraes expressed his desire to leave the club – providing little choice as they granted him a switch to Arsenal.

"What we had to weigh up was, very respectfully from Bruno with his behaviour towards us, very emotionally as well, he effectively told us he wanted to leave and move on," he said.

"That doesn't mean he can go, but it certainly plays a part in what our thinking is when we hit a certain range of number.

"There's no part in saying it was part of our strategy and was in our thinking this summer - it wasn't.

"But we have got to be flexible and react to things that happen."

## Manchester City reject first Barcelona bid for Rodri

Barcelona have had a bid for Rodri rejected by Manchester City.

The Spanish champions offered €45m (£38.7m) for Spain’s World Cup-winning captain, which City do not deem a serious price for the Ballon d’Or winner.

No meaningful talks have taken place between the clubs yet with City reluctant to enter negotiations until there is the prospect of a deal near their valuation of the midfielder.

Real Madrid have also shown interest in the midfielder.

## Arsenal hold nerve after missing out on Vinicius Jr with three alternative targets

Arsenal intend to hold their nerve and assess alternative options after missing out on Vinicius Jr with the Real Madrid superstar signing a contract extension at the Bernabeu on Thursday.

Mikel Arteta is still eager to bring in a new left winger, though, with three notable options still on the table.

Arsenal made a serious bid to sign Vinicius Jr, with an agreement close on personal terms, but the 26-year-old ultimately opted to commit his future to Madrid in a deal until 2032.

## Liverpool on the brink of loan deal for Ronald Araujo

Liverpool are close to agreeing a loan deal for Barcelona’s Ronald Araujo to give them much-needed reinforcements in defence.

The Merseyside club are looking to complete a loan with an option to buy the Uruguay international.

The 27-year-old former Barcelona captain is set to become Andoni Iraola’s second signing at Anfield, after winger Victor Munoz.

Araujo, primarily a central defender but who can also play right-back, will help give Iraola more strength in depth in both positions.
