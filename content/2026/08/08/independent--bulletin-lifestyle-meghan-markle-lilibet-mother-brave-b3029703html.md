Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, said that being a mother is her “superpower” and that her daughter, Princess Lilibet, inspires her to be “brave”.

The remarks were made during an interview with children for Instyle, coinciding with the upcoming release of the documentary Cookie Queens.

The film, produced by Meghan and Prince Harry’s company Archewell Productions, is a coming-of-age tale about Girl Scouts navigating the cookie-selling business.

The documentary, which premiered at the 2026 Sundance Film Festival, was described by Meghan as a “powerful and meaningful depiction” of an American tradition.

On being a mother, Meghan said: “I feel like we have superpowers because we juggle it all and enjoy it all with grace and grit at the same time.”
