Two cars have crashed on a main road in York.

Police, firefighters and paramedics were called to the scene in Holgate Road at 7.45pm on Friday (August 7).

Everyone was out of the vehicles when the fire crews arrived, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

The fire service said “casualties were left in the care of ambulance crews”.

Firefighters made the scene safe by isolation the vehicles' batteries.

The fire service said the incident was “left in the hands of police”.

Images of the scene showed the fronts of both cars, a navy Uber and a silver Vauxhall, badly damaged after the collision outside the Crystal Palace pub.

The stretch of Holgate Road outside the pub was cordoned off after the crash.
