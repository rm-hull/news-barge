Do you recognise these two?

Police want to talk to them about a theft from a York store.

It happened on Friday, 24 July, at The Hat Shop and involved the theft of a designer cap.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’ve issued this CCTV of two persons that we would like to speak to, following a shop theft in York city centre.

“Please contact us if you recognise the persons pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.”
