A power cut has hit dozens of homes in a North Yorkshire power.

Forty-two properties in Sutton-on-the-Forest are without power due to the unplanned outage on Saturday (August 8), Northern Powergrid said.

The company said its team has identified what caused the outage and expects power to be back on for most of its customers by 5.30pm to 6.30pm.

Louise Lowes, Northern Powergrid’s director of customer service, said its teams will work “as quickly as possible to get your power back on”.

“We know that power cuts can be really difficult,” she said, adding: “We’ll do our best to support you and get your power back on as soon as we can.

“In the meantime, if you need help, tell us – we are here to listen and support our customers whenever they need us.”

She urged anyone who needs further support to phone Northern Powergrid on 105.
