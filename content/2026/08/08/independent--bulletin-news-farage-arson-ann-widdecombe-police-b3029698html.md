Police investigating Ann Widdecombe death reopen attempted burglary probe

Counter-terror police probing the alleged murder of former Tory MP Ann Widdecombe have reopened an investigation into a “firebomb” attack at Reform UK leader Nigel Farage's home.

A “lit incendiary device” was reportedly posted through Mr Farage's mailbox in April 2025, which he described as an 'outright arson attempt' that burned itself out.

The initial investigation by the Metropolitan Police and Counter Terrorism Policing (CTP) London into an “attempted burglary” was closed without arrests.

CTP London has since identified a line of inquiry that was not pursued at the time, leading the Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) to investigate a staff member for alleged failure.

Ms Widdecombe, a Reform UK spokesperson, died on 8 July, and Joshua Kerry, 28, has been charged with her murder.
