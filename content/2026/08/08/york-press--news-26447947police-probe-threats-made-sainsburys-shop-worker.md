Police are investigating threats being allegedly made towards a North Yorkshire supermarket worker.

The incident reportedly happened at the Sainsbury’s in Ramshill Road, Scarborough, at about 10.20pm on Thursday, July 30, North Yorkshire Police said.

A force spokesperson said officers want to identify a member of the public “who is believed to have intervened during an incident after a suspect allegedly made threats towards members of staff at the location”.

“The individual may have important information that could assist our enquiries, and we would like to speak with them about what they witnessed,” they said.

The police spokesperson said it is “also believed they [the witness] might be a victim of a crime”.

They urged anyone with information to email [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260146831 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
