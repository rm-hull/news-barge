Police have issued a photograph of a man officers want to speak to after a bike was stolen in North Yorkshire.

The bike was taken from Market Place in Harrogate on Monday, July 20, at 3.20pm, North Yorkshire Police said.

Officers are urging anyone who recognises the man pictured to contact the force.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please make contact, quoting case reference 12260138531,” a police spokesperson said.

Those with information are asked to contact North Yorkshire online or by phoning 101.

Crimestoppers can be anonymously on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.
