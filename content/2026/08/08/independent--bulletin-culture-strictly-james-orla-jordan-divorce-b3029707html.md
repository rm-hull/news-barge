Former Strictly Come Dancing professional dancers James and Ola Jordan have announced their split after undergoing a trial separation.

The couple, who married in 2003, confirmed the news through their representative on Friday evening.

They said that their primary focus remains co-parenting their daughter, Ella, who was born in 2020.

The couple first met at a dance competition in 1999 and both joined the BBC series in 2006, with Ola winning the glitterball trophy in 2009.

A source close to the couple expressed shock at the news, noting that James had previously spoken about the effort required to maintain a long-term relationship.
