Barack Obama's infamous 2014 tan suit made a satirical return in the finale of Larry David's HBO series, Life, Larry and the Pursuit of Unhappiness.

The seven-episode limited series was produced by Obama's Higher Ground company, which aimed to “close the book” on the tan suit controversy.

The sketch features Larry David accidentally spilling a smoothie on Obama's navy suit, then suggesting the tan suit as a replacement for a press conference.

Obama shared a clip of the appearance on X, joking that it was time for the “truth” about the tan suit to finally come out.

Co-creator Jeff Schaffer noted Obama's strong improvisation skills on set and his enjoyment of playing an annoyed character opposite David.
