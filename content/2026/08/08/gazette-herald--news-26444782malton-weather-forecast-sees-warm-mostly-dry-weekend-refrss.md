The Met Office has forecast a warm and dry weekend for Malton.

Saturday will start cool and dry, with temperatures around 11C at 6am, dropping to 8C by 11am.

Rain is unlikely, and winds will be light at 2–3 mph.

The afternoon will see a rapid rise in temperature, reaching 24C by 6pm, and while rain remains unlikely, winds will increase to 8–9 mph by late afternoon.

The evening will be warm and dry, with temperatures climbing to 27C by 10pm.

Rain remains unlikely, and winds will hold at approximately 9 mph.

The night will stay warm, with temperatures around 27C at 10pm, gradually falling to the mid-to-upper teens later overnight.

It will remain dry, with less than a 5 per cent chance of rain throughout, and winds will gradually ease from around 9 mph to 3 mph.

Sunday will begin milder than Saturday, with temperatures around 13C at 6am, rising to 23C by midday.

There is a small chance of a shower, with rain probabilities generally 10–20 per cent.

Winds will gradually increase from around 3–4 mph early on to 8 mph by late morning.

The afternoon will see temperatures reaching around 24C from 1pm through 5pm, before easing slightly to 23C at 6pm.

The chance of a shower will generally sit between 10 per cent and 20 per cent, while winds will remain around 8–9 mph.

The evening will gradually become cooler, with temperatures falling to approximately 18C by 10pm.

Rain chances will settle at around 10 per cent, while winds will gradually ease from 9 mph to around 6 mph.

By late evening, temperatures will fall to 17C at 11pm, making for a much fresher night than Saturday.
