SPECIALIST teams focused on tackling rape and sexual offences will be in place across all police forces in England and Wales by December 2027.

This brings the deadline forward by 18 months as part of a wider government effort to strengthen protections for victims and address violence against women and girls.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham said: "The scale of violence and abuse suffered by women and girls in this country is unacceptable.

"We must be laser focused on stamping out these vile crimes."

The specialist teams will support victims, investigate offences, and bring perpetrators to justice.

Satvir Kaur, Minister for Safeguarding and Violence Against Women and Girls, said: "By ensuring every police force has specialist rape and sexual offence teams sooner, and funding for new protection order teams, we are strengthening our response, relentlessly pursuing perpetrators and helping victims live their life without fear."

The government also announced £13.3 million for up to 10 police forces to establish specialist Protection Order Teams, which will enforce measures such as electronic monitoring, exclusion zones, and behaviour change programmes for offenders.

These orders can be issued without a criminal conviction and aim to protect victims through strict monitoring and intervention.

Recent figures show that around 900,000 adults in England and Wales experienced sexual assault last year.
