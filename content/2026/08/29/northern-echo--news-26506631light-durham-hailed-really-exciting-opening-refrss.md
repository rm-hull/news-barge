Durham’s new cultural venue The Light has been hailed as "really exciting and thrilling” after a successful opening weekend.

The £17m transformed Aykley Heads building opened to the public on Saturday, August 22, with live music, circus entertainment, workshops and the first chance to see its two inaugural exhibitions.

Tony Guillan, artistic director of The Light, said: “It’s been really, really exciting and thrilling really to see so many people turn up so early this morning to be first to get in.

  Tony Guillan, Artistic Director. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

“We’re thrilled to have such beautiful weather, so we’ve got families enjoying the outside, listening to the bands, and we’re actually jam-packed inside to see so many people enjoying both exhibitions has been brilliant.”

   

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

   

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The museum closed in 2016 after the building was judged no longer fit for purpose, before a major redevelopment created a new venue for art, heritage, culture and community.

## Families, music and exhibitions

Visitors over the opening weekend were able to enjoy outdoor performances from Pittington Brass Band, North East singer-songwriters and The Cequin Circus, which brought stilt walkers, dancers and fire-spinners to the grounds.

There were also science-themed lawn games, drop-in activities and a lantern-sculpture project for families, alongside a gift shop and café.

   

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The opening gave visitors their first view of We Are The Durhams: The Durham Light Infantry Exhibition, a permanent display exploring the history and identity of the regiment through images, sound, objects and hands-on activities.

They were also able to see Victory Over the Sun: Encounters with Light, a major exhibition bringing together work by international artists who use light as a subject and material.

The exhibition includes work associated with artists such as Tracey Emin, Steve McQueen, Dan Flavin and Olafur Eliasson.

Victory Over the Sun runs until January 17, 2027.

Entry to the permanent DLI exhibition is free, while tickets are required for the temporary exhibition.


## Volunteers praised

The Light also paid tribute to the volunteers who supported its first weekend.

A statement said: “A huge thank you to all of the incredible volunteers who helped make Opening Weekend at The Light such a success.

“From welcoming visitors and supporting activities to helping everything run smoothly behind the scenes, our volunteers played a vital role in creating a memorable and enjoyable experience for everyone who joined us.”

The venue was created by Durham County Council and aims to combine the city’s military past with contemporary art, science, technology and creativity.

## Opening hours

The Light is open daily during August.

It opens daily at 10am, closing at 5pm or 4pm on Sundays. The museum has a late opening til 8pm on Thursdays.

From September, it will close on Mondays and Tuesdays, while other opening hours will remain the same. The café is open from 10am to 4pm.
