Thousands of visitors headed to Leyburn for the 112th Wensleydale Show, celebrating farming, food and countryside life in the Yorkshire Dales.

The annual event brought livestock competitions, horses and ponies, horticulture, handicrafts, trade stands and local produce to the showground, overlooking.

Families also enjoyed main-ring entertainment, including terrier racing, live music and Jurassic Earth’s life-sized dinosaur performances.

This year’s show featured its largest-ever vintage tractor section, while the expanded food from farming tent and craft marquee showcased regional businesses.

Crowds weren't put off by the rain, holding coats over their heads to avoid the showers.
