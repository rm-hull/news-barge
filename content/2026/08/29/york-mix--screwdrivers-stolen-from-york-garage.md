Thieves have stolen items from a garage in a York suburb during an early morning burglary.

North Yorkshire Police said a number of DeWalt screwdrivers were stolen in Lumley Road, Clifton around 1.47am on Thursday, August 27.

A force spokesperson said if anyone saw any suspicious activity nearby or has CCTV, doorbell, dashcam or other footage, to please get in touch.
