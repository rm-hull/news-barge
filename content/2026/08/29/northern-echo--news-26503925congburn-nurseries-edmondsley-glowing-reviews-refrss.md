A family-run County Durham garden centre has received glowing reviews from "delightfully blown over" customers.

Congburn Nurseries has an almost perfect rating of 4.6 stars from over 800 Google reviews.

The centre, on Congburn Bank in Edmondsley, offers a diverse selection of products from bedding plants to shrubs, to ceramic pots and landscaping.

   

*(Image: GOOGLE MAPS)*

Congburn Nurseries is a family-run business established in 1990 and is home to Woodlands Bistro and an on-site gift shop.

One review said: "Just visited this lovely place this morning.

"Food was lovely. Plenty of selection of healthy looking plants. Gift shop definitely deserves a look around. Such unique gifts.

"Definitely be back."

The bistro receives consistent praise from customers.

One five star reviewer praised the easy access points and the large portions.

They said: "We chose to enter by one of their great easy access points which is ramped to the inside area.

"We were greeted with a lovely friendly welcome and shown to our table where the menu had great choices.

"We were presented with a plateful of three different fresh vegetables, mash and roast potatoes, Yorkshire puddings to nearly bathe in, lamb in it's own dish and extra dishes of delicious mashed turnip and roasted parsnips.

"We were delightfully blown over and happily waded our way joyously through our hot lunch.

"It was superb.

"I can go on and on - instead get yourself booked and try this fabulous food for yourself.

"We'll definitely be revisiting."

The restaurant offers breakfasts, hot and cold sandwiches, homemade pies, fish and chips, Sunday lunches, afternoon tea, homemade cakes, and more.

Woodlands Bistro also doubles as a wedding venue.

One couple said: "Amazing place.

"We had our wedding reception here, well recommended and lovely staff and atmosphere.

"Very much recommend if you're looking for a wedding venue or even a nice meal out."
