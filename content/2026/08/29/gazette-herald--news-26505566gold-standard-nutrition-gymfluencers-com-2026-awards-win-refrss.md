A Yorkshire-born food entrepreneur is celebrating national industry success.

Craig Allen, founder and CEO of Selby-based Gold Standard Nutrition (GSN), has seen his business named the winner of the Top Protein Meals category at the Gymfluencers.com 2026 Awards.

Mr Allen said: "We’re a bootstrapped Yorkshire business, built from the ground up since 2012.

"We’ve always lived by one motto at GSN – we’re not here to be the biggest, we’re here to be the best, so we’re really proud to have received this national recognition.

"Every award like this is proof that all the hard work and sacrifice is paying off."

GSN produces a range of high-protein frozen meals including its Pots of Gold, wraps, and Signature Meals, which are now stocked in more than 1,000 locations nationwide.

The company, headquartered in Selby, was founded in 2012 by Mr Allen when he was just 21 years old.

He launched the business with 10 second-hand freezers and a decommissioned Tesco delivery truck.

The recent award comes after a period of rapid growth for GSN, which recently passed the milestone of 10 million meals sold in the UK.

Mr Allen said: "At GSN, our mission from the very beginning has been to make healthy eating more accessible, more affordable and more enjoyable for everyone.

"To be recognised for that by the industry is a genuinely proud moment for me and the whole GSN team."

The Gymfluencers Awards celebrate outstanding brands and products in the health and fitness sector.

Judges praised GSN for its hassle-free approach to premium nutrition, and the brand’s approach to making convenient frozen food healthier also impressed the independent judging panel.

GSN’s meals are designed to be nutritionally balanced, high in protein and fibre, low in sugar, and made in the UK.

They are ready to eat within minutes and cater to a wide variety of tastes.

Looking ahead, Mr Allen is working on GSN to Go, a workplace wellbeing programme aimed at businesses with more than 30 employees.

The scheme will provide employers with a cost-effective way to offer healthier food options to staff.

GSN is also developing new products, including a brand-new format set to launch in the coming months.

The company aims to become the UK’s best-loved frozen food brand while staying true to its Yorkshire origins.
