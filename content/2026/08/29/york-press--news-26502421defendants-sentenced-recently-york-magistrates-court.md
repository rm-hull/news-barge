Two thieves jailed for shoplifting will have to pay compensation to the shops they raided when they are released from prison.

They were among defendants sentenced recently at York Magistrates Court.

Jaimee Reilly, 26, of Far Moss, Selby, pleaded guilty to stealing razor blades and alcohol worth a total of £131 from Sainsbury’s, coffee worth £56 from B&M, razor blades and alcohol worth a total of £185.50 from Tesco and bank card fraud, all committed in Selby.

She was given an 18-week prison sentence suspended for 12 months on condition she does six months’ drug rehabilitation and 10 days’ rehabilitative activities. She must pay £185.50 compensation to Tesco, £56 compensation to B&M and £131 compensation to Sainsbury’s.

Keith Harrison, 52, of Buile Hill Avenue, Little Hulton, Worsley, Salford, pleaded guilty to stealing sunglasses worth £40 and £887 in separate visits to Sunglasses Hut, items worth £370 from Swarovski, items worth £139 from Hugo Boss, items worth £98 from Reiss, items worth £123 from Cosmetic Company, all at York Designer Outlet.

He was jailed for 12 weeks and ordered to pay £404 compensation.

Connor Atkins, 30, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to failure to comply with the requirements of the sex offenders’ register. He was jailed for eight weeks.

Craig Callaghan, 37, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to stealing flowers worth £40 from Tesco in Selby, and deodorant worth £80 from Poundland.

He was jailed for four weeks and must pay £120 compensation.

Eliza Carthy, 51, of Mount Pleasant East, Robin Hoods Bay, pleaded guilty to drink driving in Scarborough.

She was banned from driving for three years, fined £120 and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £48 statutory surcharge.
