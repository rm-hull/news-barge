Further tributes have been left at the scene of a house fire which killed a seven-year-old girl and her aunt.

Valentina Foster and Natalie McDonald, 34, died in the blaze on Birchington Avenue in Grangetown on Wednesday (August 26) morning.

  Tributes left at the scene of a fatal fire on Birchington Avenue, Grangetown 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

  Natalie McDonald, 34, and Valentina Foster, seven. 

*(Image: UGC)*

Two men, aged 23 and 31, arrested on suspicion of murder remain in custody.

A48-year-old woman left fighting for her life remains in a “critical but stable” condition at the RVI in Newcastle.

Following the tragic incident, tributes, messages and floral arrangements were left near the Grangetown home.

On Saturday (August 29), further tributes, balloons and stuffed animals have been left near the scene of the devastating fire.

  Tributes left at the scene of a fatal fire on Birchington Avenue, Grangetown 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Pictures of the victims have been left, with messages attached to flowers, cuddly toys and a set of balloons.

  Tributes left at the scene of a fatal fire on Birchington Avenue, Grangetown 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A 27-year-old man also injured in the fire remains at James Cook University Hospital, where he is receiving treatment.

Meanwhile, two teenage girls who suffered non-life-threatening injuries have now been discharged following treatment.

The property remains unstable and work to make it safe will be carried out in the coming days.

Police said they are not treating the fire as linked to the fatal crash on the A66 which killed seven, including two police officers, PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough.

Officers initially said they could not rule out a link with wider gang violence breaking out on Teesside after the smash, which also killed five young men in a VW Passat going the wrong way up the A66.
