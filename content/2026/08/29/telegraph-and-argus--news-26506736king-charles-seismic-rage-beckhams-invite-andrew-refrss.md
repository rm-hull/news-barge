King Charles was reportedly "seismic with rage" over a Buckingham Palace birthday invitation.

The invite, extended by the Beckhams to Prince Andrew for their daughter Harper’s sixth birthday, has resurfaced in public discussion following claims made in Alison Boshoff’s new book, Brand Beckham.

According to the book, the then Prince Charles was furious after photographs emerged from the July 2017 celebration, held at Buckingham Palace in the former Prince Andrew's private apartment.

Sir David Beckham attended the event after their daughter was invited to the tea party, reportedly arranged through Sarah Ferguson, Prince Andrew's former wife.

Sir David later shared photos on Instagram, showing Harper and her friends inside and outside the Palace, with Princess Eugenie also pictured among the children.

In the post, he wrote: "Happy Birthday to our special little young lady.

"Such a special girl who brings so much joy and happiness into our lives.

"Happy 6th birthday (I can't believe you're 6 already) have the most amazing day we love you pretty lady x."

The social media posts drew public criticism, including from Queen Elizabeth II’s former press secretary Dickie Arbiter.

Mr Arbiter said: "Is it [Buckingham Palace] being opened up as a theme park?

"It devalues what the place is all about.

"Why do the Beckhams get special treatment?"

The book claims that Palace insiders considered the posts a "serious breach of protocol," with one source saying: "Prince Charles is apparently seismic with rage and this will only deepen his feud with Prince Andrew."

The source also claimed that photographs are "absolutely not allowed in the Buckingham Palace quadrangle" and added: "Prince William will not be amused."

There was a suggestion that Sarah Ferguson was able to arrange the invitation because the late Queen was spending the summer in Scotland.

Sir David later addressed the controversy online, clarifying: "Just to be clear this wasn't the palace opening the gates for Harper's birthday party, this was a tea party where us and other guests were invited so it was a beautiful thing to do with my mum, Harper plus a few school friends.

"We were honoured to be able to be there.

"Beautiful tea party."

Victoria Beckham, who was away for work at the time, posted a photo of Harper dressed as Elsa from Frozen, writing: "Our little birthday princess x Kisses."

The incident occurred several years before Sir David was awarded a knighthood by King Charles in November 2025 for services to sport and charity.

Since then, the King and Sir David have appeared together at several events, with the former footballer increasingly involved in royal charitable work.
