Drivers in Darlington are being warned about eight sets of roadworks taking place during the August 29 to 31 bank holiday weekend.

The works include temporary traffic signals and lane restrictions.

Some are scheduled to continue into September.

## Neasham Road

Temporary traffic signals are in place on Neasham Road in both directions at Burma Road because of construction.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until August 31 at 11pm.

## A68 Woodland Road

Telecoms work is taking place on the A68 Woodland Road between Hollyhurst Road and Bracken Road.

Temporary traffic signals are in place in both directions. The restrictions are set to remain in place until September 15 at 11.59pm.

## Beechwood Avenue

Telecoms work is affecting Beechwood Avenue in both directions between the B6280 Coniscliffe Road and Southend Avenue.

Restrictions are in place and are scheduled to remain in place until August 28 at 4pm.

## A167

Gas main work is affecting the A167 between the B6444 St Andrews Way and the A1(M) J59 at Aycliffe.

A lane is closed in both directions. The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 1 at 4pm.

## Auckland Avenue

Construction is taking place on Auckland Avenue between Brinkburn Road and Auckland Oval.

Give-and-take traffic is operating in both directions. The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 22 at 11pm.


## St Alban’s Green

Electricity work is affecting St Alban’s Green in both directions at Winchester Way.

Give-and-take traffic is operating at the location. The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 9 at 11pm.

## Edinburgh Drive

Gas main work is taking place on Edinburgh Drive between the A67 Coniscliffe Road and Teesdale Avenue.

Temporary traffic signals are operating in both directions, and delays are expected. The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 9 at 4pm.

## Faverdale

Construction is affecting Faverdale in both directions after the A68 West Auckland Road.
