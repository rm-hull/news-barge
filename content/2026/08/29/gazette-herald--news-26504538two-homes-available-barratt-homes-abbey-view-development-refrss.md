Just two homes remain for sale at a new-build development in Whitby.

The final properties at Abbey View, a Barratt Homes site in the coastal town, are Kingsville-style houses priced from £259,995.

They offer three-storey living with four bedrooms, and are marketed as suitable for families and young professionals.

Steven Ball, sales director at Barratt Homes North East, said: "The final homes at Abbey View present a fantastic opportunity for a prospective buyer to find their ideal home in the vibrant community of Whitby.

"We urge anyone looking to secure a home at Abbey View, or buy property in North Yorkshire, to speak with our dedicated sales team about available homes, deals and schemes."

Located between the North Yorkshire Moors and the coast, Abbey View residents can enjoy coastal walks, restaurants, and Whitby’s historic harbour.

The Kingsville homes feature a ground-floor kitchen and family area, a first-floor lounge and en suite bedroom, and two further bedrooms and a family bathroom on the top floor.

Barratt Homes is offering schemes to help buyers move, including Deposit Boost, where the developer may contribute up to 5 per cent of the purchase price towards a deposit.

For more information about the development, visit the website at Abbey View.
