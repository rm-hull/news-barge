Kynren will light up the skies above Bishop Auckland with a 25-minute fireworks and music spectacular this Halloween.

The attraction’s Pyromusical event will take place on Saturday, October 31, bringing choreographed fireworks and music to the Kynren site following the conclusion of its main summer season.

Organisers have warned the display has sold out in previous years and have urged people planning to attend to book early.

Tickets can be purchased on the Kynren website.

## Final days for The Storied Lands

The announcement comes as Kynren’s new daytime attraction, The Storied Lands, enters its final weeks of the 2026 season.

The live-action show park, at 11 Arches Park in Bishop Auckland, features five historical and legend-inspired performances.

Visitors can see birds take flight in The Lost Feather, encounter Vikings, watch knights and horses in action and follow stories brought to life across the park.

Day Park tickets remain available on:

- Sunday, August 31
- Tuesday, September 1, to Thursday, September 3
- Sunday, September 6
- Tuesday, September 8, to Friday, September 11

The Storied Lands will close for the season on Saturday, September 12, returning in summer 2027.


## Last chances for day and night experience

There are also three remaining dates when visitors can combine a day at The Storied Lands with the evening performance of Kynren – An Epic Tale of England.

Combined day-and-night tickets are available for:

- Friday, September 4
- Saturday, September 5
- Saturday, September 12

Kynren – An Epic Tale of England is a 90-minute outdoor production charting 2,000 years of English history, performed by a cast and crew of around 1,000 volunteers.

It uses horses, pyrotechnics, lighting and a 7.5-acre outdoor stage.

Friday, September 4, currently has the best availability, according to Kynren.
