A stolen quad bike was recovered by police during a foot patrol in Wyke.

The vehicle was found in the Delph Hill area on August 20 by a PCSO and was later confirmed to have been reported stolen from Greater Manchester in January this year.

  Police are working to unite the owner with the bike. 

*(Image: WYP)*

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "This recovery highlights the value of proactive patrols and the importance of reporting suspicious activity to the police."
