Cycle parking in York city centre is set to be upgraded to improve accessibility.

Work to replace stands in poor condition and temporary stands with new “Sheffield” style stands begins in September, with additional space being created for cargo bikes, tricycles, and other inclusive cycles.

Kate Ravilious, City of York Council's executive member for transport, said: "It’s great to see city centre cycle parking having a much-needed upgrade.

"Alongside the standard cycle parking, the new spacious inclusive cycle parking will accommodate trikes, cargo and family cycles to name a few, enabling more people to choose to cycle."

The upgrades are funded by Active Travel England and will take place at locations across central York, with stands at High Petergate, Davygate, Parliament Street, Piccadilly, St. Andrewgate, Deangate, Kings Staith, Cumberland Street, and Whip-Ma-Whop-Ma-Gate.

Work at each site will be carried out between 10.30am and 5pm.

Some locations will require temporary road closures or changes to loading bays to ensure safety.

Cyclists are advised to remove their bikes and locks before work begins, as stands will be out of use during the upgrades.

The council has notified nearby residents and businesses, and information will be displayed on the stands themselves.
