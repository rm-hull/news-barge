A HIGH street bakery is set to close its doors for good today.

Liv’s Bakes n Cakes, on Commercial Street in Brighouse, will close following its final day of trading as a bakery.

26-year-old owner Liv Dennison cited personal health reasons and the tough trading environment as key factors behind the decision.

The business had already announced plans to close next month, but the date has been brought forward.

## Final closing date pushed forward

In a statement shared on social media, Miss Dennison said: "Hey all,

"Due to personal health reasons and some unforeseen circumstances the final day of Liv's Bakes n Cakes trading in the Brighouse Store has been moved to this Saturday, August 29, 10am - 4pm.

"I apologise for any inconvenience caused, and want to thank everyone for the most incredible five years of trading in Brighouse on the high street.

"This past month has been the hardest and most challenging month of my life, but I’m ready for a restart and hopefully the next chapter will be just as bright.

"Speak soon.. Lots of love always, Liv xx"

## Why is the bakery closing?

In a social media video Miss Dennison previously revealed the bakery had been struggling with a sharp drop in takings, describing the hospitality climate as "insanely rough."

She said: "From January to July this year we are £73,000 down on takings compared to the same period last year – and that's just through the till alone."

She added that her monthly electricity bills have doubled, further illustrating the mounting financial pressures facing independent traders.

Although the shop will close, Miss Dennison assured her beloved customers that this is not the end for Liv’s Bakes n Cakes.

She plans to return to her roots in birthday cake orders, with hopes to expand into wholesale and events.
