Trigger Point is back on ITV for its fourth series and Vicky McClure is returning once again to lead the cast.

The programme follows Lana Washington (McClure) who heads a Metropolitan Police bomb squad after previously working as a military bomb disposal operative.

Her team are on the frontline once again as London is "rocked by a wave of devastating bombings."

A synopsis for series four adds: "The attackers are the most skilled and dangerous adversaries Lana’s ever faced, and she and her team are at the heart of the race to track them down and prevent further casualties.


"But as the hunt for the bombers intensifies, unsettling cracks begin to appear in the investigation.

"Even those Lana works closely with hide dark secrets.

"Time is running out and Lana must confront her greatest challenge yet - one that will push her to the edge.

"The fate of the capital hangs in the balance."

Discussing where her character is at the start of the new series, McClure shared: "We’ll see Lana trying her best to get a grip on things, she’s going to therapy and balancing that with her job.


  "She’s very capable but she’s also not in great shape – she’s doubting herself, which is very dangerous in that job, you have to be certain when dealing with bombs because the stakes are

  as high as they can be.

"I’ve always loved how fearless Lana is, and even though we’ll see moments where she does feel fear, she will never show face. If she’s going down it will be with a battle.

"She doesn't want people to see that she's questioning her own ability."

## When will Trigger Point series 4 be on TV?

The first episode of Trigger Point series four will air at 9pm on ITV 1 on Sunday, August 31.

The second episode will follow the next day in the same timeslot on Monday, August 31.

After that, the remaining four episodes will air in the same pattern and will be available to watch on ITVX.
