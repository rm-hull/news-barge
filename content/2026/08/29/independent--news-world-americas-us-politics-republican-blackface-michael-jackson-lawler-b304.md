A Republican lawmaker has revived memories of his blackface scandal with a social media post commemorating Michael Jackson’s birthday.

New York Rep. Mike Lawler took to X on Saturday to post a photo of himself apparently dressed as the King of Pop. He donned a red leather jacket, a v-neck black t-shirt and sunglasses.

“It’s a little snug, but it still fits! Happy Birthday MJ,” the 39-year-old lawmaker captioned the post, seemingly referring to an outfit that landed him in political hot water two years ago.

In 2024, *The New York Times* unearthed an old photo of Lawler wearing blackface while posing in similar clothing. The image, taken in 2006, when Lawler was a college student, shows the now-congressman dressed as Jackson for Halloween.

Blackface is the practice of darkening a person’s face or skin to imitate a Black person. It is widely regarded as racist. In recent years, several other high-profile political figures have come under fire over allegations they used blackface or brownface, including former Virginia Governor Ralph Norman and former Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau.

![Rep. Mike Lawler, a New York Republican, revived memories of his blackface scandal with his Michael Jackson birthday post](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/29/19/22/GettyImages-2277730941.jpeg)

Lawler, who represents New York’s 17th Congressional District, didn’t dispute the authenticity of the image. He told the *Times* the costume was meant to be “truly the sincerest form of flattery” and “a genuine homage to my musical hero since I was a little kid trying to moonwalk through my mom’s kitchen.”

Jackson, who died in 2009, became a controversial figure after facing multiple allegations that he sexually abused children. He consistently denied those allegations.

In his statement to the *Times*, Lawler added: “The ugly practice of blackface was the furthest thing from my mind…I am a student of history and for anyone who takes offense to the photo, I am sorry.”

Lawler’s latest post drew a number of comments from users about the scandal.

“No blackface this time around?” one user wrote, while another chimed in: “Are you really this dumb? You seriously think it's a good idea to remind everyone of when you wore blackface?”

“Incredible how tone deaf you are,” a third wrote. “Early voting in NY starts October 24. Vote Michael Jackson Jr. out.”

Lawler is up for re-election in November, and is facing off against Democrat Cait Conley, a U.S. Army veteran. Multiple recent polls indicate it is shaping up to be a close race.

*The Independent* has contacted the congressman’s office for comment.
