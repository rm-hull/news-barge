## Tottenham Hotspur

**Antonin Kinsky:** Unlike his countryman, Lukas Hornicek, the Spurs goalkeeper had nothing to do in the first half but could only watch on as Elanga's shot went in off the post after the break before Wissa's improvised effort made it 2-0.**5Archie Gray:** Can never fault his effort but is he at his very best at right-back?**5Micky van de Ven:** The captain's first start of the season. Picked up a needless early booking, but went on to make a crucial sliding challenge to deny Wissa inside the box.**6Jan Paul van Hecke:** Got back to make a block to deny Willock. ## Newcastle United

**Lukas Hornicek**: Made saves to keep out Tel, Robertson and van Hecke's efforts when Newcastle were under pressure in the first half. His save from van Hecke's header left head coach Matthias Jaissle clenching his fists on the touchline.** 7Amar Dedic:** Another impressive all-round performance from the summer signing little more than a week-and-a-half into his Newcastle career. Great knock-down header to set up first goal. Tino Livramento faces a real battle to win back his place.**7Malick Thiaw:** Captain again in Dan Burn's absence. A proper old-fashioned tussle with Marmoush at times, who kept him on his toes.**6Sven Botman:** Had a shaky pre-season but looking more assured. Appears to be benefitting from a run of games with the same back four.**6Lewis Hall:** Made a huge last-ditch block inside the box to prevent Porro from getting a shot away in first half. Defensively solid and no penalty fears this time.**7Nico Gonzalez:** An impressive debut. Kept composure even after booking. A stunning cross-field ball in the build up to Elanga's goal.**8Lewis Miley:** Goes about his work quietly but keeps the ball so well.**7Anthony Elanga:** Looks a reborn player full of confidence under Jaissle. Scored his second goal in two games with a smart turn and finish in off the post. His was the name repeatedly sung by the away supporters.**7.Joe Willock:** Made an instant impact after coming off the bench against Liverpool, but not as effective as a starter on Saturday. Got one of biggest cheers from Newcastle fans for a meaty challenge on Tonali.**5Harvey Barnes**: Fresh from scoring two goals in the midweek win over West Brom in the Carabao Cup, but did not offer same threat this time. Fired one effort way over and failed to pick out a team-mate on one promising breakaway after his cutback went straight into traffic.** 5Yoane Wissa:** Led the line in injured William Osula's absence and repeatedly dropped deep to help his side. Clever overhead kick sealed the win.**7Subs:Sean Steur (on 71, Gonzalez):** Sent in teasing deliver with corner in build up to Wissa's goal.**7Nick Woltemade (on 71, Willock):** Header set up Newcastle's second from Steur's corner. 7
