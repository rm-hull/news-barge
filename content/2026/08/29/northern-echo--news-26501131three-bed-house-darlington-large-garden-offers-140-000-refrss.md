PERFECT for first-time buyers or a family looking for a little more room to spread out in, in the shape of a third bedroom, this spacious house on Wentworth Way in Darlington offers a comfortable and inviting home that’s ready to move into.

On the ground floor is an entrance hall with a useful cloakroom off.

A well-proportioned lounge to the front, flooded with natural light thanks to a large window, and a kitchen diner that extends right across the rear of the property.

Combined with its well presented and spacious accommodation, this makes it perfect for a buyer looking for a place to call their own.
