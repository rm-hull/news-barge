The Yorkshire Dales are known for being a great place for walks thanks to its huge national park.

However, it is also worth visiting the area for its pretty little villages to explore.

From thatched cottages to old-fashioned pubs, the Yorkshire Dales has villages full of charm that make perfect places for a stop during a hike.

Kettlewell is known as one of the prettiest villages in the area thanks to its cute pubs, tea rooms, and scarecrow festival.


## What is Kettlewell like?

Kettlewell is the ideal place to stay if you are looking for a base in the Yorkshire Dales during a hiking trip or need a quick break in the middle of a walk.

“Walkers are spoilt for choice” when it comes to walking trails in the area, with gentle riverside strolls and more difficult hikes with plenty to see along the way.

The &Then Tasting Deli is a great place for homemade cakes, scones, and a quick coffee to help prepare you for any Yorkshire Dales hike.

If you need something a bit stronger, the village has several pubs to visit, such as the award-winning Blue Bell Inn that provides “great home-cooked food, fine ales and cosy rooms”.

  The village also holds an impressive scarecrow festival every August. 

*(Image: Tripadvisor)*

There is also the Kings Head that offers many pub classic dishes from comforting fish and chips and sausage and mash to filled Yorkshire puddings and loaded nachos.

The village has a few shops, including Over and Under, which sells outdoor clothing and equipment, and a village store where you can stock up on food from baked goods and sweets to locally produced cheeses and the essentials.

If you visit in August, you might be lucky enough to catch the village’s scarecrow festival and see if you can guess who the scarecrows have been dressed up as.

If you want to stay in Kettlewell, there is a hotel and several inns to choose from.


## 
  **What do visitors say about Kettlewell?**

The village has a rating of 4.7 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with 207 people leaving reviews after their visit.

One person said: “Visited for the scarecrow festival today and what a fabulous time we have had!

“We had the best time doing the trail, admiring all the scarecrows, calling in to the village hall for delicious tea and scones and chatting with the very friendly lady selling raffle tickets.

“We left armed with scarecrows to put in the garden at home and the biggest smiles on our faces.

“Thank you to the village for all your hard work.”

Another person said: “We had the pleasure of visiting during the scarecrow festival.

“What is an already beautiful village was enhanced in atmosphere with the festival.

“Village well worth a visit.”
