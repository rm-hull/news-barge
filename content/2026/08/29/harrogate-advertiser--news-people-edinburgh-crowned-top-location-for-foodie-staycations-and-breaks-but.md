# Edinburgh crowned top location for foodie staycations and breaks but Harrogate also rated highly

## Harrogate is in the UK top ten after new research revealed the UK destinations serving up Britain’s best food-focused breaks for 2026.

The town was named one of Britain's tastiest staycations in research conducted for Sykes Holiday Cottages at [https://www.sykescottages.co.uk/From](https://www.sykescottages.co.uk/From) Michelin-starred menus and seafood suppers to buzzing markets, afternoon teas and street-food favourites, the latest ranking shines a light on the best foodie destinations for holidaymakers in the UK.

Edinburgh was crowned Britain’s top foodie staycation destination for 2026 while, Padstow in Cornwall and York made up the UK top three.

The Scottish capital, which is is home to seven Michelin-starred restaurants, was praised for offering traditional Scottish flavours alongside a contemporary dining scene.

Harrogate was rated the sixth top, based on consumer research of 2,000 UK adults carried out by OnePoll in December 2025.

The town was hailed for its “elegant spa-town character which goes hand in hand with its café and tearoom culture”.

The research also highlighted staycation accommodation in Harrogate, focussing on The View on Valley Drive which was described as a “contemporary apartment sleeping four with an open-plan kitchen, dining and living space giving couples or friends a sociable base, while the location keeps the town’s shops, cafés and restaurants close at hand.”
