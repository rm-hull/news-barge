THIEVES have targeted a garage in a York suburb during an early morning burglary.

North Yorkshire Police said a number of DeWalt screwdrivers were stolen from the scene in Lumley Road, Clifton around 1.47am on Thursday, August 27.

  



A force spokesperson said it wants to hear from anyone who saw suspicious activity nearby or has CCTV, doorbell, dashcam or other footage.

Information can be provided by calling North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively, you can report online through the force website or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference 12260167509 when providing information.
