BEAUTIFULLY renovated and significantly improved by its current owners, Four Winds is an exceptional home in the exclusive semi-rural development of Brancepeth Manor Farm that seamlessly blends contemporary styling with charming character features.

The porch, accessed via a private paved seating area perfectly positioned to enjoy views across the surrounding countryside and neighbouring lake, leads into a boot room with a dedicated office space ideal for those who work from home.

The porch also opens into the heart of the home and family life – an open-plan kitchen, dining and living room more than 50 feet in length.

  If you're looking for a contemporary home with character in abundance, Four Winds might be just what you're after 

*(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

This impressive space is designed for modern family living and entertaining.

As such it features a bespoke kitchen with an extensive range of fitted cabinetry, quality stone and timber work surfaces and integrated appliances, with ample space for dining and relaxation.

A feature fireplace with wood-burning stove provides a striking focal point, while sliding doors lead directly into the landscaped walled rear garden featuring a substantial paved terrace perfect for alfresco dining, a pergola with retractable roof and a generous lawned area accessed via attractive stone steps.

Completing the ground floor is a utility room with fitted storage, a well-proportioned double bedroom, a contemporary shower room and a stunning reception room with vaulted ceiling and exposed timber beams that is currently utilised as a second living room but is equally suited to being a bedroom, guest suite, playroom or home cinema room.

  If you're looking for a contemporary home with character in abundance, Four Winds might be just what you're after 

*(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

Upstairs are a luxurious family bathroom with a freestanding bath and three bedrooms, including the principal bedroom with dual-aspect views, exposed beams, a walk-in wardrobe and a stylish en-suite shower room.

Four Winds’ location in Brancepeth Manor Farm, on the edge of the village of Brancepeth, means it is well placed for those who commute to Durham, around five miles away.
