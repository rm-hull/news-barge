A Volkswagen was seized in Bradford West after police discovered a fraudulent insurance policy.

West Yorkshire Police said the driver of the vehicle on Allerton Road was reported to court following the incident.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Another day, another fraudulent insurance policy.

"This time a Volkswagen stopped on Allerton Road.

"Despite the driver's best efforts it didn't take us long to see through the cracks in his story.

"As a result, the vehicle was seized and driver reported to court."
