A MOTHER and daughter team are thrilled to be celebrating a decade in business.

Paperworks, a family-run business in Allerton, marked 10 years of trading last Friday.

The Allerton Road shop is owned by Donna and Ebony Jackson, who have built the business around personalised gifting.

Donna specialises in personalised engraving, UV laser printing and bespoke canvas prints, creating unique and meaningful keepsakes for every milestone.

*(Image: T&A)*

## A passion became a business

The pair said: "Paperworks is a proud family-run business, founded by mother and daughter Donna and Ebony.

"This year, we’re celebrating our 10th anniversary, and we’re incredibly grateful for the support of our local community over the past decade.

"We specialise in creating personalised balloon displays, from number stacks and bubble balloons to full party décor for birthdays, baby showers, weddings, corporate events, and many other special occasions.

*(Image: T&A)*

"Alongside our balloon work, we offer a wide range of personalised gifts and engraving services, creating unique keepsakes for every milestone.

"We also stock greeting cards, stationery, and party essentials, making us a one-stop shop for celebrations.

"What started as a shared passion has grown into a business we love, and nothing is more rewarding than helping our customers celebrate life’s special moments with something personal and memorable"
