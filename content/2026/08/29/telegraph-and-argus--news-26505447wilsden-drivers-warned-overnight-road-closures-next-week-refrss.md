ROADS in Wilsden will be closed overnight for "telecommunication works."

The City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council has issued a temporary prohibition of traffic order affecting Haworth Road, Cullingworth Road, and Bents Lane.

This closure will begin at 7pm on September 1 and end at 7am on September 2.

However, the council has stated that the closure could remain in force for up to 18 months if necessary.

The affected area on Haworth and Cullingworth Road stretches from its junction with Station Road to its junction with Doll Lane.

On Bents Lane, the closure extends for approximately 25 metres north from its junction with Haworth Road.

Pedestrian access will be maintained throughout the works.

Alternative routes for affected vehicles have been provided.

These include unaffected sections of the closed roads, as well as Wilsden Road, Stony Lane, Allerton Road, Thornton Road, Halifax Road, New Road, Main Road, and Manywells Brow.
