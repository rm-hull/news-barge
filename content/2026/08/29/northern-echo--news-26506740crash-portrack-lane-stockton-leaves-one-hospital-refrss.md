A person has been taken to hospital after a crash on Portrack Lane in Stockton.

Emergency services were called around 12.30pm on Saturday (August 29).

One lane of the westbound carriageway near Asda. One person was taken to hospital.

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on Portrack Lane in Stockton on Saturday shortly before 12.30pm.

"We dispatched one ambulance crew and the medicar to the incident.

"One patient was taken to University Hospital of North Tees for further treatment."

Cleveland Police has been contacted for comment.
