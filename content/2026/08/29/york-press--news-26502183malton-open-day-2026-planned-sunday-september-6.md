A highly popular open stables day takes place next weekend.

The Malton Open Day 2026, organised by Racing Welfare, will take place on Sunday, September 6, with top-class training yards opening their doors to give racing fans a behind-the-scenes look at the sport while raising funds for charity.

Visitors to the Malton Open Day, sponsored by Arena Racing Company (ARC), will get to experience the unique sights, sounds and smells of training yards and gain a greater insight into the racing industry.

The racing yards will be open to visitors from 08:45 until 12 noon. This year’s participating yards include Adrian Keatley, Craig Lidster, John Wainwright, Mick & David Easterby, Richard & Peter Fahey, Ollie Pears, Suzzanne France, Sara Ender and Mike Sowersby

Jack Berry House, the Injured Jockey’s Fund’s rehabilitation and fitness centre in Old Malton, will also be open for visits on the day from 11am-2pm.

Trainer Craig Lidster said: “Malton Open Day is a fantastic experience for people who don’t normally get to see the day-in and day-out of how a racehorse is prepared. It provides the opportunity to get up close and personal with our kind-natured racehorses and pose questions to the experienced and passionate racing staff, all whilst having a great family day out.”

In addition, Racing Welfare is once again partnering with York Racecourse to offer a combined ticket to attend both the ARC Malton Open Day and the YorkMix Family Raceday, allowing visitors to follow the journey from the training yard in the morning to the racecourse in the afternoon.

Offering a great family day out, the YorkMix Family Raceday will include top-class racing and family entertainment, with Malton Open Day combined tickets permitting entry to the Grandstand and Paddock Enclosure at York Racecourse. Gates open from 11.15am, with racing and entertainment continuing into the afternoon.

  Beck Edmunds, Racing Welfare’s Community Engagement Manager, said: *“* The ARC Malton Open Day is always a real highlight in the calendar, giving visitors a rare chance to step inside some of the area’s leading training yards and see first-hand the people, horses and dedication that make racing so special. It’s a brilliant day out for racing fans and families alike, and every ticket sold helps Racing Welfare continue its vital work supporting racing’s workforce.

“We’re hugely grateful to the yards taking part, to ARC for their generous sponsorship and to York Racecourse for helping us offer a fantastic, combined ticket. From seeing horses at the stables in the morning to enjoying the YorkMix Family Raceday in the afternoon, it’s a wonderful way to experience the journey from training yard to racecourse.”

Opening yards have designated parking at each location, giving visitors flexibility to plan their morning. Postcodes for the parking can be found on the Open Yards page of the Malton Open Day website.

Tickets are on sale now and are priced as follows:

Malton Open Day adult ticket: £10

Malton Open Day and York Racecourse combined ticket: £20

Under 18’s can attend both events free of charge but must be accompanied by an adult.

To book tickets and to find out more, please visit: www.maltonopenday.co.uk
