Many Britons will get to enjoy a day off on Monday for the UK’s last bank holiday until December.

The summer bank holiday is the sixth of eight national holidays and falls on the final day of the month and the last day of summer this year.

While much of the UK is forecast to see rain and thunderstorms over the bank holiday weekend, the weather is expected to brighten up with sunny interludes for parts of the country on Monday.

Like other bank holidays, many stores will operate on reduced hours. This is when supermarkets are open on Monday:

![Many Tesco stores will be closing early on Monday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/13/9/13/iStock-458531819.jpg)

## Tesco

Larger Tesco supermarkets in England and Wales will be open from 8am to 6pm, but express stores are expected to operate usual hours.

You can check your local store here.

## Sainsbury’s

Sainsbury’s opening times will vary depending on the store, but most larger supermarkets are expected to open from 7am until 10pm on Monday.

You can find the specific opening times for your local store, using the store locator here.

## M&S

M&S stores will operate on reduced hours on Monday and will typically close between 6pm and 8pm.

Check the store locator to find out trading hours for specific branches.

![Waitrose is operating on reduced hours for the bank holiday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/01/07/00/23144007-a205daaa-c5ac-4eae-8820-7a59b2a21af1.jpg)

## Waitrose

Most Waitrose stores will be open from 8am to 6pm on Monday. Smaller branches are expected to stay open during their normal hours, but opening times for bigger supermarkets will vary.

Check the hours for your local Waitrose store here.

## Asda

Asda stores will be operating later on Monday at 8am and closing at 10pm. Some express locations will continue to stay open for 24 hours or until their normal closing time.

You can check here.

## Lidl

Many Lidl stores will also operate on reduced hours and close early at 8pm. However, London stores are expected to close at 10pm.

Use their store locator to find out specific opening times.

![Bigger Morrisons stores will be closing early on Monday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/24/00/21163052-baa4f914-3a21-46bd-a6bb-4abc7268561b.jpg)

## Morrisons

On Monday, larger Morrisons supermarkets in England and Wales are open from 7am to 8pm, but many smaller stores are expected to operate on regular hours.

Check here.

## Aldi

Many Aldi stores will also operate on reduced hours on Monday from 8am to 8pm in England and Wales stores.

Some stores are expected to stay open until 10pm, you can check here.

## Co-op

Most Co-op stores will stay open from 7am to 10pm, however, some branches may have varying hours and stay open later.

Use the Co-op branch locator to check your store.
