ONE of the busiest major roads on the edge of York is set to close for resurfacing works.

City of York Council said the A64 between the Askham Fields junction to the former Buckles Inn pub will close from 8pm on Tuesday, September 1 and reopen at 5am the following morning or earlier if the work is completed.

  



The council said drivers will be diverted via the A64 and the A1036 Tadcaster Road.

Diversion signs and barriers will mark the closure.

Access for residents and emergency vehicles will be maintained throughout the works.

The closure has been put in place in order that the works can be carried out safely.
