# Middlesbrough fans pay respects to police officers killed in A66 horror crash

## Football fans have paid their respects to the two constables killed in the head-on crash on the A66 and other Cleveland Police officers as the force continues to investigate a spate of incidents in the area.

Pictures of the two officers were displayed on screens in the Riverside Stadium and the crowd and players gave them a round of applause.

The King has also written to their families to express his condolences. Middlesbrough FC said in a statement: “The commitment shown by officers and staff, often in challenging and demanding circumstances, plays a vital role in keeping residents safe and helping people feel secure in their homes, neighbourhoods and local areas.”

![Middlesbrough and West Bromwich Albion players observe a minutes silence in memory of PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough. Credit: PA](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/29/14/56/01M16NQSC8B86WQKPN9SKM0TDQ.jpg?trim=0,0,0,0&width=640&quality=65)



Cole Worthy, 17; Theo Rae, 17; Makai Saddington, 18; and 23-year-olds Michael Cahill and Jakub Matusiak were also killed in the crash. All of the young men had multiple previous convictions other than Mr Cahill.

Cleveland Police said its officers have been tackling alleged organised crime in the wake of the crash and, on Friday night, said some 24 people had been arrested in the crackdown.

![PC Tom Clough (left) and PC Matthew Blades, who died when a car going in the wrong direction hit a police car on the A66 near Middlesbrough.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmZkOWUyMGQ2LWIwNGQtNGZkNC05MDVkLTkxZDkxMjgxZDJhZTphNGI3M2QwZi05ODgwLTQyZjctODQ4ZC0zNDc5Y2ZiYThiY2Y=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Officers said four men, aged 35, 25, 40 and 21, were being held on suspicion of criminal damage with intent to endanger life after a vehicle crashed into a house in Steele Crescent, Middlesbrough, on Thursday morning.

They were also being questioned over alleged arson after a caravan was set alight in Atlee Road on Wednesday morning, and a drugs offence.

![Middlesbrough fans applaud](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/29/13/cs-22c78f40-a236-4835-a9a4-3155542048d2.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The force said the men were in a vehicle when they were stopped by police on Kingsley Road in Grangetown on Thursday night.

Natalie McDonald, 34, and her niece, Valentina Foster, died while four other people were injured in the blaze on Wednesday morning – with two men, aged 23 and 31, still in custody on suspicion of murder.
