A prolific County Durham shoplifter who stole items, including beer, vapes and sandwiches, has been jailed.

William Etherington, of Dennis Street, Wheatley Hill, stole hundreds of pounds worth of items, including sandwiches, beer and vapes, before being arrested on Thursday last week (August 20).

Appearing at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on Friday (August 28), the 22-year-old pleaded guilty to 11 counts of shoplifting and was jailed for 17 weeks.

Etherington was also handed a three-year Criminal Behaviour Order banning him from retail premises in the County Durham area.

If he fails to comply with the order, Etherington can be arrested and face further prosecution.
