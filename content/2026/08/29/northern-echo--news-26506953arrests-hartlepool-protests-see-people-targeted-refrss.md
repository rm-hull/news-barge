Two people have been arrested after what was claimed to be a "peaceful protest" in Hartlepool saw a man attacked.

The incident took place on Friday, August 28.

A 29-year-old man was attacked, racially abused, and had a bag stolen, he was taken to the University Hospital of North Tees, where he stayed overnight for treatment.

A 54-year-old man from Hartlepool has been arrested on suspicion of robbery and remains in police custody.

A second incident was reported at around 12.30pm today (Saturday, August 29) at another address in Hartlepool where windows were smashed with rocks and bricks while children were inside the property.

A group of people had gathered outside at the time.

A 46-year-old woman has been arrested on suspicion of obstructing or resisting a constable in the execution of duty and is currently in police custody.

Superintendent Paul Richardson of Cleveland Police said: "While police will facilitate a peaceful and lawful protest, any escalation of violence or criminality will be dealt with appropriately and robustly.
