A Darlington businessman is on course for a top five finish in a national motorbike racing championship while continuing to run a growing company.

James Logan, director of Facility Six Ltd, is competing in the No Limits Racing League, a nine-round series which attracts some of the country’s top riders.

The 33-year-old currently sits sixth in the championship after a string of strong performances, including a third-place finish and spells leading races.

Speaking on the competition, he said: “It’s not just about getting on the bike and thrashing it – at 150mph it’s split-second stuff.

“There can be up to 40 riders in my race, and I’m getting top five finishes in most races now.”

Away from the track, Mr Logan runs Facility Six Ltd from Business Central in Darlington.

The company has expanded from electrical work into plumbing, mechanical services and refurbishment projects.

  James Logan at Facility Six business central. 

*(Image: Hubspoke media)*

He said many of the skills needed in racing transfer directly into business.

He said: “I’ve started treating racing more like a project: planning, watching videos, studying maps and working out how I’m going to improve rather than just turning up and riding.”

Facility Six has been based at Business Central, owned by Darlington Borough Council and managed by the North East BIC, for five years.

Nicole Johnstone, centre manager, said staff had enjoyed following Mr Logan’s progress this season.

She said: “Facility Six has been part of the Darlington Workspace Community for five years now and it has been fantastic to follow James’ journey this season.

“Hearing about James’ passion for racing and how well he’s performing at a national level has been brilliant.”

While balancing business and racing can be demanding, Mr Logan describes being on the bike as the one time he can completely switch off.

He said: “Racing is the only time where I’m at ease – there are no thoughts, it’s just me racing the bike and that’s why I love it so much.”

The past year has brought challenges away from the circuit as well.

  James Logan at Facility Six business central. 

*(Image: Hubspoke media)*

In September 2025, his business partner Dan moved to Australia, leaving him to run the company alone.

Speaking on the challenge, he said: “It was a shock to the system; it felt like losing my right arm.

“We had worked together every day for five or six years, so it was a huge change, and I had to think about whether I could keep going on my own.”

Motorbikes have been part of the business owner's life since childhood, when his parents bought him his first trials bike.

He later moved on to track days before taking the step into competitive racing.

Now, with the championship entering its final stages, his focus is on securing a top five finish and attracting more sponsorship support.

He said: More support would help with costs, and sponsors could get their name on the bike, leathers or helmet; the ambition is still to finish top five this year and keep improving.”
