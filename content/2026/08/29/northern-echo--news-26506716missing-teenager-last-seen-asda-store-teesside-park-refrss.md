Police are searching for a missing teenager who was last seen at an Asda store.

Brandon Fox, 15, from the Yarm area, was last seen leaving the shop at Teesside Retail Park on Thursday, August 27 at around 5.30pm.

Brandon is described as a white male, 5ft 8” tall, blue eyes, of slim build with blonde, curly hair which is short on the sides.

He was last seen wearing a black T-shirt and shorts and black Nike 95 Cortez trainers with yellow writing.

Anyone who may have seen Brandon, or may know of his whereabouts, is asked to contact Cleveland Police as a matter of urgency on 101, quoting reference number 172884
