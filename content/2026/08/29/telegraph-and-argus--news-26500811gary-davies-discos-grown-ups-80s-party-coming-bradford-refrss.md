Gary Davies is bringing his Discos for Grown Ups 80s Party to St George’s Hall, Bradford, for a night of iconic hits and dancefloor anthems.

The show takes place at St George's Hall on September 4.

Dress up in your best 80s gear and dance to a night of non-stop hits from Gary Davies and the Discos for Grown Ups DJ. Expect dancers, confetti, lights, lasers and mirror balls, with songs by Wham, Erasure, ABBA, Whitney, a-ha, Queen, Depeche Mode, Duran Duran, the Human League, Madonna, Eurythmics, Soft Cell and more.

Doors open 7.15pm.

The event forms part of Davies's biggest UK tour to date.

Davies took charge of Radio 2's Sounds of the 80s in 2018, after audience response to his cover appearances for Sara Cox and Steve Wright.

He was also a regular presenter on Top of the Pops, hosting the programme more often than any other DJ during the 1980s.

Tickets are available by calling 01274 432000 or visiting bradford-theatres.co.uk.
