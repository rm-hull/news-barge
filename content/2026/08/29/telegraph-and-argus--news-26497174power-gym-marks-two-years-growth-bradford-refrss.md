A BRADFORD gym is celebrating its second anniversary today.

Power Up Gym on Beacon Road is hosting an event that includes free walk-ins, giveaways, fitness challenges, and the launch of its first kids' boxing club.

Owner Hasan Faridi said: "Our members see the sweat that goes in behind the scenes—they see us carrying heavy kit up the stairs, building machines by hand, and staying late just to chat and check in.

"That graft creates an atmosphere where everyone feels invested.

"Putting in the extra effort to make this an enjoyable, welcoming home has been entirely worth it."

  Equipment within the space 

*(Image: Hasan Faridi)*

## 'Bradford has backed us from day one'

Mr Faridi described the gym as a non-intimidating space where both beginners and experienced lifters can train comfortably alongside each other.

He said the support from Bradford has been key to its success.

Mr Faridi added: "As an independent small business, you live and die by your local community, and Bradford has backed us from day one.

"Seeing our members bring their friends, share their progress, and treat this place like their second home drives us to work harder every day.

"That support allows an independent gym to grow bigger and compete with the massive chains while keeping its heart."

  Staff at Bradford's Power Up Gym 

*(Image: Newsquest/Mike Simmonds)*

## 'An anchor for the community'

Power Up Gym was a finalist for the Telegraph & Argus Reader's Choice for Best Gym 2025.

Mr Faridi said its reputation has grown on the back of affordability and a welcoming atmosphere.

He said: "By keeping memberships fair and focussing on genuine hospitality, we’ve proven that high-quality facilities and a great atmosphere don’t need to come with a premium price tag.

"A gym should be an anchor for the community—a safe, positive space to de-stress, build healthy routines, and connect with good people.

Memberships start at £16.99 per month.

  Equipment within the space 

*(Image: Hasan Faridi)*

## What does the gym have?

The gym has grown from a basic open space to what Mr Faridi calls a "health and wellness destination" under one roof.

It now includes a beauty salon, an on-site masseuse, a pain and injury clinic, and dedicated boxing and martial arts clubs.

Mr Faridi said: "We reinvest every bit of growth straight back into the facility.

"We might be an independent gym, but we have huge heart and the drive to match."

Looking to the future, Power Up Gym plans to expand across two floors to meet growing demand, upgrade equipment, and further develop its boxing club.

Mr Faridi said: "Our mission is to keep scaling up what a local, independent gym can offer while keeping the training vibe fun, friendly, and unmatched."

“Visitors can see some brand-new equipment, meet the team, and experience the energy first-hand."
