Temporary traffic restrictions have been announced across York, affecting ten key locations for maintenance and development works.

The City of York Council has announced the closures under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

Back Lane, Wigginton, will see closures between Church Lane and the boundary of The Rowans property for highway maintenance.

The road will be shut daily from 9am to 4pm, with works expected to last until Wednesday, September 2.

Motorists will be diverted via Greenshaw Drive, Westfield Lane, The Village, and Church Lane.

The A64 from Askham Fields junction to Buckles Inn, Askham Bryan, will be closed for resurfacing from Tuesday, September 1, at midnight until 5am the following day.

The diversion route will be via the A64 and A1036 Tadcaster Road.

Also on September 2, Naburn Lane, Wheldrake, will be shut between Wisteria Lodge and Fairview for water mains works, with an alternative route via Moor Lane, York Road, and Howden Lane.

Monks Cross Link, Huntington, will be closed for housing development works from Saturday, September 5, until Friday, November 6.

The diversion will run through Jockey Lane, Malton Road, and the A1237.

Railway maintenance at York Road Level Crossing, Strensall, will see a closure from 11.25pm on Saturday, September 5, to 8.25am on Sunday, September 6, with a diversion via York Road, Princess Road, Moor Lane, and Ox Carr Lane.

Little Stonegate and Back Swinegate, York, will be closed for highway resurfacing from 7am on Monday, September 7, until 5pm on Sunday, October 25.

Moor Lane, Haxby, will be shut from Monday, September 7, to Thursday, September 10, from 8am to 4pm daily for maintenance works.

No diversion will be signed, but residents and businesses can coordinate access with site traffic management.

Common Road Level Crossing, Strensall, will see a closure for railway maintenance from 11.20pm on Wednesday, September 9, to 5.55am on Thursday, September 10, with a diversion via Lords Moor Lane, York Lane, Moor Lane, Gennell Lane, Smith’s Lane, and Common Road.

The A1079 Hull Road, Kexby, will be closed for resurfacing from 8pm on Monday, September 7, until 6am on Saturday, September 19, with a diversion via A1079, A614, A63, A19, and A64.

Tower Street, York, will be closed for survey works from Monday, September 7, to Saturday, September 12, from midnight to 5am daily, with a diversion via York Inner Ring Road.

All closures will maintain access for residents and emergency vehicles, except where ongoing works or safety concerns prevent this.

Traffic signs and barriers will indicate the extent of the restrictions.

For further information, the council has advised contacting [highway.regulation@york.gov.uk.](mailto:highway.regulation@york.gov.uk.)
