The Met Office has forecast heavy rain for Malton this bank holiday weekend.

Saturday will start off partly cloudy and dry, with temperatures around 16C at 6am, gradually dipping to 14C by 10am.

Sunny intervals are expected to develop around midday.

The chance of rain is very low, generally below 5 per cent, apart from a small 10 per cent chance around 7am.

Winds will be moderate but gradually easing, falling from around 9mph early on to 6–7mph towards midday.

In the afternoon, there should be a decent spell of sunny intervals from lunchtime through the middle of the afternoon, accompanied by a noticeable rise in temperature.

It will climb from around 14C at midday to 18C at 4pm, reaching approximately 20C by 6pm.

Cloud will increase later, with the rain probability rising from below 5 per cent early in the afternoon to 10 per cent at 4pm, 20 per cent at 5pm, and 40 per cent by 6pm.

Winds will remain fairly light at 6–7mph.

The weather will change considerably after 6pm.

Light rain is expected from around 7pm, with the probability climbing from 70 per cent at 7pm to at least 95 per cent from 8pm onwards.

Rain will become heavy from around 10pm, continuing through the late evening.

Temperatures will fall from the daytime high of 20C at 6pm to around 17C by 11pm.

Winds will ease considerably to just 3–6mph.

Heavy rain will continue around and beyond midnight before gradually becoming lighter during the early hours of Sunday.

Temperatures will settle around 15–16C.

Despite very light winds, it will remain a thoroughly wet night, with rain probabilities staying around 60–70 per cent through the early hours.

Sunday will begin wet, with light rain continuing throughout the early hours.

Rain probabilities will remain around 60–70 per cent, while temperatures will stay remarkably steady at approximately 15C, and winds will be very light at only 3mph.

The persistent overnight rain should ease around dawn, leaving overcast or cloudy conditions initially.

Some sunny intervals are possible around 9am, although cloud will return later in the morning.

Temperatures will rise gradually from 14C at 6am to around 17C by 10–11am.

The shower probability will sit around 30–50 per cent, with light winds of 4–5mph.

There could initially be some brighter intervals, particularly around 1pm, before light showers develop from roughly 2pm.

Temperatures will climb to around 19C at 2pm and 20C at 3–4pm.

Shower probability will increase from around 40–50 per cent at lunchtime to 60–70 per cent during the afternoon and 80 per cent by 5pm.

A heavy shower is indicated around 5pm, with winds remaining light at around 5–6mph.

Heavy showers around 6pm will be followed by heavy rain from approximately 7–9pm, with an 80 per cent probability of rain during this period.

Temperatures will drop quite quickly, from around 17C at 6–7pm to 16C by 8–10pm.

Showery conditions will continue later, with the rain probability actually reaching 90 per cent around 10pm, before easing somewhat towards 11pm.

Showers will continue into the late evening and potentially beyond midnight, although they should gradually become less persistent.

Temperatures will fall towards 15C, with winds remaining light at around 5–6mph.

Monday will start with a lingering light shower possible around midnight, followed by predominantly cloudy conditions.

Temperatures will fall from 15C to around 14C.

Some mist is indicated around 5am, so visibility could briefly be reduced around dawn.

Winds will remain around 6mph.

Conditions will improve considerably after sunrise, with sunny intervals expected throughout the morning.

Temperatures will start relatively cool at around 14C, reaching 16C by 10am and 17C by 11am.

Rain probabilities will be mostly around 20–40 per cent, so an isolated shower cannot be ruled out, and winds will remain fairly steady at 6–7mph.

By the evening, temperatures will gradually decline from 18C at 6pm to 14C by 11pm.
