THIEVES have targeted a North Yorkshire town overnight and made off with a commercial vehicle.

North Yorkshire Police said a white Ford Transit van with grey decals was stolen from a driveway in the Selby area overnight between 10.50pm on Monday, August 24 and 7am the following day.

  


A force spokesperson said the vehicle was driven in the direction of Long Mann Hill Road area of the town.

Police are appealing for witnesses or anyone with information to come forward.

Members of the public can contact North Yorkshire Police online and selecting ‘Tell us about’ and then ‘An existing case or report.’

Anyone contacting police is asked to quote the reference number 12260165888.
