BRADFORD pubs are set to celebrate with a Mexican-themed fiesta this bank holiday weekend.

The Crown Hotel and Malt Kiln Inn are among those taking part in the August Bank Holiday event, which will feature drinks offers, entertainment, and interactive games.

Sarah Patton of Brand Potential said: "Community pubs in Yorkshire and Humber are bringing customers together over the August Bank Holiday for a vibrant and colourful Mexican-themed weekend where they will offer great-value drink offers, entertainment and interactive games."

The fiesta will introduce Take Tequila Mini Margaritas, available in four flavours, with customers in England and Wales able to enjoy three for £7–£9 and six for £10–£12.

Customers who purchase six mini margaritas will receive a Mexi-CAN or Mexi-CAN’T card for a chance to win a free tequila shot.

Part of Proper Pubs, a division of Admiral Taverns, the Bradford pubs will add their own twist to the celebrations with themed decorations and entertainment.
