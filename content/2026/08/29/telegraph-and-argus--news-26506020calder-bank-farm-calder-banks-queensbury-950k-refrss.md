A recently converted stone-built barn with over twelve acres of land is for sale close to the village of Queensbury.

The property, Calder Bank Farm at Calder Banks, offers four or five bedrooms and is listed for £950,000.

Positioned in what the listing describes as an "enviable, idyllic setting" with views over the valley, the home offers a combination of period features and modern fixtures.

  Calder Bank Farm at Calder Banks 

*(Image: Zoopla/Dacre Son & Hartley)*

Traditional elements such as exposed stone and wooden beams feature throughout, while underfloor heating is provided across the ground floor.

Hardwood double glazing and a modern gas central heating system are also fitted.

According to the listing, the property "retains the charming features expected of a Yorkshire stone barn" but also has "high quality modern" features.

  Calder Bank Farm at Calder Banks 

*(Image: Zoopla/Dacre Son & Hartley)*

Entry is via a traditional barn arch that incorporates the main doorway, first-floor balcony, and provides views from the hallway.

This open hallway leads into a dining area and fitted kitchen.

  Calder Bank Farm at Calder Banks 

*(Image: Zoopla/Dacre Son & Hartley)*

The kitchen includes wooden units, granite worktops, high quality fittings, and a kitchen island.

A living room with a feature fireplace and dual aspect windows adjoins a sun room.

French doors open from the sun room to the rear garden.

  Calder Bank Farm at Calder Banks 

*(Image: Zoopla/Dacre Son & Hartley)*

On the ground floor, an additional living room could serve as a bedroom.

There is also a utility room, cloakroom WC, and garden access.

An oak staircase leads upstairs from the hall to a landing that includes a balcony at the front.

The main bedroom includes two Juliet balconies facing the side, private views, a dressing room, and an ensuite shower room.

  Calder Bank Farm at Calder Banks 

*(Image: Zoopla/Dacre Son & Hartley)*

Two further double bedrooms each have ensuite shower rooms.

A fourth double bedroom and house bathroom with a roll top bath are also on the first floor.

Outside, the property is approached via an entryway between terraced cottages.

  Calder Bank Farm at Calder Banks 

*(Image: Zoopla/Dacre Son & Hartley)*

A stone paved driveway provides access to a double detached garage with power, electric doors, and an alarm.

Gardens include a stone patio, lawn, mature beds, shrubs, and trees.

Beyond the gardens, a gated drive leads to a workshop with power and plumbing, and a secondary garage.

  Calder Bank Farm at Calder Banks 

*(Image: Zoopla/Dacre Son & Hartley)*

Additional outbuildings include a stable block with three stables and a stone outbuilding.

Six fields, accessed by metal gates, are bordered mainly by dry stone walls.

According to the listing, the workshop or garage could be converted to additional living space, office, or annexe, subject to the necessary permissions.

  Calder Bank Farm at Calder Banks 

*(Image: Zoopla/Dacre Son & Hartley)*

The home is described as being within commuting distance of Bradford, Halifax, and Leeds, with good access to the M62 motorway.

The property is in council tax band F and has mains services installed.
