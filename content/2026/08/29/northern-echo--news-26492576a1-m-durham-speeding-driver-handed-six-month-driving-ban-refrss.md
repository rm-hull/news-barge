A driver has been banned from the roads for six months after being caught speeding on the A1(M) in County Durham.

Lee Walker, 44, of Belle Isle Road, Leeds, was sentenced at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on Thursday, August 13.

The court heard Walker had been driving a Vauxhall on the A1(M) between Junction 61 at Bowburn and Junction 60 at Bradbury when he exceeded the 70mph speed limit on November 10 last year.

Walker pleaded guilty and was disqualified from driving for six months.

He was also fined £307.

Walker was ordered to pay £130 in costs and a £123 victim surcharge, taking the total financial penalty to £560.

A collection order was made, with payments of £100 a month due from September 10.
