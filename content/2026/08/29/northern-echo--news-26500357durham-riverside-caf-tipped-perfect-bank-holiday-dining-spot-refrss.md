As people look to make the most of the Bank Holiday weekend, one Durham café is attracting attention not just for what’s on the plate, but for what’s outside the window.

Riverview Kitchen, on Silber Street, has become one of the city’s most recognisable brunch and lunch spots thanks to its location overlooking the River Wear.

From morning coffees to towering stacks of pancakes, the venue is building a reputation as a place where diners can linger a little longer and take in some of Durham’s most iconic scenery.

  Cosy indoor seating and riverside views combine to create one of Durham's most inviting brunch and coffee spots. 

*(Image: Tripadvisor)*

The café describes itself as offering “the best views of the river” from both its indoor seating area and outdoor terrace, which looks across the water and towards the historic heart of the city.

Its appeal appears to stretch far beyond Durham, too, with Riverview Kitchen stating that visitors travel from across the North East region to enjoy its brunch menu and setting.

  A towering stack of fluffy pancakes topped with fresh fruit and sweet treats at one of Durham's most picturesque riverside cafés. 

*(Image: Tripadvisor)*

Signature pancake stacks, breakfast favourites and lunch dishes have become particular talking points among customers.

The venue’s popularity isn’t just word of mouth, though, with Tripadvisor listing the perfectly placed café as a Traveller’s Choice winner.

One recent reviewer described the setting as an “obvious beautiful location”, highlighting the café’s standout position beside the water.

Another said: "The team of servers were all so pleasant, good-humoured, fast, and efficient.

"The food was beautifully presented, was full of flavour, and was good value for money."

One other added: "The pancakes are huge."

  Diners can enjoy sweeping views of the River Wear through panoramic windows at this popular Durham café.

The business has also expanded its outdoor offering with a new terrace, giving visitors another way to enjoy the surroundings during the warmer months.

The terrace has been described by the café as ideal for al fresco dining or simply enjoying a coffee.

While Durham has no shortage of places to eat, Riverview Kitchen’s combination of scenery, relaxed atmosphere and photogenic food has become one of the city’s most enviable dining locations.
