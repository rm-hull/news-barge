A vehicle was seized in Guiseley after the driver attempted to avoid police while on patrol.

Officers found the vehicle was uninsured and the driver was not wearing a seat belt.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Whilst on patrol in Guiseley, officers dealt with a vehicle that attempted to avoid police.

"Checks confirmed the vehicle was uninsured, and the driver was not wearing a seat belt. Appropriate action was taken, and the vehicle was seized.

"If you have concerns about road safety in your area, please report them via 101 or our online reporting channels."
