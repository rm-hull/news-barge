### Galtres Treehouse

  **Sutton Park, Sutton-on-the-Forest, from £256 per night**

canopyandstars.co.uk/britain/england/yorkshire/sutton-park/galtres-treehouse

Hidden away within ancient woodland on the historic Sutton Park Estate, Galtres Treehouse is a romantic countryside escape. Surrounded by 50 acres of parkland, this beautifully crafted retreat feels wonderfully secluded, but you are conveniently within easy reach of York. The setting is every bit as impressive as the treehouse itself with woodland walks, wild swimming and sweeping views creating a true back-to-nature experience.

Accommodation: Designed for two, light-filled interiors feature a king-size bed, wood burner, fully equipped kitchen and stylish bathroom, while large windows and French doors frame the surrounding woodland. Outside, an expansive deck invites guests to slow down and soak up the scenery, complete with a firepit, outdoor bath and plenty of space to unwind among the trees.

Best for: Couples seeking a luxurious digital detox. With no Wi-Fi, days are spent exploring woodland trails, reading by the fire or simply listening to birdsong from the deck.

Nearby: The estate’s gardens are open throughout the year but in August, you can visit the stately home too. York is close enough for a day of shopping and historic sights and the surrounding Howardian Hills and North York Moors offer countless opportunities for walking and exploring. The historic Castle Howard is only 10 miles away.

Don’t miss: Start the day with a refreshing dip in the spring-fed swimming pond before warming up in the wood-fired sauna. As evening falls, run a bath on the deck, light the firepit and watch the sun disappear behind the ancient trees.

### The Wensleydale Experience

  **Swinithwaite, Leyburn, from £140 per night**

wensleydale-experience.com

Set in the heart of the Yorkshire Dales, The Wensleydale Experience offers a luxurious countryside escape where boutique accommodation meets one of Yorkshire’s most spectacular landscapes. Situated on a working family farm, this family-run glamping retreat combines peace, privacy and panoramic Dales views.

Accommodation: The six luxurious yurts, named after trees found in the surrounding woodland, combine rustic charm with high-end comfort. Expect proper beds dressed in quality linens, stylish interiors and private bathrooms, alongside wood-burning stoves for cooler evenings. Outside, spacious decking areas make the most of the scenery, while private wood-fired hot tubs provide a front-row seat to rolling hills, dry-stone walls and unforgettable sunsets with just the farm animals for company. For something a little different, there is also a cosy 18th-century Temple Folly for two, or the beautifully restored Dovecote Barn.

Best for: Those seeking seclusion and a chance to escape the pace of everyday life. It’s a place to slow down, switch off and reconnect with nature, while remaining perfectly positioned to explore the Yorkshire Dales National Park.

Nearby: The market towns of Leyburn and Hawes are within easy reach, alongside Aysgarth Falls and Bolton Castle. Food lovers are equally well served, with excellent country pubs, farm shops and artisan producers dotted throughout the valley.

Don’t miss: Enhance your stay with a selection of home-reared cuts of beef and pork raised on the farm. For a truly memorable activity, join one of the Highland

Cow experiences and get up close to the animals while discovering life on a traditional Yorkshire Dales farm.

### Riverdale

  **West Tanfield, Ripon, a minimum two-night stay from around £340**

riverdaleyorkshire.co.uk

Tucked alongside the River Ure near the picturesque village of West Tanfield, Riverdale offers a secluded take on luxury glamping. Surrounded by open countryside and with the Yorkshire Dales on its doorstep, this boutique retreat combines riverside tranquillity with stylish accommodation including lodges, safari tents and shepherd’s huts.

Accommodation: Whether riverside or overlooking a wildflower meadow, each of the four lodges available sleep up to six people with open-plan living, fully equipped kitchens and private decking. The safari tents, with spacious bathrooms, wood-burning stoves and generous verandas, provide far-reaching views of the surrounding countryside. Larger tents sleep up to eight guests, making them ideal for family gatherings. Or if it’s a solo or couples’ trip, choose one of four shepherds huts, which are all dog-friendly and come with king-size beds, fire pits and outdoor bathtubs.

Best for: Nature lovers. Riverdale’s riverside setting places guests at the heart of one of North Yorkshire’s richest natural landscapes. The River Ure and surrounding woodland attract a variety of birdlife and wildlife throughout the year, and with walking routes leading directly from the site, it’s easy to spend hours exploring the countryside.

Nearby: Riverdale is within walking distance of West Tanfield, which boasts two pubs and a village shop. Masham is three miles away, and you are also close to Fountains Abbey and Brimham Rocks.

Don’t miss: Aside from enjoying a glass of bubbles in an outdoor tub, the main highlight has to be the private sauna and cold plunge nestled beside the river. Guests receive a discount to use them. Non-residents can book too.

### Catgill Farm

  **Bolton Abbey, North Yorkshire, super premium pods from £220 per night**

catgillfarm.co.uk

Set just above Bolton Abbey, the family-run Catgill Farm campsite has the wow factor from the moment you turn into the tree-lined drive. Spacious fields cater for electric and non-electric camping and there’s an immaculately maintained toilet and shower block, and a thoughtfully stocked shop. There are plenty of glamping options too, where sweeping views of the Dales come with your own wood-fired hot tub.

Accommodation: There are two luxury bell tents, the dog-friendly Fox and the pet-free Hare, where you’ll find all the amenities you need for your stay, including bedding and towels to a kettle, fridge and crockery, as well a private patio, hot tub, fire pit, barbecue and pizza oven. Or you can opt for one of the 10 timber pods, including a Super Premium pod with its own stargazing dome and the ‘Around the World’ themed pods with their cosy Alpine, vibrant Moroccan and Safari-inspired interiors.

Best for: Dog lovers. Everything at Catgill is designed with four-legged guests in mind, from a designated dog field and agility course next door to three dog-friendly pods – the Duck, Stag and Pheasant – with enclosed outdoor spaces so you can relax while they roam freely and safely.

Nearby: Whichever direction you head, you’re spoilt for walks. Bolton Abbey and its famous stepping stones is a must and less than a 30-minute walk from the site. Amble back via the river and stop by The Devonshire Arms for a bite to eat and drink.

Don’t miss: Pop into Cat’s Kitchen on site for a freshly brewed coffee or ‘sizzlin’ sandwich. Book a picnic with the friendly alpacas or order a Yorkshire breakfast hamper or barbecue pack from reception. Come evening, enjoy a sunset dip and fireside drinks beneath the stars.

### Uppergate Farm

  **Hepworth, Holmfirth, from £290 for a two-night minimum stay**

uppergatefarm.co.uk

Located within 10 acres of peaceful woodland on a family-run farm near Holmfirth, Uppergate Farm offers a luxurious escape where nature, wellness and rural charm come together. Farming the surrounding landscape for generations, the family has welcomed guests for more than 30 years, creating somewhere that feels both deeply rooted in the countryside and thoughtfully designed for modern comfort. With private hot tubs, woodland accommodation and exclusive spa-style experiences, it’s a place made for slowing down.

Accommodation: The collection of luxury glamping retreats includes two beautifully appointed woodland yurts, a secluded safari tent and a charming shepherd’s hut, all designed with couples in mind. Rowan and Maythorn Yurts feature super king-size beds, underfloor heating, log-burning stoves, fully equipped kitchens and private hot tubs overlooking the fields. Deanwood Safari Tent combines the romance of canvas living with hotel-style comforts, while Beechwood Shepherd’s Hut offers a cosy woodland hideaway complete with a king-size bed, hot tub and private deck.

Best for: Wellness seekers. Alongside complimentary access to the heated indoor swimming pool and sauna each evening, guests can book the farm’s unique woodland sauna experience. Hand-built from timber felled on the farm by owner Klavs, the wood-fired sauna is paired with invigorating cold-water plunges among the trees, creating a Scandinavian-inspired wellness ritual in the heart of the Yorkshire countryside.

Nearby: Scenic walks begin straight from the doorstep, while the historic village of Hepworth is within easy walking distance. Follow woodland paths to The Butcher’s Arms for a relaxed pub lunch, or venture further into the Holme Valley to explore the landscapes that inspired Last of the Summer Wine.

Don’t miss: A sunset soak in your private hot tub before heading into the woodland sauna as dusk settles through the trees. Afterwards, pick up local produce from the farm shop, fire up the barbecue and spend the evening beneath some of West Yorkshire’s darkest skies.

### The Dreamery Hideaway

  **Buttercrambe Moor Wood, from £112.50 per night**

dreameryhideaway.co.uk

Tucked away among 200 acres of woodland just outside York, The Dreamery Hideaway is less a glamping site and more an enchanting woodland escape. It combines handcrafted accommodation with winding pathways, whimsical touches and a sense of magic that feels far removed from the pace of everyday life.

Accommodation: The four handcrafted woodland bothies, sleeping up to six guests, feature hand-painted interiors inspired by folklore and fairy tales, complete with wood-burning stoves, roll-top baths, cosy living spaces and spacious verandas. The four woodcutter huts, sleeping up to four, offer a more intimate retreat, pairing rustic charm with comfortable beds and private bathroom facilities just a short stroll away.

Best for: Absolute escapism. There are no televisions, no resort-style distractions and little reason to look at a screen. Instead, days are spent wandering woodland trails, gathering around the campfire, spotting wildlife among the trees and enjoying the luxury of uninterrupted time outdoors.

Nearby: While the setting feels blissfully removed from the outside world, York is less than 20 minutes away. Castle Howard, the Howardian Hills and the Yorkshire Wolds are all within easy reach, while The Woodland Kitchen and Dreamery Gardens are right on the doorstep. Guests enjoy complimentary access to the fairy-inspired gardens, including exclusive after-hours entry.

Don’t miss: Start the day with an early morning walk through the woodland before the rest of the camp has stirred. As night falls, settle beside the firepit beneath a canopy of stars or book a place at Feast by Fire, an atmospheric woodland dining experience. Groups can also enhance their stay with a range of experiences, from gin tastings and outdoor yoga to pampering sessions and murder mystery evenings.

### Dentdale Pods

  **Millbeck, Dent, from £85 per night (low season) and £95 per night (high season)** dentdalepods.co.uk

Set on a family-run farm in one of the Yorkshire Dales’ most dramatic and unspoilt valleys, near the Cumbrian border, Dentdale Pods offer a quietly luxurious escape where rugged landscape meets understated contemporary comfort. Limestone hills rise around you, dry-stone walls trace the contours of the land and vast open skies create a profound sense of space and stillness.

Accommodation: There are just three stylish, self-contained pods on-site, each providing a warm and well-appointed base for exploring the Dales. The Plough, which sleeps up to four with a double bed and a sofa bed, features underfloor heating, en-suite facilities and a kitchenette. The Old Harrow offers similar comforts and also sleeps up to four but in a double bed and bunk beds, while The Fallow, sleeping up to three, includes a roll-top bath for an added indulgence. Outside, private decking creates an easy transition into the landscape, with seating areas perfectly placed for long evenings under expansive skies.

Best for: Those seeking seclusion. Dentdale is ideal for guests who prefer landscapes on a grand scale, with walking routes leading directly into the surrounding hills and quiet lanes perfect for slow, unhurried exploration. It’s particularly well suited to those looking for a more remote-feeling Dales experience.

Nearby: The charming village of Dent, with its cobbled streets and traditional pubs, is just a five-minute walk away.

The Wensleydale Creamery is just over half an hour’s drive, while the Lake District is also within easy reach for a day of exploration.

Don’t miss: An early evening spent outside as the valley settles into silence and the light softens across the hills. With minimal light pollution, Dentdale offers exceptional stargazing, best enjoyed from a hot tub in The Plough or wrapped in a blanket on the decking beneath a vast night sky..
