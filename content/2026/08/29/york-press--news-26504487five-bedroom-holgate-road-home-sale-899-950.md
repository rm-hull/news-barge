## ‘Elegant’ period home hits the market in York for more than £890,000 – look inside

Inside the five-bedroom home for sale in Holgate Road, York, for £899,950 *(Image: Savills)*

An “elegant” period townhouse has hit the market in York – for £899,950.

The five-bedroom home is for sale in Holgate Road.

Savills, which is marketing the property, said it “seamlessly combines contemporary open-plan living with elegant period features, creating a stylish and highly versatile family home”.

  The five-bedroom home for sale in Holgate Road, York, for £899,950 

*(Image: Savills)*

Inside, a tiled entrance hall leads to a front sitting room with a bay window and fireplace.

Double doors lead to the family room which opens into the kitchen and dining room.

The extended kitchen and dining space has a breakfast bar, quality worktops, wine cooler and range cooker.

Bi-fold doors from the kitchen open directly onto the rear garden, “making this an ideal space for entertaining and relaxed family living”, according to Savills.

Upstairs, the main bedroom and another bedroom, both served by a shower room, are on the first floor.

Three more bedrooms are arranged over the second floor and share a family bathroom.

Savills’ property agent, Tanya Coffey, said: “I particularly love the way this home balances its period charm with modern family living.

“The stunning kitchen and dining space is undoubtedly the heart of the house, with the roof lantern and bi-fold doors creating a wonderfully bright and sociable space.

“Combined with the flexible accommodation, beautiful original features and excellent proximity to York city centre.”
