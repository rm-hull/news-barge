A North Yorkshire management consultancy has gone into liquidation, with debts exceeding £30,000.

Mythophonic Ltd of Alne Road, Tollerton, was founded in July 2006 and went into voluntary liquidation on July 16.

Griffin and King of Wallsall were appointed the liquidator.

A report known as a statement of affairs says the company had no assets.

Its debts were unsecured and consisted of £26,144 owed to Lloyds Bank and £6,768 owed to HMRC, giving a total shortfall or deficit of £32,912.92. With the business having issued and shared capital of £200, this left £32,712.91 of debt outstanding, the report added.
