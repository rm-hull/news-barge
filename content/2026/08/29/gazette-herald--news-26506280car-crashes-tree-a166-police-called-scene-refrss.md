A CAR has crashed into a tree on a main route to the Yorkshire coast.

Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called at 7.11am yesterday (Friday, August 28) to the A166 Garrowby Hill close to the village of Bishop Wilton.

  


A service spokesperson said one injured person was rescued from the vehicle by fire crews.


Firefighters were able to make the vehicle and the scene safe, the spokesperson added, and said the incident was left with the police.

The A166 is a principal road connecting York with the Yorkshire Wolds and the coast.

Earlier this week, a five-mile stretch of the A166 between Garrowby Hill and Fridaythorpe was closed by police following another crash.
