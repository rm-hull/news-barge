A brand new bookshop is set to ‘thrill and chill’ in York.

York has an incredible bookshop scene – and it’s about to get even better, as one of the city’s most loved independent bookshops have made a big announcement this week.

Criminally Good Books, founded by Isla Coole, opened in York in March 2024. As its name suggests, it specialises in all things crime – crime fiction, true crime, police procedurals, cosy thrillers, psychological thrillers and more.

Now, Isla and her team are expanding into all things spooky with the brand new horror-themed bookshop: Horrifically Good Books.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/60-goodramgate-york-1000x750.jpeg)

Horrifically Good Books will open at the end of October inside the Grade 1-listed building at 60 Goodramgate, and it is going to be ‘all horror, all the time’.

The bookshop will stock books across all the horror genres, such as gothic and classic horror, spooky reads for younger readers and YA horror, folk horror, translated horror, historical horror, haunted houses and ghost literature, weird horror fiction and cosmic horror, literary horror, and small press and indie titles.

The team shared on social media that ‘York is a city built for ghost stories, so it was only a matter of time’ – and as York is reportedly the UK’s most haunted city, it most certainly is.

The news has received an extremely positive response on social media, with other York and North Yorkshire bookshops chiming in on the announcement.

Toppings York commented ‘This is AMAZING’; Poetry Pharmacy on Coney Street said ‘Exciting!’; Books and Bevs in Acomb enthused ‘So excited for this!!!’; and Kemps in Malton said ‘Amazing news Isla!’.

To keep up to date with further coming announcements about Horrifically Good Books, follow their Instagram and Facebook pages.
