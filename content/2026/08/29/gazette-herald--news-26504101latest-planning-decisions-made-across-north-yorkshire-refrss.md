Here are the latest planning applications submitted to North Yorkshire Council.

Seven Leylandii trees at the rear of Wood View on Main Street in Welburn are to be reduced in height by 10 metres.

The application was received on July 29 and has been decided.

The application was received on July 29 and has been decided.

Ref. No: ZE26/00650/DNO | Received: Wed 29 Jul 2026 | Validated: Wed 29 Jul 2026 | Status: Decided

Ref. No: ZE26/00634/CAT | Received: Mon 27 Jul 2026 | Validated: Mon 27 Jul 2026 | Status: Decided

Ref. No: ZE26/00624/HN | Received: Thu 23 Jul 2026 | Validated: Thu 23 Jul 2026 | Status: Decided
