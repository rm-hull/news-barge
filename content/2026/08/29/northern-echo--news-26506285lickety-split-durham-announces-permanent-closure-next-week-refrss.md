An ice cream shop has paid a heartfelt goodbye to customers after announcing it will close its doors permanently next week.

Lickety Split Durham, which opened on Belmont Business Park in 2018, says it will serve its last cones and tubs on Wednesday, September 2.

  Lickety Split Durham 

*(Image: GOOGLE)*

In a statement online, Lickety Split thanked its customers, saying " it has been an absolute pleasure serving you all".

The brand also has a branch in Seaham, which is not closing, with the owners saying: "Our Seaham parlour remains open and we have an exciting announcement coming soon as we head into the tough winter season."


The business added: "After many happy years, it’s time for us to say a very difficult goodbye.

"We’re incredibly sad to announce that our ice cream parlour will be permanently closing from Wednesday, September 2.
