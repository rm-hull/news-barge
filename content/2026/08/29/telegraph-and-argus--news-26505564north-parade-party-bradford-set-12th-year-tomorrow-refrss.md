A free music festival and street party is set to take over the city centre this weekend.

Bradford’s North Parade Street Party returns on Sunday - promising a full day of live entertainment, food, and community celebration from 12pm to 9pm.

This year will be the 12th in its current formation.

North Parade will be transformed into a festival space featuring music, street food, and performances - a tribute to the street’s independent character.

   

*(Image: Philip Lickley)*

James Brunskill, owner of Boar and Fable and part of the organising team, said: "A free music festival in Bradford city centre - who else is doing that?"

He told the T&A that the event is not council-led and is arranged by local businesses and supporters of North Parade.

The street party has become a fixture of the August Bank Holiday period in Bradford, attracting thousands of visitors annually.

   

*(Image: Philip Lickley)*

This year’s line-up includes a by Bradford band The Fourth Act and Natural Rhythm.

Mr Brunskill said the street party "offers something different" and hopes the event will draw crowds despite the "dreadful weather" expected for the weekend.

He clarified that the event should "avoid the worst of it".

Entry is free and the event is open to all ages.
