Artisan stalls and handmade treasures return to the moors this weekend as an artisan market makes its latest stop in Thirsk.

The event, curated by Little Bird Made, will be held at the Sutton Bank National Park Centre on Saturday, August 29, and offers visitors the chance to browse a variety of crafts, gifts and treats in a scenic setting.

Stalls will feature hand-poured candles, ceramics, jewellery, homeware, artwork and freshly baked food.

Jackie Crozier, managing director and founder of Little Bird Made, said: "We’re so excited to be returning to Sutton Bank National Park Centre for our second market of 2026, especially over the August bank holiday weekend.

"It’s such a special location and the perfect spot for families, dog walkers, and day-trippers to come together, support brilliant small businesses, and make a full day of it in the North York Moors."

As well as the market, visitors can enjoy the area’s panoramic views, dog-friendly walking trails and nearby glider centre.

Carly Frame, owner of Wild Spore, added: "Little Bird Markets have been a fantastic platform to launch our small business from.

"Their professional yet friendly approach has enabled us to build a strong customer base in various locations and allowed our business to grow.

"We can’t wait to see you on Saturday at Sutton Bank.

"Come and explore the stalls, shop and enjoy the views."

Those interested in booking a stall at future Little Bird Made markets can email [info@littlebirdmade.co.uk](mailto:info@littlebirdmade.co.uk) or visit www.littlebirdmade.com.
