Discover the real Chinese heroine that inspired Disney’s animation and live-feature *Mulan* in show coming to York.

Next month, on **Friday 11 and Saturday 12 September**,* The Ballad of Mulan* comes to York Theatre Royal’s Studio – telling the story of the real woman behind the legendary Chinese female warrior who disguised as a man to join the Empire’s army.

Woman, warrior, legend. For ten years Mulan disguised as a man, has fought for the Chinese Empire. Now the fighting is coming to an end, one last battle and she will be going home – but can she return to her old life, become a woman again in a search for identity in a violent world.

Commissioned by and performed by West Yorkshire-born British-Chinese actress Michelle Yim, the show was developed with the support and funding from Arts Council England.

Produced by the team behind Edfringe Sell-Out The Unknown Soldier Grist to the Mill, in collaboration with British East Asian theatre company Red Dragonfly Productions.

Tickets for *The Ballad of Mulan* are £21 and available via the York Theatre Royal website.
