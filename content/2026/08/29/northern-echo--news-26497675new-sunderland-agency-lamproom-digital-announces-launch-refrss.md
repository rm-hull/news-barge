An award-winning North East marketer has launched a new search agency in Sunderland, driven by the ambition to create jobs and opportunities in the city.

Amy Elise Mercer, named one of Management Today's 35 Women Under 35 in 2025, co-founded Lamproom Digital with SEO specialist Frederico Cardoso.

Based in Sunderland, the new agency aims to contribute to the region’s growth and demonstrate the potential of North East talent on a national and international stage.

  Amy Elise Mercer 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Ms Mercer said: "Lamproom wasn't created around a business plan.

"It was built around a shared set of values.

"The name reflects exactly what we want to do: help businesses navigate uncertainty with clarity, confidence and direction."

The agency’s name is inspired by the Davy lamp, invented by Sir Humphry Davy and long associated with the North East’s mining heritage.

The lamp symbolised safety and guidance for miners as they worked in hazardous conditions underground.

Between them, Ms Mercer and Mr Cardoso bring nearly 20 years of experience in search and digital marketing.

They have worked with national and international brands across retail, consumer goods, home interiors and lifestyle sectors, driving growth and increasing online visibility.

Since its launch in March 2026, Lamproom has already secured clients in the food and drink, jewellery, retail and home interiors industries.

The agency was founded after both co-founders previously worked together at North East agency Silverbean.

Ms Mercer said: "I grew up in a community where hard work, resilience and looking out for one another mattered.

"As the business grows, I want it to create careers, opportunities and investment here in the same way opportunities were created for me.

"We're proud to be building a business in Sunderland.

"The North East has always been full of talented and ambitious people, and we want to demonstrate that you don't have to leave the region to build an agency with national and international ambition."

Mr Cardoso, who was born in Porto and moved to the UK in 2016, also sees deep connections between his hometown and Sunderland.

Both cities, he said, are shaped by industry, resilience and craftsmanship.

Ms Mercer hopes Lamproom can help raise the profile of North East talent.
