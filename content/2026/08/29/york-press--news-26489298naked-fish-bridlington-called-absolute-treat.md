## 'Absolute treat' Bridlington fish and chip shop that locals rave about

Yorkshire locals love Naked Fish. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

For those living in East Yorkshire, it is easy to reach the coast for a family day trip.

While it is great relaxing on the beach and paddling in the sea, for many people, the highlight of a trip to the seaside is the fish and chips.

If you are in Bridlington, you might want to head down to Naked Fish that can be found close to the harbour.

Naked Fish is also highly rated by content creator, the Yorkshireman, who said the fish is “tasty” and “fresh”.

  The fish and chip shop offers loads of different kinds of fish and seafood. 

*(Image: Tripadvisor)*

## What is Naked Fish like?

On Naked Fish, the Yorkshireman said: “Located on Bridlington seafront, Naked Fish is definitely a busy chippy that serves up tasty fresh fish coated in a delicious batter, served with a good helping of chips.

“It has rave reviews from locals and visitors alike – so you know you’re in for an absolute treat.

“Whether you like it with a side of peas, gravy or curry sauce, we know you’re going to love it.

“They also serve a range of fish that even includes seabass!”

At Naked Fish, you can enjoy cod, haddock, scampi, seabass and even lobster along with a portion of hot chips.

The North Sea Mix features pieces of white fish from the North Sea that have been covered in the shop’s crispy batter.

The fish and chip shop offers all of the classic sides including mushy peas, curry sauce, tartare sauce, and a chip butty.


There’s a lot of space to sit inside the restaurant in case the weather takes a turn but you could also take it away to sit by the sea.
