## Liverpool

**Alisson**: Well beaten for Dan Ndoye's goal but made a superb save to deny Igor Jesus on the brink of half-time and keep Liverpool in the game. Poor penalty to give away for Gibbs-White's winner.** 5Jeremie Frimpong**: Struggled defensively but got into some good positions going forward. Did really well in the build-up to Florian Wirtz's disallowed goal but was just offside. Has to improve consistency with his end delivery.** 6Jeremy Jacquet**: Made a goal-saving challenge to deny Igor Jesus an early goal and was impressive but still had a few moments where he was slack and lacked discipline.** 6Virgil van Dijk**: Will be furious with how easily the Liverpool backline was cut open for Ndoye's opener and the ease with which Forest won their penalty.** 6Milos Kerkez**: A poor display from the Liverpool left-back and was sloppy in possession. Replaced by Kostas Tsimikas in the 71st minute.** 5Dominik Szoboszlai**: Solid as usual and was much more alert defensively, compared to his display at Newcastle.** 6Alexis Mac Allister**: Back in the starting XI and played a lovely reverse ball for Cody Gakpo early on. Couldn't cope physically with Igor Jesus in the build up to Ndoye's goal.** 6Cody Gakpo**: Started down the right in what was potentially his last Liverpool appearance and was effective. Played a lovely ball for Wirtz, then did really well to create a chance for Victor Munoz. Didn't do his defensive duties for Forest's opener but did brilliantly to create Alexander Isak's equaliser.** 7Florian Wirtz**: Constantly posed a threat and unlucky not to score, having had a neat finish ruled out as Frimpong was offside and a solid header tipped over by Matz Sels. Got the assist for Munoz's goal.** 7Victor Munoz**: Made his first Liverpool start and it was down his preferred left flank. The Spaniard was direct and had a penalty shout turned down but he should have tested Sels at least when he fired a first-half effort wide from inside the box. Scored a belter to level the game in front of the Kop and rescue a point for Liverpool.** 7Alexander Isak**: Just six touches in the first half and zero shots on goal but made up for a forgettable opening 45 minutes with the first equaliser on the hour mark. That was just his ninth touch but a goal that will give him plenty of confidence.** 6Subs:Tsimikas (on 71, Kerkez):5Gravenberch (on 71, Frimpong): 5Ngumoha (on 71, Gakpo): 5Araujo (on 77, Jacquet): 5Nyoni (on 77, Mac Allister): 5Subs not used:** Mamardashvili, Endo, McConnell, Koumas.

## Nottingham Forest

**Matz Sels**: Was commanding and it was his punt up the field that led to Ndoye's goal. Made a great save to deny Wirtz but was well-beaten by Gakpo's cross for Isak's leveller and had no chance with Munoz's strike.** 5Jair Cunha**: Strong performance but will know he should have done better for Isak's goal.** 6Nikola Milenkovic**: Part of a Forest backline that initially made it incredibly hard for Liverpool to break them down.** 6Murillo**: Unafraid to drive with the ball and put in a solid shift but was well beaten by Munoz for the goal that made it 2-2. Still crucial that Forest keep him.** 6Ola Aina**: Relentless all game. Was very involved, as always, and constantly asked questions of Liverpool. Nearly created a goal for Forest.** 7James McAtee**: Getting his chance after Elliot Anderson's move to Manchester City and was very tidy on the ball in an impressive performance. Made some really good deliveries in the first half and battled well all afternoon.** 7Xaver Schlager**: The summer signing came in for the injured Ibrahim Sangare and bar getting nutmegged early on by Dominik Szoboszlai, this was a good display from the Austrian midfielder.** 7Neco Williams**: Like Aina, was full of energy down the opposite flank. Made a superb tackle early on to deny Gakpo. Was once on the Anfield books, and it was interesting to hear Steven Gerrard say pre-match that Liverpool should have kept him. Had a few defensive lapses but ultimately won the penalty that put Forest ahead for the second time.** 7Dan Ndoye**: A wonderful finish past Alisson in the first half for what was his first Premier League goal in 377 days.** 7Morgan Gibbs-White**: Quality on the ball and got the assist for Ndoye before dispatching his second-half penalty in style. Loves Anfield and how has five goal involvements (three goals, two assists) there. No Englishman has netted more Premier League goals overall this calendar year than his 13.** 8Igor Jesus**: Had a great chance early on and his hold up play was brilliant for Ndoye's goal. Caused a nuisance for Liverpool's centre-backs all afternoon.** 7Subs:Delap (on 66, Ndoye):6Netz (on 86, Aina): 5Hudson-Odoi (on 90+2, McAtee): 5Dominguez (on 90+2, Igor Jesus): 5Subs not used:** Victor, Diomande, Wood, Yates, Abbott.
