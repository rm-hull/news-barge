A CAR has caught fire on a main road in a coastal North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Scarborough were called to Scalby Road in the town at 11.41am today (Saturday, August 29).


A service spokesperson said: "Crews used one hose reel jet and one breathing apparatus to extinguish the fire.

"Police were in attendance at the scene for traffic safety."
