A North Yorkshire food company has announced a new twist on the classic Cumberland sausage.

HECK! has launched new Chicken Cumberland Sausages, aimed at people looking for a high-protein and leaner alternative to pork sausages.

The new Chicken Cumberland Sausages, which will be available nationwide at Morrisons and Tesco stores from mid-September, are made with lean chicken and a blend of bold black pepper, fragrant sage and warming spice notes.

  



Weighing 340g and priced at £3.50, the sausages are designed to be a versatile addition to various meals, from mash and sausage sandwiches to stir-fries and tray bakes.

The new product is part of HECK!'s expanding range of lean sausages, burgers, seasoned mince, and sausage rashers.

These products are aimed at people wanting to boost their protein intake without compromising on flavour.

All items in the HECK! chicken range are gluten-free and naturally lower in fat.
