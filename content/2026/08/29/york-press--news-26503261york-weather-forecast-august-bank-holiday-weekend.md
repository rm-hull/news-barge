The Met Office has forecast a wet bank holiday weekend in York.

Saturday will start off dry and bright, with temperatures around 16C.

The morning will see partly cloudy skies, with sunny intervals developing towards midday.

Rain is very unlikely, with a probability below 5 per cent, and winds will be light at 3–7 mph.

The early afternoon will remain pleasant, with sunny intervals and temperatures climbing to about 19C by 5pm.

However, cloud cover will gradually increase, signalling the approaching rain.

The probability of rain will rise from below 5 per cent around lunchtime to 10 per cent at 4pm, 30 per cent at 5pm, and 60 per cent by 6pm, and winds will remain light.

Conditions will deteriorate quickly in the evening.

Light rain will develop around 6–7pm, with the probability reaching 90 per cent by 7pm and at least 95 per cent from around 8pm.

Heavy rain will then become the dominant weather through the evening.

Temperatures will hover around 18–19C initially, falling towards 17C later.

Winds will remain light at around 4–6 mph, so the main issue will be the rainfall rather than wind.

Heavy rain will continue into the late evening before becoming somewhat lighter towards midnight.

Temperatures will fall towards 16C, with rain still highly likely.

Expect wet roads and the possibility of standing water where rainfall is persistent.

Sunday will see brief brighter spells, but showers will become widespread again.

Saturday's rain will continue into Sunday, although it will gradually become lighter.

Expect light rain or showers, with the probability falling from around 70 per cent at midnight to 50 per cent towards dawn.

Temperatures will remain mild at 15–16C, with very light winds of roughly 3–5 mph.

The morning will see a temporary improvement, starting cloudy at around 15C, but sunny intervals should develop from roughly 9am.

Temperatures will respond by climbing to 17C at 10am and 18C around midday.

Shower chances will remain at roughly 30–40 per cent, so it isn't guaranteed to stay completely dry.

Temperatures will reach around 20C between 2pm and 4pm, but the weather will become increasingly showery.

There could already be a light shower around 1–3pm, before heavier showers become increasingly likely from about 4pm.

Rain probability will rise to around 80 per cent by late afternoon, and winds will remain light at only 4–5 mph.

A wet evening is likely, with heavy showers followed by heavy rain.

Rain probabilities will remain around 80 per cent for much of the evening.

Temperatures will steadily fall from approximately 18C at 6pm to 16C by 9–11pm.

Showers may become somewhat lighter late at night, but they will remain likely.

Monday will be much improved, with longer dry and sunny periods.

Expect sunny intervals for much of the morning, with temperatures rising steadily from around 14C at 6–7am to 18C by 11am.

There is still a 30–40 per cent chance of a shower, but much of the morning should be drier and brighter than Sunday.

Winds will gradually strengthen from around 5 mph to 7 mph.

A few showers are possible around lunchtime and early afternoon, with the rain probability briefly reaching 50–60 per cent around 12–1pm.

After that, conditions will improve again, with sunny intervals expected from around 2pm onwards.

Temperatures will peak near 20C at 2pm, then remain around 19C through much of the afternoon.

Winds will become noticeably breezier, reaching around 10–13 mph.

Monday evening looks substantially drier, with sunny intervals continuing initially before skies become partly cloudy after sunset.

The chance of rain will fall from about 20 per cent at 6–7pm to just 10 per cent from around 8pm onwards.

Temperatures will gradually decline from 18C at 6pm to around 14C by 11pm.

Winds will also ease from around 13 mph to 7 mph.

Mostly dry and partly cloudy conditions are indicated for the night, with temperatures continuing to cool and a much lower likelihood of rain than during the previous two nights.
