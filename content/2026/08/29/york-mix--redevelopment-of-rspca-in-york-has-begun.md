**The RSPCA in York has begun redeveloping a rescue shelter**.

York Animal Home on Landing Lane in Clifton is the city’s main cattery and puppy unit, but the site near the River Ouse is prone to flooding.

Demolition work has already started and the buildings will be replaced with modern facilities as part of the £5m project.

Money is currently being raised for the site and another £800,000 is needed from donations to continue its work.

It has been the place generations of York families have gone to to adopt dogs and cats since it opened in 1980.

The first phase of the redevelopment is a new cattery with improved welfare provision.

They are offering supporters the chance to sponsor a brick on the walls of the new cattery or puppy unit for £50.
