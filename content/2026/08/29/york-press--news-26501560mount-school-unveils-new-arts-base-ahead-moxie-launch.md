## First look: York school unveils new dance studio as arts hub prepares to open

The Mount School pupils in its new dance studio. Photo by Duncan Lomax

  *The Mount School in York unveils new arts facilities ahead of cultural hub Moxie opening later this year in Holgate, reports MAXINE GORDON*

A YORK private school is celebrating a major investment in its creative facilities, unveiling a new performing arts studio and design technology suite as a neighbouring arts hub prepares to open.

The Mount School, in Dalton Terrace, says the developments will help strengthen Holgate's growing reputation as a centre for arts and culture.

The York independent school has unveiled a purpose-built performing arts studio featuring a new dance studio for pupils, alongside art rooms and a state-of-the-art design technology suite.

  The Mount School pupils in its new dance studio. Photo by Duncan Lomax


The developments come as The Moxie Centre for Art prepares to open next door in a former Mount School building, creating what school leaders hope will become a thriving creative hub for the wider community.

Among those benefiting from the new facilities are The Mount's successful dance students. Earlier this year the school's dance club was crowned regional winners of The Great Big Dance Off, while four pupils qualified for the Dance World Cup.

One of them, Year 7 pupil Connie, returned from the international competition with two world championship gold medals.

  The Mount School pupils in its new dance studio. Photo by Duncan Lomax


Head of The Mount, Anna Wilby, said: "We are delighted that The Moxie Centre for Art opens soon, hot on the heels of our new dance studio, DT suite and preschool," she said.

"Our values align perfectly with the visionary team at The Moxie and we look forward to collaborating on both community and school art events.

"We already run jewellery, dance, photography, sewing, acting and ceramics clubs in school, which will provide perfect opportunities for partnership working."

The Mount is celebrating a successful year in which it was named Primary School of the Year at the My Local Awards and shortlisted for Small Independent School of the Year.

  The Mount School in Dalton Terrace. Image: Supplied


The neighbouring Moxie Centre for Art will add another dimension to Holgate's emerging creative scene.

As exclusively revealed in The Press, York-based couple Lucy and Chris Haltiner are shortly to submit a planning application to transform a former Mount School building into a not-for-profit arts centre featuring a free public gallery, artist studios, photography facilities, workshop spaces and a community cafe.

  Lucy and Chris Haltiner - founders of the Moxie Centre for Art in York


Revealing their plans exclusively to The Press, the couple said the centre had been designed to fill a gap in York's arts provision by bringing exhibition, education, creative production and community facilities together under one roof.

Chris said York had a wealth of talented artists but a shortage of studio space, with many existing facilities fully occupied and long waiting lists in place.

"The Moxie isn't just about a space to work and create art," he said. "It's also about a space where people meet: artists, visitors, collectors, someone who came in just for a coffee.

  Lucy and Chris Haltiner - founders of the Moxie Centre for Art in York


"That's where conversations start, and conversations are where ideas, inspiration and new art come from."

Artists and community groups interested in using The Moxie's facilities can register their interest through the centre's website (themoxie.co.uk).

Meanwhile, prospective families will have an opportunity to see The Mount's new facilities for themselves during its open day on Saturday, October 3.
