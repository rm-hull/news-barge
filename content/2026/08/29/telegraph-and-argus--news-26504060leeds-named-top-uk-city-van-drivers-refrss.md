LEEDS has been named one of the best places in the UK for van drivers.

The city ranked fourth in a new study by MoneySuperMarket, which analysed 222 cities based on congestion, parking, fuel access, fees, and disruption.

Leeds earned a score of 6.2 out of 10, with the absence of congestion charges, low-emission zones, and toll fees contributing to its high ranking.

Alicia Hempsted, van insurance expert at MoneySuperMarket, said: "Parking availability and congestion fees, and the risk of unexpected roadworks on your route, are all complications that can make a van driver’s journey more stressful.

"Whether you’re a delivery driver or heading to a job site, the city you drive your van in can make a huge difference to your journey, and our new research highlights just how different one city can be to the next."

Cardiff topped the UK rankings with a score of 7.1, followed by Belfast, Manchester, and Leeds.
