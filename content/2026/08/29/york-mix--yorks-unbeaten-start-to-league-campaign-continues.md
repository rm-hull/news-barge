York City FC continue to be unbeaten with a 2-1 win over Exeter City in League Two.

Alex Newby’s first-half opener was added to by debutant Jovan Malcolm, who joined on loan from Stevenage earlier this week.

The visitors had not won on the road since January and with their key players, Jayden Wareham and Luca Woodhouse left out of the squad amid transfer speculation, they continued to be out of luck.

Malcolm marked his York debut with a goal**.**
