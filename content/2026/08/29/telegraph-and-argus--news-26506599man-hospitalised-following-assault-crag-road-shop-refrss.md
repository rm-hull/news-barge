A man in his 30s was taken to hospital with facial injuries after being assaulted in what is believed to be a targeted attack.

Police have confirmed that they were called at 11.27am to a report that a man had been assaulted inside a convenience store on Crag Road, Shipley yesterday (August 28).

They are now appealing for witnesses.

## 'A targeted attack'

A spokesperson for the force said: "This is understood to have been a targeted attack."

Enquiries remain ongoing.

Anyone who witnessed the incident is asked to contact Bradford CID quoting crime reference 13260490987.
