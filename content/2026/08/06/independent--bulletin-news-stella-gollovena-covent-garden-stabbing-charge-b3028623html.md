Multiple people stabbed in Covent Garden

Stella Gollovena, 47, has been charged after four men were injured in a stabbing attack in central London.

The incident took place on Endell Street in Covent Garden on Wednesday, with officers called around 12:30 pm.

Gollovena faces four counts of wounding with intent to cause grievous bodily harm and one count of possession of an offensive weapon.

The four male victims, aged 34, 39, 42, and 56, sustained stab wounds but have since been discharged from hospital as their injuries were not life-threatening.

Gollovena, of no fixed address, has been remanded in custody to appear at Highbury Corner Magistrates’ Court.
