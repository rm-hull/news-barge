A 20-year-old man has appeared before York Magistrates Court charged with burgling a guesthouse in the city.

Finlay Drinkhill, of no fixed address, is accused of entering the hotel in Clifton as a trespasser late on July 17 and stealing two electric bikes worth together £1,160 from inside the building.

He denied a charge of burglary and his case was listed for trial later this year.

Magistrates remanded him in custody and adjourned his case until August 11 when he will again appear before the same court.
