A gymnast who has trained at Harrogate Gymnastics for the past decade is preparing to take her sporting and academic career overseas after being awarded a scholarship to study and compete in Alaska.

Georgina Gibson, aged 19, has been awarded a, multi-year athletic scholarship to the University of Alaska Anchorage in the United States, where she will study Kinesiology, the science of human movement, exercise and physical performance, while continuing her gymnastics career.

Georgina, from Thorner, near Leeds, has trained at Harrogate Gymnastics for 10 years and will leave for the USA on 16 August to begin the next chapter of her journey.

The scholarship recognises many years of commitment, training and achievement, and will allow Georgina to continue competing in Women’s Artistic Gymnastics while studying at university.



Georgina said:I feel so honoured to receive a full scholarship to compete in the sport I have loved since I was five. Some people might feel nervous about moving to the other side of the world, especially somewhere as cold as Alaska, but there is no place I would rather choose. It feels like the perfect fit, not only because of the scenery, but because of the culture of the gymnastics team and the university’s academic reputation.

When I first set out to achieve this goal three years ago, it felt like an enormous task, but I was never told I couldn’t do it. I was shown that it was possible if I was willing to work hard.

There was a lot of work to do and a lot of skills to master, but having my coaches, friends and family believe in me made all the difference. There is no club I would rather have grown up in and I will always be grateful for everything Harrogate Gymnastics has given me. We are more like family than a team, and I will be proud to represent the club as I begin this exciting new chapter.




Georgina’s parents, Richard and Hayley Gibson, said:We are truly proud of what Georgina has achieved. Her journey in gymnastics, like so many, has been a mixture of cheers and tears, but the one constant has been her determination to succeed.

We knew selection would be tough and that very few places would be available for British gymnasts, but Georgina simply never gave up. In typical G style, she managed to secure an offer from the university she had chosen as her number one choice.

Although we will miss her, we are so excited for what lies ahead. We would also like to thank Chloe Carey and all the coaches at Harrogate Gymnastics for the dedication they have shown and the environment they have created. Since joining the club in 2016, Georgina has never once said she did not want to go to training. That says everything about the club’s culture. We know it will hold her in good stead for her new team in Alaska. Go Seawolves!




![Chloe Carey, Harrogate Gymnastics](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Chloe-Harrogate-gymnastic.jpg)



Chloe Carey, founder of Harrogate Gymnastics, said:We are incredibly proud of Georgina. This scholarship is a fantastic achievement and reflects years of hard work, commitment and passion for the sport.

To see a gymnast who has trained with us for 10 years preparing to continue her gymnastics career in Alaska is something the whole club can celebrate. It is a huge step for Georgina, both academically and athletically, and we are excited to see what she goes on to achieve.

At Harrogate Gymnastics, we want young people to have choice, whether they enjoy gymnastics recreationally, build confidence and skills, or progress into competitive pathways. Georgina’s story is a brilliant example of where that passion and commitment can lead.



Georgina began gymnastics aged five and joined Harrogate Gymnastics in 2016. She competes in Women’s Artistic Gymnastics and has competed at the British Championships twice and the English Championships five times.

She currently trains 20 hours a week and is coached by Chloe Carey.

Harrogate Gymnastics is based at The Zone, Hornbeam Park, Harrogate, and provides gymnastics opportunities for children, young people and adults across Harrogate and the surrounding area, from early years and recreational classes through to competitive squad training.
