The Grand Tour’s Clarkson, Hammond and May replacements take centre stage in new trailer

The Grand Tourhas placed its new presenting trio – social media star Francis Bourgeois and YouTubers Thomas Holland and James Engelsman – into the spotlight in the reboot's first teaser.

The upcoming series will see the new team tackle motoring's most absurd questions across challenging terrains, including crossing the Angolan desert and exploring Malaysia's car culture.

The trailer hints at Francis Bourgeois, known for his viral trainspotting videos, bringing his infectious enthusiasm to the show. Holland and Engelsman are the duo behind the popular automotive YouTube channel Throttle House.

All six new episodes of The Grand Tour are scheduled to launch on Prime Video on 4 September, with the original trio expected to make a cameo appearance.
