Visitors can now take home a quirky piece of Georgian York.

A new papercraft tissue box kit designed by Helen Reynolds to resemble York Mansion House is now on sale, following a prototype created for last year's York Georgian Festival.

York Mansion House, overlooking St Helen’s Square, is one of the city’s most distinctive Georgian buildings and a central part of the festival, which runs from August 6 to 10.

Ms Reynolds, founder of Viscount Folly, said: "I created a prototype last year to grace the building’s hallway during the Festival.

"For 2026, the Mansion House team offered to sell them as kits on one condition – I update the door colour, as it had since been repainted in its signature red."

As well as tours and themed souvenirs, the house is offering cooking demonstrations, talks, and fan language workshops during the event.
