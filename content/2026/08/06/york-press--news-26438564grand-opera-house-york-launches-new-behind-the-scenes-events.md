A theatre is inviting the public to experience the magic of storytelling and performance through a new series of behind-the-scenes events.

The Grand Opera House York says the programme is designed to engage the community in fresh and immersive ways, offering creative workshops, historical tours, and family activities in the lead-up to the venue’s 125th anniversary in 2027.

Grand Opera House Theatre Director Allie Long said: "As we look ahead to celebrating 125 years of the Grand Opera House York in 2027, we want to invite our community to experience the theatre like never before.

"We are incredibly proud to welcome people through our doors, not just to enjoy world-class performances, but to discover the history and creativity that make this building so special.

"These events are about bringing people together and making our theatre an even more welcoming place for everyone.

"Whether you're visiting for the first time or you've been coming for years, we hope you'll find something to inspire and connect you with this remarkable venue."

The Grand Opera House has played a central role in York’s cultural life for more than a century, hosting an array of performances from musicals and comedy to drama and dance.

Full details are available at www.atgtickets.com/york.
