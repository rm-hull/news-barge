Ugandan footballer David Owori, captain of Premier League side SC Villa and a national team player, has died after being fatally assaulted by a street gang in a suspected robbery on Monday evening near his home in Makindye, Kampala.

Assailants reportedly threw paving stones at Owori as he resisted the robbery, stealing his belongings and leaving him unconscious before he later died from his injuries at a nearby clinic.

Tributes have poured in from the football community, including SC Villa and the Federation of Uganda Football Associations (Fufa), who praised Owori as an “inspiration leader and role model”.

SC Villa and Fufa have called for urgent justice, with public anger mounting over the high level of violent crime in Kampala, following a similar fatal attack on rugby player Sydney Gongodyo weeks prior.

Uganda's Parliament held a minute's silence in Owori's honour and called for an investigation, with police detectives currently working to identify the perpetrators of the crime.
