A York-based charity event has announced the remaining acts for its annual night of entertainment, shining a light on men's mental health through music and conversation.

Menfulness Live, organised by York-based mental health charity Menfulness, will be held at the Grand Opera House on August 29 and will feature live music, guest speakers, and open conversations about men's wellbeing.

The line-up now includes local singer and Britain’s Got Talent star Finley Barrett Carter, along with guest speakers such as award-winning memoirist Adam Farrer and poet Lauren Martin.

Multi-platinum-selling singer-songwriter JP Cooper, best known for his hit singles September Song and Perfect Strangers, was announced as the evening’s headline guest, sharing his personal story and performing stripped-back tracks in an intimate setting.

Mr Cooper said: "Menfulness have been chatting with me for a while.

"I've been watching the work that they've been doing, and it's incredible bringing fellas together to talk openly about things that fellas don't talk about enough.

"So I'll be chatting a little bit, singing a little bit, and I'm looking forward to seeing you all there."

The evening will blend live music, personal testimony, humour and storytelling in support of Menfulness’ mission.

Menfulness trustee Matty Lewis said: "Menfulness Live has become a key part of our calendar, showcasing the charity to new and existing people wanting to hear more about the work we do in men's mental health.

"Owing to our amazing partnership with the Grand Opera House, we were blessed to be able to use them as our location for the 2026 event, and wanting to do the venue justice, we thought we would ask our friend JP Cooper to come along and feature on the night."

The event aims to raise awareness for the charity’s work, which centres on creating a welcoming community where men can socialise, exercise, and talk openly in a relaxed, non-judgmental environment.

Mr Cooper’s appearance marks his first return to York since his 2023 show at the York Barbican.

A limited-time promotion offers all ticket holders the chance to win VIP lounge access, including complimentary food and drink for the evening.

To enter, supporters can forward their ticket confirmation to [matty@menfulness.org.](mailto:matty@menfulness.org.)

Tickets can be purchased through the ATG Tickets website: [https://www.atgtickets.com/shows/menfulness-live/grand-opera-house-york/](https://www.atgtickets.com/shows/menfulness-live/grand-opera-house-york/)

Mr Lewis said the event is intended to do more than just entertain, but to start conversations and reach more men who could benefit from support.

He said the charity is hopeful that with a full house, the night will help bring its message to a wider audience and continue fostering connections in the community.
