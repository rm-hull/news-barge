Farmer Christopher Wright, 73, has been sentenced to a minimum of 35 years in prison for the murder and dismemberment of Richard Dyson.

Wright, a former butcher, was convicted last month of killing Mr Dyson, 55, who vanished in 2019 after living in his car on Wright’s land in Hoyland, Barnsley.

The pair fell out over suspected theft, leading Wright to shoot Mr Dyson in the arm and chest before dismembering his body and encasing it in a concrete-filled barrel.

Mr Dyson’s remains were discovered last year hidden within the barrel, which Wright had placed on a trailer and persuaded an unwitting neighbour to store.

Wright’s childhood friend, Karl Schwalbe, 72, also received a three-year prison sentence for conspiracy to pervert the course of justice for his role in concealing the crime.
