Two boys have been arrested in connection with a suspected motorbike theft.

At about 7.30pm yesterday (Wednesday), police responded to reports of two people wearing face coverings while pushing a motorbike on Watson Street, off Holgate Road.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Following a swift search of the area, officers located two suspects and recovered a motorcycle nearby.”

#### Watson Street, York

Two 13-year-old boys were arrested on suspicion of theft of a motor vehicle.

They have been released on conditional bail while enquiries continue.
