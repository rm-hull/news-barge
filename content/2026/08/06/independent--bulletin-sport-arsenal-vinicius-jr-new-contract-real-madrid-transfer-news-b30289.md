# Arsenal dealt major blow in pursuit of Real Madrid’s Vinicius Jr

![Arsenal had been keen to sign Vinicius, who had only one year remaining on his previous contract](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/05/19/01KZ6P8DQCH47DWFY63NHHE8VW.jpg)

- Vinicius Jr has signed a contract extension with Real Madrid until 2032, effectively ending Arsenal's hopes of acquiring the Brazilian forward.
- Arsenal had been keen to sign Vinicius, who had only one year remaining on his previous contract, amidst initial difficulties in his contract negotiations with Real Madrid.
- Real Madrid subsequently improved their offer to secure the extension, which was the preferred outcome for both the club and the player.
- Vinicius Jr has been a crucial player for Real Madrid, having won 14 titles, including two Champions Leagues and three La Liga titles, during his eight-year spell.
- Following the Vinicius development, Arsenal are now reportedly close to signing his Brazil teammate Bruno Guimaraes from Newcastle.
