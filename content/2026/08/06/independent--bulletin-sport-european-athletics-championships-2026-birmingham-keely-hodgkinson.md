# When are the European Athletics Championships 2026 – and how can I watch?

- The European Athletics Championships are set to take place in Birmingham's Alexander Stadium from 10-16 August, marking the first time the event has been held in the UK.
- Europe's top athletics stars will compete for continental titles and potential records, just a week after the conclusion of the Commonwealth Games.
- Key athletes include Olympic 800m champion Keely Hodgkinson, who faces fierce competition, and reigning European 100m champion Dina Asher-Smith.
- The event will also feature 10 reigning world champions, such as pole vaulter Mondo Duplantis, and Olympic 5,000m champion Jakob Ingebrigtsen, returning from injury.
- Live coverage of the European Athletics Championships will be broadcast on the BBC and BBC iPlayer.
