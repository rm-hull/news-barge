# Could the Strait of Hormuz reopen today? What we know as deal enters ‘final stages’

- Iran has stated that an agreement with Oman regarding shipping through the Strait of Hormuz is in its final stages of negotiation.
- The proposed deal would grant Tehran control over vessels entering the Gulf via the Strait of Hormuz, according to a senior Iranian source and regional officials.
- Donald Trump mentioned an 'all-day negotiation' with Iran, characterising the talks positively while simultaneously threatening severe repercussions if an agreement was not reached.
- Iran warned its Gulf neighbours that any new US attack would trigger retaliation against critical energy infrastructure across the region.
- Earlier, Iran's foreign ministry indicated an understanding had been reached with Oman concerning the opening of a shipping route through the Strait of Hormuz, with a joint announcement being finalised.
