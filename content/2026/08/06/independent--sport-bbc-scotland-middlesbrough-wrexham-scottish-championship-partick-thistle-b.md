The Carabao Cup first round continues on Friday with Middlesbrough’s clash against Championship rivals Wrexham taking centre stage.

In The Hundred, Birmingham Phoenix host Sunrisers Leeds in the men’s and women’s competitions.

A top-of-the-table meeting between Wakefield Trinity and Leeds Rhinos headlines Friday’s Betfred Super League fixtures, while there is further action in the Canadian Open and Tour de France Women.

Here the Press Association takes a look at Friday’s sporting action and where you can watch in the UK.

## Carabao Cup continues

The **Carabao Cup** first round continues on Friday night with three ties taking place.

An all-Championship clash between **Middlesbrough** and**Wrexham** headlines the trio, with coverage starting on**Sky Sports Main Event** and**Sky Sports Football** from**1900** and on**ITV1** from**1930**.

**Sky Sports +** will be showing two other fixtures from**1945**, with** Wolves**hosting** Port Vale**and** Wycombe**taking on fellow League One side** Stevenage**.

The **Scottish Championship** also continues on Friday as**Livingston** look to continue their winning start to life back in the league with a clash against**Partick Thistle**, who also won their opening game at the weekend.** BBC Scotland**are showing the contest from** 1930**.

## Can the Phoenix rise?

![Both Phoenix’s men’s and women’s teams are searching for a win in the Hundred (Bradley Collyer/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/06/15/1eb70f186e689ebd3eb7085c48ec4021Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg2MDk4NDEy-2.81240934.jpg)

There is further action in The Hundred with **Birmingham Phoenix** hosting**Sunrisers Leeds** in the men’s and women’s competitions at Edgbaston.

**Phoenix Women** only have one win from five outings in the tournament so far and they come up against a**Sunrisers** team who beat London Spirit on Tuesday for their second success this season.

**Phoenix** are bottom of the men’s table with a sole victory which came against Trent Rockets in their opening game of the competition and**Sunrisers** will be searching for back-to-back triumphs following their win over London Spirit earlier in the week.

Coverage of **Phoenix Women** against**Sunrisers** takes place on**Sky Sports Cricket at 1445** and**Sky Sports Main Event at 1500**, with the** men’s match**following on** Sky Sports Cricket at 1800**.

## What else is happening on Friday?

![Leaders Leeds Rhinos travel to Wakefield Trinity (Mike Egerton/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/06/15/58b63bdadd9a461b2a4d49242c6a414dY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg2MDk5NjM3-2.84394669.jpg)

A top-of-the-table clash takes place in the **Betfred Super League** as second-placed**Wakefield Trinity** host leaders**Leeds Rhinos** on**Sky Sports Action from 1930**.

**Sky Sports +** are showing the rest of Friday’s**Super League** bill from**2000** as**Castleford Tigers** take on**Hull KR** and**York Knights** travel to**Leigh Leopards**.

Coverage of the **Canadian Open** continues on**Sky Sports Tennis from 1600** and**Sky Sports Main Event at 2300**.

There is **PGA Tour** action with the**Wyndham Championship** taking place on**Sky Sports Golf** at**1145** and**1800**.

In the **Ladies European Tour**, the** PIF London Championship**will be shown on** Sky Sports Golf**from** 1400**.

Stage seven of the **Tour de France Women** also takes place and**TNT Sports 1** will be showing the action from Friday’s route taking place between**La Voulte-sur-Rhone** and**Mont Ventoux** from**1415**.

## On this day in history

![Erling Haaland scored his first goals for Manchester City in 2022 (Kieran Cleeves/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/06/15/eabd9ca7d0d898567fda55d9fe40002dY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg2MDk4Mjg4-2.68217025.jpg)

**1926:** The first British Grand Prix was staged at Brooklands. Known as the RAC Grand Prix, the race was won by Robert Senechal and Louis Wagner, driving their Delage at an average speed of 71.61 miles per hour.

**2012:** On a bumper day for Great Britain at the London Olympics, Chris Hoy won gold in the men’s keirin, his second triumph of the Games and his sixth Olympic gold overall. There were also golds for Alistair Brownlee (men’s triathlon), Laura Bechtolsheimer, Charlotte Dujardin and Carl Hester (team dressage) and Laura Trott (women’s omnium), while Victoria Pendleton was a silver medallist in the women’s sprint.

**2021:** Great Britain’s Galal Yafai claimed Olympic boxing gold with victory over the Philippines’ Carlo Paalam in the men’s flyweight final in Tokyo. Team GB also enjoyed success with Joe Choong in the modern pentathlon.

**2022:** Erling Haaland scored his first Manchester City goals with a brace in a 2-0 Premier League win over West Ham.
