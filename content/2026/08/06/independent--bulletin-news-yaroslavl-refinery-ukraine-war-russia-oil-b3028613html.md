The targeted facilities included a refinery in Bashkortostan, over 1,300 kilometres behind the eastern front line, and another in the Yaroslavl region.

Ukrainian forces also reportedly hit two Russian military patrol boats and vessels from Moscow’s 'shadow fleet' in the Black Sea.

Russia's Defence Ministry claimed its air defences shot down 605 Ukrainian drones overnight across several Russian regions, Crimea, and the Black and Azov seas.

Meanwhile, Russian drone and glide bomb attacks killed at least three civilians and wounded 22 in Ukraine, prompting Zelensky to urge allies for more vital air defence interceptors.
