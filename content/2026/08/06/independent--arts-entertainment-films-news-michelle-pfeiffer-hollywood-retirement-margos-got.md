Michelle Pfeiffer’s 45-year movie career might have come to an end, with the Hollywood star declaring: “I never want to star in another movie.”

The *Batman Returns* actor has been a fixture on the film since her breakout roles in*Grease 2* and*Scarface* in the early 1980s, but the star’s heart now lies in TV, following appearances in*Margo’s Got Money Troubles* and*The Madison*.

Pfeiffer, who has two Oscar nominations to her name, attended a Women of Apple TV event ahead of the forthcoming Emmys, and told her fellow nominees, as per *The Hollywood Reporter*: “I never want to star in another movie, I swear to God. I only want to do ensemble pieces. It’s so much more fun, I love it so much.”

![Michelle Pfeiffer might be calling time on movie career](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/05/11/45/Michelle-Pfeiffer.jpg)

Pfeiffer, 68, was one of the world’s highest-paid stars of the Nineties, helped by two consecutive Oscar nominations for *Dangerous Liaisons* and*The Fabulous Baker Boys* at the tail-end of the 1980s. Following this, she was cast as Catwoman in Tim Burton’s sequel*Batman Returns*(1992)**

Her other key credits include 1980s comedies *The Witches of Eastwick*(1987) and* Married to the Mob*(1988), Martin Scorsese’s Edith Wharton adaptation * The Age of Innocence*(1993), and psychological thriller* What Lies Beneath* (2000), also starring Harrison Ford. 

In 2007, she appeared in *Hairspray* and*Stardust*, following which a 10-year acting hiatus followed before her return in Kenneth Branagh’s* The Murder on the Orient Express*(2017) and Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) sequel* Ant-Man and the Wasp*(2018).

In *Margo’s Got Money Troubles*, created by Pfeiffer’s real-life husband, David E Kelley, she plays the mother of Elle Fanning’s college dropout, who signs up to OnlyFans to support herself and her new baby financially. Nicole Kidman also appears in the comedy show.

Meanwhile, in *The Madison*– from Yellowstone creator Taylor Sheridan – she plays the matriarch of a family who relocate from New York City to a valley in southwest Montana after a tragedy.

![Michelle Pfeiffer in 'Margo's Got Money Troubles'](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/05/11/52/Screenshot-2026-08-05-at-12-15-17.png)

At the Emmys, which is due to take place on 15 September, Pfeiffer is Emmy-nominated in the Best Supporting Actress category for her role in *Margo’s Got Money Troubles* alongside*Widow’s Bay* stars Kate O’Flynn and Dale Dickey, Hannah Einbinder and Megan Stalter (both for*Hacks*), Janelle James (* Abbott Elementary*) and* Shrinking* actor Jessica Williams.

Medical drama *The Pitt* received the most Emmy nominations with a whopping 25, closely followed by *Widow’s Bay*. The ceremony will be hosted by* Law & Order: Special Victims Unit* star Mariska Hargitay.
