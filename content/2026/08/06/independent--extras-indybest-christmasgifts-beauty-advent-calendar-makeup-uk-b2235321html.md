The best beauty advent calendars 2026 has to offer are arriving earlier than ever before, with retailers bringing forward their festive launches by several weeks compared with last year. While the countdown to Christmas may still feel a long way off, many of the biggest names in beauty have already unveiled their calendars, opened waitlists or launched pre-orders, meaning now is the time to start planning if you want to avoid missing out.

Harrods, Liberty, Cult Beauty, Space NK, John Lewis and Harvey Nichols have all shared details of their festive countdowns, with some already revealing their full contents. That leaves just a handful of major launches still under wraps, including Selfridges and Boots, both of which I'm expecting to hear more from soon.

Having reviewed beauty advent calendars for years (and bought them for more than a decade), I'll be updating this guide as each announcement lands. From prices and launch dates to contents, value and whether they're worth buying, here's everything you need to know before investing in this year's best festive countdowns.
