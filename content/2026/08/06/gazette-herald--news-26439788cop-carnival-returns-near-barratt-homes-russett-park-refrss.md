A week-long carnival has brought together the community with food and entertainment.

Cop’ Carnival took place in Copmanthorpe, Yorkshire, from June 29 to July 4, featuring a packed schedule of activities including wine tasting, a psychic night, an art exhibition, and a full day of entertainment on the Saturday.

The event was held near Barratt Homes’ Russett Park and included games, competitions, stalls, street food, and entertainment.

Alan Murray, Chair of the Cop’ Carnival, said: "A huge thank you again to Barratt Homes for their support and sponsorship of Cop’ Carnival 2026.

"As a provider of new housing to further enhance the village and its strong community, it is great that Barratt Homes have again recognised the importance of events such as the Cop’ Carnival to foster and maintain that.

"It was great to see the residents visiting Barratt Homes’ stall and enjoying the activities."

Paul Hogan, sales director at Barratt Homes Yorkshire East, added: "We’re delighted to have sponsored this year’s Cop’ Carnival and are pleased to see the event was a great success again.

"We hope some of our new residents at Russet Park were able to enjoy the festivities."

Cop’ Carnival is organised by the community and raises funds for the Copmanthorpe Recreation Centre.
