If you are looking for a unique experience to get involved in over the summer, you might want to check out the Flower Belt.

With a location in Harrogate and Helmsley, the flower fields are a peaceful place to enjoy nature.

The flower field grows a range of plants throughout the year and changes with the seasons.

As well as pick-your-own flowers, the Flower Belt has a café and bar to have some drinks while watching the field.


## What is the Flower Belt like?

The Flower Belt’s website reads: "Nestled in the rolling countryside of Ryedale, North Yorkshire, The Flower Belt is a pick-your-own flower and pumpkin farm offering visitors a unique, hands-on experience with nature.

"Owned and run by the Wainwright family who have farmed this land since 1962, the farm celebrates a deep-rooted passion for growing beautiful, seasonal produce.

"From vibrant tulip fields in spring to wildflowers and towering sunflowers in summer, and a pumpkin patch in autumn, The Flower Belt offers a changing landscape throughout the year."

Visitors are able to simply take a walk through the fields or handpick flowers to create their own bouquet.

Described as an "unforgettable" experience, you will be able to enjoy views of the North York Moors.

After walking through the fields, you can stop off at the Honey Tap to enjoy a coffee, tea or hot chocolate.

In the afternoon, the café serves pizzas from simple margheritas to a pizza topped with prosciutto, ricotta, sun-dried tomatoes, and pesto.

On a hot day, you can grab an ice cream or a sweet treat ranging from brownies and rocky road to a slice of cake.


### 
  **What do visitors think of the Flower Belt?**

Reviewers on Tripadvisor have praised the area for being "lovely" and "superb".

One reviewer said: "Stumbled across the Flower Belt on the way to Helmsley and it was a lovely surprise!

"Beautiful site and very quiet when we went (late September) - and I thought good value (£7 to pick a jar of wildflowers).

"It was a lovely way to spend an afternoon.

"Unfortunately, pizza wasn’t being served (otherwise we would have stayed longer) but we would come back again."

Another reviewer said: "A stunning location, particularly on a beautiful summers evening.

"Food was lovely, locally brewed ale was spot on, the views were spectacular.

"Really look forward to returning at some point in the not too distant future.

"Congratulations to all involved!"
