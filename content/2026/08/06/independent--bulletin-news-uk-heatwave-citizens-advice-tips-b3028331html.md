Citizens Advice reports that one in six Britons are taking "drastic action" to cope with heatwaves, including sleeping in kitchens or missing work.

Millions across the UK are "trapped in homes they can’t cool", with almost half feeling powerless to prevent their houses overheating.

The charity highlights that poorly designed homes act as "heat traps", and the high cost of cooling equipment leaves many helpless.

Tenants are disproportionately affected, with 51 per cent feeling they lack control over overheating, compared to 40 per cent of homeowners.

Citizens Advice is urging the government to address this "year-round crisis" by investing in better insulation, ventilation and heat network upgrades through the Warm Homes Plan.
