Strictly unveil five new professional dancers for 2026

The BBC's Strictly Come Dancing has continued to announce its celebrity line-up for the 2026 series, with several notable figures confirmed.

Confirmed contestants includeEastEndersstar Lacey Turner, Love Island's Dani Dyer, Australian singer Delta Goodrem, celebrity hairstylist Chris Appleton, reality star Cach Mercer, Paralympian Dame Sarah Storey, TV presenter Will Best and actor Jaime Winstone.

Recent additions to the cast include House of the Dragon actor Bethany Antonia, former footballer Shaun Wright-Phillips, “The Dog Father” Graeme Hall and Emmerdale star Laurence Robb.
