Lithuania fears Russia may be planning a 'false flag' drone attack in the Baltics, according to defence minister Robertas Kaunas.

Kaunas stated that Lithuanian intelligence suggests Russia could use Ukrainian-made drones for provocations against the region.

Critical infrastructure in Lithuania has been reinforced, and security institutions are monitoring the situation, though no direct threat is currently identified.

Separately, Germany has launched a counter-terrorism investigation after an explosive-laden drone was discovered near a runway at Leipzig/Halle airport.

German interior minister Alexander Dobrindt described the drone as a 'new quality of threat', noting it was found near a Ukrainian cargo plane, though Russia was not directly blamed.
