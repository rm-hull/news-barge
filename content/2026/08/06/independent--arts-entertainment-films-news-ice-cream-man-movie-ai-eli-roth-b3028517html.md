Director Eli Roth has admitted that his new horror film *Ice Cream Man* features a sprinkling of generative AI, mere days after claiming otherwise.

On Monday (3 August), the *Hostel* filmmaker and*Inglourious Basterds* actor, 54, took credit for the movie’s animation sequences, denying that the scenes used AI. He told*Polygon*: “I have an animation background, and I drew it.”

However, it has since been highlighted that the film’s credits namecheck AI visual effects company Dark Half – and when approached for comment, Roth said he “misspoke”, adding: “AI was used in a very small portion of a few scenes in the film.”

![Eli Roth has revealed his new film does use AI despite initially claiming otherwise](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/06/9/27/Eli-Roth.jpg)

“It was an opportunity where technology and creativity came together to help bring my vision for the film to life,” Roth said.

The director originally told the outlet: “I worked with an animation team and I wanted it to look and feel like – there’s an old Mickey Mouse cartoon where he goes to a haunted house and there’s brooms and dancing skeletons and this kind of flowing dancing stuff and the jittery style of the animation. So I drew the loop cycles and the styles. Then I had some animators help kind of make it a little more fluid, and I worked with an artist to give it a bit of an old-timey jittery feel. But I sat there and I drew out the frames that I wanted.”

*The Independent* has contacted Roth for comment.

*Ice Cream Man* follows a suburban town whose children are turned into homicidal killers when a deranged ice cream man (Ari Millen) serves them sweet treats. The film has been maligned by critics, with some branding it the worst release of the year.

![Ari Millen in new horror film ‘Ice Cream Man’](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/06/9/41/Screenshot-2026-08-06-at-10-33-06.png)

It currently has a 30 per cent score on review aggregate site Rotten Tomatoes – a marked improvement on the 10 per cent his previous film, sci-fi action *Borderlands*, mustered.

The star-studded movie – an adaptation of the popular video game – followed bounty hunter Lilith (played by two-time Oscar winner Cate Blanchett) as she teams up with a ragtag group of misfits to find a missing girl on her home planet. *The Independent*’s Clarisse Loughrey called the film “a total disaster.”

Roth shot to fame with low-budget horror film*Cabin Fever* in 2002. His other credits include*Hostel* and*Hostel: Part II*(2005 and 2007),* The Green Inferno*(2013) and fantasy film* The House with a Clock in Its Walls*(2018). 

He also appeared in Quentin Tarantino’s *Inglourious Basterds*, playing Donny “The Bear Jew” opposite Brad Pitt.
