**What is doubling down in blackjack?**

Doubling down involves doubling your original bet after receiving your first two cards and then receiving just one additional card.

Players can double down only after their first two cards are dealt, and they cannot hit again. Not all hands are eligible for doubling down; for example, you can’t double down if you are dealt a blackjack.

**When to double down in blackjackBest hands for doubling down**

There a few ideal situations for when to double down in blackjack – these are based on hand values and dealer upcards. Some scenarios to look out for on gambling sites include:

**When your hand totals 11**: If you’re showing an 11, one more card could bring you to blackjack, while there is also no possibiity of going bust. 

**When you have a hard 9 or 10 (Dealer shows 2-6)**: A hard hand is a hand in which you don’t have an ace. If the dealer is showing a lower card when you have a 9 or 10, it is a good time to double down as you’ve put yourself in a good position to win.

**When you have a soft 16, 17, or 18 (Dealer shows 3-6)**: A soft hand is a hand in which you do have an ace. If the dealer is showing a lower card again, you have a better chance of hitting blackjack.

**How the dealer’s cards influence your decision**

Remember that the dealer’s upcard plays a crucial role in deciding when to double down. Weak dealer cards (which are generally regarded as anything between 2 and 6) provide the best opportunities for players to double down successfully.

**When not to double down in blackjackHands to avoid doubling down on**

In this section, we have detailed some scenarios in which players should not double down. Doubling down should be avoided when:

**The dealer shows an ace**: This is because there is a high chance of the dealer getting a blackjack. 

**The dealer has a 10-value card**: This is because the dealer is then likely to score near 21. 

**Your hand totals more than 11**: If your hand totals over 11, there is a high risk of busting.

**Misunderstanding soft and hard hands**

In blackjack, a hard hand is a hand that has any two cards without an Ace. This could include pairs.

A soft hand is a hand with two cards, at least one of which is an Ace.

This distinction is essential when deciding whether to double down, as soft hands have two possible values depending on whether Ace is high or low. For example, Ace and 6 could be seven or 16. In addition, rules require the dealer to stick on soft 17 and take a card on 16.
