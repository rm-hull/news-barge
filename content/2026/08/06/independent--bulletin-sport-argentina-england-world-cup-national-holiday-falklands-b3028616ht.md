Argentina defeated England 2-1 in the 2026 World Cup semi-final in Atlanta last month, a match deeply overshadowed by political tensions regarding the Falkland Islands.

The Argentine Football Association (AFA) has declared 15 July, the date of their victory, as “National Football Teams Day” to be celebrated annually.

England initially led through Anthony Gordon, but Argentina secured a late comeback with goals from Enzo Fernandez in the 85th minute and Lautaro Martinez in stoppage time.

Following the match, Argentine players unfurled a banner stating “Los Malvinas son Argentinas” (”The Falklands are Argentine”), an act currently under investigation by Fifa, highlighting the ongoing sovereignty dispute.

The rivalry is rooted in the 1982 Falklands War and historical football encounters, such as Diego Maradona's “Hand of God” goal in 1986, with fans from both nations jeering the opposing national anthems.
