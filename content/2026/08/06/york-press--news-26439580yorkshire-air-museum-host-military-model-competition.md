## Miniature military models to go on show at Yorkshire Air Museum

The Yorkshire Air Museum in Elvington will host a Military Model Show in September *(Image: Yorkshire Air Museum)*

Intricate miniature models will take centre stage at a new competition in York.

The Yorkshire Air Museum in Elvington will host a Military Model Show on Sunday, September 13, featuring detailed military aircraft, trucks, and tanks created by model makers from at least ten clubs.

The event will be held beneath the nose of the museum’s Handley Page Halifax MkIII bomber, the only one of its kind in Europe.

Jerry Ibbotson, spokesman for the Yorkshire Air Museum, said: "Model makers are incredibly talented and creative people, with a real eye for detail.

"That’s why we’re looking forward to seeing these miniature creations on show at the Museum for the second year in a row.

"The show itself is full, but we really want more entries for the competition.

"You can enter on the day, and it only costs £2.50 per entry.

"We’re also really grateful to Key Model World for sponsoring the event with some great prizes."

The competition is open to all and includes 13 categories, with an overall prize for best in show.

The show and competition are being sponsored by Key Model World, who have donated prizes.

Terms and conditions for entry are available on the Yorkshire Air Museum’s website.
