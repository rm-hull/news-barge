Crews have tackled two field fires overnight (Wednesday).

Selby and Tadcaster firefighters went to an incident off **Sutton Lane, Byram** at**6.19pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “They confirmed a fire in field involving brash and small bushed measuring 100m by 5m.

#### Sutton Lane, Byram

“Crews used hose reels, knapsack sprayers and beaters.”

Then, at **6.20pm**, Stokesley and Thirsk crews were mobilised to a bailer and stubble field fire at a farm in** Kirby Sigston**, near Northallerton.

**Kirby Sigston**

The spokesperson said: “The crew confirmed a baler was well alight and approx 30 square metres of stubble affected by fire.

“Crews extinguished the fire using hose reels and breathing apparatus.”
