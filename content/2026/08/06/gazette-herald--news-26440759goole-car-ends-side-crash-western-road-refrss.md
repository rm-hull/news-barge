A CAR has ended up on its side after a crash in an East Yorkshire town.

Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said it was called to Western Road in Goole around 7.40pm yesterday (Wednesday, August 5).

  



A service spokesperson said: "The driver self-released before the arrival of the fire service.

"A male casualty suffered a hand injury and was left in the care of the ambulance service.

"Police were in attendance."

"The car's battery was disconnected by a passerby."
