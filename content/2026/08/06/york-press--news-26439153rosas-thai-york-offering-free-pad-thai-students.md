## Restaurant gives away free Pad Thai to students after GCSE and A-Level results

Rosa's Thai is giving away free Pad Thai to GCSE and A-Level students on their respective results days *(Image: Nico Delporte)*

A Thai restaurant in York is offering free food to students on results day.

Rosa's Thai, which has a restaurant in Coney Street, is giving away free Pad Thai to GCSE and A-Level students on their respective results days.

The offer, which is open to students regardless of their grades, requires the purchase of one of the restaurant's bubble teas.

Saiphin, the founder of Rosa's Thai, has perfected the Pad Thai sauce over the years with a secret ingredient—tamarinds from her hometown of Phetchabun.

More than 100 free Pad Thai are available at 42 restaurants across England and Wales.

The bubble tea options include Ube-Taro, Matcha-Coconut, Mango Sticky Rice, and Milo Chocolate Milk, among others.

These new flavours join the already popular Home-brewed Thai Tea with Tapioca and Lychee Mango with mango boba.

To get the free Pad Thai, students must sign up on the Rosa’s Thai website before their results day to get a unique code.

They must then visit the restaurant on their results day with their code and a copy of their results.

The full details and sign-up option are available on the Rosa’s Thai website.

For those keen to make use of this offer, the restaurant encourages early sign-ups.
