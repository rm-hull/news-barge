A STREET in a North Yorkshire town has reopened following a water leak.

St Nicholas Street in Norton was partially closed while Yorkshire Water dealt with the repairs.

The road between the junction with Welham Road and St Nicholas Street car park is now open.

  


A spokesperson for Yorkshire Water said: "The repair has been completed today. Thank you to everyone in the area for their patience.

"We’re currently dealing with more leaks and bursts than usual as a result of the dry weather.
