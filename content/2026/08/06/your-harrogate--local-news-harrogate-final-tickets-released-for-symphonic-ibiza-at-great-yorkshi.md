                                    The final tickets for Symphonic Ibiza have been released as the countdown continues to one of Harrogate's biggest summer events.

The open-air concert will take place at the Great Yorkshire Showground on Saturday 15th August, with organisers confirming that the last remaining General Admission and covered Grandstand tickets are now on sale.

The newly released tickets come after production holds were lifted, with General Admission and the Pol Roger VIP Garden both nearing capacity. Several sections of the covered Grandstand have already sold out.

The evening begins when gates open at 5.15pm, with former Love Island star Alex Bowen opening proceedings with a DJ set from 5.45pm.

BBC Radio 2 presenter Vernon Kay will then bring his Dance Sounds of the 90s Live Tour to Harrogate from 6.45pm, performing a 90-minute set featuring some of the decade's biggest dance anthems.

The main Symphonic Ibiza performance starts at 8.30pm, featuring the 40-piece Symphonic Ibiza Orchestra alongside special guests Heather Small of M People, Rebecca Ferguson MBE and Alison Limerick.

Freddy White, founder of Yorkshire-based Sound & White and lead promoter of Symphonic Ibiza, said:

"It's quite surreal to finally be this close. We've spent months building the show and seeing the response from Harrogate and across Yorkshire has been fantastic."


He added:

"With General Admission and VIP now getting very close to capacity and several areas of the Grandstand already sold out, we've released the final seats and production holds that we're able to make available.

"So if anyone has been waiting to make their mind up, this really is the point to get booked."


Freddy said the event would offer "a huge evening of entertainment" from the moment the gates open.

"It's a huge evening of entertainment from the moment the gates open, with Alex, Vernon's full 90-minute show and then the 40-piece orchestra and our special guests.

"We can't wait to finally welcome everyone to the Showground on August 15."


Food and drink will be available throughout the event, while those with General Admission and Pol Roger VIP Garden tickets are also permitted to bring picnic chairs and rugs.

*Final tickets are available now at symphonicharrogate.co.uk or through Harrogate Theatre Box Office, online, by phone or in person.*
