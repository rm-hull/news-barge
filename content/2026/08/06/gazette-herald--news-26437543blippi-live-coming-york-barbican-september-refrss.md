The children's character Blippi is set to visit York Barbican as part of the Be Like Blippi Tour.

The show, on September 5th, will feature live performances by Blippi and Meekah in an interactive adventure.

The tour aims to encourage children to play, explore, and learn with high-energy music and surprises.

Blippi's signature songs such as The Excavator Song, Monster Truck, Dino Dance, and Curious Like Me are expected to be part of the show.

The Be Like Blippi Tour is designed to engage families in a shared experience of fun and discovery.

The tour encourages children to wear their orange Blippi glasses and embrace their curiosity.

The show promises to be a theatrical adventure packed with fun, imagination, and memorable moments for all attendees.

For this tour, the characters Blippi and Meekah will be portrayed by professional stage performers specially selected for the event.

Tickets for the Be Like Blippi Tour are currently available for purchase.

The event is family-friendly and promises to be a fun-filled experience for all attendees.

Tickets can be booked via the York Barbican website.
