Shoppers have excitedly shared a new chocolate bar from Nestlé that has been found on supermarket shelves this month.

Milkybar Caramel Crunch, which is part of the new festive range for 2026, has made its way onto shelves at Morrisons already.

Nestlé said the bar offers a "new twist on a much-loved favourite" and fans can expect a "mix of creamy and crunchy textures".

  The new Milkybar chocolate bar is already available on supermarket shelves despite it being a festive treat 

*(Image: Nestlé)*

## Nestlé launches new Milkybar chocolate bar for festive season

A Facebook page that shares the latest food and drink finds in shops, UK Newest Foods, shared pictures of the bar inside its packaging as well as out and said the bar "tastes like Christmas".

They said: "NEW Milkybar Caramel Crunch, out now in Morrisons for Christmas 2026 and it tastes like Christmas!

"It’s sweet, it’s crunchy, it’s creamy, it’s really good and we will be buying this again.

"If you like white chocolate, especially Milkybar I would definitely recommend giving this a try."

It has been spotted in Morrisons for £1.50, or More Card members can get it for the discounted price of £1.25.


A Nestlé spokesperson told Newsquest: "We're excited to bring something new to Milkybar fans this festive season with the launch of the new Milkybar Caramel Crunch Flavour Sharing Bar.

"Combining the smooth and creamy white chocolate of Milkybar with crispy caramel biscuit pieces, it offers a delicious mix of creamy and crunchy textures.

"We hope shoppers enjoy this new twist on a much-loved favourite."

Excited about the new find, chocolate fans shared their thoughts on the new bar with one saying: "tell santa i want 10 bars!!!"
