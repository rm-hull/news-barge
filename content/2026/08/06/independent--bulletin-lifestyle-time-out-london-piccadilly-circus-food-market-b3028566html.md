Time Out is set to launch its first UK food market in London’s West End in summer 2028, signifying a major return to its roots.

The company has finalised a deal with The Crown Estate for the prime location at 10 Piccadilly, offering views over Piccadilly Circus.

The London venue will host approximately 15 restaurants, bars, and food businesses, complemented by a curated programme of live entertainment and cultural experiences.

This initiative underscores Time Out’s strategic pivot towards food-focused markets, a direction it embraced with its inaugural market in Lisbon in 2014, amid challenges to its traditional media operations.

Chris Ohlund, CEO of Time Out Group, described the partnership as one of the most significant milestones in the company's nearly 60-year history, celebrating its origins and future trajectory in London.
