## York luxury hotel goes on the market after less than three years in business

The luxurious New Holgate Hotel is on the market, less than three years after opening. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A well-regarded luxury boutique hotel in York has gone on the market after less than three years of trading.

The New Holgate Hotel was created by York businessman Nick Bradley, who spent around £2million transforming the former Holgate Bridge B&B at 106-108 Holgate Road.

The property featured 12 well-appointed ensuite letting bedrooms, a restaurant and downstairs whisky bar.

The hotel officially opened in November 2023 and went on to receive good reviews online.

Tripadvisor awards the New Holgate Hotel 4.5 stars out of five, based on 158 reviews, ranking it 23 out of 53 York hotels.

  The downstairs whiskey lounge at the New Holgate Hotel. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Google awards 4.6 stars out of five, based on 110 reviews.

Lambert Smith Hampton of Leeds is selling the freehold of the site and business. The price is on application and offers are invited.

  The hotel restaurant. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

The agents said: “A stylish and highly regarded period property offering an exceptional lifestyle / investment / redevelopment opportunity (STP) within walking distance of the railway station and City Centre.”

The sales particulars also noted the venue offers an “easily operated turnkey business opportunity.”

In addition, there is “consistent year-round trade in popular tourist location.”

The venue also offers “asset management and redevelopment opportunities.”

It is also withing walking distance of the city centre, they added.
