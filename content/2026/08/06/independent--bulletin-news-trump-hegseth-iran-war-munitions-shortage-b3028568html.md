Donald Trump reportedly confronted defense secretary Pete Hegseth at Camp David over a munitions shortage that could limit military options in the Iran war.

Trump questioned why he had been misled about the dwindling weapons stockpile, believing the issue had been fixed.

Hegseth reportedly blamed his deputy, Stephen Feinberg, for the shortages and for not ensuring Trump was fully informed.

The Pentagon is reportedly running low on missile interceptors, with significant reductions in Patriot and THAAD systems since the war began.

The Trump administration has denied the reporting, with White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt calling it '100% fake news' and chief pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell stating Hegseth did not mislead anyone.
