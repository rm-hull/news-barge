**UPDATE: The road appears to have cleared now, with traffic flowing freely.**

Motorists are being warned of heavy traffic following a crash along a main road in York.

The A64 is partially blocked with reports of building traffic both ways from Hopgrove Roundabout to Scotchman Lane.

  


  The A64 is partially blocked with reports of severe traffic delays from Hopgrove Roundabout to Scotchman Lane

Road users said there are severe delays along the road.
