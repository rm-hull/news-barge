A 26-year-old boxer, Sharif Ahmadzai, has appeared in an Athenscourt accused of killing Scottish aid worker Elisabeth-Jane Ross.

Ms Ross's body, 38, was discovered in a suitcase in a derelict building in the Kypseli area of the Greek capital on 18 July.

Ahmadzai faces charges of homicide with intent and allegedly stealing over 10,000 euros from Ms Ross's bank accounts using her cards.

Greek officials state Ahmadzai admitted moving the body but denies the homicide allegations, which are equivalent to murder in Scotland.

The suspect is also accused of sending messages from Ms Ross's phone to her contacts and then impersonating a jihadist to her friends after buying a new phone.
