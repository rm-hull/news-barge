**Basic rules of online roulette**

Understanding how to play roulette online is relatively straightforward as the rules of the game are simple.

A standard roulette wheel consists of 36 different sections, each corresponding to a number between 0 and 36.

The numbers between 1 and 36 are coloured red or black: from 1 to 10 and 19 to 28, odd numbers are red and even numbers are black. From 11 to 18 and 29 to 36, odd numbers are black and even are red.

The only number that isn’t red or black is zero, which is green.

Players place bets on a number, colour, or range of numbers, and the operator plays the ball. The player wins or loses depending on where the ball lands on the roulette wheel.

**Types of bets in roulette**

There are several bet types to know about when learning how to play real roulette online. Of course, narrow options – such as betting on 0 – have higher odds. However, there are other options for players who wish to focus on shorter odds with a greater spread, giving them lower odds but a greater chance of success.

**Straight** - A straight bet is a bet on a single number, with the odds of 35/1.

**Split** – This is where you place a bet on two adjacent numbers. It has odds of 17/1.

**Street** - Where you place a bet on three numbers, at odds of 12/1.

**Corner** - Where you place a bet on four numbers. This has odds of 8/1.

**Six line** – This is a bet on a line of six numbers, at odds of 5/1.

**Red or black** – This is a bet on whether the ball will land on a red or a black and has odds of 1/1.

**Odd or even** – This follows the same principle as red or black but with numbers and also pays 1/1.

**1-18 or 19-36** – This is also an even split, with odds of 1/1.

**Dozens** – This is a bet on 1-12, 13-24, or 25-26. Odds are 2/1.

**Columns** – This is a bet based on the column on the roulette table. Odds are also 2/1.

**American Roulette**: In this version, the wheel has two zeros. It is not as popular overall, but can still be found on plenty of gambling sites. This version also has a higher house edge, so is perhaps not suitable for new players.
