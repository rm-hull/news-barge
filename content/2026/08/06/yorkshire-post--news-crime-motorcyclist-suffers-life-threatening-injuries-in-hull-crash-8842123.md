# Motorcyclist suffers life-threatening injuries in Hull crash

## A 28-year-old motorcyclist has been left with life-threatening injuries after his bike crash into a lamppost in Hull.

At around 9:40pm on Wednesday (Aug 5), police responded to reports of a serious crash in Hull.

The incident occurred on Bude Road when a Yamaha motorcycle travelling southbound crashed into a lamppost on the opposite side of the carriageway.

![Bude Road, Hull](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/06/9/55/Bude-Road-Hull.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Emergency services attended and the rider, a 28-year-old man, was taken to hospital for treatment to life threatening injuries.

Police have launched an appeal for anyone with information or any witnesses to come forward.

A spokesperson for Humberside Police said: “Anyone who witnessed the collision, has dash camera footage, or has any other information about the incident, is urged to please call us on our non-emergency number 101 quoting log 566 of the 5 July.”
