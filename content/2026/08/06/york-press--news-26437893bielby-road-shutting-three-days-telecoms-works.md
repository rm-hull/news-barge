Search the Public Notice PortalA road in East Riding will be closed for three days to install a telecoms cable.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has announced a temporary traffic ban on Peters Field Farm Access Road, Bielby, due to telecom works.

From August 17 to August 19, 2026, no vehicle will be permitted to use the road from 8am to 6pm.

The road closure is necessary to ensure public safety while works to excavate and install a telecoms cable are carried out.

The road will remain open to emergency vehicles.
