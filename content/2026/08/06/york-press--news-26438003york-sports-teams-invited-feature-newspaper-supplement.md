Do you coach, manage, or support a junior sports team?

We’re inviting local grassroots sports clubs to be part of our upcoming Grassroots Junior Sports Supplement, a special celebration of the incredible young sportspeople who make our communities proud.

Whether your team plays football, rugby, cricket, netball, hockey, athletics, or any other sport, we'd love to feature your team photograph and help showcase the talent, dedication, and team spirit found across our area.

When submitting photos, please include:

· Team or club name

· Age group

· Town

We look forward to receiving your photos and helping celebrate junior sport in our area.

Email your photos to: [promotions@yorkpress.co.uk](mailto:promotions@yorkpress.co.uk)

Deadline for photos: Friday, September 11.

Supplement will be published: Tuesday, September 29.

Don't miss this opportunity to see your team featured and recognised alongside other young sporting stars from across the community.
