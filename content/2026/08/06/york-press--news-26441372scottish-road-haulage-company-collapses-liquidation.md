A road freight haulage company has been placed into liquidation.

A liquidator was appointed to the company after court action was taken by His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs.

A petition was presented to Paisley Sheriff Court for HMRC in relation to AllAbout Transport Limited, listed at Manse Court.

It called for the company to be wound up and for a liquidator to be appointed.

James Stephen, of BDO, was appointed liquidator, documents show.

The latest accounts for the company, posted in 2025 for the previous year, show capital and reserves in the red at £114,676, which had widened from £84,490 in the prior period.

A spokesperson for HMRC said it does not comment on individual businesses because of taxpayer confidentiality.

They added: "We take a supportive approach to dealing with customers who have tax debts and only file winding-up petitions once we’ve exhausted all other options, in order to protect taxpayers’ money."

The HMRC Debt Management and Banking Manual states that "time to pay" (TTP) is agreed with a customer and the arrangement "is subject to a number of conditions".

The manual states: "If they do not stick to these terms we can cancel the arrangement and take legal action to recover any outstanding debt."
