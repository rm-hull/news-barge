## Another Sam Smith's pub reopens after nearly four years' closure

The Red Lion in Bramham *(Image: Street View)*

Yet another Sam Smith’s pub has reopened in the area.

The Red Lion at Bramham, near Wetherby, was reported on social media as reopening on Wednesday July 29 after being closed for nearly four years.

The brewery has confirmed the reopening of the pub, situated on The Square, adding the management couple are Gary and Jacqui.

The Red Lion is currently a drinks only pub, but Facebook is reporting it aims to serve food once the kitchen is refurbished.

The re-openings follow that of The Tankard at Long Marston, which reopened last month and is managed by Paula Watson.

The Blacksmith Arms at Skelton re-opened in May, under the management of John and Margaret.

May also saw Sally Watkins reopen the Ebor Inn at Bishopthorpe, which until he sadly died of Covid during the pandemic had been run by Sally’s father Gordon Watkins for 39 years.

And in January, the Sam Smith’s brewery-owned Castle Inn at Sheriff Hutton re-opened after being closed for nearly five years.
