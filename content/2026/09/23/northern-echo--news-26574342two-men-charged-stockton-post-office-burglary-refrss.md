Two men have been charged after a Stockton post office was burgled, forcing the business to temporarily close its doors.

Stockton CID said two men, aged 29 and 44, were charged with burglary after a break-in at 148 High Street Post Office early on Tuesday, September 22.

It was reported that suspects entered the premises and stole a number of items, including vapes, laundry products and chocolates.

![]()Stockton Post Office after it had been burgled. *(Image: STOCKTON POST OFFICE)*

Officers reviewed CCTV and quickly identified two men believed to be involved. They were arrested and questioned.

John Paul Wilks, 44, of no fixed address, and John Bousfield, 29, of Leybourne Terrace, Stockton, were charged with burglary.

The pair were remanded in custody and were due to appear at Teesside Magistrates' Court today (Wednesday, September 23).

Detective Inspector Barnes, of Stockton CID, said: "Burglary is a disruptive crime which often has a huge impact on those involved. Anyone suspected of being involved can expect to be arrested and brought before the courts."

The charges came after Stockton Post Office said it had been targeted early on Tuesday, forcing it to close temporarily.

The business said in a Facebook post: "Unfortunately, 148 High Street Post Office was robbed again this morning at around 5am.

"As a result, we will be closed today, and all Post Office services will be unavailable."

The branch reopened at 12pm that day.

Staff said it was the second time the business had been burgled in two weeks.

Speaking after the break-in, staff voiced frustration at what they called rising crime and its impact on local businesses.

The post office, which opened in January, remained closed for several hours while staff assessed the damage and dealt with the aftermath.
