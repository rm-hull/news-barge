Two people are due in court after a dog walker was reportedly threatened with a machete in Darlington.

Bobby Duxfield, 21, of Fenby Avenue, and a teeanger, both of Darlington, have been charged with a string of offences after the incident on Monday (September 21).

The suspects were reported to have made off on an electric motorbike which was heading towards the Eastbourne area of the town.

Bobby Duxfield has been charged with possession of a cocaine with intent to supply, dangerous driving, possession of an offensive weapon in a public place, driving a motor vehicle otherwise than in accordance with a licence, use of a motor vehicle without third party insurance, failing to stop for police and possession of criminal property.

A 17-year-old who cannot be named for legal reasons has been charged with being concerned in the supply of Class A drugs, possession of an offensive weapon in a public place and possession of criminal property.

Both were remanded in custody to appear at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court today, (September 23).

Police and the National Police Air Service (NPAS) launched a search after the incident, with helicopters circling the town for nearly an hour.

A spokesperson from Durham Police previously said: “Urgent enquiries were launched to locate the suspects, coordinated by the force incident manager in our control room, with officers from Durham Roads and Armed Policing Unit and Response taking to the ground.

“Assistance was also sought from the National Police Air Service who diligently tracked the bike as it rode through the town, directing officers on the ground to its location.”

A bike was stopped on Tornado Way, and two people arrested. The bike, and a "large" machete, were seized by police.

A spokesperson from the National Police Air Service (NPAS) confirmed they were looking for two suspects.

NPAS said: “At 11.54am on Monday, September 21, a police helicopter crew from NPAS Newcastle was deployed to Darlington to support Durham Constabulary in locating and apprehending two suspects on an electric bike.

“Total flying time on the task was 58 minutes.”

Several police cars were also seen by residents rushing along Neasham Road near Darlington railway station while the helicopter remained overhead.
