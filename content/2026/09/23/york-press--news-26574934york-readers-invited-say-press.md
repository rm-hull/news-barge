## Have your say on your local newspaper The Press

Sign up to a digital subscription of The Press to access e-editions from our archive and other benefits. *(Image: Newsquest)*

Your local newspaper has been at the heart of the community for generations, reporting on the stories, people and issues that matter most.

Now, we want to hear from you.

We're inviting readers to take part in our short readership survey and share their views on what they value most about their local daily newspaper, from local news, sport and community coverage to features, supplements and weekend content.

The survey also gives you the opportunity to tell us what we're doing well and where you think improvements could be made.

Take the survey today by visiting: www.surveymonkey.com/r/NEWS2026

![]()Readers of The Press are being invited to have their say on the newspaper. *(Image: Newsquest)*

Whether you buy the paper every day, read it at weekends, receive a home delivery or access it digitally, your feedback will help shape the future of your newspaper and ensure we continue to provide the content that matters most to local readers.

The survey only takes a few minutes to complete, and every response will make a difference.

Thank you for supporting local journalism and helping us create a newspaper that reflects the interests and priorities of our community.
