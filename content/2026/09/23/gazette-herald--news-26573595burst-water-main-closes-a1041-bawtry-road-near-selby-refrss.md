A burst water main is continuing to cause disruption on a North Yorkshire road.

The A1041 Bawtry Road has been closed in both directions between Chester Court Road and Thief Lane due to emergency repairs.

**Recommended reads:**

The issue has seen the water main shooting jets of water into the air, causing police to be on the scene directing traffic.

The work is expected to end at 5pm on Monday, September 28.
