Food lovers have reacted with dismay to the news that a ‘much loved’ café in York is to shut within days.

The Old Greengrocer, on York Road, Acomb, will close at the weekend.

The news was broken in a message on its Facebook page: “Unfortunately as of this Sunday the Old Greengrocer will be closed indefinitely.

“If anyone would be interested in buying the lease please do get in touch.”

The café serves up sandwiches, cakes and pastries, full English breakfasts, as well as a full evening Friday menu and Sunday lunches.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/the-old-greengrocer-acomb-york.webp)

It began life as a greengrocers and then evolved into a delicatessen and café.

Last spring, The Old Greengrocer was added to the Good Food Guide.

At the end of 2025, the business changed hands and Naomi and Ricky took over. They too hope to sell the business as a going concern.

The closure news was greeted with sadness by diners.

Acomb Cllr Jason Rose said: “Gutted to hear this.

“It has been great, and while I also hope someone else takes it on and makes it great it’ll be sad to lose you guys even if the grocer continues to be open and good.”

The owners of nearby bar The Hand added this: “It’s such a shame to see you close, but thank you for everything you’ve brought to the area and for being such lovely people to work with.”
