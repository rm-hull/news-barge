A main road in a North Yorkshire town is currently closed.

Falsgrave Road in Scarborough is shut at the Sainsbury's lights towards Seamer Road due to an incident.  
A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "Traffic is being diverted via Victoria Road and Roscoe Street to prevent a backlog in the area. Sainsbury's car park is also very busy."
