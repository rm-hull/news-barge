# LIVE: Sixth form college closed amid "significant" police incident nearby

## A sixth form college in Yorkshire is closed today, as police deal with a significant incident nearby.

South Yorkshire Police has cordoned off Horninglow Road, off Barnsley Road, with blue and white police tape extending across the junction.

![The scene near Horninglow Road this morning](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/23/7/57/Incident-in-Longley-Park.jpeg?trim=66,0,67,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Several police cars are also at the scene, along with uniformed police officers

Longley Park Sixth Form, which is right next to the police cordon, has announced that it will be closed all day due to the incident.
