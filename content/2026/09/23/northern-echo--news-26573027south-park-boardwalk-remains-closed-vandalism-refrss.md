One of Darlington's most scenic parks remains fenced off two months after being hit by "mindless vandalism".

The area has been sealed off since July after the structure was targeted by vandals.

New photographs show the boardwalk remains inaccessible, with metal barriers still in place and red-and-white tape preventing access.

![]()Vandalism at South Park. *(Image: PETER REIMANN)*

Broken fencing and holes in sections of the boardwalk also remain visible, highlighting the extent of the damage.

Darlington Borough Council said the repeated vandalism was "disappointing" and that the area remains cordoned off to "keep visitors safe".

Council bosses also said repair work is expected to start "soon".

![]()Vandalism at South Park. *(Image: PETER REIMANN)*

![]()Vandalism at South Park. *(Image: PETER REIMANN)*

A council spokesperson said: "It is very disappointing that repeated vandalism has meant this small area of the park has been cordoned off to keep visitors safe.

"We have met with contractors and expect work to repair the damage will start soon."

A resident, who asked to remain anonymous, previously said the situation was "such a shame".
