New cycling routes designed to showcase some of the North York Moors' most striking landscapes have been unveiled.

The circular trails, developed by the National Park Authority with the help of local cyclists John and Lois Fife, are located on the northern edge of the park and aim to offer riders a sense of adventure through quiet lanes and off-road tracks.

Yorkshire cyclist and route creator Guy Kesteven said: "The North York Moors has some of the best off-road cycling in the UK.

"As well as good stretches of smooth 'champagne gravel', all-weather tracks and forest trails, there’s a wonderful mix of lost landscapes and iconic landmarks – perfect for escaping everyday stresses and immersing yourself in nature at your own pedalling pace."

![]()Gravel cycling nr Blakey Ridge North York Moors National Park *(Image: Mick Kirkman)*

The routes start near train stations on the Esk Valley Railway, which runs between Middlesbrough and Whitby, making them easily accessible without a car.

Designed for gravel, mountain, and hybrid bikes, as well as e-bike equivalents, the four routes use existing rights of way around Guisborough, Great Ayton, and Danby.

The shortest route is a seven-mile loop around Roseberry Topping, described as a "taster" for those new to off-road cycling.

At the other end of the scale is a 37-mile challenge through the "Land of Iron," which explores the industrial heritage of the region and reaches the coast at Skinningrove before returning to Guisborough.

For those without their own bikes, the two shorter routes start at Bike Base in Gribdale near Great Ayton, which offers mountain and e-bike hire.

The new circular rides add to the National Park’s existing Explorer itineraries and arrive as gravel cycling continues to grow in popularity across the UK.

This growth is being fuelled by improved infrastructure, a wider range of bike options, and events like the Minds and Miles sportive at Sutton Bank.

Full route details, including a map and gpx file, are available at www.northyorkmoors.org.uk/gravel-explorer-routes-from-north.
