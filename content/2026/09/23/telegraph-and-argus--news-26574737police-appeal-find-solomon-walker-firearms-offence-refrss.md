Police are appealing for information to help locate this West Yorkshire man.

Solomon Walker, 29, is wanted in connection with a firearms offence in Leeds.

The incident took place on August 29.

Mr Walker is also wanted on recall to prison.

![]() Solomon Walker *(Image: WYP)*

## What the force had to say

A spokesperson for WYP said: "Police are appealing for information to help locate Solomon Walker from Leeds who is wanted in connection with a firearms offence which took place in Leeds on 29 August.

"The 29-year-old is also wanted on recall to prison.

"Anyone who thinks they have seen him or knows of his whereabouts is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat) quoting reference 13260493156.

"Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111."
