A YORK takeaway could be transformed under new plans.

An application to convert Pizza Base, in Acomb, into a café has been lodged with City of York Council.

Plans from Ibrahim Kayran stated the Beckfield Café would allow the building to continue to be used for commercial purposes.

The application would see the ground floor of 147, Beckfield Lane converted to house the café.

Plans showed the business would feature a customer seating area, a serving counter and back-of-house facilities including a food preparation area.

The application stated: "The principal design approach is to adapt the existing premises for café use without unnecessary changes to the building's scale or form.

![]()Pizza Base could be converted into a café under new plans *(Image: Unsplash)*

"The internal arrangement will provide an appropriate customer seating area together with the operational areas required for the café.

"The proposal is therefore based on reuse and adaptation of the existing commercial premises.

"Any internal finishes, furniture and café fittings will be appropriate to the proposed use."

Pizza Base is still taking orders online via Just Eat.
