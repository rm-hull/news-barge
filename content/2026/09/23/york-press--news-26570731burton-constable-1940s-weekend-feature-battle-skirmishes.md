Battle skirmishes, military vehicles, live demonstrations, music and dancing will bring wartime history to life at an East Riding country house.

Burton Constable Hall and Parkland will host a 1940s weekend on Saturday, September 26 and Sunday, September 27, with wartime re-enactments, vintage displays and period entertainment.

The event follows last year’s successful VE Day 80th anniversary commemorations, with a bigger programme this time.

![]()Evening dances *(Image: Supplied)*

Alasdair Hutson, chief executive of the Burton Constable Foundation, said: "We had a really positive response to the VE Day event last year, so we wanted to build on that and see what we could do with a full weekend.

"It’s been quite a step up in terms of what we’re putting on, and a lot of work has gone into getting everything ready.

"It’ll be great to finally see it all come together this weekend.

![]()Live entertainment *(Image: Supplied)*

"There’s a good mix across the two days, so whether people come for the military history, the vehicles, the music or just to have a look around and see what’s going on, we hope they’ll really enjoy it."

Each day features live-fire demonstrations at 11.30am and battle skirmishes at 2.30pm.

Visitors can explore living history encampments, period trade stalls, wartime displays and meet re-enactors.

The event will also showcase East Yorkshire’s wartime heritage.

Burton Constable Hall will host the East Riding Yeomanry Exhibition, featuring original uniforms, medals, personal items and wartime artefacts.
