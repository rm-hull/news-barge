A jury in a murder trial at Bradford Crown Court has retired to consider its verdicts.

Mason Jones, 30, was pronounced dead on March 1 this year, three hours after receiving a stab wound from a knife wielded by John Robinson.

The incident took place at around 9.50am off Main Street in Burley-in-Wharfedale.

Robinson, 30, of Derry Hill, Menston, said the stabbing was accidental, not deliberate.

He denies murder, manslaughter, and having a bladed article in public.
