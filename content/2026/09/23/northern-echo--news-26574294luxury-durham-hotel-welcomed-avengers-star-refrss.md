This stunning North East hall offers luxury, leisure and relaxation just minutes from Durham.

Located on the outskirts of Durham and set within 350 acres of parkland, the resort blends countryside calm with easy access to the city, making it a favourite for spa breaks, golf weekends, celebrations and staycations.

Once the Pemberton family home, Ramside Hall became a hotel in 1963 and is now County Durham’s largest privately owned luxury hotel.

Its reputation has even attracted famous faces over the years. Hollywood star Chris Hemsworth stayed at Ramside Hall while filming Avengers: Infinity War in Durham, with the actor spotted by fans during his time in the city.

![]() *(Image: Supplied)*

The resort now features 127 bedrooms and suites, as well as popular woodland treehouses, which offer a secluded stay with hot tubs, modern interiors and views across the estate.

The treehouses are within walking distance of the hotel’s restaurants and spa, making them a top choice for guests seeking both privacy and convenience.

The spa is one of the resort’s biggest draws, with a reputation as one of the North East’s best.

It includes indoor and outdoor pools, thermal experiences, treatment rooms and wellness facilities, offering guests a chance to fully unwind and recharge.

Golf is another key attraction at Ramside Hall, with the Cathedral Course and Prince Bishops’ Course drawing players of all abilities year-round.

Recent investment has seen the addition of The Pin, an entertainment complex featuring a Toptracer driving range, bowling alley, sports bar and interactive games.

Food lovers are equally well served.

Visitors can choose from a range of dining experiences, from premium steaks at The Rib Room to Asian-inspired dishes at Fusion, alongside more casual options across the resort.

![]() *(Image: Supplied)*

Afternoon tea remains a popular choice, particularly for those looking to make the most of a spa day.

This is perhaps what makes Ramside Hall stand out: it has an ability to offer something different to every guest.

Some arrive seeking a romantic weekend away, others come for championship golf, family celebrations or a day of relaxation in the spa.
