Decades of community service were celebrated at this year’s Keighley Community Awards.

Residents, volunteers, and organisations across the town were honoured by Keighley Town Council at the 2026 ceremony.

The awards recognised individuals and groups who have made a lasting impact on their community through service, kindness, and leadership.

### 'Every nominee has made a meaningful difference'

Town mayor Councillor Andrea Walker said: "These awards recognise the incredible people who make our community so special.

"Every nominee has made a meaningful difference, whether through volunteering, supporting neighbours, protecting the environment, inspiring young people, improving education or strengthening community life.

"On behalf of Keighley Town Council, I congratulate all of our winners and runners-up for their outstanding achievements."

### Full list of winners

Ruth Payne received the Gary Pedley Community Award for more than 10 years of service with the Keighley Lions Club, where she has organised events and supported projects benefiting thousands across the area.

Lisa Holmes was named Climate Change Champion for her work on environmental projects, including the Eco Festival at Cliffe Castle Park, and for using photography to promote sustainability.

![]()Lisa Holmes with the MP for Keighley and Ilkley, Robbie Moore. *(Image: Keighley Town Council)*

The Lifetime Achievement Award went to Janet Armstrong and Antonia Crowley.

Ms Armstrong was honoured for her lifelong contributions to Oakworth through the Girl Guides, the Village Society, and community events.

Ms Crowley, known affectionately as "Mrs Tony," was celebrated for decades of service from her ice cream van, which has become a beloved fixture in the community.

![]()Janet Armstrong and Antonia Crowley *(Image: Keighley Town Council)*

PC Paul Beck was named Emergency Services Hero of the Year for his leadership of Operation Shipdon, a partnership to reduce speeding and anti-social driving.

![]()Paul Beck *(Image: Keighley Town Council)*

Neighbour of the Year went to Carol Owlett for her dedication to residents in Bracken Bank.

Richard Pemberton received the Muppett's Walkers Award for leading the Aire and Worth Valley Wanderers, a walking group promoting social connection.

Mohammed "Gully" Gulnawaz was named Barry Thorne Sporting Hero of the Year for his long-standing contribution to Riddlesden Cricket Club.

![]()Mohammed Gulnawaz *(Image: Keighley Town Council)*

Charlie Wilkinson was awarded Inspiring Young Person of the Year for his service in Scouting across Keighley.

Volunteer of the Year went to Vera Plumb and Marilyn Birt for their work supporting people with dementia.

Angie Powell received Teacher of the Year for her efforts in education and dementia awareness.

The Mayor’s Civic Award was presented to Steve Thorpe for his work improving Keighley's floral displays and green spaces, which contributed to the town’s success in the Yorkshire in Bloom competitions.
