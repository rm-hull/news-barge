BRADFORD has been ranked the least peaceful place to live in Yorkshire and the Humber.

The city placed 288th out of 316 local authorities in England and Wales, according to a new study by property specialists SAM Conveyancing.

Bradford also ranked last among the 15 authorities in the Yorkshire and Humber region.

The study measured peace using four factors: night-time noise, crime rates, public green space, and proximity to nightlife.

Andrew Boast, CEO of SAM Conveyancing, said: "Buyers spend months on the things they can measure – the price, the survey, the school catchment – and almost no time on the thing they will notice on their first night in the house, which is how quiet it actually is.

"A Sunday afternoon viewing tells you very little about what a road sounds like at two in the morning, or what happens when the pub two streets away turns out.

"The encouraging thing is that this is all knowable before you commit.

"What the road carries at night, how close the nearest railway line is, what is licensed within a few hundred metres – that information is public, and it costs nothing to look at.

"Anyone moving home should be asking those questions before they exchange rather than after."

Bradford recorded 117 crimes per 1,000 residents and just 18.2 square metres of green space per person.

This is the lowest amount of green space in the region and significantly below the national median of 30.4 square metres.

Leeds and Calderdale ranked just above Bradford in 14th and 13th places, respectively.

Both also recorded crime rates above 100 offences per 1,000 residents and the highest levels of night-time noise exposure in the region.

Yorkshire and the Humber as a whole performed modestly in the study.

Six of its 15 authorities ranked in the top half of the national table, but none made it into the top 100.

Nationally, more than half of people in England and Wales—around 33 million—live in homes exposed to night-time noise above the World Health Organization’s guideline of 40 decibels.

North Yorkshire emerged as the most peaceful area in the Yorkshire and Humber region.

It achieved a peace score of 58.6 out of 100, with the lowest crime rate in the area.

Nationally, it ranked 106th out of 316.

North Lincolnshire followed in second place with a score of 58 and Rotherham was third with 56.9.

East Riding of Yorkshire and Doncaster completed the regional top five.

York ranked sixth, Wakefield seventh, and Barnsley eighth.

Kingston upon Hull placed ninth and North East Lincolnshire rounded out the regional top ten.
