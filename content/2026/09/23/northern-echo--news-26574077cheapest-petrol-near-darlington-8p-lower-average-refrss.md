The cheapest petrol near Darlington is currently more than 8p a litre below the national average, according to the latest fuel price data.

With average UK unleaded prices standing at 172.8p per litre on Tuesday, September 22, motorists can make significant savings by shopping around, with the cheapest forecourt within 15 miles of Darlington charging just 164.7p per litre.

The latest figures from PetrolPrices.com show Jet B6282 at Etherley Moor Garage as the cheapest place to fill up, while four other stations in and around Bishop Auckland are also undercutting the national average.

It comes after talks between US President Donald Trump and Prime Minister Andy Burnham on Tuesday, during which both leaders discussed the impact of the Iran war on energy markets.

## Prices down when the war ends says Trump

Before the meeting, Mr Burnham said he would stress the need to bring the conflict to an end as quickly as possible because of the pressure higher energy costs were placing on households in the UK amid the ongoing cost-of-living crisis.

Following the talks, President Trump insisted oil prices would fall sharply once the conflict was over.

He said: “I think as soon as the war is over, the numbers are going to come way down, pricing generally, because oil is the big thing.

“When oil goes up, and we had to do that because we have to stop them, and I think we feel very united on that, we can't let Iran have a nuclear weapon.”

The US president added that people understood the need for military action despite the effect on prices.

![]() *(Image: Toby Melville, REUTERS)*

He said: “People understand that in the United States, and they're paying less than the Biden administration and yet he did nothing about the threat of nuclear terror.”

Asked whether he sympathised with countries such as the UK facing higher costs because of the conflict, Mr Trump again argued any increase would be temporary and linked to efforts to prevent Iran obtaining nuclear weapons.

For drivers in the Darlington area, forecourt prices remain well below the national average at several filling stations.

The gap between the cheapest pump, Jet B6282 at 164.7p per litre, and the UK average of 172.8p amounts to 8.1p per litre.

For a typical 55-litre family car, that equates to a saving of around £4.46 on a full tank.

Three of the five cheapest stations are clustered around Bishop Auckland, with Tesco, Asda and Sainsbury's all offering petrol for less than 166p per litre.
