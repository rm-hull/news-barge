## In this episode of Qatar in Motion, we explore how Qatar is building the city of the future, where artificial intelligence, autonomous mobility, smart infrastructure, and sustainability converge to transform everyday life.

Qatar is building the smart city of tomorrow: one that thinks, learns and one that quietly solves problems before we even notice them. We take a ride on Mowasalat’s bright teal robotaxis and learn about the firm’s wider strategy for public transportation. At the Autonomous e-Mobility Forum, we meet the pioneers shaping the future of transport. We also uncover how Qatar is harnessing technology to create smarter, safer and more connected cities for future generations.
