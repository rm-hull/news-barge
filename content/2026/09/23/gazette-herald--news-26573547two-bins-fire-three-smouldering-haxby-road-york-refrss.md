SEVERAL large bins were set alight in York in the early hours of this morning, Wednesday, September 23.

A fire crew from York was called out to Haxby Road at 12.44am to a report of a bin on fire.

On arrival, they found two large wheeled bins on fire and a further three that were smouldering.

Firefighters extinguished the flames and burning embers with a hose reel jet and alerted police.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service said: "Due to the believed deliberate nature of the fires and the incident has been left in the hands of police."
