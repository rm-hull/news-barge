A manure heap started smouldering near outbuildings in Ryedale yesterday evening.

Two fire crews from Malton were called to the scene after reports that a muck heap had started to smoulder near some outbuildings.

**Recommended reads:**

The incident happened at about 7.40pm in Westow.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said firefighters dampened down the muck heap with a hose reel jet to protect the buildings and used shovels to move it a safe distance away.
