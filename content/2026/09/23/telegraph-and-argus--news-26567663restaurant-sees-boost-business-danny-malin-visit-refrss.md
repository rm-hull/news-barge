A BRADFORD restaurant has seen a significant boost in business following a visit from a well-know food reviewer.

Des N Pardes, which specialises in Pakistani cuisine, is located on Wilton Street and was hailed by YouTuber Danny Malin as Bradford's "hidden gem" in February this year.

Restaurant owner Abrar Hussain said: "When we realised how good Danny's review was, we were so happy.

"He had no doubt about our food.

"It really helped us in terms of business.

"Danny is well-known, and people have come to try the same food that he had.

"It has definitely, definitely helped."

### 'This place could be Bradford's hidden little secret'

When he visited, Danny said: "This place could be Bradford’s hidden little secret.

"It’s down a bit of a side street."

During his visit to the restaurant, which specialises in Pakistani cuisine, he sampled a selection of dishes including lamb tikka masala, lamb keema, rice, naan, and a mixed grill.

He was especially impressed by the mixed grill, praising the seekh kebab.

Danny said: "You’ve got spices tickling the back of your throat, getting you ready for the rest of it."

The chicken boti also earned high praise.

Danny said: "That’s a beautiful flavour.

"That is delicious.

"Sometimes, with a chicken tikka, it can be overcooked.

"It can be a little bit hard around the edges.

"That is nice and soft."

He complimented the masala fish as well.

Danny said: "It’s got just the right amount of salt content to complement that piece of fish.

"As far as mixed grills go, that is up there."

### 'Sometimes you find places that just blow you away'

He suggested the restaurant deserved more attention within Bradford’s well-known curry scene.

Danny said: "I might have just stumbled upon Bradford’s little hidden gem.

"Everyone always goes on about these curry awards in Bradford.

"The awards come along and they're dominated by a select few.

"But sometimes you can go somewhere and find places that just blow you away.

"Little places like this."

He admitted he had "fallen in love" with the restaurant.

Danny said: "I think you’ve got a golden nugget that people don’t know about because it’s tucked down a side street."
