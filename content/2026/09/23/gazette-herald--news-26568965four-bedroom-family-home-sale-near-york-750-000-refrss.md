A “wonderful” family home has hit the market near York – for £750,000.

The four-bedroom converted barn is for sale in Plainville Lane, near Wigginton.

Hunters, which is marketing the property, said it “reflects a fashionable open plan lifestyle with beautifully landscaped outside spaces providing wonderful areas to relax and enjoy the open skies and afternoon sun”.

Inside, an entrance hall leads to the kitchen and main living area.

The kitchen opens through to the snug area with views and access out to the garden.

Beyond the kitchen is the main bedroom with an en-suite bathroom.

An inner hall on the ground floor leads to another double bedroom with an en-suite shower room.

Meanwhile, two more bedrooms and a family bathroom are on the first floor.

Outside is a gravelled drive and a garage, as well as the landscaped garden.

A Hunters spokesperson said: “This is a wonderful family home situated in a tucked away hamlet of converted farm buildings between Wigginton and Shipton yet only seven miles drive into York city centre.”
