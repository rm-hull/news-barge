Police dog Chewy brought a dramatic chase in Bishop Auckland to an end after tracking down two suspected burglars hiding in the bushes.

Chewy was called into action in the early hours of Wednesday morning (September 23), after police received reports of a garage burglary in Bishop Auckland.

Durham Police said the suspects stole a strimmer before fleeing in a Volkswagen Golf; officers circulated details of the car and spotted it about five minutes later.

Police said the car failed to stop and was driven at speeds of up to 90mph in 30mph zones.

Officers authorised a pursuit but abandoned it over concerns for public safety.

The search continued after reports of suspicious activity in Etherley Dene, where officers later found the vehicle.

Chewy and the force’s drone team helped officers find a 20-year-old man and a 17-year-old boy hiding in a front garden and nearby bushes.

Police said both suspects appeared to know the game was up once they heard Chewy barking, remaining face down as officers approached.

One even gave a thumbs up when asked if he understood the police caution.

Both suspects were arrested on suspicion of burglary, dangerous driving and failing to stop for police; the teenager was also arrested on suspicion of driving without a licence or insurance.

They were taken into police custody and remain there while inquiries continue.

As a reward for helping locate the suspects, police dog Chewy was treated to his favourite snack.
