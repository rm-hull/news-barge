Police have released this photograph as part of their investigation into racially aggravated behaviour in York.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are releasing the image of a man we would like to speak to following an incident that occurred in York city centre.  
“It happened on Bishopthorpe Road at around 11pm on Sunday 16 August 2026, and involved a suspect subjecting the victim to racial and verbal abuse.

#### Bishopthorpe Road, York

“We would like to speak to the person shown in this image, as we believe he may have information that could assist our investigation.”
