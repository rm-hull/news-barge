# Watch: Pilots eject as RAF jet crashes in Anglesey

Two RAF pilots ejected and parachuted out of an RAF jet before it crashed during a training exercise.

North Wales Police said the pilots were being treated for minor injuries following the incident in Anglesey.

Helicopters from the Coastguard and the Wales Air Ambulance were sent to the scene close to RAF Valley on the island, the force said.

A spokesperson for the RAF said it is currently investigating the crash.
