A growing North East facilities management company is launching a recruitment drive as it looks to expand its workforce and support continued growth across the UK.

UK Commercial Group, based in Gateshead, will host a recruitment open day at its Team Valley headquarters on Friday, October 2.

The company is seeking to fill management, engineering, and specialist operational roles as part of its ongoing expansion.

Tony Earnshaw, chief executive of UK Commercial Group, said: "We are an ambitious business which has grown significantly, but we know that bringing the right people into the company is absolutely fundamental to continuing that progress.

"We have opportunities across a number of areas and we are particularly interested in experienced people who want to develop their careers with a growing business.

"There is a huge amount of talent here in the North East and we want to attract more of that talent into UK Commercial Group.

"The open day is deliberately informal.

"People can come along, meet us, find out what we do and have a conversation about where they might fit into the business and the opportunities available to them.

"We want people who share our ambition and who want the opportunity to grow with us."

The open day will run from 1pm to 3pm at 14 Octavian Way, Team Valley, Gateshead, NE11 0HZ.

Attendees will have the chance to meet members of the management team, explore current vacancies, and learn about career progression within the company.

UK Commercial Group delivers facilities management services to businesses across the UK, including HVAC servicing and maintenance, commercial kitchen extraction cleaning, and other compliance-focused services.

The company is currently looking to hire operations managers, a training manager, HVAC engineers, and kitchen extraction cleaning operatives.

It is also interested in experienced operational staff who are keen to join a growing organisation.

The business has expanded both nationally and internationally and continues to invest in its workforce, including through dedicated training facilities in Newcastle.
