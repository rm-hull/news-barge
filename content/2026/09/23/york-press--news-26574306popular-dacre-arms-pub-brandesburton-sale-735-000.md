## Popular East Yorkshire village pub on the market for £735,000 freehold

The exterior of The Dacre Arms at Brandesburton *(Image: Barry Crux & Co)*

A popular pub in an East Yorkshire village has gone up for sale for £735,000 freehold.

The Dacre Arms in Brandesburton, just off the A165, has been owned by its current proprietors for more than 20 years and is now been marketed by York-based Barry Crux and Co.

The 16th Century pub is now on sale as the owners have other business commitments. They had bought the pub as a side venture but now say they no longer have the time to dedicate to it.

The Dacre Arms is a detached, two-storey building with a large car park, enclosed beer patio, and a four-bedroom owner’s flat on the first floor.

The pub is described as "heavily food driven" and currently operates on limited hours.

It generates an average weekly turnover of £14,500, excluding VAT.

![]()The interior of the Dacre Arms at Brandesburton *(Image: Barry Crux & Co)*

A small outbuilding on the property has had permission applied for to be demolished to create additional parking, although it could be converted into extra customer seating.

The pub is highly regarded, with a reputation for traditional pub food, plus steak pie and giant Yorkshire puddings.

Google awards it 4.5 stars out of five based on 1,075 Google Reviews.

Tripadvisor awards 4.3 stars out of five, based on 602 reviews, ranking it fourth out of 74 Driffield restaurants.
