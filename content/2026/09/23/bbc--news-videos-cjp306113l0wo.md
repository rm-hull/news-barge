# 'My eyelids became infected - I was scared to sleep'

Doctors specialising in surgery around the eyes say a concerning number of patients are reporting problems from having had cheap so-called "bleph" surgery on their eyelids, typically abroad.

Women have told us they have sought medical help back in the UK, because scarring has not healed properly and they have been in pain.
