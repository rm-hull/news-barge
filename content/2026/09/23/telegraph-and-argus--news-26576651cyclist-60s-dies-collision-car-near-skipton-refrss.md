A CYCLIST died in collision with a car near Skipton at the weekend.

The cyclist, in his 60s, was in collision with a blue Volkswagen Caddy on the A59 near to the Gargrave Road roundabout at around 5.30pm on Sunday.

Both the cyclist and the car were travelling in the same direction, from Broughton towards Skipton.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: “Sadly, the rider of the cycle, a man in his 60s, died at the scene.

“We are appealing to anyone who was in the area at the time and saw either the Caddy or the cyclist in the moments before the collision or who saw the collision itself, to come forward.

“We are also appealing to anyone who may have dashcam footage of the incident or the Caddy or cyclist, to get in touch.”

Anyone able to help with the police investigation is asked to email: [SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.

Quote registration number 122 6018 4905 when passing on information.

The road was closed following the collision and a Yorkshire Air Ambulance landed on the road.
