# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image after Jellycat toys stolen from Harrogate garden centre

## North Yorkshire Police has released a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to after a number of Jellycat toys were stolen in Harrogate.

The incident happened on August 31 when a number of toys were stolen from RHS Harlow Carr Plant Centre and Gift Shop.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Please contact us if you recognise the person pictured in the CCTV image as they may have information that will assist our investigation.”
