MORE than 1,200 young people and adult volunteers took part in a weekend camp.

The North Yorkshire Scouts event, Ignite, was held from September 18-20 and offered a packed programme of creative and adventurous activities, bringing together Scouts and Explorers from across the county.

One Scout said: "I really enjoyed Ignite because there was so much to do and it was a chance to try things I wouldn’t normally get to do at Scouts.

"It was also great being able to spend the weekend with my friends and meet other Scouts from across North Yorkshire."

The event was made possible by a dedicated team of volunteers who ran activities, marshalled sessions and provided support across the site throughout the weekend.

Ignite is one of North Yorkshire Scouts main events for Scouts and Explorers.

It gives young people the chance to camp together and try activities they might not otherwise experience at their local group.

North Yorkshire Scouts is encouraging more young people and volunteers to get involved.

Whether you want to try new activities, meet new people or give your time to support young people, there are plenty of ways to get involved.
