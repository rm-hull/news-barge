A RECORD amount of money was raised at a charity golf day.

The Ryedale Lions held their annual charity golf day at Kirkbymoorside Golf Club on September 4.

A total of 42 teams took part, helping to raise around £9,000.

This was a record amount raised at the event.

Will Oxley, Ryedale Lions president, said: "A huge amount of effort goes into the day and this is an amazing result.

"Many thanks to all the competitors, local businesses who supported the event with sponsorship and raffle/ auction prizes, Kirkbymoorside Golf Club for preparing the course and giving fantastic hospitality, Lions wives and partners for a superbly run half way house, and every member of the Club who helped in any way.

"Special thanks go to Lion Andrew and his committee who are already looking to next year’s event."

The money raised will help Ryedale Lions with their work in the local area.
