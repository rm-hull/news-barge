A County Durham primary school has been forced to close after its school hall ceiling was damaged.

South Stanley Junior School will be closed today, Wednesday, September 23, after the damage was discovered.

Headteacher Rachel Bell told parents and carers the decision had been made to allow time for the damage to be fully assessed and the school site to be made safe.

In a letter to families, she said: "The safety of our pupils and staff must always be our highest priority."

She added that pupils could access online learning from home through Dreambox Maths, Reading Plus, Lexia, TT Rockstars and Numbots.

Families experiencing issues accessing the platforms, including problems with passwords, have been asked to email the school.

Ms Bell said: "I appreciate that this closure will cause inconvenience for families, particularly at short notice, and I am very sorry for the disruption this may cause."

The school said it would provide an update on arrangements for Thursday as soon as further information was available.
