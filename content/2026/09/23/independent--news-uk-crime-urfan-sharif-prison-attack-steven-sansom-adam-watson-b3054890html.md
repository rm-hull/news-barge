Two inmates are set to go on trial next year after they denied attempting to murder the father of schoolgirl Sara Sharif in prison.

Steven Sansom, 47, and Adam Watson, 39, are accused of attacking Urfan Sharif at HMP Belmarsh on New Year’s Day 2025.

It is alleged that Sansom invited Sharif, who murdered his 10-year-old daughter, into a cell where Watson was waiting.

Watson then allegedly took hold of Sharif and punched him repeatedly before Sansom applied pressure to his throat.

It is claimed Watson then passed an object to Sansom, which was used to make cutting motions to Sharif’s throat.

Prison officers entered the cell and found Sharif bloodied and distressed on the floor, with injuries to his face, mouth and neck, a court has previously heard.

![Ten-year-old Sara Sharif was murdered by her father and stepmother](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/05/01/00/65cb0feb439f23289b2839b0afbb6e9eY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzQ2MTA1OTY2-2.78750260.jpg)

On Wednesday, Watson and Sansom appeared at the Old Bailey for a plea hearing by separate videolinks from Wakefield jail.

They pleaded not guilty to attempted murder and wounding Sharif with intent to cause him grievous bodily harm.

They both also denied unauthorised possession of two offensive weapons in prison relating to a tin lid and a pen attached to a handle.

Judge Lynn Tayton KC set a two-week trial for 19 July next year and remanded the serving prisoners into custody.

A pre-trial hearing will take place on 21 June.

Sharif and Sara’s stepmother, Beinash Batool, from Woking in Surrey, were jailed for life in December for years of horrific “torture” and “despicable” abuse that culminated in the 10-year-old’s murder.

Sharif was handed a minimum term of 40 years and Batool was given 33 years.
