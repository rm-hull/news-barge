A TEAM of estate agents has raised more than £3,300 after completing the Yorkshire Three Peaks Challenge.

Dacre, Son & Hartley, together with iamproperty, Kempston Parkes Chartered Surveyors, and the Mortgage Advice Bureau, took on the 24-mile trek on Thursday, September 17, to raise funds for The Principle Trust Children’s Charity.

It supports children across Yorkshire who are facing trauma, abuse, poverty, serious illness, disabilities, or mental health challenges.

Rebecca Reeves from Dacre, Son & Hartley said: “We worked with the charity last year and saw first-hand what a difference their work can make to children and families.

“The event was such a success that we were keen to build on that momentum and do something even bigger this year.

“It’s a tough challenge but knowing every step the team took was helping raise money to support children and families across Yorkshire facing adversity kept morale high.”

The team, made up of colleagues from across the region including Baildon, walked 24 miles and climbed more than 5,200 feet, raising enough to fund week-long holidays for five families.

These respite breaks give families a chance to rest, reconnect, and create lasting memories.

Kerry Magson, head of partnerships, service delivery, and engagement at The Principle Trust Children’s Charity, said: “From us all at The Principle Trust, we would like to extend a huge thanks to the team at Dacre, Son & Hartley and the partnered businesses for tackling the Yorkshire Three Peaks challenge and choosing to fundraise for our charity for the second year in a row.”
