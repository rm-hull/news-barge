A new Chinese takeaway in Darlington has left some customers doing a double take over its name.

The Rice Inn has reopened under new management on Parkgate earlier this month, with the business branding it a fresh start in a post on Facebook.

It said: "New management, new energy. Rice Inn Darlington serves authentic Chinese takeaways at prices you'll love. Fresh, fast, and flavour-packed. Try us today."

But some were quick to point out that when said aloud the name of "Rice Inn" could easily be mistaken for the word"ricin", a potentially deadly toxin.

One social media user commented: "Not to be confused with 'Ricin' the poison...."

Another user replied: "My thoughts exactly."

While another said: "I was thinking the same thing."

The business describes itself as "your go-to for tasty Chinese takeout" and says its food is "Hot, fresh, and at your door in no time."

The premises have previously traded under names including Miao Sheng Kitchen, Peking Chef and Happy Eating.

The Rice Inn is open from 4.30pm to 10.30pm on Mondays, Wednesdays, Thursdays, Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays. It is closed on Tuesdays.
