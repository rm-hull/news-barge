*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A shoplifter made off with alcohol from a convenience store in York.

Police have now released a CCTV image that they say was captured at Spar on Brockfield Park Drive, Huntington, during an incident on September 3, when alcohol was stolen.

Police are keen to speak to the individual shown in the image as they may have information crucial to the investigation.

**Recommended reads:**

Anyone with information should contact North Yorkshire Police.

This can be done by emailing PC1488 Bell at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk), using the online service, or calling 101.

Anonymous tips can be made via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
