Clothing was stolen from a high street store in York city centre, prompting a police CCTV appeal.

North Yorkshire Police have released CCTV images following the theft at Zara in Spurriergate on August 11, 2026.

![]()Police want to identify two people following the theft of clothing from Zara store in York. *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

**Recommended reads:**

Police want to identify the people pictured in the footage, who they say may have information that could help the investigation.

Anyone who recognises the individuals is asked to contact PC 1488 Bell via email at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk), or call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

When providing information, please quote reference number 12260162242.
