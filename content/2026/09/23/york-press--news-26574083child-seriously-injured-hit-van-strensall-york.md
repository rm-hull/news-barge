## Child taken to hospital with serious injuries after crash in York

The incident happened in Ox Carr Lane, Strensall *(Image: Newsquest)*

A CHILD has been seriously injured after being hit by a van in York.

The incident happened in Ox Carr Lane in Strensall, between 2.55pm and 3.05pm on Monday, September 21, and involved a white minibus van.

The road was closed for several hours as emergency services were at the scene.

North Yorkshire Police are now appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage.

![]()The accident involving a child and a white minibus van happened on Ox Carr Lane in Strensall between 2.55pm and 3.05pm on monday, September 21. *(Image: Google Maps)*

A spokesperson for the force said the incident involved an 11-year-old boy.

"The child was taken to hospital by ambulance with serious injuries.

"The driver of the minibus is currently assisting us with our enquiries.

"The road was closed for a number of hours while we investigated the scene.

"We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who witnessed the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicle prior to the incident, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage."
