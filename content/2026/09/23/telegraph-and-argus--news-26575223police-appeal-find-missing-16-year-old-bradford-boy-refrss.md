Police are appealing for information to help find a missing 16-year-old boy from Bradford.

Mustafa, from the Manningham and Lower Grange area, was last seen around 7am today (September 23).

He was wearing a black and grey Nike tracksuit, black Nike trainers, and carrying a black duffel bag.

![]()Mustafa has been reported missing *(Image: WYP)*

## Have you seen missing Mustafa?

A police spokesperson said: "Have you seen missing Mustafa from Bradford?

"Bradford CID would like to speak to anyone who has seen or has information about the 16-year-old who was reported missing this morning.

"Mustafa, who is from the Manningham/Lower Grange are was last seen at around 7am.

"He was last seen wearing a black and grey Nike tracksuit, Black Nike Trainers and carrying a black duffel bag.

"Officers are concerned for his welfare and anyone who can help us find him should get in touch on 101 referencing police log 241 of 23 September.

"Information can be given online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat"
