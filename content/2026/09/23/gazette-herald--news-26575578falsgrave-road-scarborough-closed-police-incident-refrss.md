A MAIN road through a North Yorkshire town has been closed by police after a crash.

North Yorkshire Police have warned Scarborough residents that Falsgrave Road is currently closed due to a crash.

The closure begins at Sainsbury's lights towards Seamer Road with traffic diversions in place via Victoria Road and Roscoe Street – something which the force hope will “prevent a backlog in the area.

"The road has been closed while emergency services attend the scene. Motorists are advised to avoid the area and find alternative routes where possible," said a spokesperson for the police.

They also warned that Sainsbury's car park is very busy.
