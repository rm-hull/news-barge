Owners of a particular type of vehicle are being urged to be vigilant when it comes to security.

The plea comes after two Nissan Navara pick-ups were stolen overnight on 21 to 22 September from a village in the Ryedale area.

RECOMMENDED READING:

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "From making sure you lock it, removing the keys and removing valuables, to installing a tracker, we’re urging owners to ensure they take as many crime prevention measures as possible.

"Locking it, even when filling up or parked on your drive, greatly reduces the possibility of it being targeted by an opportunist thief. Make sure you haven’t left any windows or the sunroof open. Use a lockable garage if you have one.

"If you haven’t already, consider investing in a tracking system. These make it far more likely that police will be able to find your stolen vehicle – and arrest those responsible. Steering wheel locks, pedal locks, immobilisers and CCTV systems can also provide a strong deterrent, ensuring your vehicle isn’t seen as an ‘easy target’."

Police also recommend:

If you have a keyless entry car, follow these measures:

• When at home keep your car key (and the spare) well away from the car

• Put the keys in a screened or signal-blocking pouch, such as a Faraday Bag or box and check if it is still working every few months

• Reprogramme your keys if you buy a second-hand car

• Turn off wireless signals on your fob when it's not being used.

"While no security system is fool-proof, taking precautionary measures will significantly reduce the risk of theft or damage to your vehicle.

"Visit our website for more security advice - see link in comments

"Please report suspicious vehicles or people in your neighbourhood. You know what looks out of place in your local community, so please trust your instinct and report any suspicions you have.

"To report a crime that has already happened, or report suspicious activity, you can report online via our website or by calling 101. "

If a crime is happening now, please call us on 999.
