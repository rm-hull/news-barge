UPDATE: The road has now been reopened.

A broken-down lorry is causing disruption on a North Yorkshire roundabout, leading to a road closure.

Otley Road, towards Beckwithshaw, is temporarily shut, with drivers urged to find alternative routes.

North Yorkshire Police officers are helping to recover the heavy goods vehicle that broke down on the roundabout between the A59 and the B6161.
