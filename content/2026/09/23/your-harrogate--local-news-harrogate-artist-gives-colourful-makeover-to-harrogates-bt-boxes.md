BT boxes around Harrogate have been given a colourful makeover celebrating the town's floral and spa heritage.

Harrogate BID funded the project over the summer, with mural artist Murals by Sarah creating the designs.

A Harrogate BID spokesperson said:

> “The designs welcome visitors to Harrogate while celebrating the floral and spa heritage that makes our town so distinctive.”

Among the newly painted boxes is one on Kings Road, outside Harrogate Convention Centre and next to the historic King’s Road Well.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ab383828f833271d29.jpg)

Its design draws inspiration from artwork at the Royal Baths, Harrogate’s spa water and the serpents that feature in the town’s history and imagery.

The nearby well is another reminder of Harrogate’s past as a spa destination, when visitors travelled from far and wide to experience its mineral waters.

Each of the painted boxes features smaller details for passers-by to discover as they explore the town.
