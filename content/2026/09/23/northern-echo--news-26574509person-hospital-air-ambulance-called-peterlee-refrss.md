A person has been taken to hospital after an incident in Peterlee.

An ambulance crew was flagged down at a premises on Essington Way on Monday (September 21) after a person took unwell just before 4pm.

Several other ambulance crews were then sent to the scene, including a Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS) helicopter, which landed just before 4.30pm.

One person was treated at the scene before being taken to Sunderland Royal Hospital.

Their condition remains unknown.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "At 3.50pm on 21 September, a double-crewed ambulance was flagged down at a premises on Essington Way, Peterlee to help with an unwell person.

“We also dispatched a clinical team leader and were supported by Great North Air Ambulance Service. One patient was taken by road to Sunderland Royal Hospital."

“We had a paramedic and doctor on board our aircraft and they arrived on scene at 4.28pm. They assessed and treated a patient and returned back to base.”
