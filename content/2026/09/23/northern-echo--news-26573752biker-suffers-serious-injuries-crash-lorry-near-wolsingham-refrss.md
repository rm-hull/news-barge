A biker suffered "serious injuries" when his motorcycle collided with a lorry on a major County Durham road.

The two-vehicle smash took place just before 3pm yesterday on the A689 near Wolsingham.

The motorbike rider, a man in his 60s, suffered serious injuries and was taken to Newcastle’s Royal Victoria Infirmary where he remains.

No other injuries have been reported.

The road was closed between East End Bridge and Wolsingham Park while emergency crews worked at the scene.

![]()The road was closed for some time as emergency services dealt with the crash. *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

Pictures show the road blocked off with cones and a closure sign in place.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) sent two ambulance crews and a clinical team leader before requesting help from the Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS)

A NEAS spokesperson said: “We received a call at 2.54pm on Tuesday to reports of a road traffic incident on East End in Wolsingham.

“We dispatched two ambulance crews and a clinical team leader to the scene, and requested support from our colleagues at the Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS).

“One patient was taken to the RVI by road for further treatment.”
