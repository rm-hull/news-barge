A man was taken to hospital after a car reportedly overturned in a crash on a busy North East coastal road.

Emergency services were called to the B1287 near Lord Byron's Walk in Seaham at around 1.40pm on Wednesday (September 23).

Witnesses said a car had overturned on the coastal road heading towards Ryhope.

A spokesman for Durham Police said: "We were called to reports of a one-car collision on the B1287, in Seaham, shortly before 1.40pm today (Wednesday, September 23).

"The driver was taken to hospital for treatment."

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on Lord Byron's Walk, Seaham, shortly after 1.20pm.

"We dispatched a double-crewed ambulance and a clinical team leader. One patient was taken to Sunderland Royal Hospital."

The driver's injuries and the cause of the crash are not yet known.
