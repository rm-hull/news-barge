An award-winning wedding venue is winning praise for its beautiful setting and "fabulous food".

Tucked away in Northumberland, Le Petit Chateau and its sister venue, The William de Percy, combine boutique accommodation, dining, and striking interiors that help set them apart from more traditional country hotels.

Inspired by a French country manor, the Otternburn venue has a 4.2-star TripAdvisor rating from more than 650 reviews and received the site’s Travellers’ Choice award in 2026.

![]()The outdoor wedding ceremony space at Le Petit Château, with rows of elegant seating leading to a picturesque chapel-style building set among landscaped gardens. *(Image: Nicholas Georgiou and APARTMENT GROUP)*

Upon arrival, guests enter a world of crystal chandeliers, antique furnishings, landscaped gardens and individually designed rooms.

It offers a romantic Northumberland countryside escape and a base for exploring attractions such as Northumberland National Park.

Many visitors arrive for weddings, with the venue earning a reputation as one of the region's most distinctive celebration settings.

Recent reviews suggest it continues to impress.

One guest, who visited to celebrate a wedding anniversary, praised the "beautiful setting", "great staff" and "fabulous food", describing the stay as a memorable way to mark the occasion.

Another reviewer who stayed with their young family praised the hotel’s "very quiet midweek relaxing stay".

They highlighted the spacious room, welcoming staff, quality food, and attractive grounds, adding that it was well located for exploring Northumberland.

Wedding guests have praised it too.

![]()Le Petit Château welcomes four-legged guests too, with dog-friendly accommodation designed for owners looking to enjoy a luxury break with their pets. *(Image: Kristos Kabiotis Photography and APARTMENT GROUP)*

A recent visitor said they looked out over the fountain and gardens from a Juliette balcony before attending a "beautifully decorated" ceremony.

They praised the helpful staff and said the food served throughout the celebrations was "delicious".

The food is another major draw.

![]()Dining at The William de Percy, where guests can enjoy hearty pub classics, local produce and a carefully selected range of drinks in a cosy setting. *(Image: Kristos Kabiotis Photography and APARTMENT GROUP)*

The William de Percy restaurant regularly receives positive comments from guests, while breakfast is another frequently praised aspect of a stay.

One visitor described dinner as "delicious and good value for money" and said they left feeling relaxed and eager to return.

With dog-friendly rooms, complimentary breakfast, picturesque surroundings, and a distinctive style that feels a world away from everyday life, it is easy to see why Le Petit Chateau continues to attract visitors seeking a little luxury in the Northumberland countryside.
