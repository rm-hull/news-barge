A road closed by a spectacular water mains burst could stay partially shut until the end of next week.

The A1041 near Camblesforth remains completely closed today (Wednesday).

It follows the leak which shot water high into the air from early on Tuesday morning.

#### Closure location

Today, Yorkshire Water gave ***YorkMix*** an update on the situation.

A company spokesperson said: “We’re currently repairing a burst main on the A1041 near Camblesforth.

“Though we’re working as quickly as we can, it is proving to be a complex repair which will require the manufacture of a tailor-made fitting.

“In the meantime, we’ll still be on site, and the road is closed in both directions for the safety of our colleagues and road users.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/burst-water-main-a1041-camblesforth-22-sept-2026-full.webp)

“At the moment, we expect to be in the area until next Friday (2 October), but we will look at reopening the road under temporary traffic lights as soon as it is safe to do so.

“We are hopeful that this will be in the next few days.”

”We apologise for the inconvenience and thank everyone in the area for their patience.”

In the meantime, drivers face a long diversion via the A19.
