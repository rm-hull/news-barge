The body of a 63-year-old man who was reported missing from his home earlier this year has been found.

Colin, who lived in the Hetton-le-Hole area, disappeared on May 2.

Northumbria Police launched extensive enquiries to find him, including trawling CCTV and launching several public appeals.

But on July 17, human remains were discovered in the High Moorsley area, close to Hetton.

Following forensic analysis, officers have today, Wednesday, September 23, confirmed the remains were those of Colin.

His next of kin have been informed.

Police said there are believed to be no suspicious circumstances surrounding his death.

Detective Inspector Nick Went, of the force's Missing Investigation Team, said: “Our thoughts are firmly with Colin’s family and friends at what I’m sure remains an extremely difficult time for them.

“I’d like to thank all of the officers involved in our investigation to locate him and the many members of the public who submitted information to us and shared our appeals on social media. It is much appreciated.

“As is standard practice, a report into Colin’s death will now be prepared for the coroner.”
