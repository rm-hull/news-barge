Police have released a CCTV image following the theft of Jellycat toys from RHS Harlow Carr's Plant and Gift Centre.

The theft happened at the Harrogate shop on 31st August.

North Yorkshire Police would like to speak to the person pictured, who may have information that could help their investigation.

Anyone who recognises the person is asked to email PC1488 Bell at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

*Information can also be submitted through the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting ‘Tell us about’, then ‘An existing case or report’. Live chat is available Monday to Friday, from 9am to 5pm.*

*Anyone unable to use these options can call 101. Alternatively, contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.*

*Please quote reference 12260170503 when providing information.*
