A YORKSHIRE charity is appealing for help in supporting children in need this winter.

The Yorkshire Children’s Charity (YCC) is appealing for support in filling a £142,000 funding shortfall this winter amid more bids for help across the counties.

Bosses at the charity say they already received requests to help 2,500 children across the region through its 2026 Winter Essentials campaign - exceeding the total received throughout 2025 - only with demand expecting to rise.

As the weather worsens, the charity is preparing to assist at least 3,000 children this winter - 25 per cent more than the 2,373 children supported during the first phase of last year’s campaign.

Charlotte Farrington, founder and CEO of The Yorkshire Children’s Charity, said: "Recent inflation figures are another reminder of the pressure families are under.

"When a household is already struggling to afford the basics, there is very little left to cut back on. As everyday costs continue to rise, we are seeing the consequences for children here in Yorkshire."

The Winter Essentials campaign provides each child with a £50 bundle containing a warm coat, shoes, pyjamas, hat, scarf, gloves and bedding.

These items are distributed through schools, social workers and key workers who identify children in critical need and apply for support on their behalf.

Charlotte added: "Behind every one of these bundles is a child. A child who needs a coat to walk to school, shoes that keep their feet dry or proper bedding to sleep under at night.

"We already have applications for 2,500 children and we know more are coming. Our team are working hard to make sure those children get the support they need, but we cannot do it without Yorkshire behind us.

"It costs just £50 to provide a child with a bundle of winter essentials. That is £50 that can make an immediate, practical difference to how a child sleeps, gets to school and takes part in everyday life.

"We have £142,000 still to find to deliver both phases of this year’s campaign.

![]()More than 3000 are expected to be in need of help *(Image: JO RITCHIE)*

"It is a big number, but the people and businesses of Yorkshire have shown us time and again what happens when good people come together. Every donation, however big or small, gets us closer."

The campaign has already exceeded the total received throughout 2025, with applications still open and expected to rise as conditions worsen.

Charlotte said: "This campaign is about getting children through winter with the things many of us simply take for granted.

"But it is also about dignity, confidence, attendance and helping schools build relationships with families who may be having an incredibly difficult time.

"With costs continuing to rise and demand for our support already ahead of last year, getting these essentials to children who need them has never felt more important."

Schools, social workers and other key workers can continue to apply for Winter Essentials support through Yorkshire Children’s Charity’s website or by emailing [wintersupport@yorkshirechildrenscharity.org.](mailto:wintersupport@yorkshirechildrenscharity.org.)

Members of the public and businesses can support the 2026 Winter Essentials campaign by donating through Yorkshire Children’s Charity’s website.
