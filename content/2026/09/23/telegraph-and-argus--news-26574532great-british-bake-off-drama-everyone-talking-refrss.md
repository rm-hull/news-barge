Fans of The Great British Bake Off were shocked as the first episode ended in a huge drama.

No one left the tent in a surprise twist on the opening episode.

The decision was made after contestant Shannon was forced to withdraw midway through the episode due to illness, leaving judges and producers with an unexpected dilemma.

Nigella Lawson said: "We had a big decision to make due to Shannon's withdrawal.

"We thought the fairest thing was to bring everybody back next week, and go from there."

At the end of the episode, presenter Alison Hammond announced the news.

She said: "The first baker leaving the tent is... no one!

"You are all safe, well done!"

Although no one was eliminated, a Star Baker was still named.

Law student Mo earned the title after impressing judges with his peanut and caramel chocolate stout Signature Challenge and a Showstopper featuring lemon sponge, Swiss meringue buttercream, and blueberry compote.

## How Tom got on

Among those who caught the judges’ attention was Tom, a 25-year-old hairdresser from Bradford.

Describing himself as a "northern diva," Tom is known for his witty, pretty, and delicious bakes.

He said: "I have dreamed about being on Bake Off for so long that I kind of knew one day I would get on.

"I never imagined it would be the first year I applied that I would get on."

Tom’s love of baking began in childhood, when he would visit his nan while his older brother played football.

Before he was 10, he had already mastered the brandy snap.

Now working in a busy salon, Tom regularly brings in baked treats for his colleagues, who are especially fond of his salted caramel brownies.

Outside of baking, he enjoys travelling and recently spent seven months exploring Southeast Asia with his boyfriend.

Tom said: "I couldn’t believe it when I got the call.

"Throughout the whole application process, whenever one step was completed, I just thought to myself, right, that will be the last one and they won’t be contacting me again but I couldn’t believe it when it kept on going.

"I think I just said, 'omg omg omg omg omg' over and over again, then I bought a bottle of prosecco on my way home to surprise my boyfriend with the news."

The Great British Bake Off continues next Tuesday on Channel 4.
