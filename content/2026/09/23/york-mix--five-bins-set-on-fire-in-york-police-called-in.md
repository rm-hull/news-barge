Five bins were set on fire on a York road early this morning (Wednesday).

Firefighters were called to the incident on **Haxby Road** at**12.44am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from York responded to a report of a bin on fire.

#### Haxby Road, York

“On arrival the crews extinguished two large wheeled bins and a further three that were found smouldering using one hose reel jet.

“Police were informed due to the believed deliberate nature of the fires and the incident has been left in the hands of police.”
