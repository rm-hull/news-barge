Food lovers are being invited to sample dishes from around the world when Darlington Restaurant Week returns next month.

The biannual event returns from October 23 to October 30, giving residents and visitors the chance to explore a range of restaurants, hotels and cafes across the town.

A number of businesses have already signed up to take part, offering specially created Restaurant Week menus and promotions designed to showcase the variety of food available in Darlington.

Among the venues taking part are Babul's, Bannatyne Hotel, Blackwell Grange Hotel, Café Spice, Star of Bengal, TAO Street Food and Noodle Bar, and Uno Momento.

Visitors will be able to choose from cuisines spanning South Asian, Bengali, Indian, Bangladeshi, Asian street food and Italian dishes, alongside menus offered by some of the town's hotels.

The week aims to encourage people to discover new venues and support local businesses while enjoying specially prepared menus and offers.

Restaurant owners who want to take part in Darlington Restaurant Week have been encouraged to contact organisers via [enjoy@darlington.gov.uk.](mailto:enjoy@darlington.gov.uk.)

Further details, including more venues and their menus, will be announced in the near future and will be available through the Enjoy Darlington website.
