MARKS & Spencer made a record 920,000 free birthday treats available.

The giveaways were made available to Sparks loyalty scheme members in September, with the most popular day being September 26.

Sharry Cramond, marketing and loyalty director at Marks & Spencer, said: "We are so proud to give away close to one million birthday treats to our Sparks members every month, it's one of our favourite ways to say thank you to our customers.

"Even if everyone else forgets your birthday, M&S won’t.

"It only takes ten seconds to add your birthday to your Sparks profile and it’s the easiest birthday present you’ll ever receive.

"The only question is, which treat will you choose to celebrate your special day?"

However, the retailer revealed that 60 per cent of September-born Sparks members missed out because they hadn’t added their date of birth to their profile.

Eligible members receive a free treat in the M&S app at the start of their birthday month.

Treat options include cookies, truffles, Percy Pigs, roses, or crémant.

Cookies were the most popular choice, followed by Swiss chocolate truffles and cake.
