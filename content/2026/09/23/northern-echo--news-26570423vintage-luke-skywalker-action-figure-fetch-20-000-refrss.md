A rare Kenner Star Wars Luke Skywalker action figure featuring the highly sought-after Double Telescoping lightsaber is set to go under the hammer at Vectis Auctions on October 15.

Lot 623 is an AFA-graded vintage 12-back C carded figure estimated at £15,000–£20,000, with a CIB certificate of authenticity and a Hollywood Heroes certificate detailing its provenance.

The carded figure graded 80NM C80 B80 F80, was bought on the open market in 2015 and was originally obtained from the family of a General Mills employee who worked directly on the original Star Wars line in 1977.

![]()The Luke Skywalker figure is one of the scarce Double Telescoping lightsaber variants prized by Star Wars collectors worldwide *(Image: VECTIS AUCTIONS)*

The employee handled the Early Bird mailers and fulfilled orders placed through Kenner’s “Early Bird Promotion”.

After the promotion, General Mills used the surplus figures for mass production, pairing the “Early Bird” figures with cards and bubbles.

This figure is one of the Early Bird Lukes from the original supply of mail away inventory.

![]()The back of the carded Luke Skywalker figure, showing packaging details from Kenner's original Star Wars range *(Image: VECTIS AUCTIONS)*

The Early Bird Certificate package was released in 1977, with further runs in 1978. The original Farm Boy Luke figure had a yellow lightsaber barrel containing a thinner blade that extended to a longer, film-accurate length.

The fragile inner blade was expensive to produce and posed a potential safety risk to children, so it was quickly replaced with a single blade.

Only three vintage Star Wars figures feature the double-telescoping lightsaber - Ben (Obi-Wan) Kenobi, Darth Vader and Luke Skywalker (Farm Boy).

Their rarity makes these three among the most sought-after Star Wars figures, especially in high-grade, carded condition.

The unpunched carded figure is estimated at £15,000 to £20,000 and goes under the hammer atThornaby-based Vectis Auctions on October 15.
