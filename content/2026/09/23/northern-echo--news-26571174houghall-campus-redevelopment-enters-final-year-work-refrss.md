A County Durham college redevelopment is entering its final year, with new facilities designed to train workers for some of the region’s fastest-growing industries.

East Durham College’s Houghall Campus is being transformed into a centre for land-based, environmental and sustainability education, with the upgraded campus is due to open to students in September 2027.

Scott Bullock, principal and CEO of East Durham College, said: "Exceptional facilities make a real difference to a student’s experience. The environment in which they learn inspires ambition, raises expectations and gives students the confidence to succeed.

"That is why the investment in Houghall Campus is so important. We are creating an exceptional learning environment where students can develop the technical knowledge, practical skills and experience they need to succeed in the world of work.

"When our first students arrive in September 2027, they will have the opportunity to learn in state-of-the-art facilities designed around the industries they are preparing to enter."

Construction began in January 2026, after enabling works started in October 2025.

![]()Houghall Campus redevelopment enters its final year of construction. *(Image: Supplied)*

Tilbury Douglas is delivering the project for the Department for Education, with completion expected by April 2027.

The campus will include specialist teaching facilities, digital learning spaces and areas for practical and outdoor learning.

It will also support East Durham College’s growing courses in agriculture, horticulture, environmental management, animal care and the wider land-based sector.

The investment comes as the college gains recognition for its industry-focused training and education.

![]()Houghall Campus redevelopment enters its final year of construction *(Image: Supplied)*

East Durham College recently recorded a 75 per cent apprenticeship achievement rate, well above the 65.7 per cent national average and placing it among the UK’s top 10 college providers.

The college is also the North East’s fastest-growing apprenticeship provider, based on starts between August 2025 and January 2026 compared with the previous year.

Houghall Campus ranks fifth nationally for achievement rates among land-based education providers and is the North’s highest-performing land-based college.

![]()New facilities taking shape at East Durham College’s Houghall Campus. *(Image: Supplied)*

Helen McCoy, principal of Houghall Campus, said: "Our recent apprenticeship and T Level successes demonstrate the strength of the education and training we are providing across the College.

"Houghall Campus will build on that success, giving students an outstanding environment in which to develop the skills and experience employers need.

"With one year to go, seeing Houghall Campus take shape is incredibly exciting. This is about creating opportunities for the next generation and ensuring our region has the skilled people it needs to support future growth."

Designed by Jefferson Sheard Architects, the campus will blend the site’s heritage with modern, sustainable design.

The development includes a two-storey teaching block with classrooms and digital labs, plus specialist agricultural facilities including a hay barn, sheep shed, grain store and bio-secure boot wash.

Existing buildings will be refurbished to make them more sustainable and accessible.

The plans also include new landscaping, improved site access and areas for hands-on outdoor learning.

Ms McCoy said: "September 2027 will mark a new chapter for Houghall Campus, our students and the employers we work with.

"We are looking forward to welcoming the first new cohort and seeing them make the most of these exceptional new facilities."

New aerial images show "significant progress" on the redevelopment.

East Durham College hopes the campus will help meet growing demand for expertise in sustainable food production, environmental stewardship and the green economy.
