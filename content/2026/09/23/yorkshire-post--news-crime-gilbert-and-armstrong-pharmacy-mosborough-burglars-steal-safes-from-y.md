# Gilbert and Armstrong Pharmacy, Mosborough: Burglars steal safes from Yorkshire village pharmacy and Post Office in overnight raid

## Burglars are reported to have stolen two safes from a village pharmacy and Post Office in an overnight raid.

At 10.39pm on Tuesday night, South Yorkshire Police were called to reports of a burglary at Gilbert and Armstrong Pharmacy on Queen Street in Mosborough on the outskirts of Sheffield. The building also houses the village's Post Office.

A spokesperson for South Yorkshire Police said two safes were reportedly stolen in an incident involving two individuals who are still at large.

The spokesperson said: “It is reported that two individuals arrived at the pharmacy in a silver Honda CR-V, left the vehicle and forced entry into the pharmacy.

![The pharmacy in Mosborough is also home to a Post Office. Picture: Google Maps](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjY2Y2VmYTY0LWUzY2QtNDE2Mi1hNWMxLWQzMGYyZTliMTQyMjoyNjJiY2U2Ny03OTdkLTQ3NTMtOGMxYS0zOTJhOGMyNDUyOTI=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“They are alleged to have stolen several items from the location and left in the same vehicle.

“Officers attended and carried out an area search for the Honda to no gain. An investigation has been launched and enquiries are ongoing.

“Anyone with information that could help our investigation is asked to get in touch online or by calling 101, quoting incident number 1122 of 22 September 2026.”
