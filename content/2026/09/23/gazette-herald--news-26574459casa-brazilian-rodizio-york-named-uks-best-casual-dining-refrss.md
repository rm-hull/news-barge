A YORK restaurant has been named the best casual dining spot in the UK for the second year running - as well as entering the global top ten.

Casa Brazilian Rodizio in Micklegate has topped Tripadvisor’s Best of the Best Casual Dining Restaurants list in the UK for the second consecutive year.

The independent restaurant has also secured the No. 8 spot worldwide.

The Tripadvisor Best of the Best awards are based on reviews and experiences shared by diners, making the recognition especially meaningful to Casa’s team.

Eduardo, owner of Casa Brazilian Rodizio, said: "What makes this award so special is that it comes from the experiences and reviews of our guests.

![]()Eduardo, owner of Casa Brazillian Rodizio *(Image: Provided)*

"We have an amazing team who put so much passion and hard work into Casa every single day, but none of this would be possible without the people who choose to dine with us.

"We want to say a huge thank you to everyone who has visited, returned, celebrated a special occasion with us, recommended us to friends or taken the time to leave a review.

![]()Meat at Casa Rodizio *(Image: Provided)*

"The support we receive from York, as well as visitors from around the world, is incredible.

"To be an independent restaurant here in York and be recognised as number one in the UK for two years running and number eight in the world is difficult to put into words. We’re incredibly grateful and very excited about what comes next."

![]()The team at Casa Rodizio *(Image: Provided)*

Casa offers an authentic Brazilian rodizio dining experience, featuring a continuous selection of freshly grilled meats carved at the table, alongside a hot and cold buffet and customer favourites like grilled cinnamon pineapple.

Casa, formerly known as Vaquieors, has called York home for more than 10 years, and staff say the city’s welcoming atmosphere has been central to their success.

![]()Casa Rodizio's salad bar *(Image: Provided)*

A spokesman for Casa added: "Casa is incredibly proud to be part of York’s independent hospitality scene and looks forward to continuing to welcome guests to its little taste of Brazil in the city for many years to come."
