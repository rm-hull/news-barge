A child was seriously hurt in a collision with a minibus in York.

It happened on **Ox Carr Lane**,** Strensall**, between** 2.55pm**and** 3.05pm**on** Monday** (21 September).

The child and a white minibus van collided. The child was taken to hospital by ambulance with serious injuries.

#### Ox Carr Lane, Strensall

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The driver of the minibus is currently assisting us with our enquiries.

“The road was closed for a number of hours while we investigated the scene.

“We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who witnessed the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicle prior to the incident, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage.”
