Jet2 has announced a major partnership with technology company Adobe that could see artificial intelligence play a bigger role in how customers discover, book and enjoy their holidays.

The travel giant, which first started operating from Leeds-Bradford Airport in 2003. said the multi-year agreement will help deliver more personalised recommendations, offers and support to millions of customers.

## AI-powered holiday recommendations

Under the partnership, Jet2 will use Adobe's customer experience technology to provide tailored suggestions based on customers' travel preferences and booking history.

Holidaymakers could receive personalised destination ideas, including family-friendly resorts, city breaks and customised itinerary recommendations while on holiday.

The company said the technology will help ensure customers receive more relevant offers and communications throughout their holiday journey.

## **New customer experience lab**

As part of the deal, Jet2 and Adobe will create a new Customer Experience Lab, bringing together technology specialists and Jet2 teams to develop and test AI-powered services.

The initiative will focus on finding smarter ways to match customers with holidays that best suit their interests, preferences and previous travel choices.

Jet2 said the collaboration would enable new services to be developed and rolled out more quickly.

## Yorkshire firm's latest technology investment

Jet2, which is headquartered in Leeds and operates flights from Leeds Bradford Airport, serves millions of passengers every year through Jet2.com and Jet2holidays.

The company said the partnership builds on its customer-focused approach and will help deliver a more personalised experience while maintaining the service standards it is known for.

## **'Customers are at the heart of everything we do'**

David Hills, chief customer officer at Jet2, said: "At Jet2, our customers are at the heart of everything we do.

"Customers know they will always receive a VIP experience when they holiday with us.

"Working with Adobe will enable us to serve our customers even better, using the power of AI to make every interaction more personal, more relevant, more timely and always helpful."

Nathan Hancock, vice president and managing director for the UK, Ireland, Middle East and Africa at Adobe, said the partnership would help create more personalised travel experiences and faster, more relevant customer interactions.

The companies said the new technology will also help Jet2 respond more quickly to changing travel trends and customer demand.
