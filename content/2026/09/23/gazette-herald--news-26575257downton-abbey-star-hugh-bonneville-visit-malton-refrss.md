Paddington-star Hugh Bonneville is bringing his newest children’s adventure to a North Yorkshire town.

Hugh, the actor children know best for *Paddington* - one of the UK’s most treasured family films – is heading to Malton in his latest role – bestselling children’s author.

The actor and author will appear at the Wesley Centre in Malton on Friday November 20, at 4pm for a special event celebrating his new children’s book, Rory Sparkes and the Spy Who Loved Tea.

Known by millions as Mr Brown in the loveable bear film franchise, Bonneville is also famed for playing Lord Grantham in the global phenomenon Downton Abbey – the most successful British costume drama in decades, watched by 120 million across 200 territories.

Hugh will be in conversation with acclaimed children’s author Ashley Thorpe, before both authors meet readers and sign books at a post-talk signing hosted by Kemps General Store & Bookshop.

Published by Bloomsbury Children’s Books in October, *Rory Sparkes and the Spy Who Loved Tea* is the latest adventure featuring Hugh’s irrepressible young hero Rory Sparkes.

The story sees Rory stumble across a coded message hidden in a recipe book, setting him on the trail of mysterious secret messages, some distinctly suspicious tea-drinking and a possible spy ring.

The book follows Hugh’s first children’s novel, Rory Sparkes and the Elephant in the Room, and once again combines comedy, mystery and adventure with illustrations by Loretta Schauer.

The Malton event will give young readers and families the opportunity to hear Hugh talk about creating Rory, his move into writing for children, where his comic ideas come from and how he approaches storytelling after a career bringing some of Britain’s best-known screen characters to life.

Interviewing Hugh will be Ashley Thorpe, one of the most exciting voices in contemporary children’s fiction.

Based in Manchester, Ashley is the author of fantasy adventures *The Boy to Beat the Gods and Spirit Warriors*. His work has received widespread recognition:* Spirit Warriors*was shortlisted for the Waterstones Children’s Book Prize 2026, while* The Boy to Beat the Gods* was shortlisted for the Jhalak Prize, Branford Boase Award and The Week Junior Book Awards.

Ashley is now working on his next novel, *Treasure Rush: Curse of the Golden Coin,* due to be published by Usborne in 2027. The archaeological adventure follows brothers Zac and Remy as they race to return an ancient artefact to a hidden tomb and break a curse hanging over their family.

The pairing promises a lively conversation between two storytellers whose work combines humour, adventure and larger-than-life characters, with plenty for aspiring young writers and enthusiastic readers to enjoy.

Hugh Bonneville: Rory Sparkes and the Spy Who Loved Tea. In conversation with Ashley Thorpe. Friday 20 November 2026, 4pm, The Wesley Centre, Malton.
