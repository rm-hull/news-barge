A North East school has closed for the rest of the day after a suspected gas leak severely disrupted its 'catering facilities'.

Our Lady & St Bede Catholic Academy on Bishopton Road West in Stockton closed after the incident, sending all pupils home.

Headteacher Ms Wilkinson said the decision was made as 'the safety of all pupils is paramount'.

The school is expected to reopen tomorrow, but parents will be updated through the usual channels.

A message to parents said: "Due to a suspected gas leak, Mrs Wilkinson has made the decision to close the school for the remainder of the day.

"The incident has severely disrupted the catering facilities and the safety of all pupils is paramount.

"It is the expectation that the school will be open tomorrow but communication will be sent to parents via the usual channels and updates will be posted on Facebook."
