Bus services in Darlington are set for a major shake-up, with route changes and timetable changes coming into force.

The changes, which come into effect on Sunday, September 27, follow a review of services supported by Tees Valley Combined Authority and customer feedback.

Operator Arriva said the updates would "provide new travel opportunities, improved connections and better links between communities" across the Darlington and Stockton areas.

### Major changes to Darlington town services

Service 10 will be revised to focus on journeys between Darlington and Whinfield, while new Services 86 and 87 will provide dedicated links to Sadberge, Bishopton and Stillington.

Service 16 will be withdrawn and replaced by a revised Service 18, which Arriva says will provide "enhanced cross-town links and improved connections" across Darlington.

### Darlington Arena, Mowden and West Park to gain new links

Service 17 will be extended to Darlington Arena via Neasham Road and Geneva Crescent.

The extension will create a direct link between Mowden and Darlington Rail Station.

A new Sunday daytime service will be introduced, alongside Monday to Saturday evening buses between Darlington Arena and the town centre, continuing to Mowden as Service 18A.

Service 18 is also being extended to West Park Hospital and Faverdale.

Under the new route, buses will travel via Abbey Road, Hummersknott, Mowden and Cockerton, providing links to Queen Elizabeth College, Darlington Rail Station, Cockerton Village, Faverdale's retail and industrial areas and West Park Hospital.

The revised route will also create a new connection between Mowden, Cockerton Village and Faverdale.

Sunday daytime journeys and Monday to Saturday evening services will also be introduced, with evening buses operating as Service 18A.

Service 18A will run on Monday to Saturday evenings, linking Darlington Arena, the town centre, Mowden, West Park and Faverdale.

### Earlier journeys and new connections

Passengers using Service 1 between Darlington and Tow Law will see two timetable changes.

The weekday morning journey currently departing Faverdale Grange at 6.13am will instead start at 6.12am from Westgate Crescent.

Meanwhile, the Monday to Saturday evening service from Tindale Crescent to Crook will leave five minutes earlier, departing at 10.37pm and arriving in Crook at 11.02pm.

The earlier arrival is intended to provide a connection onto the 11.05pm X46 service towards Durham.

There will also be minor timetable changes on Service 3 between Skerne Park and Hummersknott, including an adjustment to the afternoon school journey, which will leave Hummersknott at 3.09pm to better match school finishing times.

### New options for Durham and Aycliffe passengers

Changes are also planned for Service 7 between Darlington and Durham.

Peak-time weekday journeys will be revised, while an additional morning service to New College in Durham will be introduced.

Late-night travellers will also benefit from the 11.30pm service from Durham being extended to Aycliffe Village.

On Service 8 between Darlington and Spennymoor, the 2.52pm journey from Spennymoor will depart at 2.57pm and operate five minutes later throughout.

### Faverdale and Newton Aycliffe services revised

Passengers using Service 19 between Darlington and Faverdale will see the route revised to provide more direct access into Faverdale.

The service will no longer serve John Fowler Way.

One bus each hour will terminate at Aldi Warehouse, while another hourly journey will continue to Newton Aycliffe town centre via High Grange, West Park Hospital, Heighington, School Aycliffe and Newton Aycliffe Rail Halt.

### New Services 86 and 87

New Services 86 and 87 will provide links between Darlington, communities around Stillington and Stockton.

The routes will replace the existing Service 10 between Darlington and Stillington and Service X86 between Stockton and Stillington.

The services will operate via Tornado Way, Sadberge, Little Stainton, Bishopton, Great Stainton during peak hours, Old Stillington, Stillington, Whitton, Redmarshall, Carlton, Letch Lane, North Tees Hospital and Durham Road before continuing to Stockton High Street.

Journeys will serve both ends of Stockton High Street, providing improved access to the Riverside Park area.

### X66 timetable adjustment

Passengers travelling between Darlington and Middlesbrough on the X66 will see evening and Sunday journeys operate five minutes later than at present.
