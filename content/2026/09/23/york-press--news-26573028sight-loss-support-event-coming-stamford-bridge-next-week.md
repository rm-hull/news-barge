A CARE home is set to host a community talk aimed at supporting people living with sight loss.

Stamford Bridge Beaumont, located in Stamford Bridge and operated by Barchester Healthcare, will welcome Vicky Colombi from My Sight York for an informative session on Monday, September 28, at 10am.

Ms Colombi will share details about the services and support available to individuals living with sight loss in York and the surrounding areas.

Nikki Mould, operations manager at Stamford Bridge Beaumont, said: "We are really looking forward to welcoming Vicky to Stamford Bridge Beaumont.

"Sight loss can have a real impact on a person’s confidence and independence, so having the opportunity to learn more about the support that is available locally will be incredibly valuable.

"We’re passionate about opening our doors to the wider community and creating opportunities where people can come together, meet local organisations and find out about services that could make a positive difference to their lives."

The event is open to residents, relatives, and members of the local community, and is part of the care home’s ongoing programme of community events designed to connect people with local organisations and resources.

The care home has earned a strong reputation for its community-focused approach and regularly hosts events for residents and neighbours.

Stamford Bridge Beaumont provides residential, nursing, and dementia care for 89 residents, from respite care to long term stays.
