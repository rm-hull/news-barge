POPULAR collectable stuffed toys have been stolen from a garden centre attached to a North Yorkshire attraction.

North Yorkshire Police have released CCTV images of a person it wants to speak to following a theft from a garden centre last month.

The incident took place on August 31 at the RHS Harlow Carr Plant and Gift Centre in Harlow Carr Gardens.
