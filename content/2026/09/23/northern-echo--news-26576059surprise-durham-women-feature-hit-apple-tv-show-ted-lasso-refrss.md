Durham Women have appeared in the latest episode of the television comedy Ted Lasso.

Released today (September 23) the episode shows the Wildcats taking on AFC Richmond in the show's fourth season.

Durham Women teased the cameo on social media with a picture of Ted Lasso, Coach Beard and assistant coach Alice Chilton celebrating on the touchline.

Durham Women wrote: "Heard today's episode of Ted Lasso is worth watching - wonder who these three are celebrating a goal against?

"Streaming now on Apple TV."

Viewers soon discovered that the opponents were Durham Women FC.

The club later called the appearance a special moment in a website statement.

The statement read: "We won’t give any spoilers away, but you can probably guess the result by the image on this page!

"The show is a global phenomenon, and we were delighted to be asked to feature as one of the club’s opponents in Season Four – which focuses on Barclays Women’s Super League 2.

"It’s a fantastic milestone for everyone at the club and the wider women’s game, and a fantastic prelude to our next home fixture – a home Subway Players Cup fixture against Manchester United.

"If you’ve watched the series and wanted to attend a real game, consider this an invitation."

The unexpected TV appearance comes days before Durham's Subway Players Cup home tie against Manchester United, giving the Women's Super League 2 club valuable exposure to an international audience.

Durham Women's appearance is not the only County Durham link to the latest season of Ted Lasso.

County Durham actress Charlotte Riley joined the cast at the start of the season, giving the region an even greater presence in one of television's most popular shows.
