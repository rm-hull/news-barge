![Microsoft Surface Laptop](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/APBPWUnJNeAwVTRechfGSJ-320-80.jpg)

Microsoft's lowest-end systems are getting upgrades to Qualcomm's latest Snapdragon X2 Plus processors.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/bCuY5kfDXkdziu3ezQ7LtE-320-80.jpg)

Microsoft is streamlining the Surface lineup, and the Surface Laptop Studio 2 reportedly won't get a successor.

![Microsoft Surface Pro](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/E2wdVDevxKThwp7kQo5MAN-320-80.png)

Microsoft's new 12-inch Surface Pro and 13-inch Surface Laptop are colorful Snapdragon X Plus machines with relatively affordable prices.

![Microsoft Surface Laptops](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/9Wq6Vp9zcHcBpU5gNmJc5j-320-80.png)

Microsoft is rumored to announce new compact 13-inch Surface Laptop 7 and 12-inch Surface Pro 11 models at their Surface event on Tuesday.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/G9rkRbUQRuF3omzRLEvdMU-320-80.jpg)

An alleged prototype of the Surface Laptop powered by Intel's Lunar Lake chipset was spotted on a Chinese website.

![Microsoft Surface Pro 11 and Surface Laptop 7](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/G9rkRbUQRuF3omzRLEvdMU-320-80.jpg)

The team at iFixit have bought their Microsoft Surface Pro 11 and Surface Laptop 7, and promptly disassembled them to show how repairable the latest tablet and laptop are.

![Microsoft Surface Pro Laptop 5](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/avQbupaBC5SshdnyMUcme3-320-80.jpg)

The Microsoft Surface Laptop 5 is getting new 12th Gen Intel processors, but will no longer have AMD Ryzen options.

![Asus ROG Flow Z13](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/e4NHyM5M8V2WUmoLQdR5pg-320-80.png)

Asus’ CES 2022 gaming laptop lineup includes a Flow Z13 tablet, a bigger Zephyrus Duo, a redesigned G14 with AMD CPUs and GPUs and more.

![Microsoft Surface Pro 8](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qnrb8a3NjJH3boUxbAbB8W-320-80.jpg)

The Surface Pro 8 has slimmed down bezels and a higher thermal envelope, making it a reinvention inside and out.

![Microsoft Surface Duo 2](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/mGjYvH9r9V4e6QYpWnv78E-320-80.jpg)

Microsoft's Surface Duo has 5G and stronger performance due to the Snapdragon Qualcomm 888 processor. That's on top of several minor changes to make it feel more premium.
