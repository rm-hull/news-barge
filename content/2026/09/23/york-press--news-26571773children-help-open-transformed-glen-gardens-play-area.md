## Pupils help open transformed play area at York's Glen Gardens

Children from Tang Hall Primary School and Heworth Primary School attended the event alongside Cllr Bob Webb and Cllr Claire Douglas and representatives from City of York Council. *(Image: Supplied)*

Children from two primary schools have helped open a transformed play area at a popular York park.

Pupils from Tang Hall Primary School and Heworth Primary School helped shape the Glen Gardens play area by reviewing designs and sharing their views earlier this year.

Councillor Bob Webb, City of York Council’s executive member for children, young people and education, said: "It's fantastic to see the new Glen Gardens play area open and ready for local children and families to enjoy.

"From the beginning, we wanted young people to have a real say in the project, and the ideas shared by pupils from Tang Hall and Heworth schools have helped create a play space that is fun, welcoming and inclusive."

Developer contributions funded the project, which is part of the council’s wider investment in York’s green spaces.

Cllr Jenny Kent, executive member for environment, said: "We are working hard on similar schemes through our Parks Investment Fund which will address a longstanding need for parks improvement across the city, including for those that missed out on funding in previous years.

“Parks and green spaces are so important to children’s development and wellbeing which was a major driver behind our commitment to launching a new fund."
