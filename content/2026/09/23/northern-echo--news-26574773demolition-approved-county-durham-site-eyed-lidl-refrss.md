A site that could be redeveloped as a new Lidl supermarket in County Durham has moved a step closer after demolition plans were approved.

Buildings at George Street Industrial Estate in Seaham will be knocked down after Durham County Council gave the green light to a clearance scheme which developers say will prepare the land for future redevelopment.

Planning documents linked to the application indicate the site could eventually be used for a Lidl store, although the supermarket chain would still need to secure separate planning permission before any new outlet could be built.

The approved scheme allows for the demolition of a number of existing industrial buildings on the estate.

![]() *(Image: PA)*

Applicants said the structures are no longer suitable for modern business needs and clearing the site would create opportunities for new investment.

Council planners recommended the plans for approval and the application was signed off under delegated powers.

While the decision does not grant permission for a supermarket, it removes one of the main hurdles to redevelopment and has fuelled speculation over the site's future.
