A Yorkshire property firm and its business partners have raised more than £3,300 for a children's charity after taking on the Yorkshire Three Peaks Challenge.

The Yorkshire Three Peaks Challenge, completed on Thursday, September 17, was undertaken by Dacre, Son & Hartley alongside iamproperty, Kempston Parkes Chartered Surveyors, and Mortgage Advice Bureau.

The group walked 24 miles and climbed more than 5,200 feet to raise funds for The Principle Trust Children’s Charity.

![]()Yorkshire property firm Dacre, Son & Hartley and partners raised £3,300 for The Principle Trust after completing the Three Peaks Challenge *(Image: Supplied)*

Rebecca Reeves from Dacre, Son & Hartley said: "We worked with the charity last year and saw first-hand what a difference their work can make to children and families.

"It’s a tough challenge but knowing every step the team took was helping raise money to support children and families across Yorkshire facing adversity kept morale high."

The money raised will fund week-long respite breaks for five Yorkshire families facing adversity, including trauma, illness, or disability.

Donations are still being accepted online.
