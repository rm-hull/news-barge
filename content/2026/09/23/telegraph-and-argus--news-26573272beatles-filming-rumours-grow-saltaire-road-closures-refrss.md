Speculation is growing that the star-studded biopic The Beatles could be filming in the Bradford district this weekend after road closures were announced in Saltaire.

The speculation centres on director Sam Mendes' upcoming Beatles biopic project, one of the most anticipated film releases of the decade.

A highly anticipated film release, Mendes' ambitious production will tell the story of each member of the Fab Four across four interconnected films, with Paul Mescal cast as Paul McCartney, Harris Dickinson as John Lennon, Joseph Quinn as George Harrison and Barry Keoghan as Ringo Starr.

## **Road closures fuel filming rumours**

Businesses on Victoria Road have revealed they will be affected by filming activity later this week.

One Saltaire business, Rad Studio, told customers that "Saltaire will be used as a filming location" on Friday and Sunday, adding that most of Victoria Road would be cordoned off while filming takes place.

Another confirmed on the phone that much of the talk across Saltaire had suggested it was likely to be the Beatles biopic coming to West Yorkshire.

A separate notice displayed in another shop window said the business would close on September 25 and 27 due to filming in the area.

Road closure signage has also appeared on Victoria Road, further fuelling speculation about a large-scale production.

## **Why residents think it could be Beatles-related**

The rumours appear to stem from Saltaire's connection to a historic visit by Paul McCartney.

A social media post shared by a local resident suggested the filming "has something to do with The Beatles", pointing to an event nearly 50 years ago when McCartney visited Saltaire alongside the Black Dyke Mills Band.

The post referenced an appearance outside Victoria Hall, one of the district's most recognisable landmarks and a location situated close to the planned filming area.

## **Historic Telegraph & Argus paper offers clue**

Adding weight to the theory is a historic Telegraph & Argus newspaper report covering McCartney's visit to Saltaire.

![]()A historic Telegraph & Argus front page detailing Paul McCartney's visit to Saltaire, a link now fuelling speculation over the village's mystery film shoot. *(Image: T&A)*

The article details how the former Beatle worked with the Black Dyke Mills Band at Victoria Hall, with one passage stating that the renowned band had been hired to record "The Black Serenade" for his forthcoming LP.

A photograph caption in the article reads: "With Geoffrey Brand conducting, Paul listens closely to the outdoor bandstand sound."

The report also described McCartney taking part in recording sessions involving the Black Dyke Mills Band in Saltaire, creating a direct historical link between the village and one of the world's most famous musicians.

![]()The historic Saltaire visit that has sparked fresh Beatles blockbuster rumours. *(Image: T&A)*

## **No official confirmation**

Despite widespread speculation, there has been no official confirmation that the filming is connected to The Beatles or any Beatles-related film project.

Production companies involved in the shoot have not publicly announced what is being filmed, and it remains possible that the road closures relate to an entirely different television or film production.

However, with Victoria Road set to be transformed into a filming location and Saltaire boasting a documented connection to Paul McCartney, residents have been quick to wonder whether that piece of local history could be returning to the spotlight.

More details are expected to emerge as filming gets underway later this week.
