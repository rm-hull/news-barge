Firefighters were called to a smouldering muck heap in Ryedale last night.

The incident was in **Westow** at**7.39pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Two crews from Malton responded to a report of a manure heap that had started smouldering near to some outbuildings.

#### Westow, North Yorkshire

“Crews used one hose reel jet to dampen down the muck heap and protect the buildings and used shovels to move the muck heap a safe distance away.”
