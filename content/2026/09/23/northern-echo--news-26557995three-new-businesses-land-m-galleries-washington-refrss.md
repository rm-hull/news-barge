Shoppers at M The Galleries in Washington have three new reasons to visit, with a fashion store now open and a tanning salon and coffee shop on the way.

Work has also started at Unit 89a on a new coffee shop for premium coffee brand Bocca Felice. The shop will sit next to the recently upsized Greggs and will feature contemporary interior design and outdoor seating, perfect for grabbing a coffee on the go or settling in for longer. Bocca Felice is also looking to hire locally as it prepares to open.

Simon Redgrove CEO at Bocca Felice: “We’re delighted to open at M The Galleries and become part of the Washington community. As Bocca Felice grows across the UK, we seek locations with strong local communities and a positive outlook. The Galleries perfectly fits this description.

Customers can expect exceptional speciality coffee, expertly crafted by our team using premium ethically sourced Arabica beans. We also offer a carefully curated selection of artisan food in a welcoming contemporary setting. Our goal is to deliver outstanding quality friendly service and a genuinely enjoyable experience, whether you’re grabbing a coffee on the go or relaxing.

What sets Bocca Felice apart is our belief that convenience shouldn’t compromise quality. We have invested in state-of-the-art coffee technology, a rewarding digital loyalty programme, and a passionate team, dedicated to creating memorable customer experiences daily.

We are thrilled to open our doors meet the local community and become a place people look forward to visiting. We cannot wait to welcome everyone to Bocca Felice at M The Galleries.”

![]() *(Image: The Galleries)*

At Unit 69, Lumen, a state-of-the-art tanning salon and a first for the centre. Opened in Wessington Square, Lumen will feature five beds, including stand-up and lie-down models with the latest technology.

North West has opened its doors at Unit 76, bringing a wide range of fashion-forward clothing and footwear for everyone at affordable prices. The store has already proven popular with shoppers since opening.

A spokesperson for North West said: "We've been incredibly excited to open at M The Galleries. It's a centre we've had on our list for a while, and we've been waiting for the right store to become available. Since opening, we've been really pleased with how customers have responded to our products, and we're looking forward to building our customer base here over time. A special thank you to the management team, who made our opening so much easier."

Simon Eatough, Director at LCP UK, part of M Core, commented: "It's great to welcome North West to the centre and to see them settling in so well already. Lumen and Bocca Felice will add even more choice for our shoppers when they open, from a first-of-its-kind tanning salon to a new spot for coffee. It all adds up to more reasons to spend time at the Galleries."

The new openings come as footfall at M The Galleries continues to grow, up 4% so far this year, even as many shopping centres nationally report fewer visitors. According to the British Retail Consortium, UK shopping centre footfall fell 2.5% year-on-year in June 2026, as the summer heatwave and ongoing cost of living pressures kept some shoppers away.

![]() *(Image: The Galleries)*

M The Galleries is home to over 125 retailers and offers more than 2,500 free parking spaces, making it one of the region's most convenient shopping destinations.
