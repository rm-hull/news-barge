A man has been jailed for making knife threats in public and leading police on a dangerous car chase through Darlington six days later.

Gary Metcalfe was given a two-year prison sentence after his arrest following the police pursuit in the early hours of Friday, August 26.

Durham Crown Court heard that Metcalfe was at the wheel of a Nissan Pulsar pursued by police on Hundens Lane, Louisa Street, Neasham Road, Geneva Road, Emley Moor Road and Barden Moor Road at about 4am.

The court heard he was neither insured nor licensed to drive the vehicle.

![]()Gary Metcalfe given two-year total prison sentence for knife threat and dangerous driving incidents in Middleton St George and Darlington, six days apart last month

He mounted a grassed area, hit a lamppost, before abandoning the vehicle and trying to flee on foot.

Metcalfe was arrested shortly afterwards while trying to hide between two parked cars on Westminster Road.

Officers found a kitchen knife hidden down his trousers, and he asked them to remove it because it was digging into his leg.

Metcalfe was also arrested over an incident in Middleton Lane, Middleton St George, near Darlington, on Saturday, August 15, when he threatened three men with a knife.

Appearing before magistrates on August 24, 27-year-old Metcalfe, of no fixed abode, indicated guilty pleas to making knife threats, dangerous driving, driving without a licence or insurance and possessing a knife.

At his crown court sentencing hearing on Monday (September 21), Recorder Andrew Walker imposed consecutive sentences of 14 months for dangerous driving and ten months for the knife threats, totalling 24 months.

He was also banned from driving for 26 months.

Metcalfe was previously jailed for two years after threatening to kill his pregnant former partner while holding a kitchen knife.
