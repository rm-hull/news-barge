Donald Trump said that US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff held a productive meeting with Iranian officials — just hours after threatening to “annihilate” the Middle Eastern nation.

“It was a meeting that lasted for three hours”, the US president told reporters Tuesday while in New York City for the United Nations General Assembly.

“Very good meeting”, Trump added. “I can't imagine why they wouldn't want to. It's greatness, potential greatness, or obliteration”.

Earlier on Tuesday, the 80-year-old Republican delivered a combative speech at the UN, during which he said he could “annihilate” the Iranian government and “drive them into hell” if a peace deal is not reached.

He said that Tehran is now “weak and desperate”, adding: “Iran will never have a nuclear weapon”.

The Iranian delegation is reported to have walked out of the meeting upon hearing the threats, according to *The Independent’* s White House correspondent Andrew Feinberg.

Meanwhile, Iran has said it is willing to reopen the beleaguered Strait of Hormuz within seven days if the US lifts military pressure and the blockade on Iranian ports, a senior Iranian official told Reuters Tuesday.

## Exiled Iranian crown prince says there's no peace under current regime

Exiled Iranian Crown Prince Reza Pahlavi has warned that there's no peace under the current regime.

“As long as this regime continues to exist, peace cannot be attained”, Pahlavi told Fox News.

Pahlavi said the Iranian regime’s “only means of survival is to repress its people at home and use any means at their disposal to bully the world”.

When the US and Israel began striking Iran nearly seven months ago, Ayatollah Ali Khamenei was killed. Mojtaba Khamenei, the son of Iran’s late supreme leader, replaced his father.

## Mark Carney defends Trump's comments about destroying Iran

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has defended Donald Trump’s comments about destroying Iran.

When asked by a reporter at the United Nations about the US president’s rhetoric toward Iran, Carney said, “Well, there is a war underway, and he's speaking the language of war”.

But he also called for peace, telling reporters, “The important thing is to try and work consistently under international law to resolve this situation”.

## Trump attends UN reception following controversial speech

Donald Trump is attending a reception with fellow world leaders after giving a controversial speech in which he threatened to annihilate Iran.

The Associated Press reported at around 7:30 ET that the US president arrived at the Lotte Palace Hotel in Manhattan for the United Nations reception.

Earlier, Trump told those same leaders, “I have a big decision to make. Will a deal be made with Iran that lets them rebuild and create a far greater country than it ever was before?”

“Or do I annihilate the Islamic Republic, and do it quickly — Never giving them the chance to destroy people and countries again?”

## Democratic candidate slams Trump's war in Iran

Abdul El-Sayed, a Democrat running for the US Senate in Michigan, has slammed Donald Trump’s war in Iran.

When asked by Fox News how the US gets out of the conflict, El-Sayed said, “You don’t get in the first place. We had a full-on agreement that had contained nuclear weapons”.

El-Sayed was referring to the Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action, which restricted Iran’s nuclear program in exchange for the lifting of nuclear-related sanctions.

Trump withdrew from the deal during his first term in 2018, arguing, “the deal allowed Iran to continue enriching uranium and — over time — reach the brink of a nuclear breakout”.

## Pentagon officially adds one more soldier death to US count from Iran war

The Pentagon updated its official casualty database to list 19 total US military deaths related to the war with Iran after quietly adding a new fatality.

The change occurred following the death of a female Army officer who suffered a fatal medical emergency Friday while traveling to the Middle East, as reported by *The Washington Post*.

A US defense official speaking on the condition of anonymity told the outlet that the casualty was logged into the database sometime after Monday afternoon. The death was reported earlier by Task & Purpose. Her identity has not been released.
