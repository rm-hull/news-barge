A woman died in York Hospital after her health deteriorated following a fall at home, an inquest heard.

Norma Jean Wells, 79, died in York Hospital on August 21, three days after developing pneumonia.

Coroner Sarah Middleton said Ms Wells was treatment in York Hospital after being first taken to Leeds General Infirmary following the fall on July 27.

The inquest heard Ms Wells suffered broken ribs in the fall which happened after returning from holiday with her husband.

Ms Wells, who was born in Edinburgh and lived in York, received treatment at York Hospital from August 12.

The inquest heard Ms Wells had a “significant medical history” which included cancer and kidney disease.

She developed pneumonia while in York Hospital on August 18, the hearing was told.

Ms Wells died on August 21 with her death verified at 1.40pm that day. Her main cause of death was given as hospital-acquired pneumonia.

Ms Middleton said Ms Wells was “given appropriate treatment but could not recover”.

She added that the family said Ms Wells received “excellent” treatment while in hospital.

Recording a narrative conclusion, the coroner said Ms Wells “died due to naturally occurring disease due to chest injuries sustained in a fall”.

Ms Middleton offered her condolences to Ms Wells’ family after the inquest at Northallerton Coroners’ Court on Tuesday (September 22).
