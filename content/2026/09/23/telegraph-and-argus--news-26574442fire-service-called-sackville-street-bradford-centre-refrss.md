THE FIRE service have revealed why they were visible in Bradford city centre last night (September 22).

Two crews were called to a vehicle fire just before midnight.

They attended and promptly put the fire out, but noted that the fire had already caused damaged to an adjacent car.

## Full statement from fire

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the incident was reported at 11.48pm.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called at 2348 yesterday to Sackville Street to reports of a vehicle fire.

"Two crews attended and extinguished a vehicle.

"The fire from the vehicle had already damaged another car.

"Crews left the scene in the hands of WYP."

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said "Police were called at just after midnight this morning ( 23 September) to a report parked van was on fire in Sackville Street in Bradford.

"It is believed the fire was started deliberately and enquires are ongoing."
