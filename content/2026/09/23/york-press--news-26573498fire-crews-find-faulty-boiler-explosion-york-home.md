FIRE crews were called out to an address in York city centre following a report of 'explosions'.

The alarm was raised after the sound of explosions was heard at a residential property on Peckitt Street, which leads down to the riverside, accompanied by a smell of burning.

**Recommended reads:**

North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue crews from Acomb and Easingwold went to the scene at 8.30pm on Tuesday, September 22.

A spokesperson said: "This was found to be a faulty gas boiler.

"Crews isolated the gas supply and left the incident in the hands of the occupier to contact a gas engineer."
