Emergency services rushed the scene of a reported two-vehicle crash in a York town last night, Tuesday, September 22.

North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service crews from York and Haxby responded to an incident in Haxby when the alarm was raised just before 8.30pm.

**Recommended reads:**

A spokesperson said the call-out was to "a report of a two vehicle road traffic collision with a person trapped in a vehicle".

"On arrival this was found to be a single vehicle collision with a sign with no occupants trapped."

Police and ambulance services took over at the scene.
