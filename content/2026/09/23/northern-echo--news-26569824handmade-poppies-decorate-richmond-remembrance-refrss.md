Handmade poppies will adorn a North Yorkshire town this autumn in tribute to soldiers and animals who have served the country.

The Green Howards Museum’s Richmond Remembers initiative is preparing to bring poppy displays to settings throughout the town.

Richmond’s strong military links and proximity to Catterick Garrison make it a "fitting setting" for the tribute to serving personnel and animals.

![]()Creating handmade poppies to decorate Richmond as the Green Howards Museum leads a Remembrance tribute to soldiers, animals and veterans *(Image: Green Howards Museum)*

Community volunteers and Swale WI members have knitted and crocheted poppies to attach to upcycled football nets donated by the quartermaster at Catterick Garrison.

The nets will decorate several locations, including the railings of St Mary’s Church.

A Green Howards Museum spokesperson said: "Make sure you visit Richmond in November to see the hard work and generous donation of time the community has put in to decorate Richmond for Remembrance."

Richmond Town Council, Catterick Garrison, the Royal British Legion, Swale WI and local and veteran communities have helped make the project possible.

![]()Handmade poppies will adorn Richmond *(Image: Green Howards Museum)*

The next crafting session is on Friday, October 16, from 10am to 12.30pm.

Everyone is welcome, from experienced crafters to complete beginners.

Anyone unable to attend can drop off poppies at the Green Howards Museum.

![]()Handmade poppies will adorn Richmond *(Image: Green Howards Museum)*

The Green Howards Museum is leading the campaign and preserving more than 300 years of regimental history.

The Green Howards were raised in 1688 and became part of The Yorkshire Regiment in 2006.

Housed in Richmond’s former Trinity Church, the museum holds more than 35,000 artefacts and displays 4,500 medals, including 18 Victoria Crosses and George Crosses.

It also displays silver from the regiment and Richmond Town Hall.

The museum preserves and shares the regiment’s stories, artefacts and memories.

Its exhibitions and events highlight the regiment’s longstanding ties to the Richmond community.
