A woman has spoken of her heartbreak after losing her husband to a condition described as a "silent killer."

Jan Greenwood, formerly of Bradford and now living in Addingham, has paid tribute to her late husband Allan Greenwood, who died of liver cancer on September 22, 2018.

Mr Greenwood, originally from Bingley, was described by his wife as a "top bloke" who was always cheerful and generous.

Mrs Greenwood said: "He was always perky, and everyone referred to him as a top bloke.

"He would give his last penny or fiver for anybody."

In December 2017, Mrs Greenwood suffered a stroke, and Allan became her carer.

Just two months later, he began experiencing health problems of his own.

Mrs Greenwood said: "In February 2018, he went to the doctors.

"They said he had tears in his liver but at the time they didn’t mention anything about cancer.

"He was at home for a few months, and I watched him deteriorate before my eyes. I felt helpless."

On February 4, 2018, the couple returned to the doctor, and Allan was admitted to hospital for tests.

He was placed in intensive care but discharged after doctors said he was not in critical danger.

Mrs Greenwood said: "Liver cancer is a silent killer because nobody knows you have it.

"It can be detected way too late."

She said it was not until s coroner’s report arrived in 2025 that the cancer was confirmed.

Mrs Greenwood described Allan as a devoted husband, father and grandfather.

She said: "Allan was not only a good husband, he was a fantastic dad to Sarah, Andrew and Peter, and a granddad to our grandchildren.

“Allan was a rock to the family including his sister Julie.

“His best friend was more like a brother. They played snooker darts and pool.

"We all miss Allan so much."

In his memory, Mrs Greenwood is organising a charity event to raise funds for Liver UK, which supports people of all ages affected by liver disease and liver cancer.

The bingo and bake sale will take place at Addingham Memorial Hall from 10am on Saturday, September 26.

Teas and coffees and baked goods will be on offer, with bingo starting at 1am.

A raffle and tombola will also be held on the day.

Mrs Greenwood will continue to sell baked goods after the event from her "bake shed" at her home on Green Lane in Addingham.
