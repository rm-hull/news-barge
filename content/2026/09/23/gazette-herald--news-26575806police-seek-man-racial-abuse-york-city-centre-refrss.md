A PERSON was verbally and racially abused in York city centre last month.

North Yorkshire Police has released a CCTV image of a person they would like a speak to after an incident in York city centre.

It happened on Bishopthorpe Road at around 11pm on Sunday August 16, 2026.

"A suspect subjected the victim to racial and verbal abuse," said a spokesperson for the force.

They added that they would like to speak to the man shown as he may have information that could assist their investigation.

Anyone who recognises him or has information is asked to contact police by emailing [Michael.Cooper@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Michael.Cooper@northyorkshire.police.uk), using the police website, calling 101, or contacting Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via its website.
