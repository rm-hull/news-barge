A woman has been barred from Greggs and several other shops following a string of thefts.

Samantha Curley, 41, of Pasture Road, Goole, pleaded guilty to several shop thefts at Hull Crown Court on Thursday, September 17.

Curley has been issued an eight-week prison sentence and a five-year Criminal Behaviour Order (CBO).

The order bars her from entering several shops in her hometown of Goole including Greggs, Boyes, and Boots on Wesley Square, Farm Foods on North Street, as well as Lidl on Rawcliffe Road, and Tesco Express on Rutland Road.

She is also forbidden from remaining on any premises after being asked to leave by the owner.

Breaching these conditions is a criminal offence, which could result in a fine, imprisonment, or both.

Neighbourhood Policing Constable England said: “This Criminal Behaviour Order demonstrates the proactive steps we are taking to reduce crime in our communities. It should serve as a clear warning that offending will not be tolerated.

“Breaching this order is a criminal offence and could result in a fine, imprisonment or both. If you see Curley and witness any behaviour that goes against the conditions of this order, please contact us immediately."
