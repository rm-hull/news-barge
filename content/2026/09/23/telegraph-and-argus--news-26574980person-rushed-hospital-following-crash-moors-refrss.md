A person has been taken to hospital following a road traffic collision along the edge of Baildon Moor.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service received an emergency call yesterday afternoon to a report of a road traffic collision on Hawksworth Road in Baildon.

This particular stretch of road runs along Baildon Moor and Baildon Common, with open moorland views and access to walking routes.

One person was taken to hospital.

## What the ambulance said

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service (YAS) said: "We received an emergency call at 4.10pm yesterday to report a road traffic collision on Hawksworth Road in Baildon.

"An ambulance and a team leader were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."

West Yorkshire Police have been contacted for a comment.
