French authorities have confirmed a man was found dead on a beach in northern France on Wednesday.

A translated statement from a spokesperson for the Pas-de-Calais prefecture said: “The lifeless body of a man, who appeared to be of African origin, was discovered this morning on the Neufchatel-Hardelot beach.

“An investigation has been launched by the Boulogne-sur-Mer public prosecutor’s office.”

According to French media reports, the probe is set to examine whether the death was linked to an attempted English Channel crossing.

The discovery of the body comes after far-right activist Daniel Thomas was condemned by charities following footage showing him using a blade to slash a dinghy in the Channel while a rescue worker was onboard.

![Daniel Thomas was condemned by charities for slashing a dinghy in the Channel](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/21/16/40/danny-tommo-boat.jpeg)

Thomas, who is known by the name Danny Tommo, filmed himself tracking two inflatable dinghies on Tuesday afternoon, which were carrying asylum seekers from France.

In the video clip, he can be seen approaching the dinghy, which is full of life jackets left by passengers, and using his blade to repeatedly stab holes in the side of the vessel.

He then shouts towards a rescue worker onboard, who is wearing an orange hi-vis outfit, to “get off” while escaping air from the dinghy can be audibly heard.

As he slashes the boat, Thomas can be heard shouting: “This is England. This is not France any more.”

After spotting the rescue worker, he says: “Get off the boat. You’ve got time to get off the boat.”

His boat then motors away, before the rescue worker can be seen climbing onto the larger emergency boat that was positioned next to the dinghy.

Hampshire and Isle of Wight Constabulary said it was conducting inquiries into the incident.

Charities have condemned Thomas’s actions, with the Joint Council for Welfare of Immigrants branding it “violent and dangerous”.

Stand Up to Racism posted on X: “Danny Tommo already has a conviction for knife crime. The footage of him going wild with a knife and slashing a boat with [an emergency worker] on it in the Channel today is disgusting.”

Thomas – founder of anti-migration group Patriot Platform and an associate of political activist Tommy Robinson – was a leader of recent protests in Portsmouth and organised demonstrations in Dover. He regularly livestreams his actions to his followers.
