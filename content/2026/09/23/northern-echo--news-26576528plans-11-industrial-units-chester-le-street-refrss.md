Plans have been submitted for the redevelopment of a former roofing yard in Chester-Le-Street.

An application has been submitted to Durham County Council to transform the former Wensley Roofing site on Station Road.

The outline application, submitted on behalf of McCarrick Construction, proposes 11 light industrial units and the refurbishment of existing buildings.

The scheme would involve demolishing a number of older outbuildings while the existing brick office building and a larger industrial unit would remain.

Two new blocks are proposed.

Four units would be provided in a block at the northern end of the site, while seven would be created in a second block near the south-eastern corner.

The units would provide workshop, storage and office space for small businesses.

Planning documents state the development would create more than 1,600 square metres of additional industrial floorspace and could support around 38 full-time jobs.

The proposals include extra parking, cycle storage, disabled parking bays, electric vehicle charging points and refuse storage facilities.

A section of adopted road within the site boundary would also be stopped up as part of the development.

The former premises are close to Chester-Le-Street railway station.

Planning documents state Wensley Roofing ceased operating at the site in January 2026.

The site is allocated as protected employment land in County Durham’s development plan.

The applicant said the development would improve the appearance of a prominent site while helping attract new businesses to the area.

The proposals would retain part of the site while adding new buildings and supporting facilities for businesses in the Chester-Le-Street area.
