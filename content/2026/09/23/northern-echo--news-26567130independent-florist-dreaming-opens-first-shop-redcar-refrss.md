A Teesside florist says she is still "pinching herself" after getting the keys to her first shop after years of working from a summer house in her garden.

Danielle, owner of Flowers by Danielle - Cute as a Button, is opening a shop at 127 High Street, Redcar, marking a major milestone for the independent business.

Alongside bouquets and floral arrangements for every occasion, the shop will also stock a range of gifts and gift cards.

![]()Flowers by Danielle offer a range of floral arrangements including wreaths. *(Image: Flowers by Danielle)*

Sharing the news on social media, Danielle said: "As many of you know this is a big deal for me. I still can't quite believe it's happening.

"I have struggled for many, many years running the business from a summer house in my garden so this is huge for me."

With plenty of nearby parking, customers can easily call in and pick up flowers on the go.

![]()Flowers by Danielle opens on Redcar High Street on September 26. *(Image: Flowers by Danielle)*

An official opening celebration will be held on Saturday (September 26), from 10am until 4pm, with visitors invited to browse the new shop, meet the owner and enjoy complimentary prosecco and cakes.

To mark the occasion, the first five people through the door will also receive a £10 voucher to spend in store.

![]()Flowers by Danielle offer a range of floral arrangements including bouquets for every occasion. *(Image: Flowers by Danielle)*

Announcing the opening event, Danielle said: "I'm so excited to finally announce that Flowers by Danielle is opening its doors.

"To celebrate, I'm having a little shop opening party and I'd absolutely love for you to come along and celebrate with me."

"I've been dreaming of this moment and can't wait to welcome you all into Flowers by Danielle."

The shop will join Redcar High Street’s independent businesses after years of growth for the florist, which previously operated from Danielle’s garden workspace.

Locals and visitors alike are encouraged to pop along for the opening and support the new venture as Danielle begins trading from her new town centre home.
