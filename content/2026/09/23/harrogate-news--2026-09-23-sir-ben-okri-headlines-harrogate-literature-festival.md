The prestigious **Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival** has secured a major cultural coup for its upcoming autumn line-up, confirming a headline appearance by Booker Prize-winning literary icon**Sir Ben Okri**.

The acclaimed Nigerian-born novelist, poet, and cultural activist—who was awarded a knighthood in 2023 for his services to literature—will present his newly published novel, *Waking the Warriors*, to an audience in the town. The appearance falls directly within Black History Month, marking a significant milestone for the event, which runs across the town from**11 to 25 October 2026.**

> **Reflecting on the creative process behind his latest work, Sir Ben Okri noted:**
>
> Fiction can help us better understand ourselves and the world around us.

## Mythology, Resilience, and Contemporary Fables

Sir Ben’s appearance lands exactly 35 years after he transformed the contemporary fiction landscape by winning the Booker Prize for his masterpiece, *The Famished Road*. At just 32 years old, the achievement established him as the youngest-ever recipient of the award. His new work,* Waking the Warriors*, marks a return to the lyrical, philosophical storytelling that has seen his broader bibliography translated into more than 28 languages worldwide.

The novel functions as a timely allegorical fable, drawing directly on ancient global legends of sleeping armies that awaken during moments of existential crisis. Set within a carefully constructed fictional realm, the text explores the psychological mechanics of courage, collective hope, and human resilience when confronting an uncertain future. During his festival session, Sir Ben will dissect the role of myth and imagination in modern writing, exploring how centuries-old folklore can be repurposed to decode modern societal anxieties.

## Festival Scheduling and Box Office Details

The event represents a rare regional programming slot for North Yorkshire, positioning Harrogate alongside major metropolitan touring schedules for the international literary circuit. His decorated career includes landmark publications such as *Astonishing the Gods*, which was previously selected by the BBC as one of the ‘100 novels that shaped our world’.

The live interview session, titled *Waking the Warriors with Ben Okri*, will take place inside the festival’s primary programming hub at**The Crown Hotel on Sunday 11 October at 1:30 PM**. Standard admission tickets are priced flat at**£20**.
