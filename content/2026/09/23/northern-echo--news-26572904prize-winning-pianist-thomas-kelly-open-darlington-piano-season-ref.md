A prize-winning pianist whose career is taking him to some of the world’s leading concert venues will launch Darlington’s new Piano Society season.

Thomas Kelly, second prize winner at the 2026 Utrecht Liszt Competition and a prize winner at the 2021 Leeds International Piano Competition, will perform at Central Hall in the Dolphin Centre on Sunday, October 4, at 3pm.

Greg Johnson, chairman of the Darlington Piano Society, said: "We are fortunate to secure Thomas to open our season of six recitals between now and April 2027.

![]()Prize-winning pianist Thomas Kelly opens Darlington Piano Society's 2026-27 season at Central Hall *(Image: Supplied)*

"His many plaudits include references to his 'outrageous virtuosity and technical mastery' which should be very much in evidence."

Mr Kelly is also the current holder of the Benjamin Britten Fellowship at the Royal College of Music and is building an international career with 2026 appearances scheduled in Berlin, New York, Seoul and Oslo.

His recital will feature a demanding and varied programme including works by Chopin, Scriabin, Rachmaninov and Castelnuovo-Tedesco—whose solo piano pieces are rarely heard in concert.

He will also perform Liszt’s Sonata, for which he won a special award at the Utrecht competition.

Season tickets are available from the Darlington Piano Society website at dpiano.co.uk for £66.

Individual recital tickets cost £18 and can be purchased online or at the door, with both cash and contactless payment options available.

Entry is free for under-21s.
