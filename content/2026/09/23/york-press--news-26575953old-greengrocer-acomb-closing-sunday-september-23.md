A POPULAR café in a York suburb has revealed that it will be closing its doors this week.

The (Old) Greengrocer of Acomb in York Road has shared that it will be closing its doors for good on Sunday (September 27) after eight years in business.

First opening its doors in 2018, the café was previously run as a greengrocer's shop before it was turned it until a deli and brunch spot just prior to the pandemic.

Since then, it has become a staple of the Acomb community and was even featured in the Good Food Guide last year - offering both classic and innovative lunch options, alongside cakes, tarts, and Sunday dinners.

It was taken over by new owners Rick and Naomi in December last year, who continued the brunch options, and added a range of weekly specials to the menu.

In a post to Facebook, they said: “Unfortunately as of this Sunday the Old Greengrocer will be closed indefinitely. If anyone would be interested in buying the lease please do get in touch.”

Fans of the eatery flocked to the comments section to share their surprise, dubbing the closure "terrible news" and adding that they hope a new owner will step up to keep the café going.

The Old Greengrocer have been contacted for comment.
