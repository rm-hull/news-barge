A York care home is encouraging the community to support people facing hardship by launching a food bank collection point as part of this year's Harvest Festival celebrations.

The Chocolate Works care home, located on Bishopthorpe Road, has set up the donation point in its reception area in partnership with York Foodbank.

Non-perishable food items can be donated until September 29.

Ella Bond, deputy home manager at Chocolate Works, said: "At Chocolate Works, we are always looking for ways to give back and support our community and local charities, which is why we’re delighted to be partnering with York Food Bank and launching our first ever foodbank drop-off point.

"In these challenging times, we know many people may be struggling, and with Christmas fast approaching, it’s more important than ever to support foodbanks which provide a vital lifeline for many members of our local community.

"We’d encourage those who have unwanted or unused items to come and visit Chocolate Works and support us to help those in need."

The care home offers full-time residential, nursing, dementia, and short-term respite care.
