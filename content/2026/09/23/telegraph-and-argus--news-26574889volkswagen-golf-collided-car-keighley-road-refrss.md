POLICE have confirmed that a road traffic collision occurred in a residential area last night (September 22).

A Volkswagen Golf collided with another car on Keighley Road, Illingworth, at around 8pm on Tuesday.

No serious injuries were reported.

Keighley Road is a well-established residential street in the Illingworth & Mixenden ward of Calderdale.

## What police said

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Road traffic collision involving a Volkswagen Golf and another car on Keighley Road in Illingworth at around 8pm on Tuesday night.

"No serious injuries were reported."
