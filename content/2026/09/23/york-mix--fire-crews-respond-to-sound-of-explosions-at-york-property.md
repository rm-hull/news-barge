Two fire crews went to reports of the sound of explosions in York last night (Tuesday).

They went to a home on **Peckitt Street** at**8.30pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews from Acomb and Easingwold responded to a report of the sound of explosions and a smell of burning in a residential property.

#### Peckitt Street, York

“This was found to be a faulty gas boiler.

“Crews isolated the gas supply and left the incident in the hands of the occupier to contact a gas engineer.”
