The Lucas Museum of Narrative Art opens to the public in Los Angeles, where director George Lucas greets the first visitors.

The video shows fans posing with an R2-D2 droid before exploring the 'Star Wars in Motion' display.

Inside, they view Stormtrooper and Luke Skywalker memorabilia, discover other narrative artworks and browse the museum shop.

The display features props, costumes, vehicles and designs from the first six 'Star Wars' films. The museum also presents work by artists including Norman Rockwell, Jack Kirby and Banksy, underlining its wider focus on visual storytelling.

Several visitors describe the opening as a long-awaited meeting point for fans and praise Lucas for bringing different artists together.
