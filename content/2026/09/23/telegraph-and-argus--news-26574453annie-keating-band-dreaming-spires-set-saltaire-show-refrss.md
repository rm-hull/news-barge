Two acclaimed musical acts are set to perform together in a West Yorkshire village.

The Annie Keating Band and The Dreaming Spires will bring their co-headline tour to The Live Room, Saltaire, on Sunday, September 27.

A spokesperson for the tour said: "More than just a series of concerts, this promises to be a celebration of a quarter-century of musical exploration."

The Dreaming Spires, who hail from Oxfordshire, are known for their album Brothers in Brooklyn, inspired by their time in the New York borough where Annie Keating is from.

Keating, originally from Brooklyn, has become a notable figure in the UK Americana scene.

The Dreaming Spires' drummer, Jamie Dawson, also plays for The Annie Keating Band.

The spokesperson said: "With the Spires' Robin and Joe Bennett bringing sibling harmonies and jangle, and Annie bringing gritty folk-rock with her storming band, also featuring Joe Coombs and Scott Warman, each show with these two acts will feel like a Friday night gig regardless of the day."

Their previous joint performance received positive feedback from local media and the show is set to go nationwide.
