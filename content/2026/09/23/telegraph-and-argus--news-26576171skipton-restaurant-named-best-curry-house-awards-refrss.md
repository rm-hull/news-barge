A SKIPTON Indian restaurant is celebrating after being named Best Curry House at the British Curry and Catering Awards.

Zaiqa Skipton, on Keighley Road, was one of those honoured at a red carpet ceremony held at the Cedar Court Hotel in Bradford, celebrating excellence in the UK’s food and hospitality industry, recognising the best in curry, catering, and hospitality.

Jahir Ahmed, from Zaiqa, said the team was honoured to be named Best Curry House, and added: “This recognition means a great deal to our whole team.

“We are very proud to represent Skipton and would like to thank our loyal customers and the local community for their continued support.”

![]()Zaiqa Skipton's Best Curry House award *(Image: Zaiqa Skipton)*

Singer Aidan M hosted the evening, which was attended by the Lord Mayor of Bradford, Chris Herd, describing it as one of the most prestigious events he had performed at.

A spokesperson for the awards said: “The British Curry & Catering Awards 2026 was a huge success, and it was incredibly rewarding to see so many talented individuals and businesses recognised for their dedication, hard work and contribution to the industry.

“As organisers, Aidan and the entire team are extremely proud of what we created.

“It was a heartfelt and prestigious red-carpet evening, bringing together people from across the curry, catering, hospitality and events industries to celebrate their achievements.

“Our vision is for the British Curry & Catering Awards to continue growing and become one of the leading events of its kind in the UK, while keeping recognition, celebration and the people behind the industry at the heart of the event.”

The awards covered a wide range of categories, from authentic Indian and Pakistani cuisine to customer service, fine dining, and takeaways.

Zaiqa Skipton is open seven days a week offering both dine-in and takeaway services, with 15 per cent off delivery and collection orders.
