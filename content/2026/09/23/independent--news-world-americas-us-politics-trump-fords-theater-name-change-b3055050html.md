Trump administration officials are circulating a list of government sites that could potentially have the president’s name added to them, according to a report.

One of the sites in consideration is Ford’s Theater, where President Abraham Lincoln was assassinated, MS Now reported, citing people familiar with the discussions.

White House officials apparently pitch renaming buildings after Trump as a way to get on the president’s good side, a former administration official told the outlet.

“They know flattery gets them everything,” the ex-official said.

It was not immediately clear how officials hoped to attach Trump’s name to the long-standing theater. An administration official told the outlet that the idea had been discussed, but they didn’t know where it stood.

![Trump officials are reportedly considering adding President Donald Trump’s name to Ford’s Theater, where President Abraham Lincoln was assassinated](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/23/13/06/GettyImages-468286128.jpeg)

Another source added that officials were considering buildings and other sites that they could easily add the president’s name to without running into legal trouble. It comes amid Trump’s long-running legal battle to rename the Kennedy Center after himself.
