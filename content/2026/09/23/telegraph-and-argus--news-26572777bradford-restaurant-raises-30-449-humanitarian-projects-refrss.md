A CHARITY partnership has raised more than £30,000 through monthly fundraising events.

Arome, a steakhouse based on Leeds Road in Bradford, has been holding fundraising events on the last Sunday of each month since January 2024 in partnership with the Nazim Ali Foundation.

All funds raised support humanitarian projects in the UK and abroad.

Over the past 31 months, the initiative has raised an impressive £30,449.

Nazim Ali, founder of the Nazim Ali Foundation, said: "I am grateful to Hasaan Zeb (Arome Owner) & his amazing team who take no wages at each monthly charity day of giving.

"They truly are selfless & are proponents of 'giving back' through their generous actions & are an example to other businesses of sharing their success with the most vulnerable.

"I am amazed at their generosity & that of their customers who always rally around! I am also grateful to my friend Mohammed Azeem who has supported each day of giving behind the scenes. Wow! £30,449 raised is staggering!"

Arome owner Hassan Zeb and his staff have donated all their earnings from each charity day to support the foundation’s work.

The funds have helped build homes in Afghanistan, Burundi, and Azad Kashmir; install water pumps in Sindh, Pakistan; provide live goats to families in Malawi; and deliver food aid to refugees in Lebanon, Malawi, Jordan, Egypt, Pakistan, Yemen, and Burundi.

Mr Zeb said: "I am absolutely delighted to have teamed up with Nazim Ali whose work over the last 3 decades (in the UK & overseas) needs no introduction.

"Nazim has always provided feedback with all of his projects & it gives me confidence knowing every penny goes to the stated causes. I am also thankful to my staff who have taken the decision to forego their salary for each day of giving over the last 31 months as they too wanted to gain their share of the good deeds accumulated.

"God willing I hope to continue to support Nazim moving forwards just as I have since Jan 2024. As a popular Bradford-based business I feel it is my duty to give back to wider society, as they say, you make a living out of what you earn & a life out of what you give!"

The steakhouse is set to open a second branch at 92 Legrams Lane (Unit 4 The Willowfield adjacent to Mother Hubbard's), BD7 2AD, with a grand opening planned for Monday, September 21, 2026.
