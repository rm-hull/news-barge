There was a crash on the A64 near York this morning (Wednesday).

It was on the westbound lane between Bilbrough Top and Askham Bryan.

Emergency services went to the scene.

#### Map location

A witness told ***YorkMix***: “A car with a trailer carrying a load of turf has crashed and tipped the turf all over the A64 – it’s a nightmare. They’re trying to tidy it up now.”

Drivers were reporting standstill traffic.

There were also reports of a vehicle stranded on the A64 near the Askham Bar roundabout which is causing slow traffic there.

Both problems have been cleared and the A64 is open as usual.
