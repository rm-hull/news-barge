A man has been arrested after multiple shop thefts in Goole.

It’s reported a man entered multiple stores in the areas of Wesley Square, Rutland Road, and Swinefleet Road in Goole and proceeded to take items before leaving without making payment.

The crimes took place between Thursday, 17 September and Saturday, 19 September.

A Humberside Police spokesperson said: “Following numerous enquiries, a 32-year-old man was identified via Shop Watch in Goole and arrested on Monday (21 September), on suspicion of five counts of shop theft, and he remains in police custody assisting with ongoing enquiries at this time.”
