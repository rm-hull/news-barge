Miriam Margolyes has been cast in a gender-swapped role in the upcoming James Bond series.

The Harry Potter star, who was born in Oxford, is among the latest names to join the cast of Young Bond: SilverFin.

This is a new audio drama based on the first novel in Charlie Higson's bestselling Young Bond series.

The production, due for release on October 29, stars Isaac Rouse, known for The Crown, as a teenage James Bond and follows the future 007's adventures during his school years.

Margolyes will play Cleek MacSawney, a fearsome henchperson working on the Scottish estate of villain Lord Hellebore.

The character was originally written as a man in Mr Higson's novel but has been reimagined as a female role for the acclaimed actress.

Speaking about the part, Ms Margolyes said: “Cleek is a violent, probably insane semi-criminal who works for Lord Hellebore on his estate in Scotland.

"You wouldn’t want to meet her on a dark night, that’s for sure. I do enjoy playing nasty people!”

The cast expansion also sees Harry Marcus take on the role of Bond's friend Red Kelly, while Christopher Nathan will voice Eton bully George Hellebore.

Writer and director Barnaby Edwards revealed the decision to alter the character was made early in the adaptation process.

He said: “I’ve been fortunate enough to have Miriam in my life for two decades now. She’s an extraordinary human being and a phenomenal actress.

"When I began adapting SilverFin, I was determined to get more female characters into it. Thankfully, IFPL and Charlie Higson gave their blessing to gender-swapping the role of Cleek."

Mr Edwards added: “Miriam’s recording days were huge fun, with everyone upping the ante to match her brilliance.

"Cleek is a larger-than-life character, but she’s got to be believable. Miriam once said, when questioned about her acting style: ‘I build big, but I build true.’

"And that’s exactly what she did for SilverFin. Cleek is fun but also terrifying. The world of Bond has been crying out for Miriam, so it’s wonderful that we finally have her in the family."

Producer Robert Valentine also praised the casting decision, describing it as a “masterstroke”.

He said: “She’s a phenomenal actor, and absolutely relishes in making MacSawney a vile, revolting, pig-killing ogress. It was a delight watching her work from the back of the studio!”

Author Mr Higson, who is also part of the cast, previously said revisiting the story more than 20 years after writing it had been “a lot of fun”, adding that Bond arrives at school as an orphan before finding a mentor in classics tutor Michael Merriot.
