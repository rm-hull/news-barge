# TRAFFIC ALERT: Major road in Harrogate district closed after lorry breaks down on roundabout

## North Yorkshire Police are currently dealing with a broken-down lorry on a major road in the Harrogate district, causing a temporary road closure.

The heavy goods vehicle has broken down on the roundabout between the A59 and the B6161.

As recovery work gets under way, the junction of Otley Road towards Beckwithshaw has been temporarily closed to traffic.

Motorists are being urged to avoid the area if possible and to use alternative routes while the vehicle is recovered.

![The police are currently dealing with a broken-down lorry on the roundabout between the A59 and the B6161 in the Harrogate district, causing a temporary road closure](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjYwODY1ZDJlLWNhZTQtNDNhMy1iM2VjLTlhMTAxMGI1NTMyZTowYzU3MTUzZC1iZTdlLTQ4MjYtYjU5Yi03ZWFlNjkyM2YzOTQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The closure is expected to cause disruption for drivers travelling through the junction, with delays likely during the recovery operation.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: "Due to the disruption, motorists are advised to find alternative routes where possible for the time being.

"Thank you for your cooperation."
