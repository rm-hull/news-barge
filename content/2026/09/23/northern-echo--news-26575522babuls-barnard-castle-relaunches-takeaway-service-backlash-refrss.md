A popular Barnard Castle restaurant is bringing back takeaway orders after listening to customers who said the service was vital for many.

Earlier this summer, Babul’s in Market Place ended its takeaway service, citing kitchen pressure, staff workload and the need to protect diners in the restaurant.

But within days, the customer response made the restaurant’s team “stop and think”.

A petition calling for the service to return quickly gathered support, while residents shared stories about how important takeaway meals were for those unable to visit the restaurant in person.

Now, the business has confirmed takeaway orders will resume under a new scheme called ‘Babul’s Express’.

![]()The front of Babul’s restaurant in Barnard Castle, which has announced the return of takeaway orders through its new Babul’s Express service. *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

In a statement, the restaurant said: “Over the last few weeks, we've read your comments, messages and emails, and we've seen just how much Babul's takeaway means to so many of you.”

The owners said elderly residents, people with health conditions, carers and those with limited mobility had stressed how much they relied on takeaway services.

The statement continued: “For some of you, takeaway isn't just about convenience; it's the only practical way you can continue enjoying Babul's.

“We're genuinely sorry that we didn't fully consider that when we made our original decision.”

![]()Inside Babul’s restaurant in Barnard Castle, which has announced the return of takeaway orders through its new Babul’s Express service. *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

The restaurant said the challenges that led to takeaway orders being stopped had not disappeared, but customer feedback had encouraged the team to rethink how the service could operate.

Rather than returning to its previous model, Babul’s Express will run with a smaller dedicated takeaway menu, online ordering and payment, limited collection slots and reduced availability during the restaurant’s busiest periods.

Revised pricing may also apply, and orders will close once the kitchen reaches capacity.

The owners said the changes would allow them to protect restaurant service, maintain standards and support staff wellbeing.

Babul’s will initially launch the new system as a trial period, allowing the business to monitor demand and make adjustments based on customer feedback.

A restaurant boss said: "There may be fewer choices and fewer available slots than before, but this will help us protect our restaurant service, our standards and, most importantly, our team.

"We’ll initially launch Babul’s Express as a trial, giving us the opportunity to see how the new system works, listen to your feedback and make adjustments where needed."

The restaurant thanked petition organiser Malcolm and everyone who contacted the business.

“To Malcolm, everyone who signed the petition, and every customer who shared their story, feedback or suggestions, thank you,” the statement said.

One commented: "Brilliant. Thank you so much. That's so kind and thoughtful."

Another added: "Well done for listening to your customers - I'm sure that those who asked for takeaways to continue will be more than happy with your new routine."

Babul’s Express will be available to order online from September 23.
