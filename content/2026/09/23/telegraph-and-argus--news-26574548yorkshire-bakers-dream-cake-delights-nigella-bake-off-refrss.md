A Yorkshire baker has impressed judges in the opening week of a popular TV baking competition.

Tom, from West Yorkshire, made his debut in the new series of The Great British Bake Off, which returned to Channel 4 at 8pm on Tuesday, September 22, with new judge Nigella Lawson joining the panel.

![]()Nigella and Paul *(Image: Newquest)*

In the opening episode, Tom took part in Cake Week and was praised for his performance.

### 'Very neat and professional'

His chocolate stout cake was described by judge Paul Hollywood as "very neat and professional," while his Black Forest flavour, featuring cherry compote and cherry brandy, was a hit with the judges.

He won the technical challenge, despite never having made a coffee and walnut cake before.

During the challenge, Tom admitted: "no idea what to do."

While talking with Noel Fielding, Tom said: “it seems to be going to my plan, if Nigella wanted it to go to her plan, she should have left us one.”

### Nigella's dream cake

Nigella Lawson described his winning cake as her "dream cake."

Tom said: "I’m gobsmacked I can’t believe I did it, I followed Nanny Lins method. Everyone is gonna be so proud of me at home."

For the final showstopper, contestants were given four hours to create a self-portrait cake.

Tom chose a traditional carrot cake, spiced with cinnamon with a caramel sauce.

Paul Hollywood said: "Be careful with some of the rising agents, otherwise fantastic."

### In running for Star Baker

Tom was in the running for Star Baker alongside fellow contestants Gary and Mo, with Nigella praising his flavours.

However, the title went to 21-year-old law student Mo.

He will return to the tent for Biscuit Week, hoping to build on his strong start and take home the Star Baker title.
