A historic Durham building is set to open to the public for free every Sunday.

Redhills, Durham Miners’ Hall will welcome visitors every Sunday from 11am to 2pm as part of a new community programme offering exhibitions, talks, cultural events and family activities.

The weekly scheme aims to make the recently refurbished hall a regular gathering place for residents, families and visitors from across the Durham coalfield.

Alix Collingwood, head of engagement at Redhills, said: "Redhills has always been a place for people to come together.

"Redhills Sundays gives us the opportunity to open the doors every week and welcome people into this remarkable building.

"We want Redhills to be part of the everyday life of Durham and the communities of the former coalfield – not simply somewhere people visit for a special event.

"Redhills Sundays is about creating a regular space for culture, connection and community, while continuing the traditions of solidarity, participation and collective action that shaped this place."

![]()Entry will be no cost or 'pay-what-you-feel'. *(Image: Sally Ann Norman)*

Admission is free with a "pay-what-you-feel" donation.

Visitors can drop in briefly or spend several hours exploring the hall and its changing programme of activities.

Each Sunday will feature exhibitions, volunteer-led talks, film screenings, family-friendly creative sessions and explorer backpacks for children.

The Redhills café will also be open, serving drinks, snacks and seasonal food.

Afternoon events will include guided heritage tours, live music, brass bands, folk gatherings, workshops and community performances.

Recent activities have included Redhills Folk, an illustrated talk on Durham miners’ banners and special family events.

The programme is designed to reflect the heritage of the coalfield while offering opportunities for contemporary artists, musicians, performers and community groups to shape Redhills’ future.

In the long term, organisers hope to introduce themed Sundays and community-led events, including opportunities for individual villages to help programme activities at the hall.

The initiative also supports volunteers by helping them develop skills and confidence, and encourages greater involvement in Redhills membership and activities.
