TWO fire crews were called to a York suburb today after a candle set fire to a flower.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service says its crews from York and Acomb were called to Tennent Road in Acomb at 4.19pm this afternoon (September 23) after ‘a candle had set alight a decorative flower’.
