Visitors to a North Yorkshire arts and literature event are being invited to help create a community poem celebrating the people, places and memories that make the area special.

The project is part of the Promoting Yorkshire Authors (PYA) Book Fair at The Station in Richmond on Sunday, September 27.

Visitors can contribute words, phrases or lines about Richmond and their impressions of its sights and colours.

Katrina Palmer from Art and Rhyme said: "This has been an idea of mine for quite some time now.

"I have been inspired by recent writing workshops I have attended and my own poetry workshops too.

"It's amazing the beauty of creativity people can come up with given a few prompts and an opportunity."

Ms Palmer and fellow poet Kate Swann will gather the contributions and shape them into a finished poem at the end of the day.

The final poem will be published on the Promoting Yorkshire Authors website and Art and Rhyme’s news page.

Guidelines will be provided to help participants get started.

"There is no obligation to take part but it’d be great if you’d like to join in."

Visitors can share anything that captures their connection to the town, from one word to a couple of lines.
