ASTRONAUT Tim Peake is set to inspire audiences in Bradford.

The renowned spaceman will be at St George's Hall on September 26 with his new show, The Quest to Explore Space Tour.

With fewer than 650 people having reached Earth's orbit, Mr Peake is well-placed to celebrate the historic achievements and human endeavours of space exploration.

In this new show, he will share the remarkable stories of some of his fellow astronauts and explore the spectacular evolution of space travel.

From the first forays into space in the 1950s to the first human missions destined for Mars, Mr Peake will traverse the final frontier.

He will reveal what spaceflight is truly like: from the wondrous view of Earth, living in weightlessness, the unique dangers and unexpected moments of humour, to the years of training and psychological and physical pressures that an astronaut has to take on.
