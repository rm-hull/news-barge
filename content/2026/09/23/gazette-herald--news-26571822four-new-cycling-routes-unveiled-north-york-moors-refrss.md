New cycling routes designed to showcase some of the North York Moors' most striking landscapes have been unveiled.

The four new circular trails, designed by the National Park together with cyclists John and Lois Fife, are located around Guisborough, Great Ayton, and Danby.

They are suitable for gravel, mountain, and hybrid bikes, or e-bike equivalents, and make use of existing rights of way.

Starting points are close to stations on the Esk Valley Railway, allowing visitors to travel without a car.

Guy Kesteven, a Yorkshire cyclist and creator who supported the project, said: "The North York Moors has some of the best off-road cycling in the UK.

"As well as good stretches of smooth 'champagne gravel,' all-weather tracks and forest trails, there’s a wonderful mix of lost landscapes and iconic landmarks—perfect for escaping everyday stresses and immersing yourself in nature at your own pedalling pace."

Routes range from a seven-mile loop around Roseberry Topping to a more demanding 37-mile route through the 'Land of Iron', reaching the coast at Skinningrove before looping back to Guisborough.

The two shorter routes begin at Bike Base in Gribdale, near Great Ayton, where cycle hire is available.

Full route details and downloadable maps are available on the North York Moors National Park website.
