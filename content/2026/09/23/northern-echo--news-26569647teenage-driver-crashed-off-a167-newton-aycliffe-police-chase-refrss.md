An unlicensed and uninsured teenager lost control of his car and crashed off the A167 egged on by a pal during a police chase, a court heard.

Reggie Egginton was driving a Ford Fiesta north on the A167 near Newton Aycliffe when his manner of driving came to police attention, on Friday, May 15.

After being followed by police for several minutes, Egginton dangerously overtook another vehicle before losing control and coming to rest on a grass verge on Lime Road, Newton Aycliffe.

He was arrested after briefly trying to flee from the damaged car.

The 18-year-old, formerly of Liddell Road, Newton Aycliffe, appeared before Peterlee magistrates and admitted dangerous driving, failing to stop after an accident, and driving without insurance or a licence.

Stephen Andrews, in mitigation, said the defendant has learning difficulties and an autism diagnosis.

Mr Andrews said he had “perhaps slipped through the cracks” since leaving school.

He said Egginton’s passenger urged him on as police pursued the car and the same individual had pressured him to buy it in the first place.

Mr Andrews said Egginton has recently moved in with his girlfriend in Haswell, County Durham, to escape negative influences in Newton Aycliffe.

The magistrates adjourned sentencing so the Probation Service could prepare a background report.

Egginton was told to help the Probation Service prepare the report.

The magistrates imposed an interim driving ban on Egginton, with its length to be confirmed at sentencing.

He was bailed to appear at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court for sentencing on October 20.
