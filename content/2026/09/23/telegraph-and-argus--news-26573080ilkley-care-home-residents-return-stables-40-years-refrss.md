Two Ilkley care home residents returned to the stables after more than 40 years away.

Diana, 88, and Sheila, 86, from Care UK’s Mayfield View in Ilkley, visited The Ilkley and District Riding Association’s 6th Point Show, where they met and groomed horses before presenting rosettes to the race winners.

The visit was organised through Care UK's Wishing Tree initiative, which helps residents fulfil lifelong dreams or rediscover forgotten passions.

Katie Cliff, home manager at Mayfield View, said: "Diana and Sheila often speak about their love for horses, so we knew we had to take them back to the stables.

"Getting older shouldn’t mean giving up on lifelong passions.

"Seeing them reconnect with something that brought them so much joy in their younger years was incredibly moving.

"These moments highlight how important it is to support residents in staying connected with their hobbies.

"We’d like to thank The Ilkley and District Riding Association for helping us make Diana and Sheila’s wish a reality – it was truly a day to remember."

Diana’s passion for horses began at age three, riding regularly with her father, while Sheila took up horse riding as a young adult.

Neither had ridden a horse in more than 40 years.

The Wishing Tree scheme helps residents experience new activities or rediscover old interests, whether it’s horse riding, enjoying a fish and chip supper, or trying something new.

Mayfield View is designed to support active and fulfilling lifestyles for its residents.

The home features a cinema, hair and beauty salon, and a pub, all intended to promote independence.

It offers residential, nursing and dementia care, along with short-term respite care.

Mayfield View is located on Lower Railway Road in Ilkley.
