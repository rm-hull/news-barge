London-born experimental indie outfit **Man/Woman/Chainsaw** have officially announced a major nine-date UK and Ireland headline tour for spring 2027, hot on the heels of their triumphant breakout summer performance at North Yorkshire’s own**Deer Shed Festival** in Baldersby Park.

The extensive headline circuit, scheduled to launch on **Saturday 1 May 2027**, is designed to support the release of their highly anticipated 11-track debut studio album,* Cannonball*, which has dropped globally via prominent indie imprint**Fiction Records**. Formed as teenagers in a converted vicarage, the fast-rising six-piece have rapidly graduated to some of the country’s most iconic mid-scale performance spaces, including a massive regional date locked in for** Project House in Leeds on Wednesday 5 May 2027**.

> **Reflecting on the sonic evolution of the new record, drummer Lola Cherry said:**
>
> We were talking about having a whole record of bangers—having everything be really in your face like a Rage Against the Machine record. We soon realised we weren’t Rage Against the Machine and leant into the softer moments of the album, which I think has really become a big part of our sound.

## From Baldersby Park to a Sold-Out Night at the Brudenell

The band’s rapid live expansion follows a monumental festival season, where their chaotic, high-energy live setup—distinguished by its jarring time signatures, layered multi-vocal structures, and prominent, aggressive violin leads—became one of the most talked-about breakout sets on the main stages at Deer Shed. That triumphant North Yorkshire reception cemented a breakout cycle for the group, who previously recorded live showcase sessions for influential tastemakers including KEXP and Steve Lamacq at BBC Radio 6 Music.

The urgent timing of the 2027 tour layout comes as the band performs a completely **sold-out headline show tonight (Wednesday 23 September 2026) at the legendary Brudenell Social Club in Leeds**. Due to the overwhelming regional demand that completely filled the Brudenell’s room weeks in advance, tour promoters have chosen this week to lock in the massive 2027 venue upgrade. The sonic footprint of the new record was forged in collaboration with specialised underground producers Seth Evans (noted for his work with Black Midi and Geordie Greep) and Margo Broom (Fat White Family), matching intense theatricality with a raw, unpolished honesty.

## 2027 Ticket Allocations and Regional Logistics

The upcoming 2027 tour itinerary represents a substantial step up in venue scale for the collective, launching at Manchester’s historic New Century Hall before cutting through key creative student hubs including Glasgow Art School and Newcastle’s King Street Social Club. The tour is slated to culminate in a landmark hometown finale at the prestigious O2 Forum Kentish Town in London.

General admission tickets for the newly announced 2027 layout at Project House are scheduled to go on general sale this Friday morning.
