A pub is set to reopen for just one day as part of a railway heritage celebration.

The former Locomotion pub at Heighington Station will reopen on Sunday, September 27, as part of the 2026 Stockton & Darlington Railway Heritage Festival.

The festival will celebrate the area's rail heritage at what organisers call the world's first passenger station, with tours, miniature railway rides, steam engines, food, drink, music and family activities.

Much of the excitement online has centred on the temporary reopening of the Locomotion pub, which many residents will remember from its years serving railway workers, locals and visitors.

Local pizza business Weird Dough promoted the event on social media, saying: "Who remembers the Locomotion pub being open? Well for one day only it's back open."

The post added: "We are there in the car park alongside other free events and a bar. We have our 14-inch pizzas and slices on the go all day."

The Locomotion building is part of Heighington's historic station complex on the Stockton and Darlington Railway.

![]()The campaign to save Heighington Station has transformed the historic site in recent years *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

The station played an important role in the development of passenger rail travel following the opening of the railway in 1825, widely recognised as the world's first public railway to use steam locomotives.

Festival organisers say visitors will be able to discover the site’s heritage while also learning about future plans for the station and surrounding buildings.

The event runs from 10am to 4pm and includes storytelling, fairground organs and stalls selling gifts and merchandise.

Residents reacted enthusiastically online, joking that their Sunday lunch plans were sorted and saying they were looking forward to the event.

One commenter said it was "great to see" activity returning to the historic site, while another said they had recently talked about the former pub.

The festival is free, and organisers are urging visitors to travel by train, with services stopping at Heighington Station.

The one-day reopening is expected to be a highlight for former Locomotion customers.
