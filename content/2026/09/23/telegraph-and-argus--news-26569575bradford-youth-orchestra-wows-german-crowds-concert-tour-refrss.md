YOUNG musicians have returned from a concert tour in Germany.

Bradford Youth Orchestra, made up of 38 members aged 11 to 19, spent six days on tour in the Harz Mountains at the end of the summer term.

The group delivered three concerts, including one outdoor performance and two in indoor venues, all of which drew large crowds of locals and tourists.

Councillor Sally Birch, Bradford Council’s cabinet member for children and families, said: "This was a fantastic opportunity for our young musicians to perform for new audiences and put all the skills they have developed through the orchestra into practice.

"It’s great to hear how warmly they were received in Germany and they should be very proud of what they achieved."

Beyond the stage, the tour gave the young musicians a taste of German culture and adventure.

Activities included a summer bobsleigh ride, a mining museum visit, a cable car journey up a mountain, and a woodland walk featuring a treetop path.

The group also explored several scenic towns in the Harz region.

Councillor Birch said: "Being part of an orchestra gives young people much more than the chance to develop their musical ability.

"It can build confidence, friendships and teamwork and give them experiences they will remember for years to come.

"I’d encourage any young musician who is interested in taking their playing further to find out more about the opportunities available through Bradford Music & Arts Service."

Bradford Youth Orchestra, which rehearses at Titus Salt School in Shipley on Saturday mornings during term time, is one of several ensembles run by Bradford Council’s Music & Arts Service.

Membership is by audition and open to musicians playing at Grade 5 standard or higher.

The orchestra’s next performance will be at the Bradford Music & Arts Service Christmas Festival at Bradford Cathedral in December.
