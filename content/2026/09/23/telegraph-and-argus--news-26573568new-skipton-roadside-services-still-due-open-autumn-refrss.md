DEVELOPERS of the new roadside services at the edge of Skipton say it is still due to open before winter, weather permitting.

These drone shots taken by North Yorkshire Weather Updates earlier this week show very clearly how the development has changed the area off Gargrave Road and the A65/A59 roundabout.

The service station will include, in addition to fuel, ultra-rapid EV charging bays, a convenience store and vehicle valeting.

![]()The roadside services is due to open before the winter, weather permitting *(Image: North Yorkshire Weather Updates)*

Brookfield Holdings, which has other filling stations, including in Preston and Morley, say while there is not yet a date for the opening of the Gargrave Road services, it is hoped it will still be this autumn, depending on the weather.
