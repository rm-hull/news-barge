A cyclist has died in a crash near Skipton.

It happened on the **A59** near to**Gargrave Road** roundabout at about**5.30pm on Sunday** (20 September).

A blue Volkswagen Caddy and a cyclist collided.

#### Crash location

Both the cyclist and the van were travelling in the same direction from Broughton towards Skipton when the collision happened.

The rider of the bike, a man in his 60s, died at the scene.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are appealing to anyone who was in the area at the time and saw either the Caddy or the cyclist in the moments before the collision or who saw the collision itself, to come forward.

“We are also appealing to anyone who may have dashcam footage of the incident or the Caddy or cyclist, to get in touch.”
