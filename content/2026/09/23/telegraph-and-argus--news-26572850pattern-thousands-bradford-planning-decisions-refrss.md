NEARLY a third of Bradford householder planning decisions are being issued on the final day of the determination period.

In Bradford, 32 per cent of householder planning decisions have been issued exactly 56 days after validation, according to new research by PlanningLens.

Of 10,674 decisions made since January 2020, 3,446 landed on the final day of the standard eight-week determination period.

Mark Broome of PlanningLens said: "Bradford is one of 25 English councils in the study and every one of them shows the same spike.

"It shows when decisions were issued, not why, so it does not show the council holding decisions back, and PlanningLens does not claim that."

That figure is 6.8 times higher than the average for surrounding days, with 1,273 decisions made the day before and just 158 the day after.

Bradford was one of 25 English councils included in the study, all of which displayed a similar spike.

Across the sample, 21.1 per cent of 106,988 householder decisions were issued on day 56, nearly six times the surrounding-day average.

PlanningLens stressed that the data reflects when decisions were issued, not the reasons behind the timing, and does not suggest councils are deliberately delaying decisions.
