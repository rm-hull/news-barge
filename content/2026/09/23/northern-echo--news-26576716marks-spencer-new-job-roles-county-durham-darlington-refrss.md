Many of us cannot resist the picky bits and indulgent offerings at Marks & Spencer.

Now, you could spend your days among the food goodness as several new job roles have been launched.

Shoppers looking for a new job could be in luck, with Marks & Spencer currently advertising six customer assistant vacancies across County Durham and Darlington.

The retail giant is recruiting part-time staff for its food halls at Bishop Auckland Simply Food and West Park Darlington.

All six roles are for customer assistant - food positions, with a range of part-time hours available.

At Bishop Auckland Simply Food, M&S is seeking to fill three vacancies offering 26.5 hours, 21.25 hours and 12 hours per week.

Meanwhile, three further opportunities are available at the retailer's West Park Darlington store, with contracts for 24 hours and two 12-hour positions per week.

Customer assistants play a key role in the day-to-day running of M&S food halls, helping customers, replenishing shelves, maintaining store standards and supporting colleagues across different areas of the shop.

The vacancies could appeal to people looking for flexible part-time work, whether they are students, parents returning to employment or anyone seeking a second income.

Applicants typically receive training and support from the company, with access to a range of employee benefits.

However, anyone interested in applying may need to act quickly, as retail vacancies can attract significant interest and close once enough applications have been received.

### Current M&S vacancies in the area:

* **Customer Assistant - Food, Bishop Auckland Simply Food (26.5 hours)**
* **Customer Assistant - Food, Bishop Auckland Simply Food (12 hours)**
* **Customer Assistant - Food, Bishop Auckland Simply Food (21.25 hours)**
* **Customer Assistant - Food, West Park Darlington (12 hours)**
* **Customer Assistant - Food, West Park Darlington (24 hours)**
* **Customer Assistant - Food, West Park Darlington (12 hours)**

People can view the latest vacancies and submit applications through the Marks & Spencer careers website.
