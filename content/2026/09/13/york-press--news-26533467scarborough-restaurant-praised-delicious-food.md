If you are taking a trip to Scarborough any time soon, you might be looking for somewhere to enjoy a meal.

While Scarborough is known for its many fish and chip shops, it is also home to a range of great restaurants.

If you want to visit a restaurant that is ideal for a special celebration or where you can enjoy meals using local ingredients, you might want to book The View.

Keeping true to its name, The View offers guests a chance to dine while looking over the Scarborough cliff tops.


## What is The View in Scarborough like?

The View’s website says: “Perched in a stunning location with breath-taking views, The View Restaurant is more than just a dining destination—it is a celebration of exceptional food, local ingredients, and sustainable practices.

“With a deep respect for Yorkshire’s rich culinary heritage, The View is committed to sourcing the finest produce from trusted local suppliers, ensuring that every dish reflects the quality and integrity of its ingredients.”

The restaurant is looked after by Head Chef Steven Chandler, who has over 30 years in the industry.

The View’s menu offers a range of starters from a seasonal soup and shellfish bisque to pan-fried wild mushrooms and crab and brown shrimp croquettes.

For mains, you can choose from meals like fish and chips, the pie of the day, slow-braised beef short rib, and the View’s gourmet burger.

There are lots of sides you can add onto your meal as well, including creamed mash, tenderstem broccoli in garlic butter, and a Yorkshire garden salad.

The restaurant can be found in the Delmont Hotel, which could be the ideal place to stay in Scarborough if you want to extend your trip.
