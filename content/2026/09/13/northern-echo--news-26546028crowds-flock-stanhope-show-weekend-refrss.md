Crowds have flocked to Stanhope show this weekend, enjoying livestock competitions, rural crafts, and agricultural displays.

The Weardale event celebrates countryside life with main-ring entertainment and expert-led classes.

The popular show brings together British farming, rural skills and a wide range of activities including dog agility tests, stunt drivers, llama walks and World Championship Cumberland Wrestling.

The show ran on Saturday and Sunday (September 12 and 13).
