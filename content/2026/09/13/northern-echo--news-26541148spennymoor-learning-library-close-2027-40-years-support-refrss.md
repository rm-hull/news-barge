A long-established service that has supported people with additional needs in County Durham for four decades is set to close.

The Learning Library, located in Spennymoor, will close in 2027 after 40 years supporting people with additional needs.

They provide a unique service of loaning out specialist resources on a short-term basis completely free of charge.

Their resources include lap pads, egg chairs, themed discovery boxes, bubble tubes, tricycles and more.

The organisation told members and supporters it was making the announcement “with a heavy heart”.

The statement reads: "It is with a heavy heart that after 40 years of supporting individuals with additional needs, we regret to inform you that the Learning Library will be closing in 2027."

The main service will close next year, but its outreach programme will continue until the end of 2026.

The statement added: "Our Outreach Team will continue to visit our wonderful members until the end of 2026 if needed."

The organisation has not given a reason for the closure and says more information will follow later.

The announcement adds: "Further details will follow in due course."

For 40 years, the Learning Library has supported people with additional needs and their families.

Members who have relied on the service for years have reacted with sadness.
