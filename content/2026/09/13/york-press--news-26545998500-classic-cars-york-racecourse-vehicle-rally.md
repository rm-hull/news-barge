More than 500 classic cars have turned out for a rally in York.

The vehicles, which also included busses, lorries and motorcycles, were on display at York Racecourse from 10am to 4pm on Sunday (September 13).

Sunday’s event is the York Historic Vehicle Group’s 48th show to be held at the racecourse.

Speaking an hour after the gates opened at 10am, David Walker, from the club, said it was going “really, really well”.

“This is probably the best we’ve had in the past three years,” he said. “There are around 500 to 600 cars here and when I advertised it, I expected 300 to 400. So we’ve done far better than we expected.

“It’s just been a brilliant show – and the people are so nice.”

  Attendee during the classic vehicle rally at York Racecourse on Sunday (September 13) 

*(Image: Dylan Connell)*

David said the event brings people with an interest in classic vehicles together.

“The nice thing,” he said, “is that you park up next to the man you drive in after. You’ve got no idea who he is, so you’ve made another friend. And that’s good.”

  Attendees during the classic vehicle rally at York Racecourse on Sunday (September 13)

David said the event required a lot of preparation, but the group’s efforts had paid off on Sunday.

“Yesterday [Saturday] was hectic,” he said, adding that the club was at the racecourse for most of Saturday preparing for the gates to open on Sunday.

The rally supports local charities, with last year’s event raising £2,500 for good causes.

  Attendees during the classic vehicle rally at York Racecourse on Sunday (September 13)

  Jaguar E-Type at the classic vehicle rally at York Racecourse on Sunday (September 13)

David expected that Sunday’s event would raise “quite a lot of money and half of that will go to charity”.

“It’s about £3,000 to put this show on, so whatever is left is off to charity,” he said.

David, who brought his convertible Triumph TR6 to the rally, praised the dry forecast which resulted in late entries attending the show.

“A good forecast is good for us,” he said. “We’re quite pleased.”

  Kees Batist with his 1956 American Reo M35A2 truck at the rally at York Racecourse on Sunday (September 13) 

*(Image: Dylan Connell)*

  Attendees during the classic vehicle rally at York Racecourse on Sunday (September 13) 

*(Image: Dylan Connell)*

David added that the rally will “definitely” return next year on the second week in September – “hopefully with the crowd … And the same forecast would be good!”

Kees Batist, originally from the Netherlands and now living in Middlesbrough, brought his 1956 American Reo M35A2 truck to the rally at York Racecourse on Sunday.


He bought the military vehicle five years ago from a man in Goole and has since travelled to Germany in it.

He said the truck, which can reach 50mph, is “quite heavy” to drive as there is no power steering but its height means the vehicle is “easy to work on”.

Kees said he had been to the York rally before, adding: “It’s nice. I enjoy it, that’s why I come back. And it’s for a good cause.”
