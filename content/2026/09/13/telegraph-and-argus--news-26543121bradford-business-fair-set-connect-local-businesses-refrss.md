A MAJOR business event will offer networking, workshops, and support for new and established businesses.

The Bradford Business Fair will take place at the Mercure Bradford Bankfield Hotel on October 15, running from 10.30am to 3pm.

Entry is free and attendees are welcome to drop in at any time during the day.

A spokesperson said: "There is a great deal on offer for all businesses and budding entrepreneurs and it’s simple to take part."

The event will feature a B2B exhibition, workshops, business advice, and networking opportunities.

The spokesperson added: "For any companies wanting a higher profile, taking part as an exhibitor or sponsor is a simple and cost-effective way to showcase your products, services and initiatives and connect with a wide range of local businesses."
