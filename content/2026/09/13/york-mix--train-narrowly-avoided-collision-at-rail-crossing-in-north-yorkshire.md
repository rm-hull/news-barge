An investigation has been launched after a train had a near miss on a stretch of track in North Yorkshire.

A passenger train “only narrowly avoided” crashing into a telehandler at Hopperton Old Station user worked crossing.

Hopperton is between Knaresborough and York.

#### Incident location

It happened at around 8.26am on 27 August. The Rail Accident Investigation Branch has been looking into the incident.

It said: “Hopperton Old Station user worked crossing is equipped with gates and telephones at either side.

“Signs at the crossing instruct users in vehicles to contact the signaller and obtain their permission before opening the gates and crossing the railway.

“The evidence available to RAIB shows that the driver of the telehandler had telephoned the signaller and obtained permission to use the crossing before the near miss occurred.”

The RAIB added: “Having assessed the evidence which has been gathered to date, we have decided to publish a safety digest.

“The safety digest will be made available on our website in the next few weeks.”

Safety digests are a way of quickly sharing important safety messages when it decides not to undertake a full investigation.
