Andy Burnham is set to become the first serving Prime Minister to take part in the Great North Run to raise money for a homelessness charity.

Mr Burnham, wearing a Number 10 bib, is running in the half marathon to raise money for Changing Lives, which he visited last month to launch a national drive to end rough sleeping.

In a Facebook post, organisers confirmed that Prime Minister Andy Burnham will take part in the run alongside thousands of other runners.

The post read: "History will be made at the AJ Bell Great North Run today as the Rt Hon Andy Burnham MP becomes the first serving Prime Minster in the event’s 45 years to take on the world’s greatest half marathon whilst serving in office.

The PM has taken on the race before in previous years when he was the Mayor of Greater Manchester.

Speaking to the BBC before the race on Sunday morning, Mr Burnham said: “People said to me when I became Prime Minister: ‘Don’t change.’

  “I’ve been doing the *Great**North** Run* for a few years, so I thought: ‘Well, I’ll live that now. I won’t change. I’ll come and do it again.’

“And hopefully leading from the front, getting out there. I’m a big believer that everybody should do a bit of activity, whatever it is people can do.”

Wearing a pink running shirt and a number 10 race bib, Mr Burnham joked: “When I’m doing my job, I can hardly give up halfway round, can I?”

Speaking near the start line, he added: “I’ve been doing a bit of training, as people might have seen, even in the newspapers.

“But the dodgy running shorts have definitely been left at home in Manchester.

  “I couldn’t offend all of the *great* people of the*North* East.

“So they’re at home, and I bought some new ones.”

Mr Burnham donated 15% of his first month’s salary as prime minister to Changing Lives, after promising to give a share of his wages to charity each month.

63,000 runners will line up to take on the famous Newcastle to South Shields route – the biggest Great North Run yet – and they follow in the footsteps of 1.4 million before them.

He will be running alongside other famous faces including Dan Burn, Chris Kamara, Ben Shepherd and Joe Wicks.
