A young man was left unconscious and another injured after an attack in a County Durham McDonald’s.

Police were called to the McDonald’s on Ponds Court Business Park in Consett at about 4.30am on Sunday (September 13) after an assault.

A man in his 20s was left unconscious following the incident before being taken to hospital. Durham Police said his condition today is described as stable.

A second man, also in his 20s, suffered cuts to his face and nose.

Detectives would like to speak to anyone who witnessed the incident, or who may have information that could assist the investigation.

Anyone who can help is asked to call police on 101, quoting incident number 78 of September 13, or contact the force online.

Information can also be passed on anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111, or online.
