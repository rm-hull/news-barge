A long-established independent veterinary group has expanded into Skipton with the opening of a new practice at Acorn Business Park.

Dale Head Veterinary Group has opened its latest site at Unit 2, Acorn Business Park, Enterprise Way, Skipton, after converting a former office building into a fully equipped veterinary practice.

The opening comes during a landmark year for the business, which is celebrating 100 years since it was founded.

## 
  **Independent alternative**

Speaking to the Telegraph & Argus, director and small animal vet Peter O'Hagan said the new surgery offers a locally owned alternative to larger corporate veterinary groups operating in the area.

He said: "We're an independent practice and the other vets in Skipton are owned by big corporate groups.

"We're a traditional practice. We've been around in Settle for 100 years and opening a new practice ties in with celebrating that while looking forward to the next chapter and expanding."

Mr O'Hagan said the business has deep roots in the local community and regularly supports events, agricultural shows and charities across the region.

He added: "We've been an integral part of the community for a long time. We really try and get involved with local causes, and we're locally owned. It's about celebrating that heritage while continuing to provide good veterinary care at affordable prices."

## 
  **Focus on affordable care**

Mr O'Hagan said affordability remains a key priority for the practice as veterinary costs continue to rise nationally.

The group offers healthcare plans aimed at spreading the cost of treatment and routine care for pet owners.

He said: "We're acutely aware of how expensive veterinary care has become.

"Our priority is doing a good job for clients and pets while upholding animal welfare. We try to keep our prices affordable and work closely with our clients.

"We have a great team, with many staff members having worked for the practice for more than 20 years."

The practice also provides its own out-of-hours emergency service for registered clients, with discounted emergency call-out fees available to those signed up to healthcare plans.

## 
  **Centenary celebrations planned**

As part of its 100th anniversary celebrations, the veterinary group is planning an open day at its Skipton practice on October 3.

A restored 1930s veterinary van, described by Mr O'Hagan as having a "James Herriot-style" appearance, will feature at the event.

The practice is also promising a special celebrity guest, although details are being kept under wraps for now.

Several charities and organisations supported by Dale Head Veterinary Group are expected to attend, including the Friends of Sled Dog Rescue, Bentham Pet Rescue and the Cave Rescue Organisation's mountain rescue dogs.

Mr O'Hagan said: "We've always supported local projects, both on the farming side and with pet owners. Those community links are really important to us."

The new Skipton practice is located at Unit 2, Acorn Business Park, Enterprise Way, Skipton, BD23 2UE.
