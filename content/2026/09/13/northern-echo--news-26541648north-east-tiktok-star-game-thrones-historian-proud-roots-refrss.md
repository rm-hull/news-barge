If you're a Game of Thrones fan and are active on social media, there is a good chance you will have heard the voice of Kellie Soden.

Her voice, with its distinctly Mackem twang, has become the soundtrack to many young people’s daily chores, who tune in to her videos on the lore behind George R. R. Martin’s magical world of dragons and betrayal in their millions.

Since beginning her social media channels in the pandemic from her spare room, Kellie has amassed more than 52 million likes on TikTok, over two million views on YouTube and had her voice become a viral sound effect.

Now, the 36-year-old, from Sunderland, runs the channels as a full-time job – a world far away from the admin jobs she found herself in after a history degree at Northumbria University.

And her newly found success has taken her far and wide, even interviewing cast members of her beloved TV show and its spin offs, including Emilia Clarke who plays the pivotal main character Daenerys Targaryen – an experience she describes as being like ‘a kid on Christmas’.

“It’s like seeing your favourite band or football team.”

  Kellie met and interviewed Emilia Clarke, the star of Game of Thrones. 

*(Image: KELLIE SODEN)*

Audiences around the world were introduced to these characters in HBO’s highly successful series Game of Thrones in 2011, with Sean Bean’s portrayal of patriarch Ned Stark along with his trademark gruff dialect winning hearts in every corner of the globe.

“I am a very proud Mackem,” she says, explaining that she identifies with the Game of Thrones series because of its complex and relatable portrayal of Northerners.

“Even when there are Northerners in the story that you don’t always love, they’re from the North and you have to give them the benefit of the doubt.

“I feel very proud to take on the Northern cause. We don’t always get the investment that we are deserving of, we don’t always get the opportunities, but the people make the North special, and it is the same in the story.”

  She has attended red carpet events for the shows she makes videos about. 

*(Image: KELLIE SODEN)*

She began making videos during the pandemic as a hobby - a way to channel her passion about the fantasy world she loved into something creative.

And the first video she posted brought immediate success.

“I had 10,000 followers overnight,” she says.

“It wasn’t ever meant to be a big thing, it was just a fun outlet really. But then I started getting comments and I would reply to them and it just grew so fast.

“Still, I can’t wrap my head around it. It’s wild.”

Earlier this year, her videos about spin-off show House of the Dragon became even more viral than she had ever imagined.

A TikTok clip of one of her explainers, where she delves into the history behind the characters in the TV show, was used as the soundtrack of another video – someone listening to her to unwind while doing their chores.

It propelled Kellie to a level she never expected.

Being part of people’s daily routines all over the world.

“Every time I opened the app I heard my own voice,” she says.

“It was fun, it’s hilarious and it is a nice little thing that brings us all together because we are all in this community because we love these stories.

“It is crazy, there was a moment when I was at a press event and Fabian Frankel (who plays Criston Cole in House of the Dragon) was asked a question and turned to me and said ‘you’ll know’ and I was just fangirling. It was so cool.”

  Kellie, 36, is 'proud' to root for the Northerners in the stories and in real life. 

*(Image: KELLIE SODEN)*

Her videos make use of her history degree. The most successful one being an explanation of the incredibly complicated family tree of one of the ruling families of Martin’s setting.

She stands in front of a whiteboard, almost professor-like, with a pen in hand and recites the history of this fictional house as if an exam were imminent. And her followers loved it. It has more than three million views on TikTok.

  Kellie's most successful video on TikTok has more than three million views. 

*(Image: GAME OF THRONES HISTORIAN | TIKTOK)*

Now with a combined total following of well over 850,000 people, Kellie is aware that she is in a position not many people from the North East ever get to.

“I hope that more people from our region can get this representation, because we are forgotten about a lot of the time.

“And there are so many amazing talented people who live in the region who just want to live out their dreams, but nine times out of ten that is not realistic and you have to pay the bills.

  Despite never living outside of the North East, her new found fame has taken her all over the world.

“I think it is important for a northern perspective and a northern voice to be out there and to let people know from the area that it can be done.

“I film from my spare room and it has taken me to all these amazing places. You don’t have to move to the big city, people might be surprised how much you can achieve being in your home base.

“I am so proud of the North East. I’ll never leave and it is amazing to be able to do it all from home.”
