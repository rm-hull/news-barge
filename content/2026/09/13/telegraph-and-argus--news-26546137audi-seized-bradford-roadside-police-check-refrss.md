An Audi was seized by police after officers discovered it had been on the road for months without valid vehicle tax.

Officers from Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team spotted the vehicle travelling along Tong Street and decided to carry out checks after records showed its vehicle excise licence had expired in June 2026.

Police initially suspected the information could be incorrect and stopped the car to investigate.

However, enquiries confirmed that the Audi was being driven without valid tax, prompting officers to seize the vehicle on behalf of the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency (DVLA).

## Police statement in full

On social media, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said:

"Spotted driving on Tong Street this Audi was apparently without valid vehicle excise licence since June 2026.

"We stopped to check, because surely that must be a mistake. Alas, the info was correct.

"Seized on behalf of Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency."
