A sandwich shop has been given a one-star food hygiene rating by inspectors.

Sands Sandwiches, located on Dalton Lane, Keighley, received the rating following an inspection in February.

Inspectors said the shop required "major improvements" in several areas.

While food handling was rated as "generally satisfactory," concerns were raised about cleanliness, facilities, and the condition of the building.

Issues included layout, ventilation, hand-washing facilities, and pest control.

Food safety management was also found to be lacking.

The T&A has approached Sands Sandwiches for comment.
