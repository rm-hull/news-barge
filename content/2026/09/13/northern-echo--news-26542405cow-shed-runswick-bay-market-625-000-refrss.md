CONVERTED from a detached agricultural building in 2022 – almost certainly from where it inherited its name – The Cow Shed is today a contemporary and appealing barn conversion set in large gardens.

Step inside and the stylish open-plan accommodation is sure to impress, but what’s likely to take your breath away are the glorious views of the bay it enjoys from its cliff-top position immediately above the village of Runswick on one of the most picturesque stretches of the region’s coast.

Set within generous gardens and grounds and surrounded by open fields and woodland, The Cow Shed’s single-storey accommodation stretches to more than 1,200 sq. ft.

There are nods to the property’s agricultural past at every turn, with industrial fittings, original iron roof trusses and beams, and supersized barn windows catching the eye.

  The Cow Shed is a beautiful transformation of an agricultural barn into a stunning home 

*(Image: Matt Hillier Photographer)*

Throughout the property, underfloor heating has been fitted below wood-effect ceramic flooring.

At the heart of the home is the impressive vaulted kitchen, dining, living room, one elevation of which is glazed from floor to ceiling with French doors opening onto a sunny, sheltered garden terrace, while the other elevation features large sliding doors framing far-reaching south-facing views across the bay.

The room provides space for a generous seating area and a family-sized dining table, while the contemporary kitchen includes a peninsula island, range of fitted units, a larder cupboard, integrated appliances and an induction hob.

The rear hall, with a skylight, serves as both a utility area and cloakroom, with a separate WC.

  The Cow Shed is a beautiful transformation of an agricultural barn into a stunning home 

*(Image: Matt Hillier Photographer)*

Completing the accommodation are two well-proportioned double bedrooms with accessible en-suite shower rooms.

Two loft hatches, one with a drop-down ladder, provide access to roof space that offers useful storage or the potential to create a mezzanine area.

The Cow Shed’s gardens extend to nearly three-quarters of an acre, with gated parking for multiple vehicles, two sheds, an EV charging point to the side of the house, a wildflower meadow and a small orchard.

  The Cow Shed is a beautiful transformation of an agricultural barn into a stunning home 

*(Image: Matt Hillier Photographer)*

The lower garden gently descends towards a wooded area, with a beck forming the lower boundary.
