A new French bistro and wine bar has opened in York.

And it may look slightly familiar – as **Le Petit Rustique** is the little sister restaurant of the popular Rustique on Castlegate.

Located just next door, Le Petit Rustique opened its doors at 28 Castlegate, York, at the end of August.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/le-petit-rustique-castlegate.webp)

28 Castlegate was previously home to Pairings Wine Bar, which closed in January after ten years of trading.

Rustique, since it first opened in 2004, has been passionate about bringing traditional French dining to the heart of York.

What began as a small neighbourhood restaurant has grown into a popular dining destination, welcoming over 130 guests across two floors while remaining true to its French roots.

“When the lease of the neighbouring premises came to an end, an exciting opportunity arose to expand the Rustique family and create something a little different,” said a spokesperson for Le Petit Rutsique.

“Inspired by the relaxed cafés and bistros found throughout France, we saw the chance to return to the more intimate style of dining that first inspired us over two decades ago.

“Le Petit Rustique is the result!”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/le-petit-rustique-castlegate-2.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/le-petit-rustique-castlegate-3.webp)

Nestled next door to Rustique, Le Petit Rustique aims to embrace the relaxed spirit of the traditional French bistro and wine bar – where you’re looking for leisurely lunches, glasses of wine with friends, or relaxed evenings of sharing food and conversation.

On social media, people have been excited about the opening of the sister restaurant. One person commented ‘Merveilleux! Rustique has long been our favourite restaurant in York so can’t wait to pop in next door’, with another saying that they ‘Popped in for wine and nibbles. Enthusiastic and knowledgeable staff and loved the orange wine.’

The menus feature classic French favourites, from Croque Monsieur, omelettes and crêpes at lunchtime to carefully curated sharing platters and an extensive selection of French and European wines into the evening.

“Whether you’re joining us for a quick bite, an apéritif or a leisurely evening, you’ll find the same passion for French hospitality that has defined Rustique for over twenty years, now in a more intimate bistro setting.”

Le Petit Rustique is open Monday to Sunday, 10am till 11pm. Find out more at their website here.
