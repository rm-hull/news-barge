An “impressive” home has hit the market in a “sought-after” Yorkshire village – for almost £1 million.

The five-bedroom house is for sale at Manor House Farm in Fangfoss, east of Stamford Bridge, for £995,000.

RM English, which is marketing the Grade II-listed property, said it combines “fine period architecture with an impressive range of ancillary buildings, formal gardens” – and even a sauna.

  Sauna at the the five-bedroom for sale at Manor House Farm in Fangfoss for £995,000 

*(Image: RM English)*

Inside is a central reception hall with stained glass windows and a sweeping staircase.

The principal reception rooms are spacious and filled with natural light.

At the centre of the home, meanwhile, is the farmhouse kitchen featuring a traditional AGA set within an exposed brick archway, quarry-tiled flooring and space for dining.

Off the kitchen is a pantry and a laundry and boot room.

The five bedrooms are positioned across the upper floors, which offer views across the gardens and surrounding countryside.

Also included in the sale is the collection of brick-built outbuildings, extending to several thousand square feet.

The buildings include a substantial two-storey cart shed, workshops and secure storage areas.

RM English said the outbuildings could be used as equestrian facilities, workshops, studios, storage, leisure space or holiday accommodation, subject to the necessary consents.

“With its Georgian proportions, extensive accommodation and considerable scope for a variety of uses, the property presents a rare opportunity to acquire a substantial Yorkshire country house with an enviable lifestyle offering,” a spokesperson for the estate agent said.
