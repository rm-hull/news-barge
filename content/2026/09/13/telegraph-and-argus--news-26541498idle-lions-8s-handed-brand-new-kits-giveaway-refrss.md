A GRASSROOTS football team has been kitted out in style thanks to a nationwide community giveaway.

The Idle Lions Under 8s, based in West Yorkshire, received brand new football kits through the 'Octopus Kits for Kids' scheme, a national programme run by Octopus Energy to support young teams across Britain.

Now in its third year, the scheme has provided more than 44,000 free kits to over 4,500 teams.

Danny, a coach at Idle Lions, said: "I think the kits and initiatives are fantastic.

"They’ve given the club a real sense of identity and pride, while also helping bring players, coaches and families closer together.

"For the children especially, having a kit they’re proud to wear makes them feel part of something bigger and creates memories they’ll have for years."

The Idle Lions club has 12 teams across eight age groups and has been part of the community for nearly 30 years.

One young player, Jack, said: "I really like our new kit because it makes me feel really proud when I wear it.

"It makes us feel like a proper team.

"I love playing football and wearing our kit makes match days even more exciting."
