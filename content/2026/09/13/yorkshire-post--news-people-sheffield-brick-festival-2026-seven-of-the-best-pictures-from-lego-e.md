The annual Sheffield Brick Festival returned for its ninth edition on Saturday between 10am and 4pm.

The event for LEGO fans showed speed building competitions, large scale displays, activities, traders selling loose bricks, new LEGO sets, retired LEGO sets, minifigures & accessories plus a dedicated building area.
