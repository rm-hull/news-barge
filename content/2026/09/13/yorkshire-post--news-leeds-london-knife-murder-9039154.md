# CCTV footage captures moment Leeds man buys knife - before travelling 150miles to fatally stab man

## Sulaiman Tajzai, of Leeds, has pleaded guilty to the murder of Farmanullah Sherzad.

CCTV footage has captured the moment a Leeds man stopped to buy a knife before he travelled to London and fatally stabbed a man.

Sulaiman Tajzai, 18, of Wesley Road, Leeds, pleaded guilty at the Old Bailey on Friday (11 September) to murder and possession of a bladed article.

The Metropolitan Police says they were called to Abbey Orchard Street, Westminster, at around 10.15pm on 27 March 2026, after Farmanullah Sherzad was found with stab wounds by a member of the public. Despite the efforts of medical staff, the 26-year-old died around 20 minutes later.

Officers established through CCTV enquiries that Sulaiman Tajzai had purchased a knife from a corner shop in Leeds before travelling to London by train on the same day.

![Sulaiman Tajzai, 18 (01.01.08), of Wesley Road, Leeds, pleaded guilty at the Old Bailey on Friday 11 September to murder and possession of a bladed article.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/13/16/30/Custody-Image-TAJZAI.jpg?trim=20,0,21,0&width=640&quality=65)



Further footage captured Tajzai waiting outside Farmanullah’s address when he arrived home from work at around 10.15pm. Upon seeing Tajzai, Farmanullah attempted to flee. Tajzai chased him onto Abbey Orchard Street, where he fatally stabbed him.

After the attack, Tajzai fled the scene, dropping a pair of black gloves near St James' Park. Following forensic examination, Farmanullah's blood was found on the gloves, while DNA recovered from inside them was linked to Tajzai. CCTV footage also revealed that Tajzai had travelled to London in the weeks before the attack to scope out Farmanullah's home address and where he worked.

![26-year-old Farmanullah Sherzad was found with stab wounds by a member of the public.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/13/16/46/RJN1-Image-of-Farmanullah-Sherzad.jpg?trim=0,0,310,0&width=640&quality=65)



Tajzai is due to be sentenced at the Old Bailey on Friday (18 September).

Detective Chief Inspector Wayne Jolley, who led the investigation, said: "Our thoughts remain firmly with Farmanullah's family and loved ones as they continue to come to terms with his tragic loss. While no outcome can undo the suffering caused by his death, we hope today's conviction provides some measure of closure and reassurance that the person responsible has been brought to justice.

"This is a devastating case in which a young man lost his life after being subjected to a brutal and senseless act of violence. No family should have to endure the pain of losing a loved one in such circumstances.

"I would like to commend the dedication and professionalism of the investigative team, who worked meticulously to piece together crucial CCTV, and forensic evidence to build a compelling case.

"I am also grateful to members of the public who assisted officers during the investigation.”
