UNSUNG heroes were celebrated at a very special awards ceremony for York Community Pride.

More than 100 guests sat down for a three-course meal at York Racecourse and a glittering awards ceremony on Thursday (September 10).

Britain's Got Talent star Finley Barrett-Carter greeted the finalists, along with their families, friends and supporters before headline sponsor Aviva shared with the audience the importance of Community Pride and charity partners The Place, which provides activities for children, young people and adults in York.

  


  A huge congratulations go to our winners of the 2026 York Community Pride 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

Speaking about this, Holly Hennell, York Community Impact Manager at Aviva said: "The Community Pride Awards are a fantastic opportunity to celebrate the individuals and organisations that make such a positive difference across our city.

"At Aviva, we're proud to support initiatives that help create opportunities, strengthen communities and improve life chances for local people.

"Through our work with partners such as The Place and the York Pride of Place Board, we've seen first-hand what can be achieved when businesses and communities come together around a shared purpose.

"It's inspiring to recognise the people who are helping to make York an even better place for everyone."

  Editor of The Press, Ben Hardy 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

The address from Aviva came before a speech from Press Editor Ben Hardy, who said: "In a world that can sometimes feel divided, these awards celebrate something powerful: community.

"They celebrate people who step forward when help is needed, who bring others together, and who improve the lives of those around them without expecting recognition in return.

"At The Press, we are privileged to tell many of these stories, but tonight is about more than headlines. It is about recognising the character, compassion and resilience that exists in every corner of our region.

"To all our finalists, nominees and winners: thank you. Thank you for everything you do to make York and surrounding areas better places to live, work and belong. You truly are the pride of our community."
