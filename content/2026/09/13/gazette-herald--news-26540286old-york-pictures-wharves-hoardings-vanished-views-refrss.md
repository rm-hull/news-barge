These old photographs of York show a city that may seem to belong to another world.

Today's collection goes back to the 1960s, spilling into the 1970s and early 80s, featuring some scenes that would raise a few eyebrows today - from vehicles parked up on King's Staith to the striking display of large advertising hoardings covering a building on Bridge Street, promoting everything from Tetley Bitter and Benson & Hedges cigarettes to milk and Danish bacon.

  Prominent advertising hoardings were displayed on this Bridge Street building, pictured in 1975, showing a past era of advertising in Britain.


The photos offer a nostalgic reminder of a different era in York, one of eye-catching adverts above once-thriving but now-lost independent shops and Queen’s Staith when it was a busy working wharf on the River Ouse illustrating a side of the city that has largely disappeared.
