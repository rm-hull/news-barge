A 90-YEAR-OLD Bradford woman has been given a lifetime free gym membership as a result of her sheer dedication to fitness.

June Robinson, was given the luxury on her 90th birthday by owner of 6FIT gym, 52-year-old Paul Cazaux.

June is a regular at the gym, and has been attending the particular site, home to multiple gyms over the years, for 22 years.

She started going to the gym in her 30s at a time when not many gyms existed – let alone with women members.

It was her husband, a keen athlete himself, who got her into fitness.

  She's been working out long before going to the gym was mainstream 

*(Image: T&A)*

June told the T&A she first started going to the Windmill Hut which she described as a wooden hut with “dirty rugs on the floor.”

The windmill was predominantly a man’s gym but women could attend on two evenings a week - which June did.

  June with staff at 6FIT gym 

*(Image: T&A)*

Paul said June was probably “one of the first women” in Bradford to start going to a gym, and she's now the oldest member of 6FIT gym in Wibsey.

For June, going to the gym is more than a workout, it's “an outing” and a habit she has kept up with all these decades.

  For her 90th birthday she was given a lifetime free membership pass 

*(Image: T&A)*

## 'It makes me feel nervous'

Paul said that she is without doubt “inspirational to the staff” at his gym where young and old workout “side by side.”

June comes to the gym once a week and lifts barbells and dumbbells in addition to overall muscle and strength training.

  June isn't phased by a bit of hard work! 

*(Image: T&A)*

June is naturally very modest, and in Paul's words, “she doesn’t like the attention.”

On receiving the lifetime pass she told the T&A: “It makes me feel nervous, I don’t know why.”

## June's advice

The pensioner's advice to people wanting to get into the gym is simple.

June said: “Give it a go and I am sure you will enjoy it.”

Paul agrees. He added: "The hardest part of the gym is walking through the front door.

"Step out your comfort zone and step into a gym.”

June also thinks it’s important to “have some time to yourself” and believes getting into fitness can be life-changing, as it has been for her.

  June with staff at 6FIT gym 

*(Image: T&A)*

## What is 6FIT gym?

6FIT Gyms is a large independent gym based at Park View Mills on Wibsey Park Avenue, Bradford, BD6 3QA.

It is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, and offers a wide range of strength and cardio equipment, more than 100 classes, hybrid-training areas, saunas, free parking and training and meal-planning support through its coaching app.

Membership has been advertised from around £300 per year, with a seven-day trial available for £7.

Paul also mentioned that the gym, which is "always upgrading" runs competitions for people of all abilities.

He said: “We have competitions not just for the elite, for everyone.”
