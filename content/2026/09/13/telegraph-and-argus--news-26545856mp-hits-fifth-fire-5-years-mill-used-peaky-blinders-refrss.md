The MP for Keighley has demanded action after yet another major fire at Dalton Mills, warning "the situation cannot continue" following the fifth significant blaze at the historic site in five years.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service (WYFRS) were called to the Victorian textile mill at around 8pm on Saturday.

The Grade II* listed mill has featured in shows including Downton Abbey and Peaky Blinders, but was subject to an arson attack in 2011.

The interior was completely gutted by a fire in 2022 and there was a further arson attack in 2025.

In February of this year, two fires in the space of six days at the site were recorded as "deliberate" by West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service (WYFRS).

## MP says fire is 'deeply infuriating'

Robbie Moore, Conservative MP for Keighley and Ilkley, said in a video posted on X: “You already will have seen that we have had another major fire in the centre of Keighley, which is deeply infuriating.

   

*(Image: Submitted)*

“This situation just simply cannot continue.

“I’ve been calling on our local authority to secure this site as best as possible for years now, and this is our next major fire.”

## What have the fire service said?

In a statement released late on Saturday, WYFRS advised people to avoid the area, with a number of local residents evacuated.

   

*(Image: T&A)*

A spokesperson said: “Due to the severity of the incident and the potential risk to residents living in close proximity to the fire, a number of nearby residents are now being evacuated.

“Local Authority Emergency Planning officers are on scene and are providing support and guidance to those affected by the evacuation.”

A further update was issued this morning, confirming the blaze had been brought under control.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service said: “The incident at Dalton Mills remains ongoing this morning.

“Two fire engines are still in attendance, however the situation is now under control.”
