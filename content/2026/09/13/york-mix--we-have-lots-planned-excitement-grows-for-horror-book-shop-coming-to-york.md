More details have been revealed about York’s upcoming new horror book shop – and they’re spine-tingling thrilling.

It was announced last month that the team behind independent crime and thriller book shop, Criminally Good Books, was expanding into all things spooky with the brand new horror-themed bookshop, Horrifically Good Books.

The announcement had an outpouring of support on social media, with the new bookshop expecting to open at the end of October inside the Grade 1-listed building at 60 Goodramgate.

As the end of October draws nearer, excitement is growing around the new horror-themed book shop.

“Crime or horror were always on the cards for our first store,” Criminally Good Books founder, Isla Coole, told ***YorkMix***. “And I chose crime and mystery first with Criminally Good Books (CGB). 

“We actually have had our eye on 60 Goodramgate for over three years and really wanted it for CGB but it wasn’t on the market and was being restored.

“I have been pestering – nicely I hope! – the York conservation trust every now and then to check in to see how the restoration of the building is going.”

With the expansion of a second shop, it means Isla and her team can grow the services that they hope to offer with a bigger space – such as bespoke book and gift subscriptions.

“We have been looking for a second shop in York for a year or so. We don’t currently do online sales at CGB and we don’t do a bespoke book subscription service.

“These are areas we want to expand into but given the limitations on size with the CGB site on Colliergate we aren’t able to, to the level we would want to – so finding a unit where we can prepare bespoke book and gift subscriptions and do online sales packing was a priority.

“60 Goodramgate came on the market at exactly the right time. It allows us to have a bespoke book packing area for UK and International orders at a larger more professional scale and will mean we can do our subscription service.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/60-goodramgate-york-1000x750.jpeg)

The subscription service is expected to start from some point in the new year.

Talking about the inspiration behind Horrifically Good Books, Isla said that “the inspiration for a horror specific shop was York itself in a way, but also it’s an area in our current shop, CGB, that was growing.

“This is in part due to customers asking for more horror related thriller titles, and the fact that the horror genre across the UK is growing year on year.”

“I’m super thrilled and can’t quite believe we managed to secure our dream building,” said Isla.

“The building is Grade 1 listed and of significant historical importance and we are working carefully with the YCT to ensure we show off the building’s historical features whilst finding places for people to browse our selection of titles.”

And the book-lovers and other independent bookshops in York have rallied behind Isla and her team in support of Horrifically Good Books.

“We knew the response would be strong to the announcement of HGB but we didn’t realise that our first post would have over 62k views,” said Isla.

“People have been tagging their friends on our announcement, messaging us to ask when we are opening so they can plan trips to York.

“We are really grateful for all the interest and can’t wait to be open.”

The bookshop’s first horror event will be with local author Dom&Ink, whose YA Graphic novel *Queens of the Crypt* came out this summer.

Dom will be interviewed by Yorkshire-born novelist Juno Dawson at Merchant Adventurers’ Hall on Friday 23 October. Tickets are available here.

For the opening of Horrifically Good Books, Isla said that they “are also working with lots of publishers on securing goodie bags for every person making a purchase in our opening week – or two.

“We have lots planned!”
