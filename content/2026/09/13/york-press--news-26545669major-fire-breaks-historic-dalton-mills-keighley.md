## Huge fire at historic Yorkshire mill used for Peaky Blinders and Downton Abbey

The blaze at Dalton Mills *(Image: T&A)*

A major investigation is underway after a huge fire ripped through an historic mill in Yorkshire.

Reports of a blaze at Dalton Mills in Keighley - a favourite for film and TV companies - first emerged at around 8pm on Saturday night (September 12).

In a statement, West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue confirmed 12 fire engines were in attendance.


A reporter at the scene from The Press' sister newspaper, the Bradford Telegraph & Argus, confirmed there was a significant response from all three emergency services.

The cause of the fire is not yet known, and specialist teams are at the site on Sunday.

## Eyewitness accounts

One eyewitness said: "I was driving down the A650 and I could just see all this smoke.

  "I pulled over to have a look and I couldn't believe the size of the fire.

  

  "It's unbelievable."

  The blaze at Dalton Mills 

*(Image: T&A)*

A Keighley resident said: "Very saddened and shocked.

"This site has given a lot to the film industry and consequently the country."

Another eyewitness from Keighley said: "It seemed to start on the top floor. The roof had already gone, then we think the floor collapsed about 8:45pm to 9pm."

## Peaky Blinders

The mill has become a favourite location for film and television crews. In recent years it has featured in Peaky Blinders and Downton Abbey as well as the horror thriller, The Limehouse Golem.

## MP reaction

In a statement on social media Robbie Moore, MP for Keighley & Ilkley, said: "Extremely concerning scenes at Dalton Mills in Keighley this evening."

He added: "Huge thanks to our emergency services for all their efforts and bravery as they continue to tackle the blaze and put out this major fire, and ensure all are safe."

## Statement in full

On social media, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said: "At approximately 8:00pm, we were called to a large fire involving Dalton Mills, Keighley.

"We currently have 12 fire engines at the incident as firefighters continue to tackle the large incident.

"Please avoid the area while emergency crews work at the scene. Residents nearby are advised to keep their windows and doors closed until further notice.

"Thank you for your cooperation. Further incident updates to follow, link will be added to the comments."

The T&A has approached West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, West Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service for comment.
