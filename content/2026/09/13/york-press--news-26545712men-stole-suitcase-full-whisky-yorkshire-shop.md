Two men who police said stole a suitcase full of whisky from a store in North Yorkshire have been recalled to prison.

North Yorkshire Police said the two “out-of-area offenders” travelled to Northallerton early on Saturday (September 12) and stole a suitcase full of whisky from a local shop.

“When officers arrived, the pair attempted a last-minute costume change by shedding items of clothing as they made off. Sadly for them, changing outfits doesn't make you invisible,” a police spokesperson said.

They said both men were arrested in connection with the theft and were recalled to prison, “where we're confident their opportunities to stockpile whisky will be significantly reduced for the foreseeable future, meaning their next drinks will be enjoyed at His Majesty's pleasure rather than from a suitcase”.
