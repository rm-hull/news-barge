## Concert at Scarborough art gallery raises funds and cancer awareness

A music concert at Woodend Art Gallery and Studio recently raised £172 for Macmillan Cancer Support *(Image: Benjamin Adebola Visuals)*

A Scarborough concert recently raised £172 for Macmillan Cancer Support.

Organised by artist and drummer Daniel Ezeani, the event at Woodend Art Gallery and Studio united people from different backgrounds for cancer awareness and the importance of regular health checks.

The concert, titled "Live in Concert with Daniel Ezeani," combined live drumming performances, music, and community engagement, conveying a message of love, hope, and support for those affected by cancer.

The event encouraged attendees to take their health seriously, discuss concerns, and seek regular medical check-ups.

Music artists, performers, health professionals, donors, volunteers, and community members attended, highlighting the unifying power of music for an important cause.

Mr Ezeani expressed gratitude to everyone who attended, performed, donated, or volunteered.

He said the event was an "opportunity to use music not only to entertain, but also to raise awareness, inspire hope, and give back to the community".
