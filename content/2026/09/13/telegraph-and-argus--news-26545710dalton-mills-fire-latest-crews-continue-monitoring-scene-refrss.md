A major fire at Dalton Mills in Keighley is now under control, fire chiefs have confirmed.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the incident remained ongoing on Sunday morning, with two fire engines still in attendance at the historic mill complex.

The blaze prompted a major emergency response after breaking out on Saturday evening, with 12 fire engines attending at the height of the incident.

The fire service has not yet confirmed the cause of the blaze.

Emergency services remained at the scene on Sunday morning and Dalton Lane continued to be closed to traffic.

The Grade II*-listed mill has been the scene of a number of fires in recent years, including suspected arson attacks.

## Statement in full

In an update, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: “The incident at Dalton Mills remains ongoing this morning.

"Two fire engines are still in attendance, however the situation is now under control.

“We would like to thank local residents and communities for their patience, understanding and support while crews remained at the scene throughout the night.

“The incident will continue to be monitored and further updates will be provided if required.”
