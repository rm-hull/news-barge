Drivers face major disruption in central Gateshead after a burst water main forced more road closures during Great North Run traffic restrictions.

Gateshead Council said Askew Road’s westbound carriageway was closed after the burst. Traffic from Gateshead town centre on the A184 West Central Route is being diverted through the Adelaide Estate.

The A189 southbound exit slip road onto Askew Road is also closed, with motorists diverted via the Redheugh Bridge South Approach.

In a further update, Northumbrian Water said its teams were called to a major burst on a water main located on the grassed bank beside Askew Road.

Engineers are isolating the damaged main, but flooding has spread across the road network.

As a result, Askew Road has been temporarily closed between Gateshead Centre and the Redheugh Bridge in conjunction with Northumbria Police to ensure the safety of motorists.

Northumbrian Water said Village Heights is closed to pedestrians during repairs and the clean-up operation.

The company said it is working with Gateshead Council to clear the flooding and reopen the roads quickly.

Heavy traffic is expected across Tyneside during the Great North Run, with drivers urged to plan ahead, allow extra time and follow diversion signs.

A Northumbrian Water spokesperson said: "We understand the disruption that this may cause and would like to apologise for the inconvenience. Thank you for your patience, and we will continue to provide updates."

### Road Closures and Diversions

- Askew Road westbound carriageway closed.
- A184 West Central Route traffic diverted through the Adelaide Estate.
- A189 southbound exit slip onto Askew Road closed.
- Diversion in place via the Redheugh Bridge South Approach.
- Askew Road closed between Gateshead Centre and Redheugh Bridge.
- Village Heights closed to pedestrians.
- Flooding reported following a burst water main.

Drivers travelling through Gateshead should check traffic conditions before setting off and consider alternative routes while repairs and the clean-up continue.

The annual half marathon brings thousands of people to the region, adding to pressure on roads already affected by the closures.
