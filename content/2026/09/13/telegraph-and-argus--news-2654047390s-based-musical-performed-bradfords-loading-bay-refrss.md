MANCHESTER’S 1990s club scene will be celebrated in a “indie musical” coming to Bradford city centre next month.

How Soon Is Now by Box of Tricks will be held at Loading Bay on October 15 and 16, and will feature “a live soundtrack of 90s classics.”

Set in a Manchester nightclub on New Years Eve in 1997, the musical follows three girls on a night out at a pivotal time in the country’s history – at a time when Britpop was at its height.

The show will be stopping at the venue as part of a UK tour that starts at the Lowry in Salford.

Loading Bay was opened during Bradford’s year as City of Culture, and is a re-purposed retail warehouse off Duke Street.

Tickets cost £15 and are available by visiting [https://bradfordculture.co.uk/event/how-soon-is-now/](https://bradfordculture.co.uk/event/how-soon-is-now/)
