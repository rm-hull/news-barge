A special concert in York will bring together three generations of musical talent on stage.

The Steve Cassidy Band will perform at the Joseph Rowntree Theatre on Sunday, September 27, led by veteran musician and former headteacher Steve Cassidy.

Mr Cassidy will be joined by his 15-year-old granddaughter, Tess, and the choir from St George’s School — where he once served as headteacher.

Mr Cassidy said: "I’ve performed at Joseph Rowntree Theatre many times over the years.

"It’s a wonderful theatre and it’s always a special occasion to perform there.

"This one will be extra special to be sharing the stage with my granddaughter Tess and the delightful children from St George’s School.

"I’ve been fortunate to have been making music for a long time, and it’s lovely to see other generations enjoying music too."

The York-based band includes John Lewis on lead guitar, Mick Huil on bass, ukulele and vocals, Dave Cook on percussion, and George Hall on keyboard.

Known for their versatile live shows, the band’s repertoire spans pop, rock, country, ballads and blues.

The concert will begin at 7.30pm.
