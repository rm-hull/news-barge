A motorist who was caught by a speed camera travelling at 80mph on the A66 has been fined.

Graham Houliston was driving on the A66 at Bowes Moor, near Barnard Castle, when he was recorded exceeding the 70mph speed limit.

The 53-year-old, of Westwood View, West Calder, West Lothian, was driving a Volkswagen at the time of the offence on April 13, 2025.

Houliston was clocked at 80mph by a speed camera at around 2.20pm.

His case was dealt with at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on Thursday, September 3.

Houliston pleaded guilty to exceeding the 70mph limit.

He was fined £169 and ordered to pay £130 in costs and a £68 victim surcharge.

A separate charge of failing to provide information relating to the identity of the driver of the Volkswagen was withdrawn.
