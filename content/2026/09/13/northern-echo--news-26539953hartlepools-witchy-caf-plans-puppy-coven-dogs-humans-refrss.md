A magical coffee shop is casting a spell on dog lovers with plans for a 'puppy coven'.

Gina Pontoni, 61, from Hartlepool, runs a witchy café, crystal shop, and tarot reading space in Seaton Carew.

But with her business, Gina's Coven, expanding at what she describes as "lightening speed", she now hopes to transform part of her space into a cosy spot for canine companions, offering a mystical hideaway for four-legged visitors and their humans.

   

*(Image: Provided)*

Before opening her shop, Ms Pontoni says she spent most of her life as a professional singer, living in America and traveling the world.

However, her career was sadly cut short after a serious car accident left her with around 40 broken bones.

It was during her recovery from the accident that Ms Pontoni decided to make something of her other hobby.

She said: "About 15 years ago when I was getting back on my feet, I thought 'what am I going to do' - I decided to open a little room doing tarot cards in a gym.

"15 years ago, witchcraft and tarot weren't really words you would hear, but I was hoping it would all take off, and within weeks it did."

The growing demand quickly outgrew her small space, leading her to open Gina's Coven in a former betting shop, once a Post Office, in Seaton Carew during the initial Covid lockdown in 2020.

  The shop was once a Post Office. 

*(Image: Provided)*

Gina later met her partner, Kev, who would often frequent the former betting shop that once sat in the unit before the pair met.

"He told me he always wondered what would take the betting shop's place when it closed down, little did he realise he'd be working there with me all these years later", Gina added.

  Gina with partner Kev. 

*(Image: Provided)*

Now, the café - filled with themed tables set with crystals and cards - has become a welcoming space for visitors seeking a touch of magic.

The business is a family affair, with Ms Pontoni’s 90-year-old mother Betty baking cakes, pies, and scones for the shop.

Ms Pontoni said: "My mam loves it.

"She comes in to greet people with a hug, a lot of people come in on their own so it’s really special.

  Gina's friend Emma, Mum Betty, Manager Jan, and Gina on opening day.

Despite her singing career being halted, Gina says: "I loved to sing, but I always had tarot cards on me and a bag of crystals.

"It was always a big part of my life, now I’m doing it fully, it brings me so much comfort to help other people."

Gina has further plans to purchase more space next year, substantially expanding the business that started as a hobby into an attraction of the town.
