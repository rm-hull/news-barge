Police were called to a County Durham car park after a fight broke out.

Emergency services attended the Aldi supermarket car park on Front Street in Consett shortly before 1.20am today (Sunday, September 13).

It follows reports online of police being “everywhere” in the town in the early hours of the morning. The Northern Echo understands that the incident was an altercation.

The North East Ambulance Service has confirmed it was called to the scene but was stood down by officers before arriving.

A spokesperson said: "We were called to an incident at a public car park on Front Street, Consett shortly before 1.20am on 13 September, but were stood down by police before we attended the scene."

Durham Police has been contacted for more information.
