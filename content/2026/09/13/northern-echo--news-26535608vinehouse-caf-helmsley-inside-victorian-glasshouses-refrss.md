A North Yorkshire café allows customers to eat beneath trailing vines inside a collection of historic Victorian glasshouses.

The Vinehouse Café sits inside Helmsley Walled Garden, a short walk from the North York Moors market town’s centre.

Visitors do not need to pay for garden admission to use the café, making it a destination in its own right.

The café fills four Victorian glasshouses, where customers eat and drink among the historic structures.

  The Vinehouse Café sits inside Helmsley Walled Garden 

*(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

It has more than 100 indoor seats and over 170 outside, in a setting the business describes as relaxed and quirky.

Dogs are welcome both inside and outside.

The unusual setting is one of its biggest draws.

A visitor in 2026 called it a beautiful café and tearoom on the edge of Helmsley, five to seven minutes’ walk from the town centre.

They praised the cream tea, cakes and homemade menu, and confirmed that café customers can enter without paying for the wider garden.

Another visitor praised the glasshouses, where grape vines trail above diners’ tables.

The Vinehouse serves breakfast, lunch and brunch, with freshly baked scones among its specialities.


Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free choices are also available.

Recent visitors have also praised its family-friendly facilities.

A family visiting in August found a children’s play area beside tables, so the children could play while the adults ate lunch.

The café has 4.4 stars on Tripadvisor and its location is another big draw.

Helmsley Walled Garden sits near the town centre and beside the wider Duncombe Park estate, making it easy to combine a café stop with a day exploring Helmsley and the North York Moors.

The café currently advertises opening from 10am and trading from Tuesday to Sunday.

But the surroundings are what set it apart from a conventional town-centre café.

With diners able to sit among Victorian glasshouses and vines while looking out towards a historic walled garden, it is easy to see why the setting features so prominently in visitor reviews.
