Northumbrian Water has issued an update as a major North East road remains closed after a burst main flooded the carriageway and left it covered in debris.

Northumbrian Water said crews had worked through the morning after the burst near Askew Road. The main has been isolated and teams are clearing floodwater with Gateshead Council.

The company warned that heavy flooding and debris pulled from the bank were making the clean-up difficult.

As a result, Askew Road remains closed in both directions between Gateshead Centre and the Redheugh Bridge.

Northumbrian Water hopes to reopen the northbound lane within hours and will provide further updates as work continues.

Village Heights also remains closed as Northumbrian Water works with Gateshead Council and Northumbria Police.

The disruption comes as the Great North Run takes place, with heavy traffic expected across the area.

A spokesperson for Northumbrian Water said: "The water main has now been isolated, and the clean-up of the flooding is underway with Gateshead Council.

"Due to the flooding caused by the burst, and the amount of debris pulled from the bank, this is a difficult operation.
