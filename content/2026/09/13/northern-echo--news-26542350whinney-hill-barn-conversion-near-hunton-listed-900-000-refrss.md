BUYERS looking for a stylish home that will serve as the base for a quieter pace of life should make sure to put Whinney Hill on their list of properties to view.

A stone barn conversion, the house is set within approximately 4.44 acres of gardens and grounds amidst the stunning countryside and open farmland at the heart of the Dales and encompasses a wealth of beautifully presented accommodation.

Briefly, on the ground floor this comprises a cloakroom; a spacious living and dining room, with the seating area centred around a wood burning stove in a fireplace and with patio doors providing direct access to the garden; a versatile additional reception room currently used as a playroom but equally well suited to being a snug, home office or bedroom; two bedrooms – the spacious principal bedroom and another currently used as a home office; the beautifully-appointed family bathroom with elegant marble finishes complemented by an original stone wall that speaks to the age and character of the home, and the kitchen.

This extends the length of the property and is divided into three separate areas – a dining area with a door to the gardens, the kitchen itself with a range of fitted units, a Belfast sink and large windows framing spectacular views in both directions and allowing light to flood in, and a snug with a feature arched window.

Upstairs are two further double bedrooms and a shower room, creating an excellent space for children of all ages or guests.

Whinney Hill’s grounds are divided into three distinct areas – a generous family garden complete with raised beds for growing vegetables, a separate paddock and a substantial field to the front of the property that stretches to the boundary.

There is also a driveway that opens onto a generous parking area.

Planning permission has been granted for a double garage with home office above.
