Police have taped off a Middlesbrough street after a man clambered onto a roof.

Emergency services were called to Bishopton Road in Grove Hill today (Sunday, September 13) after a man climbed onto the top of a property.

Pictures posted online show a cordon has been erected at the scene, with at least four police cars and two ambulances parked near the Fairfield Road junction.

The North East Ambulance Service has confirmed it was called to the street at 12.35pm and remains at the scene on standby if required.

A spokesperson said: “We were called to concerns for the welfare of a patient at a private address on Deal Court, Middlesbrough, at 12.35pm on September 13.

“A clinical team leader and one vehicle from our Hazardous Area Response Team are currently on standby at the scene to assist police if required."

Cleveland Police has been contacted for more information.
