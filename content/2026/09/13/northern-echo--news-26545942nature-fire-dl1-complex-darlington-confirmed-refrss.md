Popcorn-carrying movie goers were evacuated from a Darlington cinema after six wheelie bins set ablaze at DL1.

Firefighters were called to the leisure complex on Friday (September 11) after black smoke started pouring from the back of the Premier Inn just after 7.30pm.

People were evacuated from the hotel, as well as Nando's and the Vue cinema, following the blaze, with pictures showing crowds standing outside the development.

The County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service sent two fire engines to the scene, with firefighters finding around six wheelie bins alight on arrival.

  Crowds outside DL1 

*(Image: NEWSQUEST)*

Group Manager Chris Williams said crews remained in attendance until 9.31pm, adding that the incident “looked worse than it was” due to the amount of smoke produced.

He confirmed that the fire was contained to the bins and dealt with “without further escalation”.

The road was cordoned off following the incident, with cinema goers posting online that they were evacuated from the screens with their popcorn and drinks.

"Our movie hadn't even begun, still showing the trailers. Spent £15 on popcorn and a drink for nothing,” one person said.

The Woollen Mill was also forced to close its kitchen on Saturday as a result, apologising for inconvenience. It remained open for drinks.

An employee from the Vue cinema said at the time: "We're not sure how it started, but as far as I know, no one is entering the complex at the moment. Everyone was safely evacuated."
