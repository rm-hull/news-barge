Police caught two thieves after they robbed a store of whiskey.

They filled a suitcase with bottles of Jack Daniels from a shop in Northallerton yesterday (Saturday).

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “When officers arrived, the pair attempted a last-minute costume change by shedding items of clothing as they made off.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/northallerton-whisky-stolen-suitcase-nyp.webp)

“Sadly for them, changing outfits doesn’t make you invisible.

“Both men were arrested and as well as being arrested for the theft they have now been recalled to prison, where we’re confident their opportunities to stockpile whiskey will be significantly reduced for the foreseeable future, meaning their next drinks will be enjoyed at His Majesty’s pleasure rather than from a suitcase.”
