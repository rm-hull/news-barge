A Darlington pet boutique named in memory of a much-loved beagle is going from “strength to strength” after moving into its own town centre shop.

Jasper’s Tasty Treats opened on Blackwellgate in May after owner Ruth Middleton relocated from Darlington Indoor Market.

The business sells natural, grain free dog treats and pet accessories, including harnesses, collars, leads, beds, coats and toys through Ruth’s Pretty Paws range.

Ruth started Jasper’s Tasty Treats in October 2023, selling at craft fairs, trade events and agricultural shows before moving into the Indoor Market.

  Ruth Middleton at the new shop in Blackwellgate, Darlington.


She says the move to Blackwellgate has transformed the business, with new customers still discovering the shop months after it opened.

Ruth said: “To be honest with you, it is going from strength to strength. The difference from being in the Indoor Market to being here on Blackwellgate has just been phenomenal.

“I cannot describe how brilliant it has been. The amount of people coming in has been fantastic and, even now, people still do not know that I am here, so I am getting new customers all the time.

“The majority of people come in and say, ‘Oh my God, this store is lovely. You have got it really nice. It is a beautiful store.’ It is quaint and I get my regulars coming back every week for their treats.”

  Jasper's Tasty Treats in Blackwellgate, Darlington.


The boutique was named after Ruth’s late beagle Jasper, who loved natural treats.

Ruth said the business keeps Jasper’s memory alive while letting her share her passion for quality treats with other dog owners.

She said: “It was just something to remember him by because I had him in my life for so long.

“Jasper loved his treats and he loved the natural treats, so I wanted to bring that out into the community for everybody else to have good, natural, grain free treats for their dogs.”

  Jasper's Tasty Treats in Blackwellgate, Darlington.


Ruth said the shop has become a meeting place, with regulars bringing their dogs to browse or simply chat.

She said: “The response has been phenomenal and the feedback has been so welcoming.

“People love to come in and browse because there is always something new. As a boutique, when something has gone, I replace it with something different, so it is not repetitive and there is always something else for them to have a look at.

“Sometimes people just come in for a chat with the dog. They do not always have to buy something, but they come in to say hello and the dog is generally dragging them here.

  Jasper's Tasty Treats in Blackwellgate, Darlington.


“I do not know all of the owners’ names, but I know all the dogs’ names.”

Ruth has also introduced natural remedies aimed at problems including allergies, skin conditions and upset stomachs.

She enjoys discussing the products with customers and helping them choose what is right for their pets.

Ruth said: “I have started doing a herbal and natural remedy range, and the number of people coming to me with dogs with ailments has been brilliant.

“I point them in the right direction, and I am getting really good feedback. I am saving people a lot of money before they have to go to the vet and they are going down the natural route.

  Jasper's Tasty Treats in Blackwellgate, Darlington.


“I jokingly say to customers that I am the free vet in Darlington, although I am not qualified. I know my products and I know what supplements I can recommend for allergies, skin conditions and bad tummies.

“Everything in the natural range is natural and people are loving it.”

Ruth said educating owners about dog food and treats is one of the best parts of running the business.

She said: “I always ask questions before anybody buys anything. I ask about allergies and whether their dog has had reactions to anything, so I can make sure they are getting the right thing.

She said: “The shop has become a lovely little community. It has been a lot of hard work, but I am really proud of how it is going.”
