A team of volunteers is helping to bring a much-loved community pool back into use.

Northumbrian Water employees have been supporting the refurbishment of Weardale Pool in Stanhope, County Durham, through the company’s Just an Hour volunteering programme.

Staff have helped with gardening, maintenance, and cleaning, as well as donating PPE, supporting fundraising, and providing a mobile phone to the charity.

Jennie Collingwood, head of corporate affairs at Northumbrian Water, said: "Weardale Pool means so much to so many people, and it's been wonderful to see our colleagues getting involved and working alongside the dedicated volunteers who are bringing it back to life.

"Our Just an Hour programme is all about supporting the communities we serve, and this project is a fantastic example of people coming together to make a real difference.

"Whether it's helping tidy up the site, donating equipment or supporting fundraising efforts, we're proud to be playing a small part in helping create something special for local families and future generations."

Volunteers have secured a 30-year lease and aim to reopen the pool by 2027.

Dr Steve Lumb, a long-time supporter of the pool, said: "On behalf of all the trustees, I would like to thank Northumbrian Water for all their help and support, which has been invaluable to us.

"The team of volunteers went far and above expectations and worked solidly from 8am until late afternoon and were an absolute credit to Northumbrian Water.

"One of the teams pumped out three years' worth of debris from the deep end of the pool, and the others helped with a variety of jobs from manual tasks, like weeding, to more technical jobs and were all great company."

The pool, originally opened in 1974 as Stanhope Pool, is one of the UK’s few heated open-air pools.
