Pasta-making classes with unlimited Prosecco are coming to York later this month.

Pasta Evangelists is launching its Pasta Academy experience in the North for the first time.

The hands-on events will take place at Hotel Indigo in York.

Yasmine Hammadache, general manager at Hotel Indigo York, said: "We are delighted to be partnering with Pasta Evangelists to bring a new foodie experience to York.

"Hotel Indigo York has always been somewhere for locals, as well as visitors, to eat, meet and experience something different, and the Pasta Academy is a brilliant addition to that."

The launch is part of an ongoing collaboration between Hotel Indigo York and Pasta Evangelists.

The Academy offers a two-hour, beginner-friendly class in pasta fatta a mano (pasta made by hand), guided by specialist Pasta Evangelists chefs.

Attendees will learn traditional Italian techniques, get hands-on making fresh pasta from scratch, and enjoy unlimited Prosecco throughout the evening.

The first session will take place on Friday, September 25 from 6.30pm to 8.30pm, followed by a second on Saturday, September 26 from 5pm to 7pm.

Both classes will be led by Pasta Evangelist chef Rosanna.

Additional sessions with chef Roberta are scheduled for later in the year, with classes planned for October 31, November 1, November 20, and November 21.

Roberta d’Elia, head chef at Pasta Evangelists, said: "Bringing the Pasta Academy to the North for the first time is an opportunity for us to share the joy and tradition behind handmade pasta, and Hotel Indigo York is a natural home for the experience.

"We are excited to welcome pasta lovers from near and far to roll up their sleeves and curate some delicious Italian dishes."

Pasta Evangelists first launched on our screens in 2016, following an appearance on Dragon’s Den. Since then, the company has gone from strength to strength.

Tickets for the Pasta Academy start at £60 per person and include unlimited Prosecco.

Students can access 15 per cent off using code YRKFRESHERS15 for the classes on 25th and 26th September.

The full list of upcoming classes at Hotel Indigo York includes Friday, September 25 (6.30pm to 8.30pm), Saturday, September 26 (5pm to 7pm), Saturday, October 31 (5pm to 7pm), Sunday, November 1 (1pm to 3pm), Friday, November 20 (5pm to 7pm), and Saturday, November 21 (1pm to 3pm).

Bookings and more information can be found at plan.pastaevangelists.com/events/booking?location=York.
