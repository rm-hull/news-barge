A community garden in Newton Aycliffe has been recognised for its work in creating a welcoming green space for all to enjoy.

The Pioneering Care Centre’s (PCC) garden won second place in the 2026 Great Aycliffe Town Council Best Community Garden Awards.

Graham Barker, centre enterprise development manager, said: "The garden is a welcoming and vibrant space for local people to enjoy, and this recognition reflects the care and commitment invested in helping it flourish.

"PCP would like to extend its sincere thanks to everyone who has contributed to the garden's success, including volunteers, staff, and members of the local community.
