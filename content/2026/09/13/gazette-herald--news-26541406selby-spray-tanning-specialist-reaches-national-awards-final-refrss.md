A spray tanning specialist has reached the national finals of a UK beauty award.

Alison Bell, who runs Lilly Rose Spray Tanning in Selby, is a finalist in the Spray Tanning of the Year category at the Beauty & Skincare Awards UK 2026.

This marks her second time reaching the finals, having previously placed eighth in 2024.

Ms Bell said: "To become a national finalist for the second time is something I'm incredibly proud of.

"After taking 2025 away from the awards following the birth of my second child, I really wanted to come back this year and see what I could achieve.

"I was thrilled to find out I had made the finals again, and now I'm excited – and nervous – to find out the final result in November."

Lilly Rose Spray Tanning was founded in 2020 after the birth of her first child and is dedicated exclusively to professional spray tanning.

Unlike many beauty businesses, Ms Bell offers spray tanning as her sole treatment and has built a year-round business despite the industry traditionally being seasonal.

She said: "Running my business while raising two young children can be challenging, but I have continued to work incredibly hard to grow what I have created.

"Spray tanning isn't an additional service within my business – it is my speciality and my full-time career.

"To be recognised nationally for something I have dedicated the last five years to is something I am extremely proud of."

Bridal tanning has become a core part of her work, with around 70 brides booked in 2026 alone.

Each bride receives a consultation and trial to ensure their chosen shade is perfect for their wedding day.

Ms Bell hopes to improve on her previous result.

She said: "I would absolutely love to achieve a Top 5 place this year, having placed 8th in 2024.

"But regardless of the final result, becoming a national finalist for the second time and having the opportunity to represent Selby and North Yorkshire at a national level is a huge achievement."

The Beauty & Skincare Awards UK, now in its fourth year, honours excellence and customer care across the beauty, skincare, aesthetics and wellness industries.

Finalists are selected by a panel of industry experts, including Katie Godfrey and Jessica Crane.

Winners will be announced during an online ceremony on Saturday, November 21, 2026.

Ms Bell also thanked her clients for their ongoing support.

She said: "My clients are the reason I have been able to turn something I started in 2020 into my full-time career.

"From my regular clients who return year after year to the brides who trust me with such an important part of their wedding preparations, I genuinely couldn't have reached this point without them."
