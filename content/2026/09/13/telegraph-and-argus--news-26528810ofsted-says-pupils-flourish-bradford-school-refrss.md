PUPILS "flourish" at a Bradford primary school where inspectors found children feel safe, valued and ambitious for the future.

The latest Ofsted inspection of Cavendish Primary School found the Eccleshill school meets expected standards across all areas of inspection, with its work on pupils' personal development recognised as a particular strength.

Headteacher Jonathan Nixon welcomed the findings, saying the report reflected the school's commitment to providing a nurturing environment for children.

He said: "We are incredibly proud of this report and, more importantly, proud of our pupils, staff, governors, trustees and wider school community.

"The inspection team's evaluation reflects our own understanding of the school and recognises the significant strengths we have developed together, particularly in safeguarding, personal development, inclusion and the nurturing environment we provide for our children."

## Personal development and wellbeing at a 'strong standard'

Inspectors noted that children feel safe in school, behave well and benefit from a broad range of opportunities beyond the classroom.

Mr Nixon said the praise was especially meaningful.

"Everything we do is centred around ensuring children are happy, safe, well cared for and able to achieve their very best," he said.

"Seeing that recognised in the report is something we are incredibly proud of."

Ofsted praised the school's extensive enrichment programme, which gives pupils opportunities to take part in activities ranging from community projects and performances to outdoor experiences.

Inspectors also recognised the school's commitment to inclusion, praising the way leaders ensure pupils - including those with additional needs - are supported.

The report highlighted improvements in attendance, which inspectors found to be in line with national averages, as well as calm behaviour and positive relationships throughout the school.

## Some improvements required

Inspectors identified some areas for further development, including continuing to strengthen attendance and ensuring classroom support is applied consistently across subjects.

Mr Nixon said the school welcomed the recommendations.

He said: "As a school committed to continuous improvement, we welcome the next steps identified in the report.

"They align closely with our own priorities and focus on continuing to strengthen attendance, ensuring effective adaptations are consistently embedded across the curriculum and further developing teaching so that outcomes continue to improve over time."

## Report highlights 'commitment of staff'

Duncan Jacques, Chief Executive Officer of Exceed Academies Trust, said: "The report highlights the clear vision of school leaders, the commitment of staff and the important role played by governors and trustees in supporting improvement.

“While we always strive to be even better, this inspection confirms that Cavendish provides a high-quality education where pupils are safe, valued and encouraged to achieve ambitious goals.”

Inspectors concluded that leaders have created a "vibrant learning community" where every pupil is "seen, heard and valued.”
