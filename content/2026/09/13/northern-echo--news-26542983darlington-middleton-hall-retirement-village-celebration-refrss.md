BALLROOM and Latin dancing sparked a week of celebration for a retirement community.

The performance took place at Middleton Hall Retirement Village in Middleton St George, near Darlington, and launched a full programme of events, including medieval re-enactments and a grand lunch.

Clare Robson, hospitality manager, took to the floor with her dance teacher, Dianne Howe of D&H Dance Studio in Shildon.

Ms Robson said: “I danced ballroom and latin for eight years when I was younger, before returning to dancing in September 2024 when I joined The Studio with Dianne Howe and Helen Tinkler.

"I entered my first competition in March 2025 and have competed in two competitions this year, reaching the finals in each.

"I’m now training for a competition at the Winter Gardens in Blackpool in early October.

"Many of our residents ask about my dancing and want to see my dresses, so this was a wonderful opportunity to perform for them.

"It was also very nerve-wracking.

"A few residents, and staff, didn’t recognise me in my competition hair and make-up.

"There were lots of smiles all round."

  (L-R) Hospitality Manager Clare Robson with her dance teacher, Dianne Howe from D&H Dance Studio in Shildon 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The Grand Luncheon, held in a marquee for people living in Middleton Hall’s independent retirement properties, raised £570 for Zoe’s Place Baby Hospice in Middlesbrough from ticket sales and a raffle.

The week concluded with the return of the popular Middleton Hall Village Show, a free event open to residents, families, and the wider community.

The show featured medieval re-enactments by Vanguard Medieval Re-enactment from Hartlepool, performances from Locos in Motion Border Morris Group, music from the Darlington Salvation Army Band, and guided garden tours.

Other attractions included a craft and produce show, shopping stalls, classic cars, a barbecue, and refreshments.

Adam Wardale, managing director designate at Middleton Hall Retirement Village, said: "It was a wonderful week packed with a variety of events and activities for people to enjoy.

"It has been lovely to see so many people enjoying themselves, and the Village Show was a fantastic way to round off our summer events programme.

"One of the highlights was seeing the reactions of our residents when the medieval re-enactors visited our care services."

The medieval re-enactors also visited Middleton Hall's care services, meeting residents and bringing a little piece of 13th-century history indoors.
