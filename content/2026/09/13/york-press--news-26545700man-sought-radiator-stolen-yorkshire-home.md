Police have issued a photograph of a man officers want to speak to in connection with a radiator being stolen from a North Yorkshire home.

The theft happened in Knaresborough Road, Ripon, at 7.55am on Tuesday, August 11, North Yorkshire Police said.

A police spokesperson said the man pictured on doorbell video footage “may be able to help the police with enquiries in relation to a theft of a domestic radiator”.

Anyone who recognises the men, or who has information about the theft, is asked to email [david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101 quoting reference 12260155872.
