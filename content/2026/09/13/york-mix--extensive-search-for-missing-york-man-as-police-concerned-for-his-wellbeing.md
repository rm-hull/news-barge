Extensive searches are underway tonight (Sunday) to find a missing man from York.

Xianwei Xi, aged 26, who is also known as Jeremiah Xi and Francis Xi, is missing from the Heslington area.

He was last known to have spoken with family members at around 7pm yesterday evening (Saturday), and concerns are growing for his welfare.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Extensive searches are currently taking place in and around York involving officers and specialist resources.

“Despite these efforts, Xianwei has not yet been located and, as time passes, we are becoming increasingly concerned for his wellbeing.

“We are urging anyone who may have seen Xianwei, or who has any information about his whereabouts, to come forward immediately.”

If you see Xianwei, you’re asked to call 999 straight away.

If you have any information that could assist police call 101 and select option 1 to speak to the Force Control Room.
