A sea of more than 63,000 runners took to the streets of Tyneside as the Great North Run returned for its milestone 45th anniversary.

The sun had its hat on in Newcastle today (September 13) as runners of all ages and abilities took on the world’s biggest half marathon.

The Red Arrows brightened up the skies with red white and blue as they soared over the Tyne Bridge on Sunday morning as runners left the city centre on their 13.1 mile route.

From Chris Kamara and Ben Shephard to Joe McElderry and Helen Skelton, a string of celebrities took part in the event today, kicked off by Newcastle legend Dan Burn.
