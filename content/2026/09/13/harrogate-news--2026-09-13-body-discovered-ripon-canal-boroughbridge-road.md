**RIPON** — Emergency services and police investigators closed a major arterial route in Ripon this morning following the discovery of a man’s body in the canal.

North Yorkshire Police officers were dispatched to Boroughbridge Road at **8:41 am today [13 September 2026]** after receiving an emergency alert from a member of the public who encountered the body in the Ripon Canal infrastructure.

Emergency response teams attended the scene immediately and recovered the body of a man in his 60s from the water. A secure police cordon was quickly established along the canal bank and surrounding highways while forensic teams and divisional investigators carried out detailed on-site enquiries.

## No Suspicious Circumstances Indicated

Boroughbridge Road was closed to all traffic for most of the morning to allow emergency service vehicles to operate safely, causing local travel delays before being fully reopened to the public early this afternoon.

A formal statement from the force confirmed that the initial scene investigation has concluded. At this current stage, detectives have found nothing to suggest any suspicious circumstances surrounding the man’s death. The active police scene has been stood down, and a formal evidentiary file is being prepared for submission to the coroner.

## Enquiries Active to Locate Next of Kin

Urgent police enquiries remain active this evening to formally identify the deceased and contact his family. Officers have indicated they believe the man’s immediate next of kin may currently reside outside the North Yorkshire area.

Anyone who possesses information that could assist investigators with tracking the man’s recent movements or helping identify his relatives is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police directly via the non-emergency 101 communications desk.


**North Yorkshire Police Spokesperson:**

Officers were called to Boroughbridge Road, Ripon, at 08.41am this morning following a report from a member of the public who had discovered a body in the Ripon Canal. Emergency services attended immediately and found the body of a man in his 60s in the water. A scene was established while enquiries were carried out. At this stage, there is nothing to suggest any suspicious circumstances surrounding the death, and a file will now be prepared for the coroner. Urgent enquiries are currently ongoing to identify and locate the man’s next of kin, who we believe may not be in North Yorkshire.



Officials have requested that anyone contacting the force control room regarding this active case should quote the official police incident reference number **12260179735** to ensure information reaches the correct deployment team.
