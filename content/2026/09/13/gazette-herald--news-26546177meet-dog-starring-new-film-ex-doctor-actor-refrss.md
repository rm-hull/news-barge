A new Christmas movie filming in York has announced its latest cast member – a rescued Beagle named Mateo.

Mateo will join former Doctor Who actor and Blue Peter presenter Peter Purves on screen in the film, the Santa Express, due to be released this Christmas.

It comes after PicturePoint Films issued a call to Press readers to find a Beagle suitable for the paid role in a new film – and more than 50 people replied.

  Peter Purves who will star in the Santa Express 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Selecting the winning dog "wasn’t an easy task", admitted screenwriter David C Thorp, "but it was fun".

Mateo's owners, named only as Mark and Amaya, said they both met for the first time in a park in Chile, with their rescue dogs Mateo and Pepita.

"Whilst our two dogs started playing, Amaya and I started talking," Mark recalled. "And a few years later, I proposed to Amaya at the exact spot where our dogs had first met!

"We married and moved from Chile to York with Mateo and Pepito in 2023."

The Santa Express is due to film in York and on the North Yorkshire Moors Railway in Pickering – making it a perfect match for Mateo, according to Mark.

"Mateo may have started life as a Chilean dog," Mark said, "but he seems to have embraced Yorkshire life completely.

"He loves nothing more than exploring the Yorkshire Dales and Moors, so it feels particularly fitting that he has now found himself starring in a Christmas film on the North Yorkshire Moors Railway."

David said Mateo will be "playing a key role" in the "feel-good family film with a heartwarming message".

"From a romantic rendezvous in a park in Chile to appearing in a proudly Yorkshire Christmas movie, he's certainly come a long way!" added Mark.
