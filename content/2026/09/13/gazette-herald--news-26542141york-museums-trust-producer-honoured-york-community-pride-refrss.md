**“We believe in the power of art, heritage and creativity to bring opportunities to people of all ages in York”.**

Those were the words of Rosie Stuart, creative learning producer at York Museums Trust upon receiving her Education Hero Award at York Community Pride.

Rosie was praised as an instructor at the Trust.

She has delivered a calendar of free and low-cost creative workshops for people of all ages and abilities across the city.

  


Her vision and dedication to helping build skills, confidence and connection in those attending her workshops, which included printmaking and hands-on art techniques, was described as “outstanding”.

  Rosie's work "has opened doors, nurtured talent, and created meaningful cultural engagement across York" 

*(Image: David Harrison Photographer)*

Her nominator said: “A standout achievement has been Rosie’s leadership in coordinating the National Saturday Club at York Art Gallery.

“Under her guidance, young people have gained opportunities they would not otherwise have access to working directly with practising artists, experimenting with high‑quality materials, and developing their own creative independence.

“Her work has opened doors, nurtured talent, and created meaningful cultural engagement across York.

“The transformation in these young participants—their growing confidence, curiosity, and sense of belonging—has been remarkable to witness.”

Receiving her award, Rosie said: “It means so much.

“I deliver the workshops alongside a really great team of people.

“We have some amazing site and collections – and we are really proud to be able to share this award with everyone.”
