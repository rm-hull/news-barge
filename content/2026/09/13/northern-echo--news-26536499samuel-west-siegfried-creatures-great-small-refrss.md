All Creatures Great and Small star Samuel West has revealed the emotion behind one of Siegfried Farnon’s most touching moments with James Herriot.

West, who plays the formidable but caring Skeldale House vet, discussed Siegfried’s relationship with James during a Channel 5 viewer Q&A.

One fan singled out a late-night conversation between the pair before James left to begin pilot training during the Second World War.

The viewer praised the Channel 5 adaptation for showing Siegfried’s vulnerable side alongside his familiar temper and eccentric behaviour.

West said Siegfried’s wartime experience was key to understanding the scene.

He explained: “There’s nothing I can say to James, but of course, I’ve been there before and he hasn’t.

“So I can’t make him scared or doubtful. I just have to love him quietly.”

Those final five words reveal the strength of their bond.

Siegfried has mentored James since the young vet arrived at Skeldale House, even as they clashed over work and Siegfried’s unpredictable behaviour.

But the Channel 5 series has also shown the affection beneath their arguments, especially in its most emotional storylines.

West’s comments suggest Siegfried understood more than James realised about what awaited him when he left Yorkshire for military service.

Having experienced war himself, Siegfried knew the risks but did not want to burden James with his fears.

Instead, West said, Siegfried had to support him quietly.


The fan also praised the programme for giving Siegfried greater emotional depth.

West has played the vet throughout the modern Channel 5 adaptation of James Herriot’s much-loved stories.

Siegfried Farnon is based on Donald Sinclair, the real-life veterinary surgeon who worked alongside Alf Wight, the Yorkshire vet and author who wrote under the James Herriot name.

The drama follows James, Siegfried, Tristan and their families as they treat animals and navigate life in fictional Darrowby.

Grassington, in the Yorkshire Dales, is transformed into Darrowby for filming, while numerous other locations across Yorkshire have also appeared in the series.

During the same fan Q&A, West revealed that one prop instantly helps him feel like Siegfried.

Asked which costume or accessory helped him get into character, West held up Siegfried’s pipe.

He said: “Well, I think it’s probably this.

“I’m not allowed to smoke very much, but I try and get one or two good pipe-smoking scenes in every episode.”
