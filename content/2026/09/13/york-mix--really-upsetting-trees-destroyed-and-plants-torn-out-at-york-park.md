Vandals have struck at a York park overnight.

When Rowntree Park was opened this morning (Sunday), deliberate damage to the Friends Railed Garden was discovered.

In a social media post, the Friends of Rowntree Park said it was “really upsetting” to find three young rowan trees, planted last year, snapped and pulled apart.

Pampas grass has also been torn out and strewn around the garden, “alongside a considerable amount of rubbish and cannabis-related paraphernalia”.

The group said: “Sadly, we’ve had increasing problems in this particular area over the last few months, including regular litter and graffiti.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/rowntree-park-york-damage-sept-2026-2.webp)

“Whoever was responsible, deliberately destroying trees and plants in a community garden is incredibly disappointing.

“We don’t have CCTV in the park. We do report incidents of antisocial behaviour to the police, and this damage has also been reported.”

The rowan trees were donated to Friends of Rowntree Park last year and were worth around up to £100 each.

They were planted by Friends’ volunteers. “So while there is a financial cost to replacing them, the real cost is much greater,” the post said.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/rowntree-park-york-damage-sept-2026-3.webp)

“Someone donated those trees, volunteers gave their time to plant and care for them, and now more volunteer time will be spent clearing up and trying to repair the damage.”

They urged anyone who witnesses “antisocial behaviour, deliberate damage or drug-related activity in the park” to report it to police.

“Individual reports help build a picture of what is happening and when, and hopefully help make the case for more community policing and evening patrols in and around the park.”

The Friends group added: “Rowntree Park belongs to all of us. The Friends Railed Garden is cared for largely by people giving their time for free. It’s incredibly frustrating to see that work deliberately destroyed for no reason.”
