Shoppers at supermarkets across the UK, including Waitrose, Tesco and Asda, have been issued warnings this week as products have been recalled.

Health concerns have resulted in a number of products being pulled from UK shelves, including food items, clothing items and skincare products.

The Food Standards Agency (FSA) has issued “do not eat” warnings on food items which may pose a potential health risk to customers, while the Office for Product Safety and Standards (OPSS) warned shoppers to “stop using” recalled items “immediately”.

These are the recalls you should be aware of at UK supermarkets and high street shops this week.

## Waitrose recall

Waitrose has issued a “do not eat” warning and a recall as a food item sold in the supermarket may contain Listeria monocytogenes.

The disease-causing bacteria can survive and even grow under refrigeration and can cause Listeriosis which can last up to several weeks depending on the severity.

Symptoms include fever, vomiting and headaches, with some cases even resulting in convulsions.

In rare cases, the infection can lead to meningitis.

The affected products are 120g packs of Waitrose Essential Pitted Black Olives.

The olives impacted by the recall have a use by date of September 13, September 18 and September 27.

Some people are more vulnerable to listeria infections, including those over 65 years of age, pregnant women and their unborn babies, babies less than one month old and people with weakened immune systems.

A spokesman for the Food Standards Agency (FSA) said: “Waitrose is recalling the above product. Point of sale notices will be displayed in all retail stores that are selling this product.

“These notices explain to customers why the product is being recalled and tell them what to do if they have bought the product.

“If you have bought the above product do not eat it. Instead, return it to your nearest Waitrose store where you will be given a full refund. For further information, please contact Waitrose Customer Care on 0800 188 884, Option 4.”

A Waitrose statement added: “We apologise that it has been necessary to recall this product and for the inconvenience caused.”

## Boots recall

Boots has issued a warning to customers after a safety concern sparked a recall from UK shops.

The pharmacist has told shoppers to “stop using the affected product immediately”, as it may cause burns and comes with a fire risk.

The Love to Dream Sleep Machine has been recalled from all stores because of the risk.

A Boots statement said: “Use of the product with specific charging devices (not supplied with the product) may result in the lithium-ion battery in the product overheating during charging, which could pose a fire hazard to consumers.

“Risk of serious burns and/or property damage if the product overheats and catches fire.”

The affected products were sold between January 2, 2025, and September 3, 2026.

The recall includes the following date codes 7T12TN, 7W15TN and 8P01TN only.

Any date codes not listed are not affected by the recall.

The date code can be found on the side of the Sleep Machine or on the back of the box.

This is their advice for any shoppers who have purchased the product already:

1. Stop using the affected product immediately
2. Disconnect the product from any charger and place in a safe location, away from anything flammable or anything that could damage the product
3. Visit the Love to Dream recall page at lovetodream.co.uk/recall for information on how to dispose of the product and claim your refund or replacement
4. Dispose of the product only in accordance with local requirements for lithium-ion products. Do not place the product in household rubbish or recycling bin

## Primark recall

A Disney-themed purse sold at Primark is being urgently recalled over safety concerns.

The 101 Dalmatians Zip Purse, sold in Primark stores across the UK between March and June 2026, has been found to contain unsafe levels of phthalates.

Phthalates are chemical compounds used to make plastics softer and more flexible, but they are strictly regulated due to health concerns, especially for children.

A Primark spokesperson said: "We are recalling our product 101 Dalmatians Faux Leather Coin Purse (Kimball no. 2595301) due to a safety issue.

Primark has advised all customers to stop using the purse immediately and keep it out of reach of children.

## Simple recall

Products sold at Tesco, Asda, Superdrug and more have been recalled by skincare brand Simple.

Simple is recalling its Skin Micellar Cleansing Water because of the potential presence of bacteria which could cause eye inflammation.

Batches of the 200ml, 400ml and 730ml bottles of the skin product have been recalled.

Simple apologised to shoppers, recognising that the recall can be “concerning”.

These are the batches of cleansing water affected by the recalls:

- 200ml – 610041, 611641, 615941
- 400ml – 611141, 611341, 611441, 613341, 613441, 614541, 614741, 614841
- 730ml – 613841, 613941, 611041, 616641, 616741

A spokesman for Simple said: “In the UK and the Republic of Ireland, we are recalling a limited number of Simple Kind to Skin Micellar Cleansing Water 200ml, 400ml and 730ml products with specific batch codes due to the possible presence of bacteria, which has the potential to cause eye inflammation for some consumers.

“We are recalling these products because the safety of the people who buy and use our products is our top priority.

“Other Simple products are not affected by this issue.

“We understand that product recalls can be concerning, and we are sorry for any concern or inconvenience this may have caused.

“Stop using it and contact our Consumer Careline by email for further guidance and a refund.”

The batch code can be found on the bottom of the bottle. It is a series of numbers printed directly onto the packaging.

Tesco added: “If you have purchased an affected batch code of one of the products being recalled, please do not use it and instead return it to any Tesco store for a full refund. No receipt is required.”

While Asda said: “If you have purchased Kind to Skin Micellar Cleansing Water 200ml or 400ml of the above affected batch codes please stop using the product. You can bring it back to your nearest store where you will be given a full refund. You do not need your receipt.

“Alternatively, you can contact Unilever’s Consumer Careline by email for further guidance and a refund at [ukicare@unilever.com](mailto:ukicare@unilever.com) . Or via phone: 0800 146 252 (UK) or 1800 604 060 (ROI).

“We are very sorry for any inconvenience caused.”

## 
  **What is a product recall?**

If there is a problem with a food product that means it should not be sold, then it might be 'withdrawn' (taken off the shelves) or 'recalled' (when customers are asked to return the product).

The FSA issues Product Withdrawal Information Notices and Product Recall Information Notices to let consumers and local authorities know about problems associated with food.

In some cases, a 'Food Alert for Action' is issued.

This provides local authorities with details of specific actions to be taken on behalf of consumers.
