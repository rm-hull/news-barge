A new private health, wellness, and aesthetics clinic has just opened at Listerhills Science Park.

Pulse and Clarity Clinic, based at Unit 24 on Campus Road, held its official ribbon-cutting ceremony on September 4 at around 4pm, which several business leaders attended.

The clinic is led by three healthcare professionals: Salim Miah, a prescribing pharmacist and clinical director; Saher Ahmed, lead sonographer; and Dr Karen Skinner, GP and clinical governance lead.

The clinic offers a range of CQC-registered services, including pregnancy scans, laser hair removal, blood testing, and IV vitamin treatments.

## 'Bradford deserves greater access'

Mr Miah said: "My wife Saher and I are both originally from Burnley, but we both studied at the University of Bradford and have had a strong connection with the city ever since.

"Even as students, Listerhills Science Park stood out to us. Seeing the science, technology and healthcare focused businesses based there, we always had the aspiration of one day building something of our own at there.

"The idea came from our own pregnancy scan experiences and our belief that Bradford deserves greater access to high-quality private healthcare.

"One of the main reasons we created the clinic was because we felt Bradford’s large and diverse population was relatively underserved when it came to accessible, high-quality private healthcare. We wanted to create a modern, medically led clinic where people could access excellent care locally.

"We are now CQC registered and provide services including private ultrasound and pregnancy scans, blood testing, IV therapy and iron infusions, NIPT and laser hair removal, with plans to continue expanding the services we offer."

Pulse and Clarity Clinic aims to provide a modern, calm, and professional environment for patients seeking both medical and aesthetic services.

The clinic's website reads: "We understand that whether you’re coming in for laser hair removal, an ultrasound scan, or IV therapy, it’s rarely just about the service - it’s about how you feel.

  Pulse & Clarity Clinic

"You might be looking for confidence, reassurance, or simply to feel like yourself again."

The clinic serves patients from across Bradford and West Yorkshire.

Its philosophy centres on providing clear communication, informed consent, patient comfort, and safety throughout every stage of treatment.

  The clinic can be found on **Campus Road, Bradford, West Yorkshire, BD7 1HR**, about half a mile west of Bradford city centre and next to the University of Bradford campus.
