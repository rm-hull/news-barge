## Prep school near York named among UK's best in prestigious Tatler guide

Terrington Hall Prep School was featured in Tatler magazine’s 2027 Schools Guide *(Image: Terrington Hall Prep School)*

A prep school near York has been named one of the best independent schools in the UK.

Terrington Hall Prep School was featured in Tatler magazine’s 2027 Schools Guide, which highlights top schools across the country.

The guide praised the school’s “close-knit community” and inclusive approach, with headmaster Huw Thomas described as “the school’s best asset".

Mr Thomas said: "We are thrilled that Tatler has captured the friendly and inclusive nature of Terrington Hall.

"It absolutely does lead to happy, confident children who can hold their own in any situation, and who are always welcoming to new pupils or visitors."

Tatler’s review highlighted the school’s welcoming atmosphere, noting that its researcher was joined by several enthusiastic pupils during a tour.

Only nine prep schools from the North and Scotland were selected for the 2027 edition.

The magazine collected feedback from current parents and pupils as part of its selection process.

Tatler’s education editor, Tori Cadogan, said: "Always at the top of our minds is: will each and every one of those covered offer the very best future imaginable, whether it’s through academic, creative or athletic skills – or, hopefully, an amalgamation of them all."
