A North Yorkshire post office has reopened following a nine-week closure, with new operators promising expanded services and a renewed focus on serving the local community.

Bedale Post Office reopened on Monday, September 7.

New temporary postmasters Andrew Hart and Asma Ola said they are committed to providing a reliable and long-term service for residents, while introducing improvements to the branch in the coming weeks and months.

The Post Office will open from 9am to 5.30pm on weekdays and from 9am to 1pm on Saturdays. A mobile service is expected to return after final checks.

Customers will continue to be served by familiar faces, including Leah, Wendy, Lauren and Bobby, who are remaining with the branch. Additional staff members will also join the team as the new operators settle in.

Mr Hart and Ms Ola already run Starbeck and King Edward's Drive post offices in Harrogate, the Banking Hub in Knaresborough and The Red Box of Harrogate, which sells cards and gifts.

The pair plan to transform the branch by adding services and improving the customer experience.

They acknowledged the disruption caused by the nine-week closure and pledged to rebuild trust in the service.

It read: "Bedale has suffered from nine weeks without a Post Office, but whilst we serve you, we intend to make up for that in every way we can.

"We are committed to ensuring a smooth reopening and can reassure you that there will be no further unexpected closures."

The new operators said their priority was to provide a friendly, reliable service while learning about local needs.

Calling Bedale “a beautiful town”, the pair said they were proud to serve the area and hoped to turn the branch into a community hub.

They added: "You can rest assured that we will be honest, listen to you and bring changes and services you deserve.

"We are here to both serve and help our new community.

"Please come along and meet us.

"Together we must grow this post office into a true community hub."
