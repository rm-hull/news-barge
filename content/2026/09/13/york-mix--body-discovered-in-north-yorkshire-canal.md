An investigation has been launched after a body was discovered in Ripon canal.

Police were called to the canal off Boroughbridge Road at 8.41am this morning (Sunday).

A member of the public had discovered a body in the water.

#### Ripon canal

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Emergency services attended immediately and found the body of a man his 60s in the water.

“A scene was established while enquiries were carried out.

“At this stage, there is nothing to suggest any suspicious circumstances surrounding the death, and a file will now be prepared for the coroner.

“Urgent enquiries are currently ongoing to identify and locate the man’s next of kin, who we believe may not be in North Yorkshire.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ripon-canal-next-to-boroughbridge-road-gsv.webp)

Boroughbridge Road was closed for most of the morning while emergency services and investigators carried out their work.

It has now reopened.
