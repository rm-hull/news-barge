For four years, millions watched Amanda and Clive Owen's children grow up on Our Yorkshire Farm.

Raven, Reuben, Miles, Edith, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy became familiar faces as Channel 5 filmed life at remote Ravenseat Farm.

But the show that made the family famous ended after five series.

Channel 5 confirmed the original show would finish while commissioning Beyond the Yorkshire Farm: Reuben and Clive, which followed Clive and son Reuben as the youngster developed his digging and contracting business.

So what happened to the nine children viewers had watched growing up?

  Amanda, Clem and Nancy lambing at Ravenseat during lambing season 

*(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

The biggest changes have come among the older children.

Raven chose a path far removed from full-time farming.

Amanda and Clive's eldest daughter studied biomedical science at York St John University, graduated with first-class honours and completed postgraduate study.

She has since worked in science, including analytical laboratories, while staying close to her family and Ravenseat.

Reuben took almost the opposite route.

The boy viewers remember tinkering with machinery has turned that passion into a career.

After starring alongside Clive in Beyond the Yorkshire Farm, he eventually became the focus of Reuben Owen: Life in the Dales.


The programme follows his contracting and groundworks business as he builds a career beyond the original show.

Miles has also stayed particularly close to farming.

He has worked at Ravenseat and joined Reuben on jobs involving diggers and machinery.

Viewers who remember the brothers as children can now watch them tackle major jobs of their own.

Sidney, or Sid, is increasingly heading in a similar direction.

As he has grown older, the younger Owen brother has taken on more machinery and practical work.

He joined Reuben, Miles and other family members during farming work shown on television and has taken an active role in the Anty John's project.

Edith and Violet have maintained considerably lower profiles.

Both still appear with the family, but little is known publicly about their career plans.

It shows viewers how dramatically the years have passed.

  The Owen family from Our Yorkshire Farm and Our Farm Next Door 

*(Image: CHANNEL4)*

Both were young children during the early years of Our Yorkshire Farm but have now grown into two of the older members of the family still regularly seen on screen.

Annas, Clemmie and Nancy remain the youngest generation at Ravenseat.

Much of what viewers watched Raven, Reuben and Miles do is now being repeated by their younger sisters.

Recent episodes of Our Farm Next Door have shown Annas and Clemmie interested in acquiring sheep, while Clive has taught Annas, Clemmie and Nancy how to work with sheepdog Nellie.

But the biggest change since Our Yorkshire Farm ended is that the Owen family never left television.

Instead, their story split in several directions.

Reuben built a Channel 5 television career of his own.

Amanda, Clive and the wider family later reunited on screen for More4's Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids.

That programme follows their attempt to rescue and restore Anty John's, an early-18th-century farmhouse close to Ravenseat.

Its third series moved the project increasingly inside, dealing with plumbing, heating, room layouts and other work needed to make the property habitable.

A fourth series has also been commissioned, meaning viewers will again be able to see how both the farmhouse and the family have changed.

Our Yorkshire Farm’s ending was not the end of the Owen children's television story.

It marked the point at which their lives began heading in different directions.

Raven went into science.

Reuben became a businessman and television presenter.

Miles stayed close to farming.

Sid is emerging as another practical member of the family, while the younger girls are learning many of the skills their older siblings picked up before them.
