If you are looking for a nice, easy autumn walk in County Durham, there are plenty of trails you can follow.

The area has lots of countryside and waterfalls to explore to keep you active even when the weather isn't as pleasant.

Low Force Waterfall is just downriver from High Force and should not be missed when visiting the area.


## What is Low Force Waterfall like?

Low Force Waterfall is a set of tiered waterfalls and cascades found in Upper Teesdale.

The waterfall can be found by taking an easy 20 to 30-minute walk from the nearby car park and is a great place for a picnic or relaxing.

You can also find the Wynch Bridge, “a historic, narrow suspension bridge that crosses the river just above the falls”.

The waterfall is surrounded by woodland and upland hay meadows, making it a good place for spotting some local wildlife.

You can park in the Bowlees Visitor Centre car park, which is free but donations are recommended.


### What do reviews say about Low Force Waterfall?

Low Force Waterfall has a rating of 4.8 on Tripadvisor with 57 visitors leaving a review.

One person said: “An easy walk from the laybys nearby on the road, this path is well maintained and signed.

“The falls, whilst not impressive in the way that High Force is, are beautiful and tumbling, the ability to walk by both sides of the river - via a wonderful one-person-at-a-time suspension bridge - was really lovely.

“Busy with walkers and people spending time just watching the river.

“Do not overlook this when visiting High Force!”
