A BRADFORD firm is applying for a Goods Vehicle Operator's Licence.

WDH SERVICES Ltd, based at 2 The Courtyard Home Farm, is seeking permission to use its premises as an operating centre for one goods vehicle.

The application, published on September 10, allows for public comments.

Anyone who owns or occupies land near the operating centre and believes their use or enjoyment of the land will be affected can write to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE.

People have 21 days from the publication of the notice to submit their comments.

They must also send a copy of their comments to WDH SERVICES Ltd at the address mentioned in the notice.

Further guidance on making representations can be found at gov.uk/object-hgv-operator-licence.
