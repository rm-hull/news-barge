Emerging entrepreneurs will pitch their business ideas at an event being staged as part of York St John University's business festival.

The Innovation Supper event, set for October 15 at 6pm at York St John, Creative Centre, promises a relaxed evening with a light supper and plenty of inspiration.

The winning business will receive funding to help give their idea a significant boost.

A panel of seasoned business professionals will be present, ready to share their wealth of business knowledge and insight, posing crucial questions, and offering invaluable feedback to those taking the stage.

On the panel is Henry Rigg, the Business Growth Manager at York and North Yorkshire Growth Hub.

With more than 20 years of experience in designing and commissioning business support, skills, and regeneration programmes, Mr Rigg brings an invaluable "eye of the commissioner" to the Innovation Supper panel.

His extensive experience working within business support gives him a broad understanding of the challenges growing organisations face and the support that can help them move forward.

Charlotte Watkins, partner and co-founder of York law firm Secerna LLP, will also be on the panel.

As a patent attorney, entrepreneur, and trusted advisor, she helps innovators turn complex ideas into valuable, commercially focused intellectual property.

Having practised in intellectual property since 2001, she co-founded Secerna LLP in May 2011 to provide businesses with a more strategic, transparent, and approachable way to manage and develop their intellectual assets.

Andrew Bishop, a serial entrepreneur known as The Firestarter, will complete the panel.

With more than 55 years of practical business experience, Mr Bishop, who lives in Ripon, has built, acquired, scaled, and sold 35 businesses, turning good ideas into practical solutions to real-world problems.

His career spans manufacturing, international trade, technology, audio, sustainability, and business mentoring.

In addition to the opportunity for funding and guidance from the expert panel, the Innovation Supper provides a unique networking opportunity for entrepreneurs and business professionals from across York and North Yorkshire.

The event not only aims to foster business growth but also to create a supportive community of entrepreneurs who can learn from each other and collaborate on future ventures.

The deadline for entrepreneurs wishing to pitch their business ideas is 5pm on Monday 21 September 2026.

Tickets to the event are priced at £10, with all proceeds going towards the supper and the prize fund.

The Innovation Supper promises to be a night filled with creativity, inspiration, and opportunity, as well as a chance to witness the next big business ideas from York and North Yorkshire.

Whether you are an entrepreneur, a business professional, or simply interested in the world of business, the Innovation Supper promises a unique and engaging experience.

With a mixture of fresh ideas, experienced advice, and community support, the growth hub adds the Innovation Supper is set to be a memorable evening for all involved.
