A man armed himself with a gym weight and brandished it at shop staff as he raided a convenience store in York, the city’s crown court heard.

As Rivo Chambers walked into Sainsbury’s store in Bishopthorpe Road, one employee ran to a colleague crying “he’s got a weapon”, said Anne Richardson, prosecuting.

The 31-year-old told them “Don’t you …..dare, don’t you …..come near me” and made for the alcohol aisle.

As he left with four bottles of Stella Artois without paying, he told a third employee “he was nice and the staff member had nothing to worry about,” said the barrister.

It was the first of two visits in three days by Chambers to steal alcohol at the Sainsbury’s store in Bishopthorpe Road.

His barrister Lily Wildman said a psychiatrist had assessed that he was suffering at the time of the raid with “psychiatric morbidity” including “severe alcohol dependency and stress disorder arising from multiple stressful life events”. He had since been put on anti-psychotic drugs.

Judge Tim Clayson said it was clear that he had a number of problems, some of them relating to his drinking, but he was currently in breach of a suspended sentence imposed after the robbery and it was likely he would not comply with a non-custodial sentence.

Chambers, 31, of Anne Street, Clifton, pleaded guilty to robbery and theft and was jailed for 26 months.

Ms Richardson said that just before 7pm on May 1, 2025, Chambers was outside the Sainsbury’s store. He covered his arm with his coat, pulled a 2.5kg gym weight out of his bag and held it in his fisted hand.

Then he walked into the shop brandishing it as a weapon.

He got away with alcohol worth £6.75. On May 3, he stole eight bottles of Stella Artois worth £13.50.

This time, he told a member of staff: “I don’t know why you are scared. I never do anything.”

He was arrested at his home where police found a set of weights matching the one used on May 1. He has 71 previous convictions.

Ms Wildman said Chambers would lose his council accommodation if he spent more than three months in jail.

He wanted to tackle his drink problems and poor mental health and after undergoing an alcohol detoxification while in custody was now clean of alcohol.
