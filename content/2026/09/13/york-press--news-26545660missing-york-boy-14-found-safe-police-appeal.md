A 14-year-old York boy at the centre of a missing person appeal has been safe and well, police said, confirming that the search has been called off.

The boy, from Holgate, was reported missing after being last seen in Manchester on Tuesday (September 8), with North Yorkshire Police saying it believed he later returned to York.

Police said the boy was found safe and well on Saturday night.

"We are delighted to say that he was found safe and well last night," a police spokesperson said.

"Thank you to everyone who shared our appeal and assisted with our enquiries."
