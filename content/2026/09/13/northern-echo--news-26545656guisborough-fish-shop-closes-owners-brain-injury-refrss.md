A beloved Teesside fish and chip shop has closed its doors after its owner suffered a serious brain injury.

Priory Fish Shop in Guisborough had been run by its owner, named Sean, for seven years and was known for its commitment to quality and traditional cooking methods.

Sean’s sibling announced the closure in an emotional social media post.

They said: "My brother Sean, owner of Priory Fish Shop, has been in hospital with a brain injury for the past six months.

"Due to his ongoing ill health, it has recently become clear that he is unable to return to running the business he put his heart and soul into.

"I will write a further post at some point, but I am sad to say that as of today Priory Fish Shop is now closed until further notice."

She thanked the Priory Fish Shop team for their hard work, kindness and loyalty, saying staff described Sean as "the kindest and best boss they have ever worked for."

She also thanked customers for their support over the past seven years.

She said Sean’s aim when he bought the shop was to 'serve the best quality Fish and Chips, using only the best quality, fresh Cod, freshly chipped seasonal potatoes and the best batter...and fry them in the traditional way.'

"I'm sure you'll agree that, with the help of his manager and team, he certainly achieved that."

The family also thanked people for the messages, cards and support they received during Sean’s illness.
