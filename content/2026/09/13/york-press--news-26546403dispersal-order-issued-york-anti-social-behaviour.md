Police have issued a dispersal order in York after reports of youths causing "significant anti-social behaviour” in the city centre.

A dispersal order, giving officers powers to arrest people who have been told to leave the area, is in place for York city centre until midnight on Monday (September 14), North Yorkshire Police said.

The force said extra patrols have also been deployed in the area after officers received reports of "individuals causing a nuisance and damage within business premises, climbing scaffolding, and being involved in shop thefts”.

"This behaviour is unacceptable and is having a negative impact on businesses, residents and visitors to the city,” a police spokesperson said.

They urged parents and guardians to ensure they know where their children are.

“If you believe your child may be among the groups involved, please contact them and arrange for them to leave the city centre,” the police spokesperson said.

“Our priority is to keep York safe for everyone.”

They added: “Additional patrols are in the area and officers will continue to take robust action against anyone involved in criminality, anti-social behaviour, disorder or theft.

“We would like to thank local businesses and members of the public for their patience and support while officers deal with the situation.

“Anyone who witnesses criminal behaviour or has information about those involved should report it.

“If a crime is in progress or there is an immediate threat to safety, call 999. Otherwise, report information via our website or by calling 101.”
