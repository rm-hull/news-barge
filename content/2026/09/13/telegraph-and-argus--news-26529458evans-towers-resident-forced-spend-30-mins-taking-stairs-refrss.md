A malfunctioning lift has become a real bone of contention for residents living in a high-rise tower block in Bradford.

Colin Doherty, 58, who lives on the 14th floor of Evans Towers on Rayhill Grove in Little Horton got in touch with the T&A back in August to complain that one of the building’s two lifts is almost always out of service.

The T&A covered the story at the time, but it seems the issue has not been resolved, and Mr Doherty is still not satisfied.

He's kept us up-to-date with the regular lift breakdowns which he describes as an "ongoing issue" and says leaves neighbours “fed up."

   

*(Image: T&A)*

## 'Everybody is always miffed'

Mr Doherty, who works as warehouse operative, said: "Everybody is fed up.

"Everybody is always miffed."

He believes the lifts were first installed in 1991 and are long overdue for replacement.

He said it requires a "major overhaul completely."

He has reported that the faulty lift frequently “judders” and makes a loud “bang, bang, bang” noise when in use.

   

*(Image: T&A)*

While the second lift is usually available, he said it often skips floors and can take a long time to arrive, making it unreliable for those living on higher levels.

Incommunities, the housing provider that manages Evans Towers, confirmed that engineers last visited the building recently and found both lifts in working order.

However, they acknowledged that the left-hand lift was out of service for a short period in August.

Mr Doherty has informed us that he regularly complains to Incommunities - and he wants to know if the housing provider has plans to replace the lift entirely.

   

*(Image: T&A)*

Since raising concerns publicly, Mr Doherty has continued to update the T&A about ongoing problems, regularly claiming that one or both lifts are out of service.

This month, he reported having to climb 14 flights of stairs, which he claims can take some residents with health conditions or reduced fitness as long as half an hour.

## What the housing provider said

An Incommunities spokesperson said: "We understand how frustrating it is when lifts break down and we encourage customers to report any issues with lifts to us - we respond to all lift call-outs within two hours.

"All our high-rise lifts, including those at Evans Towers, are serviced every month to make sure they are maintained and working safely.

"They are also independently inspected every six months."
