A silver Jaguar has been hauled away by police after residents reported late-night activity around the abandoned luxury vehicle in Shipley.

The Jaguar, alongside a light blue Range Rover, was removed following concerns the vehicles had become a magnet for anti-social behaviour.

West Yorkshire Police said a member of its Shipley neighbourhood policing team was alerted to the abandoned vehicles on Saturday, September 12.

Although the cars appeared to be parked up, residents reported seeing people gathering around them late at night, prompting concerns they were being used as a hotspot for anti-social behaviour.

In response, a recovery team removed both vehicles from the area.

## Police statement in full

On social media, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said:

"On Saturday 12th Sept, our Team 1 Shipley PCSO was alerted to two abandoned vehicles in Shipley.

"While they might have looked like someone's parked pride and joy on the surface, local residents had reported seeing people lurking around them late at night—creating a prime spot for anti-social behavior (ASB).

"Not on our watch! Safe to say, our recovery team took "double-parking" to a whole new level.

"This silver Jaguar and light blue Range Rover have officially been whisked away on a two-for-one transport special, ending their brief stint as late-night hangouts.

"Vehicles left to attract ASB and nuisance in Shipley won't be tolerated. If you suspect a vehicle is abandoned or being used as a hotspot for anti-social behaviour in your area, please report it via our online webchat or by calling 101."
