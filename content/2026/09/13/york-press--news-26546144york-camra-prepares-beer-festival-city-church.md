A beer festival is preparing to get underway – in a York church.

York CAMRA’s 52nd Beer and Cider Festival opens on Wednesday (September 16) to Saturday in St Lawrence Parish Church, off Lawrence Street.

The Bishop of Beverley is due to bless the beer on Tuesday before the doors open to the public.

Previous events have seen thousands of people fill the church where hundreds of different beers were on offer.

CAMRA volunteers have been working over the weekend to get the church ready for this year’s festival.

Organiser Karl Smith praised the volunteers, saying: "It never ceases to amaze me what we achieve within a very short time at St Lawrence's, thanks to our unpaid but committed workforce.”

He said almost all the cask beer has arrived with work underway to build the bars.

Festivalgoers will be able to sample several wooden casks, including Rudgate's Ruby Mild and Sunbeam's Parachuting Hedgehog.

The festival has free admission for CAMRA members and people that join the campaign for real ale at the festival will get two free pint tokens, Karl said.

  *For more information about the festival and tickets, visit [https://yorkbeerfestival.camra.org.uk/sessions/*](https://yorkbeerfestival.camra.org.uk/sessions/*)
