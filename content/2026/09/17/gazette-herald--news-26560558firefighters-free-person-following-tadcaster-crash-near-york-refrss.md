A person had to be freed following a crash involving three vehicles in Tadcaster this afternoon, Thursday, September 17.

Firefighters from Tadcaster rushed to the scene in the town at 3pm.

Recommended reads:

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service said: "One person was medically trapped and required assistance exiting the vehicle. Crews also administered first aid to other casualties on scene."

The fire crew used a two-metre ladder, tools and a thermal imaging camera at the scene, and left the incident in the hands of the police.
