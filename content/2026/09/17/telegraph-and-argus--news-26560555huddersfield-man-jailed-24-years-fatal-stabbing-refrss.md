A man who fatally stabbed a 20-year-old in West Yorkshire has been jailed for 24 years.

Luke Belbin, 25, of Mitre Street, Marsh, Huddersfield, was sentenced at Leeds Crown Court on Thursday, September 17 after being found guilty of manslaughter and possession of an offensive weapon following a two-week trial.

The man who helped him escape, Cameron Boylan, 20, of Fanny Moor Crescent, Lower Houses, Huddersfield, was also prosecuted in connection with the incident.

Boylan was sentenced to a two-year suspended prison sentence after admitting assisting an offender.

Speaking after the sentencing, Detective Chief Inspector Stacey Atkinson, of West Yorkshire Police’s Homicide and Major Enquiry Team, said: "We welcome the sentencing of these men today following the dreadful events of 20 January this year which resulted in a young man losing his life and robbed a family of a son.

“Luke Belbin had armed himself and, judging by the injuries he inflicted on Kyrell, was willing to use a lethal level of violence on others.

"We hope Kyrell’s family can take some comfort from seeing him jailed.”

## What happened?

Jurors heard how Belbin fatally stabbed Kyrell Fleary-Gayle, 20, after the pair encountered each other outside an address on Greenwood Street, Primrose Hill, Huddersfield, on January 20.

Following the stabbing, Belbin fled the scene and was later picked up on Bell Street by Boylan, who was driving a Nissan X-Trail.

Belbin handed himself in to police four days later.

At an earlier hearing on August 6, a jury found Belbin guilty of manslaughter and possessing an offensive weapon.

Det Ch Insp Atkinson said: “Kyrell’s manslaughter is another devastating example of the tragic consequences of knife crime in our communities.

"Significant work remains ongoing with the Violence Reduction Partnership to reduce knife crime, including continuing education programmes to deter young people from carrying knives."

Det Ch Insp Atkinson urged anyone with information about people carrying illegal weapons to contact police or provide information anonymously through Crimestoppers.
