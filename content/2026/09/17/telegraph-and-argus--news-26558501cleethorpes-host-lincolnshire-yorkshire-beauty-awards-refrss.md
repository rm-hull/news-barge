A NIGHT of glamour will take centre stage in Cleethorpes this autumn.

The Lincolnshire and Yorkshire Beauty Awards will be held at the Beachcomber in Cleethorpes, Lincolnshire on Saturday, October 17, 2026, with winners and runners-up progressing to the Miss England Final.

Regional promoter Phil Green welcomes anyone from the town to an evening of glamour.

The competition is open to women aged 16 to 28, with contestants representing towns and cities across Lincolnshire and Yorkshire.

Entry requires a £100 fee, payable to The Agency Group (UK) Limited by September 11, 2026.

Participants are encouraged to seek sponsorship or crowdfunding support to help cover the cost.

Entrants will need a long dress and sportswear for the day, and will take part in a variety of challenge rounds designed to showcase personality, talent, and confidence.

Rounds include fundraising for the Miss World charity, a makeup-free "Bare Face Beauty" challenge, a sports round, and a talent round.

A public vote will also be held on the Miss England app from October 1, with the winner advancing directly to the final of Miss England as the Yorkshire/Lincolnshire Peoples Choice winner if she doesn't win a regional title.

Anyone interested in entering the Lincolnshire and Yorkshire Beauty Awards or the Mr England competition can email [info@missengland.info.](mailto:info@missengland.info.)
