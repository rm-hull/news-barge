Rishi Sunak has visited a North Yorkshire manufacturer developing technology to make HGVs safer and more secure.

The Richmond and Northallerton MP toured The Vehicle Group’s 27,000 sq ft site in Colburn and met staff in research, development, engineering and manufacturing.

Mr Sunak saw how the company designs and builds technology for commercial vehicles.

The Vehicle Group specialises in camera systems, driver-safety aids and hazard-detection tools, all developed and built in Britain.

The company also uses artificial intelligence to develop advanced driver-assistance and vehicle-monitoring systems.

Mr Sunak said: "It was fascinating to see the technology being developed here in Colburn and to meet the people behind the products being designed and manufactured here.

"The Vehicle Group shows what can be achieved when British engineering, manufacturing and innovation come together to solve practical problems.

"It was particularly encouraging to see the company creating skilled, local jobs while developing products for customers here in the UK and around the world."

The visit included a tour of the factory and talks about the company’s plans for innovation and growth.

The Vehicle Group employs more than 70 people at Colburn, around 30 per cent of whom are veterans, reservists or from military families.

Its products are used by major operators including Royal Mail, Next and Ocado, as well as local businesses.

The company also exports its products overseas.

Mr Sunak also saw how the firm uses 3D printing and rapid prototyping to develop products and improve fuel efficiency while cutting emissions.

Martin Perks, Managing Director at The Vehicle Group, said: "It was a pleasure to show Rishi our cutting-edge technology and state-of-the-art facility here in Colburn.

"His enthusiasm for what he saw was a real boost for the whole team as we push forward with new innovations and international growth.

"Rishi’s visit was a great opportunity to demonstrate how our British-made technology delivers on that mission."
