Police have issued a warning after an intruder was disturbed in a York home.

The resident of **Stockton Lane** discovered the man in their house at**2.49amon Tuesday**.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The male suspect made off immediately on being discovered.

#### Stockton Lane, York

“It is believed the intruder gained entry via an insecure door to the property, and we are warning residents to remain vigilant and ensure they secure doors and windows overnight.”

The intruder is described as:

- a white male
- short,
- in his forties
- clean shaven
- balding but with dark hair.

He was wearing a dark top and dark trousers.
