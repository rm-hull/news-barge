A harvest-inspired gown has been unveiled ahead of the Harrogate Autumn Flower Show, which opens tomorrow (Friday 18 September) at Newby Hall & Gardens.

Floral designer Jonathan Moseley created the gown as the centrepiece of *Grain & Grandeur – The Harvest Ball*, an installation marking 50 years since the first show in 1976.

Model Anna Cartmell-Done showcased the creation at Newby Hall this morning. Made with flowers, grasses, grains and seed heads, it reflects this year’s Golden Harvest theme.

Show Director Nick Smith said:

> “This is a particularly special year for the Harrogate Autumn Flower Show as we celebrate 50 years since the first show in 1976.
>
> "Jonathan’s Grain & Grandeur installation is a wonderful way to celebrate the season and the creativity at the heart of the show. The harvest-inspired gown looks fantastic!
>
> “It’s a fitting centrepiece for our 50th anniversary and captures the richness and abundance of autumn in a really creative way. We’re delighted to be able to share it with visitors as they join us for this year’s show.”

The three-day event will feature more than 5,000 blooms in the Grand Floral Pavilion, as well as displays from specialist nurseries and horticultural societies.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aabdc395726b0fc6f4.jpg)

Prize-winning fruit and vegetables will be on show in the Incredible Edible Pavilion, including entries in the NEHS Giant Vegetable Competition and National Onion Championship.

There will also be talks, demonstrations, live music, family activities and stalls selling plants, gifts and seasonal produce.

Admission includes access to Newby Hall’s gardens and floral installations inside the house.

The show runs from 18 to 20 September. Adult tickets cost £29, while under-16s and general parking are free.

*Tickets are available**online** and at the gate.*
