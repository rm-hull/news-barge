Police are appealing for witnesses after a black Range Rover was deliberately damaged on Tentergate Road in Knaresborough.

The vehicle was parked legally and was not causing an obstruction.

The damage was discovered on 10th September, but may have happened the previous day.

A police spokesperson said:

> *"* We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident or may have ringdoorbell /CCTV or dashcam footage that can assist the investigation."

*Information can be emailed to [Fiona.Marshall@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Fiona.Marshall@northyorkshire.police.uk), reported through the North Yorkshire Police website or passed on by calling 101. Crimestoppers can also be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111.*
