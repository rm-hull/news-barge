A CANNABIS farm has been discovered by police after carrying out a drugs warrant at a Bradford address.

Officers from Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team raided a property on Daisy Street in Great Horton on Monday, where they discovered 43 plants.

A man was arrested at the scene and remains in police custody while enquiries continue.

West Yorkshire Police said the warrant formed part of ongoing efforts to tackle drug-related crime and disrupt those involved in illegal activity across the district.

## Police statement in full

On social media, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "On 14/09/2026 Officers from Bradford South NPT Team 1, executed a drugs warrant at an address on Daisy Street, resulting in the discovery of 43 cannabis plants.

"A male suspect was arrested and remains in police custody while enquiries continue.

"This is another example of our ongoing commitment to tackling drug-related crime and keeping our communities safe.

"We will continue to act on information provided by residents and target those involved in criminal activity.

"If you have information about suspected drug dealing or other criminal activity in your area, please report it via 101 or online."
