Two fire crews successfully protected buildings after a blaze threatened to get out of control last night (Wednesday).

York and Acomb crews were called to a large amount of wood on fire in **Stillingfleet** at**6.03pm**.

It had had ignited after a controlled burn had spread due to windy weather conditions.

#### Stillingfleet

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews extinguished the fire and prevented escalation by protecting nearby buildings.

“Both occupiers were checked for smoke inhalation but neither needed further medical assistance.

“The fire was extinguished with the assistance of on site farm machinery and advice was given to the occupier who will continue to monitor.”
