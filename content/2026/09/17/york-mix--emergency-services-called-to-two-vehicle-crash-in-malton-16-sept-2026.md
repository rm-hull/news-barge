There was a crash in Malton last night (Wednesday).

It happened at **6.13pm**. Firefighters from Malton went to the two-vehicle collision. 

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “All persons were out on arrival of crews and did not require any medical attention.

“Crews moved both vehicles to a safe location and disconnected both batteries using small tools.

“The incident was left with both vehicle owners and police informed.”
