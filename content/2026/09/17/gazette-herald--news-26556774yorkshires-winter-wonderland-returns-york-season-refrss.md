YORKSHIRE'S Winter Wonderland is returning for its 21st season.

The festive event will be held at Designer Outlet York, featuring the largest outdoor ice rink in the north of England, a funfair, Santa’s Grotto and a revamped Alpine Chalet café.

It will run from Friday, November 13, 2026 to Sunday, January 3, 2027, and will feature a 975sqm rink made of real ice, with a 30-foot Christmas tree at its centre.

Paul Tyler, centre manager at Designer Outlet York, said: "Christmas at Designer Outlet York just wouldn’t be the same without Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland.

"For many families, the event has become a cherished fixture in their festive calendar, offering fun outdoor activities for all ages to enjoy.

"We love seeing the joy it brings, and it truly helps spread that festive cheer while people are also able to tick off their Christmas shopping list.

"The ice rink has become a real pillar of the community, bringing together families, couples, and individuals during the festive season to create lasting memories."

The event is celebrating 20 years in York and has welcomed more than one million skaters since it began.

Its rink is surrounded by landscaped woodland, giant illuminated trees, and glowing bauble displays, designed by the team behind the Aglow! light trail at Bishop Auckland.

Jennifer Rojko, event organiser of Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland, said: "Every year we get excited about bringing York’s favourite Christmas event back to the Designer Outlet and unveiling our new ideas and festive touches.

"This season promises to be as colourful as ever, and our new look Chalet with its live music and theme nights will bring a new buzz to the event.

"Not only do we offer great winter exercise and fun for all the family, but with free parking and superb shopping Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland makes a great day – and evening – out."

The reimagined Alpine Chalet café will feature an evening bar and performance stage, hosting live music and themed nights.

Outdoor marshmallow pits with a real fire and a German Grill will offer space for visitors to relax and enjoy festive food.

A Frequent Skater Pass is available, allowing visitors to earn a free sixth session after five visits.

The funfair will return with family rides and vintage attractions, including the Brooklands Dodgems from 1937.

Santa’s Grotto will once again offer a magical experience for children, with a festive trail leading to Santa’s cabin and an opportunity to support the annual Toy Appeal for families in need.

Tickets are now on sale at yorkshireswinterwonderland.com.

Early bird discounts of 20 per cent are available until September 30.
