A North Ormesby shop has been closed for the second time in weeks over concerns about a ‘cockroach infestation’.

Middlesbrough Council secured a hygiene emergency prohibition order (HEPO) against Authentic African Foods on Kings Road.

Magistrate Heather Goodwill signed the HEPO at Teesside Magistrates’ Court on Friday, September 11.

It followed a council environmental health officer’s complaint that the “cockroach infestation” posed an imminent risk to health.

The store suffered a similar fate back in July, when it was ordered to shut by Teesside Magistrates as a result of rat droppings, crawling cockroaches and other problems.

During the July inspection, officers found rat droppings on food packaging, dead flies and cockroaches, as well as mouldy fruit and vegetables.

The shop’s retail manager told the Local Democracy Reporting Service (LDRS) at the time that staff had stepped up cleaning.

The LDRS has contacted the shop for further comment following the second closure.

The court found that the health-risk condition was met and banned the premises from being used for any food business.
