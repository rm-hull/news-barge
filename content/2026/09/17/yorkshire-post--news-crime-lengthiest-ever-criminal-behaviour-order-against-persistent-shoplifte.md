# “Lengthiest ever” criminal behaviour order against persistent shoplifter

## Redcar & Cleveland Borough Council’s Antisocial Behaviour Team and Greater Eston NPT has secured what they believe to be the district's lengthiest ever criminal behaviour order (CBO) given to a shoplifter.

Alex Wood, 23, was arrested and interviewed in connection with dozens of reports of thefts from stores - mainly across Cleveland Retail Park.

He was subsequently charged with 33 shoplifting offences involving goods with a combined total of over £5,000.

Wood was remanded in custody until he appeared at Teesside Magistrates’ Court on Monday September 14 where a five-year CBO application was granted.

![Alex Wood, 23, was arrested over the weekend and interviewed in connection with dozens of reports of thefts from stores - mainly across Cleveland Retail Park.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmYwZWZiNmM1LTgzMjQtNDIxYS1hYTU5LTJkOWY0Yjc1Y2ZhYzoyNjMxMDYxOC1mNGY5LTQxNGMtYTRiMy05NjllMWRkZTBmNTM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



He was also jailed for 44 weeks and on his release, he’ll be strictly monitored to ensure he keeps to the terms of his criminal behaviour order – namely not to enter Cleveland Retail Park, and not to remain in any store or retail premises within England if asked to leave by staff.

NPT Temporary Sergeant Robinson said: “This is a fantastic result – it’s virtually unheard of to achieve a five-year CBO against a shoplifter – and I can’t thank Redcar & Cleveland Borough Council’s ASB Team staff enough for working all hours this weekend to compile the evidence and help get it over the line in court earlier this week.

“Cleveland Retail Park stores will now have some respite from Wood's persistent offending and I can reassure retailers, security staff and members of the public that on his eventual release he’ll be monitored for any breach of the CBO conditions, risking a fine or even being sent back to jail if he does.

“Our partnership work over the past couple of weeks has now seen two persistent shoplifters dealt with and I want to reassure our local retailers that our support for them will continue.”

Coun Neil Bendelow, Cabinet Member for Neighbourhoods at Redcar & Cleveland Borough Council, said: “This is a very good result for our retailers, their hard-working staff and every decent citizen who pays their way and supports our local businesses. The fact that a CBO of this length was granted tells its own story regarding the extent of this individual’s offending. I’d like to offer my sincere thanks to the police and our own officers for their commitment and hard work in this case.”
