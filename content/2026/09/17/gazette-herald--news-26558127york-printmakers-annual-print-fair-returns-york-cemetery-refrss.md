*York Printmakers Annual Print Fair returns to York Cemetery – here are all the details...*

A GROUP of artists are preparing to showcase their work at an annual print fair.

The York Printmakers Annual Print Fair will take place this weekend – from September 19 to 20 – at York Cemetery, where visitors can explore a wide range of handmade prints, including linocuts, etchings, collagraphs, and wood engravings.

More than 20 artists will be exhibiting and selling their work during the two-day event, which runs from 10am to 5pm in the Chapel and Harriet Room.

Among those taking part is Jen Dring, who discovered printmaking after watching a linocut demonstration.

Jen said: "I was instantly fascinated.

"I’ve never looked back."

  Jen Dring in her studio 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Jane Duke, another member of the collective, said the appeal lies in both the process and the people who share it.

Jane said: "It’s not just the element of surprise.

"It’s the generosity of the printmaking community that keeps me coming back."

  Jane Duke in her studio 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Roger Goldthorpe, who began printmaking after retirement, has turned a brief exploration into a lasting passion.

Now 79, he continues to produce original work and demonstrates that creativity is not limited by age.

Each print is handmade, with subtle differences that give every piece its own character.

Visitors will have the opportunity to meet the artists, learn about their techniques, and purchase artwork directly from the makers.

Admission to the event is free.

The York Printmakers Annual Print Fair will take place at York Cemetery, YO10 5AJ, on September 19 and 20 from 10am to 5pm.

More information can be found at www.yorkprintmakers.org.uk or by following @yorkprintmakers on Instagram.
