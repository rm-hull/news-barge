AN academy is inviting families to discover what it offers at an open evening this month.

Tong Leadership Academy, located on Westgate Hill Street in Bradford, will host its Year 7 Open Evening on Tuesday, September 22, from 4pm to 6pm.

Visitors can tour the school, meet staff and students, and explore its facilities, including an indoor swimming pool, 3G sports pitches, a fully equipped gym, and a 350-seat theatre.

A spokesperson for the academy said: "The evening will also provide an opportunity to discover more about Tong’s academic curriculum, co-curricular programme and pastoral support."

Information will be available on enrichment activities, and the wider support offered to pupils.

As part of Star Academies, the school benefits from the resources and expertise of one of the UK’s leading education providers.

Tong Leadership Academy has been part of Star Academies since December 2016 and serves pupils aged 11 to 16.
