Soul band Simply Red are coming to the North Yorkshire coast next year.

Having spent a year away from the spotlight, Simply Red have announced a special UK and European headline tour for 2027 – and will play Scarborough Open Air Theatre on Tuesday 17 August 2027.

The ‘Summer Funk ‘27’ tour will see Mick Hucknall and the band play a unique high-energy set, bringing good times and a celebratory atmosphere.

Simply Red have twice headlined Scarborough OAT before, in 2016 and 2022.

The all-new show will be full of their mammoth crowd-pleasing moments and biggest hits – including Fairground, Something Got Me Started, and Money’s Too Tight (To Mention).

Led by Mick Hucknall, Simply Red also features Ian Kirkham (sax, EWI, brass), Kenji Suzuki (guitar), Kevin Robinson (trumpet, horns, percussion), Roman Roth (drums), Gary Sanctuary (keys) and Orefo Orakwue (bass).

Mick Hucknall said: “We can’t wait to get back out on the road again in summer 2027 to perform at some incredible and historic venues across the UK and Europe.

“You can expect a set full of Simply Red upbeat hits to get you on your feet having fun in the sun with friends and family.”

They will be joined by UK soul and funk legends Soul II Soul.

Fans can access a pre-sale for tickets for the tour by signing up to the Simply Red mailing list HERE. The pre-sale opens at 10am on Tuesday 22 September.

‘Summer Funk ‘27’ will be Simply Red’s first tour since their 2025 40th anniversary celebration, which sold-out almost 50 venues across the UK, Europe and Latin America.

This is the second headline announcement for Scarborough OAT‘s next summer season, with the first being Jack Savoretti and James Morrison.
