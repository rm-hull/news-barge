FIREFIGHTERS have been called to a North Yorkshire village after windy conditions threatened buildings close to "a controlled burn".

  


The county's fire and rescue service said crews from York and Acomb were called at 6.03pm last night (Wednesday, September 16) to Stillingfleet after a large amount of wood caught fire as the original burn spread due to the weather.

A North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service spokesperson said: "Crews extinguished the fire and prevented escalation by protecting nearby buildings.

"Both occupiers were checked for smoke inhalation but neither needed medical assistance.

"The fire was extinguished with the assistance of on-site farm machinery."
