A MAIN road into York is set to close for water mains works.

City of York Council said Walmgate will close on Tuesday, September 29.

The affected stretch is between the Margaret Street and Hope Street junctions.

  


The council said closure will start at 4am and is expected to end at 6am the same day and is needed to ensure the works are carried out safely.

Access for residents and emergency vehicles will be maintained, the council said.

Walmgate is a key route into the centre of York, particularly public transport in and out of the city.

During the works, a diversion will be in place via Foss Islands Road, Peasholme Green, The Stonebow, Pavement, Piccadilly, and St Denys Road.
