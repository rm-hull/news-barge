# WATCH: Moment burglar breaks into a Yorkshire home before stealing sentimental jewellery

## This was the moment a burglar forced his way into a West Yorkshire home before stealing sentimental jewellery.

Burglar Patrick Nicholson was sentenced to five years in prison for a Castleford break-in where a couple’s jewellery was taken.

Nicholson broke into a house on Spittal Hardwick Lane at around lunchtime on July 30 before making off with a jewellery box containing personal items of sentimental and financial value belonging to the homeowners.

The 41-year-old, of no fixed address, was forensically linked to the burglary and charged, West Yorkshire Police said.

Appearing before Leeds Crown Court for sentencing on Wednesday Sep 16, Nicholson - who admitted burglary - was jailed for five years.
