A County Durham pub is looking for a new publican ahead of a major refurbishment.

The Coach and Horses in Croxdale, just off the A167, is looking for someone to take over the community boozer ahead of a major refurb by owners Stonegate.

The work will overhaul the pub inside and out, with updated signage and other improvements.

Stonegate wants a "community-minded" publican to lead the pub through the refurbishment and into its next chapter.

The successful operator will be expected to build on the pub’s reputation for good-value food and drink and its loyal local following.

Regional manager Matthew Stancliffe said: "The Coach and Horses is a well-loved pub with a strong place in the Croxdale community, and the planned investment makes this a really exciting opportunity for a new publican.

"The refurbishment will give the pub a fresh new look while keeping the welcoming atmosphere and community feel that people know it for."

Stonegate says the pub is available through its pub partners scheme and currently has a three-month rent-free offer.
