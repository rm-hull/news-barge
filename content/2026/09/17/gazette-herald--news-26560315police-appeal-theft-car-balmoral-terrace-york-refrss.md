A NUMBER of items were stolen from a vehicle parked in York, prompting a police CCTC appeal.

Police are investigating the thefts which happened while the car was parked on Balmoral Terrace, York, on August 14, 2026.

Officers want to identify this man captured in an image related to the investigation as part of their enquiries.

  **Recommended reads:**

People with information can contact PC Ben Robinson-Brockhill via email at [ben.robinson-brockhill@northyorkshirepolice.uk](mailto:ben.robinson-brockhill@northyorkshirepolice.uk), or through North Yorkshire Police's website.

Additionally, Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or online.

The reference number for this case is 12260157860, which should be quoted when providing information.
