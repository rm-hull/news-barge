A County Durham woman has been banned from driving for 20 months and fined after admitting drink-driving.

Lauren Smith, 35, from South Hetton, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on September 11.

Smith admitted driving a Mini Cooper on Passfield Way, Peterlee, on May 16 with 74 microgrammes of alcohol per 100 millilitres of breath.

Smith can cut her driving ban by 20 weeks if she completes a drink-drive rehabilitation course by October 22, 2027.

The court fined Smith £674 after taking her guilty plea into account. She was also ordered to pay £85 costs to the Crown Prosecution Service and a £270 surcharge.

Smith must pay £20 a month from October 9.
