Barnard Castle shall go to the ball with Cinderella as the fairy-tale favourite returns for the town's Christmas pantomime.

The Witham's 2026 production promises laughter, music, and audience participation in the week before Christmas.

Produced by Tom Rolfe Productions for the sixth year, the panto runs from December 18 to December 24.

The cast, including Harry Privett as the Prince and Charlotte Hugill as Cinderella herself, gave a preview of their colourful costumes this week.

Dale Ibbetson and Phil Hoyles star as ugly sisters Alexa and Tesla.

  Cinderella at The Witham, Barnard Castle pictured Cinderella Charlotte Hugil and Phil Hoyles as Tesla and Dale Ibbetson as Alexa. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Cinderella and her faithful friend Buttons, played by Liam McGrath, embark on a magical festive adventure as audiences are invited to “go to the ball”, aided by a Fairy Godmother determined to make dreams come true.

  Cinderella at The Witham, Barnard Castle pictured Cinderella Charlotte Hugil. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Early-bird tickets cost from £15.

A relaxed performance is scheduled for Sunday, December 20, at 10am.

Theatre-goers can also enjoy a visit to the recently refurbished Henry's Bar in the venue.

The registered charity is a hub for theatre, music, art and community events.

  Cinderella at The Witham, Barnard Castle pictured Cinderella Charlotte Hugil with Harry Privett as The Prince and Liam McGrath as Buttons. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The venue includes an award-winning café bar, a restored Victorian music hall and a historic Georgian building, while supporting inclusive culture, lifelong learning, employment and volunteering.
