Clarkson's Farm star Kaleb Cooper has issued an emotional statement backed by Jeremy Clarkson.

The 28-year-old Diddly Squat farm manager, who became a star on the Amazon TV series, lives in Chipping Norton with his wife and children.

Also in the Cooper household is a new puppy named Rex, welcomed in September 2026, whom his children named after a T-Rex.

A popular pooch named Alfie was introduced in late 2021, frequently joining him out in the fields and on his tractor.

But sadly, his beloved dog Diesel died in September 2021, whom Mr Cooper paid tribute to as "the most amazing dog ever".


Taking to Instagram, the farmer spoke about the importance of dogs and showing kindness to them.

He said: "If you have a furry friends, remember…. They are your best friend for a short time in your life but you are their best friend their entire life."

The post was liked by Mr Clarkson, who also has dogs himself.

He and Lisa share fox red Labradors named Sansa and Arya (named after Game of Thrones characters), alongside Arya's puppy.

In July 2025, Mr Clarkson acted as a "midwife" and helped deliver Arya's litter of 11 puppies at his Chadlington home.

He named his Oxfordshire pub The Farmer's Dog (formerly The Windmill) in direct tribute to his beloved pets.
