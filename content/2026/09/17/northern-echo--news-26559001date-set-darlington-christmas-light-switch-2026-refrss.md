The date has been confirmed for Darlington's annual Christmas lights switch-on, with festive entertainment, special guests and a fireworks display planned in the town centre.

Darlington Borough Council has announced that this year's Christmas Lights Switch On will take place on Sunday, November 15, in Market Square.

The free event will begin at 4pm, with live music and entertainment leading up to the main countdown and switch-on.

The town's Christmas lights will be illuminated just before 5.30pm, followed by a short celebratory fireworks display.


   

*(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

Residents are being encouraged to head into the town centre to enjoy the festivities, which are expected to attract large crowds as the Christmas season gets underway.

Public toilets will be available at the Dolphin Centre and Market Hall, while portable toilets will also be provided on site.

Visitors who are sensitive to loud noises are advised by the council that ear defenders may be useful during the event, particularly during the fireworks display.

A full programme of entertainment and details of this year's special guests are expected to be announced closer to the event.
