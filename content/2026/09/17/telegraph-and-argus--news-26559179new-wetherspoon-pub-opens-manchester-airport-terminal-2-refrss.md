BRADFORD holidaymakers flying from Manchester Airport can now enjoy pre-flight pints and fry-ups at a new Wetherspoon pub.

Manchester Airport has officially opened its first-ever JD Wetherspoon pub, The Belle Vue, in Terminal 2.

The 300-seat pub welcomed its first customers this week and is the latest addition to the airport's recently redeveloped terminal, which serves millions of passengers each year, including travellers from Bradford.

Richard Jackson, retail director at Manchester Airport, said: "It's a proud moment to see the final new retail unit opening in our new-look Terminal 2, a world-class facility with an unrivalled range of options for passengers to eat, drink and shop.

"To be rounding off this 10-year programme by adding such an iconic name as Wetherspoon to the terminal is particularly special and we know that it will prove extremely popular with passengers."

  The Belle Vue, Manchester Airport 

*(Image: Manchester Airport)*

## Pub will be open from first flight to last

Named after Manchester's historic Belle Vue showground, the pub features design elements celebrating the city's sporting heritage.

  The Belle Vue, Manchester Airport 

*(Image: Manchester Airport)*

These include vintage event posters, memorabilia displays, a "locker wall" featuring trophies and sporting exhibits, and cricket-themed designs incorporated into the carpets and bar panels.

The Belle Vue will be open daily from the first departing flight until the final flight of the day, with food served until 10pm.

  The Belle Vue, Manchester Airport 

*(Image: Manchester Airport)*

The opening marks the final planned food and drink venue in Manchester Airport's transformed Terminal 2, bringing to a close a decade-long £1.5 billion redevelopment project that has more than doubled the size of the terminal.

The new pub is also expected to create around 150 jobs.

Pub manager Jenny Warburton said: "Myself and my team are looking forward to welcoming customers into The Belle Vue and we are confident that the pub will be a great addition to the airport hospitality venues."
