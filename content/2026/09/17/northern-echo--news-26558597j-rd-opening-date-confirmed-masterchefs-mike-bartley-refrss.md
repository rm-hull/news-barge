A long-awaited restaurant will finally open after years of delays and setbacks.

Jörd, the new restaurant from former MasterChef contestant Mike Bartley, will open on Newgate Street, Bishop Auckland, on Friday, October 16, nearly two years after a fire forced it to move.

Mr Bartley announced the project in 2023, but a fire damaged the original site in October 2024.

  The fire on Newgate Street in 2024 

*(Image: INCIDENTS ON TEESSIDE & COUNTY DURHAM)*

He later confirmed it would open instead in the former Fox's Tale café further along Newgate Street.

Mr Bartley said: "It has taken so long to get to this point, and I am beyond excited to be able to finally open the doors and welcome you in."

Staff and their families will attend trial evenings on October 9 and 10 before the official opening.

The October 16 opening will be reserved for Kickstarter backers who pledged for the exclusive opening-night reward, with invitations due to be sent soon.

Jörd will welcome its first public diners on Saturday, October 17.

Crowdfunding supporters have been asked to check their details for the venue's "Founder Wall", which will display their names.


Supporters who requested an alternative name, such as a family member's, should email Mr Bartley to check it is correct.

The team will spend the coming weeks finalising recipes and preparing for the launch.

A booking system is also being set up, with reservation details expected by the end of next week.

Kickstarter supporters who pledged for tasting-menu rewards will get priority booking before reservations open to the public.

Potential customers have already taken to the comments to share their excitement, with one person writing: "Good luck I knew you could it will pop in next time I’m bishop."
