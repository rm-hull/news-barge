A POPULAR Bradford district pub will remain closed for "a number of months" while major structural works and refurbishment are carried out, its owners have confirmed.

The Old Glen House, on Prod Lane near Shipley Glen, shut following the departure of operators Katie and Phil after two-and-a-half years running the venue.

Owners Star Pubs say the closure will allow repairs to the building's roof and wider structure to be undertaken before a major investment aimed at revitalising the pub.

## Months-long closure ahead

Confirming the plans, a spokesperson for Star Pubs said: "It will be a number of months until the pub can reopen again."

The company said it is also searching for a new operator to take it on

## Landlords announce departure

The closure was first announced on social media by Katie, who thanked customers for their support during her time at the pub.

She wrote: "Unfortunately after 2.5yrs we have decided to close the doors to The Old Glen House as of yesterday.

"Phil and I would like to thank everyone who supported us during our time there."

The pair also encouraged customers to continue supporting them at The Glen Pub in nearby Gilstead.

Their announcement prompted dozens of messages from customers praising their efforts at the venue.

One customer wrote: "You put your heart and soul into that place."

Another said: "Nobody can say that you didn't try with that place."

## Departure was 'amicable'

Asked about the circumstances surrounding the operators' exit, Star Pubs said it would not discuss the detail of individual relationships but stressed the separation had been friendly.

The spokesperson said: "We do not discuss specific details of our relationships with operators but we can confirm their departure is amicable."

They added: "The current operator has chosen to leave the pub and we are therefore looking for someone new to come on board who shares our vision for the pub's future."

## £280,000 refurbishment planned

Star Pubs says it is preparing a significant investment programme for the venue.

The spokesperson said: "The Old Glen House is a traditional Yorkshire pub nestled in the scenic town of Baildon, near the enchanting Shipley Glen, and we are planning a significant investment to transform the pub for locals and visitors alike.

"This will include a full interior and exterior refurbishment as well as an upgraded kitchen, enabling the pub to offer an extensive food and drinks menu."

Marketing materials issued by the company show around £280,000 has been earmarked for improvements to the pub.

The listing estimates the venue could generate annual sales of £451,397 and says the brewery is seeking an experienced hospitality operator to lead its next chapter once the refurbishment is complete.
