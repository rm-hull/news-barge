A York collective is celebrating more than a decade of poetry, rap and performance with a new season featuring some of the UK’s biggest names.

Founded in 2014, Say Owt is known for high-energy poetry slams and lively nights of spoken word, music and storytelling.

Say Owt artistic director Henry Raby said: "We’re always proud of our line-ups, but this could be our most exciting parade of poets yet.

  Kirsty Taylor 

*(Image: Carolyn Mendelsohn)*

"It wouldn't be possible without buzzy audiences showing up and being loud and demanding more and more shows."

This autumn’s programme features established poets, emerging voices and Say Owt’s signature poetry slam.

The slam will feature competitors from Glasgow, Manchester and Halifax, plus two special guests from Bradford.

  Hannah Davies and Stu Freestone 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Writer and performer Kirsty Taylor, who opened Bradford’s City of Culture year, will appear alongside Bradford-born rapper KG (Kemmi Gill), described by Wordplay Magazine as “one of the sharpest lyricists” in the UK rap scene.

The season continues through October and November with a showcase and a Michael Rosen performance supported by Cory Rich.

More information and booking details are available at www.sayowt.co.uk.

  Bradford-born rapper KG (Kemmi Gill)
