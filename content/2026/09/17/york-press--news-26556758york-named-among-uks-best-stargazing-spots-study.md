York has been named one of the UK’s best stargazing spots, beating many better-known rural destinations despite its urban setting.

The historic city came joint second in a holidaylodges.co.uk study measuring air pollution, annual cloud cover and recognised stargazing sites within 50 miles.

York has 121 stargazing sites within 50 miles and average annual cloud cover of 57.83 per cent.

York’s joint-second ranking was based on 121 recognised dark-sky locations within 50 miles and average annual cloud cover of 57.83 per cent.

Beyond York, North Yorkshire’s farmland, villages and quiet lanes offer plenty of chances to escape artificial light before reaching some of the region’s best stargazing landscapes.

The North York Moors offer open moorland, high ground and sweeping views across the countryside.

The Yorkshire Dales offer rolling hills, broad valleys and remote communities, all ideal for stargazing away from light pollution.
