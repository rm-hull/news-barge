One of Yorkshire’s biggest pick-your-own farms is marking a decade of pumpkin picking, with more than 175,000 pumpkins ready for harvest this autumn.

Spilmans, a pick-your-own farm near Thirsk, will mark 10 years of its Pumpkin Patch with themed events and activities.

The celebrations begin with the Pumpkin Festival on Saturday, September 26, and run on selected dates until Friday, October 30.

  There’s a busy programme of events at Spilmans’ Pumpkin Festival this autumn including the Skelebones fancy dress disco 

*(Image: Spilmans)*

Tom Spilman, owner of Spilmans, said: "We’ve pulled out all the stops to mark our 10-year anniversary.

"Our main Pumpkin Festival starts on Saturday, September 26, and runs on selected dates, including throughout the October half term.

"Visitors can look forward to a family-friendly programme including our new Wizfords Academy: The Great Fortune Flop show, Skelebones fancy dress disco, tractor rides, barrel train, spooky walk, hay bale mountain, giant slide, pumpkin cannon, giant pipe rolling, corn cannon, and BB machine gun range."

This year’s 35-acre Pumpkin Patch has more than 175,000 pumpkins across over 50 varieties planted earlier this spring.

  More than175,000 pumpkins are ready for harvesting at Spilmans near Thirsk 

*(Image: Spilmans)*

Varieties include Kratos, Early Prince and Casperita, alongside large field pumpkins, miniature display gourds, decorative squashes and edible types.

Mr Spilman said: "We have over 800 tonnes of pumpkins for picking with a really extensive variety including large field pumpkins, miniature and speciality display gourds, as well as decorative and edible squashes."

Spilmans’ Pumpkin Nights return on Saturday, October 10.

The evening events include pumpkin picking under the stars, street food, a licensed bar, fire performers, and live music.

York Mix radio DJ Laura Castle is among the performers entertaining guests during the night-time festivities.

The farm’s Back-to-Basics pumpkin-picking days run from Tuesday, October 13, to Friday, October 16, and from Tuesday, October 20, to Thursday, October 22.

The dates offer pumpkin picking without the themed activities.

Mr Spilman said: "We know some guests prefer a calmer picking experience, so we have included these extra dates to cater for everyone."

The season ends with Spilmans’ annual autumn firework displays on Wednesday, October 28, and Thursday, October 29.

Mr Spilman said: "I’m really looking forward to the fireworks.

"The displays will be orchestrated to live music, and I can’t wait to welcome guests for this incredible experience."

Mr Spilman’s grandfather founded Spilmans in 1941.

The first Pumpkin Festival, held in 2016, marked a new chapter for the family business.

Mr Spilman said: "The Pumpkin Patch was a huge change in direction for our family business, so I’m thrilled the festival is now in its tenth year and bigger and better than ever."

Spilmans’ play barn offers pedal tractors, a ball pit and a sand area alongside the outdoor activities.

Outdoor attractions include an adventure play area and bouncing pillows.

The Stone Barn Café serves brunch, lunch, wood-fired pizzas and hot and cold drinks.

Pumpkin Festival tickets start at £7.95 and include a £5 pumpkin voucher.

Visitors must book in advance.
