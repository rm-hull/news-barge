AN Emmerdale legend has reopened a newly-revamped food hall.

James Hooton, who plays Sam Dingle in the ITV soap, reopened the M&S Foodhall at Guiseley Retail Park on Wednesday after a major refurbishment.

Mr Hooton said: "So, I spend most days visiting Marks & Spencer Food Hall in Guiseley.

"I drive to and from the Emmerdale studios and location on a daily basis and I probably shop there more than most other people in the area.

  Emmerdale star James Hooton cut the ribbon at the reopening of the refurbished M&S Foodhall in Guiseley


"I’m on first name terms with a fair few of the staff and managers, so it was such a huge honour when Rachel invited me to re-open the store post its extension and refurbishment.

"Thanks also to Serita. I cannot wait to finish work today and head there on the way home.

  Emmerdale star James Hooton with Percy Pig at the reopening of the refurbished M&S Foodhall in Guiseley


"Life doesn’t get much more exciting than this at my age.

"I’m so glad it’s open again."

## What is new?

The upgraded store is now 60 per cent larger and features a 15,000 sq ft foodhall with more than 1,500 new products.

Serita Thomas, store manager at M&S Guiseley, said: "We’re thrilled to reopen our doors and welcome customers back to our transformed Guiseley store.

  Staff at the reopening of the refurbished M&S Foodhall in Guiseley


"We’ve been part of the local community for more than 20 years, so it’s fantastic to unveil a much bigger food store and give customers even more of the quality and value M&S Food is known for.

"From our expanded bakery and coffee counter to thousands more products across fresh, frozen and household essentials, we’re excited for customers to discover everything the new store has to offer."

The expanded store also boasts a larger in-store bakery and coffee counter, alongside a broader range of fresh produce from M&S’ Select Farm partners.

  Emmerdale star James Hooton inside the newly refurbished M&S Foodhall in Guiseley


The refurbishment created more than 40 jobs, taking the team to more than 90 people, including 50 existing staff.

The Guiseley upgrade is part of M&S’ plan to double its food business by opening more stores and upgrading existing sites.

Guiseley is one of several UK stores being transformed this financial year, alongside 18 new food store openings.

The Guiseley M&S Foodhall is open from 8am to 8pm Monday to Saturday and from 10am to 4pm on Sundays.
