RAIL passengers in York and Selby are facing delays and cancellations following an "operational incident" which means some lines are blocked.

National Rail said the incident is between Leeds and Micklefield and disruption is expected until 5.30pm today (Thursday, September 17).

Routes operated by Northern, TransPennine Express and CrossCountry are affected.

  


  
    ❗️UPDATE: With the line being closed between these times, TransPennine Express services between Leeds and York will divert via other lines. This will cause delays of around 20 minutes.

National Rail said trains running between Leeds and York via Church Fenton, and between Leeds and Selby in the westbound direction will be cancelled between 8.30am and 3.30pm.

The disruption began around 8.30am on routes between Leeds and York via Church Fenton, and between Leeds and Selby, with cancellations expected until 3.30pm and knock-on delays likely to continue until 5.30pm.

Replacement transport is being provided, National Rail said, and passengers travelling this way will have their journey times extended by up to 45 minutes.

The Press has contacted Network Rail.
