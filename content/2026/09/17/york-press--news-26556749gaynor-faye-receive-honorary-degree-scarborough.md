## Yorkshire star Gaynor Faye honoured for championing county on screen

Gaynor Faye will receive an honorary Master of Arts from CU Scarborough in recognition of her contribution to television as an actor, writer and producer, and for championing Yorkshire through her work *(Image: Supplied)*

Yorkshire television star Gaynor Faye is to receive an honorary degree for her contribution to the screen and her championing of the county.

The actor, writer and producer will receive an honorary Master of Arts from CU Scarborough for promoting Yorkshire through her work.

Ms Faye said: "It's extremely humbling.

"I love what I do and find it rewarding anyway, so to be recognised in this way is very special.

"The fact that it's from a Yorkshire institution makes it even more meaningful because I'm a very proud Yorkshire woman."

Born in Leeds, she is best known for playing Judy Mallett in Coronation Street and Megan Macey in Emmerdale.

Her other credits include the Yorkshire-set dramas Fat Friends and Playing the Field.

Jackie Mathers, chief academic officer at CU Scarborough, said: "Gaynor is one of Yorkshire's most respected creative talents and a passionate champion of the region, its people and its stories.

"Through her work as an actor, writer and producer, she has brought authentic Yorkshire voices to audiences across the country while inspiring others through her creativity and determination."
