Police are appealing for information after a minibus belonging to Lister House in Ripon was badly damaged.

Windows were smashed and soil was forced into the vehicle’s fuel tank while it was parked at Lister House on Judson Close.

The damage happened between 8.15am on Saturday 12 September and 9.45am on Monday 14 September.

North Yorkshire Police said officers have not found any CCTV footage showing the incident.

They want to hear from anyone who saw what happened or has CCTV, doorbell or dashcam footage that could help.

*Anyone with information can email [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk), report it through the North Yorkshire Police website or call 101, quoting reference 12260180390. Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*
