A County Durham motorist has been banned for 20 months after admitting drink-driving at more than twice the legal limit.

Adam Irving, 32, was driving a blue Ford Focus in Swan Street, Evenwood, on May 17.

Irving’s breath test showed 88 microgrammes of alcohol per 100 millilitres - more than twice the legal limit of 35 - and he pleaded guilty at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on September 11.

Irving, of Swan Street, Evenwood, was banned from driving for 20 months, but can cut the disqualification by 20 weeks by completing an approved drink-drive rehabilitation course by October 22, 2027.
