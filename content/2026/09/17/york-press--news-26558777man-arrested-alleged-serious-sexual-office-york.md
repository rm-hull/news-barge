## 22-year-old man arrested after alleged serious offence in York

Police in York are investigating an allegation of serious sexual assault which took place in an alleyway linking Spurriergate and Church Lane in York city centre *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

A 22-year-old man has been arrested following an allegation of serious sexual assault in York city centre.

North Yorkshire Police said it allegedly took place last night (Wednesday, September 16) in an alleyway which links Spurriergate with Church Lane.

A force spokesperson said the alleged victim is being supported by specialist officers.

  


The arrested man is currently in police custody, the force said.

Police are guarding a cordon in Spurriergate this morning, which runs as far as the parade of vacant shops before the passage which runs down the side of Saint Michael's Church.

North Yorkshire Police said anyone with information is asked to call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quote reference 12260182374.
