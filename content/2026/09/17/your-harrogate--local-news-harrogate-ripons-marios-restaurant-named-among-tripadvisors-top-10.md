Mario's Restaurant in Ripon has won a Tripadvisor Travellers' Choice award for 2026.

The award places it among the top 10% of restaurants worldwide on Tripadvisor, based on customer reviews.

It is the latest Travellers’ Choice award for the restaurant.

A spokesperson for Mario’s Restaurant said:

> “This recognition means so much to our entire team. It reflects the passion, hard work and consistency we put into every dish and every guest experience.
>
> "A huge thank you to all our wonderful customers for your continued support, reviews and recommendations.
>
> "You are a huge part of this achievement and without your constant love and support none of this would be possible."

Marco, Maria and the team said they would keep creating and improving following the award.
