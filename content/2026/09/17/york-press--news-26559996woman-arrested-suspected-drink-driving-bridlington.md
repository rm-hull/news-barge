A woman has been arrested for suspected drink driving after being found asleep in her car.

The 55-year-old was spotted at around 10am today, Thursday, September 17, on Wayside Crescent in Bridlington after reportedly driving to the location.

  **Recommended reads:**

Officers from Humberside Police were called to the address and asked the woman to take a roadside breathalyser test.

A police spokesperson said she failed the breathalyser test, with a reading of 123 micrograms of alcohol per 100 millilitres of breath - more than three times over the legal driving limit.

Humberside Police confirmed the woman remains in custody on suspicion of drink driving.
