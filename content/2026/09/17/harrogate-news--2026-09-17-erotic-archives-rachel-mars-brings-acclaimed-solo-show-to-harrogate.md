**HARROGATE**— Multi-award-winning theatre maker and writer Rachel Mars is set to bring her critically acclaimed solo show to the region as part of the line-up for the Harrogate Comedy Festival.

Appearing for a single night at Harrogate Theatre on Friday, 9 October 2026, the performance—titled *Your Sexts Are Shit*—unearths a century-spanning collection of historical love and sex letters to challenge contemporary digital communication.

The production triangulates the intimate correspondence of historical figures and artists—including James Joyce, Frida Kahlo, Georgia O’Keeffe, and Mozart—with modern digital sexts. Organisers describe the production as both a celebration of the human libido and an academic meditation on the construction of the queer female body.


**The Guardian, in a four-star recommendation review, described the production as:** A triumphant show…dripping with uninhibited desire.



The material explores the idiosyncratic cultural and political constructs that inform modern intimacy, heavily drawing upon the performer’s background in live art, comedy, and personal experiences in contemporary queer kink.

Regional theatre critics have noted that underneath the comedic premise, the show acts as a vital historical insight into queer narratives that are frequently erased from traditional archives.


**Fringe Review, awarding the production its highest classification, stated:** Mars is an incredible performer to watch, balancing humour with bittersweet fierce intelligence… You won’t see anything quite like this on the Fringe.



The performance carries a strict age guidance rating of 18 and above due to explicit sexual themes. Live box office tickets for the October festival fixture have officially been released via the central Harrogate Theatre Booking Platform.
