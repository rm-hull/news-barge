A STUDENT says it is a 'dream come true' after winning a place in the grand final of Miss Teen Great Britain 2026.

Isabel Gibson, 17, from Sutton-in-Craven, saw off hundreds of teenagers to be named Miss Teen North Yorkshire, securing her place in the grand finals due to take place next month when she will be up against other finalists from across the country.

“I am so excited to have made it to the final, it is a great achievement, as there were so many gorgeous semi-finalists," said Isabel.

## How will the final work?

  Isabel Gibson, winner of Miss Teen North Yorkshire 

*(Image: Caitlin Walsh)*

The final of the competition will be held at The Globe Theatre, Blackpool, on Sunday, October 11.

The finalists will spend a luxurious weekend in the seaside town staying in the top Boulevard Hotel where they will take part in fun events including a beauty queen challenge day and a pyjama diva party.

After the weekend the winner of Miss Teen Great Britain will walk away with a cash prize, dresses from The Dress Studio, and, of course, a sparkling sash and crown.

## 'Dream come true'

Isabel, who is studying A-levels in fine art, photography and English language at South Craven School, is an advocate for mental health having had her own struggles with anxiety and ADHD.

“Making it to the grand final is a dream come true – fingers crossed I will be able to win the crown," said Isabel, adding she would like to thank her sponsor for the contest, Craig Holwill from Edge to Edge Surveillance.

## Mum proud of daughter

Mum, Melissa, said: "Isabel is proud of her achievement as she has overcome many mental health struggles and would love to inspire others who live with mental health struggles that 'you can do anything'."

The contest is organised by Pageant Girl UK and, in addition to Miss Teen, also runs a Little Miss Teen and Junior Miss Teen contests, for 10 to 19-year-olds, as well as many more.

A spokesperson says the Miss Teen GB is the biggest teen pageant in the UK with the weekend of the grand final focusing on fun, friendship, and personal growth.

"Designed as a positive, confidence-building experience, it helps girls make friends, embrace opportunities, and feel like queens while recognising and celebrating their successes."
