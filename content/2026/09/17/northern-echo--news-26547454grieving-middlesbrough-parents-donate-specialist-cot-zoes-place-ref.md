A grieving couple in Teesside have created a legacy of hope and comfort for other families in memory of their baby daughter.

Sarah and Joe Jennings, from Middlesbrough, raised money to donate an Abi Cooling Cot to Zoe’s Place Baby Hospice in memory of their daughter Jasmine.

The specialist cot, provided by the baby loss charity Abigail’s Footsteps, allows bereaved families to spend extra time with their baby after death in a dignified and peaceful environment.

  Joe and Sarah Jennings with Jasmine's older sister, Jessica, and the cuddle cot donated to Zoe's Place in Middlesbrough. 

*(Image: Zoe's Place Baby Hospice)*

Sarah said: "A year ago we were in a privileged position to have never heard of a cooling cot and I never realised how significant one would actually be.

"After Jasmine was born we were able to spend several days and nights with her.

"We read stories, had professional photos taken, had cuddles and just had the time with our beautiful girl.

"All the time we had was possible because we had use of a cooling cot donated by another family who went through the loss of their daughter.

"Before we even left the hospital myself and Joe already knew we wanted to fundraise to donate a cot in memory of our beautiful Jasmine.

"The thought of families not having the option of time in the worst moment of their lives is just unbearable to think about."

The cooling cot gently cools and preserves a baby's body, allowing families to hold, touch and make memories in the days after their loss.

After Jasmine’s sudden death, Sarah and Joe used a similar hospital cot to say goodbye, read to her and take photographs over several days.

Their experience inspired them to raise money so other families could have the same opportunity and create a lasting legacy for Jasmine.

  Zoe's Place Baby Hospice in Middlesbrough. 

*(Image: Supplied)*

A spokesperson for Zoe’s Place said: "We are so grateful to Jasmine’s family for providing us with the Abi Cooling Cot.

"The opportunity for families to spend precious time together after the death of a child can have a profound emotional impact.

"This generous donation is a lasting legacy to Jasmine and a beautiful expression of the love her family have for her."

For Sarah, Joe and Jasmine’s older sister Jessica, the cot is a symbol of love and care for other families facing the same heartbreak.

Their generosity gives parents time to say goodbye in their own way, even in the darkest moments.

Abigail’s Footsteps was set up in 2011 after founders Jo and David Ward lost their daughter Abigail, who was stillborn in 2009.

David Ward MBE, co-founder of the charity, said: "When we had our daughter Abigail, we only had two or three hours with her.

"Having to say goodbye so soon was heart-breaking, and we knew we had to do something to support other grieving parents.

"The Abi Cooling Cot gives families the precious gift of time, allowing them to spend two or three days with their baby if they wish."

Through Jasmine’s legacy, families will be able to hold their child, read stories and make memories to last a lifetime.

Though Jasmine’s life was short, her memory will live on for years through families given the gift of time.
