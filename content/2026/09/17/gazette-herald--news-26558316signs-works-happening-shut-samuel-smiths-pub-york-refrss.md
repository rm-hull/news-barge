A YORK city centre pub which closed in 2019 could be opening its doors again.

The York Arms in High Petergate – owned by Samuel Smith’s Brewery – saw signs of works activity this morning (September 17), with the main door open and plastic sheeting covering panes of glass from the inside.

Contractors were seen outside the entrance and the wood panelling at the front of the premises looked as though it had prepared or sanded down.

  


It comes after Samuel Smith’s recently reopened the Wellington Inn in Alma Terrace, Fishergate, which The Press reported had been through a series of openings and closures since 2017.

In December 2024, The Press reported that a salary of £51,000 had been offered to potential landlords in a bid to reopen The York Arms which sits in the shadow of York Minster.

The brewery readvertised for live-in management couples here and 120 of its other premises in December 2025, and further ads followed in May of this year.
