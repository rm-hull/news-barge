Yorkshire housebuilder Beal Homes has unveiled new show homes while backing a children’s charity.

Beal Homes opened three new show homes at The Greenways in Goole, currently its biggest show arena, and used the launch to raise awareness of OSCAR’s, which supports children with brain tumours.

OSCAR’s fundraising officer Amy Owen officially opened the show arena on September 16.

  The Greenways show homes 

*(Image: Jordan Taylor, Beal Homes)*

Ms Owen said: "OSCAR's began life in York, but it is important to extend our reach to the wider Yorkshire region and the rest of the UK, with my work focusing on East Yorkshire initially.

"Having the support of a leading local business such as Beal Homes is invaluable, not only because of the generous financial support, but also in helping us to raise awareness of the work we do and how families can access help."

  House hunters Beth Roberts and Lewis Wood were among the visitors to Beal’s The Greenways development on the opening day for the new show arena 

*(Image: Jordan Taylor, Beal Homes)*

Beal Homes is sponsoring OSCAR’s Star of Hope Wall at its upcoming black-tie ball at Cave Castle in South Cave.

Beal Homes sales and marketing director Ross Clarkson said: "It was particularly special to welcome Amy from OSCAR’s for the occasion, underlining our support for this important charity and helping to raise its profile."
