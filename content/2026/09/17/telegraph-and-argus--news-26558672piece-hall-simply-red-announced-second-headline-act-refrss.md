ANOTHER headline act has been announced for The Piece Hall next summer.

Earlier this month, Jack Savoretti and James Morrison were the first announced for the 2027 summer schedule at the iconic venue.

As part of their double-headline tour, the pair will play Halifax on June 18.

## When will Simply Red play the Piece Hall?

On Thursday, it was revealed Simply Red will bring their unmistakable blend of soul, pop and funk to Halifax, as part of their highly anticipated Summer Funk ’27 tour.

The 80s favourites will headline The Piece Hall on Monday, August 9.

## How to buy tickets

Tickets go on general sale on September 24, with strong demand expected from fans across Yorkshire and the North.


## Who are Simply Red?

Fronted by Mick Hucknall, the legendary band is set to return to the live stage after a year away from touring, promising an evening packed with chart-topping hits and feel-good energy.

Fans can look forward to hearing classics such as Holding Back the Years, Stars, Something Got Me Started and Fairground, alongside other favourites from a career spanning more than four decades.

The band's enduring popularity was highlighted during their sold-out 40th anniversary tour, which drew huge crowds across the UK and beyond.

Joining Simply Red on the night will be special guests Soul II Soul, adding even more groove to an already impressive bill.

Set against the stunning backdrop of Halifax’s historic Piece Hall, the show promises to be one of the standout live music events of summer 2027.
