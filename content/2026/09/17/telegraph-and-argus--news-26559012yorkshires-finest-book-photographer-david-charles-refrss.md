An award-winning photographer has captured some of the region's best-known faces for a special hardback book, Yorkshire's Finest.

The book has been curated over the past 10 years by David Charles, bringing together photos of notable people who are either from Yorkshire or who have a strong connection to Yorkshire, including Dame Judi Dench, Sean Bean, Sir Michael Parkinson, Gaynor Fay, Kay Mellor, Christine Talbot, Harry Gration, and many others.

All proceeds from the book will go to York Against Cancer.

David says: 'Having gone through my own battles with cancer, I know first-hand the challenges and emotional toll it can take, not just on the individual, but on their families as well.

'I was inspired to create a unique photobook to raise funds and awareness for York Against Cancer after learning how they do so much for the Yorkshire community.'

Andy Knowles, CEO of York Against Cancer, adds: 'As CEO of York Against Cancer, I'm immensely proud that our patron, Dame Judi Dench, takes pride of place on the cover and centre pages of ‘Yorkshire's Finest’.

'This isn't just a collection of photographs and words, it is a decade of dedication from the remarkably talented and generous David Charles, who has captured so many of Yorkshire's best-loved faces with real warmth and skill.

'That he's chosen to donate every penny of the proceeds to York Against Cancer is an extraordinary act of kindness, and on behalf of all the local people we support, I want to say a heartfelt thank you.

'Every copy sold makes a genuine difference. So please buy one if you can - you'll own a beautiful piece of Yorkshire heritage and help us continue our vital work. It's the easiest good deed you'll do all year.'

A launch event for the book will take place on September 28 from 6pm until 8pm at York Against Cancer’s cancer support centre, The Leveson Centre. Due to incredible demand, tickets for the event have now sold out.

The book is available to pre-order online: yorkagainstcancer.enthuse.com/yorkshiresfinest

Books will be available following the launch event. Collection from York Against Cancer, East Stand, York Community Stadium, Kathryn Avenue, Huntington, York, YO32 9AF is preferred.
