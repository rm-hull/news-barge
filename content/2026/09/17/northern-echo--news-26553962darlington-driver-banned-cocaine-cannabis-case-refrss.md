A Darlington driver has been banned after admitting being in charge of a car while over the legal limits for cocaine and cannabis.

Damon Michael Bridges, 50, of Durham Road, Coatham Mundeville, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on September 11.

Bridges admitted being in charge of a Ford on Calder Avenue, Darlington, on December 4, 2025, while over the legal limits for THC, benzoylecgonine and cocaine.

He was fined £120 for the THC offence and ordered to pay a £48 victim surcharge and £85 in costs.

The court imposed no separate penalties for the other drug charges.

Bridges was banned from driving for 56 days and received points on his licence.

The court issued a collection order for the fines.

The court took his guilty plea into account when sentencing him.

The DVLA was notified.
