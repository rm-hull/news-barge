A UK coffee company is set to close after 76 years in business, having entered voluntary liquidation.

Joe Black Coffee Roasters dates back to 1950 and is known for its long-standing presence in Liverpool and supply of coffee to businesses across the UK.

The company supplied coffee to cafés, restaurants, hotels, and workplaces across the UK and beyond.

Its website says: "Whether you’re picking up your morning cappuccino at a local café, brewing a cafetière at home, or stocking up for your restaurant or office, we make sure each cup delivers the flavour, freshness, and quality you’d expect straight from the roaster."


Joe Black Coffee Roasters also offered various other services, including training and coffee machine servicing.

The company said on its website that it aimed to deliver "exceptional coffee experiences" and supported businesses "every step of the way."


## Joe Black Coffee Roasters enters liquidation after 76 years

Following 76 years in business, Joe Black Coffee Roasters is now set to close after entering liquidation.

Adam Price and Lane Bednash of CMB Partners UK Limited were appointed as joint administrators on September 8, according to The Gazette .


Joe Black Coffee Roasters has been contacted for comment.
