A prolific shoplifter has been banned from entering a retail park for five years.

Alex Wood, 23, was arrested over the weekend and interviewed in connection with dozens of thefts, mainly from Cleveland Retail Park.

He was charged with 33 shoplifting offences involving goods worth more than £5,000.

At Teesside Magistrates’ Court on Monday (September 14 ) he was jailed for 44 weeks and handed a five-year CBO banning him from entering Cleveland Retail Park.

It also bans him from remaining in any retail premises in England if asked to leave by staff.

Temporary Sergeant Robinson of the Greater Eston Neighbourhood Policing Team said: "This is a fantastic result – it’s virtually unheard of to achieve a five-year CBO against a shoplifter – and I can’t thank Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council’s anti-social behaviour team staff enough for working all hours this weekend to compile the evidence and help get it over the line in court earlier this week.

"Cleveland Retail Park stores will now have some respite from Wood’s persistent offending and I can reassure retailers, security staff and members of the public that on his eventual release he’ll be monitored for any breach of the CBO conditions, risking a fine or even being sent back to jail if he does.

"Our partnership work over the past couple of weeks has now seen two persistent shoplifters dealt with and I want to reassure our local retailers that our support for them will continue."

Councillor Neil Bendelow, cabinet member for neighbourhoods at Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council, said: "This is a very good result for our retailers, their hard-working staff and every decent citizen who pays their way and supports our local businesses.

"The fact that a CBO of this length was granted tells its own story regarding the extent of this individual’s offending.

"I’d like to offer my sincere thanks to the police and our own officers for their commitment and hard work in this case."
