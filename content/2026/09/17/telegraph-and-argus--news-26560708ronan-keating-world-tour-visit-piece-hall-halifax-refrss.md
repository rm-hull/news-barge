Pop legend Ronan Keating is set to light up the stage in West Yorkshire.

He will perform at The Piece Hall in Halifax on Friday, July 23 next year, as part of his Just Getting Started World Tour, which marks 30 years in the music industry.

The Irish singer will be joined by British boyband Blue and Australian pop trio the Germein Sisters.

The date is part of the 2027 Live at The Piece Hall summer series, with tickets going on general sale at 10am on Friday, September 25 via ticketmaster.co.uk.

Mr Keating will perform another Yorkshire gig during the tour, when he plays Scarborough Open-Air Theatre on July 18 next year.

Mr Keating said: "I’ve been incredibly lucky to have spent more than three decades doing what I love.

"I’ve travelled the world, made records I’m enormously proud of and, most importantly, had audiences singing those songs back to me night after night.

"The reason I’ve called this tour Just Getting Started is because I genuinely feel I’m entering a new chapter.

"I understand myself, my music and what I want to say more clearly than ever before, and I want to celebrate these songs with audiences in a way that feels fresh.

"I feel creatively energised, deeply grateful and more connected to what I do than ever, and I can’t wait to share that with people around the world."

With more than 45 million records sold across Boyzone and his solo career, Mr Keating has become one of the most successful Irish artists of his generation.

He holds a Guinness World Record for 30 consecutive UK Top 10 singles.

His debut solo album Ronan went straight to No.1 in the UK, achieving four-times Platinum status and staying in the charts for 63 weeks.

His compilation album 10 Years of Hits has now surpassed two billion streams.

Joining him on stage, Blue will continue their celebration of more than 25 years in the music industry.

The band recently released their fourth UK Top 5 album Reflections.

Blue’s original line-up - Antony Costa, Duncan James, Lee Ryan, and Simon Webbe - remains intact after a quarter-century together.

Their career includes three consecutive UK No.1 albums (All Rise, One Love, Guilty), 11 Top 10 singles, and two BRIT Awards.

Their hits include Too Close, If You Came Back, and Sorry Seems To Be The Hardest Word (with Elton John).

Nicky Chance-Thompson, chief executive of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "Just three announcements in, this is already shaping up to be an amazing season as we welcome these chart-topping superstars to Halifax.

"From your bedroom wall to our iconic venue, we’re definitely making a few teenage dreams come true with this phenomenal night.

"The UK pop scene was the envy of the world in the 90s and 00s and we’re bringing the cream of the crop to Calderdale.

"This will be an unmissable show.

"Get ready to sing and maybe scream your hearts out."

Mr Keating joins Jack Savoretti, James Morrison and Simply Red among the first headliners announced for Live at The Piece Hall 2027.

The 2026 series featured 42 headline shows from acts including Hollywood Vampires, McFly, Tom Jones, Garbage, Scissor Sisters, The Beach Boys, Turnstile, Paul Weller, and CMAT.
