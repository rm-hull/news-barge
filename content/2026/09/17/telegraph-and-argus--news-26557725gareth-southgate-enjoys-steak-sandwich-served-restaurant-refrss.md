A POPULAR restaurant has revealed it welcomed former England manager Sir Gareth Southgate this week.

The Ilkley Cow shared a photograph with the ex-Three Lions boss on social media on Wednesday evening, telling followers it was an "absolute honour" to meet him.

In the post, the restaurant wrote: "Safe to say... Sir Gareth Southgate approves The Ilkley Cow.

"Absolute honour to meet the former England manager and we hope to see him in the restaurant soon!"

## Southgate sampled restaurant's signature dishes

The meeting took place at Ilkley Golf Club during The Yorkshire Invitational.

The Ilkley Cow was hosting a stand offering food samples to players and staff.

General manager Ollie Sypniewski, who was pictured alongside Southgate, said the restaurant was attending the event for the second consecutive year.

He said: "We had a stand at Ilkley Golf Club for The Yorkshire Invitational, where players and staff were able to sample some of our products."

Mr Sypniewski added: "This was our second year attending the event, having also done it last year."

Among the dishes sampled by the former England boss was one of the restaurant's steak sandwiches.

Mr Sypniewski said: "One of the products Gareth tried was our steak sandwich, which he was pictured holding.

"It consists of sirloin steak served in ciabatta bread, with onion chutney and a wholegrain mustard and mayonnaise mix.

"He also tried our chimichurri sausage, which we make in-house using our own recipe, and he really enjoyed that as well."

According to the restaurant, the three other golfers in Southgate's group also sampled the steak sandwiches and gave them positive feedback.

Mr Sypniewski added that Southgate was "very friendly and pleasant in person".

## A football legend in Ilkley

Southgate is one of English football's most recognisable figures, having enjoyed successful careers both on the pitch and in the dugout.

As a player, the defender played for the likes of Aston Villa, Crystal Palace, and Middlesbrough.

Southgate later moved into management, first taking charge of Middlesbrough before being appointed England manager in 2016.

During his eight years in charge of the national team, he guided England to the semi-finals of the 2018 World Cup and consecutive European Championship finals in 2021 and 2024.

## About The Ilkley Cow

The Ilkley Cow is an independent steakhouse located on New Brook Street, just a short walk from the town centre.

Known for its locally sourced steaks from nearby reared livestock, the restaurant also serves burgers, chicken dishes, fish specials and popular Sunday roasts.
