Police are searching for 35-year-old Nathan Cammish.

He is wanted in connection with offences of serious assault and the breach of a Domestic Violence Protection Order.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Extensive enquiries have been carried out to locate him, and we are now asking for the public’s help to find him.

“We believe he may be in the Scarborough area, where he has known links.”

Cammish is described as:

- a white man
- about 5ft 10in (178cm) tall
- with dark brown hair
- and blue eyes.

If you have an immediate sighting of Cammish, you’re asked to call 999.

If you have seen him recently, or have any information about his whereabouts, you can contact North Yorkshire Police on the numbers below.
