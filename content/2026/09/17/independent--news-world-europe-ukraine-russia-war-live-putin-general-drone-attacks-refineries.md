Ukraine says its drone forces have killed a Russian general in eastern Ukraine just weeks after he was decorated as a "war hero" by Vladimir Putin.

Major General Anton Grunis was given the "Hero of Russia" medal after it was claimed he led the capture of Kostiantynivka in Ukraine, despite independent military analysts confirming the city remained contested and that the claim of victory was Russian propaganda.

Ukrainian drone commander Robert Brovdi said Grunis had been killed in a nighttime strike in the Donetsk region.

"General Grunis was eliminated on 12 September," Brovdi wrote in a Telegram post on Wednesday.

In Moscow, his death was confirmed by commander Apti Alaudinov, but Russian officials otherwise did not share further details.

Meanwhile, overnight Russian strikes on Kyiv and the port city of Odesa have left at least 18 injured as the Ukrainian military warned of incoming jet-powered Russian drones in several Ukrainian cities.

A child was among the three injured as Russia hit a 19-storey apartment building in Odesa, authorities there said on Telegram.

## Number of injured in Kyiv rises to 18 after overnight strikes

Russian air strikes injured 18 and damaged buildings in the Ukrainian capital of Kyiv and in the Black Sea port city of Odesa, among other locations, in the early hours today, officials said.

In Odesa, a child was among the three injured as Russia hit a 19-storey apartment building and three others were wounded in Zaporizhzhia in the southeast where a missile strike also caused a fire at an industrial facility, authorities there said on Telegram.

Kyiv mayor Vitali Klitschko said 12 people, including two children, were wounded in the city, adding that apart from apartment and inhabited buildings, debris also fell on warehouse facilities and near a petrol station, which are now a frequent target of attacks.

## Ukraine drone attack damages oil refinery in Russia's Yaroslavl

A Ukrainian drone attack damaged an oil refinery in the Russian city of Yaroslavl and efforts are ongoing to put out the fire, regional governor Mikhail Yevrayev said this morning.

## UK armed forces member killed in Ukraine road traffic accident named by MoD

A 40-year-old intelligence officer has died in a road collision in Ukraine, the Ministry of Defence has confirmed.

Major Paul Wilks was killed in a traffic accident away from the front lines on Saturday 12 September.

Having commissioned from Sandhurst in 2012, he spent his entire military career serving in the Intelligence Corps.

His Commanding Officer described him as a "man of great intellect", who frequently spoke of his deep love for his wife and two sons.

## US House passes sanctions and tariff bill to raise economic pressure on Russia

The US House of Representatives passed a sweeping sanctions and tariff bill intended to increase economic pressure on Russia for its war on Ukraine.

The bill was cleared almost one and a half years after the late US Senator Lindsey Graham, a South Carolina Republican, introduced it with dozens of other senators.

The Republican-majority House voted 262 to 159 in favour of the bill.

## 13 injured in overnight Russian strikes on Kyiv

Russian air strikes injured 13 and damaged buildings in the Ukrainian capital of Kyiv and in the Black Sea port city of Odesa in the early hours today, officials said.

City authorities said 10 people were wounded in Kyiv, with mayor Vitali Klitschko adding that apart from apartment and inhabited buildings, debris also fell on warehouse facilities and near a petrol station, now a frequent target of attacks.

Explosions were heard in Kyiv, a Reuters witness said.

A child was among the three injured as Russia hit a 19-storey apartment building in Odesa, authorities there said on Telegram.

## Russian general killed in drone attack days after being lauded for 'victory'

Ukrainian drone forces killed a Russian general in eastern Ukraine, a little over two weeks after he received a Hero of Russia award for his wartime service, Ukrainian drone commander Robert Brovdi said.

Major General Anton Grunis had won praise from president Vladimir Putin earlier this year after helping capture Kostiantynivka, territory disputed by Kyiv and independent military analysts.

Brovdi, who commands Ukraine's Unmanned Systems Forces, said Grunis had been killed in a nighttime strike in the Donetsk region.

"General Grunis was eliminated on 12 September," Brovdi wrote in a Telegram post on Wednesday.

The Independent could not independently verify Brovdi's assertions.

Russian commander Apti Alaudinov, in a post on the Telegram messaging app, said Grunis had died, without giving further details.

## A personal account of daily life in Kyiv amid intensified Russian attacks, in photos

This was my fourth trip to Ukraine as a photojournalist. During my previous visit, I found confidence and hope that the war might soon end. This time, I saw greater exhaustion, resignation and little hope of an immediate end to the fighting.

I spent a month in Kyiv and the surrounding areas as Russian drone and missile attacks on the capital intensified. Air raid alerts sounded day and night, and thousands of residents slept in metro stations when major attacks were expected. Firefighters and emergency workers responded repeatedly to strikes across the city.
