**HARROGATE**— The complete, itemised financial logs detailing political party donations, statutory government allowances, and benefits-in-kind linked to Harrogate and Knaresborough MP Tom Gordon have been formally published across Westminster transparency registers.Tracked through the Independent Parliamentary Standards Authority (IPSA) and the formal Register of Members’ Financial Interests, the legally mandated disclosures map the structural resource limits governing the Liberal Democrat representative’s operation since his election on 4 July 2024.

### Political Party Donations

The primary financial driver behind the local MP’s political operations remains a highly structured network of political party donations. Rather than being paid directly to him as an individual donee, these funds were routed directly through central Liberal Democrat party structures and the localised constituency party organisation to secure his Westminster seat.

Official registers disclose that the total political party donations received to back his campaigns and parliamentary setup break down into distinct completed yearly records, funded entirely by individual supporters rather than corporate entities or private business backers:

- **2024–2025 Financial Year:** Total political party donations reached £14,500 across verified single individual injections to his campaign, running alongside a grassroots election crowdfunder which generated an additional £10,250 made up of small individual donations from hundreds of members of the general public.
- **2025–2026 Financial Year:** Total parliamentary travel and international deployment support entries came to £1,548, compiled across three individual entries to back external diplomatic and defensive research missions. No major central or localised individual political party donations were filed on the public register for general campaigning during this period.

### Constituency Budgets and Government Allowances

Unlike personal salary packets, statutory parliamentary allowances are capped budgets provided solely to operate constituency networks and run local offices. The financial records track these resources across distinct completed terms:

- **2024–2025 Financial Year:** Initial operational entries logged during the post-election transition included standard office setup costs, drawn from an overall £237,630 IPSA office budget allocated for the 2024–2025 period. This included an initial registered claim of £720 for specialised bought-in services to establish his constituency workplace.
- **2025–2026 Financial Year:** Total parliamentary spending logs administered by IPSA reached a total of £217,333. The ledger tracks ongoing outlays for staff payroll, office rent, utilities, and strict constituency travel allocations, all capped under modern parameters to prevent legacy expense inflation.


**An IPSA regulatory compliance guidelines framework note states:**

IPSA reimburses Members of Parliament for staffing, office, accommodation, and travel costs. These essential business costs are entirely separate from the standard MP salary and remain subject to intense public scrutiny under strict rules established to ensure absolute transparency.


### Benefits in Kind and Civic Hospitality

Under Category 3 of the parliamentary rulebook, MPs must declare all local hospitality, perks, and gifts that could be perceived to influence their thematic focus. The verified ledger confirms two distinct benefits-in-kind registered locally and nationally:

- **2024 (September):** An honorary annual membership to The Harrogate Club on Victoria Avenue, running from 18 September 2024 to 1 January 2029. The private members’ club perk is valued at £600 per annum, alongside a waived one-off £60 joining fee, totaling a benefit value of £660.
- **2026 (April):** On-site start and finish line hospitality at the London Marathon on 26 April 2026, provided by London Marathon Events Ltd and valued at an estimated £350.

### Parliamentary Office Response

Responding directly to the financial disclosures, a spokesperson from the MP’s parliamentary office clarified the operational framework governing the public accounts, stressing that standard allowances are strictly bound by universal Westminster structures.


**A spokesperson for Tom Gordon’s parliamentary office stated:**

The IPSA budgets are public funds supplied directly by parliament to run the constituency office.

All Members of Parliament are allocated the exact same budget framework, and our office always seeks to secure the absolute best value for money for the taxpayer within that budget footprint.

Regarding the membership of the Harrogate Club, Tom accepted this as has been the long-standing tradition for the elected MP for the town.

All civic hospitality received throughout his tenure has been properly and transparently registered within the strict parliamentary gifts and hospitality code.
