Police are investigating after customers allegedly left without paying at a York restaurant.

It happened at Ate O’Clock on High Ousgate on Friday, 4 September at about 6.45pm.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It was reported that a customer/customer group consumed food and drink before leaving the premises without making payment.

“We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident.

“If you recognise the persons involved or have any information that may help identify them, please get in touch.”
