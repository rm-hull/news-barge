One motorcyclist has been taken to hospital after a crash with a car in Darlington.

Emergency services were called to the junction of Haughton Green and Haughton Road just before 6.46am on Thursday (September 17).

The motorcyclist was taken to hospital with what are believed to be “minor injuries”.

A spokesperson from Durham Police said: “We were called to reports of a road traffic collision involving a car and a motorbike just before 6.45am this morning.

“The motorcyclist was taken to hospital with what are believed to be minor injuries to his back.

“No one else was injured.”

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: “We received a call at 6.39am on Thursday, September 17 to reports of a road traffic incident on the junction of Haughton Green and Haughton Road in Darlington.

“We dispatched an ambulance crew to the scene and transported one patient to Darlington Memorial Hospital for further treatment.”
