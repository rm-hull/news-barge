A NEW police station has officially opened its doors.

The facility in Huddersfield is now welcoming the public with a fully operational enquiry counter, making it the second new police building to open in Kirklees this year.

Located just off Broadway, the two-storey station will serve as the base for several key teams, including the Huddersfield Neighbourhood Policing Team, Huddersfield South Patrol officers, and the Huddersfield Proactive Town Centre Team.

Residents can now report crimes in person at the helpdesk, which is open Monday to Friday.

Superintendent Warren Pittman of Kirklees Police said: "The opening of the new Huddersfield police station marks a second and final milestone in the force’s long running programme to renew our estate in Kirklees.

"It is no secret that the old police stations in Huddersfield and Dewsbury had reached the end of their lives and I think it is brilliant for communities that we've been able to invest in new buildings for them as part of wider regeneration in both towns.

"We always intended to maintain a full neighbourhood and response policing patrol presence in both towns and this new building gives us the opportunity to do exactly that in Huddersfield.

"Aside from housing the local teams who serve them, the new building also features a full public encounter for residents to attend and report incidents to their officers directly.

"I hope the new development, and the continued presence of police buildings in Holmfirth and Heckmondwike, as well as the new DHQ in Dewsbury, will reassure residents that we remain absolutely steadfast in our commitment to keep policing in the heart of communities."

The opening follows the launch of the new Kirklees District Headquarters (DHQ) in Dewsbury in early July.

Built on the former Kirklees College site, the multi-million pound facility retains the historic 19th-century Oldroyd building and includes a public helpdesk, local policing teams, and CID officers.
