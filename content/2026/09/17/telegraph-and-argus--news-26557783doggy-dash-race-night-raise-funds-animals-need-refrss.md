AN evening of friendly competition is set to raise vital funds for animals in need.

The first-ever Doggy Dash race night, organised by the RSPCA Halifax, Huddersfield & Bradford Branch, will take place on Friday, September 25.

Instead of real dogs, the event will feature wooden 'pooches' racing towards their 'forever home.'

A spokesperson for the RSPCA Halifax, Huddersfield & Bradford Branch said: "All proceeds from the event will go directly toward the branch's Really Big Refurb project and help support the care of local animals currently residing at the shelter, such as Marley (pictured) who is currently available for rehoming."

The event will be hosted by the Huddersfield Lions Club at the Lindley Liberal Club in Huddersfield, following a visit to the charity’s animal centre in Halifax and a £200 donation to kickstart the fundraiser.

Tickets are £8 per person and include a pie and peas supper.

Attendees can enjoy race night totes and cash prizes, a children's race, raffle, tombolas, and other opportunities to win throughout the evening.

A fully stocked bar and refreshments will also be available.

The RSPCA Halifax, Huddersfield & Bradford Branch is a self-funded charity focused on rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming animals across the region.

So far this year, the branch has rehomed more than 280 animals.
