A BURST water main has forced the closure of a road while repair work is carried out.

The closure is on Saltaire Road in Bradford at the junction with Bingley Road (BD18 4SB), where Yorkshire Water is working to repair the clean water main.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Water said: "We’re currently on site attempting to locate and repair a burst main as quickly as we can.

"We apologise for the inconvenience and thank everyone for their patience.

  The closure is on Saltaire Road in Bradford at the junction with Bingley Road 

*(Image: Supplied)*

"We’ll reopen the road as soon as it is safe to do so."

A signed diversion is in place via Otley Road.

Drivers are advised to allow extra time for their journeys.
