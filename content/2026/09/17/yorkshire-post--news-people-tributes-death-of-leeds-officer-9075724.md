# Police federation pays tribute after unexpected death of officer in Yorkshire

## Tributes have been paid following the unexpected death of a Leeds police officer.

PC Dominic Clegg, who served with West Yorkshire Police across Bradford and Leeds before moving to a role in learning and organisational development at its headquarters at Wakefield's Carr Gate, was honoured by the West Yorkshire Police Federation (WYPF).

Posting to Facebook, the police federation said they were “devastated” to learn of PC Clegg’s unexpected death.

![The police federation said they were “devastated” to learn of PC Dominic Clegg’s unexpected death.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/17/8/24/807284877_1518161403674777_725311068752797104_n.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



“He was a well-respected colleague, and our thoughts are with all his family, friends, and colleagues,” the post said.

“Anyone who is affected by PC Clegg’s passing is encouraged to speak with their line managers, colleagues, local Federation Reps, or contact one of the various support networks available on the force intranet.”

Funeral arrangements are yet to be confirmed but will be confirmed on the West Yorkshire Police’s website once known.

The West Yorkshire Grief and Loss Support Service offers practical advice and emotional support for anyone struggling with feelings of loss. Available from 8am to 8pm, 7 days a week, including all bank holidays, on 0808 1963833. Or visit the online chat.
