Bramham Park will host its first Christmas market this November, with more than 100 artisan traders expected across the two-day event.

The market will take place on Saturday 14th and Sunday 15th November, with independent makers selling gifts and decorations in front of the house. Local food stands will be based in the stable yard.

The Yorkshire Rock Choir will perform carols outside the house from 12pm to 2pm on the Saturday.

Entry to the market is free. Parking costs £10 per car when booked by 31st October, rising to £15 from 1st November.

James Hamilton-Goddard, Commercial Director at Bramham Park, said:

“We are looking forward to welcoming everyone to The Beginning of Christmas at Bramham Park.

"We are planning a great day out of festive cheer for all the family, filled with a variety of trade stands, music and food.

"Keep following us on social media as there are more announcements to come.”


Bramham Park will also launch Christmas house tours in December. Visitors will be able to look inside the private historic home, which is traditionally decorated with a 25-foot Christmas tree in the hall, before having tea, coffee and mince pies in the Old Kitchen.

Tour dates and prices have yet to be announced.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aabff4e6f58c4df6e7.jpg)


The estate, best known as the home of Leeds Festival and the Bramham Park International Horse Trials, says the events are part of plans to welcome more visitors.

*Early bird booking for the car park at £10 per car until 31st October. 1st November onward £15 per car. Entry to the market is free.  Visit www.Bramhampark.co.uk to find out more.*
