Three people have been arrested after police recovered cannabis and cocaine during a series of drug raids in Teesside.

Officers raided properties on Thornaby Road, Havilland Road and Rainton Drive in Thornaby on Wednesday (September 16).

Police seized a large amount of cannabis at a Rainton Drive property.

A 50-year-old woman and an 18-year-old man were arrested, both on suspicion of possession with intent to supply.

Both suspects were released on conditional bail as enquiries continue.

At a separate property on Thornaby Road, officers recovered around £4,400 worth of cocaine.

A 45-year-old woman was arrested and later charged with possessing cocaine with intent to supply.

The raids are part of Cleveland Police’s ongoing fight against serious and organised crime in the region.

Temporary Superintendent Deb Fenny, head of Cleveland Police's Major, Serious and Organised Crime team, said: "This is part of our continued disruption of serious and organised crime after acting on information that was provided to police.

"I want to take this opportunity to thank everyone who keeps providing information so we can keep taking action and continue to remove drugs and organised crime within Cleveland."

Police are continuing their investigations and have urged anyone with information about suspected drug activity in the area to come forward.

Information can be reported via Cleveland Police's website or by calling 101.

Anyone wishing to remain anonymous can contact Crimestoppers online or by calling 0800 555 111.
