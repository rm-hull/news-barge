**RIPON**— The Harrogate Autumn Flower Show officially opens tomorrow at Newby Hall & Gardens, marking a major milestone as the flagship horticultural event celebrates its 50th anniversary milestone.

Running from 18 to 20 September 2026, the three-day event stands as one of the most significant autumn tourism and commercial drivers for the Ripon and Harrogate district. Organised by the North of England Horticultural Society (NEHS), the show has anchored the region’s seasonal event calendar since its inception in 1976.

To mark the golden anniversary, a spectacular harvest-inspired floral gown was unveiled during a special press preview within the historic rooms of Newby Hall this morning. Created by renowned floral designer Jonathan Moseley and modeled by Anna Cartmell-Done, the piece serves as the core installation of a seasonal exhibition titled *Grain & Grandeur – The Harvest Ball*.


**Show Director Nick Smith said:** This is a particularly special year for the Harrogate Autumn Flower Show as we celebrate 50 years since the first show in 1976. Jonathan’s Grain & Grandeur installation is a wonderful way to celebrate the season and the creativity at the heart of the show. The harvest-inspired gown looks fantastic!



The centrepiece pavilion is set to house more than 5,000 seasonal blooms alongside specialist nurseries. Meanwhile, the adjacent Incredible Edible Pavilion will host the highly competitive NEHS Giant Vegetable Competition and the National Onion Championship—a fixture that gained international attention in 2023 when a world-record 8.97kg onion was successfully placed on the scales.

Admission gate fees have been confirmed at £29 for adults, which includes full access to the historic hall’s award-winning gardens. Children under 16 and general event parking remain free across the weekend as organisers push to maintain strong regional family attendance amid rising event production costs.
