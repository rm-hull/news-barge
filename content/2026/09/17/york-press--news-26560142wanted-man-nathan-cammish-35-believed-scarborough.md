A man wanted for serious assault and breaching a domestic violence protection order is being sought by police.

Nathan Cammish, 35, is believed to be in the Scarborough area and is the subject of an ongoing public appeal from North Yorkshire Police.

Anyone who sees Cammish is urged to dial 999 immediately.

  **Recommended reads:**

He is described as white, around 5ft 10in (178cm) tall, with dark brown hair and blue eyes.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Extensive enquiries have been carried out to locate him, and we are now asking for the public’s help to find him.

"We believe he may be in the Scarborough area, where he has known links."

Anyone who has seen Mr Cammish or knows of his whereabouts is urged to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260179870.

Information can also be shared anonymously through Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or visiting their website.
