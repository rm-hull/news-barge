WALKERS joined forces with emergency services to help a walker with a 'badly broken ankle' in Malham.

The 45-year-old man was on the remote footpath above Gordale Scar heading towards Malham Tarn on Sunday morning when he suffered a lower leg injury, said Cave Rescue Organisation.

A CRO team was dispatched, together with two vehicles.

A spokesperson said: "The vehicles met up with an ambulance service double crewed ambulance and also one of our colleagues from North Yorkshire Police.

"We then transported the ambulance paramedics off-road to the casualty site where they commenced their treatment of the casualty by providing pain relief before reducing and splinting what was a pretty badly broken ankle.

"After the initial treatment was completed, CRO team members packaged the casualty and transferred him into a vehicle in order to get him back onto the road head and the awaiting road ambulance for further treatment before being transported onwards to hospital."

The spokesperson added: "This was a great example of multi- agency working, not only between CRO, ambulance service and police, but also the fellow walkers who stopped and assisted the casualty before we were on scene, they genuinely did make someone’s bad day a little bit better – thank you.

"As always, we wish the casualty our best, and hope to see him back on the hill at some point soon."
