BONGO'S Bingo is set to make its Bradford debut this autumn.

The bingo-rave phenomenon will launch at Bradford Live, a new entertainment venue in the heart of the city, on Friday, November 20.

Jonny Bongo, co-founder of Bongo’s Bingo, said: "We’re all absolutely buzzing to launch in Bradford in November.

"Our debut date at Bradford Live is not to be missed and we’ve heard great things about how much they love to party.

"It’s been a long time coming and we’re ready to unleash Bongo’s Bingo in this amazing venue.

"We are going to bring maximum bingo vibes for everyone to Bradford and it’s going to be absolutely class."

The event transforms traditional bingo into a high-energy night out, complete with rave rounds, dance-offs, confetti, and unusual prizes such as Henry hoovers, giant pink unicorns, and mobility scooters.

Bongo’s Bingo began in Liverpool in 2015 as a quirky idea between two friends and has since grown into a global sensation.

More than six million tickets have been sold to date.

It now takes place in over 50 locations across the UK.

Bradford Live, located in the refurbished former Odeon building, is one of the city’s newest entertainment venues.

Tickets and further information are available at bongosbingo.co.uk.
