Gisborough Hall Country House Hotel has unveiled a first look at its luxurious new wellness destination.

Sakura Spa has been designed to immerse guests in the natural beauty of the surrounding North Yorkshire landscape.

Named after the Japanese word for cherry blossom, Sakura symbolises renewal, hope and new beginnings.

   

*(Image: Michael Lazenby)*

Inside, the spa's calming interiors reflect Japanese-inspired design, with a soothing palette of soft, muted tones complemented by tactile textures, natural stone and warm wood finishes.

Its signature cherry blossom scent gently fills the space, immersing guests in a tranquil sensory experience from the moment they arrive.

   

*(Image: Michael Lazenby)*

The spa's outdoor thermal garden boasts sweeping views across the surrounding countryside and woodland, inviting guests to immerse themselves in nature while enjoying a carefully curated collection of thermal facilities.

Guests can move between the vitality pool, herbal sauna, Finnish sauna, salt steam room and invigorating cold plunge.

   

*(Image: Michael Lazenby)*

The spa features six luxurious treatment rooms, including a dedicated couples' suite and a Sakura Head Spa, designed to deliver deeply restorative treatments in a peaceful setting.

   

*(Image: Michael Lazenby)*

Guests can also experience Welnamis, an innovative non-touch therapy designed to calm the nervous system and promote deep relaxation through immersive sound and gentle vibrations.

   

*(Image: Michael Lazenby)*

Gisborough has partnered with two of the industry's leading luxury spa brands, Bamford and Elemis, bringing an exceptional collection of face and body treatments to the new spa.

Completing the wellness journey is Koyo, the spa's signature dining experience.

   

*(Image: Michael Lazenby)*

Named after the Japanese word for the vibrant colours of autumn leaves, Koyo draws inspiration from the changing seasons and the Japanese tradition of appreciating nature's beauty.

The menu has been carefully curated to complement the spa experience, celebrating fresh, nourishing ingredients that promote balance and wellbeing.

   

*(Image: Michael Lazenby)*

Spa director Vicki Jackson says: 'We're incredibly proud to welcome guests to Sakura Spa and unveil a wellness destination that has been designed to help people truly switch off and reconnect with themselves.

   

*(Image: Michael Lazenby)*

'Every attention to detail has been inspired by nature, from the calming woodland setting to the carefully curated thermal experiences and treatment offering, creating a space that encourages rest, renewal and balance.'

   

*(Image: Michael Lazenby)*

For those looking to make wellness part of their lifestyle, The Sakura Spa Club offers an exclusive membership with one spa visit each month, preferential member rates, priority booking, early access to events and a range of benefits across the hotel.

Guests can experience the new Sakura Spa with a range of spa day and spa break packages, including the Sakura Spa Day, available from £185 per person.
