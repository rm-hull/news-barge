TWO vehicles have crashed in a North Yorkshire town and police have been called in to investigate.

  


North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called at 6.13pm yesterday evening (Wednesday, September 16) to the incident in Malton.

Firefighters were able to move both vehicles to a safe location and disconnected batteries.

A service spokesperson said: "All persons were out on arrival of crews and did not require any medical attention.

"The incident was left with both vehicle owners and the police were informed."
