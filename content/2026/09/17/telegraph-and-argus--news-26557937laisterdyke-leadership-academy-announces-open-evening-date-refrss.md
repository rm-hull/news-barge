AN academy will open its doors to prospective pupils and their families during a Year 7 open evening.

Laisterdyke Leadership Academy in Bradford will welcome visitors on Wednesday, September 23, from 4pm to 6pm at its Thornbury Road site.

The event offers prospective students and their families the chance to meet pupils and staff, explore the school’s facilities, and learn about the academic, co-curricular, and pastoral support available.

A spokesperson for the school said: "From the classroom to activities beyond the curriculum, the evening will showcase the opportunities available to pupils across their time at the school."

Visitors can tour the academy’s specialised learning spaces and sports amenities, and gain a better understanding of daily life at Laisterdyke.

The academy is part of the Star Academies trust, which supports schools across the UK in delivering strong educational outcomes.

The admissions deadline for Year 7 places in September 2027 is October 31, 2026.
