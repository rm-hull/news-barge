A woman will appear in court today (Thursday) charged with outraging public decency.

The 38-year-old Scarborough woman has also been charged with exposure and a sexual offence following an incident in Scarborough town centre on Monday.

She has been remanded to appear before York Magistrates’ Court today at 9.15am.

A 31-year-old Scarborough man was also arrested and has since been bailed with strict conditions.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We would like to thank the public for sharing our appeal and for assisting with providing information to help the investigation.”
