A CONVERTED double-decker bus is opening up new worlds of imagination.

Parked on the playground at Whitley & Eggborough Community Primary School, the 13-metre York Pullman bus has been transformed into a dedicated reading space thanks to a community-led fundraising campaign.

The project received a £10,000 grant from developer The Banks Group, alongside support from families and teachers.

Diane Steward, school business manager, said: "The reading bus offers our pupils so many different things, from a quiet place to spend time reading to extra space for their learning and creative activities."

*(Image: Supplied)*

Specialist contractors Unconventional Design carried out the conversion, with pupils contributing ideas for the interior.

The bus now serves as a vibrant hub for reading, learning and creativity.

Jamilah Hassan, community relations manager at The Banks Group, said: "This is an absolutely amazing project which links in perfectly with the current National Year For Reading and it will benefit generations of local children."

The Banks Group is also behind proposals for a major development west of Eggborough, which will deliver up to 1,500 new homes, enhanced local transport links, assisted living units, new areas of accessible public open space, other local services and a range of environmental benefits.

The company has worked with the Eggborough community for more than five years, providing around £25,000 in grant funding to schools, charities and community groups.
