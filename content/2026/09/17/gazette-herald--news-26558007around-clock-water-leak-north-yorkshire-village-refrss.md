Yorkshire Water has been urged to 'get its act together' following concerns over a prolonged leak.

When the local residents of a Ryedale village reported that water was flowing from a road cover, they expected Yorkshire Water to promptly fix the leak, not least because of the dry weather that the region had experienced recently.

Despite multiple contacts and reminders from members of the community in Oswaldkirk, a significant amount of water was overflowing around the clock for several weeks.

Mike Aherne, who lives on The Terrace next to the leak, first reported the issue on August 21. Yet, the constant stream of water flooding from the Yorkshire Water cover had not been stopped three weeks later.

Oswaldkirk’s representative on North Yorkshire Council, Councillor George Jabbour, said: “I am truly disappointed that an enormous quantity of water was lost. It is completely unacceptable for Yorkshire Water to have neglected dealing with this pressing problem.

“Having contacted Yorkshire Water last week, I understand that the matter is now being addressed.

“Yorkshire Water must get its act together. It should not take such a long period of time to stop wasting water.”

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson said: “We recently completed a repair to a leak in Oswaldkirk, though we understand that the length of time taken to do so will have been frustrating for residents, and we appreciate the feedback from Cllr George Jabbour. Our leakage team works 24/7, prioritising the leaks that are losing the most water.

“We’re committed to reducing the amount of leakage across the region - we’re over a quarter of the way through a £406m mains replacement scheme to renew 1,000km of clean water pipework, making leakage less likely. We’re also delivering a £38m leakage reduction plan, in which we want to reduce leaks by 12.7% by 2030.”
