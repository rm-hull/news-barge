Rail passengers travelling between Leeds, York and Selby face disruption today (Thursday).

National Rail said this morning: “An operational incident between Leeds and Micklefield means that some lines will be blocked.

“As a result, trains running between Leeds and York / Selby in this direction will be cancelled between 8.30am and 3.30pm today.

“Disruption is expected until 5.30pm.”

TransPennine Express said: “Following an operational incident yesterday between Leeds and Cross Gates, the line towards Selby, will be blocked between 8.30am and 5.30pm on Thursday 17th September.

“The line between Selby and Leeds will be unaffected during these times and services can run towards Leeds as planned.

“With the line being closed between these times, TransPennine Express services between Leeds and Selby will divert via other lines.”
