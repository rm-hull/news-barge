**HARROGATE**— Following a completely sold-out two-night debut earlier this year, Harrogate Theatre Choir has announced an ambitious, expanded sequel to its flagship production, set to take over the historic surroundings of The Crown Hotel next month.Scheduled for

**Sunday, 18 October 2026**, the performance—titled

*Love Stories*—marks the culmination of a year-long rehearsal and writing process. While the show’s initial run in February 2026 was strictly limited by the physical stage dimensions of the Harrogate Theatre Studio, this autumn configuration will utilise a full cast and an expanded line-up of vocalists to scale up the production’s sonic footprint.


![Harrogate Theatre Choir steps up scale for ‘Love Stories’](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/20260214_205303910_iOS.webp)



**Harrogate Theatre Choir’s Musical Director stated:** The show explores the fascinating psychology of love through the greatest love songs from one hundred years of stage and screen. From the passion of first romance to the angst of one-sided love and the pathos of a love that fades, the performance aims to bypass social conditioning and directly connect with your emotions.



The centennial repertoire draws heavily from the work of legendary Hollywood, Broadway, and West End composers, including Stephen Sondheim, Leonard Bernstein, Cole Porter, Irving Berlin, and Andrew Lloyd Webber. The program bridges classic and modern stage narratives, featuring numbers from blockbusters such as *West Side Story*,* Moulin Rouge*,* A Star is Born*,* Rent*, and* Evita*.

Vocal arrangements locked in for the evening span definitive arrangements including *My Funny Valentine*,* Shallow*,* One Hand One Heart*, and* Unexpected Song*.
