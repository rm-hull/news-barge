Police in York are investigating a serious sexual assault that took place last night (Wednesday).

It was in the alleyway linking Spurriergate and Church Lane.

That was cordoned off last night and remains behind tape today.

#### Incident location

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-police-incident-spurriergate-church-lane-alleway-17-sept-2026-8.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-police-incident-spurriergate-church-lane-alleway-17-sept-2026-7.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-police-incident-spurriergate-church-lane-alleway-17-sept-2026-2.webp)

The area around the Yates York beer garden is taped off and there’s a police van parked close to the Church Lane shops.

The Spurriergate entrance, between the iRepair phone shop and the Select Apple store, is guarded by a second police van.

A 22-year-old man has been arrested and is currently in police custody.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “An investigation is underway, and a scene remains in place.

“The victim is being supported by specialist officers.”

Spurriergate, Low Ousegate and Coney Street are open as usual, as is Yates York.
