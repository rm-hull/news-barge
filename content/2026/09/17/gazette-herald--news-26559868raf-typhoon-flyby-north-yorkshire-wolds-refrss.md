A MILITARY aircraft has just been heard, but perhaps not seen, circling over the Yorkshire Wolds and the reverberations were certainly felt in York.

According to Flightradar24 - the live flight-tracking service, an RAF Eurofighter Typhoon T3 has been circling over an area near Middleton-on-the-Wolds and flying directly over Pocklington.

  


The service, which says it uses aircraft transponder data and other sources to display the real-time location, route, altitude and speed of aircraft around the works had the two-seater training plane, out of RAF Coningsby in Lincolnshire, reaching a ground speed of around 450 knots on its flight today and a height of close to 20,000 feet.

The flight today so far has taken it as far north as Helmsley.

The RAF Typhoon T3 is the two-seat training version of the Eurofighter Typhoon, one of the Royal Air Force’s most advanced combat aircraft.
