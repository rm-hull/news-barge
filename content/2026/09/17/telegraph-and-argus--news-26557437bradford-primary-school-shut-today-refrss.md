A BRADFORD school has been forced to shut for the day.

Newby Primary School, on Ryan Street, West Bowling, issued an announcement on Bradford Council's Emergency School Closures list.

The school is closed all day on Thursday.

## Why is the school shut?

It is due to planned water supply works by Yorkshire Water.

The utilities company turned off the water supply at 9am this morning.

The school is expected to reopen tomorrow.

A spokesperson for the school said: "Yorkshire Water have informed us that they will be turning off the water supply to school at 9am.

"The planned works should only last for one day so school will re-open on Friday.

"We apologise for the inconvenience this may cause."

Home learning has been provided for pupils.
