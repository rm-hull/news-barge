Kate Mosse, Dame Prue Leith and Julian Clary are among the authors and broadcasters coming to Harrogate for this year’s Raworths Literature Festival.

The festival runs from 11 to 25 October, with events at The Crown Hotel and Cedar Court Hotel. Its newly announced programme spans fiction, memoir, politics, gardening and crime writing.

Mosse will discuss her new book *Autumn*, while Dame Prue Leith will share her thoughts on ageing and finding joy in later life. Clary will talk about his murder mystery* The Midmorning Murders*.

The line-up also includes Booker Prize winner Sir Ben Okri, *The Tattooist of Auschwitz* author Heather Morris and Sir Alexander McCall Smith. Broadcaster Sophie Raworth will discuss her memoir*Running on Air* at the Yorkshire Life Literary Lunch on 22 October.

Rachel Tunnicliffe, Senior Partner at Raworths Solicitors, said:

“This is our 14th year sponsoring this fantastic Festival which brings so many incredible storytellers and remarkable stories to our town.

"This year’s brilliant line-up features famous writers, rising stars and thought-provoking panel events, and we’re proud to be involved with such a world class literary celebration.”


Crime writer Vaseem Khan will open the programme with a Book and Brunch event at Cedar Court Hotel on 11 October.

A panel marking 50 years since Agatha Christie’s death will explore her work and the story of her famous disappearance, which brought her to Harrogate a century ago.

Other speakers include political analyst Sir John Curtice, former Supreme Court Justice Lord Jonathan Sumption, broadcaster John Sergeant, journalist Samira Ahmed and ITV political editor Paul Brand. Garden writer Jonny Hincks and running author Alison Stankard are also on the programme.

Sharon Canavar, Chief Executive of Harrogate International Festivals, said:

“The Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival 2026 really does have something for every kind of reader – from cosy crime and bestselling fiction to inspiring memoirs and a book about The Beatles.

“This year’s diverse programme also brings together a real mix of national treasures and exciting new names – and we can’t wait to welcome them all to Harrogate!”


*Tickets are available through Harrogate International Festivals or by calling the box office on 01423 562 303.*
