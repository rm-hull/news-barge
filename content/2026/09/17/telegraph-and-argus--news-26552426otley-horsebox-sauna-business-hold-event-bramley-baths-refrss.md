The Yorkshire couple who turned a horsebox into a wood-fired sauna have settled into a new Otley location and are preparing to take their business on the road for a special event at Bramley Baths.

Millie Sparrow and Matt Lister, founders of äyup sauna, officially relocated the business to a new base near Chevin End Guest House just weeks ago.

The move comes months after the couple's unusual business story captured attention, having launched the venture after just two months of dating.

## 
  **New home on the edge of Otley Chevin**

The sauna is now based in a rural setting close to the Chevin Inn, offering visitors scenic views across the surrounding countryside.

Ms Sparrow said the new site offered greater flexibility as the business continues to grow.

"We've actually been here for about two weeks now and it's a really lovely location," she said. "We've got an even better view where we are now and it feels like the perfect place for us."

The site has also gained some unexpected neighbours.

Ms Sparrow said: "When we first arrived there weren't any alpacas, but now there are three lovely alpacas that have joined the field.

"They've become more and more inquisitive by the day and everyone seems to be loving them."

## 
  **Business continues to grow**

The move follows a successful first few months for the business, which officially launched earlier this year after the pair spent eight months transforming a horse trailer into a fully functioning sauna.

Ms Sparrow said customers had continued to support the venture during the relocation.

"When we left our previous site, we had lots of people messaging asking where we were and whether we would be reopening soon," she said. "It kept us positive while we searched for the right location.

She added: "We've met some really lovely people since moving here, both customers and people who have simply spotted us while walking along the nearby footpaths."

## 
  **Taking the sauna on the road**

The couple are now preparing to take the sauna to Bramley Baths for a two-day event on September 19 and 20.

The pop-up sauna forms part of the historic Leeds venue's More Than a Pool project, which is exploring opportunities to expand its wellbeing offering.

Ms Sparrow said Bramley Baths had a longstanding connection with sauna culture.

"The baths have a really long history of sauna being part of the facilities there," she said. "Their own sauna isn't currently operating, so they're looking at the demand and seeing whether there's an appetite for bringing something like that back in the future."

The horsebox sauna will be installed outside the baths throughout the weekend, allowing visitors to experience the service for themselves.

## 
  **A unique offering**

While mobile saunas have become increasingly popular across the UK, Ms Sparrow believes äyup sauna stands out because the entire horsebox can be transported to events.

She explained: "A lot of sauna businesses use temporary pop-up structures for events, but we're able to take the actual horsebox sauna with us, which makes it quite unique and means it can be booked for all sorts of occasions."

The couple have already provided sauna experiences at gym events and private celebrations, including birthday parties.

"It's lovely hearing people laughing and chatting inside," Ms Sparrow laughed."Seeing people relax and enjoy themselves is probably the best part of what we do."

## 
  **Looking to the future**

Alongside regular sauna sessions at their new Otley base, the business is increasing its focus on events and partnerships.

Ms Sparrow hopes the Bramley Baths weekend will introduce even more people to the benefits of sauna experiences.

She said. "Not many people know it's happening yet, so we're hoping to spread the word and encourage people to come along and try something a little different."

The äyup sauna pop-up will take place at Bramley Baths on September 19 and 20 as part of the venue's More Than a Pool programme.
