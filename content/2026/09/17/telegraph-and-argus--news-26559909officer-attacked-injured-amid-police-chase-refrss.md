AN officer was assaulted amid a police chase across Manchester and West Yorkshire.

The officer was injured after approaching a blue Volkswagen Golf on King Cross Road, Halifax, at 11.36pm on Wednesday night, which had failed to stop earlier in the Greater Manchester area.

The driver attacked the officer before fleeing in the vehicle.

Roads policing officers began a pursuit, but it was called off after they lost sight of the car.

The vehicle was later found abandoned in the Crosland Moor area of Huddersfield.

West Yorkshire Police are continuing to investigate the incident alongside Greater Manchester Police.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At 11.36pm last night, information was received of a potential sighting of a blue Golf in the Halifax area which was reported to have been involved in a fail to stop incident in the Greater Manchester Police area.

"The driver parked up on King Cross Road, Halifax, and when officers have gone to engage with him, he has assaulted one of the officers causing minor injuries before getting back in the vehicle and driving off.

"The vehicle was pursued by roads policing officers, but the pursuit was called off after officers lost sight of the vehicle.

"It was later found abandoned in the Crosland Moor area of Huddersfield.

"Enquiries are ongoing alongside colleagues from Greater Manchester Police."
