A hand-painted sculpture inspired by the famous Sycamore Gap tree is going under the hammer in the North East, three years after the landmark was felled.

Whitley Bay artist Darren Mundy created The Dream, one of 96 sculptures on the Peter Rabbit: Tales on the Tyne trail celebrating St Oswald’s Hospice’s 40th anniversary and Beatrix Potter’s 160th birthday.

Mr Mundy said: "It just generates such emotion among people.

"And, like everyone, I felt utter disbelief when it was hacked down."

  Darren and the Dream 

*(Image: Darren Mundy)*

"The beauty of it was always that it was unfenced and unprotected and just stood where it had grown.

"So many people had such a personal attachment to it that when it was felled, we felt a genuine loss."

The sculpture is painted in acrylics and is fully weatherproof.

Mr Mundy said: "Because it’s a rabbit, decorated with a painting of one of the most famous trees in Britain, I can see it in a garden, I suppose."

  A little artistic licence was used to create this pic of Darren and The Dream at Sycamore Gap 

*(Image: Darren Mundy)*

"But I know that whoever buys it – and wherever they put it – it will look beautiful and serve as a reminder of a very special place indeed."

The Sycamore Gap tree, a Northumberland landmark and regional symbol, was felled in September 2023 in an act of vandalism that shocked the nation.

The sculpture is one of 40 unique pieces being auctioned on Tuesday, October 13, at Newcastle Civic Centre, with advance bidding already open.

St Oswald’s Hospice chief executive Steph Edusei said: "The Dream is not only exquisitely painted by Darren, but its theme – our much loved and greatly missed Sycamore Gap tree – is likely to touch a lot of hearts."

The Peter Rabbit: Tales on the Tyne trail was organised with Wild in Art, The World of Peter Rabbit, Newcastle City Council, North Tyneside Council, Northumbrian Water and North Standard.

A celebration weekend will be held on October 10 and 11, giving the public a final chance to see all the sculptures before the auction.
