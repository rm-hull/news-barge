A MULTI-MILLION pound expansion could soon deliver tens of thousands of extra diagnostic tests for patients each year.

Plans have been submitted to Leeds City Council for the £20 million upgrade of the Community Diagnostic Centre (CDC) at Seacroft Hospital, which is funded through national investment.

The expanded centre will include two MRI scanners, additional treatment rooms, and extended phlebotomy services.

Craige Richardson, director of estates and facilities at Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust, said: "This is a great step forward as we aim to provide tens of thousands of extra diagnostic tests each year for patients in Leeds.

"The extended centre will help patients get faster treatment for conditions such as cancer, heart and lung disease.

"It will also bring care closer to patients’ homes, making tests more accessible for local people."

  The proposed expanded centre 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Construction is expected to begin in early 2027, with the new facilities set to open in spring or summer 2028.

The current CDC will remain operational throughout the build.

Opened in 2024, the Seacroft CDC already offers a range of tests, including blood tests, ultrasounds, sleep studies, and self-request chest X-rays.

It is one of three CDCs in Leeds, alongside Armley and Beeston, designed to improve access to diagnostic services closer to home.
