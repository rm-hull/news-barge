A new documentary offering a fresh perspective on Palestinian stories will be screened in West Yorkshire this month.

The film will be shown at Hyde Park Picture House in Leeds on Tuesday, September 22, at 6pm.

### Comedy and identity at the heart of documentary

The documentary follows six comedians from across Palestine as they develop and perform a comedy show exploring identity through humour.

Directed by Palestinian filmmaker Alaa Aliabdallah and produced by Charlotte Knowles, the film blends observational storytelling with live comedy.

### Award-winning film recognised at international festivals

It has already appeared at major festivals including SXSW London, Sheffield DocFest, Raindance, and IDFA, earning the Raindance Spirit Award.

The screening comes ahead of the 12th edition of the Leeds Palestinian Film Festival, which begins on November 13.

Organisers have promised a full programme of Palestinian cinema, filmmaker appearances, and live conversations, with further details to be announced soon.
