*10 highlights of York Food and Drink Festival 2026 – starting tomorrow*

LOOSEN those belts and arrive hungry because York Food and Drink Festival is back on Friday for ten days of feasting.

From September 18 to 27, the city centre will become one enormous foodie playground, bringing together chefs, Yorkshire producers, independent businesses and some more unusual culinary adventures.

The familiar Parliament Street market and street-food stalls return, while a new base at St Sampson’s Centre gives the festival another hub for demonstrations, workshops and evening events.

Here are 10 festival highlights worth getting your teeth into.

  **1. Watch the experts at work**

  Master Patissier Florian Poirot with some of his macarons - he will be hosting a demo at the food festival


Fancy picking up a few tricks from the professionals? Free daily cookery demonstrations will take place in the new theatre at St Sampson’s Centre. The line-up includes MasterChef 2025 personalities Claire Syrenne and Darren Gurry, plus UK Pastry Champion Florian Poirot demonstrating his patisserie skills.

  **2. Bake Off's Freya Cox returns**

Great British Bake Off star Freya Cox takes to the festival stage on Saturday, September 19. Freya, who appeared on the hit Channel 4 show, will be showcasing her vegan cooking and proving that plant-based dishes needn't miss out on indulgence.

  GBBO star Freya Cox will be taking part in the food festival


  **3. Get your hands dirty**

Don't just watch other people cook. The Food Factory at St Sampson’s Centre invites visitors to have a go themselves. The free sessions cover everything from bread and pasta to making butter and ice cream, with extra activities planned during the festival.

  **4. Eat your way around York**

The popular Taste Trails turn sightseeing into snacking. Visitors can follow a self-guided route featuring around 20 stops at York’s independent delis, restaurants, producers and suppliers. There is also a dedicated Vegan Taste Trail offering plant-based samples and alternatives.

  Bustling crowds at the York Food Festival. Image supplied


  **5. Feast like it's the Middle Ages**

Leave 2026 behind for an evening with a Medieval Feast at historic Barley Hall. It promises to combine two things York does exceptionally well: food and history.

  **6. Mix mead with Viking sagas**

One of the more unusual items on the menu is Mead Cocktails and Sagas with Horns of Odin. Viking-inspired storytelling is combined with mead cocktails for an experience considerably livelier than your average drinks tasting.

  York Food and Drink Festival is back for another year of culinary delights Image: York Food Festival


  **7. Taste Yorkshire in a glass**

Yorkshire's drinks makers take centre stage at the Great Yorkshire Tasting at St Sampson’s Centre. Wines, beers and spirits will be showcased, with festival-goers also getting the chance to meet some of the people behind them.

York Cocoa Works will delve into the history of drinking chocolate before introducing something unexpected: a "NOT Chocolate" tasting. The event explores how cocoa pod and plant materials can be used beyond the chocolate bar.

  **9. Try your hand at Syrian cooking**

Yahala Mataam will bring Syrian flavours to the festival with a hands-on cookery session. Participants can learn to make falafel, hummus and baklava before sitting down together to enjoy a mezze-style meal.

  **10. Raise a glass, and some cash**

The historic Assize of Ale returns on Saturday, September 26, beginning at York Mansion House and taking participants around city pubs before finishing at the festival. A fish and chip supper and ale complete proceedings, with proceeds supporting the Lord Mayor’s Charity Fund.

And the entertainment doesn't stop with the food. Live for St Leonard’s brings music to Parliament Street and St Sampson’s Square from September 18 to 26. Organised by York BID, the events have raised nearly £9,000 for St Leonard’s Hospice over the past two years.

The festival's food market will be open daily from 10am to 5pm, alongside bars and evening entertainment.
