Police are appealing for help to identify a trolley and camping equipment believed to have been stolen from a vehicle in the Harrogate area.

The items were recovered following the arrest of a 37-year-old man and a 35-year-old woman on Wednesday (16th September).

The arrests form part of an investigation into theft from a motor vehicle, shop theft, handling stolen goods and other offences.

Both people have been released on conditional bail while enquiries continue.

Police are asking anyone who recognises the items, believes they may own them or has information about the investigation to get in touch.

*PC 312 Helen James can be contacted at [Helen.James@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:Helen.James@northyorkshire.police.uk.) Information can also be provided through the “Tell us about” section of the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting “An existing case or report”, or through its live chat service between 9am and 5pm, Monday to Friday.*

*Anyone unable to use those options can call 101. Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*

*Quote reference 12260180426 when making contact.*
