A MAN has been charged with a series of shoplifting offences at stores in Bradford and Guiseley.

Richard Thompson, 41, of no fixed abode, has been charged with nine shoplifting offences following incidents at the Co-op Food store on Wrose Road, Bradford, and an Asda supermarket in Guiseley.

Thompson has been bailed and was scheduled to appear before Bradford Magistrates' Court today (September 17) in relation to two outstanding court warrants.

The charges follow an earlier police appeal to trace Thompson, who was wanted.

West Yorkshire Police thanked members of the public who shared the appeal and provided information to officers during their enquiries.
