A 62-year-old man from the Pickering area has been jailed for more than 11 years after being found guilty of multiple sexual offences against a woman.

Malcolm Peirson Dale, of Rosedale, was sentenced to 11 years and six months in prison at York Crown Court on September 16.

He was also placed on the sex offenders register indefinitely.

Dale denied all charges but was found guilty by a jury on July 24 of three counts of sexual assault, three counts of assault by penetration, and one count of causing a female to engage in non-penetrative sexual activity.

The offences took place in 2022.

  


Detective Constable Liberty Ashton, of Scarborough CID, said:

"Dale showed no remorse for his offending, denying it throughout and subjecting the victim to a trial.

"However, the jury saw through his lies and he is now facing the consequences.

"I hope the outcome of this case provides the victim with some semblance of closure and that she can now start to move on with her life.

"If you are a victim of sexual abuse, please do not hesitate to come forward and get the support you need.

"If you do not feel able to report it to the police you can contact our Sexual Assault Referral Centre who provide free, confidential healthcare and compassionate support to anyone in York and North Yorkshire that has experienced sexual assault or rape no matter when it happened."

North Yorkshire Police encourage anyone who has experienced sexual abuse or assault, regardless of when it occurred, to report it through their website or by calling 101.

In an emergency, always dial 999.

Victims who do not wish to contact police directly can reach the North Yorkshire Sexual Assault Referral Centre (SARC) on 0330 223 0362 or visit [https://orlo.uk/Zq4e0.](https://orlo.uk/Zq4e0.)

IDAS is an independent charity offering support to victims of sexual violence and abuse, whether or not the incident has been reported to police.

Anyone concerned about a child can contact the NSPCC Helpline on 0808 800 5000.

Adult victims of non-recent sexual abuse can also use this service for support.

Young people can contact Childline on 0800 1111 or visit the Childline website.

Supporting Victims in North Yorkshire can be reached at [https://orlo.uk/Q5Kor](https://orlo.uk/Q5Kor) or by calling 01609 643100.
