A WALKER suffered a suspected broken ankle after slipping on rocks near Victoria Cave, Settle.

The 46-year-old woman was walking on the footpath near the cave yesterday (Tuesday) in the afternoon when she fell.

Cave Rescue volunteers went to her aid and drove her back to Settle after being alerted by the ambulance service.

A spokesperson for Cave Rescue Organisation said: "Team members carried out an assessment of the injuries and treated her for a suspected broken left ankle.

"Pain relief was given and the ankle splinted before being carried by stretcher to the team vehicles. She was driven to Settle and transferred to her partner's vehicle who then took her to hospital."
