A police federation chair has paid tribute to an officer who "brought light and laughter into every room" ahead of his funeral today (Wednesday, September 9).

PC Matthew Blades, 37, and his colleague, Tom Clough, 38, died when a Volkswagen Passat which had been pursued by police collided with their marked armed response vehicle seconds after the pursuit was terminated on August 22.

Five people travelling in the Volkswagen Passat also died at the scene.

Lauren Somerville, Chair of Cleveland Police Federation, has paid tribute to PC Blades ahead of his funeral in Hartlepool today (Wednesday, September 9) .

She said: “Matthew was a best friend, a husband, a father, a brother, a son and his family have said that words aren’t powerful enough to describe how they feel right now.

“He has been described as the heart of the family and he will be remembered beyond words, today, tomorrow and always.

“He would be so proud of all the support that has been shown from the public and from his colleagues to his family.”

She added: “Matt’s family have shown incredible dignity and courage since those awful events in August and it is our honour to play our small part in honouring his memory.

“The support and love we offer them does not end today. We will always be there to take care of them.”

And she said: “His family have said they’re going to miss everything about him more than words can say and his laughter and silly jokes made them smile even when they least expected it.

"He’s been described as bringing light and laughter into every room and now life feels unbearably quiet without him.”

The federation set up a fundraiser for the two families of the police officers in the aftermath of the tragedy, which raised £1,203,932.
