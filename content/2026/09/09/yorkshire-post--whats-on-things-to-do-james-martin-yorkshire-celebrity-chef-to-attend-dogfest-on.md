# James Martin: Yorkshire celebrity chef to attend DogFest on the Farm event at Harewood House

## DogFest on the Farm at Harewood House will feature the Yorkshire chef James Martin and his schedule has been announced.

The organisers of Yorkshire’s dog-friendly event, DogFest on the Farm, presented by Agria Pet Insurance, have unveiled the official running times ahead of the event this September.

A day out designed around dogs, DogFest on the Farm, is a weekend full of displays, socialisation opportunities, and interactive activities at Harewood House to share with your dog alongside local food and Yorkshire makers.

Attendees will be able to catch celebrity chef James Martin sharing his love of food, farming, and the great British outdoors throughout the weekend.

![James Martin. (Pic credit: DogFest)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjg4NmI0MzRkLTNlOWQtNDRlZS04OGNmLThkZGYyYWI3N2E1ZjphZDUxZDE4Yy0xZjU2LTQwN2EtOWIxYS0xMjY3MTM0N2JiNmY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The schedule is as follows:

- 11am: Your chance to ask James Martin for his top foodie tips at the DogFest Plus meet and greet, exclusive to DogFest Plus ticket holders

- 11.30am: James Martin will be kicking-off the Agria Big Dog Walk through the gorgeous grounds of Harewood House

- 12.10pm: Don’t miss James Martin’s cookery demonstration at the Kitchen Theatre

![DogFest on the Farm. (Pic credit: DogFest)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmJhNjg4MmQwLWMxMzUtNDZjYy1hMjYxLWEzOTAxN2E3ODM0YToyMzJiMWFkMC1hYTliLTQwN2YtYTI4OS1hMDBiMzJjM2IzNzc=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



- 1.30pm: James Martin will be hosting the must-see demonstrations at the Agria Display Ring

- 2.30pm: Another chance to catch James Martin sharing his knowledge at the Kitchen Theatre

The event will take place at Harewood House on September 12 and 13, 2026.

DogFest on the Farm ticket holders can also access Harewood House at a reduced entry fee of just £5 per person on the day of their visit.

Tickets start from just £27.50 per person, which includes festival entry and access to all have-a-go activities as well as an official commemorative photograph.

Attendees can also upgrade to DogFest Plus to receive fast-track entry, premium parking, and access to the Plus Paddock to attend the exclusive Q&As with James Martin as well as a covered seating area, toilets, and a private chargeable bar.

DogFest Plus ticket holders will also receive a premium goodie bag for their dog, including a lanyard, doggy bandana, and a pup cup, plus a complementary drink for humans.

Secure tickets for DogFest on the Farm now at the DogFest on the Farm website and tickets must be booked online before arrival, no tickets sales are available at the entrance.
