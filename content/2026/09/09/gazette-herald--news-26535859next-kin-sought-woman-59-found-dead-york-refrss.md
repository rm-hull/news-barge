  **UPDATE: A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "Earlier today, on behalf of the Coroner’s Office, we appealed for help to trace the next of kin of a 59-year-old woman who died in York last month."We’re pleased to confirm that her next of kin have now been located."**

A woman has been found dead in York.

The Coroner’s Office has launched an appeal to find the next of kin of Diane Carlin, 59, who sadly died in the city last month.

  


North Yorkshire Police said her death was not being treated as suspicious.

A force spokesperson added: “If you have any information that would help the Coroner to locate any members of Ms Carlin’s family please email [coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.)”
