  **Update: National Rail said in a statement issued around 12pm today (Wednesday, September 9) that services in the area are now running as scheduled.**

TRAIN services in North Yorkshire have been delayed after a vehicle hit level crossing barriers.

TransPennine Express said its services from Manchester Airport to Redcar Central and Saltburn have been affected after line were blocked following the collision with the barriers in Redcar.

The route passes through stations in York, Thirsk and Northallerton.

York-based train operator Northern said this morning that "train services running to and from Middlesbrough and Saltburn will be cancelled, delayed or terminated and started back from Redcar Central.

"Road transport has been arranged, calling at all intermediate stations between Middlesbrough and Saltburn.

Disruption is ongoing this morning, the train operators have said and TransPennine Express said response teams are investigating the crash.
