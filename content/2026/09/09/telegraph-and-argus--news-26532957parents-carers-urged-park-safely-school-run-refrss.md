Police have urged Bradford parents and carers to park safely during the school run.

PCSO Clapperton from the Bradford West Neighbourhood Policing Team carried out parking patrols at Heaton St Barnabas Primary School as part of the Vision Zero campaign, which aims to eliminate deaths and serious injuries on the roads.

A spokesperson for the Bradford West NPT said: "The school run can be one of the busiest times on our roads, with large numbers of children, parents and staff all arriving and leaving at the same time.

Police are reminding drivers to park legally, avoid stopping on zig-zag markings, keep crossing points clear, and be mindful of both residents and other road users.

Motorists are also being urged to slow down and watch for children crossing.

The spokesperson added: "By parking safely and considerately, we can all help to reduce the risk of collisions and keep children safe.

"Every child deserves to get to and from school safely.

"Small changes in driver behaviour can make a big difference."
