HOT Aire! Concert Band recently returned from Carlisle, where they entertained an audience at the Cathedral.

Says concert co-ordinator Kathryn Harrison: “This one-hour concert, part of our free live music series, was an experience to remember; the programme was enhanced by the acoustics.

Hot Aire! (pictured above at Carlisle Cathedral and left at Carlisle Castle) played a wide repertoire, including music from Braveheart, ‘Coldplay in Symphony’ and Beyond the Horizon. Bradnum vocals sang Suil A Ruin and May it Be, beautiful performances.

“We also performed a second concert at Carlisle Castle on the Sunday, in glorious sunshine.

“We are currently looking for new players to join the band, especially a keyboard player and lower brass players. Hot Aire! welcome musicians of any standard into their training band: Fresh Aire!”

* For more information go to www.hotaire.org
