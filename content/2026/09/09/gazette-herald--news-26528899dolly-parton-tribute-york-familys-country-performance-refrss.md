A HEAD teacher from York will swap the classroom for the spotlight when she performs alongside her son and daughter in a new country music spectacular coming to the city for one night.

  Ruth McNeil will take to the stage with son Tom on bass and daughter Molly on lead guitar and banjo as part of Steve Coates Music Productions' latest show, *One Night of Country*, coming to Joseph Rowntree Theatre on Saturday, September 19.

The family affair extends beyond the performers, with husband Alastair playing a key role behind the scenes as guitar technician, ensuring the instruments are stage-ready throughout the production.

  


  Ruth McNeil and family will be on stage at Joseph Rowntree Theatre in Haxby Road performing One Night of Country on Saturday, September 19 at 7.30pm 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Son Tom, 23, is a games design graduate who also composes music and soundtracks for gaming platforms, while Molly, 25, works as an outdoor instructor.

She has recently returned from a month-long expedition to Kyrgyzstan with the British Exploring Society, arriving back in time to join rehearsals and perform in the show.

Ruth said: “Music is our life, our love and our passion.

"Sharing the stage with my family is incredibly special.

"We feel very lucky to be working together with a brilliant cast and production team.”

  Molly and Tom make up the string section of the family show 

*(Image: Supplied)*

  *One Night of Country* will celebrate some of the genre's most popular songs, from classic favourites to modern chart hits.

  Audiences can expect performances of songs including *Jolene*,* The Gambler*,* Tennessee Whiskey*,* Wagon Wheel*and* Man! I Feel Like a Woman!*, alongside energetic line dancing and full-company numbers, the organisers said.

The show will also feature music inspired by artists ranging from Dolly Parton to contemporary country chart stars.

Organisers say the production is designed to appeal to all ages, whether lifelong country music fans or those simply looking for an entertaining night out.

  *One Night of Country is at Joseph Rowntree Theatre in Haxby Road at 7.30pm on Saturday, September 19. Limited tickets are available.*
