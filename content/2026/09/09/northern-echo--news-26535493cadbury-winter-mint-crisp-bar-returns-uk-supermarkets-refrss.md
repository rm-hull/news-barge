A rare seasonal chocolate bar has returned to supermarket shelves and shoppers are excited.

The Cadbury Dairy Milk Winter Mint Crisp bar is a popular flavour among Brits but is only available in the months leading up to Christmas.

However, despite the big day still being a few months away, the bar is back on sale at an array of UK supermarkets.

## Cadbury Winter Mint Crisp bar returns to UK supermarkets


Tesco is selling the 350g bar for £5, or £4.50 with a Clubcard, while Morrisons is charging £6, or £4.75 with a MoreCard.

Both Ocado and Iceland are offering the bar for £5.

The bar combines classic Dairy Milk chocolate with crispy mint pieces.

Fans have welcomed its return, with one shopper calling it their "fave of all time."

Another customer said: "It's a shame it's not sold all year round but I think my waistband would be sorry if it was."

The Winter Mint Crisp is part of a wider range of Cadbury Christmas products that have already started appearing in stores, including Cadbury Biscoff Filled Centres and Dairy Milk Buttons Smash Baubles.

While Christmas is still more than three months away, Cadbury Christmas chocolate is already back on the shelves.


## Roast Potato Crisps to return to Co-op after 3-year hiatus

In other festive food news, a fan-favourite crisp flavour is returning to shelves.

Co-op’s roast potato crisps are making a comeback this month after a three-year absence, as part of the retailer’s new 2026 Christmas range.

Described as a “surprisingly bold taste” snack, the crisps blend sea salt, garlic, rosemary, and black pepper to capture the taste of a classic Christmas roast potato.

Co-op first introduced the flavour in 2019, but it was discontinued in 2023, prompting regular requests from shoppers for its return.

Natalie Dawe, crisps, snacks and nuts development manager at Co-op, said: "Our crisps have developed something of a cult following over the years, with our customers regularly championing their favourites.

"Alongside Pigs in Blankets and our new Wagyu Beef & Caramelised Onion flavour, we're excited to bring that early festive feeling to shoppers."

The returning crisps will feature a new crinkle-cut style and will be sold under the Co-op Irresistible Ultimate Roast Potato Crinkle Cut Crisps label.

They will be joined by two other festive flavours: Irresistible Wagyu Beef & Caramelised Onion Flavour Crisps and Irresistible Pigs in Blankets Crinkle Cut Crisps.

All three will be available in 150g bags for £1.75.
