A driver convicted of bad driving on the A64 kept his licence after he told magistrates his mental health would suffer if he were banned.

Stephen Wainwright, 45, of Copperfield Close, Sherburn-in-Elmet, pleaded guilty to driving without due care and attention on the dual carriageway at Tadcaster.

He was fined £666 and ordered to pay £130 prosecution costs and a £266 statutory surcharge.

Harrogate magistrates considered banning him as well. He told the court he was an operations manager for an electrical supplier, he had domestic financial problems and used his work as an escape from mental health issues.

He regularly travelled long distances by car for work.

He said he believed that if he were banned he would lose his job, and his mental health would then suffer.

He also said he would be unable to continue coaching three football teams, as he currently does.

Magistrates decided his circumstances justified them not banning him and gave him five penalty points instead.
