BRADFORD has been named one of the worst cities in the UK for getting a good night’s sleep.

It ranked fifth in the Sleepless Cities Index, a study by Nature’s Best, which scored cities out of 80 based on environmental factors such as noise, air pollution, and artificial brightness.

London topped the list with a score of just 19 out of 80, followed by Manchester, Liverpool, Birmingham, and Bradford in fifth place.

The study highlighted high levels of artificial light, air pollution, and road traffic as key contributors to poor sleep.

Other northern cities also featured in the top 10, including Leeds in sixth place.

At the other end of the scale, Dundee was named the best city for a full night’s sleep, earning a score of 65.62.
