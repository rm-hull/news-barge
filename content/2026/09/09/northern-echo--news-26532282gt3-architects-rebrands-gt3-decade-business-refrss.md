An architecture firm has marked a decade in business with a new name, brand and website.

Newcastle-based GT3 Architects will now be known simply as GT3.

The rebrand aims to better reflect the firm’s national presence and expanded services, 10 years after it first launched.

Simon Dunstan, co-founder and director at GT3, said: "Our tenth year felt like the right moment to look at how GT3 presents itself and make sure our identity reflects the practice we are today.

"We’ve grown significantly over the past decade, not only in terms of our people and geographical reach, but in the way we support our clients.

"Architecture remains fundamental to what we do, but our interiors and consultancy expertise has developed considerably, and those skills increasingly work together to help clients make better decisions and achieve stronger outcomes.

"Becoming GT3 gives us a much clearer way to communicate that breadth while keeping the philosophy that has guided us from the beginning firmly at the centre of the practice: People Architecture."

The company now operates offices in Newcastle, Nottingham, London and Belfast, and has broadened its expertise to include consultancy and interiors alongside architecture.

The refreshed website highlights GT3’s work across sports and leisure, workplace, residential, and hospitality sectors.

"The strongest projects deliver both, and our refreshed brand and website give us a much better platform to communicate that."
