## As temperatures soar past 50°C, Qatar taps cutting-edge engineering and cooling technology to keep cities productive. From district cooling networks to climate-conscious architecture, we explore how Qatar is redefining what it means to live, work and thrive in extreme temperatures.

Despite having a dry and arid climate, Qatar isn’t simply surviving the heat. It’s engineering a cooler future. We explore how one of the world’s hottest countries is redefining what it means to live, work and thrive in extreme temperatures. As summer brings temperatures to a soaring 50°C, Qatar is tapping on cutting edge engineering, sustainable urban planning and advanced cooling technologies to keep its cities comfortable and productive. From vast district cooling networks to climate-conscious architecture, we uncover the innovations that make modern life possible in the Gulf’s harshest months.
