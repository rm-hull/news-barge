This man is wanted by North Yorkshire Police.

“Extensive enquiries” are underway to find Ethan Anderson, 26.

Now police are asking for the public’s help to find him.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We believe Anderson may be in the Minskip or Boroughbridge areas.

“He also has links to Ripon, Knaresborough and Skipton.’

Anderson is wanted on recall to prison for breaching the conditions of his licence.

If you have an immediate sighting of him, you’re asked to call 999.

Anyone with information as to where he might be is asked to call 101.

Please quote reference number **12260171970**.
