# M&S Guiseley set for reopening after major refurb

## M&S has revealed Wednesday, September 16 as the re-opening date for its Guiseley Retail Park store, following a major transformation and extension.

The transformed 15,000 sq ft fresh market-style foodhall is 60 per cent bigger than the previous store and will offer more than 1,500 new products for customers.

The store also features a bigger in-store bakery & coffee counter.

Serita Thomas, store manager at M&S Guiseley, said: "We’ve had a store here in Guiseley for over 20 years and it’s exciting to be entering a new chapter by delivering a renewed and improved store. The extension means we have plenty more space to offer almost the full M&S Food range, making it so much easier for customers to do their weekly shop with us.”

![M&S has announced the opening date for its refurbished Guiseley Retail Park store.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjFiNWEwZmM3LWY1MzctNDNmMy04Mjk4LWMzNWY2OTEzNzMzYTpmZTBhZmRiOC04MmMwLTRkMWYtODJmMi01ZmMwN2UzNjkzM2E=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The transformation has created over 40 new local jobs across the store, joining the store’s existing team of 50 colleagues.

M&S is aiming to double the size of its food business by growing its store footprint and expanding existing stores.

Guiseley is one of a number of stores M&S is working on across the UK this financial year, alongside 18 new Food store openings, and follows the renewal of its Anlaby Hull store in March.
