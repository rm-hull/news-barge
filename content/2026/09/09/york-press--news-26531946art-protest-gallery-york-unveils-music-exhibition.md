## New York exhibition explores the connection between music and visual art

The allegory of dermis and gypsum, by Peter Davis *(Image: Peter Davis)*

Art of Protest Gallery is launching a new exhibition exploring the connection between music and visual art.

The gallery, located on Walmgate in York, is presenting Art You Can Hear from September 18 to October 7, 2026, featuring original works from Manchester artist Peter Davis, street artist Mul (Alex Mulholland), and emerging York artist Sam Josebury.

The exhibition will begin with a private view on September 17, 2026.

Art You Can Hear investigates how music influences identity, memory, and culture through various mediums such as paintings, limited-edition prints, and exclusive artist-designed T-shirts.

The exhibition runs in conjunction with the annual global Band T-Shirt Day and the closing weeks of the Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer Portrait Award 2026 at the National Portrait Gallery.

Josebury merges the spirit of the Impressionists with the live music scene.

He captures the ambiance of live performances in real-time, rendering rapid sketches at gigs and responding to the movement, light, sound, and energy of performance through rapid sketches while he’s in the crowd.

Peter Davis, known for his portraits and urban landscapes reflecting contemporary life, will present six new original artworks and three silver-leaf-embellished prints.

Davis's work often challenges perceptions surrounding mental health, race, gender, and technology addiction.

Mulholland, a creative director based in Newcastle upon Tyne, is recognised for his music-inspired, large-scale public murals.

His signature “Running Heart” character frequently appears in his works.

His creations will include lyric-based original works and newly found-object pieces, some being introduced to the public for the first time.

The exhibition will display a live-drawn Leeds Festival display, including a sketch transformed into a painting by Josebury.

The launch programme will commence with a private view on September 17, from 7pm to 9pm, followed by the official launch coinciding with Band T-Shirt Day on Friday, September 18, 2026.
