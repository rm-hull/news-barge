An illegal e-bike has been seized following reports of youths riding at "extreme speeds" in a North Yorkshire town.

Ryedale Neighbourhood Policing Team responded to multiple reports about two Surron e-bikes being used on public roads in Pickering last night (Tuesday).

A spokesperson for the team said: "This report matched several other similar reports that we had received over previous weeks.

"Incidents of this nature pose a significant risk to both those riding these types of bikes and the public.

"It is also illegal to ride many types of e-bike or e-scooter on public land."

Officers identified some of the youths involved and located one of the Surron bikes during an area search.

The e-bike was seized, and the rider may now face prosecution for several offences.

Ryedale Neighbourhood Policing Team are aware that there are potentially other youths around Pickering that may own Surron bikes or other types of illegal e-bike or scooter.

The spokesperson said: "We will be working proactively to identify these individuals to offer advice on their lawful use.

"Should any other e-bikes or e-scooters be witnessed being used illegally on public land, North Yorkshire Police will continue to seize these, to ensure the safety of all our residents."
