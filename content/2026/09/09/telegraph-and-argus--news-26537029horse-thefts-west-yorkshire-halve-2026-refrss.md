The number of horses reported stolen across West Yorkshire has more than halved this year, according to new figures released by the force's Horse Watch scheme.

Data published on social media by West Yorkshire Police Horse Watch shows five horses were reported stolen between January 1 and September 7, 2026, compared to 12 during the same period last year.

On social media, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police Horse Watch said: “Whilst the figures will always be too high, when we started this scheme we were having 60 horses a year stolen.”

## Trailer thefts also down

The latest figures also show a reduction in other equine-related thefts.

Reports of stolen tack fell from 23 incidents in the year to September 7, 2025, to 14 in the same period this year.

Meanwhile, the number of horse trailers reported stolen dropped from 10 to seven.

## What is Horse Watch?

Horse Watch, which operates in a similar way to Neighbourhood Watch, was set up to help prevent equine-related crime and strengthen links between horse owners and police.

The Horse Watch scheme encourages members to share information, adopt crime prevention measures and report suspicious activity around stables and land where horses are kept.

Members are also urged to note the registration numbers of unfamiliar vehicles and alert police to any suspicious behaviour.

Anyone wishing to be a part of the Horse Watch scheme can sign up via the West Yorkshire Police website.
