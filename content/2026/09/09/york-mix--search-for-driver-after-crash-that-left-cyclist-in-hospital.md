Police are searching for the driver of an Audi after a crash near Selby left a cyclist in hospital.

It happened on the A1041 at the roundabout with the A645 near Camblesforth at about 3pm on Sunday (6 September).

The cyclist was injured and taken to hospital, where they continue to receive treatment.

#### Crash location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “As part of our enquiries, we are keen to speak to the driver of the grey Audi involved.

“It is possible the driver may be unaware a collision occurred, despite contact between the vehicle and the cyclist. The vehicle did not stop at the scene.

“We are appealing to anyone who witnessed the collision, saw the grey Audi in the area before the incident, or has relevant dashcam footage, to come forward.”

The road was partially closed for several hours while emergency services attended and the cyclist received treatment.
