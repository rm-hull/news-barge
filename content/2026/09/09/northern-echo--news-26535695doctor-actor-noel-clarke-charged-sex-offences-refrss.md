Noel Clarke has been charged with two offences of sexual assault, three charges of voyeurism and one charge of exposure, the Metropolitan Police said.

The actor and fomer Doctor Who star will appear at Westminster Magistrates’ Court on Wednesday, October 21.

The charges relate to alleged offences between 2007 and 2016, which are connected to five women.

This follows an investigation by detectives starting in September 2025, when a man then in his 40s was first arrested in relation to the investigation.

A file of evidence was later presented to the CPS, who authorised the charges.

## Noel Clarke charged with sexual offences


Detective Superintendent Mike Cagney said: “The women connected to these charges continue to receive support from specially trained officers.

“We urge anyone who has been a victim of sexual assault to contact police. The information you provide will be dealt with in the utmost sensitivity and we have trained investigators on hand to support you.”

Bethan David, Deputy Chief Crown Prosecutor for CPS London said: “The Crown Prosecution Service has decided to prosecute Noel Clarke with two counts of sexual assault, three counts of voyeurism and one count of exposure.

“This follows allegations made by five women in connection to sexual misconduct that took place between 2007 and 2016.

“Our prosecutors have worked to establish that there is sufficient evidence to bring this case to court and that it is in the public interest to do so.

“We have worked closely with the Metropolitan Police as they have carried out their investigation into the allegations.

“The Crown Prosecution Service reminds everyone that proceedings are now active, and the defendant has the right to a fair trial. It is vital that there should be no reporting, commentary or sharing of information online which could in any way prejudice these proceedings.”
