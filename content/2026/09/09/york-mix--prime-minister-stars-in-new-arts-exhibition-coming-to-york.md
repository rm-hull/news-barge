Art of Protest Gallery to host a new exhibition that explores the relationship between musical and visual culture – featuring some familiar faces.

The gallery on Walmgate will bring together award-winning Manchester artist Peter Davis with street artist Mul and emerging artist Sam Joesbury for *Art You Can Hear*: how music shapes identity, memory, atmosphere and culture through original paintings, limited-edition prints and exclusive artist-designed T-shirts.

The exhibition will open with a private view on **Thursday 17 September, and run until Wednesday 7 October.**

It will coincide with the annual global Band T-Shirt Day (Friday 18 September) and the closing weeks of the Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer Portrait Award 2026 at the National Portrait Gallery.

Prize-winning artist Peter Davis is known for portraits and urban landscapes that explore contemporary life and challenge perceptions. His work has addressed subjects including mental health, race, gender, ageing, displacement and technology addiction.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/PETER-DAVIS_Andy-Burnham_The-Bs-of-Manchester_painting_2018_2026_FINAL-1000x1215.jpg)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/PETER-DAVIS_Nirvana_painting_work-in-progress-1000x1333.jpg)

*The B’s of Manchester*, is Davis’s portrait of PM Andy Burnham, originally painted in 2018 and revisited in 2026. The painting brings together **BEES, BURNHAM and BOROUGHS** in a visual tribute to Greater Manchester.

The exhibition includes six new original works and three limited-edition, silver-leaf embellished prints by Davis.

Mul aka Alex Mulholland, an established street artist, graphic designer and creative director based in Newcastle, is known for his vibrant, large-scale public murals, often inspired by music and featuring his signature ‘Running Heart’ character.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FB-Ads-scaled-1-1000x1000.jpg)

The show will feature a selection of Mul’s lyric-based original works alongside new found-object pieces, including work being shown publicly for the first time.

York resident and Fine Art graduate of Lancaster University, Sam Joesbury is bringing the spirit of the Impressionists to the live music scene, working ‘en plein air’ but with the stage as his landscape.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/510A84E6-0B1D-4FA9-8047-2DDFE8813A9B.webp)

His live music series captures bands and artists in real time at their gigs, responding to the movement, light, sound and energy of performance through rapid sketches while he’s in the crowd.

This exhibition will feature a live-drawn Leeds Festival display including a sketch worked up to a painting by Josebury, bringing the immediacy of live performance directly into the gallery.
