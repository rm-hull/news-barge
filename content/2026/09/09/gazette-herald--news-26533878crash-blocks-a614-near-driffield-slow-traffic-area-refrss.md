  **Update: The A614 southbound is now running normally.**

A CRASH has partially closed a main road outside an East Yorkshire town this morning (Wednesday, September 9).

The AA Traffic News is reporting that the A614 southbound near Driffield is blocked after the incident which is said to have happened before 7am, close to the turn off for Wetwang.

Traffic into Driffield is running slowly at the junction which connects the A166 road for York to Bridlington.
