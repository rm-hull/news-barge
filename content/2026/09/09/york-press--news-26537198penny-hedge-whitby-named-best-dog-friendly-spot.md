A pub has been named the best dog-friendly spot in Yorkshire.

The Penny Hedge in Whitby earned the title in a nationwide search led by Rover, and was recognised for its pet-friendly facilities and welcoming atmosphere.

Adem Fehmi, Rover’s canine behaviourist, said: "The best dog-friendly venues understand that a warm welcome goes beyond simply allowing dogs through the door – having access to fresh water, enough space to settle and a calm, comfortable environment can all help dogs feel at ease.

"The winning pubs have clearly thought about the experience from a dog’s perspective, while adding plenty of fun touches for owners too."

  


Rover’s team of “tavern testers” and their four-legged companions visited 110 pubs across the UK to find the best destinations for pups and their people.

In Yorkshire, The Penny Hedge impressed with its dedicated dog station offering fresh water, treats, and bowls in various sizes.

Dogs are welcome throughout the pub's hard-floored areas and can relax in quieter spots or enjoy the gated outdoor space.

The pub’s commitment to canine welfare extends beyond hospitality, having raised more than £5,000 for a lost-dog charity.

Other Yorkshire pubs earning praise included The Woolley Sheep Inn in Skipton and The Black Lion in Richmond.

Evaluation criteria included booking systems, pup accessibility, welcoming atmosphere, staff communication, canine facilities, pet safety, and the overall experience for their pooch.

The Penny Hedge joins other regional winners such as The Brandling Villa in Newcastle, The Lion at Basford in Nottingham, and The Foxglove in Islington.

Rover’s research suggests dogs are becoming true pub regulars in the UK, with 47 per cent of owners saying their dog is known by pub staff and 44 per cent saying they’re known by the landlord and locals alike.

More information about the Dog-Friendly Pub Awards can be found at [https://www.rover.com/uk/blog/dog-friendly-pubs/.](https://www.rover.com/uk/blog/dog-friendly-pubs/.)
