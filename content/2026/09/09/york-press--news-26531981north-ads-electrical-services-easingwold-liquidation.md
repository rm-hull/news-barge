A five-star rated North Yorkshire electrical installation company has gone into liquidation with reported debts approaching £200,000.

ADS Electrical Services of Shire Bridge Business Park, York Road, Easingwold, entered liquidation on July 28.

The company, which was incorporated in 2018, appointed GS Insolvency and Restructuring Ltd as liquidator.

A report, known as a statement of affairs posted on the Companies House website, says the business had estimated total assets available for distribution of £90,952.

However, the report said the business had preferred creditors- those who are paid first- who were owed £10,886 in wages/holiday pay.

HMRC was a secondary preferential creditor- those who are paid next- which was owed £3,262 in VAT and £15,532 PAYE and National Insurance.

This left £61,272 available to unsecured or non-preferential creditors.

However, these creditors had claims of £251,313, said the statement of affairs.

There were trade and expense claims totalling ££146,953. Some £3,417 was owed in business finance. There were also employee and redundancy claims of £72,493.

This gave an estimated deficiency of £190,041. With the company having issued and called up capital of £20, this gave an overall deficiency of £190,061 outstanding.

Google awarded the business five stars out of five based on 46 Google reviews.
