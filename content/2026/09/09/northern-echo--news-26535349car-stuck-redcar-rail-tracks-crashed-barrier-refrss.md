A car smashed into a railway barrier and came to rest on train tracks in a terrifying crash.

Police were called to Redcar Lane, in Redcar, at about 8.40am on Wednesday (September 9) after a car hit a railway barrier and stopped on train tracks.

The driver got out of the vehicle and is not believed to have been injured.

  Picture shows car stuck on railway after driver crashed into a railway barrier with their car coming to a rest on train tracks. 

*(Image: NETWORK RAIL)*

The vehicle was removed from the tracks, and Redcar Lane has since reopened.

Trains between Redcar Central and Saltburn were suspended while emergency services attended the scene.

Trains were back running by 11.15am after safety checks and repairs to the level crossing fencing.

A Network Rail spokesperson said: “A car struck the barriers at Church Lane level crossing, Redcar, shortly before 8.45am.

"Trains between Redcar Central and Saltburn were suspended while emergency services attended, the vehicle was recovered from the track and our engineers inspected the railway.

"Following safety checks, normal railway operations resumed at 11.14am, with minor repairs to level crossing fencing completed.”
