# Red Kite Teacher Training in Harrogate celebrates 'strong' Ofsted outcome in 10th anniversary year

## A Harrogate-based provider of teacher training has been rated strong in its latest Ofsted report.

Red Kite Teacher Training achieved a Strong Standard across every aspect of its primary and secondary provision following its latest Ofsted inspection under Ofsted's renewed Initial Teacher Education framework.

Based at Pannal Ash Road on the same site as Harrogate Grammar School, Red Kite Teacher Training works in partnership with schools across the region and the University of Leeds to prepare future primary and secondary teachers for successful careers in the classroom.

The Ofsted success comes at a particularly significant time, as Red Kite Teacher Training reaches its tenth year as a School Centred Initial Teacher Training (SCITT) provider.

Over the past decade, the partnership has helped hundreds of trainee teachers take their first steps into the profession and go on to make a lasting difference to children and young people across the region and beyond.

Victoria Lickley, Director of Red Kite Teacher Training, said: "We are delighted with this outcome and proud that the quality of our work has been recognised through Ofsted's new inspection framework.

"As we celebrate ten years of Red Kite Teacher Training, this recognition reflects the expertise, commitment and generosity of our partner schools, mentors, Early Career Leaders and wider team."

Ofsted Inspectors recognised the quality of training, mentoring and support provided across the Red Kite partnership.

The report pointed to the strength of both the primary and secondary programmes and recognised how well trainees are prepared for the classroom and their future careers in teaching.

The inspection also highlighted the culture at the heart of the programme, recognising collaboration, continuous improvement and strong support for trainees throughout their training.

Victoria Lickley said: "We were particularly pleased to see Ofsted recognise the values of wise compassion and professional generosity that continue to shape our partnership.

“This outcome reflects the dedication of our trainees and the positive impact they go on to make in classrooms, schools and communities throughout their careers.

"We would like to thank everyone who has contributed to our success over the past decade."
