# North Yorkshire Police launch appeal to help find wanted man who could be in Harrogate district

## North Yorkshire Police has released an image and launched an appeal to help find a wanted man who could be in the Harrogate district.

Ethan Anderson, 26, from Minskip, is wanted on recall to prison for breaching the conditions of his licence.

Officers believe Anderson may be in the Minskip or Boroughbridge areas.

He also has links to Ripon, Knaresborough and Skipton.

![Ethan Anderson, 26, from Minskip, is wanted on recall to prison for breaching the conditions of his licence and could be in Minskip, Boroughbridge, Ripon, Knaresborough and Skipton](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjA0ZWMwM2I0LTBiMWQtNDE4OS04YjllLWVlMTU0NmE2MmY5OTpmOTU0YjRmYS00OTY5LTRiYTYtYmQwOS00OWVlOTQ1MGZjMDg=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Extensive enquiries have been carried out to locate him, and as part of our enquiries, we are now appealing for the public's help to find him.”

If you see Anderson, or have any information about his current whereabouts, you should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

If the sighting is immediate, you should call 999.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260171970 when providing any details regarding his whereabouts.
