Argentinian tango is taking centre stage in Selby this weekend.

World-renowned tango singer Martín Alvarado will perform at Selby Town Hall on Saturday, September 12, as part of his 15-date UK tour.

The show will feature Argentinian guitar quartet Gerardo Villar y Los Aguirre, and promises an evening of tango classics, Piazzolla compositions, Argentinian folk songs, and original ballads.

Mr Alvarado said: "I am so pleased to be returning to the UK after three years.

"The British audiences are always so appreciative and respectful of my creative mission as an Argentinian musician.

"I look forward to bringing my art back to East Yorkshire with this exceptional guitar quartet."

The tour follows a successful 2023 run that saw packed venues and standing ovations across the UK.

It also marks Alvarado’s return to British stages after three years, as he continues to promote their new album, Llevo el Sur.

UK producer Lisa Cherry-Downes, of L Tango Promotions, said: "British audiences love Martin and they also love guitars – as they showed venue after venue in 2023."

Mr Alvarado has performed regularly in the UK since 2011 and was named an "Academico" by the National Academy of Tango in 2019.

Details of the tour can be found at www.martinalvarado.info
