A MAN from a North Yorkshire village who is wanted by police may be in that area or a nearby town.

The county's police force is appealing for the public's help to locate 26-year-old Ethan Anderson from Minskip, who it said may be there, or the nearby town of Boroughbridge, a mile away.

The force said he is wanted on recall to prison for breaching the conditions of his license.

  


A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Extensive enquiries have been carried out to locate him.

"He also has links to Ripon, Knaresborough and Skipton.

"If you see or have information as to his current whereabouts, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101. "

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through its website.

Anyone with an immediate sighting should call 999, police said.

Please quote reference number 12260171970 when providing information to North Yorkshire Police.
