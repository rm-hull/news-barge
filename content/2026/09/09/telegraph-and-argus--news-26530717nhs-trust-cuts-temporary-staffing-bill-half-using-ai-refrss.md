A HOSPITAL trust has cut its temporary staffing bill by up to 50 per cent with the help of artificial intelligence.

Mid Yorkshire Teaching NHS Trust piloted the AI-powered rostering system in two departments.

The technology, developed by Patchwork Health, led to up to a 50 per cent reduction in temporary staffing costs and a marked improvement in clinician satisfaction.

Ian Carr, associate director in the medical directorate at the trust, said: "Introducing this technology has been transformational, both for our resident doctors on the frontline, and for the workforce teams who work tirelessly behind-the-scenes to ensure we have the right people in the right place at all times.

"The feedback we’ve had from staff has been overwhelmingly positive, and I’m excited to see how we can take the lessons learned during this initial phase and apply the same approach to more departments across the trust."

The system uses a purpose-built algorithm to create personalised rosters based on doctors’ shift preferences, contractual requirements, and service needs.

During the six-month pilot, 99.8 per cent of clinicians’ negative shift preferences—shifts they preferred not to work—were honoured at the time of roster publication.
