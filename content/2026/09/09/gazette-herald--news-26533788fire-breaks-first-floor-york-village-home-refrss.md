A FIRE has started on the first floor of a home in a York village.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews from the city station and Acomb were called to Main Street in Knapton at 7.27pm yesterday (Tuesday, September 8).


A service spokesperson said: "On arrival crews found a fire to the first floor of the property.

"Crews used one hose reel jet, two breathing apparatus and a thermal imaging camera to extinguish the fire."
