Two young men involved in a serious crash between two speeding cars that were allegedly racing in the moments before they collided have described the run-up to the incident.

Husayn Ali, 21, from Shipley, died three hours after his Skoda Fabia was in an almost head-on collision with a VW Golf on Bolton Road in Bradford on the evening of March 7, 2024.

A student at Leeds Beckett University, he was not wearing a seatbelt.

The driver of the Golf, described in Bradford Crown Court as “an innocent party” who was adhering to the 30mph speed limit, was left trapped in his car following the crash but escaped with just minor injuries.

Two friends travelling in Mr Ali’s Skoda were not so lucky and suffered serious injuries.

Another of Mr Ali’s friends, Mohammed Awais Iqbal, 24, of Beechwood Drive, Wibsey, denies causing death by dangerous driving and is currently on trial.

Prosecution barrister Gerald Hendron previously told the jury how Iqbal and Mr Ali “were racing or driving competitively with each other” and that Iqbal used the modified lighting system on his Seat Leon to indicate for Mr Ali’s Skoda Fabia, which was in front of him, “to go.”

Both cars then sped up to twice the 30mph limit.

The Skoda crashed moments later as it failed to round a bend, hitting the VW Golf in the opposite lane.

The crash occurred following a birthday celebration.

Several friends in three cars decided to drive to a dessert parlour.

In a witness statement read to the court, Mr Ali’s rear seat passenger Bilal Hussain said most of the people that were involved in the resulting crash were his friends.

He described Mr Ali as “kind of a friend of a friend”.

He said the three cars met at Tesco Express before leaving together and turning right onto Bolton Road to drive to the dessert parlour.

Mr Hussain said: “Husayn drove on Bolton Road for around a quarter of a mile before we seemed to approach a bend.

“For some reason, Husayn picked his speed up.

“We were travelling definitely over 40mph. I don’t know if it was an ego thing or Husayn being cocky, but we went round this bend too fast and lost control.

“The car seemed to move to the right, out of his lane, and then we crashed.

“I didn’t see what we crashed into. It all happened so fast, and I was then knocked out.

“I was in and out of consciousness and feeling lots of pain.

“I remember being cut out of the vehicle and could only see upwards as I was put on a stretcher.”

He gave his statement whilst still in hospital having suffered a broken sternum and elbow as well as cuts and bruises to his face and legs.

He also told police: “When Harris and I came into A&E we were laid side by side, and one of the doctors broke the news that Husayn had been killed in the crash.”

The front seat passenger in the Skoda, Harris Butt, said Mr Ali was in good spirits and that his driving “initially was okay.”

But he added: “He headed down Bolton Road and went through the first set of lights near to the pizza shop before then coming to a stop at a red light at the next junction where the road leads off downhill.

“We remained in the lane to head straight. As the lights changed, Husayn just gassed it. He started to build speed going through the gears.

“The music wasn’t loud and the car was silent. Nobody was speaking. He would have easily got to 50/60mph when we got to the bend to the left.

“As he steered, due to speed, the wheels weren’t turning.

“I don’t know if he froze or blacked out but he didn’t brake and he went straight into oncoming traffic where he collided head-on with another car.

“I remember it being a really bad crash, but I was fully alert. My initial reaction was to get out in case it caught fire, so I took off my seatbelt and got out.

“I looked back and saw Bilal looking all confused, and that Husayn wasn’t moving.”

He added: “I remember that when Husayn was building speed along Bolton Road, Awais [Iqbal] was keeping up.

“There was no direct conversation prior to leaving Tesco about what Husayn was going to do, and I still cannot think as to why he would travel so fast, why he wouldn’t stop, and why he didn’t brake.”

As a result of the crash Mr Butt had surgery to insert a metal rod in his broken leg.

He also broke his sternum.

Mohammed Awais Iqbal denies causing death by dangerous driving, dangerous driving, and two counts of causing serious injury by dangerous driving.

The trial continues.
