TWO new directors have joined First Bus as it expands its presence in the UK’s growing franchised bus market.

Steve Draper has been appointed operations director and Ali Raffat has taken on the role of commercial director, completing the First Bus Franchising directorate team.

Their arrival is part of the company’s continued investment in building specialist franchising capability.

Zoe Hands, managing director of First Bus Franchising, said: "We're delighted to welcome Steve and Ali to First Bus.

"Their combined operational, commercial and franchising expertise will be invaluable as we continue to grow our franchising business.

"As more local authorities explore or adopt franchising, First Bus is committed to being an active partner, helping deliver high-quality services and positive outcomes for passengers and communities across the UK."

Mr Draper brings more than 30 years of leadership experience in transport and manufacturing.

He was most recently performance director at Arriva, where he led initiatives across depot performance, driver recruitment and training, and operational excellence.

First Bus said his experience will be instrumental in supporting the delivery of high-quality services in the evolving franchising landscape.

Mr Raffat joins with more than 15 years of experience in corporate finance, investment banking and transport.

He previously served as business development director, bids & M&A at Go-Ahead, where he led major bidding and concession projects and supported business growth.

The appointments strengthen First Bus’s position as a trusted partner for local authorities seeking to deliver franchised bus networks.
