Free, potentially life-saving blood pressure checks are being offered to communities across North Yorkshire.

It comes as part of a county-wide campaign during Know Your Numbers Week, which runs from September 7 to 13.

An estimated more than 50,000 adults in the area are living with undiagnosed high blood pressure, based on 2024 data.

Councillor Michael Harrison, North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for health and adult services, said: "High blood pressure is known as the 'silent killer' – no symptoms, just risk.

"I would encourage as many people as possible to take advantage of these free and life-saving, convenient blood pressure checks.

"It only takes a few minutes and could make a significant difference to your health and wellbeing."

Residents can borrow blood pressure monitors for free from libraries across the county.

Self-service checks are also available at the health kiosk in the urgent treatment centre reception area of Whitby Community Hospital, open daily from 8am to 8pm.

Free checks are also being offered at all Active North Yorkshire leisure and wellbeing hubs during the campaign week, with the exception of Selby Health and Wellbeing Hub.

High blood pressure is a major cause of heart attacks and strokes, underlining the importance of regular monitoring.

More information is available at northyorks.gov.uk.
