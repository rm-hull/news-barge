A charity gift shop in York has unveiled a new look and a new name to the public.

York Against Cancer’s shops at York Community Stadium and York Hospital will now be known as The Hope Emporium.

The shop will continue to offer only new, quality gifts.

Jill Hutchinson, retail manager at York Against Cancer, said: "When we were looking at the identity of the shop, we wanted a name that represented much more than simply a place where we sell beautiful things.

"The word 'Hope' felt absolutely right, because behind everything we do is the reason the shop exists – to raise money for York Against Cancer and to make a difference to people whose lives have been affected by cancer.

"When someone buys something from The Hope Emporium, they aren't simply buying a gift, a treat for themselves or something lovely for their home.

"It tells people what we are about."

Andy Knowles, CEO of York Against Cancer, added: "We're delighted to introduce The Hope Emporium, the official gift shop of York Against Cancer.

"The name means a great deal to us because it came from our own staff and volunteers; the people who know this charity from the inside."
