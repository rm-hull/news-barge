NANDO'S is expected to open a new restaurant in the Bradford district by the end of the year.

Earlier this year, it was revealed that the international food chain was planning to open a Keighley branch in a new built row of units on East Parade – part of the new Aldi development.

The company has recently applied to Bradford Council for a licence to serve alcohol at the branch between 10am and midnight.

A decision on that licence application is expected imminently.

*(Image: T&A)*

The company has also now started advertising jobs for the new branch, including an assistant manager.

And a recent post on social media by the company says the branch is expected to open in early December.

Planning applications submitted to the council earlier this year show that the restaurant will be able to seat 110 diners, including an outdoor seating area.
