The price of a top North Yorkshire hotel and restaurant has been slashed in price by almost a million.

Crab Manor Hotel and the Crab and Lobster Restaurant in Thirsk is now available for £3.1million.

When Savills first began marketing the property over three months ago, the agents were seeking offers above £4million for the long leasehold.

The hotel features 20 en-suite rooms within an 18th-century Georgian manor house, each styled after destinations around the world.

Tom Cunningham, UK regional director of hotel capital markets at Savills Manchester, said: "Crab Manor Hotel is a unique boutique hotel in North Yorkshire, taking inspiration from the best hotels around the world.

"Both the hotel and restaurant are well regarded locally and nationally, making it an excellent proposition."

Many rooms include private hot tubs, saunas, and roll-top baths, while the property also boasts extensive landscaped gardens.

The restaurant offers 40 covers, alongside a 70-cover conservatory, 20-cover bar area, and seating for 60 guests outdoors.

A separate Seafood Room provides private dining for up to 16 guests.

Crab Manor Hotel is located just outside the historic market town of Thirsk, surrounded by North Yorkshire countryside and close to the A1(M).
