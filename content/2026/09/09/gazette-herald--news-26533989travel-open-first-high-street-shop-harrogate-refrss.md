A North Yorkshire travel agency is set to open its first high street store.

There Travel, based in Harrogate and founded in 2024 by Colin Ord and Jake Todhunter, will open the shop in the town at the end of September.

The agency, which already operates with a team of homeworkers, says the new shop will strengthen its connection to the town and offer a space for face-to-face customer service.

Colin Ord, co-owner of There Travel, said: "Harrogate is already home for There Travel, so opening our first shop here feels like a natural next step for us.

"Since Jake and I founded the business in 2024, we’ve wanted to build something that is genuinely about being there for people.

"This is a really exciting milestone for There Travel and one we’re incredibly proud of."

The high street shop will complement the company’s homeworking model, giving customers the choice to meet in person or continue working remotely with an advisor.

Jake Todhunter, co-owner of There Travel, said: "Our homeworkers are a huge part of what makes There Travel what it is, and the shop adds another dimension to that.

"We want customers to be able to connect with There Travel in the way that suits them – whether that’s sitting down with us in Harrogate or speaking to one of our homeworkers.

"Our name sums up what we want the business to stand for.

"It’s about being there – when someone is dreaming about their next holiday, when they’re planning it, when they’re away and when they’re ready to do it all over again."

There Travel is a member of the Advantage Travel Partnership.

This allows the agency to combine personalised, independent service with access to a broad range of travel products and partners.

The new shop will help customers plan everything from family getaways and beach breaks to cruises, luxury trips, and tailor-made adventures.

For Mr Ord and Mr Todhunter, the shop is more than just a new location.

They see it as a chance to deepen the company’s roots in Harrogate and create a central hub for the brand alongside its network of homeworkers.

The Harrogate shop is expected to open at the end of September, with more details about the launch to be shared soon.

There Travel describes itself as an independent travel agency built around the philosophy of "being there" for its customers, its people and the communities it serves.

It offers travel advice and support through its team of homeworkers and, from the end of September, through its first high street shop.

The business aims to provide a personalised and flexible service, whether customers prefer to visit in person or connect with an advisor from home.

As yet, the pair have not disclosed where the shop will be.
