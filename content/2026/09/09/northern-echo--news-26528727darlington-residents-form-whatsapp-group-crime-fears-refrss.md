Residents have joined forces after reports of people trying front doors and targeting cars and vans across parts of Darlington.

More than 140 people have joined a Neighbourhood Watch WhatsApp group sharing alerts, CCTV images and crime prevention advice in Haughton, Springfield and Whinfield.

Darlington Borough councillor Nick Wallis set up the group after what he called an “unprecedented rise” in property and vehicle crime over the summer.

He said residents had reported people trying front doors almost daily, as well as break-ins involving cars and vans across the ward.

The WhatsApp community lets residents flag concerns, exchange information and share crime prevention tips while keeping their personal details hidden from other members.

The concerns were raised at a well attended Police and Communities Together meeting for Haughton and Red Hall last week.

Councillor Wallis said residents and councillors wanted proactive night patrols because suspected offenders appeared to be following the same route through Haughton, Springfield and Whinfield.

He said: “It is evident that criminals are broadly following a predictable route when trying house doors across Haughton, Springfield and Whinfield.

“The PACT meeting agreed that we urgently need police night patrols to disrupt this activity.

“I appreciate that Durham Constabulary has been hit by an unprecedented crime spike over the summer, which has meant that beat officers are being pulled onto 999 response duties. However, our current position in Haughton and Springfield is not sustainable.

“Darlington Police are doing everything they can, but they need support from the centre to tackle this problem.

“We are asking therefore that proactive police night patrols take place to break the repeated cycle of break ins and burglaries we have been experiencing.”

After the meeting, Haughton and Springfield councillors wrote to Durham Constabulary Chief Constable Rachel Bacon calling for targeted resources to disrupt those believed to be responsible.

Councillor Wallis said anyone wanting to join the Neighbourhood Watch WhatsApp group can message him on 07960 247554.

Durham Constabulary previously said six residential burglaries had been reported in Haughton and Whinfield over four weeks, and that many affected properties had been left unlocked.

Durham Constabulary Chief Constable Rachel Bacon has been contacted for comment.
