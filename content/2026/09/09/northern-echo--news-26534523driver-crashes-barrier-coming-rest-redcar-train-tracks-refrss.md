A driver crashed into a railway barrier with their car coming to a rest on train tracks.

Police were called to Redcar Lane, in Redcar, at about 8.40am on Wednesday, September 9, after a car hit a railway barrier and stopped on train tracks.

The driver got out of the vehicle and is not believed to have been injured.

The vehicle will be removed from the tracks and Redcar Lane has since reopened.

Pictures seen by The Northern Echo show a light blue car stuck on the train tracks.

Rail passengers faced delays, with trains unable to run between Redcar Central and Saltburn in either direction.

Services to and from the stations will be delayed, terminated at Redcar Central or started there until the incident is cleared.

The Northern Echo has contacted Network Rail for a statement.
