A MAN has been rescued after falling into a works trench outside an East Yorkshire town.

Humberside Fire and Rescue Service was called to The Paddock in Molescroft, near Beverley, at 10.47pm yesterday (Tuesday, September 8).


A service spokesperson said: "Assistance was given to paramedics - a short extension ladder and working at height equipment was in use.

"The incident was left with the ambulance service."
