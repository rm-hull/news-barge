A business development manager is running the Great North Run dressed as a giant parmo.

Business development manager Jill Cull, who works at North-East law firm Muckle LLP, will run the half marathon on Sunday, September 13, to raise funds for the Middlesbrough FC Foundation.

She is one of 11 runners representing the firm and is doing so in full parmo attire - a nod to the Teesside comfort food.

Ms Cull said: "I know I will be able to finish the race, even though it is heavy and a challenge, but I can do it.

"I have done challenges before, but nothing quite like this.

"When I was doing the ultra-marathon, I ran the rest of the race in some discomfort without knowing what I’d actually done, so I know I can finish the Great North Run dressed as a parmo.

"It is not in my mentality not to, no matter how long I take to finish it."

A seasoned runner with a half-marathon personal best of 1 hour 54 minutes, Ms Cull has never raced in costume before.

The custom-made foam parmo has made training particularly challenging, as the suit absorbs sweat and becomes heavier during her runs.

Paul Murphy from the MFC Foundation said: "When we playfully suggested the parmo idea to Jill, we were absolutely thrilled when she agreed.
