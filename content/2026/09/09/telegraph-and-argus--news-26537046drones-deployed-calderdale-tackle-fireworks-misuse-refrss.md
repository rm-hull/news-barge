Drones are being deployed in Calderdale to tackle anti-social behaviour related to misuse of fireworks, with the bonfire season fast approaching.

Posts from two of Calderdale’s political parties have referenced their use and heralding a clampdown on some firework use which is deemed unacceptable.

Calderdale Council’s ruling Reform UK group have said on their social media that: “We know residents across Calderdale have been increasingly frustrated by fireworks being set off late at night, repeatedly and at unacceptable times.

Wainhouse ward Labour councillors have also posted about the issue, saying: “Residents across Wainhouse have been telling us loud and clear: late-night and out-of-season fireworks are causing real concern and disruption in our communities.”

Councillors Ann Kingstone and Dave Mendes da Costa say they have been have been working with organisations to develop a co-ordinated Fireworks Action Plan, bringing together the council, Police and partners to tackle the problem through prevention, intelligence, patrols and targeted enforcement.

Through September, this has included targeted patrols in hotspot areas, drone deployment where appropriate and pro-active visits to locations linked to complaints.

Warning letters where intelligence identifies concerns have been issued, said the councillors, with intelligence and reporting guidelines made clear.

“This is about listening to residents and taking action.

“People should not have to put up with repeated late-night disruption, frightened pets, disturbed sleep or fireworks being used irresponsibly.

“The message is simple – if fireworks are causing problems in our communities, report them.

“Your information matters and helps authorities identify patterns, hotspots and those responsible…

“Our communities deserve peace, safety and respect – and we’ll keep fighting for them,” said the councillors.
