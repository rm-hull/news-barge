Three new industrial and warehouse units in York have been fully let to growing businesses.

The units are located at Northminster Business Park in York and were developed as part of a £3.3 million investment that transformed three former agricultural stores into modern, detached industrial buildings.

Located within a self-contained area of the park known as Cropton Court, the buildings feature energy-efficient technologies such as solar panels, translucent roof lights, high-bay LED lighting, and electric vehicle charging points.

George Burgess, managing director at Northminster Properties, which owns the business park, said: "Northminster Business Park has grown significantly in recent years and is now home to more than 50 businesses, employing approximately 1,000 people.

"Our ability to provide a range of space—from industrial and warehouse units to offices and laboratory facilities—in a secure and highly accessible location is key to our success.

"Completing Cropton Court and welcoming Express Electronics, AES and Dolphin Minerals into their new buildings marks another important milestone for us.

"Each unit combines an excellent working environment with strong sustainability credentials and will give these businesses the platform they need to continue growing in this sought-after part of York."

The units, which range from 6,250 to 8,250 sq ft, are now occupied by Yorkshire-based firms aiming to expand or consolidate while remaining in the region.

The new tenants include Dolphin Minerals, a wholesaler of crystals, minerals, fossils, gemstones, and related products; Automotive Equipment Solutions (AES) UK, a supplier of garage equipment for commercial vehicles; and Express Electronics, one of the UK’s fastest-growing electronic component distributors.

The buildings were jointly marketed by property consultancies GV&Co and PPH Commercial.

Paul Mack, director at GV&Co, said: "Northminster Business Park is undoubtedly one of North Yorkshire’s most successful business parks and appeals to a wide range of occupiers seeking high-quality premises in York.

"We generated significant interest in these new buildings, and securing deals with three well-established local businesses is a real success.

"Importantly, the park also offers significant room for expansion, with more than 25 acres of developable land available.

"This could accommodate up to 300,000 sq ft of new industrial, warehouse, or office space, presenting an exciting opportunity for other growing businesses."

The business park is located less than four miles west of York city centre and is within walking distance of Poppleton Park & Ride and Poppleton railway station.
