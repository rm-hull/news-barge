!["Israel reacts with fury to UK curbs on settlements" reads the headline on the front of the Times.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/809/cpsprodpb/0b0e/live/bec15c90-abcb-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

Several of Wednesday's papers lead on the fallout from Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband's announcement that the UK will impose sanctions on illegal Jewish settlements in the occupied West Bank. The Times says Israel has "reacted with fury", describing the Labour government as "hostile". The paper also highlights comments from Chief Rabbi Sir Ephraim Mirvis, who says the measure will embolden those "who use hatred as a weapon against Jews". Like several of today's papers, the Times also carries a photo of Prince George on his first day at Eton College.

!["Israeli fury after UK accuses settlers of ethnic cleansing" reads the headline on the front page of The i Paper.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/830/cpsprodpb/955f/live/6c7175e0-abc8-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

The i lead with "Israeli fury after UK accuses settlers of ethnic cleansing". It also highlights the foreign secretary's accusation that the Israeli government is "turning a blind eye" to the actions of "settler terrorists". It says the announcement could lead to Andy Burnham's "first major dispute with Trump".

!["UK condemns 'ethnic cleansing by Israeli terrorists' in West Bank" reads the headline on the front page of the Guardian.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/765/cpsprodpb/45dd/live/788033c0-abc9-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

The prime minister has written in the Guardian that the UK has been "fearful of being accused of being anti-Israel" and that he would "not stand by as horrific suffering continues". The UK is among 12 nations in the process of imposing sanctions following "a summer of intense diplomacy against a backdrop of increasing settler violence in the West Bank", the Guardian reports.

!["Miliband accused of putting Jews in UK in danger" reads the headline on the front page of the Daily Mail.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/810/cpsprodpb/626d/live/e0ae94f0-abc9-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

"Miliband accused of putting Jews in UK in danger" is the headline of the Daily Mail, which accuses the foreign secretary of "caving in to Left-wing activists". The paper also spotlights the Chief Rabbi's words, who called it "truly a dark day for British Jews".

!["Backlash as UK accuses Israel" reads the headline on the front page of the Metro.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/827/cpsprodpb/02ec/live/4faf6660-abc8-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

The Metro writes that Miliband set out how a two-state solution in the region is the government's "North Star". The foreign secretary told MPs he is a "proud British Jew" but felt a "deep sense of shame" about the condition of the Palestinians.

!["You have put an increased target on us'," reads the headline on the front page of the Daily Express.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/571d/live/5c159310-abc9-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

The Daily Express also hones in on criticism of the announcement from the British Jewish community. Miliband was pressed on whether he expected the government's toughened stance on Israel to result in an increased threat to British Jews during his Commons appearance on Tuesday.

!["Air traffic collapse grounds flights" reads the headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/ca60/live/a6375170-abcb-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

Also featured in the top slot on several of Wednesday's front pages is air traffic control issues around the UK. "Air traffic collapse grounds flights" is the Daily Telegraph's lead story, with the fault meaning "tens of thousands of airline passengers were left stranded last night".

!["That's another flyin' mess" reads the headline on the front page of the Sun.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/2560/cpsprodpb/ccfc/live/9135bbb0-abc9-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

Tuesday's disruption at airports also dominates the front page of the Sun. It says there is "fury at latest jet farce" which follows "a string of similar outages" with the national air traffic control service. Delays and cancellations are expected to continue into Wednesday.

!["Bailey warns of 'upside' inflation risks as debt costs pile pressure on Healey" reads the headline on the front page of the Financial Times.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1056/cpsprodpb/60ad/live/58f29310-abc7-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.jpg)

Another spike in inflation could be looming for the UK economy, warns the governor of the Bank of England. Andrew Bailey's intervention is the lead story in the Financial Times - he told MPs that the ongoing conflict between the US and Iran, as well as extreme weather, could increase the strain on the public finances.

!["Why, why, why did you sack me?" reads the headline on the front page of the Daily Star.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/a6b4/live/b8d37cc0-abc9-11f1-aed2-8d6da8d75094.jpg)

Tom Jones is "furious he's been kicked out of his chair" on talent show The Voice, the Daily Star reports. The Welsh singer reportedly resisted being ousted from the programme.

!["Harry and King have not spoken" reads the headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/a143/live/42439410-abc8-11f1-a291-b542ee92de7c.jpg)

According to the Daily Mirror, the Duke of Sussex and King Charles "have not spoken" since the prince return to the UK. The papers dubs the story a "royal rumble exclusive".

Many of Wednesday's papers lead on the government's announcement it will ban trade with illegal Jewish settlement in the occupied West Bank. The Guardian describes the move as the UK's starkest condemnation since the state of Israel was founded. It quotes the prime minister, who said the government had been too hesitant to act, external for too long, for fear of being labelled "anti-Israel".

Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband has been accused of putting British Jews in danger, external, according to The Daily Mail, citing the response of the Chief Rabbi, who called the government's announcement "a dark day". The Daily Express says Jewish leaders fear rising anti-semitism, external as a result of the sanctions. "You have put an increased target on us", reads the paper's headline. The i paper focuses on Israel's reaction, saying it is threatening intelligence sharing with the UK.

The Daily Telegraph leads on Tuesday's air traffic control problems, external, saying the head of Nats has been branded an "overpaid failure". Martin Rolfe is quoted as saying "I always accept responsibility". The Times labels the situation "chaos, external". "That's another flyin' mess, external" says the Sun's headline. The paper reports that passengers were in disbelief at the disruption.

The governor of the Bank of England Andrew Bailey has warned that the war in Iran and the effects of extreme weather threaten to ignite another burst of inflation, external, according to the Financial Times. It says the Bank could revisit its forecasts, adding to pressure on the public finances ahead of the Budget next month.

Royal sources have told the Daily Mirror that Prince Harry has not spoken to his father since returning to the UK with his family two weeks ago. Under the headline "distant relative, external", the paper says the Royal Family are "perplexed" over the lack of contact.

And the sacking of Tom Jones, external as a judge on the talent show The Voice leads the Daily Star. In a nod to his 1968 hit, Delilah, its headline reads "Why, why, why, did you sack me?"
