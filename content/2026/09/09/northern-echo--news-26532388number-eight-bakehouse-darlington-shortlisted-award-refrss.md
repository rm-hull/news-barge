A Darlington bakery regularly has to close early after selling out of its freshly made stock, proving a hit with customers.

Number Eight Bakehouse, in Post House Wynd, has drawn customers from across the area with its artisan sourdough, bread and sweet treats since opening nearly six years ago.

Owner Catalin Cirimpei said the demand for its products means the team is often forced to shut early after its shelves have been cleared.

He said: “Everything is popular, we often close early because we sell out.

Three days a week on average, we close early because we sell out of everything.

  Number 8 Bakehouse in Post House Wynd, Darlington


“We have worked hard over all these years, doing artisan products from scratch, with our lovely bakers.

It shows us that we are on the right track, even if we have had many challenges over the years.

“We are still open, we are still trying, and we do attract new clientele from different towns as well, which is something we really like.

"We love it, actually.”

  Number 8 Bakehouse in Post House Wynd, Darlington


The independent business began after Catalin and his team spotted an empty shop in the town centre which had previously been used as a vape shop.

They transformed the site into a bakery over six to seven months, starting out with just two people and a plan to bring a different kind of bakery to Darlington.

  Number 8 Bakehouse in Post House Wynd, Darlington


Catalin said: "We saw there was no bakery in town offering sourdough and the products we offer at the moment, so we thought it was a good idea to offer that balance for the community as well.”

Since opening, Number Eight Bakehouse has become a familiar part of the town centre, producing its goods from scratch and growing its customer base beyond Darlington.

Catalin said the success of the bakery came down to the people working in it, both behind the scenes and serving customers.

  Number 8 Bakehouse in Post House Wynd, Darlington


He said: “It is the staff who have made the bakery so successful.

"The bakers, the front of house, everybody.

"They all make the bakery what it is.

“We have put a lot into it over the years and we are proud that people keep coming back.

"To see customers supporting us, and to see new people coming from different towns, means a lot to us.”

  Number 8 Bakehouse in Post House Wynd, Darlington


  Number 8 Bakehouse in Post House Wynd, Darlington


Number Eight Bakehouse has now been shortlisted in The Northern Echo’s Best Bakery 2026 competition after being nominated by customers.

It is the second year running the Post House Wynd bakery has made the top ten, after also being shortlisted last year.

Catalin said: “It feels very good to be nominated again by our customers.

It shows us that the work we are doing is appreciated and that we are going in the right direction.”

Readers can vote with coupons printed in the newspaper, choosing their favourite from the 10 finalists.
