  **UPDATE: The A1, between J41 and J39, has reopened.At 1.41pm, National Highways said: “Recovery and debris clear up complete.“All lanes now open. No significant delays reported in the area.”**

DRIVERS in Yorkshire are facing long delays after a crash on a major road left a caravan overturned.

  


National Highways said the A1 is closed southbound between junction 41 for the M62 and junction 39 for Barnsdale Bar and the A6369 near Pontefract.


The A1 gains motorway classification northbound close to the incident.

There are delays of 45 minutes on the approach, the agency said.
