A spectacular firework display will light up the sky in Bishop Auckland this Halloween.

The annual Pyromusical returns on Saturday, October 31, promising thousands of fireworks choreographed to music and a few seasonal surprises.

Taking place on Kynren’s outdoor stage, the 25-minute event is expected to attract about 8,000 visitors and has sold out every year.

A spokesperson for Kynren said: "There is something quite extraordinary about seeing fireworks on this scale choreographed precisely to music.

"You’re not simply watching a traditional fireworks display.

"The soundtrack and fireworks are designed to work together, so the music becomes as much a part of the experience as what is happening in the sky."

With the event falling on Halloween, organisers have promised a few seasonal touches, with fire pits providing an atmospheric glow around the site alongside warming autumn food and drink.

The spokesperson said: "We’re not going to give everything away, but there will be fire pits glowing as darkness falls, plenty of warming food and drink and a few surprises before the fireworks begin."

Gates open at 4.30pm, and the show starts at 7pm.

Standard seating costs £14 for adults and £11 for under-18s, while VIP tickets are £18 and £15 respectively.
