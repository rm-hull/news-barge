A woman secretly recorded her niece's account of being sexually abused in a bid to bring the perpetrator to justice after the teenager said she would deny it if anyone else was told, a court heard.

Mark Faulds is standing trial at Teesside Crown Court, where he denies 14 charges after the teenager made a series of allegations against him.

The 41-year-old was arrested after the teenager led police to evidence, including condoms in the woods, the court heard.

A jury was told that the teenager’s aunt made a secret audio recording after the girl told her she would deny what was alleged if anyone else was told.

The woman, who cannot be named for legal reasons, said: “I needed to tell somebody, and I told her I was going to do that. She said – ‘if you do, I will just deny it’.

“I secretly recorded it before I told anyone so that she couldn’t deny it.”

Prosecutor Annelise Haugstad said Faulds used cigarettes, drugs and compliments to groom the teenager before the abuse escalated.

Forensic expert Laura Lewis previously told the jury that Faulds’ DNA was found in semen on the condom the girl led police to.

She said traces of the teenager’s DNA were also found on the condom, but that alone did not prove intercourse.

Faulds, of Oxford Street, Stanley, denies 14 charges, including rape, assault by penetration and causing a child to engage in sexual activity.
