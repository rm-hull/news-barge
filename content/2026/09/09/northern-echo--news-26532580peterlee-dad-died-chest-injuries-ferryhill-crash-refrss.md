A devoted County Durham dad died from chest injuries after coming off his motorbike in a crash, an inquest has heard.

Sean Forster, from Peterlee, died after crashing his Yamaha 600cc motorbike on the A167 in Ferryhill on August 5.

The 38-year-old, who was described by the biking community as a “wonderful friend”, was pronounced dead at the scene after sustaining numerous injuries.

An inquest opened into his death at Crook Coroners’ Court on Tuesday (September 8) heard how Sean’s cause of death was listed as chest injuries.

  Sean Forster


Dr Sam Hoggard carried out the post mortem at Newcastle’s Freeman Hospital mortuary on August 11.

Senior coroner Jeremy Chipperfield adjourned the inquest until November 10.

Tributes poured in for Hartlepool-born Sean after his death earlier this year.

  Tributes have been left at the scene of a fatal crash on the A167 at Ferryhill


His family described him as a devoted dad to Dylan and a beloved son, stepson and brother.

“Everyone who knows Sean has commented on him being the happiest he’s been in years since joining the biking community, and although this has been an awful tragedy, he has passed doing something he truly loved,” they said.

“We are receiving lots of lovely messages from his biking community all describing Sean as a wonderful friend, which is incredibly heart warming for us to hear given the many obstacles faced over his lifetime.

“We are grateful to them giving him such a wonderful experience these past few months.

“We’d also like to sincerely thank his friends who tried to save him. Our thoughts are with them and everyone who has been affected by this awful incident.”

A female passenger, a woman in her twenties, who was also involved in the crash was left in hospital with life-changing injuries.
