The US military has destroyed five Iranian oil tankers in retaliation for strikes on a Navy warship.

American forces struck the ships — four in the Gulf of Oman and one near Kharg Island — Tuesday after the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps “targeted a U.S. Navy warship with ballistic missiles twice over the past two days”, US Central Command said.

“The U.S. warship successfully evaded the attempted Iranian attacks and continued to patrol regional waters. No American personnel were harmed”, CENTCOM added.

Meanwhile, the Iran-backed Houthi rebels have attacked several cities in Saudi Arabia, wounding at least 73 people and knocking operations at energy facilities offline, the Saudi-led coalition said Tuesday in a further sign of the conflict spreading as diplomacy stalls. The coalition vowed a firm response to the attacks.

Houthi spokesperson Yahya Sarea said that the Yemeni group would soon announce a broad military operation “deep” into Saudi Arabia.

The Houthis, who control the most populous parts of Yemen and much of the north, have been launching attacks towards Saudi Arabia since they declared a naval blockade against Riyadh in July, with strikes targeting its vessels in the Red Sea.

## Iran warns tankers near ports in Bahrain and Kuwait

Iran has warned oil tanker crews near ports in Kuwait and Bahrain to leave their vessels, saying the ships could be targeted in retaliation for the US attacks.

The warning from Iran’s Revolutionary Guards came after the US military struck multiple Iranian tankers in the Gulf of Oman and near Iran’s Kharg Island.

The developments come as Iran maintains a blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, through which about one-fifth of global oil and gas shipments normally pass.

The US has also imposed a blockade of Iranian ports.

The latest escalation has already pushed oil prices sharply higher, with crude approaching $100 a barrel in early Asian trading on Wednesday.

## Iran’s Revolutionary Guard says it attacked 2 US vessels and 8 oil tankers

Iran’s Revolutionary Guard said on Wednesday that it had attacked two US vessels and eight oil tankers in the Gulf in response to what they described as a US attack on five Iranian tankers, Iranian state media reported.

The Guard said they had also attacked 10 ships attempting to cross what they described as a “prohibited and unsafe” area of the Strait of Hormuz.

## Iran war will continue for another six months, former US defence secretary says

The US war with Iran is likely to last another six months, according to former defence secretary Leon Panetta.

Six months after US-Israeli strikes against Iran, the war has reached a stalemate after a preliminary ceasefire plan unravelled.

Little progress has been made in diplomatic efforts to get the peace process back on track, after negotiations broke down in June.

Mr Panetta, who was defence secretary under President Barack Obama and has also served as director of the CIA, said in an interview that Donald Trump’s war in Iran risks becoming another “forever war in the Middle East”.

## US strikes Iranian tankers after Iran targets US warship

The US military has destroyed multiple Iranian oil tankers after Iran twice attempted to strike an American navy warship with ballistic missiles, marking a sharp escalation in hostilities between Washington and Tehran.

US Central Command said American forces struck the vessels in the Gulf of Oman and near Iran’s Kharg Island after directing their crews to abandon ship.

The strikes came after Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps targeted a US Navy warship with ballistic missiles on two occasions over the past two days.

“The US warship successfully evaded the attempted Iranian attacks and continued to patrol regional waters. No American personnel were harmed,” US Centcom said.

## Iran War is costing Americans $100B in extra energy costs

The war in Iran is costing Americans $100 billion in extra energy costs — and that number is going up by $1 million every two minutes, according to an estimate from Brown University.

The Iran War Energy Cost Tracker from Brown’s Watson School of International and Public Affairs has tracked the price paid for gasoline and diesel since the U.S. and Israel launched joint strikes on Iran on February 28.

That added cost boils down to about $763 per household, based on an estimated 131 million households nationwide.

## Russia offers to mediate in Middle East crisis

Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov told his Saudi counterpart Prince Faisal bin Farhan Al-Saud in Moscow on Tuesday that Russia was ready to help resolve the situation in the Middle East.

Prince Faisal said Saudi Arabia was keen to deepen cooperation with Russia, including within the OPEC+ alliance, during talks between the two countries' top diplomats.
