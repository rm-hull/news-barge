THE Princess Proms will bring magical music to Bradford for one afternoon performance this weekend.

The concert will take place at St George's Hall this Sunday, September 13, beginning at 2:00pm.

Audiences can expect a show-stopping tribute to iconic, heartwarming and nostalgic songs from popular animations and musical favourites.

The programme includes music from Frozen, Wicked, K-Pop Demon Hunters, Moana, The Wizard of Oz, Beauty & The Beast, Anastasia, Pocahontas, Aladdin, The Little Mermaid, The Lion King, Willy Wonka & the Chocolate Factory, and more.

The performance will also feature songs from Taylor Swift, Coldplay and Rachel Platte.

The show has received an incredible response, with sold-out shows across the country. The stars have performed on the West End, ITV, for Royalty, and more, before touring this concert for audiences.

Tickets are priced from £22.00 to £43.00 for standard admission and can be purchased HERE.

Only a few seats remain, making this an opportunity for families and animation fans to enjoy a live celebration of familiar songs.
