Two people were taken to hospital after a house fire in Saltburn.

Emergency services were called at 3.20pm on Tuesday, September 8, following a blaze on Hilda Place.

Paramedics from the North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) confirmed they attended the incident.

A spokesperson said: "We were called to a fire at a private address in Saltburn at 3.40pm on Tuesday, September 8.

"We dispatched one ambulance crew and two clinical team leaders. Two patients were taken to James Cook University Hospital for further treatment."

Cleveland Fire Brigade said five fire engines attended the scene.

A spokesperson confirmed: “Cleveland Fire Brigade were called on Tuesday, September 8, at 3.22pm to an incident at Hilda Place, Saltburn.

"Five appliances currently in attendance."

The road was closed while crews dealt with the blaze.

Cleveland Police also attended the incident.

A force spokesperson said: "At 3.20pm on Tuesday, September 8, police were called to assist with traffic following reports of a fire at a property on Hilda Place in Saltburn.

"Two people were treated for smoke inhalation and taken to hospital.

"The fire is not believed to be deliberate."
