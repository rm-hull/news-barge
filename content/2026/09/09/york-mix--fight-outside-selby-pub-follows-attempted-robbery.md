Police are investigating a fight outside a pub in Selby.

It happened near The Bay Horse on Micklegate at around 3pm on Friday 29 August.

Both men and women were involved.

#### The Bay Horse, Micklegate, Selby

Officers are linking it to an attempted robbery that took place an hour earlier.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The affray involved an altercation between a number of men and women.

“We are particularly keen to hear from any witnesses who were in the area at the time and saw either incident, or who may have any information that could assist our enquiries.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
