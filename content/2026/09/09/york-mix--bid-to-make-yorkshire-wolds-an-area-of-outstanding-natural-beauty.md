A new Yorkshire Wolds ‘Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty’ (AONB) could be created after an official organisation submitted a bid to the government.

Natural England has submitted the proposal to Secretary of State for the Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, Dame Angela Eagle.

If the bid is successful, it will designate the Yorkshire Wolds as an AONB which will ensure legal protection and conservation of the region’s landscape, wildlife, and heritage. This will, in particular, protect planning and development in the much-loved part of East Yorkshire.

The new designation, which will officially make the Yorkshire Wolds a ‘National Landscape’, will give local authorities greater weight in protecting scenic beauty, meaning major building projects are much harder to get approved.

It will also benefit from the potential of bespoke management plans and coordinated partnerships to restore habitats, protect rare species, and fight biodiversity loss.

The proposal could also carefully balance environmental care with the social and economic needs of rural communities, supporting sustainable farming, forestry, and rural jobs.

There could be a big economic boost for East Yorkshire through increased tourism too. AONBs are better placed to promote walking and outdoor pursuits, bringing visitors and financial support to local businesses while protecting the environment from overuse.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/councillor-anne-handley-leader-east-riding-of-yorkshire-council.webp)

East Riding of Yorkshire Council leader Cllr Anne Handley welcomed the progress.

She said: “This is a key milestone, and we’re delighted that Natural England are submitting it to the Secretary of State. The Yorkshire Wolds are a stunning area of natural landscapes that fully deserve national recognition.”

The Secretary of State may confirm the order as submitted, make changes or refuse it, pending a public inquiry. If the Secretary of State approves the order, then the designation will come into force.

Natural England found that 78% of respondents to its consultation stated that they agreed that the proposed inland area should be designated as an AONB, and 85% agreed for the coastal area.

Natural England stated that there is a clear weight of evidence that the proposed inland and coastal areas meet the criterion of outstanding natural beauty required for AONB designation.

The news could be transformational for the region’s growing tourism industry and would connect the Yorkshire Wolds with new audiences, according to Chris Blacksell, Visit Hull & East Yorkshire Advisory Board chair.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/yorkshire-wolds-ldrs.webp)

“Achieving Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty status for the Yorkshire Wolds would be a hugely positive development for tourism across Hull and East Yorkshire,” he said.

“Our visitor economy is worth around £1 billion to the local economy, and the Yorkshire Wolds are an important part of what makes our destination so distinctive and appealing to visitors.

“The landscapes, villages, walking and cycling opportunities, local food and drink and wealth of experiences across the Wolds provide an important complement to the attractions of Hull and our coastal communities.

“This is another important step in putting East Yorkshire firmly on the tourism map, and we look forward to seeing the opportunities that AONB status could bring.”
