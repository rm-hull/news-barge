  **UPDATE: The A1079 Market Weighton bypass is now running normally.**

AN 'OBSTRUCTION' is blocking a major road bringing traffic into York this morning (Wednesday, September 9).

Reports are coming in of a blockage and traffic is said to be running slowly on the A1079 outside Market Weighton, on the bypass.

The road is the principal thoroughfare for traffic coming into and out of York for motorists in Hull and Beverley.

The obstruction is on the eastbound section of the town bypass - Giant Bradley Way - between Holme Road and the turn off for North Cliffe, according to AA Traffic News.

The news comes as speed limits are currently restricted to 30mph from 60mph on the same road closer to York on the outskirts of Dunnington as part of a two-week road resurfacing project.
