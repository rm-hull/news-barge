# Emotional moment as Alan Carr honoured at National Television Awards

Alan Carr has said he still feels bad about murdering singer Paloma Faith in the 2025 series of The Celebrity Traitors, as he picked up an NTA award on Tuesday night.

The comedian appeared emotional as he approached the stage to collect his Special Recognition award at the 31st National Television Awards on Tuesday night.

"I did feel bad because I'd murdered people," he said.

"I still felt bad about Paloma."

The Special Recognition award was the only category not voted for by the public.
