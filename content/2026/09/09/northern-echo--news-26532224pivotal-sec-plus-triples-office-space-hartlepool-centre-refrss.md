A business in Hartlepool has tripled its office space following a major acquisition and rapid growth.

Pivotal Sec Plus, the UK and Ireland’s largest independent provider of cash management and ATM services, has expanded its base at the UKSE Innovation Centre after acquiring Security Plus + Limited in October 2024.

The company, formerly known as Pivotal, has more than 6,000 clients and is currently recruiting for additional roles in Hartlepool to support its continued expansion.

Julieanne Crawford, head of customer service at Pivotal Sec Plus, said: "Our new, larger office in UKSE’s Innovation Centre provides the perfect base for us.

"It is close to main roads connecting the whole of the North East and beyond, and it also provides us with high-quality offices and facilities.

"The Hartlepool office is crucial to our business, as it houses our customer service and sales team, which has been growing rapidly since the acquisition in 2024, so it is fantastic that we have been able to increase our footprint at the Innovation Centre."

Ms Crawford said the firm more than doubled in size overnight following the acquisition.

She said: "When you compared Pivotal and Security Plus + Limited, Pivotal was the smaller of the two businesses, buying the larger business.

"We are continuously looking to grow — and Pivotal Sec Plus is currently recruiting more sales and customer service staff in Hartlepool, who are the hub of the business."

The company supports a wide range of businesses, including corner shops, large retail stores, and financial institutions.

Sarah Thorpe, regional manager for the North at UKSE, said: "We congratulate Pivotal Sec Plus on the move to bigger premises within our Hartlepool Innovation Centre, which reflects their growth and ambition and our ability to meet the needs of growing businesses with the facilities we offer.

"It is always pleasing to see the firms working within our offices grow and do well, and we will look forward to welcoming some more new staff joining Pivotal Sec Plus in Hartlepool in the near future."
