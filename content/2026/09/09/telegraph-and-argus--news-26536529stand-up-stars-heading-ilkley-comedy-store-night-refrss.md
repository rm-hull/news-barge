COMEDY fans in Ilkley can look forward to an evening of live stand-up this weekend.

The Comedy Store Ilkley will take place at King's Hall Ilkley this Saturday, September 12, with the performance beginning at 8:00pm.

Presented by Live Nation and The Comedy Store, the event will showcase the best in stand-up from around the globe, featuring some of the brightest names in comedy today.

With nearly 40 years’ experience in the business, The Comedy Store is renowned as a breeding ground for new comedy talent and remains a place to see tomorrow’s stars today.

The line-up includes Eddy Brimson, Darius Davies, Jenny Hart and Garrett Millerick.

The show offers an opportunity to enjoy live performances from comedians with a range of experience, making it a tempting choice for those seeking a night of laughter in Ilkley.

Tickets cost £20.50. The standard ticket price is listed for the event, and the age guidance is 16+.

A maximum of 10 tickets can be booked online HERE, while discounts are subject to availability and may not be applicable.
