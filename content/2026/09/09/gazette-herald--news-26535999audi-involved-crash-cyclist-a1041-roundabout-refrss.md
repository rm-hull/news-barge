A cyclist was taken to hospital after a crash involving a vehicle in North Yorkshire.

The incident took place on the A1041 at the roundabout with the A645, close to Camblesforth, at around 3pm on Sunday (September 6).

It involved a grey Audi, police said.

The cyclist, who was taken to hospital with injuries, continues to receive treatment.

North Yorkshire Police said: "As part of our enquiries, we are keen to speak to the driver of the grey Audi involved.

"It is possible the driver may be unaware a collision occurred, despite contact between the vehicle and the cyclist.

"The vehicle did not stop at the scene."

Emergency services attended the scene, and the road was partially closed for several hours.

Officers are appealing for witnesses, dashcam footage, or anyone who saw the grey Audi in the area before the incident to come forward.

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC Ben Bowker by emailing [ben.bowker@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ben.bowker@northyorkshire.police.uk) or by visiting the North Yorkshire Police website and selecting ‘Tell Us About’ followed by ‘An existing case or report.’
