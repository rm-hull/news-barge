POLICE have issued an update after a man was taken to hospital following a reported serious assault in Bradford city centre.

Emergency services were called to Upper Millergate, off Sunbridge Road, at around 4pm on Tuesday.

An update has now confirmed that the man suffered minor injuries and has been discharged from hospital.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Man discharged from hospital after assault sparks police cordon in Bradford city centre."

## Emergency services attended scene

Police and ambulance crews rushed to the area after reports of the incident.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police previously said: "A man has been taken to hospital following a report of a serious assault."

At the time, the force said it had no update on the man's injuries.

## Police cordon put in place

A police cordon was established around the scene on Upper Millergate while officers carried out enquiries.

Yesterday, a spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service confirmed that the patient was taken to hospital.

They said: "Ambulance resources were dispatched to an assault incident on Sunbridge Road, Bradford, just before 4pm on Tuesday afternoon.

"One patient was conveyed to hospital."

Enquiries into the incident remain ongoing.
