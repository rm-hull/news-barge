KEVAN Carling is determined to complete a 5km charity walk after breaking his leg in seven places.

Kevan, 58, is determined to complete the 5km charity walk despite breaking his leg in seven places after tripping over his cat.

The 58-year-old will take part in Sue Ryder’s Starlight Hike Worth Valley on Saturday October 3, 2026, one year after the death of his wife, Kat.

He will walk under the stars with relatives and friends to remember Kat and raise money for Sue Ryder, which runs Sue Ryder Manorlands Hospice near Oxenhope.

  Kevan Carling with his wife Kat 

*(Image: Hand-in)*

Kat, a retail worker, died aged 57 on October 11 – Mr Carling’s birthday – six months after the couple married.

The pair had been together for about 16 years. They lived in Oxenhope for 14 years before moving to Cross Roads, in the Bradford district.

Kat was diagnosed with advanced lung cancer after experiencing chest pains during summer 2025. By the time she went into hospital in September, the cancer had spread to her back and hip.

She was later transferred to Sue Ryder Manorlands Hospice, where she received specialist end-of-life care during her final weeks.

  Kevan and Kat on their wedding day 

*(Image: Hand-in)*

Mr Carling said: "It was an understandably difficult time towards the end of Kat's life."

He said the hospice gave Kat the care, dignity and compassion she deserved, while also supporting him and their family.

Mr Carling said: "But I kept thinking to myself, ‘There’s nowhere better that Kat could be at this time, given what she’s going through'."

  Kevan Carling will take part in the 5km route under the stars on Saturday 3 October, to remember his wife Kat, alongside family and close friends. 

*(Image: Hand-in)*

He said Kat received massages, while relatives were given advice and support to help them understand and cope with her illness.

Mr Carling said: "There was nothing that was too much trouble."

He added: "The information we were given was brilliant. It was direct, but it was also sensitive. It was all aimed at making sure that myself, my family and Kat were as comfortable and understanding as possible with the horrible situation."

Kat became seriously unwell while the couple were preparing for a honeymoon in Fuerteventura. She had asked Mr Carling to marry her there in February 2024, and the island held special meaning for them.

Her illness meant she could not return. Mr Carling went there in March 2026 with family and friends, where they scattered some of her ashes.

Mr Carling said: "Kat was just a wonderful mum."

He added: "She was a brilliant partner. She was a brilliant wife. Nothing was ever too much trouble for her."

The decision to enter the hike was made while Kat was receiving care.

Mr Carling said: "It was a spur-of-the-moment decision, but we thought, right, let’s start doing everything that we can to give back. And there and then we committed to do the Starlight Hike."

However, he broke his leg after tripping over his cat and falling down the stairs at home in May this year. He spent weeks in hospital and now has an external frame supporting his leg.

  Kevan's leg was broken in seven places 

*(Image: Hand-in)*

Doctors expect him to need the frame for between six and 12 months.

Mr Carling said: "After my injury I kept asking, ‘Are we still doing the walk?’ because I didn’t know if I could do it."

He said his family and friends encouraged him to continue and would help him complete the route.

Mr Carling said: "I want to do it for Kat. I want to do it for Sue Ryder. I’ll get round one way or another. Even if it's Sunday night by the time I get to the finish line."

He will be joined by his children Katie and Robbie, his son’s girlfriend Emily, Kat’s daughter Hollie and her husband Ryan, and friends Matt and Hazel.

The event will offer 5km and 10km routes through the Worth Valley, including the grounds of Manorlands Hospice.

Sue Ryder provides palliative and end-of-life care for people with terminal illnesses. It also offers online bereavement support, including a bereavement community and advice on coping with grief.

Mr Carling said: "She’d be proud that I’m still doing it."

He added: "But this is probably one of the most important causes I’ve ever been involved with."

People can register at sueryder.org/StarlightWorthValley. Donations can be made at Mr Carling’s fundraising page: justgiving.com/page/kevan-carling-1.
