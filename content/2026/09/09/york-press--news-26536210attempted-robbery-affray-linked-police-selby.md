Police are appealing for information after an attempted robbery and an affray in North Yorkshire.

Both incidents took place on Friday (August 29) in Selby, and are being treated as linked.

The attempted robbery took place before the reported affray, which occurred at around 3pm in Micklegate, near The Bay Horse pub.

  


It involved an altercation between several men and women, North Yorkshire Police said.

A force spokesperson said: "We are particularly keen to hear from any witnesses who were in the area at the time and saw either incident, or who may have any information that could assist our enquiries."

Anyone with information is urged to contact police online by emailing [jack.sykes@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:jack.sykes@northyorkshire.police.uk) or by using the live chat service on the North Yorkshire Police website, available Monday to Friday from 9am to 5pm, quoting reference number 12260169293.

If you can't use these options, call 101.
