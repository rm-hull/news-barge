Your Harrogate has been given a first look inside the town's newest interior design shop ahead of its opening this week.

Upside Down Design will welcome customers to its new West Park store for the first time on Friday (11th September).

Finishing touches are now being made to colourful displays of furniture, lighting, home accessories and gifts.

The Harrogate branch, located next to Boho Chic, will be the independent retailer’s second store.

Upside Down Design has operated from Bootham in York since 2017 and has built a reputation for its distinctive homeware and vintage-inspired pieces.

Alongside its retail range, the new store will offer interior design advice and services including room planning, made-to-measure curtains, blinds, cushions and upholstery.

The collection also includes soft furnishings, tableware, candles, jewellery, beauty products, books and children’s gifts.

The business first announced its plans to expand into Harrogate in June, with five new roles created as part of the move.
