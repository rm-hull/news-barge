Harrogate Town have signed goalkeeper Luke Hutchinson on a three-month loan from Bolton Wanderers.

The 24-year-old joined Bolton at the age of eight and signed his first professional contract with the League One club in 2021.

He gained senior experience during loan spells with Atherton Collieries, Bamber Bridge and Marine before playing in League One for Crawley Town.

Hutchinson signed a new two-year deal with Bolton in the summer of 2025, extending his association with the club beyond a decade.

He spent last season on loan at Altrincham, making 44 appearances and winning both the Players’ Player and Young Player of the Year awards.

Hutchinson will wear the number 34 shirt for Harrogate Town.

He will be available for Saturday’s match against Tamworth, subject to approval from the FA and National League.
