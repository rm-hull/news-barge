Hundreds of classic cars are expected at a rally in York.

Around 400 vehicles are due to take to York Racecourse for the York Historic Vehicle Group’s annual event from 10am on Sunday (September 13).

David Walker, who helps organise the event, said there will also be lorries, buses and motorcycles on show.

He said 17 different car clubs will turn out at the racecourse for the event’s 48th year on Sunday.

There will also be food and drinks for sale.

“The forecast looks promising so I expect we will get a lot of late entries and all of them will help to increase the donations that we make to the various charities we support each year,” David said.

Entry is £5 for adults, £4 for OAPs and children under 16 go free.

The event comes after more than 400 classic vehicles turned out for last year’s rally at York Racecourse.

Last year’s event raised thousands of pounds for local charities.
