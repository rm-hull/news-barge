A man is due to to appear in court charged with 20 offences following a spate of burglaries and attempted burglaries across Darlington.

The incidents reportedly took place in the early hours of Bank Holiday Monday, August 31, in the Whinfield, North Road and Haughton Road areas.

Extensive enquiries led to the arrest of a 22-year-old man from Middlesbrough.

He has since been charged with four counts of burglary, seven counts of attempted burglary with intent to steal, five counts of vehicle interference, three counts of theft from a motor vehicle and one theft of a motor vehicle.

The man has been remanded in custody and is due to appear at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court today, September 9.

Police said that many of the properties and cars targeted had been left unlocked overnight.

Inspector David Littlefair, from Darlington Neighbourhood Police Team, said: “Many of the properties and cars that were targeted had been left unlocked overnight.

“We’d strongly advise all residents to make their homes and property as secure as possible – the more difficult you make it for the burglar, the less likely they are to bother.

“Please make sure you lock your door and shut your windows on a night, or when you are leaving your house.

“Lock your vehicles and store the keys securely and out of sight, away from windows or doors where thieves can see them.

“If you have a garage, use it and consider installing CCTV cameras and security lighting to the front and back of your home.”
