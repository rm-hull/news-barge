![The front page headline of the Times reads: "Farage faces Met inquiry over Reform donations."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/809/cpsprodpb/ce2a/live/30a28d60-ac95-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

A criminal investigation into Reform UK over allegations it broke foreign funding laws is leading many of Thursday's front pages. The Metropolitan Police is carrying out the inquiry following a broadcast by Channel 4 News last week in which an undercover reporter recorded two senior Reform aides apparently suggesting a way around electoral law to secure foreign party donations. A Reform UK spokesman says the party "denies any wrongdoing and will fully cooperate with the investigation".

![The front page headline of the Guardian reads: "Police launch inquiry into alleged overseas donations to Reform UK."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/820/cpsprodpb/36e4/live/980dc5a0-ac95-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

The Guardian splashes on the same story, reporting that the Electoral Commission has also said it is "considering all relevant information and regulatory steps" in parallel with the police inquiry.

![The front page headline of the i Paper reads: "Police set to interview Farage over Reform UK donations."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/830/cpsprodpb/b477/live/6d1010b0-ac95-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

Police are set to interview Reform UK leader Nigel Farage as part of the donations investigation, according to the i Paper. A Met spokesperson says it received "a number of reports relating to donations and polling involving a political party", after the film by Channel 4 News and Verbatim Investigations was aired last Thursday.

![The front page headline of the Mirror reads: "Farage faces cop quiz on donors."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/148c/live/ade559f0-aca0-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

"Another day, another probe..." the Mirror declares on its front page as it also reports that Farage is facing a "police grilling over potential donation offences". The Met had already been investigating whether payments to Reform in the weeks before the last election complied with donation laws. It is also examining payments from a company controlled by Reform's deputy leader Richard Tice, which received £1m in June 2024.

![The front page headline of the Independent reads: "Met begins criminal inquiry into foreign Reform donations."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1536/cpsprodpb/2f26/live/f317a140-ac96-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

As well as the investigation into Reform's donations, the Independent covers the airport chaos in the UK on its front page. The number of flights cancelled after a technical failure of the UK's air traffic control system rose above 2,000 on Wednesday - and the UK Civil Aviation Authority says passengers are unlikely to be entitled to compensation.

![The front page headline of the Metro reads: "Ready for departure?"](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/828/cpsprodpb/b6d5/live/0147d930-ac90-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

"Ready for departure?" asks the Metro on its front page, saying the boss of National Air Traffic Services (Nats) is on the brink. Martin Rolfe, who has led Nats for 11 years, is facing pressure after the latest computer glitch affected 330,000 passengers.

![The front page headline of the Daily Telegraph reads: "Labour's new tax assault on hospitality."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/ea1d/live/85c1a100-ac95-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

Labour is planning a "new tax assault on hospitality", according to the Daily Telegraph. The paper reports that Prime Minister Andy Burnham will hand mayors the power to impose unlimited taxes on tourists that could lead to families paying hundreds of pounds more on holidays in England.

![The front page headline of the Financial Times reads: "Anthropic held back frontier AI model from British checks"](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1056/cpsprodpb/c210/live/f90cea30-ac8f-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

The Financial Times says Anthropic excluded Britain's AI Security Institute (Aisi) from tests on its latest model, with the government fearing it is a result of the US government's artificial intelligence (AI) protectionism. The $1tn tech company only made Claude Mythos 5.1 available to vetted US bodies.

![The front page headline of the Daily Star reads: "Hasta la vista."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/9c3d/live/9501baf0-ac91-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

"Hasta la vista," declares the Daily Star, which also leads its front page on Anthropic. It says out-of-control AI will lead to the apocalypse, with the dire warning coming from an Anthropic researcher, Jacob Coxon, who quit the company fearing the tech arms race is moving too quickly.

![The front page headline of the Daily Express reads: "The King has spoken with Harry since return"](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/3429/live/f0182110-ac8f-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

The Daily Express reports that the Duke of Sussex and King Charles III have spoken since Harry returned to the UK, contrary to a report that suggested there had been no contact. The paper notes, however, that the pair have not reunited in person.

![The front page headline of the Daily Mail reads: "Burnham: National security can't come at the expense of social security"](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/810/cpsprodpb/f3e5/live/551af290-ac95-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

Labour has been branded the "Welfare Party" by Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch, according to the Daily Mail, after Burnham said he would not prioritise defence spending over social security. Writing in the Mail, Badenoch criticised the admission in Prime Minister's Questions, saying Burnham had "said the quiet part out loud".

The Financial Times says the artificial intelligence firm Anthropic declined to submit its latest test models, external to Britain's AI Security Institute. Its report notes that vetted US bodies were given access, prompting alarm in Whitehall. The Daily Star focuses on the comments from Jacob Coxon, external, a former employee of the company, who warned the technology could kill all humans by the end of the decade. In a nod to the sci-fi movie, The Terminator, its headline reads "Hasta la vista".

The Daily Telegraph focuses on what it calls Labour's "new tax assault on hospitality", external - with mayors in England being given the power to impose unlimited levies on tourists. The paper says senior figures in the sector were surprised by the announcement, having initially believed the tax would be capped. One unnamed source is quoted as saying the plans will create a "jobs bloodbath".

The police inquiry into Reform UK leads the Guardian and the Daily Mirror. The Guardian says the financial scandal has deepened, external - noting that the investigation will form part of an existing inquiry into Reform's political funding. "Farage faces cop quiz on donors,", external reads the Mirror's headline. The party calls that "pure speculation" in the i Paper.

The Daily Mail says Labour has been branded the "welfare party", external by the Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch after the prime minister said he would never prioritise defence spending over benefits. The report calls it a "staggering admission". Writing in the paper, Badenoch says Andy Burnham said "the quiet part out loud". In a similar vein, the Telegraph says Burnham "let the cat out of the bag", external.

The Metro leads on the problems at air traffic control, external and calls for the head of National Air Traffic Services (Nats) to step down with its headline: "Ready for departure?" Its front page features a picture of Martin Rolfe, as well as stranded passengers sleeping at Heathrow airport.

The Duke of Sussex and King Charles III have spoken since Prince Harry returned to the UK with his family, according to the Daily Express, external. It quotes royal sources who say that, despite conflicting reports, father and son spoke to each other in the first week of the latter's arrival in the Cotswolds.
