**Red Kite Teacher Training** has secured a**Strong Standard** rating across all aspects of its primary and secondary provision following its latest inspection under Ofsted’s new Initial Teacher Education framework.

The achievement marks a double milestone for the partnership as it celebrates its **tenth year** as a School Centred Initial Teacher Training (SCITT) provider. Over the past decade, the organization has trained hundreds of educators entering the profession across the region.



![red kite harrogate](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ofsted-Outcomes-Graphic.webp)



## Inspectors Praise Mentoring and Collaboration Culture

Ofsted inspectors commended the quality of training, mentoring, and support embedded across the Red Kite partnership network. The official report highlighted the strength of both the primary and secondary training tracks, noting how effectively trainees are prepared for direct classroom teaching and long-term education careers.

The inspection team also drew attention to the core culture driving the programme, praising its focus on active collaboration, continuous structural improvement, and strong personal support systems available to trainees throughout their workload.

## Director Commends Partnership Network

![Victoria Lickley](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Victoria-Lickley.webp)


**Victoria Lickley, Director of Red Kite Teacher Training, said:**

We are delighted with this outcome and proud that the quality of our work has been recognised through Ofsted’s new inspection framework. As we celebrate ten years of Red Kite Teacher Training, this recognition reflects the expertise, commitment and generosity of our partner schools, mentors, Early Career Leaders and wider team.

We were particularly pleased to see Ofsted recognise the values of wise compassion and professional generosity that continue to shape our partnership. Most importantly, this outcome reflects the dedication of our trainees and the positive impact they go on to make in classrooms, schools and communities throughout their careers. We would like to thank everyone who has contributed to our success over the past decade and look forward to building on this success in the years ahead.
