A MAN has been found dead at his home in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is assisting the Coroner’s Office following the sad death of Glenn Hardy, 69, who passed away in Scarborough on Monday (September 7).

  


Police are keen to find Glenn’s next of kin.

If you have any information that help the Coroner locate member’s of his family, please email [coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.)
