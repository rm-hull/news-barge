An Airbnb that has “panoramic views” of the Yorkshire countryside has been named one of the most popular in the UK.

Condé Nast Traveller has revealed the most wishlisted Airbnbs across the UK from the last 12 months.

The list includes cosy countryside cabins and studio rooms that overlook the seaside as well as one that is easy to reach from Bradford.

The Mallard at Baywood Cabins in Cowling has made it onto the list thanks to its “stunning wide views” and “wood-fired hot tub”.

  You can enjoy some relaxation at the Airbnb. 

*(Image: Courtesy of the Airbnb Community)*

## What is The Mallard hut like?

On the hut, Condé Nast Traveller said: “A babbling fresh spring runs around this secluded timber hut, which is settled high on the edge of the Yorkshire Dales, showcasing stunning wide views that stretch in a panorama across the lush green valley.

“This retreat is made for slowing down, with days here revolving around getting as much fresh air as possible and enjoying some simple home comforts.

“Fire up the outdoor wood-fired hot tub and settle in to watch the sky turn gold to pink over the hills, or curl up inside next to the crackling log stove after a long day rambling on the selection of pretty local hiking routes.

“There’s a slew of cutesy nearby village pubs to work your way through, too.”

The hut can be found in the middle of the Yorkshire countryside, offering guests the chance to enjoy some peace, quiet, and fresh air.

You can either stay at home and relax in the hot tub or take a walk around the many paths surrounding Baywood.


The hut features a dining area, some kitchen appliances, a sizeable bed, and an ensuite featuring a shower, sink, and toilet.

The Bothy is another cabin found close to the Mallard and can be booked for those wanting to spend time with friends across both huts.

### What do guests say about the hut in Cowling?

Many people who have stayed at the Airbnb have left reviews about their trip.
