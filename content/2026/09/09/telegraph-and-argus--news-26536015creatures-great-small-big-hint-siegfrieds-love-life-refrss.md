All Creatures Great and Small season seven will bring Siegfried Farnon closer to possible love on Channel 5 this September.

Fans will want to see what happens between Siegfried, played by Samuel West, and Dorothy, played by Maimie McCoy.

They last appeared together kissing under the mistletoe at The Drovers during the 2025 Christmas special.

Channel 5 will show series seven later this month.

Actor Samuel West recently gave a massive hint when he said: "There's a brilliant, attractive, clever, funny, stylish woman who looks at me with regard and possibly even love.

"She’s been out of my life for five years, and not through any fault of my own, unusually with Siegfriend, who has upset a few in the past.

"So it must be love."
