A woman has died following an incident which led to the overnight closure of a stretch of the M62 which disrupted motorists across the county.

West Yorkshire Police said an off-duty officer came across a 33-year-old woman on the wrong side of railings above the eastbound carriageway at 11.42pm on Tuesday night.

Police said the M62 was closed in both directions while specialist negotiators attended.

Despite efforts to help the woman, she fell from a height.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Sadly, the woman fell from height and despite medical assistance, she died at the scene."

Following the incident, the M62 was closed in both directions between Junction 25 at Brighouse and Junction 22 at Denshaw.

National Highways first reported the closure at around 2am on Wednesday morning, describing it as a police-led incident.

Further updates throughout the early hours confirmed the motorway remained closed while emergency services attended and enquiries were carried out and diversions were put in place for motorists.

By around 5am, National Highways said the road remained closed as the incident continued.

An hour later, the westbound carriageway had reopened, although lane one of the eastbound carriageway remained closed.

Motorists faced delays of around 20 minutes, with congestion stretching for approximately two miles.

The motorway later reopened fully after the scene was cleared and enquiries were completed.

West Yorkshire Police said the woman's death is not being treated as suspicious and that the coroner has been informed.

National Highways confirmed all lanes had reopened shortly before 7am and that there were no remaining delays in the area.
