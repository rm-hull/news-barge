The next of kin of a woman found dead in York has come forward.

North Yorkshire Police has thanked those who aided a Coroner’s Office appeal to trace the family of a woman, 59, who sadly passed away in the city last month.

Police say her death was not being treated as suspicious.

  


A force spokesperson said: “"Earlier today, on behalf of the Coroner’s Office, we appealed for help to trace the next of kin of a 59-year-old woman who died in York last month.

"We’re pleased to confirm that her next of kin have now been located."
