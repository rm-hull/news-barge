North Yorkshire Police have launched an urgent public appeal to locate 26-year-old Ethan Anderson, who is currently wanted on immediate recall to prison. Officers have turned to the community for assistance following extensive, ongoing enquiries to track his whereabouts across the region after he breached the explicit conditions of his release licence.

Investigating officers believe that Anderson may currently be operating within the immediate Minskip or Boroughbridge areas. Police tracking teams have confirmed that he also maintains strong operational links to several nearby North Yorkshire locations, including Ripon, Knaresborough, and Skipton.

## How to Report Sightings and Information

Members of the public who possess any information regarding his current whereabouts, or who have seen him recently, are urged to contact North Yorkshire Police immediately by calling the non-emergency 101 number.

For individuals who spot Anderson directly and have an active, immediate sighting, police advise bypassing standard lines and calling emergency services straight away on **999**.

All information provided directly to the police must reference log number **12260171970** to ensure it reaches the correct tracking team without delay.

## Anonymity Guaranteed via Crimestoppers

Those who wish to provide intelligence but prefer to stay completely anonymous can contact the independent charity Crimestoppers. Information can be passed securely by calling their freephone intelligence line on **0800 555 111** or by completing an anonymous reporting form on the official Crimestoppers website.
