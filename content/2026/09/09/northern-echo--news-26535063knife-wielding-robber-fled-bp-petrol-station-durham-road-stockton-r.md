A 'knife-wielding' robber fled a petrol station after targeting the business shortly after midnight.

Police were called to a robbery at the BP garage on Durham Road in Stockton, shortly after 12am on Wednesday (September 9).

The man was believed to have been armed with a knife.

He fled the scene before officers arrived.

No one was injured, and an investigation has been launched.

Chief Inspector Andrew Evans from Cleveland Police said: "Shortly after 12am on Wednesday 9 September police were called to a robbery at a petrol station on Durham Road in Stockton.

"The man, who was believed to be armed with a knife, fled prior to officers arriving at the scene. There were no reports of any injuries.

"Anyone with information or footage is asked to call Cleveland Police on 101 quoting reference number 180980."
