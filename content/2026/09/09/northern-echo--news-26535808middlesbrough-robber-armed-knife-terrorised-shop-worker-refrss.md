An armed robber who terrorised a shop worker with a large knife before stealing three bottles of gin has been locked up.

Jason Dixon demanded cash from the woman, but she told him to ‘p*** off’ because she recognised him, Teesside Crown Court heard.

The 50-year-old ignored her plea and forced his way behind the counter to steal the booze from the Go Local store in Middlesbrough.

Emma Atkinson, prosecuting, said Dixon was attempting to hide his identity but the woman immediately recognised him when he entered the shop on June 15.

She said: “The defendant approached the till and demanded – ‘give me the money out of the till’ – her initial response was to tell him to ‘p*** off’ as she thought he was joking.

“However, he pulled out a knife from his pocket and waved it around the serving hatch. She managed to press the alarm and told him she knew who he was and asked him why he was doing this.”

The court heard how the woman grabbed a small stool and a bat to try and protect herself before Dixon came behind the till and stole three bottles of gin.

A discarded knife found near the scene was handed to police.

The court heard how the defendant had 20 convictions for 34 offences, including for robbery, burglary and violence.

Dixon, of Falkland Street, Middlesbrough, pleaded guilty to robbery and possession of a bladed article following his arrest.

Ralf Xhepa, defending, said Dixon had stayed out of trouble for more than a decade before his relationship broke down six months before the offence.

Dixon started using drugs and alcohol again after falling in with the wrong crowd, he said.

Judge Jonathan Carroll jailed Dixon for 40 months and two weeks for subjecting the woman to the terrifying ordeal.

“She armed herself with a stool and a bat to form some kind of shield – she must have been terrified,” he said.

“This must have terrified this poor woman and this kind of fear doesn’t disappear with a quick cigarette after the incident. It is likely to be days before the anxiety hit her.”

Dixon was also made subject to an indefinite restraining order to stay out of the shop.
