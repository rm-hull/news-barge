Police are asking for the public’s help after the death of a woman in York.

Diane Carlin, who was 59, died in the city last month.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Some sad news but we’re hoping someone may know this woman’s family.

“The coroner’s office have asked for our help to locate the next of kin.”
