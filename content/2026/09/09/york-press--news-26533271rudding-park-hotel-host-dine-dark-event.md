A unique dining event is set to challenge the senses and support a good cause.

Dine in the Dark will return to Rudding Park Hotel, Harrogate, on Thursday, October 8, offering guests the chance to experience a three-course meal without the use of sight.

Blindfolded during the main course, diners will be encouraged to focus on taste, smell, sound, and touch in a sensory exploration of food.

A spokesperson for the event said: "Dine in the Dark is more than just a culinary journey.

  



"It’s a chance to connect with others.

"The event is designed to foster creative networking, meaningful collaboration, and connection.

"Whether you're attending with colleagues, clients, friends, or on your own, you’ll be part of a dynamic event brought together by curiosity, conversation, and a shared purpose of raising funds for Action4access.

"Expect thought-provoking conversation, laughter, and a deeper understanding of some the challenges guide dog owners experience in their daily lives."

Guests are invited to gather in the event bar area from 7pm for drinks and networking, with dinner service beginning at 7.30pm.

The evening will feature spot prizes, a silent auction, and a prize draw.

A full bar will also be available.

All proceeds will help guide and assistance dog owners who have been refused access or given inferior service.

Funds will also help prevent similar incidents from occurring in the future.

Tickets are priced at £60 and can be booked through Eventbrite.
