A MAN collapsed at the top of Malham Cove earlier this week at the same time a woman was injured while walking near Thornton Force.

The first call to Cave Rescue Organisation was made at 12.10pm on Monday to attend the 78-year-old man who had fainted at the top of the cove, possibly due to low blood pressure.

While the team was preparing to head out, a second call was received at 12.25pm after a 66-year-old woman fell and injured her wrist near Thornton Force, Ingleton.

The man at the top of Malham Cove was assessed by team members before being put into a stretcher and carried down from the cove to a CRO vehicle.

He was then driven to Malham and handed over to relatives to be taken to hospital for a check-up.

A different CRO team at Thornton Force assessed the woman with the injured wrist, and gave her pain relief and an arm support.

A CRO spokesperson said: “She was then able to walk down to the vehicles with assistance from team members then driven back to the waterfalls carpark and handed over to relatives to be taken for a medical check-up at hospital.”
