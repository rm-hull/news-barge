**A rapist has been jailed for more than eight years.**

Phill Blunt, from Scarborough, was due to stand trial for the offence in July but changed his plea at the last moment, according to North Yorkshire Police.

A force spokesperson said the victim had been preparing to give evidence in court following a "long-running investigation".

Blunt, 39, was sentenced to eight years and six months in prison during a hearing at York Crown Court on Tuesday.

In a statement, the victim said: "Reporting him to the police was one of the hardest things I have ever done, but it was also the best decision I could have made."

Blunt was also made subject to a restraining order to protect the victim in the future and placed on the Register of Sex Offenders indefinitely.
