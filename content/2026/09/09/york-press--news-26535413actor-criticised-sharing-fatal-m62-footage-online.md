A television star known for roles in Doctor Who, Torchwood, Arrow, The Flash and Legends of Tomorrow has faced a wave of criticism after sharing footage from an incident on the M62 which later proved fatal near North Yorkshire.

Actor John Barrowman posted a video, since deleted, from stationary traffic shortly after midnight on Wednesday, September 9, showing emergency services on the opposite carriageway as they responded to the incident.

West Yorkshire Police later confirmed a 33-year-old woman had died after falling from a height above the eastbound carriageway.

## 
  **Video shared before woman's death confirmed**

The video was posted at around 12.21am, before police released details about the incident.

Alongside the footage, the actor wrote: "M62 Leeds to Manchester closed due to a person lying in the middle of the motorway. Possibly a suicide or a chase gone wrong.. I hope they are ok."

In the video, he said: "We are heading from Leeds to Manchester on the M62 and we have been stopped because someone is lying in the middle of the other side of the motorway and there's a lot of police, a lot of ambulances.

"We think the person may have jumped from the bridge. We don't know... but the ambulance is there, but we are now stuck here for a while, so I hope that person is okay."

## 
  **Social media users urge post to be removed**

The post prompted a strong reaction online, with many social media users criticising the decision to film and share footage from the scene.

One user wrote: "Now imagine it is your loved one how traumatic to see footage - have some decency and remove it."

Another said: "Delete now please, so insensitive."

A third commented: "Why on earth would you post this? That's someone's family member. Visuals not needed, words would have sufficed."

Others questioned why the scene was being filmed at all, with one person writing: "Why are you filming it? Have some decency for that person and their loved ones."

Another added: "Jesus wept have you no empathy at all? Filming this is awful. Posting it is despicable."

## 
  **Woman died at the scene**

West Yorkshire Police said an off-duty officer came across a 33-year-old woman on the wrong side of railings above the eastbound carriageway at 11.42pm on Tuesday night.

The motorway was closed in both directions while specialist negotiators attended.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Sadly, the woman fell from height and despite medical assistance, she died at the scene."

Her death is not being treated as suspicious and the coroner has been informed.

## 
  **Motorway reopened after overnight closure**

The incident led to the closure of the M62 between Junction 25 at Brighouse and Junction 22 at Denshaw for several hours overnight.

The motorway later reopened after emergency services completed their work and enquiries were carried out.
