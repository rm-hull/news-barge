Two crews tackled a fire at a home in a York village last night (Tuesday).

York and Acomb firefighters went to the incident on **Main Street, Knapton** at**7.27pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “On arrival of crews, this was found to be a fire to the first floor of the property.

#### **Main Street, Knapton**

“Crews used one hose reel jet, two breathing apparatus and a thermal imaging camera to extinguish the fire.”
