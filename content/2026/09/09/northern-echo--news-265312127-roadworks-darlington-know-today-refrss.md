Seven roads in Darlington are affected by works, with restrictions continuing on several routes.

Drivers are being warned about temporary traffic measures and delays across the town.

The works include emergency repairs, gas main work, telecoms work, electricity work and construction.

Here are the areas affected on Wednesday (September 9):

### Napier Street

One lane traffic is in place on Napier Street, both ways at Outram Street, because of emergency repairs and gas main work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 13, when the work is due to finish at 11pm.

### Thornton Street

One lane traffic is in place on Thornton Street, both ways between Corporation Road and Gladstone Street, because of telecoms work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 10, when the work is due to finish at 4pm.

### Winchester Way

One lane traffic is in place on Winchester Way, both ways at Rochester Way, because of electricity work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until October 15, when the work is due to finish at 11pm.


### A68 Woodland Road

Temporary traffic signals are in operation on A68 Woodland Road, both ways between Hollyhurst Road and Bracken Road, because of telecoms work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 15, when the work is due to finish at 11.59pm.

### Auckland Avenue

One lane traffic is in place on Auckland Avenue, both ways between Brinkburn Road and Auckland Oval, because of construction.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 22, when the work is due to finish at 11pm.

### St Alban's Green

One lane traffic is in place on St Alban's Green, both ways at Winchester Way, because of electricity work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 9, when the work is due to finish at 11pm.

### Edinburgh Drive

Temporary traffic signals are in operation on Edinburgh Drive, both ways between A67 Coniscliffe Road and Teesdale Avenue, because of gas main work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 9, when the work is due to finish at 4pm.

Delays are expected.

The affected roads have different levels of reported impact, ranging from low to moderate, while Napier Street, Winchester Way and Auckland Avenue are listed as low.
