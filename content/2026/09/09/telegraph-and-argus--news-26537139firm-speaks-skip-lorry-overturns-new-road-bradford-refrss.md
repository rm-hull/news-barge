A waste carrier has spoken out after one of its skip lorries overturned on a Bradford road.

A skip lorry owned by EFR Skips & Waste Recycling overturned around midday on Wednesday on New Lane, near the junction with Dick Lane in Laisterdyke.

The overturned lorry crushed a car and horse box and spilled rubbish into the gardens of three nearby homes.

The driver, local residents and others in the area escaped injury.

A spokesperson for EFR Skips & Waste Recycling said: “We’re taking this very seriously.

“We didn’t expect this to happen, and it’s rare for something like this to happen.

“Luckily no one was injured, including our driver, residents and people walking on the footpaths.

“Our driver was a bit shocked and shaken up, but he’s OK.

“Our driver is professional and has worked with us for many years.

“We deal with this internally, but I’d like to thank everyone for their work at the scene.”

The Telegraph & Argus attended the scene of the dramatic crash today.

By mid-afternoon, residents whose homes were surrounded by rubbish were asking when the “mess” would be cleared.

Khadija Haider said: “It’s so messy.”

“I was told the council will clean it up.”

“I hope they come quickly and take the rubbish.”

The lorry crushed Harry Davey’s car, while rubbish blocked access to and from his home.

Police spoke to residents and said the council was responsible for clearing the rubbish.

The crash closed the street, but police did not cordon off the scene.

Police directed traffic at the scene but declined to comment, saying the incident did not meet the terms of their service-level agreement.
