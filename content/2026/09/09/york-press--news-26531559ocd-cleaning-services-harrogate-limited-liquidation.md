A North Yorkshire cleaning company has gone into liquidation owing the taxman more than £30,000.

OCD Cleaning Services (Harrogate) Limited of Queensgate House, 23 North Park Road, Harrogate, entered a creditors voluntary liquidation on August 18.

FRP Advisory Trading Limited of Stockton-on-Tees was appointed the liquidator of the company which specialised in the general cleaning of buildings.

A report known has a statement of liabilities said the business, which was incorporated in 2021, had assets of £15,415 available to creditors.

The report said £527 was owed to HMRC for VAT as a secondary preferential creditor - someone paid before the unsecured creditors.

This left £14,888 available to non-preferential creditors (those who are paid last).

The report said £1 was owed to unsecured creditors, two directors were owed £1,639 and HMRC was owed £29,990 in corporation tax.

This gave an estimated total deficiency of £16,051.

With issued and called up capital of £100, this gave an overall estimated deficiency or debt of £16,151.
