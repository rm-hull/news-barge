Firefighters and river users combined to sort a problem on the river in York last night (Tuesday).

A York fire crew were called to the incident near **Millennium Bridge** at**8.04pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The York crew were mobilised to a untethered boat floating in the river near Millennium Bridge with no person occupying.

“As crews arrived on scene another boat passed by to assist with tethering and securing the boat. No further action required from crew.”
