Germany has a knack for producing almost untranslatable words that manage to capture an entire situation in a single expression.

From Goethe's literary masterpiece to modern political arguments, *Gretchenfrage* is a term that invariably gets to the heart of the matter.

**What does* Gretchenfrage*mean?**

A *Gretchenfrage* is a direct question that cuts to the core of an issue and forces someone to reveal what they really think.

It’s usually uncomfortable for the person being asked because it demands a clear position on a matter of principle, belief or intention.

In English, depending on the context, it might be translated "the litmus test question" or "the million-dollar question". But neither translation is perfect, because *Gretchenfrage* also carries the sense of exposing a person's true convictions.

Grammatically, *die Gretchenfrage* is a feminine noun. The plural is*die Gretchenfragen*.

The first part of the word comes from *Gretchen*, a diminutive form of* Grete*or* Margarete*(Margaret). And the specific* Gretchen*in this instance is one of the central characters in Goethe's* Faust*.

In one famous scene she asks Faust: "Now tell me, where do you stand on religion?" ("*Nun sag, wie hast du's mit der Religion?*")

Ever since – because Faust had secretly made a deal with the devil – a *Gretchenfrage* has meant a question that hits someone's weak spot.

**Why do I need to know* Gretchenfrage*?**

The term comes up in Germany whenever someone faces a fundamental dilemma or is suspected of avoiding a straight answer – and many people in the country still love borrowing the structure of Gretchen's original question.

Politicians, journalists and commentators regularly ask things like: "*Wie hältst du es mit dem Klimaschutz?*" (Where do you really stand on climate policy?)

Or "*Wie halten Sie es mit der Work-Life-Balance?*" (Where do you really stand on work life balance?)

And – for anyone reading this in the build up to Berlin’s state elections on September 20th – it’s also the name of a new AI-powered fact-checking service.

The *Gretchenfrage* app is designed to help people assess suspicious content shared on social media and can be accessed via Instagram, TikTok and X by sharing content with*@Gretchen_AI_Berlin*.

**Use it like this:**

*Die Gretchenfrage für die Partei lautet: Steuern erhöhen oder Sozialleistungen kürzen?*

The crucial question for the party is: raise taxes or cut social benefits?

*Vor der Wahl weichen die Kandidaten der Gretchenfrage aus.*

Ahead of the election, the candidates are dodging the crucial question.

*Nach Jahren des Wachstums steht das Start-up vor der Gretchenfrage: unabhängig bleiben oder verkaufen?*

After years of growth, the startup faces the ultimate question: stay independent or sell?

*In jeder Fernbeziehung stellt sich irgendwann die Gretchenfrage: Wer zieht um?*

In every long-distance relationship, the moment of truth eventually arrives: who moves?
