A volunteer will run the Great North Run in 10kg of lifesaving gear to raise money for the RNLI.

James Waters, a volunteer crew member and lifeboat press officer at Tynemouth RNLI, will take part in the 13.1-mile route on September 13 wearing around 10kg of RNLI equipment, including a dry suit and wellies.

Mr Waters said: "I've been fortunate to be part of the crew at Tynemouth, so I wanted to take on a challenge that would help raise funds for the charity I volunteer for.

"The Great North Run seemed like a good opportunity to do something different and hopefully raise some awareness of the work that the RNLI does.

"The kit is designed for lifesaving at sea rather than running, so it does make things a little more difficult.

"The dry suit and wellies are probably the biggest challenge, and with around 10kg of equipment to carry, I'm expecting it to feel quite different from a normal run.

"I've been getting used to running in the kit as part of my training, so hopefully that will help on the day."

Mr Waters has already raised more than £850 towards his £1,000 fundraising target.

Katie Hill, RNLI Community Manager for the North and East, said: "It’s great to see James taking on this challenge to support the RNLI.

"Our volunteers give their time in many different ways, both on and off the water, and fundraising is an important part of helping us continue our lifesaving work.

"We’re very grateful to James for taking part and wish him the very best for the Great North Run."

Mr Waters has trained for the event in recent months, running the North Tyneside 10k with fellow crew members and completing longer runs in his RNLI gear.

He said: "I'm really grateful to everyone who has donated so far."

"The support has been fantastic and has made the training worthwhile.

"Hopefully I can get over the finish line in South Shields, reach the £1,000 target, and raise some money for the RNLI along the way."

The Great North Run takes place on Sunday 13 September, with runners travelling from Newcastle to South Shields.
