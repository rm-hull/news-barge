**Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust (HDFT)** has invited staff, patients, and the wider public to its upcoming Annual Members’ Meeting to help steer local healthcare priorities in the region.

The public event is scheduled for **Monday 21 September 2026** in the Derwent Room at The Pavilions of Harrogate, located within the Great Yorkshire Showground (HG2 8NZ). Registration opens at 4:30 PM with networking opportunities, followed by the formal meeting from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM.

## “10 Year Plan” theme addresses deep £23.7m deficit hurdles

Centering on a forward-looking “10 Year Plan” theme, the assembly will address the long-term operational framework of the trust. While senior executives intend to showcase community service developments, the presentation follows a challenging financial review detailed in the trust’s newly published **Annual Report and Accounts 2025-26**, which exposed an adjusted financial deficit of**£23.7 million**.

According to the official accounts, the multi-million-pound deficit was driven by escalating employee expenses, ward overspends related to sickness and enhanced care cover, central funding cuts, and elective activity shortfalls. Trust leadership has committed to a sweeping Waste Reduction and Productivity (WRAP) initiative to return HDFT to a balanced breakeven position by the close of the 2027-28 fiscal loop.

## Strategic milestones and community performance targets

Alongside its financial hurdles, the trust will use the forum to highlight positive performance metrics achieved across its broader geography, which supports approximately 316,000 acute hospital patients and over 600,000 children across the North of England. Key milestones from the past year include:

- **Emergency Standards:** The urgent care pathway achieved the national target by hitting a 79% four-hour emergency care standard in March 2026.
- **Harm Reductions:** The implementation of the continuous “HDFT Impact” operating model delivered a 21% year-on-year reduction in moderate safety events, reporting 82 total incidents against a target threshold of 88.
- **Minor Injury Upgrades:** The successful transition of the Ripon Minor Injuries unit into a fully fledged Urgent Treatment Centre (UTC), expanding daily local patient attendances from 30 to 70.

## Trust leadership calls for public collaboration

The evening will feature interactive service information stands, open floor Q&A sessions, and direct access to HDFT Governors and Directors to discuss upcoming regional programs, such as the digital rollout of the NerveCentre Electronic Patient Record. The session will be recorded and published online for public access shortly after concluding.

Sarah Armstrong, Chair of Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust, said: “Our Annual Members’ Meeting is one of the most important events in our calendar because it gives us the opportunity to share openly with our communities how the Trust is performing, celebrate the achievements of our colleagues, look ahead to the future and listen to the views of the people we serve.”

Armstrong added: “Your questions, feedback and ideas help shape our services, and we look forward to welcoming you for an evening of discussion and conversation about the future of healthcare in our communities.”
