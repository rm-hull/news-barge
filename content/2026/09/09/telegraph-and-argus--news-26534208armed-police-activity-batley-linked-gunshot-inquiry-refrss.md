Armed police were seen on a residential street.

Specialist officers were seen on Hawthorn Avenue in Batley on Tuesday.

West Yorkshire Police said the activity was part of ongoing enquiries into an incident on Upper George Street, Heckmondwike.

A spokesperson for the force said: "This police activity was in relation to the firearms discharge on Upper George Street, Heckmondwike on 6 September. Enquiries are ongoing."

## Arrest made after reports of disturbance

Officers were called to Upper George Street at around 8.13pm on Sunday after receiving reports of a disturbance involving weapons.

Officers found evidence suggesting a firearm had been discharged during the incident.

A spokesperson previously said: "Evidence was also found to suggest a firearm had been discharged."

The 29-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of possession of a firearm and remained in custody while enquiries continued.

## Previous statement from West Yorkshire Police

WYP first provided a statement on Monday morning, detailing their understanding of the firearms investigation.

A spokesperson said: "At about 8.13pm yesterday (Sunday), police received a report of a disturbance involving weapons in Upper George Street, Heckmondwike.

"Further reports suggested a gunshot had been heard. Officers attended and arrested a man following initial enquiries into the incident.

"Evidence was also found to suggest a firearm had been discharged. A 29-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of possession of a firearm and remains in custody at this time.

"Kirklees CID is continuing enquiries."
