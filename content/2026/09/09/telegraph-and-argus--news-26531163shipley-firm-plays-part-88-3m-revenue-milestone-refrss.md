A CALL-HANDLING company has helped its parent group reach £88.3 million in UK revenue.

Your Business Voice, based in Shipley and part of Isher Capital’s UK outsourcing portfolio through the CallCare network, offers services including telephone answering, customer service, engineer call-out, disaster recovery, lone-worker, and helpdesk support for organisations across the UK.

Baani Kaur, managing director of iCXperience, said: "Shipley is home to an experienced team providing the dependable call-handling and operational support that clients need when an enquiry cannot wait until the next working day.

"These figures show the strength of bringing experienced regional businesses together as part of a wider group.

"We are continuing to invest strategically so teams can work more effectively and clients have the capacity they need when demand is urgent or difficult to predict."

In the last financial year, Isher Capital’s UK businesses reported aggregate revenue of £88.3 million and assets of more than £109.4 million.

The group employed 1,742 people in the UK, with expectations of surpassing 2,000 this year.

Reuben Singh, founder of Isher Capital, said: "The Bradford district is a key operating location for the group and a strong example of how skilled people remain central to business performance when supported by the right technology and operating model."
