## Yorkshire artisans to showcase traditional skills at upcoming exhibition

Jacqueline Warrington *(Image: Ampleforth Abbey)*

Two Yorkshire artisans are set to shine at a jewellery and silversmithing exhibition this month.

Karen Ward and Jacqueline Warrington will take part in Acquire at Ampleforth, a three-day, free-entry event.

It is being held at Alban Roe House in the grounds of Ampleforth Abbey from Friday, September 18 to Sunday, September 20.

Ms Ward, an artist and jeweller, said: "I love making things with metal, be it jewellery or tools to help me in my work.

"I passionately believe that we need to share traditional skills that are in danger of dying out."

She recently began incorporating engraved animal portraits into her jewellery.

Ms Warrington, who works in silver, gold and precious stones, uses traditional methods including hand raising, forging and repousse.

She said: "I am very interested in legends, myths and stories of the saints, particularly Celtic ones."

The exhibition has been curated by fine artist jeweller Joanne Gowan and will feature high-quality design from exceptional makers from Yorkshire and the North of England.

In addition to the exhibition, there will be a series of ticketed talks organised by jewellery expert Susan Rumfitt, known for her appearances on the BBC’s Antiques Roadshow.

The talks will explore the history, artistry and allure of fine jewellery.
