A MAJOR road in Yorkshire has reopened following a crash which left a caravan overturned.

National Highways announced that the A1, between junctions 41 and 39, had reopened at 1.41pm today (Wednesday, September 9).

It comes after drivers were alerted to the crash, and the associated 45-minute delays shortly after 11am.

  


A spokesperson for National Highways confirmed that a lane of the A1 had been closed to allow for clean-up and recovery.

A recent update from the agency said: “Recovery and debris clear up complete.

“All lanes now open. No significant delays reported in the area.”
