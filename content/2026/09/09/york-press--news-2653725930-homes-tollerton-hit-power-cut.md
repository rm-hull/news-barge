MORE than 30 homes in North Yorkshire have been hit with a power outage.

An unplanned power cut has affected a total of 31 homes in and around Tollerton.

The incident was reported shortly after 6pm on Wednesday (September 9).

  


Northern Powergrid said its team was trying to get the power back on “quickly” by switching electricity through alternative routes on its network.

Power restoration is estimated to be complete at approximately 11pm tonight.
