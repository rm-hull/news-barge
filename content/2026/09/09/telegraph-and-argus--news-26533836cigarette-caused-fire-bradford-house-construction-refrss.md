A CIGARETTE is believed to have caused a fire at a house under construction in Bradford.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to Hollingwood Lane, Great Horton, at 7.51pm on Tuesday.

Three fire crews were sent to the incident, and they found a fire at a house that was still under construction.

At the scene, firefighters extinguished the blaze using a hose reel.

## No injuries reported

No injuries were reported as a result of the fire.

The incident was brought under control by crews at the scene.

The fire service also confirmed no other properties are believed to have been affected.

## Cause believed to be cigarette

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the cause of the fire is believed to have been a cigarette.

The fire service believes the blaze was started accidentally, and an investigation into the incident has been carried out.

## FULL statement from West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service

A spokesperson for WYFRS said: "We were called at 7.51pm yesterday to a building fire on Hollingwood Lane.

"Three crews attended a fire in a house under construction and extinguished it using a hose reel.

"No reports of injuries- the cause is believed to be a cigarette."
