One of the UK’s oldest schools, located in York, is marking 1,400 years of history.

St Peter’s School, founded in AD 627 by St Paulinus, is the third oldest school in the country and has launched a year of celebrations ahead of the milestone in 2027.

The anniversary events began on September 5 with Peterite Day, which included sporting fixtures between current pupils and alumni, a chapel service, children’s entertainment, a reunion, and tours of the newly opened Junior School.

Jeremy Walker, headmaster at St Peter’s, said: "There is an exciting year ahead at St Peter’s as we celebrate the school’s 1400th anniversary in 2027.

"It is set to be an exceptional year with the launch of our Centre for Excellence in Teaching and Learning in October, keeping us at the forefront of educational research.

"In January, we open The Rise Nursery, already over-subscribed, where families are now able to be part of the St Peter’s community from the age of six months.

"As one of only three schools in the country to reach the 1400 milestone, we are looking forward to celebrating all that makes St Peter’s so special with pupils, families, alumni and the wider York community."

The newly opened Junior School is part of a major restructure to a two-school model, with the Senior School beginning at Year 7.

Facilities at the Junior School include a library, science labs, art studio, design technology workshop, theatre, and two sports centres.

Phil Hardy, head of the Junior School, said: "It's a privilege to welcome back our pupils and parents this term who will see our wonderful new junior school and all our incredible new facilities for the first time.

"A huge amount of work has gone on during the school holidays to transform the buildings into the best possible learning environment for our children, and I thank all the staff and contractors for their efforts."

The Junior School forms part of a continuous educational pathway from nursery to sixth form, with a new Sixth Form Centre and café already in use and additional buildings planned for 2027.

The campus remodel has been overseen by architects brown + company and Harecroft Construction.

Mr Walker added: "We were determined to appoint local Yorkshire companies for this crucially important project for the school and both have been magnificent."

As part of the anniversary, St Peter’s is collaborating with brands run by former pupils, known as Old Peterites.

One such partnership has produced the St Peter's 1400 Wold Top Beer, a special edition created by the Mellor family.

The school has more brand collaborations and events planned throughout the anniversary year, some in partnership with York Minster.
