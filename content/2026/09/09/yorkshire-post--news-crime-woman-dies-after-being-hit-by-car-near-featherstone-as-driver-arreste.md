# Woman dies after being hit by car near Featherstone as driver arrested

## A 40-year-old woman has died after being hit by a car near Featherstone, with a 26-year-old man arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

Emergency services were called to Wakefield Road, near Featherstone, just before 6.40pm on Monday (Sep 8).

A grey Cupra Born, which had been travelling towards Wakefield, was involved in a crash with a woman who was crossing the road at the junction with Common Side Lane.

![West Yorkshire Police](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjI5M2E3NDU2LWI2NTctNDBmNy04ZTZhLTE2NDc4YjNlMTJkNTplYzlhYjY4NC03YjE5LTRiYjktYTFkNy0wYjkyOTRhMDI3OGQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The woman, who was 40 and from Pontefract, was given emergency medical treatment but died at the scene.

Police arrested the driver of the Cupra, a 26-year-old man from Pontefract, on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

He remains in police custody.

Road closures were put in place in the area while investigation and recovery work was carried out at the scene.

West Yorkshire Police's Major Collision Enquiry Team is now appealing for witnesses and footage as enquiries continue.

Officers particularly want to hear from anyone who saw the Cupra or the manner in which it was being driven shortly before the crash.

Detective Sergeant Nina Roper of the Major Collision Enquiry Team, said: "A number of enquires remain ongoing into this incident which has sadly resulted in a woman losing her life.

"Officers are supporting her family and we are appealing for any witnesses and relevant footage showing the incident. We would also like to hear from anyone who saw the Cupra and the manner in which it was being driven in the moments before the collision took place.
