A LORRY overturned in a dramatic Bradford crash, crushing a parked car and horse box.

The incident happened on New Lane, near the junction with Dick Lane in Laisterdyke, and involved a vehicle belonging to EFR Skips & Waste Recycling.

Much of the lorry’s load spilled into a nearby garden, dumping a large amount of rubbish onto the property.

The Telegraph & Argus approached the police for a statement, but they would not release further information about the crash, stating that it did not meet the requirements of their service level agreement.


EFR Skips & Waste Recycling is a West Yorkshire-based skip hire and waste management company serving Bradford, Leeds, Wakefield and Dewsbury and surrounding areas.

The company supplies skips ranging from two to 16 yards, as well as larger roll-on roll-off containers for domestic and commercial use.

EFR Skips & Waste Recycling was established in 2017 and is a licensed waste carrier.

The firm offers online booking, delivery and collection, and provides waste-transfer notes to customers.

Their services cover everything from household clear-outs to large-scale commercial projects.
