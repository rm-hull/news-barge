A FRESH take on a modern stage classic is set to arrive in York late in October.

The Curious Incident of the Dog in the Night-Time, adapted from Mark Haddon’s best-selling novel, will be performed at the Grand Opera House as part of a new UK tour.

Charlie Brooks, best known for her role as Janine Butcher in EastEnders, stars as Judy.

Ms Brooks said: "I really think this is a properly brilliant show and we need to get good theatre out into the regions.

"That’s hugely important.

"It’s personal for me: I’m from Barmouth, a little town in North Wales, and regional theatre was all I saw when I was younger.

"It made a big impact."

The production is directed by Ned Bennett and adapted by playwright Simon Stephens.

It introduces the first-ever female portrayal of the neurodivergent protagonist, now named Chris Boone.

Chris is played by Alex Keenan, with Imi Price alternating in the role.

Joe McFadden, winner of Strictly Come Dancing 2017, stars as Ed.

Mr McFadden said: "I can’t remember the last time I was so excited about putting on a show and seeing what people think.

"It’s a real treat for the senses."
