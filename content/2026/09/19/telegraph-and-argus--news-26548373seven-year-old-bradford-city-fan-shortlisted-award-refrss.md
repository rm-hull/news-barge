A seven-year-old Bradford City supporter who dreams of one day working on Sky Sports has been shortlisted for a national content creator award after building a growing online following with her football videos.

Pippa Powell, 7, has been named a finalist in the Best Young Content Creator category at this year's Content Creator Awards.

The young Bantams fan, from Idle, attends Bradford City matches home and away with her family and has gained a loyal audience by sharing matchday videos and her thoughts on the team's performances online.

## **From the stands to social media**

Pippa's mum, Eleanor Powell, said her daughter's content creation journey began during the 2024/25 football season.

"She'd been saying she wanted to make football videos for ages," she said.

"We didn't really know exactly what she meant by it at first, but she kept talking about it, so we thought we'd give it a go, and from then on it just kind of grew."

![]()Pippa Powell records a video in her Bradford City shirt. The football-mad seven-year-old regularly shares matchday content and reaction videos with fellow Bantams supporters. *(Image: T&A)*

Pippa now regularly records footage during Bradford City matches before editing it at home and adding her own commentary.

"We go to pretty much every game, home and away," Ms Powell said. "When we get home, she edits the videos, watches the footage back and gives her own feedback on the game."

## **Dreaming of Sky Sports**

Despite being just seven years old, Pippa already has ambitious plans for the future.

"She wants to be on Sky Sports," Ms Powell laughed.

The youngster has already enjoyed a number of opportunities through her growing profile within the football community.

![]()Bradford City supporter Pippa Powell displays her "Little Bantam Pip" banner outside Cardiff City Stadium. The seven-year-old regularly creates matchday content from Bantams home and away games. *(Image: Eleanor Powell)*

She has attended football-related events and was invited pitch-side at a Wigan Athletic match, where she helped announce the teams to supporters over the stadium microphone.

"We've had other clubs reach out to us," Eleanor said. "When we went to Wigan, they invited her pitch-side with a microphone to announce the teams to the whole ground."

## **National recognition**

Last year, Pippa attended the Content Creator Awards in Leeds and came away with a bronze award.

Now she has once again been recognised, reaching the finalist stage of the competition for 2026.

The awards combine votes from industry judges with a public vote to determine the winners.

![]()Pippa Powell with her Bronze Award at the Football Content Awards. The seven-year-old Bradford City fan has once again been shortlisted in the Best Young Content Creator category. *(Image: Eleanor Powell)*

Voting is open until early October, with the awards ceremony due to take place in November.

Ms Powell said: "We still don't really know how she got nominated the first time, but it's lovely that people have noticed what she's doing."

## **Keeping it safe**

Despite Pippa's growing online presence, her parents carefully manage all of her social media accounts.

Ms Powell explained that Pippa does not have direct access to platforms such as TikTok and Instagram, with uploads and interactions overseen by the family.

"She's only ever really had positivity on there," she said. "She absolutely loves it, and we're comfortable with it because we're very much in control of it."

## **Opening doors for the future**

The family hope Pippa's success could help create future opportunities in sports broadcasting and media.

Earlier this year she attended a football-focused brand event and has continued to attract attention from clubs and organisations impressed by her content.

For now, though, Ms Powell says her daughter is simply enjoying the experience.

"It's been a whirlwind," she said. "But she absolutely loves it."
