Bradford is known for being Britain’s curry capital thanks to the range of great curry houses found around the city.

If you are new to the area or simply cannot decide which curry houses to try out, you might want to choose the International.

Found on Morley Street, the restaurant is close to the heart of the city centre.

The International offers “amazing food” that guests say is “full of flavour” while still being “good value for money”.

## What is the International in Bradford like?

The International has been serving the city since 1976 and has become a staple for curry lovers.

The restaurant offers a huge range of starters including vegetarian, meat, and fish options like samosas, chaat, and pakora.

There is also the International House Mix that you can share if you are struggling to choose just one starter.

For mains, you can choose from traditional Desi dishes like butter chicken, nihari, and paya as well as seafood karahis if you fancy trying out some fish.

There are also Balti dishes, biryanis, and a lot of your old school favourites such as dopiaza, bhuna, and madras curries available.

To go alongside your curries, there are vegetable sides like saag aloo, mushroom bhaji, and tarka dall as well as a range of rice.

You can also enjoy a variety of naans topped with things like garlic, coriander, and even cheese.

## What do visitors think of the International in Bradford?

The International has a rating of 4.6 out of 5 on Tripadvisor, with 1,726 people leaving reviews after their trip.

One person said: “Such a great restaurant, amazing food, full of flavour and good value for money.

“Restaurant is always full and some seems to be locals and that is always a good indication that food is very good.

“We are just visiting Bradford for a few days and we came back for our dinners.

“Staff is always very pleasant and attentive, very prompt service too.”

Another person said: “As usual this place just never disappoints!

“My absolute is poppadoms and a pickle tray, theirs is amazing!

“The portions for the mains are incredible and the taste difference between courses is perfection.

“I honestly can’t fault this restaurant, it’s authentic, friendly and the food is on point!”

“If you love curry then The International is one to visit.”
