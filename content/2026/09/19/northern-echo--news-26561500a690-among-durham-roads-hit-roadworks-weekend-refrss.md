Drivers around Durham city face four roadworks schemes over the weekend.

A variety of works are taking place in over this weekend (September 19-20), see where they are below:

### Abbey Road, Pity Me

Temporary traffic signals are in place on Abbey Road in both directions between Front Street and Smithfield for gas main work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until October 19 at 4pm.

### Front Street, Pity Me

Temporary traffic signals are in place on Front Street in both directions at Abbey Road because of gas main work.

Restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until October 5 at 4pm.

### Rock Terrace, New Brancepeth

Construction is taking place on Rock Terrace in both directions from Bewley Terrace to Waltons Terrace.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until October 4 at 11pm.

### The A690

A lane is closed on the A690 in both directions between Gilesgate and the A1(M) J62 (Carrville) for water main work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until November 4 at 11.59pm.
