A North East shop has shared the shocking moment when a man dressed in a balaclava and black clothing threatens a staff member with a knife.

Go Local Extra Shiney Row on Hindsons Crescent South in Houghton-le-Spring put out the footage late on Thursday (September 17), showing the terrified shop worker being confronted by the armed man.

![]()CCTV images of a man brandishing a knife at a staff member at Go Local Extra Shiney Row *(Image: Go Local Extra Shiney Row)*

In the footage, the man with the knife can be heard saying: "Put the money in the bag now" and "hurry up."

Alongside the footage, the Go Local Extra in has put out a post appealing for information.

It said: "Unfortunately, our shop was robbed this evening by the individual shown in these CCTV images.

"The incident has been captured clearly on CCTV and is being reported to the police.

"If anyone recognises this person or has any information that could help, please contact us privately or report the information directly to the police.

"Please do not approach or confront the individual yourself."

The spokesperson added: "Any information, no matter how small, would be greatly appreciated.

"Please share this post so we can hopefully identify the person responsible.

"Thank you to everyone in the local community for your support."

Northumbria Police has confirmed that it's investigating the incident.

A spokesperson for the police force said: "Shortly before 11pm yesterday (Thursday), we received a report of a robbery at a premises on Hindson Crescent in the Shiney Row area of Houghton-le-Spring.
