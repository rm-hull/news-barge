Firefighters from two forces combined to deal with a midnight fire at a North Yorkshire industrial site.

Two crews from West Yorkshire joined crews from Tadcaster and Selby to fight and extinguish the fire at Sherburn-in-Elmet.

The blaze broke out shortly before midnight and was out by 7am.

Crews are still at the scene making sure the area is safe.
