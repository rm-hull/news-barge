Police have issued an update after a County Durham street was cordoned off yesterday (September 18) afternoon, with officers combing the surrounding streets as part of an investigation.

At around 4.30pm, Durham Police officers were spotted on Front Street in Consett, near a Subway sandwich shop.

A large police cordon was put up between the junctions with Albert Road and Medomsley Road, blocking off several businesses.

![]() *(Image: Joshua Nichol)*

The incident left bus routes diverted and queuing traffic during rush hour.

Durham Police issued a statement, warning people to avoid the area.

A spokesperson for the force said on Friday: "Front Street, in Consett, has been closed as part of an ongoing police investigation.

"The road is currently closed in both directions between the junctions with Albert Road and Medomsley Road.

"We'd advise people to avoid the area and find an alternative route if they are travelling in the area."

![]() *(Image: Joshua Nichol)*

Today, the police force has issued a slight update, confirming that the road has now reopened.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "Front Street is now open.

"Thank you all for your patience and understanding while we carried out our enquiries."

![]() *(Image: Joshua Nichol)*

The Northern Echo has contacted the police force for further information on the incident.

During the incident, residents and business owners were left baffled by the police presence.
