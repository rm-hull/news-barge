**RIPON**— The Harrogate Autumn Flower Show has officially launched its landmark 50th anniversary event at Newby Hall & Gardens, celebrating five decades of horticultural excellence with a spectacular harvest-themed exhibition.To mark the golden anniversary of the first show in 1976, renowned floral designer Jonathan Moseley unveiled a bespoke, harvest-inspired gown during a preview photocall. Modeled by Anna Cartmell-Done against the historic backdrop of the estate, the elaborate living gown serves as the dramatic centerpiece for Moseley’s anniversary installation, titled

*Grain & Grandeur – The Harvest Ball*.

The design interpretation mirrors the show’s 2026 “Golden Harvest” theme, utilizing an intricate blend of seasonal autumn flowers, grasses, grains, and seed heads to dress the historic house and surrounding grounds.

> **Nick Smith, Show Director, stated:** This is a particularly special year for the Harrogate Autumn Flower Show as we celebrate 50 years since the first show in 1976. Jonathan’s Grain & Grandeur installation is a wonderful way to celebrate the season and the creativity at the heart of the show. The harvest-inspired gown looks fantastic! It’s a fitting centrepiece for our 50th anniversary and captures the richness and abundance of autumn in a really creative way.

### Giant Vegetables and Floral Pavilions

The three-day seasonal showcase, curated by the North of England Horticultural Society (NEHS), features more than 5,000 blooms housed within the Grand Floral Pavilion. The site features extensive displays from specialized nurseries and regional horticultural bodies showcasing late-season varieties.

Over in the Incredible Edible Pavilion, the show continues its tradition of competitive cultivation, hosting the heavily anticipated NEHS Giant Vegetable Competition. A key fixture of the weekend remains the National Onion Championship, where growers are attempting to challenge the standing world record of 8.97kg achieved on the Harrogate benches by Gareth Griffin in 2023.

The standard admission ticket provides full access to Newby Hall’s award-winning gardens and internal floral installations. The weekend features a rotating itinerary of live entertainment, family workshops, specialist gardening demonstrations, and seasonal food and craft markets.

The Harrogate Autumn Flower Show runs through Sunday, 20 September 2026, at the Newby Hall estate near Ripon. Entry tickets are priced at £29 for adults, whilst parking and accompanied children under the age of 16 are completely free. Digital bookings, full event schedules, and transport details can be accessed via the verified Harrogate Flower Shows Booking Portal.
