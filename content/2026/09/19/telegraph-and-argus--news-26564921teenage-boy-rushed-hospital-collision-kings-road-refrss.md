A teenage boy was rushed to hospital after a collision involving a car and a trail bike in Bradford.

Police put a cordon in place after the collision at the junction of Swain House Road and Kings Road in Bradford at around 11am.

West Yorkshire Police said the teenage rider was taken to hospital with injuries that are not believed to be life-threatening.

A police spokesperson said: "Police were called to the junction of Swain House Road and Kings Road, Bradford, at about 11am today to a report of a collision between a car and a trail bike.

"The rider, a teenage boy, was taken to hospital for injuries which are not believed to be life-threatening.

"A cordon was put in place at the scene and enquiries are ongoing."

Yorkshire Ambulance Service (YAS) also attended the incident.

A YAS spokesperson said: "We were also called at 10.55am today to the junction of Kings Road and Swain House Road to a road traffic collision.

"A number of resources were sent to the scene and we treated one patient."
