Police have launched a search for two Darlington girls who have been reported missing.

Officers from Durham Police are looking for the pair, who are believed to be together.

Nevaeh, 16, was last seen leaving her home in the Parkside area at around 11am yesterday (September 18).

She has long, blonde hair and was last seen wearing a black coat, black leggings, and black trainers.

Meanwhile, Keira, also 16, was last seen leaving her home in the Skerne Park area between 11.15am and 11.30am yesterday (September 18).
