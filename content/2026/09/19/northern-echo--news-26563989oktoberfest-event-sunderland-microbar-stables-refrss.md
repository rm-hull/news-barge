A tiny village pub with nearly 300 years of history is gearing up for its first Oktoberfest in Sunderland.

The Stables microbar in Cleadon will become a Bavarian-style venue on September 26, serving German beer in traditional two-pint steins.

But Oktoberfest is only part of the story at this pint-sized pub near Sunderland, which has built a loyal following since opening in a former florist’s shop in 2021.

Named after the building's original use as stables, a history which dates back to 1738, The Stables was created after a major renovation that transformed the historic property into a micro bar and brewery.

Owner Leighanne Johnson took over the business in January 2025, having worked there since it first opened.

The venue was originally due to be called The Lazy Lizard, after the microbrewery set up upstairs by Leighanne’s husband and his friends.

But, when villagers would walk past and tell them the history, it seemed only right to call it The Stables.

Today, the dog-friendly pub boasts 17 taps featuring local breweries and guest ales, with many customers regularly booking their favourite tables in advance.

The team is expanding its events programme, starting with an Oktoberfest celebration from noon on September 26.

The event will feature German beers including Brinkhoff and Bavarian decorations.

Entry is free, with a £10 stein deposit refunded when the glass is returned.

Assistant manager Jasmine Hall said the pub wanted to give people more chances to get involved.

Jasmine said: "We have loads of local, loyal customers and we're doing more social media, so as our following grows we wanted to do more for the community and get everyone involved.

"Not everyone drinks, but they'd still like to come to the pub, so events are great for them as well as being something a bit different for our regulars."

Future plans include Christmas wreath-making workshops and a brass band performance at the pub on December 20.

The team is also looking at making more use of an upstairs room, which is free to hire, and is exploring the possibility of launching a book club.

Leighanne said the pub's success is rooted in the strong community around it.

She said "There's lots of independents in Cleadon and it's a proper village community. In six years we've never had any bother, everyone looks after each other."

The Stables opens at 3pm on Wednesdays and Thursdays, and at 12pm from Friday to Sunday.
