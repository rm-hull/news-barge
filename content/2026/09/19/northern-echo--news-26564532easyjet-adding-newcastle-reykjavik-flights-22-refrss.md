North East travellers looking for a winter getaway can now fly direct from Newcastle to Iceland's capital for as little as £22.

EasyJet is offering the deal on flights from Newcastle Airport to Reykjavik this winter, with the route operating twice a week on Tuesdays and Saturdays from October 27.

In April, the airline announced three new winter routes from Newcastle, adding Reykjavik, Fuerteventura and Krakow to its seasonal programme.

The service will give the North East a direct link to one of Europe's most picturesque destinations.

![]()EasyJet will fly direct from Newcastle to Iceland this winter. *(Image: Will Foxx)*

Often described as the gateway to Iceland's dramatic natural wonders, Reykjavik offers visitors much more than a traditional city break.

The colourful Icelandic capital is home to the striking Hallgrímskirkja church, which towers above the city skyline and offers panoramic views across Reykjavik and the surrounding mountains.

The waterfront Harpa Concert Hall, known for its glass architecture, and the Sun Voyager sculpture is another among the city's best-known landmarks.

Many also use Reykjavik as a gateway to Iceland's breath-taking landscapes and natural attractions.

One of the most popular day trips from Reykjavik is the Golden Circle, a scenic route that showcases Þingvellir National Park, the bubbling Geysir geothermal area and the spectacular Gullfoss waterfall.

Iceland is renowned for its geothermal attractions, with the Blue Lagoon among its biggest draws.

The mineral-rich spa draws visitors from around the world to its warm, milky-blue waters, surrounded by lava fields.

Sky Lagoon, near Reykjavik, is another popular spot for a traditional Icelandic bathing experience.

Day trips from the capital reach black-sand beaches, volcanoes, glaciers and spectacular south-coast waterfalls including Seljalandsfoss and Skógafoss.

For many visitors, the chance to witness the Northern Lights is a major draw, with Iceland regarded as one of the best places in the world to see the natural phenomenon.

![]()Iceland *(Image: JET2)*

Between autumn and spring, a range of guided aurora tours operate from Reykjavik, taking visitors to some of the country's best viewing locations.

EasyJet says the Reykjavik route will give North East travellers direct access to some of Iceland's most iconic attractions, from dramatic volcanic landscapes and waterfalls to geothermal spas and outdoor adventures.

The £22 fare is a one-way base fare and excludes hold luggage, seat selection and other optional extras.

Flights are on sale now, although prices remain subject to change and availability.
