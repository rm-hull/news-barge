Businesses in Bridlington and nearby areas can meet the council leader and discuss the area's economy at the first of a new series of roadshows.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council leader Councillor Anne Handley will host the session at Bridlington Spa on Monday, September 28, with arrivals from 4.30pm and the event running from 5pm to 6.30pm.

Councillor Handley said: "Businesses are at the heart of our local economy, and I want to hear directly from them about what is working well, where the challenges are, and what more we can do to support their ambitions.

"These roadshows are an opportunity for them to meet businesses in their own communities, talk about the work we are doing to support economic growth, and listen to their experiences and priorities."

The event will focus on economic growth, regeneration and tourism.

The council will update businesses on coastal regeneration and Pride in Place investment.

They will also hear about support from Invest East Yorkshire’s Business Support Services.

The roadshow delivers on the council’s Business Plan 2026–27 pledge to work directly with businesses and help shape future economic priorities.

It is the first of five events planned across East Yorkshire this autumn.
