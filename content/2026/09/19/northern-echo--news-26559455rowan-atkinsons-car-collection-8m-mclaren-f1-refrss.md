County Durham-born Rowan Atkinson may be best known for playing Mr Bean, but away from television he has built a serious reputation as a motoring enthusiast.

The 71-year-old actor, who was born in Consett, has owned and driven a remarkable collection of cars ranging from classic Aston Martins and BMWs to one of the most famous supercars ever made.

Perhaps the best-known was his McLaren F1.

Atkinson bought the car in 1997 and, unlike many owners of such valuable machinery, used it regularly rather than keeping it tucked away as a collector's piece.

The McLaren F1 was one of the most advanced road cars of its era and remains one of the most sought-after supercars in the world.

![]()Rowan Atkinson *(Image: PA MEDIA)*

The more serious accident happened in 2011 when the McLaren left the road in Cambridgeshire and hit a tree and road sign before catching fire.

Atkinson escaped with a shoulder injury, while the car was returned to McLaren for an extensive rebuild.

The repair bill was widely reported to have reached around £910,000, making it one of the most expensive insurance claims involving a car at the time.

Despite the damage, Atkinson kept driving the F1.

He eventually sold it in 2015 for an estimated £8 million, having reportedly covered tens of thousands of miles during his ownership.

The McLaren is far from the only unusual car linked with the Blackadder and Johnny English star.

Atkinson has owned a 1964 Ford Falcon Sprint, a 1939 BMW 328 Roadster and a Honda NSX, while his collection has also included a number of Aston Martins.

His interest in cars extends beyond simply buying them.

He has regularly competed in historic motor racing and has appeared at events including the Goodwood Revival, where he has raced classic machinery in front of large crowds.

Goodwood has previously highlighted Atkinson as a genuine enthusiast who uses and races his cars rather than treating them purely as investments.

His driving ability was also put to the test on BBC's Top Gear.

Atkinson appeared as the Star in a Reasonably Priced Car during series 17 and completed the programme's test track in a time of 1 minute 42.2 seconds.

That put him at the top of the celebrity leaderboard at the time, underlining that his interest in motoring stretches well beyond ownership.

His passion for cars has become particularly relevant again after he was linked with the BBC's planned revival of Top Gear.

The programme is returning after being off screens following Andrew Flintoff's serious crash during filming in 2022.

The BBC has not confirmed who will present the new-look series.

Atkinson's name has nevertheless been mentioned among the possible contenders, alongside other well-known figures.

For anyone who knows him only as Mr Bean driving a battered green Mini, the contrast is striking.

Off screen, Atkinson has spent decades around some of the world's rarest and fastest cars, raced competitively and owned a McLaren F1 that survived two crashes before being sold for millions.
