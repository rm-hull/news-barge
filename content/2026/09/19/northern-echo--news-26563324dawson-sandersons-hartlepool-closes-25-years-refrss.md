A North East travel agent has bid an emotional farewell to customers after serving the town for more than a quarter of a century.

Staff at Dawson and Sanderson in Hartlepool announced they have closed the doors of the store for the final time, thanking customers for their loyalty and support over the years.

In a message on social media, the team said they were "very, very sad" to be leaving the branch.

It said: "As we close the doors of our Hartlepool branch for the final time, we wanted to take a moment to say a huge thank you to all of our wonderful customers.

"For more than 25 years, it has been our privilege to help you create unforgettable holiday memories. Your loyalty, support, friendship and trust have meant the world to us, and many of you have become so much more than customers over the years.

"We are very, very sad to be saying goodbye to the store that has been our home for so long.

"There will certainly be a few tears as we close our doors for the last time and look back on all the amazing memories we've shared together."

While the Hartlepool store has now closed, Dawson and Sanderson has reassured customers that staff will continue serving holidaymakers from a new location.

The team said: "While our location is changing, our passion for travel and looking after our customers remains exactly the same.

"We sincerely hope you'll continue your journey with us and come and visit us in our new store.

"Whether you're planning your next adventure or simply popping in to say hello, we'd love to see you.

"Thank you for being such an important part of our Hartlepool story.

"We wish you all the very best and look forward to welcoming you to our new home soon."

The announcement prompted an outpouring of support from customers, many of whom shared their own memories of using the travel agency.
