A high-energy circus show inspired by the vibrant world of K-pop is set to light up Halifax this autumn.

The Gandeys K-Pop Dragon Circus will make its debut at Eureka! The National Children's Museum from September 24 to October 4, 2026.

A spokesperson said: "We’re absolutely delighted to be bringing Gandeys K-Pop Dragon Circus to Eureka! for the very first time.

"Eureka! has inspired generations of children through imagination, discovery, and play, making it the perfect location for our newest family production.

"We’re excited to introduce Yorkshire audiences to a contemporary circus experience that combines world-class performers, spectacular production, and unforgettable entertainment for all ages."

The show features more than 30 international performers and promises a fusion of circus artistry with the colour, energy, and style of K-pop culture.

Visitors can expect aerial acts, acrobatics, comedy, and dazzling visual effects—all under the big top.

Tickets start from £9.99.
