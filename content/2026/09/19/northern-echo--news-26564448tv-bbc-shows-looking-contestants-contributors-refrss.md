From testing your general knowledge on national television to showing off a hidden talent, people hoping to be in the television spotlight have plenty of opportunities.

Several BBC programmes are currently recruiting contestants and contributors, offering TV fans the chance to appear on some of the broadcaster's biggest shows.

## New Gordon Ramsay show

![]()Gordon Ramsay's television production company is making a new show for the BBC *(Image: Jas Lehal/PA Media Assignments)*

There is a new cooking show, yet to be named, which is being produced for the BBC by Studio Ramsay Global - Gordon Ramsay's television production company.

The description for applicants reads "Whether you're hoping to build confidence in the kitchen, master a cherished family recipe, learn a new cooking skill for an important life moment - if food has the power to change something in your life, we'd love to hear from you."

Anyone aged over 18 can apply now until October 31, but has to be available for filming in November in the UK.

## Home renovations

![]()Homes under the Hammer aims to help those who have bought a house at auction *(Image: BBC)*

Homes Under the Hammer is perfect for those looking to make the most out of property they have recently bought at auction.

Presenters Martin Roberts, Dion Dublin, Martel Maxwell, Tommy Walsh and Jacqui Joseph can help create a dream home or undertake a renovation before selling it on.

The programme is open for people to apply with applicants required to be 18 or over.

## Face The Dragons

![]()The Dragons are looking for more entrepreneurs to invest in *(Image: BBC)*

The Dragons are on the hunt for new applicants to find entrepreneurs to invest in.

People looking to pitch ideas and gain advice from some of the most successful business moguls in the country can register their interest for the next series now.

## You're hired!

![]()Applications for The Apprentice are now open *(Image: BBC)*

For other business-savvy individuals, people are also able to apply to the next series of The Apprentice until January 2027.

After beating 11 other candidates, the winner will receive an investment of up to £250,000 and will go into a 50:50 business with Lord Alan Sugar.

## Thrifting with Stacey

![]()New participants are needed for Joanna Page's show Shift that Thrift *(Image: BBC)*

Joanna Page of Gavin and Stacey fame needs couples, friends or family duos who are happy to compete against each other for the second series of her show Shift the Thrift.

The pairs, who should apply by September 27, will try to make the most money for a good cause close to their hearts by auctioning off items they have found in a charity shop.
