The bomb squad destroyed the object in an controlled explosion, surprising walkers, cyclists and others on Hob Moor.

Army bomb experts arrived, identified the object as a flare and took it to Hob Moor, a distance by car of more than a mile.

They also ordered the family and others living nearby to evacuate their houses and for a time officers prepared to seal off part of nearby Acomb Road.

They called in police, who called in the Army.
