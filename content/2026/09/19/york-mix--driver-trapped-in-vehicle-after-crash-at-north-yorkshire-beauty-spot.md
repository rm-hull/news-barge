Emergency services were called to a single vehicle crash in North Yorkshire last night.

Crews from Skipton and Harrogate went to the incident at **Bolton Abbey** at**11.34am**.

The driver was trapped in the vehicle.

#### Bolton Abbey

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews stabilised the vehicle and used e-draulic cutting equipment to release the casualty into the care of paramedics.”
