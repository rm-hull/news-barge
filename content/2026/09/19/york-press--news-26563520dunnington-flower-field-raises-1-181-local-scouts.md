A £1,181 donation is helping support new adventures for young people in Dunnington.

The funds were raised by Dunnington Flower Field during its £1-a-bunch end-of-season sale and given to Dunnington Scouts.

Roger Sturmey, group lead volunteer for Dunnington Scouts, said: "We were bowled over by the generosity of Dunnington Flower Field and everyone who bought a bunch of flowers this past weekend.

"It shows what a community can achieve a pound at a time, and it will make a real difference to the young people we support.

"On behalf of our leaders, our Board of Trustees and all our Beavers, Cubs, Scouts and Explorers, thank you."

The donation is one of the largest single donations the Scout Group has received from a local business and will help fund equipment, activities and trips for the Beavers, Cubs, Scouts and Explorers who take part in Scouting across Dunnington and the surrounding area.

Dunnington Flower Field, based at Church Balk, welcomed visitors from July until its seasonal closure, offering hand-picked bouquets from thousands of blooms alongside refreshments at its Horse Shoe Café.

Organisers have encouraged people to return when the field reopens next season.

Dunnington Scouts says it will share more details in the coming months on how the donation is put to use supporting local children and young people.
