## Ska-pop band Madness to headline York Racecourse evening of racing and music

Legendary ska-pop band Madness will headline York Racecourse's Music Showcase Friday on 23 July 2027, combining live racing with a major concert as part of the band's wider UK tour *(Image: Supplied)*

Legendary ska-pop band Madness will headline an evening of live racing and music at York Racecourse, it has been announced.

They will perform at York Racecourse on Friday, July 23, 2027, as part of their wider tour and Music Showcase Friday.

James Brennan, head of marketing and sponsorship at York Racecourse, said: "The energy and sense of fun that Madness bring to every show is just what people need – a fun evening, hopefully in summer sunshine, that they can really look forward to enjoying.

"Everyone will have a favourite Madness song and it will be fantastic to hear them ring out around the Knavesmire.

"We will be on sale with a price freeze for next year, so it makes sense to lock in the date."

With 15 UK top-10 singles, including One Step Beyond, Baggy Trousers and It Must Be Love, Madness promise a night of nostalgia and high-energy entertainment.

Our House remains their biggest US hit.

The concert will follow six races, including the £70,000 European Breeders Fund Lyric Stakes.

Tickets cost from £42 per person for groups of six and are available at yorkracecourse.co.uk.
