Amanda and Clive Owen already lived on one of Britain’s best-known farms when they took on a huge second property.

Millions knew Ravenseat from Our Yorkshire Farm, where viewers watched Amanda, Clive and their nine children build a life on the remote 2,000-acre Swaledale farm.

So why did the couple buy a derelict farmhouse nearby and spend years turning it back into a family home?

The answer starts with one crucial fact about Ravenseat.

Although Ravenseat became synonymous with the Owen family, it was never theirs to own forever.

Amanda and Clive farmed there as tenants.

Anty John’s offered them something different.

## Why did Amanda and Clive Owen buy Anty John's?

Amanda and Clive bought the neighbouring property in 2020.

Known locally as Anty John’s, the old stone farmhouse was in ruins and needed major work.

Channel 4 says the Owens bought it to secure their family’s future.

![]()Anty John's *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Ravenseat supports the family and is where Amanda and Clive raised their children, but they farmed it as tenants rather than owners.

Anty John’s gave them the chance to build a permanent home in the Swaledale landscape where their children grew up.

On Our Farm Next Door, Amanda said she wanted the family to have a home of their own.

That ambition became the More4 programme’s central story.

## What is Anty John's?

Anty John’s is a centuries-old farmhouse near Ravenseat.

When Amanda and Clive bought it, the farmhouse was nowhere near ready for family life.

They have tackled the roof, walls, floors, access and interior while working on a historic property in one of the Yorkshire Dales’ most exposed areas.

Channel 4 described the programme as the family’s attempt to "breathe life back" into the derelict property, with all nine children helping alongside local tradespeople.

The project has taken years rather than months.

Early episodes focused on securing and rebuilding the farmhouse.

The family then tackled the interior, planning room layouts, installing plumbing and bringing in running water.

The renovation’s success on television led Channel 4 to order third and fourth series of Our Farm Next Door.

## How big will Anty John's be?

The project grew as the work progressed.

Programme information says planners allowed the work to extend into an adjacent barn, creating a four-bedroom family home.

That matters for a family of 11.

Amanda and Clive have nine children, Raven, Reuben, Miles, Edith, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy, although several older siblings are now building independent lives and careers.

Anty John’s is not replacing Ravenseat; it is becoming a long-term base for the wider family.

## Why not simply stay at Ravenseat?

Ravenseat remains at the heart of the Owen family's farming life.

It remains Amanda and Clive’s working base and appears alongside Anty John’s in Our Farm Next Door.

Miles works on the family hill farm, while Reuben’s contracting company still lists Ravenseat as its registered office.

But being a tenant is very different from owning a property.

However attached a family becomes to a rented farm, owning a property offers greater long-term security.

That makes Anty John’s more than an ambitious television makeover.

Channel 4 repeatedly says the property was bought with the family’s future in mind.

It also explains why Amanda and Clive continued working together despite announcing the end of their marriage in 2022.

Their relationship changed, but the farmhouse’s purpose remained.

Ravenseat may always be the farm that made the family famous.

But Anty John’s could give Amanda, Clive and their children what Ravenseat could not guarantee: a permanent home of their own in the Yorkshire Dales.
