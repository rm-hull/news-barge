Families will be able to enjoy a festive day out in Darlington this Christmas as the town's popular ice sculpture trail returns.

The free event, organised by Darlington Borough Council, will take place across the town centre on Saturday, December 12.

Shoppers and families will be able to explore a series of ice carvings displayed at locations around the town throughout the day.

![]()The date for Darlington's popular town centre ice sculpture festival has been set. *(Image: ANDY FUTERS)*

The sculpture trail has become a popular part of Darlington's Christmas programme in recent years.

It draws crowds into the town centre to see the intricate ice creations.

This year's sculptures have not yet been revealed, but previous trails featured famous characters and festive favourites.

![]() *(Image: ANDY FUTERS)*

Last year's event featured 14 ice sculptures across the town centre.

Among them were carvings inspired by popular characters including Peppa Pig and SpongeBob SquarePants, alongside a selection of traditional Christmas-themed designs.

Families followed the trail through DL1, Grange Road, Duke Street and Northgate to find each sculpture.

More details about the sculptures and accompanying activities will be announced closer to the event.
