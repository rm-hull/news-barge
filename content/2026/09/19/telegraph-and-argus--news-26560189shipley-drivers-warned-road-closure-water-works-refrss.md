A SHIPLEY road is set to close temporarily due to water works.

Back of Moorview Avenue will shut from 7am on September 28, until 7pm on October 2.

The closure will affect the stretch from outside the side of Number 2 Moorview Avenue to its junction with Moorview Avenue.

Bradford Council stated the closure could remain in effect for up to 18 months if needed.

However, the prohibition will not apply to vehicles linked to the works.

Pedestrian access will be maintained.

The council has not provided any alternative routes for affected vehicles.

The notice reads: "NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council by reason of water works proposed to be executed on or near Back of Moorview Avenue, Shipley intends not less than seven days from the date of this Notice to make an Order the effect of which will be that no person shall proceed in the length of Back of Moorview Avenue."
