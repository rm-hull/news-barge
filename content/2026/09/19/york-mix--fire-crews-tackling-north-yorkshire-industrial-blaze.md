Fire crews are tackling a blaze in a North Yorkshire town this morning (Saturday).

They were called to the incident in **Sherburn in Elmet** at**12.05am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews from Tadcaster and Selby, with assistance from two appliances from West Yorkshire, responded to an industrial fire.

#### Sherburn in Elmet

“Crews used two breathing apparatus, two hose reel jets, saws, small tools and ladders in order to access and extinguish the fire.

“This incident is ongoing.”
