Strictly Come Dancing is back for its 2026 series, and it will see a whole host of new professional dancers join the show.

Aleksandra Isaeva, Cristian Priori, Lethabo Monametsi, Maddie Ingoldsby, and Mark Karmalita will be the new faces joining up with celebrity contestants.

They replaced Karen Hauer, Nadiya Bychkova, Luba Mushtuk, Michelle Tsiakkas, and Gorka Marquez.

With the new series getting underway, viewers might be curious to know how much the professional dancers get paid.

## How much do the pro dancers on Strictly Come Dancing get paid?

Dancers are reportedly paid a flat fee of between £35,000 and £50,000 per series, according to The Metro.

They can also earn additional income by taking part in the Strictly Come Dancing: The Professionals tour, which takes place in the first half of the year following each series.

## Who are the new Strictly presenters?

Claudia Winkleman and Tess Daly left Strictly at the end of the 2025 series and have been replaced by three hosts.

Strictly professional Johannes Radebe will host alongside TV presenter Emma Willis and comedian Josh Widdicombe.

The BBC show has never had three people hosting it at one time until now.
