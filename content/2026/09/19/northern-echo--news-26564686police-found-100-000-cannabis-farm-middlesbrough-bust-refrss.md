Three men have been charged after a cannabis farm worth up to £100,000 was found in Middlesbrough.

Officers from Middlesbrough’s South Neighbourhood Policing Team attended an address on Addington Drive on Thursday, September 17, after receiving intelligence about suspected drug activity at the property.

A cannabis farm was discovered inside the property and subsequently dismantled by Cleveland Police.

Three men aged 23, 26 and 33 were arrested at the property and have since been charged with being concerned in the production of a Class B drug.

![]() *(Image: Picture: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Chief Inspector Ian Mitchell, of the Neighbourhood Policing Team, said: “Illicit drugs cause significant harm in our communities and our officers will continue to remove them from our streets.

“Please continue to report things to us; your information helps us build a bigger picture of what’s happening in our communities.”

The discovery follows a series of large-scale cannabis farm finds in Middlesbrough over the last few years.

In February 2024, police seized 826 cannabis plants from two Middlesbrough properties, with the drugs valued at about £826,000.

Anyone with information about suspected drug activity can report it to Cleveland Police by calling 101 or through the force’s website.

Information can also be passed anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers online or by calling 0800 555 111.
