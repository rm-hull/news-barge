A road in York is set to close for more than a month.

City of York Council has announced that Monkgate Yard will be shut between its junction with Monkgate to the south-western boundary of property No. 1.

The closure is necessary for water mains works and will start at 8am on Monday, September 21, and is expected to end at 5pm on Thursday, October 22.

The road will be closed to vehicles, with parking and waiting also prohibited.

Access for residents and emergency vehicles is not expected to be maintained during this period.

Alternative routes for diverted vehicles will not be signed.

The council has stated that traffic signs and barriers will indicate the extent of the closure.

The order does allow for emergency access and access to premises if it is not blocked by the works.

The road will reopen earlier if works are completed sooner.
