A new shop opened at Darley Street Market on Saturday.

Skincare brand TALLOWÉ opened its first ever retail space at the market this morning.

An Instagram post by Bradford Markets said: "TALLOWÉ is now open at Darley Street Market.

"Come on down, discover more about their amazing business, grab a special goodie bag and a cake pop and see the Mirror Man.

"TALLOWÉ is an independent British skincare brand founded and owned by Nuzhat Rashid, built around the belief that skincare should be pure, ethical, handcrafted and personal.

"You can find them on the Lifestyle Level from Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5.30pm.

TALLOWÉ makes ethical, handcrafted, personalised skincare using halal, grass-fed tallow as a key ingredient.

Before the opening, Ms Rashid said: "I created TALLOWÉ because I wanted skincare to feel more personal and purposeful.

"My background has always been in customer care, so listening to people and understanding what they actually need has naturally become a huge part of the brand.

"Seeing customers share their skin journeys and the difference our products have made for them has been incredibly rewarding.

"Opening our first physical space in Bradford is an exciting next step, but as we grow, I never want us to lose the personal care and attention TALLOWÉ was built on.

"We’re really hoping to make the day feel welcoming, exciting and a lovely introduction to TALLOWÉ for people visiting Darley Street Market."

The brand was developed with input from a pharmacy professional with more than 20 years' experience and a passion for natural apothecary.
