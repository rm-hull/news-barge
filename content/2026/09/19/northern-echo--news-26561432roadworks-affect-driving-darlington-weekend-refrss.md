Roadworks will affect four roads in Darlington this weekend.

Here are the roads impacted in the town this weekend (September 19-20):

### Sadberge Road

Temporary traffic signals are in place on Sadberge Road in both directions between Flat Hill Close and the A67.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 20.

### Auckland Oval

Construction is affecting Auckland Oval in both directions at A68 West Auckland Road.

Restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 22.

### Auckland Avenue

One lane traffic is in place on Auckland Avenue in both directions between Brinkburn Road and Auckland Oval because of construction.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 22.

### Winchester Way

One lane traffic is in place on Winchester Way in both directions at Rochester Way because of electricity work.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until October 15.
