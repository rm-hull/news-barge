Rail passengers travelling between Newcastle and Edinburgh are being warned of major disruption this weekend as upgrade works close the East Coast Main Line.

No trains will run on the usual route between Newcastle and Edinburgh on Saturday, September 19, and Sunday, September 20, with replacement buses operating for passengers using stations including Morpeth, Alnmouth and Berwick-upon-Tweed.

The engineering work is the first of three consecutive weekends of disruption, with further work planned for September 26 and 27 and October 3 and 4.

National Rail says all lines between Newcastle and Dunbar/Edinburgh will be closed this weekend, affecting LNER, CrossCountry, Lumo, Northern, TransPennine Express and ScotRail services.

![]()An LNER train at Darlington train station *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

LNER will not run trains between Newcastle and Edinburgh via Berwick-upon-Tweed.

Instead, an hourly service will operate between Newcastle and Edinburgh after being diverted via Carlisle, meaning journeys could take considerably longer than normal.

Replacement buses will also operate between Newcastle and Edinburgh, calling at Morpeth, Alnmouth, Berwick-upon-Tweed, Reston and Dunbar.

CrossCountry trains will also not operate between Newcastle and Edinburgh, with replacement buses calling at Morpeth, Alnmouth and Berwick-upon-Tweed.

TransPennine Express will have no trains between Newcastle and Edinburgh and will instead provide replacement road transport.

The operator says buses will run between Newcastle and Berwick-upon-Tweed, calling at Cramlington, Morpeth and Alnmouth, while other replacement services will operate between Berwick and Edinburgh via Reston, Dunbar and East Linton.

There will also be changes to Northern services in Northumberland.

Buses will replace trains between Newcastle and Morpeth and Chathill, while replacement transport will also be used between Northumberland Park and Ashington.

Passengers travelling between Newcastle and Northumberland Park will be able to use their rail tickets on the Tyne and Wear Metro at no extra cost.

Lumo will run a reduced and amended timetable, with its trains operating between London King's Cross and Newcastle only rather than continuing north to Edinburgh.

The disruption forms part of planned upgrade works taking place over three consecutive weekends between Newcastle and Edinburgh.

Similar restrictions will be in place on Saturday, September 26, and Sunday, September 27, before a further closure on Saturday, October 3, and Sunday, October 4.

TransPennine Express says there will be no direct trains on the usual route between Newcastle and Edinburgh during the first two weekends, with replacement bus services provided along the route.

On October 3 and 4, the disruption will affect the route between Newcastle and Dunbar, while trains will be able to operate between Dunbar and Edinburgh.

Passengers have been urged to check their journeys before setting off and allow additional time, with National Rail warning that replacement vehicles may be busier than usual.
