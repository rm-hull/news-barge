Many cinema-goers factor parking into the cost of a trip to the pictures, particularly when venues advertise free or discounted stays for customers.

However, a closer look at the parking arrangements for three popular cinemas in Bradford and Leeds shows that the offers are not always as straightforward as simply turning up and watching a film.

While ODEON Luxe Leeds Bradford, The Light Bradford and Cineworld Bradford all provide parking concessions for customers, each venue operates a different system. Depending on where you're visiting, you may need to register your vehicle on a touchscreen tablet, validate a parking ticket with a member of staff, or complete additional steps before leaving.

The details are available online, but they can easily be overlooked when booking tickets or heading straight into a screening. As a result, some visitors may be unaware that claiming free parking often requires action on their part.

To help film fans navigate the different arrangements, here's a breakdown of what each cinema offers and what customers need to do.

## ODEON Luxe Leeds Bradford

Movie-goers visiting ODEON Luxe Leeds Bradford can park at Gallagher Leisure Park free of charge for the duration of their film.

However, customers are required to enter their vehicle registration number into the touchscreen tablets located inside the cinema lobby.

The free parking period covers the length of the screening, with normal restrictions applying outside of that allowance.

## The Light Bradford

Customers visiting The Light Bradford can take advantage of up to five hours of free parking at The Broadway car park.

To receive the concession, visitors must register their vehicle on the in-cinema tablet before paying through the car park app.

The five-hour allowance means many guests will have plenty of time to enjoy a film as well as food, drinks or shopping in the city centre, but registering the vehicle remains an important part of the process.

## Cineworld Bradford

At Cineworld Bradford, customers using The Leisure Exchange car park can benefit from free parking after 6.31pm.

For those visiting earlier in the day, the current daytime charge is £1.50.

Unlike the other cinemas, visitors must take their parking ticket to a member of staff at the cinema counter, who will validate it for exit from the car park.

Without validation, customers may not be able to access the cinema parking concession.

## Different cinemas, different rules

Although all three cinemas offer parking benefits to customers, the process differs significantly between venues.

Some require motorists to register their vehicle electronically, while others rely on ticket validation by a member of staff.

For regular cinema-goers, the varying arrangements may be second nature. But for occasional visitors, the differences can be easy to miss, particularly when heading straight to a screening.

With parking costs often adding to the price of a night at the cinema, understanding the different systems in place can help visitors make the most of the concessions available at each venue.
