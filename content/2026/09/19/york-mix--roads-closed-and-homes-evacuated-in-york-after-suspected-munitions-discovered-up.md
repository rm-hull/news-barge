Homes were evacuated in York after the discovery of suspected munitions today (Saturday).

Police cordoned off **Windmill Rise** in Holgate this afternoon and called in Army bomb experts.

One man who was visiting on the street said police had told him that it was a “suspected Second World War bomb” that had been found.

The suspect device was found in the garage of a house very close to the famous Holgate Windmill.

Homes closest to that property were evacuated. People elsewhere on the street were told to stay indoors until the all clear.

That has now been given and the cordon lifted.

This afternoon North Yorkshire Police gave this update: “Thank you for your patience this afternoon.

“A flare device was taken away and destroyed in a controlled explosion at a safe location. The cordons have now been removed and local residents are heading back home.”

The controlled explosion was on Hob Moor.

Earlier, North Yorkshire Police posted: “Our officers are in the Windmill Rise area of York, because suspected historic munitions have been found in a garage there.

“Experts from the Army are on their way to make sure it’s safe, but in the meantime a number of surrounding homes are being evacuated.

“Some roads in the area – including Acomb Road – will also need to be closed.

“Your safety is our priority, so thank you for your support and understanding while we do this.

“Please avoid the area for the time being, and speak to our officers at the scene if you have any concerns.”
