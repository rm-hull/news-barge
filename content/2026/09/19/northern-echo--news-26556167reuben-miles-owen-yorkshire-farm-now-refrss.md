Amanda Owen’s children worked on Ravenseat Farm long before millions of viewers knew their names.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess and her former husband, Clive, raised their nine children at the remote Swaledale farm, where Our Yorkshire Farm cameras captured lambing, livestock care and wall repairs.

The children viewers watched grow up are now building adult lives and careers of their own.

Amanda and Clive’s eldest sons, Reuben and Miles Owen, have stayed closest to farming.

Reuben has built a business and television career around machinery, while his younger brother Miles is now a full-time farmer.

![]()Reuben Owen *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Now there is a clue about which Owen child could eventually take greater responsibility for the family farm.

### What is Reuben Owen doing now?

Reuben was barely a teenager when viewers first saw his love of diggers, tractors and machinery.

He has since turned those skills into his own career.

Reuben runs Reuben Owen Ltd, a groundworks company based at the head of Swaledale which specialises in environmental and drainage work.

Companies House records show the business remains active in 2026, with its registered office at Ravenseat Farm.

Reuben remains its director.

The company website says it operates a range of machinery and has taken on plant and groundwork projects since expanding into the sector in 2020.

Reuben has also carved out a television career away from his parents.

He has fronted Reuben Owen: Life in the Dales on Channel 5, following his contracting work and rural life with friends and family.

Its third series aired in 2026, with Miles and younger brother Sidney among family members appearing alongside him.

Reuben remains rooted in the Yorkshire Dales, but his working life now stretches far beyond helping his parents at Ravenseat.

### What is Miles Owen doing now?

Miles is following a more traditional farming path.

His current professional biography describes him as a full-time farmer who divides his time between Ravenseat and working alongside Reuben on his groundwork and digger business.

He is particularly interested in sheep and cattle and hopes to move into arable farming.

Miles has also developed considerable experience operating diggers and plant machinery - skills he has put to use during his parents' enormous restoration of Anty John's farmhouse.

He has also continued appearing on television, including in Our Farm Next Door and alongside Reuben in Life in the Dales.

But Miles’s future ambition will interest viewers who have followed the family for years.

### Who could eventually run Ravenseat?

Miles' current biography says that at some point in the future he hopes to "take charge of the family farm" or establish a farm of his own.

Amanda and Clive have not announced a formal succession plan, so Ravenseat cannot be described as promised to Miles.

![]()Miles in a tractor near Anty John’s *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

But there is a major complication.

The Owens do not own Ravenseat outright, but have farmed the property as tenants.

Any long-term future at Ravenseat would depend on the family’s tenancy and decisions by Amanda, Clive and their children.

Miles is the Owen child who has most clearly expressed an ambition to take responsibility for the family farm.

Reuben, meanwhile, is increasingly established in his contracting business.

The brothers still work together, however.

Reuben’s programme has shown Miles learning more machinery and contracting skills, while both remain involved in the family’s farming life.

Their different paths are another sign of how much has changed since Our Yorkshire Farm first made the Owens household names.

Some of the nine children have moved far from hill farming, while Reuben and Miles remain closely tied to the rural world where they grew up.

For viewers wondering whether the next generation will continue the family’s farming story, Miles’s ambitions offer the clearest clue yet.
