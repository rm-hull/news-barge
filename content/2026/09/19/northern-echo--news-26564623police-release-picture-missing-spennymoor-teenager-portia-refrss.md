Police have released a picture of a missing 16-year-old girl from County Durham with links to Sunderland, as efforts to locate her continue.

Concerns grow for Portia, who was last seen leaving her home in Spennymoor at around 10am on Wednesday, September 16.

Officers say the teenager may be in the Newcastle area and is also known to have links to Sunderland.

Portia is 5ft 3in tall, has blonde hair and was last seen wearing a grey fur coat.

![]()Portia, 16, from Spennymoor has been reported missing. *(Image: Durham Constabulary)*

A spokesperson for Durham Constabulary said: "Have you seen Portia, who has been reported missing?

"The 16-year-old was last seen leaving her home in Spennymoor at around 10am on Wednesday (September 16).

"Portia may be in the Newcastle area and has links to Sunderland.

"She is 5ft 3in tall with blonde hair and was last seen wearing a grey fur coat."

Anyone who has seen Portia or knows where she is should call police on 101, quoting incident number 537 of September 17.
