DESIGNED to embrace its remarkable setting and with expansive glazing, balconies and terraces creating a wonderful connection between the interior and the surrounding landscape, Goldrill can truly be described as a house that stands out from the crowd.

After all, how many homes have Durham Cathedral and Castle as their backdrop?

It is thanks to its elevated position on Farnley Hey Road in Durham that Goldrill enjoys not only exceptional views towards Durham Cathedral, but also a leafy and established residential setting that offers convenient access to the city.

![]()Everything about Goldrill is stunning, from its contemporary accommodation to the view over Durham City that encompasses the cathedral and castle. *(Image: Bradley Hall)*

Its stylish accommodation – which extends to approximately 3,067 sq. ft – starts at an impressive entrance hall with herringbone flooring that immediately sets the contemporary tone of the interior.

From here the accommodation flows into a beautifully appointed kitchen fitted with an extensive range of quartz-topped cabinetry, underfloor heating and appliances that include an AEG microwave oven, dishwasher, wine cooler and integrated washing machine, together with an induction hob complemented by gas cooking.

The kitchen flows naturally into the dining and living space, creating a superb area for both everyday family life and entertaining, with bifold doors that open onto a Juliet-style balcony and bring an abundance of natural light into the room.

![]()Everything about Goldrill is stunning, from its contemporary accommodation to the view over Durham City that encompasses the cathedral and castle.ault *(Image: Bradley Hall)*

Centred around a contemporary media wall with an inset electric fire is the principal living room, the highlight of which is a large picture window that frames far-reaching views across Durham towards the Cathedral and Castle, creating an exceptional focal point and making the outlook part of the room itself.

To the rear of the property is an additional kitchen comprehensively fitted and incorporating an induction hob, dishwasher and central island.

Completing this floor are an additional sitting room which could be used as a home office, with French doors opening directly onto a balcony overlooking the gardens; a cosy snug off the living room; a shower room and two double bedrooms, each with their own en-suite shower room.

![]()Everything about Goldrill is stunning, from its contemporary accommodation to the view over Durham City that encompasses the cathedral and castle.ault *(Image: Bradley Hall)*

Off the first floor landing which is spacious enough to be used as a home office or study area are a family shower room and three bedrooms, including one with an en-suite bathroom and the principal bedroom with a dedicated dressing area and an en-suite shower room.

Both this bedroom and the bedroom with the en-suite bathroom have bifold doors opening onto their own private balcony from where you can soak up the views that make this home so special.

At basement level the property offers further useful ancillary space comprising a wine cellar and workshop that is ideal for storage, hobbies or practical household use.

The outside space is a significant feature of Goldrill.

![]()Everything about Goldrill is stunning, from its contemporary accommodation to the view over Durham City that encompasses the cathedral and castle.ault *(Image: Bradley Hall)*

The house opens onto extensive elevated terraces and balconies with contemporary glass balustrading, creating superb spaces for outdoor dining, entertaining and enjoying the views.

Steps descend from the upper terrace to the lower garden, with the wine cellar positioned beneath.

Beyond the terraces lie a substantial lawned garden bordered by mature trees and established planting.

![]()Everything about Goldrill is stunning, from its contemporary accommodation to the view over Durham City that encompasses the cathedral and castle.ault *(Image: Bradley Hall)*

Goldrill’s grounds extend to around 0.4 acres and also include electric gates that open onto a generous driveway which provides ample off-street parking and access to a single garage with a door into the kitchen.
