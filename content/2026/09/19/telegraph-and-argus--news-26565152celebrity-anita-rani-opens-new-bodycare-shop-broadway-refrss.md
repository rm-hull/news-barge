Celebrity Anita Rani celebrated the opening of a new Bradford city centre shop by having a “red-carpet” makeover.

Ms Rani, TV presenter and recent contestant on Race Across the World, officially opened the Bodycare Studio inside the new Bodycare shop in The Broadway Bradford on Saturday.

Ms Rani said: "I wanted to launch this new shop because I used to go to Bodycare at the Arndale Centre when I was growing up.

![]()Anita Rani holds a pair a giant scissors ready to do a ribbon cutting at the new Bodycare Studio *(Image: Newsquest)*

"I was born in Bradford and spent a lot of time here. I also love a good beauty product that is good value.

"There is a vibrancy in the city centre and a shop like Bodycare will help bring the centre to life.

"You can do all your shopping in Bradford. There is no need to go to Leeds or anywhere else."

![]()Min Sandhu gives Anita Rani a 'red-carpet' makeover *(Image: Newsquest)*

Crowds gathered to see Ms Rani cut the ribbon and officially open the new store.

Tash Courtney-Smith, chief marketing officer for Bodycare, said: "We’re really excited Anita is going to be the subject and model for a Asian party make-up masterclass hosted by celebrity make-up artist Min Sandhu."

Ms Rani also addressed the crowd, adding: "It’s so nice to be back in the city where I was born and bred with these comedy giant scissors that I’m going to be cutting the ribbon with.

"Then I’ll be in this room with Min having my make-up done, an Asian glam red-carpet makeover.

"I hope you’re all going to spend a lot of money in this fantastic store.

"Do you know what we love in Bradford? A bargain, and Bodycare is full of them.

"It’s all about being iconic on a budget."

![]()Min Sandhu gives Anita Rani a 'red-carpet' makeover *(Image: Newsquest)*

Ms Courtney-Smith said: "It’s fantastic Bodycare has been brought back to the High Street.

"It’s great to have support from the community and it’s wonderful to have Anita here."

Emma Bellamy, head of retail operations at Bodycare, explained the company’s recent journey.

She said: "It’s welcome home.

"I’d been working for Bodycare for 28 years when I started out as a Saturday girl at age 16.

"We’ve opened this shop now after having completely empty shelves only ten days ago."

Shoppers Jade Logan and Taty Smith, both from Bradford, welcomed the reopening.

Ms Logan said: "We started shopping at Bodycare maybe five or six years ago.

"We used to meet up and shop together, quickly go to Bodycare and then go for a night out.

"It was great to see Anita Rani today because I watched her on Race Across the World."

Dr Freyaa Ali, who runs Dr Freyaa Aesthetics, said: "It’s super exciting to see Anita Rani.

"She’s Bradford-born and is an inspiration for South Asian women."

Her sister, Aiman Ali, said: “I feel she’s representing South Asian women on social media.

“Anita is breaking the norms and is showing everyone anything is possible.”
