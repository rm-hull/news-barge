# Man jailed after online sex chats with children

## A man who pretended to children he was a police officer before carrying out sexual offences against them has been jailed.

Aaron Grimshaw, of no fixed abode, would communicate online with children using social media and Bradford Crown Court heard the conversations were of a sexual nature.

After his arrest his phone was seized, and evidence of the conversations were downloaded.

He was sentenced to five and a half years, made to sign the Sex Offenders’ Register and given a Sexual Harm Prevention Order.

![Aaron Grimshaw](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjZlZWU0NjgzLTA0NTctNDVjZi1iNDU4LWEzMWZkMmU3MGViNjoyMzQzMDkwMi1iODFjLTRhZGUtYmQxNC1mYjFlOWRjNTkzYTE=.jpg?trim=228,0,705,0&width=640&quality=65)


There were multiple offences including sexual assault on a female, sexual activity with a child, engage in sexual communication with a child and making indecent photograph of a child.

Grimshaw also was sentenced for offending in Gloucestershire.

In May, Grimshaw, from Sedbury, admitted assaulting two girls at Tutshill recreation ground, near Chepstow, after approaching them while claiming to be a police officer.

DC Emma Moist, of Bradford Districts Safeguarding Unit, said: “Grimshaw’s sentence reflects the seriousness of his actions.

“Sexual communication with a child can cause significant harm to young people and their families.

“We remain committed to protecting children, supporting victims and bringing offenders to justice.

“We take all reports of this nature seriously and will investigate thoroughly."
