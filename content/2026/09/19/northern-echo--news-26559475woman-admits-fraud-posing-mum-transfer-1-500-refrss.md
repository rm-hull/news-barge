A fraudster posed as her mother to transfer almost £1,500 into her own account.

Rebecca Cowie admitted nine charges of fraud by false representation when she appeared in Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court.

The 41-year-old contacted the bank several times in June last year to transfer sums ranging from £50 to £265.

Her solicitor, Jaxon Taylor, said illness had stopped Cowie attending a probation appointment and asked magistrates to adjourn sentencing until a report was completed.
