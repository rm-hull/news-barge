From a weekend shopping trip to dinner and a trip to the cinema, the Metrocentre has been a familiar destination for generations of North East families.

The Gateshead shopping centre is home to more than 270 shops and 60 places to eat, alongside a wide range of leisure and entertainment venues.

Located just off the A1 at Gateshead, the centre attracts shoppers from across the North East and beyond, with fashion, beauty, technology and homeware retailers all under one roof.

Whether you're planning your first visit or simply want to know what is happening on your next trip, here's everything you need to know about the Metrocentre.

## When does the Metrocentre open?

![]() *(Image: METROCENTRE)*

The Metrocentre's regular shopping hours are:

* Monday: 9am to 9pm
* Tuesday: 9am to 9pm
* Wednesday: 9am to 9pm
* Thursday: 9am to 9pm
* Friday: 9am to 9pm
* Saturday: 9am to 7pm
* Sunday: 11am to 5pm
* Bank holidays: 10am to 6pm

Restaurants and leisure venues can stay open later than the main shopping centre, while individual shops may also operate different hours, so visitors are advised to check with the retailer before travelling.

## What shops are at the Metrocentre?

![]() *(Image: Kevin Gibson Photography)*

There's plenty to choose from if you're planning a shopping trip, with the Metrocentre saying it has more than 270 stores.

Some of the biggest names shoppers can expect to find include Primark, Marks & Spencer, Next, TK Maxx, Zara, H&M and JD Sports, covering everything from fashion and footwear to beauty and household shopping.

There are also specialist stores throughout the centre, including jewellers H. Samuel and Ernest Jones, while the Metro Retail Park next to the centre is home to other major retailers, including Smyths Toys Superstores.

As opening times can differ between individual retailers and can change during bank holidays and the Christmas period, shoppers should check their chosen shop before setting off.

## Where can you eat at the Metrocentre?

![]() *(Image: FILTO)*

Visitors certainly shouldn't be short of options if they want something to eat, with around 60 food and drink venues available at the centre.

Options highlighted by the centre include restaurants and food outlets such as Pizza Express, Pizza Hut, YO!, Chiquito, Thaikhun, Maki & Ramen and Wingstop, alongside cafes and places to pick up something sweet.

Many food and leisure businesses remain open after the main shops shut, making it possible to stay at the centre for an evening meal or entertainment after a day of shopping. Exact closing times vary by operator.

## What is there to do besides shopping?

![]() *(Image: KEVIN GIBSON)*

The Metrocentre isn't just about shopping.

Visitors can combine a trip to the shops with a film at the ODEON cinema, while leisure attractions include Namco Funscape and other entertainment venues.

The centre's events listings also regularly feature activities aimed at families and children, while its wider leisure offering means there are options for visitors who aren't looking to spend the entire day browsing shops.

## What events are coming up at the Metrocentre?

![]() *(Image: Kevin Gibson Photography)*

The Metrocentre has a programme of events throughout the year, ranging from family activities to shopping events and employment fairs.

One of the next major dates in the diary is the Big Student Shop, which takes place on Thursday, September 24, 2026.

Students who register can collect a wristband giving them access to more than 100 offers, with DJs, activities, giveaways and food discounts also planned.

The special student offers are scheduled to run from 4pm until 9pm, and students need valid student identification to use them.

The Metrocentre Job Fair, held in partnership with the Newcastle United Foundation, is also scheduled to return to Exhibition Square on September 24 and again on October 15 and October 22, with sessions running from 4pm until 7pm.

Businesses involved in the September programme have included the likes of Primark, Footasylum, Hotel Chocolat and Boots.

Elsewhere, the centre's current What's On listings include its Retailer Awards 2026, a 40th Birthday Giveaway with Feeding Families, and activities ranging from a British Sign Language taster programme to Young Drivers sessions.

And for families, Smyths Toys Superstores at the Metro Retail Park is holding a catalogue launch event on Saturday, September 26, beginning at 9am.

The retailer says activities are set to include face painting and giveaways, subject to availability.

## Where is the Metrocentre?

The Metrocentre is located at Russell Way, Gateshead, close to the A1 and on the western side of Gateshead.

It can be reached by road as well as public transport, with a railway station and bus interchange serving the shopping centre.

## Is the Metrocentre open on Sundays?

![]() *(Image: METROCENTRE)*

Yes. The main shopping centre is normally open between 11am and 5pm on Sundays.

Food, cinema and some leisure businesses can operate outside the centre's core retail hours, however, so anyone visiting for a particular restaurant, film or activity should check its individual opening time.

## What else should I know before visiting?

With hundreds of shops as well as restaurants and leisure attractions, it can be worth checking the centre map and deciding which part of the Metrocentre you want to head to before arriving, particularly if you're visiting for one retailer or event.
