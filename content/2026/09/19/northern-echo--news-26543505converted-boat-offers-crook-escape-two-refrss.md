A converted boat offers a one-of-a-kind escape in the heart of County Durham.

Betsy's Boat, situated beyond the market town of Crook, provides a tranquil escape amid rolling countryside, just a short drive from Durham's historic city centre.

The barge has been transformed into an open-plan space with a lounge, kitchen, and sleeping area.

![]() *(Image: BETSYS BARGE)*

![]()Betsy's Barge. *(Image: BETSYS BARGE)*

![]() *(Image: BETSYS BARGE)*

Its curved ceiling and large windows flood the interior with natural light and offer panoramic countryside views.

![]() *(Image: BETSYS BARGE)*

The compact kitchen is fully equipped with a toaster, microwave, fridge, and kettle.

![]() *(Image: BETSYS BARGE)*

At the rear of the barge, a modern shower room offers a walk-in shower and toilet facilities.

![]() *(Image: BETSYS BARGE)*

Betsy's Boat also has a private decked balcony.

![]() *(Image: BETSYS BARGE)*

![]() *(Image: BETSYS BARGE)*

The furnished outdoor space is ideal for al fresco meals or enjoying the peaceful views.

One guest left a five-star review: "This is a beautiful barge and it was lovely and quirky.

![]() *(Image: BETSYS BARGE)*

"So peaceful, it’s wonderful getting up in the morning and looking at the view.

"It’s amazing.

"It was well equipped and lovely and clean and onsite restaurant is gorgeous."

The boat is about 2.5 miles from Crook, a market town with local shops and a weekly food market.

The barge is a base for exploring Weardale and the North Pennines, from peaceful moors to riverside walks.

A local pub, The Dairy Barge, is within 500 metres of the barge.

The café/restaurant offers ice cream, afternoon tea, breakfast, loaded fries, hot drinks and more.
