A boozed-up man assaulted his then-partner after she refused to give him money.

Liam Shipley grabbed the woman and bent her fingers back when she tried to escape.

The 29-year-old turned up at her home after a night of drinking and demanded entry, Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard.

She let him in through a window, but he became angry when she refused to give him cash, the court heard.

Prosecutor Jade Houston said: “At this point, he has pushed her to the floor and she could hear her children shouting.

“She managed to get away from him and he pulled her back and bent her finger back.”

Shipley, of Hawthorne Close, Langley Park, County Durham, pleaded guilty to two counts of assault by beating on March 6 this year on the day his trial was due to start.

His solicitor Joshua Peacock said: “He accepts that he has committed these offences which is why he pleaded guilty and he has since been co-operating with the probation service.

“He was under the influence of alcohol at the time. He says it isn’t a massive problem but at the time he was probably drinking too much.”

Magistrates’ chairman Thomas Reay gave Shipley a 12-month community order, including 80 hours of unpaid work, and a 12-month restraining order.

He said: “If you should see her, it is in your interest to come away from her as there is only one person who would get into trouble and that would be you.”

Shipley must also complete a six-month alcohol treatment programme and 24 days of rehabilitation activity.
