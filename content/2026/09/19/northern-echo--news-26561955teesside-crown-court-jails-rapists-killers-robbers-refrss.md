Here are some of the people locked up last week at Teesside Crown Court.

They include rapists, killers and robbers.

### Spennymoor rapist

A County Durham rapist who preyed on three vulnerable girls was branded “entitled” and a “misogynist” as he was caged for 13 years.

Harrison Strange was a teenager when he sexually abused the three girls, including two while on bail after his arrest for the first rape.

The 21-year-old targeted the teenagers when they were heavily intoxicated before forcing himself on them, Teesside Crown Court heard.

His victims described the devastating impact the abuse had on their young lives.

Annie Richardson said the first teenager was attacked in a field while drinking with friends in County Durham.

Miss Richardson said the second teenager was raped at Strange’s home after turning up for what she believed was a party.

She said Strange plied the girl with alcohol before attacking her in his bedroom while her friend slept on the floor.

The third attack took place at a campsite in North Yorkshire, where Strange raped and sexually assaulted a teenage girl who had been drinking while her friend slept in the same tent.

![]()Harrison Strange *(Image: Durham Constabulary)*

Judge Richard Bennett sentenced Strange to 13 years in prison for the three rapes.

“The impact on each of these girls has been significant,” he said. “Your pre-sentence report makes troubling reading."

### Norton manslaughter

Three men who lured a man into an alleyway on New Year’s Eve and assaulted him over a £900 drug debt have been jailed.

Max Cashman, Declan Moor and Lee Bradshaw conspired to attack Aiden Matthews over a debt he had run up to fund his crack cocaine addiction.

The 31-year-old was ‘given a slap’ before his head was smashed against the ground, fracturing his skull.

Moor and Bradshaw followed the father-of-two into the alleyway, where he was knocked unconscious, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Jamie Hill KC said Mr Matthews sold drugs for Moor but had run up a personal debt with 30-year-old Cashman.

“Mr Matthews was assaulted because of that debt,” he said. “Although, he was communicating with Moor about the money there is some evidence that he owed the debt to Cashman.”

Cashman, of no fixed abode, and 22-year-old Bradshaw, of Vale Drive, Thornaby, both pleaded guilty to manslaughter.

Moor, of Centenary Crescent, Norton, was convicted following a trial.

![]()Max Cashman, left, Lee Bradshaw, centre, and Declan Moor, right, have been jailed for the manslaughter of Aiden Matthews. *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Cashman, of no fixed address, was jailed for six years after pleading guilty.

Bradshaw, of Vale Drive, Thornaby, was jailed for five years and four months after pleading guilty.

Moor, of Centenary Crescent, Norton, was jailed for six years and six months after being found guilty at trial.

He died at University Hospital of North Tees on January 3, 2026, with his family beside him.

### E-bike robbers

Two thugs who forced a man off his £6,000 e-bike have been jailed for robbery.

Callum Briggs and Joshua Peters blocked the victim’s path as he rode through Middlesbrough, then asked him if he sold drugs.

The pair then forced the man off his bike on a bridge over the A66 before fleeing, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Judge Nathan Adams jailed Briggs for two years in a young offenders’ institute and Peters for 30 months.

He said: “This must have been quite a scary incident for him, with the two of you ganging up on him out of public sight.”

### Abusive driver

An "abusive" driver spotted outside a suspected drugs den was caught carrying a lock knife on his keyring.

Carl Farrington stopped in the Stockton street but drove off when officers approached his car, Teesside Crown Court heard.

The 57-year-old pulled over a few streets away and was found with a lock knife on his car keyring.

Prosecutor Elisha Marsay said officers were on Brittannia Road dealing with suspected drug dealers when Farrington drove into the street.

Farrington became abusive before driving off and being arrested a short distance away on Castlereagh Road, she said.

![]()Carl Farrington *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Farrington, of High Street, Wolviston, admitted possessing a bladed article after his arrest on July 24.

Farrington was serving a suspended sentence for possessing a class A drug with intent to supply when he committed the latest offence, the court heard.

“The police saw you pull up at a property they were investigating,” he said. “It was a marked police car. It was an officer in police uniform who asked you to stop and instead you drove off.

“I don’t accept for a moment that you didn’t realise they were police - it would have been abundantly clear.”

### Darlington rapist

A man who raped an unconscious girl as a teenager, left her asleep, then returned to rape her again, showed no remorse when he was sentenced.

Charlie Dobbing attacked the girl who fell asleep after a cannabis-smoking session, a court heard.

He left her, then returned to the bedroom and raped her again.

Dobbing was a teenager when he carried out the two rapes, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Paul Cleasby said the girl was heavily intoxicated.

He said Dobbing straddled the teenager’s chest, pinned her to the bed and forced himself on her during the second rape.

![]()Charlie Dobbing *(Image: Durham Constabulary)*

Dobbing, of Coxwold Drive, Darlington, was convicted of two rapes after a trial.

“While she was drifting in and out of consciousness, she felt like she was still aware of what was going on but she couldn’t move,” he said.

“You took the opportunity to go upstairs where she was effectively incapacitated – aware of what was going on but unable to consent to the activity.”

Dobbing was given a 10-year restraining order and ordered to remain on the sex offenders’ register for life.
