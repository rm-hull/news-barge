A new artist has joined an art auction in a York town raising money for humanitarian work in Gaza.

Eve Hart is the latest artist to contribute to the auction, which will raise money for Medical Aid for Palestinians to provide urgent care in Gaza and the occupied West Bank.

The auction will be held at Haxby and Wigginton Methodist Church on September 25.

![]()One of the paintings on offer at an art auction in Haxby supporting Medical Aid for Palestinians *(Image: Supplied)*

Sue Cooke, event organiser, said: "This is a great opportunity to come together in support of MAP's vital work and show solidarity with besieged Palestinian families."

The evening will feature homemade cakes, savoury bakes and a talk on MAP’s work by its community events manager, Zahra Yassine.

![]()One of the paintings on offer at an art auction in Haxby supporting Medical Aid for Palestinians *(Image: Supplied)*

A Banksy print paying tribute to Damien Hirst will also go under the hammer.

Catherine King, Eve Hart and Roswitha Jarman’s family have donated artworks, including a collection of watercolours.

Donations can also be made through JustGiving at justgiving.com/page/sue-cooke-1.
