**\*\* This problem has now been fixed \*\***

From Saturday 19 September 2026 at 9:34AM

There’s disruption on the railway this morning (Saturday).

It’s due to a points failure between **Leeds and Doncaster**.

LNER says: “Trains running towards Doncaster will be delayed when passing between these stations. Services may also be altered or cancelled at short notice.

“We’re working with Network Rail to restore normal working as quickly as possible.”

You can find more information on its website here.

Northern Trains posted on X: “There is disruption between Leeds and Wakefield Westgate due to a points failure.

“Train services running through these stations may be cancelled or delayed by up to 30 minutes.”
