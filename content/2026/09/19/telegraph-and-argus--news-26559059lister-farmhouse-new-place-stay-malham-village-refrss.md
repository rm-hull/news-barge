Visitors to the Yorkshire Dales have a chic new spot to rest their heads after a day of exploring.

Lister Farmhouse - which has seen the transformation of part of a 19th-century coaching inn in Malham - is a collection of 13 uniquely designed country bedrooms.

![]()Lister Farmhouse *(Image: Submitted)*

It joins The Lister Arms and Lister Barn as part of Thwaites’ wider Lister collection in Malham, giving guests a choice of farmhouse bedrooms, a traditional country inn and larger group accommodation. Room types at Lister Farmhouse include family rooms.

Interiors were led by Jane Goff of Goff Associates, who also headed up interior design at Langdale Chase on Lake Windermere.

![]()Lister Farmhouse *(Image: Submitted)*

The 13 bedrooms have been individually designed to reflect the character of the building and surrounding landscape, with rich textures, bespoke furnishings and premium finishes.

Mick Horan, director of inns at Thwaites, says: 'This exciting project has diversified and bolstered the ways visitors and locals alike can enjoy the charming village of Malham.

![]()Lister Farmhouse *(Image: Submitted)*

'It means that in the village you can now stay at our original inn, our spacious barn, or our brand-new farmhouse bedrooms. Malham welcomes such a wide range of visitors, so it was important to us to create versatility in our offering, with something for everyone.'

Executive chairman Rick Bailey adds: 'With a building of this age and significance, the design process was all about careful stewardship – a sensitive renovation that honours the past rather than overwrites it.

![]()Lister Farmhouse *(Image: Submitted)*

'By introducing rich textures, bespoke furnishings and premium finishes, we’ve been able to deliver a sense of understated elegance.

'We were also keen to incorporate notes of the neighbouring landscape; Lister Farmhouse so luckily stands in one of Yorkshire’s most stunning vistas.

'This is a thoughtful reimagining, and we’ve allowed place, heritage, and luxury to take centre-stage.

![]()Lister Farmhouse *(Image: Submitted)*

'With nearly 220 years of hospitality behind us, projects like this one really show what we’re all about.

'We’re big on craftsmanship, quality, innovation and warm hospitality, and the Lister Farmhouse is only one part of our continued investment across Thwaites.

![]()Lister Farmhouse *(Image: Submitted)*

'One renovation at a time, we’re highlighting the country’s irreplaceable hospitality and hotel offering. We’re really excited about this opening, and we hope guests love it as much as we do.'

![]()Lister Farmhouse *(Image: Submitted)*

Thwaites has also appointed a new executive head chef to oversee dining at The Lister Arms and The Buck village pub.

The Lister Arms focuses on seasonal takes on classic dishes alongside daily-changing chalkboard specials, while The Buck serves home-cooked pub food and ales in a more casual setting for family lunches, walkers and post-hike drinks.

From Malham, guests can explore some of the Yorkshire Dales’ best-known walking routes and landmarks, including Janet’s Foss, Gordale Scar, Malham Tarn and Malham Cove.

Room rates for the Lister Farmhouse start at £185 B&B per night in low season and £230 in high season.
