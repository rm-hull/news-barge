STUDENTS will have the chance to take part in a high-energy shopping challenge this September.

Trinity Leeds is bringing back its Student Shopper Sweep, a full day of giveaways, entertainment and the chance to win shopping sprees.

The event will take place on Thursday, September 24, from 11am to 7pm, with a new £100 shopping spree winner selected every 30 minutes from 12pm onwards.

Steven Foster, centre director at Trinity Leeds, said: "We’re excited for the return of our student event and the Shopper Sweep competition, giving students the chance to kick off the new academic year in style with an unforgettable shopping experience.

"It’s a fantastic way to welcome new students for the first time, or to welcome returning students back to the city."

Over 25 brands will take part, including Footasylum, Levi’s, Nike, and H&M.
