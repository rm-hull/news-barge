A BRADFORD barber is bringing some darts-themed fun to customers by offering discounts to those who can impress on the oche.

Christopher Blake, owner of Blake's Barbershop in Wibsey, said the challenge has become a popular feature of the business, with more than 100 customers already giving it a go.

After initially introducing it last year, the barbershop owner has reintroduced the scheme to see if anyone else would like a chance.

Mr Blake said the idea was introduced to bring a bit of entertainment to the shop.

![]()The interior of Blake's barbershop, where the Luke Littler special is now available. *(Image: Christopher Blake)*

## 'Adults and kids love it'

He said: "To bring some fun and laughter to the barbershop. Adults and kids love it.

"Children usually go for bullseye and stand a bit closer. I have also give discount and more sweets for the children that do."

The challenge has proven so popular that customers often take part before leaving after their haircut.

![]()The dart board set up, so that anybody who can score 180 wins a free haircut. *(Image: Christopher Blake)*

## Inspired by darts boom

The promotion was first launched last year and quickly proved a success.

Mr Blake said: "We ran the special offer last year and it was a big hit. People were really trying hard to get that 180.

"We even have our mascot, our border terrier called Mabel. Every customer usually has ago before they leave. Over 100 people have had a go."

![]()Christopher Blake, owner of Blake's Barbershop *(Image: Christopher Blake)*

## Praise for Luke Littler

Mr Blake is also a fan of teenage darts sensation Luke Littler, whose rise has helped bring new audiences to the sport.

He said: "Yes, what an amazing guy - to do what he has done for the sport.

"He has got young and older fans.

Mr Blake added: "Unbelievable darts player, best I've ever seen, although I may be a secret Michael van Gerwen fan."

![]()Luke Littler

## More specials could follow

The barber said he has previously run similar darts-themed promotions for younger customers.

He added: "The bullseye discount I've done before for kids as they love it..[its] so much fun and great banter - even if they may hit my wall."

With customers continuing to test their aim in search of a discount, the challenge has become a regular part of life at the barbershop.
