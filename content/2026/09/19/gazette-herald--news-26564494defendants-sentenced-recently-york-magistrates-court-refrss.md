Two thieves, a violent man, an axeman, a suspected drink driver and a knifeman all kept their freedom after their cases were heard recently at York Magistrates Court.

Melanie Longstaff, 45, of Wilfred Street, Sunderland, pleaded guilty to stealing a television worth £389 from Tesco on Clifton Moor and stealing cosmetics worth £165.50 from Boots in Thirsk, and failure to attend court.

She was given an 18-month community order with nine months' drug rehabilitation and 10 rehabilitative activities and ordered to pay £389 compensation to Tesco and £165.50 compensation to Boots.

Michaela Allan, 36, of Brook Street, off Clarence Street, York, pleaded guilty to theft of Nutella spread worth £3.75, theft of trifles and Nutella worth £10.35 and theft of food worth £10 on separate occasions from the BP garage on Lawrence Street, York, theft of food worth £70.65 from the Co-op on East Parade, York, and theft of food worth £38.20 from Tesco on Gillygate, York.

She was given a 12-month community order with six months' drug rehabilitation and 20 days' rehabilitative activities and ordered to pay a total of £132.95 compensation to the shops.

Lee Botterill, 37, who gave his address as Hamilton Drive East, Holgate, pleaded guilty to failure to provide a sample of breath when suspected of drink driving and two charges of failure to attend court.

He must wear an alcohol abstinence tag for 120 days and was banned from driving for 17 months.

Deborah Madden-Brooke, 66, of The Crescent, Filey, pleaded guilty to breaching a restraining order and was jailed for 20 weeks including a period previously suspended.

Adam Thackway, 38, of Flaxman Avenue, Tang Hall, pleaded guilty to assaulting a police emergency worker, assaulting a woman, and breach of a restraining order.

He was given an eight-week prison sentence suspended for 18 months.

He must pay the woman £100 compensation and the police officer £50 compensation.

Jordan Fletcher, 30, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to carrying a Stanley knife in Shambles Market and possessing cocaine.

He was given a 12-month community order with 15 days' rehabilitative activities and 120 hours' unpaid work and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

The knife was confiscated.

Kevin Jackson, 44, of Cemetery Road, central York, pleaded guilty to assaulting a woman, criminal damage to two cars owned by the woman and a man and one of carrying an axe in public in York.

He was given a 12-month prison sentence suspended for two years on condition he does a 26-day rehabilitative programme and 12 days' rehabilitative activities.

He was made subject to two two-year restraining orders to protect the owners of the car and must pay £650 compensation to the owner of one of the cars.
