CAMPAIGN group ACORN has announced a date for negotiations with Yorkshire Water bosses following a protest outside the company's Bradford headquarters last month.

Representatives from the organisation are due to meet Yorkshire Water chief operating officer Matt Rice on Monday discuss a range of issues including water bills, debt collection practices, executive bonuses and environmental performance.

The talks follow ACORN's national "Dignity Not Debt" day of action on August 20, when around 50 members marched to Yorkshire Water's Bradford offices and called for discussions with company leaders.

## Campaigners to present demands

Ahead of the meeting, ACORN said it will urge Yorkshire Water to end the use of bailiffs for debt collection and prevent water bill increases above inflation for the next five years.

ACORN national chair Chelsea Phillips said the meeting offered Yorkshire Water an opportunity to respond to concerns being raised by customers.

## Meeting follows growing criticism

ACORN says it has previously secured negotiations with a number of utility providers, including Welsh Water, Severn Trent Water, United Utilities and Octopus Energy.

The organisation argues that water companies should invest more heavily in infrastructure and customer support, rather than prioritising shareholder returns and executive pay.

Yorkshire Water have also confirmed that a meeting is due to take place on Monday.
