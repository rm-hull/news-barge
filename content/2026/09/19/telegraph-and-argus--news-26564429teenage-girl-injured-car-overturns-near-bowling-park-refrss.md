A TEENAGE girl was injured when a car overturned in a collision near a Bradford park.

Police were called to the scene on Avenue Road near Bowling Park at 3.30pm on Friday.

An teenage girl suffered minor injuries in the incident which saw the vehicle overturn on the road.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police were called to Avenue Road, Bradford at about 3.30pm on Friday to a report of a collision involving a car on Avenue Road.

"A teenage girl received a minor injury in the incident and collection was arranged for the vehicle."

The Telegraph & Argus has also asked Yorkshire Ambulance Service for comment.
