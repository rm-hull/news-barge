## North Yorkshire charity seeks runners to help transform lives through music

Trustee Andrea Hayes (right) with Foundation CEO Cathy Grant (left) who will join the team to run the 10k *(Image: Supplied)*

A North Yorkshire charity is urging runners to take on a major challenge to transform children's lives through music.

Ten runners from Music Builds Minds will take part in the Yorkshire Coast 10K in Scarborough on Sunday, October 11, 2026, to raise money for the Richard Shephard Music Foundation.

The group aims to fund weekly music lessons for primary school pupils in Scarborough and across the region.

![]()Music lessons at schools across the region are supported by the Foundation *(Image: Supplied)*

Andrea Hayes, a former headteacher and trustee of the foundation, said: "Music inspires, unites and empowers. The Foundation brings that power into classrooms – ensuring every child, whatever their background, can access high-quality music teaching."

The Richard Shephard Music Foundation currently delivers weekly curriculum music lessons to more than 500 pupils in Scarborough.

Funds raised through the 10K will help the foundation reach even more children.

![]()Foundation logo *(Image: Supplied)*

The charity believes every child should have the chance to experience the benefits of making music, including greater confidence, creativity, and communication skills.

A spokesperson for the Richard Shephard Music Foundation said every donation will support its work in Scarborough.

More information about the foundation and its programmes is available at www.rsmf.org.uk.
