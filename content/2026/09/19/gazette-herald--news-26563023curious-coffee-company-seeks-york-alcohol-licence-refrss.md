A York coffee shop has applied for permission to sell alcohol, according to a public notice.

The Curious Coffee Company at 86 Clifton, York, could soon sell alcohol if its new premises licence is approved.

Edward Copley-Farnell of Brooklands, Thirsk Road, Easingwold, York, submitted the application.

The proposal would allow alcohol sales from 11am to 10pm on Mondays to Wednesdays and Sundays.

On Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays, alcohol sales would continue until 11pm.

The application was filed on August 26, and objections or other representations can be made until September 23.

Representations must be made in writing to the City of York Council Licensing Team at Hazel Court, EcoDepot, James Street, York, YO10 3DS, or by email to [licensing@york.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing@york.gov.uk.)

The application can be viewed by appointment at the City of York Council Licensing Team, Hazel Court, EcoDepot, James Street, York, YO10 3DS, from 8.30am to 5pm on weekdays.

The council will decide the application in due course.
