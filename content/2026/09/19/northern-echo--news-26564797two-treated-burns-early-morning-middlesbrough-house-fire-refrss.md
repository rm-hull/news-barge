Two people were treated by paramedics after a fire caused severe damage to a house in Middlesbrough.

Emergency services were called to Hollyhurst Avenue in Saltersgill at 5.02am on Friday, September 18, after reports of a house fire.

Firefighters used four breathing apparatus sets, two hose-reel jets and two thermal-imaging cameras to control the fire.

The flames appear to have ignited in the living room, which was left completely destroyed in the fire.

The kitchen suffered some damage too, and the rest of the house was left with "heavy smoke damage", the brigade said.

Two people suffered smoke inhalation and minor burns to their hands.

Crews from North East Ambulance Service treated them at the scene.

Cleveland Fire Brigade said the blaze caused extensive damage to the property, destroying 100 per cent of the front living room.

A further 20 per cent of the kitchen was damaged by the blaze, with the rest of the house left with heavy smoke-damage.

Crews also used large fans to ventilate the property and clear the smoke from inside the house.

Three fire engines from Middlesbrough and Thornaby attended the scene, bringing the fire under control shortly after 6am on Friday.
