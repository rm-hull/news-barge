A thug violently attacked his former partner having just claimed he wanted to rekindle the relationship and left her a ‘hollowed-out shell’.

Matthew Hurst wrecked the woman’s house before leaving her unconscious in front of her young child, Teesside Crown Court heard.

The 25-year-old had previously strangled his partner in an earlier attack.

Prosecutor Richard Wilson said the injuries were consistent with the victim being repeatedly hit while on the ground, causing significant brain damage.

He said Hurst attacked the victim after she confronted him about messages to another woman, grabbed her by the neck with both hands and ended the relationship.

Mr Wilson said Hurst tried to rekindle the relationship that September before brutally attacking her.

“She heard shouting in the street and her next memory was waking up in a hospital bed six days later,” he said.

“The defendant had assaulted her causing a laceration to her left eyebrow, bruising to the face, bruising to her ribs and thigh as well as a bleed on the brain.”

She was unconscious when paramedics arrived at the Hartlepool property, the court heard.

Police found several smashed windows, while CCTV placed Hurst near the victim’s home.

Mr Wilson said the woman continues to suffer memory and hearing problems as a result of the brain injury.

Hurst, of Dunbar Road, Hartlepool, pleaded guilty to intentional strangulation in June 2023 and to causing grievous bodily harm and criminal damage on September 16, 2023.

His lawyer, Albany Kidd, said Hurst recognised the serious injuries he inflicted on his former partner were a ‘source of profound regret’.

She said: “He is remorseful and can’t begin to express to his victim and her family. He is disgusted with himself and his actions.”

Judge Jo Kidd jailed Hurst for five years and four months after he left his victim a ‘hollowed out shell’.

“I have seen no positive evidence that you have any remorse,” she said.

“In fact, your conduct during these proceedings has been distinctly lacking in any sort of reflection whatsoever about the horrendous consequences of your persistent violence towards your former partner.”
