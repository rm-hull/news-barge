DOMESTIC abuse crimes in West Yorkshire spiked during the World Cup, reaching their highest level on the night of England’s semi-final defeat.

Newly released police data shows 168 domestic abuse-related crimes were recorded on July 15, when England lost 2-1 to Argentina, marking the highest total on any England match day during the tournament.

The figures were obtained by 101 Great Goals through a Freedom of Information request.

Jon Fisher from 101 Great Goals said: "Football should bring people together.

"Whatever happens on the pitch, nobody should ever have to fear what might happen at home when the final whistle blows.

"Domestic abuse is complex and these figures alone cannot tell us what was behind every incident, but no football result can ever excuse abuse.

"We hope sharing these findings helps raise awareness, encourages people to look out for those around them and reminds anyone experiencing abuse that support is available."

Crimes rose above 150 on two other match days: June 23, when England drew 0-0 with Ghana, and June 27, when they beat Panama 2-0.

Of the 168 crimes recorded on July 15, a total of 65 resulted in one or more arrests, according to West Yorkshire Police.
