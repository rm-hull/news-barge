## Prince Harry said 'no child' should have to walk behind their mother's coffin

Prince Harry has previously spoken about the experience of walking behind his mother’s coffin, saying no child “should be asked to do that under any circumstances”.

In an excerpt of his memoir, Earl Spencer wrote about how he fought plans for William and Harry, then aged 15 and 12, to walk behind their Diana’s coffin during the funeral procession.

The Earl is said to have insisted to the King that Diana would not have wanted her young sons to have made the difficult public walk.

“He went nuts. ‘That’s the problem with your family. They’ve got no bloody sense of duty!’ he started, before descending into a general rage,” Earl Spencer wrote.

In June 2017, the Duke of Sussex said: “My mother had just died, and I had to walk a long way behind her coffin, surrounded by thousands of people watching me while millions more did on television.

“I don’t think any child should be asked to do that, under any circumstances. I don’t think it would happen today.”

In his memoir, Spare, the duke said he felt “numb” as he walked behind his mother’s coffin but was nearly brought to tears while listening to Sir Elton John perform during the service.
