One of the UK's biggest comedy stars is heading to Teesside later this year, bringing her razor-sharp wit to a major stand-up event.

Katherine Ryan will headline the next Comedy Forum at Forum Theatre Billingham on Sunday, November 22.

The Canadian comedian, writer and television personality is the latest big-name act to join the theatre's growing comedy programme, which has previously featured household names including Frankie Boyle and Nina Conti.

![]()Katherine Ryan is heading to the Forum Theatre in Billingham in November. *(Image: Tees Active)*

The announcement comes ahead of another big night at the Forum Theatre, with Irish comedy star Dara Ó Briain taking the stage in Billingham this weekend.

Best known for appearances on programmes including Mock the Week, 8 Out of 10 Cats and Taskmaster, Ryan has become one of the UK's most recognisable comedians.

Ryan has also enjoyed success with her Netflix stand-up specials and is known for her blend of fearless honesty, intelligent storytelling and observations on modern life, relationships and celebrity culture.

Danny McLoughlin, Faizan Shah and Scottish comic Mark Nelson will join Ryan on the Billingham bill.

Since launching, Comedy Forum has established itself as a regular stop for some of the UK's biggest comedy names, while continuing to showcase up-and-coming acts.

*Tickets for the November show are* * available from the theatre’s box office and website.*
