A crash involving a lorry earlier this morning has partly closed the A1 in the North East and caused traffic issues for those travelling the route.

The incident happened at around 6.15am on September 19 between junction 73 A694 Derwenthaugh Road (Blaydon / Consett Turn Off), and junction 74 (Newcastle City/Newburn Turn Off).

Emergency services and National Highways workers are currently on the scene to deal with the vehicles.

According to traffic site Inrix: "One lane closed and slow traffic due to a crash; lorry involved on the A1 Western Bypass, both ways, from J73 A694 Derwenthaugh Road (Blaydon / Consett Turn Off) to J74 (Newcastle City / Newburn Turn Off). Lane two (of two) is closed in both directions due to the accident and barrier damage."
