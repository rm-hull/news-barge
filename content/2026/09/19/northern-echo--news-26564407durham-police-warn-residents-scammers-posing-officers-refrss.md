Durham Constabulary have put out a warning to residents after reports of cold-calling from people pretending to be officers from the police force.

On Friday (September 18) evening, police said that the people pretending to be officers were trying to get personal details, including addresses, bank details, and driving licence details, from residents across County Durham.

Following the incidents, Durham Constabulary has put out a warning to residents to end the call if they're unsure who they're speaking with and check with family members or a trusted individual.

A spokesperson for the police force said: "Officers have received several calls this evening from people across the country who have been cold-called by someone purporting to be an officer from Durham Constabulary.

"The offender has attempted to con people into giving their personal details, including addresses, bank details, and driving licence details.

"The number displayed by the scammer is 0345 6060 365, which is the old number used by the force control room before changing to 101."

They added: "The police or other reputable organisations will never ask you for your bank account details.

"They will also never ask you to confirm personal details over the phone like this.

"If you are unsure whether a call is genuine, please end the call and contact a family member, a trusted individual, or call Durham Constabulary on 101 for advice.

"As these fraudsters often target the elderly or the vulnerable, officers are asking you to warn your parents, grandparents, and elderly neighbours.

"If you or someone you know has been targeted by this scam, call Report Fraud on 0300 123 2040 or contact them via their website, www.reportfraud.police.uk."
