A NEW bar that 'feels like a treat but remains affordable' has opened in a town centre.

Vinoteca Brighouse, on Bradford Road, is aiming to bring together Italian flavours and shareable dishes.

Although Silvia Ceperkovic is overseeing her first venue as manager, the business forms part of a wider hospitality group that already operates venues in Morley.

Rather than focusing on large meals, the venue specialises in smaller plates designed for sharing, alongside wines, cocktails and beers.

![]()A selection of cocktails served at Vinoteca Brighouse, the new Italian-inspired tapas and wine bar in Brighouse. *(Image: T&A)*

## Inspired by Italy from north to south

Ms Ceperkovic said: “We wanted something a little more relaxed where people can come in, enjoy a couple of drinks, have a few small plates and spend time together. It doesn't always have to be about sitting down for a big meal.

"We wanted to create somewhere that feels comfortable, welcoming and easy-going for everyone who walks through the door.”

The menu takes inspiration from across Italy, reflecting the backgrounds of team members from different regions of the country.

Ms Ceperkovic said: “We're not inspired by one particular place because the people behind the business all come from different backgrounds and different parts of Italy.

"Some of us have roots in the north and some in the south, so we wanted to bring a little bit of everything together and showcase the variety that Italian food has to offer.”

![]()The friendly staff at Vinoteca Brighouse, the new Italian-inspired tapas and wine bar in Brighouse. *(Image: T&A)*

## A menu built around sharing

While tapas is traditionally associated with Spain, Vinoteca has put an Italian twist on the concept.

Customers can choose from sharing boards, arancini, pizzas, pasta dishes and other small plates intended to be enjoyed together.

Ms Ceperkovic said the emphasis on sharing was central to the venue's identity.

She said: “Everything is meant to be shared because that's what food is all about for us.

"We want people to come together, spend time with friends and family, try different dishes and enjoy the experience.

"Sharing really is caring and that's something we've tried to build into the whole concept of the restaurant.”

![]()A wine display inside Vinoteca Brighouse, which specialises in Italian-style tapas, cocktails and wines. *(Image: T&A)*

## What's most popular on the menu?

Among her personal favourites are the grazing boards featuring cheeses and cured meats, as well as the restaurant's arancini dishes.

Ms Ceperkovic said: “The grazing boards are probably my favourite because they allow people to try a little bit of everything.

"The arancini has also been incredibly popular.

"We wanted a menu where people could order several dishes, put them in the middle of the table and enjoy a real variety of flavours rather than being limited to just one thing.”

![]()Vinoteca Brighouse's wine list and branding on display inside the town centre venue. *(Image: T&A)*

## Passion for wine

Wine will play a major role in Vinoteca's offering, with Ms Ceperkovic bringing her own expertise to the business.

Currently studying towards a Level 3 Wine & Spirit Education Trust qualification, she hopes to introduce customers to a wide range of wines while keeping the venue accessible and affordable.

Ms Ceperkovic said: “Wine is a huge passion of mine and something I've spent a lot of time studying. I'm currently working towards my next qualification and I love helping people find wines that they enjoy."

"We want to offer quality wines for people who know a lot about them, but also make the experience approachable for people who simply want to try something new, affordability has also been a major focus."

Ms Ceperkovic added: “Prices are going up everywhere at the moment and we know people are thinking carefully about where they spend their money.

"We wanted to create somewhere that still feels like a treat but remains affordable, so people can come in, relax and enjoy themselves without feeling worried about the bill at the end of the night.”

![]()A distinctive camper van-inspired table feature inside Vinoteca Brighouse, which offers Italian-style tapas, wines and cocktails. *(Image: T&A)*

## Creating a home from home

Looking ahead, Ms Ceperkovic said her ambition is for Vinoteca to become more than simply another bar or restaurant.

She hopes the venue will establish itself as a comfortable and familiar destination where residents can meet friends, unwind and feel part of the community.

Ms Ceperkovic said: “We want people to feel like it's their second home.

"From the very beginning, we've focused on creating somewhere warm and welcoming where people can come after work, meet friends at the weekend or just enjoy a quiet glass of wine.

"If people walk through the door and immediately feel comfortable, then we've achieved exactly what we set out to do.”
