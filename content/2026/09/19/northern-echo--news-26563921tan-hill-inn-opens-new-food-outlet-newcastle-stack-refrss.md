A North East entertainment venue has teamed up with the UK’s highest pub to serve British favourites to diners.

The Tan Hill Inn in upper Swaledale has opened a food outlet at St James’ Stack, serving dishes inspired by its classics.

The menu includes Yorkshire pudding wraps, traditional pies and fish and chips.

Andrew Hields, owner of the Tan Hill Inn and the new Stack outlet, said: "We have visitors who come to the Tan Hill Inn from around the world because it’s such an iconic place.

![]()an Hill Inn, the UK’s highest pub, has expanded to St James’ STACK in Newcastle, bringing its popular Yorkshire puddings, traditional pies, fish and chips and other British favourites to the North East *(Image: Supplied)*

"And along with its long history, it is also renowned for its fantastic, hearty food and that’s exactly what we’re now recreating at St James’ Stack."

Yorkshire pudding wraps come with slow-roast beef or pork, lemon and herb chicken, or vegetarian nut roast, plus buttered mash, honey-roasted carrots, seasonal greens and gravy.

The fillings are also available in stuffed Yorkshire puddings, alongside a dessert version with warm spiced apples and ice cream.

Tan Hill Inn pies are now on the menu at Stack.

Choices include steak and ale, chicken and ham, and vegetarian saag aloo with spinach, potatoes and Indian spices.
