Portakabin’s York manufacturing site has won recognition for its commitment to workplace health, safety and staff wellbeing.

The site received a Merit in the British Safety Council’s 2026 International Safety Awards, which recognise organisations that put employees’ health, safety and wellbeing first.

Greg Wood, head of manufacturing at Portakabin, said: "Achieving a Merit in the British Safety Council International Safety Award demonstrates that our organisation is committed to maintaining high standards of health, safety, and wellbeing.

"This achievement provides assurance to our employees, customers, and stakeholders that safety remains the primary focus within our business and that we are continually striving for improvement."

Wood said the company had built a culture where staff feel comfortable raising concerns, helping cut accidents by 61 per cent.

Mike Robinson, chief executive of the British Safety Council, said: "Our winners have all made significant and continued efforts to protect and improve the health, safety and wellbeing of everyone in their organisations.

"All of those working at Portakabin should be enormously proud of their achievement."

Portakabin CEO Dan Ibbetson presented the award to the team.
