A pilot raised the alarm during a late-night flight, prompting a large emergency response.

The incident happened at Leeds Bradford Airport, where the pilot of a private light aircraft sounded a crash alarm before midnight last night due to concerns about his fuel level.

A spokesperson for Leeds Bradford Airport said: "We had a private light aircraft come into land unexpectedly last night.

"The pilot was concerned about his fuel level so a crash alarm was sounded.

"The airport responded to the crash alarm and there was no emergency.

"He landed safely and taxied to Multiflight.

"The emergency services arrive as standard if a crash alarm is sounded.

"All OK."

Police confirmed there was an emergency presence at the scene but have not issued a statement.

Social media users reported seeing a large emergency services presence at the airport, with crews spotted "airside".

Despite the alarm, Leeds Bradford Airport confirmed that the pilot landed safely.
