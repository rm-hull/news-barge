PINING for the aromas and flavours of your Greek island holiday? Then head for Darley Street Market, where a bustling food outlet is serving up an array of tastes of Greece in delicious street food dishes.

Budhi Man Rai, owner of Let’s Go Greek, tells us more.

\* What kind of food do you serve?

We serve authentic and freshly prepared Greek cuisine, bringing the vibrant flavours and traditions of Greece to our local community.

Our menu features a selection of popular Greek dishes - gyros with lamb, chicken, halloumi, falafel, houmous and olives options, loaded fries and salad bowls. We’ve got sides as well, bringing the best healthiest food from Let’s Go Greek.

Our aim is to offer delicious, authentic Greek food made with quality ingredients and served with the warmth and hospitality that Greece is known for.

![]()'Greek cooking is strongly connected to family, tradition and sharing food with others' *(Image: Bradford Markets)*

\* How would you describe the business?

Let’s Go Greek is a popular and community wise place where people love to be inspired by the flavours and culture of authentic Greek street food. We are passionate about bringing Greek cuisine to our customers and creating an experience that feels warm, friendly and enjoyable. Whether customers are familiar with Greek food or trying it for the first time, we want everyone to feel welcome and leave with a taste of Greece.

![]()Falafels are among the traditional treats

\* How long has the business been going and what’s the background?

Let’s Go Greek was established this year with a passion for Greek food, culture and hospitality. The idea was to bring authentic flavours of Greece to the local community while creating a place where people could enjoy freshly prepared food in a relaxed, welcoming environment.

Since starting the business, Let’s Go Greek has continued to grow and build relationships with customers, becoming a recognised name for quality Greek food.

\* Do you see your business as part of the local community?

Absolutely. We are proud to be part of the community and enjoy serving customers from a wide range of backgrounds.

Food has always been an important part of Greek culture, bringing people together around the table. We hope to bring that sense of community, friendliness and togetherness to our customers.

\* Do you have a chef?

Yes. Our kitchen team is experienced in preparing Greek cuisine and takes pride in maintaining the quality, flavour and presentation of our food.

Our food is freshly prepared on-site using quality ingredients. Fresh preparation is an important part of delivering the authentic flavours and quality that our customers expect.

![]()The team at Let's Go Greek *(Image: Bradford Markets)*

\* Do you have themed events?

We are interested in taking part in themed events and special occasions celebrating Greek food, culture and community.

\* What do you enjoy about being part of Darley Street Market?

We love being part of the local food scene and having the opportunity to introduce customers to the flavours and traditions of Greece. The diversity of the local community makes it an exciting environment for a food business. We enjoy meeting new customers, hearing their feedback and seeing people come back to enjoy their favourite dishes.

\* Do you watch food programmes on TV?

We enjoy discovering new food ideas and learning about different cuisines and cooking techniques. Food programmes can be a great source of inspiration, particularly when they showcase traditional recipes, different cultures and the stories behind food.

\* What kind of food do you like to cook at home?

We enjoy preparing traditional dishes and family-style meals. Greek cooking is strongly connected to family, tradition and sharing food with others, and these values are an important source of inspiration for Let’s Go Greek.

It’s more than just a place to eat - it is about bringing people together through great food, Greek culture and genuine hospitality. Our goal is to introduce more people to the flavours of Greece while creating a friendly and welcoming experience.

We are grateful for the support of our customers and the local community, and we look forward to continuing to grow, serve great food and bring a little taste of Greece to the area.

\* Let’s Go Greek, Unit 68, Darley Street Kitchen, Bradford.
