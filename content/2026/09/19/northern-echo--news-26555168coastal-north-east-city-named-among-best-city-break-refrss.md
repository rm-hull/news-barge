A spot in the north east has been named one of the best places for a city break in the UK.

The Times has released its list of the best cities to take a trip to in the UK, with the list featuring your classics like London and Manchester to other cities that might get overlooked like Nottingham and Portsmouth.

Sunderland made it onto the list thanks to its "long and sandy" beach and opportunities to create the ultimate bar crawl.

## What is Sunderland like?

On the city, The Times said: “The Venerable Bede used to live in what became the northeastern beachside city of Sunderland.

“Bede was one of the smartest people to have graced the Earth, and the fact that he opted for Sunderland should be instructive.

“The city is brimming with fantastic indie establishments, while the new Culture House (an arts and library space conceived as a ‘living room in the city’) opening in September 2026 will provide the high street with a welcome shot in the arm.

“The beach is long and sandy, Roker Pier is a sight for sore eyes and there’s a stack of Lowry paintings at the Sunderland Museum.

“Coasteering is an option (think mountaineering but along the shoreline), skiing is doable (on a repurposed slag heap out at Silksworth) and there’s one heck of a bar crawl to be undertaken (that ought to include the Ship Isis and the Peacock).

“Eat at North, caffeinate at Pop Recs and Fausto Coffee then nip along the coast to Newcastle, which ain’t half bad either.”

Sunderland has plenty of history to explore at the Sunderland Museum, where you can learn more about the city’s history from its prehistoric roots to modern day with plenty of interactive displays for children.

It is also worth visiting the museum’s Winter Gardens that are filled with exotic plants from around the world.

The National Glass Centre is a great spot to visit for art lovers, as you can watch demonstrations of glassblowing techniques.

You can even take a tour at the Nissan Automotive Factory to learn about modern car manufacturing.

If you want to enjoy a more relaxing break in the city, you can visit Roker Pier for a walk along the coastline and look at the panoramic views of the sea.

You can then head to Seaburn beach for a stroll along the sand or to play a beach game.

After exploring, there are plenty of places to go for food, from pizza at Wild Fire to delicious curries and tandoori specials at My Delhi.

If you need somewhere to pop into for brunch, you might want to try Café Floriana to try out its speciality sandwiches, sourdough, and cakes.
