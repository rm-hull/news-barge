RESIDENTS are being invited to have their say on the Mayor’s draft Local Transport Plan at a series of drop-in events across North Yorkshire.

People can meet the team, learn more about the proposals and share their views in person at the sessions.

All events are open to the public, with attendees welcome to stay for as long as they wish.

A spokesperson for the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority said: "These events are an opportunity for residents to meet the team, find out more about the draft plan and share their views in person."

The events will take place at various markets and public venues across the region.

The schedule includes Tuesday, September 22 at the Great Harrogate Transport Fair, Friday, September 25 at Helmsley Market, and Saturday, October 3 at Malton Market, and Saturday, October 17 at York Designer Outlet.

Further details can be found on the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority website.
