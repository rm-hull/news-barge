A tiny North Yorkshire tearoom sitting beside one of the county's best-known waterfalls has won over visitors so much that one customer said their husband would make a four-hour round trip just to return.

Mill Race Teashop can be found inside historic Yore Mill at Aysgarth Falls in Wensleydale, overlooking the River Ure close to the Upper Falls.

The family-run business specialises in homemade baking and uses locally sourced ingredients and traditional family recipes.

![]()Mill Race Teashop can be found inside historic Yore Mill at Aysgarth Falls *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

Yore Mill itself dates back to 1784, giving visitors somewhere to stop for lunch, cake or afternoon tea in particularly historic surroundings after exploring the falls.

It has also proved a major hit with visitors on Tripadvisor, where Mill Race currently holds a rating of 4.9 out of five from more than 600 reviews and is ranked number one for coffee and tea in Aysgarth.

One family who visited in March 2026 said they had been “blown away” by the food and friendly service.

The reviewer added that although they wished they lived closer, her husband had said he would happily make the four-hour round trip simply to visit the café again.

Another visitor, writing in August, praised a generously sized quiche lunch accompanied by fresh salad and highlighted the large selection of cakes and bakes.

Others have singled out the afternoon teas, homemade traybakes, sandwiches, salads and locally inspired food, while the business also sells its baking online for delivery across the UK.

Its location makes the tearoom particularly convenient for people visiting Aysgarth Falls, one of the Yorkshire Dales' best-known beauty spots.

The café welcomes both dogs and muddy boots and does not take table bookings.

According to its website, Mill Race Teashop is currently open from 11.30am until 4pm from Monday to Saturday and is closed on Sundays.

The business does warn that it can occasionally close at short notice because of bad weather, however, and advises customers to check its social media pages before travelling.
