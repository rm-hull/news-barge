by Robin Longbottom

THE first Baptist church was built in the early 19th century and demolished later in the century to make way for a much grander building.

The late 19th century church was known locally as the Cathedral of Craven and included a chapel, school and minister’s house.

The chapel and school were demolished in 1970 after dry rot had taken its toll.

A new, smaller and modern chapel replaced it.

Today only the minister’s house survives as a reminder of this once grand structure.
