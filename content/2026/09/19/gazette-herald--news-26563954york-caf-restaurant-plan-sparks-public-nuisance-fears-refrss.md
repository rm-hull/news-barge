PLANS for a new café and restaurant in York have sparked fears over potential public nuisance.

An application to licence Unit 10, Ryedale Court in Haxby for its conversion into a new venue run by 99 Seats Ltd is set to go before councillors for a decision.

The business stated the café would serve hot and cold food during the day and the venue would operate as a restaurant with a full menu in the evening.

But a City of York Council report stated two objections had been made against the application.

It comes as the plans are set to go before a council licensing hearing on Friday, September 25.

The venue would open from 9am to 11pm daily and be licensed to serve alcohol from 11am if the council approves its application.

Additional conditions controlling how the venue would operate have been agreed with North Yorkshire Police.

The café and restaurant's application stated the venue would be well-managed.

Plans said: "We will maintain high standards of staff training, customer service, health and safety and responsible management to prevent crime and disorder, ensure public safety, prevent public nuisance and protect children from harm.

"The premises will provide a safe, welcoming and family friendly environment while operating with consideration for residents, neighbouring business and the wider community.

![]()The venue would operate as a café during the day and as a restaurant in the evening *(Image: Unsplash/Ibrahim Boran)*

"Staff will be trained in responsible customer service and where alcohol is supplied, in the responsible sale of alcohol and incidents of crime and disorder will be managed promptly and recorded."

The council's report stated the plans had prompted concerns.

It stated: "The representations are predominantly based on the grounds of the prevention of public nuisance objective.

"They state that this objective will be undermined if the application is granted."
