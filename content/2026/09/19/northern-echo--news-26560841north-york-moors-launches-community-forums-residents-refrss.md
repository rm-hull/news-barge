Residents can put their questions directly to the authority running the North York Moors at a new series of community forums.

The North York Moors National Park Authority is launching the forums so people can question its work, raise concerns and discuss local issues.

Three forums will be held each year, covering the park’s Southern, Western, and Northern and Coastal areas.

Tom Hind, chief executive officer at the North York Moors National Park Authority, said: "There are more ways than ever for a National Park Authority to share information far and wide, but nothing quite replaces being in a room together and having a two-way conversation.

"The forums give people the opportunity to hear directly from us about the work we are doing and the projects underway.

"They also give us a chance to listen to the people who know these communities and landscapes best, answer questions and hear what matters locally.

"By bringing these forums to different parts of the National Park, we hope to make it easier for people to find out what is happening, ask questions and have those valuable conversations face to face."

The first forum, covering the Southern Area, will be held at the Old Vicarage in Helmsley on October 8.

The Northern and Coastal Area forum will be held at Danby Lodge National Park Centre on October 21.

The Western Area forum will follow at the Old Vicarage in Helmsley on October 28.

Each meeting will run from 7pm to 9pm, with doors opening at 6.30pm.

The sessions will also be available online, helping people who cannot travel because of work or caring responsibilities to take part.

Topics will vary by area and include nature recovery, tourism, heritage and planning.

Full agendas for the 2026 forums are available at northyorkmoors.org.uk/communityforums.

Book a place online or call the authority on 01439 772500.
