A woman died after suffering a medical episode in a Bradford shopping centre’s food court.

Emergency services were called to The Broadway Bradford at around 1pm today after a woman suffered a medical episode in the food court.

The woman sadly died at the scene.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police were called to the food court at the Broadway shopping centre at about 1.12pm after a woman suffered a medical episode.

"To help preserve the dignity of the patient, members of the public were asked to leave the food court area while she was provided with emergency treatment.

"The woman sadly passed away at the scene."

A spokesperson for the Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We were called at 12.56pm today to a medical incident at the Broadway Shopping Centre in Bradford and a number of ambulance resources were sent to the scene.
