## Anna reveals life after Sewing Bee win as support pours in from York

Anna said she feels "so creatively charged" after winning The Great British Sewing Bee *(Image: BBC One)*

A York woman has received an outpouring of support after being named this year’s Great British Sewing Bee champion.

Anna, who was crowned this season’s winner of the BBC hit show in an episode which aired on Tuesday (September 15), said she the recognition since the show first reached our screen has been a “really nice experience”.

She added: “York is a city but it is like a small town, and I have lots of support from meeting people on the street and on social media.

“It is a really friendly city.”




On social media, Anna has been hailed for her skills and creativity.

One commenter, Claire Marvell said: “I was rooting for Anna from the beginning - I thought she’d do it, so creative and you could tell Patrick Grant in particular was mesmerised by her ‘thinking outside the box ‘

“It was definitely the right result.”

![]()The Great British Sewing Bee began with 12 contestants, with Anna shining in almost every episode *(Image: BBC/Love Productions/Neil Sherwood))*

Hilary Thomspon added: “You could tell from the way she herself dresses that she had a very unique and talented flair.

“Exceptional talent but all three finalists were outstanding. An enjoyable series.”

Anna, who begun working at St Leonard’s Hospice, won Garment of the Week in episodes three, five and seven and eight transformation challenge wins.

Speaking about this, she said: “I did think that maybe I had been doing quite well, but I was looking at what everyone else was making and saw that there was huge competition, so I was never 100% sure.”

Judge Patrick Grant said that after seeing a “spellbinding” dress from Anna’s competitor Beth, the competition had indeed been tough.

![]()Anna faced tough competition from contestant Beth (right) *(Image: BBC)*

Some on social media agreed.

Peter Clarke said: “She [Anna] smashed it. I thought Beth may have snatched it at the end.”

For Joanne Lamb, Anna was a firm favourite in her household.

She said: “[My daughter and I] watched episode 1 separately and I said, ‘I have my winner, let’s see if you pick the same’. We did.

“Was a close call with Beth who was amazing too.”

Anna began working at St Leonard’s Hospice in January this year.

Currently, she lives on a narrowboat with her boyfriend Sebastian, whom she met at a gig while at university.

After graduating they travelled around Europe together in a camper van before moving to York.

Anna described the boat they live on as “a shell” when they bought it, but with a little TLC and Anna’s creativity, they built their dream artsy home.

A lover of nature, Anna often wanders along the riverbank barefoot in the early morning, foraging for ingredients and watching the sunrise.

## 'I have so many ideas of things I want to make'

She said: “The main thing I will take away from this experience of being on The Great British Sewing Bee is a huge boost to my confidence and self-belief in my skills and talents.

“I am not sure what is out there for me in the sewing world.

“But I feel so creatively charged at the moment, I have so many ideas of things I want to make.

“I am going to keep pushing myself to make new things and learn new skills. I am also starting to take on commissions, and I’ve really enjoyed the challenge of making and designing for other people.”
