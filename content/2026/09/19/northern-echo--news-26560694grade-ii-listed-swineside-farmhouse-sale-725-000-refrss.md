Situated within the rural hamlet of Swineside, Windrush is a grade II listed farmhouse that offers buyers a wonderful combination of stunning views over Coverdale and characterful accommodation with an abundance of features including ceiling beams, stone flagged floors, fireplaces and window shutters.

The property is entered via the porch which leads into the dining area of the kitchen diner.

A spacious open-plan room at the heart of family life, this has stone flagged flooring and a beamed ceiling throughout, with the dining area being centred around an impressive stone fireplace housing a multi-fuel stove.

![]()Stunning views and characterful accommodation makes Windrush a Dales delight *(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

The adjoining kitchen is home to a range of oak fitted units complemented by granite worktops, with a cream AGA and a Belfast sink.

Steps lead down from here into the garden room which has a tiled floor with underfloor heating and, as you would expect, provides access to the gardens.

Also off the kitchen is a traditional utility room/pantry with stone shelves which leads into a cosy snug with a stone feature fireplace and a door out to the gardens.

![]()Stunning views and characterful accommodation makes Windrush a Dales delight *(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

Completing the ground floor are a home office and the living room which has a beamed ceiling, a stone open fireplace, an alcove with shelving and a window with a window seat to the front.

Two staircases lead to the upstairs accommodation — the main staircase which provides access to the family bathroom with a corner bath and three double bedrooms, and another in the office that leads to two bedrooms and a shower room.

This configuration would enable buyers to create self-contained accommodation for multigenerational living, should they wish.

The house sits in gardens laid to lawn, with a gravelled patio area, an enclosed wildflower patch, a useful garden store and a large specimen tree that makes a pleasant feature.

![]()Stunning views and characterful accommodation makes Windrush a Dales delight *(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

To the side of the property is a detached barn with a lean-to which has previously benefitted from planning permission for conversion into ancillary accommodation.

Whilst it has lapsed, a recent pre-planning application supported conversion to a holiday let or ancillary accommodation.

There is also a garage with parking for two vehicles together with a car port.

Windrush is located in Swineside, six miles from Middleham and eight miles from Leyburn.
