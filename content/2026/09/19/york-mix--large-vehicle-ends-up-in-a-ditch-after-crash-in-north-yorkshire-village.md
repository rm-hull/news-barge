Emergency services were called to a crash in a North Yorkshire village early this morning (Saturday).

It was in **Kilburn**, south of Thirsk, at** 12.49am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from Thirsk responded to a single vehicle road traffic collision involving a large vehicle that had slipped into a ditch.

#### Kilburn

“No people were trapped. Crews carried out general scene safety and inspected the vehicle for any fuel leaks.

“No action required and scene left with police.”
