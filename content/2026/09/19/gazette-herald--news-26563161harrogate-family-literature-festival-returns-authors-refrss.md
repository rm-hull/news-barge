A North Yorkshire town's popular family literature festival returns this autumn with bestselling authors, creative workshops and interactive adventures.

The Raworths Family Literature Festival takes place at Harrogate’s Crown Hotel on October 24, alongside the wider Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

The day will feature storytelling and hands-on activities designed to spark children’s imaginations and foster a love of reading.

![]()Rob Biddulph *(Image: Supplied)*

Rachel Tunnicliffe, senior partner at Raworths Solicitors, said: "We're delighted to welcome these brilliant, award-winning authors and illustrators to Harrogate.

"Stories and books can really inspire children and help them use their imagination, which is why we're proud to support this wonderful Festival."

Award-winning writer Joseph Coelho will lead an interactive poetry session for children aged 6 and over.

![]()Woodlore by Kevin Parr and Anja Susanj *(Image: Supplied)*

Illustrator Rob Biddulph, creator of the #DrawWithRob videos, will teach children to draw.

Bethany Walker will present Medusa Gorgon’s Deadly Side Eye, the second book in her diary-style series.

Kristyna Litten will combine storytelling, science and puzzles.

Kevin Parr will lead a forest adventure through Harrogate’s Pinewoods.

Fleur Hitchcock will invite children to join the Malory Towers Mystery Club.
