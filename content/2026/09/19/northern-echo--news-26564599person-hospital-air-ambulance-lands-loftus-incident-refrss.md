A person has been taken to hospital after the air ambulance landed in Loftus on Friday (September 18), following an "incident" in the town.

Emergency services were called to the Dam area of Loftus at around 3.17pm, following reports of an incident.

The Great North Air Ambulance and North East Ambulance Service both attended the scene.

![]()The air ambulance at the scene of the incident at Loftus *(Image: GNAAS)*

An air ambulance was seen landing in Loftus by residents.

It's since been confirmed that doctors from the air ambulance helped paramedics on scene, before a person was taken to hospital via road ambulance.

A spokesperson for the Great North Air Ambulance said: "Our doctor and paramedic team were activated to an incident at 3.17pm near Loftus; they flew to the scene and arrived within 12 minutes.

"They worked with the North East Ambulance Service to treat the patient before accompanying them to hospital."

Cleveland Police have been contacted for more information about the nature of the incident in Loftus.
