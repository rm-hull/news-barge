A dementia-friendly disco at an East Yorkshire care home brought residents, families and community members together for music, dancing and memories.

Stamford Bridge Beaumont Care Home hosted the event on Thursday, September 17, with MG-DJ Disco and Karaoke.

Nikki Mould, general manager, said: "We were delighted with the turnout for our Community Dementia Disco and it was wonderful to see so many people enjoying themselves.

"Music has an incredible way of bringing people together and creating meaningful moments."

The disco offered a supportive space for people living with dementia, their carers and loved ones.

Guests sang, danced and shared memories throughout the afternoon.

A resident at the care home said: "I had a lovely time.

"It was great to listen to the music, have a dance and meet new people.

"Everyone was smiling and enjoying themselves."

The care home hopes to make the disco a regular event.
