Anglers from across the UK and Europe will return to the East Yorkshire coast for a major beach fishing competition.

The 33rd Paul Roggeman European Open Beach Championship will run along the coast from March 5 to 7, 2027.

The competition will stretch from the northern end of North Marine Promenade in Bridlington to Pilot Jetty at Spurn Point.

Councillor Nick Coultish, cabinet member for culture, leisure and tourism at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "We are proud to host this prestigious angling competition on our beautiful coastline, and we look forward to welcoming visitors from across the UK and Europe to our region.

"The event will play a key role in supporting local economies in early 2027, and we encourage residents and visitors to take part if they can."

Weekend tickets go on sale on September 11 and cost £30.

Juniors can compete for the overall championship title for the first time.

Every entrant will go into a golden-ticket draw to win a stay at Sand-Le-Mere Holiday Park.

A warm-up Flattie Bash event will also be held on March 5.
