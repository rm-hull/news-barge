NURSERIES and schools have raised more than £850 for Yorkshire Air Ambulance.

The fundraising campaign was inspired by a motorcycle accident involving a friend of nursery manager Lydia Spencer-Plumb.

Ms Spencer-Plumb, who manages Partou Habergham Day Nursery and Pre-school in Burnley, said: "I knew Gary and Kelly from when I was younger, so knowing the very real personal story behind the fundraising made me particularly keen to support it and encourage our children and families to get involved, and the response was absolutely fantastic."

The campaign began with a £60 bake sale at Ms Spencer-Plumb’s nursery, where children wore yellow or their favourite colour.

Partou Little People Day Nursery and Pre-School, also in Burnley, raised £85.

Two primary schools and another nursery also took part, bringing the total raised to £714.25.

Ms Spencer-Plumb said: "I was so pleased with how well the initiative was received locally that I also shared the fundraising opportunity with the Partou Yorkshire region."

This led to two more nurseries joining in—Woodlands House in Bradford and Ashmoor in Shipley—which raised a further £140, bringing the final total to £854.25.

Ms Spencer-Plumb stated the campaign was meaningful for everyone involved.

She said: "They were able to be part of something meaningful in a way that was appropriate and exciting for them—wearing yellow, taking part in bake sales, talking about the helicopter and understanding, at an age-appropriate level, that fundraising can help an emergency service continue to be there for people when they need it most."

Laura Clay, Partou regional childcare director, praised the effort.

She said: "The response from the settings and families exceeded all expectations.

"What started as a small idea to encourage schools and nurseries to ‘Turn Yorkshire Yellow’ became a community effort and, most importantly, gave the children an opportunity to support a service that had a very real impact on someone they knew."
