## ‘Exceptional’ home hits the market in ‘desirable’ York location – for £1.85 million

The six-bedroom house for sale in Dalton Terrace, off the Mount in York, for £1.85 million *(Image: Churchills/Zoopla)*

An “exceptional” period townhouse has hit the market in a “highly desirable” York location – for £1.85 million.

The six-bedroom house is for sale in Dalton Terrace, off the Mount.

Churchills, which is marketing the property, said it is an “impressive home” that “showcases the scale and grandeur associated with fine period architecture”.

![]()Inside the six-bedroom house for sale in Dalton Terrace, off the Mount in York, for £1.85 million *(Image: Churchills/Zoopla)*

“Arranged over five floors, the accommodation offers tremendous versatility, with a superb selection of reception and bedroom spaces that can be adapted to suit modern family life, entertaining and working from home,” a Churchills spokesperson said.

They said the property has been refurbished by its current owners, “retaining many attractive period features throughout the house” while updating the townhouse with “modern smart home technology”.
