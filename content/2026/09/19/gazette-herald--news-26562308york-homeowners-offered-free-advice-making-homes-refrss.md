HOMEOWNERS in York are being offered free practical advice on how to make their properties more energy efficient.

York Community Energy, in partnership with the Stockholm Environment Institute at the University of York, is hosting a workshop on Saturday, September 19, at Southlands Methodist Church on Bishopthorpe Road.

Jana Busch, communications specialist at the Stockholm Environment Institute, said: "York Community Energy and the Stockholm Environment Institute at the University of York are running a free workshop this Saturday to help local homeowners understand where to start with energy-saving home improvements.

"A qualified retrofit assessor will introduce the principles of a successful home upgrade, followed by a York homeowner sharing her experience of installing insulation and an air-source heat pump.

"Attendees can also join a visit to her nearby home to see the improvements in practice."

The workshop, which runs from 10am to 1pm, includes an introduction to retrofit principles, a homeowner case study, and a guided visit to a nearby property where upgrades have been completed.

A few places are still available.
