A County Durham deli has issued a statement after crime scene investigators were spotted outside the venue.

Filthy's Seaham has been operating under restricted conditions after telling customers it was unable to open yesterday due to "unforeseen circumstances".

Pictures seen by The Northern Echo show white-suited investigators and police in the car park.

The venue has confirmed it is open today but for delivery only, with a slightly restricted menu.

In a statement on Facebook, Filthy's said: "Filthy's Seaham update- We are back open today.

"For the time being, we’ll be operating delivery only with a slightly restricted menu while we continue getting the building back up to speed.

"Food - open for delivery. Slightly restricted menu. Bar - closed to the general public until further notice.

"We really appreciate everyone’s patience and support while we get everything sorted."


They continued saying all booked functions, parties and events are completely unaffected and will be going ahead as normal.

The day before the business had announced that it would not be opening that morning.

Stating: "Due to unforeseen circumstances, we’re unfortunately unable to open this morning.

"We’re currently working with the relevant authorities and, as soon as we’re given the all clear, we’ll be straight back open and serving the Filthy scran you love!

"Sorry for the inconvenience, everyone. We’ll keep you updated as soon as we know more."

The deli is based at Unit 1, The Mill Inn on Stockton Road in Seaham, and opened as the takeaway deli in January this year.

The nature of the incident is not yet known. The Northern Echo has contacted Durham Police for information.
