# Tour of Britain 2026 Cycle Race: When and where are the road closures across Yorkshire ahead of the cycle race this weekend?

## For the Tour of Britain 2026 Cycle Race, various roads will be closed on the day of the race in North Yorkshire to ensure the safety of the people participating.

The fourth stage of the Tour of Britain will pass through Helmsley to Leyburn on Saturday, September 5, 2026.

As a result, there will be a number of both fixed and rolling road closures in place, as well as parking restrictions.

The Yorkshire stage of the annual race is organised by British Cycling and will finish in Leyburn market place.
