Jet2 has added more than 10,000 extra seats to its summer 2027 flight programme from Leeds Bradford Airport.

The additional capacity - 10,7000 seats - expands options to several popular destinations including Chania, Heraklion, Almeria, Halkidiki, and Verona.

The new seats are part of Jet2’s wider Summer 27 expansion, which increases capacity on 22 routes from none of its UK bases.

David Hills, chief customer officer at Jet2 and Jet2holidays, said: "We know how much our customers are looking forward to planning their summer holidays next year, and we are seeing continued demand for holidays earlier and later in the summer season as well as during the peak.

"By adding over 10,700 extra seats from Leeds Bradford Airport across destinations including Chania, Heraklion, Almeria, Halkidiki and Verona, as well as increasing availability to Palma throughout the year, we’re giving holidaymakers based in Yorkshire even more opportunity to book early, plan with confidence and choose the dates, flights and destinations that suit them."

Season extensions include weekly Thursday flights to Chania (April 1–29), Wednesday flights to Heraklion (April 7–28), and Saturday flights to Almeria (April 3–24).

Jet2 has also extended its season to Halkidiki and Verona into October, with weekly flights from Yorkshire.
