A man who has admitted stabbing a woman in the back was told the only sentence he can expect is one of imprisonment, with only its length in question.

The warning was given to Cameron Green after the 21-year-old admitted a charge of wounding with intent, arising from an incident in Pelton, near Chester-le-Street, last month.

Green was initially arrested on suspicion of attempted murder, after the stabbing in Alder Close, on the evening of Thursday, August 6.

His victim was taken to the Royal Victoria Hospital in Newcastle, suffering a single stab wound to the back.

*(Image: Amelia Anderson (The Northern Echo))*

Appearing at Durham Crown Court via video link from nearby HMP Durham, Green pleaded guilty to a single charge of unlawfully and maliciously wounding the woman with intent to cause her grievous bodily harm.

His counsel, Chris Knox KC, told the court the defendant made full admissions in his police interview after his arrest.

  Police at the scene of stabbing in Pelton, last month 

*(Image: Amelia Anderson (The Northern Echo))*

Mr Knox said arrangements were in hand for preparation of a psychiatric report on the defendant, “who has a certain number of problems”, of which the appointed psychiatrist has been made aware.

Judge Richard Clews said a background report should also be prepared by the Probation Service, prior to the sentencing hearing, to assess the danger posed by the defendant.

He told Green: “You have pleaded guilty to this serious charge and you will receive a substantial amount of credit for that plea.

“You should understand, in reality, the only available sentence will be one of imprisonment and the exact length of that will depend on the information presented to the court on the day of sentence.

“That’s the sentence you should expect.

“The pre-sentence report by the Probation Service will assist as to dangerousness.”

Green, of no fixed address, was remanded to remain in custody pending sentence at the court on October 30.
