A BURST water main has flooded a railway line in North Yorkshire.

Passengers can expect severe delays and cancellations on train services to and from Saltburn-by-the-Sea and Middlesbrough until the end of the day.

  


All lines between the two destinations, National Rail said, are blocked.

Northern trains will be affected, with disruption expected till the end of the day.
