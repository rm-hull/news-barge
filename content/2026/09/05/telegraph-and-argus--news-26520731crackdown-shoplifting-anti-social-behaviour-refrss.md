Police are cracking down on shoplifting and anti-social behaviour at retail sites along Valley Road, Bradford, as officers set out their latest neighbourhood priorities.

Bolton and Undercliffe’s policing team says it will step up high-visibility patrols at stores affected by repeat calls and disorder, concentrating resources on identified crime hotspots.

The force will also use intelligence-led patrols to disrupt prolific and repeat shoplifters, working directly with retailers, security teams and CCTV operators to identify suspects and gather evidence.

Bradford Council and other agencies will be involved in the response, with police promising targeted operations throughout the priority period to reduce repeat demand and improve safety for shoppers, shop workers and businesses.

Officers are urging retailers to report incidents, preserve evidence and use available crime-prevention support.

Residents and businesses will also receive updates and advice through Community Alert messages and local engagement events.
