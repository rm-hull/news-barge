A ram raid has taken place at a jewellers.

Police were called to reports of an incident at a jewellers on Duckworth Lane, Bradford, just before 1.30pm today (5 September).

Images on social media show police and fire crews attending the scene.

Enquiries are ongoing.

### What police said

  West Yorkshire Police said: "At 1.19pm today (5 September) officers were called to reports of a ram raid at a Jewellers on Duckworth Lane, Bradford.

  

  "A scene is in place whilst enquiries are carried out.

  

  "Anyone who witnessed the incident is asked to contact police via the Live Chat options at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat or via 101 quoting log 737 of 5 September."
