IT’S been quite the week in Emmerdale.

Joe and Dawn’s wedding day ended in tragedy when a car crashed into the marquee - killing the driver, Jimmy King.

Dawn got out of the collapsed marquee, leaving Joe trapped as the car exploded and the tent with it. Later, in hospital, Dawn went into premature labour and died, leaving Joe drowning in grief.

Meanwhile, Nicola is grieving for husband Jimmy - only to discover that he may have been living a double life.

Jimmy’s ex-wife Sadie sent jaws dropping when she arrived at the crash site in a helicopter, laying a single red rose to remember the man she calls ‘my love’. Has Sadie come back to pay her respects, or is there more to her dramatic return to Emmerdale, after 20 years?

As if all that wasn’t enough, Kim, heartbroken by Dawn’s death and Joe’s grief, is blindsided when DS Ramsden arrests her on suspicion of causing the fatal crash. Turns out Ramsden has an agenda - in a bombshell episode this week, she was revealed as Kim’s secret daughter. Fuelled by a deeply personal vendetta, she wants to make Kim pay for giving her up.

As the drama continues, three actresses at the heart of the powerful storylines tell us what to expect from the fall-out.

In the police interview room Ramsden has been relishing the pain she’s inflicting on Kim. Then a flashback revealed that motherless Baby Tate in the NICU was baby Thea - the future DS Thea Ramsden.

“It certainly surprised me when the producers told me Kim has a daughter!” laughs Bradford-born Claire King, who has played Kim since 1989, initially for 10 years then returning to Emmerdale in 2019. “The viewers get to know about Thea before Kim does. She wanted to wipe her (the baby) out of her brain. She thought she was giving her to a good home.”

How will Kim react when she finds out Thea is her daughter? “She’ll want to establish a bond with her, because it all went sour with Jamie (Kim’s son),” says Claire. “But she’ll be hesitant, treading carefully.”

Scheming businesswoman Kim has revealed a maternal side since stepdaughter Dawn and her young children came to live with her at Home Farm. “She’s like a lioness when it comes to her family,” says Claire. “Kim saw Dawn as a sort of daughter. She loved her, probably didn’t say it enough. She sees Joe as a son but she’s reticent when he wants to give the baby up.”

Claire is relishing additions to the Tate empire: “We’re outnumbered by Dingles! They keep whittling the Tates away. Maybe Kim will find her mother next...I’d love Amanda Barrie or Steph Beacham to play her. That would be bonkers,” she laughs.

Dawn’s death is felt deeply at Home Farm, and Claire will miss Olivia Bromley, who played her: “Filming those final scenes was heartbreaking but we had a laugh too. We gave her a good send-off.”

Nicola Wheeler, who plays Nicola King, is missing co-star Nick Miles, who played Jimmy. In emotional scenes after the crash, Nicola was horrified to find Jimmy trapped in the car wreckage. As he lay dying, he gave her a ring he’d planned to surprise her with at their vow renewal.

In a tender dream sequence, the couple danced together before Jimmy slipped away. Nicola reveals that filming the scene was far from romantic: “The dream sequence took all day to film. And we were in that car, inside a tent, in temperatures hotter than the sun. I stuck my head through the car window and there was a repulsive smell. It was real cheese (in Jimmy’s romantic picnic) which had been inside the car for three days! Nick said ‘I wondered what that was’.

“I’m glad we had a heartfelt goodbye though. I’ll miss Nick terribly. It ends and you go, ‘Oh gosh, that’s it’. They were beautifully written scenes, you want to do them justice but you don’t want them to end.”

Nicola is left struggling financially; Jimmy sold his haulage shares and the cash is missing, and their home is remortgaged and in arrears. Then Sadie claims she and Jimmy have been together for three years...

“Nicola’s grieving, with three children and two businesses to keep afloat. They’re in serious debt,” says Nicola. “At first she can’t accept Jimmy’s betrayal, she thinks Sadie’s lying, but she has evidence that seems to confirm they were together. Nicola thinks of this other world going on behind her back and panics that Jimmy was going to leave her. It’s interesting to take a character who’s really loved, and do that. It’s something Nicola would do - not Jimmy.”

The whirring of a helicopter shattering the peace of the Emmerdale countryside heralds Sadie King’s arrival. After laying a rose for Jimmy she heads for the Woolpack and encounters a few ghosts from her past. Soon everyone’s reeling from her declaration about Jimmy. Sadie seems devastated to find she’s been sharing him.

“We don’t know if she’s a reformed character or not, but I’m protective of her,” says Patsy Kensit, who first played Sadie from 2004-2006. “Arriving in a helicopter, throwing my coat at Charity, was a great nod to Sadie’s first arrival. I had to walk across a field in heels, I don’t wear heels anymore, I was very proud of myself when I managed it.

“Nicola Wheeler is phenomenal. We had a great slap scene; I walk into the pub and they’re like a pack of wolves. I tell Nicola that Jimmy and I were together and I get a wallop.

“I was so nervous coming back but everyone’s been so welcoming. There are some fun scenes, people will be shocked.”

With Robert suspicious when Sadie gifts Jimmy’s son Elliot an expensive watch, and Caleb acting shifty in her presence, what can we expect now that she’s back? “She has her sights set on Caleb and Ruby. And when she sees Cain she reveals some of what happened to her,” says Patsy. “Everyone wants Sadie to leave the village but she says it’s the one place where she belongs, where she had family.”
