A parish council is looking to reduce the height of its village Christmas tree because it’s got too tall for fairy lights.

The meeting of Gargrave Parish Council on Wednesday heard that because the tree, next to the A65, is protected with a Tree Preservation Order (TPO) the council will have to apply to North Yorkshire Council for permission to carry out the work.

If permission is granted, the village events committee will pay for the work to be carried out, the meeting heard.

It was also suggested that the lights could just go up on part of the tree - if planning permission was rejected.

Councillors agreed to put in for planning permission, after hearing it would not cost anything, and get the opinion of a tree surgeon.

The clerk pointed out that it could be that trimming would be opposed by the council because the tree is healthy.

A member of the public interjected that he understood that trees of that type had shallow roots and could be blown down in high winds, so it might be that it needs to be trimmed, for safety reasons.
