Residents across Darlington can look forward to a pleasant weekend as highs of 22C and sunshine are expected.

Bright, sunny weather is expected for much of Saturday and temperatures rising into the low 20s on Sunday.

According to the Met Office forecast, Saturday will see sunny intervals throughout the day with a maximum temperature of 19C and light winds.

Any chance of rain is low, with only a slight risk of showers during the early morning before conditions become dry and bright.

Overnight temperatures are expected to fall to around 11C.

The warmest weather of the weekend is forecast for Sunday, September 6, when temperatures could reach 22C.

Sunny spells are expected for much of the day, although there is a small chance of a brief shower around late morning and lunchtime.

Winds will remain relatively light, helping to make it feel warmer.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

The Met Office says pollen levels will remain low on Saturday before increasing to moderate on Sunday as the warmer conditions develop.

Overall, the weekend is shaping up to be a favourable one for outdoor activities, with long sunny spells, comfortable temperatures and only a slight risk of isolated showers.
