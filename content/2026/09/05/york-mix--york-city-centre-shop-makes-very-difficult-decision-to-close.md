A York city centre shop, known as a place where you can ‘dig up treasure’, is to close.

Vault Seven is the vintage shop opened on Lendal House in Lendal in December 2024.

Created by business partners Sammy Clews and Betsy Vaca, it sells vintage clothes, jewellery and other items.

It had five-star Google reviews. One called it a “quality store” that was “highly recommended”.

Another said “you can dig up treasure” there.

The Vault Seven team announced the closure on Instagram.

“After an incredible chapter, we’ve made the very difficult decision to close the doors of Vault Seven on 13th September,” they wrote.

“Since opening on 12th December 2024, we’ve been incredibly lucky to have met so many amazing people, found some brilliant pieces of clothing, and created a shop that we’ve genuinely loved coming to every day.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/lendal-house-lendal-york.webp)

“This has been a tough decision, and one that certainly wasn’t made lightly, but we feel that now is the right time to bring this chapter to a close.”

They thanked all their customers. “As a small, privately owned business, every bit of support has meant more to us than you probably realise.”

The store has now launched a closing down sale: “Over the next week, the discounts will be getting bigger and bigger as we work towards our final day on 13th September. If there’s something you’ve had your eye on, now’s the time to come and grab it before it’s gone!”
