While viewers of ‘Our Yorkshire Farm’ watched Raven Owen grow up helping with life on the windswept hills of Ravenseat, Amanda Owen’s eldest daughter has since forged a very different path away from the family farm.

The 25-year-old became a familiar face on Channel 5 while appearing on the show about her family’s remote North Yorkshire farm, but chose science over full-time farming.

She regularly shares snaps on her Instagram page of holidays, enjoying drinks with friends, riding horses, and exploring the countryside.

But between all the fun, she has an impressive career and educational background.


Raven studied biomedical science at York St John University, graduating with first-class honours in 2022 before completing a master’s degree in the same field.

Her academic achievements were celebrated by proud mum Amanda, who has often spoken about her daughter’s hard work and determination.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess expressed her pride on Instagram, writing: "Result! A first! Her dedication & determination has paid off.

"Always in the library, a laboratory or a catering van. She works so hard. The sky is the limit - massively proud of Raven."

According to her professional profile, Raven’s research focused on oncology and ways of improving chemotherapy treatments.

She has previously stated ambitions of progressing even further academically, with hopes of eventually studying for a PhD.

Since leaving university, Raven has established herself in the scientific sector.

Wolfsong Media lists her as an assistant scientist, while a more recent professional profile says she works in analytical chemistry and chemical residue analysis.

The profile says her work includes mass spectrometry and laboratory testing.

Raven’s scientific career has taken her away from daily farm work, but she has not forgotten her roots.


She still returns to Ravenseat to help her family and has appeared with Amanda, Clive and her siblings on More4’s Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids.

Her journey marks a striking contrast to the life many viewers expected after watching her grow up on screen in one of Britain’s best-known farming families.

Raven has built a career in laboratory science rather than staying on the farm, while maintaining strong links to her North Yorkshire home.
