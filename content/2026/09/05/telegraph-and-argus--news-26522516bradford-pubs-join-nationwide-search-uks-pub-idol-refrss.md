A NATIONAL singing competition is set to hit pubs across Bradford, offering locals the chance to become the UK’s next Pub Idol.

Proper Pub Idol 2026, sponsored by VK and Take! Tequila, will kick off in community pubs across Yorkshire and the Humber from September 7.

Among the Bradford pubs taking part are the Crown Hotel and Malt Kiln Inn.

It promises weekly heats for contestants, with each heat winner taking home a bottle of Prosecco.

Pub finals will take place the week beginning October 12, with a £50 bar tab for the winner.

Pub champions will then advance to the regional final, starting the week of October 26, where a £100 bar tab is up for grabs.

The national winner will receive £1,000 and a studio recording day.

Second place will win £500, and third place will receive £250.

Finalists will perform two songs at the regional and national stages, including one upbeat or high-tempo track.
