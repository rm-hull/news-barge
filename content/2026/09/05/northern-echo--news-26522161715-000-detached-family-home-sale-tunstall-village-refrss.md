BUILT just two years ago, Ricksholme is a substantial detached family home with a handsome stone facade behind which lies a wealth of spacious and versatile accommodation that combines high-quality craftsmanship and stylish finishes.

At the heart of the home is the open-plan kitchen, dining and family room which has been designed for both everyday family life and entertaining.

The kitchen – upgraded from the original specification – features two-tone cabinetry complemented by Silestone quartz work surfaces and is home to a comprehensive range of integrated Neff appliances including an induction hob, Slide & Hide oven, combination microwave oven, fridge freezer, dishwasher, plate warmer and dual-zone wine cooler as well as a boiling water tap, an InSinkErator waste disposal unit and a substantial central island which incorporates a breakfast bar.

Adjoining the kitchen is the dining area, and beyond this a relaxed living area enhanced by a striking lantern roof which floods the space with natural light.

A remote-controlled blind provides shade and comfort when required, whilst bifold doors open directly onto the rear patio, creating a seamless connection between the indoor and outdoor living spaces.

Also on the ground floor are a spacious entrance hall, a utility room with access to the integral garage, a cloakroom, a beautifully presented sitting room featuring a multi-fuel stove set in a brick surround with a stone hearth, and a further reception room currently used as a home office but equally suited to being a snug, playroom or additional reception room.

*(Image: ROBIN JESSOP LTD)*

Upstairs are the family bathroom and four bedrooms: one with an en-suite shower room, one ideal for use as a nursery, dressing room or home office and the principal bedroom also with a stylish en-suite shower room.

Ricksholme sits in around 0.86 acres of land which includes additional land to the rear purchased separately by the current owners.

These grounds include a driveway which leads to a generous parking area and the garage, a lawned front garden and the south-west facing rear garden which is home to a substantial patio, expansive lawned areas and well-stocked borders.

The property also benefits from an electric vehicle charging point.

Ricksholme is located on the edge of the village of Tunstall and is on the market at a guide price of £715,000.
