The first images of a new housing development on the outskirts of York have been revealed.

Mulgrave Properties is building six detached family houses at Pinfold Court in Knapton, located at the junction of Main Street and Back Lane on the northern edge of the village.

The development features three- and four-bedroom homes designed to reflect the character of the village with contemporary elements.

Debbie Foster, sales and marketing director at Mulgrave, said: "At Mulgrave, we pride ourselves on building beautiful homes in desirable locations.

"Situated on the edge of open countryside and yet just three miles from York city centre, Knapton offers the perfect combination of rural and city life."

Planning permission was granted in January this year, and construction was underway by May 2026.

The scheme includes a range of house types, from the three-bedroom Rawcliffe priced at £435,000 to the four-bedroom Heslington at £815,000.

For more information about the homes, visit [https://www.mulgraveproperties.co.uk/developments/pinfold-court/](https://www.mulgraveproperties.co.uk/developments/pinfold-court/)
