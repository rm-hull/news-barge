The English language can be tricky even for people born in the UK, with many words not being said exactly how they look.

If you are not from Yorkshire, many of the names of the towns and villages are very difficult to pronounce.

Places like Keighley, Masham, and especially Appletreewick do not sound like how they are written out.

If you happen to be visiting the town of Guiseley any time soon, this guide on how to pronounce the town’s name might be useful for you.


## How to pronounce Guiseley

Just looking at the word Guiseley makes it quite confusing, as it could be pronounced in a range of different ways.

It could be said goo-ee-seley or gwee-seley, but both of these ways are very wrong.

If you want to impress the locals when visiting, you should be saying it like guys-lee.

It might be confusing but that is the correct pronunciation.

The town’s name appears in the Domesday Book as Gisele and had a few different variants.

### 
  **What is Guiseley like?**

The area was largely a farming community up to the 18th century when the woollen industry started to rise.

The area became industrialised in the Victorian era and got a railway in 1865 and a town hall in 1867 that is now used as a Guiseley Theatre.

If you want to spend some time outdoors, you can take a trip to Parkinson Park, which is on the edge of town and offers great views of Guiseley, or Springfield Park, which is a lot easier to reach from the centre of town.


For those who love history, you should visit St Oswald’s Church or Guiseley Wells, which is often visited by people to reflect on the past or make a wish.

If you need some food during your trip, Guiseley is home to a Wetherby Whaler that is known for its incredible fish and chips, or you could head to Sancerre Guiseley if you want somewhere to have a sit-down meal for a special occasion.

Cena Bistro is also another popular spot and is loved for its cosy vibes and Italian dishes.
