A man has appeared in court after allegedly using his car as a weapon to drive into a pedestrian on a footpath, during a street disturbance, last month.

Karl Daryl Devine appeared at a Durham Crown Court plea hearing on Thursday (September 3) by video link from Holme House Prison, where he is detained on remand.

Charges of causing grievous bodily harm with intent and driving while disqualified were both put to the defendant.

He denied both counts and a date was agreed for the case to go to trial early in the New Year.

  Trial of man accused of driving at a pedestrian on a kerb causing serious injuries was fixed at Durham Crown Court, for January next year 

*(Image: The Northern Echo)*

The incident took place in Braeside, Edmondsley, County Durham, arising from an altercation between the defendant and the injured man, on Wednesday, August 5.

It is understood the rear window was smashed in the defendant’s Volkswagen Polo vehicle, which was then driven towards him, mounting the kerb and hitting him, before it was driven off, leaving the scene via Congburn Bank.

Martin Towers, prosecuting, said the alleged victim has been re-admitted to hospital due to complications with his injuries and has yet to be interviewed about the incident.

Judge Amanda Rippon, therefore, extended the time limit for the prosecution to present its evidence in the case to the court by two weeks, until October 9.

A signed defence case must be submitted in response by November 6, with the trial listed to start on Monday January 18.

The 52-year-old defendant of Viola Crescent, Sacriston, was remanded to remain in custody until the start of the trial.
