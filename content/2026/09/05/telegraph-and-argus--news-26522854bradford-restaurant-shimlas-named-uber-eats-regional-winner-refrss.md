A Bradford restaurant with a history spanning more than 55 years has been named an Uber Eats regional winner for Yorkshire and the North East.

Great Horton Road-based Shimlas, which was founded in the early 1970s by the late Haji Allah Ditta, received the accolade in recognition of its food, customer service and long-standing reputation across the region.

Nasir Uddin, regional manager at Shimlas, said the award was a proud moment for the business and reflected the support it has received from customers over several generations.

Mr Uddin said: "It was a huge honour for us and something the whole Shimlas team is incredibly proud of.

"We are extremely grateful for the recognition, particularly because it reflects the support and loyalty of our customers.

"It is a motivating moment for the entire team and inspires us to keep raising the bar."

  Shimlas' signature chicken donner, one of the Bradford restaurant's most popular menu items. 

*(Image: Shimlas)*

## More than five decades of family tradition

Shimlas was established by the founder after he migrated from Pakistan with the aim of building a better future for his family.

What began as a small venture has grown into one of Bradford's best-known restaurant brands, serving generations of customers while remaining family-run.

The restaurant's menu is influenced by the culinary traditions of Kashmir, Punjab and the North-West Frontier, with many recipes passed down through generations.

Today, the third generation of the family is helping steer the business into the future while preserving the traditions it was built upon.

  The exterior of Shimlas in Bradford, winner of the 2026 Uber Eats regional award for Yorkshire and the North East. 

*(Image: Shimlas)*

Mr Uddin said: "The award means a great deal to the whole Shimlas family.

"We have been serving our community for over 55 years, and the business has been passed down through generations.

"To receive this recognition is a testament to the hard work, dedication and passion of everyone who has been part of the Shimlas journey."

Mr Uddin added: "We believe our longevity, consistency and commitment to quality have helped Shimlas stand out.

"We have evolved with the times while staying true to our roots."

## Community at the heart of the business

Mr Uddin said Bradford and its communities have played a vital role in Shimlas' journey.

He said the restaurant has built strong relationships with customers over the years, serving generations of the same families.

"The community has been at the heart of Shimlas throughout its journey," he said.

"We are privileged to serve generations of families, loyal regulars and new visitors who quickly become part of the Shimlas community.

"For us, it is about much more than simply serving food. It is about building long-lasting relationships and being a place that people are proud to recommend."

  A feature seating area inside Shimlas, which was named Uber Eats Regional Winner for Yorkshire and the North East. 

*(Image: Shimlas)*

## Looking to the future

Despite receiving regional recognition, Shimlas says it has no plans to stand still.

Mr Uddin described the award as motivation to continue developing the business while remaining true to the values established more than five decades ago.

He said: "The third generation will continue developing new recipes, shaping the menu and modernising the experience, while remaining true to the values and traditions that have made Shimlas what it is today.

"Our focus will always be on quality over profit, memorable flavour, consistency and giving our customers something they are proud to come back to."
