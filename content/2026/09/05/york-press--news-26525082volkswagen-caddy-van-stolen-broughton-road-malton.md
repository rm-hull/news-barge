A Volkswagen Caddy van has been stolen from a North Yorkshire street.

The van was stolen from Broughton Road in Malton overnight from August 27 to 28, North Yorkshire Police said.

  


A force spokesperson said: “It was last sighted at 05:25hrs, heading northbound out of Northallerton. The van may stand out due to the roof bars and ladders.

“It may also be displaying a different number plate.

“If you see the van please contact North Yorkshire Police.”

You can contact police by emailing PC 1505 Jackson at [richard.jackson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:richard.jackson@northyorkshire.police.uk), or call 101 with reference number 12260168322.
