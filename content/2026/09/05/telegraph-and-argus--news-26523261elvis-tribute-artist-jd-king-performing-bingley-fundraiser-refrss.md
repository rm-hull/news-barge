An Elvis tribute concert will raise vital funds to support humanitarian aid deliveries to Ukraine.

Aloha From Hawaii will take place at Bingley Arts Centre in January and is being organised by The Adventurers Rotary Club and the Aire Valley Eagles Rotary Group.

The event will mark more than 50 years since Elvis Presley's 1973 performance and will raise funds for the Huddersfield to Ukraine Humanitarian Aid Group.

Gary Gardner, group director of the Aire Valley Eagles Rotary Group, said: "We have worked with Tim and Deborah’s group for several years now and can only say that he is an amazing person to work with.

"I’ve personally never – in all my years as a Rotarian – met anyone who is so passionate about supporting a cause where men, women, and children’s lives are shattered daily."

The concert will take place on Sunday, January 17, 2027.

It will feature Elvis tribute artist JD King, the Burning Love Band, and the Sweet Soul Singers.

Tim and Deborah Marsden, organisers of the aid group, said: "JD King’s evening of music, The Air Valley Eagles, and your generosity through supporting this event by buying tickets enable humanitarian aid to travel thousands of miles, changing lives."

Tickets are available at bingleyartscentre.co.uk or calling 01274 567983.
