John Pickles, creator of the Jive Bunny music mixes, has died aged 84, Mastermix has confirmed.

Pickles formed Jive Bunny And The Mastermixers in Rotherham in 1989. The novelty pop act went on to reach number one in the UK with Swing The Mood, That’s What I Like and Let’s Party.

Mastermix said: “It is with great sadness that we announce the passing of John Pickles, founder and creator of The Music Factory Entertainment Group, Mastermix and Jive Bunny.

“John was a leader, an entrepreneur, a businessman, and an incredibly talented songwriter. He was a true pioneer in the music industry, whose creativity, vision and passion for music touched the lives and careers of so many people around the world, and for the most fortunate of us, he was dad.”

Jive Bunny And The Mastermixers comprised Pickles, his son Andrew and Doncaster DJ and producer Les Hemstock. Andrew also worked as a director for Mastermix.

The group’s records typically combined contemporary pop with music from the 1950s, 1960s and 1970s, using synthesisers and sampling.

It had five UK top 10 hits, including its three number ones, and a top 10 album, Jive Bunny The Album.

Cartoon rabbit artwork featured on the group’s album covers and music videos. Costumed actors appeared as the character.

In December 2024, Mastermix released a Jive Bunny production, 3, 6, 9. The company said it was “celebrating the origins of Jive Bunny while adding a modern twist”.
