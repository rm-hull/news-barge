A tip-off from a member of the public has led to the seizure of an illegal off-road motorbike.

Police in Bradford East were alerted to a Sur-ron Light Bee being ridden through Eccleshill Recreation Ground.

A suspect was named by a witness, and officers later found the individual and the bike in a garden.

Using entry powers under the Police Reform Act, they seized the vehicle.

West Yorkshire Police said: "Your calls make a real difference to the success of tackling the illegal use of off-road motorcycles."

"If you know somebody using an off-road bike illegally, especially if you can tell us where it's being kept, let us know and we'll take action."
