THIS is the terrifying moment masked raiders used a 4x4 to smash their way into a Bradford jewellers.

Six masked raiders rammed the store in broad daylight before fleeing in two cars.

A vehicle repeatedly hit the shop on Duckworth Lane, Bradford, shortly before 1.30pm on September 5.

The men entered the premises and took jewellery while staff and customers watched.

Staff tried to fightback but the whole incident was over in a matter of seconds.

## What have police said?

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At 1.19pm today (5 September) officers were called to reports of a ram raid at a Jewellers on Duckworth Lane, Bradford."

"A scene remains in place while officers investigate the incident.

"Police have asked anyone who witnessed what happened to contact them through the Live Chat service on the West Yorkshire Police website or by calling 101.

"They should quote log 737 of September 5."

## What does the footage show?

CCTV footage shows the pick-up vehicle reversing towards the shop after its first impact failed to break through the window. It then appears to ram the premises several times.

Customers inside were seen hugging each other as the vehicle struck the shop. Footage also shows six people dressed in black from head to toe, wearing hoods and masks.

They are shown getting out of three vehicles and helping themselves to jewellery while staff look on.

A shopkeeper is seen holding a baseball bat before throwing objects at the group.

The raiders later leave in two more cars, described as a blue hatchback and a silver sports utility vehicle. Before they drove away, a man from the store smashed the rear window of one car with the bat.

## Damage caused

The entire frontage of the shop has been destroyed.

Police and fire crews attended, while the area was taped off and traffic queues formed around Duckworth Lane.

A grey pick-up truck was also pictured partly on the pavement, with its flat bed facing the shop window.

Police have not confirmed whether it was the vehicle used in the raid.

The business appears to be Pakistani Jewellers, which specialises in Mirpuri designs.

## Drivers avoid area

It is not the first time a jeweller on Duckworth Lane has been targeted this year. Another jeweller was hit in March.

Akhlaq Ahmed said on social media: "That explains why the roundabout was sealed off. Got stuck in traffic, I wondered what happened."

Drivers were advised to use a different route if they needed to go past Duckworth Lane. Enquiries are continuing.

Witnesses with information, footage or details about the vehicles are being asked to contact West Yorkshire Police.
