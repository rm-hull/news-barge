Firefighters were called on an unusual rescue mission this morning (Saturday).

It was to a deer which had got itself stuck.

The crew from York responded to the incident on **Chantry Lane, Bishopthorpe** at**7.18am**.

#### Chantry Lane

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “York rescued a small deer that was trapped in metal railings.

“Crews used spreaders to release the deer that ran off uninjured.”
