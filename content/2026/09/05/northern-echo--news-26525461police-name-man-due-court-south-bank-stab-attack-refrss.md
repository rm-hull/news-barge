A man is due to appear in the dock following a 'stab attack'.

Stephen Gibson, 34, of no fixed address, will appear at Teesside Magistrates' Court, in Middlesbrough, on Monday, following an attempted murder probe.

An investigation was launched after an incident in South Bank, on Monday, August 3.

Cleveland Police said a 20-year-old man reported being ambushed, beaten and stabbed at an address on Oxford Street.

A spokesperson confirmed: "Detectives from Redcar CID have now charged a 34-year-old man, of no fixed address, in connection with the attempted murder of a 20-year-old man.

"He has been remanded in custody to appear at Teesside Magistrates' Court on Monday, September 7.

"It relates to reports of a 20-year-old man being ambushed, beaten and stabbed at an address on Oxford Street in South Bank on Monday, August 3."
