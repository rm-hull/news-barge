Emergency services have been called to an incident near a York bridge.

North Yorkshire Police, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service and York Rescue Boat are currently attending an incident along Ouse Bridge.

The bridge is currently closed to traffic.

Pedestrians are still able to walk around the cordon.
