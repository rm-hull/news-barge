A special needs college in Harrogate has received a donation of furniture for its staff room from a local housebuilder.

Barratt Homes has donated a table and stools to the Henshaws Arts and Crafts Centre, where they are now in use in the staff room.

The furniture was given by the housebuilder as a gesture of support for Henshaws College, which is currently building at Knights Gate in Huntington.

Clare Cunningham, fundraiser for Henshaws College, said: "We are very grateful to Barratt Homes for the donated table and stools.

"Our staff bring so much energy, creativity and care to the art workshops they deliver every day.

"Having a space that supports their wellbeing means they can continue to give their best and continue supporting our Art Makers to try new things, grow in confidence and become more independent."

The refreshed staff room now offers a more comfortable and welcoming environment for the team to rest and recharge.

Paul Hogan, sales director at Barratt Homes Yorkshire East, said: "We are delighted to donate this furniture to the dedicated staff at Henshaw’s Arts and Crafts Centre.

"As an essential part of the community, we hope this donation shows our appreciation."

For more information, visit the Barratt Homes Knights Gate website.
