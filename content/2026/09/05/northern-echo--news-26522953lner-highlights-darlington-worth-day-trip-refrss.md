The weekend is here, so it's time for a fun adventure - and whether you already live in Darlington or have never been, there is so much to see and do that you may be unaware of.

A new LNER Instagram video is urging more people to discover the North East town this autumn.

Just 30 minutes from York by train, Darlington offers history, independent shops, green spaces and luxury experiences for a memorable day out.

The LNER team began in the town centre, where visitors can see Darlington’s iconic clock tower.

The Victorian landmark is often compared to London’s Big Ben and remains one of Darlington’s most recognisable sights.

The video also highlighted some of the town’s independent businesses, including Hyve, a shop selling handmade gifts, stationery and homeware.

LNER says purchases of some products help fund donations to the Sheldrick Wildlife Trust.

   

*(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

The team also stopped at Hatch, a café praised for its coffee, cakes and speciality drinks.

Content creators described the venue’s strawberry iced matcha as “10 out of 10”.

Those looking to stretch their legs can head to South Park, around a 25-minute walk from the town centre.

The Grade II-listed park has picturesque gardens, open green spaces and a peaceful escape from the high street.

One of the standout attractions featured by LNER was Hopetown Darlington, the town’s free-entry railway heritage attraction.

The site celebrates Darlington’s role in the development of the railways and includes exhibitions, historic buildings and a café overlooking the railway line.

   

*(Image: Hopetown Darlington)*

The creators called Hopetown their “highlight of the trip”, praising its railway memorabilia and historical exhibits.

Darlington is home to the Stockton and Darlington Railway, widely recognised as the world’s first public railway to use steam locomotives.

At Hopetown, visitors can explore the former booking office and discover Darlington’s engineering history.

The LNER team also saw Locomotion No. 1, the historic steam locomotive built in 1825, as part of a special exhibition running until June 22.

Before leaving, they sampled the attraction’s exclusive Black Diamond ice cream.

For those wanting to end the day in style, Rockliffe Hall is a perfect choice - offering a mix of luxury and relaxation.


The sophisticated hotel and spa offers a range of treatments and facilities, including a glass-fronted sauna, foot spas and outdoor relaxation areas.

After spending the evening enjoying the spa’s sunset package, the creators concluded that Darlington had provided the “perfect summer day out”.

From railway history and independent shops to scenic parks and luxury relaxation, Darlington offers plenty to fill a day trip this summer.
