BRADFORD Bulls hold their end of season awards at the Cedar Court Hotel on Friday, September 18.

I’ve been tasked with choosing the T&A Player of the Year, but we need your help.

I’ve picked four players who have stood out during a gruelling season.

Is Andy Ackers, who has manipulated the ruck well all season and scored some wonderful tries, your pick?

Caleb Aekins had to be in there too, having played every game and adapted superbly to the rigours of Super League having never been at this level before, could he be the right call?

We’ve put forward the skipper Joe Mellor too, who has led the team around the field with aplomb, despite having to adapt to an all-new 13 role, will he get the nod?

Or how about Ryan Sutton, who arrived at Bulls after a long spell out with a knee injury, yet has played a remarkable amount of minutes and made ferocious tackles and tackle busts down the middle every week?


You have until 11:50pm on Sunday night to cast your vote, and you can only choose one player.

We will collect the votes and send them on to Bulls, with the winner to be announced on the night of the awards.

You’ve got all weekend Bulls fans, so get picking before it’s too late!
