Gillian Anderson has returned to M&S as its Chief Compliments Officer, and this time she is helping shoppers discover the autumn fashion pieces set to dominate wardrobes over the coming months.

The actress stars in the retailer's latest Love That campaign, filmed inside the iconic V&A Library, where she is surrounded by books, fashion and some seriously stylish women.

But it is the clothes that are likely to catch shoppers' attention.

From a £70 faux fur jacket to a £28 satin shirt and £28 skirt, the new M&S collection is packed with autumn pieces designed to make an impact without always carrying a hefty price tag.

In fact, more than 50% of the autumn collection costs less than £30, while 74% of M&S outerwear is priced at £70 or below.


## Gillian Anderson's autumn fashion moment

Anderson takes centre stage in the new campaign as she researches the art of giving compliments in the V&A Library.

It is the latest chapter in M&S's Love That campaign, which has already seen Anderson become the face of the retailer's fashion push this year.

M&S says the Spring Love That advert became its highest-scoring fashion campaign ever tested by independent marketing effectiveness agency System1.

Now Anderson is back for autumn, surrounded by some of the pieces M&S expects shoppers to be talking about next.


## The M&S pieces Gillian is showcasing

The full range is available here.

One of the standout looks is the £70 Faux Fur Jacket, worn with a pinstripe suit featuring an £85 blazer and £46 trousers.

For those wanting something a little more colourful, there is a blue satin co-ord, with a £65 jacket and £28 skirt.

Anderson herself wears a luxe aubergine satin shirt costing £28, showing how a relatively small purchase can add a strong hit of colour to an autumn wardrobe.

There are also plenty of ways to copy the looks without buying an entire outfit.

M&S accessories include brooches from £14, bags from £26 and ballet flats from £28, while earrings start from £12.50 in some of the campaign looks.

Another outfit features a £28 purple shirt, £26 black trousers, £16.50 earrings and £55 shoes.

Meanwhile, a £42 dress is teamed with a £65 jacket and £28 skirt, with £15 sunglasses, £12.50 earrings and £28 shoes completing the look.

## The autumn trends M&S is betting on

The campaign is packed with the pieces likely to make the biggest impact this season.

Statement faux fur, chocolate wool coats, satin, oversized wraps and modern tailoring all feature prominently, alongside relaxed suiting and refined knitwear.

For anyone looking for a new coat or jacket, the prices could also prove tempting.

M&S says 74% of its outerwear is priced at £70 or less, with lightweight padded jackets available from £40.

M&S says 73% of UK adults plan to buy new clothing for autumn, while 51% of the UK population are already planning their wardrobes before September.

That makes Anderson's new campaign particularly well timed for anyone starting to think about what they will actually wear once temperatures drop.

## Why Gillian Anderson is back

The actress has become closely associated with M&S's Love That campaign, with the latest advert continuing the theme of people noticing something they love and telling someone else about it.

Sharry Cramond, Marketing Director of Fashion, Home & Beauty at M&S, said: "After celebrating a ‘Summer of Love That’, we're excited to keep building that momentum into autumn, the single biggest style moment of the year."

She added: "This season is all about the pieces that catch your eye, spark a compliment and inspire you to share the look with someone else."

The campaign follows a summer that saw M&S stage its first-ever fashion show in Ibiza and what it described as the world's first F1 pit lane catwalk at the Silverstone British Grand Prix.

## M&S has another fashion first coming

Anderson's autumn campaign arrives just before another major moment for the retailer.

M&S is set to make its London Fashion Week schedule debut this September, with a see-now-buy-now catwalk show celebrating 100 years of style.

The show will bring together M&S's Collection, Per Una and Autograph brands.

For shoppers, however, the autumn collection is arguably the more immediate attraction.

With Anderson putting the spotlight on everything from £14 accessories to £70 faux fur and £85 tailoring, there are plenty of ways to give your wardrobe a new-season update.
