A North Yorkshire firm has seen a surge in profits after a busy 2025 full of expansion and investment.

Dale Power Solutions Limited, which celebrated its 90th anniversary in 2025, saw a significant increase in operating profit and turnover, with operating profit rising from £1.6 million to £2.5 million and turnover jumping from £32 million to £38.6 million.

The company, which manufactures, installs, and services generators, UPS (uninterruptible power supply) systems, and ancillary equipment, has also broadened its offerings to include renewable energy solutions like battery storage systems.

The firm, whose headquarters is at Salter Road, Eastfield Industrial Estate, Scarborough, completed the acquisition of Calibre Power in Aberdeen, extending its reach into Scotland and enhancing its ability to serve customers in energy-critical environments, such as offshore and remote locations.

This move has reinforced the company's service offering and supports its ambition to provide national coverage, continued a report published with the annual results for 2025.

The company also opened a new hub in St Albans to better support customers across the South of England.

This strategic move is part of Dale Power Solutions Limited's commitment to investing in its national infrastructure to support future growth and improve customer service.

Dale Power Solutions has also expanded its field-based engineering workforce, deploying more service engineers across the UK.

This expanded coverage enables the business to provide faster response times, improved resilience, and enhanced service continuity for customers operating mission-critical power systems.

In addition to geographical expansion, the company continued to grow its activity in energy transition and resilience solutions during 2025.

Demand for renewable energy solutions and hybrid power solutions, including BESS (battery energy storage systems) and UPS, remained strong as customers increasingly focused on energy security, carbon reduction, and net-zero objectives.

The company's technical expertise and ability to deliver bespoke solutions position it well to support customers as these markets continue to develop.

Dale Power Solutions Limited maintained a disciplined approach to cash management and working capital throughout the year.

Net assets increased to £8.1 million, reflecting strong profitability and retained earnings growth.

Liquidity remained robust, supported by ongoing trading performance and continued support from the company's funding partners.

In December 2025, the group refinanced its banking facilities, moving to a new National Westminster Bank Plc (Natwest) facility.

The refinancing delivers lower financing costs, increased headroom, and greater flexibility, strengthening the group's funding platform and supporting continued investment and growth.

Dale Power Solutions Limited entered 2026 with a record high forward order book, providing strong revenue visibility and underpinning the board's confidence in the outlook.

At December 2025, the forward order book stood at almost £24 million, representing a 15% increase on the prior year.

Overall, 2025 represented a year of strong financial progress, strategic delivery, and national expansion, the company added.
