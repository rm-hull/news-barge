A BRADFORD man who stored a stash of crack cocaine and heroin after getting into debt with his dealer has avoided jail.

When police officers executed a search warrant at Tariq Mahmood’s home in Thornville Court, Manningham, he directed them to a pair of trainers near his bed.

Bradford Crown Court heard that a yellow tub was found in one of the trainers and inside it there was a quantity of crack cocaine worth £290 and heroin valued at £70.

## Man admitted drug dealing

In a police interview, Mahmood, 55, explained that he was a user of heroin and crack cocaine and had run up a debt of £800.

He admitted that he had held onto drugs and had been involved in packaging some of them.

Mahmood admitted possessing both of the Class A drugs with intent to supply and his barrister Soheil Khan highlighted the fact that the defendant had no previous convictions for drugs offending.

Mr Khan submitted that Mahmood had tried his best to rid himself of his drug habit and had made other changes in his life.

Recorder Ella Anderson referred to Mahmood’s basis of plea which indicated that he had been holding onto the drugs for others who had previously threatened and attacked the defendant.

## What was his sentence?

She agreed that Mahmood had played a “lesser role” and his 15-month jail sentence should be suspended for 18 months.

Mahmood will have to comply with a nine-month drug rehabilitation requirement and be subject to a six-month trail monitoring requirement.

Mahmood must also attend 20 rehabilitation activity requirement days.
