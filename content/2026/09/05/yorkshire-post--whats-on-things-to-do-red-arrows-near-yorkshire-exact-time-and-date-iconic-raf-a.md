# Red Arrows near Yorkshire: Exact time and date iconic RAF aircraft will be flying over Chatsworth Country Fair 2026

## This weekend the iconic Red Arrows will be flying over the Chatsworth Country Fair which could be observed from nearby areas in Yorkshire.

TV and radio presenter, Angelica Bell, best known for presenting on CBBC between 2000 and 2006 and winning the 2017 series of Celebrity MasterChef, will be appearing at the event.

There will be the Grand Ring entertainment and traditional country activities including the live cooking demonstration from James Martin, Nisha Katona and Vito Coppola.

![Chatsworth House. (Pic credit: Brian Eyre)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjRmOGI1NTYwLTUzZjgtNGU5Ni1iMGI1LWUyOTMxMTMyMjMyMDo5M2NiMWE5ZS0xNDMxLTQ5N2QtYmI3OS0xMDZmODU2NzZiZmE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The Red Arrows are set to make a special appearance over Chatsworth Estate on Sunday, September 6, 2026.

The flypast will take place between 11.30am and 12pm, with the duration being around six minutes and it is subject to weather and operational conditions.

The nearest Yorkshire suburbs to Chatsworth Estate are Dore, Totley or Bradway, so it is possible you could see the flypast if you live in any of these areas.

Dogs are welcome to the event but must be on leads and children aged 15 and under can enter for free.

Chatsworth House is a stately home in the Derbyshire Dales that belonged to the Cavendish family since 1549.

It is situated on the east bank of the River Derwent, across from hills between the Derwent and Wye valleys, amid parkland backed by wooded hills that rise to heather moorland.
