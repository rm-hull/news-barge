York City FC has kicked off the new season with a sponsorship deal with Specsavers York.

The opticians, based in Low Ousegate, will support the club throughout the 2026/27 season as an official club sponsor and partner of the YCFC Foundation.

The foundation promotes access to sport, physical education and healthy lifestyle opportunities across the city.

Pete Sutton, business development officer at York City FC, said: "It's great to be partnering with such a key business in our city—a household name across the UK that aligns with our core value of supporting the local community.

"Our players need to make sure they’re on top form this season, and what better way than by ensuring their eyes and ears are performing at their best."

Charlotte Turner, director at Specsavers York and Vangarde, added: "The whole team is really excited for the season to start.

"We can’t wait to be a part of the fan experience, giving supporters a chance to connect with the team like never before.

"The Minstermen played so hard last season, and we're eager to see what they achieve this year.

"We’re excited to keep supporting them and wish them the best of luck for their game on Saturday."
