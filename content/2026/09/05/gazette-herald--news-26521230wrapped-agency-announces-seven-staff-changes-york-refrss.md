Wrapped Agency has appointed two new team members and announced five internal promotions.

The new appointments come as the agency continues to grow its work with organisations in regulated sectors.

Andi Olssen joins as Creative Lead, bringing more than 25 years’ creative experience.

Mr Olssen said: "The creative opportunity is to keep questioning what is expected, push ideas further and show clients what is possible while working confidently within the requirements that shape their sectors."

Sarah Todd also joins the agency as Senior Account Manager, bringing more than 10 years of experience in communications, content, and project management.

Ms Todd said: "I’m looking forward to building strong relationships at Wrapped and becoming a trusted partner to the organisations we support."

Mark Robinson, co-owner and chief commercial officer at Wrapped Agency, said: "These appointments and promotions ensure we have the senior thinking, specialist expertise and delivery capability to support them as their briefs become larger and more complex."
