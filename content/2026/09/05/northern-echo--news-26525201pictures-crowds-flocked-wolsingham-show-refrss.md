Here are some great pictures as crowds flocked to a traditional County Durham farming show this weekend.

Wolsingham Show welcomed visitors for a packed programme celebrating farming, countryside life and family entertainment.

Crowds enjoyed everything from showjumping to vintage vehicles, birds of prey and traditional countryside competitions.

  The annual Wolsingham Show takes place at Scotch Isle Park. Produce on offer. 

*(Image: CHRIS BOOTH)*

Sheep, cattle and horses were among the animals taking centre stage, while major showjumping classes provided plenty of action.

Visitors also watched dog agility, strongman competitions and countryside games, while others browsed more than 100 trade stands.

  The annual Wolsingham Show takes place at Scotch Isle Park. Horse and pony classes. 

*(Image: CHRIS BOOTH)*

The showground was packed with stalls offering everything from tractors to toys, alongside arts and crafts, shopping and upcycled and vintage marquees.

An artisan food market also offered visitors the chance to grab a bite to eat.

Other attractions included donkey rides, alpacas, Smallbrook Gundogs, sheep dog trials, bike display teams, ferret racing, harness racing and live music.
