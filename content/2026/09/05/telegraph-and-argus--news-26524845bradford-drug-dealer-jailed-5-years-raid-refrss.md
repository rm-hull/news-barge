A drug dealer has been jailed for more than five years following a police raid at his home.

Andrew Leeming, 42, of Cockcroft Grove in Holme Wood, Bradford, was sentenced to five years and six months at Bradford Crown Court on September 4.

He admitted being concerned in the supply of crack cocaine and heroin, and acquiring criminal property.

The conviction follows a warrant executed at his home in September 2024 by the Operation Sweetgill team.

Officers seized multiple phones and more than £13,000 in cash during the raid.

Sergeant Charlie Smallwood of the Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team said: "We welcome the sentence handed down to Leeming today.

"Drugs have no place in society and we as a team are working hard to make Holme Wood a safer place.

"I would encourage people to report drug dealing to us through the report it options on our website."
