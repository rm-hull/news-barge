A popular Northumberland park has been blighted by caravans, campervans and tents pitching up and camping against regulations.

The problem has been a significant issue at Tyne Green in Hexham throughout the summer.

The town’s mayor, Coun Derek Kennedy, said visitors had burned piles of wooden pallets for fires despite wildfire warnings and even kept horses enclosed in the playpark. He is calling on the relevant authorities to take action.

While the park is often used on an annual basis by travellers heading to the famous Appleby Horse Fair in Cumbria, this year has seen other users flouting the rules.

Coun Kennedy, who also represents Hexham on Northumberland County Council, said: “They aren’t necessarily travellers. They’re just turning up.

"There has been confrontations and I am very concerned that there could be conflict between those abusing Tyne Green and the legitimate users

“I’m really worried this is going to escalate. We shouldn’t be having people use Tyne Green as a pretend caravan park – the rules support that.

“We need to be clear on what the response is. There were even horses in the children’s play park at one point using it as a pen with horse muck ruining the park – you can’t have kids and horses in the same place.

“Tyne Green is a green space used by so many groups including a golf club, rowers, park run, children’s play park and many others. It has been most unfortunate that it has been abused by people having their caravans on the green space and fires being lit.

“The popular Hexham Park Run has to be cancelled one week and a new course put into action as caravans were blocking the route. Large perimeter stones have been moved and a bolt cut off the gate so the unauthorised caravans could get in the green.

“I have raised these matters with Northumberland County Council and have asked for an inter agency meeting to discuss what actions can be taken to stop these terrible behaviours. We have had several people camping out in tents for weeks.

“This is not what the intention of Tyne Green is. We need to bring back control of this area so it can be used as intended. It is so frustrating to get all these complaints but nothing seems to be happening to rectify the situation.

“I have added an agenda item on Hexham Town Council’s September meeting to discuss the matter and drive forward an action plan.”

Coun Kennedy said a multi-agency meeting was set to take place with the council and Northumbria Police set to attend.

Asked to comment on the issue, a spokeswoman for Northumberland County Council said: “We are dedicated to protecting our environment and our award-winning green spaces across the county.

“Our Love it Like it’s Yours campaign aims to educate as many people as possible on the dos and don’ts of visiting the county. Any illegal activities should be reported to the police and visitors should only camp in legal, designated campsites to respect these areas for all to enjoy.

“There has been an increase in unauthorised camping on public open spaces this year including on or near to Tyne Green. The council and its partners are meeting to discuss the issues raised, review the current position, and consider any appropriate actions that may be available to us collectively.”
