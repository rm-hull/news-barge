North Yorkshire Council has received planning applications covering a change of use, a certificate of lawfulness, an extension and alterations to residential and other properties across the area.

### Proposal to change office building into a home

An application concerns the change of use of an office building into a residential dwelling at 103 Riverside View, Norton, North Yorkshire, YO17 9RB.

The application has been refused.

Ref. No: ZE26/00584/GPCOU | Received: July 9, 2026 | Validated: July 13, 2026 | Status: Refused

### Certificate sought for petrol station opening hours

An application was made for a Certificate of Lawfulness for an existing use or development at Penny On The Move, Kirkbymoorside, North Yorkshire, YO62 6DL.

It sought confirmation that the petrol station is unrestricted in respect of its hours of operation.

The application was approved.

Ref. No: ZE26/00568/CLEUD | Received: July 7, 2026 | Validated: July 7, 2026 | Status: Approved

### Plans for rear extension in Helmsley

An application for a proposed single-storey rear extension has been approved at 48 Bondgate, Helmsley, North Yorkshire, YO62 5EZ.

The application covers work to the rear of the property.

Ref. No: ZE26/00557/HOUSE | Received: July 2, 2026 | Validated: July 7, 2026 | Status: Approved

### Roof space conversion proposed at Gennell Farm

Listed building consent has been approved for the conversion of roof space into habitable space at Gennell Farm, Gennell Lane, Flaxton, North Yorkshire, YO60 7QT.

The proposal includes two bedrooms, a bathroom and three rooflights.

Ref. No: ZE26/00518/LBC | Received: June 22, 2026 | Validated: June 22, 2026 | Status: Approved

### Retrospective alterations approved at Ryegate property

An application for retrospective internal and external alterations has been approved at 27 Ryegate, Helmsley, North Yorkshire, YO62 5AA.

The work includes two bathrooms, two rooflights, changes to rear doors and fenestration, a staircase in the annexe, and the removal of chimney breasts and a chimney stack.

Ref. No: ZE26/00316/LBC | Received: April 14, 2026 | Validated: July 2, 2026 | Status: Approved

### Householder application for alterations at Ryegate

A separate householder application for the same retrospective internal and external alterations at 27 Ryegate, Helmsley, North Yorkshire, YO62 5AA, has been approved.

The proposal includes two bathrooms, two rooflights, changes to rear doors and fenestration, a staircase in the annexe, and removal of chimney breasts and a chimney stack.

Ref. No: ZE26/00315/HOUSE | Received: April 14, 2026 | Validated: July 2, 2026 | Status: Approved
