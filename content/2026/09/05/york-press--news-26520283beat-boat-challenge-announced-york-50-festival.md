## Age UK York launches Beat the Boat challenge for over-50s festival

A new riverside challenge, Beat the Boat, is coming to York as part of this year’s 50+ Festival 

A new riverside challenge is coming to the city as part of this year’s 50+ Festival.

Taking place in York, Beat the Boat will see participants race along the River Ouse in a light-hearted contest that supports a good cause.

The event, organised by Age UK York in association with City Cruises, is open to those aged 50 and above and will take place on Sunday 4 October 2026.

Carl Wain, Deputy CEO at Age UK York, said: "We couldn’t resist the opportunity to celebrate this year’s 50+ Festival with an original York event that brings people together in a fun and memorable way.

"The event will encourage people aged 50 and over to take part in a community-based race, while giving spectators of all ages the chance to join in the fun from the boat itself – cheering on and supporting the runners as they make their way along the river.

"At Age UK York, we champion the national Act Now, Age Better campaign, which encourages people aged 50 and over to stay active and take positive steps to support their health and wellbeing in later life.

"This event is a fantastic opportunity to put that message into action in a light-hearted and entertaining way.

"For those who have a competitive streak, our age-accessible approach is to use an age- and gender adjusted percentage rather than the first over the line.

"So, with this challenge, an 83-year-old has the same chance of winning as a 51-year-old.

"We’re thrilled to be partnering with City Cruises and can’t wait to see runners taking part and spectators of all ages getting behind them, cheering them on as they sail along the river.

"It promises to be a fantastic celebration of community, activity and having fun at any age."

The 2.7km route will start at Kings Staith and follow the riverside to Millennium Bridge before looping back to Terry Avenue.

Participants will race against a City Cruises boat filled with cheering spectators, who can support the runners from the water.

Christopher Pegg, Head of Regional Commercial, Sales & Marketing at City Cruises, said: "We're delighted to support Age UK York with such a brilliant event.

"The River Ouse is York's oldest landmark, and it's great to see it put to use in a completely new way, pitting one of our passenger boats full of cheering supporters against runners on the riverside.

"We hope it raises a fantastic amount for such a great cause."

Beat the Boat is part of the wider 50+ Festival, organised by York Older People’s Assembly (YOPA) and Age UK York.

The festival celebrates life in York for those aged 50 and over, with a full programme of activities, entertainment, and information sessions designed to inspire and engage.

Beat the Boat is expected to be just one of the highlights of this year’s programme.

It offers a unique way for people to get involved, try something new, and help raise funds for a local charity.

Entry for runners is £10, and participants must be aged 50 or older.

Spectator tickets for the boat cruise also cost £10 and include unlimited tea and coffee.

The event begins at 10am from Tower Gardens and Kings Staith, with all funds raised supporting Age UK York.
