A NEW bar has applied for a licence to sell alcohol.

Mr Eric Jean-Marie Alfred Poli submitted the application to City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council on July 28, 2026.

The proposal is for the establishment, La Buvette, located at 91 Kirkgate, Shipley.

The application seeks permission for the sale of alcohol both on and off the premises from 11am to 10pm, Monday to Sunday.

Bradford Council is inviting written representations regarding the application until September 25, 2026.

These can be sent to the Licensing Team at Shearbridge Depot, Shearbridge Road, Bradford.

The licensing register is available for public inspection at the address, from 9am to 5pm, Monday to Thursday, and 9am to 4.30pm on Friday.

Making a false statement in connection with the application is an offence and carries an unlimited fine.
