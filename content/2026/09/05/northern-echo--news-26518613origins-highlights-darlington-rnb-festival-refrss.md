NEXT weekend, the Darlington Rhythm ‘n’ Blues Festival celebrates its 30th anniversary with a huge programme of gigs lasting three days.

Since 1996, the festival has attracted some very famous rock blues names who have played to many thousands of people.

The festival began in the aftermath of a council project to relay the Market Place.

For 20 years, Darlington had been attempting to beautify the square, which for most of the week was a car park, and it finally persuaded the European Commission to hand over 40 per cent of the £1.2m cost by saying that it would boost tourism.

Work began in August 1995, with a Saxon graveyard being unearthed, and when in early 1996, the Market Place was relaid in pink granite, the work to boost tourism had to begin.

A six month Darlington Festival of street events and pageants was planned with telecoms company Hutchison Telecom putting in £40,000 as principal sponsors.

  Chris Farlowe, of Atomic Rooster, headlining the first Darlington R 'n' B festival 30 years ago 

*(Image: Mike Prendergast)*

The Lazy Sunday Rhythm ‘n’ Blues, put on by the Darlington R’n’B Club, was a small part of the larger festival but with Chris Farlowe, once in Atomic Rooster, headlining, it was one of the most popular – the whole festival, the European funders were told, attracted 40,000 people, 5,000 of whom came for the R’n’B, as the festival filled Darlington town centre with live music.

  Little Stevie and the Rhythm Kings play outside the Pennyweight in Darlington Market Place as part of the R 'n' B festival in September 1998 

*(Image: Mike Gibb)*

Hutchison Telecom turned into Orange, which carried on as headline sponsors until 2003. Its departure caused the months of street events to be scaled back, but the R’n’B festival successfully stood on its own.

  Mungo Jerry at Darlington R 'n' B festival 

*(Image: Mike Prendergast)*

Some really big names appeared: The Animals, The Yardbirds, The Pretty Things, Spencer Davis, Andy Fairweather Low of Amen Corner, Mungo Jerry, The Blockheads…

  The Blockheads at Darlington R 'n' B festival 

*(Image: Mike Prendergast)*

And they drew the crowds: in 2018, a record 7,500 were in the Market Place, as crowds flocked to the return of Darlington’s annual R&B show.

  The 2014 crowd enjoying the sunshine at the Darlington R 'n' B festival 

*(Image: Mike Prendergast)*

The 30th free festival begins on Friday (September 11) with four fringe gigs in town centre venues from 6.30pm. On Saturday, there are tribute acts in the Market Place, starting at 2pm with Blondied, and then Sunday is the main day, with bands starting on the Market Place stage at 2pm. Band of Friends – Rory Gallagher’s band – headlines at 5pm. Sunday also has 15 fringe gigs in town centre venues running from 2pm till late in the Quaker House. For a full programme, visit enjoydarlington.co.uk
