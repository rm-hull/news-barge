A man wanted by police in connection to an 'attempted murder' has been arrested.

It follows an incident in South Bank, on Thursday, August 3.

It was reported that a man suffered stab and slash wounds to his head and arm. He was taken to hospital where he was treated for injuries which are not believed to be life-threatening.

In a previous statement, police said enquiries were carried out by CID officers and two 25-year-old men were arrested on suspicion of conspiring to murder. They have been bailed with conditions pending further enquiries.

Cleveland Police previously issued an appeal to find a 34-year-old man in connection with the incident.

In a new update, a force spokesperson said: "We're pleased to say a 34-year-old man who was wanted by our detectives in connection with an attempted murder in South Bank has been arrested.

"He is currently in police custody for questioning."
