A TAKEAWAY in the Bradford district has a one-star food hygiene rating.

Super Wok, located on Rawling Street in Keighley, was inspected on April 29 and found to be in breach of multiple food hygiene regulations.

## Raw meat stored next to ready-to-eat food

Inspectors raised serious concerns about contamination risks, including the improper storage of raw meat alongside ready-to-eat foods.

The report said: "It is recommended that raw meat is stored on the bottom shelf inside the fridge to avoid contamination."

## Chopping boards stored on stairs

Chopping boards were found stored on a staircase, which inspectors said posed a risk of cross-contamination.

Inspectors said: "The storage of the chopping boards on the stairs is not suitable as it creates a risk of cross contamination.

"You must relocate the chopping boards to a more suitable location away from potential sources of contamination."

## Food kept on the floor

Food was found being stored on the floor of the premises.

Inspectors ordered staff to stop the practice of storing containers of food directly on the floor and then on to work surfaces as it poses a contamination risk.

## Whole building required a 'deep clean'

It stated that the entire premises required a deep clean, and highlighted a mouldy and damaged fridge seal, food stored directly on the floor, and damaged kitchen flooring.

Inspectors said: "Cease the practice of storing containers of food directly on the floor and then on to work surfaces, as bacteria from the floor will then transfer to work surfaces."

## Staff not properly trained

A lack of staff training was also noted as a recurring issue.

The report said: "At the time of inspection, questioning of staff revealed a lack of training relevant to their work activities.

"You must arrange for relevant food handlers to be provided with instruction and appropriate supervision."

## What customers have to say

Super Wok currently holds a 3.4-star rating out of five on Google.

Julie Anderson said: "Got chicken and mushroom, egg fried rice and chips on 2nd August 2026.

"Was disgusting.

"Rice tasted like been reheated. Mushrooms were black and chewy, carrot was hard and chips overcooked.

"First takeaway in ages. Was so disappointed. Went in the bin."

However, another customer, Lee York, said: "Never had a bad meal from Super Wok, my go to Chinese food."

The Telegraph & Argus approached Super Wok for a comment.
