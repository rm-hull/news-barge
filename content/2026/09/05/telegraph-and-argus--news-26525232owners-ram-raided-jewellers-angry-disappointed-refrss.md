The owners of a jewellers shop that was ram-raided in Bradford have expressed their anger and frustration.

Pakistan Jewellers, on Duckworth Lane, was targeted when a 4x4 crashed through its front window on Saturday.

Police cordons were in place when the T&A visited, and a police van was outside.

The front window of the shop was smashed.

  Pakistan Jewellers on Duckworth Lane was ram-raided earlier today 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## Owners feel 'angry' and 'disappointed'

Repairs are expected to take three to four hours, and the shop will also have to close for a day.

Mr Ali said: “I feel angry.

"And disappointed.

"But I'm glad everyone is okay."


## Woman struck but not injured

Three customers were inside the shop when the incident occurred.

According to Muhammad Ali, who runs the jewellers with his father, Muhammad Ilyas, a woman was hit when the truck crashed through the window.

  CCTV footage of the incident 

*(Image: CCTV)*

Mr Ali said: "There were three customers in the shop at the time.

"One woman got hit, but not injured.

"We called the ambulance and gave her a bit of water, but she was okay.”

## No items were stolen

Mr Ali said said he took a bat to defend himself against the attackers.

No items were stolen.

Mr Ali said: “We saw a truck coming through the window.

"I jumped over the counter with a bat to attack them.

"I threw something at one of them and it hit the back of his head.

"The whole thing probably lasted around 35 seconds.”

Police enquiries are ongoing.
