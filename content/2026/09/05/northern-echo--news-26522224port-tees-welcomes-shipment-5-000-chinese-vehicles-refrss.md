A MAJOR shipment of 5,000 vehicles has arrived at Teesport.

The shipment arrived from China and marks one of the largest automotive vessel calls in recent years.

The delivery was part of a major UK import operation and included models from the Chery Automotive portfolio, including Jaecoo and Chery vehicles, as well as the first 1,300 Lepas plug-in hybrid cars to enter the UK market.

This import highlights the port’s capacity to manage high-volume automotive logistics and strengthens its role as a key gateway for vehicle distribution across northern England and beyond.

Paul Foreman, chief executive officer of PD Ports, said: "This vessel call is a significant milestone for Teesport and demonstrates the important role the port can play in supporting the UK's automotive supply chains.

"Handling 5,000 vehicles from a single vessel showcases both the scale of our infrastructure and the expertise of our teams.

"With deep-water access, established automotive handling facilities and excellent connections to major markets across the north of England, Teesport is ideally positioned to support vehicle import and logistics operations at scale.

"We're pleased to welcome Chery to Teesport and hope this will be the first of many future calls as manufacturers continue to look for efficient, resilient and strategically located UK gateways."

  Image shows the arrival of the vessel carrying 5,000 vehicles into Teesport, unloading on to PD Ports' Tees Dock and the vehicles in the Teesport automotive storage area 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Teesport’s long-established automotive logistics operations include handling more than 100,000 vehicles annually at its peak.

The port offers deep-water facilities, dedicated storage compounds, and strong links to the A19 and A1 corridors, ensuring rapid distribution to dealer networks nationwide.

Catherine Zhu, logistics director of Omoda & Jaecoo & Chery UK, said the port would help ease pressure elsewhere.

She said: "The opening of this route to Teesport is of strategic importance to our UK operations.

"It will help relieve congestion at the southern and western ports, significantly improve delivery efficiency to our dealer networks across the north of England and provide robust logistics support for our future UK manufacturing ambitions.

"This is a strategically significant port development for Chery in the UK."
