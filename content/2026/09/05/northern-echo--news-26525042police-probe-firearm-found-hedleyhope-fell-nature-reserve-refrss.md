Police are investigating after a firearm was found in a County Durham nature reserve.

Officers said the weapon was discovered in the area of Hedleyhope Fell Nature Reserve, near Tow Law.

Police have since recovered the firearm.

Durham Police is now appealing for information from the public.

The full force statement reads: "A firearm has been located by a member of the public in the area of the Hedleyhope Fell Nature Reserve near to Tow Law, which has now been recovered by police.

"If anyone has any information regarding this firearm being in this location, please contact Durham Constabulary on 101."
