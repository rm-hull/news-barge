PUBS in the North East are on the hunt for the UK’s next pub singing sensation as part of a national competition.

Proper Pub Idol 2026, sponsored by VK and Take! Tequila, kicks off on September 7 - and the national final will be held at a North East pub in November.

The competition will see contestants perform in weekly pub heats, with each heat winner earning a bottle of Prosecco.

A spokesperson said: "Whether you’re a karaoke regular, a shower-singing superstar or simply ready to step into the spotlight, this is your moment."

Regional and national finalists will perform two songs in each round, one of which must be upbeat or high-tempo.

Winners at the pub finals will receive a £50 bar tab, with regional champions earning £100.

The pubs within the North East taking part include: Crown Colliery, Boldon Colliery; Rosies Tavern, Darlington; Turks Head, Darlington; Blue Bell, Felling; Miners Arms, Middlesbrough; Spring Gardens, North Shields; Royal George Tavern, Shildon; Stag Inn, Stockton; Beehive, Sunderland; Bird, Washington.

The finalists will then have the chance to represent their pub at the National Final on November 22, at the Miners Arms, Middlesborough

The overall winner will receive a £1,000 cash prize and a studio recording day.
