IF YOU’RE looking for a home with a little added extra, make sure to view Kings Head Cottages which comes as a delightful package combining a modern detached family home with a two-acre field.

The cherry on the top is the fact that this home has been finished to an outstanding specification with triple glazing, solar panels, mechanical ventilation and air source heat pump heating for efficient, future-focused living.

Combined, this has helped Kings Head Cottages to achieve an A-rated energy performance, with a score of 98/100.

  Great eco credentials and the addition of a two acre plot make this home in Heighington something special 

*(Image: CARVERS)*

Set over three floors, the property offers spacious contemporary accommodation with views over the adjoining open countryside.

It forms part of an exclusive setting alongside two further building plots, ensuring a cohesive and thoughtfully designed environment once complete.

The ground floor centres around a reception hall that leads to a study, lounge, cloakroom and a reception room that could be used as a bedroom for guests or multigenerational living.

Completing the ground floor are a utility room and the dining kitchen, with a large central island with breakfast bar, fitted units and a spacious dining area benefitting from a double-height arched window that allows light to flood into the room and frames wonderful views from the mezzanine level seating area above the kitchen.

To the first floor are a family bathroom and three bedrooms – one with an en-suite shower room.

Upstairs again is the principal suite also with an en-suite shower room.

Landscaped gardens provide an enclosed and picturesque setting, complemented by a driveway offering ample off-street parking and a double detached garage with an EV charging point.
