A police incident has closed a York bridge tonight (Saturday).

Ouse Bridge is closed to traffic. Pedestrians can still get through via the pavement on the Skeldergate side.

The road and the North Street side pavement are both closed.

Police, firefighters and paramedics are on the bridge.

The York Rescue Boat is in the water on the northern side of the arches.

It appears someone has climbed over the wall of the bridge.

A firefighter on a tether is leaning over the wall, flanked by police officers.
