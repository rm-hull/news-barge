A fundraiser has been launched to help pay for the funeral of Natalie McDonald, who was killed alongside her seven-year-old niece in a house fire.

Teresa Boland set up the appeal for Natalie, 34, who died in the blaze at a house on Birchington Avenue, Grangetown, on Wednesday, August 26 alongside Valentina Foster, seven.

The fundraiser has raised £208 towards its £10,000 target. Natalie’s funeral is due to take place on September 14.

  Natalie McDonald, 34, died alongside her niece Valentia Foster, seven, in a house fire 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

The appeal says the money will help Natalie’s mam, Lisa, and the family cover her funeral and other expenses.

It states: "Help Us Give Natalie the Farewell She Deserves.

"On August 26, 2026, our family suffered an unimaginable loss.

"Natalie was tragically murdered in a devastating house fire, alongside her beloved seven-year-old niece, Valentina.

"We are now coming together to support Natalie’s mam and the family as they face the heartbreaking task of arranging Natalie’s funeral and saying goodbye to a much-loved daughter, Mum, sister, auntie and friend.

"We are hoping to raise £10,000 to help cover the cost of Natalie’s funeral and the many additional expenses that come with giving someone the beautiful farewell they deserve - including the funeral itself, flowers, the order of service, funeral transport, the wake and refreshments, notices and other unexpected costs that the family are now facing.

"Natalie was never ordinary, and her final farewell shouldn’t be either."

  Floral tributes left at the scene of the devastating fire on Birchington Avenue, in Grangetown 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

The fundraiser includes a tribute from Natalie’s mam, Lisa, who called her a "bright and bubbly beautiful girl with a heart of gold".

She said: "Natalie was a bright and bubbly beautiful girl with a heart of gold. She was full of life and the life and soul of any party. She was a major part of our family and our street. Our whole family, our street and our community have been destroyed by the grief and loss of our loud, proud and gorgeous girl.

"Her daughter loved her mam more than words can say, and she wants to give her mam the best possible send-off she can, especially in these tragic circumstances. She wants her mam to have the beautiful farewell she deserves and for it to reflect the amazing woman she was.

"Natalie was someone who did so much for everyone around her. She was loved not only by her family and friends, but by so many people throughout Grangetown and our wider community.”

  Valentina Foster, seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, have been named locally as the two victims who died following a house fire in Grangetown. 

*(Image: Provided)*

The appeal says Natalie was known for her big personality, love of "bling" and leopard print.

Her family have chosen a silver coffin and horse-drawn carriage for the funeral, the appeal adds.

"The basic cost of a funeral is already around £6,000, before the additional touches that the family feel are important to give Natalie the send-off she deserves," the fundraiser says.

It also says the family are facing "many costs that can easily be overlooked during such an overwhelming time".

The appeal urges people to donate or share it if they cannot contribute financially.

Natalie was killed alongside her seven-year-old niece Valentina Foster after emergency services were called to the Birchington Avenue property at 5.23am.

  Police at the scene of the fire on Birchington Avenue in Grangetown 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Natalie’s family previously described her as the "heart and soul" of their street and community.

They said Natalie married Anthony McDonald nine months before her death and had "the biggest, most beautiful wedding".

They said: "Natalie Louise was known by everyone as Natalie Louise Kirton, she is Natalie Louise McDonald having married Anthony McDonald only nine months ago.

"She had the biggest, most beautiful wedding; she sparkled like the shining beauty she was. She was loved by everyone, she was bright, bubbly and full of life, the heart and soul of our street and community, loved by all.

“Her family has lost its beautiful, kind soul. She was the life and soul of every party, and she will be sadly missed by our family, her two brothers and sister, all who are broken.

"Her nieces and nephews, with her being such a big part of their lives, being like a second mother to them. Her nana, grandad, mam and dad are all heartbroken.

“Also, her only daughter; who will forever miss her mam.

“Our Nat will be forever missed."

A man accused of murdering Natalie and Valentina has appeared in court.

Jaden Wright, 23, faces two murder charges, two counts of grievous bodily harm with intent and two counts of attempted grievous bodily harm with intent.

He did not enter pleas during a 10-minute hearing at Teesside Crown Court.

Wright was remanded in custody until a hearing on September 23.

  Valentina Foster 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

A GoFundMe appeal has also been launched to support Valentina’s family following the seven-year-old’s death.

Her family previously described her as their “beloved daughter”, saying she was “deeply loved” and would be “missed beyond words”.

They said: “Our lives have changed forever, and we are struggling to come to terms with this devastating loss.” The family added: “Our daughter will always be in our hearts, and her memory will live with us forever. Forever missed by her brothers and sisters.”

Dozens of floral tributes have been left at the scene for Natalie and Valentina.
