A North East garden centre has received praise from customers, with visitors highlighting its food, coffee and friendly service.

Dobbies Garden Centre Birtley, on Durham Road, has a Tripadvisor rating of 3.1 out of five from 217 reviews.

The centre offers flowers, plants, gardening tools, furniture and homeware, and is home to a café serving breakfast and lunch.

Tripadvisor lists the venue as a café and British restaurant, with outdoor seating, parking, highchairs and wheelchair access.

One customer said: “You probably wouldn’t normally think of a garden centre for a good lunch but here’s one.

“This excellent garden centre also actually has a good value hearty food restaurant.”

The reviewer ordered lasagne and fish and chips, calling the meal “a good portion” with “excellent chips and cakes”.

They added: “Good efficient service and staff very attentive to cleanliness.”

Another visitor praised the centre’s afternoon tea.

They said: “Last year I even surprised my wife with the St Valentine’s Day afternoon tea which was absolutely great.

“We were treated like royalty by the staff and the food was second to none.”

Dobbies offers afternoon tea bookings at its Birtley restaurant, including traditional, luxury, children’s and gluten free options.

The garden centre is open 9am to 6pm Monday to Saturday and 10.30am to 4.30pm on Sundays.

The restaurant is open 9am to 5.30pm Monday to Saturday and 10.30am to 4pm on Sundays.
