John Dewhirst has been a prolific writer about the history of Bradford City and of sport in Bradford dating back to 1984 when he co-founded The City Gent fanzine.

He has written a number of books on both the history of the Bantams and the club’s rivalry with Bradford (Park Avenue).

  His latest work, due out on September 15, is called *The Bantams of Valley Parade*.

It charts the history of the club from its beginnings in 1903, when predecessors Manningham FC abandoned rugby, up to the present day.

  City were formed in 1903 and quickly found early success. 

*(Image: Sent by Amberley Publishing)*

The famous 1911 FA Cup win is covered in the book, as is the devastating Valley Parade Fire Disaster in 1985.

It also covers City’s time in the Premier League, their giant-killing run to the League Cup final and their last-gasp League Two promotion in 2025.

  For Dewhirst, who is the managing editor of the History Revisited series published by bantamspast, this is first book since *Wool City Rivals: A History in Colour* with co-author George Chilvers in 2020. 

  The cover of John Dewhirst's new book. 

*(Image: Sent by Amberley Publishing)*

His blog Wool City Rivals features the history of Bradford City and Avenue and he also edits VINCIT, the online journal of Bradford sport history.

Publishers Amberley Publishing said of his latest work: “This approachable history of Bradford City charts the fascinating story of the football club from its beginnings to the present-day.

“It will appeal to followers of the Bantams everywhere.”

The book includes picture sections, and is also available in Kindle, Kobo and iBook formats.
