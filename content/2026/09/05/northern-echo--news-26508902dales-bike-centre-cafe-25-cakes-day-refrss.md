A Yorkshire Dales café has become a destination for cyclists, walkers and cake lovers thanks to a counter which can feature more than 25 different varieties of cake in a single day.

Dales Bike Centre Cafe and Cakery is based at Parks Barn in Fremington, close to Reeth in Swaledale.

Surrounded by some of the Dales' most celebrated cycling countryside, the centre combines a café and cakery with bike hire, a shop and workshop and accommodation.

But plenty of visitors appear to be travelling there for the cake alone.

  Dales Bike Centre Cafe and Cakery 

*(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

The centre says its team makes more than 25 different types on most days, all baked on site, alongside breakfasts, soups, sandwiches, paninis and other homemade meals.

One visitor this year went even further, saying they counted 35 cakes during a visit and describing the café as their favourite.

The café currently carries a 4.8-star Google rating from almost 300 reviews.

Its setting underneath Fremington Edge is also repeatedly mentioned.

A visitor in May described it as a "perfect pit stop" whether arriving by bicycle, on foot or by car, praising the food, prices and Swaledale scenery.

Dales Bike Centre was established in 2009 by husband-and-wife team Stuart and Brenda Price and describes itself as being as much about creating a community as cycling.

The family-run business says it employs people from ten local families and tries to source as much of the café's produce locally as possible.


Ingredients include eggs, meat, milk, fruit and vegetables and locally roasted coffee from suppliers around the Dales and neighbouring areas.

The café caters for gluten-free and dairy-free customers, with around five or six gluten-free cakes normally available each day.

Visitors can eat inside, in the courtyard or outside looking towards the surrounding hills.

Cycling remains at the centre of the wider operation.

More than 50 mountain and road bikes are available to hire, while routes from the site include the Swale Trail towards Gunnerside and Keld and the 130-mile Yorkshire Dales Cycle Way.

The location has also made the café popular with walkers and motorists touring Swaledale.

Recent reviews have included some criticism of queues and service during busy periods, but its overall ratings remain particularly high.

Dales Bike Centre is currently open seven days a week from 9am until 5pm.

And despite everything else available on site, the business's own philosophy about refreshments remains pleasingly simple: there is always cake.
