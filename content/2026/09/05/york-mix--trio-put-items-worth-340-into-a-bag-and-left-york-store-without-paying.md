Police have issued this photograph after a theft at a York store.

It happened at Matalan, the clothes and homeware shop at Clifton Moor.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Three people entered the store at about 1.30pm on 4 August, put clothing worth a total of £340 into a bag, and left without paying.

“The woman pictured here on CCTV may have important information to assist our investigation. If you recognise her, please get in touch.”
