A traditional café in the heart of the Yorkshire Dales has built up a loyal following among motorcyclists, walkers and tourists visiting one of the region's busiest villages.

Penny Garth Cafe sits in the Market Place in Hawes, surrounded by some of Wensleydale's most popular roads and walking country.

The café has become particularly well known among bikers touring the Dales and currently has a 4.6-star Google rating from more than 900 reviews.

On TripAdvisor it has a 4.4 rating, with breakfast, lunch and brunch served alongside vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options.

  Penny Garth Cafe sits in the Market Place in Hawes 

*(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

Visitors repeatedly highlight its straightforward, homemade food rather than anything overly elaborate.

One regular customer described Penny Garth as one of their favourite places to eat in North Yorkshire, praising its "simple, wholesome food" and generous plates.

Another visitor who had been searching Hawes for somewhere reasonably priced described the café as a "gem on Hawes main street" and said their bacon sandwich was the best they had eaten for years.

Breakfast is a particular draw.

A couple visiting during colder weather said their vegan breakfasts were among the best they had eaten, praising the portions, coffee and choice of vegan sweet treats.

The café has also earned praise from people with dietary requirements.

One customer said they were surprised to discover four gluten-free cakes available during their visit, as well as gluten-free bread, and praised staff's understanding of cross-contamination concerns.

But its popularity with motorcyclists is perhaps the most distinctive part of Penny Garth's identity.


Hawes is surrounded by roads which draw riders from across northern England, with routes leading through Wensleydale towards places including Buttertubs Pass, Aysgarth, Leyburn, Ingleton and Sedbergh.

The café's Market Place location means bikes can regularly be seen outside while riders stop for breakfast, a mug of tea or cake before continuing through the Dales.

Penny Garth has been serving visitors for decades, and its current operators have gradually developed its menu towards fresh and homemade food while maintaining its traditional café character.

The venue also has an outdoor webcam which riders can use to see conditions around Hawes before setting off.

As with any busy café, reviews are not universally positive. One returning customer criticised the service during a visit in October 2025, although they acknowledged having visited many times previously.

The overall picture remains considerably more positive, with hundreds of Google reviewers giving Penny Garth an average score of 4.6.

Current listings show the café opening at 9am at weekends and from 10am on most weekdays, with Friday listed as closed.

For bikers heading through Wensleydale, its combination of traditional food, central location and Yorkshire Dales roads has helped turn Penny Garth into a familiar stopping point.
