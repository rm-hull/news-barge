An action plan has been drawn up to address concerns relating to noise, traffic and disruption at a luxury wedding venue.

The Monal by Jalwa, in Bradford Road, Cleckheaton, has been the subject of complaints due to claims of disruption caused by events at the venue.

Now, a meeting has taken place to discuss the next steps, with police, politicians and the business all taking part.

Kim Leadbeater, Labour MP for Spen Valley, said: "Over recent months, residents have raised serious concerns about noise, traffic, and disruption around The Monal in Cleckheaton.

"This week I hosted a multi-agency cross-party meeting at my office that brought together Kirklees Council, West Yorkshire Police, The Monal and all three Cleckheaton ward councillors, Cllr Micheal Howard, and Cllr Kath Pinnock and Cllr Andrew Pinnock, to agree what needs to happen next.

"Following my meeting, there are now clear actions for the venue, the council and the police.

"I know how frustrating the ongoing disruption has been for people living nearby.

"It is not fair on people in our community who simply want to enjoy their homes, particularly elderly residents and those with young children or pets."

Specific complaints include cars being revved in the car park and on the street, fireworks and flares being set off, and double parking causing congestion.

On some days, the venue hosts two weddings, which residents say doubles the disruption.

## A series of actions have now been agreed

The venue will strengthen its terms and conditions for guests, clarify its policy on fireworks, explore additional parking options, and increase the use of parking attendants and registered security staff, according to the MP.

Kirklees Council will visit the venue to discuss practical measures and review management arrangements, while also monitoring the premises during periods of high activity.

West Yorkshire Police will set up a Problem Solving Occurrence (PSO) to monitor the site until November 2026, with a review planned for spring 2027.

Councillor Pip Harvey, cabinet member for place, culture and volunteering, said: "Residents shouldn’t have to put up with the disturbances reported in recent months.

"That’s why the council is working with the police, the venue and other partners to address these concerns and help local people enjoy their homes in peace.

"We’re working with the venue to manage noise more effectively during events, while the police are addressing concerns around nuisance parking and vehicles in the area.

"We’ll continue to monitor the situation to make sure the relevant rules and regulations around noise and events are being followed."

## The response from the venue

A spokesperson for the Monal said: "We’d like to thank Kim Leadbeater MP, Cllr Kath Pinnock, Cllr Andrew Pinnock, Cllr Michael Howard, the team at Kirklees Council and West Yorkshire Police for their time, support and constructive approach in working together.

"We value the opportunity to continue working closely with all those involved and already have a range of robust measures in place, with further initiatives being introduced to strengthen guest management, safety and consideration for the local area.

"As a proud part of Cleckheaton and the wider Spen Valley, our community means a great deal to us. We’re committed to working positively with residents and local partners, and we look forward to sharing some exciting ideas that we hope will make a meaningful difference for our community."
