While the summer might be ending and you will be expecting the weather to get a lot worse in the coming weeks, things can be made better by a trip to the seaside.

Even throughout the autumn, it can be great to visit the coast for a family walk or to spend some time exploring the area.

Of course, any trip to the seaside must be topped off with enjoying some fish and chips.

If you are visiting the popular seaside town of Filey any time soon, you will want to stop off at the famous Inghams Fish Restaurant.


## What is Inghams Fish Restaurant in Filey like?

Inghams Fish Restaurant has been serving traditional fish and chips for over 30 years in Filey.

The restaurant was once visited by the popular content creator, the Yorkshire Man, who described it as “one of the best chippies on the Yorkshire coast”.

The Yorkshire Man was “immediately impressed by the extra crispy golden batter” and added that it was “so much better than your average chippy”.

The restaurant has space for you to sit in and enjoy your meal as well as a takeaway next door that is ideal for taking your food to eat while watching the sea.

At the restaurant, you can choose from a range of fish including classics like haddock and cod to fish cakes and plaice goujons.

You can also grab a range of sides to enjoy alongside your fish such as homemade curry sauce, baked beans, and mushy peas.


Inghams Fish Restaurant also offers sweet options for those who fancy some ice cream or a slice of hot chocolate fudge cake to finish off their meal.
