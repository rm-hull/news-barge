A “fabulous” family home with Minster views has hit the market in York – for £650,000.

The three-bedroom house is for sale in Belle Vue Terrace, off Heslington Road.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said the Victorian home “retains many period features that blend beautifully with modern living”.

Inside, the reception hall leads to the living room with a large bay window and log burning stove.


A reception/dining room is behind the living room overlooking the garden.

The large dining kitchen is to the rear of the property featuring French doors that open to the rear garden.

Upstairs, two double bedrooms and the main bathroom are on the first floor.
