# Man whose body was discovered in woods named as tributes paid by family

## The man whose body was found in Ecclesall Woods earlier this week has been named by police.

Ryan Kettlewell, 38, was described by his family as a lovable character who will be missed.

His family said: “We will always continue to love him and will miss him greatly."

Ryan’s family ask for their privacy to be respected.

![The man whose body was found in Ecclesall Woods this week has been named as Ryan Kettlewell.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjQ2ZmViNzc5LWFhNjMtNGZiMi1hZTQ0LTdjYWZjMmFhZGNmNjo2NGY3YzEyZC00YjQ1LTQ1NGMtOWNhMy05MWNlMTdkMDI2MDA=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Following the discovery of Ryan’s body on Wednesday September 2 at 6.46pm, a murder investigation was launched.

A forensic post-mortem has revealed that Ryan died as a result of a gunshot wound.

Two people, a woman aged 34 and man aged 36 were arrested on September 3 on suspicion of murder.

They have since been released on police bail as enquiries continue, police said.

Detective Chief Inspector Andy Knowles, who is the senior investigating officer for this case, said: "We are committed to finding answers of how Ryan lost his life.

“I continue to urge anyone with information about his death to get in touch, no matter how irrelevant or insignificant you believe it to be, it could be the missing piece we need.

“Our thoughts are with all those who knew and loved Ryan.”

To report information, please call 101 or get in touch via the police website.

Quote incident number 821 of September 2, 2026 when you get in touch.
