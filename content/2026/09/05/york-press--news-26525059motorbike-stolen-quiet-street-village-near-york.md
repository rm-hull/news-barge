A MOTORBIKE has been stolen from a quiet residential street in North Yorkshire.

The incident occurred at 1am on Sunday (August 23) in Cowstail Lane, Tockwith.

  


North Yorkshire Police said officers would like to speak to the male pictured in CCTV in relation to the theft.

A force spokesperson said: “If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact the OIC PC 1505 Jackson [Richard.jackson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Richard.jackson@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101 with reference 12260164440 when providing information.”
