Two women who were arrested after a fatal collision have been bailed.

Emergency services were called to reports of an incident on Killinghall Road, near Bradford Moor Park, just before 9pm on Thursday, 3 September.

A man died in the collision.

Two women, aged 19 and 47, were arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving.

They were held in custody but have since been bailed.

### What police said

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Two women, aged 19 and 47, who were arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving have been bailed pending further enquiries.

"Enquiries remain ongoing by the Major Collision Enquiry Team."
