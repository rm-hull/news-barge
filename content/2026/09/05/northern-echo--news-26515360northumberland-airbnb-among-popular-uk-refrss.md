A “lovely and peaceful” spot near the Northumberland coast has been named one of the most popular Airbnbs in the UK.

Condé Nast Traveller has revealed the most wishlisted Airbnbs in the UK over the last year.

A spot in Northumberland has made it onto the list and was named the best for “a room with a view”.

Skylark Studio has “panoramic” views of the dunes and beaches of Alnmouth Bay and offers guests a relaxing place to escape from daily life.

  You can enjoy sea views from the studio. 

*(Image: Courtesy of the Airbnb Community)*

## What is Skylark Studio like?

On the Airbnb, the publication said: “Panoramic ocean vistas over the dunes and beaches of Alnmouth Bay take centre stage at this light-filled hilltop studio flat.

“It’s a perfect spot for a couples’ getaway, forming one wing of a self-built timber and stone converted barn.

“Inside uses a thoughtful mix of sustainable materials, selected by the home’s architect owner – expect birch plywood joinery and natural plaster walls with bamboo flooring and cool acoustic ceilings.

“A soapstone log burner and air-source underfloor heating keep the open-plan interior cosy through stormy days, and there’s a cute-as-can-be handcrafted window seat opening onto a front row view of the local birds and sunrises over the sea.”

The studio has been built in a timber and stone converted barn and has been decorated with natural features.

It has an open-plan living and sleeping area with a well-equipped kitchen where you can enjoy a meal while watching the sunset over the coast.

The studio is placed close to the beach and is just a few miles away from both the village of Alnmouth and Warkworth.

If you would rather stay home and enjoy the studio, it is a great spot for reading, birdwatching, and enjoying the views while sitting in the garden area.
