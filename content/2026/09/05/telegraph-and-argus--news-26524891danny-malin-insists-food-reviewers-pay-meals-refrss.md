A YouTube food reviewer has sparked debate after insisting on paying for his meal at a fish and chip shop.

Danny Malin, a Leeds-born content creator who is known for his food reviews, made the comments during a recent visit to a Sheffield business believed to be the UK’s oldest fish and chip shop.

Danny, formerly of Rate My Takeaway and now running the channel Danny v Food, was offered a free meal by the owners while filming a review but politely declined.

He said: "I’ve got to pay.

"I have to pay, honestly.

"I have to pay the full price.

"That’s one of the things I say.

"You’re a small business.

"I’ll make my money off the YouTube video.

"I’m not here to get a free dinner."

Danny explained that while content creators can offer valuable exposure, this should not come at the expense of independent businesses.

He said: "Hopefully, you’ll make money off this and then it’s happy days.

"So I’ll pay full price, please.

"Thank you very much anyway, very kind of you."

He told the owners not to feel obligated to offer free food to content creators, emphasising that those who choose to film at a business do so because they believe it is worth featuring.

He said: "When creators come to you, they're doing it for a reason.

"One, they know you’re good and two, they’re wanting to create the content.

"It does help you.

"But don’t mention it, okay?"

Reflecting on the encounter, Danny praised the owners’ generosity but stood by his decision to pay.

He said: "Sometimes, you come across kind people.

"But let me pay for it.

"Let me give an honest review, because it might be no good.

"I don’t want free food if it’s no good."
