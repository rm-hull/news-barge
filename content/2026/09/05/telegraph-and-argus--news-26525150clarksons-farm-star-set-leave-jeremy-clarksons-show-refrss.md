Clarkson's Farm fans will see Kaleb Cooper in a new light in an upcoming spin-off show.

Kaleb: Down Under will showcase Jeremy Clarkson's farming advisor as he embarks on his first solo venture in Australia in a four-part series on Prime Video.

The venture marks Kaleb's first flight, as the farming contractor, known for his deep love for Chipping Norton, takes on the challenge of expanding his business into one of the world's largest farming markets.

The series will follow Kaleb Cooper meeting his Australian counterparts, engaging with the local culture, and exploring farming operations in the distinct environment of the country.

Fazia Khan, head of unscripted UK Originals at Amazon MGM Studios, shared the news at the Edinburgh TV Festival.

She highlighted how the series showcases a different side of Kaleb, pushing him out of his comfort zone.

Fazia said: "Our Kaleb Cooper series has been announced and we're very excited.

"He's one of the stars of Clarkson's Farm.

"He's a young farmer, his fan base has grown, and he really resonates with our audiences.

   

*(Image: Cheffins)*

"It will be launched early next year, and for those of you who know Kaleb, he never wants to leave Chipping Norton, but he's gone entrepreneurial and he's got this idea of going to Australia to explore farming there so we'll be seeing him take his first flight, which is to Australia.

"He is the expert on Clarkson's Farm, he's the one telling Jeremy off, but we'll see him out of his comfort zone doing something he's never done before.

"We'll see another side to him which I think is very special, so we're really excited to launch that."

Kaleb, in a statement, expressed his enthusiasm for the venture.

He said: "I spend most of my time with the most travelled man in the world, so I got brave and booked my first ever flight to see what all the fuss was about. Australia here I come!

"And Australia, you’ll be gaining a new farmer for a couple of months as I discover if I can make my farming contracting business go international."

Kaleb gained public attention in 2021, starring in the first series of Clarkson's Farm as an adept farm hand supporting Jeremy, a novice farmer.

Initially working on the farm under Howard Pauling's management, Kaleb's impressive local knowledge and work ethic earned him a permanent role, assisting Jeremy, which led to his breakout role in the Amazon Prime Video series.

Kaleb is married to Taya, and the couple shares three children.
