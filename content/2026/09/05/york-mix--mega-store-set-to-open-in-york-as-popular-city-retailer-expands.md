A York retailer is set to open a second location before the end of the year – and the owners described it as a “mega store”.

Fullyfloored & Furnished, on James Street, is set to expand with the opening of a new premises in Acomb.

The furniture and flooring specialist will open at 52 Front Street in Acomb in early December 2026.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/fullyfloored-and-furnished-acomb-1000x1333.jpeg)

52 Front Street was previously home to York accountancy practice Sunley & Co.

“In times of doom and gloom within the retail world with more retailers either closing shop or down sizing it’s exciting that Fullyfloored & Furnished are expanding,” said owner, Dominic Shortle.

The new retail shop and showroom will be stocking furniture old and new, beds and mattresses handmade in Yorkshire, antiques and collectables, hardware and tools, every day essentials, and food and clearance bargains.

It’s “a mega store which will be jammed packed making us a one stop shop in Acomb,” said Dominic.

Fullyfloored will be expanded with more products and brand new ranges for the opening – with over 2000 roll end carpet and vinyls available ready to takeaway or be fitted by their in-house teams of fitters.

James Street is set to undergo a big transformation, with the former Barnitts warehouse at 95 James Street planning to transform into eight padel courts.

However, the two locations will run concurrently, with the James Street shop to “remain open until well into 2027.”

“We’re very excited to offer this amazing range of products to the local community of Acomb and further afield,” said Dominic.

“It’s going to be mega!”
