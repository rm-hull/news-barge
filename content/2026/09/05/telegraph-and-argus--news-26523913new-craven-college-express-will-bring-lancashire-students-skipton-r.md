A NEW daily bus route is set to make college journeys easier for students travelling to Skipton from East Lancashire.

Craven College has partnered with bus operator Transdev to launch a direct term-time service to its Aireville Campus in Skipton, supporting the around 25 per cent of students who commute from East Lancashire.

The service responds to feedback from students and parents who raised transport as a barrier to education, particularly for those unable to access their preferred courses due to limited travel options.

Anita Lall, Principal and CEO of Craven College, said: "We are delighted to be launching this important bus service, which reflects the needs of our local communities.

"When students are planning their next steps, travel is an important part of that process and we hope this dedicated service will make travel to college easier for our current students whilst also making Craven College an attractive, accessible and affordable option for students looking to apply for their next steps."

The bus will depart from Nelson at 7.30am, stopping in Colne, Earby, and Barnoldswick before arriving at the college by 8.30am.

The return journey leaves the campus at 4.30pm, offering a direct route home via the same stops.

Gareth Dixon, Deputy Principal and CEO of Craven College, said: "We identified that our students from the west of Skipton were struggling with transport to the college, resulting in many not being able to attend their course of choice and not being able to maximise their potential in their chosen area.

"Through working with Transdev we identified an appropriate route to capture as many current and prospective students as possible between Nelson and Skipton to provide them with access to the learning opportunities available at Craven College."

Ticketing options start from £3 for a single journey, payable on board, with termly passes also available for purchase from Aireville Campus.

Maria Jones, head of admissions at Craven College, said: "Conversations with applicants and their parents made us aware that transport may be a barrier for students travelling in from East Lancashire.

"This new route will help to make Craven College an even more viable option for them to access the opportunities here, and it has been a really good experience to partner with Transdev to provide this service."

The first bus will depart Nelson Bus Station at 7.30am on Monday September 7.

David Wallington, head of commercial at Transdev, said: "With a reliable and affordable transport option now in place, we look forward to welcoming Craven College students on board from 07 September."
