Cleveland Police has thanked the community for their support after an A66 crash funeral.

  The funeral of Jakub Matusiak - one of the **__five people in the Volkswagen Passat who died__** in the crash on August 22 - took place in South Bank on Friday (September 4). 

  The incident, which involved a marked Cleveland Police vehicle and the Passat, which was travelling the wrong direction, also took the lives of __PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough.__

Several schools, as well as Normanby Medical Centre on Normanby Road, announced they would close early due to the disruption expected from the funeral.

However, in a force update, Cleveland Police has thanked the community for their continued cooperation and support and said the day passed without issue.

Superintendent John Wrintmore said: “Today’s events have passed without issue and very minimal traffic disruption.

“Our policing operation to ensure public safety will take the same approach next week when more funerals are taking place.

“We will continue to work closely with our partners to minimise disruption in these areas and keep the public safe.

“No schools, services or businesses have been instructed to close, and any decision to do so have been made by individual organisations and their own risk assessments.

“We would like to thank our communities for their continued cooperation and support. Our visible police presence in the area will continue throughout the week to provide reassurance and allow everyone to continue with their daily lives as normal.”
