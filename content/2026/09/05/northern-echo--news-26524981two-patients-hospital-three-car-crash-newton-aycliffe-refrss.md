Two patients have been taken to hospital following a three-car smash in Newton Aycliffe this morning.

Police, paramedics and fire crews were called to the crash on the junction of the B6443 and Horndale Avenue at 11.19am.

All three cars sustained damage with one vehicle left on its side. The road remains closed and two people have been taken to hospital.

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) said: "We received a call at 11.19am on September 5, 2026 to reports of three vehicles involved in a collision on the junction of the B6443 and Horndale Avenue in Newton Aycliffe.

"We sent three resources to the scene. Two patients, were taken to Darlington hospital and a third was treated at the scene."

County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service said they were alerted to the crash at 11.20am.
