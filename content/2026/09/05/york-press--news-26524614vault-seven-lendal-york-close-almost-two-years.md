## Well-loved vintage shop set to close in York city centre

Vault Seven will close its doors after nearly two years of trading on Sunday (September 13) *(Image: Newsquest)*

A well-loved York vintage shop is preparing to close its doors after nearly two years.

Launched by business partners Sammy Clews and Betsy Vaca, Vault Seven has welcomed customers at its store in Lendal since December 12, 2024.

Packed with pre-loved clothing, jewellery, vintage toys and books – the shop has been described as “a real treasure trove”.

  


Posting on social media, its owners said they had made the “very difficult decision” to close the doors of Vault Seven on Sunday (September 13).

  Sammy outside Vault Seven (inset: some of the clothes) 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

A statement read: “Since opening on December 12, 2024, we’ve been incredibly lucky to have met so many amazing people, found some brilliant pieces of clothing, and created a shop that we’ve genuinely loved coming to every day.

“This has been a tough decision and one that certainly wasn’t made lightly, but we feel that now is the right time to bring this chapter to a close.

“For myself and the whole team, thank you to every single person who has visited, bought something, recommended us to a friend, followed along, stopped for a chat, or supported us in any way.

“As a small, privately owned business, every bit of support has meant more to us than you probably realise.

“We’re incredibly grateful for the time we’ve had, and we’ve genuinely enjoyed ourselves along the way.

“We’ll look back on Vault Seven with a lot of very good memories.”

  “This has been a tough decision and one that certainly wasn’t made lightly, but we feel that now is the right time to bring this chapter to a close" 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

In the week leading to the shop’s closure, there will be a special 20 per cent sale on items.

The team behind Vault Seven said these discounts would be getting “bigger and bigger” as the shop nears its final day of trading.

Prior to operating as Vault Seven, 11 Lendal was the home of Jack Wolfskin.

Sammy and Betsy, both 25 at the time, helped transform the site – naming it Vault Seven.

Speaking to The Press, the pair explained: “Seven is a lucky number, and both mine and Sammy’s birthday, and we wanted to try to keep in line with the history to the street, with The Old Bank, The Old Post Office, and we thought a vault fitted in quite nicely.”

  Vault Seven will be holding a 20 per cent off sale in the run-up to its closure on September 13 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Sammy, who had previously owned a kilo sale business, selling vintage clothes by weight at universities across the country, said he made the decision to open in York after spotting a gap in the market.

He said: “Coming from Wetherby, I’ve seen that Leeds is absolutely full of vintage shops.

“I have a friend who tried to open one in Harrogate and that didn’t go so well so I thought York has a few, it would be nice to try and open a shop here.”

The building is now being marketed to let by surveyors Carter Towler.
