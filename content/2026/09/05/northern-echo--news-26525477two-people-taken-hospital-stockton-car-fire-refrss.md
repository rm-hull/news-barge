Two people have been taken to hospital after a car fire.

Emergency services were called to a blaze on Ragpath Lane in Stockton.

A clinical team leader, medicar and three ambulances were sent to the scene.

Two people were taken to the University Hospital of North Tees, in Stockton, with minor injuries.

A North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) spokesperson said, "We received a call at 15.46 to reports of a car on fire on Ragpath Lane in Stockton.

"We sent a clinical team leader, the medicar and three emergency ambulance crews.

"We took two patients to North Tees hospital with minor injuries."
