The scores are in — the latest batch of food hygiene ratings for York restaurants, cafes, takeaways and businesses has been revealed.

Environmental health officers have been visiting businesses across the city to see if they are meeting hygiene requirements.

Based on guidelines set by the Food Standards Agency, inspectors look to see how clean the facilities are; what food safety management procedures are in place and how hygienic food handling is.

Ratings are then dished out from five to zero — with the higher score the better the result.

  **Five — Very Good**

2 Many Wines in 3 Bishopthorpe Road, York, rated 5 on 19 August 2026.

Bistro Guy in 19 Heath Ride, Strensall, York, rated 5 on 19 August 2026.

Charles XII in Main Street, Heslington, York, rated 5 on 19 August 2026.

kWh Coffee Roastery in Unit 39 The Raylor Centre, James Street, York, rated 5 on 19 August 2026.

Cookery School at The Grand York in Grand Hotel, Station Rise, York, rated 5 on 24 August 2026.

Treasurers House Cafe in Treasurers House, Minster Yard, York, rated 5 on 24 August 2026.

York Minster Refectory in Deangate, York, rated 5 on 24 August 2026.

Rebel Tea in York Railway Station, Station Road, York, rated 5 on 26 August 2026.

Smokehouse Burritos in Shambles Market Food Court, Newgate, York, rated 5 on 27 August 2026.

Businesses may have been reinspected since, and this will be reflected in our next round-up.
