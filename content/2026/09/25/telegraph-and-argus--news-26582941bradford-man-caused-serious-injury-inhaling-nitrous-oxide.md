A driver from Bradford who was shown on his own dashcam footage crashing into multiple vehicles whilst under the influence of nitrous oxide has been sentenced.

Cheten Bhardwaj, aged 35, of New Cross Street, West Bowling, admitted to causing serious injury by dangerous driving, possession of nitrous oxide for wrongful inhalation, and driving a motor vehicle with a proportion of a specified controlled drug above the specified limit.

On May 9 last year, police received several calls reporting multiple collisions on Halifax Road.

Bhardwaj was driving a Volvo V40 whilst high on nitrous oxide.

He crashed into multiple vehicles, leaving one woman with serious injuries.

Dashcam footage from his own vehicle shown in court showed how dangerous the driving was, with the passenger repeatedly telling him to “slow down” and “use the brake.”

Today (September 25) at Bradford Crown Court, Bhardwaj was sentenced to three years and eight months in jail and disqualified from driving for three years and ten months.

Chief Inspector James Farrar of the Roads Policing Unit said: “The actions of Bhardwaj were simply reckless and selfish; this case highlights the very real and often catastrophic consequences of dangerous driving and driving whilst taking nitrous oxide.

“Bhardwaj made a choice to get behind the wheel, whilst under the influence and put other road users at risk. I am thankful nobody was killed.

“Nitrous oxide has been a banned drug since November 2023 and the footage in this case highlights the dangers of using it at the wheel. It has similar effects to other drugs and can severely impair your ability to drive.

“Officers from the Roads Policing Unit are committed investigating serious road traffic offences and bringing those offenders to justice.

“West Yorkshire Police is part of the Vision Zero partnership which is committed to reducing the number of serious injury and fatal collisions in our area.”
