Hartlepool Restaurant Week returns next month, as is still open to eateries wanting to sign up.

The event runs from Monday, October 19, to Sunday, October 25, and will include restaurants, pubs, and cafes from across the borough.

As well as boosting trade during the quieter autumn period, the week aims to help businesses reach new customers and promote their menus.

**Venues confirmed to be taking part so far include:**

* No. 5 New York Pizza
* Daisy & Beas @ Highlight
* The Marine Hotel
* Portals
* Dhamaka
* Larry’s Lanes and Games
* Flamingos
* Wallis & Co.
* Radici Pizza Restaurant
* Dilshad
* Mary Lambert
* Place To Be

Councillor Rob Darby, chairman of the council’s housing, growth and communities committee, said: "With a fresh, new look, next month’s Hartlepool Restaurant Week is set to be bigger and better than ever, building further on the great results last January.

"It’s a fantastic opportunity for Hartlepool businesses to showcase their menu, attract new customers, boost footfall during the quieter autumn season and raise their profile as part of Hartlepool’s thriving food and drink scene.

**Read More:**

"I hope as many of our marvellous Hartlepool eateries as possible will get involved."

The previous Restaurant Week in January saw 28 businesses take part and 2,500 customers enjoy special offers.

Businesses wanting to sign up must be able to offer a dine-in experience to take part.
