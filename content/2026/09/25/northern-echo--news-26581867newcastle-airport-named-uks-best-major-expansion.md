New routes, expansion and investment have seen a North East airport named the best in the UK.

Newcastle Airport won Airport of the Year at the Aviation Industry Awards UK, beating Manchester Airport to the title.

The award recognises the airport’s customer service, network growth, infrastructure upgrades and sustainability work.

Nick Jones, chief executive at Newcastle Airport, said: "We are incredibly proud to have been named Airport of the Year at the Aviation Industry Awards UK.

"This award recognises a period of significant growth and investment for Newcastle Airport, during which we have expanded our route network, further enhanced our facilities, and continued to deliver an award-winning experience for our passengers.

"This could not have been achieved without the hard work and dedication of our people and business partners, who continue to be the driving force behind our success.

"To be recognised by our industry peers in this way is a real honour and one that everyone connected with Newcastle Airport should take great pride in."

Industry experts chose Newcastle Airport for its network growth, customer service and sustainability work.

The airport launched 22 routes in 2025-26, including new flights to Berlin, Copenhagen, Lisbon and Nice.

easyJet also opened a base at the airport and expanded its network to 32 destinations.

Nearly £60 million has been spent on passenger facilities over the past two years.

Upgrades include a three-storey terminal extension, a larger departure lounge, expanded baggage facilities and a bigger security area.

The airport says 99 per cent of passengers now clear security in under 10 minutes.

It is also working towards net zero carbon by 2035.

Newcastle Airport scored 100 per cent in the 2025 Global Real Estate Sustainability Benchmark (GRESB), ranking first in its sector for carbon reduction, biodiversity and sustainable travel.
