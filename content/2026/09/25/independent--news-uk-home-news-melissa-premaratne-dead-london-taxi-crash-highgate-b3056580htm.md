Police have named a 16-year-old private school pupil who died after being hit by a taxi in north London, with her family describing her as a "light in our lives".

Highgate School student Melissa Premaratne died following the crash on North Road in Highgate shortly after 8am on Thursday, according to the Metropolitan Police.

The driver of the Addison Lee car, a 68-year-old man, was arrested at the scene under suspicion of causing death by careless driving.

He has subsequently been released on bail.

In a statement released through the police, Melissa’s family said: "Melissa was a light in our lives and an amazing person.

"We will miss her deeply, every single day.

"Her kindness, warmth, and beautiful spirit will always remain in our hearts."

![Addison Lee confirmed it was one of their cars that hit the schoolgirl (PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2021/10/15/07/14171218-1e5ffbfa-9175-46de-a6ea-187b0056470b.jpg)

Private hire group Addison Lee confirmed on Thursday that the vehicle involved in the fatal incident belonged to its fleet.

"A collision took place in Highgate this morning at approximately 8:30am involving one of our vehicles which has tragically resulted in the death of a 16-year-old girl," a spokesperson for the company said.

"Our thoughts are with her family and all those affected at this incredibly difficult time.

"As is standard for an incident of this nature, the response is being led by the police, and we are supporting them fully with their investigation.

"As the facts are still being established, we are unable to comment further at this stage."

Highgate School stated on Thursday that the victim was a Year 12 pupil who was making her way to the private school when the collision occurred.

"It is with deep sadness that we share the news that one of our Y12 pupils died today on the way to school in a road traffic incident," the spokesperson for the private school said.

"This incredibly distressing news has come as a terrible shock to everyone at Highgate.

"Emergency services attended the scene and the police are currently investigating the situation.

"The family are being supported by specialist police officers and are receiving all the appropriate support.

"Out of respect for the family’s privacy, no further details will be shared at this time.

"We thank you for your understanding and compassion."
