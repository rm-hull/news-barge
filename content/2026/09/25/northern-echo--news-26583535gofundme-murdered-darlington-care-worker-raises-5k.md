A fundraiser for the family of a 'murdered' Darlington care worker has raised more than £5,000.

The amount has been raised in just 18 hours for the family of Leanne, who was found dead in a flat above The Greyhound pub on Parkgate on Wednesday (September 23).

A GoFundMe appeal for her family has raised £5,223 since it was set up by Natasha Nicholson.

![]()Leanne Park, who worked at Ventress Care Home, was found at a flat above The Greyhound pub on Parkgate on Wednesday, September 23. *(Image: FACEBOOK)*

She wrote: "As a community, we want to come together to support Leanne's family at this devastating time. We are raising funds to support the family through this difficult time and to provide for any additional needs the family may have as they navigate this heartbreaking loss.

"Every contribution, no matter how small, will help ease the burden and allow them the space to grieve without further worry.

"Thank you for your kindness, generosity, and compassion. Together, we can show them they are not alone. With heartfelt thanks."

Two men have been arrested after police were called to the scene at around 9.15pm.

A 44-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of murder, while a 27-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender.

Police remain at the scene today, Friday, September 25, with a cordon still in place and white-suited CSI officers seen working inside.

![]() *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Leanne worked at Ventress Care Home in Darlington, where colleagues described her as someone who "would do anything for anybody".

![]() *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

In a Facebook post, the home said: "I can't actually believe I am writing this, but it is with a heavy heart that I say we have lost our friend and work colleague, Leanne.

"Leanne was always so kind and would do anything for anybody, especially our residents.

"The world is beyond cruel and you have been taken too soon. We love you much (sic) Leanne. Love all your Ventress Work Family. You will never be forgotten. Our condolences to all Leanne's family."

Tributes have been left outside The Greyhound, including bouquets of flowers.

One tribute reads: "Always in our hearts, taken too soon. Heaven has gained a beautiful angel".

Another says: "Our deepest sympathy and love during this hard time".

A further message reads: "RIP our beautiful Auntie. You are so loved and going to be a huge miss, love you always".

Another of Leanne's loved ones wrote: "RIP my best friend, I will miss you forever".
