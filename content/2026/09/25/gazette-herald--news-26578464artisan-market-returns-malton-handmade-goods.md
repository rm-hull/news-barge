An artisan market showcasing creativity and craftsmanship is returning to a North Yorkshire town.

Little Bird Made will take over Malton's Market Place on Saturday, September 26, from 10am to 3pm with handmade goods, food and seasonal treats from across Yorkshire.

Stalls will offer ceramics, jewellery, home fragrance, seasonal décor, artwork, fresh bakes, preserves, cheeses and craft drinks.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "Malton has a soul that is perfectly aligned with what we do, celebrating the local, the handmade, and high quality artisan products.

"Our markets are all about creating a warm, social atmosphere where people can catch up with friends, soak up the atmosphere, and discover incredible small businesses."

![]()Dan Leaver, founder of Dan’s Pies *(Image: Supplied)*

Visitors can also explore Malton’s independent shops and cafes while enjoying the town’s autumn atmosphere and Yorkshire hospitality.

Dan Leaver, founder of Dan’s Pies, said: "You can’t beat the smell of freshly baked pies as you wander through the market.

"We love seeing our regular customers, meeting new customers, and being part of such a passionate community of local makers."
