# Leeds announces Christmas 2026 programme featuring ice rink and festive food

## This year's Christmas market is set to return to the city square

Leeds has announced its Christmas programme with festive food and an ice rink set to arrive in the city.

This year, the Leeds Christmas Market is set to return to City Square on November 19. Leeds City Council has said the market will feature a "brand-new look", and will feature local food, drink, music, and entertainment.

The Ice Cube at Christmas will also return to Millennium Square, Victoria Gardens, and Cookridge Street and advance tickets for the ice rink are now on sale. This 400 square metre ice rink will have a transparent marquee, offering skating sessions daily, and Pengiun Club sessions for young people under nine on the weekends.

The City Square Market is being organised by Fabler and Co, who are being THOR's Tipi. This will bring stalls from 60 traders and food vendors, as well as two new bar areas.

There will also be 200 Christmas trees and an Elf Hunt trail in addition to the Christmas Tipis on Victoria Gardens. The Big Wheel will also return to Victoria Gardens, alongside the Golden Gallopers on Cookridge Street, and the 30 metre Starflyer.

In terms of music, there will be solo acts, brass bands, and choirs. Cabins will also be found around the market operated by Real Markets Yorkshire, featuring artisans.

Councillor Salma Arif, Leeds City Council’s executive member for economy, said: "Christmas is a really special time in Leeds and it’s always amazing to see the city transformed each year by all the unique magic of the festive season.

"Our Christmas programme is always hugely popular, attracting tens of thousands of people to the city centre and helping families and friends to create some incredible memories.

"This year we’ve worked closely with our new operator to ensure Leeds has a high quality, local market that showcases the very best our region has to offer, so Christmas in Leeds is shaping up to be bigger and better than ever and we can’t wait to welcome people to the city."

The Leeds Christmas Market will be open from November 19 to December 23. The Ice Cube at Christmas will also open on November 19, and will remain in place until December 31.
