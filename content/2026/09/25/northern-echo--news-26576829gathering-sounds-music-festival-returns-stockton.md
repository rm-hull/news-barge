More than 50 artists are heading to Teesside this weekend as a local music festival returns with its biggest line-up yet.

Organisers have unveiled the full line-up for The Gathering Sounds 2026, with seven Stockton venues hosting live music on Saturday, September 26, from 1pm to 11pm.

North East favourite Tom A. Smith, Birmingham indie band Overpass, Manchester shoegaze act TTSSFU and Glasgow rave-punk group VLURE headline the festival.

![]()TTSSFU will perform at Gathering Sounds in Stockton this weekend. *(Image: Gathering Sounds)*

![]()Vlure will perform at Gathering Sounds in Stockton this weekend. *(Image: Gathering Sounds)*

The final wave of artists announced includes Esmeralda Road, Little Grandad, 24 Hours In Paris, MOSS, PROJECTOR and The Youth Play.

![]()Little Grandad will perform at Gathering Sounds in Stockton this weekend. *(Image: Gathering Sounds)*

![]()Youth Play will perform at Gathering Sounds in Stockton this weekend. *(Image: Gathering Sounds)*

The festival will span some of Stockton's best-known grassroots venues, including the Georgian Theatre, KU Stockton, ARC Stockton Arts Centre, The Social Room and The Link.

Among the headline performances, Tom A. Smith will top the bill at the Georgian Theatre, while overpass will headline The Point at ARC, Basht will play KU Stockton, Shelf Lives will headline ARC2 and TTSSFU will close out the evening at The Social Room.

![]()Basht will perform at Gathering Sounds in Stockton this weekend. *(Image: Gathering Sounds)*

The line-up will also showcase a strong North East presence, with Charlie Floyd, Marketplace, Swindled, Daisy Brown and George Bailey confirmed for the day.

Festival organiser Jordan Kean said: "We deliberately kept a few big names back for this final announcement and we're really happy with how the whole line-up has come together.

"But what makes this year particularly special is the strength right through the line-up. We've got Tom A. Smith, Marketplace, Charlie Floyd, Swindled and some of the best live talent coming out of the North East alongside artists from across the UK and Ireland.

"We say it every year, but I genuinely think this is our best line-up yet."

Since launching in 2018, The Gathering Sounds has developed a reputation for showcasing emerging artists, with previous festivals welcoming acts including The Royston Club, Sports Team, and Lottery Winners.

One wristband gives festivalgoers access to every participating venue, subject to capacity limits.
