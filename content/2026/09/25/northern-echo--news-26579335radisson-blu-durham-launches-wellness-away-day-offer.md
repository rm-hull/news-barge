Businesses can swap traditional boardroom away days for cathedral tours, Pilates sessions and riverside walks under a new County Durham wellbeing partnership.

Radisson Blu Durham has teamed up with Onda Pilates and Durham Cathedral to give teams meetings with movement and mindfulness in the heart of the city.

The package includes guided cathedral tours, tower climbs, River Wear walks and on-site Pilates classes, all close to the hotel’s conference facilities.

Kayleigh Letman, founder of Onda Pilates, said: "At Onda, we’re passionate about making movement feel accessible, enjoyable, and genuinely beneficial to everyday life.

"For many people, a corporate away day can mean spending even more time sitting down, so we loved the idea of working with Radisson Blu Durham to offer something that breaks up the traditional meeting format.

"Even a short Pilates or mobility session can give people an opportunity to reset, move and reconnect before heading back into the rest of their day.

"Being a Durham business ourselves, it’s also really exciting to collaborate with other organisations in the city and showcase what Durham can offer businesses looking for something beyond the traditional corporate experience."

The partnership comes as organisations demand more from their away days.

Wendy Bryant, director of groups, meetings and events at Radisson Blu Durham, said: "We’re seeing it across almost every booking now.

"Clients don't just want a room and a projector any more.

"They want their people to leave feeling better than when they arrived, whether that's through movement, fresh air or simply a proper break in the middle of the day.

"Wellbeing has gone from a nice-to-have to a key factor."

Delegates can leave a fully equipped meeting room and climb the cathedral tower or join a Pilates class within an hour.

The hotel has also introduced its ‘Brain Boosting Lunch Menu’ to help delegates stay focused through the afternoon.

Radisson Blu Durham has also launched its Back to Nature Team Building Experience at Raby Castle, just half an hour away.

The package includes exclusive use of an estate event space, a grazing lunch from Raby Estate and a choice of clay pigeon shooting or a deer safari.

Dinner, bed and breakfast and return transport are included, with packages from £200 per delegate.

Ms Bryant said: "It's a genuinely different proposition for the region.

"You get the reliability of a five-star hotel with proper facilities, and then a completely different environment for the team-building element itself.

"Clay pigeon shooting or a deer safari isn't something most away days offer, and it gives people something to talk about long after the debrief."

The hotel caters for groups of every size, from boardroom meetings to large conferences, with refurbished bedrooms and Presidential Suites for organisers and VIP guests.

The wellness-led corporate days build on the hotel’s decade-long reputation for hosting conferences, weddings and awards nights.

The location and facilities were already in place. Now, recharging is built into the agenda rather than added on afterwards.
