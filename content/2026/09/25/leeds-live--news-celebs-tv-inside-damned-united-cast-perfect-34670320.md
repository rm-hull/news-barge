# Inside The Damned United cast as 'perfect' Leeds football drama lands on Netflix

## The Damned United revisits an infamous time in Leeds United's history with outspoken manager Brian Clough in the 1970s.

Leeds United's turbulent transition from Don Revie to Brian Clough takes centre stage in Netflix's The Damned United.

The "perfect" sports film has climbed into Netflix's top 10 most-watched list since arriving on the platform over the weekend. The 2009 drama looks back at one of the most extraordinary managerial appointments in the club's history.

Leeds United were reigning champions when Clough took over in 1974, but his time in charge lasted just 44 days as relations with players and the board unravelled.

Adapted from David Peace's 2006 novel, the film dramatises that short-lived reign, with Michael Sheen playing Clough. Alongside him are several familiar faces from the worlds of Adolescence, Harry Potter and Line of Duty.

For Leeds supporters watching the story unfold, here is a guide to the actors portraying the club's key figures and the people around Clough.

### Brian Clough – Michael Sheen

Taking over from Don Revie meant inheriting a title-winning Leeds side. It was also an unlikely move for Clough, who had been an outspoken critic of his predecessor.

Sheen plays the manager as he attempts to establish his authority at the club. His other screen credits include Good Omens, The Queen, Frost/Nixon, Masters of Sex and The Twilight Saga.

### Billy Bremner – Stephen Graham

Leeds captain Bremner is central to the film's dressing-room tensions. Portrayed as a leading opponent of Clough, he becomes one of the manager's biggest obstacles during his brief spell in charge.

Graham takes on the role, with viewers also likely to recognise him from Adolescence, This Is England, A Thousand Blows and Boardwalk Empire.

### John O'Hare – Martin Compston

Compston swaps police work for football to play O'Hare, the striker who played under Clough at Leeds.

Best known as DI Steve Arnott in BBC drama Line of Duty, Compston had experience of professional football before becoming a familiar television face. He made a couple of appearances for Scottish club Greenock Morton.

### Don Revie – Colm Meaney

Revie's departure to manage England sets the Leeds story in motion, leaving Clough to step into the role vacated by a formidable predecessor.

Meaney portrays the departing manager. His credits include Star Trek: Deep Space Nine and In the Land of Saints and Sinners.

### Peter Taylor – Timothy Spall

The film also explores Clough's relationship with Peter Taylor, his trusted assistant and close confidant. Taylor provides a steadying influence on the outspoken manager.

He is played by Spall, whose roles include Peter Pettigrew in the Harry Potter films, as well as appearances in Mr Turner and The King's Speech.

### Sam Longson – Jim Broadbent

Clough's story extends beyond Leeds to his earlier time at Derby County, where Sam Longson was chairman.

Actor Jim Broadbent's extensive screen career includes the Harry Potter and Bridget Jones films, Game of Thrones and Only Fools and Horses.

*The Damned United is available to watch on Netflix.*
