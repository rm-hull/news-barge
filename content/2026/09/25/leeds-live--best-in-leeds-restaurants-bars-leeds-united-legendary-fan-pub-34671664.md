# Leeds United legendary fan pub welcomes visit from Harry Potter star

## The venue is absolutely loved by Leeds United supporters

A famous Leeds United pub has welcomed an appearance by a Harry Potter legend.

The Old Peacock, based in Elland Road, is absolutely loved by Whites supporters. It is a matchday haven when Leeds United are playing.

The team from the pub have posted photos from a recent visit by Matthew Lewis who is known as Neville Longbottom in the famous film series. The star is one of Leeds United’s many celeb fans.

On Instagram, the Old Peacock team posted: “Matthew Lewis, Neville Longbottom from Harry Potter.” The actor posed for photos with staff and was even spotted behind the bar at the popular pub.
