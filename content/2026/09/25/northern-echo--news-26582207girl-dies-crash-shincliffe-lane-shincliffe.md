A girl has died in a crash involving a car and a van in County Durham.

Emergency services were called to Shincliffe Lane, Shincliffe, at around 5.30pm on Thursday (September 24). A Fiat 500 collided with a Ford Transit van.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "Despite the best efforts of emergency services, a passenger in the Fiat was pronounced dead at the scene.

"Her family have been informed and are being supported by specialist officers."

A second passenger was taken to University Hospital North Durham for treatment.

Both drivers suffered minor injuries.

The road was closed for hours but has since reopened. Police are now appealing for witnesses.

A spokesperson added: "Anybody who witnessed the collision, or who has dashcam footage of the vehicles, is asked to contact the Serious Collision Investigation Unit.

"You can get in touch via [sciu@durham.police.uk](mailto:sciu@durham.police.uk) quoting incident number 402 of September 24."
