A PATIENT grabbed a senior nurse round the throat and threatened to kill her when she was told to leave a hospital, York magistrates heard.

Vanessa Kershaw, 31, pulled out clumps of the nurse’s hair, “ragged her around”, ripped her clothes, damaged her glasses and tried to knee her in the face, said Kathryn Walters, prosecuting.

“I am going to kill you,” Kershaw said during the attack at Cross Lane Hospital in Scarborough on September 22.

She had arrived voluntarily at the psychiatric hospital and was angry that staff had told her she was not ill enough to need hospital care.

Ms Walters said while waiting for her hearing in the court cells, Kershaw had threatened to harm herself if she was released and she had told a member of the court’s liaison and diversion team that she wanted to go to jail.

Magistrates gave her an 18-week prison sentence including 14 weeks of a sentence previously suspended by York magistrates for assaulting emergency workers.

Kershaw, of Harewood Road, Harrogate, pleaded guilty to assault and breaching the suspended prison sentence for the third time.

Defence solicitor Harry Blaza told the court Kershaw wanted access to mental health help.

He said it was possible Kershaw had an “undiagnosed mental health condition” and urged the bench to adjourn the case for a psychiatric report.

If she were sent to prison she could lose her accommodation, he said.

A probation officer told the court mental health help in the community had been arranged for Kershaw but she wasn’t co-operating with it.

There was a “risk of harm both to herself and to the public” if she were given her freedom.

The suspended sentence was originally passed in April 2025 and had been extended when she had breached it and when she had committed another offence.
