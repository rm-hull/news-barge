One person has been taken to hospital after a fire at Catterick Garrison.

They suffered smoke inhalation in the blaze.

Fire crews from Richmond, Reeth and Bedale were called to the barracks at **7.48pm last night**.

An accommodation block was found to be well alight when the arrived.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Additional resources from Leyburn, Northallerton, Masham and an aerial ladder from Darlington were also mobilised.

“After the fire was brought under control, the last fire resources left the scene at 2.43am.

“On-site personnel will keep a watching brief overnight, with additional Fire Investigation due to take place later this morning.”
