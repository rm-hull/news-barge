Jeremy Clarkson has teased opening a second pub.

The Yorkshire TV personality's establishment, The Farmer's Dog, has emerged as something of a pilgrimage site, with enthusiasts travelling from across the country to Oxfordshire to sample his Hawkstone lager and ciders from Diddly Squat Farm.

While it's uncertain whether Doncaster-born Mr Clarkson might launch a second venue, he's not dismissed the possibility outright.

Floating an idea on X, one user highlighted that Symonds Yat East, a village nestled in the Wye Valley, is available for purchase, observing: "A beautiful part of the country. Maybe someone can scoop up this little piece of heaven?

"It would be a fantastic spot for Jeremy Clarkson to run another pub in a popular area."

In what appeared to be a playful tease, Mr Clarkson swiftly reshared the post on Thursday, September 24, simply writing "Hmmm."

While the former Top Gear presenter is remaining tight-lipped, supporters have already urged Mr Clarkson to seize the opportunity.

Busier than ever, Mr Clarkson continues juggling the management of his pub alongside the day-to-day operations of Diddly Squat Farm, while also filming for Clarkson's Farm.

"We've nearly wrapped on six," the television personality told Radio Times this month.

"A couple of weeks away from finishing that, and then it'll be edited and translated and so on.

"But nearly done six, and then who knows?"
