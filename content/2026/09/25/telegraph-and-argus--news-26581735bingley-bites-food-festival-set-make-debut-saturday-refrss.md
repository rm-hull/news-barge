A new festival is set to bring food, fun and entertainment to the heart of Bingley.

The first-ever Bingley Bites Food & Drink Festival will take place on Saturday, September 26, from 11am to 5pm in Bingley Market Square, promising a lively day filled with food, drink, and family-friendly activities.

The event is a collaboration between Northern Indie Collective and Bingley Arts Centre, building on the popularity of the town’s monthly market.

Tara Broe, co-founder of Northern Indie Collective, said: "We thought we were going to wait until next year to start it, but then we thought, let's do it this year. Why wait, we know we’re capable.

"We're both foodies, we're very passionate about food and we've been to other food festivals.

"There is no reason why Bingley can't have one."

### Over 30 stalls

The festival will feature 30 stalls from across West Yorkshire, offering everything from grilled meats to Mediterranean treats and sweet bakes.

Confirmed vendors include Greek Corner from Shipley, For Bake's Sake, Chilli Sauces coming from Leeds, and Bradford’s Istanbul Grill.

It’s not just about food—there will also be stalls selling handmade goods such as crystals, jewellery, and skincare.

![]()Brick Bank stall *(Image: Tara Broe)*

![]()Naturally Soapy *(Image: Tara Broe)*

Entertainment will include a live performance from Bingley-based singer Matthew Broadbent and a children’s baking competition, judged by Maria at Hedgehog Organics.

The event is just one of several new ideas from Northern Indie Collective and Bingley Arts Centre, who are working together to inject new life into the town centre.

### Breathing life back into Bingley High Street

Tara said: "Myself and Holly both used to trade on Bingley Market. When it was ran by someone else, another organizer, and unfortunately they had to give up the license.

"We just kind of saw it go downhill after that and we were both really upset about it, especially with Bingley having historic market.

"We got together and thought lets do something about it."

![]()Yorkshire Coffee Bar *(Image: Tara Broe)*

The team approached Bingley Arts Centre with the idea of reviving the market and were met with enthusiasm and support.

### Support from local community

Tara said the response from the community has been overwhelmingly positive.

She said: "So many people have commented about it being so good to see so much life in the in the market square again.

"We're wanting to give people a reason to come down and use the high street and remember that Bingley has a high street and it can be thriving."

The organisers hope Bingley Bites will become an annual event and are already considering expanding it into a full weekend in the future.
