Harrogate has won a fifth consecutive Yorkshire in Bloom Gold award, while a colourful town-centre trail celebrating 60 years of arts and culture also took top honours.

Harrogate BID won Gold at the 2026 Yorkshire in Bloom awards, with its Floral Summer of Celebration named the winner of the Yorkshire Rose Town/City Centre/BID category.

Harrogate BID operations manager Bethany Allen said: "We’re absolutely delighted to have won Gold and been named the category winner at this year’s Yorkshire in Bloom awards.

"This is the fifth consecutive year that Harrogate BID has achieved Gold, which is a fantastic reflection of the work that goes into our floral programme and the support we receive from partners across the town."

The trail featured eight floral installations across the town centre and ran from July 3 to 17, in partnership with Harrogate International Festivals and Helen James Flowers.

It marked 60 years of Harrogate International Festivals.
