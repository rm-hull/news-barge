A major UK holiday park operator has acquired an award-winning resort "with over 2,500 pitches".

Haven, one of the country’s largest operators with 40 sites, has acquired the Seal Bay Resort on the Selsey peninsula.

The well-known West Sussex site sits to the east of Portsmouth on England’s south coast.

Haven, a Blackstone portfolio company, which owns parks in Ayrshire and East Lothian, has announced that Seal Bay Resort has been acquired by Blackstone.

The holiday park was previously owned by Cove Communities.

Seal Bay will continue to trade under its current name for the remainder of 2026, before transitioning to the Haven brand for the 2027 season.

Holidaymakers can continue to book holidays on the current Seal Bay resort website, including for breaks in 2027.

Haven already operates 39 holiday parks "in stunning coastal locations in England, Scotland and Wales".

Each park has an indoor swimming pool, supermarket, a pub-restaurant and branded food outlets, including nine parks with JD Wetherspoon pubs, 32 Papa Johns, 22 Burger Kings, six Millies Cookies and five Slim Chickens.

Haven was acquired by Blackstone in 2021. Since then it has invested over £700 million in its parks, upgrading facilities, extending the food & beverage options for guests, and developing additional pitches and upgrading the holiday home fleet. In recent years, it has acquired the Richmond Holiday Centre (now Haven Skegness Holiday Park) and Holivans Caravan Park in Mablethorpe.

Simon Palethorpe, Haven chief executive, said: “I’m delighted that we are welcoming Seal Bay to the Haven family.

"It’s a fantastic park in the most stunning location and will sit brilliantly alongside our 39 other parks.

"Haven’s mission is all about making memories that last a lifetime and we can’t wait to start working with the Seal Bay team to deliver this for thousands of holiday makers and owners in the years to come.”

The park was acquired for an undisclosed sum.
