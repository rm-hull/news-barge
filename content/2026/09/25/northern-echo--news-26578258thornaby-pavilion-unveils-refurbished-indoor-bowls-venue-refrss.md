Indoor bowls players are set to benefit from a major investment at a popular North East sporting venue following the completion of a significant refurbishment project.

Tees Active has fitted a new blue bowling carpet, redecorated Thornaby Pavilion and upgraded its furniture and communal areas.

Tees Active managing director Leon Jones said: "We're delighted to unveil these improvements at Thornaby Pavilion.

![]()The new blue indoor bowls carpet *(Image: Supplied)*

"The new bowling carpet and wider refurbishment have transformed the facility, creating a welcoming space for both existing and new bowlers.

"Indoor bowls is an accessible sport that can be enjoyed by people of all ages and abilities, and this investment reflects Tees Active's commitment to making it easier for more people to get involved."

Tees Active funded the refurbishment as part of its commitment to improving facilities for customers and members.

![]()Players on the new blue indoor bowls carpet *(Image: Supplied)*

English Indoor Bowling Association chief executive Peter Thompson said: "It's a great boost for the sport of indoor bowls to see investments such as this at Thornaby Pavilion.

"Modern, high-quality facilities play an important role in attracting new participants, keeping existing bowlers and ensuring clubs remain at the heart of their local communities."

Councillor Nigel Cooke, the cabinet member for environment, leisure and culture, welcomed the investment.

![]()Players on the new blue indoor bowls carpet *(Image: Supplied)*

He said: "It's great to see Tees Active investing in Thornaby Pavilion's indoor bowls facility.

"These improvements have transformed the space and will help provide even more opportunities for local people to stay active, socialise and enjoy the sport."

The upgraded venue is open to members, visiting bowlers and anyone trying indoor bowls for the first time.

The pavilion will hold a free open day on Saturday, October 24, from 10am to 2pm.

![]()Celebrating the refurbishment at Thornaby Pavilion *(Image: Supplied)*

Visitors can tour the refurbished facility, try indoor bowls and find out about its programmes.

The pavilion hosts social bowls every Tuesday evening for beginners and people returning to the game.

Support is available, while regular roll-up sessions offer relaxed opportunities to play.

The venue also offers improved facilities for corporate events and private hire.
