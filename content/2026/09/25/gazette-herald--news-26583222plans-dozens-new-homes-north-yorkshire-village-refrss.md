PLANS have been submitted for dozens of new houses in a North Yorkshire village.

Mandale Homes has applied for permission to build 32 properties on land east of Field View Close in Ampleforth.

The application also includes associated landscaping and external works.

RECOMMENDED READING:

The plans, which have been submitted to North Yorkshire Council, will be decided by the authorities Thirsk and Malton planning committee.
