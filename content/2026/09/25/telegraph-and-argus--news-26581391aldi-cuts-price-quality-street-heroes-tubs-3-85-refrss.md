ALDI has slashed the price of two of its Christmas chocolate tubs to just £3.85.

The offer covers 500g tubs of Quality Street and 475g tubs of Cadbury Heroes, making them the cheapest on the market.

The price is 40 per cent lower than Sainsbury’s, saving shoppers £2.65 on each tub.

Even with a Nectar card, Sainsbury’s Cadbury Heroes tub is still 22 per cent more expensive than Aldi’s offer.

The price drop is part of Aldi’s effort to help shoppers budget for the festive season.

The offer is available in stores now, excluding Scotland.
