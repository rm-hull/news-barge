The army has issued a statement after a blaze tore through an accommodation block at military barracks in Catterick Garrison.

Firefighters were called to the single living block at Gaza Barracks last night (September 24) after a fire broke out just before 8pm.

Footage taken from the scene showed flames and smoke pouring from the roof of the block while firefighters doused the flames from a platform in the air.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service sent fire engines from Richmond, Reeth, Bedale, Leyburn, Northallerton, Masham, and an aerial ladder from Darlington, to the incident.

And now, an army spokesperson has confirmed that one service person was taken to hospital for treatment. An investigation has since been launched.

A statement said: “We can confirm that a fire broke out within a Single Living Accommodation block at Gaza Barracks in Catterick on September 24.

“North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service attended, and the fire was brought under control. We are grateful to the fire service for their swift action.”

“As the matter is still under investigation we cannot comment further.”

Everyone affected by the fire is now being provided with alternative accommodation and relevant welfare support, The Northern Echo understands.

The fire was brought under control in the early hours and firefighters left the scene at 2.43am.

A fire service spokesperson previously said: “Fire appliances from Richmond, Reeth and Bedale responded to a report from on-site personnel of a fire within an accommodation block.

“On arrival, with the building found to be well alight, additional resources from Leyburn, Northallerton, Masham and an aerial ladder from Darlington were also mobilised.

“After the fire was brought under control, the last fire resources left the scene at 2.43am.

“On-site personnel will keep a watching brief overnight, with additional Fire Investigation due to take place later this morning. One person was taken to hospital with smoke inhalation.”
