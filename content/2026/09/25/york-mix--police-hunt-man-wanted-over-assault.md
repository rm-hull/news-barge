He’s 24-year-old Lewis Beal, and is wanted by police on suspicion of assault.

He’s described as a white man with short ginger hair, a short ginger beard and brown eyes.

If you see him, or know where he is, you’re asked to contact North Yorkshire Police. Call 999 with an immediate sighting, or report information via 101 or the police website.
