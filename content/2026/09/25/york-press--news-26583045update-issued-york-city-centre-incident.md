AN update has been issued after emergency services were seen in York city centre yesterday evening (Thursday, September 24).

As reported by The Press, paramedics and officers from North Yorkshire Police were in Walmgate around 5pm at the junction with George Street - police were reported to be at the scene until 7pm.

An ambulance was seen with lights flashing and a Yorkshire Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "Ambulance resources have responded to a medical incident at a private address in the Walmgate area this afternoon.

"No further details are available due to patient confidentiality."

North Yorkshire Police was contacted but the force did not provide details as it was a private medical incident.
