LNER has upgraded its onboard digital service ahead of World Tourism Day, giving international passengers live travel information in several languages.

London North Eastern Railway (LNER) has upgraded its ‘All Onboard’ portal, accessed by scanning a QR code on the back of each seat.

Passengers can now check live updates, order food and drink and plan journeys in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Simplified Chinese.

Nick Bibby, customer experience improvement manager at LNER, said: "Every year we welcome customers from across the UK and around the world onboard our trains.

"We know that English may not be everyone's first language, and that many people may be unfamiliar with the UK rail network, so we're really pleased to be making our onboard portal available in a number of languages.

"This update is a brilliant way for our customers to access travel information while travelling with us, and to help them to travel with greater confidence."

The portal also features Routes, the LNER podcast hosted by Jenni Falconer, and the LNER Kids Club podcast for younger passengers.

World Tourism Day is held on September 27.
