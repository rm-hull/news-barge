Three teenage boys have been arrested following an "appalling attack" at a bus stop in Crook -bringing the total number of arrests to seven.

Police were called at around 8.40pm on Saturday, September 19, after reports of a fight at a market place bus stop.

Video footage from the incident was also widely circulated on social media which appeared to show two teenage boys being attacked by a large group of youths.

Today, Durham police confirmed three further youths, two aged 14 and one 13, were arrested on Thursday (September 25), with enquiries still ongoing.

All three have since been released on bail.

A force spokesperson said: “Three further arrests were made on Thursday in relation to this incident.

“The boys, two aged 14 and one aged 13, were brought into police custody, where they were questioned by officers.

“They have been released on bail while further enquiries are carried out. No one has been charged at this time.”

Four boys were previously arrested on September 20 on suspicion of affray.

They have since been released on bail with a curfew while further enquiries are carried out.

Under their curfew, the boys must stay at home between 4.30pm and 7am Monday to Friday.

On weekends, they are only allowed out with a parent.

Inspector Andy Reeves, from Crook Neighbourhood Police Team, previously said: “The scenes we saw in Crook this weekend were absolutely appalling.

“Our enquiries have so far resulted in the arrest of four youths, and we have identified further suspects who will be brought in for questioning.
