A driver had their vehicle seized after police stopped them over the inappropriate use of a car horn in Bradford.

Officers from West Yorkshire Police's Bradford West neighbourhood policing team said the vehicle was spotted on Horton Grange Road after being "sighted and heard" due to the manner in which the horn was being used.

After speaking to the driver, officers discovered they only held a provisional driving licence.

Police said the motorist was driving without displaying L plates and did not have a qualified supervising driver with them, both of which are requirements for provisional licence holders driving on public roads.

The vehicle was subsequently seized and the driver was reported to court.

## What police said

In a post on social media, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Honk if you want your vehicle seized!

“Sighted & heard on Horton Grange Road West Yorkshire Police - Bradford West due to inappropriate use of the horn, we discussed the matter with the driver only to discover that they were a provisional licence holder only, with no L plates displayed or a supervisor present.

“Vehicle seized and driver reported to court.”
