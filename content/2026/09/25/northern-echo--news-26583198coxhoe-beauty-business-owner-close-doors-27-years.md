A County Durham beauty business owner is set to close her doors after 27 years, blaming rising costs and ongoing health problems.

Lindsay, who runs her business from 16a Church Street in Coxhoe, has announced she will step away from the business, with her final working day on Thursday, October 22.

She said she made the "incredibly difficult decision" after 27 years in the beauty industry, including 10 years as a self-employed business owner.

Lindsay said rising living costs, stock and business expenses, along with her ongoing health problems, had made it increasingly difficult to continue.

![]()Beauty by Lindsay on Church Street in Coxhoe, Durham *(Image: GOOGLE)*

She thanked customers who had supported her throughout her career, saying many had been with her for years and had supported her through difficult times.

She said: "You haven't simply been clients - you have been a huge part of my life."

Lindsay said the industry had given her "so many wonderful friendships and memories" and that she would remain "incredibly thankful" for them.

She added: "Walking away from something I have loved for 27 years is emotional, but I also feel that now is the time to put my health, my wellbeing and my future first."

The business will remain open until October 22. Lindsay urged customers to keep existing appointments and invited anyone seeking a final appointment to get in touch.

She said: "Thank you from the bottom of my heart to every person who has ever booked an appointment, recommended me, bought products, supported my little business, or simply walked through my door. You have helped make the last 27 years so special."

The announcement has prompted an outpouring of support from customers and colleagues.

One customer said: "You are amazing at what you do you will be a big miss. But things are harder and additional to trying to be well you need to look after yourself. 27 years, be proud and look forward to your next steps."

Another said: "Aw you be a big miss and I’m so sorry to hear this …. wishing you the very best for the future."

A third added: "So sorry to hear this Lindsay. I know this will have been an extremely hard decision for you to make and the costs to run your business have risen massively, plus your health is more important. Take care of yourself first then you can decide what you want to do going forward."
