A popular artisan market featuring independent makers, food producers and creative businesses is set to bring visitors into the heart of a North East city.

The Little Bird Made Artisan Market returns to Ripon’s Market Place on Sunday, September 27, from 10am to 3pm, with shopping, food and community activities.

Jackie Crozier, managing director and founder of Little Bird Made, said: "Our markets are all about bringing people together in vibrant, friendly spaces where they can connect with the creators behind the products.

![]()Little Bird Made Artisan Market returns to Ripon Market Place, featuring handmade gifts, art, street food and local North Yorkshire makers. *(Image: Supplied)*

"Ripon’s historic market place provides the perfect atmosphere for a beautiful Sunday out, whether you’re coming to grab a coffee and browse the stalls, support local businesses, or enjoy a day out with the family, there is something for everyone at Little Bird Markets."

Artists, crafters and food producers will offer one-off items at the market.

Tim Gomersall, owner and designer of Tim Gomersall Art and Illustration, said: "Ripon has become a firm favourite place to bring my art to over the last couple of years.

"There's always a nice buzz around the market square, and a lot of local talent on display from my fellow creators.

"If I get a moment away from my stall, it is a great place to go and sketch too – one or two of my favourite artworks started life here as quick pen drawings."

Organisers are urging visitors to explore Ripon’s cathedral, independent shops, canal towpath and Spa Gardens while they are there.

Find details of upcoming markets and book a stall through the Little Bird Made website or by emailing [info@littlebirdmade.co.uk.](mailto:info@littlebirdmade.co.uk.)
