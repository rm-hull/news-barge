President Donald Trump said on Friday morning that Chinese President Xi Jinping agreed with about renaming AI “super intelligence.”

The president posted on Truth Social the day after Trump hosted Xi for a state dinner.

“Very productive meeting with President Xi for both the U.S.A. and China,” the president said. “Tremendous things will be happening. Like almost everyone else, he seemed to like calling the poorly and inaccurately named Artificial Intelligence to a far more accurate and important name, SUPER INTELLIGENCE. That would be ‘SUPER!’”

The president has so far rebuffed calls for Congress to place new regulations on AI, saying that “The only control or 'guardrails' that AI needs is a STRONG AND SMART (High IQ!) PRESIDENT, and the U.S.A. has that, in spades.”
