A new food business is putting a savoury spin on the celebration cake with handcrafted cheese and charcuterie centrepieces.

The Grown Up Cake Co, founded by Emily Pulsford creates, savoury cakes from selected cheeses, cured meats and accompaniments for birthdays, weddings and other occasions.

Ms Pulsford said: "I have always loved the way good food brings people together.

![]()One of the creation's of York's The Grown Up Cake Co *(Image: Supplied)*

"I wanted to create something that still feels special and celebratory, but for people who would choose cheese and charcuterie over a traditional cake."

Customers can customise each cake with ingredients to suit the event and their taste.

The cakes are designed as centrepieces for engagements, anniversaries, hen parties, baby showers, brunches, dinner parties, Christmas celebrations and corporate functions.

![]()One of the creation's of York's The Grown Up Cake Co *(Image: Supplied)*

Ms Pulsford said: "For me, the best occasions are the ones where people gather around a table, share great food and spend time together.

"That is really what The Grown Up Cake Co. is about."

The company also makes charcuterie boards to accompany the cakes and create a larger spread.

Ms Pulsford backs other independent businesses by sourcing ingredients from Yorkshire producers whenever possible.

She said: "There are so many fantastic food businesses and producers across Yorkshire and I want to support them wherever I can.

"It means a lot to be able to create something special for our customers while also supporting other independent Yorkshire businesses."

The Grown Up Cake Co. is taking orders and event enquiries in York and the surrounding area.
