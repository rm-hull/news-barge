A LOCAL poppy appeal has asked for volunteers to come forward.

Keighley’s Poppy Appeal team are calling for support ahead of this year’s campaign, which runs from October 24 to November 11.

Volunteers are needed to assemble and fill poppy boxes on October 7 and 8.

They are expected to deliver boxes and collect tins locally by October 12, and spend a few hours in the Airedale Shopping Centre or supermarkets selling poppy merchandise.

Anybody interested in volunteering is asked to contact Jenny Mercer on 07929 385240.
