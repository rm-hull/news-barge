A rider was caught trying to flee police on a Sur-Ron bike.

He was spotted in the Horsforth area by officers from the Leeds District Off Road Bike Team.

### Illegal e-bike seized after police chase

The rider headed down a dead-end track, abandoned the e-bike, and tried to escape on foot but was chased and detained by officers.

![]()A modified bike pictured in Bradford city centre. *(Image: T&A)*

He was found to be riding without a licence or insurance, and the Sur-Ron was seized.

A West Yorkshire Police post said: "Another illegally used off-road bike taken off the streets following proactive patrols by the team."

### Police focus on e-bike crackdown

The force said it remains focused on tackling anti-social vehicle use, including e-bikes and e-scooters, through ongoing neighbourhood operations.

West Yorkshire Police has also expanded the use of SelectaDNA Defence Spray across the county.

The forensic tagging spray helps identify suspects and vehicles involved in anti-social behaviour and related crimes.
