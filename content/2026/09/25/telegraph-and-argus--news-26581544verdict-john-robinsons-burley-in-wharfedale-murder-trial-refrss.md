**A jury in the trial of a man charged with murder has delivered its verdict this morning at Bradford Crown Court.**

John Robinson had been on trial denying murdering Mason Jones, 30, who was pronounced dead from severe abdominal injuries on March 1, three hours after he was stabbed outside a flat off Main Street in Burley-in-Wharfedale, near Ilkley.

The jury heard that Robinson, a former chef, wielded the 17cm “Katto” chef’s knife during a confrontation between his on/off girlfriend Annabel Ramsay and two men, one of whom was Mr Jones.

![]()Burley-in-Wharfedale's main street *(Image: Google Streetview)*

Robinson, 30, of Derry Hill, Menston, had denied murder, manslaughter, and having a bladed article in public.

On Thursday, His Honour Judge Jonathan Carroll told the jury he was prepared to accept a majority verdict if they are unable to reach a verdict that they are all agreed on.

On Friday morning, the jury returned with its verdict.

Robinson was found **not guilty** of**murder.**

However, he was found **guilty** of**manslaughter** on a majority verdict of 10:1.

He was also found **guilty** of**possession of a knife** on a unanimous verdict.

Judge Carroll will sentence Robinson this afternoon.

He told Robinson: "You will be sentenced at 2pm. You are remanded in custody."
