A YOUNG person who admitted starting a fire has been referred to a Fire Safe Course following an arson incident in North Yorkshire.

The incident had taken place on land off Milford Road in Sherburn in Elmet on July 31.

It resulted in a media appeal for more witness statements and further information.




PC Nicholas Wood of the Tadcaster and Rural Neighbourhood Policing Team said: "Whilst this incident was resolved through a CRD, it is important that young people understand the dangers associated with fire-setting behaviour.

"I welcome the young person's acceptance of responsibility and hope that the Fire Safe Course will provide a valuable opportunity for education and reflection."

The Fire Safety course aims to educate young people on the dangers and consequences of fire-setting and encourage safer decision-making.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police thanked the public for their help with the investigation and urged anyone with information about anti-social behaviour to continue reporting it to police.
