Care workers and organisations across North Yorkshire are being urged to enter national awards celebrating excellence in the not-for-profit health and care sector.

The free-to-enter Markel 3rd Sector Care Awards honour individuals and teams making a difference through care and support services.

Simon Johnston, UK marketing director at Markel, said: "Behind every thriving community are people and organisations working tirelessly to make a difference."

"From supporting those who need it most to bringing people together and creating lasting change, the not-for-profit sector plays a vital role across the UK.

"Now, we want to celebrate the incredible work happening in Malton.

"We’re looking for inspiring individuals and organisations whose dedication is making a genuine difference in their communities to give them the recognition they deserve."

The awards are open to everyone in the not-for-profit care sector, from individual care workers to organisations of any size.

Lisa Werthmann, director at Care Management Matters (CMM), said: "I’d encourage everyone across the sector to get involved by nominating someone who deserves to be celebrated.

"And don’t forget to acknowledge your own contribution too – the work you do matters."

Nominations close on November 13.
