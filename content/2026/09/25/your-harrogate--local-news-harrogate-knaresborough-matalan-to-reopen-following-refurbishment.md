Matalan in Knaresborough is holding a reopening event tomorrow (Saturday) following a refurbishment.

The retailer has updated departments, layouts and displays throughout the store, with changes across its womenswear, menswear, kidswear and homeware sections.

The launch event begins at 9am on Saturday 26th September, with early visitors in with a chance of receiving a golden envelope containing an exclusive discount.

Additional offers and hidden vouchers will also be available during the reopening week.

The refurbishment includes new fixtures, mannequins, fashion displays and signage, with an updated layout designed to make the store easier to navigate.

Chris Wike, Store Manager at Matalan Knaresborough, said:

> “We’re excited to welcome our customers back to the refreshed Knaresborough store and share the new-look space with the local community.
>
> "The investment has created a much brighter and more contemporary environment, while the updated layout makes it easier for customers to browse and discover our latest collections.
>
> “We’re looking forward to celebrating the reopening on Saturday and welcoming customers through the doors to see the changes for themselves, explore the new collections and enjoy some of the special offers we have planned.”

Customers will also find a selection of concessions alongside Matalan’s own ranges.
