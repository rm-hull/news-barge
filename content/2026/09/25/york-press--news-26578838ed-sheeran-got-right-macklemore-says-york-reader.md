## A singer's job is to entertain, not preach

Religion, politics and worldly opinions are for you to 'keep one's own council'.

Although not a great fan of Ed Sheeran, the supporting act left Ed in a embarrassing conundrum regarding the pro-Palestine rapper Macklemore.

I fully agreed with Ed's stance on the whole issue of the turmoil in the world and his none comments on these events.

A person should not be allowed to use his personal fame to promote his own personal controversial opinion to the general public.

**D M DeamerPenleys Grove StreetMonkgateYork\_\_\_**

## Time to move on from Archer and aristocrats?

Why do England's cricket selectors continue to chose Jofra Archer as their main opening bowler, for he is expensive, inconsistent in line and rarely takes an early wicket?

On another matter, Earl Spencer is still living in the 16th century, does he not realise no one is the slightest bit interested in what he thinks, to over 90% of the population he is a relic from the past.

**Peter Rickaby Moat Way
Brayton
North Yorkshire\_\_\_**

## Burnham's UN debut hits the right note

Andy Burnham’s inaugural speech at the UN was a good ’un.

Feisty, frank and firm but not quite crossing the boundary into fighting talk, declaimed through northern lips, the message was clear.

Words weren’t minced.

Good start to the PM’s international career.

**Derek Reed Middlethorpe Drive
York\_\_\_**

## Why would aliens want to visit us?

I am shocked that our British nation has and is involved with Star Wars in space.

Hostile nations threaten our sovereignty and peaceful coexistence within planet Earth. Fake websites and disinformation wreck that world.

We live in a free world and values and this should be gospel in respect.

Imagine an extra-terrestrial species visiting our world and all they observe is numpties in control.

That is why there cannot ever be a First Contact on planet Earth.

I gaze in wonder as I sit at my eyepiece telescope surfing the black inkiness of the universe; they are out there but sadly I feel isolated from their first contact and that saddens me at these short sighted individuals now in control of our freedom.
