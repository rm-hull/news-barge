One person has been taken to hospital after a blaze ripped through an accommodation block at military barracks in Catterick Garrison.

A fleet of fire engines were called to the major fire at 7.48pm last night (September 24) where they found the building "well alight".

Pictures and video posted online showed flames and smoke pouring from the roof of the block while firefighters doused the flames from a platform in the air.

Fire engines from Richmond, Reeth, Bedale, Leyburn, Northallerton, Masham, and an aerial ladder from Darlington were all sent to the scene.

The fire service has confirmed that one person was taken to hospital with smoke inhalation.

The blaze was eventually brought under control and firefighters left the scene at 2.43am. An investigation is due to take place later this morning.

A spokesperson said: “Fire appliances from Richmond, Reeth and Bedale responded to a report from on-site personnel of a fire within an accommodation block.
