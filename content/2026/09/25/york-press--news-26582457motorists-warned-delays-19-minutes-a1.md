## Motorists warned of delays of 19 minutes on main Yorkshire road after crash

The A1 from J45 to J44, which leads to York and Tadcaster on the A64 has become partially blocked after a multi-vehicle crash *(Image: Newsquest)*

A MAJOR road in Yorkshire has become partially blocked after a crash involving multiple vehicles.

Motorists have been warned of severe traffic on the A1 from J45 on the Wattle Syke roundabout, outside Boston Spa, to J44 leading to Tadcaster and York.

The incident was first reported at 12.45pm today (Friday, September 25).




![]()Motorists have been warned of severe traffic delays of up to 19 minutes along the A1 Southbound *(Image: AA Traffic)*

AA Traffic said: “Severe delays of 19 minutes and delays increasing on A1(M) Southbound.

“Average speed ten mph.”

The Press has contacted Yorkshire Ambulance Service for comment.
