Ofsted inspectors praised a North Yorkshire secondary school’s “calm, welcoming and ambitious” environment after finding major improvements across school life.

Bedale High School was praised for its “rapid transformation” after inspectors found improvements in achievement, attendance, behaviour and wellbeing under headteacher Chris Stokes.

The school was rated “requiring improvement” two years ago.

![]()From leftCllr Annabel Wilkinson, headteacher Chris Stokes and North Yorkshire Council’s corporate director for children and young people’s service, El Mayhew *(Image: NYC)*

Chris Stokes, who joined Bedale High School two years ago shortly after the previous inspection, said: "We are absolutely delighted with the findings of this inspection, which recognises the tremendous progress that has been made across every aspect of school life.

"The report reflects the school we see every day – a calm, inclusive and ambitious environment where pupils feel valued, want to learn and achieve their very best.

"We are particularly pleased that inspectors recognised the major improvements in attendance, behaviour, achievement and the quality of education, as well as the strength of our support for vulnerable pupils."

He added: "While we are proud of how far we have come, we remain committed to building on this success and ensuring every young person at this school continues to benefit from the highest standards of education, support and opportunity."

The Ofsted report singled out attendance and behaviour, saying attendance is now in line with national figures and still improving.

Inspectors also praised the school’s inclusive culture and support for every pupil.

Cllr Annabel Wilkinson, North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for education, learning and skills, said: "This glowing inspection outcome for Bedale High School is a clear reflection of the hard work, commitment and ambition shown by the school's leadership team, staff, governors and pupils.
