BLAMIRES Place in Bradford will close to traffic for water works on October 8 and 9.

City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council has issued a notice under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

The closure will cover the section between the junctions with Great Horton Road and Back Great Horton Road South.

It will begin at 7pm on October 8 and end at 6am on October 9.

Although the order is intended to end then, the closure can remain in force for up to 18 months if necessary.

Vehicles used for the works will be allowed through.

Pedestrian access will be maintained at all times.

Drivers affected by the closure can use Blamires Place (unaffected section), Blamires Street, Hollybank Road and Great Horton Road, in that order, in either direction.

The notice was dated September 24 and gives the council’s City Hall, Bradford, BD1 1HY, address for Legal and Democratic Services in Bradford.
