Bishop Auckland Town Hall could close for an extra day each week under proposals by Durham County Council aimed at helping plug a multi-million-pound budget gap.

The council has revealed plans to reduce the town hall's opening hours by one day a week from 2027 as part of a package of savings measures.

The town hall is one of Bishop Auckland's key cultural venues, hosting live performances, exhibitions, cinema screenings and community events throughout the year and was subject to a £1.5m refurbishment in 2019 - it is currently open Monday to Saturday from 10am until 4pm.

Paul Darby, the council's corporate director of resources, said: “It is important to stress that, at this stage, what we are looking at are proposals, which are currently subject to public consultation, and that no decisions have been made.

“Like many local authorities across the country, we are facing significant financial pressures, with government funding expected to fall well short of the unavoidable spending increases we have to factor in."

The proposal comes as Durham County Council faces significant financial pressures, with an estimated budget deficit of £83.662 million until 2030, with four years of maximum council tax rises, job cuts and a greater reliance on artificial intelligence being mulled over by the local authority's decision makers ahead of next year's budget.

Plans under consideration include reducing office costs, increasing income, changing how some services are delivered and making greater use of technology and artificial intelligence.

An estimated 18 jobs could disappear, including nine customer service posts and nine management roles.

Officials say no decisions have yet been made, with residents being urged to have their say before the consultation closes on November 15.
