Members and guests at a County Durham golf club have raised thousands of pounds for a hospice.

Brancepeth Castle Golf Club donated £5,888 to St Cuthbert’s Hospice after raising the money through events held throughout the year.

Men’s captain Neil Tiplady presented the cheque.

He said: "This wonderful amount will go directly towards supporting the invaluable care they provide in the local community."

The club thanked everyone who bought raffle tickets, joined events or donated.

Mr Tiplady will take on the Scafell Pike Sunrise Challenge on October 11, to raise more money for the hospice.
