![The headline on the front page of the Financial Times reads: “Xi urges rivals to 'coexist in peace' as Trump hosts White House visit”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1056/cpsprodpb/3c73/live/4f263ef0-b85a-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

Many of the papers on Friday include photographs of Chinese President Xi Jinping's historic visit to the White House. The Financial Times leads on quotes from his address, where Xi said the US and China should "coexist in peace" and avoid being drawn into conflict.

![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: “Met chief: Stop putting offenders before victims”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/809/cpsprodpb/827c/live/525b7520-b861-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

US President Donald Trump and his wife Melania are pictured alongside Xi on the front page of the Times, standing on the White House balcony after a military show. The main story for the paper focuses on a sex offender who was repeatedly allowed to return to his home overlooking the playground he targeted. It leads on a statement from the Metropolitan Police commissioner, who said police officers felt "hamstrung" by a system that he said results in "seemingly unfathomable decisions". Sir Mark Rowley told reporters that "the children should be put first, not the paedophile".

![The headline on the front page of the Sun reads: “Fergie's squeaky bum time for royals”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/cc29/live/f7c40730-b85b-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

The Sun says Sarah Ferguson has received offers of up to seven figures for an autobiography, and is just "days away" from a book deal. The paper adds that the Royal Family are still reeling from the revelations in Earl Spencer's memoir about his sister, Princess Diana. The paper's front page also includes a nod to the upcoming fight between Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua. The headline reads "It's on at last".

![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: “Two-year delay in assessments of autism due to pressures on NHS”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/782/cpsprodpb/8e0f/live/efd5b190-b85b-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

People who believe they have ADHD or autism must wait two years before they can be assessed, according to the Guardian. The paper reports that the decision to delay assessments is because the NHS is struggling to meet the "surging demand", and cannot afford the cost of the number of assessments people are seeking. It adds that the NHS disclosure of this approach precedes a major government-commissioned review of the "colossal increases" in diagnoses of ADHD, autism and mental illness.

![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: “NHS corridor care killing 550 people every week”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/830/cpsprodpb/f15d/live/53c76560-b85a-11f1-a430-4d16ee157c41.jpg)

The NHS is also the focus for the i Paper, which reports that overcrowding in A&E has been linked to more than 500 deaths a week across the UK. It says that, on average, emergency rooms are operating at 175% capacity.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: “Fiennes kept in care home illegally”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/d197/live/4a9719e0-b85a-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

The Daily Telegraph carries a series of allegations regarding the care of celebrated explorer Sir Ranulph Fiennes, who has been open about his battle with Parkinson's disease.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mail reads: “Labour's army of migrants”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/810/cpsprodpb/ab66/live/11a67660-b85c-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

"Labour's army of migrants", reads the Daily Mail, which says that the number of small boat migrants who have reached the UK under the Labour government is "larger than the entire British Army". The paper says that 83,279 migrants have reached British shores since the last general election, according to figures quoted by Shadow Home Secretary Chris Philp.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Express reads: “Desperate to reach Britain”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/42a1/live/5b892cc0-b85a-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

A photograph of migrants in the English Channel leads the Daily Express under the headline: "Desperate to reach Britain".

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: “I was sexually assaulted by celeb at home”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/3639/live/3c28fd50-b85b-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

Former England striker Andy Carroll is pictured on the front page of the Mirror, after he revealed he was sexually assaulted by a man in 2021. The paper reports the incident took place at Carroll's home, with the unnamed attacker set to stay over after arriving to watch a boxing match.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: “Andy Carroll: I was abused by male celeb”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/5681/live/35b9cda0-b85b-11f1-a430-4d16ee157c41.jpg)

Carroll is also the leading story for the Star, which says the perpetrator assaulted the former footballer while he slept. His attacker was jailed for three years following the incident, the paper adds.

![The headline on the front page of the Metro reads: “Now pay through the nose”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/827/cpsprodpb/42d7/live/465af6d0-b85a-11f1-a430-4d16ee157c41.jpg)

The UK has launched its first retail facial recognition system in a London coffee shop, which the Metro claims "endangers civil liberties" and "risks unleashing fraud on a dangerous new level". The paper explains that when a customer goes to pay, the system, which is powered by online bank Revolut, checks their biometrics against those provided when they first signed up.
