Two donkeys, Class A drugs and counterfeit cash have been seized as part of a major police operation in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police said Operation Super Tornado 2 had brought officers to a site in Drax, near Selby.

The 24 hours of action during Operation Super Tornado Two on Wednesday (September 23) marked the "biggest operation in the force's history".




Police carried out an animal welfare warrant alongside the RSPCA, North Yorkshire Council and Trading Standards, where two donkeys were seized over concerns about unnecessary suffering.

The donkeys were found among other animals, and the site was also issued a number of animal welfare notices.

North Yorkshire Police said: “Officers and partners also identified suspected illegal puppy breeding, which is now subject to further investigation and enforcement activity.”

In Selby, officers executed a drugs warrant where a quantity of Class A drugs and counterfeit cash were found.

One person was arrested as part of this investigation, police confirmed.

Meanwhile, officers working with the DVSA filed 14 Traffic Offence Reports in Escrick – one of which involved a vehicle being seized for being driven with no tax and another seized for no insurance.

Inspector Rob Campbell said: “Operation Super Tornado Two gave us the opportunity to bring together our neighbourhood teams, specialist resources and partners to focus on the issues that matter to communities across Selby district.

“The activity shows the breadth of work taking place locally, from tackling drugs and road offences to animal welfare and speaking directly with residents.

“Partnership working is a huge part of what we do, and I’d like to thank colleagues from North Yorkshire Council, Trading Standards, the RSPCA and DVSA for their support throughout the operation.

“Although this was a dedicated day of action, the work doesn’t stop when the operation finishes. We’ll continue acting on information from our communities, targeting those causing harm and working with our partners to make Selby district a safer place.”

If you have information about crime or anti-social behaviour in your area, report it through the North Yorkshire Police website or call 101.

In an emergency, always call 999.
