ALLERTON Lane is set to close for street lighting works.

Bradford Council announced that the closure will affect the section from Old Road to Beck Bridge Lane.

The closure will take effect from October 5, 2026 at 8am and is scheduled to be lifted on October 23, 2026 at 6pm.

However, the council noted that the closure could be extended up to 18 months if necessary.

The restriction does not apply to vehicles involved in the works, and pedestrian access will remain open.

Alternative routes for affected vehicles include other sections of Allerton Lane, as well as Cote Lane, Allerton Road, Saffron Drive, Bell Dean Road, Thornton Road, School Green, and Old Road.

The council's legal and democratic services department issued the notice.
