A new store opened its doors in York this morning (Friday) – causing quite the kerfuffle.

People queued for hours to be first into the **Space NK** branch when it started trading on Coney Street at 10am.

Shoppers lined up along Coney Street, into St Helen’s Square, past the Mansion House and on through Lendal.

The personal care and beauty brand is very popular, but the crowds were lured by the opening offers.

Space NK was offering a beauty treat for the first 100 customers who bought something; a goody bag for the first 200 customer who spent £100 or more; and a “pick and mix” treat to those who spent £30 or more.

One of those in the queue was York St John University student Genie Hunt.

“People have been queuing since 8am,” she told ***YorkMix***.

“Me and my friend actually got here around 9-ish, and we’ve been waiting in the queue for about an hour and a half so far.

“We’re all just looking forward to free stuff. Everyone loves free stuff!”
