WEST Yorkshire rail passengers are facing two weekends of disruption due to major improvement works on the East Coast Main Line.

Network Rail has announced that essential upgrades will take place on October 24 and 25 and again on October 31 and November 1.

The work will affect services between Doncaster or Leeds and Darlington, with buses replacing trains during both weekends.

Gunnar Lindahl, joint operations director for Network Rail and LNER, said: "Engineers will be carrying out a major programme of improvements across North Yorkshire to make the East Coast Main Line more reliable for passengers and freight customers.

"We encourage anyone planning to travel over the two weekends to check their journey in advance with National Rail or their train operator."

The programme includes renewing and replacing points at Tollerton North, Colton Junction, and Northallerton, as well as upgrading track, drainage, and overhead line equipment.

Additional work will take place between York and Leeds from October 24 to November 1 as part of the Transpennine Route Upgrade.

A spokesperson for the train operating companies said: "We’re working closely with Network Rail and industry partners to help customers travel during these important railway improvements."

Passengers are advised to plan ahead and check for the latest travel updates.
