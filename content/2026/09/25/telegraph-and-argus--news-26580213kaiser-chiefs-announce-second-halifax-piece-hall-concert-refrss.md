KAISER Chiefs have announced a second headline show at Halifax’s Piece Hall due to high demand.

The Leeds band will now perform on both Friday, June 4, and Saturday, June 5, as part of the Live at the Piece Hall 2027 concert series.

They will be joined on both nights by special guests White Lies.

A spokesperson for the Piece Hall said: "Due to demand Yorkshire’s very own Kaiser Chiefs have announced a second headline show at The Piece Hall, Halifax, next summer.

"A huge rush for pre-sale tickets for the band’s original date on Friday, June 4 has led to Leeds indie heroes adding an additional show at the historic venue on Saturday, June 5."

Remaining tickets for the Friday show as well tickets for the new Saturday show go on general sale at 10am today via ticketmaster.co.uk.

Club 1779 members of The Piece Hall can already access pre-sale tickets for the extra date.

The concerts mark a return to the Halifax venue for the Kaiser Chiefs, who last played two sold-out shows there in 2021.

The band, formed in 2000, are known for hits including Oh My God, I Predict A Riot, Ruby, and Never Miss A Beat.

They have released eight original studio albums and sold eight million albums.
