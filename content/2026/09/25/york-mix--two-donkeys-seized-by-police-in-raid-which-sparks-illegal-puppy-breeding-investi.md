Two donkeys were seized by police during a raid in North Yorkshire.

It took place at an undisclosed location in Drax.

Officers executed the animal welfare warrant alongside the RSPCA and North Yorkshire Council on Wednesday.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A number of animals were found at the location, with animal welfare notices issued and two donkeys seized over concerns about unnecessary suffering.

“Officers and partners also identified suspected illegal puppy breeding, which is now subject to further investigation and enforcement activity.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/puppies-kennels-drax-nyp.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/police-rspca-drax-sept-2026-nyp.webp)

It was part of the county-wide police day of action called Operation Super Tornado 2.

In Selby, officers executed a drugs warrant where a quantity of Class A drugs and counterfeit cash were recovered. One person was arrested as part of the investigation.

And a road traffic operation in Escrick, also involving the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency, DVSA, resulted in 14 traffic offence reports.

One vehicle was seized for no tax and another being seized for no insurance.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/police-seize-car-escrick-nyp.webp)

Inspector Rob Campbell said: “Operation Super Tornado Two gave us the opportunity to bring together our neighbourhood teams, specialist resources and partners to focus on the issues that matter to communities across Selby district.

“The activity shows the breadth of work taking place locally, from tackling drugs and road offences to animal welfare and speaking directly with residents.”
