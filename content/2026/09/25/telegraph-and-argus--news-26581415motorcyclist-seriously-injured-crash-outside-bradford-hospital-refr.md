A motorcyclist was seriously injured in a crash near Bradford Royal Infirmary late last night.

The 45-year-old rider was seriously injured in a collision involving a silver Seat Leon and a red Yamaha R6 in Smith Lane, outside the hospital's Gate 6 entrance.

Emergency services were called to the scene at 10.16pm on September 24.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service confirmed a number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene. The motorcyclist suffered critical injuries and was taken to hospital, where his condition was later described as stable.

West Yorkshire Police are investigating the circumstances of the collision and are appealing for witnesses.

Officers are particularly keen to speak to anyone who saw the crash or the moments leading up to it, especially motorists who may have captured dashcam footage.

## Statements in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for witnesses following a road traffic collision in Bradford in which a motorcyclist was seriously injured.

"At 10.16pm last, officers were called to the incident involving a silver Seat Leon and a red Yamaha R6 motorbike in Smith Lane, outside the Gate 6 entrance to the Bradford Royal Infirmary site.

"The rider of the motorbike, a 45-year-old man, was critically injured and was taken to hospital for emergency treatment where his condition was described as stable.

"Officers are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the collision or the circumstances leading up to it, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam footage."

Anyone with information is asked to contact officers via online live chat quoting reference 13260545846.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 10.20pm last night (24 September) to report a road traffic collision on Smith Lane in Bradford.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including an ambulance and a rapid response vehicle, and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
