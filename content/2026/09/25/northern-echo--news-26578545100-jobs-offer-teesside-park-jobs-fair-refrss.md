More than 100 jobs will be up for grabs at a North East shopping centre next week when retail and hospitality employers join forces for a recruitment event.

Teesside Park will host its annual Jobs Fair on Tuesday, September 29, offering full-time, part-time and seasonal roles.

Jobseekers can meet retail and hospitality recruiters in person and get free careers advice.

Catherine Furlong, centre director at Teesside Park, said: "As one of the area’s biggest employers, we see first-hand the difference that access to the right opportunities can make.

"Our annual Jobs Fair is a great way to bring local people and businesses together, giving people the chance to find out more about the roles available and have those important first conversations.

"With employers, training providers and practical support all in one place, there’s something for people at every stage, whether they know exactly what they want to do next or are still exploring their options.

"We’d encourage anyone looking for their next opportunity to come along and see what’s available."

Employers taking part include M&S, Nando’s, Boots, Mango, LUSH, The Works, Schuh and Teesside Park.

The Stockton Employment and Training Hub, DWP, Atomix Adult Learning, Middlesbrough College and Stockton Riverside College will also offer advice on training and further education.

The Jobs Fair runs from 1pm to 7pm, with some retailers recruiting in-store and others based at the centre’s ‘Hello’ building.

Jobseekers should bring copies of their CV.

Students can travel free to Teesside Park from 1pm on Stagecoach and Arriva buses by showing valid student ID.
