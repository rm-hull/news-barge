THE A59 at West Marton, west of Skipton, remains closed after an HGV overturned just after midnight.

Skipton and Ripon fire crews responded following reports at 12.20am of someone trapped inside the vehicle on the A59 close to the crossroads in the centre of the village.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: “On arrival of crew was found to be an overturned vehicle. Crews used reciprocator saw and small tools to rescue the casualty and make the scene safe.”

The road is still closed while the vehicle is being recovered. There are no reports of any injuries, say police.
