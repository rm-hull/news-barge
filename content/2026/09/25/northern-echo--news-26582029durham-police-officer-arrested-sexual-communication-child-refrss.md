A serving Durham Police officer has been arrested on suspicion of sexual communication with a child.

The 31-year-old man, who has worked for the force for less than two years, was taken into custody in the early hours of this morning (September 25).

Durham Police confirmed he was arrested on suspicion of both sexual communication with a child and misconduct in a public office.

The officer has been immediately suspended while a criminal investigation is carried out.

The Northern Echo understands that he was arrested while off duty and taken into police custody in a different force area.
