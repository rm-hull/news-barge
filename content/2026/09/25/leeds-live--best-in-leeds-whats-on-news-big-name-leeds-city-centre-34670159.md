# Big name Leeds city centre closure as heartfelt message issued

## We seen some other notable closures in Leeds

Leeds has seen a big name closure in the city centre.

Notices have gone up in the now vacant unit of 4Sight Eyecare. It was based at St Johns Centre.

A notice in the window reads: “We would like to take this opportunity to express our sincere thanks and apologise for any inconvenience to all of our customers and patients that have been loyal to 4Sight over 40 years.”

Elsewhere in Leeds city centre, we have brought you news of two other notable closures. We have seen Gant shut at Victoria Leeds and a Pound Bakery branch close.
