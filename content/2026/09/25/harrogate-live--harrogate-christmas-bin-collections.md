Christmas Day and New Year's Day 2026 both fall on a Friday, a Harrogate collection day. What changed last year, tip closures and when new dates land.

North Yorkshire Council has not yet published its 2026 Christmas bin dates. What we
can say now is that **two Friday collections in the Harrogate area will have to move**,
because Christmas Day and New Year’s Day both fall on a Friday this year.

Since **21 April 2025** every rubbish and recycling round in the Harrogate area has run
**Tuesday to Friday**, according to the council’s
news release on the change.
That keeps Monday bank holidays clear. It does not help when the holiday lands on a
Friday.

## The quick answer

**Christmas Day is Friday 25 December 2026 and New Year’s Day is Friday 1 January
2027**, and both are normal collection days in the Harrogate area.** Last year the council
gave no street-by-street table for household bins**: it told residents to check the
online bin collection calendar,
which carries the revised dates for each address. **The tips close on Christmas Day,
Boxing Day and New Year’s Day**, and on every Wednesday as usual.

## The 2026 calendar, and why Fridays are the problem

| Date | Day | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25 December 2026 | Friday | Christmas Day, a Harrogate-area collection day |
| 26 December 2026 | Saturday | Boxing Day |
| 28 December 2026 | Monday | Substitute bank holiday for Boxing Day |
| 1 January 2027 | Friday | New Year’s Day, a Harrogate-area collection day |

National Rail’s Christmas travel summary confirms the Boxing Day bank holiday moves to Monday 28 December this year. Because Harrogate rounds never run on a Monday, that substitute day should not disturb your bins. The two Fridays will.

We are not going to guess where the Friday collections go. The council decides that, and in past years it has moved some collections earlier and some later, including onto a Saturday.

## What the council did last Christmas

For Christmas 2025 the council’s
Christmas and New Year services page
said only that there were “some changes to our waste and recycling collections over the
Christmas and New Year holidays”, asked residents to have bins out by **6am**, and
pointed to the bin calendar for details. It published full revised tables for
**commercial** customers only. (The live page now returns an access-denied error between
seasons, so the link above is the archived copy.)

The year before, under the old Monday to Friday rounds, it did publish a Harrogate table.
The
December 2024 version
moved Christmas Day collections to Friday 27 December, Boxing Day collections to
Saturday 28 December, and Friday 27 December collections to Monday 30 December, with the
knock-on running into the New Year. The rounds have changed since, so none of those
dates carry over. What carries over is the habit: **expect Saturday working, and expect
the knock-on to run into the first week of January.**

## Businesses: last year’s Harrogate commercial table

If you pay for a North Yorkshire commercial waste collection, the 2025 Harrogate-area changes were the clearest guide to how the council handles a holiday:

*Monday 22 December collections were brought forward to**Saturday 20 December**
*Tuesday 23 moved to Monday 22, Wednesday 24 to Tuesday 23, and Christmas Day to**Wednesday 24 December**
*Boxing Day collections moved to**Saturday 27 December**
* New Year’s Day moved to Friday 2 January, and Friday 2 January to Saturday 3 January

That is from the same archived council page. The 2026 table has not been published.

## The tips over Christmas

The three household waste recycling centres serving the Harrogate area are Harrogate on Wetherby Road (HG3 1DE), West Harrogate on Penny Pot Lane (HG3 2SN) and Ripon on Dallamires Lane (HG4 1TT). All three keep the same rules:

* **Open 8.30am to 4pm** from October to March.
* **Closed every Wednesday**, and on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year’s Day.
* You must register your vehicle first. Registration became compulsory on 27 April 2026.

Put those rules on to this year’s calendar and the fortnight looks like this:

| Date | Tips |
| --- | --- |
| Wednesday 23 December | Closed (Wednesday) |
| Thursday 24 December | Open, 8.30am to 4pm |
| Friday 25 December | Closed (Christmas Day) |
| Saturday 26 December | Closed (Boxing Day) |
| Sunday 27 December | Open, 8.30am to 4pm |
| Monday 28 December | Not stated by the council (substitute bank holiday) |
| Wednesday 30 December | Closed (Wednesday) |
| Friday 1 January | Closed (New Year’s Day) |

The council’s pages name the three holidays, not the substitute Monday, so check before a Monday 28 December tip run.

There is no fixed tip at Knaresborough, Boroughbridge or Pateley Bridge. The council’s
mobile recycling centre
makes its last pre-Christmas stops at **Pateley Bridge (Bewerley Park) on Saturday 5
December**and** Boroughbridge (Back Lane car park) on Saturday 12 December**, both 9am to
1pm.

## Christmas trees

The council takes Christmas trees **free at every household waste recycling centre**,
according to its Christmas trees page:

* **Real trees** go in the garden waste skip. They are shredded and composted.
* **Artificial trees** go in the reuse container if still in good condition, the small electrical cage if they light up, the scrap metal skip if made of metal, or the waste-for-energy skip otherwise.

If you pay for garden waste collection, you can chop the tree up and put it in your
garden waste bin, but it will not be emptied until **March, when the service restarts**.
There is no winter garden waste round.
