A couple will bring fresh produce, homemade bakes and deli goods to a North Yorkshire town centre when their family-run shop opens next month.

Alice Norton and her husband plan to open a greengrocer, deli and bakehouse at 133 High Street, in Northallerton, in the former Nick Mitchinson Greengrocer unit between Argos and Lakeland.

The shop is due to open in mid-October, selling fresh fruit and vegetables, locally sourced products and homemade baked goods.

Announcing the venture on social media, Alice said: "Myself and my husband will be opening our new shop between Argos and Lakeland on the high street in October.

"Living in Northallerton ourselves, we are very excited for this opportunity and can't wait to meet you all."

The couple want to support local suppliers while offering residents high-quality produce and freshly made food.

The in-house menu will include Victoria sponge cakes, brownies, quiches, sausage rolls and Bakewell tarts.

The shop will combine a traditional greengrocer and deli, allowing shoppers to buy fresh ingredients and handmade treats under one roof.

The shop will bring the former Nick Mitchinson Greengrocer premises back into use on Northallerton High Street.

Residents have been quick to show their support.
