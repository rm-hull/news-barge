Consett Empire Theatre’s café could close and cinema screenings could be reduced under proposals being considered by Durham County Council.

The plans form part of a package of savings proposals currently out for public consultation ahead of the council setting its next budget in February 2027.

Proposals suggest the council could close the theatre café while reviewing cinema activities at the historic venue.

Paul Darby, Durham County Council’s corporate director of resources, said: “The savings we are suggesting include a proposal to close the cafe at the Empire Theatre in Consett in recognition of private sector cafe operators in the area, and to review cinema activities.

“The aim of these proposals is to reduce opening hours to align mainly with live performances and focus income generation on ticket and bar sales. This would deliver a saving through reduced staffing costs from 2027.

“We are actively looking at a range of options for the future of the cafe, including opportunities to lease out the facility to an interested business or organisation.”

The Empire Theatre is currently open from 11am to 3pm from Wednesday to Saturday, with extended opening for film screenings and live shows.

The 500-seat venue, which dates back to 1913, hosts a programme of touring theatre productions, music concerts, comedy, dance performances and children's shows.

Cinema screenings are currently scheduled around live events.

It was closed for seven months in 2024 while essential roof repairs were carried out, costing £470,000 and affecting the venue’s income.

A consultation is open until November 15, with residents invited to give their views on the proposals and the local authority stressed that no final decision has been made.
