A THEARTRE performance that mixes the life of Charlotte Bronte with a silent disco will be held at Keighley Library on Saturday.

Senseless Trash' is a 30-minute theatre show using silent disco headphones.

It explores the modern day struggles of identity and motherhood through the lens of Charlotte Brontë’s inspiring life and work.

Visitors are invited to join the literary pioneer on a “phonic field trip over the Yorkshire Moors and listen in to the sounds that shaped the Brontë sisters’ lives.”

The howling winds at Top Withens, tranquil trickling of nearby waterfalls and the angelic tones of… Beyoncé will accompany the crowd on their journey.

It is suitable for audiences aged 14+ and some walking/movement is required - the show moves throughout the library.

Three performances available. 11am, 12.30pm and 2.00pm. They are free but booking essential.
