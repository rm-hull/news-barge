Police have issued an update after a York school was placed in lockdown after a report of a threat made to pupils.

As ***YorkMix*** reported last night, officers went to St Peter’s School on Clifton yesterday.

It followed a report from the school that a threat was made to children who had struck up a conversation with a woman believed to be camping or living rough near to the school grounds.

As a safety measure all pupils were kept inside the school while officers carried out enquiries.

A search failed to locate the woman and the lockdown was lifted.

Police patrols were stepped up in the area to provide reassurance to the local community.

This afternoon (Friday) North Yorkshire Police issued this update: “Officers investigating a report of a threat made to children in the playground of St Peter’s School in York on 24 September, have carried out a thorough and expeditious investigation into the report.

“Following extensive enquiries including speaking with the children who made the report, school staff, CCTV enquiries and local searches, officers are now satisfied that there was no threat to the children and the investigation has now been closed.

“Our thanks go to the school for their support with the enquiries and for taking the right safety precautions when the initial concern was raised.”
