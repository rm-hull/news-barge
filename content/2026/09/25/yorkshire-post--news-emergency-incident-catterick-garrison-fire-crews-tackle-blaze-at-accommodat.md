# Catterick Garrison: Fire crews tackle blaze at accommodation block as injury update released

## Fire crews in North Yorkshire tackled a blaze at Catterick Garrison on Thursday night (Sep 24).

Fire appliances from Richmond, Reeth and Bedale responded to a report from on-site personnel of a fire within an accommodation block at the garrison.

On arrival, with the building found to be well alight, additional resources from Leyburn, Northallerton, Masham and an aerial ladder from Darlington were also sent to assist.

After the fire was brought under control, the last fire resources left the scene in the early hours of Friday morning at 2:43am, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

On-site personnel kept a watching brief overnight.

Fire investigation work is due to take place later on Friday morning.

One person was taken to hospital with smoke inhalation, the fire service said.
