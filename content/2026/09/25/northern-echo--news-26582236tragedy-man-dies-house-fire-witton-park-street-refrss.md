A man has died after being pulled from a house blaze in Witton Park.

Police and paramedics were called to a terraced home on Park Road on Friday (September 25) after a fire broke out just after 8am.

Firefighters entered the building after reports a man remained inside, with an air ambulance also landing at the scene nearby.

The man, in his 40s, was brought out of the house but, despite the best efforts of emergency crews, was sadly pronounced dead at the scene.

![]()Police at the scene of a fire on Park Road in Witton Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Durham Police said early indications suggest that the fire appears to have started in the kitchen.

The force said that although there is no evidence at this stage to indicate anything suspicious, a joint police and fire investigation is underway to determine the precise cause of the fire.

Police officers remain on the scene while forensic investigations continue.

Pictures revealed the devastating aftermath of the fire, which has scorched the outside of the home above its window and door.

![]()Police at the scene of a fire on Park Road in Witton Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

An officer was also seen standing at the back of the house, where pictures show the blackened window frame and door and fire-damaged brickwork.
