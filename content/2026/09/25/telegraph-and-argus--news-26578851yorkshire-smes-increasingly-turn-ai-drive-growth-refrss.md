ARTIFICAL intelligence is being hailed as a key driver of business growth.

A recent report shows that 60 per cent of SMEs in Yorkshire and the North East believe AI is important to their longer-term growth ambitions.

The research, conducted by BGF and Beauhurst, highlights the growing optimism around AI, with 84 per cent of businesses becoming more trusting of the technology in the past year.

Chris Boyes, partner and head of BGF in Yorkshire, said: "The debate is largely finished about whether AI matters strategically, but a gap remains between ambition and integration.

"Many businesses are held back by organisational and cultural issues."

Despite the enthusiasm, just 68 per cent of SMEs have integrated AI into some business functions, and only 68 per cent have a responsible AI policy.

Key barriers to adoption include security concerns (48 per cent), data quality (32 per cent), and employee adoption and change management (28 per cent).
