A YORK employment agency has gone into voluntary liquidation owing more than £31,000.

VNPro Ltd of Tower Court, Oakdale Road, York, was incorporated in January 2025 and went into voluntary liquidation on August 27.

The website of Companies House lists Stephen Quinn of Durham as sole director.

Bespoke Insolvency Solutions of Bolt were appointed liquidator.

A liquidator’s report known as a Statement of Affairs has reported the financial situation.

It said the company had no assets and no preferential creditors – those who are paid first.

However, HMRC was owed £15,132 as a secondary preference creditor – those who are paid next.

In addition, were five trade and expense creditors owed £16,637 between them.

This gave a total estimated deficiency or shortfall of £31,769.

With issued and called up capital of £1, this meant an estimated £31,770 was owed overall.
