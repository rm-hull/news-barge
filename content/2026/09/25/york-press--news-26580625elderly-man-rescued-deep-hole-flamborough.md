A MAN has been fallen into a deep hole in a Yorkshire coastal town.

Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called at 10.02am yesterday (Thursday, September 24) after the elderly man fell near a footpath in South Sea Road, Flamborough.

A service spokesperson said: "The man was rescued by crews using manual handling.

"He was left in the care of paramedics."
