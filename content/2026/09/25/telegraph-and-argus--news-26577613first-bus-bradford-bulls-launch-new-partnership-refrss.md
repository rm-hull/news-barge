A NEW partnership is helping wheelchair users travel with greater confidence.

First Bus has teamed up with Bradford Bulls to deliver mobility training and promote safer, more independent journeys on Bradford’s bus network.

A recent session, held near Bartercard Odsal Stadium, was part of the bus operator’s Safer Independent Travel Training programme and included manual and electric wheelchair users, as well as mobility scooter users.

The training covered how to safely board and leave a bus, the support available from drivers, and how to obtain a Mobility Scooter Pass.

Mubs Mahmed, social value manager at First Bus, said: "Our drivers receive full training in how to support customers with mobility needs but these sessions let everyone see travelling on the bus from both sides.

"It can support people with mobility needs to access employment, education, healthcare, leisure and other vital services, while also helping to reduce social isolation and transport barriers."

The event is part of a wider season-long partnership to promote public transport accessibility.

Ian Stafford, commercial manager at Bradford Bulls, said: "We must give a special shout out to the drivers, who were absolutely fantastic.

"I learnt so much from them and the wheelchairs users, which has given our team real insight. We will use that in supporting people with mobility needs."
