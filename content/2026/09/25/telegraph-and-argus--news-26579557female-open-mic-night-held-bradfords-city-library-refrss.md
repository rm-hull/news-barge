A FEMALE only open mic night will be held in Bradford’s City Library tonight (Friday), and will be followed by a festival next month.

Sisterverse is a “space to share poetry, connect and meet like minded creatives”, and will run an open mic night from 7-9pm in the City Park library.

It offers poetry, words, songs, and also welcomes people who just want to listen. Refreshments are provided.

For more information and to book a space visit [https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/sisterverse-open-mic--tickets-1990626830436](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/sisterverse-open-mic--tickets-1990626830436)

The event will be followed next month by a Sisterverse Festival, which will be held at the library on October 18 from noon until 6pm.

The event promises “autumnal, ancestral and witchy energy.”
