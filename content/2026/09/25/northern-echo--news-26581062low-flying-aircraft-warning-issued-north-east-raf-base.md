Residents near a North East RAF base have been warned to expect low-flying aircraft today.

RAF Boulmer warned that low-flying aircraft would operate near its main site between 11.30am and 12.30pm on Friday, (September 25).

In a statement, the base said: "Please be aware there will be low-flying aircraft in the vicinity of RAF Boulmer main site between 11.30am and 12.30pm."

While RAF Boulmer is based away from the village itself, the noise may be noticeable across a wider area.

RAF Boulmer provides continuous surveillance of UK airspace and tactical control of combat and support aircraft, with around 1,000 service personnel and civilians based there.
