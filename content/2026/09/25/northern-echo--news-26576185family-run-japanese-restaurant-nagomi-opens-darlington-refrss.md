A family-run Japanese restaurant has opened in Darlington, serving handcrafted sushi, steaming ramen and food shaped by more than 25 years of experience.

Nagomi Darlington recently welcomed its first customers at 67 Gladstone Street, where owner David Chan hopes to introduce people to the flavours and ingredients that have inspired him throughout his career.

For David, the restaurant is the culmination of 26 years spent working in Japanese restaurants across the North East, learning new techniques, refining his skills and developing an expertise in Japanese cuisine.

![]()Nagomi owner David Chan Picture: SARAH CALDECOTT *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

![]()Nagomi, Darlington *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Now, he is hoping to share that experience with food lovers in Darlington.

Sitting in the restaurant just days after opening, David's enthusiasm is clear as he talks about sushi, ingredients and the challenge of bringing something different to the town.

He said: "We want people to taste authentic Japanese food.

![]()Nagomi, Darlington *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

![]()Nagomi, Darlington *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"We have worked in Japanese restaurants for many years and now we want people to experience the quality and flavour for themselves."

Although the menu includes ramen, Japanese small plates and a range of traditional dishes, sushi is the star of the show.

David said while many sushi restaurants focus on the sharp acidity of vinegar, his approach is different, creating a more delicate balance that allows the natural flavour of the rice to shine through.

![]()Nagomi, Darlington *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

He said: "Our sushi tastes different.

"Sometimes sushi can be too sour because of the vinegar. We use less vinegar and bring out more sweetness from the rice. It gives a better balance and lets people enjoy the ingredients."

Many of the products used at Nagomi are sourced directly from Japan, including the rice, helping to create a dining experience David believes stays true to the country's culture.

![]()Nagomi, Darlington *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

He said: "We use products from Japan because we want it to be authentic.

"For us, it's important that people can enjoy proper Japanese food."

The restaurant serves chicken and pork dishes, caters for halal customers and offers traditional Japanese sake that David says is unavailable elsewhere in the UK.

David said he saw an opportunity in Darlington after the sushi scene in Newcastle became increasingly crowded.

He said: "Newcastle has a lot of sushi restaurants already, so I wanted a new challenge.

![]()Nagomi, Darlington *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"Darlington is a lovely town with lovely people."

"I wanted to bring something different here and give people the chance to try quality Japanese food in a calm, relaxed setting."

There are also plans to make use of the building's outdoor terrace, offering diners the chance to enjoy their food and drinks while overlooking the nearby park.

Despite opening only recently, the restaurant has already given David an encouraging response from customers.

He added: "We put a lot of care into the food.

"I am passionate about bringing delicious food packed with traditional flavours to Darlington and am excited for customers to try our food."

Nagomi Darlington is open from noon until 9.30pm Tuesday to Sunday and is closed on Mondays.
