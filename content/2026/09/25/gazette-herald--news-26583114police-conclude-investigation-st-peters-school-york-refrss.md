A POLICE investigation which began with the lockdown of a York school following reports of a threat made to children has now concluded.

North Yorkshire Police has been out on patrol around St Peter’s School, in Clifton, today (September 25) after the school was placed in lockdown on Thursday (September 24).




A force spokesperson said: “Officers investigating a report of a threat made to children in the playground of St Peter’s School in York on September 24, have carried out a thorough and expeditious investigation into the report.”

“Following extensive enquiries including speaking with the children who made the report, school staff, CCTV enquiries and local searches, officers are now satisfied that there was no threat to the children and the investigation has now been closed.

**“** Our thanks go to the school for their support with the enquiries and for taking the right safety precautions when the initial concern was raised.”

Following the lockdown, a spokesperson for St Peter’s said: “We took immediate action in line with our established safeguarding and emergency procedures.

“The safety and wellbeing of our pupils and staff is always our highest priority.

“Following appropriate checks and advice from the police, the lockdown was lifted, and the school returned to normal operations.”
