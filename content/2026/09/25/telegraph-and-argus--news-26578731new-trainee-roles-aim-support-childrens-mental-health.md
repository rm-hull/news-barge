TRAINEE educational mental health practitioner roles are now being offered across West Yorkshire.

The positions, based within Mental Health Support Teams (MHSTs), combine paid work with a fully funded postgraduate diploma and focus on improving children’s emotional wellbeing in educational settings.

Sonya Robertshaw, Workforce Project Lead at Leeds and York Partnership NHS Foundation Trust, said: "Trainee Educational Mental Health Practitioners play a vital role in supporting the mental health and wellbeing of children and young people in schools and colleges across West Yorkshire.

"The programme combines learning with practical experience, giving trainees the opportunity to support children and young people when they need it most while building a rewarding career."

Trainees will provide one-to-one and group support using cognitive behavioural therapy and other evidence-based methods, helping pupils manage issues like anxiety and low mood.

The roles are full-time, one-year fixed-term posts at NHS Agenda for Change (AfC) Band 4, with successful trainees progressing to Band 5 upon qualification.

Ms Robertshaw said: "We want our workforce to reflect the diverse communities of West Yorkshire and strongly encourage applications from men, people from ethnically diverse backgrounds, and individuals with a range of life experiences and perspectives."
