A market showcasing some of the region’s best independent traders is set to return to a North Yorkshire town, giving shoppers the chance to browse artisan food, crafts and seasonal products.

The Little Bird Made Artisan Market will take place in Richmond’s historic Market Place on Sunday, September 27.

Running from 10am to 3pm, the event will feature a curated selection of makers and producers from across North Yorkshire.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "We always receive such a warm welcome when we bring our community of artisan traders to Richmond, and we can’t believe we are already on our second to last visit of the year.

![]()w *(Image: Supplied)*

"As the seasons shift, our market comes alive with amazing autumn products and crafts, as well as a fantastic energy.

"With only two visits left for 2026, we encourage everyone to come down, enjoy the atmosphere, and support the incredible talent we have right here in North Yorkshire."

Set against the backdrop of Richmond Castle and the town’s historic architecture, the market is known for its seasonal crafts, unique gifts and local delicacies.

Visitors will also have the chance to meet the artisans behind the products.

Tara Marshall, owner of Angel Skin Products, said: "To me, Richmond is the perfect market town, with its beautiful cobbled streets, wonderful people and that lovely community feel.

"The people who come to the market and follow Little Bird Made are looking for something a little different, handmade, unique and created with love.

"I think that’s what makes the artisan market so special.

"The atmosphere, the creativity and the opportunity to discover something you won’t find on the high street."

She said: "As a trader, I absolutely love coming to Richmond.

"There’s such a fantastic buzz and market vibe, and when the sun is shining, people are walking around with smiles on their faces, stopping for a chat, enjoying some good food and supporting small independent businesses.

"It really makes trading here something special."

For more information or to apply for a stall, visit www.littlebirdmade.com.
