Police are appealing for witnesses after an e-bike was reported stolen from a driveway in York.

The theft took place on Monday (September 21) at around 9.30am on Queenswood Grove, where a Swifty AT656 e-bike in black and red was taken by two male youths.

The suspects are believed to be between 14 and 16 years old and were wearing black or grey sports clothing with grey trainers.




A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who has CCTV, doorbell camera, or dashcam footage that may have captured the suspects in the locality at the time or have seen the e-bike since it was stolen."

Anyone with information is urged to contact PC 1864 Marshall by emailing [Fiona.Marshall@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Fiona.Marshall@northyorkshire.police.uk) or through the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting 'Tell us about' and then 'An existing case or report.'

The force is also reminding residents to ensure all property is secure.

The spokesperson said: "Residents are encouraged to ensure vehicles, homes, garages/sheds are locked and secure and check that home security measures such as lights and CCTV are in working order."

The crime reference number is 12260185486.
