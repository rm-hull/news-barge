An independent estate agency launched by two staff is celebrating two years of growth after selling and letting hundreds of North East properties and raising thousands for charity.

Middlesbrough-based Joseph Harris Estate Agents was founded by Theo Alali and Adeel Khaliq to combine traditional estate agency with digital marketing and social media.

The five-strong agency has sold and let properties across Teesside and raised more than £2,500 for Teesside Hospice.

Mr Alali said: "When we first opened, nobody really knew who Joseph Harris was.

![]()The Joseph Harris Estate Agents team outside their Middlesbrough office. From left, James Archer, Hashim Din, Theo Alali, Mia Brennan and Adeel Khaliq *(Image: Supplied)*

"We had to build the name from scratch and convince people to give us an opportunity.

"The first few months were difficult and there were definitely times when Adeel and I questioned whether we had done the right thing.

"But people started to buy into us, our experience and what we were trying to do.

"Once we started getting results and recommendations, things really began to grow from there.

![]()The Joseph Harris Estate Agents team at their Middlesbrough office. From leftAdeel Khaliq, Theo Alali, Mia Brennan, James Archer and Hashim Din *(Image: Supplied)*

"Looking at where we started compared to where we are now, I am really proud of how far we have come."

The founders brought years of property experience to the business.

Mr Khaliq has more than 20 years’ estate agency experience, while Mr Alali has more than a decade in property investment and development.

Mr Khaliq said: "Starting from scratch was always going to be a challenge, particularly in an industry where there are so many established names.

![]()The Joseph Harris Estate Agents team after completing the Teesside Hospice 10K. From left,James Archer, Hashim Din, Theo Alali, Mia Brennan and Adeel Khaliq *(Image: Supplied)*

"To go from just Theo and me in the office to the team we have today has been great to see.

"We have worked hard to build the business and earn people’s trust, and we are excited about where we can take it from here."

Joseph Harris stands out by combining traditional property portals with social media and bespoke video walkthroughs.

One property video has topped 200,000 views, while the business has clocked up more than one million across its social media channels.

Mr Alali said: "We have always believed that putting a property online and waiting for the phone to ring is not enough.

"We still use all of the traditional property portals, but social media gives us another way of getting our clients’ properties in front of a much bigger audience.

"Some of our videos have received more than 200,000 views.

"That is exposure a property might never have received through the traditional platforms alone."

He said the agency’s growing reputation is his proudest achievement.

Mr Alali said: "Seeing more than 100 five-star reviews, recommendations and people coming back to use us again shows that we are doing something right.

"There are estate agents in Teesside that have been around for a very long time, so to establish ourselves and build the reputation we have in just two years is something we are really proud of."

The team has also made supporting the community a priority.

In their second year, they sponsored Teesside Hospice and ran the Middlesbrough 10K, raising more than £2,500 for the charity.

Mr Alali said: "Teesside Hospice is a fantastic local charity which provides an essential service to people and families across the area, so we were really proud to support them.

"Not everyone in the team enjoys running, so there was quite a bit of training involved before the 10K.

"James then went one step further and took on the Great North Run as well, which was a brilliant achievement.

"Finishing the Middlesbrough 10K together and knowing we had raised more than £2,500 was a really proud moment for all of us.

"It also made us realise how far we have come together as a team."

Mr Alali said the business would keep supporting the community as it grows.

He said: "People trusted a new business with their properties, recommended us to friends and family and supported us from the beginning.

"It is only right that we try to give something back."
