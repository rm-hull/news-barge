## Police to continue patrols around top York school following lockdown

North Yorkshire Police officers will be making additional patrols in and around the site of St Peter's School in York today after the school was placed in lockdown on Thursday (September 24) *(Image: Newsquest)*

PUPILS at a York private school have been told to expect an increased police presence around the site after the school was placed in a lockdown yesterday.

North Yorkshire Police said there will be additional patrols taking place in and around the site of St Peter’s School, in Clifton, today (Friday, September 25).

It comes after the school was locked down as a “precautionary measure” on Thursday (September 24) following reports that a threat was made to children by a woman believed to be camping or living rough close to school grounds.




A spokesperson for St Peter’s said reports of the threat were “at no point” confirmed yesterday, but the lockdown was implemented as a precaution and to reassure pupils, staff and parents that appropriate steps were being taken to ensure their safety.

They added: “We took immediate action in line with our established safeguarding and emergency procedures.

“The safety and wellbeing of our pupils and staff is always our highest priority.

“Following appropriate checks and advice from the police, the lockdown was lifted, and the school returned to normal operations.”

## 'Enquiries remain ongoing'

North Yorkshire Police has since launched an appeal to trace the woman in connection with the reports.

A force spokesperson said: “An extensive search was carried out [on Thursday, September 24] however the woman was no longer in the immediate area, and officers were satisfied that the precautionary measures could be lifted.

“Enquiries remain ongoing to trace the person involved.

“Anyone with information that could assist the investigation is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference NYP-24092026-0238.”

![]()An "extensive" search was made by police around the school to locate the woman in connection with the reports *(Image: Submitted)*

St Peter’s thanked its pupils and staff for the “calm, sensible and mature manner” in which they had responded to the incident.

They added: “We appreciate that any mention of a lockdown may cause concern.

“Please be reassured that these procedures are designed to be precautionary, allowing us to respond quickly and appropriately whenever there is any potential concern, however remote.

“You may notice an increased police presence in and around the school over the coming days.

“This is a precautionary measure and should provide reassurance that appropriate steps are being taken to ensure the continued safety of our community.”
