Pupils from Masham CE Primary School have paid a special visit to a lifelong resident to celebrate her 100th birthday.

Children and staff visited Jean this week, singing Happy Birthday to mark the milestone.

Jean, who has lived in Masham all her life, was delighted by the surprise, with pupils from across the school joining in the celebration.

Headteacher Jane Wood said:

> “We were delighted to help celebrate Jean's incredible 100th birthday.
>
> "As someone who has spent her whole life in Masham, she is a treasured part of our community.
>
> “The children thoroughly enjoyed sharing this special occasion with her and helping to make her birthday even more memorable.
>
> "Experiences like this embody our vision of Belonging, Growing, Making a Difference and demonstrate our values of Compassion, Courage, Respect and Resilience in action.”

The visit was part of the school’s work to strengthen connections with the local community and help pupils learn about caring for others.

Children are also looking forward to taking part in Young Voices in Sheffield, with the whole school set to join the musical event.

Meanwhile, families considering a place at Masham Church of England Primary School are being invited to two upcoming open mornings.

These will take place on Saturday 10th October and Thursday 12th November, both from 10am until 12pm.

Visitors will be able to meet staff and pupils and explore the school, including its recently launched library and sensory area.

*For more information or to arrange a visit, contact the school office at [admin@masham.ycst.co.uk.](mailto:admin@masham.ycst.co.uk.)*
