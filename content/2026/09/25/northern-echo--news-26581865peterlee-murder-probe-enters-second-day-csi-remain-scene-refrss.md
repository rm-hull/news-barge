A murder probe has entered its second day after a man in his 50s was stabbed to death in County Durham.

The man died in hospital on Wednesday (September 23) having been found with stab wounds at an address on Thames Road in Peterlee.

Three people, a 73-year-old woman and two men, aged 53 and 77, have been arrested on suspicion of murder.

![]()Police and CSI remain on Thames Road, Peterlee *(Image: Northern Echo)*

Police and crime scene investigators remain at the scene today.

A police welfare vehicle was seen arriving this morning (Friday, September 25).

![]()A welfare vehicle arrived at the scene this morning (Friday, September 25) *(Image: Northern Echo)*

Residents previously said they were "shocked" to see police on what is usually a "peaceful" street.

One said: “It’s a fairly peaceful place to live, but we all keep ourselves to ourselves. I didn’t see anything happening so I’m not sure what’s gone on.”
