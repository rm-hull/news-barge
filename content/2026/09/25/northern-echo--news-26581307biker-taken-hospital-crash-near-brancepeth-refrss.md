A biker has been taken to hospital after a crash near Brancepeth.

Police and paramedics were called to Stockley Lane, just north of Willington, on Thursday (September 24) after a crash between a motorbike and a car.

An air ambulance was called to the scene just after 6.30pm, with the helicopter landing close to the road.

Paramedics assessed and treated the biker who was then taken to hospital by road. Their condition remains unknown.

A Great North Air Ambulance Service spokesperson said: “Our critical care team was activated at 6.40pm to reports of a road traffic collision near Willington.

“We had a paramedic and doctor on board our aircraft and they arrived on scene at 6.47pm.
