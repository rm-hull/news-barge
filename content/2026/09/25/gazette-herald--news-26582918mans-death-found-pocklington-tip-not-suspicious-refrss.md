POLICE have issued an update after the body of a man was found at a Yorkshire tip.

Humberside Police said the death of a man, whose body was discovered at the Pocklington Household Waste Recycling Centre in Pocklington Lane, Burnby, was “not being treated as suspicious”.

It comes after officers were in attendance and a scene guard was in place at the site after the man was found at around 3.15pm on Wednesday (September 23).




A spokesperson for Humberside Police said: “On arrival, officers found a man was found deceased."

![]()The recycling centre reopened this morning *(Image: East Riding of Yorkshire Council)*

They added: “Enquires have since confirmed that there is no evidence of third-party involvement, and the death is not being treated as suspicious.

“A file will be prepared for the coroner.”

East Riding of Yorkshire Council announced the centre had been reopened to the public at 10.05am on Friday (September 25).
