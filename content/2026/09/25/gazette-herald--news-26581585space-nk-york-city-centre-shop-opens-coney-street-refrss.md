**HUNDREDS of people lined up in York this morning after a much-loved shop returned to the city centre.**

Queues stretched down Coney Street and into Lendal this morning (September 25) as popular cosmetic retailer Space NK returned to the city centre with a bigger and better shop.

Opening its doors in 3 Coney Street, a Grade II-listed property that was previously home to JD Sports, the shop has undergone a major transformation with a new floor, wall and ceiling finishes, lighting, ventilation and a ground floor platform lift installed.

Spread over two floors, the shop will bring a range of make-up products, skincare, haircare, and a large range of perfume to the city - offering brands that were not otherwise available in York.

There are also a number of new releases for Space NK stocked in the York location including Beyonce’s new haircare range Cécred, which was only launched in the UK yesterday.

![]()A section of the shop *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

There is also a 20 per cent off offer currently in place across the shop for the opening and the first 200 people to spend at least £100 will be rewarded with an 'exclusive goody bag'.

The beauty retailer also gave the first 100 customers who bought something a beauty treat as part of the opening celebrations and anyone parting with £30 or more will receive a 'Pick & Mix treat'.

![]()The services station *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Space NK has more than 80 stores across the UK and Ireland, including **Leeds**, Harrogate and Beverley and will be returning to the city after a four year hiatus.

The brand, which was founded in 1991, previously operated in Davygate from 2015 until 2022, where TAG Heuer watch shop is now located.
