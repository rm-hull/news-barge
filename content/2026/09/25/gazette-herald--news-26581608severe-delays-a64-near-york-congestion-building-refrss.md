Severe delays are affecting the A64 westbound with motorists facing increasing journey times near York this morning.

The AA reported delays of 12 minutes at 9.08am on Friday morning, with average speeds reduced to 15mph between the A64 and Towthorpe Moor Lane. Congestion is extending as far as Barton Hill.

The AA then stated there was an average speed of 25mph just after 10am on the stretch of road.

The A64 is a key route for people travelling from York to the coast, including Scarborough.

The delays follow water main work on the A64, where temporary traffic signals are in place in both directions between North Lane and the A1237.

The work and signals were first reported on the morning of Tuesday, September 22.

The AA warned that delays were increasing, with westbound traffic particularly affected between the A64 and Towthorpe Moor Lane.
