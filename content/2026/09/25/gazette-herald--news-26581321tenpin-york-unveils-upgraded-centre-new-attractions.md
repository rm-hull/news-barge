A newly refurbished entertainment centre on the edge of York is welcoming visitors to try its upgraded attractions.

Tenpin York, located at Clifton Moor Centre, now offers a wider range of activities following a recent transformation.

The refreshed venue features 24 upgraded bowling lanes, down from the previous 28 to make room for new experiences.

Marc Couget, regional manager at Tenpin, said: "We’re incredibly excited to share these new experiences with our customers.

"With the addition of brand-new karaoke rooms, interactive darts and a soft play area, we’re proud to be one of York’s leading indoor entertainment venues.

"We’re not just about bowling lanes at Tenpin, our guests can now enjoy a wide range of activities all under one roof, making it the perfect place for family days out.

"We can't wait to welcome both new and returning visitors from York and the surrounding area, including communities such as Pocklington, Ripon and beyond, to experience everything our newly transformed centre has to offer."

Among the new additions are two private karaoke rooms, two interactive dartboards with 11 immersive games, and a soft play area for younger children.

Existing attractions remain, including Houdini’s escape rooms, which challenge groups to solve puzzles under pressure.

The venue also continues to offer its laser tag arena, marketed as a 4D interactive experience for all ages.

Other options include American pool tables and a large arcade area featuring both classic and contemporary games.

Visitors can also enjoy cocktails in the upgraded dining area, while sports fans have access to live broadcasts on TNT and Sky Sports throughout the centre.

Tenpin Ltd operates 60 entertainment centres across the UK and was founded in 2003.

The company manages more than 1,400 bowling lanes nationwide and promotes itself as a destination for competitive socialising.

Mr Couget said: "We’re not just about bowling lanes at Tenpin, our guests can now enjoy a wide range of activities all under one roof, making it the perfect place for family days out.

"We can't wait to welcome both new and returning visitors from York and the surrounding area, including communities such as Pocklington, Ripon and beyond, to experience everything our newly transformed centre has to offer."

Tenpin describes its venues as offering "fun bowling vibes with a twist, day or night," and aims to provide entertainment options for both families and groups of friends.
