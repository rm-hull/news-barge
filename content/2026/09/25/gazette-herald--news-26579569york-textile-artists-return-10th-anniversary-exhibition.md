A FRESH collection of textile art will go on display in a city this autumn.

York Textile Artists will host their annual exhibition, Fragments of Imagination, at York Cemetery Chapel on October 10 and 11 from 10am to 4pm.

The show will feature new works from the group’s 13 members, exploring a range of techniques including embroidery, felting, eco-printing, batik, screen-printing and weaving.

Fran Brammer, chairperson of York Textile Artists, said: "We began when a few textile artists showing in 2017's York Open Studios got together for a coffee and a chat.

"We so enjoyed exchanging ideas and sharing experiences that we started meeting and began to show our textile art together as York Textile Artists."

The group, now in its 10th year of exhibiting, has shown work across North Yorkshire, from The Station at Richmond to Inspired by… gallery at Danby.

The exhibition will include demonstrations, a pop-up shop, and refreshments from Divine Coffee Roasters.

Visitors will have the opportunity to meet the artists, learn about their creative processes, and purchase original pieces, prints and other textile works.

Admission is free and the event is open to all.

York Cemetery Chapel is located on Cemetery Road, York, YO10 5AJ.
