# Yorkshire singer told to 'die' while busking in Leeds city centre reveals how she deals with abuse

## Charlotte Branson says she loves the city and the abusive woman was 'off her head'

A Yorkshire singer told to "die" while busking in Leeds has an uplifting "keep calm" message for fellow performers.

Charlotte Branson, from Chapeltown, in Sheffield, has been busking for a few years, “on and off”. While she busks a few times each month, the 25-year-old also sings and DJs in bars and clubs and works part-time as a sales assistant at a bridal shop.

Charlotte, who performs house music as ‘Charli B’ when she’s behind the decks, has varied her musical output as she’s also active on various social media platforms. Music runs in her blood as her grandad Michael Brothers was a professional opera singer. Her dad is a lorry driver and mother is a seamstress, she says they’re “massively supportive” of her singing career.

Charlotte says busking gives her “more confidence” and it’s great for “putting yourself out there”. She says summer is the best time of year to busk, and she can earn anywhere between £20 and £200 any given day.

Charlotte said: “I was terrified at the beginning but now, not so much. You still have a bit of nerves, but you get used to it. You’re seen in public, you never know what you might deal with.”

Expanding on things she’s had to deal with, Charlotte added: “I had some woman tell me to die but that says more about them than me. Some of the people can be on drugs or alcohol so they sometimes can behave in a certain way, so I’ve had to deal with that a few times, but it just is what it is, harmless. It’s just being drunk and saying stupid stuff.”

The singer said she’d been threatened by the woman on Briggate, in Leeds city centre, and thinks she was “off her head”. Charlotte won’t let it mar her love of the city, however, as otherwise, she’s had “nothing but love” in Leeds. She added that when she does receive abuse while performing, she feels it’s important to carry on.

### 'Keep calm'

Charlotte said: “It’s my time to do what I need to do and I’m not going to stop doing it for the people who have different opinions to what I do. If people do give you grief, just try not to retaliate, just keep calm and just keep doing your thing. You can’t do much more than that.”

Charlotte performs mainly soul, pop and R&B. While the young busker writes her own songs, she only performs covers when she takes to the streets. Her favourites include ‘Let it Go’ by James Bay and the Sam Smith version of ‘How Will I Know’.

Charlotte gave her advice to anyone else considering busking, she said: “Post what you’re doing on TikTok and social media. Don’t be afraid, have your wits about you and enjoy the journey. It’s a really great way to build your confidence. I think if you can sing in the street, you can sing anywhere.”
