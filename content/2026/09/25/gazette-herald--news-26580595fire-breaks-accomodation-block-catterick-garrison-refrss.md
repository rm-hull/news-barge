ONE person has been taken to hospital after a fire broke out at an accommodation block in a North Yorkshire town.

The county's fire and rescue service was called to Catterick Garrison at 7.48pm yesterday (Thursday, September 24).




A service spokesperson said crews from Richmond, Reeth, and Bedale responded, with additional support from Leyburn, Northallerton, Masham, and an aerial ladder from Darlington.

The spokesman added: "On arrival, the building was found to be well alight.

"After the fire was brought under control, the last of our resources left the scene at 2.43am.

On-site personnel will keep a watching brief overnight, with additional fire investigation due to take place later this morning.

"One person was taken to hospital with smoke inhalation."
