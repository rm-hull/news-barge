The death of a man whose body was found at a waste tip in Pocklington is not being treated as suspicious, police said today.

The Pocklington Household Waste Recycling Site was cordoned off by officers on Wednesday.

It followed the discovery of the body at about 3.15pm.

In an update on Friday Humberside Police said: “On arrival, officers found a man was found deceased.

They added: “Enquiries have since confirmed that there is no evidence of third-party involvement, and the death is not being treated as suspicious.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/police-pocklington-waste-site-24-sept-2026-1.webp)

“A file will be prepared for the coroner.”

The tip reopened to the public at 10am this morning.
