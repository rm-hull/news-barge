Three men have been charged after police uncovered what is believed to be the UK's second-largest single seizure of ketamine at a property in a West Yorkshire village.

Officers recovered around 500kg of the Class B drug, with an estimated street value of £10 million, during a raid at an address on Rose Avenue in Upton, near Pontefract.

The operation was led by the Yorkshire and Humber Regional Organised Crime Unit and resulted in the arrest of three men on Wednesday evening, September 23.

![]()Officers found 20 black binbags of ketamine at the scene. *(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

Two of those charged are from Upton.

## What have they been charged with?

Curtis Binns, 32, of Sunny Avenue, has been charged with possession of a Class B drug with intent to supply and money laundering.

Paul Turner, 40, of Common Lane, has been charged with possession of a Class B drug with intent to supply.

Christopher Wills, 42, from Wolverhampton, has been charged with supplying a Class B drug.

![]()Officers found 20 black binbags of Ketamine at the scene. *(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

All three men have been remanded in custody and are due to appear before Leeds Magistrates' Court today.

## Police statement

In a statement, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said:

"Three men have been charged with drugs offences following the seizure of ketamine worth an estimated £10 million in a West Yorkshire village.

"In what is believed to be the UK's second largest single seizure of the drug, officers recovered approximately 500kg of ketamine at an address in Rose Avenue, Upton, Pontefract, in an operation led by officers from the Yorkshire and Humber Regional Organised Crime Unit."
