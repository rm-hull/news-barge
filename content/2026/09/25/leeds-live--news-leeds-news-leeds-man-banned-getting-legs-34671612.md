# Leeds man banned from 'getting his legs out' continued showing off grim sight

## James Karmen is not allowed to show people wounds on his legs

A homeless Leeds man banned from showing his legs has been jailed after repeatedly lifting his trousers to show students his gruesome wounds. Leeds Crown Court heard on Friday, September 25, that James Karmen, 39, had been suffering with ulcers on his legs after years of taking crack cocaine and heroin.

Prosecutor Jonathan Foy said he had previously been made the subject of a criminal behaviour order banning him from showing his legs in a public place. He was also banned from visiting the city centre, with a Probation Service worker said made it difficult for him to access help as he was "arrested on the spot" when trying to visit probation, housing agencies and drug support.

Mr Foy told the court: "The particular predicament this defendant has been revealed to me. It would appear in the face of it, the Crown does have the power to vary the order out down by the Magistrates' court.

"The criminal behaviour order was imposed in 2015 and amended in 2021. The particular provision relevant to this case is that the defendant must not expose the wounds to his legs to any person in a public place." The court heard Karmen breached that order on September 9. Mr Foy said: "Just before 1pm in Woodhouse Lane in Leeds he was seen talking to a student and observed to be showing his legs and eventually asking for money before leaving.

"I should say that the defendant appears to be known by university staff as someone who is persistent in engaging. On the 24th of August, CCTV again shows him approaching a person and lifting his trousers to show them his legs."

As well as the breaches, Karmen fell to be sentenced for a burglary and fraud by false representation after he went into a student accommodation in Leeds and took a member of security staff's bank card and used it.

Speaking of the breaches, Mr Foy told the court: "The breaches aren't very serious but they are persistent and clearly irritating and sometimes upsetting for the students. I'm not sure I can submit it is very serious harm caused."

The Probation Service worker said: "He tells me he has been homeless for 25 years, after leaving home at 15 because of domestic violence. He has taken crack cocaine and heroin as a lot of homeless people do. He has abstained from heroin and crack cocaine and has been on methadone."

Recorder Withington jailed Karmen for 14 months.
