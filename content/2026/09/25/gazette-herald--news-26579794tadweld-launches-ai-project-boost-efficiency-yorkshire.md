A steelwork manufacturer has launched an AI project to boost efficiency and drive innovation.

Tadweld, based in Tadcaster, is the first manufacturer in North Yorkshire to begin an artificial intelligence project through the Made Smarter Yorkshire and Humber programme.

The scheme supports small and medium-sized manufacturers in adopting digital technologies.

The project will focus on practical applications of AI that could improve efficiency, support employees, and strengthen the business as it grows.

Chris Houston, managing director at Tadweld, said: "This is an exciting milestone for Tadweld and a strong reflection of the culture we are building across the business.

"There is often a misconception that long-standing manufacturers and family-run businesses aren't willing to move with the times, but at Tadweld, innovation is one of our three core values.

"It means continually looking for better ways to work, supporting our people and strengthening the service we provide to our customers.

"We are proud to be the first manufacturer in North Yorkshire to begin this type of support through the programme, and we hope our experience will encourage other manufacturers to explore what AI could mean for their own businesses."

Tadweld’s AI project is being delivered with support from Orah AI Consultancy.

Omar Haider, founder of Orah AI Consultancy, said: "Tadweld already knew where the friction was in the business.

"Our job is to give their team the skills to build the fix themselves, so the capability stays in the business long after the project ends.

"People across operations, health and safety and sales are already building their own tools, and that is the outcome that matters."

Tadweld designs, manufactures, and installs steelwork solutions for sectors including food and drink, construction, pharmaceuticals, renewables, and oil and gas.

The company’s involvement in the AI project aligns with its broader strategy of investing in people, processes, and technology to support sustainable growth.

Mike Pennington, business relationship manager for York and North Yorkshire Made Smarter, said: "Tadweld is a fantastic example of a forward-thinking manufacturer taking a practical and considered approach to digital transformation.

"As the first business in North Yorkshire to commence AI Intensive Technical Support through Made Smarter Yorkshire & Humber, the company is helping to demonstrate how emerging technology can be translated into meaningful improvements on the factory floor and across wider business operations."

Through the Made Smarter programme, Tadweld aims to gain the expertise, confidence, and support needed to adopt the right digital technologies for its future development.

Mr Houston said: "Manufacturing has always evolved through innovation.

"AI represents another important step in that journey, and we want Tadweld to approach it with the same focus on people, quality and practical outcomes that we bring to every other part of the business.

"It offers genuine first-mover advantage, so it’s great to be leading the way for the region."
