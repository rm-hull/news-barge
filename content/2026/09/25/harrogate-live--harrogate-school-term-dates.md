Harrogate half term is 26 to 30 October 2026 and Christmas starts 21 December, but Ashville breaks 11 December. Council dates plus six schools' own dates.

October half term in Harrogate, Knaresborough and Ripon runs from **Monday 26 October to
Friday 30 October 2026**at council-run schools, and the Christmas holiday from** Monday 21
December 2026 to Friday 1 January 2027**. Most of the big secondaries follow those weeks,
but several add their own training days, and the independent schools break up much earlier.

## The quick answer

* **October half term: Monday 26 to Friday 30 October 2026**, with a council training day on Friday 23 October, so most children finish on** Thursday 22 October**.
* **Christmas: last day Friday 18 December; back Tuesday 5 January 2027** (Monday 4 January is a training day at council schools).
* **Summer 2027: most pupils finish Friday 23 July.** The council’s term officially ends on Monday 26 July, but that day is a training day.

## North Yorkshire Council dates for 2026/27

These are the dates North Yorkshire Council publishes for community and voluntary controlled schools. Academies, voluntary aided and foundation schools set their own, so check with your child’s school.

| Period | Dates |
| --- | --- |
| Autumn term starts | Monday 7 September 2026 |
| Training day | Friday 23 October 2026 |
| October half term | Monday 26 to Friday 30 October 2026 |
| Christmas holiday | Monday 21 December 2026 to Friday 1 January 2027 |
| Spring term starts | Monday 4 January 2027 (training day) |
| February half term | Monday 8 to Friday 12 February 2027 |
| Easter holiday | Monday 22 March to Friday 2 April 2027 |
| Summer term starts | Monday 5 April 2027 |
| Bank holidays | Monday 3 May and Monday 31 May 2027 |
| May half term | Tuesday 1 to Friday 4 June 2027 |
| Term ends | Monday 26 July 2027 (training day) |

The council’s 2026/27 calendar counts 195 term days. Up to five are training days when pupils stay at home: the council sets three (23 October, 4 January and 26 July) and each school picks the other two and tells parents itself. So your child’s school may have two more days off than the table shows.

## Six local schools that publish their own dates

We checked each school’s own page on 25 September 2026. The half terms mostly match the council’s weeks. The training days do not.

The differences that catch parents out:

* **Harrogate Grammar School** is the one big secondary back on**Monday 4 January**, a day earlier than the council schools. Its staff development days are 7 and 8 September,** Friday 2 October**,** Friday 27 November**, Friday 2 July and Monday 26 July.
* **St Aidan’s** has two Friday training days this half term,**9 October and 23 October**, as well as 4 January and 26 July.
* **Harrogate High School** stays closed to students on**Monday 2 November**, a trust training day, so its pupils return on Tuesday 3 November. It is also closed on 4 January and 26 July.
* **Ripon Grammar School** training days are 7 September, 4 January,**Friday 9 April** and**Friday 16 July 2027**. Its 2026/27 dates also list boarding arrivals and departures.
* **Ashville College** has a two-week October break starting on Tuesday 20 October, finishes for Christmas a week before everyone else on**Friday 11 December**, and breaks up for the summer on** Wednesday 7 July 2027**.

## The rest of the year at a glance

February and May half terms line up across almost every school above: **8 to 12 February**
and **1 to 4 June 2027** (Ashville breaks from Friday 28 May and returns Monday 7 June;
Harrogate Grammar and St Aidan’s show the May break as 29 May to 6 June). Easter runs from
**Monday 22 March to Friday 2 April 2027**, with pupils back on** Monday 5 April**, except
at Ashville, which returns on Wednesday 7 April.

The council has also published 2027/28: the autumn term starts on **Monday 6 September 2027**,
October half term is **25 to 29 October 2027**, and Christmas runs from** 20 December 2027 to
3 January 2028**.

## Primary schools and academies not listed here

Most primaries in Harrogate, Knaresborough and Ripon that are still council schools follow the council table. Academy primaries usually do too but add their own two training days, which they publish on their websites and in letters home. If your school is not in the table above, the school’s own term dates page is the one to trust.
