**UPDATE: The man has since been found.Humberside Police has thanked those who helped officers in the appeal.**

Humberside Police are appealing for help to find a missing man who may be in a "distressed state".

The man, from Snaith, was last seen at around 11.45am on Friday (September 25).




Officers are concerned for his safety and have asked members of the public to help them locate him.

Anyone who sees him is urged not to approach him, but to call 101 quoting log 140 of 25 September.
