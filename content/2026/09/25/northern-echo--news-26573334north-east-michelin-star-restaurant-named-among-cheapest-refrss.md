A “wonderful” restaurant with “exceptional” food has been named one of the best Michelin-starred restaurants that is on the more affordable side.

You do not have to spend hundreds of pounds to enjoy a Michelin-starred meal with many restaurants across the UK offering cheaper menu options.

The Michelin Guide has revealed the more affordable options including one spot in the north east.

House of Tides in Newcastle offers a three-course dinner that costs £65 per person.

## What is House of Tides like?

On the restaurant, the Michelin Guide says: “Kenny Atkinson is big news in Newcastle, providing the city with MICHELIN-Starred dining at both House of Tides and his chef's-table operation SOLSTICE.

“The former is the home of his most affordable cooking, with a prix fixe that provides a more concise taste of his cuisine than the usual tasting menu.

“Expect to find quality ingredients and detailed execution in each of the dishes.”

House of Tides was opened in 2014 by Kenny and Abbie Atkinson in an old merchant’s town house.

The restaurant features “exposed beams, a big open fireplace as well as views over the River Tyne".

Alongside its Michelin star that it gained in 2015, the restaurant has a range of other awards including four AA Rosettes and being named as one of Square Meals’ top 100 restaurants out of London in 2023.

The fixed price menu includes a range of dishes such as sourdough loaf with butter to start as well as lemon sole and Creedy Carver duck.

You can also add on a selection of cheeses for an extra £17.

The meal finishes with a sweet pineapple parfait with coconut, woodruff, and sesame.

### **What do reviews say about House of Tides?**

House of Tides has a rating of 4.4 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with 1,571 people leaving a review after their visit.

One person said: “A wonderful evening at House of Tides.

“The food was fabulous, the beetroot ball with a liquid centre was amazing - the kind of food you only get at a restaurant with a great chef.

“The whole eating experience is supported by the excellent staff, who welcome you from the minute you walk in the door, and make the evening such a special occasion.”
