A MAJOR new exhibition on the history of video games will open in Bradford next year.

The exhibition at the National Science and Media Museum will trace how the rapid growth of gaming technology over the last century has shaped the ways we play, create and connect with each other, with its impact now felt far beyond the games themselves.

The new came as part of an announcement of upcoming events at the Bradford city centre museum.

Opening in late-March, the interactive exhibition will explore the history, impact and cultural significance of video games, “revealing how the universal human desire to play has evolved from board games to the expansive digital worlds we can experience today.”

Visitors will be invited to take a journey through the story of video gaming past, present and future, examining how gaming shapes society, identity, communities, technology and how we learn, uncovering the surprising ways that video games impact our everyday lives.

A spokesperson for the museum said: “From video games superfans to newcomers, there is something for everyone to enjoy.

“Visitors can explore how different generations have experienced play and journey through the story of iconic gaming memorabilia and moments that changed history.

“Designed for families and curious minds as well as gamers, the exhibition will encourage visitors to discover together through interactive activities, creative challenges and hands-on experiences.”

The exhibition also shines a spotlight on the growth of the video games industry, revealing a sector that is both global and growing right on our doorstep in the North of England.

Alongside the exhibition the Museum has announced the return of the Yorkshire Games Festival, which will run from May 29 for a packed half term of family friendly gaming activities across the museum.

The popular event invites visitors of all ages to engage with the latest gaming technologies and will include Big Screen Gaming, live shows, workshops, film screenings and gameplay.

The festival’s industry-focused Game Talks strand will return earlier in the spring, taking place in March.

Game Talks brings together aspiring future game makers with leading global voices in the gaming industry. Past events have featured creators of games including Star Wars Jedi: Survivor, Alan Wake 2, Baldur's Gate 3 and Thank Goodness You're Here.

The 2027 programme will feature talks, workshops, masterclasses and opportunities to network across the event, helping attendees make that crucial first step towards a career in games.
