A sex offender who tried to persuade a ‘young girl’ to send naked pictures has been jailed just weeks after escaping prison for similar offences.

Ameer Ali was handed a suspended sentence in April and warned to stay out of trouble.

By June, the 26-year-old was targeting children again when a decoy posing as a young girl caught him online, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Ali encouraged the decoy to send sexually explicit images and naked videos.

Ali also sent images and videos of himself to the person he believed was the young girl.

The court heard that Ali used an unregistered alias on Snapchat.

Ali, of Trimdon Avenue, Middlesbrough, admitted five offences: trying to incite a child under 13 to engage in sexual activity, trying to sexually communicate with a child, breaching a sexual harm prevention order (SHPO), attempting to breach an SHPO and failing to comply with notification rules.

His barrister, Jonathan Gittins, said Ali was isolated and lonely when he committed the offences.

He said: “Since being in custody, he has received the best mental health care he has ever had. He appears to be benefiting from it by understanding himself and his offending.

“He accepts that it is wholly wrong and abhorrent.”

Judge Anthony Dunne jailed Ali for four years and six months.

He said: “You communicated with that 12-year-old girl, as you thought, and throughout that communication you repeatedly demanded that they take naked photographs of themselves and send it to you.

“You did, it seems, stop that communication after three days when your online account disappeared.”

Ali was ordered to sign on the sex offenders’ register for life and given a new ten-year sexual harm prevention order.
