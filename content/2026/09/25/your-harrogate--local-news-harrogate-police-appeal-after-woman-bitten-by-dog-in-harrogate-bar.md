Police are appealing to identify a couple after a woman was bitten by a dog at a Harrogate bar.

The incident happened at Husk on Station Square at 4:24pm on Sunday 9th August.

The woman and her partner had just entered the bar with their golden retriever when a terrier-type dog belonging to a couple already seated inside reportedly attacked their dog.

The woman was then bitten as she tried to separate the dogs, suffering an injury to her knee.

North Yorkshire Police have released an image of a couple they would like to identify as part of their enquiries.

*Anyone who can help is asked to email [peter.henderson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:peter.henderson@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101 and ask for PC1668 Henderson.*

*Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*

*Please quote reference 12260155379 when providing information.*
