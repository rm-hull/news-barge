AN event will be held this weekend to bringing the community together to understand what Bradford needs to thrive in the future.

Bradford People’s Property Portfolio will be teaming with the Friends of Bradford Resource Centre to hold a “Resources for Democracy” workshop on Saturday September 26.

It will be held at the Kirkgate Community Centre in Shipley between 2pm and 5pm, and followed by a social between 7.30pm and 10pm.

The social will feature music from Melosa Jazz, DJs, a film screening of an award winning film about BRC, food from Miski and a bar provided by The Triangle.

The events draw on over 40 years of Bradford Resource Centre (BRC) serving the people of Bradford.

In November 2025, PPP became the owners of 17-21 Chapel Street where BRC was based.

Speaking about the events, PPP Director Harry Jelley said: “This is a coming together of an organisation that has been vital to community power in the past in Friends of Bradford Resource Centre, and People’s Property Portfolio which has the ambition to build positive change in the future through generating community wealth and creating spaces in which communities can thrive. We would love to hear from the people of Bradford to help shape our vision for that future.”
