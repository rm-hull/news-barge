Police issued eight parking fines during a crackdown on "obstructive and inconsiderate" parking in a Bradford neighbourhood.

Officers from the Bradford East Neighbourhood Policing Team carried out targeted patrols following community complaints made at a recent community meeting.

Eight Fixed Penalty Notices, each carrying a £30 fine, were issued to drivers whose vehicles were causing obstructions on Park Side Road, Springwood Gardens, Park Way and Woodroyd Terrace in BD5.

## What police said

West Yorkshire Police said the operation was launched in response to residents' concerns about "obstructive and inconsiderate" parking.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "This action was undertaken in response to concerns raised by local residents and forms part of our ongoing commitment to tackling issues that matter most to the community.

Police thanked residents who attended the meeting and reported concerns, adding that information from the public helps officers identify problem areas and improve safety for road users and pedestrians.

The spokesperson added: "Please park responsibly and considerately, ensuring roads, footpaths and access routes remain clear for residents, pedestrians and emergency services."
