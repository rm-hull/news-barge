A serving police officer faces trial accused of careless driving in a police van crash that left a woman injured.

PC Daniel Ward was at the wheel of a Cleveland Police van which crashed with a car on Bishopton Road West, Stockton, on Sunday, October 12 last year.

The van came to rest on a grass verge at the junction with Castle Close, while the VW hit a lamppost. The car’s driver suffered a shoulder laceration, and was one of two casualties taken by ambulance to North Tees Hospital in Stockton.

A number of emergency vehicles attended, and the road was closed.

The 40-year-old officer, from Middlesbrough, was charged with causing serious injury by driving without due care and attention.

He appeared at Durham Crown Court on Wednesday (September 23) and denied the charge.

Prosecutor Timothy Jacobs said he and defence counsel Laura Marshall expected the trial to last five days as crash experts may be called to give evidence.

A trial was fixed with a start date on August 14, 2028.

Judge Nathan Adams bailed the officer to return for that hearing and told him: “You have pleaded not guilty to this matter, and it’s now timetabled through to trial.

“I’m afraid it goes to the back of a very long queue.”

Cleveland Police confirmed PC Ward remains a serving officer.
