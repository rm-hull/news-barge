EIGHT new apprentices have joined a growing team.

Leeds-based G&H has welcomed the recruits as part of its ongoing commitment to developing talent in the mechanical, electrical and public health engineering sectors.

The newcomers will take part in a programme lasting between 18 months and five years, combining on-the-job training with classroom learning and working towards a professional qualification.

Paul Churchill, development manager at G&H, said: "There is more than one route into a successful career, as Andy Burnham has highlighted with a renewed focus on technical education.

"From the beginning, apprenticeships enable talented people to enter the construction industry, gain recognisable qualifications, and develop valuable, hands-on experience."

This year’s apprentices will train through a mix of site-based and office roles while completing studies at either Leeds College of Building or Heart of Yorkshire.

Among the new starters is Dan Brook, a 27-year-old electrical apprentice who previously spent seven years working in IT.

Mr Brook said: "Since starting, I’ve really enjoyed putting my training into practice and learning from experienced people.

"For me, the apprenticeship is about more than gaining a qualification; it’s about developing my skills and building a career for the future."
