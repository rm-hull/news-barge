# Barnsley robber jailed after stabbing shop worker more than 20 times

## A Barnsley man has been sentenced after stabbing a shop worker more than 20 times during a robbery in which he stole around £50.

Alex Hill, 27, of Balk Lane, Barnsley, carried out the attack in May 2026 after entering a shop armed with a knife.

He stabbed the shop worker more than 20 times before stealing approximately £50 and fleeing the scene. The victim survived his injuries.

Police launched an immediate manhunt to locate Hill and released a CCTV image of him to the public.

Following the appeal, an officer located Hill as he was on his way to hand himself in at a police station.

![Alex Hill](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/25/16/37/alex-hill.png?trim=50,0,51,0&width=640&quality=65)


Hill appeared at Sheffield Crown Court on Tuesday (Jun 9), where he pleaded guilty to wounding with intent, robbery and possession of a bladed article.

He returned to the same court on Friday (Sep 18), where he was handed an eight-year sentence, reduced to six years in prison and three years on licence.

Detective Constable Chris Shirt, of Barnsley CID, who led the investigation, said: "This was a horrific incident and a frenzied, prolonged attack during which a man could have potentially lost his life.

"This was clearly a calculated attack with Hill entering the shop with a knife in his possession.

"Once his image was circulated publicly he had no choice but to hand himself in.

"There is no place for a violent attack such as this in Barnsley. I would like to thank the public for their support during our investigation."
