A MAJOR employer has pledged to back a £20 million investment scheme.

Barrett Steel will represent the business voice on the Pride in Place Board, supporting the 10-year programme aimed at delivering long-term economic and social improvement.

The Bradford South MP, Judith Cummins, welcomed the company's involvement and said its input would help ensure the scheme delivers lasting benefits in the Holme Wood community.

Ms Cummins said: "I am delighted that Barrett Steel has agreed to represent local business on the Pride in Place Board.

"I look forward to working with Barrett Steel, the Pride in Place Board and other local employers to make sure this investment delivers for the people of Holme Wood."

The government-backed scheme will see £2 million invested annually over a decade.

Guy Barrett, chief executive of Barrett Steel, said: "We are pleased to support the Pride in Place programme and to represent the voice of local business on the board.

"We look forward to working with Judith, the Pride in Place Board and partners across Holme Wood to help ensure this investment delivers lasting benefits for the community and the wider Bradford area."

The partnership aims to strengthen links between employers, schools and the community.
