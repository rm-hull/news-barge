A pretty Yorkshire Dales town is set to be the backdrop to Tesco’s Christmas advert for 2026.

Hawes, which can be found in Upper Wensleydale, will be making an appearance in the brand’s new Christmas advert this year.

An X user posted a short clip of the behind-the-scenes of the Christmas advert, with a Tesco delivery van driving through the town with the shops and walls covered in bright Christmas decorations and a little bit of fake snow on the pavements.

## What can I do in Hawes?

Hawes has been described as a great place for those wanting an “immersive Dales base with scenery, walking and Wensleydale character right around them”.

Whether you are taking a walk through the area and just need a quick place to stop or want to extend your stay in Hawes, the town has something for everyone.

  Hawes, North Yorkshire as it looks when snow really covers the ground 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

There are plenty of walks on AllTrails that you can follow that go through the town or start from there.

Many of the walks will take you to the nearby Hardraw Force Waterfall, which is the highest single drop waterfall in England.


In the town, you can visit the Wensleydale Creamery, which is the home of Yorkshire Wensleydale cheese and the award-winning Flagship Cheese Experience.

You can go on a self-guided tour around the centre where you will learn about the process of cheesemaking or enjoy some cheesemaking demonstrations.

The former railway station has been transformed into a museum with an exhibition in a stationary train.

There is also the Wensleydale Railway where you can enjoy rides from Leeming Bar to Redmire and the Dales Countryside Museum where you can learn more about rural life through history.


As well as things to do, Hawes is lucky to have a range of places to eat such as Caffé Curva which is a “Yorkshire Dales gem” where you can enjoy coffee, homemade cakes, and savoury brunch options.

There is also the Crown, a dog-friendly pub in the town that offers visitors plenty of food choices such as a Sunday roast with all of the trimmings.

If you want to pick up something to remember your trip to Hawes by, you can stop off at the Alchemist’s Cottage, which sells coffee, tea, and quirky gifts you would struggle to find anywhere else.
