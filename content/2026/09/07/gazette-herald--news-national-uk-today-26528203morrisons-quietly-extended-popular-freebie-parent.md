Morrisons is giving parents a free sweet treat this week as Baby & Toddler Club members can claim a free cake with any hot drink.

The supermarket has extended its popular free cake offer from one day to a full seven days, giving members the chance to enjoy a treat every day from Monday, September 7 to Sunday, September 13.

The offer is available at Morrisons Cafés nationwide and comes as part of the supermarket's Baby Event.

Parents can choose from a tempting selection of cakes and sweet treats, including Blueberry Muffins, Salted Caramel Brownies, Coffee and Walnut Loaf Cake and Biscoff Cheesecake.


Other options include Sticky Toffee Muffins, Lemon Muffins, Toffee Crunch Pie, Apple Crumble Tart and Raspberry & Almond Slice.

To get the freebie, Baby & Toddler Club members simply need to buy any hot drink and scan their Morrisons More Card through the app at the till.

The offer is only available to Baby & Toddler Club members and iced drinks are excluded. All items are subject to availability.

The free cake deal is not the only saving available to families at Morrisons.

The supermarket also runs its Kids Eat Free scheme nationwide, allowing parents to get a free children's meal when they buy an adult main meal worth £5 or more.

With the free cake offer running for just one week, parents who want to take advantage will need to claim it by Sunday, September 13.
