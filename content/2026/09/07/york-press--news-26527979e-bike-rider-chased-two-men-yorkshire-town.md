An e-bike rider has been chased by two men in a North Yorkshire town, police said.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information about what it is treating as affray in Harrogate.

The force said the incident happened at about 9.15pm on Sunday (September 6) in North Park Road and Coach Road.

“It involved two men chasing another man on an electric bike,” a police spokesperson said.

They said the force is appealing for information about any descriptions of the suspects or their vehicles.

Anyone with information is asked to email [Emily.Williams@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Emily.Williams@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or using its website.
