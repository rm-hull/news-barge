# Young man denies murdering elderly woman in Yorkshire house fire

## A man accused of starting a house fire that killed an elderly woman has denied being responsible for her death.

Joshua Hodlin appeared at Leeds Crown Court this morning where he formally entered a not-guilty plea to murdering the pensioner in Knottingley in July.

The 24-year-old was over a video link from HMP Leeds.

The trial, set to start on January 12 next year, is expected to last around a week.

Hodlin, of Harwood Avenue, Pontefract, will remain in custody until his trial. Further stage dates were set which will take place before the trial begins.

![Hodlin is accused of murdering the pensioner who died in a house fire on Yew Tree Walk, Knottingley.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/13/7/40/Hodlin.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Hodlin is accused of starting the blaze at the property on Yew Tree Walk, Knottingley, on the evening of Sunday, July 5.

Shirley Hodlin, 75, died in the fire.
