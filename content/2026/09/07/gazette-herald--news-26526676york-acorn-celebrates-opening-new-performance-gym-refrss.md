York Acorn ARLFC celebrated a major milestone on Saturday as rugby league legend Jamie Peacock officially opened the club’s new performance and development gym at Thanet Road.

Players, coaches, sponsors, former players, juniors and supporters came together for the opening ahead of Acorn’s first-team fixture against West Bowling.

The new facility represents a major investment in the future of the community club and will give players throughout York Acorn access to high-quality strength and conditioning facilities.

The gym was designed and installed by BLK BOX and features new racks, free weights, conditioning equipment and a dedicated sled track, alongside existing equipment that has been restored and incorporated into the facility.

Mr Peacock, a former Bradford Bulls and Leeds Rhinos forward and one of rugby league's most decorated players, having previously won the Man of Steel award.

The project has been made possible through the backing of the club’s sponsors, businesses and volunteers, with a permanent sponsor wall installed in the gym recognising those who have invested in the facility and the future of York Acorn.

A huge amount of voluntary work also went on behind the scenes to ensure the facility was ready for its official opening.

The club hopes the gym will benefit far more than its current senior players, with junior development and creating a pathway through York Acorn at the heart of the project.

A spokesman for the gym said: “We can’t thank our sponsors enough for believing in what we are trying to achieve, and BLK BOX has done a fantastic job bringing the facility together."
