A train service between York and London Kings Cross has been delayed due to a fault.

The 2.30pm service from London Kings Cross to Edinburgh has been delayed and is expected to be 12 minutes late.

A LNER spokesperson said: "The 2.30pm London Kings Cross to Edinburgh due at 6.40pm is being delayed between London Kings Cross and York and is now expected to be 12 minutes late.

"This is due to a fault with the signalling system."

It comes after all train lanes between Reading and London were closed for hours this morning amid an emergency services incident between the stations.

This temporary closure was announced shortly after 11.30am this morning (Monday, September 7) by Great Western Railway (GWR).

A statement from the train service on the social media site X said: "Travel update.

"Due to a road vehicle colliding with a bridge between Reading and London Paddington, the line is blocked.

"Train services running to and from these stations may be delayed.

"Disruption is expected until 12.30pm.

"Check your journey online and follow us for updates."
