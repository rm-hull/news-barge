**HARROGATE**— Local charities, voluntary groups, and non-profit organisations are facing a rapidly approaching deadline to secure essential funding.** Harrogate Town Council**is urging community entities to submit their applications for the** 2026/27 Small Grants Scheme**before the window slams shut on** Tuesday 15 September 2026**.

A total financial package of **£74,045** has been made available across two targeted funding streams, with individual awards normally capped at**£5,000 per project**. Dedicated administrative support is also being provided to help smaller organisations navigate the application form or access documents in alternative formats.

## Two Funding Streams Open for Local Impact

The 2026/27 funding package has been split into two specific pots designed to cover both immediate operational needs and long-term improvements:

- **The General Grants Fund (£24,045):** Earmarked for localized projects, community activities, services, and distinct civic events.
- **The Infrastructure and Capital Grants Fund (£50,000):** Allocated for physical improvements, equipment purchases, and long-term assets.

While applicants should normally be located within the **Harrogate Town Council boundary**, the council confirmed it will consider outside organisations if they can explicitly demonstrate that the funding directly benefits a Harrogate-based project or its residents.

## Leadership Focus: Partnerships and Civic Support

**Councillor Mark Warr**, Chair of the Finance & General Purposes Committee, emphasized the simple nature of the application process and the council’s ongoing commitment to grassroots initiatives:

Since our inception in 2025, a pivotal part of Harrogate Town Council’s role has been working with partners, stakeholders and community groups within the town. As part of this work, there is often a real need for financial support to start and run projects that can make a real difference to local residents.

We hope that the recent launch of our Small Grants Scheme will help local community groups, charities and partner organisations fund some of their work. I would like to encourage as many as possible to apply to our Small Grants Scheme before the deadline of 15th September 2026.

The application process is not onerous and support is available from our website, where applicants can also see examples of previous grants awarded, or over the phone from our office team.


## Success Story: Keeping the Stray Bonfire Free to Attend

To highlight the tangible impact of the scheme, council officials pointed to the **Harrogate Round Table**, which successfully secured a**£1,000 grant** to deliver the**2025 Harrogate Stray Bonfire**.

Faced with overall event costs exceeding £16,000, the organisers relied heavily on this grant to offset the essential costs of hiring portable toilets. This crucial funding ensured the massive, highly popular community event could safely accommodate thousands of visitors while remaining completely free to attend, ultimately boosting local civic pride and community cohesion.

## How to Apply Before the Deadline

Harrogate Town Council has set up multiple channels to ensure the application process remains highly accessible to all community leaders:

- **By Phone:** Call the dedicated office team at**01423 642542** for step-by-step guidance.
- **By Email:** Contact**[grants@harrogatetowncouncil.gov.uk](mailto:grants@harrogatetowncouncil.gov.uk)** to raise eligibility queries or request alternative document formats.
- **Online:** Visit the official Harrogate Town Council website to review eligibility criteria and inspect previous grant success stories.

*All completed applications must be submitted by 23:59 on 15 September 2026 to be considered for this financial cycle.*
