A NEW play centre has opened after a major investment, creating jobs and expanding facilities for families.

Yorkshire Garden Centres has launched its third Grass Hoppers Play Area at its Otley site, following the success of similar attractions at Tong and Tingley.

After a soft launch, the new venue was officially opened during a civic and charity preview event attended by Otley Town Mayor Councillor Mick Bradley and Deputy Mayor Councillor Jo Allen, who performed the ribbon-cutting ceremony.

## What will the play centre feature?

The new facility offers almost 6,000 square feet of play space and can accommodate up to 75 children per session.

The expansion has created 17 full and part-time jobs across play operations and hospitality.

## 'Fun-filled, all-weather experience for families'

Mark Farnsworth, managing director of Yorkshire Garden Centres, described the opening as an important milestone for the business.

He said: “Bringing Grass Hoppers to a third location is a major business milestone for our group.

"We’ve committed significant investment to ensure Otley offers a fun-filled, all-weather experience for families.

“The reception from the wider Wharfedale community has been outstanding, and we are proud to continue growing our presence across West Yorkshire.”

  The new play centre Grass Hoppers at Otley Garden Centre

The launch event also highlighted Yorkshire Garden Centres’ ongoing partnership with Martin House Children’s Hospice.

Families supported by the hospice were invited to enjoy complimentary play sessions during the preview day.

The occasion marked another milestone for the partnership, with Yorkshire Garden Centres announcing it has now raised more than £100,000 for Martin House since the collaboration began in April 2025.

Lizzie Philpott, philanthropy and partnerships team manager at Martin House Children’s Hospice, said: “It was wonderful to see siblings supported by Martin House enjoying the new Grass Hoppers play centre and having the chance to simply play and have fun together.

“Experiences like this mean a great deal to families, offering a chance to make happy memories away from daily pressures.

"We’re incredibly grateful to Yorkshire Garden Centres for providing free access and making our families such a central part of their opening celebrations.”

Grass Hoppers Otley is now open daily.
