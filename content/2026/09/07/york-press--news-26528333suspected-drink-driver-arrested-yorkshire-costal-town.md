A suspected drink driver has been arrested after police reportedly saw a man trying to start a car in East Yorkshire while under the influence.

Humberside Police said its officers suspected the man to be under the influence then watched as he entered a vehicle in Lairgate, Beverley, on Sunday afternoon (September 6).

The force said the man sat in the driver’s seat of the vehicle at around 12.30pm “before attempting to start the car”.

Officers breathalysed the man during which they alleged he blew a reading of 148, more than four times the legal drink-drive limit.

Police said a 46-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of being in charge of a vehicle under the influence. He remained in police custody on Monday pending further inquiries.
