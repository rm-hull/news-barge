A well-known and much-loved member of the school community in the Bradford district is retiring after 32 years of service.

Eric Shaw, site supervisor at Steeton Primary School, has been described as a cornerstone of the school and a "reassuring presence" for generations of pupils, families, and staff.

Known affectionately as "Mr Shaw," he was recognised for his dedication to maintaining the school’s buildings and grounds, as well as his willingness to help with any task, no matter how challenging.

Headteacher Claire Redman said: "There are very few people who become part of the fabric of a school in the way that Eric has.

"Over 32 years, he has quietly gone about making Steeton a better place every single day.

"He has never sought recognition, but he has earned the respect and affection of everyone who has had the privilege of working with him.

"Eric represents everything that makes a great school community.

"His legacy isn't just the things he has built – it's the confidence he has given children.

"He will be hugely missed."

His contributions extended beyond his official duties.

Mr Shaw was instrumental in supporting school sports as a playleader and coach, drawing on his experience as a former Yorkshire Judo Champion.

He cycled to and from school for many years and dedicated countless hours to coaching football, rugby, and cricket teams.

Among those he inspired was Kerri Holland, who went on to become an international footballer representing Wales.

Long before equality in sport became a national conversation, Eric championed opportunities for girls to participate and excel, believing every child deserved the chance to discover what they were capable of.

He also made a lasting mark on the school environment by building bespoke furniture and creating imaginative play areas, including the school’s pirate ship, the Jade Pearl.

His hands-on approach and creative solutions made the school a more welcoming and engaging place for all.

Mr Shaw retires having built more than just physical structures; he leaves behind a stronger, more supportive school community shaped by his generosity and care.

Staff, pupils, and families have expressed their gratitude for his years of service and wished him a happy and well-earned retirement.

The school community said in a statement: "Everyone at Steeton Primary School wishes Eric and his family a long, happy and well-deserved retirement."
