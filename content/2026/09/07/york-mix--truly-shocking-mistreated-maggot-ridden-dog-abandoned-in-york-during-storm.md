A spaniel abandoned in a York was in a ‘truly shocking’ state, the RSPCA has said.

The charity has issued an urgent appeal for information about the elderly liver and white-coloured dog who was discovered in heavy rain in a field in Moor Lane at about 7am on Sunday 30 August.

Two members of the public who found him took him to their nearby home to warm him up.

He was then seen by a local vet before being transferred to the RSPCA’s York, Harrogate & District Branch animal centre in Landing Lane.

Nicknamed Albie, he was severely matted and suffering from chronic dental disease which had caused one of his canines to break off, exposing the root.

#### How to help

Anyone with information about Albie is urged to contact the RSPCA’s inspectorate appeal line, in confidence, on 0300 123 8018, quoting reference number 01893084

His back legs were splayed. X-rays have revealed he has a degenerative condition called spondylosis and compressed joint spaces.

He is receiving ongoing veterinary care but remains in a serious condition.

Marie Sandall, from the York RSPCA branch, said: “Seeing an elderly dog in such terrible condition is absolutely heartbreaking. Despite the pain he must be in, he’s the sweetest dog.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/albie-spaniel-abandoned-moor-lane-york-rspca-1.webp)

“He must have known we were here to help him as he seemed to enjoy all the attention and you could see the instant relief when we shaved him.

“He had pieces of straw stuck in the mats, and even more heartbreaking was when the vet called us to say they had also found live maggots in his coat.

“No animal should ever be subjected to neglect like this and I am just glad we are here to help him.”

RSPCA animal rescue officer Mollie Masters is looking into the incident

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/albie-spaniel-abandoned-moor-lane-york-rspca-2.webp)

She said: “When I first saw Albie, my heart broke. I have seen animals in desperate need before, but the condition this elderly dog was found in was truly shocking.

“His entire body was encased in thick, painful mats of fur. There wasn’t a single part of him that was free from them.

“He could barely stand, let alone walk, and his teeth were in an appalling condition.

“He’s such a lovely, friendly-natured dog and it’s very difficult to imagine the level of pain and discomfort he must have been living with.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/albie-spaniel-abandoned-moor-lane-york-rspca-4.webp)

“What is even harder to comprehend is that someone must have known how badly Albie was suffering, yet they still chose to do nothing and dumped him in a field in the middle of a rainstorm; cold, wet and alone.

“We desperately need to find where Albie has come from as he is unfortunately not microchipped and I am urging anyone with any information to please come forward.”

The RSPCA’s York branch – a separate self-funding charity which has been re-homing animals at its current site for nearly 50 years – has begun redeveloping its animal home so that it can help even more animals like Albie in the future.
