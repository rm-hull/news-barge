# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image of man after motorbike stolen near Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police has released a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to following a theft near Harrogate.

The incident happened at 1am on August 23 when a motorbike was stolen from Cowstail Lane in Tockwith.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: "If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us.”

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260164440 when providing any details regarding the incident.
