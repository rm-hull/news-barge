Concern grows for a young man who has been missing for over five days.

Northumbria Police launched an appeal on September 3, regarding 20-year-old John from Gateshead.

In the initial appeal, it was reported he was last seen in Archbold Terrace in Jesmond just after 2am on September 2.

Officers said "extensive" enquiries were ongoing to locate him.

The force has now posted an update following the release of CCTV footage.

John, who is still missing as of September 7, is described as “5ft 8in tall, of slim build, with dark brown, mullet-style hair".

He was last seen wearing a grey T-shirt and black jeans.

Following the release of CCTV footage from Northumbria Police, two people were seen speaking to John on Tower Street in Newcastle City Centre.

They have both approached police to assist with their enquiries.

The two individuals confirmed that at around 3am on September 2, roughly an hour after he was last seen in Jesmond, John asked for directions from them before travelling towards City Road.

Police confirm that the search for John remains ongoing, and have asked anyone with information related to John’s whereabouts to come forward- “no matter how small or insignificant you think it may be.”

Northumbria Police request anyone looking to make contact send a direct message on social media, use the live chat and report forms on their website, or call 101 using reference number: 26000439507.
