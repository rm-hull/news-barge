An MP has said he is 'disgusted' after five dead rabbits were found lined up on a bus stop bench in County Durham.

The animals were discovered on Front Street in Pelton at around 8.30am on Saturday (September 5) by a member of the public who branded it 'horrifying' and 'quite alarming'.

The rabbits were removed that morning by staff from the local authority, police have now confirmed.

Luke Akehurst MP said the incident had caused 'great distress' to residents.
