A mock employment tribunal will take place in York next month to help businesses understand future workplace law.

Hosted at York’s Guildhall, the half-day event on October 8 will bring employment law to life through a fictional tribunal scenario set in 2027.

It will explore the impact of the forthcoming Employment Rights Act 2025 and address emerging workplace issues such as artificial intelligence, neurodiversity, and generational dynamics.

Torque Law, the organiser behind the event, said: "Our focus will be on what lessons can be learned today to avoid costly mistakes tomorrow.

"Whether you’re preparing for the impact of the Employment Rights Act 2025 or trying to understand the implications of AI in workplaces disputes, this event will be one not to be missed."

The tribunal scenario will cover unfair dismissal claims under a new six-month qualifying period, age discrimination allegations involving Generation Z, and the influence of AI-generated content on workplace investigations and tribunal submissions.

It will also address the complexities of handling cases involving key witnesses who have left the business.

Delegates will have the opportunity to observe how employment tribunal hearings are conducted, including cross-examination, legal argument, and evidence handling.

The tribunal’s judgment will be delivered live, followed by analysis from a panel of experts who will explain the key legal and practical takeaways.

Attendees will also be able to ask questions and network with peers over a buffet lunch.

The event is aimed at HR professionals, business owners, senior leaders, line managers, and in-house legal teams—essentially anyone involved in people management who wants to gain a clearer understanding of how employment decisions could be scrutinised in a legal setting.

The mock tribunal will run from 8.30am to 1.30pm on October 8 at the Guildhall in York.

Tickets are priced at £200 plus VAT per person, with a discounted rate of £180 plus VAT per person for bookings of two or more people from the same organisation.
