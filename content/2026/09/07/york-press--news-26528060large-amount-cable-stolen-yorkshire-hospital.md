Police are investigating a “large amount” of cabling being stolen from a North Yorkshire hospital.

The incident happened at Harrogate Hospital overnight from Monday, August 31, to Tuesday, September 1, North Yorkshire Police said.

Police said it is believed that a vehicle, at least the size of a transit van, would have been needed to remove the cabling.

Officers are urging anyone with information about the theft to email [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260171012 when passing on information to police or to Crimestoppers.
