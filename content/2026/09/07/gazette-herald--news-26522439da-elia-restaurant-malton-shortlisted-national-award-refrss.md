A family-run Italian restaurant in Malton has been named a finalist in the UK Small Business Awards.

Da Elia Pizza & Restaurant has been shortlisted in the Best Restaurant/Food Business category, after moving from a food van into a permanent location in Castlegate in April 2024.

The restaurant has since introduced dine-in, takeaway, and delivery services while staying true to its family-run roots.

Mariana Madalina Horea, co-owner of Da Elia Pizza & Restaurant, said: "We are incredibly proud to be recognised as finalists again.

"We started in 2023 with a small food van, and seeing how far our family business has come means so much to us.

"We are very grateful to our customers, our team, and the local community who have supported us throughout our journey."

In 2025, the business launched Elia's Corner, a morning takeaway serving breakfast, lunch, and fresh food to go.
