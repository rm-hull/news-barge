A 19-year-old woman has been arrested on suspicion of causing serious injuries by dangerous driving after a car crashed onto its roof.

The crash happened on the B1249 Main Street in Foxholes, between Malton and Filey, at around 11.45pm on Tuesday 11 August.

A grey Seat Leon car was heading north towards Scarborough.

#### B1249 at Foxholes

It left the road and rolled onto its roof in a field, causing significant damage to the vehicle.

A 19-year-old woman who was driving the car was arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by dangerous driving on 25 August.

She suffered serious injuries along with three boys aged 17, 16 and 16 who were passengers in the vehicle.

The woman has been released on bail while enquiries continue.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The passengers are assisting the investigation as officers work to determine the full circumstances surrounding the collision.

“Although it is almost one month on from the incident, they are hoping motorists, homeowners and businesses may still have relevant CCTV and dashcam footage of the collision or the grey Seat Leon in the build-up to it.”
