A teaser for Inbetweeners 3 has been released.

Following the huge success of The Inbetweeners Movie (2011) and The Inbetweeners 2 (2014), fans have long hoped for a third film featuring Will McKenzie, Simon Cooper, Jay Cartwright and Neil Sutherland.

## What is The Inbetweeners?

The original Channel 4 sitcom became a cultural phenomenon thanks to its awkward humour, relatable teenage experiences and memorable characters.

## New movie on the way

Netflix has now released a teaser for a new movie that is "now in production".

It shows all four characters in the iconic yellow Fiat Cinquecento.
