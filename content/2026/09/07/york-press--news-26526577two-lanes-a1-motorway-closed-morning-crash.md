TRAFFIC has returned to normal on a North Yorkshire motorway close to its junction for York this morning after an earlier crash closed two lanes (Monday, September 7).

Lanes one and two of the A1(M) northbound from junction 44 for York and Tadcaster to junction 45 for the A659 and Boston Spa were closed following the crash, which was first reported around 6.30am.

Motorway cameras from Highways England between junction 43 and 44 northbound showed significant tailbacks in the area with average speeds reported as being down to ten miles per hour.

Junction 43 is where the M1 meets the A1 (M), bringing traffic from West Yorkshire.


AA Traffic News had reported northbound delays of up to 40 minutes in the area south of the crash, but traffic is now indicated to be running normally in the area.
