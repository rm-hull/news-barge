A man was seriously hurt after being attacked in York.

It happened on **Clifford Street** at around**1am** on**Friday 27 June**.

The victim was punched in his face several times causing serious injuries.

#### Clifford Street, York

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “As well as locating the man in the footage, we need to trace the taller man who he was with as a potential witness.

“We are also urging people to come forward who have witnessed the incident or have mobile phone video footage.”
