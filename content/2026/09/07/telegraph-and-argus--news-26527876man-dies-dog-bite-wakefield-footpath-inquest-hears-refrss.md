A 55-year-old man died after a dog bite on a Wakefield footpath, an inquest opening heard.

James Judge died after an incident on the old train line footpath off Blacker Lane in Wakefield on August 15.

A post-mortem examination gave a provisional cause of death as complications of a right leg wound caused by a dog bite, Wakefield Coroner's Court heard.

Judge was taken to Leeds General Infirmary after the incident, where he died, Acting senior coroner Oliver Longstaff said. He was identified by his mother.

The court heard Judge was unmarried and had been living in Horbury, where he was born.

Lee Ellis, 55, of Calder Grove in Wakefield, is charged with manslaughter and being in charge of a dog dangerously out of control causing injury resulting in death.

He initially appeared at Bradford Magistrates' Court last month and is due to appear at Leeds Crown Court on September 16.

Longstaff said the inquest would be adjourned pending the outcome of the criminal proceedings.

Acting senior coroner Oliver Longstaff said: "Whether an inquest is required to be resumed cannot be answered until the conclusion of that process."

The hearing was adjourned, pending the outcome of the criminal proceedings.
