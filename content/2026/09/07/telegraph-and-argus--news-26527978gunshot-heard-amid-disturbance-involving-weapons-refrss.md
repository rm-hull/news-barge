AN arrest has been made after a gunshot was heard amid a "disturbance involving weapons".

Officers were called to Upper George Street in Heckmondwike just after 8pm on Sunday.

Police said a 29-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of possession of a firearm.

According to police, evidence was found during enquiries which suggested a firearm had been discharged.

The arrested man remained in custody on Monday while enquiries continued.

Detectives from Kirklees CID are continuing to investigate the circumstances surrounding the incident.

## 
  **What police have said**

On Monday morning, a spokesperson for police said: "At about 8.13pm yesterday (Sunday), police received a report of a disturbance involving weapons in Upper George Street, Heckmondwike.

"Further reports suggested a gunshot had been heard.

"Officers attended and arrested a man following initial enquiries into the incident."

They added: "Evidence was also found to suggest a firearm had been discharged.

"A 29-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of possession of a firearm and remains in custody at this time.

"Kirklees CID is continuing enquiries."
