HIT musical Billy Elliot is coming to Bradford's Alhambra Theatre.

Based on the much-loved BAFTA-winning and Oscar nominated film, the show is set in a northern mining town against the background of the 1984/85 miners’ strike and follows Billy’s journey from the boxing ring to the ballet class, where he discovers a passion for dance that inspires his family and whole community and changes his life forever.

Acclaimed by audiences and critics alike, Billy Elliot the Musical has been seen by just over 12 million people across five continents and is the recipient of more than 85 awards internationally, including 10 Tony Awards and five Olivier Awards.

In 2006, Liam Mower, James Lomas and George Maguire, the first three boys to play the title role, became the youngest performers ever to have won the Olivier Award for Best Actor, with the show also picking up the Best Musical prize.

In total, more than 125 boys have now played the iconic title role on stage worldwide.

Billy Elliot the Musical is brought to life by the multiple award-winning creative team behind the film, including writer Lee Hall (book and lyrics), director Stephen Daldry and choreographer, Peter Darling, joined by Sir Elton John who composed the show’s score.

Universal Theatrical Group and Working Title Films have announced that the cast of this production of Billy Elliot the Musical will star Annette McLaughlin as Mrs Wilkinson, Deka Walmsley as Dad, Jack Bandeira as Tony. The role of Grandma has yet to be announced.

Annette McLaughlin is no stranger to the role of Mrs Wilkinson, having played the part in the last UK tour of Billy Elliot. Her West End theatre credits include Mrs Wormwood in Matilda The Musical, Dolores Umbridge in Harry Potter and the Cursed Child and Velma Kelly in Chicago.

Deka Walmsley returns to the role of Dad having played the part to great acclaim in both the West End production and on screen in Billy Elliot the Musical Live. His other theatre credits include Macbeth and The Pitmen Painters and TV dramas include Vera, Black Mirror and Happy Valley.

The previously announced Noah Mannion, 13, Rafferty Smale, 12, and Spencer Collins, 13, and William Gurney, 13, will share the role of Billy and Liam Mower will play older Billy.

Billy Elliot the Musical is presented by Universal Theatrical Group and Working Title Films and is based on the Universal Pictures/Studio Canal film.

The show is produced by Tim Bevan, Eric Fellner, Jon Finn and Sally Greene. Executive producers are David Furnish, Angela Morrison and Rebecca Quigley for Working Title Films.

This will be the second time the show has been to Bradford's Alhambra Theatre. It first ran there in 2016.

* Billy Elliot the Musical runs at the Alhambra Theatre from October 19 to November 6, 2027.

There will be a signed performance on October 27 an audio described performance on October 28 and a captioned performance on November 4.

Tickets go on sale on Monday, September 14, 2026 at 9am. Call (01274) 432000 or visit bradford-theatres.co.uk
