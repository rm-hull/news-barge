A NEW café/bar and restaurant could be coming to an East Yorkshire town centre.

A planning application has been submitted to the county council on behalf of Loungers UK Limited to convert the former Nat West bank branch at 60-61 Saturday Market in Beverley's conservation area.

Loungers UK currently operates 310 sites across the UK through two brands named The Lounges and The Cosy Clubs offering all-day dining.

  


Vallado Lounge in Vangarde Leisure Park York operates under The Lounge brand and Cosy Club York is in Fossgate.

Planning documents said "The Lounges are all uniquely designed, with an emphasis on creating a welcoming and inclusive environment for families, as well as four-legged friends.

"The aim of every Lounge is to tie in with local customers, so that they can make of the Lounge what they will, whether a customer wants to come in for a coffee after dropping children to school, a working lunch, or a tipple after work, Lounges can provide an environment for all these purposes."

A statement issued with the planning application said: "Each new site will generate in the region of 30 new jobs for the local community across a variety of roles – from leadership positions to front of house staff.

"These staff will also have available to them a very high standard of training with regards to health and safety, licensing etc which will help prepare them for other positions in the future.

"This is in addition to providing the best customer service available - Lounges work through creating the desire for customers to come multiple times per month, as opposed to just a special event. "

Alcohol is available at Loungers UK Limited sites, the plans say, but sales are a smaller proportion than that of food.

The plans say that the 393 square metre site within the Grade II listed building in the centre of Beverley will not involve any material change to the external appearance of the building.

Internally, the ground floor layout would comprise a bar area, customer space, store, staff room and the kitchen would be on the first floor.

Minor internal works would facilitate the installation of plant and extraction equipment.

Saturday Market has been a focal point of trade and community life in Beverley since medieval times and today remains one of the town’s biggest visitor attractions, drawing tourists with its heritage setting, independent shops and a weekly market.

The consultation date for the plans submitted to East Riding of Yorkshire Council ends on Friday, September 25.
