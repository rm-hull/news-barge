A Bishop Auckland pub is on the lookout for a new operator to take the reins.

The Tap & Tun, on Newgate Street, is seeking someone “passionate” and “community-minded” to manage the establishment.

The opportunity, provided by Amber Taverns, offers a well-refurbished pub selling national brands.

   

*(Image: AMBER TAVERNS)*

The pub also has full live sports packages, budgets for entertainment and security, and regular support from an Amber Taverns Business Development Manager.

The new operator will also have access to dedicated health and safety and licensing support.

The Tap & Tun recently underwent a makeover, known as a "sparkle" in the Amber Taverns world, making it more spacious and comfortable.

The venue now boasts new low-level seating throughout, creating a relaxed and welcoming environment.

   

*(Image: AMBER TAVERNS)*

   

*(Image: AMBER TAVERNS)*

Additional features include a pool table, four top-quality darts boards, and a private karaoke room.

For sports enthusiasts, the pub has a brand-new big screen TV and multiple screens around the venue showing Sky Sports, TNT Sports, Racing TV, and pay-per-view events.

The bar offers a variety of popular drinks, including Cruzcampo, John Smith's, Fosters, Birra Moretti, and Strongbow Strawberry, alongside a wide selection of gins, spirits, and classic cocktails.

   

*(Image: AMBER TAVERNS)*

The pub also has a spacious roof terrace for sunny days, where patrons can enjoy a drink in hand and live sport on the screen.

A spokesperson for Amber Taverns said: "Have you ever dreamed of running your own pub?

"Amber Taverns is looking for a passionate, community-minded operator to take the reins at The Tap & Tun in Bishop Auckland – with no upfront costs.
