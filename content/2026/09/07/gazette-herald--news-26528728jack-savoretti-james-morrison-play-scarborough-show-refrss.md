Promoters for a major outdoor venue on the North Yorkshire coast have announced the first acts for its 2027 season.

Jack Savoretti and James Morrison will bring their double-headline tour to Scarborough Open Air Theatre next summer.

The chart-topping singer-songwriters will perform at the coast venue on Friday, July 9, 2027, with Savoretti set to close the show.

  Chart-toppers Jack Savoretti, pictured, and James Morrison are teaming up next summer for a double-headline tour which heads to Scarborough Open Air Theatre on the Yorkshire coast on Friday July 9. 

*(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

Tickets go on sale at 10am this Friday, September 11, through Scarborough Open Air Theatre and Ticketmaster.

The announcement comes just weeks after the end of what promoters described as a record-breaking 2026 season at Scarborough OAT, with a 28-show summer programme and more than 175,000 tickets sold.

The season attracted music fans from 64 countries and was estimated to have generated an economic boost of more than £18m for the Yorkshire coast.

  James Morrison will be making a return visit to the venue after having been The Script’s special guest in 2022. 

*(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

Julian Murray, from promoters Cuffe and Taylor, said: “The 2026 Scarborough OAT season was truly sensational and we are delighted, just two weeks after the final show of the summer, to be able to announce our first headliners for 2027.

“Jack and James are two of the UK’s most beloved contemporary singer-songwriters so to be bringing them here, on the same night, will be something truly special.

“It will be a much-requested Scarborough OAT debut for Jack and it’s a welcome return to the venue for James, who was The Script’s very special guest here back in 2022, so he is delighted to be back as a headliner. This will be an unmissable night for fans of these two incredible talents.”

The Scarborough date is one of seven outdoor shows on Savoretti and Morrison’s Summer 2027 tour.

The pair will alternate closing the concerts, with Morrison headlining the Halifax, Llangollen and Margate dates.

Savoretti has returned this year with his ninth studio album, We Will Always Be The Way We Were, released 20 years after his debut record Between the Minds.

The singer-songwriter has scored two UK number one albums and five UK top 10 albums, and recently played a sold-out 20th anniversary show at the Royal Albert Hall.

Morrison, whose hits include You Give Me Something, achieved his highest UK chart position for 14 years with his seventh album, Fight Another Day, which reached the top five last autumn.

During his career, he has had two UK number one albums, five UK top 10 singles, several platinum-certified releases and a BRIT Award.

Scarborough OAT’s 2026 programme included performances by Alanis Morissette, Michael Bublé, Hollywood Vampires and Teddy Swims.

Ticket sales surpassed the venue’s previous record, set in 2024, by more than 60,000.

More than one million tickets have now been sold for concerts at the North Yorkshire Council-owned venue since it reopened to the public in 2010.
