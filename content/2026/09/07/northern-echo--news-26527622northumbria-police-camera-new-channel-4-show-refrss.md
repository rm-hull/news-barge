A new Channel 4 documentary series will shine a spotlight on the people working behind the scenes to keep one of the North East's busiest city centres safe.

Geordie Coppers will air on Channel 4 next week, from September 14.

It will give viewers an inside look at Newcastle's City Safe partnership and the work carried out by Northumbria Police, the NE1 Street Rangers and Newcastle City Council.

Candour Productions said the series follows the organisations as they tackle challenges across the city and support residents, businesses and visitors.

They said: "Our new C4 series Geordie Coppers starts on September 14 and shows what it takes to make Newcastle one of the best cities in the UK.

"We go behind the scenes with Newcastle’s City Safe partnership, showing how Northumbria Police, the NE1 Street Rangers and Newcastle City Council work together to keep the city centre safe, bustling, and full of character."

The series promises “warmth, wit and plenty of Geordie spirit”, alongside frontline incidents and the daily work that keeps the city centre bustling.


The announcement trailer also offers a glimpse of what's to come, featuring officers and city centre teams dealing with nightlife-related incidents, stop and searches, police chases and making arrests.

In one lighter moment, the trailer even shows the discovery of an abandoned adult nappy.

The announcement has already generated excitement on social media, with Northumbria Police sharing details of the upcoming series.

Commenting on the social media announcement, they said: “Roll on episode 1.”

Residents also shared their excitement, with one writing: “Oh I can’t wait to see this!”

Geordie Coppers begins on Channel 4 on Monday, September 14, at 10pm, with new episodes weekly.
