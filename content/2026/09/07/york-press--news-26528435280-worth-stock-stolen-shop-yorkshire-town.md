Thieves have struck at a North Yorkshire shop and made off with £280 worth of stock.

North Yorkshire Police has issued images of two men sought in connection with the thefts from Boyes in Whitby.

The theft happened on Monday, August 10, at the store in Station Square, the force said.

A police spokesperson urged anyone who recognises the men to come forward, saying: “Please contact us if you recognise the men pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.”

Those with information are asked to email [Ben.Hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Ben.Hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260155289 when passing on information to police or to Crimestoppers.
