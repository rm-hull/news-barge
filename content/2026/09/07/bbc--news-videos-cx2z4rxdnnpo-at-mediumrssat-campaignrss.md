# What we know about anti-migrant protests and organiser Danny Thomas

Hundreds of demonstrators gathered in Dover and Portsmouth over the weekend in protests against small boat crossings.

But what is the connection between the two protests?

The answer is one man - Danny Thomas, more often known as Danny Tommo.

UK correspondent Daniel Sandford looks into the background of Danny Tommo and how he has galvanised these protests through social media and local support.
