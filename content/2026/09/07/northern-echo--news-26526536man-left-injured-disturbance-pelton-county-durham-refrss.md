A man suffered head and back injuries and four people were arrested after a disturbance at a house in a County Durham village.

Emergency services were called to Constance Street in Pelton at around 1am on Saturday, August 29, after the incident.

The man, who is in his 30s, was taken to hospital with head and back injuries but has since been discharged.

Three men aged 62, 40 and 33, and a 44-year-old woman were arrested on suspicion of aggravated burglary.

Police said they were later released on bail.

A spokesperson for Durham Police added: "Officers believe this is an isolated incident and are not looking for anyone else in connection with what happened.

"An investigation has been launched, and anyone with any information is asked to call Durham Constabulary on 101, quoting incident reference 230 of August 29.
