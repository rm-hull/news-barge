A Toby Carvery restaurant in Bradford showed signs of activity this morning after firefighters were called to a kitchen fire at the restaurant last night.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to Rooley Lane at 4.37pm on Sunday following reports of a kitchen fire.

Three crews attended, but the blaze had already been extinguished by the time they arrived, the fire service confirmed.

When a Telegraph & Argus reporter visited the site this morning, there were signs of activity at the venue.

A Toby Carvery spokesperson has since confirmed that operations were resumed as normal today.

Lights were visible inside the building and there appeared to be no obvious signs of damage to the restaurant.

## Restaurant operating as normal

A spokesperson for Toby Carvery said the Rooley Lane restaurant experienced what was described as an "extremely minor" kitchen issue on Sunday, which was resolved quickly.

The company said normal operations had resumed on Monday and the restaurant was welcoming guests as usual. Toby Carvery thanked emergency services for attending to carry out routine checks and said disruption had been minimal, with no staff members or customers harmed.

## FULL statement from West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Services

A spokesperson said: “We were called yesterday to Rooley Lane at 1637 to reports of a kitchen fire.

“Three crews attended but upon arrival the fire was already extinguished.”

## 
  **What Toby Carvery have said**

A spokesperson said: “Unfortunately, our restaurant was impacted by an extremely minor kitchen problem on Sunday which was resolved very quickly. Happily, normal operations have resumed today and we are welcoming guests as usual. We are grateful the emergency services visited to carry out their usual checks, but pleased that disruption was minimal with no harm caused to any team members or guests.”
