The top bars in Europe have been named – and there’s one in York and another in North Yorkshire.

Each year the European Bar Guide publishes its 100 best watering holes across the continent.

The new list has just come out. It includes everything from a self-styled ‘ruined’ bar in Budapest to a chic Brussels cocktail joint.

And the list makes very happy reading for drinkers in our neck of the woods.

Altogether 12 UK pubs and bars are included. And North Yorkshire has two – the most of any British county.

So which are they?

## The Blue Bell, York

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/John-Pyrus-Timothy-Taylors.webp)

The Blue Bell may be one of York’s smallest pubs, but it is also one of the city’s most garlanded.

It’s won numerous awards and accolades, and appearing in the European Bar Guide’s Top 100 is just the latest.

Fossgate’s own Blue Bell was put at number 48 in the list.

“Two snug rooms and a tiny old side hatch in a corridor constitute the entire pub which originates in the late 18th century but whose 1904 Edwardian restoration has been kept intact ever since, earning it a Grade II* heritage listing,” the article says.

“The red hues and worn-in surroundings in such a tiny place create an intense cosiness and intimacy that is difficult to truly convey, you must experience it.

“It can be tricky at times to get a seat in either room and you have to accept that may involve sharing tables. However, rather than seeing that as a negative, enjoy the fact you will very often enter into conversation with your fellow patrons.”

Landlord John Pybus was delighted that the Blue Bell was in the continental elite.

“We’re immensely proud to be one of only 12 UK pubs to make it into the European Top 100,” he said on social media.

“As ever, thank you to all the brewers, drinkers and pint-pourers that make everything possible.”

## Birch Hall Inn, Beck Hole

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/birch-hole-inn-exterior-north-yorkshire-geograph.webp)

Placed at number 32 in the list, putting it fourth in the UK contingent, is the Birch Hall Inn at Beck Hole, between Pickering and Whitby.

This honour comes barely a month after the inn made it into a national guide to the Best Pubs In The UK 2026.

Here’s what the European Bar folk said about its appeal: “One of England’s one-offs, a surviving country inn still running a sweet shop as well as a pub.

“Three little rooms each accessible from individual doorways, this charming and unique family-run cottage serves local ales, very basic butties, pork pies, snacks, sweets and occasionally locally made produce like juices.

“Inside the pub it is very cosy and intimate, a social space that’s exciting, out of time and an instant conversation starter.

“As the proprietor says himself, on average 3 times a day he’ll hear someone say the words ‘Never change’.”
