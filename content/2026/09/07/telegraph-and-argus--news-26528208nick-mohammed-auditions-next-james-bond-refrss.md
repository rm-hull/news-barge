Nick Mohammed has put himself forward as the next James Bond after taking to the stage at the Royal Albert Hall for a special BBC Proms event.

Leeds-born Mohammed, best known for his role in Ted Lasso and his memorable appearance on The Celebrity Traitors, hosted Celebrating James Bond at the Proms at the end of August.

The show formed part of a series of presenting duties he has undertaken for the BBC Proms in 2026, with the Yorkshireman using the occasion to make a playful pitch for cinema's most famous secret agent.

## Nick Mohammed as the next James Bond?

Mohammed told the audience: "While a decision hasn't yet been made about who's going to be the next James Bond I am kind of seeing this as my audition.

"I know it's a bit unconventional, I'd usually just do a self-tape on my phone, but I thought why not hire out the Royal Albert Hall with the full support of an orchestra?"

He quipped: "Am I too short to be Bond?"

Bringing the show to a close, he made one final appeal to Bond producers, saying: "From me specifically to anyone from the James Bond casting department, the name is Mohammed, Nick Mohammed, which doesn't quite work because Mohammed is also a legitimate first name."

His comments come as anticipation continues to grow over the future of the Bond franchise.

In May, Amazon MGM Studios, which now oversees the series, confirmed that casting for the next 007 was under way.

## Yorkshire roots

While Mohammed's remarks were firmly tongue-in-cheek, the actor's career has continued to go from strength to strength in recent years, with audiences around the world recognising him for his portrayal of Nathan Shelley in the award-winning comedy Ted Lasso.

Despite his international success, Mohammed retains strong Yorkshire roots.

He was born in Leeds and educated at Abbey Grange Church of England Academy, then known as Abbey Grange High School, near Horsforth.

Earlier this year, he also brought his Show Pony tour to Bradford Live, performing at the city's newly opened entertainment venue in May.
