A WOMAN'S death is being treated as unexplained after she was found dead inside a flat in Bradford city centre.

Officers were called to an address in Holmfield Court, on Thornton Road, at about 1.30pm on Sunday after a welfare check was requested.

After entering the property, officers found a woman inside.

She was pronounced dead at the scene.

  A police vehicle outside Holmfield Court on Thornton Road, Bradford, on Monday morning. 

*(Image: T&A)*

## Holmfield Court history

  Holmfield Court was previously home to __Stephen Griffiths, who became known as the "Crossbow Cannibal" after murdering three women in Bradford between 2009 and 2010.__

Griffiths lived in the building at the time of the murders and was later jailed for life.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At about 1.30pm yesterday (Sunday), police attended an address in Holmfield Court, Bradford after a welfare check had been requested.

"Officers entered the property and found a woman inside.

"She was pronounced deceased at the scene.

"A scene remains in place while enquiries continue into her death, which at this time is being treated as unexplained."

## Ambulance service statement

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said ambulance crews were called to an incident at a private address in the area.

A spokesperson said: "Ambulances resources responded to an incident at a private address in the area, therefore no further details are available."

## Police cordon was in place

Crime scene investigators, detectives and uniformed officers were seen at the address on Sunday, while members of the public reported a significant emergency services presence in the area.

A police cordon was put in place as enquiries were undertaken. Officers remained at the scene on Monday morning.

Police enquiries into the woman's death are ongoing.
