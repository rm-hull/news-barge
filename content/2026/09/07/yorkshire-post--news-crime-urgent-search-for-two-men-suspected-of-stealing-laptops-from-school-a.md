# Urgent search for two men suspected of stealing laptops from school as police release CCTV

## Police are looking for the two men pictured after laptops were stolen from a Sheffield school.

On July 5, two suspects entered the school grounds of Meadowhead School on Dyche Lane, before leaving according to South Yorkshire Police.

The following night on July 6, it is believed the same two offenders again entered the property and stole items, including laptops before fleeing.

We are now keen to speak to the two men photographed as part of our inquiry.

![From officers’ enquiries it is understood that on 5 July two suspects entered the school grounds of Meadowhead School on Dyche Lane, before leaving.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmU1MzRiMTY0LTNiODAtNDYyNS1hYjdkLThkNzgyM2M1ZmE3ZDozZjgyYmM3NC1kOGJjLTQ2YmMtYTE3My0xODU3ZTU1YzIzOGM=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



One man is described as white, aged between 18 and 21, slim build, around 5ft 9ins tall with thick brown curly hair.

The second man is described as white, aged between 21 and 25, slim build, around 5ft 5 to 5ft 7ins tall, with facial stubble.

If you believe you can help, get in touch with police quoting crime reference number 14/120605/26.

Alternatively you can provide information anonymously via independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or www.crimestoppers-uk.org.
