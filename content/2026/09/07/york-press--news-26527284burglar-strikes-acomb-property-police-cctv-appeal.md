*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A burglar has struck at a property in York.

North Yorkshire Police has issued a photograph of a hooded figure they want to trace in relation to the incident in Manor Drive South, off York Road in Acomb, at 2.10am on Friday, August 28.

The force is urging anyone nearby with footage of the person, pictured wearing a face covering and gloves, to come forward.

“We are appealing for any footage from nearby, around this time, that may show the suspect without their face covering, or with their hood down,” a police spokesperson said.

Anyone with information is asked to email [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously via North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260170073 when passing on information to police or to Crimestoppers.
