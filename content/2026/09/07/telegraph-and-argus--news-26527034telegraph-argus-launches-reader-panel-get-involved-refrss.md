WOULD you like a chance to shape the news as well as read it?

The Telegraph & Argus is giving readers a chance to have a say in its coverage.

A new Reader Panel is being launched to give subscribers and readers a direct say in the stories that appear in the paper and online.

Panel members will be able to share their views on key issues, highlight stories and issues they would like us to cover and suggest ways to improve their local news brand.

Editor Nigel Burton explained: "If you would like to help shape the future of your local newspaper, we would love to hear from you.

"Join the Telegraph & Argus Reader Panel today and make your voice count."

Members will be invited to suggest topics for investigation through a regular “What should we investigate?” callout, with the best ideas followed up publicly by our reporting team.

Participants will be asked to complete a short monthly survey to tell us what they like - and what we could improve.

They will also be invited to attend quarterly forums, either online or in person, with editors and reporters to discuss topics affecting Bradford district and the wider region.

This is your chance to talk directly to the T&A reporting team.

If you would like to be part of the reader panel please email [nigel.burton@newsquest.co.uk](mailto:nigel.burton@newsquest.co.uk) and mark your email subject 'Reader panel'.
