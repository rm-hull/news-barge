An 84-year-old Yorkshire woman is preparing to trek through the mountains of Albania to raise money for Marie Curie.

Liz Madden from Bingley, will spend five days walking through the remote Zagoria Valley later this month, covering up to 10 miles a day and tackling steep climbs and descents as part of the charity challenge.

The former nurse said her love of walking has taken her around the world, but she is now taking on one of her latest adventures in support of a cause close to her heart.

"I love walking," Ms Madden said. "Walking keeps me fit, keeps me active and has given me wonderful friendships and adventures around the world."

## Raising money in memory of her mother

Ms Madden is fundraising for Marie Curie after the charity cared for her mother at the end of her life.

She said the support provided to her family left a lasting impression.

Ms Madden explained: "The kindness, compassion and dignity they gave Mum, and the support they gave our family, is something I will never forget, so now it's my turn to give something back."

The charity provides end-of-life care and support to people living with terminal illnesses and their loved ones across the UK.

## A lifetime of adventure

Despite being 84, Ms Madden shows no signs of slowing down.

Over the years she has completed challenges across the globe, including treks in Peru, Iceland, India and Borneo.

One of her most memorable adventures was a jungle trek in Borneo, where she was told she was the oldest participant ever to complete the challenge.

"I was 83 at the time," she said. "There were parts of the jungle where it would have been very easy to fall, but I managed it.The local guides were extremely impressed."

Ms Madden also completed the famous trek to Machu Picchu in Peru, describing the moment she watched the sunrise over the ancient Incan site as "inspiring".

"You look down and can't believe how big it is," she said. "It's absolutely amazing."

## Inspired by a friend

Ms Madden said her passion for trekking began after a friend encouraged her to take on the Yorkshire Three Peaks challenge.

At first, she doubted whether she could complete it.

"I didn't think I could do it," she said. "But we did it in just over 10 hours and I realised I could do these kinds of challenges."

The experience sparked a love of adventure that continues more than three decades later.

## Ready for Albania

The upcoming trek will take Ms Madden through some of Albania's most dramatic scenery, including mountain trails, traditional villages and waterfalls.

Participants will stay in guesthouses hosted by local families while tackling daily walks ranging from four to nine miles.

While many people her age might be slowing down, Ms Madden is focused on training and preparing for the challenge ahead.

"Every training walk reminds me why I'm doing it," she said. "I'm doing it for Mum, for Marie Curie and for all the families who need their incredible care."

She added: "I love the camaraderie, the laughter and the friendships that come with every trek."
