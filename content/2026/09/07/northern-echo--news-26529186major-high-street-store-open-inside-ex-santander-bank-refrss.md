Residents of Northallerton have expressed excitement as a major fashion retailer is coming to the high street.

Recently, advertising for a new branch of clothing and lifestyle brand Joules was spotted on an empty high street unit.

The window signage has appeared at the former Santander branch, announcing an October 2026 opening, with job vacancies appearing online, inciting further interest.

A snap, which has gained hundreds of likes and comments, was posted on Love Northallerton's Facebook page, and said: "Exciting news!

"Joules will be coming to our lovely High Street this October!

"What a great addition to Northallerton and wonderful news that a large, empty premises is being utilised which is keeping vacancy rates low for our town."

The store, located next to Natwest on High Street, has been empty since May 2026.

Earlier this year, Santander announced its closure due to declining in-store customers and the growing use of online banking.

Northallerton’s high street was voted one of the best in Britain by the Times’ readers in 2024, and has consistently shown resilience amidst a national decline in in-store shopping.

Northallerton residents have welcomed the development after it was shared online, with many grateful that the number of empty high street units is decreasing.

One local praised the arrival on Facebook: "Love the childrenswear at Joules! Great addition to the town, their sales are always great too!"

Another Northallerton resident added: "Brilliant addition to the high street! Especially as there are plenty of equestrian riders in the area and surrounding villages!"

One other said: "It’s good to see empty units being taken on.

"We live 25 miles north of the town but choose to shop in Northallerton because it has a tremendous range of stores."

Other residents remained skeptical of Joules’ mid-range pricing, calling for more affordable brands in the area.

One said: “Another expensive shop! Just what the high street needs! No wonder charity shops are getting busier.”

Joules has been approached for further comment.
