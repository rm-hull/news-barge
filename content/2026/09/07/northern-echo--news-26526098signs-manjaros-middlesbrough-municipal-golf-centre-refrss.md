Anticipation is growing over a new chain restaurant coming to Middlesbrough as signs go up and jobs are advertised.

Manjaros Kitchen & Cocktails is opening a restaurant at Middlesbrough Municipal Golf Centre on Ladgate Lane.

The Afro-Caribbean restaurant and cocktail bar is recruiting for its new Marton venue, with roles including waiting staff, bartenders, managers and receptionists.

  Anticipation is growing as Manjaros is set to open on Ladgate Lane at Middlesbrough Municipal Golf Centre as signs go up and jobs are advertised 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

A recruitment post said: "We're Hiring for Our New Marton Restaurant!

"Manjaros Kitchen & Cocktails is opening a brand-new restaurant in Marton, and we're looking for passionate, enthusiastic people to join our opening team."

The company added: "This is an exciting opportunity to be part of a brand-new restaurant from day one, working in a fast-paced and friendly environment while helping us deliver an exceptional experience for our guests."

Hospitality or customer-facing experience is preferred, while management experience is required for managerial roles.

Reports of a Manjaros restaurant opening at the site first emerged in June 2025.

  What the new Manjaros building used to look like 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

At the time, a trusted source told The Northern Echo the restaurant would be located in the main building of the golf club, with golf-related activities expected to move to the outhouse.

Manjaros is known for its African-Caribbean food and signature dishes, including the Teesside favourite parmo.

The brand launched in Middlesbrough in 2012 and has since expanded across the UK.

It now has sites in Darlington, Middlesbrough, Ingleby Barwick, Billingham, Sunderland, Newcastle, Leeds, Birmingham, Ilford, Preston, Huddersfield, Southall, Leyton, Brick Lane and Leicester.

The chain also delivers in Middlesbrough, Hartlepool and Redcar.
