A 12-year-old boy died from a severe traumatic brain injury after falling through the roof of a farm building, an inquest heard.

Jaxon Robert Harker, from Bishop Auckland, suffered fatal injuries during an incident at Woodhouses on July 24.

The 12-year-old was taken to the Newcastle’s Royal Victoria Infirmary but tragically passed away four days later.

An inquest looking into the circumstances surrounding his death was opened at Crook Coroner's Court on Friday.

Dr Kumar, a doctor at the Royal Victoria Infirmary, in Newcastle, recorded Jaxon’s cause of death as severe traumatic brain injury following cardio-respiratory arrest and polytrauma.

Jaxon had been with a group of friends when the tragedy happened.

Jaxon's body was formally identified on July 29 in accordance with County Durham and Darlington Foundation Trust hospital protocol.

It is understood that police are now working alongside the Health and Safety Executive to establish the circumstances surrounding the incident.

Following Jaxon's death, his family thanked those who had supported them and urged parents to keep their children away from the area.
