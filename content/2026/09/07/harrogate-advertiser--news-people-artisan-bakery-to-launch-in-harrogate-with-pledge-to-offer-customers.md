# Artisan bakery to launch in Harrogate with pledge to offer customers 'best coffee you'll have ever tasted'

## A new artisan cafe is to open soon in Harrogate promising to offer customers the “the best coffee you'll have ever tasted”.

Located at 26 Mayfield Grove in Harrogate, Meraki Artisan Baker will be a high-quality bakery and cafe with artisan breads, sandwiches and fine coffee.

The much-anticipated new independent business takes over a spot which has been empty since November last year when Decoris, a preloved goods shop, closed down after 18 months.

The arrival of Meraki Artisan Baker adds more strength to the independent bakery sector in Harrogate which also includes Bakeri Baltzersens and The Secret Bakery.

The owners are refurbishing the unit in time for the launch of Meraki Artisan Baker.
