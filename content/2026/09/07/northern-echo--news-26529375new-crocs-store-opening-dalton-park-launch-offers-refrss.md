A major shoe retailer will launch a new store at Dalton Park shopping centre very soon.

Opening in Seaham, the store will be the footwear company's second standalone store in the North East and will be located at the outlet shopping centre.

Crocs have said they are opening the store in the autumn.


To celebrate the new opening, Crocs is offering a 20 per cent discount across the opening weekend.

Customers who spend £60 or more will also get five free Jibbitz charms - customisable charms that attach to Crocs shoes.

The shop will sell clogs, sandals, slippers and accessories for adults and children.

A variety of styles and colours will be available, along with a wide selection of Jibbitz charms.

Dalton Park shared the news of Crocs opening on social media, telling shoppers to "get ready for classic clogs, standout shades and customisable Jibbitz charms to make every pair your own".

   

*(Image: Dalton Park)*

The Crocs shop adds to Dalton Park’s growing retail line-up.

Dalton Park will also welcome Badge, an independent North East menswear retailer.
