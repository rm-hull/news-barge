A family-run bakery is in the running to be named the best in Bradford for the third year in a row.

G&J Pitt Family Baker’s, based on Swain House Road, has once again been shortlisted in the Telegraph & Argus Best Bakery competition.

## A brief history of the business

The bakery has been run by the Pitt family since 1985, when Geoff and Janet Pitt took over the business.

Their son Stephen joined the team 20 years ago and has helped run the bakery ever since.

Stephen said: "My mum and dad bought the shop in 1985.

"I came into the business about twenty years ago.

"My kids might too, in the future."

## Community ties lead to success

Stephen believes that the bakery’s close ties with the community are at the heart of its success.

He said: "It’s the relationship between the shop staff and our customers that makes it special.

"We are a community place, doing a job since 1985."

## Chosen to stay offline

Despite the growing importance of online promotion, the bakery has chosen to stay offline.

Stephen said: "We have no online presence – no Facebook.

"I did have a Facebook account, but I was getting messages at six in the morning asking how much six tea cakes cost.

"And I felt pressured to reply."

## 'Very local customer base'

Instead, the bakery relies on its reputation and word-of-mouth from loyal customers, many of whom have been coming in for generations.

Stephen said: "We have a very, very local customer base.

   

*(Image: Newsquest)*

"Generations of the same family have been coming in for years.

"We get a real good crop of new customers, but at the same time, a lot of locals come into me and say things like, ‘my grandparents used to come here’.

"There’s definitely a nostalgic element for a lot of the younger generations.

"And, being a family bakery, that makes a lot of sense."

## 'The nomination is the important thing'

He is proud of the nomination but would be pleased to see another bakery take the top spot this year.

Mr Pitt said: "The nomination is the important thing.

"Among the businesses on the list, it would be nice to see somebody else win.

"A lot of them aren't as established as we are."
