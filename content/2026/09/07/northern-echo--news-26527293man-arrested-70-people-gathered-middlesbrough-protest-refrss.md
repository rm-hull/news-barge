A man has been arrested after dozens of people descended on Middlesbrough town centre for a 'save our town' peaceful protest.

About 70 people gathered in Centre Square on Saturday, September 5 for the demonstration, which lasted for about two hours.

The event was advertised online as a 'save our town' peaceful protest.

One man was arrested on suspicion of racially aggravated public order and has been released on conditional bail.
