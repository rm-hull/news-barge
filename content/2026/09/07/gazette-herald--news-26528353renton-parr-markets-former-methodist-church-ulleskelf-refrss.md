A former church in a North Yorkshire village is up for sale.

Located in Ulleskelf, the former Methodist church is being promoted as a development opportunity.

The unlisted venue was built in the late 19th Century and has been closed for worship since 2024.

The building, which is around 3,000 square feet, is on Church Fenton Lane and is described as "characterful".

Estate Agents Renton & Parr said the former church "offers a rare opportunity to acquire a substantial and characterful property with excellent potential for a variety of alternative uses, subject to the necessary consents".

It retains many period features typical of its original ecclesiastical use, including arched windows, high ceilings, and stone detailing.

The property is within easy reach of local amenities and transport links.

Ulleskelf, which is between Tadcaster and Selby, has a railway station providing direct services to York and Leeds and is conveniently located for access to the A64, A1(M), and wider regional road network.

The site offers significant development potential, either for conversion to a residential dwelling, community use, or other commercial or leisure purposes, subject to planning, the agents added.
