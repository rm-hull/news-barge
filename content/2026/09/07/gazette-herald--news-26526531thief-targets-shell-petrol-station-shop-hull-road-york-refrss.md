A THIEF has targeted a petrol station shop in York.

North Yorkshire Police said the theft happened at the Shell Garage in Hull Road on Tuesday, July 28.

The force issued a CCTV image of a man it said it would like to speak to, saying he may have information which will assist its investigation.

  


A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured.

"Email [ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk) if you can help.

"Please quote reference 12260146717 when passing on information."

Alternatively, you can visit the North Yorkshire Police website, or call the force on 101, or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.
