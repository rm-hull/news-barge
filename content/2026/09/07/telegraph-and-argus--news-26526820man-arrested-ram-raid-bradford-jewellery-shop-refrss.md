*(Image: CCTV - Pakistani Jewellers)*

A man has been arrested after a ram-raid at a Bradford jewellers which left the shopfront smashed and a customer injured.

Officers were called to Pakistan Jewellers on Duckworth Lane at 1.19pm on Saturday, September 5, following reports that a Nissan Navara had been deliberately driven into the front of the premises.

According to West Yorkshire Police, a number of people then entered the shop and attempted to steal jewellery.

## 
  **Arrest made**

A 38-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of aggravated burglary.

Police said he has since been bailed pending further enquiries.

Investigations into the incident are continuing and detectives from Bradford CID are appealing for information.

## 
  **Customer suffered minor injuries**

A 47-year-old woman who was inside the shop at the time as a customer was treated for minor injuries.

Police have not released further details about her injuries.

## 
  **Shop remains boarded up**

When the Telegraph & Argus visited the scene at around 9am on Sunday, September 6, Pakistan Jewellers remained shuttered and boarded up.

Debris, including wooden planks, was still visible outside the premises.

The front window, which was smashed during the ram raid, appeared to have been covered over, while police tape remained in place on the road outside.

## 
  **Appeal for information**

West Yorkshire Police is continuing to investigate the incident.

Anyone who witnessed the ram raid, or has information that could assist enquiries, is asked to contact police via 101 or through the live chat service on the West Yorkshire Police website, quoting reference 13260506012.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

## 
  **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A spokesperson said: "Detectives investigating a ram raid at a jewellers on Duckworth Lane, Bradford on Saturday have made an arrest.

"Officers were called at 1.19pm on Saturday (5 September) to reports a Nissan Navara has deliberately driven into the shop front on Duckworth Lane.

"A number of people entered the jewellers and attempted to steal a quantity of jewellery.

"A 38 year old man was arrested on suspicion of aggravated burglary and has since been bailed pending further enquiries.

"A 47 year old woman who was a customer in the shop at the time, was treated for minor injuries.

"Enquiries remain ongoing by Bradford CID.

"Anyone who witnessed the incident or has any information is asked to contact police via 101 or use the Live Chat Options at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 13260506012

"Information can always be given anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111"
