A flash flood that caused “a danger for any pedestrian or road users” is to be investigated.

Yorkshire Water have said they are to probe the incident, which saw a significant flood in Heslington.

It happened after the storm which brought torrential rain over the August Bank Holiday weekend.

Parts of Main Street and Heslington Lane were underwater as the sewers and drains failed to cope with downpour on Monday 31 August.

Now Lib Dem campaigners Andrew Mortimer and Luke Draper have written to both Yorkshire Water and City of York Council to ask about the situation.

Andrew said “Long parts of Heslington Lane and Main Street from the golf course up to Heslington Hall were inches deep in water which in many places stretched right across the road.

“It also pooled heavily near the Post Office creating large areas of standing water.

“I have seen water seeping from drains in the past, but never to the extent it did over the Bank Holiday.

“We have asked the council and Yorkshire Water to explain what happened this time and what they plan to do to prevent a repeat.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/andrew-mortimer-and-luke-draper.webp)

“Yorkshire Water have replied to say they will send someone out to have a look at the situation.”

Luke said “On parts of Main Street there was so much water coming out from the drains and sewers that it lifted many of the large manhole covers.

“Not only did this create the flooding problem but more worryingly a danger for any pedestrian or road users who were not able to see the open covers because of the standing water.”

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Water said they would be investigating if there was a specific problem, or if it was a case of the downpour just overwhelming capacity.

Surface water drainage is generally the responsibility of the council, but surface water drains interact with Yorkshire Water sewers so that’s why it is going to investigate.

We have also asked City of York Council for their response.
