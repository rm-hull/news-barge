The line between York and Scarborough is blocked this afternoon (Monday).

There is a safety inspection of the track between Scarborough and York, blocking some lines.

And there’s another safety inspection between Scarborough and Filey means trains have to run at a reduced speed.

National Rail says: “Trains between Scarborough and York / Bridlington may be cancelled or revised.

“Disruption is expected until 7.30pm.”

The disruption affects Northern and TransPennine Express services.

**TransPennine** says customers can use their ticket at no extra cost to travel on Coastliner bus services travelling in both directions.

**Northern** says road transport has been recruited with York Pullman to run as follows:

- 4.30pm Scarborough calling at all stations to Filey and then setting down as required to Bridlington
- 5.15pm Filey to Scarborough, calling at all stations
