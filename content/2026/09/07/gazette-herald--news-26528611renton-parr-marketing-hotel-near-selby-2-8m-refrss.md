A HOTEL on the A63 between Leeds and Selby is up for sale for £2.8m.

The Best Western Plus Milford Hotel, on the Great North Road, South Milford, has been put on the market.

The hotel, which is close to the A1(M) and approximately 12 miles West of Selby and 15 miles East of Leeds, is being sold as a freehold.

The two-storey building, which sits on a site of about one acre, features 47 en-suite bedrooms, three small function rooms, and a large restaurant and bar area with seating for up to 100 customers.

The property also includes ancillary areas such as a catering kitchen, customer toilets, an office, a laundry room, and storerooms.

Outside, the hotel has parking for around 80 vehicles.

The Best Western Plus Milford Hotel is currently owned by a large hotel company and is run under management.

However, the listing states that the hotel presents an opportunity for a new owner who can be more involved in the day-to-day operations to "significantly improve" the business's performance.

The listing continued: "The hotel's very central location and quantum of bedrooms (46) provide an excellent opportunity to run a very successful operation under the right ownership.

"Good-sized urban hotels in relatively close proximity to Leeds rarely become available, so we expect significant interest to be shown in the property."

  A bedroom at the Best Western Plus

The property, which has an energy rating of B, also offers potential for alternative use, subject to planning permission.

The hotel's accounts for the year ending March 31, 2026, show net of VAT sales of £732,000, with room sales making up around £550,000 per annum.

The listing is being handled by Christie & Co.

Prospective buyers are invited to make offers of £2,800,000.

TripAdvisor rates the venue 4.5 stars out of five, based on 1,509 reviews.
