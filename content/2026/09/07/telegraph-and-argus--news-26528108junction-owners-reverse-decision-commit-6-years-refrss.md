THE owners of a popular Bradford district pub have revealed they almost walked away from the business before deciding to commit to another six years.

The Junction, on Bingley Road, Baildon, had reached a break clause in its lease, leaving its operators with a choice between leaving or signing up for a further six years.

After initially handing in their notice, they ultimately changed their minds and announced they would be staying.

## Hospitality challenges sparked uncertainty

Beth and Nathan High, owners of The Junction, said reaching the break clause forced them to make a major decision about the pub's future.

They said: "If we're completely honest, we panicked.

"Hospitality is a frightening industry to be in at the moment.

"Costs are rising, every week can feel uncertain, and six years suddenly felt like a very long commitment."

The couple said that, at the time, handing in notice on the lease felt like the safest option.

  Nathan and Beth High, owners of The Junction in Baildon 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## More than just a business

However, the owners said the reality of leaving soon made them reconsider.

They said: "In nearly three years, we've built so much more than a business.

"We have an incredibly loyal group of regular customers, we sponsor Shipley Town FC and Baildon Cricket Club, we have a fantastic darts team and an amazing quiz host who runs our Thursday night quiz every week."

Beth and Nathan added that the pub had become a place where staff and customers alike felt welcome.

## Customers' reactions changed everything

The owners said the reaction from customers was a major factor in their decision to stay.

They said: "When we started telling customers we were leaving, seeing their reactions was devastating."

They also reflected on the effect the closure could have had on staff, who would have needed to find alternative employment.

The owners added: "We wrote the announcement telling everyone we were leaving several times, but we could never bring ourselves to actually post it.

"In the end, I think that told us everything we needed to know."

## Staying for the next six years

After further discussions, the couple decided to reverse their decision and remain at the pub.

In a Facebook post announcing the news, they told customers: "We couldn't leave The Junction."

They added: "We're staying, we're committing to the next six years and we're ready to give this next chapter everything we've got."

## Appeal to support local pubs

The couple also used the announcement to encourage people to support local hospitality businesses.

They said: "If there's one message we'd really like people to take from our experience, it's to support your local pub."

They added: "Pubs are facing an incredibly difficult time and they can only survive with the support of the communities around them."
