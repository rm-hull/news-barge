# Father denies murdering one-month-old son in Yorkshire in 2020

## A father accused of murdering his newborn baby boy more than six years ago has denied the charge and will stand trial.

Niall Worsfold appeared at Leeds Crown Court this morning (Sep 7) over a video link from HMP Leeds where he is being held on remand.

The 29-year-old formally entered a not-guilty plea. He also entered a not-guilty to a charge of child cruelty.

Worsfold, of Farrow Road, Armley, will now stand trial in February 22 next year. It is expected to last up to four weeks.

![Worsfold is charged with murdering his one-month-old baby following an incident on Lincombe Drive six years ago.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/13/7/17/Worsfold.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The child was admitted to hospital on July 4, 2020 from an address in Lincombe Drive, Gledhow, and died six days later.
