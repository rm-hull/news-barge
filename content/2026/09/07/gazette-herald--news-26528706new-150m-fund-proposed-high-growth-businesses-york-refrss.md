A new investment fund of up to £150m is proposed to support high-growth businesses across the North of England.

The British Business Bank has announced plans for a Northern scale-up fund designed to fuel the expansion of innovative companies with high growth potential.

The fund will target businesses across multiple cities, including York.

Leandros Kalisperas, chief investment officer at the British Business Bank, said: "This new Northern scale-up fund reflects the Bank’s commitment to addressing regional equity gaps and helping ambitious, innovative businesses in the North of England to have the same opportunity to access the capital they need to grow and thrive as other parts of the UK.

"The proposed fund would provide an important new source of scale-up capital building on the success of our Northern Powerhouse Investment Fund II, which continues to deliver support to smaller businesses of up to £5m of capital."

The fund is expected to offer investments of between £5 million and £15 million and is intended to complement the bank’s existing regional programmes, including the Northern Powerhouse Investment Fund II and the Regional Angels Programme.

It aims to attract additional private investment and support university spinouts and other high-growth firms.

Investment priorities will align with regional sector strengths, such as creative industries, clean energy, and space and technology.

Chancellor of the Exchequer John Healey is expected to welcome the announcement in a speech today (September 7).

He is expected to say: "Today I can announce a new £150m allocation from the British Business Bank to lever in more capital behind the most innovative and fast-growing firms in the North of England."

The announcement coincides with the launch of the £210 million South East Investment Fund in Brighton and forms part of the British Business Bank’s broader strategy to improve access to finance across the UK.
