Christmas is coming to York Theatre Royal – and we met the spellbinding cast of *Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs*.

The family pantomime will star Marcavia McCarthy, CBBC and CBeebies’ Richard David-Caine, Jenny Gayner, alongside York panto favourites Tommy Carmichael, Christian Mortimer, and Robin Simpson – who return after delighting audiences in last year’s *Sleeping Beauty*.

Alongside the all-important Snow White, the cast of the fabulous Seven Dwarfs will be led by Dean Whatton – with Lucy Blenkiron, Richard Blenkiron, Willie Coppen, Stijn Keuleers, Maria Popska and Harrison Taylor joining the YTR panto family this Christmas.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/snow-white-2026-ytr-cast-2.webp)

Robin Simpson is once again returning as the Dame, following a fab run of of main stage pantomime appearances including *Aladdin, Jack and the Beanstalk*, and* Sleeping Beauty*.

“This is the first time that *Snow White* has ever been done at York Theatre Royal ever,” said Robin. “So that’s quite an incredible achievement. 

“I’m really looking forward to getting in front of the York audiences again because they’re absolutely fantastic.

“There’s no other theatrical form where you can just have that direct connection with the audience, and you can see their faces, and they feel comfortable enough to speak to you.”

“This is my first time in York, and it’s beautiful – I’m very excited” said Marcavia, who’s playing the titular Snow White.

“I like how brave she is,” she said. “The fact that she’s got a henchman and her evil stepmother coming after her, but she makes the choice to go into an unknown wilderness to try and survive and makes a life for herself.”

And this year’s panto stars CBBC and CBeebies’ Richard David-Caine, as Herman the Henchman.

“The great thing about being a henchman is you get to be the cheeky one that comes in, banters with the audience, gets some laughs, and then goes back to his dressing room,” said Richard.

“I believe there’s going to be a great little mega mix involved somewhere, but that’s all I’m going to say…

“The world is a hard place at the moment, and at the end of the year, we get to come together as a family and just have a good laugh and be entertained and and celebrate live local entertainment.

“There’s something very immediate and present and local about it, and I love being a part of that.”

Juliet Forster, creative director at York Theatre Royal, said: “We are so delighted to have such a brilliant cast lined up for the show.

It is the first time *Snow White* has been performed on our stage and audiences can expect something really special! 

“I can’t wait to get started on rehearsals and see this fabulous show come to life with this amazing team.”

The show is written by Paul Hendy, directed by Juliet Forster and co-produced with award-winning Evolution Productions

*Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs* is at York Theatre Royal from **4 December 2026 to 3 January 2027**. With lavish costumes, stunning sets, hilarious jokes and dazzling special effects, this will be an unmissable treat for the whole family.

Family tickets are available for all performances with savings of up to £61 on bookings with 4 tickets. Find out more on the York Theatre Royal website.
