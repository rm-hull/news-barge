A County Durham off licence has been ordered to close for three months after thousands of illegal cigarettes were seized.

More than 9,000 illicit cigarettes were found at The Avenue off licence on Deneside in Seaham and a vehicle linked to the shop.

Trading standards officers found 215 packets of cigarettes - 4,360 illicit individual cigarettes - and 44 pouches of hand-rolling tobacco weighing 2.2 kilograms in an outbuilding behind the shop after a tip-off in July.

A warrant was also executed on a vehicle suspected to be linked to the off licence.

An additional 242 cigarette packets (4,840 cigarettes), 64 pouches of hand-rolling tobacco weighing 3.2kg, and a set of keys for the shop were found.

Magistrates in Newton Aycliffe granted the closure order, meaning the shop must stay shut until December 2.

The owner did not attend court and was ordered to pay £1,384 in costs.

Owen Cleugh, Durham County Council’s safer places manager, said: "Illegal tobacco is far from a victimless crime.

"It can put young people at risk by increasing access to tobacco products, while generating profits for organised crime groups operating across the region and beyond.

"At the same time, it reduces tax income that funds essential public services and undermines law-abiding local businesses.

"This closure order reflects the seriousness with which these offences are treated.
