Two people have been discharged from hospital after a fatal A1(M) crash near Darlington.

Emergency services descended on the southbound side of the major road just after 11.30am on Friday (September 4) after three vehicles were involved in a crash.

The serious smash left drivers facing hours of delays, with the road being shut in both directions between Junction 57 and the Burtree Interchange at Junction 58.

Durham Police confirmed on Friday that the driver of the Mokka, a man in his 60s, was sadly pronounced dead at the scene despite the best efforts of emergency services.

His family are being supported by specially trained officers.

Meanwhile, two other people were taken to the Darlington Memorial Hospital for further treatment - and the force has today (September 7) confirmed that they have since been discharged.

It is understood they were mainly taken to hospital as a precaution.

Police, firefighters, ambulances and an air ambulance were among the large-scale response following the incident, which saw the road closed for several hours.

Officers are keen to speak to anyone who witnessed the incident, or who may have dashcam footage of the vehicle prior to the crash.

Anyone with information is asked to contact police’s serious collisions investigation unit on 0191 375 2996 quoting incident number 154 of September 4.
