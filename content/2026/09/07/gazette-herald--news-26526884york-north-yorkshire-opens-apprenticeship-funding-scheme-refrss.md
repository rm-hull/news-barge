A new apprenticeship scheme is now available to employers in York and North Yorkshire.

The Apprenticeship Levy Transfer Scheme, launched by the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority, aims to make apprenticeship funding more accessible to businesses.

The scheme can cover all of the costs of training and assessments for eligible businesses, helping them invest in skills development and long-term business growth.

David Skaith, the Mayor of York and North Yorkshire, said: "Apprenticeships are a great way to learn the skills you need for work, at work.

"Helping more of our young people get the tools they need to build a fulfilling career closer to home.

"It's great for the apprentices and the businesses, but we know the costs of training can often hold smaller businesses back from creating these vital opportunities.

"That's why we're backing this new scheme, which can cover all of the costs of training and assessments for eligible businesses, with applications open today."

The new scheme forms part of the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority's commitment to building an integrated, responsive skills system that supports economic growth across the region.

By removing financial barriers, it aims to help employers develop the skilled workforce they need while enabling more people to start or progress their careers through apprenticeships.

Sara Jeffs, Director of People and Transformation at York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority, said: "We are pleased to open applications for funding businesses across York and North Yorkshire through our apprenticeship levy transfer scheme.

"At York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority, we are committed to creating opportunities for local people whilst also supporting local employers to invest in developing the skills they need within their workforce.

"We welcome applications from businesses that meet the funding criteria and share our commitment to creating local opportunities and building a thriving region."

The scheme will support apprenticeship training and assessment costs through levy transfers, allowing employers to offer fully funded apprenticeships.

Both businesses and levy-paying employers who have already committed their own levy funds are encouraged to explore the available support.

Applications will be considered subject to the availability of funds and the published eligibility criteria.

Employers in York and North Yorkshire who meet the criteria are now invited to apply.

By increasing access to apprenticeship funding, York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority is helping employers invest in the future of their workforce.

For more information or to apply, visit yorknorthyorks-ca.gov.uk/project/apprenticeship-levy-transfer.
