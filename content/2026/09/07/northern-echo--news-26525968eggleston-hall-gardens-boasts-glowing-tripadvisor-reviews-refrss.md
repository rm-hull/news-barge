A County Durham garden centre and café has received glowing reviews from visitors.

Eggleston Hall Gardens, near Barnard Castle, boasts 4.3 stars from 361 TripAdvisor reviews and 4.5 stars from 779 Google reviews.

Described as a "magical" place, the gardens are a major draw for plant lovers, with visitors praising its unusual plants, artistic displays and knowledgeable horticulturalists.

The historic gardens date back to the late 16th century and include a former private chapel, with some of the earliest tombstones dating to 1607.

The chapel closed in 1868, fell into disrepair and was later transformed into the peaceful feature seen today.

The gardens also have a plant nursery, where visitors praise the quality and say prices are a fraction of those at garden centres.

The gardens were redesigned in the Italian style during a Victorian-era renaissance, when heated glasshouses were also introduced.

More recently, two Victorian glasshouses were rebuilt, shade houses and beehives were added, and a Pottager Garden, new nursery beds and an upgraded sales area were created.

Visitors can explore the landscaped gardens year-round, while the café and gift shop open from March until Christmas.

Eggleston Hall Gardens is on Eggleston Lane, Barnard Castle, DL12 0AG.

The gardens are open from 9am to 5pm Monday to Saturday and from 10am to 4pm on Sundays.

The café is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays, while the site is dog-friendly.

The Coach House tearoom is housed in the hall's carriage house
