A Darlington man was caught with a four-inch kitchen knife in his pocket after plainclothes officers chased him down.

Joshua Norman was spotted with two electric bikes in an underpass near Shelley Road by officers patrolling the streets around 2.30am on Wednesday (September 2).

Norman tried to run when challenged, but was arrested and searched, where a four-inch kitchen knife was found in his jacket pocket.

  Joshua Norman's mugshot 

*(Image: DURHAM CONSTABULARY)*

The 31-year-old was brought into police custody, where he was charged with possession of a knife in a public place and two counts of vehicle interference.

  Joshua Norman during his arrest 

*(Image: DURHAM CONSTABULARY)*

He was also charged with two counts of fraud by false representation after using stolen bank cards to buy around £300 worth of cigarettes.

Norman, of Westmoreland Street, in Darlington, appeared at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court on Thursday, September 3, where he pleaded guilty to the offences.

He was jailed for 25 weeks, and the knife was also destroyed.

Following sentencing, Chief Inspector Rachel Hills, from Durham Constabulary, said: “This was a great piece of proactive policing by these officers, which has resulted in a prolific offender being put behind bars.”
