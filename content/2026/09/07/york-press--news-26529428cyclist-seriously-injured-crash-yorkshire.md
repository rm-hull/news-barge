A cyclist has been left seriously injured after a crash in North Yorkshire.

The man in his 50s came off his bicycle when travelling downhill on the B6161 Leathley Lane, near Harrogate, at about 4.45pm on Tuesday, August 11.

North Yorkshire Police said paramedics treated the man, from Otley, at the scene before he was taken to hospital by ambulance for urgent treatment.

“He is continuing to receive medical care,” a police spokesperson said on Monday (September 7).

They said the force is urging people who saw the crash or the cyclist before to come forward.

Police are also asking motorists with relevant dashcam footage to get in touch, the spokesperson said.

Anyone with information is asked to email [Conor.Taylor@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Conor.Taylor@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101 quoting reference number 12260155539.
