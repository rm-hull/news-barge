Five West Yorkshire firefighters have completed a 110-floor climb in the US in memory of colleagues killed in the 9/11 attacks.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service (WYFRS) took part in the Terry Farrell Firefighters’ Fund Memorial Stair Climb in Atlanta, Georgia, in memory of the 343 firefighters who died in the September 11 attacks.

This year will mark the 25th anniversary of the terrorist attacks carried out by Al-Qaeda.

The stair climb marked the height of the World Trade Centre in New York and saw participants ascend 110 floors in full fire kit in 38-degree heat.

*(Image: West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service)*

The team included Kev Sutcliffe, Zoe Ricketts, Spencer Priestley, Luke Garnett, and Matty Birch. They travelled to the United States to take part in the event alongside other firefighters from the UK and Ireland.

Kev Sutcliffe said: "It was an emotional and humbling experience."

Each firefighter carried a lanyard bearing the photo of a firefighter who died on September 11, 2001.

In describing the experience Zoe Ricketts said: "Really emotional, they had pictures of all the 343 firefighters in the twin towers displayed as we were doing it - was really hot but we all did it in full kit and we didn't stop."

Spencer Priestley said: "I'm absolutely shattered but it was an absolute privilege to do it - I need some refreshments now!"

WYFRS praised the team’s efforts in a Social Media post on Facebook, saying: "The team honoured those who lost their lives while serving others."

Their commitment has received recognition from event organisers, senior fire service leaders in both the US and UK, and families of New York firefighters who lost their lives on 9/11.

Those paying tribute included Chief Fire Officers from Cobb County Fire & Rescue Service (Atlanta) and the Deputy Chief Fire Officer from New York Fire Department.

The firefighters also visited the Atlanta Fire Department Training Facility and toured fire stations across the city.

The event raised funds for The Fire Fighters Charity, The Blue Light Card Foundation, and The Terry Farrell Firefighter Foundation.

The Terry Farrell Firefighter Foundation has helped hundreds of fire police and EMS departments with gear, tools and vehicles over the past 25 years. The organisation was formed in 2001 to support first responders in New York.

In September they will host their Annual Charity Outing which will memorialise the 25th Anniversary of the events of September 11, 2001.
