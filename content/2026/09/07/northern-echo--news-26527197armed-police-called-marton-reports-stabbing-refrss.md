A man was stabbed in an attack on a Middlesbrough street in the early hours of the morning.

Armed police descended on Holdenby Drive in Marton at about 2.45am on Sunday (September 6) where the man had suffered injuries to his head and leg.

Paramedics treated him before taking him to hospital. The seriousness of his injuries is not known.

Detective Chief Inspector Dan Cook, from Middlesbrough CID, said: “At 2.45am on Sunday, September 6, police were called to reports of the stabbing of a man on Holdenby Drive in Middlesbrough.

“The man suffered injuries to his head and leg and was treated by paramedics before being taken to hospital.

“Enquiries are ongoing and anyone with information or footage is asked to contact Cleveland Police on 101 quoting reference number 178915.”

A second person was also taken to hospital.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We were called at 2.39am on Sunday 6 September to an incident at a private address on Holdenby Drive Middlesbrough.

"Three ambulance resources attended alongside police and took two patients to hospital."
