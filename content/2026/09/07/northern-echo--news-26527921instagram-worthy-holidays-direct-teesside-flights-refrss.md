While many of us choose our holiday destinations based on adventure, relaxation or low prices, it can also be because the place is beautiful and aesthetically pleasing.

If your holiday isn't complete without the perfect photo, these stunning destinations are hailed as Instagram-worthy- and the best part is they're all accessible via direct flights from Teesside International Airport.

From colourful Mediterranean streets to iconic European waterfronts, there are plenty of picture-perfect spots waiting to fill up your camera roll this year.

Whether you're chasing sunsets, historic architecture or postcard-worthy views, here are six incredible locations that should be on every travel lover's list.

## Paleokastritsa, Corfu

  The picturesque spot is worth a visit 

*(Image: TripAdvisor)*

Located on Corfu, Paleokastritsa is known for crystal-clear waters, dramatic cliffs and lush green surroundings.

Viewpoints overlooking the bays provide striking backdrops for photographs, with scenery that looks almost too good to be real.

## Palma Cathedral, Palma de Mallorca

  Palma de Mallorca Cathedral La Seu, Spain 

*(Image: Teesside International Airport)*

In Palma de Mallorca, the majestic Palma Cathedral dominates the city skyline.

One of the most Instagrammable views is from Parc de la Mar, where reflective waters frame the Gothic landmark, particularly around sunrise or sunset.

## Santa Cruz neighbourhood stairs, Alicante

  Alicante city panoramic aerial view with seaside, harbour and houses. Spanish city with beautiful beach. Travel and holidays concepts with a popular European summer destination. 

*(Image: WILLIAM PERUGINI)*

Alicante’s Santa Cruz neighbourhood is home to colourful, decorated staircases lined with brightly painted flower pots.

The cheerful setting has become one of the city’s most photographed attractions.

## Gibralfaro Castle, Malaga

  Malaga, Spain. Cityscape Topped View Of Malaga. Plaza De Toros De Ronda Bullring In Malaga, Spain. 

*(Image: Ryhor Bruyeu (Grisha Bruev / Grigory Bruev))*

Perched above Malaga, Gibralfaro Castle offers sweeping views across the city, harbour and coastline.

The historic fortress provides a spectacular backdrop for holiday photographs overlooking the Costa del Sol.

## Sunset in Ria Formosa, Faro

   

*(Image: Teesside International Airport)*

The Ria Formosa lagoon in Faro offers a memorable setting as the sun sets.

The protected natural park can become awash with orange, pink and purple shades, creating an attractive scene for photographers.

## Damrak canal houses, Amsterdam

  Houses in Amsterdam 

*(Image: Teesside International Airport)*

Amsterdam’s Damrak canal houses are among Europe’s most recognisable sights.

The colourful buildings reflected in the water create a classic Dutch postcard scene and a popular social media image.
