Bradford Traitors fans should know the start date of the new series of Celebrity Traitors may have been confirmed.

The second instalment of the celebrity spin-off show has dropped its first trailer but the starting date of the new series has not yet officially been announced.

However, during a live episode of The Rest is Entertainment podcast, Richard Osman may have let slip the show’s start date.


## Richard Osman may have confirmed the start date of Celebrity Traitors

Richard was joined by his podcast co-host Marina Hyde and Joe Marler, who featured on the first series of Celebrity Traitors and quickly became a fan favourite.

Joe was asked about the advice he would give to the new contestants and he replied: “What is it, September 23, the next series?"

Richard added: “By the way, to point out, it’s absolutely embargoed that it starts on September 23…

“Let’s talk about what we call the events of September 23 this year…

“The cat’s out of the bag, isn’t it? Not to repeat it."

Joe talked more about his experience on the show and how everyone was really “enjoying” it.

He said: “I believe it was a slightly different emotion than our experience.

"Ours was very laugh-led, and everyone was sort of enjoying catching up, you know, pratting about to the point where they [the producers] went: ‘Guys, we’re trying to play a game.’

"Whereas this lot really want it from the start, and then I guess they see how well the first series has gone and there’s a little bit of pressure on them to be like: 'Oh, I’d quite like a bit of that.'"
