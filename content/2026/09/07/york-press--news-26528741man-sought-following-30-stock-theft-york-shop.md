A THIEF has struck at a York shop and made off with £30 of stock.

North Yorkshire Police said the theft took place at a store in Fossgate on Monday, August 17.

  



The county's police force has issued a CCTV image of a man the force said it would like to speak to, saying he may have information that will assist its investigation.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Email [ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk) if you can help."

Information can also be provided via the police force's website, or by calling North Yorkshire on 101, or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Police asked anybody providing information to quote reference 12260160388.
