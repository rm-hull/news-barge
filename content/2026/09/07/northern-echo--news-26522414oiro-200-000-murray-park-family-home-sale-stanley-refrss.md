SITUATED on Murray Park in Stanley, this impressive home offers a combination of contemporary styling, practical features and generous living space that makes it particularly well suited to modern family life.

The accommodation, which is arranged over three floors, starts at the entrance hall, with cloakroom off, and continues into an open-plan kitchen and living area at the heart of family life, with ample room for relaxing, dining and entertaining.

The kitchen is stylishly appointed with modern white high-gloss fitted units providing a clean and modern backdrop to the open-plan space and a breakfast bar perfect for informal family meals.

Adjoining this is the living area which features a focal point media wall and has French doors opening directly into the garden.

These allow plenty of natural light to flow into the room.

Off the spacious first floor landing are the family bathroom and three bedrooms, one of which is perfect for use as a child’s bedroom, guest room or home office.

Upstairs again is the principal bedroom suite, a private retreat separate from the rest of the house.

It encompasses a bedroom with attractive wall panelling and a useful walk-in wardrobe cupboard with integrated lighting, alongside which is a stylish fully tiled en suite bathroom that’s home to a P-shaped bath with a rainfall shower above.

The property’s rear garden has been designed with ease of maintenance in mind.

It is laid mainly to artificial lawn with a dedicated decked seating area which provides an attractive setting for outdoor dining, relaxing or entertaining during the warmer months.

An added bonus is the fact that it enjoys convenient access to a range of local amenities and schools in Stanley.
