Armed police have arrested a man in Teesside after reports that he was trying to break into a property with a knife.

Officers from Cleveland Police were called to Belle Vue Court in Norton at about 8.15pm on Sunday (September 6), following the alleged incident.

During the disturbance, a window of one of the properties on Belle Vue Court was smashed.

Police made searches for the man involved, with Cleveland Police since confirming that a 32-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of criminal damage.

He remains in police custody.

Detective Inspector Paul Edmonds, from Stockton CID, said: “At 8.15pm on Sunday, September 6, police were called to Belle Vue Court in Norton following reports of a man attempting to break into a property while armed with a knife.

"Officers attended the scene to find the windows had been smashed.

"Following a search of the area, a man, aged 32, was located and arrested on suspicion of criminal damage.

"He is currently in police custody for questioning.”
