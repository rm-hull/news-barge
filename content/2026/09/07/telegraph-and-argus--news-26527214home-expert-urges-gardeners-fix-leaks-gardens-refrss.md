As the autumn starts, the weather is likely to get a lot cooler and wetter, which should help revive plants in your garden.

However, the damper conditions can bring pests to your garden such as slugs, snails, and even frogs.

You might find frogs cute, but they can be quite noisy especially if there are a lot of them and can keep you awake at night.

A home expert from Angi, a platform that connects homeowners with local service professionals, has revealed the one thing homeowners should do to deter frogs from your garden.

  Frogs can be incredibly noisy if they shelter in your garden. 

*(Image: Getty)*

## How to keep frogs away from your garden

You might think frogs need a pond to survive but they can make any wet area their home.

Deirdre Sullivan from Angi has urged homeowners to do one thing this September.

She said: “Ensure proper drainage and fix leaky faucets to discourage frogs from seeking refuge in soggy areas.

“Also, eliminate stagnant water from containers, gutters, and other spots in your yard.

“Consider creating dry spots throughout your yard using stones and gravel—this will make your lawn less inviting.”

Any dripping water in your garden can cause frogs to shelter in the nearby area.

You should check any dripping taps, drains, gutters, birdbaths, and anywhere that rainwater can start to pool.

Fixing any of these leaks and damp areas can reduce the number of pests from coming into your garden, such as gnats, snails, water beetles as well as frogs.


### 
  **Other ways to get rid of damp areas in your garden**

You can also trim hedges, mow grass, and cut shrubs in your garden to prevent frogs from sheltering in the wet foliage.

If you place stones and gravel in areas, it can improve drainage but try to avoid doing this next to flowerbeds, as it can stop the plants’ roots from getting water.

Getting rid of damp areas around your garden is an easy, long-term solution to create a pest-free garden.

There are a few popular hacks online including using salt, vinegar, and lemon juice to keep frogs away but this can be harmful to them or kill them so it is best to avoid these methods.
