A new food stall serving up one of the North's favourite dishes has opened at Darlington Indoor Market.

Parmo Bros by TLC Gourmet launched on August 17, serving specialist chicken parmos and lunchtime deals.

The menu includes the 'Signature Classic Parmo 'and 'Hot Shot Parmo', as well as a 'Kids Parmo' option.

  Parmo Bros has opened in Darlington indoor market pictured Vinod Eslavath, owner Tom Leatherbarrow and Alex Wood 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Alex Wood, 35, from Newton Aycliffe, works for TLC Gourmet owner Thomas Leatherbarrow.

Alex said: "It was a no brainer really, I grew up here and there's a chance for people to grab food in the market. It's a nice environment with a variety of different food options but there was nothing like this with chicken.

"Parmos are a staple of the North and they are a fantastic product. It's nice to bring that to Darlington town centre.

"It's been going really well, we have had a lot of compliments and everyone is happy with their food - we have had really nice feedback."

  Parmo Bros has opened in Darlington indoor market 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

TLC Gourmet is part of TLC Catering which also caters for events and weddings.

Alex said the Darlington stall is TLC Catering’s first market venture, with more potentially on the way.

Adding: "This is the first market stall we have but we are planning to potentially do some more, maybe bringing pies to Darlington market too."

Parmo Bros is open from 11am to 4pm on Mondays and Thursdays to Sundays, but closed on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.
