**Harrogate Town have signed Bolton Wanderers goalkeeper Luke Hutchinson on a three-month loan deal.**

The 24-year-old spent last season on loan with Altrincham and made 44 appearances in the National League.

Hutchinson could make his debut for Town, who are second in the National League after six matches, against Tamworth on Saturday.
