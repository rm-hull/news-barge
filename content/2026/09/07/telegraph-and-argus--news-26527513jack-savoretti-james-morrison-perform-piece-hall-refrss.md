THE Piece Hall has announced its first headliner for next summer.

The announcement follows a record-breaking 2026 season at the iconic venue, which saw 225,000 tickets sold across 42 shows.

## Who is the headliner?

Jack Savoretti and James Morrison will perform at The Piece Hall on June 18, 2027, as part of a double-headline tour.

The Halifax date is one of seven outdoor performances on the tour, with the artists alternating who closes each night.

James Morrison will close the show in Halifax.

## 'Our courtyard is an incredibly special place'

Nicky Chance-Thompson, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "I’m really not sure how we’re going to top our 2026 season, but we’re certainly going to try.

"And what a wonderful first announcement for 2027 as we get ready to welcome two brilliant artists to Halifax who are both brand new to The Piece Hall.

"Steeped in history and stories, our courtyard is an incredibly special place for singer-songwriters, and I know they will love it here as much as we do.

"With our much-praised acoustics, audience intimacy, and stunning backdrop, I know this one will be emotional, in the very best way."

## Who are James Morrison and Jack Savoretti?

Jack Savoretti’s latest album, We Will Always Be The Way We Were, marks a return to his musical roots on the 20th anniversary of his debut album, Between the Minds.

The album showcases the raw storytelling and cinematic soul that have defined his career, which includes two number one albums and five UK Top 10 albums.

In 2026, Savoretti headlined a sold-out 20th anniversary show at the Royal Albert Hall and made a surprise appearance with KT Tunstall at the Isle of Wight Festival.

James Morrison, best known for his hit You Give Me Something, continues to captivate audiences with his soulful sound and emotional depth.

Over his two-decade career, Morrison has earned two UK number one albums, five UK Top 10 singles, multiple platinum records, and a BRIT Award.

Last autumn, his seventh album, Fight Another Day, reached the Top 5, marking his highest chart position in 14 years.

Morrison is also recognised as a powerful live performer who consistently sells out tours.
