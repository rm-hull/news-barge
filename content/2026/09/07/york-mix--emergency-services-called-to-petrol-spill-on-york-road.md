Both police and fire crews were called to a petrol spill on a York road this morning (Monday).

It happened on **Etty Avenue** at**5.30am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from York responded to a request from police to assist swilling away a road after a petrol spillage.

#### Etty Avenue, York

“Crews used one hose reel jet to wash the fuel away.”
