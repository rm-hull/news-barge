Tools have been stolen during a burglary at a home in York.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information about the incident in Clifton.

The force said the garage of the home was burgled at about 1.45am on Thursday, August 27.

“We are keen to hear from anyone who may have seen or heard anything suspicious at the time, or who may have relevant CCTV or doorbell footage,” a police spokesperson said.

Those with information are asked to email [emily.hill1@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:emily.hill1@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through its website.

Quote reference 12260167509 when passing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
