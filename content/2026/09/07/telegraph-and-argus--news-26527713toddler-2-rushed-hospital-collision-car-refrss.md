A TODDLER was rushed to hospital after a collision with a car in Bradford.

Emergency services were called to Derby Street, Little Horton, at around 7.30pm on Sunday evening.

A two-year-old was taken to hospital for treatment.

Their injuries were described as non-life threatening, a spokesperson for police confirmed.

Police said enquiries into the circumstances of the incident remain ongoing.

## Emergency services called to scene

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said it received the initial emergency call at 7.31pm reporting a collision between a car and a pedestrian on Derby Street.

An ambulance was dispatched to the scene and one patient was taken to hospital for further treatment.

## What police have said

A spokesperson for WYP said: “Police were called by the ambulance service to reports of a road traffic collision on Derby Street, Bradford at around 7:36 yesterday evening (Sunday 6 September).

“A child was found to have been in collision with a car and was taken to hospital to be treated for non-life-threatening injuries.

"Enquires remain ongoing.”

## What emergency services have said

A spokesperson for YAS said: “We received an emergency call at 7.31pm last night (6 September) to report a collision between a car and a pedestrian on Derby Street in Bradford. An ambulance was dispatched to the scene, and one patient was conveyed to hospital.”
