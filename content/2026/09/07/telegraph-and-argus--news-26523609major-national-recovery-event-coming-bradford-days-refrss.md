A NATIONAL event celebrating recovery from addiction is set to take place in Bradford this month.

The UK Recovery Walk Weekend will be hosted by Bradford’s Lived Experience Recovery Organisation (LERO), Create Strength Group, in partnership with Faces & Voices of Recovery UK (FAVOR UK).

Taking place from September 11 to 13, the event will bring together individuals in recovery, their families, professionals, mutual-aid groups, and community organisations.

Dave Memery said: "Excitement is building across Bradford and throughout the recovery community.

"This is much more than a walk.

"It is three days of learning, connection, remembrance, and celebration.

"Bradford is ready to offer a warm welcome to people from across the UK and beyond, showing the strength, hope, and possibility that recovery brings.

"We are proud to deliver this weekend alongside FAVOR UK, Bradford Council, and our many partner organisations and supporting individuals who have helped make it possible."

The weekend will feature a Recovery Conference on Friday, September 11, at Bradford Live, focusing on the role of lived experience in improving treatment and sustaining recovery outcomes.

Speakers will include Professor Dame Carol Black, Anne Marie Ward of FAVOR UK, and public health representatives from Bradford.

A spiritual service will follow at 6pm at Guru Gobind Singh Gurdwara, with a conference dinner at 7.30pm at Jinnah on Leeds Road.

The main event, the UK Recovery Walk, will take place on Saturday, September 12, starting and ending at Centenary Square, with the Recovery Village opening at 11am.

The one-mile walk through the city centre will begin at 12pm and finish back at Centenary Square, where the village will remain open until 5pm.

The Recovery Village will include stalls from recovery organisations, mutual-aid groups, and community partners, along with entertainment, creative performances, food, and activities for all ages.

Craig Blundred, Director of Public Health at Bradford Council and chair of Bradford’s Combating Alcohol and Drugs Partnership (CADP), said: "We’re proud to be working with our partners to deliver the UK Recovery Walk in Bradford.

"Visibility, the sharing of lived experience and the breaking down of stigmas are all vital parts of the recovery journey, and these events provide the perfect platform for all those things.

"The Recovery Village on the Saturday promises to be a brilliant day out for anyone whether in recovery or not, so we hope that as many residents as possible will join us Centenary Square."

The weekend will conclude with a Recovery Ramble in Saltaire on Sunday, September 13.
