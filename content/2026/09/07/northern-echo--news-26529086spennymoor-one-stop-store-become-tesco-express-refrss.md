A County Durham convenience store is set to become a Tesco Express.

Spennymoor's One Stop, located on Whitworth Terrace, will temporarily close while it is turned into a Tesco Express.

The shop is expected to reopen later this year.

A Tesco spokesperson confirmed the change and said: "All One Stop store colleagues will be transferred to the new Tesco store.
