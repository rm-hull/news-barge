Bricks have been thrown at a home in York – smashing several windows, police said.

Officers are appealing for information about what police are treating as criminal damage in Clifton on Friday night (September 4).

North Yorkshire Police said bricks were thrown at a property in Kingsway North at 8.30pm with multiple windows broken.

A police spokesperson said officers are seeking information about a person seen in the area at the time riding a mountain bike and wearing a light-coloured hoodie with the hood up.

They urged anyone with information about the person or incident to email [Rob.Gooch1@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Rob.Gooch1@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101 and ask for Rob Gooch.

Information can be anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260173880 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
