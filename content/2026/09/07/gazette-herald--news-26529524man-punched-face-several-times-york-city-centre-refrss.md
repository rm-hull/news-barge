A man was left seriously injured after being punched in the face several times in York, police have said.

Officers are appealing for information about the “violent incident” in Clifford Street at about 10am on Saturday, June 27.

North Yorkshire Police on Monday (September 7) issued photographs of two men officers want to speak to in connection with the incident.

  Police want to speak to this man about the 'violent incident' in Clifford Street, York 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Officers want to locate the man in the footage along with the taller man who potentially witnessed the incident, police said.

A police spokesperson added: “We are also urging people to come forward who have witnessed the incident or have mobile phone video footage.”

Those with information are asked to email [helen.barrett@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:helen.barrett@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 via its website.

Quote reference 12260123802 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
