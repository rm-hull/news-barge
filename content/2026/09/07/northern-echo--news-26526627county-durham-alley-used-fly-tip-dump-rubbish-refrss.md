A County Durham alleyway has been used as a dumping ground for fly-tipping and waste, as wardens working for the council have warned that they will catch those responsible.

Over the last few months, the back lane of William Street, South Moor, has been the regular site for rubbish and fly-tipping, according to Durham County Council's neighbourhood wardens.

  Fly-tipping on the back lane of William Street, South Moor 

*(Image: DURHAM COUNTY COUNCIL WARDENS)*

The council group has praised the majority of residents for disposing of their waste correctly, but officials want to identify numerous other people who have chosen to ditch furniture and other items in the alleyway.

In a statement, they said: "Durham County Council Neighbourhood Wardens have been inundated with complaints regarding the continued fly-tipping of waste in the back lane of William Street, South Moor.

  Fly-tipping on the back lane of William Street, South Moor 

*(Image: DURHAM COUNTY COUNCIL WARDENS)*

"While the vast majority of residents in this area dispose of their waste responsibly, a small minority continue to believe it is acceptable to dump unwanted items in the back lane and expect the Council to clear it up for them.

"Neighbourhood Wardens are now carrying out enquiries to identify those responsible, and where evidence is available, enforcement action will be taken.

"It is time for everyone to take pride in and respect the area in which they live. Fly tipping is not only a criminal offence, but it is also unsightly, attracts further anti-social behaviour, and can pose a significant safety risk.

"This particular location is regularly used by young people and, as can be seen from the waste that has been discarded, presents a clear hazard to those using the area.

"If you have any information that could assist with enquiries, please contact the Neighbourhood Warden Service on 03000 260000 or email [help@durham.gov.uk](mailto:help@durham.gov.uk), quoting reference 1673980.

"To report fly tipping, visit: www.durham.gov.uk/flytipping

"Together, we can help keep our communities clean, safe and welcoming for everyone."
