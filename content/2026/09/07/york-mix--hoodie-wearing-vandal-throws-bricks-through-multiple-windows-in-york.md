Police are searching for a hoodie-wearing cyclist after windows were smashed on a York street.

It happened on **Kingsway North**, Clifton, at** 8.30pm**on** Friday**.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Bricks were thrown at a property breaking multiple windows.

#### Kingsway North, York

“We’re appealing for information about a person wearing a light-coloured hoodie with the hood up and riding a mountain bike in the area at the time.”
