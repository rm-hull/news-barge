Youths have been given “strong advice about their actions” after officers were called to reports of anti-social behaviour at a York takeaway, police said.

A police van and an unmarked police car were seen outside Pepe’s in Piccadilly at about 1.30pm on Monday (September 7).

Officers could be seen talking to a group of youths outside the takeaway.

North Yorkshire Police said officers were called to the scene after a report that a group of young people were causing anti-social behaviour inside a takeaway at 1.10pm.

“There is not believed to have been any damage or injuries,” a force spokesperson said.

“Officers attended straight away and took details of those involved and gave them strong advice about their actions.”
