# Murder arrest more than two decades after death of Nora Tait

## A man has been re-arrested on suspicion of murder as part of an ongoing investigation into the death of Nora Tait more than two decades ago.

Last October, on the 20th anniversary of Nora's death, South Yorkshire Police appealed for information in relation to her murder after the 69-year-old was killed in her home in Stone Close Avenue, Doncaster in 2005.

After new information was obtained following that appeal, a 57-year-old man from Doncaster has been re-arrested on suspicion of her murder.

He remains in police custody at this time after being arrested on September 6.

![A man has been re-arrested on suspicion of murder as part of our ongoing investigation into the death of Nora Tait in Doncaster in 2005](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjFiYWRhYmE4LTY0ZjUtNDk5ZS1iZTU3LWU2ODBmZTQ0MjYwODowYzM0NzBlMC05YzJiLTQyOTUtYjMwNy1hMDY2NTAxNGUyY2M=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Detective Chief Inspector Ben Wood, who is the senior investigating officer for this case, is continuing to urge anyone with information that could help them find Nora's killer to get in touch with them so they can secure justice on behalf of Nora and her family.

"Our drive throughout this long-running investigation has been to give Nora's family the answers they need after decades of hurt”, DCI Wood said.

"Nora's family have been informed of this latest development and we remain in contact with them as we seek to give them the solace they deserve.

"Someone knows what happened to Nora and we believe those answers lie in the local community in and around Stone Close Avenue in Hexthorpe.

"If you know something and you are keeping it to yourself, I urge you to think of the pain this is causing Nora's family and loved ones.

"Even the smallest piece of information might prove to be a really important piece of the jigsaw and could help us secure that all-important justice after all these years.

"Do it for Nora, and do it for her family to help give them the closure they so desperately need."

**How to report information to police**

Anyone with information can get in direct contact with the investigative team through the dedicated Major Incident Public Portal.

You can also call on 101, quoting incident number 199 of October 8, 2025.
