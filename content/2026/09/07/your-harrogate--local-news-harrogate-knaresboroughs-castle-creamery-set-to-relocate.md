Castle Creamery in Knaresborough is set to move to new premises - and its recruiting additional members of staff.

The independent ice cream parlour will relocate around 150 yards along Castlegate to number 15.

It is now looking for baristas and front-of-house team members to join the business from Thursday 19th November.

The roles will operate on a four-days-on, four-days-off basis.

Castle Creamery said it is looking for positive, friendly and reliable applicants who enjoy welcoming customers and working as part of a team.

Experience or an interest in food, coffee, cooking, retail and customer service may also be beneficial.

*Applications can be made in store or by emailing a CV to [castleicecreamparlour@gmail.com.](mailto:castleicecreamparlour@gmail.com.)*
