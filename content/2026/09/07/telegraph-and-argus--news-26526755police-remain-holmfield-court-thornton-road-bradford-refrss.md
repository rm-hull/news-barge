POLICE remain at a block of flats in Bradford this morning after a cordon was put in place and forensic officers were there.

A marked West Yorkshire Police vehicle was parked outside Holmfield Court on Thornton Road when a Telegraph & Argus reporter visited the scene on Monday morning.

The continued police presence comes after crime scene investigators, detectives and uniformed officers were seen at the address on Sunday.

Members of the public reported a significant emergency services presence in the area, while a police cordon was put in place as enquiries continued.

On Monday morning, no police tape was visible from the front of the property, but officers continued to maintain a presence at the location.

## What is Holmfield Court?

Holmfield Court is a large residential block on Thornton Road, close to Bradford city centre.

It was previously home to Stephen Griffiths, who became known as the "Crossbow Cannibal" after murdering three women in Bradford between 2009 and 2010.

Griffiths lived in the building at the time of the murders and was later jailed for life.

The T&A has approached West Yorkshire Police for a comment.
