A REMINDER that closures are planned for a major road outside York for a fortnight from tonight (Monday, September 7) while resurfacing work is carried out.

The council said the closures on the A1079 from tonight until Saturday, September 19 are to ensure the works can be carried out safely and it expects the road will only be closed between 8pm and 6am on each day of the works period.

Across all three phases of the works the A1079 will be closed from York Road, Dunnington to the City of York boundary in Kexby, the council said.

A council spokesperson said: "The works will be carried out by Lane Rentals Services on behalf of the council, who will be working for 14 nights under a road closure between the hours of 8pm to 6am.

"The road will reopen each morning, unless there are exceptional circumstances.

"Phase one will be from the York Road, Dunnington junction up to the Common Road junction, starting on Monday 7 September 2026 at 8pm and working each night before completing by 6am Saturday 12 September.

"Phase two is from the York Road, Dunnington junction to near Kexby Stray junction with the closure point at Dauby Lane to allow for turning traffic.

"This will be done on the night of Saturday 12 September and will be finished by 6am on Sunday 13 September.

"During this phase we will need to completely close the Common Road junction for this one night to enable it to be resurfaced safely.

"It will reopen at 6am on Sunday 13 September.

"Phase three will be from Common Lane junction to City of York boundary at Kexby Bridge with the closure point at Long Lane to allow for turning traffic.

"This phase is expected to last for eight nights.

"This means the road will be closed between 8pm and 6am on Sunday 13 September and finish at 6am on Monday 21 September.

"The road will reopen each morning, unless there are exceptional circumstances."

The closure covers access to the villages of Dunnington and the A166 Stamford Bridge Road, Elvington and Kexby to the A1079 where the resurfacing works are taking place.

A council spokesperson said where there are footways, routes for pedestrians and mobility aid users will remain open to the public, to allow residents safe access to properties and homes.

Businesses will also remain open, emergency services will be allowed through the works at all times and bus services will be escorted through the works, the council said, adding that the X46 and X47 service from York to Hull will be operating to their usual timetable.

The A1079 is one of a limited number of main roads crossing the River Derwent.

Traffic signs and barriers have already gone up on the A1079 warning motorists of the proposed closures.

An alternative route for diverted vehicles out of York will be signed during the works period via the A64, A19, A63 and A614 to rejoin the A1079 at Shiptonthorpe, two miles from Market Weighton.

A council spokesperson said: "Motorists and cyclists may choose to take alternative routes, but due to the capacity the A1079 holds for large vehicles, the council must offer a diversion able to cope with the size and weight that the road usually carries."

Access for residents and emergency vehicles will be maintained throughout the works period.

Traffic signs and barriers will mark the restrictions.

Councillor Kate Ravilious, City of York Council's executive member for transport said: “We continue to deliver our record programme of planned Highways Maintenance works for 2026/27 alongside emergency repairs.

"This stretch of the A1079 is the latest to receive significant repairs that will make journeys easier for people in the area and across the region.

“We are working to minimise disruption by carrying out these works on nights and working to reopen the road each morning.

"When the road is closed diversions are in place and we have sent letters to local residents and businesses to help them plan ahead.

"Once complete this will bring smoother and safer journeys for all who use this road and we’re grateful for everyone’s patience while we get this work done”
