The first headliners of Scarborough’s summer 2027 season has been announced – and it’s two of the UK’s most beloved singer-songwriters.

Chart toppers Jack Savoretti and James Morrison are teaming up next summer for a double-headline tour which heads to Scarborough Open Air Theatre on **Friday 9 July**.

The announcement follows a record-breaking summer of live music at Scarborough OAT with 175,000 tickets sold across an incredible 28-gig summer series.

Julian Murray, from promoters Cuffe and Taylor, said: “The 2026 Scarborough OAT season was truly sensational and we are delighted, just two weeks after the final show of the summer, to be able to announce our first headliners for 2027.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/JAMES-MORRISON.webp)

“Jack and James are two of the UK’s most beloved contemporary singer-songwriters so to be bringing them here, on the same night, will be something truly special.

“It will be a much-requested Scarborough OAT debut for Jack and it’s a welcome return to the venue for James who was The Script’s very special guest here back in 2022, so he is delighted to be back as a headliner.

“This will be an unmissable night for fans of these two incredible talents.”

This year has seen Jack Savoretti make a bold return to his musical roots, with the release of his ninth studio album, *We Will Always Be The Way We Were*.

With millions of global album sales, two number one albums and five within the top 10 UK album charts he continues to tour across the UK and Europe. 2026 has also seen Savoretti headline a sold-out special 20th anniversary show at the Royal Albert Hall, while also making a surprise appearance alongside long-time friend and collaborator KT Tunstall at the Isle of Wight Festival.

Twenty years into his career, *You Give Me Something* hitmaker James Morrison’s music still simmers with the soul, emotion and quiet authority that have defined one of the UK’s most enduring singer-songwriters.

Across that time, he has amassed two UK number 1 albums, five UK Top 10 singles, multiple Platinum-certified releases and a BRIT Awards win, alongside millions of global sales. Last autumn saw him achieve his highest chart position in 14 years with his seventh album, the Top 5 record *Fight Another Day*.

Tickets will go on general sale at 10am this Friday 11 September via Tickemaster.
