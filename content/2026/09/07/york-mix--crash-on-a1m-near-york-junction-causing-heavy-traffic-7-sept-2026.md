There was a crash on the A1M this morning.

It was on the northbound carriageway of the motorway near Bramham Park, close to the junction 44 turn-off to the A64 and York.

It caused slow traffic all along the A1M past the junction.

#### Crash location

Earlier the AA reported: “Severe delays of 38 minutes and delays easing on A1(M) Northbound between M1 and J45 A659 Wattlesyke (Boston Spa). Average speed five mph.”

The crash has now been cleared and traffic is running normally again.
