# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after large amount of cabling stolen overnight from Harrogate hospital

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after cabling was stolen from a hospital in Harrogate

The incident happened overnight between 31 August and 1 September when thieves stole a large amount of cabling from the grounds of Harrogate District Hospital.

It is believed that a vehicle, at least the size of a transit van, would have been needed to remove the cabling.

If you witnessed anything, captured any footage on your phone, CCTV, or das hcam, or have any information that may help, you should get in contact with the police.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260171012 when providing any details regarding the incident.
