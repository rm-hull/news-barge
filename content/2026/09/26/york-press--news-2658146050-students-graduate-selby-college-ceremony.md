More than 50 students have celebrated the successful completion of their degree-level studies at a graduation ceremony in North Yorkshire.

The graduates from Selby College’s University Centre gathered at Selby Abbey on Friday, September 18 to mark the occasion.

Students were awarded qualifications in subjects including art, engineering, computing, childcare, public services, sport and teaching.

![]()Dr David Powell presenting graduates with their certificates *(Image: Supplied)*

Sam Wright, principal at the Heart of Yorkshire Education Group, said: "Graduation is always a momentous occasion, and it is a privilege to celebrate the hard work and achievements of our graduates.

"Seeing them reach this milestone and embark on new journeys is incredibly rewarding, and we are proud to share this special moment with them today alongside their loved ones, course tutors and special guests."

Guest speaker Leon Greenwood, a 2026 Winter Olympian and former student, delivered an inspirational address.

![]()Leon Greenwood delivering his 'inspirational' speech *(Image: Supplied)*

He encouraged graduates to visualise their goals, work hard and persevere, reminding them that "the other side of failure is success."

Selby MP Keir Mather also spoke at the event, urging graduates to stay optimistic and ambitious.

The University Centre at the Heart of Yorkshire Education Group spans three campuses in Castleford, Selby and Wakefield, offering a wide range of degree-level courses.
