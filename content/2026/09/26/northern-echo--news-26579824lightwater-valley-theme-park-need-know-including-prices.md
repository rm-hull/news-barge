Rides, games, fun and plenty of delicious treats - there's nothing like a thrilling day out at a theme park.

Lightwater Valley Family Adventure Park has been a go-to for family days out, school trips and fun adventures for decades.

It boasts roller coasters, water rides and entertainment suitable for all ages.

It’s perfect for thrill-seekers and families looking for a full day of amusement in North Yorkshire.

![]()The Caterpillar ride at Lightwater Valley *(Image: Lightwater Valley)*

The park in North Stainley, near Ripon, began as a small self‑pick fruit farm attraction created by the Staveley family in 1969.

Over time, the popularity of its lake and early attractions led to its development into a full family adventure park.

This year, the park was taken over by new owners in a move that's an "exciting new chapter" for the theme park. Plus, a new ride was recently welcomed.

If you're planning a trip to Lightwater Valley, we have all the information you need - from rides, opening times, how to get there and more.

### Where is Lightwater Valley?

Lightwater Valley is located in North Stainley, North Yorkshire.

It sits on 175 acres of picturesque North Yorkshire countryside, approximately four miles north of Ripon.

### How to get there by car

The theme park is located 10 minutes from junction 50 of the A1 motorway.

From here, take the A61 towards Ripon.

On arrival at the ‘Clocktower’ traffic light junction, take the A6108 towards the village of North Stainley.

A spokesperson for Lightwater Valley is warning people to not use the postcode to find it.

They said: "Please do not use our postcode on satellite navigation as this is currently directing people to the wrong address. Please follow Lightwater Valley signs from A1/Ripon. "

### Nearest train station

Your best bet is getting a train to Thirsk, which boasts direct services from Manchester, Leeds, York, Middlesbrough and London.

Once you arrive into Thirsk, you can get a taxi ride which will take around 20 minutes.

### Opening times

The park is open from 10.30am until 4pm every day.

Park times can change throughout the year and during events.

### Rides at Lightwater Valley

![]() *(Image: Lightwater Valley)*

There are over 35 attractions to enjoy at the park.

The newest rides include the Spinning Racer, The Lightwater Wheel and Junior Driving School. Also new is the Little Monsters Spooky Soft Play.

There is also two mini rollercoasters, including the Ladybird ride and the Caterpillar.

![]() *(Image: Lightwater Valley)*

Other rides include Flying Cutlass, Skyrider, Splash Falls, Powder Kegs, Lightwater Valley Express Train, Spin Bug, Dragon Express, Dragon Drop, Monkey Drop and Elephant Flight.

There is also Eagles Creek Farm, a swan boat ride, and a miniature golf course, plus plenty of playzones.

### Lightwater Valley wins Best New Attraction

Lightwater Valley recently won Gold for Best New Attraction at the UK Theme Park Awards, marking a significant milestone for the park as it continues its revival under new ownership.

In a post on social media, the theme park said: "Putting Lightwater Valley back on the map.

"We're hugely delighted to see Lightwater Valley take home gold for Best New Attraction at the UK Theme Park Awards.

"This is Lightwater Valley's first award in a long time, and it feels like a significant moment for everyone involved.

### Where to stop for a bite to eat

There are a number of different food and drink pit stops in the park.

If you fancy a British classic, stop at Lakeside Fish and Chips shop.

Recently it was announced a new Gyros fan has arrived at the park from The Grill House.

Burgers are available at The Grill Cafe, or stop for a sweet treat at The Bakehouse.

There is also The Chicken Shak as well as Carvery and Grill, popular for its hot dogs.

Elsewhere, doughnuts and warm waffles available, plus a candy floss stand, and a coffee cart for the adults from The Little Yorkshire Coffee Company.

### Ticket prices

Lightwater Valley has been named the second cheapest theme park in the UK.

As of autumn 2026, the prices are as follows:

* Standard 90cm+ ticket - £22 online, £25 to pay at the park
* Under 90cm Ticket - free
* Disabled 90cm+ Ticket (includes 1 x free carer - min age 16) £22 online, £25 if you pay at the park
* NHS, Military, Emergency Services, Blue Light Card, Max Card Ticket £21 online, £24 if you pay at the park
* Senior Citizen Ticket (65 years+) £21 online, £24 to pay at the park

### New owners take over theme park

The Mellors Group based in Nottingham, announced it has taken over Lightwater Valley Family Adventure Park in Ripon, promising “an exciting new chapter” for the cherished family attraction.

The park holds a special place in the hearts of many families across the UK, including the Mellors family, who visited the park often as children.

Now, the Mellors Group said they are committed to bringing ambitious plans and significant new investment to the beloved adventure park including new rides and attractions set to debut this year.

While the park will continue to cater for younger families and visitors under 12, the group aims to extend the appeal for all ages, creating an experience that the whole family can enjoy together.
