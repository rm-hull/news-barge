Vintage vehicle enthusiasts packed out Bishop Auckland on Saturday as dozens of classic cars took over the town centre for its annual heritage festival.

Crowds gathered around an impressive display of classic vehicles in the marketplace.

The free event on Saturday, September 26, was part of the fourth Bishop Auckland Heritage Festival, celebrating the people, places and stories that shaped the town and wider County Durham.

![]()Classic cars at the Heritage Fayre in Bishop Auckland

Photos show proud owners standing alongside their immaculately polished motors as they chatted with visitors interested in learning more about the cars' histories.

Many enthusiasts could be seen lifting bonnets and inviting people to take a closer look at the engines and craftsmanship that have helped keep the vehicles on the road for decades.

A wide range of classic cars from different eras were on display, attracting people of all ages to the marketplace throughout the afternoon.

![]()Classic cars at the Heritage Fayre in Bishop Auckland

Families, local residents and visitors from across the region were among those who stopped to admire the collection, with many taking photographs and speaking to owners about their treasured vehicles.

![]()Classic cars at the Heritage Fayre in Bishop Auckland

The classic vehicle display was organised by the Northern Bygone Society and was one of the main attractions at the popular annual event.

Alongside the vehicle showcase, the festival featured heritage displays, exhibitions and free talks from local historians and community groups across several venues in the town centre.

The event returned to Bishop Auckland for its fourth year and once again drew large crowds eager to celebrate local history and heritage.
