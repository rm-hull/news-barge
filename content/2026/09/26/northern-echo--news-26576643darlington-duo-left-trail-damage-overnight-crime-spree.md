A second man has been jailed for stealing a works minibus and a parking machine.

James Peacock and Gavin Smith broke into a unit on Cleveland Trading Estate, Darlington, in the early hours of November 27 last year.

They arrived on a blue moped wearing motorcycle helmets, then ransacked the offices and took property.

Prosecutor Saba Shan told Teesside Crown Court the haul included numerous keys, among them a set for a minibus, and a bottle of whisky.

The pair escaped in the minibus after forcing their way through locked gates at the secure compound.

![]()Gavin Smith, left, and James Peacock have both now received prison sentences, albeit nine months apart, over the aggravated taking of a minibus from a Darlington industrial unit compound and theft of a parking meter at Catterick Garrison

The pair drove to Catterick Garrison, stole a pay-and-display parking meter and strapped it to the back of the minibus, the court heard.

It was dragged behind the minibus, sending sparks across the road.

The North Yorkshire Council machine contained an estimated £300 to £400 and was due to be emptied later that day.

Due to the force used in stealing it, the machine had to be replaced at a cost of several thousand pounds.

Police spotted the minibus still in the Catterick area as it reversed towards a marked patrol car, forcing the driver to take evasive action.

The minibus was driven into a confined space, mounted a grass verge and crashed into a concrete bollard, a parked van and a car.

After the minibus was abandoned, members of the public reported seeing men trying to bury clothing in a nearby field.

Police found the pair at a McDonald’s restaurant on Whessoe Road, Darlington, where the officers recovered money bags, coins, a large quantity of keys and the adjustable wrench used in the burglary.

Peacock, 41, of Brighton Road, Darlington, was jailed for nine months after admitting burglary, aggravated vehicle taking and theft at a magistrates’ court hearing in December.

![]()James Peacock received a nine-month prison sentence for his role in the crimes, imposed by magistrates last December

His co-accused, 34-year-old Smith, of Bramall Lane, Darlington, is now serving 16 months after admitting the charges on the day of his recent trial.

![]()Gavin Smith received a total prison sentence of 16 months

It put him in breach of a suspended 14-month prison sentence imposed just six weeks earlier.

Jailing Smith at Teesside Crown Court, Judge Deborah Sherwin told him that he and Peacock, “embarked on quite a spree of theft, causing a lot of damage in the course of it”.

Peacock was believed to be the driver, but the judge said there was “not a great distinction” between their roles.

She sentenced Smith to 10 months for the November 27 offences, adding six months consecutively for breaching the suspended sentence.

He was also disqualified from driving for 20 months.
