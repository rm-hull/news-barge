*Update: In a statement issued at 2.48pm today (Saturday, September 26), North Yorkshire Police said the missing 19-year-old has been located.*

A TEENAGER from York has been reported missing, prompting an urgent police appeal.

The 19-year-old, from the Tang Hall area, was last seen in Stonebow outside the city centre just after 5pm yesterday (Friday, September 25).

The force believes he travelled to Northallerton by train in Friday evening.

North Yorkshire Police have been carrying out extensive searches and say they are increasingly concerned for his safety.




A police spokesperson said: "He was last seen wearing a tight black t-shirt, black baggy joggers, and black trainers with a coloured stripe on them.

"Any immediate sightings should be reported by calling 999 so we can make sure he is safe."

Anyone with other information is asked to call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference number 12260188777.
