Care-home residents enjoyed a wildlife encounter when a fox, barn owl, dove, corn snake and hedgehog visited for International Fox Day.

Leeming Bar Grange welcomed the animals from Zante Fox and Friends on September 24.

General manager Kathryn Billett said: "Our residents have really enjoyed their unusual visitors.

"We are always looking for new activities for the residents and today we have seen the enjoyment and therapeutic influence animals can have on people."

![]()Resident Michael Cuddling Zante Fox *(Image: Supplied)*

The visitors included Sapphire the barn owl, Valentine the dove, Freddie the corn snake, Eugene the African pygmy hedgehog and Zante the fox.

Resident Michael said: "I never thought I would ever have the opportunity to cuddle a fox. It was wonderful and a memory that will always stay with me."

Ms Calvert said: "It was a wonderful opportunity to learn about these incredible animals and discover how special foxes really are.

The home provides residential and dementia care for 60 residents, including respite and long-term stays.
