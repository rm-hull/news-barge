A car has crashed into a building close to the A19 in Selby overnight.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Selby was called at 12.58am today, Saturday 26th September, to assist police at the scene in Thornden Buildings in the town.

A service spokesperson said a structural damage inspection was required.
