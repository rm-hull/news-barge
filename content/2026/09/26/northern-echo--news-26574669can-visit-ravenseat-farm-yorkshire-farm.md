Fans of Our Yorkshire Farm grew used to the spectacular views around Amanda and Clive Owen's remote Yorkshire Dales home.

Ravenseat Farm became almost as recognisable as the Owen family during the hugely popular Channel 5 show, which followed them and their nine children at work on the sprawling hill farm.

Visitors can once again stop at Ravenseat for a tradition Amanda made famous before the family found television success.

Cream teas have returned through a new pop-up venture involving Amanda and Clive's daughter Edith.

But where is Ravenseat, how can you visit, and is it really as isolated as it looks on television?

## Where is Ravenseat Farm?

Ravenseat is a remote hill farm in Upper Swaledale in the Yorkshire Dales National Park.

The 2,000-acre working farm lies in Whitsundale, near the head of Swaledale, with Keld as the nearest village.

It is one of the area's most isolated farms, surrounded by moorland and reached by narrow roads through the upper dale.

![]()Ravenseat Farm *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Ravenseat is around three-and-a-half miles from Keld, while the picturesque village of Muker lies further down Swaledale.

Its remote setting became central to Our Yorkshire Farm, especially in winter, when viewers saw the Owens battle snow, harsh weather and the demands of caring for livestock.

## Was Our Yorkshire Farm really filmed at Ravenseat?

Our Yorkshire Farm followed everyday life at Ravenseat as Amanda and Clive raised their nine children and farmed sheep and other livestock.

The family had already appeared on television, including in ITV's The Dales and Ben Fogle's New Lives in the Wild, before landing their own Channel 5 show.

Our Yorkshire Farm subsequently turned Ravenseat into one of Britain's best-known farms.

Viewers saw lambing, shearing, the children helping with farm jobs and the family coping with life in the high Yorkshire Dales.

## Can you visit Ravenseat Farm?

Visitors can once again stop at Ravenseat when its cream tea pop-up is open.

The new venture serves homemade scones, tea, coffee and cold drinks, with outdoor seating overlooking the Yorkshire Dales.

Parking and toilet facilities are also available.

Their daughter Edith helps run the venture, reviving Ravenseat's cream-tea tradition after the farm closed to visitors in 2023.

Visitors should check the Cream Teas at Ravenseat Instagram account, @cream\_.teas, just before travelling.

The pop-up is not open every day, and the account announces temporary closures or changes to opening times.

That matters especially for visitors travelling a long way to the remote farm.

The farm remains a working agricultural site, not a conventional visitor attraction.

## Why did Ravenseat stop serving cream teas?

Cream teas had been closely associated with Ravenseat for years.

Amanda began serving refreshments to walkers long before Our Yorkshire Farm made the family famous.

Ravenseat sits alongside the Coast to Coast walking route, meaning walkers regularly pass through this remote part of Swaledale during the warmer months.

But it stopped after Amanda announced in 2023 that Ravenseat was closed to visitors "until further notice" and cream teas would no longer be served.

The new pop-up marks a major change after years of telling visitors not to travel to Ravenseat for refreshments.

## Who runs the Ravenseat cream teas now?

Their daughter Edith helps run the new cream-tea venture.

She is one of the couple's nine children, alongside Raven, Reuben, Miles, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy.

Viewers watched Edith grow up at Ravenseat on Our Yorkshire Farm, where the Owen children regularly helped with farm jobs.

The new venture has her helping revive one of the family home's best-known traditions.

## Where is Our Farm Next Door filmed?

The family's more recent programme, Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, remains rooted in the same part of the Yorkshire Dales.

But its main focus is another property, not Ravenseat.

The programme follows Amanda, Clive and their children as they restore an old farmhouse known locally as Anty John's.

The family bought the property near Ravenseat as part of their plans for the future.

The programme was filmed at both Anty John's and Ravenseat, following the restoration alongside the family's daily farming life.
