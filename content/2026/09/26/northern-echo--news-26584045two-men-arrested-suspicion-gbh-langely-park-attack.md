Two men have been arrested on suspicion of grievous bodily harm after a man was taken to hospital with head and arm injuries.

Police were called to the incident shortly after 3.30pm following a disturbance in Front Street, Langley Park on Friday (September 25).

A man sustained injuries to his head and arm and was taken to hospital for treatment.

Two men, aged 46 and 22, have been arrested on suspicion of causing grievous bodily harm and have been taken into police custody where they remain.

A spokesperson from Durham Police said: “Enquiries are ongoing but officers believe this was an isolated incident in which all those involved are known to each other.”

Anyone who may have witnessed the incident or has any information is asked to call Durham Constabulary on 101, quoting incident number 307 of September 25.
