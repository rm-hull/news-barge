## This beautiful Yorkshire walk hides the remains of a lost industrial past

Pateley Bridge looking south

IN the heart of Nidderdale is the attractive village of Pateley Bridge. Today tourism and sheep thrive, but in the past industry dominates. The contrast between the remains of the lead mining industry and the beautiful countryside makes for an enjoyable walk.
