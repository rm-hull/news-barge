A TEENAGER from York has been reported missing, prompting an urgent police appeal.

Zinedine Boubacir, 19, from the Tang Hall area, was last seen in Stonebow outside the city centre just after 5pm yesterday (Friday, September 25).

North Yorkshire Police is carrying out extensive searches in and around the city and say they are increasingly concerned for his safety.




A police spokesperson said: "Zinedine is described as mixed ethnic background, aged 19, brown hair, 5ft 6in tall, with a slim build.

"He was last seen wearing a tight black t-shirt, black baggy joggers, and black trainers with a coloured stripe on them.

"Any immediate sightings of Zinedine should be reported by calling 999 so we can make sure he is safe."

Anyone with other information is asked to call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference number 12260188777.
