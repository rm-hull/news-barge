The second housemate evicted from Big Brother 2026 has been revealed.

The hit reality show returned for a fourth series on ITV (after the 2023 reboot) on September 13.

Millie was the first person evicted from Big Brother 2026 last Friday (September 18).

Chelsey and Pauline were up for eviction last night (Friday, September 25), having been nominated by their fellow housemates earlier in the week.

## Who was the second housemate evicted from Big Brother last night?

Following the public vote, hosts AJ Odudu and Will Best revealed Chelsey was the second housemate evicted from Big Brother 2026.

Chelsey, who formed close bonds with Lynx, Rhinna and Omar during her time in the house, said that making those friendships was the highlight of her experience.

She said: "The best part of the Big Brother experience was meeting Rhianna, Omar and Lynx.

"I really did enjoy meeting them.

"The whole experience as well was actually really good."

She also revealed that the group has already made travel plans for when they are all out of the house.

Chelsey said: "Me and Lynx have made a lot of plans to go to Ibiza, to go to Morocco, to go to Munich.

"But also with Rhianna and Omar."

When asked if she would do anything differently, Chelsey replied: "If I was going back in, after hearing what everyone's had to say, probably make more effort with other people."

## Fans react to Chelsey's eviction

During the live show, members of the audience could be heard chanting: "Get Chelsey out."

Viewers online also felt the same way, with one person commenting: "I’ve never voted for a tv show in my life.

"I used all my votes on her."

Another added: "The right person went, NEXTTTT."

Jeward made a brief appearance in the house this week and were involved in an argument with Chelsey, Rhainna, Omar and Lynx.

They posted on X (formerly Twitter) following the latest eviction, saying: "Hope Chelsey is not feeling too de-FEET-ed."

## How long will Big Brother be on TV for?

It has been revealed previously that this series of Big Brother will last an additional seven days, giving the show a full seven-week run.

Meanwhile, Celebrity Big Brother will not be back on our screens until the spring of next year.
