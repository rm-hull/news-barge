Police have started an investigation following a violent incident involving a pick-up truck which resulted in a man being airlifted to hospital with serious injuries and four arrests.

Reports were initially made to North Yorkshire Police Control Room at 9.45pm on Friday (25 September 2026) after an altercation at an address on Eden Drive in Scarborough

Around five minutes later, further reports were made about a man being injured by a dark-coloured pick-up truck along Seamer Road towards the Lidl store.

The 18-year-old injured man was attended to by paramedics before being airlifted to hospital. He is currently in a serious but stable condition.

Officers have made four arrests in connection with the investigation: a 47-year-old man, a 36-year-old man, a 23-year-old man, and a 17-year-old boy.

They remain in custody for questioning while enquires continue.

To support the investigation, we are appealing for witnesses to come forward and anyone with relevant CCTV, doorbell and dashcam footage.

If you can help, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.
