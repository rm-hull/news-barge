Police are currently investigating an egg throwing incident that occurred in York on the evening of Thursday 24 September 2026.

About 8pm a cyclist was travelling along Piccadilly when a group of youths threw eggs at a man who was cycling before going off towards Coppergate direction.

The youths are described as 3 males and 1 female about 4- 15 years of age.
