Older people in a North East town are being invited to discover support, activities and services at a celebration of later life.

The event will be held at Middlesbrough Town Hall on Monday, September 28, from 10am to 2pm.

Age Friendly Middlesbrough is hosting the event to mark the International Day of Older People on October 1.

![]()Older people in Middlesbrough are being invited to discover support, activities and services at a celebration of later life *(Image: Supplied)*

More than 25 stallholders will showcase local services, activities and opportunities.

Visitors can try movement and dance sessions, attend workshops, see equipment demonstrations and get digital and welfare support.

Visitors can get free blood pressure checks, winter wellness guidance and advice on independent living.

British Sign Language (BSL) interpreters will be available, and light refreshments will be provided.

Visitors can also enter a free raffle and prize draw.

The event is open to all older people living in Middlesbrough.

For more information, email [agefriendly@middlesbrough.gov.uk.](mailto:agefriendly@middlesbrough.gov.uk.)
