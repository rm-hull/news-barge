The heartbroken family of a woman 'murdered' in a flat above a Darlington pub have said she was “loved by everyone.”

A 44-year-old arrested on suspicion of her murder remains in custody while a 27-year-old arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender has been released on bail.

In a statement, the care worker's family said: “Leanne was just the best mam, nana, daughter and friend we could wish for.

“She was loved by everyone and would do anything for anyone.

“She was the life and soul of the party and loved to dance.

![]()Leanne Park was found dead above a pub in Darlington *(Image: DURHAM POLICE)*

“We love her so much and miss her more than words can describe. We are completely devastated. Her death has ripped the heart out of our family.

“We are grateful for all the kind messages and the support since her death. We ask that everyone remembers her for the kind-hearted woman she was.”

Leanne’s family are being supported by specialist family liaison officers.

![]()Leanne Park, who worked at Ventress Care Home, was found at a flat above The Greyhound pub on Parkgate on Wednesday, September 23. *(Image: FACEBOOK)*

Crime scene investigators are continuing to examine the scene.

Durham Police said a cordon outside the pub is expected to remain in place for some time.

One tribute read: "Always in our hearts, taken too soon. Heaven has gained a beautiful angel".

Another said: "RIP our beautiful Auntie. You are so loved and going to be a huge miss, love you always".

![]() *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

![]()Floral tributes at the scene *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Her colleagues at the Ventress Hall care home in Darlington previously said she would "do anything for anybody".

In a Facebook post the home said: "I can't actually believe I am writing this, but it is with a heavy heart that I say we have lost our friend and work colleague, Leanne.

"Leanne was always so kind and would do anything for anybody, especially our residents.

![]()The cordon remains in place at the scene *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

![]()Floral tributes at the scene *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

"The world is beyond cruel and you have been taken too soon. We love you much Leanne. Love all your Ventress Work Family. You will never be forgotten. Our condolences to all Leanne's family."
