# Benjamin Street stabbing: West Yorkshire Police appeal for information after man was stabbed in Wakefield

## Detectives in Wakefield are appealing for information after a man was stabbed on Benjamin Street.

West Yorkshire detectives are appealing for information after a man was stabbed on Benjamin Street on Friday afternoon, September 25, 2026.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Officers on patrol in the city came across the man at 3.45pm yesterday (25 September).

“He was taken to hospital by ambulance where his injuries are thought to be serious but not life threatening.

![Police. (Pic credit: John Devlin)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmVhMTE1ZDVkLTliNWMtNDIyYi1iZmYxLTdhOTE1Y2VhMjAwNDo4MzYxY2Y4Ny1iMjdhLTRhYmEtOGY5NC1iNzY5Y2Y1MTdiZWU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“Witnesses or anyone with video footage that could assist the investigation are asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat) quoting reference 1043 of 25/9.

“Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.”
