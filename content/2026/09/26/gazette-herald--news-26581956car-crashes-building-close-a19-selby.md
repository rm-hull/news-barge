*(Image: Newsquest)*

A CAR has crashed into a building in a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Selby was called at 12.58am today to assist police at the scene in Thornden Buildings in the town.

A service spokesperson said a structural damage inspection was required.

The spokesperson added: "Crews used a salvage sheet to weatherproof the damaged areas and left in the hands of police on the scene."
