AN update has been issued after North Yorkshire Police issued an appeal to help find a teenager missing from York.




The 19-year-old, from the Tang Hall area was last seen in Stonebow just after 5pm yesterday (Friday, September 25).

Police believe he travelled by train to Northallerton that evening and concern had been growing for his safety.

In a statement issued at 2.48pm today, a North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said the 19-year-old had been located and the force thanked everybody who had supported its appeal.
