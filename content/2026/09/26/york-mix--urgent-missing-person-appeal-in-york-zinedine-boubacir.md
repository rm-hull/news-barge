Police are are urgently appealing for information and sightings to help locate a missing man in York.

Zinedine Boubacir is from the Tang Hall area.

The 19-year-old was last seen in Stonebow just after 5pm on Friday (25 September).

Zinedine is described as mixed ethnic background, aged 19, brown hair, 5ft 6in tall, with a slim build.

He was last seen wearing a tight black T-shirt, black baggy joggers, and black trainers with a coloured stripe on them.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Extensive police enquiries and searches are ongoing in and around the city as concerns are growing for his safety.”

Any immediate sightings of Zinedine should be reported by calling 999.

If you have any other information that could help us to find him, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and speak to our Force Control Room.

Please quote reference number 12260188777.
