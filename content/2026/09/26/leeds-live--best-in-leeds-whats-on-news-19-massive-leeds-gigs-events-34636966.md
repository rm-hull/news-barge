# 19 massive Leeds gigs and events to look forward to this October

## Some huge music stars will be performing in venues across Leeds this October

Some of the biggest names in music will appear in Leeds this October

We know it is Halloween month, so fittingly, there’s lots of treats to come. In terms of music, huge stars performing include The Pussycat Dolls, Duran Duran and home favourite Paul Heaton.

Additionally, Westlife and Milburn will be some of the acts performing. You can buy tickets for the majority of the shows listed in this article by visiting Ticketmaster and Live Nation.

As you will see, it is a packed schedule of events in Leeds this October.

Take a scroll below and see what takes your fancy.

* October 1 - Cast - Stylus, Leeds
* October 2 - The Pussycat Dolls - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* October 5 - We Are Scientists - Leeds Irish Centre
* October 6 - Westlife - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* October 6 to 10 - Leeds International Festival of Ideas - Leeds Playhouse
* October 8 - Hans Zimmer - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* October 8 - Stone Foundation - Old Woollen, Leeds
* October 10 - The Coral - Project House, Leeds
* October 10 - Transvision Vamp - Leeds O2 Academy
* October 13 - Don Broco - Testbed, Leeds
* October 16 - Danny Clement - Brudenell Social Club, Leeds
* October 17 - Milburn - Leeds O2 Academy
* October 23 - Heaven 17 - Leeds Beckett University Students Union
* October 24 - Duran Duran - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* October 24 - Paul Marshall - Hyde Park Book Club, Leeds
* October 24 - Barrieoke - Old Woollen, Leeds
* October 26 - Korn - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* October 30 - Suede - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* October 31 - Paul Heaton - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
