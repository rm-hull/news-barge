Nestled in the village of Wolsingham, this spacious end of terrace house seamlessly blends traditional character and a wealth of original features including sash windows, deep window sills, window shutters and coving with modern comforts.

Its spacious accommodation includes, on the ground floor, two generous and beautifully presented reception rooms, both centred around multi-fuel burners with fitted shelves and storage in the alcoves that flank them; a cloakroom; a small cellar/boot room positioned at the end of the hall that provides the convenience of plumbing for a tumble dryer, plus a well-proportioned kitchen.

This is home to contemporary fitted units including a plate rack and illuminated glass-fronted cabinets, a contrasting central island with additional storage and workspace as well as a breakfast bar for informal family meals and a Belfast-style sink.

![]()If you're looking for a village home that simply oozes contemporary style, this could be the one! *(Image: Weardale Property Agency)*

It has room for a range-style cooker in a tiled alcove.

Glazed double French doors not only let natural light flood into this room but also offer access to the rear garden.

Upstairs are the spacious family bathroom with a freestanding ball and claw foot bath, plus four beautifully presented bedrooms – three doubles and the other perfect for use as a nursery or home office.

![]()If you're looking for a village home that simply oozes contemporary style, this could be the one! *(Image: Weardale Property Agency)*

Externally, the property has a west-facing front garden laid to cobblestone and bordered by wrought iron fencing.

It offers space for outdoor seating and plant pots that catch the afternoon sun.

To the rear is a private and secluded garden accessed directly from the kitchen or externally via a wooden pedestrian gate from the adjacent lane.

![]()If you're looking for a village home that simply oozes contemporary style, this could be the one! *(Image: Weardale Property Agency)*

This space is laid to gravel, providing a low-maintenance yet versatile area ideal for alfresco dining or relaxing with friends and family.

The garden is further enhanced by a stone-built outbuilding and a wooden storage shed offering ample storage for tools, bikes or gardening equipment.
