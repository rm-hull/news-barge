A North Yorkshire supply chain and logistics consultancy business has gone into liquidation owing the taxman almost £80,000.

Transall Solutions Ltd was incorporated in 2013 and until recently was registered in Slingsby, near Malton.

The company entered a voluntary liquidation on August 21, with BGT of Clifton Moor appointed liquidator.

Companies House lists the sole director of Transall Solutions as Matthias Menck, 64, of Slingsby.

A liquidators’ report into the financial circumstances of the business, known as a statement of affairs, says the business had estimated and realisable assets of £68, namely cash in the bank.

There were no preferential or secondary preferential creditors – those who are paid first.

However, there were unsecured creditor claims totalling £78,922.

This was made up of £78,122 owed to HMRC in corporation tax and £800 owed to Pearsons accountants of Malton.

This gave an estimated deficiency or shortfall of £78,854.

With the business having issued and called up capital of £1, this gave a total deficiency of £78,855 outstanding.
