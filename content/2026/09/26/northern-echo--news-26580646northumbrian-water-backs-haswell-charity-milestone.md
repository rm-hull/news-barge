A County Durham charity is celebrating a major milestone with help from volunteers.

Haswell and District Mencap is celebrating 50 years at its Lisa Dixon Centre base in Haswell.

Northumbrian Water volunteers helped prepare for the celebrations through the company’s ‘Just an Hour’ scheme.

The team spent the day tidying the centre’s allotment, making crafts for the anniversary event and helping with fundraising.

Scarlett Davidson, content creator at Northumbrian Water, said: "Just an Hour is a brilliant opportunity to step away from our day-to-day roles and give something back to the community.

"We all had a fantastic day at the Lisa Dixon Centre, from getting stuck into the gardening and painting to making crafts.

"It was lovely to see how much the centre means to the people who attend, and I’m so pleased to have played a small part in helping them celebrate such a special 50th anniversary year."

Volunteers painted fences, made autumn wreaths and trimmed lavender bushes during their visit.

The lavender was turned into bundles for sale in the centre’s charity shop.

The team also made signs and pom-poms with centre users to cheer participants in an upcoming swimming triathlon.

The day helped volunteers meet centre users and learn more about its work.

Hayley Hood, chief executive officer of the Lisa Dixon Centre, said: "We’re so grateful to the team for giving their time and helping us get ready for our 50th anniversary celebrations.

"Their support has made a real difference."

Haswell and District Mencap has supported people with learning disabilities for 50 years, providing a welcoming space and activities.

The anniversary marks 50 years of service to the community.

Northumbrian Water’s ‘Just an Hour’ initiative lets employees volunteer their time and skills with organisations across the North East and beyond.

More than 1,000 colleagues have taken part this year alone.

People can support the charity by visiting its shop or café at the Lisa Dixon Centre on Burt Close, Haswell.

The centre remains a vital community resource, thanks to its staff, volunteers and supporters.
