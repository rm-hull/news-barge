A FIRE has spread to trees in a village outside York and firefighters are at the scene.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said it was called to Green Hammerton at around 2.26pm today (Saturday, September 26).




The service said crews from Acomb and Harrogate were called.

A water bowser from Tadcaster also responded.

A service spokesperson said: "The fire measured 20 metres by five metres, consisting of wood which had spread to nearby trees.

"This incident is ongoing."
