A man was taken to hospital with cuts to his upper body after being assaulted in Darlington.

Emergency services, including police and ambulance crews, were called at 6.47am on Saturday (September 26), to Northgate.

One person was treated at the scene and taken to hospital for further treatment.

A spokesperson for North East Ambulance Service said: “We were called at 6.47am today (September 26) to reports of a suspected assault on Northgate in Darlington.

“We dispatched two emergency ambulance crews who treated one patient with cuts to his upper body before conveying to hospital.”

The Northern Echo has contacted Durham Police for more information.
