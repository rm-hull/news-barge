A GROUP of youths have thrown eggs at a cyclist riding in the centre of York.




North Yorkshire Police said it happened about 8pm in Piccadilly on Thursday, September 24.

The youths, said to be three boys and one girl, aged between 14 and 15 then headed off in the direction of Coppergate, police said.

Anyone who witnessed the incident or has information is asked to contact PC 1223 Colin Irvine via email at [colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk), quoting North Yorkshire Police reference 12260188040.
