A MAN has been airlifted to hospital after an alleged "violent incident" involving a pick-up truck in a North Yorkshire coastal town.

The county's police force said the 18-year-old is in a serious but stable condition after reportedly receiving his injuries in Scarborough last night (Friday, September 25).




A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Reports were initially made at 9.45pm after an altercation at an address in Eden Drive.

"Around five minutes later, further reports were made about a man being injured by a dark-coloured pick-up truck along Seamer Road towards the Lidl store.

"Officers have made four arrests in connection - a 47-year-old man, a 36-year-old man, a 23-year-old man, and a 17-year-old boy.

"They remain in custody for questioning while enquires continue."

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for witnesses to come forward and anyone with relevant CCTV, doorbell and dashcam footage.

Anyone who can assist should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.
