Boasting both kerb appeal and a beautiful interior, this characterful home situated on the edge of Masham, yet just a couple on minutes walk from the Market Square, has much to admire.

Its spacious entrance hall with both a cloakroom and a large coat cupboard gives a first glimpse of the scale of the accommodation that lies within.

Briefly, this comprises a south-facing study perfect for use as a home office; a light and bright dual aspect sitting room centred around a focal point exposed brick open fireplace with a stone hearth and mantle piece over, and the breakfast kitchen and interconnected sun room which is at the heart of both the home and family life.

![]()A short walk from the centre of Masham, this spacious family home is packed with character *(Image: Giles Edwards Yorkshire Property)*

The breakfast kitchen encompasses a range of fitted units with wooden worksurfaces and has space for a range-style cooker with an extractor fan above as well as a table for informal family meals.

Through an archway is the sun room with a wooden floor, corner log burner set on a stone hearth, bifold doors out to the garden and French doors that open into a side garden.

The sun room easily accommodates both a dining and a seating area, with a part-glazed ceiling, large windows and bifold doors ensuring it is a light-filled space perfect for relaxing in or entertaining.

![]()A short walk from the centre of Masham, this spacious family home is packed with character *(Image: Giles Edwards Yorkshire Property)*

Completing the ground floor – which benefits from underfloor heating throughout – is a utility room off the kitchen.

On the first floor are the family bathroom and four double bedrooms, including one with Jack ‘n’ Jill access to the family bathroom, one with an en-suite shower room and the principal suite comprising a bedroom, dressing area and an en-suite shower room with a double shower cubicle.

Upstairs again are a further two double bedrooms and a stylish family bathroom plus excellent eaves storage units.

![]()A short walk from the centre of Masham, this spacious family home is packed with character *(Image: Giles Edwards Yorkshire Property)*

The property has a brick paved driveway leading to a double garage at the front and a good-sized garden laid mainly to lawn, with an evergreen hedge and well-stocked borders, a paved patio dining area, steps that lead up to a garden hot tub room – complete with hot tub, a garden shed and a log store to the rear.
