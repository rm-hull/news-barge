**There’s a warning to young people in North Yorkshire to be careful when in play parks.**

York crew were mobilised to a teenager stuck in a baby seat at 6pm on Friday 25th September **on Fordlands Road in York**.

On arrival, crews used small tools to release the teenager and make him and the seat safe to use again.
