# Man airlifted to hospital after being hit by pick-up truck as four arrested

## Four people have been arrested in Scarborough following a violent incident involving a pick-up truck which resulted in a man being airlifted to hospital.

Reports were initially made to the Force Control Room of North Yorkshire Police at 9.45pm on Friday September 25 after an altercation at an address on Eden Drive.

Around five minutes later, further reports were made about a man being injured by a dark-coloured pick-up truck along Seamer Road towards the Lidl store.

The 18-year-old injured man was attended to by paramedics before being airlifted to hospital, police said.

![Reports were initially made to the Force Control Room at 9.45pm on Friday (25 September 2026) after an altercation at an address on Eden Drive.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjM0YTgwNWU1LWIxZDgtNDgyMi1hMzI1LTRkODc0ZjgyMmU4MzpiMTAwZDc3YS1iNTcxLTQ0MmYtYmJjZC1hNjY0MWJiZWVmMTc=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


He is currently in a serious but stable condition.

Officers have made four arrests in connection with the investigation - a 47-year-old man, a 36-year-old man, a 23-year-old man, and a 17-year-old boy.

They remain in custody for questioning while enquires continue, police said.

To support the investigation, the force are appealing for witnesses to come forward and anyone with relevant CCTV, doorbell and dashcam footage.

Call North Yorkshire Police on 101.
