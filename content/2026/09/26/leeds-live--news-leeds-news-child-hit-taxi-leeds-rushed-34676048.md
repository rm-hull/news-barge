# Child hit by taxi in Leeds rushed to hospital

## West Yorkshire Police issued a statement

A child has been rushed to hospital after being hit by a taxi in Leeds.

They were hit on North Farm Road, in Gipton, at around 3.40pm yesterday, on Friday, September 25. The suffered a broken leg. West Yorkshire Police have issued a statement.

A force spokesperson said: "Police were called to reports of a road traffic collision involving a taxi and a child pedestrian on North Farm Road, Gipton, Leeds, at around 3.40pm yesterday. The child was taken to hospital with a suspected broken leg."
