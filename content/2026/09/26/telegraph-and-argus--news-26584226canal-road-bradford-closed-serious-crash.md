A serious crash has closed a major road this morning.

The incident happened on Canal Road in Bradford.

In a statement, West Yorkshire Police said the incident was "serious" and motorists should avoid the area.

A spokesperson said: "Police are at the scene of a serious collision on Canal Road, Bradford.

"The road is likely to remain closed for some time. Motorists are advised to avoid the area."

## The scene this morning

The T&A spoke to a member of the public at the scene, who told us the incident involved a motorbike and a car.

![]()The scene in Canal Road, Bradford *(Image: T&A)*

A member of the public at the scene said: "It looked really bad.

"I hope he gets well soon."

![]()The scene in Canal Road, Bradford *(Image: T&A)*

## Where did it happen?

The incident happened near the Skoda garage along Canal Road. The road is closed from the Aldi store and the Costa coffee outlet.

A number of sports playing fields are located nearby and people watching junior football reported 'hearing the bang' from the crash.

When the T&A visited the scene at about 11am, the road remained closed and a number of ambulance and police vehicles were in attendance.

Police have confirmed the road is expected to remain closed for some time.
