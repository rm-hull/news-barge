Staff at a North Yorkshire furniture company raised £1,500 for a hospice by taking on a 1,042-mile cycling challenge inspired by their work on a major healthcare redevelopment.

Colleagues from Hill Cross Furniture completed a virtual Land’s End to John O’Groats ride on static bikes for Overgate Hospice’s Big Bikeathon, part of the hospice’s corporate fundraising challenge.

The team took part after supplying furniture for Overgate’s new 16-bedroom inpatient unit in Elland, West Yorkshire.

![]()Left Laura Taylor, Group Management Accountant and Matt Waters, Group Finance Director at Hill Cross Furniture *(Image: Supplied)*

Matt Waters, group finance director at Hill Cross Furniture, said: "Working on Overgate’s new Inpatient Unit gave us a real appreciation of the care and thought that goes into creating the best possible environment for patients and their families.

"Our team wanted to do something to support the hospice beyond the furniture project, so taking on the Big Bikeathon felt like a brilliant way to get everyone involved.

"Completing 1,042 miles certainly took some determination, but knowing what we were raising the money for kept us going.

![]()Overgate Hospice *(Image: Alasdair McIntosh)*

"We’re delighted to be able to present Overgate with £1,500 and would like to thank everyone who sponsored us."

The new inpatient unit is part of Overgate’s wider Big Build redevelopment and has en-suite bedrooms, private terraces and a family lounge.

Hill Cross Furniture also supplied bespoke modular seating for the unit’s north and south wings.

![]()Overgate Hospice *(Image: Alasdair McIntosh)*

Rebecca Ryan, Head of Fundraising at Overgate Hospice, said: "We’re incredibly grateful to everyone at Hill Cross Furniture for taking on the Big Bikeathon and raising £1,500 for Overgate Hospice.

"It’s been wonderful to see their support extend beyond their work on our Big Build, with the team really getting behind the challenge.

"Every pound raised helps us continue to provide vital care and support for patients and their families across Calderdale."

![]()Overgate Hospice *(Image: Alasdair McIntosh)*

Hill Cross Furniture has built expertise in the hospice sector, working with healthcare interior designer Elaine Lewis on projects across the UK.

The company’s Sample Showcase lets patients, families and hospice teams try furniture before agreeing the final specifications.

Nurses, physiotherapists, occupational therapists and housekeeping teams give feedback to ensure the furniture is comfortable, durable and practical.

![]()Overgate Hospice *(Image: Alasdair McIntosh)*

Hill Cross Furniture is still working with Overgate Hospice and now supports phase two of the Big Build.

The next stage will add an activity room, social hub and therapy rooms.

Hill Cross Furniture is a family-owned contract furniture specialist with more than 25 years’ experience in hospitality, leisure, healthcare and commercial interiors.

The company provides design, sourcing, project management, delivery and installation.

Overgate Hospice provides specialist palliative and end-of-life care to people in Calderdale and supports their families.
