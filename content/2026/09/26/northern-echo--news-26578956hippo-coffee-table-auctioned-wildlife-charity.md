A North East man's lifelong fascination with hippos is set to make one final splash as the centrepiece of a collection spanning more than half a century goes under the hammer for charity.

The eye-catching bronze and glass coffee table, featuring a submerged hippo, was created by Scottish artist and sculptor Mark Stoddart and will be sold at Anderson & Garland’s Modern Art & Design Auction in Newcastle on September 29.

It comes from Alnwick resident Philip Angier’s collection, built with his wife Elizabeth over more than 50 years and containing more than 1,000 hippo figurines, artefacts and soft toys.

![]()Scottish artist Mark Stoddart with his Hippo table *(Image: Supplied)*

Earlier this year, the couple held a two-day sale at Alnwick Castle, raising £4,500 for Northumberland Wildlife Trust and selling more than two-thirds of their collection.

They held back the bronze coffee table for a special auction send-off.

Harry Pickup, auctioneer at Anderson & Garland, said: "It isn’t every day you get to sell a bronze and glass hippo coffee table, and it’s rarer still to sell one with such a lovely story behind it.

"Philip’s passion for these wonderful animals shines through in every piece of his collection, and we’re delighted to be able to help him give this one a great send off while raising money for such a worthwhile cause."

![]()Hippo table *(Image: Supplied)*

Titled Hippo Childhood and numbered 61 of 99, the table comes with a certificate of authenticity and is expected to fetch £3,500 to £4,500.

Mike Pratt, chief executive of Northumberland Wildlife Trust, said: "Philip and Elizabeth, who have donated the table, really are an inspiration to others with their love of wildlife and passion for fundraising.

"They have been supporters of the Trust for over 30 years, so I am delighted that money raised at the auction will be a fitting legacy to their fundraising efforts."

The auction takes place at Anderson House, Crispin Court, Newbiggin Lane, Westerhope, Newcastle.
