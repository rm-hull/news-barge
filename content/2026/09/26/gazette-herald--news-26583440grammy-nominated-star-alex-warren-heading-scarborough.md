A chart-topping artist has announced a major outdoor show as part of a new world tour.

Alex Warren will perform at Scarborough Open Air Theatre on Wednesday, June 30, as part of the Finding (More) Family On The Road world tour, which includes seven dates across the UK and Ireland.

The Grammy-nominated singer’s 2027 tour will also take in Cardiff, Belfast, Dublin, Glasgow, Exeter, and London, before concluding in Paris.

A spokesperson said: "Grammy Award-nominated and chart-topping multi-platinum artist Alex Warren will head to the Yorkshire coast next summer as part of his 2027 world tour.

“Warren is playing seven dates in the UK and Ireland as part of the Finding (More) Family On The Road world tour, including a night at Scarborough Open Air Theatre on Wednesday 30 June."

The tour follows the release of Warren’s second album, Wildchild, which reached number one on the Official Albums Chart in August.

He celebrated the achievement with a homemade Number 1 award crafted by his wife, Kouvr.

The full list of UK and Ireland tour dates includes:

* June 20 – Cardiff, Blackweir Fields
* June 21 – Belfast, Belsonic
* June 22 – Dublin, Malahide Castle
* June 25 – Glasgow, Live At Glasgow Green
* June 26 – Exeter, Live at Powderham
* June 30 – Scarborough, Scarborough Open Air Theatre
* July 3 – London, Crystal Palace Park Live

Warren will conclude the tour in Paris on July 13.

Tickets go on general sale at 10am on Friday, October 2.

More information is available at scarboroughopenairtheatre.com.
