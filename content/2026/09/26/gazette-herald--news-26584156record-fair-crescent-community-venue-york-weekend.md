VINYL enthusiasts will be delighted to know that a great selection of records will be on sale at a city centre event this weekend.

The Crescent Record Fair is returning to The Crescent Community Venue, just off Blossom Street, on Sunday, September 27 from 11am to 3pm.




The annual event brings together specialist dealers and collectors.

Organisers said eight stallholders will be present - previous events have featured sought-after first pressings alongside more recent releases by current artists.

The May 2025 fair saw eight traders occupying 20 tables, offering thousands of records, including collectable and rare LPs and singles.

The event is free to attend.
