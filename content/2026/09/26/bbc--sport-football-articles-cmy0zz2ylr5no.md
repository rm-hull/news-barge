Alex Baena curled in Spain's second goal

**England were beaten 3-2 in an entertaining game by world champions Spain in the Nations League - the Three Lions' first match since this summer's World Cup.**

Lamine Yamal gave Spain the lead before goals from Anthony Gordon and Harry Kane put England ahead.

Kane then missed a penalty before Alex Baena curled in to level the score, with Mikel Oyarzabal scoring the winner.

Here's how we rated England's players - and you can have your say in the comments at the bottom.

**James Trafford:** Showed early on his ability to play with the ball at his feet but conceded after just two minutes when Guehi gifted the ball to Lamine Yamal. An important save stopped Torres making it 2-0 in the first half.**6Jarell Quansah:** Continued at right-back after showing what he could do in the position at the World Cup. However he gave the ball away for Spain's second goal.**6Elliot Anderson:** Nearly punished for giving the ball away in a dangerous position in the first half but Ferran Torres rolled his shot wide. Replaced after 70 minutes.**6Jude Bellingham:** The Real Madrid midfielder produced another dominant display and was unlucky to be on the losing side. Curled just wide with the score level but a quiet night for the winger.**6Anthony Gordon:** The Barcelona winger scored an excellent goal on the counter-attack.
