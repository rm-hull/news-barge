A serving Durham Police officer and veteran has appeared in court accused of child sex offences.

Haydyn Brunsdon, a police constable, was in the dock at Teesside Magistrates Court this morning.

Durham Police previously said he had been charged with two counts of sexual communication with a child, but those charges were revised in court to two counts of attempted sexual communication with a child.

The 31-year-old who has served on the force for less than two years, wore a light grey T-shirt as he spoke only to confirm his name and details.

Prosecuting, Paul Doney told the hearing the alleged offences took place between June and September of this year.

Mr Doney added that Brunsdon, previously of an address in Darlington, was allegedly communicating with two female decoys aged 13 and 15.

Brunsdon is also said to have served in the Royal Navy for ten years.

Bench chair Rhys Maybrey granted Brunsdon bail.
