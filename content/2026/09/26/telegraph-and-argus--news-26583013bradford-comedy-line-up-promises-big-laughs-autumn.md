A COMEDY line-up is promising big laughs this autumn.

Rosie Holt, Seann Walsh and Fatiha El-Ghorri will take to the stage at The Studio, Bradford, for a series of performances in October, November and December.

A Bradford Theatres spokesperson said: "As the nights are getting shorter, cheer yourself up with some top comedy at The Studio, Bradford.

"This Autumn, we welcome Rosie Holt, Seann Walsh and Fatiha El-Ghorri!"

Ms Holt will open the season with her sci-fi satire The Illegal Aliens Have Landed on Friday, October 16, while Mr Walsh will perform on Saturday, November 7.

A spokesperson said: "Don't miss his trademark high-energy exasperation and razor-sharp observations that leave audiences in stitches and on their feet all around the country.

"This particular show is named after the thing he says most in life – This is Torture."

Ms El-Ghorri, a breakout star from Taskmaster, brings her debut stand-up tour Cockney Stacking Doll to The Studio on Saturday, December 12.

A clean, family-friendly performance will take place at 3pm, with the later 7.30pm show already sold out.

Tickets start from £21 and can be booked at bradford-theatres.co.uk or by calling 01274 432000.
