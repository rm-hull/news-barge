House-hunters can snap up this luxury County Durham barn conversion, complete with an indoor pool, detached cottage and 28 acres of land.

Wadsworth Barns, near Crook, has been brought to the market with an asking price of £1.8 million.

The period conversion blends traditional stonework with contemporary design and offers more than 6,400 square feet across the main house and cottage.

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

At its heart is an open-plan kitchen and breakfast room with bespoke cabinets, integrated appliances and a large island with bar seating.

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

The property also has a reception room with a feature fireplace, a formal dining room and a light-filled garden room opening onto the grounds.

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

One of the home's standout features is its dedicated leisure suite, centred around a large heated indoor swimming pool.

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

The space also includes a relaxing seating area and a mezzanine overlooking the pool, creating an impressive setting for entertaining guests or unwinding at home.

The main house has four bedrooms and three bathrooms, including a principal suite with a dressing room and spacious en-suite.

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

A second bedroom also benefits from en-suite facilities.

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

A private driveway leads to extensive parking, a double garage and a detached triple garage.

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

The landscaped grounds offer countryside views, while a sheltered courtyard provides year-round entertaining space with composite decking, a stone fireplace and log burner.

A detached two-bedroom cottage could house guests, extended family or holiday visitors.

![]() *(Image: STRUTT AND PARKER)*

The cottage has its own kitchen, utility room, sitting room and dining area.

Modern features include roof-mounted solar panels and biomass heating, which can improve energy efficiency.

Wadsworth Barns is located in the peaceful hamlet of High Grange on the outskirts of Crook, offering a rural setting while remaining within easy reach of amenities in Crook, Bishop Auckland and Durham.

With four reception rooms, four bedrooms, an indoor pool and spa area, detached guest accommodation and 28 acres of land, the property offers a rare opportunity to secure a luxury countryside home in County Durham.
