Firefighters extinguished a blaze involving a fly-tipped mattress and tyres.

West Yorkshire Fire Service was called to Hallwood Green in Bradford at around 9pm on Thursday September 24, after reports of a fire in the open.

Images shared on social media showed several mattresses burning in a field off Roundwood Avenue.

A spokesman for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called to land at Hallwood Green at 2054 to reports of a fire in the open."

One crew attended and extinguished a fly-tipped mattress and tyres which were on fire.

The scene was left the scene in the hands of police.
