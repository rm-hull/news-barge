Police are appealing for information to help locate a 16-year-old boy who has been reported missing from Bradford.

Mohammed was reported missing at 10am today, September 26.

He is described as an Asian male, around 5ft 10in tall, with short black shaven hair.

He was last seen wearing a grey hooded top, black trousers and black Nike trainers.

Officers are asking members of the public to come forward if they have seen him or know where he may be.

Anyone who believes they have seen Mohammed or has information about his whereabouts is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police on 101 or via the force's online Live Chat service, quoting reference 1774 of September 25.
