Greggs has opened a 'much larger' store in a North East town.

The Manor Walks branch opened on Friday, September 25, and is more than twice the size of Greggs’ former unit there.

Customers now have 4,277 sq ft of space in the heart of the mall.

![]()Greggs opens a 4,277 sq ft store at Manor Walks in Cramlington *(Image: Greggs)*

Lee Farrier, head of commercial and workspace at Advance Northumberland, said: "It’s fantastic to see the new Greggs ready to open at Manor Walks.

"We know just how popular Greggs is with our customers, so to see the brand more than double its footprint at the centre is something we’re really pleased to celebrate.

"The opening is another positive step in the continued evolution of Manor Walks and importantly, gives our customers an even better experience from a brand they already know and love.
