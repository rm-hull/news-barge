Post Office services are returning to a North Yorkshire community after its branch closed when the convenience store housing it shut.

The Esk Close Post Office in Guisborough will reopen under new management at 1pm on Monday, October 5.

It closed on August 19 when the Morrisons Daily store at the same address shut.

Colette McAtter, corporate partner lead at Post Office Ltd, said: "We know how important a Post Office is to a community and we are delighted to soon restore service at this location."

The branch at 4 Esk Close will reopen in the same premises under a temporary postmaster while Post Office Ltd seeks a permanent solution.

The reopened branch will offer all its previous services.

The branch will open from 6am to 9pm Monday to Saturday and from 7am to 9pm on Sundays, offering 104 hours a week.

Customers of major UK banks will be able to use banking, postal and bill-payment services.
