A 761-acre County Durham farm is up for sale for £3.6 million, offering an "exceptionally rare" opportunity.

High Hedley Hope Farm, near Tow Law, is being marketed as a large-scale investment property combining productive farmland, woodland, and established dairy and renewable energy infrastructure.

The site includes a five-bedroom farmhouse, a three-bedroom bungalow, a full suite of livestock buildings, and an anaerobic digestion plant.

Andrew Black, rural agency director at Savills York, said: "Opportunities of this scale and nature are exceptionally rare in the market.

"High Hedley Hope Farm combines a substantial ring-fenced landholding with significant and diversified income streams, extensive modern dairy infrastructure, and a renewable energy asset that offers considerable long-term potential."

![]()High Hedley Hope Farm, near Tow Law. *(Image: SAVILLS)*

The property is currently let under a Farm Business Tenancy until June 2041, generating £120,000 per year in rental income.

The AD plant, leased until June 2044, brings in an additional £30,000 annually, with the option to extend the lease until 2064 - it was acquired by Bio Capital in 2025 and is currently being refurbished.

![]()High Hedley Hope Farm, near Tow Law. *(Image: SAVILLS)*

Farm buildings cover nearly 100,000 square feet and are equipped to support a 600-cow dairy herd, including a 70-point rotary parlour installed in 2019.

Of the total acreage, 657 acres are productive farmland and 84 acres are woodland.

"It is a unique offering that we expect to attract interest from a broad range of buyers across the agricultural, investment and energy sectors."
