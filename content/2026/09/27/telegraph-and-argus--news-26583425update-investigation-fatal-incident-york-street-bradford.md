An investigation into the death of a 22-year-old man after a large sheet of glass fell from a lorry remains ongoing, the Health and Safety Executive (HSE) has confirmed.

The investigation was launched following the fatal incident on York Street, off Thornton Road in Bradford, on the evening of July 23.

In an update provided to the Telegraph & Argus, a HSE spokesperson said: "The joint investigation with West Yorkshire Police is ongoing."

West Yorkshire Police were called at 10.23pm after colleagues from Yorkshire Ambulance Service reported that a man had suffered serious injuries.

The 22-year-old was pronounced dead a short time later.

## What we know so far

Witnesses and local residents reported that the man had become trapped beneath a large pane of glass that had fallen from a lorry.

![]()Aerial photo as the scene unfolds on York street, where a young man died aged 22. *(Image: Mike Simmonds)*

The incident prompted a major emergency response, with police, firefighters and paramedics attending the scene.

A police cordon remained in place while forensic investigators examined the lorry and its load.

Images from the scene showed specialist officers carrying out detailed examinations of equipment as part of the joint police and HSE investigation.

## Resident reaction

The incident shocked residents living nearby, with several describing hearing shouting and seeing emergency crews rush to the scene.

One parent previously told the Telegraph & Argus that their son had witnessed the aftermath while walking across a nearby field.

They said the youngster had been left "stood there in shock" after seeing efforts to save the injured man.

![]()The police scene *(Image: Newsquest)*

The parent said: "I'm worried about my son because of what he's seen. He can't get that out of his head."

Other residents recalled hearing a loud bang followed by screams, before realising the seriousness of the situation as emergency vehicles arrived.

One neighbour said local people tried to help but were unable to free the man.

## The investigation continues

In the immediate aftermath, West Yorkshire Police confirmed enquiries were being carried out jointly with the HSE, while HSE inspectors also attended the scene.

![]()A forensic investigator examines a lorry at the scene on York Street, Bradford, following an incident in which a 22-year-old man died. *(Image: Mike Simmonds)*

More than two months on, the HSE has now confirmed that the investigation remains active.

No further details about the circumstances surrounding the incident, or any potential enforcement action, have been released while enquiries continue.

Anyone with information that may assist investigators should contact West Yorkshire Police.
