Settle Fire and Rescue crews and Skipton and Ripon swift water rescue worked with Mountain Rescue to rescue a Labrador called Penny.

Penny had become stuck 40ft down a cliff in the Ingleton waterfalls trail in Ingleton.

Mountain rescue set up rope rescue and Penny was successfully rescued and reunited with her owner.
