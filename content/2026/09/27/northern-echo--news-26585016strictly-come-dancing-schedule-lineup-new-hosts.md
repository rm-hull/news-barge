Strictly Come Dancing has returned for its 2026 series with new hosts, professional dancers and celebrities.

If you’re looking for key information about the new Strictly series, look no further.

Here we will share all the Strictly information from the full celebrity lineup, the new hosts, professional dancers and more.

## When are the live shows and when is the Strictly final?

The live shows start on Saturday, September 26 and will air every Saturday until December 19 – the final weekend before Christmas.

December 19 will be the weekend of the grand final and the week viewers will find out who takes home the Glitterball trophy.

## Who are the new Strictly presenters?

Claudia Winkleman and Tess Daly left Strictly at the end of the 2025 series and have been replaced by three hosts.

Strictly professional Johannes Radebe will host alongside TV presenter Emma Willis and comedian Josh Widdicombe.

The BBC show has never had three people hosting it at one time until now.

## Who are the Strictly judges?

The Strictly judges haven’t changed for the 2026 series, so you can expect the same four familiar faces to be judging the performances.

Craig Revel Horwood, Anton Du Beke, Motsi Mabuse and Head Judge Shirley Ballas will raise their paddles as they rate each dance.
