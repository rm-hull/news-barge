## 'Super' garden centre next to historic Yorkshire country house with 'helpful' staff

Castle Howard has its own garden centre to explore. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

Most people would take a trip out to Castle Howard to explore the beautiful country house or take a walk around its gardens.

However, Castle Howard is also home to a great garden centre that is worth a visit if you are looking for ways to spruce up your garden.

The Castle Howard Garden Centre prides itself on its “fantastic value and traditional customer services”.

## What is Castle Howard Garden Centre like?

The Castle Howard Garden Centre offers a “huge selection” of plants, herbs, ornamental trees, and roses.

You will be able to find plants to suit your garden no matter what time of the year you visit, with the team available to help you.

The store also has garden furniture, terracotta pots, and any garden equipment you might need to help you create your ideal space.

The garden centre even has a pets and wildlife section with pet beds and toys as well as bird boxes, bird feeders, and lots of treats.

The Castle Howard Garden Centre has lots of free parking outside and is free to enter.

### What do reviews say about the Castle Howard Garden Centre?

Castle Howard has a rating of 4.3 out of 5 on Tripadvisor, with several people leaving reviews about the garden centre.

One person said: “Excellent Garden Centre with extensive range of well tended plants at competitive prices.

“The staff were very helpful and knowledgeable about growing conditions.

“The estate gardens were magnificent considering the many visitors tramping around the lawn borders.

“Don’t think we could have walked further than the faraway ‘temples’ so didn’t view the House as well as estate, on this occasion.

“We enjoyed well prepared and tasty food, cakes and coffees from the cafeteria takeaway. Well done - we will be back soon.”

Another person said: “All the reviews for Castle Howard are generally for the main house and beautiful gardens, but we often like to visit their super garden centre.
