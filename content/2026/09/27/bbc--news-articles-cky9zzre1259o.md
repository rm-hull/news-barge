![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: 'Noel's crying his heart out.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/2dc6/live/dcbe05d0-b9eb-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

The Premier League drama, after Manchester City was found guilty of breaking the Premier League's financial rules, features again on the front page of some of Sunday's papers. Oasis's Noel Gallagher is "crying his heart out" over the news, the Daily Star reports, as the musician says he is "sad for City fans"

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday People reads: 'Gutted for fans.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/3ea5/live/3095cc60-b9ec-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

"True Blue Noel Gallagher" is on the cover of the Sunday People, saying he is "gutted for fans" over the Man City financial rule "breaches". Manchester City have always denied wrongdoing and plan to appeal

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Mirror reads: 'Losing Dad fuels my social care mission.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/69b8/live/c45c1c20-b9eb-11f1-a430-4d16ee157c41.jpg)

Andy Burnham's "social care mission" was inspired by "seeing his dad Roy suffer with Alzheimer's", the Sunday Mirror reports. Burnham's father died earlier this month. Ahead of Labour's annual conference, the prime minister said "I won't leave office without having grasped this nettle," the paper reports

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: 'Anguish as couples lose thousands in "global IVF disaster".'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1536/cpsprodpb/c2ab/live/c95562c0-b9ed-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

The Independent leads with an image of Prime Minister Andy Burnham, with his wife, arriving at a Labour conference at Liverpool on Saturday. Elsewhere, the paper reports on dozens of patients losing thousands of pounds in savings in a "global IVF disaster". The paper says an international clinic filed for insolvency "leaving many in limbo mid-treatment". Investors in the company that runs the clinic said claims for prepaid treatment can be registered with the insolvency administrator

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Telegraph reads: 'Fiennes family "snubbed by police".'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/a4bd/live/c2bf4a20-b9ed-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

The Telegraph leads again on the pleas from the family of retired explorer Sir Ranulph Fiennes. In recent days the paper has reported on allegations regarding the care of Sir Ranulph, who has been open about his battle with Parkinson's disease

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Express reads: 'When will you stop this tragedy?'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/e063/live/196a8df0-b9ec-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

The Sunday Express headlines on the deaths of people crossing the Channel on small boats, asking the government: "When will you stop this tragedy?" The government has previously said its "progress this year in tackling small boats is indisputable"

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Times reads: 'Hamas chief's bodyguard "plotted terror" from UK home.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/85c1/live/c48311d0-b9fb-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

A former bodyguard to the leader of Hamas secured asylum and citizenship in the UK before "plotting" an attack in Europe ahead of the second anniversary of the 7 October attacks, the Sunday Times reports. The man, who isn't named for legal reasons, moved to the UK more than a decade ago, according to the paper - and now faces extradition to Germany

![The headline on the front page of the Observer reads: 'The most powerful woman in Britain.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/770/cpsprodpb/6351/live/f2cd81c0-b9f0-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.png)

Meanwhile, the Observer splashes on who it calls the "most powerful woman in Britain" - Louise Haigh, the first secretary of state. Haigh, "Burnham's de facto deputy" has brought her "Sheffield steel to Westminster", the paper says

![The headline on the front page of the Sun on Sunday reads: 'Hands off my man Shirley.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/8cfc/live/480b0c20-b9ec-11f1-a430-4d16ee157c41.jpg)

And as dancers take to the Stricly Come Dancing ballroom for the first time, the Sun on Sunday has a "Strictly warning" to head judge Shirley Ballas from her new partner's ex-fiance
