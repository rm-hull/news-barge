Dozens turned out for one of the region's best-loved agricultural events as the Langdon Sheep Show returned under glorious sunshine.

Our pictures capture the atmosphere of the event, with proud owners presenting their sheep for judging, spectators gathering around the show ring, and plenty of smiling faces enjoying the warm weather.

![]()Langdon Beck show

The popular show is regarded as one of the highlights of the agricultural calendar, with Swaledale sheep taking centre stage as exhibitors competed for top honours.

Away from the sheep classes, visitors also enjoyed browsing trade stands, listening to music from the Teesdale Silver Band and trying their luck with a range of prizes on offer throughout the day.

The produce and industrial tent proved a major attraction, featuring everything from impressive cauliflowers and onions to carrots and other home-grown vegetables entered into competition by keen growers.

With blue skies overhead and a strong turnout throughout the day, the event once again showcased the community spirit and agricultural heritage that make it a favourite in the Teesdale calendar.
