A GHOSTLY adventure is set to bring spine-tingling stories to life in the heart of the city.

Whispers in the Wool City is a free guided walking tour in Bradford featuring ghost tales, local history, and atmospheric animation.

Organised by Bradford BID, the event will run on October 29 and 30 with tours at 2pm, 4pm, and 6pm.

Leanne Holmes, project manager for Bradford BID, said: "Bradford has a rich and fascinating past, with stories hidden around every corner.

"Whispers in the Wool City brings some of those tales to life in a fun, accessible way.

"We’re inviting people to gather their friends and family, wrap up warm or in their favourite fancy dress, and join us for an unforgettable journey through the city’s more mysterious side."

The six-stop walking tour will explore some of the hidden legends and eerie history woven into the streets of Bradford.

Booking is essential via the Bradford BID website.
