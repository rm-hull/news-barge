Following an arson incident reported on 31 July on land off Milford Road in Sherburn in Elmet, North Yorkshire Police launched an investigation and subsequently appealed for information from the public.

As a result of enquiries undertaken by officers and the subsequent media appeal, a young person came forward and admitted responsibility for the offence. They accepted their involvement and have been dealt with by way of a Community Resolution Disposal (CRD).Following an arson incident reported on 31 July on land off Milford Road in Sherburn in Elmet, North Yorkshire Police launched an investigation and subsequently appealed for information from the public.

In order to address the behaviour and reduce the risk of future offending, the young person has been referred to complete a Fire Safe Course delivered by North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service. The course is designed to educate young people about the dangers and potential consequences of fire-setting, whilst encouraging positive decision-making in the future.

PC Nicholas Woods of the Tadcaster and Rural Neighbourhood Policing Team said: “Deliberately setting fires can have serious consequences, placing members of the public, emergency services, wildlife and property at risk. Whilst this incident was resolved through a CRD, it is important that young people understand the dangers associated with fire-setting behaviour. I welcome the young person’s acceptance of responsibility and hope that the Fire Safe Course will provide a valuable opportunity for education and reflection.”
