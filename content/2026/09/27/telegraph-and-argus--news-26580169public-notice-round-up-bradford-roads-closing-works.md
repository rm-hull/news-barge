BRADFORD residents face a series of road closures due to scheduled works.

Allerton Lane will be closed for street lighting improvements from Old Road to Beck Bridge Lane.

This closure will start on October 5 at 8am and is expected to end on October 23 at 6pm, although the council has noted it could extend up to 18 months if needed.

The closure won't affect vehicles involved in the works, and pedestrian access will remain open.

Alternative routes for affected vehicles include other sections of Allerton Lane, as well as Cote Lane, Allerton Road, Saffron Drive, Bell Dean Road, Thornton Road, School Green, and Old Road.

Blamires Place in Bradford will shut to traffic for water works on October 8 and 9.

This closure will affect the area between the junctions with Great Horton Road and Back Great Horton Road South.

The road will be closed from 7pm on October 8 and will reopen at 6am on October 9.

The closure can be extended for up to 18 months if necessary.

Vehicles used for the works will be allowed through, and pedestrian access will be maintained at all times.

Drivers affected by the closure will be able to use Blamires Place (unaffected section), Blamires Street, Hollybank Road, and Great Horton Road.

Lidget Place in Bradford will also be closed for water works from October 7.

The closure will affect the road from outside No. 65 to outside No. 79.

Work-related vehicles will have access, while pedestrian routes will remain open.

Alternative routes for affected drivers are via the unaffected section of Lidget Place, Beckside Road, and Lidget Terrace.

The restriction will be active from 8am on October 7 to 6pm on October 9.

Hill Top Road in Thornton will shut temporarily for telecommunication works next week.

The closure will be in place from the road's junction with West Lane to its junction with Long Row from 8am on September 28 to 5pm on October 2.

Drivers will be diverted via Hill Top Road (unaffected section), West Lane, Lower Heights Road, Rock Lane, Back Heights Road, and Half Acre Road.

Caroline Street will be temporarily closed due to gas works from 8am on September 28 until 5pm on November 13.

The shutdown won't affect vehicles working on the road section, and pedestrian access will be upheld throughout.

Alternative routes for drivers include the unaffected section of Caroline Street, Victoria Road, Saltaire Road, Bingley Road, Hirst Lane, and Dallam Avenue.

Gresham Avenue will be closed for water works from 7am on September 28 until 7pm on October 2.

No alternative routes have been advised, but pedestrian access will remain open, and vehicles involved in the works will be exempt from the prohibition.
