A stunning manor house that feels a world away from Durham city centre has gone on the market for £1.95 million.

Fernhill Manor in Crossgate Moor has five bedrooms, extensive gardens and a separate cottage on the city’s edge.

Despite being just a short distance from Durham's shops, restaurants and attractions, the property enjoys panoramic countryside views and a private position hidden behind mature trees and landscaping.

![]()Fernhill Manor

The detached home extends to almost 3,900 square feet and combines period features with modern comforts throughout.

Inside, the property boasts a spacious reception hall with a mosaic tiled floor, alongside a living room and formal dining room, both featuring fireplaces and large bay windows overlooking the grounds.

![]()Fernhill Manor

![]()Fernhill Manor

There is also a family room with a wood burning stove, a private office and a country style kitchen complete with an Aga, Belfast sink and central island.

A bespoke utility room and separate laundry room lead off the kitchen.

Upstairs, the manor has five double bedrooms, including one with its own ensuite bathroom, as well as a luxurious family bathroom.

![]()Fernhill Manor

One of the bedrooms is currently used as a games room and features a full sized snooker table.

With windows on three sides, the room offers sweeping views across the gardens and surrounding countryside.

![]()Fernhill Manor

![]()Fernhill Manor

Outside, the landscaped grounds include lawns, mature planting, a large patio area and a courtyard garden.

The property is accessed via a private gated driveway screened by trees and shrubs, creating a high level of privacy despite its city location.

The listing also highlights outline planning permission for a rear orangery and a substantial side garage, offering future owners the opportunity to expand the home further.

As well as the main house, the estate includes a separate self-contained cottage.

The additional property features a kitchen with dining space and a living room on the ground floor, while upstairs there is a bedroom with an ensuite shower room.

Estate agents describe Fernhill Manor as offering the best of both worlds, combining a secluded countryside feel with easy access to Durham city centre.

With its period character, extensive grounds and separate guest accommodation, the property is among the most expensive homes currently on the market in County Durham.
