POLICE have issued an appeal after a stolen bank card was used at "various locations" in a coastal North Yorkshire town centre.

The theft took place in the early hours of Sunday, August 30, 2026 and the card was used in the middle of Scarborough.




North Yorkshire Police released images of a man the force said it would like to speak to, saying he may have information which could assist its investigation into the theft.

A force spokesperson said: "If you have any information, please contact the officer in charge PC Tyson 55 by email [Dom.Tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:Dom.Tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

"Or you can call 101 with the reference 12260169947.

"Alternatively, you can contact Crimestoppers anonymously with the reference number."
