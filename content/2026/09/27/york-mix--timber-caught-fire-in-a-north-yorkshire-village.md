A crew from Acomb responded to a fire to a timber stack measuring 20m by 30m on The Green in Green Hammerton.

The crew requested further assistance from a water bowser from Tadcaster.

Crews used a 45mm hose and ground monitor to extinguish tit just before 10 this morning, Sunday 27th September.
