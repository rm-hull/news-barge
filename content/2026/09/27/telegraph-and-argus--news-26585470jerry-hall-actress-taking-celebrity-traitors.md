Jerry Hall is an American actress and model who is taking on the second series of the Celebrity Traitors.

After the success of the first series that saw Alan Carr win the entire prize pot for his charity, the Celebrity Traitors is returning.

Before you decide who to root for on the show, you might want to find out more about each contestant including Jerry Hall.

## **Who is Jerry Hall?**

Jerry Hall was discovered by fashion agent Claude Haddad while she was in the French Riviera.

She then moved to Paris and lived with Grace Jones and Jessica Lange where she began her modelling career.

Jerry featured on the front of many magazine covers including Italian Vogue and Cosmopolitan.

She has also starred in a range of films including Urban Cowboy and Tim Burton’s Batman.

Jerry made her professional stage debut playing Cherie in a revival of Bus Stop and went on to reprise the role for her West End stage debut.

In 1990, Jerry got married to the Rolling Stones singer Mick Jagger and had four children together but they ended up separating in 1999.

In 1993, she starred in the British television series Cluedo and in The Detectives.

Jerry has also made appearances in shows like Hotel Babylon and French and Saunders.

In 2016, Jerry married media business giant Rupert Murdoch just eight weeks after announcing their engagement.

Jerry filed for divorce in 2022 and cited irreconcilable differences as the reason.

As well as joining the cast of the Celebrity Traitors, Jerry starred on another BBC competition show in 2012 when she was a contestant on Strictly Come Dancing.

She was partnered with Anton Du Beke and was the second celebrity to be eliminated.
