A “truly one of a kind” York home has hit the market – for £1.75 million.

The six-bedroom Georgian townhouse is for sale at 4 Clifton.

The family home is believed to have been built around 1825 by Thomas Bell and William Belleby, according to Ashtons which is marketing the property.

![]()Inside 4 Clifton as the home hits the market for £1.75 million *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

Ashtons said the house contains an “abundance of original features, including classic proportions, high ceilings and fine period detailing throughout”.

“This exceptional home offers highly versatile accommodation arranged over four floors,” a spokesperson for the estate agent said.

![]()Inside 4 Clifton as the home hits the market for £1.75 million *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

Inside there is a reception hall and a staircase rising throughout the home.

On the ground floor are two reception rooms, a spacious breakfast kitchen with adjoining utility and storage rooms and a cloak room.

The first floor features a grand drawing room with an adjoining sitting room.

Also on the first floor is the main bedroom with an en-suite bathroom.

Five more bedrooms are on the third and fourth floors, along with the family bathroom and another en-suite toilet.

Meanwhile, a “substantial cellar” provides more storage space beneath the house, Ashtons said.

Outside, the home has a forecast garden to the front and a large walled garden to the rear.

“Despite just moments from York city centre, the garden is wonderfully secluded, with an almost rural feel,” the Ashtons spokesperson said.
