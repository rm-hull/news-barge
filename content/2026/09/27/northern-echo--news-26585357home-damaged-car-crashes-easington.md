A home has been boarded up after a car smashed into the front of it in a County Durham village.

Emergency crews were called to East View in Easington Colliery at 3.18pm on Saturday (September 26).

Firefighters helped make the home safe after it was left with a hole in the wall, and the gas board dealt with a suspected gas leak.

Pictures show a red car still outside the property with its bonnet up and airbag deployed, while substantial damage can be seen to the front of the house.

![]()The damaged house in East View, Easington Colliery.

Bricks have been left strewn across the ground including from a small outhouse building, with fencing knocked down.

No injuries were reported.

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service said: "We were alerted to a collision between a car and a house in Easington Colliery at 3.18pm on Saturday.

"We dispatched an emergency ambulance crew, a hazardous area response team crew, and a clinical team leader.

"Fortunately there were no injuries. We kept one crew on scene while the fire service and gas company secured the property against a suspected gas leak before standing down."

A County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service spokesperson said: "We attended an incident yesterday in Easington village involving one private motor vehicle and a domestic property.
