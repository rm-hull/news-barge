A cordon will remain in place for "some time" after Darlington mum Leanne Park was 'murdered' above a town-centre pub.

A 27-year-old man arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender has been released on bail.

![]()Floral tributes at the scene

![]()Floral tributes at the scene

Crime scene investigators are continuing to examine the scene four days after the incident, and Durham Police said a cordon outside the pub is expected to remain in place for “some time”.

Floral tributes for Leanne, who was paid tribute by her family as the "best mam, nana, daughter and friend" continue to grow outside.

One tribute reads: “Leanne you were taken too soon. You will be forever missed.”

While another reads: “RIP to my bestest friend. I will miss you forever.”

They said in a statement: “She was loved by everyone and would do anything for anyone.

“She was the life and soul of the party and loved to dance.

“We are grateful for all the kind messages and the support since her death. We ask that everyone remembers her for the kind-hearted woman she was.”

Her colleagues at the Ventress Hall care home in Darlington previously said Leanne would "do anything for anybody".
