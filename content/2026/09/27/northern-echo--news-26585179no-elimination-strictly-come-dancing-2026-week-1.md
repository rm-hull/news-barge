There will be no elimination on Strictly Come Dancing this weekend.

The first live show of the series aired on Saturday (September 26), giving viewers and judges their first look at the new pairings.

Judges Craig Revel Horwood, Motsi Mabuse, Anton Du Beke, and Shirley Ballas have returned to the judging panel again in 2026.

It was also the first live show for the new Strictly presenting team of Emma Willis, Johannes Radebe and Josh Widdicombe.

They have replaced long-time hosts Tess Daly and Claudia Winkleman, who stepped down last year.

## Strictly Come Dancing week 1 scores and leaderboard

Saturday's live show opened with a group performance from the judges and professionals.

Will Best and Dianne Buswell then kicked off the 2026 series with a samba to Rock DJ by Robbie Williams.

John Nellis and Katya Jones topped the leaderboard with a foxtrot to Ordinary by Alex Warren, scoring 31 points.

At the bottom with 15 points were Shaun Wright-Phillips and Alexis Warr, Melanie Walters and Aljaz Skorjanec, and Dame Sarah Storey and Julian Caillon.

## Why is there no elimination on Strictly Come Dancing this week?

There will be no elimination on Strictly Come Dancing this week.

Scores will instead be carried over to week two, when viewers will have their first opportunity to vote for their favourites.

The BBC said: "For now, our couples can enjoy their dance floor debut safe in the knowledge that they’ll be back for more."

Voting can be done via the BBC website (viewers must have a BBC account) or by scanning the QR code displayed on screen during the show.

## Is Strictly Come Dancing on tonight?

Strictly Come Dancing usually airs on both Saturday and Sunday nights.

The Sunday show is typically the results programme, where the bottom two couples compete in a dance-off following a public vote, with one then sent home by the judges.

However, with no elimination in week one, there will be no show tonight (Sunday, September 27).
