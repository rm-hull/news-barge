Bradford is known as the curry capital thanks to its huge variety of curry houses found dotted around the city.

With so much choice, it might be hard for you to choose where the best place to go is.

Having been open since 1979, you might want to try the "phenomenal" Mumtaz on Great Horton Road.

## What is Mumtaz in Bradford like?

Mumtaz has a huge range of items on its menu to choose from, including chaat, samosas, and small plates to start with.

Whether you fancy some chana chaat, pani puri or a few vegetable samosas to kick off your evening, you can enjoy them at Mumtaz.

In terms of the restaurant’s mains, you can choose from the tandoor and grill specials like chicken pakora, lamb chops, and masala fish.

There is also a range of curries on offer that can be shared with your whole family, including lamb dishes like lamb Rogan josh, tava gosht, and keema aloo.

![]()Mumtaz also has lots of fish and vegetarian dishes on offer. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

If you prefer chicken, there are lots of options like classic butter chicken, chicken methi and chicken korma for those who do not like spicy food.

There are also lots of vegetarian and fish dishes so everyone can be catered for.

To help cool you down, Mumtaz has a range of yoghurt-based lassi drinks you can choose from, including mango, strawberry, blueberry, peach and cinnamon.

There is an extensive list of mocktails, from different flavoured mojitos to the chocolate chinar that has 70% dark chocolate in it.
