ONE of the most famous festive tales will be performed at a Bradford city centre venue this December.

Loading Bay will host a version of Charles Dickens’ classic A Christmas Carol by The Book of Darkness & Light Theatre Company on December 3.

Adapted and performed by Adam Z. Robinson, this production ofthe Christmas classic teases out the gothic aspects, presenting Dickens’ remarkable story as you’ve never seen it before.

This version has been described as “by turns hair-raising, thrilling and heartwarming.”

It uses gripping narration, eerie recorded voices and an immersive soundscape, the show transports audiences to the office of Scrooge and Marley, the humble Cratchit household and the fearful, final resting place of that most infamous miser.

Robinson will portray 28 different characters in the show.

Loading Bay is an arts venue created in a former storage building on Duke Street as part of Bradford’s year as City of Culture.

Tickets start at £12 – for more information visit [https://bradfordculture.co.uk/event/a-christmas-carol/](https://bradfordculture.co.uk/event/a-christmas-carol/)
