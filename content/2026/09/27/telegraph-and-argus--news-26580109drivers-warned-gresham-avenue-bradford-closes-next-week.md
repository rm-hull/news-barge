GRESHAM Avenue will be closed for water works.

The temporary closure, announced by the City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council, will affect the road from outside No. 2 Gresham Avenue.

The order, issued under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, will be in effect from 7am on September 28 until 7pm on October 2.

While no alternative routes have been advised, pedestrian access will remain open, and vehicles involved in the works will be exempt from the prohibition.

The closure could potentially extend for up to 18 months if needed, according to the council.
