Floral tributes have been left outside a County Durham home after a man was 'murdered' in a stabbing.

Officers were called to Thames Road in Peterlee, shortly before midnight on Wednesday (September 23).

Dean Fishwick, 51, was found with stab wounds and taken to hospital where he was pronounced dead a short time later.

Kenneth Fishwick, 53, also of Thames Road, has since been charged with murder.

![]()Floral tributes at the scene

He appeared at Teesside Magistrates Court on Saturday (September 26).

A 73-year-old woman arrested on suspicion of murder has been bailed, and a 77-year-old man arrested on suspicion of murder has been released under investigation.

Pictures taken today (September 27), show flowers laid outside the home in tribute to the 51-year-old.

One tribute reads: “You will be sadly missed, love you. Fly high.”
