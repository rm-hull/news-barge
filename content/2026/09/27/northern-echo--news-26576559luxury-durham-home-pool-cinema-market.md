A luxury County Durham home featuring an indoor swimming pool, private cinema and a passenger lift serving all three floors is on the market.

Located around three miles from Durham City Centre, the contemporary detached property extends to more than 5,550 square feet and offers six bedrooms alongside a host of leisure and entertainment facilities rarely found in a family home.

A dramatic triple-height entrance hall forms the centrepiece, while a wheelchair-accessible lift connects every floor for multigenerational living and people with mobility needs.

The home also has underfloor heating, air conditioning in all six bedrooms and key living areas, smart-home technology, integrated audio and mechanical ventilation with heat recovery.

The landscaped gardens include several seating areas, a sunken fire pit, water features and architectural lighting.

The home is close to shops, restaurants, schools and transport links, and is among the area’s most striking properties currently on the market.
