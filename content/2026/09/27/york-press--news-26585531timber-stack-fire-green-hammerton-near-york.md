A TIMBER stack has caught fire in a village near York.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Acomb was called to The Green, in Green Hammerton, at 9.56am today (Sunday, September 27).

The stack measured 20 metres by 30 metres and crews needed assistance from a water bowser from Tadcaster.

A service spokesperson said: "Crews used a 45mm hose and ground monitor to extinguish the fire."
