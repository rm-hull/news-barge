Visitors to a much-loved North East attraction are being warned that its café will temporarily close while maintenance work is carried out.

Park Café at Preston Park Museum and Grounds will close on September 28 and 29 while kitchen maintenance is carried out.

The café is expected to reopen at 9am on Wednesday, September 30.

Announcing the temporary closure on social media, Preston Park Museum and Grounds apologised for any inconvenience caused.

A spokesperson said: "A quick reminder and heads up if you're planning a visit next week.

"The Park Café will be closed on September 28 and 29 while maintenance work takes place in the kitchen.

"Thank you for your understanding while work takes place. We look forward to welcoming you back for a well deserved cuppa and a tasty treat."

Public toilets and changing facilities will remain open as normal throughout the closure.

Visitors heading to the attraction on Tuesday (September 29) will still be able to use the Museum Café, which will remain open as normal for pass holders.

Preston Park Museum and Grounds on Yarm Road is one of the region's most popular attractions, featuring a museum, Victorian Street and historic parkland.
