BUYERS looking for a country property that combines a spacious, practical and beautifully modernised family home with substantial gardens and grounds in a stunning rural location, yet with excellent connectivity, will find all that and much more besides at Primrose Hill Farm.

This handsome former farmhouse, with origins in the 17th century, was largely rebuilt in 2010 to create a substantial home bordering open countryside.

Set at the end of a sweeping driveway, it enjoys considerable privacy within its grounds, with the cherry on the top being panoramic views towards Roseberry Topping and Captain Cook’s Monument high on Easby Moor, part of the Cleveland Hills that form the north-western edge of the North York Moors National Park.

![]()While the views from Primrose Hill Farm are stunning, they are not the only aspect of this home sure to appeal. *(Image: Blenkin and Co)*

The house itself is distinguished by attractive sandstone and brick banding, tall windows and a steeply pitched roof with gable-end chimneys.

Inside it has been thoughtfully updated in recent years, with careful attention to building in character while introducing high-quality finishes throughout.

Its light-filled kitchen/breakfast room is fitted with granite-topped country-style units and is home to a range of integrated appliances including a Rangemaster cooker plus an exposed wood ceiling beam that adds character.

![]()While the views from Primrose Hill Farm are stunning, they are not the only aspect of this home sure to appeal. *(Image: Blenkin and Co)*

An adjacent utility room provides direct access to the integral garage.

Accessed from the kitchen via double doors is the dual aspect dining room which includes a bay window overlooking the moorland hills and French doors opening onto the south-facing terrace.

Like many of the principal rooms, it has feature reclaimed ceiling beams.

![]()While the views from Primrose Hill Farm are stunning, they are not the only aspect of this home sure to appeal. *(Image: Blenkin and Co)*

The main sitting room occupies the south-west corner of the house.

Extending to nearly 30ft, it features a wood-burning stove, large windows and French doors leading out to the terrace that enjoys views over the gardens, fields and countryside beyond.

A separate snug, centred around a substantial wood-burning stove, enjoys a striking picture window framing a view of Roseberry Topping.

Completing the ground floor accommodation is a rear porch, cloakroom and a dramatic galleried living room with a split staircase and distinctive bullnose step rising to the first floor.

![]()While the views from Primrose Hill Farm are stunning, they are not the only aspect of this home sure to appeal. *(Image: Blenkin and Co)*

The first floor is home to a family bathroom with contemporary Duravit fittings, a fully fitted dressing room and four bedrooms, including the principal suite with an en suite bathroom.

Upstairs again is a vaulted bedroom with dormer window, a galleried study area and an unused shower room, making it ideal as a self-contained space for an older child or for use as a guest suite.

Adding versatility to the accommodation is a granary annexe located on the first floor of the northern wing of the house.

![]()While the views from Primrose Hill Farm are stunning, they are not the only aspect of this home sure to appeal. *(Image: Blenkin and Co)*

It comprises a bedroom, bathroom and two large vaulted rooms, one with plumbing and electrics in place for a kitchen.

Primrose Hill Farm is accessed via a long privately-owned drive bordered by woodland that passes three dwellings before reaching the sweeping driveway that leads to the rear of the house.

An extensive tarmacadam parking area, accessed via two separate drives, provides ample space for multiple vehicles and offers potential for further development in the form of an additional garage block, subject to obtaining the necessary consent.

![]()While the views from Primrose Hill Farm are stunning, they are not the only aspect of this home sure to appeal. *(Image: Blenkin and Co)*

Gardens wrap around the house and adjoin open countryside.

A flagged terrace extends along three sides of the house, perfectly positioned to take in the surrounding views whilst offering an ideal setting for alfresco dining and entertaining during the warmer months.

The grounds include raised planters, sweeping lawns and a variety of mature trees, including a notable sycamore.

![]()While the views from Primrose Hill Farm are stunning, they are not the only aspect of this home sure to appeal. *(Image: Blenkin and Co)*

There is also an apple orchard, a grass field to the south and a belt of woodland along the western boundary.

In all the gardens and grounds extend to 3.6 acres.

Primrose Hill Farm, which is two miles from Stokesley and ten miles from both Middlesbrough and Yarm, is on the market for the first time in 40 years.

It is being offered with additional land available by separate negotiation.
