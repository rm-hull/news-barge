Police have issued a wave of new parking fines in a Bradford neighbourhood, just days after eight drivers were penalised in a similar crackdown.

Officers from the Bradford East Neighbourhood Policing Team carried out further patrols on Saturday evening following ongoing concerns about "obstructive vehicles."

Seven vehicles were issued £30 Fixed Penalty Notices after officers found them causing obstructions on Park Side Road, Summerlands Grove and Springwood Gardens.

Officers made clear that the crackdown is set to continue, warning that drivers who park obstructively can expect to face further fines.

The latest enforcement action comes after eight drivers were fined during a previous operation in the area earlier this week.

That patrol took place on Thursday, September 24, in response to concerns raised by residents at a community meeting.

## Police statement

Sharing details of the latest operation on social media, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said:

"This evening, seven obstructing vehicles were issued £30 Fixed Penalty Notices on Park Side Road, Summerlands Grove, and Springwood Gardens by officers from Bradford East T1 NPT.

"Please park responsibly and avoid causing obstructions to other road users and pedestrians, as we will continue to issue tickets."
