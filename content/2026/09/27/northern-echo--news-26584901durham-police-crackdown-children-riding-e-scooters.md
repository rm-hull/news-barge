Fears someone could be seriously hurt have sparked a police crackdown on children riding e-bikes and e-scooters through traffic in a County Durham village.

Durham Police said it has received a number of reports of young people riding e-scooters in and out of traffic in Cockfield raising concerns for their safety and that of other road users.

Officers are now asking members of the public to help identify those involved so they can engage with parents and guardians and address the issue.

A force spokesperson said: "We have received a number of reports of children riding e-scooters in and out of traffic, placing themselves and other road users at significant risk of harm.

"It is important to be aware that privately owned e-scooters cannot be legally used on public roads, pavements or other public spaces.

"In addition to being unlawful, their misuse can lead to serious injury.

"Our aim is to identify those involved, engage with parents and guardians, and prevent this behaviour."

Police said any information provided will be treated in confidence and could help tackle concerns within the community.

As part of efforts to address the issue, local officers will also be working with Cockfield Primary School on road safety concerns and wider anti-social behaviour.

Anyone with information is asked to email [barnardcastlebeats@durham.police.uk.](mailto:barnardcastlebeats@durham.police.uk.) Officials said all information received will be handled sensitively.
