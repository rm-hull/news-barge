Police are searching for missing 15-year-old Dominik from Bradford.

Dominik was last seen wearing a black jacket, navy jumper, black jeans and grey trainers.

West Yorkshire Police said they are "concerned for his safety".

Police urged anyone with information about Dominik’s whereabouts to call 101.

## What police could say

In a statement, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Have you seen Dominik?

"Dominik is 15 years old and from Bradford, he was last seen wearing a black jacket, navy jumper, black jeans, and grey trainers.

"Police are concerned for his safety and ask if you have seen him or know where he is, please call 101 and quote log 1880 19 September 2026. Thank you. "
