A man in his 40s has been arrested after a two-car crash in County Durham left a woman with a suspected neck injury.

Police were called to Lorne Terrace in Coundon shortly after 7.40pm on Sunday, September 20, following a crash involving two vehicles.

The driver of one of the cars is believed to have suffered a neck injury.

A 44-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of a number of offences, including assaulting an emergency worker and being unfit to drive through drink or drugs.

He remains in police custody.

A Durham Police spokesperson said: “Police were called just after 7.40pm on Sunday to a two-vehicle collision on Lorne Terrace, in Coundon.

"The driver of one of the cars is thought to have injured her neck.

"Officers arrested a 44-year-old man on suspicion of several offences, including assaulting an emergency worker and being unfit to drive through drink or drugs.

"He was taken into police custody where he remains today.”

The North East Ambulance Service has been contacted for comment.
