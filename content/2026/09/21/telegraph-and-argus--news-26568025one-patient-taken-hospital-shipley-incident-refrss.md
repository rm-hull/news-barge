A patient was taken to hospital after emergency services were called to an incident on Leeds Road in Shipley during the early hours of Monday morning.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service (YAS) said it was alerted to the incident by police colleagues at around 4am and dispatched an ambulance crew to the scene.

The ambulance service confirmed that one patient was treated by responders before being taken to hospital.

No further details regarding the nature of the incident or the patient's condition have been released at this stage.

## **Emergency response**

Emergency services attended Leeds Road following the report in the early hours of September 21.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received a call from Police colleagues at 4.00am this morning to report an incident on Leeds Road in Shipley.

"An ambulance was dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."

The ambulance service has not provided any further information about the circumstances surrounding the incident.

## **Further details awaited**

The exact nature of the incident remains unclear.

West Yorkshire Police has been contacted for further information about what happened and whether any road closures or wider emergency responses were required.
