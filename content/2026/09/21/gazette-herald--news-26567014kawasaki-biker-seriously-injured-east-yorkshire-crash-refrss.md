A BIKER has been seriously injured after a crash in rural East Yorkshire.

Humberside Police is appealing for information after a crash involving a car and a motorbike in Speeton Gate, Buckton, East Riding of Yorkshire, on September 11.

It was reported at 4.30pm after a Kawasaki motorcycle travelling north-west from Buckton collided with a Citroen DS3 travelling in the opposite direction.

A spokesperson for the force said: "The motorcycle driver, a 43-year-old man, was taken to hospital with serious injuries."

Anyone who witnessed the collision or has information, including dashcam footage, should contact Humberside Police on 101, quoting crime reference number log 321 of September 11.
