A sickening groomer persuaded two girls to send him intimate images and one to perform sex acts on herself.

Kevin Appleby built trust with both girls through lengthy online chats before turning the conversations to sexual matters.

He was caught after one victim’s sister saw her 13-year-old sibling filming herself performing a sex act in her bedroom and told their mother.

She contacted police, who visited Appleby’s County Durham home in October 2021 and seized several devices for examination.

Durham Crown Court heard that Appleby initially claimed he did not know anyone of the girl’s name, but his phone and tablet had been examined by the time of his second interview.

The devices contained 129 indecent images and videos of children, including 37 in the most serious category.

Chat logs revealed exchanges with the 13-year-old and a second victim, a 15-year-old girl in South Africa who has not been traced.

Prosecutor Nick Peacock said the chats showed classic grooming by Appleby, who was in his early to mid-20s at the time.

He said both conversations began with general chat before becoming sexual, with intimate photos exchanged and Appleby suggesting what one girl should do to herself.

Mr Peacock said the offending lasted from April 2020 to June 2021.

He said the delay was partly contributed to by the police's unsuccessful efforts to trace the girl in South Africa.

Appleby, now 30, of Faulkner Road, Newton Aycliffe, admitted sexual communication with a child, three counts of causing or inciting a child to engage in sexual activity and two similar offences, classed as 'attempts', involving the untraced South African girl.

He also admitted three offences relating to the indecent images of children found on his devices.

Philip Morley, in mitigation, said Appleby was of previous good character and has committed no further offences in the five years since coming under investigation.

Mr Morley said the defendant had shown genuine remorse and was, “ashamed and disgusted”, by the harm he had caused.

“He has lost his good name and let down his family,” who Mr Morley said had remained supportive.

Mr Morley said Appleby was an “immature and naïve young man” with social functioning well below his age, and struggled to form relationships.

He is awaiting an autism assessment.

Mr Morley added that Appleby now lives in a flat and does casual factory work.

Judge Tom Mitchell told Appleby: “You have a sexual interest in children and that’s how all of this came about.”

The judge said the exchanges continued for a long time before one girl’s mother discovered what was happening.

“Mercifully and thankfully the videos were not very long, but it was still reprehensible.”

He said Appleby encouraged the second girl to take part in “all sorts of depravity”, but added: “there was very little chance of you ever meeting her.”

The judge sentenced Appleby to six years in prison, with release on licence after he has served two-thirds.

The judge imposed a Sexual Harm Prevention Order “until further order”, restricting Appleby’s future internet use, and ordered him to register as a sex offender for life.

He imposed a lifetime restraining order prohibiting Appleby from contacting the girl who was traced.

The judge also ordered the phone and tablet seized from Appleby to be forfeited and destroyed.
