A man was due in court today charged with making threats to kill in York city centre.

Police were called to the incident on Friday afternoon (18 September).

There were reports of a man abusing members of the public and York BID Street Rangers.

A 41-year-old man was arrested, and subsequently charged with making threats to kill, a public order offence, and a racially-aggravated public order offence.

He was due to appear at York Magistrates Court today.

Sergeant Jack Milner, of North Yorkshire Police, said: “There is zero tolerance for behaviour like this in our towns and cities, and such incidents are treated with high priority.

“We will continue to work with partner agencies such as York BID to ensure offenders are brought to justice.”
