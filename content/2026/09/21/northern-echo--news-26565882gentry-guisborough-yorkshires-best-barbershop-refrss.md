A young business owner has been giving a Best Barbers award in his first year of trading.

The Gentry in Guisborough recently won the Yorkshire Best Barbers title at a regional business awards competition, a success owner Lucian Tanase never expected so early.

But while the award has put the spotlight on the new venture, he says his greatest ambition is to create opportunities for young people in the town that has welcomed him.

"We genuinely never expected to win, especially so soon after opening The Gentry," he said.

"Although the business itself is still in its first year, my journey in barbering began much earlier."

![]()The Gentry Guisborough won Best Barbers in the Yorkshire regional UK business awards for 2026. *(Image: The Gentry Guisborough)*

Having trained and qualified in barbering in England in 2017, he spent years developing his skills before taking the leap to open his own place.

Those years of experience shaped a different vision for what a visit to the barbers could be.

Lucian said: "Winning the Yorkshire Best Barbers award during our first year of trading was an incredibly proud and emotional moment.

"It reflects years of training, experience and dedication, as well as the different approach we have introduced at The Gentry."

Lucian believes every client should receive a personalised service tailored to their needs, not just a standard haircut.

"Our customers are not simply booking a haircut. They are reserving dedicated time for a personalised experience built around care, lasting value and specialist expertise," he said.

Every appointment starts with a detailed consultation covering hair growth, density, scalp condition and previous cuts before a style is recommended.

The approach has built The Gentry a loyal customer base, but Lucian wants its impact to reach beyond the shop floor.

He has already written to Prior Pursglove College about supporting students with work placements, live barbering demonstrations and careers advice, and is constantly on the lookout for other ways he can inspire the next generation of barbers.

Lucian said: "We would also love to discuss the possibility of delivering barbering workshops or a course in partnership with the college.

"The Gentry was founded with the hope of becoming a lasting part of Guisborough while creating skills, careers and future employment opportunities for local people."

For Lucian, the award is not simply recognition of a successful first year, but motivation to keep investing in both the business and the community around it.

The Gentry will now represent Yorkshire in a national final in Birmingham in November.

Whatever the outcome, Lucian says winning the regional title has strengthened his determination to continue growing the business while supporting the town he now calls home.

He said: "Becoming a Yorkshire winner has already given us even more determination to continue developing The Gentry, maintaining our standards and supporting our local community."
