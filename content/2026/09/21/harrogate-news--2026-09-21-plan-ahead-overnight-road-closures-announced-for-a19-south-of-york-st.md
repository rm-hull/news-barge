Motorists and residents are advised to plan their journeys ahead as essential overnight maintenance works are scheduled to begin on the **A19 south of York**. Starting from** 8:00 PM on Monday, 28 September 2026**, a critical stretch of the carriageway will be subject to full night-time closures for resurfacing and patching treatments.

The safety improvements, managed by contractor *Lane Rental Services* on behalf of the City of York Council, are planned to take place exclusively on weeknights to minimize disruptions to local commerce and daytime commuters. The project is expected to last up to ten weeknights, with an estimated completion time of**6:00 AM on Saturday, 10 October 2026**.

## Closure Details & Operational Hours

The A19 will be fully closed in both directions between the **A64 Designer Outlet roundabout and Skipwith Road in Escrick**. The closure will be enforced strictly between** 8:00 PM and 6:00 AM** Monday through Friday. The road will completely reopen to all traffic at 6:00 AM each morning, and will remain fully open over the weekends.

The York Designer Outlet roundabout itself will remain unaffected by the works, allowing standard traffic flow to continue unimpeded to the north, east, and west via the A64.

## Impact on Public Transport, Cyclists, and Services

* **Bus Services (42 and 415):** Arriva bus routes operating between York and Selby will continue running on their regular timetables. Because no viable alternative routes exist, buses will be actively permitted through the live roadworks zone.
* **Designer Outlet Access:** Evenings shoppers and staff exiting the complex can safely use the roundabout to travel toward York or hook onto the A64. Those needing to head south must take the A64 westbound toward Tadcaster to join the official vehicle diversion.
* **Cyclists:** No specific cycle diversions are available on the A19 itself; however, cyclists can utilize the*B1222 Naburn Lane route* to travel between York and Escrick, or use the off-road*Solar Way* paths linking central Selby directly to York.
* **Domestic Services:** Residential waste and recycling collections will continue entirely as normal. Emergency services will retain full, unrestricted access through the works area at all times.

## Official Vehicle Diversion Route

Due to the high volume of heavy goods vehicles (HGVs) using the A19 corridor, a heavy-vehicle compliant diversion route will be clearly signed. Drivers traveling south must follow the **A64 Tadcaster Road** from the Designer Outlet roundabout, proceed along the**A162 through Towton** and the Sherburn in Elmet bypass, navigate east along the**A63 past Selby**, and rejoin the A19 at Barlby to head north into Escrick (and vice versa for northbound travelers).
