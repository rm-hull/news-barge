One person was taken to hospital after a road traffic collision at a junction in Bradford.

Emergency services were called to the junction of Swain House Road and King's Road at 10.55am on Saturday, September 19, following reports of a collision.

## **Ambulance Resources Dispatched**

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said it received an emergency call reporting the incident shortly before 11am.

A number of ambulance resources were sent to the scene, including an ambulance and a team leader.

## **Patient Conveyed to Hospital**

The ambulance service confirmed that one patient was conveyed to hospital following the collision.

No further information about the circumstances of the crash or the condition of the patient has been released.

Emergency services attended the scene while the incident was dealt with.

## **What emergency services have said**

A Yorkshire Ambulance spokesperson said: "We received an emergency call at 10.55am on Saturday 19 September to report a road traffic collision at the junction of Swain House Road and King’s Road in Bradford.

They added: "A number of ambulance resources, including an ambulance and a team leader, were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
