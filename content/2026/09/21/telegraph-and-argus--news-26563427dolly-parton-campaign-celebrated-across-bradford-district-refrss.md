A DAY of giving is being celebrated in honour of a country music legend and her dream of childhood literacy.

Imagination Library schemes across the Bradford district are marking the first-ever Give Like Dolly Day, which takes place on September 25 in honour of Dolly Parton and her lifelong commitment to giving every child the gift of reading.

The Imagination Library, founded by Ms Parton, delivers free books every month to children under five.

A spokesperson for Bradford’s three Imagination Library schemes said: "We’re inviting people everywhere to contribute to Give Like Dolly Day on Friday, September 25 and support organisations doing meaningful work for children where they live – beginning with their local Dolly Parton’s Imagination Library programmes."

In Bradford, hundreds of children receive books each month through schemes including Canterbury Imagine, which funds 500 books a month in Little Horton, Canterbury, and Manningham.

The Baildon Imagination Library is organised by Councillor Debbie Davies and voluntary trustees.

Keighley’s Imagination Library scheme was paused earlier this year due to funding shortages.

Helen Jones, chair of the Keighley scheme and headteacher at Strong Close Nursery School, said: "We are so desperate to restart it again as we know we were making a difference."
