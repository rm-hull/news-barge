# A66 crash: Man arrested during investigation denies drug offences

## A man arrested during an investigation into events leading up to a fatal crash on the A66 has denied a drugs offence and possessing criminal property.

Cleveland Police arrested 17 people in the days following the collision near Middlesbrough which killed seven people, including two police officers.

On Monday Cameron George appeared at Teesside Crown Court, where he pleaded not guilty to possessing cocaine with intent to supply, and possessing criminal property on August 22.

The criminal property charge relates to money, the court heard.

![A66 crash: Man arrested during investigation denies drug offences](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjQ3YzAyZjQ2LTJkMjAtNDliNi1hMWEzLWUxOGNjMmE1ZDEyYTo4ZDJiZGMyZS1iYTY2LTQ0YTItYTUwYi1mNjMzNTJmYzE3OGY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


George, 26, of Micklow Close, Redcar, was remanded in custody by Judge Francis Laird, the Recorder of Middlesbrough until his trial on February 4 next year.

The trial is expected to take two days, a judge heard.

Pc Matthew Blades, 37, and Pc Tom Clough, 38, were killed when a VW Passat going the wrong way along the dual-carriageway hit their marked armed response vehicle on August 22.

The five young men in the Passat were also killed.
