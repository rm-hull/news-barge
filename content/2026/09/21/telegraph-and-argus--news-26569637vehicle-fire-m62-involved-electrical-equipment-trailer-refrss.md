Multiple fire crews were called to a large vehicle fire on the M62 after electrical equipment inside a heavy goods vehicle trailer caught alight.

Greater Manchester Fire and Rescue Service (GMFRS) said the incident was reported shortly after 10.13am on Monday, September 21, with emergency services dispatched to the motorway near Birch Services.

Three fire engines from Blackley, Whitefield and Chadderton fire stations attended the scene, alongside the service's Enhanced Rescue Unit from Ashton-under-Lyne.

## Specialist equipment deployed

According to GMFRS, firefighters arrived quickly and began tackling the blaze using breathing apparatus, a hose reel and other specialist equipment.

The fire was located within a heavy goods vehicle trailer and involved electrical equipment being transported by the vehicle.

Crews worked to extinguish the fire before carrying out operations to ensure the area was safe.

The incident prompted a significant emergency response, with firefighters remaining at the scene for around three hours before leaving.

## No injuries reported

Information recorded in the incident log indicates that no injuries were reported as a result of the fire.

Details regarding any disruption to motorists or whether traffic restrictions were put in place have not been confirmed.

The cause of the fire has not been disclosed.

## FULL statement from fire services

A spokesperson from GMFRS said: "A spokesperson for Greater Manchester Fire and Rescue Service (GMFRS) said: “Shortly after 10.13am on Monday 21 September, three fire engines from Blackley, Whitefield and Chadderton fire stations, along with the Enhanced Rescue Unit from Ashton-under-Lyne, were called to attend a large vehicle fire on the M62 motorway involving electrical equipment within a heavy goods vehicle trailer.

“Crews arrived quickly and used breathing apparatus, one hose reel and other specialist equipment to extinguish the fire.

“Firefighters made the area safe before departing after approximately three hours at the scene.”
