POLICE are searching for a 'small red vehicle' after a van was reportedly set ablaze in a York suburb.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information into a suspected arson after a van was set on fire in Acomb over the weekend.

As reported by The Press today (September 21), the incident happened on Saturday night (September 19) on Southfield Crescent and involved a white Ford Transit van.

In a recent update, the police said it is now believed to have happened at 11.45pm.

"We’re particularly appealing for information about any suspicious activity, including sightings of a small red vehicle in the area at the time of the suspected offence," said a spokesperson for the force.

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC 1050 Robertson by email at [ellyn.robertson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ellyn.robertson@northyorkshire.police.uk), reporting through their website, or by calling 101. Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
