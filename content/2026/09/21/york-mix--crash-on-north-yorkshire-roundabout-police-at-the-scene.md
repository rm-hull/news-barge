There’s been a crash at North Yorkshire roundabout this morning (Monday).

It’s at the Wishing Well roundabout in Selby, which connects the A63 to Bawtry Road.

Drivers are reporting police at the scene.

#### Crash location

The incident is causing slow traffic at the approaches to the roundabout.
