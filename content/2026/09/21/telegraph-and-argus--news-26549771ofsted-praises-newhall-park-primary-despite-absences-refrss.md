A PRIMARY school in Bradford has been praised for its high standards but told to improve attendance and behaviour.

Newhall Park Primary School in Bierley, was inspected by Ofsted on July 7.

On receiving the report, a spokesperson from the school said: "'We’re delighted that Ofsted has recognised the school's strengths and that our pupils are happy and safe.

"We'd like to thank our dedicated staff, governors and supportive parents for everything they do to make our school such a positive place to learn."

![]()Newhall Park Primary School *(Image: Newsquest)*

### 'Pupils are persistently absent'

Inspectors highlighted that too many pupils are persistently absent, including those with special educational needs, disabilities, and those who are disadvantaged.

Ofsted said: "Leaders track attendance closely. They understand the barriers to good attendance, and they work closely with families to reduce the levels of absence.

"While there are some examples of the positive impact of this work, leaders recognise that pupils’ attendance remains too low."

The school acknowledged work still needs to be done, saying "While we are proud of our progress, we know there is always more to do, and we remain focused on continuing to improve and ensuring every child thrives.'"

### Notable achievements in comparison to national average

The rest of the inspection was positive, with the school meeting the Strong Standard in Achievement, Curriculum and Teaching, and Inclusion.

Ofsted said: "Pupils achieve well, including in national tests.

"This is particularly notable in the significantly above average number of pupils who reach the expected standard in reading, writing and mathematics.

"Pupils who are disadvantaged achieve consistently well and above their peers nationally."

Leadership under headteacher Karren El Kheir was identified as a strength, with inspectors noting a well-informed understanding of the curriculum and improvements that have increased pupil engagement.

Ofsted said: "Leaders previously identified that some pupils were too passive in lessons.

"Leaders have embedded a modified approach to lesson activities and questioning that ensures that all pupils are now actively engaged in learning across the curriculum."

### Inclusion as a key strength

Inclusion was also highlighted as a key strength, with inspectors noting an "extremely effective culture of inclusion."

The school met the Expected Standard in Early years, Leadership and Governance, and Personal Development and Wellbeing.

Ofsted described Newhall Park as "a highly inclusive school" with "a vibrant and engaging learning environment."
