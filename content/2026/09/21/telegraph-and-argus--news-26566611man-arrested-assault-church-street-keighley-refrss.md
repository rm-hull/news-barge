POLICE have confirmed that a 32-year-old man has been arrested following an incident in Keighley.

On Saturday afternoon, September 19, the Yorkshire Ambulance Service alerted police to an alleged assault near Church Street, Keighley.

The victim, a 48-year-old man, was taken to hospital for treatment.

His injuries were found to not be serious and described as "cuts and bruising".

Police arrested the 32-year-old man at the scene on suspicion of Section 47 assault occasioning actual bodily harm. He was later released on bail pending further enquiries.

## What the police said

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At 4.31pm on Saturday, September 19, police were contacted by the ambulance service who were attending an incident where a man had been assaulted in the vicinity of Church Street, Keighley.

"The victim, a 48-year-old man, was taken to hospital for treatment. His injuries were found to not be serious and were confirmed as cuts and bruising.

"A 32-year-old man was arrested at the scene on suspicion of Section 47 assault occasioning actual bodily harm.

"He was later released on bail pending further enquiries."
