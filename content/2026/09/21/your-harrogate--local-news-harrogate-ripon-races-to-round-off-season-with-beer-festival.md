Ripon Racecourse will close its 2026 season this Saturday (26th September) with an afternoon of racing, beer and live music.

The season finale will feature the Theakston Beer Festival, with a selection of ales for racegoers to enjoy alongside the action on the track.

Gates open at 11:30am, with the first race scheduled for 1:44pm.

The meeting will also support Ripon Community House through the racecourse’s Harvest Festival Initiative.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a7eb58793dcdc8396a.jpg)

Visitors are encouraged to bring a food donation and leave it at the collection point to support the charity’s work.

*Saturday’s fixture marks the final chance to enjoy racing at Ripon this year, with tickets available through the racecourse’s**website**.*
