Also known as ‘Pateley Show’, the event is a traditional Dales agricultural Show.

Taking place in the surrounds of Bewerley Park, Pateley Bridge, it has grown to become one of the county’s foremost agricultural shows and regularly attracts crowds of 15,000.

The Nidderdale Show traditionally marks the end of the agricultural show season.

The Yorkshire Post photographer Tony Johnson went to capture the best of the action in these pictures:
