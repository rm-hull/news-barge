Police investigating a sexual assault in York say this woman could be a key witness.

The incident happened late on Sunday 31 May and into the early hours of Monday 1 June.

The victim, a woman aged in her 20s, reported being sexually assaulted by a man.

#### Popworld, York

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/popworld-george-hudson-street-york.webp)

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A further incident then occurred inside the female toilets at the Popworld nightclub on George Hudson Street.

“The woman in the CCTV image is believed to have supported the victim inside the toilets.

“If this is you or you know where we can find her, please get in touch as soon as possible.”

A 28-year-old man has been arrested in connection with the investigation.

He is on conditional bail while police enquiries continue.
