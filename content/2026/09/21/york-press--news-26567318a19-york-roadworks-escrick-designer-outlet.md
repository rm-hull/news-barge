ROADWORKS are set to begin next week in a major York road.

The works will take place on the A19 south of York, between Escrick and the Designer Outlet roundabout, starting at 8pm on Monday, September 28.

The resurfacing and patching works - which are part of City of York Council’s 'wider highways maintenance programme' - are expected to last for up to ten weeknights until October 10.

The road will be closed in both directions from 8pm to 6am each night to allow the repairs to be carried out safely.

The closure will start from the Designer Outlet roundabout and stretch down the A19 to Skipwith Road in Escrick, with the roundabout itself set to remain open for travel to the north, east, and west and southbound drivers advised to use the A64 towards Tadcaster to join the diversion.

The A19 will reopen each morning at 6am and will remain open at weekends.

Buses will be allowed through the closure, with the 415 service between York and Selby to continue to operate as normal "because there is no suitable alternative route for these vehicles".

A signed diversion will be in place for all other traffic, including HGVs.

The diversion route will use the A64 Tadcaster Road from the Designer Outlet roundabout, the A162 through Towton, the Sherburn in Elmet bypass, and the A63 past Selby, rejoining the A19 at Barlby.

There are no suitable cycle diversions along this section of road, however, the B1222 Naburn Lane route will remain accessible and could be used to cycle between York and the A19 south of Escrick.

A spokesperson for the council said: "We apologise for any disruption caused by the work and thank you for your patience and understanding."

The works will be carried out by Lane Rentals Services on behalf of the council.

Street parking in and near the work area will be suspended during the closure, but waste and recycling collections will continue as normal.

Residents and businesses are encouraged to sign up for email alerts about roadworks and potential disruption via www.york.gov.uk/roadworks.
