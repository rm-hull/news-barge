A North Yorkshire primary school has received a grant to develop a dedicated room for before and after school care.

Crakehall School, a Church of England school near Bedale, received £350 from Broadacres’ Community and Sustainability Fund, which supports local social, environmental and community projects.

Charlotte Johnson, chair of the Friends of Crakehall School, said wraparound care was increasingly important because of parents’ working hours.

Crakehall offers care every day from 8am to 6pm, with breakfast and tea provided.

Charlotte said: “We like to get children outdoors in the fresh air, but that’s not always possible, so this dedicated room will help us provide stimulating indoor activities.

“We look to create a safe, inclusive and stimulating environment that supports children’s wellbeing, as well as their social, emotional and physical development.

“Some of the resources we have had for years are now looking a little worn, so we are very grateful for the grant.

"It will be used to purchase Lego and other indoor toys and games, creative materials and quiet-area resources.”

Places at the school’s Breakfast Club cost £5, or £2.50 without breakfast.

The Tea Timers after-school club costs £3 until 4pm or £10 until 6pm.

The school also runs after-school clubs including karate, science, music and Lego.

To find out more about the Fund, including how to apply, visit the Broadacres website.

Broadacres’ Community and Sustainability Fund offers grants of up to £500, rising to £1,000 in exceptional cases.

Its community grants support local organisations, groups and projects in areas where it has homes.
