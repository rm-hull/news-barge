Yorkshire’s very own Kaiser Chiefs are heading home next summer a spectacular live show.

The Leeds indie heroes will headline Scarborough Open Air Theatre on **Friday 11 June 2027**.

Tickets will go on sale at 9am this Friday (25 September) via Ticketmaster.

Kaiser Chiefs – Ricky Wilson (vocals), Simon Rix (bass), Andrew ‘Whitey’ White (guitar), Nick ‘Peanut’ Baines (keyboards) and Vijay Mistry (drums) – are undoubtedly one of the most treasured bands of their generation.

Since forming back in 2000, they have truly made their mark on the British music scene with such anthemic hits as *Oh My God, I Predict A Riot, Everyday I Love You Less and Less, Ruby, Never Miss A Beat and Hole In My Soul*.

The band have released eight original studio albums which includes their Mercury nominated debut *Employment*, which sold more than two million copies, its Number 1 follow up* Yours Truly, Angry Mob,*which went twice-platinum in the UK alone, and* Education Education Education and War* which entered the UK Album Charts at number 1.

They have achieved top ten singles including the infamous number 1 hit *Ruby*, three BRIT Awards, an Ivor Novello for Album of the Year, toured with U2, Foo Fighters and Green Day and sold eight million albums.

Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, venue programmers at Scarborough OAT, said: “You can’t not love a night with Kaiser Chiefs.

“They always bring the party and where better to do that than Scarborough. This will be a truly momentous show next summer and we can’t wait.”

Find out more at the Scarborough Open Air Theatre website.
