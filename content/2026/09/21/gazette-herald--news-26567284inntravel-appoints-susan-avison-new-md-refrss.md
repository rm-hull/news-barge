A North Yorkshire-based tour operator has appointed a new managing director.

Inntravel, has announced the appointment of their new MD, Susan Avison, who has worked at the business for nearly 30 years.

Susan began working at the company as the PA to the founders, Richard and Linda Hearn, working her way to finance director, general manager, and managing director.

Based near Castle Howard, Inntravel was established in 1984.

The company are specialists in self-guided walking and rail holidays in the UK and Europe, focusing on immersive, slow travel that embraces local food and culture.

With their insider knowledge, Inntravel supports guests at every step of the way, taking care of all the planning, luggage transfers between hotels, routes, and walking notes.

In May 2025, Inntravel also became B Corp accredited, re-certifying in September 2026.

Susan said: “I feel immense pride and a sense of achievement. Having worked here for nearly 30 years, I’ve seen a lot of change and evolution. I had the privilege of working with the founders and the opportunity to work with an amazing group of people and mentors who champion progression. I really believe in this business. I want to continue to steer Inntravel on a positive trajectory and be a champion of our people, our partners, and our customers.”

Ben Boesch, CEO of Dertour, said: “I couldn’t think of anybody better suited to lead this organisation. I admire her passion for Inntravel and of course her inside knowledge of its culture. I see a very bright future for Inntravel under Susan’s leadership.”
