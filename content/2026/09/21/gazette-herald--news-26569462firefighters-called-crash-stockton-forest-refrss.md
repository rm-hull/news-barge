FIREFIGHTERS have been called to a village near York after a crash this afternoon.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service says its crew from York was called to Stockton on the Forest this afternoon after a three vehicle crash.

It happened at 4.49pm and one of the vehicles was said to be leaking fluid onto the road.

“All occupants were out on arrival of crews and no injuries reported. Crew carried out scene safety only,” said a spokesperson for the service.

The incident has taken place near the A64 junction with North Lane.

An eyewitness said a road closure is in place with Highways staff assisting with diversions.
