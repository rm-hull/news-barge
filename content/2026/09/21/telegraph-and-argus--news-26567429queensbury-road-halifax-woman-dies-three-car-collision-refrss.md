A WOMAN in her 40s has died following a three-vehicle collision near Queensbury, police have now revealed.

The crash happened shortly before 10.45am on Sunday, September 20 on the A647 Queensbury Road, north of the junction with Howcans Lane.

A Honda Civic travelling towards Queensbury collided with a Seat Leon travelling in the opposite direction.

A VW Golf, also travelling in the opposite direction, was damaged at the scene.

A rear-seat passenger in the Honda, a woman in her 40s, died at the scene.

![]()Emergency services at the scene of a fatal crash in Queensbury Road *(Image: Gazz Hall)*

Three other people were taken to hospital with serious injuries.

## Witness appeal

The Major Collision Enquiry Team (MCET) is appealing for witnesses or anyone with footage of the incident or the events leading up to it.

The driver of the VW Golf, a man, was arrested on suspicion of drug driving and causing death by dangerous driving.

He has been released on bail pending further enquiries.

The male driver of the Seat Leon was also arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and has been bailed.

Anyone with information is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police on 101 or via the force’s LiveChat, quoting reference 0518 of September 20.

Enquiries remain ongoing.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 10.47am yesterday morning to report a road traffic collision on Queensbury Road near Boothtown.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including five ambulances and the air ambulance, and four patients were conveyed to hospital."
