Join West End and Broadway star Alfie Boe for a dazzling night on the North Yorkshire coast.

He is set to perform at the Scarborough Open Air Theatre on **Sunday 25 July 2027**– and joined by***Gravity*** stars Louise Dearman, Kerry Ellis and Rachel Tucker as his very special guests.

Tickets to go on general sale at 10am Friday 25 September via Ticketmaster.

One of Britain’s best-loved and most celebrated tenors, Alfie Boe’s commanding voice and versatility has earned him huge praise and success across opera, musical theatre, and popular music.

A graduate of the Royal College of Music who went on to train at the Royal Opera House in Covent Garden, Alfie achieved global recognition performing leading roles with major opera companies before cementing his reputation as the definitive Jean Valjean in Les Misérables.

He has starred in the production both in the West End and on Broadway, and in the recent 40th anniversary world tour, performing at Radio City Music Hall in New York, and in the Australia and China casts.

The trip to Scarborough OAT will be a welcome return for Alfie who has performed at the venue no less than four times previously, and joining him as very special guests will be *Wicked* stars Louise Dearman, Kerry Ellis and Rachel Tucker with their incredible*Gravity* show.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/GRAVITY-1000x667.jpg)

With Ellis playing the first British Elphaba in *Wicked*, Dearman being the first and only performer to play both Elphaba and Glinda in * Wicked* and Tucker playing Elphaba on both Broadway and the West End, the trio have earned their titles as musical theatre’s leading women.

*Gravity* celebrates their collective back catalogue and features a range of stage favourites including*We* * Will Rock You*, * Cats*, * Oliver!*, * My Fair Lady*, * Miss Saigon*, * Les Misérables*, * Guys and Dolls*, * Evita*, * Joseph*, * The Great Gatsby*, * Sunset Boulevard,* * Chicago* and more.

Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, venue programmers at Scarborough OAT, said: “This will be another special night on the Yorkshire coast.

“Combining the powerhouse vocals of Alfie Boe with the triumvirate of musical theatre royalty that is Gravity will make this an incredible night and one that will be absolutely adored by fans of musical theatre.”
