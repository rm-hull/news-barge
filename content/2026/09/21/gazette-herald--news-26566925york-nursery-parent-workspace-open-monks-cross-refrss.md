A new purpose-built nursery is planning to open in York - with an on-site workspace for parents.

Little Years Nursery is set to open at Monks Cross, in early 2027 to offer flexible childcare for children aged up to five years old.

The venture will create about 45 jobs, and a recruitment drive is currently underway.

![]()The new Little Years Nursery at Monks Cross is expected to create about 45 jobs. *(Image: Submitted)*

It will be the sixth location for the growing childcare provider which also has nurseries in Acomb, Sand Hutton, Helmsley, Hutton le Hole, and Kirkbymoorside.

![]()Little Years nursery in Acomb. *(Image: Little Years)*

The business on Kathryn Avenue replaces the former children's nursery retailer Paul Stride that was on the site until it closed at the end of April 2026 when the owner Kevin Clarkson retired.

The long-standing York family business had been a popular destination for generations of families since it opened in 1982.

![]()Paul Stride children's nursery and its warehouse and storage unit on Kathryn Avenue, Monks Cross, was put on the market when owner Kevin Clarkson retired in April 2025. *(Image: Image Nadia Jefferson-Brown)*

Natalie Bishop, chief executive at Little Years, said: "At Little Years, every new nursery begins with one simple question: how can we bring exceptional early years education to more families?

"As we prepare to open our sixth nursery in early 2027, we're proud to continue our mission of creating nurturing, inspiring environments where every child feels valued, supported, and encouraged to thrive."

The nursery will open from Monday to Friday from 7.30am to 6pm. It will feature interconnected playrooms, dedicated studio spaces for different developmental stages, and direct access to an integrated garden for outdoor learning.

![]()Little Years Nursery is due to open on Kathryn Avenue, Monks Cross in 2027. *(Image: Submitted)*

Facilities will also include immersive role-play areas and real-life learning environments.

The nursery will also include an on-site parent workspace to support families with busy schedules.

The company emphasises a family-led approach, believing that children thrive when families and communities are involved in their learning journey.

![]()Little Years Nursery plans to build partnerships with organisations and facilities across York to create enriching experiences beyond the nursery setting. *(Image: Submitted)*

Natalie said: "Our continued growth is about giving more communities access to an approach to early years education that truly places the child at the heart of everything we do.

"By investing in thoughtfully designed learning environments, experienced teachers, and strong relationships with families, we're creating places where children can flourish from their very first day."

Little Years plans to build partnerships with organisations and facilities in Monks Cross and across York to create enriching experiences beyond the nursery setting.

The nursery will use a mixed-age learning model, encouraging children to learn from one another and develop empathy through daily interactions.

Its curriculum draws on Montessori, Reggio Emilia, Forest School, and Steiner philosophies to promote independence, curiosity, creativity, and emotional wellbeing.

Natalie added: "Above all, we're committed to recognising every child's unique strengths and supporting them through inclusive, high-quality experiences that help them become confident, capable learners.

"The opening of our sixth nursery marks another exciting milestone for Little Years, but our purpose remains unchanged.

"We're here to provide exceptional childcare that supports children, empowers families, and strengthens local communities."

The nursery will offer term-time or full-year childcare options. Families interested in learning more can contact the nursery team at [monkscross@littleyearsnursery.co.uk.](mailto:monkscross@littleyearsnursery.co.uk.)
