BRADFORD Bulls captain Joe Mellor scooped two awards during the club’s end season dinner at The Cedar Court Hotel.

The T&A readers voted him as their Player of the Year, ahead of the other nominees Ryan Sutton, Caleb Aekins and Andy Ackers.

Not only that, but the loose forward was also named as Coach’s Player of the Year by Kurt Haggerty.

Around 200 readers had their say for the T&A award, with Mellor scooping 47 per cent of the vote.

It was little surprise, with Mellor top of the tackle count in Super League for 2026, even if that is something the captain wants to be doing less of next year.

Ackers was named as Players’ Player of the Year, while Sutton claimed the ASN Doors Hitman of the Year prize.

![]()Andy Ackers was the pick of the bunch among his fellow Bulls team-mates. *(Image: Alex Whitehead/SWpix.com)*

Top try scorer Ethan Ryan was chosen as the MNA Group Fans’ Man of Steel.

Connor Wynne was awarded Try of the Year for his incredible solo run and score in the defeat at Hull KR in July.

The Reserves Coach’s Player of the Year prize went to Finn Thompson, while the Reserves Players’ Player of the Year was Joel Walsh.

Finally, the Elite Academy Coach’s Player of the Year was Marley Williams, with the Elite Academy Player’s Player of the Year given to Mason Peach.
