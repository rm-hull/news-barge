A LONE walker at risk of becoming hypothermic in the Yorkshire Dales was helped by Cave Rescue volunteers and police after she got lost late at night n

The 22-year-old was close to Nick Pot, near Ingleborough, on the Yorkshire Three Peaks route when Cave Rescue got a call from police at just after 11pm on Friday to help find her.

A spokesperson for the Clapham-based Cave Rescue Organisation said following the call team members and vehicles were deployed and met up with the walker and police officers.

“She was warmed and recovered by our team, and subsequently transferred back to our base in Clapham.

“She was transferred to the care of our colleagues from Yorkshire Ambulance Service for assessment and further treatment. We wish her well.”

It was the 64th call out so far this year for the mountain rescue team.
