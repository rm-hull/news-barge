A 39-year-old man from Killinghall has been sentenced at York Crown Court after assaulting his partner on multiple occasions and leaving her in fear for her life.

**Andrew Stenson** appeared at court on**Thursday, 17 September 2026**, where he was sentenced to** 21 months imprisonment, suspended for 18 months**. The judge also handed Stenson a stringent** 15-year restraining order** to protect the victim.

The court heard that Stenson’s relationship with the victim began in 2020. Over time, his behaviour became increasingly volatile, culminating in several physical assaults. The violence escalated significantly in January 2024 when Stenson strangled the woman, leaving visible marks around her neck and prompting her to flee the property and report the abuse to North Yorkshire Police.

## Investigation and Conviction

Stenson initially denied the offences during police interviews. However, a meticulous investigation uncovered a recorded phone call in which Stenson explicitly admitted to putting his hands around his partner’s neck. Faced with the evidence, he later pleaded guilty to **one count of intentional strangulation** and**three counts of assault**.

> **In a personal victim impact statement read aloud to the court, the victim detailed the severe toll the abuse took on her daily life:**
>
> Throughout the relationship, my mental and physical health deteriorated. I was frequently ill, lost weight, and became uncharacteristically anxious over small things. I felt isolated, and ashamed that I could not protect myself.

The victim noted that since leaving the relationship, her health and well-being have improved significantly.

## Police Statement and Support Advice

> **DC Kirsty Greenbank of North Yorkshire Police commended the victim’s resilience:**
>
> The victim suffered nightmares, weight loss, and anxiety, all as a result of Stenson’s treatment of her. Throughout the investigation, he had ample opportunity to admit what he had done—but instead, he lied again and again.
>
> Thankfully, the victim displayed extraordinary courage to pursue justice and ensure he could not treat anyone else in the same way. I hope this case shows to any other victims of domestic violence: you are not alone, and the police are here to help.
