A leading York brewery is raising a toast to the spooky season with a new Halloween-inspired beer collection.

Brew York, based in Walmgate and Osbaldwick, and founded in 2016 by friends Wayne Smith and Lee Grabham, is launching its latest series of brews under the title Dark Chapters.

The range includes Dracula’s Bite, a raspberry and marshmallow pastry sour; Franken Spice, a pumpkin spiced stout; and Jekyll & Haze, a 6.66 per cent ABV hazy pale ale.

Yousef Doubooni, sales director at Brew York, said: "Our previous Halloween series have always gone down very well in our venues and with our trade customers too.

"We know many of our stockists and consumers will be embracing the ‘spooky season’ with their drink selections – and of course, pubs and bars in our hometown of York will be no exception to that.

"We can’t wait to see them pouring."

The new beers will be available on keg and in cans starting the week of September 21, with the stout also offered on cask.

The can artwork, created by York-based agency UnitedByDesign, features illustrations of each literary character set against backdrops inspired by the classic horror stories that inspired the names.

Andrew Stark, head of marketing at Brew York, said: "Halloween gives us lots of inspiration to build on the visual side of our brand identity.

"Previous years’ ranges were influenced by classic horror video games and horror films; the feedback was so good from these that we were looking at 2026’s theme as soon as 2025’s beers became available.

"The Dark Chapters range really supports our visual approach.

"As well as creating great beers, we really want our product to stand out on shelves and in pubs and this range will do just that."

Mr Doubooni said: "Our Christmas range has been created and we’re well underway with our plans for 2027 which, as ever, will include a whole host of new beers as well as the return of some old favourites.

"Watch this space."

The Dark Chapters beers will be available through Brew York’s own venues, selected trade customers, and its online shop.

Brew York brews all of its beer in York, at its original city centre brewhouse in Wal;mgate and a larger production facility in Osbaldwick.
