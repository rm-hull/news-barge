A body has tragically been found in the search for a missing North East man.

Aurel, 41, from Newcastle, was last seen in the Shafto Street area of Scotswood around midday on Monday, September 14.

Despite extensive enquiries by Northumbria Police, he was not located.

On Sunday, September 20, a body was discovered in the Whitburn area.

Formal identification is yet to take place, but the man is believed to be Aurel.

Detective Inspector Nick Went, of the force’s missing investigation team, said: "Our thoughts are firmly with Aurel’s loved ones at this extremely difficult time.

"I’d like to thank the officers involved in the searches, our partner agencies for their assistance and those in the community who shared information with us.

"We'd like to request the family's privacy is respected at this time", DI Went added.

Police do not believe there was any third-party involvement.

A report will now be prepared for the coroner.
