## York cruise beer beats Stella Artois to become top seller (on the Ouse)

City Cruises York has teamed up with Kirkstall Brewery which has developed a beer for guests cruising along the Ouse. *(Image: Submitted)*

A new craft beer is making waves on the River Ouse in York.

Smooth Sailing, a 4% ABV pilsner, has become the top-selling beer across City Cruises York’s fleet since its launch, outperforming mainstream brands.

The vegan and gluten-free beer is the result of a partnership between City Cruises York and Leeds-based Kirkstall Brewery, brewed specifically for guests cruising along the Ouse.

![]()On board City Cruises York where Smooth Sailing beer is outselling global brands such as Stella Artois. *(Image: Submitted)*

Christopher Pegg, head of regional commercial, sales and marketing at City Cruises, said: "Our passengers visit us from all across the globe, but when they step aboard in York, they want an authentic taste of Yorkshire.

"Partnering with Kirkstall Brewery has allowed us to deliver exactly that.

"Smooth Sailing was designed to be crisp, light, and accessible to everyone - being 4% ABV, vegan, and gluten-free - and the response from our guests has exceeded all expectations.

"Seeing an independent local brewer out-pull commercial global brands on our taps is proof that championing Yorkshire produce works."

Developed specifically for City Cruises York, Smooth Sailing replaces the previous house line and features custom branding inspired by York’s waterways and landmarks.

The design includes artwork of York Minster and City Cruises vessels on the river set against bright summer skies.

The collaboration reflects a growing demand for locally-sourced food and drink experiences, with visitors increasingly seeking regional flavours during their travels.

Since its introduction on board vessels including the River Palace, Smooth Sailing has become a standout hit, officially surpassing mainstream brands such as Stella Artois across the fleet’s onboard bars.

City Cruises York and Kirkstall Brewery celebrated the launch with a special event onboard the River Palace.

Gary Lynch, account manager at Kirkstall Brewery, said: "Working alongside the City Cruises York team to bring Smooth Sailing to life has been an incredible collaboration.

"From developing the brew profile to crafting a visual identity that captures the beauty of cruising past the Minster, this project showcases the best of our region.

"The feedback from the launch event and the sales figures so far show just how well our teams work together.

"We look forward to keeping the beer flowing across the Ouse for seasons to come."

Smooth Sailing is currently available on tap and in cans across City Cruises York's daytime sightseeing, evening, and private charter sailings.
