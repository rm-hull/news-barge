X Factor favourite Chico is set to perform live at Bilton Club next year.

The singer and entertainer will take to the stage at the Skipton Road venue on Saturday 27th March 2027, with doors opening at 7:30pm.

Chico Slimani became one of the most memorable contestants in the talent show’s history after appearing in its second series in 2005.

He went on to reach the quarter-finals before releasing his signature single, ‘It’s Chico Time’, which topped the UK Singles Chart in 2006.

A spokesperson for Bilton Club said:

> "Chico became one of the biggest personalities of the show, and now he’s bringing the energy, nostalgia and absolute chaos to our stage.
>
> "Expect a huge feel-good night, plenty of throwback X Factor memories and, of course… IT’S CHICO TIME!"

Tickets cost £10 for Bilton Club members and £12 for non-members, while meet-and-greet packages are available for £25.

*To find out more and get your ticket, visit www.biltonclub.co.uk/*
