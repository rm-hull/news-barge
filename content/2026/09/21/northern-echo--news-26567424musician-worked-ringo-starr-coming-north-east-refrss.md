A musician whose bass playing has featured alongside stars including Ringo Starr, Bonnie Raitt and Crosby, Stills & Nash is set to perform in the North East this autumn.

Freebo will join award-winning American singer-songwriter Alice Howe for two regional dates after the acclaimed folk and Americana duo announced a new UK tour.

The pair will take to the stage at Hindmarsh Hall in Alnmouth on October 24 before appearing at Whitby's Musicport Festival the following day.

Freebo is best known for his decade-long stint as Bonnie Raitt's bassist, but has also performed with a host of other rock legends, including John Mayall, Joe Walsh, and Dr John.

His most recent collaboration with Alice Howe has earned the pair a strong following on the international folk and Americana scene.

California-based Howe won Best Female Artist at the International Acoustic Music Awards and is praised for her heartfelt song writing and distinctive voice.

![]()Award-winning singer-songwriter Alice Howe and veteran musician Freebo have announced North East dates on their latest UK tour. *(Image: Jim Shea)*

Since forming their musical partnership in 2017, the pair have performed extensively across the United States and Europe.

Their latest album, Alice Howe & Freebo Live, topped the Folk Radio Charts’ Most Played Albums list and later made the outlet’s album-of-the-year list.

Critics have also praised the duo’s live shows.

Americana UK called them "the perfect fit," while The Folk Music Notebook praised their "powerful song writing, mesmerizing vocals, outstanding musicianship, and a palpable camaraderie".

The announcement follows another busy spell for the duo, who play more than 80 shows a year.

![]()Alice Howe and Freebo will bring their acclaimed folk, rock and blues collaboration to Alnmouth and Whitby as part of a newly announced UK tour. *(Image: EvansAbove PR)*

Howe’s most recent studio album, Circumstance, was produced by Freebo and recorded at the legendary FAME Studios in Muscle Shoals, Alabama.

Fans in the North East will now have the chance to experience their blend of rich harmonies, seasoned song writing, and decades of musical experience when the tour arrives in Alnmouth and Whitby this October.
