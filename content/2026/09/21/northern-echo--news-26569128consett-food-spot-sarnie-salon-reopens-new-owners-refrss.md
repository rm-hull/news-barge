A popular Consett food spot has officially reopened under new ownership after months of preparation and anticipation.

Sarnie Salon welcomed customers back on Monday, September 21, beginning a new chapter for the town-centre business.

The new owners announced the takeover in June and promised to bring the shop back to the heart of the community.

![]() *(Image: SARNIE SALON)*

Ahead of opening day, they teased a range of changes and additions, including new menu options, freshly made food, speciality coffee, sweet treats and a selection of hot and cold meals.

![]() *(Image: SARNIE SALON)*

![]() *(Image: SARNIE SALON)*

The owners said they were excited to reconnect with regulars and welcome new customers.

Residents backed the reopening, which the team described as a huge success.

After the launch, the team thanked Consett residents for their support.

They said: “We honestly cannot thank the wonderful folk of Consett enough for coming out and supporting Sarnie Salon Consett on our very first day back.

“The support, kind words, smiles and encouragement have meant more to us than you’ll ever know.

![]() *(Image: SARNIE SALON)*

![]() *(Image: SARNIE SALON)*

“After everything it has taken to get our doors open again, seeing so many familiar faces and meeting lots of new ones made every bit of it worthwhile.

“Thank you, Consett. We’re only just getting started, and we can’t wait to welcome you all back through our doors.”

The team said they were excited to join the community and promised “plenty more to come”.

![]() *(Image: GOOGLE MAPS)*

The opening also drew support from local residents.

One person commented: “Looks amazing guys, best of luck.”
