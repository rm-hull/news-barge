Emergency services were called to a road traffic collision on the A59 near Skipton on Sunday, September 20.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service confirmed that ambulance resources attended the scene following reports of a crash on the busy North Yorkshire route.

Few details have been released about the collision so far, and the circumstances surrounding the incident remain unclear.

The A59 is one of the county's major roads and regularly carries motorists travelling between Skipton, Harrogate and York.

## **Emergency services attend**

Ambulance crews were dispatched to the scene after the collision was reported on Saturday.

It has not yet been confirmed how many vehicles were involved in the incident or whether anyone sustained injuries.

No information has been released regarding whether any patients required treatment at the scene or were taken to hospital.

## **Police approached for information**

North Yorkshire Police has been approached for further details about the crash.

The force may be able to provide information about the cause of the collision, the number of people involved and whether the incident resulted in any disruption to traffic.

## **FULL statement from emergency services**

A Yorkshire Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "Ambulance resources responded to a road traffic collision on the A59 near Skipton yesterday (20 September). North Yorkshire Police may be able to assist you further."
