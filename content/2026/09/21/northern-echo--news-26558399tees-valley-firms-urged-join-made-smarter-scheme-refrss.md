A popular North Easdt programme is seeking more firms to boost their productivity, improve efficiency and unlock growth through digital technology.

The Made Smarter programme, backed by the Tees Valley Combined Authority, offers hands-on support, specialist training and grant funding to help businesses take the next step in their digital journey.

One recent beneficiary is Sawdust CIC, a Middlesbrough-based fabrication studio that secured a laser cutter, laptop and 3D printer through the scheme.

Will Hughes, founding director of Sawdust CIC, said: "These pieces of equipment really save us time.

"Speeding up our workflow means we can go from job to job much quicker and make more money."

Sawdust CIC celebrated its first anniversary last month and is currently aiming to raise £1,000 through donations to support its community work.

With guidance from the Made Smarter sessions since formation, workflow at the company has also improved.

Mr Hughes said: "We were surprised at the breadth and depth of businesses we met at our first event.

"We wondered if it was for us, given that we are a quite creative organisation.

"But there was a bakery there, and an artisan maker, so we felt at home on that programme.

"I would recommend Made Smarter because it definitely helps you think about where you need to go as an organisation to build your skills in what you do next."

The programme offers fully funded workshops, one-to-one expert support and grants of up to £3,500 for technical advice.

Capital grants of up to £20,000 are also available to help businesses invest in digital equipment and software.

Participants can receive tailored guidance to develop a digital roadmap, whether they are just starting with technology or looking to expand existing systems.

Tees Valley Mayor Ben Houchen said: "Investing in the right technology can help our businesses save time, cut costs and win more work.
