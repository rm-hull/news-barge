A councillor has spoken of his frustrations at the lack of progress on a ‘vital’ route improvement in York.

Last March, plans to upgrade the riverside path by the Ouse were moving forward, with designs being drawn up.

The £1.7m project includes raising the path between Jubilee Terrace and Scarborough Bridge, to reduce closures due to flooding.

It was seen as particularly vital given the closure of Leeman Road.

But Holgate ward member Cllr Kallum Taylor says progress since last year has been too slow.

In a video posted to social media, he said: “We all know that this route needs improvement, but the closure of Leeman Road means that it’s now vital that they happen.”

He says there has been lots of work going on behind the scenes, and “the scheme is more complex than meets the eye.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/york-councillor-kallum-taylor.webp)

“There have been a number of geographical restrictions and questions to address, the latest being whether the riverbank is stable enough to cope with any works nearby.”

But Cllr Taylor said: “Even if you take those things into account, I still don’t think it’s been sufficiently prioritised, and I share residents’ frustrations with how long it’s taking.

“Those who attended the public meeting last year, when the project was already delayed, will recall the council saying that works would start in July this year, which clearly hasn’t happened.

“Communication’s not been great either. We’ve had residents asking us as councillors what’s happening, some thinking it might not even be happening any more.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/jubilee-terrace-to-scarborough-bridge-riverside-path-york-cyc.webp)

“But even as a councillor, it’s been like pulling teeth trying to get regular updates.”

He said City of York Council “should be praised when they do things well” but added: “I make no apologies for speaking up for the part of the city I represent on such an important issue for the people who live here when it so obviously isn’t delivering or even communicating as well as it should be.”

Holgate councillors have now organised another public meeting to get an update on what’s going on with the riverside path project.

It’s at **6.30pm** at**St Barnabas Church** this**Thursday** (24 September).

**A spokesperson for City of York Council said:** “We recognise this has taken longer than everyone would like and are committed for residents to benefit from the improvements as soon as possible.

“This is a hugely complex project to deliver which has been compounded by internal resourcing challenges in 2024 / 2025.

“Following recruitment within the Transport Projects team to support project delivery across the city, progress is being made.

“In recent months a lot of work has been taking place on the riverside path project with a new detailed designer partner appointed to address the key outstanding risks for the project.

“This includes undertaking surveys in relation to the riverside embankment which will help us to progress construction of the project.

“These proposals were developed following extensive engagement with local residents.

“Members of the project team are attending a ward committee meeting next week to provide an update and answer questions from residents, this is something we have done over recent years and will continue to do as the project progresses to construction.”
