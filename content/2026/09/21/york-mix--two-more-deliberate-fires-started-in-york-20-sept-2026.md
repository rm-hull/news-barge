Crews were called to two more fires started deliberately overnight (Sunday to Monday).

The first incident was a fire in woodland off **Grange Lane, Acomb**.

Acomb firefighters responded at at **7.14pm. last night**.

#### Grange Lane, Acomb

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said the fire measured 1m by 1m, adding : “Crews extinguished the fire using knapsack sprayers and small tools.

“The cause of this fire was believed to be deliberate and police were informed.”

Then, at **3.40am**, the Acomb crew were called again to an incident near** Monkgate** in York city centre.

The spokesperson said: “Acomb responded to a wheelie bin on fire which had spread to a tree.

“Crews extinguished the fire using a hose reel jet and thermal imaging camera. The cause of the fire was believed deliberate and police were informed.”
