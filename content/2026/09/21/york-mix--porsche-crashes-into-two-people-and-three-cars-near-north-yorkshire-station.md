Two people were taken to hospital after a serious crash in Northallerton yesterday (Sunday).

It happened close to the station on **Boroughbridge Road** at**around 7.30pm**.

A black Porsche apparently collided with three other vehicles and two pedestrians.

#### Boroughbridge Road, Northallerton

The two people were taken to hospital for treatment to their injuries.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The road was closed while we investigated the scene and to allow the vehicles involved to be recovered.

“We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicle prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, who has not yet spoken with police to contact us.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
