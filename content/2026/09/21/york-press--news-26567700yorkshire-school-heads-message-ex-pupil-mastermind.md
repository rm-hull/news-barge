An East Yorkshire school headteacher has said she will be cheering on one of her former pupils who is due to appear on Mastermind.

Becky Lovelock, head at Pocklington School, said she was "very proud" to see Beth Goodliff appearing on the BBC Two quiz show at 7.30pm on Monday (September 21).

Ms Lovelock said: "We’re delighted to see Old Pocklingtonian Beth Goodliff taking on the Mastermind chair.

"It’s wonderful that, more than 25 years after studying English Literature at Pocklington School, her love of literature is still going strong.

"We’re very proud to see Beth representing the Pocklington community and will all be tuning in to cheer her on."

The Mastermind specialist subjects on Monday night are the novels of Jeanette Winterson, the films of Nicole Kidman, the classic BBC radio comedy series Round the Horne and the Paris Commune of 1871.

Host Clive Myrie will ask the questions the four contenders take on the black chair under the spotlight and the ticking clock in an attempt to become the next Mastermind champion.
