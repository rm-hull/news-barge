Drivers in North Yorkshire could face a diversion of almost 40 miles as a road closes for more than a month for bridge replacement works.

North Yorkshire Council has confirmed that Ravenseat to Keld, will be closed from Monday, September 28, until Friday, November 6.

The closure is required for work at Beck Meetings Bridge, where the existing three-span stone clapper structure is set to be replaced.

Roadworks information published for the scheme shows a signed diversion of around 60.88km, or almost 38 miles, which is expected to take motorists about an hour to complete.

The bridge carries the B6270 over the Crooked Sike watercourse.

According to information for road users, the work will involve the "replacement of a 3-span stone clapper with a precast concrete u-duct and concrete top slab".

The diversion will be in force throughout the closure period.

The lengthy alternative route takes motorists away from the affected stretch before eventually bringing them back towards their original route, with part of the diversion travelling towards Cliff Gate Road.

The reverse route will apply for traffic travelling in the opposite direction.

A North Yorkshire Council traffic order states: "North Yorkshire Council intends to make this Order to prohibit any vehicle from using Ravenseat To Boundary, Keld due to necessary bridge repair works.

"It is envisaged that the restriction will be in force from September 28, 2026 until November 6, 2026."

Although the legal traffic order allows for a maximum duration extending until 20 March, 2028, this does not mean the road is expected to remain closed for that length of time.

The council says the restriction will only apply when traffic signs are in place, with a local alternative route also signed where appropriate.

Motorists travelling through the area are being advised to follow diversion signs and allow additional time for their journeys.
