Fans of Yorkshire soap Emmerdale have been speculating about the future of Lawrence Robb's character after the actor signed up for this year's Strictly Come Dancing.

The 34-year-old actor, who plays Mackenzie Boyd, was recently confirmed as one of the celebrities taking part in the new series of the BBC dance competition.

The announcement delighted many fans in Bradford and across the UK, but it also sparked questions over whether Robb's appearance on Strictly could mean he is preparing to leave Emmerdale.

The actor described securing a place on the programme as a "dream come true" and admitted he is both excited and nervous about taking part.

## 'It's very exciting, but I'm terrified'

Robb's Strictly signing prompted speculation among fans that he could be stepping away from Emmerdale, with soap stars rarely remaining in major roles while competing on the BBC show.

However, Robb has suggested he has no plans to walk away from Emmerdale.

![]()Emmerdale *(Image: ITV)*

Speaking in a video shared by Strictly Come Dancing, the actor said he would be balancing his work on the ITV soap alongside his new challenge on the dancefloor.

He said: “For the next couple of months, alongside strutting my stuff on the Emmerdale fields, I'm going to be strutting my stuff on the dancefloor.

“It's very exciting, but I'm terrified.”

## Who is Lawrence Robb?

Neither ITV nor Robb has announced any plans for Mackenzie Boyd to leave the soap permanently.

Robb, who joined Emmerdale in 2020 as Moira Dingle's long-lost brother Mackenzie, has become one of the programme's most recognisable characters and has been at the centre of a number of major storylines during his time in the Dales.

The character remains heavily involved in ongoing storylines, meaning viewers are likely to continue seeing him on screen while he competes for the glitterball trophy.
