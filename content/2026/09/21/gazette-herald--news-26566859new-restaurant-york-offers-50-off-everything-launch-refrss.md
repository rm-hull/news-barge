A NEW family-run restaurant in York is offering 50 per cent off everything during its launch.




Flame N Fry, at 41, Heslington Road in York, opens at 4pm today (Monday, September 21) and serves flame-grilled peri-peri chicken, Southern fried chicken, burgers, and more and owners said they were bringing together grilled and crispy chicken under one roof.

Sohail Saleem, owner of Flame N Fry, said: "We've put a lot of passion into Flame N Fry, and we can’t wait to finally welcome people through the doors.

"We wanted our opening to be something special, so we’re giving everyone 50% off everything to come and try us."

The site was formerly the home of Kickin Chicken, which opened in May last year.
