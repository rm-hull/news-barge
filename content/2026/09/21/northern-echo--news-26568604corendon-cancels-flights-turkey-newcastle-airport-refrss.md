Holidaymakers flying from Newcastle Airport will have fewer options for Turkey next summer after a major shake-up to one airline's UK schedule.

Turkish carrier Corendon Airlines will serve Antalya from three UK airports in 2027 instead of six, dropping Newcastle from its schedule.

The revised schedule has two weekly flights from Glasgow and three each from Manchester and London Stansted to Antalya.

Services from Newcastle Airport, along with routes from Gatwick Airport and Birmingham Airport, will not operate next summer.

![]() *(Image: Getty)*

The airline said it was taking a “disciplined” approach to capacity after a difficult year for the aviation industry.

Matthew Carpenter, UK country manager for Corendon Airlines, said: "Corendon Airlines is an agile private company that has the ability to respond to the market quickly to maintain a strong business.

"In response to a difficult trading year owing to the impact of the Iran conflict, we've therefore decided to reduce the number of UK airports we serve.

"We have also decided to end some aircraft agreements, with a consequent reduction in the overall fleet, which has a natural impact on our UK schedule and capacity."

The airline cited geopolitical uncertainty, rising fuel costs and changing consumer demand as reasons for the decision.

But Carpenter suggested that the cuts could be temporary.

He said: "This is a short-term reaction and we plan to review the trading conditions again in January and hope to re-introduce flights to Birmingham, Newcastle and Gatwick as soon as oil prices stabilise and trading conditions improve."

The carrier says it remains committed to the UK market and plans to continue offering direct connections to Antalya from its remaining UK airports.

Carpenter added: "The global economic landscape changed significantly during 2026, creating a challenging environment for the aviation industry.

"For 2027, our focus is on disciplined consolidation and strengthening our operational and financial resilience.

"In the UK, we remain committed to providing reliable direct connections to Antalya from Glasgow, Manchester and London Stansted, while positioning the market for sustainable development over the longer term."

Passengers from Newcastle can still fly to Turkey with Jet2, SunExpress, TUI and easyJet.
