The York Dungeon has a fantastically frightening new show for Halloween…

And here’s how you could **win two tickets to the Exclusive Preview Evening for the brand new York Dungeon Halloween Show,* Mother Shipton: The Great Prophetess***on** Thursday 8 October** – as well as a souvenir guidebook, VIP digital photo package, and bottle of limited edition Villains Rum.

Step back into the 16th century and become immersed in the world of Mother Shipton; the infamous prophetess of Knaresborough.

Born in a cave on a stormy night. They called her monster. A witch. Something to be feared. But what if there was another side to the story?

This Halloween, enter the shadowy hideout of the prophetess, where flickering fires tell tales of the past and the line between prophecy and reality begins to blur.

This is more than a show, you are stepping inside a piece of live theatre, transported back to a world of superstition, fear and fate.

But Mother Shipton has seen something coming… and once her prophecy is revealed, there may be no escaping what the future holds.

To be in with a chance of winning, simply answer the question on the entry form below.
