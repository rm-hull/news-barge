Two people were taken to hospital after a “serious crash” saw a Porsche collide with other cars and pedestrians.

Emergency services were called to Boroughbridge Road, Northallerton, near to the train station at around 7.30pm on September 20.

Police said a black Porsche is believed to have collided with three other vehicles and two people.

The two people were taken to hospital for treatment for their injuries.

North Yorkshire Police said: “The road was closed while we investigated the scene and to allow the vehicles involved to be recovered.

“We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicle prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, who has not yet spoken with police to contact us.”

The Northern Echo contacted Yorkshire Ambulance Service for further details.

Anyone with information can contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing Ruby Rutter at [ruby.rutter@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ruby.rutter@northyorkshire.police.uk)
