A cornerstone of Harrogate’s historic Montpellier Quarter has entered a brand new era following the completion of an extensive exterior and interior restoration campaign. The prominent **Herald Buildings**, which famously housed the headquarters of the* Harrogate Herald*and* Harrogate Advertiser* newspapers for a century until 1990, has been completely revitalised from the outside in.

Originally constructed in the 1850s as Saynor’s Crescent Hotel, the landmark building later functioned as the Yorkshire Home for Chronic and Incurable Diseases before its defining chapter as the beating heart of Harrogate’s local newsrooms. After the newspaper relocated its printing presses to Cardale Park in 1990, the lower levels served as a popular local retail and dining hub for nearly three decades before falling vacant in 2021.

## Iconic Golden Gilded Heralds Restored

The newly unveiled restoration, carried out by regional heritage preservation specialists, heavily prioritised retaining the distinctive architectural features that anchor the property inside Harrogate’s strict conservation area. Most notably, the historic stone ‘herald’ birds that have cast a watchful eye over Montpellier Hill since the mid-19th century have been painstakingly repaired and returned to their original golden-gilded glory.

To maintain the architectural integrity of the period facade, the project collaborated with regional specialists, incorporating handmade components from Furness Bricks alongside custom-manufactured timber sash windows designed by Longwood Joinery to match original archival schematics. Inside, the spaces feature bespoke detailing by interior woodwork specialists Ryan McGinty Interiors to nod to the building’s original charm.

## New Era for Montpellier Parade

In creating this new era for the Herald Buildings, the project has also transformed the ground-floor spaces for commercial use. The first of these spaces has already brought an end to the building’s period of vacancy, welcoming independent speciality tile retailer Claybrook Studio to open its first and only northern showroom on the vibrant Montpellier high street.

> **Nina Barker, spokesperson for the restoration project, said:**
>
> Herald Buildings has been a distinctive, notable landmark in prime central Harrogate for over a century, and we were keen to respect and cherish this important building and to ensure it sustains long into the future.
