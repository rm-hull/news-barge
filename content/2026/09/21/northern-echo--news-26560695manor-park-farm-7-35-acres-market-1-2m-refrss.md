On paper Manor Park Farm, which was built less than ten years ago, is a beautiful individually designed family home that occupies a private yet accessible position on the edge of the village of Hudswell, from where it offers both a proximity to local amenities and village life and an excellent degree of space, privacy and tranquillity.

In real life, it is so much more...

Designed and developed by the current owners, Manor Park Farm is a modern, energy-efficient home complemented by a detached studio, an extensive range of outbuildings and around 7.35 acres of land which encompasses grassland, newly planted woodland, established woodland, an orchard and a newly constructed wildlife pond.

![]()Manor Park Farm is a family home and so much more *(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

These attributes – and many more – open the door to a wide range of purchasers, whether they're looking for an environmentally focused smallholding, an equestrian property, a home with the potential to accommodate a business or home working, or simply those who want an exceptional rural property to live in and enjoy.

A tree-lined driveway leads to a generous parking area with EV charging point to the rear of the property.

Access to the house – which benefits from full double glazing and air source heating – is via a lobby which opens into the dining hall with high ceilings, a turned staircase and oak flooring which extends throughout the ground floor.

![]()Manor Park Farm is a family home and so much more *(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

Off the dining room is the living room centred around a modern log-burning stove set within the chimney breast and with bifold doors that open into the rear garden, plus the kitchen/dining room.

A stunning space, this benefits from an abundance of natural light thanks to numerous windows plus bifold doors that open onto a patio, creating an excellent connection between the house and gardens and making the room particularly well suited to entertaining.

The kitchen is home to an extensive range of fitted units with quartz work surfaces, integrated appliances and a central island with breakfast bar seating.

![]()Manor Park Farm is a family home and so much more *(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

Alongside this is ample space for a large family dining table together with a comfortable seating area.

Completing the ground floor is a boot/utility room, a cloakroom and a home office, accessed from the kitchen and suitable for being used as a snug, playroom or additional reception space, if required.

Upstairs are the family bathroom and four well-proportioned bedrooms including one with an en-suite shower room and the principal bedroom with a dressing room and an en-suite bathroom.

![]()Manor Park Farm is a family home and so much more *(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

A detached studio offers a variety of potential uses, including as a home office or workspace or, subject to obtaining the necessary consents and permissions, annexe or holiday let accommodation.

It comprises a lobby with a cloakroom, a large open-plan room currently used for parties and functions, a small kitchenette and useful storage rooms to the rear.

Beyond the studio is a substantial range of stabling and outbuildings.

![]()Manor Park Farm is a family home and so much more *(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

There are 14 stables and tack rooms, together with a fully equipped workshop, plus a greenhouse, log store, potting shed and tractor shed.

Adjacent to the stable block is an outdoor arena which has recently been converted back to a lawned paddock.

Landscaped gardens wrap around the house.

![]()Manor Park Farm is a family home and so much more *(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

The rear garden is laid mainly to lawn with established flower beds, borders, trees and shrubs, together with a patio and dedicated barbecue area.

To the front of the house is a patio with direct access to the kitchen/dining room.

Beyond the immediate gardens extends approximately 7.35 acres of grounds comprising grassland suitable for grazing, newly planted woodland, established woodland, an orchard and a newly constructed wildlife pond.

![]()Manor Park Farm is a family home and so much more *(Image: Robin Jessop Ltd)*

The grounds have been carefully designed with the environment and biodiversity in mind, incorporating a range of features intended to enhance wildlife and provide natural habitats for a variety of species.
