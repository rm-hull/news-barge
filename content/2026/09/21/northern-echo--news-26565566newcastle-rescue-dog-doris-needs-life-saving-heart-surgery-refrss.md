More than £2,500 has been raised to help a North East rescue dog get life-saving heart surgery.

Doris, a three-year-old mixed-breed street dog from Romania, urgently needs specialist treatment for a congenital heart condition which, without intervention, can be fatal.

Animal lovers are backing the appeal to give Doris a longer, healthier life in her new Newcastle home.

Doris was born with a heart condition called patent ductus arteriosus (PDA).

PDA happens when a small blood vessel near a dog's heart fails to close after birth.

The defect sends blood to the lungs instead of the body, putting extra strain on the heart.

PDA is one of the most common congenital heart conditions in dogs and can be fatal.

![]()Doris, a rescue dog living in Newcastle, needs specialist heart surgery *(Image: KELLY MCGILL)*

Many dogs with severe untreated PDA die in their first year, but surgery to close the vessel can give them a much longer, healthier life.

Doris has already outlived many dogs with PDA, but her enlarged heart means the condition is still life-threatening.
