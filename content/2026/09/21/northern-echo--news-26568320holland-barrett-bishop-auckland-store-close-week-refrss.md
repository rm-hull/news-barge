Bishop Auckland is set to lose another familiar high street name this week as Holland & Barrett prepares to close its town centre branch.

The major retailer has confirmed that its Newgate Street store will close on Wednesday, September 23.

Signs displayed in the shop window inform customers of the upcoming closure, stating: "This shop is closing, but your body still deserves the good stuff."

The retailer, which is known for stocking a wide range of vitamins, supplements, health products and natural foods, has been a familiar presence for shoppers in the town.

The nearest branch after the closure will be at Tesco in Newton Aycliffe.

A spokesperson from the retailer said the closure is part of an ongoing £70 million investment into transforming the stores.

A spokesperson said: "The Holland & Barrett store in Bishop Auckland is due to close on Wednesday, September 23.

"This is part of an ongoing £70 million investment into transforming our stores, which includes new store openings, refurbishments, relocations, lease expiries and closures, with decisions focused on ensuring we are operating from the right locations to best serve customers both in-store and online.

"Over the past three years, we have significantly expanded and upgraded our UK and Ireland footprint through new locations and refitted stores, while continuing to adapt to changing shopping patterns and evolving high streets.

"These changes form part of Holland & Barrett’s wider transformation to support long-term, sustainable growth and we remain committed to maintaining a strong presence across the UK and Ireland."

The closure has prompted reaction from residents online, with some expressing concern about the future of the town centre.

One person wrote: "Sadly, most of the major shops in Bishop town centre are closing down. It's so disappointing."

Another said they were "devastated" to see another major UK retailer shutting its doors in the town.

The closure comes after several businesses have left Bishop Auckland town centre in recent years, adding to concerns among some shoppers about the number of empty units on the high street.

It marks the latest change for the town centre, where residents have repeatedly voiced concerns about the loss of major retailers and the impact on footfall.

The closest store at Tesco Extra on Greenwell Road in Newton Aycliffe, opened in October 2025, offering vitamins, supplements, beauty products and natural foods.
