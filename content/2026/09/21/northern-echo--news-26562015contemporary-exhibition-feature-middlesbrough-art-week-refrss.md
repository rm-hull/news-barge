A North East artist will bring film and live performance to Middlesbrough Art Week, exploring how people communicate through everyday gestures and conversations.

Kate Stobbart will showcase a hand gesture film installation alongside a one-off live performance during the 2026 festival, which runs from September 24 to October 3.

The Newcastle-based contemporary artist works across live events, performance, sculpture and text-based art, and will show her latest work at a pop-up space in Queen's Square, Middlesbrough.

Kate's hand-gesture film installation will run throughout the festival, while her one-off live performance will take place on Saturday, October 3.

She said: "Middlesbrough Art Week is great in supporting a diverse range of artists in our region, and I'm pleased to be able to bring these particularly human pieces from Newcastle to Middlesbrough to connect with Tees Valley audiences.

"Through the mix of my 'hand gestures' film and a live 'overheard mobile phone conversations' performance, I'll be exploring how our everyday communication is shaped by pauses, gestures, hesitations, and the fragments of conversation that connect or separate us."

![]()Kate Stobbart's exhibit will focus on the power of hand gestures when communicating with others. *(Image: Kate Stobbart Art)*

Visitors can explore the installation throughout the festival and are encouraged to see the live performance too.

Middlesbrough Art Week - the North East's largest annual contemporary-art festival - will feature a series of new exhibitions for 2026, including the Little Boro display and the opening of the Auxiliary venue.

The creative event began with 17 artists in three Albert Road spaces back in 2017, before developing into a showcase for dozens of local and hundreds of international artists every September.

The exhibition will run at 15 Queen's Square from September 24 to October 3, closing on Mondays and Tuesdays.

It will open from 10am to 5pm on open days, except Sundays, when it will run from noon to 4pm.

Kate's live performance will take place between midday and 3pm on Saturday, October 3.
