A GROUP of friends has turned humour into heartfelt fundraising in a project that's putting their village in the spotlight.

The Burton Belles, from Burton-in-Lonsdale, have created a charity calendar featuring tastefully photographed, partially clothed portraits taken around the village to raise money for the Rosemere Cancer Foundation.

The 2027 calendar includes 12 A4-size, full-colour images capturing the personality and charm of the village and its residents.

Marilyn Smith, one of the Belles, said: "The calendar started off as a bit of a jokey conversation in the pub, but interest grew and we decided to make it happen to raise funds for Rosemere Cancer Foundation.

"A number of villagers and friends of villagers have been treated at Rosemere Cancer Centre.

"One of the Belles went there for radiotherapy in 2015 as part of her treatment for endometrial cancer."

Each month features a different member of the group in a light-hearted scene, with one, Karen, photographed behind the bar of the community-owned Punch Bowl Inn, managed by her husband Lee.

The front cover shows most of the group gathered by the Burton-in-Lonsdale village sign.

The majority of the photographs were taken by Belle Susan Hirst, who appears in the August shot at the bowling club.

![]()A group of friends from Burton-in-Lonsdale has created a saucy charity calendar *(Image: The Burton Belles)*

Artwork, posters and flyers were created by another Belle, Kate.

Ms Smith is featured in the June photo, sitting among plants and flowers in her garden summer house.

She spoke about the personal connections that inspired the project, sharing a story about the care one of the Belles received during her cancer treatment.

Ms Smith said: "She is a big tennis fan.

"Wimbledon was on and she was following Andy Murray.

"The radiotherapists timed her radiotherapy sessions so she would be home to watch all his matches, which was a kindness she will never forget.

"I have only ever heard people say good things about the centre and its staff."

A first run of 150 calendars is currently being printed and will be available to order for £10 each from early October.

They will be available to buy at the village’s community-run shop, which also appears in the calendar.

To celebrate the launch, the Belles are holding a special event at the Punch Bowl Inn on November 20.

The evening will include a raffle, auction, and live entertainment from singer Tim Riley.

Ms Smith said: "We have received some great prizes from local and national businesses, including a Virgin balloon flight for two, worth more than £350, and a barrel of lager from Carlsberg.

"Our hope is to combine everything to make a significant donation to Rosemere Cancer Foundation."

The Rosemere Cancer Foundation funds the latest in cutting-edge equipment, clinical research, staff training and innovative services and initiatives that the NHS cannot afford at the Rosemere Cancer Centre at the Royal Preston Hospital, as well as at local hospital cancer units across Lancashire and South Cumbria.

Hannah Leahy, fundraising assistant at the charity, said: "We are very grateful to the Belles for taking an idea and making it into something that a lot of people in Burton-in-Lonsdale are very much looking forward to having.

More information about the charity and ways to support its work can be found at rosemere.org.uk.
