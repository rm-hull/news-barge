OVER one hundred venues across the city are set to get involved as a popular York event gears up to return to the city next month.

York Restaurant Week is set to run from Monday, October 5 to Sunday, October 18, with more than 115 venues preparing to take part.

The event - which is returning for the 14th time - will feature 244 special offers at restaurants, cafés, pubs, bars and street-food outlets across the city.

York Restaurant Week is organised by York Business Improvement District (York BID) to promote the city’s hospitality sector and encourage residents and visitors to discover new dining experiences.

Eleanor Richardson from York BID said: "More than 120 venues have put themselves on the menu for this October’s York Restaurant Week, giving people a brilliant opportunity to eat their way around the city for less.

"There is an enormous range of offers to explore, from quick lunches and coffee-and-cake stops to evening meals, cocktails and special tasting experiences.

"York’s hospitality businesses are a huge part of what makes the city so special, and Restaurant Week is all about celebrating that."

Since the event began, more than 1,000 businesses have taken part, generating an estimated £3.5 million in direct customer spend and a wider economic impact of around £7 million.

As reported by The Press earlier this year , the most recent Restaurant Week, held in March 2026, saw almost 30,000 vouchers downloaded over 14 days.
