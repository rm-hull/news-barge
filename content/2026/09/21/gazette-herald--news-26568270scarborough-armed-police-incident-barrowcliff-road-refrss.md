Police were called to a man making threats to harm other people and himself with a firearm and bladed weapon during an incident in a North Yorkshire town.

A police operation was put in place in the Barrowcliff Road area of Scarborough at 9.48pm on Saturday (September 19) following reports of the incident involving a 32-year-old local man.

The 32-year-old was arrested at 10.23pm when he agreed to come out of his house.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "Following questioning in custody and liaison with partner agencies, the man was later released on conditional while enquiries continue into an offence under the Communications Act 2003 - improper use of public electronic communications network."
