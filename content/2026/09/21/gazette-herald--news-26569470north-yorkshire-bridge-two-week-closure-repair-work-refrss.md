A North Yorkshire bridge is closed for repair work.

Ashberry Bridge, in Rievaulx, is closed from today (Monday) until Friday, October 2.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Council said: "This closure is to allow us to carry out parapet wall repair work. Scaffolding will be installed during the closure, resulting in no vehicle access until the works are completed."

"We are sorry for any inconvenience this may cause."
