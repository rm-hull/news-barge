North Yorkshire firm Econ Engineering has invested £1.1 million in a "significant expansion" of its manufacturing capability with the purchase of a state-of-the-art laser cutter.

The UK’s leading manufacturer of winter maintenance and specialist highways vehicles says the new machine, equipped with an upgraded 12kW laser head, will cut faster and more efficiently than its existing equipment, boosting production capacity and precision.

Mixed-gas technology will let Econ cut a wider range of materials and thicknesses in-house.

![]()The laser cutting system at Econ *(Image: Victor De Jesus)*

Thomas Lupton, Quality & Compliance Director at Econ Engineering, who has led the investment on behalf of the company, said: “At Econ, continued investment in our people, facilities and technology is central to delivering the highest quality products and service for our customers."

“This significant £1.1m investment into state-of-the-art laser cutting machinery further enhances our manufacturing capacity and flexibility, supports more complex fabrication requirements and strengthens our ability to respond quickly to evolving customer needs."

The investment comes as Econ launches Built in the UK | Built for the UK, a campaign highlighting the people, skills, partnerships and innovation behind British manufacturing.

Thomas added: “UK manufacturing does far more than build products. It creates skilled jobs, strengthens communities, supports innovation and helps keep essential public services running."

“Investments such as this aren’t just an investment into our business; they’re an investment in people, skills, resilience and the future of our industry."

Econ says it is the country’s leading manufacturer of gritters and snow ploughs, supplying 85 per cent of winter maintenance vehicles bought by local councils and private contractors from its North Yorkshire factory.
