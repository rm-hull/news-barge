A much-loved coffee shop founded by a mother and daughter team is set to expand with a new venture in York.

MAE Café, in Holgate Road, is hoping to open its latest shop MAE Market in late October.

The site will open in the former shop owned by Cameo Engraving, also located in Holgate Road, which moved to a new premises in Clifton Moor in March.




It comes just over a year after Laura Mae Bowes, then 25, and her mum Joanne Bowes, opened MAE Café, which is named after Joanne's own mother Mae.

![]()The overall vibe of MAE Market has been described by Laura as 'The Yorkshire Dales meets M&S' *(Image: MAE)*

Joanne, who has more than 40 years' experience in the catering and hospitality industry, said the venture had been "very special".

She joined her daughter, whom she credited for designing and building the space.

The overall vibe of MAE Market has been described by Laura as 'The Yorkshire Dales meets M&S'.

She said: "We have had so much success with the café that we decided to build on our brand.

"The shop will sell fresh, organic supplies, along with essentials, and we hope to introduce MAE's meals - a service prepared by our wonderful in-house chef.

"We want these meals to a provide convenient, healthy alternative, which can be purchased and ordered online."

![]()'A new space is slowly taking shape - built around the same MAE feeling, with a focus on taking MAE into your homes' *(Image: MAE)*

MAE's Market features ample kitchen space for meal preparation.

A representative for the company said on social media: "A new space is slowly taking shape - build around the same MAE feeling, with a focus on taking MAE into your homes.

"We're currently in the renovation stage: planning, building, choosing finishes, watching the space change week by week and bringing the next part of MAE to life."

![]()The new space will sell fresh, organic produce *(Image: MAE)*

They added: "It's still a work in progress, but every little update is getting us closer."

Laura Mae said she hopes to open the new space to the public on Friday (October 23).
