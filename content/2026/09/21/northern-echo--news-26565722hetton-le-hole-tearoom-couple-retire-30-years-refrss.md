A husband and wife who have spent more than 30 years in business are preparing to retire from a popular North East tea room.

Michael and Carol McGlinchey will finish at The Little Peppercorn Tea Rooms in Hetton-le-Hole, Sunderland, next Saturday (September 26), bringing to an end a long career serving customers in Houghton and Hetton.

The couple have run The Little Peppercorn Tea Rooms at 23A Logan Terrace on Front Street, with the café becoming known locally for its homemade meals, cakes and afternoon teas.

The business will remain open after their departure and is being taken over by Kayley Jamieson, the new director and owner of Little Peppercorn Cafe Ltd.

A message announcing the change via the tearoom's social media page said: “Michael & Carol are now officially retiring!

“They will be finishing on 26th September.

“We would like to thank all our lovely customers for their continued support over the past 30+ years in Houghton and Hetton.

“We would love to see you this week to say goodbye and thank you.”

The announcement also confirmed that Tracey has left the business, while Sue will remain for a little longer to help with the handover to the new owner.

It added: “Sue will be staying a while longer to help the new owner, Kayley, get acquainted with her new business.”

The message was signed off: “With love and thanks, Michael, Carol and Sue.”

One commented: "All the very best! Enjoyed many family meals with you over the years."

Another added: "Good luck in your retirement Michael and Carol, I was very lucky to have my first job at the Peppercorn Cafe in 2009 in Houghton, wishing you all the best."

One other said: "Going to really miss you both. Thoroughly enjoy your retirement. Its greatly deserved."

The Little Peppercorn Tea Rooms has built up a strong reputation among customers and is highly rated online, with diners praising its friendly service, homemade food and traditional tea room atmosphere.

While Michael and Carol are now stepping away, the cafe is set to continue trading under its new ownership.

Their departure will mark the end of more than three decades in business for the couple and the start of a new chapter for one of Hetton-le-Hole’s familiar independent cafes.
