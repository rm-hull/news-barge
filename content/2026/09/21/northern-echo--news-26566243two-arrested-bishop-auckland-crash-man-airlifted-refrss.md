Two men have been arrested after a biker was airlifted to hospital with serious injuries after a crash in Bishop Auckland.

The North East Ambulance Service confirmed a man airlifted to James Cook Hospital in Middlesbrough.

A spokesperson for the Durham roads and armed policing unit confirmed two people have been arrested after the crash on suspicion of “several offences”.

They said: “A juvenile male has been taken to James Cook Hospital with serious injuries.

“He is currently in a stable condition.

“Two juvenile males linked to the motorbike were arrested near the scene on suspicion of several offences, including driving offences, and an off-road motorbike seized nearby.

“The driver of the car involved is being treated as a witness.

“Officers are still working at the scene, and enquiries will be continuing this evening and into tomorrow.

“At this time, we will be making no further comment.”

Newgate Street was closed for "quite some time" at the junction with Princess Street, with drivers asked to avoid the area.

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service said: "We received a call at 4.45pm on Sunday 20 September to reports of a road traffic incident on South Church Road in Bishop Auckland.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews to the scene and requested support from our colleagues at the Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS).

"One patient was transported to James Cook University Hospital by air for further treatment."
