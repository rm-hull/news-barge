West & North Yorkshire Chamber has taken part in a trade mission to Ireland.

Delegates from across our region visited Dublin and Drogheda, with headline sponsorship coming from professional services giant Azets.

The trade mission is being delivered in partnership with West Yorkshire Combined Authority, built environment specialists Arup and Flutter, the world's largest sports and online gambling firm.

Delegates were in Ireland for three days last week, and, as part of the agenda, were hosted by Drogheda Chamber of Commerce, as well as engaging with global brands such as Arup, Azets and Flutter.

The trade mission is the latest of a run of successful visits to the likes of Saudia Arabia, China, Bahrain, UAE and the United States.

James Mason, chief executive of West & North Yorkshire Chamber of Commerce, said: "As a region which has been trading internationally since the days of the Ancient Romans, Yorkshire businesses remain hungry to forge new trade routes and transactions globally.

"As one of the United Kingdom's largest trading partners, Ireland is the logical next step for our trade mission journey and our members have shown huge interest in this trip.

"Rest assured more trade missions will follow in 2027 and beyond as we utilise our status as the only accredited Chamber in West and North Yorkshire to access global markets the Chamber of Commerce brand affords businesses across the world."

Other businesses taking part in the trade mission included Anytime Couriers, Boost, Cello, Cloudwyse, Deanhouse Group, Love in Care, Madison May, Privilege, Ronis, SCC, the Grand Hotel in York and HC Slingsby.
