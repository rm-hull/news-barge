Irish singer-songwriter and actor Niall McNamee has announced a highly anticipated return to Leeds as part of his upcoming *Limited Edition UK and Ireland Tour*. The artist will perform an intimate headline show at**Oporto on Sunday, 15 November 2026**.

Following a busy 2026 spent on high-profile acting projects—including starring roles alongside Pierce Brosnan, Steve Coogan, and Jackie Chan—McNamee is returning to his first love: music. The winter tour follows the massive success of his 2025 debut album *Glass & Mirrors*, which soared into the**Top 40 of the UK Official Folk Charts**. For this run of dates, McNamee has deliberately chosen compact, grassroots venues to get closer to audiences, strip back his sound, and road-test brand-new material ahead of his next record.

For McNamee, the Leeds date is a true emotional centerpiece of the tour. “I’ve spent my entire life going back and forth to Elland Road to watch my beloved Leeds United,” McNamee says, sharing how his father Gerry firmly passed down a lifelong obsession with the club. “Leeds has always meant a huge amount to me, so whenever I thought about playing there, I wanted it to feel like a homecoming gig.”

His debut show at Oporto last year was a packed-out, breakout success, championed heavily by *The Square Ball* podcast and local media. “I had this horrible thought that I was going to turn up in a city that meant so much to me and play to an empty room,” McNamee admits. “Thankfully, the gig was packed to the rafters and became one of my favourite shows I’ve ever played. I’m so excited to come back to the same venue and build on what we started there.”

Described by *The Telegraph* as “as if Springsteen wound up in a studio with The Pogues,” McNamee’s music brings a passionate, Celtic post-punk energy to 21st-century alternative rock. With over 300,000 streams to his name and past festival slots at Isle of Wight and Cambridge Folk, this tiny room show at Oporto promises to be an unforgettable, up-close encounter with one of indie-folk’s rising forces.
