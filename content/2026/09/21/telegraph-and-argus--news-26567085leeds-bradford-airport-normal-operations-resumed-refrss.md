YORKSHIRE'S main commercial airport has been hit by a technical problem causing disruption to flights, although the issue has now been resolved.

At around 10am this morning (September 21), Leeds Bradford Airport advised that the air traffic control service NATS was "experiencing a technical issue" which they described as "impacting flights at airports across the UK, including at LBA.

This morning's flights to Perpignan and Palma are both delayed by about two hours, according to the LBA departure board.

Posting on social media, a spokesperson for the Yeadon airport said: “The air traffic control service, NATS, is experiencing a technical issue which is impacting flights at airports across the UK, including at LBA.

"LBA is working alongside its airline partners to ensure customers are on the move as soon as possible.”

Then at 10:50am, they said: “We have been informed that normal operations have resumed.

"Any affects into this afternoon are the result of this mornings delays.

"Passengers are still advised to check with their airline for the latest flight information.”
