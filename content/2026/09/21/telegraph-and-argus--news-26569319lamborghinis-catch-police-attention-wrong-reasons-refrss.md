Two Lamborghinis have been seized by police following reports of anti-social driving.

Officers took action against the luxury vehicles in Halifax under Section 59 powers after members of the public raised concerns about their use on local roads.

## **Reports of speeding and excessive revving**

One of the vehicles was seized after being reported for driving without due care and attention, with complaints of anti-social driving and excessive engine revving around Halifax town centre.

The second Lamborghini attracted police attention following a number of reports of speeding.

## **Police issue warning to motorists**

West Yorkshire Police reminded motorists that Section 59 warnings and seizures can be issued when vehicles are driven carelessly, inconsiderately, or in a manner that causes alarm, distress or annoyance to members of the public.

Officers thanked residents who reported the incidents, saying information from the public helps identify problem vehicles and allows positive action to be taken to improve road safety.

## **'Not the attention most owners are looking for'**

In a light-hearted message shared on social media, police said: "Whilst Lamborghinis are built to stand out, being loaded onto the back of a recovery truck isn't the kind of attention most owners are looking for!"

The force encouraged anyone concerned about dangerous or anti-social driving to continue reporting incidents.

## **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A spokesperson said: "In the last couple of days, officers have seized not one, but two Lamborghinis under Section 59 powers following reports of anti-social driving in Halifax.

"One vehicle was reported for being driven without due care and attention after reports of anti-social driving and excessive engine revving around the town centre. The second attracted attention after numerous reports of speeding.

"A reminder that Section 59 warnings and seizures can be used where vehicles are being driven carelessly, inconsiderately, or in a manner causing alarm, distress or annoyance to members of the public.

"Thank you to the people who reported the incidents. Information from the public helps us identify problem vehicles, take positive action where appropriate and keep our roads safe.

"Whilst Lamborghinis are built to stand out, being loaded onto the back of a recovery truck isn't the kind of attention most owners are looking for!"
