SKIPTON is set to host an international crowd this weekend for the annual Yarndale festival.

The award-winning event, celebrating yarn, wool, and creativity, will take place at Skipton Auction Mart on Saturday, September 26, and Sunday, September 27.

The festival brings together exhibitors, makers, designers, artists, and yarn enthusiasts from across the UK and beyond.

Yarndale promises a packed programme for fans of knitting, crochet, and crafting, with the sheep pens at Skipton Auction Mart transformed into a “sheepshed of woolly wonder”.

Visitors can explore a range of exhibitors showcasing yarn, wool, patterns, kits, tools, and handmade creations.

The festival will also feature workshops, talks, live music, and other activities, offering plenty of opportunities to learn and be inspired.

This year, the festival will showcase an installation of more than 20,000 woolly dots, made by knitters and crocheters worldwide as part of Yarndale’s Worldwide Creative Project.

The popular "Making Space" will be available for visitors to try something new, share skills, and enjoy crafting alongside others.

With free activities throughout the weekend, it’s an opportunity to discover a new craft or spend time making with others.

Yarndale will also focus on the relationship between craft and the environment through Craft for Climate.

This year’s programme includes the new "Squaring The Risk" project, exploring climate change and its impact through knitting and making.

For those seeking inspiration to take home, "A Yarndale Flock", a new book of eight sheep-themed knitting patterns, will be launched.

Created by Sue Stratford Knits, the patterns use Yarndale’s limited-edition West Yorkshire Spinners Signature 4ply Sock Yarn colourways.

Entertainment will be provided by live music from The Gatsbys and The Ginjammers, and songs from DripDrop Theatre’s musical "Close Knit".

With visitors from across the globe expected, Yarndale offers two days filled with yarn, creativity, and inspiration, providing chances to discover new exhibitors, learn new skills, and meet fellow yarn enthusiasts.

Online tickets are available until Thursday, September 24, at 5pm, with a limited number available on the door.

For more information, tickets, and the full programme, visit the Yarndale website: www.yarndale.co.uk.
