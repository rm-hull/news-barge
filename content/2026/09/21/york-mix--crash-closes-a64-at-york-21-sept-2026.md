**\*\* The road has now reopened \*\***

Original story from Monday 21 September 2026 at 5:45PM

A crash has closed the A64 at York.

Emergency services are at the scene, at the junction with North Lane, Huntington.

North Yorkshire Police posted at 5.34pm: “A road closure is on place with highways staff assisting with the diversions.

#### Crash location

“Please avoid the area until further notice.”

Drivers are reporting standstill traffic.
