Since early Monday morning, Straße des 17. Juni between Großer Stern and the Brandenburg Gate, as well as Yitzhak-Rabin-Straße, have been closed. Starting Thursday, Ebertstraße, John-Foster-Dulles-Allee, Scheidemannstraße, Dorotheenstraße and the government district will also be closed. On Saturday, Potsdamer Straße between Potsdamer Platz and Reichpietschufer, Unter den Linden between Pariser Platz and Friedrichstraße, and the skating route will be closed during the day until approximately 5:30 p.m. On Sunday, the streets along the marathon route, particularly in Tiergarten and Mitte, will be closed almost continuously until the end of the event. In addition, there will be road closures in Friedrichshain, Kreuzberg, Neukölln, Schöneberg, Steglitz, Wilmersdorf and Zehlendorf.
