A council worker lost his job after stealing £80 of beer from Morrisons and B&M while wearing his work uniform.

Lloyd Ames became homeless after a relationship broke down and increasingly turned to alcohol, a court heard.

The 40-year-old admitted stealing £80 worth of beer in a shoplifting spree, Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard.

Prosecutor Jade Houston said Ames was under a community order after being convicted of a drink-driving offence.

Ames stole £48 worth of alcohol from Morrisons in Bishop Auckland on three occasions and £32 worth of beer from B&M in Shildon, she said.

Ames, who lives at Goosepool Hotel near Middleton St George, admitted four shoplifting charges committed between April and June this year.

His solicitor, Sam Brewster, said Ames left his job as a landscaper for Durham County Council after being caught shoplifting in his work uniform and facing disciplinary action.

He said: “He has a community order from his conviction earlier this year and is getting help from various services.”

Mr Brewster said Ames is taking steps to address his drinking habits and is determined to get back into work and secure a permanent address again.

Magistrates’ chairman Thomas Reay said: “We are quite happy to let the community order continue because you are working well on it.”

Ames was ordered to pay £213 in compensation and court costs.

Mr Reay added: “It is good that you are working with the Probation Service, it shows that you are on the right road to get your life back on track.”
