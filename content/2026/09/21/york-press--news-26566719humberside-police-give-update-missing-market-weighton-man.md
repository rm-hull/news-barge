POLICE have issued an update following news that a 26-year-old man from a town near York had been reported missing.

The man from Market Weighton was last seen in the town yesterday morning (September 20).

Humberside Police said in a statement issued this morning that the man has been located and thanked everybody who shared its appeal to find him.
