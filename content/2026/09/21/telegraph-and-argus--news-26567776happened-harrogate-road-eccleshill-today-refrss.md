THE YORKSHIRE Ambulance Service has provided a statement on this morning's crash in the Eccleshill.

An ambulance and a team leader were dispatched to the scene of a road traffic collision, not far from a Lidl supermarket on Harrogate Road following an emergency call at 8.38am, but no patients were conveyed to hospital.

Photographs shared by social media users appeared to show a busy junction, with multiple vehicles strewn across the road.

It looked as though at least three cars had suffered some damage.

## What the emergency service had to say

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 8.38am this morning to report a road traffic collision on Harrogate Road in Bradford.

"An ambulance and a team leader were dispatched to the scene, but no patients were conveyed to hospital."

West Yorkshire Police were contacted for a comment.
