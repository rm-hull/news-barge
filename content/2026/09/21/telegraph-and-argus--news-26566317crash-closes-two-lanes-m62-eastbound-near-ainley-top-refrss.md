A CRASH on the M62 Eastbound closed two lanes this morning (September 21).

The AA has reported very slow traffic and congestion due to a crash and recovery work on the M62 Eastbound.

There's congestion after Ainley Top (J24 A629) right up until Rishworth Moor (J22).

This incident was first reported at 5.38am today, with the lanes now having reopened.
