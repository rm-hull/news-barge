Drivers are being warned that roadworks will close the A19 near York for ten nights.

It will shut in both directions from where the Designer Outlet roundabout joins the A19, along to Skipwith Road in Escrick.

The A19 will be closed between 8pm and 6am each week-night from Monday (28 September).

The works, to resurface and patch the road, are expected to last up to ten week nights, finishing by 6am on Saturday 10 October.

City of York Council says: “Each morning at 6am the road will reopen to all traffic unless there are exceptional circumstances.”

It will remain open at weekends.

## Bus route unaffected

The 415 bus service between York and Selby will be running as usual and will not be diverted.

Buses will be allowed through the road closure, due to there being no suitable alternative route.

Buses will serve the Designer Outlet as normal.

## Other traffic diverted

For all traffic other than buses, a signed diversion will be in place.

The council says: “Due to the capacity the A19 holds for large vehicles, we must offer a diversion able to cope with the size and weight that the road usually carries.

“This will use the A64 Tadcaster Road (from the Designer Outlet roundabout), A162 through Towton and Sherburn in Elmet bypass, A63 past Selby, rejoining the A19 at Barlby continuing north to Escrick, and vice versa in the other direction.”

Staff and shoppers leaving Designer Outlet York will be able to use the Designer Outlet roundabout to travel north to York or east and west on the A64.

If travelling south, drivers should use the A64 towards Tadcaster to join the diversion.

Staff living in the works area will receive a letter setting out their access arrangements.

## Cycling advice

The council says there are no suitable cycle diversions for this section of road.

However the B1222 Naburn Lane route will remain accessible and could be used to cycle between York and the A19 south of Escrick.

Also, the Solar Way links the centre of Selby to York via an off-road route suitable for cyclists.

The works will be carried out by Lane Rentals Services on behalf of the council.

You can see a map of the works on this City of York Council webpage.
