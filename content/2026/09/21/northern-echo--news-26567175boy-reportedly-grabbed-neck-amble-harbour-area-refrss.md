Police are hunting a man after a boy was reportedly grabbed by the neck near a North East pier.

At about 6.15pm on Friday, July 17, police received a report of a disturbance in the Amble Harbour area.

It was reported that a number of children had been jumping and pushing each other off the pier into the sea.

A man – believed to be aged in his 30s – is then reported to have grabbed a 15-year-old boy around the neck on the steps leading down from the pier, causing him injuries.

Police have now issued an appeal for witnesses to come forward.

A Northumbria Police spokesperson said: "Anyone with information or footage should send Northumbria Police a direct message on social media or use the live chat or report forms on the Force’s website.

"Those unable to make contact in these ways can call 101.

"Please quote reference number: 26000398374."
