**UPDATE: Humberside Police said in a statement issued this morning that the 26-year-old man has been located.**

A man is missing from an East Yorkshire town.

Humberside Police is appealing for help the man last seen in Market Weighton around 9am yesterday (Sunday, September 20).

Humberside Police said anyone who sees the man or has any information should call 101 quoting reference HP-20260920-0492.
