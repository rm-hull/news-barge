Residents across Darlington were left asking what was happening after police helicopters circled overhead and patrol cars raced through the town this afternoon.

Residents reported seeing and hearing a police helicopter over several areas, including Haughton, from about 12.30pm today (Monday, September 21).

Pictures taken in the area show the aircraft hovering overhead, with people taking to social media to ask what was happening.

One resident said: “It's all over the place, I live near the train station and can hear and see it.”

Another added: “I can see it from my garden, it's been going on for half an hour now.”

![]()Police helicopter spotted over Darlington this afternoon (September 21).

The helicopter was seen over Haughton and was reportedly heard near Firthmoor and Harrowgate Hill.

Several police cars were also seen rushing along Neasham Road near Darlington railway station while the helicopter remained overhead.

At this stage, the reason for the helicopter's presence remains unknown.

The Northern Echo has contacted Durham Constabulary and the National Police Air Service (NPAS) for information.
