The developers of RPCS3 have found a workaround for an Nvidia driver bug and are touting up to 37% performance gains.

![AMD GPU](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/FKEfLV3WiguWYAz37vRmyW-320-80.png)

A developer has wired up the ZLUDA project to AMD's HIP libraries for Windows, putting a small but signifcant bridge over NVIDIA's CUDA moat.

![Nvidia GeForce RTX 5090](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Axh3XNQ6DqK4KMfxtoMRBU-320-80.jpg)

![Nvidia GeForce RTX 4060, Asus Dual OC, installed in test PC](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/EoAk4RMrsrfyKYk8DrbKJL-320-80.jpg)

Borrowing a driver from the upcoming RTX Spark, VoidTech managed to get x86 Windows games running on a Huawei Arm workstation.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/kUeEajuJ6YeihJQTw4858d-320-80.jpg)

Hidden settings suggest AMD is testing Multi Frame Generation.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/XCxGXQPUTqQ7sr5bD4otmL-320-80.png)

Zluda is back to a hobby, as the open-source project has lost commercial funding with version 6 but added early 32-bit PhysX support.

![AMD RX 7000 Series Reference Card](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/sqBJe69q8iHjtHHaBL2NCG-320-80.jpg)

If you own an RX 7000 series GPU, you can update your graphics driver today and enjoy native FSR 4.1 in over 300 games thanks to INT8 fallback.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/A5B8BfAZTyVWEpy5TRr3UY-320-80.jpg)

NVK, the community-built open-source Vulkan driver for Nvidia GPUs in Mesa, has gained experimental DLSS support.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/TpoDkkbYgXcWudvRBhR3je-320-80.jpg)
