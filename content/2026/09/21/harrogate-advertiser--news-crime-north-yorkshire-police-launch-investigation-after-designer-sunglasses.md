# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after designer sunglasses stolen from vehicle in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire is appealing for information and witnesses after a pair of designer sunglasses were stolen from a vehicle in Harrogate.

The incident happened at 2.05am on Tuesday, September 15 when a vehicle parked on Coppice Drive was entered and a pair of Michael Kors sunglasses was stolen.

Officers are now asking anyone who may have information that could help identify those responsible to come forward.

Police are particularly keen to hear from anyone who heard or saw anything suspicious in the area around the time of the incident.

![The police are appealing for information and witnesses after a pair of designer sunglasses were stolen from a vehicle parked on Coppice Drive in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjg2MTNhYmU4LTY0ZGEtNDg5NC05MWE1LTk4ZjlmNDJkNzczZTo5NjJlZmYwYS1jZDZhLTQ0NzEtYTUzMC01ZjA0MjViZjVlOGU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


They are also appealing to residents with CCTV, doorbell camera or dash cam footage that may have captured the theft, or any suspicious activity on the street before or afterwards, to get in touch.

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260181172 when providing any details regarding the incident.
