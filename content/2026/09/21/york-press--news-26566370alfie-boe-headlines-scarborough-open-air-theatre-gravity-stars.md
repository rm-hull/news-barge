## Star 'adored by fans of musical theatre' set for show in iconic North Yorkshire venue

Alfie Boe will be headlining Scarborough Open Air Theatre in July 2027 *(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)>*

WEST End and Broadway stars will light up the Yorkshire coast for a night of musical theatre magic.

Internationally acclaimed tenor Alfie Boe will headline the performance at Scarborough Open Air Theatre (OAT) on Sunday, July 25, 2027, joined by special guests Louise Dearman, Kerry Ellis, and Rachel Tucker.

Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, who programme the Scarborough venue, said: "This will be another special night on the Yorkshire coast.




"Combining the powerhouse vocals of Alfie Boe with the triumvirate of musical theatre royalty that is Gravity will make this an incredible night and one that will be absolutely adored by fans of musical theatre."

Alfie Boe has performed at the venue no less than four times previously, is best known for his role as Jean Valjean in Les Misérables, which he has played in the West End, on Broadway, and during the 40th-anniversary world tour.

![]()Gravity stars Louise Dearman, Kerry Ellis and Rachel Tucker will accompany Alfie Boe as his very special gusests *(Image: Contributed)*

He has also performed at major events including the Queen’s Diamond Jubilee at Buckingham Palace and has released more than a dozen studio albums.

The Gravity show features West End stars Dearman, Ellis, and Tucker, who are best known for their roles in Wicked.

Ellis was the first British actress to play Elphaba, Dearman remains the only performer to have played both Elphaba and Glinda, and Tucker has played Elphaba on both sides of the Atlantic.

Gravity celebrates their collective careers and includes songs from iconic shows such as We Will Rock You, Cats, Oliver!, My Fair Lady, Miss Saigon, Les Misérables, Guys and Dolls, Evita, Joseph, The Great Gatsby, Sunset Boulevard, and Chicago.

Mr Boe trained at the Royal College of Music and the Royal Opera House before performing leading roles with major opera companies worldwide.

Tickets go on general sale at 10am Friday September 25.
