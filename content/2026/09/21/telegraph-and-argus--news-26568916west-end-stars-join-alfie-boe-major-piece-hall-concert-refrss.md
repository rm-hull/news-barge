ALFIE Boe is set to headline a major outdoor concert next summer.

The internationally acclaimed tenor will perform at The Piece Hall in Halifax on Tuesday, July 27, 2027, as part of the venue’s Live at The Piece Hall series.

Nicky Chance-Thompson, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "The Piece Hall is a place for everyone and one of my favourite things about our summer season is the diversity of the musical programme.

"We’ve had so many requests for the amazing Alfie Boe and I’m delighted we’ve managed to secure him for 2027.

"I can just imagine the goosebumps of hearing his magnificent voice filling every corner of this breathtaking historic venue.

"And how magical that he’s accompanied by some of the brightest musical theatre stars around, performing the biggest show tunes going, something completely new for us – I can already hear the shouts of ‘encore’."

Mr Boe will be joined by Louise Dearman, Kerry Ellis, and Rachel Tucker, stars of the West End and Broadway, as his special guests for the evening.

The trio will perform their Gravity show, celebrating their collective stage careers with songs from musicals including We Will Rock You, Cats, Les Misérables, and Evita.

Ms Ellis was the first British actress to play Elphaba in Wicked, Ms Dearman remains the only performer to play both Elphaba and Glinda, and Ms Tucker has played Elphaba on both Broadway and the West End.

Tickets go on general sale at 10am on Friday via ticketmaster.co.uk.
