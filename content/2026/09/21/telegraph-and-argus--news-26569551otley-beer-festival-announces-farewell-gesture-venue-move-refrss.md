Otley Beer Festival is marking its move to a new venue with a farewell gesture.

After years at Otley Rugby Club, the festival will move to Otley Common for its next event on November 20 and 21.

As a farewell to its long-time host, organisers will sponsor Otley RUFC player Freddie Ziegler during the 2026/27 season.

An Otley Beer Festival spokesperson said: "This season, Otley Beer Festival is incredibly proud to sponsor Otley RUFC player Freddie Ziegler for the 2026/27 season.

"We're chuffed to be backing one of Otley's own and keeping a little bit of festival spirit on the rugby pitch.

"After many brilliant years, this summer we said goodbye to our long-standing home at Otley Rugby Club.

"The rugby club has played a huge part in the festival’s story and its success, and while we may be moving on, we’re incredibly proud to remain friends with the club and continue investing in an organisation that has supported us for so many years.

"We’re hugely excited to be entering a new era for Otley Beer Festival as we move to our new home at Otley Common but we’ll always have a lot of love for the place where so many pints, friendships and festival memories began."

The new venue, Otley Common, is a community-run social, cultural and enterprise hub that operates from the former Methodist Church on Walkergate.

Over the past 20 years or so, the festival has raised more than £100,000 for local charities.
