Brew York have released three new beers for spooky season – inspired by classic literary horror.

The York brewery have **today** (Monday 21 September) launched the ‘Dark Chapters’ series.

It comprises of; *Dracula’s Bite*– a raspberry and marshmallow pastry sour,* Franken Spice*– a pumpkin spiced stout and* Jekyll & Haze* – a 6.66% ABV hazy pale.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Screenshot-2026-09-21-at-11.52.34.png)

The can artwork, designed by York-based agency UnitedByDesign, shows each protagonist against appropriate backdrops inspired by the classic texts.

The beers will be available on keg and in can, with the stout also being available on cask too.

Andrew Stark, head of marketing, said: “Halloween gives us lots of inspiration to build on the visual side of our brand identity.

“Previous years’ ranges were influenced by classic horror video games and horror films; the feedback was so good from these that we were looking at 2026’s theme as soon as 2025’s beers became available.

“The Dark Chapters range really supports our visual approach. As well as creating great beers, we really want our product to stand out on shelves and in pubs and this range will do just that.”

The Dark Chapters Halloween range will be available in Brew York venues, selected trade customers and on the Brew York website from today.

And despite the end of the year being in sight, the brewery has no plans to take it easy.

Yousef Doubooni, sales director, said, “Our Christmas range has been created and we’re well underway with our plans for 2027 which, as ever, will include a whole host of new beers as well as the return of some old favourites. Watch this space.”
