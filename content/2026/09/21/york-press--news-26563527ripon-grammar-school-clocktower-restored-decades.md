A historic North Yorkshire school clocktower is chiming again after a painstaking restoration brought its mechanism back to life.

Ripon Grammar School caretaker Matthew Buller spent around 140 hours restoring the 1889 clocktower, which is now fully operational.

Mr Buller said: "It was relief, excitement and overwhelming joy. I was ecstatic at the results.

"To look back from where the project started to where it is now is very rewarding."

![]()Caretaker Matthew Buller spent around 140 hours restoring Ripon Grammar School's 1889 clock mechanism to full working order *(Image: Supplied)*

He began restoring it in November 2025, working through weekends and school holidays.

Built by W Potts & Sons of Leeds, the clock last worked properly 40 to 50 years ago.

Its mechanism was designed by Sir Edmund Beckett, also known as Lord Grimthorpe, who also designed the machinery controlling Big Ben’s great bell and clock.

Headmaster Jonathan Webb praised Mr Buller’s efforts.

![]()Caretaker Matthew Buller spent around 140 hours restoring Ripon Grammar School's 1889 clock mechanism to full working order *(Image: Supplied)*

He said: "Matthew has devoted an enormous amount of his personal time and expertise to this project.

"His extraordinary dedication has brought more than an old clock back to life.

"He has restored a living piece of RGS history and, with the clock and bells now working again, reconnected generations of our school community."

The clock is powered by a traditional gravity system and uses cast-iron weights and bronze bells, as described in an earlier account of the restoration.
