Get ready for two days of full throttle action – in an epic weekend of speed.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Logo.png)

It’s the season finale for Straightliners 2026, with the **Firework Frenzy** taking place at the legendary Melbourne Raceway on**Saturday 3 and Sunday 4 October**.

The two day event will feature high-speed action, family fun, and spectacular fireworks finale.

Spectators can watch car drivers and motorcyclists push their car or bike to the limits – in a chance to experience the thrill of straight-line speed.

The event is also open to all drivers and riders looking to test their vehicle’s performance on the track. Plus, if you don’t have a car there’s one at the raceway you can rent to have a go.

There will also be an appearance from the high performance jet dragster ‘Split Second’.

It’s set to be an adrenaline packed weekend for the whole family, with the electrifying day at the strip culminating with a fantastic firework display in the evening (Saturday only).

There will be grandstand viewing, food stalls, and a bar – so the whole day is sorted.

Spectator tickets start from £17.50, and camping tickets are an option too. Find out more and book your tickets at the Straightliners website here.
