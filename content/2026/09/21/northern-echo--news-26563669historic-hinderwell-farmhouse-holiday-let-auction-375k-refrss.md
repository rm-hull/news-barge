A historic North Yorkshire farmhouse with a holiday let and a barn ripe for conversion is going under the hammer later this month.

Holme Farm, in Hinderwell near Staithes, includes the converted holiday let The Folly and an 18th-century barn with scope for redevelopment.

![]()Holme Farm, Hinderwell. *(Image: Auction House North Yorkshire & Tees Valley / Inglebys)*

The Grade II-listed farmhouse has a guide price of £375,000 and was built in the 1860s for the Tyreman farming family.

The farmhouse has four bedrooms, a lounge with a wood-burning stove, a sitting room, a kitchen and a dining room.

![]()Holme Farm, Hinderwell. *(Image: Auction House North Yorkshire & Tees Valley / Inglebys)*

Two additional loft rooms could also be converted into further living accommodation subject to planning permissions.

![]()Holme Farm, Hinderwell. *(Image: Auction House North Yorkshire & Tees Valley / Inglebys)*

Outside, the property benefits from off-road parking and a private courtyard.

Converted from a barn in 2022, The Folly holiday let sits beside the farmhouse and has a glazed gable window overlooking fields and moorland from its mezzanine.

The additional 18th-century barn provides further scope for future owners and could potentially be transformed into another holiday let, studio or workshop.

The property sits on the High Street in Hinderwell, a village close to both the North York Moors National Park and the Yorkshire coast, with cosy seaside spot Staithes just a five minute drive away.

![]()Holme Farm, Hinderwell. *(Image: Auction House North Yorkshire & Tees Valley / Inglebys)*

Residents have easy access to walking routes, coastal trails and other nearby destinations including Saltburn and Runswick Bay.

Andy McFaull, auction manager for Auction House North Yorkshire & Tees Valley, said: "This is a particularly versatile property offering broad market appeal thanks to its unique layout and the added benefit of a self-contained annexe, currently operating as a successful holiday let.

"The combination of substantial family accommodation and income-generating potential is likely to attract a wide range of purchasers, including investors seeking a ready-made revenue stream, owner-occupiers looking to offset living costs through holiday letting, and multi-generational families requiring flexible living arrangements."

Auction House North Yorkshire & Tees Valley will auction the property with Inglebys Estate Agents on Tuesday, September 22, with bidding beginning at noon.
