A 37 YEAR old man sent sexual messages to someone he believed to be a 14-year old girl, heard Skipton Magistrates Court.

Adam Longden messaged the girl called 'Ellie' in Skipton on Snap Chat sending her pictures of his penis and saying it 'would be hot' if she was naked in the shower with him.

'Ellie', who was in fact a decoy alias set up by online group Punishers North Wales, told Longden she was 14 years old, the court heard today (Friday).

Punishers North Wales informed police in Wales who passed the information on to North Yorkshire Police.

Longden was arrested and his phone seized where the Snap Chat communication was found along with several indecent images of children, the court heard.

Longden, of previous good character, pleaded guilty to attempting to engage in sexual communication with a child in Skipton between February 12, 2025 and February 15, 2025.

He also admitted making indecent photographs of a child between February 14, 2025 and April 18, 2025 in Skipton, namely nine Category B images, and making an indecent image between February 14 and April 18, 2025 in Skipton, which was the most serious Category A.

Longden, of Spencers Holt, Harrogate, is due to be sentenced at Harrogate Magistrates Court on October 23 following a pre- sentence report. He could also be sent to Crown Court for sentencing.

Longden was told in court on Friday he had three days to report to Harrogate Police Station as a sexual notification requirement.
