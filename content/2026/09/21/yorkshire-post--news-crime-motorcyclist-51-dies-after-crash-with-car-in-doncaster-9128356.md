# Motorcyclist, 51, dies after crash with car in Doncaster

## A 51-year-old motorcyclist has died following a crash involving a car in Doncaster.

At 11:36am on Saturday (Sept 19), police responded to reports of a crash in Doncaster.

The incident occurred on Bawtry Road, at the turning with Tickhill Road, and involved a white Honda and a red and black Triumph motorbike.

The driver of the motorbike, a 51-year-old man, was pronounced dead at the scene.

![Bawtry Road, at the turning with Tickhill Road,](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/21/12/53/Bawtry-Road-at-the-turning-with-Tickhill-Road.png?trim=0,101,0,100&width=640&quality=65)


Police have said his family have been informed and are being supported by Family Liaison Officers.

The driver of the Honda stayed at the scene and assisted with police enquiries.

An appeal has been launched for witnesses or anyone with information to come forward.

A spokesperson for South Yorkshire Police said: “We are now appealing for anyone who may have witnessed the collision and who may have dashcam footage to please come forward.

“If you have any information you can either call 101 or report through our online reporting system. Please quote incident number 369 of 19 September 2026 when you get in touch.

“You can submit any relevant footage here.”
