A fire crew took part in a rescue mission in York early this morning (Monday).

They were called to a home on **Thief Lane** off Hull Road at**4.17am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb responded to a person that had accidentally become stuck inside their bathroom after the locking mechanism failed.

#### **Thief Lane, York**

“Small tools were used to release the person who was safe and well. Advice was given.”
