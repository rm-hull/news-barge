After more than 30 years of serving bread and pastries to customers across East Cleveland, a popular bakery has announced the next phase of its expansion as it prepares to open a fourth store.

Stonehouse Bakery will open a new branch in Marske, joining existing shops in Saltburn, Loftus and Skelton.

The family-run business announced the news on social media: "The wait is over – Stonehouse Bakery is coming to Marske."

![]()The Stonehouse Bakery in Skelton. *(Image: Google Maps)*

Renovations are already under way at the new premises, with owners promising to keep customers updated on progress, as well as the official opening date which is yet to be announced.

In an earlier teaser post, the business said: "Stonehouse Bakery has some exciting news.

"We're expanding – we've got a new shop coming soon.

"It'll be our fourth shop and we can't wait to show you."

Customers welcomed the announcement after speculating about where the new bakery would open after the business posted the teaser.

Established in 1993, Stonehouse Bakery began life as a café in Danby before founders Ian and Fiona Clacherty opened their first bakery shop in Loftus three years later.

More than three decades on, the family business has built a loyal following across East Cleveland, with son Harvey Clacherty taking over the reins earlier this year.

The bakery's product range includes everything from classic loaves and crusty cobs to flavoured ciabattas, to scones, croissants and flapjacks.

Alongside its retail shops, Stonehouse also supplies wholesale products to a variety of local businesses, including pubs, hotels, restaurants and independent shops.

The expansion into Marske marks another milestone for the company as it continues to grow its presence across the region.

While an opening date has yet to be confirmed, customers will not have to wait too much longer, with work already underway to bring the newest Stonehouse Bakery shop to life.
