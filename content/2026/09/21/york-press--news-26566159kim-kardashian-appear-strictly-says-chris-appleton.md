Kim Kardashian could make a surprise appearance in the audience of Strictly Come Dancing, according to celebrity hairdresser Chris Appleton.

The 43-year-old was partnered with professional dancer Amy Dowden during Saturday’s launch episode of the BBC One dancing show.

The new series of Strictly will see him compete against stars such as singer Delta Goodrem, former Love Island winner Dani Dyer and former England footballer Shaun Wright-Phillips.

**For all things Strictly, read our article here -** Latest Strictly Come Dancing updates including start date, new hosts, lineup and more

Asked whether Kardashian, whose reality TV show The Kardashians Appleton has appeared on, would be in the audience during the show, he told the Press Association, “Who knows? Watch this space, but I don’t know, anything could happen.

"I think being in Strictly, I’ve realised anything is possible and magic happens."

He admitted that demand for tickets is high, saying, "I have no idea, I know a lot of people want to come to the show and we’re only allocated two tickets."

Kim Kardashian has publicly supported Mr Appleton since his involvement in the show was announced, even sending him a bouquet of flowers.

Mr Appleton said, "Kim’s so sweet, I mean, she’s been such an amazing person throughout my whole career.

"She’s obviously fantastic at what she does, but obviously I know Kim personally, and she’s just always been so kind, and she sent me a beautiful bouquet of flowers, which was just so kind of her to do, she didn’t have to.

"And she said, ‘break a leg’, and just commenting on my post when I did it, and that’s so nice of her to support me because she’s always been so supportive.

"Like I say, she’s always been so supportive, I’m very lucky to work with her, and she’s my hair muse, she’s always been my girl."

The first live show of Strictly Come Dancing will air on BBC One and iPlayer at 6.15pm.
