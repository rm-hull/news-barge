A busy burger joint appears to have shut for good as the business stands lifeless in Darlington.

Salt, on Grange Road, has built a reputation for burgers, loaded fries and street food after launching for deliveries and collections in 2020.

However, recently it appears to have shut its doors; furniture has seemingly been removed from its premises, and Google has marked it ‘permanently closed’.

![]()The outside of Salt on Grange Road, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

After Covid-19 restrictions eased, the venue opened fully as a restaurant and continued serving new specials alongside its regular favourites.

But concern about the business grew after furniture was removed from its Grange Road premises.

![]()Chairs and tables have been removed from Salt, Grange Road, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Images from Salt show chairs piled on tables, while several tables have been removed from the front of the restaurant.

There is no notice on the door, while Google lists the venue as ‘permanently closed’.

![]()A few remaining items in Salt, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Several people have asked on social media whether Salt has closed, with some saying they visited between 5pm and 10pm but found it shut.

Others reacted with disappointment, crediting the restaurant as "the best burger ever" and saying "tell me it isn't so".

![]()The outside of Salt on Grange Road, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

The business has not said whether it has closed, but has not posted on social media since June and has taken down its website.

All days and time slots are blocked out on its online delivery service, while adding items to the basket produces an ‘invalid’ message.

Salt’s table-booking service can still be accessed.

Salt opened a second venture in Richmond, North Yorkshire, in December 2023.

That branch is also listed as ‘permanently closed’ on Google and has not posted on social media since April 2024.

The Northern Echo has contacted Salt for a statement about its future.
