POLICE have been brought in to investigate two fires started deliberately in York.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said it received the call outs yesterday evening and early this morning (Monday, September 21).




At 3.40am this morning, firefighters from Acomb were called to a wheelie bin fire which had spread to a tree near Monkgate just outside the city walls.

A service spokesperson said the fire was put out using a hose reel jet and thermal imaging camera.

At 7.14pm yesterday, a crew from Acomb was called to a woodland fire in Grange Lane.

A services spokesperson said it measured one square metre and was extinguished using knapsack sprayers and small tools.
