A partnership with a North East football club is putting college artists’ work in front of a much bigger audience.

Hartlepool’s Northern School of Art has teamed up with Middlesbrough FC to create illustrations for the club’s official matchday programmes during its 150th anniversary season.

The project gives aspiring artists and designers a chance to showcase their work while celebrating the club.

The BA (Hons) Illustration students have created artwork inspired by the club’s history and the town of Middlesbrough.

![]()Ace Edwards' Roseberry Topping-inspired illustration featured on the programme cover for Middlesbrough FC's league opener against Lincoln City *(Image: NORTHERN SCHOOL OF ART)*

The project began in early August with Ace Edwards’s Roseberry Topping-inspired artwork for Middlesbrough’s league opener against Lincoln City.

Speaking on the project, Ace said: “It’s been an honour working with my local club and I’ve loved seeing fans’ reactions to the programme covers.

“I’m especially grateful for the chance to illustrate a beautiful landmark so close to home for such a large audience.”

Other artworks include Amelie Smith’s cover for the Carabao Cup fixture against Wrexham.

![]()Amelie Smith's artwork for Middlesbrough FC's Carabao Cup tie against Wrexham featured a red lion facing a Welsh dragon, drawing inspiration from the clubs' crests *(Image: NORTHERN SCHOOL OF ART)*

The design showed a red lion duelling a Welsh dragon, inspired by the two clubs’ crests.

“I feel incredibly proud seeing my work on the cover,” Amelie said.

"Seeing the final programme in professional imagery promoting MFC’s game against Wrexham was a bit of a ‘pinch me’ moment.

“I am extremely grateful to have been given the opportunity to create work like this.

"I also feel incredibly hopeful.

"It is deeply inspiring seeing a club like Middlesbrough FC proudly representing and supporting artists and their unique creativity.”

![]()The Northern School of Art's Play Among the Stars illustrated fanzine, which featured members of Middlesbrough FC's men's and women's teams and helped pave the way for the club's latest student-artist collaboration *(Image: NORTHERN SCHOOL OF ART)*

The project follows the success of Play Among the Stars, an illustrated fanzine produced by students on The Northern School of Art’s illustration course, which launched at the end of last season and featured members of both the men’s and women’s teams.

The programme will continue at Middlesbrough FC’s home matches throughout the club’s 150th anniversary season.

Middlesbrough Football Club marketing manager Matthew Barber, who worked with the students, said: “It has been a pleasure to work with the students from The Northern School of Art on this project, and to showcase the talent and creativity we have here on Teesside.

“We’re looking forward to seeing more artwork take pride of place in our matchday programme throughout the season.”

More student designs will be unveiled in the coming months.
