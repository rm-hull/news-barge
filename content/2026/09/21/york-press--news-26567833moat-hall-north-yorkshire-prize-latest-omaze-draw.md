## PICTURES: See inside £4million North Yorkshire home in latest Omaze prize draw

Moat Hall between York and Boroughbridge is the £4million Omaze draw prize *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A luxurious country home near York could be yours for just the price of a raffle ticket.

Omaze has launched its latest Million Pound House Draw, offering entrants the chance to win a Grade II-listed property worth £4 million, located in Little Ouseburn in North Yorkshire.

The prize also includes £250,000 in cash, and the draw will raise funds for Blood Cancer UK.

The property, known as Moat Hall, was earlier on the market this year for £4,750,000.

Helen Rowntree, CEO of Blood Cancer UK, said: "We're delighted to be partnering with Omaze on their Million Pound House Draw for the third time.

"Thanks to the incredible Omaze community, we've already helped fund research that's improving the lives of people affected by blood cancer.

"But blood cancer remains the UK's fifth most common cancer and its third biggest cancer killer.

"This partnership gives us an opportunity to go further.

"Together, you’ll be helping fund pioneering clinical trials to help treat and beat the deadliest blood cancers.

![]()The drawing room at Moat Hall *(Image: Pic supplied)*

"With your help, you'll be giving more people more time with their loved ones."

Omaze has guaranteed a donation of £1 million to Blood Cancer UK from the Yorkshire house draw.

The funds will support clinical trials for some of the most challenging forms of blood cancer.

![]()Tennis Court at Moat Hall *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The property itself is a six-bedroom family home, refurbished to a high standard while retaining its period charm.

It features flagstones, historic beams, and two 18th-century fireplaces.

The winner will receive the house mortgage-free, with all stamp duty and legal fees covered, plus £155,000 worth of furnishings.

They can choose to move in, rent out the property, or sell it.

![]()The outside of Moat Hall *(Image: Pic supplied)*

If rented, the home could generate between £6,000 and £7,000 per month.

The ground floor includes a reception hall, drawing room, lounge, dining room, kitchen, breakfast room, conservatory, potting shed, boot room, and utility room.

Upstairs, there is a main bedroom with a dressing room and en suite, five additional bedrooms (one with an en suite), a library, and a family bathroom.

![]()Moat Hall in the evening *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The grounds include mature woodland, a lake, orchard, vegetable garden, tennis court, paddock, and outdoor pizza oven.

![]()Moat Hall library *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A separate two-bedroom cottage is also part of the property.

Matt Pohlson, founder and CEO of Omaze, said: "Everyone at Omaze is delighted to be partnering with Blood Cancer UK again for our latest house draw.

![]()The Moat Hall Kitchen *(Image: Pic supplied)*

"By offering this incredible country home in North Yorkshire, plus £250,000 in cash, Omaze gives someone the chance to win a truly life-changing prize, while also raising vital funds for Blood Cancer UK's crucial work."

Online entries for the Omaze Million Pound House Draw, Yorkshire, close on Sunday, October 25, 2026.
