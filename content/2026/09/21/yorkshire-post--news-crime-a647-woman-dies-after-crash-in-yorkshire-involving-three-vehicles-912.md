# A647: Woman dies after crash in Yorkshire involving three vehicles

## A woman has died and two people have been arrested after a crash involving three vehicles in Halifax.

Police are appealing for witnesses following the collision which is believed to have involved three vehicles in Halifax on Sunday September 20.

The collision happened on the A647 Queensbury Road north of the junction with Howcans Lane shortly before 10.45am, West Yorkshire Police said.

A Honda Civic being driven towards Queensbury was in collision with a Seat Leon being driven in the opposite direction.

A VW Golf, which was also being driven in the opposite direction, was also damaged at the scene.

A rear-seat passenger in the Honda, a woman aged in her 40s, suffered fatal injuries.

She died at the scene, police said.

The road was fully closed as emergency services tended to a number of injured people.

Three people were taken to hospital with serious injuries, police said.

The Major Collision Enquiry Team (MCET) would like to speak to anyone who saw or has footage of the collision or the circumstances leading up to it.

The male driver of the VW Golf was arrested on suspicion of drug driving and causing death by dangerous driving.

He has been bailed pending further enquiries.

The male driver of the Seat Leon was also arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and has also been bailed pending further enquiries.

Anyone with information that can assist the investigation is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat) quoting reference 0518 of 20/9.

Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
