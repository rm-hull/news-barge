A major two-and-a-half-hour rescue effort involving air ambulance and mountain rescue crews was sparked after a walker was injured on moorland.

Cleveland Mountain Rescue Team was requested by Yorkshire Ambulance Service to help with the incident on Farndale Moor on Sunday, September 20.

The team said both of its Land Rovers, along with team members, were deployed to a rendezvous point at the Lion Inn on Blakey Ridge before travelling to the casualty's location.

![]()Rescue crews were called after a stranded walker suffered an injury *(Image: CLEVELAND MOUNTAIN RESCUE)*

In a social media update, the team said the vehicles collected available members for the four-kilometre journey to the scene, while others who arrived later remained on standby at the Lion Inn.

One of the Land Rovers also guided an ambulance towards the casualty's location.

The injured walker was treated at the scene by medics from Yorkshire Air Ambulance before being carried to the waiting ambulance.

![]()Rescue crews were called after a stranded walker suffered an injury *(Image: CLEVELAND MOUNTAIN RESCUE)*

Cleveland Mountain Rescue Team said: "The team were requested to provide assistance to Yorkshire Ambulance Service with the extrication of an injured walker with a lower leg injury on Farndale Moor."
