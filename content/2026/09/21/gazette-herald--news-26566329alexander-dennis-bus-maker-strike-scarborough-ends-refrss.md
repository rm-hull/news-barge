Bus workers in North Yorkshire have ended strike action after accepting an improved pay offer.

400 staff at Scarborough’s Alexander Dennis site in Cayton Low Road, who are members of Unite the union, have secured a 4.5 per cent pay rise backdated to January 2026 and a further 3.5 per cent increase from January 2027.




Sharon Graham, general secretary of Unite, said: "By taking a stand to protect living standards, these workers have achieved a deal that values their skills and protects their conditions."

Dan Stephens, regional officer for Unite, said: "With the dispute now settled, our members are focused on getting back to proudly building world class buses.

"Unite will continue to campaign relentlessly to ensure public transport authorities buy UK-made buses that support skilled unionised jobs like those at Alexander Dennis."
