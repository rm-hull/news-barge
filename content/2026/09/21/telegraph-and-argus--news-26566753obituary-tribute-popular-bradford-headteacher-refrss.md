A FORMER headteacher at Eldwick First School, who was awarded an MBE for services to education, has died, aged 89.

Norma Hall was born and brought up in Blackpool. She met her husband, Les, at Beckett Park teacher training college. They were married for 68 years, 66 of them in Baildon, where Mrs Hall taught biology and maths at West Lane Secondary School.

When schools were reorganised in Bradford, she took the opportunity to move into early years, teaching at Baildon C of E and Nessfield (Keighley) before being appointed Headteacher at Eldwick First School. Always committed to progressive, child-centred learning, she held that post for 16 years and was awarded an MBE for services to education.

Among many skills, Mrs Hall was an accomplished artist and created many works across a range of media, including watercolour, pastel, pencil, clay and embroidery. In retirement, she helped to organise the archives of the Northern Horticultural Society at Harlow Carr before it became part of the RHS.

She leaves her husband, Les Hall, her daughter Karen Jones, son-in-law Philip Jones, two granddaughters, Hannah and Eliza, their husbands Nico and Alexander, and five great grandchildren Elsie, Rupert, Sebastian, Tristan and Caspar.

Mrs Hall’s daughter Karen said: “Her sensitivity, kindness and twinkly sense of humour will be missed by all who knew her.”

A celebration of her life is to be arranged at a future date.
