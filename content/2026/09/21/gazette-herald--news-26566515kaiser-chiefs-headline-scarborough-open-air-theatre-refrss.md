Indie rock band Kaiser Chiefs will headline Scarborough Open Air Theatre next summer.

The Leeds group, famed for their anthemic hits such as Ruby and I Predict A Riot, will return to the seaside venue on Friday, June 11, 2027.

Tickets for the show will go on sale at 9am Friday via ticketmaster.co.uk and scarboroughopenairtheatre.com.

![]()Indie rock band Kaiser Chiefs will headline Scarborough OAT in summer 2027. *(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

This performance marks a return for the band, who last headlined the theatre in 2021.

The group, which consists of Ricky Wilson on vocals, Simon Rix on bass, Andrew 'Whitey' White on guitar, Nick 'Peanut' Baines on keyboards, and Vijay Mistry on drums, has been a staple of the British music scene since their formation in 2000.

The band has released eight original studio albums.

Their debut album, Employment, was nominated for a Mercury Prize and sold more than two million copies.

The follow-up, Yours Truly, Angry Mob, went twice-platinum in the UK alone, and Education Education Education and War entered the UK Album Charts at number one.

With a string of top ten singles, including the number one hit Ruby, the band have also amassed three BRIT Awards and an Ivor Novello for Album of the Year.

They've toured with big names like U2, Foo Fighters, and Green Day, and have sold a total of eight million albums worldwide.

Julian Murray, from Cuffe and Taylor, the venue programmers at Scarborough Open Air Theatre, said: "You can't not love a night with Kaiser Chiefs. They always bring the party and where better to do that than Scarborough.

"This will be a truly momentous show next summer and we can't wait."

The band is the latest addition to the 2027 line-up at the theatre, which will also see performances from Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti, James Morrison, and Simply Red.

For more information, visit the Scarborough Open Air Theatre website.
