A new Danish homeware store is preparing to open its doors in Durham, promising shoppers a taste of Scandinavian style.

Søstrene Grene will open at The Riverwalk shopping centre as the chain expands across the UK.

The store will sell Scandinavian-inspired homewares, kitchen essentials, soft furnishings, craft supplies and seasonal décor.

![]() *(Image: SOSTRENE GRENE)*

Mikkel Grene, group CEO and co-owner of Søstrene Grene, said: "It’s always special to open a Søstrene Grene store in a new city and we’re particularly pleased to be bringing our universe to Durham.

"We hope the new store will become a destination for those looking for something a little different, whether they’re searching for a new craft project, something for the home or simply enjoying an afternoon of browsing.

"We look forward to becoming part of life in Durham and seeing what customers discover."

The store will use Søstrene Grene’s signature layout, inspired by the Danish tradition of hygge.

![]() *(Image: SOSTRENE GRENE)*

Soft lighting, classical music and carefully arranged displays will guide shoppers through the winding store.

Founded in 1973 and based in Aarhus, Denmark, the brand has more than 400 stores in 19 markets and plans to reach 100 in the UK within two years.

The Durham branch will occupy the former YuMe World unit at The Riverwalk, after plans were submitted to take over the premises.

René Jensen, head of retail for Denmark, the UK, and Ireland, said: "Durham is a wonderful addition to our growing presence in the North East, and The Riverwalk is a great home for the new store.

"We’re looking forward to seeing customers explore the winding aisles and discover the many different collections on offer.

![]() *(Image: SOSTRENE GRENE)*

"There’s always a sense of curiosity when you visit Søstrene Grene, and we believe the Durham store will give people plenty of reasons to return."

![]() *(Image: SOSTRENE GRENE)*

Toby Saul, investment director at Clearbell Capital, owner and developer of The Riverwalk, said: "Søstrene Grene is a fantastic addition to The Riverwalk, bringing a distinctive retail experience to Durham and further strengthening the destination’s offer.
