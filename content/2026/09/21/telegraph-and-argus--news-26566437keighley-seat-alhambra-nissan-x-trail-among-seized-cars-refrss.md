Four vehicles were seized in a single day during police operations in Keighley, including a stolen car and others found without insurance.

Yesterday (September 20) officers from the Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team were alerted to a stolen Seat Alhambra leaving the area.

The vehicle had been reported stolen a week earlier by South Yorkshire Police.

With support from West Yorkshire Police in Shipley, officers stopped the car as it passed through Saltaire.

![]()Police on patrol *(Image: WYP)*

A police spokesperson said: "Although the driver was able to provide enough documentation to prove that they appeared to be an innocent third-party purchaser. The vehicle has been seized to allow South Yorkshire Police to conduct further enquiries into the alleged theft."

Later the same day, officers received a report of a Nissan X-Trail seen in suspicious circumstances near Keighley town centre.

The vehicle was found parked nearby and checks revealed it had no insurance, tax, or MOT.

It was also seized.

Throughout the day, officers stopped several other vehicles for speeding.

Two of these were found to be uninsured, bringing the total number of vehicles seized to four by 3pm.
