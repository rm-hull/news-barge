A County Durham country house hotel known for its farming roots and countryside setting has been crowned one of the North East's top luxury retreats.

Headlam Hall Hotel & Rural Retreat, near Darlington, won the Most Authentic Luxury Rural Retreat & Hotel 2026 – North East England title at the LUXlife Hospitality Awards.

Headlam Hall is an independently owned Jacobean country house hotel on the Robinson family’s County Durham farm estate.

![]()Dining area, Headlam Hall Hotel. *(Image: Headlam Hall Hotel)*

![]()Headlam Hall Hotel. *(Image: Headlam Hall Hotel)*

Since buying the property in 1977, the family has turned it into a 38-bedroom retreat with a spa, nine-hole golf course, landscaped gardens and fine dining.

![]()Golf course, Headlam Hall Hotel. *(Image: Headlam Hall Hotel)*

![]()Fine dining, Headlam Hall Hotel. *(Image: Headlam Hall Hotel)*

Despite expanding significantly over the years, the hotel says it remains committed to the values that have shaped the business from the beginning - including personalised customer service, local connections and links to the surrounding countryside.

The hotel sources many kitchen ingredients locally, including organic dairy products from suppliers around 10 miles away, meat from Barnard Castle and Teesdale farms, and produce from its own gardens.

![]()Spa facilities at Headlam Hall Hotel. *(Image: Headlam Hall Hotel)*

![]()Headlam Hall Hotel. *(Image: Headlam Hall Hotel)*

From spa days and rounds of golf to countryside walks and seasonal menus showcasing local produce, guests are encouraged to slow down and make the most of the rural surroundings.

That experience has been enhanced by recent investment in a new spa terrace, hydrotherapy pool and sauna, allowing visitors to enjoy sweeping views of the countryside while relaxing with the feeling of a traditional country house retreat.

![]()Spa facilities at Headlam Hall Hotel. *(Image: Headlam Hall Hotel)*

Thomas Robinson, director at Headlam Hall Hotel, said: "We're delighted to receive this award and especially pleased to be recognised for authenticity because it is something that has always been very important to us.

"Headlam Hall has grown gradually from our family's farming roots, but throughout that time we've tried to protect the character and sense of place that makes it distinctive.

"We want guests to be able to properly switch off when they come to Headlam, whether that's spending time in the gardens, enjoying the countryside or making use of the spa.
