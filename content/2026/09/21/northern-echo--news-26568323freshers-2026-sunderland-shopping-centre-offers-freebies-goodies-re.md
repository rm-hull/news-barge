Students are in for an afternoon of exclusive discounts, live entertainment, and giveaways in a special shopping event.

The Bridges Shopping Centre in Sunderland will host its Big Student Shop on Wednesday, September 23, running from 3pm to 6pm.

The event will feature one-off retailer discounts, a DJ set from Pride Radio, interactive activities, and prize opportunities including multiple "Spin the Wheel" games.

![]() *(Image: Dave Charnley Photography)*

Karen Eve, centre director at the Bridges, said: "It’s always brilliant to welcome new and returning students to the centre.

"We’ve been working with our retailers to line up incredible offers, prizes, and samples across food, fashion, music and beauty - there’s something for everyone at the Bridges."

Students can register in advance to secure an event wristband and entry into a prize draw for a £100 Sunderland Gift Card.

![]() *(Image: Dave Charnley Photography)*

Participating retailers are offering 20 per cent off, including Chopstix, Footasylum, JD Sports, New Look, River Island, Schuh, Subway, and Yours.

Other highlights include free popcorn and candyfloss, beauty consultations at Boots, product-making workshops at Lush, and a tombola at The Perfume Shop.

At Slice, students can enter to win a full-size 24-inch pizza by taking an in-store selfie.

![]()Lush will host product-making workshops *(Image: Dave Charnley Photography)*

Exhibitor stalls will include Sunderland Empire, Out North East, Red Sky Foundation, Sunderland BID, and JD Gyms, giving students the chance to connect with local organisations, collect giveaways, and take part in challenges.

Wristbands can be collected from the check-in desk opposite the O2 store from 2.30pm on the day.

The first 200 students to check in will receive a goodie bag, which includes a voucher for a free Greggs vegan or sausage roll.

Students must show a wristband and valid student ID to redeem offers in participating stores.

To register for the event, visit the official Bridges website.
