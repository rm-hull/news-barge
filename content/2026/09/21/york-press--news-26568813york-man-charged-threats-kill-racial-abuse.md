A MAN in his 40s has been charged after threats to kill in York city centre last week.

North Yorkshire Police says that a 41-year-old man is due in court today (September 21) charged with threatening and racially abusive behaviour in York city centre.

It happened last Friday (September 18), after police received a report of a man abusing members of the public and York BID Street Rangers.

A 41-year-old man was then arrested, and subsequently charged with making threats to kill, a public order offence, and a racially-aggravated public order offence.

Sergeant Jack Milner, of North Yorkshire Police, said: "There is zero tolerance for behaviour like this in our towns and cities, and such incidents are treated with high priority."

He is due to appear at York Magistrates Court today.
