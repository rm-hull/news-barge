Hundreds of dogs and their owners headed to the Museum Gardens for a new event yesterday (Sunday).

The first ever York Dog Show proved a hit, with the park filled with wagging tails and happy families.

Organised by York Museums Trust and the team from York Dog Park, the event was hosted by author and TV star Amanda Owen, of Yorkshire Shepherdess fame.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-dog-show-museum-gardens-sept-2026-1.webp)

Some of Amanda’s children were also on hand to meet everyone.

There were thrilling canine activities taking across the gardens throughout the day, including hay bale racing, the have-a-go dog park, training and agility demonstrations, and lots of dog treats to buy at the stalls.

Best In Show went to Chloe from York and her dog Hunter.

A York Museums Trust spokesperson said: “We welcomed around 1,000 people on Sunday to the Museum Gardens, and just as many, if not more, dogs, to celebrate the first city centre dog show.

“It was a fantastic day, with the crowd enjoying the different competition categories like Best Trick and Cutest Puppy.

“The hay bale racing was also a hit! All the stall holders, Amanda Owen and all of us here at York Museums Trust loved meeting all the dogs, so we hope to do more events like this in the future. Watch this space!”
