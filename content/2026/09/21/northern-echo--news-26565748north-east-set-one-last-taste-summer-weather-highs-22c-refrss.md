North East residents could be digging their summer clothes back out this week, with temperatures set to climb into the low 20s as a muggy spell sweeps across the UK.

After a wet and unsettled start to September, Tuesday (September 22), is expected to bring above-average temperatures, sunshine and humid air, reaching 22C in parts of the North East.

According to the Met Office, the warmest conditions are expected in Darlington, where temperatures are forecast to peak at around 22C during the early afternoon.

However, it may feel even warmer thanks to high humidity levels throughout the day.

The morning is expected to begin with humidity levels close to 96 per cent, creating a notably muggy start before gradually easing through the afternoon.

The mini warm spell comes as summer draws to a close and many people prepare to bring out their autumn and winter jumpers.

While the temperatures will not challenge any September records, they are expected to sit several degrees above what many would typically expect at this stage of the month.

Middlesbrough should reach 21C in similarly humid conditions, with a chance of a brief light shower during the afternoon.

A slightly stronger breeze could also make conditions feel more comfortable.

Further north, Gateshead should be slightly cooler, with temperatures of 20C to 21C and a greater chance of light showers.

Humidity will remain high but should be less oppressive than further south in Teesside and County Durham.

Richmond and wider parts of North Yorkshire should stay largely dry, with temperatures reaching 20C and humidity levels similar to Darlington.

Temperatures across the North East should peak at around 2pm before easing through the evening.

Humidity is expected to rise again after sunset, reaching around 90 per cent in many areas by late evening.

Anyone hoping for one last taste of summer could make the most of Tuesday’s warmer weather before autumn takes hold.
