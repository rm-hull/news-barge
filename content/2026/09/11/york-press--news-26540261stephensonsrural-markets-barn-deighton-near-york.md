## Smallholding near York on the market for £995,000

A view from above of The Barn, near Deighton. *(Image: StephensonsRural)*

A smallholding near York has gone on the market with a guide price of £995,000.

The Barn, located just outside Deighton, is up for sale and comes with a two-bedroom barn conversion and a one-bedroom annex.

Estate agents StephensonsRural says the property has a detached garage block, an outbuilding currently used as a studio, an agricultural building, and 11.5 acres of grass paddocks.

In total, the smallholding spans approximately 12.24 acres.

The 2,154 sq ft barn also excludes loft storage and gallery areas.

A large garden, a fair-weather equestrian arena, and a separate agricultural building with two stable blocks complete the outdoor space.

The barn, under a tile roof with hardwood windows, offers a range of amenities.

The kitchen is spacious and comes with integrated cooking appliances.

  The modern kitchen 

*(Image: StephensonsRural)*

A pantry provides additional storage.

The sitting/dining room has a brick fireplace, a wood-burning stove, and large glazed doors offering garden views.

The master bedroom has a Juliet balcony overlooking the garden, while the second bedroom provides space for freestanding furniture.

The annex, which has been used as a holiday let, offers independent accommodation.

It comes with an open-plan kitchen/living room, a double bedroom with fitted wardrobes, and a bathroom.

  A spacious lounge 

*(Image: StephensonsRural)*

The annex can be accessed via a separate external staircase and entrance, as well as internally.

The property also includes a detached building with four individual garages, located on the gravel drive to the west of the property.

The timber outbuilding, previously used as a dog grooming studio, offers potential for various uses, including a work-from-home space.

The agricultural building, located to the southwest of the house, provides 134.8 sq m of internal space, including two loose boxes.

The land, classified as Grade 2 on the Agricultural Land Classification, comprises four grass paddocks and a fair-weather equestrian arena, extending to a total of 11.5 acres.

The paddocks are partly fenced and suitable for grazing and equestrian use.

Access to the land is available via a track located in the north-eastern corner of the property.

Stephensons Rural is based at York Auction Centre, Murton, York.
