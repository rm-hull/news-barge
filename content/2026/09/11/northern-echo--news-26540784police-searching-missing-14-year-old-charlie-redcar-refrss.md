Police are searching for a missing 14-year-old boy.

Charlie Wilson was last seen in Redcar yesterday morning (Thursday, September 10) wearing a school uniform with a blue hoodie.

Cleveland Police is appealing for information to help find the 14-year-old, who was riding a red pedal bike at the time of his disappearance.

Anyone who thinks they might have seen Charlie, or anyone who has any information, is asked to contact police on 101, quoting reference number SE26182275
