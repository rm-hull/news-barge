Extra winter flights to Spain and Portugal have been added from Teesside International Airport by Ryanair.

The decision follows a surge in passenger demand and will give travellers in the region more options for autumn, the festive season, and early spring getaways.

Ryanair will operate additional flights to Faro on October 28 and 31, and to Alicante on October 28, Christmas Eve, New Year’s Eve, and March 24 next year.

Phil Forster, managing director at Teesside International Airport, said: "It's fantastic to see Ryanair responding to the strong demand we're seeing from our passengers.

"Adding extra services to Alicante and Faro gives people even more flexibility when planning holidays through autumn, Christmas and into next year."

The announcement follows the airport’s busiest winter in 17 years last year and a strong summer.

Airport bosses say the extra flights will meet rising demand for late-season breaks, Christmas escapes and early spring getaways.

The expanded schedule strengthens Teesside’s winter offering, which already includes year-round Ryanair flights to Alicante and Malaga, plus KLM’s daily services to Amsterdam.

Passengers can also book festive trips to Lapland, with extra capacity added for winter 2026/27 after strong demand from families.

Mr Forster said: "With year-round schedules to Malaga and Alicante, KLM offering daily Amsterdam links and TUI package holidays continuing to perform strongly, we're giving people choice, convenience and a high-quality experience from their local airport.

"There is still plenty of work ahead, but it's encouraging to see airlines adding services as more people choose to fly from Teesside."

The airport’s growth is reflected in rising passenger numbers, with Alicante flights up 15 per cent on last year and Malaga routes seeing more than 25 per cent growth.

Tees Valley Mayor Ben Houchen said: "These extra flights are another vote of confidence in Teesside Airport and the growing demand we're seeing across our region.

"Whether it's families looking for some late autumn sunshine, a Christmas break in Spain or an early spring getaway next year, it's brilliant to see more options being made available from our airport."

Mr Houchen said the airport’s growth would help secure its future and support jobs and investment across Teesside, Darlington and Hartlepool.

Teesside International Airport has also been recognised for its accessibility, earning a "very good" rating from the Civil Aviation Authority for the third year in a row.
