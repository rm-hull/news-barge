A Disney-themed purse sold at Primark is being urgently recalled over safety concerns.

The 101 Dalmatians Zip Purse, sold in Primark stores across the UK between March and June 2026, has been found to contain unsafe levels of phthalates.

Phthalates are chemical compounds used to make plastics softer and more flexible, but they are strictly regulated due to health concerns, especially for children.

A Primark spokesperson said: "We are recalling our product 101 Dalmatians Faux Leather Coin Purse (Kimball no. 2595301) due to a safety issue.

"This was sold in Primark stores in the UK between March 2026 and June 2026.

"We have learned the product does not meet our usual high safety standards.

"The product has been found to contain phthalates above permitted levels, and this may cause harm to health and the environment.

"Please stop using the product immediately. Please return the product to any of our Primark stores. It's not necessary to show proof of purchase.

"For more information, please email [help.uk@help.primark.com.](mailto:help.uk@help.primark.com.)"

Phthalates are known to pose serious health risks, particularly to children, as they can potentially damage the reproductive system.

The recall was issued after the product was found to breach the REACH Enforcement Regulations 2008 and the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

A spokesperson for the Office for Product Safety & Standards said: "The product presents a serious chemical risk because it contains phthalates above permitted levels.

"Phthalates may harm the health of children, possibly causing damage to the reproductive system.

"The product doesn't meet the requirements of the REACH Enforcement Regulations 2008 or the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

"The product has been recalled from end users by Primark.

"Owners should stop using the product immediately and keep it away from children."

Primark has advised all customers to stop using the purse immediately and keep it out of reach of children.
