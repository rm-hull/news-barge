**HARROGATE**— A comprehensive strategic plan designed to upgrade public transport networks across York and North Yorkshire has received official cabinet approval at a Combined Authority meeting today.

The newly approved **Bus Service Improvement Plan (BSIP)** outlines the authority’s long-term framework for connecting urban hubs with rural and coastal communities. According to executive papers, the overarching strategy focuses heavily on improving network reliability, accelerating transitions to green vehicle fleets, and standardizing regional fare structures.

## A Strategy Driven by Key Priorities

Rather than rolling out immediate infrastructure changes, the newly adopted framework establishes a baseline for the authority to explore several targeted transport initiatives over the coming financial years:

- **Rural and Evening Connectivity:** Undertaking targeted reviews to potentially enhance Sunday schedules and late-evening services across isolated districts.
- **Intermodal Integration:** Developing frameworks to create seamless ticketing linkages between regional bus networks and existing rail hubs.
- **Ticketing Reform:** Exploring the feasibility of introducing multi-day passenger passes and dedicated visitor ticketing models to support the regional tourism economy.

The infrastructure strategy builds directly upon ongoing localized joint work conducted alongside North Yorkshire Council and the City of York Council, which has previously delivered real-time information display upgrades and the introduction of a flat £1 fare structure for passengers under the age of 19.

## Executive and Regional Endorsement

The policy document marks a key statutory milestone for the authority’s broader economic and environmental targets, aiming to link public transport directly to local high street resilience.


**Metro Mayor David Skaith endorsed the approval, stating:**

This approved plan builds on our work with local councils and operators to deliver the improvements we all deserve. By making public transport work for all our rural and urban communities, we are strengthening our high streets and ensuring our county stays connected.
