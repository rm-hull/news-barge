# Woman, 65, dies after car crashes into wall

## A 65-year-old woman has died after the car she was driving crashed into a wall in Huddersfield.

At 7:45am on Thursday (Sept 10), police responded to reports of a crash in Huddersfield.

It was reported a black Kia Sportage crashed into a wall at the junction of Heaton Road and Church Street in Paddock.

The driver, a woman aged 65, was pronounced dead at the scene.

![junction of Heaton Road and Church Street in Paddock](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/11/10/16/The-junction-of-Heaton-Road-and-Church-Street-in-Paddock.png?trim=0,69,0,69&width=640&quality=65)



Police launched an appeal for any witnesses or anyone with information to come forward.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Officers from the Major Collision Enquiry Team are carrying out enquiries. They are keen to speak to anyone who saw or who has dash-cam of the collision or the moments leading up to the collision.
