# Teenager charged after boy, 13, stabbed in Leeds during Bank Holiday weekend

## A teenager has been charged after a 13-year-old boy was stabbed in Leeds during the Bank Holiday weekend.

The 16-year-old boy, who can’t be named for legal reasons, has been charged with wounding with intent, possession of a knife or bladed article in a public place and possession of a Class B drug.

The 13-year-old received a non-life threatening stab wound to the abdomen during the incident.

The teenager appeared at Leeds Magistrates’ Court yesterday where he was remanded in custody ahead of a further hearing on 16 September.

The charges relate to an incident on Shepherd’s Lane in Leeds.
