A man has been arrested after a police incident prompted the closure of part of a motorway.

The M621 was shut in both directions in the early hours of Friday morning between Junction 4 for the A61 Hunslet Distributor and Junction 2 for the A643 Elland Road, causing delays for drivers travelling through Leeds.

National Highways said emergency services were at the scene while the road was closed, with motorists facing congestion on approaches to the affected stretch.

## Arrest made

West Yorkshire Police has now confirmed a man was arrested in connection with the incident.

A spokesperson for the force said a man was arrested on suspicion of public nuisance and remains in police custody.

## Delays cleared after road reopened

The closure was first reported at around 3.32am, with traffic monitoring service Inrix warning of disruption in both directions.

The road reopened before the morning rush hour, although some delays continued on the eastbound carriageway for a short time afterwards.

Traffic has since returned to normal.

## Police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "A man was arrested on suspicion of public nuisance and remains in custody."
