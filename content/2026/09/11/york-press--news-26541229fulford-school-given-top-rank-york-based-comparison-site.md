## Obstruction in key York road being prepared to be cleared

Traffic moved slowly through temporary traffic lights set up around the broken-down road roller this morning (Friday, September 11) *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

A BROKEN-DOWN road roller blocking a main road in York is being prepared to be moved this morning.

As reported by The Press earlier today (Friday, September 11), the eastbound lane of the A1079 before the village of Dunnington is blocked by the vehicle with temporary traffic lights directing traffic around it.

Queues of commuting traffic built back to Common Road in Dunnington on the way into York this morning.

Contractors on site told us that the road roller broke down overnight and crews had managed to get the engine started again this morning and were preparing to move it a very short distance into a layby on the opposite side of the road.

  The road roller being prepared to be moved to a nearby layby before the road is cleaned and opened for traffic this afternoon (Friday, September 11) 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

  


  Temporary lights were taking traffic round the obstruction on the A1079 this morning (Friday, September 11) 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

The recently re-laid road surface underneath the roller will need cleaning before the temporary traffic lights can be removed.

Contractors on site suggested to The Press that the repair work would be complete and the temporary traffic lights removed before today's school-run, if not sooner.

Overnight resurfacing work is taking place between York Road in Dunnington along a three-mile stretch of the A1079 up to the village of Kexby, which is scheduled to close between 8pm and 6am overnight until Saturday, September 19.
