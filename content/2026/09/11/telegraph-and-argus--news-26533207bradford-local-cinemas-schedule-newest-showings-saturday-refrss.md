FROM superhero action to family favourites, Bradford has a packed schedule of cinema screenings to enjoy this Saturday.

At the Bradford Odeon, Practical Magic 2 sees the Owens family of witches confront dark secrets in a bid to break a curse, with showings from 11.10am through to 11.10pm.

Pressure follows General Eisenhower and Captain Stagg in the tense days before D-Day, screening at 12.30pm, 3pm, 5.30pm and 8pm.

Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey offers a 'mythic action epic' shot in IMAX, with tickets from £4 and showtimes at 11.50am, 3.40pm, 5.30pm, and 7.20pm.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day finds Peter Parker fighting crime in a world that doesn’t remember him, with screenings from 12.50pm to 11pm.

Hoppers, an ODEON Kids pick, brings 'BIG screen family adventures' for just £1 at 10.10am. Shaun the Sheep: The Beast of Mossy Bottom offers Halloween fun for all ages, with a free headband for early birds and showtimes from 10am to 5pm.

Oasis: Don’t Look Back in Anger charts the Gallagher brothers’ reunion tour, screening at 12pm, 2.50pm, 5.40pm, 8.30pm and 11.20pm on the iSense screen with tickets from £17.50.

Runner is a thriller about a courier racing against time, on at 4pm, 6.30pm, 9pm and 11.25pm. Insidious: Out of the Further brings supernatural chills at 9.40pm and 11.15pm.

Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie is perfect for little ones, with £4 tickets and morning shows at 10am and 10.30am.

Fall 2 offers high-altitude suspense at 6.20pm, 8.50pm and 11.05pm. Raiders of the Lost Ark (45th Anniversary) is back for fans at 2.50pm.

Awarapan 2, Bethlehem Kudumba Unit, Haiwaan, Minions & Monsters, Mirzapur: The Movie, Moana, Sardar 2, Singh vs Kaur 2, The Gruffalo + The Gruffalo’s Child and Toy Story 5 round out a diverse line-up with various showtimes and prices.

The Light Bradford also has a great selection. Spider-Man: Brand New Day, The Odyssey, and The Dog Stars offer action and drama from 1.15pm into the evening.

Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie, Minions & Monsters, Toy Story 5 and Moana provide family-friendly fun from 10am.

Insidious: Out of the Further and Pressure are on in the evening, while Don’t Look Back In Anger screens at 8.15pm. Practical Magic 2, Shaun the Sheep, Raiders of the Lost Ark, The Gruffalo, The Boxtrolls, Laputa: Castle in the Sky and Runner complete the day’s listings with showtimes throughout.

For further information visit the venue website.
