BUILDINGS across the Bradford district are preparing to throw open their doors as part of Heritage Open Days.

Taking place from September 11 to 20, the national festival will see thousands of events held across the country, with more than 30 free activities and events taking place across the Bradford district.

Bradford Council-run museums will once again be taking part in the festival, with special events planned at Cliffe Castle Museum in Keighley and Bradford Industrial Museum.

At Cliffe Castle Museum on Sunday (September 13) between 11am–3pm, visitors can explore the district’s sporting and leisure heritage through Everyday Histories: Sports and Pastimes, uncovering stories of local clubs, games, hobbies and pastimes that have brought communities together over generations.

Families can enjoy hands-on activities while discovering how people spent their free time in years gone by.

Meanwhile, Bradford Industrial Museum will host a special celebration of traditional skills, craftsmanship and making on Sunday September 20 from 11am-3pm.

Inspired by the museum’s Made by Many Hands exhibition, the event will showcase heritage crafts and the people who continue to keep these important traditions alive today.

Darley Street Market will also be hosting a programme of activities, such as traditional heritage games such as roll‑a‑coin, ball‑in‑a‑bucket and table quoits all led by a Victorian carnival host.

There will also be live music and an appearance by Florence White, former mill worker and forgotten Bradford trailblazer who fought tirelessly for justice for ‘spinsters’ forced to work until 70.

Cycling heritage will also be celebrated at Darley Street Market on September 20, where visitors can watch a film exploring the history of bicycle making at Pennine Cycles. The event will include a display of historic bicycles and archive photographs, family craft activities, and the chance to meet local cycling organisations, including Capital of Cycling, cycling clubs and the Beryl Bikes team.

Also at Darley Street Market as part of Heritage Open Days, visitors can enjoy a special archive film celebrating Bradford’s retail and trading heritage on the market’s big screen. Screenings will take place at 10am, 2pm and 4pm each day from 20 to 23 September. The screening is a partnership between Bradford’s Heritage Action Zone (HAZ) programme and the Yorkshire and North East Film Archive and forms part of a wider programme of heritage activities and leads into the next HAZ workshop, ‘Repointing with Lime Mortar’, delivered by Womersley’s Ltd in the courtyard at Darley Street Market on September 23.

Councillor Ian Walker, Bradford Council's Executive Member for Neighbourhoods and Communities, said: “The Heritage Open Days festival is a fantastic opportunity for people to discover the remarkable history, culture and heritage that can be found right across the Bradford district.

“There are so many organisations taking part in the district, there is something for everyone to enjoy. The festival allows people to explore places they may never have visited before, learn more about the stories that have shaped our communities and celebrate the people, traditions and cultures that continue to make our district unique.”

For information about all Heritage Open Days events taking place across the Bradford district, visit www.heritageopendays.org.uk or www.visitbradford.com.
