A SKINCARE brand is set to open its first retail space this weekend.

TALLOW, the independent British brand founded by Nuzhat Rashid, will launch at Darley Street Market in Bradford on Saturday, September 12, with a special event from 11am.

The launch will include desserts, cake pops, entertainment from the Mirror Man, and goody bags for the first 50 customers from 9am.

TALLOW focuses on ethical, handcrafted, and personalised skincare, using halal, grass-fed tallow as a key ingredient in its formulations.

Ms Rashid said: "I created TALLOW because I wanted skincare to feel more personal and purposeful.

"My background has always been in customer care, so listening to people and understanding what they actually need has naturally become a huge part of the brand.

"Seeing customers share their skin journeys and the difference our products have made for them has been incredibly rewarding.

"Opening our first physical space in Bradford is an exciting next step, but as we grow, I never want us to lose the personal care and attention TALLOW was built on.

"We’re really hoping to make the day feel welcoming, exciting and a lovely introduction to TALLOW for people visiting Darley Street Market."

The brand was developed with input from a pharmacy professional with more than 20 years of experience and a passion for natural apothecary.

It has grown through customer recommendations and repeat purchases since launching online.

TALLOW offers tailored skincare for sensitive, dry, acne-prone, and eczema-prone skin, as well as products for pigmentation, scarring, mature skin, and general body care.

The product range recently expanded to include the Mummy & Baby collection, featuring gentle skincare suitable from birth and nourishing body care for pregnancy and beyond.

The new shop will be located on the Lifestyle Level of Darley Street Market, open Monday to Saturday from 9am to 5.30pm.

Councillor Ian Walker, Bradford Council’s executive member for neighbourhoods and communities, said: "I am delighted to welcome TALLOW to Darley Street Market and wish Nuzhat all the very best in her new venture."

As part of the launch, the brand is offering £5 off all products for one day only.

TALLOW’s approach to skincare goes beyond products, with a focus on community and customer care through social media, live interaction, and direct conversations.

This has helped the brand build a loyal following and support customers in finding the right solutions for their individual skin needs.
