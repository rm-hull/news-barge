A coastal Yorkshire town has been named one of the prettiest towns to visit in the UK.

The Independent has revealed its list of the prettiest towns and villages you should visit around the UK.

The list features places including tiny quintessential English towns such as Castle Combe and Cockington to seaside escapes like Port Isaac in Cornwall.

The Yorkshire coastal town of Whitby has made it onto the list thanks to its “pristine sands” and “gothic” abbey you can visit.


## What is Whitby like?

On the town, The Independent said: “Whitby’s quirky streets, lively harbour and moorlands are full of character and charm.

“Pristine sands, the North York Moors National Park and the gothic Whitby Abbey on the East Cliff create a dramatic Yorkshire landscape that begs to be discovered – the seaside town was even a setting for Bram Stoker’s Dracula.

“In summer, Whitby’s golden beaches are a hit with holidaymakers, with several offering the quintessential British seaside experience – think coloured huts, ice cream vendors and salted fish and chips.

“Take a refreshing dip in the sea or catch your breath after taking on the 199 Whitby Abbey steps, which were originally used as a test of Christian faith.”

Whitby is loved by locals and tourists with it being described as one of the “best places to go in search of a little seaside fun”.

Whitby features several different sandy beaches that are ideal for long walks or building sandcastles on, with lots of colourful beach huts brightening up the area.


Even if you are not a fan of the beach, there is still a lot to do in the town such as visiting one of its many museums from the Captain Cook Memorial Museum to the Whitby Museum and Pannett Art Gallery.

The Whitby Abbey is also a “must visit” spot and offers “fantastic views” that overlook the rest of the town.

The gothic ruins even helped inspire Bram Stoker to write his famous novel Dracula and made the town the host of the Goth Weekends that happen twice a year.

You are spoilt for choice when it comes to food in Whitby with it being home to a range of pubs, cafés, and bars including the Quayside, an award winning fish and chip restaurant, and the Velveteen French Café Bar and Brasserie.

If you want to extend your stay in the area, the publication recommends booking The Horngarth apartments, which is just a short walk away from the train station and Whitby’s gardens.
