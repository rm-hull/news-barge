There have been delays on key roads near York this morning (Friday, September 11).

Traffic has been slow on the westbound A64 at Flaxton Moor where delays reached 15 minutes before easing.

The AA said average speeds were 15 mph at 9.40am.

Delays were recorded between Barton Hill Crossroads and Towthorpe Moor Lane.

It comes as there were also delays on the A1079, overnight resurfacing is taking place from York Road in Dunnington along a three-mile stretch to Kexby. A road roller was behind cones on the eastbound lane near the layby by The Scrap Box fish and chip shop.

Temporary traffic lights were controlling vehicles, while York-bound traffic queued back to Dunnington village before the morning rush hour.

The A1079 will close for scheduled resurfacing works.
