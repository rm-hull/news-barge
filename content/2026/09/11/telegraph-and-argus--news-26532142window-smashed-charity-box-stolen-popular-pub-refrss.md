A BRADFORD pub owner has spoken of his frustration after a charity box was stolen during a burglary.

The incident happened at The Watchmaker in South Square, Thornton, last month where a window was also smashed.

Gareth Abraham, who runs the pub with his wife Amy, said the money had been collected as part of fundraising efforts linked to Alzheimer's Society.

He said CCTV footage showed a man smashing a window before taking the collection tin and leaving the area.

West Yorkshire Police says it is investigating the reported burglary.


## Charity fundraiser targeted

Mr Abraham said: "There was probably about £200 in the tin because Amy was doing a challenge for Alzheimer's and we'd been collecting money for that.

He added: "It's not even the money, it's the fact it's a charity box. It just feels wrong."

  Damage to a window at The Watchmaker in Thornton following the burglary. 

*(Image: The Watchmakers)*

## Frustration over police response

Mr Abraham said he was initially informed the case had been closed despite clear CCTV footage being available.

However, after raising concerns, he said he was later told the investigation had been reopened and that officers had identified a possible suspect.

He said: "We were told the case had been closed, which was the first we'd heard of it.

"We were frustrated because the CCTV is absolutely clear.

"After speaking to police again, another officer came forward and said he recognised the person and wanted me to give a statement."

  A brick believed to have been used during the burglary at The Watchmaker in Thornton. 

*(Image: The Watchmakers)*

## 'It's the aftermath'

Although the premises reopened shortly after the break-in, Mr Abraham said dealing with the damage created extra work for staff.

He said: "It's the aftermath of it all.

"The glass has to be cleaned up and cleared away so it doesn't get into drinks, then you've got to get the window repaired."

## A village landmark

Forming part of South Square in Thornton village, The Watchmaker occupies a prominent spot among the historic buildings.

The venue takes its name from the building's former use as a watchmaker's workshop.

Documents, held by the Thornton Antiquarian Society, show the premises were occupied by a watchmaker in 1916.

Amy and Gareth Abraham took on the venue in November 2019 after previously running pop-up bars at community events including Thornton Gala and Saltaire Festival.

## Investigation ongoing

West Yorkshire Police confirmed enquiries are continuing.

A spokesperson said: "Police are investigating a report of a burglary at licensed premises in Thornton on Tuesday 11 August.

"Enquiries are continuing."

Anyone with information that could assist the investigation can contact West Yorkshire Police via 101 or the force's Live Chat service, quoting the relevant crime reference number.
