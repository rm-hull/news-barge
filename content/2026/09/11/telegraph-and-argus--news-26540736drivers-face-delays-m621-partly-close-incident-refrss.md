A section of the M621 was closed in both directions during the early hours of Friday morning following a police-led incident.

National Highways said the motorway was shut between the M62 and Junction 2A near Leeds, with emergency services remaining at the scene.

Traffic monitoring service Inrix reported the closure affected the M621 between Junction 4 for the A61 Hunslet Distributor and Junction 2 for the A643 Elland Road from around 3.32am.

## Congestion built on approaches

The incident led to delays on both approaches to the motorway.

National Highways reported delays of around 10 minutes while the closure was in place, with congestion affecting routes around Leeds.

Earlier traffic reports indicated queues back to Junction 1 at Beeston on the eastbound carriageway and Junction 6 at Belle Isle Road westbound.

## Eastbound delays remain

The westbound carriageway has since reopened.

However, according to the latest Inrix update, traffic remains slow on the eastbound carriageway between Junction 2 (Elland Road) and Junction 4 (Hunslet Distributor) following an earlier crash.

The latest traffic alert remains in place until approximately 6.28am.

## Police approached for comment

West Yorkshire Police has been approached for further information about the incident.

Drivers travelling through the area are advised to allow extra time for their journeys while delays continue.
