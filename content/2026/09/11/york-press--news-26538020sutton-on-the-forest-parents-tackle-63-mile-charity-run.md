Four parents are set to tackle a challenging 63-mile run to raise funds for a primary school classroom.

On September 19, the group will participate in the Hardmoors 60 Relay, covering some of Yorkshire's most difficult trails.

The parents aim to raise funds for a new Year 5/6 classroom at Sutton-on-the-Forest Primary School.

The initiative is expected to further support the school, offering additional opportunities and resources to its pupils.

As integral members of the local community, the group expressed a strong desire to make a "real difference" to the school, while also embracing a challenge that is outside their comfort zones.

The demanding and potentially rewarding task will see them navigate the tough Yorkshire terrain over the span of the day.
