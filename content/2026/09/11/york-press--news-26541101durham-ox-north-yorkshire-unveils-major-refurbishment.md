## Top North Yorkshire pub unveils refurbishment backed by £580,000 loan

The exterior of the historic Durham Ox at Crayke. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A North Yorkshire country inn has unveiled a major refurbishment.

The Durham Ox in Crayke, near Easingwold, has invested more than £500,000 in upgrades to enhance its guest experience and support long-term growth.

The inn, which dates back 300 years and has been owned by the Ibbotson family since 1999, is well known for its pub, restaurant, and accommodation, as well as for hosting weddings and events.

Mike Ibbotson, owner of The Durham Ox, said: "We’ve always wanted The Durham Ox to be a proper village inn, somewhere you can eat, drink and sleep, and to keep improving what we offer without losing the character of the place.

"The changes we’ve made are about making the experience better for guests and more efficient for the team, especially at busy times.

  The beer garden of the Durham Ox at Crayke. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

"Lloyds has supported us through a long-standing relationship, and that backing has helped us invest for the long term."

Recent improvements include a new wine cellar, welcome desk, hot-desk office space, and upgraded table-management facilities.

The upgrades aim to streamline guest arrivals, improve team efficiency during peak periods, and increase capacity for growth while preserving the inn’s traditional charm.

The refurbishment was supported by a £580,000 commercial funding package from Lloyds, which also enabled the business to refinance existing borrowing and invest further in the property.

The Durham Ox recently expanded its accommodation to nine rooms with the addition of two luxury pool suites.

Complete with private terraces, minibars, outdoor pools and sweeping views across the North Yorkshire countryside, the suites have strengthened The Durham Ox’s appeal as a staycation destination and given wedding and celebration guests more opportunity to stay on site.

The inn currently employs around 50 people and is recognised for its historic interiors, including flagstone floors, exposed beams, and oak panelling.

It has received numerous accolades, including a listing in the Michelin Guide, a ranking of 51 in the Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs 2024, and five-star status from VisitEngland.

Alexandra King, relationship manager at Lloyds, said: "The Durham Ox has built a brilliant reputation over many years by focusing on quality and investing carefully in the guest experience.

  One of the luxurious bedrooms at the Durham Ox. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

"We’re proud to support Mike, Sasha, and the team strengthen the business and continue investing in a destination that plays an important role in the local visitor economy."

Looking ahead, the business is exploring further development opportunities as part of its long-term growth strategy.

The Durham Ox forecasts turnover of £3 million in two years, as it continues to enhance the guest experience, expand its offering, and support the team.
