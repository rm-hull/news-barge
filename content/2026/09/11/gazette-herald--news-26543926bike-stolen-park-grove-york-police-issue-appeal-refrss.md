*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A bike has been stolen in York.

North Yorkshire Police has issued an image of a person officers want to speak to after the theft in Park Grove, the Groves, on August 10.

The force on Friday (September 11) issued the photograph, saying the person pictured "may be able to assist with our enquiries".

"As part of our investigation, we would like to identify the person pictured as they may have information that could help us progress our enquiries," a police spokesperson said.

Anyone with information is asked to email [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260155447 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
