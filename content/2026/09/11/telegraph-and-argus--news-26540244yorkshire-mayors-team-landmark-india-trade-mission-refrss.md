YORKSHIRE is preparing for its first-ever joint trade mission to India.

In a show of regional unity, the three mayors will travel together this October in a collective push to secure new jobs, exports, and investment for the region.

Tracy Brabin, Mayor of West Yorkshire, said: "Our ties with India run deep, through our businesses, our universities, our communities and our cricket clubs.

"It’s a friendship that means a great deal to our region, and it’s one we’re proud to keep building.

"I’ve seen first-hand what these trade missions deliver – a thousand jobs in Leeds off the back of a meeting in Bangalore, £7 million of inward investment after a visit to Mumbai, and local firms striking up trade deals in one of the World’s fastest-growing economies."

The 'White Rose' delegation will represent Yorkshire’s businesses, universities, and creative institutions, starting in New Delhi before continuing to Hyderabad and Mumbai.
