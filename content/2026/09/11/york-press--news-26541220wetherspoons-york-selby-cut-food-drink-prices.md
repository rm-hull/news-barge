Pub-goers in York and North Yorkshire are set to enjoy cheaper pints and meals for a day this month.

Three Wetherspoons pubs; The Punch Bowl in Blossom Street and The Postern Gate in Piccadilly in York, will join The Giant Bellflower in Gowthorpe, Selby, in supporting Tax Equality Day on Thursday (September 17).

All food and drink sold by the pubs will be reduced by 7.5 per cent.

  


Nicola Hill, manager of The Punch Bowl, said: "We are proud to support the campaign to reduce the level of VAT within the hospitality industry.

"Customers coming to the pub on Thursday 17 September will find the price of their food and drink is lower than normal.

"We are keen to highlight the amount customers could save if VAT in pubs was lowered permanently."

Currently, pubs pay 20 per cent VAT on all food and drink, while supermarkets benefit from a zero VAT rate on food and can use that saving to sell alcohol at lower prices, a spokesperson for Wetherspoons said.

Nicola added: "A reduction in the level of VAT on a long-term basis will generate growth and create jobs in the important hospitality sector and help the high street, of which pubs are an intergral part."
