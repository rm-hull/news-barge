## ‘Don’t blame pubs for lack of public loos’, council told amid 3am licensing row

 *(Image: Supplied)*

A NIGHTCLUB’S bid to extend its opening hours until 3am has sparked a debate about the availability of public toilets in a North Yorkshire town.

Fusion, near Scarborough town centre, wants to extend its opening hours until 3am on Friday and Saturday, as well as 2am from Sunday to Thursday.

Concerns about antisocial behaviour have been raised by neighbouring residents and council officers who have been dealing with complaints since the start of the year.

One objection to the plan raised concerns about public urination; however, locals have also highlighted the absence of toilets in the town centre, which has been a long-running issue for residents and visitors.

  94 100 St Thomas Street, Scarborough. Google Maps


One local said there was “a distinct lack of public conveniences these days”, while another added: “Don’t blame this pub for the mismanagement of our council.”

At a meeting of Scarborough Town Council earlier this year, locals warned that a lack of toilet facilities in the town centre could be putting people off visiting the high street and negatively impacting local businesses.

The premises licence application submitted by JPH Venues Ltd would also allow the bar at 94-100 St Thomas Street to sell alcohol past 2am. Currently, the business can sell alcohol until midnight and until 1am on Saturday.

An objection, submitted by a neighbour ahead of a council meeting to decide on the plans next week, states: “There have been fights outside of my house away from Fusion, and people have been pissing in the street.

“There must have been three or four call-outs from the police to Fusion.

“I don’t believe Fusion should have its licenses extended. It is disturbing my health and sleep, and my mental health isn’t great.”

The bar, which opened in November last year, describes itself as a “new intimate late-night venue” on its social media page.

The playing of recorded music could also be extended as part of the application and would match the new proposed opening hours, if the application is approved.

Readers had their say on the proposed new opening hours and the lack of public facilities in the comments section of The Press website and on Facebook.

Dean0n0 commented: “Assuming the nightclub has toilets, surely it gives plenty of time to urinate there.”

Sandra said: “The venue has toilets for customers to use, shame the same can’t be said for the rest of the town.”

Leelaa said: “People used to travel here for a good night out, with multiple nightclubs to choose from. Now we have to travel out of Scarborough for a decent night out and a nightclub.”

Matt said: “Who is scared that someone might urinate in an alleyway? Strange phobia that one.”

Carol noted: “Any excuse; it’s like they don’t want a nightclub in Scarborough. Glad I was a teenager in the seventies. Plenty to choose from in those days.

“There’s also a distinct lack of public conveniences these days.”

Jerry added: “Urination is a problem any time, day or night. We don’t have any public toilets; I have not just seen people pee in the street, never mind alleyways […], so don’t blame this pub for the mismanagement of our council.

“Because if they aren’t bothered about it throughout the day, then why does 3am bother them?”

A meeting of North Yorkshire Council’s licensing sub-committee will discuss the proposals at a meeting on Wednesday, September 16.

Following a negotiation between the licence holder and North Yorkshire Police, additional conditions have been agreed.

The conditions include the installation of a colour CCTV system, a requirement to have at least two security door staff on Fridays, at weekends and bank holidays, as well as participation in local crime reduction initiatives.

The licensing committee can approve, reject, or modify the proposed application and members of the public are invited to attend the meeting at Scarborough Town Hall.
