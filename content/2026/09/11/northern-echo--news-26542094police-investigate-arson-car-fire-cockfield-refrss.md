A car was totally burnt out after an arson attack in a quiet County Durham village.

The car, which has been left unrecognisable after the blaze, was set alight shortly after 9pm on Thursday (September 10).

Police and fire crews rushed to Prospect Square in Cockfield and spent about an hour-and-a-half at the scene.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

The blaze is being treated as arson, Durham Police said.

Pictures show the car completely burnt out, reduced to a scorched metal shell.

Debris and ash has been left strewn across the road while.

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

   

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A spokesperson for County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service said: "At 9.09pm we received a report of a vehicle on fire which had spread to a neighbouring property close to Front Street, Cockfield.

"Two crews from Bishop Auckland fire station attended and firefighters wearing breathing apparatus used hose reels to extinguish the fire. The last crew left the scene at 10.37pm."

A Durham Police spokesperson said enquiries were continuing.
