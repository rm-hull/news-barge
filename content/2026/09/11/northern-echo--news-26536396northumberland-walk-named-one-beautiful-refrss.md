A walk in Northumberland has been named as one of the most beautiful one-day walks in the UK.

Love Exploring has released a list of the best one-day walks around the UK featuring walks in the New Forest and the Peak District.

The Sycamore Gap walk made it onto the list thanks to its “great scenery” and “historic sites”.

The route takes a couple of hours to complete and offers great views of the surrounding countryside.


## What is the Sycamore Gap walk like?

On the walk, Love Exploring said: “The five-mile (8km) route which takes in the World Heritage-recognised Hadrian’s Wall offers a double whammy of great scenery and historic sites.

“You’ll begin at Housesteads visitor centre, passing by Housesteads Roman Fort and following the wall up to the storied Sycamore Gap.

“Although the popular tree that once stood here was sadly illegally felled in 2023, the dramatic dip is still a beautiful spot.

“Pause to take a photograph before crossing over the gap, then follow the Roman Military Way all the way back to Housesteads.”

The Sycamore Gap walk takes around two hours to complete and is described as being moderately difficult.

The walk will take you past two Roman castles and allow you to see parts of Hadrian’s Wall.

You will walk past the famous Sycamore Gap tree that was cut down in 2023, but its stump remains and might grow back in the future.

During the walk, there is also a “great view point” of Steel Rig.

The trail is described as being “great for those looking to get out into nature or those interested in historical sites”.


## What do hikers say about the Sycamore Gap walk?

The route has a rating of 4.6 out of 5 on AllTrails, with 140 hikers leaving a review after completing the walk.

One person said: “Lovely walk along and besides Hadrian’s Wall.

“The crags made the walk a little difficult especially with the rain and wind.

“The memorial for the Sycamore tree was lovely to see and it was wonderful to read that it has shoots.

“The final walk along the wall was a highlight.”
