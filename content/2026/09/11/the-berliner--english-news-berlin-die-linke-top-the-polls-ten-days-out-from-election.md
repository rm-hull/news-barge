Ten days before the parliamentary elections in Berlin, Die Linke overtakes the CDU. This comes from the current BerlinTrend by infratest dimap on behalf of ARD.

According to the survey, Die Linke gained two percentage points and is now ahead at 21%. The CDU is behind with 20%. The AfD follows with 18%. Die Grünen have lost two percentage points and land at 16%. The SPD gained a point and is at 12%. BSW stands at 4%.

The survey interviewed 1,527 people between September 7 and September 9.

Recent investigations against the SPD’s lead candidate Steffen Krach were announced late on Wednesday afternoon and therefore largely had no impact on the results.
