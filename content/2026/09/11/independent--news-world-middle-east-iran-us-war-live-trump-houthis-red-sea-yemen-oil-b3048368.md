Satellite imagery showed smoke in the area of a crucial oil pipeline in Saudi Arabia, raising fears of another heavy attack by the Houthis on energy infrastructure.

Images verified by Reuters showed smoke in the vicinity of the East-West oil pipeline, as the Houthis captured a strategic island of Perim in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. The waterway in the Red Sea is key to global oil shipping.

Earlier, two sources from the Saudi-backed, internationally recognised Yemeni government said its forces had withdrawn from Perim and that the Houthis had also taken the mainland Red Sea coastal town of Dhubab, which faces the island.

The developments threaten to bring further chaos for energy exports. The price of oil touched $108 a barrel on Friday for the first time since May after the Houthis captured Yemen’s strategic port city of Mokha.

Saudi Arabia, the world's largest oil exporter, has relied on the Red Sea route since the Iran conflict effectively closed Hormuz, through which a fifth of global oil used to flow.

Mokha is around 50 miles from Bab el-Mandeb, a geopolitical chokepoint connecting the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden and through which about 12 per cent of the world’s goods are typically shipped.

## Recap: UK economy grows despite Iran war toll

Britain's economy grew by a much stronger than expected 0.4% in July, extending the strong performance seen in the first half of the year despite headwinds from the US-Iran war, figures from the Office for National Statistics showed on Friday.

Compared with a year earlier, output in July was 1.6% higher, the fastest annual rate since February 2025 and above economists' 1.2% forecast.

Growth in the three months to July held at the 0.4% recorded in the three months to June, compared with economists' median forecast for a slowdown to 0.3%.

Britain, like other European economies, has taken less of an immediate hit to growth from the US-Iran war than economists had feared at the outset.

But the conflict this week pushed oil prices above $105 a barrel with knock-on impacts on government borrowing costs, and the BoE expects inflation to rise to around 3.2% later this year.

## Smoke seen in satellite images of crucial Saudi oil pipeline

Satellite imagery showed smoke on Thursday in the vicinity of Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline.

There was no confirmation from Saudi Arabia of an incident in the area, and the Saudi government media office and state oil giant Aramco did not immediately respond to requests for comment on the smoke visible in the imagery verified by Reuters.

The East-West Pipeline has become vital to Saudi Arabia since the outbreak of the war as state oil giant Aramco used it to ramp up crude flows from its eastern coast to the Red Sea to its west, diverting crude away from the Strait of Hormuz after Iran effectively blockaded it.

## Trump rejected Saudi calls for support against the Houthis - report

Donald Trump rejected pleas from allied Saudi Arabia for support in tackling the Yemeni Houthi rebels, according to a report.

Citing two US officials, Axios reported that Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman called president Trump twice on Thursday urging him to launch strikes against the Houthis.

But Trump reportedly rejected the request and has not intervened in the escalating conflict. US officials said the administration does not plan to take direct military action at this time.

The US is still supporting Saudi Arabia in Yemen, but has decided to stop short of direct military engagement.

## What would the Houthis gain from controlling Perim Island?

Four Yemeni government sources told Reuters this morning that the Houthi rebels have reached the strategic Perim Island at the mouth of the Bab el-Mandeb Strait.

That comes after weeks of attacks on Saudi shipping through the Strait, limiting access into the Red Sea and out to the ocean.

If the Houthis gain full control of the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, on the opposite side of the Arabian Peninsula from the Strait of Hormuz, it could give their backer Iran a critical advantage in the war with the US, reducing supplies through a second major transit corridor and sending oil prices surging.

## Iran-backed Houthis reach Yemen's strategic Perim Island - report

Iran-aligned Houthi militants reached Yemen's strategic Perim Island at the mouth of the Bab el-Mandeb Strait early on Friday after government forces withdrew, four Yemeni government sources told Reuters.

The move marked the group's most significant advance yet towards control of the key shipping waterway.
