Bradford Bulls have welcomed lifelong fan Andrew Calvert back into their ownership group, more than a decade after his turbulent spell at the club.

Calvert, a lifelong Bulls fan, became a shareholder and finance director in October 2013 after Omar Khan stepped down as owner.

But just two months later, he and fellow directors Mark Moore and Ian Watt released an alarming statement which read: “As each day goes by the board continues to receive more information detrimental to the stability of the business”.

They said at the time that £400,000 had to be cut from the budget, with this after John Bateman had been sold to Wigan for a significant five-figure sum.

After a dispute with Khan over the club’s shareholding, the trio walked out on Christmas Eve 2013, only to return a few days later.

Ok Bulls Ltd, established by Khan, was placed into administration in January 2014, with the assets ultimately transferred to Bradford Bulls 2014 Ltd.

Watt, Moore and Calvert were directors of the company, with Robbie Hunter-Paul acting as chief executive.

*(Image: UGC)*

But shortly afterwards, Bulls were docked six points for entering administration, prompting an angry walk-out from Calvert, Watt and Moore, who felt this was unfair and made relegation a certainty.

Marc Green ultimately took over the following month.

But Bulls are now on a much steadier financial footing, and chief executive Jason Hirst has warmly welcomed Calvert back.

He said: “On behalf of Bradford Bulls, I am delighted to announce that the club has added to its shareholding group, with lifelong fan Andrew Calvert joining the existing ownership group.

“To have yet another Bradford Bulls-centric person join our ownership group is fantastic news and only serves to strengthen our business, sustainability and future on and off-field prospects.

“Having developed a close personal friendship with Andrew over these last four years, I know how dearly he and his family treasure our club.

“I have no doubt that he’ll only help us all further build upon the success of our current ownership group.”

Calvert’s arrival comes hot on the heels of Philip Sharp’s departure.

He stepped down as director in July following his increased business commitments elsewhere.
