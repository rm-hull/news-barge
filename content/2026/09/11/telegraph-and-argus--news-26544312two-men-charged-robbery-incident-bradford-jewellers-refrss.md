Two men will appear in court this weekend charged with robbery over what police described as an "attack" at a Bradford jewellers.

West Yorkshire Police said Mohammed Balmakhear, 27, and Denzel Deneus, 34, both of no fixed abode, had been charged and remained in custody.

The charges relate to an incident at Royal Jewellers on Manchester Road on Thursday, September 10.

Both men are due to appear before Bradford Magistrates' Court on Saturday.

Police previously confirmed that officers were called to the business following reports of an incident at the premises.

Following enquiries, two men were arrested and have now been charged in connection with the investigation.

## Police statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Two men are due to appear before Bradford magistrates after they were charged in connection with an attack on a business premises in the city.

"Mohammed Balmakhear, aged 27 and Denzel Deneus, aged 34, both of no fixed abode, have been charged with robbery and remanded to appear tomorrow (Saturday).

"The charge relates to an incident at Royal Jewellers in Manchester Road on Thursday, 10 September."
