A four-bedroom detached house is being marketed in a sought-after location near Horsforth Hall Park for £750,000.

The property, which covers more than 1,700 square feet, has been significantly extended and designed for modern family living.

Its standout feature is an open-plan kitchen and living space with premium integrated appliances and a breakfast bar.

Bi-folding doors in this room provide access to the private gardens.

Additional rooms include a lounge and a room which could be used as a snug or home office.

Underfloor heating is fitted, and there is an indoor and outdoor speaker system.

All four bedrooms are doubles, with the principal suite including a dressing area, en-suite and air conditioning.

Outside consists of landscaped gardens and generous off-road parking, with an EV charging point and CCTV security also present.

Shops, cafes and amenities are close by, along with what the listing calls "highly regarded schools".
