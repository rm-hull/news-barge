Police are investigating a reported burglary at a property in Queensbury after officers were seen attending the scene.

The incident is understood to have taken place overnight, with police enquiries continuing into Friday.

Images shared on social media showed a police vehicle outside a property on Sand Beds on Friday morning.

No arrests have been announced and police have not yet released any further details about what was taken or the circumstances of the incident.

## Police statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "This is a report of a burglary at a premises on Sanbeds, Queensbury.

"Reported to have happened last night.

"Enquiries ongoing."
