A trip to a garden centre nowadays can become a fun outing for the whole family.

While garden centres are known for their plants and gardening equipment, many now have restaurants and cafés as well as activities for children.

Woodbank Garden Centre can be found just outside of Bingley and has a lot on offer, including its own vintage penny arcade.

## What is Woodbank Garden Centre like?


Woodbank Garden Centre has plenty of shrubs, trees, and outdoor plants to choose from as well as furniture to spruce up your garden and seeds to grow your own fruit and vegetables.

You can pick up a few houseplants as well to improve your home.

The gift shop stocks a range of products from Jellycat toys and candles to bags and gift sets.

If you need some food after exploring the garden centre, you can make a stop at The Potting Shed restaurant.

At the restaurant, you can get a whole variety of options from breakfasts to light bites, sandwiches, and even main meals to keep you going for the rest of the day.

Children will love walking around the pets and aquatics section where you can find a new addition for your family, such as some fish or a guinea pig.

The Old Penny Arcade is the most unique part of the garden centre and will offer hours of fun with over 100 coin-operated amusement and novelty machines from the 1920s to the 1960s.
