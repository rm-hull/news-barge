THE West Yorkshire Organic Group will hold its 35th annual show.

The event will take place tomorrow, September 12, from 10am at the Exhibition Hall in Saltaire.

The show, which will run until 3.30pm, will feature over 50 categories of fruit, vegetables, flowers, and other produce.

Respective categories are open to adults, children, and schools, and entries will be accepted on the day between 10am and 11.15am.

Each entry costs 20p to 40p and must be paid in cash on the day.

The show will also include stalls, tomato tasting, and growing advice. The produce will be judged based on taste.

The event is free to enter.
