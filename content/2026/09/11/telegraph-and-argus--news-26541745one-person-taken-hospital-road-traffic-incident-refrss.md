*(Image: Newsquest)*

One person was taken to hospital following a road traffic collision on Beacon Road in Bradford.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said it received an emergency call at 7.56pm on Thursday, September 10, reporting the incident.

An ambulance was dispatched to the scene.

## Patient taken to hospital

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said one patient was conveyed to hospital following the collision.

The ambulance service has not released any further details about the patient's condition.

## Police approached for comment

West Yorkshire Police were approached for comment, but said the incident does not meet its Service Level Agreement for media enquiries and is not issuing any further information.

## FULL statement from Yorkshire Ambulance Service

A YAS spokesperson said: "We received an emergency call at 7.56pm yesterday (10 September) to report a road traffic incident on Beacon Road in Bradford. An ambulance was dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
