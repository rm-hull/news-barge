If you are a fan of the Yorkshire Shepherdess Amanda Owen and keeping up with her family, you will be excited to hear you soon might have more episodes of Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids to watch.

The series is said to be returning this October, meaning fans only have a few weeks to wait.

The series will follow Amanda alongside her former husband Clive and their children, as they carry on the task of restoring a derelict farmhouse near where they live in Ravenseat.


## Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids set to return soon

The show’s synopsis reads: “Yorkshire Shepherdess Amanda Owen and her family realise their ambitious vision of renovating a derelict farmhouse in the picturesque Yorkshire Dales.”

The series is set to have five episodes and will carry on from where viewers left the family at the end of the third season.

Series three ended with Anty John’s, the abandoned farmhouse they are renovating, looking very different to how it was when the family started the renovation.

The Owens will now need to start thinking about how to make the inside of the property habitable by decorating Anty John’s.

Viewers can also expect to see more of the family’s everyday life on Ravenseat Farm as well as watch Amanda explore more of the history surrounding the old farmhouse and who lived there.


When series three and four were confirmed by Channel 4, Amanda said: “I am beyond delighted that we have been given the opportunity to showcase this unique and beautiful part of our British countryside and for people to join us on our exciting journey through the restoration of our farm next door.

“We’re grateful for Channel 4’s support and the effort and utter dedication of the film crew who have had to work in challenging weather conditions, coupled with the unimaginable chaos of both children and animals.
