St Francis Village Hall has been designated as the new Parish Centre of Worship.

The move was designated by the Rt Revd Dr Toby Howarth, Bishop Suffragan of Bradford, on August 21, 2026.

The hall, located in the former parish of Saint Peter Allerton, will now serve the new Parish of Thornton and Allerton.

The designation was made pursuant to Section 24 of the Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011.

This change will be reflected in the community's worship activities and gatherings, as the hall takes on its new role.
