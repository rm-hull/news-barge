These are the Brontë sisters like you’ve never seen them before…

York Settlement Community Players present Sarah Gordon’s play *Underdog: The Other Other Brontë*, which premiered at The National Theatre in 2024. 

Charlotte Brontë has a confession about how one sister became an idol, and the other became known as the third sister. You know the one. No, not that one. The other, other one… Anne.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/UNDERDOG-full-cast-and-director-Jon-Cook-Pamela-Gourley-Lara-Stafford-Millie-Rhodes-Jess-Murray-Maggie-Smales-Nick-Patrick-Jones-with-Claire-Morley-by-John-Saunders.webp)

*Underdog: The Other Other Brontë* with director Claire Morley

It tells the story of the rivalry between the Brontë sisters from Charlotte’s perspective, and reveals some truths behind why we only know Anne as the ‘third sister’, or the *Other Other Brontë*.

*Underdog: The Other Other Brontë* is at Theatre@41, Monkgate, from**Tuesday 13 to Saturday 17 October**.

Director Claire Morley, who last directed for YSCP in 2014 with Brecht’s *Drums in the Night* said: “Directing*Underdog* really appealed to me because whilst it tells the story of this incredible literary family who are so entwined in the national (and Yorkshire!) cultural fabric, it doesn’t put them on a pedestal. We see them as flawed human beings. 

“The dialogue and stagecraft is also very contemporary, which I love. I am looking forward to reflecting that further in aspects like the soundtrack. Expect current chart hits rather than choral music!”

Lara Stafford, Jess Murray, and Millie Rhodes lead the cast as Charlotte, Emily, and Anne Brontë. They are joined by a multi-rolling ensemble made up of local actors Nick Patrick Jones, Jon Cook, Maggie Smales, and Pamela Gourley.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Underdog-Cast-in-Haworth-by-Claire-Morley.webp)

“We’re so thrilled to bring this very Yorkshire literary play to Theatre@41 this October,” said YSCP Chair, Natalie Roe.

“We went on a cast and crew trip to the Brontë Parsonage in Haworth, which helped everyone get into the world of the play. There’s such a great energy in the team already, we can’t wait to share *Underdog* with you.”

Tickets for *Underdog: The Other Other**Brontë* start at £12 and are available via the Theatre@41 website.
