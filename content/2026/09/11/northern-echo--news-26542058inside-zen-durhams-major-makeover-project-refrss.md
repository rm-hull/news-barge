A major restaurant makeover is underway, with new features and a refreshed look to transform the dining experience.

Zen, on Court Lane in Durham, closed in August for a major makeover and is due to reopen later this month.

Co-owner Zak has shared a video update on social media, revealing that the outdoor area has already been transformed with a new steel structure, with glass roof panels also being installed.


He said: "It's just going to be so much more functional all year round.

"It's really going to make a difference to the overall floor space that we've got and how many people we can have in the restaurant."

The upgrades will allow the restaurant to accommodate more guests in a comfortable setting, regardless of the season.

Inside, workers have temporarily removed the venue’s iconic tree feature to install new LED lighting.

The refurbishment will also bring new seating and redesigned booths for the extended space.

The restaurant’s private dining area is being completely revamped to include air conditioning, a new lighting concept, and a “jungle vibe” for larger parties.

The toilets have been stripped back to bare brick and will feature what Zak called "Durham's very first Japanese toilets."

  A 3D render of what Zen will look like after the renovations

The kitchen is also getting new flooring, equipment and extraction units.

Zak said: "This place hasn't been touched for a number of years, so we're really taking it upon ourselves to strip it out.

"New floors, all new kitchen equipment, new extraction units going in, prolonging the life of Zen."

Zen Group has shared behind-the-scenes footage and refurbishment updates on Instagram.

The restaurant said: "There's plenty happening behind the scenes as we prepare to reopen later this month and welcome you back to a refreshed Zen Durham."

The newly renovated restaurant is expected to reopen in late September.
