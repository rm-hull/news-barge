A 58-year-old man wanted on warrant was arrested by police in Bradford city centre on Friday morning.

Police said officers attended the Darley Street area at around 11.27am and detained the man following enquiries.

In a statement, West Yorkshire Police confirmed he remains in custody.

The arrest took place on one of Bradford city centre's busiest streets and attracted attention from passers-by as officers carried out enquiries.

Images shared on social media showed at least two police vehicles and officers at the scene.

West Yorkshire Police later confirmed the attendance was linked to the arrest.

## Statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers attended in the Darley Street area at about 11.27am today and, following enquiries, arrested a 58-year-old man wanted on warrant.

"He remains in custody."

No further details about the warrant or the man's alleged offences have been released at this stage.
