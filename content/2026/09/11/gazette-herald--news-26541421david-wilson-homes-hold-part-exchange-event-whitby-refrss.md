A North Yorkshire homebuilder is inviting prospective buyers to a special weekend exchange event.

David Wilson Homes will host its Part Exchange weekend at the Chaloners Green development in Whitby, on Saturday, September 12 and Sunday, September 13.

The event aims to support existing homeowners through the moving process by offering its Part Exchange scheme and a £10,000 deposit contribution.

The scheme removes the property chain by making David Wilson Homes the guaranteed buyer of the customer’s existing home, helping to minimise the risk of delays and transaction breakdowns.

Steven Ball, sales director at David Wilson Homes North East, said: "We are dedicated to helping buyers find their ideal homes and making the moving process as simple as possible.

"By removing the need for a property chain, we can ease a lot of the stress off the buyer so they can focus on preparing for their move."

A February 2026 report from Barclays Property Insights found that 32 per cent of UK adults who bought or sold a home in the past three years were affected by property chains, with 46 per cent experiencing severe delays or transaction failures.

David Wilson Homes is encouraging anyone interested in a new home in North Yorkshire to attend the event and speak with its sales team.
