## Wartime comedy launches Yorkshire tour with sold-out village hall show

Badapple Theatre's "Back to the Land Girls" opened in Stillingfleet with a sold-out performance at the local village hall *(Image: Badapple Theatre Company)*

A popular wartime comedy has kicked off its tour in North Yorkshire.

Badapple Theatre's "Back to the Land Girls" opened in Stillingfleet with a sold-out performance at the local village hall.

Artistic Director and playwright Kate Bramley expressed her delight at the opening night, saying: "It was fantastic to see a full house for the opening night of 'Back to the Land Girls'."

The play, based on interviews with former Yorkshire Land Girls, tells the story of Buff and Biddy, who navigate life in the countryside during World War II.

The show aims to highlight the vital role these women played in feeding the nation during the war.

Sarah, also known as Yorkshire Land Girl, joined the cast at the end of the performance for a photograph.

She is recognised as a passionate speaker and educator who contributes to preserving the memory of the Land Girls from World War II.

The play will continue its tour throughout Yorkshire and nationally until October 25.

Upcoming performances include All Saints Hall, Poppleton on September 10 at 7.30pm, Catton Village Hall on September 18 at 7.30pm, Skirlaugh Village Hall near Beverley on September 26 at 8pm, and Green Hammerton Village Hall on September 27 at 7.30pm.
