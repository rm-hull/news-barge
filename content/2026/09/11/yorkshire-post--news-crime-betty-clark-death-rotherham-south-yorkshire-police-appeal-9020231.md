# 'Unimaginable grief': New police appeal one year after death of pensioner, 91

## Police have issued a new appeal a year on from the death of a 91-year-old woman in Rotherham.

Betty Clark was attacked in the Kiveton Park area of the town on Sept 10, 2025, in a ‘callous assault’ which also saw her bank card stolen.

She was found with serious injuries in her home the next day and was taken to hospital for treatment, but died on November 12.

Detectives believe her stolen bank card was used to buy scratch cards and alcohol between September 10-16.

Police made an arrest on suspicion of murder in December, before further arrests in May and June this year, but have yet to charge anyone in connection with Betty’s death.

Three men arrested on suspicion of murder have all since been bailed.

![Betty Clark is 'greatly missed' by her family](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/11/8/59/betty-clark.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Senior Investigating Officer Detective Inspector Adam Watkinson, from South Yorkshire Police, said: "It has been a year since Betty was attacked. A year in which her family have had to live every day with an unimaginable grief they did not deserve.

"All while knowing those responsible for this callous assault remain free to go about their lives.

“I know Betty is greatly missed by her family and our priority remains the same - to provide answers for her loved ones and ensure those involved in this incident are brought before the courts.

"We have made progress in our investigation, arresting three people, but we continue to appeal to our communities for any information which could assist us in securing justice for Betty.

"Did you see something suspicious in the Kiveton Park area between 10 and 16 September? Any information, no matter small, could prove to be crucial to our investigation. If you know something, please get in touch."

Anyone with information related to Betty’s death can share it anonymously through CrimeStoppers UK either by calling 0800 555 111, or through their website.
