A Bradford padel business has announced a major expansion of its flagship venue, more than doubling its capacity and creating what it describes as one of the region's leading indoor facilities.

Strike Padel, based at Beckside Business Park, has increased the number of courts at its centre from five to 12, with the seven new courts set to open to players from September 12.

Managing director Fahad Qureshi said the expansion marked a significant step forward for both the company and the sport's increasing popularity in the district.

Mr Qureshi said: "This is a huge milestone for Strike Padel and for the growth of the sport in Bradford.


"We opened Beckside with five courts and the response from the local community has been incredible.

"Expanding to 12 courts allows us to offer greater availability while creating a genuinely premium environment that players will be excited to experience."

He added: "A 12 court facility is a massive facility. You don’t really see many of these facilities in Yorkshire.”

## What's new?

The T&A was granted access to the facility ahead of its official opening to see what has changed.

The seven new courts have been fitted with purple Mondo turf, a specialist surface used in padel, along with 360-degree halo lighting.


The expansion means the company can accommodate more members and casual players, while also increasing the number of coaching sessions, social events, leagues and community programmes it can host.

Mr Qureshi said: "More courts means more availability.

"It also increases our capacity to give away the courts for free as well, to the schools and charities, which we already have been doing.”

## Facility caters to 'all abilities'

The enlarged facility is intended to support the company's aim of making padel more accessible across Bradford, giving existing players more opportunities to play while encouraging newcomers to try the sport for the first time.

Mr Qureshi said: "We do a free intro to padel to get people through the door, and then we have coaching sessions.

"At our facility, not only do you get the courts, but you also get the coaching as well, which is catered to all abilities.''

## National plans

The business said demand for court time has grown steadily since the Beckside venue first opened, prompting investment in the additional facilities.

Strike Padel said the investment demonstrated its ongoing commitment to Bradford and to raising standards within the indoor padel sector.

Mr Qureshi said the company also has plans to expand nationally.

The expanded Beckside centre is now taking bookings through Strike Padel's usual channels ahead of the new courts becoming available later this week.
