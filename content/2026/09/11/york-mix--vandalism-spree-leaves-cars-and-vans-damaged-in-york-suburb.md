Police are investigating a vandalism spree in the Groves, York.

Both cars and vans were damaged on **Garden Street**,** Townend Street**and** Lowther Street**overnight between** Sunday**and** Monday**, 6 and 7 September.

A vehicle was also damaged nearby on **Gillygate** on the same night.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A disturbance was heard on Garden Street just before the junction with Brook Street at around 11.30pm on the 6th September which may be linked.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation including CCTV, dashcam or ringdoorbell footage, please contact us online in the first instance.”
