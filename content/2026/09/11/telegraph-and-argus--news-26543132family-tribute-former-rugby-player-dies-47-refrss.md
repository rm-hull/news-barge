A FORMER Keighley Cougars player has died at the age of 47, his family have announced.

The club shared the news about Kris Kirk on social media, with relatives saying they were "heartbroken" by the loss of their beloved son, brother, uncle and father.

In a statement released through Keighley Cougars, the family said Mr Kirk had died following a short spell in hospital.

They said: "We are devastated to have lost Kris so young, following a short time in hospital."


## Keighley were a 'huge' part of his life

His family described the club as a "huge and much-loved part" of his life and have invited former teammates, coaches and staff to attend his funeral.

Born on September 16, 1978, Kirk made his first-team debut as a teenager and featured across four seasons for Keighley.

Kirk came through the ranks at the and made 15 first-team appearances between 1998 and 2001.

Predominantly a full-back, he scored two tries during his senior career.

Keighley were the only professional club he represented.

## Funeral information

The service will take place at York Crematorium on Thursday, September 17, at 1.40pm.

Instead of flowers, the family have requested donations to the Motor Neurone Disease Association, a charity they said was particularly close to Mr Kirk's heart.

The family concluded their tribute by saying: "Rest in peace, Kris. You will be deeply missed and forever loved."
