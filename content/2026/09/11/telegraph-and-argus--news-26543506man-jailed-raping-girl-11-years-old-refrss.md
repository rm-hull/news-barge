A MAN who raped a young girl when he was only 11 and 12 years old has been jailed after a jury found him guilty following a trial.

Bradford Crown Court heard that Madasar Hussain, who is now 32, still denied committing the rape offences against the complainant, who must not be identified for legal reasons.

But Judge Ahmed Nadim said that Hussain’s offending had caused harm to the complainant which had been profound and enduring.

He said: “Your victim believed that she was going to die before you commenced raping her.”

During Friday's sentencing hearing, the judge said Hussain’s offending was accompanied by “intimidation, coercion and on occasions physical force”.

Judge Nadim said the offending had resulted in decades of psychological injury and the complainant had been unable to describe what had happened from many years.

Hussain, of Halifax, was found guilty by the jury of six charges of rape and as part of his sentence he will have to register as a sex offender with the police for the rest of his life.

Judge Nadim also imposed a 20-year restraining order which bans the defendant from contacting the complainant.

The judge noted that Hussain still maintained that the complainant had “fabricated” the allegations, but he said that was in stark contrast to the detailed and compelling evidence accepted by the jury.

The court heard that Hussain had gone on to amass convictions for offences including violent disorder, dangerous driving and possession of drugs with intent to supply and Judge Nadim said those subsequent offences painted a deeply troubling picture of the defendant’s character.
