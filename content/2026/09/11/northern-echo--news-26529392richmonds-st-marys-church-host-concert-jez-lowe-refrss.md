Renowned folk musician Jez Lowe is set to perform at St Mary’s Church in Richmond later this month.

The concert, organised by the Friends of St Mary’s, will take place at 7.30pm on Friday, September 25, as part of ongoing fundraising efforts to preserve the historic church.

A spokesperson said: "The concert, organised by the Friends of St Mary’s, brings this legendary artist to Richmond as part of the ongoing fundraising efforts by the Friends, to help protect and develop the fabric and environment of this beautiful parish church which contains parts originating from the 12th century."

Tickets cost £15 and are available from The Book Stop in Richmond’s Market Hall, through Eventbrite, or from members of the church congregation.

A booking fee applies to online purchases.
