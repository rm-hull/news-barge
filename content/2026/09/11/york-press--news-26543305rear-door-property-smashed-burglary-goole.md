BURGLARS have smashed the rear door of a property during a break-in an East Yorkshire town.

Humberside Police said the burglary happened in Churchill Walk, Goole, between 5.28pm on Monday, September 7 and 5.06pm on Tuesday, September 8.

  


A force spokesperson said: "The suspect or suspects gained entry to the property by smashing the rear door then carried out an untidy search of each room.

"The suspect or suspects then left through the smashed rear door."

Humberside Police is appealing for information.

Anyone with CCTV footage, who saw suspicious activity in the area, or who has information about the offenders is asked to call 101, quoting reference 26*127381.
