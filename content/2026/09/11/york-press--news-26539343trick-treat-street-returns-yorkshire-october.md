## Halloween adventure returns to William's Den near North Cave this autumn

Trick or Treat Street is returning to William’s Den, Castle Farm, this autumn *(Image: The Wild Child Photographer)*

An immersive Halloween adventure is returning to an award-winning adventure destination in Yorkshire this autumn.

Trick or Treat Street will run from Saturday, October 24 to Saturday, October 31 at William’s Den, Castle Farm, near North Cave.

Now in its fourth year, the event combines immersive theatre, interactive storytelling, and Halloween activities in a magical outdoor setting, with a new detective-themed storyline.

Tor Carver, co-founder and creator of William’s Den, said: "Every year we love creating a brand-new adventure for families, and this year's detective mystery is our most interactive yet.

"From the moment visitors arrive, they'll become part of the story, meeting our wonderful village characters, solving clues, and helping uncover the mystery of the missing village chime."

Visitors will step into the fictional village of Trick or Treat Street, meeting eccentric residents, gathering clues, and solving puzzles as part of the adventure.

The experience is designed to be “spooky rather than scary” and includes live performances, games, and seasonal fun for children and adults alike.

William’s Den is also hiding 30 bells across Yorkshire in the lead-up to the event, offering families a 10 per cent booking discount if they find one.

For more information and ticket bookings, visit: [https://www.williamsden.co.uk/events/view/october-half-term](https://www.williamsden.co.uk/events/view/october-half-term)
