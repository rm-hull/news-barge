**TRANSPORT**— Motorists from the Harrogate district planning evening travel around the east side of York are being warned to prepare for significant delays, as a major section of the

**A1237 Outer Ring Road** prepares to close for overnight resurfacing works.

The engineering project, managed by Lane Rental Services on behalf of the City of York Council, is scheduled to commence on **Monday 21 September 2026**. The works will target the immediate approaches to the Haxby Road roundabout, the roundabout infrastructure itself, and adjoining sections of Haxby Road.

## Nightly Closures and Diversion Routes

To facilitate safe working conditions, the A1237 will undergo a **full road closure in both directions** between the Wigginton Road roundabout and the Strensall Road roundabout. The restrictions will operate strictly overnight to minimize daytime disruption:

- **Operational Hours:** 8:00 PM to 6:00 AM nightly.
- **Duration:** Programmed for up to five nights, with completion expected by the morning of Saturday 26 September 2026, weather permitting.
- **Daily Reopening:** The carriageway will reopen to all traffic at 6:00 AM each morning.

A formal, signed diversion capable of accommodating heavy goods vehicles (HGVs) will be deployed. Inbound traffic will be rerouted via Malton Road, Heworth Green, Lord Mayor’s Walk, Gillygate, Bootham, and the A19 before reconnecting with the open sections of the A1237.

Connecting routes off the Strensall and Wigginton roundabouts will remain open to allow standard access to those specific residential communities. Localized diversions using Wigginton Road will also be active for properties directly affected along Haxby Road and New Earswick.

## Pedestrian and Cycling Access Maintained

The off-road cycling infrastructure running between Wigginton Road and Haxby Road will remain operational throughout the project. Cyclists will be guided via a dedicated alternative route linking the off-road pathway with Haxby Road, Hawthorn Terrace, Link Road, Huntington Road, and Strensall Road.

City of York Council confirmed that existing pedestrian underpasses will stay open, local bus schedules remain unaffected, and emergency services will retain priority access through the construction zone at all times. On-street parking within the immediate working perimeters will be suspended during operational hours.

Cllr Kate Ravilious, Executive Member for Transport at City of York Council, stated that working overnight was essential to reduce the regional economic impact on commuters and commercial deliveries serving the nearby Clifton Moor commercial sectors.
