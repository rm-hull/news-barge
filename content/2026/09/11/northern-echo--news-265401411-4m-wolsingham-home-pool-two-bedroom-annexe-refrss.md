This £1.4m, seven-bedroom County Durham home offers luxury, plenty of space and sweeping countryside views.

Behind electric gates in the exclusive Holywood area of Wolsingham, the home offers far more than kerb appeal.

The five-bedroom main house and self-contained two-bedroom annexe suit family life, multigenerational living or guests.

Inside, the house feels huge from the moment you walk in.

Two lower-ground-floor reception rooms serve as a library and games room, while a grand staircase links all three levels.

The main floor has a large hallway, kitchen and utility room, formal dining room and spacious lounge.

  A photo of the indoor pool at Holywood, Wolsingham property, that is on the market for £1.4m.


The home also has an indoor swimming pool and jacuzzi, creating a private retreat for year-round use.

Upstairs, all five bedrooms have en-suite bathrooms, while three also have large dressing rooms.

The adjoining annexe has two bedrooms, a lounge, bathroom and kitchen-diner overlooking its private garden, giving occupants independence near the main house.

  A look inside the Holywood, Wolsingham property, that is on the market for £1.4m.


The grounds are just as striking.

To the rear, south-facing gardens lead across sweeping lawns and mature hedgerows to a picturesque pond and wooden bridge.

A raised terrace overlooks the countryside and is ideal for outdoor dining.

The lounge and swimming pool both open directly on to the terrace, linking indoor and outdoor living.

The grounds also include flower gardens, a private annexe garden and raised lawns overlooking the driveway.

The property’s practical features are just as strong.

A large double garage, parking for at least eight vehicles and a storeroom that could become a gym or leisure room add practical appeal.

The home offers privacy and grand-scale country living in County Durham.
