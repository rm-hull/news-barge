For more than 30 years, a family-run Bishop Auckland bakery has been serving up freshly baked treats and winning over generations of loyal customers.

Longstaff's Family Bakers makes everything on site at its Newgate Street shop, where customers can watch the bakers through a large viewing window.

Emma Longstaff and her husband, David, run the family business together, with David having worked as a baker since the age of 15 after starting his career as an apprentice in Sunderland.

Emma left her job as a primary school teacher to join the bakery full-time.

"It was the best decision I ever made," she said.

"It is more hours and harder work, but it is so rewarding. On a morning when the shelves are full and the shop is busy, we can stand back and know that we did all that."

Customers have nominated the bakery for The Northern Echo's top 10 bakeries list.

Reacting to the news, the owners said: "If people have nominated us, that's wonderful. We're over the moon about that.

"It's the third year we've been shortlisted, which is lovely. It shows people appreciate what we do."

Asked what has helped make the bakery so popular, they said: "We work very hard, we try to be consistent and we're always trying to improve our products.

"We're family bakers and hard work is what we're about really. Nothing has changed. We just keep doing what we've always done and try our best every day.

"It's lovely to know customers think highly enough of us to nominate us. We're very grateful for that support."

Emma said the family atmosphere among staff and customers has been central to the bakery's success.

She said: "We are like a family. Most of our bakers have been with us for around 18 years and the counter staff a similar amount of time.

"It is always a lovely atmosphere in here, and we have got to know lots of our customers really well."
