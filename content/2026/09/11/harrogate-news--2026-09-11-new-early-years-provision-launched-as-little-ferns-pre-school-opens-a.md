**HARROGATE**— A brand new early years setting has officially opened its doors to local families this week, expanding the educational offering within the Harrogate district.

**Little Ferns Pre-School**, based at

**Coppice Valley Primary School**, marked its official launch on Tuesday 8 September 2026. The Mayor of Harrogate,

**Councillor Viv Poskitt**, led the celebrations by cutting the ceremonial ribbon alongside children, local families, and representatives from the Red Kite Learning Trust and main contractor NSM.

## Nurturing Foundations and Forest School Learning

The newly refurbished facility provides a calm and structured environment tailored specifically for three and four-year-olds. Little Ferns introduces 26 active places to the local community, focusing heavily on communication, language development, and foundational learning blocks.

The curriculum emphasizes learning through play, curiosity, and exploration across both indoor and outdoor spaces. Enrolled children will benefit from direct access to the main school grounds alongside structured **Forest School experiences** to foster early outdoor development.


**Hannah McNamara, Headteacher at Coppice Valley Primary School, welcomed the addition to the school campus, stating**:

It means we can welcome and nurture children right from the very beginning of their educational journey, helping them build strong foundations for learning and for the people they will grow to become. Our school values – to be brave, kind and curious – will be at the heart of Little Ferns.


## Collaborative Trust Development

The pre-school represents a collaborative investment across the **Red Kite Learning Trust** multi-academy network, which oversees the central development and ongoing asset management of the new early years space.


**Ian Tucker, Chief Executive Officer of Red Kite Learning Trust, commended the delivery team, adding:**

*High-quality early years provision gives children such an important start in their education, and it is something we are rightly proud to be developing across Red Kite Learning Trust. There has been a huge amount of hard work, commitment and care behind the scenes to bring Little Ferns to life.*



Local families interested in securing remaining places or viewing the refurbished facilities can access enrollment guidelines and detailed provision information via the official Coppice Valley Primary School website.
