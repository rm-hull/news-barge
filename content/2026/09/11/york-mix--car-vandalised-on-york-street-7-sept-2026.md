Police are investigating after a car was vandalised on a York street.

It happened on **Bellfarm Avenue** off Huntington Road between**6am** and**8.45am** on**Monday** (7 September).

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It was reported that a Red Vauxhall Corsa was damaged whilst parked in the area, and officers are now asking for the public’s help with their enquiries.”

#### Bellfarm Avenue, York

They want to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident or any suspicious activity in the area, or who has CCTV, doorbell, dashcam, or mobile phone footage that may have captured the incident or those involved.
