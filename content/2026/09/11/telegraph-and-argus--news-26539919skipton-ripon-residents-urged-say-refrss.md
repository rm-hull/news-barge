A FRESH call has been made for residents to help shape the future of transport in their region.

Sir Julian Smith, MP for Skipton and Ripon, is urging people to take part in a public consultation on York and North Yorkshire’s draft Mayor’s Local Transport Plan, which will set priorities and investment decisions through to 2050.

Sir Julian said: "Transport is one of the issues residents raise with me most often, whether it is concerns about potholes, bus services, road safety, access to appointments or improving rail links across our area.

"This consultation is an important opportunity for people across Skipton and Ripon to have their say on the long-term future of transport in our part of North Yorkshire.

"The decisions taken now could influence investment and transport priorities for decades to come, so I would encourage residents, businesses, community groups and local organisations to take a few minutes to complete the survey.

"Our area has unique challenges, particularly for people living in rural communities, and it is vital that their concerns are taken into account."

The draft plan includes proposals for road and rail improvements, better bus services, active travel routes, road safety measures, and improved links to major cities and towns.

It also aims to boost access to jobs, healthcare, and education, with a particular focus on rural communities.

The public consultation will remain open until November 4, and feedback can be submitted via the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority website.
