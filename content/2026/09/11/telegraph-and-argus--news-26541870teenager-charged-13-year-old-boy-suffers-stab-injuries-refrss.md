A TEENAGER has been charged after a 13-year-old boy suffered stab injuries on Bank Holiday Monday.

The 16-year-old, who cannot be identified for legal reasons, has been charged with wounding with intent, possession of a knife or bladed article in a public place, and possession of a Class B drug.

The teenager appeared at Leeds Magistrates' Court on Thursday, September 10.

He was remanded in custody and is due to appear at a further hearing on September 16.

The charges relate to an incident on Shepherd's Lane in Leeds.
