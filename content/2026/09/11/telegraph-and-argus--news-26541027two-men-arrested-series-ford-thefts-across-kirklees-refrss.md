Two men have been arrested after a series of Ford vehicle thefts across Kirklees.

West Yorkshire Police said the pair were arrested in the Mirfield area during the early hours of Thursday, September 10, on suspicion of vehicle theft.

The arrests come as officers investigate a number of thefts reported in Batley, Cleckheaton, Liversedge and Mirfield in recent weeks.

## 
  **Ford models targeted**

According to police, Ford Focus, Ford Puma and Ford Kuga models have been targeted by thieves.

The force said four Ford Kuga vehicles were stolen this week alone, prompting increased concern among vehicle owners.

Roads policing patrols have since been stepped up in affected areas as enquiries continue.

## 
  **Drivers urged to take precautions**

Police are advising owners of the affected models to take extra security measures, including storing keys securely, using steering wheel locks and parking in well-lit locations where possible.

The force has also encouraged motorists to make use of CCTV systems and other security devices that may help deter thieves.

## 
  **Appeal for information**

West Yorkshire Police said the investigation remains ongoing.

Anyone with information about vehicle thefts in Batley, Cleckheaton, Liversedge or Mirfield, or anyone with relevant CCTV, doorbell camera or dashcam footage, is asked to contact police.

## 
  **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A spokesperson said: "Do you own a Ford Focus, Ford Puma or Ford Kuga?

"We are urging drivers to be vigilant following the theft of several Ford cars in parts of Kirklees.

"Officers have increased roads policing patrols due to recent thefts of cars from Batley, Cleckheaton, Liversedge and Mirfield area.

"Ford Focus, Focus Puma and Ford Kuga models have been targeted by thieves in the last few weeks with four Ford Kuga cars stolen this week alone.

"Owners of these cars are being urged to take extra precautions such as:

• Keeping keys in Faraday pouches and leaving cars in well-lit or secure locations at night if possible.

• Extra security such as wheel mounted steering locks

• Making sure their cars are covered by home CCTV cameras, if possible, in order to deter thieves

"In the early morning of Thursday 10th September 2026, two men were arrested on suspicion of vehicle theft in the Mirfield area. This is an active investigation and enquiries are ongoing.
