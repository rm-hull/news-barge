OPEN any fridge in any kitchen across the UK and there is likely to be a jar of mayonnaise inside.

Ideal on its own as a dip to accompany chips or salad, it is often mixed with egg or tuna for a sandwich or jacket potato filler.

Mayonnaise is a mix of oil, egg yolk and either vinegar or lemon juice. The ideal mayo, as it is often called, should taste rich, smooth, and creamy, with a bright, clean tang that balances the large amount of oil - usually olive oil or vegetable oil - it contains.

There is no definitive proof as to how and when mayonnaise was invented, but it is widely believed to have been made by the chef of the French Duke of Richelieu in 1756 after he captured Port Mahon on the Spanish island of Minorca. The chef needed cream for a victory feast sauce but he did not have any, so he used olive oil and eggs instead. He named his new creation after the city.

Some food historians believe the French chef learned a local egg and oil emulsion recipe from the residents of Minorca or from Spain.

Hellmann's, currently owned by Unilever, is the market leader for mayonnaise, holding a commanding 51.5 per cent market share in the UK and ranking as the biggest-selling brand globally. Richard Hellmann began serving mayonnaise in his New York City deli in 1905, launching the brand.

I tested six supermarket own brands, as well as Hellmann’s and Heinz. They are made with free range eggs. I opted for mayonnaise in a glass jar - I prefer it to plastic, and it’s easier to extract when there’s not much left.

Here’s what I thought:

*Morrisons Mayo 500ml, 99p

A pale cream colour, this was smooth in consistency. It felt quite oily in my mouth - I know there is a high oil content in mayonnaise, but I wouldn’t expect to taste it. It had a slightly sour edge and aftertaste which I didn’t like either.

Pros: appearance, consistency.

Cons: taste.

Marks: 1/5

*Batts Real Mayonnaise, Lidl, 500ml, 89p

This one was quite white in appearance, like Morrisons, had a gloop n consistency, but on the positive side it had a pleasant, slightly eggy taste with a bit of a tang. It wasn’t bad, but not as flavoursome as Hellman’s and Sainsbury’s. A very competitive price.

Pros: taste, price.

Cons: none.

Mark: 3/5

*Real Mayo by Sainsbury’s, 500ml, 89p

White in colour, this one was creamy and smooth in texture, with a slightly sweet, tangy taste. It was really tasty and a lovely consistency. It was very similar in taste and texture to Hellman’s mayonnaise. A really nice taste without being too heavy. Like Tesco, this was price matched with Aldi.

Pros: texture, taste, price.

Cons: none.

Marks: 4/5

*Hellmann’s Real Mayonnaise, 400g, £2.75 (bought in Sainsbury’s)

An appealing cream colour, the consistency is thick and creamy, and the taste rich and flavoursome. I could taste egg more than with any of the other jars. It has a pleasant aftertaste. On the downside, it is more than three times the price of some. Note: the weight is in grams.

Pros: consistency, taste.

Cons: price.

Mark: 4/5

*Bramwells Real Mayo, Aldi, 500ml, 89p

This one had a nice creamy colour and smooth consistency, but it had a vinegary edge which didn’t disappear on swallowing. It was quite sharp. It’s an affordable price, but I wasn’t at all keen.

Pros: appearance, consistency, price.

Cons: taste.

Mark: 2/5

*Tesco Mayonnaise, 500ml, 89p

A creamy colour and consistency, and just the right level of thickness. It was smooth, and I could taste the eggs and a hint of mustard. It was tasty but lacked the depth of flavour of Sainsbury’s and Hellman’s. Good value - like Sainsbury’s, this was price matched with Aldi.

Pros: consistency, taste.

Cons: none.

Mark: 3/5

*Heinz Seriously Good Mayonnaise, 460g (480ml), £2.50

A white colour, this had a far thicker consistency than the others which I wasn’t keen on. It didn’t live up to expectations in other areas either, and tasted very bland, almost cardboardy. Like Hellmann’s, it’s also far more expensive than the own-brand products.

Pros: none I could find.

Cons: consistency, taste, price.

Mark: 1/5

*Asda Classic Real Mayonnaise, 500ml, 89p

A dark cream colour, this was a little gloopy in consistency. There’s not much to report because I didn’t get much of a taste of anything, it was flavourless.

Pros: none.

Cons: taste

Mark: 1/5
