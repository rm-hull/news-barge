SALTAIRE Festival is set to return this weekend, once again transforming the UNESCO World Heritage village into a vibrant hub of music, food, arts, and community spirit.

Events run from September 12 to 20, with organisers promising a packed programme for all ages and interests.

Jurata Reed, events and programme lead and trustee, said: "Despite the financial challenges, we are back with a strong programme in the park and across the village.

"So many people have stepped forward to share their talents, skills, and enthusiasm – and we can’t wait for everyone to enjoy what they have helped create."

## What to expect

The festival opens with Piano Village, featuring performances from top pianists at venues across Saltaire, including Leeds-based pianist and composer Simeon Walker and Belgian pianist and composer Maxime Marquette.

Visitors can also enjoy open gardens, YardFest, art exhibitions, and other community events.

Roberts Park will serve as one of the festival’s main hubs, hosting food, drink, cocktails, and a vibrant marketplace filled with independent traders offering handmade crafts, gifts, and artisan products.

The park will also feature workshops, live music, family entertainment, and performances throughout both weekends.

Organisers said the festival is designed to offer an immersive experience where visitors can "eat, shop, explore, and stay awhile."

## Mega closing weekend

The programme continues throughout the week with comedy, dance, poetry, and more, all leading up to the Food, Beer & Music finale on September 19 and 20.

The closing weekend will include the Continental Street Market, a funfair, and three stages of live entertainment – the Main Stage and Bandstand in Roberts Park, as well as another stage on Caroline Street.

Each stage will feature a different act every hour, offering visitors a chance to experience a wide variety of performances as they explore the festival.

## 'Come hungry and curious'

Ian Andrews Swailes, festival organiser and chair of trustees, said: "Saltaire Festival is about coming together and celebrating what this incredible place and its people have to offer.

"Come for the music, the food, the markets, or simply the atmosphere – bring your family and friends and be part of it."

Events will take place across Saltaire and Roberts Park, all free and family-friendly.

The festival invites everyone to "come hungry, come curious, come and enjoy Saltaire at its best."
