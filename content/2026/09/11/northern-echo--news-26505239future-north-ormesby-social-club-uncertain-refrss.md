The future of a Middlesbrough social club remains uncertain after the man tasked with keeping it running admits: "I don't know what the future holds."

The North Ormesby Institute, known to many as the Big Tute, has faced several problems in recent months, with staff working hard to keep the venue open after it was repossessed.

However, in a video posted to social media on Wednesday (August 26), the institute said the club's future is now uncertain.

The club said: "We've done our damnedest to keep this club open."

"We've been operating off a generator, which has cost in excess of £2,000 a week to keep the lights on.

"We got burgled, we've had the locks glued up on the front. We've been through 12 padlocks on the side gate.

"We really have been up against it here, but we have strived on and we've made improvements."


The update comes after the club was repossessed by Newday Group Holdings, with CEO John Taylor explaining that the individual who previously owned the venue is now in a position to regain control.

Taylor said: "The person who got the club repossessed is in a position now where he can redeem his loan.

"What that means is, unbelievably, he takes back control of the club."

Despite that development, he said there had been little communication from the owner regarding the future of the Big Tute.

He added: "What does the future hold for this club? The honest answer is, I really don't know."

Taylor said instructions from the receiver were to clear the building, with stock, equipment and newly installed tills all removed.

"The power's now disconnected, the generator's getting collected, all the stock's gone, the new tills we put in, they've been removed," he said.

"It is an incredibly sad day, to be honest, because it's just uncertainty."

Taylor also paid tribute to staff who had continued operating the club through its difficulties.

He said: "I'd like to say a special thank you to Kevin and Kendra, who have kept the doors open of this club throughout, and the rest of the team have been amazing."

The Big Tute has long been at the heart of the North Ormesby community - hosting events and serving generations of local residents.

"People have amazing memories here," Taylor said.

"This is the last video for me and, you know, it is lights out. I don't know what the future holds for the club."
