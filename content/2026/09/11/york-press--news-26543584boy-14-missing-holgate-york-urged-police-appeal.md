Police have issued an urgent appeal to find a missing 14-year-old boy from York.

Jerome, from Holgate, was last seen in Manchester on Tuesday (September 8) but is believed to have returned to York, North Yorkshire Police said.

The force said Jerome is described as black with a slim build.

A police spokesperson urged anyone who sees Jerome to dial 999.

“If you see him, please call 999,” they said. “If you have any other information that could help us find him, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“Please quote 12260178181 when sharing information.”
