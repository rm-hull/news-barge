Durham may be one of the UK's smallest university cities, but its student nightlife packs a serious punch.

With Freshers' Week around the corner, starting on September 28 until October 4, it's a great time to get to know the best spots in the city.

With more than 20,000 students attending Durham University, the city's pub scene has become a central part of student life.

There is a bit of everything from historic inns and riverside beer gardens to quirky college bars hidden within ancient buildings.

Here are the student favourites widely regarded as Durham's best pubs.

## The Half Moon Inn

  A picture of the Half Moon Inn. 

*(Image: Google Maps)*

Sitting on New Elvet, The Half Moon Inn is a longstanding favourite among Durham students thanks to its prime location close to university buildings and the city centre.

Housed in a historic Tudor building, the pub combines traditional character with a lively student atmosphere.

Its large outdoor courtyard overlooking the River Wear is particularly popular during the warmer months, while big screens showing live sport ensure a busy crowd throughout the year.

The Half Moon is also a Grade II-listed building with 17th- and 18th-century origins and has featured in the BBC detective drama George Gently.

## The Shakespeare

The Shakespeare on Saddler Street remains one of Durham's most distinctive pubs. Its low ceilings, traditional interior and wide range of ales draw students, locals and visitors.

It also has a refurbished upstairs gin bar and a reputation as one of Durham's most haunted pubs. Believers and sceptics alike still make it a popular stop on Durham pub crawls.

## The Boat Club

  A picture of the Boat Club in Durham city. 

*(Image: Chris BOOTH)*

The Boat Club, on the River Wear, is a go-to spot for cocktails and nights out. Students flock there for drink offers, DJ events and riverside views.

Its combination of affordable cocktails and relaxed atmosphere makes it particularly popular for midweek socials and celebrations after exams.

## The Dun Cow

  The Dun Cow, Durham 

*(Image: Trip Advisor)*

The Dun Cow claims to be Durham's oldest pub and offers a quieter alternative to the city's busier venues.

On Old Elvet, the centuries-old pub is known for its traditional interior, real ales and cosy atmosphere.

It draws smaller student crowds than the city's nightlife hotspots but remains a favourite for a quieter evening near the university.

## The Undercroft Bar

No list of Durham student pubs would be complete without The Undercroft Bar, inside Durham Castle.

Known as 'The Undie', it is regarded as the world's oldest student bar and remains central to Durham's university culture.

The stone-walled venue hosts quizzes, live music and student events, making it a rite of passage for many undergraduates.

## Tin of Sardines


Tin of Sardines offers students one of Durham's most unusual drinking experiences.

The compact riverside bar serves more than 200 varieties of gin and has won a loyal student following.

Its riverside outdoor seating makes it one of the city's most picturesque spots for an evening drink.

## Student nightlife remains a Durham institution

Durham stands out for its strong college-bar culture alongside its independent pubs.

Students can choose from historic city-centre venues, riverside bars and college bars across the university.

While Newcastle may be only a short train ride away, many Durham students are happy staying local, with the city's combination of characterful pubs, cheap drinks and unique university traditions continuing to define the student experience.
