  **Adam Kennedy speaks with North East-based pensive pop star Isabel Maria about her new single, which was recorded in New York, and her upcoming release show on Tyneside.**

Isabel Maria released her atmospheric new single, Ruin, today.

Recorded in Brooklyn, New York, just before Halloween, the song is a thunderous follow-up to her critically acclaimed sophomore EP, Because I Care, unveiled in the spring.

This year has been productive for the award-winning singer/songwriter.

  Isabel Maria has enjoyed a busy year of touring, recording and festival appearances across the region 

*(Image: howywhite.com)*

"I've had an insane start to the year with my EP coming out and the tour, and that was kind of a baptism of fire in 2026," said Isabel.

"I've had a really nice summer.

I've just been playing the odd show and writing some new tunes, and I've been in the studio, and it's all just been very nice."

During the summer, the artist performed at The Gala Theatre in Durham as part of Brassed On at the Durham Brass Festival.

  Award-winning North East artist Isabel Maria ahead of her headline show at The Cluny 2 in Newcastle 

*(Image: howywhite.com)*

The event allowed Isabel to reimagine her repertoire with the aid of the Brazen Brass band.

"Durham Brass is something that I had attended for the past two or three years because my friends had done it, and it was like a big bucket list thing for me.

So, I was really thrilled to get to do that," reflects Isabel.

"It was like a total dream to hear my songs with Brass.

It was very exciting."

  Isabel Maria, whose new single Ruin is available on streaming platforms now 

*(Image: howywhite.com)*

Isabel also performed a high-profile show at Durham Fringe Festival last month, where the artist performed solo on a Steinway grand piano.

"I love that Steinway.

It makes me very emotional.

I love playing it," said Isabel.

"I think it's been a really nice summer and a really nice year for gigs in the sense that a lot of them have been quite different.

It's not been the same thing over and over again, like the same set and the same setup.

It's been different songs and different people that I've been playing with, which has been really nice and entertaining for me."

  Singer-songwriter Isabel Maria recorded her new single, Ruin, in Brooklyn, New York 

*(Image: howywhite.com)*

Today, Isabel releases her new single Ruin.

A track which she recorded with producer Chris Gaskell in New York last year.

Isabel explains the inspiration behind the song.

"I was having a conversation with my friend Naomi about how much I hate horror films, and I was like, that would kind of be an interesting thing to write about," she says.

"I sat down at my piano, and I was thinking about a conversation, and it was kind of a situation where I blended two concepts.

It was how much I hate horror films and then also using, self-sabotage as a way to kind of pose that."

Isabel adds: "It was a few months later when I was at anti-social camp, I met this amazing guy.

We were assigned to write for someone else together, but he was amazing.

And when I ended up being back in New York later in the year, I think it was the day before Halloween, which feels fitting.

But I texted Chris, and we got in the studio together.

It felt really strange going into it because I was like, oh my God, I'm recording a song in New York, and this is so big, but actually it's the same as recording a song here.

Like it felt like such a big, scary deal.

And it really wasn't.

It was just like hanging out with a friend and making music.

It just happened to be in a big high-rise building in Brooklyn instead of in Newcastle.

It was so much fun.

Chris is a genius, and I love the song."

Whilst in the Big Apple, Isabel performed at a restaurant owned by Lady Gaga's father.

"That was such a crazy day and such a crazy gig.

I'd been asked to play that venue when I was over in New York in June, and I couldn't make it because I had an event that night.

So, I went over in October, and luckily, they let me play, and it was so lush," said Isabel.

"You would play some songs, and you would be interviewed by Lady Gaga's dad before and after.

He'd ask you questions about the songs and ask you questions about your career and what you're up to.

I'd never really done a gig like that before, but it was pretty nice."

Isabel Maria will celebrate the release of her new single with a headline show at The Cluny in Newcastle on Sunday.

"I'm really excited.

I think it's always nice when you get to put out a song and then immediately a few days later or the day off, you get to meet real people and see their real-time reaction," explains Isabel.

"It's always a bit scary putting out a song, and you don't come across anyone for a couple of days, and it's like, oh my God, what do people think?

I really like to

experience new songs in the room with people.

And I've already been playing it at some shows by myself.

  North East singer-songwriter Isabel Maria, who has released her new single, Ruin 

*(Image: howywhite.com)*

But it's going to be the band debut of Ruin."

Isabel will be supported at the show by Hels Pattison and Robyn Errico.

"I think it's going to be a really nice one.

I'm such a big fan of Hels and Robyn, so I'm so excited that they jumped on the bill.

And I've never played a big headline show in The Cluny 2, and it's just like one of my favourite venues.

It's so cosy," proclaims Isabel.

For ticket information and further details, please visit: [https://thecluny.com/.](https://thecluny.com/.)

Looking beyond the single release, Isabel has plans afoot for the rest of the year and beyond.

"I just received some funding from Sunday for Sammy.

I'm excited to use that in the studio in a couple of weeks to start making some new stuff.

I just got a mix back yesterday, which I'm really thrilled with.

And that will be heard at some point next year.

And yeah, I'm just excited to see what happens with all of that."
