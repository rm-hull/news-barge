Nine drivers were caught speeding during a police speed watch on a Bradford road.

The operation took place on Back Lane in Wilsden this past Monday after complaints about drivers breaking the 30mph limit.

## What police have said

The police have now said they will conduct further speed watches in the area.

The drivers were caught speeding in just 40 minutes, similar to the nearly 40 vehicles clocked speeding in Baildon and Wilsden.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "On Monday, September 7, 2026, Bingley Rural Ward PCSOs conducted a speed watch following complaints of vehicles speeding on Back Lane, Wilsden.

"Nine vehicles were caught exceeding the 30mph limit and approximately 100 cars were travelling down Back Lane towards Howard Road, Wilsden.

"The officers were there between 5.10pm and 5.50pm. Further speed watches are planned."
