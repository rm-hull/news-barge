Punters looking for a fun night out in Sunderland are spoilt for choice, with plenty of pubs and bars within easy walking distance of the uni and the city centre.

Arcade games, cocktails and classic pub atmospheres are all on offer for University of Sunderland students and freshers exploring Sunderland’s nightlife.

Whether you’re craving a quirky cocktail bar, traditional pub, or somewhere to belt out karaoke with friends, Sunderland has something for everyone.

### 
  **SpaceBar Cocktails & Arcades**

SpaceBar is a standout option for those who enjoy a bit of friendly competition with their drinks, and is around a ten-minute walk from the city centre campus.

Customers on Tripadvisor say the venue has the "best cocktails" and "biggest arcade and pinball collection".

The bar also hosts karaoke, live music, quizzes, pinball tournaments and comedy nights, making it a lively option for groups looking for more than just a drink.


### 
  **The Dun Cow**

For those who prefer a more classic pub atmosphere, The Dun Cow offers historic Edwardian surroundings, real ales and a welcoming vibe.

At just a five-minute walk from the city centre campus, or 20 minutes across the river from the St Peter's campus, customers often praise the venue as a "fantastic back bar" with "great real ales".

The pub also serves food and features live music.

### 
  **The Ship Isis**

Another character-filled venue is The Ship Isis, known for its old-fashioned charm.

Tripadvisor reviewers call it a proper "old-fashioned pub" with "great décor" and "excellent pizza".

The Ship Isis is a five-minute stroll from the city centre campus, often hosting live music and other performances, making it a great spot for a longer evening out with a bit of everything.

### 
  **Diego’s Joint**

Around 20 minutes on foot from both campuses, and for something more modern, Diego’s Joint in Sunniside offers cocktails, beer, food and a packed events calendar.

The bar regularly hosts live music, comedy nights and quizzes, making it an all-rounder for students making a night of it.


### 
  **The Manor Bar**

The Manor Bar adds to the city’s nightlife mix with cocktails, wine, beer, live music and a cosy, trendy setting.

The venue, which is around 15 minutes from the city centre campus, is popular with groups and also features decent outdoor seating.

With such a variety of venues on the doorstep, freshers can make the most of their time in Sunderland by exploring everything the city has to offer.

From arcade games and karaoke to traditional ales and comedy nights, there’s plenty to help students settle in, make new friends and start university life with a bit of fun.
