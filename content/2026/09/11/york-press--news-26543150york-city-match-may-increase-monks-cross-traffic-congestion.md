## Heavy traffic expected around York shopping park this weekend, council says

Heavy traffic is expected because of on-going work around Monks Cross *(Image: Supplied)*

HEAVY traffic is expected around the area of a main shopping park in York this weekend, according to City of York Council.

The local authority said York City's EFL Division Two fixture versus Swindon Town kicking off at 3pm at the LNER Community Stadium could increase congestion.

It follows news that the northbound lane of the Monks Cross Link road between the shopping park and the A1237 is closed until November.

  


The council issued a reminder to the public via its Facebook page of local bus services available to and from Monks Cross.

The number 9 bus runs from York city centre to Monks Cross, while number 12 connects Woodthorpe and south-west York with the city centre and Monks Cross.

The 81 bus operates from Malton to Monks Cross and the city centre, and number 20 links Rawcliffe and Osbaldwick via the city centre and Monks Cross.

A special matchday shuttle service, operated by Pullman, will also be available.

Services depart from Stonebow in York city centre for home fans, and from York Railway Station and Exhibition Square for away fans.
