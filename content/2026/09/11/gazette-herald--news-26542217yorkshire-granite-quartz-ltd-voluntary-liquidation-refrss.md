A North Yorkshire construction business has gone into liquidation with creditors owed more than £300,000.

Yorkshire Granite and Quartz Ltd of Kirkgate, Knaresborough, was incorporated in 2022 and it entered voluntary liquidation on August 11.

Leeds-based Ellis Breese Business Recovery and Insolvency Ltd was appointed the liquidator.

The liquidator has produced a report outlining the financials behind the company known as a statement of affairs.

This report was published this week on the website of Companies House.

The statement of affairs said the company had estimated assets of £95,000.

Among the liabilities, were two members of staff owed £4,156 between them. There are classes as preferential creditors and are paid first.

HMRC came next as a secondary preferential creditor- those who are paid next- and it was owed £10,556.58. This left £80,287.42 available for distribution to unsecured creditors.

The report lists the unsecured creditors- those who are paid last- who between them were owed £334,208.95.

There were 12 trade and expense creditors owed £298,435.49. Two employees were owed £5,405 between them. Two banks/ financial institutions were also owed 30,368.46 between them.

The largest unsecured creditor listed was the company’s sole director Ian Graham who was owed £251,958.

Altogether, this gave a total deficit with regard to creditors of £253,921.53.

With issued and called up capital of £100, this gave an overall deficit of £254,021.53.
