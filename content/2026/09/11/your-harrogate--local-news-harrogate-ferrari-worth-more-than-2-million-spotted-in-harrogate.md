A Ferrari worth more than £2 million has attracted attention after being spotted parked in Harrogate.

The red Ferrari F40 was photographed on the roadside near the Yorkshire Hotel.

Ferrari produced the F40 between 1987 and 1992 to celebrate the Italian manufacturer’s 40th anniversary.

It was the final road car personally approved by company founder Enzo Ferrari before his death in 1988.

Powered by a 2.9-litre twin-turbocharged V8 engine, the F40 produces around 471bhp and can reach a top speed of more than 200mph.

Just over 1,300 examples were produced, making it highly sought-after among collectors.

Values vary according to a car’s mileage, condition and history, but examples now commonly sell for more than £2 million - with particularly rare cars commanding considerably higher prices.
