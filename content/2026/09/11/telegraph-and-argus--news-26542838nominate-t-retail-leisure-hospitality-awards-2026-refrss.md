TIME is running out to nominate the businesses, teams and individuals that make Bradford a great place to shop, dine, stay and enjoy.

Nominations for the Telegraph & Argus Retail, Leisure & Hospitality Awards 2026 are closing on Sunday.

The awards will take place on November 15 at The Broadway Bradford, where the district’s best will be celebrated beneath the spectacular Christmas lights.

## How do I nominate?

To submit a nomination, scan the QR Code or visit [https://forms.office.com/e/xGkwP7nBcs?origin=lprLink](https://forms.office.com/e/xGkwP7nBcs?origin=lprLink) today to help celebrate the very best of Bradford’s retail, leisure and hospitality sector.

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The awards celebrate the very best of Bradford's retail, leisure and hospitality sector, recognising everything from new or old businesses, independent retailers and cafés to hotels, restaurants, leisure venues, sports and fitness organisations, outstanding teams and individuals who are helping the district thrive.

One of the night’s most anticipated honours is the Hospitality Hero Award, recognising an individual who goes above and beyond for customers, colleagues and the wider community.

Get nominating today for a chance to see someone you know be star of the show.
