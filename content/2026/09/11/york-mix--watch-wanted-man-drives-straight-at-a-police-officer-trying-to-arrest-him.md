A wanted man who drove at a police officer in Scarborough has been jailed for 14 months.

Harrison Douglas Long, 35, who was previously from Selkirk and is now of no fixed address, was spotted by police driving a van in the town.

Wanted by another force, a uniformed officer signalled him to stop.

**Video**

Long drove right at him and his car, narrowly missing him.

Driving off, he damaged the police vehicle and smashed into another car.

He was later arrested in Crambeck near Malton while the passenger in another car.

Long was charged with dangerous driving, driving while disqualified and driving without insurance.

He was sentenced at York Crown Court yesterday (Thursday).

The vehicle belonging to a member of the public was written off due to the cost of repairs and its owner no longer has a car through no fault of their own.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/harrison-douglas-long.webp)

The police car needed extensive repairs meaning it was off the road for a lengthy period of time.

**Investigating officer, Detective Constable Alfie Thomlinson of Scarborough CID, said:** “Long made a significant attempt to get away from a police officer carrying out his lawful duty.

“The 14-month custodial sentence and two-year driving ban is a welcome outcome.

“Let it be a message to anyone else who thinks that police officers are just a uniform. We are human, we have loved ones to return home to after each shift, and we will not tolerate dangerous behaviour like this from anyone.

“Not to mention the cost to the innocent member of the public whose car he hit.

“Thankfully, the officer involved escaped injury, but it could have ended very differently that day.”
