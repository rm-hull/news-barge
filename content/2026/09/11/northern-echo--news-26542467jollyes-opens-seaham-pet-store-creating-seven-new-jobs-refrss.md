An award-winning pet superstore has opened its doors in County Durham, creating seven new jobs.

Jollyes opened its latest branch at Byron Place Shopping Centre in Seaham on Friday, September 11.

The store is the retailer’s 127th in the UK and has created seven new jobs.

  Award-winning pet retailer Jollyes has today opened the doors to its latest store in Seaham, County Durham 

*(Image: JOLLYES)*

Janet Long, store manager, said: "We’re delighted to open the doors to our new Seaham store and become part of the local community.

"As we continue to grow, it’s really important to us that we’re not only bringing great value and choice to local pet owners, but also investing in the areas we serve.

"Creating seven new jobs in Seaham is a fantastic way to start, and we’re looking forward to welcoming customers and playing an active role in the community, and for pet parents, for years to come.”

  Award-winning pet retailer Jollyes has today opened the doors to its latest store in Seaham, County Durham 

*(Image: JOLLYES)*

The opening was marked by a visit from the Mayor of Seaham, Councillor Milford Lingard, who cut the ribbon alongside retired service dogs Ulrich, Pip, and Vicky.

Ulrich and Pip are retired police dogs who served for a combined 13 years.

Pip, an explosives search dog, even travelled to the 2024 Paris Olympic Games during her career.

The trio attended the event on behalf of the National Foundation for Retired Service Animals (NFRSA), Jollyes’ nationwide charity partner.

Customers were treated to limited-edition goody bags, a spin on the store’s ‘wheel of fur-tune,’ and entertainment from animal-themed stilt walkers.

Jollyes mascot Molly was also on hand to greet shoppers.

A spokesperson said: "Over the past three years Jollyes has almost doubled the number of stores across the UK, becoming a clear challenger brand in the UK pet retail market."
