Police have launched an urgent search to find a missing boy from York.

Jerome is 14 and from the Holgate area of the city.

He was last seen in Manchester on Tuesday (8 September) but is since believed to have returned to York.

He is described as black, with a slim build.

If you see him, please call 999. If you have any other information that could help us find him, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.
