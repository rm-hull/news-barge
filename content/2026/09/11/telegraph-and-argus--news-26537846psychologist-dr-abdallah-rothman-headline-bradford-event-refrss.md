AN internationally recognised psychologist will headline a new community event in Bradford.

Dr Abdallah Rothman will feature at Cultivating Presence: A Journey of Healing & Return, which will take place this Saturday, September 12, from 8pm to 10pm at Bradford Muslim College, Al-Mustafa Centre, Young Street.

He will lead a lecture and guided workshop exploring the connections between faith, spirituality, and emotional wellbeing.

Farkhandah Faraz, a community pharmacist and co-organiser of the event, said: "Mental health and emotional wellbeing are issues that touch many individuals and families in our community.

"We would especially encourage mental health professionals, healthcare practitioners, carers, community leaders and anyone seeking support for their own wellbeing to attend.

"Dr Rothman’s work offers valuable insights into how Islamic spiritual traditions can contribute to healthier individuals and stronger communities, while providing practical tools that people can apply in their everyday lives."

Dr Rothman is a renowned Islamic psychologist and educator with more than 20 years of experience.

His work focuses on integrating traditional Islamic teachings with modern counselling and psychological practice.

The event is free to attend, but advance registration is required due to limited capacity.

It is co-produced by Waseem Mahmood, founder of Hijrah Hub Travels, alongside Ms Faraz.
