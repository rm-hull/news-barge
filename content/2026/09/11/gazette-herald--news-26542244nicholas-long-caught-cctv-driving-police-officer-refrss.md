North Yorkshire Police has released CCTV and bodycam footage of the incident where a wanted man drove his van at an officer.

Nicholas Long, 35, was on the run and the officer was trying to block him into a cul-de-sac in Scarborough with his car, York Crown Court heard on Thursday.

The 35-year-old was in a white van which he drove at the police officer, who was in uniform and telling him to stop.

Police have released footage which shows he drove at the police officer and over part of a parked red car to escape.


The red car that Long collides with in the video was written off.

Long, of Bridge Street, Selkirk, Scotland, is today serving 14 months in jail after pleading guilty to dangerous driving, driving without insurance and driving whilst disqualified.
