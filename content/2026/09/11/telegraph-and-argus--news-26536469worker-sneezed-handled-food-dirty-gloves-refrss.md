A FOOD business has been handed a long list of hygiene warnings following two inspections.

Bradford Council’s Food Safety Team visited Zubis, based at Cannon Mills on Union Road in Great Horton.

An inspection was carried out at the premises on May 24, then a follow-up at a second site took place on June 12.

A visit to a 'home' site was also recorded, but it's not clear if that refers to the second site, or a separate site altogether.

The team identified multiple breaches of food hygiene law, prompting an urgent warning for the business to take action.

## Sneezing and not washing hands

At site one, they observed a person handling foods with dirty gloves, eating food, sneezing into arm and handling a personal mobile - all while not washing hands.

The report said: "These practices place ready to eat food at risk of contamination."

The council warned that Zubis must review its procedures and make immediate improvements.

## Cooked meat next to raw meat

The officer also observed unsanitary and unsafe practices at the BBQ station.

The report read: "Cooked meat skewers were being held hot on the top rack of the BBQ next to raw uncooked meat/poultry skewers."

"The cook removed cooked meat from a skewer for a customer order with his hands without washing his hands after handling raw meat/poultry skewers.

"These practices place ready to eat food at risk of contamination with food poisoning bacteria."

Concerns were also raised about hand towels being stored on open work surfaces near raw meat skewers.

Food hygiene officers recommended installing hand towel dispensers at both sites.

It also advised that ready-to-eat salad items should be kept separate from raw meat and poultry to avoid contamination.

The report continued: "Ensure foods are protected from sources of contamination at all stages of production."

"Cover foods displayed for sale at the front counter."

"I recommend using cloches and tongs for the samosas and pakoras and other fried items displayed for sale."

It was advised fruit and sugar cane used for drinks should also be stored in a way that prevents contamination.

## What was found at the second site

At a second site, further problems were observed.

The report said: "At the time of the inspection, a cook at the BBQ was seen cutting into uncooked meat to check if ready to serve."

"The meat was undercooked and the cook did not sanitise the knife used to conduct the physical check of the food."

"This is a contamination risk."

It added: "Ensure that equipment used for cooking checks are sanitised between use.

"It is recommended that calibrated temperature probe is used."

The cook was also observed handling raw meat and then cooked meat without washing his hands.

Raw meat stored in large buckets was found above ready-to-eat foods in fridges.

Inspectors said this posed a contamination risk and instructed the business to ensure proper separation.

## 'Deep clean' required

Other warnings included stopping the practice of storing a jug in the bucket of yoghurt sauce.

The report read: "This can lead to contamination from the food handlers hand onto the handle and into the yoghurt stored within the container."

"Store the jug separately and maintain in a clean condition."

The equipment wash sink and the wooden surfaces around the boiler also needed sealing to ensure easy cleaning.

Vinyl coverings on equipment that prevented adequate cleaning were ordered to be removed.

At the second site, a deep clean was required.

Electrical and gas pipework must also be kept clean and clear of hazards, the report continued.

Inspectors also said that despite having a completed Safer Food Better Business pack and up-to-date records, it was found that that written procedures were not being followed in practice.

## What a spokesperson for Cannon Mills had to say

A spokesperson for Cannon Mills said: "We have majority of really good ratings for the food stalls."

They claim that Zubis was "in middle of renovations and improvements" which they say the "council is aware of."

They spokesperson added: "The food traders are always welcoming so if you wish come down and have a try of different multicultural foods you will be more than welcome to do so."

## What the Council said

A council spokesperson said: “The Food Business Operator at Zubis did enquire about a re-rating visit," but has not "formally requested a reinspection at the time of writing".

"As such, we have no cause to amend their Food Hygiene Rating score.”
