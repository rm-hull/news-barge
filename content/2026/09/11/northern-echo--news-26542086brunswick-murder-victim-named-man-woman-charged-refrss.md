Two people have been charged with murder after a man died in an attack in a North East village.

Douglas Moffat died having after being assaulted on Drysdale Crescent in Brunswick Village, near Newcastle Airport, on Wednesday (September 9).

The 56-year-old, who lived on the street, was found with life-threatening injuries and was pronounced dead at the scene.

Alan Richards, 43, and Sarah Shorter, 40, both also of Drysdale Crescent, have been charged with his murder.

Richards and Shorter have also both been charged with possession of a bladed article.

Shorter has further been charged with attempted murder of a woman in her 50s.

The woman was taken to hospital for treatment of injuries sustained in the incident and later discharged.

Both are due to appear before Newcastle Magistrates’ Court on Friday (September 11).

A 38-year-old man and a 40-year-old woman remain in police custody after being arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender.

Detective Inspector Barry McAtominey, of Northumbria Police’s Major Investigation Team, said: "We continue to offer support to the victim’s family at this time.
