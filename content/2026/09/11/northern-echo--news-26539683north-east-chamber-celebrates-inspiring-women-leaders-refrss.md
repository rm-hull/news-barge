INSPIRATIONAL women taking the lead in business across the North East were celebrated at a high-profile regional conference.

The North East Chamber of Commerce’s Inspiring Women in Business Conference, sponsored by PD Ports and now in its fourteenth year, was held at Ramside Hall Hotel in Durham and attracted more than 150 business leaders, entrepreneurs, and community figures.

Themed ‘Women Who Rise’, the event honoured the vital role women play in shaping organisations and communities, while championing the importance of leadership, workplace equity, and empowerment across the region.

Deborah Walton, president of the Chamber and chief financial officer of Palintest, opened the conference.

She said: "We are truly inspired by the strength, determination, and positivity of so many women across our community.

"They are the leaders, innovators, and disruptors – the beating heart of our region.

"It’s only right that we should celebrate and share their achievements and give them a platform to connect with other like-minded individuals."

The conference featured keynote speakers, panel discussions, and interactive workshops.

Dr Birju Bartoli, CEO of Northumbria NHS, reflected on her own career journey in her keynote address.

She said: "When I enrolled on the NHS graduate management scheme, I never thought I’d become a CEO of an organisation that employs 12,000 people.

"I’d describe myself as an accidental leader – I’m evidence that leadership is for everyone if they’re willing to work hard and grasp the opportunity."

Kay Carrick, chair of the Chamber’s Women’s Leadership and Social Equity Forum and director of fundraising and development at Beamish Museum, underlined the importance of the programme.

She said: "It inspires women and motivates them to thrive.

"Female leadership and workplace equity is key to the prosperity of our region."

The conference also included a panel discussion on Workplace Equity, chaired by Ms Walton and featuring leaders from PD Ports, New College Durham, Moonrise 24hr Recruitment, and The Experience Effect.

Discussions centred on embedding inclusion in business strategy, building women’s support networks, and practical ways to advance gender equity in the workplace.

The event also highlighted the upcoming Inspiring Women in Business Awards 2026, sponsored by Newcastle International Airport.

Award categories include Business Leader of the Year, Most Inspiring Employer, Apprentice of the Year and three Community and CSR Inspiring Woman of the Year awards.

The awards ceremony will take place on Friday, November 27 at Newcastle Civic Centre.
