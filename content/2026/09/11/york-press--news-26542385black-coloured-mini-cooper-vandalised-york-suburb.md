VANDALS have damaged a car parked up in a York suburb overnight.

North Yorkshire Police said criminal damage was caused to a black-coloured Mini Cooper in White Rose Avenue, New Earswick, between midnight and 5am on Monday, September 7.

The was parked outside the owner's property at the time, police said.

The news comes after cars and vans in three streets in The Groves were targeted in the same period.

  


North Yorkshire Police is asking for the public’s help.

Anyone who saw or heard anything suspicious, witnessed the incident, or has CCTV, doorbell, or dashcam footage is urged to come forward.

Information can be provided anonymously to North Yorkshire Police by calling 101, or by contacting Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference 12260175366 when providing information.
