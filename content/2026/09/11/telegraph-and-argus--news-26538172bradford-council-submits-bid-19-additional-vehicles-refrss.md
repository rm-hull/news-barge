BRADFORD Council is applying to keep 19 extra goods vehicles at a Shipley operating centre.

The operating centre is Parks & Landscapes Depot, Northcliffe Park, Rae Bradford Road, Shipley, BD18 3DE.

City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council, based at Shearbridge Depot, Great Horton Road, Bradford, BD7 1PU, is seeking the change.

The application asks for the licence to be changed to allow the additional vehicles. No trailers are included in the proposal.

Owners or occupiers of land, including buildings, who believe their use or enjoyment could be affected can make representations.

Representations must be sent to the Traffic Commissioner at Hillcrest House, 386 Harehills Lane, Leeds, LS9 6NF, within 21 days of the notice.

Those making representations must send a copy to the council at the address given at the top of the notice.

The notice was published on September 5, 2026.
