THE Met Office has forecast a mix of rain and sunshine for Bradford this weekend.

Saturday will start off clear, with temperatures around 9C.

The sky will become partly cloudy by 11am, with temperatures rising to 10C.

Cloud cover will increase by 1pm, with a 30 per cent chance of rain.

Light rain is expected from 3pm, with temperatures peaking at 18C.

The rain will continue into the evening, with a 70 to 80 per cent chance of precipitation.

The rain will continue past midnight, with light showers through the night.

Sunday will start off overcast, with temperatures around 16C.

The sky will remain overcast throughout the morning, with a 30 to 40 per cent chance of rain.

The afternoon will see sunny intervals from 1pm, with temperatures peaking at 19C. The chance of rain will decrease to 20 per cent by 3pm.

The evening will be partly cloudy, with temperatures dropping to 14C by midnight.

And the wind will be light throughout the weekend, with speeds ranging from 3mph to 10mph.
