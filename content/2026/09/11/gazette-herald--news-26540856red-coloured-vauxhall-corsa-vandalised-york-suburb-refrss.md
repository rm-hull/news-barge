VANDALS have targeted a York suburb and damaged a car.

North Yorkshire Police said a red-coloured Vauxhall Corsa in Bellfarm Avenue, Heworth was subjected to "criminal damage" between 6am and 8.45am on Monday, September 7.

The county's police force said it is appealing for information and are asking for the public's help with its enquiries.

  



A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident or any suspicious activity in the area.

"Also, anyone who has CCTV, doorbell, dashcam or mobile phone footage that may have captured the incident or those involved, or saw anyone acting suspiciously near the vehicle around the time of the damage, or has any information that could assist the investigation."

Police said anyone with information should email the officer in charge [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk), or report online via the North Yorkshire Police website.

If you are unable to use the online options, call 101 - alternatively information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via their website.
