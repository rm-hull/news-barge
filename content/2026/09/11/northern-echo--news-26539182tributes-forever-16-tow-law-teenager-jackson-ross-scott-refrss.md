A County Durham teenager who "lit up the room" has been remembered by his family as a caring young man who lived life to the full.

Jackson Ross Scott, from Tow Law, tragically died on August 1 aged just 16.

In a heartfelt tribute, his mum, Anna Ross, 42, described Jackson as a happy, funny and loving young man whose infectious personality touched the lives of everyone around him.

She said: "Jackson was a happy, funny, loving and caring young man who had such a zest for life and excitement for what his future had to hold.

  Jackson Ross Scott, from Tow Law, sadly died on August 1 at the age of 16


"His sense of humour, his loving nature and his infectious personality made him someone his family and friends loved to be around."

Jackson was a beloved son to Anna and Darren and stepson to Jamie.

He was a much-loved grandson of Larry, Sandra and Peter, devoted boyfriend to Ava, a dear stepbrother to Alfie, Bobby and Tommy, a cherished godson and a friend to many.

  Jackson Ross Scott with his family


His family said he had a unique way of brightening every room he entered and creating lasting memories with those around him.

"Jackson had a way of lighting up a room and there was never a dull moment when he was around," his mum said.

"Life was fun, full of laughter and wonderfully unpredictable."

  Jackson Ross Scott with his family


Those who knew Jackson remembered him as someone who cared deeply about others and was always willing to stand up for people when they needed support.

His mum said: "He cared deeply about others and would look out for those around him. He was never afraid to stand up for someone who needed a voice.

"He had a kind heart and a need to make things better for those he cared about."

The impact of his loss has been felt across the community, with family, friends and his football club paying tribute to the teenager.

  Jackson Ross Scott with his family


  Jackson Ross Scott with his family


Tow Law Town AFC held a minute's silence before a match in August in memory of Jackson.

In a statement, the club said: "It was a moment for everyone at the ground to stand together, pay their respects and remember Jackson.

"Everyone at the club sends their deepest condolences to Jackson's family, friends and all those who knew and loved him during this incredibly difficult time."

  Jackson Ross Scott with his family


His mum Anna said the family had been left "devastated" by Jackson's death.

She said: "Everybody is devastated that he’s not here anymore.

"Jackson has left a mark on our lives that will remain forever.

  Jackson Ross Scott with his family


"He will be remembered in the stories we tell, the laughter we share and the memories that we will carry in our hearts.

"Deeply loved and deeply missed. Forever 16."

  Jackson Ross Scott with his family

A service celebrating Jackson's life will take place at Wear Valley Crematorium on Friday, September 11, at 11.30am.

Family flowers only have been requested, with donations in his memory welcomed for 4Louis.

Following the service, family and friends will gather at The Newmarket, Tow Law, and a special balloon release in Jackson's memory will take place at 7pm.
