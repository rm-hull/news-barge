A man has been arrested after a cyclist died in a crash with a car in the early hours of the morning.

Emergency services were called to Nissan Way in Washington where a black Mercedes AMG had struck a bike shortly before 1am on Friday (September 11) , police said.

The cyclist, a man in his 50s, was pronounced dead at the scene. His next of kin have been informed and are being supported by specialist officers.

A 30-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

  Generic view of Nissan Way. 

*(Image: Google Maps)*

He has since been released under investigation.

Sergeant Greg Huntley, of Northumbria Police, said: "Our thoughts are with the man’s family and loved ones at this incredibly difficult time.

"I would like to express my heartfelt condolences following this tragic incident, and we will continue to support them in any way that we can.

"Enquiries are underway to establish exactly what happened in the moments leading to this collision.

"As part of our investigation, we are keen for any motorists or witnesses who were travelling in the area at this time to come forward.

"We are particularly keen to hear from those who may have dashcam footage which may have captured the moments leading up to the collision or may show the man riding his bicycle in the area of Nissan Way."

"I would urge anyone who has information about the incident - no matter how small you feel it may be - to get in touch with us."

Anyone with information or footage is asked to contact Northumbria Police by sending a direct message on social media, using the force’s live chat or report forms on their website, or by calling 101, quoting reference number NP-20260911-0031.
