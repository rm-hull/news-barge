Concern is growing for a missing teen from County Durham.

Durham Police are searching for the missing boy from the North East who has not been seen for more than two days.

16-year-old Zak was last seen heading towards Prince Avenue, in Bowburn, County Durham, on Wednesday morning (September 9).

Zak was last spotted around 9.20am wearing a black and grey tracksuit and a dark puffer jacket.

Durham Police described the teen as "around 5ft 7inches tall, with short dark hair."

The force added: "If you have seen him or know where he is, please call us on 101, quoting incident number 50 if September 10."
