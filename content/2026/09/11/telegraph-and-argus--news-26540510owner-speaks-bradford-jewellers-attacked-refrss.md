THE owner of a jewellers has spoken of their shock after their shop was "attacked".

The incident took place at Royal Jewellers on Manchester Road on Thursday with two men arrested at the scene.

The owner, who wished to remain anonymous, said: "My husband was in the store at the time.

"It's just shocking."

The owner said that they have been campaigning Bradford Council to allow them to place bollards outside of the shop.

They said: "I have been trying to get hold of the council since March.

"All jewellers in Bradford should have bollards outside."

Azeem Javed, who works at Raja's Pizza Bar next door, said: "We saw the men get arrested.

"There had to have been more than 15 officers outside."

When the T&A visited the scene on Thursday, a police cordon was still in place and officers were in attendance.

Forensics were being conducted inside the shop.

Broken glass could be seen on the floor where police forced their way in.

## 
  **Full police statement**

On Thursday, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At about 1.21pm today, police received a report of business premises being attacked in Manchester Road, Bradford.

"Officers attended and arrested two men on suspicion of aggravated burglary.

"Enquiries are continuing."
