Rather than symbolising loss, the German term for a receding hairline conjures ideas of wisdom, experience and the sort of sage advice once offered by trusted political counsellors.

**What does* Geheimratsecken*mean?**

*Geheimratsecken* is the everyday German term for a hairline which is receding at the temples, the classic M-shaped pattern that often develops over time. It’s a feminine compound noun made up of*Geheimrat*("Privy Councillor" or "Secret Councillor") and* Ecken* ("corners"). The ‘s’ is there to link the two words.

Literally, the word means "privy councillor corners". In practice, it’s almost always used in the plural – *die Geheimratsecken* – on the basis that most people have two temples rather than one.

*Geheimratsecken* is often paired with the verbs*haben*(“to have”) and* kaschieren* (to conceal).

**Why do I need to know* Geheimratsecken*?**

Generally, any word which turns a perceived flaw into a compliment should be worth celebrating.

Historically, a *Geheimer Rat* was a senior adviser to a king, prince or government. These men were typically older, highly educated and politically influential. As a result, their receding hairlines became associated with hard-won wisdom, status and intellectual authority rather than loss or decline.

In other words, *Geheimratsecken* suggests that your hair isn't disappearing. It's being exchanged for gravitas.

The term also offers a glimpse into regional varieties of German. In Austria, you may hear *Hofratsecken*, referring to a* Hofrat*("court councillor"). In parts of Switzerland,* Ratsherrenecken* performs a similar function.

Another, tongue-in-cheek term for a receding hairline in German is *Ehestandswinkel* or “matrimonial angles” – a word which attempts to imply a connection between marriage and hair loss.

**Use it like this:**

*Er hat deutliche Geheimratsecken, trägt sie aber mit Stolz.*

He has a pronounced receding hairline but wears it with pride.

*Sie sagt, dass Geheimratsecken oft sehr charaktervoll aussehen.*

She says that a receding hairline often looks very distinctive.
