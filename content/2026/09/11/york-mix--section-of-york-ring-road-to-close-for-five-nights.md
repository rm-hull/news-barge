A stretch of the York Outer Ring Road will be closed to traffic for five nights later this month.

It’s to enable resurfacing works on the **A1237approach** to the** Haxby Road roundabout**, the** roundabout itself**and to** Haxby Road**.

Work will start on **Monday 21 September** at**8pm** and is expected to take place for up to five nights.

It should be completed by **6am** on**Saturday 26 September**, weather permitting.

Between **8pm** and**6am** each night, the**A1237 will be closed in both directions** between the**Wigginton Road roundabout** and**Strensall Road roundabout**.

The other connecting roads off Strensall and Wigginton roundabouts will be open for traffic to allow access to Strensall and Wigginton.

## Diversions

A signed diversion will be in place.

This will use Malton Road, Heworth Green, Lord Mayors Walk, Gillygate, Bootham/ A19, and the A1237.

Drivers and cyclists may choose to take alternative routes, but due to the capacity the A1237 holds for large vehicles, the council must offer a diversion able to cope with the size and weight that the road usually carries.

Local diversions for New Earswick and Haxby Road properties will be in place using Wigginton Road in addition to the A1237 diversion.

The off-road cycle route between Wiggington Road and Haxby Road will remain in use.

A separate cycle diversion will be in place linking the off-road route with Haxby Road, Hawthorn Terrace, Link Road, Huntington Road and Strensall Road.

Any street parking in the working area and within close proximity to the works will be suspended during the work times.

Specific access requirements may be arranged by speaking to the site traffic management operatives, who will co-ordinate with residents’ and businesses’ requirements.

## List of works

The works will be carried out by Lane Rentals Services on behalf of City of York Council.

Cllr Kate Ravilious, the council’s executive member for transport, said; “Haxby roundabout is the latest in a long list of works that have been completed this year, from pothole repair on residential side streets to resurfacing of major roads.

“We do know that these projects can be disruptive so in many cases, like this one, we are working overnight, reopening the road each morning, to reduce the impact on local residents and businesses.”
