Fundraising will provide 180 million litres of clean water to flood-hit families in Nepal over five years.

Rotary Club of Adventurers and Bradford West Rotary Club raised the money at Tesco Great Horton Road on August 29 and 30, 2026.

The Huddersfield to Ukraine Humanitarian Aid project raised more than £3,000, including £600 from Rotary Club of Shipley, taking the total to about £4,000.

The donations will buy AQUABOX clean-water filters for families affected by the floods.

Flooding followed a glacier collapse and landslide near Nepal’s border with China.

By August 31, officials had confirmed 903 deaths and more than 4,200 people remained missing. It is Nepal’s deadliest natural disaster since the 2015 earthquake.

Humanitarian assessments say 65,000 people need emergency help with water, sanitation and hygiene.

More than 22,000 children need protection, while damaged schools and disrupted health, water and sanitation services are increasing the risks of malnutrition and infectious disease.

Contaminated supplies can spread waterborne illness while healthcare and public services are already under pressure.

Rotary Club of Adventurers president Aneela Ahmed said: “We are deeply grateful to everyone who donated and to Tesco Great Horton Road, their Community Champion Sameera and her colleagues for hosting the collection.

"The generosity shown across the weekend will help families in Nepal gain access to safer drinking water when they need it most”.

The floods also washed away or badly damaged homes, roads, bridges, hydropower facilities and other critical infrastructure, making it difficult for response teams to reach isolated communities.
