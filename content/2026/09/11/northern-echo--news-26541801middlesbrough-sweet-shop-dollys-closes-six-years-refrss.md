A popular Middlesbrough sweet shop has shut its doors with immediate effect.

It comes as the owner has taken a step back to focus on family life.

Dolly’s owner Vicky Walker announced the closure of the shop on social media on Thursday, September 3.

Vicky said she closed the shop to put her family first after struggling to balance running the business with time spent with loved ones.

She said: "I’m writing this post with great sadness today to tell you all that Dolly’s is now closed for business.

"I have had a wonderful six years meeting all of my gorgeous customers that have been by my side the whole time, it has been some of the best years of my life.

"However, sometimes life just gets in the way and I’m not going to sugarcoat it, these past couple of years have been a struggle in business and at home."


The shop opened as Annabelle’s before becoming Dolly’s and serving Middlesbrough customers for the next six years.

Vicky now wants to spend more time with her husband, children and grandson ahead of the arrival of her new granddaughter.

She said it was “100% time” to put herself and her family first after missing important moments with loved ones.

One commented: "What am I going to do now for a Milky Bar sundae?"

Another said: "Aw no! My kids will be gutted, no more trips to Dolly’s. Thank you for your excellent customer service and fab sweets!

"Best of luck for the future and enjoy being with your family."

Despite the closure, Vicky says she has no plans to leave the sweet business behind, with plans in place for a new venture that will be slightly tweaked to allow for a better work-life balance.

While keeping details under wraps for now, Vicky said she plans to reveal more about her next venture on social media in the coming weeks.

Thanking those who had supported the business over the past six years, she added: "From the bottom of my heart, thank you to every single customer that came through my doors and ordered from me."
