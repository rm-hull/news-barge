JONATHAN and Paul Addy-Armitage run Wuthering Delights Café and Bistro on Thornton Road, Thornton. We spoke to Paul about their business.

Q: When was Wuthering Delights established?

Just over a year ago - Jonathan and I took over the cafe/bistro on September 1, 2025.

A: Who was behind it and how do it come into being?

This was Jonathan's dream - he had worked in education for 35 years and felt that it was time for a change. In March 2025 we started looking for an opportunity to take on a cafe.

Q: Do you have a background in catering/hospitality?

A: Not at all, although I come from a family of chefs and Jonathan has always loved cooking and is very good at it. I have run my own business for 12 years so understand how businesses work and the challenges we would face.

Q: Is the cafe in a good location?

A: Yes, it is on the main road through Thorton and is very close to the Bronte Birthplace.

Q: Describe the interior.

A: The interior has warm rich colours - it has a log fire and is styled around a literary theme with book wallpaper and artwork of Haworth and the surrounding moors. Customers have described it as cosy, warm and welcoming.

Q: What sort of food and drink do you serve?

A: We have a wide range of dining options including delicious breakfasts, eggs Benedict and our best seller shakshuka, homemade burgers, salads, cold sandwiches, hot sandwiches including our popular hot beef sandwich and a range of homemade cakes made on the premises.

Q: What dishes are among the most popular?

As stated above our full breakfast, shakshucka - eggs poached in a spiced tomato sauce with chorizo, onion and peppers served with bread, or a vegetarian option. Also popular are hot beef sandwich, homemade burgers, omlettes and our range of gluten and dairy free cakes.

Q: Do you have any specialities and if so what?

A: Gluten free pizza and our range of cakes

Q: Do you change your menus seasonally?

A: Yes, we have a festive menu including Christmas lunch and festive sandwiches, plus a wider selection of cold sandwiches and our ploughmans lunch in summer.

Q: Do you have a lot of regulars from the local area and what do they say about the cafe?

A: We have a lot of regular customers from the village and the surrounding area and we are very grateful to them, and for all the positive comments on Tripadvisor.

Q: And visitors from further afield?

A: Yes, as we are in Bronte Country and close to the Bronte Birthplace. We recently had visitors from the US.

Q: Do you host any functions at the cafe?

A: We have regular bistro evenings, the last Friday of the month which has themed menus and great musical entertainment - customers can book via the website. The cafe is available for small functions and we have hosted a number of birthday parties.

We also have a monthly networking group which meets to help local business grow through more sales.

Q: How do you cater for children?

Our menu is adaptable to children and we also have some kids' options such as fish fingers, chicken nuggets etc..,

Q: Do you sell food to take away?

A: Yes, our food is available to takeaway

Q: Do you feel like part of the community in Thornton - how important is this to you?

A: We really want to become the hub of the community, a place where people come to eat great food, feel at home and feel welcomed by our team.

Q: What are your plans for the future?

A: In the next year we want to continue to grow our presence and our reputation as the place to be and where our customers feel welcome and safe.

Q: Please add anything else you would like to say.

A: We have loved having the cafe and being part of the community and we are so grateful to our customers, they are brilliant and we are grateful to our team, who represent our values well.
