Few meals are as synonymous with the North East as fish and chips.

Whether it’s a Friday night tradition, a post-work treat or a seaside favourite brought inland, Darlington is home to some highly rated chippies serving everything from freshly battered cod to homemade curry sauce.

One Darlington takeaway was named 2025 Fish and Chip Takeaway of the Year, underlining the town’s reputation for a quality chippy tea.

We’ve rounded up the Darlington fish-and-chip shops most praised by customers, review sites and local food fans.

### 
  **Yarm Road Fish & Chips**

   

*(Image: Yarm Fish and Chips)*

If there is one Darlington chippy that regularly tops local conversations, it is Yarm Road Fish & Chips.

The takeaway was named UK Fish & Chip Takeaway of the Year and has built a reputation for freshly cooked fish, generous portions and friendly service.

TripAdvisor customers have called it “the best fish and chips in Darlington”, praising its cooked-to-order fish and homemade mushy peas; another described it as “more than a chippy.”

### 
  **Barton’s Fish and Chips**

Barton’s Fish and Chips on Longfield Road has built a loyal following among Darlington residents.

The family-run shop is regularly praised for its crispy batter, freshly cooked fish and welcoming staff.

   

*(Image: @lunchingwithloz/Instagram)*

Boasting a 4.8 star TripAdvisor rating, the takeaway recently attracted attention after being featured by social media food reviewer “Lunching With Loz”, who described the curry sauce as “10/10”, with other online reviewers going as far as calling it “the best chippy in town.”

### 
  **North Road Fish Bar**

North Road Fish Bar is a regular contender when Darlington residents debate the town’s best chippies.

Reviewers have praised its freshly cooked fish, traditional chips and reliable quality.

One customer called it “easily the best chippy in Darlington”, and it remains popular with residents in the north of town.

### 
  **Thirlmere Chippy**

Thirlmere Chippy is a longstanding local favourite, earning strong customer ratings and praise for its friendly service.

Customers regularly praise the takeaway’s fish and chips for their value and consistency.

### 
  **Haughton Chippy**

Haughton Chippy has served the area for decades and continues to attract loyal customers.

Recent TripAdvisor reviews praised the cod, fish patties and chips, while regulars praised its family-friendly service and traditional menu.

It is one of east Darlington’s best-known chippies and remains popular for delivery and takeaway.
