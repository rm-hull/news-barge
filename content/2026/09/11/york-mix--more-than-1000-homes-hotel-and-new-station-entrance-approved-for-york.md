A massive project decades in the making has taken a huge step forward as the first phase of plans for a brand-new city quarter in York received planning permission.

City of York Council has approved the first phase of York Central, aiming to unlock a £2 billion transformation of 110 acres of brownfield land behind the station.

The site, which leaders say has been “hidden in plain sight for decades,” will be turned into a hub of new housing, shops, green spaces, and commercial hubs.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-central-innovation-hub-coal-drops-square.webp)

Initial site investigation work is starting straight away, with main construction expected to launch next summer.

The initial buildings will be ready by early 2029, with the full first phase set to finish by 2030.

Here is what the newly approved first phase includes:

- **1,014 new homes** , with at least 20% set aside for social and affordable rent and sale.
- A new **western entrance at York Station** , with a civic square.
- A **213-bedroom hotel** and**67,447 sq ft of retail and leisure space** for shops, cafes, and amenities.
- A **99,190 sq ft innovation hub** and a separate 134,000 sq ft Government Property Agency hub for up to 2,600 civil servants.
- Extensive **public parkland** , new public squares, courtyards, and direct cycleways and footpaths to link the site seamlessly with surrounding streets.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-central-coal-drops-square-wider.webp)

The overall plan aims to bring 2,500 homes, 6,500 jobs, and more than a million square feet of commercial space to the city.

Housing Secretary Angela Rayner hailed the decision as a massive win for the region. She said: “York Central is a fantastic opportunity to transform derelict brownfield land into thousands of new homes, jobs, and green spaces, giving more people the chance to live and work in this great city.

“We’re turning years of talk into action.”

York leaders have also welcomed the planning breakthrough.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-central-first-phase-mayor-colleagues.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-central-craig-bentley-nrm-garry-taylor-cyc-allan-cook-arlington-claire-douglas-cyc-tom-gilman-mclaren-property-richard-webb-cyc.webp)

**David Skaith, Mayor of York and North Yorkshire**, said: “York Central is going to transform the city, delivering thousands of much-needed new homes, including affordable housing, and creating thousands of high-quality jobs. Soon we’ll see York Central take shape and transform how people see our county.”

**Cllr Claire Douglas, Leader of City of York Council**, added: “This is fantastic news for York. It brings us closer to delivering the homes, jobs and opportunities that residents want to see and to growing York’s economy, which will benefit communities across our fine city for generations to come.”

Under the plans, the **National Railway Museum** will get a new Central Hall, create a new permanent gallery space, and introduce step-free access throughout its site for the first time.

More than £135 million of government-funded infrastructure works are already completed on site, including new access roads and cycle routes.

Developers McLaren Regeneration and Arlington Real Estate say they are ready to get shovels in the ground.

To look through the wider masterplan or see how the project develops, visit the official project site at yorkcentral.uk.
