Businesses in Darlington town centre were plunged into darkness and forced to close after a power outage.

The outage on Friday afternoon (September 11) affected businesses from the corner of Blackwellgate to Natwest on High Row, whose neighbours Poundland remained unaffected.

Businesses said they were told the blackout was due to an 'engineering problem with the power lines,' with power returning around 2pm. Northern Powergrid is yet to confirm the cause.

Signs were seen in the window of Greggs reading "sorry we're closed, no power," as disappointed customers were turned away and the doors closed. St. Teresa’s Hospice charity shop also closed.

  Businesses were forced to close on Friday in Darlington. 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Information from the Northern Powergrid website showed that 25 buildings were impacted by the outage.

  A map showing the power outage in Darlington. 

*(Image: NORTHERN POWERGRID)*

Natwest employee Richard and his manager remained outside the bank, offering customers help on the mobile app and assisting with queries if possible despite systems being down.

Richard said their training for a range of emergency situations had readied the pair to deal with the otherwise chaotic scenes, and explained how branch closure across the area motivated them to turn to mobile services:

“I just helped someone cancel a standing order on their app,” he said.

“Some people travelled here from Bishop Auckland and elsewhere, so it's unfair to turn them away completely without trying to help how we can.”

  Staff at Cooplands on High Row stayed open.

Workers at Deli-cious expressed concerns about their sandwiches and cakes, which had just been restocked, and how the power cut had forced the cafe to lose income after turning away customers.

Cooplands remained open, only accepting cash and operating under a strict 'four-hour rule' in line with food safety guidelines.

Staff were told to monitor temperatures of their products, selling what they were authorised to by regional management.

Northern Powergrid has been contacted for comment.
