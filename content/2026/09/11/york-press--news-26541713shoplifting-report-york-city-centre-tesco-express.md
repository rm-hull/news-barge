Police have been called to a report of shoplifting at a York city centre shop.

A police van was parked outside the Tesco Express in Piccadilly at about 3.15pm on Thursday (September 10).

North Yorkshire Police said it followed a report of shoplifting at the store.

“A report of shoplifting has been fully investigated with no further action to be taken,” a force spokesperson said.

A nearby worker said they saw the van parked with its blue lights on and praised police for “taking prompt action”.

"I saw the police van with its blue lights on and originally wondered if someone had got into difficulty in the water nearby,” said the eyewitness, who did not want to be named.

"It appears the officers were, in fact, investigating an incident at the Tesco store.

"We have had issues with anti-social behaviour in Piccadilly recently so hopefully this was all resolved relatively smoothly.

"We don't want this part of the city to become a haven for crime, so it is good to see the police taking prompt action."
