Police are investigating after a shopfront was damaged in a North Yorkshire town.

Officers have issued a photograph of a man they want to speak to about the damage in Westborough, Scarborough.

North Yorkshire Police said the shopfront was damaged at 11.53pm on Thursday, September 17.

The force said the man pictured may have "information that could help our investigation."

Anyone who recognises the man or who has information that could help police is asked to email [steven.holloway@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:steven.holloway@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting 12260183295.
