Shoppers at one of Teesside's Aldi stores will face a temporary closure next month while the supermarket undergoes a major makeover.

The Billingham supermarket, in Belasis Avenue, will close at 8pm on Saturday, October 17, and reopen on Thursday, October 22.

The work follows a major refurbishment of Aldi's Yarm store in June, which added 353 square metres of floor space, as well as a new entrance lobby, staff room and customer toilet.

An Aldi spokesperson said: “Our Billingham store will temporarily close for refurbishment works to enhance the shopping experience for our customers.

"Shoppers will see a number of upgrades – including an updated store layout, new-look Bakery and Health & Beauty sections, and eco-friendly fridges that will help to reduce our carbon footprint.

“We apologise for any inconvenience caused and thank customers for their patience. Whilst works are being carried out, shoppers can visit our nearby Portrack Lane and Newport Road stores.”

Aldi has advised customers to visit its Stockton, Middlesbrough or Norton branches while refurbishment work takes place.
