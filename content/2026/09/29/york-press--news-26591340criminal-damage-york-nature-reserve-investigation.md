Police are investigating criminal damage at a York nature reserve.

Officers are appealing for information about the damage at the Hassacarr Nature Reserve, near Dunnington.

North Yorkshire Police said a gate was damaged between the afternoon of Sunday, September 20, and Monday, September 21.

The force said it is "particularly appealing for information from anyone who saw this take place or may know the persons involved".

Anyone with information is asked to email [david.ellison@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.ellison@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.

Quote reference 12260190458 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
