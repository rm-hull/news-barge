Two US navy aviators ejected as they were landing their plane on the USS Dwight D Eisenhower aircraft carrier off the coast of Virginia on Monday, and four sailors onboard the vessel were injured in what naval officials characterized as a “mishap”.

The two aviators, assigned to Carrier Air Wing 3, successfully ejected as “a mishap occurred during aircraft recovery operations” and were rescued by search and rescue personnel, the navy said in a short statement.

Both were returned to the ship and were being evaluated by medical personnel, the navy said. The four shipboard personnel also were receiving medical care, including one who was transported to a hospital in the Norfolk, Virginia, area for treatment of non-life-threatening injuries.

The navy did not say which type of two-seater plane was involved in the accident, nor what happened to the plane or what might have caused the accident. The navy did not immediately answer a request for further details.

The nuclear-powered Nimitz-class USS Dwight D Eisenhower was commissioned in 1977.
