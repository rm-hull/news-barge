A County Durham man must pay £200 after admitting he damaged a police vehicle.

Jaydyn Raison, 24, from Mansell Crescent, Peterlee, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on September 25.

Raison damaged the vehicle in Durham on June 30 without lawful excuse. The court said the cost of the damage was unknown.

The court took Raison’s guilty plea into account when sentencing him.

Magistrates ordered him to pay £200 in compensation to Durham Constabulary.

A collection order was also made.
