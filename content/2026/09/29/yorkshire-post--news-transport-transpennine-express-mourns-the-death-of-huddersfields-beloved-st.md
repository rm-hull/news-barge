# TransPennine Express mourns the death of Huddersfield’s beloved station cat Bolt following a road traffic accident

## TransPennine Express (TPE) has announced the passing of Bolt, Huddersfield’s much-loved former station cat after a road traffic accident.

Bolt joined the team at Huddersfield station in 2018 as an apprentice to Felix, the station’s famous senior pest controller, and quickly became a firm favourite with colleagues, customers and visitors from all over the world.

Together, Felix and Bolt became international stars, featuring in two books and an annual charity calendar.

Their popularity also helped raise more than £266,000 for charitable causes, while their shared Facebook page received more than 24 million views each year.

![Bolt the cat. (Pic credit: TransPennine Express)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/news/transport/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


Bolt retired from station life in July 2025, settling into a new home with a former TPE colleague who worked at the station.

Customer experience and transformation director for TPE, Andrew McClements, said: “We are deeply saddened by the loss of Bolt. He was a much-loved member of the Huddersfield station family and brought joy and comfort to so many people over the years.

“From his early days learning from Felix to becoming a star in his own right, Bolt left his paw prints on the hearts of colleagues, customers and fans across the world. He will be very sadly missed.”

Bolt’s former companion Felix, another beloved member of the Huddersfield station team, sadly passed away in 2023.
