Police searching for a missing York woman say they have found a body.

Humberside officers had been searching the area of Dolegate, Skirpenbeck.

A Humberside Police spokesperson said: “We can confirm, as a part of those searches, a body has been located and whilst formal identification is yet to take place, it is believed to be missing Alison from York.

#### Dolegate, Skirpenbeck

“Alison’s family have been informed and are being supported by officers, our thoughts remain with them at this difficult time.

“Initial enquiries have been carried out and Alison’s death is not being treated as suspicious.”

Alison was 61 years old.
