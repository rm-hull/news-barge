A grade II-listed Georgian manor house is one of hundreds of properties going under the hammer this month.

Estate agent Savills has released a catalogue for its largest September auction to date with houses and land worth millions of pounds.

The £2.39m property in Tetsworth, Oxfordshire is one of the most sought after in the portfolio.

Copcourt Manor is set within 4.95 acres and comprises a four-bedroom principal residence, a self-contained flat, a separate cottage, various outbuildings including a gym, equestrian facilities, a tennis court and extensive grounds.

![The manor house has been given a guide price of £2.39m](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/28/16/54/Copcourt-Manor-Savills-Auction-(5)-4fb08add.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Set in a picturesque landscape, surrounded by gardens with mature trees including a cherry and apple orchard, lawns and a moat.

A tree-lined driveway leads to the house, crossing a bridge to a parking area near the property.

![Copcourt Manor is set within 4.95 acres and has a separate flat and cottage](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/28/16/51/Copcourt-Manor-Savills-Auction-(6)-9df1a054.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The main property has a reception hall, dining room, drawing room, a breakfast room, playroom, bedroom with an en suite and a separate dressing room. It also has three further bedrooms and a self contained flat.

The land also boasts a stable yard with six stables and a manège, as well as a garage with space for three cars.

Copcourt Manor is currently vacant and the auction will be held on 29 and 30 September along with 380 other commercial, residential and mixed-use lots across the country.

![The house is surrounded by gardens with mature trees including a cherry and apple orchard, lawns and a charming moat](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/28/16/04/Copcourt-Manor-Savills-Auction-(4)-e0765616.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

One of the houses going under the hammer is a vacant 3,043 sq ft four-bedroom garden apartment in Holland Park, west London. It has a garden, private entrance, with a guide price of £4.85m.

Another is a 10.12-acre former industrial site in Dursley, Gloucestershire, with 71,444 sq ft of accommodation, with industrial buildings, offices, yards, storage land and two residential dwellings, has been offered at a guide price of £1.35m.

Commercial highlights include a substantial freehold mixed-use investment in Soho, central London, comprising a ground-floor barbershop, a basement office and a three-bedroom apartment above. It’s currently let at £173,844 a year and has a £3.25m guide price.

In Putney, south-west London, a vacant virtual freehold office building extending to around 6,000 sq ft over three storeys will feature in the auction with a guide price of £2m.
