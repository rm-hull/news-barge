A SURVIVOR of a grooming gang has been forced to flee her home after receiving abuse and death threats.

Fiona Goddard, 33, was 14 when she was groomed and sexually abused by gangs of men in Bradford while living in a care home.

Nine men were jailed in 2019, but Ms Goddard learned in 2025 that her abusers are to be released early.

Ms Goddard is reported to have said people have started harassing her and have been "threatening to come and deliver me justice."

She said she would get a food delivery and then receive a message from an anonymous account asking how her food was.

"They would message me, telling me that I made up the abuse," she said.

She said the threats intensified after returning home from temporary accommodation.

Ms Goddard said the problem was only going to escalate once her abusers are released from prison; and added that "we can't stay in Bradford" when people are doing this.

She says she feels like any sense of justice has now been taken away from her.

She and her family have now relocated to an Airbnb for safety.

A GoFundMe page set up by Amy Hinchliffe to help cover living costs has raised nearly £13,000.

Ms Hinchliffe said: "Fiona has had to flee for her safety following her experiences of grooming gangs and child sexual exploitation.

"Since then, she and her children have been left living with uncertainty, moving from place to place without the stability they desperately need.

"This isn't simply about having a roof over their heads.

"It is about giving a mother and her children the opportunity to finally feel safe, secure and settled, without constantly having to worry about where they will be next."
