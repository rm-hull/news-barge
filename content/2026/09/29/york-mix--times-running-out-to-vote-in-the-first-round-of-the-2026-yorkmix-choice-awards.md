It’s the last week to vote in the first round of the 2026 **YorkMix Choice Awards** – which of the hundreds of local businesses has your vote?

Sponsored by P.J Staffing, the awards aims to celebrate businesses in our area that are making a difference.

It’s a unique event as there are no judges – all the finalists and winners are all chosen by you!

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2032/03/sponsor-1786314037.png)

PJ Staffing is an award winning staffing agency, providing industry experienced, skilled and reliable Catering, Hospitality & Event Staff across Yorkshire and the UK.

From last-minute event cover to long-term staffing support, thee supply experienced workers who are ready to step in and deliver from day one.

Voting for the first round will **close on Sunday 4 October** at 23:59pm. Then the second round of voting will open and run from Tuesday 6 October and close on Wednesday 28 October at 19:59pm.

The winners will then be announced on **Thursday 29 October** in a grand awards ceremony at the Milner Hotel in York.

Take a look at the award categories below, then read the nominations and vote here.

### Award categories

* **Best Restaurant**
* **Best Pub**
* **Best Bar**
* **Best Gym, Spa or Wellbeing**
* **Best Personal Care, Beauty or Health Care Provider** (From hair dressers to nail salons, opticians to cosmetic companies)
* **Best Day Out** (If you run an attraction or place to take the family, this one is for you)
* **Best Café or Tearoom**
* **Best Takeaway, Street Food or Sandwich shop**
* **Best Place to Stay** (Hotels, B&Bs, camp sites, glamping and more)
* **Best Venue** (Theatres, live music venues, wedding venues, conference centres etc)
* **Best Event** (Festivals, markets, one off events or short seasonal runs such as Halloween or Christmas venues)
* **Best Independent Shop** (For a locally based business within the retail environment)
* **Best Customer Experience** (For a business or individual who excels at providing the best customer journey)
* **Best Professional Services Provider** (Legal services, financial services, estate agents, marketing agencies etc)
* **The Fix It Award** (For trades, mechanics or others who fix things when they go wrong!)
* **Best Wedding or Celebration service or supplier**
* **Travel provider of the year** (Taxi and private hire providers, tour operators, coach companies etc)
* **Best online offering** (For locally based businesses with outstanding online products or services, or a website which aids their day to day business)
* **Best Leisure Time Activity or Supplier** (Acting groups, sports clubs/teams, dance groups, singing clubs and more!)
* **Business Champion Award** (Nominate someone who makes a difference in the wider business community)
