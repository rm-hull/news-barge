Anthony Gordon scored for the second England game in a row.

**England got their first win in the Nations League as Anthony Gordon and Harry Kane scored to beat the Czech Republic 2-0.**

Here's how BBC Sport England reporter **Alex Howell** rated the players. You can see how you scored them below.

**James Trafford:** Made a good save in the second half. Trafford was very calm with the ball at his feet and showed his passing range which pleased Tuchel throughout.**6Trent Alexander-Arnold:** Started his first England game for nearly two years. Not much to do defensively but put in a great ball for the England opener.**7Trevoh Chalobah:** A shaky start from the centre back who lost possession a couple of times and lost out in challenges which frustrated manager Thomas Tuchel. But Chalobah settled and ensured England kept a clean sheet against 10-man Czech Republic.**6Marc Guehi:** The usual reliable performance from Guehi. England didn't have much defending to do but he put in a couple of important blocks.**6Lewis Hall:** The left-back combined well with Anthony Gordon and offered a good outlet for England. Hall was a threat going forward and should have scored in the second half.**7Elliot Anderson:** Dominated the midfield, kept the ball ticking for England and won possession back the few times that the Czech Republic ventured forward. A mature performance.**7Myles Lewis-Skelly:** Started the game well and was given a lot of responsibility in England's build up play. However, Lewis-Skelly was booked for a mis-timed tackle and then taken off at half time.**6Morgan Rogers:** Played Bukayo Saka through with a nice pass early on and hit the post twice. Not given much room by a Czech side who defended very deep.**6Bukayo Saka:** Not much of note to mark for Saka in what was a cagey match as England laboured when breaking down the Czech defence before Gordon's goal.**6Anthony Gordon:** A constant threat in behind and put in a dangerous cross in the first half that deserved to end up with a goal. Scored the opener and continues to show why he is becoming so important for the Three Lions.**8Harry Kane:** A quieter night for Kane who dropped deep and went looking for the ball but the England captain still got on the score sheet with a good finish inside the area in the second half.**7Subs:Eberechi Eze (on 46, Lewis-Skelly):** Added a spark to midfield.**6Jude Bellingham (on 70, Kane):** Took the captains armband and led the line.**6Alex Scott (on 70, Anderson):** Made his England debut.**6Rio Ngumoha (on 71, Saka):** His first thought was to go forward.**6Morgan Gibbs-White (on 81, Gordon):** NA
