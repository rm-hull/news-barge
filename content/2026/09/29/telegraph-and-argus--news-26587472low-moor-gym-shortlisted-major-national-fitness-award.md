A GYM has been shortlisted for a national fitness award.

The Pride Gym, Low Moor, is in the running for Gym of the Year (North) at the National Fitness Awards, which recognise excellence in fitness facilities across the UK.

Terry Holt, owner of The Pride Gym, said: "To be shortlisted is fantastic and testament to the hard work of our team and reflects the amazing community of members we have.

"We strive to continually improve and make the gym a place where people can’t wait to come, and it be one of the highlights of their day.

"We’re all now looking forward to the awards event."

The next stage of judging may include a site visit by a judge and the collection of additional information.

A detailed report will be compiled for each finalist and reviewed by an independent judging panel.

Winners will be announced at a black-tie gala on Friday, November 27, at The Athena in Leicester.

Mike Hewitt, event director for the National Fitness Awards, said: "We have had a record number of entries this year, with the standard across the board being excellent – a true testament to the innovation and strength of the vibrant UK fitness industry."
