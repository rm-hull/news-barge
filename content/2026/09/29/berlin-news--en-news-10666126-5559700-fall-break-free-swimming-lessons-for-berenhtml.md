According to the Senate Department for Education, Youth and Families, a total of 2,420 spots are available for the free intensive swimming courses. Of these, 1,960 spots are reserved for elementary school students. The remaining 460 spots are reserved for students in grades 7 through 10 who cannot swim yet or are not confident swimmers and have not yet earned the bronze swimming badge.
