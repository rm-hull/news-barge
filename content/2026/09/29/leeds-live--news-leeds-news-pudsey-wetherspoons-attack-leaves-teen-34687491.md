# Pudsey Wetherspoons attack leaves teen with serious head injuries - police statement in full

## The teen suffered "serious head injuries" outside of the Crossed Shuttle Wetherspoons pub in Pudsey

Leeds police have issued a CCTV appeal after a teenager was left with "serious head injuries" outside of a Pudsey Wetherspoons.

West Yorkshire Police have released the photos of two men they wish to identify, after a report of grievous bodily harm was made outside the Crossed Shuttle Wetherspoons pub in Manor House Street, on Saturday, September 5, in the early hours of the morning.

According to police, the victim was a teenage male and was rushed to the hospital with serious head injuries after the incident at around 1am.

A spokesperson for the force has urged anyone who may recognise the two men to call 101. The full police statement read: "Police in Leeds have issued images of two men they want to identify following a report of grievous bodily harm outside a pub in Pudsey.

"The incident happened outside the Crossed Shuttle Wetherspoons pub in Manor House Street, on Saturday, September 5, at 1.20am. The victim, a male in his teens, suffered serious head injuries as a result of the incident.

"The victim, a male in his teens, suffered serious head injuries as a result of the incident. Anyone who can identify the pictured men or has information that could assist the investigation is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online. "
