Save the date - Hartlepool Fireworks Spectacular will return to Seaton Carew this autumn.

The free family event, organised by Hartlepool Borough Council, will take place on Sunday, November 1, supported by headline sponsor X-energy.

Councillor Rob Darby, chair of the council's housing, growth, and communities committee, said: "The Hartlepool Fireworks Spectacular is one of the biggest and most eagerly anticipated events in our calendar, bringing together thousands of residents and visitors.

"We are extremely grateful to X-energy for once again coming on board as headline sponsor.

"Its support helps us to deliver an event that people of all ages can enjoy and reflects its commitment to our borough, our communities, and our future."

The programme will feature live entertainment, a children’s fairground, food stalls, and a fireworks display set to music.

Leon Flexman, X-energy’s UK corporate affairs director, said: "We're thrilled to be supporting the Hartlepool Fireworks Spectacular once again and playing a part in one of the borough’s best-loved events."

He added: "We know how much people look forward to this every year and we're delighted to help make it happen.

"Hartlepool has an incredible ability to bring people together and events like this showcase the pride, energy, and spirit that make it such a special place."

Councillor Rob Darby said: "The Fireworks Spectacular creates a wonderful atmosphere, celebrating the very best of Hartlepool, and we look forward to welcoming everyone to what promises to be another truly memorable event."
