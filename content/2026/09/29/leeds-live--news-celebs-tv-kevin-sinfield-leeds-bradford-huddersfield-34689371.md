# Kevin Sinfield in Leeds, Bradford and Huddersfield on Wednesday - exact streets and times

## Kevin Sinfield's route through West Yorkshire tomorrow as exact times and streets revealed

Kevin Sinfield will run through Leeds, Bradford and Huddersfield tomorrow as his final 7 in 7 challenge comes through West Yorkshire. The rugby league legend is taking on his seventh and final annual challenge to raise awareness and money for people affected by motor neurone disease.

Sinfield and his team are completing an ultra marathon every day for seven days, visiting all 12 English Super League grounds before finishing at Old Trafford on Saturday. The challenge began in Hull on Sunday and has already seen Sinfield travel through Yorkshire, with Tuesday's route taking him from York to Wakefield.

Wednesday will see the team leave the DIY Kitchens Stadium in Wakefield and make their way across West Yorkshire. The route includes a return to Headingley Stadium, a stop at Bradford Bulls' home at Odsal and a finish at Huddersfield's Accu Stadium. The challenge has a particularly personal connection to the area, with Sinfield paying tribute to his former Leeds Rhinos team mate and close friend Rob Burrow, who died from MND last year.

The team has raised more than £11 million for the MND community through previous challenges, with six charities set to benefit from this year's fundraising.

## Kevin Sinfield's West Yorkshire route and times

The times below are approximate arrival times published by Leeds Rhinos. Kevin Sinfield is expected to stop at each checkpoint before continuing on his route.

### Wakefield

Sinfield will set off from the DIY Kitchens Stadium in Wakefield at **8.40am** .

He will then head towards Leeds, with the first roadside checkpoint at Lawns Lane at around **9.14am** .

He is expected to leave that checkpoint at around **9.40am** .

### Middleton Grove

The team is due to reach the roadside checkpoint at **Middleton Grove at around 10.13am** .

Sinfield is expected to remain there until around **10.40am** before continuing towards Leeds.

### Headingley

Sinfield will then make his way to **Headingley Stadium**, where he is due to arrive at around** 11.14am** .

The team is expected to leave Headingley at approximately **11.40am** .

This is expected to be an emotional return for the former Leeds Rhinos captain, who spent much of his playing career at the stadium.

### Pudsey

The next checkpoint is **Pudsey Civic Hall**, with Sinfield expected to arrive at around** 12.14pm** .

He is scheduled to leave at approximately **12.40pm** before continuing towards Bradford.

### Bradford

Sinfield's route then takes him towards Bradford and the **Cedar Court Hotel** .

He is expected to arrive there at around **1.16pm**, with the scheduled departure time around** 1.40pm** .

The challenge route also takes in **Odsal** , the home of Bradford Bulls, although the published checkpoint list gives Cedar Court Hotel as the scheduled stop.

### Appleton Academy

The next published checkpoint is **Appleton Academy**, where Sinfield is expected at around** 2.15pm** .

The team is scheduled to leave at approximately **2.40pm** and continue towards Huddersfield.

### Huddersfield Road

Sinfield is then expected at the roadside bus stop on **Huddersfield Road, just before the M62 bridge**, at around** 3.14pm** .

The published schedule gives a departure time of approximately **3.40pm** .

### Huddersfield finish

The final stop is **Accu Stadium** , the home of Huddersfield Giants.

Sinfield is due to arrive at around **4.02pm** , bringing the West Yorkshire leg of his final 7 in 7 challenge to an end.

There is an extra personal reason for the Huddersfield finish, with the team highlighting Huddersfield Town's connection to Marcus Stewart, a close friend of the 7 in 7 team who is living with MND.

The published times are approximate and could change during the day depending on how the run progresses.
