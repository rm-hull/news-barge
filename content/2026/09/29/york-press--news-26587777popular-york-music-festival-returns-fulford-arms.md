A pub in York is set to host the eighth One For The Road festival next month.

The event, being held at The Fulford Arms, will showcase an array of punk, folk, and rock bands from around the world.

Running from October 16 to 18, the festival is a staple in York's live music calendar.

Festival-goers can look forward to a performance from rising Irish punk producer Meryl Streek, headlining Friday night in celebration of his new album, Songs They Don't Teach You At School.

Saturday will see festival favourites Headsticks take the stage, promising an unmissable performance.

Local artist Harrison Rimmer, who has played every year since the festival's inception, will close the weekend with his first headline set.

York's music scene will be represented throughout the festival, with performances from The Bad Nights Sleep, Texas Forever, Lyon Estates, and folk duo Sing Ramona Sing.

Jasmine Lowe, a rising singer-songwriter, will also perform.

International artists are also making their mark on the festival, with Australian folk-punk artist Blake Cateris making his One For The Road debut as part of his UK and European tour.

Canadian artists Northcote and Rob Moir will also be performing, touring the UK around their appearances in York.

Dutch singer-songwriter Tim Vantol, who has performed on major festival stages across Europe, will complete the international line-up.

One For The Road Festival is celebrated for its community spirit, with many attendees returning annually irrespective of the performers.

The festival continues to be a highlight of York's vibrant music scene.
