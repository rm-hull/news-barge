A LONG established Bradford folk band will perform at two of the district’s museums next month.

May Contain Notes will perform at Bradford Industrial Museum on October 18 at 2pm, and Cliffe Castle Museum in Keighley on October 25 at 2pm.

The Bradford‑based acoustic band is known for their evocative, provocative and reflective performances — always delivered with warmth, humour and a strong sense of storytelling.

The group performs a wide mix of material, from folk and contemporary songs to protest pieces and original work, often blending unaccompanied harmony singing with acoustic arrangements.

The current line‑up of three musicians brings together an eclectic mix of brass, banjo, guitar and percussion, alongside rich a cappella harmony. Their music explores the world around us with insight and playfulness, offering audiences a thoughtful and engaging experience.

Bot performances are free.

For more information visit [https://bradfordmuseums.org/whats-on/](https://bradfordmuseums.org/whats-on/)
