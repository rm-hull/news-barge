Police are investigating damage at a York nature reserve

Damage was caused to the gate of Hassacar Nature Reserve in Dunnington between the afternoon of Sunday 20 September and the morning of Monday 21 September.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re particularly appealing for information from anyone who saw this take place or may know the persons involved.

#### Hassacar Nature Reserve, York

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
