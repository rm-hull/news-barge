CHILDREN will be able to explore Bradford’s most haunted building this Halloween.

Spooky tours will be taking place at Bolling Hall Museum, home to the spectral White Lady, on October 28 from 5pm to 7pm.

As well as the tours, there will be spell-making activities and Halloween excitement.

Bolloween is a family event designed for children aged 7 to 12. All children must be accompanied by a responsible adult, and adults must be attending with a child.

There are six tours available**,** each lasting approximately one hour.

People are asked to select their preferred tour time when booking.

Tour times are 5.30pm, 6.00pm, 6.30pm, 7.00pm, 7.30pm and 8.00pm.

To book a slot and for more information visit [https://bradfordmuseums.org/event/bolloween/](https://bradfordmuseums.org/event/bolloween/)
