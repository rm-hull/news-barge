A FRESH set of street food flavours has arrived just in time for autumn.

Trinity Kitchen in Leeds has welcomed five new independent vendors to its line-up, serving everything from bao buns and burgers to tacos and Thai fried chicken.

Josie Towning, food and beverage manager at Trinity Leeds, said: "For autumn we wanted to bring a mixture of warming dishes whilst also keeping things light and fresh with bao buns and tacos.

"As always, we pride ourselves on offering an exciting range of cuisines, bold flavours and a place where people can sit down and enjoy their favourite meals or even discover something new."

The new vendors include Ka Bao, Shoot the Bull, Spuds ’n’ Bros, Tikks Thai Kitchen, and El Piloto.

El Piloto, a new addition to Trinity Kitchen, specialises in Mexican street food with fiery tacos, quesadillas, and loaded fries.

The latest line-up will be available until November 22.

For more information, visit trinityleeds.com.
