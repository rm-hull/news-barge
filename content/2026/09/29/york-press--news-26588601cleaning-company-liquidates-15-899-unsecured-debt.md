A specialist cleaning company in North Yorkshire has gone into liquidation with debts exceeding £15,000.

Z Booths Ltd of Honey Garth, Ripon, entered into a creditor’s voluntary liquidation on September 8.

The liquidator is Manchester-based Clark Bell Ltd.

Companies House records the sole director as Zachary Booth.

A liquidator's report known as a statement of affairs outlines the financial situation at the company.

It said the company has no assets and no preferential or secondary creditors – those who are paid first and second respectively.

However, there were unsecured creditors who were owed £15,899 between them.

They consisted of a trade and expense creditor owed £10,793.43, a bank owed £3,879.73 and the company director owed £1,226.00.

This gave a shortfall of £15,899.29.

With the business having £1 in issued and called up capital, this gave a total deficit of £15,900.29.
