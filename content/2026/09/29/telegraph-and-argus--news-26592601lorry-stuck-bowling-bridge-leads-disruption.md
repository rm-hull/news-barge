A lorry became stuck under a railway bridge, causing travel disruption for road and rail users.

The vehicle struck the bridge on Birksland Street in Bowling, leading to delays and cancellations on train services between Leeds and Bradford Interchange.

Northern said: "There is disruption between Leeds and Bradford Interchange due to a road vehicle colliding with a bridge.

"Train services running through these stations will be cancelled, delayed or revised."

Services resumed later that afternoon.

Affected stations included Leeds, Bramley, New Pudsey, Bradford Interchange, Hebden Bridge and Halifax.
