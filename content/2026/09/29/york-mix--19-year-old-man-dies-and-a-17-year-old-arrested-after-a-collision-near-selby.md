A 19-year-old man has died after a collision with a tractor near Selby.

It happened at approximately 7.30pm yesterday evening (Monday 28 September) on the A163 at Barlby, near the junction with Whitemoor Lane.

It involved a blue Range Rover SVR and a Massey Ferguson tractor towing a large trailer loaded with hay bales.

Both vehicles are believed to have been travelling eastbound when the Range Rover collided with the rear of the trailer.

The 19-year-old was travelling in the Range Rover and died at the scene.

His family have been informed and are being supported by specialist officers.

A second 19-year-old man who was also travelling in the Range Rover remains in hospital.

A 17-year-old boy has been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and remains in police custody.

The driver of the tractor was also taken to hospital for treatment following the collision. He is helping officers with their enquiries.

The A163 was closed for most of the night as officers conducted investigation work at the scene.

They are appealing to anyone who witnessed the collision, saw either vehicle in the moments leading up to it, or has relevant dashcam footage, to come forward.
