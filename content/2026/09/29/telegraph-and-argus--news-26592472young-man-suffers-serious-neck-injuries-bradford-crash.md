A YOUNG man was rushed to hospital with serious neck injuries after a crash between a car and a motorbike in Bradford.

The collision, involving a black Vauxhall Corsa and a motorbike, occurred on Brighouse Road in Queensbury at 10pm on Sunday.

The Corsa passenger, an 18-year-old man, was seriously injured.

Two 18-year-old men and a 17-year-old boy were arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by dangerous driving.

They have since been bailed pending further enquiries, a spokesperson for the force confirmed.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers are keen to speak to anyone who witnessed the collision or who saw the motorbike being removed prior to police arrival."

"Anyone with information is asked to contact police via Live Chat at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting log 1725 of September 27."
