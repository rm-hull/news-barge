Emergency services were called to a street in Bradford during the morning rush hour, according to a witness.

According to witnesses on the scene, the incident happened on Dawson Lane near Wakefield Road, with an ambulance seen attending the scene.

## Witness describes emergency response

One passer-by said they spotted emergency services while travelling to work and noticed a crowd gathered nearby.

They said: "I was driving on my way to work. It was busy and slow rush hour traffic on Wakefield Road.

"I looked over on to the side road and saw an ambulance with flashing lights."

The witness said several people appeared to have gathered around the incident.

"There was a crowd gathered around and there appeared to be a person lying on the ground," they added.

## 'Really concerning'

The passer-by said they were shocked by what they saw during the morning journey.

"I was shocked to see so many people around," they said. "It's really concerning. I hope the person will be all right."

## Emergency services approached for comment

It is currently unclear what happened or the condition of the person involved.

The Telegraph & Argus has approached West Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service for further information.
