The Orchid at the Studley Hotel in Harrogate is opening for Saturday lunch for the first time in its 25-year history.

The new opening follows the restaurant’s success at the Harrogate Business Awards, where it was named Best Restaurant following a public vote.

To celebrate, The Orchid is offering diners 20% off food from its à la carte menu Monday to Thursday and at Saturday lunchtime.

Plus - in what is also a first for the restaurant - The Orchid is also set to start offering Saturday lunch.

Ryan is the general manager at the Orchid Restaurant at the Studley Hotel in Harrogate.

He told Your Harrogate:

> "By popular demand, The Orchid is now opening for Saturday lunch for the first time in its history.
>
> "We’re really excited to welcome both familiar faces and new guests on Saturday lunchtimes."

The Orchid was one of 14 winners at this year's Harrogate Business Awards.

Ryan said:

> “What makes the award particularly meaningful because it was a public vote, and receiving 42% of the vote means so much to all of us.
>
> "We were thrilled simply to be nominated alongside such fantastic independent restaurants in Harrogate, so to win is a wonderful honour."

He added:

> "This award belongs to our whole team, past and present, and to the families behind them who have supported us throughout the years. Most importantly, it belongs to our guests.
>
> "Their loyalty has allowed The Orchid to remain part of Harrogate for 25 years, and we never take that support for granted.
>
> "We’d love people to come and enjoy our A la carte menu, including our specials, and celebrate this award together."
