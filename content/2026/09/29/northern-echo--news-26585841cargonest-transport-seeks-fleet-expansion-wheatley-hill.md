A haulage firm has applied to expand operations by increasing its fleet at a County Durham site, according to a public notice.

Cargonest Transport Ltd is seeking permission to add four extra goods vehicles and four trailers to its operating centre at Rock Farm Storage, Wheatley Hill.

The company, based on Thornholme Road, Sunderland, has submitted the application to the Traffic Commissioner.

The application details that the additional four vehicles and four trailers will be based at the Cargonest Transport Ltd site at Rock Farm Storage, Wheatley Hill.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

Owners or occupiers of land near the operating centre who believe their use or enjoyment of the land may be affected have been invited to make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner.

These must be submitted within 21 days of the notice, published on September 23, and should state the reasons for any objections.

A copy of the representations must also be sent to Cargonest Transport Ltd, Rock Farm Storage, Wheatley Hill, DH6 3NL."

A full guide to making representations is available on the government’s website.
