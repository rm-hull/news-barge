FURTHER casting has been announced for this year’s pantomime at Bradford’s Alhambra Theatre.

Further casting has been announced for The Pantomime Adventures of Peter Pan, which will run from Saturday, December 5, 2026, to Sunday, January 17, 2027.

Jamie John and Sarah Pearson will return as Tink and Wendy, with Mya Louise Fenton making her professional debut in the title role of Peter Pan.

They join the previously announced cast, including Billy Pearce as Smee, Myra DuBois as Magical MerMyra, and Ryan Thomas as Captain Hook.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21509115.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mya Louise Fenton *(Image: Supplied)*

Mr John said: "I had such a brilliant time here last year, so I’m thrilled to be returning to the Alhambra Theatre this Christmas.

"The audiences and the team at the Alhambra are fantastic and I can’t wait to be back – this time as Tink and bring my own special flair to the role."

Ms Pearson, who will appear in her fourth Bradford pantomime, said: "It’s always so special to perform as part of my local panto and there’s nothing quite like performing for a Yorkshire audience.

"I can’t wait to be back on the Alhambra Theatre stage this year, it wouldn’t be Christmas without it."
