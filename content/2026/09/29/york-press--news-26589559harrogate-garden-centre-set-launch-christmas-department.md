A garden centre is to launch its Christmas department with a weekend of festive fun.

Harrogate Garden Centre, part of the UK’s largest family-run group British Garden Centres, will kick off the season with a Christmas launch on October 10 and October 11.

Zoe Sutcliffe, centre manager at Harrogate Garden Centre, said: "Christmas at British Garden Centres is about so much more than decorations; it’s about bringing people together and making the season the best experience for everyone.

"Alongside our expanded Fern Christmas range and a fantastic selection of gifts for all ages, you will find the perfect present, find inspiration for your home, or make memories; there’s something special for everyone."

The launch weekend will feature the debut of the expanded Fern Christmas and Lighting collection, exclusive to British Garden Centres.

The range includes lights, decorations, and home accessories designed to help families celebrate the season.

Visitors will also be able to browse a wide variety of toys, games, puzzles, homeware, and pet gifts.

The centre’s gardening department will be fully stocked with winter plants and living gifts for plant lovers.

Family Card members will receive an exclusive 15 per cent discount across the Christmas range during the launch weekend.

British Garden Centres is the largest family-owned garden centre group in the UK, operating 80 centres nationwide.

The group is owned by the Stubbs family, who also own Woodthorpe Leisure Park in Lincolnshire.

The business began in 1990 with the opening of Woodthorpe Garden Centre by brothers Charles and Robert Stubbs.

It has expanded rapidly since 2018, growing through the acquisition of additional garden centres.

British Garden Centres now spans locations from Carmarthen to Ramsgate, Wimborne to East Durham.

The group employs more than 3,500 people across its garden centres, restaurants, growing nurseries, distribution centres, leisure park, and Woody’s Restaurant & Bar.

The Christmas launch is part of this year’s campaign, themed Where Magic Begins.

Visitors are encouraged to share their festive experiences on social media using the hashtag #MagicBegins.

Harrogate Garden Centre is promoting its Christmas themes as ideal for creating both classic and contemporary holiday looks.

The centre is described as a one-stop destination for gift shopping, home décor, and seasonal inspiration.

British Garden Centres says it remains committed to delivering value, variety, and a welcoming atmosphere across its sites.

The group continues to focus on customer experience while expanding its product offerings.

More information about Harrogate Garden Centre and its Christmas range is available on the British Garden Centres website.

Customers can also follow British Garden Centres on Facebook, Twitter (@BGCentres), and Instagram (@BritishGardenCentres).
