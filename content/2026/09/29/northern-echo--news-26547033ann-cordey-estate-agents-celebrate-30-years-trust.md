Respected property specialists Ann Cordey Estate Agents are celebrating more than three decades of helping Darlington stay on the move.

For more than 30 years, Ann Cordey Estate Agents has been at the heart of the Darlington property market, helping thousands of people take the next step on their home-moving journey. The independent estate agency with a passion for exceptional customer service founded by Ann in 1996, has grown into one of the area’s most respected property specialists. Run now by owner and director Jayne Saddique, it’s recognised for its trusted reputation, local knowledge and personal approach.

While the property market has experienced its fair share of economic challenges, changing legislation and evolving customer expectations over the last three decades, one thing has remained constant – Ann Cordey Estate Agents' commitment to putting people first.

From first-time buyers searching for their dream home to families upsizing, investors growing their portfolios and homeowners looking to downsize, the team has guided generations of clients through one of life’s biggest decisions with professionalism, honesty and genuine care.

‘Reaching this milestone is something we're incredibly proud of,’ says Jayne, who took over the business from Ann six years ago. ‘More than anything, it’s a celebration of our clients. Many of the people who trusted us to sell their home 20 or 30 years ago have returned to us again, and now we're helping their children. That loyalty means everything to us and is something we never take for granted.’

Operating from its Duke Street office in the heart of Darlington, the business has built a trusted reputation by combining traditional values with modern marketing. Professional photography, high-quality property presentation, extensive online advertising, social media campaigns and dedicated sales progression ensure every client benefits from a service designed to maximise results while making the moving process as smooth and stress-free as possible.

The team believes that estate agency is about far more than bricks and mortar. It is about listening, understanding people’s circumstances and offering honest advice every step of the way. That philosophy has earned Ann Cordey Estate Agents countless recommendations and repeat instructions, with much of the company's business continuing to come from personal referrals and returning clients.

However, this landmark anniversary is not simply an opportunity to reflect on the past. It also marks the beginning of an exciting new chapter for the business. Investment in technology, marketing and staff development will ensure Ann Cordey Estate Agents remains at the forefront of the local property market, while maintaining the personal relationships that have always set the business apart.

Recognising the changing needs of the local property market, Ann Cordey Estate Agents has recently expanded into residential lettings, offering landlords and tenants the same high standards of customer service that have become synonymous with the company’s sales department. The move enables the business to provide a complete property service, supporting clients whether they are buying, selling, letting or investing.

‘The lettings market has evolved significantly in recent years, and we saw an opportunity to bring our customer-focused approach to landlords and tenants across Darlington,’ adds Jayne. ‘Our aim is to build the lettings department with the same principles that have made our sales business successful – honesty, communication and delivering exceptional service.’

Looking ahead, the ambition is clear. While the company is proud of everything it has achieved over the past 30 years, it has no intention of standing still.

The team’s vision is to strengthen its position as one of Darlington’s leading independent estate agents and to continue earning the trust of homeowners for generations to come. From its beginnings in 1996 to today, one thing has never changed: helping people move with confidence. More than three decades on, Ann Cordey Estate Agents continues to prove that experience, integrity and exceptional customer care never go out of style. As the business celebrates this remarkable milestone, its focus is firmly on the future — continuing to innovate, expanding its services, supporting the local community and striving to remain one of Darlington’s top estate agents for the next 30 years and beyond.
