A person fell into the river in York late last night.

Emergency services and the York Rescue Boat team were called to the incident at the **River Foss** off**Huntington Road** at**11.43pm**.

York and Acomb fire crews went to the scene.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews arrived and liaised with police, paramedics and the York Rescue Boat team who were also in attendance and had located the person stood on the river bank.

“Crews set up a triple extension ladder for the person to climb safely out the river.

“The incident was left with ambulance and police.”
