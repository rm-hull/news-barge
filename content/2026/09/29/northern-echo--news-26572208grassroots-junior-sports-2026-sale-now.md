The Grassroots Junior Sports 2026 supplement is on sale with The Northern Echo on Tuesday, September 29, celebrating junior sports teams from across the region.

Packed with team photographs from a wide variety of sports, the publication recognises the young players, coaches, volunteers and clubs who make grassroots sport such an important part of local communities.

* If you miss out, you can order a copy online from the Newsquest Store: Order your Grassroots Junior Sports 2026 supplement.

Whether your child, grandchild, family member or local team appears in the supplement, it's a fantastic keepsake capturing another year of junior sport.

You can pick up your copy with The Northern Echo from your local newsagent.
