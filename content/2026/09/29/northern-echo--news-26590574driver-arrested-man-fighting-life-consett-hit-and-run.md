A man was left fighting for his life and another was arrested after a suspected hit-and-run in County Durham.

Sherburn Terrace in Consett was closed after a pedestrian was hit by a van at around 9.45pm on Monday (September 28).

The victim, a 33-year-old man, suffered serious head injuries and was taken to the Royal Victoria Infirmary in Newcastle for treatment.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21513100.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police on Sherburn Terrace, Consett *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A 29-year-old man, was identified, arrested and taken into police custody where he remains.

The road remains closed whilst officers conduct their enquiries.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "The investigation is ongoing, and officers are appealing for anyone with information, CCTV or dashcam footage to come forward.

"If you can help, call 101 quoting incident reference number 493 of September 28."

Pictures show a large cordon in place while a police van guards the scene.
