FUEL in Bradford has now surpassed the £10 a gallon mark forcing drivers to shop around for the best prices.

According to the Fuel Finder comparison site, a gallon of diesel in the Bradford district now costs an astonishing £9.06.

A gallon of premium diesel will set drivers back an average of £9.88.

But the most expensive, the BP service station on Keighley Road, Bingley, is charging £10.13 for a gallon of premium diesel.

Petrol costs less - because diesel refining capacity has been hit by the war in the Gulf - but there are still savings to be made if you shop around.

To help you get the best deal on your petrol or diesel, we've compiled a list of the cheapest garages in and around Bradford, according to petrolprices.com.

Petrol prices get their information directly from garages and from users.

## Cheapest unleaded around Bradford

* Murco Bingley (Sun Garage) 166.9p
* Shell Whetley Hill (Whetley Hill Service Station) 167.9p
* Shell Manningham Lane (Valley Parade Service Station) 168.9p
* BP Canal Road (Canal Road) 168.9p
* Tesco Bradford Extra 168.9p
