The Telegraph & Argus is once again on the hunt for the district’s best beauty salon – and today we can reveal our list of finalists.

Bradford is home to some top-class salons, whether it’s a revitalising facial, a fresh set of nails, or a full face of makeup you’re after.

After receiving hundreds of nominations, we can now reveal the top ten shortlist for our Best Beauty Salon prize 2026.

Last year's winner, The Perks of Beauty in Shipley, will be aiming for back-to-back titles after making the shortlist for this year's competition.

The top ten finalists are

* Beauty & Aesthetics by Emma JB – Granby Barn, 90 High Street, Queensbury, Bradford, BD13 2PD
* Beauty First RR – 955 Thornton Road, Bradford, BD8 0JD
* Bliss Beauty – 9 Stony Lane, Eccleshill, Bradford, BD2 2HL
* Blush 22 Hair & Beauty Lounge – 22 High Street, Idle, Bradford, BD10 8NN
* F S Beauty – 16 Newburn Road, Bradford, BD7 3JQ
* Faces by MSK – 73 Odsal Road, Wibsey, Bradford, BD6 1PN
* House of Laser – 7 Hawes Mount, Bradford, BD5 9AX
* Radiant by Rosie – Unit 46A, Albion Mills Business Centre, Albion Road, Greengates, Bradford, BD10 9TQ
* The Beauty Loft – 2 Frank Street, Bradford, BD 3BT
* The Perks of Beauty – 22 Commercial Street, Shipley, BD18 3SP

Over the coming days, the Telegraph & Argus is aiming to publish a profile of each of these businesses to help readers decide which they should vote for.

To have your say in the destination of the award, all you have to do is pick up a copy of the T&A any day between now and Saturday, October 10 and then fill out the coupon printed in that edition.

Votes will be counted shortly after voting closes.

Nigel Burton, Editor of the T&A, said: "We had an excellent response to our request for nominations and we're delighted to be able to reveal the shortlist.

"Congratulations to all the fantastic businesses that have made it into the Top 10. Now, we're inviting readers to have their say on whihc of the finalists should be crowned as the best beauty salon 2026."
