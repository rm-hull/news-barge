A mobility charity in York has won a new adult tricycle worth £1,000.

ShopMobility York was named the winner of Jorvik Tricycles’ charity giveaway.

The competition was open to charities and social enterprises across Yorkshire and aimed to support organisations promoting sustainable and inclusive mobility.

Joanne Ellis, manager at ShopMobility York, said: "We are absolutely delighted to have been chosen as the winner of Jorvik Tricycles’ community giveaway.

"This fantastic tricycle will be a wonderful addition to ShopMobility York and will help us to continue supporting people to get out and about, enjoy York and stay connected with their community.

"We are incredibly grateful to Jorvik Tricycles for their generosity and for recognising the work we do."

Based in the Coppergate Centre car park, ShopMobility York has provided affordable mobility equipment for more than 20 years.

The charity helps people of all abilities navigate the city with confidence and independence.

James Walker, founder and CEO of Jorvik Tricycles, said: "The work ShopMobility York is doing is truly inspiring, and we’re thrilled to support its mission.

"At Jorvik, we believe everyone should have the opportunity to experience the freedom and enjoyment that cycling can bring, regardless of age or ability."
