A popular Bradford takeaway has reported a burglary at its Duckworth Lane premises.

Photographs from the scene show damage to a window at the Mother Hubbard's branch, with black plastic sheeting visible behind the glass following the incident.

## Police called to burglary report

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police were called to reports of a commercial burglary at premises in Duckworth Lane, Bradford, at 10.26am yesterday (28 September)."

No further details about the incident have yet been released.

![Duckworth Lane in Bradford, where officers were called to reports of a commercial burglary at a local business.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21513786.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Duckworth Lane in Bradford, where officers were called to reports of a commercial burglary at a local business. *(Image: T&A)*

## Damage visible at premises

Images taken outside the takeaway show apparent damage to a ground-floor window at the corner of the building.

One photograph shows black sheeting covering part of the window from the inside, while a wider image shows the affected section of the premises on the busy Duckworth Lane thoroughfare.

Mother Hubbard's is a well-known fish and chip chain with branches across Yorkshire and beyond.

![A damaged window at the Mother Hubbard's takeaway on Duckworth Lane following the reported break-in.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21513787.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A damaged window at the Mother Hubbard's takeaway on Duckworth Lane following the reported break-in. *(Image: T&A)*

## Enquiries ongoing

It is not currently known whether anything was taken during the reported burglary.

Anyone with information about the incident can contact West Yorkshire Police via 101 or through the force's online reporting channels.
