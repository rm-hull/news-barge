*Update: He has been arrested.*

A HUNT has been launched for a man who has broken a domestic violence protection order.

North Yorkshire Police have launched a search for a 39-year-old man.

He is described by police as "a white man of slim build with short hair" and is believed to be in Selby.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate the man and we are now appealing for your help to find him."

They added that anyone who sees him is urged to call 999 immediately.

If you have any other information that could help, please contact North Yorkshire Police online or call 101. Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through their website.
