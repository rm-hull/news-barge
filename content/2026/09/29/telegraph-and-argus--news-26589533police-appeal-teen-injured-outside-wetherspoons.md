Police are appealing for help to identify two men after a teenage boy was seriously injured outside a Wetherspoons pub.

The report of grievous bodily harm happened by the Crossed Shuttle on Manor House Street in Pudsey on Saturday, September 5 at around 1.20am.

The teen sustained serious head injuries.

## Appeal to identify two men

West Yorkshire Police has now issued photographs of two men it wants to identify as part of its enquiries.

Officers believe the men may be able to assist with the investigation.

Anyone who recognises the men, or who has information that could help police, is being urged to come forward.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21513155.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Officers are appealing for information after a teenager suffered serious head injuries in an incident on Manor House Street, Pudsey. *(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

## How to contact police

Anyone with information is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or via the force's online Live Chat service.

Those getting in touch should quote reference 13260505419.

Police are particularly keen to hear from anyone who was in the area of the Crossed Shuttle Wetherspoons pub at the time of the incident and may have witnessed what happened.
