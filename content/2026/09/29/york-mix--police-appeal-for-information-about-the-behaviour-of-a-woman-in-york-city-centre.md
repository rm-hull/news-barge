Police have put out an appeal for information in relation to the behaviour of a woman in York.

The incident happened in the city centre around High Ousegate, Spurriergate and Coney Street between 4:50pm and 6:30pm yesterday, Monday 28th September.

York Police say: “If you were a witness or were personally a victim of this individuals behaviour and have not already reported the matter to us, please contact us via the following methods.”

No further details have been released.

Please quote reference 12260190833 when providing information.

• Email: [email protected]

• Online: Visit the website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’ – – link below in the comments.

• Live chat: Available via the website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm

If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.
