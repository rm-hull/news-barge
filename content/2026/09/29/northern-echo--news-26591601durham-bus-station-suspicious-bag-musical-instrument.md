A 'suspicious bag' that partly closed Durham Bus Station turned out to be a musical instrument, police have confirmed.

Officers from Durham Police were called to the bus station on North Road at around 12.30pm on Tuesday (September 29), following reports of a man carrying a 'suspicious bag'.

During the incident, the bus station was partly closed to allow officers to conduct searches, which impacted buses arriving and departing from the station.

![Durham Bus Station](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21483724.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Durham Bus Station *(Image: NEWSQUEST)*

However, in a statement, Durham Police has confirmed that its enquiries now suggest that the object was a musical instrument, and thanked the member of the public who alerted the police to the item.

A spokesperson for the police force said: "We received a call just before 12.30pm today reporting a man carrying a suspicious bag at Durham Bus Station.
