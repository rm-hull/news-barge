Kaiser Chiefs have revealed their special guests when they perform in North Yorkshire next summer.

Indie rockers White Lies will take to the stage alongside the Leeds band when they headline Scarborough Open Air Theatre on Friday, June 11.

Tickets for the much-anticipated show will go on sale at 9am tomorrow, Tuesday, September 29, via ticketmaster.co.uk and scarboroughopenairtheatre.com.

White Lies formed in Ealing as teenagers before releasing their debut album To Lose My Life ...in 2009.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21511180.jpg?type=mds-article-620)White Lies will join Kaiser Chiefs at Scarborough. *(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

Over the past decade, the band has gained international recognition with sold-out tours in Europe and beyond, including a sold-out show at Mexico City's Pepsi Center in 2023.

Their last two album tours also saw them play multiple sold-out Brixton Academy shows and the Hammersmith Apollo in London.

The upcoming Scarborough date marks a return for Kaiser Chiefs, who last headlined at the seaside venue in 2021.

The band, comprising Ricky Wilson (vocals), Simon Rix (bass), Andrew 'Whitey' White (guitar), Nick 'Peanut' Baines (keyboards), and Vijay Mistry (drums), have been influential in the British music scene since their formation in 2000.

Known for hits such as Oh My God, I Predict A Riot, Ruby, and Never Miss A Beat, the band has released eight original studio albums.

Kaiser Chiefs are the latest headline artist announced for 2027 at Scarborough Open Air Theatre, which will also see performances from Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti and James Morrison.

Simply Red will also perform after announcing details of their Summer Funk '27 UK and European tour, which will include a date at **Scarborough** Open Air Theatre (OAT).

For more information go to scarboroughopenairtheatre.com.
