Stockeld Park's Christmas Adventure runs 21 November to 24 December. Santa and illuminations from £21 an adult; lights and maze from £18.50, to 3 January.

Stockeld Park’s Christmas Adventure opens on **Saturday 21 November 2026** and
runs to **Christmas Eve**. The illuminations, the ice rink and the park’s
play areas stay open after Christmas, to **Sunday 3 January 2027**
(Stockeld Park: Christmas).

## The quick answer

* **Christmas Adventure:** Saturday 21 November to Thursday 24 December 2026.
* **Illuminations, ice skating and the park:** to Sunday 3 January 2027.
* **Santa’s Grotto** is in the Gold and Silver tickets.**Silver** starts at £21 an adult and £31 a child.
* **Cheapest ticket:** Illuminations, Forest and Maze, from £18.50.
* **Where:** on the A661 between Wetherby and Spofforth, sat nav LS22 4AN. The number 7 bus from Harrogate stops outside the gates.
* **Tickets are on sale now.** The park is closed until 3 October while it sets up for Halloween.

The details that change the price:

* **Silver has peak and off-peak tickets.** Off-peak is Monday to Thursday, up to 17 December.
* **Under-2s** are free for the main activities, but need a ticket for the Grotto and the show unless they are babes in arms. The park prices those from £18 on Gold and £14 on Silver.
* **Add-ons:** ice skating is £6 a person on the Gold and Illuminations tickets. The Playhive indoor play centre is from £4.25 on Gold and from £3.75 on the others. On the midweek meal deal, Santa’s Grotto can be added at £12 a child and £2 an adult.
* **Tinsel Tots**, the weekday deal for pre-schoolers, is from £9 a person with one activity and £3.50 for a second, and Santa can be added at the same £12 and £2 subject to availability. The park gives two different date ranges for it on the same page, 1 to 18 December and 30 November to 17 December, so check the date when booking.
* **Memberships:** an off-peak Christmas membership at £45 a person covers unlimited visits on selected dates to 31 December, and an ice skating membership at £60 runs from 21 November to 21 February 2027.
* **One free essential companion** is allowed for every paying disabled guest.

Under-16s must be with an adult aged 18 or over at all times. Dogs are not allowed, apart from assistance dogs.

## Santa’s Grotto and the show

The grotto is a walk through a North Pole village, a “magic” lift to Santa’s
log cabin, Mrs Claus, and then Santa. Each family meets him on its own, not in
a group, and every child gets a photo opportunity and a gift. The park says
Santa is in the grotto **until 7pm**, and suggests midweek slots if you want it
quieter
(Stockeld Park: Christmas).

The Christmas show, only in the Gold ticket, is TaleGate Theatre’s **Jack and
the Beanstalk**, a panto with sing-alongs and magic tricks.

## The illuminations and the ice rink

After dark the Enchanted Forest woodland is lit with installations,
projections and soundscapes along a trail of almost a mile, with four outdoor
playgrounds along the way. The Snowflake Maze is a giant yew maze with lights
and hidden objects. The ice rink is **covered**, 630 square metres, and
skating is unlimited from your entry time.

You do not need children to go. The park pitches the illuminations and skating as an evening out for couples and friends, and those two carry on to 3 January.

## Santa’s Arrival Weekend, 21 and 22 November

The opening weekend has a parade. Santa and Mrs Claus tour the park by carriage
and set off from the **forecourt at 1pm on both days**. The park opens** 10am
to 9pm** that weekend, and the parade is included in every Christmas ticket
type (Stockeld Park: Santa’s Arrival Weekend).
The park’s two pages disagree on what pulls the carriage, horses or unicorns.
