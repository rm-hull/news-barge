Police in York are appealing for witnesses and information following an assault in the city centre which left a man with a broken jaw.

The incident happened at around 5.15pm on Friday 18 September, outside the public toilets on St Leonard’s Place, near the junction with Low Petergate.

The incident involved three men and a woman.

Officers are particularly keen to hear from anyone who was in the area at the time and witnessed a physical or verbal altercation, or who may have information that could assist the investigation.

If you can help, please contact online in the first instance:

Online: Visit the website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’.

Live chat: Available through the website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Please quote reference 12260183554 when providing information.
