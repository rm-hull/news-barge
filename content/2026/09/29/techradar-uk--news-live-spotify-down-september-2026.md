![Spotify app on a smartphone in a car](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/vdFBZfBeD5GsGZtvKZfhPA-1920-80.jpg)

Spotify is down for thousands of users today, with the music streaming app given many log-in issues on both iOS and Android.

The problems started at around 10.32am ET / 2.32pm GMT, when a large spike on Downdetector showed a high number of users were experiencing issues — mostly with the mobile app.

At the time of writing, reports on Downdetector have hit over 17,000 in the US and over 3,000 in the UK. Spotify has confirmed on X that it's "aware of some issues right now" and is "checking them out".

However, there's still no sign of a fix — here's everything we know about the outage so far...

## The story so far

* **Spotify has been down for many users since 10.32am ET / 2.32pm GMT**
* **Spotify says it's "aware of some issues right now" and is working on a fix**
* **The issues seem to be mostly affecting the Spotify mobile app**

While Spotify still hasn't issued a follow-up to its confirmation that some users were experiencing issues with the app, the audio-streaming platform seems to has recovered a good bit. Reports on Down Detector are now under 1,000 and have been for over an hour, currently at 651 reports right before 2 PM ET.

On my end, the Spotify app on my iPhone has begun working again, and many of my colleagues can stream music, podcasts, and audiobooks.

Let us know in the comments below if Spotify is still down for you or back to normal operation, as we await further comment from the platform itself.

## Some advice from Spotify

> Hey folks. We’re aware of some issues right now with logging in and are checking them out! We’ll keep you posted here.September 29, 2026

Spotify's other accounts on X haven't been very forthcoming with advice, but the aptly-named SpotifyCares one has commented on the main issue for many — not being able to log into their accounts on the mobile app.

Its stock response to the many who are experiencing this is to "try accessing Spotify through the web to see if your concern persists" by heading to the official web version (on either mobile or desktop).

It also says to "be sure to keep automatic updates switched on so you get the fix when it's ready". On iOS, go to your device's **Settings > iTunes & App Store**, then make sure** App Updates is switched on**.

It's much the same process on Android. Open the Google Play Store and search Spotify, then tap the three dots at the top and check **Enable auto-update**.

That pretty much confirms that the issue is a faulty app update, so hopefully that new version will be pushed out very soon...

## Spotify says there's still no fix

> We’re aware of issues affecting the Spotify app and are actively working to resolve them.September 29, 2026

This must be a serious outage — Spotify has just posted from its main account on X, rather than its smaller support ones, to give us the update above.

Unfortunately, it's a bit of a non-update, with Spotify saying that it's still "actively working to resolve" the problems. It's now almost exactly three hours since they started.

Fortunately, its official SpotifyCares account is now helping with some more specific advice...

## Don't log out!

![A phone on a green and blue background showing the Spotify app](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/cSECqKBWM2VpBtCyvJVEc6-1200-80.jpg)

A word of warning, based on our experiences — if you're already logged into Spotify on the mobile app, don't log out. Because you probably won't be able to get back in again.

This advice might be too late for many, but my colleague Jeremy Kaplan found his Spotify app was working fine when he was already logged in — but then, after bravely testing logging out of his account, he was unable to log in again.

These kinds of reports are pretty common in the hundreds of angry replies to Spotify's single post on X about the issues. Particularly as many followed Spotify's general advice to delete and reinstall the app when you're having problems with it.

Meanwhile, those reports of Spotify issues are continuing to climb on Downdetector...

## Oh dear...

Just as I posted that Downdetector graph below showing reports were trending down, they've just ticked up again in the US for the first time in over an hour — and are now approaching 16,000 reports again.

Spotify also just got back to me and pointed me in the direction of the Spotify Status account on X for updates — it unfortunately wasn't able to give me any further info on a cause or estimated time for a fix.

So it looks like many of us will need to continue using the desktop app or browser version for the time being...

## Reports are still high

![A Downdetector graph showing reported issues on Spotify](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/tU7wcH3npaMmbWyHxzHaeA-1200-80.jpg)

There was a brief glimmer of hope about an hour ago at 10.40am ET / 3.40pm BST when reported Spotify issues dropped quite significantly on Downdetector (above).

However, unlike other outages today on the likes of Claude, the reports didn't continue to plunge from their peak — and instead have settled at just over 14,500 in the US and 3,400 in the UK.

In other words, these issues are very much continuing — and it's been a few months since we've had a Spotify problem last this long, with the last one hitting back in May 2026. The bad news? That one lasted a good three hours before it was fixed...

## The mobile app is the problem

![A phone on a blue and green background showing an error message in the Spotify app](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/u5hFt33JkWzsY8W6ExUFkJ-1200-80.jpg)

I've asked Spotify directly for some advice for the thousands who are still experiencing issues with the mobile app, and will update this liveblog as soon as I hear anything.

While Spotify hasn't confirmed this, it does appear that the mobile app is the culprit. Many on social media are reporting being able to log into Spotify in web browsers like Chrome, but not the app. And my colleague Lance Ulanoff also reported successfully logging into his account on desktop, but not the iOS app.

Unfortunately, simply reinstalling the Spotify app doesn't appear to be working for most people, which suggests it could be an app update that's the culprit. Hopefully Spotify will give us an update soon, as it's been over an hour since its last (and only) one.

## Not much support

![A laptop screen showing the Spotify support forum](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/jLsH9MxKAZPiswXfNKELn9-1200-80.jpg)

Unfortunately, Spotify's 'Ongoing issues' forum — which is usually helpful on days like today, if only for moral support — isn't being updated right now, so the only place to get the latest updates is the Spotify Status account on X.

Spotify also says in its Support section that "the wait time to speak to an advisor is longer than usual", which isn't hugely surprising considering the scale of the outage.

On the plus side, the number of reports on Downdetector has just dropped for the same time, to just under 17,000 in the US (and 3,200 in the UK), which suggests that a fix could be en route...

## Does this look familiar?

![A phone on a blue and green background showing a Spotify error message](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/AdauJtuRPLdUaDZTAsQZkA-1200-80.jpg)

My colleague Lance Ulanoff says he's still getting the error message above while trying to log into Spotify on a new phone — an issue that many are reporting.

However, the problems do seem to mostly be confined to the mobile app. He says he's able to log into the desktop app okay, and most of the reports on Downdetector (58%) are still related to the mobile app. Let me know in the comments below if you're also having issues with the desktop or browser versions.

Unfortunately, there's been no update from Spotify since its post 40 minutes ago that it was aware of the issues and is "checking them out", so we may need to brace ourselves for a lengthy wait on this one...

## Reinstalling the app isn't working

![Help for when your Spotify app isn't working - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/nvj6LtcfIgs/maxresdefault.jpg)

Many Spotify users have followed the streaming giant's official advice (above) for when Spotify isn't working — namely, restarting the app, updating the app or, if all else fails, reinstalling the app.

However, that doesn't appear to be working on this occasion. Many are reporting being unable to log into the app after reinstalling it, so it might be best to hold tight and hope the fix lands soon instead.

As I reported below, Spotify is aware of the issues and says it's "checking them out"...

## Spotify confirms there are issues

Ah, there it is — Spotify has just confirmed on X that it's aware of the issues that thousands are experiencing right now.

There's no mention of what's causing the outage that's left many unable to log into the app or stream music. But the streaming giant simply says it's "checking them out". Let's hope its engineers do that sooner rather than later...

## 'Something went wrong'

![A graph on Downdetector showing a Spotify outage](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/3fmWqRrcU8KzbNipPMVbXQ-1200-80.jpg)

While Spotify is currently working okay for me, Downdetector is showing a huge spike in reports of issues in both the US and UK – with many reporting a "something went wrong" error message.

Right now, over 18,000 people in the US say the music streaming app isn't working for them, primarily on the mobile app (63% of reports) but desktop users are also affected. Many in the UK are also facing similar issues, with over 4,000 reports and counting there.

There's no sign of an official acknowledgement from Spotify about the issues yet, but I'm trying to get one...
