A car dealership in Bradford has been recognised for outstanding customer service.

Ray Chapman Motors Bradford, based on Canal Road, received a Highly Rated 2026 award from Autotrader, reflecting consistently high levels of customer satisfaction.

The award is based on positive feedback, with the dealership holding a 4.8 out of 5 rating on Autotrader.

Duncan Chapman, managing director at Ray Chapman Motors Bradford, said: "We’re thrilled to have received the Highly Rated 2026 award from Autotrader.

"This achievement reflects the dedication and professionalism of our team at Ray Chapman Motors Bradford, who consistently deliver a customer experience that mirrors the high standards expected of the Volvo brand.

"Their commitment ensures customers receive expert advice, a welcoming environment, and a level of care that builds long-term trust and loyalty, resulting in strong and sustained sales performance and customers continuing to return to us for many years.

"We’re proud to represent Volvo in Bradford and are already focused on building on this success and maintaining and perfecting our Autotrader score throughout the year ahead."

Customers have praised the dealership for going "above and beyond," with multiple reviews highlighting the team’s professionalism and personal approach.

The dealership represents the Volvo brand in Bradford.
