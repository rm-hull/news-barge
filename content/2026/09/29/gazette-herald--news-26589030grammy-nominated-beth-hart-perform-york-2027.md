A GRAMMY nominated singer-songwriter is set to perform in York as part of her 2027 UK tour.

Beth Hart is set to take the stage at York Barbican on September 23, 2027 - one of four UK dates on her tour.

The full UK leg includes Manchester’s Bridgewater Hall on September 21, Glasgow’s SEC Armadillo on September 25, and London’s Royal Albert Hall on September 27.

A spokesperson for Beth said: "Beth Hart is recognized as one of her generation's most talented voices. She plays by her own rules.

"She has collaborated with legends and icons, traversed the globe, topped the Billboard Blues charts six times, gone double platinum and had a string of Top 10 charting albums across Europe, Top 30 Official Billboard US charting albums and surpassed 600 million streaming figures and counting."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21512438.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Beth Hart *(Image: Mal Whichelow)*

Hart has worked with industry legends including Jeff Beck, Slash, and Joe Bonamassa, and has performed at major venues such as Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium, Amsterdam’s Ziggo Dome, and the Royal Albert Hall.

Her most recent album, You Still Got Me, was released in 2024 and a previous album, Seesaw, was nominated for a Grammy in 2014.

Tickets for the York show go on general sale at 10am on Friday (October 2) on [https://www.yorkbarbican.co.uk/whats-on/beth-hart-2027/](https://www.yorkbarbican.co.uk/whats-on/beth-hart-2027/)
