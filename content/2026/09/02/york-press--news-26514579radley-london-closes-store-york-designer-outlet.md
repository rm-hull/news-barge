## Luxury retailer loved by Pippa Middleton and Paloma Faith closes York store

Radley London sold luxury handbags. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A luxury retailer of handbags loved by celebrities including Pippa Middleton and Paloma Faith has closed its branch at the York Designer Outlet.

Radley London has closed its last remaining stores in a nationwide closure programme after falling into administration earlier this year.

This included the branch at the York Designer Outlet, which reports other changes at the major shopping venue.

Radley London, which began 28 years ago, entered administration in May following what FTI Consulting described as a "sustained period of challenging economic conditions."

After entering administration, Gordon Brothers, which owns Poundland and LK Bennett, acquired the brand, intellectual property and some assets in a pre-pack administration deal.

However, the purchase did not include Radley’s 21 UK stores.

  Some of the luxury handbags sold by Radley London 

*(Image: Radley London)*

Customers were offered up to 70 per cent off in clearance sales in the weeks that followed, with Radley’s website taken offline on August 19.

The closure resulted in the loss of 42 jobs.

York Designer Outlet manager Paul Tyler told the Press that Radley London closed on Sunday.

Paul continued: “We are in discussions with a couple of brands to take the Radley site.”

  York Designer Outlet Manager Paul Tyler reveals some changes at the thriving shopping centre. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

But he says don’t expect a sudden replacement as “it will take a few weeks to finalise anything.”

The move comes amid other changes at the designer outlet.

In August the upmarket Savante opened, promising ‘chocolate artistry.’

Last week, the Le Creuset homeware brand relocated to a new site twice its old site, with the French company now situated by Polo Ralph Lauren near the Western Entrance.

Potion’s Cauldron last month also vacated a small unit, which will be replaced by the Café Valeria Sicilian deli.

And later this month, Kouzina Greek will open in the food court.

Paul added the traditional Green street food chain has just started work on the shop fit-out.

The designer outlet has said of its new arrival: “Welcome to the best traditional Greek street food chain in the UK – serving Gyros wraps, Greek street food boxes, loaded fries and much more.

“Inspired by the home recipes of their Mothers and Grandmothers, food is Kouzina’s way of celebrating life – pouring their passion, life experiences, and happy memories into every dish. The team takes pride in knowing every first bite a customer takes is the same first bite they themselves took as children.”

The outlet website added: “Tuck in and enjoy authentic, meaningful recipes made with love and care – all at Designer Outlet York.”
