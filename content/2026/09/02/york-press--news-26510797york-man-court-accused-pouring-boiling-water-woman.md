A man has appeared before York Crown Court accused of pouring boiling water onto a woman's skin.

Joseph Kendra, 29, of Chapelfields Road, Acomb, denied a charge of causing grievous bodily harm with intent to cause grievous bodily harm.

He also denied a charge of causing actual bodily harm to the same woman.

Both offences were allegedly committed on July 26 in York.

Kendra attended court via a video link from Hull Prison where he is being held on remand.

The Recorder of York, Judge Sean Morris, listed the case for trial at York Crown Court on January 25 and remanded Kendra in custody until then.

He also listed the case for a case management hearing on November 20.
