The countdown to Christmas has officially started – oh yes it has! – as we met the cast of *The Further Adventures of Peter Pan: The Return of Captain Hook* at the Grand Opera House York.

The cast are looking forward to bringing all the festive fun to the city in what promises to be a pantomime full of magic, music and mayhem.

It stars musical theatre & TV star Tom Lister (*Emmerdale, 42nd Street*) as Captain Hook, Nick Jr Go Go Go! star Holly Atterton (* Mamma Mia, Rocky Horror*) as Tinker Bell, and after delighting audiences as Buttons in last year’s Cinderella, fan favourite Jimmy Bryant is back as Smee.

The esteemed cast also includes West End stars Joe Sleight (*Blood Brothers*) as Peter Pan and Kirsty Sparks (* Top Hat, Starlight Express, Hairspray*) as Ethel Mermaid.

*The Further Adventures of Peter Pan: The Return of Captain Hook* will run from **Saturday 5 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027**.

**YorkMix**‘s Laura Castle gave the cast the challenge of explaining the plot while acting it out – can a plot be explained in panto? Watch the video to find out!

We also chatted with this year’s cast to find out more about *The Further Adventures of Peter Pan: The Return of Captain Hook*.

“What’s really special about this version of Peter Pan is that it brings it completely up to date,” said Holly, aka Tinker Bell. “The original Peter Pan that everyone knows and loves is set in Edwardian times, so I think bringing it up to date means that we can really be relatable to the new audiences.”

Tom Lister, who plays the formidable Hook, is already a celebrated panto villain, having first claimed national recognition in the genre when he won Best Villain at the inaugural Great British Pantomime Awards.

“It’s great fun,” said Tom. “It’s great to hear the children booing at you and shouting at you. But then if you can get some laughs along the way as well the fact that people hate you, they want to love you as well.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/the-further-adventures-of-peter-pan-goh-2026-2.webp)

And it’s Jimmy Bryant’s second pantomime at the Grand Opera House York, after becoming a fan-favourite last year. “It’s an absolute pleasure to be back,” said Jimmy. “Last year was fantastic, we set a very high bar, and I just hope the people here can live up to it…”

It’s also a return for Kirsty Sparks (Ethel Mermaid) too, who dazzled in *Hairspray* when it came to York in 2024. “I am so excited to be in York. It’s one of my favourite theatres, and I cannot wait to bring the glamour to the panto with Ethel Mermaid!”

This year’s panto will once again be produced by UK Productions, with a script by Jon Monie – writer of last year’s production of *Cinderella* and winner of Best Script at the Great British Pantomime Awards 2019.

“We love that pantomime is just part of Christmas tradition for families up and down the country, and it’s going to be no different in York this year,” said Tom. “This pantomime is stellar, it’s absolutely brilliant, and it’s my favourite – so come and see it!”

*The Further Adventures of Peter Pan: The Return of Captain Hook* runs at Grand Opera House York from **Saturday 5 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027**.
