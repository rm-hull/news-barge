A woman has denied a charge relating to an attack said to have been committed by her dog on a man who suffered injuries in the incident and subsequently died.

Claire Lumley appeared at a Durham Crown Court plea hearing over the incident on March 2, last year, when, what is understood to have been a lurcher-type dog attacked Michael Mount and his own dog, in Elemore Woods, near Durham.

Mr Mount, 46, from Sherburn Hill, was said to have collapsed and died later that day at the side of a road in Durham.

A charge of being the owner of a dog which, while dangerously out of control, attacked and injured Mr Mount, resulting in his death, was denied by the defendant.

*(Image: The Northern Echo)*

Judge Amanda Rippon said the Coroner’s report stated that Mr Mount had heart issues which he was unaware of and, as a result, suffered a heart attack later that day.

She said a trial would have to be fixed with a four-day time-estimate.

All parties agreed on a start date of April 19, next year, for the trial.

But Judge Rippon said a further case management hearing should take place ahead of that date to ensure it is on track for the trial.

She bailed the 41-year-old defendant of Elemore Grange Farm, Littletown, near Durham, to return to court for that hearing, on February 8, then the trial itself.
