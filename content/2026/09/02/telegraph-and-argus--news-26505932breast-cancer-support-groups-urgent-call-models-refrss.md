A breast cancer support group could be forced to cancel a fashion show due to a lack of support.

Bosom Friends Bradford, an independent, patient-led charity supporting people affected by breast cancer, is calling for participants to take part in its runway event, which aims to celebrate resilience and build confidence among those living with or beyond breast cancer.

The show, planned for October 27 at Bradford Grammar School, needs more people to take part in order to go ahead.

  Before makeover 

*(Image: Bosom Friends Bradford)*

A spokesperson for Bosom Friends Bradford told the T&A: "We’re at risk of cancelling the show is we don’t get enough support."

The group is inviting individuals of all backgrounds and genders who have received a breast cancer diagnosis to take part.

Those newly diagnosed, in treatment, or years beyond cancer are welcome, and no modelling experience is required.

  After makeover 

*(Image: Bosom Friends Bradford)*

## This is more than fashion

Bosom Friends Bradford said: "This is more than fashion.

"It’s about courage, community and showing the world what strength truly looks like."

Founded to reduce isolation and offer practical help and social support, Bosom Friends Bradford organises social gatherings, fundraising activities, and events with support from Bradford’s Breast Care Team.

  This could be you! 

*(Image: Bosom Friends Bradford)*

The charity’s stated purpose is to offer a supportive environment for people to connect and share experiences.

Anyone interested in taking part in the fashion show can message the group on its Facebook page or contact Barbara on 07854 932918 or Sue on 07718 976158.

Bosom Friends Bradford is registered with the Charity Commission and operates independently to offer friendship and support to those affected by breast cancer.
