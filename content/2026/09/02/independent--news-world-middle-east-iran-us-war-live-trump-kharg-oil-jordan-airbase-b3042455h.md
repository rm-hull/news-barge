American forces have denied a claim from Iran’s paramilitary that it struck a US base in Jordan.

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard said it launched a ballistic missile attack at the base, hours after Donald Trump threatened the country, Al Jazeera and the West Asia News Agency reported.

But a U.S. official told *The Independent* that the reporting was false. 

The US launched fresh strikes Tuesday following recent attempted attacks by the IRGC against shipping in the Strait of Hormuz and American service members in the Middle East.

When announcing the strikes, the US president warned, “If the failed Nation of Iran retaliates for this very justified attack, they will be hit again at a much harder and higher level, but it will not be the biggest attack of them all, that is waiting in the wings and, when it is over, there will be very little left of the Islamic Republic of Iran!”

The US fired rocket launchers on Larak Island Sunday in the first clash in more than a month, causing Iran to retaliate by launching ballistic missiles at American bases in Jordan on Monday.

## US military struck two Iranian tankers for the first time, report says

The US military struck two Iranian government tankers during Tuesday’s attacks on Iran, Axios reported.

A US official said the strikes were carried out under a new “tanker for tanker” policy approved by president Donald Trump in response to Iranian attacks on tankers passing through the Strait of Hormuz.

According to the report, the tankers were anchored off Iran’s coast, north of the US naval blockade line.

The missiles hit the engine rooms of the vessels.US officials said around 100 targets were struck on Tuesday, including defence sites, radar systems and anti-ship cruise missile launchers.

It was the first time the US military had targeted Iranian tankers in retaliation for Iranian attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz.

## Shipping traffic via Strait of Hormuz stays below 10-day average, data shows

Four commodity vessels transited the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday, down from 10 a day earlier and below the 10-day average of around 13, preliminary shipping data showed on Wednesday.

The four vessels that transited were one very large crude carrier, one Panamax tanker, one Kamsarmax carrier and one intermediate tanker, initial data from shiptracker Kpler showed at 0200 GMT.

## Bad news for Trump as more Americans admit they regret voting for him

Buyer's remorse is rising among Americans who voted for US President Donald Trump in 2024 — vividly illustrating the “clear, consistent signs” that his political base is crumbling, according to survey results released Monday.

About one in six Trump voters told pollsters from the University of Massachusetts-Amherst that they wouldn't vote for him again, as the president's overall approval rating fell to a dismal 32 percent.

“There are no takebacks in electoral politics, but around 15 percent of Trump voters now tell us they might or would vote differently or not vote at all if they had it to do over again”, the poll's co-director, Alexander Theodoridis, said in a prepared statement.

“This is hardly a mass exodus from the Trump tent, but is enough that it certainly could’ve been decisive against Trump in what was a relatively close 2024 election”, Theodoridis added. “More notably, though, there are now clear, consistent signs of an erosion trend“.

## Iran says US attacked a wedding party, killing four

The Iranian Red Crescent said four people, including a young child, have been killed in a US attack after shrapnel struck a home where a wedding celebration was taking place in Sirik in southern Iran.

The US had resumed strikes against Iran following "recent attempted attacks" by the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) against commercial shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, Centcom said.

Iran retaliated by launching attacks at US military bases in Iraq and Jordan, according to local state media and the Jordanian Armed Forces. Attacks were also reported in Kuwait and Bahrain.

Esmaeil Baghaei, the spokesman for the Iranian foreign ministry, said the “brutal” attack hit a residence in the city of Kuhestak early on Wednesday.

Reza Shahidiyan, governor of Sirik county, later told state broadcaster IRIB that 63 others were wounded, including 50 women and children. All the injured were taken to a hospital in the nearby city of Minab and were in “stable condition”, he said.

## Latest wave of US strikes against Iran targeted 'mine laying capabilities': CENTCOM

The latest wave of US strikes against Iran has concluded with American forces targeting “mine laying capabilities” and other military assets, according to US Central Command.

“US forces struck Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) targets including air defense sites, radar systems, maritime assets and facilities, mine laying capabilities, and communications sites”, CENTCOM wrote on X.

CENTCOM warned that “more than 50,000 US service members are currently operating across the Middle East and remain vigilant, lethal, and prepared to continue executing operations directed by the Commander in Chief”.
