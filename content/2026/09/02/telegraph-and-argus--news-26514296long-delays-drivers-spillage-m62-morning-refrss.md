Motorists face delays of 35 minutes after a spillage closed two lanes on the M62.

The incident is near Junction 28, Bradford Road, Tingley, affecting westbound traffic between Junction 31, the A655 at Castleford, and Junction 27, the M621 at Gildersome.

One traffic alert said average speeds had fallen to 10mph.

A spokesperson for National Highways: Yorkshire said: "Treatment of the spillage is currently underway."

"There are delays of 30 minutes and approximately four miles of congestion on approach."

An earlier update said lanes two and three remained closed following the spillage.

National Highways: Yorkshire spokesperson said: "Resurfacing works are likely to proceed later this morning."

Drivers have been urged to allow extra travel time while the spillage is treated.
