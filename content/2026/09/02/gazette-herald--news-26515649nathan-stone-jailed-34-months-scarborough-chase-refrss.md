A DRUG dealer who bragged about living "like a rock star" and was caught by police within minutes of starting a police chase has been jailed for nearly three years.

Nathan Stone, 32 of Barrowcliff Road in Scarborough, was sentenced to 34 months in prison at York Crown Court today (September 2).

Stone pleaded guilty of possession with intent to supply Class A drugs at York Crown Court on July 6.

He was arrested on June 4 after officers pulled him over on King Street, where he was "spotted behaving suspiciously".

As his car was searched, he tried to run away, but was caught by police officers.

DC Alfie Thomlinson of Scarborough CID said: "Nathan Stone thought he could exploit others for his own ill-gotten gain, making a significant amount of money from selling cocaine.

"This case is an example of what proactive officers out on the beat patrolling can achieve by stopping, searching – even chasing down and arresting – those involved in the supply of drugs.

"Stone paid no heed to the misery that illegal drugs cause in our communities. Those like him should know that detectives at North Yorkshire Police will relentlessly investigate and disrupt their selfish crimes."


During the arrest, Stone tried to destroy his phone by snapping it in half - later telling police that he had "panicked", but there would be no evidence of drug dealing on the device.

After analysis of the phone, police were able to recover messages from the phone showing communication with dozens of people about the supply of Class A drugs.

The most recent messages had been sent just moments before his arrest.

Officers also found weighing scales and a bottle containing a blue plastic bag filled with white powder in his car.

Stone had previously sent messages boasting about staying in five-star self-catering flats and living "like a rock star" in Scarborough, however in his interview, he said he had worked as a bricklayer but had been unemployed for at least three months.
