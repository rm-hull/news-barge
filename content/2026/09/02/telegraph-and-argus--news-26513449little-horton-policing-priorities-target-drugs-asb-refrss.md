Police have set out neighbourhood priorities for Little Horton as officers step up action against anti-social behaviour and suspected drug dealing in three parts of the Bradford ward.

The Marshfield area remains a focus following reports of groups of young men gathering and allegedly intimidating residents, alongside concerns over possible drug activity.

Targeted patrols are being used to gather intelligence, identify suspects and tackle offending.

Officers said stop-and-search powers will be used where appropriate, with arrests made when offences are identified.

In West Bowling, police are working with religious organisations and community representatives to improve trust, communication and information-sharing.

Residents are also being urged to help develop community-led responses to anti-social behaviour.

Police activity has increased and officers are investigating recent disorder.

Little Horton Lane has been identified as an emerging hotspot for drug-related activity and anti-social behaviour.

High-visibility patrols are continuing with partner agencies, while officers engage with residents and community groups to help shape future enforcement work.
