York’s traditional Bavarian beer festival is back!

Get ready for two weekends of barrels-full of fun, live entertainment and lederhosen – as Yorktoberfest returns to York Racecourse on **Friday 16 to 17 October,** and**Friday 23 to Saturday 24 October**.

For the first time, the popular event will be staged in a ‘Big Top’, making it bigger and better than ever.

The blue Big Top will be transformed into a spectacular fully-themed German Beer Haus.

Event organiser James Cundall, CEO of Jamboree Entertainment, said, “We are really excited to be staging Yorktoberfest in a Big Top for the first time – it will make for a unique and unforgettable party.”

The Big Top will feature rows of tables and benches, two performance stages, rustic carts full of wooden beer barrels, wooden barns serving German-themed food and drinks, and flags galore.

“We are also delighted to say that we will be keeping our ticket prices the same as last year,” added James. “We are mindful that everything everywhere seems to be going up in price and we wanted to keep Yorktoberfest as affordable as we possibly can.”

Traditional or fancy dress is encouraged – with competitions for the best dressed – as is dancing and merry-making, in keeping with the original Oktoberfest in Munich in 1810 where so much fun was had that an annual tradition was born.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Yorktoberfests-Bavarian-Strollers-band.webp)

Returning this year is the internationally-acclaimed seven-piece live band, The Bavarian Strollers who will perform thigh-slapping, foot-stomping oompah tunes as well as feel-good disco classics.

Adding to the party and returning to the stage by popular demand will be international drag artist Velma Celli.

The Bavarian Bar will serve a variety of beers from Brew York as well as a range of wines, prosecco, spirits, zero beers and soft drinks. German-inspired food will also be available from The Dog Haus, catering for all tastes with favourites like sausages, schnitzels and pretzels as well as vegan and vegetarian options.

Yorktoberfest will have a strictly limited capacity, with opening times of Fridays and Saturdays from 7pm until 11pm, and daytime sessions on Saturdays from 1pm until 5pm.

VIP tickets are situated closest to the main stage and have table service for drinks and food. All VIP customers will be able to book specific seats in their preferred location whether for individuals or tables of six.

Standard individual seats are unreserved while standard tables of six can be reserved. An online booking fee applies. Tickets will also be available at the entrance, subject to availability.

Prices are £25 per person for standard and £35 for VIP.
