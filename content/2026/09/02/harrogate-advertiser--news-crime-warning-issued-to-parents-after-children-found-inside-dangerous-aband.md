# Warning issued to parents after children found inside dangerous abandoned building in Harrogate

## Parents and carers are being urged to speak to their children about the dangers of exploring abandoned buildings after two youngsters were found inside a derelict property in Harrogate.

The children had gained access to the former Kimberley Hotel on Kings Road, prompting North Yorkshire Police to attend the scene.

Fortunately, both children were found safe and uninjured.

However, officers have warned that the former hotel poses serious safety risks, including the presence of asbestos and large holes in the flooring, which could result in significant injury.

![Parents and carers are being urged to speak to their children about the dangers of exploring abandoned buildings after two youngsters were found inside the derelict Kimberley Hotel on King's Road in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmMxOWM1YTk4LTNiMzUtNGUyMi05ZmI3LTU1MmE0YWNmYmM4ZTplMGQzZTExYi1kZmVlLTQ5YmItOGE4NS1jZWI1OGJjZjVmZDA=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Police say the incident is not an isolated case and that they have received regular reports of children entering the abandoned building in recent weeks.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "While exploring abandoned buildings may seem exciting, these locations can contain hidden hazards that pose a real threat to safety.

"What may appear to be harmless fun can quickly result in serious injury or exposure to dangerous substances."

Police are now appealing to parents and carers to ensure they know where their children are spending their time and to discuss the risks associated with entering vacant or derelict properties.

They are also encouraging children to stay in safe locations and reminding them never to enter buildings that are boarded up, fenced off or appear abandoned.

The force have thanked the public for their continued support in helping to keep young people safe throughout the summer.
