A MAN has been arrested after police uncovered a haul of suspected stolen agricultural vehicles in the Bradford district.

Police found the vehicles and equipment during a raid on Tuesday at a farm on Trough Lane, Denholme.

## What have police said

A spokesperson for the Shipley Neighbourhood Policing Team said: "A stolen trailer was located in Denholme and a male was arrested on suspicion of theft.

  Police discovered a number of suspected agricultural vehicle thefts in Denholme


"Following a search of the site, several other items were also discovered by officers, all of which are believed to have been stolen, and which were subsequently recovered.

"The suspect has been interviewed and released on bail, pending further enquiries."

Items recovered included a JCB dumper truck, a Decon/welfare unit trailer, red and blue William horse boxes, a low-roof animal trailer, a box trailer, a generator, a fridge van and another trailer.
