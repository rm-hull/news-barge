Restaurants, cafés, pubs and bars across Harrogate are preparing to offer discounted menus as part of Restaurant Week.

The event, held in association with Harrogate BID, will run from Monday 14th until Sunday 20th September.

Participating businesses will offer food and drink deals at set price points ranging from £10 to £30.

Here are the businesses and offers announced so far...

**£10 offers**

- **Pitcher & Piano Harrogate** - Spend £10 and receive £20 of food.
- **Jenny’s Tearoom** - A panini or toastie with any drink for £10.
- **Elysian Bar Harrogate** - Cocktails for £10 and 10% off other drinks, excluding champagne.
- **Harrogate Brewing Co, Royal Parade** - A slice of homemade chicken and mushroom pie with a pint of Harrogate Brewing Co beer for £10.
- **The Crown Hotel** - Spend £10 and receive £20 off the final bill.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6926cac2c1ee8.jpg)


**£20 offers**

- **Konak Meze Turkish Restaurant -**  A two-course meal from a set menu for £20.
- **Abu’s Indian Dining -**  A three-course banquet menu for £20.
- **The Inn South Stainley -**  Two courses for £20.
- **The Crown Hotel – Churchill Suite -**  Spend £20 and receive £30 off the final bill.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/680a2c02598dc.jpg)


**£25 offers**

- **The Drum & Monkey -**  A starter and main course from a selected menu for £25.
- **Giggling Squid -**  Two courses from a set menu and a drink for £25 per person.
- **Hotel du Vin Harrogate -**  A two-course autumn prix-fixe menu with a glass of wine or bottled beer for £25.
- **Bettys Harrogate -**  A Yorkshire Cream Tea for two or two courses from the Evening Dining Menu for £25.
- **Ambers Restaurant at Cedar Court Hotel -**  A three-course lunch for £25 per person.
- **The Inn South Stainley -**  Three courses for £25.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a8ed19caa0e530bf14.jpg)


**£30 offers**

- **Three’s a Crowd -**  Three courses for £30.
- **The Crown Hotel (Churchill Suite) -**  Spend £30 and receive £40 off the final bill.

Customers should contact their chosen venue directly to book and quote “Restaurant Week”.

Deals will be available on a first-come, first-served basis, with different dates, serving times and exclusions applying to individual offers.
