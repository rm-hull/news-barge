## Poppleton's historic railway nursery to host open day and plant sale

Poppleton Community Railway Nursery is hosting a Heritage Open Day and Sale *(Image: Poppleton Community Railway Nursery)*

Poppleton Community Railway Nursery is hosting a Heritage Open Day and Sale.

Taking place on Saturday, September 12, the event will run from 10am until 4pm.

The open day provides a unique chance to delve into the origins and history of the nursery, which has been a fixture in Poppleton since 1941 and stands as the UK's last surviving railway nursery.

Attendees can expect a diverse array of plants, homemade refreshments, and stalls featuring books, bric-a-brac, and various homemade crafts.

The nursery's narrow-gauge and model railways will also be showcased.

Entirely volunteer-run, the nursery is a sanctuary for individuals with mental health challenges and collaborates with health agencies in the York area to provide therapeutic sessions for adults.

The nursery operates throughout the year on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays from 10am to 3pm, welcoming new volunteers from all backgrounds.

Further details can be found at poppletonrailwaynursery.co.uk.
