## 'But is he happy?' - readers speak out on North Yorkshire billionaire aged just 25

James Dacombe, 25, is Europe's youngest self-made billionaire. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A story about a Harrogate college drop-out now being worth almost £1 billion attracted much reader interest, becoming our most well-read story of August.

James Dacombe, 25, acquired such wealth - on paper at least - through one of his companies being revalued after it raised around £232 million in capital investment.

The company is called Olix, which was launched just two years ago and develops specialist AI chips designed to improve the speed and efficiency of artificial intelligence systems.

The London-based business is growing rapidly, having tripled in value this year to $3.3 billion (around £2.5 billion). It is increasingly seen as a potential alternative to US-based Nvidia, which has a market capitalisation of $5 trillion.

James has another business CoMind, a London-based brain monitoring company, he launched in 2018. James remains its chief executive.

Such success comes despite, or maybe because he famously dropped out of sixth-form after just two days, to take up a scholarship from Silicon Valley billionaire Peter Thiel, who founded businesses including PayPal and Palantir. The scholarship supports entrepreneurs creating their own projects or businesses outside college.

As previously reported, James grew up in Harrogate, attending Ashville College before studying at the St Aidan's and St John Fisher Associated Sixth Form.

September-born James began teaching himself computer programming from a young age, building apps and websites at 13 years old.

Reports on his new-found billionaire status led to much coverage in the UK national press, as well as trade and technology titles globally.

Among the global coverage of the Yorkshireman, US-based Forbes magazine noted James was one of a growing number of young billionaires. In this Spring’s Forbes listing of billionaires, the rankings included 35 under 30, with 12 of them self-made.

Forbes magazine added that at the age of 25, James was Europe’s youngest self-made billionaire.

The Press attempted to contact James through his Olix press office, but we received no response.

Perhaps he might have had some tips for young people succeeding in today’s world, especially if AI is set to become a major part of everyone's future.

Nonetheless, our readers were supportive of the ‘boy made good’ from Harrogate.

One said: “Well done James. My own son has just graduated from Berkeley, USA and now continuing in the 'Silicon Valley' region in California. I know that a lot of hard work goes into these achievements.”

Another said: “Enjoy it kid. Don’t let the envious bother you. And there’s a lot of them around. Lots on here actually, normally Liberal Democrats.”

And another said: “It's great to see a young person succeeding, instead of faking an illness and scrounging benefits.”

However, one questioned: “Yes, but is he happy?”
