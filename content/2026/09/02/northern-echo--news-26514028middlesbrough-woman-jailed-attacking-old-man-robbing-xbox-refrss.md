A woman has been jailed for more than five years after attacking, robbing and threatening to kill an elderly man at his Middlesbrough home.

Leoni Prince, 39, of Crescent Road, Middlesbrough, was jailed for five years and three months at Teesside Crown Court on Thursday, August 27, after admitting the burglary.

Prince targeted a man in his seventies at a home on Glamis Grove on April 19, the court heard.

At around 9.30pm, she arrived at the victim’s home with two hooded men and forced her way inside.

The group dragged him along the hallway, punching and kicking him while demanding his bank card and searching his pockets.

They ransacked the bedrooms, threatened to kill him and fled with cash, a bank card, a laptop, headphones and an Xbox.

The victim called emergency services after the group fled.

Following an investigation by Middlesbrough CID, Prince was identified and charged with robbery.

Prince was also given a 10-year restraining order banning her from contacting the victim after her release.

Detective Constable Itsharm Ramzan, of Middlesbrough CID, thanked the victim for supporting the investigation.

He said: "The bravery shown by the victim throughout this investigation has been nothing short of extraordinary.

"He was violently attacked, yet he demonstrated immense courage and determination in helping officers bring the offender to justice."

Police are still trying to identify the two men who allegedly carried out the robbery and attack with Prince.

A man arrested during the investigation was later released without charge.

Anyone with information should contact Cleveland Police on 101, quoting reference 073730, or Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

Prince had previously been made subject to an 18-month court order banning her from part of Middlesbrough after reports of repeated shoplifting and aggressive begging.
