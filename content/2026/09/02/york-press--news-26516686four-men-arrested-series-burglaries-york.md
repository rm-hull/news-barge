FOUR people have been arrested after a series of suspected burglaries and attempted thefts involving cars, trailers, a horsebox, a chainsaw and jet washers.

North Yorkshire Police says a group of suspected burglars were arrested after a series of incidents which took place in Tang Hall, Heworth, and Strensall between August 23 and 24.

During the two-day period, five trailers and a horsebox were allegedly stolen, along with other items including a chainsaw and jet washes.

Police say there were also attempts to steal high-value vehicles, including an Audi S5 and a Porsche and in several cases, offenders are believed to have targeted properties to obtain vehicle keys - causing damage to gates and door handles in the process.

A spokesperson for the force said: "As part of the investigation, officers identified a grey Mitsubishi linked to several of the offences. The vehicle was spotted towing the stolen horsebox.

"When officers attempted to stop the vehicle, it came to a stop and all four occupants fled on foot. Following an extensive search of the area, two suspects were located, detained and arrested."

An 18-year-old and a 19-year-old were arrested on suspicion of burglary, two counts of attempted burglary, theft of a motor vehicle, theft, and vehicle interference. They have since been released on conditional bail.

After further enquiries, two others - a 37-year-old man and a 35-year-old man -were arrested on suspicion of three counts of attempted burglary dwelling, burglary other than dwelling, and theft and are currently in custody for questioning.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "Burglary is far more than a property crime. It violates people's sense of safety and security and can have a devastating impact on victims and our communities.

"That's why preventing burglaries, pursuing offenders and bringing them to justice remains an absolute priority for North Yorkshire Police."

Police added that they are appealing for information from the public and anyone who has CCTV, doorbell camera, or dashcam footage that could help the investigation should contact 101, use the website, or live chat.
