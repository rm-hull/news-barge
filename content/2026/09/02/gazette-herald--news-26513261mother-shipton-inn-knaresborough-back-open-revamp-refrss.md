A well-known North Yorkshire pub named after an iconic figure in its town reports a “absolutely crazy” reopening.

The Mother Shipton Inn in Low Bridge, Knaresborough, reopened on Friday following a £250,000 investment from Admiral Taverns.

Internally, the pub has been upgraded, including the kitchen, to boost its food offering. Externally, there is brand-new lighting and signage, with seating for upto 100 customers.

New manager Matt Chapman has praised how the refurbishment has retained the warmth and character of the pub whilst feeling ‘fresh and exciting.’

A launch event on Friday evening with live singer and free buffet attracted around 250 people.

Matt said: “They were like sardines, all squashed in. Trade started at 12 and it didn’t stop all day. We had a great night. It was great to see all the locals and everybody was pleased with the refresh.”

  Knaresborough Food bank was supported on the opening night. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

“We also served double the number of customers we expected on Sunday. We had over 100 people for Sunday lunch. We were very happy with it. Monday was fantastic as well.”

  The outside of The Mother Shipton Inn 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Matt added: “At the moment, it’s a bit of a blur.”

Going forward, he will be hosting a busy schedule of regular entertainment, including a quiz night every Thursday from 7pm.

In addition, as part of his mission to give back to the local community, the Mother Shipton Inn also raised £100 for Knaresborough food bank on the opening night.

The Mother Shipton Inn will offer drinks including Guinness and Birra Moretti. The pub will also be introducing “Thirsty Fridays”, a special weekly drinks promotion. Every Friday, customers who purchase two large glasses of wine will receive a third glass free.

The pub will also offer a selection of classic pub dishes made with quality ingredients which are locally sourced, including traditional fish and chips, steak and ale pie, lasagne and much more.

Drinkers and diners have raved over the opening Bank Holiday weekend.

One said of the opening night: “A great evening, many thanks to Joe, Matt, Jess and team for all of your hard work, see you all again soon.”

  The outside beer garden 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Another said: “We had a great lunch with you guys yesterday, so happy to see you back, fresh! Will definitely be coming over often.”

And another added: “Looks brilliant. Used to love Mother Shiptons, and it now looks as though it will be a great place to go again. Good luck to you all and well done!”

The pub is owned by Admiral Taverns, who have around 1,365 pubs across England, Scotland and Wales.

Ian Lester, Business Development Manager at Admiral Taverns, added: “It has been an absolute pleasure working alongside Matt throughout this refurbishment journey. Watching his vision come to life has been incredibly rewarding.

“On behalf of myself and everyone at Admiral Taverns, I wish Matt and the entire team at the Mother Shipton Inn the best of luck in the future.”
