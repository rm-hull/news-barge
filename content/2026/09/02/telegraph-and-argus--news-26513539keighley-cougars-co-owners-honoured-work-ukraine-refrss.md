KEIGHLEY Cougars co-owners Ryan O’Neill and Kaue Garcia have been honoured for their work helping Ukrainian rugby league.

The pair have been appointed as honorary members and trustees of the governing body that runs the sport in the country.

The dedication to the pair reads: “In honour of the 20th anniversary of rugby league in Ukraine with the title of ‘Honorary Member of the Ukrainian Rugby League’ for significant personal contribution to the development of rugby league in Ukraine.”

Their certificates of recognition have been signed off by the president of Ukraine RL, Artur Martyrosian.

Cougars produced free kits for Ukraine’s senior and age group teams last year, with O’Neill and Garcia flying out to Kyiv to hand deliver them.

The duo then organised for a group of rugby league playing Ukrainian youngsters to come over for a match, where the unlikely spectators saw Keighley fall to a dramatic 42-30 defeat against Hunslet at Cougar Park this April.
