Passengers across Teesside and County Durham can now pick up Northern train tickets for as little as £2 in a major flash sale.

Northern Railway has released four million £2 advance single tickets, available to buy from September 2 to September 4, to be used on selected services between September 9 and October 23.

The sale covers routes linking Darlington with Teesside Airport, Middlesbrough, Saltburn, Whitby and Bishop Auckland, plus connected services to Durham and Newcastle.

Northern connections also cover wider areas, including Sunderland, Seaham, Carlisle and the Metrocentre.

Northern said the discounted tickets are available through all sales channels, but customers should use its fare finder tool to check whether their route is included before booking.

Railcard discounts cannot be used on flash sale tickets, but children’s fares will be half the price of an adult ticket.

Northern said once the £2 tickets sell out, they won't be returning, so customers should act quickly to save money on travel this autumn.

Anyone having trouble booking online can call Northern’s retail support team on 0344 811 5111.

Customers can use Northern's Low Fare Finder to find eligible routes across the network.
