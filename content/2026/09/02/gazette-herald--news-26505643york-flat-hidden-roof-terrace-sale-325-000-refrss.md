A York flat with a hidden roof terrace has hit the market.

The two-bedroom home is for sale in Driffield Terrace, off the Mount, for £325,000.

  Outside the two-bedroom flat for sale in Driffield Terrace, York, for £325,000 

*(Image: Churchills/Zoopla)*

Churchills, which is marketing the top floor property, said it is part of a “characterful period building in a central and convenient part of York”.

Double doors from the lounge/dining room lead to the outdoor terrace with views over the city.
