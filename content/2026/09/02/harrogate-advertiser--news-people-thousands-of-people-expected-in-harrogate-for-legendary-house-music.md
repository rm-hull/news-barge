# Thousands of people expected in Harrogate for legendary house music festival this weekend

## A Harrogate bar is to host a ‘Love to Be Pre Party’ this weekend to get the celebrations started for the biggest ever dance music event held on the Stray.

Three's a Crowd is inviting fans heading to the Stray for a feast of superstar DJs and great music at the legendary Love to be...Festival to get the day started at this popular gastro pub with its outdoor terrace.

Kicking off at 11am, Three’s A Crowd is offering a house DJ, brunch-style bites and plenty of drinks to get you in the festival spirit.

Gather your friends, grab a drink and get the party started, says Three’s A Crowd, then, when you’re ready, it’s just a short walk to The Stray.

Since it first opened at 11 West Park in 2019, Three’s a Crowd has won a reputation for being a contemporary gastro pub that also offers special events.

For tickets, visit: [https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/love-to-be-pre-party-tickets-1997439881455?aff=oddtdtcreator&keep_tld=true](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/love-to-be-pre-party-tickets-1997439881455?aff=oddtdtcreator&keep_tld=true)

Love to be...Festival, which first came to Harrogate’s Stray in 2024, attracts a broad crowd spanning generations of clubbers with audiences nearly hitting the 10,000 mark.

The line-up includes Claptone, Sam Divine, Paul Woolford, Mouse-T, Todd Terry, Gok Wan, Julie McKnight (Live), K‑Klass (Live), Trimtone, Cash Only, Grant Nelson, and more across three stages.

Guests can expect a new VIP garden with interactive games, an upgraded stage hosted by funkylove with exclusive DJ sets and live performances, expanded seating and table areas.

The festival will run from 1pm to 11pm.
