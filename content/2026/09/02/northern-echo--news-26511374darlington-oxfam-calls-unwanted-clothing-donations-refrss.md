A charity shop is encouraging people to donate their unused clothes as part of Oxfam’s Second Hand September campaign.

The Darlington Oxfam store in Skinnergate is calling on residents to clear out their wardrobes and support the campaign, which aims to promote sustainability by giving pre-loved clothing a second life and reducing textile waste.

Second Hand September encourages people to donate clothes they haven’t worn in the past year.

Rachel Parry, Oxfam manager in Darlington, said: "There are loads of ways to shop second hand now, but I don’t think you can beat a good old rummage in a charity shop.

"You never really know what you’re going to find, and that’s half the fun.

"You can go in looking for one thing and come out with something completely different.

"We really rely on our local community to keep the shop going, from people donating clothes to those who come in and shop with us.

"Charity shops are a great way to keep clothes out of landfill and push back against fast fashion.

"We love seeing our regulars, but we’d love to see some new faces through the door this September too."

Actor Richard E. Grant will be appearing on Oxfam posters in shops across the North East this September, encouraging people to discover original second-hand style, give garments a longer life and make their fashion choices count.

Rachel Cosgrove-Pearce, Oxfam’s head of retail operations, said: "Oxfam is the original Second Hand retailer.

"We’ve been selling quality pre-loved fashion since 1947, and that success is down to people supporting our local shops.

"Every donation makes a difference, and while we’re grateful for the fantastic support we receive throughout the year, September is a particularly important time for our shops."

For more information, visit www.oxfam.org.uk/get-involved/second-hand-september.
