Brackenfield School Headteacher Joe Masterson is preparing to step down after six years in the role.

The Harrogate independent preparatory school has announced that Phil Soutar will become Interim Head from September.

Joe has overseen a period of growth and development at Brackenfield, with the school praising his commitment to pupils’ academic achievement, personal development and emotional wellbeing.

He will leave during Brackenfield’s 50th anniversary year.

Joe said:

“After six wonderful years leading Brackenfield School, the time has come for me to embark on a new challenge.

“I do so with immense pride in all that we have achieved together as a school community.

“I would like to thank our pupils, families and staff for their unwavering support throughout my time as Headmaster. Being part of such a warm, caring and ambitious community has been a genuine privilege.”


Phil brings more than 30 years of teaching and leadership experience to the role, including 20 years as a prep school head.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a97c646b3997f4ade1.jpg)


His previous roles include several years as Head of Prep at Ashville College in Harrogate.

He also served on the Executive Council of the Independent Schools Association for 15 years.

Phil said:

“I am thrilled to be joining the Brackenfield community during such a special milestone year and would like to thank Joe for his warm welcome and outstanding leadership.

“Brackenfield has a well-deserved reputation for nurturing happy, confident and successful young people, and I am excited to build upon the excellent foundations already in place.”


John Forsyth, CEO of Forfar Education, thanked Joe for his contribution to the school.

He said:

“Joe has made an exceptional contribution to Brackenfield School over the past six years.

“His leadership, dedication and unwavering commitment to the pupils have helped shape the school into the thriving and successful community it is today.”


Joe and Phil will work together during the autumn term to provide continuity for pupils, staff and families.
