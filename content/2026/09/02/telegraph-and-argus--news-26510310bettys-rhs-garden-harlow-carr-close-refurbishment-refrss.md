A POPULAR tearoom will close for a month-long refurbishment to refresh its look and improve visitor experience.

Bettys Café Tea Rooms at RHS Garden Harlow Carr will close temporarily from September 7 to October 5 while upgrades are made to both the café and shop.

Liz Thwaite, head of site at RHS Garden Harlow Carr, said: "We’re delighted to see the refurbishment of Bettys Harlow Carr and to continue our long-standing partnership with such an iconic Yorkshire brand.

"We know Bettys is a much-loved part of a visit to the garden, and we’re excited to welcome guests back to a refreshed café this autumn."

The rest of RHS Garden Harlow Carr will remain open during the works, with a selection of alternative food and drink options available for visitors.

Fans of Bettys can also visit the nearby Harrogate branch or order products online during the closure.

When the tearoom reopens, guests can look forward to a refreshed interior, the full menu, and a new Bettys To Go Window at the garden entrance for takeaway treats.

Nicky Sellers, branch director at Bettys, said: "We’re excited to be investing in Bettys Harlow Carr and creating an even more welcoming experience for our customers."
