A Bishop Auckland bakery which produces around 150 handmade pies every day has been named among the top 10 in a regional search for the best in the business.

Gregory's Bakers and Deli, on Newgate Street in Bishop Auckland, has been shortlisted in The Northern Echo's Best Bakery 2026 competition, which celebrates the finest bakeries across County Durham and Darlington.

Founded in 1850, the business has remained a familiar sight in the town for more than 175 years and continues to attract hungry customers with its homemade pies, pastries and sandwiches.

Owners Sarah Hall, 36, and Claire Brown, 37, took over the bakery from their parents almost five years ago, continuing a long-standing family connection with the business.

  Gregory's Bakers & Deli in Bishop Auckland pictured Karen Kitch, Debra Quarmby Manager, Sarah Hall Owner, Stacey Wright and Julie Downing Picture: SARAH CALDECOTT 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Sarah said: “The shop's been here since 1850. It used to be a butchers as well, but now it's just a bakery.

“My mam and dad had it for 20 years and they bought it from the Gregory family, who owned it before them. Me and my sister have had it for just under five years now.”

The sisters are also the bakery's bakers, producing hundreds of items by hand each day.

Sarah said: “We’re best known for our pies. We do corned beef, steak and onion, mince and onion and cheese pies.

“On a normal day we'll make about 150 large pies, and then we do small pies, quiches, sausage rolls and sandwiches as well.”

The bakery has built a loyal following over the years, while busy festive periods often see staff working around the clock to keep up with the high demand.

Sarah said: “At Christmas it's absolutely crazy. We have to do night shifts and everything just to get there.”

She believes Gregory's popularity comes from its commitment to traditional baking methods.

Sarah said: “Everything's handmade. We make our own pastry and all our fillings are cooked here.

  Gregory's Bakers & Deli in Bishop Auckland pictured Karen Kitch, Debra Quarmby Manager, Sarah Hall Owner, Stacey Wright and Julie Downing Picture: SARAH CALDECOTT 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

“Everything that goes into the pies is made here and rolled out by hand. I think that's why everyone knows a Gregory's pie.”

Taking on the business was a natural step for the sisters, who had both worked there since leaving school.

Sarah said: “We worked here for mam and dad anyway and we were the bakers, so we knew what we were doing.

“When they decided to retire, me and Claire decided we would carry on and take it on ourselves.”

The bakery has now been nominated by customers as one of the finalists in this year's Northern Echo competition.

  Gregory's Bakers & Deli in Bishop Auckland pictured Karen Kitch, Debra Quarmby Manager, Sarah Hall Owner, Stacey Wright and Julie Downing Picture: SARAH CALDECOTT

Sarah said: “It's great to be nominated and know that people appreciate the effort and everything that goes into it.

“We have early mornings and work hard, so it's nice to know all of our hard work is appreciated.”
