GABRIELLE will perform at Bradford Live next spring as part of her upcoming UK tour.

The show, taking place on Friday, April 2, 2027, will feature British R&B group Damage as special guests.

Gabrielle is known for hits including Rise, Dreams, Out of Reach, Sunshine, and When A Woman.

Her 1999 album Rise achieved 4x Platinum status, and her greatest hits collection Dreams Can Come True also reached the same milestone.

Across her career, Gabrielle has achieved six Top 10 albums, including 2018’s Under My Skin, and returned in 2024 with A Place In Your Heart.

Gabrielle has won two BRIT Awards, two MOBO Awards, and an Ivor Novello Award.

Damage, who will open the show, first reached the charts in 1996 with Love II Love and are known for UK hits including Wonderful Tonight, Ghetto Romance, and Love Guaranteed.

Their debut album Forever sold millions of copies and helped establish their reputation on the UK R&B scene.

Tickets for the show are available now from bradfordlive.co.uk.
