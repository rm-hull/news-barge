## Two women fined after household waste found fly-tipped in East Yorkshire

Two women have been fined after waste linked to their homes was found dumped in separate fly-tipping incidents in Goole and Bridlington *(Image: East Riding of Yorkshire Council)*

Two women have been fined after waste linked to their homes was found dumped in separate fly-tipping incidents.

Sophie Smith, formerly of Jefferson Street in Goole, and Elizabeth Walker of Little Beck Road in Bridlington, were prosecuted at Beverley Magistrates' Court on Wednesday, August 19, for failing to properly dispose of household waste, breaching their legal duty of care.

Smith was fined £262 after rubbish was found dumped in an alley between Jefferson Street and Carter Street on three occasions.

CCTV footage showed someone leaving her address and dumping waste on May 14, May 20, and June 2 last year.

Walker was fined £232 after household waste from her home was found dumped in Woldgate in Bridlington on March 9 last year.

She told officers she had given the waste to someone else to dispose of.

Carl Skelton, director of streetscene services at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "Dumping waste is never acceptable.

"We provide proper and legal ways of disposing of it.

"Even if you give it to someone else to dispose of it, you are responsible for your own rubbish, and if it gets abandoned, we will take action where we can."

Fly-tipping can result in a fixed penalty of up to £1,000.
