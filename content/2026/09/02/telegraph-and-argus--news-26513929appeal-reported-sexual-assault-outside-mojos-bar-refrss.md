Police are appealing for two women to come forward following a reported sexual assault.

The incident happened outside Mojo’s bar on Merrion Street, Leeds, between 1am and 2am on Wednesday, July 29.

A man allegedly approached and sexually touched the women.

A 19-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of sexual assault and released on bail as the investigation continues.

Officers have not yet identified the two women.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers from the Leeds Adult Safeguarding team have been conducting multiple enquiries into reports of a sexual assault on two female victims outside Mojo’s bar on Merrion Street, Leeds, on July 29."

The spokesperson added: "A 19-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of sexual assault in connection with the matter, and has been released on bail pending continuing investigation.

"Despite a number of enquiries, detectives have so far been unable to identify the two victims and would ask them to come forward to speak with specialist officers.

Police urged the women, or anyone who knows who they are, to contact the Leeds District Adult Safeguarding Unit.

Anyone with information can call 101 or use the online live chat at westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat, quoting crime number 13260431258.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
