POLICE have issued an update after a man was found dead near an Asda petrol station in Bradford.

Emergency services were called to the site on Rooley Lane at around 6.20am on Monday, August 17, after a concern for welfare was reported.

A man in his 40s was found dead at the scene.

His death was initially treated as unexplained.

  The scene after a man was found dead near the Asda petrol station on Rooley Lane 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## Police update

The force have now said the death is being treated as non-suspicious.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "This is now being treated as a non-suspicious death.

"A file will be prepared for the coroner."

## Police scene in place

Officers kept a police presence at the petrol station for a few days while enquiries continued.

Forensic specialists examined the area near the forecourt, where a police tent had been set up.

The petrol station was closed during the investigation, and drivers could not use the neighbouring car wash.

Shortly after the death, Bierley and Holme Wood ward Councillor Matt Edwards (Greens) said: "Any news like this is deeply sad, and my thoughts are with the man's family and loved ones."
