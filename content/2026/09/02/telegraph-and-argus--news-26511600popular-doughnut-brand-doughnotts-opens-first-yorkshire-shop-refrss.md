DOUGHNUT fans are in for a sweet surprise.

Independent doughnut brand Doughnotts is opening a new shop at Frasers Plus Designer Outlet Leeds, its first location in Yorkshire, and is giving away a free doughnut with the first 100 purchases on launch day to celebrate.

Darren Winter, centre director at Frasers Plus Designer Outlet Leeds, said: "Doughnotts’ incredible offering will give our visitors another great place to stop and enjoy a treat during their day with us.

"We’re especially honoured that Doughnotts has chosen us as the home of its first Yorkshire location.

"It’s a really exciting first for the outlet and we can’t wait for our shoppers to get a taste of what they have to offer."

Doughnotts was founded in 2015 by Meg Scaddan and Wade Smith in Nottingham, starting in their family kitchen and growing into a well-loved Midlands brand.

The shop will serve handmade doughnuts and changing seasonal specials, with drink and doughnut deals also available.
