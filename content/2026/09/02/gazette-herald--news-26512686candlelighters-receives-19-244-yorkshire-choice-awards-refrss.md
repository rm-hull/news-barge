A regional awards event has raised more than £19,000 to support children with cancer.

The Yorkshire Choice Awards, held in June, raised £19,244 for Candlelighters, a charity supporting families of children with cancer across Yorkshire.

Melanie Malcolm and Jo Maltby, directors of the Yorkshire Choice Awards, said: "We are incredibly proud to have raised £19,244 for Candlelighters, our chosen charity after they won the Yorkshire Choice Awards Charitable Excellence Award 2025.

"A huge thank you to everyone who donated and helped us raise such a fantastic amount for Candlelighters and the wonderful work they do."

The funds will help Candlelighters provide emotional, practical, and financial support to families.

Emily Wragg, CEO of Candlelighters, said the donation will make a significant impact.

She said: "The amazing amount raised could fund over 1,100 hours of support from our dedicated family support workers, based at Leeds Children's Hospital."
