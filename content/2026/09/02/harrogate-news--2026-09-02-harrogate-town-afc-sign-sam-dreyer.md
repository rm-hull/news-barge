Harrogate Town AFC have signed Torquay centre back Sam Dreyer, for an undisclosed fee.

The 22-year-old signs having made over 80 appearances for The Gulls and on the back of a season where he was named the club’s Young Player of the Year.


**Sam Dreyer said:**

I’m really happy to be here, there’s a really good vibe about the place and the project seems really good so I’m excited to get going.

I’m a centre half that can do both sides of the game, can play but can also head, tackle and put my body on the line to stop goals.

I want to play as many games as possible here, play as well as possible and hopefully it’ll be a successful season for us.



Town’s newest recruit started his career at Stevenage and featured for Biggleswade, Hendon and Bedford before signing for Peterborough in 2023.

A loan spell at Hampton and Richmond is where Dreyer caught the eye of Torquay, who swooped for his services ahead of the 2024/25 season.

The centre-back was an instant success, being named in the 2024/25 National South Team of the Season and eventually being made club Vice-Captain.

Dreyer will wear the number 16 shirt at Town and is available for this weekend’s trip to Scunthorpe, subject to FA and National League approval.
