An uninsured motorbiker whose 'recklessness' killed a beloved grandfather has been jailed.

The family of Alan Lloyd, 75, said in a statement: "Our lives have been shattered, the stress and anxiety that the loss of Dad has caused, has been immense on all the family."

  Granddad Alan Lloyd was 75 when he was hit by motorcyclist Reece Manley as he walked across a road 

*(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

Reece Manley, 26, of Welford Road, Filey, was riding a Kawasaki EX motorcycle without a licence or insurance on the evening of March 26, 2022, when he struck Mr Lloyd on the westbound A6110 ring road.

Mr Lloyd was crossing the road when Manley, travelling toward Wortley from the direction of the M621, collided with him.

Mr Lloyd's family said in their statement: "Our Dad wasn’t ready to go and we as a family were not prepared to lose him in such a way.

"We didn’t get to hug Dad, tell him we loved him and say goodbye.

"Dad was a loving and brilliant Grandad to his grandkids."

Manley was jailed for 14 months after admitting causing Mr Lloyd's death by careless driving.

  Motorcyclist Reece Manley caused the death of a beloved granddad on the Leeds Outer Ring Road 

*(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

Detective Sergeant Nina Roper of West Yorkshire Police's major collision enquiry team said: "Mr Lloyd’s death has clearly devastated his family as their victim statement has made clear, and our thoughts remain with them today.

"The actions of Reece Manley robbed them of making more precious memories with a much-loved father and grandfather who was enjoying his retirement after a lifetime of hard work.

"Manley’s riding on that night was clearly unsafe and has resulted in Mr Lloyd and his family paying the ultimate price for his recklessness.

"The fact that Manley was also prepared to ride unlicenced and uninsured speaks volumes for his attitude to the safety of others."
