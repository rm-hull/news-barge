A man in his 60s was airlifted to hospital with suspected “serious abdominal injuries” after being thrown from his motorbike in County Durham.

Emergency services were called to a crash on Tuesday (September 1), at around 2.05pm, on Greencroft Parkway, Stanley.

Durham Police said the rider was travelling towards the junction with the A693 on a green Kawasaki ZX6 motorcycle.

It is believed he overtook another vehicle.

The bike then lost control and struck a kerb, throwing the rider into the air.

The man was taken to hospital by air ambulance with suspected serious abdominal injuries.

Police are now appealing for witnesses to come forward, particularly the driver of the vehicle which the motorbike is believed to have overtaken shortly before the crash.

A spokesperson said: “We would like to speak to anyone who saw the bike around this time, in particular the driver of the vehicle the bike overtook prior to the collision.

“If you can help, please contact Durham Constabulary’s Serious Collision Investigation Unit by email at [SCIU@durham.police.uk](mailto:SCIU@durham.police.uk), quoting incident number 242 of September 1.”
