A popular Darlington wine bar is set to close its doors, leaving punters "gutted".

Vesuvio, on Houndgate, has announced that it will be calling last orders in the town when its lease comes to an end on September 30.

Owners Les and Kendra Fry said they were "immensely grateful” for all the support and that they were sad to leave Vesuvio - but added that “new adventures are on the horizon”.

The couple have been in the industry for 20 years and are the brains behind the former Voodoo Café on Skinnergate and bar, which shut in 2021, and off-license Angel's Cut on Duke Street.

Vesuvio has been a staple of Houndgate for more than a decade and was taken over by the couple in 2021.

Punters have already voiced their devastation over the closure, with one person branding it the “end of an era” and another saying the couple will “very much be missed”.

A statement from the couple, posted on Facebook, said: “A big thank you to all our amazing friends, loyal customers and Darlington Bar scene.

“Our time at Vesuvio is coming to an end. The lease will officially conclude on September 30 and Vesuvio will close.

“After 20 years of running a bar, from our crazy Voodoo days, to Angels Cut and finally Vesuvio, we are still here having racked up some hours, have a huge knowledge of our products and trade, made many friends and won quite a few awards along the way.

“We will be sad to leave Vesuvio as we have worked hard to rebuild it back into the great bar that it was in the past.

“We are immensely grateful to all of you who have continued with us on our journey through much fun, many trials and triumphs.
