  **A LANDMARK bridge on the North Yorkshire coast is currently fully blocked after getting stuck again.**

North Yorkshire Council have warned motorists and pedestrians after Whitby Swing Bridge has become blocked - with access prevented to all.

  This follows **__closures over the past few months__** following malfunctions, with a member of the council adding in July that they were excavating behind the steel plate to create additional space for the bridge to swing

Yesterday evening, a spokesperson for the council said: “Whitby Swing Bridge is currently stuck and there is no access across the bridge. Engineers will be on site early tomorrow morning and will we update as soon as the bridge is open again."

They also apologised for the inconvenience.
