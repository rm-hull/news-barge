A stolen AJS motorcycle has been recovered by police.

The Cadwell model appeared to be in good condition apart from a missing side panel. It was found in a shed on Central Avenue in Shipley.

The bike had been taken from Kirkstall Bridge Shopping Park last month.

A spokesman for West Yorkshire Police said: "Great work from our partners and local PCSO for locating and identifying the bike."

AJS motorcycles are modern, small-capacity models with classic styling, sold in the UK under a historic British badge but manufactured in partnership with Chinese firms.

The name dates to 1909 and A. J. Stevens & Co. of Wolverhampton, famous for roadsters, racers and Isle of Man TT success.

The current business, based in Andover, Hampshire, trades on that heritage but serves a distinctly modern market: new riders, CBT holders, commuters and people who want classic-looking machines without the cost, weight or power of a large-capacity motorcycle.
