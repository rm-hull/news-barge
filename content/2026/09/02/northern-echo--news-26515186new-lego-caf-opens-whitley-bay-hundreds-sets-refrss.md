A new Lego-themed café has opened in the North East, giving families and brick-building fans a place to build, relax and enjoy a coffee.

Bricks & Brews Café has opened on Whitley Road in Whitley Bay, offering hundreds of Lego sets, refreshments and dedicated play areas.

The new venue in North Tyneside combines a traditional café experience with hands-on building sessions designed for all ages and abilities.

  The seating area inside Bricks & Brews Café where visitors can build LEGO sets and relax. 

*(Image: Bricks & Brews Cafe)*

Visitors can book one, two, or three-hour building sessions. Prices start from £5 per person and going up to £10 per person for those who want to build for longer.

Adults are also admitted free of charge during designated Teen Time sessions.

Visitors can choose from a library of official LEGO sets for different ages and skill levels.

Free-play brick stations are available, as well as a car-building and ramp-racing area where children can test their designs.

  LEGO figures sit beside coffee cups at the new Bricks & Brews Café in Whitley Bay. 

*(Image: Bricks & Brews Cafe)*

The café also serves hot and cold drinks, including coffee, speciality teas and soft drinks.

Cakes, traybakes and locally sourced scones are also on the menu.

The owners say the café is fully wheelchair-accessible and sensory-friendly, with ear defenders available for visitors who need them.

The attraction is expected to be popular with families seeking indoor activities at weekends and during school holidays.
