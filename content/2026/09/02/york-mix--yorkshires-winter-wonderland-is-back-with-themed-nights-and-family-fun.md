One of York’s favourite festive events has announced its return.

Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland will return to York Designer Outlet for its 21st season – and celebrates over one million skaters since its debut in York two decades ago.

With the largest outdoor ice rink in the north of England, funfair, Santa’s Grotto and alpine Chalet, the popular festive event offers a fun-filled winter experience for all ages.

Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland will open from **Friday 13 November 2026 until Sunday 3 January 2027**.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Yorkshires-Winter-Wonderland-at-night.webp)

This year the iconic ice rink will have a landscaped woodland scene as its backdrop, featuring giant trees edged with ever-changing colourful neon ropes bringing them to life, as well as glowing Christmas Bauble trees.

The illuminations have been designed by the team behind Aglow! at Bishop Auckland.

Other new features for 2026 include a re-designed Alpine Chalet café with an evening bar and a performance stage which will host live music and fun theme nights throughout the season – such as Fizz and Fondue, Acoustic and Aperol, and Charcuterie and Cocktails.

The fully-licensed Chalet serves an all-day menu of tasty winter-warming food and sweet treats, hot and cold drinks, as well as seasonal favourites such as hot chocolate, mulled wine and prosecco.

Outside, a specially constructed pit with real fire will offer marshmallows to toast, and the German Grill ‘bratwursterie’ will be serving up gourmet sausages, mulled wine and hot chocolate.

The funfair offers fun for all ages with rides old and new – including the Waltzer, Twister, and the Brooklands Dodgems dating from 1937.

Christmas wouldn’t be the same without a visit to Santa and his elves. After meeting Santa and receiving a special gift, visitors can also support the annual Toy Appeal, helping bring Christmas magic to local children and families living in need across York & Selby.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Children-at-The-Chalet-cafe-at-Yorkshires-Winter-Wonderland.webp)

Paul Tyler, centre manager at York Designer Outlet said: “For many families, the event has become a cherished fixture in their festive calendar, offering fun outdoor activities for all ages to enjoy.

“We love seeing the joy it brings, and it truly helps spread that festive cheer while people are also able to tick off their Christmas shopping list!

“The ice rink has become a real pillar of the community, bringing together families, couples, and individuals during the festive season to create lasting memories.”

Jennifer Rojko, event organiser of Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland also added: “Every year we get excited about bringing York’s favourite Christmas event back to the Designer Outlet and unveiling our new ideas and festive touches.

“This season promises to be as colourful as ever, and our new look Chalet with its live music and theme nights will bring a new buzz to the event.

“Not only do we offer great winter exercise and fun for all the family, but with free parking and superb shopping Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland makes a great day – and evening – out!”

Tickets are now on sale here with Early Bird 20% discounts applying until 30 September, offering some super-savings for those wanting to book their slot early.
