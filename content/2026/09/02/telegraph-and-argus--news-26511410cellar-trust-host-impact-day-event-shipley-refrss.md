THE Cellar Trust will host an afternoon celebrating its work and future plans in Shipley.

Impact Day 2026 will take place at Farfield, Shipley, on Thursday, September 24, 2026, from 2pm to 4pm.

The local event will celebrate another year of supporting people across Bradford, Airedale, Wharfedale and Craven.

Its Impact Report will showcase the difference made through services, partnerships and community support, while outlining ambitions for the future and sharing more about The Cellar Trust’s new strategy.

Visitors can explore the latest impact report, highlighting its community impact.

Service experts will discuss challenges and opportunities, answer questions and meet people involved in delivery.

The programme will cover future plans, partnership opportunities, Farfield’s next development phase, and how attendees can help shape what comes next.

Tea and coffee will be available during networking, giving attendees time to build relationships and explore shared opportunities.

It offers celebration, learning and networking for local attendees.
