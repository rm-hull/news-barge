A man has been arrested after several vehicles were damaged in a North East hospital car park.

Cleveland Police said a 31-year-old was arrested and charged with criminal damage after vehicles in a car park at Roseberry Park Hospital, on Marton Road in Middlesbrough, were damaged late on August 31.

The incident prompted Tees, Esk and Wear Valleys NHS Foundation Trust to say staff should be able to come to work without worrying about damage to their personal property.

The damage prompted a warning from the Trust, which runs Roseberry Park.

A spokesperson said: “Our staff deserve to come to work without worrying about damage to their personal property.

"They care for some of the most vulnerable people in our region and should not have to experience this kind of behaviour.

“We take incidents of this nature very seriously and are working closely with the police. Any criminal activity on our trust premises will be reported immediately.”

A spokesperson for Cleveland Police said: "Several vehicles parked in Roseberry Park Hospital car park on Marton Road in Middlesbrough, were damaged late in the evening of August 31.

"A 31-year-old man has since been arrested and charged with criminal damage."

The incident came as Cleveland recorded the highest rate of criminal damage and arson in England and Wales, at 15.0 offences per 1,000 people in the year to March 2026.
