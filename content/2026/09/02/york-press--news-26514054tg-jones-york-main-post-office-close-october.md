## Long-standing York city centre shop and Post Office set to close next month

TG Jones - previously WHSmiths - will be closing its doors as will York's main post office *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

A LONG-STANDING York city centre shop is set to close its doors next month – in a move that will also shut the city’s main post office for the second time in less than 10 years.

TG Jones, formerly known as WHSmith, has revealed that it will be shutting its Coney Street shop – which also contains the city’s main post office - on October 19.

The Post Office is set to close too, seven years after the branch moved into the shop following the closure of the standalone Lendal location.

As reported by The Press on Monday, the TG Jones brand has secured court approval for a major restructuring, which is expected to lead to the closure of around 150 stores and affect hundreds of jobs.

Shops across the country have revealed that they are set to close, with banners in the window of the Coney Street shop reading “Store Closing Down” and “Everything Must Go”.

On the Post Office website, an advert to fill the main city centre post office has been advertised with a closing date of next Thursday (September 10).

It says: “These contracts will be awarded directly as Post Office Ltd must act with extreme and unavoidable urgency to protect essential services relied upon by communities."

A Post Office spokesperson added: “We fully recognise the challenging trading conditions that TG Jones and many other high street retailers are facing at the moment.

"TG Jones had its restructuring plan approved by the High Court on 1 July, which includes seeking new commercial terms with landlords for the majority of their estate.

"Consequently, TG Jones is looking to close their York store. Regrettably, York Post Office is due to close on Wednesday 14 October.

“We know how important our products and services are to the local community. We want to open a replacement branch as soon as possible. This can take around six months to re-open a replacement branch."

The nearest Post Offices in York city centre are Micklegate and Colliergate.

A spokesperson for TG Jones said: "As part of our work to secure the long-term future of TG Jones, we have confirmed that our York store will close.

"We appreciate this will be disappointing news for our colleagues, customers and the local community. Our priority now is on supporting those affected colleagues while continuing to serve our customers until the store closes.

"We would like to thank our colleagues in York for their valued contribution to TG Jones."
