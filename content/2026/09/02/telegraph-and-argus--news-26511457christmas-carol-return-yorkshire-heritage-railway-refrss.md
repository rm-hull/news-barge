A FESTIVE theatre experience is set to return this winter, bringing a classic Christmas story to life on a Yorkshire heritage railway.

A Christmas Carol will once again be performed on board the Keighley and Worth Valley Railway (KWVR) during December, combining live theatre with a steam train journey through the Worth Valley.

Passengers will join Ebenezer Scrooge, Jacob Marley, and the Ghosts of Christmas Past, Present, and Yet to Come on a magical evening ride set in the Victorian era.

Noel Hartley, marketing manager at KWVR, said: "A Christmas Carol has become one of the highlights of our festive programme because it brings together two wonderful traditions – the magic of live theatre and the nostalgia of travelling by steam train.

"The story is as relevant today as it has ever been and experiencing it aboard one of Britain's most famous heritage railways creates something genuinely memorable.

"Whether you're rediscovering Dickens' classic or introducing it to a new generation, it's a magical way to begin the Christmas celebrations."

*(Image: Tom Marshall)*

The immersive production transforms the railway into a moving stage, where passengers become part of the story as it unfolds around them.

Actors guide audiences through Scrooge’s journey of redemption and the true spirit of Christmas, all while travelling in heritage carriages between beautifully preserved Victorian stations.

The event has proved popular in previous years, drawing visitors from across Yorkshire and further afield, and is recommended for families, couples, and groups of friends.

With steam trains running after dark, the experience captures the magic and nostalgia of a Dickensian Christmas.

Performances will take place at 5pm and 7pm on select evenings in December.

  A Christmas Carol will once again be performed on board the Keighley and Worth Valley Railway (KWVR) during December 

*(Image: Tom Marshall)*

Tickets are priced at £39.50 for December 3, 4, and 10; £45 for December 5, 11, 17, 21, and 22; £48 for December 18, 19, and 23; and £35 for Christmas Eve.

With previous productions selling quickly, early booking is strongly recommended.

For more information or to purchase tickets, visit www.kwvr.co.uk.
