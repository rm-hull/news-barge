Children collected stamps on activity cards, wore festival wristbands, and even made friends with some visiting goats.

It was all part of a day of fun at Rosedene Stokesley Forest School’s first birthday celebration, as the nursery transformed its outdoor space into a family-friendly mini festival.

Named Rosedene Fest, the event took place at the nursery’s forest school site on Stokesley Business Park and featured a live singer, sensory play stations, face painting, flower crown making, and an ice cream van.

Goats were among the guests of honour, much to the delight of the children.

Cheri Higgins, operations manager at Rosedene Nurseries, said: "It was wonderful to see so many of our children and families together enjoying Rosedene Fest and celebrating everything that has happened during our first year in Stokesley.

"We’ve had such fantastic support from families and the wider community since the nursery opened last September, so we wanted to bring everyone together and say ‘thank you’ before the start of the new term.

"The children absolutely loved having their own wristbands and working their way around all the different activities, and the goats were definitely a highlight.

"It was a really special way to celebrate our first year and everything the team and our families have helped us achieve."

The nursery, based on Ellerbeck Way, opened in September last year and offers places for up to 100 children aged from birth to four years old, as well as a holiday club for children up to 11.

Its purpose-built forest school is designed to encourage outdoor learning, helping children explore nature, build confidence, and learn new skills.

The Rosedene team said they are now looking forward to a second year of nurturing children to be "kind, curious, confident, and ready to shape the world."
