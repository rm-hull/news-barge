New rules coming into force this week will cut school-uniform costs for thousands of children returning to classrooms across Teesside and County Durham.

From this September, schools will be limited in the number of compulsory branded uniform items they can require pupils to wear.

Most schools will be restricted to a maximum of three branded items, while secondary and middle schools will be allowed up to four, if one of the items is a tie.

  The Government's new rules on uniform costs come into force this month, as thousands of pupils in England prepare to go back to school. 

*(Image: Getty Images)*

The measures aim to make school uniforms more affordable and stop costs becoming an extra barrier for families during the cost-of-living crisis.

A branded item is defined as any piece of clothing with features unique to a particular school, such as a logo, name or distinctive design.

Examples include embroidered blazers, jumpers with coloured trims and specially designed trousers sold by only one supplier.

The guidance encourages schools to let parents buy generic skirts, trousers and shirts from a range of retailers instead of school-specific alternatives.

Schools should also avoid single-supplier contracts where possible, giving parents more choice, and to make second-hand uniforms readily available with clear information on how families can access them.

Schools are being encouraged to consider the overall cost of preparing a child for the new school year, particularly for lower-income families, and to minimise the use of expensive items such as blazers and jumpers while avoiding frequent changes to uniform requirements.

The changes were announced by the Government in July, as part of efforts to make the cost of going back to school more affordable and prevent uniform expenses from becoming an additional burden on families during the cost-of-living crisis.

In the Tees Valley, a partnership between Uniting Uniforms and Ramsay Health Care UK is collecting and distributing pre-loved uniforms, with figures from the Department for Education putting average primary-school uniform costs at £343 per child.
