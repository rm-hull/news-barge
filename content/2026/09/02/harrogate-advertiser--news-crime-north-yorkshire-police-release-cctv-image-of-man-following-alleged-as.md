# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image of man following alleged assault in Harrogate town centre

## North Yorkshire Police has issued a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to following an alleged assault in Harrogate.

The incident happened on July 22 at the Tesco Express store on Cambridge Road.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “If you recognise the person, please contact us.”
