A woman has been left seriously injured after being struck by a car at a roundabout in Northumberland.

The crash happened just before 11am on Sunday, August 30, at the junction of Ridley Avenue and Wellington Street in Blyth, when a white Ford Kuga hit a pedestrian.

The woman, who is in her 40s, was taken to hospital with serious injuries and is now in a stable condition.

The Ford Kuga driver stayed at the scene after the collision and is now helping police with their investigation.

Northumbria Police said: "Anyone who was in the area at the time, saw what happened or has dashcam footage is asked to contact Northumbria Police.

"We're keen to hear from anyone with CCTV, dash cam or mobile phone footage of the collision, or the moments leading up to it, to come forward.

"If this is you, please send us a direct message on social media quoting reference: NP-20260830-0408."

Part of the junction was closed after the crash but reopened just after 3pm the same day.
