A dog walker was left "distressed" while on a walk near a fancy Darlington hotel yesterday afternoon.

Police are appealing for witnesses after the woman was approached by a man while walking near Rockliffe Hall Hotel and Spa, near Croft.

The woman, who is in her 50s, was walking alone at around 4pm on Tuesday, September 1.

Durham Police said: "As she walked along the riverbanks, with the river to her left and golf course to her right, she was suddenly approached by a man close to the marked private fishing area.

"He made a number of comments and moved towards her causing her to move back and run away to raise the alarm.

"She was left distressed, but physically unharmed."

The woman gave officers a partial description of the man, describing him as a white male, middle-aged, slim and around 5ft 10in tall.

He was wearing dark clothing and a hoodie, with the hood pulled up over his face.

Detectives are investigating the incident and have urged anyone who was in the area at the time, or who may have information, to come forward.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Durham Constabulary on 101, quoting incident number 340 of September 1.
