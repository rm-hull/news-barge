Major works at a key Middlesbrough junction are due to be completed this weekend after months of disruption for drivers.

Repairs and improvements at the A172/A174 Marton Interchange began in March, with a contraflow system in place on the A172 and occasional overnight road closures in place over the last six months.

Middlesbrough Council said the 54-year-old structure, including the A174 slip roads, is expected to reopen fully in the early hours of Saturday, September 5.

Improvements have included concrete repairs beneath the bridge, replacement waterproofing and expansion joints, resurfacing work, new kerbs and footways, and the installation of a pedestrian parapet and guardrails.

Motorists face final overnight disruption on Thursday, September 3, and Friday, September 4, as crews add road markings and remove traffic controls before reopening.

A Middlesbrough Council spokesman said: “These essential repairs and improvements are a key part of our ongoing bridges and structures maintenance programme.

“Working closely with Esh Construction, we’ve worked hard to avoid lengthy disruptions and keep traffic flowing across the bridge throughout.

“We’re grateful to the public for their patience and understanding throughout this important project.”

Steven Garrigan, divisional director at Esh Construction, said: “This scheme has involved a significant programme of repairs and upgrades to extend the lifespan of an important piece of Middlesbrough’s infrastructure.

“Working in close partnership with Middlesbrough Council, we’ve successfully delivered a range of structural repairs and improvements that will enhance the resilience of the structure and ensure it continues to serve the community for decades to come.”

The work previously prevented drivers crossing the bridge from turning onto the A174, while traffic joining Stokesley Road from below could turn left only.
