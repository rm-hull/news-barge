According to the education authorities, approximately 62,000 new school seats were created, and a total of more than seven billion euros was invested. Forty-one new school buildings and 76 new gyms were constructed, and numerous facilities were expanded and renovated. This was celebrated this morning in the new building of the Heinrich-Hertz-Gymnasium in Friedrichshain.
