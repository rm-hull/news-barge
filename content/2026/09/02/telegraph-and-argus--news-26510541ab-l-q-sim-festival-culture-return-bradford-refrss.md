A festival celebrating Islamic heritage and culture is returning to Bradford.

The Abū’l-Qāsim Festival of Culture will take place on Sunday, September 6, at Mustafa Mount on Emm Lane.

The event is free to attend and will run from 12pm to 8pm.

Set within the grounds of Mustafa Mount, the former University of Bradford School of Management campus, the festival is designed to celebrate Islamic heritage, community and family life.

Organised by Souq Uthman, the monthly marketplace held at Mustafa Mount,

It was launched in 2025 in line with what many Muslims marked as the 1,500th year since the birth of the prophet Muhammad.

A spokesperson for Souq Uthman said: "The Abū’l-Qāsim Festival of Culture is an opportunity for families and communities to come together in a spirit of celebration and learning.

"We want people to experience the beauty of our heritage through activities, creativity, food, storytelling and shared experiences.

"Whether you are coming for the workshops, the marketplace or simply to enjoy a day out with family and friends, there will be something for everyone."

Attendees can take part in archery, duff drumming, storytelling, and interactive workshops exploring Islamic arts and traditions.

There will also be horses, outdoor games, arts and crafts, sensory play, face painting, henna, and a bouncy castle.

The marketplace will showcase local businesses, artisans, and community organisations.
