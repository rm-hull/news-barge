## Railway society to showcase historic steam photographs in Driffield

GNER HST passing Doncaster *(Image: East Yorkshire Railway Society)*

A railway society is hosting a special meeting showcasing preserved steam photographs.

The East Yorkshire Railway Society (EYRS), founded in 1991, will hold its next gathering on Monday, September 14, at Driffield Community Centre, featuring a presentation by member Tony Arnold.

The meeting, set to start at 7pm for a 7.30pm start, will kick off with an hour-long display of preserved steam slides from the collection of the late Charlie Williams.

Mr Williams was an early member of EYRS and was known for his prolific photography of preserved steam, capturing the scenes with lineside passes at both the North Yorkshire Moors and Keighley & Worth Valley Railways.

His extensive collection of slides was left to Mr Arnold after Mr Williams tragically passed away in a train-photographing accident near Selby in 2014 at the age of 70.

After a 20-minute interval, another hour-long presentation, 'Around the Regions from Devon to Yorkshire', will feature Mr Arnold's own slides.

Mr Arnold, a longstanding society member, is also a prolific transport photographer.

His interests span the UK rail scene, both past and present, London Underground, and buses.

He is a volunteer signaller on the Derwent Valley Light Railway, and a member of both the Hull Railway Correspondence & Travel Society and Hull Bus Restorers.

Admission for members is £3, and non-members £4, with visitors able to become a member on the night for a £5 annual subscription.

The EYRS meets monthly, excluding August and December, on the second Monday of each month.
