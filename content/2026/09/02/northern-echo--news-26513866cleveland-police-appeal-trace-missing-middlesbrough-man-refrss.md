Police are looking for a missing Middlesbrough man who disappeared over the weekend.

Liam Loughlin, 33, was last seen leaving Tudor Lodge Service Station on Marton Road at 1.34am on Sunday, August 30.

Officers from Cleveland Police said he was heading towards the James Cook University Hospital in Middlesbrough - and he hasn't been reported as seen since.

Liam was wearing pink Nike shorts, a pink Nike zip-up jacket and black trainers.

A Cleveland Police spokesperson said: "Liam, aged 33, is missing from Middlesbrough and officers are appealing for information to help find him."

Anyone who has seen Liam or knows where he is should call Cleveland Police on 101, quoting reference SE26175054.
