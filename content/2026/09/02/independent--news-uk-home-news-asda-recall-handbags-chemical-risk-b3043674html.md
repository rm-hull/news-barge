Asda has recalled three lines of women’s handbags after they were found to pose a "serious" chemical risk that could cause damage to the reproductive system.

The all-black items, which have an "unknown" country of origin, feature excessive levels of Di(2-ethylhexyl) phthalate (DEHP), the Office for Product Safety and Standards (OPSS) reported.

The OPSS stated that phthalates, which soften plastic to make it more flexible, can harm children's health and potentially damage reproductive organs.

The OPSS said: “We recommend that consumers stop using the product immediately.

“Return the product to any Asda store for a full refund.”

Asda has recalled the bags: the £10 Front Pocket Xbody Bag carrying the product code 1100258937001; the £16 Opp Tote Bag with the product code 1100260834001; and the £8 Black Nylon Phone Bag with the product code 1100258939001.

Meanwhile, sales at Asda are on track to grow for the first time in two years, despite pressure on household budgets caused by the broader economic environment.

The private equity-backed supermarket chain stated that it remains in the "foothills of recovery" as it moves forward with a major turnaround strategy.

Boss Allan Leighton suggested that the company has now completed roughly one third of this restructuring process.

“We have been very consistent with the fact the turnaround will take between three and five years,” Mr Leighton said.
