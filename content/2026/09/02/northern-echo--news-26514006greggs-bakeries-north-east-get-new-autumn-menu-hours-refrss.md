Greggs fans have less than 24 hours left to wait before the bakery chain's eagerly anticipated autumn menu lands in stores across the North East.

The North East-founded bakery will launch its seasonal line-up on Thursday, September 3.

Customers can expect 11 new and returning items in around 200 stores across Darlington, Teesside and the wider North East, as it rolls out its autumn menu at 200 North East bakeries.

  Greggs' autumn menu for 2026 launches in less than 24 hours. 

*(Image: Greggs)*

The Steak & Stilton Bake is returning, with blue Stilton and grilled onions alongside Greggs’ classic steak filling in a puff-pastry parcel.

The autumn menu will feature seasonal treats including a Pumpkin Spice Latte, Pumpkin Spice Doughnut and Bonfire Toffee Muffin.

Other new additions include a Buffalo Chicken Baguette, Buffalo Pizza Slice and Chicken & Pepperoni Toastie, as well as a range of cold drinks such as the Iced Pumpkin Spice Latte, Iced Caramel Chocolate, Iced Mocha and Iced Chocolate.

The full autumn range will be available in participating Greggs stores and through click and collect on the Greggs app from Thursday, September 3.

The launch follows weeks of social-media speculation from Greggs fans about the 2026 autumn menu.

The returning Pumpkin Spice Latte and Iced Pumpkin Spice Latte will also feature a new salted caramel drizzle, Greggs has confirmed.
