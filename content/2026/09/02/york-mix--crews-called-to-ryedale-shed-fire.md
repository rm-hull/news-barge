Firefighters were called to an incident in a Ryedale village early this morning (Wednesday).

They responded to a shed fire in **Keldholme** near Kirkbymoorside at**4.27am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews from Malton and Helmsley attended a fire to a shed measuring 10 by 20 metres of breeze block construction.

#### Keldholme

“Crews used one hose reel jet and a main jet to extinguish and dampen down the fire. Crews remain on scene.”
