Is it just me, or is Taylor Sheridan's idea to have two simultaneous timelines in *Lioness* season 3 making the show harder to follow by the minute?

Over the course of the five episodes we've seen, the plot has followed Joe being kidnapped by unknown Russian operatives, and, the six months leading up to her capture. But with three episodes left to go, nothing is adhering to a traditional structure of revealing plot twists, and that's leaving my head scrambled.

Still, things are incredibly fun, which is unusual to say about subject matter that's this dark. So, when does *Lioness* season 3 episode 5 arrive on Paramount+?

## What time can I watch Lioness season 3 episode 6 on Paramount+?

![Lioness | Season 3 Official Trailer | Paramount+ - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/OrunH41K3Ps/maxresdefault.jpg) 

*Lioness* season 3 episode 6 will drop on one of the world's best streaming services in the US and Canada on**Sunday, September 6 at 12am PT / 3am ET**. 

Here's when it will be released in other nations globally:

- **US**  – 12am PT / 3am ET
- **Canada** – 12am PT / 3am ET
- **UK** – 8am BST

- **Singapore** – 3pm SGT

- **New Zealand** – 7pm NZST
