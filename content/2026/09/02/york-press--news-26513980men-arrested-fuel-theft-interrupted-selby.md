TWO men have been arrested after police interrupted a fuel theft in progress at a building site in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police say its officers were called to the scene at 10.15pm on Saturday, August 29, following reports of suspicious activity at a building contractor’s site in Selby.

After arriving, police found two men sitting in a vehicle next to a generator, with a 1,000-litre container and syphoning equipment.

  


A spokesperson for the force said: "At the time of our arrival they had syphoned nearly 350 litres of diesel. Both men were quickly handcuffed and arrested, and the van was seized for being used in crime.

"Inside the van, apart from the container and syphoning equipment, various tools including a large set of bolt croppers and a crow bar were also found."

A 51-year-old man and a 49-year-old man, both from West Yorkshire, were arrested on suspicion of theft of fuel and burglary - with the younger man also arrested for going equipped with the intent to steal.

Both have been released on conditional bail while the investigation continues.

The incident has been recorded under reference number 12260169619.
