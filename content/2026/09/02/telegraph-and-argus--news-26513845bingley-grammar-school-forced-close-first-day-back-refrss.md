BINGLEY Grammar School will be closed today, which was due to be its first day for students.

The headteacher at the school, on Keighley Road, Bingley, has announced that the school will be shut today over a safeguarding issue.

Mr Weston explained the school has electrical problems, no working phones and no internet access.

## What the headteacher has said

In a message to parents and carers, headteacher Luke Weston said: "Unfortunately, the school has electrical issues around the site and no working phone system or internet which represents both a safeguarding and health and safety issue.

"We will be closing the school to all students today (September 2) and reopening tomorrow (September 3).

"The original arrangements for Wednesday will occur on Thursday.

"Therefore, all students in years 8-11 will be in an extended tutor period 8:30am to 10am to receive timetables etc.

"Year 7 will be directed to N6 upon arrival at 8:30am.

"I apologise for the late nature of this message as we have been trying to resolve the issue and for any inconvenience this causes you.

"Many thanks in advance for your help."
