TWO iconic voices of British indie rock are set to take the stage.

Guiseley Sounds Unplugged returns to Guiseley Theatre on Saturday, October 17, 2026, for its third edition.

The event will feature acoustic performances from Tom Hingley, former frontman of Inspiral Carpets, and Nigel Clark of Dodgy.

Mr Hingley will open the show with songs from the Inspiral Carpets’ back catalogue, including hits like This Is How It Feels and Saturn 5.

He fronted the band for more than 20 years, performing on their biggest tracks and touring internationally.

Mr Clark returns to Guiseley after headlining the first-ever Guiseley Sounds Festival and will perform Dodgy’s best-known songs, including Good Enough, Staying Out for the Summer, and Grassman.

With platinum-selling albums, a string of Top 20 singles, and a BRIT Award nomination, Dodgy became one of the defining British bands of the 1990s.

Amy Campbell, one of Yorkshire’s rising artists, will also perform in the theatre bar before the show and during the interval.

Described as having a "huge voice" and "strong stage presence," Ms Campbell’s polished pop sound has already begun to attract attention.

Doors open at 6.30pm, with the main show starting at 7.30pm.
