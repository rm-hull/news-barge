A cyclist was rushed to hospital after suffering serious injuries in a collision with a vehicle.

The incident happened on Huddersfield Road in Mirfield at around 3.20pm on Tuesday.

Police sealed off the road as the cyclist was taken to hospital with serious but not life-threatening injuries.

On Wednesday, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At 3.20pm yesterday afternoon (September 1) officers were called to Huddersfield Road, Mirfield, to reports a vehicle and cyclist had been in collision.

"The cyclist, a man, was taken to hospital with serious but not life-threatening injuries."

Yorkshire Ambulance Service confirmed it attended the scene.

On Wednesday, a YAS spokesperson said: "We received an emergency call at 3.15pm yesterday afternoon to report a road traffic collision on Huddersfield Road in Mirfield.

"A number of ambulance resources, including an ambulance and a team leader, were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
