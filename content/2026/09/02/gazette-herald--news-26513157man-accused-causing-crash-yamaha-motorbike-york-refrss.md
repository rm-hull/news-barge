A man has appeared before York magistrates accused of seriously injuring two people by causing a crash four years ago.

Kaylon Bruce, now 24, is alleged to have been riding a Yamaha MT motorcycle in Burdyke Avenue, Clifton, in May 2022.

He faces two charges of causing serious injury by dangerous riding of the motorbike.

Bruce, of Gipton Square, Gipton, Leeds, did not enter a plea to either charge and his case was sent to York Crown Court where he could be tried by a judge and jury.

He was released on bail until September 28 when he will appear before the higher court for a plea and trial preparation hearing and will be expected to enter his plea.
