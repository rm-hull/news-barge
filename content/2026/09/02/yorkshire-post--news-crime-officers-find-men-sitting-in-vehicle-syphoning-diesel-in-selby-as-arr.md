# Officers find men sitting in vehicle syphoning diesel in Selby as arrests made

## Two men have been arrested after police were called to a building contractor’s site in Selby.

At 10.15pm on Saturday August 29, police were called to a building contractor’s site in Selby due to reports of two men being on site, suspected of syphoning diesel from their generator.

Officers were on scene within minutes and located two men sitting in a vehicle next to a generator with a 1,000 litre container in the back of their van, and syphoning equipment between the container and the generator.

At the time of the arrival they had syphoned nearly 350 litres of diesel, North Yorkshire Police said.

![Officers find men sitting in vehicle syphoning diesel in Selby as arrests made](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmIxNDRmNDkzLTRjNDQtNGYwZS1hOWZjLTBjN2E3YjU0Y2JjNDo4YjA1ODdhMy0xZWY0LTQzZWItYmU0NC02MDcyOTZkMTBkY2I=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Both men were handcuffed and arrested and the van was seized for being used in crime.

Inside the van, apart from the container and syphoning equipment, various tools including a large set of bolt croppers and a crow bar were also found.

A 49-year-old man from the West Yorkshire area was arrested on suspicion of theft of fuel, going equipped with the intent to steal, and burglary.

A 51-year-old man also from the West Yorkshire area was arrested on suspicion of theft of fuel and burglary.

Both men have since been released on conditional bail, police said.
