Police have thwarted an attempt to steal fuel in Selby.

They were called to a building contractor’s site in the town at 10.15pm on Saturday (29 August) after reports of two men syphoning diesel from a generator.

Officers found two men sitting in a vehicle next to a generator with a 1,000 litre container in the back of their van.

There was syphoning equipment between the container and the generator.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “At the time of our arrival they had syphoned nearly 350 litres of diesel.

“Both men were quickly handcuffed and arrested, and the van was seized for being used in crime.”

They found various tools inside the van including a large set of bolt croppers and a crow bar.

A 49-year-old man from the West Yorkshire area was arrested on suspicion of fuel theft, going equipped with the intent to steal, and burglary.

A 51-year-old man also from the West Yorkshire area was arrested on suspicion of fuel theft and burglary.

Both men have since been released on conditional bail, whilst the investigation progresses.
