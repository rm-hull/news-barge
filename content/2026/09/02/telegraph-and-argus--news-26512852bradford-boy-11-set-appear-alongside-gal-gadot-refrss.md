A RISING young actor is making a name for himself on the big screen alongside some of Hollywood’s biggest stars.

Eleven-year-old Rory Wilmot, from Bradford-based Articulate Drama School, stars alongside Gal Gadot in The Runner, a new action thriller available on Prime Video from September 2 (today), 2026.

Wilmot plays Noah Marten, the son of Gadot’s character Maia, a high-powered attorney forced to race across London to save her abducted child while following cryptic instructions from a mysterious caller.

The film also features Damian Lewis and Alfred Enoch.

Wilmot said: "I loved working with Kev, the director; he was fun.

"He listened to me, and I listened to him.

"The cast were great too and friendly."

   A professional still of Rory Wilmot from the film 

*(Image: Supplied)*

He described director Kevin Macdonald as having "the full creative vision of the film."

Wilmot attends Articulate Drama School and Agency, a Shipley-based institution that trains and represents actors across the UK.

The school has helped secure opportunities for children, young people and adults in television and film.

At the heart of The Runner is the relationship between Maia and her son Noah, a dynamic Wilmot said felt authentic on screen.

He said: "My character is an only child so they are very close as mother and son.

"Gal was really natural and real playing my mum, so it felt easy.

"I hope the people watching will be at the edge of their seats."

  A headshot of Rory Wilmot 

*(Image: Emily Goldie Photography)*

The Runner marks another milestone in Wilmot’s growing career.

He has previously appeared in the television series FBI: International.

He also featured in the Netflix drama series Unchosen, where he played the character Anthony.

   A professional still of Rory Wilmot from the film 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Wilmot is due to play Neville Longbottom in HBO’s forthcoming Harry Potter television series.

Trained through in-person screen acting lessons at Articulate Drama School, he represents a new wave of northern talent breaking into the industry.

Wilmot is slowly establishing himself as one of the emerging young British actors to watch.

The Runner is available on Prime Video from September 2, 2026.
