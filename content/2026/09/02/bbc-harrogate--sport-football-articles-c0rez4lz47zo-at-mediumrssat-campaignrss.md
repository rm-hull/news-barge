**Harrogate Town have signed Torquay United defender Sam Dreyer for an undisclosed fee.**

The 22-year-old made 87 appearances in the National League South for the Gulls.

He could make his debut for Simon Weaver's men against Scunthorpe on Saturday.

"I know supporters will be disappointed to see Sam go and I completely understand that. He has been an important player for us and good players are difficult to replace," Torquay boss Jimmy Ball told the club website., external
