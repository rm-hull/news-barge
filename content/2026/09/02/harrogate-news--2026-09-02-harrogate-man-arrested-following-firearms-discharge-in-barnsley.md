A man from Harrogate has been arrested following an overnight firearms discharge in Barnsley.

At 10.08pm on Saturday 29 August, South Yorkshire received reports of shots being fired on Smithies Lane. It is reported a man was threatened and a firearm was discharged. No one was injured during the incident.

Officers responded and evidence consistent with a firearms discharge was found.

An investigation has been launched and initial enquiries has led to the arrest of a 39-year-old man in Harrogate, North Yorkshire. The man was arrested on suspicion of firearms offences and remains in police custody at this time.


**Detective Inspector Joanne Chambers said:**

When a gun is fired the consequences can be devastating. Thankfully no one was injured as a result of this incident, but next time this might not be the case.

Our communities should not be put at risk because of the reckless actions of those willing to pull a trigger and place innocent lives in potential danger. In South Yorkshire we have felt the destructive aftermath gun crime brings and we remain committed as ever to tackle this problem.

We have a team of specialist detectives and officers investigating this discharge which has led to us making an arrest in the early hours of this morning. But we need your help too.

If you know anything about this incident please get in touch. No matter how small or insignificant you might think your information is, it could prove to be crucial to our investigation.
