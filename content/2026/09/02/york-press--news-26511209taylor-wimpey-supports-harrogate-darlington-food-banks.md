A housebuilder has donated £1,000 and essential supplies to food banks.

Taylor Wimpey North Yorkshire has supported King’s Church Foodbank in Darlington and Harrogate District Foodbank as part of a national initiative to back food banks and food redistribution charities across the UK.

Caroline Todd, manager at King’s Church Foodbank, said: "Thank you so much to Taylor Wimpey North Yorkshire for their amazing donation.

"It was such a lovely blessing to us.

"At King’s Church we want to make everyone feel welcome, from home-cooked meals every Wednesday, to an emergency bag of food, this donation will allow us to continue our support to those who need it most in Darlington."

Harrogate District Foodbank has helped 4,500 people in the past year and prevented more than 100 from becoming homeless over the last four years.

Dawn Cussons, district manager at Harrogate District Foodbank, said: "I would like to say a very big thank you to Taylor Wimpey and everyone who has supported us over the last 13 years.

"We ask if anyone can help in the future to please get in touch, so we can continue our vital work in the community."

Taylor Wimpey said the donations reflect its commitment to supporting the communities it builds in.
