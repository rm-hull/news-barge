A “wonderful” spa in the Yorkshire countryside has been named one of the best in the UK.

Experts from SpaSeekers.com have compiled a list of the best spas and spa packages from across the UK.

For those looking for an escape in Yorkshire, you are in luck as the Yorkshire Spa Retreat made it onto the list thanks to its “wild-swim experience” and “beautiful Yorkshire parkland”.

You can enjoy a simple spa day at the retreat or extend your stay by booking one of the lodges, giving yourself plenty of time to try out treatments and explore the surrounding area.


## What is the Yorkshire Spa Retreat like?

On the spa, SpaSeekers.com said: “For something a little different this summer, the Yorkshire Spa Retreat's natural swimming pond is a genuine wild-swim experience set among the North York Moors.

“The Botanical Meadow Retreat package includes a 45-minute treatment, two-course lunch and three hours' use of the facilities, the ideal combination of a tranquil summer spa stay.”

The Yorkshire Spa Retreat is described as being a “tranquil space for relaxation amidst the stunning Yorkshire Moors countryside”.

The spa has a range of facilities including the wild swim pond, a sauna, a herbal steam room, and a mixture of organic treatments.

The Dome Bar and Restaurant offers guests a place to relax and enjoy different dishes created using local produce.

The restaurant has a few different menus, including for lunch, afternoon tea, pizza nights, and an a la carte offering.

If you want to explore the surrounding area, including the national parks, woodland walks, and the nearby villages, you can rent an E-bike from the spa.
