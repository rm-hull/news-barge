A long-running community event has been given a fresh identity and a helping hand to grow.

Now known as Northallerton Family Funday, the event was formerly called Northallerton Carnival but has been rebranded to better reflect its focus on inclusive family activities and community participation.

Organisers aimed to blend traditional fun with newer activities, offering something for all ages.

Phil Craig, chair of the Funday, said: "When you think of carnival, you think of a parade and that has confused some people about what the day is all about."

A £500 grant from the Broadacres Community and Sustainability Fund helped cover the cost of a gazebo, event banners, and prizes for the children’s fun run.

The event took place on July 12 and included a children’s fun run, arts and crafts, nature activities, and main arena attractions such as gymnastics and a motorcycle display.

Mr Craig added: "It was Northallerton at its best – a chance for the town to come together for a fun day out that everyone can afford."

The Broadacres Community and Sustainability Fund supports projects that create social, environmental or shared benefits for local people.

Grants of up to £500 are available, with awards of up to £1,000 considered in exceptional cases.
