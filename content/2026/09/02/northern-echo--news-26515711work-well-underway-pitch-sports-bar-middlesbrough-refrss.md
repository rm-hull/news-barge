Work is well underway on a new sports bar hoping to bring “stadium level energy” to Middlesbrough town centre.

Pitch! Sports Bar is set to take over the former Bar Fresco site, underneath the A66 and opposite STACK, later this year.

Two golf simulators, a lounge area and bar, a big screen, and a darts space are among the plans to “add something new and exciting" to Middlesbrough’s leisure scene.

Hospitality firm Pneuma Group, which is behind the project, initially planned to transform the former Doctors Browns on Corporation Road into the concept but shifted plans in May.

Bosses previously said the new location was an “exciting opportunity to further strengthen that part of Middlesbrough as a destination for food, drink, live sport and social occasions”.

  Pitch! Sports Bar in Middlesbrough 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Pitch! will be located within a growing area of nightlife in the town centre, beside STACK, Bloom, Honky Tonk, and Flares and close to other spots on Albert Road.

New pictures taken this week show work is now well underway on the project, with signs now up advertising the venue and glass windows whitewashed.

Pitch! was initially scheduled to open in time for the World Cup but bosses then later gave a ‘summer’ opening window. It is not yet known when the venue will open to the public.

Meanwhile, a deal has been struck for a new operator to take over Doctor Browns, which opened in 1867 and has been closed since 2023.

However, the pub became the victim of a fire last month and it is not known whether this has impacted plans for the site to open this Autumn.

Plans for what it will become remain under wraps at this time.

A spokesperson for Pneuma previously said: “Pneuma Group is pleased to confirm that Pitch!, its new sports bar concept, is being progressed at 12-13, Exchange Walk, in the heart of one of Middlesbrough town centre’s most vibrant leisure and hospitality areas.

"The Exchange Walk location places Pitch! across the road from STACK and next door to Bloom, two of the town’s most popular venues, creating an exciting opportunity to further strengthen that part of Middlesbrough as a destination for food, drink, live sport and social occasions.

"Pitch! will bring a dedicated sports bar experience to the town centre, offering a high-quality setting for fans, groups of friends and the wider community to come together and enjoy major sporting events.

“We are continuing to work through the required licensing and pre-opening preparations and look forward to sharing further details, including confirmed opening timings as soon as we are in a position to do so.

“Our focus is firmly on delivering a venue that complements the surrounding hospitality offer and adds something new and exciting to Middlesbrough’s leisure scene.”
