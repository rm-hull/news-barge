AER Lingus has launched a major end-of-summer sale, offering discounted flights to North America and Ireland.

The sale includes UK departures to destinations such as New York, Boston, Toronto, Los Angeles, and Cancún, with travel valid between October 1, 2026, and March 19, 2027.

Susanne Carberry, chief customer officer at Aer Lingus, said: "The Aer Lingus Sale gives customers the perfect opportunity to plan their next big adventure.

"While many customers across the UK know Aer Lingus for our flights to Ireland, they may be less aware of the extensive network we offer across North America via Dublin.

"For customers connecting through Dublin, Aer Lingus offers the convenience of US Preclearance."

Travellers can also save up to 30 per cent on short-haul flights between Great Britain and Ireland.
