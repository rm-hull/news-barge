This is the moment when a North Yorkshire drug dealer who claimed to live “like a rock star” was taken down by police.

Nathan Stone, 32, sent texts boasting about his lavish lifestyle while he stayed in rented holiday houses and luxury hotels in Scarborough.

But today, he is starting a 34-month jail sentence, after he was convicted of supplying Class A drugs in the town.

Stone was was spotted behaving suspiciously on King Street on 4 June by police.

His car was pulled over. As it was being searched he vaulted a fence and fled. But officers set off in hot pursuit and caught up with him after a chase.

Stone then attempted to snap his phone in half.

In a police interview, he stated that he had panicked, but that there would not be any evidence of drug dealing on the phone.

However, forensic analysis of the damaged device found messages between Stone and dozens of other people, discussing the supply of Class A drugs.

The most recent messages had been sent moments before he was arrested.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/nathan-stone-drug-dealer-scarborough.webp)

There were also messages from Stone describing staying in five-star self-catering flats, and living life “like a rock star”. By contrast, in police interview, Stone stated he had previously worked as a brick layer, but had been unemployed for at least three months.

In the car, officers found weighing scales and a bottle with a blue plastic bag containing white powder inside.

Stone was charged with possession with intent to supply Class A drugs. Faced with the evidence, he pleaded guilty on 6 July at York Crown Court.

He’s now been sentenced to 34 months’ imprisonment.

DC Alfie Thomlinson, of Scarborough CID, arrested Stone, and oversaw the case against him. After the sentencing hearing, he said: “Nathan Stone thought he could exploit others for his own ill-gotten gain, making a significant amount of money from selling cocaine.

“This case is an example of what proactive officers out on the beat patrolling can achieve by stopping, searching – even chasing down and arresting – those involved in the supply of drugs.

“Stone paid no heed to the misery that illegal drugs cause in our communities. Those like him should know that detectives at North Yorkshire Police will relentlessly investigate and disrupt their selfish crimes.”
