EXPLORER Scouts are being put in the driving seat as part of a new youth-shaped programme launched across Bradford.

Bradford Scouts has introduced the refreshed Explorer Scouts brand and programme, designed to give 14 to 18-year-olds more choice, ownership and flexibility in shaping their Scouting experience.

The updated programme focuses on outdoor adventure, real-world skills, wellbeing, and making a positive difference, giving young people a greater say in what they do and how they develop.

Toby Hammond, County Lead Volunteer, said: "We’re incredibly excited to be launching the new Explorer brand and programme across Bradford.

"Explorers are at a really important point in their lives, becoming more independent and starting to think seriously about their futures, and this programme is designed to support them through that journey.

"What I particularly like about the new programme is that it gives young people more ownership.

"They can choose what interests them, shape their experiences and develop skills that will be useful well beyond Scouts.

"Whether that’s learning practical life skills, exploring STEM, developing leadership or simply having an adventure with friends, there’s something for everyone."

The national programme was developed collaboratively, with input from more than 3,000 young people and volunteers.

It aims to build confidence, resilience and practical skills for life and work.

For Bradford’s Explorers, the relaunch means more opportunities to try new activities, take on challenges and make lasting memories with friends.

Mr Hammond said: "We want every young person in Bradford to feel that Explorers is a place where they belong, where they can be themselves and where they can build the confidence to take on whatever comes next."

The refreshed Explorer Activity Badges offer flexibility, allowing Explorers to choose how they complete them based on their interests.

The programme encourages learning through discovery, creativity, experience, problem-solving and teamwork.

New badge topics include Life Skills, Content Creation, Democracy, Employability and STEM, alongside familiar options such as Pioneering, Survival Skills, Camper and Caving.

Six new Challenge Awards have also been introduced.

The rebrand includes a new logo, colours, badge designs and resources, all developed in collaboration with young people to ensure the identity feels relevant, exciting and inclusive.

The programme is now available, with new badges available to earn.

Explorer Scouts is open to all young people aged 14 to 18, offering opportunities to make friends, learn new skills, take on challenges and work towards Top Awards.
