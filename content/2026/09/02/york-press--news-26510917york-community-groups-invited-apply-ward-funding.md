Funding is now available to support projects that strengthen communities and improve residents’ lives.

City of York Council has opened the second round of ward funding applications for 2026/27, inviting community groups, charities and voluntary organisations to apply before the deadline on 27 September 2026.

Ward funding supports a wide range of projects that promote equality of opportunity, address affordability and the cost of living, improve health and wellbeing or contribute to climate and environmental goals.

Councillor Anna Perrett, executive member for housing, planning and safer communities, said: "Ward funding is a fantastic way of supporting the local organisations, volunteers and community groups that make such a positive difference in neighbourhoods across York.

"We have seen funding help deliver projects that improve wellbeing, bring people together, support young people, reduce isolation and improve local facilities.

"I would encourage any eligible organisation with an idea that benefits their community to apply."

Previous recipients include Door 84 Youth & Community Centre’s 10-week cooking programme, improvements to Fulford Scout Group’s outdoor shelter and Tang Hall Breakfast Club’s pay-as-you-feel Friday breakfasts.

Applicants are advised to check their ward and ensure their project aligns with specific ward priorities.

More information, including the online application form, is available at york.gov.uk/WardGrantApplications.
