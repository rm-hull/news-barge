Emergency services were called to Thornton on Wednesday morning after a vehicle was found on its side.

West Yorkshire Police have since confirmed that they attended the incident on Back Lane at around 10.07am.

## 
  **No collision reported**

The incident was logged as highway disruption, with initial information indicating the vehicle had been found overturned rather than being involved in a collision.

As a result, the incident does not currently appear to have been treated as a road traffic crash.

## 
  **Driver advised to attend hospital**

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Poice said crews attended the scene and assessed the situation.

No injuries were reported, although the driver was advised to attend hospital as a precaution.

The circumstances surrounding how the vehicle came to rest on its side have not been disclosed.

## 
  **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A spokesperson said: "This has been logged as highway disruption in that the vehicle was found on its side – so doesn’t appear to have been a collision.

"We’ve attended at about 10.07am and suggested that the driver attends hospital. No injuries reported."
