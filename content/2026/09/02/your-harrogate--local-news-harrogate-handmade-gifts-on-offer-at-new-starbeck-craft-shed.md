A garden shed filled with handmade gifts, decorations and trinkets has opened in Starbeck.

Ringo’s Crafty Shed, described as “the littlest gift shop”, is located on Fairways Avenue and operates using an honesty payment system.

Sarah Khanye said:

“[It’s] filled with handmade clay decorations, earrings, paint-your-own boxes for the kids... there’s a little something for everyone and as the seasons change, so will the shed with its items!”


Customers can also place orders for items they would like to purchase.

Ringo’s Crafty Shed is open every day from 9am until 6pm.

Visitors can pay with cash or by scanning the QR code provided at the shed.
