Getting to the airport is ruining the holiday mood for nearly half of northern travellers, new research has revealed.

According to a study by TransPennine Express (TPE), 48 per cent of northern holidaymakers identified the journey to Manchester Airport as the most stressful part of their trip.

More than a third (37 per cent) said travel costs had put a dampener on the start of their holiday.

Adam Fairclough, deputy customer experience director and head of customer experience at TPE, said: "With TPE Advance Purchase fares to Manchester Airport starting at £4.20, passengers can travel for a fraction of that cost, and with Aperol Spritz now available onboard, they can settle into a comfortable seat, enjoy a drink and get into the holiday spirit before they even reach the terminal."

The research found travellers using taxis or driving, and parking, spent an average of £63.90 before reaching the terminal, while one in five (20 per cent) spent more than £100.

Half of northern holidaymakers said they are now more likely to take the train to the airport for their next holiday.

TPE has introduced 250ml Aperol Spritz cans on all catered services, including trains serving Manchester Airport, and now offers all-night trains between Manchester Piccadilly and the airport.
