Yeehaw! York has gone mad for country.

Since Ruby Rae’s opened in the former Jalou nightclub, thousands have been dancing to country and western every weekend.

A soft launch took place on Thursday July 31, with the official Grand Opening on Thursday July 6.

The popular nightclub opens from Wednesday to Sunday.

Ruby Rae’s added to York’s thriving country and Western scene, which in May also saw the launch of the Silver Spur Bar and Canteen a few doors away on Bridge Street, where the Rio Brazilian Steakhouse used to be.

The business was founded in Newcastle by business partners Dean Saunders and Dan Cooke, who opened their first Ruby Rae’s in the city last June.

Dean says the first month or so in York has been “absolutely amazing.”

  Cowgirls won't get the blues at Ruby Rae's 

*(Image: Sam Cooper)*

He told the Press: “It’s exceeded our expectations. The people of York are amazing and friendly. They appreciate what we have brought to the city. They are massive country fans as well.”

Dean says some nights will have seen ‘many hundreds’ visiting the venue, with weekends attracting thousands.

He said: “We open at 12 noon and stay open until 3am. It doesn’t stop. They keep coming. Thousands go through the door every weekend.”

Last Friday, Ruby Rae’s began line dancing classes, which from Sunday September 13, will run every Sunday from 4pm until 6pm. The classes will be free.

Dean says the first class filled the building, which was originally a church.

  A full house for laine dancing 

*(Image: Sam Cooper)*

Ruby Rae’s is about to announce it’s first national artist performing theire.

This will add to Twinnie-Lee Moore, a York born country legend and star of TV’s Emmerdale.

Dean said: “Twinnie has been amazing, performing two nights here. She is absolutely worshipped in York.”

  The magnificent setting of Ruby Rae's

Dean added everyone has been enjoying themselves at Ruby Rae’s/

“A big thank you from myself and our team for how people have enjoyed themselves. It makes it more fun for us as well.”

The joy Ruby Rae’s has brought to York is reflected by rave reviews on social media.

Google awards the venue 4.9 stars out of five, based on 127 Google reviews.

Among the most recent comments, all gave five stars.
