McDonalds has reopened its Thornaby restaurant today following a major makeover to improve customer experience.

The restaurant in Allensway welcomed customers back from 11am on Wednesday morning (September 2) after completing the redesign.

The refurbishment follows the month-long closure in August, when the restaurant shut for renovation work to start.

Customers will now find a refreshed dining area, a redesigned kitchen and the new technology intended to improve speed and efficiency when ordering food.

Among the changes is a new additional drive-thru order canopy, designed to help staff take orders and serve customers more efficiently.

The revamp also includes dedicated spaces for different ordering channels, including the McDonald’s App and delivery services, alongside a courier waiting area and separate entrance intended to reduce congestion inside the restaurant.

Dean Fitzmaurice, who owns and operates six McDonald’s restaurants across North Yorkshire, said: “I’m really glad we are able to give our Thornaby restaurant a fresh start and it’s exciting to be welcoming customers back.

“The additional order point will help us take orders and serve customers more efficiently in the Drive-Thru, while the refreshed dining area creates a brighter more modern space.”

The redesign also includes a larger order assembly area in the kitchen, dedicated preparation spaces for dine-in orders and upgraded staff facilities.

Improved crew rooms and break spaces have been created to provide a more comfortable environment for employees during breaks.

McDonald’s said the changes have been introduced in response to increasing demand for quicker service, greater convenience and more choice in how customers order their food.

The Thornaby restaurant originally opened in 2014 and is one of more than 1,560 McDonald’s restaurants operating across the UK and Ireland.
