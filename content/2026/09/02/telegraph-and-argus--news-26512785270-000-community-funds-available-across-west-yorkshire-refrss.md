A £270,000 community fund is set to open for applications later this month.

The funding is part of the Transpennine Route Upgrade (TRU) and will support projects that create lasting community impact along the rail corridor between Manchester, Huddersfield, Leeds, and York.

Applications open on Monday, September 28, and are welcome from groups within five kilometres of the route, including those near the Shipley Traincare Centre.

Anna Humphries, head of sustainability and social value for TRU, said: "Over the last two years, we've seen the Community Fund support a wide range of projects that are making a real difference to local people."

Eligible organisations can apply for grants of up to £5,000, with the possibility of an increase to £10,000 for projects with greater impact.

The fund has already awarded more than £400,000 to over 60 community groups, schools and charities.

Past recipients include Friends of Ravenshall and Lord Deramore's School in York.
