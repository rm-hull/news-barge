The first buses in West Yorkshire's publicly controlled network will hit the roads next spring.

Under the new system, the West Yorkshire Combined Authority (WYCA) will set routes and fares, while First Bus and Transdev run the services under contract.

First West Yorkshire will run buses in Huddersfield and parts of Leeds, while Transdev Blazefield covers Dewsbury.

Mayor Tracy Brabin said the deals were "the next step to improving public transport" for passengers.

The franchise forms part of the Weaver Network, West Yorkshire's unified public transport system, which will integrate buses with trains, walking and cycling, and eventually the region's mass-transit tram network.
