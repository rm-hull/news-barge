A jewellery specialist from BBC's Antiques Roadshow is set to appear at a fundraising event in Knaresborough.

Susan Rumfitt will host a talk as part of Henshaws’ Tiaras and Tea event on Thursday 8th October.

Guests will hear about Susan’s work and expertise before enjoying a champagne afternoon tea at Henshaws Arts & Crafts Centre.

Susan is a qualified gemmologist and one of the regular jewellery specialists featured on Antiques Roadshow.

The event is being organised by Henshaws’ fundraising committee in support of the charity’s Secret Garden.

The garden forms part of the Arts & Crafts Centre in Knaresborough, where Henshaws provides creative workshops for people living with disabilities.

*Tickets are available through the**Henshaws website**.*
