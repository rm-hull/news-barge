A North Yorkshire village pub reports a “fantastic” re-opening after 13 long years of closure.

The Appletree Country Inn in Marton, between Malton and Pickering, opened its doors on Friday, with further celebrations for family and friends on Saturday.

Owner Matt Stanley said: “It was absolutely slammed Friday night. Pretty much the whole village turned up. It was just a big party.”

Saturday afternoon saw further celebrations for family and friends. The bar was opened up for added atmosphere and the venue was “really busy.”

Sunday also delivered a “strong, successful” day.

Matt added: “We are all very happy and proud of the team. I just can’t thank the team enough. Everybody has worked really hard to get us across the line.”

As the Press has previously reported, Matt came across the AppleTree Country Inn whilst out walking the dog one day.

  Lewis Sadler & wife. (Tree surgeon / landscaper for the pub). 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

The 28-year-old thought it “a shame to see it rotting away” and likened the pub to the Mary Celeste, noting it had been left abandoned for 13 years.

He spent six weeks tracing its former owner to Guernsey and after a quick sale started renovating the venue last May.

The restored inn aims to offer food at the “higher end of gastropub, breaching into fine dining.”

At its heart is The Hearth, an open-kitchen restaurant centred around live-fire cooking.

  Matt Stanley and Mum Sheila 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Head Chef Connor Anderson has developed a seasonal menu with an ambitious 25-mile sourcing scheme.

Suppliers include Dales the Chef’s Choice of Malton for fruit and vegetables, Glaves Butchers of Scarborough for meat and Hodgson Fish of Hartlepool for seafood.

The inn also has plans for its own kitchen garden.

Locals have already given the Appletree Country Inn the thumbs up.

One said after the launch party: “Thank you so much for a lovely evening Matt and team. The Appletree looks fabulous and it's great to see a hub in the village again. Looking forward to our lunch tomorrow!! Wishing you every success.”

Another posted on Facebook: “Thank you for a fabulous evening. The pub looks fantastic and already has a great vibe.”

And another added: “Thank you for a fabulous evening, the place looks fantastic, the food was delicious and the wine excellent. Well done to all involved.”
