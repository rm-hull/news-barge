A Bradford woman has denied causing the death of a man in a high-speed motorway crash involving a Lamborghini.

Humaira Batool, who was just 22 years old at the time of the incident, is accused of causing the death of 20-year-old Mohammed Shaan Hussain by dangerous driving.

The crash took place in a hired Lamborghini Urus on the M62 near Ripponden, in West Yorkshire in the early hours of December 21, 2022.

Prosecutor Christopher Dunn told Leeds Crown Court that the Lamborghini was being driven "at grossly excessive speeds" of up to 143mph, in wet conditions on the M62.

Mr Hussain, who rented the vehicle, had planned to use it to pick up a friend from HMP Leeds.

The court heard that he collected Ms Batool from her Bradford home at around 4am.

Mr Dunn said CCTV footage showed Ms Batool initially in the passenger seat before switching to the driver’s side at a set of traffic lights, with Mr Hussain moving into the passenger seat.

The Lamborghini was travelling at around 120mph as it passed Stott Hall Farm, known as the "house in the middle of the motorway" . Seconds later, as it was travelling between junctions 22 and 23 the vehicle left the carriageway, struck a barrier, and overturned.

Mr Dunn said drivers who had been overtaken by the stricken supercar stopped to help but their efforts to rescue Mr Hussain were thwarted by fire and an explosion.

Ms Batool was found on a grass verge near the motorway by a second group of witnesses.

Mr Dunn said: "Batool told witnesses the flames had melted the seatbelt, enabling her to escape the vehicle."

She later told police she had been asleep as a passenger and only woke up when the car rolled.

However, the prosecution argues that Ms Batool was driving at the time of the crash.

Ms Batool denies the charge and claims she was not driving the vehicle.

The trial continues.
