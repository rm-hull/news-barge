DRIVERS are only just realising the meaning of a small arrow next to the fuel pump icon on their dashboard.

The symbol indicates which side of the car the fuel cap is on, a feature that has surprised many motorists who have driven for decades without noticing it.

Mark Reece, a motoring expert at Rev Monkey UK, said: "We've been genuinely blown away by the reaction to this.

"It's such a small detail – a tiny arrow sat next to the fuel pump symbol on your dashboard – but the amount of people messaging us saying 'I've been driving for fifteen, twenty, thirty years and had no idea that was there' is honestly incredible."

Drivers often discover too late that they've parked on the wrong side of the pump, leading to awkward manoeuvres or stretching the hose across the vehicle.

The arrow has been a standard feature in most cars for years, but has gone largely unnoticed by the public.
