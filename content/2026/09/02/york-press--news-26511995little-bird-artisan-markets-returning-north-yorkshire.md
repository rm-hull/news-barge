## Free artisan markets coming to Knaresborough and Harrogate this weekend

Little Bird Made will host its first events of the season in North Yorkshire, at Knaresborough Castle and Harrogate Valley Gardens *(Image: Little Bird Artisan Markets)*

A weekend of outdoor artisan markets is set to celebrate the start of autumn with a showcase of handmade goods.

Little Bird Made will host its first events of the season in North Yorkshire, starting at Knaresborough Castle on Saturday, September 5, and continuing at Harrogate Valley Gardens on Sunday, September 6.

Both markets will run from 10am to 3pm and are free to attend.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "We are heading into the vibrant autumn trading season and couldn't be more excited to spend our first weekend of September in Knaresborough and Harrogate.

"Both Knaresborough Castle and Harrogate Valley Gardens offer breathtaking backdrops for our markets.

"It’s a wonderful opportunity for visitors to support talented local artisans, enjoy the seasonal changes, and discover unique handmade treasures you simply won't find on the high street."

Visitors can browse stalls offering seasonal fashion, accessories, organic skincare, homeware, artwork, photography, and street food.

Janine Calvert, owner of Beau Pens, said: "Knareborough Artisan Market is our favourite place to be, with beautiful views and fantastic fellow artisan traders.

"We can’t wait to welcome you all this Saturday."

Both venues are family and dog-friendly.

For more information, visit: www.littlebirdmade.com
