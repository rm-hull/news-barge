THERE’S a vibrant mix of comedy, storytelling and South Asian dance at Bradford Arts Centre this autumn.

A highlight of the line-up will a performance from the world-class Kala Chethena Kathakali Company - the last date on its UK tour before heading back to India.

Bradford Arts Centre has collaborated with international partner Kala Sangam Global to present major Indian classical performances as highlights of its new programme. Kala Sangam Global was formed last year by Bradford Arts Centre founders Dr Shripati Upadhyaya and Dr Geetha Upadhayaya.

Based in St Peter’s House building, Bradford Arts Centre is a leading venue for South Asian dance and contemporary performance, playing a key role in nurturing new artistic practice in the region. Its work covers performance, artist development, dance classes, education and community programmes.

The centre provides performance and rehearsal spaces, meeting room hire and spaces for social events, and offers performance and studio space to artists.

Through its performance seasons, artist development, dance classes, schools and community programmes, the venue promotes artistic innovation across the district.

Founded as Kala Sangam in 1993 - In Sanskrit, ‘Kala’ means arts and ‘Sangam’ means a meeting point - the arts organisation moved into Bradford’s former General Post Office building in Forster Square in 2008.

Following a major multi-million-pound re-development, it re-opened as Bradford Arts Centre last autumn with new facilities, including a theatre, rehearsal studios, meeting rooms, a conference venue and office space.

Here’s the Bradford Arts Centre programme for the coming months:

  The Setlist Comedy Night features comic stars and rising talent 

*(Image: Bradford Arts Centre)*

l Setlist Comedy Night on Saturday, September 5: The popular comedy circuit returns with a high-energy line-up of top UK stand-up talent. Hosted in an intimate performance space, the comedy night features established circuit headliners alongside rising stars in British comedy.

l Aradhana Arts: Sangeet Yatra on Saturday, September 12: Presented in partnership with Kala Sangam Global, Sangeet Yatra brings the pinnacle of British South Asian classical music and dance to Bradford.

Featuring mesmerising melodies, the jingling gungroo of Jaymini Sahai, and the reverberating rhythms of world-renowned tabla maestro Sanju Sahai, this spellbinding sensory journey bridges timeless heritage with contemporary mastery.

  Divija Melally's Saili Katebe: One Foot in the Dark. Pic: Dan Martin 

*(Image: Dan Martin)*

l Divija Melally & Saili Katebe: One Foot in the Dark Double Bill on Wednesday, September 23: This compelling double bill combines contemporary dance and spoken word performance to navigate identity, displacement, and cultural transformation.

Bringing together artist Divija Melally and writer Saili Katebe, One Foot in the Dark offers an intimate, atmospheric exploration of shadow and light, using physical storytelling to articulate complex modern experiences.

l Pahari-Pothwari Mushaira at Ilkley Literature Festival on Saturday, October 3: An enchanting literary evening celebrating poetry and heritage in Pahari-Pothwari, the native mother tongue of many British Pakistanis.

Gathering acclaimed poets, reciters, and literature enthusiasts, this traditional mushaira offers a rare cultural platform to experience oral tradition, and contemporary poetic expressions in a warm community setting.

l The Kala Chethena Kathakali Company on Thursday, October 8: Presented with Kala Sangam Global, the Kala Chethena Kathakali Company brings authentic South Indian dance-theatre straight from Kerala.

Featuring master artist Vijaykumar, this spectacular production unites striking hand-applied makeup, elaborate traditional costuming, intense facial mime, and ancient storytelling for an unforgettable, highly visual cultural experience.

l Bradford Writes at Ilkley Literature Festival on Wednesday, October 14: Curated and hosted by multilingual poet Nabeela Ahmed, Bradford Writes provides a vital showcase platform spotlighting 10 regional published authors. Celebrating the rich literary diversity of Yorkshire, the showcase features live readings, poetry, and prose, bringing local readers and creative writers together for an engaging, story-driven evening.

l Anirudh Varma Collective -Classical Quintet featuring Supriya Nagarajan on Friday, October 16: The internationally acclaimed Anirudh Varma Collective presents a dynamic classical quintet featuring guest vocalist Supriya Nagarajan. Blending classical Indian music traditions with modern Western improvisational structures, this performance creates an innovative, soul-stirring soundscape that pushes musical boundaries while honouring deep cultural roots.

l Ri Ri’s Dance Academy: The Magic Stick on Wednesday, October 28: A vibrant, interactive half-term dance production created by Ri Ri’s Dance Academy.

Designed specifically for young children and families, The Magic Stick weaves together rhythm, colourful choreography, and magical storytelling, encouraging young audiences to ignite their imagination, move to upbeat tracks, and join in the theatrical adventure.
