A FRESH push to put more decision-making power in the hands of residents is taking shape in Bradford.

Bradford Council hosted a "Your City, Your Voice" event last week, focusing on devolution and strengthening community involvement in shaping local decisions.

The event, led by council leader Councillor Stephen Place, brought together representatives from town and parish councils, the voluntary sector, and community groups to share their views on their involvement in local decision making.

Councillor Place said: "Devolved power ensures that local communities—who best understand their own unique challenges and opportunities—are the ones setting their own priorities.

"This event was a great opportunity to explore ways we could improve and enhance this further, helping to ensure local people are fully engaged with democratic and robust decision making which most effects them."

The district already has 21 town and parish councils, as well as area committees.
