FAMILIES are invited to enjoy a free Islamic heritage celebration in Bradford.

The Abū’l-Qāsim Festival of Culture returns to Mustafa Mount on Sunday, September 6, running from 12pm until 8pm.

Organised by Souq Uthman, the day-long festival celebrates Islamic heritage, family life and community during Rabiʿ al-Awwal, the third month of the Islamic calendar.

Now in its second year, the free event welcomes families from Bradford and beyond. No ticket is required for entry, and visitors can turn up to enjoy the atmosphere, marketplace, food and entertainment.

Held in the grounds of Mustafa Mount on Emm Lane, the festival offers interactive activities for children and adults. Visitors can try archery, experience traditional duff drumming, explore Islamic arts and enjoy storytelling.

There will also be horses, outdoor games, arts and crafts, sensory play, face painting, henna and a bouncy castle.

Food and refreshments include offerings from Fitra Café, while local businesses, artisans and community organisations feature in the souq marketplace.

Some workshops and activities require advance booking. These include archery, duff drumming, traditional Islamic arts, children’s storytelling and sensory play sessions.

Visitors can enjoy the festival throughout the day at Mustafa Mount with family.
