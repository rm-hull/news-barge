Members of the public helped pull two people from a burning car after a crash on the A19.

A 27-year-old man and a 28-year-old woman were helped from the white Ford Focus after it left the southbound carriageway near the A174 and Crathorne slip road shortly after 4pm on Monday, August 31.

The car came to a stop in a ditch before catching fire.

The pair were taken to James Cook University Hospital with injuries.

Police said the woman suffered several broken bones, while the man had cuts and bruises.

A Cleveland Police spokesperson said: "Members of the public then helped the driver, a 27-year-old man, and the passenger, a 28-year-old woman, out of the car."

Police said a black Volvo XC40 may also have been involved but did not stop at the scene.

Four fire appliances from Middlesbrough, Stockton, Thornaby and Coulby went to the scene.

The service confirmed that both patients were taken to James Cook University Hospital.
