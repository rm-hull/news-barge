A man has been found dead after being reported missing over the weekend.

Liam Loughlin, 33, was last seen leaving Tudor Lodge Service Station on Marton Road, in Middlesbrough at 1.34am on August 30.

Police said he was believed to have been heading towards the James Cook University Hospital area and had not been seen since.

Cleveland Police confirmed today (Wednesday, September 2), that a body had been found during the search for Mr Loughlin.

A spokesperson for the force said: “Sadly, a body was located today in the search for missing 33 year old Liam Loughlin, from Middlesbrough.

“His death is not being treated as suspicious, and a file will be prepared for the coroner.

“Our thoughts are with Liam’s family and friends at this extremely difficult time.”
