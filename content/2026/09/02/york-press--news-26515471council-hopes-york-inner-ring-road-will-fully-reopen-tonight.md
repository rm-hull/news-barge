The council has said it hopes the York inner ring road will have fully reopened by late Wednesday night (September 2) after damage caused during a thunderstorm uplifted part of the route.

Yorkshire Water crews are on site repairing the damaged pedestrian crossing at the Fishergate Gyratory, close to the junction of Fishergate with Paragon Street.

Traffic has been building in Walmgate and surrounding streets throughout Wednesday morning (September 2) due to the closure.

  Queuing traffic in George Street, off Walmgate, on Wednesday (September 2) 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

It comes after motorists described “chaotic” scenes in the Walmgate area on Tuesday due to the diversion along Piccadilly, Lead Mill Lane and Walmgate causing queues.

A City of York Council spokesperson thanked residents for their “patience while a solution was put in place”.

“We know it's been a tough few days on the network with delays across the city,” they said.

The council spokesperson said the York inner ring road will fully reopen once the new surface at Fishergate has set and the surface is safe to drive on.

“We're hoping that this will be by late tonight,” they said on Wednesday.

They added that the council cleared the drains and gulleys in Fishergate in July as part of its Highways Maintenance Programme, “and we’ll continue to work with Yorkshire Water to monitor the situation”.

Yorkshire Water has removed the damaged road surface in Fishergate, creating a pile of rubble close to the cordoned off area.

  Drivers are diverted off the York inner ring road due to the storm damage at Fishergate on Wednesday (September 2) 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

The company on Tuesday said traffic management measures would remain in place until the road had been reinstated and set.

“We’re currently working with the local council to assess and repair damage on Fishergate following the storm over the weekend, which we understand caused water to push through gaps under a manhole cover and frame, lifting the road surface,” a Yorkshire Water spokesperson said on Tuesday.

"We’ll be repairing the damage caused tomorrow (September 2). We apologise for the inconvenience.”


First Bus said its number seven and 10 services are being diverted due to the closure.

A First spokesperson said: "Our services seven and 10 are currently being affected due to closure of a section of the Fishergate Gyratory.

"Service seven is being diverted but no bus stops are being missed, while the service 10 is currently not being diverted as this would result in a number of stops being missed. Both routes are experiencing some delays and we urge everyone to check our app for the most up-to-date information."

A Press commenter described the scene in the Walmgate area as “chaotic”, writing: “Usual morning traffic blended with the diverted traffic from Tower Street and Fishergate – and even in the afternoon there was a constant stream of cars trying to get to Walmgate Bar.

“Lots of people were getting impatient and honking their horns.”
