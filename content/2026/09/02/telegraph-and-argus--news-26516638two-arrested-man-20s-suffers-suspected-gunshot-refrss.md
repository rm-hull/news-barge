TWO men have been arrested after a man in his 20s was injured with injuries consistent with gunshot wounds to both arms.

The incident happened in Leeds on September 1, with police called at 10.11pm.

The victim arrived at hospital with injuries consistent with gunshot wounds to both arms, which are not understood to be life-threatening.

A scene has since been established on Cambrian Street in Holbeck.

Two men, both aged 26, have been arrested on suspicion of wounding with intent and are currently in custody.

Detective Chief Inspector Daniel Bates, of the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team, said: "We understand that any discharge of a firearm will cause some concern in the wider community and local residents can expect to see an increased police presence in the area.

"I would like to reassure members of the public that we have extensive enquiries ongoing to establish the circumstances of what has happened and those involved."

Enquiries are ongoing and anyone with information is urged to contact police.
