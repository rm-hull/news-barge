Residents are being urged to keep umbrellas close at hand as a wet and gloomy Wednesday is forecast for Darlington.

The Met Office is predicting a day dominated by cloud and outbreaks of light rain, with the highest chance of precipitation arriving during the morning hours.

Rain chances are expected to reach 90 per cent between around 7am and 10am, before gradually easing later in the day.

Despite the soggy conditions, temperatures will remain fairly mild, climbing to around 19C during the afternoon.

Overnight lows are expected to dip to about 16C.

The Met Office forecast shows rain and drizzle lingering throughout much of the day, although there could be some brighter spells by late afternoon and evening as the chances of rainfall begin to fall.

Winds are expected to remain light, generally around 5mph to 6mph.

Looking ahead, conditions are expected to improve on Thursday, with sunny intervals and warmer temperatures of around 21C forecast.
