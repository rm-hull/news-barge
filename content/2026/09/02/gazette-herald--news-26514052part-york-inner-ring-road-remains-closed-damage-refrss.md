Part of York’s inner ring road remains closed as work gets underway to repair damage caused during a thunderstorm.

Traffic was building in Walmgate and surrounding streets on Wednesday morning (September 2) due to the closure at the Fishergate Gyratory, close to the junction of Fishergate with Paragon Street.

It came after heavy rain during a thunderstorm on Sunday lifted a section of the pedestrian crossing, forcing City of York Council’s highway officials to cordon the section of the road.

  Traffic building in Margaret Street, off Walmgate, on Wednesday morning (September 2) 

*(Image: Maxine Gordon)*

Yorkshire Water chiefs said the damaged road is set to be repaired by Wednesday.

Its crews remained on the scene on Wednesday morning – with the pedestrian crossing dug up.

Yorkshire Water has removed the damaged road surface, creating a pile of rubble close to the cordoned off area.

The company on Tuesday said traffic management measures would remain in place until the road had been reinstated and set.

“We’re currently working with the local council to assess and repair damage on Fishergate following the storm over the weekend, which we understand caused water to push through gaps under a manhole cover and frame, lifting the road surface,” a Yorkshire Water spokesperson said on Tuesday.

"We’ll be repairing the damage caused tomorrow (September 2). We apologise for the inconvenience.”

Drivers travelling eastbound from Tower Street are continuing to be diverted through Piccadilly, Lead Mill Lane and Walmgate, which is causing queues – particularly during peak times.

It follows motorists, including buses, being seen in the long traffic queues throughout Wednesday.


First Bus said its number seven and 10 services are being diverted due to the closure.

A First spokesperson said: "Our services seven and 10 are currently being affected due to closure of a section of the Fishergate Gyratory.

"Service seven is being diverted but no bus stops are being missed, while the service 10 is currently not being diverted as this would result in a number of stops being missed. Both routes are experiencing some delays and we urge everyone to check our app for the most up-to-date information."

City of York Council on Monday said Fishergate would remain closed "for a few days”, adding: “While we work with Yorkshire Water to understand the cause, then get it fixed.

"For now, all traffic (including buses) will be diverted."

A Press commenter described the scene as “chaotic”, writing: “Usual morning traffic blended with the diverted traffic from Tower Street and Fishergate – and even in the afternoon there was a constant stream of cars trying to get to Walmgate Bar.

“Lots of people were getting impatient and honking their horns.”
