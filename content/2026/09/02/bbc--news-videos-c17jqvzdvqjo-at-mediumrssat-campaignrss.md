# Stars of The Gentlemen react to Meghan rumours

Stars of Netflix's The Gentlemen react to rumours that Meghan, Duchess of Sussex may be joining the cast.

Theo James, Hugh Bonneville and Maya Jama are some of the cast members in attendance at the shows London premiere tonight, where they caught up with the BBC's Noor Nanji.

In August the BBC learnt that the Duchess of Sussex was in talks to return to acting following her and Harry's move back to the UK.
