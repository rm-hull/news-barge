Police probing an alleyway stab attack say there is no evidence to suggest a link to wider organised crime.

  Armed police and paramedics swarmed Britannia Place in Dormanstown, near Redcar, on Monday (August 31) __after a man was stabbed at around 5pm.__ 

The victim is believed to have been attacked in an alley behind homes on Broadway West before stumbling to a home on Britannia Place and collapsing outside homes.

  Police remained at the taped off scene yesterday, where __one woman recalled the traumatic moment the victim said “I am going to die here” while lying on the ground.__ 

Now, Cleveland Police has confirmed that enquiries into the incident remain ongoing and that there is currently no evidence to suggest it’s linked to anything wider.

The victim’s injuries are not believed to be life threatening.

  Police on Britannia Place in Dormanstown after a stab attack 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The street was taped off while an air ambulance landed nearby following the ordeal, with armed officers dressed in all black seen walking through the estate.

A woman who gve her name as Marian, who has lived on the street for 26 years, was in the garden when she heard a knock at the door and her friend shouting: “Somebody has been stabbed and is dying on the front.”

She frantically rang 999 before joining her friend in going to the man’s aid.

The 59-year-old said: "My mate knocked on my door and asked for me to get the phone and call 999 because someone had been stabbed.

“This was at around 4pm or 5pm, apparently [the incident took place] down the alley.

“The man had collapsed outside number 42. There was blood everywhere.

“When the police got there, paramedics came and had oxygen on him.

“He wouldn’t keep it on so I kneeled down and tried to keep it on him while the paramedics worked on him. He was out of it.

“He kept saying: ‘I am going to die here’. It was traumatic.”

A force spokesperson said: “Officers are at the scene of an incident in the Dormanstown area after responding to reports that a man had been assaulted.

“The incident happened shortly before 5pm in Britannia Place.

“Officers remain at the scene conducting enquires to establish the full circumstances surrounding the incident.

“Anyone who witnessed the incident or who has any information, is asked to contact Cleveland Police on 101, quoting reference number 174954.”
