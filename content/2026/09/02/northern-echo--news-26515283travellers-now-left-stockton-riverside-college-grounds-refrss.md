Travellers who camped at a North East college as students returned for the new term have now left.

About four caravans moved onto Stockton Riverside College’s private grounds, among student and staff cars in the car parks.

Stockton Council and Cleveland Police have now confirmed the travellers had left the college site.

  Caravans set up in amongst the college traffic in the car park 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The caravans arrived as staff and students returned for their first day back, with pictures showing them parked alongside college traffic.

Signs near the pitches read: "Restricted parking - private land. Terms and Conditions apply."

  The restricted parking signs in the car park where the travellers were 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

A spokesperson for Stockton Riverside College previously said: “We are aware of the situation in the local area. College operations are continuing as normal and students should attend as planned, in line with the information provided at enrolment.

"Any queries regarding the start of term should be directed to college reception on 01642 865400 or, for returning students, via curriculum staff using usual communication methods.”

The college was one of several council-owned and private sites across Teesside and County Durham targeted by travellers in recent weeks.

Travellers have also set up camps at locations including parks, showgrounds and football pitches.

Police and council officials confirmed the travellers had left Stockton Riverside College.
