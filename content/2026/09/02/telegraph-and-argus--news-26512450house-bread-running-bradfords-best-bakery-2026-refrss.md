CUSTOMERS queue down the street at a Bradford district bakery "come rain or shine", according to its owners.

House of Bread, located on Bradford Road in Frizinghall, has been named as one of the finalists in our best bakery competition.

Sara Laszkowski, who co-owns the bakery with her siblings, said: "We’re so happy to hear that we’ve made the top 10.

"We feel over the moon and really grateful.

"We know how lucky we are with the community around this bakery, so having that support recognised makes it even more special.

"There's something lovely about doing a job you genuinely love and then having the people around you say they think you're doing it well.

"So, thank you to everyone who nominated us, whether you've been coming since day one or only found us last week."

## It's family business

House of Bread, which opened in March 2017, is run by five siblings who each manage different parts of the business.

Ms Laszkowski said: "It all started with our mum, Barbara.

"Baking has always been at the centre of our family and that's down to her.

"Every family gathering came with a table full of homemade cakes and many of those recipes have been passed down through generations and are still baked here today.

"What began as sourdough for family and friends grew as more people asked for it, which led to supplying a few local shops, and eventually to opening a bakery of our own."

## 11 varieties of sourdough

The idea for the business began with a search for the perfect sourdough loaf.

"We just couldn't find a loaf we loved, one that tasted incredible and was made with nothing more than flour, water and salt, so we thought we'd have a go at making our own," Ms Laszkowski said.

The bakery now produces 11 varieties of sourdough and a wide selection of pastries, all made by hand in small batches.

Ms Laszkowski said: "We love weaving our Polish heritage into what we bake, whether that's a traditional recipe like jagodzianki (a sweet bun filled with blueberry) or just the flavours we grew up with, but we're proper foodies too and take inspiration from all over the world.

"We only want to make food we'd be excited to eat ourselves.

"We're always trying new things, but every bake has to be genuinely delicious and made to a really high standard."

## Importance of customer base

She said: "customers are at the heart of everything we do.

"Rain or shine, they’re out there queuing for their weekly bread and sweet treats and on a busy day that queue can stretch right to the end of the street.

"We're so grateful for everyone's patience and support.

"Winning would mean the world, mostly because it would come from the community that has backed us the whole way."
