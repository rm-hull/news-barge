A popular beer festival is set to return for a milestone tenth year.

The Wold Top Big Sky Beer Festival will take place on July 9 and 10, 2027, at Hunmanby Grange, home of Wold Top Brewery.

The festival, which sold out earlier in the year, welcomed 300 guests who enjoyed 24 beers, ciders, and live music from acts including Rachel Mckenna and The Midnight Special.

Sam Smith, part of the team at the brewery, said: "This year’s Big Sky Beer Festival was a real success.

"Not only was it a celebration of great beer, but people enjoyed the whole experience – the live music, the food, our top of the Wolds setting and the camping.

"We can’t wait to do it all again next year."

Early bird tickets are on sale until October 31, priced at £17 for Friday, £20 for Saturday, or £32 for the weekend.
