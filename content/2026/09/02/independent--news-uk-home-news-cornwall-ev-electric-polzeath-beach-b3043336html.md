A car was found stranded on a beach in Cornwall after getting stuck in the sand as the rising tide gradually closed in.

A crowd of beachgoers gathered around the vehicle, which appears to be an electric car, after it was discovered on Polzeath beach on Saturday.

Footage shows the car sitting helplessly on the sand with its wheels buried as seawater slowly moves in. 

No one was found inside the vehicle, leaving the unattended car at the mercy of the sea.

It is unclear how the vehicle ended up stuck on the beach or what happened to it afterwards, as reported by NeedToKnow.
