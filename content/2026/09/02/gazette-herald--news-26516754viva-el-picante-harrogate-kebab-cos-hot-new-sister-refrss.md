A new take-away is set to add spice to Harrogate’s dining scene.

Opening in early October will be Viva el Picante, offering burritos, tacos and other TexMex classics.

It will operate on Station Parade, opposite the bus station, in the location of the former I Am Doner, which last month changed its name to The Harrogate Kebab Co.

Viva el Picante describes itself as “a brand-new Mexican-inspired food concept bringing 12-hour slow-cooked birria, loaded tacos, filthy mac & cheese and big flavours to Harrogate.”

“Then comes the best bit…That deep, glossy consomé on the side for dipping.”

Paul Baron, founder of I Am Doner and Viva el Picante, says the Harrogate Kebab Co isn’t closing and Viva el Picante is a sister concept that will operate alongside it.

The Michelin-trained chef added he had long sought such a food offering due to a passion for all things Latin American.

Longer term, he has a bigger dream to one say see Viva el Picante grow into a tequila and taco bar in its own right, with food, music and atmosphere.
