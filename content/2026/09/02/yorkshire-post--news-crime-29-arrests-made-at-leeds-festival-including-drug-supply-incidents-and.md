# Police reveal 29 arrests made at Leeds Festival 2026 including sexual offences

## Just under 30 arrests were made at this year’s Leeds Festival, police said.

Leeds Festival took place in Bramham Park in Wetherby across the Bank Holiday Weekend from Thursday August 27 to Sunday August 30.

Campers were also able to access the site the day before and after the festival.

![A total of 29 arrests were made at this year's Leeds Festival](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/29/6/14/01M140W3WCM4GN4VCV4RZN7YHB.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Police have confirmed that a total of 29 arrests were made throughout the Leeds Festival period.

Incidents included drug supply offences, sexual offences, and criminal damage.

A spokesperson from West Yorkshire Police said: “During the Leeds Festival policing operation, which ran from Wednesday 26 August to Monday 31 August, a total of 29 arrests were made.

“These related to a range of offences, including drug supply, sexual offences, assault, criminal damage, drink-related disorder and road traffic offences.

“The vast majority of those attending the festival enjoyed the event safely and responsibly.”
