A gothic musical comedy is bringing dark humour and heart to the stage in Monkgate this autumn.

Wharfemede Productions is presenting The Addams Family at Theatre@41, running from Tuesday, October 27 to Saturday, October 31.

This marks a return following the company’s acclaimed productions of A Little Night Music and Little Women.

Directed by Dr Helen 'Bells' Spencer, the show features music and lyrics by Andrew Lippa, with a book by Marshall Brickman and Rick Elice.

Dr Spencer said: "At its heart, this is a brilliantly funny show, packed with wit, warmth, and wonderfully eccentric characters, and I feel incredibly fortunate to be working with such an exceptionally talented cast.

"From our first read-through, the room has been filled with laughter, creativity, and a real sense of ensemble.

"I cannot wait for audiences to meet this company and experience the energy, humour, and heart they are bringing to the production."

The Addams Family follows Wednesday Addams as she falls in love with a "normal" young man and invites his parents to dinner, setting off a night of chaos and hilariously dark mishaps.

Packed with sharp comedy, lively dance numbers, and a witty score, the musical is described as a "spooky celebration of family, love, and embracing the wonderfully weird".

Dr Spencer added: "One of the reasons we chose The Addams Family is that, beneath its gothic exterior, lies a story about celebrating difference and the importance of family and community.

"The Addamses may be unconventional, but they love fiercely, support one another, and find strength in their uniqueness.

"These are values that resonate strongly with us at Wharfemede.

"We believe that our differences should be celebrated, that everyone deserves equality and respect, and that we achieve our best work when we support and learn from one another.

"This production is, in many ways, a celebration of those ideals."

The director also plans to incorporate elements of commedia dell’arte into the show.

Dr Spencer said: "I am also particularly excited to bring elements of commedia dell’arte into our interpretation of the show.

"Having had the privilege of working with commedia companies in Italy, I have long admired the genre’s physical storytelling, heightened characters, and joyful theatricality.

"Those influences feel perfectly suited to the world of The Addams Family, and I look forward to blending them with the musical’s unique humour to create something that is both fresh and deeply entertaining."

The cast includes Zander Fick as Gomez, Henrietta Linnemann as Morticia, and Abbie Law as Wednesday.

Performances take place at 7.30pm each evening, with a Saturday matinee at 2.30pm.

Tickets cost £20, or £18 for concessions, and are available at www.tickets.41monkgate.co.uk.
