Tom Gordon, The Liberal Democrat MP for Harrogate and Knaresborough is encouraging local business to share their experiences about the pressures they’re facing, what’s working, and what the Government could do differently.

This follows a successful roundtable with hospitality representatives from across the constituency held at North Bar.

The MP was joined by business owners and operators from across the sector, representing cafes, bars, hotels and merchants in Harrogate and Knaresborough as well as co-hosts the Harrogate BID and Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce.


![](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/tom-hospitality.webp)



**Tom said:**

The roundtable was a great opportunity to listen to the challenges and opportunities facing the hospitality sector in our towns.

I want to hear from as many businesses in the area as possible, which is why I’ve launched my survey.

They’re the lifeblood of our towns and every response will help me raise concerns in Parliament, with ministers and campaign for changes that make a difference.



The survey can be found here: [https://digitallibdems.typeform.com/to/aIJar3Np](https://digitallibdems.typeform.com/to/aIJar3Np)
