# Watch: Trump 'happy' that Prince Harry and Meghan left the US

President Donald Trump said he is "happy" that Prince Harry and Meghan left the US because he was not a fan of them. He added that they treated the Royal Family with "great disrespect".

His comments come after the couple announced they are moving back to the United Kingdom after living in California for six years.
