About 20 horses were reported running loose in Bishop Auckland in the early hours, prompting residents to share sightings on Facebook.

Residents shared reports of the loose horses in different parts of Bishop Auckland on Facebook.

One post online said: "Who's horses are running around Tindale at 3am? I have just woke up to ridiculous screaming, thinking someone was being chased but no.... horses running out the estate in a perfect line with no one to be found.

"It was like something out of a horror movie or something. It was so weird.

"It sounded like somebody was in trouble being chased down until I looked out the window. All brown or brown/black and white."

Durham Constabulary confirmed receiving multiple reports overnight on September 1 as residents spotted the horses running through the town.

Police said they were called at 2.45am on September 1 after about 20 horses were spotted loose on Station Road.

Officers were sent to the area, and a second call at 2.59am reported the horses between Tesco and West Auckland.

Police arrived soon afterwards, and at 3.20am the horses were seen beside the road, not on the carriageway.

At 3.34am, police received another call reporting that a black horse was in the road “near the vets”.

Officers returned to the area but could not find any horses.

No further calls were logged until 5.47am, when police received a report of five loose horses on Greenfields Road.

The Northern Echo has contacted Durham County Council for comment on the incident.

Durham County Council has powers under the Control of Horses Act 2015 to seize stray or tethered horses on its land or land open to the public.
