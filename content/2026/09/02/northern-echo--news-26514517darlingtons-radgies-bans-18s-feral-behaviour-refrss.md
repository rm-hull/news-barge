A Darlington restaurant has confirmed that anyone under 18 is no longer welcome without an adult, after "feral" and "bizarre" behaviour.

Radgies, in Blackwellgate, said the rule was introduced to protect the “safety, comfort and enjoyment” of customers and staff.

In a post on social media, the owners said: "Important notice. Due to ongoing issues with anti-social behaviour, we have made the decision that anyone under the age of 18 must now be accompanied by an adult when visiting Radgies.

"Unfortunately, this is a decision we’ve had to make to ensure the safety, comfort and enjoyment of our staff and customers."

The post continued: "We appreciate that the majority of younger customers are respectful, but the behaviour of a small minority has left us with no choice.

"Thank you for your understanding and cooperation."

The announcement prompted a strong reaction online, with many customers expressing support for the move and describing incidents they said they had witnessed in and around the town centre.

One said: “That’s why we never went back, young lads packed in, sat on the floor, shouting at one another.

"Not letting people past, just generally intimidating - felt like an out of control youth club. It was bizarre.”

Another customer said: "This is understandable but frustrating on both parties", while one person highlighted that the behaviour was part of a wider issue.

"Sick of these little terrors to be fair," commented one other. "Feral teenagers everywhere now," agreed another.

"This is understandable but frustrating for both parties," added another.

The business said it had listened to feedback and introduced the stricter measures so customers could visit without feeling intimidated.

The venue ended its statement by thanking customers for their understanding and cooperation as the new rule takes effect.
