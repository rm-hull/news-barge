A major redevelopment is set to transform the main entrance of Harrogate District Hospital.

Work will begin in early September and is expected to be completed by spring 2027.

The project aims to create a modern, accessible, and welcoming entrance for patients, visitors, and staff, and will include new retail and refreshment facilities.

Jonathan Coulter, chief executive at Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust, said: "Coming into a hospital as a patient, carer or visitor can be an anxious and emotional experience.

"We want everyone who walks through our doors to feel supported and reassured from the moment they arrive.

"This project has been designed to create a space that meets the needs of a modern healthcare setting, while improving the experience for everyone who uses it."

The trust is delivering the project in partnership with property developer Noviniti and One Retail, the retail division of Compass Group UK & Ireland.

Planning approval was granted in February 2026.

The current entrance will remain open throughout construction, and the trust has said it will work with partners to minimise disruption.

The project is primarily commercially funded, with no impact on frontline care or staff budgets.

New retail options will include a Costa Coffee and M&S Food store.

The Café Bistro will close at the end of September, while WH Smiths closed in July.

Jonathan Houlston, chief executive of Noviniti, said: "We are delighted to be working with Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust to support its transformation strategy through the creation of a new main entrance at the front of Harrogate District Hospital.

"Working alongside One Retail, this new development will significantly enhance the experience for staff, patients and visitors by providing improved retail facilities and a more welcoming, accessible route into the hospital."

The entrance will also feature new self-check-in and reception facilities, informal seating areas, and additional toilets.
