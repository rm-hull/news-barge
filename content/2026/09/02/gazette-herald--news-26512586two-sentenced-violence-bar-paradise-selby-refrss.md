The victim of a “glassing” and his attacker have both received suspended prison sentences following an incident at a cocktail bar, York Crown Court heard.

Friends Jack Collinson and Luke Williamson injured each other when they went together to the Bar Paradise in Selby, said Rachael Landin, prosecuting.

Williamson, 30, struck first, hitting Collinson, 28, with a broken glass shortly after midnight and was thrown out by staff, the court heard.

But he returned and Collinson punched him more than once, forcing him to the ground where he stamped on his head.

Collinson, of Riverside Avenue, Barlby, pleaded guilty to causing actual bodily harm.

He was given a 10-month prison sentence suspended for two years on condition he observe a nightly curfew from 8pm to 8am for three months, does 15 days’ rehabilitative activities and was barred from the Bar Paradise.

The Recorder of York, Judge Sean Morris, told Collinson he was the first defendant before him in 18 years convicted of stamping on someone’s face who had not been sent straight to jail.

“This was punch after punch,” the judge said. “It was a prolonged attack.”

He said Williamson’s sentence had been identical apart from the length of the prison sentence because his offence had been more serious and he had previous convictions.

Williamson, of Northfield, Barlby, had earlier pleaded guilty to wounding and was given a 14-month prison sentence suspended for two years on conditions.

For Collinson, Brogan Riley said he was remorseful for his actions. He had behaved out of character and was normally law-abiding.

He had been drinking before he had attacked in the way he had and had himself received a “nasty” injury immediately before responding as he had.

Ms Landin said the two men had gone to the Bar Paradise on December 8, 2024. Williamson appeared to have misinterpreted some “play fighting” in the pub and had wounded Collinson.

Matters had then escalated.

Williamson had been arrested first and had asked police what they were going to do about the man who had attacked him. He had shown police facial injuries.
