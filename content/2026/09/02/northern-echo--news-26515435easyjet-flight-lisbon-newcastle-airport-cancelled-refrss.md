More than 100 travellers were stranded after their flight home to the North East was cancelled at the last minute.

An EasyJet flight from Lisbon to Newcastle was cancelled on August 28, shortly before it was due to depart.

Passengers then faced a detour via Bristol before making their own way to Newcastle, The Northern Echo understands.

A source told the Northern Echo: "EasyJet cancelled the last flight from Lisbon to Newcastle - leaving over 100 travellers stranded.

"The only option was to fly into Bristol and Uber from Bristol to Newcastle."

The easyJet flight was due to arrive at Newcastle International Airport at 10.45pm on Friday, August 28.

Instead, passengers had to fly to Bristol before travelling to the North East by Uber on an around four-hour drive.
