A York-based chamber choir is looking for new singers.

The South Bank Singers, a 34-voice chamber choir, is inviting new members to join its ranks from September.

Rehearsals take place at St Clement’s Church in Scarcroft Road on Tuesday evenings.

The choir sings a variety of a cappella choral music, from Renaissance pieces to modern compositions.

Chair Jill Turner said, "If you have good sight-singing skills, have a voice that blends well with others, and you would like to sing a rich and diverse repertoire - all while raising money for local charities - South Bank Singers could be the perfect fit for you."

Formed in 2011, the choir is known for its themed concerts, which raise money for York charities.

Recently, the choir's two summer concerts, performed at St Clement’s Church and Holy Trinity Church, Goodramgate, collected £842 in donations for Brunswick Organic Nursery and Holy Trinity Church.

The choir is open to singers of all voice parts, with a particular interest in hearing from tenors and basses.

More information can be found at southbanksingers.co.uk.
