A York store has shut after its parent company went into administration.

Luxury bags brand Radley has closed its last remaining branches, including the one at Designer Outlet York.

Radley went into administration earlier this year.

The brand was then bought out of administration by Gordon Brothers, the owners of Poundland.

But the deal didn’t include the shops.

The one at Designer Outlet York shut after it had sold all its stock on Sunday.

Centre manager Paul Tyler said: “We are in discussions with some potential new brands to take over this prime unit, which we hope to be able to confirm in the coming weeks.”

The centre also lost pottery shop Denby due to the retailer going into administration earlier this year.

But there have been openings too, including upmarket chocolate shop Savante, while the Café Valeria Sicilian deli is to move into the site vacated by the closure of the Potions Cauldron shop.
