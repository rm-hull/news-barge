A public consultation has opened on the first phase of a major new employment-led development in North Yorkshire.

Scarborough Group International, together with joint venture partner Greyfriars Investments, is seeking feedback on plans for Flaxby Park, located on the eastern side of the former golf course site site near Junction 47 of the A1(M).

Adam Varley, development director at Scarborough Group International, said: "From the outset, we've been clear that Flaxby Park's future should be shaped through open and meaningful engagement.

"We've worked closely with local stakeholders and community representatives to develop proposals that respond to local priorities while supporting the county's long-term economic ambitions.

"We've listened carefully to the issues people have raised, from prioritising and enhancing the landscape and ecology to creating high-quality jobs, carefully considering connectivity and ensuring development comes forward in a considered and phased way.

"This consultation is a further opportunity for people to review the plans, share their views and help shape the final proposals before they are submitted to North Yorkshire Council later this year."

The proposed scheme covers approximately 99.6 acres and includes up to 810,000 sq ft of employment space, a transport and mobility hub, landscaping, ecological enhancements, and sustainable drainage infrastructure.

It is expected to create between 1,075 and 2,090 operational jobs.

Buildings are being designed to target BREEAM Excellent and EPC A standards.

The public consultation will run until Wednesday, October 7, and full details are available at flaxbypark.consultationonline.co.uk.

A public exhibition will take place at Goldsborough Cricket Club on Wednesday, September 23, from 3pm to 7pm, giving residents the chance to meet the project team and discuss the plans in person.

Feedback gathered during the consultation will inform the planning application, which is due to be submitted to North Yorkshire Council later this year.

This stage of the consultation is focused solely on the first phase of employment development.

A spokesperson for Scarborough Group International said the broader vision for the 280-acre Flaxby Park site, which is being considered through the North Yorkshire Local Plan process, is not included in the current proposals.

Scarborough Group International has spent the past 18 months working with local stakeholders and community representatives to create a long-term vision for the wider site.

This engagement has directly influenced the first phase of development now under consultation.

The wider masterplan for Flaxby Park continues to be developed separately and will be subject to future planning processes.

Scarborough Group International and Greyfriars Investments hope the new employment space will help address a shortage of modern business facilities in North Yorkshire, support inward investment, and promote long-term economic growth in the region.
