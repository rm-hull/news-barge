A man sexually assaulted a girl as she slept.

James Eisen is now beginning a three-and-a-half-year jail sentence after a judge described his offending as “secretive”.

In a statement read to the court on her behalf by prosecuting barrister Jeremy Hill-Baker, Eisen’s victim said she felt “sick, violated, and disgusted”.

She had suffered panic attacks, felt unsafe, and had received therapy to take away what had happened.

She said: “I feel like it has scarred me for life. I hope one day I will feel like me again.”

Bradford Crown Court heard how 53-year-old Eisen’s offending took place in the Bradford district.

He had also made searches for information on date rape drugs and the age of consent in foreign countries.

In addition, his mobile phone history indicated an interest in schoolgirls.

The court heard that he also made many indecent images of children.

Eisen, of Lantern Court, Hall Lane, Manchester, pleaded guilty to offences.

Mitigating, Adam King conceded that Eisen’s offending was “completely appalling and perverted” but said there was an absence of what he described as “a general long-standing interest in children”.

He said that offending was “part of a fantasy life that ran alongside his ordinary life” and that Eisen had been “horribly bullied” at boarding school, which he offered not as an explanation but as part of relevant context.

He added: “This defendant understands the horror of what he has done.”

The court heard that Eisen was of positive previous good conduct.

Sentencing Eisen, His Honour Judge Ahmed Nadim said his offending had been carried out “for your sexual gratification.”

Judge Nadim told Eisen he will serve “the requisite proportion” of his sentence in custody before being released on licence.

He added: “By virtue of the sentence that I have passed upon you, you must register as a sex offender. That obligation will endure for the entirety of your life.”

Judge Nadim also imposed a 15-year Sexual Harm Prevention Order.
