A MAN denied 13 charges of having illegal firearms, weapons and ammunition when he appeared before York Crown Court.

Miles Frankland, 40, also denied supplying cocaine between December 11, 2023, and December 14, 2024, and charges of possessing ketamine, edibles containing cannabis, and psilocybin on December 13.

In the dock with him was Danielle Falconer, 32, of Stuart Avenue, Richmond.

She denied supplying cocaine between December 11, 2023, and December 14, 2024, and possessing ketamine on December 13, 2024.

Frankland, of Mill Close, Ravensworth, Richmond, faces one charge of having a lock knife.

He aces two charges of having offensive weapons involving two extendable batons and two stun guns.

He also denied two charges of having prohibited firearms relating to blank firing pistols, two charges of having prohibited weapons relating to pepper sprays, five charges of having ammunition that is prohibited or without a certificate, and one of having a stun gun disguised as a car key.

Their cases were adjourned for trial until November 8 next year at York Crown Court and they were both released on bail.
