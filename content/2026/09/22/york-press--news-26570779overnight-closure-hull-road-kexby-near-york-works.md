DRIVERS face an overnight road closure while resurfacing works are carried out near York.

Hull Road, Kexby, just east of York, will be shut in three separate sections with the closure scheduled to begin at 8pm on Wednesday, September 23 and end at 6am on Thursday, September 24, unless the works are completed earlier.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place only during the works period.

The first closure is between the junction with Common Lane and a point 222 metres north-east of the junction with Kexby Bridge Loop.

The second section is between the junctions of Common Road and Hagg Lane. The third runs between the junction with Dauby Lane and a point 130 metres south of that junction.

Vehicles will be prohibited from driving along or waiting in each closed section while the resurfacing work is under way. Traffic signs and barriers will show the extent of the closure.

A signed diversion will direct affected vehicles via the A1079, A614, A63, A19 and A64. Access for residents and emergency vehicles will be maintained throughout the works period.

The notice does not prevent access to premises where that access is not blocked by the ongoing works. It also does not prohibit works or emergency access within the closed road.

Any existing traffic order affecting vehicles proceeding, parking or waiting in the closed road will be suspended while the road is physically closed during the works period.

City of York Council said the closures were needed to allow the resurfacing works to be carried out safely.
