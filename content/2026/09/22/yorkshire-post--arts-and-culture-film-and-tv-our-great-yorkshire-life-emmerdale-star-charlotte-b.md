# Our Great Yorkshire Life: Emmerdale star Charlotte Bellamy revisits her favourite escape in Yorkshire fishing village on new episode of Channel 5 series

## In the next episode of Our Great Yorkshire Life Emmerdale star Charlotte Bellamy will be returning to her favourite escape along the Yorkshire Coast.

In the upcoming episode of Our Great Yorkshire Life, viewers are invited on another journey through the heart of the region - celebrating the places, people and passions that make Yorkshire so special.

In the beginning of the episode, Emmerdale star Charlotte Bellamy, known for her long-running role as Laurel Thomas, revisits her favourite escape - the idyllic fishing village of Sandsend.

From nostalgic stays in a converted railway carriage to seaside lunches and a bracing dip in the North Sea, Charlotte shares why this area of the shoreline is her sanctuary.

![Charlotte Bellamy arrives for the TV Choice Awards. (Pic credit: John Phillips / Getty Images)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjY5ODZiMjcyLTdhOTMtNGQ1Ny1hODFhLWVlYTA0OGQwODcyODoyZjhiMjkzZi04MzQ3LTQwODAtOTliYS0wZTdjNDNmY2UyMWU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Her day ends with hot saunas and cold water plunges, courtesy of a mobile wellness experience - based in a converted horsebox.

Further afield in West Yorkshire, is farmer Stephen Short, whose day is split between worming lambs and shearing ewes alongside his promising young sheepdog, Red.

With deep family ties to the land and a wry Yorkshire wit, Stephen shows that tradition, care and graft are still the backbone of rural life.

Meanwhile in Dewsbury, talented seamstress Jenny Makin puts the fishing touches to her spectacular steampunk creation before heading to the vibrant Steampunk Weekend in Haworth.

![Jenny Makin and Regan Gerard Dickinson - Haworth Steampunk Festival. (Pic credit: Channel 5)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmE2MDc2MmFmLWU4NzAtNDBhNS1hNzMxLTZlYzY0ZTczYzQyNzo2YjkzNzg4ZC1iNzI4LTRmOWEtYTdkNC0wN2Q4NmNlYzM3NzQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


It’s a joyous celebration of creativity, community and self-expression - complete with tea duelling and top hats.

Back in Emley, Tom and Lauren Truelove put the final polish on their remarkable restoration of The Three Acres - a cherished family pub nearly lost to fire.

After 18 months of tireless efforts, they are preparing to reopen the doors to hundreds of loyal patrons, proving that Yorkshire grit and hospitality go hand in hand.

This episode is a love letter to heritage and reinvention - from windswept beaches to woollen hills, bustling kitchens to fantasy costumes - all proudly rooted in the Great Yorkshire spirit.
