Our Yorkshire Farm star Reuben Owen has become a familiar face on television in his own right after growing up at Ravenseat alongside his parents Amanda and Clive Owen.

Now 22, Reuben fronts Channel 5 series Reuben Owen: Life in the Dales, which follows his groundwork business, farming life and work across the Yorkshire Dales and beyond.

And his girlfriend, fellow farmer Jessica Ellwood, has become an increasingly important part of both his personal life and the programme.

The couple have now taken another major step in their relationship after moving in together at Jessica's family farm at Brough Castle in Cumbria.

Reuben and Jessica met at a Young Farmers' convention in October 2024 and went public with their relationship the following month.

![]()Reuben Owen and Jessica Ellwood *(Image: CHANNEL 5)*

Like Reuben, Jessica has grown up surrounded by farming and works alongside her family at Brough Castle Farm, where sheep and cattle are among the livestock kept.

Their shared rural backgrounds helped bring the pair together, with Jessica previously explaining that they quickly discovered how much they had in common.

Since becoming a couple, they have regularly appeared together on Reuben Owen: Life in the Dales, with Jessica joining Reuben on farming and machinery jobs.

Their relationship has now progressed further, with Reuben setting up home with Jessica at her family's farm.

The couple have even joked about already having their "first child" together after taking on responsibility for raising a pet lamb.

Despite the demands of early starts and working outdoors in all weather, both have made clear that they have no desire to swap their rural lifestyle for living in a city.

Jessica's farming background has also made her a natural fit with Reuben's family.

Reuben has previously said Jessica and his mum Amanda get on well and can often be found talking about sheep, while Jessica has spoken warmly about the wider Owen family.

The relationship follows Reuben's previous romance with Sarah Dow, who appeared alongside him in the first series of Life in the Dales.

Reuben confirmed in 2024 that he and Sarah had separated but remained friends.

Since then, Jessica has become a regular part of his life on and off screen.

The couple's move to Brough Castle Farm also marks another change for Reuben, who first became known to viewers as one of Amanda and Clive Owen's nine children at remote Ravenseat Farm in Swaledale.

While he remains closely connected to his family and the Yorkshire Dales, his life is increasingly centred around his own business, television work and his relationship with Jessica.
