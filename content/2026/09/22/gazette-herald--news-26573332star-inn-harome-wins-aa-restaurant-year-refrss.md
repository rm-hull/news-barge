A MUCH-admired North Yorkshire fine dining establishment has been celebrated for its culinary excellence by being named restaurant of the year for England.

The Star Inn at Harome, a thatched-roof pub in a village near Helmsley was named one of the UK’s best restaurants at the AA Hospitality Awards, which took place at Grosvenor House in London yesterday (Monday, September 21).

Owned by chef-patron Andrew Pern, it has long been recognised for exemplary hospitality and refined modern cooking.




The restaurant’s links with York are particularly strong through The Star Inn The City, a riverside restaurant opened in the shadow of Lendal Bridge in 2013 and inspired directly by the Harome original.

Housed in a 14th-century building, The Star Inn has held a prominent position in the British dining scene for 30 years.

The dining experience is spread across a series of interconnected rooms and cosy nooks, creating an intimate and atmospheric setting, according to AA inspectors.

The AA inspector described the restaurant as "proper old-school dining in the best sense," noting that it stays true to its rustic pub heritage while delivering a refined experience.

The inspector added that the menu is built around seasonal, hyper-local ingredients, and each dish combines classical richness with modern technique and presentation.

The wine list also received praise for its strong selection by the glass.

The AA Hospitality Awards celebrate the best hotels and restaurants across the UK each year.

A spokesman for AA Hospitality said: "Congratulations to all this year's winners on their amazing achievements, surviving and thriving at a difficult time for the hospitality industry."
