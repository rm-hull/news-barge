Newcastle Airport is preparing to welcome thousands of Sunderland AFC supporters as the club embarks on its first European campaign in more than 50 years.

The Black Cats kicked off their long-awaited return to European competition with a 1-0 victory over AZ Alkmaar in the UEFA Europa League.

Away fixtures are now scheduled across the continent - and the airport has secured 13 additional direct flights to help fans follow the team across Europe.

Chris Ion, head of aviation development at Newcastle Airport, said: "Sunderland's return to Europe for the first time since 1973 is an incredibly exciting moment for the club, its supporters and us here at Newcastle Airport.

"We are proud to have worked with our airline partners and trusted ATOL-protected tour operators to secure 13 additional flights alongside our existing scheduled services, helping thousands of fans to follow the team across Europe."

The first away match is set for October 15 against Torreense in Portugal.

Fans can fly directly to Lisbon with easyJet or Porto with Jet2.

On October 22, Sunderland travel to Poland to face Lech Poznan.

A direct charter flight is available through WorldChoice Sports, while Ryanair also offers flights to Gdansk and Wroclaw, with onward rail and road links to Poznan.

One of the most anticipated fixtures is against AC Milan at the San Siro on December 10.

EasyJet will operate a direct flight to Milan Malpensa, and Ryanair flies to Milan Bergamo.

Charter flights are also available through Wonky Sheep, Sport Options, and World Choice Sports.

Fans heading to Anderlecht on January 21 can fly direct to Brussels with EasyJet or to Brussels Charleroi with Ryanair.

Mr Ion said the airport is looking forward to welcoming both Sunderland supporters and visiting fans throughout the campaign.

He said: "We are also looking forward to welcoming visiting supporters to the North East, giving them the opportunity to experience everything the region has to offer while enjoying what promises to be some unforgettable European nights at the Stadium of Light."

Sunderland will also host Dinamo Zagreb and Jagiellonia in November and Levski Sofia in January as part of their European campaign.

Additional connections are available via Heathrow, Amsterdam, and Paris for fans travelling from further afield.
