Simply Red have added a second show at The Piece Hall next year.

The British band will now perform on Tuesday, August 10, following a rush for pre-sale tickets for their previously announced show on Monday, August 9.

Soul II Soul will join them as special guests for the additional date.

### Another chance for fans

Pre-sale tickets for the second show are currently available for members of The Piece Hall’s Club 1779.

These can be accessed by signing up via the venue’s website.

Remaining tickets for both dates will go on general sale at 10am on Thursday via Ticketmaster.

![]()Performances at the Piece Hall usually draw sizeable crowds. *(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

The shows are part of Simply Red’s 'Summer Funk '27' European Tour.

This will be the band’s first tour since their 2025 40th anniversary celebration, which sold out almost 50 venues across the UK, Europe, and Latin America.

Fronted by founder Mick Hucknall, Simply Red have been a mainstay in the British music scene for over 40 years.

### Five UK number one albums

The band has achieved five UK number one albums, a UK number one single, and nine UK top 10 hits.

They have also won three BRIT Awards, two Ivor Novellos, and a MOBO Award for Outstanding Achievement.

In North America, they have had two number one hits on Billboard’s Hot 100, several Platinum certifications, and three Grammy nominations.

Their 1991 album Stars was the best-selling album for two consecutive years in Britain and Europe.

Simply Red join Kaiser Chiefs, Ronan Keating, Alfie Boe, Jack Savoretti, and James Morrison among the first 2027 headliners announced for Live at The Piece Hall 2027.

Additional shows and artists are expected to be revealed in the coming months, as The Piece Hall continues to confirm its programme for next summer.
