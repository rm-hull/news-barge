A MAN has been accused of causing the deaths of six people by dangerous driving following a crash on the A61.

Christopher Cockell, 50, from Gresford Close in Woolley Grange, is accused of six counts of causing death by dangerous driving after a collision on Barnsley Road in the Wakefield district on July 21.

The crash claimed the lives of Shane Roller, 33, Shannen Morgan, 30, Rubie Morgan-Roller, nine, Lillie Morgan-Roller, four, Christopher Barton, 56, and Janine Barton, 48.

He has been summoned following an investigation by the Major Collision Enquiry Team (MCET) into the collision which took place on Barnsley Road, in Wakefield district, between Staincross and Newmillerdam on July, 21 2024.

Mr Cockell is due to appear before magistrates in Leeds on October 28.
