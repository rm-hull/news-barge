RUGBY fans enjoyed a day to remember thanks to a special outing to one of the sport’s biggest fixtures.

Around 30 people supported by Milewood, including individuals from York, were invited to Headingley Stadium to watch the Leeds Rhinos take on the Wigan Warriors in a sold-out match, courtesy of Booth FM (Booth Facilities Management Ltd).

The group included people supported by Milewood from across Yorkshire, along with staff from services in Doncaster, York, Leeds, Dewsbury, and Whitby.

Martyn Heginbotham, chief executive of Milewood, said: "This was a fantastic opportunity for the people we support and a great example of what can be achieved through organisations working together.

"Experiences like this are about much more than simply attending a sporting event.

"They provide opportunities to get out, socialise, share interests and experiences with others, and be part of a major community occasion.

"I would like to thank Juliette and everyone at Booth FM for their generosity, as well as the Leeds Rhinos Foundation for making our group feel so welcome.

"From all the feedback we have received, it was a brilliant day and one that will be remembered for a long time."

The Leeds Rhinos Foundation arranged a private room for the group to relax and enjoy refreshments before heading to their seats.

Booth FM later made a donation to the Foundation in thanks, with Milewood also contributing.

Juliette Booth of Booth FM said: "We were delighted to be able to help make this day possible.

"There is something very special about the atmosphere at Headingley, particularly for a fixture like Leeds Rhinos against Wigan Warriors, and it was wonderful to see so many people from Milewood able to enjoy it."

Milewood services from York included Heworth House, Harlington Road, and Hawthorn House.
