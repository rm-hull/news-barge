BRADFORD Council is investigating a report that fireworks were set off outside City Hall as part of a wedding this past weekend.

The Telegraph & Argus had been contacted by someone who visited Bradford city centre on Saturday afternoon and heard four loud bangs while walking through Norfolk Gardens.

They said the bangs came just seconds after they had seen a wedding car pulling up to the City Hall entrance.

The T&A asked Bradford Council for a comment on the potential use of fireworks at the civic building.

The Council had previously said it offers advice to wedding venues across the district in a bid to limit the use of nuisance fireworks.

A Council spokesperson said: "City Hall staff are looking into this to try to identify the perpetrators.

"We would encourage people to take part in the national consultation on fireworks which invites people to share their views on whether tougher laws are needed to control the use and sale of fireworks.

"Our 'Bang out of order' campaign aims to raise awareness of the distress caused by fireworks, getting wedding venues on board to discourage their use in celebrations. Many of these venues support this."
