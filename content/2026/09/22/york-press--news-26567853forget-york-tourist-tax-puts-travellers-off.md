## Forget the tourist tax – this is what puts travellers off

Some owners of hotels, B&Bs and guest houses seem to think that a tourist tax will put people off coming to stay because of having to pay an extra pound a night.

As a frequenter of such places, the only thing that makes me think twice about staying somewhere is dynamic pricing.

Under this system, the price of a stay can literally DOUBLE overnight!

An extra quid here and there wouldn't even cover the cost of a local newspaper.

## One man survived a wartime crash – thanks to a fox

In your recent Country Walk article on Buckden Pike it was good to see a description of the Polish war memorial.

There was only one survivor of a crash in a blizzard of a six-man Polish air crew on January 24, 1942.

This only survivor, the rear gunner, had a broken leg.

![]()The Polish War Memorial at Buckden Pike. *(Image: (Image Jonathan Smith))*

Fearing death from his injury and from exposure he saw the tracks of a fox in the snow and calculated that they would lead him to the nearest source of food.

He dragged himself agonisingly along the tracks which did indeed lead to houses and safety.

In 1973 he returned for the unveiling of a memorial cross on the Pike which incorporated some aircraft wreckage and also the bronze mask of a fox as a tribute to his guide.
