## Traffic building near scene of crash in York city centre

A white Suzuki car and a white was involved in the crash. Both had the airbags activated *(Image: Newsquest)*

**UPDATE: Two patients were taken to hospital in separate ambulances following the incident, Yorkshire Ambulance Service said.**

Traffic is building around the site of a two-vehicle crash close along a main York city centre street.

The incident, involving a white Suzuki and a white van, took place in Bishopgate Street, near Skeldergate Bridge on Tuesday (September 22).

Both vehicles were seen with the airbags activated.




![]()Police were directing vehicles around the crash site in Bishopgate Street, near Skeldergate Bridge *(Image: Newsquest)*

North Yorkshire Police and North Yorkshire Highways were in attendance at the scene at 9am.

Officers were redirecting traffic around the scene of the crash.

Traffic was moving but had become heavy along the bridge.
