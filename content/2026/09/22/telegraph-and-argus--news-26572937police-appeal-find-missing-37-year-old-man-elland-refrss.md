Police are appealing for information to help locate a 37-year-old man who has been reported missing from the Elland area.

West Yorkshire Police say Timothy, 37, was reported missing earlier this morning and officers are becoming increasingly concerned for his welfare.

He was last seen near the woodland at the junction of Sunnybank Drive and Saddleworth Road in Greetland.

## **Description released by police**

Timothy is described as a white male of slim build with mousey-coloured hair.

Police believe he may be wearing a black T-shirt and carrying a bag at the time of his disappearance.

## **Links to Bradford and Cleckheaton**

Officers say Timothy is known to have links to both Bradford and Cleckheaton and are asking members of the public to remain vigilant.

Anyone who believes they may have seen him, or who has information about his whereabouts, is urged to contact West Yorkshire Police.

## **How to contact police**

Anyone with information is asked to call 101 or use the live chat service on the West Yorkshire Police website, quoting reference 401 of September 22.

Police said they are increasingly concerned for Timothy's welfare and are continuing enquiries to trace him.

## **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A WYP spokesperson said: "We're appealing for information to help locate Timothy who has been reported missing from Elland.

"Timothy, aged 37, was reported missing earlier this morning.

"He was last seen close to the woodland at the junction of Sunnybank Drive and Saddleworth Road in Greetland.

"Timothy is described as a white male, of slim build with mousey-coloured hair. He's believed to be wearing a black t-shirt and carrying a bag.

"He is known to have links to Bradford and Cleckheaton.

"Officers are increasingly concerned about Timothy's welfare and ask that anyone who knows of his whereabouts or thinks they may have seen him to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat) quoting reference 401 of 22/9."
