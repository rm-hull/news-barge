Dozens of firefighters have been tackling a large fire in the south of the county overnight.

Multiple crews from West Yorkshire, Selby, Tadcaster, York, alongside Acomb’s aerial ladder platform appliance and Tadcaster’s Water Bowser were called to the blaze at **Brotherton**, near Knottingley at** 10.17pm last night** (Monday).

The former Thaal Indian Restaurant on the Old Great North Road went up in flames.

#### Fire location

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Multiple crews attended a derelict building fire measuring 25 x 20 metres.

“Police assisted with road closures whilst the incident was sectorised for effective firefighting tactics.

“Crews have worked throughout the night extinguishing the fire.

“The incident has been scaled down and one appliance and the ariel ladder platform remains on scene damping down hotspots.”

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The A162 (the old Great North Road) is currently closed in the centre of Brotherton following a fire at a restaurant overnight.

“The closure is expected to remain in place for some time while investigation work is carried out at the scene.

“Please avoid the area if you can and use an alternative route.”
