A suburban York pub has reopened after being closed for six months.

The Blacksmith Arms in Huntington reopened on Saturday with a new publican.

Pub owner Stonegate Taverns says the team there is now managed by “Babs, who has an impressive 30 years of experience in the pub trade.”

A spokesperson continued: “Babs and the team are so excited to take on this fantastic community pub and we’re looking forward to settling in, getting to know the locals and making The Blacksmiths Arms a place for the whole community to enjoy.

“We’ve got lots planned including a full kitchen refurb to enable us to offer a full restaurant menu, in the meantime we’ll be serving light bar snacks.

“There’s plenty more to come - we can’t wait to welcome you back.”

A Facebook post from the pub added afterwards: “A huge, genuine thank you to everyone who came through the doors tonight. Whether you popped in for a nosey, came for a drink, or somehow ‘just happened to be passing’ - we’re so grateful!

“We’ve had some lovely conversations, plenty of laughs and already picked up some great ideas to take forward as we get the kitchen sorted.

“Most of all, we’ve never felt so welcome or so at home so quickly. You’ve made our first night one we won’t forget.”
