A person was rushed to hospital after two crashes caused chaos on the A1(M) in County Durham.

The incidents happened during rush hour on Monday afternoon (September 21), leading to long tailbacks on the southbound carriageway stretching from Durham city near the A690 to Birtley, with delays of around an hour.

The first crash, involving two vehicles, was reported on the northbound side just after 4pm.

A second crash, involving four vehicles on the southbound carriageway, occurred shortly after in the same area.

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service said: "We attended two separate road traffic incidents on the A1 near Durham.

"We took a call at 4pm to reports of a two-vehicle collision near Durham services on the A1 northbound, for which one emergency ambulance attended to treat and discharge the patient at the scene."

A second call was received at 4.41pm to a four-vehicle collision near junction 62 southbound,

"A clinical team leader, emergency ambulance and rapid response vehicle attended and transported one patient to University Hospital North Durham for further treatment."

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "We assisted National Highways with recovery of four vehicles following a road traffic collision on the A1 Southbound at J62 at around 4.30pm yesterday."

The force believes there were no serious injuries.

One lane was closed on the southbound side for vehicle recovery.

Both sides of the A1 were clear and reopened by 9pm.
