Police are investigating after graffiti appeared on fire doors outside Marks & Spencer in Harrogate.

Harrogate BID said the incident has been reported to North Yorkshire Police, with investigations ongoing.

The doors were repainted - within 48 hours of the graffiti being reported - using two coats of specialist paint said to provide up to eight years of protection.

A spokesperson for Harrogate BID said:

> “We’ll continue to respond to issues like this as quickly as possible to help keep Harrogate clean, welcoming and looking its best.
>
> “If you spot graffiti or other issues affecting the town centre, please report them to us so we can help get them addressed.”

Before-and-after photographs show the graffiti-covered doors following the incident and their appearance after the work.
