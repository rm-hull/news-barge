A man who admitted stabbing two people will have to wait for medical reports before he is sentenced.

Leo Brown was arrested in Darlington while in possession of a baseball bat on Saturday February 28, this year.

The arrest came after the stabbing in Foundry Lane, Stockton, late the previous night.

A 23-year-old man was taken to hospital with serious injuries described at the time as “potentially life-changing”.

Brown was arrested after the vehicle he was travelling in with another man was stopped near Darlington.

He admitted two counts of wounding with intent: one against the stabbing victim and one against a woman who intervened as he attacked him.

Brown also admitted possessing a machete during the Stockton attack on February 27 and a baseball bat in Darlington the following day.

His defence lawyer, Richard Herrmann, requested an updated report on the seriousness of the victim’s injuries.

Judge Francis Laird, the Recorder of Middlesbrough, adjourned sentencing until November 20.
