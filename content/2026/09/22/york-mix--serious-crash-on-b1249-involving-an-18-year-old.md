Police are appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage following a serious crash near Foxholes. Both drivers are still in hospital with serious injuries.

It happened at around 8.40pm on the night of Monday 21 September on the B1249 between Foxholes and Langtoft near the border with East Yorkshire.

It involved a blue Ford Fiesta driven by an 18-year-old man that was travelling from the East Yorkshire / Humberside area and a blue Volvo V50, driven by a 48-year-old man, that was travelling in the opposite direction.

In particular, officers are appealing to anyone who saw the blue Ford Fiesta as it travelled through Humberside / East Yorkshire and into North Yorkshire, to get in touch.
