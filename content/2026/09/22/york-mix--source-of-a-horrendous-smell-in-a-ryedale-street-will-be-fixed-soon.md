Problems with a collapsed sewer in Langley Drive in Norton will be fixed.

Conservative Cllr Keane Duncan says this has been ongoing for several months, causing a “horrendous” odour, and requiring a tanker to visit daily to clear the drain.

He says Yorkshire Water has now confirmed that this will be repaired, with a date to be confirmed shortly.

The repair will require excavating to a depth of around four metres to access the affected section of sewer. There have been delays because of the need to remove nearby telegraph poles that are preventing access.

Yorkshire Water has now paid Openreach and Virgin Media to remove these. The sewer repair can proceed soon.
