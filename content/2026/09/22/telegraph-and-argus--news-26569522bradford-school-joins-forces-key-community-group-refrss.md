YOUNG people are set to take a seat at the table and help shape their communities through a new partnership.

Secondary school Carlton Bolling has teamed up with the community centre Community Works to strengthen youth involvement in decision-making and community development.

Under the partnership, three students from Carlton Bolling will join the Community Works board of trustees as Young People’s Representatives, offering their perspectives on services and projects that affect local families.

Mohammed Azum, head of school at Carlton Bolling, said: "This partnership is a natural extension of the outreach work we already do across our community.

"By working closely with Community Works and giving our students a voice as trustees, we can strengthen the support available to local families while ensuring young people help shape the services and opportunities that matter most to them."

Vikrant Bhatia, CEO of Community Works, said: "Community Works is absolutely delighted to welcome students from Carlton Bolling Secondary School to our Board as young people's representatives.

"We are excited to collaborate closely with the school to realize our shared vision of creating meaningful opportunities for local young people, ensuring their voices directly shape the future of our community."
