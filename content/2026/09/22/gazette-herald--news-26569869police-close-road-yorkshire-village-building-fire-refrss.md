## Our live feed has now finished.

* Emergency services have closed the A162 Old Great North Road following a large fire which engulfed a derelict building in Brotherton last night.
* York, Selby and Tadcaster Fire and Rescue Service crews, along with teams from West Yorkshire, were called to the area at 10.17pm.
* Police closed the road, warning residents to avoid the area and close their windows.
* As of 3.52am on Tuesday (September 22), the incident remained ongoing.
* The road was still closed at 7.30am.
