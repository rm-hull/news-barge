# None Go Bye Farm: I visited amazing farm where family can feed animals for £2, the play area is £2 and with a fully-stocked farm shop

![None Go By Farm](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmE3NDZhYmY1LTQxMDMtNGIwZC04MzRiLWRiYWRhNmI5ZTYxMTpiYjAwYjNiNy0yZTdjLTRjNTktYWUzNS05NjE5YTBhZWZlYzE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=70&enable=upscale)


## Trips to farms which are both buggy and wallet friendly are not the easiest to come by.

However, on one of my latest hunts across the internet for something to do on a Saturday morning, I came across None Go By Farm.

The Leeds farm – situated just a five minute drive from Leeds Bradford Airport – had hundreds of positive Google reviews praising its price point and accessibility with a stroller.

Feed for the animals starts at just £1 for adults, with children given a free bag.

![None Go By Farm](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmJiY2YwZDIwLTgyYjAtNGM2NC04MzM1LTI5OWNmYWU0ZTZmYzo0MTkwM2RiNy1hNDhlLTQwODYtOTE3ZC02YWE1Yzc4NDQ5ZjA=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


A family of two adults and two children would get a bag of feed each for £2 in total, giving access to a walking loop to the side of the farm shop to see and feed the animals.

Although relatively small – the loop probably takes around half an hour to get around – there are plenty of animals to see including goats and alpacas.

In a covered area to the side of the loop, animals born within the last few months are also settled.

Donkeys, geese, llama, Shetland Ponies, sheep, goats, rabbits, guinea pigs and hens round out the list of animals available.

![None Go By Farm](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjE2ZjE3MmQ3LWIxNmQtNGZhNS1iOWFjLTUyNWE0Mzk1NGZmNjpjMDRjN2U0OC1hY2NjLTQxYWUtYTRhMy00ZTQzY2ViZmFmOTc=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


There are washroom facilities including changing areas, sinks at multiple points to wash hands and a few covered areas to hide from the rain.

Inside, a fully-stocked farm shop has everything you could think of including home-made jams, a butchery section and even toys for children.

The farm said “we pride ourselves on being a great place for the young ones”; to the rear of the farm shop, a play area is just £2 a child to enter.

Fishing ponds are also located at the bottom of the site.

![None Go By Farm](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmU3OWRmOTFlLTlmZDMtNDE3Ny05YmIwLTdhMTkwZWM5YzQ1OTozYjI0NmYzMi01NTg0LTQwZGQtYmRjYi1kYjI2OWU2ZGM4ODI=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


On the day we arrived, the car park was filling fast so get there early to secure a spot. Parking is free, which is another bonus.

The farm shop is open from 9am to 5pm from Tuesday to Saturday.

One reviewer said: “The farm shop has great quality products and the staff running the whole place are very friendly” – I agree.

There is a minimum charge to use a card, so be sure to bring cash.

Where else can you have a morning at a farm and have change from £10?
