A FESTIVAL marking 20 years of autumn traditions and family fun is set to return this season.

Farmer Copleys Pumpkin Festival, held at the family-run farm in Yorkshire, has grown into a major autumn attraction, with thousands of families visiting each year.

Founded by Rob and Heather Copley after a trip to the United States, the festival was inspired by the American tradition of pumpkin picking and has since become a staple of the region’s autumn calendar.

Heather Copley said: "Twenty years ago, we came home from America with an idea and we really had no idea just how much it would grow.

"When we look back at photographs from the early Pumpkin Festivals, what really stands out is the people and the memories.

"We’ve had comments from people saying that children in some of those old photographs are now in high school, and that really brings home just how many generations have grown up visiting us.

"Of course, we love growing the pumpkins and creating something exciting for people to enjoy, but ultimately, it’s about giving families somewhere to make memories together.

"That is what has always mattered to us."

The festival now features more than 300,000 homegrown pumpkins in over 40 varieties, spread across the farm in fields of orange, white, green, and striped pumpkins.

Visitors can enjoy tractor rides, live shows, archery, vintage fairground rides, and daily fancy dress competitions.

There are also special appearances from family favourites like Velcro the Scarecrow and themed events such as Witches’ Storytime.

The festival site is filled with photo opportunities, including the Pumpkin House, vintage Chevy Truck, and the How Tall This Fall height board.

Food stalls will offer pumpkin-inspired treats, and visitors are encouraged to take their pumpkins home to cook seasonal dishes like pumpkin soup and pies.

This year’s festival also includes Twilight Pumpkin Picking nights, featuring live music, marshmallow toasting, and drinks at the Pumpkin Bar.

The Farmer Copleys Pumpkin Festival runs on selected dates from September 26 to November 1, 2026, with evening events, live country music nights, and extended evening sessions.
