A handcrafted 'teaser' in the window of an empty York shop close to the Bar Walls reveals the detail of a new opening.

Last Leaf Furniture, founded by York craftsman Sam Parkin, will open its doors at 10 Bootham "very shortly", just steps from York Minster and built onto the historic 13th-century walls of St Mary’s Abbey.

The shop window has been dressed with a black sheet with a handmade stool on display and a wooden block featuring the company brand.

![]()Last Leaf works are all handcrafted as this teaser in the shop window in Bootham reveals *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

Work like this - handmade by Sam - will be available to order from him via a ground floor showroom workshop once the business opens.

Sam said: "It all hands on deck at the moment, I'm fitting out the shop and making all the stock.

"There will be two floors - the first will be a gallery and exhibition space showing big and one-off pieces and the ground floor more of a shop with smaller stuff built by me on display, like the stool that's in the window, and a workshop."

He said it didn't take long to make a decision to fill the space in Bootham, as he had previous shown work there in an exhibition "that went really well" around eight or nine years ago when it was Lotte Inch Gallery, prior to its time as The Blue House Bookshop.

Sam's main workshop is in Flaxton outside York where he has been for ten years and the shop takes him into new territory from his usual month-long exhibitions elsewhere.

Last Leaf Furniture is known for its bespoke, handcrafted pieces that blend traditional woodworking techniques with contemporary design.

Sam uses carefully selected hardwoods and often highlights the unique character, grain, and imperfections of the timber in his work.

His portfolio includes dining tables, desks, cabinets, stools, benches, and plant stands, all created with a focus on durability, beauty, and sustainability.

![]()Sam said the Last Leaf Showroom in Bootham will run alongside the main workshop outside York *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

With a background in lutherie—instrument making— Sam incorporates detailed woodcarving, marquetry, and inlay work into many of his pieces.

He's worked on large projects for Ripon Cathedral, Christchurch College Oxford and St Martin in the Fields in the centre of London.

The new showroom takes over the space previously occupied by The Blue House Bookshop, which closed after seven years to focus on community outreach and creative programmes.
