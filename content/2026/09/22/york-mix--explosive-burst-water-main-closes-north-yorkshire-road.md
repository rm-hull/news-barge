A burst water main is causing serious problems on a North Yorkshire road this morning.

It shooting jets of water into the air on the A1041 Camblesforth straight.

The problem has closed the road. Police are on the scene directing traffic.

#### Map location

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/burst-water-main-a1041-camblesforth-22-sept-2026-full.webp)

It’s leading to long rush-hour queues. The road will be affected until engineers can fix the problem.
