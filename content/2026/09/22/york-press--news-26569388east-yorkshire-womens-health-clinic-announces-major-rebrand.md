A WOMEN'S health clinic has announced a rebrand as part of its ongoing growth.

East Riding Wellness and Aesthetics, founded by consultant nurse practitioner Tracey Dennison, will now operate under the name The Dennison Clinic.

The clinic specialises in women’s health, menopause and hormonal care, alongside medical aesthetics, weight management and longevity services.

Tracey Dennison, twice named UK Menopause Nurse of the Year, said: "The business has changed significantly since we first launched.

"Today we’re supporting women with everything from menopause and hormonal health to weight management and medical aesthetics, both in clinic and online.

"The Dennison Clinic reflects that broader vision for the future."

The rebrand follows the clinic’s recent expansion beyond East Yorkshire, with additional locations in York and Milton Keynes, and online consultations now available nationwide.

Earlier this year, The Dennison Clinic was named Best Menopause Clinic (North of England) at the Menopause in Practice Awards 2026.

Ms Dennison is a British Menopause Society registered advanced specialist and teaches on the organisation’s advanced programme, training healthcare professionals across the UK in menopause care.

She is also involved in research collaborations with Durham University and Manchester Metropolitan University.

Ms Dennison said: "Things are definitely changing and it is encouraging to see more recognition of the importance of women’s health.

"But too many women still struggle to get the right information, diagnosis and support.

"We want to provide a place where women feel listened to and where their care is based on the best available evidence."

Supporting the clinic’s expansion, Ms Dennison is now joined by GP Dr Matthew Johnson, who has more than 22 years of experience and a specialist interest in women’s health.

Surgeon Dr Arturo Almeida performs minor surgery, fat-dissolving procedures and varicose vein treatments.
