Big Brother is back on ITV2 and ITVX, with North East fans tuning in for diary-room confessions, explosive rows and surprise evictions.

The reality show returned in 2023 after a five-year break and has continued to attract millions of viewers.

But before ITV’s revival, several North East contestants made their mark in the house, with some winning the show and others becoming household names.

Here are some of the region’s most memorable Big Brother stars and what they are doing now.

### Anthony Hutton

![]()Anthony Hutton *(Image: Supplied)*

The region's most successful Big Brother contestant is Anthony Hutton, who won the sixth series in 2005.

Born in Consett and now based in Darlington, Anthony became one of the show’s most popular winners.

Since leaving the house, he has built a successful barbering business and had previously announced plans to open a training academy.

When ITV confirmed Big Brother's return, Anthony told viewers he was "over the moon" to see the show back on TV.

He has since spoken publicly about suicidal feelings after his sudden rise to fame and launched the Throw In The Towel Project to challenge stigma around men's mental health and suicide.

### Jonny Regan

![]()Jonny Regan pictured in 2023. *(Image: Splash)*

Former firefighter Jonny Regan, from County Durham, appeared on the third series in 2002.

He finished runner-up to Kate Lawler alongside future stars Jade Goody and Alison Hammond.

### Michelle Bass

![]()Michelle Bass. *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Michelle Bass, from Dinnington in Newcastle, was one of Big Brother’s most memorable early contestants.

She appeared on series five in 2004, became known for her relationship with Stuart Wilson and returned for Ultimate Big Brother in 2010.

### Liam McGough

![]()Liam McGough. *(Image: PA)*

County Durham tree surgeon Liam McGough found fame on Big Brother 8 in 2007.

He finished third and received a £100,000 bonus, making him one of that year’s most talked-about contestants.

Nearly 20 years after leaving the Big Brother house, Liam is making a name for himself as a stand-up comedian.

He has also returned from his New Orleans wedding to his wife, who prefers to stay out of the spotlight.

### Jennifer Clark

![]()Jennifer Clark. *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Teesside-born Jennifer Clark appeared in the 2008 series and became known for her on-screen romance with housemate Dale Howard.

After briefly modelling for magazines and making nightclub appearances, Jennifer stepped back from the spotlight and now leads a quiet life in County Durham.

### Charlie Drummond

![]()Charlie Drummond. *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

County Durham contestant Charlie Drummond finished fourth in the 2009 series.

After the show, Charlie faced legal trouble and was jailed in 2014 for GBH in Newcastle city centre.

### Jay McKray

![]()Jay McKray. *(Image: CHANNEL 5)*

Newcastle bodybuilder Jay McKray appeared in Channel 5’s first series in 2011.

He struck up a romance with housemate Louise Cliffe. They later married but separated less than a year later.

Jay later appeared on Signed by Katie Price and released his 2013 single Dance before becoming a DJ under the name Geordie Dutch.

### Alex Lee

![]()Alex Lee. *(Image: CHANNEL 5)*

Former footballer Alex Lee, from Sunderland, appeared on the same 2011 series and finished third.

Alex later appeared on a BBC Three revival of Snog, Marry, Avoid? before returning to her job as an air hostess in 2018.

### Zoe Birkett

![]()Zoe Birkett. *(Image: CHANNEL 5)*

Consett-born singer and actress Zoe Birkett entered the house in 2014.

Already known for finishing fourth on the first series of Pop Idol, she went on to a successful West End career, starring in Moulin Rouge! and The Bodyguard.

### Simone Reed

![]()Simone Reed was jailed for 32 months in October 2025 for her role in a petrol bomb attack in Redcar, but was released just weeks later. *(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

Teessider Simone Reed entered the house in 2017 after appearing on The X Factor.

The 37-year-old was jailed for 32 months in October for her role in a Redcar bar brawl in which an innocent bystander was petrol-bombed.

However, she was released just weeks later.

### Lewis Flanagan

![]()Lewis Flanagan. *(Image: CHANNEL 5)*

Stockton-born Lewis Flanagan appeared on the final Channel 5 series in 2018.

One of that year’s most controversial contestants, he was removed from the house for using offensive language.

Lewis spent time out of the spotlight in Australia before returning to the UK in March.

## The ITV reboot

![]()Hosts AJ Odudu and Will Best in 2025. *(Image: ITV)*

Since ITV revived the show in 2023, few North East contestants have appeared.

Jordan Sangha from Scunthorpe won the 2023 revival, while 2024 winner Ali Bromley was from Leicester.

The 2025 series also had no North East contestants in its final line-up.

## Are there any North East contestants in Big Brother 2026?

The 2026 series launched on September 13 with 16 housemates from across the UK.

But none of this year’s housemates are from the North East; they come from places including Manchester, Liverpool, London, Oxford, Essex and Paisley.

## When is Big Brother on TV?

![]()Big Brother 2026 kicked off on September 13. *(Image: ITV)*

The 2026 series airs on ITV2 and ITVX, with episodes broadcast nightly. Companion programme Big Brother: The Group Chat, hosted by former winner Kate Lawler and influencer GK Barry, follows the main show from Sunday to Thursday.

The latest run, dubbed the "House of Chaos", began on September 13, with 16 housemates competing for £100,000.
