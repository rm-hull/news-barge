## Two taken to hospital after crash in busy York city centre road

Police were directing vehicles around the crash site in Bishopgate Street, near Skeldergate Bridge <i>(Image: Newsquest)

Two people have been taken to hospital following a crash along a main road in York city centre.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said it deployed two ambulances for the patients, who were involved in a crash in Bishopgate Street, near Skeldergate Bridge, after receiving an emergency call at 7.15am on Tuesday (September 22).




It comes after a white Suzuki car and a white van were seen with the vehicle airbags activated shortly after 9am.

The incident caused heavy traffic in the region, with motorists redirected by officers from North Yorkshire Police.

North Yorkshire Highways were also deployed to the area.

Traffic has since returned to normal.
