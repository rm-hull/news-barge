KAISER Chiefs are set to headline a major show next summer.

The Leeds indie rock band will perform at The Piece Hall in Halifax on June 4 as part of the Live at The Piece Hall concert series.

It marks a return to the historic venue after they played two sold-out shows there in 2021.

Nicky Chance-Thompson, chief executive of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "I cannot wait to welcome back these true Yorkshire legends and show them just how much Live at The Piece Hall has grown.

"They last played in 2021 when there weren’t even 10 shows per season – there are now more than 40.

"The stage is bigger, the sound is better, the crowds are larger and louder, combine that with Kaiser Chiefs' immense back catalogue of incredible indie bangers and I predict a riotously good time will be had by one and all."

Formed in 2000, the band has released eight studio albums, including the Mercury Prize-nominated Employment and the chart-topping Yours Truly, Angry Mob.

They have sold eight million albums, earned three BRIT Awards, and won an Ivor Novello for Album of the Year.

Other headliners confirmed for The Piece Hall in 2027 include Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti, James Morrison, and Simply Red.

**Tickets go on sale at 9am Friday via** ticketmaster.co.uk.
