A MAN has suffered a broken jaw and another has been injured in an alleged "serious assault" in York.

North Yorkshire Police said it happened on Parliament Street at around 2.45am on Sunday, August 23, when two men were "assaulted" by a group of four people.

One of the victims sustained a broken jaw and required extensive medical treatment, while the other suffered minor injuries, including facial bruising, police said.




North Yorkshire Police has arrested two 17-year-old boys in connection with the alleged incident.

A force spokesperson said one remains on police bail, while the other has been released without charge.

Officers are now appealing for help to identify two people captured in images released by the force, as they believe these individuals may have information that could assist the investigation.

They are also appealing to a girl or woman who is believed to have recorded the incident to come forward.

Anyone who recognises the individuals or has information that could help identify them is asked to email [Lyn.Knight@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Lyn.Knight@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101, quoting incident number 12260164429.

Information can also be provided anonymously through Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or submitting a report online.

Enquiries remain ongoing.
