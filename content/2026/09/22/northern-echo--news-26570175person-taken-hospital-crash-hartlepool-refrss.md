One person has been taken to hospital following a crash in Hartlepool.

Police and paramedics were called to the junction of West View Road and Cleveland Road on Monday (September 21) after a crash just before 10pm.

Cleveland Police shut the road between Old Cemetery Road and Ropery Road for five hours following the incident overnight.

The road was reopened around 3.30am.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) has confirmed that one person was taken to the James Cook University Hospital in Middlesbrough for further treatment.

Their condition remains unknown.

A NEAS spokesperson said: "We were called to reports of a road traffic collision at the junction of Cleveland Road and West View Road in Hartlepool at 9.49pm on September 21.

"We dispatched a clinical team leader and one ambulance crew, who transported one patient to James Cook Hospital for further treatment."

Cleveland Police has been contacted for more details on the nature of the crash.
