York’s medieval pub crawl is set to return this weekend.

The Assize of Ale and Bread is a medieval tradition that requires the Sheriff of York to visit local pubs, tasting the ale to ensure it is of good quality.

The annual event will take place this year on **Saturday 26 September**, and forms as part of the York Food Festival.

The expensive equipment required for brewing led to a commercial market for these goods, and so, a need for regulations to control quality and pricing. Therefore the Sheriff summoned their sergeants to visit the city’s ale houses to test the quality of the ale.

Any sergeant who failed to answer the summons was guilty of an offence and liable to fines or time in the pillory, so be warned!

The event starts at 2pm at York Mansion House, where the Town Crier will start proceedings by introducing The Sheriff of York to perform the traditional proclamation.

Then, participants will visit several pubs in the city centre with the final stop at the York Food and Drink Festival Marquee in Parliament Street for the closing declaration.

Guests are then invited for a Fish and Chip Supper and an ale sponsored and supported by ‘Mr Chippy’, ‘Brew York’, ‘Dough’ and ‘The Potions Cauldron Group’.

Tickets for the Fish and Chip Supper are £20, with all proceeds going to the Lord Mayor’s Charity Fund – which this year is local charity ‘York Cares’ and community group ‘Shared York’. The event is held at St Sampson’s Centre, York.

Participants wearing medieval or ancient costumes will be eligible to 10% discount off drinks in all the participating pubs. Should you need inspiration, Festival of Fun on Goodramgate is offering 10% off purchases if you quote ‘Assize of Ale’.

The Sheriff of York, Geoff Cossins said, “This event is truly a highlight of the Sheriff’s year in office and I will taking the ale tasting very seriously, I mean what is not to enjoy, fantastic local beers, award winning bread, ending with fish and chips.

“Plus what is even better is that we raise funds for local charities.”
