York Minster to host a spellbinding organ concert – featuring music from the *Harry Potter* film franchise.

Taking place on **Saturday 31 October**, the concert will include newly commissioned arrangements of music from the series with organist Andrew Forbes using his own brand of magic to celebrate the central role of pipe organs in the spookier side of cinema.

Iconic film scores by John Williams (*Hedwig’s Theme* and* Harry’s Wondrous World*), Alexandre Desplat (* Statues*and* Farewell to Dobby*), Nicholas Hooper (* Ministry of Magic*) and Patrick Doyle (* Hogwarts’ Hymn)*, plus arrangements of orchestral music by Gounod, Holst, and Stravinsky, will be heard alongside a ‘thrilling’ classic by Michael Jackson and original pieces of fabulous organ music by J S Bach, Cecilia McDowall, and Louis Vierne.

Gounod’s *Funeral March for a Marionette*— the theme of* Alfred Hitchcock Presents,* Bach’s famous and thunderous* Toccata in D minor*, and Vierne’s phantasmagorical* Feux Follets* will be just some of the pieces used to display our Grand Organ’s formidable array of orchestral colours and demonstrate why the organ was cinema’s original surround-sound system.

Andrew Forbes said: “York Minster’s towering stone walls and mighty organ make a dramatic combination; the perfect setting for an evening of magic and mystery.

“For over a century filmmakers have used the power of organ music, holding audiences spellbound or making them jump out of their seats, but nothing compares with experiencing it live.”

Promising magic, mystery and unforgettable music, this enchanting evening is a must-see for fans of cinema and organ music alike.

*The Concert That Must Not Be Named* will take place on Saturday 31 October at 7:30pm in the nave of York Minster.
