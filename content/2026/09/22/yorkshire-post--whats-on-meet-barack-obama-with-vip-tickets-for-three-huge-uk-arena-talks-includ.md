## Former US President Barack Obama is heading to Belfast, Glasgow and Manchester for three special arena appearances – with the chance to have your photograph taken with him.

Obama’s famous campaign slogan was “Yes We Can.”

Now, **yes you can** meet him and have a photo with the 44th President of the United States. But it will cost you £2,000, with other packages also available from hospitality promoters**Seat Unique**.

![An Evening With President Barack Obama](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/22/16/38/An-Evening-With-President-Barack-Obama-banner.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&width=640&quality=65)


The 44th President of the United States will appear at **The O2 Belfast on Tuesday, October 13**,** OVO Hydro in Glasgow on Wednesday, October 14**, and** Co-op Live, Manchester, on Thursday, October 15**.

The Manchester appearance could prove particularly attractive to fans travelling from Yorkshire and across the North to hear the former president in person.

Fans can get much closer than the back row when the former US President arrives in the UK and Ireland this October.

For those wanting the ultimate experience, Seat Unique is offering a **Photo VIP package at £2,000 per person**.

It includes a photo opportunity with President Obama, with **one photograph provided per two guests**, although the experience is subject to security clearance.

Other premium options currently advertised for the **Manchester Co-op Live event** include a**Row 4 Premium Ticket for £499 per person**, guaranteeing a fourth-row seat as well as access to the Backstage Club an hour before general admission and a welcome drink.

At the time of writing, Seat Unique was showing only **five tickets remaining** for that package.

The **Hideaway package costs £349 per person** and includes early arena access, entertainment in a secret nightclub and seating among what Seat Unique describes as 200 of the venue's best seats in Blocks 102 and 103.

A **16-seater Private Suite** is also listed, with the price to be confirmed. It includes exclusive use of the box, early entry and access through a private VIP entrance.

Row 11 Premium Tickets, previously priced at **£379 per person**, and the**£249 Seat Unique Suite** package were listed as sold out.

Presented by Seat Unique alongside Fane, Kilimanjaro, Singular Artists and Regular Music, **An Evening with President Barack Obama** will give audiences the opportunity to hear the former president discuss America and wider global issues.

He served as US President from 2009 to 2017 and was the first African-American president.

The three appearances take place during the year marking the 250th anniversary of the US Declaration of Independence.

Doors open at 6pm and under-14s must be accompanied by an adult aged 18 or over. The events start at 8pm.

**Photo VIP — £2,000 per person:** Photo opportunity with President Barack Obama, with one photo per two guests and subject to security clearance.

**Premium Ticket – Row 4 — £499 per person:** Fourth-row seating, early Backstage Club access and welcome drink. Only five tickets were showing as remaining at the time of writing.

**Hideaway — £349 per person:** Early arena access, live entertainment in the Hideaway and premium Block 102/103 seating.
