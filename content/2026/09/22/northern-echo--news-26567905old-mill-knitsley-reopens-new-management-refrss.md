A popular pub and restaurant near Consett has reopened under new ownership, with its new boss promising to restore the venue as "the heart of the community once again".

The Old Mill in Knitsley reopened its bar for drinks on September 18, while work continues on the kitchen ahead of the full food launch.

Operator Wissem Amara has taken over the historic pub, promising new menus, entertainment and community events.

![]()The Old Mill Knitsley. *(Image: GOOGLE MAPS)*

In a message to customers, Wissem said: "It is a real privilege to take over such a well-loved local pub with so much history and character.

"My aim is simple: to create a warm, welcoming place where everyone feels at home, whether you're joining us for a drink with friends, a family meal, a celebration, or just calling in for a coffee."

The reopening has generated excitement among residents, with the business launching a giveaway offering a £100 food voucher for one winner and a £50 voucher for another to celebrate the start of its new chapter.

![]() *(Image: GOOGLE MAPS)*

While the kitchen is not yet ready to reopen, the bar has begun serving customers and bosses say updates on the restaurant launch will be announced soon.

Mr Amara said the pub will offer "exciting new menus, regular entertainment, themed events, and a few surprises along the way" while keeping its friendly, local atmosphere.

He added: "I can't wait to meet you all, hear your ideas, and become part of this fantastic community. Your support means everything as we begin this exciting new journey together.

"Please pop in, say hello, and help us make The Old Mill the heart of our community once again.

"Here's to new beginnings, great food, great drinks, and even better company."

The reopening has won support from punters.

One pub-goer said: "The guy who has taken this on is amazing.

"He has the experience and stamina and charisma to make this place a very special and truly lovely place to go and spend time with friends, family and whoever else.
