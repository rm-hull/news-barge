A new café will be opening in York city centre as efforts to redevelop a key shopping street take a step forward.

Federal will open its fifth store in Coney Street this autumn with work due to start at the site in late September.

The Manchester-founded Australia and New Zealand-inspired café, which also has a branch in Leeds, will open in the former Lush unit at the St Helen’s Square end of Coney Street.

![]()The former Lush site in Coney Street, York, which Federal will move into *(Image: Google Street View)*

A Federal spokesperson said the 100-cover venue will feature a large outdoor area and a second level offering additional seating, as well as the potential to host private hires and events.

They said Federal’s expansion into York forms part of a £3 million investment from Foresight Group to support Federal’s national rollout strategy.

![]()Inside a Federal as the café prepares to open in Coney Street, York *(Image: Supplied)*

Federal founder Claudio Ribeiro said: “York feels like a natural fit for Federal. It has a strong independent spirit, a huge student community and a thriving food and drink scene, alongside an incredible year-round visitor economy.

“We’re incredibly lucky to have found a fantastic location and we’re excited to create something that feels unmistakably Federal while celebrating the history and character of the building.

![]()Food at Federal as the café prepares to open in Coney Street, York *(Image: Supplied)*

“York will give customers everything they know and love about Federal with amazing brunches, top-quality speciality coffee and five-star service.

“Our people are fundamental to the energy and personality of our cafes, and we’re looking forward to building a brilliant team in York.”

The Federal spokesperson said its customers in York “can expect the same Federal experience already enjoyed by diners in Manchester and Leeds with a core menu featuring Federal favourites, alongside seasonal food and drink specials that change regularly throughout the year”.

![]()Drinks from Federal as the café prepares to open in Coney Street, York *(Image: Supplied)*

Federal’s opening in Coney Street comes as work gets underway on a major redevelopment to open the area up to the nearby riverside.

Phase one of the scheme, by developers the Helmsley Group, began in June with work ongoing to renovate shopfronts at the St Helen's Square end of Coney Street.

Max Reeves, development director at the York-based Helmsley Group, said the work to the shopfronts is planned to be completed by September.

The work includes the former JD Sports shop being renovated before the Space NK beauty store opens in the unit on Friday (September 25).

Mr Reeves said later work will see the upper floors of JD Sports and its neighbouring units converted into flats.

The director welcomed work starting in Coney Street, which came four years after the plans were first submitted and two years after planning permission was granted.

“It’s great to see the work coming forward on Coney Street after a long period of consultation and planning,” Mr Reeves told The Press in June after the work started. “I’m looking forward to seeing what it looks like.”
