A York whose life was changed by a violent assault is helping launch one of the UK’s toughest fundraising challenges in the Scottish Highlands.

Iain Forbes is supporting the 2027 Cateran Yomp, a trek of up to 54 miles through the Highlands, alongside double-amputee ambassador Gregg Stevenson.

Mr Forbes said: "I’ve made lifelong friends there, and want to continue my support for a charity that helped me when the chips were down.

"I’ve been inspired to help others, which is what we do in the military family."

The Army Benevolent Fund supported Forbes after a violent assault left him unable to work.

He has since raised more than £14,000 through the Yomp, making him one of its biggest fundraisers.

Former Royal Engineer Commando Stevenson, who lost both legs in Afghanistan in 2009, said: "I’m thrilled to be the 2027 Yomp ambassador and I’d urge as many people as possible to sign up for the event.

"It’s been a journey since my injury, but with the ABF’s support, I've been able to get the best out of myself and I couldn't be more thankful."

The 2027 Yomp will take place in Perthshire on June 5 and 6, 2027.
