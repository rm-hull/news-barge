# Watch: Threats, deals and passionate speeches at UNGA event

The United Nations General Assembly event is underway in New York, with delegations from around the world meeting to give speeches and hold meetings.

Key moments included US President Donald Trump's threat to "annihilate Iran", the UK's new prime minister Andy Burnham meeting some world leaders for the first time, and a farewell speech from UN Secretary General António Guterres.
