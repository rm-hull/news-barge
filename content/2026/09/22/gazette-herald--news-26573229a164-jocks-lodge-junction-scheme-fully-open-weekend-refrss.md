A MAJOR new road scheme is set to fully open this weekend, which should improve journey times to and from York to major conurbations in Yorkshire.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council said the "full" A164/Jock's Lodge junction scheme will be open by Saturday, September 26.

Traffic restrictions, including a 30mph speed limit on the A164, will be lifted overnight between Thursday, September 24, and Friday, September 25, including the new Bentley bridge.




However, work will still be ongoing around the scheme, including the removal of the site compound and further landscaping works, the council said.

The £86.9 million A164 and Jock's Lodge Improvement Scheme is one of the largest highways projects ever undertaken by East Riding of Yorkshire Council.

Planning permission was granted in 2020, followed by the approval of compulsory purchase and side road orders in 2021.

Preliminary works began in 2023, including changes at Skidby and the construction of a new roundabout.

Main construction started in January 2024.

Since then, the scheme has delivered a new roundabout on the A1079, new link roads connecting the A164 with Beverley's Minster Way and Lincoln Way, the construction of Bentley Bridge, improvements to the Dunflat Road junction and sections of new dual carriageway.

New walking and cycling routes have also been created alongside upgraded drainage infrastructure.

During 2025 and 2026, traffic was progressively switched onto newly built sections of road.

The scheme is expected to be completed in 2026, improving safety, capacity and journey times.

The A164/Jock's Lodge roundabout connects York with the Humber Bridge and Kingston-upon-Hull.
