A drunken man attacked a police officer launching a "running kick" before going on the run for eight months.

Lee Spencer assaulted the officer as they tried to arrest him at around 11pm on March 7, 2025, in Bishop Auckland.

The 44-year-old was heavily intoxicated when the assault took place, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Elisha Marsay said Durham Police were called to an address after reports of a disturbance.

She said: “The defendant was asked to leave the property and was arrested. As he was escorted to the police van the defendant continued to tense up his body, preventing them from putting him in the cage.”

Miss Marsay said the defendant then launched a "running kick" on the officer before he was eventually subdued and arrested.

Spencer, of Walton Road, Liverpool, admitted assaulting an emergency worker and breaching bail by failing to appear in court after his release.

His barrister, Tabitha Buck, said Spencer had longstanding problems with drugs and alcohol and had been in custody since pleading guilty.

Explaining why Spencer went on the run for eight months, she said: “He did bury his head in the sand and has been open and honest about that.”

Judge Richard Bennett sentenced Spencer to 16 weeks in prison, suspended for 12 months.

He said: “You were involved in some sort of incident which resulted in the police being called out.
