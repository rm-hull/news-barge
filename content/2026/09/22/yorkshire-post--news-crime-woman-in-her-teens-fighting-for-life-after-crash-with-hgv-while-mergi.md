# Woman in her teens fighting for life after crash with HGV while merging onto A1

## A woman is fighting for her life after she suffered severe injuries in a crash while joining the A1 in Yorkshire.

The collision happened on the northbound A1, at the junction for the B6474, between Wentbridge and Darrington, shortly before 2.20pm today on September 21.

A blue VW Polo travelling northbound was joining the A1 when it was in collision with a Scania HGV car transporter which was travelling northbound on the A1.

The driver of the Polo, a woman aged in her teens, suffered life-threatening head injuries and was taken to hospital.
