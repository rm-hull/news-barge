A collaborative community initiative in **Knaresborough** has successfully relaunched its monthly digital literacy campaign, providing local seniors with hands-on assistance to navigate modern devices and communication platforms.

The recurring **Tech Support Café**, hosted at** Gracious Street Methodist Church**, returned on** Monday 21 September 2026**following its scheduled summer operational hiatus. Driven under the wider* Digitally Connected Knaresborough* strategic banner, the drop-in workshop operates in partnership with localized healthcare and social network providers, including Right at Home Harrogate, Knaresborough Connectors, and Thistle Hill Care Centre.

The sessions, which launch at **1:00 PM on the third Monday of each month**, are structured to build technical confidence across generations in a relaxed environment complete with complimentary refreshments. Attendees are invited to bring along their personal hardware or borrow a device on-site, with volunteer teams addressing questions spanning basic mobile layouts, tablet and iPad configurations, smart speakers, applications, and emerging consumer artificial intelligence systems.

> **Veronica Manolache, Managing Director of Right at Home Harrogate, said:**
>
> From engaging activities to exploring innovative technology designed to make homes safer, we are excited to deliver an informative and interactive experience for all involved. Together, as a community, we can empower people to feel secure and supported in their own homes.

## Sustaining Local Digital Inclusion Hubs

The continuation of community tech hubs highlights an essential response to the rapid, forced migration of vital civic services toward online-only portals across North Yorkshire. As regional banking branches shutter and local authority services centralize behind mobile applications, elderly and isolated populations face an increasing risk of digital exclusion. The Gracious Street hub functions as a critical local node, removing cost barriers and administrative anxiety by replacing structured instructional courses with informal, self-paced troubleshooting environments.

This localised method heavily fosters social interaction, leveraging the physical infrastructure of urban community spaces to combat senior isolation. By matching community support workers directly with local residents, the framework ensures vulnerable individuals retain independent access to telehealth, remote public transport tracking, and domestic communication lines without relinquishing personal data control.

## Corporate Sponsorship Integration

The operational framework of the Knaresborough hub relies on a hybrid funding design, matching volunteer grassroots networks with resources supplied by major corporate care operators. The initiative receives long-term personnel and baseline logistics backing from the nearby Thistle Hill care home facility, a 92-bed specialised nursing and dementia care complex operated by Barchester Healthcare.

By integrating personnel from national networks—which manage over 200 care properties across the UK—the local scheme gains access to robust safeguarding compliance and specialized communication techniques tailored for visitors navigating early-stage cognitive decline or Alzheimer’s. The upcoming winter schedule of workshops, session resource maps, and regional community transport options can be reviewed directly via the official Barchester Corporate Portal.
