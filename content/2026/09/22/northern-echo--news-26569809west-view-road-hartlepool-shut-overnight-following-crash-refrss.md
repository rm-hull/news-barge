A busy road was shut for five hours overnight following a crash.

West View Road, in Hartlepool, was closed between Old Cemetery Road and Ropery Road on Monday (September 21) following a crash before 10.13pm.

Drivers were unable to get down the Cleveland Road and West View Road junction, with the closure stretching towards the former Admiral Court Care Home.

While the seriousness of the scratch is not yet known, Cleveland Police said at the time that the road would likely be closed for some time.

The force confirmed it had reopened around 3.30am.

Cleveland Police has been contacted for more details.
