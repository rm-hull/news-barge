Independent brunch brand **Federal** is set to open a new café in**York city centre this autumn**, marking its second expansion into Yorkshire following a highly successful launch in Leeds last year.

The upcoming **100-cover venue** will be located on historic**Coney Street** within a prominent Grade II listed building. Backed by a**£3 million national investment** from the Foresight Group, the Manchester-born brand plans to blend its signature relaxed Antipodean style with the building’s heritage features, retaining the property’s original internal brickwork. Alongside two storeys of indoor seating available for private hire events, the site will feature a large dedicated outdoor terrace.

> **Claudio Ribeiro, founder of Federal, said:** “York feels like a natural fit for Federal. It has a strong independent spirit, a huge student community and a thriving food and drink scene, alongside an incredible year-round visitor economy. We’re incredibly lucky to have found a fantastic location.”

## Antipodean Brunch Culture and Local High Street Impact

The arrival of a high-profile independent operator highlights a broader economic shift on regional high streets, where experiential hospitality brands are increasingly taking over large retail footprints to drive footfall. Founded in 2014, Federal has built a substantial digital footprint, ranking as the UK’s most popular independent café online and generating over 1.1 million views on TikTok.

The brand’s operational model is heavily inspired by the neighbourhood café cultures of Australia and New Zealand, focusing on high-turnover all-day brunch menus, sustainable local sourcing, and a dedicated specialty coffee programme. By targeting Coney Street—a zone traditionally dominated by retail giants—the rollout injects vital evening and weekend daytime activity into the night-time economy hub, appealing directly to York’s massive student population and year-round tourist influx [1].

## Menu Strategy and Launch Timeline

The York venue will mirror the brand’s established culinary framework, combining staple items like sourdough avo-toasts and French toast stacks with a rotating selection of hyper-seasonal food and drink specials. To maintain strict quality control across the group, the kitchen team prepares the vast majority of sauces, compotes, and baked items completely in-house daily, alongside beans sourced from premium independent coffee roasters.

Major internal fit-out and construction teams are scheduled to move onto the Coney Street site in **late September 2026**. The venue is expected to open its doors to the public before the end of the year, with local recruitment drives for hospitality staff launching over the coming weeks. Full menu listings and operational updates can be viewed on the official Federal Café Portal.
