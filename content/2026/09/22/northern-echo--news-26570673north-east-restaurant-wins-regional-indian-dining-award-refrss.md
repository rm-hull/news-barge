A North East restaurant is celebrating after being named Fine Dining Indian Restaurant of the Year at a major industry awards event.

Raval Indian Brasserie & Bar in Gateshead, which sits beside the Tyne Bridge overlooking the Newcastle skyline, received the accolade at the Indian Restaurant Summit & Honours 2026 Regional Edition.

The event was held at The Edwardian Manchester on Monday (September 21), bringing together restaurateurs, chefs, hospitality entrepreneurs and other figures from across the industry.

The regional event follows the Indian Restaurant Congress & Awards Global Edition, which was held in London earlier this year.

![]()Raval Indian Brasserie & Bar in Gateshead *(Image: RAVAL)*

Raval general manager Nas Islam said the award was a "proud moment" for the whole team and thanked diners across Newcastle, Gateshead and the wider North East for their support.

He said: "We are incredibly proud and genuinely humbled. This is a very special moment for the whole Raval family.

"This recognition belongs to our fantastic team. Behind every plate that leaves our kitchen and every guest we welcome through our doors are passionate people who care deeply about what they do.

"Their skill, commitment and teamwork make Raval what it is, and I couldn't be prouder of them."

He added: "Most importantly, we want to thank the wonderful people of Newcastle, Gateshead and across the North East.

"People choose to dine with us, celebrate important moments with us, bring their families and friends, recommend us and come back to see us. That loyalty means more than any award ever could."

Raval specialises in contemporary Indian dining, drawing inspiration from regional and coastal cuisines from across India.

Fire, charcoal and tandoor cooking form a central part of its menu, which combines British produce with Indian spices and traditional techniques.

The restaurant also offers a six-course tasting menu featuring dishes inspired by areas including Bombay, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Delhi, Goa and North India.

Nas said bringing the recognition back to the North East made the occasion particularly significant.

He said: "There are some fantastic restaurants and incredibly talented hospitality people across our region.
