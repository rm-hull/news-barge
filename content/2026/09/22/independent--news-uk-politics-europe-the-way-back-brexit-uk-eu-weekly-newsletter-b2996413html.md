Brexit may be over – but the debate about Britain's future in Europe is not.

A decade on from the Brexit referendum, Britain's relationship with Europe remains one of the most important questions facing the country.

That's why *The Independent* has launched*Europe: The Way Back* – a major editorial campaign exploring how the UK can rebuild its relationship with Europe and what comes next.

At the heart of the campaign is our **free weekly newsletter**, bringing you expert analysis, exclusive reporting and the latest developments shaping relations between Britain and Europe.

## What you'll receive

Delivered to your inbox every **Wednesday**,* Europe: The Way Back* is written by political editor David Maddox and political correspondent Millie Cooke.

Each edition includes:

* Exclusive reporting on the latest developments in UK-EU relations
* Analysis of the political decisions shaping Britain's future in Europe
* Insight from politicians, experts, business leaders and campaigners
* Essential updates on major developments across Europe
* Explanations of what the week's news means and why it matters
