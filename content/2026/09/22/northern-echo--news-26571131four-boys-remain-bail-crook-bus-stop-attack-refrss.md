Four boys including an 11-year-old remain on bail after an “appalling” group attack at a Crook bus stop.

Police were called at about 8.40pm on Saturday (September 19) following reports of a fight at a marketplace bus stop.

Video footage from the incident was also widely circulated on social media which appeared to show two teenage boys being attacked by a large group of youths.

Today (September 22), police confirmed the boys remain on bail after they were arrested on suspicion of affray and the investigation remains ongoing.

The boys – one aged 17, two aged 15, and one 11-year-old – were brought into police custody where they were questioned by officers.

Under their curfew, the boys must stay at home between 4.30pm and 7am Monday to Friday.

On weekends, they are only allowed out with a parent.

Inspector Andy Reeves, from Crook Neighbourhood Police Team, previously said: “The scenes we saw in Crook this weekend were absolutely appalling.

“Our enquiries have so far resulted in the arrest of four youths, and we have identified further suspects who will be brought in for questioning.

“Violence and disorder like this will not be tolerated in our community, and those involved in Saturday’s incident will be dealt with robustly.”
