A North East council plans to dispose of two open spaces, including land for a proposed sewer and an industrial site.

Stockton Council announced its intention to dispose of the plots in a public notice.

The first area, located to the north of Bothal Walk, Stockton, is earmarked for a proposed foul sewer.

The second plot, adjacent to 2 Yorkway, Thornaby, Stockton, is proposed to be sold on a freehold basis, restricted to B2 and B8 usage.

This plot measures approximately 137.6 square metres.

The council has made a plan of the affected areas available for inspection.

To view this, interested parties can send a request via email.

Residents and businesses have been invited to submit any objections or representations regarding the proposed disposals.

These must be made in writing to the Valuation and Property Manager at Stockton Council and sent to at [landandproperty@stockton.gov.uk.](mailto:landandproperty@stockton.gov.uk.)
