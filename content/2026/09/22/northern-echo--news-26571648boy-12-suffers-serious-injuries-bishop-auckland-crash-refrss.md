A 12-year-old remains in hospital with "serious injuries" after a smash in Bishop Auckland which saw two teenagers arrested.

Emergency services were called to a crash involving a motorbike and a car on Newgate Street at around 4.45pm on Sunday (September 20).

A 12-year-old boy, who was a pillion passenger on the motorbike, was taken to James Cook Hospital by air ambulance. He is being treated for serious injuries.

A spokesperson from Durham Police said: “Two 14-year-old boys were arrested and have since been released under investigation whilst enquiries continue.

“A 12-year-old boy, who was a pillion passenger on the motorbike, was taken to hospital by air ambulance with serious injuries where he remains.

“An investigation is ongoing.”

Newgate Street was closed for hours at the junction with Princess Street, with drivers asked to avoid the area. It has since reopened.

Paramedics from the North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) attended the incident.

A spokesperson said: "We received a call at 4.45pm on Sunday, September 20 to reports of a road traffic incident in Bishop Auckland.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews to the scene and requested support from our colleagues at the Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS).

"One patient was transported to James Cook University Hospital by air for further treatment."
