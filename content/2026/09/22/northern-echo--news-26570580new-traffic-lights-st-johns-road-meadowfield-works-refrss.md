New traffic lights have been installed at a County Durham junction as part of a £2.94million upgrade.

The St John’s Road junction in Meadowfield has been upgraded to improve safety and cut congestion.

The work includes lights for vehicles turning right on to the A690 and upgraded crossings for pedestrians, cyclists and wheelchair users.

Councillor Tim McGuinness, cabinet member for rural, farming and transport at Durham County Council, said: "We’re pleased to announce that these works are now complete, improving safety for residents crossing at the junction.

"They will also offer better connectivity and support more sustainable travel choices, helping communities and businesses feel more connected."

The scheme has been funded by the Department for Transport’s £2.94million Safer Roads Fund award for safety improvements, which supports safety improvements along the A690 corridor between Crook and Neville’s Cross.

The project was coordinated with the North East Mayoral Strategic Authority’s Bus Service Improvement Plan, known as BSIP.

The wider BSIP programme includes £2.7m for A690 corridor upgrades, with signal improvements planned at Nevilles Cross, Crossgate Peth and Sutton Street, plus new signals at North Road roundabout.

Cllr McGuinness thanked residents for their patience during the work.
