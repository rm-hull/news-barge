A Yorkshire baker who became the first-ever winner of The Great British Bake Off continues to inspire a new generation of talent.

Edd Kimber, from Bradford, rose to fame in 2010 after winning the inaugural series of the popular baking competition, which first aired on August 17 that year.

### 'I jumped at the chance to follow my passion'

His victory launched a successful career and made him one of the UK’s best-known baking personalities.

Mr Kimber said: "I grew up in a house where baking was a regular occurrence, my first memory was standing on a stool in the kitchen helping my mum to make mince pies.

"After studying politics at university I realised the corporate world wasn't for me, and after entering and winning the first series of The Great British Bake Off, I jumped at the chance of following my passion for baking."

![]()Paul Hollywood (second right) has been a judge since the inaugural series of Bake Off. *(Image: Mark Bourdillon/Channel 4/PA)*

### Edd Kimber's rise after winning Bake Off

Since his win, he has built an impressive career as a baker, food writer, and television personality.

He has published six cookbooks, starting with The Boy Who Bakes in 2011 and Say It With Cake in 2012.

His other titles include Patisserie Made Simple (2014), One Tin Bakes (2020), One Tin Bakes Easy (2021), and the Sunday Times bestseller Small Batch Bakes (2022).

Mr Kimber has also contributed recipes and features to leading food publications such as BBC Good Food, Olive, Delicious, The Washington Post, and Bake From Scratch.

His television appearances have further cemented his reputation in the baking world.

He has featured on shows including Sunday Brunch, Saturday Kitchen, Good Morning America, and served as resident baker on The Alan Titchmarsh Show.

In addition to his media work, he has built a large social media following, with more than half a million followers on Instagram.

### Great British Bake Off begins Tuesday September 22

The latest series of The Great British Bake Off begins Tuesday, September 22 (tonight), with West Yorkshire’s Tom among the new hopefuls entering the tent.

![]()New host Nigella Lawson with Paul Hollywood *(Image: Newsquest)*

Describing himself as a "northern diva", Tom developed his love of baking from an early age, often visiting his grandmother to bake while his older brother attended football matches.

According to the programme, he had mastered brandy snaps before the age of 10 and now enjoys creating bakes that are "witty, pretty and, most importantly, delicious".

He works in a busy salon and is known for spoiling his colleagues with homemade treats, with his salted caramel brownies a firm favourite.

As a new group of bakers take on the challenges of the Bake Off tent, Mr Kimber’s success story continues to inspire.
