A plan to divert a public bridleway and footpath in Great Aycliffe has officially come into force, according to a public notice.

Durham County Council confirmed the diversion of Public Bridleway No. 10 and Public Footpath No. 8 in Great Aycliffe Parish on April 17, with the order coming into force on August 19.

The changes were made under the Town and Country Planning and the Wildlife and Countryside Act.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

This means the bridleway and footpath have been shifted from their old paths to new routes as shown on the confirmed order map.

The council has also certified that all the requirements laid out in Article 2 of the order have been met.

This certification was essential for the order to come into force.

The map detailing the new paths is available and can be requested via email at [highways.orders@durham.gov.uk](mailto:highways.orders@durham.gov.uk) .
