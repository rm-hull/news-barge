# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after two e-bikes stolen from property in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after two electric bikes were stolen from a property in Harrogate.

The incident happened between 11pm on Wednesday 10 and 5am on Thursday 11 September when two e-bike were taken from a property on Forest Avenue in Starbeck.

Among the bikes taken were a grey Haibike All Trail 7 with white lettering and a grey and green Cube Reaction Pro with black lettering.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us.”
