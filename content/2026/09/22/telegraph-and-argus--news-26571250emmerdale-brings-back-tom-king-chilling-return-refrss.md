Emmerdale viewers are set for a "chilling shake-up" later this year as James Chase reprises his role as abusive villain Tom King.

The character was last seen in prison after a major domestic abuse storyline in 2024, which followed Belle King's ordeal at the hands of her controlling and manipulative husband.

Over several months, viewers watched as Belle, played by Eden Taylor-Draper, suffered coercive control, psychological abuse and stalking before Tom was ultimately convicted and jailed.

Now, two years on, Tom is due to return to screens after becoming eligible for parole.

## **Belle forced to confront past trauma**

ITV has revealed that Tom's return will threaten to reopen old wounds for Belle as she continues rebuilding her life.

The broadcaster said viewers previously watched "in horror" during the long-running storyline, which saw Tom isolate Belle, monitor her movements and even harm her beloved dog Piper as part of his campaign of control.

With Tom seeking his release from prison, Belle will once again find herself confronting the trauma he inflicted.

ITV teased: "Can the Dingles find a way to keep Belle's abuser behind bars and will Belle find the strength to keep free from Tom's psychological grip or will he succeed in manipulating those around her once again?"

## **James Chase on returning as Tom**

Actor James Chase said stepping back into the role comes with significant responsibility.

He said: "Stepping back into Tom's shoes is a responsibility I don't take lightly. He is a character driven by obsession and a desperate need for control, and it is both fascinating and challenging to step back into that mindset.

"The audience knows exactly who he is and what he is capable of, so the challenge now is to show the lengths he is willing to go to in order to get what he wants, even if it means dismantling Belle's life all over again.

"It's a dark path, and I'm ready to see how far Tom will push it."

## **Producer promises complex new chapter**

Emmerdale producer Laura Shaw said the storyline will focus on the long-term impact of abuse and the challenges survivors face even after their abusers have been convicted.

She said: "James's return isn't just about bringing back a familiar face, it's about reigniting a complex and challenging story.

"When Belle gets the news that Tom is up for parole, she's forced to confront a trauma she battled hard to overcome.

"With her family and new partner Kammy by her side, we explore the lasting impact of Tom's abusive actions on Belle as she fights to keep Tom away from the village."

## **Storyline sparked viewer reaction**

One of the most talked-about moments of the original storyline came when Tom poisoned Belle's dog, Piper, in a bid to manipulate her into seeing him after she entered a mental health facility.

The episode prompted hundreds of complaints to media regulator Ofcom, with 513 complaints lodged on one day and a further 363 the next.

Following the backlash, Emmerdale reassured viewers that no animals had been harmed during filming.

In a statement shared on social media, the programme said: "These scenes can be distressing to watch, but we want to remind you that Minnie (aka Piper) is a very good actor and is always living her best life on set.

"We can confirm that no harm has come to her whatsoever."

## **Award-winning storyline**

Despite generating controversy, the domestic abuse storyline was widely praised and helped Emmerdale secure the Best Serial Drama award at the 2024 National Television Awards.

Taylor-Draper's portrayal of Belle was also recognised with an award nomination.

Tom King's return is set to air later this year, with Emmerdale continuing to screen on weekday evenings at 8pm on ITV1 and STV.
