A man has been arrested after a head-on crash in Hartlepool left a man trapped inside a van with broken legs.

Emergency services were called to West View Road after a crash between a white Volvo xc60 and a black Peugeot Expert just before 10pm on Monday (September 21).

The driver of the Volvo fled the scene of the incident, which took place at the junction with Cleveland Road, before later being found by police.

The 66-year-old man driving the Peugeot van was trapped inside of the vehicle and had to be freed by firefighters from Cleveland Fire Brigade.

He was then taken to the James Cook University Hospital in Middlesbrough with breaks to both legs and an injury to his lung.

The 31-year-old driver of the Volvo was taken to hospital in Stockton with lacerations to both knees.

A 31-year-old man has since been arrested on suspicion of aggravated vehicle taking, causing serious injury by dangerous driving, failing to provide a sample, driving without a licence and no insurance.

Cleveland Police shut the road between Old Cemetery Road and Ropery Road for five hours following the incident overnight before reopening the road around 3.30am.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We were called to reports of a road traffic collision at the junction of Cleveland Road and West View Road in Hartlepool at 9.49pm on September 21.

"We dispatched a clinical team leader and one ambulance crew, who transported one patient to James Cook Hospital for further treatment."
