Police have released an image of a man they would like to speak to following the theft of cooking oil from a Harrogate restaurant.

The theft happened at Lilypad on Kings Road on 31st July.

North Yorkshire Police are asking anyone who recognises the man or has information that could help their enquiries to come forward.

Anyone who can help should email [joe.simms@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joe.simms@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101 and ask for PC 1113 Simms.

*Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*

*Please quote reference 12260146895 when providing information.*
