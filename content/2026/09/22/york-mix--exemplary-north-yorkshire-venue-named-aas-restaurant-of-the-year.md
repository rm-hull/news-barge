The AA has named its Restaurant of the Year 2026 – and it’s a place of “exemplary hospitality” right here in North Yorkshire.

Simon Numphud, managing director of AA Hotel & Hospitality Services, said: “Every year, the AA Hospitality Awards shines a light on hotels, restaurants, B&Bs, inns and chefs going above and beyond to deliver memorable experiences for guests and diners.

“This year’s winners showcase the breadth and quality of hospitality across the UK, giving consumers a host of trusted recommendations and inspiration for their next meal or stay.”

It’s awards were dished up at a ceremony last night.

And taking the **AA Restaurant of the Year** for**England** is…**The Star Inn, Harome**.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/star-inn-harome-reopens-1-tw.webp)

Chef owner Andrew Pern’s reaction was delight: “Nailed it! Well done team, thank you for all your hard work.”

It marks another high point for the much-loved venue, four years after it reopened after being devastated by fire in November 2021.

The AA describes The Star Inn like this: “A lovely thatched-roof country pub in a pretty moorland village, the 14th-century Star Inn at Harome is Yorkshire through and through.

“For the past 30 years, it has deservedly held a hard-earned position within the British dining scene for its exemplary hospitality and consistency.

“Everything here feels thoroughly considered and demonstrates proper old-school dining in the best sense, whilst retaining the rustic DNA of the original pub.”

It wasn’t the only North Yorkshire venue to win at the awards.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/black-swan-oldstead-bar-aa-awards-2026.webp)

**The Black Swan at Oldstead** was named**AA Sustainable Restaurant of the Year**.

The restaurant, part of the group run by chef Tommy Banks, is the first recipient of the honour.

The AA says: “The Black Swan at Oldstead is the first AA Sustainable Restaurant of the Year, recognising its commitment to environmentally responsible hospitality and a farm-to-table philosophy.

“Over the years, this has become one of the UK’s leading examples of sustainability and fine dining existing side by side.”

All of 2026’s restaurant award winners and new Rosette holders feature in the *AA Restaurant Guide 2027*.
