Cooking oil was stolen from a North Yorkshire restaurant, prompting a police appeal.

North Yorkshire Police want to identify this man following the theft of cooking oil from Lilypad at 134 Kings Road, Harrogate, on July 31, 2026.

**Recommended reads:**

Anyone with information is being urged to call 101 and ask for PC 1113 Simms.  
  
If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111. Please quote reference 12260146895 when passing on information.
