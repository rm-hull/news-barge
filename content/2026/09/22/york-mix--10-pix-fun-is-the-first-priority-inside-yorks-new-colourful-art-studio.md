A new art studio has opened in York – where you can make your own cute and colourful creations.

MeowCraft Cafe on the Shambles is a well-established and much-loved venue among locals and tourists alike, and now they’ve taken on a new venture: art workshops!

Running concurrently alongside their Shambles cafe, MeowCraft Studio is located just a few metres up the street at King’s Court. The studio has been open for just over a month now, and the reaction has been very positive.

The cafe is known for its coffee, bubble tea and refreshing drinks menu.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/meowcraft-art-studio-11.webp)

MeowCraft Studio offers ‘fluid figure’ workshops, which involves choosing, mixing, and pouring paint over a figurine to create a one-of-a-kind design.

“All the feedback we’ve gotten so far has been 100% positive,” said MeowCraft founder and owner Phoebe Lai.

“People really enjoy this experience as it’s not really a serious class,” she said. “We always tell the customer to enjoy the process and that fun is the first priority.”

The workshops also feature a complete demonstration, so participants can learn the correct technique.

“It’s quite an easy craft to pick up,” said team member Amber Thomas. “Obviously, it’s hard to perfect because there’s all these techniques you can learn, but it’s easy just to make the paint pour on and still make something good.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/meowcraft-art-studio-5.webp)

Phoebe was inspired to start the art workshops as she found “there’s not a lot of family activities in the city centre. I’d seen the fluid art figures on social media and I thought I could give it a try and bring this experience to York.”

The workshops aren’t just limited to families though.

“We cover everything,” said Phoebe. “If the customer wanted to come by themselves they’d be welcome, and we also do party groups as well – it’s a minimum from one and the maximum we can fit is 12.”

Since they’re a small, independent business, they’re hoping more people will find out about the art studio.

“I want to try and let people know that we are here and we are open, because we are not on the street, so we need to be advertised a bit more,” explained Phoebe.

“Also since we’re a small business, we’re quite easy to get in touch with,” added Amber. “They can just come to the shop and talk to me or try to find Phoebe directly through the Instagram or website and just ask any questions about the shop or workshops that they have.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/meowcraft-art-studio-8.webp)

There’s also the opportunity to have a go at home, as the shop on the Shambles sell DIY kits.

“When I sell a DIY kit, I tell them how to do it, give them instructions and email it to them as well,” said Amber.

There are a range of experiences available at MeowCraft Studio, where you can create small figures (approx 6-9cm) to larger figures at approx 30-35cm. Classes start from £28.

To find out more and to book your experience, visit the MeowCraft website.
