A HORSE has become stuck in a ditch close to an East Yorkshire town.




Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to Watton, near Beverley, at 10.08am today (Tuesday, September 22).

The service said a vet was called in for guidance to help the release effort from the ditch that was filled with water.

A service spokesperson said animal rescue equipment was used alongside a farmer teleporter, strops and a slide sheet.

Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said the incident was left with the horse owner and the vet.
