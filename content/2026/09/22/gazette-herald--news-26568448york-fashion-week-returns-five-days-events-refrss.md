York Fashion Week returns for five days of runway shows, exhibitions, markets and creative events celebrating talent from across the region and beyond.

The event runs from Tuesday September 22 to 26, marking the second instalment of the 2026 programme after a successful first edition earlier this year.

Nicky Hayer, creative director of York Fashion Week, said: "We’re incredibly excited to be bringing York Fashion Week back for Part Two of 2026.

![]()The week closes with the Student & Graduate Runway at York Art Gallery *(Image: Supplied)*

"York has such an incredible creative community, and our aim is to continue creating opportunities for designers, students, graduates, retailers and creatives to showcase what they do and connect with new audiences.

"We’ve grown a lot in the last year due to the support from our network of businesses, models and artists, and we’re very proud to be putting fashion firmly on the cultural calendar in York."

The schedule includes ticketed and free runway shows, exhibitions, shopping experiences, creative events and installations.

![]()York Fashion Week returns to the city with five days of fashion, creativity and culture *(Image: Supplied)*

Highlights include Tuesday’s Twilight Runway with WYSE London, the two-night Night Market at Another Brother Cafe on Wednesday and Thursday, and The Yorkshire Collection by Tendai Murairwa on Friday.

The week closes with the Student & Graduate Runway at York Art Gallery on Saturday, showcasing emerging talent from fashion schools and universities.

Other events include Layer Your Story: An Autumn Jewellery Edit with Palenque Jewellery, a week-long Mannequin installation and the invitation-only Dolce Vita by DOLLSFACE event.

![]()Emerging designers and independent brands take centre stage at York Fashion Week *(Image: Supplied)*

Since 2018, York Fashion Week has hosted more than 350 events, supporting emerging designers and creatives and giving fashion businesses and talent from across the UK a platform.

The event is backed by JCT600 Mercedes-Benz of York, Camel Event Production, Mayor David Skaith and the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority.

Organisers are urging residents, visitors and fashion fans to book tickets and attend events across the city.

![]()York Fashion Week returns to the city with five days of fashion, creativity and culture *(Image: Supplied)*

York Fashion Week brings together established designers, independent brands, students and emerging talent while supporting the next generation of creatives.

Tickets start at £5, and several events are free.

See the full programme and book at www.yorkfashionweek.co.
