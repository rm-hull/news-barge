The countdown is on until the return of the Altra Yorkshire Marathon Festival – with thousands of participants preparing to take to the start line in York.

A highlight of York’s sporting calendar, it brings together first-time marathoners, experienced runners, wheelchair participants and charity fundraisers for a day of achievement, determination and celebration.

With race day taking place on **Sunday 18 October**, runners are entering the final stages of their training and counting down the days until they reach the start line.

Known for its flat, fast and scenic route, the Altra Yorkshire Marathon attracts participants of all abilities, from those aiming for a personal best to others taking on the 26.2-mile challenge for the very first time.

The event is delivered by Jane Tomlinson’s Run For All in partnership with title sponsor Altra.

Starting and finishing at the University of York, the route showcases some of the city’s most iconic sights, including York Minster, before heading out through picturesque surrounding countryside and back into the heart of the city.

Thousands of spectators are expected to line the route alongside volunteers, schools, local groups and performers who come together to support and celebrate every runner – and **YorkMix** will be there with our own cheer station to give some rousing encouragement!

As with all Run For All events, fundraising remains at the heart of the marathon.

Participants are encouraged to run for a charity close to their heart or support one of the event’s partner charities, which include York Foodbank, Yorkshire Cancer Research, the Jane Tomlinson Appeal, Dementia Forward, The Island, St Leonard’s Hospice, Macmillan Cancer Support, York Mind and Children with Cancer UK.

Tristan Batley-Kyle, operations director at Run For All, said: “We’re incredibly excited to be bringing the Altra Yorkshire Marathon Festival back to York next month.

“With just four weeks to go, the countdown is well and truly on for the thousands of runners who have been training hard for race day.

“Whether participants are aiming for a personal best, completing their first marathon or fundraising for a cause close to their heart, they should be incredibly proud of the commitment they’ve shown to reach this stage.

“The Altra Yorkshire Marathon is known for its fantastic atmosphere, supportive crowds and stunning route, and we’re looking forward to welcoming runners, volunteers and spectators for what promises to be another memorable day in York.”
