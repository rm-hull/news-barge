A man has appeared in court charged with attempted murder after shots were fired during a car-ramming in Consett.

David Railton, of Brackenfield Road in Framwellgate Moor, appeared at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court on Monday (September 21).

The 37-year-old is accused of attempted murder and possession of an imitation firearm with intent to cause fear or violence.

It follows an incident on Front Street in Consett on September 13 where an Audi was rammed by a Mitsubishi at 1.05am.

One of the occupants of the Audi then reportedly fired shots towards someone in the Mitsubishi before making off.

Nobody was injured.

No pleas were entered on Monday and Railton was remanded in custody until his next appearance at Durham Crown Court, for a plea and trial preparation hearing, on October 19.
