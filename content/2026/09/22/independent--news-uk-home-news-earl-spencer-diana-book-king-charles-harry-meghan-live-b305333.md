Prince Harry is in talks to make a documentary about his mother Princess Diana, according to a report, as the fallout over Earl Spencer’s bombshell memoir continues.

The Duke of Sussex wants to make a documentary to mark the 30th anniversary of her death and is “considering the most meaningful way to honour her legacy during this important anniversary year”, a source close to Prince Harry told *The Telegraph*.

The documentary would be produced by Archewell Productions, the Sussexes’ production company, and would likely be bought by Netflix due to a ‘first-look’ deal between the companies, the outlet reported.

“The Duke has discussed a number of possible projects to mark the 30th anniversary of his mother Diana, Princess of Wales’s passing,” the source said, adding that “nothing has been finalised or confirmed”.

It comes after Earl Spencer has claimed he has “really nothing to do” with King Charles anymore ahead of the release of his memoir *Swan Song* which hits shelves on Tuesday.

Spencer claimed Charles said “we’ll forget her soon enough” after Diana died, and “sounded giddily elated – like a lottery winner” after her death in 1997.

Buckingham Palace responded by suggesting that “fraternal grief can cloud reason”.

## Harry ‘completely fine’ with Diana memoir, Earl Spencer says

The Duke of Sussex is “completely fine” with a new memoir chronicling the life of his mother, Diana, Princess of Wales, his uncle Earl Spencer has said.

The earl has made a series of claims about the King in Swan Song: Diana, My Sister – including that Charles said “we’ll forget her soon enough” after she died – which has prompted a war of words between him and Buckingham Palace.

A palace spokesman said last week that the King is “mindful that the pain of fraternal grief can cloud reason, affect judgment and colour memory in ways others do not recognise”.

Asked about telling Harry and the Prince of Wales about the book, the earl told People magazine: “I can say that Harry is completely fine about it.

“He said, I hope there’s nothing embarrassing about him in it. I said, ‘There isn’t’.

“I think they were actually worried where I was going up to with the book.

“It pretty much ends in 1997 with Diana’s death, and there’s a tiny postscript by the grave.

“So I think there was this, ‘Gosh, what’s he written about?’ And I think it’s natural that people so in the public eye are concerned how they will come across in the book.

“But I could reassure both of them that they’re of course in the book, but really as examples of two little boys who had the most exceptional mother, I’m not in any way compromising their privacy at all.”

## What is Earl Spencer's relationship with William and Harry?

Earl Spencer has remained outwardly close with Prince Harry over the years amid the Duke of Sussex’s strained relations with the Windsors, while his relationship with his nephew Prince William remains a lot more private.

The earl said in an interview with the BBC that he did not take sides in the row between the Prince of Wales and the Duke of Sussex.

He told the BBC: “I’m on my deceased sister’s sons’ side, both of them.

“And if either of them wanted anything then I’d be equally there for them.

“And I never get involved in one camp or the other, except as a benign figure I hope.”

Earl Spencer said there are a “lot of reasons on both sides” why the brothers’ relationship had “got to this stage”.

He added: “I hope one day it’s resolved, but I don’t know all – I certainly don’t know what’s been done in the finer detail and so I would feel intrusive if I got more involved than to say of course a mother would want her two sons to be happy together and love each other.”

## The latest: What we know ahead of the release of Earl Spencer's book

Earl Spencer’s bombshell memoir is set to be released on Tuesday after a week of allegations being released through serialisation in *The Mail.*

So far, the Earl has made claims about King Charles’ behaviour around his ex-wife’s death before he was king.

These include:

* The then-prince said “we’ll forget her soon enough” after Diana died in a “stream of anger” after the Earl protested to the idea that William and Harry should walk in the procession.
* Charles “sounded giddily elated – like a lottery winner” in a phone call to the Earl following Diana’s car crash.
* The Earl also told of his memories of the funeral, and his concern for Harry, after he observed a “lack of obvious care” for the then 12-year-old prince.

Earl Spencer has also spoken to the BBC where he doubled down on claims made in the memoir.

He also opened up about the Martin Bashir scandal and Diana’s relationship with the disgraced former owner of Harrods, Mohammed Al Fayed, father of her partner Dodi.

## Prince Harry fights back tears as he hails 'mums and dads' caring for seriously ill youngsters during WellChild awards

The Duke of Sussex fought back the tears as he hailed “mums and dads” caring for seriously ill youngsters during the WellChild awards.

Harry welled up when he publicly acknowledged their efforts during a speech at the end of the ceremony that recognised and rewarded youngsters living life to the full despite complex medical needs.

In a speech to recipients, their families and some of the WellChild nurses, Harry became emotional after saying: “To all the mums and dads – most of us will never truly know the emotional ups and downs of your day.”

He had broken down during his 2019 WellChild speech when host Gaby Roslin was on hand to support him and six years later she came to the duke’s side after he said “It’s happening again” then joked as she held his arm “that doesn’t help, that makes it worse”.

The duke finished his tribute to parents: “But I sincerely hope that you take strength from nights like this.

“And while we may not know the detail of your day, in this room and beyond, we recognise the commitment of your care and all that comes with it.”
