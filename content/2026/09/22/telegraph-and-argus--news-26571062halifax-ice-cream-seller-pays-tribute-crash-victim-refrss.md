A Halifax ice cream seller has paid tribute to a woman who died following a three-vehicle crash near Queensbury, describing her family as "friendly" and "lovely".

The woman, locally named Zulekha, who was in her 40s, died after the collision on the A647 Queensbury Road on Sunday morning.

Among those remembering her is Imran Shabir, known locally through his ice cream business, who said he had known the family for several years through regular home deliveries.

## **'A lot of love and respect'**

Mr Shabir said he would often see the woman's husband and daughter when making deliveries and described the family as well-known and respected in their local community.

Speaking to the Telegraph & Argus, he said the family had been customers for around four or five years and that he would regularly encounter the husband in local shops.

According to Mr Shabir, the family lived in the King Cross and Hyde Park area of Halifax, not far from where he resides.

Speaking about the family, Mr Shabir described them as "friendly" and "lovely", saying there was "a lot of love and respect" within the household.

While he said he had only seen the mother a handful of times over the years, he recalled that it was usually Tarik and his daughter, who would come out to collect their dessert orders.

He said news of the crash had come as a shock to the local community, adding that Halifax is a close-knit place where "everyone knows each other".

Mr Shabir said news of the collision had spread rapidly across Halifax, where many residents either knew the family personally or were aware of them through community connections.

## **'It's shocking'**

Reflecting on hearing of the Zulekha's death, Mr Shabir said it had been difficult to come to terms with.

He said it was particularly upsetting to learn that "an innocent life" had been lost and expressed sympathy for the family as they deal with the aftermath of the tragedy.

Mr Shabir said the incident had prompted renewed concern about dangerous and drug-impaired driving, adding that such behaviour "needs to stop".

## **A community in mourning**

Tributes have continued to appear online since news of the woman's death emerged, with many people offering condolences to her loved ones and praising the emergency services who attended the scene.

One social media user described the collision as a "terrible accident", while another said their "deepest sympathies" were with all those involved and their family and friends.

Police have confirmed that the woman died after a collision involving a Honda Civic, Seat Leon and VW Golf on Queensbury Road shortly before 10.45am on Sunday.

The driver of the VW Golf was arrested on suspicion of drug driving and causing death by dangerous driving, while the driver of the Seat Leon was arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

Both have been released on bail pending further enquiries.

Anyone with information is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police quoting reference 0518 of September 20.
