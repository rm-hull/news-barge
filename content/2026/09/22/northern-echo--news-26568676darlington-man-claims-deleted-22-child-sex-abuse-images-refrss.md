A Darlington paedophile downloaded sick images of children as young as four being abused.

William Dixon appeared before Peterlee magistrates accused of three counts of making indecent images of children, one for each severity category.

The charges involve 22 images, including 13 in the most serious category, made up of stills and videos.

All were downloaded between September 2023 and February last year, prosecutor Michael Empleton told the court.

The 38-year-old defendant of Brougham Street, pleaded guilty to all three counts.

Mr Embleton said, despite the wording, the charges concerned possession, not the actual creation of the images.

He told the court the children shown were aged between 4 and 14.

Mr Embleton said the images were not accessible because they were found concealed in a different folder.

The defendant told the court: “There was nothing there. It was all deleted.”

Ben Pegman, in mitigation, said Dixon was of previous good character and had pleaded guilty at the first opportunity.

He said the case was “on the cusp” of whether magistrates should sentence Dixon or send him to the crown court.

The magistrates decided to send Dixon to the crown court for sentencing.

Bench chair Sian Allan-Gilbert said: “We’re declining jurisdiction due to the number of images, the ages of the children and the fact they (the images) were concealed.”

To speed up sentencing, magistrates asked the Probation Service to prepare a background report on Dixon before his crown court hearing.

Dixon was bailed on condition that he has no unsupervised contact with children and must appear for sentencing at Teesside Crown Court on Monday, October 19.

He was also ordered to register with police as a sex offender until then.
