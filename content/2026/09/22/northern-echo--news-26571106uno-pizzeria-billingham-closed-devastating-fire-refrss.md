A pizza shop has been gutted by flames after a blaze broke out overnight - leaving bosses “heartbroken”.

Firefighters were called Mill Lane shops in Billingham after a fire broke out in the garage beside Uno Pizzeria at around 10pm on Monday (September 21).

Shocking pictures show the blackened aftermath of the pizza shop blaze, with rubble visible beneath smashed windows and charred brickwork.

Uno Pizzeria said it has been heartbroken by the ordeal and will be closed until further notice - but will do “everything we can to come back stronger”.

Fire crews worked through the night tackling the smoke, leaving the scene just after 8am. Nobody is believed to have been injured and the cause of the fire is thought to be accidental

![]()The aftermath of the fire on Mill Lane in Billingham *(Image: MARK GRAVES)*

A statement from the owners said: “We are absolutely heartbroken by the recent incident that involved a devastating fire spreading from the next door garage into our shop.

"Unfortunately, the damage caused means we will be closed until further notice.

“Thankfully everyone is safe and we thank Cleveland Fire Brigade for spending seven hours controlling and putting out the blaze, keeping everyone safe.

“We urge everyone to please be careful when passing until the area is clear.

“Uno Pizzeria means so much to us, and most importantly, it means so much to all of you who have supported us, visited us, and made us part of your community.

“Seeing our shop affected like this has been incredibly difficult for all of us. We are so grateful for all your kind words and messages.

“Thank you for supporting us during this difficult time. We promise to do everything we can to come back stronger and will keep you all up to date with any news.”

Cleveland Fire Brigade sent five fire engines to the scene, saying the fire started in the garage, understood to be used by the laundrette, before spreading.

A spokesperson said: “Cleveland Fire Brigade was called at midnight last night to a fire on Mill Lane, Billingham. Five appliances were at the scene at the height of the incident.
