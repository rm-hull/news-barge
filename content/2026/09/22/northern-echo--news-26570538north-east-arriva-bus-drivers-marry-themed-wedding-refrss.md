A couple who met while driving buses for Arriva in the North East are celebrating after getting married earlier this month, with several bus-related items used to make the day extra special.

Sarah and Mark Devine, from Blyth, tied the knot with a white wedding ribbon tied to an Arriva bus, while also featuring little touches, like miniature buses as table decorations.

The pair both work at Arriva's Blyth depot, where their relationship began after Sarah joined the driving team in February 2023.

![]()Sarah and Mark Devine on their wedding day *(Image: ARRIVA)*

Mark was already working for the company at the time, and Sarah said the pair initially got to know each other through occasional conversations at work.

She said: "At first, we were simply colleagues, sharing the occasional conversation, but those little chats gradually became more frequent as we got to know each other.

"Before long, we had fallen for each other."

The couple officially got together in March 2024, although Sarah said their relationship did not begin with a conventional first date.

She added: "We never had a traditional first date, instead, we had a takeaway and a movie night at mine, and from that moment on, we were pretty much inseparable."

With Arriva having played such an important role in bringing the couple together, they decided to make their workplace a key part of their wedding celebrations.

As well as an Arriva bus decorated for the occasion, the wedding featured Arriva-themed bridesmaids, miniature buses and an Arriva-inspired wedding cake.

![]()The wedding was full of Arriva-related items *(Image: ARRIVA)*

Sarah said: "Arriva has played such a huge part in both our lives. We absolutely love our jobs at Arriva Blyth, and the people we work with have become such an important part of our journey together.

"So, when it came to our wedding day, there was never any question that Arriva would be part of it too."

She said the theme helped make the day particularly personal for the newlyweds.

Sarah added: "Our wedding simply wouldn't have been the same without the Arriva theme.

"It was a celebration not only of our love for each other, but also of the workplace that brought us together in the first place."

Arriva also congratulated Mark and Sarah, wishing the couple "many happy years together".
