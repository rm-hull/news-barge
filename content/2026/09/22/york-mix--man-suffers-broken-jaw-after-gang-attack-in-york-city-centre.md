A man was seriously hurt after being set upon by a gang in York city centre.

It happened at around **2.45am** on**Sunday 23 August** on**Parliament Street**.

Two men were attacked by a group of four people.

As a result, one victim suffered a broken jaw requiring extensive medical treatment.

The other sustained slight injuries including bruising to his face.

Two boys, both 17, have been arrested in connection with the incident.

However, officers want to trace the two people shown in the images as they believe they will have information that could assist the investigation.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “If you recognise them or have any information that can help to identify them, please get in touch.

“Officers are also appealing to a girl or woman who recorded the incident to get in touch.”
