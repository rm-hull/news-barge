Homebuyers across Yorkshire can claim up to £27,250 in cash incentives from a developer’s autumn campaign.

Bellway is offering the deal at developments in Beverley, Boroughbridge, Brigg, Brough, Harworth, Hoyland, Kirk Ella, Skellow, Skipton and Snaith.

Melanie Smith, Bellway Yorkshire’s sales director, said: “Bellway has been building high-quality homes for 80 years and we take feedback from our customers very seriously.

"And when they consistently told us that financial incentives make the cost of moving more manageable, we acted, which is why we’re giving potential customers a helping hand to buy an energy efficient, low-carbon house that really will feel like home from day one.”

Buyers can put the cash towards a deposit, mortgage payments or upgrades such as flooring and appliances.

Simon Smith said his family’s winter energy bills fell from around £600 to £200 a month after moving into a three-bedroom Bellway home with low-carbon technology.

New homes produce 33 per cent fewer carbon emissions than older properties, according to Home Builders Federation research.
