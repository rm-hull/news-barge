'Further defects' have been discovered at Durham Bus Station, delaying repairs to the £10.4 million transport hub.

Durham County Council said additional problems have been uncovered in work carried out by the building's original contractor while crews continue a major programme to replace defective cladding.

Now clad in white coverings, the bus station is expected to see a "short delay" to the completion of its repair works - they had expected to take around seven months in May.

Kevin Lough, Durham County Council's interim head of corporate buildings, said: "The white coverings currently in place at Durham Bus Station are there for safety reasons as part of the planned programme of repairs.

"While work to repair the building is progressing well, we have uncovered some further defects in work carried out by the original contractor.

"These defects are still being assessed, but it is likely they will result in a short delay to the completion of the project.

"We would like to thank the public for their patience and understanding and apologise for any inconvenience caused."

The latest setback comes after contractors began removing and replacing defective cladding in May as part of repairs to the city centre bus station.

At the time, Durham County Council said the work was expected to take around seven months to complete.

Scaffolding and fencing remain in place around the building, including at the station's main North Road entrance, while sections of surrounding pavement continue to be blocked off.

The bus station opened in January 2024 following a £10.4m redevelopment project, although the scheme itself suffered a number of delays before welcoming passengers.

The facility had originally been due to open in autumn 2023, but the opening date was pushed back because of issues encountered during construction.

When the redevelopment was completed, council leaders said the station would become a major transport hub for County Durham and help attract more shoppers and visitors into the city centre.

The discovery of further defects means work is now set to continue beyond the timetable originally announced when repair works began earlier this year.
