A North Yorkshire road has been blocked in both directions due to emergency repairs.

The A1041 Bawtry Road is shut due to emergency burst water repairs, with traffic congestion between Chester Court Road and Thief Lane.

**Recommended reads:**

Motorists are also being delayed by temporary traffic signals due to emergency repairs and construction on Canal Road in both directions from Vivars Way to A1041 Bawtry Road.
