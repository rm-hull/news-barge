POLICE have made four arrests and are appealing for witnesses after an affray incident in Halifax yesterday afternoon.

Officers were called to Maltings Road in Fountain Head Village at 2.03pm on Monday, September 21 to a report that a group of adults had been seen coming out of woodland there with injuries.

Fountain Head Village in Halifax is about two miles north-west of Halifax town centre, not far from Ovenden Wood Road.

## Bladed weapons

West Yorkshire Police located four people with non-life threatening injuries believed to have been caused by bladed weapons - and they are now appealing for any witnesses to come forward.

Three men aged 30, 53 and 56 respectively, and a 26-year-old woman were arrested.

They added that three injured men required hospital treatment.

Enquires remain ongoing into the circumstances of the affray.

In the meantime, Halifax Neighbourhood Policing Team officers have been conducting reassurance patrols in the area.

## Did you see anything?

Detectives at Calderdale CID would like to speak to anyone who has footage or information about the incident in which a group of people were fighting near Maltings Road in Halifax on Monday afternoon.

The force explained that there had been a large police presence in the area to secure the scene for examination, but said this was expected to reduce throughout yesterday evening.

Images on social media appear to show multiple police vehicles and officers at the scene yesterday afternoon.

Enquiries remain ongoing.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 2.08pm yesterday to report an incident on Maltings Road in Halifax.

"An ambulance and a team leader were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital. "

Anybody with information about the incident is encouraged to contact Calderdale CID via 101 or online at ([https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat) quoting reference number 13260537619.
