Otley Common will celebrate its first anniversary with a free open day of tours, performances and community activities.

The event runs at Otley Common on Walkergate from 11am to 3pm on Saturday, October 17.

Residents can explore the developing community space, meet building users and speak to local organisations.

Visitors can enjoy tea, homemade cakes and stalls run by building users and community groups.

The Pop n Rock Choir will perform at 11am and 12.30pm.

Guided tours of the building will take place at 11.45am and 1.15pm.

The tours will show the ongoing renovations and give visitors a behind-the-scenes look at the work.

Activities include table tennis and jujitsu, with people of all ages invited to take part, while visitors can find out about upcoming events, volunteering and membership.

Otley Common development manager Andrew Howarth said: "We’re absolutely thrilled to celebrate our first year with the community."

Mr Howarth said: "This open day is a fantastic opportunity to showcase what we’ve achieved together and engage people in our exciting vision for a vibrant, inclusive community space that will serve everyone."

The group’s share offer is currently open. Visitors can find out how to invest, as more funds are needed to continue renovating the building.

The celebrations continue that evening with Common Varieties, compered by Leeds-born comedian and Ted Lasso actor Sapphire McIntosh.

Doors open at 7pm, with tickets available at otleycommon.org/events.

Everyone is welcome.

Otley Common took ownership of the former Otley Methodist Church and Hall on October 3, 2025, after raising more than £830,000 for the community project.
