TWO pedestrians have been injured in a serious crash close to a North Yorkshire train station.

The county's police force said it happened in Boroughbridge Road, Northallerton at around 7.30pm on Sunday, September 20.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said it involved a black Porsche, which the force said was believed to have collided with three other vehicles and two pedestrians who were treated in hospital for their injuries.




The road was closed while police investigated the scene and the vehicles involved were recovered.

Police are asking anyone who witnessed the collision, saw the vehicle before it happened, or has relevant dashcam footage and has not yet spoken to officers to come forward.

Information can be provided online in the first instance by emailing Ruby Rutter at [ruby.rutter@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ruby.rutter@northyorkshire.police.uk), or through North Yorkshire Police’s website.

Those unable to use these options can call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through its website.

Please quote reference 12260185025 when providing information.
