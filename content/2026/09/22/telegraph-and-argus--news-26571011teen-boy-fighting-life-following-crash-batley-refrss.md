A teenage boy has suffered life-threatening injuries following a collision between a car and an electric motorcycle.

The incident happened shortly before 6.05pm on September 21 at the junction of Ealand Road and Ruby Street in Batley.

Both vehicles were travelling away from Carlinghow Lane when the collision occurred.

The rider of the Lexmoto Cypher motorcycle sustained a serious head injury and was taken to hospital for treatment.

Police are now urging any witnesses to come forward.

## What police have said

In a full statement, a spokesperson for the police said: "Police are appealing for witnesses to a collision in Batley in which a teenage motorcycle rider suffered life-threatening injuries.

"The collision happened shortly before 6.05pm last night (21 September) at the junction of Ealand Road and Ruby Street.

"A Ford S-Max car and Lexmoto Cypher electric motorcycle were both traveling on Ealand Road away from Carlinghow Lane when the collision took place.

"The rider of the motorbike, a teenage boy, suffered a serious head injury and was taken to hospital for treatment.

"Anyone who may have seen the collision or the circumstances leading up to it, or who may have video footage, is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat) quoting reference 1326 of 20/9."
