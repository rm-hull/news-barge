A major manufacturer in North Yorkshire has invested £1.1 million in new technology to boost production.

Econ Engineering, the UK’s leading producer of winter maintenance and specialist highways vehicles, has installed a Bystronic ByStar 6225 laser cutting system at its Ripon headquarters.

The state-of-the-art system features a 12kW laser head and advanced mixed gas cutting technology, enabling the company to process a wider range of materials and thicknesses in-house.

Thomas Lupton, quality and compliance director at Econ Engineering, said: "At Econ, continued investment in our people, facilities and technology is central to delivering the highest quality products and service for our customers.

"This significant £1.1m investment into state-of-the-art laser cutting machinery further enhances our manufacturing capacity and flexibility, supports more complex fabrication requirements and strengthens our ability to respond quickly to evolving customer needs.

"The result is improved efficiency throughout the production process, helping us maintain the highest standards of quality and delivery.

"By embracing modern manufacturing technologies, we are ensuring our customers continue to benefit from innovative engineering, dependable quality and the responsive service that has become synonymous with our brand."

The investment comes as Econ launches Built in the UK | Built for the UK, a campaign exploring the people, skills, partnerships, innovation and resilience behind UK manufacturing, and the value they create for communities and the economy.

![]()The new machinery has been installed in the Ripon headquarters of Econ Engineering *(Image: © Victor De Jesus)*

Mr Lupton said: "For more than five decades, Econ has been designing, engineering and manufacturing winter maintenance and highways vehicles here in the UK, supporting local authorities and contractors responsible for keeping roads safe and communities connected.

"UK manufacturing does far more than build products.

![]()The new machinery is expected to transform productivity. *(Image: © Victor De Jesus)*

"It creates skilled jobs, strengthens communities, supports innovation and helps keep essential public services running.

"Investments such as this aren’t just an investment into our business; they’re an investment in people, skills, resilience and the future of our industry."

Econ supplies 85 per cent of the winter maintenance vehicles to local councils and private contractors from its North Yorkshire-based factory.

The company, in operation for more than 55 years, also has engineering hubs in Alloa, Sowerby, Halesworth, and Cardiff.
