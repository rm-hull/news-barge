One person was taken to hospital after a two-vehicle crash near Bishop Auckland today.

Emergency services were called a crash involving a HGV and a motorbike on East End, Wolsingham, at 2.54pm.

The crash closed the road between East End Bridge and Wolsingham Park while emergency crews worked at the scene.

Pictures show a wrecked motorbike lying on its side with debris strewn across the road.

![]() *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

![]() *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

The North East Ambulance Service sent two ambulance crews and a clinical team leader, then called in the Great North Air Ambulance Service.

A NEAS spokesperson said: “We received a call at 2.54pm on Tuesday to reports of a road traffic incident on East End in Wolsingham, Bishop Auckland.

“We dispatched two ambulance crews and a clinical team leader to the scene, and requested support from our colleagues at the Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS).

“One patient was taken to the RVI by road for further treatment.”

The patient's injuries have not been disclosed.

County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue crews also attended.

A spokesperson said: “At 2.52pm on Tuesday, we received a report of a two-vehicle collision on the A689 in Wolsingham, Durham.

“One crew from Stanhope Fire Station attended and extinguished a small vehicle fire before leaving at 4.09pm.
