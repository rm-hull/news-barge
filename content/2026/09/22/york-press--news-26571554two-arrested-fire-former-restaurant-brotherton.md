TWO boys have been arrested on suspicion of arson after a large building fire in North Yorkshire.

The incident happened at the empty former Thaal Indian Restaurant in Great North Road, Brotherton, near Selby, on Monday (September 21).

North Yorkshire Police say the fire is believed to have caused “considerable” damage to the property.




The boys, aged 12 and 15, were both arrested and currently remain in custody.

A police spokesperson said: “Officers have not ruled out further arrests.

“No reports of any injuries or missing people have been received, however, a full search of the property will be carried out once it is safe to do so.

“Officers have warned that deliberately setting fires is a serious criminal offence that puts lives at risk and can cause devastating damage to homes, businesses, vehicles and the environment.

“Even a small fire can quickly spread, placing firefighters, emergency workers and members of the public in danger.

“Anyone with information about last night’s fire should call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and pass information from incident number 12260185851.”
