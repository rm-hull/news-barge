THE dedication of hospital volunteers has been celebrated with long service awards.

At the Friends of Airedale AGM, certificates were presented to 25 volunteers, recognising more than 300 years of combined service to Airedale General Hospital, Steeton.

The awards highlight the contributions of 200-plus volunteers who support patients, visitors, and staff in roles such as running shops and cafés, providing trolley services, and guiding people around the hospital.

Alison Dixon, outgoing chair of Friends of Airedale, who was also honoured for 25 years of service, said: "Our annual long service awards are always a moment to reflect on the dedication and commitment of the 200-plus volunteers who work around the hospital in various roles.

"From running the hospital shops and cafes to guiding people around the building, providing a trolley service on the wards and many other unseen but essential jobs, they are a vital cog in the wheel.

"They enhance the hospital experience for thousands of patients and visitors each year and the time and effort put in by them all is incredible."

Ms Dixon will continue to support the charity as joint vice-chair alongside David Openshaw, while Jan Turner has been named the new chair.

Certificates recognising 310 years of service were awarded to 25 volunteers, including Vincent Curran, Pauline Starkey, and Dinah Whittingham for 25 years of service.

Pamela Rycroft, for 20 years; 15 years- Susan Duncan, Susan Ingham, Judith Nevin and Idris Wade; 10 years – Richard Bateson, Susan Bateson, Cynthia Bevan, Irene Green, Jennifer Martin, June Mosley, Josie Palmer, Anne Sedgwick, Sandra Walton and Linda Waterhouse; five years - Christine Bank, Gillian de ’Cort, Linda Hudson, Colin Surrey, Susan Surrey and Jan Turner.

Friends of Airedale has raised more than £5.5 million over the past decade to purchase equipment and improve patient care.
