The Princess Royal will join Sir Kevin Sinfield in York during the rugby legend’s latest fundraising challenge for the motor neurone disease (MND) community.

Anne will join Sir Kevin as he arrives at the University of York on Monday (September 28) on his final '7 in 7' challenge, in which he will try to complete an ultra-marathon every day for seven days.

Anne, the MND Association’s Royal Patron, will visit York as Sir Kevin runs through the city to Bishopthorpe Palace.

![]()The Princess Royal who will meet Kevin Sinfield in York next week during his latest MND challenge *(Image: Fabio De Paola/PA Media Assignments)*

Sir Kevin, 46, will start the challenge at Betfred Super League champions Hull KR and will visit the other 11 English top-flight rugby league grounds before concluding at Old Trafford in time for the Super League Grand Final on Saturday, October 3.

He will run to York from Shiptonthorpe, near Market Weighton, during the second day of the challenge.

Sir Kevin and his team will visit newly promoted York Knights at the LNER Community Stadium, then pass York Minster and York Racecourse before finishing at Bishopthorpe Palace.

![]()Kevin Sinfield sporting bin bags to cover his footwear as he runs through flood water in Cattal during his MND challenge last December *(Image: Danny Lawson/PA Wire)*

It will be Sir Kevin’s seventh endurance challenge, inspired by former Leeds Rhinos team-mate Rob Burrow, who died in 2024 after living with MND for four-and-a-half years.

Sir Kevin has already raised more than £11 million for MND causes.

In a letter to supporters ahead of the challenge, Sir Kevin said: “Every card, message, hug and handshake has fuelled us to do more and go even further in our fight against MND.

“To everyone who has turned out to support us, anyone who has donated, shared a message, told us their story, or given their time to volunteer – thank you.”

He said: “Seven years ago this December my friend and Leeds Rhinos teammate Rob Burrow CBE was diagnosed with motor neurone disease. In that moment, as anyone who has been affected by MND will tell you, life changed in an instant for him and his loved ones.

“While Rob and his incredible family; his adoring wife Lindsey, their three wonderful children and his parents Geoff, who we have since sadly lost, and Irene bravely came to terms with his diagnosis, his friends, teammates, colleagues and the wider Rugby League community were determined to make a difference.”

![]()Rob Burrow and Kevin Sinfield cross the finish line of the 2023 Rob Burrow Leeds Marathon *(Image: Danny Lawson/PA Wire)*

Mike Rogers, director of research innovation at the MND Association, said: “Sir Kevin Sinfield’s 7 in 7 challenges really demonstrate what extraordinary teamwork and determination can achieve particularly when it comes to fighting a disease like MND.

“The money Sir Kevin and the team has raised on behalf of the MND Association is now driving nine research projects and has helped us to establish our research nurse network, giving people with MND the opportunity to take part in clinical trials much more easily.

“This is real progress which will continue to have a lasting impact on the work we do. More importantly, it offers real hope for a world free from MND. Thank you, Sir Kevin – we couldn’t be more grateful for your support.”
