A FORMER Hollywood actor turned communication coach has won a top national award for his latest book.

York-based Dominic Colenso took home the title of Best Business Self Development Book 2026 at the Business Book Awards in London for Cut-Through: The pitch and presentation playbook.

The book, published by Practical Inspiration Publishing in December 2025, offers practical strategies for high-stakes communication.

Mr Colenso said: "Pitches and presentations are often the biggest moments in people’s careers.

"When the stakes are high, you can’t leave things to chance.

"I wanted to share a method that works, so people can succeed when they find themselves in the spotlight.

"I’m thrilled the judges recognised the book’s practical tools and techniques, and it’s a proud moment to bring this award home to Yorkshire."

Judges praised the book as a standout entry in the category.

The judges said: "The Judges felt that this book had ‘cut-through’ and stood head and shoulders above other personal skills development books.

"The ideas, models and tips are so evidently drawn from the author’s lived experiences and introduce practical solutions that anyone can adopt."

Mr Colenso, a former professional actor, is known for his role as Virgil Tracy in the Hollywood remake of Thunderbirds.

He has since transitioned to coaching, helping FTSE 100 leaders and global sales teams improve their communication skills.

Cut-Through is built around Mr Colenso’s six-step methodology—Discover, Distil, Design, Drill, Deliver, and Debrief.

The book includes actionable exercises, downloadable tools, and a diagnostic scorecard, all influenced by his background in acting and directing.

Mr Colenso also hosts a popular YouTube channel offering pitching and presenting tips to more than 2.5 million viewers.
