A highly-regarded North Yorkshire restaurant and takeaway has issued a 714 word 'use us or lose us' appeal on social media.

Manor Italia on Millgate, Thirsk, says the market town has lost a number of independent businesses in recent months, which 'really hits home just how difficult things are for small businesses right now.'

Independent businesses, the post continued, are difficult to replace and the restaurant, which opened in 2023, wanted 'to be open with you.'

The post on Facebook argued: 'If you want independent businesses like Manor Italia to remain part of Thirsk, we need you to use us. It's as simple as that.

'We can have thousands of followers on social media. We can receive lovely comments about our food. People can tell us how much they love Manor Italia and how much they want to see independent businesses succeed.

'And we appreciate every single one of those things. But ultimately, a small business can only survive if people use it.'

The post continued: 'Like every independent business, the cost of keeping Manor Italia going has increased enormously. Ingredients, energy, wages, stock, insurance and all the other costs that people don't necessarily see behind the scenes continue to rise.

'We know we're not alone in that, either. Everyone is feeling the pressure. Household bills have increased and people quite rightly have to make choices about where they spend their money.

'But there's an uncomfortable truth that we think needs saying: If we don't use the independent businesses we say we want in our town, eventually they won't be here.'

The restaurant said it didn't want people to say how much they missed Manor Italia after it had gone, as it wanted a future in Thirsk.

It wanted to keep making pizza, employing staff and supporting local suppliers.

'But we can't do that without customers. Every order genuinely counts,' it continued.

'We're not asking anyone to spend money they don't have, and we're certainly not asking people to support us out of sympathy.

'We're asking that, if you enjoy what we do and you want Manor Italia to have a future, please choose us when you can.

'Don't wait until another independent business disappears before realising how important it was to the town.

'This applies to us, but it applies to so many other small businesses around Thirsk too.

'A town full of thriving independent businesses doesn't happen because people say they support local.'

Manor Italia calls itself 'possibly the world's smallest pizzeria' on its website, adding 'Manor Italia is all about great food, carefully crafted with passion and attention to detail.'

Tripadvisor awards 5 stars out of five, based on 23 reviews, ranking it 23rd out of 50 Thirsk restaurants.

Google awards 4.8 stars out of five, based on 110 reviews.

However, the restaurant's plea has had a mixed response.

Whilst one said Manor Italia makes 'the best pizza in Thirsk', one criticised its use of AI in social media posts, another called for gluten-free options, and another branded the appeal 'desperate.'
