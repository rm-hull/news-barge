Readers of The Press are being offered a will at a special discounted rate through Ramsdens Solicitors. *(Image: Pexels)*

Many readers will already have a will in place, but for a large number it will be years since it was written.

Families grow, relationships change and finances evolve, yet the will often stays the same.

That can mean the document no longer reflects a person’s current wishes, or fails to take account of new partners, children, stepchildren or property arrangements.
