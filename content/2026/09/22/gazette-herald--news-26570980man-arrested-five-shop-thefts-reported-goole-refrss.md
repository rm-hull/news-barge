A man has been arrested in connection with several shop thefts in Goole.

Humberside Police said the 32-year-old was identified through Goole's Shop Watch system following reports of thefts between September 17 and 19.

The Shop Watch scheme sees retailers and business owners work directly with local policing teams to make the town centre a safer environment.

A spokesperson for Humberside Police said: "It is reported a man entered multiple stores in the areas of Wesley Square, Rutland Road, and Swinefleet Road in Goole and proceeded to take items before leaving without making payment.

"Following numerous enquiries, a 32-year-old man was identified via Shop Watch in Goole and arrested on Monday, September 21, 2026, on suspicion of five counts of shop theft."

He remains in police custody assisting with ongoing enquiries.
