A man has died after falling from a people carrier in a car park.

The 32-year-old was taken to hospital after reportedly falling from a silver Ford Torneo in the Station Car Park on Holmeside, Sunderland shortly after 6am on Sunday (September 20).

He died in hospital on Monday.

His next of kin have been informed and are being supported by police.

A 36-year-old woman was arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by dangerous driving. She has since been released under investigation.

Police are appealing for witnesses to help establish how the man died.

Sergeant Dave Roberts, of Northumbria Police's Serious Collision Investigation Unit, said: "This is a totally tragic incident which has resulted in a man losing his life.

"As always, the thoughts of everyone here at Northumbria Police are with his friends, family, and loved ones as they process their loss.

"Our enquiries are at an early stage, but as part of our investigation, we're looking to speak to anyone who may have information which could assist us in establishing how the man came to exit the vehicle.

"We're especially keen to hear from anyone who may have seen four people walking towards Station Car Park in Sunderland at around 6am, just before this incident took place.
