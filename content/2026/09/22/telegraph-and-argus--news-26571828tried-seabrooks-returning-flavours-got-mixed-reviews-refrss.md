Bradford crisp giant Seabrook's has introduced four flavours in the last week, including the return of two fan favourites, catering to a range of unique tastes.

Last week, the popular crisp brand announced the return of its Lamb & Mint flavour on social media.

Just days later, it revealed Sweetcorn would also be making a comeback, having last been available in 1997.

Both flavours are available exclusively in Home Bargains stores.

## Boots on the ground reporting

The T&A was quick on the scene following the announcements to try the returning flavours, with the newsroom soon smelling like the Seabrook's factory - based on Duncombe Street since 1980.

![]()Seabrook's returning flavours. *(Image: T&A)*

After sampling the Lamb & Mint flavour, one reporter said: "I wasn't sure about the combination, but they're nicer than I expected."

"Tastes like a roast dinner," another said.

One was less convinced by the Sweetcorn flavour.

"They're okay, but they don't really taste like sweetcorn," she said.

## Two new flavours to mark an anniversary

The remaining two new flavours have been launched to celebrate the 100th anniversary of Winnie the Pooh.

![]()The new flavours *(Image: Seabrook Crisps)*

Seabrook's has released a honey-flavoured lentil puff and a cheese-flavoured lentil puff, catering to both sweet and savoury tastes.

## Bradford roots

Founded in Bradford in 1939, Seabrook's traces its origins back to fish and chip shop owner Charles Brook, who began frying crisps for customers to enjoy while waiting in line for their Friday night fish and chips.

What started as a small local venture has since grown into one of the UK's best-known crisp brands.

The company has remained closely associated with Bradford throughout its history and continues to manufacture its products in the city.

Last year, Seabrook's teamed up with Morrisons to create three exclusive flavours, inspired by Bradford culture as part of celebrations linked to Bradford's year as UK City of Culture 2025.
