Plans to build more than 200 homes on the Burtree Garden Village site will be discussed by Darlington Borough Council this week.

The scheme for 230 homes at High Faverdale Farm, near Rotary Way, is due to go before the authority's planning applications committee on Wednesday (September 23).

The proposed development would form part of the wider Burtree Garden Village project in Faverdale, which is expected to deliver around 2,000 homes over the next 20 years.

Plans for the wider site also include a new primary school, community centre, pub, health facilities and employment land.

![]()Darlington Borough Council. *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

Committee members are being asked to approve the application, which has been submitted by Homes by Esh for land known as Burtree Cell CH.

According to a council report, the development would include 46 affordable homes, areas of open space, landscaping, drainage features and links to walking and cycling routes.

The site would also have electric vehicle charging points and solar panels on every home.

Planning officers say the scheme would make an important contribution towards housing growth in Darlington and fits with the long-term vision for Burtree Garden Village.

They have recommended approval, subject to legal agreements and conditions.

Two public comments were received, including concerns about nearby road infrastructure and noise from neighbouring industrial buildings.

The report notes that traffic generated by the development would be dealt with through improvements and contributions towards local roads and junctions, including the A1(M) Junction 58.

The developer has also agreed to contribute money towards extra primary school places and transport improvements.

Councillors will be asked to decide on the plans on Wednesday at 1.30pm.
