A MOTORIST who pulled over to drink from a can of beer before carrying on driving was over the limit when he was stopped by police.

Police pulled up Harold Inskip, 75, in Main Street, Kildwick, on August 25, heard Skipton Magistrates Court.

There was an open can of beer in the car and Inskip smelt of alcohol, the court heard on Friday.

He provided a positive roadside breath test and was arrested and taken to Harrogate Police Station where he was found to have 48 microgrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres of breath.

The legal limit is 35.

Inskip, who admitted drink driving, had been acting out of character because his partner had just been assaulted by being pushed down some stairs, it was claimed in mitigation.

Julian White, mitigating, said Inskip had taken his partner to her home from hospital before continuing to his own home in Keighley.

On his way, he had bought some cans of beer before pulling over into a lay-by in Kildwick to have a drink.

"He decided to go back and see her again when he was stopped by police," said Mr White.

Mr White said Inskip deeply regretted what had happened and that as his partner's unofficial carer needed his car to drive her around.

Inskip, of Portland Court, Keighley, was banned from driving for 12 months, fined £238 and ordered to pay a surcharge of £95 and costs of £85.

If he completes a drink-driver rehabilitation course, his ban will be reduced by three months.
