A man in his 30s was taken to hospital following a road traffic incident on a major road in Bradford last night.

Police are now appealing for witnesses after a man on a motorcycle was seriously injured in a fail to stop collision on Bolton Road just after 8pm on September 21.

The man, who was believed to have been on the bike, had been found at the roadside with serious head injuries. He was taken to hospital for treatment.

Bolton Road runs from the city centre through areas including Barkerend, Bolton and Undercliffe, towards Eccleshill.

The collision between the van and off-road motorbike occurred close to the junction with Leeds Road.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service (YAS) confirmed that multiple ambulance resources were sent to the scene.

Officers were then called by the ambulance services to the location.

## Appeal for footage

Roads Policing Officers would like to speak to anyone who saw or has footage of the crash

No vehicles were found at the scene upon police attendance, but it is believed a collision took place at the junction involving an off-road bike being ridden by two people and a light-coloured vehicle believed to be a people carrier or a van.

It is thought the bike was turning left onto Bolton Road when the collision occurred.

The other person on the bike is thought to have made off, with the bike.

Multiple enquires remain ongoing today (September 22) to locate vehicles involved and investigate the circumstances.

Anyone who has footage or information which can assist is asked to contact the Western Area Roads Policing Unit on101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat referencing log 1603 of 21 September.

Information can also be given anonymously to the independent Crimestoppers charity on 0800 555 111.

## The statement from YAS

A spokesperson for YAS said: "We received an emergency call at 8.08pm last night (21 September) to report a road traffic incident on Bolton Road in Bradford.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including an ambulance and a rapid response vehicle, and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
