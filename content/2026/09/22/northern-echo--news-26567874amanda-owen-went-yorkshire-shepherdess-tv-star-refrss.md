She is a bestselling author, TV star and one of Britain’s biggest rural media personalities.

But Amanda Owen’s journey to fame began with a life far from the spotlight, on a remote Yorkshire Dales hill farm.

Born in Huddersfield, Ms Owen did not grow up on a farm, but her childhood fascination with the countryside eventually led her to a life working with livestock.

That path would take her to Ravenseat, the isolated Swaledale farm that later became a household name.

![]() *(Image: CHANNEL4)*

She first attracted attention on social media by sharing candid stories and photographs of life as a working shepherdess.

Her stories of lambing, harsh winters and raising a family in the hills struck a chord, earning her the nickname “The Yorkshire Shepherdess”.

In 2015, she published her first book, The Yorkshire Shepherdess, followed by several others including A Year in the Life of the Yorkshire Shepherdess, Adventures of the Yorkshire Shepherdess, and Celebrating the Seasons.

Her writing success led to appearances on television, including The Dales and Ben Fogle's New Lives in the Wild.

Viewers were captivated by life at Ravenseat and the demands of raising nine children while running a hill farm.

![]() *(Image: CHANNEL4)*

This interest led to the creation of Our Yorkshire Farm, the Channel 5 documentary series that made Ms Owen a household name.

Millions tuned in to watch the family’s daily life, from sheep gathering to navigating the unpredictable Yorkshire weather.

The show’s success cemented her status as one of Britain’s biggest rural media personalities.

Even after the series ended, her TV career has continued with Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, which follows the restoration of a derelict farmhouse near Ravenseat.

Ms Owen has also branched out into live appearances, publishing and children’s books inspired by farm life.
