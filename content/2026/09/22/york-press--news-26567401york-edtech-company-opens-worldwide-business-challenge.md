York-based edtech company SimVenture is inviting students worldwide to compete for £2,000 in a global business simulation contest.

SimVenture has launched its annual Evolve! competition, challenging students to run a virtual business in a realistic setting.

The competition is open to schools, colleges and universities worldwide, helping students build teamwork, commercial awareness and decision-making skills.

Peter Harrington, co-founder and CEO of SimVenture, said: "We’re proud to be a York business with a global reach, and Evolve! is a great example of how technology can connect people and create opportunities that extend far beyond our city.

"We believe people learn best when they’re given the opportunity to put knowledge into practice.

"Running a business simulation gives students the chance to make decisions and learn from both their successes and mistakes."

The four-day competition will run entirely online from November 9 to 12 using SimVenture’s Evolution software.

Student teams will launch and grow virtual bicycle companies, making decisions and responding to the results in real time.

Evolve! is now in its third year, growing from 147 students in eight countries to 257 students in 13 countries in 2025.

Mr Harrington said SimVenture hopes to attract even more students in 2026.

The 2025 winners, Team JKonic from Universidad Modelo Mérida in Mexico, said the competition helped them apply classroom learning in practice.

Webinars will show educators how to use Evolution and answer their questions before the entry deadline.

Teams can practise with the simulation for six days before the competition to ensure everyone starts on an equal footing.

Entry is free and the competition is sponsored by York edtech company Genio.

Entries close at 5pm GMT on Wednesday, October 28.
