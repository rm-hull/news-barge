## York restaurant named best in Yorkshire at Northern England Curry Awards

Who are the winners of the 2026 Northern England Curry Awards? *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A York restaurant has been named the best in Yorkshire.

29 States – The Indian Kitchen in York won the Best Restaurant award for Yorkshire at the Northern England Curry Awards 2026, held earlier this month in Huddersfield.

The awards celebrate the best curry restaurants, takeaways, chefs and hospitality professionals across the North of England, recognising those who help make the region a standout destination for South Asian cuisine.

Yasmin Mahmood, speaking on behalf of organisers Oceanic Awards, said: "The Northern England Curry Awards are dedicated to recognising the incredible talent, hard work and passion that exists throughout the region’s curry industry.

"These businesses are at the heart of their communities and play a significant role in the hospitality sector.

"We congratulate all of this year’s winners and finalists on their achievements.

![]()Jaswant Mehra, Owner & Head Chef of 29 States – The Indian Kitchen. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

"Their commitment to quality, innovation and customer service continues to raise standards across the industry, and they should be extremely proud of this recognition."

The award is a significant achievement for the team at 29 States, who have been serving Indian cuisine in York since 2015.

Owner and head chef Jaswant Mehra said: "We’re extremely proud and grateful.

"It’s wonderful recognition for our entire team and the hard work that goes into the restaurant every day.

"We hope the award introduces 29 States to even more people, while encouraging our existing customers to continue supporting us."

29 States was not the only York restaurant to be recognised on the night.

Bombay Spice was commended in the Yorkshire Curry Experience of the Year category, while Atithi was recognised for excellence in both the Customer Dining Experience of the Year and Curry CNCT Restaurant of the Year categories.

The awards also included a special honour for the Bengal Lounge in Wetherby, which was named the Most Voted Restaurant of the Year.

Jaswant continued: "I trained at the Institute of Hotel Management in Pusa, India, and have been lucky to work with some wonderful places – from Holiday Inn, Taj Hotels, and Radisson Blu Leeds, to Zizzi, Browns Sheffield, and The Ivy York.

"I even taught hotel management and hosted a TV cookery show back in India before moving here.

"In 2015, I followed my dream and opened 29 States – The Indian Kitchen in York."

He said the award was a testament to the team’s "consistency and genuine passion for good food and hospitality."

He added: "Finally, I just want to say a big thank you to our guests – without their support, none of this would be possible."
