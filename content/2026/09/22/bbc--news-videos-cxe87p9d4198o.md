# Watch: What to expect from the UN General Assembly

World leaders have descended on New York for this year's UN General Assembly. The 81st High-Level Week brings together nearly 130 leaders under the theme, “Restoring trust, managing transformation: a United Nations that delivers for all.”

The BBC’s Sarah Smith outlines who US President Donald Trump is set to meet and the key topics expected to be on the agenda.
