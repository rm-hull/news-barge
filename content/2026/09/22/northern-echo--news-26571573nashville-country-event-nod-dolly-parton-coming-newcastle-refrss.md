A new indoor country music festival is set to bring a taste of Nashville to the North East.

Ruby Rae’s XL – Newcastle Indoor Country Festival will transform Times Square in the city into a Tennessee-style celebration on Friday, October 23, 2026, from 5pm to 11pm.

The event promises an evening of live music, line dancing, and southern-inspired entertainment, all under a giant marquee designed to capture the spirit of Nashville.

It will welcome 1,500 country music fans for a night of immersive fun and tribute performances.

Dean Saunders, co-owner of country music bar Ruby Rae’s, said: "We are super passionate about country music and Ruby Rae’s, and we wanted to create an XL version of what we do every single week in Newcastle.

"Ruby Rae’s XL will have six incredible tribute acts, line dancing, Nashville décor, BBQ food, country music merchandise and 1,500 people coming together for one unforgettable night."

![]()Ruby Rae's bar in Newcastle *(Image: Ruby Rae's)*

The six-act tribute line-up will include The Ella Langley Experience, Zachbryanuktribute, The Lainey Wilson experience Tribute, Almostwallen, Shania Twain by Tia Dodds, and Jo Fry as the iconic Dolly Parton.

The festival is being organised in partnership with EVNT Inspirations.

EVNT Inspirations Co-Director Nigel Holliday said: "We are very excited to work with Dean and his team at Ruby Rae’s.

"Having known and worked together for more than 15 years, it was the perfect synergy to use our large Times Square site and combine it with Ruby Rae’s, a huge brand in Newcastle and York, to offer an XL version of the Ruby Rae’s experience."

Mr Saunders co-owns Ruby Rae’s with Dan Cooke, who said the event will bring something fresh to the region.

Mr Cooke said: "The North East knows how to embrace a great night out, and this festival will bring a completely different energy to the region."

"Country music has such a feel-good spirit; it brings people together," he added.

Ruby Rae’s has built a reputation as a leading name in live country music entertainment, with venues in both Newcastle and York.

The brand continues to grow, following the launch of its York venue earlier this summer.
