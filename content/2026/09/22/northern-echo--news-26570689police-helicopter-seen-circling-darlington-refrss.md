A police helicopter was spotted circling Darlington on Monday afternoon as officers searched for two suspects on an e-bike.

Residents reported seeing and hearing a police helicopter over several areas, including Haughton, from about 12.30pm on September 21.

A spokesperson from the National Police Air Service (NPAS) confirmed they were looking for two suspects.

NPAS said: “At 11.54am on Monday, September 21, a police helicopter crew from NPAS Newcastle was deployed to Darlington to support Durham Constabulary in locating and apprehending two suspects on an electric bike.

“Total flying time on the task was 58 minutes.”

Pictures taken in the area show the aircraft hovering overhead as people took to social media to alert others.
