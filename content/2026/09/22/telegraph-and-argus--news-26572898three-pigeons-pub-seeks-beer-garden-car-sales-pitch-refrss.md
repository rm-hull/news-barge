A car sales pitch could become a beer garden for a popular hostelry loved by fans of football and real ale alike, if planners agree.

The Three Pigeons at South Parade, Halifax, is highly rated by commentators on Tripadvisor, and the Campaign For Real Ale (CAMRA) – highly commended in last year’s Halifax and Calderdale Pub of the Year awards – and a popular stopping off point for FC Halifax Town supporters on their way to matches nearby at The Shay.

The art deco pub, which CAMRA says was built by Halifax’s Webster’s Brewery in 1932, is Grade II-listed.

Sami Foster of Ossett and Salt Pubs and Bars have now applied to Calderdale Council seeking planning permission to change neighbouring land from car storage space to a beer garden for the pub.

A supporting statement with the application says it relates to the change of use of an existing car sales pitch – approval for which dates back to 1985 – to an outdoor seating area including tables and chairs for use by the adjacent Three Pigeons.

An entrance formed in the side wall for patrons to be able to access the garden from the public house, says the supporting statement.

The proposal would represent an improvement to the setting of the listed building, it argues.

The change of use application, number 26/00759/FUL, and supporting papers can be viewed on the council’s Planning Portal.
