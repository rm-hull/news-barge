A York hotel boss will cycle more than 100 miles to raise money for volunteers who feed vulnerable people in the city every weekend.

Lionel Chatard, director and general manager of Middlethorpe Hall Hotel and Spa, is preparing to cycle the Ćiro Trail from Mostar, in southern Bosnia, to Dubrovnik in southern Croatia, for HOPING Street Kitchen in York.

Lionel, 54, said he often travels by bicycle in York but is “absolutely not” a keen cyclist.

He said he wanted to do something “original and special” to raise money for HOPING after attending one of its street kitchens in King’s Manor.

“I spent some time with the team at King’s Manor and was so impressed with the passion and care they showed – and I wanted to raise funds for them,” he said.

“It’s not only the food they provide – it’s the respect they show to everyone and anyone. It’s wonderful.”

![]()Volunteers from HOPING Street Kitchen in York *(Image: HOPING)*

The cycling trip will also be a chance to enjoy a “slow holiday”, Lionel said, praising the growing movement that values immersive, respectful travel over rushing from one destination to another.

Lionel said he plans to fly to Dubrovnik on October 2 then travel by coach to Mostar.

From Mostar, he will rent a bicycle and start the journey back to Dubrovnik.

Lionel plans to cycle for up to five hours a day for five days, saying: “I’m taking it slowly and trying to understand more of the culture.”

Lionel admitted to feeling “slightly worried” about cycling the distance but said he was “looking forward to the challenge”.

He also said that raising awareness of HOPING Street Kitchen would help him cross the finish line.

“They [HOPING's volunteers] serve more than 100 covers every Sunday and I’m just spending five days away,” he said.

“I’m hoping to raise awareness of the care they bring to other people.”

Jayne Venables, from HOPING, praised the efforts of Lionel and other Hospitality Association York (HAY) members in supporting the street kitchen.

She added that “every penny raised” through Lionel’s efforts will be used by the HOPING to help feed its vulnerable diners as winter approaches.

“To have many of York's most prestigious hotels stepping in for this year to help us support homeless and struggling folk feels really appropriate,” she said.

“They've even offered to take turns to provide meals. Lionel's chef has produced huge apple crumbles using Middlethorpe Hall orchard's first apples, all served with chef's delicious custard.

“Our diners were treated to boxes full of hotel toiletries and even their beloved dogs received treats.

“To see the hotel director and general manager roll up his sleeves and serve our hungry diners was very touching so when Lionel chose HOPING for his HAY Croatian fundraiser we were thrilled.

“He never stops and is full of great ideas. This cycle trip is actually rather challenging and we've total faith in his endless energy and staying power.”
