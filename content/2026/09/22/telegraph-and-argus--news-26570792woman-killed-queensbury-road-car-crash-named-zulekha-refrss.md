A woman who died in a three-car collision in Halifax has been named locally as Zulekha.

Online reports suggest that her husband was also seriously injured in Sunday's crash.

A poster circulating on social media has called for a "Dua request for sister Zulekha."

The text goes on to describe Zulekha as having "tragically passed away in a serious collision on Queensbury Road Halifax / Boothtown."

There was an additional Dua request for her husband, who is not named in the post.

It reads: "Dua request for her husband who is hospitalised in a serious condition."

## What we know about Sunday's collision

The crash occurred shortly before 10.45am on September 20 on the A647 Queensbury Road, north of the junction with Howcans Lane.

A Honda Civic travelling towards Queensbury collided with a Seat Leon heading in the opposite direction.

A VW Golf, also travelling in the opposite direction, was damaged at the scene.

A rear-seat passenger in the Honda, a woman in her 40s who has been named locally as Zulekha, died at the scene.

Three other people were taken to hospital with serious injuries.

![]()Emergency services at the scene of the crash *(Image: Gazz Hall)*

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 10.47am yesterday morning to report a road traffic collision on Queensbury Road near Boothtown.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including five ambulances and the air ambulance, and four patients were conveyed to hospital."

The road was closed while emergency services responded.

The Major Collision Enquiry Team (MCET) is now appealing for witnesses or anyone with footage of the incident or the moments leading up to it.

## Arrests have been made

The driver of the VW Golf, a man, was arrested on suspicion of drug driving and causing death by dangerous driving.

He has since been released on bail pending further enquiries.

The driver of the Seat Leon, also a man, was arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and has also been bailed.
