A person has been charged and is due to appear in court after being arrested for breaching a Criminal Behaviour Order during police patrols in Halifax town centre.

Officers carrying out high-visibility foot patrols across Halifax arrested an individual for allegedly breaching a Criminal Behaviour Order (CBO).

Police said the person has since been charged and is scheduled to appear before the courts.

The patrols form part of an ongoing policing operation aimed at tackling issues affecting residents, businesses and visitors in the town centre.

## **Action taken over anti-social behaviour**

Officers also responded to reports of anti-social behaviour involving groups of young people in the Woolshops area.

Those involved were identified and dealt with, with police taking what they described as appropriate action to help prevent further disruption to businesses, shoppers and members of the public.

## **Continued town centre patrols**

Police say they will continue conducting regular patrols in Halifax town centre and working with partner agencies to address concerns raised by the community.

The force said the activity forms part of its commitment to keeping Halifax a safe and welcoming place for everyone.

## **Reporting concerns**

Anyone who witnesses anti-social behaviour or has concerns about issues in Halifax town centre is encouraged to report them to West Yorkshire Police.

## **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A spokesperson said: "Officers have been carrying out high-visibility foot patrols across Halifax Town Centre as part of our ongoing commitment to tackling the issues that matter most to local residents, businesses and visitors.

"As a result of proactive policing activity, one individual was arrested for breaching a Criminal Behaviour Order. They have since been charged and are due to appear before the court.

"Officers have also been addressing reports of anti-social behaviour involving groups of young people in the Woolshops area. Those involved were identified and dealt with, with appropriate action taken to help prevent further disruption to businesses, shoppers and members of the public.

"We will continue to carry out regular patrols, work with partner agencies and respond to concerns raised by the community to help keep Halifax town centre a safe and welcoming place for everyone.

"If you witness anti-social behaviour or have concerns about issues in the town centre, please continue to report them to us."
