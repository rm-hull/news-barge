A vehicle displaying cloned registration plates has been recovered by police in Bradford Moor following information received from members of the public.

Officers attended Nottingham Street after reports of an abandoned vehicle were made by concerned residents.

## **Vehicle found with cloned plates**

After carrying out enquiries, officers established that the vehicle was displaying cloned registration plates.

The vehicle was subsequently recovered and police said appropriate action had been taken.

Cloned registration plates are used when a vehicle is fraudulently given the identity of another vehicle, often allowing offences to be committed without being traced back to the correct owner.

## **Public information praised**

Police have thanked local residents for reporting their concerns, describing the recovery as another example of how information from the community can help tackle issues affecting neighbourhoods.

A spokesperson said reports from the public play a vital role in identifying suspicious activity and enabling officers to take positive action.

## **Residents urged to report concerns**

Officers are encouraging anyone who spots a suspicious or abandoned vehicle in their area to report it.

Members of the public can contact police via 101 for non-emergency matters, or call 999 in an emergency.

The force said community information remains an important tool in helping keep local neighbourhoods safe.

## **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A spokesperson said: "Cloned Vehicle Recovered in Bradford Moor

"Acting on information provided by members of the public, officers attended Nottingham Street, Bradford Moor, to investigate reports of an abandoned vehicle.

"Following enquiries, it was established that the vehicle was displaying cloned registration plates. The vehicle has been recovered and appropriate action taken.

"This is another great example of how information from our communities helps us identify and deal with issues affecting local neighbourhoods.

"Thank you to those who took the time to report their concerns.

"If you spot a suspicious or abandoned vehicle in your area, please let us know.

"Reports from the public help us take positive action and keep our communities safe.

"Report via 101, or 999 in an emergency."
