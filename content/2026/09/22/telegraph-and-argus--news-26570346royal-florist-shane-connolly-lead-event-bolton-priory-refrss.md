A ROYAL florist will be sharing his expertise at a new sustainability event.

Shane Connolly, floral designer to King Charles III and Queen Camilla, will be demonstrating at the first Sustainable Church Flowers conference at Bolton Priory on Friday, September 25.

Mr Connolly is known for his foam-free, environmentally conscious arrangements and created the floral displays for the coronation at Westminster Abbey.

He will give a live demonstration using British-grown blooms at Bolton Priory.

The Reverend Nicholas Merchant, rector of Bolton Priory, said: "Stewardship of God’s creation is one of the two marks of mission at Bolton Priory and we are delighted to host and support the Sustainable Flower Initiative."

The Sustainable Church Flowers movement began as a grassroots effort by volunteers in Harpley parish and has since grown into a national, ecumenical campaign.

It promotes eco-friendly practices in church flower arranging by offering practical advice and inspiration.

Speakers at the conference will include Zanna Hoskins of Spindle, a British grower and agro florist who runs courses with a focus on growing foliage for flower arranging.

Dr Jill Timms will also address attendees, explaining the science behind sustainable flowers.

A team of more than 20 volunteer flower arrangers at Bolton Priory has already begun shifting to more sustainable methods.

Liz Clayton from the flower team said: "Our next job is to persuade wedding planners that it’s much better to choose flowers and colours that reflect the seasons."

The team has compiled a list of local growers to help supply seasonal flowers for church displays.

The conference is supported by the Bolton Abbey Estate, which is encouraging attendees to explore the grounds after the event.

It also coincides with the build-up to the Priory’s Harvest Festival All Ages service on Sunday, October 4, at 10.30am.

The church will be decorated with seasonal arrangements, and visitors will have the opportunity to view Mr Connolly’s floral work.

A spokesperson for the event said that only a few free tickets remain and can be booked through Eventbrite by searching for "SCF Northern Conference Tickets."

The Priory Church is open daily from dawn until dusk for visitors who would like to see examples of Shane Connolly’s work.

Journalists are invited to attend all or part of the conference.

Refreshments will be served at Bolton Hall from 9.45am to 10.15am, and media interviews with speakers can be arranged by arrangement.

To reserve a seat, members of the press should contact Liz Clayton.
