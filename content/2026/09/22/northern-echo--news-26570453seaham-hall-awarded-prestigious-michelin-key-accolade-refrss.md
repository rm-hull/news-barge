A luxury County Durham hotel has been awarded a prestigious accolade by the Michelin Guide.

Seaham Hall has received a Michelin Key in recognition of its exceptional hospitality and dedication to outstanding guest experiences.

The Michelin Key is a highly selective honour, considered the hotel equivalent of the Michelin Star.

Tucked away on the Durham coast, Seaham Hall Hotel & Serenity Spa has fast become one of the UK’s best-kept secrets among the A-list.

![]() *(Image: Seaham Hall)*

Over the years, guests have included Kylie Minogue, One Direction, Lionel Richie, Robbie Williams and footballers.

It's latest accolade celebrates properties that excel in design, service, character, value, and sense of place.

Ross Grieve, managing director of Seaham Hall, said: "Receiving a Michelin Key is a fantastic achievement for our entire team at Seaham Hall and something we are incredibly proud of.

"To be recognised among just two hotels in the North East is a real honour and reflects the care and attention that goes into every aspect of the guest experience, from our outstanding levels of hospitality to our approach to food and our connection to the region.

"It is incredible to see that commitment recognised by the Michelin Guide, and we look forward to continuing to build on this achievement."

The award follows anonymous, in-depth inspections and recognises hotels that deliver an exceptional overall stay while reflecting a strong sense of place and individuality.

Seaham Hall is County Durham’s only Michelin Key hotel and one of just two in the North East.

![]()The recently revamped spa garden *(Image: Seaham Hall)*

It is now one of 150 Michelin Key hotels across the UK and Ireland, including 18 new additions.

The hotel is marking its 25th anniversary this year and has undertaken a number of significant refurbishments over the past year.

In April, it reopened the Serenity Spa’s Zen Garden after a £1 million investment and launched two venues: Japanese-inspired spa restaurant Geko and modern speakeasy Harvey’s Bar.

Seaham Hall recently topped the North East rankings in the prestigious La Liste, where it claimed the top spot in the North East.

It has also made the shortlist for the AA Spa and Hospitality Awards.

Built in 1791, Seaham Hall hosted the wedding of Annabella Milbanke and poet Lord Byron in 1815, adding historical context to its sense of place.
