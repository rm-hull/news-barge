A garden centre in Northallerton has received glowing reviews from customers.

Strikes Garden Centre has a strong 4.3-star rating from nearly 1,400 Google reviews.

The Darlington Road centre sells plants, gifts, garden equipment and pet accessories.

One customer left a five-star review and said: "What a great garden centre, can't believe I have never been before.

"Great range of garden stuff, plants, sheds and the normal garden centre stuff.

"Plus whole lots of other things such as birthday cards, clothes and not to mention a café.

"So an excellent place and we got a bargain for some nice plants for the garden

"Highly recommended."

It also hosts gardening masterclasses and pop-up restaurants.

The café receives consistent praise from customers.

One visitor said: "Had a lovely meal in the café at lunchtime.

"Was busy but we were served really quickly.

"The food and service were excellent."

The café offers a selection of food including breakfast, sandwiches, jacket potatoes, fish and chips and more.

The centre supports a different charity to fundraise for each year.

Last year, the centre raised £56,000 for the British Heart Foundation.

This year, they are raising money for Dementia UK.

The opening times are:

* Garden Centre: Monday to Wednesday - 9am-6pm, Thursday - 9am-7.30pm, Friday and Saturday - 9am-6pm, Sunday - 10.30am-4.30pm.
*Café: Monday to Wednesday - 9am-5pm, Thursday - 9am-7pm, Friday and Saturday - 9am-5pm, Sunday - 10am-4pm.* Last food orders 4pm (3pm Sunday).*
