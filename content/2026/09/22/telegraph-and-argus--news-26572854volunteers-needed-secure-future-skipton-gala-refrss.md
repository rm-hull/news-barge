ENTHUSIASTIC people with a love of community events are being invited to help secure the future of Skipton Gala.

Skipton Town Council, which has organised the annual event for the last two years, is planning to set up a new Skipton Gala Charitable Trust.

The aim of the trust will be to support and develop the gala as a valued community tradition, bringing people together, and helping to secure the event for generations to come.

Louise Close, chief officer, Skipton Town Council, said: “The aim of the trust will be to support and develop the Skipton Gala as a valued community tradition, bringing people together and helping to secure its future for generations to come.

“We are looking for people who are enthusiastic about Skipton, community events and making a positive difference. You may have experience in areas such as charity governance, fundraising, finance, event organisation, marketing, business or community engagement — but, most importantly, we are looking for people who share a passion for the gala and want to help make it a success.

“This is an opportunity to be part of a small group at the beginning of an exciting new venture and to help shape how the trust develops.”

Mrs Close added: “We would love to hear from anyone who feels they could bring ideas, enthusiasm, skills or simply a willingness to get involved. Together, we can help secure the future of Skipton Gala and ensure it continues to be a much-loved part of our community.”

Skipton Gala has been going for more than 100 years and is always held on the second Saturday in June.

It includes a procession of floats that travels through the town to the gala field, at Aireville Park.

Every year a girl or boy is crowned Gala Queen or King and there is entertainment on the gala field including a funfair and fireworks.

![]()Chief officer Louise Close and deputy Jenny Dean at Skipton Gala *(Image: Lesley Tate)*

The long-term former organising committee of the gala stood down at the start of 2025.

Geoff Dunn was, in 2023, made an honorary freeman of the town for his 30 years as gala chair.

His partner, Vera Longthorne, who served as gala president for 30 years, sadly died in August.

Anyone interested in becoming a member of the new Skipton Charitable Gala trust, or to find out more, should contact Louise Close at: [chiefofficer@skiptontowncouncil.gov.uk.](mailto:chiefofficer@skiptontowncouncil.gov.uk.)

Expressions of interest need to be made by October 2.
