Police are seeking help to identify a man following an incident in which someone attempted to enter a van.

The incident took place on Low Street, Brotherton at 11.15am on Thursday, September 3, 2026, when a man, who was a passenger in a blue Ford S-Max, approached a parked van and tried to get into it.

When he found the vehicle locked, he returned to the Ford and left the scene, said a spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police.

The police have released a CCTV image of a man they want to speak to as part of their investigation.
