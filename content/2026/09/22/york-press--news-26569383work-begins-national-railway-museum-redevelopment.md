CONSTRUCTION has begun on a major redevelopment project in York.

Work has gotten underway at the National Railway Museum, including the new Central Hall and Railway Futures: The Porterbrook Gallery, which is set to create a 'welcoming new gateway to the museum.'

Earlier this month, the NRM hosted a special event to mark the start of construction with key funders, supporters, and partners, including Porterbrook and Mott MacDonald, in attendance.

Craig Bentley, director of the National Railway Museum, said: "This celebration marks the beginning of an exciting new chapter for the National Railway Museum.

"Central Hall and Railway Futures: The Porterbrook Gallery will transform how people experience the museum. We are enormously grateful to the funders, partners, and supporters who have helped bring us to this point.

"Together, we are laying the foundations for a museum that preserves the past, serves the present, and inspires the future."

Guests at the event heard from Mr Bentley and Porterbrook CEO Mary Grant before taking a guided tour of the construction site - which included a look at excavation work and the laying of foundations.

![]()Exclusive celebration event of Central Hall and Futures Gallery:The Porterbrook Gallery at National Railway Museum to mark the next steps of the projects. *(Image: Jason Hynes / Trustees of the Science Museum Group.)*

To commemorate the occasion, guests were invited to sign copies of the gallery’s design plans, which will be placed in a time capsule.

The Railway Futures gallery will celebrate the history of railways while encouraging visitors to imagine and explore the future of rail travel.

Mary Grant, CEO of Porterbrook, said: "Inspiring a new generation of talent to join this great industry is of the upmost importance.

"Young people are fascinated by trains, and this new gallery will keep that spark alive by showcasing the vast possibilities the future holds.

"The journey of rail innovation does not have a final destination, and that’s the story that Railway Futures will tell."

![]()Exclusive celebration event of Central Hall and Futures Gallery:The Porterbrook Gallery at National Railway Museum to mark the next steps of the projects. *(Image: Jason Hynes / Trustees of the Science Museum Group.)*

A spokesperson for the Friends of the National Railway Museum added: "To be included in such a prestigious gathering of guests was a real privilege.

"Over the next 18 months it will be great to watch the progress of the building work, but to be ‘in at the start’ will be something which will remain with me for a long time."

The National Railway Museum has thanked its community of funders and partners for their support in making the project possible.

In particular, the museum recognised the contributions of Porterbrook, Wick and Bonnie Moorman, and Mott MacDonald.
