A STEAKHOUSE has come under fire after posting an unusual video online.

Porterhouse Bros, on Thornton Road, posted a TikTok video targeting "drug dealers," which attracted online criticism and almost 300 comments.

A young woman in the video says: "This is a message for all the drug dealers in Bradford.

“So if you’re not a drug dealer, keep scrolling.”

The woman displays a platter of food, which includes bone marrow.

She said: "This right here is the most gangster, hardest platter you’re gonna find in the whole of Bradford, mush.

"You’ve got a whole bone marrow right there, filled to the brim.

"Sirloin steak and a lobster tail.

"Are you mad?

"That’s crazy.

"So if you’re a real bad man, come down to Porterhouse Bros and get your bone."

Social media users labelled the video "embarrassing".

One user said: "How not to get someone to come to your restaurant."

Another said: "Well, I guess I won't be trying this place then."

A final user commented: "No decorum."

However, others were not as quick to criticise.

One commenter said: "Lovely, looking forward to it mush."

Another said: "I'm not in Bradford but I'll still come."
