A care home resident has found a new sense of purpose by helping others feel at home.

John 'Paddy' Carrol, 76, has become a familiar and friendly face at Eastside Gardens in Wallsend, where he greets other residents and visitors each morning and helps newcomers settle in.

![]()John with other residents *(Image: Prestwick Care)*

Mr Carrol, a former bin man with Durham County Council, has lived at the home since July 2025.

Determined to ensure that everyone who enters the home is given the warmest of welcomes, Paddy takes his place in the foyer every morning to greet and help others find their way around the home and introduce them to fellow residents.

Mr Carrol said: "I love it here and wouldn’t want to be anywhere else.

"I love it, I love my job, I like to work with the activities team and we have great fun."

![]()“I love it, I love my job" *(Image: Prestwick Care)*

Having now earned the title of activity buddy at the home, which provides accommodation, nursing, and personal care, Mr Carrol's daily routine includes guiding visitors to the right rooms, helping set tables, and handing out newspapers.

Jade Richardson, deputy manager at Eastside Gardens, said: "He still likes to get up early and be on the go.

"Being our activity buddy gives him a purpose since he loves to be active and kept busy.

"And, along with meeting and greeting visitors and new residents, his rota includes setting tables three days per week, playing dominoes with fellow residents, and handing out daily newspapers.

"He really encourages other residents to participate in the activity fun within Eastside Gardens."

The CQC recently praised Eastside Gardens for its "exceptional service" and "highly skilled staff."

Staff say Mr Carrol’s positive attitude and willingness to help have made a lasting impression on everyone at the home.
