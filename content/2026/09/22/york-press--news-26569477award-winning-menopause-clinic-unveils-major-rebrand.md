A WOMEN'S health clinic has announced a rebrand and expansion as part of its ambitions for future growth.

East Riding Wellness and Aesthetics, founded by consultant nurse practitioner Tracey Dennison, will now be known as The Dennison Clinic.

The new name reflects its evolution into a specialist provider of women’s health, menopause and hormonal care, alongside medical aesthetics, weight management and longevity services.

Tracey Dennison, who has twice been named UK Menopause Nurse of the Year, said: "The business has changed significantly since we first launched.

"Today we're supporting women with everything from menopause and hormonal health to weight management and medical aesthetics, both in clinic and online.

"The Dennison Clinic reflects that broader vision for the future."

The business began in East Yorkshire and now serves women across Yorkshire through clinics in East Yorkshire and York, with a third location in Milton Keynes.

It also offers online consultations to women nationwide.

Earlier this year, the clinic was named Best Menopause Clinic (North of England) at the Menopause in Practice Awards 2026.

Ms Dennison, a British Menopause Society (BMS) Registered Advanced Specialist, retained her UK Menopause Nurse of the Year title for the second year running.

She said: "It is encouraging to see more recognition of the importance of women’s health.

"But too many women still struggle to get the right information, diagnosis and support.

"We want to provide a place where women feel listened to and where their care is based on the best available evidence."

The clinic’s team now includes GP Dr Matthew Johnson, who has more than 22 years’ experience and a specialist interest in women’s health.

Surgeon Dr Arturo Almeida also joins the team, offering minor surgery, fat-dissolving procedures and varicose vein treatment.
