A County Durham college is counting down to the arrival of its first students at a new, state-of-the-art campus.

East Durham College is set to welcome learners to its redeveloped Houghall Campus in September 2027, with construction scheduled for completion by April 2027.

The project, delivered by Tilbury Douglas on behalf of the Department for Education, will transform the campus into a modern centre for land-based, environmental, and sustainability education.

Scott Bullock, principal and CEO of East Durham College, said: "Exceptional facilities make a real difference to a student’s experience, the environment in which they learn inspires ambition, raises expectations and gives students the confidence to succeed.

"That is why the investment in Houghall Campus is so important, we are creating an exceptional learning environment where students can develop the technical knowledge, practical skills and experience they need to succeed in the world of work.

"When our first students arrive in September 2027, they will have the opportunity to learn in state-of-the-art facilities designed around the industries they are preparing to enter."

Construction began earlier this year, with enabling works starting in October 2025 and the main building phase commencing in January 2026.

With students having recently celebrated a positive results day at the college's Peterlee campus, the new campus in Durham will support education in agriculture, horticulture, environmental management, animal care, and other land-based sectors.

It aims to address the rising demand for skills in sustainable food production, environmental stewardship, and the green economy.

The investment comes as East Durham College continues to build its reputation for delivering high-quality, industry-focused education and training.

The college recently achieved a 75 per cent apprenticeship success rate, well above the national average of 65.7 per cent, and was named among the top 10 college providers nationally for apprenticeship achievement.

It is also the fastest-growing apprenticeship provider in the North East, based on starts between August 2025 and January 2026.

The Houghall Campus currently ranks fifth nationally for achievement rates among land-based education providers and is the highest-performing land-based college in the North.

Helen McCoy, campus principal at Houghall, said: "Our recent apprenticeship and T Level successes demonstrate the strength of the education and training we are providing across the college.

"Houghall Campus will build on that success, giving students an outstanding environment in which to develop the skills and experience employers need.

"With one year to go, seeing Houghall Campus take shape is incredibly exciting."

"This is about creating opportunities for the next generation and ensuring our region has the skilled people it needs to support future growth."

Designed by Jefferson Sheard Architects, the new campus will feature a two-storey teaching block with modern classrooms and digital learning labs, alongside specialist agricultural facilities such as a hay barn, sheep shed, and grain store.

Existing buildings will be refurbished to improve sustainability and accessibility, and the site will benefit from new landscaping, improved access, and expanded outdoor learning spaces.
