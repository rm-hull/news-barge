A new American-style diner has opened at Leeds Bradford Airport.

Monty’s Diner, located in the departures lounge, is part of ongoing upgrades at the airport and promises to offer travellers a relaxed dining experience before take-off.

The all-day eatery is operated by SSP UK & Ireland, which already runs the Tap + Brew Craft Beer Bar and Kitchen at the airport.

Vincent Hodder, CEO of Leeds Bradford Airport, said: "We’re thrilled to welcome Monty’s Diner to LBA.

"We are constantly seeking to improve our passenger experience and offering at the airport.

"We hope this new addition will provide travellers with even more dining options, especially for those travelling with children as we expect Monty’s to be a hit with families.

"As more and more people choose to travel through Leeds Bradford Airport, we are continuing to work with our retail partner SSP to open new concepts like Monty’s."

Breakfast is served until 11am, with waffles, French toast bites, chicken and waffles, cooked breakfasts and classic American bagels.

The all-day menu features American favourites including smash burgers, wings, hot dogs, mac and cheese and a barbecue platter.

Children can choose waffles, beans on toast or scrambled eggs on toast for breakfast, with burgers, chicken tenders and mac and cheese served all day.

The drinks menu includes soft drinks, thick shakes, cocktails and ‘hardshakes’ for adults.

Stuart Buchanan, chief property and business development officer at SSP UK & Ireland, said: "Passengers are heading to the airport earlier and being called to their gate later, meaning there is a greater expectation for unique, value-led propositions that enhance their time at the airport before boarding.

"Monty’s is more than just a food and beverage offer, it’s an immersive American diner concept that improves the overall airport experience and will quickly become the best part of the journey for passengers flying from Leeds Bradford Airport."

The launch of Monty’s comes as part of broader improvements at Leeds Bradford Airport.

A new central search area is set to open next month, and the terminal has already been expanded to offer 83 per cent more seating, along with new shopping and dining options.

Monty’s Diner will open daily, from the first flight to the last.
