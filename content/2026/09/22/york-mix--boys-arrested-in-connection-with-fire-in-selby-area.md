Two boys were arrested in the early hours of this morning (22 September) following a fire at a disused restaurant near Brotherton in the Selby area.

The fire at the empty Thaal restaurant on the Great North Road was reported late last night (21 September) and is believed to have caused considerable damage to the property.

The two boys, aged 12 and 15 were both arrested on suspicion of arson and remain in custody at the time of writing. Officers have not ruled out further arrests.

No reports of any injuries or missing people have been received, however, a full search of the property will be carried out once it is safe to do so.

Officers have warned that deliberately setting fires is a serious criminal offence that puts lives at risk and can cause devastating damage to homes, businesses, vehicles and the environment.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Even a small fire can quickly spread, placing firefighters, emergency workers and members of the public in danger.”

Anyone with information about last night’s fire should call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and pass information from incident number 12260185851.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson issued this update at 12.35pm: “The fire has now been extinguished, and crews have completed checks for any remaining hotspots.

“A crew from Acomb, supported by an aerial ladder platform, remains at the scene to assist with the ongoing fire investigation.

“The investigation is continuing. While roads in the area have now reopened, the footpath surrounding the building remains closed.”
