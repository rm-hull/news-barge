POLICE are appealing to the driver of a distinctive-coloured vehicle who may have witnessed an assault in a North Yorkshire town.

The county's police force said the incident happened on Lowmoor Road, Selby around midday on September 5, 2026.




Officers said they are particularly keen to speak to the driver of a "teal or green" Porsche, who may have witnessed the incident.

Anyone with information is asked to email [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

Quote reference 12260174213 when providing information.
