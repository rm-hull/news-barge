# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image of man after cooking oil stolen from restaurant in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police has released a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to following a theft from a restaurant in Harrogate.

The incident happened on July 31 when cooking oil was stolen from LILYPAD on King’s Road.

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and ask for PC 1113 Simms.
