A York accountancy firm has welcomed a new intake of trainees as it invests in the industry’s next generation.

Azets has taken on ten trainees in York, as 23 school leavers and graduates start roles across its Yorkshire offices.

Claire Needham, regional managing director for Yorkshire and the North East at Azets, said: "A very warm welcome to our new trainees.

"Their recruitment represents continued investment in Yorkshire, providing opportunities for both school leavers and graduates across the region.

"Nurturing the next generation of talent with on-the-job training, mentoring and opportunities to gain professional qualifications and hands-on experience is key to the future of Azets and the continued success of our clients."

Azets has also taken on 11 trainees in Leeds and two in Bradford.
