Three new soldier silhouettes have been created to honour the lives and service of fallen heroes.

The silhouettes, presented to Devizes Town Council and the Royal British Legion by the Devizes Men's Shed, were crafted following a discussion about the absence of "Tommy Silhouettes" during last year’s Remembrance Day.

John Stephens, chairman of Devizes Men's Shed, said: "This came about after I discussed the lack of these 'Tommy Silhouettes' in the town at last year's Remembrance Day.

"I was subsequently invited to join a sub-committee set up by Councillor Jonathon Hunter.

"The sub-committee met in July and asked me to make some silhouettes as I am the chairman of the Devizes Men's Shed."

Unable to secure council funding due to time constraints, Mr Stephens approached Devizes Trailer Centre, which donated scrap wood for the project.

The silhouettes are inscribed with the names of local gallantry medal recipients, including three Victoria Cross honourees and 10 others recognised for bravery.

Mr Stephens said: "We hope that the council will find suitable places to display our soldiers for all to see and reflect on the sacrifices made by those that served this country so bravely."
