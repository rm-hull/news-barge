Firefighters are warning students to stay safe as they celebrate the start of the university year and enjoy Freshers' Week.

With the North East being home to some of the best nights out in the country, and many of our universities being on or close to a river, firefighters are warning of the real dangers of walking by the riverside after a couple of drinks.

Tyne and Wear Fire and Rescue Service (TWFRS) is urging students not to take a shortcut along the river, to stay away from the water's edge, and to stick together on nights out.

In a statement commending the region as home to some of the UK's best nights out, TWFRS said: "Whether you're studying at Newcastle University, Northumbria University or the University of Sunderland, or know someone who is, remember that many of our region's most popular nightlife destinations are located alongside rivers, quaysides and waterfronts."

The service urged students, especially after a few drinks, to:

* **Avoid taking a shortcut along the river**
* **Not wander off alone**
* **Not to assume you'll be fine near the water**
* **Stay together**
* **Plan your route home**
* **Make sure everyone gets back safely**
* **Keep your phone charged**

"One night can change lives forever", the service added.

The service also issued the safety video above, showing the dangers of walking along a river's edge during a night out.

In another social media post, the service welcomed new students to the region while reminding them to look out for each other.

The service wrote: "A great night out should end with everyone getting home safely - don't drink and drown.

"Whether you're starting university or waving goodbye to someone who is, let's work together to make sure Freshers' Week is remembered for all the right reasons."
