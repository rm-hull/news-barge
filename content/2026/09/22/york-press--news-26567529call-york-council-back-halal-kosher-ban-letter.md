## Council should back halal and kosher meat ban

Ritual slaughter of animals under halal and kosher rules is a barbaric medieval abhorrence which should have no place in modern society.

Refusing to support proposals to eradicate this abomination amounts to defending the indefensible.

The accusations of “racism” against Councillor Walter for proposing a motion to ban halal/kosher food in York council contracts was as predictable as it is erroneous.

**Recommended reads from Letters:**

Islam is not a race. It is a belief system which is followed by people of all ethnicities including white Caucasians. Followers of the Jewish faith also include people of all colours.

The objection to halal/kosher slaughter is not about skin pigment or any other ethnic distinction.

It is about conduct and cruelty.

A society which believes food animals should be treated well and their end of life should be as humane as possible would have nothing to do with halal/kosher slaughter.

**Matthew LaverackLord Mayors WalkYork**
