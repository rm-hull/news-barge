Visitors to a popular Bradford venue will earn a free play session quicker than they used to be able to.

Tong Garden Centre has announced changes to its Grass Hoppers indoor play scheme for pre-school children and families.

### 'claim fifth session free'

Customers using the Green Card loyalty scheme can claim their fifth session free. Previously, the reward was available after eight visits.

The free session can be used at any time, including weekends and school holidays.

Read More:

The centre has also launched an Off-Peak Pass costing £29.99. It provides unlimited off-peak play for 12 months at Grass Hoppers sites in Tong, Tingley and Otley.

The pass is aimed at pre-school children and families who visit during the week. Off-peak sessions run from Monday to Friday during term time. They do not include weekends or school holidays.

Families can either collect free visits through the Green Card scheme or buy the annual pass instead.
