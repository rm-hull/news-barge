There’s been a crash on a main route into York this morning (Tuesday).

It was on Bishopgate Street, near to Skeldergate Bridge.

The city-bound lane was closed while emergency services dealt with the incident.

Police then directed traffic around the damaged vehicles. They have now been removed and the road is open as normal.

A white Suzuki hatchback was damaged in the collision. Two vans, one red and one white, were also at the crash scene.

The Suzuki’s passenger side airbag deployed.

Two police ambulances and two police vans were at the scene.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said they took two casualties to hospital.
