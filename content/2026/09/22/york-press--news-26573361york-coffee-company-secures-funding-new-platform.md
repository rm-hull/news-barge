## York coffee company secures funding and appoints new chair 'at pivotal moment'

kWh founders Caitlin Walker and Shaun Feakins *(Image: © Glen Minikin)*

A SUSTAINABLE York-based coffee company has secured new investment to help launch a fresh approach to roasting.

York-based kWh Coffee, which runs a fully electric roasting operation, has closed a seed funding round backed entirely by York Angels and secured a grant from the Mayor of York and North Yorkshire’s Great Ideas programme.

As part of its growth plans, kWh Coffee has appointed Laurence Beardmore as chair.

The funding will support the launch of a new end-to-end coffee platform and a roasting-as-a-service model designed to improve transparency for businesses, a company spokesperson said.




Caitlin Walker, co-founder of kWh Coffee, said: "We're delighted to have York Angels behind us and really grateful for their confidence in what we're building.

"Most businesses have no idea what their coffee costs at each step or what it emits, and we think there's a huge opportunity to hand that over.

"Having Laurence join as Chair, with everything he learned scaling York Coffee Emporium, is a massive boost as we take the platform to market."

![]()First - KWh Coffe Roast van at the Askham Bar Park and Ride on the outskirts of York where First bus operates *(Image: © Glen Minikin)*

The platform will allow businesses to compare green coffees online, track every cost from farm to cup, access per-kilo Scope 3 emissions data, and benefit from clear, upfront pricing.

It will launch next month with a pilot group of businesses in Yorkshire and London.

A kWh spokesperson said Mr Beardmore previously grew York Coffee Emporium to a seven-figure business before its sale to the Upton Group and is a former president of the York & North Yorkshire Chamber of Commerce.

![]()The Bellwether Coffee Roaster *(Image: Contributed)*

Mr Beardmore said: "I am thrilled to take on the role of Chair at kWh Coffee Ltd at such a pivotal moment in the company’s journey.

"Securing this startup funding from York Angels is a strong vote of confidence in our vision and team.

"kWh Coffee represents a truly innovative startup—one that has the potential to introduce the next generation of fresh ideas and disruptive offerings to the coffee industry.

"I look forward to working closely with the founders and our investors to accelerate our growth and drive meaningful innovation in the market."

Founded in 2026 by Ms Walker and Shaun Feakins, kWh Coffee roasts speciality coffee using electricity at the Raylor Centre in York.

The company times roasting batches to coincide with the grid’s cleanest half-hours, delivers by electric vehicle, and emphasises ethical sourcing of green coffee.

kWh Coffee already serves up to 750,000 passengers annually through its bar at First Bus’ Askham Bar Park and Ride.

It also supplies wholesale coffee to businesses across Yorkshire and has appeared at industry events including Everything Electric.
