## Major York car dealership introduces two new brands at its city site

The new Jeep Avenger *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Evans Halshaw has expanded its York showroom offering with two new brands.

The company has introduced Jeep and Leapmotor to its Centurion Park site, adding to its existing line-up of Vauxhall, Citroën and Peugeot vehicles.

The expansion strengthens the retailer’s presence in North Yorkshire, creating a broader multi-brand destination for motorists seeking new and used vehicles, servicing and aftersales support.

Jonathon Whitehead, head of business at Evans Halshaw York, said: "It’s fantastic to officially welcome Jeep and Leapmotor into our York store and add two more exciting brands to our diverse offering.

"We already have a strong multi-brand presence here with Vauxhall, Citroën and Peugeot, and adding Jeep and Leapmotor gives new and existing customers even more choice when they come to us, whether they are looking for a new or used car, ICE or EV, and are in need of servicing or aftersales support.

"We’re seeing motorists become increasingly open to exploring new brands and technologies when choosing their next car, and this expansion is a direct response to that customer demand.

"We’re ready to help new and returning customers, whatever their motoring requirement."

![]()The new Leapmotor *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The expanded York site operates alongside Evans Halshaw’s neighbouring MG York store.

This makes York the third retail location for Evans Halshaw Jeep, following existing sites in Hull and Cardiff.

It also becomes the third Evans Halshaw Leapmotor site, joining locations in Hull and Leeds.

Both brands are seeing growing interest from UK motorists.

![]()The showroom exterior *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Leapmotor, which is backed by Stellantis, has experienced rapid growth since entering the UK market in 2025 and offers a range of electric, plug-in hybrid and hybrid vehicles.

The York store will stock Leapmotor’s full range, including the brand’s first hatchback, the new Leapmotor B05.

Customers can also browse Jeep’s expanding line-up, including the Jeep Avenger 4xe and Jeep Compass.

A spokesperson for Evans Halshaw said: "The latest additions form part of Evans Halshaw’s continued expansion of its brand portfolio across the UK, giving customers access to a broader range of vehicles and technologies through its established retail network."

The Evans Halshaw network is part of Lithia UK, one of the country’s largest automotive retail groups.

Lithia UK also owns Stratstone, car parts distributors QuickCo and TPS, and fleet and leasing provider Driveway Vehicle Solutions.

The group operates 150 stores across the UK and represents 36 automotive brands.

Throughout 2026, Lithia UK has continued to grow its national presence through strategic investments and acquisitions, including acquisitions from Hatfields, Group 1, Read Motor Group and Agility Fleet.

More information is available at www.evanshalshaw.com.
