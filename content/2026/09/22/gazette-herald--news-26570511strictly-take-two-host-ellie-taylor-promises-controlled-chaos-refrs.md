Ellie Taylor is bringing a "really gossipy" twist to Strictly: It Takes Two.

The comedian and presenter, who competed on Strictly Come Dancing in 2022, is taking over the BBC spinoff show following the departure of former hosts Fleur East and Janette Manrara.

Ms Taylor told The Sun’s TVBiz column that viewers can expect a livelier, more candid format.

She said: "You can’t predict what’s going to happen on Strictly.

![]() *(Image: IAN WEST/PA)*

"If something happens and it’s being spoken about in the outside world, we’re here to address it.

"It’ll be really fun to have that licence to make it really gossipy."

Known for her role on Ted Lasso and as host of Bake Off: The Professionals, Ms Taylor said the show will not shy away from the “gossip and the big comments.”

She said: "That will undoubtedly at some point include the judges and their comments and, ‘Oh my God, that score was so mean,’ or, ‘That score’s a bit generous, what were you thinking, Shirley?’

"I’m certainly hoping for a little bit of controlled chaos."

Despite stepping into the shoes of two popular presenters, Ms Taylor said she is trying not to focus on potential criticism.

She said: "In life, some people aren’t going to like you.

"But I hope I can go in there not worrying about that, because if I start, it’s paralysing."

She quipped: "I feel sorry for the production staff who have to manage a Labrador level of energy!"

In addition to changes on It Takes Two, the BBC has announced a new spinoff show for iPlayer.

Strictly: Lights Up will air immediately after the results show on Sunday nights and will be hosted by Abi Clarke and Tyler West.

The programme will feature exclusive reactions from eliminated couples and more behind-the-scenes content.

Sarah James, senior executive producer for BBC Studios, said: "With Strictly's iPlayer audience growing year-on-year, this is the perfect time to announce a range of new exclusives that will bring viewers closer to Strictly than ever before.

"Hosted by the brilliant Abi and Tyler, Lights Up will be a post-results show like no other – taking you straight on to the Strictly dancefloor for all of the immediate reaction that previously remained unseen."

BBC iPlayer will also offer weekly training room episodes, a weekly highlights show, and every single dance from the series.

**Strictly Come Dancing returned with its launch show on Saturday, September 19, with the competition beginning in full on Saturday, September 26.**
