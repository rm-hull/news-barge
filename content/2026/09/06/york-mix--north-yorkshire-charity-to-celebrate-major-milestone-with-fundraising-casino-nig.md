A North Yorkshire charity is getting ready to celebrate a huge fundraising milestone.

Following the success of last year’s event, Lucy’s Legacy will hold its second Bands & Blackjack night.

Guests can expect an evening of live music, roulette, blackjack, entertainment, fundraising and plenty of fun, all while helping Lucy’s Legacy continue its work supporting families across the local area.

Lucy’s Legacy was created in honour of Lucy Pearson, a farmer’s wife from the Ryedale area, who sadly lost her life in September 2024 after suffering with postnatal depression, leaving behind her husband and two young children.

In collaboration with Scarborough, Whitby and Ryedale Mind, Lucy’s friends and family set up Lucy’s Legacy, with the aim of raising awareness and funds to support those struggling with maternal mental health issues.

Thanks to the incredible support of the local community, Lucy’s Legacy is approaching £50,000 raised, and Bands & Blackjack will be a chance to celebrate that achievement while raising even more money for this important cause.

The team are hoping people will come along, enjoy a fantastic night out and help them reach the £50,000 milestone.
