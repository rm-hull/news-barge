A BRADFORD mum has hailed Bible study as a key ingredient in elevating her son's confidence and poetry-writing.

Blessing Mandikate, 14, who was diagnosed with autism and ADHD, saw his work featured in the Bradford PHAB anthology Your Voice Matters and read aloud at the Bradford PHAB Festival.

The festival, held last Monday at East Bierley Cricket Club, aimed to celebrate the achievements of people of all abilities.

## 'This is so much more than a poem being published'

  Blessing Mandikate 

*(Image: Rudo Chengeta)*

Blessing’s mother, Rudo Chengeta, said: "It is a testimony of growth, perseverance and opportunity."

His mum added: "The poem is a thoughtful reflection on identity, the people who shape our lives and the gift of life itself".

She believes the themes reflect Blessing's own path to self-expression.

  Blessing Mandikate with family and friends 

*(Image: Rudo Chengeta)*

One of the key influences in his communication development has been his involvement with the Bradford Central Seventh-day Adventist Church, his mum claims.

She told the T&A that he had struggled with speech and communication, but that Bible study had helped his poetry "become centre stage".

Through the church’s Adventurer Bible Experience (ABE) and Pathfinder Bible Experience (PBE), Blessing spent time studying scripture and memorising Bible verses - a process his mother believes played a significant role in his progress.

Ms Chengeta said: "His dedicated study of Scripture helped his speech develop and gave him the confidence to express his thoughts."

Now a student at St Bedes & St Joseph’s school, Blessing is enjoying exploring creative pursuits such as art and poetry.

  Blessing Mandikate's poem 

*(Image: Rudo Chengeta)*

His achievements, Ms Chengeta said, show that a diagnosis does not limit a young person’s future.

She said: "His journey is a powerful reminder that every single person has a voice, a gift and a purpose.

"As his mum, I am incredibly grateful to God for my son Blessing and every person who has encouraged him, believed in him and given him opportunities to be heard."

  Blessing Mandikate 

*(Image: Rudo Chengeta)*

The Bradford PHAB Festival was attended by Dr Manoj Joshi MBE DL, Deputy Lieutenant of West Yorkshire, and Kim Leadbeater, Labour MP for Spen Valley.

His achievement also underlines the role of organisations like Bradford PHAB and Seventh-day Adventist Possibility Ministries, which work to create inclusive environments and support the development of people with disabilities.

## What does the future hold for Blessing?

Blessing's mum explained that he "has several ambitions" including becoming "a professional swimmer, a professional footballer, and an artist".

She added: "He is very creative and enjoys expressing himself through his drawing or poetry artwork, while swimming and football are also activities he is passionate about."
