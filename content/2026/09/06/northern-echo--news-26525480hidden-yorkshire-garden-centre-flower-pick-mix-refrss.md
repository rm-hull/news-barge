While summer might be coming to an end, that does not mean your garden can’t still look lovely throughout the autumn and winter.

Visiting a garden centre has become a popular day out for families and keen gardeners who want to pick up some plants to decorate their gardens with.

If you are visiting the Yorkshire coast any time soon, you might want to make a stop on the way back at the Reighton Nurseries.

The nine-acre nursery offers a huge range of plants to brighten up your garden for the autumn and winter.

## What is the Reighton Nurseries like?

Reighton Nurseries was recently visited by content creator, Off The Map Yorkshire, who showed off the colourful plant nursery .

The video’s caption said: “Come with us to North Yorkshire’s plant and bulb pick & mix! *AD

“Reighton Nurseries, a family-run garden centre just outside of Filey.

“From just £1.10 per plant, select a tray and fill it with a mix of beautiful perennial flowers.

  The garden centre has been running for 60 years. 

*(Image: Reighton Nurseries)*

“At the bulb pick & mix, prices start at only 60p. There’s such a large selection of your spring flowering favourites.

“Make sure you visit Richardsons bakery van for a sandwich or pastry before you leave.

“We didn’t bring Murph with us this time, but they are dog friendly.”

Reighton Nurseries has been running for 60 years and offers “a fantastic range of outdoor bedding, perennials, shrubs and trees at low prices”.


For the autumn, the nursery has brought in a range of bright plants that can survive the colder weather.

You might also want to pick up some clematis or olive trees to add to your garden.

Reighton Nurseries also offers plant pick and mix where you can pick up a tray to buy a range of different flowers and plants for as little as £1.10 per plant, making it an affordable place for your garden essentials.

After walking around the nursery, you might want to visit the Richardsons bakery van for a hot coffee.
