**Joseph Henry Stott**, the driver of a car which tragically collided with** 19-year-old Bailey Chadwick** as he walked home from work near Pateley Bridge in the Yorkshire Dales, has admitted he had been drinking alcohol before he got behind the wheel.

At a trial held at **Harrogate Magistrates’ Court on Thursday 3 September 2026**, the 25-year-old changed his plea to** guilty regarding driving whilst over the prescribed limit for alcohol**.

Significantly, this had been determined by a **forward calculation** due to the defendant not being arrested until four days following the collision. It is the**first time such a conviction has been secured by North Yorkshire Police using the forward calculation method**.

## The Incident and Investigation

The incident happened on the **B6265 at Lupton Bank**, between Pateley Bridge and Glasshouses, during the early hours of** Sunday 20 July 2025**. North Yorkshire Police launched an intensive suspected fatal hit-and-run investigation which led to the arrest of Stott.

Stott denied two other charges of failing to stop at a road accident and failing to report a road accident. This was accepted by the court following the guilty plea for the drink-driving offence. He had previously admitted to driving without insurance.

## Sentencing Details

Following his guilty plea, Stott received the following court penalties:

- **20-month driving ban**
- **Community Order** (12-month supervision order)
- **135 hours of unpaid work**
