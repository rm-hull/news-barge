An electric bike has been seized following reports of anti-social riding.

The Sur-Ron motorcycle was taken by police in Addingham on September 4 after officers identified an address linked to the rider.

West Yorkshire Police said: "Over the past month, our team has received multiple reports of a male riding a Sur-Ron electric motorcycle in an anti-social manner throughout the streets of Addingham.

"Through collaborative efforts with the community, our Neighbourhood Policing Team successfully identified a potential address associated with the offender.

"On the evening of the 4th of September 2026, officers visited the address, located the offender and the Sur-Ron in question.

"The vehicle was subsequently seized.

"We acknowledge that the illegal and anti-social riding of such motor vehicles is a prevalent issue across the force, and our officers are working diligently with local communities to address this concern."
