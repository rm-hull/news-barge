A ROAD in York is set to close for resurfacing works.

Beckfield Lane, in Acomb, will be closed from Monday (September 7) to Tuesday (September 8) between Wetherby Road and Lidgett Grove for the works.

The road is scheduled to close from 8am till 5pm tomorrow and from 9.30am till 3.30pm on September 8.

  


A City of York Council spokesperson said: “An alternative route for diverted vehicles will be signed during the works period via Wetherby Road, The Green, York Road, Carr Lane, Boroughbridge Road.”

The route will be signed – with emergency vehicles and residents still able to access the road.

For further information please contact: [highway.maintenance@york.gov.uk.](mailto:highway.maintenance@york.gov.uk.)
