A North Yorkshire farm has hit the market – for £1.25 million.

Treetops Farm in Stutton, near Tadcaster, has been put on the market.

The property sale includes a five-bedroom family home, a range of livestock and storage buildings, a stone-faced barn and around 17 acres of land. Riverside land is also included, with the farm bordered by Cock Beck along much of its eastern boundary.

  Land included in £1.25 million sale of Treetops Farm in Stutton, near Tadcaster 

*(Image: Savills)*

Andrew Black, rural agency director at Savills York, which is handling the sale, said Treetops Farm is an “unusual offering because it brings together many of the features that buyers increasingly look for but rarely find in one property”.

“It combines a substantial family home, useful farm buildings, productive grassland and extensive land with genuine environmental and conservation interest, all within a highly accessible location close to Tadcaster, York and Leeds,” he said.

“The riverside setting, mature woodland, species-rich habitat and elevated views create a wonderful sense of seclusion, while the acreage also provides practical opportunities for grazing, equestrian use or small-scale farming.

“As the farmhouse is subject to an Agricultural Occupancy Condition, we anticipate interest from buyers with a genuine agricultural, forestry or land-based connection who are looking for a property that offers both a home and a significant landholding.”

The farmhouse, built in 2000, has views across the valley from its elevated setting.

A range of agricultural and equestrian facilities are also included in the sale of the home, “making it well suited to buyers seeking a rural lifestyle within commuting distance of major Yorkshire centres”, according to Savills.

The estate agent added that the stone-faced barn previously had planning consent for conversion to a holiday cottage, “presenting potential opportunities for future adaptation, subject to planning approval”.
