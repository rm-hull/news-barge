## Everton

**Jordan Pickford:** Not the busiest afternoon for the England keeper - might have done better for the goal.**6Merlin Rohl (off 74, Hackney):** Encouraged to get forward more by team-mates but seemed wary of leaving too much space behind him. Did provide the cross that Thierno Barry should have headed in.**6James Tarkowski:** Long ball straight to Senne Lammens in the second half drew groans from the crowd and a stream of invective from his manager.**6Jarrad Branthwaite:** Strong defensive performance, not afraid to get his body in the way of Man Utd shots and a brilliant tackle on Rashford. Found time to have a go at goal himself at the other end.**8Vitalii Mykolenko:** Found himself in space to create something inside the Man Utd box but failed to make the most of the opportunity. Got behind Bryan Mbeumo at the start of the second but again the outcome was poor.**6Harrison Armstrong:** Cautioned for needlessly dragging Bruno Fernandes round the neck, but a minor blot on an otherwise impressive performance. ## Manchester United

**Senne Lammens:** Made a mess of a routine ball into the box, although he had been impeded by Dewsbury-Hall. Aside from that, usual unflappable self. Saved the shots you would expect.**6Diogo Dalot (off 84, Yoro):** Another fortunate to escape an early yellow card for hauling back Tyrique George. Solid after that though and strong in the tackle.**6Harry Maguire:** Needed treatment for a bloody nose in the opening minutes but recovered and produced usual calm display.
