Two York men caught with more than £110,000 of criminal cash and items of jewellery have both received suspended prison sentences.

Police found bundles of banknotes and jewellery when they carried out drug warrants at the homes of Brian Smith, 49, and Tom Peacock, 33, said Daniel Lee, prosecuting at York Crown Court.

Neither man had declared any income to the taxman in the two years leading up to the raids on January 12, 2023.

Police did not find any drugs and could not say what crimes had produced the cash, he said.

Peacock, of Ryedale Caravan Site, Water Lane, and Smith, of Peterhill Drive, Clifton, both pleaded guilty to having criminal property. Both deny that they were involved in drug offences and that the cash was profits from drug deals. They entered their pleas on the day they were due to stand trial.

All the criminal cash and items were seized by police and the judge ordered them to be confiscated. Peacock had cash and jewellery worth £107,186.32 confiscated and Smith had cash and jewellery worth £10,569 confiscated.

Peacock, who had more than £100,000 in bank notes and cash in his home, was given a 20-month prison sentence suspended for 18 months on condition he does 200 hours’ unpaid work. He had a single conviction for theft from 2011.

His barrister David Outterside said he had only had the cash and items for a short time and knew very little of any criminality connected to them.

Peacock had worked since leaving school, said his barrister. He now had a job as a scrap metal worker earning £500 a week.

Smith, who had just under £10,000 in cash, was given a 10-month prison sentence suspended for 18 months on condition he does 12 days’ rehabilitative activities. The court heard he was unable to do unpaid work.

His barrister Deborah Smithies didn’t give any mitigation after Judge Simon Hickey said he would suspend the prison sentence.
