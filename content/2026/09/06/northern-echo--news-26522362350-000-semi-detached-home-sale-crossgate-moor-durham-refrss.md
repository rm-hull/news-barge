LOCATED in a quiet cul-de-sac within easy walking distance of schools, this semi-detached home in the Crossgate Moor area of Durham has everything a family could need or want, from well-presented and versatile accommodation to an attractive outdoor space.

Its accommodation comprises an attractive lounge with French doors opening into the garden, a feature fireplace with inset gas fire, wooden flooring and double doors through to the dining room.

A spacious room, this has a side window ensuring it remains light and bright plus wooden flooring that continues from the lounge and on into the kitchen next door.

  Packed full of character, this semi-detached home is within easy reach of Durham city centre 

*(Image: JW WOOD)*

Recently refitted, the kitchen/breakfast room is a standout feature of the property that benefits from natural light flooding into it via French doors to the garden and Velux windows.

It is fitted with a range of modern units and has a recently installed range cooker with extractor fan over and a central island incorporating a Belfast sink that separates the kitchen area from an adjoining dining area.

A separate utility room provides plumbing and space for a washing machine and tumble dryer, space for a freestanding fridge and freezer, and a side door to the garden.

A cloakroom completes the ground floor accommodation.

  Packed full of character, this semi-detached home is within easy reach of Durham city centre 

*(Image: JW WOOD)*

Arranged around the first floor landing are the family bathroom and three bedrooms, while upstairs again is a spacious attic room enhanced by Velux windows to the front and a dormer window to the rear from where it enjoys attractive views.

This room offers excellent flexibility for a variety of uses.

The property has gardens to both the front and the rear, where an attractive covered decking area and patio provide ideal spaces for outdoor seating and entertaining, complemented by a range of flowering shrubs and planted borders.

*(Image: JW WOOD)*

To the front, a driveway provides off-street parking.

This property in St Bedes Close has good road links to both north and south and is within easy reach of the immediate city centre.
