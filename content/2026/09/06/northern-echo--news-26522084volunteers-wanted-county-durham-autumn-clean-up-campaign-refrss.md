A county-wide clean-up campaign is calling on residents to help tackle litter and keep communities tidy.

The Big Autumn Clean in County Durham is the latest seasonal litter-picking scheme from Durham County Council, following the success of its annual Big Spring Clean.

Cllr Kyle Genner, Durham County Council’s cabinet member for neighbourhoods, environment and police relations, said: "People across County Durham rightly want to live in a clean and tidy environment but, like many places across the UK, litter has become a real issue.

"Recently in County Durham we announced a series of new measures to help tackle the problem, and one of those was the introduction of our Big Autumn Clean.

"We're incredibly heartened by the large number of volunteers and litter picking events we already have lined up, and we'd love for more people to get involved.

"Please get in touch if you'd like to do your bit to keep County Durham clean and tidy."

The campaign began with a beach clean at Crimdon, organised in partnership with Durham Wildlife Trust.

A total of 52 volunteers collected 35 bags of rubbish along with larger items.

The council encourages residents, schools, community groups and businesses to take part.

More information is available at durham.gov.uk/bigautumnclean.
