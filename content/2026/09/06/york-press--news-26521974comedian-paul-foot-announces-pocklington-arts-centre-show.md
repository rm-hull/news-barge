Pioneering alt-comedian Paul Foot is bringing his critically acclaimed stand-up show to Pocklington Arts Centre.

On Saturday, May 22, audiences can expect an evening of laughter as Paul Foot takes the stage at 7.30pm in his show titled "The Future".

The show explores the concept of future expectations, or the lack thereof, in a world where jetpacks, friendly robots, and solar-powered bidets remain a distant dream.

Paul Foot, known for his appearances on "Would I Lie To You?", "Never Mind the Buzzcocks" and "8 Out of 10 Cats Does Countdown", has been described as "Funnier and more daring than I’ve ever seen him" by Chortle.

The show is presented by Plosive Live.

The event is suitable for those aged 14 and above.

The venue is wheelchair accessible, with spaces and companion seats available in the auditorium.

The bar will open an hour before the event, and the auditorium will open 30 minutes before the show starts.

Tickets can be booked by calling the box office on 01759 301 547 and are priced at £17.

Other upcoming events at the venue include Hope & Social on September 24, Rapunzel on September 26, and the PAC Comedy Club on October 1.
