A jewellers is shuttered and boarded up following a ram raid.

Pakistan Jewellers, on Duckworth Lane, was the target of a ram-raid yesterday afternoon (September 5).

Just before 1.30pm, a 4x4 crashed through the front window of the shop.

  The front of the shop was shuttered and boarded up 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

  Debris remained outside the shop 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

  Police tape could be seen on the road outside the shop this morning 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

When the T&A visited the scene just before 9am this morning, debris including wood planks was still visible outside Pakistan Jewellers.

The shutters were down and the window, which was smashed in the raid, appears to be boarded up.

Police tape remained on the road outside.
