## York's popular ghost trail returns with 70 sculptures across the city

A total of 70 sculptures will appear around York as part of Ghosts in the Gardens *(Image: AMY D'AGORNE)*

Ghostly figures will soon haunt city streets once again as part of a popular public art trail.

A total of 70 sculptures will appear around York as part of Ghosts in the Gardens, a free trail running from Saturday, September 19, 2026, to Sunday, November 1, 2026.

Now in its sixth year, the event has become a staple of York’s cultural calendar, and this year’s trail is said to be the largest to date.

Carl Alsop, operations manager at York BID, said: "We know better than to try to keep York’s ghosts away, and this year, more of them than ever will use the city as their playground.

"With 70 ghosts appearing in 32 different locations, this will be our biggest trail yet.

"The trail is completely free, so you can make a day of hunting down all 70 or simply keep your eyes peeled as you wander through the city.

"Just don’t forget to look up.

"Some of our ghosts have a habit of appearing in unusual places."

Ten new sculptures will join returning favourites, with each ghost telling a story from York’s past, representing people, creatures or legends from the city’s history.

Previous figures have included a bear from the Museum Gardens menagerie and a Georgian stonemason inspired by Mary Anne Fisher at Holy Trinity Church.

The sculptures are created by York-based Unconventional Design.

They will be displayed at popular locations including Museum Gardens, Clifford’s Tower, Barley Hall, York Castle Museum, the Merchant Adventurers’ Hall and York Railway Station.

Since it began, the trail has attracted thousands of visitors and encouraged people to explore lesser-known parts of the city.

Organisers say the event offers a fresh way to discover York’s gardens, ruins and hidden corners.

More details about this year’s new ghosts, their stories and locations will be announced soon.

The city’s spectral residents will take centre stage after dark in a special night-time event.

From November 4 to 8, 2026, all 70 ghosts will gather in York Museum Gardens for Ghosts After Dark – five nights of live storytelling, light, smoke and sound.

The ticketed experience will also feature a night market and new spaces to explore.

Ghosts After Dark is delivered in partnership by York BID and York Museums Trust.

Tickets are available now at www.theyorkbid.com/ghosts-after-dark/.

Organisers promise "a few surprises waiting in the shadows" for those brave enough to explore York’s ghostly history after dark.
