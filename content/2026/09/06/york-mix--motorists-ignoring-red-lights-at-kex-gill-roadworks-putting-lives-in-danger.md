The new stretch of the A59 at Kex Gill between Harrogate and Skipton is open but roadworks remain in place with traffic lights and that has caused some pretty long queues this week.

Now there are reports of frustrated drivers jumping the lights.

In an interview for Sunday night’s Late Show at **10pm on YorkMix Radio**, North Yorkshire Council leader Carl Les says they have had to install cameras to crack down on the dangerous manoeuvres.

Cllr Les said: “It would be impossible to to finish off the new road and the two tie-ins without taking the traffic somewhere else, so the traffic has to be taken off those tie-ins onto the new road to get round and that allow the workmen to actually then finish off the the tie-in at at both ends.

“It’s the Harrogate end that is the most complicated. We’re also rebuilding the road that comes down from the left as you’re going towards Kex Gill, which has always been a very steep gradient and a difficult junction because of poor visibility.

“So all that has been taken care of now in the last few weeks of the project. The traffic lights are there, and unfortunately, they are leading to some congestion.

“We have had some very unfortunate incidents of people actually jumping the lights and then meeting the oncoming traffic halfway through the lights, so we’ve put up some more CCTV in to catch people who are actually jumping the lights because that has not been helpful at all.”

Carl Les was asked if opening the new road up to traffic had been premature.

“Doesn’t matter which way the traffic was going, on the old road or the new road, there would still have to be some way of actually transferring the traffic from one to the other.

So there would have still been these traffic lights either way he says. Cllr Les also confirmed what would happen to the old route.

“It’s going to be dug up so that it won’t be a tarmac surface any any longer, but there will be a hard surface there, which will be suitable for walkers, cyclists, and horse riders.”
