A GP practice is already getting into the festive spirit by sponsoring a popular community event.

Fisher Medical Centre, in Skipton, is backing this year’s Great Skipton Santa Fun Run for the second year in a row.

  Staff at Fisher Medical are taking part in the fun run 

*(Image: Fisher Medical)*

Dr Shane Beggan, GP partner, said: "As a long established GP practice in the town, we are delighted to be sponsoring this year’s Santa Fun Run and supporting the event by volunteering and running on the day.

"It’s an amazing event for the town, raising vital funds for local charities and a fantastic way to start the Christmas season."

Staff from the practice will volunteer and take part in the run, taking place in Skipton on Sunday, November 29.

Paul Ellis from Rotary club of Skipton said: "Applications are open now for the 17th Great Santa Fun Run, and it’s a great opportunity to support your local communities."

To register, go to: [https://skiptonsantafunrun.com/](https://skiptonsantafunrun.com/)
