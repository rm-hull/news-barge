**After every round of Premier League matches this season, BBC football pundit Troy Deeney will give you his team and manager of the week.**

Here are this week's choices. Do you agree? Tell us your thoughts using the comments form at the bottom of this page.

![Troy's Team of the Week](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1200/cpsprodpb/88bd/live/21567ed0-aa23-11f1-b109-879e35c24276.png)

![Goalkeeper banner](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/3b60/live/45097c50-00a8-11ef-a9f7-4d961743aa47.jpg)

![Defenders banner](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/2b10/live/551c52c0-00a8-11ef-a9f7-4d961743aa47.jpg)

**Ezri Konsa (Arsenal):** Solid performance. He comes in again and just makes that massive tackle on Morgan Rogers. I think he slots into what is an already incredible Arsenal team. I can see a position now that they just can't live without him, he'll have to play right-back when everybody comes fit and available. 

**Ronald Araujo (Liverpool):** I've given him a bit of stick for his games at Barcelona and his ability to not defend properly, because he's normally the one who's really aggressive. But I have to give it to him, he's been really good for Liverpool and played at right-back in that situation.

**Tyrick Mitchell (Crystal Palace):** Two goals from left-back. He deserves to be in any team of the week. He came off as well, but he's just Mr Consistent. He's never really spoken about enough.

![Midfielders banner](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/287e/live/6522a610-00a8-11ef-8369-47dc4454b972.jpg)

**Harvey Barnes (Newcastle):** Gets a goal. Good finish into that far corner as well. He's been excellent and not spoken about a lot. Anthony Elanga and all the others get the headlines, but Barnes has been fantastic.

**Martin Odegaard (Arsenal):** Captain, leader and a massive winner. It's just one of those moments where you just think, how did they talk about getting rid of Odegaard? It's scary.

**Cody Gakpo (Liverpool):** Two goals created from midfield. Imagine thinking you're playing for Spurs and then for Man City, and then somehow you have to stay at Liverpool and compose yourself, and still perform.

**Cesar Palacios (Fulham) and Vitaly Janelt (Brentford):** Both very similar: solid and get the job done. They don't really get noticed in games unless they score, but if you're watching and you've played the game, you know these two are really important.

![Forwards banner](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/a6df/live/72361f30-00a8-11ef-8369-47dc4454b972.jpg)

**Alexander Isak (Liverpool):** The guy they keep telling you isn't good. They say last year he wasn't good, even though he had two injuries, one being a broken leg. This season, he doesn't score in one game against Newcastle and, 'oh, he's not good enough, he's bottled it'. Just remember, someone tipped him for the Golden Boot.

**Kai Havertz (Arsenal):** The best striker for Arsenal, hands down. They keep telling us that Gyokeres will come good, but Havertz is their best striker. He proves it week in, week out. He's the best footballer they've got, and also their best finisher.

![Manager banner](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/3cb1/live/1110c890-5990-11ef-aebc-6de4d31bf5cd.jpg)

**David Moyes (Everton):** Bad transfer window, loses his attacking options and still delivers a result.
