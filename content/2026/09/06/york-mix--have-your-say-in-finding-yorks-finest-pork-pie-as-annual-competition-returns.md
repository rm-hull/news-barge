Calling all pork pie lovers as the search for the perfect pork pie is back.

The annual competition to find the perfect pork pie is set to return for its fifth year at Bedern Hall – and what better place to host the event as before restoration Bedern Hall was part of William Wright’s Pork Butchers.

The annual competition will take place on **Friday 25 September** at 6pm at Bedern Hall.

In search of the perfect pie, organisers have invited local pie makers to enter their very best pies for you to judge and vote for your favourites.

So expect to be able to sample the pies and then cast your vote – it’s up to you to decide the winner.

Local ales from Yorkshire Heart Brewery will also be available to buy.

Last year, the winner was Dringhouses’s Ged Bell Butchers, who won in both the Traditional and Speciality categories.
