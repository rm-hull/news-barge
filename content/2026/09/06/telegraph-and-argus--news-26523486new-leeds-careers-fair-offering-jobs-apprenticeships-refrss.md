A NEW careers fair is coming to Leeds, promising jobs, training, and a fresh start for job seekers.

The first-ever Unity Careers Fair, organised by Unity Homes and Enterprise, will take place on Tuesday, September 22, from 12pm to 5.30pm at the Ramgarhia Sports Centre on Chapeltown Road.

The free event will feature job opportunities, training programmes, volunteering roles, and careers advice from a variety of employers and agencies.

Sean Kelly, regeneration manager at Unity Homes and Enterprise, said: "This is a fantastic event for anyone seeking work, a change in career, an apprenticeship, training or returning to work.

"We are excited by the quality of employers and trainers who have already signed up with numerous vacancies on offer."

Exhibitors include Leeds United FC, Arriva, Equans, and First Response Group, alongside training providers such as Bradford College and Catch 22.

Cedric Boston, chief executive of Unity Homes and Enterprise, said: "The first Unity Careers Fair is a landmark moment for us as a BME housing association committed to working with local communities to boost their life prospects."

Although entry is free, attendees are encouraged to register in advance via Eventbrite by searching for 'Unity Careers Fair.'
