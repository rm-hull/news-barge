A spa found on the edge of the Yorkshire Dales has been named one of the best in the UK.

With the popularity of spa trips rising, experts at Spaseekers.com have put together a list of the best spas across the UK.

Found at the Coniston Hotel just outside of Skipton, the Nàdarra Spa made it onto the list.

The spa has been described as “wonderful” with “views that are to die for” of the nearby Yorkshire Dales.


## What is the Nàdarra Spa at the Coniston Hotel like?

On the spa, Spaseekers.com said: “Overlooking the Yorkshire Dales, Nàdarra Spa's two outdoor infinity pools offer some of the most scenic sunbathing in the north of England, offering the ideal pampering paradise this summer.

“The Rise and Shine Spa Experience includes a glass of fizz, brunch and two hours' access to the spa, including both infinity pools and the indoor bubble pool.”

Nàdarra Spa has a whole range of facilities from a 15m indoor pool and aromatherapy steam room to a variety of different saunas.

If you want to enjoy views of the surrounding countryside, you can take a dip in one of the outdoor infinity pools.

You can also get a workout in by visiting the fitness suite.

The spa offers a range of packages including spa days and retreats featuring different treatments.

The Blossom Kitchen is available for spa-users to relax while enjoying a meal.

The restaurant “brings together Yorkshire ingredients and Asian flair” with brunch, lunch, and dinner options to choose from.
