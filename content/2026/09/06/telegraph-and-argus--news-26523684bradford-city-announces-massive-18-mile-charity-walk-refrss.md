AN 18-mile charity walk is set to take place in support of mental health.

Walk and Talk with the Bantams, organised by Bradford City AFC, will start and finish at Valley Parade on September 23.

The event will raise funds for Bradford District and Craven Mind, the club’s official mental health partner.

A spokesperson said: "We are fundraising to help them reach more people in our local area who need their vital support.

"Please consider supporting the Club and donating to BDC Mind.

"Bradford District and Craven Mind's mission is to make sure that no one in our community has to face a mental health issue alone."

The route will take walkers through Centenary Square, Saltaire, Five Rise Locks, Shipley Glen, Baildon Moor, Denso Marston Nature Reserve and Lister Park.

Bradford District and Craven Mind supported more than 17,000 people last year.
