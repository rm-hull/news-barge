Emergency services were called to a three-vehicle crash last night (Saturday).

It happened on **Ilkeston Avenue**,** Goole**, at** 11.57pm**.

A Humberside Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Fire service stood by. No further action required. Incident left with police.”
