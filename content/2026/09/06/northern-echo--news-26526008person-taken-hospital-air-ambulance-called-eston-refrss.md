A person has been taken to hospital after the air ambulance was called to a medical incident.

Emergency services were called to a private address in the Burns Road area of Eston this morning.

Police reportedly closed the road as the air ambulance landed on the Eston Recreational Ground nearby.

Three ambulances, a specialist paramedic also attended the scene.

The patient was taken to hospital by road for further treatment.

A North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) spokesperson said, "We received a call at 10.36am this morning (6/9/26) to a medical incident at a private address in the Eston area of Middlesbrough.

"We sent three ambulances, a specialist paramedic and the air ambulance and transported one patient to hospital by road for further treatment."

The Northern Echo has contacted Cleveland Police and the Great North Air Ambulance Service for more information.
