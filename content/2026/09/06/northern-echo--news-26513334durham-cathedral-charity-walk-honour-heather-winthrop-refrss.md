A group of family members and friends will walk 16 miles to Durham Cathedral this month in memory of a woman who spent more than two decades living with cancer.

More than 30 people are expected to take part in the charity walk on Saturday, September 19, raising money for Cancer Connections South Tyneside, a charity which supported Heather Winthrop.

The walkers will set off from Heather's home at 8am before making their way to Durham Cathedral, where they will light a candle in her memory and in memory of all those affected by cancer.

Heather lived with cancer for 21 years and was described by her family as a passionate advocate for cancer care and a source of support for others facing the disease.

She helped raise money for a number of cancer-related causes, including the chemotherapy unit at Queen Elizabeth Hospital, Macmillan and Cancer Connections.

Her family said: "Heather never allowed cancer to define her.

"For 21 years she showed incredible courage, compassion and determination, always finding the strength to support other people even while facing her own challenges.

"Cancer Connections meant so much to Heather because she had experienced their support herself.

"This walk is our way of celebrating her life and continuing something that was incredibly important to her, helping others affected by cancer.

"Lighting the candle at Durham Cathedral will be a very emotional moment.

"It will be for Heather, but also for everyone living with cancer, those who have survived it and those who have lost someone they love."

More than £2,600 has already been raised by 54 supporters, and the family hopes the walk will generate even more donations for the charity.

They are encouraging people to join them for part, or all, of the route.
