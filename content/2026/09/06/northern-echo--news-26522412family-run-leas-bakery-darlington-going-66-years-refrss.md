For 66 years, the smell of freshly baked goods has drawn customers through the doors of a much-loved Darlington bakery where the same family has served generations of shoppers.

The Leas Bakery, on Thompson Street East, is once again in the running to be named one of The Northern Echo’s best bakeries after being nominated by customers.

The family business is now run by Simon Kershaw, who took over from his father six years ago and is continuing a tradition that stretches back three generations.

Simon said: “It’s been going 66 years. I took over from my father six years ago.”

The Kershaw family has long been behind the counter at the independent bakery, which was started by his grandfather, Norman Kershaw, in 1960.

Named after the neighbouring street, The Leas first operated elsewhere in the town before moving to its present location on Thompson Street East.

Simon began learning the trade at an early age, working at the bakery as a child alongside his father and grandfather before eventually taking on the business himself.

Over the years, the bakery has built a loyal customer base, with familiar faces returning for its fresh food, cakes and traditional bakery favourites.

Simon said: “Our bakery is a favourite among the locals. We get a lot of repeat customers, and a lot of regulars.”

The Leas Bakery sells sandwiches and celebration fare, as well as breads, pies, cakes and pastries.

Customers can also choose their favourite sweet treats including cream cakes and seasonal specials.

Simon said: “We do lots of cream cakes, Japanese buns, Eccles cakes. We always use proper fresh cream too.

“We do lots of seasonal specials at Easter and Christmas which are always very popular.”

He added that the support from regular customers meant a great deal to the family.

Simon said: “Our customers are fantastic. Many remember my dad and a few remember my grandad, and have seen me grow up here.

“We have a lot of regulars and it is great to be recognised by them.”

The bakery was crowned The Northern Echo’s Best Bakery in 2024 after readers voted it to the top spot in the competition.

Now, Simon and the team have been nominated again in the 2026 competition, with customers putting the bakery forward for recognition once more.

Simon said: “It feels great to be nominated again; it shows customers like what we’re doing.”
