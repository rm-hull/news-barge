THE UK's first flower carpet will be displayed in Bradford city centre later this month.

The 29-metre-long installation is designed by Bradford artist Shiraaz Ali.

It will be on public display in Centenary Square from September 24 to 27.

Mr Ali explained his inspiration behind the 300,000-flower artwork.

Titled "We Are One", the piece draws inspiration from Bradford’s textile heritage and diverse communities.

Mr Ali said: "The flowers bloom whilst working together to create one living design.

"The flowers are awe inspiring and beautiful yet short lived which I believe gives it meaning.

"Here, is our reminder to live our life to our fullest potential, as the flower's life is beautiful yet short—so is ours.

  The Flower Carpet installation attracts crowds in Belgium, where the event has been a tradition since the 1970s and will make its UK debut in Bradford this September. 

*(Image: Bradford Culture Company)*

"It is this intention that is embodied in the design with each flower having its own unique life yet all coming together as one to become an emergent and greater whole than they could be on their own.

"We are all interconnected through a sacred web with each piece however great or small has an important role to play in the collective."

The design incorporates archival patterns and symbolic motifs, exploring themes of identity, community and connection.
