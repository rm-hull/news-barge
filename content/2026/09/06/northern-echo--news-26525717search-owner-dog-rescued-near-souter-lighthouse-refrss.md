A search is underway for the owner of a dog rescued from the foot of a cliff on a North East beach.

The dog was recovered from the beach cliffside near Souter Lighthouse on September 4 by coastguard rescue teams.

The pooch is believed to be registered to a Seaham address.

  The dog was then taken to the vets in South Shields for a check up 

*(Image: VETS FOR PETS SOUTH SHIELDS)*

Seaham Coastguard, Sunderland Coastguard Rescue Team and South Shields Volunteer Life Brigade (SSVLB) said the dog appeared fit and well before appealing for its owners to come forward.

The dog was taken to Vets for Pets in South Shields following the rescue.

The dog was then handed over to the dog warden.

  The dog straight after he was rescued by a multi-agency rescue team response 

*(Image: SUNDERLAND COASTGUARD AND RESCUE TEAM)*

A spokesperson for Seaham Coastguard said: "Appeal for dog owner, Seaham area.

"Seaham Coastguard, Sunderland Coastguard Rescue Team and South Shields Volunteer Life Brigade (SSVLB) have recovered a dog from the base of the cliff near to Souter Lighthouse, she seems fit and well, but we are trying to locate the owner.

"We believe it is registered to a Seaham address.

"If you have any information can you please comment or message the page"
