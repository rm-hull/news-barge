A NEW cafe is already exceeding its owner's expectations just weeks after opening.

Helen Charlton launched Merienda Coffee in Market Court, Otley in July, offering speciality coffee, cakes, books, gifts and artwork.

Six weeks on, she said customers and neighbouring businesses have given the venture a warm welcome.

"I wasn't sure what to expect," she said.

"I don't know Otley as well as I know places like Headingley or Chapel Allerton, but everyone has been lovely.

  Homemade brownies on display at Merienda Coffee in Otley. 

*(Image: Merienda Coffee)*

## A new addition to Market Court

Ms Charlton said she was drawn to Otley's market-town atmosphere and thriving independent business community, having visited the town regularly through family connections.

"I love a market town," she said.

"To be part of that community and hopefully grow within it is something I'm really looking forward to.

"So far, everybody has been lovely, really nice and supportive."

  Brownies available at Merienda Coffee in Otley's Market Court. 

*(Image: Merienda Coffee)*

## A different offer for busy customers

Named after the term ‘merienda’ meaning light meal or snack, the café has been designed to cater for customers looking for a quick takeaway option, a concept Ms Charlton thought was missing in the town centre.

She said: "There are loads of great sit-down places in Otley, but there wasn't anywhere doing that faster takeaway offer, other than the larger chains.

"You can go into lots of fantastic independent coffee shops and restaurants, but if they're busy, they're understandably prioritising people sitting in.”

Ms Charlton added: "I wanted somewhere that people could either stop and have a chat, browse the books and gifts…somewhere that people could get in and get out if they needed to, while still getting something made with care."

  Locally sourced honey stocked at Merienda Coffee in Market Court, Otley. 

*(Image: Merienda Coffee)*

## Independent businesses backing each other

Rather than seeing neighbouring cafés as competition, Helen believes a strong independent business scene benefits everyone.

"My first reaction was, 'Oh my God, there's lots of other cafés here'," she said.

"But in my experience, having businesses near each other brings people to an area.

"Everybody offers something slightly different. It gives people a reason to come into town and then decide where they'd like to go."

  The coffee counter inside Merienda Coffee, which opened in Otley in July 2026. 

*(Image: Merienda Coffee)*

## Building a business around family life

For Ms Charlton, one of the main motivations behind opening the business was creating a better work-life balance.

She previously ran a hospitality business that involved long hours and seven-day weeks, and said it quickly became ‘all-consuming’.

Ms Charlton explained: "I wanted something that worked around my children…this allows me to be there for the school run and have a bit more balance."

The business currently operates from Wednesday to Saturday; a schedule she hopes will allow it to grow sustainably while fitting around family life.

## 
  **Looking to become part of the community**

While Ms Charlton has ambitions for Merienda to develop over time, she said her immediate goal is simple: creating something that people in Otley value and continue to support.

She said: "I'd love for it to be somewhere people enjoy and want to keep coming back to…I don't have huge expectations.

"I just want it to be consistent, valued and still here in a few years' time."
