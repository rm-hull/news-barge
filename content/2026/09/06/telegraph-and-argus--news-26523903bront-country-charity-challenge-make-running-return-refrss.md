A CHARITY challenge is calling on runners and riders to take on the rugged terrain of Brontë Country.

Set to take place between Haworth and Burnley on Sunday, September 20, the Sue Ryder Brontë Half Marathon, 10k, and Brontë Off Road Bike Challenge 2026 offer participants the chance to support Sue Ryder’s end-of-life care through an active challenge.

And for runners and riders who want to add a little friendly competition to their day, there is also the chance to take part in a unique “heels vs wheels” challenge, with a special app enabling them to compare their performances.

Runners and riders will be able to put their endurance to the test on foot or by bike through the scenic, challenging terrain that inspired the Brontë sisters.

Andrew Wood, senior regional fundraiser for Sue Ryder Manorlands Hospice and Sue Ryder Wheatfields Hospice, said: "The Brontë events are always a fantastic challenge, but what makes the day really special is knowing that every mile ridden or run is helping us support people living with a terminal illness and the people important to them.

"The event has raised an incredible amount over the years, and we’d love to see even more people take on the challenge this year, whether that’s on two wheels or two feet.

"We would like to say a special thank you to Lee Bilbrough from our sponsors Pure Residential Lettings and Keith Binns, who have worked hard to promote the event and come up with the algorithm so the runners and riders can compare their performances."

Funds raised will support Sue Ryder’s palliative and end-of-life care services, including those across West Yorkshire.

The charity also provides online bereavement support, resources, and a bereavement community to help people cope with grief.

Mountain bikers, including e-bike riders, can choose from four Brontë-themed routes: Heathcliffe at 35.6 miles, Charlotte at 26.4 miles, Emily at 22.3 miles, and Anne at 15 miles.

Half marathon runners will cover 13.1 miles on foot, while cyclists on the Charlotte route will travel twice that distance.

All participants will receive a bespoke snood and a hot meal of pie and peas at the finish line.

The event is sponsored by Pure Residential Lettings and All Terrain Cycles.

Over the years, it has raised around £200,000 to support Sue Ryder’s services.

For more information and to register, visit sueryder.org/brontembc for the bike challenge or sueryder.org/brontehalfmarathon for the running events.
