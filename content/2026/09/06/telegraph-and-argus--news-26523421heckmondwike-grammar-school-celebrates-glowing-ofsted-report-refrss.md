A SCHOOL has been praised for its high standards and academic excellence.

Heckmondwike Grammar School received a glowing Ofsted report following a recent inspection, earning the 'Strong Standard' rating in six key areas, including Achievement; Attendance and Behaviour; Curriculum and Teaching; Inclusion; Leadership and Governance; and Personal Development and Wellbeing.

The school’s post-16 provision was judged to meet the expected standard, and all safeguarding requirements were fully met.

Peter Roberts, headteacher at Heckmondwike Grammar School, said: "We are absolutely delighted with this report and immensely proud of our students, staff, governors and wider school community.

"The inspection reflects the culture of high expectations, hard work and care that exists throughout Heckmondwike Grammar School.

"To be recognised as operating at a Strong Standard in six areas is a tremendous achievement, and we understand places the school in approximately the top 10% nationally of schools inspected under the new Ofsted framework.

"We are particularly pleased that inspectors recognised the exceptional quality of teaching, pupils' outstanding attitudes to learning, our inclusive culture and the extensive opportunities available beyond the classroom.

"The report reflects our motto, 'Nil Sine Labore' – Nothing Without Work – and celebrates the commitment and dedication shown by our students every day.

"Additionally, our unofficial motto of ‘work hard and play hard’ was very clearly exemplified by the exceptional work students produce and their immersion in the myriad of extra-curricular activities on offer.

"While we are proud of what has been achieved, we remain committed to continuous improvement and ensuring every student can fulfil their potential."

The Ofsted report described the school’s academic culture as "outstanding," noting that pupils achieve highly and meet the school’s high expectations.

Inspectors said students develop "detailed knowledge over time" and produce "very high-quality work" across subjects, with GCSE results "among some of the best nationally," including for disadvantaged pupils.

The curriculum and teaching were also praised, with inspectors noting: "Teachers have excellent subject knowledge.

"They bring this knowledge to life in classrooms through high-quality teaching."

Pupils were found to be proud of their school, behaving "calmly and respectfully" and showing "high levels of self-discipline."

The report also highlighted the school’s extensive enrichment programme, with more than 100 clubs and activities available to students, many led by pupils themselves.

Ofsted concluded that pupils leave Heckmondwike Grammar School "very well prepared for future education, employment and life beyond school."
