## 'Absolute chaos' - severe traffic along part of York Outer Ring Road

Drivers have been warned of severe traffic along the A1237, into Monks Cross on York's Outer Ring Road - and the A64 *(Image: Google Maps)*

UPDATE: Traffic has since eased.

Drivers have been warned of severe traffic along a major road on York’s outer ring road.

Traffic is building along the A1237 towards the Hopgrove Roundabout and surrounding the Monks Cross Shopping Centre, and extends to the A64.

  


  AA Traffic map showing the heavy traffic around Monks Cross and along the A1237 

*(Image: AA Traffic)*

One commenter said: "Monks Cross and Vanguard at a standstill. Cannot get out.

"I've spent 30 minutes now in a queue. If you don't need to go shopping here...DONT. Hellish."

Another described the traffic around Monks Cross as “absolute chaos”, adding that it “could be a long journey back”.
