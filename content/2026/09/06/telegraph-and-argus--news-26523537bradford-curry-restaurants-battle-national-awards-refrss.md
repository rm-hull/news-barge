FOUR Bradford restaurants have been shortlisted for a national award.

International Restaurant, Café Jadore, My Peshawar and Lala's will compete for top honours at the British Curry & Catering Awards, which celebrate excellence across the curry and catering industry.

The awards ceremony will take place at the Cedar Court Hotel in Bradford on Thursday, September 17, 2026.

A spokesperson for the British Curry & Catering Awards said: "Bradford has always had a special place in Britain's curry story, and it is fantastic to see local restaurants being recognised at this year's awards.

"International Restaurant, Café Jadore, My Peshawar and Lala's are all representing Bradford on a major industry stage, and we want the people of Bradford to celebrate their achievements.

"Being shortlisted is a significant achievement.

"Behind every nomination is a team of people working incredibly hard to provide great food, outstanding hospitality and memorable experiences for their customers."

The event will bring together restaurant owners, chefs, caterers and hospitality professionals from across the UK.

It will also include appearances by special guests and industry figures.

Organisers say the evening is an opportunity to celebrate Bradford’s food culture and the businesses that continue to put the city on the culinary map.

International Restaurant is described as "a highly regarded Bradford name representing the city's reputation for exceptional curry and hospitality".

Café Jadore has earned its nomination for its contribution to Bradford's vibrant restaurant scene.

My Peshawar is celebrated for its distinctive cuisine and strong reputation among diners.

Lala's is another established favourite flying the flag for the city at this year's awards.

This year’s ceremony will begin at 5.30pm and continue into the evening.

The awards cover a range of categories, with winners announced during the ceremony.

Bradford’s strong presence on the shortlist reflects the city's reputation as one of Britain’s curry capitals.

The awards are not only a chance to recognise individual achievement but also to showcase the wider impact of the city’s thriving restaurant scene.

The nominations are seen as a tribute to the hard work, creativity and commitment of those working in Bradford’s hospitality industry.

Winners will be revealed during the ceremony on September 17, 2026.
