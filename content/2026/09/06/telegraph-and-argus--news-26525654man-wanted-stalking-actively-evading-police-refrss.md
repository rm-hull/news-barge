Police are asking for information to help them find a man wanted on suspicion of stalking.

Police want to speak to Daniel Howsam, 40, over an alleged stalking offence involving fear of violence against a woman.

Police believe he is actively evading them.

Howsam was last known to be living in Armley, Leeds.

Police have made several attempts to arrest him, but his whereabouts remain unknown.

Anyone who sees Howsam or has information that could help police find and arrest him should contact the Leeds Domestic Abuse Team via the West Yorkshire Police website’s live chat or by calling 101, quoting crime reference 13260010371.
