A village café on one of Britain's most famous long-distance walking routes is earning glowing reviews from walkers, cyclists and visitors exploring North Yorkshire.

The Rusty Bike Cafe, on Black Horse Lane in Swainby, sits close to the Coast to Coast route as it passes through the western edge of the North York Moors.

The café has a 4.6-star Google rating from almost 500 reviews, while it is also rated 4.6 on TripAdvisor and ranks as Swainby's number one coffee and tea spot on the site.

Its location makes it particularly useful to walkers tackling the Coast to Coast, but visitors say the welcome is as much of an attraction as the food.

One walker stopping there in August described it as a "perfect stop on the Coast to Coast".

They praised the cakes, chai tea and warm atmosphere after encountering difficulties during their walk, adding that the owner had been particularly helpful.

Another recent customer called the café a "lovely surprise", praising its dog-friendly welcome, sandwich and coffee.

  The Rusty Bike Cafe, on Black Horse Lane in Swainby 

*(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

Dogs appear to be regular customers too, with the café frequently praised by visitors travelling with pets.

Inside, the cycling theme is difficult to miss.

The café occupies premises with links to a former bike shop, and bicycles and cycling memorabilia remain part of the interior.

A visitor earlier this year highlighted those reminders of its history while praising its selection of meals, hot and cold drinks and cakes.

Other customers have singled out bacon paninis, cheese scones and sweet treats, while vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free choices are also offered.

Swainby itself adds to the attraction.

The village sits beneath the Cleveland Hills and is a popular starting point for walks into the surrounding countryside, with the Coast to Coast bringing a regular stream of long-distance hikers through the area.

That means The Rusty Bike can serve everyone from people out for a short Sunday walk to those several days into a journey across northern England.

There is indoor and outdoor seating as well as parking, giving visitors somewhere to stop regardless of whether they arrive on foot, by bicycle or by car.

The café is currently listed as opening from 10am until 4pm on Friday through Tuesday, with Wednesdays and Thursdays closed.

For tired walkers emerging from the Cleveland Hills, however, its cakes and warm welcome appear to be making it one of Swainby's most welcome stopping points.
