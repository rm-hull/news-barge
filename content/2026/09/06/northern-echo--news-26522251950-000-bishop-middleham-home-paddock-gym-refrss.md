DESIGNED to embrace its spectacular setting, this bespoke architectural masterpiece is perfect for a discerning buyer in the market for a home that’s not only decidedly different but also offers a wealth of everything from stylish accommodation (which extends to over 4,100sq.ft) to panoramic countryside views, a substantial south-facing garden, extensive entertaining terraces and a private paddock beyond.

Approached as an impressive bungalow from the front elevation, the property reveals its true scale as the land falls away to the rear, creating three levels that maximise the uninterrupted views.

Every principal room has been positioned to capture the ever-changing landscape, offering a home that’s as inspiring as it is luxurious.

Making a grand first impression and offering a glimpse of the quality of finishes that lie beyond is the spacious reception hall with a seating area bathed in light thanks to three windows.

Off this is a spectacular open-plan kitchen, dining and family space that forms the true heart of this home.

Contemporary cabinetry, premium integrated appliances, an oversized central island and generous dining space combine effortlessly, with a large window – with window seat – framing the views.

Positioned separately is an elegant and characterful lounge centred around a media wall and featuring a vaulted ceiling with exposed timber beams and a dramatic full-height glazed gable that floods the room with natural light at the same time as perfectly framing the stunning rural outlook.

Completing this floor is a cloakroom offering internal access into the double garage.

Situated on the floor below are a contemporary family bathroom, a study ideal for use as a home office, a utility room and three spacious double bedrooms.

These include the master bedroom suite – a private sanctuary flooded with natural light and with direct access onto its own private sun terrace overlooking the gardens and countryside beyond.

Complementing the bedroom is a dressing room plus a luxurious en-suite shower room.

Last, but certainly not least, is the ground floor at the heart of which is a spectacular family room designed for entertaining, relaxing or simply spending quality time with family in.

A beautifully crafted integrated kitchenette enhances its functionality, while bifold doors open onto the extensive south-facing terrace, creating a seamless connection between the indoor and outdoor spaces.

Alongside this room are a dedicated gym, a contemporary family bathroom, a cloakroom and two generously proportioned bedrooms, each with French doors onto the patio.

Externally, the property is equally impressive.

Extensive natural stone terraces provide multiple seating and entertaining areas, all positioned to take advantage of the south-facing aspect and uninterrupted countryside views.

Beautifully landscaped gardens create a wonderful extension of the living accommodation, while beyond lies an impressive private paddock, offering an exceptional opportunity for those seeking space, recreation or simply the luxury of owning a substantial piece of countryside.

Situated within the heart of the highly desirable village of Bishop Middleham, this exceptional home offers an unrivalled lifestyle where countryside tranquillity meets an outstanding village community.
