Road users are being warned that a key York road is soon to be hit by roadworks.

Around three miles of the A1079 Hull Road between York Road, Dunnington and the city of York boundary at Kexaay Bridge is to be resurfaced.

It starts next week (Monday 7 September).

Contractors will be working for 14 nights with the road closed between 8pm and 6am. The road will reopen each morning, unless there are “exceptional circumstances”.

The work will be completed in three phases:

**Phase One** of the works will be from the York Road, Dunnington junction up to the Common Road junction, starting on Monday (7 September0 at 8pm and working each night before completing by 6am Saturday 12 September.

**Phase Two** of the works are from the York Road, Dunnington junction to near Kexby Stray junction with the closure point at Dauby Lane to allow for turning traffic. This will be done on the night of Saturday 12 September and will be finished by 6am on Sunday 13 September.

During this phase we will need to completely close the Common Road junction for this one night to enable it to be resurfaced safely. It will reopen at 6am on Sunday 13 September.

**Phase Three** will be from Common Lane junction to the York boundary at Kexby Bridge with the closure point at Long Lane to allow for turning traffic. 

This phase is expected to last for eight nights. This means the road will be closed between 8pm and 6am on Sunday 13 September and finish at 6am on Monday 21 September. The road will reopen each morning, unless there are exceptional circumstances.

**Councillor Kate Ravilious, executive member for transport said:** “This stretch of the A1079 is the latest to receive significant repairs that will make journeys easier for people in the area and across the region.

“We are working to minimise disruption by carrying out these works on nights and working to reopen the road each morning. When the road is closed diversions are in place and we have sent letters to local residents and businesses to help them plan ahead.

“Once complete this will bring smoother and safer journeys for all who use this road and we’re grateful for everyone’s patience while we get this work done”
