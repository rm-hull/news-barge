A new director has been appointed to lead plant and machinery services at a Harrogate-based business.

Tom Witten has joined Pantera Advisory Group in Cardale Park, bringing more than seven years of experience in asset valuation.

He previously worked as a manager at Gordon Brothers in London and as a business asset valuer at John Pye.

Tom Snook, founder and CEO of Pantera, said: "We are really pleased to welcome Tom to the Pantera team.

"His experience in plant and machinery valuation, particularly across restructuring, insolvency and lending, adds further depth to our asset valuation offering."

Mr Witten will be based mainly in Pantera’s London offices and will work alongside managing director Kevin Counihan and the wider asset valuation team.

He said: "Pantera has built a strong reputation in the valuation and advisory market and I’m excited to be joining the business at such an important stage of its growth."
