A pregnant woman has gone missing after leaving hospital prompting an urgent police hunt.

Emma has not been seen since she left the RVI in Newcastle at about 6.45pm on Friday, September 4.

Police believe she is with another named Andrew in the Chester-le-Street area.

Officers said they are getting 'increasingly concerned for Emma's welfare' due to her being pregnant.

She is described as being in her early 20s, around 5ft 2ins tall and of slim build, with brown hair.

She was last seen wearing a green fur-hooded coat, a blue tracksuit top and black leggings.

Andrew is described as being in his early 40s, bald with very short grey hair at the sides and a grey stubbled beard.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "Have you seen missing Emma and Andrew?

"They have not been seen since Friday evening when Emma left the RVI Newcastle at around 6.45pm, and are now believed to be together in the Chester-le-Street area.

"Emma is currently pregnant and officers are becoming increasingly concerned for her welfare.

"She is described as 5ft2, early-20s of slim build with brown hair, and was last seen wearing a green fur-hooded coat with a blue tracksuit top and black leggings.
