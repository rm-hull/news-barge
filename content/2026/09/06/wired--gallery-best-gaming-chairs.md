## Featured in this article

The best gaming chairs will be different from person to person. It boils down to the one that you prefer based on your own priorities and sitting styles, but gaming chairs can sometimes fall into the trap of focusing on gamer aesthetics and gimmicks over comfort. There are, however, a few that avoid this pitfall. As I continue the hunt for gaming chairs that don't suck, here are a few picks I've tested that are a good place to start.

Whether you get in some gaming sessions between work and the gym, or you play from 5 on Friday to 11 on Sunday, we've got a recommendation for you here.

**What Should I Look for in a Gaming Chair?**

The best way to get comfortable and to sit ergonomically is to have the option for a lot of adjustments. You want to be able to tweak and dial in the armrests, seat height and depth, level of lumbar support, and seat tilt and recline. A good chair should feel seamless and supportive. Adequate ventilation and cooling are also important, especially if you game for hours at a time.

**How Does WIRED Test Gaming Chairs?**

By being a degenerate gamer. I play *Path of Exile 2* and*League of Legends* and*Stardew Valley* for hours at a time. I sit in them cross-legged, I rock back and forth, I shove myself away from my desk in them when my teammates start selling. I'm fully eating Hot Cheetos and drinking Baja Blast.

I also work from them most of the time, so they're getting at least 8 hours of testing per day. I use every feature, I adjust every adjustment point, and I assemble them myself unless noted otherwise. Next I'll be testing chairs from Staples, SecretLab, Steelcase, and a few other brands.

**How Does WIRED Decide Which Gaming Chairs to Test?**

I'm prioritizing chairs that are branded as gaming chairs specifically. I'm looking for chairs from trusted brands with unique features that set them apart from office chairs, whether that be through design or a gaming-specific trait.
