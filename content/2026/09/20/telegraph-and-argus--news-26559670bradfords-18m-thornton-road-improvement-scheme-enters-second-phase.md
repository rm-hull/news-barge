The first phase of a major improvement scheme on one of Bradford's busiest roads has now been completed, with the next stage of work underway.

Bradford Council has announced that phase one of the Thornton Road Improvement Scheme has finished, while phase two is now taking place between Whetley Lane and Spring Head Road.

The wider project, which began in July, is expected to take around 18 months to complete and forms part of a major upgrade aimed at improving connections between west Bradford and the city centre.

![]()Works have begun on Thornton Road *(Image: Mike Simmonds)*

In a statement on its Facebook page, the council said: "Thornton Road Improvement works are progressing well, with phase one now complete and phase two underway between Whetley Lane and Spring Head Road."

## What is planned?

According to the council, the scheme will deliver safer pedestrian crossings and junctions, upgraded bus stops, improved walking and cycling routes, new landscaping and better links for people travelling into and out of the city.

Thornton Road will remain open wherever possible while the work is carried out.

The project covers the stretch of Thornton Road between Spring Head Road and Girlington Road and incorporates the West Bradford Cycle Superhighway Phase 2 and the Four Lane Ends Corridor Improvement Scheme.

![]()Works have begun on Thornton Road *(Image: Mike Simmonds)*

Planned improvements include a largely segregated cycle route connected to the existing cycle superhighway, upgraded crossings, changes to improve capacity and safety at Four Lane Ends, resurfacing work, new lighting and drainage, and additional tree planting.

The scheme is being delivered by Bradford Council in partnership with the West Yorkshire Combined Authority and is funded by the UK Government through investment in sustainable transport infrastructure.

Residents can sign up to the Thornton Road Improvements e-newsletter to receive updates on upcoming works, traffic management arrangements and any temporary access changes.
