Sheffield artist Amadora Frogson is the recipient of the Peak District Artisans Young Emerging Artist Award (YEA), which celebrates and supports exceptional creative talent aged 18–30 at the start of their careers

**How do you spend your days?**

My days vary from week to week as my schedule revolves around whichever exhibitions or projects I’m working towards. Alongside painting, there’s lots of behind-the- scenes work involved in running a creative business – from packaging print orders and updating my website, to managing social media and admin.

Right now, most of my time is spent in the studio as I’m working on a new portrait collection for my first solo exhibition, so there’s lots of painting and preparation as the exhibition starts in August.

**Friday night – cocktails or chill?**

I enjoy both – I love catching up with friends over a drink at the pub. But also after a busy week, I enjoy chilling and watching a good film or series.

**Saturday morning – parkrun or papers?**

I’m not much of a runner, but I do enjoy starting the weekend by going to the gym or a yoga class. After that, I’ll often spend the morning exploring the city, discovering new coffee shops or visiting independent retailers and makers markets.

**Feeding time?**

One of my favourite things to do on a weekend is trying new restaurants or going to food markets. I also have go-to restaurants in my hometown of Sheffield that I always love to visit – North Town on Abbeydale Road and Domo in Kelham Island to name just a couple!

**Retail therapy?**

Having recently moved into a new flat, most of my shopping has been focussed around homeware. I love browsing home decor/furniture stores and local markets to find handmade pieces from small businesses. Now that summer is here, I’ve also been looking for balcony furniture and visiting plant shops to create a relaxing outdoor space.

**Saturday night perfection?**

My ideal Saturday evening is spending time with my boyfriend and friends, whether that’s meeting at our local pub, going out for a meal or trying a new bar. I always enjoy making the most of the weekend and spending time outdoors – especially going somewhere to watch the sunset during the summer months.

**Sunday relaxation?**

My perfect Sunday would include going for a walk in nature – whether that’s through our local park or going out to the Peak District. Then relaxing at home, cooking and watching a film.

**Hopes for the year ahead?**

I decided to leave my part-time job at the end of last year so I could focus full time on painting, which feels like a real milestone for me and my business. I’ve got some exciting exhibitions coming up (including my first solo show!) as well as some new collaborations that bring together art and local independent businesses. I’m looking forward to seeing what opportunities this year brings and continuing to grow my creative practice.

I also painted one of the lionesses for this year’s Pride of Yorkshire Trail, to raise money for Sheffield Children’s Hospital Charity. I’m looking forward to seeing the sculptures across South Yorkshire over the summer and to being involved in this project and its upcoming events to support the charity.
