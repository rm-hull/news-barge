The M62 is shut between junctions 22 and 24 due to a “concern for safety” incident involving a person on a bridge.

West Yorkshire Police confirmed that the eastbound carriageway was closed from junction 22 and the westbound from junction 24.

A police spokesman said: "A closure is currently in place on the M62 in both directions between junctions 22 and 24 while emergency services respond to a concern for safety incident involving a person on a bridge.

![]()Traffic on the M62 near junction 25 *(Image: Highways England)*

"The eastbound carriageway is closed from junction 22 and the westbound carriageway is closed from junction 24.

"Drivers are advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes where possible.

"Motorists affected by the closure are asked to show patience while officers work to bring the incident to a safe conclusion."
