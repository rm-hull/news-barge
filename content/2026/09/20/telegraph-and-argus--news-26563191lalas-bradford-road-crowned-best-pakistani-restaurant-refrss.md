A BRADFORD curry restaurant has scooped three major awards.

Lala's Restaurant, located on Bradford Road, was named Best Pakistani Restaurant - North Region at the British Curry Awards.

It also bagged Asian Restaurant of the Year – North of England at the Asian Restaurant Awards 2026 and took home the Outstanding Achievement – Best of Yorkshire Prize at the British Asian Restaurant Awards 2026.

## What makes it so special?

![]()Mr Rashid with previous awards in 2022 *(Image: Mr Rashid)*

Owner Junior Rashid, who was first introduced to the catering world by his dad and grandad, believes his restaurant is "not your average run-of-the-mill curry house."

Mr Rashid puts his success down to the quality of the food, the "second-to-none" service and the elaborate decor.

As much as anything, Mr Rashid said the awards are a tribute to the restaurant’s loyal customers.

He said: "Every single customer is important, we want them to come back."

On Tripadvisor, Lala's has a score of 4.1 out of 5 from more than 600 reviews.

It also holds a 4.5 out of 5 rating on Google from nearly 3,000 reviews.
