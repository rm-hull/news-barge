Vandalism, break-ins and graffiti have prompted plans for beefed-up security measures in York.

Plans lodged with and approved by City of York Council would see two new gates installed on escape routes for the Stonegate Court flats in Blake Street.

An application from Watson Property Management Ltd stated existing gates were in a poor condition, leading to security becoming compromised.

Council planning officers stated the installation of the gates would not harm nearby buildings in Stonegate.

Plans lodged with the council are set to see a single and a double metal gate with rose features installed on escape routes which exit onto Stonegate.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/stonegate-court-york-ldrs-2.jpg)

The application stated the installations were essential to improve the security of residents in the flats.

They stated: “Stonegate Court is a prestigious residential and serviced apartment development.

“The proposal has been devised due to multiple occasions of compromised security where passers by have managed to gain entry or locks have become faulty.

“The existing gates are in a poor condition and have deteriorated.

“They have been subject to vandalism including break-ins and graffiti.”

Council planning officers stated the works were acceptable.

Officers stated: “The application proposed to replace the existing gates due to ongoing security and maintenance issues.

“The proposed replacement gates would appear visually similar to the existing gates, appropriately replicating their details and scale.”
