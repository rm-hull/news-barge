Part of a city centre road will close for six days for sewer maintenance later this month.

Kings Square will be closed from the Goodramgate junction to a point 15 metres south-east from 8am on Monday, September 28, until 6pm on Saturday, October 3, or until works are complete.

The closure is to allow sewer maintenance to be carried out safely.

The one-way traffic direction on Colliergate and Kings Square will be suspended during the closure to allow two-way traffic flow.

A signed diversion will be in place via Church Street, Parliament Street, and Pavement.
