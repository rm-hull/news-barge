Police have received two reports of burglaries, that may be linked, that happened overnight in Drax Village.

The first incident occurred at an address on **Main Road, Drax**.

The occupant awoke to discover that locks from a rear door had been removed, allowing offenders to gain entry to the property.

House and vehicle keys were subsequently stolen. Two sheds on the premises were also entered and a motorcycle was stolen.

The vehicle associated with the stolen keys is still at the property.

The second report was received from an address on **Castle Hill Lane, Drax**.

At approximately 3:45am, dogs at the property began barking aggressively and security lighting was activated. Upon investigation, it was discovered that a conservatory door at the rear had been opened and blinds had been disturbed.

An oil tank door was also found open too. At this stage, it is unknown whether anything has been stolen.

Police enquiries remain in their early stages, and they are appealing for information which may assist the investigation. An officer said: “We would like to hear from anyone who may have CCTV, Ring doorbell, dashcam or other security footage from the area during the overnight period.

“If you have seen any suspicious persons, vehicles or activity in Drax between the late evening of 19 September and the early hours of 20 September, get in touch.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on **101**, quoting the following references:

**NYP-20092026-0128** (Main Road, Drax)

**NYP-20092026-0165** (Castle Hill Lane, Drax)
