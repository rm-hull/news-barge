For millions of film fans around the world, Jamie Bell will always be Billy Elliot - the talented young boy who danced his way from a working-class North East community to international stardom.

Long before the BAFTAs, red carpets and blockbusters, Bell was growing up in Billingham, the Teesside town he still calls home.

![]()Jamie Bell. *(Image: IMDB)*

Born Andrew James Matfin Bell in 1986, the actor was raised by his mother, Eileen, alongside his older sister, Kathryn.

Like Billy Elliot, Bell discovered his passion for dance as a child.

His grandmother, mother, aunt and sister were all dancers, and he took up dancing himself at the age of six after accompanying his sister to lessons.

## Growing up in Billingham

A Teesside town known for its industrial heritage, Billingham has produced sports stars, musicians and entertainers alongside generations of industrial workers.

It was there that Bell started school and first developed his love of performing, before joining Stagecoach Theatre Arts and pursuing an acting career alongside dance.

His working-class upbringing helped him land the role that changed his life.

## The role that made him a star

![]()Jamie Bell as Billy Elliot and Julie Walters as his dance teacher Mrs Wilkinson from the 2000 film Billy Elliot. *(Image: Agency)*

In 1999, Bell beat more than 2,000 boys from across the North East to win the title role in Billy Elliot, director Stephen Daldry's acclaimed coming-of-age drama about a young boy discovering a passion for ballet during the miners' strike.

The film turned him into an overnight star.

At just 14, he won the BAFTA for Best Actor, beating Hollywood heavyweights including Russell Crowe, Tom Hanks, Michael Douglas and Geoffrey Rush. More than two decades on, Bell remains the youngest person ever to win the award.

## From Teesside to Hollywood

Bell moved beyond Billy Elliot in early adulthood, to become one of Britain’s most versatile actors.

His film credits include King Kong, Jumper, Jane Eyre, Snowpiercer, Film Stars Don’t Die in Liverpool, Rocketman and All of Us Strangers.

![]()Jamie Bell as Bernie Taupin with Taron Egerton as Elton John in Rocketman (2019). *(Image: IMDB)*

He also voiced Tintin in Steven Spielberg's The Adventures of Tintin and portrayed Ben Grimm, better known as The Thing, in Fantastic Four.

Film critic Mark Kermode called Bell “unbelievably versatile”, a verdict reflected in the career he built beyond Billy Elliot.

![]()Jamie Bell and Claire Foy in All of us Strangers (2023). *(Image: IMDB)*

## What is Jamie Bell doing now?

![]()Jamie Bell (right) with Richard Gadd (left) in BBC drama Half Man. *(Image: PHOTOGRAPHER:Anne Binckebanck)*

Now 40, Bell is as active as ever in Hollywood.

Recent projects include the fantasy thriller series Shining Girls and award-winning drama All of Us Strangers. His latest work includes Half Man, The Uprising and Rosebush Pruning.

Bell is also rumoured to be lined up for major future projects, including a Peaky Blinders sequel series and a long-discussed Fred Astaire biopic.

![]()Jamie Bell in unnamed upcoming Peaker Blinders sequel series (2026). *(Image: IMDB)*

Away from the screen, Bell divides his time between London and New York and is married to American actress Kate Mara - known for House of Cards, The Martian, Shooter, Brokeback Mountain and American Horror Story.

The couple met while filming Fantastic Four, married in 2017 and now have two children.

Despite spending much of his adult life in the United States, Jamie Bell remains one of Teesside’s greatest success stories.
