A NEWLY restored horror classic is returning to the big screen in 4K.

Pictureville Cinema in Bradford will host the Northern UK premiere of Hammer Films’ 1958 Dracula on October 15 as part of a special Halloween season presented in partnership with Hammer Films.

The screening at Pictureville marks the start of a full programme of horror, including a selection of Hammer classics and cult favourites throughout October.

A spokesperson for the National Science and Media Museum said: "The National Science and Media Museum’s Pictureville Cinema in Bradford is collaborating with Hammer Films for a Halloween season of iconic horror, bringing the studio’s films together with the museum’s own extensive horror collection."

The Dracula screening will include after-hours access to the museum’s Sound and Vision galleries, which house Christopher Lee’s original fangs from the film.

VIP ticket holders will also be offered a behind-the-scenes tour of the museum’s Ashton and Leakey collection, which is not currently on public display.

As part of the wider Halloween programme, Pictureville will also screen Monster Mash: Four Monsters That Changed Horror, curated by writer and film critic Christina Brennan.

This series will feature films such as Frankenstein (1931), The Thing (1982), and Re-Animator (1985).
