A popular gin and cheese rail experience will return to County Durham this autumn after delighting visitors.

Run by Weardale Railway, the Gin & Cheese Train will leave Bishop Auckland West at 1pm on Saturday, October 10, 2026, for a three-hour round trip to Stanhope through the Durham Dales.

Passengers will sample locally made gin and a selection of regional and continental cheeses during the journey.

![]()An array of cheeses on offer at Weardale Railway *(Image: Dan Millard)*

Claire Gibbons, general manager of Weardale Railway, said: "Our Gin & Cheese Train has proved incredibly popular, so we're delighted to be bringing it back for 2026.

"There's something really special about enjoying fantastic locally produced gin and cheese while travelling through the beautiful Weardale countryside.

"It's a wonderfully relaxed way to spend an autumn afternoon and a great opportunity to celebrate a local producer here in the North East.

![]()Guests enjoying Weardale Railway's Gin and Cheese Train *(Image: Dan Millard)*

"Seats are limited, so we'd encourage anyone hoping to join us to book early."

The event is staged with Durham-based WL Distillery, where passengers can sample handcrafted gin and hear about the products and their makers.

Weardale Railway is part of The Auckland Project, a regeneration charity that promotes heritage and creates opportunities through attractions, galleries and gardens across Bishop Auckland and the wider region.

Supported by volunteers, the heritage railway has run services and special events since 2020, including themed experiences throughout the year.

Tickets cost £120 for two people or £240 for four, and must be booked as pairs or groups of four.

More information and bookings can be found on the Weardale Railway website at [https://www.weardale-railway.org.uk/gin-cheese-train.](https://www.weardale-railway.org.uk/gin-cheese-train.)
