A small independent café on the edge of the North York Moors has been recognised by Tripadvisor after earning an almost perfect rating from visitors.

The Feather Trail Café can be found on Castlegate in Helmsley and currently has a score of 4.9 out of five from 119 Tripadvisor reviews.

It has also received a Travellers' Choice award for 2026 and is currently listed among the highest-rated places to eat in the town.

The independent café specialises in breakfast, brunch, homemade cakes and coffee roasted in North Yorkshire, with its menus built around locally sourced ingredients.

![]()The Feather Trail Café can be found on Castlegate in Helmsley *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

But there is one feature which makes The Feather Trail a little different from some of the town's larger cafés.

The business deliberately caters for small groups, with a maximum party size of four, and operates on a first come, first served basis rather than taking bookings.

Its unusual name was inspired by the owners' own walks around Yorkshire.

The café explains that they collected memories from exploring the county's trails, bridleways and footpaths in the form of feathers, eventually inspiring The Feather Trail name.

Visitors have been particularly enthusiastic about its breakfasts, brunches, coffee, hot chocolate and homemade cakes.

One 2026 customer who stopped during a gravel-bike ride through the North York Moors said the café was “worth a detour”, praising its coffee and homemade baking.

Another visitor described receiving a warm welcome with their dog before ordering a full English breakfast, while other recent reviews have praised dishes including poached eggs and bacon on toast.

The Feather Trail currently sits at number one on Tripadvisor's list of cafés in Helmsley, ahead of a number of much longer-established businesses in the market town.

Its location also places it within easy reach of the North York Moors, making it a natural stopping point for walkers, cyclists and visitors exploring Helmsley.

The café is at 3 Castlegate, Helmsley, YO62 5AB.

With its 2026 Travellers' Choice recognition now added to a 4.9 rating, it has become one of the town's highest-rated independent café stops.
