A Chinese restaurant in Spennymoor has reopened under new owners after closing over a poor food hygiene rating.

The Fortune Inn on Durham Road has reopened as Summer Place Chinese Buffet & Bar after securing a five-star food hygiene rating.

The Spennymoor restaurant announced its launch on social media, telling customers: “The wait is finally over.”

In a post, the business said: "We're incredibly excited to announce that Summer Place Chinese Buffet & Bar is opening today.

"Come and join us for a delicious selection of Chinese favourites, chef's specialities, noodle boxes, set dinners and more.

"Whether you're joining us for a family meal, a catch-up with friends or simply fancy some delicious Chinese food, we can't wait to welcome you through our doors."

![]()The Fortune Inn in Spennymoor has reopened under the name Summer Places Chinese Buffet & Bar. *(Image: GOOGLE)*

The reopening follows criticism over the restaurant’s damning one star food hygiene rating in February.

The earlier inspection found rodent droppings, chicken being thawed unsafely and mouldy fridge seals among a series of hygiene failings.

After a reinspection under new management, the revamped restaurant received a new five-star rating.

The new owners have given the venue a fresh identity.

![]()Kyle V Food reviewed the revamped Summer Places (formerly Fortune Inn) in Spennymoor. *(Image: Kyle V Food / FACEBOOK)*

The relaunch has already attracted attention online, including from local food reviewer Kyle V Food, who praised the restaurant’s turnaround.

In a video review, he said: "A little while ago, this place got a bad rap, didn't it? Low hygiene rating - but now it's got a five-star."

After ordering beef in black bean sauce, honey chilli chicken, chicken curry, chow mein and salt-and-pepper chicken, Kyle delivered his verdict on his first meal from the Chinese takeaway since reopening.
