Police have found a woman who was reported missing in Teesside this morning (September 20).

Officers from Cleveland Police were looking for Sarabjit Kaur, 25, who disappeared in Middlesbrough in the early hours of the morning.

In an update, the police force has since said: "Sarabjit, aged 25, from Middlesbrough has now been found safe and well."
