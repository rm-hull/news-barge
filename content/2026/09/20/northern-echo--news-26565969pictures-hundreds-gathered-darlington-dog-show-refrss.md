The Darlington Championship Dog Show returned to Ripon Racecourse this weekend, bringing together hundreds of dogs, owners and handlers from across the country.

Founded in the late 1960s, the show has become a fixture in the dog-showing calendar, attracting competitors across a wide range of pedigree breeds.

Competitors can also win Challenge Certificates, or CCs, which could help a dog in becoming a Kennel Club champion.

The Darlington Dog Show also features stakes classes and Young Kennel Club qualifying competitions for exhibitors hoping to secure places at Crufts dog show.

This weekend (September 18 to September 20), Ripon Racecourse welcomed dogs of all shapes and sizes - ranging from toy breeds and terriers to gundogs, working dogs and utility breeds.

For many owners, stepping into the ring follows months or even years of preparation, grooming and training.

While competition in the ring can be fierce, the show is just as much about bringing together dog lovers from across the UK and beyond, with exhibitors, owners and visitors united by a shared passion for dogs.
