BRAMHAM Park is set to launch its first-ever Christmas market this November.

The event will take place on Saturday, November 14 and Sunday, November 15 at the historic estate, best known as the home of Leeds Festival and the Bramham Park International Horse Trials.

More than 100 artisan traders will gather for the market, which aims to mark the start of the festive season.

James Hamilton-Goddard, commercial director at Bramham Park, said: "We are looking forward to welcoming everyone to The Beginning of Christmas at Bramham Park.

"We are planning a great day out of festive cheer for all the family, filled with a variety of trade stands, music and food.

"Keep following us on social media as there are more announcements to come."

The market will feature independent makers offering gifts and decorations, as well as food stands in the stable yard.

The Yorkshire Rock Choir will perform carols in front of the main house on Saturday from 12pm to 2pm.

Parking is available for £10 per car until October 31, rising to £15 after that date.

Admission to the market is free.

In December, Bramham Park will also offer Christmas house tours, including refreshments in the Old Kitchen.
