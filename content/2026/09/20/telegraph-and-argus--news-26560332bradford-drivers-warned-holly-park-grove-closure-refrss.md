A ROAD in Bradford is set to be temporarily closed for water works.

Holly Park Grove will be shut from its junction with Holly Park Drive to outside No. 6 from October 1.

The closure, beginning at 8am, will last until October 2 at 6pm, although it can be extended if necessary.

The notice was issued by the City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, Section 14, with the closure necessary to allow for water works.

Access for pedestrians will be unaffected throughout the period.

However, vehicles not directly involved in the works will not be allowed on the road.

The council has confirmed that no alternative routes for the affected vehicular traffic will be available during the closure.

The notice, dated September 17, highlights that the closure could potentially last for up to 18 months if required.

Legal and Democratic Services at City Hall, Bradford are handling the closure.
