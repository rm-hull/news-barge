Something sweet is coming to York…

Cheesecake lovers rejoice, as Aly’s Cheesecake have announced on social media that they’re opening a second shop in York.

Aly’s Cheesecake, founded by chef Ali Açıkgül, opened their first location on Gillygate in 2022. They’re known for creating delicious desserts like the San Sebastián style cheesecake, and their variety of creative flavours such as Purple Basil & Blueberry Cheesecake and Turkish Coffee.

And soon, there’s going to be even more chance to taste one of these iconic cheesecakes.

Aly’s Cheesecake have been counting down to the launch of the second shop via social media, but haven’t yet revealed the opening date and exact location. Though eagle-eyed viewers may have spotted that it’s a unit adjacent to the Clifton Moor Retail Park.

They shared on social media that ‘most of our fitting and fixtures are in’, and to ‘keep your eyes on our socials for the full launch countdown, and more sneak peeks of the new shop’.

There’s been a outpouring of positive support on social media, with one person commenting ‘This is so exciting’ and another saying it’s ‘the beautiful results of work and success’.

With four decades of experience, chef Ali has an unending passion for the culinary arts.

Originally from Turkey, he has authored two acclaimed culinary books: his foundational masterclass, Peynirli Lezzetler (Cheesy Delights), has become a respected guide to baking with dairy, while his second book, Unutulmuş Tatlar (Forgotten Flavours), earned 3rd Place at the prestigious 2022 Gourmand World Cookbook Awards.

Chef Ali is perhaps known best for making incredible cheesecake – his own creation the San Sebastian Cheesecake.

Stay tuned for the announcement of the opening date for Aly’s Cheesecake’s second shop!
