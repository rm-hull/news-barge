A popular community-run Hartlepool café has officially reopened in its new home following a summer relocation.

The Dancing Cup reopened on Thursday, September 10, after moving from its long-standing York Road premises to a new unit in Middleton Grange Shopping Centre in Hartlepool.

![]()The Dancing Cup staff at the grand reopening on September 10. *(Image: The Dancing Cup)*

The café announced plans to relocate in June, saying it would close its York Road site for an "exciting chapter" in a "bigger and better" venue.

The owners said the new site would be "not too far" away but kept its location secret.

Now, after a short closure, The Dancing Cup has confirmed it has reopened inside Middleton Grange Shopping Centre, serving a range of breakfasts, lunches and sweet treats.

After opening its doors at 10am on September 10, the café was soon bustling with customers.

![]()The Dancing Cup welcomes customers back after reopening in Middleton Grange Shopping Centre. *(Image: The Dancing Cup)*

On social media, the café wrote: "A very hectic but busy first day. Yes, we have had some teething issues which we have put plans in place to fix these.

"Thank you to everyone who has come to visit us today and for bearing with us during a very busy time."

![]()The Dancing Cup reopens in new Middleton Grange location. *(Image: The Dancing Cup)*

Middleton Grange Shopping Centre also welcomed the business to its new home following the launch, encouraging shoppers to pay a visit.

A spokesperson said: "The wait is over. The Dancing Cup has officially landed at Middleton Grange Shopping Centre.

"Whether you're a coffee enthusiast or simply looking to try something new, this is a must-visit.

"Head down, grab a drink, and help us give them the warm Middleton Grange welcome they deserve."

![]()The Dancing Cup welcomes customers back after reopening in Middleton Grange Shopping Centre. *(Image: The Dancing Cup)*

The relocation marks a new chapter for the independent café, which built a loyal following and a reputation for homemade food, afternoon teas and its community atmosphere on York Road.

Customers flooded social media with support after the move was announced, saying they were eager to see what the larger café would offer.

Bookings are now open at The Dancing Cup's new home for Sunday carveries, afternoon teas and buffet breakfasts, with the team ready to welcome familiar faces and new customers alike.
