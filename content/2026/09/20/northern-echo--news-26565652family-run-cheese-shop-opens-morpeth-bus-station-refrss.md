Buying cheese and catching a bus might not usually go hand in hand, but a new family-run business has brought the two together after opening a shop inside a Northumberland bus station.

It’s All About The Cheese opened its new base at Morpeth Bus Station on September 11, selling a range of local cheeses alongside chutneys, crackers, olives and other accompaniments.

The business has already attracted plenty of attention from shoppers in Morpeth, but cheese lovers in Darlington will also get the chance to sample its products when the team trades at Darlington Market later this month.

![]()It's All About Cheese has opened in Morpeth Bus Station *(Image: It's All About Cheese)*

It’s All About The Cheese will have a stall at the market in Darlington on Saturday, September 26, bringing what it describes as a range of “amazing cheeses”, as well as chutneys, pickles, jams and biscuits.

The Morpeth shop is open from 9am until 3pm on weekdays and from 9am until 5pm on Saturdays, giving commuters and visitors the unusual opportunity to pick up cheese before or after their journey.

Announcing the arrival of the shop, Sanderson Arcade said: “Big news at the bus station! Check out our new family-run cheese store, featuring local cheeses and trundles.

“Grab the perfect gift or treat yourself before your journey home or when you arrive!

“Don’t forget to pick up delicious accompaniments like chutneys, crackers and olives right here. Support local and savour the flavours, cheese lovers.”

Customers have also been quick to praise the new addition.

One person who had already visited said: “The cheese is lovely. We got nettle cheese. I would like to say it is the best cheese I ate.”

Another added: “We love everything about this. It’s going to be a huge success.”

The opening has been a significant moment for the people behind It’s All About The Cheese, who thanked those who had helped them make the move into their new permanent location possible.

In a social media post, the owners said: “So at long last it’s happened.

“Just want to say a massive thanks to Rob Grant, Grant’s Family and Catering Butchers Ltd, Prudhoe.

“Without your help, encouragement, advice and undying friendship I wouldn’t have had the belief to do this.

“You picked me up from when I was lost and showed me the way. I will be forever grateful.”

While Morpeth is now home to the business’s cheese shop, the team continues to take its products on the road.

Its next visit to Darlington Market on September 26 will include cheeses, chutneys, pickles, jams and biscuits, with the business promising “everything you need for that cheese-loving experience”.

The Darlington appearance comes just over two weeks after the Morpeth Bus Station shop opened its doors.
