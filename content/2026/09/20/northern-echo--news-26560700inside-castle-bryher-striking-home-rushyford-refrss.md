Extensively renovated and finished to an exacting standard throughout, Castle Bryher brings together contemporary design, impressive proportions and a spectacular countryside outlook to create a home that really is something special.

Step inside and the property’s spacious entrance hall creates a striking first impression and offers a glimpse of what lies within.

At the heart of Castle Bryher’s accommodation is a generously proportioned and light-filled open-plan kitchen, dining and living space designed around modern family life, entertaining and the remarkable outlook beyond.

![]()Castle Bryher is a rural retreat with a contemporary feel *(Image: Ryan James Estate Agents)*

The stylish kitchen, with a range of fitted units and integrated appliances, is separated from the dining area by a peninsular island.

Large enough to accommodate a family-sized table, the dining area leads into the living room with large sliding doors that both frame the views over the countryside beyond and open directly onto a rear patio, allowing it to be used as a natural extension of the accommodation and the perfect setting for entertaining or alfresco dining during the warmer months.

Completing the ground floor is a shower room and a second reception room which could be used as a more intimate lounge, snug, home office or even as a bedroom.

![]()Castle Bryher is a rural retreat with a contemporary feel *(Image: Ryan James Estate Agents)*

Upstairs are two generous bedrooms – one with a freestanding bath within the bedroom itself plus an en-suite WC, and the principal bedroom which is undoubtedly one of the defining features of the home.

It benefits from an impressive walk-in wardrobe, a luxurious boutique hotel style en-suite bathroom where a freestanding bath takes centre stage, and a private balcony from where there are uninterrupted panoramic views.

The large rear garden is laid mainly to lawn with the aforementioned patio, while to the front is a lawned garden and a substantial double-width driveway that provides off-road parking and access to a double garage.

![]()Castle Bryher is a rural retreat with a contemporary feel *(Image: Ryan James Estate Agents)*

Far more than simply somewhere to park vehicles, this incorporates a dedicated utility area together with a generous office, making it perfect for anyone wanting to work from home.
