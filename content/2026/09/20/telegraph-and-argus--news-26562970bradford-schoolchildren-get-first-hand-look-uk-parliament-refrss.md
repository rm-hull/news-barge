A GROUP of young pupils have taken a front-row seat in British democracy.

Children from Bradford’s Priestley Academy Trust travelled to London for a visit to the Houses of Parliament.

The trip aimed to broaden their understanding of politics and law-making.

Mathew Atkinson, chief executive of the Priestley Academy Trust, said: "We are incredibly proud to offer our pupils this fantastic opportunity.

"Walking the halls of Westminster and seeing where our laws are made is something they will remember for the rest of their lives.

"We want our children to know that these spaces belong to them too, and by raising their aspirations today, we hope to inspire the leaders of tomorrow."

![]()Priestley students visit London *(Image: Supplied)*

Funded in part by the UK Parliament Education Centre, which offers free curriculum-linked workshops and travel subsidies, the visit formed part of the trust’s wider educational programme.

The day began with an early morning train to King’s Cross before a tour of some of London’s most iconic landmarks, including Trafalgar Square, The Mall, Buckingham Palace, and St James’s Park.

Pupils then enjoyed a walk along the Thames and a break at Jubilee Gardens playground before heading to the Palace of Westminster.

There, they took part in a ‘Parts of Parliament: Building a law’ workshop, which explained the journey of a bill through Parliament.

The session, designed for children aged seven to 11, included a guided tour of both the House of Commons and the House of Lords.

![]()Priestley students travel on The Underground *(Image: Supplied)*

Following their time at Parliament, the group walked past Downing Street before returning to King’s Cross for the journey home.

The pupils will now serve as ambassadors within their schools, sharing what they learned and reflecting on their experience at the heart of the UK’s democracy.

![]()Priestley students take in the sights *(Image: Supplied)*

The Priestley Academy Trust is made up of six schools in the Manningham area of Bradford: Atlas Community Primary School, Green Lane Primary School, Lilycroft Primary School, Margaret McMillan Primary School, Miriam Lord Primary School and Westbourne Primary School.

The trust’s mission is “Together, creating opportunity and aspiration for every child.”
