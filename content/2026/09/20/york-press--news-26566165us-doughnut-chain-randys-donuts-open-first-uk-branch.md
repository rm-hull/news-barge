A famous American doughnut chain is set to make its UK debut.

Randy’s Donuts, which opened its first store in Inglewood, Los Angeles in 1952, looks set to launch in Edinburgh.

The brand is known for its giant 30-foot rooftop doughnut sign and its menu of doughnuts, breakfast sandwiches, coffees, and cold drinks.

A planning application has been submitted to Edinburgh City Council for a site on Forrest Road, formerly occupied by Barburrito.

The location is in the heart of the city’s Old Town, close to the University of Edinburgh, the National Museum of Scotland, and the Royal Mile.

The application is for exterior changes, including an illuminated Randy’s Donuts logo.

An opening date has not been confirmed, but the company’s website states the Edinburgh site is "coming soon."

Mark Kalegian said: "We are thrilled to announce that Randy's Donuts is coming to the UK.

"We are confident that out unique brand and delicious doughnuts will be a hit with UK customers as it has been in Asia, the Middle East and the USA."

Randy’s Donuts currently operates more than 30 locations in the US and dozens more worldwide.
