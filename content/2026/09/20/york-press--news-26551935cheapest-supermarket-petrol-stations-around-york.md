The cost of living crisis has a lot of people working hard to budget and save money wherever they can.

Filling up your car with petrol is one of the biggest expenses for most families, with the prices of petrol and diesel varying depending on which petrol station you visit.

To help save you some money without having to waste extra petrol by driving around checking the prices of each petrol station, it is worth looking at which petrol stations are offering the best prices before setting off.

Here are the cheapest supermarkets to buy petrol from in and around York.

![]()It is worth checking the prices before setting off to fill up your car. *(Image: Getty)*

## Cheapest supermarket petrol stations in and around York

The average price of petrol at petrol stations is currently 166.4p and the cost of diesel is 188.5p.

York has 20 different active petrol stations that are either connected to supermarkets or are standalone stations with a small shop.

The cheapest supermarket to get your petrol from is Asda on Jockey Lane with its unleaded costing 159.7p and its diesel costing 180.7p, according to Daily Petrol.

Sainsbury’s, which is also found on Jockey Lane, is the second cheapest spot to fill up your tank with it costing 159.9p for its unleaded and 180.9p for its diesel.

Sainsbury’s also offers super unleaded for 171.9p.

The third cheapest petrol station can be found at the Tesco on Stirling Road, which offers unleaded for 161.9p and diesel for 182.9p.

Tesco also offers super unleaded for 172.9p.

The most expensive petrol station is a Hewson and Robinson Ltd found on Barmby Moor on the way out of York.

Its unleaded costs 169.9p, which is 3.5p above the average, and its diesel costs 193.9p, which is 5.4p above the average.

It might be worth topping up your car in the city centre before heading out on a long trip to save some money.

If you went to Asda in York rather than a petrol station selling petrol for the local average cost, you can save around £3.69 on a full 55-litre tank.
