As the price of a TV licence is rising each year, many people are opting not to have one.

While you are not able to watch BBC channels or watch live TV on any streaming service, you can still keep up to date with lots of popular shows using streaming services like Netflix and Disney+.

If you do not own a TV or do not need a TV licence, there are steps you need to take to confirm this information.

![]()You might still be visited by TV Licensing if you say you do not need a TV licence. *(Image: Getty)*

## Do I need to declare I do not need a TV licence?

If you do not need a TV licence, you must declare this information.

There is a form on the TV Licensing website that you will need to fill out to officially declare you do not need a TV licence.

After filling out the form, you will be sent confirmation of your No Licence Needed declaration.

Sometimes TV Licensing will visit your house to confirm you do not need a TV licence.

You might also continue to receive letters from TV Licensing.

The TV Licensing website says: “If you have recently completed a No Licence Needed declaration and have received a letter from us asking you to buy a TV Licence, please ignore it, as you do not require a TV Licence.”

A No Licence Needed declaration usually lasts for two years meaning you will need to reconfirm you still do not need a TV licence after this time.

TV Licensing says: “Your no licence needed confirmation will let you know how long it will last.

“After this time, we'll contact you to check if the situation remains the same.

“If you made a No Licence Needed declaration at your old address and have moved home, you'll need to let us know if you still don't need a TV Licence.”

If you do not have a TV licence and watch any live TV, you can be fined up to £1,000.

### **Who can get a TV licence for free or a discounted price?**

If you are 75 or older and get Pension Credit or live with your partner who gets Pension Credit, you can get a TV licence for free.

Some people can also get a TV licence for £7.50.

You are eligible for a discounted TV licence if you live in eligible residential care home, supported housing or sheltered accommodation and are retired and over 60 or disabled.

You can get a 50% discount on your TV licence if you are registered blind or live with someone who is.
