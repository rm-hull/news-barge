A BRADFORD teaching assistant is in the running for a national award in recognition of her dedication to supporting pupils.

Michelle O’Brien, who has worked at Blakehill Primary School in Idle, Bradford, for five years, has been nominated for a Teaching Assistant Award ahead of National Teaching Assistants' Day 2026.

Miss O’Brien said: "I have been nominated for a national Teaching Assistant Award, with the winner due to be announced on National Teaching Assistant Day itself.

"I’m a teaching assistant at Blakehill Primary School in Idle, Bradford, where I’ve worked for five years, and supporting children is something I absolutely love.

"I’m particularly proud of the relationships I build with the children in my care.

"I think being a good listener, making children feel heard and understood, and simply being a positive and joyful presence in school can make a huge difference to a child’s day."

In addition to her classroom duties, Miss O’Brien also set up and runs the school’s uniform swap shop.

She said: "It is completely donation-based and allows families to access good-quality, pre-loved school uniform for free whenever they need it throughout the year.

"I organise and run it myself, and it has become a really useful resource for families in our school community."

As a parent of two children at the school, Miss O’Brien said she is passionate about creating a welcoming, supportive environment for families and pupils.

She hopes her nomination will highlight the vital work of teaching assistants everywhere.

Miss O’Brien said: "Although obviously it would be wonderful to win the national award, I’m equally excited about the opportunity to shine a light on teaching assistants and the huge contribution they make in schools every day.

"TAs often work quietly behind the scenes, building relationships with children, supporting their learning and wellbeing, helping families, and providing that extra bit of care and encouragement that can make such a difference.

"I would love the nomination to be an opportunity to celebrate all the teaching assistants across Bradford who do so much for their schools and communities."

The winner will be announced on Friday, September 25.

The award is part of National Teaching Assistants Day, when colleagues and pupils can nominate classroom helpers.
