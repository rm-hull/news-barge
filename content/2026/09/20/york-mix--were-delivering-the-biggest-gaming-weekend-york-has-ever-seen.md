York is preparing to host the biggest tabletop gaming event in the city’s history.

The York Board Games Convention returns for its third year next month, featuring TV stars from *The Traitors*, a 600-game library, extended opening hours, and free entry for under-16s.

Held at the historic St Peter’s School on **Friday 23 to Sunday 25 October**, the 2026 convention promises to be larger, more accessible, and open later than ever before.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ybgc-2026-4.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ybgc-2026-5.webp)

*The Traitors*.

Headlining this year’s convention are special guest stars Ivan Brett and Wilfred Webster, fan favourites from the hit television series *The Traitors*.

With the brand-new season of Celebrity Traitors set to air in a few weeks, attendees will enjoy exclusive behind-the-scenes insights, strategy breakdowns, and opportunities to play alongside two stars who have experienced the nation’s favourite game of social deception firsthand.

The core of the event remains its impressive board games library, which has expanded to feature around 600 titles ranging from family classics to the latest international releases.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ybgc-2026-3.webp)

Visitors can borrow games to play in the vast open gaming hall or explore a packed schedule of dedicated activities, including:

* The Forge: A specialized zone dedicated to tabletop role-playing games (RPGs).
* Blood on the Clocktower: Facilitated sessions of the popular social deduction party game.
* Playtesting Zone: An interactive area where aspiring local game designers showcase prototype
* games for public feedback.
* Bring & Buy Sale: A community market for attendees to buy and sell pre-loved gaming treasures.
* Independent Traders: Stalls featuring independent publishers and creators
* Giant Games: Oversized versions of beloved games.
* Food & Drink: A variety of local food vendors serving refreshments throughout the day

Originally launched in 2024, the convention has rapidly grown into a popular annual event. To meet rising demand, organisers have hired more space and extended evening opening hours until 11pm, giving players even more time to gather around the table.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ybgc-2026-2.webp)

Venue facilities at St Peter’s School offer complete level access throughout, ensuring full mobility support for all visitors, and entry is completely free for children under the age of 16 making it an affordable day out for local families. On-site parking is also available for guests.

“We have been overwhelmed by the incredible response from the York community over the past two years,” said Ryan, one of the event directors at York Board Games Convention.

“For 2026, we wanted to deliver something truly memorable. By extending our hours, bringing in world-class TV guests, and stocking our largest game library to date, we’re delivering the biggest gaming weekend York has ever seen.”

Tickets for the York Board Games Convention start from £25 and are available here.
