An “extraordinary” country home has hit the market in “one of Yorkshire’s most celebrated and sought-after villages” – for £1.395 million.

The five-bedroom barn conversion, called Bishop Barns, is for sale in Bishop Wilton, north of Pocklington.

Carter Jonas, which is marketing the property, said it has “truly unrivalled panoramic views across the Vale of York and the rolling landscapes of the Yorkshire Wolds”.

![]()Inside the five-bedroom home for sale in Bishop Wilton, north of Pocklington, for £1,395,000 *(Image: Carter Jonas/Zoopla)*

The house has around 1.24 acres of landscaped grounds and blends “contemporary luxury and timeless rural elegance”, the estate agent said.

Inside, the property has a collection of living spaces, grand reception areas and a kitchen breakfast room located at the heart of the home.

Two bedrooms, both with en-suite facilities, are on the ground floor.

The main bedroom suite is on the first floor with an en-suite bathroom.

Two more double bedrooms are on the first floor, each with their own en-suite facilities.

A one-bedroom annex is also included in the sale, which Carter Jonas said could provide an “attractive additional income stream alongside excellent potential for guest accommodation or multi-generational living”.

Outside, the home has maintained gardens, extensive external storage areas, generous parking and a double garage.

“The surrounding scenery provides a constantly changing backdrop of extraordinary natural beauty,” a Carter Jonas spokesperson said.

They added: “Rarely does a home of such calibre, setting and distinction come to the market.”
