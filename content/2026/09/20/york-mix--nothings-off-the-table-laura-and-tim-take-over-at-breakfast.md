Rise and shine with Laura and Tim in the morning!

If you’ve been tuning into the YorkMix at breakfast this week, you may have heard a slightly new arrangement.

Our brand new Breakfast duo, **Laura Castle and Tim Lichfield**, officially launched their new show this week.

So, after a first full week of the show, we caught up with Laura and Tim to find out how it’s all been going…

Tune into Laura and Tim on YorkMix Radio, weekdays 6-10am. Listen across York & North Yorkshire on DAB+, on your smart speaker, via the app or online.

## Q&A with Laura & Tim

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/DUO-2-1000x667.png)

**How has the new show been going so far?LC:** It’s going really well. I feel like the show in general has been refreshed because whilst we’ve kept a couple of the favourite features and things, because we’ve got a new presenter in Tim – but also Tim is known to the station already – it’s refreshing, but also familiar, and that’s a nice combination to have.

But the dynamic between me and Tim is new because obviously we’ve never really been on air together before, and that’s been really fun and exciting to to explore and also find out that we do have great like chemistry and banter. So I’ve found it really exciting.

**TL:** I said to Laura this morning, I like the energy that we’ve got, and it does feel fresh.

It feels new, and it does feel exciting, so we just want to have fun. I think that’s the biggest thing for that time of the morning, especially. And it is only our first week, but we’ve had a lot of feedback saying, ‘Oh, it sounds like you’ve known each other for years.’

**What has the feedback been like so far?LC:** The feedback from the listeners has been good. We’ve had a lot of interaction, which has been nice. So we’re happy!

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/LAURA-1-1000x667.png)

**It’s not only a new show, but it’s also a new studio – what’s that like?LC:** My favourite thing about the new studio is that we have a window to the outside world.

I know that sounds ridiculous, but just having a little bit of daylight, which you don’t often get in all studios, it just brings a new bit of energy, particularly for me because you may think I’m a morning person, but I’m not.

**TL:** We see a lot of wildlife, and it’s distracting for Laura.

**LC:** But one of our favourite things about the new studio is the cameras.

**TL**: Yeah, so you get a bit of an insight into what we do. I mean, to be fair, if you watch people do radio, it’s not really that exciting. But we’re trying to add a bit of another element to it.

**So, do you enjoy being on breakfast Tim?**

Do you know what, I am a morning person. My wife not so much – so at the moment we’re in separate rooms just so I can get into the routine and not wake her up. But I enjoy getting up and and getting energised in the morning.

**And there’s some new games alongside the familiar features, isn’t there?LC:** One of the new features is Five Seconds to 500 Pounds – which does what it says on the tin. You have five seconds to name something or name five things to win 500 pounds, which sounds easy and it can be easy if you’re quick off the ball like our winner this week, Richard, who named five legitimate flavours of crisps in five seconds. He smashed it and he won 500 pounds.

But we’re trying to give people a little bit of a heads up as to what they need to research, which is quite nice as well because I think one of the best things about radio is that you want people screaming the answer at the radio, because they’ve done the research the night before so I think that’s brought in a lot more interaction, which is very fun.

**TL**: Yeah, and we want people to win. We don’t want it to be so hard that people just go, ‘Oh, I’m not going to take part in that.’

Another new feature, the Mystery Mix, is also for a little bit of fun. There will be prizes along the way, but it’s not about that really, it’s just about the interaction. So we’re trying to describe something, and it’s through three clues altogether.

The first one is so broad that it could almost be anything, and then the second one we give it a little bit more away. By which time we hope we don’t get to the third clue, but we always give it anyway. And it’s just another interactive thing that people can take part in first thing in the morning.

**LC:** And we’ve kept things like family fortunes, little laughs will be making a return. Just things that the the listeners know and love.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/TIM-2-1000x667.png)

**Anything else you’d like to add?LC:** Please listen to us!

**TL:** I think the biggest thing is it is a new show, give it time to kind of bed in. But a big thing is we want you to be a part of the show so nothing’s off the table. We’d love for you to be part of the show by interacting, whether it’s calls, voice notes, just a text, whatever it is. Just be a part of the show!
