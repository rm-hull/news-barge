A former village pub is up for sale, complete with land for up to eight houses.

What was the Cross Keys Inn, on Cawood Road in Stillingfleet, south of York, is on the market.

It was run as a Sam Smith’s pub for many years. According to Camra (the Campaign for Real Ale): “Prior to closing this was a friendly village pub catering for locals and visitors alike.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/cross-keys-inn-stillingfleet-york-estate-agent-3.webp)

“Well kept Old Brewery Bitter at a very low price, a main bar with real fire and traditional pub games, a separate restaurant for diners and a large beer garden and car park.”

The building is now being marketed by Fleurets, which says the “pub was closed in May 2019 and the majority of trade fixture and fittings have been removed”

It says the two-storey detached property is on “a prominent corner position at the junction of Cawood Road and York Road in the village of Stillingfleet, an attractive commuter village seven miles south of York and six miles north of Selby.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/cross-keys-inn-stillingfleet-york-2021-ii-gsv.webp)

#### Property location

The former pub has two bars, a restaurant and kitchen downstairs and three bedrooms, a large sitting room, kitchen, office and bathroom on the first floor.

As well as the main building, there are single storey sections to the left and rear under tile and flat roof coverings.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/former-cross-keys-inn-stillingfleet-york-estate-agent-1.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/cross-keys-inn-stillingfleet-york-estate-agent-2.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/cross-keys-inn-stillingfleet-york-estate-agent-4.webp)

There’s also a patio area and a car park to the rear for 15-20 cars, a grassed garden area and an additional field to the north side.
