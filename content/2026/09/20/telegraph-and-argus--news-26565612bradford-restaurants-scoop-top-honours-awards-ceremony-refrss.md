Bradford restaurants have scooped top honours at a national awards ceremony.

The British Curry & Catering Awards 2026, held at the Cedar Court Hotel in Bradford, celebrated excellence in the UK’s food and hospitality industry, recognising the best in curry, catering, and hospitality.

Three Bradford restaurants were among the night’s biggest winners.

![]()International Restaurant was named Best Restaurant 2026 at the British Curry & Catering Awards 2026 *(Image: UGC)*

International Restaurant was named Best Restaurant 2026, My Peshawar Restaurant took home Best Karahi Restaurant 2026, and Café J’Adore won Best Restaurant Café 2026.

Lala's, based in Pudsey near the Bradford border, was awarded Best Pakistani Restaurant North Region.

Singer Aidan M hosted the evening, describing it as one of the most prestigious events he had performed at.

![]()Lala's was awarded Best Pakistani Restaurant North Region at the British Curry & Catering Awards 2026 *(Image: UGC)*

A spokesperson for the awards said: "The British Curry & Catering Awards 2026 was a huge success, and it was incredibly rewarding to see so many talented individuals and businesses recognised for their dedication, hard work and contribution to the industry.

"As organisers, Aidan and the entire team are extremely proud of what we created.

"It was a heartfelt and prestigious red-carpet evening, bringing together people from across the curry, catering, hospitality and events industries to celebrate their achievements.

![]()Café J’Adore won Best Restaurant Café 2026 at the British Curry & Catering Awards 2026 *(Image: UGC)*

"Our vision is for the British Curry & Catering Awards to continue growing and become one of the leading events of its kind in the UK, while keeping recognition, celebration and the people behind the industry at the heart of the event.

"Aidan, on behalf of the organising team, would like to congratulate every finalist, winner and guest who helped make the evening so special.

"We are delighted to see Bradford’s incredible hospitality industry receiving the recognition it deserves."

The event, sponsored by The Courts Banqueting Suite, was attended by the Lord Mayor of Bradford and the CEO of Cobra Beer.

The awards covered a wide range of categories, from authentic Indian and Pakistani cuisine to customer service, fine dining, and takeaways.

They also recognised the vital role these businesses play in shaping Bradford’s diverse restaurant scene.

Winners from across the UK were recognised for their contribution to hospitality, while Bradford’s haul underlined the city’s strength in the industry.

Organisers aim to grow the awards into one of the UK’s leading celebrations of curry and catering.

The night also featured the Luxe Global Beauty Contest and Awards, directed by Tushita Roy, with guests and contestants travelling from Sweden and the US.
