A historic church has been modernised to better serve its community.

St Laurence’s Church in Kirby Misperton, a Grade II\* listed building, recently underwent a £400,000 refurbishment project to improve accessibility and support wider community use.

The scheme was funded by the National Heritage Lottery Fund, the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority Capital Projects and Delivery Fund, the Yorkshire Historic Churches Trust, and other charities.

The Bishop of Whitby, the Rt Revd Barry Hill, led a service of dedication and blessing at the church on Sunday, September 6.

The Rt Revd Graham Cray, speaking on behalf of the congregation, said: "We are delighted with the work, and very grateful to Stockill’s, our contractors.

"The church building, which is located alongside Flamingo Land, is now equipped for the future and provides the congregation with increased opportunities to serve both our local community and its many visitors."

Improvements include an accessible entrance path and toilet, a kitchenette, new lighting and heating, and a flexible space at the rear for informal meetings and events.

Additional upgrades feature new louvres in the tower and solar panels with battery storage, installed on the side aisle roof.

The church’s new facilities will allow it to host a wider range of community activities and events, increasing its role as a hub for residents and visitors alike.

St Laurence’s Church is located alongside Flamingo Land and serves both the local community and its many visitors.
