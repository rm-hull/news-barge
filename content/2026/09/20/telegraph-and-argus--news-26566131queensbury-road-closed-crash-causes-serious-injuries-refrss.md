A road remained closed this evening following a serious collision earlier today.

Crash investigation work has been ongoing for much of the day on the A647 Queensbury Road between Queensbury and Boothtown.

Witnesses have described a "very bad" three-car collision resulting in "serious injuries."

The air ambulance was called to the scene, along with police and the fire service.

As of 7.30pm, the road remiend closed both ways from Ploughcroft Lane to Swales Moor Road.

![]()The incident happened on the A647 between Queensbury and Boothtown *(Image: Google Streetview)*

As a knock-on effect, Howcans Lane and Churn Milk Lane are also closed.

Police are yet to release a statement on the incident, but reports suggest that a serious three-vehicle collision took place.

One onlooker, commenting on Facebook, said: "Massive emergency service presence on Queensbury Road this morning. Ambulance, police and fire service all in attendance."

Another observer said: "There was a huge turnout from the emergency services. It must have been a really terrible accident I'm afraid.

"I'm hoping and praying everyone is alright but, with the road being closed for that long, you can't help but fear the worst."

Another Facebook commenter said: "We were one of the first people there just behind it. Very bad three-car RTA with some serious injuries.

"Hats off to emergency services who were there within minutes and in numbers too, and credit to the other people nearby who tried to help before they arrived despite one car badly on fire."
