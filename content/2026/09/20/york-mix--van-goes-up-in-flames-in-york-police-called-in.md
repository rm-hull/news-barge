A van went up in flames in York late last night (Saturday).

A fire crew from Acomb went to the incident on Southfield Crescent, Dringhouses, at** 11.57pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews used to hose reel jets to extinguish the fire within the cab and engine.

#### **Southfield Crescent, York**

“Police were made aware of incident. Crews used a thermal image camera to ensure no further fire spread.”
