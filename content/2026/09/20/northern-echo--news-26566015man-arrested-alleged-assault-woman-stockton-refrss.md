A man has been arrested after a woman was taken to hospital after an alleged assault in Teesside.

Police were called to reports of an assault near the old Debenham's store on Stockton High Street at around 4.50pm on Saturday (September 19).

A Cleveland Police spokesperson said officers attended the scene after receiving reports that a 37-year-old woman had been punched in the face on Saturday afternoon.

Cleveland Police Inspector Anthony Ford said: "At 4.50pm on Saturday 19 September police were called to reports of an assault on High Street in Stockton.

"A man is reported to have punched a 37-year-old woman to the face.

"A man, aged 53, was arrested on suspicion of grievous bodily harm with intent, possession of a bladed article and possession of class A drugs.

"The woman was taken to hospital for treatment.

"He has since been charged in relation to possession of class A drugs and possession of a bladed article and released on conditional bail for the assault."
