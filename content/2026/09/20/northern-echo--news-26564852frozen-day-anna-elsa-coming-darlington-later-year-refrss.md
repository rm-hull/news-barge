A Frozen-themed family day will bring a touch of Arendelle to Darlington later this year.

Hopetown Darlington will host its Frozen Day on Saturday, November 28, with a programme of activities inspired by the hit animated films.

The free event will run from 10am until 4pm and will include appearances by Anna and Elsa inside North Road Station Museum.

Visitors will also be able to take part in snowman-themed crafts in The Stores, pose for photographs and enjoy glitter face art.

A series of Frozen singalong performances will take place throughout the day, led by Claire Willmer at 11am, 12.30pm and 2pm.

Organisers say there will not be a formal meet-and-greet with Anna and Elsa, although visitors may be able to ask for a selfie if they see the characters during the event.

Frozen Day is being organised and hosted by As You Wish and Hopetown Darlington and is not sponsored, endorsed or managed by Disney or any of its affiliates.

Tickets are free but must be booked in advance, either online or in person at the Welcome Desk in the Goods Shed.

People booking online are advised to create an account so they can amend their booking if needed.

The event will take place at Hopetown Darlington on Saturday, November 28, from 10am to 4pm.
