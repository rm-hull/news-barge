GOLF legend Colin Montgomerie has officially opened a new academy designed to inspire the next generation of players.

The Grove Golf Academy at Woodhouse Grove School was launched on Thursday, September 17, with a special visit from Mr Montgomerie, OBE, who led a masterclass and took part in a Q&A session.

James Lockwood, headmaster at Woodhouse Grove, said: "How wonderful to welcome one of my personal sporting heroes, Colin Montgomerie, into school for the official opening of our Grove Golf Academy! Our Academy pupils were certainly inspired.

"The Academy will enable Grove golfers to reach their sporting potential – whether they are aiming to simply enjoy golf on a social level, represent the school, or succeed at the highest levels of performance and competition - both in the UK or across the Atlantic.

"This is a very exciting time to be a pupil at The Grove!"

The academy is the only on-site school golf programme of its kind in the region.

It combines elite coaching with a golf professional, cutting-edge TrackMan technology and an exclusive partnership with Bradford Golf Club.

Students will have the chance to develop their skills through a structured programme designed to support players at all levels.
