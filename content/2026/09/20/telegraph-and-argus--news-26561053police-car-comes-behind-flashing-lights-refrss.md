POLICE have issued advice on what to do if you're signalled to stop by a patrol car.

Police in the Skipton area have reminded drivers that officers are legally authorised to stop vehicles on public roads, and most stops are routine and not a cause for concern.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police (Craven) said: "See blue lights? Stay calm, pull over safely, and let us do the rest.

"Indicate to show you've seen the police vehicle.

"Pull over safely as soon as practical.

"Remain in your vehicle unless instructed otherwise.

"Keep your hands visible, preferably on the steering wheel.

"Lower your driver's window.

"At night, switch on your interior light."

Drivers are also reminded that failing to stop when instructed by police is an offence.

Choosing a safe place to stop helps keep both the driver and the officer safe during the interaction.
