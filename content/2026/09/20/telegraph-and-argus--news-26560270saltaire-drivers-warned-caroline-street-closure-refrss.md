CAROLINE Street in Saltaire will be closed for gas works.

Bradford Council has announced the temporary closure from Albert Road to Victoria Road.

The closure starts at 8am on September 28 and is expected to last until November 13.

Only vehicles working on the gas project will be allowed in the area.

Pedestrians can still use the street.

Drivers will need to follow a different route via the unaffected section of Caroline Street, Victoria Road, Saltaire Road, Bingley Road, Hirst Lane, and Dallam Avenue.

The closure could last up to 18 months if needed.

This order is made under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, section 14.

The closure is due to necessary gas works on or near the road.
