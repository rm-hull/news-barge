Knitters and crocheters are being urged to help bring an underwater adventure to life at a North Yorkshire theatre’s festive pantomime.

The Georgian Theatre Royal in Richmond has launched its latest knitting campaign for this year’s pantomime, The Pantomime Adventure of The Little Mermaid.

Audiences have long played a part, throwing handmade items on stage at a key moment in the show.

![]()The Georgian Theatre Royal in Richmond is calling on knitters and crocheters to help create underwater-themed decorations for its festive pantomime, The Pantomime Adventure of The Little Mermaid *(Image: Supplied)*

Jo Mawhinney, chief executive of the Georgian Theatre Royal, said: "We’re delighted to reveal that this year’s knitted item will be jellyfish.

"Our knitting appeal has become such a special part of the pantomime tradition, and every year we are amazed by the creativity and generosity of the people who get involved.

"Whether you’re an experienced knitter or crocheter or completely new to the craft, we’d love you to join in and help create a whole swarm of jellyfish for our Little Mermaid adventure."

The campaign began in 2011 with a call for knitted bananas for The Adventures of Sinbad. It has since grown into a global tradition, with contributions from across the UK, Australia and New Zealand.

Past pantomimes have featured knitted doughnuts, snowballs, apples, hearts, fish, ice creams and stars, all inspired by the storyline.

The theatre is offering free wool this year after receiving a generous donation.

The wool and printed knitting and crochet patterns are available from the box office on a first-come, first-served basis.

Patterns can also be downloaded from the pantomime page on the Georgian Theatre Royal website.

A theatre spokesperson said: "All shapes, sizes and colours of jellyfish are welcome, so knitters and crocheters are encouraged to get creative."

Completed jellyfish can be dropped off at the box office or posted to The Georgian Theatre Royal, Victoria Road, Richmond, North Yorkshire, DL10 4DW, to arrive by Saturday, November 28, 2026.

The jellyfish will be used during the pantomime before being donated to local nurseries, playgroups and charities for play, decoration and education.

Book tickets from the Georgian Theatre Royal box office on 01748 825252 or online at www.georgiantheatreroyal.co.uk.

For more information and knitting patterns, visit www.georgiantheatreroyal.co.uk/whats-on/pantomime.
