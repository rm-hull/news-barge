Police have been called in after a fire at a North Yorkshire nature reserve today (Sunday).

A crew from Selby were called to the incident in **North Duffield**, between York and Selby, at** 1.17pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “On arrival the crews located a fire measuring 4m by 4m.

“Crews used knapsack sprayers and wildfire tools to extinguish the fire.

“Police were informed due to the cause being believed to be deliberate.”
