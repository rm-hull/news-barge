## Drama in York as Army explode device on Hob Moor

The explosion on Hob Moor *(Image: Submitted)*

Army bomb experts carried out a controlled explosion on Hob Moor and police evacuated houses in Holgate this weekend.

Walkers, cyclists, pet owners and others enjoying themselves on the nature reserve were startled to see the Army arrive and begin preparations for the explosion in the early afternoon of Saturday.

The drama had begun shortly before in Windmill Rise in Holgate when a family were clearing out the house that had belonged to their parents.

The family found what they suspected could be old munitions and decided to call the police.

Police looked at the object and called in Army bomb experts.

Police also ordered the family and others living nearby to evacuate their houses as a safety precaution.

For a time officers prepared to seal off part of nearby Acomb Road.

Army bomb experts arrived, identified the object as a flare and took it to Hob Moor, a distance by car of more than a mile.

There the bomb squad destroyed the object in a controlled explosion.

Police thanked residents for their patience.

The family whose discovery led to the drama said they were reassured that the police had taken the precautions they had.
