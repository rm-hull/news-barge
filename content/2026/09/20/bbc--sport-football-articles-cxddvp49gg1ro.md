**Matheus Cunha's deflected last-minute equaliser sparedManchester Unitedmore misery after Lisandro Martinez's own goal had threatened to giveFulhamvictory at Craven Cottage.**

Here's how I rated the players and you can see how you scored them below.

## Fulham

**Bernd Leno:** On his toes to make an early save from Lisandro Martinez and scrambling when Matheus Cunha's effort looped on to a post. ## Manchester United

**Senne Lammens:** Key first-half saves to keep Manchester United level and came out bravely at Iwobi's feet to prevent another Fulham chance. Distribution could have been better and he didn't cover himself in glory with the Fulham goal.**6Diogo Dalot:** Tidy spin and dash into space to create a chance for Rashford but had too much work to do defensively as he lacked cover far too often.**6Harry Maguire:** Used his experience to draw a foul out of King as United tried to deal with Fulham pressure near their own goal.**7Lisandro Martinez:** Well-struck half-volley drew an excellent save out of Leno.
