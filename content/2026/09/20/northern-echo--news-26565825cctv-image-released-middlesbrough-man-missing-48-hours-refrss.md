Police have released a CCTV image of a Middlesbrough man who has been missing for almost 48 hours as efforts to locate him continue.

Cleveland Police are searching for Christopher Moy, 46, who was last seen at around 4.45pm on Friday (September 18).

Christopher was last seen at the Heron store on High Street in Ormesby, and officers have now issued a CCTV image from the area in a fresh bid to locate him.

Cleveland Police said: "Do you have information that will help officers find Christopher Moy who has been reported missing?"

![]()Christopher Moy, 46, has been missing for almost 48 hours. He was last seen in Ormesby. *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

The force added: "The 46-year-old was last seen at 4.45pm on Friday 18 September at Heron on High Street in Ormesby."

Officers are urging anyone who has seen Christopher or knows where he is to get in touch as soon as possible.

Anyone with information should call Cleveland Police on 101 quoting reference number 187597.
