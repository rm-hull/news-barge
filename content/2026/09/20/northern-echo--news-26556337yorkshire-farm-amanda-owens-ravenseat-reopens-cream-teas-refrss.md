Amanda Owen's famous Ravenseat Farm is welcoming visitors for cream teas again, with daughter Edith helping bring back one of the best-known traditions from Our Yorkshire Farm.

The remote Swaledale farm became familiar to millions through the Channel 5 programme, which followed Amanda, former husband Clive and their nine children as they raised livestock in the Yorkshire Dales.

Ravenseat also became a popular stopping point for walkers and fans, with visitors travelling to the farm for homemade cream teas against the backdrop of the surrounding moors.

That came to an end when the farm closed to visitors in 2023, with Amanda saying cream teas would no longer be served "until further notice".

Now the tradition has returned.

## Ravenseat cream teas return

A new cream tea pop-up is operating at Ravenseat Farm, with Amanda and Clive Owen's daughter Edith involved in running the venture.

The Cream Teas at Ravenseat Instagram account, @cream\_.teas, advertises homemade scones, tea, coffee and cold drinks, with outdoor seating overlooking the Yorkshire Dales.

Parking and toilet facilities are also available.

However, visitors should check the @cream\_.teas Instagram page before making a journey to Ravenseat.

The pop-up is not necessarily open every day, and temporary closures or changes to opening arrangements are announced through its social media account.

That is particularly important for anyone travelling a long distance to the remote Swaledale farm.

The return of cream teas marks a significant change for Ravenseat after several years in which visitors were advised not to travel there for refreshments.

Amanda had announced in 2023 that Ravenseat was closed to visitors "until further notice" and that cream teas were no longer being served.

## Edith Owen takes on Ravenseat tradition

The new venture also represents another step towards independence for Amanda and Clive's children.

Edith is one of their nine children, alongside Raven, Reuben, Miles, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy.

Viewers first saw her growing up at Ravenseat on Our Yorkshire Farm, where all of the Owen children were regularly shown helping with jobs around the farm.

Cream teas have a particularly long connection with the family.

Amanda started serving refreshments to walkers passing Ravenseat years before the family became television stars.

Ravenseat sits alongside the Coast to Coast walking route, meaning large numbers of walkers pass through the remote valley during the warmer months.

Now Edith is helping revive that tradition for a new generation.

## Can you visit Ravenseat Farm now?

Visitors can once again stop at Ravenseat when the cream tea pop-up is open.

However, anyone planning a visit should check @cream\_.teas on Instagram immediately before travelling for the latest opening information.

The business sometimes announces closures or changes to its opening arrangements on social media, so visitors should not assume it will be serving every day.

That is especially important given Ravenseat's isolated location in Upper Swaledale.

The farm also remains a working agricultural property rather than a conventional visitor attraction.

Ravenseat lies near Keld and remains at the centre of the Owen family's farming life.

It became famous through Our Yorkshire Farm, which showed the challenges of raising livestock and a large family in one of the more isolated corners of the Yorkshire Dales.

## What happened to the original Ravenseat cream teas?

For years, cream teas were closely associated with Amanda's life at Ravenseat.

The farm's position on the Coast to Coast route meant walkers regularly passed close by, and serving refreshments provided another source of income alongside traditional hill farming.

But the operation stopped when Ravenseat closed to visitors in 2023.

At the time, Amanda said the farm would remain closed "until further notice".

The arrival of Edith's pop-up therefore restores something many long-time followers of the Owen family will remember from before the closure.

## Ravenseat remains part of the Owen family's future

Much has changed for the family since Our Yorkshire Farm ended.

Amanda and Clive announced their separation in 2022 but have continued farming and working together while raising their children.

The family later returned to television in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, which follows their ambitious restoration of nearby Anty John's farmhouse.

Several of the older Owen children have also begun building careers of their own.
