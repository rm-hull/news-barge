Wayne Rooney could be the next major star to enter the jungle.

The former England striker is reportedly in advanced talks to join the upcoming series of I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out Of Here!, following in the footsteps of his wife, Coleen Rooney, who finished as a runner-up on the show.

According to reports, ITV bosses believe Mr Rooney could be the biggest signing in the show’s history.

## Wayne Rooney could be appearing on I'm A Celebrity

A source told The Sun on Sunday: "Wayne is in advanced talks for I’m A Celebrity and if they secure him, he’ll be their biggest signing in the show’s history."

Mr Rooney is said to be eager to impress his four sons and take on the infamous Bushtucker Trials.

The source said: "Wayne wants to show their sons what their dad can do and has joked he’ll be giving Coleen a run for her money.

"He is really competitive and hates losing so will give I’m A Celebrity his all."

Coleen Rooney has reportedly been preparing her husband for jungle life.

The source continued: "She’ll be backing him all the way and has been giving him hints and tips about life in the jungle."

Known for his blunt humour and quick wit, Mr Rooney is expected to be a hit with viewers.

A friend said: "Wayne is incredibly funny.

"If you watched his punditry during the World Cup, he’s incredibly knowledgeable but he doesn’t mince his words.

"He’s honest and hilarious at the same time.

"Wayne would be brilliant in the line-up."

### **Who else is rumoured to be joining the show?**

A few rumours have started going around about who else could be involved in the new series of I'm A Celebrity.

Other rumoured contestants for this year’s series are Gavin and Stacey actress Joanna Page, and former Paralympian Ade Adepitan.
