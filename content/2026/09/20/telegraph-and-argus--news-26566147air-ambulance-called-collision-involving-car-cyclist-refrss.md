A serious collision involving a car and a cyclist has occurred on a busy road.

The crash happened on the A59 in Skipton at around 5.30pm on Sunday, September 20, prompting a road closure between Broughton and the Gargrave Road roundabout.

North Yorkshire Police said: "The road is expected to remain closed for some time, so please avoid the area and use an alternative route."

The Yorkshire Air Ambulance is believed to have landed on the carriageway in order to gain quick access to the patient.

One residents said they saw the air ambulance flying over Bingley earlier today and said: "The neighbour and I thought it was.... an accident on the bypass or A59."

Anyone with information is asked to call 101, quoting reference NYP-20092026-0389.
