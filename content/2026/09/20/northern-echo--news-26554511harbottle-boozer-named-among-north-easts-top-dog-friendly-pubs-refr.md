A village pub in the heart of rural Northumberland has earned recognition as one of the North East's top five dog-friendly pubs.

The Star Inn in Harbottle made Rover’s regional top five after a nationwide search for the UK’s most welcoming pubs for dogs and their owners.

Rover enlisted a team of expert 'Tavern Testers' to review 110 pubs across the country, assessing venues on criteria including accessibility for dogs, atmosphere, staff communication, canine facilities, pet safety and the overall experience offered to four-legged visitors.

![]()Cosy interior of The Star Inn in Harbottle with a roaring log burner and traditional seating. *(Image: The Star Inn)*

The accolade is likely to come as no surprise to many regulars and visitors to The Star Inn, which sits on the edge of Northumberland National Park and has built a reputation as a welcoming stop for walkers exploring the surrounding countryside with their pets.

TripAdvisor reviews repeatedly highlight the pub's dog-friendly approach.

One visitor, who stayed at the inn during a weekend break with their dog, described it as an ideal destination for pet owners.

The reviewer praised the enclosed patio, comfortable rooms and dog-friendly facilities, including crates, blankets and bowls.

Another recent guest called The Star Inn a “peaceful, dog-friendly gem”, praising its enclosed patios, welcome pack and location for exploring the national park.

The pub's reputation for welcoming canine companions stretches back several years.

Another TripAdvisor reviewer called it a “lovely, warm, dog-friendly pub”, praising its atmosphere, log burner and food.

![]()The Star Inn, Harbottle, has earned a place among the North East's top five dog-friendly pubs following a nationwide assessment of venues welcoming canine visitors. *(Image: Star Inn)*

A separate reviewer called it a “cosy, dog-friendly community pub”, praising the welcoming staff and returning several times during their stay.

Alongside its dog-friendly credentials, The Star Inn has attracted praise for its food, accommodation and role as a community hub, also operating as a village shop and information point for visitors to the national park.

The Star Inn was ranked second in the North East, behind Newcastle's Brandling Villa, which was crowned Britain's most dog-friendly pub after Rover's nationwide review.
