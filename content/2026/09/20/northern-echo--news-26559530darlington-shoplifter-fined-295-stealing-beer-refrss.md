A shoplifter who stole £5 of beer from a Darlington shop with a friend has been fined almost £300 in court.

Piotr Krause and a friend walked out of Surtees News Food and Wine Store with beer after an altercation with staff.

The beer was recovered from the 26-year-old, but it was unclear whether it could still be sold, Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard.

Prosecutor Jade Houston said: “The defendant attended the shop with another male and that male has been in the till area with the alcohol in his hands.

“He has then passed it to this defendant who has left without paying.”

Miss Houston said the defendant received a suspended sentence for possessing a knife, but that offence happened after the shoplifting.

Krause, of Tees Drive, Darlington, pleaded guilty to stealing from a shop on June 14 this year.

His solicitor, Sam Brewster, said the Polish father of two had lived in the UK for seven or eight years.

He said: “He had gone into the shop with his friend who was under the influence of alcohol. There had been no pre-planning to steal before going in.

“The defendant accepts that the beer was passed between him and his friend and there was a confrontation with staff in the shop.

“The defendant was not involved in that but was outside the shop in possession of the beers.
