A MOTORWAY has reopened after a woman was injured when she fell from a bridge.

The incident happened just before 1pm on Sunday on a bridge near junction 22 of the M62.

The woman suffered serious injuries in the fall and the incident forced closure of the motorway in both directions.

![]()The M62 near Junction 22 earlier this afternoon

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "The westbound M62 has now reopened, and the eastbound carriageway has also reopened apart from one lane following a concern for safety incident which had required a full closure in both directions between junctions 22 and 24.

"At 12.57pm today, police received a report of a woman on the wrong side of railings on a bridge on the M62 at junction 22.

"While officers were at the scene, the woman fell from the bridge at 2.51pm.

"She received injuries that are described as serious but are not considered life-threatening and is receiving medical treatment.

"A post-incident procedure has been initiated, which is normal practice for incidents such as this, and a scene remains in place as part of this process.

"We thank motorists for their continued patience while traffic returns to normal in the area."
