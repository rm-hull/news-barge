Drivers and rail passengers are being warned to prepare for months of disruption around Darlington railway station as the final phase of the station's redevelopment project gets underway.

## What is happening?

Roadworks are planed around the station to complete the wider infrastructure linked to the multi-million pound redevelopment.

The work will include road improvements, drainage upgrades, resurfacing roads, changes to footpaths and pavements, and improvements to pedestrian, cycling and bus access.

New bus shelters and pedestrian crossing points will also be installed.

## Where will the work take place?

  CGI of the proposed Victoria Road improvements outside Darlington Station, showing a realigned road layout, upgraded pavements, pedestrian facilities and enhanced public spaces. 

*(Image: Tees Valley Combined Authority)*

The scheme will be split into two areas.

The Eastern Gateway works will focus on Neasham Road, between the station development and the mini-roundabout connecting Yarm Road and Parkgate.

The Western Gateway works will focus on Victoria Road and Park Lane, on the opposite side of the station.

In addition, Victoria Road will be realigned to create more space around the station and help improve roads, pavements and access routes, following demolition work carried out as part of the wider redevelopment.

## When will it start?

  Aerial view of Darlington railway station as major roadworks move closer 

*(Image: Tees Vallley Combined Authority)*

The works are expected to begin in late September or early October, subject to final approval from Darlington Borough Council.

The project is expected to be completed by late spring next year.

## How will traffic be affected?

While the final traffic management plans are still being agreed, Tees Valley Combined Authority has confirmed the scheme will involve temporary traffic lights on Neasham Road, reducing traffic to a single lane at times.

Drivers are being warned to expect delays and allow extra time for journeys.

Rail services are not expected to be affected.

## Why is it needed?

  Tees Valley Mayor Ben Houchen, who says disruption from planned roadworks around Darlington Station is necessary to deliver long-term improvements. 

*(Image: Tom Banks)*

Tees Valley Mayor Ben Houchen said the work was essential to ensure the redeveloped station is fully connected and accessible for people arriving by train, bus, car, bicycle or on foot.

He acknowledged the disruption would be significant, particularly around Neasham Road.

"To finish the work and ultimately deliver something that's going to be vastly improved, unfortunately we're going to have to go through that pain."

The completed scheme is intended to improve access to the station, ease movement around the area and create a better arrival point for passengers using Darlington's upgraded transport hub.
