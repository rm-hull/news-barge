North Yorkshire Police said it closed a main bridge in York following concern for the safety of a teenage girl.

Emergency services were called to Bridge Street at 10.21pm on Monday (September 7).

  


Ouse Bridge was subsequently closed to all traffic, including pedestrians, while police worked to make the scene safe.

Drivers were told to take alternative routes through the city centre.

A force spokesperson said: “It [the incident] was brought to a safe conclusion at 11.15pm and the road reopened soon after.”
