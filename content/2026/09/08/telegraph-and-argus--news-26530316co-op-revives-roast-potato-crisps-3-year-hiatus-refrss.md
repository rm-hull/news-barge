A fan-favourite crisp flavour is set to return to Co-op stores across the UK this month after a three-year hiatus.

Co-op first launched Roast Potato Crisps back in 2019.

The crisps quickly developed a loyal fan base thanks to their "surprisingly bold taste".

They combine sea salt, garlic, rosemary, and black pepper to "capture the taste of a classic Christmas roast potato".

However, in 2023, Roast Potato Crisps disappeared from Co-op shelves, and shoppers have been calling for their return ever since.


One fan commented: "Bring back the Ultimate Roast Potato flavour."

Another added: "Any chance of Roast Potato flavour returning please?"

A third person said: "Where did your roast potato crisps go?... please can we have them back?"

## Roast Potato Crisps to return to Co-op after 3-year hiatus

Now, after three years, Co-op's popular Roast Potato Crisps are set to make a return in a new crinkle-cut style.

Irresistible Ultimate Roast Potato Crinkle Cut Crisps (150g) will be one of three flavours in Co-op's new 2026 Christmas range.


The two other flavours that are a part of Co-ops new festve range are:

- Irresistible Wagyu Beef & Caramelised Onion Flavour Crisps
- Irresistible Pigs in Blankets Crinkle Cut Crisps

The three flavours will be available in 150g bags for £1.75.

  Co-op's popular Roast Potato Crisps haven't been seen in-store since 2023. 

*(Image: Co-op)*

Crisps, snacks and nuts development manager at Co-op, Natalie Dawe, said: "Our crisps have developed something of a cult following over the years, with our customers regularly championing their favourites.

"This year, we're delighted to bring back our Roast Potato Crisps and what I love about them is that the flavour always sparks discussion… it’s much more than meets the eye.

"Combining salt, garlic, rosemary and black pepper to recreate the taste of perfectly seasoned roasties.

"Alongside Pigs in Blankets and our new Wagyu Beef & Caramelised Onion flavour, we're excited to bring that early festive feeling to shoppers."

## When will Co-op's Christmas crisp range land in stores?

Co-op's three Christmas crisp flavours have already begun landing in stores across the UK, ahead of a nationwide rollout on Wednesday (September 9).
