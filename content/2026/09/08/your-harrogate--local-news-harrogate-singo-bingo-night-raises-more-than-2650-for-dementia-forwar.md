A charity Singo Bingo night in Harrogate has raised more than £2,650 for Dementia Forward.

The event was organised by accountancy firm Wild & Co and brought together clients, colleagues and members of the local business community.

Held at Rooster’s Brewing Co, the evening replaced traditional bingo numbers with songs played by host Shane.

The amount raised for Dementia Forward has reached £2,650 and is continuing to rise.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aa011d04021e947f5f.jpg)


The charity provides advice, information, activities and practical support for people living with dementia and their families across North Yorkshire.

Tasha Jacques, managing director of Wild & Co, said:

“We’re absolutely delighted with how the evening went and, most importantly, with the amount that everyone has helped us raise for Dementia Forward.

“It was fantastic to see so many people from our local business community there. We’re very lucky to be part of such a supportive network in Harrogate and people really got behind the event.

“Thank you to everyone who came along, donated and helped make the evening such a success. We hope everyone had as much fun as we did.”


Wild & Co also thanked Rooster’s Brewing Co for hosting the event, Pizza Social for providing the food, Shane for running the entertainment and representatives from Dementia Forward who attended.
