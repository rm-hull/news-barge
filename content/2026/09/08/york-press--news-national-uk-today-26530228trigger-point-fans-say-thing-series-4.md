Trigger Point viewers are all saying the same thing as the fourth episode of the fourth series aired last night (Monday, September 7).

The programme follows Lana Washington (played by Vicky McClure), who heads a Metropolitan Police bomb squad that deals with numerous threats.

Her team has been on the frontline once again as London is "rocked by a wave of devastating bombings."

A synopsis for the fourth series said: "The attackers are the most skilled and dangerous adversaries Lana’s ever faced, and she and her team are at the heart of the race to track them down and prevent further casualties.


"But as the hunt for the bombers intensifies, unsettling cracks begin to appear in the investigation."

As the current series heads into its final two episodes, events have certainly been dramatic, but it appears to have gotten too ridiculous for many viewers.

## Trigger Point viewers all say the same thing as fourth series episodes air

Following Monday night’s episode, many took to social media to share their thoughts, with some calling out what they saw as plot holes and weak writing.

One viewer posted:"Too many plot holes in #triggerpoint or is it just bad writing?"

Another said:"#TriggerPoint must be the worst acting and even worse production, padding out time, & casting lacking also.

"Who signed this drivel off?!"


A third commented: "Did @ITV use AI to write this series of #TriggerPoint?

"It’s got so many plot holes."

Another described the series as "ludicrous nonsense", while a person replying to them said it was "balderdash".

Not everyone agreed, with another viewer posting: "It's brilliant isn't it?!

"I'm only two episodes in but I think it's already better than the last series."

## Where is Trigger Point filmed?

Trigger Point is primarily filmed across London and some parts of Kent.


Some locations in series one include the Bishop's Terrace warehouse in Lambeth, the St George's Wharf area in Vauxhall and the Ark Putney Academy in Putney.

Locations across the other series include the Old Royal Naval College in Greenwich, the Lancaster Estate in Southwark and The Victoria pub in Deptford.
