**North Yorkshire Police** have launched an urgent appeal for information following a sexual assault on**The Stray** in Harrogate over the weekend.

The incident occurred at approximately **8:30 pm on Sunday 6 September 2026** on the section of parkland situated between**Knaresborough Road and Wetherby Road**.

The victim was walking along The Stray when an unknown man approached, sexually assaulted her by touching as he ran past her, and then fled on foot in the direction of Wetherby Road.

## Suspect Description Released

Detectives are appealing for anyone who was in the vicinity at the time or who witnessed the incident to come forward. The suspect is described as:

- **Ethnicity:** White man
- **Build:** Skinny build and tall
- **Clothing:** Wearing shorts, a blue/green running jacket, and a white baseball cap

## How to Help the Investigation

If you have any information that could assist officers, North Yorkshire Police request that you make contact online in the first instance:

- **Email:** Contact PC Sarah Moore directly at [Sarah.Moore@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Sarah.Moore@northyorkshire.police.uk)
- **Online Report:** Visit the North Yorkshire Police website, select ‘Tell us about’, and choose ‘An existing case or report’.
- **Live Chat:** Available via the force website, Monday to Friday, between 9 am and 5 pm.

If you are unable to use the online systems, you can call **101** and ask for**PC 957 Moore**.

To pass information completely anonymously, contact the independent charity Crimestoppers on **0800 555 111** or via their website.

Please quote police reference number **12260175247** when providing details.
