York Disability Week organisers are calling for event proposals to mark the 2026 celebrations.

The week, which brings together York’s disabled community and raises awareness of disability issues, welcomes contributions from both individuals and community groups.

Submissions can be made until October 19 via the York Disability Week website, a shortened link, or by calling 01904 702060.

Celebrating its tenth year, York Disability Week will run from Saturday, November 28 to Saturday, December 5.

The celebration encourages a diverse range of events, including talks, workshops, creative sessions, support and advice sessions, and exhibitions.

Organisers are keen to hear new ideas and are offering small grants to help with access to events and other operating costs.

Participation in York Disability Week 2026 provides local groups with a chance to connect with other organisations, reach new potential members, and promote awareness of disabled people’s experiences and histories.

The event is coordinated by local organisations and volunteers from the York Human Rights City Network.

York Human Rights City is a catalyst for York people, businesses and organisations to champion a vibrant, diverse, fair and safe city.

Organisers hope the 2026 celebration will be another successful year in championing the disabled community.
