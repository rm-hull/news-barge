A FAMILY have paid tribute to a man who died in a Bradford collision - saying he " made the world brighter".

Eugen Matta, 45, was pronounced dead at the scene on Killinghall Road, near Barkerend Road, after reportedly being struck by a black Cupra Leon at around 8.50pm on Thursday, September 3.

His family described him as a “guide, a friend and someone who made the world brighter just by being in it”.

They said: "He had a way of making everyone feel seen, heard, and valued.

"His kindness was quiet but constant, his strength steady, and his laugh something we will all miss deeply."

  Remnants of police tape on a gate opposite Bradford Moor Park 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Two women, aged 19 and 47, who were in the Cupra Leon, were arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

They have been released on bail while enquiries continue.

## Tributes flood in after collision

Tributes from local councillors and the community flooded in for Mr Matta after the fatal collision.

The incident happened near the home of Councillor Zafar Iqbal (Labour, Bradford Moor).

Cllr Iqbal said: “This is an incredibly tragic and heart-breaking incident.

"Behind this tragedy is a family who have lost someone they love, and a community trying to come to terms with what has happened.

"My thoughts are with the man’s family and loved ones at what must be an unimaginably difficult time.

“I also want to pay tribute to the emergency services who responded and did everything they could to help in such difficult circumstances.

“Their dedication and compassion are greatly appreciated.”

Cllr Riaz Ahmed (Lib Dem, Bradford Moor) also expressed his sadness about the tragedy.

He said: "I extend my deepest sympathies and condolences to the family and loved ones of the person who lost their life in the road traffic collision.

"I understand that two people have been arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving.

"I appeal to anyone who witnessed this incident or has dashcam or CCTV footage to get in touch with police.

One resident said: “I heard the police cars and saw the cops.

“It happened at the edge of the road.

“It's very upsetting.”

Another resident said: “I feel sorry for his family.”

One person said: “I didn't see it happen, but it's quite sad.”

## Police appeal for information

West Yorkshire Police’s Major Collision Enquiry Team is appealing for witnesses and anyone who saw the vehicle travelling along Barkerend Road towards Bradford Moor Park around the time of the incident.

Information can be provided via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat or by calling 101 and quoting log reference 1622 of September 3.
