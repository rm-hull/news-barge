M&S is set to reopen a store after a major transformation, with the new foodhall now 60 per cent larger.

The refreshed branch at Guiseley Retail Park will welcome customers back from Wednesday, September 16.

It features a 15,000 sq ft foodhall and more than 1,500 new products.

Serita Thomas, store manager at M&S Guiseley, said: "We’ve had a store here in Guiseley for over 20 years and it’s exciting to be entering a new chapter by delivering a renewed and improved store.

"The extension means we have plenty more space to offer almost the full M&S Food range, making it so much easier for customers to do their weekly shop with us.

"We can’t wait to open the doors next week and showcase the amazing new products we now have in-store."

The expanded store includes a larger in-store bakery and coffee counter, as well as a wider selection of fresh produce sourced from M&S’ Select Farm partners.

More than 1,000 of these partners are based in Yorkshire.

Customers will also find the full M&S Food frozen range, a broader variety of household essentials, and the new Paws & Pals pet food line.

The transformation has created more than 40 new jobs, bringing the total team to over 90.

This includes the existing 50 staff members already working at the site.

Throughout the store, shoppers can find more 'Remarksable Value' and 'Bigger Pack Better Value' products, as well as the latest product launches.

These include gingerbread and white chocolate cookies and new organic options.

M&S has also recently lowered the prices of more than 50 organic items.

These include staples such as carrots, lamb, pasta and potatoes.

The Guiseley upgrade is part of M&S’ wider strategy to double the size of its food business.

Plans include growing its store footprint and expanding existing locations to offer a broader range of products.

M&S aims to be a shopping list retailer for families.

Guiseley is one of several stores being transformed across the UK this financial year.

The programme also includes 18 new food store openings.

It follows the recent renewal of the Anlaby store in Hull in March.
