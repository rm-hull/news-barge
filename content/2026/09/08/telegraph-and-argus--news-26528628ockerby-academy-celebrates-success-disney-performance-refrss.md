Ockerby Academy has marked three months on Bingley High Street after performing at Disneyland Paris.

The Bingley dance school opened its studio on Main Street on May 3.

Owner Daniella Ockerby said: “The studio has been going from strength to strength, we absolutely love our new home on Bingley, Main Street.”

A major event came on June 26, when 27 academy students performed on the Videopolis stage in Discoveryland at Magic Kingdom, Disneyland Paris.

  The Disney performance 

*(Image: Daniella Ockerby)*

Owner Daniella Ockerby said: “It was amazing! We auditioned back in 2025 with a cast of 27 students who all were successful to perform on the Videopolis stage in Discoveryland, Magic Kingdom, Disneyland Paris. There were 85 of us all together it was epic!”

The group’s preparation included choreography, music, costumes and rehearsals.

Daniella said: “The hard work paid off, choreographing, setting, music choices, costuming and rehearsals all paid off and it was truly the most magical 4 days with our dance family ever!”

  Students in the studio 

*(Image: Daniella Ockerby)*

The academy teaches multiple dance styles including Jazz, Ballet, Commercial and Tap. Its students range from 18 months to 20 years old.

Daniella said: “When setting up Ockerby Academy I always wanted to give as many opportunities possible to my OA Superstars and their families, because thats what it is all about.”

She also said: “Watch this space adults there are more classes on the horizon!”

Daniella has danced since she was three and worked professionally on cruise ships, show tours, stadium tours, pantomimes and television. After having her daughter, she chose to settle and teach.

She said: “my professional performance career was amazing and I loved every second of it but I felt as I got older the time came for me where I wanted to settle down and have a family”

She first taught classes through social media during the Covid pandemic. Her first in-person classes were held at Eldwick Memorial Hall in January 2021.

The academy’s work includes helping students prepare for future opportunities and arts careers.

Owner Daniella Ockerby said: “we have so much fun but work so hard to get that progress and work towards those opportunities and for some of them, working towards moving to college to start their career in the arts.”

Fundraising events are also planned to support future opportunities for students.

The academy will hold its sixth annual show at Bingley Arts Centre on November 6 and November 7. Tickets go on sale on September 14 through the venue’s box office and website.

Daniella said: “Ockerby Academy isn’t just a dance school to me, and it certainly isn’t just a job, it’s my life, and I genuinely love it.”
