A BMW driver is facing court after being spotted without insurance, tax or a legal number plate during police patrols in Bradford city centre.

The driver of the BMW M140i has been summoned to court for all three offences.

West Yorkshire Police said: "While out conducting proactive patrols in Bradford City Centre, officers from the Neighbourhood Policing Team spotted a BMW M140i displaying a non-conforming number plate, which immediately caught their attention.

"Further checks revealed the vehicle was being driven without insurance and had no valid vehicle tax.

"The driver has now been summonsed to court for all three offences.

"Proactive patrols play a vital role in keeping our roads safe and ensuring vehicles are being driven legally.

"If you're behind the wheel, make sure your vehicle is road legal, insured, taxed, and displaying a compliant registration plate."
