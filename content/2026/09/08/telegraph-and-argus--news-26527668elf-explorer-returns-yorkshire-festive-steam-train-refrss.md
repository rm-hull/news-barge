TICKETS are now on sale for a festive railway adventure promising Christmas magic for the whole family.

The Elf Explorer Experience returns to the Keighley and Worth Valley Railway this year, running from November 28 to December 24.

The experience transforms the heritage railway into a Christmas wonderland, complete with mischievous elves, interactive storytelling, and a visit from Father Christmas.

A spokesperson said: "The Elf Explorer has become one of Yorkshire's most popular Christmas attractions, drawing visitors from across the region and beyond to experience festive magic aboard one of Britain's best-loved heritage railways."

Passengers are invited to join Father Christmas' elves as they prepare for the big day, with each journey filled with festive surprises and live entertainment set against the nostalgic charm of a steam train ride.

The experience concludes with a special visit from Father Christmas himself, making it a memorable day out for families.

Carriages are beautifully decorated and brought to life by live actors, creating a theatrical experience that captures the excitement of the season.

Tickets start from £26 and are expected to be in high demand, particularly for weekend departures and the final run-up to Christmas.
