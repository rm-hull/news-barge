# 'I failed her massively' - Mother's heartbreaking statement as child sex offender is jailed

## A child sex offender has been jailed after admitting to dozens of offences - as the mother of one of his victims issued a heartbreaking statement in court.

Kasey Fitton was arrested in February last year after police suspected he had been sending sexualised messages to four children who lived outside the Yorkshire area.

West Yorkshire Police’s investigation was initially launched by West Mercia Police and Lancashire Police.

The Bradford Safeguarding Unit at West Yorkshire Police then launched a probe which uncovered a large scale sexual offending against children.

In February 2026, Fitton was charged with 16 counts of sexual communication with a child and 20 counts of making/possession of indecent images of children.

The 20-year-old, of Amundsen Avenue, in Bradford, admitted all 36 counts.

Police identified five child victims, and one of their mothers read out a emotionally charged impact statement in court.

She said: ““I am a mother and it is my job to protect her, but I feel like I failed her massively, I felt like I didn’t protect her from the evil of this world.

“I spent many days and nights wondering where I went so wrong or what more I could have done.

“In reality, it wasn’t me that that didn’t protect her, it was you, a predator that prayed on her vulnerability and innocence at the age of 14.

“You stole her light for a long time, but it was me as her mother that was there every minute of every day picking up the pieces you left her in.”

Fitton was jailed for seven-and-a-half years and given an extended licence period of five years. He will also be required to sign the sex offenders register for the rest of his life, and was made the subject of a sexual harm prevention order for 15 years,

DC Dominika Marcinkowska of Bradford District Safeguarding Team said: “I would like to thank the victims in this case for their courage in speaking to police and reporting these crimes to us.

“This has been a lengthy and complex investigation, and I hope today’s sentence can bring them some closure.”

Senior Investigating officer Detective Superintendent Alan Weekes of Bradford District Police, added: “Fitton is a very dangerous individual to children, and we welcome the courts sentencing today.

“There are further investigations ongoing and we would appeal to anyone who may have been a victim to come forward to us.

“We are keen to speak to anyone who interacted with the Snapchat accounts Kaseyfitton23, Kaseyfitton24 and MyCroft110.

“We take all reports of this nature extremely seriously and they are treated with sensitivity. We have specially trained officers who will guide you through the process and do everything we can to get justice.”
