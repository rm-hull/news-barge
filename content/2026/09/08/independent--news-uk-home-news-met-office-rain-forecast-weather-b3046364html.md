Milder conditions are set to return following a rainy start to the week as temperatures are forecast to creep back up to 24C.

Heavy rain is set to sweep across the UK on Tuesday with temperatures plunging to 11C in Scotland and 15C in the south of England.

Although the weather has changed bringing more unsettled conditions, all of Wales and most of England remains in drought. That’s because sustained rainfall is needed to reverse the drought conditions.

In the south of England Tuesday will be the wettest day of the week, but in the north of England Manchester is set to see showers from Thursday afternoon.

![Most of the UK is set to see heavy rain on Tuesday before warmer conditions return](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/08/7/47/Screenshot-2026-09-08-at-07-45-19.png)

By Saturday temperatures in London will reach 23C, with highs of 19C in Cardiff and 17C in Manchester. On Monday the capital will see highs of 24C and most of England and Wales will see temperatures in the low 20C in a warm start to the week.

It comes after the Met Office this week confirmed the UK experienced its hottest summer since records began nearly 150 years ago.

Five heatwaves between May and August left the majority of the country in drought and have sparked wildfires and travel disruption.

The UK recorded a mean average temperature across June, July and August of 16.5C, beating the previous seasonal high of 16.1C set last year, the Met Office said.

The highest temperature recorded this summer was on 13 August at Kew Gardens, when the mercury hit 38.1C.

![The capital will see temperatures of 23C on Saturday before it reaches 24C on Monday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/08/7/37/Screenshot-2026-09-08-at-08-31-28.png)

Changeable conditions with occasional rain or showers arriving from the North Atlantic, are set to be frequent in northern and western parts of the UK until the end of September, according to the Met Office.

But there will also be some dry and more settled periods in the south.

Towards the end of September high pressure may become more dominant bringing more settled conditions, with warmer daytime temperature and cooler nights.
