Emergency services were called to a person trapped in their home last night (Monday).

Both police and firefighters were called to **Coggan Close** in**7.38pm**.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A crew from Acomb responded to a request from police to gain entry to a property where the occupant had become locked inside their living room due to the door mechanism failing.

#### **Coggan Close, York**

“Crews used a triple extension ladder to gain access to the living room via a second floor window and released the occupant using small tools to remove the door mechanism.”
