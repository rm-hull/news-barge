A MAN has been charged with a drink-driving offence in East Yorkshire.

Daniel Tyler, 46, of Wood Lane in Beverley, was charged with being in charge of a motor vehicle while under the influence and driving without insurance in Lairgate, Beverley on Sunday (September 6).

  


Tyler was arrested on September 6 and has been remanded in custody.

He is due to appear at Hull Magistrates Court this morning (Tuesday, September 8).
