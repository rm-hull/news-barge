## Emergency services close York city centre road following incident

Police officers in York city centre. *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Emergency services were called out to an incident on a major York city centre road.

Bridge Street was closed to both vehicles and pedestrians after an incident shortly after 11pm on Monday (September 7).

North Yorkshire Police said officers had closed Ouse Bridge while emergency services responded to the incident.

  Emergency services were called to Ouse Bridge following an incident at 11pm on Monday (September 7) 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The road has since reopened.

  


A statement read: “Please avoid the area if you can and use alternative routes where possible.”

The road has since reopened.

The Press has reached out to North Yorkshire Police for an updated statement.

It comes after there was a separate emergency incident on the bridge on Saturday night (September 5).

Emergency services, including North Yorkshire Police, Yorkshire Ambulance Service, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service and York Rescue Boat were at the scene with a section of the bridge being closed to all vehicles.

A police cordon was in place before the fear for welfare incident was safely resolved around 10pm.
