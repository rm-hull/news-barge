Drivers have been warned of possible disruptions as road closures and traffic restrictions will be in place on Wednesday due to a funeral.

Hartlepool Borough Council said a large private funeral will take place at St Joseph’s Church in Hartlepool on Wednesday, September 9, with nearby roads closing from 7.30am.

The restrictions will support the funeral and control access to the surrounding area.

### List of road closures:

• Hutton Avenue from the junction with Mulgrave Road

• St Paul’s Road

• South Road from the junction with Mitchell Street

The council said traffic measures will keep South Road accessible, including the health centre, local businesses and homes.

Stewards will help residents, visitors and drivers throughout the day.

Residents on Hutton Avenue and St Paul’s Road will not be allowed to park there while the roads are closed.

Affected residents have received letters explaining the arrangements and alternative parking.

The council has asked residents on the affected roads to put out food and grey recycling bins by 7.15am on Wednesday, as crews will collect them earlier than usual.

Waste and recycling collections on nearby roads will continue as normal.

Hartlepool Borough Council said vehicles left on the affected roads after they close will be removed.

Drivers near the church should allow extra time and follow stewards’ directions and road signs.
