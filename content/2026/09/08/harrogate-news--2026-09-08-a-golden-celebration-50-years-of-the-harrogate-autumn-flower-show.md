**The Harrogate Autumn Flower Show** is celebrating its**50th anniversary**, bringing three days of spectacular flowers, inspiring gardens, giant vegetables, and seasonal produce to** Newby Hall & Gardens**from** 18-20 September 2026**.

First staged in **1976**, the show has spent half a century celebrating the very best of gardening, growing, and horticultural creativity in the North of England. This year’s milestone anniversary brings that rich heritage together with a packed programme of competitive horticulture, specialist plant displays, floral art, live talks, family activities, and seasonal shopping.

## Grain & Grandeur: The Harvest Ball

To mark five decades of the show, renowned floral designer **Jonathan Moseley** will create a spectacular new installation inside Newby Hall titled*Grain & Grandeur: The Harvest Ball*. Inspired by the abundance and vibrant colours of autumn, the centrepiece will feature a spectacular harvest-inspired gown designed and created by Jonathan using seasonal materials.

## The Best Flowers and Produce

At the heart of the event is the **Grand Floral Pavilion**, hosting specialist nurseries and horticultural societies. Visitors can explore a showcase featuring over** 5,000 blooms**, ranging from asters to zinnias.

Alongside the blooms, the **Incredible Edible Pavilion** will showcase the extraordinary achievements of growers, featuring prize-winning fruit and vegetables alongside the mighty**NEHS Giant Vegetable Competition**. The results of the giant vegetable weigh-in will be presented at** 9am on Friday 19 September**.

The thrilling **National Onion Championship** also returns this year. The giant onion weigh-in, hosted by Christine Talbot, will take place at**12pm on Friday** in the Incredible Edible Pavilion. Audiences will be watching closely to see if anyone can top the colossal**8.97kg world record** set at the show by Gareth Griffin in 2023.

## Live Inspiration for Gardeners and Cooks

New for 2026, Newby Hall will open a private garden exclusively for the show to host the brand-new **Secret Garden live stage**. Top chef** Steph Moon**will host a series of fun demonstrations inspired by the 50-year anniversary and the nostalgia of 1970s cookery. Leading florist** Sarah Richardson** of Leafy Couture will also explore creative, retro-styled flower arranging.

Additional live entertainment and expert advice will be spread across two more dedicated stages:

- **The Grow! Stage:** Offering practical gardening advice, live demonstrations, and seasonal tips from a knowledgeable team to help visitors make the most of their autumn gardens.
- **The Human Gardener Stage:** Hosted by Dr Sarah Owen-Hughes (Head Gardener at Rudding Park Hotel & Spa) and Faith Douglas (Landscape Curator from the Royal Botanic Gardens Edinburgh), featuring a fascinating programme of conversations exploring gardens, nature, and wellbeing.

## Family Fun and Stunning Gardens

Show admission includes full access to Newby Hall’s award-winning gardens, allowing visitors to explore the distinctive collection of garden rooms and magnificent herbaceous borders. Families can enjoy the adventure playground, a miniature railway, and the **Little Nature Rangers** zone, which offers paper posy-making workshops, storytelling, a Flower Show Farm, birds of prey, and free face painting. Visitors can also follow the**Green Man Trail**, created in partnership with Horticap and Artizan Harrogate.

Show Director Nick Smith said: “This is a particularly special year for the Harrogate Autumn Flower Show as we celebrate 50 years since the first show in 1976. We’re incredibly proud of the heritage behind the event, but we’re also looking firmly to the future, bringing together the very best of horticulture, creativity and growing in a beautiful new setting.”

## Visitor Information

- **Dates:** 18-20 September 2026
- **Location:** Newby Hall & Gardens, Ripon, North Yorkshire, HG4 5AE
- **Prices:** Adult tickets are**£29**(plus a single £2 administration fee per online order). Children under 16 and general parking are** free** . Tickets are available online and on the gate.
