Concern is growing for a man missing miles away from home.

Cleveland Police has appealed to the public for information regarding a man from Manchester, who has been reported missing while in the Stockton-on-Tees area.

44-year-old Ryan Morris was last seen near the University Hospital of North Tees at 7.15 am on 8th September.

It is believed he took a taxi to near Norton Road on Stockton High Street.

Police say there is a possibility Ryan Morris went on to travel to his home city of Manchester.

Ryan, who is 5’10”, was last seen wearing black joggers, a navy blue hoodie with yellow writing on the front, and grey and orange trainers.

He has brown hair, as can be seen in the picture provided by police.

Cleveland police urge anyone who has seen Ryan Morris, or has any knowledge of his whereabouts, to contact Cleveland police via 101, using the reference number 180349.
