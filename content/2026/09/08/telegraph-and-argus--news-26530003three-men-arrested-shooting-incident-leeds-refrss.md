THREE more men have been arrested in connection with a shooting incident which left a man hospitalised with gunshot wounds.

The men - two aged 19 and one aged 21 - were arrested on suspicion of attempted murder and possession of a firearm with intent to endanger life. They remain in police custody.

The arrests relate to an incident believed to have taken place on Cambrian Street, Holbeck, on the evening of Tuesday, September 1.

## Man suffered gunshot wounds to both arms

West Yorkshire Police said officers received a report at 10.11pm that night that a man in his 20s had attended hospital with injuries consistent with gunshot wounds to both arms.

His injuries were not believed to be life-threatening.

Following enquiries, officers established a scene on Cambrian Street, where the offence is understood to have taken place.

## Earlier suspects released with no further action

The latest arrests come after two 26-year-old men were arrested on suspicion of wounding with intent.

Police have now confirmed both men have been interviewed and released with no further action.

## Appeal for information continues

Detective Chief Inspector Daniel Bates, of the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team, previously said any discharge of a firearm would understandably cause concern among residents and that an increased police presence would remain in the area.

He said extensive enquiries were ongoing to establish the circumstances of the incident and identify those involved.

Police are continuing to appeal for information from anyone who may have seen or heard anything in the vicinity of Cambrian Street on the night of September 1.

Anyone with information is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police via Live Chat or by calling 101, quoting crime reference 13260498911. Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

## Latest statement from police

A WYP spokesperson penned: "Detectives investigating after a man was injured in a firearms discharge in Leeds last week have made three further arrests.

"Three men, aged 19, 19 and 21, are currently in custody having been arrested on suspicion of attempt murder and possession of a firearm with intent to endanger life.

"It follows an incident which is understood to have taken place on Cambrian Street, Holbeck, during the evening of Tuesday 1 September.

"Police received a report at 10.11pm that a man is his 20s had attended hospital with injuries consistent with gunshot wounds to both arms.

"Two 26-year-old men who were previously arrested in connection with this incident have now been interviewed and released facing no further action."
