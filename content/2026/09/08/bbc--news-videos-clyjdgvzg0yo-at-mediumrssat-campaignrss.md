# Prince George attends first day at Eton College

Prince George has officially started his first term at Eton College.

The 13-year-old, who is second in line for the British throne, was dropped off at the all-boys boarding school by his parents, the Prince of Wales and Catherine, Princess of Wales.

The future king follows in the footsteps of his father in attending the elite private school, which has taught 20 British prime ministers and where fees are around £65,000 a year.

The BBC's royal correspondent Daniela Relph explains what the prince is likely to expect at his new school.
