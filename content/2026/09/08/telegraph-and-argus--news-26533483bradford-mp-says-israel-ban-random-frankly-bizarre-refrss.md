Bradford West MP Naz Shah has described the reported decision to bar her from entering Israel as "rather random and, frankly, bizarre".

Ms Shah is among 11 sitting MPs named by the Israeli government as being banned from entering the country, following the UK's announcement of sanctions targeting illegal Israeli settlements in the West Bank.

Israeli foreign minister Gideon Saar claimed those barred had been "involved in antisemitic and anti-Israel activity, as well as those who deny the very existence of the State of Israel".

The move was announced after Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband set out the Government's measures as part of a reset in its approach to Benjamin Netanyahu's administration.

## MP's response

In a statement, Ms Shah said: "I have just been informed that the Israeli Foreign Ministry has added my name to a list of 11 MPs reportedly banned from entering Israel."

"I have no idea how this list was compiled and, as many people who have already contacted me have said, the selection appears rather random and, frankly, bizarre."

The Bradford West Labour MP said she had consistently campaigned for the UK to stop trade with settlements in the occupied Palestinian territories.

She said: "What I am clear about, however, is the position I have consistently campaigned for over many years: the UK Government should take meaningful action to end trade with illegal Israeli settlements in the occupied Palestinian territories, which are recognised as unlawful under international law.

"I am proud to support the UK Government's actions today, taken in line with our international legal obligations."

## Call for two-state solution

Ms Shah said the UK must continue to pursue a long-term peace settlement in the Middle East.

"The UK must continue to play its part on the international stage in securing a genuine and lasting pathway to peace in the Middle East," she said.

"That means working towards a viable two-state solution: an independent, vibrant and sovereign Palestinian state alongside a safe and secure state of Israel.

"My commitment to international law, peace, security and the rights of both Palestinians and Israelis remains unchanged."

The MPs named by Israel are Ms Shah, Jeremy Corbyn, Zarah Sultana, Diane Abbott, Hannah Spencer, Green Party MPs Carla Denyer, Sian Berry, Ellie Chowns and Adrian Ramsay, as well as John McDonnell and Leeds East MP Richard Burgon.

Fahad Ansari, director of advocacy group Riverway to the Sea, has also reportedly been barred from entering Israel.

Israel's response followed the UK measures on trade connected to West Bank settlements.

The Israeli government also announced wider diplomatic countermeasures, including action concerning the British consulate in East Jerusalem.
