A former nurse has swapped scrubs for stories with the launch of a new bookshop and tearoom.

Hayley Beckinsale launched Chapter 13 on Frederick Street in Sunderland city centre on July 13, following a 16-year career in nursing and support from the North East BIC.

The shop offers both new and pre-loved books, with a focus on romance, fantasy, thrillers, and contemporary fiction, along with book-related gifts and accessories.

Ms Beckinsale said: "I’d always joked about having a little bookshop and coffee shop when we were on holiday, but I never thought it would actually happen.

"I’d been in nursing for 16 years, and then there were so many changes in my job that I became unhappy and knew I needed a change.

"My husband said, ‘Why don't you just try?’ So, I started looking into how I could make it happen, and it just grew from there."

With no previous business experience, Ms Beckinsale received a government start-up loan and was introduced to the North East BIC, where she began working with business adviser Hina Joshi.

Ms Joshi said: "Hayley had a clear passion for what she wanted Chapter 13 to become, but she was completely new to running a business.

"It has been fantastic to see her take that idea and turn it into a real space that has already received such a positive response from the local community."

Ms Beckinsale added: "The BIC has been a massive help because I’ve never done anything like this before.

"I honestly don't think I would have known where to start without their help.

"It’s made the whole process feel much more manageable."

Councillor James Jackson, portfolio holder for housing, business, and regeneration at Sunderland City Council, said: "It’s great to see local entrepreneurs like Hayley turning their ideas into reality and creating new businesses that add something special to Sunderland’s communities.

"Independent businesses play an important role in our local economy, creating new opportunities and helping our city’s high streets and neighbourhoods thrive.

"Providing the right support to people taking that first step into business is vital, and it’s encouraging to see Hayley making the most of the support available to build her new venture."
