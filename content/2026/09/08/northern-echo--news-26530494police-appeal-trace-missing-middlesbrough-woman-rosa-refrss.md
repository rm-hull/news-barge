Police are urgently appealing for help to trace a woman in her 60s who has been reported missing.

Rosa Moone, 67, from the Lytton Street area in Middlesbrough, was last seen on Sunday, September 6.

She is described as white, around 4ft 8in tall and of medium build, with white hair worn in a bob.

It is not known what she was wearing when she was last seen.

A spokesperson from Cleveland Police said: “Anyone who has seen Rosa, or who has information which could help officers trace her, is asked to contact Cleveland Police on 101, quoting reference number 179988.”
