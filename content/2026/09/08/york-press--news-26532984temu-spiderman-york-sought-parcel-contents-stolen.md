## ‘Temu Spider-Man of York’ sought by police after items stolen from parcel

The 'Temu Spider-Man of York' who police want to speak to after items from a parcel were stolen in the city *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Police have issued a photograph of a man, dubbed the “Temu Spider-Man of York” by officers, sought in connection with items being stolen from a parcel delivered to a home in the city.

The parcel was left behind locked gates when its contents were stolen on Tuesday, August 11, North Yorkshire Police said.

  The 'Temu Spider-Man of York' who police want to speak to after items from a parcel were stolen in the city 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

CCTV footage from the housing management company was shared with the force showing a man “climbing the fence and stealing the contents of the parcel”, a police spokesperson said.

They said police want to speak to the man pictured about the incident.

Anyone with information is asked to email [peter.henderson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:peter.henderson@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Quote reference 12260155379 when passing on information to police or to Crimestoppers.
