Two artisan markets will take place this month, offering a taste of North Yorkshire’s creativity, community spirit, and seasonal flavours.

Announced for Saturday, September 12, in Easingwold and Acomb, the family- and dog-friendly Little Bird Made markets promise a day out filled with handcrafted goods and food.

The Easingwold Artisan Market will run from 9am to 2pm, featuring handmade gifts, fresh produce, and treats from independent makers.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "As we move into autumn, our September markets are all about celebrating the change of season, showcasing incredible local Yorkshire talent, and bringing the community together.

"Whether you’re hunting for unique handcrafted goods in Easingwold or enjoying delicious local food, drink and treats in Acomb, it’s a fantastic weekend out for the whole family."

Alison Jackson, owner of Little Jackson Makes, added: "This is my third year trading at the Easingwold Artisan Market, and I always love attending the market here.

"The marketplace is such a special place, and there’s always lots of support from the local community.

"It’s a really lovely day out."

The Acomb Flourish and Feast market starts at 10am and focuses on street food, baked goods, and beverages.

For more information, visit: www.littlebirdmade.com
