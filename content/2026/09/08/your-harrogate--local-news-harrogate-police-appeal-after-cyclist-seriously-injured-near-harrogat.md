Police are appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage after a cyclist was seriously injured in the Harrogate area.

The man, aged in his late 50s and from Otley, came off his bicycle while travelling downhill on the B6161 Leathley Lane.

The incident happened at around 4.45pm on Tuesday 11th August.

Paramedics treated the cyclist at the scene before he was taken to hospital for urgent treatment.

He remains in hospital receiving medical care.

North Yorkshire Police is asking anyone who witnessed the incident or saw the cyclist or other vehicles in the area at the time to come forward.

Officers would also like to hear from motorists who may have relevant dashcam footage.

*Information can be emailed to [Conor.Taylor@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Conor.Taylor@northyorkshire.police.uk) or reported by calling 101 and asking for Conor Taylor.*

*Anyone providing information should quote reference 12260155539.*
