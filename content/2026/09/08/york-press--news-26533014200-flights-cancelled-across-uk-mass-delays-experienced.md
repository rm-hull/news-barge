Approximately 200 flights have been cancelled as a result of a UK air traffic control issue, FlightRadar24 has said.

The worst affected airports are Heathrow, Gatwick, Manchester and Birmingham.

Leeds Bradford Airport is not affected but many people living in North Yorkshire and York use Manchester Airport.

The air tracker found 352 flights to and from Heathrow were delayed out of its daily average of about 1,300.

Meanwhile, 281 Gatwick flights were delayed out of its 850 total operations average.

This was 249 out of 650 for Manchester, and 101 out of 300 for Birmingham.

In a statement on its website, air traffic control services provider Nats said: “We are investigating a technical issue which is causing some disruption to flight departures. Our engineers are on site and we will provide an update as soon as we can.

"We apologise for the delay this will cause to journeys, passengers should check with their airline on the status of their flight."

Heathrow Airport said arriving flights are not affected.

A statement said: “The air traffic control service @NATS is experiencing a technical issue which is impacting departing flights at UK airports.

“Arriving flights are not currently impacted.

“We are supporting our local Nats team in their efforts to resolve the issue as quickly as possible. We will provide updated information when we have it.

“We apologise to those disrupted and advise passengers to check with their airline for latest flight information.”

Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander said she was aware of the issue impacting flights and that engineers were “fixing it as we speak”.

She said: I’m aware of a technical issue impacting flights at airports including Heathrow and Gatwick.

“Technicians have identified the issue and are fixing it as we speak.

“I know this will be frustrating for passengers and I’m sorry.

“Please check with your airline for latest advice.”
