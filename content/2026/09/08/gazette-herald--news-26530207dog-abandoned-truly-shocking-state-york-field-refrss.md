A dog has been found abandoned in a “truly shocking” state in York, the RSPCA said, urging anyone with information about the animal to come forward.

The liver and white-coloured elderly spaniel, nicknamed Albie, was found in a field in Moor Lane during heavy rain at about 7am on Sunday, August 30.

Albie was found by two members of the public who took him home before being seen by a vet and transferred into the care of the RSPCA in Landing Lane.

  Albie was found with rotted canines after being abandoned in a field in York 

*(Image: RSPCA)*

The RSPCA said Albie was severely matted and suffering from chronic dental disease which had caused one of his canines to break off, exposing the root.

His back legs were splayed and x-rays have revealed he has a degenerative condition called spondylosis and compressed joint spaces.

Albie is receiving ongoing veterinary care but remains in a serious condition, the RSPCA said.

  Albie was found severely matted after being abandoned in York 

*(Image: RSPCA)*

Marie Sandall, from the RSPCA’s York branch, said she was glad “we are here to help” Albie.

“Seeing an elderly dog in such terrible condition is absolutely heartbreaking,” she said. “Despite the pain he must be in, he’s the sweetest dog.

“He must have known we were here to help him as he seemed to enjoy all the attention and you could see the instant relief when we shaved him. He had pieces of straw stuck in the mats, and even more heartbreaking was when the vet called us to say they had also found live maggots in his coat.

“No animal should ever be subjected to neglect like this.”

  Albie after being cared for by the RSPCA 

*(Image: RSPCA)*

RSPCA animal rescue officer Mollie Masters, who is looking into the incident, urged anyone with information about where Albie is from to come forward as she said he is not microchipped.

“When I first saw Albie, my heart broke,” she said. “I have seen animals in desperate need before, but the condition this elderly dog was found in was truly shocking.

“His entire body was encased in thick, painful mats of fur. There wasn't a single part of him that was free from them. He could barely stand, let alone walk, and his teeth were in an appalling condition.

“He’s such a lovely, friendly-natured dog and it’s very difficult to imagine the level of pain and discomfort he must have been living with.

“What is even harder to comprehend is that someone must have known how badly Albie was suffering, yet they still chose to do nothing and dumped him in a field in the middle of a rain storm; cold, wet and alone.

“We desperately need to find where Albie has come from.”

Anyone with information about Albie is urged to contact the RSPCA’s inspectorate appeal line, in confidence, on 0300 123 8018, quoting reference number 01893084.
