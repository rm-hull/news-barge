York Minster is opening its doors to showcase the craftsmanship behind its conservation and heritage work.

Visitors will be able to explore the rarely seen Heritage Quad, meet the teams who preserve the historic cathedral, and learn about ongoing projects during a special event on Friday, September 18, during Heritage Open Days.

Experts including stonemasons, gardeners, scaffolders, and glaziers from York Glaziers Trust will be on hand to demonstrate their work and explain how they help protect and maintain the Minster.

Alexander McCallion, director of works and precinct at York Minster, said: "We are delighted to open the doors of the Heritage Quad and showcase the craft skills, dedication and innovation involved in caring for the Minster.

"Visitors will be able to meet the people behind our conservation work, ask questions, and discover how we are combining heritage preservation with ambitious sustainability and biodiversity initiatives to ensure the Minster continues to thrive for generations to come."

Visitors will also have the chance to learn about major projects currently underway, such as the conservation of the Rose Window and the restoration of the south quire aisle pinnacles.

The event runs from 9.30am to 3pm and is free to attend, with no booking required.
