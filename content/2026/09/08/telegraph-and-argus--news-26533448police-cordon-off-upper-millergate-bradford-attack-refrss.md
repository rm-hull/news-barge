Police cordoned off a street in Bradford city centre after an attack this afternoon.

Emergency services rushed to Upper Millergate at around 4pm today after reports of a serious assault.

A police cordon is in place around the scene of the incident, which is located off Sunbridge Road.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "A man has been taken to hospital following a report of a serious assault.

"No update on injuries at this time."

A Yorkshire Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "Ambulance resources were dispatched to an assault incident on Sunbridge Road, Bradford, just before 4pm on Tuesday afternoon.

"One patient was conveyed to hospital."
