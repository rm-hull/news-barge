DRIVERS faced delays on the motorway this morning after traffic slowed significantly.

According to traffic monitoring service Inrix, delays of around seven minutes were being reported between the M62 at Junction 27, Gildersome, and Junction 2 at Elland Road.

The disruption was first reported during the morning rush hour.

## Average speeds drop

Traffic was said to be moving at an average speed of around 15mph as congestion built on the route.

The delays affected the eastbound carriageway between Gildersome and Elland Road, a busy commuter route used by motorists travelling into Leeds.

## Delays now easing

Inrix later reported that delays were easing in the area, although traffic remained heavier than normal.

It is not yet known whether the congestion was linked to an earlier incident, and enquiries have been made to West Yorkshire Police for further information.

Motorists travelling through the area are advised to allow extra time for their journeys while traffic continues to clear.
