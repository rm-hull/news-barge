Last weekend, nine North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service firefighters took on the Terry Farrell Firefighters Fund Memorial Stair Climb in Atlanta.

They climbed 110 floors in full fire kit with temperatures exceeding 38°C to honour the 343 New York (FDNY) firefighters who made the ultimate sacrifice on 11 September 2001.

Joining more than 1,200 firefighters from around the world, including 243 from the UK, the team climbed the equivalent of the World Trade Center’s 110 floors in honour of the 343 FDNY firefighters who lost their lives while responding to the 9/11 terrorist attacks .

The climb took place within the **Atlanta Braves Baseball stadium** and each firefighter carried the photograph of a firefighter who went to work that day and never returned home.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/796788081_1368142141969313_367453297985254280_n-1000x738.jpg)

**A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said:** “We are incredibly proud of our firefighters for representing North Yorkshire at this moving event. 

“Their achievement stands as a tribute to the bravery, dedication and sacrifice of those who gave their lives in the line of duty.

“Congratulations to the team on completing the climb.”

During their visit, they also had the opportunity to tour fire stations across Atlanta and visit the city’s fire department training facility.

The challenge has also raised money for three charities: The Fire Fighters Charity, the Terry Farrell Firefighters Fund and the Blue Light Card Foundation.

Donations are still welcome via the team’s *JustGiving* page.
