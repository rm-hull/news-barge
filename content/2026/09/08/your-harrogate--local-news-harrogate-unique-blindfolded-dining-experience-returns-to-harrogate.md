A unique dining experience which challenges guests to eat without relying on their sight is returning to Harrogate.

Dine in the Dark will be held at Rudding Park Hotel on Thursday 8th October.

Guests will enjoy a three-course meal exploring flavour, scent, sound and touch, with the main course eaten while blindfolded.

The experience aims to give diners a greater understanding of some of the challenges faced by guide and assistance dog owners.

The evening will raise money for Action4access, supporting work to help people who are refused access or receive inferior service because of their assistance dog.

There will also be spot prizes, a silent auction and a prize draw.

Guests can gather in the event bar from 7pm, before taking their seats for dinner at 7.30pm.

*Tickets cost £60 and are available through**Eventbrite**.*
