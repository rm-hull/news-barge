A brewery is gearing up to host its fifth Oktoberfest celebration.

Wold Top Brewery, based in Wold Newton on the Yorkshire Wolds, will host the beer and music festival on Saturday, September 26.

The event promises a specially brewed Oktoberfest beer, German food with a Yorkshire twist from Nabs Nosh, and live entertainment from an Oompah Band.

Kate Balchin, managing director at Wold Top, said: "We had such good feedback from last year’s event, and our brewers were excited to have another opportunity to brew a special Oktoberfest beer, as we’re repeating our German beer festival with a Yorkshire twist.

"We’re all really excited for what will be a fabulous, fun-filled afternoon and evening."

The event runs from 3pm to 11pm, with tickets priced at £18 per person, including a pint or a soft drink.

Camping, caravan, and motorhome pitches are available for £12 per pitch.

Tickets are available online at: [https://bit.ly/WTEvents26](https://bit.ly/WTEvents26) or by calling 01723 892222
