## Easingwold Town Band brings curtain down on Summer of Music festival

The Easingwold Summer of Music festival has finished for another year *(Image: Supplied)*

The Easingwold Summer of Music festival has concluded for another year.

The end of the festival comes as summer draws to a close, which has made it less likely for outdoor events to be held in the coming days.

The festival saw visitors enjoy a range of music, including performances from the Hollywood Sisters.

The group, from York, performed a range of songs from classics to more modern hits.

This included the Chordettes’ Lollipop, Gloria Gaynor’s I Will Survive, and ABBA’s The Winner Takes It All.

The final day of the Summer of Music was on Sunday, and saw Easingwold Town Band performing to close the festival.

The band played a mix of songs, including Elton John’s Crocodile Rock, ELO’s Mr Blue Sky, and Christina Perri’s A Thousand Years.

There was one particularly special moment during the band’s performance when they played a piece written many years ago by a former brass band leader for his granddaughter.

She was reportedly sitting in the audience, holding up the original LP recorded by her grandfather, complete with a photograph of herself as a young child on the sleeve.

Now that the event has wrapped up, the town is looking forward to next year’s planned festival.
