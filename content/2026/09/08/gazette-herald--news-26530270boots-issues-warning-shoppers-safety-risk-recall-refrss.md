Boots has issued a warning to customers after a safety concern sparked a recall from UK shops.

The pharmacist has told shoppers to “stop using the affected product immediately”, as it may cause burns and comes with a fire risk.

The Love to Dream Sleep Machine has been recalled from all stores because of the risk.

A Boots statement said: “Use of the product with specific charging devices (not supplied with the product) may result in the lithium-ion battery in the product overheating during charging, which could pose a fire hazard to consumers.

“Risk of serious burns and/or property damage if the product overheats and catches fire.”

The affected products were sold between January 2, 2025, and September 3, 2026.

The recall includes the following date codes 7T12TN, 7W15TN and 8P01TN only.

Any date codes not listed are not affected by the recall.

The date code can be found on the side of the Sleep Machine or on the back of the box.

This is their advice for any shoppers who have purchased the product already:

1. Stop using the affected product immediately
2. Disconnect the product from any charger and place in a safe location, away from anything flammable or anything that could damage the product
3. Visit the Love to Dream recall page at lovetodream.co.uk/recall for information on how to dispose of the product and claim your refund or replacement
4. Dispose of the product only in accordance with local requirements for lithium-ion products. Do not place the product in household rubbish or recycling bin

## Simple recall

Products sold at Tesco, Asda, Superdrug and more have been recalled by skincare brand Simple.

Simple is recalling its Skin Micellar Cleansing Water because of the potential presence of bacteria which could cause eye inflammation.

Batches of the 200ml, 400ml and 730ml bottles of the skin product have been recalled.

Simple apologised to shoppers, recognising that the recall can be “concerning”.

These are the batches of cleansing water affected by the recalls:

- 200ml – 610041, 611641, 615941
- 400ml – 611141, 611341, 611441, 613341, 613441, 614541, 614741, 614841
- 730ml – 613841, 613941, 611041, 616641, 616741

A spokesman for Simple said: “In the UK and the Republic of Ireland, we are recalling a limited number of Simple Kind to Skin Micellar Cleansing Water 200ml, 400ml and 730ml products with specific batch codes due to the possible presence of bacteria, which has the potential to cause eye inflammation for some consumers.

“We are recalling these products because the safety of the people who buy and use our products is our top priority.

“Other Simple products are not affected by this issue.

“We understand that product recalls can be concerning, and we are sorry for any concern or inconvenience this may have caused.

“Stop using it and contact our Consumer Careline by email for further guidance and a refund.”

The batch code can be found on the bottom of the bottle. It is a series of numbers printed directly onto the packaging.

A Tesco spokesman added: “If you have purchased an affected batch code of one of the products being recalled, please do not use it and instead return it to any Tesco store for a full refund. No receipt is required.”

While an Asda spokesman said: “If you have purchased Kind to Skin Micellar Cleansing Water 200ml or 400ml of the above affected batch codes please stop using the product. You can bring it back to your nearest store where you will be given a full refund. You do not need your receipt.

“Alternatively, you can contact Unilever’s Consumer Careline by email for further guidance and a refund at [ukicare@unilever.com.](mailto:ukicare@unilever.com.) Or via phone: 0800 146 252 (UK) or 1800 604 060 (ROI).

“We are very sorry for any inconvenience caused.”
