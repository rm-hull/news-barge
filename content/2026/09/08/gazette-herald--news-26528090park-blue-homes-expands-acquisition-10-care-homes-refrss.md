A care provider in Harrogate has expanded its operations with the acquisition of 10 homes.

Park Blue Homes now operates 23 therapeutic residential care homes across England following the purchase of Flourish Care Holdings, which runs homes in the Midlands and South West.

The expansion forms part of Park Blue’s wider growth strategy and extends its reach across South Yorkshire, including Doncaster and Sheffield, as well as Newcastle, Sunderland, and Bishop Auckland.

Richard Parker, owner of Park Blue, said: "We have been looking to increase our therapeutic residential care provision across England, with Flourish sharing the same values, professionalism and commitment to supporting children and young people who have experienced trauma, abuse or other complex life circumstances.

"We are grateful to the Azets team for ensuring the transaction was a smooth one, completed to the satisfaction of all parties."

All Flourish homes are Ofsted-registered and rated either Outstanding or Good.

Park Blue started 14 years ago and now employs nearly 200 staff, with the company owned by Mr Parker and Ruth Parker-Pollard.

The acquisition was supported by Azets, a UK top 10 accountancy and business advisory firm.

Tariq Javaid, corporate finance partner at Azets, said: "Park Blue is built around providing safe homes and nurturing environments for children, with Flourish matching those same values, including emotional and educational support, structured around a therapeutic care model.

"There are clearly strong synergies between Park Blue and Flourish, hence the deal, and it has been a privilege for Azets to advise our client through the acquisition."

The deal was led by Mr Javaid, with support from Adam Mulla and Josh Wright, and financial and tax due diligence from Graham Dotchin, Jamie Hepworth, and Leigh Davenport.

Clarion, led by Hitesh Tailor, advised Park Blue on the legal aspects of the acquisition.
