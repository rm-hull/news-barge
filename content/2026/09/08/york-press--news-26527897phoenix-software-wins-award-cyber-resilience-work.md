York-based Phoenix Software has been named the winner of Rubrik’s 2026 EMEA Partner Award in the Pipeline Contributor category.

The IT specialist, which serves the public sector, was named a winner at the EMEA Partner Summit Awards, which were announced at Rubrik Forward EMEA in London.

The awards recognise organisations that have demonstrated excellence in delivering Rubrik-led cyber resilience and AI operations.

Clare Metcalfe, managing director of Phoenix Software, said: "Thank you to Rubrik for this recognition.

"The recognition reflects our team's ongoing commitment to helping the public sector build its cyber resilience while accelerating its AI transformation securely and safely."

In 2025, Phoenix Software achieved Rubrik’s Transform Elite Plus Partner status, the highest level within Rubrik’s Transform Partner Programme.

The event brings together Rubrik leadership and its top partners to explore how organisations across industries are strengthening data security and cyber resilience in response to a rapidly evolving threat landscape.

Alok Agrawal, chief solutions officer at Rubrik, said: "Building cyber resilience starts with trusted partnerships, and Phoenix Software has shown exceptional dedication to helping customers navigate an increasingly complex threat landscape.

"Their ability to identify opportunities, deliver value, and champion Rubrik’s vision has been instrumental to our shared success."

Phoenix Software has more than 35 years’ experience supporting the UK public sector, including services for clinics, classrooms, control rooms, and councils.

The company offers a full range of IT strategy and delivery services, including software licence management, cyber security, artificial intelligence, managed IT services, and professional services.

Phoenix Software’s team works closely with public sector organisations to design and deliver technology solutions tailored to their specific needs, rather than relying on generic, off-the-shelf options.

More information about Phoenix Software’s services and the sectors it supports can be found at www.phoenixs.co.uk/sectors.
