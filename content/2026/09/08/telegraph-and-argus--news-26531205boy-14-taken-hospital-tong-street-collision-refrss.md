A teenage boy has been taken to hospital following a collision with a car.

Emergency services scrambled to the scene of the incident, which happened on Tong Street in Bradford at around 8am this morning.

West Yorkshire Police said the 14-year-old sustained non-life-threatening injuries.

## What police have said

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police were called by the ambulance service to reports of a road traffic collision on Tong Street, Bradford at around 8.02am this morning (8 September).

"The incident was a collision involving a car and a 14-year-old who has been taken to hospital to be treated for non-life-threatening injuries.

"Enquires remain ongoing at this time."
