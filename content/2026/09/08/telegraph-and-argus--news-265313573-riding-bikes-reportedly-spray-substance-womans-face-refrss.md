A WOMAN was taken to hospital after a substance was reportedly sprayed in her face by three males travelling on off-road bikes in Bradford.

Police said they were called at 1.56pm on Monday following reports of an incident on Bierley Lane.

According to West Yorkshire Police, the female victim attended hospital for treatment after the substance was sprayed into her face.

Officers have since spoken to the woman and a crime has been recorded while enquiries continue.

A police scene was established on Bierley Lane as investigators carried out further enquiries into the circumstances surrounding the incident.

## FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police

A spokesperson for the force said: "At 1.56pm yesterday (Monday), police received a report of an assault which was said to have occurred in Bierley Lane, Bradford.

"It was reported that the victim, a female, had attended hospital for treatment after a substance was sprayed in her face.

"A scene was put in place while further enquiries were carried out into the incident, which was said to have involved three males on off road bikes.

"The victim has been spoken to by officers and a crime has been recorded for further investigation.

"Anyone who witnessed this incident is asked to contact Bradford CID via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat or by calling 101, quoting crime reference 13260509558.

"Information can also be reported anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555111 or online."
