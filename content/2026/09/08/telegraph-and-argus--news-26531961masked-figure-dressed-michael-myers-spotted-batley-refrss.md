A masked figure dressed as horror film character Michael Myers has been spotted on the streets of West Yorkshire.

The character, known for his chilling silence and white mask in the Halloween films, was seen in Batley in Kirklees, prompting concern and anger after a video surfaced online.

Residents claimed the figure had chased people, including a mother and child, leaving them terrified.

Commenting on the Batley Matters Facebook page, Ibby Husy Hussain said: "Whilst this may be funny to some, this idiot chased a young mother and her toddler.

"The mother, out of sheer panic and fright, ran straight on to Dark Lane with the child and narrowly avoided being hit by traffic.

"The outcome could’ve been a lot worse."

Others expressed their shock and disgust at the behaviour.

Nicola Rammy Ramsden said: "This is disgusting.

"It’s not normal behaviour."

Adele Brindley said: "This is absolutely awful."

Fatima Mamaniat said: "He chased me on Manor Way a few weeks ago."

Danielle Nicola Brown said: "Same guy was in B&M Batley.

"Me and my sister nearly s**t ourselves."

Vicky Tootill said: “Well I would s**t myself.”

Some residents said it was too early for Halloween antics.

Rashda Hussain said: "Omg, unfortunately little too early for Halloween Michael."

David Logue said: “Is it Halloween already!”

Others speculated it could be part of a social media trend.

One anonymous commenter said: "It’s some guy trying to get famous from dressing up and scaring people.

"There’s a whole trend on TikTok with people dressing as Cat in the Hat.

"Don’t give him the attention he craves."

Christine Belmonte said: “Seems he has everyone talking about him, so he achieved his objective.”

But not everyone found the stunt amusing.

Jules Hanson said: "I really don't think this is funny at all.

"Surely there is somewhere private he can do this.

"If I saw him, I would be petrified and I am an adult."

Alexandra Ignac said: "Oh gosh, that's not funny mate.

"Get help."

The Michael Myers character first appeared in John Carpenter’s 1978 film Halloween and has featured in a total of 12 films.
