FIRE crews have been called to a crash along a residential street in East Yorkshire.

The crash, which left a car on its side, happened in Lairgate, Beverley, at 7.06pm on Monday (September 7).

  


A woman was given oxygen therapy by Humberside Fire and Rescue Service until the arrival of paramedics.

She was able to leave the car prior to the arrival of emergency services.
