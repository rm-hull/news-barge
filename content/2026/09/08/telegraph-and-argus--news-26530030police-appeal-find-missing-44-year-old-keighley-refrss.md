*(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

Police have issued an appeal for information to help trace a missing 44-year-old from Keighley.

Officers say they are concerned for Gareth's welfare and are urging anyone who may have seen him to come forward.

The appeal was issued on September 7, as enquiries continue to establish Gareth's whereabouts.

## 
  **Description released by police**

Gareth is described by police as being of average height and build with short brown-grey hair, a beard and moustache.

Police said he was last known to be wearing blue jeans, a grey jacket, a grey hat and white Adidas trainers.

As part of the appeal, officers released a photograph showing Gareth sitting inside a vehicle.

In the image, Gareth is wearing a dark sweatshirt and a silver chain necklace.

## 
  **Appeal for sightings**

West Yorkshire Police are asking anyone who has seen Gareth, or who has information that could help locate him, to get in touch.

Anyone with information is asked to contact police, quoting log 1892 of September 7.

## 
  **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A spokesperson said: "Concerns for missing 44 year old Gareth from Keighley. Described as being a white male, average build and height, short brown, greying hair, with moustache and beard."

They added: "Wearing blue jeans, grey jacket, white adidas trainers, grey hat. Any sightings log 1892 07-09 refers."
