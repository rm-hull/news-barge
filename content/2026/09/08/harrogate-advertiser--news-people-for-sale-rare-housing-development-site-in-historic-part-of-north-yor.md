# For sale: Rare housing development site in historic part of North Yorkshire

## A rare development site with consent for nine homes is for sale in a village near a historic North Yorkshire market town which is mentioned in the Domesday Book.

The 1.97-acre development site with outline planning permission for up to nine new homes is for sale in Patrick Brompton, four miles from Bedale, with rural property consultancy Lister Haigh.

Known as The Joiners Yard, the site occupies a position on the north eastern side of the village, with access from Hackforth Road.

It is being offered for sale with a guide price of £1.1m.

North Yorkshire Council granted outline planning consent in July for residential development of up to nine dwellings.

The plan for the site provides for five detached and four semi-detached houses, with a combined indicative floor area of approximately 7,593 sq ft.

The proposals also include open space, a play area and land to provide biodiversity net gain.

Giles Chaplin, RICS Planning and Development Surveyor at Lister Haigh, said: “This is an interesting development opportunity because outline planning permission is already in place.

“The site occupies a contained position on the edge of Patrick Brompton, and the indicative scheme provides a nice mix of detached and semi-detached properties, alongside open space and a play area.

“There aren’t a huge number of sites with established planning consent available locally, so this is a great opportunity for a residential development in a popular village so close to Bedale and all that the historic market town has to offer.”

The development site at Patrick Brompton currently comprises detached garages, sheds and external storage within its southern section, with grazing land occupying the northern part. It extends to approximately 0.79 hectares.

Last year, Lister Haigh was acquired by Dacre, Son & Hartley, which has a 200-year heritage and offers a comprehensive range of services from its 17 offices, spanning residential sales and lettings, agricultural management, planning and commercial investment advice.

Founded in 1919, Lister Haigh has long been synonymous with Yorkshire’s finest houses and rural property market.
