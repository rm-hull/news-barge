A prominent hotel on a main road near York is up for sale for £1.5 million.

The Thai Season Restaurant & Hotel is on the A1079 at Wilberfoss.

Described as a ‘landmark hotel’ by agents Christie & Co, it is “arranged to provide a destination restaurant, bar and boutique hotel operation”.

Set in a plot of nearly one acre, up to 20,000 vehicles go past it to and from York on the A1079 every day.

The business has ten bedrooms, a Thai restaurant, a bar and four themed dining rooms.

The agent says: “The premises have been extensively refurbished and fitted out to a high standard, with themed décor inspired by the regions of Thailand creating a unique trading environment.
