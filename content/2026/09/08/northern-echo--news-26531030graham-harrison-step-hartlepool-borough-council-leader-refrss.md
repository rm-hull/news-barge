A North East council leader has announced his intention to step down from his role due to changes in his personal circumstances.

Graham Harrison, who leads Hartlepool Borough Council, said the demands of the role now exceed the time he is able to give, describing the decision as "very difficult."

He said: "It has been a tremendous honour and privilege to serve as Leader of Hartlepool Borough Council and to represent our town in this role.

"In recent weeks, there has been a change in my personal circumstances and, after careful consideration, I have concluded that I am no longer able to commit the time required to fulfil the duties of Leader."

He said: "I would like to thank my fellow councillors, council officers, partners and residents for their support during my time as leader.

"It has been a privilege to work alongside so many dedicated people who care passionately about Hartlepool and its future.

"While I will be stepping down as leader, I remain fully committed to public service and will continue to represent the residents of Burn Valley as one of their ward councillors."

"I look forward to continuing to serve the community and support the town in that capacity."

A new leader will be appointed at an extraordinary meeting of full council on Tuesday, September 15.
