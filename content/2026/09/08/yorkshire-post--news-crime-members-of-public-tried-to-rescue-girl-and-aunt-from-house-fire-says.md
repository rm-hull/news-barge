# Members of public tried to rescue girl and aunt from house fire, says coroner

## Members of the public tried to save a seven-year-old and her aunt who were killed in a house fire, an inquest heard.

Valentina Foster and Natalie McDonald, 34, died from the effects of fire following a blaze in Birchington Avenue, Grangetown, near Middlesbrough, Teesside coroner Paul Appleton said.

He held brief, separate, inquest openings into their deaths and suspended further hearings after saying that criminal proceedings for murder has begun.

The coroner said Cleveland Fire Brigade was called to the address on August 26 after receiving reports that people were believed to be trapped in the house.

Mr Appleton said: “Prior to the arrival of the emergency services, members of the public made attempts to rescue people in the property.

“Very sadly, Valentina was recovered from the property and was subsequently pronounced deceased at the James Cook University Hospital.”

He said a post-mortem examination revealed the cause of death was the effects of fire.

![Flowers near the scene on Birchington Avenue](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/01/16/cs-8cb59f5b-bed0-4a19-9367-ea3439cf0056.jpg?trim=208,0,208,0&width=640&quality=65)



Valentina’s body was identified by her mother, the coroner said.

She attended the hearing with relatives.

The same cause of death was given for Ms McDonald, who died at the scene.

The coroner said she was identified from her dental records and a tattoo.

Jaden Wright, 23, has appeared at Teesside Crown Court charged with two counts of murder, as well as offences related to other people who were injured in the blaze.

![Valentina Foster and Natalie McDonald died in the house fire in Middlesbrough.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/03/11/56/VALENTINA2.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



In a statement released by Cleveland Police, Ms McDonald’s family said she had been married for just nine months.

They said: “She had the biggest, most beautiful wedding; she sparkled like the shining beauty she was.

“She was loved by everyone, she was bright, bubbly and full of life, the heart and soul of our street and community, loved by all.

“Her family has lost its beautiful, kind soul.”

Valentina’s family has previously said: “It is with overwhelming sadness that we share the heartbreaking news that our beloved daughter has passed away.

“She was deeply loved and will be missed beyond words by her family, friends and everyone whose life she touched.”
