# ‘He made the world brighter’: Tribute to man, 45, killed in crash

## The family of a 45-year-old man killed after being struck by a car in Bradford have paid tribute to someone who “made the world brighter just by being in it”.

Eugen Matta was hit by a black Cupra Leon on Barkerend Road at around 8.50pm on Thursday (Sep 3).

The 45-year-old was pronounced dead at the scene.

Two occupants of the Cupra Leon, women aged 19 and 47, were arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

They have since been released on bail pending further enquiries.

![Eugen Matta](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/08/10/20/eugenMATTA.jpeg?trim=103,0,138,0&width=640&quality=65)



Mr Matta's family have now released a photograph and paid tribute to him.

They said: "My uncle wasn’t just family — he was a guide, a friend, and someone who made the world brighter just by being in it.

“He had a way of making everyone feel seen, heard, and valued. His kindness was quiet but constant, his strength steady, and his laugh something we will all miss deeply.”

West Yorkshire Police's Major Collision Enquiry Team is continuing to investigate and has issued a further appeal for witnesses.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Officers want to hear from anyone who witnessed the crash or saw the Cupra Leon travelling along Barkerend Road towards Bradford Moor Park at around the time of the incident.

“Anyone who can assist the investigation is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting log reference 1622 of September 3.”
