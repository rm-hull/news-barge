## Is the proposed £1 tourist tax enough - that can't even buy a coffee?

Though it is good news that a tourist tax is being considered for visitors to York, £1 seems an unnecessarily low amount.

For £1 one can’t even buy half a cup of coffee.

  **Rose BerlDe Little CourtVine StreetYork___**

## York's planning decisions need to be fair

A pergola in a hidden yard behind La Piazza restaurant in Goodramgate is refused because “it would form an incongruous and intrusive feature”.

But the very same planning authority have on four occasions allowed a stack of steel shipping containers to sit on a prominent Piccadilly site.

Those intrusive alien containers not only blight the Central Historic Core Conservation Area but also their use is the cause of a serious nuisance to local residents.

Government guidance says users of the planning system have a right to expect decisions which are fair and consistent.

Little wonder the planning regime in York is viewed by many as anything but fair or consistent.

  **Matthew LaverackRetired ArchitectLord Mayors WalkYork**
