# Man jailed for sexual offences against 14-year-old more than 20 years ago

## A 63-year-old man has been jailed for sexual offences committed against a 14-year-old in Calderdale more than 20 years ago.

James Hartley, formerly of Shaw Hill, Halifax, committed the offences between 2004 and 2005, when the complainant was aged 14.

The complainant later came forward as an adult to report what had happened, prompting a police investigation.

Hartley subsequently stood trial at Bradford Crown Court and was found guilty in June this year of three counts of sexual activity with a child.

![Bradford Crown Court](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmUzNTZkZWQ2LTI2NGEtNGI4MS1iYmQ3LTc3NTBhNDRlYzVlMTo0YTliNGI4MC03ODE3LTQ4ODAtODJjNS1kNTI3ZWU0MGEwNjA=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



He returned to Bradford Crown Court on Monday (Sep 7), where he was sentenced to six years in prison.

Hartley was also made subject to a Sexual Harm Prevention Order and will remain on the Sex Offenders Register for life.

Following the sentencing, detectives praised the survivor for the courage shown in coming forward and supporting the prosecution.

Detective Constable Carly Hemingway of Calderdale Child Vulnerability Exploitation Team, said: “The complainant in this case has shown significant bravery in coming forward and supporting the investigation and prosecution which has resulted in Hartley being jailed.

"The complainant has suffered significant trauma which has impacted on his life, and I hope this sentence has provided some comfort and helped towards closure.

"We encourage anyone who has experienced sexual abuse, whether recent or non-recent to come forward and report it.

“As this case, and many others demonstrates, the passage of time is not a barrier to investigating these offences. Victims will be supported, listened to and treated with dignity throughout the process.”
