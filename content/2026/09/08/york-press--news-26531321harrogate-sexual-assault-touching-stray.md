A woman was sexually assaulted by a man as she walked through parkland in a North Yorkshire town, police said.

Police said the "assault by touching" happened in The Stray in Harrogate, in the area between Knaresborough Road and Wetherby Road, at around 8.30pm on Sunday, September 6.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said an unknown man sexually assaulted the victim whilst running past her.

  


The spokesperson added: "The man then ran off in the direction of Wetherby Road.

"We are appealing for information about the man, or to hear from anyone who was in the area at the time, and who witnessed the incident."

North Yorkshire Police has described the man as white, with a skinny build, tall, wearing shorts and a blue/green running jacket and a white baseball cap.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police online in the first instance, by emailing [sarah.moore@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:sarah.moore@northyorkshire.police.uk) or use the ‘Tell us about’ section on the North Yorkshire Police website.

A live chat service is also available on the website from Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

Those unable to use these options can call 101 and ask for PC 957 Moore.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
