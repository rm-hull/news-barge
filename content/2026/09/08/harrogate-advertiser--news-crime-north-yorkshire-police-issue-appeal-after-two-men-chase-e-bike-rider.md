# North Yorkshire Police issue appeal after two men chase e-bike rider through Harrogate streets

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses following an affray incident involving three men in Harrogate.

The incident happened at around 9.15pm on Sunday, August 7, on North Park Road and Coach Road.

Police say the incident involved two men chasing another man who was riding an electric bike.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “We’re appealing for information about any descriptions of the suspects or any vehicles involved.”
