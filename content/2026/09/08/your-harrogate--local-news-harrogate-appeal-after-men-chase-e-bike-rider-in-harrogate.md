Police are appealing for information after two men reportedly chased another man riding an electric bike in Harrogate.

The incident, which is being investigated as an affray, happened at around 9.15pm on 7th August 2026.

It took place around North Park Road and Coach Road.

North Yorkshire Police would like information about the suspects or any vehicles believed to have been involved.

Anyone who can help can email PC Emily Williams at [Emily.Williams@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Emily.Williams@northyorkshire.police.uk) or use the live chat service on the force’s website between 9am and 5pm, Monday to Friday.

Alternatively, call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and ask for Emily Williams.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111.

Anyone providing information should quote reference 12260175252.
