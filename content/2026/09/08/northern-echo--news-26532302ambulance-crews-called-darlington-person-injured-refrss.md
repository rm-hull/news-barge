Emergency services were called to a Darlington street following reports of a person injured.

Two ambulance vehicles were pictured on Tubwell Row, just outside of The Tubwell Tap, on Tuesday (September 8) following an incident.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) has confirmed that a rapid response paramedic and an ambulance crew were sent to the scene at 11.27am.

One person was assessed at the scene, but was not taken to hospital.

A NEAS spokesperson said: "We were called to a person injured on Tubwell Row in Darlington at 11.27am on 8 September 2026.

"We dispatched a rapid response paramedic and an ambulance crew. They assessed one patient who was not taken to hospital."
