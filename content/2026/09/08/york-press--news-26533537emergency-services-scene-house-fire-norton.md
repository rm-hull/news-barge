EMERGENCY services are at the scene of a fire at a property in a North Yorkshire town. *(Image: Max Downing)*

EMERGENCY services are at the scene of a fire at a property in a North Yorkshire town.

A fire crew is at the house in Vine Street, Norton.

Crews are currently using a hose reel.

The police and ambulance are also in attendance.
