Free body composition scans are now available as part of a new health and wellbeing initiative in the East Riding.

East Riding Library and Leisure members can access the Boditrax scans at select libraries and all leisure centres.

The scans offer a detailed breakdown of body health in just 30 seconds, including 15 measurements such as body fat percentage, muscle mass, hydration, and metabolic age.

Nick Coultish, cabinet member for culture, leisure and tourism at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "Boditrax gives residents a fast and convenient way to learn more about their health.

"By making the service available in libraries and leisure centres, we're helping more people access the information and support they need to make positive lifestyle changes."

The scans are free for library and leisure members, who can view results and track progress using the Boditrax app.

The scheme supports the council’s wider public health goals, including cardiovascular health.

Residents can also borrow blood pressure monitors from libraries, helping them monitor one of the most important risk factors for heart disease.

Boditrax is available at all East Riding leisure sites and selected libraries.

For a full list of locations and more information, visit www.eastridinglibraries.co.uk/more-than-books/health-zone/boditrax.
