A SUNDAY pub visit could end with a £1,000 cash prize.

Bradford pubs are taking part in The Sunday Social £10k Giveaway, a regional promotion offering football fans the chance to win £1,000 every Sunday until September 20.

The scheme is sponsored by Sky and open to the first 100 eligible adults through the door at participating pubs from 3pm.

Amy Keane said: "Be one of the first 100 eligible adults through the door from 3pm to grab your raffle ticket."

Fans can enjoy the late Sunday fixture, and if their pub is selected, a draw will take place within 15 minutes after the final whistle.

The winner must be in the building to claim the cash prize.

Even if the pub isn’t picked for the main draw, one customer will still win a free drink through a raffle.

Participating Bradford pubs include the Crown Hotel and Malt Kiln Inn.
