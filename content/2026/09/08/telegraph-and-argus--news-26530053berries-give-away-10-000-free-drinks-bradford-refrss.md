A BRADFORD business is set to give away 10,000 free drinks to primary school pupils as part of a new initiative recognising achievement, effort and positive behaviour.

Berries has launched its School Reward Initiative, which will see teachers across Bradford rewarded with vouchers they can hand out to children throughout the academic year.

The scheme aims to celebrate a range of accomplishments, including academic achievement, improved attendance, kindness, good behaviour and pupils who go the extra mile.

## Teachers to decide who receives rewards

Under the initiative, schools will have the freedom to decide when a child deserves recognition.

Each voucher will entitle pupils to a complimentary drink from Berries, which offers a range of shakes, smoothies, juices, lassis and speciality drinks.

The Bradford-based business says the programme has been designed to support schools while giving something back to the community that has helped the company grow.

## Giving back to the community

Raza Zulfiqar from Berries, said: "Bradford has shown Berries an incredible amount of support as we've grown, so we wanted to create something that genuinely gives something back.

"Schools and teachers do an amazing job encouraging young people every single day, and sometimes something as simple as recognising a child for trying harder, being kind or achieving something they're proud of can mean a lot.

"Our aim is to give away 10,000 drinks to primary school pupils across Bradford."

## Schools invited to get involved

Berries says the first participating schools are now being contacted, with plans to roll out the scheme across Bradford during the current academic year.

Schools interested in joining the Berries School Reward Initiative can contact the company via [info@berriesdrinks.co.uk.](mailto:info@berriesdrinks.co.uk.)

## Expansion across Bradford

Berries, which describes itself as "The Drink Crafters", has expanded its presence across Bradford and says it is continuing to look for new ways to support local communities.

The business currently operates branches on Killinghall Road and Great Horton Road, while a drive-in location opened on Manchester Road last year.

After opening earlier this year, the Broadway venue became Berries' fifth location in Bradford as the company continues to grow its presence across the city.

## More than 180 drinks on the menu

Known for its extensive menu, Berries serves more than 180 drinks, including fresh juices, smoothies, shakes, mocktails and bubble teas.

Customers can also choose from a range of freshly prepared bagels and speciality drinks, with the company regularly introducing new flavours and seasonal additions.

Popular menu items include matcha-based drinks, tapioca bubble teas and a variety of handcrafted beverages, helping the Bradford-founded brand build a loyal following ahead of its latest opening.
