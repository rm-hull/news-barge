A next of kin appeal has been launched for a 67-year-old Pickering man who died at home.

David Belt died at his home address in May. There are not believed to be any suspicious circumstances surrounding his death.

The coroner is seeking information to identify Mr Belt’s next of kin.

RECOMMENDED READING:

Anyone who can help should email [coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk), marking the email for the attention of Sandra Prince. The appeal seeks assistance from members of the public.
