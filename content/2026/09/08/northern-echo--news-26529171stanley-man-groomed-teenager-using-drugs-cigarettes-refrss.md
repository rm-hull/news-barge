A man allegedly used drugs and cigarettes to groom a teenager into a sexual relationship and called her his ‘sexy s***’.

Mark Faulds faces a string of sexual charges after the teenager made a series of allegations against him.

The 41-year-old was arrested after the teenager told police where to find evidence, including condoms recovered from woodland near his home, Teesside Crown Court heard.

A jury heard how the teenager also told officers where Faulds had hidden a rag he used to clean himself.

Prosecutor Annelise Haugstad said police found Faulds’s DNA on condoms and the rag during searches of his home and nearby woodland.

She said Faulds told the teenager he fantasised about her after she told him she liked him, before the grooming began.

“It was a couple of days after this conversation that the first sexual activity took place,” she said. “They kissed together and he put his hands on her bottom and placed her hands on his penis over clothing.”

Miss Haugstad said the abuse became more serious after the first incident. Faulds allegedly offered her cigarettes or a ‘blow-back from his bong’ before calling her his ‘sexy s***’.

“It is our case that the cigarettes and drugs, the compliments and the attention were all calculated grooming behaviour so that he could carry out the sexual abuse,” she said.

“She says it got to the point where she was under the influence of cannabis or drunk because of his grooming behaviour towards her.”

The court heard that Faulds took the teenager to woodland near his home and had sex with her after buying a packet of condoms.

Faulds, of Oxford Street, Stanley, denies 14 charges, including rape, assault by penetration and causing a child to engage in sexual activity.

The jury heard that Faulds denied all the charges in police interviews.

The trial is expected to continue all week.
