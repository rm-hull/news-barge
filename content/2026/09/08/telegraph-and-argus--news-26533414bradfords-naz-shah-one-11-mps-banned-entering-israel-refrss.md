A BRADFORD MP is one of 11 sitting Members of Parliament to be banned from entering Israel.

The Israeli government banned 11 MPs, including Bradford West MP Naz Shah, from entering the country after the UK announced sanctions on illegal West Bank settlements.

Israeli foreign minister Gideon Saar said in a statement that the MPs were “involved in antisemitic and anti-Israel activity, as well as those who deny the very existence of the State of Israel”.

Israel announced its own counter measures after Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband set out the sanctions as part of a reset in the way the Labour Government will deal with Benjamin Netanyahu’s administration.

Those barred from entering Israel are:

- Naz Shah
- Jeremy Corbyn
- Zarah Sultana
- Diane Abbott
- Hannah Spencer
- Carla Denyer
- Sian Berry
- Ellie Chowns
- John McDonnell
- Richard Burgon
- Adrian Ramsay

Fahad Ansari, director of advocacy group Riverway to the Sea, has also been banned.

The Telegraph & Argus has approached Naz Shah, a Labour MP, for a comment about the ban.

Responding to all its MPs being barred, the Green Party vowed not to be “bullied”.

A party spokesperson said: “We will not be bullied into silence by the Israeli government.

“We are proud of our MPs for standing up for Palestinian rights and demanding an end to British complicity in genocide and illegal occupation.

“These threats only strengthen our resolve.

“The British government must stand firm and follow these overdue restrictions with a full arms embargo and wide-ranging sanctions.”

Mr Corbyn meanwhile said he would “keep speaking up for peace, freedom and justice for the Palestinian people”.

He said: “I have visited all parts of Palestine on several occasions, where I have witnessed the horrors of occupation, as well as the resilience and humanity of the Palestinian people.

“They are the ones who suffer from Israel’s apartheid.

“It is their freedom which is egregiously curtailed, alongside thousands of Palestinian refugees who are denied the right of return.

“As the world wakes up to the genocide in Gaza, it is shocking that Israel continues to deny entry to UN investigators and international journalists.

“Israel doesn’t want the world to see what it is doing – but we can never give up in our attempts to expose the truth.

“That is the real scandal. Israel’s genocide cannot go unpunished – and I will keep speaking up for peace, freedom and justice for the Palestinian people.”

The former Labour leader is a vocal critic of the Israeli government, regularly attending pro-Palestine marches.

The now-independent MP for Islington North had the Labour whip in Parliament removed in October 2020 over his response to the equalities watchdog report on antisemitism in the party during his tenure as leader.
