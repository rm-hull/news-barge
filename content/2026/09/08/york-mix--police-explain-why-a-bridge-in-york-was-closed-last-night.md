Police have issued an update on why Ouse Bridge in York was closed last night (Monday)

The incident related to the concern for the safety of a teenage girl.

The bridge was closed to vehicles and pedestrians at 10.21pm.

It was brought to a safe conclusion at 11.15pm and the road reopened soon after.
