A cannabis grow was found in a flat above a County Durham deli after police were called about "suspicious activity".

Officers were called to the flat above Filthy's on Stockton Road in Seaham around 10am on Friday, September 4.

A large number of cannabis plants were found inside the upstairs flat when police arrived.

Police dismantled the grow and launched an investigation.

The discovery came after crime scene investigators were seen outside nearby business Filthy's Seaham.

Pictures seen by The Northern Echo showed white-suited investigators and police officers in the car park.

The deli had told customers it could not open on the morning of Friday, September 4, because of “unforeseen circumstances”.

Filthy's later confirmed it had reopened, but was operating on a restricted basis, with the bar closed and operating at takeaway only.

The business said all booked functions, parties and events were “completely unaffected” and would go ahead as normal.

The deli, at Unit 1 of The Mill Inn on Stockton Road, opened as a takeaway in January this year.

After reports and speculation about the site, Filthy's issued a further statement.

The business said it had stayed quiet to avoid “speculate, spread rumours or comment on something that has nothing to do with the running of our business and completely outside of our control”.

It said the incident had a “very real and damaging impact” on the business, forcing it to close parts of its operation and change how it worked.

The statement added: “Behind the name Filthys is a small independent business run by two people who work unbelievably hard to make it succeed, alongside a team of staff who genuinely care about this place and the customers we serve.”
