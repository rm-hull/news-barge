A busy Redcar café has thanked customers for their support after a break-in forced it to close.

The Boathouse and Terrace, on Redcar seafront, announced that it would temporarily shut after discovering it had been broken into during the early hours of Sunday, September 6.

The incident happened just hours after the food spot had hosted a wedding celebration, with staff describing the contrast between the successful event and the discovery of the burglary as “one extreme to another.”

In a later update, the owners said they had spent the day working to get the business ready to reopen, adding that they were determined not to let the incident define the venue.

They wrote: “We were buzzing at how gorgeous our little Boathouse looked and at what a fab time everyone was having

"To be woken up early this morning to be told we had been broken into was beyond a kick in the stomach.

“We have worked hard all day to get things turned around so we can open tomorrow – we won’t be beaten by scumbags.”

"Running a small business is so hard, especially with the current climate, and to say we put our hearts and souls into it is no joke," they added.

People rushed to comment, as one said: "This is awful, hope you are OK.

"You guys work so hard to have a lovely place. It boils my pee how these people get away with doing this."

"I am so sorry this has happened again to your gorgeous place I love what you have created," added another.

A third wrote: "Aw, so sorry this happened."

The business put an appeal for help on social media, asking anyone with Ring doorbell footage or CCTV recorded between 4am and 5am to check their cameras and help identify the direction in which the offender travelled.

The post highlighted the pressures facing small businesses and said every customer purchase helps keep the venue open and staff employed.

Dozens of people praised the business’ resilience and urged staff not to let the break-in get them down.

One commenter said: “Improvise, adapt and overcome – don’t let the oxygen thieves ever win.”

Another praised the venue’s “fantastic staff”, while a third said it was “an amazing place to enjoy our summer holidays.”

The Boathouse and Terrace reopened at 9am on Monday, September 7.

The food spot has a 4.6 rating on Google and is best known for its hot sandwiches, coffee, "comfortable" atmosphere and "great" service.

The Northern Echo has approached The Boathouse and Terrace for further comment.
