A councillor has raised concerns about loose paving, after a woman was taken to hospital following a fall in Pudsey on Monday morning.

Pudsey councillor Simon Seary said he was sorry to hear that a resident had reportedly tripped on a loose paving stone and required an ambulance, adding that he had requested an urgent repair.

## 
  **Emergency call received**

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said it received an emergency call at 11.13am reporting that a person had fallen on Robin Lane.

An ambulance was dispatched to the scene following the call.

## 
  **Councillor raises paving concerns**

Mr Seary said he hoped the woman would make a speedy recovery and had marked the loose paving stone with yellow paint, placed a cone over it and requested an urgent repair.

He said the incident highlighted the damage that vehicles parking on pavements can cause, adding that there had been a significant reduction in loose paving stones since bollards were installed on Robin Lane and Lowtown.

The councillor also questioned whether more bollards were needed across Pudsey town centre to protect pavements and improve pedestrian safety.

## 
  **Patient taken to hospital**

The casualty was assessed by paramedics before being taken to hospital for further treatment.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service confirmed the patient was conveyed to hospital but did not provide details about the nature of the injuries.

## 
  **Link not confirmed**

Concerns had previously been raised locally about a loose paving stone on Robin Lane, with reports suggesting a resident had tripped and required medical attention.

A Yorkshire Ambulance Service spokesperson said: “We received an emergency call at 11.13am on Monday 7 September to report a person who had fallen on Robin Lane in Pudsey.

“An ambulance was dispatched to the scene, and the patient was conveyed to hospital.”

However, it is not known whether the fall attended by ambulance crews on Monday was linked to the paving stone highlighted by Mr Seary.

## 
  **'Let me know what you think'**

Mr Seary wrote on Facebook: "I’m sorry to hear that a resident tripped on a loose paving stone on Robin Lane today and needed an ambulance. I hope she makes a speedy recovery.

"I have marked the loose paving stone with yellow paint, placed a cone over it and requested an urgent repair.

"This incident highlights the damage that vehicles parking on pavements can cause. We have seen a significant reduction in loose paving stones since bollards were installed on Robin Lane and Lowtown.

"Do we need more bollards to protect pavements across Pudsey town centre and keep pedestrians safe? Let me know what you think."
