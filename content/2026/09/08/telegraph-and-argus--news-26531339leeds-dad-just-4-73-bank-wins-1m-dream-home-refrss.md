A father-of-three with just £4.73 in his bank account has won a £1 million dream home.

Mohamed Mahdi, a security worker from Leeds, was surprised with the life-changing prize through a BOTB competition.

The five-bedroom property is located in Saham Toney, Norfolk.

Mr Mahdi said: "I never even dreamt of this.

"I've just been trying to help my mum.

"I've also got kids who are in school and have just been trying my best."

He has been working extra shifts to support his mother, who is battling cancer in Africa.

He said: "Priority number one is my mum and kids and that they live in happy environments.

"I'm the only one in the family, so I have to take good care of my mother."

BOTB presenters Christian Williams and Nicole Jeffery initially told Mr Mahdi he had won a Mercedes and £100,000, before revealing the true prize.

Mr Mahdi, a BOTB Pass member, is considering taking the £700,000 cash alternative.

He said: "It has really changed my life.

"I will send my kids to a better school and take good care of my mum.

"I need the cash, but next time I get an opportunity like this, I'll take the house."
