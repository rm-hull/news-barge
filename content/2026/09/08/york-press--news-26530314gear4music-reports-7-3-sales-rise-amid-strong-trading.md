Hot weather has been blamed for slowing the growth of sales at York-based Gear4music.

However, the company, which is the UK's largest retailer of musical instruments and music equipment, still reports strong trading.

Gear4music said in a trading update today (Tuesday, September 8) that business for the current financial year so far is in line with expectations, with sales rising 7.3 per cent in the five months to August 31, 2026.

Andrew Wass, executive chair of Gear4music, said: "We are pleased with this progress, particularly given that sales growth moderated during July and August amid an unusually prolonged period of hot weather, and against the very strong growth achieved in the corresponding period of FY26."

  


The company plans to issue a trading update for the six months to September 30, 2026, on October 20, followed by half-year results on November 17.

The retailer reported consensus market expectations for the year ending March 31, 2027, are for revenues of £200.2 million, EBITDA of £16 million, and profit before tax of £6 million.

Today's update follows a strong set of full-year results published in June, which showed a more than 500 per cent increase in profit before tax, supported by record revenues.

For the year to March 31, 2026, Gear4music reported revenues of £190.7 million, a 30 per cent rise from £146.7 million the previous year.

Gross profit climbed 37 per cent to £54.2 million, up from £39.7 million.

In the fourth quarter of FY26, revenue growth reached 26 per cent, continuing the company’s upward trajectory.

Gear4music said at the time: "EBITDA was above market expectations, the company continued, as was profit before tax."

Growth, the company added, was driven in part by recent technology and systems upgrades, which the company believes will deliver further benefits in the coming year.
