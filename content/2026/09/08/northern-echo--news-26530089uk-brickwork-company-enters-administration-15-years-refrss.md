A UK brickwork company has fallen into administration after more than 15 years in business.

Harlequin Brickwork, founded in 2011, operated across London and the South East on major residential schemes.

At its peak, it employed more than 500 site operatives and over 50 office staff and recorded a turnover of around £24 million.

Its collapse follows a winding-up petition filed by ACS Stainless Steel Fixings, which was due to be heard at the High Court in Leeds on Tuesday, September 8, Construction Enquirer reports.


Administrators Marco Piacquadio and Alan Coleman of FTS Recovery have been appointed.

Billy Sutcliffe, founder and director, remained as the only director after James McGrail stepped down as finance director last month.

The company’s portfolio included high-profile projects such as Berkeley Homes’ One Tower Bridge, valued at £3.5 million, and schemes at Bracknell town centre, Colindale Gardens, Kew Bridge, Brentford Football Stadium, and sites in Staines and Wandsworth.

Turnover fell from £18.5 million in 2022 to £10.5 million in 2023, with pre-tax profit reported at £151,000.

The company specialised in brickwork, masonry, and scaffolding for large-scale residential developments.


Discussing its work, Harlequin Brickwork said: "With years of industry experience and a proven track record of successful project delivery, we are dedicated to providing our clients with superior workmanship and exceptional service.

"Our proactive approach, combined with a highly trained and skilled workforce, enables us to meet demanding timelines without compromising quality, integrity, or the highest standards of health and safety."

Harlequin Brickwork has been contacted by Newsquest for comment.
