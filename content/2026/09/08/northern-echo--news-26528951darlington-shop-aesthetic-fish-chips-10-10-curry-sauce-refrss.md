When it comes to fish and chips, everyone has their favourite local.

One Darlington chippy, though, is attracting a growing number of fans thanks to its crispy batter, generous portions and highly-rated curry sauce.

Barton's Fish and Chips, on Longfield Road, has quietly built a loyal customer base over the years and is now earning praise well beyond its regulars.

The family-run takeaway currently boasts a 4.8-star rating on TripAdvisor, where customers regularly highlight the quality of the food, friendly service and value for money.

One customer wrote: "The staff were both really friendly - my fish and chips were delicious.

"We all enjoyed our food and are looking forward to the next time we’re in the area so we can call again."

The takeaway specialises in traditional fish and chips and seafood and has carved out a strong reputation amongst diners in the area.

More recently, the shop attracted attention on social media after food influencer 'Lunch With Loz' featured Barton's in a post to their Instagram followers.


Sharing a photo of a meal from the takeaway, the influencer encouraged followers to consider a 'chippy tea' and explained that Barton's has become their go-to choice when visiting family in Darlington.

They said: "When we go to my in-laws for chippy, their local is Barton's just off Longfield Road, and this has become our go-to when we are at their house.

"The batter is always super crispy here, and if you ask for scraps, they give you so many, plus the curry sauce is 10/10 which is a must for me."

The post also highlighted the shop's use of takeaway boxes rather than traditional wrapping paper, something the influencer said makes the food easier to eat while helping to keep the batter crisp.

Lunching With Loz added: "One thing I love here is how it comes in a box. I do wish everywhere did this its so much nicer to eat and avoids any batter stuck to the paper."

One individual commented: "This is the most aesthetic fish and chips I've ever seen."

"Oh wow," added another. "Could eat this right," insisted another.

One other said: "Those fish and chips look so good, scraps and curry sauce are a must."

"This has made me want a chippy," insisted one other.

It is closed on a Sunday and Monday, and open Tuesday to Thursday from 4.30pm until 8.30pm, and 11.30am until 2pm on a Saturday.

On fish and chip Friday it is open from 11.30am until 2pm, closed for a short period before reopening at 4.30pm while 8.30pm.

Prices range from £9 up to £14.50 for fish and chip portions, depending on size and type of fish.
