FIREFIGHTERS have issued a warning about the dangers of cooking with hot oil after a kitchen fire in Bradford left one person requiring first aid.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to Morton Road, Laisterdyke, at 2.28pm.

Five fire engines attended the incident and extinguished a fire involving a chip pan using breathing apparatus and a hose reel.

One person was treated with first aid at the scene.

## Warning over dangers of hot oil

Following the incident, the fire service urged people to take care when cooking with hot oil, warning that it can ignite quickly if left unattended.

A spokesperson said: "Hot oil can ignite quickly.

"Consider safer alternatives, such as thermostatically controlled appliances.

"To cook with hot oil safely there are several simple tips which can help protect you.

"Never leave hot oil unattended, do not fill chip pans or frying pans more than one-third full and change cooking oil regularly."

## Simple steps can help prevent fires

Firefighters are encouraging residents to follow basic safety precautions when cooking to reduce the risk of fire in the home.

As well as never leaving hot oil unattended, people are advised to avoid overfilling pans and to ensure cooking oil is regularly replaced.

Further cooking safety advice is available on the West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service website.
