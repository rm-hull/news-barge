As part of the nationwide warning day, a test alarm will be triggered at 11 am, followed by the all-clear at 11.45 am, as announced by the Senate Administration on social media. According to information on the Civil Protection website, warning messages will also be disseminated via the Nina warning app, on the radio, on television and through other channels.
