A man was taken to hospital after suffering suspected stab wounds in Bishop Auckland.

Police were called just after 11am on Sunday, September 6, after reports that a man had been attacked in Wensleydale Square.

The man, in his thirties, was taken to hospital for treatment.

Police believe the attack happened on the Woodhouse Estate about an hour before they were called.

An investigation is now underway and officers are appealing for witnesses.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: " An investigation is underway after a man was assaulted in Bishop Auckland.

"Police were called just after 11am on Sunday (September 6) following reports that a man had been found with stab wounds in Wensleydale Square.

"The incident is believed to have happened on the Woodhouse Estate around an hour earlier.

"The man, who is in his thirties, was taken to hospital for treatment.
