A new scheme is bringing the joy of reading to the seafront.

Little Libraries have been introduced along Sunderland’s coastline to encourage visitors to borrow or exchange books while enjoying coastal views.

The scheme is a partnership between Sunderland’s Seafront Volunteer Group and Sunderland Seafront BID, with support from the National Literacy Trust.

Roberta Redecke, head of business services at Sunderland BID, said: "People are really embracing the Little Libraries and finding them an incredibly useful way to take the opportunity to read against the backdrop of Sunderland’s magnificent coastline.

"It’s a small but really effective way to make a visit to the seafront even more enjoyable and something everyone can take part in."

The four Little Libraries are located at The Seaburn Inn, The Tram Shelter, Adventure Sunderland, and the Marina.

Each library is uniquely decorated by Su Devine from Art Café Sunderland, and all are proving popular with visitors.

The project supports the National Year of Reading in 2026, which aims to inspire more people to read for pleasure.

The aim is to get visitors to enjoy reading by the sea by choosing one of the offerings in the libraries, while at the same time giving a new home to books which might otherwise just be gathering dust at home.

Plans are underway for a special celebration event on October 3.

Visitors will be invited to join Look For the Book, with books hidden at locations between the Little Libraries at Roker and Seaburn.

The hope is the event will also encourage people to explore the coastline and go home with another story to read.
