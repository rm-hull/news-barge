A new 426-space multi-storey car park is set to open in a Yorkshire seaside town later this year.

Development of the Beck Hill car park has reached its final stage will open to the public in October.

Located between Manor Street and Hilderthorpe Road, the car park is designed to improve accessibility for shoppers, workers, and visitors.

  


The structure includes 31 accessible parking bays and electric vehicle charging points.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has invested £15.5 million into the project - which forms part of its town centre regeneration plan.

Councillor Anne Handley, leader of East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "The opening of the new Beck Hill multi-storey car park this October is a transformative moment for Bridlington.

"This facility directly addresses historic parking challenges during busy seasons while creating a clean, modern gateway into the town.

"By making it seamless for residents to shop locally and for tourists to explore our brilliant town centre, this multi-million-pound investment will provide a long-term boost to the local economy."

Designed by architecture firm Carey Jones Chapman Tolcher and built by Henry Boot Construction, the car park features an exterior inspired by ocean waves.

The scheme also includes improvements to the nearby Gypsey Race watercourse and green space.

An official ribbon-cutting ceremony will be held in October, with details about parking fees, opening hours, and payment options to be published on the council’s website ahead of the launch.
