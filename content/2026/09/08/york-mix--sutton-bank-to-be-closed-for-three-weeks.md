The annual closure of the A170 at Sutton Bank will last three weeks this year.

This is to allow for further drainage work and repairs.

The closure will last from **7am on Sunday, 13 September, until 7pm on Sunday, 4 October.**

As usual a diversion route will be signposted.

Regular maintenance is vital to preserve the road’s condition, protect the public and prevent deterioration that could result in unplanned disruption.

**Executive member for highways and transport, Cllr Malcolm Taylor, said:** “The works will include planned maintenance to the rock face and routine maintenance of the road and drainage. 

“Carrying out these improvements at the same time will maximise the benefits of the road closure and help minimise the need for disruption to road users in the future.”

**Cllr Alyson Baker, who represents Hillside and Raskelf, added:** “We will do everything possible to minimise disruption and would like to thank motorists, residents and businesses for their patience and cooperation while the work is carried out.”

Access to homes will be maintained whenever possible, but the nature of the work means there could be times when that access has to be restricted.
