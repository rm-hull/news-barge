The Yorkshire Air Ambulance was called to a serious collision in a village near Selby yesterday (Monday).

Emergency services were called to the incident on Holme Road, Spaldington, between Selby and Holme-on-Spalding Moor.

An HGV and a motorbike had collided.

#### Spaldington

A Humberside Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A reciprocating saw was in use to give helimed paramedics access to a casualty.

“Oxygen therapy in use on motorcyclist until arrival of helimed. Incident left with the police and ambulance service.”
