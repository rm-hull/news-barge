A popular Thai restaurant and hotel has been put up for sale.

Thai Season Restaurant & Hotel in Wilberfoss has been listed on the market for £1.5 million.

The venue has sister restaurants/take aways in York on Bishopthorpe Road and on Shambles Market.

Located on the A1079 Hull Road, the business, which has been extensively refurbished, promises a unique trading environment with themed décor inspired by various regions of Thailand.

  



  The hotel and restaurant from the main A1079. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

The property comprises a standalone hospitality facility, featuring a destination restaurant, bar, and boutique hotel operation.

The hotel provides ten individually themed en-suite letting bedrooms, each designed to represent different regions of Thailand, enhancing its appeal to overnight visitors.

The property also includes a reception area, four themed dining rooms, a feature bar area, a commercial catering kitchen, customer WC facilities, and ancillary storage and staff facilities.

  The restaurant interior. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

The property stands on a substantial plot just shy of an acre, with extensive frontage to the A1079, which is one of the main routes linking York, Pocklington, Market Weighton, Beverley, and Hull.

The agents, Christie & Co, say this position benefits from significant daily vehicle movement, with traffic counts indicating more than 20,000 cars travel to and from York on the A1079.

The site also provides customer parking, servicing, and landscaped external areas complementing the overall hospitality offer.

The surrounding area comprises a mix of village housing, rural catchments, tourism attractions, and commercial traffic, providing the business with a broad customer base.

  The hotel interior. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

The business, the agents continued, has shown a consistent turnover, with the year ending July 2025 seeing a turnover of £770,041, and the year ending July 2026 projected to reach around £780,000.

The property is freehold and has a rateable value of £21,500, effective from April 1, 2026.

The business operates with a premises licence and opens from Wednesday to Sunday from 5.30pm to 9.30pm, while remaining closed on Mondays and Tuesdays.

Christie & Co, which is handling the sale, said: "Rarely do hospitality opportunities of this nature come to the market.

"The combination of a substantial freehold site, prominent main road position, established restaurant business, and hotel accommodation provides a platform for significant future growth.

"Opportunities exist to further develop the accommodation offering, increase corporate and tourism trade, expand event and private dining revenues, and capitalise on the property's strong roadside presence.

"The success of the wider Thai Season brand demonstrates the strength of the concept and provides an excellent foundation for future operators or investors."
