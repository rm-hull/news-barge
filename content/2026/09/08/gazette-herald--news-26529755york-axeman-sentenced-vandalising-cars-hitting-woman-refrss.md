An axeman who attacked a woman and vandalised her car in York has been sentenced by magistrates.

Kevin Jackson is also now subject to two restraining orders aimed at protecting the woman and a man whose car he also vandalised.

Because the 44-year-old, of Cemetery Road, central York, pleaded guilty to four offences, York magistrates decided not to send him for sentence by a crown court judge who could give him a sentence of more than a year.

  


Instead, they gave him a 12-month prison sentence, the maximum that can be passed at a magistrates court. Under a law passed earlier this year, they had to suspend it and did so for two years on condition he does a 26-day rehabilitative programme and 12 days’ rehabilitative activities. His axe was confiscated and he must pay £650 compensation to the man.

He pleaded guilty to causing criminal damage to the woman’s car and assaulting her, criminal damage to the man’s car and carrying an axe in public. All the offences occurred on July 31 in York.

The two restraining orders each last for two years and between them ban him from contacting the man and the woman and from going to their home addresses.
