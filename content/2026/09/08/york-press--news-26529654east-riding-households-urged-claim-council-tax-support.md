Households in the East Riding are missing out on financial help that could reduce their council tax bill.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council is urging residents to check if they are eligible for council tax support and other schemes that could reduce household bills and provide extra help during difficult times.

Councillor Nigel Wilkinson, cabinet member for finance and governance at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "We know that many households are continuing to feel the pressure of rising living costs, and some residents may not realise that help is available.

"This support can make a real difference to household budgets, whether you are working, self-employed, receiving benefits, or have recently experienced a change in circumstances.

"I would encourage anyone who is struggling financially to check the support they are entitled to and take advantage of the additional help available."

Council tax support is available to low-income households, part-time workers, the self-employed, and those who have recently seen a change in their financial situation.

The amount of support offered depends on income, savings, household makeup, and whether other benefits are received.

Additional assistance is available through the Crisis and Resilience Fund (CRF), a government scheme that helps people on low incomes who have sudden money problems.
