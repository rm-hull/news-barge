A COMMUNITY library is reopening its doors following essential refurbishment.

Thornbury Community Library, based in Bradford’s Thornbury Centre, will welcome visitors back to a refreshed space offering an expanded collection of books, improved facilities, and a renewed focus on serving the BD3 community.

The reopening marks a new chapter for the Thornbury Centre as it continues its work with families, women, and young people.

The updated library will offer something for all ages, with free Wi-Fi and computer access, children’s storytime sessions, homework clubs, and a programme of workshops and events.

A grand reopening celebration will be held on Friday, September 18, at 11am at the Thornbury Centre, with activities for families, children’s crafts, and refreshments.

The event will be attended by Bradford Libraries and MP Imran Hussain.

The Thornbury Centre first opened in 1999 following the demolition of St Margaret’s Parish Church.

Instead of rebuilding a traditional church, the congregation chose to create a modern community centre that would serve all residents while also providing a new home for the parish.

The Thornbury Charity was established in 2026 with a mission to revitalise the centre as a hub of community life in BD3.

The centre continues to support people across Bradford.
