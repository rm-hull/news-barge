A new dog-grooming salon offering puppy pamper sessions and full grooms will open in County Durham next week.

GoGo Grooming will welcome its first customers at 17 High Street, Willington, on Wednesday, September 16.

The salon promises full grooms, puppy grooming and bath-and-dry appointments in a clean, comfortable setting, with a friendly service.

The salon said: “We are so excited to be joining the community and cannot wait to meet all your wonderful dogs.

“We are currently busy renovating our salon to create a clean, comfortable and relaxing space for your furry friends.

“We cannot wait to welcome you and your lovely dogs very soon.”

Full grooms will cost from £40 for extra-small dogs to £85 for extra-large breeds. Puppy grooms cost from £30, while nail trimming is £10 and teeth brushing is £3.

The business says prices are a guide and may vary by breed, size, coat, condition and appointment time.

The salon will open from 9.30am to 5.30pm Monday to Saturday and close on Sundays.

An online booking system is expected to go live before opening, with updates posted on the salon’s social media pages.
