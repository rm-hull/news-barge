A BRADFORD bakery built on family recipes could be named best in the district.

Hermano Bakery, founded by Safian Mahmood, secured a place on the T&A's Best Of shortlist following support from customers and businesses that stock its products.

The business, which has been operating for just two years, has grown from a Bradford dessert shop into a wholesale supplier serving cafés, restaurants and retailers across the UK.

## Family recipes at the heart of success

Mr Mahmood said the bakery was founded on recipes and baking techniques passed down by their mother, alongside encouragement from their parents.

The business initially focused on producing hand-baked cakes and desserts using premium ingredients for customers visiting its Bradford dessert shop.

Mr Mahmood said: "Many of our recipes and techniques were passed down by my mum, and we wanted customers to be able to taste the difference that quality, freshness and care can make."

  Slices of cake prepared by Bradford-based Hermano Bakery, which has been named among the Top 10 finalists in a national Bakery of the Year competition. 

*(Image: Hermano Bakery)*

## Growth beyond Bradford

What started as baking for customers at Hermano's own dessert shop soon developed into a growing wholesale operation after other businesses began requesting its products.

Mr Mahmood said: "People didn't just enjoy our cakes; they started asking if they could buy them for their own cafés, restaurants and dessert shops...that was the moment we realised we had created something special."

## Focus on quality

Mr Mahmood said the business has deliberately avoided mass production in favour of maintaining high standards.

Among its best-known products is its Matilda chocolate cake, which helped establish the bakery's reputation among customers.

However, Mr Mahmood said the long-term goal was always broader than a single product.

They said: "The vision was never about building a business around one cake.

"It was about creating a bakery built around quality...we don't want to simply produce cakes that look impressive. They have to taste exceptional too."

  Chocolate-covered cakes produced by Hermano Bakery, one of the products helping the Bradford business earn national recognition. 

*(Image: Hermano Bakery)*

## Recognition

Mr Mahmood described the Top 10 placing as a significant milestone for both the business and its staff.

"Making the Top 10 genuinely means a huge amount to us," they said.

"We are only two years into our bakery journey, so to be recognised alongside established and respected bakeries is something my family and the whole team are extremely proud of."

  One of the cakes produced by Bradford-based Hermano Bakery, featuring biscuit toppings and fruit sauce decoration. 

*(Image: Hermano Bakery)*

## Looking ahead

Hermano Bakery hopes to expand its product range, increase production capacity and continue reaching new customers across the UK while maintaining the standards on which it was founded.

Mr Mahmood said winning Bakery of the Year would represent recognition not only for the business but also for the family story behind it.

They added: "What began with my mother's recipes, my parents' support and a belief that people deserved better-quality cake has grown from a dessert shop in Bradford into a bakery supplying businesses across the UK...I want to continue building what my mother started."
