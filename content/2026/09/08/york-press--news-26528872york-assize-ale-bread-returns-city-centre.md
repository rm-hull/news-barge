## Medieval ale tasting tradition returns to York with pubs and costumes

The Assize of Ale and Bread tradition will return to York later this month *(Image: Supplied)*

The Assize of Ale and Bread tradition will return to York on Saturday, September 26, 2026.

The medieval tradition requires the Sheriff of York to visit local pubs and taste the ale to ensure its quality.

The custom originated from a need to regulate the commercial market for brewing equipment, which was expensive and required regulations to control quality and pricing.

The Sheriff would summon sergeants to test the quality of ale in the city's alehouses, with those failing to answer the summons facing fines or even time in the pillory.

The event will start at York Mansion House at 2pm, where the Town Crier will introduce the Sheriff of York to perform the traditional proclamation.

YO1 Radio will provide a fanfare in St Helen’s Square, and owls will also be in attendance.

Participants will then visit several pubs in the city centre, with the final stop at the York Food and Drink Festival Marquee in Parliament Street for the closing declaration.

All proceeds from the event will go to the Lord Mayor’s Charity Fund, this year supporting local charity York Cares and community group Shared York.

The event will conclude with a fish and chip supper, with ale sponsored and supported by 'Mr Chippy', 'Brew York', 'Dough', and 'The Potions Cauldron Group'.

The supper will take place at St Sampson’s Centre, York, and tickets are now on sale.

Participants who wear medieval or ancient costumes will receive a 10 per cent discount off drinks in all participating pubs.

Those seeking costume inspiration can visit Festival of Fun on Goodramgate, which is offering 10 per cent off purchases when mentioning “Assize of Ale”.

Sheriff Geoff Cossins said: "This event is truly a highlight of the Sheriff’s year in office, and I will be taking the ale tasting very seriously, I mean, what is not to enjoy, fantastic local beers, award-winning bread, ending with fish and chips."

People can pre-register their attendance for the Assize, which is free to join, by emailing [civicyorkfundraising@yahoo.com.](mailto:civicyorkfundraising@yahoo.com.)

Attendees are asked to mention any access needs in their email.

Tickets for the fish and chip supper can be purchased on Eventbrite.

The Assize of Ale and Bread is a historic tradition dating back to the Middle Ages, originally established to regulate the quality and pricing of ale in the city.

The tradition is upheld annually in York, providing a unique blend of history, community spirit, and charity fundraising.
