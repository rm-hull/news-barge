A children’s hospice doctor is launching a book exploring the realities of paediatric palliative care.

Dr Michelle Hills, chief medical officer at Martin House Children's Hospice, has written The Wrong Order of Things, which draws on her experiences supporting seriously ill children and their families.

The book will be published on September 10, with a launch event taking place at the White Rose Book Café in Thirsk on Saturday, September 12.

Dr Hills said: "Society needs to talk more openly about death, dying and grief.

"We need to extend this conversation to include the reality that children can die too.

"And we need to consider how we talk honestly and kindly to children about ill health, death, grief and bereavement."

The Wrong Order of Things uses anonymised, real-life composite stories to explore the emotional and practical challenges faced by families, as well as the moments of hope and happiness found amid hardship.

Dr Hills also wants the book to challenge misconceptions about palliative care and children's hospices.

She said: "Palliative and hospice care is about living before you die.

"The myth that they are just for end-of-life care creates barriers for referrals.

"So many children and families can benefit from hospice support."
