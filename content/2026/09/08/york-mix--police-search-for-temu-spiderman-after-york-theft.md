Do you know this fence hurdler?

Police want to talk to him as part of their enquiries into a theft in York.

It happened in August and the CCTV images have been released today (Tuesday).

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Please can you assist in identifying the Temu Spiderman of York?

“On the 11 August 2026 it has been reported by the victim that a parcel that had been delivered and left behind locked gates had been stolen.

“CCTV has now been obtained from the housing management company which has captured footage of the male in the attached images climbing the fence and stealing the contents of the parcel.

“We can now release the images to the public in the hopes someone can come forward with a name for the suspect.”
