An independent Harrogate travel agency is set to open its first high street shop later this month.

There Travel was founded by Colin Ord and Jake Todhunter in 2024 and currently operates through a growing team of homeworkers.

Its new Harrogate shop will give customers the opportunity to meet the team face-to-face and receive personal advice when planning their holidays.

The agency can arrange family holidays, beach breaks, cruises, luxury trips and tailor-made adventures.

Colin said:

“Harrogate is already home for There Travel, so opening our first shop here feels like a natural next step for us.

“We’ve established our base here in Harrogate and now we want to strengthen it, become even more visible within the town and give customers a place where they can come in, meet us and start planning their next adventure.

“This is a really exciting milestone for There Travel and one we’re incredibly proud of.”


The shop will operate alongside the company’s existing homeworking service, allowing customers across the country to continue arranging trips remotely.

Jake said:

“We want customers to be able to connect with There Travel in the way that suits them - whether that’s sitting down with us in Harrogate or speaking to one of our homeworkers.”


There Travel is a member of Advantage Travel Partnership, giving the independent agency access to a wide range of travel companies and products.

The shop is due to open at the end of September, with its location and official opening details set to be announced soon.
