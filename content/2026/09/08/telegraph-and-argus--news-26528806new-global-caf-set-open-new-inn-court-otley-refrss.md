TWO former colleagues are preparing to open a new cafe, hoping to bring globally-inspired food and a fresh concept to the town's thriving hospitality scene.

Roam Café, nestled in New Inn Court, is expected to open next week after the pair transformed a former bakery into a new venue centred around international breakfasts, brunches and seasonal lunches.

The business marks a major career change for new owners Rob Wardale and Emma Woodford, who first met while working together in advertising before deciding to pursue a shared passion for food.

## From advertising agency to café owners

Mr Wardale said the idea developed after he and Ms Woodford left the advertising industry.

He said: "Emma and I used to work together at an advertising agency and when we both moved on, she started talking about opening this sort of place.

"We sat in a bar one afternoon and started building the idea up, looking at what it could be and how it could work."

  The dining area inside Roam Café, a new independent venue inspired by flavours, cultures and stories from around the world. 

*(Image: T&A)*

Mr Wardale added: "I had absolutely no intention of getting involved at first, but the more we talked about it, the more invested I became and now I'm in the kitchen helping to make it happen."

While Mr Wardale previously ran a bar and music venue and Ms Woodford has worked in hospitality in Ilkley, Roam Café will be their first venture running a business of their own.

## Breathing new life into a former bakery

The pair have spent months renovating the premises after taking on what Mr Wardale described as a rundown former bakery.

He said: "When we first saw the building it was an old, ruined bakery. It was horrible, to be honest.

  Cookbooks from Mexico, India, Japan and Lebanon take pride of place inside Roam Café, reflecting the venue's global inspiration. 

*(Image: T&A)*

"But our landlord had a vision for it, and we worked closely with them to get it to where it is today.

Mr Wardale added: "There's still a few finishing touches to do, but it's been completely transformed and we're really proud of what we've created."

The owners are currently dealing with a handful of final practical issues ahead of opening, including getting internet and phone services installed in the older building.

## Food from all over the world

The cafe's concept is built around what Mr Wardale describes as "food from all over".

He said: "We'll have a small, focused menu with dishes inspired by different countries and cultures, alongside really good pastries, coffees and sweet treats.

"The idea is that every few months the lunch menu changes completely and takes customers somewhere new."

  The counter at Roam Café, where customers can enjoy speciality coffee and food influenced by cuisines from across the globe. 

*(Image: T&A)*

Customers can expect breakfast and brunch dishes including favourites from around the world, alongside pastries and baked goods from local suppliers.

The cafe's first seasonal lunch menu will be inspired by Lebanese cuisine.

Mr Wardale said: "The first seasonal menu is going to be inspired by Lebanese food and then we'll move on to somewhere else. We've already had local people asking for a Spanish menu.

"That's the exciting part for us. It means the cafe can keep evolving and people can always find something different when they come in."

## Warm welcome from Otley

Even before opening its doors, Roam Cafe has already received strong support from local residents and neighbouring independent businesses.

Mr Wardale said: "Everyone around here has been really supportive. There's a brilliant independent business community and people have been incredibly welcoming.

## Countdown to opening day

With final preparations under way, the owners are now looking ahead to welcoming their first customers this week.

For the former advertising colleagues, the opening represents the culmination of months of work and the beginning of an entirely new chapter in Otley's independent food scene.
