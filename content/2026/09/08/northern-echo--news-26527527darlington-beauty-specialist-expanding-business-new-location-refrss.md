A Darlington beauty specialist is expanding her business with a new location and a host of new treatments.

Danielle Smith, 35, who runs Danielle’s Beauty Room, has worked in the nail and beauty industry for 14 years.

Now she is branching out from nails to offer facials, eyelash and brow services from her new beauty room above hairdressers Luminous at 19 Post House Wynd, Darlington.

  Danielle Smith, 35, from Darlington, who runs Danielle’s Beauty Room 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The move marks a new chapter for the Darlington mum-of-two, who wanted to expand her skills and create a relaxing space for clients.

She said: "I've been in the nail and beauty industry for 14 years and after focusing mainly on just nails I wanted to grow my knowledge, retrain so I can offer new treatments.

"I’ll be adding facials, eyelash and brow services to my treatments. I wanted to create a relaxing space so my clients look forward to visiting.

"I’m so excited to grow my business and meet new clients and of course to see all of my regular clients here too."

  Danielle Smith, 35, from Darlington, who runs Danielle’s Beauty Room 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

After loving her time with the Embellish team, Danielle is taking the next step with her own beauty room.

She announced the move on social media, saying: "I’m so excited to announce that from September 1 I’ll be offering new treatments - and from a new location!

"I’m moving into my own beauty room in the beautiful Luminous. I’m not far, just around the corner, and I can’t wait to see you all there ."

  Danielle Smith, 35, from Darlington, who runs Danielle’s Beauty Room 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Away from work, Danielle is kept busy by her daughters, Isabella and Isla, and her French bulldogs, Bonnie and Dolly.

She said getting to know clients and catching up with them are among the things she loves most about her work.
