Families are invited to dust off their glowsticks for a night of 90s nostalgia and fun on the dancefloor.

Acomb Parish Church Hall will be transformed into a family-friendly club night on Saturday, September 26, from 6pm to 8pm, with all proceeds supporting St Stephen’s Church Oasis Youth and Kids.

The alcohol-free 90s Club Classics Throwback Night promises classic club anthems, dancing, and a lively atmosphere for all ages.

Dan Savage, one of the organisers, said: "Bring the kids, bring your friends, or just bring yourself.

"Everyone’s welcome.

"Come and dance like nobody’s watching – just like it’s 1999."

Parents are encouraged to relive the soundtrack of their youth while introducing younger generations to some of the era’s popular tunes.

Guests are welcome to dress up in 90s-inspired outfits, with glowsticks provided to complete the look.

Tickets are priced at £5 per adult, while children can attend for free.

The event is designed to offer all the fun of a night out without the late finish, wrapping up by 8pm.

Organisers say it’s the perfect way to enjoy a dance and some nostalgia before heading home at a sensible hour.
