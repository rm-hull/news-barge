**BIRMINGHAM** — Grassroots organizers within Reform UK have reported an overwhelmingly positive reception to the party’s expanded annual conference at the NEC, pointing to enhanced corporate infrastructure and a successful reboot of its local government strategy.

Local party figure **Mike Jordan** described the atmosphere at the three-day event as “buzzing,” noting that attendance and physical scale easily eclipsed previous party gatherings, including past events held in Doncaster.

## Corporate Expansion and Policy Focus

The event marked a noticeable shift toward mainstream political infrastructure. Attendees highlighted a significant influx of **charities and corporate businesses**, alongside an extensive schedule of fringe events surrounding the main conference halls. Logistical improvements, including a wider variety of food and drink options, also drew praise from traveling party delegates.

The conference framework centered heavily on the party’s flagship **“First 100 Days”** policy agenda. Grassroots members reportedly rallied behind the core platform, which outlines a sweeping roadmap for national emergency declarations and immediate tax overhauls should the party take power.

## Key Speeches and Media Resilience

While the lead-up to the conference was dominated by headlines surrounding a **Channel 4 undercover sting**—which alleged campaign donation rule violations and resulted in the departure of two senior aides—delegates on the ground largely dismissed the controversy.


**Mike Jordan said:**

Hardly anyone was talking about the C4 sting, the main stage remained packed throughout the weekend.



The weekend’s momentum was heavily anchored by high-profile speeches, most notably from recent high-level acquisition **Suella Braverman**. Braverman, now serving as Reform’s education spokesperson, delivered a Saturday address focusing on patriotic education values and severe cuts to underperforming university degrees, which earned her a major standing ovation from the crowd. Anticipated disruption from political hecklers during the high-profile Saturday sessions also failed to materialize inside the primary auditoriums.

## Reboots to the Local Government Strategy

Crucially for the party’s long-term electoral prospects, leadership used the conference to orchestrate a major overhaul of its **local councillor package**.


**Mike Jordan explained:**

The party has also relaunched it’s offer to cllrs as it hadn’t done what it was supposed to.



Mike Jordan noted that the revamped strategy—complemented by case-study presentations from sitting Reform council leaders showcasing recent municipal achievements—successfully drove a fresh wave of sign-ups from local politicians over the weekend.
