Aldi will create 120 jobs by opening four new stores across the UK during September.

One store has already opened, while three more are due to welcome shoppers this month.

The new Balsall Common shop, near Solihull, opened on September 3. Aldi will open its Waterloo Road, Ketley Point, Telford, store on September 10.

A third branch will open at Weir Retail Park in Rayleigh, Essex, on September 17. The final opening is planned for Hackney Road, Hoxton, London, on September 20.

Up to 30 roles are available at each of the three stores yet to open, in Telford, Rayleigh and Hoxton.

The openings are part of Aldi’s £370 million investment in new stores this year. The supermarket also has a long-term ambition to operate 1,500 stores across the UK.

Managing Director of National Real Estate at Aldi UK Jonathan Neale said: “We know there are still communities across the country that want access to an Aldi store, and we are working hard to bring our unbeatable value to more of them.”

Managing Director of National Real Estate at Aldi UK Jonathan Neale said: “Opening a new store every week throughout September demonstrates the pace of our expansion, while creating new jobs and bringing high-quality, affordable food to even more shoppers.”

Aldi opened its first store in Essen, Germany, in 1913. Its first UK shop opened in 1990.

The retailer now has more than 1,080 UK stores, employing more than 43,000 colleagues.
