Police are appealing for witnesses after a woman was sexually assaulted on The Stray in Harrogate.

The incident happened at around 8.30pm on Sunday 6th September, between Knaresborough Road and Wetherby Road.

North Yorkshire Police said the woman was walking across The Stray when an unknown man sexually assaulted her by touching her as he ran past.

The man then continued running towards Wetherby Road.

A police spokesperson said:

"We’re appealing for information about the suspect or anyone who was in the area at the time, and who witnessed the incident.

"The suspect has been described as a white man, skinny build, tall, wearing shorts, a blue/green running jacket and a white baseball cap."


Information can be emailed to PC Sarah Moore at [Sarah.Moore@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Sarah.Moore@northyorkshire.police.uk) or submitted through the North Yorkshire Police website.

The force’s live chat service is also available between 9am and 5pm from Monday to Friday.

*Alternatively, call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and ask for PC 957 Moore.*

*Information can be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*

*Anyone providing information should quote reference 12260175247.*
