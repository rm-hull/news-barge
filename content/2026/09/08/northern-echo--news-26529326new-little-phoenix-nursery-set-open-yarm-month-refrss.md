A nursery in Yarm with a focus on nature and outdoor learning is set to open later this year.

Little Phoenix Nursery, located in Yarm on The Spital, will welcome its first children on September 14, offering year-round childcare for children aged six months to three years.

The nursery is designed around Froebelian early years principles and places a strong emphasis on nature, play, and exploration.

Sarah Moon-Gatford, nursery manager and a qualified early years teacher, said: "Little Phoenix has been created around a very clear vision of what we believe an exceptional early years’ experience should look and feel like for babies and little ones.

"Every detail has been carefully considered, from the resources children encounter to the opportunities they have to be outdoors, explore nature and to develop their own interests.

"We hope children feel happy, secure and curious from the moment they arrive."

The nursery is open from 7.30am to 6pm and aims to support working families, including those returning to work after parental leave.

A total of 15 staff members have already been appointed, with further recruitment underway.

Children will be able to progress from Little Phoenix into pre-school, with a pathway available to Yarm School for those who choose it, though there is no requirement to do so.

Dr Huw Williams, headmaster at Yarm School, said: "The opening of Little Phoenix is an exciting development for us and, importantly, for families across the local area given the demand for high-quality provision of this sort.

"We wanted to create something very special - a nursery which combines outstanding early years expertise with an environment in which children have the freedom and encouragement to explore, play, discover and develop, expanding our age range whilst retaining our determination to continue Educating for Life.

"It also gives families continuity should they wish to continue into our pre-school and ultimately Yarm School, while remaining a nursery that is very much open to families regardless of where they intend their child to continue their education."
