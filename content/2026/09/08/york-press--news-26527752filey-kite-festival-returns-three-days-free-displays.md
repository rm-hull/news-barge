## Filey Kite Festival returns with spectacular new Friday Night Flight

Colourful displays will be on show once again at the Filey Kite Festival, which is being held on a third evening for the first time *(Image: Ron Ella)*

Illuminated kites will light up the Yorkshire coast as part of a new Friday night launch.

The Filey Kite Festival, now in its sixth year, will take place from Friday, September 18 to Sunday, September 20 at Filey Brigg Country Park.

Organised by North Yorkshire Council and the Northern Kite Group, the free event is expected to draw thousands of visitors.

Cllr Simon Myers, North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for culture, arts and housing, said: "This promises to be a fantastic event with the skies above Filey Brigg filled with amazing kites.

"Families will have plenty of opportunities to get involved, and children can take part in kite-making workshops, where they can create and decorate their own kites before taking them outside to see them fly."

For the first time, the festival will begin on a Friday, featuring a dusk-time Friday Night Flight with illuminated kites.

Crowds can enjoy a variety of displays including giant inflatables, precision stunt kites, and artistic designs.

The event also includes a "learn to fly" arena, where visitors can borrow kites and receive guidance from experienced flyers.

Cllr Myers added: "The festival offers a fun day out for residents and visitors alike, combining spectacular entertainment with hands-on activities suitable for all ages."
