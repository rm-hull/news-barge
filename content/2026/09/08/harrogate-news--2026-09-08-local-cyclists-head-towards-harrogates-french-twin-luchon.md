A team of local cyclists is heading to Harrogate’s twin town of Luchon in southern France from 11 to 14 September 2026. They will not be alone as they will have VIP support from Harrogate’s cycling mayor Cllr Viv Poskitt.

This is the weekend of the Lapebie Cyclosportif, one of Luchon’s road cycling events now in its 35th year. There are three routes: 121 km, 105km and 60 km in the Pyrenees around Luchon concluding on one of the most famous Tour de France climbs at Superbagnères.

This will be the first visit to Luchon by a mayor of Harrogate for a number of years.

See: www.harrogatetwinning.org


**Cllr Poskitt said:**

I was delighted to be able to accept the Mayor of Luchon’s invitation, especially after chatting with members of Harrogate’s Nova Cycling Club about the visit when I unveiled the plaque celebrating cyclist Beryl Burton.

It is always invidious to single anyone out but worthy of note is our lone female rider Emma Dooley and two 18 year olds, Magnus Denwood and James Beagley, fresh from completing their A Levels.

I am also looking forward to meeting the newly-appointed Mayor of Luchon M Sebastien Denard and hope that we can forge closer links between our two towns and strengthen this historic twinning link.




**Michael Newby, Chair of Harrogate International Partnerships:**

I remember the hospitality that Luchon gave me when I was Mayor of Harrogate so I am sure that Viv will have a fantastic time. It would be great to be able to invite M.Denard to Harrogate so we can return that hospitality. He would become the first Mayor of Luchon to visit Harrogate since 2014 when the town hosted the Grand Depart of the Tour de France.

I would also like to thank Chris Bentley at Hornbeam Park Developments, Aidan Robson of Endless and Matthew Stamford at Verity Frearson for their generous sponsorship of our twinning events.


The mayor is paying for her flights out of her own pocket. The Harrogate International Partnerships was formed in 2017, as the operational company reporting to the town’s twinning management committee, developing awareness and opportunities for exchange visits with our international partners.

The HIP is a not for profit organisation, and a registered charity to provide support to twinning groups by way of advice, support and grant funding.

Harrogate has been twinned with Bagnères-de-Luchon, France (since 1953), Wellington, New Zealand (since 1953), Montecatini Terme, Italy (since 1963) and Barrie, Canada (since 2013).
