A MASKED duo entered a home in the Bradford district and stole a BMW.

The reported break-in happened on Marley Court, Crossflatts, at 2.10am on Sunday, September 6.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Two suspects, wearing balaclavas and gloves, entered the address through an unlocked patio door.

"They made their way through the house before locating the house and car keys in the front door lock.

"They used the keys to exit the house, locking the door behind them, and then made off in the victim's car, a blue BMW 116D."

Anyone with information should call police on 101, quoting crime reference 13260507078.

Police are also asking residents to check CCTV and doorbell cameras for suspicious activity.

## Another break-in

A separate break-in was reported at a property on Swan Avenue, Gilstead.

The break-in happened between 8am on Sunday, September 6, and 8am on Monday, September 7.

Police said thieves cut a hole in the garage door to reach the lock and get inside.

Thieves reportedly stole a mountain bike and a black Yamaha MT motorcycle.

Anyone with information should call police on 101, quoting crime reference 13260508650.
