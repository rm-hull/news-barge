An aunt and her niece died from the effects of a fire despite neighbours' attempts to rescue them from a burning home, an inquest heard.

Natalie McDonald, 34, and her seven-year-old niece Valentina Foster died in the fire on Birchington Avenue, Grangetown, on Wednesday, August 26.

A 23-year-old has been charged with murder.

Inquests into their deaths opened today (September 8) at Teesside Coroners Court.

  Valentina Foster, seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, died following a house fire in Grangetown. 

*(Image: Provided)*

Coroner Paul Appleton heard multiple people were believed to be trapped inside the property and members of the public made attempts to rescue the occupants inside the house before the emergency services reached the scene.

Cleveland Police were also called to the fire, but sadly two of the six in the house died.

Natalie was pronounced dead at the home and Valentina at James Cook University Hospital.

  Police at the scene of the fire on Birchington Avenue in Grangetown 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Pathologist Dr Hoggard concluded both died from "the effects of fire".

Mr Appleton offered his condolences to Valentina’s mum, nana, uncle and three aunties in court for the brief hearing.

He adjourned the inquests due to ongoing criminal proceedings.

Valentina was remembered by her family as a beloved daughter whose loss had "changed their lives forever".

  Valentina Foster, seven, died alongside her auntie Natalie McDonald in a house fire 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

Natalie had married her husband Anthony McDonald just nine months before her death, with her family describing their wedding as “the biggest, most beautiful wedding”.

Jaden Wright, 23, is charged with two counts of murder, two counts of grievous bodily harm with intent and two counts of attempted grievous bodily harm with intent.

He appeared at Teesside Crown Court on September 3, and did not enter any pleas during a 10-minute hearing.

Judge Jonathan Carroll set a provisional trial date of April 14 next year, which is expected to be tried by a High Court Judge.

Wright was remanded in custody until a hearing on September 23, when he is expected to enter pleas.

A 31-year-old man arrested over the incident was released without charge.

  Floral tributes left at the scene of the devastating fire on Birchington Avenue, in Grangetown 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

Dozens of floral tributes were left at the scene on Birchington Avenue following the tragedy.

They said: "Natalie Louise was known by everyone as Natalie Louise Kirton, she is Natalie Louise McDonald having married Anthony McDonald only nine months ago.

"She had the biggest, most beautiful wedding; she sparkled like the shining beauty she was. She was loved by everyone, she was bright, bubbly and full of life, the heart and soul of our street and community, loved by all.

“Her family has lost its beautiful, kind soul. She was the life and soul of every party, and she will be sadly missed by our family, her two brothers and sister, all who are broken.

"Her nieces and nephews, with her being such a big part of their lives, being like a second mother to them. Her nana, grandad, mam and dad are all heartbroken.

“Also, her only daughter; who will forever miss her mam.

“Our Nat will be forever missed."
