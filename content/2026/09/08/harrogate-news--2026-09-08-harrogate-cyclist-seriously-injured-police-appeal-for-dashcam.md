**North Yorkshire Police** have launched an appeal for witnesses and dashcam footage after a cyclist was left hospitalised with**serious injuries** following an incident near Harrogate.

The cyclist, a man in his late 50s from Otley, came off his bicycle while travelling downhill on the **B6161 Leathley Lane**. The incident occurred at approximately** 4:45 pm on Tuesday 11 August 2026**.

Paramedics treated the man at the scene before he was blue-lighted to the hospital by ambulance for urgent medical treatment. Police have confirmed that he remains in the hospital receiving ongoing medical care.

## Witnesses and Dashcam Footage Sought

Traffic officers are continuing to investigate the circumstances surrounding the crash and are urging anyone who was in the area at the time to come forward.

Investigators want to speak with anyone who witnessed the incident, recalls seeing the cyclist, or remembers any other vehicles travelling along Leathley Lane around 4:45 pm that afternoon. Drivers who were in the vicinity with active dashcams are being asked to check their footage immediately.

## How to Help the Investigation

If you have any information or recorded footage that could assist the police, you can contact the force through the following channels:

- **Email:** Send details directly to [Conor.Taylor@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Conor.Taylor@northyorkshire.police.uk)
- **Phone:** Call**101** , select option 1, and ask for Conor Taylor.

When providing information, please quote the police reference number **12260155539** to ensure it reaches the investigating officer directly.
