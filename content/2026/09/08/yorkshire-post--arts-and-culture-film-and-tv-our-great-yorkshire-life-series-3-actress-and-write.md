# Our Great Yorkshire Life series 3: Actress and writer Gaynor Faye gets back on the horse after ‘confronting a fear that haunted her since childhood’ in Channel 5 episode

## The Channel 5 series Our Great Yorkshire Life returns to screens with a new episode featuring Gaynor Faye who gets back on the horse after ‘confronting a fear’ she’s had since childhood.

There will be many faces featured in the series, from five familiar characters to stars of stage, screen, and sport - alongside new and returning local heroes.

Each episode offers heartwarming stories with a backdrop of the region’s breathtaking and recognisable landscapes.

![Gaynor Faye and Toffee the horse on Our Great Yorkshire Life. (Pic credit: Channel 5)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjlkNjA2NWM1LWViYjgtNGUwMi1iM2JlLWYxOGU0MTY3MGUzZDpiMzM3NmNjNy1iYWI5LTQ1ZDctYjZkYy03OWRiMmU5NjQ3MDc=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The third series of the show will explore Yorkshire’s rich heritage, creative spirit, and vibrant communities, highlighting everything from innovative industries to culinary craftsmanship.

It starts with four moving and visually stunning stories from across Yorkshire.

In Bramhope, actress and writer Gaynor Faye, known for her roles in Coronation Street and Emmerdale, who is the daughter of the late Kay Mellor, will be conquering her fear that has haunted her since childhood.

After being thrown from a horse as a young girl, she vowed never to ride again. But with the help from holistic horse therapist Lynn Henry and the gentle guidance of a pony named Toffee, Gaynor embarks on an emotional journey of healing and rediscovery, aiming to rebuild her confidence in the saddle.

![Tom Addison on Our Great Yorkshire Life. (Pic credit: Channel 5)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjkwYjI5YmEwLWFhNTQtNDY4My05ZDgzLTA0MTk3ZDAxMWU3NToyMjAzMjYwYS1mOGUzLTRkOTktYTQ3MC0zM2QwY2QyZGQzODg=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Meanwhile in Halifax, lifelong farmer Steven Short prepares for an important day at the auction.

Having raised a flock of Hampshire Down lambs on his family farm, Steven hopes all of his hard work will pay off at market.

As costs continue to rise for farmers, every penny counts - and Steven’s trip to Skipton Mart carries real weight.

Up in North Yorkshire, Castle Howard is undergoing one of the most significant projects in its history. Following years of meticulous work, curators Eleanor and Matthew race against time to prepare the house for reopening, revealing restored rooms not seen since they were destroyed by a fire in 1940.

![Stephen Short on Our Great Yorkshire Life. (Pic credit: Channel 5)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjg0YTExMDFiLTAyZWEtNGI1Mi05OTcwLTc5MDlhNmZiMzA0MTplOTJhNjUwMi01MTVlLTQ2YmUtYTdhMS0zN2MxZmVhMjhjYjE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



In the Yorkshire Dales, talented furniture maker Tom Addison completes the final phase of a special project.

From his rural workshop in Askrigg, Tom has crafted eight bespoke oak louvres for the bell tower of the village’s 15th century church.

As the final pieces are lifted into place, the whole community gathers to celebrate an incredible restoration - and a legacy built to last.

From emotional breakthroughs to skilled craftsmanship, this episode showcases the heart, heritage, and everyday heroes of Yorkshire life.
