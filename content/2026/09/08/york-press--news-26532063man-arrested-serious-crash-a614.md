A MAN has been arrested after a serious crash in East Yorkshire left a man hospitalised with injuries thought to be life changing.

A 56-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by careless driving following the incident on the A614 at the junction with Spaldington Road on Monday (September 7).

Humberside Police said the incident, which involved two vehicles, resulted in the rider of motorcycle, a 62-year-old man, being taken to hospital with serious injuries.

  


A force spokesperson said: “At around 12pm it is reported that a HGV travelling southbound on the A614 collided with a red Ducati motorcycle in the opposite direction.

“If anyone witnessed the collision, has any dashcam or CCTV footage that may assist officers with ongoing enquiries, please contact our non-emergency number 101 quoting log 10 of 11 March 2026.

“As part of the investigation so far, the driver of the HGV, a 56-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by careless driving, he remains in police custody at this time.”
