Artists have just a few days left to enter Beverley Art Gallery’s annual Open Art Exhibition.

Submissions close on Saturday, September 12, for the 25th edition of the event, which showcases creative talent from across Yorkshire and the Humber.

Entry costs £5 but is free for artists aged 25 and under, with artists invited to submit one original piece in any medium.

Councillor Nick Coultish, cabinet member for culture, leisure and tourism at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "We are excited to welcome the 25th Open Art Exhibition to Beverley Art Gallery.

"The exhibition shines a light on the immense talent of artists across our region, and I cannot wait to see the artwork selected this year."

Around 100 artworks will be chosen by guest selectors Gill Crawshaw, an art curator and researcher, and award-winning photographer Andrew Quinn.

Hannah Willetts, curator at Beverley Art Gallery, said: "The Open Art Exhibition is about giving artists across our region a platform and celebrating the breadth of work being created here."

Selected pieces will go on display at Beverley Art Gallery from September 26 to December 17.

All submitting artists are invited to the exhibition launch on September 26, regardless of whether their work is selected.
