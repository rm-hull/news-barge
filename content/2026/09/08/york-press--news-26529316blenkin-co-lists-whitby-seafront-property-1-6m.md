## Yorkshire Victorian seafront property with 12 holiday lets listed for sale at £1.6m

The Sandbeck Hotel in Whitby is on the market for £1.6million *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A Victorian seafront property in Whitby has been listed for sale at £1.6million.

The Sandbeck Hotel, a six-storey, nearly 7,000 sq ft building, is a freehold hospitality business on the West Cliff.

Constructed in the 1840s, the property on Crescent Terrace has been extensively renovated and now operates as a self-check-in holiday let building.

It includes 12 self-contained studio units and a fourth-floor penthouse apartment typically used by the owners.

Ten of the 12 units have sea views.

The studio rooms come with kitchenettes, while the two ground-floor apartments, fully self-contained, feature open-plan living areas and separate bedrooms and bathrooms.

The current owners have run the property for nearly 20 years, during which time they have developed the business and maintained its clientele.

Over the years, the property has been refurbished to meet contemporary standards while preserving its architectural features, such as original fireplaces and an elegant staircase.

  One of the rooms in The Sandbeck Hotel, Whitby 

*(Image: Blenkin & Co)*

Bay windows and balconies in some rooms offer views of the coastline.

The building also boasts a passenger lift serving five floors and private rear parking for up to four cars.

This property is being marketed as a versatile investment for potential buyers.

It could be turned into a luxury boutique bed and breakfast with an on-site restaurant, continue as a holiday-let business, or become a private holiday home.

The owner's penthouse, with floor-to-ceiling glazing and large sliding doors, can either be retained for personal use or added to the holiday-let portfolio for extra income.

On the lower ground floor, there are facilities for a potential restaurant with an independent entrance, sea views, and natural light.

Though not currently operational, it offers the potential to serve both visitors and locals, subject to any necessary consents.

Further business information and trading accounts are available through Blenkin & Co.

The Sandbeck, located directly overlooking the seafront, is a short walk from popular Whitby landmarks such as the Whalebone Arch, Captain Cook Memorial, and the beach.

The historic streets and the town centre are also within easy walking distance.

The property is connected to all mains services, has gas central heating that can be app-controlled, and features a Mixergy integrated heat-pump hot water cylinder.

It is being sold with all items mentioned in the sales particulars, while other items seen in photographs but not mentioned are excluded from the sale.

The Sandbeck can be found at the corner of Crescent Terrace and North Terrace/Esplanade in Whitby and is available for viewing strictly by appointment.
