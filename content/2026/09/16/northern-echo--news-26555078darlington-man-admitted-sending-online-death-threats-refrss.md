A man admitted sending a series of death threats on social media.

Christopher Fothergill denied a further stalking charge at Teesside Crown Court.

The charge relates to him allegedly making unwanted contact with a woman via numerous emails and bank transfer before turning up at her workplace last month.

The 42-year-old’s case was adjourned for two weeks to allow time for the prosecution to consider the pleas and whether to go ahead with a trial on the remaining charge.

Judge Nathan Adams remanded Fothergill in custody until his next hearing on October 2.

Fothergill, of Hundens Lane, Darlington, admitted sending three messages threatening death or serious harm on August 13.
