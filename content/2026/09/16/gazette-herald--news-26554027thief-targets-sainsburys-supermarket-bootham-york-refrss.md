A THIEF has targeted a supermarket in York, making off with groceries and alcohol.

North Yorkshire Police said it happened at the Sainsbury's Local in Bootham on Tuesday, July 14.

  



The force issued CCTV images of a person they want to speak to, saying he may have information which will assist its investigation.

Anyone who recognises the person or has information is asked to contact PC1488 Bell by email at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260133471.

Information can also be given anonymously via Crimestoppers.
