A POPULAR food reviewer and YouTuber has urged people to give good businesses in deprived areas a chance.

Danny Malin, a Leeds-born content creator who runs the Danny vs Food YouTube channel, visited Jaywick, Essex, to review a restaurant and see what the area offered.

The seaside town has often been described as one of the most deprived places in the UK.

During his visit, Mr Malin said he was struck by what he saw.

He said: “To be honest with you, driving through this place, looking out to sea, I didn't realise that in the UK we'd have somewhere as deprived as this.”

He said his purpose was to give a business an opportunity and encourage people to visit venues before making a judgement based on the area.

Mr Malin said: “The reason I've come here today is because this place has been voted probably one of the most deprived places in the UK.”

He added: “So, do you know what, I wanted to come and give it a chance.

Mr Malin said he would assess the pub according to its food and service, rather than its location.

He said: “I'm going to give an honest review.

"And if it is good, I'm going to tell you.”

He also said publicity could bring visitors and money to deprived areas.

Mr Malin said: “If it can bring more money into this little town, this little village, hopefully you guys will come and visit.”

He said businesses could lose customers when people are put off by views of the area before visiting.

Mr Malin said: “I think it's been given a bit of a bad name, this place, and I think people need to look at it a little bit different.

“Let's support local businesses within the area.”

"We need business owners and the public to support each other.”

He also called for action from councillors and work to improve the area.

Mr Malin said: “Let's try and get this area back to its former glory.

"Councillors need to get on it and revive it and get it back."
