A County Durham business has applied to expand the number of goods vehicles it operates from its base, according to a public notice.

Terri Gibson, trading as J W Services, has put forward a proposal to increase the number of goods vehicles at the operating centre in Woodland Park Bungalow, Burnhope, from one to three.

The public notice published on September 12 stated that anyone living near the operating centre who believes they would be affected by this change has the right to voice their concerns.

- 
    **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo (publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be foundhere.**

These representations must be sent in writing to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE, within 21 days of the notice's publication.

A copy of the concerns must also be sent to Terri Gibson at Woodland Park Bungalow.

A guide on how to make these representations is available on the government website.
