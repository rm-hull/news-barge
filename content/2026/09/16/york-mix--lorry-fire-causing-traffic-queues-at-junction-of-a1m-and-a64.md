There are rush hour problems at a major road junction near York this morning (Wednesday).

A lorry fire on the slip road between the A1M and the A64 is slowing traffic.

The slip road has been closed and drivers are being diverted via Wetherby.

The AA reports: “Exit ramp blocked and queueing traffic due to truck fire on A1(M) Northbound at J44 A64 (York / Tadcaster).”

#### Map location

Emergency services are at the scene.

Some drivers are reporting standstill traffic on the A1M north.
