A new parking restriction has been introduced on a Yarm street, with motorists facing a ban on waiting at any time along sections of a busy road.

The ban on B1264 Green Lane, Yarm, comes into force following Stockton Council's decision to enforce the a Prohibition of Waiting Order.

The order affects various parts of the B1264 Green Lane and was implemented on September 11.

The council made the decision following consultation with the chief officer of police.

The details of the affected sections are listed in the order, which can be accessed on the council’s website under 'Traffic Orders'.

The council has also provided an option for residents to raise any concerns or questions regarding the validity of the order.

People have six weeks from the date of implementation to apply to the High Court if they believe the order is not within the powers granted by the Road Traffic Regulation Act.

This application can also be made if any requirement of the Act, or any instrument made under it, has not been complied with in relation to the order.
