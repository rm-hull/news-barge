A man has died in a crash on a road near the North Yorkshire coast.

It happened last night (Tuesday) at about 6.05pm on the A171 Whitby to Scarborough road, near Helwath Beck.

A black Volvo XC40 and a black and white Triumph Daytona motorcycle collided.

#### Crash location

They were both travelling in the same direction towards Scarborough when the collision happened.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Sadly, the motorcyclist, a man aged in 30s from the Whitby area died at the scene of the collision.

“We are appealing to witnesses who have not yet spoken to the police who saw either of the vehicles travelling along the A171 prior to the collision, or who witnessed the collision to come forward along with anyone who may have caught either vehicle on their dashcam before or during the collision.”

If you can help police, you can contact them on the numbers below.

The road was closed for eight hours to allow emergency services to work at the scene then to enable a collision investigation, and recover the vehicles. It reopened at 2am this morning (Wednesday).

Officers would like to thank the members of the public who stopped at the scene to help.

The driver of the Volvo, a woman in her 40s, sustained slight injuries but did not need hospital treatment. She has been interviewed under caution in relation to the collision.
