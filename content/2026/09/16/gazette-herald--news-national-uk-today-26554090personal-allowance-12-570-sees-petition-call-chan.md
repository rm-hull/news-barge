A campaign to increase the amount people can earn before paying Income Tax is gathering support as a petition calls for the £12,570 Personal Allowance to be unfrozen.

The Personal Allowance has been frozen at £12,570 since 2021 and is currently due to remain at that level until April 2031.

A new petition claims the freeze could leave families facing an increasing tax burden as wages rise, because more of their income can be dragged into taxation while the amount they can earn tax-free stays unchanged.


Created by Kenneth Carmichael, the petition is calling for the Personal Allowance and other income tax thresholds to automatically rise every year from April 2031.

It wants thresholds to increase in line with CPI inflation or average wages, whichever is higher. Today, the Office for National Statistics (ONS) said Consumer Prices Index (CPI) inflation increased to 3.1% in August, compared with 2.9% in July.

It was in line with predictions from economists and points to an upward trajectory for inflation since striking a 15-month-low of 2.6% in June.

Many economists have suggested the Bank is still likely to keep interest rates – which help dictate mortgage and loan rates – at 3.75% but that hikes are likely in future months.

The campaign is also calling for a one-off increase in 2031 to restore the real value lost during the decade-long freeze.


The petition states: "The Government has frozen core income tax thresholds (personal allowance £12,570, higher rate £50,270) at 2021/22 levels until April 2031. Nearly a decade of unprecedented fiscal drag."

It argues that the freeze amounts to a stealth tax and claims it could "pull millions more people into poverty by 2031".

However, this is the petition's claim, rather than an established Government forecast.

The campaign estimates that if the Personal Allowance had risen with inflation, it would now be around £4,920 higher.

That would put the allowance at roughly £17,490, compared with the current £12,570 figure.

The petition is also calling for the higher-rate threshold to be increased, but its central demand is for the amount people can earn before paying Income Tax to rise automatically again.

It needs 10,000 signatures to receive a Government response and 100,000 signatures for it to be considered for debate in Parliament.

The petition was published on May 6, 2026 and closes on November 6, 2026.

For taxpayers, the key figure to watch is the £12,570 Personal Allowance – the amount most people can earn before Income Tax normally becomes payable.

With that figure frozen until 2031, the allowance will not automatically increase simply because wages or prices rise.
