Yorkshire Water has said it is "working as quickly as we can" to complete repair work in a busy York shopping street.

Work is underway to repair a sewer at the St Helen’s Square end of Coney Street.

Fencing has been installed around the work from outside the York Mansion House to near the Church of St Martin le Grand.

Yorkshire Water apologised for the disruption and said the work to fix the issue beneath Coney Street are "complex as the sewer is exceptionally deep – around four metres".

  Yorkshire Water work underway in Coney Street, York 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Businesses remain open during the closure with several organisations placing notices on the fencing redirecting customers to their venues.

A walkway is open for pedestrians to pass through Coney Street and a marshal has been on site to support deliveries to businesses, Yorkshire Water said.

Blue Badge holders have been diverted via the York Inner Ring Road, as the section of Coney Street remains closed to vehicles.

  Yorkshire Water work underway in Coney Street, York 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson noted that the work has been "designed so that we can minimise impact on businesses" but said: "We understand that this work will be disruptive, but this closure is necessary to enable us to complete the work safely."

They added: "We would like to apologise and thank everyone in the area for their patience – we're working as quickly as we can."

Yorkshire Water said it expects the work to be completed on Friday (September 18).

"Once the repair is complete, reinstatement will be carried out using existing materials where possible to help preserve the character and appearance of the location," the company spokesperson added.

The Yorkshire Water repairs comes as redevelopment work gets underway in Coney Street as part of plans to open the historic street up to the nearby riverside area.

Phase one of the scheme, by developers the Helmsley Group, began in June with work ongoing to renovate shopfronts at the St Helen's Square end of Coney Street.

Max Reeves, development director at the York-based Helmsley Group, said the work to the shopfronts is planned to be completed by September.

  How Coney Street could look under the plans 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The work includes the former JD Sports shop being renovated before the Space NK beauty store opens in the unit on Friday, September 25.

Mr Reeves said later work will see the upper floors of JD Sports and its neighbouring units converted into flats.

The director welcomed work starting in Coney Street, which came four years after the plans were first submitted and two years after planning permission was granted.

"It’s great to see the work coming forward on Coney Street after a long period of consultation and planning," Mr Reeves told The Press in June after the work started. "I’m looking forward to seeing what it looks like."
