A motorcyclist has admitted causing the death of his pillion passenger in a horror crash.

Gabriel Horsley-Ling was riding along Yarm Road, Darlington, on February 28, 2024, when he hit an HGV.

Nineteen-year-old Carla Scown died in the smash near the junction with Barrington Way at about 5.30pm.

  Picture from the scene of the crash on Yarm Road, Darlington.


A post-mortem examination found that the teenager suffered fatal head, chest and abdominal injuries.

Horsley-Ling, of Thorntree Gardens, Middleton St George, admitted causing death by dangerous driving after crashing his Honda CBF 125 motorbike.

The 23-year-old will be sentenced at Teesside Crown Court on October 15.

  Floral tributes at the scene of the crash.


He was released on unconditional bail and given an interim driving ban.

Her heartbroken mum Fiona previously said her daughter's death had left a huge hole in her heart.

“Carla will be very sadly missed every single day; she has left a huge hole in so many hearts of her family and friends.”

“She had the time of day for everyone and made all her friends and family so happy with her loving attitude towards them and life.”
