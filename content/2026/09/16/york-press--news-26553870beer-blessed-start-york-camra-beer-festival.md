## The beer is blessed, so York's beer festival is good to go!

The blessing of the beer is a festival tradition. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The beer has been blessed so the York CAMRA beer festival is good to go.

Rev Geoff Mumford performed the blessing at St Lawrence Parish Church yesterday (Tue).

York CAMRA’s 52nd Beer and Cider Festival opens today (WED) at the church, off Lawrence Street.

The popular event, which attracts thousands, runs until Saturday.

  



  The Volunteers at the York CAMRA beer festival, which starts today. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

The festival will feature more than 200 beers and ciders in a wide variety of styles and strengths, sourced from local, national, and international brewers.

In addition to beer and cider, attendees will also be able to enjoy gin, wine, and mead.

This year’s festival will also include a Locale blind-tasting competition, and a range of ticketed, tutored tasting sessions and presentations from local brewers and beer experts.

  Rev Geoff Mumford blesses the beer in St Lawrence Church. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Festival organiser Karl Smith said: "It's all systems go at York Beer & Cider festival, the handpumps are on and the beer has been blessed by Rev Geoff Mumford in St Lawrence's Church.

"He took the opportunity to both sample the festival special that he helped to brew at Trig brewery (100th Meridian, a "Mountain" IPA at 5.8%) and this year's souvenir arrived Pint glass."

  There are many beers to bless. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Today (Wed) sees the trade session preview opening at 1pm.

Karl added: "Almost 500 attendance requests have been received so it's going to be a busy start, before CAMRA members arrive from 3pm and public customers from 5pm."

  *For more information about the festival and tickets, visit**[https://yorkbeerfestival.camra.org.uk/sessions/*](https://yorkbeerfestival.camra.org.uk/sessions/*)
