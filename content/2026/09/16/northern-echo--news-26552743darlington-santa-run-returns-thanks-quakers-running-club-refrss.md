A popular Christmas fundraising event has been given a new lease of life.

Quakers Running Club has stepped in to organise this year's Santa Run in aid of St Teresa's Hospice.

The hospice had hoped to stage its annual festive fundraiser at West Park, but the event was cancelled due to logistical challenges.

However, members of Quakers Running Club were determined not to see the much-loved run disappear from the calendar.

The club, which has chosen St Teresa’s as its charity of the year, will host the event at South Park on Saturday, December 13.

  The Darlington running community have paid tribute to 'one of a kind' Gary Read, dad-of-two, after his tragic death aged 45. (Image: ANDY FUTERS) 

*(Image: Andy Futers)*

Organisers are hoping to attract runners, walkers and families from across the region for a day of festive fun and fundraising, with a 2km junior run and a 5km event open to participants of all abilities.

Everyone taking part will receive a Santa hat.

The Santa Run will raise money for St Teresa’s Hospice and honour Gary Read, a much-loved club member and member of the wider running community who died earlier this year.

Club chairman Rob Gillham said the hospice cared for Read in his final days, making the charity especially important to members.

  A picture of Quakers running club after the Darlington 10k. 

*(Image: Quakers Running Club on Facebook)*

He said: "When we heard the hospice could no longer host the Santa Run, we wanted to make sure the event could still go ahead.

"St Teresa's Hospice is our charity of the year and, after everything they did for Gary and his family, it felt right for us to step in and help.

"This event is very important for the hospice, and Gary is missed by all of us.

"Holding the run in his honour is a way of remembering a much-loved friend while supporting a cause that means so much to our club."

The club is now encouraging as many people as possible to sign up and take part, with all money raised helping St Teresa's Hospice continue its work supporting patients and families across the area.

Registration details can be found via the event poster and organisers are hopeful the revived Santa Run will become a fitting tribute to Reid while raising vital funds for the hospice.
