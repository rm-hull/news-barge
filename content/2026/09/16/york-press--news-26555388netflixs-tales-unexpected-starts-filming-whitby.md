## Netflix crews and household names spotted filming series in North Yorkshire

Bridgerton star Nicola Coughlan has been spotted, sporting a baby bump, filming for Netflix's adaptation of Tales of the Unexpected *(Image: James Manning/PA Wire)*

A NETFLIX series starring Dame Imelda Staunton, Nicola Coughlan and Toby Jones has begun filming in Whitby.

The household names will feature in an adaptation series of Roald Dahl’s Tales of the Unexpected.

Nicola, who is best known for her hilarious role as Claire in Channel 4’s Derry Girls and Penelope Featherington in Netflix period drama Bridgerton, was spotted filming in Sandgate on Wednesday (September 16).

  


It comes after Nicola, sporting a baby bump and Toby, were seen filming the six-part series last month.

  The star cast features Toby Jones, best known for his role in Mr Bates vs The Post Office 

*(Image: PA)*

The series is described as a dark and twisty short story collection.

It also stars Dominic West, who played Prince Charles in the Crown alongside Dame Imelda Staunton, who also features in the Dahl series.

  Imelda Staunton as Queen Elizabeth II spotted outside York Minster in 2023 

*(Image: Newsquest - Haydn Lewis)*

As reported by The Press, Imelda was seen in her role as Queen Elizabeth II in York Minster in 2023.

They join Naomi Scott and Carmen Ejogo for filming, which is taking place across Northern England this year.

According to IMDb, Netflix acquired the Roald Dahl Story Company back in 2021, giving the streamer unprecedented access to the author’s entire catalogue of characters and stories.
