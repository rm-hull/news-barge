A Darlington-based company has become the latest corporate partner of Bishop Auckland charity Angel Trust.

Threaded Workwear UK, which supplies embroidered and printed workwear to businesses and tradespeople across the UK, has joined a growing network of local businesses supporting the charity through financial donations, promotion and community engagement.

  Megan Campbell, founder of Threaded Workwear, and Elliot Pole, the Angel's Trust head of marketing 

*(Image: ANDY FUTERS/NORTHERN ECHO)*

Founded in 2025 by former offshore engineer Megan Campbell, Threaded Workwear is independently run from its Darlington base.

The business has already built a loyal customer base among local and regional companies.

  Megan Campbell, founder of Threaded Workwear, and Elliot Pole, the Angel's Trust head of marketing 

*(Image: ANDY FUTERS/NORTHERN ECHO)*

Megan, who became a mother in 2025, decided to leave offshore engineering to build a business that would allow her to spend more time at home while remaining connected to the industries she knows well.

She said: “I am so excited to come on board as a Corporate Partner of the Angel Trust, a charity close to my heart.

  Megan Campbell, founder of Threaded Workwear, and Elliot Pole, the Angel's Trust head of marketing 

*(Image: ANDY FUTERS/NORTHERN ECHO)*

“As a female founder who works closely with local businesses, it’s great to support my community and give back where I can.”

The Angel Trust was founded in 2018 by CEO Clair McGregor and supports people across County Durham facing a wide range of challenges, from crisis counselling and homelessness to providing toys and essential goods to families in need.

  Megan Campbell, founder of Threaded Workwear, and Elliot Pole, the Angel's Trust head of marketing 

*(Image: ANDY FUTERS/NORTHERN ECHO)*

The charity employs about 25 people and also runs Durham County Council’s Safe Night Hub, the only facility of its kind in the region, providing emergency accommodation and support to people who have become homeless following a serious life event or who have fled domestic abuse.

  Megan Campbell, founder of Threaded Workwear, and Elliot Pole, the Angel's Trust head of marketing 

*(Image: ANDY FUTERS/NORTHERN ECHO)*

Its community campaigns include the annual Toy Appeal, which saw more than 15,000 toys donated to children across the region in 2025.

As a Corporate Partner, Threaded Workwear will also benefit from opportunities to network with other organisations, gain early access to Angel Trust events and receive exposure through the charity’s social media channels, which reach more than two million viewers each month.

Angel Trust’s Head of Marketing, Elliot Pole, welcomed the partnership.

He said: “It’s great to see Megan join our Corporate roster, and even better to see a young, entrepreneurial female founder doing so well in her industry.

“We’re delighted to have Threaded Workwear on board and look forward to working together to support our local community.”
