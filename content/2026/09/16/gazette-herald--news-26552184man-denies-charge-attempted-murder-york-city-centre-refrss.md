A YORK man has appeared in court charged with attempting to murder another man in the city centre.

Jordan Ellerby is accused of trying to kill a man by stabbing him in Trinity Lane, off Micklegate, on February 19.

The 31-year-old denied the charge of attempted murder and an alternative charge of wounding the alleged victim with intent to cause him grievous bodily harm.

He appeared before York Crown Court via video link from HMP Hull where he is being held on remand.

Because he is charged with attempted murder, Judge Simon Hickey ordered that the case be transferred to Leeds Crown Court for trial.

Ellerby will appear before the West Yorkshire court for a case management hearing on October 9.

No date has yet been set for his trial.

He was remanded in custody until his next court hearing.

Ellerby was arrested and charged after police were called to the scene of the alleged stabbing at 3.20pm on February 19 and a man was taken to hospital.

Officers put a cordon in place and properties in Trinity Lane remained sealed off from the public until the following day.
