Millions of benefit claimants across the UK could see a welcome boost to their DWP payments from April 2027.

The Office for National Statistics (ONS) said Consumer Prices Index (CPI) inflation increased to 3.1% in August, compared with 2.9% in July. It was in line with predictions from economists and points to an upward trajectory for inflation since striking a 15-month-low of 2.6% in June.

Economists are predicting that inflation will continue to rise over the coming months as continued pressure from the conflict in the Middle East pushes up the cost of living further.

Benefit upratings in recent years have been based on the CPI figures for September. If this trend carries on, anyone who claims Universal Credit, Child Benefit, Carer's Allowance, and the State Pension could be in line for an increase in the next financial year.

ONS chief economist Grant Fitzner said: “Sharp price rises for petrol and diesel pushed inflation up again in August."


## How much will DWP benefits increase by?

Nothing is certain until the budget itself, but any increase is usually pegged to September's inflation figure.

That was just 1.7% in September 2024 – the lowest it had been in three years. Last year it was 3.8%, so many benefits were set to that rate.

Some - notably those paid to people over state pension age should increase more, based on the triple lock.

## How much did Universal Credit increase by in 2026?

Last year, the government announced new proposals – which became law under the Universal Credit Act – that it was changing how it would decide any increase in the universal credit standard allowance.

The new rules mean that - for the next three years at least - universal credit payments should increase by the rate of inflation as before, but with an additional top-up cash payment.

In April 2026, Universal Credit Claimants saw their payments rise by a combined total of 6.2%, reflecting September's 3.8% inflation rate, plus the standard allowance increase of 2.3%.

- For a single person aged 25 or over, the weekly standard allowance rose from £92 to £98, a £6 increase per week.
- For a couple where one or both are aged 25 or over, the weekly allowance rose from £145 to £154, a £9 increase.

It's worth noting that that this increase is only for the standard allowance — the basic universal credit payment rate — and the health element of the payment affected by cuts.

As of this April, any claimants of the health element from this date will have that rate frozen and halved until 2029-30.


## State Pension rise expected

The State Pension is on course for a rise of nearly £500 a year. The final increase is not yet confirmed. Under the triple lock, the State Pension rises each April by whichever is highest of earnings growth, September CPI inflation or 2.5%.

The latest earnings figure of 3.9% means September's inflation figure would need to come in above that level for inflation to determine the increase.

The full new State Pension would reach around £13,036 a year if it rises by 3.9%.

That would take it above the £12,570 personal tax allowance, which has been frozen since 2021.

The Government has said pensioners who only just exceed the personal allowance will not face the administrative burden of paying small amounts of tax during this Parliament, although ministers have yet to set out exactly how the measure will work.
