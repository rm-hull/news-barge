Thousands of pounds have been raised for patient care at a North Yorkshire hospice after cricket supporters turned out in pink and purple for a special fundraising event.

Scarborough Cricket Club’s first-ever Wear it Pink and Purple for Saint Catherine’s Day took place on Friday, August 28, during Yorkshire’s match against Leicestershire in the Rothesay County Championship.

The event raised £3,053.98 for Saint Catherine’s in Scarborough.

   Susan Stephenson and Jezz Pratt of Saint Catherine’s, Dan Gregory and Tim Watts of Scarborough Cricket Club 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Susan Stephenson, fundraising operations coordinator at Saint Catherine’s, said: "What a fabulous day and we are thrilled with the amount raised.

"The club and supporters were so generous in giving donations, buying merchandise, and generally showing an amazing level of support.

"The weather may not have been kind, but this did not dampen people’s spirits.

"We’re already looking forward to next year."

Dan Gregory, operations manager for Scarborough Cricket Club, said: "We're thrilled that we managed to get this event off the ground this year, and it definitely gives us a great platform to build from moving forward.

"Given the poor weather on the day, to raise just over £3,000 is a fantastic achievement."
