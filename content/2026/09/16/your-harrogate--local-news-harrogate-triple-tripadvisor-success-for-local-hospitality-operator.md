Three North Yorkshire inns owned by hospitality operator Simon Wade have received Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice Awards.

The Grantham Arms in Boroughbridge, the Blue Bell Country Inn in Arkendale and the Punch Bowl Inn in Marton cum Grafton have all received the accolade.

Tripadvisor gives Travellers’ Choice Awards to venues that consistently receive strong reviews and rank among the top 10% of properties listed on its site.

Simon said:

> “I’m honoured that all three inns have received Travellers’ Choice Awards.
>
> "It’s a testament to the hard work and care our teams put into welcoming guests every day.
>
> "I’m grateful to everyone who has visited and taken the time to leave a review.”

The award marks a successful first few months under Simon’s ownership for the Punch Bowl Inn, which reopened in May following a refurbishment.

Simon also operates The Bay Tree in Stillington and the Fox and Hounds in Langthorpe.
