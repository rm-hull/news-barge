‘Could of put a better photo on slinks - ya never gonna find him’

That was the message from a person on Facebook to officers searching for a man in connection with a string of driving offences.

Spoiler alert - Durham Police did find the teen - with officers arresting an 18-year-old man in the Longfield Road area of the town this morning.

The force clapped back at the comment in a new Facebook post - saying the arrested man has now been brought into custody where he will be quizzed.
