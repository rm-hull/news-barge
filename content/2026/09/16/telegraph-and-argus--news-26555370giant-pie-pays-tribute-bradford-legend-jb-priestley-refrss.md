A GIANT, steaming pie and a perambulation through Bradford helped mark the birthday of one of Britain’s best-loved writers.

Glyn Watkins, a poet and showman Bradford-based, organised two events to celebrate the legacy of novelist and playwright JB Priestley, who was born in the city on September 13, 1894.

Mr Watkins said: "The original pie man was Mr Roberts, with a shop where the upper car park entrance to the abandoned Kirkgate Centre is now.

"I really hope whatever replaces the Kirkgate Centre has some memorial to the pie that defied Hitler, and that all the historic plaques that are actually there now are resited or replaced."

He hosted a show at Napoleon’s Casino and a Priestley Perambulation the following day.

Both events featured steaming, homemade pies in honour of Priestley’s famous wartime BBC broadcast about a Bradford meat and potato pie shop that survived a near miss by a German bomb.
