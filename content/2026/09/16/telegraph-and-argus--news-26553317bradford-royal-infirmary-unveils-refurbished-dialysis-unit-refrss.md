A NEWLY refurbished Acute Renal Dialysis Unit has opened.

The upgraded unit at Bradford Royal Infirmary (BRI) features a modern, patient-focused environment designed to improve comfort and care for inpatients who require dialysis, including those transferred from Airedale Hospital.

Dr Mansoor Ali, consultant nephrologist at BRI, said: "This makeover has transformed the unit into a new, bright, modern designed space which is much needed for this busy service.

"It’s made an airy, light and calming environment which aims to make dialysis patients feel more comfortable and provide them with the best care possible."

The unit includes a new procedure room, three single-bed facilities, and shared bed bays.

Other upgrades include a new air handling unit, improved flooring and ceilings, and stained glass-style covers on ceiling lights to reduce glare and help ease patient anxiety.

During the refurbishment, the service temporarily relocated to Ward 10 at BRI.

Dialysis sessions have now fully resumed in the updated space, with morning, afternoon and newly introduced twilight appointments.

Roughly 380 patients rely on the trust’s main dialysis services, which are delivered at St Luke’s Hospital and Skipton Hospital.

Acute dialysis units provide life-saving kidney filtration for patients with severe kidney injury or sudden kidney failure.
