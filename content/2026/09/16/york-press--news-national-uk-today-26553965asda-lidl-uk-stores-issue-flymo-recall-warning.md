Shoppers at Asda, Lidl and more UK stores have been warned “stop using this product immediately” following a safety recall.

The Flymo Simplitrim 230 Strimmer has been recalled because of a defect that may cause “physical injury”.

The spool cover on the affected units may break. If the spool cover breaks, it may be ejected at high speed while the product is in operation.

The affected product has a batch code of 97083576-01. This number can be found on the label at the bottom of the handle.


An Asda recall notice stated: “Stop using this product immediately. If you have purchased one of the affected products, do not use it again under any circumstances.

“You can bring it back to your nearest Asda store where you will be given a full refund. You do not need your receipt.

“Alternatively, you can contact Flymo to arrange collection of your product from home at [https://www.flymo.com/uk/c/support/flymo-faqs/product-recall-information.](https://www.flymo.com/uk/c/support/flymo-faqs/product-recall-information.) Or via phone: 03448444558.

“We are very sorry for any inconvenience caused. If you would like any further information, please contact: Asda Customer Relations – 0330 053 0111.”

A Lidl statement added: “The spool cover on affected units may break and be ejected at high speed during operation.

“This poses a risk of physical injury to the user and to bystanders. The failure has been observed during the first or second use of the product.

“Do not continue to use this product.

“If you have purchased one of the affected products, do not use it again under any circumstances.

“Customers are asked to return this product to the nearest store where a full refund will be given. Or customers should contact the supplier to obtain a full refund.

“We apologise for any inconvenience caused and thank you for your cooperation.

“For further information, contact Customer Care on [customer.care@lidl.co.uk](mailto:customer.care@lidl.co.uk) or 0203 966 5566.”
