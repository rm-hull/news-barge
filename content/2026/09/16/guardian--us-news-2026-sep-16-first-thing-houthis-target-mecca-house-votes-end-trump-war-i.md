Good morning. Saudi Arabia’s air defenses intercepted and destroyed a Houthi drone south of Mecca yesterday before it entered prohibited airspace over the holy city. A spokesperson for the kingdom, which is fighting the Iran-aligned group in Yemen, said the security of Islam’s two holiest sites and of pilgrims was a “red line”. Mecca, home to shrines sacred to Muslims worldwide, is Islam’s holiest city and the focal point of the annual hajj pilgrimage.

Meanwhile, in Washington, the US House of Representatives has voted for a third time to end the war in Iran, approving a war powers resolution a few hours after a Congressional Budget Office (CBO) report showed the conflict had cost at least $38bn and left the US stockpile of defensive missiles so depleted it could take five years to rebuild.

## **All supporting acts withdraw from Ed Sheeran’s tour in solidarity with Macklemore**

![Ed Sheeran on stage](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/b27a59bcc91d1bff8523c4e6e164b3f1dd27e3bf/223_657_3293_2636/master/3293.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

All four supporting acts for upcoming dates of Ed Sheeran’s Loop tour of the US and Latin America have withdrawn in solidarity with fellow opening act Macklemore, as backlash grows against his removal from the tour over onstage comments in support of Palestine.

Grammy-winning Macklemore had said on stage he wanted the people of Gaza and “the occupied West Bank” to know “we have not forgotten about them”, along with the words “free Palestine”. The remark prompted a petition calling for Macklemore’s removal from the tour from the Israeli-American Council and the opposition of some venue owners – including Robert Kraft, the billionaire owner of the New England Patriots and a prominent donor to Israel.

- 
  **How has Ed Sheeran responded?** In a lengthy Instagram post, the British singer said: “I have always used my platform and music to bring people from all backgrounds and cultures together … I am not complicit. I have my personal views on this devastating conflict. Just because I choose not to speak publicly, it doesn’t mean I don’t have them, and it doesn’t mean that I don’t care. Macklemore coming off tour was the promoter’s decision, it was not mine.”

## **NBC helicopter crash in LA kills at least three people, with others injured**

![Emergency personnel work at the scene of the helicopter crash.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/aae04bed86e79a9ca6a8db46f88c198351020e1b/104_0_2500_2000/master/2500.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

A news helicopter crash in a Los Angeles neighborhood has killed at least three people, after it hit the ground and burst into flames near the site of a bus crash that occurred hours earlier.

The helicopter crashed near a one-storey commercial building in the neighborhood of Chatsworth in the San Fernando Valley, as news crews were covering the bus crash that left at least two people dead and six others injured, according the Los Angeles Fire Department.

## **New report reveals prevalence of physical and sexual violence in US**

![Silhouette of a young woman](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/5e0bb45da08f958b6d2c8b3136317371d2739b3c/0_0_4320_3456/master/4320.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

In their lifetime, more than half of US adults have experienced physical violence – and nearly half have experienced sexual violence, according to a new national study. The study, led by researchers from Tulane University’s Newcomb Institute and the University of California, San Diego, examined experiences of physical, sexual and intimate partner violence.

The study found that 52% of US adults have experienced physical violence during their lifetime, including cases involving a gun or knife. The rate was the same among women and men: 52% for each.
