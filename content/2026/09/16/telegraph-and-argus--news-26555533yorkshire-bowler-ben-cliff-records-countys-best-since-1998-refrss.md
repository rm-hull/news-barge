BEN Cliff has just recorded Yorkshire’s best bowling figures in a first-class innings this century.

During the ongoing Rothesay County Championship match against Somerset, the seamer took 8-76 as opponents Somerset were dismissed for 201 at Headingley.

Frank Smailes is the only Yorkshire bowler to take 10 first-class wickets in an innings, doing so against Derbyshire in June 1939.

A handful of players have taken eight wickets in an first-class innings for Yorkshire, but no-one since Steve Kirby, who managed 8-80 against Somerset in 2003.

To better Cliff’s figures, you have to go back to 1998, when Craig White took 8-55 for Yorkshire against Gloucestershire.

And in 1995, Peter Hartley claimed 9-41 for Yorkshire as they won a thrilling Championship match against Derbyshire by seven runs.
