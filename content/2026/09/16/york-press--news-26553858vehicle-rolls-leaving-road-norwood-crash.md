A VEHICLE has rolled over in a crash in a North Yorkshire village.

  



The county's fire and rescue service said two crews from Harrogate were called to Norwood at 7.35pm last night (Tuesday, September 15) to reports of an incident involving one vehicle.

The vehicle was reported to have left the road and rolled.

A service spokesperson said: "All occupants were out of the vehicles on arrival of the crews.

"Crews carried out scene safety and left the incident in the hands of Ambulance and Police crews."
