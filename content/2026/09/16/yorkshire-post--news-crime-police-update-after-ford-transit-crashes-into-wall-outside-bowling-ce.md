# Police update after Ford Transit crashes into wall outside bowling centre

## Police are appealing for witnesses after a crash into a wall on Leeds Road, Huddersfield at around 8.07pm on Tuesday (Sep 15).

A silver Ford Transit was in collision with a wall outside of UK Superbowl, police said.

It's believed a 58-year-old man suffered a medical episode at the wheel.

He was taken to Leeds General Infirmary where he remains in a critical condition.

![Police update after Ford Transit crashes into wall outside bowling centre](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmQ2MzhmOGI1LTdlZGQtNDY5ZS1hMDEzLWY3MmZjODE4Nzg3ZTo2NGEwYTZmMC1lNmM3LTQ2NjktOTFjMi01NTM1NjA0OWFhMDY=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Enquiries remain ongoing and anyone who witnessed the collision or who saw the vehicle prior to the collision is asked to come forward.

Officers are keen to see any dash-cam or mobile phone footage of the incident.

Information can be given via 101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 13260526688.
