TRAFFIC is continuing to be affected after a burst water main damaged part of a key residential road in York.

Works by Yorkshire Water to repair and reinstate Haxby Road are continuing this morning (Wednesday, September 16) near the junction with Walpole Street, close to the entrance for York St John University City Residence, with a hole across nearly half of the York-bound side.

  


Temporary traffic lights continued to operate, controlling vehicles around the work area.

The burst caused flooding near the junction with Fountayne Street on Monday, September 14.

A bus stop for services entering York has been temporarily suspended and moved nearer the Wigginton Road junction.

Haxby Road is a route into and out of the city, serving New Earswick and the A1237 Outer Ring Road.

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson said yesterday afternoon: "We repaired the burst main on Haxby Road overnight and are currently working to reinstate the road.

"For the safety of colleagues and road users, there are temporary two-way traffic lights currently in place around our working area.

"We apologise for the inconvenience and thank everyone in the area for their patience."
