A North East leisure centre's £1million gym transformation has earned national recognition after it was shortlisted for one of the fitness industry's top awards.

Billingham Forum in Billingham has been named a finalist in the Build, Design or Refurbishment of the Year category at the 2026 ukactive Awards.

The gym redevelopment, led by Tees Active in partnership with Stockton Council, increased floor space by 50 per cent and introduced a new group exercise studio.

It also brought in £300,000 worth of state-of-the-art fitness equipment.

Leon Jones, managing director of Tees Active, said: "Being named a finalist in the ukactive Awards is a fantastic achievement and recognition of the vision behind this investment.

"To be shortlisted for a ukactive Award for the second consecutive year makes this recognition even more special.

"Billingham Forum has always been at the heart of the community, and this redevelopment demonstrates our commitment to providing facilities that people can be proud of.

"We wanted to create a fitness environment that offers the quality, design and experience often associated with premium operators while remaining affordable and accessible to everyone."

The ukactive Awards celebrate excellence and innovation in the physical activity sector, highlighting organisations and projects that make a measurable impact on public health and activity levels.

The Build, Design or Refurbishment of the Year category honours facilities that have been created or transformed to improve customer experience, boost participation, and inspire active lifestyles.

Since reopening, the upgraded gym has earned positive feedback from members who have praised the additional space, improved layout, and new equipment.

A spokesman for Tees Active said the investment is part of the company's broader strategy to upgrade fitness facilities across Stockton-on-Tees, ensuring high-quality leisure options remain affordable for all residents.

This marks the second consecutive year Tees Active has been recognised by ukactive.

In 2025, IB Leisure was shortlisted in the Regional Centre of the Year category.

Councillor Nigel Cooke, cabinet member for environment, leisure and culture, said: "This national recognition is a testament to the quality and ambition of the investment at Billingham Forum.

"Working in partnership with Tees Active, we are proud to have delivered this impressive redevelopment that provides residents with greater opportunities to improve their health and wellbeing."

Winners will be announced at the ukactive Awards ceremony, which will take place on Thursday, October 8, 2026, at the ICC Birmingham.
