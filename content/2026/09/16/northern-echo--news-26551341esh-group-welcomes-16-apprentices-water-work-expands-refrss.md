A new intake of apprentices has joined a growing North East engineering partnership as it expands to help deliver a major investment programme aimed at improving water and wastewater infrastructure across the region.

Esh Group has welcomed 16 new apprentices in its latest annual intake, with seven joining Esh-Stantec to support Northumbrian Water's AMP8 investment programme.

David Pratt, divisional director at Esh-Stantec, said: "It's fantastic to welcome seven apprentices into Esh-Stantec as we continue to grow.

"These apprentices will gain valuable experience working on major infrastructure projects while helping us strengthen our workforce and continue delivering for communities across the region.

"We are committed to supporting their development and helping them reach their full potential as they build their careers within the industry."

The new apprentices will work alongside experienced teams while studying towards industry-recognised qualifications, gaining hands-on experience and skills for future careers.

One of the new starters, Ona-Naura Lum, has begun a Level 4 Quantity Surveying apprenticeship after a year-long placement in Esh Group’s affordable housing division.

Ms Lum said: "My placement year gave me a real insight into the industry and confirmed this was the career path I wanted to pursue.

"Everyone was incredibly supportive, and I knew Esh was somewhere I could build a strong foundation for my future career.

"I'm looking forward to gaining more experience on site and developing my skills as part of the apprenticeship programme."

Darush Dodds, corporate affairs and social value director at Esh Group, said: "Investing in young people remains one of the most important ways we can secure the future of our industry.
