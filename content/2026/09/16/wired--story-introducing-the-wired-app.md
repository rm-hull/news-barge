The new WIRED app is now available on iPhone and Android. Every day, WIRED brings you exclusive, future-looking journalism. Now there are more ways to make the most of it. Download it for free on iPhone or Android.

**In the WIRED app, you’ll have more ways to read, watch, listen, and connect:**

- Make WIRED yours with a personalized Daily Digest that will catch you up on the best of WIRED.
- Go beyond the article with original video, vertical podcasts, and more in the Watch tab.
- Take WIRED with you by listening to podcasts, narrated stories, and playlists wherever your day takes you.
- Join the conversation in our comment section and participate in live AMAs with WIRED journalists and experts.
- Get notified about breaking stories as soon as they go live.

Check out the WIRED app today on iPhone or Android.
