On 9 September this year, the 350th tenancy agreement was signed, said Cansel Kiziltepe, Senator for Social Affairs, during a press conference. “That means 420 people have now found a home of their own, including single parents, couples, families with children, but above all people for whom the path back to a new home of their own had been virtually blocked for a long time.”
