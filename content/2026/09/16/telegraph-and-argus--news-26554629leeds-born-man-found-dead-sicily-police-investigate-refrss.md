A WEST Yorkshire man has died in unexplained circumstances in Sicily.

Benjamin John Braithwaite, 35, who was born in Leeds was found dead near Noto in the Province of Syracuse at around 3am on September 13, according to Italian police.

A British Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO) spokesperson said: "We are supporting the family of a British man who died in Italy and are in touch with the local authorities."

A rope was found near the scene, and officers are still determining whether there were any injuries or signs of violence.

Authorities are also investigating the possible involvement of drugs or other substances.

Despite some reports, no evidence of esoteric rituals was found near the body.

A drawing with "esoteric connotations" was discovered on a perimeter wall of the property, but not close to where the body was located.

The investigation is being led by Carabinieri in Noto, under the direction of the public prosecutor's office in Syracuse.
