# Watch: Why has the Federal Reserve raised interest rates?

The Federal Reserve has raised US interest rates to 3.75%-4% from 3.5%-3.75%, in a bid to curb inflation. This is the first such increase in three years.

Why has the increase taken place and what does it mean? The BBC’s Samira Hussain explains.
