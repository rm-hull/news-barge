A 15.5-mile charity walk has raised more than £1,700 to help children facing adversity.

The team at Don Whitley Scientific (DWS) completed the 15.5-mile walk on September 12, starting from their headquarters in Bingley and finishing at Granary Wharf in Leeds.

The fundraiser marked the 15th anniversary of The Principle Trust Children’s Charity, which provides free respite breaks for children across Yorkshire facing serious challenges.

Tom Walton, Service & Business Continuity Director at DWS, said: "Taking on the canal walk was a fantastic challenge, and it was brilliant to see everyone come together to support such an important cause.

"We’d like to thank everyone at The Principle Trust Children’s Charity for their support throughout the campaign.

"We are incredibly proud of what we’ve raised and hope the funds will make a meaningful difference to children and families across our local community."

The £1,760 raised is enough to fund week-long respite breaks for three children and their families.

Kerry Magson, Head of Partnerships, Service Delivery, and Engagement at The Principle Trust Children’s Charity, said: "From us all at The Principle Trust, we would like to extend a huge thanks to the team at DWS for taking on the Locks to Leeds challenge and choosing to fundraise for our charity."
