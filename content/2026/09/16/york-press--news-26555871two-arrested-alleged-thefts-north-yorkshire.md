A man and woman have been arrested over a spate of alleged thefts in North Yorkshire.

Police said a 37-year-old man and a 35-year-old woman were taken into custody on Tuesday, September 15, as part of an investigation into theft from a motor vehicle, shop theft, handling stolen goods, and other offences.

Both suspects have since been released on conditional bail while enquiries continue.

As part of the probe, officers recovered a trolley and camping equipment, believed to have been stolen from a vehicle in the Harrogate area in recent days.

North Yorkshire Police have issued an appeal to anyone who recognises the items to come forward.

A spokesperson said: "If you recognise any of the items pictured, or believe they may belong to you, please get in touch with North Yorkshire Police.

"If you have any information that could assist our investigation, or believe the belongings are yours, please contact us online in the first instance."

Information can be passed on via email to PC 312 Helen James on [Helen.James@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Helen.James@northyorkshire.police.uk), or by calling North Yorkshire Police on 101.

For those who wish to remain anonymous, information can be provided to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online.
