A selection of childhood staples, ranging from Tickle Me Elmo to the humble playground slide, are among the finalists for this year's National Toy Hall of Fame.

The Strong’s National Toy Hall of Fame in Rochester, New York, announced its 12 nominees for the Class of 2026 on Wednesday. Public voting runs from Sept. 16-23 and a panel of judges will select the inductees for an official announcement in November.

The shortlist also includes Care Bears, the dice game Yahtzee, the ancient tile game Mahjong, the Pokémon Trading Card Game, and Bingo.

![The 12 toys that could end up in the National Toy Hall of Fame](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/16/15/31/NTHOF-Nominees-2026-SQ.jpg)

Completing the field of candidates are the handheld electronic game Bop It!, Monster High dolls, miniature Polly Pocket playsets, and the classic board games Mouse Trap and Sorry!

"It’s debating season, and this year’s finalists give everyone plenty to talk about — from classic games to plush icons and other toys that have shaped generations of play,” Michelle Parnett-Dwyer, senior curator at the museum, said in a statement.

“The debate is part of the fun because every toy sparks different memories and makes its own case for why it matters.”

While public nominations are open to any toy, final honorees must achieve iconic standing, appeal across multiple generations, foster creative play, or fundamentally alter toy design.

The top three selections will enter the Hall alongside 90 existing inductees, a collection that already features Cabbage Patch Kids, Dungeons & Dragons, Hot Wheels, Frisbee, G.I. Joe, and Mr. Potato Head.

A few of this year's contenders have been previously nominated without making the final cut, including Tickle Me Elmo, Mahjong, and the Pokémon Trading Card Game.
