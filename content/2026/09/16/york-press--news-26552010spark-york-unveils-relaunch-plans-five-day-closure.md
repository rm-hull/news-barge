A double-weekend celebration kicks off at a popular York community venue this Friday after a huge operation to install its covered roof.

Spark York has revealed plans for the relaunch after its five-day closure for seasonal upgrades, including a canopy to protect guests from the elements over autumn and winter.

  Spark York is holding two weekends of celebrations to show off a new covered roof, installed during a five-day closure. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The venue on Piccadilly is holding a two-weekend celebration to showcase its fresh look and introduce its new seasonal offerings.

  Spark York closed for five days while the roof was installed. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The festivities will kick off on Friday, September 18, at 5pm, with new bar menus featuring autumn-inspired cocktails, draught beers, and expanded low-and-no-alcohol options.

  Spark York has revealed plans for the relaunch after its five-day closure. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The opening night will feature a live set at PicTap by DJ Jonny Hooker, alongside an airbrush tattoo station by FacePaint York.

Key events across the first weekend include the launch of the Wellbeing Collective on Saturday, September 19.

  The festivities will kick off on Friday, September 18, at 5pm at Spark York. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

It will start with complimentary, bookable sessions, including yoga with Harriet and a candlelit sound bath with Cultivate Kindness, ahead of a full season of regular classes and meditation events.

Spark's newly-refurbished pop-up unit, Canvas York, will debut, hosting an evolving line-up of diverse food, drink, and creative projects throughout the autumn and winter.

  The new roof in place above the upper level at Spark York ready for cooler weather. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The space opens with guest concept George Cares, serving elevated sharing plates from Saturday to Sunday.

Visitors can also experience personalised live poetry by typewriter poet Molly Ovendon, evening DJ sets by Issy Green, and a free photobooth running at select times throughout the weekend.

  Spark York on Piccadilly overlooks Clifford's Tower. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Sunday, September 20, will see Family & Craft Sundays, a day focused on mindful activities for all ages, featuring a free kids' crafting workshop with Avocado Events, upstairs watercolour classes, and venue-wide games.

The second weekend, September 25-27, will be focussed more on community connections, local collaborations, and charity fundraising.

The festivities will begin with tunes from local favourite DJ Jono and authentic Thai cuisine from Bee's Khraw operating out of Spark's newly refurbished pop-up unit.

Key events across the second weekend include fundraiser film screenings and a Q&A on Saturday, September 27, featuring the Oscar-winning No Other Land and Andrew McConnell's Gaza.

  The covered roof, part-way through installation during the venue's five-day closure. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The event will be followed by a brief Q&A with urban sociologist Manal Massalha and consultant surgeon Dr Khaled Dawas, who recently returned from emergency medical missions in Gaza.

Sunday, September 28, will see a breathwork and meditation session hosted by Teapot Solidarity Space, followed by a shared community meal prepared by Waqif Arabian Kitchen, with all proceeds supporting The Hanoon Foundation.

Becca Bowers, head of creative at SPARK, said: "After a brilliant summer, we’re closing briefly to get our space fully prepped and roofed for the colder weather.

"We aren't just opening back up for one night; we’ve put together two full weekends that capture everything SPARK stands for: great independent food and drink, community wellbeing, creative spaces, and bringing people together for meaningful events."

For full event schedules, wellbeing class bookings, or film screening tickets, visit the Spark website.
