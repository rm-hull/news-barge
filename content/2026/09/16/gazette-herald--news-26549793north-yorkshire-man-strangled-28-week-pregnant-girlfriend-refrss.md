A MAN who strangled his girlfriend when she was 28 weeks pregnant with his child has been jailed.

Kelly Clarke, prosecuting, said the woman had to bite Ben Alexander Stevenson’s arm to stop him holding it across her neck and choking her.

She couldn’t breathe and believes at one point she blacked out.

The woman told York Crown Court that since the incident in May, her voice has suffered and there are days when she has no voice at all.

“I am being punished for the consequences of his actions,” she said. “He has never apologised for his actions. He knows what he has done.”

She no longer feels safe and her mental health had deteriorated since the incident.

She said she now feels embarrassed and guilty for helping him when he was recalled to prison while on parole.

The judge that sentenced him then accepted he was not among the leaders of the gang.

The woman told York Crown Court: “There were times I believed he wanted to change. Looking back, I realise I was wrong. I am now trying to rebuild my life.”

Stevenson, 27, of no fixed address and formerly living near Knaresborough, pleaded guilty to strangulation and was jailed for two years.

He was also banned from contacting the woman in any way for the next ten years.

Recorder Christopher Knox said it was a “very unpleasant” offence.

"She was clearly very distressed and worried about her pregnancy,” he told Stevenson.

The court heard that the pregnancy was not affected.
