A NATIONAL optician chain could be set to open an additional branch in an East Yorkshire town.

Specsavers has submitted a planning application to East Riding of Yorkshire Council for the refurbishment of a vacant retail unit at Unit Three, 117-119 Walkergate in Beverley, the site of a former GP surgery.

The optician and hearing care retailer also has a branch in Toll Gavel in the town centre.

  


The 120-square-metre unit within the Grade II listed building in Walkergate would see new signs in a "non-illuminated heritage style", as well as minor internal alterations and a replacement skylight, window vinyls, a new "logo-pod" and new air-conditioning to maintain temperature and fresh air flow within the medical test rooms, according to documents issued with the application.

The plans say the scale of the proposals have been kept to a minimum to match the existing buildings layout.

"The new shopfront signage is important as it will give the new store brand continuity with the Specsavers brand and make the store recognisable to the general public within the high street location, the plans said, adding, "the new logo-pod is required as the current signage area is small and leave no room for both the logo and the strapline.

"The strapline is important as it highlights that Specsavers offer hear-care services, something that often gets overlooked."

East Riding of Yorkshire Council said that consultation on the application ends on October 1.
