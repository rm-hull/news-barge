A North East attraction is gearing up for its biggest festive season yet, with tickets on sale for a Christmas programme packed with a light trail, seasonal experiences and family attractions.

The Alnwick Garden will be transformed with sparkling displays, seasonal food and immersive activities throughout November and December, including its signature Winter Light Trail and Father Christmas’ Lilidorei Grotto.

Mark Brassell, CEO of The Alnwick Garden, said: "There is something really special about the dark nights arriving and the feeling that Christmas is just around the corner.

"This year, we’ve made it our mission to ensure visitors experience that magic the second they arrive.

"From November, the whole attraction will be completely transformed.

"With lights and decorations throughout The Garden, and an illuminated Christmas tree forest in Lilidorei, visitors will feel the festive spirit wherever they turn."

The Winter Light Trail will run from November 20, 2026, to January 3, 2027, and promises a magical experience for visitors of all ages.

Glowing installations, festive soundscapes and interactive surprises will light up The Garden and Lilidorei along the trail.

Families can enjoy festive breakfasts with Father Christmas or visit him in his Lilidorei grotto, with extra slots added after high demand.

Other events include Lilidorei Christmas from November 16 to January 3, a festive menu at The Treehouse from November 18 to January 3, and afternoon tea from November 19 to December 24, Thursday to Sunday.

Highlights include the Alnwick Garden Christmas Fair on November 21 and 22, a wreath-making class on November 27 and a Charlotte Tilbury festive make-up masterclass on November 29.

Festive feasts will be held at The Pavilion on December 10 and 17, with breakfasts at The Treehouse and Lilidorei on December 5, 12 and 19.

Mr Brassell said: "Our Winter Light Trail has become such a special tradition for so many, and every year our team challenges itself to create something even more magical.
