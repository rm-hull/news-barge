Redcar Bulk Terminal (RBT) has notified residents of upcoming controlled explosive demolition works as part of the project to remove the terminal's unloaders.

The demolition is scheduled for:

- Friday, September 18, at about 10.30am
- Monday, September 21, at about 9:30am

The controlled blasts are part of the terminal’s decommissioning and improvement work.

People in the surrounding area may hear a loud noise at the scheduled times as the demolition charges are detonated.

  Redcar Bulk Terminal and where the explosions will happen. 

*(Image: PROVIDED)*

Safety controls will be in place throughout the work.

Anyone working nearby should note the timings.

Redcar Bulk Terminal is a deep-water port on the south bank of the River Tees in north-east England.
