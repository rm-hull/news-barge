A West Yorkshire market town has been named one of the cheapest towns to retire to in England.

World Atlas has released a list of the seven wallet-friendly towns to retire to in England.

Each of the towns has a “personality of their own” with affordable housing and plenty of things to keep you occupied.

Todmorden made it onto the list thanks to its great train links and wonderful walks you can take around the town.


## What is Todmorden like?

On Todmorden, World Atlas said: “High in the Upper Calder Valley, this town lies close to the Lancashire border, surrounded by the steep hills of the South Pennines.

“The housing market makes a strong case for budget-conscious retirees, with homes selling for an average of about £228,000 over the past year.

“Regular trains connect the town with Manchester and Leeds.

“Todmorden Market Hall has occupied the town centre since the Victorian era, with traders selling local produce alongside books and household goods.

“For something out of the ordinary, walk the Incredible Edible Trail, where community vegetable beds and herb patches are planted in public spaces around town.

“Whatever is growing is there for anyone to pick.


“Above the valley stands Stoodley Pike, a 121-foot (37 m) obelisk reached by a walk across the moors.

“The bold can climb the 39 steps of its unlit spiral staircase to the viewing gallery.

“Closer to town, the Rochdale Canal towpath passes the retaining wall locals call the Great Wall of Tod and ducks under the Victorian Gauxholme Viaduct.”

The majority of properties sold in Todmorden over the last year were terraced and cost around £177,377, according to Rightmove.

Semi-detached properties cost around £277,504, with the average price of detached properties jumping up to £457,250.

Todmorden has lots of history to explore thanks to its importance during the Industrial Revolution and its grand town hall that you can take a tour of.


You can also take a walk through Centre Vale Park, which is just a short walk away from the town’s centre and features a children’s play area, cycle way, and bowling greens.

You can try out the heritage trail that takes you to 16 different points of interest around the park.

If you are up for a longer walk, there are several trails you can follow to see more of the surrounding countryside, and you might want to take a picnic to enjoy the scenery for even longer.

You do not need to leave the town to enjoy some high-quality food, as you can visit Grasso, a restaurant offering authentic pasta dishes from Campania, or 3 Wise Monkeys for some fresh Thai food.

If you want to catch up with friends, you can head to the Bridge Café for a home-cooked meal or Amicis for a coffee and a slice of cake.
