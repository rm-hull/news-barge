A YORK man has lost his job but kept his freedom after he admitted online sex crimes involving children committed between five and 24 years ago.

Laura Peto, prosecuting, said police found more than 3,700 indecent pictures and videos of youngsters when they raided Timothy Binder’s home in December 2021.

They had been downloaded from the internet between 2002 and 2021 and some had been deliberately filed in folders on his devices.

Binder told a police officer: “I’m addicted to pornography” and admitted downloading “child pornography” but denied being sexually interested in children when he was interviewed following his arrest.

The 60-year-old from St Stephen’s Road, Acomb, who attended York Crown Court on crutches, Timothy Binder pleaded guilty to three offences of making indecent images of children.

His barrister Nicholas Hammond said he had been sacked as a result of his convictions and would be making an application to live on state benefits.

He gave no mitigation after Judge Simon Hickey said the prison sentence would be suspended.

The judge told Binder children had to be sexually abused to produce the images and added: “They wouldn’t be abused unless someone like you wants to view them.”

He gave him a nine-month prison sentence suspended for 18 months on condition that Binder does 20 days’ rehabilitative activities, barred him from ever working with children and vulnerable adults and ordered him to pay £300 prosecution costs.

Binder will be on the sex offenders’ register for 10 years.

He declined a prosecution request for Binder to be made subject to a sexual harm prevention order that would enable police to monitor and control his use of the internet, saying Binder had not reoffended in the four years and nine months since his arrest.

Binder had no previous convictions.

Ms Peto said Binder had two videos of the worst kind of sexual images of children, six pictures of the middle category and 3,744 images of the least serious category including 25 videos.

Police carried out the raid on Binder’s home after receiving a tip-off that someone at his address had indecent images of children.
