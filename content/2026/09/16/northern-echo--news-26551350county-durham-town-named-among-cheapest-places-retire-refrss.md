A market town in County Durham has been named one of the most wallet-friendly spots to retire to in England.

World Atlas has released a list of the best towns in England to retire to that will not cost you loads of money.

While remaining affordable, the towns all have their own personality with lots of things to see and do.

Bishop Auckland made it onto the list thanks to its affordable housing and beautiful attractions to visit.


## What is Bishop Auckland like?

On the town, World Atlas said: “Years of investment have given Bishop Auckland a new lease of life, particularly around its historic centre.

“Housing remains one of the strongest arguments for retiring here, with homes selling for an average of roughly £147,000, about half England's average.

“Traditional shops still run along Newgate Street, while the Auckland Project has restored several heritage buildings and opened new cultural venues around them.

“At Auckland Palace, you can walk through what was the residence of the Prince Bishops of Durham, taking in the Georgian state rooms and Francisco de Zurbarán's series Jacob and his Twelve Sons, bought by Bishop Trevor in 1756.

“Outside are a 17th-century walled garden and the 150-acre deer park the bishops once hunted.

“Art lovers should not skip the Spanish Gallery on Market Place.

  The area also has lots of cafés and restaurants to try out. 

*(Image: Tripadvisor)*

“It holds one of the largest collections of 16th and 17th-century Spanish Golden Age art outside London, with work by El Greco, Murillo and Velázquez.

“No. 42 brings residents together through hands-on creative sessions, exhibitions by local artists and a monthly makers' market.

“The town's own market fills the Market Place every Thursday and Saturday with produce, crafts and street food.”

The majority of properties in Bishop Auckland over the last year were terraced and sold for an average price of £100,601, according to Rightmove.

Semi-detached properties sold for around £160,683 and detached properties sold for an average of £238,446.


The town is home to the Auckland Gardens that can be found next to Auckland Palace that were originally designed for the Prince Bishops of Durham.

There are several areas to explore, including the 17th-century Walled Garden that has been restored and now produces lots of fresh produce that can be enjoyed in the site’s cafes and restaurants.

After walking around the gardens, you can visit Auckland Palace and explore the many rooms to learn more about the area's history.

There are a few museums and galleries in and around the area, such as the Mining Art Gallery and the Spanish Gallery.

As well as the great outdoor spaces and historical attractions to visit, Bishop Auckland is home to great cafés and restaurants, including Fifteas Vintage Tea Room.

There is also Panda Panda, a Chinese restaurant, and the March Hare, where you can relax in the beer garden with a pint or enjoy a pub meal inside.
