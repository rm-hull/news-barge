A Bradford man has been charged with a number of vehicle-related offences following an arrest by neighbourhood policing officers.

Lukas Dawson, 37, of Holme Lane, was arrested on suspicion of driving offences and has since been charged.

## Multiple charges brought

West Yorkshire Police said Dawson has been charged with aggravated vehicle theft without the owner's consent, failing to stop, dangerous driving, driving without insurance and possession of cannabis.

The charges were brought by local neighbourhood officers in Bradford South.

## Court appearance

Following the charges, Dawson was remanded into custody.

He appeared at Bradford Magistrates' Court on Monday, September 14.

## Next hearing scheduled

Dawson is due to appear next at Bradford Crown Court on January 6.

As criminal proceedings are active, he is entitled to a fair trial.

FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police

A spokesperson said: "Local Neighbourhood Officers in Bradford South have charged a man with vehicle offences.

"Lukas Dawson age 37 of Holme Lane, was arrested on suspicion of driving offences.

They added: "He was charged with aggravated vehicle theft without the owner’s consent, failing to stop, dangerous driving, driving without insurance and being in possession of cannabis.

"He was remanded into custody and appeared at Bradford Magistrates’ Court on 14 September. He’ll next appear at Bradford Crown Court on 6 January."
