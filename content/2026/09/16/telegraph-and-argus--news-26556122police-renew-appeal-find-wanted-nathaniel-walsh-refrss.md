Police have renewed an appeal to trace a man with links to bradford who is wanted in connection with disqualified driving and assault offences.

Nathaniel Walsh, 35, is being sought by officers and remains wanted almost two months after West Yorkshire Police first appealed for information about his whereabouts.

## Links across West Yorkshire

When the appeal was first issued on July 16, police said Walsh was known to reside in Huddersfield and had links to several areas across Kirklees, including Deighton, Dewsbury, Fartown and Lindley.

He also has links to Brighouse, Ovenden and Illingworth, as well as parts of Bradford.

Officers have now reissued the appeal as enquiries to locate him continue.

## Distinctive features

Police have described Walsh as being around 5ft 10in tall and of medium build.

He has a pierced left ear, a chipped upper tooth on the left side of his mouth and a gold tooth.

Officers also said he has a tattoo on his right arm bearing the words: "Loved by some hated by many".

## Information sought

West Yorkshire Police is urging anyone who may have seen Walsh or knows where he is to get in touch.

Anyone with information is asked to contact police on 101 or via the force's LiveChat service, quoting reference 13240165590.

Information can also be passed anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online.

## FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police

On Sept 16, West Yorkshire Police said: "We're still on the lookout for Nathaniel Walsh, 35, who is wanted in connection with disqualified driving and assault offences.

"He is believed to have links to Huddersfield, Deighton, Dewsbury, Fartown and Lindley.

"He also has links to Brighouse, Ovenden and Illingworth as well as parts of Bradford.

"If you have any information of his whereabouts then please contact police on 101 or online using LiveChat quoting reference 13240165590. "
