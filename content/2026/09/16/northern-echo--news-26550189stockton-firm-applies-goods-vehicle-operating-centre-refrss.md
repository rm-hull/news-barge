A company is seeking permission to operate goods vehicles and trailers from a North East site.

According to a public notice, Katmex Ltd, based on Bowron Street, Stockton, has applied for a licence to use their address as an operating centre.

The company plans to run two goods vehicles and three trailers from the location.

Owners or occupiers of land (including buildings) near the operating centres who believe that their use or enjoyment of that land would be affected, should make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE stating their reasons.

Representations must be submitted within 21 days of this notice.

- 
    **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo (publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be foundhere.**

Representors must at the same time send a copy of their representations to the applicant at the address given at the top of this notice.

A Guide to Making Representations is available on the government website.

The notice was published on September 12.
