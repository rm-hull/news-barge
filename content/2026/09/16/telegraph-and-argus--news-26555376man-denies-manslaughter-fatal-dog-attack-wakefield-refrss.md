A man has denied manslaughter after his dog fatally bit another man during a dispute on a footpath.

James Judge, 55, died following the incident on the old train line footpath off Blacker Lane in Wakefield on August 15.

Lee Ellis, 55, of Calder Grove, Wakefield, appeared in court on Wednesday, where he pleaded not guilty to manslaughter and being in charge of a dog dangerously out of control causing injury resulting in death.

At a previous hearing, solicitor Rob Fones, representing Mr Ellis, said there had been "a verbal altercation in regards to right of way."

Judge Tom Bayliss KC granted bail to Mr Ellis, with a trial set to begin on May 17 at Leeds Crown Court.

A further pre-trial hearing was scheduled for April 15.

An inquest into Mr Judge’s death was opened and adjourned last week.

Acting senior coroner Oliver Longstaff said Mr Judge was taken to Leeds General Infirmary after the incident, where he later died.

He was identified by his mother.

A post-mortem examination gave a provisional cause of death as complications of a right leg wound caused by a dog bite.
