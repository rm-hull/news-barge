BRADFORD Council is inviting residents and businesses to explore the future of electric vehicles and sustainable transport.

The Plug In Bradford District event will take place on Friday, September 18, in Centenary Square from 9.45am to 4pm.

Hosted in partnership with Let’s Experience Electric, the event aims to promote the transition to electric vehicles (EVs) by showcasing the latest EV models, charging technologies, and sustainable transport solutions.

A spokesperson for Bradford Council said: "We will be engaging with residents and businesses to highlight the latest advancements in EV technology, our plans for the EV charging infrastructure and to sign posting towards grants and funding, so that informed choices based on your own needs can be made whatever your budget.

"For anyone thinking about their next vehicle, this free event, delivered with the support of Let’s Experience Electric, provides a valuable opportunity to see first-hand the growing range of electric vehicles now available and to speak directly with experts about the practicalities of ownership."

Attendees will be able to explore a variety of EVs, view charging demonstrations, and learn about ongoing EV projects supported by Bradford Council and the West Yorkshire Combined Authority.

These include the installation of public charging points and the purchase and rollout of electric buses on the region’s public transport network.

Visitors can also take a look at LNER Bradford Beryl electric bikes.

Business support sessions will be held at City Hall for Bradford district businesses.

These will cover fleet decarbonisation, infrastructure planning, and government support schemes, with speakers from Cenex, Northern PowerGrid, and the Energy Saving Trust.

An exhibition area will feature companies involved in EV infrastructure, including charge point installers and operators.

The Energy Saving Trust will provide updates on Office of Zero Emission Vehicles grant programmes.

Cenex will outline the steps involved in fleet and depot electrification, while Northern PowerGrid will discuss regional network updates and new connection processes.

The event is free and open to the public, with registration encouraged through the council’s website.
