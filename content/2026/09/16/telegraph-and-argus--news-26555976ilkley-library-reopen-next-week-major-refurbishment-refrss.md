Ilkley Library and Visitor Information Centre will reopen next week following major refurbishment work.

The newly-upgraded space, designed to be more welcoming and accessible, will open to the public on Monday, September 21.

It has accessible toilets, better lighting and heating, plus a kitchenette for community groups.

Councillor Ian Walker, Bradford Council’s executive member for neighbourhoods and communities, said: "We are delighted to be returning to our newly refurbished library and look forward to welcoming everyone back on September 21.

"The improvements have created a brighter, warmer and more accessible space for our customers and community groups.

"We'd like to thank everyone for their patience and support while the refurbishment work has been taking place."

During the work, library services and the Visitor Information Centre were temporarily relocated to Ilkley Town Hall.

The refurbishment was designed to make the centre more comfortable for residents, visitors and community groups.

The Visitor Information Centre moved from the town hall into the library in 2018, with Ilkley Parish Council funding two part-time posts.
