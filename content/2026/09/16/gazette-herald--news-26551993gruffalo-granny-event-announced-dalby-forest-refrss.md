Families are being invited to step into a magical new adventure this half term as a North Yorkshire forest hosts special storytelling events celebrating the latest addition to one of children's best-loved book series.

Dalby Forest will host a day of Gruffalo Granny-themed fun on October 31, part of a partnership between Forestry England and Macmillan Children’s Books to mark the launch of the new title by Julia Donaldson and Axel Scheffler.

A Forestry England spokesperson said: "Forestry England is delighted to be partnering with Macmillan Children’s Books to celebrate the brand new story that comes 22 years after the publication of The Gruffalo’s Child."

  Dalby Forest will host a day of Gruffalo Granny-themed storytelling and family activities as part of a partnership between Julia Donaldson, Axel Scheffler, Forestry England and Macmillan Children’s Books 

*(Image: Forestry England/Crown copyright)*

The free event will include live storytelling, themed activities, and special appearances from the Gruffalo himself.

Children will be able to meet the character, enjoy Gruffalo Granny activity booklets, and explore the story through interactive sessions.

Copies of the new book will also be available to purchase on the day.

  Dalby Forest will host a day of Gruffalo Granny-themed storytelling and family activities as part of a partnership between Julia Donaldson, Axel Scheffler, Forestry England and Macmillan Children’s Books 

*(Image: Forestry England/Crown copyright)*

No booking is required, but activities will run on a first-come, first-served basis and visitors are encouraged to arrive early as sessions may become busy.

The event is timed to coincide with the changing colours of the season, offering families a chance to enjoy the forest’s autumn scenery.
