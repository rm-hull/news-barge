The latest fuel prices come amid growing concerns over global oil supplies as tensions in the Middle East continue to escalate.

Analysts have warned that disruption to major export routes could eventually have an impact on pump prices in the UK, although the full effects remain unclear.

Saudi Arabia recently closed its East-West oil pipeline after an attack it said was carried out by drones launched by Iranian-backed militias in Iraq.

Reports suggest repairs could take between three and five weeks, temporarily affecting one of the region's most important routes for transporting crude oil to international markets.
