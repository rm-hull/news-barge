A traditional Italian restaurant found along the coast has been praised for being “absolutely wonderful”.

If you are taking a trip to Saltburn-by-the-Sea, it is common to go for some fish and chips while enjoying the seaside.

However, if you are not a fan of fish and chips or fancy something different, the coastal town has plenty of other highly rated restaurants to try.

Alessi’s is an Italian restaurant found in the town that offers a taste of Italy in the northeast.


## What is on the menu at Alessi’s?

Alessi’s has an extensive menu with plenty of options to choose from depending on what you fancy.

You can choose between different hot and cold starters, from bruschetta and Caprese salad to garlic mushrooms, king prawns, and calamari.

If you feel like enjoying a pasta dish, there are loads to choose from, including classics like lasagne, spaghetti carbonara, and bolognese, as well as a few dishes created by the restaurant.

Of course, Alessi’s offers a range of pizzas with lots of toppings to choose from, such as plain margheritas and mushroom pizzas, to onions, peppers, and meat options.

If you need something sweet to finish off your meal, Alessi’s has tiramisu and a limoncello flute for something light, as well as banoffee pie, sticky toffee pudding, and chocolate fudge cake.


### What do reviews say about Alessi’s in Saltburn-by-the-Sea?

Alessi’s has a rating of 4.2 out of 5 on Tripadvisor, with 793 people leaving reviews after their visit.

One person said: “Absolutely wonderful Italian restaurant.

“Very friendly and well established.

“Would highly recommend this place.

“One staff member has worked for the family 25 years!

“This place has a really good vibe. I will be back!”
