A GROUP of "suspicious" men claiming to be from BT/Openreach has prompted a police warning.

The men were reported knocking on doors in Cleckheaton on Tuesday asking to check residents' phone lines and internet access.

Officers from the Batley and Spen Neighbourhood Policing Team responded but were unable to locate the group, described as smartly dressed and believed to be in their late teens to early twenties.

## What have police said

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "A search of the area did not locate the group, and it is at present not known if the group were legitimate or not.

"It was great to see that residents were vigilant, and we are not aware of anyone letting the males into their homes.

"All legitimate callers will have identification which should be displayed to confirm their identity.

"If anyone is unsure about a caller, then please contact the agency/company the person purports to be from, but don't use a contact number provided by the person."

Police have asked anyone with CCTV or doorbell footage to contact them on 101, quoting log reference 1507 of September 15, 2026.
