A MAN is in critical condition after his van crashed into a wall in Huddersfield.

The incident happened at around 8.07pm on Leeds Road, outside UK Superbowl.

Police believe the 58-year-old driver of a silver Ford Transit may have suffered a medical episode at the wheel.

He was taken to Leeds General Infirmary, where he remains in critical condition.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Enquiries remain ongoing and anyone who witnessed the collision or who saw the vehicle prior to the collision is asked to come forward.

"Officers are keen to see any dashcam or mobile phone footage of the incident."

Anyone with information is asked to contact police, quoting reference 13260526688.
