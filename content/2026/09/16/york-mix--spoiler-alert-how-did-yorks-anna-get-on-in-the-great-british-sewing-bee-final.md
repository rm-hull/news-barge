If you haven’t watched the final of *The Great British Sewing Bee* 2026, look away now.

Because we’re about to reveal how York contestant Anna got on in the show, broadcast on BBC One last night.

Still with us… Well, we can now say…

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/anna-york-great-british-sewing-bee-2026-final-3.webp)

**She won!**

Anna, 27, who is part of the St Leonard’s Hospice retail team in York, has blown the judges away all through the series and was crowned champion after shining in all three challenges in last night’s show.

“Combining imagination with a passion for historical garment construction,” Anna had seven wins at the transformation challenge before the final.

Anna first got into sewing by repairing and altering pre-loved clothes she’d bought. Then she discovered online videos on how to sew historical garments, and she developed a love for costume making.

During the show, Anna, was seen relaxing on the narrow boat she and her boyfriend built from a shell. And her parents Steve and Brenda spoke of how proud they were of her.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/anna-york-great-british-sewing-bee-2026-final-2.webp)

There are three challenges in each *Sewing Bee* episode. In the final, the first of these, the pattern challenge, saw the three finalists asked to create a cummerbund and bow tie.

Judges Patrick Grant and Esme Young were impressed by Anna’s “sharp” and “very good” cummerbund/bow tie combination and awarded her first place.

The transformation challenge saw the sewers turn tableware made for weddings and parties into a chic outfit you might wear at a beach club.

In this, Anna’s outfit, with its “dramatically slashed open front” was declared both “super sexy” and “super successful”, and won the day.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/anna-york-great-british-sewing-bee-2026-final-4.webp)

Finally, the three faced their last made-to-measure challenge – creating an outfit a friend could wear to something like the Met Gala, with the theme “metamorphosis”.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/anna-york-great-british-sewing-bee-2026-final-5.webp)

Anna’s dress was inspired by the lifecycle of a damsel fly, featuring iridescent blues and greens and including organza wings on a silk dress.

Judges loved these colours and the “fan of really beautiful delicate wings”.

After much deliberation, Patrick and Esme declared Anna the winner of *The Great British Sewing Bee* 2026.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/anna-york-great-british-sewing-bee-2026-final-6.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/anna-york-great-british-sewing-bee-2026-final-7.webp)

After she was awarded the trophy, Anna said: “I can’t think of words to say that aren’t just screaming!

“I never saw this coming and I’ve learnt so much and I’ve met so many amazing people. It’s so incredible.”
