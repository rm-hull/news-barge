TG Jones is closing down in Darlington - along with the town centre’s only Post Office.

The national chain, formerly WHSmith, will be pulling down the shutters on its store at the Cornmill Shopping Centre later this year, it has been confirmed.

The closure means that Darlington Post Office will also shut.

Bosses say a closing date is still being finalised but is likely to be around the end of November.

However, a spokesperson for the postal service confirmed that it is looking to open a replacement branch as soon as possible, and has already heard from interested retailers.

It could take around six months to set up, meaning customers will have to use branches on Yarm Road, Skerne Park, and Haughton-Le-Skerne for a period of time.

The closure means that TG Jones no longer has a presence in the town centre, with nearest stores now being in Richmond and at Teesside Park.

At the time, it said the closures are part of a wider plan to rescue the business from insolvency, with hundreds of jobs affected as a result.

Tom Smith KC, representing TG Jones in court, said at the time that the company had suffered from "long-term sales decline" due to high inflation, increased online shopping, lower consumer spending, and rising labour costs.

Mr Smith said the rebranding from WHSmith to TG Jones also negatively impacted sales.

Chief executive of TG Jones, Alex Willson, said: "We welcome the court’s approval of our restructuring plan.

"This decision allows us to move ahead with our turnaround strategy.

"The plan protects the substantial core of the store estate and makes TG Jones a stronger, more sustainable business."

A spokesperson for the Post Office said: “We fully recognise the challenging trading conditions that TG Jones and many other High Street retailers are facing at the moment.

“TG Jones had its restructuring plan approved by the High Court on 1 July, which includes seeking new commercial terms with landlords for the majority of their estate.

“Consequently, TG Jones is to close their Darlington store. Regrettably, Darlington Post Office is due to close and the date is being finalised - it is likely to be the end of November.

“We know how important our products and services are to the local community."
