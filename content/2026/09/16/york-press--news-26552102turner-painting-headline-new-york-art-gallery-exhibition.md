## Turner masterpiece to headline new York Art Gallery exhibition inspired by Flaxman

Joseph Mallord William Turner, 1775–1851. Ulysses deriding Polyphemus - Homer's Odyssey, 1829 *(Image: The National Gallery, London)*

An exhibition featuring one of JMW Turner’s most celebrated paintings is set to open in York, with curators putting the finishing touches to a showcase bringing together works by some of Britain's and Europe’s most renowned artists.

Turner, Wedgwood & Beyond: Inspired by Flaxman opens at York Art Gallery on Friday, September 18, and will run until January 17, 2027.

The centrepiece of the exhibition is Turner’s Ulysses deriding Polyphemus – Homer's Odyssey, on loan from the National Gallery.

  Turner's Ulysses deriding Polyphemus - Homers Odyssey in the process of being transported 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The display also features works by Josiah Wedgwood, William Blake, Angelica Kauffman, Jean-Auguste-Dominique Ingres, and William Etty.

A spokesperson said: "The curators behind York Art Gallery's recent successful exhibition Making Waves: The Art of Japanese Woodblock Print are currently installing the new Inspired by Flaxman exhibition and have hung the impressive Turner.

"There has been a flurry of activity, as all the amazing artworks have now been delivered and the team at York Art Gallery are completing the finishing touches to the exhibition."

The exhibition is included in general admission to York Art Gallery.

A series of creative workshops and gallery talks will be announced for the autumn and winter, and related retail items are already available in the gallery shop.
