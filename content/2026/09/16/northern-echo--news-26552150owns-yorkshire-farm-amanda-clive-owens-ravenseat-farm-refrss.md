Amanda Owen is so closely linked to Ravenseat that many viewers assume the Yorkshire Shepherdess owns it.

Millions discovered the remote Upper Swaledale farm through Our Yorkshire Farm, which followed Amanda, her former husband Clive and their nine children raising sheep and cattle.

But one fact about Ravenseat has shaped the family's plans.

Amanda and Clive do not own Ravenseat Farm. They are tenant farmers.

The couple have spoken openly about the arrangement and their wish to give their large family greater security.

  An overview of the Ravenseat Farm 

*(Image: CHANNEL 4/LIME PICTURES)*

Farmers Weekly described Amanda and Clive as first-generation tenant farmers and said Ravenseat covers about 2,000 acres of exposed upland near Keld.

Clive moved to Ravenseat in 1989, years before he met Amanda, and the family later built their lives there.

Amanda has said their tenant status was one reason she sought other income and wanted eventually to own a home.

That ambition led to the purchase of a nearby property.

## Who owns Ravenseat Farm?

Ravenseat belongs to a private estate, not Amanda or Clive.

Published reports name billionaire businessman Robert Miller, co-founder of Duty Free Shops, as the owner of the wider Ravenseat estate.

The Owens run the farm as tenants.

The arrangement lets the family live at Ravenseat and run the farm without owning the land.

Amanda remains closely tied to the address.

Companies House records list Ravenseat Farm as the correspondence address for Yorkshire Shepherdess Ltd, where Amanda remains director and the person with significant control.

### Could Amanda Owen ever have to leave Ravenseat?

As a tenant, Amanda does not have the same permanent control over Ravenseat as an owner would.

A farm tenant's rights depend on the tenancy's terms, which are not publicly available.

The Owens have long recognised that Ravenseat offers less security than owning their own home.

That helps explain the importance of Anty John's, the derelict farmhouse bought by Amanda and Clive in 2020.


Channel 4 said the neighbouring property was bought to help secure the family's future.

The centuries-old farmhouse has since become the focus of Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids.

Amanda, Clive and their children have spent years restoring it, with cameras capturing exterior work, plumbing and interior plans.

Channel 4 commissioned a third and fourth series of the programme in 2025.

### Why Anty John's matters to the Owen family

Ravenseat's ownership makes Anty John's more than a television renovation project.

It gives the family what Ravenseat cannot: a home they own.

Amanda has spoken of putting down roots and giving her children stability after years as a tenant farmer.

That matters even more as her children grow older and build careers of their own.

Despite the changes since Our Yorkshire Farm ended, Ravenseat remains at the heart of the family's working life.

Amanda still farms there, while Reuben's company lists Ravenseat Farm as its registered office.

For now, Ravenseat remains central to the Owen family's story.
