PUPILS feel safe, valued and ready to learn at a Bradford school.

Ofsted inspectors have praised The Academy at St James in Allerton for its inclusive ethos, strong behaviour and positive school culture following a recent inspection under the new Ofsted framework.

The academy, part of the Bradford Diocesan Academies Trust, was rated "good" in 2022 and has now been recognised for meeting the "Strong" standard in attendance and behaviour.

Inspectors identified that further work is needed to improve the number of pupils reaching national standards by the end of Year 6, noting that "some have gaps in their knowledge."

The report acknowledged that leaders had correctly identified achievement as an area for development.

Ruthi Thompson, headteacher at The Academy at St James, said: "We are delighted to share the outcome of our recent Ofsted inspection with our school community.

"The new Ofsted framework sets very high standards, and the inspection was rigorous and thorough.

"To securely meet the ‘Expected’ standards in all but one area, with Attendance and Behaviour recognised as a significant strength, is incredibly pleasing and provides assurance to parents that The Academy at St James is providing a high quality of education for their children.

"We are incredibly proud of our school and are thrilled that the report recognises and celebrates our strengths, success and our dedication to our students.

"We are especially proud that our school has been recognised as having an inclusive ethos and highlights the strong relationships that we have with our community."

Inspectors described the school environment as "warmly welcoming" and noted that pupils "enjoy coming to school because they know that they belong and that they are safe."

They also highlighted that "leaders and staff prioritise getting to know the children and their families."

The academy also met the "Expected" standard in areas including personal development and wellbeing, curriculum and teaching, inclusion, and leadership and governance.

Carol Dewhurst OBE, chief executive officer at Bradford Diocesan Academies Trust, said: "I am delighted that Ofsted has recognised the welcoming, inclusive and caring environment that makes The Academy at St James such a special place.

"The report reflects the commitment of our staff, the positive attitudes of our pupils and the strong relationships with families.

"We are proud of what has been achieved and could not ask for a better start to the new academic year."

The Academy at St James is part of a network of 16 primary schools and five secondary schools across the region under the Bradford Diocesan Academies Trust.
