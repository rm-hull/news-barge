The ‘father’ of an Ilkley club has celebrated his 99th birthday.

Desmond Longfellow, described as the "father" of the Rotary Club of Ilkley, celebrated the milestone during a recent lunch meeting with fellow Rotarians.

Peter Settle, the club secretary, said: "Desmond has led a full life starting out as a young man working in the former Ilkley Railway as an apprentice stoker.

"He went on to be a successful businessman running a number of businesses in Ilkley including an electrical shop, a pet shop and a motor trader."

Mr Longfellow joined the Rotary Club of Ilkley in 1967 and served as president twice, first in 1971 and again in 1998.

He remains a regular attendee at club meetings and has declined honorary membership, preferring to be considered an active member.

Mr Longfellow continues to live in Asquith, close to his daughter Helen and son-in-law Vernon.

His wife, Winifred, known as Winnie, died last year after they had been married for many years.
