A MAN has been arrested on suspicion of drink and drug driving offences after a collision in Helmsley, police said.

North Yorkshire Police said the incident happened at 12.55pm on Saturday, September 12, on the B1257 outside The Feversham Arms and involved a bronze Mitsubishi colliding with two parked cars.

  



A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "The driver of the Mitsubishi, a 41-year-old local man, was arrested following the collision on suspicion of drink and drug driving.

"He was taken to hospital by ambulance to have his injuries assessed."

The road was closed for a short time to allow for investigation by the police and recovery of the vehicle.

North Yorkshire Police are appealing for witnesses to the collision and any motorist with dashcam footage to contact the force.
