A TEENAGER has been taken to hospital following a reported assault.

Police were called to reports of an assault that took place on Skipton Road, Keighley, at around 3.20pm yesterday (15 September).

The victim, a 17-year-old man, was taken to hospital with non-life-threatening injuries.

An 18-year-old man was arrested and remains in custody.

Images of the scene posted on social media show a police cordon in place, as well as several attending officers.

## Full police statement

On Wednesday, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers were called to a report of an assault on Skipton Road, Keighley at around 3.20pm yesterday (15 September).

"A 17-year-old man was taken to hospital, his injuries are not thought to be life threatening.

"An 18-year-old man was arrested.

"Enquiries are ongoing."
