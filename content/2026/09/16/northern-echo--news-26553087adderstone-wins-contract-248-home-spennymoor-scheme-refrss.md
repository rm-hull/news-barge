A multi-million-pound infrastructure contract has been awarded in County Durham as a housebuilder is making its North East debut with a 248-home development.

Adderstone Civils has secured the contract from Strata to deliver roads, sewers and plotworks for the scheme in Spennymoor.

This marks Strata’s first project in the North East.

Jonathan Grant, managing director of Adderstone Civils, said: "This is a good example of what civil engineering can do when you think past roads and sewers.

"Getting the technical delivery spot on is a given – but doing it in a way that leaves something behind for the local area, and does right by the environment, is the standard we hold ourselves to.

"Securing this with Strata is a vote of confidence in what our team is capable of, and it's the kind of project that reflects where we’re taking the business – supporting local employment, keeping investment in the region and delivering to a standard that brings clients back for the next one."

The contract includes construction of adoptable roads, foul and surface water drainage, utilities and external works.

Work began in May 2026 and is expected to continue for more than three years.

Adderstone Civils is implementing a materials strategy designed to eliminate unnecessary material movements.

Instead of importing quarried aggregate and removing excavated material, the contractor is reprocessing around 22,000 cubic metres (48,000 tonnes) of the site’s own stone into a graded Type 1 sub-base.

This approach has avoided the need for virgin aggregate, prevented an estimated 2,000 HGV movements, and reduced embodied carbon by an estimated 480 tonnes of CO?e—roughly equivalent to taking 100 cars off the road for a year.

No material from the site has been sent to landfill.

The contract will also create 12 permanent jobs, including six apprenticeships.

Adderstone Civils said it is committed to sourcing 100 per cent of its supply chain within 25 miles of the site, including subcontractors, suppliers, plant hire and materials.

Martyn Strong, chief operating officer at Strata, said the scheme would deliver much-needed family homes and a "thriving new community" in Spennymoor.

He said: "Entering the North East for the first time is a major milestone for Strata, and this scheme will help deliver much-needed family homes and a thriving new community in Spennymoor.
