Thousands of residents will soon be able to secure their place at one of County Durham's most popular Bonfire Night events.

The annual event, Great Aycliffe Town Council’s Annual Fireworks Display, returns to Oak Leaf Sports Complex on Thursday, November 5, with spectacular fireworks, family fun and entertainment.

Great Aycliffe Town Council has confirmed that free wristbands will be available to collect from Tuesday, October 20, ahead of what is expected to be another hugely popular event.

While the display is free to attend, organisers have reminded residents that entry will only be permitted with a wristband, and numbers are limited.

Wristbands can be collected from Oak Leaf Sports Complex reception between 1pm and 7pm each day from October 20 until all have been distributed.

### What do I need to qualify for a wristband?

To qualify, residents must live within the parish of Great Aycliffe and provide proof of address, such as a driving licence or recent utility bill.

One person can collect wristbands on behalf of their family.

### When does the event start?

The event programme will begin at 5pm when the fairground opens, before a beacon lighting ceremony at 6.30pm.

As well as the main fireworks display, visitors can enjoy a children's fairground and a selection of food stalls throughout the evening.

The fireworks display is scheduled to begin at 6.45pm.

Organisers have also confirmed security measures will be in place on the night, including wristband checks, bag searches and random body scanning, and have advised visitors to allow plenty of time to enter the venue.

The annual display, which is sponsored by The Fish Tank, is regarded as one of the biggest community events in Great Aycliffe's calendar and regularly attracts large crowds from across the area.

Residents hoping to attend are being encouraged to collect their wristbands early to avoid disappointment.
