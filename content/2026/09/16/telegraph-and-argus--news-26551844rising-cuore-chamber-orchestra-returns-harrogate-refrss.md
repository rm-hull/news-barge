A RISING orchestra is set to perform its second season of music.

The Cuore Chamber Orchestra will return to Harrogate's Royal Hall on November 6 for the opening concert of its 2026/27 season, featuring works by Grieg and Mozart.

The performance follows a successful debut year, with concerts across three venues in Yorkshire.

William Dutton, founder and artistic director, said: “After such an exciting and successful first year, I am delighted to be back in the Royal Hall performing with the incredible musicians of Cuore.”

British violinist Jennifer Pike, the youngest ever winner of BBC Young Musician of the Year, will appear as soloist.

The programme will also include the Yorkshire premiere of Meditations for Strings by composer and broadcaster Michael Berkeley.

Mr Berkeley said: “I’m delighted that Cuore, who have done so much to promote orchestral music in Yorkshire and in particular the work of living or 21st century composers, will be playing Meditations.”
