# Two people arrested as police recover suspected stolen camping equipment in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for the public's help after recovering a trolley and a quantity of camping equipment believed to have been stolen from a vehicle in the Harrogate area.

The appeal follows the arrest of a 37-year-old man and a 35-year-old woman on Tuesday (September 15) as part of an investigation into theft from a motor vehicle, shop theft, handling stolen goods and other offences.

Both have since been released on conditional bail while enquiries continue.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “If you recognise any of the items pictured, or believe they may belong to you, please get in touch with us.”
