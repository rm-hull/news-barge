A "charming" period home has hit the market in York.

The four-bedroom end terrace townhouse is for sale in Heslington Road for £525,000.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said it offers "spacious living" in an "excellent location" with "easy access to York city centre".

Inside, a long reception hall leads to the main living spaces and stairs rising to the upper floors.

  Inside the four-bedroom townhouse for sale in Heslington Road, York, for £525,000 

*(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

A living room with large sash windows overlooking the courtyard is to the front of the home.

Next door is a dining room with French doors leading to the rear courtyard.

Upstairs, a double bedroom, smaller second bedroom and family bathroom are on the first floor.

Two more double bedrooms are on the top floor, along with under-eaves storage.
