Cowboy boots and country tunes are set to take over a city centre pub for a night of line dancing fun.

The Mayfair Pub and Kitchen, on Durham River Walk, will host a free Cowboy Night on September 25 from 6pm to 8pm, inviting guests to join in.

Promoted with a simple "Howdy Durham!” message on social media, the event has already sparked excitement among locals eager to try something different.

The post read: "Cowboy Night September 25th - beginner-friendly line dancing class.

"A bit of fun and free to enter! 6-8pm"

  The poster advertising the Cowboy event at the Mayfair Pub and Kitchen on September 25. 

*(Image: The Mayfair Durham on Facebook)*

Several people tagged friends, with one calling the event "right up your street."

Another person wrote: "I'm so up for this."

A third said: "We should go, we would love it."

The Mayfair has built a reputation for its themed nights, live entertainment, and community events, regularly drawing a mix of students, residents, and visitors looking for something out of the ordinary.


The resurgence of country music and western-themed events across the UK has contributed to the growing popularity of line dancing, which is praised for its simple routines.

The free line dancing class is aimed squarely at beginners, meaning no previous experience is needed.

With free entry, no experience required, and two hours of instruction, the event could prove a popular addition to Durham’s autumn social calendar.
