FIRE-ravaged Dalton Mills joins a list of nationally significant mills that are being allowed to deteriorate, according to The Victorian Society.

The complex in Keighley was hit by another fire at the weekend - the fifth in as many years - leaving many fearing for its future.

Northern Conservation Advisor Thomas Ollivier said: "Dalton Mills fits into a wider trend of mills that we are seeing across the country deteriorating."

  The current state of Dalton Mills in Keighley 

*(Image: Anna Dyson-Clarke/T&A Camera Club)*

He said the site had been disused for a long time, and unused buildings often attract urban exploring and anti-social behaviour.

The Grade II* listing makes Dalton Mills significant culturally and architecturally, he said.

"There are not many Grade II* buildings out there.

  The current state of Dalton Mills in Keighley 

*(Image: Anna Dyson-Clarke/T&A Camera Club)*

"For a mill, it's very impressive.

"It's got very rare features.

"For example, it's still got its chimney, which is quite a rare survivor."

He said fires were not necessarily the biggest issue, but inaction by local authorities was the concern.

  The current state of Dalton Mills in Keighley 

*(Image: Anna Dyson-Clarke/T&A Camera Club)*

Mr Ollivier said: "These are preventable incidents but they keep happening."

Mills can be converted into housing, hotels, gyms and offices, he said.

He said: "Mills are a bit of a blessing because of all the open space.

"It's a bit of a Catch-22, because it is a drain on fire service resources, but at the same time, it's such a significant building."

*(Image: Anna Dyson-Clarke/T&A Camera Club)*

Recent decades have presented a mixed picture in terms of the Bradford district's mills, with some being regenerated for residential or commercial use, and others being left to decay or being destoyed by fire.

Salts Mill, with its mix of art, dining and shopping, is one of the more notable success stories, while the redevelopment of nearby Victoria Mills into 449 apartments secured the future of another historic mill.

  The current state of Dalton Mills in Keighley 

*(Image: Anna Dyson-Clarke/T&A Camera Club)*

The regeneration of Lister Mills in Manningham, once the world's largest silk mill, saved another of Bradford's most famous buildings, while the transformation of Conditioning House at Forster Square won Best Residential Development Yorkshire 2018/19.

On the down side, January 28, 2016, saw more than 150 years of Bradford history go up in smoke when a huge fire destroyed Drummond Mill. Within a week the 10-acre Manningham site was rubble. Around 125 firefighters tackled the blaze and Lumb Lane was closed and residents were evacuated.

There has also been concern in recent years for the future of the Barkerend Mills - the site of a large fire in May 2025.
