A star-studded dance spectacular featuring four of Strictly Come Dancing's most popular professionals is heading to York as part of a new UK tour.

Here Come The Boys - featuring Aljaž Škorjanec, Gorka Marquez, Carlos Gu, and Julian Caillon -will arrive at the York Barbican on June 25, 2027, as part of a 24-date tour.

The show promises a high-energy evening of dance, music, and entertainment from some of TV’s best-loved dancers.

  Strictly Come Dancing stars Aljaž Škorjanec, Gorka Marquez, Carlos Gu and Julian Caillon will bring the new dance show Here Come The Boys to York Barbican on 25 June 2027 as part of a 24-date UK tour. 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Škorjanec said: "I genuinely think Here Come The Boys is going to be one of the must-see shows of 2027.

"We’re putting everything into making this our biggest, most exciting show yet, and I can’t wait for audiences to experience what we’ve got planned."

Marquez said: "It’s brilliant to be sharing the stage with three of my Strictly favourites.

"We all bring something different to the show, and that mix of personalities, styles and a little friendly competition is going to make it incredibly exciting—for us and for the audience."

The tour kicks off in Eastbourne on June 1 and finishes in Brighton on June 29, with a London date at the Adelphi Theatre on June 20.

Tickets go on general sale from September 18 via the official Here Come The Boys website.
