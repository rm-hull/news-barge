The pavements behind Harrogate’s Royal Pump Room have been given a deep clean by one of Harrogate BID's Street Rangers.

Sam Ashbyblew and swept away leaves that had gathered along the paths before using a jet washer to clean the area outside The Place.

Harrogate BID said the work had made a “real difference” and thanked Sam for helping to keep the town "looking its best."

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aaa6faf17bb4eded33.jpg)

The BID’s Street Rangers carry out planned and reactive work across the town centre.

Their tasks include deep cleaning busy areas, litter picking and maintaining street furniture, while also providing a welcoming presence for residents, visitors and businesses.

Most recently, they carried out work on Royal Parade and on Harrogate's One Arch.
