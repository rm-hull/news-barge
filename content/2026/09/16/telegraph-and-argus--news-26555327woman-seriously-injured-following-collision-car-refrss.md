A WOMAN was seriously injured following a collision with a car.

Emergency services attended Keighley Road in Halifax just after 8am this morning (Wednesday).

The victim, an 80-year-old pedestrian, was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

They are not believed to be life-threatening.

### Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At 8.08am today (16 September) we were called by the ambulance service to Keighley Road, Halifax to reports a vehicle was in collision with a pedestrian.

"The pedestrian, a woman in her 80s, has been taken to hospital with serious but not believed to be life threatening injuries."

Enquiries are ongoing.
