A main road in North Yorkshire has reopened following a serious crash.

The A171, near Helwath Beck between Whitby and Scarborough, was closed following a collision on Tuesday evening.

North Yorkshire Police last night warned drivers to avoid the area and follow diversions.

  **Recommended reads:**

The road reopened just before 2am on Wednesday.

A force spokesperson said: "An appeal will be issued later today."

Police are urging witnesses or anyone with dashcam footage to call 101, quoting reference NYP-15092026-0449.

Further updates will follow as more information becomes available.
