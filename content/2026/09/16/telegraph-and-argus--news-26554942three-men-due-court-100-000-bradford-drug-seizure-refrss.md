THREE men are due in court after police recovered heroin and crack cocaine with a street value worth more than £100,000 in Bradford.

Officers from Operation Phoenix carried out a joint operation in the Leeds Road alongside officers from Operation Sweetgill, which targets organised crime and drugs supply within the Holme Wood estate.

The operation targeted a drugs line operating in Bradford and travelling into North Yorkshire, South Yorkshire and the Humber.

The trio were arrested on Tuesday.

## Who has been charged?

Lucas Zerola, 18, of Maple Drive, Wetherby, has been charged with being concerned in the supply of heroin and crack cocaine between May 1 and September 15, 2026.

He also faces charges of possessing heroin and crack cocaine with intent to supply, plus possession of criminal property relating to cash and drugs seized on September 15.

Mohammed Ahmed, 22, of East Parade in Little Germany, Bradford, has been charged with possessing heroin and crack cocaine with intent to supply on September 15.

Lobib Shourav, 22, of St Johns Close, Leeds, faces the same charges relating to September 15.

He has also been charged with being concerned in the supply of cocaine and ketamine between February 13 and April 3, 2026.

All three were remanded to appear at Bradford Magistrates’ Court on Wednesday.
