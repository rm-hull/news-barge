A logistics and operations management company has strengthened its presence in the North East with the opening of a new regional office, supporting a growing portfolio of major energy, infrastructure and industrial projects.

ASCO has opened the new site at the Wilton Centre in Teesside, which will serve as a hub for its operations in central UK and the North East.

The company is already supporting major projects including Net Zero Teesside, Hornsea 3, and Dogger Bank offshore wind farms, as well as defence, marine, and industrial sectors.

Ryan Thompson, business development manager for central UK at ASCO, said: "Opening our Teesside office is an important step for ASCO and reflects the scale of activity we are already supporting across the region.

"Teesside is playing a major role in the UK’s changing energy and industrial landscape, from decarbonisation and offshore wind to defence, port, marine and complex logistics activity.

"By establishing a permanent presence at the Wilton Centre, we are placing our people and expertise closer to the customers, infrastructure and partners driving that growth."

ASCO delivers integrated services across quayside delivery, ships agency, storage, workforce and recruitment, logistics, materials management, fuels, lifting assurance and environmental services, including hazardous and non-hazardous waste management.

The company is the exclusive provider of quayside management services at Steel River Quay, forming an integral part of the Teesworks complex, supporting nearly 70 jobs.

Allan Scott, UK managing director at ASCO, said: "Teesside and the wider North East have a long industrial heritage, and we are seeing that evolve again as the region builds on its established strengths, takes on a bigger role in the UK’s energy transition and responds to growing opportunities in defence.
