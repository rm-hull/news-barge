A MYSTERY author has helped secure a £1,000 award for a Leeds bookshop.

Truman Books in Farsley received the donation from publisher HarperCollins as part of its 'The Bookshop I’d Like to Thank' scheme, which celebrates independent bookshops during the National Year of Reading.

Truman Books was nominated by a bestselling HarperCollins author who chose to remain anonymous, in recognition of the shop’s support in helping their work reach readers.

Amanda Truman, owner of Truman Books, said: "What an honour to be nominated.

"And a big thank you to the mystery author.

"We’ve built our reputation with publishers and authors over the past five years, showing them how we can promote their books and get audiences out for book events.

"The award is a generous boost that will help us continue to support the community, as they support us."

HarperCollins UK is distributing a total of £21,000 across 21 independent bookshops through the scheme, with each shop nominated by one of its authors.

The funding can be used to enhance services and attract more readers throughout the National Year of Reading.

Truman Books, located on Town Street, regularly hosts author talks, book launches, workshops, and community events in Farsley.
