LEGENDARY magician Penn Jillette is heading out on tour.

He will perform alongside Piff the Magic Dragon in a new UK show titled Piff & Pop’s Magic Shoppe.

The tour includes a stop at York’s Opera House on Saturday, September 19.

The show marks Jillette’s first time performing without his long-time stage partner Teller in more than 50 years.

It also sees Piff the Magic Dragon, also known as John van der Put, return to the UK stage for the first time in over a decade.

Penn Jillette said: "We’ve done our TV show, Penn & Teller Fool Us, for well over a decade and saw almost 400 magicians perform for us.

"Piff the Magic Dragon grabbed me right away – perfect magic, funny, and totally original.

"We became fast friends, and now we’ve decided to work together."

The show promises a mix of comedy, illusion, and on-stage chemistry, with solo performances in the first half and a joint act in the second.

Piff, who has performed more than 2,000 shows in Las Vegas, said: "After ten years and over 2,000 shows at the Flamingo Las Vegas, I’m coming home to where it all started, and I’m bringing my Pop (which is American for Dad)."
