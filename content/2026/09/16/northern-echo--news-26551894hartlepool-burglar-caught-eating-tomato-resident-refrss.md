A brazen burglar who broke into a house in broad-daylight was caught munching on a tomato by the homeowner.

Stephen Young climbed over a five-foot fence to enter the Hartlepool property.

Police identified the 45-year-old after he fled the scene, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Rachel Butt said Young, a third-strike burglar, targeted the home while the householder was outside speaking to her son and his girlfriend.

Doorbell footage captured Young scaling the fence and entering the house, she said.

“She has gone inside and asked him to leave but she couldn’t make out what he was saying as he was slurring his words,” she said.

“He was eating a tomato which he has picked up from the counter and walked out of the house.

“When he leaves the address, he seems to be staggering and under the influence.”

The woman said in a victim impact statement that the burglary left her shaken and she now locks the door every time she leaves, even to bring in the bins.

Young, of Inverness Road, Hartlepool, pleaded guilty to the burglary, which took place on May 12 this year.

His lawyer, Rod Hunt, said Young now appeared clean and sober after being remanded in custody following his arrest.

“He is fully aware of what he did and what your duty is today,” he said. “To risk a three-year sentence for the sake of a tomato may seem absurd but the gravity in this case is the impact on the unfortunate householder.”

Recorder Andrew Dallas jailed Young for 876 days after he pleaded guilty.

He said: “In the middle of the day, you climbed a five-foot fence and got into the house where the lady occupier was outside but came in when she was alerted you were in her house.

“You were disturbed in the act of eating one of her tomatoes. You were ordered out of her house and you left.

“This has considerably shaken up the victim.”

Young had previously been jailed for 18 months after admitting another Hartlepool burglary in April 2022, when he caused £1,000 of damage.
