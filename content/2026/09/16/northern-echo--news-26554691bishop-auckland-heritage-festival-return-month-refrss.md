Bishop Auckland’s popular heritage festival returns later this month with classic vehicles, local history and free talks from experts.

The fourth Bishop Auckland Heritage Festival takes place on Saturday, September 26, with free talks, exhibitions, displays and family activities across the town centre.

Hosted by Bishop Auckland Town Council, the festival will run from 10am to 3pm and will be based in Bishop Auckland Marketplace, the Artists Hub and the Spanish Gallery.

The Artists Hub will feature displays from local history and heritage groups, coordinated by the County Durham Forum for History and Heritage.

A series of free talks will also take place at the Spanish Gallery throughout the day.

Local historian Tom Hutchinson will present a talk called 100 Pictures.

John Phelan will share memories of "Four Visits to Wembley to Watch Crook Town AFC", while Anne Allen will discuss preparations for the 2027 European Year of the Normans through a project involving Durham Cathedral, Auckland Palace and Raby Castle.

The marketplace will also feature a display of classic vehicles organised by the Northern Bygone Society.

Visitors will have the chance to get a closer look at a range of vintage cars and speak to their owners about the stories behind them.

The free event aims to showcase the people, places and stories that have helped shape Bishop Auckland and the wider County Durham area.

Sarah Harris, town clerk at Bishop Auckland Town Council, said the festival had become an important date in the town's calendar.

She said: "The Bishop Auckland Heritage Fayre is a wonderful opportunity to celebrate the stories, people and places that have helped shape our town and our wider County Durham community.

"Each year, the fayre brings together local heritage organisations, historians and members of the public, creating a day where everyone can discover something new and share in our collective history.

"We are delighted to bring the event back for another year and look forward to welcoming the community to enjoy everything it has to offer."

The Bishop Auckland Heritage Festival takes place on Saturday, September 26, from 10am to 3pm and is free to attend.
