## Poetry festival returns to Bishop Burton for eighth year

The High Wolds Poetry Festival will take place at Bishop Burton Village Hall *(Image: Supplied)>*

A POETRY festival is set to return with a packed day of readings, reflection and creativity.

The High Wolds Poetry Festival will take place at Bishop Burton Village Hall on Saturday, September 26, between 10am and 5pm.

Now in its eighth year, the event is open to poets of all abilities and is free to attend.

Sarah Mills, director of the High Wolds Poetry Festival, said: "The High Wolds Poetry Festival has gone from strength to strength in its eight years and I can’t wait to see our poets take to the stage and perform.

"It’s a delight to provide a space for poets of all abilities to engage with poetry in rural settings which makes this event unique in the area.

"It’s a wonderfully friendly day and I look forward to welcoming new faces and our long-standing supporters.

"See you in September."

Supported by East Riding of Yorkshire Council and Arts Council England, the festival will feature readings by both established and emerging poets, including those performing for the first time.

This year’s theme is Weaving Words, Harnessing History, inspired by the Bayeux Tapestry’s connection to Stamford Bridge and its arrival in the UK.

A poetry collection featuring work from many of the performers will be available on the day for a small donation.

Councillor Nick Coultish, cabinet member for culture, leisure, and tourism at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "This is a fantastic event to bring poetry into the heart of the Yorkshire Wolds.

"We highly encourage interested residents to find out more and get involved if they can."
