A road safety crackdown led to dozens of traffic offence reports, vehicle seizures, and prohibitions in North Yorkshire.

The joint operation in Eggborough on Tuesday, September 15, involved North Yorkshire Police’s Roads Policing Group and Road Safety Team, alongside the Motor Insurers’ Bureau, Humberside Police, and the DVSA.

It targeted uninsured, unroadworthy, and dangerously driven vehicles.

Officers issued 25 traffic offence reports for issues including tyres, trailers, documentation, and vehicle condition.

They also handed out six immediate prohibitions, three delayed prohibitions, and seized four vehicles - two for being untaxed and two for lacking insurance.

Inspector Rob Campbell, of Selby’s Neighbourhood Policing Team, said: "Road safety is consistently one of the biggest concerns raised by our communities, and today’s operation shows we are listening and acting on that.

"Working alongside our partner agencies allows us to target dangerous and illegal vehicle use effectively, and the results speak for themselves."
