## 'Local gem' York bistro named one of UK’s best informal fine-dining restaurants

Flashback to Martin Gore and Chris Pragnell in 2024 celebrating Cafe No 8's 20th anniversary. *(Image: Haydn Lewis)*

A CITY bistro has earned a spot in the Good Food Guide after impressing diners and inspectors alike.

Café No 8 Bistro on Gillygate, York, has been recognised as one of the best informal fine-dining restaurants in the city.

Co-owners Chris Pragnell and Martin Gore have run the bistro since it opened in 2002 with both still playing to their strengths today.

  Flat iron steak and boulangère potatoes at Cafe No.8 Bistro in York. 

*(Image: Picture: Chris Pragnell)*

Chris shapes the culinary vision in the kitchen, while Martin leads the front of house.

Chris said: "Over the last year, we have been working hard to evolve our evening and Sunday Lunch menus without compromising on quality.

"This includes curating menus where dishes can be easily adapted to cater for a wider range of dietary needs, whether this be gluten-free or vegan dishes.

"Our gluten-free Sunday Roasts, for example, are fast becoming one of our most popular options. Both Martin and I are incredibly pleased that our dish adaptations are being noticed by guides such as The Good Food Guide, and we are delighted to have been added."

  **Recommended reads:**

The Good Food Guide described Café No 8 as a "local gem," praising its careful, seasonal cooking and highlighting the "front-row people-watching from the table in the bow window".

  One of the mains at Cafe No.8 Bisto in York: A flamed grilled lamb rump, potato and celeriac gratin, fondant turnip, celeriac puree, fresh garden peas and lamb jus. 

*(Image: Picture: Ian Pragnell)*

It also said that it is a “great choice for a delicious reboot after a day’s tourism around the city”.

The guide publishes reviews based on readers' feedback and anonymous expert inspections.

Café No 8 employs a small team of eight, including Chris's son, Ian, who works alongside him in the kitchen as a chef.

  Flashback to Martin Gore and Chris Pragnell in 2024 celebrating Cafe No 8's 20th anniversary. 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The bistro has also recently launched an art exhibition with contemporary painter Lucie Wake, whose Yorkshire landscapes are currently on display throughout the restaurant.

  For starters at Cafe No.8 Bistro, a pea panna cotta with flame grilled asparagus, sou vide egg yolk, lemon hazelnut crumb. 

*(Image: Picture by Ian Pragnell.)*

Martin said: "The window table is one of our most requested tables in the bistro, both Chris and I laughed when we read about it in the guide.

"I am aware that The Good Food Guide has a long-standing reputation for showcasing the best restaurants, pubs and cafés across the UK, so I still can't quite believe we are now part of that, especially as we are only a small, independent restaurant.

"A big thank you to everyone who supports our bistro, we couldn't have secured this accolade without you."

Café No 8 Bistro is open Wednesday to Saturday from 5pm to 10pm and on Sunday from 12noon to 4pm.

The Good Food Guide is the latest platform to recognise Café No.8’s culinary excellence, having been acknowledged in recent months by SquareMeal, Olive and Harden’s.
