Chris Rea's enduring connection to the North East will be celebrated when a tribute show inspired by the singer-songwriter's remarkable career returns brings his music back to the area where it all began.

The Chris Rea Experience – Rea-magined will be performed at the Arc in Stockton on Friday, October 23, at 8pm.

Born in Middlesbrough, Rea started his career on Teesside and went on to become one of Britain’s most successful singer-songwriters.

Paul Casey, who fronts the show and knew Rea personally, said: "We were close by in Sunderland at the Fire Station in November, and if you follow the A19 further up, it takes you exactly to where it all began – Middlesbrough.

"It will be hugely emotional."

The tribute is more than a standard musical performance.

Casey said: "The bits in between the songs reflect my long association with Chris and what it was like working on the road and in the studio with him.

"It is a totally unique and special link, and Chris is stamped right through the show.

"It isn’t just about playing the songs.

"It is about the memories, the stories and the experience of being part of that incredible musical journey."

His connection with Rea began when he was just 16.

He said: "Chris was the reason I got into all of this as a boy of just 16.

"He told me that he saw so much of himself in me, and that will stay with me forever."

The show also features Eoghan O’Neill, who played with Mr Rea from Shamrock Diaries through to The Road to Hell.

This personal touch, Casey says, is what sets the show apart from other tributes.

He said: "Nobody could ever replace Chris – I do this to honour my friend and hero.

"It is about celebrating his music, his story and the incredible legacy he left behind, and to say a huge thank you for everything he had done for me.

"And because so much of that story began right in the very heart of Teesside, it makes this show incredibly special."

Rea’s early days were shaped by the Teesside music scene and figures like agent John McCoy, who managed him during his formative years.
