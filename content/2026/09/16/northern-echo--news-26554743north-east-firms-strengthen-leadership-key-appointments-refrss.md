Senior appointments across the North East show businesses strengthening their leadership teams as they pursue growth, with hires spanning infrastructure, private equity and commercial property finance to support investment and regional growth.

Louise Thompson has joined Commsworld as commercial director, taking on a newly created role at the managed network and fibre provider for Northumberland County Council, supporting plans to expand across the UK and improve services for public and private-sector customers; she brings nearly 30 years’ commercial leadership experience in telecommunications, IT and healthcare and will work with the board on growth, partnerships and new business models as digital infrastructure becomes increasingly important to the region’s economic growth and connectivity.

Maven Capital Partners has strengthened its North East team by appointing Catherine Earl and Graham Palmer as portfolio managers, helping it support ambitious businesses across the region while managing funding programmes including the British Business Bank’s Northern Powerhouse Investment Fund II and Business Durham’s Finance Durham Fund.

Catherine has experience in mergers and acquisitions, finance transformation and business expansion, while Graham has worked in corporate finance and private equity-backed growth, including Indigo Software’s sale to Aptean, and their expertise will help portfolio companies scale, innovate and build long-term value as experienced advisers and investment expertise remain vital to North East firms pursuing growth.

Aldermore has strengthened its commercial real estate team by appointing James Evans as business development manager, who is based in the North and will help property developers and investors secure funding for new commercial and residential projects, drawing on experience at Nexa Finance, StreamBank and Assetz Capital that reflects demand for specialist finance expertise in the region’s development market.
