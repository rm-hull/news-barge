A 62-year-old man has been jailed for over 11 years for repeatedly sexually assaulting a much younger woman who had been left “psychologically damaged”.

Malcom Peirson Dale, from Pickering, appeared for sentence at York Crown Court today (September 16).

He had been convicted of seven separate offences against the victim – including six counts of sexual assault and one count of inciting her to engage in sexual activity – following a trial in July.

Prosecutor Anne Richardson said that the victim, who can’t be named for legal reasons, was sexually assaulted at a property in North Yorkshire in 2022 when Dale was a 58-year-old married man.

In the first assault, all on the same evening, Dale touched the young woman on an intimate area which was followed by a penetrative sexual assault.

In a separate incident on the same night, he touched her on an intimate part of her body and then subjected her to two penetrative sexual assaults. He then placed her hand on an intimate part of his body.

Defence barrister Zarreen Alam-Cheetham said that Dale had never been in trouble before and was previously a man “of good character”.

Judge Simon Hickey told Dale: “You assaulted (the victim) in the most intimate and penetrative way.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/york-crown-court-summer-2024-2.webp)

He said that Dale’s offences had had a “significant and lasting effect” on the victim.

“Psychologically, this woman has been damaged,” he added. “She struggles to trust men. It’s affected her family.”

Dale, of Rosedale Abbey, was jailed for 11-and-a-half years and placed on the sex-offenders’ register for life.

## ‘Saw through his lies’

Following sentencing, investigating officer, DC Liberty Ashton, of Scarborough CID said: “Dale showed no remorse for his offending, denying it throughout and subjecting the victim to a trial.

“However the jury saw through his lies and he is now facing the consequences.

“I hope the outcome of this case provides the victim with some semblance of closure and that she can now start to move on with her life.

“If you are a victim of sexual abuse, please do not hesitate to come forward and get the support you need.

“If you do not feel able to report it to the police you can contact our Sexual Assault Referral Centre who provide free, confidential healthcare and compassionate support to anyone in York and North Yorkshire that has experienced sexual assault or rape no matter when it happened.”
