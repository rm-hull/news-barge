Drivers in Darlington face five roadwork restrictions, including closures and traffic controls, on September 16.

Here are the five roads to watch out for delays on Wednesday:

### Kendal Close

Kendal Close will be closed in both directions between A167 Grange Road and Blackwell Lane for water main work.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 16 at 11pm.


### Stanhope Road South

Stanhope Road South will be closed in both directions from Duke Street to Coniscliffe Road.

Closure is required for water main work, and the restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 16 at 11pm.

### Auckland Oval

Construction will affect Auckland Oval in both directions at A68 West Auckland Road.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 22 at 11pm.

### Auckland Avenue

One lane traffic will operate on Auckland Avenue in both directions between Brinkburn Road and Auckland Oval.

The traffic control is due to construction, and the restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 22 at 11pm.

### Winchester Way

One lane traffic will be in place on Winchester Way in both directions at Rochester Way.

Restrictions are due to electricity work and are scheduled to remain in place until October 15 at 11pm.
