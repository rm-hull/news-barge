Pokémon fans have marked three decades of adventure with their favourite characters and cards.

The Metrocentre became a magnet for collectors, families and long-time fans celebrating Pokémon’s 30th anniversary today.

Ahead of the celebrations, the Gateshead shopping centre revealed that several of its stores would be stocking the special addition cards released for the anniversary.


Taking to social media, they said: "Get Pokémon 30th Anniversary ready at Metrocentre!

"Whether you’re hunting for trading cards, collectibles or the latest Pokémon must haves, get ready to celebrate 30 years of Pokémon with our retailers!"

Today (September 16), shoppers flocked to the centre to snap up trading cards, toys, clothing, and collectibles featuring beloved characters like Pikachu, Eevee, and Charizard.

Demand for Pokémon merchandise has surged, with huge queues forming at the Metrocentre for a new trading card shop in July.

Aqua TCG opened at The Crescent, Upper Green Mall, offering a wide selection of cards, graded collectables, sealed products and trading card accessories.

  Pokémon fans can pick up collectible merchandise across Metrocentre, including the LEGO Pikachu and Poké Ball building set available from the LEGO Store. 

*(Image: Metrocentre)*

The 30th anniversary marks three decades since Pokémon first captured imaginations around the world, growing from a video game phenomenon into a global cultural icon.

Metrocentre retailers joined the celebrations with special 30th Anniversary Trading Card Game releases and themed products.

The event attracted seasoned collectors and new fans, with many visitors calling it a nostalgic trip back to childhood.

   

*(Image: AQUA TCG)*

Parents and children could be seen browsing the aisles together, sharing stories of their favourite Pokémon and searching for the latest must-have items.

For some, the day was about reliving fond memories, while others were eager to add to their growing collections.

The celebrations served as a reminder of Pokémon’s enduring appeal and its ability to bring generations together through a shared love of adventure and imagination.
