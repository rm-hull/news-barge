North East-born actor Charlie Hunnam returns to Netflix’s hit Monster anthology as The Lizzie Borden Story airs this week.

The 46-year-old, born in Newcastle upon Tyne, joined the franchise by playing notorious serial killer Ed Gein in its third season.

The actor earned widespread acclaim for the role, which followed previous instalments centred on Jeffrey Dahmer and brothers Lyle and Erik Menendez.

Now, the former Byker Grove star is back for season four, but this time he swaps the lead role for that of Andrew Borden, the father of accused axe murderer Lizzie Borden.

The eight-part series revisits one of America’s most infamous murder mysteries. Lizzie Borden stood trial in 1892 accused of murdering her father and stepmother with an axe, but was acquitted.

The case remains one of the most notorious in US history.

Ella Beatty stars as Lizzie alongside Vicky Krieps as Bridget Sullivan, Rebecca Hall as Abby Borden, Billie Lourd as Emma Borden and Jessica Barden as Nance O’Neil.

Lead actress Ella Beatty has called the latest instalment the franchise’s “first female Monster”, exploring female rage and repression through the notorious Borden murders.

Hunnam plays family patriarch Andrew Borden.

  Charlie Hunnam Portraying Ed Gein in the last season of 'Monsters'


Andrew was brutally killed in his home, along with his wife, in a brutal attack with a hatchet.

His daughter was accused of the killings, but she was tried and acquitted of the August 4, 1892, axe murders.

Fans have begun debating Hunnam’s performance, particularly his accent, after trailers and early clips emerged.

Reports suggest the Geordie actor deliberately avoided using a strictly historically accurate New England accent, opting instead for an approach that felt more accessible to a modern audience.

The Rebel Moon actor uses a lisp and stutter for the character.

Hunnam said: “That stutter and that voice just started to present themselves to me," Lad Bible reports.

### Charlie's North East connection

Hunnam remains one of the North East’s biggest acting stars.

Born in Newcastle in 1980, he was famously discovered while shopping in a JD Sports store before landing an early role in the beloved Tyneside teen drama Byker Grove.
