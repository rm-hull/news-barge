FIREFIGHTERS from two counties have been tackling a blaze close to a former power station.

  



The county's fire and rescue service said three crews, including one from Pontefract in West Yorkshire, were called to the scene of a fire in Eggborough, near Selby close to the former power station at 7.44pm yesterday.

A water bowser has also been deployed to the fire, said to involve a large stack of hay bales.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service issued a statement this morning about the incident.
