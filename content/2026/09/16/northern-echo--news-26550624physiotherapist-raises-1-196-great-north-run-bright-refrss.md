A North East physiotherapist has raised more than £1,100 for charity by running the Great North Run in memory of her grandfather, helping patients and staff across the region.

Francesca Moulton raised £1,196 for Bright Northumbria Healthcare Charity by completing the half marathon for Northumbria Healthcare NHS Foundation Trust.

She was one of more than 80 runners representing the trust’s official charity.

Ms Moulton said: "Over the past year, I've had the privilege of working as a physiotherapist at Northumbria Healthcare NHS Foundation Trust, and during that time I've seen first-hand the extraordinary care the trust provides to patients, their families and support networks.

  Francesca Moulton 

*(Image: Supplied)*

"I feel incredibly honoured to be part of such a compassionate and dedicated team, all working together to make a real difference to people's lives.

"Bright plays a vital role in improving facilities, equipment, patient support and overall care.

"Donations help fund things the NHS simply cannot, allowing the trust to continually develop, improve and go above and beyond for those who need it most."

She dedicated the run to her grandfather, who died more than two years ago, and remembered the care he received in hospital during his final months.

Ms Moulton said: "It made me truly appreciate how vital compassionate care and a strong sense of community are, not just for patients, but for their families too.

"Seeing that support first-hand reinforced just how important it is to give back to the organisations that give so much to us."

Bright Northumbria Healthcare Charity funds projects, equipment, facilities and initiatives that improve care for patients, families and staff across the trust.

Rob Graham, head of charity at Bright, said: "We're incredibly grateful to Francesca for taking on the Great North Run in support of Bright.

"As a member of Team Northumbria, she sees every day the difference charitable funding can make to patients, families and staff.

"To then go out and complete one of the world's most iconic running events while raising such a fantastic amount is an incredible achievement.

"Francesca's story is both inspiring and heartfelt, and we're delighted that she has raised more than £1,100 to help us continue making healthcare experiences brighter across our communities."
