Nine firefighters from North Yorkshire climbed 110 floors in full fire kit in memory of their fallen colleagues.

The team from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service took part in the Terry Farrell Firefighters Fund Memorial Stair Climb in Atlanta, Georgia, in the United States, raising almost £2,000 through their efforts.

  The USA flag was raised between two fire engines during the Atlanta memorial stair climb in honour of 9/11 firefighters. 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service)*

The challenge honoured the 343 FDNY firefighters who lost their lives during the terrorist attacks on September 11, 2001.

  Nine firefighters from North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue were among more than 1,200 firefighters to complete the 110-floor memorial stair climb in Atlanta honour of 9/11 firefighters. 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service)*

Station manager Mike Sutcliffe said: "This Stair Climb event was about far more than a physical challenge.

"It was an opportunity to remember the courage and sacrifice of the 343 firefighters who died on 9/11 and to stand shoulder-to-shoulder with our American colleagues in their honour.

"The support shown to our team was overwhelming, and we were proud to represent North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service. The event reminded us that, wherever we serve, the firefighting community is one family."

  The team from North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue outside the Atlanta Brave stadium where the 9/11 memorial climb took place. 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service)*

Each firefighter carried a photograph of a firefighter who went to work on September 11, 2001 and never returned home.

They climbed the equivalent of the World Trade Center towers in temperatures above 38°C.

They joined more than 1,200 firefighters from around the world, including 243 from the UK, in the international event held at the Atlanta Braves baseball stadium.

  Each firefighter carried a photo of one of their colleagues who died on September 11, 2001. 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service)*

Chief fire officer Jonathan Dyson said: "Taking part in the Memorial Stair Climb was both a physical and emotional challenge for our firefighters.

"As well as honouring the memory of the 343 FDNY firefighters who lost their lives on 11 September 2001, the event is a reminder of the courage, selflessness and dedication that firefighters demonstrate every day in service to their communities.

"We are hugely proud of the team for representing North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service with such professionalism and humility. Their achievement reflects the very best of our Service and has also helped raise vital funds for charities that continue to support firefighters and their families."

  The nine firefighters from North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue who honoured their fallen colleagues by taking on the Atlanta memorial stair climb. 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service)*

The nine North Yorkshire firefighters who completed the climb were Station Manager Mike Sutcliffe, Watch Manager Daz Smith, Crew Manager Steve Simpson, Firefighters Ash Davis, Toni Wooff Pepe, Josh Beagle, Dom Wood, Rik Duckworth and Tim Weller.

Funds raised will support The Fire Fighters Charity, the Terry Farrell Firefighters Fund, and the Blue Light Card Foundation.

Donations are still being accepted through the team’s JustGiving page: [https://www.justgiving.com/page/northyorksfireclimb?utm_medium=FR&utm_source=CL](https://www.justgiving.com/page/northyorksfireclimb?utm_medium=FR&utm_source=CL)
