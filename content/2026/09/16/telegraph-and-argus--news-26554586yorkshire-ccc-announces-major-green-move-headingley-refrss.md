YORKSHIRE County Cricket Club has signed a new partnership with clean energy tech firm UrbanChain.

The agreement will see Headingley powered by 100 per cent renewable energy from January 2027, supporting the club’s ten-year "Chasing Glory" strategy and long-term vision for Headingley.

Daniel Matson, commercial director at Yorkshire Cricket, said: "This new partnership reflects our desire to build a club with a sustainable future and demonstrates our commitment to being a responsible, forward-thinking organisation making a positive impact.

"Partnering with UrbanChain means the energy powering Headingley is renewable, and we’ll have visibility over where it’s come from.

"Over time we’ll be drawing on more energy generated right here as Yorkshire is powered by Yorkshire."

UrbanChain, founded in 2017, operates a private energy market that directly connects renewable energy generators with buyers, offering greater transparency and control over energy sourcing.

The company aims to supply more energy generated within Yorkshire as part of the deal.

Charlie Parry, chief growth officer at UrbanChain, said: "Yorkshire County Cricket Club is more than a cricket club.

"It’s one of Yorkshire’s most recognisable institutions.

"That’s why this partnership matters.

"We’re not just supporting the club, we’re powering it."
