Emergency services were called to a crash in a North Yorkshire village last night (Tuesday).

It was in **Norwood**, near Harrogate, at** 7.35pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Two crews from Harrogate responded to a report of a single vehicle road traffic collision where the vehicle had left the road and rolled.

#### Map location

“All occupants were out of the vehicles on arrival of the crews.

“Crews carried out scene safety and left the incident in the hands of ambulance and police crews.”
