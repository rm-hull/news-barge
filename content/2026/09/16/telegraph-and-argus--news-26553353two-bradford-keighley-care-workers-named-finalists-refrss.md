TWO care workers have been named finalists in the Yorkshire and Humber region of the Great British Care Awards.

Raymond "Ray" Morrisroe, from Longfield House in Clayton, is a finalist for the Care Home Worker Award, while Karen Jones, from Cygnet Hawkstone in Keighley, has been shortlisted for the Putting People First Award.

Mr Morrisroe said: "I feel very proud that my work has been recognised and acknowledged.

"I feel very overwhelmed and surprised that I have been nominated for just doing my job."

He has worked in social care for 15 years and was recognised for his dedication to delivering high-quality, person-centred support.

Longfield House is a specialist residential service in Clayton supporting adults with learning disabilities and complex needs.

The award celebrates care workers who treat individuals with dignity and respect, promote independence and consistently go above and beyond in their roles.

Ms Jones was shortlisted for her commitment to empowering the people she supports and helping them gain greater choice and control over their lives.

Hawkstone, on Shann Lane in Keighley, is a specialist residential service for adults with learning disabilities.

The Yorkshire and Humber regional awards ceremony will take place at the Royal Armouries in Leeds on Friday, November 6, 2026.
