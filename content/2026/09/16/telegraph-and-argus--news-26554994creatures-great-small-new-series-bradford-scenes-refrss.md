NEW pictures have been released by a television company showing how Bradford's City Hall provided a atmospheric backdrop for the latest series of All Creatures Great and Small.

As reported previously in the T&A, filming for eagerly-anticipated show took place in Bradford city centre in March, with streets, including Centenary Square, rolled back in time to the 1950s.

Actors and actresses in period costumes could be seen walking outside City Hall in Centenary Square.

Vans containing props were also just behind the cameras.

  Nicholas Ralph (James Herriot). 

*(Image: ©5 Broadcasting Limited /)*

## When will it air?

Now, with the series due to begin at 9pm on Thursday, September 17, Channel 5 has released a series of intriguing still shots - and Bradford is very much in the spotlight.

  An atmospheric scene outside Bradford City Hall 

*(Image: ©5 Broadcasting Limited /)*

Some of the images show Nicholas Ralph, playing the part of James Herriot, striding along the pavement outside City Hall.

Others show scenes of the seventh series shot inside the magnificent Bradford building.

  Nicolas Ralph (James Herriot) 

*(Image: ©5 Broadcasting Limited /)*

## What to expect in the new series

In 'Back to School', we're taken back to the beautiful Yorkshire Dales in 1946.

The war is finally over, and the characters we've come to know and love are reunited under one roof and looking ahead to a hopeful new chapter.

"James is excited about new developments in veterinary medicine, but a new vet in the area threatens the status quo," says 5.

  Nicholas Ralph (James Herriot) 

*(Image: ©5 Broadcasting Limited /)*

"The world at large may be changing, but inside our slice of Yorkshire, the Dales always offers the same perfect escapism.

"Packed with warmth, humour and community spirit, the series brings more heart-warming tales of people and animals alike.

  Imogen Clawson (Jenny Alderson) Rachel Shenton (Helen Herriot) Nicholas Ralph (James Herriot) 

*(Image: ©5 Broadcasting Limited /)*

"In the first episode, James impresses as a mature vet, and Helen considers plans to invest in the future of the farm - can she get Richard on board?

"When a new vet arrives in Darrowby, Siegfried is suspicious of his tactics, Tristan’s impressed with his style, and James begins to question himself.

"James, Helen and the kids visit the local sweet shop where confectioner Geoff Hatfield is concerned about his cat Alfred. James becomes increasingly stumped as to what’s wrong.

"Dorothy seems to be having a positive effect on Siegfried, and Mrs Hall enjoys having her friend around. Jimmy’s excited to start school but has to adjust to the changes that it brings."
