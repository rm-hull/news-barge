A Post Office branch in a North East town is set to reopen just days after its closure, with plans now in place to restore services under a new operator.

The Post Office in Redcar has confirmed that following the closure of the former branch on Redcar High Street on September 5, work is underway to revive the service from the same town centre location.

The new branch is expected to offer extended opening hours, including Sunday availability, in a move likely to be welcomed by residents who feared Redcar would be left without a full-service Post Office.

An update shared by the Post Office on Wednesday, September 16, said: “Following the closure of Redcar Post Office (TG Jones), plans are underway to reopen the branch at the same High Street location under a new operator.

“The reinstated service will feature extended opening hours, including Sunday availability.

“Follow our page for further updates.”

Full details of the new branch have not yet been revealed, although the Post Office said it would continue to provide updates as plans progress.

The announcement comes just weeks after the closure of the previous branch sparked concern among residents and staff.

The former Post Office, based inside the TG Jones store on High Street, closed permanently on Saturday, September 5, after the retailer announced the closure of its Redcar branch.

At the time, staff confirmed all Post Office employees were facing redundancy as a result of the store's closure.

Residents shared their disappointment at the loss of the service, with some raising concerns about the impact on the town centre.

One resident wrote on social media: “There's no wonder people complain about our dying town, no banks, empty shops and now no post office.”

Another added: “It's awful for the staff. Some have been there for decades and are now out of a job. They deserve better.”

The closure has left Redcar without its main Post Office branch.

While a Post Office counter continues to operate from the Banking Hub at Redcar Civic Centre, it only offers cash transactions and does not provide postal services or parcel facilities.

The opening date for the reinstated branch has yet to be announced.

Residents are being urged to keep an eye on further updates on their Facebook page.

The closure forms part of a wider TG Jones restructuring plan approved by the High Court in July, which will see 150 of the retailer's roughly 450 stores shut.
