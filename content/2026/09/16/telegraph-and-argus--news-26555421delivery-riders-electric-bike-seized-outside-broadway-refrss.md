A DELIVERY rider had an electric bike seized after checks in Bradford city centre found it failed to meet requirements.

Motorcycle officers from Operation Steerside were patrolling when they spotted the rider outside The Broadway shopping centre.

Checks found the vehicle did not meet requirements for an Electrically Assisted Pedal Cycle, or EAPC.

West Yorkshire Police released the below video of officers seizing the bike.


It was treated as a motor vehicle and subject to the same requirements as vehicles on the road.

The electric bike was seized and the rider was reported for driving otherwise than in accordance with a licence and using a motor vehicle without insurance.

## What did police say?

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Motorcycle officers from Operation Steerside were on proactive patrols in Bradford city centre when they spotted a delivery rider using an electrically powered bike outside The Broadway.

"Following checks, officers established the vehicle did not comply with the requirements for an Electrically Assisted Pedal Cycle (EAPC).

"As a result, it was treated in law as a motor vehicle and subject to the same legal requirements as other motor vehicles used on the road.

"The rider was reported for offences of driving otherwise than in accordance with a licence and using a motor vehicle without insurance.

"The vehicle was seized under Section 165 of the Road Traffic Act."
