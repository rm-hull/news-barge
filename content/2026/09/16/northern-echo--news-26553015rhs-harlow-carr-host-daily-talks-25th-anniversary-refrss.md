Gardeners are being offered a rare chance to hear directly from the experts behind one of the North of England's most beautiful gardens as part of a special anniversary celebration.

RHS Garden Harlow Carr is marking 25 years under RHS stewardship with a series of daily talks from September 21 to October 4.

The Gardener Talks will take place every day at 3pm and will be led by members of the RHS Harlow Carr team, offering a behind-the-scenes look at the creation and care of the over 60-acre garden.

  RHS Garden Harlow Carr is celebrating 25 years under RHS stewardship with a series of expert-led gardening talks, giving visitors a rare insight into one of the North of England’s most celebrated gardens 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Liz Thwaite, head of site at RHS Harlow Carr, said: "As we celebrate our 25th anniversary, we're delighted to offer visitors the chance to hear directly from the people who care for the garden every day.

"These talks will provide a fascinating glimpse behind the scenes, while sharing practical horticultural advice and seasonal inspiration that gardeners of all levels can enjoy."

Each informal 25-minute talk will cover a different aspect of the garden, from plant collections and seasonal highlights to long-term planning and daily maintenance.

  RHS Garden Harlow Carr is celebrating 25 years under RHS stewardship with a series of expert-led gardening talks, giving visitors a rare insight into one of the North of England’s most celebrated gardens 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Visitors can expect practical gardening advice, inspiration, and insights into the challenges and rewards of caring for over 60 acres of stunning garden.

The talks are part of a wider programme of events celebrating the anniversary, marking a quarter of a century since the RHS took over stewardship of the much-loved Yorkshire garden in 2001.

"Whether you're a keen gardener, a regular visitor or discovering RHS Harlow Carr for the first time, the Gardener Talks promise expert insights, inspiring stories and a deeper appreciation of the people and plants that make the garden such a special place."

Admission to the talks is included with garden entry, which is free for RHS members.
