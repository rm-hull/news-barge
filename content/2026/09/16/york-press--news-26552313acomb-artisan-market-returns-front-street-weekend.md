ARTISAN markets are returning to Acomb, bringing together community spirit and handmade creations.

The Acomb Artisan Market will be held on Front Street on Saturday, September 19, from 10am to 3pm.

Organised by Little Bird Made, the event will feature a curated selection of Yorkshire makers offering seasonal baked goods, handcrafted homeware, clothing, artwork, and jewellery.

Jackie Crozier, managing director and founder of Little Bird Made, said: "The Autumn season brings a shift in what people look for, from cosy homeware to rich seasonal flavours.

"Shopping locally keeps our high streets vibrant and sustains small-batch artisans who pour their heart and soul into everything they make.

"We’re excited to be bringing another lively market to Acomb’s Front Street this September, we can’t wait to see you all."

Visitors will have the chance to meet the makers and support small businesses directly.

Emma Proudly-Midgley, owner of Golden Triangle Scents and Soak, said: "Acomb Artisan Market is one of my favourite places to trade, the stalls are wonderfully diverse, and the high street has an unmistakable buzz.

"We are proudly rooted in the local community, with every product handcrafted in my home studio just fifteen minutes away."

For more information, visit littlebirdmade.com.
