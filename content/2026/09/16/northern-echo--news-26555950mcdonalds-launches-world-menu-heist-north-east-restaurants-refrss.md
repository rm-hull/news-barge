McDonald's fans across the North East are about to get a taste of menu favourites usually only available thousands of miles away.

A limited-edition menu lands at all 62 North East McDonald's restaurants on Tuesday, September 22.

It will feature favourites usually sold only in Germany, Canada, Australia, China, India, the Netherlands and the UAE.

The fast-food giant's latest 'World Menu Heist' promotion brings eight international menu items to the UK and Ireland for a limited time, including burgers, breakfast items, sides, desserts and drinks.

Germany's Big Rösti comes topped with a crispy potato rösti, bacon, cheese and two sauces, while Canada's Maple & Cheddar Chicken pairs a chicken fillet with maple-flavoured mustard sauce and cheddar.

McDonald's said the range was inspired by the popularity of overseas menu items among customers travelling abroad.

The company said it had "heisted" international favourites after customers repeatedly asked for them in the UK.

The menu launches across the UK and Ireland on Tuesday, September 22, with some items available until October 19 and others until November 16, subject to availability.

Prices will vary, but the Big Rösti is expected to cost around £6.99, the Maple & Cheddar Chicken around £7.29, the S'mores McFlurry approximately £2.69 and the Mac & Cheese Triangles from £2.89.

The promotion follows previous World Menu launches that brought popular McDonald's favourites from around the globe to UK customers for a limited time.

Once the international line-up disappears, many customers will already be looking ahead to the chain's much-anticipated festive menu.
