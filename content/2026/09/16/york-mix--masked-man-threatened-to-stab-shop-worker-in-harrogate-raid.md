A man wearing a balaclava threatened to stab a shop worker during a robbery in Harrogate last night (Tuesday).

It happened at Morrisons Daily on King Edward’s Drive in Bilton just before 8pm.

The assistant was told to open the till or he would be stabbed. He did, and the suspect took cash and fled.

#### Map location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The staff member was understandably shaken by the incident but, thankfully, was not injured.

“Officers swiftly deployed to the area, carrying out CCTV enquiries and circulating images of the suspect to our local team.”

A Police Community Support Officer recognised a man from the footage.

About two hours after the incident, a 51-year-old local man was arrested at his home address on suspicion of robbery.

Further enquiries led him being further arrested for three additional counts of theft.

He remains in police custody for questioning.

The spokesperson said: “If you were in the area at the time, or have any information that can assist our investigation please contact us online in the first instance.”
