# North Yorkshire Police issue appeal to help find next of kin following death of Harrogate woman

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for help to locate the next of kin of a 59-year-old woman from Harrogate following her death.

Roksana Matwijiszyn, aged 59, died on Sunday, September 13.

There are no suspicious circumstances surrounding her death.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “If you knew her or know of any relatives that we can contact, please get in touch.”
