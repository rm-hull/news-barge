EMERGENCY services have now left the scene of a major blaze involving thousands of bales and several trailers.

The blaze broke out near a former power station in Eggborough, near Selby, and burnt into the night.

As reported, fire crews from North Yorkshire and West Yorkshire were called at about 7.45pm on Tuesday, September 15.

A water bowser was also deployed to help tackle the flames.

In an update, a spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "The incident involves around 3,500 bales and trailers on fire. Crews worked hard to protect the surrounding area and stop the fire spreading.

"The incident was scaled down overnight, with some crews being released from the scene. "The remaining crews continued to monitor the incident throughout the night and into this morning.

"All crews have now left the scene, but the incident remains open for revisits throughout the day."
