Hundreds of broadband customers in and around Ripon and Thirsk have reported that their service has stopped working.

The outage appears to be affecting people who have broadband from Sky.

People in Ripon, Thirsk, Topcliffe , Dalton, Kirby Wiske and other villages have reported being without a connection for hours.

YorkMix has asked Sky for more information and will update when we hear from them. Some customers say Sky has told them it could take up to 48 hours to fix the problem.

It appears that other internet service providers are not affected.
