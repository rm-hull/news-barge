**The US House of Representatives has voted to hold billionaire Leon Black in contempt of Congress for defying legal summons related to its investigation into convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein.** 

Black - who had business ties to Epstein - did not comply with a subpoena ordering him to appear before the House Oversight Committee earlier this month and share documents related to Epstein.

The billionaire has filed a legal challenge to the subpoenas.

In a statement after the House voted to hold Black in contempt of Congress, Oversight Committee Chairman James Comer said: "No one is above the law."

"We will continue to seek transparency for the American people and justice for survivors."
