A LARGE police scene was seen inside a Bradford cemetery at the weekend.

Crime Scene Investigators were at Windhill Cemetery on Saturday.

Forensic officers were using cameras and several police vans were present.

However police have since confirmed that the matter has been left in the hands of Bradford Council.

## What has Bradford Council said

A spokesperson for the council explained that questions had been raised regarding damage to the "top covers" of graves that had left a "cavity".

They said: "We understand that the top covers of graves had become damaged and left a cavity in the graves.

"This issue is quite common in graveyards and occurs naturally on burial grounds.

"The graves have since been repaired and made safe.

"There is no ongoing risk to public safety, and Bereavement Services will continue to monitor the site as part of normal operations."

They also addressed the fact that some social media reports may alarm families and visitors, but insisted that "on this occasion the matter related to damage to the grave covers... has now been addressed.

"We understand West Yorkshire Police attended the location following concerns being reported.

"We subsequently inspected the graves, identified damage to the top covers and arranged for the necessary repairs to be completed."
