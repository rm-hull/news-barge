A 51-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of robbery at a Morrisons Daily in North Yorkshire.

The incident is reported to have taken place at the supermarket in King Edward’s Drive in Harrogate just before 8pm on Tuesday (September 15).

  


North Yorkshire Police said: “A member of staff was working in the shop when a man entered wearing a balaclava and demanded that the till be opened, threatening to stab him if he did not comply.

“The suspect then took cash from the till and left the store.

“The staff member was understandably shaken by the incident but, thankfully, was not injured.”

A force spokesperson said the suspect was arrested at his home address around two hours after the incident.

He remains in custody for questioning while an investigation takes place.

If you were in the area at the time, or have any information that could assist officers, please contact police on 101, quoting reference 12260181606.
