**HARROGATE**— The full programme for the 14th annual Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival has been officially unveiled, bringing a characteristically high-profile mix of television household names, political analysts, and major memoirs to the town this October.

Running from 11 to 25 October across central venues including The Crown Hotel and Cedar Court Hotel, the fortnight-long event stands as a major commercial and cultural pillar for the region. Organisers at Harrogate International Festivals regularly highlight that their annual portfolio generates an estimated £8.2 million for the local economy, heavily relying on corporate sponsors like Raworths Solicitors to sustain its reach.

While autumn literary festivals are fundamentally built around major authors launching and selling their latest titles, Harrogate’s 2026 line-up fields several heavy-hitting public thinkers set to tackle deeply serious national debates.


**Rachel Tunnicliffe, Senior Partner at Raworths Solicitors, said:** This is our 14th year sponsoring this fantastic Festival which brings so many incredible storytellers and remarkable stories to our town. This year’s brilliant line-up features famous writers, rising stars and thought-provoking panel events, and we’re proud to be involved with such a world class literary celebration.



Among the most anticipated fixtures is Britain’s foremost political analyst, Sir John Curtice, who will attempt to make sense of an increasingly fragmented UK electoral landscape. He is joined on the heavy non-fiction bill by former Supreme Court Justice Lord Jonathan Sumption, who is slated to deliver a lecture questioning whether British democracy is in imminent danger from public complacency.

The festival will also dive directly into major ethical battlegrounds. Award-winning journalist Paul Brand will present an investigation into the deeply divisive assisted dying debate, moving past Westminster rhetoric to map the raw human experiences driving the headlines. Emergency planner Lucy Easthope will also lead a session on disaster recovery, detailing how communities physically rebuild after major national tragedies.


**Sharon Canavar, Chief Executive of Harrogate International Festivals, said:** The Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival 2026 really does have something for every kind of reader – from cosy crime and bestselling fiction to inspiring memoirs and a book about The Beatles.



For mainstream audiences, the commercial core of the festival remains robust. High-profile appearances include veteran broadcaster John Sergeant, culinary icon Dame Prue Leith, and BBC journalist Sophie Raworth, alongside blockbuster novelists Kate Mosse and Julian Clary.

Tickets officially go on sale this week via the central box office, as the charity simultaneously pushes forward with its “HIF@60” endowment appeal to secure a £1 million long-term funding buffer against volatile arts cuts.
