A MAJOR chemicals site has secured its future through a new operations partnership.

Nufarm and px Group have entered into a multi-year operations and maintenance management agreement for Nufarm’s agricultural chemicals manufacturing site in Wyke.

The partnership, effective from 1 September 2026, aims to strengthen the site's competitiveness and long-term sustainability within Nufarm’s global manufacturing network.

Dave Allen, Group Executive - Supply Chain Operations at Nufarm, said: "This partnership allows us to combine Nufarm's agricultural, technical and manufacturing expertise with px Group's specialist operational capability, helping to strengthen the future of the site while ensuring our global operations remain competitive, resilient and focused on sustainable growth.

"We look forward to working alongside px Group and building on Wyke's important role within Nufarm in the years ahead."

## Firm has full control of the site

Under the agreement, Nufarm will retain full control of the site, its assets, and all products manufactured there, while px Group will take over day-to-day operations, maintenance, and site management.

The new operating model is designed to boost the site’s operational resilience and long-term viability, allowing Nufarm to concentrate on strategy, product development, and customer-facing activities.

It also enables the company to benefit from px Group’s experience in safely managing major industrial assets.

A total of 275 employees in operational, maintenance, and support roles have transferred from Nufarm to px Group under the new arrangement.

## 'Significant addition'

Geoff Holmes, Chief Executive Officer of px Group, said: "We're delighted to be entering into this partnership with Nufarm at Wyke.

"It is a significant addition to the chemicals business we're building at px Group, and exactly the kind of longterm operating relationship we want more of, in the UK and internationally."

## 'Manufacturing at Wyke for more than a century'

The agreement marks a significant step for px Group as it expands its portfolio in the chemicals sector and strengthens its position as a long-term operations and maintenance partner for industrial asset owners.

Marek Pawlak, managing director of px Group's chemicals division, said: "There has been manufacturing at Wyke for more than a century, and we are excited to work closely with the Wyke team to achieve great results built upon the outstanding capability and expertise across the team.

"Operating and maintaining the site safely, efficiently, and effectively becomes our full-time focus, and we are looking forward to getting started."

Both companies have emphasised their commitment to ensuring a smooth transition and maintaining safe, compliant, and effective operations at the site.
