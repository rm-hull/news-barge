POLICE have released a new image of a missing three-year-old boy.

Noah Woods disappeared from Brantham in Suffolk on Tuesday, September 15.

He is believed to have run down an alleyway beside the playground towards a nearby new-build housing estate.

Noah is non-verbal, has partial hearing loss, and is currently undergoing an assessment for autism.

Due to his vulnerabilities, he will not respond to his name.

Noah is described as white, with curly ginger hair, and was last seen wearing a white Bluey T-shirt, shorts and black Vans trainers with a white stripe.

Brantham Parish Council posted an update just before 6am today, saying: "At this time, there have been no confirmed sightings of Noah, and the police continue to lead the search operation.

"If the police ask you to move away from an area, please follow their instructions. This may be to allow specialist search activity to take place."
