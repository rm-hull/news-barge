Organisers have confirmed Christmas Town will return to Bishop Auckland this December after concerns were raised when bookings were temporarily closed and passes taken off sale.

The announcement follows a social media post saying that bookings were “temporarily closed” and all passes had been taken off sale, sparking speculation among visitors.

On Tuesday, September 15, event co-founder Thomas Wales reassured visitors that preparations are continuing ahead of the event's return to Bishop Auckland Market Place from December 3 to 6.

Mr Wales said: "Christmas Town is going ahead in Bishop Auckland Market Place from December 3-6, and preparations are continuing.

"We've temporarily paused pass sales while we make some exciting changes to the offer.

"We'll be able to share the full details on that this Thursday at 5pm."

"It's positive news for visitors, and we're looking forward to welcoming everyone back to Christmas Town in December," he added.

Organisers have urged customers to keep their eyes peeled for what they have described as an "exciting" announcement at 5pm on Thursday, September 17, when further details about the changes will be revealed.

Christmas Town will return to Bishop Auckland this winter with festive markets, live entertainment, family attractions and food vendors.

The event has grown rapidly since its launch and is now a major part of the town’s festive calendar.

Organisers previously said around 35,000 people attended Christmas Town in 2023, while more than 60 local traders took part in last year's event.

Baccanalia for Culture CIC co-produces the event with Bridge Creative Enterprise CIC, which helps neurodivergent adults and people with learning disabilities find work in the creative and events sectors.
