A 62-YEAR-OLD motorcyclist was injured after crashing into a traffic island.

The incident happened on Ingrow Lane in Keighley at around 1.20pm on Tuesday.

He was taken to hospital with non-life-threatening injuries.

Images posted on social media show a cordon in place, as well as a police car and attending officers.

### What did emergency services say?

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers were called to reports that a motorbike had collided with a traffic island on Ingrow Lane, Keighley at around 1.20pm yesterday (15 September).

"A 62-year-old man has been taken to hospital with injuries that are not thought to be life threatening."

A spokesperson for the Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 1.07pm yesterday to report a road traffic collision on Halifax Road in Keighley.

"An ambulance was dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
