THOUSANDS of students are being urged to get vaccinated before starting university.

In West Yorkshire, the NHS is encouraging young people to take up a time-limited offer for the meningococcal B (MenB) vaccine, which protects against a serious bacterial infection that can lead to meningitis and septicaemia.

First-year university students are around seven times more likely to develop meningococcal disease compared to their peers who do not attend university.

Dr Sohail Abbas, deputy medical director at NHS West Yorkshire Integrated Care Board, said: "Starting university or residential college is an exciting time, but it can also bring an increased risk of infections such as meningococcal B disease.

"Vaccination is the best protection against becoming seriously unwell with meningococcal disease.

"We would strongly encourage everyone who is eligible to book their appointment as soon as possible and complete both doses before starting university or residential further education."
