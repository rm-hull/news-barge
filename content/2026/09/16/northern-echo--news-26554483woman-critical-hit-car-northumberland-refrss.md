A woman remains in a critical condition in hospital five days after being hit by a car.

Emergency services were called to the A69 at Haltwhistle, in Northumberland, on Friday (September 11) after a pedestrian was hit by a car.

It was reported that, for reasons yet to be established, a white Vauxhall Mokka had struck a woman who was on the carriageway on Haltwhistle Bypass Road.

The woman, who is in her 60s, was taken to hospital with life-threatening injuries and remains in hospital in a critical but stable condition today.

The driver of the Vauxhall, a woman in her 50s, remained at the scene and is assisting Northumbria Police with its enquiries.

A section of the road was closed for a period of time but later re-opened.

A force spokesperson said: “Our Roads Policing Unit (RPU) have launched an investigation to determine the full circumstances surrounding the collision – and are appealing for witnesses to come forward.
