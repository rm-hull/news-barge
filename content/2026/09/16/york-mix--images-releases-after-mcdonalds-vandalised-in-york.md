Do you recognise this man?

Police investigating an incident of criminal damage in York city centre want to speak to him.

On Sunday 16 August, a man entered the McDonald’s restaurant on Blake Street and punched a menu screen causing it to break.

#### McDonald’s, Blake Street

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/cctv-mcdonalds-york-criminal-damage-16-august-2026-2.jpg)

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are appealing for anyone to come forward if they know who this male is.”
