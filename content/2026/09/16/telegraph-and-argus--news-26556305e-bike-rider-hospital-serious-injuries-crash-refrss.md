AN e-bike rider in his 30s has been rushed to hospital with serious injuries following a crash in Bradford.

West Yorkshire Police said officers were called to Buttholme Gate, Buttershaw, at around 10.50am on Wednesday morning.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service have also confirmed that they were called at 11.02 and that a number of ambulance resources were called to the scene. They also confirmed that one patient was taken to hospital.

The incident involved a van, police have confirmed.

When the Telegraph & Argus visited the scene, a small police cordon was in place in the area, off Revvy Road.

Three cones were holding it up with pieces of shattered glass on the road.

  Part of Buttholme Gate was cordoned off as police carried out enquiries following the collision on Wednesday morning. 

*(Image: T&A)*

## 
  **Residents describe incident**

One local resident alleged the rider turned a corner before colliding with the van.

The resident also claimed the injured man had been taken to St. James' Hospital in Leeds.

Another resident said anti-social riding involving e-bikes was a regular occurrence in the area.

They said: "We see them all the time around here, especially in the park.

"It’s not good at all."

## 
  **West Yorkshire Police statement**

A spokesperson for the force said: "At 10.50am officers were called to Buttholme Gate to reports an e-bike was in collision with a van.

"The rider of the e-bike, a man in his 30s has been taken to hospital with serious injuries.

"Enquiries are ongoing at the scene."

## 
  **What emergency services have said**

A spokesperson from Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 11.02am this morning to report a road traffic collision on Buttholme Gate in Buttershaw, Bradford.

They added: "A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including an ambulance and a team leader, and one patient was conveyed to hospital.
