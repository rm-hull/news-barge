York students pay nearly £1,600 more than the national average for accommodation, according to new research released as thousands return for the new academic year.

An average room in York costs £205 a week—£36 more than the UK average of £169, according to Accommodation for Students.

Over a standard 44-week tenancy, that adds up to £1,584 more.

Simon Thompson, founder of Accommodation for Students, said: "York offers a fantastic student experience, combining a lively university community with the character and attractions of one of the UK’s most historic cities.

"That understandably makes it a popular place to live, but students need to recognise that its accommodation costs are above the national average."

Despite its higher rents, York ranked among the UK’s ten safest student locations, with police-recorded crime around student housing below the median.

The findings come as students start looking for accommodation for the next academic year.

York is home to the University of York and York St John University, which draw students from across the UK and overseas.

The rental data comes from listings recorded in June 2026, while the crime figures cover May 2025 to April 2026.
