A North East dad has landed an incredible cash prize after waking up to a surprise phone call, initially thinking it was a wind-up.

Kevin Allison, 43, from Stockton, won £25,000 in tax-free cash through BOTB’s weekly Lifestyle Competition.

The father-of-two, who had been under the weather and was taking a day off work, received the news via a video call from BOTB presenter Christian Williams.

Mr Allison said: "That’s a lot more than what I was expecting."

Still half-asleep when he answered the call, he said he initially assumed the call was work-related.

He said: "I was completely caught off guard when Christian phoned.

"I’d taken the day off work and was asleep when he called, so I initially assumed it was someone from work with a question.

"Because I’d only just woken up, I didn’t fully understand what the call was about and asked if he could call me back in five minutes."

He added: "I got up, splashed some water on my face and then realised I couldn’t remember working with anyone called Christian.

"When I checked my phone again, I immediately spotted the BOTB logo.

"My next thought was that someone must be winding me up."

Even after having time to process the news, Mr Allison said it didn’t feel real.

He said: "I think it will properly sink in once the money lands in my bank account.

"I’ve won competitions on other platforms before, but this is by far the biggest win I’ve ever had."

He works during the week at data centres abroad and helps run his family’s Stockton nightclub on weekends, which means he often doesn't have time to enter competitions.

However, after signing up to the BOTB Pass around four months ago, he is automatically entered into a number of its competitions.

Mr Allison said: "I told the lads at work first, and they were absolutely made up for me."

He is already planning how to use the money and wants to take his family on holiday.

He said: "For me and my wife there are a couple of places in the States we want to go, like California and Vegas, but the kids will probably want to go to Disneyland or Universal Studios."
