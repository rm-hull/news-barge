Plans have been lodged for 28 holiday chalets at a popular County Durham country pub.

The Hammer and Pincers, in Preston le Skerne near Newton Aycliffe, wants to transform its existing caravan and campsite into an “attractive” lodge park.

Owner Henry Harrop wants to refresh the space - which can currently hold 20 touring caravan pitches and ten tent pitches - in a bid to boost visitor numbers and local spending.

The new “high quality” chalets have been designed with timber-effect cladding, a slate-style roof, and decking, with the aim of maintaining a “low profile”.

They would be available year-round, and each boast their own parking and cycle storage.

Fahim Farooqui, from Total Planning Solutions, said the project is in an “excellent location” offering visitors an “attractive base from which to explore County Durham”.

![]() *(Image: Christie & Co.)*

He said it would replace a “relatively low-quality existing tourism use with a more considered, higher-quality and sustainable development”.

Mr Farooqui told the Echo: “The proposal represents a significant improvement to the site, replacing an existing visually poor touring caravan and tent use with a high-quality, professionally managed and extensively landscaped holiday lodge development.

“The scheme has been carefully designed to provide a much better overall layout, improved parking, landscaping and site management.

![]()Plans for new holiday chalets at the Hammer and Pincers near Newton Aycliffe *(Image: TOTAL PLANNING SOLUTIONS)*

“Importantly, the development will also support the long-term viability of the adjoining public house.

"Traditional rural pubs continue to face significant economic pressures, and providing high-quality visitor accommodation alongside the existing business will help generate additional customers, increase local spending and support its continued operation.”

New plans include a dedicated manager’s chalet, as well as new landscaping, the retaining of trees and vegetation, and a drainage pond.

![]()Plans for new holiday chalets at the Hammer and Pincers near Newton Aycliffe *(Image: TOTAL PLANNING SOLUTIONS)*

The site is largely enclosed by vegetation, so it would “not result in any change to the character of the local landscape type”, and would be supervised and monitored by CCTV.

Guests will have direct access to food, drink and hospitality at the traditional pub, with hopes it could deliver “significant economic benefits” to the business.

![]()An aerial shot of Hammer and Pincers and its current campsite *(Image: GOOGLE)*

Bosses argued last year that static caravans could be used instead of the current site’s set up, which was approved in March 2017 alongside a portable toilet and shower block.

They said this could be done without breaching conditions from the previous plans, but the council disagreed and the proposal was thrown out.

Formerly the Blacksmith Arms, Hammer and Pincers rebranded in 2017, the same year its small caravan and camping site was opened.

Under the previous ownership of Rachel and Peter Walton and Neil Skinner, who took over in 2016, the venue went on to open a delicatessen and farm and gift shop.

It was taken over by Harrop Leisure Ltd in 2024.
