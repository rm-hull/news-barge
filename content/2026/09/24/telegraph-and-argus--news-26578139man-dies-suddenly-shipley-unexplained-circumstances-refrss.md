POLICE have confirmed that a man died suddenly this morning in Shipley.

West Yorkshire Police were called to Crag Road at 6.33am on Thursday, September 24 to reports of a sudden death in a public place.

The force has said a scene remains in police.

Enquiries are ongoing.

## What police could say at this stage

A spokesperson for WYP said: "At 6.33am this morning (24 September) officers were called to Crag Road in Shipley by the ambulance service to reports of a sudden death in a public place.

"Enquiries are in their very early stages.

"The man’s death is currently being treated as unexplained.

"A scene is in place whilst enquiries are carried out. "

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "Ambulance resources responded to an incident on Crag Road in Shipley this morning (24 September)."

### Local Reactions

A local cyclist, said “I cycled there last night, it was fine then but then I got up this morning and there was a policeman down the end there, they taped it off.”

He claimed, “Somebody had said it was a bloke who was reasonably well dressed.”

Another individual said “It was still tented up when we got here, around 7:30, don’t really know what it was, seems a bit weird”

A resident said he had heard about the incident from Facebook.

The post said **“** Sad News. Anyone that walks on the side walk near the new houses on crag road, leading down to Aldi/mcdonalds. Unfortunately there has been a dead body found & it’s all corned off by the police.

“Thought I’d post here as I know families walk on there, and a lot of doggy walkers.”
