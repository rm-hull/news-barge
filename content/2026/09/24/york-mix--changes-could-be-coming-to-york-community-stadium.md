Changes could be coming to the home of York City, York Knights and York Valkyrie.

A ‘Box Bar’ could be created at the LNER Community Stadium if a licensing application lodged by its operator Greenwich Leisure Ltd is approved by City of York Council.

The application stated the new bar would help to cut queues during peak times by increasing the points of sale for alcohol.

Changes in the application also include reducing the hours when the 8,500-capacity stadium would be licensed to sell alcohol.

Sales would end at 11.30pm across all licensed areas in the stadium if the plans are approved, two and a half hours earlier than the current 2am.

Non-sporting events such as showing televised matches on screens would also be permitted.

Approval of the application would see the stadium licensed to sell alcohol from 8am to 11.30pm, every day.

It would also be licensed to hold indoor sporting events and other performances, stage live music and play recorded music during those hours.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/york-stadium-sign.webp)

The stadium, at Huntington’s Monks Cross Shopping Park, would operate from 6am to midnight daily.

The LNER Community Stadium opened in February 2021 after work on the site began in December 2017.

It cost £44.2 million and replaced York City’s and York Knights’ former home ground at Bootham Crescent.

The football and rugby club both play at the LNER Community Stadium, along with the York Valkyrie Women’s team.
