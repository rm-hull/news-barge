A County Durham tarmac worker died after his car crashed into a metal barrier on the A1(M), an inquest heard.

Ryan Edward Scarth, 31, from Sherburn Village, died following a single-vehicle crash on the A1(M) at Bowburn on August 22.

An inquest into his Mr Scarth's death was opened at Crook Coroner's Court on Tuesday (September 22).

Coroner's officer Sue Rooney told the short hearing: "Ryan was involved in a single-vehicle collision.

"He struck the central reservation barrier, headed towards the nearside of the carriageway, crossing the hard shoulder and colliding with a metal barrier to the nearside of the hard shoulder."

Mr Scarth died at the scene.

A post-mortem examination was carried out by Dr S McKenna Favier at Newcastle's Royal Victoria Infirmary Mortuary on August 28.

The court heard the cause of death remains pending while histology and toxicology investigations are completed.

Mr Scarth was formally identified by his sister, Emma Yard, to PC Adam Tombling on August 22.

The inquest was adjourned for further inquiries.

In a tribute, his family said previously: "The loss of Ryan has truly broken us as a family.

“Ryan was a son, daddy, brother, uncle, and partner. He lived for his two boys, and his memory will live on through them.

“Ryan has not only left a huge void in our lives but in his friends and the local community too.

“He could walk into a room with his cheeky smile and light it up.

"We are truly heartbroken.

"We will love you forever and always.”

Emergency services, including the air ambulance, were called at around 2.30am to the one-vehicle crash involving a red Peugeot 208.

The car was travelling southbound between junction 61 at Bowburn and 60 at Bradbury.

The road was closed for several hours while emergency services worked at the scene.
