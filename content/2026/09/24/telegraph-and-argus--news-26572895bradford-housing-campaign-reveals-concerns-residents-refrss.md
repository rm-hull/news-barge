A HOUSING chief has taken part in an engagement campaign aimed at improving neighbourhoods through direct conversations.

Accent Housing chief executive Nick Apetroaie joined colleagues in Bradford for the organisation’s Big Local Conversation, which took place on September 15-16 across 43 communities nationwide.

Mr Apetroaie visited residents in Clayton and Allerton to hear their experiences and gather feedback.

He said: "Being out in Bradford and speaking directly with customers is a powerful reminder of why listening locally matters.

"Every community is different, and the people who live there understand better than anyone what is working and where we need to improve."

The campaign focused on informal, face-to-face conversations and was designed to make Accent more visible in its communities.

Feedback will be used to inform future plans.

Early figures show that around 1,729 conversations took place and 4,539 digital forms were completed.

One in four customers said they wanted cleaner, better-maintained neighbourhoods, while one in six raised concerns about repairs and maintenance.

Jo Gallagher, Head of Customer & Community Engagement at Accent, said: "The Big Local Conversation is our largest customer engagement event, providing an important opportunity for colleagues and customers to come together and have meaningful conversations about the issues that matter most to local communities."
