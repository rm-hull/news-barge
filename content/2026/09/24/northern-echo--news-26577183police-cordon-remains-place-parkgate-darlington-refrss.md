A police cordon remains in place outside a Darlington pub this morning as CSI officers continue enquiries at an upstairs flat.

Police, CSI and ambulance crews were called after an incident above The Greyhound pub, on Parkgate, on Wednesday night.

Emergency services were spotted at around 10.20pm and remain at the scene this morning.

![]()Police cordon remains in place on Parkgate in Darlington outside The Greyhound pub *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Several police cars, rapid-response vehicles and an ambulance were pictured outside the pub.
