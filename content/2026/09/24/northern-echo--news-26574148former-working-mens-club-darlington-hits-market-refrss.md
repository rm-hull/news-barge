A derelict former working men’s club in Darlington has hit the market.

Darlington Central WMC, which has been a feature of Beaumont Street since the 1970s, has remained shuttered since its closure in 2017.

And now, the former club is up for grabs as a “rare and flexible opportunity” - with the owner looking for someone to rent the site for £1,000 per month.

Located between two other derelict buildings - the former Inside Out nightclub and Seen bar - the site is made up of two floors of open-plan accommodation.

![]()The former Darlington Central WMC *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The building, available as a whole, also boasts a separate owner's accommodation.

Agent Align Property Partners said there is scope for a wide variety of uses and configurations and could suit office, leisure, hospitality, community, training, fitness, studio or other uses.

![]()The former Darlington Central WMC *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

“The property is therefore well placed for an occupier seeking a central Darlington base with the flexibility of a larger multi-floor building,” it said.

The listing comes almost a year after plans were approved to transform the ground floor of the building into three new shops while improving its ”utilitarian and basic” appearance.

No visible changes have been made to the front of the building since then.

Previous planning documents described how the works would “enhance and uplift the building's overall presence” by adding new modern shopfronts and pedestrian access.

The inside of the former club has fallen into a state of disrepair over the years since its closure, with large sections of the ceiling panels missing or hanging loose.

Last year, plans also revealed that the building, which had undergone very little changes since its original construction, had exposed beams and pipes.
