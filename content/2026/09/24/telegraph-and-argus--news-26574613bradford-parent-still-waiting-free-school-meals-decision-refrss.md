A Bradford parent says she is still waiting to hear whether her children qualify for free school meals, despite applying on the first day applications opened earlier this month.

The parent, who asked not to be named, said she became eligible under the Government's expanded free school meals criteria announced earlier this year.

After receiving information from her children's school, she checked Bradford Council's website in June but found applications would not open until September 1.

She submitted her application as soon as the system opened but says she is still awaiting a response several weeks into the new school term.

## **'I haven't even had a response'**

The parent has two children at secondary school and estimates she is currently spending around £160 a month on school meals.

Although she said she is not in financial hardship, she believes the additional expense could be better spent elsewhere.

She said: "My incomings haven't gone up, but my outgoings have...An extra £80 is a lot of money when I could be saving it."

The parent also said she had contacted the council for an update but had not yet received a reply.

## **Questions over delays**

The mother said her main frustration is that applications could not be submitted until September despite the changes being announced months earlier.

She said: "My frustration is why hasn't it been in place? Why did we have to wait until September to apply?"

The parent believes families should have been able to submit applications over the summer so that decisions could be made before children returned to school.

## **Call for answers**

While she said the cost is manageable for her family, she is concerned that other households may be feeling the impact more acutely.

She added: "I'm not struggling, but some people are."

The parent is now calling for the issue to be resolved as soon as possible and says she wants answers as to why families have faced a wait for applications and decisions.

## Council says rule changes led to surge in applications

A Bradford Council spokesperson said recent changes to free school meals eligibility had resulted in a significant rise in applications.

They said: "Changes to the free school meals scheme from 1 September mean more families can now qualify for support.

"We had to update our application system to reflect the new rules and have since received a high number of applications.

"Our team is working hard to process applications as quickly as possible, and we thank families for their patience."
