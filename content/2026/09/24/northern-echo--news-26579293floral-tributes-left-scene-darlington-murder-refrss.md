Floral tributes have been left at the scene after a woman was 'murdered' in Darlington.

Several bunches of flowers have been placed outside The Greyhound pub on Parkgate after a woman was found dead in the flat above on Wednesday evening.

Two men have been arrested – a 44-year-old on suspicion of murder, and a 27-year-old on suspicion of assisting an offender.

Some of the bouquets had messages on them reading "love you always my friend" while another said: "I am so sorry, sending all the love in the world".

This afternoon white-suited crime scene investigators returned to the scene to continue examining the flat.

![]()CSI forensics back at the scene this afternoon *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Formal identification has not yet taken place but police believe the victim was in her 40s.

![]()Floral tributes left at the scene for the suspected murder victim *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Heartbreaking tributes have also poured in online.

![]()Floral tributes left at the scene for the suspected murder victim *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

One person wrote: "Such a lovely woman. Just the other day we were chatting. So so gutted for your family and friends at this sad time."

Another said: "RIP amazing lady you will be missed"

A further message read: "heart breaking. Sending love to the family"

Another wrote: "RIP god bless their soul rest in peace angel the world is way to cruel"

![]()Floral tributes left at the scene for the suspected murder victim *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

A 42-year-old woman who lives nearby said she saw five or six police cars, an ambulance and scores of officers outside the property.

![]()Floral tributes left at the scene for the suspected murder victim *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

She said: “I’ve heard it’s a woman who’s died in the flat above, it’s just awful and so sad. I think she was well known around Darlington it seems as well.

"I saw about five maybe six police cars, an ambulance and lots of officers last night probably around 10.30ish. I couldn’t stop thinking about what had happened.”

![]()Police, CSI and ambulance crews were called to a property above The Greyhound Darlington pub where a police cordon was put in place around 10pm last night *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Specialist crime scene investigators have since been seen examining the scene, with officers continuing to carry out enquiries in the area.

Shoppers and residents were seen looking on as officers guarded the scene on Thursday morning.

One woman who often visits the area said she was shocked to see the cordon.

She said: "We always come here at this time but I was shocked to see the cordon up. I have no idea what’s happened.”

![]()Police cordon remains in place on Parkgate in Darlington outside The Greyhound pub *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Following the incident Durham Police have realised a statement.

A spokesperson said: "A man has been arrested on suspicion of murder after a woman was found dead in Darlington last night (Wednesday, September 23). Officers were called to an address in Parkgate at about 9.15pm.

"Sadly, a woman was pronounced dead at the scene. Formal identification has yet to take place, but it is believed to be a woman in her 40s.

"An investigation has been launched, and two men have been arrested – a 44-year-old on suspicion of murder, and a 27-year-old on suspicion of assisting an offender.

"Both men have been taken into police custody where they remain.

"Specialist crime scene investigators are currently examining the scene, and officers are carrying out enquiries in the area.

"A cordon is in place at the scene and is expected to remain for some time.

"Anyone with any information is asked to call Durham Constabulary on 101, quoting incident number 521 of September 23.

"Information can also be passed on to Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800555111."

The North East Ambulance Service confirmed paramedics also attended.

A spokesperson said: "We were called to an incident at a private address in Darlington at 9.15pm on Wednesday, September 23.

"We dispatched an ambulance crew, a clinical team leader and a duty officer."
