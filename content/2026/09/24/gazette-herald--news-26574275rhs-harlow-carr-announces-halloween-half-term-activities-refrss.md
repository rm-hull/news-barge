Families can explore spooky plants, creepy crawlies and magical mysteries at a Yorkshire garden’s Halloween half-term programme.

RHS Garden Harlow Carr will host activities from October 24 to November 1, including a Spooky Plant Trail, animal encounters and hands-on crafts.

Sarah Mullan, public programming manager at RHS Harlow Carr, said: "October half term is a fantastic opportunity for families to discover a different side of the garden.

![]()Potion-making, spooky plants and ZooLab encounters promise Halloween fun for all ages at RHS Harlow Carr *(Image: Supplied)*

"From fascinating plants with surprising stories to hands-on activities and close encounters with creepy crawlies, we've created a programme that combines fun, learning and a little Halloween magic.

"Whether you're following our Spooky Plant Trail, creating a monster planter or joining a potion-making adventure, there's something to spark curiosity and imagination in visitors of all ages."

The Spooky Plant Trail will showcase unusual plants and let children design their own spooky creations.

![]()Potion-making, spooky plants and ZooLab encounters promise Halloween fun for all ages at RHS Harlow Carr *(Image: Supplied)*

ZooLab Animal Encounters will feature snakes, snails and spiders from October 26 to 30.

On October 28, children can decorate pots and plant seeds at Monster Hair Planting.

Garden Detectives returns on October 29 and 30 with experiments and potion-making.
