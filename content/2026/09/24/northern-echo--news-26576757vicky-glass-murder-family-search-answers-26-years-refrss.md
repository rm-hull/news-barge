The family of murdered Vicky Glass have spoken of the pain as their loved one's 'cold-blooded' killer remains at large 26 years on.

Vicky was last seen at around 4am on Union Street, Middlesbrough, on September 24, 2000, just days after celebrating her 21st birthday.

Her body was discovered in a stream near Danby, in North Yorkshire, on November 3, 2000.

Police believe Vicky was murdered, but two decades on no one has been brought to justice.

![]()Vicky Glass. *(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

Ahead of the 26th anniversary of her disappearance, Vicky's family said unanswered questions about her murder still overshadow their lives.

They said: "As another year passes, as we sit to write another plea, we are finding it hard to express how deeply we feel.

"We have spoken about our pain year after year. How can we emphasise that this pain increases as the time goes on? It is vicious and brutal.

"It breaks us down, but it will never stop us from fighting for Vicky, to keep asking for your help, to get answers, to get justice for Vicky."

The family said they not only grieve for the loss of Vicky's life, but for the future she never had.

"We grieve for our family, for who we could have been, a family with Vicky with us.

"We mourn every single day, not being able to talk to her, go shopping with her, sitting around a table with her having a family meal, laughing with her, making memories with her. All the small things that are taken for granted we do not have."

Vicky's mother died before her family got the answers they had spent years searching for.

![]()A young Vicky Glass. *(Image: FAMILY)*

The family said: "For 26 years we have been waiting for the cold-blooded monster that murdered Vicky to be named, for justice to be served, for them to pay the price for what they have done."

"For 26 years this killer has lived a life they had no right to live, a life free of consequence with no thought for the life they stole - our Vicky's life and our lives.

"Our lives are plagued; there is a dark shadow that follows us."

"We can't get justice for Vicky on our own so we ask once again, we implore anyone with any information, big or small, to please come forward."

Earlier police appeals identified a lorry driver who spoke to Vicky in Middlesbrough on the day she disappeared; he was described as white, aged 34 to 38 at the time, broad-built and with a distinctive mole.

Three arrests had previously been made as part of the inquiry.

A fourth followed in June this year, when a 65-year-old man was arrested in connection with the murder before being released on bail pending further investigation.

Senior investigating officer Detective Chief Inspector Steve Chatterton said officers remain determined to find the person responsible.

He said: "Vicky's family have longed for answers regarding her death and the person responsible for 26 years.

"As we continue to investigate Vicky's murder, we would again appeal for anyone with information regarding Vicky's whereabouts between the last sighting of her and her body being found six weeks later to contact us.

"Any information at all could assist us in finally bringing to justice the person who murdered Vicky Glass, and bringing an end to this nightmare for her family."

Anyone with information about Vicky's disappearance or death should contact Cleveland Police on 101 or 01642 301773.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800555111.
