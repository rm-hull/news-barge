A 34-year-old woman has been reported missing and is believed to be in the Keighley area.

Natasha, 34, is from Glusburn in North Yorkshire.

She is described as a white woman with brown hair, usually worn in a ponytail.

She has a dragonfly tattoo on her neck and a tattoo of a necklace with a cross pendant on her lower neck.

She was last seen wearing a black dress or long T-shirt, black shorts with a floral pattern down the side, leopard-print trainers, and a black coat.

Officers are increasingly concerned about Natasha's welfare and ask that anyone with information contacts West Yorkshire Police by calling 101.

Alternatively you can go online at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat) quoting reference 0161 of 24/9.
