# A military threat from US? Canadians react to Carney's concerns

Prime Minister Mark Carney told The New York Times that he had examined the possibility of a US-led military action against Canada, describing it as an "extreme tail risk" that would be irresponsible not to prepare for, even if it remains highly unlikely.

The BBC spoke to Canadians from across the country about their concerns over the comments.
