A new North East housing development has won an industry award for the quality of its work.

Banks Homes’ Cornfields development on the southern edge of Yarm has won a Quality Recognition Award from national warranty and building control provider Premier Guarantee.

Joe Cable, senior project manager (housebuilding) at the Banks Group, said: "From the very beginning, we set out our stall to deliver a higher build quality and higher specifications than our peers in the housebuilding industry, and this sort of recognition is proof of how we're doing so.

"This award is testament to the exemplary ways of working that we set out for the Banks Homes team, our sub-contractors and our suppliers before a brick has been laid at our sites, as well as to the work of our site managers in upholding them and our site teams in delivering them on a day-to-day basis."

The award puts the development in the running for Premier Guarantee’s Excellence Awards next summer.

It will compete with top-performing sites from across the UK for national recognition.

Only a small number of sites win the award, which requires them to meet rigorous standards in every inspection category for at least six consecutive months.

Dan Hodgson of Premier Design said: "Our inspections adhere to exacting quality standards for every aspect of the life cycle of a development, from the management of the site and the first foundations being laid right through to the overall quality of workmanship and the final finishing touches within each new home.

"Only a small proportion of the projects we see reach the standards required to earn this level of recognition, but the Banks Homes team has consistently shown how it's doing just that every time we've visited The Cornfields."

The Cornfields is the first Banks Homes development to feature show homes from the company’s bespoke house-design range.

The 109-home scheme offers three- to five-bedroom properties, all with smart heating systems, air-source heat pumps and electric vehicle charging points.

Four- and five-bedroom homes include ground-floor underfloor heating, while selected Elevated-range house types have 2.6-metre ceilings downstairs.

Homes at The Cornfields start at £309,950.
