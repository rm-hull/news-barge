Business leaders at every stage of growth could benefit from stepping back and focusing on their personal development.

That’s the message from Dave Callan, founder of ONTO Coaching and former Manchester United and Team Sky marketing leader, ahead of his keynote at this year’s York Business Festival.

Taking place on Wednesday, October 14 and Thursday, October 15 at York St John University, the festival will feature around 20 talks, workshops, exhibitions, and networking opportunities.

Mr Callan said: "Founders and business leaders should all find the time to take themselves away from the day-to-day to focus on themselves and to work on their business, not in it.

"York Business Festival have worked hard to provide a platform to make these opportunities really accessible for those who want to get more clarity and take some practical takeaways into the next phase of their growth."

Mr Callan’s career began in financial services at First Direct before moving into sports marketing with Manchester United’s commercial team during a period of rapid growth.

He later joined Team Sky as chief marketing officer before transitioning to a role at Travel Counsellors, where he helped the company reach £1 billion in revenue.

Despite his corporate success, Mr Callan decided to pursue a long-standing passion for personal development and coaching, launching ONTO Coaching to help others achieve peak performance.

He will open this year’s festival, with Sir Richard Harpin, founder of HomeServe and Growth Partner, delivering the keynote on Thursday, October 15.

Sir Richard, who was born and educated in Yorkshire, is one of Britain’s most successful entrepreneurs and a strong advocate for developing entrepreneurial skills in others.

He said: "Yorkshire is where some of my earliest entrepreneurial ideas started, so I’m really looking forward to coming back and being part of the York Business Festival.

"I’m a big believer that you can accelerate your own success by learning from people who have already made the mistakes, found what works and are willing to share it.

"Events like this are a great opportunity to meet other ambitious people, build your network and come away with practical ideas you can put into action.

"In my keynote, I’ll be sharing some of the lessons I’ve learned from building HomeServe from an idea into a £4 billion business, and the nine steps I believe can help entrepreneurs build and scale a great business.

"I want people to leave with things they can actually use, whether they’re just starting out or already running a growing company."

The festival is organised by York Business School at York St John University in partnership with York Press, and is supported by City of York Council, York & North Yorkshire Growth Hub, Make It York, and York BID.

All events are free to attend, but booking is essential via [https://www.yorksj.ac.uk/events/york-business-festival/](https://www.yorksj.ac.uk/events/york-business-festival/)
