Police, CSI and ambulance crews were called to a property above a Darlington pub where a police cordon was put in place.

Police alongside ambulance crews attended a property above The Greyhound pub in Darlington.

Officers cordoned off the property while investigations were carried out, at around 10.20pm last night.
