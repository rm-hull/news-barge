A market town in Northumberland has been named one of the best places to retire to in England.

World Atlas has revealed its top seven places to retire to in England, with different spots across the country making it onto the list.

Alnwick featured on the list thanks to its affordable housing and impressive castle found on the edge of the town.

As well as this Northumberland spot, Barnard Castle in County Durham also made it onto the list.

![]()There are lots of things to see and do around the town. *(Image: Getty)*

## What is Alnwick like?

On the market town, World Atlas said: “Alnwick stands five miles inland from the North Sea, and the castle on the northern edge of town has belonged to the Percy family since the fourteenth century.

“Around 8,430 people live here.

“Property across Northumberland averaged about £213,000 in June 2026, roughly £80,000 below the England figure.

“The castle grounds and state rooms open through the warmer months, and The Alnwick Garden next door runs to a terraced water cascade and a walled Poison Garden that can only be entered on a guided tour.

“Barter Books occupies the town's Victorian railway station, keeps something like 350,000 second-hand books in stock, and is where an original wartime Keep Calm and Carry On poster surfaced in a box bought at auction in 2000.

“The Bailiffgate Museum, a few minutes from the castle gates, covers the history of the town and the villages around it.

“None of these demands much walking, and the castle, the garden, and the bookshop all sit within about ten minutes of each other.”

If you are interested in moving to Alnwick, the majority of properties that sold in the area over the last year were detached and cost an average of £458,209, according to Rightmove.

However, terraced properties are a lot cheaper costing an average of £226,707 and semi-detached properties cost around £238,382 if you are looking to downsize.

Alnwick Castle is a must-visit and is most famous for its role in the Harry Potter films, Downton Abbey, and Robin Hood, Prince of Thieves.

The castle has plenty of things to see and do so you can make multiple trips to the site as well as book onto tours if you are interested in learning more about specific filming locations.

If you want to enjoy some time outside, you can visit Alnwick Garden, which features a range of different areas like the Rose Garden, Grand Cascade, and Ornamental Garden.

The garden has lots of different seasonal blooms making each trip different from the last.

Alnwick Garden also has its own restaurant called the Treehouse that is ideal for a special occasion thanks to its magical feel.

Alongside the many activities available, there are plenty of restaurants to visit including Mumbai of Alnwick, a highly rated Indian spot.

You could also try Di Sopra, an Italian restaurant offering lots of authentic dishes from calamari and bruschetta to start to pastas like amatriciana, boscaiola, and carbonara.

If you are looking for somewhere for a relaxing drink, you can visit Grannies Tea Room where you can enjoy a quick coffee and slice of cake or spend some time walking around the delicatessen for some local produce.
