# Big Leeds Christmas Market 2026 update as return of popular ice rink confirmed

## There's lots of Christmas cheer you can enjoy in Leeds city centre this festive season

Leeds Christmas season fun has been announced.

We have details of what is happening in Leeds city centre this winter. This year’s annual Christmas market will return to City Square with a new look from Thursday, November 19 with a host of local food, drink, music and entertainment.

Additionally, we’ll see the popular Ice Cube at Christmas will also be back on Millennium Square, Victoria Gardens, and Cookridge Street with advance tickets for the ice rink now on sale. You can find out more here.

This year’s exciting City Square market will be organised by Fabler and Co, the company behind Thor’s Tipi, bringing stalls from 60 traders and food vendors from across Yorkshire as well as two new bar areas, around 200 Christmas trees and a new elf hunt trail around the attraction, all in addition to the return of their much-loved traditional Christmas tipis in Victoria Gardens.

This year the market will also feature a selection of special cabins, operated by Real Markets Yorkshire, featuring a rotating selection of artisan traders. The return of Ice Cube at Christmas will see the covered ice rink in Millennium Square return.

It will welcome skaters beneath a transparent marquee, offering daily skating sessions and dedicated weekend Penguin Club sessions for youngsters under nine. Alongside skating, the event will also include a wide-selection of festive street food, Bavarian-style market stalls, offering festive favourites, an alpine-inspired ski bar and winter themed games, rides and attractions for all ages including the iconic big wheel in Victoria Gardens, the Golden Gallopers carousel in Cookridge Street and the 30-metre-high starflyer in Millennium Square.

Councillor Salma Arif, Leeds City Council’s executive member for economy, said: “Christmas is a really special time in Leeds and it’s always amazing to see the city transformed each year by all the unique magic of the festive season. Our Christmas programme is always hugely popular, attracting tens of thousands of people to the city centre and helping families and friends to create some incredible memories.
