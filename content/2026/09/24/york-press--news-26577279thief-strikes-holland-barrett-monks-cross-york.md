A thief has struck at a York shop.

North Yorkshire Police has issued photographs of two people officers want to speak to after the theft at Holland and Barrett at Monks Cross on Saturday, September 5.

The force said the people pictured may have information about theft.

“Please contact us if you recognise any of the persons pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation,” a police spokesperson said.

Those with information are asked to email [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260174418 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
