A giant Ferris wheel offering panoramic views across Newcastle is set to return as a centrepiece of the city’s Christmas celebrations.

Towering 22 metres above Old Eldon Square, the wheel will be part of this year’s expanded Christmas Village and is 50 per cent larger than last year’s 15-metre model.

Nigel Holliday of EVNT Inspirations said: "To have a 22m wheel is so exciting.

"It will give people incredible views over the city which will be particularly magical when it gets dark.

"It just puts the cherry on the Christmas cake in terms of making Newcastle’s Christmas Market something nobody will want to miss."

The wheel is part of a festive programme developed by Newcastle City Council and NE1.

The Christmas Village features paid events including Santa Stories, Mrs Claus’ Bakery, and performances on the Rockin’ Reindeer stage.

Other attractions include Marshmallowland, a vintage carousel, and food and drink outlets.

Ben Whitfield, director of communications at NE1, said: "We are ‘wheely’ excited that the Ferris wheel is returning to Newcastle’s Christmas market this year – and that it has been supersized.

"50 per cent bigger and giving even better views of the city from the top.

"It was a hugely popular attraction last year, alongside the traditional carousel, and created a real hub for Newcastle’s Christmas village in Old Eldon Square.

"We are fortunate to have such a unique festive family offer in the heart of the market, which we think sets Newcastle’s Christmas Markets apart from other UK cities.

"We are looking forward to taking a ride when it makes its welcome return in November."

The Festive Flix outdoor cinema will also return, showing favourites such as Home Alone, Elf, The Grinch, The Greatest Showman, and Love Actually.

Councillor Rob Austin, cabinet member for culture, communities and youth services at Newcastle City Council, said: "Newcastle’s Christmas celebrations are all about creating memorable experiences for local people and visitors to enjoy, and the new 22-metre Ferris wheel will be a fantastic addition to this year’s Christmas Village.

"Whether you’re enjoying the views during the day or seeing the city lit up after dark, it will offer a really special perspective of Newcastle and add to what is already a packed programme of festive entertainment and activities."

The Christmas Village opens on November 19.

Santa Stories will run until Christmas Eve, with the rest of the village open until December 31.
