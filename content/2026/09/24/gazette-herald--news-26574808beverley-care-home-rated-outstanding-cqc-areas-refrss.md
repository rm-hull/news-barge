An East Yorkshire care home has been rated ‘outstanding’ in all areas by the Care Quality Commission (CQC).

The Old School House in Beverley, which is run by East Riding of Yorkshire Council, is one of only two care homes in the East Riding to achieve this rating and joins a select group nationally to receive the top grade across all five key areas.

It provides residential and dementia care for older people and was rated outstanding for being safe, effective, caring, responsive and well-led.

Councillor Jonathan Owen, cabinet member for adults, health and care at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "This is an exceptional achievement and one that everyone at The Old School House should be immensely proud of.

"Residents are at the centre of decisions about their care, staff know and understand the people they support, and there is a clear commitment to continually listening, learning and improving.

"Most importantly, it is the residents and their families who benefit from that commitment every day."

The CQC report praised the home for delivering care that "placed people at the centre of decision making and care delivery."

The home was previously rated outstanding in four areas and good in one in 2020.
