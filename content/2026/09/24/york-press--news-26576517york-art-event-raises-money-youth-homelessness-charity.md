Artists, musicians and families came together in York for a day of creativity that raised money for a youth homelessness charity.

Sharon McDonagh and Sola of RARE Collective organised The Board Meeting at The Carlton Tavern in Acomb on Saturday, September 12, with Art of Protest.

The event featured street art, spray-painting workshops, artist stalls, food, drink and DJ sets to raise money for SASH (Safe and Sound Homes).

Sharon McDonagh, founder of RARE Collective and SASH’s Creative Charity Champion, said: "The Board Meeting was a celebration of York’s incredible creative community and everything that can happen when artists and people come together with a shared passion.

"The atmosphere on the day was fantastic.

"We had artists creating, DJs playing, families getting involved and the local community supporting us.

"It felt like a real celebration of creativity and community."

A live auction of donated artworks raised more money for SASH, with further donations expected.

Jeff Clark, founder and group creative director of Art of Protest, said: "The Board Meeting has carried that spirit forward, creating another opportunity for York’s creative community to come together."
