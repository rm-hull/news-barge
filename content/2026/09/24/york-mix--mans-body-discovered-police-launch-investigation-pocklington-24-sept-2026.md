A body has been found in Pocklington.

Police say a scene guard is in place on The Balk after the discovery of the man’s body at around 3.15pm yesterday afternoon.

A Humberside Police spokesperson said: “Enquiries remain ongoing to ascertain the full circumstances around the discovery, and the man’s death is currently being treated as unexplained at this time.

“Whilst formal identification has not yet taken place, the man’s family have been informed.”

Police ask anyone who has any information that may assist officers with their enquiries, please to contact them on 101 quoting log 272 of 23 September.

Alternatively, if you wish to report anonymously, you can do so via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Meanwhile, East Riding of Yorkshire Council say: “The Pocklington Household Waste Recycling Site is currently closed.

“The nearest alternative household waste recycling sites are at Market Weighton (also known as Holme-Upon-Spalding-Moor) and Driffield.”
