York’s National Railway Museum is bringing back its popular Father Christmas experience this winter.

Families can meet the character in a historic railway carriage and enjoy free festive activities across the museum.

Sarah Price, head of public programmes at the museum, said: "Our Father Christmas experience sold out within days of release last year, so this year we can’t wait to bring it back bigger than ever, along with a whole range of festive activities.

![]()York’s National Railway Museum will brings back its Father Christmas experience this winter, with festive activities, winter science, carols and gifts for families *(Image: Drew Forsyth)*

"Our amazing free museum is the perfect day out for families all year round, so we’re delighted to be sprinkling a little Christmas magic across the whole museum to make it extra special for all our visitors this festive season."

The Father Christmas experience costs £15 per child and includes a gift, hot chocolate and 10 per cent off at the museum shop.

Children can write to Father Christmas, join winter science activities and design their own calendars.

A choir will sing Christmas carols in Station Hall on selected days.

The Wonderlab gallery will host winter science demonstrations, while the museum’s Explainers run hands-on seasonal experiments at the Demo Bar.
