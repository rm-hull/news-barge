A brand new furniture showroom is set to open in York.

Last Leaf, which specialises in handcrafted hardwood furniture, made the announcement on social media that it will be opening a showroom at 10 Bootham in York ‘very shortly’.

Founder and craftsman Sam Parkin, said that the Last Leaf showroom will run alongside his main workshop in Flaxton, just outside York.

“I can’t wait to get open and show you all,” he wrote on Instagram.

Sam, who has operated Last Leaf Furniture for a decade, creates every bit of furniture himself – from the design and timber selection to hand crafting each individual piece.

He crafts bespoke one-of-a-kind pieces as well as exhibiting his furniture, and combines hand-cut decorative work with showcasing the natural characteristics of the timber he uses.

Sam originally studied luthierie at the London Metropolitan University (previously the London College of Furniture) – and went on to work on large furniture projects such as Ripon Cathedral, Christchurch College Oxford and St Martin in the Fields on Trafalgar Square.

10 Bootham was previously home to the Blue House Bookshop, which closed at the end of August.

An exact opening date for the opening of the Last Leaf showroom has yet to be announced, but Sam says to “Keep watching for more info and updates!”
