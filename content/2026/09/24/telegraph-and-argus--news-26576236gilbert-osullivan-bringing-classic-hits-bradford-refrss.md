THE singer-songwriter Gilbert O’Sullivan is bringing an intimate evening of music to St George’s Hall this weekend.

Gilbert O’Sullivan will perform at the Bradford venue this Sunday, September 27, 2026, with the show starting at 7.30pm.

Joined by acclaimed guitarist Bill Shanley, he promises an evening of unforgettable songs from one of music’s most distinctive voices.

The concert marks more than five decades in music for O’Sullivan, whose career began with his 1970 breakthrough, Nothing Rhymed. The singer has since achieved 16 Top 40 records, including six number one songs.

His timeless classics include Clair, Get Down and Alone Again (Naturally), which topped the US charts for six weeks and earned three Grammy nominations.

He has released 20 studio albums and received three Ivor Novello Awards, including Songwriter of the Year in 1973.

His last three album releases have also achieved Top 10, Top 20 and Top 30 positions in UK album charts.
