What started as a leap of faith by a local husband-and-wife team has grown into one of Durham most vibrant destinations for great food and great music.

At its heart, El Pincho remains a family-run business built on more than 20 years of experience in hospitality, a passion for bringing people together and, having lived in Spain for a number of years, a love of the Spanish way of life.

The journey to opening the doors of El Pincho has been anything but conventional. From running a bungee trampoline attraction in the Metrocentre to managing The Three Horseshoes Inn in Lanchester, they have always embraced new challenges. Those experiences, combined with a passion for authentic Spanish food and culture, inspired them to create something truly special in Durham.

![]() *(Image: El Pincho)*

Opening a new hospitality venue is challenging at the best of times. Opening one during the Covid pandemic was an even bigger gamble. But six years later, that bold decision has paid off, with El Pincho now recognised as one of the city's favourite spots for dining, drinks and entertainment.

## **More than just a restaurant**

During the week, El Pincho offers everything guests would expect from a welcoming Mediterranean-inspired venue. Authentic tapas, carefully selected wines, expertly crafted cocktails and relaxed surroundings create the perfect setting for catching up with friends, celebrating special occasions or simply enjoying an evening out.

The family atmosphere that has been there from day one remains central to everything they do. Warm service, a friendly welcome and a genuine passion for hospitality help make El Pincho feel as much a community hub as it is a restaurant.

![]() *(Image: El Pincho)*

## **When the weekend arrives, the party begins**

As the weekend approaches, El Pincho transforms into one of Durham's most exciting entertainment venues.

Every Saturday from 3pm, live DJs bring a festival-style atmosphere to the city centre, creating the perfect soundtrack for an afternoon and evening of food, drinks and socialising. Live music, packed crowds and an energy-filled atmosphere have helped make the venue a must-visit destination for locals and visitors alike.

Their hugely popular £35 Bottomless Brunch has become a weekend favourite, combining delicious food, flowing drinks and live entertainment in one unforgettable experience.

## **Bringing big names to Durham**

El Pincho's commitment to entertainment has seen the venue attract some major names from the music world.

Earlier this year, dance music legends K-Klass took to the decks, delivering a memorable set that showcased exactly why El Pincho has become known for much more than tapas.

And the line-up keeps getting bigger.

Clubland favourites Flip & Fill and Jesse Bloch arrive on September 25, while one of the UK's most iconic DJs, Judge Jules, headlines a huge event on November 13

These headline acts sit alongside a regular calendar of live music, DJs and special events, ensuring there's always something happening at El Pincho.

## **The perfect finish to the weekend**

![]() *(Image: El Pincho)*

After a weekend of sharing tapas, dancing to live music and enjoying Durham's buzzing nightlife, El Pincho offers one final reason to visit.

Their homemade Sunday lunches, available from just £13.95, provide the perfect way to round off the week, bringing families and friends together around delicious, freshly prepared food.

## **A true Durham success story**

From authentic Spanish tapas and quality wines to live DJs, major music events and traditional Sunday lunches, El Pincho has evolved into far more than a restaurant.

It's a family-run success story built on hospitality, passion and a commitment to creating memorable experiences.

Whether you're looking for a relaxed meal, a lively brunch, an afternoon of music or a big night out, El Pincho delivers it all under one roof.
