Nearly 1,800 York pupils are set to take on a 12-hour running challenge in support of Sir Kevin Sinfield’s latest fundraising challenge.

Archbishop Holgate’s School in York will stage the event on Monday, September 28, to coincide with Sir Kevin Sinfield passing the school during his own running feat for the motor neurone disease community (MND).

Named “7 Til 7”, the event will see every student from Year 7 to Year 13 run at least seven laps of the school field, with year groups taking part in staggered slots from 7am to 7pm.

![]()1,800 pupils at Archbishop Holgate’s School in York are to take on remarkable 12-hour challenge as Kevin Sinfield runs past. *(Image: Submitted)*

Inspired by Sir Kevin's "7 in 7" challenge, in which the former rugby league star has run seven ultra-marathons in seven days in memory of his friend and former teammate Rob Burrow , the school hopes pupils will together complete more than 12,600 laps throughout the day.

Joanna Kitney, assistant headteacher, said: "Sir Kevin Sinfield has shown the whole country what's possible when you refuse to give up on the people you love.

"We wanted our pupils to feel a small part of that spirit, not by doing something easy, but by doing something hard, together, for twelve hours, as one school.

"Whatever lap seven feels like, they'll have raised money for people facing something far harder than tired legs.

"That's what we want them to take from the day."

Mid-morning, the entire school will head to the roadside to cheer Sir Kevin as he runs past, with parents, carers and the wider community encouraged to join.

A community session will take place from 4pm to 7pm, allowing families to take part in laps of their own.

Funds raised will be divided between three causes: the MND Association, the school’s AHS Unity Fund - which supports Archbishop Holgate’s families in need - and Team Uganda, which is building a new nursery for a primary school that students will visit in July 2027.

The school is inviting the wider community to get behind the event in whatever way they can by sponsoring a pupil, joining the roadside cheer, or coming along to the after-school session.

Donations can be made through the school’s GoFundMe page at www.gofundme.com/f/local-national-and-international-charities
