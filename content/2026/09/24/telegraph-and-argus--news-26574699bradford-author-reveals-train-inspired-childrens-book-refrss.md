A children's author has revealed how a love of rail travel and stations, including Keighley, helped inspire his latest adventure book.

Stephen Maltas' latest book follows character Tia on a grand tour across Europe, taking readers on a journey through some of the continent's best-known rail routes.

The adventure begins in the UK before heading to London, where Tia travels from King's Cross to St Pancras to catch a train to Paris.

Once in France, the story takes in a number of famous landmarks, with real-life railway and metro routes helping guide readers around the city.

Mr Maltas said he included genuine railway locations throughout the book, allowing young readers to follow Tia's travels step by step.

## **Publishing success came as a surprise**

The author said he had long been encouraged by his wife to pursue writing but initially doubted whether publishers would be interested in his work.

However, his confidence grew after his first book was accepted for publication.

Mr Maltas said: "I always thought nobody would ever want to publish it, but the first book got accepted straight away for publication and then this one did the same.

"In a way it's been a surprise that it's been so successful for me."

![]()Author Stephen Maltas with copies of his children's books, including his latest release, Tia's Grand Tour. *(Image: Stephen Maltas)*

## **Finding inspiration in the swimming pool**

Mr Maltas said many of his best ideas arrived while swimming, away from the distractions of everyday life.

He explained: "I never really got writer's block. I knew where the books were going to end up. It was just how I was going to get from where I was to the ending.

"Most of my ideas came to me when I was swimming. You're not really thinking about anything other than swimming, and that's where a lot of the ideas came from."

## **Looking ahead to future projects**

While Mr Maltas is keen to continue writing, he says he has not yet decided whether his next project will be another children's book or something aimed at an older audience.

He said: "I'd like to do another book in the future. I just haven't decided whether it will be another children's book or whether I'll try something a bit more serious next time."

Offering advice to aspiring writers, he encouraged people not to be discouraged by setbacks.

"If you enjoy writing and you like what you're creating, that's the most important thing," he said. "Just keep going and write what you want to write."

Copies of Tia's Grand Tour and Mr Maltas' previous book, Tia Misses Christmas (Almost!), are available now, priced at £14.99.

Both titles can be purchased through Amazon, publisher Pegasus, and a range of online booksellers.
