A North East hair specialist has been named Professional Hair Influencer of the Year at a major national awards ceremony.

Rob Wood, founder and creative director of County Durham-based Novo Cabelo, took home the title at the Pro Hair Awards 2026 in London on September 7.

The award recognises his powerful use of social media to challenge perceptions of hair loss and promote modern hair replacement solutions.

Rob Wood said: "To bring this award home to Murton is an incredibly proud moment for me and the whole Novo Cabelo team.

"Our social media has never just been about creating views.

"It is about telling real people’s stories, helping those experiencing hair loss feel less alone and showing them that there are solutions available."

Mr Wood’s emotional client transformation videos have reached millions worldwide, offering hope and support to those affected by hair loss.

His first viral transformation video was viewed more than 30 million times globally, and his content continues to reach millions across TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

He said: "When we started talking openly about hair systems, they were still widely seen as something secretive or embarrassing.

"We have worked hard from here in County Durham to change that perception, not only across the UK but internationally.

"To be named the best in the country for our social media work, and then shortlisted three times at the Most Wanted Awards, is difficult to put into words.

"It shows how far modern hair replacement has come and proves that a business based in Murton can have a genuine global impact."

Novo Cabelo was founded in 2020 with a mission to modernise hair replacement, remove stigma and improve support for clients and professionals.

The company now has a network of more than 200 trained hair professionals, with its education programme officially endorsed by HABIA, the UK’s hair and beauty industry standards body.

Rob said the company remains committed to its roots.

He said: "We are incredibly proud of our County Durham roots.

"Novo Cabelo has grown from a local business into an internationally recognised company, but Murton remains our home.

"Everything we create starts with listening to clients and understanding what would make their experience safer, easier and more positive.

"Whether it is a new product, a training course or a video that helps someone take the first step, our goal is always to restore confidence and remove the stigma surrounding hair loss."

Novo Cabelo has developed its own hair systems, scalp care products, adhesives and specialist aftercare lines.

Its latest innovation, TrueBlend, allows suitable clients to wear a hair replacement system without shaving their remaining natural hair.

In addition to his recent award win, Mr Wood is a triple finalist at the 2026 Most Wanted Awards.

He is recognised for Collaboration (recognising Rob’s work with Little Lady Locks), Engagement (celebrating Novo Cabelo’s ability to educate, inspire and connect with its audience), and Innovation (recognising TrueBlend, Novo Cabelo’s non shave hair replacement method for women and men with thinning hair).

The winners will be announced at the Most Wanted Grand Final at Drumsheds in London on October 5.
