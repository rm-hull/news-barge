Beamish Museum, which welcomes around 800,000 visitors a year, is offering an aspiring engineer the chance to preserve historic vehicles and machinery while earning a qualification.

The open-air museum is looking for a metal fabricator apprentice for a four-year training programme with its Transport Engineering Team and New College Durham.

The apprenticeship will run until September 2030.

Beamish said: “We are looking for an enthusiastic apprentice to join our Transport Engineering Team on a fixed-term basis until September 2030.

“You will combine college study with hands-on workplace training in the museum’s engineering facilities, building the knowledge, confidence and technical skills needed for a career in fabrication and heritage engineering.”

The role is fixed-term and pays £13.01 an hour for 37 hours a week.

The apprenticeship is supported and part-funded by Rob Ribchester, a trustee of Henry Smith's Charity.

Applicants will need to meet specific eligibility criteria to be considered for the role.

Candidates must live in Durham city or the immediate surrounding area, a requirement set by the funding provider.

They must also hold four GCSEs at grade 4/C or above, including maths, English and a science subject, to meet the entry requirements for the course at New College Durham.

As part of the programme, the apprentice will study towards a Level 3 Advanced Apprenticeship in Metal Fabrication at New College Durham while working at the museum.

The museum employs more than 400 staff and has around 400 volunteers across its 300-acre site.

Benefits include a museum pass offering free entry for the successful applicant and three guests, catering and retail discounts, free parking and access to the Bike2Work scheme.

Employees receive 25 days’ annual leave plus bank holidays, rising to 30 days after five years’ service, two wellbeing days a year and access to an employee assistance programme.

Applications close on October 4, 2026.

People interested in applying can find further information and submit an application through the Beamish Museum recruitment website.
