# Big name Leeds city centre bar to shut with 'it's time to say goodbye' message issued

## A final party will be happening this weekend

A big name Leeds city centre bar will soon shut with an important message issued.

On Saturday, September 26 we will see Revolution, in Call Lane, close for the last time. The team from the Leeds city centre bar has issued a message on social media.

Revolution’s Instagram post states: “It’s time to say goodbye. We have loved shaking your cocktails and creating unmissable parties over the years but on September 26 we will sadly be closing our doors in Leeds.

“We’d love you to join us for one last huge party on September 26. We want to extend an enormous thank you for joining the Revolution party and making some unforgettable memories.”
