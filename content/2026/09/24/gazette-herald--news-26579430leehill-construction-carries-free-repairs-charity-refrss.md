A CONSTRUCTION company has given a helping hand to a charity by repairing its long-troubled plumbing free of charge.

Ripon-based LEEHILL Construction stepped in to support Jennyruth Workshops, a charity supporting adults with learning disabilities, after years of recurring toilet blockages due to poorly installed pipework.

Jennyruth Workshops, located near Ripon, provides a supportive environment where adults with learning disabilities design, create, paint, and package handmade items from wood, paper, and fabric.

The charity’s products are sold online and at local events, helping to raise crucial funds for its operations.

Up to 20 adults use the site each day, alongside 15 staff and eight volunteers, making reliable toilet facilities essential for daily activities and the comfort and dignity of everyone involved.

However, toilets installed five years ago had been regularly blocking, sometimes leaving the fully accessible disabled toilet out of action for days at a time.

The problem was traced to incorrectly fitted pipework, including unsuitable pipes installed at the wrong angles.

Deb Sleep, chair of trustees at Jennyruth Workshops, contacted LEEHILL Construction for advice.

Anthony Allinson, director of the company, visited the site, diagnosed the issue, and offered to carry out the repairs free of charge.

Ms Sleep said: "We are incredibly grateful to Anthony and the whole LEEHILL Construction team for their generosity, professionalism and practical support.

"Their help has made an immediate and lasting difference to everyone who uses our workshop."

Over two weeks, Mr Allinson and a team of three dug out and replaced the faulty pipework, improving the overall layout and the way the system feeds into the septic tank.

Since the repairs, Jennyruth Workshops has not had a single blockage and now has fully functioning toilets for the first time in years.

Mr Allinson said: "When we saw the scale of the problem and the impact it was having on everyone at Jennyruth Workshops, we wanted to help.

"It is a fantastic local charity, and we were pleased to be able to provide a practical solution that should make day-to-day life easier for the people who use the workshop."

The charity hopes this story will highlight the value of practical support from local businesses and the real difference it can make to community organisations.

Jennyruth Workshops offers adults with learning disabilities a chance to engage in meaningful, creative activity and build skills in a supportive environment.

LEEHILL Construction is a three-generation, family-owned business based in Ripon.

The company completed the plumbing repair work entirely free of charge to support the charity and its users.
