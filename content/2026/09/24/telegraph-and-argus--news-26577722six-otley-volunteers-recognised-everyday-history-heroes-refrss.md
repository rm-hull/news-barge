Six heritage volunteers have been recognised as Everyday History Heroes.

The team from Otley Courthouse received the honour as part of the Everyday History Heroes competition, organised by Findmypast and Heritage Open Days.

The volunteers were presented with a plaque for their win.

This year’s Heritage Open Day at Otley Courthouse celebrated 170 years of the Otley Police Force.

Children enjoyed a hands-on police experience, having their photos and fingerprints taken for a souvenir “charge sheet”.

Two community police officers brought a van so children could try on uniforms and switch on its lights and siren.

Volunteer Dudley Nixon led a “Beat” walk, while Vickie Orton entertained visitors with stories.

More than 200 people visited Otley Museum’s courtroom display.

The volunteer Heritage Group has researched the lives of police officers, prisoners, cleaners, solicitors and residents linked to the building.
