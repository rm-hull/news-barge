A police vehicle was involved in a collision with a member of the public’s car.

A battered police vehicle could be seen in the area of Knowles Lane and Tong Street.

Knowles Lane is a mixed residential and industrial/commercial road in the Dudley Hill / Holme Wood area.

Close to Tong Street (A650) and Rooley Lane, Knowles Lane gives access to the Bradford ring road and the M606 motorway.

Police confirmed the driver of the public vehicle is being checked over "as a precaution".

## What police could say

A police spokesperson said: "This is a report of a police road traffic collision with a member of the public's car on Knowles Lane.

"The driver of the members of the public's car is being checked over as a precaution."
