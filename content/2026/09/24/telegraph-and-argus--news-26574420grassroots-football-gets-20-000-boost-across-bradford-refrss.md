GRASSROOTS football clubs across Bradford can now take advantage of a new £20,000 fund aimed at providing teams with equipment for the new season.

The West Riding County FA, the football governing body across the Bradford region, has partnered with sports education and equipment brand Davies Sports to give all affiliated clubs the opportunity to access high-quality equipment.

Clubs in Bradford need to register for the new grassroots football fund to be considered for a £100 credit voucher to use on equipment including footballs, football goals and matchday equipment.

Additional Davies Sports benefits which clubs can access by registering for the fund include an annual 20 per cent off voucher, exclusive club prices on equipment throughout the season and free delivery on orders above £40.

Affiliated clubs have until Wednesday, September 30 to sign up to the first part of the £20,000 annual grant, and can register through the link here.

The West Riding County FA is one of the largest county football associations in the country.

As well as Bradford and Craven, it oversees all aspects of the game across Leeds, Calderdale, Kirklees, Wakefield, Selby, and Harrogate.

Commenting on the grassroots fund, CEO of the West Riding County FA, Hannah Simpson, said: “We are delighted to partner with Davies Sports to provide this fantastic opportunity for our affiliated accredited clubs.

“Grassroots football thrives when clubs have the right resources in place, and this fund will help ensure our teams have access to quality equipment to support players, coaches and volunteers throughout the season.”

Davies Sports is a leading supplier of equipment and education resources to football clubs, as well as a range of education environments from primary schools up to universities.

The retailer’s range includes products from its wholly-owned sports equipment brand Shoshin, which are available for clubs to purchase at exclusive prices via the West Riding County FA fund.

Gareth Pashley, partnership and relationships marketing manager at Davies Sports, said: “We’re thrilled to support the West Riding County FA with this £20,000 fund to help its affiliated clubs across Bradford access high quality football equipment for the season ahead.

“As the largest county football associations in the county, West Riding County FA has a fantastic reputation for the level of support it provides its clubs and players. This partnership with Davies Sport is evidence of this in action.

“We encourage all affiliated grassroots teams in Bradford to register and take advantage of the fantastic benefits available on football equipment from Davies Sports to make their club stronger for the season ahead.”

The grassroots fund partnership between Davies Sports and the West Riding County FA is now open, with registration closing on Wednesday, September 30.

For more information and to register for the fund, search “Davies Sports West Riding FA grassroots fund” or visit this LINK.
