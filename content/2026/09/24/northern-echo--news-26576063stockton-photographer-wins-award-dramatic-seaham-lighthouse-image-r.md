A North East photographer has won a one-off award celebrating dramatic weather photography after capturing a striking image of the sea engulfing a harbour lighthouse.

Oliver Sherratt, from Stockton, earned the one-off 'Pressure' Award from the Royal Meteorological Society for his photograph, The Tempest, taken at Seaham Harbour in County Durham.

The image captures a dramatic moment as waves crash over the harbour lighthouse, highlighting the force and unpredictability of severe coastal weather.

Mr Sherratt said: "As there was a low tide, but the wind was up, I treated myself to a coffee at the aptly named Lookout.

"It provides a vantage point to capture the lighthouse relatively close up.

"For much of the time I could relax on the veranda watching the waves but every now and again – woosh! – the sea out of nowhere would swamp the lighthouse.

"Fortunately, I caught one of those occasions, albeit I spilt my coffee jumping up excitedly to take this shot."

![]()Pressure *(Image: Supplied)*

The winner was selected by Pressure director Anthony Maras to mark the release of Pressure, which is now showing in UK cinemas.

Pressure tells the story of Group Captain James Stagg, the meteorologist whose D-Day forecast helped shape the timing of the Normandy landings, and who later became President of the Royal Meteorological Society.

A spokesperson for the society said: "The ‘Pressure’ Award was created in collaboration with the Royal Meteorological Society as part of its Weather Photographer of the Year activity and judged for its cinematic impact, visual tension and connection to extreme weather.

"The link is especially fitting for RMetS: Pressure tells the story of Group Captain James Stagg, the meteorologist whose D-Day forecast helped shape the timing of the Normandy landings, and who later became President of the Royal Meteorological Society.

"Inspired by the themes of the film, the award highlights the power of weather to shape both real-life events and dramatic storytelling."

The Tempest was chosen by film director Anthony Maras.
