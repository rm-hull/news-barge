A sea of black and white flags will accompany live music, a football quiz and surprise guests in a one-off Stack event.

Wor Flags, the volunteer group behind the displays at the home of NUFC, is hosting a fundraising event at St James’ Stack on Sunday, October 4.

The event will feature live music, flag waving, surprise guests and a football quiz hosted by the BBC’s Matthew Bailey.

Claire James of Wor Flags said: "Everything Wor Flags does is made possible by the incredible support of Newcastle United fans.

"The displays at St James’ Park are created by the fans, for the fans, and our aim is always to help make matchdays special.

"This fundraiser is a fantastic opportunity for supporters to come together, have a great day, enjoy some brilliant entertainment and, most importantly, help us continue creating the displays that bring passion to St James’ Park."

Attendees can take part in a raffle and a live auction featuring signed NUFC memorabilia.

All funds raised will go towards supporting Wor Flags’ future displays.

As the group marks a decade of bringing colour and atmosphere to St James’ Park, Ms James said: "As we celebrate our 10-year anniversary, we’d love to meet as many people as possible and celebrate this milestone with the people who make Wor Flags what it is."

Tickets, available at buytickets.at/stjamesstack, cost £11 for adults, £5.75 for children aged three to 16, and £25 for a family ticket covering two adults and two children.

Chelsea Morton, marketing manager for sports and events at Stack, said the venue was honoured to host Wor Flags’ annual fundraising event.

Ms Morton said: "The fact that both Wor Flags and ourselves are so closely associated with NUFC means that we are the perfect venue to host this important event.

"Wor Flags does such an amazing job in uniting fans and showing how proud the city is of its team and its long football legacy.

"We hope people will turn out in force and ensure they raise a huge amount of money."
