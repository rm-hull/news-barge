EasyJet has unveiled its latest route from Newcastle Airport.

Flights to Larnaca, Cyprus, will begin on March 31, 2027, and will operate once a week on Wednesdays.

The new service is part of easyJet’s wider expansion at Newcastle Airport, following the launch of its base in March 2026.

![]()Old Town, Larnaca, Cyprus. *(Image: Agency)*

Kevin Doyle, UK country manager at easyJet, said: "We’re delighted to be offering another route and packages for customers in the North East from their local airport to Larnaca in Cyprus.

"We’re proud to have more than quadrupled our destination line-up from Newcastle since launching our base in March 2026, giving travellers even more choice, convenience and great value for next summer to book and fly with ease."

The Cyprus route brings easyJet’s total destinations from Newcastle to 32.

Larnaca is known for its beaches, lively seafront, and the nearby Salt Lake.

Flights and package holidays, including hotel, 23kg luggage, and transfers, are available through easyJet holidays.

EasyJet has expanded rapidly at Newcastle since establishing its base.

The airline has more than quadrupled its network from the airport and will launch nine new routes this winter, including Barcelona, Berlin, Copenhagen, Fuerteventura, Hurghada, Krakow, Marrakech, Reykjavik, and Rovaniemi.

Flights to Larnaca are now on sale through the easyJet website and mobile app.

Leon McQuaid, director of aviation development at Newcastle Airport, said: "It is fantastic to see further investment from easyJet at Newcastle Airport with the addition of an exciting new route to Larnaca in Cyprus for summer 2027.

"Today’s announcement, along with the nine new services this winter, provides even more choice for our passengers and further strengthens the airline’s growing network from the airport.

"We are confident all of easyJet’s new routes will prove to be hugely popular and we look forward to welcoming passengers onboard the first flights."
