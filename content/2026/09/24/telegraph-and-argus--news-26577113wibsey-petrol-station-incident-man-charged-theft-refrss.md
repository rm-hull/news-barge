A Bradford man has been charged following a reported theft at a petrol station in Wibsey.

The incident happened just before 5pm on September 20 on St Helena Road, near Wibsey Park.

West Yorkshire Police said suspects threatened staff and stole stock from a nearby petrol station.

A 43-year-old Bradford man was arrested in connection and has now been charged with theft.

The force said enquiries remain ongoing.

Photographs shared on social media suggest it is the Park & Shop petrol station - but police have not confirmed this.

## What police could say

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Enquiries remain ongoing following an incident at a petrol station in St Helena Road in Bradford in which suspects were reported to have threatened staff and stole stock.

"The incident took place at around 4.45pm on 20 September.

"A 43-year-old Bradford man was arrested in connection with the matter and has now been charged with theft.

"He will appear before Bradford Magistrates' Court on 16 November."
