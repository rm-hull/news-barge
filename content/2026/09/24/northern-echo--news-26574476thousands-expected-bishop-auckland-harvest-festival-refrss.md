Thousands of visitors are expected at Bishop Auckland this weekend as the town's annual Harvest Festival returns with a packed programme of growing, food and family activities.

The festival runs at Auckland Palace on September 26 and 27, from 11am to 4pm, with free entry to the grounds.

The weekend will feature horticulture, food, entertainment and hands-on activities for all ages.

![]()Bishop Auckland Harvest Festival returns to Auckland Palace with free family activities, food, music and a produce show. *(Image: Bec Hughes)*

Laura Roberts, creative producer at Hearth, said: "Harvest Festival is a celebration of the things people grow, make and share, and of the traditions that bring communities together.

"The Horticultural & Produce Show is at the heart of the weekend and we’re looking forward to welcoming growers, bakers, families and visitors back to Auckland Palace for two days of friendly competition, entertainment and hands-on activities."

The Bishop Auckland Horticultural & Produce Show is the festival’s centrepiece, running from 12pm to 4pm each day.

![]()Bishop Auckland Harvest Festival returns to Auckland Palace with free family activities, food, music and a produce show. *(Image: Bec Hughes)*

Local growers, bakers and makers can enter traditional horticultural and produce classes to show off their skills.

This year’s new attraction is the Plot to Plate: Potato Pot Luck competition.

Children who planted heritage potatoes at this year’s Bishop Auckland Food Festival can bring their crops for judging at 2pm on Saturday.

![]()Bishop Auckland Harvest Festival returns to Auckland Palace with free family activities, food, music and a produce show. *(Image: Bec Hughes)*

Ms Roberts said: "This year’s Plot to Plate challenge is particularly exciting because it connects Harvest Festival with the Bishop Auckland Food Festival earlier in the year.

"We hope the children who planted their heritage potatoes have been busy looking after them over the summer and will bring their crops back to see how they measure up."

The Growlympic Games return with countryside challenges including welly wanging, harvest crown making and apple pressing.

Traditional games such as Hook-a-Duck and Roll-a-Ball will also be available.

![]()Bishop Auckland Harvest Festival returns to Auckland Palace with free family activities, food, music and a produce show. *(Image: Bec Hughes)*

Live performances and roaming acts will entertain visitors throughout the weekend.

The Ran Tanners will perform Georgian and Victorian music, while the Fairly Famous Family’s giant comedy gardeners stage wheelbarrow tricks and lawn games.

Their Grow Your Own Show combines comedy, fruit juggling and vegetable cookery, with a special appearance by Peter Rabbit.

Saturday’s events will also be part of the Bishop Auckland Heritage Festival, bringing the town’s history and stories into the harvest celebrations.

Food and drink will include Palace Pizza Van fare, plus fresh bread, pies, doughnuts and sweet treats from the Farm Shop.

The festival is part of Hearth, a community regeneration programme run with The Auckland Project and supported by SHED.

It is the second in a series of Beacon Events aimed at bringing people together and encouraging creativity.

All activities in the grounds are free, but visitors need a ticket to enter Auckland Palace itself.

The Bishop’s Kitchen café will be open to all visitors without a palace admission ticket.
