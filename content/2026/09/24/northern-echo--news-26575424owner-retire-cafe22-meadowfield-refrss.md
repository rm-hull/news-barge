A popular Durham café is to welcome new bosses after its longstanding owner announced she will retire following more than 13 years behind the counter.

Keely Kay, who owns Cafe22 at Unit 22, City West Business Park, Meadowfield, will retire on September 28.

But customers have been assured the café will stay open under new bosses.

In an emotional message, Keely thanked the community for supporting Cafe22 since it opened and reminisced on the friendships and memories made there.

She said: "After more than thirteen happy years, it's now time for me to hang up my apron, and say goodbye as I retire on Monday. September 28.

"But it's certainly not goodbye for Cafe22."

Keely said the café has become much more than a business, filled with "fab food, laughter, friendship and memories".

Thanking her loyal customers, she said: "You, our fabulous loyal customers, have been the reason I have been here for thirteen years and I want to thank every one of you for making me smile, laugh and enjoy every day."

She also praised current and former staff members for their dedication over the years, giving special thanks to long-serving colleague Leanne for her support throughout the café's journey.

Keely revealed that team members will take over as owners, keeping Cafe22 open for customers.
