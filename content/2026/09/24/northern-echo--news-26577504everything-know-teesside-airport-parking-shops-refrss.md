If you need an autumn/winter holiday to chase the sun or enjoy a cosy getaway with family, friends, a partner, or solo, consider flying from Teesside International Airport.

Opened in the 1960s and easily accessible from areas across the North East, it offers a range of flight options to exciting places.

Recently, it was ranked as one of the best in the UK for cheap drop-off fares as well as flights being on time. Plus, it has previously clinched the title of Best UK Airport for Leisure Travel.

Smaller in size, it is easy to get around with plenty to offer including shops and plenty of food and drink spots.

If you are planning on travelling from Teesside and need any information, we have it all.

Read on to find out everything you need to know.

### Where is Teesside International Airport?

![]() *(Image: Getty)*

Teesside Airport is just a stone’s throw away from major north-south and east-west connections of the A1(M) and A66.

No matter where you live in Tees Valley, you’ll never be more than 45 minutes away from our doorstep.

It is just 35 minutes from Hartlepool and Redcar, 20 minutes from Middlesbrough and around 15 minutes from Darlington and Stockton.

Teesside Airport is also just over 50 minutes from the centre of Newcastle and 75 minutes from Leeds.

The airport’s postcode is DL2 1LU.

### Nearest train station to Teesside Airport

The nearest train station is Darlington, just a 12-minute drive from the airport. The station also has a dedicated taxi rank.

It is served by the East Coast Main Line and has regular connections to Edinburgh, Newcastle, York and London as well as regional links to Saltburn, Redcar, Middlesbrough and Bishop Auckland.

### Parking at Teesside Airport

Parking for the airport is located directly opposite the terminal building, so there is no need for shuttle buses or waiting.

The car park is monitored 24/7 and has also been awarded safe parking status by Park Mark, which assesses car parks every year on their safety.

### Price for parking

The airport has a number of different tariffs for airport parking, so whether it is for pick-up or drop-off, or you are looking to park your car for a number of days while you are on holiday, there are options for everyone.

Passengers who have not booked online can pay by credit or debit card as well as cash at any of the pay-on-foot machines within the arrivals area of the terminal building.

### Where can you fly to from Teesside Airport - and airlines available

There is a small selection of flights that travel from the airport, with Amsterdam serving as the primary hub for global onward connections.

As well as Amsterdam, the airport offers flights to Alicante, Malaga, Faro, Palma de Mallorca, Corfu, Jersey, and Antalya, operated mainly by KLM, Ryanair, Aurigny, and MGA Airlines.

### Lapland added to the roster

Recently, Teesside Airport confirmed a magical day trip to Lapland from a North East airport.

Travel company Transun has added a direct flight from Teesside Airport to Swedish Lapland on Sunday, December 13, after huge demand.

Passengers will fly from Teesside Airport to the Arctic for a Christmas-themed day trip before returning home that evening.

Visitors can enjoy a reindeer sleigh ride, husky sledding and a snowmobile trip through the snowy Lapland landscape.

The trip also includes a private meeting with Santa, giving families the opportunity to meet Father Christmas in his Lapland home ahead of the festive season.

### One of UK's most accessible airports

Teesside Airport has secured the same top rating for a second year running as part of a push to become one of the UK’s most accessible regional airports.

The CAA’s Airport Accessibility Performance Framework was introduced in 2014 and sets out clear standards for how airports should assist, from free help moving through terminals to boarding and disembarking aircraft.

The CAA’s latest Aviation Consumer Survey found overall passenger satisfaction with air travel at 88 per cent – the highest level since 2016.

### Hand luggage liquid restrictions for Teesside Airport

As of 2026, Teesside Airport still has a 100ml limit on liquids in hand luggage.

Passengers are advised to pack liquids in their hold luggage where possible, as there are no restrictions in checked bags.

If you do need to carry liquids onboard, each item must be in a container of no more than 100ml.

### Food, drink and shopping at Teesside Airport

There are several ways to stay entertained while waiting for your gate to be called at Teesside Airport.

If you're looking for a bite to eat, there is Landside Cafe, SkyBar, The Transporter Bar & Kitchen, The Ramside Lounge or The Goosepool Bar.

For some retail therapy, pop into Teesside Boutique, World Duty Free or News & Essential by Linthorpe Pharmacy.
