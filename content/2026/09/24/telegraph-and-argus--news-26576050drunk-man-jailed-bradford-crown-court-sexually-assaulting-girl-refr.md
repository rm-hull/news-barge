A drunk who sexually assaulted a young girl in her own bed has been jailed for 51 months at Bradford Crown Court.

Jacob Brown claimed to have no recollection of entering a strange house and climbing into a bed that was already occupied by a father and two of his children.

But whilst in that bed Brown, now 24, sexually assaulted the girl by touching her breasts and genitalia under her clothing over a period of around five minutes.

Prosecutor Harry Crowson told the court how the child called out to her father, and it “became obvious that there was someone else in the bed”.

Mr Crowson said: “The defendant said sorry. He did not know where he was and it was very obvious that he was intoxicated.”

After he was “extricated” from the property, Brown asked the victim’s father to put his phone in a bin where he could collect it later.

Afterwards the girl’s father said she was very, very shocked, her heart was beating very fast, and she was shivering, shaking, and could not speak at all.

The court heard that Brown left his phone behind following the incident, in the early hours of November 30, 2024, and that he was traced through it to his partner’s address, where he was arrested.

He accepted being the man responsible and that he thought he was going to a friend’s house who left the door open.

The court heard that the girl’s family had since had to move out of the area as she did not feel safe or secure.

The court heard that Brown, of Burley Road, Warley, Halifax, who had no previous convictions, said that he was not targeting a child for sexual gratification.

He did not know the family concerned.

He pleaded guilty prior to a trial to two counts of sexual assault on a girl under 13.

Mitigating, Gerald Hendron described the incident as “in any view, an unusual set of circumstances” and that Brown had drunk “way beyond excess” as he abused alcohol and cannabis.

He had “no recollection” of the incident.

Mr Hendron said: “He accepts [that] he is responsible for the damage and the harm that resulted that night.

“The defendant could not believe that he would have acted in the way that was outlined.”

He said Brown pleaded guilty after hearing the girl’s video recorded interview and “did not bury his head in the sand.”

Mr Hendron added: “He wanted to express his genuine sorrow not only to the girl but to the family for all that they had been through.

“He hopes that the victim is able to recover unblemished.”

Sentencing Brown to 51 months’ imprisonment concurrent on each count, His Honour Judge Jonathan Carroll said Brown did not spare the victim the trauma of having to relive the incident by giving evidence.

And he said Brown made a choice to sexually molest the child as she was in her bed.

He told him: “That, even in your drunken state, was an act of choice.

“Having discovered you found yourself in somebody else’s house that you had no business being in, you could have walked away.

“You didn’t.

“It is certainly something more than mere randomness.

“You made a choice — a drunken choice — to sexually molest that child.”

He added: “The poor child has been left absolutely devastated.”

Judge Carroll told Brown he would serve half of his sentence in custody before being released, and that he would have to register as a sex offender until further order.
