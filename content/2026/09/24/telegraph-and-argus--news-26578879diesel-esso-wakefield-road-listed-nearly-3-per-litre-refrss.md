A driver was shocked to see a Bradford petrol station’s diesel price on his way to work this morning.

The Londis Esso on Wakefield Road in Bowling displayed diesel at 299.9p per litre at 3.40am on Thursday, September 24.

It is unclear whether the diesel was mispriced.

![]()The petrol station in the Bowling / East Bowling area.

The commuter told the T&A: "I was driving to work this morning around 3.40am, and every day when I pass the station, I look to see what’s changed.

"I actually stopped on purpose just to take a picture."

![]()A photo of the sign in the early hours of this morning (September 24).

The UK average diesel price is currently about 195.5p per litre according to the latest weekly UK Government fuel price statistics (week ending 21 September 2026).

UK fuel prices are currently high because the ongoing Middle East war has severely disrupted global oil supplies, pushed oil prices above $100 a barrel, and created a diesel shortage, all of which are being passed on to drivers at the pump.

The T&A tried to contact the petrol station by phone.

We would welcome a response from the petrol station and diesel supplier should they wish to respond to our query.
