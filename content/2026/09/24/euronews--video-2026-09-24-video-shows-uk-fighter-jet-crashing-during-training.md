Video footage shows a British air force Hawk fighter jet crashing during a training exercise in north Wales, with what appears to be two pilots ejecting moments before the aircraft hits the ground and bursts into flames.

A British air force Hawk fighter jet has crashed during a training exercise in north Wales. UGC footage appears to show the aircraft flying low before suddenly nosediving and crashing in flames.

Two pilots can be seen ejecting from the jet moments before the crash. UK Defence Minister Wes Streeting said an investigation is underway. The incident happened on Wednesday on the island of Anglesey.
