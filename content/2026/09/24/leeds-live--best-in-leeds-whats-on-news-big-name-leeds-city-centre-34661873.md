# Big name Leeds city centre closure as message issued to customers

## A message has been issued to customers following the closure

A big name shop in Leeds city centre has closed down.

Many will be disappointed to see it shut. The branch of Pound Bakery in Kirkgate has ceased operating.

A notice is in the window of the now vacant unit. It reads: “We apologise for any inconvenience and thank you for your support.”

We have also seen a Pound Bakery store in Sheffield city centre close in recent weeks. While, elsewhere in Leeds city centre, we have said goodbye to Gant based at Victoria Leeds.

It notice reads: “It’s time to say goodbye to our Leeds store and we’d like to thank all of our customers for their continued custom and support. We kindly invite you to visit your nearest Gant store which you can find using the store locator on our website.”
