## Top York school placed into lockdown after police incident

St Peter's School in York was placed into a lockdown after a police incident *(Image: Newsquest)i>*

A PRIVATE school in York has been placed into a lockdown following a police incident.

St Peter’s School, in Clifton, said it had been placed into a lockdown as a “precautionary measure” following information received by the school from North Yorkshire Police.

Officers attended the school on Thursday (September 24).




Pupils and their families have been notified that there may be an increased police presence around the site over the coming days.

A school spokesperson said: “This is a precautionary measure and should provide reassurance that appropriate steps are being taken to ensure the continued safety of our community.”

The school added that “at no point” had there been any confirmed threat to pupils, staff or visitors.

![]()St Peter's School in York was placed into a lockdown after a police incident *(Image: Newsquest)*

The lockdown, it said, had been implemented “swiftly and calmly”, with pupils “safe, supervised and settled throughout”.

It said: “We took immediate action in line with our established safeguarding and emergency procedures.

“The safety and wellbeing of our pupils and staff is always our highest priority.

“Following appropriate checks and advice from the police, the lockdown was lifted, and the school returned to normal operations.

“We appreciate that any mention of a lockdown may cause concern.

“Please be reassured that these procedures are designed to be precautionary, allowing us to respond quickly and appropriately whenever there is any potential concern, however remote.

“We would like to thank our pupils and staff for the calm, sensible, and mature manner in which they responded, and we are grateful for your continued support and understanding.”
