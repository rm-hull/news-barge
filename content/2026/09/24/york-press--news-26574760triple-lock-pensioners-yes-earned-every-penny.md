**Why York doesn't deserve prized UNESCO status**

I AM grateful to Quentin McDonald for his 100 per cent support that York is a dirty shabby mess that needs much improvement.

The former York councillor expands on the subject referring to the Historic Urban Core being put on the UNESCO Tentative List of World Heritage Sites.

He asks why there is a hold up in the designation and suggests the Mayor David Skaith should direct tourist tax funds to addressing issues that may be preventing UNESCO status.

York’s Historic Core does not deserve to be recognised as a World Heritage Site of Outstanding Universal Value when the ‘Powers that Be’ allow it to be blighted by a stack of completely alien unsightly shipping containers inside the city walls on a site owned by that very same local authority.

**Where is the evidence?**

MATTHEW Laverack's letter (The Press, September 22) calls kosher slaughter "barbaric" and an "abomination" – strong words, offered without a single citation.

He gives us no academic or scientific credentials, and no reference to any peer-reviewed research supporting his claims.

The actual peer-reviewed evidence tells a different story. Studies recently published in Frontiers in Veterinary Science and the American Journal of Veterinary Research report rapid loss of consciousness and a near-instantaneous collapse in cerebral blood flow and pressure following carotid sectioning.

This evidence undercuts the claim that residual blood flow through the vertebral arteries can sustain consciousness after the cut, and a related systematic review cautions against treating neurological signs of nociception as proof of conscious pain.

None of this supports Mr Laverack's claim that kosher slaughter is inherently less humane simply because it omits a separate mechanical pre-stun. If he wants to make that case, he should engage with the science – not just assert it.

*Shimon Cohen,*

*Shechita UK,*

*Ballards Lane,*

*London*
