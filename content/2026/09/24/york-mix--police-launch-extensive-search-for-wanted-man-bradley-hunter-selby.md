This is Bradley Richard Hunter.

He’s, 32, from Selby – and he’s wanted by police.

Hunter is wanted for breaching a restraining order, failing to attend court and failing to comply with bail conditions and appointments.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Extensive enquiries have been ongoing to locate him and we’re now appealing for your help to help find him

“We believe that Mr Hunter could be in York and he also has links to the Tadcaster, Howden, Goole and Wakefield areas.”

If you have an immediate sighting of Hunter or know where he is now, you’re asked to call 999.

Ring police on 101 with other information.

Alternatively, you can pass on information anonymously at Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or via their website.
