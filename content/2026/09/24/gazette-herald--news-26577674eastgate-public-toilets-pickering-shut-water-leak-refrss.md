PUBLIC toilets in a North Yorkshire town are shut this morning (Thursday).

Pickering's Eastgate toilets are temporarily closed as there has been a water leak in the electrics.

Cllr Joy Andrews, said: "I have been told that the issue will be looked at today. Hopefully this will be fixed ASAP - thank you for your patience - I know it is frustrating as these toilets are very well used but public safety is paramount."
