Nato aircraft were deployed over Polish and Romanian airspace overnight and on Wednesday in response to the latest wave of Russian drone and missile attacks on Ukraine.

Russia pounded Kyiv with drones and ballistic missiles, with falling debris causing damage at a maternity hospital in the capital. At least seven people, including two in Kyiv and five in the eastern ‌Donetsk region, have been killed in the attacks.

Poland scrambled fighter jets “to ensure safety” due to Russian "strikes using jet-powered unmanned aerial vehicles on targets in western Ukraine” but said no violations of its airspace were recorded. The alert ended after about an hour.

He also spoke out against the use of AI in war, and warned: “The longer Putin can wage war, the more evil he will leave behind.”

## Zelensky says Russia launched ballistic missile strike on Kyiv as people were sleeping

Volodymyr Zelensky said Russia launched a ballistic missile strike on Kyiv as he met members of the US Congress to discuss sanctions against Moscow.

The Ukrainian president said the overnight attack targeted Kyiv and civilian infrastructure, including residential buildings, a maternity hospital, energy infrastructure and logistics.

“As people were sleeping, flashes from explosions lit up the sky. Once again, the main targets of their strike were Kyiv and civilian infrastructure, residential buildings, a maternity hospital, energy infrastructure, and logistics,” he said on X.

## Nato aircraft deployed over Poland and Romania amid Russian strikes on Ukraine

Nato aircraft were deployed over Polish and Romanian airspace overnight and on Wednesday morning as Russia launched another wave of drone and missile attacks on Ukraine.

Ukraine’s air force said Russia launched 161 drones, including 82 jet-powered drones, and an unspecified number of Banderol cruise missiles.

Poland’s Armed Forces Operational Command said Polish fighter jets were scrambled “to ensure safety” amid Russian strikes using jet-powered drones against targets in western Ukraine.

The alert lasted about an hour, with Polish authorities saying no violation of Polish airspace was recorded.

Romania also scrambled two F-16 fighter jets after an aerial target was detected near the Ukrainian border.

Romania’s defence ministry said the aircraft responded to a target manoeuvring near Valkove, on Ukrainian territory, close to the Romanian border. The alert ended about an hour later, with authorities reporting no incursion into Romanian airspace.

## Ukraine sent two North Korean prisoners of war to South Korea

Ukraine has sent two North Korean prisoners of war to South Korea, Ukrainian Volodymyr Zelensky told the UN General Assembly on Wednesday, following months of consultations between Kyiv and Seoul after the soldiers were captured in Russia's Kursk region in 2025.

"These guys are not easy to capture alive," he said. "One of them, when he realised he was going to be taken prisoner, tried to kill himself and he was shocked that fate didn't let him die. This is how they raise people in the North."

Following the soldiers' capture in Kursk in January 2025, South Korea urged Kyiv not to send them back to North Korea, where they could face persecution, and said it was willing to accept them if they wished to resettle in the South.

South Korea's Foreign Ministry did not directly confirm Zelenskyy's comments but said it had been consulting with Kyiv and other relevant parties to resolve the matter in a manner that respects the soldiers' free will and complies with international law and humanitarian principles.

The ministry said in a statement that it would not confirm details about the soldiers' "disposition, the timing or route of their transfer or specific contents of consultations" with Ukraine, calling it a sensitive matter concerning the safety of the soldiers and their families.

"Ukrainians understand that this winter could be very harsh for us. But in response, we will have no choice but to make it painful for Russia too," Zelensky told the UN General ⁠Assembly.

Although Russia ​has ⁠a far bigger arsenal of missiles which it has used to pound its enemy throughout the war, Ukraine has developed and used thousands of long-range drones to strike back at Russian warehouses, port infrastructure and oil refineries.

"That’s exactly why we ⁠say that de-escalation steps are needed before winter," Zelensky said.
