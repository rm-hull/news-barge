Emergency services have been called to a large fire in a wooded area near York.

Two fire crews were called to the blaze in Kexby at 5.30pm on Wednesday (September 23).

The fire measured around 50m x 10m in the woodland area, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

A service spokesperson said the crews used hose reel jets to extinguish and dampen down the area.

Firefighters also used a thermal imaging camera to check for hot spots, they said.
