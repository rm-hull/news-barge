A new County Durham shop selling American professional sports merchandise will open next month, with fans expected to travel from across the UK for its launch.

Vintage World Order will open on October 3 on Langley Moor High Street as Durham's first shop dedicated to the NFL, NHL, NBA and MLB.

The business is run by couple Sharon Cotterell and Paul Frampton, whose passion for American sports has grown from a lifelong hobby into a full-time venture.

![]()Vintage World order will open its doors next month. *(Image: SHARON COTTERELL)*

Paul began collecting at nine and spent decades building a vast haul of sports clothing and memorabilia.

Sharon said: "Once our children left home it kind of exploded. We had a spare bedroom and it all came down from upstairs and I said, 'You either start selling or stop collecting'."

![]() *(Image: SHARON COTTERELL)*

The personal collection soon became a business.

Sharon added: "We had a storage unit which became huge and we started selling on WhatNot and eBay Live. Then we started doing student markets up and down the country.

"People kept asking us if we had a shop and that kind of planted the seed in our minds."

The pair have built a successful online business under the Vintage World Order name, but this will be their first shop.

![]() *(Image: SHARON COTTERELL)*

Sharon left her job as a support worker to join the business full-time.

The couple say working together is one of the biggest rewards of running the company.

Paul said: "It's lovely because we're able to work together, we live stream together, we go sourcing together, we do the shop out together."

The shop will stock jerseys, caps, clothing, collectibles and memorabilia from teams across America's biggest professional sports leagues.

![]() *(Image: SHARON COTTERELL)*

Sharon wants the shop to be more than a place to buy sports gear.

She said: "We only sell NFL, NHL, NBA and MLB stock. We have a lot of interest with people travelling from all over the UK for the opening day.

"We are very happy with the progress so far.

"We want the shop to be a destination rather than just a shop.

"We love all US pro sports and we have made a shop we would like to visit."

Interest has grown during the refurbishment as regular social media updates draw fans from across the country.

Sharon said: "We've been sorting the shop out for the past 22 days and the amount of people who have accidentally wandered in because they can't wait."

American sports, particularly the NFL, have surged in popularity in the UK, and the pair believe fans want a specialist shop.

Sharon added: "We hope you all like it and we can't wait to welcome you all here very soon.

"Thanks for all the lovely comments and support."
