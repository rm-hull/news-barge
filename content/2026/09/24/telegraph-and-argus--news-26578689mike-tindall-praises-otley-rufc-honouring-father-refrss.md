Former England rugby star Mike Tindall has praised Otley RUFC for honouring his father during a match.

Otley RUFC, where Phil was once captain, held the tribute before their match against Chester after his death aged 79.

Phil Tindall, a former fly-half and centre, made his first-team debut for Otley in the 1963–64 season and captained the club in 1972–73.

He retired from rugby in 1974 because of a knee injury.

The club described him as "one of five members of the Tindall family to play rugby for Otley RUFC."

A spokesman for Otley RUFC said: "We are deeply saddened to share the news of the passing of Phil Tindall, a former Otley RUFC captain and player.

"Phil passed away after a 25-year battle with Parkinson’s disease.

"Phil was one of five members of the Tindall family to play rugby for Otley RUFC.

"A talented fly-half and centre, he made his first-team debut aged 17 in the 1963–64 season and helped Otley reach the Yorkshire Cup Final against Halifax that same year."

Prior to last Saturday's game, the club said: "Our thoughts are with all of Phil’s family and friends, and we will be holding a minute's silence at the Otley v Chester game to honour his passing."

His son, former England international Mike Tindall, thanked the club on his podcast, The Good, The Bad & The Rugby.

Mr Tindall said: "Big shout-out to Otley Rugby Club.

"They played Chester on the weekend, and they did a minute’s silence to my dad, who was captain in ’72/73.

"That little moment, I got to watch it.

"Thank you very much to Otley Rugby Club for it."

Phil Tindall was diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease in 2003.

Mr Tindall previously described the condition as "brutal," and became a patron of Cure Parkinson’s in 2018.

Phil married his wife Linda in 1969, and Otley RUFC reported he "limped down the aisle" after breaking his leg in a match the week before.

He worked for Barclays Bank in Otley, Wakefield and Ripon during his career.

Phil played much of his rugby at Otley alongside Harry Melville, a gifted winger.

Both men would later see their sons represent England at international level.

The Otley spokesperson said: "Remarkably, both men had sons who went on to play international rugby for England; one became a World Cup winner, while the other represented the British Lions."

Mike Tindall, who earned 75 caps for England between 2000 and 2011, has been married to Zara Tindall, the eldest niece of King Charles, for 15 years.

Mike Tindall was born in Otley and moved away at the age of three when his father's work took the family to Ripon and later the Wakefield area.
