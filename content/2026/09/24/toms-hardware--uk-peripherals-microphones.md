After years without a major successor, Logitech’s Yeti 2 arrives with new features designed to simplify microphone setup and audio adjustments.

![Best Microphones](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/PxNMbJZVwCHCdjyq8By7TX-320-80.png)

Whether you're chatting with teammates or streaming on Twitch, these are the best gaming microphones to buy in 2026. While the majority connect via USB-C, some also feature XLR connectivity.

![Razer Seiren V3 Pro](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/WBJLXURfUDj7yNSRyZVTY8-320-80.jpg)

Razer's new Seiren V3 Pro is an end-address mic with both USB-C and XLR connectivity, and it also supports 32-bit float.

![HyperX FlipCast](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ib5zFJQvSrukmZsBTbtapW-320-80.jpg)

The HyperX FlipCast is an end-address professional-quality microphone with both USB-C and XLR connectivity and tons of on-mic controls. It looks and sounds good, but is it worth the $230 price tag?

![HyperX SoloCast 2](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/p2XZqXPCDsqhrJDZDzFVYe-320-80.jpg)

The HyperX SoloCast 2 is an impressive $60 cardioid mic with a clever built-in stand, but you'll still want to mount it on a boom arm for the best results.

![Hollyland Lark M2 Wireless Lavalier Microphone](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/GDE5YpnRVRc3Ef5cf4LWpH-320-80.jpg)

The Hollyland Lark M2 Wireless Lavalier Microphone is now cheaper than when I bought it in the last sale!

![Elgato Wave Neo](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/UGE7EsqELS4U2HMACKrYZG-320-80.jpg)

The Elgato Wave Neo is a sub-$100 plug-and-play USB mic that's easy to set up — maybe a little too easy.

![HyperX QuadCast 2 S](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/4bD28YsDbAuJPNtdFWpbPm-320-80.jpg)

The HyperX QuadCast 2 S brings a long-awaited update to the iconic gaming mic with unnecessarily high sample/bit rate and a redesigned shock mount.

![HyperX Quadcast 2 S](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/KwpjPGB6w895jhWCYLgioj-320-80.jpg)

HyperX introduces QuadCast 2 and QuadCast 2 S gaming mics with high-res recording resolution and modest redesign.

![Real Deals](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/6P5E94rPedZc4JXkNwfdpg-320-80.jpg)

Available on Amazon in a limited-time deal, Logitech's G Yeti GX USB microphone hits an all-time low price of $119.
