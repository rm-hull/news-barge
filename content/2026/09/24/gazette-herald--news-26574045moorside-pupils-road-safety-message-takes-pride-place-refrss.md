A WINNING road safety sign design is now on display in a new housing development.

The sign, created by 10-year-old Arthur from Moorside Primary School, is now installed at Taylor Wimpey’s Fountains Walk development in Ripon, following a competition to promote safer driving in the area.

Claire Rowett, headteacher at Moorside Primary School, said: "Partnering with Taylor Wimpey on the competition has given our pupils a great opportunity to learn about the importance of road safety, which we are very grateful for.

"We are so proud of Arthur and pleased to see his design now installed at Fountains Walk.

"The competition has highlighted how important it is to encourage parents and other drivers to think twice about the way they drive."

The competition was organised by Taylor Wimpey North Yorkshire to involve young people in raising awareness about road safety.

Nicki Hope, sales and marketing director at Taylor Wimpey North Yorkshire, said: "It was great to see the pupils of Moorside Primary School taking an active role to help encourage drivers to slow down in and around Fountains Walk.

"We were really impressed by the creativity of all the pupils who entered the competition, with Arthur demonstrating a real understanding of how to get his speed awareness message across."
