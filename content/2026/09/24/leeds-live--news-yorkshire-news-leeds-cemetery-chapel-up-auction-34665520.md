# Leeds cemetery chapel up for auction could be turned into homes

## The chapel is being offered with a guide price of £175,000-plus

A chapel has been put up for auction as part of a disposal of cemetery buildings aimed at generating funds for the council.

Harehills Cemetery Chapel is being offered with a guide price of £175,000-plus.

The Kimberley Road structure is one of four chapels deemed surplus to requirements by council officials who stated they were underutilised and too expensive to maintain.

Chapels in Armley, Horsforth and Otley were also earmarked for sale, Leeds City Council confirmed.

Horsforth's Victorian cemetery chapel, located on Calverley Lane, has already been listed on the BTD Eddisons auction platform with a guide price of £50,000-plus.

A listing on the same platform for the Harehills property stated: "Requiring renovation, the property offers potential for alternative uses or redevelopment, subject to the necessary consents.

"The property occupies an attractive and unique position within Harehills Cemetery and comprises a single-storey former chapel with considerable character."

Planning consent would be required to redevelop the empty chapel. Prospective buyers were advised to seek guidance from the local authority.

The auction listing noted: "The building requires renovation but provides an interesting opportunity to create a distinctive new use or scheme.

"Harehills is situated approximately two miles north-east of Leeds city centre, with a range of local amenities nearby and good access to the wider road network."

A council document confirmed sales of the four chapels would be conducted on a 250-year leasehold basis. An auction was considered the most effective method of achieving the highest possible sale price.

The report stated: "It is likely that the new owners will redevelop the property, which could result in new employment and residential opportunities which are known to bring health and well-being benefits.

"A sale will generate capital receipts for the council and alleviate financial pressure due to continued maintenance and running costs."

BTG Eddisons confirmed that bids for the Harehills chapel would be accepted until October 14.
