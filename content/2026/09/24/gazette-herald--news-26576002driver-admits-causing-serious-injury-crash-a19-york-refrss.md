A PICK-UP truck driver has admitted causing a crash that left a motorcyclist fighting for her life in hospital.

Kathryn Walters, prosecuting, said Barry Lofthouse, 62, didn’t give way at the junction of Howard Drive and Shipton Road, Rawcliffe.

His Ford pick-up truck crashed into the motorcyclist on her Yamaha motorbike at 7.47pm on March 15, 2024.

Lofthouse, who lives in Doe Park, Rawcliffe, pleaded guilty to causing serious injury by careless driving.

York magistrates adjourned sentence for a pre-sentence report and released him on unconditional bail.

They also banned him from driving until the sentencing hearing on November 3.

Shipton Road, the main road heading north out of York, was closed for seven hours by the crash and police said at the time the motorcyclist suffered life-threatening injuries.
