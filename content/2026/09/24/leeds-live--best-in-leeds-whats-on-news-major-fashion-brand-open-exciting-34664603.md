# Major fashion brand to open exciting new store in Leeds city centre

## Work is ongoing ahead of the new opening launching in Leeds

A major new fashion store will soon open in Leeds city centre.

Arne has signed for its third permanent UK store at Victoria Leeds, the lifestyle destination owned and operated by Redical, as the brand continues its retail expansion eight years on from its launch. Arne will occupy a 3,658 sq ft store located within Victoria Quarter.

Customers will be able to explore Arne's full offer across menswear, womenswear and footwear. James Edmondson, asset manager at Redical, commented: “Victoria Leeds remains distinct in its ability to secure brands with outstanding reputations and the quality offer to match. The destination continues to evolve because we've accumulated the best of the best tenants, and the signing of Arne is entirely in keeping with this strategy.

“It not only reflects Victoria Leeds' position as a prime asset, but also the intelligent leasing undertaken to give visitors the continuously refreshed, leading lifestyle destination they demand."
