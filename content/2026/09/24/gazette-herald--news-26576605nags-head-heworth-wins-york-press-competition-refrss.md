A SUBURBAN pub has been named York’s best, just a year after new management took over.

The Nags Head in Heworth Road was crowned the winner of The Press’ Best Pub 2026 competition, earning top spot from a shortlist of 10 which attracted votes from readers across York in a postal competition.

The pub, run by Ryan Cooper and Ellie Hughes, has quickly made its mark.




Ryan said: "Winning Best Pub 2026 is an absolutely amazing achievement for everyone here at The Nags Head, and we’re incredibly proud to receive the award.

"When we took over the pub, we wanted to create somewhere that felt welcoming to everyone – a proper community pub where locals, families, students, sports fans, workers and visitors could all come in, feel comfortable and enjoy themselves.

"To see that recognised by the people who voted means a huge amount to us."

![]()The owners said they've only had the pub open for just over a year and the progress has been amazing *(Image: Supplied)*

Since reopening under new leadership, the pub has focused on community engagement and charity events, recently marking its first anniversary with a fundraiser for the MND Association that raised £1,108.

### 'We genuinely couldn’t have achieved this without them'

Ellie said: "We had live music, our Speed Quiz, a raffle and plenty of other entertainment throughout the day.

"It was a fantastic celebration and a huge success - being able to celebrate our first year while raising money for such an important cause made it even more meaningful."

This latest award marks the second major recognition for The Nags Head in just 12 months, and the team credits its success to both staff and supporters.

Ryan said: "A massive thank you has to go to our staff.

"They work incredibly hard every day and put so much effort, personality and care into making The Nags Head what it is.

"We genuinely couldn’t have achieved this without them.

"We also want to say a huge thank you to our regulars and everyone who has supported us, recommended us, attended our events, enjoyed our food and drinks, and continued to walk through our doors.

"This award belongs to you just as much as it does to us."

The pub is set for further improvements, with a major refurbishment recently approved by the brewery.

The team said the venue will remain open during the works, promising to keep the "friendly, welcoming atmosphere" that has earned them the city’s top pub title.
