Concerned residents rushed to the scene after a car crashed into the wall of a house near Darlington, leaving the vehicle badly damaged.

Police attended the crash on the A167 at Hurworth at around 5.30pm on Thursday (September 24), with part of the road affected while officers dealt with the incident.

Pictures from the scene show a dark blue Volkswagen smashed in the wall of a property, causing significant damage to both the car and the pavement.

A bollard was also knocked over.

![]()A marked and unmarked police car are on the scene on the A167 in Hurworth where a VW has crashed into a wall. *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The car appears to have suffered extensive damage, with large dents visible along the side panels, the front bonnet crumpled and airbags deployed inside the vehicle.

A marked police car and an unmarked police vehicle were at the scene as officers spoke with residents involved.

The road remained open, although traffic was being diverted by police.

![]()A marked and unmarked police car are on the scene on the A167 in Hurworth where a VW has crashed into a wall. *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Several residents gathered nearby after hearing a “loud bang.”

One resident said: “I was in my flat and I heard this huge bang. I came out and saw the car had gone into the building.
