A York school was put into lockdown after a threat was made to pupils.

Police were called to St Peter’s School, Clifton, today (Thursday) following to reports of a threat made to students .

It happened as they “struck up a conversation with a woman believed to be camping or living rough near to the school grounds,” a North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said.

#### St Peter’s School, York

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/st-peters-school-york-2025-sign.webp)

All students were kept inside the school as a security measure while police carried out an investigation.

“An extensive search was carried out. However the woman was no longer in the immediate area and officers were satisfied that the precautionary safety measures could be lifted,” the spokesperson said.

“Enquiries remain ongoing to trace the person involved.”

Anyone with information that can help the police investigation is asked to contact police – details below.
