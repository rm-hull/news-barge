Adults across East Riding are being urged to take up free courses to build confidence, gain qualifications and improve their job prospects during Adult Learning Week.

The campaign runs from September 21 to 27 and is backed by East Riding of Yorkshire Council’s Adult Learning and Employability service, which offers courses to residents aged 19 and over.

Adult Learning Week gives residents the chance to improve everyday skills, return to learning, gain qualifications or move towards work.

Courses are available in Beverley, Bridlington, Cottingham and Goole, as well as online.

The programme helps adults improve their English, maths or digital skills, prepare for work or gain qualifications.

One learner who has benefited from the scheme is Mark Evans, who said: "Doing this course has been absolutely fantastic, I couldn’t praise it enough.

"You’re never too old to learn new things – every day is a learning experience."
