A car was vandalised in North Yorkshire on Sunday evening, prompting a police appeal.

The vehicle, parked on Weaponness Valley Road, Scarborough, was 'keyed' at about 10.45pm on September 20, 2026.

**Recommended reads:**

Police are urging anyone with information, home cameras, or dashcam footage from the area to come forward.

People can contact police via email at [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk), phone 101 quoting reference 12260185782, or anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or their website.
