A former bank in Harrogate town centre is set to reopen as the new home of Newtons Solicitors.

The firm is preparing to move into the former Royal Bank of Scotland premises on Cambridge Crescent, near Bettys and overlooking the town’s war memorial.

Newtons bought the building in 2024 with plans to refurbish it for its Harrogate team. The property has stood empty for several years after RBS closed its branch in 2018.

The move marks another stage in the firm’s growth, which has seen it expand to ten offices and more than 110 legal professionals.

A spokesperson for Newtons Solicitors said:

> “This next chapter builds on a journey that's taken us from one office to ten, with a team that's grown to over 110 legal professionals along the way.”

The move will bring part of the Grade II listed Cambridge Crescent back into use.

**Newtons**’ Harrogate team currently operates from Princes Square, offering legal services to individuals and businesses, including advice on property, family law, wills and probate, employment and commercial disputes.
