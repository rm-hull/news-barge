Drivers in County Durham face paying almost £1.88 per litre at the county's most expensive petrol station, according to the latest fuel price data.

The figures show Falcon Service Station in Chester-le-Street charging 187.9p per litre, making it the most expensive non-motorway forecourt in County Durham.

It is almost 9p per litre more expensive than the next priciest stations in the county, Shell Easington West and Shell Easington East in Seaham, which were both listed at 178.9p per litre.

Tow Law Service Station near Bishop Auckland and Acre Rigg Filling Station in Peterlee complete the top five, each charging 175.9p per litre.

## Price rises 'slowing' says expert

The latest figures come as fuel price analysts say there are signs of improvement for drivers after wholesale diesel prices fell sharply in recent days.

James Hitchman, director at PetrolPrices.com, said: "After several difficult weeks for motorists, we are finally seeing some encouraging movement in the wholesale market.

"Diesel wholesale prices have fallen by more than 10p per litre since September 15, and the number of pump price increases is already slowing significantly.

"However, motorists shouldn't expect prices to suddenly fall. Almost one in four stations is currently sitting at 199.9p per litre for diesel, and supermarkets still have some room to increase prices before reaching wider market averages."

He added: "For now, we expect the immediate impact of falling wholesale costs to be a slowing or pause in price rises rather than widespread cuts at the pump.

"If wholesale prices continue on their current trajectory, the focus will then turn to how quickly retailers pass those reductions on to motorists."

The data highlights significant differences in fuel costs across the county, with drivers able to save money by shopping around before filling up.
