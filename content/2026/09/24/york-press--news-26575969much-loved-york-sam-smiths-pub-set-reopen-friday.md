## Much-loved Sam Smith's brewery-owned pub set to reopen in York next week

The pub is due to reopen next Friday "all being well." *(Image: PA)*

A much-loved York Sam Smith’s pub is set to re-open in the days ahead.

Alison and Gary Clarkson are taking the reins of the Trafalgar Bay on Nunnery Lane.

It has been closed since August 2022 when former managers Sarah Walmsley and Phil Roper called time after four-and-a-half years.

Alison and Gary had hoped to open on Tuesday September 29, but they have faced a few glitches, such as the venue needing a new dishwasher, which has caused some delay.

However, Alison says when this arrives and a “few other tweaks” are made to the pub, it should reopen next Friday “all being well.”

The re-opening follows some renovations at the pub last year.

It also follows banners being placed on the exterior in July calling for managers.

![]()The Trafalgar Bay in Nunnery Lane *(Image: NQ)*

The couple are both former managers of a Sam Smith’s pub in Heckmondwike and until recently ran a pub in Stokesley with another company.

Alison said: “We had the opportunity to come back to Sam Smith’s and we chose the Trafalgar.

“It seems a very nice area here. Everybody is friendly.”

The couple have been in the pub trade for six years.

Before that, Alison was a SEND manager in education and Gary was a facilities manager for a chemical firm.

Alison said: “We wanted to slow down a bit.”

The couple looked at 4–5 Sam Smith’s pubs in Wetherby, Boston Spa and the surrounding area.

Alison added: “We did not think about York but we wanted to be in North Yorkshire. This pub is the nicest of them all. It’s quite a community here, which we wasn’t expecting. It’s really nice.”

“We also like the fact that we have the wall opposite us. You can sit and watch people walking on the wall.”

The re-opening follows others from the Tadcaster-based brewery that have re-opened in recent months.

They include the Wellington Inn at Fulford, the Ebor Inn at Bishopthorpe, Angel & White Horse, Tadcaster, the Tankard Inn at Rufforth; and the Red Lion at Bramham, near Wetherby.
