A YORK artist has been named a finalist in a major international exhibition at London’s Saatchi Gallery.

Ellis Newlove, originally from Hull, will exhibit a painting in the ArtEvol 2026 group show, running from October 3 to 11.

The selected work, titled Dressed Down, is a detailed painting of a black disposable lighter.

Mr Newlove said: "They’re cheap, ordinary objects, but they can tell you a lot about a person and the life around them.

"You can look at one and immediately imagine who it belongs to.

"A lighter can almost become a portrait without showing a face.

"That’s what interests me."

The painting is part of a growing series focused on disposable lighters, an object Mr Newlove has been drawn to since childhood.

His grandmother owned novelty lighters, and he eventually began collecting them himself.

Growing up in Hull, a city with the nation’s highest smoking rates, lighters became a familiar part of daily life.

He describes them as "almost a portrait of the city where he grew up."

Mr Newlove’s broader artistic practice explores how people construct and romanticise versions of themselves through everyday objects, images, and materials.

He is particularly interested in items that fall outside the traditional language of fine art, reflecting his working-class Northern background.

The international jury that selected his work included curators, artists, critics, and scholars.

Mr Newlove works across painting, sculpture, and installation, and is currently expanding his series of lighter paintings into a wider body of work.

ArtEvol 2026: A View from Everywhere is organised by the London Art Collective and curated by Nelson Qin.

The exhibition will run at the Saatchi Gallery in London from October 3 to 11.
