A 10-year-old has won first place in a poetry competition.

Ayaan Irfan, a pupil from Lilycroft Primary School in Bradford, took the top spot in the under-18 category at this year’s Saltaire Festival with his poem Echoes of the Aire.

Mrs Ajaib, English lead at Lilycroft Primary School, said: "We are incredibly proud of Ayaan.

"He has worked hard to develop his writing and speaking skills, and to know that he stood on stage and deliver his poem with such confidence was truly special.

"'Echoes of the Aire' is a wonderful piece of writing, and this recognition from the Saltaire Festival is well deserved."

Ayaan was praised by judges for the depth and creativity of his poem, as well as his confidence during the live reading at the Kirkgate Community Centre in Shipley.

Festival judges said his words "His words resonate with the spirit of Saltaire and the flow of the River Aire."

The awards evening featured guest poets and open readings, celebrating both new and experienced voices in spoken word.

Nine-year-old Abdullah Ouedraogo from Margaret McMillan Primary School was named runner-up in the under-18 category.

Both schools are part of The Priestley Academy Trust, which includes four other schools in the Manningham area.
