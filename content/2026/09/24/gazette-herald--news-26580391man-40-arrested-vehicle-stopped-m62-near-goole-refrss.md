A 40-year-old man has been arrested after weapons, including a knife and hammer, and suspected drugs were found in a car on a motorway in East Yorkshire.

The incident happened on the M62, near Goole, at around 10am on Thursday (September 24).

The man was arrested on suspicion of theft of a motor vehicle, driving without insurance, driving without a licence, possession of an offensive weapon in a private place and possession of a controlled Class B drug.




Following the incident, the suspect was further arrested on suspicion of theft after two bank cards under a different name were found within the vehicle, Humberside Police said.

A spokesperson for the force said: "A thorough search of the vehicle also discovered weapons including a knife and hammer, along with suspected drugs.

"This led to the driver being asked to provide a roadside drugs specimen, of which he reportedly failed."

The man remains in police custody as enquiries continue.
