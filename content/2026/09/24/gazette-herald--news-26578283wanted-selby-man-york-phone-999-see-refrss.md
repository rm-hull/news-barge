Police have said a wanted man could be in York - urging anyone who sees him to dial 999.

Bradley Richard Hunter, 32, from Selby, is wanted for allegedly breaching a restraining order, failing to attend court and failing to comply with bail conditions and appointments, North Yorkshire Police said.

The force said “extensive enquiries” are ongoing to locate Hunter.

“We believe that Mr Hunter could be in York and he also has links to the Humberside and West Yorkshire areas,” a police spokesperson said.

They urged anyone who sees Hunter to dial 999 and those with information about his whereabouts to call 101.

Information can be passed anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference number 12260166491 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
