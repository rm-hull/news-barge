A Yorkshire housebuilder is inviting homeowners to reserve a new home the very same day as part of a special weekend designed to take the stress out of moving.

Bellway is launching its Part Exchange event across nine developments in Yorkshire.

The event will begin at 11am on Saturday, September 26, and will offer instant property valuations and same-day reservations on selected plots.

![]() *(Image: Supplied)*

Melanie Smith, sales director for Bellway Yorkshire, said: "Moving house is generally regarded as one of the most stressful life events, with the whole buying and selling process adding massively to the stress levels associated with the big move.

"The aim of the part-exchange weekend is to completely cut out the stress and make moving home as simple as taking a trip to the shops."

The offer allows buyers to move without a property chain.

Homes are available in Beverley, Boroughbridge, Brigg, Brough, Harworth, Hoyland, Kirk Ella, and Snaith.

Prices start at £259,995 for a home at Buttercross Meadows in Brigg.

They rise to £489,995 for a newly released home at Lancaster Fold in Boroughbridge.

Bellway’s sales teams will be on hand, supported by mortgage advisers, valuers, and the New Homes Mortgage Helpline.
