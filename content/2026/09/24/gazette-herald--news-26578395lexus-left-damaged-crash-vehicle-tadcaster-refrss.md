A car has been damaged after being hit by an “unknown vehicle” in North Yorkshire, police said.

The crash happened in Chapel Street, Tadcaster, at 11pm on Friday, September 18.

North Yorkshire Police said a blue Lexus was left damaged by the unknown vehicle travelling along Chapel Street.

“It is believed a white vehicle was observed on the road after the collision driving away,” a police spokesperson said.

They urged anyone with information about the crash to email [colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting reference 12260185542.
