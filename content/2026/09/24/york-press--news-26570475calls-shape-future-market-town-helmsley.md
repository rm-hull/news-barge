## Residents urged to shape future of thriving market town

Helmsley Market Place. Photo Tony Bartholomew

Residents are being urged to shape the future of thriving North Yorkshire market town

Helmsley is embarking on an exciting journey as one of the settlements that North Yorkshire Council is preparing an investment plan for.

A total of 32 towns and key centres are set to benefit from £1.2 million of funding through the Town Investment Plans programme. The plans will be used as part of a co-ordinated approach to identifying local priorities and directing future investment.

Each plan will provide a clear framework setting out an area’s strengths, challenges and key priorities, which will be used to target and allocate future funding effectively.

This project is supported by David Skaith, Mayor of York and North Yorkshire and part-funded by York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority.

Mr Skaith said: "The people who live in our towns and villages know what their areas need, so we are putting them at the heart of deciding what they want to see improve in their areas.

"Working closely with North Yorkshire Council and City of York Council, we're setting out the first community-led plans for our towns, cities, and villages right across York and North Yorkshire."

North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for open to business, Cllr Mark Crane, whose responsibilities include economic development and regeneration, said: “As England’s largest county covering more than 3,000 square miles, we know our market town areas are incredibly diverse, each with their own distinctive character, strengths and heritage.

“While every town is unique, we believe that many will share the same issues and challenges. These plans will help us clearly identify priorities for investment and highlight where there are shared opportunities across different areas.

“Crucially, the plans are being shaped through direct engagement with the communities who know their areas best. By working together, we can look at the right opportunities and ensure future funding is used where it will have the greatest impact.

Helmsley’s representative on North Yorkshire Council, Councillor George Jabbour said the purpose of the Town Investment Plan is to devise a coordinated strategy for Helmsley to continue to be the successful and thriving place that it is now.

He said: “It is important that residents, businesses, local organisations and other stakeholders shape our approach to Helmsley Town Investment Plan by highlighting both the opportunities and challenges facing our community.

“Please make your voice heard by completing the Online Survey of Helmsley Town Investment Plan consultation by 28th September.”

The Online Survey could be completed by visiting [https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/your-council/consultations-and-engagement/current-consultations/helmsley-town-investment-plan](https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/your-council/consultations-and-engagement/current-consultations/helmsley-town-investment-plan) or searching for "Helmsley Town Investment Plan" on the internet.

In addition to completing the Online Survey, residents are invited to attend a Drop-in Session on Friday, September 25, between 5pm and 7pm, at Helmsley Town Hall (1st floor) - the entrance is on Market Place, next to Helmsley Library.
