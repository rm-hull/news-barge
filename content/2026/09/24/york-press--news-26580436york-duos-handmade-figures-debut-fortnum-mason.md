## York duo's handmade figures go on sale in one of London's most iconic stores

Fiona and Miranda's Ethelanna Bunnies and Meeces collection has gone on sale in Fortnum & Mason in London *(Image: Supplied)*

Handmade hanging creatures crafted by a York duo have made their debut in one of London’s most iconic stores.

Fiona and Miranda, from Dunnington, make collectible figures known as Ethelanna Bunnies and Meeces.

They have been commissioned to create a limited-edition collection now on sale at Fortnum & Mason.




Having spent over 25 years running a wedding flower business, the pair said they were "over the moon" to have been asked to make a limited edition of the iconic figures as hanging decorations for Christmas.

Ethelanna Bunnies and Meeces went on sale at the iconic store in London's Piccadilly on September 14.

Miranda said: "We still can't believe it. We are so excited to take on our newest chapter."

After leaving the wedding industry, Fiona and Miranda launched Preserve My Wedding Bouquet, transforming occasion flowers into keepsakes using natural dyeing to imprint floral patterns onto silk and cashmere scarves and cushions.

The Ethelanna Bunnies and Meeces, handcrafted as unique, collectible decorations, have long been a side passion.

They are made using cashmere, silks, luxury wool and vintage fabrics.

Their latest achievement marks a major milestone for the pair.

Fiona and Miranda said: "We were so excited when we received an email from the company saying that they would be 'thrilled' to have our Bunnies and Meeces in their iconic store."
