A woman has appeared in court accused of murdering a pensioner.

Harry Heslop died at the supported-living retirement home, Cedar House on Foxwood Drive, Stockton, in the early hours of July 21, Cleveland Police said.

Rebecca Hackett was not asked to enter pleas to charges of murder and possession of a bladed article at Teesside Crown Court today.

The 41-year-old, of Hartington Road, Stockton, will next appear at court on November 16 for a pre trial preparation hearing.

The case was adjourned to allow further time for a psychiatric report to be completed.

Judge Francis Laird, the Recorder of Middlesbrough, remanded Hackett in custody, and her trial is due to begin on January 11 next year.

Harry Heslop was 77 when he died.

Pictures taken at the time show a police car stationed outside Cedar House.
