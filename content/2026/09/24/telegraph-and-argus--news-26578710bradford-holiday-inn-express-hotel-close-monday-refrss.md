A MAJOR hotel in Bradford city centre is closing next Monday.

The Holiday Inn Express will close its doors on September 28, with multiple readers getting in touch to express their concerns, with staff allegedly only given a few days' notice.

In trying to verify the situation, the T&A tried to book a room online and over the phone for dates in October, but it proved impossible to do so.

And we have now received a copy of an official letter from a guest, seemingly signed by the hotel manager, confirming the closure.

It reads: “We would like to inform you that Holiday Inn Express Bradford City Centre will ceasing [sic] trading and be permanently closing on Monday 28th September 2026.

“During this transition period, the hotel will continue to operate.

“However, some facilities and services may be limited or unavailable as we prepare for closure.

“Our team will make every effort to minimise disruption and will be happy to assist with any questions regarding the services available during your stay.

“If your plans extend beyond the closure date, we will be unable to accommodate you.

“Please speak with any member of the team if there is anything we can do to support you.

“On behalf of everyone at Holiday Inn Express Bradford City Centre, thank you for your support and understanding.”

The T&A has contacted parent company InterContinental Hotels Group (IHG) for comment.

Booking.com states: “We’re sorry, but this property isn’t taking reservations on our site right now.”

In late 2002, the T&A reported how a £5 million Express by Holiday Inn hotel would be opening in the Vicar Lane Leisure Exchange the following year.

At the time, it was tipped to give the city a tourism boost, helping to draw visitors wanting to use the leisure scheme and see the city's other major attractions.

The hotel would be a loss to the city, with Booking.com saying: “Holiday Inn Express Bradford City Centre by IHG is ideally located in the heart of Bradford, directly opposite Bradford Interchange, offering convenient rail and bus connections across the region.

“Leeds Bradford Airport is just a 20-minute drive away, while guests travelling by car can take advantage of nearby parking at the Leisure Exchange Multi-Storey Car Park.

“Popular nearby attractions include the National Science and Media Museum, the historic Alhambra Theatre and The Broadway Shopping Centre.

“Guests can also visit the UNESCO-listed Saltaire Village, explore Bradford's industrial heritage at the Bradford Industrial Museum, or enjoy the open spaces of City Park with its famous Mirror Pool and fountains.

“Whether travelling for business or leisure, guests can relax in comfortable rooms featuring free Wi-Fi, a TV and blackout curtains.

“The hotel also offers on-site meeting facilities, ideal for business meetings and small events.

“Start each morning with a free breakfast buffet before discovering all that Bradford and Yorkshire have to offer.”
