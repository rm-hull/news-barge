# Eight adorable Dogs Trust pups desperate to find their forever home with you

## These gorgeous four legged friends are looking for their forever home

![Jesse and Nezuko](https://i2-prod.leeds-live.co.uk/news/leeds-news/article34658673.ece/ALTERNATES/s1200/0_Jesse-and-Nezuko-Dogs-Trust-Leeds.jpg)

These adorable rescue dogs are waiting patiently for loving families to come forward and give them somewhere to call home this October.

For dogs living in rescue centres, the wait for the right family can sometimes be a long one. Some animals spend months in kennels, while others can wait even longer before the perfect owner comes along.

Autumn can be a lovely time to welcome a new four legged friend into your home, with the colder evenings offering plenty of opportunities for cosy nights in and getting to know your new companion.

However, bringing a dog into your family is a huge commitment and is a decision that should never be rushed. Prospective owners need to think carefully about the time, care, exercise and attention a dog will need throughout its life.

For those who feel ready to take on that responsibility, adopting a rescue dog can be an incredibly rewarding experience. It can give a dog a second chance while providing a loving family with a loyal new companion.

There are currently plenty of dogs at Dogs Trust Leeds hoping that their search will soon come to an end. Among them are eight lovable pups who are all waiting for someone to notice them and decide that they could be the perfect match.

Each dog has their own personality, needs and little quirks, but they all have one thing in common. They are looking for a safe and loving home where they can settle in and become part of the family.

The dedicated team at Dogs Trust Leeds will be working hard to find the right homes for each of them this autumn.

Could one of these eight adorable rescue dogs be the new best friend your family has been waiting for? Find out more and register your interest on the Dogs Trust website here.

* ### Sasha

  ![Sasha](https://i2-prod.leeds-live.co.uk/news/leeds-news/article34658668.ece/ALTERNATES/s1227b/0_Sasha-Dogs-Trust-Leeds.jpg)

  Sasha is a gorgeous 4-year-old American Bulldog who loves people, snuggling, and being close to her humans. She would do well with secondary school-aged children as long as they are comfortable with affectionate, large dogs but needs to be the only pet.

  1 of 7  
  While she walks beautifully on a lead, she is strong. Sasha is working hard on her dog socialisation and will need her new family to continue her training with full support from the centre.  
  She was previously house trained and will just need a little refresher as she settles in. A few visits to the centre will be needed to build a relationship and learn training techniques.
* ### Buddy

  ![](https://i2-prod.leeds-live.co.uk/news/leeds-news/article34658667.ece/ALTERNATES/s1227b/0_Buddy-Dogs-Trust-Leeds.jpg)

  Buddy is a stunning 2-year-old German Shepherd Crossbreed looking for a calm, adult-only home in a quieter area, with people that love big dogs.

  2 of 7  
  He can be anxious around new people and needs multiple meets at the centre to build trust. A bark first, think later kind of boy, he loves learning and needs dedicated adopters to continue his training with full support from the centre.  
  Buddy requires someone home all the time while settling in. While he has mild hip dysplasia, it doesn't slow him down, and our vet can discuss details and ongoing support.
* ### Daisy

  ![Daisy](https://i2-prod.leeds-live.co.uk/news/leeds-news/article34658672.ece/ALTERNATES/s1227b/0_Daisy-Dogs-Trust-Leeds.jpg)

  Daisy is a beautiful 4-year-old Rottweiler Cross who is super affectionate and loves leaning in for a snuggle once she gets to know you! Because she can be nervous when meeting new people, she needs calm, patient owners who can give her plenty of time to settle in and build a strong bond.

  3 of 7  
  She would benefit from basic training and needs adopters who can initially be around 24/7 before slowly expanding her alone time. To help her decompress and feel secure, Daisy requires a quieter adult-only home or one with children aged 14 and over.
* ### Milo

  ![Milo]()

  Milo is a super gorgeous 5-year-old Staffy who adores people once given a little time to get to know you. He needs to be the only pet in a reasonably peaceful, adult-only home with a secure garden due to being slightly noise sensitive.

  4 of 7  
  His friends at the centre describe him as "one of a kind" due to his affectionate nature and the strong bond he forms with his friends. While he gets along with other dogs and would love walking buddies, he needs a home without other pets to help him settle.  
  Being very food-motivated, Milo loves learning fun tricks and forms a strong, affectionate bond with his humans. He takes medication for a skin condition and anxiety, but our vet can discuss this and potential cost support from Dogs Trust.
