A man is fighting for his life in hospital after crashing into a County Durham roundabout after a a police chase.

Officers responded to reports of a disturbance at an address in Catchgate, Stanley, at around 10.10pm on Wednesday (September 23).

A suspect vehicle was spotted nearby but failed to stop for police.

After a short pursuit, the white Renault Kangoo van collided with Villa Real roundabout in Consett.

![]() *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The driver, a man in his 30s, sustained serious injuries and was taken to the Royal Victoria Infirmary in Newcastle, where he remains in a critical condition.

A large police cordon was put in place around the roundabout following the crash, with part of the Esso garage also shut.

The van was left with its front end badly crumpled.

![]() *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The incident has been referred to the Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC).

The road was closed for around 12 hours, with heavy traffic building during Thursday morning's rush hour and buses diverted away from the area.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on Leadgate Road in Consett at 10.27pm on Wednesday.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews, one clinical team leader and one hazardous area response team. One patient was taken to the RVI hospital for further treatment."

According to the AA, traffic in the area has now cleared and the roundabout has reopened.
