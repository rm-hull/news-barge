Good morning. Donald Trump has welcomed Xi Jinping to the US, greeting the Chinese leader on the asphalt at Joint Base Andrews at the start of his state visit. Xi’s trip coincides with the UN general assembly in New York, but he is not attending the gathering.

The pageantry will continue with a formal welcome on the South Lawn of the White House, private tea with the US president and first lady, and a tour of the National Archives, among other special events. The White House also invited major tech leaders to a state dinner being held for Xi.

* **What will the two leaders discuss?** Amy Hawkins notes in this explainer that topics are likely to include the development of AI technology, frosty trade relations and Taiwan.
* **What developments have there been in US-China relations?** The US treasury secretary, Scott ⁠Bessent, announced that the US ⁠and ​China agreed on Wednesday to extend the so-called Busan ⁠agreement, a temporary trade truce between the world’s two biggest economies.
* **Who else has been invited to the US?** The US has invited Vladimir Putin to take part in the G20 meeting scheduled to take place in Miami in December, in what would be the Russian leader’s first attendance at such a summit since he launched his invasion of Ukraine in 2022. The US secretary of state, Marco Rubio, said: “We hope that’s an invitation he’ll accept.”

## **Iran’s president hits out at ‘bully’ Trump in UN speech**

![Masoud Pezeshkian](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/aece55705f95ae54a84d9e97036afc15bcf48fca/225_0_2735_2188/master/2735.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

In a defiant speech to the UN general assembly in New York, Iran’s president, Masoud Pezeshkian described the US as the true bully and terrorist in the Middle East while insisting that Iran had always shown a commitment to the negotiating table. But he balanced that with a willingness to talk, saying: “We are ready for dialogue and diplomacy and negotiations but without the language of force.”

Pezeshkian accused Washington of “terrorism” for assassinating the country’s supreme leader, Ali Khamenei, at the start of the US-Israeli war. He held up photographs of the ayatollah and some of the more than 150 schoolchildren killed in the opening days of the conflict. He also insisted that Tehran had never started a war.

* **Why is Israel facing a water supply crisis?** Israel is facing weeks of water shortages after an exceptional algae bloom crippled much of its desalination capacity, in a crisis scientists say was triggered when Israeli attacks destroyed much of Gaza’s wastewater infrastructure, causing a huge discharge of untreated sewage.

## **Judge orders White House to restore access to CNN, MS Now and Politico**

![Camera operators and sound recordists with mike booms in a huddle in front of Donald Trump who is seated at his desk](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/2753d4849c21c0d640d42bf13a52a48c7086472e/601_433_2793_2234/master/2793.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

In a blow for the administration and Donald Trump’s repeated attacks on US media outlets, a federal judge has ordered the White House to temporarily restore ⁠access ​to journalists from CNN, MS Now and Politico. The US district judge Tim Kelly said the ban on the three media outlets was ⁠likely to be unconstitutional.

A lawyer for the Department of Justice told Kelly the administration had provided enough explanation for the revocation of the ​press passes, but the judge signaled skepticism of that argument, ​noting that the letters were sent after the revocation occurred and the lawsuit was filed.

* **Where else has Trump suffered a legal setback over media coverage?** A judge has also dismissed Trump’s lawsuit accusing the Des Moines Register and its former pollster of misrepresenting the state of the 2024 presidential race, rejecting the president’s claim that the newspaper’s poll coverage violated Iowa’s consumer fraud law.

## **Eight USS Lincoln personnel have attempted suicide, US navy confirms**

![The USS Abraham Lincoln](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/7ca69eef43171e60c4ddf25e1a71c02c3b804e2c/0_0_5000_4000/master/5000.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Eight US navy personnel assigned to the USS Abraham Lincoln carrier strike group have attempted to take their own lives since the start of their lengthy deployment as part of the US war on Iran, the acting US navy secretary, Hung Cao, revealed in a letter to Senator Kirsten Gillibrand. Donald Trump described reports of poor conditions on the ship as fake news in August.
