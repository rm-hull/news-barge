A man has been sentenced for child sex offences after police investigated his online exchanges with someone he believed was a teenager.

David Richardson, 31, sent sexually explicit images and videos to someone he believed was a 14-year-old boy.

Richardson was actually speaking to an officer from the North East Regional Organised Crime Unit (NEROCU), who was investigating him.

Richardson was arrested at his Newcastle home on July 17 and charged with attempting to make a child watch sexual images and engage in sexual communication.

Both charges were attempts because Richardson was actually communicating with a police officer, not a boy.

Richardson, of Denton Burn, appeared at Newcastle Crown Court for sentencing on Wednesday, September 23, after pleading guilty to both charges.

Recorder Jonathan Sandiford handed Richardson a 12-month prison sentence, suspended for two years.

He was also given a Sexual Harm Prevention Order and ordered to register as a sex offender for ten years.

Speaking after the sentencing hearing, NEROCU detective constable Lee Robertson said: “This is just the latest investigation which has seen a dangerous predator have their day in court.

“Our work will not stop, and we will continue to work to tackle online child abuse and sexual exploitation.

“We would urge anyone who has been a victim of this type of offending, or has concerns about someone’s behaviour, to come forward and talk to police.”

Anyone concerned about child abuse or wanting to report a crime can contact their local police force through its website or by calling 101.

Always dial 999 in an emergency.
