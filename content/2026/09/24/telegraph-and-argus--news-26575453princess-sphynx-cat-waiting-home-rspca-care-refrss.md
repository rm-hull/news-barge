A SPECIAL Sphynx cat is still waiting for her forever home after spending more than 270 days in RSPCA care.

Princess, described as "extremely friendly," is currently being looked after at the RSPCA Southport, Ormskirk and District Branch.

She needs a home with someone who understands the unique needs of a hairless cat, including regular bathing, ear cleaning, and keeping warm indoors.

Sophie Hubble, from the branch, said: "Princess is an extremely friendly girl with everyone that she meets; she purrs and meows at you and will climb onto your shoulder in a practised fashion.

"When she is not climbing on you and asking for attention, she can be found curled up in a blanket keeping warm.

"She truly is a lovely girl and while taking on a cat with a known health condition is a big commitment, Princess will reward her new owner tenfold with love and affection."

Princess was one of eight cats rescued by the RSPCA from a property in West Yorkshire last year.

She was one of six cats who survived following treatment, but a heart murmur was later discovered while in care.

Potential adopters must live within travelling distance of Greater Manchester Hospital for a dental procedure Princess will need after adoption.

Please contact the branch on [info@rspcasouthport.co.uk](mailto:info@rspcasouthport.co.uk) or 01704 567624 to find out more about adopting Princess.
