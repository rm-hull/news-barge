A man has been charged in connection with an incident that saw police swoop in a York street.

Tesco Express staff members called police to the scene in Low Ousegate on Wednesday afternoon (September 24) following a reported shoplifting.

North Yorkshire Police said Matthew John Dodson, 52, has been charged with being drunk and disorderly in a public place and theft from a shop.

He is due to appear at York Magistrates’ Court on Monday, November 9, the force said.

Police were called to the Tesco Express in Low Ousegate at about 2.15pm on Wednesday.

In a statement on Wednesday afternoon, North Yorkshire Police said its officers attended the scene and arrested a man who was taken into custody.

The force said the arrest followed "reports that a suspected shop lifter became abusive towards staff after being refused entry to the store".

Staff in a nearby pub in Low Ousegate said they were alerted by the blue lights and came out to Low Ousegate to see the two vans.

An eyewitness said there was a commotion with a lot of pedestrians and shoppers stopped in their tracks on the busy street.

They said multiple police officers and one police van were in Low Ousegate, and that it looked to them like local businesses were not affected and carrying on in the usual way.

Workers in Low Ousegate said police activity in the area was a common occurrence.
