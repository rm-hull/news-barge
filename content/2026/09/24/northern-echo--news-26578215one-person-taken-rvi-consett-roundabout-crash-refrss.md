One person was taken to hospital after a late-night crash on a County Durham roundabout.

A large police cordon was put in place around the Villa Real roundabout in Consett, with part of the Esso garage also shut after the crash around 10.30pm on Wednesday (September 23).

A white van in the cordon was left with its front end badly crumpled.

One person was taken to the RVI in Newcastle for treatment. The extent of their injuries is not yet known.

The road was closed for 12 hours with heavy traffic building during this morning's rush hour, while buses were diverted away from the area.
