Whether it's commuters heading to work, people travelling for a day out or a weekend away, Darlington Train Station sees 2.83 million passengers every year.

The number of people passing through the station has risen by 20 per cent following a major makeover this year.

A £140m project has transformed the station, giving it plenty of fresh updates and a modern look, which was completed in May.

There is a new gateway entrance linked to Central Park, a transport interchange, shops and modern waiting areas.

![]() *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The redevelopment, which has been years in the making, means the historic Grade-ll listed station is now connected to a modern new concourse, with an elevated walkway connecting the two as well as a 650-space multi-storey car park.

An LNER spokesperson said: "We're thrilled to see that Darlington has been our fastest-growing station over the last year.

"In what was such a momentous year for the industry, celebrating 200 years since the birth of the modern railway, we're very proud that more people visited the town where it all began."

It's a big deal for Darlington, making travel into the town or getting a connecting train from the station a much more enjoyable experience.

If you are taking a train soon or want to know more about the station since its upgrade, we have everything you need.

Read on to find out all you need to know about Darlington Train Station - from opening times to train routes, plus new updates and roadwork disruption.

### How to get to Darlington Train Station

Darlington Train Station is just a short walk from the town centre.

Address: Darlington station, Bank Top, Darlington, County Durham, DL1 4AA.

### What time does it open and close?

It is open from 6am until 7pm from Monday to Friday.

On Saturdays, it opens from 7am until 7pm. If you are travelling on a Sunday, the train station opens at 8am and closes at 6pm.

Passengers should note that the ticket office is closed on weekends.

Travellers without tickets who need to purchase them on weekends can do so via machines in the station or get digital tickets.

### Train routes available

![]() *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

Darlington Railway Station offers a range of direct and connecting train services to 120 different stations around the UK.

Passengers can head to major cities, including Edinburgh, York, Durham and Bristol.

Other destinations can be reached via connecting services, such as London, Newcastle, Leeds, Manchester, and Glasgow, depending on the chosen route and train operator.

### Shop and food options inside station

If you fancy picking up a magazine or browsing books before your journey, there is a WHSmith inside Darlington Train Station.

There is also a Costa Coffee shop with seating available for those who fancy some food or drink, plus a Pumpkin café.

### Greggs on the go!

![]() *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Greggs is now serving its iconic bakery goodies to rail passengers in Darlington, after opening in July this year.

Situated within the station’s newly launched eastern concourse, the shop promises convenience for commuters, travellers, and visitors from early morning until late evening.

The opening created 16 new job roles.

### Road work disruption

Drivers and rail passengers are being warned to prepare for months of disruption around Darlington Railway Station as the final phase of the station's redevelopment project gets underway.

The works are expected to begin in autumn this year, subject to final approval from Darlington Borough Council.

The project is expected to be completed by late spring next year.

Tees Valley Combined Authority has confirmed the scheme will involve temporary traffic lights on Neasham Road, reducing traffic to a single lane at times.
