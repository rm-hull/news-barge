A specialist medical practitioner in North Yorkshire has gone into liquidation, owing almost £100,000, largely to himself.

Dr Dan Leeder Limited of Whixley, near York, entered voluntary liquidation on September 3.

Cheshire-based Parker Walsh Ltd were appointed administrator.

The company was incorporated in 2021, with Daniel James Leeder listed as the only director.

A liquidator’s report known as a Statement of Affairs posted on the Companies House website set out the financial situation.

It said the company had no assets and no preferential creditors—those who are paid first.

However, there was a secondary preferential creditor—someone paid next—namely HMRC which was owed £900 in PAYE.

In addition, there were four unsecured trade and expense creditors—those who are paid last—that were owed £93,048.46.

This gave an estimated total deficiency of £93,948.46 with regards to creditors.

With the business having £100 of issued and called up capital, this gave a total deficiency or debt of £94,048.46 owed.

A list of creditors says in addition to HMRC’s £900; Capital on Tap was owed £3,067.73; New Wave Capital was owed £3,180.73 and North Yorkshire Council was owed £6,800.

But the biggest creditor was Daniel James Leeder who was owed £80,000.
