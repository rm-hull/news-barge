## North Yorkshire fish and chip shop named among in the top in the country

Jonathan France, owner of The Fish Bank in Sherburn in Elmet, with Melissa Meachen and Karen Culkin *(Image: Supplied)*

A NORTH Yorkshire chippy has been named among the UK's top 40 in a national awards competition.

The Fish Bank in Sherburn-in-Elmet, Yorkshire, has been longlisted in the Takeaway of the Year category at the National Fish & Chip Awards.

The Scrap Box - located in Dunnington on the outskirts of York - currently holds the Takeaway of the Year title, with several other York and North Yorkshire businesses receiving other titles and commendations.

Organised by the National Federation of Fish Friers (NFFF), the awards are now entering their 39th year and celebrate excellence, innovation, and dedication within the industry.

Andrew Crook, president of the NFFF, said: "As we celebrate the 39th year of the National Fish & Chip Awards, it's brilliant to see talent, passion, excellent professionalism and dedication to their craft on display.

"Every year, the Takeaway of the Year standard continues to rise and this new list of top 40 contenders exemplify that. It’s the people behind these businesses that make our industry what it is.

"We are proud to celebrate the owners, teams and young talent whose passion and hard work keep this much-loved tradition thriving.

![]()Fish and chips *(Image: PA)*

"This year we are particularly keen to shine a light on the opportunities our industry offers, from developing lifelong skills to fulfilling dreams of running a successful business."

The Fish Bank will now progress to the next round of judging, with only 20 takeaways set to make it through to the semi-finals.

The top 10 finalists will be announced in the coming months, ahead of the awards ceremony on February 24 at the Park Plaza Westminster Bridge in London.

Aman Dhesi, co-owner of Dunnington's The Scrap Box - this year's winner of Takeaway of the Year, said that more than six months on from winning, they were still seeing the impact of the award.

![]()Gavin and Aman Dhesi of The Scrap Box - Takeaway of the Year winner. *(Image: The National Fish & Chip Awards / SWNS)*

He said: "It gave the business a huge boost, as did reaching the top 10 the year before, but probably the biggest thing has been the opportunities it’s opened up for us outside the shop, from media and travel to meeting people across the industry and getting involved in some really exciting projects."

For those currently in the running, Aman advised making the most of the experience.

He said: "The awards journey really makes you look closely at your own business – what you do well, what makes you different, and where you can still improve.

"We learned a huge amount through the process before we ever won. And don’t try to second-guess what the judges want. Be proud of what makes your shop yours and tell that story."
