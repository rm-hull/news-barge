A LIVE hospital construction site is helping adult learners build the foundations of a new career.

Four learners from Bradford College recently undertook a two-week industry placement at Lynfield Mount Hospital through the college’s Adult Construction Skills course.

The placement was part of a £65 million redevelopment project at the Bradford District Care NHS Foundation Trust site, aimed at improving patient and staff facilities.

Louise Rigg, project director of the Lynfield Mount Hospital redevelopment programme, said: "As well as making a difference to people who use the Trust’s services and staff members, we want the redevelopment to have a positive and lasting impact on the hospital’s local communities.

"We are pleased that the programme is able to give people the chance to learn new skills and help them think about their future careers."

During the placement, students worked alongside contractors from McAvoy, shadowing a variety of roles involved in constructing a new ward block and refurbishing the current building.

One student described the experience as "unforgettable," praising the supportive environment and the insight gained into both trade and management roles on site.

John Wells, project manager at the Construction Academy, said: "Work experience is an invaluable part of the programme as it gives learners the opportunity to see the trades they have been studying put into practice."
