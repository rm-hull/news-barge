A SCHOOLBOY from Bradford died after getting into difficulty in the sea in Wales, an inquest has heard.

Marcin Michal Komoszewski, 15, died after going into the water at Marloes Sands beach, Pembrokeshire, on 30 August during a family holiday.

Two other people who were in difficulties were able to get out of the water.

Rescue attempts were made by his family and a member of the public.

The search was called off after two days.

A passing yacht spotted Marcin's body on September 8, with the RNLI helping with the recovery operation.

Marcin, a student living in Bradford, was born in Poland in 2010, the inquest was told.

The inquest was adjourned pending further investigations.
