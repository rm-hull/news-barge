TEZ Ilyas is bringing his latest stand-up show to audiences.

The comedian will perform *Tez Things I Hate About You* at Bradford's St George's Hall this Friday, September 25, 2026, with the show starting at 7:30pm.

Described as the grumpiest uncle in showbiz, Ilyas returns with a brand new show after travelling the world.

Audiences can expect fresh jokes, furious rants and ridiculous stories, delivered through his smart, silly and subversive humour.

He is known for *Man Like Mobeen*,* Live at the Apollo*and* Fighting Talk*, and is the Sunday Times bestselling author of* The Secret Diary of a British Muslim Aged 13*.

Tickets cost between £18 and £38, with Friends Members and access options listed as open.

Up to 10 tickets can be booked online HERE, while discounts are subject to availability and may not apply.

The event is for those aged 16 and over.
