A BARN conversion has fetched more than £1 million, making it the most expensive property sold in the Bradford district this year.

Accerhill Hall, located on Stackhouse Lane in Settle, was the highest-value residential sale recorded in 2026 so far, going for £1,090,000 according to Land Registry data.

It was listed on Rightmove from September 2024 to April 2026.

The detached home, which was sold through Ashdown Jones, has five bedrooms, four bathrooms and a cinema room.

It measures approximately 5,469 square feet.

### Top ten most expensive sales in the district

Among the 10 most expensive sales in the district, six had previously been sold, with an average value increase of 67.2 per cent.

The biggest rise was at 4 Highgate, BD20 8BE, which sold for £910,000.

The property was previously bought for £400,000 in December 2023, highlighting a rise of 127.5 per cent.

Figures are based on Land Registry Price Paid Data provided by Brickview.ai.
