CARE home residents have taken part in a talent show celebrating creativity and self-expression.

Residents from Beacon House in Bradford, a specialist service for adults with learning disabilities and autism, performed in the second Cygnet’s Got Talent competition, held in Manchester on Tuesday, September 8.

They showcased their skills in singing, dancing, poetry, and rapping before a live audience and panel of judges.

Gareth Williams, chief executive of Cygnet Social Care, said: "I am delighted that this year’s event was even bigger and brighter than the last.

"With more talents on show from the fantastic individuals we are proud to support, this really is a highlight of the year for us all at Cygnet Social Care."

More than 30 people took to the stage, earning a standing ovation from the audience.

Mr Williams said: "It was once again a privilege to witness such an inspiring display of talent and happiness.

"This event was a wonderful opportunity to bring our services together, celebrate the unique abilities of the people we support, and simply have fun."

Beacon House is located on Beacon Road in Bradford.
