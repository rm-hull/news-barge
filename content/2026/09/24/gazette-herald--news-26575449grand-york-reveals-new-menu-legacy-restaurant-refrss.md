*The Grand York reveals new menu and concept for Legacy restaurant*

A MICHELIN-celebrated chef has unveiled his ambitious new vision for one of York's leading fine-dining restaurants following the departure of its head chef.

Kevin Bonello, culinary director at The Grand, York, is relaunching Legacy with a concept firmly focused on Yorkshire ingredients, producers and craftsmanship.

The new-look dining experience will launch on November 4, with Kevin setting his sights on establishing Legacy among the UK's leading fine-dining destinations and competing with some of London's best restaurants.

The announcement follows the departure of Great British Menu chef Ahmed Abdalla, who had led the Legacy kitchen and helped establish the restaurant as one of York's top dining destinations.

The Press reported on September 6 that Ahmed was leaving to pursue his ambition of opening a restaurant of his own, centred around local produce, community and Yorkshire hospitality.

Kevin, who joined The Grand in 2023, previously held a Michelin star at de Mondion in his native Malta and has more than two decades' experience.

![]()Kevin Bonello, Culinary Director at The Grand, York

He has led Legacy's culinary team since its 'Evolution through the Eras' menu was introduced in June 2024.

His new concept will revolve around three seasonal menus, with ingredients changing according to availability rather than culinary trends.

Game & Wild will launch in November, followed by Foraged & Roots in February and Fish & Harvest from June.

Kevin says every ingredient will have a connection with Yorkshire provenance, from local farmers, producers and fishers to honey from The Grand's own rooftop beehives.

Kevin is also bringing a highly personal touch to the dining room.

Ingredients will be foraged from the village where he lives outside York, while Legacy will use utensils and wooden cutlery rests Kevin has hand-carved himself.

The restaurant will feature murals hand-painted by local artist and former Legacy employee Lily Roberts and a bespoke cheese trolley made by York woodworker Ollie Abell.

Fermentation cupboards will also form part of the restaurant.

Kevin said: "Legacy's new future is one that allows the ingredients to shine. The only thing taking centre stage in our restaurant is the food and the guest experience.

![]()Fine dining at Legacy at The Grand

"Anyone who knows me will say I am more than a little obsessed with food and the story behind it. This is my food love story played out.

"We will connect guests to the entire food journey when they dine in Legacy.

"I've travelled all over the world in my career but found the inspiration I was looking for in my very own village."

Describing his style as “proud, bold and precise,” Kevin says his cooking is influenced by his Maltese heritage and the island’s rich culinary history, blending Sicilian, Arabic, French and Spanish flavours. His work is further shaped by early experience in the kitchens of Gordon Ramsay, Marco Pierre White and Erin Frechon, as well as his extensive travels.

The Grand says the investment comes as food and drink plays an increasingly important role in York's visitor economy.

Figures from Visit York's 2025 STEAM report show the city welcomed almost nine million visitors in 2025, contributing £2.08 billion to the local economy, with 27 per cent of visitor spending going on food and drink.

Adam Dyke, general manager at The Grand, York, said: "This is a new Legacy and this is Yorkshire served in a way that has never been done before."

He described the concept as "formal dining done in an informal way", adding: "It's inviting, innovative and full of his personality."
