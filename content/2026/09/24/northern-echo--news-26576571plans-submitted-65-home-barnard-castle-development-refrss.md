Plans have been submitted for 65 homes in a County Durham town, including affordable and accessible properties.

Bellway Strategic Land wants to build on an eight-acre site north of Darlington Road, in Barnard Castle, with two- to five-bedroom houses and two-bedroom bungalows.

Thirteen of the 65 homes would be affordable, available for low-cost rent or at a discounted sale price.

Shaun Cuggy, senior strategic land manager for Bellway in the North, said: "County Durham has a significant shortfall in housing land supply and our proposals for this site in Barnard Castle would contribute to helping meet this need.

"This is a sustainable location for a residential neighbourhood, within walking distance of the town’s many and varied amenities for day-to-day living, including shops, schools, a medical centre and leisure facilities.

"The homes we are proposing would be highly energy-efficient, while we are also including a range of bungalows to ensure the development meets the needs of buyers at all stages of their lives."

Some 72 per cent of the homes would meet higher accessibility and adaptability standards for residents with different needs.

Mr Cuggy said the design would complement its surroundings.

He said: "We have also carefully designed the development to reflect its surroundings on the edge of Barnard Castle, with stone-fronted houses facing Darlington Road, and a woodland tree buffer facing the countryside to the east.

"This would be an attractive place to live, with areas of public open space, a new footpath along the eastern boundary and the planting of new trees and plants to promote both wellbeing and wildlife.

"There would also be substantial benefits delivered for the wider community, through the creation of new affordable homes, jobs and encouraging spending within the local economy."

Durham County Council validated the full planning application earlier this month, and its planning team will now consider it.
