Rishi Sunak has brought transport leaders together to tackle long-running parking problems at Northallerton station.

The Richmond and Northallerton MP met TransPennine Express, York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority and North Yorkshire Council to tackle parking problems at the station.

Councillors Peter Wilkinson and Caroline Dickinson also attended, bringing residents’ concerns.

Mr Sunak said: "I know how frustrating the situation at Northallerton station can be, and it is an issue which has been raised with me by passengers and local residents.

![]()Rishi Sunak has convened representatives of TransPennine Express, York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority and North Yorkshire Council to address long-standing parking issues at Northallerton station *(Image: Supplied)*

"TransPennine Express has done work on a proposal which could provide additional capacity.

"The challenge now is establishing how a scheme could be delivered and funded.

"That is why I wanted to get TransPennine Express, the Combined Authority and North Yorkshire Council around the same table, alongside Peter and Caroline, so everyone could understand the position and look at what options may be available.

"I will continue working with all those involved to make sure the different options are properly explored."

The station serves a wide rural area, so many passengers drive there before catching a train.

Parking has long been a problem, made worse when the nearby Arla car park closed in February and removed extra spaces for rail users.

TransPennine Express, which runs the station car park, has drawn up plans for more spaces, but funding remains the main hurdle.

Mr Sunak organised the meeting to review the plans, discuss funding and work out how to take the project forward.

After the meeting, he responded to the Mayor of York and North Yorkshire’s transport consultation, highlighting the need for more spaces at Northallerton station, and wrote to the Transport Secretary.

Councillor Wilkinson said: "The closure of the former Arla car park has caused major parking problems at Northallerton station, with vehicles spilling into Romanby.

"Rishi and Romanby Parish Council have brought together a multi-agency group to find a solution, including extra parking at the long-stay car park."

Councillor Dickinson said: "I was pleased to join the meeting about parking at Northallerton station.

"The closure of the Arla car park has made the problems worse.

"This is a busy station for people from across the area, not just Northallerton residents.

"More spaces are needed for rail users, and I look forward to seeing the proposals."
