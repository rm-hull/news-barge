## EU defence chief speaks to Euronews as US President Donald Trump calls on every country belonging to the International Criminal Court to quit. Meanwhile, as war in the Middle East disrupts global oil supplies and refining costs soar, European consumers are feeling the pinch more and more.

## On today's show:

**Shona Murray** breaks down why more EU member states are engaging with Palantir. This American company builds enterprise data integration, analytics, and artificial intelligence platforms for government and commercial clients. But concerns remain about its questionable ethics.

Interview with European Commissioner for Defence and Space, **Andrius Kubilius.Donald Trump** is calling on every country belonging to the International Criminal Court to quit, with Washington also preparing sweeping sanctions aimed at crippling the court and its judges. We talk to**Chloé Ridel**, a French socialist in the European Parliament, who sits on the Justice Committee.

As the Middle East war disrupts global oil supplies and refining costs soar, European consumers are feeling the pinch more and more. We interview **Osama Rizvi**, Global Market & Product Strategist at Primary Vision, who analyses the situation.

We also speak to **Kamal Amakrane**, Managing Director of the UN’s Rising Nations Initiative, about a landmark UN declaration aimed at ensuring that countries whose territory may become uninhabitable because of rising sea levels do not lose their statehood or their place in the international system.
