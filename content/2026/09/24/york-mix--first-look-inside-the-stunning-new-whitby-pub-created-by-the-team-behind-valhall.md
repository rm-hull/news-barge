Welcome to the House of Mëmento Möri.

It’s the newest pub in North Yorkshire – but you wouldn’t know it once you stepped inside.

Found on Grape Lane in Whitby, the pub has been created from a former woodworker’s shop.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/house-of-memento-mori-whitby-pub-4-815x1500.webp)

And now it’s like stepping back in time, with its leather armchairs, wooden panelling and beamed ceiling.

Every care has been taken to create something special in this Grade II listed building. And given that the team behind it created Valhalla, the Viking inn on Patrick Pool in York, that should come as no surprise.

Matt Bedingham and Vincent Roberts first saw the building in January 2024 and bought it last year.

“We always fancied a second venue and Whitby was always up there at the top of the list. It’s a place we’ve both enjoyed visiting since childhood.

“We love its history, people and vibe. When the building on Grape Lane came up we couldn’t resist and decided to jump in.”

At that point “it was in need of a lot of work,” Matt admitted.

But as it opens its doors tomorrow, it brings a stunning gothic addition to a town with so much related history.

## That name

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/house-of-memento-mori-whitby-pub-2.webp)

So the name… Why House of Mëmento Möri?

Memento Mori is Latin and means “remember you must die”, Matt says. “Which you’d be forgiven for thinking is maybe a bit macabre! But that’s missing the point.

“That word ‘remember’ is crucial! In simple terms the meaning behind Memento Mori is to never lose sight that time is valuable and you should live a full and good life.”

“There’s many interpretations and take-aways from Memento Mori. For us a key one – perhaps not surprisingly given the industry we are in – is that in our fast paced, modern world it’s easy to lose sight of what really matters. And we can often take our time for granted.

“We should maybe take the time to enjoy the ride more. Spend some time with friends and loved ones and enjoy ourselves… Ideally in an independent bar with some excellent beer or ale and some great music to boot!”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/house-of-memento-mori-whitby-pub-3.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/house-of-memento-mori-whitby-pub-5-792x1500.webp)

Matt said the bar is for all. “We give our customers and friends places with unique interiors and places that, we certainly like to think, are built on great meaning. All wrapped up in something with a historical nod.

“Oh, and you won’t find anything but the likes of Led Zepp, Motörhead and Iron Maiden playing.

“We push to be as friendly as you’re likely to find. So in Whitby, like in Valhalla York, please bring along the kids, and your four legged friends if you’d like. They’re very welcome!

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/valhalla-vince-matt.webp)

House of Mëmento Möri isn’t doing food yet, although that is planned further down the line. “We’ve got a very well stocked bar, great coffee, some of the finest spirits you’ll find and we will be hosting events through the week once we get into the swing of things,” Matt told ***YorkMix***.

He added: “We hope we’ve done the people of Whitby proud and we hope the people that come through our door like what we’ve done and get the positive spirit of Memento Mori.”

House of Mëmento Möri opens tomorrow (Friday 25 September) and is open at these times:

Monday to Thursday: 12pm-10.15pm  
Friday-Saturday: 11.30am-11pm
