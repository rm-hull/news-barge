A HOUSING association has been awarded Pledge Pioneer status for its commitment to LGBTQ+ inclusion.

Manningham Housing Association (MHA), which manages more than 1,400 homes across Bradford and Keighley, received the LGBTQ+ Housing Pledge Pioneer accreditation for its efforts to create safe, welcoming, and inclusive services for LGBTQ+ residents.

The accreditation is valid until 2028 and recognises MHA’s work to embed equality, diversity, and inclusion throughout its policies, staff training, and community engagement.

It follows a detailed evidence-gathering and assessment process led by Stonewall Housing in partnership with HouseProud, which supports social housing providers in improving the experiences of LGBTQ+ residents.

Lee Bloomfield, chief executive at MHA, said: "We are incredibly proud to have achieved LGBTQ+ Housing Pledge Pioneer status.

"For us, this is more than achieving an accreditation, it is about assuring our LGBTQ+ residents that that they should feel safe, secure and supported as part of the MHA family.

"The accreditation also demonstrates that we taking practical action, listening to our residents, and delivering high-quality services in communities where everyone can be themselves."

To achieve the status, housing providers must meet three core commitments: involving LGBTQ+ residents in decision-making, increasing LGBTQ+ visibility, and delivering relevant staff training.

MHA’s submission included evidence from across the organisation, showing how inclusion is reflected in its strategies, staff development, and partnership work.

The assessment panel described MHA’s submission as "rounded, thoughtfully considered and strong across several areas," taking into account its size, geographical location, and community context.

The panel also praised MHA’s awareness of intersectionality and the ways in which residents’ experiences of inequality can overlap and shape their lives.

They encouraged MHA to continue developing its work by creating more opportunities for LGBTQ+ residents from minority backgrounds to be heard.

The assessment also highlighted MHA’s partnerships with organisations like HouseProud North and its support for LGBTQ+ charities and regional equality, diversity and inclusion activity.

Specialised LGBTQ+ training has also been delivered to staff, including work to better understand the experiences of young LGBTQ+ people in their homes.

MHA has made clear that the LGBTQ+ Housing Pledge is not a stand-alone initiative but part of its wider mission to build inclusive communities where everyone feels welcome and supported.

The organisation says it will continue to develop its work in this area and build on the progress recognised by the new accreditation.
