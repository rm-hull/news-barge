COMMUNITY pubs across Yorkshire are set to host a disco-themed payday celebration later this month.

The Crown Hotel and Malt Kiln Inn in Bradford will take part in the event, taking place from September 25 to 27 and featuring disco classics, ABBA hits and glitter shots.

Both pubs are part of Proper Pubs, the community wet-led operator division of Admiral Taverns, which has more than 200 pubs across England, Scotland and Wales.

A spokesperson for Proper Pubs said: "This is set to be a sparkling payday weekend not to be missed!"

The event will offer Tofka Glitter Shots, including the 6-Shot Crown serve, Gold Brew (Tofka with Tia Maria) and Solid Gold (Tofka with Take Blanco Tequila).

Special offers on drinks will also be available throughout the weekend.

The full list of participating Yorkshire pubs includes venues in Keighley, Leeds, Hull, Sheffield, Grimsby, Wakefield and other towns.

Other pubs in the region include the Cavendish in Keighley and the Black Lion Hotel in Leeds.
