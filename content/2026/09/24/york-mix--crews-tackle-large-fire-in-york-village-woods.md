Three fire crews tackled a blaze in woodland last night (Wednesday).

They were called to the incident in **Kexby** near York at**5.30pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Two crews from Acomb and one from York responded to a report of a fire in a wooded area.

#### Kexby

“On arrival crews located a fire measuring approximately 50 by 10 metres in the woodland.

“Crews used hose reel jets and wildfire tools to extinguish and dampen down the area and a thermal imaging camera to check for hot spots.”
