A “beautiful” restaurant in York has been named one of the best neighbourhood restaurants in the UK.

Condé Nast Traveller has revealed a list of the best neighbourhood restaurants around the UK that are worth planning a staycation around.

The publication said neighbourhood restaurants are “smaller and more intimate”, offering guests a break away from crowded tourist spots.

Melton’s in York made it onto the list thanks to its “friendly and inviting atmosphere” that makes people feel “instantly at home”.

## What is Melton’s like?

On the restaurant, Condé Nast Traveller said: “South Bank and Scarcroft Road, where Melton’s is located in York, have previously been described as a ‘Goldilocks neighbourhood’ where everything is just right.

“For the past three decades, Melton’s has fit right in, winning over locals and visitors alike with exceptional food and a warm ambience.

“Run by husband-and-wife team Michael and Lucy Hjort, this fine dining restaurant manages to create a relaxed, friendly and inviting atmosphere – no intimidating white tablecloths, stuffy waiters or scary wine lists here.

“It’s a real community gem, where locals are greeted by name and first-time visitors feel instantly at home.

“The three-course menu is limited, with just three options per course (one meat, one fish, one vegetarian) to choose from, but this is a sign of a restaurant that is really confident in what it does, and you won’t walk away disappointed.”

Melton’s has been featured in the Michelin Guide and was awarded three AA Rosettes in 2025.

The restaurant offers a range of British dishes with three courses costing £85 per person.

There are a few dishes to choose from in terms of its starters, including Orkney scallop and Isle of Wight tomatoes with black garlic, fennel, and basil pistou.

The three mains include Yorkshire lamb, Cornish cod, and kohlrabi fondant.

To finish off your meal, you can choose from a range of desserts such as apple crumble soufflé and the selection of British cheeses served with biscuits, chutney, and Yorkshire tea loaf.

You can also opt for the tasting menu, which costs £98 per person.
