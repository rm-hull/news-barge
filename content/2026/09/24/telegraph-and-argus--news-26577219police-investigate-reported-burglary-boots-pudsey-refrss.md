Police are investigating after a report of a burglary at a business in Pudsey and here's what we know so far.

The incident was reported on Galloway Lane in the early hours of Wednesday, September 23.

A source told the T&A: "There has been a break in at the Boots pharmacy on Galloway Road."

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are investigating after a report of a burglary at a business on Galloway Lane, Pudsey in the early hours of Wednesday 23 September.

"Enquiries are ongoing."
