Families, art lovers and fans of Beatrix Potter are being invited for a final chance to see dozens of Peter Rabbit sculptures brought together before they are auctioned to raise money for charity.

The Farewell Event will take place at Newcastle Civic Centre on Saturday, October 10 and Sunday, October 11, marking the end of the Peter Rabbit: Tales on the Tyne public art trail.

The event will showcase 43 large and 53 small Peter Rabbit sculptures, which were displayed across Newcastle and North Tyneside during the nine-week trail.

![]()w *(Image: Supplied)*

Six additional sculptures, created exclusively for the auction, will also be on display.

Visitors will have the chance to win their own Peter Rabbit sculpture and a car through a raffle.

The event will include face painting, refreshments, and a final opportunity to purchase trail merchandise.

Some of the artists who decorated the sculptures will be present to answer questions about their designs.

Visitors who followed the trail map but missed some locations can also collect final codes to unlock offers and claim their finisher’s certificate and sticker.

Timed entry slots are available throughout both days, with a quiet hour from 10am for those who would benefit from a calmer environment.

A spokesperson said: "All proceeds support the work of St Oswald’s Hospice."

Tickets are on sale now at www.talesonthetyne.co.uk/auction.

They cost £8 per adult, £3 per child, and are free for children aged three and under.

A family or group ticket, admitting up to four people including up to two adults, costs £15.

The trail was delivered in partnership with Wild in Art, The World of Peter Rabbit, Newcastle City Council, North Tyneside Council, Northumbrian Water, and North Standard.
