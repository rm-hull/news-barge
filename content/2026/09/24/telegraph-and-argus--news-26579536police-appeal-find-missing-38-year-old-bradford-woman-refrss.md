Police are appealing for information to help find a missing woman from Bradford.

Qamari, 38, has been reported missing from Bradford and was last seen wearing black or dark blue tracksuit bottoms, a beige jacket and a jumper.

She is described as 5ft 5in tall, with black hair and blonde highlights.

Anyone with information or sightings has been asked to call 101, quoting log 1829.

West Yorkshire Police can also be contacted through a LiveChat.
