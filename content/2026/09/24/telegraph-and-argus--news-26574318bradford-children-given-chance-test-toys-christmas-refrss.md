A DREAM job is up for grabs.

Children in Bradford are being invited to apply for the chance to become Mini Toy Testers, with successful applicants testing out the latest toys and taking them home just in time for Christmas.

The opportunity comes from the Toy Retailers Association, which is assembling a panel of young testers to try out toys featured on the 2026 DreamToys list—an annual guide predicting the most popular toys for the festive season.

Paul Reader, chairman of the DreamToys Selection Panel, said: "Each year, the DreamToys list shines a spotlight on the toys that are set to capture children’s imaginations and become the must-have gifts at Christmas.

"After the incredible response to last year’s search for our Mini Toy Testers, we’re back and now looking for children from Bradford to apply."

Eight children will be selected to test 15 different toys, games, and gadgets in Birmingham on Friday, October 30, and will also get the 15 toys for themselves.

The scheme is open to children aged three to 12, with parents or guardians able to apply on their behalf.

Applications must be submitted online by midnight on Saturday, October 17.
