A woman who lost her brother to blood cancer in August has raised an incredible £8,300 after completing the Great North Run.

Becky Flint, 40, from Guisborough, aimed to raise an ambitious £7,500 for Blood Cancer UK, but has already surpassed that target with donations still coming in.

Becky completed the Great North Run in memory of her brother Matthew Flint, 43, also of Guisborough.

Along with running partner Hannah Hardy, Becky completed the half-marathon in two hours and six minutes.

She said: "When Matt relapsed in May, I decided I wasn’t going to do the GNR because of how devastated I was. However, my brother was so brave, so strong and so determined to fight, I decided to use his strength and do the run.

![]()Becky Flint and Hannah Hardy ahead of the GNR *(Image: Becky Flint)*

"It’s because of him I’ve been able to raise so much money and hopefully help others like him. I wanted to make him proud and I’m just so sad he’s not here to see how highly thought of he was.

"I thoroughly enjoyed the run. It’s my first ever half marathon. I was very emotional.

"I would firstly like to thank my running partners, Hannah Hardy, who was with me on the day, and my fiancé, David Howells. They’ve been by my side every step of the way and I couldn’t have done it without their encouragement and love. I’d also like to thank my family and friends. I’ve had so much support and feel incredibly blessed.

![]()Becky Flint and Hannah Hardy during the GNR *(Image: Becky Flint)*

"Lastly, I want to thank my brother. My brother’s taught me to love, be fair, be respectful and to never stop fighting. I’m going to use his example for the rest of my life."

Becky and her family's fundraising plans continue, with her sister-in-law Nichola Flint entering next year's GNR for Blood Cancer UK.
