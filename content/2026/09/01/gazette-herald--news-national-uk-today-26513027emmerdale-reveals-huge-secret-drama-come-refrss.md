A long-buried secret has finally come to light in the Dales.

On Tuesday night’s episode of Emmerdale, viewers learned that Detective Thea Ramsden, played by Chelsea Edge, is the long-lost daughter of Kim Tate.

The revelation came during a flashback scene showing a younger Kim, portrayed by Claire King, in a hospital’s baby unit.


## Shocking revelation on Emmerdale

A nurse asked Kim if she was sure about giving up her baby for adoption and whether she had chosen a name.

Kim replied that she had always liked the name "Anthea."

The camera then cut to the present day, focusing on Ramsden’s police name badge, confirming her true identity.

Ms Edge said: "I have loved joining Emmerdale and keeping this secret.

"I hope that now it’s out in the open the viewers will follow the story and see how Thea’s determination and bitterness develops.

"It’s clear she’s very keen to understand why Kim, who seemingly has everything, status, wealth and power to give her away all those years ago.

"As to how far the apple falls from the tree… I guess we’ll have to wait and see, but there’s certainly a lot more drama to come."


### 
  **Kim Tate remains in the dark**

Emmerdale producer Laura Shaw said that while viewers now know the truth, Kim remains in the dark.

Ms Shaw said: "While the viewers have been granted access to the mystery surrounding DS Ramsden’s true identity, it will be some time before Kim suspects that the child she surrendered is operating within her immediate circle.

"She is completely unaware that the daughter she left behind is actually right under her nose."

The storyline adds a new layer to Kim’s character, who is currently trying to stop Joe Tate from giving up Baby Tate for adoption.

Ms Shaw said this plotline brings back painful memories for Kim.

Emmerdale airs at 8 pm weeknights on ITV1 and STV or from 7AM on ITVX and STV Player with the episodes also being uploaded to the official Emmerdale YouTube channel.
