## Our live feed has now finished.

- Motorists are being reminded of heavy and building traffic in York city centre after part of the city's inner ring road was closed.

That's all for now. Thanks for following our live coverage today.

This just in from Yorkshire Water, which says traffic measures will remain in place until the road reinstatement has set.

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson said: “We’re currently working with the local council to assess and repair damage on Fishergate following the storm over the weekend, which we understand caused water to push through gaps under a manhole cover and frame, lifting the road surface.

## Rush hour delays

## Traffic builds along Margaret Street

Seven buses, including a City Sightseeing Tour bus, are in standstill traffic along Walmgate and along Margaret Street.

Contractors are yet to revisit Fishergate to remove the rubble and re-tarmac the road.

   

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## Traffic building in Walmgate

Heavy traffic and delays are continue to affect Walmgate, as diversions - coupled with usual morning traffic - cause standstill queues.

   

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## All traffic, including buses, being diverted

Traffic heading eastbound from Tower Street is being diverted through to Walmgate Bar - causing heavy and building traffic.

   

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## Road to remain closed "for a few days"

A recent City of York Council update said Fishergate would remain closed "for a few days" following the repair work, which was started by Yorkshire Water yesterday.

Vehicles heading eastbound from Tower Street are being diverted via Piccadilly, Lead Mill Lane, Margaret Street and Walmgate.

   

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## Road surface removed

The Fishergate pedestrian crossing, which was uplifted during the storm, has been removed by Yorkshire Water.

A pile of rubble, which was cut with a concrete saw yesterday, can be seen next to the cordoned off area.
