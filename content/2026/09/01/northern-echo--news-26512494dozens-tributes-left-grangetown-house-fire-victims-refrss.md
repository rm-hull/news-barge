Balloons, flowers, and teddies are among the dozens more tributes left at the scene of a tragic house fire that killed a seven-year-old girl and her aunt.

  __Natalie McDonald, 34, and Valentina Foster died and four others were injured__ in a house blaze in Grangetown, near Middlesbrough, on August 26.

The incident on Birchington Avenue triggered an outpouring of tributes to the youngster and her aunt, with bouquets of flowers left beside the devastating scene.

  Valentina Foster, seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, have been named locally as the two victims who died following a house fire in Grangetown.

New pictures taken today (September 1) show how dozens more bouquets of flowers have been laid on the grass beside the home.

Numerous teddies, emotional notes, and a small bag of sweets were also among the items paying tribute to the pair.
