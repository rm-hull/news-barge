Police are "concerned" for the welfare of a missing man who disappeared at the weekend.

Joseph, 30, of Stanley, was last spoken to on August 29 and had plans to travel to a family event outside the area, which he did not attend.

He is described as 6ft tall, of stocky build, with a large tattoo extending from his neck to his chest, and speaks with a Manchester accent.

A spokesperson from Durham Police has urged anyone with information to come forward.

They said: "If you have seen Joseph or know where he is, please contact police on 101, quoting incident number 439 of 30 August."
