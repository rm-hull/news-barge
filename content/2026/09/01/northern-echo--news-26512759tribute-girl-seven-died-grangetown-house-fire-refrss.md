The family of a “beloved” seven-year-old girl who died in a devastating house fire alongside her aunt have paid tribute to her, saying their lives “have changed forever”.

Valentina Foster died alongside Natalie McDonald, 34, when a house set ablaze on Birchington Avenue in Grangetown, near Middlesbrough, on August 26.

The incident triggered an outpouring of tributes for the youngster and her aunt, who Cleveland Police say has yet to be formally identified.

And today (September 1), as a man appeared in court charged with two counts of murder and a string of other offences, Valentina’s family have paid tribute to their daughter.

  Valentina Foster, seven, and Natalie McDonald, 34, have been named locally as the two victims who died following a house fire in Grangetown. 

*(Image: Provided)*

They said: “It is with overwhelming sadness that we share the heartbreaking news that our beloved daughter has passed away.

“She was deeply loved and will be missed beyond words by her family, friends, and everyone whose life she touched.

“Our lives have changed forever, and we are struggling to come to terms with this devastating loss. Our daughter will always be in our hearts, and her memory will live with us forever.

  Valentina Foster 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

“Forever missed by her brothers and sisters.”

Dozens of tributes have been left at the scene of the house fire, which also saw four other people injured.

Two teenage girls have both been discharged from hospital but a man and a woman remain there receiving treatment for their injuries.

  The tribute comes just hours after __Jaden Wright, 23, of no fixed address, appeared at Teesside Magistrates’ Court charged with two counts of murder.__

  Police at the scene of the fire on Birchington Avenue in Grangetown 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Wright, who was also charged with two counts of Section 18 grievous bodily harm with intent and two counts of attempted Section 18 grievous bodily harm with intent, entered no pleas during the short hearing on Tuesday morning.

Wearing a grey tracksuit, he was remanded in custody until his next appearance at Teesside Crown Court on September 3.

A 31-year-old man who was previously arrested in connection with the incident was released without charge on Sunday.

  Floral tributes left at the scene of the devastating fire on Birchington Avenue, in Grangetown 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

The force is not treating the fire as linked to the fatal crash on the A66 which killed seven, including police officers PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough.

Katie Smith, senior investigating officer from Northumbria Police, said: “This has been a truly tragic incident, and my thoughts remain with the families of all those involved.

“I would like to thank local residents for their support and patience during this complex investigation.
