The YorkMix Family Raceday is back this Sunday! Here’s everything you need to know about all the family fun and racing entertainment.

***YorkMix*** have once again teamed up with York Racecourse to put on a day of sport and fun for all ages.

The event will include record prize money for a seven-race card, a free-to-all YorkMix Entertainment Zone, and appearances from a much-loved children’s character.

The YorkMix Family Raceday will take place on **Sunday 6 September**. Gates open from 11:15am, with the first race at 1:30pm. Take a look at all the details below.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/gruffalo-york-racecourse-yorkmix-family-raceday.webp)

The special guest this year is none other than the star of millions of books and a TV series or two, **the Gruffalo**!

A range of activities will also be taking place in the YorkMix Entertainment Marquee, including giant inflatables, diggerland, a children’s zoo, zorb football, face painting, and lots more.

Part of this Sunday is to offer a big stage to the young riders taking part in the Pony Racing Series.

## Parking and travel

#### Parking

There is a plentiful supply of free car parking on racedays.

#### Shuttle Bus

The 197 Shuttle Bus service links York Railway Station to the Racecourse.

**From York Station** The shuttle buses operate from Stand RJ opposite the Railway Station entrance between 11am and 1.00pm.

**From the Racecourse** The shuttle buses return to the Railway Station from Campleshon Road, next to the Racecourse entrance, from 4pm onwards.

Fares: Return – £4

Single – £2.50

## Dress code

For the YorkMix Family Raceday the County Stand dress code is relaxed, with smart casual being the advice. Gentlemen are not required to wear jacket, collar and tie.
