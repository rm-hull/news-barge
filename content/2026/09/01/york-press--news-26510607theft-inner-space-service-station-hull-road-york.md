POLICE are investigating an alleged theft from a York service station.

North Yorkshire Police has issued CCTV of a person officers would like to speak to in connection with the alcohol theft from the Inner Space Service Station in Hull Road on July 9.

  


A force spokesperson said: “If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance by emailing [scott.nixon@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:scott.nixon@northyorkshire.police.uk)

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

“Please quote reference 12260129950 when providing information.”
