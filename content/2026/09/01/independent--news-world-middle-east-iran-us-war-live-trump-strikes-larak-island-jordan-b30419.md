## Trump threatens to hit Iran ‘hard’

Donald Trump has threatened to “hit them hard” after the US and Iran exchanged fire for the first time in a month, warning that “there will be a response” following Iranian missile strikes on US military bases in Jordan.

He called Iran “officially a failed nation without an airforce, a navy or a currency”.

The latest escalation began when US forces struck two Iranian missile launchers on Larak Island in the Strait of Hormuz, saying Iran was preparing to deploy naval mines in the crucial waterway.

Iran retaliated by targeting US bases, while the UAE said it intercepted an Iranian drone.

The renewed fighting comes as Washington intensifies sanctions aimed at crippling Iran’s economy and seeks to loosen Tehran’s control over the Strait of Hormuz.
