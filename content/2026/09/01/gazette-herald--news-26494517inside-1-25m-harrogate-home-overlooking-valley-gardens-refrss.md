A six-bedroom Victorian house is for sale in Harrogate with offers over £1,250,000.

The property, in Valley Drive, has four storeys of "versatile accommodation" and is in an elevated position overlooking Harrogate’s iconic Valley Gardens.

The property is said to have a significant historical connection, as it was once home to musician Thomas Rutling.

It dates back to the early 1900s and has been restored from a collection of individual apartments into a four-storey family home.

The property also has period features, including high ceilings, bay windows, and original fireplaces.

There is a lounge with bespoke cabinetry and a feature fireplace, which has folding doors that lead to the dining room.

The kitchen boasts a Rangemaster, central island, and bespoke pantry storage.

The master bedroom offers park views, a dressing room, and an en-suite.

There is a dedicated laundry room on the second floor with bespoke shaker storage and a sink.

The house is said to have outstanding potential for multi-generational living due to its versatile accommodation.

There is also a private driveway providing two off-street parking spaces.

The estate agent, Enfields Luxe, added that the house is in a prime town centre setting and is moments from high street amenities and Harrogate railway station.

There are six double bedrooms and versatile living spaces throughout.

The property's original Victorian floor tiles and an intricate stained-glass window greet you at the entrance.

The lounge features a cast iron fireplace, a ceiling rose, café-style shutters, and luxury carpets.

The dining room, connected to the lounge by triple folding French doors, has parquet wooden flooring and dark panelled walls.

The kitchen has dual-aspect windows, a Rangemaster oven, and shaker-style cabinetry.

A split stable door provides exterior side access to the driveway.

The property also has a family bathroom and a snug, which could be used as an office or music room.

The second floor has three double bedrooms and a three-piece shower room.

The current owners use one of the bedrooms as a sitting room.

The upstairs laundry room, with shaker-style storage cupboards, is described as a "gamechanger" by the current owners.

The fourth floor has two large double rooms with sloped ceilings and Velux windows.

There is also a four-piece bathroom suite with a roll-top slipper bath under the roof skylight.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact the estate agents Enfields Luxe on 01977 308848.
