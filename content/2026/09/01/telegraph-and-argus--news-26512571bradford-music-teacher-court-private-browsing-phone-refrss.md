A former freelance music teacher who was banned from the profession for life after downloading indecent images of children has been given another suspended jail sentence for breaching his Sexual Harm Prevention Order.

Back in December 2023 music teacher Donal Donnelly was given a suspended eight-month jail sentence at York Crown Court after he was caught with more than 2,000 indecent images.

As part of his sentence the then 58-year-old, of Rossefield Road, Heaton, Bradford, was also ordered to comply with the terms of a five-year Sexual Harm Prevention Order.

But in April last year, a routine visit by public protection officers led to the discovery that Donnelly had been using “private browsing” mode on his mobile phone and its internet history was not being retained.

Donnelly, now 61, admitted in his police interview that he had deleted his internet history because he was embarrassed about viewing adult pornography.

The case against Donnelly did not come before the courts until last month and when he appeared before the magistrates he pleaded guilty to two breaches of the Sexual Harm Prevention Order.

The case was then committed for sentence and today (Tues) Donnelly appeared before a judge at Bradford Crown Court.

Recorder Ayesha Smart said the Sexual Harm Prevention Order had been imposed to protect the public, particularly children, and to enable the authorities to monitor Donnelly’s internet use.

She accepted that Donnelly had committed the offences out of embarrassment, but she said he had deliberately breached a court order.

The judge decided that the appropriate sentence was six months in prison, but current sentencing legislation means that all prison sentences under 12 months should be suspended unless there are exceptional circumstances.

Recorder Smart ordered that the six months should be suspended for 18 months and Donnelly must also comply with 30 rehabilitation activity requirement days.

Donnelly will now be subject to another Sexual Harm Prevention Order which will run until 2031.

In 2025 Donnelly was banned from teaching for life after a hearing before the Teaching Regulation Agency.
