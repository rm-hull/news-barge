FIRE crews are expected to remain at the scene of a major blaze at a farm in North Yorkshire ‘for some time’.

The fire, at the farm near Kirby Misperton, started during a violent thunderstorm on Sunday evening.

The crews, from Malton, Kirkbymoorside, Scarborough, along with Tadcaster’s water bowser, responded to the two barns on fire at 8.32pm.

  


A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said one crew from Malton remained in attendance.

They said: “Farm machinery is being used to break up bales and crews are using a hose reel jet to cool machinery and dampen down. Crews will remain there for some time.”

They said one of the barns had been empty while the other had straw in it.

Crews had dealt with the incident by setting up a dam to cool three propane cylinders.

“Crews monitored the temperature using a thermal imaging camera.

“Four hose reel jets were used to extinguish the fire.

“Fire crews remain in scene at this incident, fire investigation to be conducted in daylight hours.”

  The aftermath of Sunday evening's barn fire near Kirby Misperton 

*(Image: Max Downing)*

Posting on social media, property owner William Easterby said: “Just to let everyone know Lottie, Miles and I are ok after the barn fires last night. Thank you to everyone who came up to help us and for all your kind messages.

“We are extremely grateful to all the Fire crews who attended and did such an amazing job to contain the fires.”

North Yorkshire Cllr Steve Mason, who lives nearby said: “It was quite a shocking scene on Sunday night, but its welcome news that everyone, as well as the animals are ok.

“The emergency services were amazing yet again.”

Local people responded to the incidents with messages of support.

One neighbour said: “Pleased to hear you’re all ok and well done to those firefighters.”

Another said: “Good to hear that you are all safe and ok after what must have been a terrifying night.”

While another resident added: “What a massive shock for you, so pleased you’re all safe. Please let us know if you need anything.”

It came as York and areas of North Yorkshire experienced a ‘spectacular’ storm for several hours on Sunday night.

The storm, which included heavy rain, thunder and frequent lightning, slowly headed eastwards throughout the evening.

The Met Office placed the area under a yellow weather warning for heavy showers and thunderstorms for Sunday, August 30, which concluded at midnight.

At around 8pm, the storm hit York, moving steadily east with frequent lightning, thunder and heavy rain.

One reader said the horizon was ‘flashing and flickering for almost four hours’.

Another described it as ‘a spectacular show all over’.

Other comments added: “We were in Whitby and it was beautiful weather all day but drove home in this thunderstorm! We had just stopped to charge the car up when we heard the biggest crack of thunder we’ve ever heard.”

Some people commented that they were ‘terrified’ and ‘petrified’ by the storm, while one said: ‘the power of nature can be both destructive and awesome at the same time’.

The Met Office forecast for today through to Friday remains changeable with showers or longer spells of rain for most, although some drier and brighter spells will also develop. Temperatures will be near normal for early September, although often mild by night.
