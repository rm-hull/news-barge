A SURGERY in North Yorkshire is facing issues with its telephone lines this morning (Tuesday).

Derwent Practice in Norton is unable to receive or make phone calls.

A statement from the surgery said: "We are currently experiencing technical issues with our telephone lines due to a network problem with our telephone provider, which is affecting our ability to receive and make telephone calls.

"We apologise for any inconvenience caused and appreciate your patience while the issue is being resolved."
