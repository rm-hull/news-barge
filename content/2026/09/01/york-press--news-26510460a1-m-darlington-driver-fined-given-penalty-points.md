A man has been fined and handed six penalty points after failing to provide information about who was driving a BMW following an alleged motoring offence.

Ayman Al Ammar, 24, of Auckland Avenue, Darlington, appeared at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court on Monday, August 10.

The court heard that on June 30 last year, Al Ammar failed to provide information requested on behalf of Durham Constabulary’s Chief Officer about the identity of the driver of a BMW 118.

The vehicle was alleged to have been involved in an offence.

Al Ammar had previously entered a not guilty plea in January, but changed his plea to guilty at the August hearing. His guilty plea was taken into account and the court applied a 10 per cent discount.

He was fined £308 and his driving record was endorsed with six penalty points.

Al Ammar was also ordered to pay £400 in prosecution costs and a £123 victim surcharge, taking the total financial penalty to £831.

A collection order was made, with payments of £100 a month beginning on August 24.

The court records also show that a separate allegation that the same BMW had exceeded the 70mph speed limit on the A1(M) between Junction 61 at Bowburn and Junction 60 at Bradbury on May 22, 2025, was withdrawn.
