## Take a look inside refurbished six-bedroom Harrogate home for £875,000

A six-bedroom terraced house in Harrogate, which has been comprehensively refurbished, is now for sale for £875,000 *(Image: Myrings Estate Agents)*

A six-bedroom terraced house which has been comprehensively refurbished is now for sale in Harrogate for £875,000.

Arranged over four floors, the home offers more than 2,200 square feet of accommodation.

The recent renovation included new heating, plumbing and hot water systems, as well as a full rewire, new doors, windows, and repaired or replaced floorboards.

Wi-Fi cabling has been installed to support modern living.

Located in Spring Mount, the property is within walking distance of Harrogate town centre, shops, cafés, and amenities.

The house is also close to reputable local schools and public transport links to Leeds and York.

The ground floor contains an entrance hall, lounge with bay window, dining room with doors onto the garden, and a kitchen with island, utility area and bi-fold doors.

Underfloor heating is fitted throughout the ground floor.

On the first floor, there are three double bedrooms, including a principal bedroom with en-suite shower room and twin sinks.

The floor also contains a modern family bathroom and landing storage.

Two more double bedrooms can be found on the second floor, one with a skylight and en-suite shower room.

The top floor contains a sixth double bedroom, fitted wardrobes, skylights, and a separate WC.

The property has an enclosed rear patio garden.
