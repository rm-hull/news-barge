Bettys Café Tea Rooms at RHS Garden Harlow Carr will temporarily close for a refresh from 7 September.

It will be back from 5 October with a brand-new look, and the warm welcome customers know and love.

Bettys is a much-loved part of a visit to RHS Garden Harlow Carr, and the team has said that is sorry for any inconvenience caused if the temporary closure changes customers’ plans.

During the closure, both the café and shop will be shut while work takes place to refresh the destination, which sits at the heart of one of Yorkshire’s most beautiful gardens.


![harlow carr bettys](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Harlow-Carr-182.webp)


RHS Garden Harlow Carr will remain open as usual throughout the refurbishment, with a range of alternative food and drink options available for visitors. Devoted Bettys fans can still visit nearby Bettys Harrogate or shop online for freshly baked Bettys favourites delivered to their door.


**Liz Thwaite, Head of Site at RHS Garden Harlow Carr said:**

We’re delighted to see the refurbishment of Bettys Harlow Carr and to continue our long-standing partnership with such an iconic Yorkshire brand.

We know Bettys is a much-loved part of a visit to the garden, and we’re excited to welcome guests back to a refreshed café this autumn.

While the work takes place, RHS Garden Harlow Carr remains open as usual, with plenty for visitors to enjoy, including a range of additional catering and refreshment options available across the garden throughout the closure period.



When Bettys Harlow Carr reopens on 5 October, customers can look forward to a refreshed café experience.

There will be a new Bettys To Go Window at the entrance to RHS Harlow Carr, making it easier for visitors


**Nicky Sellers, Branch Director at Bettys said:**

We’re excited to be investing in Bettys Harlow Carr and creating an even more welcoming experience for our customers.

The café tea rooms are a very special part of a visit to RHS Garden Harlow Carr, and we look forward to welcoming everyone back in early October.
