The Trump administration is set to host its own *Shark Tan* k-style competition later this month, with rapper Nicki Minaj and*Superman* actor Dean Cain as judges.

Minaj and Cain, both ardent supporters of President Donald Trump, are two of the celebrity guests who will feature in the Small Business Administration’s “Million Dollar Pitch Competition,” where five entrepreneurs will compete to win a share of the $1 million prize.

Airbnb co-founder and U.S. Chief Design Officer Joe Gebbia will join Minaj on the judging panel, along with private equity fund manager Grant Cardone.

The agency’s administrator, Kelly Loeffler, said the Trump-supporting stars “will only add to the excitement of this historic celebration of free enterprise” in a news release.

“From building globally recognized personal and business brands and multibillion-dollar portfolios to launching innovative wellness solutions and digital platforms, each brings a unique, real‑world perspective on business growth and impact,” Loeffler said.

![The Trump administration is set to host its own Shark Tan k-style competition later this month, with rapper Nicki Minaj as a judge and Superman actor Dean Cain as host](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/02/13/06/GettyImages-2258686448.jpg)

“Their presence will be invaluable in highlighting the unique and inspiring stories of the five finalists competing for $1 million in growth capital, and the importance of Main Street businesses in powering American strength,” Loeffler added.

The competition’s format is similar to the hit TV show *Shark Tank* and the president’s former reality show*The Apprentice,* which aired on NBC from 2004 to 2017.

Minaj has become one of the president’s most vocal allies in the music industry and has been hosted by Trump at the White House several times. She helped the president to launch the administration’s “Trump Accounts” earlier this year.

The 43-year-old rapper previously opened up about her support for Trump. “When I saw how he was being treated over and over and over, I just couldn't handle it,” she said on *The Katie Miller Podcas* t earlier this year. “I felt that...a lot of that bullying, and the smear campaigns and all of the lying, I felt that that had been done to me for so many years.”

![The competition’s format is similar to The Apprentice , originally hosted by Trump and which aired on NBC from 2004 to 2017](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/04/30/3/35/GettyImages-57049425.jpg)

Cain similarly declared his “love” for Trump in a 2025 interview and endorsed the president during the 2016, 2020 and 2024 presidential elections. “Trump is actually one of the most empathetic, wonderful, generous people you’ll ever meet,” he told *Variety*. 

The former *Superman* star attended a Trump administration event in August to promote the president’s “No Tax on Tips” policy.

The competition is part of the Freedom 250 celebrations marking America’s birthday. The White House-aligned group was also behind the Great American State Fair, marked by several controversies, including cancelations from musicians and weather-related closures, and the Freedom 250 Grand Prix in Washington, D.C.

According to the agency, the $1 million cash prize is being funded by Clover Network, Inc, a payments processing platform.
