A MAN in his 50s was sexually assaulted and robbed at a bus stop in Scarborough last month.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a man was attacked at a bus shelter under Spa Bridge between Monday, August 10 and Wednesday, August 26 - with the force adding that they are unable to know exactly when the incident took place.

A spokesperson for the county police said: "The victim, a man in his 50s, was approached by a woman who struck up a conversation with him and offered him a drink while he was in a bus shelter under Spa Bridge.

"He reluctantly accepted the drink then lost consciousness and was sexually assaulted. The victim also had cash and belongings stolen from him including some artwork he was carrying."

The suspect is described as a woman in her 30s with red streaks in her hair, wearing heavy makeup, a long black skirt, and a black top with white markings on the sleeves.

Police added that they are also keen to speak to a member of the public who approached the victim while he was sitting in the bus shelter after the attack and asked if he was okay.

Officers believe the victim may have appeared disoriented at the time.

They are also appealing to anyone who may have found a discarded wallet or artwork, described as black and white pencil-drawn landscapes, to get in touch.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing [laurie.simpson1@northyorkshirepolice.uk](mailto:laurie.simpson1@northyorkshirepolice.uk), submitting information online, or by calling 101.

Please quote reference 12260166702 when providing information.
