A crash on the A1M is causing slow traffic this morning (Tuesday).

The AA is reporting that a collision involving a lorry happened shortly before 6am.

It says: “Queueing traffic due to crash, now on the shoulder on A1(M) Northbound from J44 A64 (York / Tadcaster) to J45 A659 Wattlesyke (Boston Spa).”

#### Crash location

Police are at the scene diverting traffic.
