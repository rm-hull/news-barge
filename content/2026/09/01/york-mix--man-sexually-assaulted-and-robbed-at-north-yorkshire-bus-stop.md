Police are searching for a woman who apparently drugged a man at a bus stop before sexually assaulting and robbing him.

The victim, a man in his 50s, was approached by a woman who struck up a conversation with him while he was in a bus shelter under Spa Bridge.

She offered him a drink which he reluctantly accepted. He then lost consciousness and was sexually assaulted.

#### Spa Bridge, Scarborough

The victim also had cash and belongings stolen including some artwork he was carrying.

It is not known exactly when the incident took place, but it was between Monday 10 August and Wednesday 26 August.

The suspect is described as being

- a woman in her 30s
- in heavy makeup
- with red streaks in her hair
- wearing a long, black skirt
- and a black top with white markings on the sleeves.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Following the incident, it is believed that a member of the public approached the victim while he was sat in a bus shelter and asked if he was OK.

“We are appealing for that person to come forward along with anyone else who may have come to the assistance of a man who may have appeared disorientated.

“We’re also appealing to anyone who may have found a discarded wallet or artwork consisting of black and white pencil-drawn landscape scenes to get in touch.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
