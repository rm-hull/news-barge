A POPULAR York beer festival is set to return this autumn with a twist.

Yorktoberfest will return to York Racecourse’s Clocktower Enclosure for its sixth year, running across two weekends on October 16 to 17 and October 23 to 24.

This year, the event will be held in a blue Big Top for the first time, transforming the space into a fully themed German beer hall.

James Cundall, CEO of Jamboree Entertainment and event organiser, said: "We are really excited to be staging Yorktoberfest in a Big Top for the first time – it will make for a unique and unforgettable party.

"Big Tops always bring a super-charged vibe to any event, and the space inside this structure will make our live music and outrageous entertainment even more thrilling.

  Partygoers at Yorktoberfest 2025 

*(Image: Provided)*

“It will feel as though you’re stepping into another world inside the Big Top - a Bavarian festival with rows of tables and benches, two performance stages, rustic carts full of wooden beer barrels, wooden barns serving German-themed food and drinks, flags galore and generally everything needed for a great night out.

“We are thrilled that Yorktoberfest has become so popular over the last six years. This year promises even more fun with the new experience of a Big Top as well as beer, a band and bratwurst, not to mention a sensational drag queen!"

  'Prost!' at Yorktoberfest 

*(Image: Provided)*

The festival will feature live entertainment, Bavarian inspired food and drink from Brew York and The Dog Haus, and a competition for best fancy dress.

Returning to the stage is The Bavarian Strollers, a seven-piece live band and drag queen Velma Celli - with additional circus themed entertainment “in keeping with the new Big Top” also planned.

  Drag Artist Velma Celli with partygoers at Yorktoberfest 

*(Image: Provided)*

Tickets are available at ticketsource.co.uk/yorktoberfest and are priced at £25 for standard entry and £35 for VIP - the same as last year.
