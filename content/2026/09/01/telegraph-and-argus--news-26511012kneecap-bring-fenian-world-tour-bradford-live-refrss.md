A HIP-HOP trio is set to raise the roof in West Yorkshire later this year.

Northern Irish group Kneecap will perform at Bradford Live on Friday, December 11, as part of their 2026 Fenian World Tour.

The tour follows a breakthrough year for the group, who have built a reputation for their politically charged lyrics, genre-blending sound, and high-energy live performances.

Their latest album, Fenian, marks a bold step forward in their artistic evolution.

A statement from Kneecap said: "Kneecap return in 2026 to bend genre, language, and rules.

"There will be no backing down.

"No reticence.

"No cowardice.

"No retreat.

"The most talked about artists in the world are turning the page.

"A new chapter, new sounds, new manifestos."

Fenian is produced by Dan Carey, whose credits include Fontaines D.C., Kae Tempest, and Wet Leg.

The album explores an expansive sonic palette, including acid house, trip-hop, and dubstep, while remaining rooted in the group’s signature style.

Kneecap’s journey began in 2017 as a DIY project with basic hip-hop production, but has since grown into a global movement.

Their rise has been fuelled by headline-grabbing performances and controversial media coverage, including a BAFTA and Sundance-winning self-titled biopic, a critically acclaimed debut album Fine Art, and a widely discussed appearance at Coachella that positioned them at the centre of the Palestine solidarity movement.

Other standout moments include a powerful set at Glastonbury and two sold-out shows at Dublin’s 3Arena in 2025, performing to 24,000 fans.

Their 2026 tour will also feature their biggest headline show to date at Crystal Palace Park in London, along with multiple festival appearances.

Bradford Live, where the group will perform, is a restored 1930s art deco venue in the heart of the city.

Once an Odeon Cinema, the building reopened after a £50 million renovation and now serves as a major entertainment, conferencing, and live music hub.

The venue is operated by Trafalgar Entertainment.

Priority Live members can access an exclusive pre-sale from 10am on Wednesday, September 2 by signing up at bradfordlive.co.uk.

General sale tickets will be available from 10am on Friday, September 4, also via the Bradford Live website.

The show is expected to draw fans from across the UK for a night of music, energy, and Kneecap’s signature style.
