The finalists for the Harrogate Mumbler Awards 2026 have been announced, with dozens of local businesses, organisations and individuals shortlisted for this year’s awards.

The awards celebrate the people and organisations providing services, activities and experiences for families across Harrogate and the surrounding area.

Residents can now cast their votes across the different categories, with voting open following the announcement of the shortlist.

Categories include Eating Out With Kids, Favourite Children’s Retailer, Favourite Class or Activity, Favourite SEND Provider, Favourite Children’s Attraction and Favourite Family Event.

The shortlist also recognises local teachers, childcare professionals, coaches and leaders, as well as business parents, digital creators and providers of children’s clubs and holiday camps.

Among the finalists are a number of familiar Harrogate names, including RHS Harlow Carr, Harrogate Theatre, Harrogate Town AFC, Harrogate BID, Stockeld Park, Newby Hall & Garden and Mother Shipton’s Cave.

The awards also include categories covering children’s activities at different ages, from pregnancy and babies through to activities for children aged five and over.
