A 49-year-old man has been made subject to a Criminal Behaviour Order (CBO) after being sentenced at Bradford Magistrates' Court.

Kevin Bracewell was sentenced on August 27 after pleading guilty to breaching a Community Protection Notice (CPN), vagrancy, criminal damage and assault offences committed in the Kirklees district.

As part of the sentencing process, officers from the Kirklees Anti-Social Behaviour Team successfully applied for a Criminal Behaviour Order, which was granted by the court.

## 
  **Restrictions aimed at tackling anti-social behaviour**

The two-year order places several restrictions on Bracewell's behaviour in public places.

Under the terms of the CBO, Bracewell is prohibited from placing himself in a public place to beg or gather alms.

The order also bans him from asking people unknown to him in public places for money or goods, including cigarettes and alcohol.

Exceptions apply in certain circumstances, including legitimate employment arrangements, benefit claims, retail purchases and banking transactions.

## 
  **Police warn breaches are criminal offences**

West Yorkshire Police said the order forms part of efforts to address anti-social behaviour within the Kirklees district.

Officers from the Kirklees Anti-Social Behaviour Team will monitor compliance with the conditions during routine patrols and are encouraging members of the public to report any suspected breaches.

## 
  **Further action possible for breaches**

Police have warned that failure to comply with the terms of a Criminal Behaviour Order is a criminal offence.

Anyone found breaching the conditions of a CBO can face further court action, which may result in a fine or a custodial sentence.

The force has asked residents to report any incidents they believe may breach the order's conditions.

## 
  **Full statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A spokesperson said: “Kevin BRACEWELL 49, was sentenced on 27th August 2026 at Bradford Magistrates court after pleading guilty to Breaching his Community Protection Notice (CPN ), Vagrancy, Criminal damage, and Assault within Kirklees District

“As part of his sentencing, officers from the Kirklees Anti-Social Behaviour Team (ASB) applied for him to be made the subject of a Criminal behaviour order (CBO ), which was granted, The Two-year CBO prohibits him from:

“Not to:

1.Place himself in a public place to beg or gather alms

2.Ask another person not known to you in a public place for money or goods (including cigarettes or alcohol) except as part of: contract of employment or legitimate benefit claim: a retail purchase or banking transaction

“Our team will be keeping their eyes out as part of our regular patrols, but you can also help by reporting if you witness any potential breaches of these conditions

Failing to comply with the terms of the order is considered a criminal offence for which Kevin BRACEWELL could receive a fine or even a prison sentence.”
