27 people have now been arrested as police continue their crackdown on serious and organised crime in the aftermath of the fatal A66 crash.

Cleveland Police are investigating the circumstances of the crash on the A66 on August 22, when a Passat, carrying five young men drove the wrong way down the road, crashing into a police car, containing two officers.

All seven died at the scene, with police saying that the five in the vehicle "could have links to organised crime".

  Two police officers are among seven people who have died following a devastating crash on the A66 in Teesside 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

Since then, a total of 27 arrests have been made in connection with organised crime groups and the crash.

Two men, aged 19 and 24, were arrested on suspicion of possession of a firearm with intent to cause fear of violence.

Both remain in custody while enquiries continue.

  From left to right: Makai Saddington, 18, Theo Rae, 17, Cole Robert Worthy, 17, Jakub Matusiak, 23, Michael Robert Cahill, 23 - all who died in the wrong-way vehicle in the crash 

*(Image: SOCIAL MEDIA)*

Assistant Chief Constable Wayne Fox said the latest arrests showed the force's crackdown would continue, and police have said there are “significant links to serious and organised criminal activity” in the events being investigated.

More than 200 additional specialist officers have been drafted in to support Cleveland Police.

Officers from Durham, Northumbria and North Yorkshire Police have also supported the operation.

  Pcs Tom Clough, left, and Matthew Blades, right 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

Kai Beattie, 19, of Foxrush Court, Redcar, was charged with possession of a firearm with intent to endanger life. He appeared at Teesside Magistrates' Court on Tuesday, August 25.

Cameron George, 26, of Micklow Close, Redcar, was charged with possession with intent to supply Class A and Class B drugs and possession of criminal property. He was remanded in custody and is due to appear at Teesside Crown Court on Monday, September 21.

Kai Inico, 21, of Alverton Green, Middlesbrough, was arrested on suspicion of participating in an organised crime group. He was later charged with possession with intent to supply a Class B drug and released on conditional bail in relation to the organised crime group allegation.

A further 18-year-old charged with dangerous driving appeared at Teesside Magistrates’ Court on Monday, August 31, and was remanded in custody.

Inquests into the deaths of all seven who died were opened at Teesside Coroner's Court on August 27.

Area coroner for Teesside and Hartlepool Paul Appleton said full inquests would be held after post-mortem examinations and the IOPC and police investigations had concluded.

The investigations into the circumstances surrounding the crash are continuing.
