A drug-dealing addict who benefited by almost £26,000 will pay back… just £1.

That was the nominal figure agreed in a Proceeds of Crime Act hearing at Bradford Crown Court and comes seven months after Safraz Riaz, his boss, and six others were locked up for more than 41 years.

Riaz, 39, and formerly of Essex Street, Halifax, was one of a gang of addicts recruited by Uzain Mohammed to sell heroin, crack cocaine, and cocaine on the streets of Calderdale.

## Man jailed earlier this year

The court heard that Riaz began street dealing to pay off a £10,000 drugs debt.

He received a total of five years and eight months’ imprisonment for conspiracy to supply Class A drugs plus unrelated charges of burglary, assault occasioning actual bodily harm, and a public order offence.

He was also handed a 10-year restraining order.

## From £25,000 to £1

During the brief hearing last Thursday, His Honour Judge Colin Burn heard that the benefit figure attributed to Rashid was £25,998.56.

The available figure was a nominal £1.

That is the amount he will pay back via a confiscation order.

Judge Burn ordered the money to be paid within seven days and set a default period of 28 days’ imprisonment if it is not.

Riaz, who is serving his sentence in HMP Humber, was not produced in court and the order was made in his absence.

## Drug boss sent to prison in January

In January this year, drug boss Uzain Mohammed received 11 years and eight months for conspiracy to supply 1.6kg of Class A drugs in Halifax during 2023 and 2024.

Mohammed, and his gang of dealers, was caught as part of two separate police investigations to dismantle drugs lines that resulted in drugs worth more than £25,000 being seized and £100,000 in cash being recovered.

Mohammed, along with street dealers Riaz and Zaeem Tufeeque, were arrested in September 2023 by armed police after being seen dealing drugs from a VW Golf in Halifax.

At the time, Mohammed, 27, of Clay Pits Lane, Pellon, Halifax, was wearing a £13,000 Rolex watch.

More than £1,800 in cash was found in the Volkswagen plus 404 packages of cocaine and heroin worth £7,505.

A search of Mohammed’s address revealed more cash totalling £16,617.

The court heard that Mohammed’s phone showed he was involved in the supply of street-level Class A drugs “on a substantial scale” by operating a drug-dealing line named “Billy” that sent out bulk advertising messages multiple times per day.

Notes saved on the phone included a drugs “tick list” with amounts totalling up to £122,500, which was money owed by Mohammed to his suppliers.

An analysis of the telephone traffic on the “Billy” line over an eight-week period between July and September 2023 estimated that 1.594kg of drugs valued between £88,600 and £159,480 had been sold to almost 600 customers.

Mohammed resumed dealing in November 2023 with the “Waq” drugs line and recruited users – Amaan Ifraz, Scott Shaw, Michael Kilner, Josef Pihik, and Rebecca Henderson – to help him supply customers.

Kilner, Pihik, and Henderson were drivers.

In January the sentencing judge said Mohammed, 27, of Clay Pits Lane, Pellon, Halifax, was “at the helm” of a “commercial scale” drugs enterprise.

## Others also jailed

Zaeem Tufeeque, 29, of Norfolk Place, Halifax, received two years and three months in prison.

Amaan Ifraz, 22, of Athol Gardens, Halifax, was handed seven years and two months.

Scott Shaw, 48, of Houghton Towers, Halifax, received seven years and eight months. Plus a six-year driving ban.

Michael Kilner, 29, of no fixed abode, received two years and three months.

Josef Pihik, 49, of Queen’s Road, King Cross, Halifax, was sentenced to a total of four years and eight months in prison and banned from driving for five years.

Rebecca Henderson, 46, of Unity Terrace, Halifax, got a three-year community order, 20 rehabilitation activity requirement days, and was ordered to complete a mental health treatment programme.
