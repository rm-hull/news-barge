Closing down sales are now underway at all Leading Labels stores as the UK fashion retailer gets set to shut its remaining 15 sites after falling into liquidation.

Leading Labels, founded in 1993, is a long-running clothing retailer that draws shoppers with major brands at outlet prices.

It stocks discounted fashion items from big-name brands, including Calvin Klein, Wrangler, and Joules.

At its peak, Leading Labels ran around 30 stores with more than 200 employees.


## Leading Labels falls into liquidation

Leading Labels is now closing down after falling into liquidation earlier this year.

Jeremy Bleazard of XL Business Solutions Limited was appointed liquidator on May 26, according to The Gazette.

Companies House also said Leading Labels has accounts overdue dating back to 2024.

## Full list of Leading Labels stores set to close

As a result of the company's collapse, all 15 of Leading Labels' remaining stores are set to close:

- Basildon
- Boston
- Bury
- Carlisle
- Cleethorpes
- Clowne
- Evesham
- Hornsea
- Ipswich
- Kidderminster
- Lincoln
- Norwich
- Stevenage
- Balloch (Scotland)
- Cumbernauld (Scotland)


The company's website has also stopped working.

## Closing-down sales now underway at all Leading Labels stores

Closing-down sales are currently running at Leading Labels' remaining stores to help clear stock.

Leadinglabels.co.uk, an informational website which is not affiliated with the actual company, said: "Discounts typically deepen as a liquidation progresses and stock thins out, so ranges, sizes and the best-known brands sell through quickly.

"Once a store's stock is gone, that branch closes for good.

"If you are looking for the same brands Leading Labels carried — in full size runs and with stock guaranteed — the most reliable option now is to shop branded outlet fashion online."

XL Business Solutions Limited has been contacted for comment.
