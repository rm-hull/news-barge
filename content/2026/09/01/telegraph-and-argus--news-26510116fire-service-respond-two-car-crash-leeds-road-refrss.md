A person was helped from a vehicle after a two-car crash on Leeds Road, Bradford.

The incident happened at 3.19pm on Monday, August 31.

The T&A understands the incident took place near Al Halal Foods.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called to Leeds Road at 15.19 to reports of an RTC.

"Two crews attended a two-vehicle RTC and assisted moving a vehicle so a person could exit the car."
