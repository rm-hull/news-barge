A major restaurant chain is closing all of its UK branches in the coming days with several sites across Yorkshire affected.

All 195 Beefeater and Brewers Fayre sites across the country will shut permanently as part of a major restructure by parent company Whitbread.

The closures are part of a five-year growth strategy announced in April, which also includes the closure of Bar + Block, Table Table, and Cookhouse + Pub outlets.


## Brewers Fayre locations closing across Yorkshire

Dominic Paul, chief executive of Whitbread, said: "We always challenge ourselves to improve and, in light of significant cost increases in the form of business rates and national insurance, as well as the implied market discount to our inherent value, we’ve looked hard at the options open to us to maximise value creation over the medium and long-term.

"This has been a rigorous process and we’ve approached all options with an open mind.

"Our new five-year plan builds on our strengths and drives a significant acceleration of our strategy.

"This plan will transform Whitbread into a higher-margin, higher-returning pure-play hotel business.

"We’re going to go further and faster to deliver a great experience for our guests and high-quality growth and returns for our shareholders."

The company said it aims to redeploy many of the affected employees, but job losses are expected.

Around 3,800 roles are at risk.


### 
  **What locations are affected?** 

Here are all of the Yorkshire Brewers Fayre sites that will close:

- Aire & Calder
- The Carousel
- Cheswold Lodge
- Dalesway
- Water Mill
- Turnpike
- Rhubarb Triangle

Whitbread’s restructure is focused on cost-cutting and business refocusing, with plans to save £250 million through the changes.

Some restaurant locations will be converted into Premier Inn hotel rooms.

Around £1.5 billion of freehold properties will be sold to fund further expansion.

The group currently operates approximately 86,600 hotel rooms and aims to reach 96,000 by the 2031 financial year.

A spokesman for Whitbread said: "We recognise the impact of this proposal on colleagues who work at the affected sites.

"As a business which recruits around 15,000 people every year, we expect to be able to retain a significant proportion of those affected and will be looking to redeploy as many of our impacted colleagues as possible.

"However, we do anticipate that the proposed changes, which are subject to consultation, would result in a reduction of around 3,800 roles of a total UK and Ireland workforce of around 30,000.

"We will do all we can to support those colleagues affected."


### 
  **When will Brewers Fayre close?**

Brewers Fayre restaurants will close on Monday, September 7.

Beefeater sites following on Thursday, September 10.

Both brands have been long-standing fixtures in the UK dining sector.

Beefeater was established in 1974 and expanded in 1995 when Whitbread acquired and rebranded the Berni Inn chain.

At its peak, the chain operated more than 250 sites.

Brewers Fayre began in 1981 and had over 100 locations by the late 1980s.

Other Whitbread-owned brands will also shut down this month, including 20 Bar + Block, 32 Table Table, and 14 Cookhouse + Pub sites.

Table Table will close after 18 years of operation.
