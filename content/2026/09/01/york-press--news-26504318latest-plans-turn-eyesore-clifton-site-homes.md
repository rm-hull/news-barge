Developers have said they are optimistic about plans to redevelop an “eyesore” site into new luxury homes in an area of York which has been praised for its growing number of independent businesses.

The Helmsley Group is awaiting planning permission to redevelop a Victorian villa and former apartment building in Shipton Road into housing.

It comes after The Press revealed in January that the Shipton Road site had been sold to the developers for £2.25 million.

  The area off Shipton Road in Clifton, York, that is to be redeveloped 

*(Image: Savills)*

Max Reeves, development director at the York-based Helmsley Group, told The Press the work will turn what is “currently an eyesore of a site into a high-quality residential development”.

Plans were submitted to City of York Council in March to build 14 new homes on the Ashbank villa and former 27-apartment Barleyfields sheltered housing building site, next to the traffic lights at the junction of Rawcliffe lane.

It follows approval being given to demolish Barleyfields and part of Ashbank.

Under the plans, the currently derelict Ashbank villa will be turned back into a family home with the 1960s/70s extensions currently on the property removed.

A new two and a half-storey apartment building with five units will be built to replace Barleyfields, along with with eight new semi-detached family homes.

  Max Reeves, director of developments at the Helmsley Group 

*(Image: Harry Archer)*

The project is being delivered by the Helmsley Group and Dunnington-based housebuilders Mulgrave Properties.

Mr Reeves said: “We look forward to planning permission being granted so that we can see the proposed transformation of what is currently an eyesore of a site into a high-quality residential development, enhancing this part of Clifton and contributing positively to York’s housing supply through a mix of accommodation types.”

## 'Residents have been hoping for movement on the redevelopment for some time' - cllr

Ward councillor Danny Myers welcomed the plans to redevelop Barleyfields and Ashbank, which come as other businesses announced interest in the Clifton area.

Cllr Myers said: “Barleyfields and Ashbank have been a real eyesore for too long and residents have been hoping for movement on the redevelopment for some time.”

  Rachael Maskell MP and Cllr Danny Myers at Brew York's reopening of the Old Grey Mare pub in Clifton last year 

*(Image: Harry Booth)*

He said he welcomed the redevelopment but noted that the York Civic Trust has called on the developers “to further enhance Clifton’s Conservation area”.

“The existence of an ancient public right of way should also be noted and I hope the developers also clearly support the retention of this footpath,” Cllr Myers said.

“Clifton is a brilliant place to live and I’m sure we’ll continue to feel a real boost from further investment.”

  Eddie Copley-Farnell, of the Curious Coffee Company which has lodged plans to open a new outlet in Clifton 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The ward councillor said other businesses have been attracted to Clifton, most recently the Curious Coffee Company which has lodged plans to turn the area’s former Boots pharmacy into a café.

Brew York also reopened the Old Grey Mare pub in Clifton Green last February after the venue closed in July 2024.

“Alongside Brew York’s Old Grey Mare investment and the new Curious coffee opening soon on Clifton Green, it’s clear that Clifton is a place that people want to come and do business, because we have a really positive and vibrant community,” Cllr Myers said.

“The independent shops at Clifton Green are a real treasure and I urge people to support their local businesses.”
