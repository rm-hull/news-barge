Three members of a North East estate agency team have raised more than £1,700 for Macmillan Cancer Support after completing a gruelling 26-mile hike through the Lake District.

Marie Wilmot, Polly Scaife and Murray Brock, from My Property Box, took part in Macmillan's Mighty Hike around Ullswater, raising £1,766 for the cancer charity.

Murray was joined on the challenge by his partner, Emily.

The route, which started and finished at Dalemain Mansion, circled Ullswater and included a climb over the 1,578ft Gowbarrow Fell, testing participants with steep terrain, rain and long distances.

  Marie Wilmot (left) and Polly Scaife after completing the Macmillan Mighty Hike. 

*(Image: MY PROPERTY BOX)*

Marie, sales and lettings director, and assistant manager Polly completed the course together in just under nine-and-a-half hours.

Murray and Emily crossed the finish line in eight hours and 37 minutes.

For Polly, the challenge held special personal significance as she walked in memory of her mother Amanda, who died from breast cancer in 2013.

She said: “The last eight miles were definitely the hardest; by then my legs were feeling numb, but we kept each other going and just focussed on reaching the finish.”

Murray and Emily dedicated their walk to two of Emily’s grandparents, both of whom were diagnosed with cancer.

Marie took part in memory of her brother-in-law, who died from an aggressive blood cancer shortly before her wedding last year.

She said: “We wanted to do something positive in Jim’s memory; taking part in the Mighty Hike felt like a fitting way to support Macmillan and help other families affected by cancer.”

Thanking supporters, she added: “It was an amazing day – we were all incredibly proud to complete it and would like to say a huge thank you to everyone who donated.”

Money raised through Macmillan’s Mighty Hike helps provide practical, emotional and financial support for people living with cancer across the UK.
