# Watch: Key moments from Andy Burnham's first Commons speech as PM

Andy Burnham pledges "more substantial change" during his first speech to MPs as prime minister.

He also pays tribute to his predecessor Keir Starmer - who earlier announced he will stand down as an MP.

Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch said the new prime minister will be judged on "what he actually delivers".
