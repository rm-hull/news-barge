A fundraiser launched for the families of two Cleveland Police officers killed in the A66 crash has reached its target, surpassing £1.2 million.

PC Matthew Blades, 37, and PC Tom Clough, 38, died when a Volkswagen Passat which had been pursued by police collided with their marked armed response vehicle seconds after the pursuit was terminated on August 22.

Five people travelling in the Volkswagen Passat also died at the scene.

44,000 people have since donated to the fundraiser, created by Cleveland Police Federation which closed today (September 1), at 9am, raising £1,203,932.

Organisers said money raised would be split equally between the two officers’ families, while the federation would continue to provide support.

They described the kindness and generosity shown by the public as “overwhelming”.

Organiser Carl Baker said: “We wanted to take a moment to thank every single person who has donated, shared this fundraiser or supported the families of PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough.

“The response has been truly overwhelming. The kindness and generosity shown by people across the country and beyond has meant an enormous amount, and we are incredibly grateful to everyone who has stood behind both families during such an unimaginably difficult time.

“After careful consideration, we have made the decision to close the fundraiser to new donations on September 1, at 9am.

“We are working directly with the team at GoFundMe to make sure everything is appropriately set up for the funds raised to support both families.

“If you would still like to support Matt and Tom's families before the fundraiser closes, please consider sharing this fundraiser with your friends, family and wider community before 9am tomorrow.

“There are simply not enough words to properly thank everyone for what you have done.

“Every donation, every share and every message of support has contributed to the incredible response for Matt and Tom's families.
