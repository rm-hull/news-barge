A motorcyclist was seen attempting to get off a moving Honda travelling at about 30mph before crashing into a wall and running from the scene, according to police.

The incident was spotted by officers from West Yorkshire Police's roads policing unit while they were working in the Bradford South area.

Police said the motorcycle was seen on Stanage Lane, Shelf, just across the border in Calderdale.

## Attempt to abandon bike ends badly

According to Operation Steerside, the rider tried to dismount while the motorcycle was still travelling at roughly 30mph after noticing police nearby.

The manoeuvre did not go to plan, with the rider losing control and ending up colliding with a wall.

After the impact, the rider abandoned the motorcycle and fled on foot.

## Rider escapes but bike does not

While the motorcyclist managed to leave the scene, officers were able to seize the Honda.

The circumstances surrounding why the rider attempted to leave the motorcycle while it was moving have not been disclosed.

No information has been released regarding any injuries.

## Roads policing operation

The incident was highlighted by West Yorkshire Police's Operation Steerside team, which focuses on road safety and traffic-related offences across the county.

The seized motorcycle was recovered by officers following the incident.

## FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police

A spokesperson said: "The hapless rider of this Honda was spotted on Stanage Lane, just across the border into West Yorkshire Police - Calderdale Valleys whilst we were working in the West Yorkshire Police - Bradford South area. In a ridiculous turn of events, on seeing us he tried to dismount at roughly 30mph, sort of running himself over before running into a wall a little more forcefully than he probably intended."

They added: "As the saying goes, where there's no sense there's no feeling and this was obviously the case as the rider made off on his toes, hopefully to have a darn good think about what he's done.

"In the meantime, the bike was seized. #opsteerside #RoadSafety #VisionZero Vision Zero - West Yorkshire"
