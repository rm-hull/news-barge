A village street was flooded last night (Monday).

Emergency services were called to **Sandy Lane, Stockton on the Forest**, near York, at** 7.45pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from York responded to a report of a burst water main that was flooding a residential street.

#### Sandy Lane, Stockton on the Forest

“Crews assisted home owners to manage the water levels using sandbags.”
