Two North Yorkshire cafés will close at the end of this week.

Both are owned by the National Trust which is facing “significant challenges”.

Their cafés at the Old Coastguard Station at Robin Hood’s Bay and Ravenscar near Whitby will cease trading and close to customers on Sunday (6 September).

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/old-coastguard-station-robin-hoods-bay-north-yorkshire-gsv.webp)

A National Trust spokesperson said: “We have taken the difficult decision to close our cafés at Ravenscar and the Old Coastguard Station following a period of consultation with affected staff.

“Like many hospitality businesses, we have faced significant challenges in recent years.

“Despite efforts to improve performance, these cafés are no longer viable in the long term. As a charity, we have a responsibility to use our resources effectively so we can continue to deliver our conservation work.”

The charity confirmed that a number of roles have been made redundant.

“This was not a decision we wanted to make, and our priority is to support affected colleagues through this transition.

“We are grateful for the dedication and professionalism shown by the teams at both venues who have provided a warm welcome and excellent service to visitors over many years.

“We know this news will be disappointing for staff, local communities and visitors.

“We remain committed to helping people enjoy the Yorkshire coast and will work with colleagues, communities and stakeholders to explore future opportunities for these buildings and how they can best support our ambitions for the North Yorkshire coast.”
