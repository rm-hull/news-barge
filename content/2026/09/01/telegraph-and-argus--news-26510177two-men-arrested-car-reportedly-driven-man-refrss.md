POLICE have now confirmed two men were arrested after a car was reportedly driven at a man on a street in the Bradford district.

The force were called on Sunday night to the junction of Lawkholme Lane and Cavendish Street in Keighley.

It happened just after 8pm when the victim, a man in his 30s, was reportedly assaulted in the street by a man who then got into a BMW car and drove it at him.

The victim suffered a leg injury and was taken to hospital.

## Two arrests made

Two men, aged 36 and 40, have seen been arrested on suspicion of a section 18 attempt wounding with intent offence.

They have now been bailed, the force has confirmed.

## Most recent police update

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Two men have been arrested in connection with an incident where a car was driven at a man on Cavendish Street, Keighley.

"A 36 and 40-year-old man were arrested on suspicion of a section 18 attempt wounding with intent offence.

"They've both been bailed pending further enquiries. "

## Police appeal

A spokesperson for the force said: "Multiple enquiries remain ongoing into the incident and anyone who can assist the investigation is asked to contact Bradford CID on 101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat

"Those getting in touch should reference police crime number 13260495112.

"Information can also be given anonymously to the independent Crimestoppers charity on 0800 555 111."
