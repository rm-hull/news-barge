## 'It's chaotic' - drivers caught in traffic queues following York road closure

Heavy traffic was seen along Walmgate throughout the day *(Image: Newsquest)i>*

A road closure on part of York’s inner ring road was continuing to blight motorists today - causing long queues of traffic.

But Yorkshire Water chiefs said the damaged road was set to be repaired tomorrow (Wednesday, September 2).

Fishergate was closed by City of York Council after a pedestrian crossing, where the road meets Paragon Street, became damaged in a storm on Sunday (August 30).

As reported by The Press, the road was uplifted by a rush of rainwater which flooded the area’s drains.

  


Yorkshire Water had removed the damaged road surface, creating a pile of rubble close to the cordoned off area.

The company today (Tuesday, September 1) said traffic management measures would remain in place until the road had been reinstated and set.

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson said: “We’re currently working with the local council to assess and repair damage on Fishergate following the storm over the weekend, which we understand caused water to push through gaps under a manhole cover and frame, lifting the road surface.

"We’ll be repairing the damage caused tomorrow (September 2). We apologise for the inconvenience.”

  A pile of rubble from the damaged road surface was left by Yorkshire Water workers on the morning of September 1 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Drivers travelling eastbound from Tower Street today (September 1) continued to be diverted through Piccadilly, Lead Mill Lane and Walmgate, which is causing queues – particularly during peak times.

Motorists, including buses, were seen in the long traffic queues throughout the day.

One commenter said: “It’s chaotic down Walmgate today.

“Usual morning traffic blended with the diverted traffic from Tower Street and Fishergate – and even in the afternoon there was a constant stream of cars trying to get to Walmgate Bar.

“Lots of people were getting impatient and honking their horns.”

  The traffic lined streets adjoining including Margaret Street, George Street and Lead Mill Lane 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Another urged City of York Council to change the way the traffic lights operate at the end of Walmgate, close to the Bar.

They said: “It’s notorious for only letting a few cars out, even less if the tour buses are going through slowly.”

One woman added on a social media post: “Traffic in York is ridiculous as it is, but yesterday was something else.”

City of York Council has been asked for a comment and update but The Press had not received a response at time of publication.

  Buses were seen queuing for long periods of time on Tuesday (September 1) 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

A statement published on Monday (August 31) said Fishergate would remain closed "for a few days”, adding: “While we work with Yorkshire Water to understand the cause, then get it fixed.

"For now, all traffic (including buses) will be diverted."

The routes of many travel companies - including First Bus - have been affected by the diversions.

The Press was awaiting a comment from First Bus at time of publication.
