This October, Liz and Kevin Hill will celebrate the landmark tenth anniversary of their colourful and captivating Kirkgate store, Karma.

The Boho boutique and gift shop, which sells a range of ethically sourced own-brand clothing and accessories, exudes calm and peace—as you might expect from Liz, who is also a leading exponent of Yoga Nidra guided meditation, which induces deep physical and mental relaxation. Liz has built a worldwide following on her YouTube channel (@lizzyhill222), where her 20-minute video has attracted a staggering 18 million views and counting.

## Keeping calm through challenges

But even the calmest and most relaxed of people with businesses in the retail, leisure, and hospitality sectors across Ripon, the UK, and worldwide, were severely challenged by the unexpected hammer blow of the Covid pandemic that struck in March 2020.

Liz and Kevin aimed to maintain their own morale and that of passers-by on largely empty city streets with messages of hope written on the shop’s windows. One read: ‘You can’t have rainbows without the rain.’

Fortunately, the couple weathered the storm. Liz said: “Being closed for so long was a bit scary, but the people of Ripon were amazing and kept us going by ordering online or by phone and I would drop off their goodies. It was so lovely to see regular faces… albeit behind masks and two metres away!”

## A supportive independent business community

Turning the clock back to 2016, the couple decided to open their distinctive business after falling in love with the historic city. Liz soon found a warm welcome from the owners of other independent businesses along the ancient street, which links the historic Market Square with the stunning façade of the cathedral.

“The business community of Ripon are very supportive and I love the way ideas are shared and then events created off the back of them,” Liz pointed out. “We have such a varied selection of amazing businesses and we can direct people to where they will find what they are looking for. Visitors love that. I give them a Ripon map with all the businesses on and make suggestions from food and drink to gifts and groceries.”

## Working in partnership

Various events have been created on Kirkgate alongside other independent businesses. The main one is the Yoga & Wellness event, which this year was a collaboration between Karma and the Old Deanery owners Rebecca and Chris Layton, to coincide with International Yoga Day.

“In 2025, with the tremendous support we received from Ripon BID, our inaugural event was held on Kirkgate Park,” Liz added. “But this June our move to the magnificent Jacobian Deanery building with its fabulous gardens gave the additional space needed for local businesses and wellbeing practitioners to showcase their skills and products. We attracted in excess of 800 people of all ages and experience, from newcomers to regular Yoga class attendees, who took part in the exercise and meditation sessions.”

For 2027, the Old Deanery will again be hosting the event, and plans are already underway to make it even bigger.

## A friendly community vibe

Almost ten years after Liz and Kevin opened Karma’s doors, the couple’s stylish operation has been fully embraced by fellow businesses and a growing number of regular customers.

Liz said: “We are blessed as Ripon has a very friendly community and vibe which visitors see and love, parking is easy and cheap, we have great eateries that attract people to the city as well as having the BID who sponsor many events and are as supportive as they can be.”

## A destination business for the region

Ripon BID manager Lilla Bathurst praised the business’s reach, noting that in addition to Liz’s global Yoga followers on YouTube, Karma has regular customers travelling from the Northeast, Scotland, Lancashire, Derbyshire, and across Yorkshire.

Lilla said: “This, combined with online overseas customers, has seen Liz and Kevin skilfully using social media to promote their superb products and services to an ever-increasing audience. As a destination business, Karma attracts people who visit Ripon specifically to go to Liz and Kevin’s shop, bringing spin-off benefits to other businesses in the city.
