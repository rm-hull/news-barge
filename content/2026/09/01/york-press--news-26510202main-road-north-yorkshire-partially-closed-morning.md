A MAIN road in North Yorkshire has been partially closed this morning (Tuesday).

Traffic is facing issues on the A170 going down Sutton Bank.

A lorry going down the hill is waiting for another HGV which is stuck.

RECOMMENDED READING:

Sutton Bank is a key route on the county’s highway network, and regular maintenance is vital to preserve the road’s condition, protect the public and prevent deterioration that could result in unplanned disruption.

A programme of works will require the bank to be closed from 7am on Sunday, September 13, until 7pm on Sunday, October 4.

North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for highways and transport, Malcolm Taylor, said: “The works will include planned maintenance to the rock face and routine maintenance of the road.

"The longer scheme will also allow for further drainage and repairs."
