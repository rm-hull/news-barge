A man made sexual gestures at a young girl near a York primary school.

It happened on Sim Balk Lane in Bishopthorpe, between the junction of Lang Road and up to the entrance of Bishopthorpe Infant School, at about 1pm on Tuesday 25 August.

Now police are looking for CCTV, dashcam or phone footage of this area at this time.

#### Incident location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A white car has been driven on the wrong side of the road (heading away from Lang Road), with the driver leaning out of his window, making sexualised gestures towards where a young girl is.

“Do you have any footage of this incident?”
