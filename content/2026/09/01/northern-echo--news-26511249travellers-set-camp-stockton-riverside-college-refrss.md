Travellers have set up camps on the grounds of a North East college, as students return for their first day back.

Caravans and pitches have moved in on the private grounds of Stockton Riverside College.

Pictures show around four caravans in the car parks, among student and staff cars.

A spokesperson for the college has said they are aware of the situation but college operations are continuing as normal and students should attend as planned.

Signs close to the caravans read: "Restricted parking - private land. Terms and Conditions apply."

A spokesperson for Stockton Riverside College said: “We are aware of the situation in the local area. College operations are continuing as normal and students should attend as planned, in line with the information provided at enrolment.

"Any queries regarding the start of term should be directed to college reception on 01642 865400 or, for returning students, via curriculum staff using usual communication methods.”

The Northern Echo has contacted Cleveland Police for a statement.
