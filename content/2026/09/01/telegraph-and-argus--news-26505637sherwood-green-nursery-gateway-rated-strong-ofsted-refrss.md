A BRADFORD nursery has been praised for its “joyful and curious learners” and nurturing environment.

Ofsted rated Sherwood Green Nursery Gateway, on Thackeray Road in Ravenscliffe, “strong” in all six areas after an inspection on June 15.

Inspectors praised the nursery’s high-quality care and education.

Emilia Olkowska, managing director of Sherwood Green Nursery, said: "Following our latest Ofsted inspection, we are incredibly proud to have achieved ‘Strong’ in all six areas, with safeguarding met.

  Sherwood Green Nursery Gateway was rated 'strong' by Ofsted


"This report highlights the dedication of our team and the high-quality care and education we provide for our children.

"We are incredibly grateful to our wonderful families for continuing to trust and support us."

  Emilia Olkowska, managing director of Sherwood Green Nursery


## What does the report show?

Inspectors said children “arrive with enthusiasm, settle quickly and are supported by adults who understand their personalities and experiences”.

The report said: "Children are joyful, curious learners who arrive with enthusiasm and settle quickly because they feel known, valued and deeply secure in this nursery.

"They move through the day with confidence, supported by warm, trusting relationships with adults who understand their personalities, interests and experiences beyond the setting.

"Babies instinctively reach out for cuddles, showing the deep attachments they have formed.

"Children who are new to the nursery receive nurturing, soothing interactions that help them feel safe from the very beginning.

"Children see themselves reflected in the environment through photos and stories, helping them to develop a strong sense of identity and belonging."

Ofsted found children make “rapid and sustained progress” despite barriers to learning and are well prepared for their next steps, from moving rooms to starting school.

Inspectors also praised the nursery’s behaviour management, saying staff and children understand leaders’ high expectations.

Children were observed demonstrating positive attitudes to learning and engaging with activities enthusiastically.

Inspectors called the curriculum highly inclusive and tailored to each child’s interests and needs, with “communication, language and mathematics woven throughout daily routines and play”.

Ofsted also praised the nursery for celebrating diversity and making the full curriculum accessible to every child.

Inspectors described leaders as “ambitious and reflective” and said they use rigorous self-evaluation to drive improvement.

Inspectors also said: “Parents speak warmly about the setting and the positive impact on their children.

“This shared trust and pride underpins the inclusive and nurturing culture of the nursery.”

Inspectors made one recommendation in the report, saying: “Leaders should continue to build on their highly successful work to remove barriers for children who need extra help, ensuring this continues to make a sustained and transformational impact on all the children's achievement and wellbeing.”
