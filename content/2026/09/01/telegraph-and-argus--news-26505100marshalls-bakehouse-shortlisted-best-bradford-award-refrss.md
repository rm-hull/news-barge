A WELL-KNOWN bakery that has recently undergone a refurbishment could be named the best bakery in Bradford.

Marshall's Bakehouse, on High Street in Queensbury, is a finalist in the recently-launched Telegraph & Argus Best Bakery competition.

The bakery's refurb, including the installation of a new three-metre-long counter to better display its products.

Owner Holly Marshall said: "It’s given our customers so much more space in the shop and much more room to display our goods so it all just looks so much better.

"It’s the best thing we’ve ever done."

On being nominated, Holly added: "We were very pleased to learn that the people of Bradford and beyond have nominated us for the best bakery of the year.

"It means a lot to us to be considered."

*(Image: Holly Marshall)*

## Customer relationship

The bakery has quickly built a loyal following, and Holly credits much of its success to the strong relationships built with customers.

She said: "We wouldn’t be here without our customers.

"So many we know by name, it doesn’t feel right to just say 'customers' because we genuinely enjoy seeing them, catching up and chatting."

  The front of the bakery 

*(Image: Holly Marshall)*

## Baking roots

Marshall’s Bakehouse combines both traditional family recipes and modern treats, with a focus on fresh cream cakes, blondies, and brownies.

Baking runs in Holly’s family, with roots dating back to 1980.

She launched the business in 2023 at the age of 20, blending classic bakes from past generations with her own new creations.

Holly said: "There are many types of bakeries across Bradford and some very, very good ones at that.

"Our mixture of traditional unchanged recipes from the 80s and 90s along with our new modern recipes makes it so we have something for everyone."

  A picture wall commemorating the family 

*(Image: Holly Marshall)*

## 'Bert vanilla slice'

Community is at the heart of the business.

The bakery sponsors the Queensbury Celtic lady’s football team and supports the local foodbank.

One of its standout treats is the ‘Bert vanilla slice,’ named after Holly’s grandfather.

She said: "We have had people travel for them.

"It is always our best seller."

## How to vote

Marshall’s Bakehouse has also built a large online following, with more than 63,000 followers on Facebook and 19,000 on Instagram.

Holly said: "I still think you can’t beat word of mouth, but we hear many, many times a day 'I’ve followed you on Instagram for so long' or 'I saw you on Facebook.'

"It makes a huge impact."
