A mum has recalled the traumatic moment a man was left lying in the street saying “I am going to die here” after a horror alleyway stab attack.

Armed police swarmed Britannia Place in Dormanstown, near Redcar, on Monday (August 31) after a man was stabbed at around 5pm.

The victim is believed to have been attacked in an alley behind homes on Broadway West before stumbling to a home on Britannia Place and collapsing outside.

Marian, who has lived on the street for 26 years, was in the garden when she heard a knock at the door and her friend shouting: “Somebody has been stabbed and is dying on the front.”

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

She frantically rang 999 before joining her friend in going to the man’s aid, remaining with him before emergency crews descended on the estate.

“I was out the back at the time," the 59-year-old explained. "My mate knocked on my door and asked for me to get the phone and call 999 because someone had been stabbed.

“This was at around 4pm or 5pm, apparently [the incident took place] down the alley.

“The man had collapsed outside number 42. There was blood everywhere.

  Police on Britannia Place in Dormanstown after a stab attack 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

“When the police got there, paramedics came and had oxygen on him.

“He wouldn’t keep it on so I kneeled down and tried to keep it on him while the paramedics worked on him. He was out of it.

“He kept saying: ‘I am going to die here’. It was traumatic.”

Marian, who has lived in Dormanstown all of her life, also had to make sure her son, 25, who is autistic, stayed in the house while the situation unfolded.

  Police on Britannia Place in Dormanstown after a stab attack 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

She admitted that she did not feel safe on the estate anymore: “It used to be alright.

“When James was little he could come out. Now there are too many drugs and we have had armed response here sometimes.

“I have lost two cars off my drive, one was burnt out. My house has been ransacked, I lost a few grand.”

## 'Massive shock'

The street was taped off while an air ambulance landed nearby following the ordeal, with armed officers dressed in all black seen walking through the estate.

One 26-year-old, who has lived on the estate for two years, said she looked out the window to see the police and ambulances “rushing down the street".

  Britannia Place in Dormanstown 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

“I know they cordoned the street off and I think the armed police were here but that might have been later,” the mum said.

“I don’t know what has happened, I try to keep myself to myself. It isn’t usually bad around here, we don’t usually get drama.

“It was just out of the blue, we’ve had nothing like it before. It was a massive shock.”

Another resident, a woman who has lived on the road for 12 years, said: “I was in the kitchen doing my tea, it was about 5.10pm yesterday, when I saw the carnage.

"I just saw the people in the street and the helicopter, ambulances and police cars. I don’t know how many there were but it was quite a lot.

“I have lived here for 12 years and I haven’t had any bother. It is normally quite quiet around here.”

Police remained at the scene on Tuesday morning (September 1), where a North Yorkshire Police van and a car were parked either side of the alley.

The cordon remained in place across the rear of several homes.

Various other police vehicles were also seen driving through nearby streets.

Cleveland Police said last night that his injuries are not believed to be life threatening.

A force spokesperson said: “Officers are at the scene of an incident in the Dormanstown area after responding to reports that a man had been assaulted.

“The incident happened shortly before 5pm in Britannia Place.

“Upon arrival, officers discovered a man who had been wounded with a knife. He was treated at the scene and subsequently transferred to hospital, where treatment is continuing. His injuries are not currently believed to be life threatening.

“Officers remain at the scene conducting enquires to establish the full circumstances surrounding the incident.

“Anyone who witnessed the incident or who has any information, is asked to contact Cleveland Police on 101, quoting reference number 174954.”

The public have been urged to refrain from speculation.
