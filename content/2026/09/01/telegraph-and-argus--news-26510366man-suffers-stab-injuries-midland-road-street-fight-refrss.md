A MAN was reportedly stabbed in a Bradford street after a fight took place in the early hours of the morning.

Police say four men arrived in a vehicle on Midland Road at around 3am on Monday where the victim and other men were in the street.

One of the men who arrived by car reportedly stabbed the victim during the altercation.

A 36-year-old man later attended a hospital for treatment to a stab injury which is not believed to be life threatening, the force added.

Officers have been carrying out enquiries in the Midland Road area to establish the full circumstances.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police are appealing for witnesses and footage after a man was seriously injured in an affray incident in Bradford.

"Detectives would like to speak to anyone who can assist enquiries following an incident on Midland Road between 3.10am and 3.20am on the morning of Monday 31 August in which fighting took place involving several men.

"A 36-year-old man later attended at hospital for treatment to a stab injury which is not believed to be life threatening."

The spokesperson added: "It is believed the incident started after four men arrived in a vehicle on Midland Road where the victim and other men were in the street.

"One of the four newly-arrived men (reportedly) stabbed the victim in the altercation which followed.

"Officers have been conducting enquiries in the Midland Road area to ascertain the full circumstances of what took place.

"Anyone who can assist the investigation in any way is asked to contact Bradford CID on 101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat refencing crime number 13260495582

"Information can also be given anonymously to the independent Crimestoppers charity on 0800 555 111."
