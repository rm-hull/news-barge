## Part of major road in York city centre remains closed following storm damage

A barrier on part of the York inner ring road after storm damage forced the road's closure (Image: Newsquest) *(Image: Staff)*

Part of a major road in York city centre remained closed today (Tuesday, September 1) after a thunderstorm caused damage to the road surface.

It came after a flash of rainwater during a thunderstorm on Sunday lifted a section of the pedestrian crossing, forcing City of York Council’s highway officials to cordon the section of the road.

  Yorkshire Water arrived at the scene with a digger shortly before 3pm on Monday (August 31) (Image: Newsquest) 

*(Image: Staff)*

The council yesterday (Monday) said the road would remain closed "for a few days" and vehicles heading eastbound on Tower Street would be diverted via Piccadilly and Walmgate Bar.

  


A council statement said: "The road will need to be closed for a few days while we work with Yorkshire Water to understand the cause, then get it fixed.

"For now, all traffic (including buses) will be diverted."
