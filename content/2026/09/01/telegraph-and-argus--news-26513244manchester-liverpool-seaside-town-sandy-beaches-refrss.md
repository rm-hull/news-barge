THERE is a stunning seaside town with great restaurants and sandy beaches between Manchester and Liverpool.

Set along this stretch of coastline is Lytham St Annes, an urban area comprising the adjoining historic settlements of Lytham and St Annes-on-the-Sea.

With Lytham occupying a prime position beside the Ribble estuary, visitors will find a quaint town centre near its landmark windmill, with streets lined with independent shops and restaurants serving everything from Mediterranean small plates to traditional British fare.

Lytham St Annes has long been prized for its refined atmosphere, offering a more genteel alternative to the brasher attractions of nearby Blackpool.


The area's heritage is on show at its landmark country estate, alongside independent shops, seafront gardens and a world-famous golf course that has hosted some of the sport's greatest champions.

It is roughly an hour's drive from Manchester and around 70 minutes from Liverpool John Lennon Airport, depending on traffic.

### Exploring Lytham's shops and restaurants

Visitors can stroll along Lytham's genteel streets and the wide green expanse of Lytham Green, where independent boutiques, delicatessens and cafés cluster around Clifton Street and Park Street.

At the heart of the green stands Lytham Windmill, a Grade II-listed tower mill built around 1805.

It ground wheat and oats for more than a century before a violent gale caused it to catch fire and burn out in January 1919.

*(Image: Supplied)*

Restored in the late 1980s, the windmill reopened in 1989 and now operates as a seasonal museum managed by Lytham Heritage Group in partnership with Fylde Borough Council, telling the story of the landmark and its connection with the town.

The windmill is widely regarded as one of Lytham's most treasured landmarks and a reminder of the town's long relationship with the sea and the surrounding marshland.

For those who have worked up an appetite exploring, the town has a wide range of eateries, from cosy neighbourhood bistros to seafront restaurants.

At the time of writing, Tripadvisor gives Solito Posto a 4.8 out of 5 rating, while popular local restaurants including Elvin's and Anatolia Sea View Mediterranean Cuisine have also drawn strong reviews from diners.

In nearby St Annes, family-run cafés and restaurants around the pier and Clifton Drive add further choice, with Jai Lounge and Greek Flame Taverna among the highly rated options close to the seafront.


### Historic estate and championship golf near Lytham

Lytham St Annes is home to one of Lancashire's most storied country houses: Lytham Hall.

Dating back to the 12th century, when the site was home to a Benedictine priory associated with Durham Cathedral, the estate eventually passed to the Clifton family after the Dissolution of the Monasteries and remained in their hands for more than three centuries.

The present Georgian house, designed by celebrated York architect John Carr and completed in 1764, replaced the family's earlier Jacobean manor and today sits within around 80 acres of historic parkland open to visitors, with opening times varying according to the season and events.

The family's colourful history includes John Talbot Clifton, who as a six-year-old boy laid the foundation stone for the St Anne's Hotel in 1875, an event regarded as the official founding of the neighbouring resort of St Annes-on-the-Sea.


His son Henry's lavish spending contributed to the family's financial decline and the eventual sale of the estate in the 1960s.

The hall subsequently passed through corporate ownership before being acquired by the charitable Lytham Town Trust in 1997.

Today, Heritage Trust for the North West manages the Hall under a long-term lease, with the estate offering tours, a café, gift shop and other attractions.

Golf enthusiasts flock to Royal Lytham & St Annes Golf Club, founded in 1886 and considered one of the finest links courses in the world.

The club has hosted The Open Championship eleven times since 1926, when the legendary Bobby Jones claimed his first Claret Jug there, with further famous victories for Tony Jacklin, Seve Ballesteros and Ernie Els in the decades since.

It has also hosted the AIG Women's Open six times, with the most recent coming earlier this summer, when Japan's Shiho Kuwaki emerged victorious in a play-off.

The men's championship is scheduled to return to the course in 2028, marking its 12th staging at the venue and continuing more than a century of history at one of the sport's most cherished addresses.

With a historic windmill, a centuries-old country estate, championship golf and well-reviewed places to eat, Lytham St Annes offers far more than many passing between Manchester and Liverpool may expect.
