Officers from Ripon Response and the Neighbourhood Policing Team attended reports of a pit bike style off-road bike being ridden around the Gallows Hill area of Ripon.

Police were called in and quickly located the bike and rider, and say they have taken action in relation to the offences identified.

The bike has now been seized and removed from the streets.

The illegal use of these vehicles can cause significant nuisance to local residents and pose a risk to both riders and other members of the public.

There have been many complaints in the city.

If you witness similar issues continue to report them to North Yorkshire Police. Your information helps them take action to keep communities safe.
