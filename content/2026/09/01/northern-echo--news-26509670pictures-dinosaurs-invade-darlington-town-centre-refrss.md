Darlington town centre was transported back to prehistoric times as families flocked to Dinosaur Day for an afternoon of roaring entertainment.

Young dinosaur fans gathered in the Market Square on Thursday, August 27, where a giant Tyrannosaurus rex and smaller prehistoric creatures drew crowds of children and parents throughout the day.

  Dinosaur Day in Darlington. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The free event, staged from 11am until 3pm, formed part of Darlington Borough Council’s summer programme of family activities.

It featured live dinosaur shows, fossil-dig activities and arts and crafts.

  Dinosaur Day in Darlington. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Children watched from behind barriers in the Market Square as a stegosaurus and baby dinosaurs were brought out by handlers, while the imposing T-rex made its way through the town centre, stopping shoppers in their tracks along High Row.

The giant dinosaur proved a particular attraction, with families gathering around it for photographs and children alternately squealing, laughing and cautiously stepping back as it moved through the crowds.

  Dinosaur Day in Darlington. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Many youngsters came dressed for the occasion, with dinosaur costumes, colourful outfits and explorer hats adding to the atmosphere.

  Dinosaur Day in Darlington. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Meanwhile, children were able to get closer to the smaller 'creatures' during performances in the Market Square, reaching out to touch the stegosaurus as handlers explained more about the prehistoric animals.

The event was one of two free themed days held in the town during the school holidays, following Legends and Fairytales Day the previous week.
