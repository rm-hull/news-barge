This is the unusual moment a man appeared to be caught snoozing in bed during a property photoshoot for a house in North Tyneside.

The five-bedroom terraced home on Charles Avenue in Whitley Bay is currently on the market for offers in the region of £328,000.

The spacious property boasts generous living accommodation, several reception rooms and a rear yard.

But while the house's period charm and sought-after coastal location may be enough to catch the eye of prospective buyers, it was a rather unexpected feature that got social media users talking.

TikToker Housing Horrors, who regularly shares bizarre and eye-catching property listings with his followers, stumbled across the advert while browsing online.

The creator said that the house contained "about five fridge freezers", but otherwise described it as a fairly typical family home.

  The property looked normal at first, but a string of fridge freezers left one TikToker scratching his head. 

*(Image: Housing Horrors)*

That all changed when he reached one of the bedroom photographs.

There, tucked under the duvet, appeared to be a man peacefully snoozing while the estate agent’s camera clicked away around him.

Pointing out the bizarre scenario to his viewers, the creator said: “Wait until you see this next room because there is a man asleep in bed.

“I actually cannot believe that he is just in bed on Rightmove.

"This is the bloody icing on the cake – I genuinely cannot believe this guy is in bed in the estate agents’ photos, crazy."

Alongside the photo, the content creator painted a tongue-in-cheek picture of the resident enjoying a late Saturday night only to be awakened by a Sunday morning knock from an eager estate agent armed with a camera.

The creator questioned how the photograph had ended up online at all, wondering whether the sleeping man’s appearance in the listing constituted an invasion of privacy.

Despite his astonishment, he admitted the rest of the property was “pretty standard”, suggesting it gave off an “HMO vibe”.
