Staff at a County Durham family play park have faced a rise in verbal abuse and threats of violence from visitors during the six-week summer holidays.

Holmside Park, in Edmondsley, said it had seen an increase in abuse towards members of staff over the summer break.

The family-owned 50-acre adventure park said the incidents involved staff being sworn at, threatened and screamed at, with some of the abuse directed at teenage and student staff members.

In a Facebook post, the park said: "Unfortunately, over the summer holidays, we've seen an increase in verbal abuse towards members of our staff by visitors, so we must reiterate that this is not acceptable and will not be tolerated.

"However we've had several examples recently of repeated verbal abuse towards our staff. We've even had threats of violence.

"Nobody deserves to come to work and be sworn at, threatened and screamed at, least of all teenagers and students, especially when it is in front of other guests and children trying to enjoy their family day out with us.


"Don't get us wrong, this is a very small minority - the majority of you who have been bringing your families along for days at Holmside have been nothing but lovely, and we thank you all for supporting our family-owned business during such a tough year for the leisure industry.

"We are all just trying to do our jobs and create the best experience for your family with every visit."

Holmside Park opened in May 2018 following a £500,000 investment, creating more than 50 jobs.

The former Mini Moos Fun Farm was transformed into an adventure park featuring more than 20 activities, including a giant indoor inflatable obstacle course, roller rink, soft play, pedal karts, ziplines and an adventure fort.
