An independent councillor in Darlington is set to fight May's election as a member of Reform UK.

Councillor Kevin Nicholson has been elected as an independent to Darlington Borough Council three times, but said he has joined Reform UK ahead of the May 2027 ballot.

Cllr Nicholson, who represents Eastbourne ward, will stay as an independent councillor until the election in nine months' time.

"It has been something that a lot of local residents have been approaching me about," Cllr Nicholson told The Northern Echo.

"So I spoke to some of the people I know in the area about what I should do and pretty much every person I asked said to join Reform."

  Cllr Nicholson will remain as an independent until the 2027 election. 

*(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

Cllr Nicholson has sat on the local authority for 11 years and was part of previous administrations led by the Conservative Party prior to the 2023 election.

"People feel let down by the political system at the moment," he said, explaining why he chose to join Reform UK.

"I don't see how I, as an independent councillor, can affect change to really change Darlington."

Reform UK won control of Durham County Council in 2025 before making further gains in the North East by winning power in Sunderland, Gateshead and South Tyneside this May.

Nigel Farage's party currently has one councillor in Darlington.

Cllr Michael Walker, chair of Reform UK Darlington, said: "Local government has become far too consumed by petty political squabbles, personal attacks and political games. Cllr Kevin Nicholson has always stood apart from that.
