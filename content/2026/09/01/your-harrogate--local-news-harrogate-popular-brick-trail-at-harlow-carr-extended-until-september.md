Families visiting RHS Garden Harlow Carr will have extra time to explore its Brick Explorers sculpture trail after the popular attraction was extended until 27th September.

The wildlife-themed trail features a collection of sculptures made from hundreds of thousands of bricks, showcasing plants, animals and fungi found within the gardens.

Visitors can pick up an activity booklet packed with facts, challenges and activities to complete while following the trail around Harlow Carr.

The extension means the sculptures and activity booklets will remain available until Sunday 27th September.

However, visitors should be aware that the Build Stations will no longer be running from Saturday 5th September.

The trail has been extended following demand from visitors, giving families more opportunities to explore the sculptures during September.

RHS Garden Harlow Carr will remain open as usual, with the Brick Explorers trail available until 27th September.
