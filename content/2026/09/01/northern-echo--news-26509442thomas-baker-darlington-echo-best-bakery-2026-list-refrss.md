A beloved bakery chain's Darlington branch has been named among the top 10 in a regional search for the best in the business.

Thomas the Baker, based on High Row, has been shortlisted in The Northern Echo’s Best Bakery 2026 competition, which celebrates the best bakeries across County Durham and Darlington.

The competition invites readers to nominate their favourite bakeries, shining a spotlight on the businesses that fill high streets with the smell of freshly baked bread, cakes, pies, and pastries.

Thomas the Baker is joined on the shortlist by Bakerman, Bank Top Bakery, Number Eight Bakehouse, and The Leas Bakery, all based in Darlington.

Thomas the Baker has built a loyal following since it was founded in 1979 by John Thomas, who had spent some 20 years working as a managing director for national and international bakery companies.

The first Thomas the Baker shop opened in Malton in July 1981, followed by a second in Helmsley in 1982.

  Thomas the Baker, based on High Row, Darlington 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Over the decades, Thomas the Baker has become known for its award-winning products, including its sweet mince pies, made to a closely guarded recipe known by only three people in the business.

The company remains a family affair, with multiple generations involved in the day-to-day running and creative direction of the bakery.

Although the business has received requests to expand nationally, it has remained focused on serving towns within a 90-minute delivery radius of its Helmsley bakery, which is located on the edge of the North Yorkshire Moors National Park.

Readers can vote using coupons printed in the newspaper and are invited to choose their favourite from the 10 finalists.

Coupons should be posted to The Northern Echo Best Bakery 2026, LOCALLY House, 1 Dalston Road, Carlisle, CA2 5NP.

Voting closes on Wednesday, September 16, 2026. Photocopies or illegible voting coupons will not be counted. The winner will be announced after voting has closed.
