A viral jacket potato chain looks set to open a new store in County Durham.

Spudbros, which started as a trailer selling jackets in Preston, soared to fame on Tiktok, attracting more than five million followers.

The brothers behind the trailer, known as the "tram", Jacob and Harley Nelson, launched a rapid expansion earlier this year, partnering with petrol station firm EG on the move to open branches in 30 locations - known as Spudbros Express. Fifteen openings had been confirmed for 2026.

In June, the chain opened at a service station near the Cobalt business park in North Tyneside, adding to sites in Liverpool, Sheffield, Portsmouth and Wakefield.

And now Spudbros looks set to open in Bowburn, after a job ad appeared online looking for a manager to be based at the store.

The advert is for a full-time manager with the location listed as the Integra 61 site in Bowburn.

Work started on the £4m EG on the move last month, with bosses aiming to open at the development, just off the A1(M) "later this year".

The permanent role carries a salary of £31,500 plus a performance-based bonus, according to the listing.

Spudbros and EG on the move have been contacted for comment.

The family enterprise dates back to 1955, when Ernie Rhodes operated a hot-potato cart in Preston’s Flag Market.

The brothers transformed the business into a major social-media success by posting videos of their huge loaded potatoes on Tiktok.

Its menu is built around large jacket potatoes filled with toppings including cheese, beans, chilli, and topped with the brand’s signature "tram sauce".
