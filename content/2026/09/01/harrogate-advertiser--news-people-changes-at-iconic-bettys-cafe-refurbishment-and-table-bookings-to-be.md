# Changes at iconic Bettys cafe: Refurbishment and table bookings to be introduced at Harlow Carr gardens

## Bettys tea rooms at RHS Garden Harlow Carr is to close for a “refresh” for the first time in its history.

The temporary closure, which will bring the first significant changes at the popular cafe since it first opened in 2004 at one of Yorkshire’s most beautiful gardens, is designed to create an even more welcoming experience for customers.

Liz Thwaite, Head of Site at RHS Garden Harlow Carr said: “We’re delighted to see the refurbishment of Bettys Harlow Carr and to continue our long-standing partnership with such an iconic Yorkshire brand.

"We know Bettys is a much-loved part of a visit to the garden and we’re excited to welcome guests back to a refreshed café this autumn.

"While the work takes place, RHS Garden Harlow Carr remains open as usual, with plenty for visitors to enjoy, including a range of additional catering and refreshment options available across the garden throughout the closure period.”

Bettys Café Tea Rooms at RHS Garden Harlow Carr will temporarily close for a refresh from Monday, September 7 and is scheduled to reopen on Monday, October 5 with a brand-new look.

RHS Garden Harlow Carr will remain open as usual throughout the refurbishment, with a range of alternative food and drink options available for visitors.

When Bettys Harlow Carr reopens next month, customers can look forward to a refreshed café experience with a relaxed sense of elegance, a full, freshly prepared menu, and views across the gardens.

A brand-new Bettys To Go Window will launch at the entrance to RHS Harlow Carr, making it easier for visitors to pick up favourite picnic essentials before heading into the gardens.

Table bookings at Bettys Harlow Carr will also be available, helping customers plan their day with ease, particularly families visiting later in the afternoon.

Devoted Bettys fans can still visit nearby Bettys Harrogate or shop online.

Nicky Sellers, Branch Director at Bettys said: “We’re excited to be investing in Bettys Harlow Carr.

"The café tea rooms are a very special part of a visit to RHS Garden Harlow Carr and we look forward to welcoming everyone back.”
