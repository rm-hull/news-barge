- Motorists are being reminded of heavy and building traffic in York city centre after part of the city's inner ring road was closed.
- The closure remains in place owing to a section of Fishergate which had uplifted during the storm.
- Part of the road - the pedestrian crossing where Fishergate joins with Paragon Street - has been cordoned off.
