AMBULANCE resources were visible in the Bradford district on Friday.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service confirmed that they responded to an incident at premises on Cragg Road in Shipley on Friday, August 28.

They said an ambulance was dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital.

They also received an emergency call at 9.39pm on Friday to report a road traffic collision on Muffit Lane in Cleckheaton.

An ambulance and a rapid response vehicle were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital.
