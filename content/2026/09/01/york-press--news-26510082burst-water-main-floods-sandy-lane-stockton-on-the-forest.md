A BURST water main has flooded a residential street in a village near York.

The incident happened in Sandy Lane, Stockton-on-the-Forest, at 7.45pm on Monday (August 31).

  


North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service crews helped home-owners manage the water levels using sandbags.
