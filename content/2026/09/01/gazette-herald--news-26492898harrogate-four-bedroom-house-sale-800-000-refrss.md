A four-bedroom house in Harrogate is on the market for offers over £800,000.

The semi-detached character home is located on The Grove, in a quiet cul-de-sac just a short walk from Harrogate town centre and The Stray.

The house overlooks the parkland and has been updated and modernised by the current owners to a high standard.

The house has a porch with a stained glass door, which leads into a spacious reception hall with wood flooring throughout the ground floor.

There is a bay-fronted sitting room with a living flame gas stove, fitted cabinets and an attractive fireplace.

An open-plan living kitchen provides sitting and dining areas with glazed doors overlooking and opening onto the garden.

The kitchen is fitted with a range of units, a range cooker and integrated appliances.

The ground floor also has a WC.

On the first floor, there are three bedrooms with fitted wardrobes, including the main bedroom which has an ornamental fireplace and an en-suite shower room.

The house bathroom is fitted with a modern suite comprising a WC, washbasin set within a vanity unit, walk-in shower and freestanding bath.

The second floor has another double bedroom with built-in wardrobes and large eaves storage on both sides.

The house has a driveway for off-road parking, as well as a south-facing garden with a patio to the rear, overlooking the parkland.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact estate agents Verity Frearson on 01423 578252.
