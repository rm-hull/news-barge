Have you captured dangerous or careless driving on your dashcam, CCTV, or a passenger’s mobile phone?

**Operation Snap** allows members of the public to submit footage of driving offences, helping to make North Yorkshire’s roads safer for everyone.

**Examples include:** * Dangerous driving / Driving without due care Using a mobile phone while drivingFailing to stop at a red lightIgnoring road signsCrossing solid white lines*

If you’ve captured an offence while going about your day, send it to North Yorkshire Police.

Their staff will review the footage the footage and, where appropriate, drivers may receive a visit for a chat about their driving, a fixed penalty, penalty points, or be summoned to court.

**Remember:** 

• Submit footage **within 10 days** of the incident

• Footage must be original and must cover 2 minutes before and 2 minutes after the event.

• The vehicle **registration must be clearly visible**

Submit your footage here: [https://www.northyorkshire.police.uk/ro/report/rti/rti-beta-2.1/report-a-road-traffic-incident/](https://www.northyorkshire.police.uk/ro/report/rti/rti-beta-2.1/report-a-road-traffic-incident/)
