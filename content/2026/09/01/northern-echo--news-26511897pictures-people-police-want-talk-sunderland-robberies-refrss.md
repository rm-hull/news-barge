Police are appealing for help to identify two men they want to speak to as part of an investigation into two reported robberies in the Sunderland area.

The incidents were both reported on Saturday, July 25, and officers are treating them as linked.

At about 7.30am, a man was approached by three men on Villette Road in Hendon.

One of the men was reportedly carrying a knife and the victim was threatened into allowing the offenders to take his blue Peugeot 308.

The car was later found burnt out a short distance away.

Later that morning, at about 9.55am, police received another report of robbery on High Street in Easington Lane.

It was reported that three men approached a man who was sitting in a silver Vauxhall Corsa and pulled him from the vehicle in an attempt to steal it.

The victim managed to fend off the men and they left the area on foot.

  One of the two men Police would like to speak to in connection with the July reports. 

*(Image: Northumbria Police)*

Officers have today (September 1) released CCTV images of two men they would like to identify.

Both men were believed to be in the area at the time of the Easington Lane incident and police believe they may be able to assist with their enquiries.

A 34-year-old-man has been arrested on suspicion of robbery in connection with the incidents.

“Anyone unable to make contact in those ways can call 101 quoting reference number NP-20260725-0358.”
