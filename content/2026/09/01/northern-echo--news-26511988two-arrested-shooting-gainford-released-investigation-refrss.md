Two people arrested after a man was shot in a suspected attempted murder in a quiet County Durham village have been released under investigation.

A 20-year-old man and a 16-year-old boy were arrested after a 46-year-old was shot at a home in Balmer Hill, in Gainford, on Saturday evening.

Both have since been released while police carry out further enquiries.

The victim remains in a critical but stable condition in hospital after suffering birdshot injuries to his abdomen. No one else at the property was injured.

Police were called at about 8.10pm after reports that a car had been driven to the address and shots fired.

A cordon remained in place on Sunday while forensic officers examined the scene.

Images from the scene showed holes in the front door, a smashed front window and broken glass scattered across the driveway.

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A convertible Jaguar was found abandoned 50 minutes later, and police issued an appeal to the public.

The soft-top red F-type cabriolet is believed to have been used in a drive-by shooting.

One resident said: "I was just sat watching the TV. They were talking about Newcastle on Sky Sports News, then we heard like a pop.

"At first I thought it was a car backfiring. We jumped.

"And then there was another pop, and it was a case of 'What the heck was that?'

"Then the next thing we saw was a red soft-top car go down the road.

"After the pops, I looked along towards next door and we saw all the glass that was outside, and the front windows were gone.

"It is something you see in movies or television.

"You don't expect to see it in a little village, in your own of street."

Another neighbour, Jenny, who has lived on the estate since 1973, said: “It’s a very quiet estate usually.

“You never get anything like this.”

Durham Police said the investigation remains ongoing.

Anyone with information is asked to call 101, quoting incident reference 479 of August 29.
