PLANS to close a major stretch of road in North Yorkshire have been delayed again.

A section of the A19 which was set to be closed for six months from September is now set to be postponed.

The stretch of road between Wand Lane and Chapel Haddlesey was originally set to be closed from March of this year while an electrical circuit was going to be laid from land close to Brayton to the former Eggborough power station.

Suggested diversion routes meant that there may be limited access to main junctions such as with Roall Lane and that HGV traffic would be routed through some of the smaller villages before joining the A64.

As reported by The Press in February, it was revealed that the work had been delayed until the autumn instead with North Yorkshire Council’s head of network strategy, Allan McVeigh, saying at the time that it would be taking place in September.

This was to allow Power On to consult with councillors, residents and others about the planned work – with several residents voicing their concerns about the impact that the works would have.

In response to this delay, a spokesperson for Power On said: “The planned A19 works have been delayed pending coordination with other planned works and associated diversion routes in the area.

“At this stage, Power On is not in a position to confirm a revised start date.”
