EMERGENCY services scrambled to a collision between a vehicle and pedestrian in the Bradford district.

The incident occurred on Kensington Street in Keighley on Tuesday morning.

Police and ambulance crews could be seen on the street.

The force says it received a call just after 11am.

The pedestrian was taken to hospital with injuries not believed to be serious, police added.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "We took a report at 11.09am this morning of a vehicle and pedestrian in collision on Kensington Street.

"The pedestrian was taken to hospital with injuries not believed to be serious."
