  **UPDATE: The road has since cleared.**

Motorists are being warned of heavy traffic following a crash involving a lorry on a major road.

The incident happened on the A1 Northbound between J44 A64 (York/Tadcaster) and J45 A659 Wattle Syke (Boston Spa) shortly before 6am on Tuesday (September 1).

  


The crash had caused one lane to become blocked.

AA Traffic said: “Very slow traffic due to crash, [lorry] now on the shoulder, on A1(M) Northbound from J44 to J45.”
