An "eyesore" leisure centre surrounded by "antisocial behaviour" could be pulled down this year.

Demolition plans have been submitted for the Mill House Leisure Centre in Hartlepool, which is “fully redundant” following the opening of the town’s new multi-million pound waterfront active wellbeing hub, Highlight.

Hartlepool Borough Council, which is behind the application, said the building is now "fully redundant" and has created "ongoing security and public safety issues".

The planning application states: "Unlawful access to the site continues to cause antisocial behaviour, which requires 24/7 on site security teams to manage risks and protect the surrounding community.

"Demolishing the structure directly resolves the antisocial behaviour concerns while removing a visual eyesore from the local landscape.

"Once cleared, the footprint will be transitioned into a soft landscaping arrangement, enhancing the immediate area and creating a cleaner, safer environment for the community."

The plans describe the building as having "reached the end of its lifespan," with the structure originally designed in the late 1960s for heavy daily use.

Demolition is scheduled to begin on September 7, 2026, according to the documents.

The proposed works include full site clearance, breaking out the swimming pool, backfilling with inert subsoil, removing foundations, and asbestos disposal.

Waste from the demolition will be transported to a recycling centre in Middlesbrough, with materials to be used in groundworks projects across Teesside.

The application also states that there are no plans for future redevelopment or rebuilding on the site at this time.

A decision on the demolition plan is expected following a consultation.
