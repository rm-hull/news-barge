RESIDENTS reported seeing armed officers and multiple police vehicles on a Bradford street this afternoon as specialist teams attended a property.

Photos circulating on social media appeared to show armed police outside a home on Sunningdale, Allerton, with several officers in specialist protective equipment gathered near the front of the property.

## Armed officers seen outside property

The photographs, shared online, appeared to capture a significant police presence in the residential neighbourhood.

Several officers could be seen positioned outside the address, while police vehicles lined part of the street.

The images prompted speculation among residents about the nature of the incident.

## Here's why police were there

West Yorkshire Police has since confirmed officers attended the property as part of an ongoing criminal investigation.

A force spokesperson said: "Police attended at a property on Sunningdale, Bradford this afternoon (1 September) as part of an ongoing criminal investigation.

"Specialist officers conducted a search of the property with the operation concluding just after 4pm."

## Investigation ongoing

Police have not disclosed further details about the investigation and have not confirmed whether any arrests were made.

The force has also not released information regarding any items that may have been recovered during the search.

The operation concluded shortly after 4pm.

Anyone who saw the police activity in the area may have noticed officers carrying out inquiries as part of the ongoing investigation.
