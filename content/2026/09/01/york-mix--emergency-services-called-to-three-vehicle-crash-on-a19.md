Emergency services were called to a crash on the A19 in North Yorkshire early this morning (Tuesday).

It was on the northbound carriageway between **Thirsk** and**South Kilvington** at**1.41am**.

Fire crews from Thirsk, Northallerton and Ripon went to the incident.

#### A19 at South Kilvington

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “On arrival of crews all occupants were out of the vehicles.

“Crews assisted paramedics in assessing the casualties and providing first aid assistance.

“Crews made the vehicles and scene safe before leaving the incident with police awaiting recovery.”
