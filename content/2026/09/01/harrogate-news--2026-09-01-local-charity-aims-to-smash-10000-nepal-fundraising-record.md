Following recent devastating floods, local charity Harrogate Brigantes Rotary launched a Nepal floods appeal to beat their previous £10,000 record set after the 2015 earthquake.

President Guy Wilson expressed shock and sadness over the disaster, noting the club’s long-standing ties to Nepal through the ‘Changing Young Lives in Nepal’ project and local partners like The Himalayan Gurkhas Rotary Club in Kathmandu.


**President Guy Wilson said:**

We’re sure, like us, you will have been shocked and saddened by the terrible scenes coming out of Nepal and our thoughts are with everyone who has lost their life, loved ones, home or livelihood, as well as for those who are just waiting for news.



Co-organiser Charlotte Gale noted that the Himalayan Gurkhas reached out for assistance following the catastrophic floods, prompting the new appeal. A generous legacy left to the club will match-fund donations up to £7,500.

Donations can be made securely via their Just Giving link. Registered charity number: 1046738.
