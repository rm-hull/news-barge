A company linked to a Middlesbrough barber has been handed a £40,000 penalty after employing illegal workers.

TS3 Barbers Limited, linked to Ali Barber at 8 Norfolk Place in Middlesbrough, appeared on the latest Home Office list of employers issued with penalties for illegal working.

The £40,000 penalty appears in figures covering the period from January 1 to March 31, 2026.

Civil penalties can be issued to employers found to have employed people who did not have permission to work in the UK or who were working in breach of restrictions attached to their immigration status.

  A badge on the front of an immigration enforcement officer 

*(Image: PA MEDIA)*

Businesses are required to establish that anyone they employ has the legal right to work in the UK.

The Home Office says employers should carry out prescribed right-to-work checks before somebody starts work.

Further checks can also be required where an employee has time-limited permission to work in the country.

TS3 Barbers Limited was among eight North East companies included in the Home Office’s latest quarterly release.

The largest penalty in the region was £135,000, issued to Jana Shawarma Ltd, which is linked to Shawarma & Grill House in Gallowgate, Newcastle.

Mr Bubbles Mini Valet Centre NE Ltd in Newcastle received a £90,000 penalty, while Sunderland Hand Car Wash Ltd, linked to Fence Houses Hand Car Wash, was fined £80,000.

Elsewhere, KLW NE Ltd, linked to the former Hollathan’s restaurant in Chester-le-Street, Pizza Hot NE Ltd in Ferryhill and Style Barbers Birtley Ltd were each fined £40,000.

Lobstar Leisure Ltd, linked to Tarantinos in Sunderland, received a £45,000 penalty.

The quarterly report lists employers whose civil penalty cases have reached the relevant stage of the objection and appeal process.
