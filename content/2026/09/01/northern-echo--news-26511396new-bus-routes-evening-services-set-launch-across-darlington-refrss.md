Residents across Darlington are set to benefit from a range of new and improved bus services later this month.

The enhanced routes will include more frequent journeys and extended evening and Sunday services to better connect communities in and around the town, with a particular focus on rural areas.

Darlington Borough Council has teamed up with the Tees Valley Combined Authority (TVCA) to enhance services across the borough, with council leaders saying the changes will help connect people to jobs, education, healthcare and other key destinations.

Among the improvements is a regular hourly daytime service between Darlington, Sadberge and Bishopton from Monday to Saturday, while passengers in Middleton St George will benefit from a more frequent half-hourly service.

A new direct hourly route linking Darlington, Heighington, School Aycliffe and Newton Aycliffe will also be introduced.

People living in Mowden and the west end of Darlington will see a half-hourly service, including new direct buses to Darlington Railway Station and Queen Elizabeth Sixth Form College.

Routes will broadly follow the current 16 and 17 services.

An hourly service to West Park Hospital will also be launched, while evening and Sunday buses will return between Mowden, West Park, the west end of town and the new Esh housing development on Neasham Road.

Other changes include a new hourly service along Glebe Road and a direct bus connection to Teesside Park.

The existing evening and Sunday services on the 6, 6A and 6B routes, which were introduced last May, will also continue.

The improvements are being funded in part by the TVCA, which has committed more than £8.9 million to support bus services across the Tees Valley until at least March 2028.

Meanwhile, Darlington Borough Council is funding the new evening and Sunday services using money secured through developer contributions from sites around West Park and Darlington Arena.

Councillor Steve Harker, leader of Darlington Borough Council, said: "One of our ambitions laid down in the Council Plan is to create thriving and connected communities and reliable and accessible bus services are a key part of that.
