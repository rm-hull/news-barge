A popular Darlington music festival will return later this month, bringing free live performances to venues across the town centre.

Darlington Rhythm & Tunes will take place from September 11 to 13, featuring a mix of original artists and tribute acts performing across a range of genres including rock, pop, country and soul.

Organised by Darlington Borough Council in partnership with Darlington Rock n Blues Club and Gasto Promotions, the event has evolved from its roots as a blues festival into a broader celebration of live music.

Performances will be held at venues including The Forum Music Centre, Vesuvio, The Britannia and The Quaker House.

   

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The festival’s main Market Square stage will host tribute acts Blondied, Fu Fighters and Chilifornication from 3pm on Saturday, September 12.

On Sunday, September 13, music fans can enjoy performances from The Boneshakers, The Addiction, John Verity Band and Band of Friends from 1pm, although organisers said the line-up is subject to change.

The event will also feature the Rhythm & Blues Fringe, now in its 30th year, with performances taking place in the town centre from 2pm.

Councillor Libby McCollom, Darlington Borough Council’s cabinet member for local services, said the festival had grown significantly over the years.

She said: “One of our ambitions in the Council Plan is to create thriving places and connected communities and nothing brings people together more than music.

“I hope lots of people will come down and enjoy the wide variety of acts who are performing.”

Councillor McCollom also revealed the festival will undergo a revamp next year, with organisers planning a new musical theme designed to embrace an even wider range of music.

Further information, including performance times and venues, is available via Enjoy Darlington.
