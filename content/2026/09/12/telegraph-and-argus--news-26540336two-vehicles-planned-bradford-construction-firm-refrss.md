A BRADFORD construction company plans to expand its fleet.

Chellow Construction Ltd is looking to add two more goods vehicles to its existing operator's licence.

The vehicles would be stationed at its operating centre on Teasdale Street.

The company has assured that no extra trailers will be brought in.

Neighbours and nearby landowners who might be affected by this change can voice their concerns.

Written representations should be sent to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Leeds, within 21 days of the notice's publication on September 10.

The same representations should also be forwarded to the applicant, Chellow Construction, at its Bolton Hall Road address.
