![The headline on the front page of the Mail on Sunday reads: “Farage: £72m war chest will ready us for No 10”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/810/cpsprodpb/831f/live/2c318100-aeed-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

Almost every paper on Sunday leads on a record-breaking double donation of £72m to Reform UK over the weekend. The Daily Mail declares "Nigel hits the jackpot", describing the way the "jubilant" Reform UK leader hailed it as an "investment in democratic renewal". Farage told the paper that the "£72m war chest" would be used to "professionalise" the party.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Times reads: “£72m gift to Reform stuns politics”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/825e/live/9f65bb90-aeee-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

The donation to Reform UK was courtesy of two cryptocurrency billionaires, according to the Sunday Times, who each gifted the party £36m. The paper calls it an "unprecedented cash injection" for Reform UK, and says the previous highest political donation was £10m, which was left to the Conservative party in a will. The donors, Christopher Harborne and Ben Delo, have both said the gift is to help the party prepare for government.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Express reads: “£72 million!”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/ce33/live/f3682680-aeec-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

Similarly, the Sunday Express says the "turbocharged war chest" will allow Reform UK to to take on Labour and the Conservatives at the next general election. The paper says the money will be viewed as a "major vote of confidence" in the party at a time when there has been a dip in support.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Telegraph reads: “Farage: £72m war chest will ready us for power”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/0787/live/32a5e0d0-aeed-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

The double donation brings Reform's total donations for the year to £86m, which the Sunday Telegraph says dwarfs the £10.2m raised by Conservatives and £7.7m by Labour. The paper notes Farage still faces a parliamentary inquiry into the £5m personal gift from Harborne, in addition to a police inquiry into donations from elsewhere.

![The headline on the front page of the Observer reads: “£72m plan to buy Britain 'could be illegal'”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/789/cpsprodpb/9c07/live/0a1e5e30-aefc-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

The Observer characterises the donations as a "£72m plan to buy Britain", and questions whether they break new anti-corruption laws.

![The headline on the front page of the Mirror reads: “Dark day for democracy”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/78c8/live/0d052840-aeed-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

The Sunday Mirror says the £72m donation has sparked fears for the future of democracy, with Fair Vote UK campaigner Kyle Taylor describing it as a "dark day indeed".

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: “Lisa: I'm so proud of my Jezza”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/b543/live/17021290-aeed-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

Television presenter Jeremy Clarkson and partner Lisa Hogan beam on the front page of the Star, with the latter detailing her pride after "Jezza" revealed his prostate cancer diagnosis earlier this year.

![The headline on the front page of the Sun on Sunday reads: “US spies smuggle paedo out of UK”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/a14b/live/25377670-aeed-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

The headline of the Sun on Sunday reads: "US spies smuggle paedo out of UK". The paper says the "sneaky move" has sparked "crisis meetings" between Scotland Yard and senior Cabinet ministers.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday People reads: “Is it worth risking your life for these ops”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/2ab7/live/1eebfb60-aeed-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

"Is it worth risking your life for these ops?" asks the Sunday People, leading on a campaign from a grieving father whose daughter died in Turkey after complications from surgery.
