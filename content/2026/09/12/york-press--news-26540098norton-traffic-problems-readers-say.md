READERS have shared their views on action to tackle traffic chaos that has turned a North Yorkshire neighbourhood into a "rat-run."

As reported by the Gazette & Herald, people living on St Nicholas Street in Norton say the congestion is part of a wider traffic problem affecting Church Street, Commercial Street, and Welham Road, and fear it will only get worse once a new supermarket opens in the town.

The report has generated strong views from readers with mixed views on how to solve the issue.

HT said: "We need a ring road. It is no good to have a major route to Hull going through Mill Street - a tiny road, where only ever one lane is available and also through the T-junction with Welham Road.

"The pressure of trying to get through is what makes people go via St Nicholas Street.

"A ring road would sort it. And make other ends of our town safer and air cleaner.

"A ring road could also serve as anchor for more industrial type development. "Maybe even move most of businesses traffic out of the Peasey Hill side and make it safer around the two primary schools.

"A ring road is the key."
