A motorbike has been stolen from a home in North Yorkshire.

The theft happened in Eastfield, near Scarborough, on Friday morning (September 11), North Yorkshire Police said.

Officers are urging anyone with information about the theft at the property in the Boulevard between 7.30am and 10am on Friday to come forward.

North Yorkshire Police said anyone who can help the investigation should phone 101 or email [jake.fairbotham@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:jake.fairbotham@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting reference number 12260178668.
