Three members of a community club have been honoured for their long-standing service and dedication.

Keighley Lions members Julie Walters, Ruth Payne and David Ogden were recognised at the club’s most recent meeting for their contributions.

Ms Walters and Ms Payne received the Melvin Jones Fellowship Award, the highest award given by the club.

Ms Walters, who has served as club secretary for three years and has been a member since 1997, said: "I was very honoured.

"It’s my job to order the awards, and I never thought I would be ordering one for myself.

"Lions is an international organisation, but we do a lot in Keighley.

"It’s a lovely service and we have fun doing it."

Mr Ogden, 83, was presented with a service award for 35 years of continued service at the club.

He said: "Every five years, we get an award.

"I'm not as involved as I used to be, but we've got a good team and we've done a lot over the years.

"I enjoy the friendships and the fact that in the Lions club, everything is for charity.

"It's very rewarding."

Mr Ogden stressed that there is more to the club than sports.

He said: "It’s difficult sometimes because people think we’re just a rugby team, and we’re not.

"We do a lot of fundraising.

"We raise thousands of pounds.

"We enjoy the social side and we put the fun in fundraising."
