BEAUTIFULLY presented accommodation, a village location and stunning views over the adjoining countryside and towards the River Tees are just three of many reasons Russett House in Mickleton is sure to appeal to a wide range of househunters.

The fact that it is offered for sale with no onward chain is another.

Russett House’s ground floor accommodation starts at a vestibule which leads into a split-level entrance hall with a cloakroom off.

  Stylish Russett House is perfect for buyers looking for a village home 

*(Image: GFW)*

To the left off the hall lies the living room, a light and airy dual aspect space in which an electric flame effect stove provides a focal point.

To the other side of the hall is a versatile room currently used as a study, but which could be utilised for a number of purposes, as well as a utility room and the dining kitchen to the front of the house.

This is home to a range of modern fitted units topped with solid oak worktops and with integral appliances including an electric four-ring hob with an extractor fan, an electric oven and a dishwasher, with space for a freestanding American-style fridge/freezer.

Alongside the kitchen is a dining area with ample space for a family-size table and with a glazed door opening into the garden.

  Stylish Russett House is perfect for buyers looking for a village home 

*(Image: GFW)*

Upstairs, off the spacious split-level landing, are the family bathroom and four bedrooms, two well-proportioned doubles, a good sized single and the principal suite.

This comprises a generous bedroom, a walk-in wardrobe and an en-suite shower room.

Russett House’s gardens predominantly extend to the front and are laid to lawn with a pleasant patio area providing an ideal space for entertaining.

To the rear is a small enclosed area laid to lawn.
