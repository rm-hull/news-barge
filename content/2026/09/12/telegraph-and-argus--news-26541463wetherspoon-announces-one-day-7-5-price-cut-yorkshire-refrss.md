THREE Wetherspoon pubs will cut the price of all food and drink by 7.5 per cent for one day only.

The Myrtle Grove in Bingley, The Turls Green in Bradford, and The Obediah Brooke in Cleckheaton will take part in Tax Equality Day on Thursday, September 17, to support a campaign calling for lower VAT in the hospitality sector.

At present all food and drink in pubs is subject to 20 per cent VAT, compared to the zero rate applied to food sold in supermarkets.

Chris Ashworth, manager of The Turls Green, said: "We are proud to support the campaign to reduce the level of VAT within the hospitality industry.

"Customers coming to the pub on Thursday, September 17 will find the price of their food and drink is lower than normal.

"We are keen to highlight the amount customers could save if VAT in pubs was lowered permanently."

The price change means a meal and drink costing £10 would be reduced to £9.25.

Wetherspoon argues that supermarkets can use the VAT saving to sell alcohol at lower prices.

The discount will apply for one day only.
