Plans to transform a long-empty car dealership site into an autocentre and drive-thru coffee shop could create around 27 new jobs.

A proposal submitted to Hartlepool Borough Council would see the former Evans Halshaw site off Usworth and Brenda Road, Hartlepool, brought back into use for commercial development.

The plans for the site, which has sat empty since 2020, include converting the old car showroom and vehicle workshops into an autocentre offering MOT testing, vehicle servicing, and repairs.

The scheme also calls for a partial demolition, external alterations, and the construction of a new drive-thru coffee shop.

A planning statement said the redevelopment would "make efficient use of the site and create a significant number of new job opportunities."

If granted, the project is expected to create 15 full-time and 12 part-time roles.

Developers stated that the plans are in keeping with the surrounding area and would make good use of previously developed land.

They added: "There will be no unacceptable impacts on the amenities of adjoining occupiers and the proposals have been designed to minimise opportunities for crime."

The drive-thru coffee shop is described as a "complementary facility" serving employees within the Usworth Road/Park View West industrial estate and other visitors to the area.

The proposed scheme also includes revised vehicle access, parking and servicing areas, electric vehicle charging points, and landscaping.

The applicant said there were "no sequentially preferable sites that would be both available and suitable for the proposed development."

The council is expected to make a decision once the public consultation period concludes.
