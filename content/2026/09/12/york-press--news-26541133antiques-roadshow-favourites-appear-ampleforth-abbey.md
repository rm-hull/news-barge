## Antiques Roadshow favourites to share their expertise at Ampleforth Abbey

Susan Rumfitt, known for her jewellery expertise on Antiques Roadshow, will deliver three talks at a jewellery showcase at Ampleforth Abbey *(Image: Ampleforth Abbey)*

Antiques Roadshow favourites will be sharing their expertise in a series of talks in North Yorkshire next weekend.

Susan Rumfitt, known for her jewellery expertise on the BBC programme, will deliver three talks at a jewellery showcase at Ampleforth Abbey from September 18 to 20.

Ms Rumfitt said: "I’m really excited to give these Talks at Acquire Ampleforth, bringing to life the magnificent jewellery history of the 20th century.

"From the elegance of the Edwardian period, where lace work was a fashion must and an influence on other garland styles of jewellery, to the abundance of colour we see post-World War II, these talks have something for all tastes."

She will also be joined by fellow Antiques Roadshow expert Steven Moore for a conversation about his book, The Stuff of History.

Talks from other experts will include Brett Payne, who will discuss contemporary silversmithing, and Sarah Leighton, who will share insights into the world of gemstones.

The full schedule and tickets are available via: [https://www.ampleforthabbey.org.uk/acquire-ampleforth](https://www.ampleforthabbey.org.uk/acquire-ampleforth)

Entry to the showcase is free, though talks are ticketed.
