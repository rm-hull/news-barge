A NEW live show is shining a spotlight on the female storytellers behind some of the world’s best-loved wildlife documentaries.

Wild Women Live – The Tour will visit Ilkley’s King’s Hall on Monday, May 10, 2027, as one of 29 dates across the UK, including nearby Harrogate on May 13.

The tour celebrates the women working behind and in front of the camera who have brought the natural world to life for millions of viewers.

Broadcaster Michaela Strachan said: "Wild Women Live is for anyone who has ever marvelled at the natural world on screen – as well as being an invitation to the next generation of girls and young women to imagine themselves behind the lens, in the field and at the forefront of conservation."

Joining Ms Strachan on stage will be wildlife camera operator and presenter Anna Dimitriadis, filmmaker Libby Penman, and environmental journalist and author Sarah Roberts.

The show will feature stories from jungles, Arctic ice, and the UK countryside, highlighting the courage, exceptional skill and resilience of women in wildlife filmmaking.

Wild Women Live is sponsored by the RSPB and Volvo.

Beccy Speight, chief executive of the RSPB, said: "We’re thrilled to be supporting the tour, which I’m sure will not just be immensely entertaining but great inspiration for people."

  **Tickets are on sale now viawildwomenlive.comor direct from venues.**
