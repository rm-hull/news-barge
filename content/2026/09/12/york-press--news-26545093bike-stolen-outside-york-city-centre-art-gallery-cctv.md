*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A bike has been stolen outside an art gallery in York city centre.

North Yorkshire Police has issued photographs of three people officers want to speak to about the theft outside the Lucy Pittaway Art Gallery in Coppergate.

The force said the theft happened at 12.30am on Sunday, August 16.

A police spokesperson said the people pictured “may have information that could help us progress our enquiries”.

“We appreciate that the quality of the image is not perfect,” they said.

“Unfortunately, this is beyond our control as the footage was captured on a CCTV system owned and operated by a third party.

“Despite this, we are hopeful that someone may recognise the individual and help us with our enquiries.”

Anyone with information is asked to email [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260159486 when passing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
