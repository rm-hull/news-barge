A partial closure on a major road near York’s Outer Ring Road, which has caused “absolute chaos” for drivers, will remain in place until November, the council has confirmed.

City of York Council has placed a one-way closure along the Monks Cross Link to enable infrastructure works associated with the building of roughly 970 homes, as part of the development of Monks Cross Village Gardens.

A council spokesperson said: “The closure is for getting out of Monks Cross (the northbound lane) and we have permitted it until November 6th 2026 to enable the work on one roundabout (northern access to the site) and other utility works associated with the site.”

  


As The Press reported, drivers were warned of large and building traffic queues in connection with the closure on Sunday (September 6).

One commenter on social media described traffic around Monks Cross as “absolute chaos”, adding that she had anticipated a “long journey” back home.

Another said: “It was gridlock around Monks Cross.

“Queues in every direction, horns blaring and only just over an hour until the supermarkets closed.”

Gillian Chapman said that a journey home which normally takes ten minutes, lasted an hour.

Katie Atkinson added: “I’ve never seen anything like it.

“Gridlock. I was stuck in a traffic jam for two hours. I’ll aim to avoid Monks Cross from now on.”

  An impression showing how houses planned as part of the Monks Cross Garden Village, in Huntington, York, could look. 

*(Image: Redrow Homes/City of York Council's planning portal)*

Planning permission to build the homes was granted by the Secretary of State in 2022.

It followed an appeal from the developer to the Planning Inspectorate and it was ruled the council had taken too long to decide on the application.

The latest plans feature 170 homes, including affordable ones, along with landscaping, street scene features and electric vehicle charging points.

A new community hub, shops and a country park are also planned as part of the garden village which is being led by Redrow Homes and Bellway.

  A phasing plan for the Monks Cross Garden Village, in Huntington, York, could look. 

*(Image: Redrow Homes/City of York Council's planning portal)*

They come after the council’s planning committee approved Redrow Homes’ application in October 2025 for 314 homes as part of the first phase of the garden village.

A council spokesperson said: “Further closures are highly likely afterwards and into 2027/28 as the developers need to form another new roundabout (southern access) to provide access to the development site as well as undertake various utility works and other highway works, some of them already completed.

“The Network Monitoring team have been actively managing the network by optimising traffic signal times in the area to suit the changing travel demands since Monday (September 7) morning and will continue to do this throughout the road works period.

“This is of course limited to the team’s working hours.”
