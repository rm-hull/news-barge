A York pub closed since March is preparing to reopen.

The Blacksmiths Arms in Huntington shut after Don and Ana Mattless retired from running it after 14 years behind the bar.

It left the village without a pub.

Owners the Stonegate Group advertised the tenancy earlier this year, saying: “The Blacksmiths would suit an operator who enjoys running a traditional village pub with a strong food offer and a real sense of community.

“This is a well-loved local with a loyal customer base, so someone who values consistency, quality, and personal service will thrive here.”

Now the wait for the pub, in The Old Village, Huntington, to reopen is nearly over.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/the-blacksmiths-arms-huntington-york-gsv.webp)

A post shared by the Blacksmiths Arms on Facebook said: “It’s been a little quiet around here with the pub closed since March, but we’re excited to finally be able to say that good things have been happening behind the scenes!

“The pub has now been taken over by a new publican, with the team being managed by someone with an impressive 30 years of experience in the pub trade.

“Babs and the team are so excited to take on this fantastic community pub and we’re looking forward to settling in, getting to know the locals and making The Blacksmiths Arms a place for the whole community to enjoy.”

The announcement was met with widespread delight. “Amazing news. Looking forward to supporting you,” one commented.

Another wrote: “Such good news, can’t wait to come back.”
