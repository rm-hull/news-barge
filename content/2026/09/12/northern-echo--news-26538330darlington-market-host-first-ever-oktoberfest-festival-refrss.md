Darlington Market’s German-inspired bar and eatery is hosting its first-ever Oktoberfest celebration next month.

Haus der Bratwürste will bring a taste of Bavaria to the town centre during a three-day festival running from Friday, October 2 to Sunday, October 4, transforming Darlington Market Square into a traditional Oktoberfest.

The event promises authentic German beers, bratwurst, live oompah bands, DJs, steins and classic Bavarian entertainment.

A spokesman for Haus der Bratwürste said: "We’ve had a fantastic response since opening Haus der Bratwürste and wanted to bring something different to Darlington.

"Oktoberfest is one of the world’s best-known food and drink festivals, and we’re excited to recreate that atmosphere here with great beer, authentic food and live entertainment for everyone to enjoy."

On Friday and Saturday, there will be two sessions each day running from 12pm to 4pm and 6pm to 11pm.

   Haus Der Bratwürste 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Tickets are available in three tiers, starting with a £5 entry ticket which gives access to the marquee, entertainment, bars and food vendors.

A £25 Bratwurst & Stein ticket includes a bratwurst and a stein of German beer, while the £65 All You Can Eat & Drink option offers unlimited selected German beer and bratwurst throughout the session.

The organisers described the event as one of the town’s biggest food and drink gatherings of the year.

Tickets can be purchased from the Haus der Bratwürste website or at the bar inside Darlington Market.

   Haus Der Bratwürste 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Sunday will take on a more relaxed, family-friendly tone with free entry all day and entertainment for all ages.

There will be DJs, German food, bratwurst, pretzels and authentic Bavarian beers for adults.

The family day is designed to be a fun outing for both children and adults, offering the Oktoberfest experience in a more laid-back setting.
