A NEW report from Adoption England warns that by 2030 there may only be enough approved adoptive families for just 13 per cent of children in need of adoption if registrations decline further.

The report has prompted a renewed call for families to consider adoption, with 1,600 more children currently waiting to be adopted than there are approved adopters, a gap that has grown by 52 per cent over the past five years.

During the same period, the number of approved adopters has dropped by 46 per cent.

Susy White, chief executive of Yorkshire-based voluntary adoption agency Adoption Matters, said: "Behind every statistic is a child waiting for the chance to belong.

"The longer children wait, the more precious time is lost."

The You Can Adopt campaign, backed by Adoption Matters, is urging more people to come forward and consider adoption, emphasising that every day a child spends without a family is a day of lost childhood.

Children waiting to be adopted spend an average of 646 days in care before finding a permanent home.

The campaign’s new short film, Every Day is Everything, features former Paralympian Ellie Simmonds OBE, The Traitors stars Roxy and Judy Wilson, and former Love Island winner and post-adoption social worker Sanam Fagan.

Heather and Dan, adopters through Adoption Matters, welcomed their son into their blended family when he was 16 months old.

Heather said: "Dan is amazing with the girls and it just felt right for us to explore adoption as we knew so many children were waiting for a family."

Their experience highlights the campaign’s message that adopters come from all walks of life and do not need to fit a traditional mould.

Ms White said: "We hope this campaign encourages more people to explore adoption and discover the life-changing impact they can have on a child's future.

"We know there are individuals and families who could offer a safe, loving home, and our message is simple: don't rule yourself out.

"Adoption Matters is here to support people every step of the journey."

Adoption Matters regularly hosts information events for those interested in adoption.

Their next online sessions will take place on October 20 at 10.30am and November 3 at 7.30pm, where adopter Steve will talk about his adoption journey.
