A prominent bar and restaurant in York city centre has closed for good.

Las Iguanas, on Back Swinegate, has been shut for several days.

A note in the window reads: “Closing down permanently. We apologise for any inconvenience.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/las-iguanas-york-closed-1.webp)

The York branch of the Latin American-inspired cocktail bar and restaurant opened in 2016, moving into premises vacated by La Tasca.

It made a big splash on its opening night, with dancing girls, free cocktails, and the Lord Mayor of York.

A firm favourite on the York nighttime scene, it had a 4.7/5 TripAdvisor rating, putting it at number 25 in the site’s top city restaurants.

But in recent years the group has been in trouble.

In June, a plan to save the company which runs Las Iguanas restaurants from entering administration was approved by a High Court judge.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/las-iguanas-outside-wonky-1170x780.jpg)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/las-iguanas-york-opening-16-1170x780.jpg)

Iguanas Holdings Limited ran 44 Las Iguanas restaurants across the UK, but its lawyers told the hearing that the company was “heavily loss-making” and would “inevitably enter administration” if the restructure was not approved.

Ryan Perkins, for the firm, said it had been able to stay afloat only thanks to financial support from its parent company, The Big Table Group, which also owns chains including Frankie & Benny’s, Bella Italia and Banana Tree.

The restructuring plan, which was approved by the majority of the company’s creditors, included wiping out debts of around £37 million owed to one of Iguanas Holdings’ creditors and Big Table injecting £3 million into the company as part of a “turnaround strategy”.

But this has not been enough to save the York branch.

It is the second sudden closure of a venue in this part of the city. The nearby Turtle Bay branch on Little Stonegate shut without notice on the 15 July.

It’s staff were left in shock when they were told their workplace was shutting with just 45 minutes’ notice.
