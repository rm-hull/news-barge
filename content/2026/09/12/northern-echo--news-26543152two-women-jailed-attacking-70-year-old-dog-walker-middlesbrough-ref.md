Two women who helped a gang viciously attack a pensioner walking his dog have been jailed.

Leanne Yates and Chloe Thompson helped knock the 70-year-old down before kicking and stamping on him and rifling through his pockets.

The pair found nothing before continuing to attack the pensioner, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Cainan Lonsdale said two other people involved in the attempted robbery near Parliament Road, Middlesbrough, had not been charged.

He said: “The male in the group struck him in the face and he threw some punches back but then received another blow to the face which knocked him to the floor.

“He was repeatedly kicked by the defendants. From the CCTV footage it is difficult to say if Thompson does deliver any kicks but certainly Yates can be seen kicking and stamping on him.

“It was difficult to distinguish what role each played because one wouldn’t be able to do it without the other's assistance.”

Mr Lonsdale said the group grabbed the man’s dog-walking satchel, but it contained nothing valuable.

“They began rifling through his pockets only to find he didn’t have anything,” he said. “He is dragged from the grassed area onto the pavement where further kicks and stamps were delivered.”

  Leanne Yates


Yates, 41, of no fixed address, admitted attempted robbery and assault after her arrest over the March 9 attack.

Charlie Thompson, in mitigation, said: “This was clearly deeply unpleasant offending for which she is remorseful.

“She is absolutely disgusted in herself.”

  Chloe Thompson


Thompson, of New Street, Thornaby, also pleaded guilty to the same charges.

Andrew Mark said the 27-year-old’s mother pushed her into prostitution and drug use before recently dying.

“Without that individual, she has lost a person who dragged her down and would have continued to do so,” he said.

Recorder Richard Wright said there was no excuse for the attack as he jailed both women.

“He was put to the ground, dragged about, kicked and stamped upon.

“This was a terrifying ordeal and a sustained incident – it targeted a 70-year-old man who was on his own.”

Yates was jailed for three years and nine months. Thompson received a three-year sentence.
