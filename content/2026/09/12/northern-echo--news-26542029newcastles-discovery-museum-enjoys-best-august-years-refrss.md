Newcastle's Discovery Museum has recorded its busiest August since before the pandemic, welcoming more than 28,000 visitors.

The family-friendly Things That Go! exhibition, focused on transport and held in the newly developed Special Exhibitions Gallery, proved a major draw.

The free exhibition is sponsored by Lumo Travel, Stagecoach and Tyne and Wear Metro.

Keith Merrin, director of North East Museums, said: "August has been a tremendous month for Discovery Museum.

"It’s been great seeing the museum packed with happy families.

"The Things That Go exhibition in the new gallery has been a big boost and occupies a new space which will house more exciting exhibitions to come, to showcase our world-class regional heritage and collections."

The Special Exhibitions Gallery was supported by The Wolfson Foundation and The National Lottery Heritage Fund.

Other venues in the North East Museums group also reported high summer footfall, including the Great North Museum: Hancock, Laing Art Gallery, Shipley Art Gallery, South Shields Museum & Art Gallery, Arbeia South Shields’ Roman Fort and Hexham Old Gaol.

The museum is currently undergoing a £3.9 million roof and windows refurbishment.

It recently acquired the Union flag flown from HMS Royal Sovereign, the flagship of Newcastle-born Vice-Admiral Collingwood at the Battle of Trafalgar.
