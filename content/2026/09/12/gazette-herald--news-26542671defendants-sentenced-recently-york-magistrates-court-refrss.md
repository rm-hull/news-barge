A violent woman, three drivers and a thief were among defendants sentenced recently at York Magistrates Court.

Kayle Hudson, 35, of Fossway, York, denied assaulting a woman in central York and was convicted at trial.

She was given a 12-month community order with 12 days’ rehabilitative activities, fined £60 and ordered to pay £650 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge. She was made subject to a 12-month restraining order to protect the victim.

Jenny Jobling, 54, of Chapel Street, Kirk Hammerton, pleaded guilty to drink driving and driving without a licence in Kirk Hammerton.

She was given a 12-month community order with 25 days’ rehabilitative activities and must wear an alcohol abstinence tag for 30 days. She was banned from driving for three years and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

Michael Ellis, 33, of Moor Lane, Sherburn-in-Elmet, pleaded guilty to driving whilst disqualified and driving without insurance.

He was given a 12-month community order with 15 days’ rehabilitative activities, banned from driving for 12 months and was ordered to pay a £250 fine, £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

Callum Walker Kendall, 21, of Stockton Road, Thirsk, pleaded guilty to cocaine driving in Thirsk, driving without insurance and without licence.

He was given a 12-month community order with 60 hours’ unpaid work and 15 days’ rehabilitative activities, banned from driving for 12 months and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

Evie Stables, 29, of Lowther Street, The Groves, pleaded guilty to theft of items from Co-op on Beagle Ridge Drive, Acomb, and three failures to attend court.

She was given a 18-month community order with six months’ alcohol treatment and 15 days’ rehabilitative activities.
