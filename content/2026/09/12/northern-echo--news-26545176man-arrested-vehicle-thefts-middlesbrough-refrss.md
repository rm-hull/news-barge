A 35-year-old man has been arrested over a spate of thefts from vehicles as police try to trace the owners.

Middlesbrough’s Proactive Team made the arrest yesterday morning (Friday, September 11).

He was held on suspicion of stealing from a vehicle and driving while disqualified.

A search of a Lansdowne Road property uncovered stolen goods, including tools and golf clubs.

Some items have been traced to vehicles parked at local hotels and reported stolen in the two days before the arrest.

Cleveland Police are asking the public to help identify the owners of the remaining items.

Officers seized a silver Ford Focus parked nearby with false number plates.

Police believe the car may be linked to the thefts.

After questioning, he was charged with stealing from a vehicle and driving while disqualified.

He was kept in custody overnight and appeared in court this morning (Saturday, September 12).

He was also questioned over two other vehicle-theft reports before being bailed while the investigation continues.

Anyone who recognises the goods or has information or footage should contact Cleveland Police on 101, quoting reference SG26050295.
