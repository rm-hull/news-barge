Beauty fans have been given a first look at the M&S Beauty Advent Calendar 2026, with a collection of products worth more than £460 available for £75.

The highly anticipated calendar returns with 25 beauty products, including names such as Estée Lauder, Clinique, Benefit, Aveda, Color WOW, Floral Street, ICONIC London, ESPA and bareMinerals.

But shoppers hoping to get their hands on one won't have to wait until the main launch date.

  Beauty fans can get early access to the M&S Beauty Advent Calendar from October 1. 

*(Image: M&S)*

M&S Sparks members can get exclusive early access from October 1 to October 7, giving them a week to shop before the calendar becomes available more widely. Sign up here to be one of the first to get it.

The M&S Beauty Advent Calendar officially launches in-store and online on October 13. Shoppers can get their hands on the calendar at M&S stores across the Bradford district, including Bradford city centre, Bingley and Ilkley.

This year's edit is the retailer's biggest yet, with 10 new brands joining established beauty favourites. It spans skincare, make-up, fragrance, haircare and wellness, with more full-sized products included than ever before.
