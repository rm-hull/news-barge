Roadworks will affect Darlington routes this weekend, with traffic controls remaining on each.

Here are the three roads in the town which are impacted by the roadworks this weekend (September 12-13):

### Auckland Avenue

One lane traffic is in operation on Auckland Avenue, both ways, between Brinkburn Road and Auckland Oval.

Construction started July 1, and restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until September 22 at 11pm.


### Winchester Way

One lane traffic is in operation on Winchester Way, both ways, at Rochester Way.

Electricity work started July 22, and restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until October 15 at 11pm.

### A68 Woodland Road

Temporary traffic signals are expected on A68 Woodland Road, both ways, between Hollyhurst Road and Bracken Road.

Telecoms work started August 18, and restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 15 at 11.59pm.
