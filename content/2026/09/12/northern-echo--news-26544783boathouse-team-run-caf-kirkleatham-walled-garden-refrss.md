Residents can continue visiting Kirkleatham Walled Garden this winter under a temporary deal while the council seeks a permanent operator.

Redcar Labour MP Anna Turley said Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council had agreed with the Boathouse team to run the café and keep the site active while it seeks a long-term operator.

The Boathouse café and bistro next to Redcar boating lake is run by local businesswoman Jenny Archer.

The Boathouse team will work with the Walled Garden's head gardener over winter to keep the attraction active for visitors.

  Kirkleatham Walled Garden. 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The plans could also include café-linked activities involving local schools and creative groups to keep the site a lively community destination.

Ms Turley welcomed the move and praised Jenny Archer and the Boathouse team for stepping in during the transition.

She said: "I want to say a huge thank you to Jenny and the Boathouse team for stepping in to keep the gardens active and welcoming while longer-term plans are worked out."

She called Kirkleatham Walled Garden “one of the gems of our area” and welcomed continued public access this winter.

The council is still seeking a long-term operator for Kirkleatham Walled Garden.

Councillor Alec Brown for Kirkleatham said: "We believe Kirkleatham Walled Garden has enormous potential, but we also believe it can and should be run in a way that delivers greater value for residents and visitors, without the council continuing to carry such a substantial financial burden.

"We also wanted to find an operator with a proven local track record in hospitality, someone who understands the area, understands what people want and has demonstrated the ability to create a successful hospitality offer.

"We want to see an offer that goes beyond daytime visitors, including the potential for an evening restaurant and a destination hospitality experience that brings even more people into Kirkleatham.

"Jenny and her team have stepped up, kept things moving and ensured that people can continue to enjoy this fantastic place while we work through the process of securing its long-term future."

He added that the council had received at least 17 bids as it sought a sustainable future for the venue.

Kirkleatham Walled Gardens were in the spotlight earlier this week when mourners gathered there to bid a final farewell to PC Tom Clough.

   

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

PC Tom Clough and PC Matthew Blades were killed in the line of duty on the A66 when their marked police car was hit head-on by a vehicle travelling the wrong way on Saturday, August 22.

His coffin, draped in the Union Jack and surrounded by flowers, was carried down Plantation Road as the cortege travelled towards the private service.

   

*(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Before joining Cleveland Police, PC Clough served in the military, the Civil Nuclear Constabulary and the British Transport Police.

The Grade II listed garden reopened to the public in 2021 following a major restoration project worth around £10m.

It now includes formal gardens, a glasshouse, an art gallery, a splash area and an events space.

The café is based at Kirkleatham Walled Garden, Plantation Road, Redcar, TS10 4AS.
