If you are visiting Whitby, you might plan on seeing Whitby Abbey or spending some time on the beach.

However, Trenchers is also a must-visit spot in the town and has been named the UK’s Best Fish and Chip Restaurant at the 2026 National Fish and Chip Awards.

The restaurant was first opened in 1980 and offers a traditional fish and chips menu as well as other seafood dishes.


## What is Trenchers in Whitby like?

The restaurant’s website says: “At Trenchers New Quay Road, we fry using the traditional method of beef dripping to ensure the best possible product for our customers.

“With options including seafood salads, crab, lobster, fresh fish, homemade pies and lasagnes, Whitby scampi, vegetarian options and a dedicated children’s menu, there truly is something for everyone.

“Situated on Whitby’s iconic harbour side, Trenchers provides the perfect setting for all of the family and parties of any size, with booth seating throughout, and friendly service at every visit.”

Since it first opened, Trenchers has become “an instant hit” with both locals and tourists.


The menu features a range of options from oysters and tempura-battered vegetables to start to salads and sandwiches.

You can get traditional fish and chips, choosing from hake, cod, haddock, plaice, and halibut that are served with the restaurant’s chips, which are fried in beef dripping.

Alternatively, you can have seafood chowder, fishcakes, Whitby scampi, salmon or steak pie.

Trenchers also has an extensive dessert menu featuring dishes like sticky toffee pudding, lemon and ginger cheesecake, and sherry trifle.


## What do reviews say about Trenchers in Whitby?

Trenchers has a rating of 4.5 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with 6,192 people leaving reviews after their trip.

One person said: “Coming from the United States, I thought I knew fish and chips…until we ate at Trenchers!

“The fish was incredibly fresh, and the batter was wonderfully light and crispy.

“We arrived around 4pm and only had a short wait, but the restaurant and its takeaway counter became very busy.

“We finished with dessert, and my Knickerbocker Glory sundae was a delicious treat we don’t often find in the US.

“Afterward, we enjoyed walking around Whitby’s charming harbor during Pirate Week, which made the experience even more fun.

“Definitely worth a visit!”
