A new “floating" house has hit the market in a York street – a decade after the plans were first announced.

The one-bedroom home is for sale in Howard Street, off Fulford Road, for £275,000.

It marks the end of an almost 10-year process to make the development a reality, after nearby residents opposed the plans when they were first submitted in 2017.

  Inside the one-bedroom home for sale in Howard Street, off Fulford Road, York, for £275,000 

*(Image: Savills)*

The property has been built in the gap across an access lane in Howard Street at upper floor level.

An archway beneath the home leads to land at the rear where a block of six flats is being built and two Victorian coach houses are being turned into new homes.

Savills, which is marketing the one-bedroom home, said it “combines high-quality contemporary design with a setting of considerable character”.

“The exterior is particularly appealing, with mellow brick elevations beneath a slate roof with decorative detailing,” a spokesperson for the estate agent said.

“Timber sash windows to the front sit comfortably within the period street scene, while charcoal aluminium-framed windows to the rear provide a more contemporary contrast.”

A rear entrance leads to the first floor where there is an open-plan kitchen, dining and sitting room.

The bedroom is on the second floor and includes an en-suite shower room.

“This is a superb opportunity to acquire a stylish and low-maintenance home in a highly regarded setting,” the Savills spokesperson said.
