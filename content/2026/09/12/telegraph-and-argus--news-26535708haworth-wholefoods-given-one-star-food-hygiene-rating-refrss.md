An organic grocers and food shop in the Bradford district has been told it needs to improve after receiving a one-star food hygiene rating.

Haworth Wholefoods, located on Main Street, Haworth, was inspected in January.

Inspectors said food handling was "generally satisfactory," including areas such as preparation, cooking and storage.

However, concerns were raised about the cleanliness and condition of the building, including ventilation, hand-washing facilities, layout and pest control, which were found to need improvement.

The most serious issues were related to the management of food safety, which inspectors said required "major improvement."

This includes ensuring that proper systems are in place to keep food safe to eat, that staff understand food hygiene rules and that a food safety officer can be confident the standards will be maintained over time.

The T&A has requested the full inspection report and have repeatedly attempted to contact Haworth Wholefoods.
