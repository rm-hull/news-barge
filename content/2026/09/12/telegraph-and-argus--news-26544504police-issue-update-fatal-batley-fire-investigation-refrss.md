Investigation work at the scene of a fatal fire at a large building in Batley remains ongoing, police have said.

Emergency services were called to the scene on Warwick Road in Batley just after 3am on August 15.

Two bodies were later recovered from the building.

In an update issued on Friday, West Yorkshire Police said the badly damaged condition of the site means enquiries are taking longer than expected.

## Police statement in full

On social media, spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "We can update that investigation work remains ongoing at the site last month's fatal fire on Warwick Road in Batley.

"The building was very badly damaged in the fire and due to this necessary enquires to investigate the incident, and work to make the area safe, is taking some time.

"Traffic restrictions remain in place on Warwick Road while we carry out this work.

"We do wish to apologise to residents for the ongoing disruption and are seeking to return the site to normality as soon as we can."

In an earlier statement, police said cannabis had been found at the scene, which is believed to be a disused factory building.

Motorists and residents continue to face disruption around Warwick Road while investigators remain at the scene.

No further information has been released at this stage.
