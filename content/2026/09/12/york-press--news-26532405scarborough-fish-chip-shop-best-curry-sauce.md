Even though summer is over, taking a trip to the seaside can still be a fun way to spend a weekend.

Scarborough is one of the most popular seaside resorts to visit thanks to its long sandy beach and many child-friendly attractions.

The area is also known for its many great fish and chip shops that are a must-visit when you are in Scarborough.

The Famous Fishpan is a family-owned fish and chip restaurant that has been serving Scarborough since 1960.

  You can either sit inside the restaurant or take away your meal. 

*(Image: Getty)*

## What is the Famous Fishpan in Scarborough like?

The Famous Fishpan has both indoor seating with views of the sea and a takeaway area for those wanting to enjoy their meal on the go.

The restaurant offers plenty of fish options including cod, haddock, fish cakes, and scampi.

You can get the fish served with traditional chips or even opt for the fish and chip special that comes with chips, your choice of sauce, bread and butter, a drink.

If you are not a fan of batter or want something lighter, you can choose the steamed fish that is served with a salad and mushy peas.

There are lots of options for those who do not like fish from sausages and chips to burgers, baked potatoes, lasagne and even sandwiches.

You can add some extras to your meal such as onion rings, beans, gravy, and a side salad.

The Famous Fishpan even offers some vegetarian dishes so everyone is catered for.
