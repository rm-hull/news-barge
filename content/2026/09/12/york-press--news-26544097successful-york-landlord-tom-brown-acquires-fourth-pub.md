## York landlord, 28, acquires his fourth pub - which is now being refurbished

Cheers! Tom Brown behind the bar of the Lord Nelson in Poppleton- his first pub. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

A successful young York landlord has now acquired his fourth pub.

Tom Brown of Poppleton has announced he has taken over the White Swan at Wighill, near Tadcaster.

The move follows Barry Crux and Co recently marketing the lease of the village pub.

Tom says he has acquired the pub as the owner drinks in one of his, the Knapton Lion in Knapton.

Tom told The Press: “He owns the pub. We have been to the White Swan as customers. It snowballed from there. It’s a beautiful property.”

The White Swan has been closed since June after the previous tenants of ten years retired.

  The White Swan at Wighill 

*(Image: Barry Crux & Co)*

Tom continued: “It’s going for a refurb. We have three to four weeks to get it looking nice. It will get a new kitchen. I am interviewing for good chefs. The pub also has letting bedrooms.”

“We will do what we do best, good beer, good food in a nice and warm atmosphere.”

Tom has only just picked up the keys for the White Swan, which he says he plans to have back open in early October.

The 28-year-old says his three other pubs continue to do well.

They include the Lord Nelson in Poppleton, which Tom took over in 2022.

In 2023, Tom took over the Red Lion in Knapton, which was rebranded the Knapton Lion.

And last September, Tom took on his third pub – The Fox at Stockton-on-Forest.

And looking to his fourth venture, he added: “I am looking forward to getting a good name for The White Swan.”

All of Tom’s pubs are highly rated on the reviews websites.

The Fox at Stockton-on-Forest receives five stars out of five, based on eight reviews, ranking it first out of two Stockton-on-Forest restaurants.

Google awards 4.6 stars out of five, based on 361 reviews.

The Knapton Lion has 4.9 stars out of five based on 51 reviews, ranking it 196 out of 950 York restaurants.

Google awards 4.6 stars out of five, based on 467 Google reviews.

The Lord Nelson has 4.5 stars out of five on Tripadvisor, based on 47 reviews.

Google awards 4.7 stars out of five, based on 287 Google reviews.

Earlier this year, The Knapton Lion was shortlisted in The Press Award for Best Pub and also for Best Restaurant or Gastropub.

In 2024, the Lord Nelson was Press Best Pub winner.
