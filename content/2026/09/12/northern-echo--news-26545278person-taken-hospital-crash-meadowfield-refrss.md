One person has been taken to hospital following a crash in County Durham.

Emergency services were called to John Street North in Meadowfield just before 3.30pm today (Saturday, September 12) after a one-vehicle crash.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) sent several crews to the scene and was supported by a helicopter from the Great North Air Ambulance Service.

One person has since been taken to the University Hospital of North Durham by road. Their current condition remains unknown.

A NEAS spokesperson said: "We were called to a one vehicle road traffic incident on John Street North, Meadowfield, Durham, at 3.25pm on September 12.

“We dispatched two double crewed ambulances, a clinical team leader and were supported by Great North Air Ambulance Service.

“One patient was taken to University Hospital of North Durham by road."
