Here's the ultimate runners' guide for those taking on the Great North Run this weekend.

Thousands of runners and spectators will descend on Newcastle's city centre as the Great North Run kicks off on Sunday (September 13).

### Baggage Drop

Baggage buses will operate at different times according to each runner's wave.

Fill in your baggage label beforehand and use only the bus assigned to your wave colour.

You need your run number to drop off your bag and collect it at the finish in South Shields.
