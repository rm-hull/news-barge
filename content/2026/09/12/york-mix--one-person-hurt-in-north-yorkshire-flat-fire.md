One person was taken to hospital after a fire in a North Yorkshire town.

Crews from Richmond, Leyburn, Reeth and Darlington responded to a residential fire in a third floor flat in Victoria Road, Richmond, at 6.27pm last night (Friday).

Six firefighters in breathing apparatus put out the fire with a hose reel jet.

#### Victoria Road, Richmond

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A positive pressure ventilation fan was used to ventilate the property and an Immediate Emergency Care kit was used on a casualty whilst awaiting the arrival of ambulance.”
