The exact times the Red Arrows will fly over Great North Run runners and spectators this weekend.

The seven aircraft are due to leave RAF Waddington at 10.18am and fly north through Yorkshire and the North East, reaching the Great North Run route shortly before 11am.

### Red Arrows Timings

The team is due to follow this schedule:

- 
      **10.18am** – Depart RAF Waddington
- 
      **10.20am** – Whisby
- 
      **10.24am** – Scunthorpe area
- 
      **10.34am** – South of Ugthorpe
- 
      **10.40am** – Sunderland area
- 
      **10.43am** – North-west of Consett
- 
      **10.45am** – Haydon Bridge
- 
      **10.47am** – South-west of Sweethope Loughs
- 
      **10.50am** – South-east of Ponteland
- 
      **10.52am** – Newcastle upon Tyne area
- 
      **10.53am** – South Shields

### Where to Look

The aircraft are due to approach South Shields from the west after passing close to Newcastle. Spectators along the seafront, at the finish and across Tyneside should see the distinctive red jets as they head for the coast.

Those watching in Newcastle should look out for the aircraft at around 10.52am, with the team reaching South Shields approximately one minute later.

### Return Journey

After the flypast, the Red Arrows will head south along the Yorkshire coast before returning to RAF Waddington:

- 
      **11.02am** – West of Robin Hood's Bay
- 
      **11.11am** – South-east of Patrington
- 
      **11.15am** – Scartho area
- 
      **11.17am** – South of Wragby
- 
      **11.20am** – Whisby
- 
      **11.22am** – Arrive RAF Waddington

All times are approximate and may change because of weather, air traffic or operational demands.

Spectators should take their places 10 to 15 minutes before the scheduled flypast to avoid missing it.

For Great North Run runners and supporters, the key time is 10.53am, when the Red Arrows are due over South Shields.

The RAF has confirmed the planned flypast will go ahead, despite cancelling appearances at air displays in Guernsey and Belgium.
