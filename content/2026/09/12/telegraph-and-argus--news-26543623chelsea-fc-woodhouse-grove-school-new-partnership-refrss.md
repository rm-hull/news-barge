A SCHOOL has announced a major new partnership with Chelsea Football Club.

Woodhouse Grove School in Apperley Bridge has teamed up with Chelsea Football Club to launch a Football Specialism programme aimed at developing young talent through elite coaching and support.

James Lockwood, headmaster at Woodhouse Grove, said: "Woodhouse Grove's sporting success has always been built on determination, resilience and a willingness to compete with the very best.

"Those foundations will continue to underpin every aspect of the programme as pupils benefit from Chelsea FC's expertise and global network.

"This partnership is not about changing who we are.

"It is about enhancing what already makes Woodhouse Grove distinctive: academic excellence, exceptional pastoral care, innovative pathways for the future, and a sporting culture built on character, ambition and opportunity."

  Woodhouse Grove students wearing Chelsea FC merchandise 

*(Image: Woodhouse Grove)*

Under the programme, boys and girls entering Years 9 and 12 will be able to access Chelsea’s coaching, development pathways, and industry-leading support, with boarding and day places available.

Applications will open this month.

A spokesperson for Woodhouse Grove said the partnership further strengthens Woodhouse Grove's growing reputation as a destination for ambitious young athletes, and will complement existing specialist programmes including the Grove Golf Academy.

Bradford City AFC, a long-standing partner of the school, will continue to work with Woodhouse Grove.

A spokesperson emphasised: "The education of Academy scholars, the use of our facilities and our collaborative work with the Club will continue exactly as before.

"This new relationship with Chelsea FC is complementary, creating additional opportunities for our young people within our existing football provision."

Steve Shipp, Chelsea FC Football Projects Manager, said: "We’re delighted to be partnering with Woodhouse Grove School and are genuinely excited about what this programme can offer the next generation of young footballers.

"At Chelsea Football Club, we believe that developing a player is about developing the person too; helping young people grow in confidence, resilience and character, while giving them the opportunity to discover just how far their potential can take them.

"Woodhouse Grove shares that belief and has created an environment where ambition, education and sporting excellence can thrive together.

"We’re incredibly excited to be part of that journey and to see the dreams, opportunities and experiences this partnership will create for pupils at The Grove."

The spokesperson for Woodhouse Grove said the partnership will allow pupils to pursue their sporting ambitions while receiving a high-quality education, gaining the skills, qualifications, and experiences needed for future success both on and off the pitch.
