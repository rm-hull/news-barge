A man who raped an unconscious girl as a teenager, left her asleep, then returned to rape her again, showed no remorse when he was sentenced.

Charlie Dobbing attacked the girl who fell asleep after a cannabis-smoking session, a court heard.

He left her, then returned to the bedroom and raped her again.

Dobbing was a teenager when he carried out the two rapes, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Paul Cleasby said the girl was heavily intoxicated.

He said Dobbing straddled the teenager’s chest, pinned her to the bed and forced himself on her during the second rape.

In a victim impact statement, she said the rapes severely affected her mental health and she disclosed them only have considered ending her own life.

Dobbing, of Coxwold Drive, Darlington, was convicted of two rapes after a trial.

His barrister, Victoria Lamballe, said he had a low IQ and urged the judge to consider his age when he offended.

Judge Nathan Adams jailed the 21-year-old for four years.

“While she was drifting in and out of consciousness, she felt like she was still aware of what was going on but she couldn’t move,” he said.

“You took the opportunity to go upstairs where she was effectively incapacitated – aware of what was going on but unable to consent to the activity.”

Dobbing was given a 10-year restraining order and ordered to remain on the sex offenders’ register for life.
