A York venue will be transformed into a ‘sinister’ immersive experience – as part of annual horror film festival.

Dead Northern Horror Film Festival is back at City Screen Picturehouse in York from **Friday 25 to Sunday 27 September**, bringing three days of independent horror cinema to the city.

But that’s not all. For 2026, Dead Northern have partnered with Behaviour Interactive’s Dead by Daylight – the popular multiplayer horror game – for a weekend of ‘unique horror experiences’.

Dead by Daylight, developed and published by Behaviour Interactive, has become one of the most recognisable names in modern multiplayer horror, bringing together iconic horror characters and original killers, survivors and worlds in its asymmetrical 4v1 gameplay.

Behaviour and Dead Northern are set to collaborate on a series of special events and experiences throughout the festival.

As part of the partnership, Dead Northern will host a special late-night screening of James Cameron’s *Aliens (1986)* on the Friday night.

Celebrating its 40th anniversary in 2026, *Aliens* is a landmark piece of science-fiction horror, a huge cinematic crowd-pleaser and a film with an enduring connection to the world of gaming and interactive horror.

The screening will form part of a special late-night Dead Northern event, giving horror fans the chance to experience one of the genre’s most beloved films on the big screen.

And for the festival weekend, the basement space at City Screen will be transformed into a Dead by Daylight-inspired immersive experience, giving festivalgoers the opportunity to step away from the cinema and into something altogether more sinister.

Festival attendees can ‘expect challenges. Expect surprises. And expect things not to go entirely according to plan.’

Josh Lawson, festival director of Dead Northern, said: “Dead Northern has always been about creating experiences for horror fans, so the opportunity to bring Dead by Daylight into the physical festival was incredibly exciting.

“The 10th anniversary makes this particularly special, and we’re absolutely delighted to have Behaviour on board as our headline partner.

“We’re particularly excited about *Aliens* as our Friday night screening. It’s exactly the sort of film that works brilliantly with a festival audience, and celebrating its 40th anniversary gives us another great reason to put it on the big screen.

“But the screening is only part of what we’ve got planned. The basement experience and some of the other surprises we’re working on should give festivalgoers something they simply wouldn’t get from playing the game or watching the film at home.”
