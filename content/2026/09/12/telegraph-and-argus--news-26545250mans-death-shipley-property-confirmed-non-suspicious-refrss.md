Police have confirmed the death of a man at a property in Shipley is being treated as non-suspicious.

Officers were seen attending an address on Poplar Road at around 4pm on Friday afternoon, September 11.

Images from the scene shared on social media showed at least one police vehicle and a number of officers in attendance.

West Yorkshire Police have since confirmed that a man's death has been reported. The force said the death is not being treated as suspicious.

## Police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “The death has been confirmed as non-suspicious.”

No further details have been released at this stage.

The T&A has approached West Yorkshire Police for further comment.
