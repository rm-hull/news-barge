A new multi-sports bar with a 23ft LED screen and interactive darts is set to open in Middlesbrough this month.

  Pitch! Sports Bar is preparing to bring “stadium level energy” to the town centre after radically transforming the __former Bar Fresco site, underneath the A66 and opposite STACK.__

Featuring two 32 inch TVs, two 75 inch TVs, and 23 55 inch TVs, the venue, set across two floors, will show up to six sports at any one time from F1 and rugby to football and darts.

On the first floor, visitors will find two sports simulators and interactive darts, complete with a mezzanine deck of the mammoth 23ft screen for uninterrupted viewing.

First-look visuals of the venue have revealed a bold and “high energy” design space centred around big screen sport with exposed brick, booths, high tables, and a stylish bar.

  First look visuals of Pitch! Sports Bar in Middlesbrough 

*(Image: PNEUMA HOSPITALITY)*

And now, hospitality firm Pneuma Group has confirmed that Pitch! will open to the public on Monday, September 21.

The bar was initially touted to be opening in the former Doctors Browns on Corporation Road, but plans shifted to near STACK and Flares in May.

Bosses previously said the new location was an “exciting opportunity to further strengthen that part of Middlesbrough as a destination for food, drink, live sport and social occasions”.

It was initially due to open in time for the World Cup but this was then pushed back to summer.

  First look visuals of Pitch! Sports Bar in Middlesbrough 

*(Image: PNEUMA HOSPITALITY)*

Pneuma said the bar, located in a growing area of nightlife in the town centre, is designed to be a destination for matchdays, big fixtures, group bookings, and nights out.

On the menu will be a range of match-day favourites including crispy chicken burgers, smash beef burgers, plant burgers, wings, tenders, loaded nachos, salads, sides, sharing platters, sweets and a kids menu.

Customers will be able to use the free Pitch! Sports Bar app to book seats, tickets for exclusive events and book sports activities as well as order food and drink or enjoy a VIP experience in one of the five VIP booths that are available to reserve.

Food orders will be collected from designated areas in the venue, helping to keep service simple and efficient during busy sporting fixtures and events.

  Pitch! Sports Bar in Middlesbrough 

*(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Steve Brooke, CEO of Pneuma Group, said: “Pitch! Sports Bar represents an exciting investment in a prominent Middlesbrough location, and we are proud to be bringing this unique, immersive sports and social venue to the town.

“Our ambition is to create much more than a place to watch live sport.

“Pitch! Sports Bar will be a high-quality hospitality venue, engaging visitors through interactive sports experiences that combine the thrill of real sports with the fun of high-tech simulation, whilst supporting sustainable local employment opportunities, strengthening existing local partnerships and community links, and adding another reason for people to visit and enjoy this already vibrant part of Middlesbrough.

“Middlesbrough has a huge passion for sport and a strong sense of community, and Pitch! Sports Bar has been designed exactly around that energy.

“Whether people are coming in for the football, darts, F1, food, drinks with friends, or a big night out - set around a major sporting fixture - we want Pitch! Sports Bar to feel like a venue that belongs to the town.”

Meanwhile, a deal has been struck for a new operator to take over Doctor Browns, which opened in 1867 and has been closed since 2023.

However, the pub became the victim of a fire last month and it is not known whether this has impacted plans for the site to open this Autumn.

Plans for what it will become remain under wraps at this time.
