A well-known York restaurant is up for sale.

The news comes as it prepares to stage a new season of experience-led events this autumn.

The **Old Liquor Store**, based at The Chocolate Works, Bishopthorpe Road, has designed a series of new concepts and themed events designed to “encourage repeat visits, attract new audiences and create stronger reasons for customers to dine out in the current hospitality climate”.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/the-old-liquor-store-york-chef.webp)

It includes post-races entertainment, Oktoberfest parties, chef-led tasting menus and Christmas party menus.

Manager Matt Leivers says: “There’s a shift in the way people dine out and we want to stay ahead of the curve. People are looking for more than just a meal now – they want an experience, something social, memorable and good value.

“These events are about giving people more reasons to come together, try something different and keep supporting local hospitality businesses.”

## On the market

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/the-old-liquor-store-york-outdoor.webp)

The new events come as The Old Liquor Store goes on the market for offers in excess of £150,000.

Founder Ben Williams told ***YorkMix***: “I’ve been doing hospitality for 25 years now so fancy something else now that I have two small children. 

“It’s set in a very iconic location so hopefully we will get some interest in the sale.”

The restaurant is being marketed by business agent Ernest Wilson.

It says: “Unique and very popular 60 cover good food restaurant located within a historical site utilising its period features and adding innovative interior designer flair.

“Predominantly run by a full complement of skilled staff, the owner has only an administrative role in the day-to-day operation of the restaurant and bar.

“Tremendously popular despite its discreet location, this is a destination restaurant for many regular customers, both locals and visitors looking for a top dining venue following a day at the races.

“On sunnier days, the south-facing champagne terrace is one of York’s best spots for sipping cocktails, sharing oysters and soaking up the atmosphere al fresco.”

The business has an annual turnover of £783,470.

As well as the £150,000 for the leasehold, the rent £30,000 per annum plus service charge of £1,500.

## Events

While the restaurant’s core food offering (brunch, lunch, dinner & Sunday roasts) remains at the heart of the business, a major focus for the business this year is increasing awareness that The Old Liquor Store remains open after York Racecourse events.

This “creates an opportunity to capture additional evening trade from racegoers looking to continue their day beyond the track”.
