A North East police force has introduced temporary powers in Newcastle city centre as part of measures being used during planned protests expected to take place today.

Northumbria Police said the order will run from 7am to 7pm on Saturday, September 12, allowing officers to make people remove face coverings or anything else hiding their identity.

In a post on X, the force said: "We have been informed of protests taking place in Newcastle city centre today.

"Legal orders are in place allowing officers to control the movement of those taking part.

"Demonstrators will be limited to assembling at the Civic Centre.

"There are also restrictions on the movements of an organised march.

"We also have a separate legal order in place from 7am-7pm in the city centre making it an offence for someone to conceal their identity.

"If you're in the city and have concerns, please speak to our officers.

The move makes Newcastle one of three places in England where police have imposed the restrictions, alongside Tamworth and Portsmouth.

Anyone who refuses to remove a face covering while the order is in force could face action from police.

Northumbria Police urged demonstrators to remain peaceful and follow officers’ instructions.
