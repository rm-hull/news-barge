Part of the York outer ring road is closed after a crash this lunchtime (Saturday).

It’s on the A64 between the Hopgrove roundabout and Grimston Bar.

The collision appears to be on the westbound carriageway but both carriageways are very slow.

#### Crash location

Drivers are reporting traffic at a standstill, with police on the scene.

The AA says: “Road closed, traffic problem and queueing traffic on A64 Westbound from A1237 Malton Road to A1079 Hull Road (Grimston Bar Interchange).”
