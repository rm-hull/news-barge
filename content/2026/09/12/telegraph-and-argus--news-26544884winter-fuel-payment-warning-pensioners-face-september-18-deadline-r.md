Pensioners in Bradford who do not want the Winter Fuel Payment have just days to opt out, with a key online deadline of September 18.

The move could be particularly important for people whose annual income exceeds £35,000, as they will still receive the payment unless they opt out - but HMRC will then reclaim it through the tax system.

Pensioners can call the Winter Fuel Payment helpline, but must do so before 6pm on September 18.


## How to opt out of Winter Fuel Payment

There are other ways to opt out of receiving the Winter Fuel Payment for winter 2026 to 2027.

If you receive a State Pension and can login online, you can use the Manage your State Pension service before 11.59pm on September 20, 2026.

Alternatively, you can complete the Government's online opt-out form by the same deadline.

You will need your National Insurance number when using the online form or calling the helpline.


Those using the Manage your State Pension service will need to sign in. Anyone without existing sign-in details can create them.

Importantly, opting out will not affect your State Pension.

You also do not need to opt out every year.

Once you have opted out, you will not receive the Winter Fuel Payment in future years unless you choose to opt back in.

## What if you change your mind?

Pensioners who later decide they want to receive the payment can opt back in by contacting the Winter Fuel Payment Centre.

To receive the Winter Fuel Payment for winter 2026 to 2027, you must contact the centre before March 31, 2027.

The Winter Fuel Payment Centre can be contacted by phone or post, while an online email enquiry form is also available.
