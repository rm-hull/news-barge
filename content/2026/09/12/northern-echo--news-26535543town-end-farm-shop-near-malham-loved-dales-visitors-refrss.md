A Yorkshire Dales farm shop with spectacular views towards Malham Cove has proved so popular that visitors have even planned their walks around returning to it.

Town End Farm Shop and Tearoom sits in Airton on the road to Malham, with a farm shop and café popular with walkers, cyclists and Dales visitors.

It has more than 1,100 Google reviews, averaging 4.6 stars, while Tripadvisor users rate the tearoom 4.4 stars from almost 400 reviews.

Customers can eat indoors or outside while looking across Malhamdale towards Malham Cove.

The tearoom serves classic Yorkshire Dales fare, including breakfasts, toasties, baguettes, homemade soup, cakes, bakes and scones.

Much of that meat travels only a short distance to the tearoom.

The farm breeds its own grass-fed lamb, traditional-breed cattle and rare-breed pigs, then serves the meat in the tearoom.

It also makes its own cured bacon, ham and charcuterie.

The food and views are a hit with walkers.

One Tripadvisor reviewer said their group enjoyed the stop so much while walking near the Pennine Way that they chose a next-day route taking them back there.

They praised the coffee, cakes and Malham views, and noticed customers tucking into cooked breakfasts.

Another visitor said they returned for afternoon tea and cake after having coffee there that morning, while cyclists stopped for breakfast.

Other customers have singled out the cooked breakfasts.


One customer called the breakfasts “something to behold”, while another praised the farm’s bacon and sausages after eating an English breakfast overlooking the countryside.

The farm shop gives visitors another reason to stop.

Alongside its own meat and charcuterie, it sells local cheeses, fish, game, fruit, vegetables, preserves and Yorkshire-made food and drink.

The site also has a Post Office, and Town End runs butchery, charcuterie and salumi courses.

Its location draws drivers heading to Malham, cyclists travelling through the Dales and walkers setting out on nearby routes.

Dogs are welcome in the tearoom, while the ground-floor shop and café have accessible facilities.

Town End Farm Shop opens at 9.30am from Tuesday to Saturday and at 10am on Sundays. It is usually closed on Mondays, except bank holidays.

Breakfast is served until 11.45am, lunch until 3pm, and tea and cake until 4pm.
