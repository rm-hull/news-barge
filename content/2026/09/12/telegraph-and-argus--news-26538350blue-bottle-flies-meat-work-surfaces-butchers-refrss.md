A BRADFORD butchers boasting 'quality halal meat and poultry' was forced to close following a damning food hygiene inspection.

Hussain Food Store, on Carlisle Road in Manningham, was inspected on July 16 after a member of the public made a complaint regarding unhygienic practices.

Not only did inspectors find an infestation of blue bottle flies on meat, equipment, and work surfaces throughout the premises, but they also noted that staff were "unaware of the hazard of operating whilst there was an infestation of blue bottle flies."

Contaminated meat was voluntarily discarded, and the business voluntarily closed.

## Utensils washed in hand wash sink

The report mentioned that a hand wash basin was being used to wash utensils because the equipment sink was dirty.

Inspectors explained that a hand wash basin must be used only for handwashing.

Disposable paper towels were also not adequately protected from contamination.

Officers also found weighing scales and other equipment were stored on the floor, which pose a contamination risk.

## 'Cease practice of singeing trotters'

Animal trotters were being singed with a blowtorch without adequate ventilation - which inspectors noted was a 'structural' issue.

They added that mechanical ventilation should be installed or ventilation improved.

The outside toilet also lacked a wash hand basin with hot and cold running water, soap, and hygienic hand-drying facilities.

## Premises in 'dirty condition throughout'

Hussain Food Store was in a poor condition, says the report.

As a result, the owner was ordered to deep clean entire premises, seal gap beneath external door to prevent pest access, install fly screens on open doors/windows or keep them closed, thoroughly clean equipment sink, seal exposed breeze blocks in the outside toilet and leave surfaces smooth and easy to clean.

In terms of management issues, there were concerns surrounding the fact that staff were unaware of the risks associated with operating during a blue bottle fly infestation and so a voluntary closure was agreed due to the health risk.

The business must not reopen until the infestation is removed and an authorised officer lifts the voluntary closure.

Hussain Food Store received a one food hygiene rating.

The T&A has made attempts to contact the business and would welcome a response should they wish to provide a statement.

It is not clear whether the butchers is still closed or has been allowed to re-open.
