Residents have spoken of sleepless nights after loud late-night drumming echoed through a Bradford neighbourhood, with one woman saying the noise was so sudden and powerful it "sounded like a bomb".

A video shared with campaigners appears to capture drums being played at around 11.20pm in a residential area of Frizinghall.

It has prompted fresh complaints from residents who say noise nuisance is becoming increasingly common.

  The footage was recorded from a bedroom window and has since been circulated among community groups campaigning against anti-social behaviour in Bradford.*.*


## 
  **'It sounded like a bomb'**

One resident, who asked not to be named, said the disturbance left her shaken and unable to sleep.

She told the T&A: "I had finally fallen asleep and then I was woken up. Honestly, my heart was beating...for me, it sounded like a bomb. It was extremely loud."

The resident said the recording was made from her home on August 28 and claimed the drumming could be clearly heard across the neighbourhood.

She said: "This was recorded from my back bedroom. I couldn't tell exactly which house it was coming from, but it was incredibly loud.

She added: "It was around 11.20pm and it just kept going. People have children, elderly relatives, health conditions and jobs they need to get up for in the morning."

## 
  **Residents say problem is getting worse**

The Frizinghall resident believes noise disturbances have become more frequent in recent years.

She said: "I've noticed a significant change over the last year.It seems to be getting louder and later at night because people feel there are no consequences."

The woman said noise from drumming, fireworks, loud music and vehicles could often be heard across the area.

She said:"If you're constantly being woken up or losing sleep, it affects your health, your mood and your wellbeing...you work all your life to live somewhere nice and then you can't even get a decent night's sleep."

## 
  **Campaigners raise concerns**

Community campaigner Inayah Sher said the footage had been shared in a WhatsApp group set up to discuss noise nuisance and anti-social behaviour.

She said the video had prompted widespread concern among residents.

Ms Sher said: "We do hear drums during the day and sometimes near wedding venues, but not within a residential area and certainly not at half past 11 at night.

"This is the first time we've had a video like this and it's clearly created a lot of upset amongst the community."

She added: "It's reached a point where some people feel entitled because nobody says anything to them.

"If there are no consequences, the behaviour continues."

## 
  **Residents fear reporting incidents**

Ms Sher said some residents were reluctant to speak publicly because they were worried about repercussions from neighbours.

She said: "People are frightened. They don't want to expose themselves because they're worried about what could happen afterwards.

"But if nobody reports these incidents, anti-social behaviour becomes more common."

The campaigner said she had spent years highlighting concerns about noise nuisance and the misuse of fireworks in Bradford.

## 
  **Demonstration planned**

The latest complaints come ahead of a demonstration planned outside Norfolk Gardens on September 12.

Campaigners say the event will focus on concerns surrounding fireworks, noise nuisance and anti-social behaviour, and several Bradford councillors have been invited to attend.

Residents hope the campaign will encourage authorities to take action and address growing concerns over repeated late-night disturbances.

Bradford Council has been approached for comment.
