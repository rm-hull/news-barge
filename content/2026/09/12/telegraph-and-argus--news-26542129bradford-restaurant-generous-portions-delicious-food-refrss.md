Bradford is known for being home to a huge range of restaurants serving cuisines from around the world.

The city has lots of curry houses and restaurants serving traditional curry dishes from their own regions.

The award-winning Jinnah can be found in the centre of Bradford and might be worth trying if you are struggling to choose from all of the great restaurants around the city.


## What is Jinnah in Bradford like?

Jinnah specialises in traditional Kashmiri Pakistani cuisine with lots of other global dishes on offer.

The restaurant has five different locations across Yorkshire, including one in Bradford.

The restaurant has won many awards over the years, with it being named Regional Restaurant of the Year in 2019 at the Asian Food and Restaurant Awards.

Jinnah is open for breakfast, lunch and dinner with plenty to choose from.

The breakfast menu features a range of both traditional English options as well as things like a full Desi breakfast and halva puri.

The lunch menu has plenty of choices that cost less than £10, including samosa chaats and karahi lamb served with rice and naan.

For the evening, there are lots of starters like chapli kebabs, garlic king prawns, and chicken pakora as well as Desi mains ranging from gobi gosht and lamb handi to karahi fish and biryanis.

If you are struggling to decide what to choose, you can pick one of the tawa dishes that comes with a variety of things from the menu on a sharing platter.
