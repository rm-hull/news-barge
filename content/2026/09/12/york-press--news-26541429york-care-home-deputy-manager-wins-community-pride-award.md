## 'Dedicated' York care home worker receives Community Pride Award

Ella Bond was presented with the Health Service Hero Award for her work as Deputy Manager of the Chocolate Works Care *(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

A YORK care home deputy manager whose compassion and leadership have transformed lives has been named The Press Community Pride 2026 Health Service Hero.

Ella Bond has spent five years at the Chocolate Works Care Home, rising from care assistant to her current leadership role, overseeing day-to-day management for up to 90 residents across residential, dementia and nursing care.

Manager Judy Wray praised her colleague's 'dedication, ambition, and compassionate leadership', highlighting Ella's relentless drive to improve standards and enhance residents’ lives.

  


Ella's approach centres on treating each person as unique, asking what has shaped their life and what matters most to them when completing new-resident assessments in homes or hospitals.

  Ella has spent five years at the Chocolate Works Care Home, rising from care assistant to her current leadership role 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

She makes time each day to walk around the home, chatting to residents and building relationships that enable truly person-centred care.

Judges heard that one resident now trusts only her with personal care; when his relatives struggled to get him to surgery appointments, she stepped in, ensuring he received the right treatment.

Ella also attends monthly residents’ and relatives’ meetings, listens to concerns, and has an open-door policy so families feel heard. Recently, she helped one relative secure full funding for her husband’s permanent placement, following the application through and sharing the good news personally.

  "For staff, she is a mentor and motivator, sharing her own story and guiding colleagues toward training and apprenticeships to advance their careers" 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

For staff, she is a mentor and motivator, sharing her own story and guiding colleagues toward training and apprenticeships to advance their careers.

Judy said: “Her natural leadership qualities, exceptional clinical understanding, and unwavering commitment to resident-centred care make a remarkable difference."

Speaking about her award, Ella said: “People come to live at the Chocolate Works Care Home because of the care we are able to deliver.

“Some days can be difficult, but I try my best every day.

“It’s never just one person – it’s a team effort and without all of my incredible colleagues this wouldn’t be possible.

“All of us as a team are so proud of this award.”
