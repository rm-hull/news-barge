Police have launched an investigation after reports of a vehicle being “egged” in a North Yorkshire town.

The incident of what police are treating as criminal damaged happened in Pavilion Square, Scarborough, on Wednesday morning (September 9).

North Yorkshire Police said it involved “a vehicle being egged” while in the car park between 11pm and 11.20pm.

The force is urging anyone with information about the incident to get in touch.

“We’re particularly appealing for information about anyone seen acting suspiciously in the area during the time frame and/or seen causing the damage,” a police spokesperson said.

Anyone with information is asked to email [alastair.graham-merrett@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:alastair.graham-merrett@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.

Quote reference 12260177707 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
