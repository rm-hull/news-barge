Police are appealing for witnesses after a serious collision on the M1 near Wakefield in which a man suffered potentially life-changing injuries.

The crash happened shortly after 11pm on Friday, September 11, on the northbound carriageway of the M1 near Junction 41.

According to police, a blue Kia Proceed was travelling the wrong way along the motorway after failing to stop for officers when it collided with a red Dacia Sandero.

The driver of the Dacia, a man in his 20s, was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

Police said his injuries are being treated as potentially life-changing.

Three passengers in the Kia suffered minor injuries. One person left the scene following the collision.

## Three arrests made

An 18-year-old man, a 15-year-old girl and a 30-year-old woman have been arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by dangerous driving.

All three remain in custody while enquiries continue.

The 30-year-old woman was also arrested on suspicion of being over the prescribed alcohol limit.

Police are now appealing for anyone who witnessed the collision, or who may have dashcam footage from the area at the time, to come forward as investigations continue.

## Police statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for witnesses after a collision on the M1 near Wakefield in which a man suffered serious injuries.

"This collision happened shortly after 11pm yesterday (11 September) on the M1 northbound near junction 41.

"A blue Kia Proceed was driven the wrong way up the carriageway after failing to stop for police and collided with a red Dacia Sandero.

"The driver of the Dacia, a man in his 20s, was taken to hospital with serious injuries which are being treated as potentially life changing.

"One person left the scene. Three passengers in the vehicle suffered minor injuries.

"A man, aged 18, a girl aged 15 and a woman aged 30 have been arrested for causing serious injury by dangerous driving and remain in custody while enquiries are ongoing. The 30 year old woman was further arrested for being over the prescribed limit.

"Anyone who witnessed the collision, including anyone who may have dashcam or other video footage of the incident, is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police’s Roads Policing Unit by calling 101 or go online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 13260519370.

"Information can also be provided to independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111."
