A CADET from Bradford has been named as one of the Lord-Lieutenant’s Cadets of West Yorkshire.

Cadet Flight Sergeant Shreya Patel, who serves with 44F (Bradford) Squadron, was awarded the title at the 2026 Lord-Lieutenant’s Awards ceremony held at the Huddersfield Army Reserve Centre.

The event was the first ceremony of its kind for His Majesty’s Lord-Lieutenant of West Yorkshire, Professor Adeeba Malik CBE, since her appointment in December 2025.

Professor Malik said: "Congratulations to all those recognised at this year’s Lord-Lieutenant’s Awards.

"It is a privilege to celebrate the exceptional dedication and commitment of outstanding members of the Reserve and Cadet Forces, whose contribution makes such a profound and lasting difference to the Armed Forces community in West Yorkshire.

"I am equally proud of the young people appointed as the 2026 Lord-Lieutenant’s Cadets.

"They are an inspiration to their peers and a credit to their families, units and communities.

"I look forward to their support at events and ceremonies throughout the year ahead."

The awards honour individuals from the reserves and cadet forces for outstanding service and dedication, with cadets selected to support the Lord-Lieutenant at official events throughout the year.

Cadet Flight Sergeant Patel said: "For me becoming Lord-Lieutenant Cadet is an honour as it is a very highly respected achievement within the cadet forces.

"It has been an achievement I have wanted to achieve since I started and now that I have I’m overwhelmed with joy that I’ve managed to achieve my dream after only three years in the cadet forces."

Other award recipients from Bradford included Cadet Sergeant Ethan Bowles of Bradford Grammar School CCF, who was also named one of the 2026 Lord-Lieutenant’s Cadets.

He said: "Becoming a Lord-Lieutenant’s Cadet is a great honour, and I’m incredibly grateful to have been selected to represent my squadron and the Air Cadets.

"It’s something I’ve wanted to achieve since I first discovered the position at the start of my cadet career, so I’m really looking forward to taking on the role."

The ceremony also recognised the service of reservists and cadet force adult volunteers, including Staff Sergeant Rachel Stones of Yorkshire ACF, D Company, Odsal Detachment.

The awards are hosted by the Reserve Forces and Cadets Association for Yorkshire and the Humber.
