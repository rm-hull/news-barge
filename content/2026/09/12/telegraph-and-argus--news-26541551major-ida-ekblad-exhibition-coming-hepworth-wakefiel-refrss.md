NORWEGIAN artist Ida Ekblad will open a major solo exhibition at The Hepworth Wakefield later this year.

It marks her first major solo exhibition in the UK and will feature more than 50 works, including paintings and sculptures.

Marie-Charlotte Carrier, curator at The Hepworth Wakefield, said: "We are delighted to present Ida Ekblad's first major solo exhibition in the UK and give audiences here the opportunity to encounter the incredible breadth of her work."

Ekblad, born in 1980 in Oslo, is known for her richly layered oil paintings and monumental sculptures in metal and glass.

She draws inspiration from contemporary pop culture, Expressionist painting, graffiti, and folklore.

The exhibition, titled WORLD'S LARGEST METAPHOR, will run from November 21, 2026, to May 3, 2027.
