# Cyclist seriously injured after hit-and-run in Yorkshire

## A cyclist has suffered serious injuries after a collision on a residential road in Leeds yesterday (September 11).

It was reported that a vehicle collided with a stationary e-bike at 11.11pm on Savile Drive in the Chapel Allerton area.

The driver of the vehicle failed to stop at the scene and was last seen driving off in the direction of Scott Hall Road.

The cyclist was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

A spokesperson from West Yorkshire Police said: “Anyone who witnessed the collision, including anyone who may have dashcam or other video footage of the incident, is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police’s Leeds District police by calling 101 or online quoting reference 13260519560.
