Alesha Dixon and Stacey Solomon have been announced as the new hosts of The Voice UK.

The pair replace Emma Willis, who has fronted the ITV singing competition since 2014.

Ms Willis recently left the show to take up a presenting role on the BBC's Strictly Come Dancing.

In a wider shake-up, ITV has also confirmed that Cheryl and rapper Aitch will join the programme as new coaches, taking up seats in the show's iconic red chairs.


Destiny's Child star Kelly Rowland will remain on the coaching panel, alongside Black Eyed Peas frontman Will.I.Am, who has been a coach since the show's launch.

However, long-serving coach Sir Tom Jones won't be returning, alongside McFly’s Tom Fletcher and Danny Jones, who shared a joint chair.

The changes will take effect from the next series, which began filming on Friday (September 11).

The series currently airing on ITV was filmed in 2025.

ITV also paid tribute to the departing cast members.

A spokesperson said: "We thank Emma Willis, Tom Fletcher and Danny Jones for their brilliant contributions, and who all brought so much dedication, expertise and energy to The Voice UK and wish them the very best of luck as they embark on their next chapters."

Sir Tom, 86, said he had been offered a "much-reduced role" on the show, which he was "not thrilled about".

He said: "The reason given is because of a financial difficulty with insurance.

"They now say in the press they wanted to 'refresh' the show, and with a new coaching line-up there wouldn't be a chair for me."

"He has brought joy to audiences, and to every singer who performed for him and with him."

## The history of The Voice UK

The Voice UK first aired in 2012, hosted by Holly Willoughby and Reggie Yates.

Emma Willis took over hosting duties in 2014, and joined for several series by Marvin Humes of boyband JLS.

The show moved from the BBC to ITV in 2017.

Unlike other singing competitions, The Voice UK is known for its blind auditions, where coaches face away from contestants and judge solely on vocal talent.

If impressed, they turn their chairs to offer mentorship.

Other previous coaches on the UK series include Olly Murs, Jennifer Hudson, Kylie Minogue, Rita Ora, Anne Marie and LeAnn Rimes.
