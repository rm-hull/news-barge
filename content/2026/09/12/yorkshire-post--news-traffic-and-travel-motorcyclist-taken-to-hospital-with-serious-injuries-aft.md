# Motorcyclist taken to hospital with serious injuries after late-night crash in Yorkshire

## A motorcyclist has suffered serious injuries after a crash on a residential Leeds street on Thursday (September 10).

A green Moto Madness pit bike had been driving up Newlay Lane in Bramley, towards the Ganners Mount junction with no lights on, when it collided with a black Vauxhall Insignia at around 10.15pm.

![The incident took place on Newlay Lane, Bramley, on Thursday night](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/12/12/11/Newlay-Lane-2.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



A man, aged 20, was arrested for possession of Class A drugs and driving under the influence but he has since been bailed pending further enquiries.

A spokesperson from West Yorkshire Police said: “Anyone who witnessed the collision, including anyone who may have dashcam or other video footage of the incident, is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police’s Leeds District police by calling 101 or online quoting reference 13260517191.

“Information can also be provided to independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.”
