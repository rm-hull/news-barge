A much-loved Darlington gift shop has relocated to the town centre.

Krazy Gifts has opened its doors at Unit 18 in the Cornmill Shopping Centre, relocating from its former Queen Street location and bringing its selection of cards, homeware and gift items to a new audience.

Tony Reale from Krazy Gifts said: "We are delighted to be opening in Cornmill Shopping Centre.

"This is an incredibly exciting new chapter for the business.

  Krazy Gifts has reopened at the Cornmill, Darlington


"We have some wonderful loyal customers who we cannot wait to see in our new store. Cornmill's location and strong mix of retailers make it the perfect place for us to continue growing our business."

The shop stocks a variety of products including greeting cards, home accessories and novelty items, with an emphasis on affordable, thoughtful and quirky gifts for all occasions.

  Krazy Gifts has reopened at the Cornmill, Darlington


Zoe France, centre manager at the Cornmill Shopping Centre, said: "Independent businesses play an important role in creating a diverse shopping experience at Cornmill.

"Krazy Gifts is a fantastic addition to our retail offering."

The arrival of Krazy Gifts supports the centre’s mix of independent and national retailers and follows the recent openings of Pepe’s Piri Piri and North West.
