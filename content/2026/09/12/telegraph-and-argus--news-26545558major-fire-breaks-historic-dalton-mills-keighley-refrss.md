Emergency services are at the scene of a major fire at Dalton Mills in Keighley this evening.

Reports of a blaze at the historic mill complex first emerged at around 8.30pm on Saturday night.

A T&A reporter at the scene has confirmed a significant response from all three emergency services, with multiple fire engines, police units and ambulances in attendance.

  The blaze at Dalton Mills 

*(Image: T&A)*

One eyewitness said: "I was driving down the A650 and I could just see all this smoke.

  "I pulled over to have a look and I couldn't believe the size of the fire.

  

  "It's unbelievable."

A Keighley resident said: "Very saddened and shocked to see another attack on the much prized heritage of the town.

"This site has given a lot to the film industry and consequently the country."

  The blaze at Dalton Mills 

*(Image: T&A)*

The cause of the fire is not yet known, and no further details have been released.

The T&A has approached West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, West Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service for comment.
