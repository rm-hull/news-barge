A bestselling author is set to visit the North East for a special literary event.

Ben Kane, author of the acclaimed Hannibal series, will give a talk in Hartlepool about A Land Aflame, the fourth instalment in the Hannibal series.

Mr Kane is the author of more than 20 historical adventure novels and is known for bringing dramatic moments from the past to life with vivid detail.

The event will take place on Tuesday, September 15 at 6.30pm at Seaton Carew Community Hub on Station Lane.

Councillor Rob Darby, Chair of Hartlepool Borough Council's Housing, Growth and Communities Committee, said: "We are delighted to welcome Ben Kane to Hartlepool.

"As one of the UK's leading historical fiction writers, he has built a loyal following through his gripping storytelling, and I'm sure his talk will be a fascinating and enjoyable evening for readers and history enthusiasts alike."

A Land Aflame is set in 211 BC as Hannibal’s struggle against the Romans in Italy continues.

The story follows characters Hanno and Aurelia as they flee to Spain after the Carthaginians' devastating defeat in Sicily, only to be shipwrecked and captured by local tribesmen.

The event was originally planned for June as part of the Crossing the Tees Book Festival but was postponed.
