## 'Selfless' chair of York charity branch honoured with top volunteer award

Tim was surrounded by his family and supporters from York's Parkinson's UK branch as he accepted his award *(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

A “selfless” volunteer and chair of the York branch of Parkinson’s UK has been honoured with a Community Pride Award.

Tim Bryan was presented with Volunteer of the Year at a glittering ceremony at York Racecourse on September 10 for the “extraordinary impact” he has had on the branch.

Speaking on the night, Tim said he wanted to dedicate the award to his “beautiful family and wife”, and all those who work tirelessly behind the scenes with the group.

  Tim dedicated his award to his "beautiful" wife and family, along with the volunteers from the Parkinson's UK branch 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

He said: “I’d just like to pay tribute to the resilience and the determination of all the people that we work with.

“A big thank you to everyone who dedicates their time to volunteer with us.”

  


In the five months since Tim was elected as chair, he has made a “real” difference to those living with Parkinson’s in the city, leading with compassion and using his dedication to the group to transform lives.

One of Tim’s standout achievements was organising a high-profile awareness and fundraising event at York Station on World Parkinson’s Day.

  Tim joined the other finalists at the ceremony at York Racecourse on September 10 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

The event involved more than 40 volunteers, live musical performances, and raised awareness on a large scale.

He has also helped secure inspiring guest speakers for the group’s monthly meetings, which now attract around 70 attendees.

These gatherings offer information, friendship, and emotional support to those affected by Parkinson’s, and Tim has also played a key role in setting up therapeutic classes designed to improve members’ wellbeing.

  Tim Bryan, chair of York's Parkinson's UK branch, took home Volunteer of the Year from the Community Pride Awards 

*(Image: Supplied)*

In addition to his volunteer work, Tim is a full-time care partner for his wife, Lynette, who also contributes to the group.

His nominator said: “Many people living with Parkinson’s now enjoy greater support, friendship and opportunities because of his efforts.

“Tim embodies the spirit of community pride, and I feel privileged to make this nomination for Volunteer of the Year.”
