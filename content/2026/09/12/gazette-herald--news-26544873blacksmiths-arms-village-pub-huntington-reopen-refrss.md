A pub in a York village will be reopening months after its former landlords announced they would be retiring.

The Blacksmiths Arms in Huntington is due to reopen after closing in March.

A new publican has taken over the venue with “lots planned” for the reopening, according to a post on the pub’s social media.

The Blacksmiths Arms is yet to announce the full details of who is taking over the venue, but a spokesperson for the pub said the team will be managed “by someone with an impressive 30 years of experience in the pub trade”.

“Babs and the team are so excited to take on this fantastic community pub and we’re looking forward to settling in, getting to know the locals and making the Blacksmiths Arms a place for the whole community to enjoy,” they said.

“We’ve got lots planned and there’s plenty happening behind the scenes as we get ready to welcome you through the doors!”

They added: “There’s plenty more to come – we can’t wait to welcome you!”

Local residents welcomed news that the Blacksmiths Arms will be reopening.

“Such good news, can’t wait to comeback. Can we buy season tickets?” one wrote on social media.

“It'll be good to have the local back,” added another.

The Blacksmiths Arms closed in March after Don and Ana Mattless retired from running the venue following 14 years behind the bar.

“What a beautiful run, 14 years,” they wrote on social media after retiring. “Thank you to all of our customers, new and old. It’s been a blast.”

  Don Mattless and the team at the Blacksmith Arms 

*(Image: Blacksmith Arms- Facebook)*

Owners the Stonegate Group advertised for a fixed-term tenancy to reopen the Blacksmiths Arms earlier this year with a guide rent of £894 a week and a forecast turnover per year of £449,799.

“The pub has a good reputation for food and a good range of regularly changing cask beers. It is well supported by the village and has a good range of both local and passing clientele. The pub has a good reputation for live music at weekends,” the listing said.

“Internally, it is very cosy with a separate dining area for 40 covers with a further 10 tables outside for customers who wish to dine/drink al fresco. The Blacksmiths also boast a large car park.”

It added: “The Blacksmiths would suit an operator who enjoys running a traditional village pub with a strong food offer and a real sense of community. This is a well-loved local with a loyal customer base, so someone who values consistency, quality, and personal service will thrive here.”
