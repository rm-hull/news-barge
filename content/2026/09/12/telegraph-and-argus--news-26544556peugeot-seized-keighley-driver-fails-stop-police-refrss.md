A driver who tried to evade police after being spotted speeding in Keighley was reported for four motoring offences after officers quickly boxed him in on a nearby road.

Officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team said they were just seconds away from the police station on Friday afternoon, September 11, when they saw the vehicle "trying to break the land speed record" near Alston Retail Park.

According to police, the driver initially attempted to make off from officers.

However, a second police vehicle quickly joined the pursuit and the driver stopped on Bradford Road.

Following checks, officers reported the driver for driving without insurance, driving otherwise than in accordance with a licence, driving without due care and attention, and failing to stop for police.

The Peugeot was seized by police.

In a social media post, officers said the driver "must have seen the blue light and quickly gave up" after finding two police cars behind him.

## Police statement in full

On social media on Friday, a representative for West Yorkshire Police said: "This afternoon, officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team were only 30 seconds away from the station when we spotted this Peugeot trying to break the land speed record coming off the roundabout towards Alston Retail Park.

"The driver initially fancied his chances at making off. However with a second police car also behind him in a matter of seconds, he must have seen the (blue) light and quickly gave up on Bradford Road.

"After some document checks the driver was reported for driving with no insurance, driving otherwise than in accordance with a licence, driving without due care & attention, and failing to stop for police.

"The Peugeot was seized by police."
