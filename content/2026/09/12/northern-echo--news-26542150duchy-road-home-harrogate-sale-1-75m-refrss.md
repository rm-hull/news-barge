THE Duchy estate, set in Harrogate’s conservation area, enjoys the feel of a more genteel bygone time thanks to its wide roads where the houses – many of which are late Victorian and early Edwardian – are set back behind well-kept gardens and screened by greenery to protect their privacy, and from where residents can enjoy a leisurely walk to the town centre, the shops of Coldbath Road and the area around the Pump Rooms and Montpelier where independent shops, art galleries, pubs and cafes await.

One such house is 76 Duchy Road, a substantial stone-built period home with four floors of spacious accommodation that has been beautifully, yet sympathetically, renovated and modernised to blend period charm with contemporary chic to create a home perfect for modern family living.

Its accommodation, which extends to approximately 3,250 sq. ft, retains much of the home’s period splendour throughout beautifully proportioned rooms flooded with light to create a feeling of space and grandeur and seamlessly blending architectural and decorative features into an altogether more modern setting.

A perfect example of this is the ground floor, which has been altered to create a wonderful open-plan family living space.

The property is entered via a covered porch which opens, through a door with leaded stained-glass windows above and alongside it, into an eye-catching reception hall with panelled walls and monochrome checked floor tiles which sets the tone for what lies beyond.

To the right of the hall is the drawing room centred around a decorative fireplace and with a ceiling rose and square bay window with stained glass.

To the left of the hall, running from the front of the house to the rear, is a large room divided into a study area and a sitting/music room, each of which has feature fireplaces, wall panelling, stained glass and leaded windows, and ornate ceiling plasterwork.

From the rear hall a staircase leads down to the almost entirely open-plan lower ground floor which is home to a more informal family living area.

This encompasses dining, seating and kitchen areas with herringbone flooring throughout.

Alongside the spacious seating area/snug is a dining area with panelled walls, a feature fireplace and a large built-in wine/drinks cooler.

At the heart of the spacious kitchen is a large central island breakfast bar which matches the comprehensive range of modern fitted units.

Bi-fold doors open from the kitchen directly to the level rear garden which is ideal for alfresco entertaining.

Completing the lower ground floor are a separate utility room and cloakroom.

To the first floor is a stylish family shower room and three double bedrooms, while upstairs again is a luxurious family bathroom with a freestanding contemporary slipper bath plus two further bedrooms.

The property is set back from the road behind double gates and a mature hedge.

Its south-facing lawned front garden is home to a paved patio area and a gated parking area.

To the rear of the property is a private sunny enclosed garden with raised borders, a paved patio and lawn.

Number 76 Duchy Road, Harrogate, is on the market at a guide price of £1.75m.
