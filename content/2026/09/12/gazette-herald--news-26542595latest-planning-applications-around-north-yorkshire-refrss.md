North Yorkshire Council has considered planning applications covering tree works, home extensions, garden buildings and a larger residential development.

The applications relate to properties and land in Settrington, Kirkbymoorside, Oswaldkirk, Helmsley, Bossall and Harome.

### Plans to reduce trees at Yew Tree House

An application covers work to one Common Yew tree and one Bird Cherry tree at Yew Tree House, Back Lane, Settrington, North Yorkshire, YO17 8NP.

The Yew tree would be reduced from 9m to 6m in height and from 8m to 5m in spread. The Bird Cherry tree would be reduced from 8m to 5m.

Ref. No: ZE26/00668/CAT | Received: August 3, 2026 | Validated: August 3, 2026 | Status: Decided

### Tree work proposed at Hawthorn Cottage

Work to one Copper Beech tree is covered by an application at Hawthorn Cottage, Tinley Garth, Kirkbymoorside, YO62 6AR.

The proposal is to raise the tree’s canopy and remove a number of dead limbs from the tree identified as T1.

Ref. No: ZE26/00649/CAT | Received: July 29, 2026 | Validated: July 29, 2026 | Status: Decided

### Application to remove two trees at Oswaldkirk Hall

An application has been made for the removal of one Copper Beech tree, identified as T3, and one Ash tree, identified as T4.

The site is Oswaldkirk Hall, Main Street, Oswaldkirk, YO62 5XT.

Ref. No: ZE26/00647/CAT | Received: July 29, 2026 | Validated: July 29, 2026 | Status: Decided

### Proposal for an enclosed porch in Helmsley

Plans for a single-storey, front enclosed porch have been approved at 5 Ashdale Road, Helmsley, North Yorkshire, YO62 5DE.

The application relates to the construction of the porch at the front of the property.

Ref. No: ZE26/00581/HOUSE | Received: July 9, 2026 | Validated: July 9, 2026 | Status: Approved

### Approved extension and garage plans at The Lodge

An application has been approved for the demolition of an outbuilding and the erection of a detached garage and garden room at The Lodge, Bossall, YO60 7NU.

The plans also include a part double-storey and part single-storey rear extension.

Ref. No: ZE26/00419/HOUSE | Received: May 11, 2026 | Validated: May 18, 2026 | Status: Approved

### Summer house planned for garden in Harome

Approval has been given for the installation of a summer house in the far corner of the garden at Mill Cottage, Mill Street, Harome, YO62 5JG.

The application is recorded as relating to listed building consent.

Ref. No: ZE26/00384/LBC | Received: April 29, 2026 | Validated: June 29, 2026 | Status: Approved

### Decision recorded for housing scheme in Kirkbymoorside

An application covers 61 dwellings at the Micro Metalsmiths Ltd site, Ings Lane, Kirkbymoorside, York, YO62 6DW.

The proposal comprises five five-bedroom homes, 15 four-bedroom homes, 20 three-bedroom homes, 11 two-bedroom homes and 10 one-bedroom homes, with garages, parking, public open space, a children’s play area, and the demolition of a single dwelling and industrial premises.

Ref. No: 07/00924/MFUL | Received: October 8, 2007 | Validated: November 22, 2007 | Status: Unknown
