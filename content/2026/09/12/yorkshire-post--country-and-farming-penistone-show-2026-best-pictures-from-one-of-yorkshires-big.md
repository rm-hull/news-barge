The Penistone Show is popular not just in Yorkshire, but draws people from across the UK – attracting around 20,000 every year.

Its 152nd edition, which took place on Saturday (Sep 13), was once again a celebration of all things rural, from farming to food.

As well as showcasing the best of Yorkshire farming, the show also gives the opportunity for some of the youngest farmers in the country to strut their stuff.
