**HARROGATE** — Professional tourism training is set to return to the region for the first time in more than a decade, as applications officially open for the prestigious Yorkshire Blue Badge Tourist Guide Training Course.

The qualification, widely recognised as the international gold standard within the professional guiding sector, has not been staged across the region since 2014. Organisers have launched a comprehensive recruitment drive looking for local history enthusiasts, passionate storytellers, and career-changers to train as flagship freelance ambassadors for the region’s massive visitor economy.

The intensive training framework actively encourages applicants from across the geographic footprint, with specific focus on attracting recruits from South and West Yorkshire alongside multi-lingual residents capable of welcoming an increasingly diverse international tourist base.

## Earning While Training via a Dual Badge Structure

The rigorous training schedule is slated to commence in **January 2027**, combining classroom-based historical research with extensive practical coach tours, walking masterclasses, and commercial business development modules. To help students build their businesses early, the curriculum is structured so that recruits will qualify for the localized** York Green Badge by summer 2027**, enabling them to earn freelance guiding income while finishing their master Yorkshire Blue Badge credentials.

Accepted students must commit to approximately 10 hours of independent private study each week in addition to mandatory weekend field training sessions, site inspections, and live assessments at regional landmarks.


**Sarah Cowling, Course Director and Blue Badge Tour Guide:**

Yorkshire has an extraordinary story to tell, but telling it well takes genuine craft. A great guide doesn’t just list facts – they make people laugh, see a familiar landscape differently, and give visitors a memory they’ll talk about for years. You don’t need a history degree to do this. We’re looking for personality, enthusiasm, and a deep love for the region. Being a Blue Badge Guide is one of the most varied jobs in the world – one day you’re taking a coach across the Dales, the next you’re leading a private tour through York Minster. We want this cohort to reflect Yorkshire itself: curious, full of history and welcoming.


## Accredited Professional Industry Foundations

The return of the course marks a significant collaboration within the national visitor economy. The training programme is delivered directly by the British Guild of Tourist Guides, the UK’s largest professional guide body tracing its roots back to the 1950 Festival of Britain, and remains fully accredited by the **Institute of Tourist Guiding**.

Prospective applicants do not require formal guiding experience or academic history degrees to qualify. Evaluation teams are prioritizing strong communications skills, clear public speaking aptitude, resilience, and an authentic commitment to showcasing regional heritage.

The formal application window remains open immediately and will close strictly on **Friday 9 October 2026**. Local residents looking to review enrollment terms, tuition frameworks, or submit formal entries are instructed to visit the official regional hub at** Yorkshire’s Best Guides**.
