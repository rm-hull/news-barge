Two men arrested after a haul of explosives 'used to make homemade fireworks' was found at a County __Durham__ home remain on police bail.

Sixty homes were evacuated and a 100-metre cordon was erected around Robert Terrace in Shield Row in Stanley on Tuesday (August 11) afternoon.

Two men, aged 71 and 46, were arrested on suspicion of manufacturing ammunition without a licence after a large quantity of potentially explosive substances was found.

The materials are believed to have been used to make homemade fireworks.

   

*(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

The men both remain on bail, Durham Police confirmed.

The incident on August 11 prompted the evacuation of around 60 homes.

Emergency services, including army ordnance experts, were called to the scene at around 1pm.
