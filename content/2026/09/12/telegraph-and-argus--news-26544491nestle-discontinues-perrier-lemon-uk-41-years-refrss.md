Nestle has discontinued a long-standing favourite drink in the UK after more than 40 years.

The food and drink company is well known for a range of products, including KitKat, Cheerios, Quality Street, and Nescafé coffee.

Nestle has discontinued a range of products in recent years, including:

- Curiously Cinnamon Churros Cereal
- Dark Mint KitKat
- Vegan KitKat
- Smarties Buttons
- Quality Street Collisions chocolate bar
- Breakaway and Yorkie biscuit bars


## Nestle discontinues popular drink after more than 40 years

Now, Nestle has confirmed it has axed another product from its UK range - Perrier Lemon.

Perrier first launched in the UK back in 1863, with the Lemon variation introduced globally in February 1985.

  Perrier Lemon was introduced in the UK back in February 1985. 

*(Image: Amazon)*

Shoppers have taken to social media, asking Nestle why they can't find Perrier Lemon in the UK.


One shopper, posting on X (formerly Twitter), asked: "@NestleCare I'm unable to find the original Perrier Lemon.

"Instead, the Maison Perrier line is stocked everywhere which definitely is a much lower quality product.

"Can you please advise if this is still being manufactured and if so, which retailers / suppliers can we get it from?"

Nestle replied, saying: "We understand it's disappointing when a favourite product is no longer available.

"Perrier Lemon has been discontinued in the UK."
