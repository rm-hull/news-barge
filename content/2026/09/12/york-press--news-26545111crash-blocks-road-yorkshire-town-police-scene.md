A crash has blocked a main road in North Yorkshire.

Police are on the scene on A170 in Stepney Hill, near Scarborough, on Saturday afternoon (September 12).

Officers are urging people to avoid the area.

“It is reported that the road is blocked. Please consider another route or prepare for delays in the area,” a North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said.

They said there is slow moving traffic around the top of Seamer Moor Lane and at the junction with Seamer Moor Hill.
