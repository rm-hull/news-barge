Emergency services have been called to an unattended bonfire in a North Yorkshire village.

Firefighters were called to the bonfire in Middleton, near Pickering, at 7.55pm on Friday (September 11).

The bonfire in Middleton Lane measured around 1m by 1m, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

A service spokesperson said the fire crew extinguished the bonfire and dampened down the area using one hose reel jet.

The incident came hours after another crew were called to a bonfire that the fire service said was “at risk of becoming out of control” in Little Studley, near Ripon.

Firefighters from Ripon extinguished the bonfire shortly after 5.51pm and “advice was given to a responsible person” on the scene, the fire service spokesperson said.
