Bratwurst, beer, and Black Forest gateau are on the menu as a venue in the North East introduces a taste of Bavaria.

Hardwick Hall Hotel near Sedgefield will host its own Oktoberfest celebration on Saturday, October 10, offering guests an evening of German food, drink, and entertainment.

The event will feature an oompah band, a beer steins drinking game, and a yodelling competition.

Helen Attley, sales and events manager at Hardwick Hall, said: "The hotel is renowned for its amazing themed events which are well attended year after year.

"Oktoberfest is no different – a night packed with superb entertainment which will really bring the spirit of this world-famous event to Hardwick."

Guests will be seated at traditional long tables and served a two-course German-inspired meal, including bratwurst with onions and sauerkraut, followed by Schwarzwalder Kirschtorte, also known as Black Forest gateau.

While fancy dress is not mandatory, there are special prizes on offer for those who decide to dress up.

The event will run from 7pm to 12.30am, and tickets are priced at £29.95 per person.

Oktoberfest is an internationally renowned celebration of German food, drink and music and draws thousands of people every year.

For more information or to book, email [events@hardwickhall.co.uk](mailto:events@hardwickhall.co.uk) or call 01740 620253.
