An abandoned Volkswagen linked to an earlier police incident was seized after alert residents spotted it hidden away in Oakworth.

Officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team were alerted on Friday evening, September 11, after members of the public raised concerns about a car abandoned in "suspicious circumstances".

Police said a man was seen quickly making off from the Volkswagen Passat after it was left at the scene.

The vehicle had been left with its windows open, and was also reported to smell strongly of cannabis.

When officers arrived at the scene, checks confirmed the Passat had failed to stop for police earlier that evening.

No further details have been made available.

## Police statement in full

On social media, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said:

  "Thanks to the vigilant residents of Oakworth, this evening officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team were made aware of a car being abandoned in suspicious circumstances.

  

  "This Volkswagen Passat was parked in a secluded location, with a male seen quickly making off from the vehicle. It was also described as smelling strongly of cannabis with the windows left open.

  

  "Upon our arrival the Passat was confirmed to be one that had made off from officers earlier in the evening, it has now been seized by police."
