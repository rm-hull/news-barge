A tiny tea garden on a working hill farm has become a favourite stop for walkers exploring one of North Yorkshire’s most remote corners.

Dale Head Farm Tea Garden sits at the head of Rosedale, overlooking the valley in the North York Moors National Park.

The secluded café has won a big following, with ratings of 4.8 on Tripadvisor and 4.7 on Google.

Its stunning setting is as much a draw as the food.

  Dale Head Farm Tea Garden


Dale Head is a working hill farm where visitors can enjoy tea, coffee or homemade cake in the garden overlooking Rosedale.

The owners even describe it as a "well-kept secret" and an "oasis on the moors" .

Walkers exploring the area repeatedly echo that description.

One visitor completing a circular walk from Rosedale Abbey described Dale Head as the "perfect halfway house", praising both the views across the valley and its cosy, rustic interior.

Another walker said an eight-mile route had stretched to nearly 10 miles when the tea room appeared halfway round.

They called it one of the great walkers’ tea rooms and praised its loose-leaf tea and homemade cakes.

Another visitor called Dale Head a "hidden pearl" after following a sign to refreshments and discovering the café’s spectacular setting.

Its homemade baking is a major draw.


The café makes its cakes in the farmhouse, while visitors regularly praise its cakes, scones and Yorkshire brack.

One recent customer praised the homemade lunches and cakes and said they time their walks around a stop at Dale Head.

There are outdoor tables for good weather and indoor seats when conditions on the moors turn nasty.

Dogs are welcome, making the café a handy stop for walkers exploring Rosedale.

Dale Head was highly commended in Yorkshire Life’s Best Teashop of the Year competition in 2017 and featured in Terry Wogan and Mason McQueen’s television tour of Britain.

Visitors wanting to stay longer can book the farm’s shepherd’s hut overlooking Rosedale.

The tea garden is at Dale Head Farm in Rosedale East, near Pickering.

The website says it opens from 11am to 3pm, Wednesday to Sunday, between June and September, with shorter Friday-to-Sunday hours in winter.

Visitors should check the café’s opening hours before travelling.
