SEVEN uninsured vehicles, including two quad bikes, were seized over one weekend by police.

West Yorkshire Police took action in Keighley, removing vehicles from the road.

Officers issued a reminder about insurance responsibilities.

West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team have just finished seizing this Toyota Landcruiser on Bradford Road, making it our seventh uninsured vehicle of the weekend.

"It is your responsibility to ensure your vehicle is insured, or you might also be watching it disappear on the back of a recovery truck."
