## Under the theme “The Future is Now”, IFA Berlin 2026 put the focus on technology designed for everyday life.

Thousands of brands brought their latest products and ideas to IFA Berlin 2026, one of the world’s biggest consumer electronics trade fairs. But one trend was stood out: smart devices, working together.

TCL placed that idea at the heart of its “AI-inspired Life” concept, combining AI, energy solutions and next-generation displays to create a more connected home. The idea is for different devices to work together and respond to the way people use them, instead of functioning as separate products.

Innovative gadgets that focused on mobility, health and wellbeing were also on display as well as humanoid robots that played football on the RoboCup competition.

IFA 2026 offered a snapshot of how AI and automation are already making life better and showed how fast innovations are reaching our homes.
