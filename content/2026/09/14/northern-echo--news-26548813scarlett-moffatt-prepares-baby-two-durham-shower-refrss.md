County Durham TV star Scarlett Moffatt has shared a glimpse inside her baby shower in Durham as she counts down to the arrival of her second child.

The Bishop Auckland-born former Gogglebox and I’m A Celebrity star celebrated with family and friends in Durham.


Scarlett, 35, called it her “one last big celebration” before welcoming her baby with partner Scott Dobinson.

She looked stunning as she wore her hair down, a patterned dress, and brown suede heels.

Taking to her Instagram page, the TV personality said: "Baby in Bloom. One last big celebration before our littlest love arrives.

"My heart (and belly) are VERY full."

  Scarlett with her close friend, drag queen Miss Tesstickle. 

*(Image: SCARLETT MOFFATT AND TESS TICKLE)*

The pastel 'Baby in Bloom' shower featured flowers and a balloon display by Balloons by Molly.

Scarlett’s mum Betty and her friend Miss Tesstickle organised the shower.

Scarlett said: "Thank you to my mam and bestie @bettymoffatt @miss_tesstickle for organising it all - especially getting up at the crack of dawn to make finger sandwiches.

"To everybody who came we love you so much, honestly what a giggle I've had."

The celebration came in the final weeks before Scarlett and Scott welcome their second child.

The couple's first son, June, was born in 2023 - four years after the pair started dating.
