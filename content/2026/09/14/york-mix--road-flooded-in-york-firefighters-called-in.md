Fire crews were called in after a road flooded in York this morning (Monday).

They went to the incident on **Haxby Road** at**3.23am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews diverted water away from some affected properties using lengths of hose and waited until the water had decreased to an acceptable level.

#### Haxby Road, York

“The incident was left with Yorkshire Water informed of burst pipe in the area.”

Writing on Facebook, one resident said this morning: “Water leak on Haxby Road overnight, same place multiple times earlier this year.

“Expect that to be closed later today for repairs, which will contribute to the ever increasing traffic nightmare that is York.”

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson said: “We’re currently repairing a burst main on Haxby Road in York and doing so as quickly as we can.

“We apologise for the inconvenience and thank everyone in the area for their patience.”
