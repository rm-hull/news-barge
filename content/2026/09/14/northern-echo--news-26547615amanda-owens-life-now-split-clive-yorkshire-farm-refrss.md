Amanda Owen became one of Britain’s best-known farmers when millions watched her extraordinary family life on Our Yorkshire Farm.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess, her former husband Clive and their nine children became household names as cameras followed life at remote Ravenseat Farm in Swaledale.

However, much has changed since the Channel 5 programme came to an end.

Amanda and Clive announced their separation in June 2022 after 22 years of marriage, but said they would continue farming and raising their family together.

Four years on, Amanda remains in the Yorkshire Dales, back on television with Clive and their children while building a career beyond the farm.

So what is Amanda Owen doing now?

## Amanda Owen’s life after splitting from Clive

Amanda and Clive confirmed their separation in June 2022 after describing their marriage as going through a “rocky patch”.

At the time, Amanda said the pair believed separating was the right choice for their family.

Although no longer a couple, they said they would continue working together at Ravenseat and co-parenting their nine children.

Amanda said farm life had to continue, with children to care for and animals needing constant attention.

She told ITV’s Lorraine in September 2022 that the separation had been difficult, but added: “Life carries on on the farm.”


Clive later publicly took responsibility for his part in the breakdown of the marriage.

Appearing on Lorraine in 2023, he said he struggled to cope with the dramatic changes brought by Our Yorkshire Farm’s success and should have supported Amanda more.

But the former couple have maintained an unusually close working relationship.

That relationship has become central to their return to television.

## Amanda and Clive return to television together

Our Yorkshire Farm ended in 2022, but the Owen family’s television story continued.

Amanda, Clive and their children returned in More4's Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids.

Instead of focusing solely on life at Ravenseat, the programme follows one of the family's biggest projects yet – bringing a centuries-old derelict farmhouse known as Anty John's back to life.

Amanda and Clive bought the property in 2020 and began restoring it with help from their children and local tradespeople.

The project has continued despite their separation.

Channel 4 said the farmhouse was bought for the family’s future, while the programme charts its painstaking transformation into a modern home.

The programme has become a success in its own right.

Channel 4 commissioned two more series in August 2025, following the Owens as they moved from restoring Anty John’s exterior to tackling its vast interior.

The work includes planning the rooms and installing plumbing and running water.

A behind-the-scenes event in Northallerton on September 30, 2026, will include an exclusive preview of the new series, organisers say.

## Does Amanda Owen still live at Ravenseat?

Ravenseat remains at the centre of the Owen family's life.

The remote 2,000-acre hill farm in Upper Swaledale featured in Our Yorkshire Farm, where Amanda and Clive raised their nine children: Raven, Reuben, Miles, Edith, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy.

Amanda has continued farming while working on Anty John's nearby.

Speaking in 2025, she said raising her children in rural Yorkshire gave them freedom while teaching them independence, common sense and a strong work ethic.

She described the Anty John’s renovation as “all-consuming”, saying its scale could make progress feel slow.

Several of the older Owen children have increasingly built lives and careers of their own.

Raven left home for university and pursued a career beyond farming, while Reuben built a contracting business and television career.

The changing lives of the nine children have increasingly become part of the family's story as the younger siblings grow up at Ravenseat.

## Amanda Owen’s career beyond Our Yorkshire Farm

Television is now only one part of Amanda's work.

She has built a career as an author and public speaker alongside farming.

Her latest book, Cosy Bedtime Stories from the Farm, was published by Puffin on September 3, 2026.

The children's book contains ten stories inspired by life on a working farm, covering everything from wildlife and camping to sheepdogs and evenings beside the fire.

Amanda has written several books about her experiences in the Yorkshire Dales, and her publisher describes her as a bestselling author, shepherdess and TV personality.

It is another step in a career that began far from television.

Born in Huddersfield, Amanda became a first-generation farmer before eventually arriving at Ravenseat, where she met Clive and went on to raise their large family.

## What is Amanda Owen doing now in 2026?

Amanda’s life looks very different from the early days of Our Yorkshire Farm, but the ingredients that made it popular remain.

She continues to farm in Swaledale, raise her family and work alongside Clive despite their marriage ending.

She is also transforming Anty John’s, filming Our Farm Next Door, writing books and making public appearances.

And there is plenty more of the Owen family’s story to come.

With series four of Our Farm Next Door returning to More4, viewers can once again see life for the family years after Our Yorkshire Farm made them famous.
