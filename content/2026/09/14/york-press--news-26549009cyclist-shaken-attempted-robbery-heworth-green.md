A cyclist has been left “shaken” after two men tried to rob him in York, police said.

Officers are appealing for information following the incident in Heworth Green, near the junction with Eboracum Way, at 10.35am on Monday, September 7.

The cyclist, a man in his 60s, was heading away from the city centre when two men attempted to steal his road bike, North Yorkshire Police said.

A police spokesperson said the victim “refused to give them the bike and they went away”.

The victim was left uninjured apart from a small graze to a leg but was “left shaken by the incident”, the police spokesperson said.

They said the suspect who grabbed the man’s bicycle is described as white with tanned skin, stubbled face, dark hair, aged late 20s or early 30s, about 5ft 11in tall, and he was wearing a grey two-tone shell suit tracksuit.

The suspect also had “noticeable fresh grazes on his knuckles which looked like he had hit them on a wall", the police spokesperson said.

They urged anyone with information about the attempted robbery to email [Toby.Spencer@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Toby.Spencer@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260175505 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
