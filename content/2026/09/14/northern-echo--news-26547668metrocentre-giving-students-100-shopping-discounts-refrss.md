The Metrocentre is hosting an evening of huge discounts for students in days.

The Big Student Shop, described as the largest event of its kind in the North East, will take place on Thursday, September 24, from 4pm to 9pm.

It promises a night of exclusive offers, giveaways and entertainment to help students start the academic year with a bargain.

Helen Atkinson, marketing manager at Metrocentre, said: "Without doubt, the Big Student Shop is one of the highlights on Metrocentre’s events calendar, offering freshers and continuing students alike all manner of ways to pick up a bargain, helping get the new academic year off to the best possible start."

"Whether it’s serious savings on big brand fashion, free giveaways, money off eating out, or simply a fun night with your student pals, Metrocentre’s Big Student Shop has you covered," she added.

Participating retailers at the centre will be offering student-only savings on a variety of items, just weeks after the venue launched its new independent shopping village, 'The Crescent'.

Town Square will feature pop-up stalls from major brands and indie labels, including Schuh, The Perfume Shop and Nando’s.


Nearby Exhibition Square will host interactive games such as mega beer pong and a human fruit machine, with prizes including a year’s supply of Millie’s Cookies, milkshakes from Five Guys, and gift cards for retailers like Greggs.

Entertainment will include live DJ sets in Red Mall, Green Mall, Town Square and Exhibition Square.

Students can also enjoy free popcorn and take part in bracelet-making sessions with Sostrene Grene.

A variety of food and drink offers will be available from outlets including Frankie & Benny’s, Pizza Express, Yo! Sushi and Wingstop.

To access the discounts and offers, students must register for a Big Student Shop wristband either in advance or on the night with valid student ID.

All registered students will be entered into a prize draw to win a Metrocentre gift card worth £500.
