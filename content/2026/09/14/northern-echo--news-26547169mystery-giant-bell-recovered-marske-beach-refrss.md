A giant bell hauled on to an East Cleveland beach has left locals asking where it came from and how it ended up underwater.

Online pictures show divers in Marske hauling the large metal bell across the sand with ropes and specialist equipment.

  A giant bell was recovered by divers in Marske over the weekend. 

*(Image: Donna Louise/FACEBOOK)*

The bell's origins remain a mystery for now, but locals already have plenty of theories about how it ended up offshore.

A social media user who claimed to witness the recovery said local divers brought the bell onto the beach at low tide.

They wrote: "It was recovered by divers using air lift bags."

Another local, who said they had spoken to those involved, suggested the bell came from a wreck known about for years off the Teesside coast.

They said: "It was recovered from a wreck that was 3.5 miles offshore, according to the group. They didn't know the name of the wreck, it's been known to be there for years. They said it could be from a buoy."

Another commenter backed the theory, suggesting the bell was once part of a North Sea navigation marker.

They said: "The top half of the Longscar buoy, along with its bell about that size, was lost about 15 years ago."

  A giant bell was recovered by divers in Marske over the weekend. 

*(Image: Donna Louise/FACEBOOK)*

But others linked the find to a more recent mystery on land, with several people asking whether it could be the bell that was stolen from Peterlee Cemetery earlier this year.

One commenter asked: "Not the bell stolen from Peterlee graveyard is it?"

The bell at Peterlee Cemetery disappeared in February 2026, although there is currently no evidence linking that theft to the latest discovery, and any theories remain speculative.

While the bell appears to show signs of having spent some time underwater, uncertainty around its age, origin and the conditions it has been exposed to means that nothing can yet be confirmed.

The Northern Echo has contacted those involved in the recovery for more information about how the bell was found, who brought it ashore and where it may have come from.
