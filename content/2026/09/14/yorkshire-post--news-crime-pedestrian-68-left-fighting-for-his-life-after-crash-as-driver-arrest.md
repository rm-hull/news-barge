# Pedestrian, 68, left fighting for his life after crash as driver arrested

## A 68-year-old pedestrian has suffered life-threatening injuries following a crash with a car in Keighley.

At around 6.29pm on Sunday (Sep 13), emergency services were called to Sand Street in Keighley following reports of a crash involving a car and a pedestrian.

A 68-year-old man was taken to Leeds General Infirmary, where he is being treated for life-threatening injuries.

![Sand Street, Keighley](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/14/17/02/Sand-Street-Keighley.png?trim=0,23,0,23&crop=&width=640&quality=65)



A 57-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury through careless driving and enquiries remain ongoing.

Police have launched an appeal for anyone with information or any witnesses to come forward.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Anyone who witnessed the collision, including anyone who may have dashcam or other footage of the incident, is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 13260522175.”
