A man has been arrested on suspicion of attempted arson after reports petrol was poured over a vehicle.

Cleveland Police said they were called to St Johns Close in Stockton at around 5pm on Sunday, September 13.

Officers arrested a 54-year-old man at the scene on suspicion of attempted arson, possessing a class A drug and driving without a licence or insurance.

He remains in police custody.

Chief Superintendent Rachel Stockdale, from Cleveland Police, said: “At 5pm on Sunday, September 13, police were called to St Johns Close in Stockton following reports that petrol had been poured onto a vehicle and could be set alight.
