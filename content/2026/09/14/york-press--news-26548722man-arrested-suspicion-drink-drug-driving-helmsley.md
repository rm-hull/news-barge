A 41-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of drink and drug driving after a crash in Helmsley.

The collision happened at 12.55pm on Saturday, September 12, on the B1257 outside The Feversham Arms and involved a bronze Mitsubishi, which collided with two parked cars.

RECOMMENDED READING:

The driver, a 41-year-old local man, was arrested following the collision and taken to hospital by ambulance for assessment of his injuries.

The road was closed for a short time to allow for investigation and vehicle recovery.

North Yorkshire Police are appealing for witnesses, including anyone who saw the vehicle before the collision or who has relevant dashcam footage.

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC 1142 at [Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk) or use the live chat service on the North Yorkshire Police website.

The reference number for the incident is 12260179180.
