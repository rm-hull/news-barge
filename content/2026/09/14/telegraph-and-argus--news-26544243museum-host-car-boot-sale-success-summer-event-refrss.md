BRADFORD Industrial Museum will host a car boot sale later this month, after the success of a similar event earlier this year.

The iconic Victorian mill site in Eccleshill will host the event on September 26 from 8am until noon.

Pitch fees are £10 per car and £20 for a Transit van or larger vehicle. Gates open from 7am for sellers.

Entry for shoppers is free.

After the sale the museum will be open from 11am–4pm for visitors wishing to explore the exhibitions.

To book a pitch or find out more, email [industrial.museum@bradford.gov.uk](mailto:industrial.museum@bradford.gov.uk) or call 01274 435 900.

There will be no on-site public parking, so visitors are encouraged to plan their journey in advance and use alternative off-site parking.
