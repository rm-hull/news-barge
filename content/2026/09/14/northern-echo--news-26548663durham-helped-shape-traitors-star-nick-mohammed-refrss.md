Nick Mohammed is now known around the world as the Emmy-nominated star of Ted Lasso, but before television success and sold-out comedy tours, he spent some time in the North East.

The actor, comedian and writer spent four years at Durham University between 1999 and 2003, graduating with a degree in geophysics while immersing himself in student life at St Aidan’s College.

He was involved in music, theatre and even performed magic shows during his time in the city, long before becoming a household name - and is not the only celebrity to study at the university.

Born in Leeds in 1980, Mohammed chose Durham over an offer from Cambridge and went on to study geophysics, becoming a member of St Aidan’s College and playing violin in the university orchestra.

For Mohammed, the city became the backdrop to a formative chapter of his life.

   

*(Image: PA)*

## Where Nick Mohammed found his creative spark

Although comedy would eventually make him famous, Durham University says it was music, theatre and magic that occupied much of Mohammed's time as a student.

He performed with the Durham University Orchestral Society, conducted the Hill Orchestra and appeared in student theatre productions.

One of the more surprising details from his university years is that he unsuccessfully auditioned for the Durham Revue comedy group twice.

Durham University notes that the rejection did little to slow his progress, with Mohammed later becoming one of Britain's most successful comedy performers.

He also developed a reputation as a talented magician, regularly performing at student events and college balls while balancing his studies.

After graduating from Durham in 2003, Mohammed began a PhD in seismology at Cambridge but eventually left academia behind to pursue comedy full-time.

   

*(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

## From Durham graduate to Emmy nominee

Mohammed's breakthrough came through a series of comedy and acting roles, including his popular alter ego Mr Swallow, before wider audiences discovered him as Nate Shelley in Apple TV hit Ted Lasso.

The role earned him Emmy nominations and international recognition.

His career has since spanned television, film, radio and live performance, including creating and starring in the comedy series Intelligence, appearing in Chicken Run: Dawn of the Nugget, Bridget Jones's Baby and Celebrity Traitors.

That was where a geophysics student quietly built the creative foundations for an unlikely journey to global stardom.
