**Dominic Calvert-Lewin wreaked havoc asLeeds Unitedmoved up to third place in the Premier League table followinga 4-1 win against Newcastle Unitedat Elland Road.**

Here's how I rated the players and you can see how you scored them below.

## Leeds United

**James Trafford:** A relatively comfortable night against his former suitors. A couple of nervy clearances early on but went on to make an important save to deny Harvey Barnes.**7James Justin:** Teasing cross set up Leeds' second goal. Has become a key component in this system.**7Nico Elvedi:** Only his second Premier League start yet already looks at home at Elland Road. Solid at the back but got it all wrong at the other end after volleying wide at the back post when unmarked.**7Tarik Muharemovic:** The club's player of the month for August for a reason. Made it look rather comfortable at the back on Monday night.**7Jayden Bogle:** Second goal credited to him. Did not know much about it, but has found his shooting boots after his goal at Brighton last time out in the league. Twice forced Lukas Hornicek into big saves before second goal took final touch off him. A real threat.**8Anton Stach:** Won a corner inside 32 seconds. That set the tone for Leeds' relentless display, even if he later wasted chance to score.**7Ethan Ampadu:** Ensured Leeds kept pushing for more as he barked instructions. Saw Dominic Calvert-Lewin inadvertently block a vicious goalbound effort**. 7Ao Tanaka:** His goal took two heavy deflections but had the confidence to take the shot. Looking back to his best in recent weeks even if he was relieved to see one misplaced pass unpunished.**7Gabriel Gudmundsson:** Caused Newcastle real problems with his runs down the left. Looked more a winger than a wing-back.**7Noah Okafor:** Fine strike for Leeds' fourth, having also coolly pulled the ball back for Tanaka to let fly for his side's opener.**8Dominic Calvert-Lewin:** Just loves this fixture. A trademark header came off Bogle for Leeds' second and then showed other side of his game to finish calmly on the floor for the hosts' third. Has scored eight goals against Newcastle - more than any other side.**9Subs:Melvin Bard (on 72, Gudmundsson):** Perfect environment to make his Premier League debut.**6Brenden Aaronson (on 72, Tanaka):** Lots of energy.**6Harry Wilson (on 72, Okafor):** Eager to impress.**6Lukas Nmecha (on 78, Calvert-Lewin): 6Daniel James (on 83, Bogle)Sean Longstaff (on 88, Stach)Subs not used:** Zetterer, Bijol, Bahoya, Nmecha

## Newcastle United

**Lukas Hornicek:** This would have been even uglier for the visitors were it not for Hornicek making fine saves to twice deny Bogle and also to foil Calvert-Lewin.**6Amar Dedic:** Shook off hamstring issue to start, but a tough night as Leeds repeatedly targeted his flank**. 4Malick Thiaw:** Captain for night but this was far from a captain's performance. Beaten easily by Calvert-Lewin for Leeds' second goal, slapped turf in frustration after the striker fired past him for the third and then failed to block Okafor's fourth.**3Sven Botman:** Like Thiaw, could not cope with Calvert-Lewin and was skinned so easily for Leeds' second goal.**3Lewis Hall:** Dragged out of position in build-up of Leeds' second goal. Not the night he had in mind after signing a new deal.**4Nico Gonzalez:** Belatedly came off after just 19 minutes after the ball struck him in the head in the early stages.**5Lewis Miley:** Little he could do for deflection for Tanaka's goal, but was overrawed by Leeds' tenacious midfield. Had little time to find passing rhythm.**4Jacob Murphy:** Continued in injured Anthony Elanga's absence, but did not offer enough protection and caused Leeds few problems going the other way.**3Yoane Wissa:** Created Newcastle's best chance and at least battled and chased, even if his final ball was not always great.**5Harvey Barnes:** Has a fearsome record against Leeds and eyes lit up when he danced inside the box only for Trafford to deny him.**5Matias Fernandez-Pardo:** Full debut following his £51.4m move from Lille. Had little to feed off as Newcastle repeatedly played long balls forward.**4Subs:Sean Steur (on 19, Gonzalez):** Thrown into the cauldron after Gonzalez went off.
