**HARROGATE** — The highly acclaimed Cuore Chamber Orchestra has confirmed its return to its resident home venue this autumn, launching a high-profile second season following a celebrated inaugural year.

The elite regional ensemble will take the stage at **Harrogate’s Royal Hall** on**Friday 6 November 2026** for the opening performance of their 2026/27 concert series. The launch follows a successful debut year that saw the professional chamber collective draw widespread critical praise across five major performances in three of Yorkshire’s most iconic heritage venues.

Founded by Artistic Director and Conductor **William Dutton**—a former BBC Young Musician of the Year Strings Finalist and alumnus of the prestigious Italian Conducting Academy in Milan—the orchestra has rapidly established a reputation for technical precision and vibrant programming choices designed to champion both classical masterpieces and 21st-century compositions.

## International Soloist and a Regional Premiere

The season opener features a headline appearance by one of Britain’s foremost classical instrumentalists, violinist **Jennifer Pike**. Renowned globally for her exceptional depth and technical integrity, Pike first rose to international prominence in 2002 when, at just 12 years old, she became the youngest ever winner of the BBC Young Musician of the Year accolade.

Alongside standard repertoire pieces by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart and Edvard Grieg, the performance will showcase the official Yorkshire premiere of *‘Meditations’ for Strings*, composed by celebrated BBC Radio 3* Private Passions*broadcaster**Michael Berkeley**.


**Composer Michael Berkeley:**

Meditations’ was the first of my pieces to gain any real attention and audiences seem to respond to its emotional directness. I’m delighted that Cuore, who have done so much to promote orchestral music in Yorkshire and in particular the work of living or 21st-century composers, will be playing ‘Meditations’ under their inspiring young conductor William Dutton.



## Concert Logistics, Timings, and Corporate Contact

The professional ensemble brings together premier young musical talent from across the United Kingdom, specifically aiming to revitalize the live orchestral concert experience within the heart of the North Yorkshire cultural sector.

- **Event Name:** Cuore Chamber Orchestra: 2026/27 Season Opening Concert
- **Date:** Friday 6 November 2026
- **Venue:** The Royal Hall, Ripon Road, Harrogate, HG1 2RD
- **Featured Soloists:** Jennifer Pike (Violin) | Directed by William Dutton
- **Repertoire:** Works by Mozart, Grieg, and the Yorkshire premiere of Michael Berkeley’s ‘Meditations’
- **Booking:** Advanced reserved seating is required. Tickets can be booked directly via the official platform at Cuore Chamber Orchestra.
