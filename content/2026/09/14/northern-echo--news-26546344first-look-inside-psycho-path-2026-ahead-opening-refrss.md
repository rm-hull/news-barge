Get ready for spine-tingling scares and “more terror than ever before” - a County Durham horror maze is set to open in just weeks.

The award-winning Psycho Path, at Lintz Hall Farm in Burnopfield, will be back for another year of ghostly thrills on September 26.

The event, which will run across multiple weekends until November 5 and has already seen surging ticket sales, is this year unveiling a new site map.

For the first time ever, visitors will be able to enjoy a VR experience and Flight Path 666, a terrifying experience set inside a real passenger plane.

Existing attractions such as Vandalised, Dolls House, and IScream have been extended and added to while a new bar, Bite Club VIP, will also be open.

Bosses say the experience has been tweaked to such a high level that even the most dedicated fans will feel like it's a first time visit.

Christiano Crawford, one of the co-directors of Psycho Path, warned that one of the most popular scares is now “one of the most terrifying experiences anyone can have.”

“We have changed the scares that lurk behind three of the doors of Isolation,” he said.
