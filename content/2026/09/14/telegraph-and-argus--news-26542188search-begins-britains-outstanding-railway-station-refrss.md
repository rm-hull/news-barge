A NATIONAL competition is on the hunt for Britain’s most outstanding railway stations.

Nominations open on Monday, September 14 for the 2026 World Cup of Stations, which celebrates the positive impact stations have on their communities.

The public is invited to nominate stations that make a meaningful difference, whether through community events, business support, or simply being a welcoming space.

Dan Mann, co-CEO of Rail Delivery Group, said: "Every station has its own story and plays a different role in bringing together people and experiences.

"This year’s World Cup of Stations is about celebrating those stories and recognising the stations that make a real difference to the people who use them.

"As we move towards a simpler, more joined-up railway through Great British Railways, it also reflects the role our stations play in creating one railway that connects us all."

Last year’s winner was Ashington station on the Northumberland Line.

Paul Henry, programme manager for the Northumberland Line, said: "We were delighted when Ashington was crowned the winner of last year’s World Cup of Stations competition, following a swell of support from thousands of local people.

"It shows the station has had a really positive impact on their lives."
