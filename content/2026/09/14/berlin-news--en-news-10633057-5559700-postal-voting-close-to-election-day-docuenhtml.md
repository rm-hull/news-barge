This applies in all cases to those who have not yet applied for postal voting documents, including the ballot paper. There is no central office for this across Berlin. The districts are responsible – the relevant district electoral office issues the postal voting documents. The addresses can be found on the State Election Director’s website and on the websites of the district offices. Applications can be submitted, for example, by post, online via the State Election Director’s website, by email or in person at a postal voting office in the district. There is also a postal voting application form on the back of the polling card – the postal voting documents can be requested using a QR code. The online and QR code options are only available until Tuesday (15 September).
