Do you recognise this man?

Police want to talk to him in connection with a fraud in York.

It happened on Monday, 29 June at the Tesco supermarket, Askham Bar.

#### Tesco at Askham Bar

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’ve issued this CCTV of a person that we would like to speak to, following a fraud in York.

“A mobile phone was obtained fraudulently.

“Please contact us if you recognise the person pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.”
