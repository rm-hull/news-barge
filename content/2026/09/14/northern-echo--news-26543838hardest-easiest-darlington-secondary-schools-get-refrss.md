Data has revealed the hardest and easiest secondary schools to get into around Darlington.

The Great British Schools Guide has put together data using “an equal-weighted combination of catchment tightness and fullness relative to recorded capacity (each measured by percentile rank)”.

The fullness of each school provides an indication of the demand for a place and is not a direct measure of admissions difficulty.

Here are 7 hardest and easiest secondary schools to get into around Darlington.

  Only 7 Darlington secondary schools matched the requirements. 

*(Image: Getty)*

## Hardest Darlington secondary schools to get into

The data does not include independent schools, grammar schools, and other selective schools, leaving only seven secondary schools in Darlington eligible for the lists.

Haughton Academy was named as the hardest secondary school in Darlington to get into with a fullness percentage of 99%.

The school was last inspected by OFSTED in May of this year and received a rating of exceptional standard in multiple categories.

The school wants to “provide the best possible educational opportunities and success” for its pupils.

Haughton Academy provides a “positive culture for learning” and helps to develop students through “praise and encouragement”.

Top 7 hardest Darlington secondary schools to get into:

1. Haughton Academy – 99%
2. Hummersknott Academy – 103%
3. St Aidan’s Church of England Academy – 85%
4. Wyvern Academy – 56%
5. Hurworth School – 102%
6. Polam Hall School – 95%
7. Longfield Academy – 80%


### 
  **Easiest Darlington secondary schools to get into**

Hurworth School came out in first place as the easiest secondary school to get into with a fullness of 102%.

The school was last inspected by OFSTED in September 2022 and achieved a rating of good in all of its categories.

The school says its core values allow students “to be responsible and resilient lifelong learners who strive for excellence by developing positive relationships and learning to respect themselves and those around them”.

Top 7 easiest Darlington secondary schools to get into:

1. Hurworth School – 102%
2. Polam Hall School – 95%
3. Longfield Academy – 80%
4. St Aidan’s Church of England Academy – 85%
5. Wyvern Academy – 56%
6. Hummersknott Academy – 103%
7. Haughton Academy – 99%
