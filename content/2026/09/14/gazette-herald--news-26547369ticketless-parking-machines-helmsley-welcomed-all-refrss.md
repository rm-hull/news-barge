A NORTH Yorkshire councillor has said the new parking machines being installed across the county have had a positive impact

Cllr George Jabbour, who represents Helmsley, said a few weeks after the introduction of new ticketless car parking machines in the town, both visitors and businesses have praised the improvements they bring.

He said the previous parking machines had become increasingly outdated and were less intuitive for customers to use, which could sometimes lead to delays when making payment.

Cllr Jabbour said: "Furthermore, because the old parking machines issued tickets, a visitor had to walk back to their car after collecting the paper receipt to display it inside their vehicle.

"This is no longer necessary with the new ticketless machines as the driver only needs to enter their vehicle registration number and make a payment by cash or card."

Civil Enforcement Officers can then use handheld devices to check that the appropriate parking tariff has been paid against the vehicle registration number.

Cllr Jabbour, said: “I regularly saw the queues that used to form next to the old car parking machines in the Market Place in Helmsley.

“Visitors repeatedly complained about how unreliable and difficult to use the old machines were. The feedback was communicated to the Council.

“Therefore, I am pleased that the reaction from both customers and businesses to the new ticketless machines that North Yorkshire Council installed last month has been positive.

“An extra bonus is that drivers now don’t have to go back to their vehicles to display a car parking receipt. Instead, they can spend more time enjoying our beautiful town.

"The new machines allow customers to pay by cash or card and eliminate the need for paper tickets, reducing waste while making parking simpler and more convenient."
