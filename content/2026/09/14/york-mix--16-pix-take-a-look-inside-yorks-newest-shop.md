Fancy a wee dram in York? Get yourself to Royal Mile Whiskies, now open in the city centre.

We reported earlier this year that a Royal Mile Whiskies outlet was set to open in one of York’s key shopping streets – and it has opened its doors for all the whisky lovers out there.

***YorkMix*** spoke with James Charlton, assistant manager, to find out more about the opening.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/royal-mile-whiskies-coney-street-16.webp)

Royal Mile Whiskies are retailers of whisky who specialise in different types of Scotch, Irish whiskey, Japanese whisky, and more.

It opened its flagship England store earlier this month, and the first week has been going well.

“It’s been really good so far,” said James. “We’ve got an amazing range – we’re like kids in a sweet shop in that regard!

“We want to be part of the community, and we’re passionate about these spirits, and we want to see what we can do with local companies, as well as try and do our own events in the city, like we’re already doing in Edinburgh.

“The main thing is we just want to be serving people. It’s not corporate – it’s giving a bit of passion and trying to make someone’s day.”

Owned by the Scottish company the Dormant Distillery Company, Royal Mile Whiskies launched in 1991 with the opening of a shop in the street of the same name in Edinburgh’s old town.

It launched a website in 2000 which has gone on to sell whiskies to customers globally and also has a store in St Andrews.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/royal-mile-whiskies-coney-street-13.webp)

The Dormant Distillery Company also owns the Cigarbox store in Edinburgh and the Drinkmonger shops, also in the Scottish capital and Pitlochry.

Some of the events Royal Mile Whiskies are planning to hold are tasting events, and Whisky and Cigar pairings.

Inside the shop, the walls are stocked floor to ceiling with whisky.

“Most of what we do is Scotch, with the company being largely based there, said James.

“But we do have a decent range of Irish whiskies and Japanese from fairly affordable to more expensive collectible stuff.

“We’re also looking particularly at a couple of distilleries in the Yorkshire area. There’s a couple of other names I won’t mention just yet – but we do already have Filey Bay, which obviously is Spirit of Yorkshire Distilleries.

“We’re looking to get a few other products like beers and other whiskeys or local spirits from the area.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/royal-mile-whiskies-coney-street-6.webp)

11 Coney Street was previously home to the stationer Smiggle, which closed last June after the landlord’s decision not to renew the store’s lease.

Royal Mile Whiskies will be open on Coney Street seven days a week, 10am to 8pm. Find out more more at their website here.

I think we’ve noticed people, as well just walking past, are coming in to check us out,” said James. “We’ve started to see people deliberately coming to us and making a deliberate visit to us, which is great.”
