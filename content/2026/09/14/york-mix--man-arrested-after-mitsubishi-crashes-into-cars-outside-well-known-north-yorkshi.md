A man has been arrested after a crash near a well-known North Yorkshire pub.

A bronze-coloured Mitsubishi collided with two parked cars on the B1257 outside The Feversham Arms in Helmsley.

It happened at 12.55pm on Saturday (12 September).

#### Incident location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The driver of the Mitsubishi, a 41-year-old local man, was arrested following the collision on suspicious of drink and drug driving.

“He was taken to hospital by ambulance to have his injuries assessed.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/feversham-arms-b1257-high-street-helmsley-gsv.webp)

“The road was closed for a short period while we investigated the scene and to allow the vehicle involved to be recovered.

“We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicle prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us.”
