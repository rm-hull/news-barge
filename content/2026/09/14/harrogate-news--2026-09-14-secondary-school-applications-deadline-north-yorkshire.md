**HARROGATE** — Council education chiefs have issued an urgent advisory to parents and carers across the Harrogate district, urging them to complete secondary school placement applications well ahead of a strict autumn deadline.

The secondary school application portal formally opened on **Saturday 12 September 2026** for all children currently in Year 6 who were born between 1 September 2015 and 31 August 2016. Families looking to secure positions for the 2027 intake have until the hard closing date of**Saturday 31 October 2026** to submit both online and paper submissions to the local authority.

Under the North Yorkshire coordinated admissions scheme, families can list up to **five schools** in order of preference on their common application form. Education managers have strongly advised parents to utilize all five preference slots to maximize their chances of securing a local placement, rather than relying on a single choice.

## High Historical Success Rates Across the County

Recent admission figures released by the authority indicate a strong track record for regional placements. In the previous academic intake cycle, **90 per cent** of families across North Yorkshire successfully secured their absolute first-preference school. Furthermore, a total of**97 per cent** of all local applicants received a formal offer from one of their top three preferred secondary institutions.

Following the close of the application window, the council will lock down submissions, with **30 November 2026** representing the final cutoff date for any exceptional changes. The long-awaited National Offer Day is locked in for**1 March 2027**, when families who applied online can log in to the admissions portal from early morning to view their allocated school place.

**Cllr Annabel Wilkinson, Executive Member for Education, Learning, and Skills:** “Starting secondary school is a major milestone in a child’s education and we always want the admissions process to be as easy and smooth as possible for families. There is always a high demand for school places and we do our utmost to ensure that children are offered a place at their preferred secondary school. It is a tight window so I would urge parents and carers to submit their application as early as they can.”


## Critical Travel Policy and Catchment Discrepancies

The council has issued a specific warning regarding home-to-school travel support, reminding parents that an admission catchment area does not automatically guarantee free council transport. Free travel assistance is strictly limited to children attending their nearest suitable school with available places, provided the distance exceeds the statutory walking thresholds.

Authority mapping software underwent its annual update on **Friday 11 September 2026** to account for new housing developments across Harrogate. As a result, families are being instructed to re-verify their status using the council’s online nearest school finder tool before finalizing their digital application forms.

Applications, guide booklets, and specific admissions policies for individual academies and voluntary-aided schools across the town can be accessed directly through the master portal at North Yorkshire Council School Admissions.
