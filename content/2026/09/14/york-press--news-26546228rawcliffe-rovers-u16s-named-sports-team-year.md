## Most successful team in club's history wins Community Pride Award

Rawcliffe Rovers U16s Football Team won the Sports Team of the Year Award for the player's exemplary performance on and off the pitch - championing grassroots football *(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

The MOST successful team in a club’s history, which has championed grassroots football, has been presented with a prestigious award.

Rawcliffe Rovers U16s boys’ football team was presented with Sports Team of the Year at York Community Pride Awards, held at York Racecourse on September 10.

The team was named Rawcliffe Junior Football Club’s most successful.

  


Five of its players have played together since they were six years old, and over the past ten years, they have grown into a tight-knit team of talented footballers, with a shared ambition.

In 2022, the team lifted the York FA Cup at York Stadium, having defeated Brooklyn Lions in a memorable final.

A year later, the players were runners-up in the same cup and crowned U13 Division One League champions.

The team’s nominator said: “It is this past season that truly defines them as a breakthrough team.

“During the 2024/25 campaign, the squad faced significant adversity.

“Several players suffered serious injuries, including broken bones, and their striker underwent major back surgery. Week after week, the team often had just a bare eleven available.

“Despite these challenges, they refused to let setbacks define them.

“Their resilience was extraordinary.

“The squad reached the County Cup semi-finals, only to suffer heartbreak in a penalty shootout. Rather than dwell on disappointment, they bounced back to win the York FA Cup final against Scalby and secure another Division One title.

“Their resilience was tested even further by a gruelling fixture schedule, with matches every three days following winter postponements, all while balancing the demands of GCSE studies.”

Winning multiple trophies year after year, the team embodies grassroots football, showing respect for those on and oof the pitch.

The boys have been guided by coaches Gaz and Ryan – achieving “far more than trophies”.

The award was sponsored and presented by York College & University Centre.
