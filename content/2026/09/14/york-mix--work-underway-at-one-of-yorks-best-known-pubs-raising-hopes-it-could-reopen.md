There are hopes that one of York’s best-known pubs could soon reopen.

Work is underway today at The York Arms, on High Petergate.

The Sam Smith’s Brewery pub, which sits in the shadow of York Minster, has been shut for more than seven years.

Today a van from a plumbing and drainage firm was parked outside the pub.

Inside, the lights were on and new cleaning materials could be seen inside. This included a bucket, a brush, gloves, a mask and a set of Wonder Wipes.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-arms-high-petergate-inside-sept-2026-2.webp)

Cleaning had already begun on the windows of The York Arms.

***YorkMix*** reported in July that, following the death of the owner of Sam Smith’s Brewery, Humphrey Smith, messages including “He has gone” and “Please open the pub” has been scrawled on the dirty windows.

Most of the glass has now been cleaned.

Once a thriving city centre pub, The York Arms shut suddenly in July 2019. It has been left empty ever since.

In February 2020, a notice appeared on the pub’s door and window from an enforcement agency saying the premises had been “taken in possession by the Landlord under the authority of a High Court Writ”.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-arms-high-petergate-outside-sept-2026.webp)

There had long been a notice in the window urging anyone who wanted to run the pub to apply via the Sam Smith’s website, runapub.co.uk.

That notice has now been taken down.

The York Arms is housed in a Grade II listed building, parts of which date back to the early 18th century.

It was previously Carr’s Coffee House, The Eclipse and The Chapter Coffee House.

According to Hugh Murray’s book A Directory of York Pubs, it had settled on the name the York Arms by 1861.

And in 1938 it was extended into a next-door property.
