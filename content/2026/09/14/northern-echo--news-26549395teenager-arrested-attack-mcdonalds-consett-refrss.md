A teenager has been arrested after a man was left unconscious in an attack at a County Durham McDonald’s.

Police were called to the drive-thru on Ponds Court Business Park in Consett at about 4.30am on Sunday (September 13) following an assault.

A man in his 20s was left unconscious before being taken to hospital in a stable condition while a second man, also in his 20s, suffered cuts to his face and nose.

Durham Police has today (September 14) confirmed that a 19-year-old man was arrested in connection with the incident yesterday evening.

He was taken into police custody and questioned by officers, before being released on bail while further enquiries are carried out.

Detectives would like to speak to anyone who witnessed the incident, or who may have information that could assist the investigation.

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) said: "We were called to an incident in Ponds Court Business Park, Consett shortly after 4.30am on September 13.
