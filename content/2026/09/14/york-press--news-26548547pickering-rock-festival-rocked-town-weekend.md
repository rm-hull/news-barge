## Crowds enjoy day of music, entertainment and fun

Crowds filled Pickering market place at the weekend *(Image: Tim Bruce)*

A MARKET town rocked at the weekend as a popular event brought together residents and visitors.

Pickering Rock filled the town's centre with live music, entertainment, and family-friendly activities.

Crowds enjoyed performances across two stages, with the main stage at the heart of the event and a relaxed acoustic stage set up at the Black Swan.

A spokesperson for Pickering Rocks said: "We are incredibly grateful to everyone who helped make this year’s Pickering Rocks such a success.

"Events like this take a huge amount of work behind the scenes, and it simply wouldn’t be possible without the generosity and support of our sponsors, volunteers, performers and the wider community.

"It was wonderful to see so many people enjoying themselves and getting involved, and we hope everyone who came along had a fantastic day."

This year’s theme, Under the Sea, inspired creative fancy dress from adults and children alike, adding colour and fun to the day.

Children’s rides ensured there was plenty to keep younger visitors entertained.

Organisers credited the event’s success to the backing of local businesses, organisations, and individuals who donate their time, services, and funding.

A spokesman said: "The organisers would like to extend a huge thank you to everyone who supported the event, including the performers, volunteers, stallholders, sponsors and supporters, as well as everyone who came along and helped make the day such a memorable occasion."

Throughout September, the Pickering Rocks Facebook page will feature posts recognising sponsors and supporters who made this year’s event possible.

Organisers are already looking ahead and have begun seeking sponsors and supporters for Pickering Rocks 2027.

Businesses, organisations, or individuals interested in getting involved, offering support, or exploring sponsorship opportunities are encouraged to contact the Pickering Rocks team.

The organisers hope to continue building on this year’s achievements and bring more music, entertainment, and family fun to Pickering in the years ahead.
