Wynsors World of Shoes is set to close 17 stores across the UK, with sales already underway at several affected branches.

The budget shoe chain currently has more than 45 shops nationwide and sells brands including Adidas, Skechers, Clarks, Timberland, Nike and Crocs.

These closures follow a warning from private equity owner Modella Capital earlier this summer about job cuts and changes to its store estate.


Wynsors Shoes, in a previous statement, said: “It’s with sadness that we announce that certain Wynsors stores will be closing their doors.

“We’d like to say a huge thank you to all of our customers who have shopped with us and supported the store over the years. Your support has meant so much to the whole team.”

## Full list of 17 Wynsors World of Shoes stores closing

The 17 Wynsors Shoe stores set to close in the UK, according to The Sun, are:

- Crewe
- Leeds
- Sheffield
- Wakefield
- Fleetwood
- Burnley
- Oldham
- Salford
- Bury
- Rochdale
- Hanley
- Birkenhead
- Bootle
- Bacup
- Bolton
- Gateshead
- Sunderland


An exact closure date has not been announced.

Closing-down sales are under way at some affected stores, including Crewe, Rochdale and Gateshead, with discounts of up to 60%.
