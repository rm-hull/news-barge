ONE lucky North-East child will win the memory of a lifetime this Christmas – the chance to ride on Santa’s sleigh when it’s unveiled later this year.

To celebrate the 50th anniversary of Santa’s sleigh in Darlington, Darlington Round Table commissioned a new sleigh, which will be revealed at the town’s Christmas Lights Switch-On on November 15.

The community group has now announced that one lucky winner will ride on the sleigh with Santa as he helps switch on the lights, becoming the first person ever to travel on his new sleigh.

  The Darlington Round Table Santa's Sleigh has been touring the town for 50 years this Christmas 

*(Image: Chris Barron)*

James Taylor-Garthwaite, fundraising officer for Darlington Round Table, said: “We’re thrilled to invite one lucky child to be an ‘honorary elf’ for the evening, and join Santa himself for the very first flight of our magnificent new sleigh.

“The new look of the sleigh is a closely guarded secret until it is unveiled to the public for the first time at the Lights Switch-On, so this is an amazing chance for someone to be part of Darlington’s history and make a truly magical Christmas memory.”

The new sleigh is currently under construction by artisans at The Viking Boat Company, a Darlington-based charity which supports armed forces veterans and people at risk of social isolation through woodworking and heritage craft skills, and is best known for building a replica of a Viking longboat.

  Woodworkers from The Viking Boat Company are working hard to build Santa a new sleigh to use when he visits Darlington 

*(Image: Chris Barron)*

Proceeds from the raffle will help cover the costs of rebuilding the sleigh, with any remaining funds donated to local causes in Darlington.

- Tickets for the Santa’s Sleigh Raffle cost £2 each. They are available from tinyurl.com/sleighraffle

Darlington Round Table Chairman Alan Britton added: “We were delighted to partner with The Viking Boat Company to reimagine Santa’s sleigh, and we think this is the best possible way to reveal its new look to the people of Darlington.

"We're also very grateful to Darlington Borough Council for helping make this happen.

“Not only will it help us raise money to support more local charities, groups, and individuals, but it’s going to give one child an unforgettable experience.

“A lot of kids have been on the Polar Express or the Santa Express – but only one will ever be the first child to ride through Darlington on Santa’s new sleigh!”
