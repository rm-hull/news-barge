Police have released a doorbell camera image following the theft of a domestic radiator in Ripon.

The radiator was stolen from a property at 22 Knaresborough Road at around 7:55am on Tuesday 11th August.

Officers would like to identify the man pictured as they believe he may be able to assist with their enquiries.

*Anyone with information should email Constable David Fulford at [david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk) or contact North Yorkshire Police online.*

*Those unable to make contact online can call 101.*

*Information should be provided quoting reference 12260155872.*
