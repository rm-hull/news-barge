Earlier access for new residents’ homes at a Halifax estate is the aim behind an amendment to a planning application.

Calderdale Council planners have agreed to Newett Homes’ request to amend a planning condition related to the 35-home site at land opposite Park Lane, Siddal, which already has planning permission.

The development is next to Siddal Cricket Club.

Planning officers noted the proposal seeks a non-material amendment to vary a condition so that occupation of the development is not prevented until the majority of the landscaping has been completed.

  In supporting papers with the application, Newett Homes say: “This

  non-material amendment has been submitted to facilitate the occupation of much needed affordable housing without detriment to the site landscaping.”

A compromise solution has been reached with the council, the developer said.

Officers said: “Currently, the condition requires the majority of landscaping works and associated biodiversity monitoring arrangements to be in place before occupation of the first dwelling.

“The applicant argues that this is impractical because landscaping carried out during active construction could be damaged and may not establish successfully.

“The amendment would instead allow dwellings, including much-needed affordable housing, to be occupied before the landscaping trigger is reached, with the majority of landscaping to be completed prior to occupation of the 25th dwelling, rather than before first occupation, while still ensuring the landscaping and biodiversity enhancements are ultimately delivered.”
