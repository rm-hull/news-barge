Harrogate Car Enthusiasts Club has announced a new venue for its monthly meet this evening (Monday).

The gathering and autojumble will take place at The Curious Cow from 6:30pm.

It has moved from The Squinting Cat after the pub began closing on Mondays and Tuesdays.

The Curious Cow will initially host the meeting on a trial basis.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aa798e923793d79202.jpg)


Vintage, classic, historic, modern and modified vehicles are all welcome, with fellow petrolheads invited to attend and learn more about the club.

Organiser Barry Busby said:

“We are open to anyone motoring related! Any age classic, historic, Modern or modified. Everyone is welcome and it is free.”


Visitors can also bring motoring items to sell as part of the autojumble, although organisers have asked people to limit how much they bring to ensure there is enough room for the cars.
