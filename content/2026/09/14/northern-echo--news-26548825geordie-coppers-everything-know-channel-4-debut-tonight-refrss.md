A brand-new documentary series following the people who police the North East will hit TV screens tonight.

Geordie Coppers will make its debut on Channel 4 at 10pm on Monday, September 14, giving viewers unprecedented access to the frontline teams working to keep one of the North East's busiest cities safe.

According to Channel 4, the series follows Newcastle's neighbourhood police teams and their partners as they tackle crime, disorder and the unexpected challenges that come with policing a major city.

Here's everything we know so far.

## What is Geordie Coppers about?

   

*(Image: CANDOUR)*

The documentary follows the work of Northumbria Police, the NE1 Street Rangers and Newcastle City Council through the city's City Safe partnership.

Producers say the programme goes behind the scenes to show how the organisations work together to keep Newcastle city centre "safe, bustling and full of character".

Channel 4 describes the series as following neighbourhood officers and their partners as they deal with crime, public disorder and the many unpredictable situations they encounter while protecting one of Britain's liveliest cities.

The show has been produced by Candour, the company behind a range of high-profile factual programmes including The Essex Millionaire Murder and The Dunblane Tapes.

   

*(Image: Channel 4)*

## What happens in episode one?

The opening episode centres on PC Siobhan, who investigates a string of robberies along a busy road in Newcastle.

According to previews released ahead of transmission, the case takes an unexpected turn following a house search and the discovery of a suspiciously warm McDonald's meal, which helps officers identify a suspect.

Viewers will also meet Callum, a Street Ranger who hopes to join the police.

He finds himself thrust into the action when he arrives first at the scene of a street fight in Newcastle city centre.


## What can viewers expect from the series?

Trailers released by Channel 4 suggest the programme will feature everything from nightlife incidents and stop-and-search operations to police pursuits and arrests - and even an adult nappy.

Producers have promised a mix of frontline policing, community engagement and what they describe as "warmth, wit and plenty of Geordie spirit".

The series aims to show not only police work but also the wider network of people involved in keeping Newcastle running, including council staff and city-centre safety teams.

## When is Geordie Coppers on TV?

The first episode airs on Channel 4 at 10pm tonight (Monday, September 14).

New episodes will then be broadcast weekly on Monday nights, with each instalment also available through Channel 4's streaming platform.

For North East viewers, it promises a rare chance to see familiar streets, landmarks and faces on national television as the cameras follow those tasked with keeping Newcastle safe after dark.
