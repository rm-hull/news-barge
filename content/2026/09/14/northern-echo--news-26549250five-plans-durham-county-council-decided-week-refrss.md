Five planning applications were decided by Durham County Council last week, covering education, housing, retail, leisure and children’s care.

Three applications were approved, one was refused and one was found not to require an environmental impact assessment.

Here's what was decided and what you might have missed:

### Decision on redevelopment of former college site

A proposal to redevelop the former Fyndoune Community College site at Findon Hill, Sacriston, has been assessed by Durham County Council.

The plans are for a specialist educational facility. The application was received and validated on August 18.

The council decided that an environmental impact assessment was not required, with the decision issued on September 7.

### ATM retention plans approved in Seaham

An application to retain an ATM cash machine at the shop at 7 Seaton Lane, Seaham, has been approved by Durham County Council.

The application was received on July 23 and validated on July 24.

The decision was issued on September 11.


### Children’s care home approved near Sedgefield

Plans to change the use of Middle West Lodge, Old Durham Road, Sedgefield, from a dwelling to a residential care home for children have been approved.

The home would provide accommodation for up to two children and include new boundary treatment.

The application was received on July 6, validated on July 7 and approved on September 7.

### Children’s home proposal refused in Consett

An application to convert the dwelling at 32 Stuart Court, Consett, into a children’s home has been refused.

The proposal was for accommodation for up to four children aged between seven and 18.

The application was received on May 28, validated on July 6 and refused by the council on September 7.

### Padel and pickleball courts approved in Peterlee

Plans to change the use of premises at 2 North East Industrial Estate, Stephenson Road, Peterlee, from industrial use to a leisure facility were discussed at a planning committee meeting this week.
