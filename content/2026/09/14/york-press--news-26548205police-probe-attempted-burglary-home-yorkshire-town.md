Police are investigating an attempted burglary at a home in a North Yorkshire town.

The incident reportedly happened in Tindall Street, Scarborough, between 11pm on Thursday (September 11) and 6.30am on Friday, North Yorkshire Police said.

A police spokesperson said its inquiries suggest the “suspects may have accessed the rear of the property via an alleyway connecting Tindall Street to both Barwick Street and Barwick Terrace”.

“Officers are therefore keen to hear from anyone who was in or around the area during this time and may have seen any suspicious activity, persons, or vehicles,” they said.

“Residents are also asked to check any CCTV or doorbell cameras during the relevant timeframe that may assist the investigation.”

Anyone with information is asked to email [Liberty.Ashton@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Liberty.Ashton@northyorkshire.police.uk) or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 quoting reference NYP-12092026-0066.
