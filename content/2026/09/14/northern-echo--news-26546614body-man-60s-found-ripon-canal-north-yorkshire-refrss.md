The body of a man was found in a North Yorkshire canal by a member of the public.

Police were called to Boroughbridge Road, Ripon, at 8.41am on Sunday, September 13, after being alerted to a body in the canal by a member of the public.

Emergency services, including North Yorkshire Police, attended and found the body of a man in his 60s in the water and efforts are ongoing to locate his next of kin, who officers think may not be in North Yorkshire.

The road was closed for most of Sunday morning but has since reopened.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "At this stage, there is nothing to suggest any suspicious circumstances surrounding the death, and a file will now be prepared for the coroner."

Enquiries remain ongoing.
