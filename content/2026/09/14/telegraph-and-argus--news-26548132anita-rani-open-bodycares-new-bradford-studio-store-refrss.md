A NEW store is set to open in The Broadway shopping centre this weekend.

Bodycare will see in its latest store with a 'fun-packed' launch event and a focus on beauty experiences.

The retailer will unveil its new-generation Studio Store at The Broadway on Saturday, September 19, with a day of celebrations including a flashmob, goody bags, beauty masterclasses, and surprise guests.

The event will begin at 10am with a ribbon-cutting ceremony by BBC presenter and Bradford native Anita Rani.

  Bradford born Anita Rani 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Charles Denton, CEO of Bodycare, said: "With around one in three Bradford residents aged under 25, the city has one of the youngest populations in the UK, making it the perfect home for Bodycare's new-generation Studio Store.

"Bradford is exactly the kind of community we want to be part of as we rebuild Bodycare.

"Our Studio Stores are about much more than selling products, they are places where people can discover new beauty trends, access services, create content, meet friends and enjoy a great retail experience, all while getting fantastic value.

"We're incredibly excited to open our doors at The Broadway and bring the Bodycare experience to Bradford.

"The response to our first three stores has been hugely encouraging and we're looking forward to making our fourth opening a really memorable day for the people of Bradford."

  Bodycare Derby - Bradford will follow the design 

*(Image: Shine Pix/Shaun Fellows)*

The Bradford store is the fourth of Bodycare's new Studio Stores, following launches in Sheffield, Derby, and Leicester.

Designed to offer more than just retail, the Studio Stores combine value beauty products with services, technology, and interactive experiences.

A key feature of the Bradford store will be a free Creator Studio, providing space for influencers, content creators, community groups, and customers to produce and share content.

The store will also offer a wide selection of beauty, cosmetics, haircare, skincare, and personal care products, including an expanded Technic cosmetics range and a variety of Korean beauty items.

A nurse-staffed Piercing Studio will be available on-site, adding a service-led element to the shopping experience.

Ian Ward, general manager at The Broadway, said: "We're delighted to welcome Bodycare's new-generation Studio Store to The Broadway.

"It's an exciting addition to our retail mix and a great example of how The Broadway continues to evolve, bringing customers not only fantastic value and choice, but also experiences that give them a reason to visit, discover and spend time in store.

"The Creator Studio, beauty services and launch-day entertainment are a brilliant fit for Bradford's young and diverse community, and we're looking forward to seeing the store become a real destination for our customers and the wider community."
