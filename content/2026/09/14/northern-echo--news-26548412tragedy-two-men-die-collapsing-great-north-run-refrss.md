Two men in their 20s have died after taking part in this year's Great North Run.

The runners, both 25-year-old men from the North East, collapsed during Sunday's event and later died despite the efforts of medical teams.

Organisers confirmed the deaths on Monday (September 14) and said they are continuing to support the men's families.

A spokesperson for The Great Run Company said: "It is with deep sadness, The Great Run Company can confirm the death of two participants at yesterday's Great North Run.

"The two runners, both 25-year-old males from the North East, collapsed at the event, and despite the very best efforts of our medical teams later passed away.

"In each case the cause of death will be determined in due course.

"Everyone involved in organising the Great North Run extends their heartfelt condolences to the family and friends of the two participants.

"We are continuing to support their families during this incredibly difficult time, and our thoughts and sympathies are with all those who loved and knew them.

"In respecting the privacy of the families, no further details will be released."

The Great North Run is the world's largest half marathon and attracts tens of thousands of runners each year.

No further details about the two men have been released, and the cause of death in each case will be established in due course.
