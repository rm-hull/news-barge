A YOUNG teenager has been named the winner of the Community Pride 2026 Against All Odds award for her extraordinary courage and resilience.

Lexi Medforth, from Pocklington, was born with amniotic band syndrome, a rare congenital condition caused when strands of tissue restrict blood flow to a developing baby’s limbs.

  Lexi, 13, raised more than £20,000 for a state-of-the-art bionic arm after losing hers to amniotic band syndrome 

*(Image: Supplied)*

She entered the world missing her left hand but refused to let early challenges and her difference define her or limit her potential, growing into a confident, creative young person with a love for football and art.

  Lexi was honoured with an Against All Odds award, presented by Press Editor Ben Hardy 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

  


Her proud mum Becki said Lexi had always shown remarkable strength.

As everyday tasks became more frustrating, Lexi trialled an NHS prosthesis but longed for greater independence and set her sights on the Hero Arm, a state-of-the-art multi-grip bionic arm by Open Bionics that uses sensors to detect muscle movement for precise control. Although she met NHS criteria, a years-long waiting list left her deflated.

  "The arm is brilliant, and it’s definitely made a difference. I am so happy to have been named this year’s winner.” 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

Through the Open Bionics Foundation, Lexi and her family learned that raising £20,000 could secure a privately-fitted arm before Christmas.

Inspirational fundraising campaigns including #HandItToLexi and a GoFundMe page united Pocklington and beyond, with people donating money, goods, time and energy.

  Lexi's proud mum Becki said Lexi had always shown remarkable strength 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

Thanks to overwhelming support, Lexi received her upgraded Hero Pro arm at the Open Bionics Clinic in Bristol. Within days, the impact was life-changing: she can now brush her teeth, eat, drink, shake hands - simple moments that carry extraordinary meaning.

  Lexi’s story is one of resilience in the face of adversity, courage through disappointment, and the power of perseverance and community 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

Lexi’s story is one of resilience in the face of adversity, courage through disappointment, and the power of perseverance and community. Against all odds, she refused to let circumstance define her future and inspired others.

Picking up her award, she said: “The arm is brilliant, and it’s definitely made a difference.

“I am so happy to have been named this year’s winner.”
