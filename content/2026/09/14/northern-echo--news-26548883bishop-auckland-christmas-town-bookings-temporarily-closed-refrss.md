Questions have been raised over this year's Bishop Auckland Christmas Town event after organisers temporarily closed bookings and removed all passes from sale.

In a statement posted on social media today (Monday, September 14), organisers announced that Christmas Town bookings had been "temporarily closed" and said a full statement on this year's event would be released later this week.

The post said: "Christmas Town bookings are temporarily closed.

"We have taken all Christmas Town passes off sale.

"We know this will raise questions. A full statement about this year's event will be issued on Thursday.

"Please share this update with anyone who was planning to book."

The announcement has prompted speculation among residents planning to attend the popular festive attraction, which has become one of the biggest Christmas events in County Durham.

Christmas Town was due to return to Bishop Auckland this year from December 3 to 6, with organisers previously promising festive markets, live entertainment, family attractions and food vendors.

The event has grown significantly since it was first launched, becoming a major visitor attraction for the town.

Organisers previously said around 35,000 people attended Christmas Town in 2023, while more than 60 local traders took part in last year's event.

The celebration is co-produced by Baccanalia for Culture CIC and Bridge Creative Enterprise CIC, which supports neurodivergent adults and people with learning disabilities into creative and events sector employment.

Christmas Town co-founder and Baccanalia managing director Thomas Wales previously said Bishop Auckland was becoming a key festive destination for visitors from across the region.

He said: "The first event we put together in just eight weeks.

"Now we have people coming from Teesside, Newcastle and York.

"It is becoming a bit of a go-to destination."
