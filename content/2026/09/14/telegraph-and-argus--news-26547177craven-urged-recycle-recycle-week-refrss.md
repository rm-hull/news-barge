CRAVEN residents are being urged to recycle more and recycle better.

The call comes as part of Recycle Week 2026, which runs from today (September 14) to September 20.

North Yorkshire Council is encouraging people to double-check that all recyclable items are placed in the correct containers to improve recycling rates and reduce waste.

Councillor Carl Les, leader of North Yorkshire Council, said: "Every year, billions of recyclable items are placed in the wrong bin across the UK.

"By taking a little extra time to recycle correctly, residents can help save energy, reduce waste and ensure materials can be turned into new products rather than being lost to disposal."

Households across North Yorkshire already recycle more than 42 per cent of their waste, but the council believes further improvements are possible through small changes at home.

To support the campaign, the council is hosting events during Recycle Week, in Hawes (tomorrow, Tuesday) and Catterick Garrison (Thursday) with the help of Deo, its recycling mascot shaped like a deodorant can.

Deo will appear at two community events to help engage residents and answer questions about recycling.

The first event will take place on Tuesday, September 15, at the New Recycling Service Roadshow in Hawes Market Place from 9.30am to 3pm.

The second event will be held on Thursday, September 17, at the Co-op in Colburn, Catterick Garrison, from 11.30am to 2.30pm.

Councillor Les said: "At these events, residents can pick up advice and put their recycling questions to the council's waste and recycling team, such as ‘which container does it go in?’ and ‘what happens to my recycling once it's collected?’

"Simple changes at home can make a real difference, and I would encourage anyone with questions about recycling to come along and speak to our team at one of the Recycle Week events."

The council is also reminding residents that a wide range of materials can be recycled at the kerbside, including:

- Paper, cardboard, and magazines
- Envelopes, including those with windows
- Food and drink cartons
- Metal food and drink cans and empty metal aerosol cans
- Aluminium foil and aluminium food trays
- Plastic bottles, pots, tubs, and trays
- Glass jars and bottles

Councillor Les said: "Residents across North Yorkshire already do a fantastic job recycling, but there is always more we can do.

"By putting the right items in the right bin, we can all play a part in reducing the amount of waste being thrown away and help to protect the environment."
