A walker suffered a leg injury on a North Yorkshire countryside trail, sparking a major mountain rescue operation.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service called Cleveland Mountain Rescue Team at around 11.50am on Thursday, September 10, to help a casualty near Great Ayton.

Four team members reached the casualty to assess them, while a Land Rover and more volunteers brought medical equipment and a stretcher.

Team medics treated the walker before volunteers carried them about 500 metres to the road on a stretcher.

  Cleveland Mountain Rescue Team members attended to an injured walker between Foxton Bridge and Middleton-on-Leven. 

*(Image: Cleveland Mountain Rescue)*

The Yorkshire Ambulance Service crew met the walker at Foxton Bridge and continued treatment.

Sixteen members of Cleveland Mountain Rescue Team took part in the operation, which lasted about two and a half hours.

After the rescue, the team said: “We hope the casualty makes a full and speedy recovery.”
