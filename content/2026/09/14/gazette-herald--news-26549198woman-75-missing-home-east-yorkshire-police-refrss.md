A missing 75-year-old woman from East Yorkshire has been found safe and well, police said.

The woman was reported missing after being last seen in Seaton Ross, a village west of Market Weighton, at 11.30am on Monday (September 14).

On Monday night, Humberside Police confirmed that the woman had been found safe and well.
