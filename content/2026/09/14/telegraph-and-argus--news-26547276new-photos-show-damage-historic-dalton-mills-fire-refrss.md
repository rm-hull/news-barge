NEW photographs of a historic mill that caught fire reveal the full extent of the damage.

Keighley's Dalton Mills, known for its appearances in Downtown Abbey and Peaky Blinders, had its roof destroyed in the blaze on Saturday night (September 12).

This is the fifth time in five years that the mill has caught fire.

This newspaper has contacted the fire service this morning (Monday) to ask if a cause has yet been determined.

A spokesperson said: "No cause determined as yet."

### What happened?

Reports of a fire emerged around 8pm on Saturday night.

Emergency services, including 12 fire engines, scrambled to the scene.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service confirmed that they were still tackling the blaze at 8am the following morning.

A spokesperson said: "The incident at Dalton Mills remains ongoing this morning.

"Two fire engines are still in attendance, however the situation is now under control."

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police were called to reports of an industrial fire by West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service at around 8.28pm on Saturday 12 September.

"Officers were called to contain the scene at Dalton Lane, Keighley.

"At approximately 1.16am on Sunday 13 September the fire was extinguished."
