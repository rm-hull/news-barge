# Manslaughter charge after man dies following altercation at Yorkshire pub

## A 33-year-old man has been charged with manslaughter after a man in his 50s died following an altercation at a Barnsley pub.

On Friday (Sept 11), police were called to reports of an altercation at The Star Hotel in Cudworth, Barnsley.

A man in his 50's was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

He later died as a result of the injuries.

![The Star Hotel in Cudworth](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/12/12/11/The-Star-Inn.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



His family have been informed and are being supported by officers at this difficult time.

Liam McGuire, 33, of Stocks Lane, Barnsley, was charged with manslaughter.

He was remanded into custody and is due to appear at Barnsley Magistrates’ Court on Monday (Sept 14).
