Parts of a major North East road remain closed after a burst main flooded the carriageway and left it covered in debris.

Gateshead Council and Northumbrian Water confirmed a major burst on a water main beside Askew Road on September 13.

Today (September 14), Gateshead Council has confirmed that parts of the road remain closed and that teams are working to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

Drivers are advised to follow all signed diversion routes and plan for possible delays while the work is ongoing.

The incident caused heavy disruption in the area, adding to the Great North Run traffic restrictions.

  Images from the scene show water flooding into the road.

Gateshead Council thanked residents and road users for their patience and said it would provide a further update later today.

Motorists should check travel updates before setting out and allow extra time for their journeys.
