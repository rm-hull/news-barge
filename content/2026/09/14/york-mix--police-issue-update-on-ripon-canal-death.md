Police have issued an update on the death of a man found in the canal in Ripon.

As ***YorkMix*** reported yesterday (Sunday), officers were called to the canal near Boroughbridge Road in the town at 8.41am that morning.

A member of the public had discovered the body.

A scene guard was put in place while the body of a man in his 60s was recovered from the water.

Today, North Yorkshire Police issued this update: “The family of the man who was found in Ripon canal on Sunday morning have now been informed.

“The death is not believed to involve any suspicious circumstances, and a report will be prepared for the coroner.

“Thank you to everyone who shared our original post.

“Our thoughts are with his loved ones at this very sad time.”
