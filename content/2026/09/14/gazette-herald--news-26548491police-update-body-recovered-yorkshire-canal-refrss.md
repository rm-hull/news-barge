Police have said the family of a man whose body was recovered from a canal in North Yorkshire has been informed about his death.

The man in his 60s, whose name has not been released, was pulled from Ripon Canal on Sunday morning (September 13).

North Yorkshire Police said the death is “not believed to involve any suspicious circumstances, and a report will be prepared for the coroner”.

“Our thoughts are with his loved ones at this very sad time,” a police spokesperson said.

Emergency services were called to the scene in Boroughbridge Road, Ripon, shortly after 8.40am on Sunday.

Police said the body was discovered by a member of the public.

Boroughbridge Road was closed for most of the morning while emergency services and investigators carried out their work but reopened later on Sunday.
