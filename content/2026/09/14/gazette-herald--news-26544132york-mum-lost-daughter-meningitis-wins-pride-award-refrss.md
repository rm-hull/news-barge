An inspirational fundraiser who has dedicated herself to raising awareness of the condition which took her daughter’s life has been presented with a York Community Pride award.

Alison Goude turned unimaginable heartbreak into a powerful force for good following the death of her daughter, Livia Wilson to meningitis.

Her courage and resilience inspired her to begin fundraising for the Meningitis Research Foundation in Livia’s memory to raise awareness of the symptoms of Meningitis B and its deadly speed.

  


Their campaign, Liv Forever, hit the £40,000 milestone, prompting Livia's friend and campaign supporter Molly Grace to nominate Alison for the Charity Fundraiser of the Year Award, which she subsequently won in a glittering ceremony on September 10.

Molly said: “After losing Liv to meningitis in 2022, Alison showed incredible strength, courage and determination by creating a campaign in Liv’s name to raise awareness of the disease and help protect other families from experiencing the same loss.

"Through her tireless dedication Alison has, along with family and friends, raised more than £40,000 for charity while continuing to educate others by sharing Liv’s story.

"Ali has organised and supported annual darts nights, bake sales, marathons, charity golf club days and even completed the Three Peaks challenge to raise funds and awareness.

"To celebrate Liv’s 21st birthday, she also arranged a charity evening at York Racecourse, bringing people together in Liv’s memory.

"Alison has worked closely with the local press, giving interviews and continuing to speak openly about Liv to spread awareness of meningitis and its symptoms.

"Her courage, resilience and selflessness inspire everyone around her. She has transformed personal tragedy into hope and education for others.

“If the awareness Ali spreads helps save even one life, then everything she has done will have made an incredible difference."

The award was presented and sponsored by ISF York.
