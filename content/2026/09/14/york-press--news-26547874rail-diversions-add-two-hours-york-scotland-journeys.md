Rail passengers travelling north from York face a diversion that will lengthen journey times by up two hours to over the coming weekends.

Major planned upgrade works between Newcastle and Edinburgh will take place over three consecutive weekends, from Saturday, September 19.

  **Recommended reads:**

On these weekends, London North Eastern Railway (LNER) will run an approximately hourly service to and from Scotland, diverted via Carlisle, adding up to two hours to the journey time.

  **Dates affected are:** 

  Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 September

  

  Saturday 26 and Sunday 27 September

  

  Saturday 3 and Sunday 4 October

Other operators will also modify schedules.

A spokesperson for LNER said: "Rail replacement coaches will be in place for customers travelling to and from Morpeth, Alnmouth, Berwick-upon-Tweed, Reston and Dunbar.

"We recommend planning ahead and allowing extra time if you are travelling over this period."
