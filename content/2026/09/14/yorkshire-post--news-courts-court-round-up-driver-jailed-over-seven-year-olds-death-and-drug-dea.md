# Court round-up: Driver jailed over seven-year-old's death and drug dealer locked up after crash

## A driver jailed over the death of a seven-year-old boy, a drug dealer who left a woman injured after a crash and two men sentenced over a stabbing are among the latest cases heard in Yorkshire courts.

A number of serious criminal cases have recently been heard in courts across Yorkshire. Here is a round-up of some of the latest sentences and hearings.

## **Driver jailed over death of seven-year-old Huddersfield boy**

A driver has been jailed following a crash which killed seven-year-old Jack Rooke in Huddersfield.

At around 6pm on Wednesday (Aug 30), 2023, emergency services were called to Longfield Avenue in Golcar following reports of a crash involving a BMW X4 being driven by Nathan Shaw and Jack, who was in the road.

![Nathan Shaw (left) and seven-year-old Jack Rooke](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/10/16/29/MixCollage-10-Sep-2026-05-13-PM-5424.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Jack was given emergency treatment but died a short time later.

Shaw, 35, from Huddersfield, was arrested at the scene and later charged following an investigation by West Yorkshire Police's Major Collision Enquiry Team.

He pleaded guilty to causing death while driving uninsured and two offences of perverting the course of justice.

At Leeds Crown Court, Shaw was sentenced to 42 months in prison and disqualified from driving for 57 months.

In a victim personal statement, Jack's mother described how the loss of her "beautiful and loving boy" had "wrecked" her life.

She said: "I can't accept that Jack has gone and I never will."

Detective Sergeant Nina Roper said Jack's death had been an "absolute tragedy" for his family.

## **Drug dealer jailed after leaving woman injured in crash**

A drug dealer who left a woman seriously injured after crashing into her car before making a false vehicle theft report has been jailed.

Mohammed Khan, 22, of Abbeydale Road, Sheffield, caused the crash at a four-way crossroads on Wostenholm Road, Sharrow, during a crime spree in May.

![Mohammed Khan](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/10/8/26/Mohammed-Khan.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Sheffield Crown Court heard Khan was being pursued by a marked police vehicle when he crashed into the side of a woman's car as she safely travelled through the crossroads.

Khan fled on foot without checking on the woman, who suffered injuries including fractured ribs.

He later called the emergency services to falsely report his vehicle as stolen and claimed he was in London.

Police searches of Khan's car and home uncovered thousands of pounds worth of cocaine, ketamine and cannabis, as well as cash, mobile phones and weapons including a machete.

Khan pleaded guilty to offences including possession with intent to supply Class A and B drugs, possessing bladed articles in public, dangerous driving and perverting the course of justice.

Recorder Richard Thyne KC jailed him for six years and four months.

## **Two men jailed after 19-year-old stabbed**

Two men have been jailed after a 19-year-old was stabbed before being seen severely injured and clinging to the roof of a moving van.

Charlie Hoe, 20, from Middlesbrough, and Michael Flanigan, 30, from Redcar, attacked the teenager outside a shop on Derwentwater Road at around 4pm on Wednesday (Mar 4).

![Charlie Hoe and Michael Flanigan](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/08/10/53/jail.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Footage gathered by police showed Hoe lunging at the 19-year-old while armed with a knife.

A white van later arrived to collect the injured man and footage showed him clinging to its roof as it travelled away.

He was eventually dropped off at James Cook University Hospital at around 10.30pm, where he received emergency life-saving treatment.

Hoe pleaded guilty to causing grievous bodily harm with intent, aggravated vehicle taking and possession of a knife and was jailed for six years and eight months at Teesside Crown Court on Friday (Sep 4).

Flanigan pleaded guilty to wounding and aggravated vehicle taking and was jailed for three years and two months. He was also banned from driving for two years and eight months.

## **Dealer behind £223,000 county lines operation jailed**

A drug dealer who ran a phone line supplying heroin and crack cocaine between West and North Yorkshire has been jailed for six years.

Abubakr Ahmed, 23, was linked to the "Khanny line", which operated between February 11 and June 11 this year.

West Yorkshire Police valued the line at £223,380 from the supply of 2.23kg of crack cocaine and heroin.

![Abubakr Ahmed](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/10/8/42/Abubakr-Ahmed.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Officers executed a warrant at Ahmed's home in Sladen Street, Keighley, on Thursday (Jun 11), where they found phones, including the handset used for the drugs line.

Police also recovered individual deals of crack cocaine and cocaine, larger bags of heroin, cash, scales and drugs packaging.

Weapons were also discovered, including a knuckle duster stun gun classed as a prohibited weapon under firearms law.

Ahmed, of Leeds Road, Bradford, admitted a series of drug supply offences as well as possession of criminal property and possession of an electrical incapacitation device.

He was sentenced at Bradford Crown Court to six years in prison.
