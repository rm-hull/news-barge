A hoodie-clad man smashed a car windscreen after driving 'recklessly' to follow a man on a North East road.

The incident happened on the A689 between Hartlepool and the Wolviston roundabout between 1pm and 1.20pm on Friday, September 4.

The victim was followed by a grey or silver Ford SUV, which was described as being driven recklessly.

At the traffic lights at the roundabout prior to Wolviston services, a man wearing a grey hoodie got out of the SUV and smashed the windscreen of the victim’s car.

A police spokesman said: "We would appeal for anyone who was on the A689 at that time or who may have captured footage of the SUV travelling along that road – or the criminal damage incident itself – to get in touch."

Anyone with information is asked to contact Cleveland Police quoting reference 17731.
