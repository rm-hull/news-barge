A woman was taken to hospital with a head injury after an incident near a York hotel.

The victim was allegedly hit by a man on North Street, close to the Radisson Hotel, at around 8pm on Friday.

A 23-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of assault and later released on conditional bail.

#### North Street, York

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re appealing for witnesses and information about an assault in York involving a woman who was allegedly hit by a man.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
