A man has been arrested and charged after a series of assaults in the early hours of Sunday morning.

Adam Moore, 31, of Station Road, Ilkley, faces three assault charges over alleged incidents on Brook Street and Station Road, Ilkley, early on Sunday.

West Yorkshire Police said the charges involve alleged offences against a man and a woman.

Witnesses reported a large police presence near the Midland Hotel pub on Station Road over the weekend.

A spokesman for West Yorkshire Police said: "A man has been charged with assault offences against a man and a woman in Ilkley in the early hours of Sunday morning.

"Adam Moore, of Station Road, Ilkley, has been charged with assault occasioning actual bodily harm, assault by beating and common assault."

"He was remanded in custody to appear at Bradford Magistrates’ Court this morning."

"He has since been released on conditional bail ahead of his next appearance on October 15.

"The charges relate to reported offences in Brook Street and Station Road."
