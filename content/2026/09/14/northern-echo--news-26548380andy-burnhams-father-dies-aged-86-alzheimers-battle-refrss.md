Prime Minister Andy Burnham is understood to have cancelled engagements for Monday and Tuesday (September 14 and 15) due to the death of his father, Roy.

His father had been living with Alzheimer’s and died at the age of 86.

In an interview with The Times before moving into Downing Street in July, Mr Burnham told of his “sadness” that his father’s battle with Alzheimer’s meant he would not know of his son’s success.

“That’s a sadness because he would be (proud),” Mr Burnham said.

“The way I look at it, it’s his achievement as well.

“A whole family achievement although he doesn’t know it.”

Mr Burnham has also vowed to “grasp the nettle” of reforming the adult social care system, drawing on his father’s experience in care.

He described the “broken social care system” in England as “the worst of all worlds” and said that it is a “massive” priority for the public.
