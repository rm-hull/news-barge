A MAN is fighting for his life following a collision with a car in a Bradford district town centre.

Police are appealing for witnesses to the incident on Sand Street, Keighley, at about 6.29pm on Sunday, September 13.

Emergency services, including an air ambulance, scrambled to the scene.

A 68-year-old man was taken to Leeds General Infirmary, where he is being treated for life-threatening injuries.

A 57-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury through careless driving.

### Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for witnesses following a collision on Sand Street, Keighley at around 6.29pm on Sunday 13 September involving a car and a pedestrian.

"A man, aged 68, was taken to Leeds General Infirmary to be treated for life threatening injuries."

The spokesperson added: "Anyone who witnessed the collision, including anyone who may have dashcam or other footage of the incident, is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 13260522175."

### Yorkshire Ambulance Service statement

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 6.27pm yesterday (13 September) to report a road traffic collision on Sandywood Street in Keighley.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including two ambulances and the air ambulance, and one patient was conveyed to hospital.
