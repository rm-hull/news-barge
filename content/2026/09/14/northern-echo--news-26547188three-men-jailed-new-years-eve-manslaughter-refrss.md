- Good morning and welcome to the live coverage of the sentencing of three men for the manslaughter of Aiden Matthews.
- He suffered a fractured skull after he was punched to the ground in a Norton back alley on December 31, 2025.
- Declan Moor, aged 21, of Centenery Crescent in Norton was found guilty of manslaughter following a trial at Teesside Crown Court.
- Max Cashman, aged 30, of no fixed abode, and Lee Bradshaw, aged 22, of Vale Drive in Thornaby, both pleaded guilty to manslaughter earlier this year.
- During the trial, Mr Matthews' mother, Kim Butler, said her son had been at their house most of the day before heading out for drinks with family and friends.
- The 31-year-old was assaulted on New Year's Eve and died days later in hospital due to a brain injuries.
