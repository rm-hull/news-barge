A stolen vehicle with false registration plates has been recovered by police.

Officers from Batley and Spen Neighbourhood Policing Team (NPT) found the car during patrols in the Scholes area on Sunday evening.

The vehicle, displaying false licence plates, had been reported stolen from the Huddersfield area.

It will now undergo forensic examination as part of ongoing investigations.

West Yorkshire Police said: "Whilst carrying out proactive patrols in the Scholes area on Sunday evening, Officers from Batley and Spen NPT identified a vehicle displaying false registration plates.

"Further enquiries revealed the vehicle had been reported stolen from the Huddersfield area.

"The vehicle has since been recovered and will now undergo forensic examination to assist with ongoing investigations."
