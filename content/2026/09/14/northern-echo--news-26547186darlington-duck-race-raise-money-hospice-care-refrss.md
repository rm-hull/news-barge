A charity duck race is set to make a splash in Darlington next week as hundreds of rubber ducks take to the River Skerne in support of St Teresa’s Hospice.

The Darlington Duck Race starts at 2pm on Saturday, September 26, behind St Cuthbert’s Church in the Market Place and finishes near the Premier Inn.

Residents may already have spotted a number of illuminated and decorated ducks displayed in shop windows and at landmarks across Darlington town centre as excitement builds for the event.

Organised by St Teresa’s Hospice in partnership with Darlington Borough Council and Darlington Rotary Club, the fundraiser will see numbered rubber ducks race along the River Skerne for the chance to win cash prizes.

Participants can purchase a duck for £5, with every entry linked to a uniquely numbered ticket.

The owners of the first three ducks across the finish line will win prizes.

The event will raise money for St Teresa’s Hospice, which celebrates its 40th anniversary in December.

St Teresa’s Hospice provides palliative and end-of-life care for people with life-limiting illnesses and support for their families and carers.

Ducks are available from Angel’s Cut, Charles Clinkard and Leggs, as well as online.

Sales through participating outlets close at midnight on September 20, but limited entries may still be available on race day.

Families and spectators are expected at the riverside, with visitors urged to explore the town centre’s colourful duck displays before the race.

The race is licensed as a Small Society Lottery and is open to people aged 16 and over.

Only winners aged 16 or over can claim prize money.

Organisers have also issued a reminder about water safety, urging spectators to follow instructions from marshals and volunteers throughout the event.

Only authorised personnel will be allowed to enter the river.
