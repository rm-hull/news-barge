MOTORISTS face overnight closures on two York roads as Station Gateway works continue this week.

City of York Council said Queen Street and Station Road will close from 8pm tonight (September 14) while the works are undertaken.

The closure will apply from in Queen Street and its junction with Station Road between its junction, carrying on to the Milner Hotel in Station Road.

  



The roads are scheduled to remain closed until 5.30am on September 16, or until the works are completed, whichever is earlier.

It is envisaged that the roads will be closed between 8pm and 5.30am each day during the works period.

Vehicles will be prohibited from proceeding or waiting within the affected sections when the restrictions are in force.

Traffic signs and barriers will show the extent of the closures.

A signed alternative route for diverted vehicles will be provided via York’s inner ring road. Pedestrian and cycle diversions will also be put in place.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place to allow the Station Gateway works to be carried out safely. The notice does not prevent works or emergency access within the closed roads.

Access to premises will continue where it is not prevented by the ongoing works. Other traffic orders affecting vehicles proceeding, parking or waiting in the closed roads will be suspended while the roads are physically closed during the works period.
