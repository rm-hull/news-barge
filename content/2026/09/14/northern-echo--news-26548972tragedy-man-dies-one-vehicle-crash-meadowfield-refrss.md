A man has died following a crash in County Durham.

Emergency services, including an air ambulance, were called to John Street North in Meadowfield on Saturday (September 12) after a one-vehicle crash.

Paramedics gave first aid to the driver of a Citroen following the incident, which took place at around 3.20pm, but a 74-year-old man was pronounced dead shortly after.

Durham Police has confirmed that an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the crash remain underway.

An image taken from the scene of the crash on Saturday showed a flurry of 999 crews parked along John Street North at the time.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) sent several crews to the scene and was supported by a helicopter from the Great North Air Ambulance Service.

One person was then taken to the University Hospital of North Durham by road.

A Durham Police spokesperson said: "Officers responded to reports of a single-vehicle collision on John Street North, Meadowfield, at around 3.20pm on Saturday, September 12.

Paramedics administered medical aid to the driver of the Citroen, but sadly the 74-year-old man was pronounced dead shortly after.

An investigation into the circumstances of the crash is underway, and anybody who witnessed it is asked to contact Durham Police via 101, quoting incident number 240 of September 12.”
