POLICE were "appalled" after stopping a quad bike rider with an 18-month-old child on the front of the vehicle.

The incident took place on Knowles Lane in Holme Wood - where officers also found the vehicle had no insurance, MOT or excise licence.

West Yorkshire Police said: "Whilst working in the Holmewood area, local PCSO's stopped this quad on Knowles Lane.

"Not only was it being driven on the road without insurance, MOT, Vehicle Excise Licence or licence but the rider had an 18 month old child balancing on the front.

"To say we were appalled is an understatement, especially when the rider's main concern was getting points rather than the danger they were putting the young child in.

"Vehicle seized, rider reported to court and appropriate safeguarding referrals submitted."
