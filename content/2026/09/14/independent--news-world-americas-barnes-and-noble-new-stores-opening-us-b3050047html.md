Barnes & Noble is expanding with a slew of new bookstores across America.

So far this year, 28 stores have opened, or are scheduled to open soon, in a handful of states and Washington, D.C. It will put the bookseller’s footprint at more than 720 locations nationwide, according to *USA Today.* 

In September, the chain plans to open four new locations in Orlando, Florida; San Antonio, Texas; Elk Grove, California and Jenkintown, Pennsylvania, according to press releases from the company.

The Jenkintown store, which opens this Wednesday, marks the return of Barnes & Noble to the town after its location closed 14 years ago.

Elk Grove, just south of Sacramento, has not had a major bookstore since its Borders Books closed in 2011. It is one of eight locations opening in California this year, “reflecting a strong resurgence of the brand’s brick-and-mortar footprint,” Barnes & Noble said.

The bookseller’s revival of physical stores comes after many closed in the face of fierce competition from Amazon, which began as an online bookseller.

However, a CBS News poll in June found that 36 percent of Americans are reading fewer books than they did a decade ago, while only 23 percent said they are reading more.

Still, Barnes & Noble doesn’t seem discouraged. CEO James Daunt told *Axios* in July: “The revival is there.” 

Much of the chain’s comeback has been as a result of changes made under Daunt, who previously led the turnaround of British bookstore chain Waterstones. He has placed an emphasis on allowing each local Barnes & Noble store to be in charge of their sale strategies.

The company emphasized that local connection in a statement to *USA Today* last December. 

"Barnes & Noble is enjoying a period of tremendous growth as the strategy to hand control of each bookstore to its local booksellers has proven so successful," the bookstore chain said in a statement.

"In 2024, Barnes & Noble opened more new bookstores in a single year than it had in the whole decade from 2009 to 2019."

It was not immediately clear what other locations are opening this year. *The Independent* has contacted Barnes & Noble for more information.
