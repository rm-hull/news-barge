![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: "'Enhanced" checks may block £72m to Reform.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/809/cpsprodpb/db2b/live/dabd1d60-b082-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

The row over Reform UK's mega donations continue to make headlines. The government is considering tightening laws on political donations, the Times reports, which could see Reform UK's billionaire cryptocurrency donors facing "enhanced checks" after their £72m donation to the party. Reform UK leader, Nigel Farage, said the donations were 100% "compliant with the law today", the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: 'Ministers seek law change to make £72m donations to Reform illegal.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/809/cpsprodpb/99a9/live/03a56a60-b084-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

The Guardian also leads with UK ministers seeking a law change "to make donations to Reform illegal" and to thwart more cryptocurrency donors.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: 'Heartbroken.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/dbf0/live/c8e69360-b081-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

The UK prime minister has cancelled all engagements to be with family following the death of his father, the Daily Mirror reports. Andy Burnham's dad Roy, 86, died after a battle with Alzheimer's, and is said to have encouraged Burnham's interest in politics, according to the paper.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: 'Actress Sydney's booby trap..!'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/fe5b/live/71635100-b081-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

Female athletes from around the world have criticised American actress Sydney Sweeney for posing nude in an advert campaign for a sports betting company, the Daily Star reports. Sweeney, 29, is known for her role in the television series Euphoria and the films Anyone But You and The Housemaid. She posed naked while covering parts of her body with various pieces of sporting equipment.

![The headline on the front page of the Metro reads: 'Post Office fraud blitz.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/828/cpsprodpb/739d/live/b4f40e90-b07d-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

The Metro also reports on the backlash to Sydney Sweeney, saying English athlete Amy Hunt "takes aim" at her. Sweeney's move sparked a strong reaction from female athletes. Meanwhile, a "fraud blitz" involving 500 new officers will be part of a crackdown on gangs who are accused of using Post Offices and High Street shops to "launder illicit billions", the Metro reports.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Express reads: 'This is for our children.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/5c15/live/9ca2c9e0-b081-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

Mothers in the village of Piddington, in Oxfordshire, are calling on women MPs to back their fight against a planned migrant camp near their homes, the Daily Express reports.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: 'Villages join revolt over migrant camps.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/e7f7/live/5842b5c0-b082-11f1-bc1f-3f186ca4140c.jpg)

Similarly, the Daily Telegraph headlines on villages joining the "revolt over migrant camps", as Piddington creates a "blueprint" for other parishes being "forced into housing thousands of asylum seekers". Piddington locals are protesting plans to move 1,250 men into a former army barracks near the tiny town of 350 people, the paper reports.

![The headline on the front page of the Sun reads: 'Definitely Mayhem.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/3840/cpsprodpb/d34a/live/44c1fe30-b081-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

The Sun splashes on millions of fans scrambling to register for Oasis tickets after the band announced another huge tour for 2027 following the success of their comeback last year. The duo are set to play 38 gigs across 11 venues in Britain, Europe and the US, according to the paper.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mail reads: 'Harry and Meghan pull children out of their new school after just two days.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/810/cpsprodpb/079d/live/a7be7530-b082-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

The Duke and Duchess of Sussex have pulled their children from their new school in the UK over security concerns, the Daily Mail reports. Prince Archie, seven, and Princess Lilibet, five, started at their prep school last week, the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: 'ADHD not being overdiagnosed in the UK, major report set to find.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/830/cpsprodpb/e8ec/live/6264a680-b07d-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

A landmark report, set to be published within weeks, will find that ADHD is not being widely overdiagnosed in the UK, according to the i Paper. The Department of Health will look for a new national approach following the report, the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the Financial Times reads: 'Ten-year Treasury yields tops 5% as inflation fears batter bond markets.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1056/cpsprodpb/8a15/live/92e619b0-b07d-11f1-a540-61c3f7fc4e6c.jpg)

The 10-year US Treasury yield has hit 5% for the first time since 2023, the Financial Times says, after surging oil prices from the Iran War and inflation batter global government bonds. "The 10-year yield acts as a benchmark for trillions of dollars in assets worldwide", the paper explains, and its jump to 5% is "viewed as a worrisome threshold".

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: '"Westminster's time is over": Nationalists seek breakup of UK.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1536/cpsprodpb/2e56/live/483af0f0-b084-11f1-b1d1-571ed4d7ff2c.jpg)

And the Independent splashes on "Westminster's time is over" after leaders in Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland declared "constitutional change is coming" during a summit on independence.
