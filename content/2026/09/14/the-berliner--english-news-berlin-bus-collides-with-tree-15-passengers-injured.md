15 people were injured in an accident involving a BVG bus on the M46 line in Neukölln late on Sunday evening. According to police, the 41-year-old bus driver and 14 other passengers suffered injuries. The fire department had to free one passenger because his leg was trapped. Firefighters on the scene freed the 25-year-old using specialised tools.

The bus driver and six of the affected passengers were taken to hospital for treatment. None of them suffered life-threatening injuries. Eight other people received treatment at the scene.

According to police, the bus driver lost control of his vehicle on Fulhamer Allee in the Britz district at approximately 11:20 p.m. and subsequently crashed into a tree.

On Sunday, the BVG press office announced: “We wish all those affected a speedy and full recovery. We thank the rescue and emergency services for their swift and professional response.” The BVG will fully support the police investigation.
