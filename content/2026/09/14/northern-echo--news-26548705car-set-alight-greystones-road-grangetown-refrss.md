A car was completely destroyed in a suspected arson attack.

Cleveland Fire Brigade was called to Greystones Road, in Grangetown, during the early hours of Monday, September 14.

One fire appliance from Grangetown attended the incident.

Firefighters wearing breathing apparatus used a hose to tackle the blaze.

The brigade said the vehicle was “well alight” and suffered 100 per cent damage.

A Cleveland Fire Brigade spokesperson said: “Cleveland Fire Brigade were called to an incident at Greystones Road, Grangetown.
