A cyclist had to fight off two thieves who tried to steal his bike as he rode along a busy York street.

It happened at 10.35am last Monday (7 September) on Heworth Green, close to the junction with Eboracum Way.

The cyclist, a man in his 60s, was riding away from the city centre.

#### Incident location

The victim refused to give them the bike and they went away.

He was uninjured apart from a small graze to a leg, but he was left shaken by the incident.

The suspect who grabbed the bike is described as

- white with tanned skin
- a stubbled face
- dark hair
- aged late 20s or early 30s, about 5ft 11in tall.

He had noticeable fresh grazes on his knuckles which looked like he had hit them on a wall.

And he was wearing a grey two-tone shell suit tracksuit.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re appealing for information about anyone who may have been passing through the area at the time and may have witnessed the incident.

“Also, for motorists to come forward who were in the area at the time and who may have relevant dashcam footage.”
