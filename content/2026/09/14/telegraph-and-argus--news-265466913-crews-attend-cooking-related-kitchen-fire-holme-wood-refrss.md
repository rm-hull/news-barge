CREWS were called to a kitchen fire that broke out at a home in Holme Wood.

The incident occurred just before 8.30pm on Friday.

Three crews were called to the scene, which was reported as a cooking-related fire.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called at 20.20 on September 11 to reports of a kitchen fire on St Margarets Avenue.

"Three crews attended and extinguished it.

"It’s been recorded as a cooking-related fire."
