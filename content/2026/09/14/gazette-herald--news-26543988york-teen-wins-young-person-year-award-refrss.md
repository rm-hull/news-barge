A teen who consistently gives up her Saturdays to help maintain her local cemetery has been commended at the York Community Pride Awards.

Grace Kirby was named Young Person of the Year, with judges praising her for being an “excellent example” of a youngster making a difference.

  


Her nominator said: “Grace understands how important these spaces are to people.

“The cemetery is especially meaningful to her personally, as her grandad, grandma, and her sister Ruby — who she sadly never had the chance to meet — are all buried there."

  Grace turned her grief into something positive, giving up her Saturdays to help maintain her local cemetery 

*(Image: David Harrison Photography)*

They added: “Rather than letting this sadness stop her, Grace has turned it into something positive by helping care for the cemetery for others as well as for her own family.

“Her work helps create a calm and cared-for environment where visitors can reflect, remember loved ones, and feel comforted.

“Tasks that may seem small, such as tidying, cleaning, and helping maintain the grounds, make a huge difference to people visiting graves of family and friends.”

Grace chooses to volunteer, not for recognition or reward, but simply to keep the area she cares about well-maintained.

Her nominator said she shows compassion “far beyond her years” – leading with commitment, empathy and selflessness, making a truly deserving person for the award.

Grace said the award would feature pride and place in her room.

The award was presented and sponsored by York St John University.
