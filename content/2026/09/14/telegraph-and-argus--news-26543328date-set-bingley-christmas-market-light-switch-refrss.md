Hundreds of people are expected to flock to a Bradford district town for its annual Christmas market and lights switch-on.

The event in Bingley will take place on Sunday November 22, from 10am to 5pm, with the lights switched on at 4.30pm.

Holly Pennell, who runs the Bakes for Bingley market stall, has organised the event with representatives from Bingley Little Theatre, Bingley Town Council and local market traders.

Holly also formed the Northern Indie Collective, which runs a market stall in Bingley every Saturday.

*(Image: Bingley camera club)*

## 'All sorts' of traders expected

More than 40 stalls are expected, offering gifts, food and Christmas crafts.

Holly said: “We’re getting fairly booked up with lots of gift stalls, lots of food traders. All sorts.”

The lights switch-on will include free rides for children and a Santa’s grotto with elves.

Families will be able to book time slots for the grotto.

Holly said: “We’re going to put a booking system in place so there’s not big queues”.

Children’s activities will be held inside Bingley Little Theatre, including card making and bauble decorating. Bingley Creates is also potentially joining the programme.

She said: “We’ve got quite a big space inside the theatre because we’re running it on a Sunday. They haven’t got performances on so we’re utilising their space as much as we can”.

Two large gazebos will be placed outside, with seating and a coffee bar available for visitors.

  Bakes offered on Holly Pennell's bakery stall 

*(Image: Holly Pennell)*

## 'It's shaping up to be a big event'

Local choirs are in talks for performing.

Organisers are also working with Bingley Music Town, following the success of its bandstand performances during the summer.

Holly said: “We’re pulling entertainment from their knowledge.”

Organisers are also considering an auction or raffle involving businesses on the high street, including restaurants, beauticians and shops.

Holly said: “I’m going down there to get them to donate prizes."

She added: “It’s shaping up to be a big event”.

  Bingley arts centre

## 'Hopefully this will be start of putting Bingley on the map'

Community groups are being invited to contribute in different ways.

Holly said: “There’s all the community groups and we’re bringing as many as we can into it and everybody can contribute where and however they can”.

The event will use the art centre’s outdoor light-up board to promote activities across Bingley.

Holly's aim is to “really make it a full town event rather than a market square event”.

Organisers are hoping for suitable weather, although wind could affect traders.

Holly said: “we can get through rain, we can get through snow, we just don’t want wind”.

She said: “Hopefully this will be start of putting Bingley on the map.”
