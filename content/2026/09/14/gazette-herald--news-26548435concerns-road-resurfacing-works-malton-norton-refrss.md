Concerns about the quality of recent highway works in two North Yorkshire towns have been raised by a local councillor.

Cllr Keane Duncan has spoken to the authorities involved in the works in Kingston Drive, Leahurst Close, Glenmore Drive, Furlongs Avenue, Rosedale Avenue, Dickens Road and Fitzjohn Close in Malton and Norton after the concerns from residents.

Cllr Duncan said the project engineer has responded as follows:

“I will be inspecting the sites once the full programme of work is complete. This is expected to be by the end of next week. Workload will dictate the exact day but this will be before any ‘sign-off’ of the works .

“In the meantime, I would appreciate if you could send me any photographs you have. This will give me the opportunity to review any issues and address anything that may require immediate action.

“There are further stages of work still to be completed on the sites surfaced so far. These include an additional sweep, raising of the ironwork, and the removal of the covers from gullies.

“With regard to the micro surfacing itself, I would note that it is often considered ‘unattractive/ unsightly’ when first laid. I try to make residents and stakeholders aware of this if i’m contacted directly prior to works starting, as its initial appearance can be somewhat sludgy.

“As the water evaporates, the surface can look patchy; however, over the following week the material gradually darkens and begins to take on the finished appearance that most people would expect from a road surface.

“As this process is unfamiliar to many people, I completely understand why it may cause concern in those early days.

“Please rest assured any defects identified will be investigated and addressed accordingly.” See less
