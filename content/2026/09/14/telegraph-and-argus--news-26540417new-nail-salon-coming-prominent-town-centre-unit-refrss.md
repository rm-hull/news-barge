NEW plans for a vacant unit will “help diversity the offer” of a Bradford district town centre – planners have decided.

Over the summer, a planning application to convert 19-20 Low Street in Keighley into a nail salon was submitted to Bradford Council by Star Nails.

The application said the business would create six full-time jobs, as well as lead to the re-opening of a prominent empty unit on Keighley’s pedestrianised Low Street.

The plans have now been approved.

Over the years, the unit has been home to a linen store and a bargain shop, and its current planning use is as a takeaway.

Approving the new business, planning officers said: “The development would secure the occupation of a vacant commercial unit, support employment opportunities and help diversify the service offer within Keighley Town Centre.

“These factors weigh positively in favour of the proposal.”
