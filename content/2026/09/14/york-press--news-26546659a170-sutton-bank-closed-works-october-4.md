## REMINDER - principal North Yorkshire road closed for maintenance until next month

The A170 at Sutton Bank is closed until October 4 for maintenance works *(Image: Supplied)*

ROAD users are reminded that a main road in North Yorkshire has closed for three weeks while essential safety and improvement work is carried out.

The A170 is closed at Sutton Bank in both directions from Cross Hill to Sutton Bank National Park Centre.

It's a principal road in the area connecting the A1(M) with the North York Moors and the North Yorkshire coast.

  


The closure began yesterday (September 13) and is scheduled to remain in place until October 4.

The work is part of the road’s annual maintenance programme, which has been extended this year, North Yorkshire Council said.

It includes planned maintenance to the rock face, routine road repairs, drainage work and further improvements.


North Yorkshire Council said Sutton Bank is an important route on the county’s highway network and regular maintenance is needed to protect the public, preserve the road’s condition and reduce the risk of unplanned disruption.

The council said carrying out several improvements during the same closure would maximise the benefits of the work.

It added that this year’s programme is the start of a wider three-year improvement plan aimed at strengthening Sutton Bank and improving its long-term condition.

Councillor Malcolm Taylor, the council’s executive member for highways and transport, said when works were announced last month that combining them would help reduce the need for future disruption to road users.

Councillor Alyson Baker, who represents Hillside and Raskelf on North Yorkshire Council, said the A170 was a vital transport link for communities, businesses and visitors travelling across North Yorkshire.

She said the work would help keep the route safe, reliable and well maintained, and thanked motorists, residents and businesses for their patience and cooperation.

Delays are expected while the closure is in place.

Access to homes will be maintained whenever possible, although the nature of the work means there may be times when access is restricted.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until October 4.

The road is expected to reopen after the planned works are completed.
