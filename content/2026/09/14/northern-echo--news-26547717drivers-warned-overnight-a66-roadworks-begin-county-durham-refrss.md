Drivers face overnight lane closures and speed restrictions on the A66 in County Durham this week as essential maintenance work takes place.

National Highways will replace road markings and studs between Smallways and Rokeby, west of Barnard Castle, overnight from September 14 to September 20.

The work will be carried out between 8pm and 6am when traffic levels are lower in a bid to minimise disruption.

To allow the work to be completed safely, lane closures will be in place and vehicles will be escorted through the works using a convoy system.

A temporary 40mph speed limit will be enforced for around 5km on either side of the work area, while traffic travelling through the convoy section will be restricted to 10mph.

In a post, National Highways said: "We need to replace line markings and road studs on a stretch of the A66 between Smallways and Rokeby.

"To do this safely we are working overnight (8pm to 6am) when traffic flows are lighter.

"We will be working using lane closures with traffic being escorted by convoy through the working area."

Drivers travelling on the route near Barnard Castle are being advised to allow extra time for their journeys during the overnight works.
