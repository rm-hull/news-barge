FOUR people were taken to hospital after a two-car collision on a busy road.

The crash happened at around 3pm on Boothtown Road in Halifax on Sunday afternoon.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service alerted West Yorkshire Police of the incident.

None of their injuries were considered life-threatening, a spokesperson for the force confirmed.

## What the force had to say

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At around 3pm yesterday (13 September) police were called by the ambulance service to Boothtown Road, Halifax where there had been a collision involving two cars.

"Four people were taken to hospital, none of their injuries are thought to be life threatening."
