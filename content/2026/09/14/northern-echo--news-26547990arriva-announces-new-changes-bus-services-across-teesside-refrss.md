College students and commuters across Redcar and East Cleveland will get more bus journeys and better connections under Arriva’s new timetable.

The changes take effect on Sunday, September 27, after feedback from passengers and local groups, and aim to improve links to colleges, workplaces and town centres.

The biggest change is two new weekday journeys on Service 28 between Middlesbrough and Marske Estate.

Arriva says the extra journeys will help commuters and college students travelling to and from Redcar.

The new morning bus leaves Rosedale Crescent in Guisborough at 7.50am and reaches Redcar High Street at 8.41am.

An extra afternoon bus leaves Marske Estate at 4pm, reaches Redcar Clock at 4.25pm and continues to Guisborough.

Arriva says the extra journeys will give people more choice when travelling to work, college and other educational settings.

Passengers on the X2 and X3 services between Middlesbrough, New Marske and Lingdale will also benefit from the new timetable.

Buses that start or finish in Dormanstown will run to and from Middlesbrough Bus Station, giving passengers a direct town-centre link without a change.

An early-morning X3 service is also being brought forward after passenger requests.

The Monday-to-Friday service will leave Dormanstown at 7.32am instead of 7.37am, running five minutes earlier throughout the route.

Under the new timetable, the service leaves Middlesbrough Bus Station at 7.15am, helping pupils reach Outwood Academy earlier.

Arriva said passenger and local-group feedback had helped shape the changes, which would improve transport links across the region.

Arriva urged passengers to check the new timetables before travelling from September 27.
