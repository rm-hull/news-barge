Emergency services were called to a crash on the A19 this afternoon (Monday).

It happened at **4.33pm** on the**northbound carriageway** at**Skelton** near York.

A fire crew from Acomb went to the scene.

#### A19 near Skelton

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “All persons were out on arrival and there was no requirement for the fire service.

“Crews left the incident with police.”
