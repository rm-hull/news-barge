A burglary was reported at a business premises in Lidget Green in the early hours of today (September 14).

The Telegraph & Argus has seen images which suggest the burglary took place at Gregg's at U6 at the Quora Retail Park.

One photograph appears to show a shattered window or door.

Gregg's have been contacted for a statement.

Police have confirmed that enquiries are ongoing.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers received reports of a burglary that happened in the early hours of this morning (14 September) at a business premises on Clayton Road, Bradford.

"Enquiries are ongoing."
