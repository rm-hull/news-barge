A Lamborghini hire ended abruptly after police seized the supercar on a Bradford road.

Police seized a green Lamborghini Aventador SVJ in Bradford on Sunday, September 13, after reports of alleged anti-social driving on Planetrees Road and Bowling Back Lane.

A spokesperson for Bradford East Neighbourhood Policing Team said: "On the evening of Sunday, September 13, officers from the Bradford East NPT responded to reports from members of the public of several supercars being driven in an anti-social manner around Planetrees Road and Bowling Back Lane.

  This Lamborghini was seized on Planetrees Road in Bradford 

*(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

"Witnesses reported that a green Lamborghini and other vehicles were revving their engines and being driven at excessive speed, causing concern and disturbance to local residents."

Police later located the Lamborghini on Planetrees Road.

Following enquiries, the vehicle was identified as the one involved in the complaints and was seized under Section 59 of the Police Reform Act 2002.

The police spokesperson said: "The occupants were subsequently required to make alternative travel arrangements to attend a wedding for which the vehicle had been hired."

West Yorkshire Police urged the public to report anti-social driving on 101, or call 999 in an emergency.

In June, police seized a Lamborghini and a Mercedes G-Wagon in Bradford after a roads policing officer saw both travelling on the wrong side of Leeds Road.
