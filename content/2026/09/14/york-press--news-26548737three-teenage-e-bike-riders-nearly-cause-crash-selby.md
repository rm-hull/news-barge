Three teenage boys on e-bikes almost caused a crash then hurled abuse at a dog walker in a North Yorkshire town, police have said.

Officers are appealing for information following complaints from the anti-social behaviour in Selby.

North Yorkshire Police said the incident happened at 8pm on Tuesday, September 8, in Hemlock Drive with the youths heading towards Hornbeam Close.

“Due to the manner of riding, a vehicle had to take evasive action to avoid a collision,” a police spokesperson said.

“The teenagers, all wearing black clothing, then went onto verbally abuse a local dog walker.”

They urged anyone with information that could help identify the youths to email [alastair.graham-merrett@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:alastair.graham-merrett@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260176745 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
