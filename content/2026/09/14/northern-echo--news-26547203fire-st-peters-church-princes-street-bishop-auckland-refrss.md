A fire at a disused County Durham church saw firefighters battling the flames and smoke for about two hours.

Emergency services were called to St Peter’s Church on Princes Street, Bishop Auckland, at about 4.40pm yesterday (Sunday, September 13), following reports of a small fire.

Firefighters from Bishop Auckland fire station attended the scene and spent two hours extinguishing the fire and making the church safe.

A spokesperson for County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue said: “At 4.41pm yesterday (Sunday), we received a report of a small fire in St Peter’s Church, Princes Street in Bishop Auckland.

“One crew from Bishop Auckland fire station attended, and firefighters used one hose reel to extinguish the fire. The crew left the scene at 6.39pm.”

The church closed its doors permanently in 2013, with local groups since exploring community renovation plans to give the derelict building a new lease of life.

But as of now, the building remains unused.

The building famously hosted the baptism of the comedy legend Stan Laurel and, for many years, was used as a key community asset for people in Bishop Auckland.
