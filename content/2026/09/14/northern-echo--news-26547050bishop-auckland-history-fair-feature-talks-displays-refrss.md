History enthusiasts, families and residents are being invited to step back in time at the Bishop Auckland History and Heritage Fair later this month.

The fair on Saturday, September 26, will celebrate Bishop Auckland and County Durham’s history with displays, activities and talks.

Hosted at The Artists Hub, No. 43 Market Place, Bishop Auckland, the fair will feature an array of attractions including classic cars, heritage displays, traditional crafts and interactive activities designed to engage visitors of all ages.

The fair hopes to bring the community together to explore history, heritage projects and stories which have shaped the area.

Visitors can also hear talks about Bishop Auckland’s past and efforts to preserve the region’s heritage.

The County Durham Forum for History and Heritage is supporting the fair, which will offer forum members free tables to showcase their work and meet the public.

Organisers hope the theme, “Celebrate our past. Inspire our future,” will spark interest in local history and help preserve community traditions.

Entry is free and everyone is welcome.
