A BRADFORD man who fatally injured a pensioner when he assaulted him on the doorstep of his home has been jailed.

Stephen Jedynak died in Pinderfields Hospital on December 27 last year after being found unresponsive at his home in Thorntree Court, Crofton, Wakefield, three days previously on Christmas Eve.

The Coroner’s Office referred his death to the police for further investigation on New Year's Eve.

A murder investigation was launched by detectives from West Yorkshire Police Homicide and Major Enquiry Team on January 4 after a forensic post-mortem examination showed he had serious head injuries consistent with an assault.

## How police caught Bradford man

CCTV footage, taken at a distance, showed Mr Jedynak being assaulted during an altercation outside his address on December 23.

His attacker, who was subsequently identified as 27-year-old Hamza Shafique, from Barkerend, was seen to punch him to the side of the head, causing him to hit his head on the front door before he slumped to the ground.

Shafique then moved Mr Jedynak inside the address and closed the door before driving off.

## 4 arrested

Enquiries to trace the vehicle involved led to the arrest of Shafique on January 8 along with two other men, aged 32 and 23.

All three were arrested on suspicion of murder and on suspicion of being concerned in the supply of Class A drugs.

A fourth man, aged 36, was arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender.

Three were later released on bail and enquiries into the drugs matters remain ongoing.

Shafique was charged with manslaughter in relation to Mr Jedynak’s death on January 9 and was remanded in custody awaiting trial.

## Man 'did not deserve to lose life in such harsh and unnecessary way'

On Monday at Leeds Crown Court, Shafique, now aged 28, of Maidstone Street, pleaded guilty to manslaughter and was sentenced to six years and three months imprisonment.

Senior Investigating Officer, Detective Chief Inspector Stacey Atkinson said: “Stephen Jedynak was assaulted on the doorstep of his home and lay fatally injured for several hours inside the address before later being found unresponsive.

“A full and thorough investigation by detectives established the circumstances in which he was assaulted and resulted in Hamza Shafique being traced and subsequently charged with his manslaughter, of which he has now been convicted and sentenced.

“Stephen was a vulnerable 72-year-old man who lived alone and who did not deserve to lose his life in such a harsh and unnecessary way as a result of Shafique’s violent actions.

“We hope it will provide some measure of comfort to Stephen’s family to see him held accountable for causing his death.”

## Family tribute

Mr Jedynak's family paid tribute to him, saying: "Stephen was 72 years old when he passed away as a result of someone's reckless actions.

"He was a dad, brother, and uncle and he is missed by our family, especially at gatherings where he used to reminisce about our childhood to the younger generation.

“We do appreciate how quickly the police investigation progressed to identify the person responsible and making an arrest, to charging him.

"We would like to express our thanks to Pinderfields Hospital's bereavement team, the Crown Prosecution Service and the police investigation team and our family liaison officer, DC Jan Burrill.”
