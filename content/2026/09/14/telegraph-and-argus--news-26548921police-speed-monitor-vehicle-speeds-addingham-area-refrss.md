SPEED checks carried out by police in part of the Bradford district found that 15 vehicles were travelling faster than the 30mph limit.

West Yorkshire Police (Keighley) officers carried out speed monitoring in Addingham on Friday, September 11 as part of the force's ongoing commitment to check the speeds of vehicles in the area.

The result was that 40 vehicles were travelling below the 30mph limit and 12 were travelling in excess of 35mph.

  Police have been monitoring vehicles in Addingham and Silsden 

*(Image: WYP)*

A further three vehicles were found to be going at more than 40mph.

Last week, a similar operation carried out on Skipton Road, Silsden, found that 63 vehicles were at or under the 30mph limit.

Five were going at more than 35mph and one was travelling in excess of 40mph.
