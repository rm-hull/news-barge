Three men who lured a man into an alleyway on New Year’s Eve and assaulted him over a £900 drug debt have been jailed.

Max Cashman, Declan Moor and Lee Bradshaw conspired to attack Aiden Matthews over a debt he had run up to fund his crack cocaine addiction.

The 31-year-old was ‘given a slap’ before his head was smashed against the ground, fracturing his skull.

Moor and Bradshaw followed the father-of-two into the alleyway, where he was knocked unconscious, Teesside Crown Court heard.

  Max Cashman


Mobile phone records showed all three defendants working together to recover the debt from the occasional drug dealer.

Prosecutor Jamie Hill KC said Mr Matthews sold drugs for Moor but had run up a personal debt with 30-year-old Cashman.

“Mr Matthews was assaulted because of that debt,” he said. “Although, he was communicating with Moor about the money there is some evidence that he owed the debt to Cashman.”

  Aiden Matthews 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

Moor gave Mr Matthews money for a taxi home, but he went to bed at his mother’s house, the court heard.

Mr Matthews was found unconscious in bed by his sister on New Year’s Day and died three days later from a fractured skull and a brain bleed, Mr Hill said.

  Declan Moor


Cashman, of no fixed abode, and 22-year-old Bradshaw, of Vale Drive, Thornaby, both pleaded guilty to manslaughter.

Moor, of Centenary Crescent, Norton, was convicted following a trial.

Richard Wright KC, representing Cashman, accepted that his client was a drug dealer but said a ‘one-punch’ incident caused Mr Matthews’ death.

  Lee Bradshaw


Toby Hedworth KC, representing Bradshaw, said his 22-year-old client accepted responsibility early and approached prosecutors to give his account.

Peter Makepeace KC, representing Moor, said his 21-year-old client was 'pals' with Mr Matthews but accepted he would receive no sentencing credit after being found guilty.

Judge Francis Laird KC said Mr Matthews was ‘desperate’ for more work to pay off his debt before Moor lured him into the alleyway where he was knocked unconscious.

“Aiden Matthews was knocked to the ground and you, Cashman and Bradshaw, quickly left the area,” he said.

CCTV showed the three defendants meet back up at a pub in Norton shortly after Mr Matthews was attacked.

Cashman, of no fixed address, was jailed for six years after pleading guilty.

Bradshaw, of Vale Drive, Thornaby, was jailed for five years and four months after pleading guilty.

Moor, of Centenary Crescent, Norton, was jailed for six years and six months after being found guilty at trial.

He died at University Hospital of North Tees on January 3, 2026, with his family beside him.
