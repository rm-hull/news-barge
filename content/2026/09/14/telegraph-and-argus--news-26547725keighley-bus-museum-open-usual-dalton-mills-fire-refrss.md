A museum that was previously affected by a mill fire has confirmed it is “open as usual” following another blaze.

Keighley Bus Museum reopened last month after a fire at the nearby Dalton Mills damaged its roof.

The building was unaffected by the latest blaze on Saturday night, but owners are still worried.

Norman Shepherd, chairman of the Keighley Bus Museums Trust, said: "We haven't been affected this time.

"But we were there, keeping an eye on the building.

"The wind was blowing in a different direction, fortunately."

Mr Shepherd added: "It's really worrying, but we carry on.

"We are open as usual."

## Dalton Mills fire damaged the museum previously

Last July, embers from a fire at Dalton Mills blew onto the roof of the Keighley Bus Museum.

The roof suffered damage but, due to the high costs of repair, remains.

A lot of the renovation work was carried out by staff and volunteers.
