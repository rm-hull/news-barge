RAIL passengers in North Yorkshire are facing delays of up to 30 minutes due to a speed restriction over defective track.

The disruption is affecting services to and from Scarborough, as the track between Filey and the coastal town is inspected.

Trains have been forced to run at reduced speeds, with delays, cancellations, and revised timetables expected until 2am on 16 September, according to train operator Northern.

  


A spokesperson for the York-based company said: "Network Rail are on site and have advised that trains must run at a reduced speed.

"Repairs are being arranged but are not expected to be completed until Wednesday. September 16."

To minimise disruption, services departing Scarborough and Seamer towards Hull and Sheffield will be retimed to depart earlier than usual.

Passengers are advised to check journey times before travelling.

Affected stations include York, Goole, Driffield, Howden, Selby, Bridlington and Beverley.

The Northern spokesperson said: "You can travel on the next Northern service to your destination, where available.

"Northern train ticket restrictions have been lifted in the affected areas, including Advance and Peak restrictions, during this disruption. Restrictions will be reinstated once the disruption ends."
