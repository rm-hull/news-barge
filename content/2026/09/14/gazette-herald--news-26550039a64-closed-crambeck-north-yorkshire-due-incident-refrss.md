A MAIN road in North Yorkshire is closed both ways.

The A64 is shut at High Hutton due to an incident.

One motorist reported that they were turned away from the Barton Hill junction.

It is not known at this stage what the incident involves.

Another driver said traffic is reported to be stationary at the brow of the hill . Anyone heading to Malton with other motorists turning round.

Everything driving from York is being told to turn left towards Welburn.
