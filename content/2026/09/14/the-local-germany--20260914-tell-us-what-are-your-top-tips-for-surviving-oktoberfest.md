Oktoberfest 2026 is set to kick off at midday on Saturday when the Mayor of Munich taps the first keg in the Schottenhamel tent with the famous cry *O'zapft is!* (The keg is tapped!)

Over its 16-to-18-day run, the event is expected to welcome more than six million visitors from around the world.

Navigating a festival of this scale isn't just about showing up. From scoring a table in a packed tent without a reservation to pacing yourself through litres of heavy Bavarian beer, first-time visitors have a lot to figure out.

So, if you’ve been to the *Wiesn* and lived to tell the tale, please take a moment to answer the questions below – and help out this year’s newbies with your insider tips, favourite spots and "must-know" hacks.
