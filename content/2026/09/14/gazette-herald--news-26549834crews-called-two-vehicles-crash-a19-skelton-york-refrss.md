Two vehicles have crashed on a main road in a York village.

Police and firefighters were called to the crash on the A19 northbound in Skelton shortly after 4.30pm on Monday (September 14).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said everyone was out of the vehicles when its crew from Acomb arrived on the scene.

A fire service spokesperson said the incident was left with police.
