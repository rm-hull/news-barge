More details have been revealed for York’s first ever city centre dog show.

York Museum Gardens are preparing to host the York Dog Show, in partnership with the team from York Dog Park on **Sunday 20 September**, running from 10am to 4pm.

The inaugural event promises a packed day out for pet owners and families, and more details of the show has been revealed.

It will include a series of technical agility classes, workshops in scent work, interactive sessions on general obedience training with True Pawtential, and guided grooming tips from Pampered Paws will be available periodically throughout the day.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/York-Dog-Show-Coming-to-York-Museum-Gardens.-Image-Courtesy-of-York-Dog-Park-1.webp)

As previously announced, the Dog Show Competition will be hosted and judged by special guest, Yorkshire Shepherdess, Amanda Owen.

The six categories for each event will include awards such as ‘Handsomest Dog’, ‘Fanciest Female’, ‘Cutest Puppy’, and ‘Best Trick’.

There will be two opportunities to take part, morning and afternoon, and places can be reserved on a first come, first serve sign up on the day upon arrival. Registration is £2 per dog, per category.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/York-Dog-Show-in-York-Museum-Gardens-Courtesy-of-York-Dog-Park-1.webp)

In the ‘Have a Go’ Dog Park, agility equipment will be available for use all day whilst the bale racing competition will take place between 12:30 and 1:30pm. Dog photographers will be available to capture the events as well as posed shoots for pets.

A pop-up shopping village will be selling everything from treats to toys and accessories alongside informative stalls and tips from veterinary experts. Food vendors will be selling a range of delicious treats and snacks for pets and people.

Tickets for attendees will cost £5 for adults and £2 for children. Tickets are on sale via the York Museums Trust website.
