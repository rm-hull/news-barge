A TRAVELLER encampment is in a popular Bradford district park.

Multiple caravans and other vehicles were seen by the Telegraph & Argus on a grassy area in Victoria Park, Keighley on Monday morning.

Bradford Council says Environmental Health are aware and they have started the legal eviction process.

## Full council statement

  A photo of the camp taken this morning (September 14) 

*(Image: T&A)*

A spokesperson for Bradford Council said: "Environmental Health are aware of travellers at Victoria Park in Keighley.

"They have started the legal eviction process for the encampment."

Victoria Park is a public park on Hard Ings Road, Keighley.

It is open 24 hours a day, year-round.
