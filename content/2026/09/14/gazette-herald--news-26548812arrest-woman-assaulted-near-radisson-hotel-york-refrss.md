A woman has been taken to hospital after being reportedly assaulted outside a hotel in York.

North Yorkshire Police said a man has been arrested in connection with the alleged incident in North Street, near the Radisson Hotel, at about 8pm on Friday (September 11).

The force said the woman required hospital treatment for a head inquiry.

A police spokesperson said the woman “was allegedly hit by a man”.

They urged anyone with information about the reported assault to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing [daniel.freeman@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:daniel.freeman@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phoning 101 quoting reference 12260178827.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Police said a 23-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of assault in connection with the alleged incident and later released on conditional bail.
