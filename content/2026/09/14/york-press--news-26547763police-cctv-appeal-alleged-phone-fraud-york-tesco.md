Police have issued CCTV footage as they investigate a fraud in York.

The incident occurred on June 29, at Tesco Askham Bar, where a mobile phone was obtained fraudulently.

North Yorkshire Police is asking anyone who recognises the individual in the CCTV footage to come forward, as they may have information that could aid the investigation.
