A high street brand has revealed its opening date in York city centre.

Space NK will be throwing open the doors of its new store in Coney Street on Friday, September 25 - with free gifts on offer.

The beauty retailer which sells luxury cosmetic brands will be giving the first 100 customers who buy something a beauty treat as part of the opening celebrations and anyone parting with £30 or more will receive a 'Pick & Mix treat'.

  An impression showing how Space NK, in Coney Street, could look (Image LDRS) 

*(Image: Image LDRS)*

The first 200 people to spend at least £100 will be rewarded with an 'exclusive goody bag'.

The store will be open from 10am until 6pm on its first day.

Space NK has taken over the Grade II-listed property that was previously home to JD Sports after securing planning permission to convert the store, including internal and external refurbishment works.

  Space NK, in Coney Street, York. 

*(Image: LDRS)*

Three new air conditioning units have been installed as part of the revamp after City of York Council planning officers agreed that they wouldn't harm the historic building.

Previous fixtures and fittings have also been removed to make way for a new floor, wall and ceiling finishes, lighting, ventilation and a ground floor platform lift along with external company branding, including signs and a flag.

JD Sports relocated into a larger store in Davygate last year.

  Space NK confirms York city centre store opening date - pictured, how Coney Street could look under the redevelopment plans for the area. 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Space NK shared the much-anticipated opening date for its York store on social media.

The company has more than 80 stores across the UK and Ireland, including Leeds, Harrogate and Beverley.

This is a return to the city for the popular brand, which was founded in 1991 - it previously operated in Davygate from 2015 until 2022, where TAG Heuer watch shop is now located.

The opening has been welcomed as a vote of confidence in the area which is at the heart of a major regeneration project centred around Coney Street involving York-based developers Helmsley Group.

Once the bustling heart of York's shopping offer, Coney Street has looked forlorn in recent years with numerous empty properties.

  How St Martins Yard, off Coney Street, could look under the plans (Image: Helmsley Group) 

*(Image: Image: Helmsley Group)*

The redevelopment follows a lengthy period of consultation and planning, and includes the creation of a new public space in the Guildhall by demolishing the buildings near what was once the Lush unit in Coney Street.

It will take pedestrians from Coney Street down some stairs to the new public space, called St Martin’s Yard after the nearby St Martin’s Church, linking to the riverfront.

The first phase of work to transform Coney Street is now underway, with work also set to convert the upper floors of the former JD Sports and its neighbouring units into flats.
