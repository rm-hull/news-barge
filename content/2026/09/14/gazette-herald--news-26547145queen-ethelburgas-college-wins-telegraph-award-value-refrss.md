An INDEPENDENT school near York has been named as the best value for money in the sector for Yorkshire.

Queen Ethelburga's College, 12 miles outside York on the way to Harrogate, was recognised for its high A-level results and as a top mixed school, as reported by The Telegraph in a guide published on September 8.

Last year, 79 per cent of its A-levels were graded A* to A, placing it in the top 20 nationally, according to the national title.

  


The school was also awarded The Telegraph's silver award in a nationwide guide, with King Edward VI High School for girls in Edgbaston, Birmingham taking first place.

Daniel Machin, the headmaster at Queen Ethelburga's, told The Telegraph Money: "We have some wealthier parents, but the vast majority are really pushing themselves to be able to afford private education.

"They are making sacrifices to be able to afford this.

"That's why we work really hard to make sure we can continue to support them."

Queen Ethelburga's College, known locally as QE, has been an independent boarding and day school for more than a century.
