ON THURSDAY (September 10), the fire service were called out to a report of a kitten "stuck in a car engine" in the Listerhills area.

Just before 10pm, firefighters were called out to a road not far from the University to a report that a kitten was in danger.

Unfortunately, the fire service were not able to free the feline.

A spokesperson for the West Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service said: "One crew was called to Summerville Road at 9.42pm to reports of a kitten stuck in a car engine.

"Firefighters attempted to free the kitten but could not, so left the scene in the hands of the owner."
