**YORK** — Police have deployed specialist search teams and issued an urgent emergency appeal to the public following the disappearance of a 26-year-old man from the Heslington area of York.

North Yorkshire Police confirmed that **Xianwei Xi**, who is also known locally as** Jeremiah Xi**or** Francis Xi**, was last known to have spoken with family members at approximately** 7:00 pm on Saturday 12 September 2026**. Concerns for his safety and welfare have escalated significantly over the last 36 hours.

Divisional officers alongside specialist tracking resources are currently conducting extensive physical searches in and around the York district. Authorities have stated that despite these concentrated efforts, he has not yet been located, and anxiety for his safety is growing as time passes.

## Immediate Emergency Reporting Protocol

Search coordinators are urging regional commuters, students, and residents across the Heslington and wider York area to report any potential sightings or clothing descriptions immediately to help narrow down the active search perimeter.

**North Yorkshire Police Deployment Desk:** “Extensive searches are currently taking place in and around York involving officers and specialist resources. Despite these efforts, Xianwei has not yet been located and, as time passes, we are becoming increasingly concerned for his wellbeing. We are urging anyone who may have seen Xianwei, or who has any information about his whereabouts, to come forward immediately. If you see Xianwei, please call 999 straight away.”


Members of the public who spot him are instructed to dial **999 straight away** to ensure rapid deployment. Anyone possessing historical location data or background details that could assist search teams should contact North Yorkshire Police on**101**, select option 1 to connect to the Force Control Room, and explicitly quote the incident case reference number 12260180062.
