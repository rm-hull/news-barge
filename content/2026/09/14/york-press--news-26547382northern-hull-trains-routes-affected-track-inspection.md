RAIL passengers in North Yorkshire are facing delays this morning due to a track safety inspection.

National Rail said York-based Northern, Hull Trains and TransPennine Express services are affected by the work being carried out on the line between Selby and Hull, which means trains are running slowly on some lines.

Engineers are being sent to the site to resolve the issued, a National Rail spokesperson said.

  


Services affected include Hull Trains, Northern, and TransPennine Express, with delays of up to 20 minutes expected.

National Rail is reporting Northern customer service as saying train services are unable to run from Selby towards Hull, but its Hull to Selby services may also be disrupted due to the displacement of trains.

Its services between York and Bridlington and Hull and Halifax are affected.

TransPennine Express customer advice is that tickets will be accepted at no extra cost on the following routes:

- Northern services between Hull, Brough, Doncaster and Sheffield. Customers can then connect with the following services:
- Alternative TransPennine Express services between Liverpool Lime Street and Leeds
- East Midlands Railway services between Manchester Piccadilly / Liverpool Lime Street and Sheffield
- LNER services between Doncaster and Leeds
- Northern services between Sheffield and Leeds
- TransPennine Express services between Manchester / Liverpool Lime Street and Sheffield

Rail replacement transport has been requested.

The disruption is expected to last until 1.30pm.
