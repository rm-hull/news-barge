The family of a man killed with one punch over a £900 drug debt have spoken of the devastating impact of his death.

Three men lured Aiden Matthews to an alleyway on New Year’s Eve to recover the debt.

The 31-year-old’s killers Max Cashman, Declan Moor and Lee Bradshaw have all been jailed at Teesside Crown Court.

Mr Matthews’ sister Nadine wrote a victim impact statement that was read to the judge during sentencing.

“Aiden was a very much-loved member of our family,” she said. “He was a brilliant son, brother, father, uncle and friend to all.”

She added: “The pain of losing Aiden has broken us as a family and our lives will never be the same again.

“We can’t get our heads around losing Aiden in the way we have, even listening to what has been said in court.

“We will never understand why they used such violence towards him.

“All the memories we would have created with him are now only something we can dream of.

“Aiden didn’t deserve to have his life cut short. The actions of these men that night have devastated our family, and our lives will never feel the same again.”

  Max Cashman, left, Lee Bradshaw, centre, and Declan Moor, right, have been jailed for the manslaughter of Aiden Matthews.


Mr Matthews never regained consciousness after the assault and died three days later, Teesside Crown Court heard.

A post-mortem examination found he had been punched once and suffered a fatal skull fracture when his head hit the ground in a Norton alleyway on New Year’s Eve.

Cashman, of no fixed address, was jailed for six years after pleading guilty to manslaughter.

Bradshaw, of Vale Drive, Thornaby, was jailed for five years and four months after pleading guilty to manslaughter.

Moor, of Centenary Crescent, Norton, was jailed for six years and six months after being found guilty at trial.

Judge Francis Laird KC, the Recorder of Middlesbrough, said Mr Matthews was ‘desperate’ to find more work to pay off his debt when Moor lured him into the alleyway, where he suffered his fatal injury.

During the trial, his mother, Kim Butler, said she found him unconscious in bed after he returned home shortly after midnight.
