A man has been charged with arson after Darlington’s DL1 complex was evacuated due to a fire.

Police were called to the town centre leisure complex at 7.55pm on Friday (September 11) after a fire broke out in an underground bin area.

Firefighters also attended and extinguished the blaze, which involved around six wheelie bins and sent black smoke pouring from the rear of the Premier Inn.

The Premier Inn, Nando’s and Vue cinema were evacuated with movie-goers forced to leave screenings carrying their popcorn and drinks.

Nobody was injured.

Raymond Coglan, 42, was arrested and taken into police custody, where he was questioned by officers.

The Forcett Street, Darlington resident was later charged with arson with recklessness as to whether life was endangered.

The man was remanded in custody and is due to appear at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court today (Monday, September 14).


At the time of the fire, County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service sent two fire engines to the scene.

Group Manager Chris Williams said crews remained at the complex until 9.31pm and that the incident had “looked worse than it was” because of the amount of smoke.

He said the fire was contained to the bins and dealt with “without further escalation”.

Crowds gathered outside DL1 while the complex was evacuated and the surrounding area was cordoned off.

One cinema-goer said their film had not started when they were told to leave.

They said: “Our movie hadn't even begun, still showing the trailers. Spent £15 on popcorn and a drink for nothing.”

The Woollen Mill was also forced to close its kitchen the following day as a result of the incident, although it remained open for drinks.
