POLICE are searching for a missing man from York.

Xianwei Xi, 26, who is also known as Jeremiah Xi and Francis Xi, is missing from the Heslington area and was last in contact with family at around 7pm on Saturday, September 12.

North Yorkshire Police said there are increasing concerns for his welfare.

  


A police spokesperson said: "Extensive searches are currently taking place in and around York involving officers and specialist resources.

"Despite these efforts, Xianwei has not yet been located and, as time passes, we are becoming increasingly concerned for his wellbeing.

"We are urging anyone who may have seen Xianwei, or who has any information about his whereabouts, to come forward immediately."

Anyone with an immediate sighting is urged to call 999.

Anyone with information is asked to call 101, quoting reference 12260180062.
