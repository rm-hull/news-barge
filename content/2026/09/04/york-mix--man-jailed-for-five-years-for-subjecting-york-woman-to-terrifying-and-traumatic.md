A man has been sent to prison after subjecting a York woman to a “terrifying” sexual assault that left her feeling “less than human”.

Matthew Thomas Banks, aged 40, of Wheldrake, was jailed for five years at York Crown Court today.

He admitted sexual assault by penetration and sexual assault by touching.

Banks was at the victim’s home when he carried out the offences in the early hours of Sunday 25 July 2021.

The victim managed to escape from the property and alerted neighbours, who encouraged her to report the incident to police.

Banks was arrested at his home later that day on suspicion of assault by penetration but denied the offences during police interviews.

However, forensic evidence supported the victim’s account and linked him to the crimes.

After being charged, Banks initially pleaded not guilty and a trial date was set for August. However, on the first day of the trial, he changed his plea to guilty.

## ‘Night terrors and flashbacks.’

In an impact statement read to the court, the victim described the devastating and long-lasting effect the assault has had on every aspect of her life, including her family life, career and relationships.

She suffers from panic attacks and anxiety.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/york-crown-court-entrance.webp)

She told the court: “Over the last five years, I have been affected by the sexual assault as I have woken up panicking in sweats, and I have had night terrors and flashbacks.”

She also described how the assault has left her fearful of visiting places she once enjoyed, as well as locations she needs to go to, with the constant fear of seeing him affecting her day-to-day life.

The victim added the attack left her feeling “less than human”, “less of a woman” and as though she was “damaged goods”.

She ended by saying: “It has affected almost every aspect of my life, and it will take a long time for me to heal fully from this.”

## ‘Immense courage’

Investigating officers praised the victim for her courage in reporting the offences and supporting the prosecution, which ultimately led to Banks being brought to justice.

Detective Constable Sarah Scott of North Yorkshire Police’s City & Selby Criminal Investigation Department said: “Banks subjected his victim to a terrifying and traumatic ordeal, which she showed immense courage in reporting to police.

“Not only did he commit these serious offences, but he continued to deny them, only admitting his guilt on the first day of trial.

“In doing so, he caused further distress to the victim, who faced the prospect of reliving her experience in court.

“Despite the significant impact these offences had on her, the victim remained committed to seeking justice.

“She bravely provided detailed accounts to investigators and supported the prosecution throughout the case, helping to ensure Banks was held accountable for his actions.

“I hope this sentence provides some closure to her, and reassures other victims that sexual offences are treated with the utmost seriousness by both the police and the courts.”

Det Con Scott said the police were fully committed to supporting victims and bringing offenders to justice.

“If you have experienced any form of sexual offence or abuse, I would encourage you to come forward and report it,” she said.

“We will listen, we will support you, and we will investigate.”
