A LOGISTICS firm has won an award for transforming its financial operations.

Bradford-based Speedy Freight took home the Finance Transformation and Change (Up to £100m Turnover) title at the 2026 Finance Awards, which celebrate excellence across the profession and recognise organisations and individuals who are driving innovation, leadership and business performance.

James Pugh, chief finance officer at Speedy Freight, said: "This award is a fantastic recognition of the commitment, professionalism and ambition shown by our finance team every day.

"The transformation we've delivered has been a true team effort, driven by colleagues across the business who have embraced change and challenged us to continually improve.

"I'm incredibly proud of what we've accomplished together."

The company was shortlisted in three categories and won for its efforts to modernise financial systems, improve reporting, and streamline processes—boosting efficiency and business performance.

Mike Smith, CEO of Speedy Freight, said: "Awards like these are never about one individual or one team.

"They are the result of talented people being empowered to challenge, improve and innovate every day.

"Winning the Finance Transformation and Change award demonstrates what can be achieved when you invest in people, embrace technology and build a culture focused on long-term excellence."
