Here's how I rated the players and you can see how you scored them below.

## Ipswich Town

**Kjell Scherpen:** Desperately poor for Alexander Isak's early goal. Weak and meant Ipswich got off to the worst possible start. A couple of decent saves but the damage was done.**4Dara O'Shea:** Led from the front as always but Ipswich's defence struggled against the movement of Isak and the creation of Gakpo. He had a hard time.**5Issa Diop:** Towering figure always willing to indulge in the physical changes but this was a tough night after those two early Liverpool goals.**6Jacob Greaves:** Stern test against the inspired Cody Gakpo, showing the quality he will face in the Premier League.**5Leif Davis:** Tested defensively but was always competitive. Lent weight to attacks and had a chance with a far post header.**6Sasa Lukic:** Will bring experience to Ipswich Town but on this night Liverpool's midfield was too powerful.**5Exequiel Palacios:** Tried to bring some composure to Ipswich Town's game but was fighting a losing battle.**6Abdul Fatawu:** Pace but little end product. Energetic but erratic.**5Julio Enciso:** Ipswich's main threat. Tireless. Might have done more with the final ball but never gave up even when odds were against him.**7Daizen Maeda:** Anonymous. Made no impact at all and no surprise when taken off.**5Emersonn:** Battled hard but no luck against Van Dijk and Jacquet.**5Subs:Jack Clarke (on 66 Maeda):** Introduced when the game was lost.**5Zian Flemming (on 66, Emersonn):** Couple of bustling contributions.**5Kasey McAteer (on 79, Fatawu).Abdoul Outarra (on 78, Palacios).**

## Liverpool

**Alisson:** Lucky to get away with misjudging a first-half corner. The occasional anxious moment but no harm done.**6Ronald Araujo:** Strong in the air and on the ground on an impressive Premier League debut at right-back. Important interventions by being in the right place at the right time.**7Virgil van Dijk:** Usual unruffled self in the face of some frantic Ipswich Town attacking.**7Jeremy Jacquet:** Solid alongside Van Dijk. Continues to look a very fine acquisition.**6Milos Kerkez:** Occasionally troubled by Fatawu's pace but never in huge difficulties.**6Dominik Szoboszlai**: Driving runs from midfield and always willing to test the keeper. Iraola should never use him at right-back.** 7Alexis Mac Allister:** Still not quite at his best. Too many misplaced passes but never hides or shirks responsibility.**6Florian Wirtz:** Busy but occasionally lightweight. As so often, showed subtle touches that hint at good things to come.**6Alexander Isak:** The best of Alexander Isak at last. Running behind a defence with pace and clever movement with cool finishes. Finally showed why Liverpool bought him.**8Victor Munoz:** Willing and pacy out wide but could have delivered more end product.**6Cody Gakpo:** Could have been playing for Manchester City. How glad Liverpool are he wasn't. Two superb passes for Isak's opening goals, Truly outstanding performance.**9Subs:Bradley Barcola (on 64, Munoz):** Showed one or two flashes of the lightning pace the £123m signing will bring to Liverpool.**5Ryan Gravenberch (on 64, Isak):** Brought on to shut down any potential Ipswich Town revival.
