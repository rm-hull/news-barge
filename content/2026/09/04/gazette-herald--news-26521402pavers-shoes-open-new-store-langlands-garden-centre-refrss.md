Pavers Shoes is expanding its retail presence with a new store.

The footwear retailer has announced the opening of its latest branch at Langlands Garden Centre in Shiptonthorpe, with doors opening to customers on Thursday, September 17.

Darren Hanna, area manager at Pavers Shoes, said: "We’re delighted to be opening our new Pavers store at Langlands Garden Centre in Shiptonthorpe.

"It’s a fantastic location and gives us the opportunity to bring Pavers’ combination of quality footwear, great value and friendly, knowledgeable service to the local community.

"We’re really pleased to be joining that community and look forward to welcoming both existing Pavers customers and those discovering us for the first time."

To mark the launch, visitors to the Shiptonthorpe store will have the chance to enter a competition to win a free pair of shoes every month for a year.

The store will offer a range of footwear for men and women, including trainers, boots, wide-fit styles, and everyday shoes.

David Lazenby, property and concessions director at British Garden Centres, said: "We are delighted to welcome Pavers to Langlands Garden Centre, creating a fantastic new shopping experience for our customers.

"Pavers shares our commitment to quality, excellent service, and traditional British values, making them a wonderful fit for the Langlands family."

The new location aims to offer both style and comfort at affordable prices.

Mr Hanna said the store is expected to become "a valued part of the Langlands Garden Centre community for many years to come."
