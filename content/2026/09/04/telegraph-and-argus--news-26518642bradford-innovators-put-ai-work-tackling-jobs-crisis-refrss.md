YOUNG people gathered to tackle youth unemployment using artificial intelligence at a new event.

More than 70 attendees took part in the first-ever Bradford Buildathon, held on Sunday, August 30, at 50 Nelson Street in Bradford.

The hands-on event focused on AI and prompt engineering, giving participants the tools to create digital solutions aimed at addressing Bradford’s youth employment gap.

Nire Adetimehin, lead organiser of the event, said: "The day was proof that ideas can be turned into products.

"It's proof that young people in Bradford already have solutions to the problems around them.

"They just need the AI skills to bring those solutions to life and take a step ahead in the labour market."

Ms Adetimehin led a session on building AI-powered products before presenting the challenge of the day: using technology to help young people find mentors, work shadowing placements, and connections to professionals who could help launch their careers.

She created a sample Product Requirement Document (PRD) and built a live demo app, First Step, before setting participants to work in teams to develop their own solutions.

Teams spent an hour identifying barriers in the job market and refining AI-powered applications to address them.

Entries were judged by a panel including Oluwatimilehin Oni, software test manager at the NHS, Nelson Uzenabor, AI consultant at Multiverse, Grant Uzoanya, product owner, and Ms Adetimehin.

Submissions were evaluated on user value, product thinking, prototype quality, and creativity.

The winning team, Future Path, created a platform connecting young people to mentors and work shadowing opportunities.

Mr Uzoanya encouraged participants to keep developing their ideas.

He said: "This should not stop here.

"Participants should go ahead and build these products properly, maximise what AI can do, and keep finding solutions to the problems in their communities."

The event was supported by lead sponsor Lovable, with additional backing from DishPal.ai and RCCG, The Sanctified Church.

Volunteers and facilitators included Gabriel Oso (brand designer), Oluwatobiloba Afolayan (event photography), Oluwagbotemi Ige, Ezinne Okalanwa, Akinlose Israel, David Oregunwa, and Tobi Akanbi.

The Bradford Buildathon is part of a broader programme aiming to launch similar initiatives in other UK cities, equipping young people with practical AI and product development skills to improve their career prospects in a digital economy.

Planning for the next edition is already underway.
