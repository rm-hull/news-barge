BRADFORD cinema-goers have a packed Saturday of films, from family adventures to darker thrillers.

Two cinemas, Bradford Odeon and The Light, are showing a wide selection of films this Saturday, with options for children, families and adult audiences.

At Bradford Odeon, Spider-Man: Brand New Day is showing at 10.40am, 12.40pm, 2.10pm, 4.10pm, 5.40pm, 7.40pm, 9.10pm and 11pm.

The 12A film follows Peter Parker as he fights crime full-time in a world that does not remember him. The film has a runtime of two hours and 25 minutes. Adult prices start from £4, with web tickets listed at £4.

The Odyssey, rated 15, is screening at noon, 3.40pm, 5.20pm and 7.20pm. The mythic action epic was shot across the world using IMAX film technology.

It has a runtime of two hours and 53 minutes, with adult prices from £4 and web tickets listed at £4.

Horror film Insidious: Out of the Further has screenings at 4pm, 7pm, 9.30pm and 11.50pm.

Amelia Eve stars as Gemma, a young mother who discovers she can travel into The Further. The 15-rated film runs for one hour and 46 minutes. Adult prices start from £4, while web tickets are listed at £4.

The Death of Robin Hood, a darker reimagining of the classic tale, is showing at 2.10pm, 5.10pm and 8pm. The 15-rated film has a runtime of two hours and three minutes. Tickets are from £4 for adults, with £4 web tickets available.

Family films include The Super Mario Galaxy Movie at 9.30am. The PG-rated animated film follows the world of Super Mario Bros. and has a runtime of one hour and 38 minutes.

Adult tickets start from £1, with ODEON Kids screenings offering family adventures for as little as £1.

Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie has screenings at 9.40am, noon and 2.20pm. The U-rated film sees the pups crash land on an uncharted tropical island filled with dinosaurs. Adult prices start from £4 and web tickets are listed at £4.

Other Bradford Odeon listings include Minions & Monsters, Cars (20th Anniversary), Toy Story 5, Moana and Coyote vs. ACME. Adult prices for these films start from £4, with web tickets listed at £4.

Cars has screenings at 10am, 11.30am, 12.30pm and 3pm, while Toy Story 5 is showing at 9am, 10am, 11.30am, 2pm and 4.30pm.

Younger viewers can also see Bing & Friends: Birthday Celebration and CBeebies Musical: The Great Ice Cream Hunt, both with adult tickets priced from £1 and £1 web tickets. The Pout-Pout Fish is showing at 9.10am, with adult tickets from £4.

The Odeon programme also includes One Night Only, The Dog Stars, The End of Oak Street, Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets, Michael, Mirzapur: The Movie, Mutiny, Obsession, Rise of the Footsoldier: Retribution, Leviticus, Awarapan 2, Bol Bhavein Na Bol and Toxic: A Fairy Tale For Grown-ups (Hindi). Adult prices are listed from £4 for these films, unless otherwise stated.

At The Light, Spider-Man: Brand New Day is showing at 9.20am, 11am, 12.30pm, 2.10pm, 5.20pm, 6pm and 8.30pm. The Dog Stars is listed at 8.50pm, while The Odyssey has screenings at 11.30am, 4.15pm, 5.10pm and 8pm.

Family options at The Light include Minions & Monsters at 9.15am, 11.30am, 1.30pm and 3.40pm; Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie at 8.45am and 11.15am; Toy Story 5 at 8.50am, 1.45pm and 3.50pm; and Coyote vs. ACME at 9am, 12.10pm and 2.40pm.

The Light is also showing Insidious: Out of the Further at 8.50pm, The End of Oak Street at 9.10pm, Moana at 9.30am and 3.10pm, Obsession at 9.10pm, The Death of Robin Hood at 6.20pm and Terminator 2: Judgment Day (35th Anniversary) at 5.50pm.

Ticket prices and sale details for The Light are not specified in the listings provided.
