A MAN has been charged with a series of drug offences following an investigation by a police team targeting organised crime and drug supply on a Bradford estate.

Anthony Tidswell, 23, of St Margarets Avenue, Holme Wood, has been charged with being concerned in the supply of a Class A drug, namely cocaine, possession with intent to supply Class A drugs (cocaine), and possession of criminal property.

The charges follow enquiries by officers from Operation Sweetgill, a dedicated team focused on tackling organised crime and drug dealing in Holme Wood.

## Court appearance

Tidswell appeared before Bradford Magistrates' Court on Friday morning (September 4), where he was remanded in custody.

He is next due to appear at Bradford Crown Court on October 6.

## Team continues work in Holme Wood

West Yorkshire Police said Operation Sweetgill continues to work alongside partners through the Holme Wood Community Partnership in an effort to reduce crime and improve safety in the area.

The specialist team is dedicated to tackling organised crime groups and disrupting the supply of illegal drugs across the estate.

Police said the ongoing work forms part of wider efforts to make Holme Wood a safer place for residents and the wider community.
