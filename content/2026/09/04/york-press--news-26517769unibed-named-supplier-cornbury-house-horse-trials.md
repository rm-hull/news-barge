## South Milford bedding firm lands partnership with leading horse trials

Unibed, based in South Milford, will supply its high-performance equine bedding to Cornbury House Horse Trials *(Image: Unibed)*

A bedding manufacturer has been named the official supplier for one of the UK’s leading horse trials.

Unibed, based in South Milford, will supply its high-performance equine bedding to Cornbury House Horse Trials in Oxfordshire from September 9 to 13.

Jessica Vickers, commercial and operations director, said: "We are so excited to announce that UNIBED - High-Performance Equine Bedding is now the official bedding partner of the Cornbury House Horse Trials.

"For me, this partnership represents something bigger than simply supplying bedding.

"There’s still a lot of hesitation across the equestrian industry, particularly at events, about making a change from more traditional bedding options.

"When horse welfare is involved, quality and performance simply cannot be compromised."

A spokesperson for Cornbury House said: "We are delighted to be partnering with Unibed as our official bedding provider this year.

"UNIBED High-Performance Equine Bedding produces premium bedding solutions that combine exceptional performance with an unwavering commitment to sustainability."

Unibed is a family-run business rooted in agriculture, producing sustainable equestrian bedding from Yorkshire-sourced straw for both professional and everyday horse owners.

The company manages the entire production process, from harvesting to delivery.

Cornbury House Horse Trials features international and British Eventing classes, including the Howden Way Youth Championships.
