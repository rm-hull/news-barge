An historic North Yorkshire bridge is shut for the foreseeable future after being hit by a truck.

The collision caused severe damage to one of the decorative arches on the Grade II Middleham Bridge, which links Middleham and Leyburn.

Large sections of masonry fell into the roadway due to the impact.

North Yorkshire Council engineers inspected the bridge today (Friday) and found the bridge arch was too unstable for temporary repairs to be carried out to allow the road to reopen.

It will remain closed to vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists while a plan for repairs is developed.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/middleham-leyburn-bridge-damage-4-sept-2026-3-nyc.webp)

Council leader Cllr Carl Les said: “The damaged arch has not only lost sections of stonework, but has also become displaced creating a significant structural risk.

“Concrete barriers and fencing have been installed to secure the site and prevent access to the bridge to ensure the safety of all road users and pedestrians.

“We thank residents and motorists for their patience and understanding while this important safety work is carried out. Further updates will be provided as work progresses and more information becomes available.”

While the bridge remains closed, motorists are advised to use the following diversion routes:

- From the south towards Leyburn: A6108, B6268 and A684 (via Masham, Bedale and Leyburn)
- From the north towards Leyburn: A1 and A684 (A1, Bedale and Leyburn).

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/middleham-leyburn-bridge-damage-4-sept-2026-2-nyc.webp)

The closure comes ahead of the fourth stage of the Tour of Britain cycle race which is due to finish in Leyburn, near to Middleham, tomorrow (Saturday, September 5).

The event will take place as planned, but spectators are advised to allow extra time for their journeys and follow the signed diversion routes.

Due to the rolling road closure while the cyclists make their way to Leyburn, motorists are advised to expect delays so that the race can be completed safely.
