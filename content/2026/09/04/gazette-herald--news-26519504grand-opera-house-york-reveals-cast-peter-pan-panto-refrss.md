Panto season is on the horizon, and this year’s cast for the Grand Opera House York is ready to deliver a magical adventure.

The theatre has assembled its cast for The Further Adventures of Peter Pan: The Return of Captain Hook, set to run from December 5, 2026, to January 3, 2027.

Emmerdale and 42nd Street star Tom Lister leads the cast as Captain Hook, with returning favourite Jimmy Bryant as Smee.

Holly Atterton, known for Nick Jr Go Go Go!, takes on the role of Tinker Bell, while West End actor Joe Sleight plays Peter Pan.

Kirsty Sparks joins the cast as Ethel the Mermaid.

Martin Dodd, producer at UK Productions Ltd, said: "We are delighted that Kirsty Sparks will be making waves in York as the wonderfully named Ethel Mermaid.

"With her comic talents and infectious energy, she’s sure to be a brilliant addition to our brand-new 'Peter Pan' adventure."

Allie Long, theatre director at the Grand Opera House, added: "It was great seeing the cast assembled for the first time.

"Our audience will be in for a festive treat this Christmas with a panto packed full of laughs and chaos."
