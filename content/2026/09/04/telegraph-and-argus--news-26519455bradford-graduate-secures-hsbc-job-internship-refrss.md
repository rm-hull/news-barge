A GRADUATE landed a full-time job at a global bank after impressing during an internship.

Aliya Iqbal, a 23-year-old University of Bradford graduate, secured a permanent role with HSBC Group in corporate and investment banking technology following a successful placement with the company last year.

Ms Iqbal, who earned first-class honours in BEng (Hons) Software Engineering in July 2026, said: "During my internship, a huge part of the process was just mapping out how HSBC Group is structured globally, simply because it's such a massive organisation.

"Moving into global payment solutions in CIB Tech, I'm really looking forward to building on that experience and taking on new challenges."

Her graduate role, based between Canary Wharf and Sheffield, focuses on global payment solutions, supporting financial technology systems and banking platforms for international business transactions.

It follows an internship in financial crime prevention, where she developed database tools to help identify fraud.

Ms Iqbal began her academic journey with plans to study medicine but changed direction during her A-levels and the pandemic, switching to software engineering from clinical sciences.

She said: "As a kid, I used to take apart computers with my dad and put them back together.

"Eventually, I had to admit to myself that engineering was what I truly enjoyed.

"As much as I respected medicine, technology was where my future was."

Despite having no background in computer science, she embraced the challenge.

She said: "At first, I worried I’d struggle against students who had been coding for years, but the lecturers at Bradford were incredible.

"They went out of their way to make sure I had everything I needed to succeed.

"Bradford gave me the skills and confidence to build a career in something I love.

"I grew so much during my time at Bradford.

"The University backed me in every aspect of my life, and looking at where I am now, this was definitely the path I was meant to take."

The University of Bradford is currently offering places on computing and engineering programmes through clearing, including chemical, civil, mechanical, software, and biomedical engineering.

The BEng (Hons) Software Engineering degree is accredited by the British Computer Society for full Chartered IT Professional (CITP) status and partial Chartered Engineer (CEng) status.

Clearing runs until October 9.

The university’s clearing phone line is currently open on 0808 196 9129.

An on-demand Clearing Hub is also available, offering details on courses, virtual tours, application tips, and additional support.
