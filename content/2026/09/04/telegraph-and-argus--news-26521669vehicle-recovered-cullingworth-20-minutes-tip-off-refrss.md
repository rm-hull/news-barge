A stolen car was recovered just 20 minutes after police received a tip-off from a member of the public.

PCSOs Ditta and Masoud were at the Weymouth Avenue Contact Point on Wednesday, September 2, when a resident mentioned seeing a Facebook post in a Cullingworth community group about a silver Nissan Qashqai stolen during a burglary.

The eagle-eyed resident believed they had seen the vehicle driving past their home on the night it was taken.

Officers searched the surrounding area and located the stolen car by 11.20am, just 20 minutes after receiving the information.

West Yorkshire Police said: "This is a great example of how valuable information from the public can be.

"A quick conversation at a contact point, a vigilant resident, and some proactive policing helped lead to the recovery of a stolen car in just 20 minutes."
