A NIGHT of thrilling ocean adventure is coming to the big screen.

The Ocean Film Festival World Tour returns to the UK this autumn, bringing a fresh selection of short films that explore the beauty, power, and vulnerability of the sea.

It arrives at the Carriageworks Theatre, Leeds, on September 24.

Now in its 13th year, the festival features stories from above and beneath the waves, focusing on marine life, adventure, and conservation.

Nell Teasdale, UK tour director, said: "As we head into our 13th year, the Ocean Film Festival World Tour remains a celebration of the ocean in all its beauty, power and vulnerability.

"Each film in the 2026 programme will offer something special, whether it’s extraordinary adventure, breathtaking marine life or people dedicating their lives to protecting the sea.

"We can’t wait to bring this year’s line-up to audiences around the UK."

  Another image from one of the films 

*(Image: Ismaele Tortella)*

One of the standout films is The Raftsmen, which tells the story of 12 men from seven countries who attempted to cross the Pacific Ocean in 1973 on three handmade wooden rafts.

Their six-month journey, guided only by the sun and stars, became the longest raft expedition ever completed.

Ms Teasdale said: "It’s always incredible to see audiences come out year after year – not just to watch beautiful films, but to feel part of something bigger.

"The ocean connects all of us, and this event is a chance to celebrate that connection on the big screen."

The journey was documented on 16mm film, which has since been restored in 4K for modern audiences.

Other films include Granny Grommets, a nine-minute short about a group of elderly women who defy age – and the concerns of their children – to surf together each week.

Tides of Life, a 16-minute short film, profiles Richard Tesore and his work at Uruguay’s first marine mammal rescue and rehabilitation centre.

The film follows his efforts to care for injured penguins, sea lions, otters, and other marine life.
