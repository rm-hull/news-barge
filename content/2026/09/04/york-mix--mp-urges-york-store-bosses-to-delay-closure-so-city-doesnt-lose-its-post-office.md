York Central MP Rachael Maskell has contacted the owners of retailer TG Jones to urge them to keep the store open until a new home is found for the Post Office.

As ***YorkMix*** revealed on Wednesday, the TG Jones store on Coney Street is due to shut on 14 October, taking the city’s main Post Office with it.

Post Office bosses are looking for a new retail partner but say it could take six months to set up a replacement branch.

Ms Maskell led an unsuccessful campaign to stop the Post Office closing its Lendal HQ in 2019.

When it was relocated to what was then the WH Smith store, “I did question whether or not that was a long-term, sustained solution,” the MP told ***YorkMix***. 

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/rachael-maskell-westminster-portrait.webp)

“I was given assurances by the Post Office that it would be, and that they would ensure that that Post Office would continue to support our residents and businesses.

“And therefore I feel deeply disappointed that here we are today, just seven years on, with the the news that we’re going to lose our postal services.”

She said the remaining smaller branches on Colliergate and Micklegate were small and are “not going to have the capacity, particularly Colliergate in the city centre, to be able to support the demand placed upon them, so we do need to find resolution”.

TG Jones is owned by the private equity firm Modella Capital. It is shutting around 150 branches as part of a major restructuring.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/tg-jones-post-office-coney-street-york-closing-2.webp)

Now Ms Maskell has asked Modella Capital “if they would consider postponing that date in the light that we need to get a solution to this.

“Modella Capital itself is a private equity firm. I cannot see why postponing that date would have a negative impact on its ambition, other than clearly it is an organisation set up to to profit and asset strip.

“So, as a result of that, I want to ensure that we are securing the services for our city. But I do believe that the Post Office has got an obligation to come up with a resolution quickly.”

Ms Maskell raised the loss of the Post Office in the House of Commons yesterday, and secured a meeting over the issue with a business minister.

She added: “We’ve seen the banking sector close branches and the Post Office step in and and support that with the banking hubs.

“I want to see perhaps a reciprocal arrangement so that we could be able to see the banking sector supporting the deficit of a Post Office.

“But it’s absolutely clear we need to have that in place, not least because we know Christmas is coming.

“People right across our city really do depend on our postal services, and if those services aren’t there, it leads into a wider decline of our high street.”

We have contacted TG Jones asking about the York store closure but have yet to receive a response.
