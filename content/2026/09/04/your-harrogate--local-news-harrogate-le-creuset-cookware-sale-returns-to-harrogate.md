Le Creuset is bringing its biggest UK Factory to Table Sale back to Harrogate later this month.

The three-day event will take place at the Great Yorkshire Events Centre from Friday 25th until Sunday 27th September.

Visitors will be offered discounts of up to 50 per cent on Le Creuset cookware sourced from around the world.

The collection will include rare products and limited-edition colours which are not usually available in the brand’s UK boutiques or outlets.

Seasonal designs such as its signature pumpkin and petal casseroles will also be available.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a9aa566545af545e1d.jpg)


Organisers said the sale will feature Le Creuset’s largest-ever UK showcase of cast-iron cookware.

The event will run from 9.45am until 7pm on the Friday, 9am until 6pm on the Saturday and 9am until 5.30pm on the Sunday.

General admission tickets cost £10 and must be booked for a specific time slot.

Anyone aged under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.
