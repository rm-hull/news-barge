A MAN has been arrested after police executed a warrant at an address following information provided by members of the public.

Officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team carried out the warrant on Friday morning as part of an operation targeting suspected criminal activity.

During the search, officers seized a quantity of cash, cannabis and a significant number of cannabis vape products.

One male was arrested and enquiries remain ongoing.

In a statement, Keighley NPT thanked local residents for sharing information that helped officers take action.

The force said: "This morning, officers from Keighley NPT executed a warrant at an address in Ilkley following information received from the community.

"One male was arrested, and officers seized a quantity of cash, cannabis, and a significant number of cannabis vapes.

"Thank you to everyone who continues to share information with us. Your intelligence helps us take positive action against suspected criminal activity in our local communities."

Police have not released further details about the arrested man or any potential charges at this stage while investigations continue.
