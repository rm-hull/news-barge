## New shops and 800,000 summer shoppers - life at popular York shopping centre

Paul Tyler, manager of Designer Outlet York. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Designer Outlet York welcomed more than 800,000 shoppers during a busy summer season.

The centre reported strong trading results for July and August, with year-on-year growth in both footfall and sales despite challenges facing the wider retail sector and spells of hot weather.

August proved especially successful, with footfall rising by six per cent compared to the same month last year, and sales increasing in line with the uplift in visitors.

Paul Tyler, centre manager at Designer Outlet York, said: "We are incredibly pleased with our summer trading results, which underline the enduring appeal of Designer Outlet York as one of the region’s leading shopping and leisure destinations.

"The consistent growth we have seen in both visitor numbers and sales, even against a challenging retail backdrop, is testament to our strong and evolving brand mix, the engaging experiences we create for our guests and the dedication of everyone across the centre."

Sportswear led the centre’s category performance, followed by food and beverage, beauty, and homeware.

  The summer social proved very popular. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Mr Tyler said: "While we were sorry to say goodbye to Radley and Denby following their companies entering administration, we were delighted to welcome new brands Savante and R.M.Williams earlier this summer.

"Both brands have been very well received by our guests, with R.M.Williams also marking its first UK outlet store.

"Le Creuset has also relocated to a larger store next to Fred Perry near our West Entrance, while Kouzina Greek will be the next new opening in our first-floor food quarter.

"We look forward to announcing further additions as we move through autumn and towards Christmas."

The centre’s family-friendly events also played a key role in attracting visitors during the school holidays.

  Children enjoyed the attractions. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

The Great Summer Social, held near the South Entrance, returned with a free nine-hole mini golf course, sandcastle play zone, and outdoor games.

More than 4,000 families took part in the summer trail, which involved searching for hidden seagulls displayed in store windows throughout the school holidays.

Mr Tyler said: "The consistent growth we have seen in both visitor numbers and sales, even against a challenging retail backdrop, is testament to our strong and evolving brand mix, the engaging experiences we create for our guests and the dedication of everyone across the centre."

Designer Outlet York says it continues to attract shoppers from across the region and beyond.

With new brand openings, an expanding food and beverage offer, and more announcements expected in the coming months, the centre adds it enters the autumn and festive season with positive momentum.
