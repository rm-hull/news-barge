# Two arrested in murder probe after man found shot dead in Sheffield woods

## A murder investigation has been launched after a man was found dead with a gunshot wound in Sheffield woodland, with two people arrested.

Emergency services were called to Ecclesall Woods at 6.46pm on Wednesday (Sep 2) after a member of the public discovered a man's body.

Police were called to the woodland by the ambulance service.

Formal identification has yet to take place, but a forensic post-mortem examination has since established that the man died as a result of a gunshot wound.

Following initial enquiries, officers arrested a 34-year-old woman and a 36-year-old man on suspicion of murder in Manchester on Thursday (Sep 3).

Both remain in police custody for questioning.

A police scene remains in place in Ecclesall Woods, near Whirlowdale Road, while officers carry out further enquiries.

Police patrols are also taking place in the area and anyone with concerns or questions is encouraged to speak to officers.

Detective Chief Inspector Andy Knowles, the senior investigating officer, said: "This is a devastating incident which has led to a man losing his life and our thoughts remain with all those who knew him.

"Our investigation into this incident is in its early stages and we are continuing to work relentlessly to piece together the events leading up to this man's death.

"If you have any information which could help us please contact us immediately.

“Anyone with information is asked to contact police on 101 or online, quoting incident number 821 of September 2, 2026.

“Information can also be provided anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.”
