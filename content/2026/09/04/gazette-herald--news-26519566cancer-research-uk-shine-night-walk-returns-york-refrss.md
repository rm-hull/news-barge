A 10k night walk will once again light up the streets of York this September in support of Cancer Research UK.

The Shine Night Walk returns on September 26, inviting people of all ages and abilities to walk through York’s illuminated streets in honour, celebration or remembrance.

The event begins at Rowntree Park at 7pm and follows a route past some of the city’s best-known landmarks, including the Yorkshire Museum, Micklegate Bar, York Minster, and Fishergate Postern Tower.

Jose Ramos, Cancer Research UK’s spokesperson for the north of England, said: "Shine Night Walk is an uplifting and unforgettable evening where thousands come together to take on a unique challenge, make memories and make a difference for people affected by cancer.

"It’s not just the glowsticks and glitter that light up the night, it’s the hope everyone brings."

Funds raised will support research into cancer types of the participants’ choosing, from bowel cancer to childhood cancers.

Anyone who registers before September 7 can receive 30 per cent off the entry fee using the code SNWAUG26.

Volunteers are also needed to help with setting up the event and guiding walkers along the route.

To sign up or volunteer, visit shinewalk.org.
