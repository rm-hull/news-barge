  *UPDATE: In a statement issued this afternoon (Friday, September 4) a North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said a 45-year-old man has been arrested and thanked everybody who shared its appeal.*

A man wanted by police is believed to be in a specific area of North Yorkshire.

The county's force is appealing for sightings of a 45-year-old man from the former Hambleton area wanted for failing to attend court.

  


The North Yorkshire Police spokesperson added: "If you have an immediate sighting of him or know where he is now, please call us on 999.

"If you have any other information that could assist the search for him, please call 101.

"If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

"Please quote reference 12260157746 when passing on information."
