Six more people have been arrested after a man was shot in a suspected attempted murder in a quiet County Durham village.

The 46-year-old was shot at a home on Balmer Hill, Gainford, on the evening of Saturday, August 29.

He remains critically ill but stable in hospital after being hit by birdshot in the abdomen.

No one else at the property was injured.

  Police and CSI on the scene on Sunday (August 30) 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Police were called at about 8.10pm after reports that a car had arrived at the address and shots fired.

A 20-year-old man and a 16-year-old boy were initially arrested after the shooting.

Both have since been released while police carry out further enquiries.

The teenager was also arrested on suspicion of theft, failing to stop and driving without a licence.

  Police and CSI are at the scene of a house on Balmer Hill, Gainford 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

In an update today (September 4) police have now confirmed six more arrests have been made as the investigation continues.

Three men, aged 32, 31 and 26, have been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder.

A 31-year-old woman has also been arrested on suspicion of conspiracy to murder.

A 39-year-old man and a 16-year-old boy have been arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender.

All six remain in custody while further enquiries are carried out.

  Police and CSI are at the scene of a house on Balmer Hill, Gainford 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A red convertible Jaguar was also found abandoned around 50 minutes after the shooting.

Police in an appeal for information said they believe the soft-top F-type cabriolet was used in the suspected drive-by shooting.

Neighbours described hearing two loud pops before seeing a red soft-top car driving away.

  Police and CSI are at the scene of a house on Balmer Hill, Gainford 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

One resident said: "I was just sat watching the TV. They were talking about Newcastle on Sky Sports News, then we heard like a pop.

"At first I thought it was a car backfiring. We jumped.

"And then there was another pop, and it was a case of 'What the heck was that?' Then the next thing we saw was a red soft-top car go down the road.

"After the pops, I looked along towards next door and we saw all the glass that was outside, and the front windows were gone. It is something you see in movies or television.

"You don't expect to see it in a little village, in your own of street."

  Balmer Hill, Gainford 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

  Police and CSI are at the scene of a house on Balmer Hill, Gainford 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A police cordon remained in place on Sunday while forensic officers examined the scene.

Images from the scene showed holes in the front door, a smashed front window and broken glass scattered across the driveway.

Another neighbour, Jenny, who has lived on the estate since 1973, said: “It’s a very quiet estate usually.

"You never get anything like this.”

Durham Police said the investigation remains ongoing.

Anyone with information is asked to call 101, quoting incident reference 479 of August 29.
