## Tributes to 'legendary' York musician who played with Shed Seven

Jem Corner-Walker who has died aged 72. Image supplied

  *Tributes paid to York musician Jem Corner-Walker who has died aged 72*

MOVING tributes are being paid to a York "music legend" who played with Shed Seven and other city and British bands.

Jeremy Alan Corner-Walker – known as Jem – devoted much of his life to playing music and performing with a number of bands, including Shed 7, Casabooboo Buzz, Motel, Bad Habit, Toned F, Wofflers, Bogus Brothers, and The Goosehorns.

He even toured Ireland with the Bogus Brothers and worked with top artists such as Heather Small, Blue, and Girls Aloud as a sound engineer.

  Jem Corner-Walker wiht the Bogus Brothers. Photo supplied


Born on April 16, 1954, Jem spent his childhood living in many different places, including Nigeria and Ireland.

Jem, lately of Copmanthorpe, York, died on August 21 after a period of ill health.

  Jem with daughter Emily


He leaves behind his three daughters, Emily, Jessica (Jess) and Charlotte, and his five beloved grandsons, Riley, Alfie, Eddie, Archie and Teddy, as well as his sister Susan, brother Patrick, nieces, nephews and two great-nephews.

  Jem Corner-Walker (second from right) in Toned F. Photo supplied


His daughter Jess said her dad lived "courageously with Parkinson’s disease and dementia, fighting admirably until the very end". He passed away peacefully in hospital with Jess by his side.

Jess said her father's early years shaped his character.

"Dad worked as an insurance underwriter at NFU Mutual, before taking the brave step of leaving to follow his passion and create his own business, JSS Audio.

  Jem with Jess as a child


"It was a venture that combined his working life with his deep love of music and sound, something that remained a huge part of who he was."

She said he was a "wonderfully talented self-taught saxophonist" who devoted much of his life to playing music and performing.

She added: "Music was very much a family affair. In Toned F, Jem had the pleasure of playing alongside both of his brothers, Andrew and Patrick, as well as his great friend Tom Hume, who would later become his father-in-law.

  The late Jem Corner-Walker with his saxophone. Photo supplied


"His love of music never left him, and he made sure that his three daughters grew up surrounded by it too. He took his girls to festivals and gigs whenever he could, sharing with them the music and memories that meant so much to him."

Jess recalls meeting some of her childhood idols at these gigs, including Blue and Girls Aloud. "It was fantastic," she said.

  Jem Corner-Walker at one of his mixing desks. Photo supplied


And she remembers her dad meeting Olly Murs but having no idea who he was. "He went up to Olly Murs and asked him if he was in a boy band!"

She added: "Dad was an unforgettable character who brought music and laughter wherever he went. Above all, his family meant the world to him."

York musician John Fletcher, who runs the popular Facebook group York Bands of the Past with fellow city music man Rob Parker, paid this tribute Jem, who has been described as a York "music legend".

John told The Press: "It’s hard to run a Facebook group called York Bands Of The Past and not realise that a huge number of members have had their lives touched, enhanced and enriched by knowing Jem Corner-Walker.

  Jem Corner-Walker who has died aged 72. Image supplied


"There used to be an old joke about connecting to Kevin Bacon through just six people and I think the same is true in Jem’s case.

"If you were a York musician of any description between 1970 and 2015, you knew Jem as a performer, a friend, or a supplier of high-level PA systems and engineers.

"My own experience with smaller PAs brought us together in the 80s and 90s, and we worked the York scene a lot as he passed on small gigs to me, concentrating on larger and more lucrative ones.

  2003: Shed Seven's Rick Witter at Fibbers. The popular York band was among one of many associated with the city & music legend Jem Corner-Walker who has died aged 72.


"He supported the Band Blitz at the Barbican, and donated prizes of free PA to many Schools Battle Of The Bands.

"There are a small number of people involved in local music in York who can have the term 'legendary' applied to them. Jem was one of these, and the description was never more fitting."

Jem's funeral will take place at York Crematorium on Thursday, September 10 at 2.20pm, followed by refreshments at Bishopthorpe Spots and Social Club.
