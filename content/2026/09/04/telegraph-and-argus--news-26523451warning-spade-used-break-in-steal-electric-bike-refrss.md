A thief allegedly used a garden spade to break into a Bradford district home and steal an electric bike.

The burglary happened on Harry Lane, Oxenhope, between 8pm on Wednesday, September 2, and 8am the next day.

The suspect reportedly forced open a cellar door using the spade and cut a cable lock to steal the electrically assisted pedal cycle (EAPC).

Officers are appealing for information and are asking residents to check any CCTV that may have captured the suspect or the incident.

Anyone with footage or information is asked to call 101 quoting crime reference 13260501421.

Police warned residents not to leave garden tools lying around.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesman said: "Try to avoid leaving any garden tools lying around that burglars could use to help them force entry to your home, garages or sheds."
