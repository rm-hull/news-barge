THE Met Office has forecast a mix of sunshine and clouds for Bradford this weekend.

Saturday will start off partly cloudy, with temperatures around 14C.

Sunny intervals are expected from 12pm, with temperatures gradually rising to 15C by 4pm.

The chance of rain will be low throughout the day, peaking at 40 per cent at 11am.

Winds will be light, reaching up to 10mph in the afternoon.

The evening will see sunny intervals, with temperatures dropping to 12C by midnight.

Sunday will start off cloudy, with temperatures around 12C.

A light shower is expected at 11am, with a 50 per cent chance of precipitation.

The afternoon will see sunny intervals, with temperatures peaking at 22C by 3pm.

The chance of rain will decrease to 10 per cent by 5pm. The evening will be clear, with temperatures around 18C by 11pm.

Winds will be light throughout the day, reaching up to 7mph in the afternoon.
