Two care services have teamed up and raised hundreds with a charity football match.

Cygnet St William’s and Cygnet Victoria House, both based in Darlington, raised £463 in support of The Listening Post, a charity that helps young people with mental health and wellbeing needs.

Georgia Nelson, regional lead occupational therapist at Cygnet Health Care, said: "The match was a huge success – not just for the football on display, but for what it achieved.

"Thanks to the generosity and enthusiasm of both our staff and individuals we support, we raised an amazing amount for a wonderful local charity."

The match ended in a 1-0 win for Cygnet St William’s, with individuals supported by the services cheering from the sidelines.

Ms Nelson added: "We are so proud of those who organised such a brilliant event, and grateful to everyone who took part, donated, and supported the cause.

"Our fundraiser offered a real sense of giving back to and bettering the local community."

Located in Cornwall Avenue, Cygnet St William’s is a 12-bed neuropsychiatric service for men with acquired brain injuries.

Cygnet Victoria House, on Barton Street, is a 26-bed hospital for men with mental health needs.
