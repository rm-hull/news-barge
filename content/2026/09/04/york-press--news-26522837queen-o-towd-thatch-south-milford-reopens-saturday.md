A village gastropub which closed just over a week ago is to re-open tomorrow (Saturday).

The Queen O' T'Owd Thatch in South Milford announced its closure ‘with a heavy heart’ last Thursday.

A statement on social media said there were ‘many factors’ for this, including rising costs, summer being quieter than expected and an inability to find a manager.

The statement also thanked those who had supported the pub, calling it “incredibly sad” to close the pub until a “suitable tenant can be found.”

Yesterday (Thur), the pub announced “good news” that the pub would re-open its doors at 3pm today (Saturday).

The Facebook post continued: “We’re delighted to welcome Donna, who will be joining us as our temporary manager and helping us get the pub back up and running.

“For the time being, we will be operating on a wet sales only basis, so it will be drinks only while we get everything settled and moving in the right direction.

“We’re really grateful to everyone who has supported us, especially over the last few weeks. We know there has been a lot of uncertainty, and we really appreciate everyone’s patience and understanding.

“We’re looking forward to welcoming you all back, having a few drinks, and getting the pub feeling like a pub again!

“Please come along, say hello to Donna and show her your support as she gets started.”
