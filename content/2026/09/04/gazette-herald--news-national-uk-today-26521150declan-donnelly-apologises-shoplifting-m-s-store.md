Declan Donnelly has apologised after shoplifting from an M&S store this week.

The TV presenter, best known as one half of the presenting duo Ant and Dec, revealed the incident took place at Bridgewater Services following a visit to Cornwall.

Speaking on their podcast, Hanging Out with Ant and Dec, Donnelly said: "So we'll go in, pick our sandwiches, take it to the self-service checkout.

"So I start bleeping it – bleep, bleep, bleep.

"Put everything in the bag, bleep me Sparks card, and then I just walk off."

His co-host Ant McPartlin was quick to point out the error.

He said: "You didn't pay for it?!"

Donnelly replied: "I know.

"Put it all in me bag and wandered off."


## Declan Donnelly thought he was "going to get arrested" after shoplifting incident

He only realised the mistake as he started eating his sandwich.

Donnelly said: "As I tucked into me egg and maple bacon sandwich.

"With a mouthful of egg, I went, I haven't paid for this."


He tried to contact the services to apologise but was unable to get through and instead called his office.

Donnelly, speaking to the office, said: "I'm really sorry, I have just shoplifted from Bridgewater Services.

"My heart rate is slightly raised.

"I think I'm going to get arrested, because they know it was me."
