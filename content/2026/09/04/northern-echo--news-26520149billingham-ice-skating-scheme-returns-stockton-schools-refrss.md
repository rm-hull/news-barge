A school ice skating programme in Stockton is returning for a second year.

Tees Active has relaunched its Schools Ice Skating Initiative, giving secondary students free entry to Billingham Forum Ice Arena’s Friday night disco sessions.

Running from September 2026 to May 2027, the scheme offers up to 200 complimentary tickets each week to one secondary school.

Louise Wilson, assistant principal of Thornaby Academy, said: "Initiatives like this are incredibly valuable for schools.

"With many young people not currently meeting recommended activity levels, opportunities like this help us engage students in a positive and rewarding way, while supporting attendance, encouraging positive behaviour and promoting wellbeing.

"It also gives students the chance to spend time with their friends, try something new and build confidence outside of the classroom."

Schools can use the tickets to reward achievement, encourage attendance, or offer enrichment opportunities.

The programme supports Tees Active’s wider aim to improve young people’s physical and mental health through fun, community-based activities.

First launched in September 2025, the scheme proved popular with schools, helping thousands of students try ice skating for the first time.

Many schools reported that the sessions boosted confidence, social interaction, and physical activity outside the classroom.

Leon Jones, managing director of Tees Active, said: "We are delighted to be bringing the Schools Ice Skating Initiative back for a second year.

"As a charitable leisure trust, we are committed to creating opportunities that support the health and wellbeing of young people and strengthen our partnerships with schools across Stockton-on-Tees.

"The feedback from schools during the first year was extremely positive, and we look forward to welcoming more young people to our popular Friday night sessions over the coming months."

Schools and colleges will be contacted directly by Tees Active with details about their allocated sessions and ticket distribution.

More information is available at www.forumicearena.co.uk/pulse-school-initiative/.
