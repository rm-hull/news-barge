## Life-saving equipment donated to Burton Constable parkland team

A defibrillator and five water rescue throw lines have been donated to the Burton Constable parkland team *(Image: Burton Constable Foundation)*

A defibrillator and five water rescue throw lines have been donated to the parkland team.

The life-saving equipment was donated to Burton Constable by Humberside Search and Rescue (HUMBSAR) and HFR Solutions Community Interest Company.

The defibrillator will be kept in the parkland team’s vehicle, ensuring it can be quickly transported anywhere across the 330-acre estate in an emergency.

Alasdair Hutson, chief executive of the Burton Constable Foundation, said: "We’re very grateful to HUMBSAR and HFR Solutions CiC for this generous donation.

"With 330 acres of Parkland to look after, having this equipment close at hand and able to be taken quickly wherever it may be needed is incredibly valuable.

"The training provided to our team has also been a great addition, helping us to be better prepared should the equipment ever be needed."

The five throw lines will improve the team’s ability to respond safely to water-related emergencies on the estate.

Staff received hands-on training during the handover, ensuring they are confident and prepared to use the equipment if needed.

The donation was made possible through the involvement of Burton Constable’s Wildlife and Parkland Officer with HUMBSAR, a volunteer lowland search and rescue team operating throughout the Humber region.
