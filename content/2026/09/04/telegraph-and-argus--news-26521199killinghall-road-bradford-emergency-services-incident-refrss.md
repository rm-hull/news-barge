EMERGENCY services scrambled to a major Bradford road last night.

It is understood a collision involving a vehicle and a pedestrian took place on Killinghall Road.

Pictures on social media show the road cordoned off on Thursday evening.

Police vehicles and ambulances can also be seen.

The T&A has approached West Yorkshire Police for a comment.
