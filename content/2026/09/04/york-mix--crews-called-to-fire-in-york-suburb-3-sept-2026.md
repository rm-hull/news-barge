Fire crews were called to an incident in a York suburb last night (Thursday).

They went to **Crossfield Crescent** at**8.50pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Both York and Acomb’s crews responded to a fire in a residential property due to a mechanical fault on a fan.

#### Crossfield Crescent, York

“The fire was out on the arrival of crews. A thermal imaging camera was used to scan for any remaining hotspots.

“Advice was given to the occupiers.”
