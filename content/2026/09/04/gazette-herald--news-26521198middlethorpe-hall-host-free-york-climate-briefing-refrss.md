A free event is set to highlight the urgent need for action on climate and nature crises.

The session is set to take place at the Barlow Room in Middlethorpe Hall & Spa on Tuesday, September 8th, 2026.

The Lord Mayor, Councillor Margaret Wells, will be present to hear from experts about the urgent need for action on climate and nature crises.

The event is part of a wider campaign launched by the National Emergency Briefing, which is now urging the government to hold a televised national briefing to inform the public about the situation.

The briefing follows a similar event held in November 2025, where ten leading UK experts briefed more than 1,200 politicians and leaders from various sectors, outlining the implications of climate and nature breakdown on health, food systems, national security, and the economy.

Lionel Chatard, director and general manager of Middlethorpe Hall, will host the session and introduce a short film produced as part of the National Emergency Briefing.

The film aims to highlight the threats posed by climate change.

Attendees will then hear from academics and experts sharing their perspectives on the challenges faced, ongoing actions, and potential future steps.

There will also be an open discussion for guests to share their thoughts and questions.

The event, which starts at 6.30pm, is supported by GoodGym York and the York Hoteliers’ Association.

Due to limited numbers, attendees are urged to reserve their place for free.
