FACIAL recognition cameras have identified more than 80 wanted suspects since their rollout began.

West Yorkshire Police has been using live facial recognition (LFR) cameras since November 2025, including recent deployments in Wakefield.

The technology scans faces in real time and matches them against a watchlist of individuals wanted by police.

Chief Superintendent Stuart Bainbridge, District Commander of Wakefield Police, said: "Live Facial Recognition is an incredibly useful tool and this latest deployment in Wakefield has assisted our officers in keeping communities safe.

"The cameras are there to pick out persons already logged on police systems as wanted by the courts, wanted for arrest or as posing a risk to the public."

The system has led to the detection of 82 wanted individuals, including suspects wanted for serious domestic and retail offences.

It has also helped locate missing and vulnerable people, and allowed officers to check 95 individuals subject to Sexual Harm Prevention Orders.

Councillor John Thomas, deputy leader of Wakefield Council, said: "After speaking directly with the police, it’s clear this is not a system of mass surveillance.

"The technology is used only to identify individuals already on police watchlists."

Images of the general public are deleted almost instantly.
