## 5 'lost' restaurants from York's eating-out scene we miss – in photos

The Blue Bicycle Restaurant, Fossgate, York..

  *5 'lost' restaurants of York we still miss today – in photos*

TODAY we are heading down memory lane and sharing photos of some of York's lost restaurants.

We are focussing on York's Fossgate and Walmgate, one of the city's dining hot spots.

  Le Cochon Aveugle, in Walmgate, is now the Little Asia Korean restaurant


For years, one of the most popular city restaurants was the Blue Bicycle, in an idyllic spot on Foss Bridge. It is now Polish eaterie The Blue Barbakan.

Directly opposite, the former Loch Fyne seafood restaurant is Marzano Italian.

At the top of Fossgate, Rish (which later became J Baker's Bistro Moderne) is now the Everest Gurkha restaurant.

  Rish in Fossgate - now the Everest Gurkha Nepalese Restaurant


And still on Fossgate, Harville's steakhouse is now the Indian restaurant, Mumbai Lounge.

The upmarket Le Cochon Aveugle in Walmgate is now Little Asia, a great place to eat Korean food.

  Harvilles in Fossgate - now Mumbai Lounge
