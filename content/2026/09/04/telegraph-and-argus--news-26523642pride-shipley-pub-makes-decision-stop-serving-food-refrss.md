A Bradford pub has closed its kitchen for good after food service was deemed “financially unsustainable.”

The Pride of Shipley, located in Market Square, Shipley, made the announcement on Facebook, explaining the decision to stop serving food was based on low demand and high costs.

A spokesperson said: "After one final hurrah, we brought food back following requests from our lovely customers.

"Unfortunately, it still hasn’t proved popular enough to make it financially sustainable.

"With the cost of ingredients and the amount of food going to waste, we’ve made the difficult decision to hang up the apron and close our kitchen for good.

"We genuinely appreciate everyone who supported us and enjoyed a meal with us."

“We gave it our very best shot, but sometimes you have to accept when something simply isn’t working.

“The Pride of Shipley remains very much open for great drinks, brilliant offers, live sport, entertainment and the friendly atmosphere you know and love.

“Thank you for your understanding and continued support.”
