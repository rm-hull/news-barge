Pubs will donate 5p from every pint of Carling sold this September to support food banks in their communities.

The scheme is part of Proper Pubs’ annual Heroes of Hunger campaign, in partnership with Carling, and aims to tackle food poverty by raising funds and collecting essential food donations.

Nathan Wall, managing director at Proper Pubs, said: "Championing community initiatives such as local foodbanks continues to sit at the heart of everything we do at Proper Pubs.

"This support remains crucial during a challenging time for many people across the UK as cost-of-living pressures continue to impact many families and individuals."

The campaign will run throughout September and includes pubs across England, Scotland, and Wales.

Last year, the campaign raised more than £15,000 and enough food to feed 1,300 people for a week.

Proper Pubs is the community-focused, wet-led division of Admiral Taverns, and operates more than 200 pubs across the UK.
