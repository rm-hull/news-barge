A Saltburn play café is offering Teesside emergency service workers free sessions with their young children.

The Nook, an independent play café on Station Street in Saltburn, launched the sessions after recent events across Middlesbrough and Teesside have seen emergency services under increased pressure.

The sessions aim to give first responders calm, quality time with their families during a difficult period.

  The Nook in Saltburn. 

*(Image: The Nook)*

After several tragic incidents, including the A66 crash on August 22 that killed two police officers and five young men, Cleveland Police brought in 200 extra officers to help relieve some of that pressure.

Owner Becky Lewis said: "With everything that's happened recently, I've been thinking a lot about the people in our emergency services who are there for us during circumstances most of us can't even begin to imagine.

"It's only a small gesture, but it's something I can do. I'd like to offer free play at The Nook to members of our emergency services and their little ones."

  The Nook in Saltburn. 

*(Image: The Nook)*

She added: "If they could use a little time somewhere calm with their family, a change of scenery or simply an hour or two of normality, I'd love to welcome them.

"Whether that's now or further down the line, the offer is there.

"Hopefully The Nook can provide a little bit of light when it's needed."

  The Nook in Saltburn. 

*(Image: The Nook)*

Emergency service workers with children aged five and under can contact The Nook to book a free session, subject to availability.

The Nook opened their doors on Station Street in April, and was designed as a quieter alternative to traditional soft play centres.

Babies and young children can enjoy interactive play while parents and carers have freshly prepared food, homemade bakes and coffee in the café.

Becky said she hoped the initiative would give families time together away from the pressures of work and daily life.

The play café caters for babies and children aged 0-5 and has become a popular addition to Saltburn town centre since opening.

Becky, a former journalist, launched The Nook after noticing a gap in local child-friendly venues where parents could relax while their children played.
