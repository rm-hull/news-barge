A celebration of seasonal food and homegrown produce is coming to RHS Garden Harlow Carr this weekend.

Flavours of Autumn will take place on Saturday 5th and Sunday 6th September.

The two-day event will feature live cookery demonstrations from chefs and nutritionists, alongside talks offering practical advice on growing food at home.

Visitors will also be able to join guided tours of the Kitchen Garden and ask RHS experts for gardening advice.

Local food and drink producers will sell ingredients and seasonal produce, while a range of street food will be available throughout the weekend.

Guided cookery sessions will also be held for families.

Live music will be provided by Chicago Joe and the All Stars on Saturday and Leo Hicks on Sunday.

*The event is included with normal garden admission. Find out more**here**.*
