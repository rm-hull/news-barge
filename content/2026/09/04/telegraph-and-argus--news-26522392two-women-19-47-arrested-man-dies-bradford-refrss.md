TWO women, aged 19 and 47, were arrested after a man died in a collision in Bradford.

A detective inspector has said it was a "very serious incident" on Killinghall Road last night.

Emergency services scrambled to the scene near Bradford Moor Park just before 9pm and sadly a man was pronounced dead at the scene.

The women were arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving and remain in custody at this time.

## What police have said

Detective Inspector Paul Conroy of the Major Collision Enquiry Team said: “We are investigating the exact circumstances of the collision and enquiries remain ongoing into this very serious incident which has sadly resulted in a man losing his life.

"Our thoughts are with the victim and would like to speak with anyone who saw or has footage of the incident.”

Anyone with information can contact The Major Collision Enquiry Team (MCET) by calling 101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting log 1622 of 3/9.
