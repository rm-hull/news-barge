A YORK woman has spoken of her shock after her Norse Atlantic Airways flight to New York was forced to make an emergency stop on the runway.

Press reporter Alice Kavanagh, originally from Darlington, was onboard Norse Atlantic Airways flight Z0701, bound for New York’s John F Kennedy International Airport (JFK), from London Gatwick Airport at 1.05pm on Friday (September 4).

The 30-year-old described how her plane had ground to a sudden halt after gaining reported speeds - according to The Aviation Herald - of 138mph for an anticipated take-off.

  


All flights at the airport were brought to a standstill for nearly an hour as a result of the plane being stuck.

A Gatwick spokesperson said the runway was closed on Friday afternoon because of a "technical issue with a departing vehicle".

The airport said the runway was shut for 50 minutes while the plane was towed away before reopening shortly afterwards. Some flights were diverted to other airports including London Stansted and Birmingham.

Alice said: "Honestly, the whole situation was pretty scary.

“We were starting to speed up for take off and all of a sudden, the pilot slammed the brakes on the plane.

“It took of a few minutes then they explained that they’d spotted a technical fault."

  Press reporter Alice Kavanagh at the airport 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

Alice said passengers remained in their seats while engineers tried to understand the nature of the fault.

Emergency services, including fire and rescue crews, were called to the runway.

Alice said: "It’s so lucky that the pilot spotted it.

“We couldn’t even drive off the runway - god knows what would happen if we’d taken off.”

Alice praised the "fantastic" crew aboard the plane, who she said did a "great job" at keeping everyone calm.

  “We were starting to speed off for take off and all of a sudden, the pilot slammed the brakes on the plane" 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

A London Gatwick spokesperson told ITV: "The runway was closed for 50 minutes this afternoon (Friday, September 4) due to a technical issue with a departing aircraft.

"The aircraft was towed from the runway, which reopened shortly afterwards following a full safety inspection."

Alice and passengers of the Z0701 flight are now in the airport waiting for a new plane to New York, which is expected to be ready in an hour and a half.
