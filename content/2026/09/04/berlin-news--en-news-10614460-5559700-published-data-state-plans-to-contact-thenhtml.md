“If individual affected persons are identified during the analysis, they will be notified by the relevant Senate departments on a risk-based basis and in accordance with legal requirements,” according to a statement issued by the Senate Chancellery on Friday. Citizens who, independently of this, become aware “that their data has been published or is being used for criminal offenses such as fraud or identity theft should file a criminal complaint.”
