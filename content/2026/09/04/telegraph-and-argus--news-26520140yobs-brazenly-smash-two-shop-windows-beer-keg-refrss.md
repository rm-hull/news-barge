A SHOP owner in the Bradford district has spoken of their disgust after yobs smashed two windows with a beer keg.

UpCycle, based on Westgate in Shipley, discovered the damage over the bank holiday weekend.

The shop, which refurbishes and sells bicycles while supporting vulnerable young people and people in prison, has since launched a fundraising appeal to help cover the costs.

West Yorkshire Police is investigating an attempted burglary at the location.

Anyone who witnessed suspicious activity in the area is asked to contact police via Live Chat at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat or by calling 101.

  One of the windows damaged during an attempted burglary at UpCycle's shop on Westgate, Shipley. 

*(Image: UpCycle)*

## Damage discovered after bank holiday

Mark Flannagan, owner of UpCycle, said he first heard about the incident when a colleague noticed something was wrong at the shop.

Mr Flannagan said an empty beer keg had been thrown through two windows.

He told the Telegraph & Argus: "A member of staff who normally looks in on the shop was passing and noticed the window had been damaged.”

Another member of staff, Suzanne, spent the day liaising with police, forensic officers and glaziers to secure the premises.

Mr Flannagan said: "She gave up a bank holiday to deal with everything, making sure the shop was secured and sorted out. That's what small charities do.

"When there's a problem, people step up and help."

  UpCycle staff member Suzanne stands inside the charity's Westgate shop in front of a boarded-up window damaged during an attempted burglary. 

*(Image: UpCycle)*

## Charity hit with costly repairs

The incident is particularly frustrating for the charity as one of the damaged windows had only recently been replaced.

Mr Flannagan said: "We'd literally just had one of the large panes replaced a couple of weeks ago because it had a chip in it.

"Now we've had to commission another replacement.

"It's all in hand but it's a significant cost for a small charity."

The organisation estimates repairs will cost around £1,000, although it has budgeted up to £1,500 in case further work is required.

Mr Flannagan said: "When you're a small charity, that's a big hit.

"The money is going to have to come out of charitable funds and we'd obviously rather be spending it on our work."

## Community rallies around charity

  Damage to a window at UpCycle in Shipley after an attempted burglary, which has left the charity facing repair costs of around £1,000. 

*(Image: UpCycle)*

A fundraising appeal launched on social media has already received more than £300 in donations.

Mr Flannagan said he had been touched by the response.

"We've already had donations coming in and people have been incredibly supportive," he said.

## 'Not a reflection on Shipley'

Despite the incident, Mr Flannagan said he did not believe it reflected the wider community in Shipley.

He said: "I don't think it's a reflection of Shipley at all.

"I love coming to Shipley and people are always lovely when they visit us.

"We get all sorts of people coming into the shop, lots of families with children, and we've always found the community really welcoming.

"It's a shame that this has happened, but I don't think it reflects the place we work in."

## 
  **Police investigation underway**

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are investigating an attempted burglary at a business on Westgate in Shipley.

"Unknown suspects have smashed a window to try and get into the property sometime between 3pm on August 28 and 8am on August 31.

"Enquiries are ongoing by Bradford Police."
