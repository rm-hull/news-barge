There’s a new boss at the helm of a popular pub near Darlington following an “eye catching” £415k facelift.

The Oak Tree Inn, in Middleton St George, finally reopened its doors to punters in May following a huge refurbishment to bring the local back to its roots.

The venue, which had been shut since August 2025, had its traditional character restored to create a countryside-inn feel alongside a refreshed beer garden.

And today, months after it reopened to the public, pub chain owner Punch Pubs and Co has confirmed there is a new manager at the helm.

Morgan Stanton, who has almost 30 years of experience in the hospitality industry, has taken the reigns of the popular village pub.

  Inside The Oak Tree at Middleton St George 

*(Image: PUNCH PUBS)*

He was most recently a director of food and beverage at a leading leisure business, overseeing operations across multiple sites in the UK and Europe.

Following redundancy, Morgan said it was “the time was right for me to do my own thing” and decided to become a management partner with Punch.

“I’d visited the pub half a dozen times over a two-week period and, each time I came, I could see the potential. I knew this was the right pub for me,” he said.

“I want to run a business that thrives. We’ll say yes when we can, and hospitality will always be front and centre of everything we do.

  Inside The Oak Tree at Middleton St George 

*(Image: PUNCH PUBS)*

“My ambition is for the pub to become a real hub for the community - somewhere people feel welcome, somewhere they want to spend time, and somewhere that can accommodate whatever they need.

“I can’t wait to welcome people through the door - locals, regulars and people from further afield. I’m really excited about what we can build here.”

  Inside The Oak Tree at Middleton St George 

*(Image: PUNCH PUBS)*

Between 1pm and 6pm the pub will play host to live music, a magic act and party food and Morgan has encouraged people to visit and say hello.

Julie Richardson, operations manager at Punch Pubs, said: “I am really excited to work with someone who has as much experience as Morgan does within the hospitality industry.

“Morgan is keen to share his knowledge and skills with his staff and to make The Oak Tree Inn a roaring success.

“The pub reopened a couple of months ago following a refurbishment and it looks great. Since Morgan arrived the feedback from our guests has been terrific so I am confident we can continue on an upwards trajectory and give the community the pub it craves.”
