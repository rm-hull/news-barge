TWO teenagers have been arrested following the alleged theft of an RNLI quad bike from a North Yorkshire coastal town.

The incident took place in Whitby promenade at around 5.20pm on Saturday (August 29).

North Yorkshire Police said officers had arrested two teenage boys for the theft of a motor vehicle.

  


The pair have since been released on conditional bail while police enquiries remain ongoing.

Efforts to locate and recover the quad bike continue.

A police spokesperson said: "If you have any information that can help our investigation, you can call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

"Please quote 12260169389 when passing on information."
