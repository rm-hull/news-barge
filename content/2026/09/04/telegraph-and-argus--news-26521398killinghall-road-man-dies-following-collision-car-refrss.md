A MAN has died following a collision with a car in Bradford.

Emergency services scrambled to Killinghall Road on Thursday evening just before 9pm.

Two people have been arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving.

They remain in custody at this time, the force have confirmed.

  A crime scene investigation van 

*(Image: Gazz Hall)*

Pictures on social media show the road was cordoned off.

Police vehicles and ambulances could also be seen.

When the T&A visited the scene this morning, the road had reopened.

  Remnants of police tape on a gate opposite Bradford Moor Park 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## What have police said?

A spokesperson for the force said: “Police were called to reports of a road traffic collision at around 8.56pm last night (September 3) on Killinghall Road, Bradford.

“Sadly, a man has died after being involved in a collision with a car.

“Two people have been arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving and remain in custody at this time.

“Enquires remain ongoing to establish the full circumstances of what took place.

“The Major Collision Enquiry Team (MCET) would like to speak to anyone who saw or has dashcam footage of the collision. Anyone with information can contact MCET by calling 101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting log 1622 of 3/9.”

A spokesperson for the Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "Ambulance resources responded to a collision between a car and a pedestrian on Killinghall Road in Bradford last night (September 3)."
