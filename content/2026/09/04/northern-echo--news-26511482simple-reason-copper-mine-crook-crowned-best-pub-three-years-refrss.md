A County Durham pub whose owner says "customers are friends and staff family" is celebrating after winning a Best Pub award for the third year in a row.

The Copper Mine pub in Crook has been crowned The Northern Echo Best Pub 2026 after winning the title in both 2024 and 2025.

Owner Anne Binks said she was "over the moon" after the latest victory, praising staff and customers for their support.

  Best Pub 2026 winner The Copper Mine, Crook pictured Jess Rowell, Anne Binks (owner) Scott Brunning, Cheryl Carson, Jess Littlefair and Liv King 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

She said: "We are all over the moon that we have won for a third year running..how amazing!

"What an achievement by all of the staff, both front of house and all those working behind the scenes.

"They all put a huge effort in and a tremendous amount of work in at The Copper Mine , all of them being so important."

The pub, at 26 Job’s Hill Bank, was named among the ten finalists after readers nominated their favourite local in The Northern Echo Best Pub 2026 competition.

Anne said the team was "so, so grateful" to everyone who voted.

She said: "They and myself would like to say a huge thank you to all the people who voted for us, we are so, so grateful. Their support is incredible."

  Best Pub 2026 winner The Copper Mine, Crook pictured Jess Rowell, Anne Binks (owner) Scott Brunning, Cheryl Carson, Jess Littlefair and Liv King 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The victory comes after the pub had already secured the title in the previous two years.

Anne, who has run The Copper Mine for 11 years, previously described it as a "proper family, community-oriented pub", with staff referring to themselves as the "famalam".

Now the pub is turning its attention to giving back to the community, with a charity cancer morning planned for September 25.

Anne said the event will feature more than 100 tombola prizes, alongside raffle prizes and other stalls.

She said: "A charity cancer morning on September 25 which we have planned has some amazing raffle prizes and over 100 other prizes on the tombola and other stalls which again have been donated by our customers."

  The Copper Mine in Crook 

*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

She added: "All willing to help such a great cause as so many people have been touched by cancer."

Anne said the response from customers showed just how strongly the pub's community is behind it.

She said: "How lucky are we to have such loyal and kind customers! Thank you once again to everyone, absolutely delighted."

The Copper Mine describes itself as a family-run pub on the edge of Weardale, serving home-cooked food ranging from traditional pub meals to restaurant-style dishes, alongside cask ales and house cocktails.

The pub's latest victory makes it a three-time Northern Echo Best Pub winner.
