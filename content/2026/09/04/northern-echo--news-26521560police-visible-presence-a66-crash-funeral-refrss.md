Police have said they will have a visible presence at an A66 crash funeral amid concerns over its impact and school closures.

  The funeral of Jakub Matusiak - one of the __five people in the Volkswagen Passat who died__ in the crash on August 22 - is set to take place in South Bank this afternoon (September 4). 

  The incident, which involved a marked Cleveland Police vehicle and the Passat, which was travelling the wrong direction, also took the lives of __PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough.__

  Pc Matthew Blades, 37, and Pc Tom Clough, 38 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

Concerns have been growing over the funeral since yesterday after schools felt they had no option but to shut due to fears about road disruption or potentially dangerous behaviour.

Archway Academy Teesville, South Bank Primary School, Outwood Academy Normanby and St Margaret Clitherow’s Catholic Primary School are among those shutting early, as well as Normanby Medical Centre on Normanby Road.

And now, Cleveland Police has warned of traffic disruption as a result of the funeral, which is taking place at St John the Evangelist Church on Normanby Road.

  Two police officers are among seven people who have died following a devastating crash on the A66 in Teesside 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

Superintendent John Wrintmore said any decisions to close schools has not been as a result of a police recommendation and said there would be a proportionate visible police presence.

He said: “Some potential traffic disruption and delays are expected from 3pm through to around 5pm on September 4, due to a funeral taking place in the area.

“This will affect areas of South Bank, Normanby Road through Teesville, Ormesby and Marton, and Acklam Road.

“We are aware of some school closures in the area and have liaised with partners in the local authorities regarding the expected disruption today.

“Any decisions made by schools or other services to close has been a decision made by those organisations in isolation, which has not been a police recommendation.

“We are working closely with highways departments from the local authorities, who are leading on the traffic management for this event, in order to minimise disruption.

“There will be a proportionate visible police presence in the affected areas to ensure public safety, in line with our plans for any significant gathering.

“Should anyone have any concerns, please speak with officers in the area.”

Several politicians have raised concerns over the disruption, with Education Secretary Lucy Powell saying there were “huge anxieties and worries in the community” earlier today.

  Lucy Powell 

*(Image: Richard Townshend/UK Parliament/PA Wire)*

She said it is a “very difficult and delicate situation” and has been in touch with local MPs and the Home Office to “calm those tensions down and make the streets safe”.

She said she trusted headteachers to make the judgments they need to keep communities safe, adding: “I hope this doesn’t have to happen again.”

Stockton West MP Matt Vickers argued that schools “should never be forced to close because a safe journey home cannot be guaranteed”.

Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council leader Alec Brown also said it is “awful” that children’s education is being disrupted and said any dangerous riding, reckless driving, or antisocial behaviour is “not acceptable”.

  Jakub Matusiak, 23. 

*(Image: SOCIAL MEDIA)*

A spokesperson for the council said the decision to close schools early followed discussions between local headteachers and the authority over anticipated traffic and transport disruption.

They said the decision was made after "careful consideration of the potential impact on home-to-school transport and the departure of pupils, staff and families at the end of the school day".

St Peter’s is also expected to close early today while Mackenzie Thorpe School will be shut all day.

A previous memorial for Matusiak near the crash site, featuring orange balloons, fireworks and masked bikers, sparked mixed reactions within the local community over the tone of the event.

Police investigations into the circumstances of the crash are ongoing - and since the incident, 27 people have been arrested in a crackdown on serious and organised crime.

Earlier this week, a fundraiser launched for the families of the two Cleveland Police officers reached its target, surpassing £1.2 million.
