## York college celebrates accreditation for veterinary nursing degree

Askham Bryan College’s Foundation Degree in Veterinary Nursing has secured full accreditation *(Image: Darren Casey)*

A veterinary nursing degree has secured full accreditation from the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons (RCVS).

Askham Bryan College’s Foundation Degree (FdSc) in Veterinary Nursing has been formally recognised by the RCVS, confirming it meets the rigorous professional standards required by the veterinary industry.

The accreditation enables graduates to apply for entry to the RCVS Register of Veterinary Nurses and begin practising as Registered Veterinary Nurses (RVNs).

Claire Pullan, head of veterinary nursing at Askham Bryan College, said: "This is a fantastic achievement for our veterinary nursing team and a proud moment for University Centre Askham Bryan.

"Achieving full RCVS accreditation is recognition of the excellence, commitment and professionalism demonstrated by our staff, students and industry partners."

The programme is delivered at both the York and Gateshead campuses.

The accreditation follows a comprehensive review process and reflects the quality of education, clinical learning, and student support offered by the college.

Yvette Foulds-Davis, assistant principal HE and academic registrar at University Centre Askham Bryan, said: "Receiving full accreditation from the RCVS is a significant endorsement of the quality of our higher education provision and the strength of our veterinary nursing programme."

Limited places for 2026 entry are still available through Clearing.
