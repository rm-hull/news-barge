  **UPDATE: The traffic has since eased to normal levels.**

An incident on a stretch of main road in North Yorkshire is causing slow and building traffic.

The incident, reported to involve a camper van, took place close to Sutton Bank on the A170 this morning (Friday, September 4).

  


A commenter on social media said: “A camper van was camped up in emergency lay-by.”
