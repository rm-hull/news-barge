RESIDENTS have spoken of their sadness after a man died in a collision on a road near a Bradford park.

Emergency services were called to Killinghall Road just before 9pm on Thursday night, after a pedestrian and a car were involved in a collision.

A police cordon was put in place near Bradford Moor Park and emergency crews stayed at the scene for several hours.

Two women, aged 19 and 47, were arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving.

As of Friday afternoon, they remained in custody.

## Councillor lives nearby

The incident happened near the home of Councillor Zafar Iqbal (Labour, Bradford Moor).

Cllr Iqbal said: “This is an incredibly tragic and heart-breaking incident.

"Behind this tragedy is a family who have lost someone they love, and a community trying to come to terms with what has happened.

"My thoughts are with the man’s family and loved ones at what must be an unimaginably difficult time.

“I also want to pay tribute to the emergency services who responded and did everything they could to help in such difficult circumstances.

“Their dedication and compassion at this incredibly difficult time are greatly appreciated.”

## 'Important we all let investigation take its course'

Cllr Riaz Ahmed (Lib Dem, Bradford Moor) also expressed his sadness about the tragedy.

He said: "I extend my deepest sympathies and condolences to the family and loved ones of the person who lost their life in the road traffic collision last night.

"I understand that two people have been arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving.

"At this stage, it is important we all let the investigation take its course.

"I appeal to anyone who witnessed this incident or has dashcam or CCTV footage to get in touch with police.

"Even the smallest bit of information could assist the investigation and help establish what happened."

  Bradford Moor Councillor Zafar Iqbal


## 'Very upsetting'

The incident has also shocked the local community, with residents telling the Telegraph & Argus of their sadness.

One resident said: “I didn't see anything because I was putting my child to bed. But I heard the police cars and saw the cops.

“It happened at the edge of the road.

“It's very upsetting.”

  The scene of a collision where a man died on Killlinghall Road


Another resident said: “I feel sorry for his family.”

One person said: “I didn't see it happen, but it's quite sad.”

## Police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire said: “Police were called to reports of a road traffic collision at around 8.56pm last night (September 3) on Killinghall Road, Bradford.

“Sadly, a man has died after being involved in a collision with a car.

“Two people (a woman aged 19 and a woman aged 47) have been arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving and remain in custody at this time.

“Enquiries remain ongoing to establish the full circumstances of what took place.”

## 'Very serious incident'

Detective Inspector Paul Conroy, of the Major Collision Enquiry Team, said: “We are investigating the exact circumstances of the collision and enquiries remain ongoing into this very serious incident, which has sadly resulted in a man losing his life.

“Our thoughts are with the victim and we would like to speak with anyone who saw or has footage of the incident.”

Anyone with information can contact MCET on 101 or via the West Yorkshire Police live chat, quoting log 1622 of 3/9.
