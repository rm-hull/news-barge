A road in the Bradford district was closed yesterday after a large vehicle caught fire.

Two fire crews were called to the scene on Halifax Road, Cullingworth, near the former Flappit pub, at 3.54pm.

A cordon was put in place while firefighters tackled the blaze, and the road remained shut during the incident.

Firefighters extinguished the blaze but have yet to establish its cause.

A spokesperson for the West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called to Halifax Road yesterday at 3.54pm to reports of a large vehicle on fire.

"Two crews attended and extinguished it.

"No cause recorded yet for this one."
