## Award-winning chart-topper adds York date to 13-day UK tour

Emeli Sandé is set to perform at York Barbican this February *(Image: Supplied)*

Award-winning singer-songwriter Emeli Sandé is set to perform at York Barbican as part of a 13-date UK tour celebrating the 15th anniversary of her debut album.

The tour will see Sandé perform Our Version of Events in full, alongside a selection of her greatest hits, at venues including the London Palladium, Liverpool Philharmonic Hall and York Barbican.

Released in 2012, Our Version of Events became the UK's best-selling album of both 2012 and 2013, selling more than five million copies worldwide.

  


The record features some of Sandé's best-known songs, including Next to Me, Read All About It (Pt. III) and Clown.

The album won British Album of the Year at the 2013 BRIT Awards and spent a record-breaking 66 consecutive weeks in the UK Official Albums Chart Top 10, surpassing The Beatles' Please Please Me as the longest uninterrupted Top 10 run for a debut album.

Throughout her career, Sandé has sold millions of records, won four BRIT Awards and established herself as one of the music industry's most sought-after songwriters, working with artists including Rihanna, Katy Perry and Alicia Keys.

She was also awarded an MBE for services to music.

Known for her powerful live performances, Sandé has headlined tours and festivals around the world and performed at both the opening and closing ceremonies of the London 2012 Olympic Games.

She will be appearing at York Barbican on Thursday (February 4) from 7pm.

Tickets go on sale on Friday (September 11) at 9am.

Fans can also purchase an Emeli Sandé Premium Seat VIP Package, which includes a premium seat, a 15th anniversary commemorative gift, a limited-edition cotton tote bag and a pre-signed anniversary tour poster featuring the original artwork from her iconic debut album.
