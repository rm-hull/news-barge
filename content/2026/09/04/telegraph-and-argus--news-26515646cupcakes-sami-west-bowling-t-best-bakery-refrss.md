PEOPLE from Norway to Scotland come and visit a Bradford bakery, says its owner.

Cupcakes by Sami, on Manchester Road, is competing against nine other businesses for the T&A Best Bakery title.

The bakery sells milk cakes, ice cream sundaes, brownies, cookie pies and cupcakes.

## Origins of the business

It is owned by Saima Khan, who started the business online during the Covid-19 pandemic.

Ms Khan said: “I started selling cupcakes online during the pandemic.

“By 2022, I couldn’t manage the business at home anymore and opened up a shop.”

## Fresh ingredients and traditional recipes

The business uses only fresh ingredients, with products baked on the premises.

Ms Khan said traditional recipes and ingredients were important to the bakery’s success.

She said: “The reason we are where we are is because everything is baked in house and we follow traditional recipes that everybody loves.

“I think that's what sets us apart.”

## Brownies and cupcakes recommended

She said it was difficult to choose one product to recommend, but highlighted the brownies and cupcakes.

Ms Khan said: “If I had to recommend something, it would either be the brownies or the cupcakes.

“But that’s a very difficult question.

“Our cupcakes are all made with traditional buttercream and a light fluffy sponge.”

Cupcakes by Sami has also supported local suppliers in the area.

Ms Khan said: “We support our local farms and local supermarkets by buying from them.”

## 'I'm quite emotional about it'

This is the first time the bakery has reached the T&A's Best Bakery shortlist.

Ms Khan was not expecting to be nominated.

She said: “When I got the letter, I thought it was a prank.

“I wasn’t expecting it at all.

“I’m quite emotional about it, to be honest.”

## International customer base

The bakery’s customers come from Bradford and other parts of the UK, as well as overseas.

Ms Khan said people visiting the city often visit the shop.

Ms Khan said: “I’ve got a customer base from Bradford, all the way to Scotland and Norway.

"When people visit the city, they like to visit the shop."

The bakery plans to continue making seasonal products, including apple crumble and new hot chocolate flavours around Christmas.
