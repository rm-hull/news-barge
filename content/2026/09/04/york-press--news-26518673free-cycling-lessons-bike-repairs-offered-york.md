A new cycling scheme is aiming to help adults build confidence on two wheels.

Free adult sessions will begin in York at Burnholme Leisure Centre, starting on September 9 and running for six weeks on Wednesday afternoons.

Cyclists of all abilities are welcome, whether they're complete beginners or nervous about riding on the road.

Councillor Kate Ravilious, Executive Member for Transport at City of York Council, said: "We recognise that not everyone feels confident on a cycle, or had the chance to learn how to ride when they were younger.

"These sessions are a fantastic opportunity for adults to gain the freedom and independence that cycling can offer and open a whole new world of travel opportunities."

Participants can drop in at any point during the sessions, which run from 1.30pm to 2.30pm, and can bring their own bikes and helmets or use equipment provided on-site.

The sessions are part of a wider programme funded by the Mayor of York and North Yorkshire’s Active Travel Fund, with further training planned in other areas of the city through to December 2027.

Basic bike repairs will also be offered free of charge, with trained instructors on hand to help.

No registration is required, though booking is recommended via: [https://www.york.gov.uk/LearnToRide](https://www.york.gov.uk/LearnToRide)
