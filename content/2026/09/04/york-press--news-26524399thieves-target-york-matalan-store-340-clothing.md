THIEVES have targeted a national retailer's premises in a main York shopping park.

North Yorkshire Police said three people entered the Matalan in Clifton Moor at about 1.30pm on Tuesday, August 4th, put £340 of clothing into a bag and left without paying.

The force released a CCTV image of a woman it said wants to speak to, saying she may have information which could assist its investigation.

  



A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "If you recognise her, please get in touch."

Anybody wishing to contact police with information can email [ben.rhodes@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ben.rhodes@northyorkshire.police.uk) directly, or report online through North Yorkshire Police’s website.

Information can also be given by calling 101.

North Yorkshire Police said anyone providing information should quote reference 12260150095.
