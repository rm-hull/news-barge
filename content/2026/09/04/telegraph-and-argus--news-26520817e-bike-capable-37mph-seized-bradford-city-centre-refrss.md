A rider has been reported for driving offences after police seized an e-bike capable of reaching 37mph in Bradford city centre.

West Yorkshire Police said the Ridstar Q20 e-bike was spotted being ridden "at speed without pedalling" across Norfolk Gardens before officers stopped it in City Park.

## 
  **Bike allegedly exceeded legal limits**

According to police, testing showed the vehicle was capable of far higher speeds than those permitted for electrically assisted pedal cycles (EAPCs).

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "The maximum reading on the speedometer using the throttle alone was 37mph, far in excess of the 3.7mph (6kph) permitted under EAPC Regulations, making this e-bike a motor vehicle and subject to the usual requirements."

Officers said the vehicle was therefore classed as a motor vehicle rather than an electrically assisted pedal cycle.

## 
  **Rider reported**

Police said the rider was reported for driving without a licence and driving without insurance.

The e-bike was also seized under Section 165 of the Road Traffic Act.

A spokesperson added: "Rider reported for no licence and no insurance, and vehicle seized under s.165 Road Traffic Act."

## 
  **Road safety warning**

The incident formed part of West Yorkshire Police's ongoing road safety campaign.

The force shared details of the seizure as part of Operation Steerside, which targets uninsured and illegally used vehicles on the region's roads.

In previous social media posts, police have repeatedly warned that some high-powered e-bikes may be treated as motor vehicles if they exceed legal limits, meaning riders must comply with the same licensing, insurance and registration requirements as other motor vehicle users.
