BUSINESSES from across Leeds North West gathered in Otley for the first-ever Business Brew networking event, launched by local MP Katie White to help local firms build connections and influence government policy.

Held at the River Café in Wharfebank Mills, the event brought together entrepreneurs, independent businesses and community leaders from across the constituency to discuss opportunities and challenges facing the local economy.

Representatives from award-winning food producers, creative agencies, technology firms, retailers and manufacturers attended, creating new connections while sharing their experiences of doing business in the area.

Discussions focused on issues affecting small businesses, including rising costs, recruitment, skills shortages and attracting new customers. Attendees also highlighted success stories from firms that are growing, creating jobs and gaining national and international recognition. Businesses involved with the FIFA World Cup, British Cycling and the International Cheese & Dairy Awards were among those present.

Leeds North West Labour MP Katie White said: “Small businesses are the backbone of our local economy. They create jobs, support our high streets and sponsor local sports clubs and community organisations. But too often they don't get enough opportunities to speak directly to decision-makers.

“Business Brew is about changing that. I wanted to create a space where local businesses can meet each other, build new relationships and have honest conversations about what's helping them grow and what's holding them back.

“One of the best things about the morning was seeing businesses that hadn't met before making new connections. If we can help local firms collaborate, win more work and grow together, that's good for our economy and good for our communities.”

Katie said feedback gathered during the session would be shared directly with the Business Secretary to help inform future government policy.

Julie Milner, of Otley-based Global Scarves, who helped develop the idea for the event, said: “We were really excited to hear that Katie was going to organise some business brews, and we suggested hosting her first one at River Cafe, in Wharfebank Mills where I’m based. We thought it was such a nice idea to bring the local business community together, to share ideas, see what other businesses are up to and to connect with other businesses. It’s a brilliant idea, we’ve loved it.”

The inaugural event attracted businesses from Otley, Horsforth and across Leeds North West, with Ms White hoping Business Brew will become a regular fixture in the constituency calendar.
