Police are looking for a missing school girl, who was last seen in Middlesbrough town centre.

Concerns are growing for Ava Rose-White, 14, who has not been seen since yesterday.

She was last seen around 4pm on Thursday, September 3, and officers want anyone with information to come forward.

Ava is 5ft 2in, with a medium build, brown eyes and thick black hair.

She was wearing black Crocs, black flared trousers, a black HIM band T-shirt and a black zip-up hoodie with red writing on the arms.

A spokesperson said: “If you think that you have seen Ava, or know where she might be, please call 101 quoting reference number 177422.”
