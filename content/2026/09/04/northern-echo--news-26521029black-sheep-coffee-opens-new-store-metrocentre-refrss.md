Coffee lovers are in for a treat as Black Sheep Coffee opens up a new North East store.

The popular chain, which already has a branch on Newcastle’s Grainger Street, has launched its latest location at the Metrocentre’s Red Mall in Gateshead.

The café has seats for 80 customers, who can choose to either enjoy their drink in the relaxed vibe inside or with an “al fresco feel” out on the concourse.

It comes as the firm, which is known for being the first to serve speciality-grade Robusta coffee as its house bean, continues its expansion across the UK.

  Black Sheep Coffee at the Metrocentre in Gateshead 

*(Image: TWIST / METROCENTRE)*

On the menu at the Metrocentre is Black Sheep Coffee’s core range of house brews and espresso, as well as ceremonial-grade matcha, refreshing smoothies and iced drinks.

For those feeling a little bit peckish, the new store will also serve firm favourite Norwegian waffles with an array of toppings, as well as fresh pastries, cakes and all-day breakfasts.

  Black Sheep Coffee at the Metrocentre in Gateshead 

*(Image: TWIST / METROCENTRE)*

The opening follows a range of new brands opening at the shopping complex this year, including Hollister, Timberland, fashion jewellery brand Lovisa, and Asian lifestyle firm Kenji.

Ben Cox, director at Sovereign Centros from CBRE, asset managers of Metrocentre, said: “Introducing new brands and further strengthening our offer has been key to growing footfall, increasing dwell-time and driving up sales.

“Bringing popular names like Black Sheep Coffee to Metrocentre is an important part of this strategy, ensuring we continually evolve the customer experience, and we’re pleased to welcome the team to Upper Red Mall.”

Gabriel Shohet and Eirick Holth, co-founders of Black Sheep Coffee said: “Opening at Metrocentre is another exciting step for Black Sheep Coffee as we continue to bring our take on coffee to more communities across the UK.

“From the very beginning, we’ve wanted to challenge what people expect from a coffee shop and create places that feel energetic, different and worth coming back to.

“Metrocentre is a brilliant fit for that, and we’re looking forward to welcoming shoppers, families and the local community to experience Black Sheep for themselves.”
