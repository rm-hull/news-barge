A new community initiative is calling on residents and businesses to help spruce up Harrogate town centre.

Harrogate Business Improvement District (BID) has launched the first Harrogate Action Days, taking place from September 16 to 19, with a focus on environmental improvements and civic pride through hands-on volunteering.

Volunteers will work alongside businesses, community groups and organisations on projects including litter picking, weeding, planting, and cleaning street furniture.

The move echoes the 'Rejuvenation Days' that York Bid runs in its area.

Jason Maxwell, CEO of Harrogate BID, said: "Harrogate Action Days are about bringing people together to take practical action and take pride in the town we all share.

"There are lots of ways to get involved, whether you can spare an hour or a full day, and we hope businesses, community groups and individuals will get behind the initiative.

"There’s a real opportunity here to bring back a sense of civic pride and encourage people to take an active role in looking after the town they live and work in."

The BID has set a target of 80 volunteer hours for the inaugural event, equivalent to 20 people volunteering for four hours each.

While the hours are important, the main goal is to inspire wider community participation and pride in Harrogate.

Businesses are being encouraged to consider the event as a team volunteering opportunity.

No specialist skills are required, and training will be provided where required, and tools and protective equipment will be provided.

Mr Maxwell said: "We know that councils are facing significant financial pressures and difficult decisions around how resources are used, but Harrogate Action Days aren’t about taking away from or replacing the work that the council does.

"They’re about asking what we can do together, as businesses and as a community, to make a positive difference to the place we all share."

Anyone wishing to take part can email [info@harrogatebid.co.uk](mailto:info@harrogatebid.co.uk) before September 11.
