Emergency crews responded to two separate incidents at Shipley addresses yesterday afternoon.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service confirmed that firefighters attended an incident on Wycliffe Gardens, off the A657, at 6.26pm on Thursday, September 3.

One crew was sent to the scene and assisted Yorkshire Ambulance Service colleagues.

The nature of the incident has not been disclosed.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service later confirmed that ambulance resources had responded to two separate medical incidents at private addresses in the area during the afternoon.

Images shared on social media showed a fire engine and an ambulance at the scene, although it is unclear whether they related to one of the incidents or both.

No further details have been released by either emergency service.

## Statements in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said: “We were called to Wycliffe Gardens last night at 18.26- to assist ambulance colleagues with an incident.

"One crew attended.”

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: “Yesterday afternoon, ambulance resources attended two separate medical incidents at private addresses in this area, but no further details are available.”
