Darlington is set to have a mixed day of weather with sunny spells and a small chance of rain.

The Met Office has predicted a generally dry day for the town today (September 4) with good spells of sunshine but also a risk of showers.

There will be some very strong westerly winds developing in the morning, with gales a risk over high ground across the day. Gusts are expected to reach 39mph around midday.

Temperatures look to be mild to warm, reaching 19C between 2pm and 4pm.

Tonight, it will be mostly dry with clear skies, although there may be some showers at times.

The strong winds will start to ease through the evening, though strong gusts will still be possible. The minimum temperature is expected to be around 9C.
