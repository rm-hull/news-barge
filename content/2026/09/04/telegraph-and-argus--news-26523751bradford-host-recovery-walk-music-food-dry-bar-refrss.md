Bradford Council is hosting a walk celebrating recovery.

The event will be held next Saturday (September 12) in the city centre.

The mile-long walk will feature music, entertainment, food, a dry bar and information stalls along the way.

Bradford Council has partnered with Create Strength Group - Bradford and Faces & Voices of Recovery UK.

### What the council have said

Bradford Council said: "Join us for the UK Recovery Walk next Saturday for a day of celebrating recovery.

"As well as a 1 mile walk around the city centre, there will be music, entertainment, food, a dry bar and information stalls.

  "Thanks to our partners Create Strength Group- Bradford and Faces & Voices of Recovery UK for putting together this fantastic event.

  The council are also looking for volunteers to help run the event. 

They said: "We're looking for volunteers to help ensure the event runs smoothly.

"If you're interested contact [info@createstrengthgroup.org.](mailto:info@createstrengthgroup.org.)"
