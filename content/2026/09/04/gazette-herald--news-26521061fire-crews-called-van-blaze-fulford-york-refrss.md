FIRE crews have been called to a van fire in York.

The incident happened in a property in Crossfield Crescent at 8.10pm on Thursday (September 3).

The fire had begun, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said, due to a mechanical fault.

  


A service spokesperson said: “The fire was out on the arrival of crews, a thermal imaging camera was used to scan for any remaining hotspots.

“Advice was given to the occupiers.”
