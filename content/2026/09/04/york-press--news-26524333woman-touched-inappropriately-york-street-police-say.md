A WOMAN has been "touched inappropriately" in a York city centre street, police say.

North Yorkshire Police said it happened in the early hours of Saturday, July 25 in Blake Street, close to some bicycle racks.

A force spokesperson said: "The victim was with her boyfriend at the time and afterwards had an argument with the alleged suspect.

"The alleged suspect - who was with two other men - then left and entered a taxi."

  



An image has been released by North Yorkshire Police of a man the force said it wanted to speak to, saying he may have information that could assist the force's investigation.

The police spokesperson asked for anyone who recognises the man to get in contact with them.

The force said contact can be made via email to [ben.rhodes@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ben.rhodes@northyorkshire.police.uk), or via the North Yorkshire Police website, or by calling it via 101.

Anyone who wishes to remain anonymous can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Police asked anyone making contact to quote reference 12260142156 when providing information.
