# Mason: 'Not what Farage wanted today to look like'

Reform UK's annual conference is under way in Birmingham - a chance for the party to talk about its policies and what it hopes to do should it win a future general election.

Yet questions over money and donations are dominating the news after two of the party's senior figures stepped down on Friday morning following a Channel 4 News report.

Chris Mason reports from the conference.
