A CHARITY event has highlighted the "strength, compassion and generosity" of a community supporting women and girls.

Held by the Millan Centre, Manningham, the event received donations and support for the charity, which offers educational and training opportunities, support for women experiencing domestic violence, and support for those facing mental health difficulties.

Saliha Sadiq, CEO and mental health director at the Millan Centre, said: "Our community has so many true unsung heroes – people and local businesses who quietly give their time, resources and generosity to help others.

"They may not always receive the recognition they deserve, but without their support, charities like Millan Centre could not continue the work we do."

Ms Sadiq extended special thanks to businesses including Kesar Jewellers, My Lahore, Aziz Catering, Freshco, and Café Regal, along with all individuals and organisations who contributed to the event.

She said: "Despite the rain, people still came out to support this important cause.

"It was a wonderful reminder of the strength, compassion and generosity that exists within our community.

"To every business that donated, every person who attended, every volunteer who gave their time, and everyone who contributed in any way – thank you."
