The White House has launched a new arcade game website where users can build the border wall and make school meals that follow President Donald Trump’s “Make America Healthy Again” movement.

Earlier this year, the Trump administration faced pushback when it shared compilation videos of airstrikes against Iran mixed with clips of popular movies like *Top Gun* and video games like*Grand Theft Auto* on social media. Now, the administration is doubling down on the gamification of serious issues like immigration with five arcade games promoting Trump’s agenda. 

The White House announced the new games Thursday in an X post that read, “Can’t stop winning.”

The games are on the official White House website, usually where the public can find press releases about what’s happening in Washington, livestreams of Oval Office events and Trump’s executive orders.

![The White House has launched a new arcade website where users can build the border wall and make school meals that are in line with President Donald Trump’s 'Make America Healthy Again' movement](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/04/1/28/Screenshot-2026-09-03-at-9-18-40-PM.jpg)

Here’s a look at each game offered on the site:

## Flappy Bill

“Flappy Bill” is seemingly inspired by the game “Flappy Bird,” which went viral in the 2010s. Players need to keep the bill the Bald Eagle is holding in the air while flying through Washington’s monuments.

![In 'Flappy Bill,' players need to keep the bill the Bald Eagle is holding in the air while flying through Washington’s monuments](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/04/1/21/Screenshot-2026-09-03-at-9-54-21-PM.jpg)

## Build The Wall

In “Build The Wall,” players need to “protect the border from the coming horde,” according to the game’s description. The Tetris-esque game has players position colorful blocks to block alien-looking pixelated creatures from getting through the wall.

![In 'Build The Wall,' players position colorful blocks to block alien-looking pixelated creatures from getting through the wall](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/04/1/57/Screenshot-2026-09-03-at-9-56-15-PM.jpg)

## Rio Run

“Rio Run” is reminiscent of the classic video game “Snake,” but instead of controlling a reptile moving around the board to eat food, players are Border Patrol moving along the Rio Grande to pick up immigrants trying to cross the southern border and deport them.

![In 'Rio Run,' players are Border Patrol moving along the Rio Grande to pick up immigrants trying to cross the southern border and deport them](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/04/1/33/Screenshot-2026-09-03-at-9-57-37-PM.jpg)

## Supply Line

In “Supply Line,” players sort school lunch foods to “Make America Healthy Again.” The foods are on a conveyor belt, and players must knock off the ones with seed oils, colorings, additives and those that are sealed in packaging.

![In 'Supply Line,' players must knock school lunch foods with seed oils, colorings, additives and those that are sealed in packaging off a conveyor belt](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/04/2/07/Screenshot-2026-09-03-at-9-57-51-PM.jpg)

## Trump Savings Tycoon

The goal of “Trump Savings Tycoon” is to fill kids’ “Trump Accounts” based on the president’s child investment accounts. Players have to swing a net to catch all the money being thrown at them.

![In 'Trump Savings Tycoon,' players have to swing a net to catch all the money being thrown at them](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/04/2/56/Screenshot-2026-09-03-at-9-58-05-PM.jpg)

The arcade has seen some mixed reactions, with several people on social media taking issue with the administration making games out of life-altering matters.

“The official White House account is dropping cutesy little arcade games about ICE while they’re out here terrorizing children and families,” one X user wrote.

The post included a screenshot of a February article by *The Independent*’s Alex Woodward about reports of staff at a Texas detention center calling 911 to report children with severe fever, broken bones, respiratory distress, seizures, plunging oxygen levels and other medical emergencies.

“We are not a serious country. We are a bunch of idiots! The White House just made an ‘arcade’ where kids can build a wall and deport people. Absolutely embarrassing!” another X user wrote.

But some X users were excited about the games, with one calling it “a hoot.”

“This White House is AWESOM [sic],” another user wrote.

There is a spot on the arcade’s webpage for a sixth game that reads, “Coming soon...A new adventure awaits.” *The Independent* has reached out to the White House for comment.
