Police are investigating after a woman was touched inappropriately in York city centre.

It happened in the early hours of Saturday, 25 July, near the bike racks on Blake Street.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Do you recognise this man? We want to speak to him after a woman was touched inappropriately.

#### Blake Street, York

“The victim was with her boyfriend at the time, and afterwards had an argument with the suspect.

“The suspect – who was with two other men – then left and entered a taxi.”

Anyone who recognises the man in the picture is asked to contact police.
