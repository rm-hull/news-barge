An air ambulance was called after a person fell from their bike down an embankment yesterday.

Emergency services rushed to Seave Green in the Bilsdale area of the North York Moors National Park at 11am on Thursday, September 3.

A person reportedly fell from their bike down the embankment.

A Yorkshire Air Ambulance helicopter and a road ambulance were sent to the scene.

One patient was taken by road to James Cook Hospital in Middlesbrough.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call just before 11am on Thursday morning to report a person falling from a bike down an embankment near Seave Green in the Bilsdale area of the North York Moors National Park.

"An ambulance and an air ambulance were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed by road to James Cook Hospital."
