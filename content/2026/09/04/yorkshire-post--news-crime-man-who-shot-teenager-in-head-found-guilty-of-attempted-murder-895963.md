# Man who shot teenager in head found guilty of attempted murder

## A man who shot a teenage boy in the head in Sheffield has been found guilty of attempted murder.

Tanzir Ali, 27, shot a 16-year-old boy on London Road, causing serious and life-changing injuries, on Monday November 24, 2025.

Officers and the ambulance service attended the scene and the boy was taken to hospital in a critical condition.

The victim survived his injuries, but remains in hospital with life changing injuries.

![Tanzir Ali, 27, shot a 16-year-old boy on London Road, causing serious and life-changing injuries](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjQwNGIzNzhlLWY3YzQtNDU5Zi04OWNlLThhYjQ0YzEyZWQ4MzpkZTYxYzI2Yy1kZTNkLTQ4YTUtYWFkYy1lZjVkMmRiYzQ2MGI=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Ali fled scene following the shooting and travelled to Aberdeen where he was arrested alongside Sharna Osborne, who had booked a hotel for them using false details.

Officers arrested Ali on suspicion of attempted murder and firearms offences.

Osborne, 24, of Duthie Terrace, Aberdeen, was arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender.

A thorough investigation was launched immediately following the shooting, with officers carrying out forensic enquiries at the scene and securing vital CCTV evidence which captured the exact moment of the shooting.

Ali and Osborne were charged, with Ali pleading guilty to possession of a firearm with intent to cause fear of violence but not guilty to attempted murder. Osborne pleaded not guilty to assisting an offender.

At court, Ali said that he did not mean to press the trigger on the murder weapon, and that he didn’t know it was loaded.

However, CCTV evidence compiled by officers and shown at court tracked Ali’s actions in the minutes before, during, and after the shooting.

CCTV footage showed Ali in the company of several others and engaged in an altercation with the victim. Ali is seen to pull a gun out of his pocket, a loud bang is heard, and the victim falls to the ground.

CCTV further captured Ali returning the firearm to his pocket and fleeing the scene.

Ali, of Glencoe Road, Sheffield, was found guilty of attempted murder at Sheffield Crown Court on Wednesday September 2.

Osborne was found guilty of assisting an offender at the same court on Thursday September 3.

They have both been remanded into custody ahead of their sentencing at Sheffield Crown Court on November 18.

Senior Investigating Officer Greg Dakin said: “The victim and his family’s lives have been changed forever by this devastating attack. The boy’s family were forced to endure the pain of waiting at his bedside, not knowing if he would pass away from his injuries.

“Over the course of this investigation, we have had the victim and his family at the forefront of our minds, and I am glad that we have helped secure justice for them.

“Ali attempted to kill a child, and then fled hundreds of miles away in a cowardly effort to escape the consequences, aided by Osborne.

“I am very proud of the officers who worked tirelessly on this investigation and would like to thank Police Scotland for their assistance in the apprehension of Ali and Osborne. I hope that these convictions further demonstrate our commitment to rid South Yorkshire of gun violence.”
