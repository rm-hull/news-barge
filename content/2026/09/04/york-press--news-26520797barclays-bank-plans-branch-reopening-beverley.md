A UK bank could be reopening a branch in the centre of an East Yorkshire town, plans have revealed.

Barclays has submitted a planning application to East Riding of Yorkshire Council for the vacant 39 Saturday Market, Beverley.

A "retail bank fit out" is proposed on the site of a former retailer, according to documents issued with the plans, with changes to the existing space including "a retail bank at ground floor and banking back-office space at first floor."

The shop front would be altered with automatic doors installed and an external ramp for accessibility.

An illuminated sign is proposed and a lit sign above a new ATM, the plans say.

The application also proposes the use of two internal digital screens for advertising and marketing.

  


The proposed opening times for the new bank are Monday to Saturday, from 9am to 5.30pm and if approved, the bank will employ five full-time staff, the plans said.

Barclays closed its Beverley branch in North Bar Within in March 2024, and some services were moved to a Barclays Local "hub" in Champney Treasure House just outside the town centre.

The planning application details highlighted the intention to improve banking provision in the town, stating: "We believe that these works are proposed in the spirit of improving the existing banking provision to the town in a sensitive manner and do not represent any harm to the property."

Beverley Town Council raised objections to the application "on the grounds of pedestrian accessibility and highway safety."

It said: "The proposed ATM ramp would project into the pedestrian area of Saturday Market and create and unnecessary obstruction for pedestrians, particularly wheelchair and mobility scooter users, people with visual or mobility impairments, and those using prams and pushchairs."

East Riding of Yorkshire Council's building conservation team said it did not object to "reconfiguration of this area to accommodate a new shopfront" but said it "cannot support the use of internal illumination for the signage.

"Its introduction would create a jarring and insensitive intrusion, distracting from the well-preserved nature of the historic character and appearance of Saturday Market."

The proposed Barclays branch in Beverley is not a listed building but is situated adjacent to listed buildings, such as The Kings Head Hotel.

A spokesperson for Barclays told The Press: "We’re exploring plans to open a branch in Beverley, as part of how we continue to expand our branch network.”

Consultation on the plans is set to come to an end on Thursday, September 17.
