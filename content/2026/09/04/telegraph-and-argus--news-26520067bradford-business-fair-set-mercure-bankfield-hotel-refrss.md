A BUSINESS fair will take place in Bradford this autumn.

The Bradford Business Fair will take place on Thursday, October 15, from 10.30am to 3pm at the Mercure Bradford Bankfield Hotel.

It will feature a business-to-business exhibition, free seminars and workshops, start-up support, and one-to-one advice from industry experts.

Tony Haines from Liverpool BA said: "The Bradford Business Fair is a great opportunity for businesses to promote their products and services and engage with their local community."

A networking session will take place at 1pm, and visitors can book free places through the event website.

Exhibitors and sponsors can also reserve packages online, with a 10 per cent discount available to local businesses using the code EXCLUB.

More information is available by calling 0151 709 8932 or emailing [tony@liverpoolba.com.](mailto:tony@liverpoolba.com.)
