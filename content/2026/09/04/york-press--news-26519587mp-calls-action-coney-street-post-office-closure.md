An MP has said she is “working to restore Post Office services in our city” after it was announced that York’s main branch will close.

Rachael Maskell has asked the government for a statement on the closure of post offices, after it was revealed that the Coney Street branch would be closing.

It followed TG Jones, formerly known as WHSmith, announcing it would be shutting its Coney Street branch, which contains York’s main post office, on October 19.

The nearest post offices in York city centre are Micklegate and Colliergate.

  TG Jones in Coney Street, which is closing 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

TG Jones’ Coney Street store will be among the 150 branches to close as part of restructuring plans to prevent the retail chain from falling into administration.

A Post Office spokesperson said it wants to open a replacement branch "as soon as possible”, but it can “take around six months to re-open a replacement branch”.

A TG Jones spokesperson said its “priority now is on supporting those affected colleagues while continuing to serve our customers until the store closes”.

  The Post Office branch in Lendal, York, which closed in 2019 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Ms Maskell told the Commons York residents were “given assurances” that the Post Office moving to Coney Street after its main branch in Lendal closed in 2019 would be a “long-term solution”.

“Now that we have Modella Capital, a private equity, buying out WHSmiths, we have seen the closure of WHSmiths on our high street and 150 across the country, meaning that we’re going to lose our post office once again,” the Labour MP for York Central said.

“So can we have a statement to make sure we secure the jobs, but also the services to my residents and businesses?”

  Rachael Maskell, MP for York Central 

*(Image: UK Parliament/PA Wire)*

Responding, Sir Alan Campbell, leader of the House of Commons, said he would “make sure that the relevant minister hears what [Ms Maskell] has said today and writes to her with an update on what further steps can be taken”.

“My honourable friend will have heard from the murmurs behind that this is not just a matter for her constituency, it’s a matter for others too,” he said.

“It’s an important point and she’s right to raise them.

“The government’s priority is to maintain the current size of the Post Office network, and ensure that communities continue to have access to Post Office services.”

The Post Office spokesperson said: “We fully recognise the challenging trading conditions that TG Jones and many other high street retailers are facing at the moment.

"TG Jones had its restructuring plan approved by the High Court on 1 July, which includes seeking new commercial terms with landlords for the majority of their estate.

"Consequently, TG Jones is looking to close their York store. Regrettably, York Post Office is due to close on Wednesday 14 October.

“We know how important our products and services are to the local community. We want to open a replacement branch as soon as possible. This can take around six months to re-open a replacement branch."

The TG Jones spokesperson said: "As part of our work to secure the long-term future of TG Jones, we have confirmed that our York store will close.

"We appreciate this will be disappointing news for our colleagues, customers and the local community. Our priority now is on supporting those affected colleagues while continuing to serve our customers until the store closes.

"We would like to thank our colleagues in York for their valued contribution to TG Jones."
