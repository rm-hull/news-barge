PUBS in Bradford are raising a glass for a good cause this September.

The Crown Hotel and Malt Kiln Inn are among those taking part in the Heroes of Hunger campaign, which will see 5p from every pint of Carling sold donated to local food banks.

The month-long fundraiser is running at participating pubs across Yorkshire and the Humber, with each pint helping to combat food poverty in the community.

Last year, the scheme raised more than £15,000 for food banks.

The campaign is a collaboration between Carling and Proper Pubs, the community wet-led division of Admiral Taverns.

A spokesperson for Proper Pubs said: "Proper Pubs and Carling are encouraging local customers to pop in, pull up a chair, and show their support throughout September."

Other participating pubs in West Yorkshire include the Cavendish in Keighley and the Black Lion Hotel, Broadway, and Butterbowl Hotel in Leeds.

Funds raised will support food banks across the region.
