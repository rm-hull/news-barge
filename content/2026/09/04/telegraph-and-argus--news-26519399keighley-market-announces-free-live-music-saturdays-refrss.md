LIVE music is coming to Keighley Market this September.

Shoppers will be able to enjoy free Saturday lunchtime performances throughout the month, starting with Bradford musician Jake Keating on September 5.

Mr Keating, who has previously performed at Bradford’s Darley Street Market and as part of the Busk:BD programme, will play two acoustic sets at 1pm and 3pm.

He said: "I’m really looking forward to performing at Keighley Market.

"Markets are fantastic community spaces and there’s something special about playing live music in the heart of a busy town centre.

"I’m excited to bring a mix of soulful acoustic favourites and hopefully create an atmosphere that people can enjoy while they shop, eat and spend time with friends and family."

On September 19, The Homburg will take to the stage.

The band is known for its blend of indie folk, country blues, and gypsy ska.

A third artist is yet to be confirmed and will perform on October 3.

Councillor Ian Walker, Bradford Council’s executive member for neighbourhoods and communities, said: "Following a successful trial earlier in the year, we're delighted to welcome more musicians to Keighley Market this autumn.

"Live music helps create a vibrant atmosphere, supports local artists and gives visitors another reason to spend time at the market and discover everything it has to offer.

"Our markets are at the heart of our communities, bringing people together and supporting local businesses, and these performances will encourage more people to visit, enjoy a great day out and explore the fantastic range of traders and products available at Keighley Market."

The events are free and open to all.

Visitors can enjoy the music while exploring the market’s traders, food stalls, and businesses.

Updates on the October artist and other events will be announced by Bradford Markets in the coming weeks.
