DARLEY Street Market is kicking off student life with a packed programme of Freshers’ Week events.

New and returning students are being invited to explore the city centre market through a line-up of food, entertainment, and shopping experiences.

Councillor Ian Walker, Bradford Council’s executive member for neighbourhoods and communities, said: "We're looking forward to welcoming new and returning students to Darley Street Market this September and showcasing everything the market has to offer.

"Starting college or university is an exciting time and our programme of events gives students plenty of opportunities to meet new people, make friends, and get to know the city.

"Whether it's picking up those all-important student essentials, doing the weekly food shop, enjoying affordable food and drink with friends, or taking part in our fantastic programme of events, Darley Street Market has something for everyone.

"It has quickly become much more than a market, developing into a vibrant city centre destination where people can shop, socialise, and enjoy culture and entertainment."

The Freshers’ Week programme begins on Wednesday, September 16, with live music from Bethany Gibson in the Darley Street Kitchen from 4pm to 6pm, followed by Shane's Singo Bingo from 6pm to 8pm.

On Thursday, September 17, students can join a guided food tour of the market’s Fresh Food Floor, with tickets priced at £5.

That evening, the Courtyard will host a screening of tHE iNVISIBLES, written and directed by Bradford filmmaker Sean Grundy.

The screening will mark the launch of monthly film events celebrating Bradford's growing film culture and independent filmmaking scene.

The entertainment continues on Friday, September 18, with live music from singer-songwriter Will Matthew from 6pm to 8pm, and a performance from Yolo Solo on Saturday, September 19, from 5pm to 7pm.

A three-day Oktoberfest celebration will also take place from Friday to Sunday, featuring a wide selection of beers from Bradford CAMRA and live music across the first two days.

The week concludes with a Heritage Open Day on Sunday, exploring Bradford’s cycling history from 10am to 2pm.

Students can also take advantage of the market’s retail and lifestyle traders, with the Lifestyle Level open Monday to Saturday from 9am to 5.30pm.

Adam Tasker of the University of Bradford Union of Students said: "The Students' Union are really excited about the Freshers' activities that Darley Street Market are hosting.

"Our students will get to see the city centre, sample the various food and retail outlets and have a great time doing it."
