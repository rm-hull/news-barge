West Yorkshire Police are appealing for information following a burglary which occurred last month.

Suspects are believed to have broken into a residential property in Staincliffe Road, Dewsbury, on Friday, August 28, between 6pm and 11pm.

Detectives have released images of the jewellery taken and are urging anyone who may have seen the jewellery or seen it for sale to contact them.

A car key and cash were also stolen.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Kirklees District Crime Team via [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat,](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat,) quoting crime reference 13260492235.

Alternatively, information can be given anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers via 0800 555 111 or online.

For more information and advice to prevent burglary, visit: [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/advice/home-security/home-security.](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/advice/home-security/home-security.)
