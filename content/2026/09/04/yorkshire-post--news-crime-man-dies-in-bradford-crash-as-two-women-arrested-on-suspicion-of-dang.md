# Man dies in Bradford crash as two women arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving

## Two women have been arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving after a man died in a crash in Bradford.

Police were called to Killinghall Road at around 8.56pm on Thursday (Sep 3) following reports of a crash.

Emergency services attended, but a man was pronounced dead at the scene.

Two women, aged 19 and 47, were arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving.

![Two women have been arrested after a man died in a crash on Killinghall Road](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmM5ZTYyM2IzLWYyYjktNDQ3MS1hZjNjLTQzYWIwNjUyZGVlZjo5YWU4NDAxOS04MmVmLTQxMGEtOGZkMC1kYjkwMDBlNWVmZWE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Both remain in police custody while enquiries into the circumstances of the crash continue.

Detective Inspector Paul Conroy of the Major Collision Enquiry Team said: “We are investigating the exact circumstances of the collision and enquiries remain ongoing into this very serious incident which has sadly resulted in a man losing his life.

"Our thoughts are with the victim and would like to speak with anyone who saw or has footage of the incident.”
