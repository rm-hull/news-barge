A man has been arrested after “threatening a woman with a blade” in Sunderland.

Armed police were called to an address in the Shiney Row area at 1.30pm on Thursday (September 3) after reports of a disturbance.

Northumbria Police said it was reported that, following an altercation, a man had threatened a female victim with a bladed weapon.

A cordon was erected at the time while firearms officers were sent to the scene as a precaution, although nobody was reported to be injured.

The force has confirmed that a 44-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of possession of an imitation firearm with intent to cause fear of violence and is currently in police custody.

"There is an increased police presence in the area to provide reassurance to the community,” a spokesperson said.

  "Anyone with concerns is encouraged to speak with one of our officers on duty."
