A NEW cyber security programme is aiming to strengthen digital defences across the manufacturing sector.

Made Smarter Yorkshire has launched the fully funded scheme to support SME manufacturers, aiming to build cyber resilience and reduce disruption across West Yorkshire.

The £400,000 pilot is being delivered in partnership with Sheffield Hallam University and will support the development of Cyber Security Champions within businesses, improving awareness and encouraging safer working practices.

Jessica Armitage, programme manager for Made Smarter Yorkshire, said: "Cyber attacks are no longer something manufacturers can afford to think will happen to somebody else.

"This isn't simply an IT problem.

"An attack can stop production, delay orders, increase costs and disrupt customers and suppliers.

"For smaller manufacturers, one of the biggest challenges can simply be knowing where to start."

The programme’s first stage, Cyber Security Awareness, is a 2.5-hour online session designed for manufacturers who are new to cyber security.

It will help businesses begin developing Cyber Security Champions who can spot common threats and promote safer digital practices.

Participants will leave with a practical action plan to improve their cyber resilience.
