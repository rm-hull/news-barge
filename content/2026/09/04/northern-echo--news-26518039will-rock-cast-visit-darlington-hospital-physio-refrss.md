Cast members from a hit musical tuned up for their latest show with a special visit to a Darlington hospital.

Performers from the stage production of We Will Rock You, which is set to arrive at Darlington Hippodrome this October, received physiotherapy support from the team at Woodlands Hospital to help them prepare for the demanding run.

With 11 high-energy performances and complex choreography to deliver, staying injury-free is vital for the cast.

Debbie Dobbs, executive director of Woodlands Hospital, said: "The cast of We Will Rock You puts an incredible amount of strain on their bodies during rehearsals and performances.

"We were thrilled to support them as they prepare for their Darlington run and help keep them in tip-top shape.

"It's not every day we have rock stars dancing through our doors, but we're certainly ready to help them stay fit for the show."

The cast received tailored advice and treatment from the hospital’s physiotherapy team, aiming to keep any aches and injuries at bay throughout their busy schedule.

We Will Rock You, featuring more than 20 of Queen’s greatest hits, will run at Darlington Hippodrome from Wednesday, October 28 to Saturday, November 7.

Tickets are available by calling the ticket hotline on 01325 405405 or at www.darlingtonos.org.uk.
