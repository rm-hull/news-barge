THE 1980s are set to return to Bradford with a high-energy party night.

Gary Davies Discos for Grown Ups 80s Party is coming to Bradford tonight, Friday, September 4.

Doors open at 7.15pm, with the performance starting at 8pm. Tickets are priced from £27.50, with the standard ticket range listed as £27.50 to £42.95.

The event will feature legendary 1980s DJ Gary Davies, host of BBC Radio 2’s Sounds of the 80s show, alongside the Discos For Grown Ups DJ.

The evening promises non-stop 1980s hits, floor-shaking anthems and plenty of opportunities to sing, dance and party like it is the 1980s.

Guests are encouraged to dress up, with shoulder pads, teased hair and old Wham and Frankie T-shirts suggested for the occasion.

The party will begin with the Discos For Grown Ups DJ before Gary Davies takes over with 1980s classics and what is described as “'woo' Gary fun”.

Those attending can expect dancers, confetti showers, lights, lasers and mirror balls, alongside music from Wham, Erasure, ABBA, Whitney, Aha, Queen, Depeche Mode, Duran Duran, Human League, Madonna, Eurythmics, Soft Cell and many more.

The event listing says only one performance is scheduled, with the last few seats available.

A maximum of 10 tickets can be booked online. Discounts are subject to availability and may not be applicable. Priority Access, Pre-Show Meet & Greet, Friends Members and Access are listed as open.
