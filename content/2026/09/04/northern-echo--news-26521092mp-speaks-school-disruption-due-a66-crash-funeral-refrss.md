An MP said schools “should never be forced to close because a safe journey home cannot be guaranteed” amid anger over disruption due to an A66 crash funeral.

  The funeral of Jakub Matusiak - one of the __five people in the Volkswagen Passat who died__ in the crash on August 22 - is set to take place in South Bank this afternoon (September 4). 

  The incident, which involved a marked Cleveland Police vehicle and the Passat, which was travelling the wrong direction, also took the lives of __PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough.__ 

  Pc Matthew Blades, 37, and Pc Tom Clough, 38 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

A growing number of schools announced yesterday that they would be closing early due to the funeral, which is taking place at St John the Evangelist Church on Normanby Road.

Normanby Medical Centre on Normanby Road also announced it was closing early.

Concerns continued to grow last night as schools felt they had no option but to shut due to fears about road disruption or potentially dangerous behaviour.

Archway Academy Teesville, South Bank Primary School, Outwood Academy Normanby and St Margaret Clitherow’s Catholic Primary School are among those shutting early.

And now, Stockton West MP Matt Vickers has written to Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood due to the significant disruption.

  Matt Vickers 

*(Image: CONTRIBUTION)*

He said: "Schools should never be forced to close because a safe journey home cannot be guaranteed.

“Cleveland Police must be given the officers and fair funding it needs to keep our communities safe.”

“The scheduled funeral should be able to take place without disrupting children’s education.

“Schools should never be put in a position where closing their doors is considered necessary to protect pupils, staff and families.”

He added: "Our local officers have been placed under enormous pressure while continuing to police our communities and respond to other serious demands.

“The fact that almost 200 officers have reportedly been drafted in from outside the area shows the scale of the challenge facing the force.”

  Two police officers are among seven people who have died following a devastating crash on the A66 in Teesside 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council leader Alec Brown also said it is “awful” that children’s education is being disrupted and said any dangerous riding, reckless driving, or antisocial behaviour is “not acceptable”.

A spokesperson for the council said the decision to close schools early followed discussions between local headteachers and the authority over anticipated traffic and transport disruption.

They said the decision was made after "careful consideration of the potential impact on home-to-school transport and the departure of pupils, staff and families at the end of the school day".

A Department for Education spokesperson said: “We are in contact with police and the local authority, and are clear that schools must ensure disruption to learning is minimised.

“Our thoughts remain on the community affected by this tragedy.”

## 
  **Closures will ‘ensure safety and wellbeing of pupils’**

South Bank Primary School head teacher Tammy Cooper said the closure was “to ensure the safety and wellbeing of our pupils”.

“This decision has been reached after careful consideration of the potential impact on the departure of pupils, staff and families,” she said.

  Jakub Matusiak, 23. 

*(Image: SOCIAL MEDIA)*

“We recognise the inconvenience this may cause and thank our families for their understanding and co-operation.”

In a letter to parents, Mark Robinson, principal of Outwood Academy in Normanby, also said the school would close from midday after discussions with the council.

Cllr Brown said he “fully understands that funerals are deeply personal occasions, and I respect the right of families to grieve” but is “deeply concerned over the school closures.

“Our children should be able to attend school safely and without their education being affected by events happening elsewhere,” he said.

St Peter’s is also expected to close early today while Mackenzie Thorpe School will be shut all day.

A previous memorial for Matusiak near the crash site, featuring orange balloons, fireworks and masked bikers, sparked mixed reactions within the local community over the tone of the event.

Police investigations into the circumstances of the crash are ongoing - and since the incident, 27 people have been arrested in a crackdown on serious and organised crime.

Earlier this week, a fundraiser launched for the families of the two Cleveland Police officers reached its target, surpassing £1.2 million.
