A MAN wanted by police is believed to be in a specific area of North Yorkshire.

The county's force is appealing for sightings of Thomas Carter, 45, from Husthwaite near Thirsk.

A force spokesperson said he is wanted after failing to attend court and breaching a non-molestation order.

  


The North Yorkshire Police spokesperson added: "Extensive enquiries have been carried out to find him and officers are now appealing to members of the public to report any sightings of him.

"He is believed to be in the former Hambleton area of North Yorkshire in the villages between Thirsk, Easingwold and Malton.

"If you have an immediate sighting of him or know where he is now, please call us on 999.

"If you have any other information that could assist the search for him, please call 101.

"If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

"Please quote reference 12260157746 when passing on information."
