## 'Absolutely heartbroken' - top York restaurant faces closure for major repairs

Sloppy's Bar and Kitchen in Castlegate was forced to close last week after reports of a blocked pipe - and may need further work to address a collapsed pipe beneath the building *(Image: Newsquest)*

A TOP York burger restaurant may have to close its doors for the second time since opening following the discovery of a collapsed pipe.

Sloppy’s Bar & Kitchen in Castlegate said it is investigating the collapsed pipe, which may require major repair work – closing the restaurant for some time.

It comes after the restaurant, which relocated to the city centre from its previous bar and kitchen in Woodthorpe on August 21, closed for a blocked pipe last week.

  


A spokesperson for the restaurant said: “As some of you will remember, we had to close for a couple of days last week.

“This was to unblock a pipe under our restaurant.

“Upon further inspection they found a collapsed pipe.

  Sloppy's opened in Castlegate on August 21 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

They added: “While the blockage has been fixed, the ongoing issue of the collapse is being investigated.

“As far as we know for now, major works will need to be done to repair it.

“This will mean digging down through our beautiful new restaurant, as well as the removal of some walls, meaning we will have to close while the work is carried out.”

  Partners Molly McGrath and Leon Sledmore outside the Woodthorpe restaurant, which is now closed. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

Owners Molly McGrath and Leon Sledmore said they were “absolutely heartbroken” about the news.

They said: “It’s been hard to come to terms with, and although we don’t know exactly when or for how long we’ll need to close for at the moment, we thought it was best to share it with you all.

We’re open as normal for now, so please come, enjoy, eat and drink to your hearts content.

“As soon as we have more information on the matter we will update everyone.”
