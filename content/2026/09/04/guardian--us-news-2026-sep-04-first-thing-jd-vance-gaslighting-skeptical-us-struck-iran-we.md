Good morning. The US vice-president, JD Vance, has responded to claims that an American strike was responsible for killing four people, including a child, at a wedding celebration in southern Iran by saying that “sometimes things happen”. The deaths were reported by the Iranian Red Crescent, which operates independently of Tehran and is treated by most international monitors as a credible source on casualty figures.

In a further comment, described by the congressman Ro Khanna, a Democrat from California, as “gaslighting”, the vice-president said the conflict between the US and Iran, which has stretched into a seventh month and caused the deaths of 18 US service members, was not presently “a war”. Iranian authorities have put the overall death toll from US and Israeli strikes in the thousands, figures outside observers, including US-based monitoring groups, have broadly corroborated.

- 
  **What didVance say?** At a White House press briefing he said: “I will say that the Iranian state media has not been a very good scribe about what’s happened in the conflict thus far, so I’m extremely skeptical of this. What I can say with 100% confidence is that, unlike the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, the US never targets civilians in combat. We never will do that. We never have done that, and unfortunately, obviously, sometimes things happen”. Donald Trump has repeatedly threatened to blow up Iran’s civilian infrastructure.
- 
  **What is happening with US military leadership?** Trump on Thursday named Adam Telle as the acting army secretary after the departure of Daniel Driscoll amid widespread reports of tensions with the defense secretary, Pete Hegseth. Telle is currently the assistant secretary of the army for civil works.

## **Far-right Israeli minister outlines planto removeall Palestinians from Gaza**

![Itamar Ben-Gvir at mic.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/3c5c293f876645fbb4bcf7541a4b499a9b57f9b0/750_0_3750_3000/master/3750.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Israel’s far-right national security minister, Itamar Ben-Gvir, has outlined a plan for the illegal forced expulsion of all Palestinians from Gaza, with the intention of sending them abroad. The forced displacement of civilians in occupied territory is considered a war crime under the Geneva conventions, but as a tightly fought election approaches in Israel, political figures have competed over the harshness of their visions for the strip.

Ben-Gvir’s comments follow those of the defence minister, Israel Katz, who said Israel was seeking the Trump administration’s approval to displace Palestinians from Gaza, which he said was still under discussion after being “frozen” by the US president. Katz said Israel would facilitate the emigration “by any means”, implying a policy of coercion.

- 
  **What has the House decided about university boycotts of Israel?** The House has passed a Republican-backed bill that would penalize universities that participate in boycotts of Israel. “This legislation is about a simple principle: our colleges and universities should not discriminate against Israel,” said Tim Walberg, the Republican chair of the House education committee. Thirty three Democrats broke with the rest of their party to vote in favor. It now goes to the Senate for consideration.

## **Two workers pulled alive from tunnel nine days after Nepal-Tibet flood** 

![Rescue workers surround survivor on orange stretcher, all covered in mud](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/db4cb8002d8ff5c389774138e2f706493280eedb/70_0_1066_853/master/1066.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Two people have been pulled out alive from a hydropower tunnel in Nepal nine days after being trapped by deadly flash floods that buried the project in deep mud. The workers were rescued during a continuing operation at the Trishuli 3A tunnel in one of the areas worst affected by last week’s deadly Nepal floods.

Officials said the men had been flown to a hospital in Kathmandu. Nepal’s communication minister, Bikram Timilsina, said officials still hoped more workers would be rescued. At least 1,287 people were killed in the floods and more than 5,000 are missing, Nepal’s disaster management agency said.

## **Missouri supreme court throws out new congressional maps backed by Trump**

![People gathered at the Missouri statehouse under dome and decorated ceiling](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/1a149a9b00972d5a2a6a051aaa33af932440dbb4/250_0_2500_2000/master/2500.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

The Missouri supreme court has unanimously blocked new congressional districts backed by Trump from being used in the November election, and ordered a statewide vote on whether to uphold or reject the new map. The decision on Thursday marks a rare setback for Republicans in a national redistricting battle.

Missouri’s new districts were used in the August primaries. The state supreme court said they cannot be used in the November general election – or ever again – unless voters first uphold the new districts, after citizens submitted petition signatures seeking a referendum.
