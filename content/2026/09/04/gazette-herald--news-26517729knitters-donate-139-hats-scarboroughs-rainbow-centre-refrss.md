A Scarborough charity has received a boost from a winter community initiative.

The Rainbow Centre, which supports the homeless, was the focus of a challenge launched by Threads & Tales, Thornton Le Dale, to knit 100 hats.

The hats were aimed at helping those in need during the colder months.

King Cole Yarn, Threads & Tales and HPE Print Pickering sponsored the project.

Both seasoned and beginner knitters joined the cause, collecting a free ball of wool and a free knitting pattern from Threads & Tales and returning the completed hats to a collection point at the shop.

The challenge was met with enthusiasm, with 139 hats being handed in, exceeding the initial target.

Alongside the hats, 10 scarves and eight cardigans were also donated.

The knitted items were delivered to the Rainbow Centre on September 1, 2026, ahead of the winter season.

The initiative organisers extended their gratitude to all participants who contributed to supporting the Rainbow Centre community.
