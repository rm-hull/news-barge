YORKSHIRE Building Society is celebrating decades of loyalty from customers and colleagues.

The mutual has sent 50 bespoke hampers to some of its longest-standing and most loyal members.

Hampers were also given to 15 of its longest-serving colleagues.

Tina Hughes, director of savings at Yorkshire Building Society, said: "As a mutual organisation, our customers and colleagues are at the very heart of everything we do."

At the Huddersfield branch, Mr and Mrs Hatch, members since 1969, were among those recognised.

Roz Oak, a customer consultant at the society’s Tenby branch, was also honoured for nearly 50 years of service.

Ms Oak said: "One of the most rewarding parts of my role has been seeing generations of the same families come through our doors."

The events were part of the society’s UK Savings Week celebrations.
