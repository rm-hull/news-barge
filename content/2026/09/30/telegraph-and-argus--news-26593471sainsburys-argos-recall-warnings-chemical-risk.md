Three tote bags sold by Sainsbury’s and Argos have been urgently recalled due to a serious chemical risk.

The bags, part of the Tu range, were found to contain excessive concentrations of phthalates, chemical compounds used to soften plastics that may pose health risks, particularly to children.

A Sainsbury’s spokesperson said: "Sainsbury’s are recalling the above products due to excessive concentrations of phthalates, which pose a chemical safety risk.

"Prolonged exposure to phthalates may be harmful to human health.

"As a result, the products don't meet Sainsbury’s safety requirements.

"We're asking customers who have purchased any of these items to stop using them and return them to their local Sainsbury’s store for a full refund."

The recall affects the Tu Pu Tan Curved Top Shopper (item number 147160412), the Tu Black Croc Winged Tote Bag (item number 147166081), and the Tu Brown Belt Handled Core Shopper (item number 147789704).

These bags were available from selected Sainsbury’s stores, the Tu website, and the Argos website.

A spokesperson for the Office for Product Safety & Standards said: "The products present a serious chemical risk because they contain excessive concentrations of phthalates.

"Phthalates may harm the health of children, potentially causing damage to the reproductive system.

"The products don't meet the requirements of the REACH Enforcement Regulations 2008.

"The products have been recalled from end users by Argos and Sainsbury’s.

"We recommend you stop using the products immediately and return them to your nearest Sainsbury’s or Argos store for a full refund."

An Argos spokesperson said: "Argos are recalling the above products due to excessive concentrations of phthalates, which pose a chemical safety risk.

"Prolonged exposure to phthalates may be harmful to human health.

"As a result, the products don't meet Argos safety requirements.

"We're asking customers who have purchased any of these items to stop using them and return them to their local Argos store for a full refund.

"Please share this recall notice with anyone who may have purchased, received or used one of these products.

"No other products have been affected by this issue. Argos would like to apologise for any inconvenience caused."

Customers seeking further information can visit the Sainsbury’s website at sainsburys.co.uk/help or call the Sainsbury’s careline on 0800 028 8303.

Argos customers can visit argos.co.uk or call 0800 011 3462.
