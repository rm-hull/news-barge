A red Ford Fiesta rolled over on a busy Darlington road after a late-night crash.

Durham Police said officers were made aware of the crash on the A167 near Hurworth at around 11.45pm on Tuesday (September 29).

Pictures show the car still at the roadside with airbags deployed, visible damage and wrapped in police tape.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21519479.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A red Ford Fiesta rolled on its side on the A167 near Hurtworth.

No injuries were reported.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: “Police were made aware of an incident at 11.45pm yesterday (September 29) in which a Ford Fiesta had rolled on the A167 near Hurworth.

“The driver has arranged their own recovery of the vehicle.”
