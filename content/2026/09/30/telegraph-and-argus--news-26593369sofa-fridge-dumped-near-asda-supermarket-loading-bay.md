A sofa and a fridge have been dumped behind a busy Bradford supermarket.

The items were spotted at the Asda supermarket on Rooley Lane's loading bay area.

The waste was seen by the Telegraph & Argus on Tuesday afternoon.

## Council says landowner responsible

Bradford Council said the fly-tipping had not been reported to the authority.

A spokesperson said: "This fly-tipping has not been reported to us, so we were not aware of it.

"Because this has taken place on privately-owned land, it is the landowner's responsibility to remove the waste or arrange for it to be removed."

The council added that anyone arranging for the waste to be cleared must ensure the work is carried out by a licensed contractor.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21518516.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Murky water can be seen by the fly-tipping at the ASDA superstore. *(Image: T&A)*

## CCTV could help investigation

The spokesperson added: "If they use a contractor to remove the waste, they need to ensure they are an authorised waste carrier and that the contractor gives them a Controlled Waste Transfer Note.

"If Asda have CCTV footage of the waste being fly-tipped we can carry out an investigation."

An Asda spokesperson confirmed to the T&A that a clearance has been arranged.
