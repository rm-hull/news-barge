A Harrogate care home is inviting local dogs and their owners to take part in a charity dog show this weekend.

Southlands Bupa on Ripon Road will host the event on Saturday (3rd October), with seven categories and prizes up for grabs.

The show will be judged by Laura Biggs, from Laura’s Pets Dog Walking & Pet Services Harrogate.

A spokesperson for Southlands said:

> “This is something our residents absolutely love and are really looking forward to, and we’re now looking for some lovely dogs to come along and take part!”

Owners are invited to make an optional £5 registration donation to support the home’s Charity of the Year, Saint Michael’s Hospice.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6abcba919edc0cceb2f.jpg)

All money raised will go to the charity, with Bupa matching donations at the end of the year.

The spokesperson added:

> “We can’t wait to see all the wagging tails and happy faces!”

*To enter, call 01423 526203 and ask to speak to Julie, Helene or Aron to register your dog.*
