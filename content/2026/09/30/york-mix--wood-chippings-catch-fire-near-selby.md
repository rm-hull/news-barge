Firefighters in North Yorkshire have been dealing with a blaze at a business premises which created smoke visible from the M62.

Crews were called to wood waste and wood chippings on fire on Heck and Pollington Lane, near Great Heck, yesterday (Tuesday 29th September) at 23:41 BST.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a fire of about 323 sq ft (30 sq m) was being fought by crews from across the county, including appliances from Selby, Harrogate, Thirsk and Richmond. Crews from Humberside and South Yorkshire had also attended.

The fire service said it was expected they would be at the scene for most of the morning.
