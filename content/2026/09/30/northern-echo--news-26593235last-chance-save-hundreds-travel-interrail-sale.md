Brits have one last chance to access 33 European countries for up to half the usual amount when they buy a popular rail pass, saving hundreds of pounds.

Interrailing is a popular way to explore Europe and now tickets are up to half price, but you need to be quick as the sale ends today (September 30).

While you need to be fast at securing your ticket, it must be used within 11 months, so it's ideal if you know you’ll be travelling soon.

Both flexi and continuous passes are included in the sale, and you can select which one suits you best based on your age and your travel plans.

## Interrail's final tickets are available for up to half price

The Interrail sale includes 50% off travel for one, two and three continuous months, saving young travellers (12-27 years) hundreds of pounds.

The sale also includes tickets for adults and seniors, meaning they can save hundreds too.

One month for young travellers is usually £454 but is discounted to £227, while two months will cost £270 instead of £539.

If you’re travelling for three continuous months, you’ll pay £311 instead of £624.

## 9 of the most scenic rail routes in the UK

If you'd like to travel first class, you can still save up to 50%.

The tickets allow you to travel on as many trains as you want during the time period you select.

## Which pass is right for me?

If you’re wondering which pass to use, you can check Interrail’s guide, which explains that anyone travelling to more than one country would benefit from having the global pass.

For those travelling to one country, the one country pass is the one to choose.

A plus pass (beta) is ideal if you’d like to reserve seats on your trains.
