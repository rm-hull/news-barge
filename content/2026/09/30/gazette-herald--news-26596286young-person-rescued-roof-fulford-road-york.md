A YOUNG person has been rescued from the roof of a 20-foot-tall building in York.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service crews from York and Acomb were called to Fulford Road at 11.32pm last night (September 29) to rescue a young person from the roof of a 20-foot-high building.
