# Horsforth police incident in pictures as 'large police cordon' in place

## A large police presence is in the area.

![](https://i2-prod.examinerlive.co.uk/incoming/article34698064.ece/ALTERNATES/s810/0_128ca7bc-6fce-40d0-b08d-174c7f4d8d23.jpg)

These pictures show how a "significant" area has been cordoned off by police in Leeds.

Pictures taken from the scene show "a large police cordon" in place in Lister Hill in Horsforth. According to local reports, the cordon has been in place following an "incident" near Screwfix.

The nature of the incident is not yet known. West Yorkshire Police has been contacted for more information. Pictures show a number of plain-clothed officers among the police response.
