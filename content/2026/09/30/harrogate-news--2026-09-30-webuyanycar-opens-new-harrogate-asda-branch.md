Online automotive buying service Webuyanycar has expanded its presence in North Yorkshire this week with the launch of a second branch in Harrogate.

The new site, structured as a self-sustainable pod running entirely on solar power, has opened in the car park of the Harrogate ASDA store on Dragon Road to meet rising local demand. The firm operates a completely paperless appraisal process, allowing motorists to finalise online valuations via instant bank transfers on-site.

The expansion marks the brand’s second footprint in the immediate town alongside its established seven-day branch on Bower Road, with a third regional site operating ten miles north at the Ripon Morrisons.

> **Richard Evans, Head of Technical Services at Webuyanycar, said:** The opening was a testament to the company’s regional growth, aiming to provide local motorists with “more local branches in the area, saving time, hassle and making the whole process even more convenient.
