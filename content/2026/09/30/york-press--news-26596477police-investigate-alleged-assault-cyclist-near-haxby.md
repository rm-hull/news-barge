POLICE are investigating the alleged assault of a cyclist in York.

The incident took placed on the approach to Haxby, between the A1237 and the railway crossing, at around 5.45pm on Thursday (September 24).

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said the cyclists had been approached by two individuals riding an electric bike.

They added: "They filmed themselves attempting to push the victim from his bicycle before riding off towards Haxby.

"We’re appealing to anyone who witnessed the incident, saw the electric bike in the area, or has any relevant dashcam or CCTV footage to come forward."

Those unable to use online options can call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or email PC Maddi Feeney at [maddi.feeney@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:maddi.feeney@northyorkshire.police.uk.)
