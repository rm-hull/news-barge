An East Yorkshire housebuilder has sponsored the 150th annual Driffield Show.

Barratt and David Wilson Homes, which is currently building new homes in Driffield at Mortimer Park and Porters Way, backed this summer’s historic show.

Cheryl Lynch, a spokesperson for the Driffield Show, said: "We were delighted to have the support of Barratt and David Wilson Homes at the 2026 Driffield Show and are extremely grateful for its sponsorship.

"Its support helped us to deliver another successful Show for our visitors."

The Driffield Show, a highlight of the town’s social calendar, features entertainment, food and craft stalls, livestock competitions, and displays of modern farm machinery.

Paul Hogan, sales director at Barratt and David Wilson Homes Yorkshire East, said: "We're delighted to have sponsored the 150th Driffield Show.

"The event has a huge impact on the community, and it was extra special to be able to sponsor the event in this milestone year."
