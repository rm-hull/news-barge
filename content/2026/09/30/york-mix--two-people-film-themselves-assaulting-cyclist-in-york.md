Police are investigating after two people filmed themselves assaulting a cyclist in York.

It happened at around **5.45pm last Thursday**(24 September) on the** approach to Haxby**, between the A1237 and the railway crossing.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A man was cycling home when he was approached by two individuals riding an electric bike.

#### Incident location

“They filmed themselves attempting to push the victim from his bicycle before riding off towards Haxby.

“We’re appealing to anyone who witnessed the incident, saw the electric bike in the area, or has any relevant dashcam or CCTV footage to come forward.”
