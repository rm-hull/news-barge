# Pudsey police probe ramped up in search for three men after GBH pub attack

## A teenager suffered serious head injuries

A CCTV appeal has been issued after a teenager suffered serious head injuries outside a pub in Pudsey.

West Yorkshire Police said they received a report of grievous bodily harm outside the Crossed Shuttle Wetherspoons pub in Manor House Street, Pudsey. This took place on Saturday, September 5, at 1.20am.

The victim, a teenage man, suffered serious head injuries. Now, the police are looking to identify three men. West Yorkshire Police said: "We want to identify these three men following a report of grievous bodily harm outside a pub in Pudsey.

"The incident happened outside the Crossed Shuttle Wetherspoons pub in Manor House Street, on Saturday 5 September at 1.20am.

"The victim, a male in his teens, suffered serious head injuries as a result of the incident."

If you have any information, you can contact the police by calling 101 or by using live chat online. You can quote reference number 13260505419.
