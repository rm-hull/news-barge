A music bingo night with a spooky twist is returning for Halloween.

Wold Top Brewery will host the Halloween Singo Bingo event at Hunmanby Grange on Friday, October 30.

Sam Smith from Wold Top’s events team said: "Get ready for a brilliant evening of music, laughs, and a little bit of friendly competition as Singo Bingo returns to Wold Top for a Halloween special, where we’re encouraging people to dress up.

"Expect a fun-filled night with a great mix of music, which is bound to get everyone singing along.

"There will also be delicious jacket potatoes from Yorkshire Spuds and camping for those brave enough."

Tickets are priced at £16 per person and include a pint or soft drink on arrival.

A camping pitch can also be booked for an additional £12.

The brewery, based on the Mellor family farm near Filey, uses home-grown barley and borehole water to produce its beers.
