A stylish period home has hit the market in York.

The two-bedroom terraced house is for sale in Albemarle Road, next to Little Knavesmire, for £375,000.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said it is a “charming” period home that “retains a wealth of character features and has been attractively decorated throughout in keeping with the age and style of the property”.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21517714.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the two-bedroom period house for sale in Albemarle Road, York, for £375,000 *(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

An enclosed forecourt to the front provides access to the entrance door, opening into a welcoming tiled hallway.

The living room is positioned to the front of the property and features timber flooring, a wood-burning stove and a shelved alcove.

Beyond the living room is the dining room leading to the kitchen.

And beyond the kitchen is a rear lobby and utility area.
