A British jeweller is marking a landmark anniversary in Teesside and is inviting customers to join in the celebrations.

F. Hinds is celebrating 25 years at its Wellington Square Shopping Centre store in Stockton, which first opened its doors in 2001.

To thank customers for their support over the years, the family-owned jeweller is inviting shoppers to visit the store before Sunday (October 4) to take part in its anniversary celebrations, including a special draw to win a £50 gift card.

The Stockton branch has become a familiar choice for Teesside shoppers searching for engagement rings, wedding jewellery, anniversary gifts and Christmas presents over the last quarter century.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/16970026.jpg?type=mds-article-620)F. Hinds jewellery *(Image: F Hinds)*

The Stockton anniversary coincides with another major milestone for F. Hinds, as they celebrate 170 years in business.

Founded by George Henry Hinds in 1856, the company continues as an independent family-owned retailer now run by the sixth generation of the Hinds family.

Jeremy Hinds, sales director at F. Hinds, said: "Celebrating 25 years in Stockton-on-Tees is a very special milestone for us.

"When we first opened the doors in 2001, we hoped to build a store that local people would return to time and again, and it is wonderful to be celebrating a quarter of a century in the town."

He added: "Jewellery plays a part in so many important moments in people's lives, and over the past 25 years our Stockton team has been there for countless engagements, weddings, birthdays, anniversaries and celebrations."

F. Hinds now has 117 stores across England, Scotland and Wales and has won several industry awards over the years.

Customers can join in the anniversary celebrations and enter the draw by visiting the Wellington Square branch before Sunday, October 4.
