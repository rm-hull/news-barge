Doctor Who star Samuel Anderson will headline a North East comic convention for fans of television, gaming and pop culture.

Samuel Anderson, who played Danny Pink in the sci-fi series, will appear at Teesside Unleashed Comic-Con in Middlesbrough this autumn.

The event takes place on Sunday, October 4, at Teesside University Students’ Union, with celebrity guests, cosplay and retro gaming.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21516816.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Supplied)*

Anderson will meet fans throughout the day and answer questions on stage at 2pm.

Kathryn Marshall, co-owner of Unleashed Events, said: "The response to our recent events, including the BBC Look North coverage from Sheffield, has demonstrated the appetite for accessible and welcoming pop-culture events across the North.

"We are delighted to bring Unleashed back to Teesside with Samuel Anderson, a packed programme of activities and 90 trader tables representing independent artists, creators and small businesses.

"We want people of every age and level of fandom to feel welcome, whether they arrive in full cosplay or are attending their first Comic-Con."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21516815.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Samuel Anderson as Danny. *(Image: ADRIAN ROGERS)*

Attractions include a Gremlins display, a TARDIS, a craft zone, an interactive graffiti wall, a 360-degree photobooth and gaming activities.

A cosplay contest for under-10s starts at 12.15pm, followed by stage games at 1pm and the main Cosplay Masquerade at 3pm.

Visitors can relax and read in the quieter Comic Book Zone on the second floor.

Around 90 trader tables will sell comics, artwork, collectibles and merchandise from independent artists and small businesses.

The event runs from 11am to 4pm, with early entry from 10am.

Early-entry tickets bought in advance cost £12 for adults and £6 for children, with family discounts online.
