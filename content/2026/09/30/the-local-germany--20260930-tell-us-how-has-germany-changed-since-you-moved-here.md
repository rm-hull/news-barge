From the shifting political landscape to everyday developments – like the growing number of places that will accept a credit card – Germany has seen a lot of changes in the last few years.

We're keen to hear what it's been like from your perspective: how has the country evolved since you first arrived, whether that was three years ago or two decades ago?

Was it easy to get a flat 10 years ago? What were the prices like for eating out? Have the efforts to reduce bureaucracy and digitalise services made any difference? What else has changed about Germany and how has it affected your life?

Whether you've settled in one of the big cities or moved around, we'd love to hear your views. Answer the questions in our short survey below. If you can't access the survey, click here.
