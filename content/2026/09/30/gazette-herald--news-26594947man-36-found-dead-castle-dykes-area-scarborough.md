A 36-year-old man has been found dead in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police has launched an appeal on behalf of the Coroner’s Office to help trace the relatives of Bradley Morgan, who lived in Scarborough.




A force spokesperson said: “He sadly died in the Castle Dykes area of the town on Monday (September 28).

“There are no suspicious circumstances.

“If you knew Bradley or know of any relatives that we can contact, please email [coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting reference number 12260190399.”
