A popular beer and gin festival is set to return for another weekend of drinks, music and community spirit.

The Glusburn and Cross Hills Beer and Gin Festival will take place at Glusburn Institute on Friday, October 2, and Saturday, October 3.

The festival will offer more than 20 cask and keg beers, along with artisan gins, lager and cider.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21513280.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Glusburn and Cross Hills Beer Festival will be held on Friday and Saturday *(Image: UGC)*

A packed line-up of live music is also planned, with Hit The Lights Band opening the festival on Friday night.

Saturday’s line-up includes Sharp Dressed Men, Taila-Mae, Daniel Chapman and Sarah Widdup, with Night Patrol closing the evening.

A spokesperson for the Glusburn and Cross Hills Beer and Gin Festival said the event had become an important date in the Glusburn Institute calendar, bringing people together and raising funds to maintain the Grade II-listed community building.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21513285.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Glusburn and Cross Hills Beer Festival will be held on Friday and Saturday *(Image: UGC)*

The Institute’s Old Chapel will offer a quieter place to sit, enjoy a drink and catch up with friends.

Food will be available throughout the weekend from Dave’s Artisan Wood-Fired Pizza.

Leightons Estate Agency is sponsoring the festival, alongside businesses that have sponsored barrels and contributed to the event.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21513314.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Glusburn and Cross Hills Beer and Gin Festival will be held on Friday and Saturday *(Image: UGC)*

Admission is included with all tickets, allowing visitors to come and go throughout the weekend without needing separate tickets for different sessions.

The festival will run from 4pm to 11.30pm on Friday and from 12pm to 11.30pm on Saturday.

A token system will be used for drinks, with both cash and card payments accepted.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21513303.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Glusburn Institute will host the Glusburn and Cross Hills Beer Festival *(Image: UGC)*

The spokesperson said: "The beer and gin festival has become a highlight in the calendar at Glusburn Institute.

"It’s about much more than just beer and gin – it’s a chance to bring people together, showcase some brilliant live music and create a great atmosphere in the building.

"We’re incredibly grateful to all the local businesses who support the festival through sponsorship, as well as all the volunteers who help make the weekend happen.

"Most importantly, the festival raises valuable funds for both Glusburn Institute and 1st Kildwick and Farnhill Scout Group.

"Both organisations play an important role in our local community, and the support people give simply by coming along and enjoying the weekend makes a genuine difference."

Advance ticket sales have been strong, and only a limited number of pre-booked tickets remain.

To book tickets, visit www.gicac.org.uk
