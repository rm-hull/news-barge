A PERSON has been rescued after getting trapped inside their car after driving into a hedge.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service say both of their Scarborough engines were called to the A165, close to Filey, after a crash involving a car driving into a hedge and a person becoming stuck inside.

“Crews arrived on scene and liaised with paramedics already in attendance who confirmed one occupant was out on arrival and the other climbed out the back of vehicle, both with minor injuries,” said a spokesperson for the service.

They added that fire crews “assisted with scene safety” before leaving.
