## Our live feed has now finished.

* A large fire at a biomass company depot in North Yorkshire is being brought under control by firefighters
* Residents and anyone passing by north of the village of Heck in the direction of Selby are being asked to keep doors and windows closed
* Police are asking people to follow Fire Service advice
* Firefighters are currently at the scene in Heck and Pollington Lane
* North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the fire could last for days
* Crews were first called at 11.41pm yesterday (Tuesday, September 29)
