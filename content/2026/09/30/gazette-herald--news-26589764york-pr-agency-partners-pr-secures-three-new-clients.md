A PR agency has secured three new client contracts across the engineering, technology, and rail sectors.

Partners PR, based in York, announced the new business wins, highlighting growing demand for strategic communications in B2B industries.

Monica Green, co-owner and director of Partners PR, said: "We are delighted to be working with these three ambitious businesses, each of which has a compelling story to tell and clear growth ambitions.

"What particularly attracted us to these appointments is the opportunity to use PR strategically, not simply to generate publicity, but to build reputation, demonstrate expertise and help our clients achieve their wider business objectives.

"Although the three businesses operate in very different markets, they all recognise the value of being visible, credible and well known among the people who matter to their growth."

The agency has been appointed by Air Control Industries, IT Covered, and Raspberry Software.

Air Control Industries, an engineering specialist, will work with Partners PR to launch and promote Hyroic Systems, its new modular range of energy-efficient drying solutions developed for food and beverage processing and packaging operations.

IT Covered, a York-based strategic IT services provider supporting SMEs across Yorkshire, has also brought Partners PR on board to boost its profile.

The agency will position IT Covered as experts in IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud solutions, and AI.

IT Covered recently secured a contract to provide York Racecourse with strategic IT support, including managing its IT infrastructure, cloud services, cybersecurity systems and training, as well as advising on its AI strategy.

The third client, Raspberry Software, is a specialist software business serving the rail industry.

Partners PR will help raise its profile among key industry stakeholders, supporting Raspberry Software’s sales strategy and growth plans.

The agency’s client base spans manufacturing and engineering, rail, food processing, property and construction, professional services, and the charity sector.
