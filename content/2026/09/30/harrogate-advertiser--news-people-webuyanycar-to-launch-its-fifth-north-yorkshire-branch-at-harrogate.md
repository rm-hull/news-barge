# Webuyanycar to launch its fifth North Yorkshire branch at Harrogate superstore

## The UK's largest online car-buying service has announced it is to open a new shop in Harrogate.

High-profile company webuyanycar has confirmed the opening of a new branch this week located at Harrogate ASDA superstore on Bower Road.

The celebrated brand that lets people sell their cars or vans quickly without the hassle of a private sale says its latest physical store will offer better access for local customers

The company says the branch will be fully self-sustainable, running off solar power, and operates a completely paperless sales process, allowing for a more streamlined and environmentally friendly customer experience.

![High-profile online company webuyanycar has confirmed the opening of a new branch in Harrogate this week. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/people/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


“Our latest opening in North Yorkshire is a testament to the continued growth of webuyanycar and our support for customers in the surrounding areas, who we have been buying many thousands of cars from over the years,” said Richard Evans, Head of Technical Services at webuyanycar.

"We aim to provide our customers with even more local branches in the area, saving time, hassle and making the whole process even more convenient.”

webuyanycar runs more than 550 local branches nationwide, where its experts finalise purchases from car owners who have secured a free, no obligation valuation for their motor via the website in less than 30 seconds.

Headquartered in Manchester, the automotive firm is one of the most recognised British brands with more than 350,000 customer reviews on Trustpilot.

Richard Evans said: “We are continuing to grow and put customers back in the driving seat, as more and more motorists realise, they can negotiate a better deal by not part-exchanging.

"Our new Harrogate branch will be open seven days a week and will offer instant bank transfers to motorists, saving valuable time.”

The new branch at Harrogate ASDA is the second webuyanycar within a 10 mile radius, along with an existing one at Ripon Morrisons on Harrogate Road, Ripon.

Since it was founded in 2006, the company has bought more than five million cars and vans and also operates in the USA.

Unlike auction-comparison platforms, webuyanycar makes a direct offer for your vehicle rather than sourcing bids from third-party dealers.
