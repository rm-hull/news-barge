A specialist cleaning company in Whitby has entered liquidation owing more than £132,000, according to a liquidator’s report.

More than half of this figure was owed to HMRC and almost half was owed to its sole director, the report also noted.

Seaside Cottages (Whitby) Ltd of Rohilla Close, Whitby, was incorporated in 2022 and entered a creditors’ voluntary liquidation on August 25.

London-based Exigen Group was appointed liquidator.

Companies House records Rachel Hall as sole director of the cleaning company.

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs outlines the financial situation.

It said Seaside Cottages (Whitby) Limited had assets of £3,600.

There were no preferential creditors – those who are paid first.

But HMRC was a secondary preferential creditor – one who is paid next.

It was owed £40,326.89 in VAT and £26,884.59 in PAYE, giving £67,211.48 in total.

Altogether, this left a deficiency of £63,611.48 in total.

The statement also lists unsecured creditors owed £68,600 in total.

This was made up of £5,000 owed to a non-preferential creditor and £63,600 owed to a director.

This, the report added, gave a deficiency with regard to creditors of £132,211.48.

With the business having issued and called up capital of £100,000, this gave a total deficit of £123,311.48 owed.
