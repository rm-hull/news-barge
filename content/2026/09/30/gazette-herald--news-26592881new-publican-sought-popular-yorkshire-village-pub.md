A publican is being sought to take over a popular village pub in North Yorkshire.

The Grey Horse in Brayton, near Selby, is searching for a new publican to take on the pub.

Owners the Stonegate Pub Partners said the Grey Horse, in Doncaster Road, has a “traditional community pub feel” and attracts a “mix of regulars and visitors.”

“Live sport, quiz nights and regular entertainment form an important part of its offer throughout the week,” a Stonegate spokesperson said.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21517759.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Beer garden at the Grey Horse in Barlby, near Selby *(Image: Stonegate Group)*

The dog-friendly pub has a spacious bar area with pool and darts and outside is a large beer garden.

Stonegate Pub Partners said it is “looking for a new publican to build on the pub’s existing community focus and continue developing its offer".

Stephen Marritt, regional manager at Stonegate Pub Partners, said: “The Grey Horse has a real community feel and is an established part of village life in Brayton.

“It already has a strong foundation, with live sport, entertainment and a fantastic outdoor space. We’re looking for a publican who can build on that and continue making the Grey Horse a welcoming pub for the local community.”

The Grey Horse is currently available through Stonegate Pub Partners on a fixed-term tenancy.
