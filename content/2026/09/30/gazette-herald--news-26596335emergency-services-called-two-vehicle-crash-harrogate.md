Emergency services have been called to a two-vehicle crash in North Yorkshire.

A Harrogate crew from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service arrived at the scene in Granby Road, Harrogate, at 7.06am on Wednesday (September 30).




The incident was left with North Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service after firefighters made the area safe using stabilizer blocks.

The Press has contacted Yorkshire Ambulance Service for further comment.
