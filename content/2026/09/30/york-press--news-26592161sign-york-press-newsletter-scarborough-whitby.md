Stay up to date with the stories shaping the Yorkshire coast with our new Yorkshire Coast News email bulletin.

We want to bring readers the local stories that matter most.

That's why we're delighted to launch a new email newsletter from The Press dedicated to life on the Yorkshire coast, covering the latest news, community stories and updates from seaside towns and villages across the region.

Sent every Wednesday and Friday, the Yorkshire Coast News newsletter delivers a lunchtime round-up of news closer to home from across Whitby, Scarborough, Robin Hood's Bay and Filey, straight to your inbox.

The newsletter brings together the latest and biggest stories from our team across news, crime, local government, planning and development and local business, as well as nostalgia and things to do locally with this article here an example of news from Scarborough.

From council decisions affecting coastal communities to tourism updates, seaside events and community stories from towns and villages along the Yorkshire coast, it helps you stay on top of the headlines that matter in your area.

Signing up is simple.

Just visit our newsletters page here and add the Yorkshire Coast News to your newsletter subscriptions.

You'll also be able to choose from more than 15 other newsletters from The Press, including our flagship daily newsletter, the Morning Briefing, which delivers a carefully curated round-up of the morning's top headlines fresh off the press.

Signing up to newsletters from The Press means you'll never miss the updates that matter to you, whether that's checking in on the latest news, crime or business stories, keeping up to date with sport or breaking news, or looking for the best things to do in the city.

Readers with a digital subscription can also receive our competitions newsletter for the opportunity to win exciting prizes every month, including tickets to local events, restaurants and food, hotel stays, sports tickets and memorabilia and more.

It's free to sign up to York Press newsletters, and if you change your mind, you can unsubscribe from our newsletters at any time.

Just click the 'unsubscribe' link at the bottom of a newsletter or log into your account to view and amend your newsletter subscriptions.
