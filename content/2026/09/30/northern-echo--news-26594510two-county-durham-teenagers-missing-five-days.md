Police are searching for two County Durham teenagers who have been missing for five days.

Portia, 16, from Spennymoor, and Alfie, 17, from Bishop Auckland, were last seen on September 25.

The teenagers are believed to have travelled together and may have gone to the Northumbria area, possibly Wallsend.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21519952.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portia, 16, from Spennymoor, and Alfie, 17, from Bishop Auckland, were last seen on September 25. *(Image: DURHAM CONSTABULARY)*

Portia is 5ft 3in tall with long blonde hair and was last seen wearing a grey fur coat.

She previously went missing for over 72 hours on September 17.

Alfie is 5ft 6in tall with short brown hair and was last seen wearing a black coat, pink T-shirt, black joggers and black Crocs.

Anyone who can help find the teenagers should call 999.
