Viewers of the Great British Bake Off have all been talking about Nigella Lawson’s strange habit on the show.

The new judge was revealed to be taking over from Dame Prue Leith after she announced in January she would be stepping down from her role.

Many fans of the show have loved watching Nigella on Bake Off but have noticed her strange way of trying the bakes.

## Nigella Lawson’s strange Bake Off habit

Many avid Bake Off watchers have taken to social media to share their thoughts on the way Nigella eats.

One person said: “I love seeing Nigella tucking in to the cookies.

“She’s not being polite. She’s getting in there. Brilliant!”

Another person said: “Here again for Nigella’s big massive bites.”

A third person noted: “I love Nigella so much.

“What do you mean you are going to take them huge mouthfuls of cake but not eat and iced gem in one #GBBO.”
