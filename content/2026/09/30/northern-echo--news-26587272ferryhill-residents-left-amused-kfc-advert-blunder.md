Ferryhill residents were left baffled after spotting a glaring error on a KFC advert in the town centre.

The bus stop poster, promoting the fried chicken chain's limited-edition Dirty Louisiana burger, tells passers-by they can pick one up just a "three minute walk" away at the Thinford services branch.

However, locals were quick to point out that walking to the services from the bus stop on Ferryhill's Main Street would take almost 30 minutes.

![KFC advert.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21509629.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: IAN TEMPLETON)*

Google Maps suggests the walk, following the route of the A167, would take 28 minutes.

The mistake was highlighted in a local Facebook group, prompting amusement from residents.

One asked: "Would you even get there that quick in a car?"

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21515342.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The walk from Ferryhill Marketplace to the KFC at Thinford Services. *(Image: GOOGLE MAPS)*

KFC declined to comment.
