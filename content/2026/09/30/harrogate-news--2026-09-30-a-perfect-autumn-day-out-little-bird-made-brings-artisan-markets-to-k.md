As October arrives to transform North Yorkshire’s landscape into a backdrop of vibrant golds and deep ambers, residents and visitors are being invited to experience a weekend of outdoor shopping and local heritage.

The popular regional collective **Little Bird Made** has confirmed a double-header of artisan markets across the district this weekend, providing a platform for independent producers and makers to showcase their handcrafted goods.

The weekend launches on **Saturday, 3 October**, with the artisans setting up in the grounds of** Knaresborough Castle**from** 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM**. Visitors will be able to browse bespoke handmade crafts, art, and photography, alongside curated local food and drink, all while taking in the iconic panoramic views across the River Nidd and the historic viaduct.

On **Sunday, 4 October**, the collective moves to** Harrogate’s Grade II listed Valley Gardens**. Shoppers can stroll through the tree-lined pathways between** 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM** to meet independent makers and discover unique, one-of-a-kind autumn finds.

## Local Makers Hold the Line

The monthly events continue to foster strong ties within the North Yorkshire independent business community.

> **Jackie Crozier, Managing Director of Little Bird Made, highlighted the seasonal draw of both historic backdrops:**
>
> October is our favourite time of year to bring our markets to North Yorkshire. Both Knaresborough Castle and Harrogate’s Valley Gardens offer truly breathtaking backdrops as the autumn leaves begin to turn. It is a wonderful opportunity for families and visitors to spend the day outdoors exploring these iconic grounds while discovering exceptional handmade goods.

The sentiment is matched by long-term traders who rely on the regular face-to-face connection with the district’s shoppers.

> **Lita Krumina, owner of local floral business Smitten with Flowers, emphasised the value of this direct local alignment:**
>
> We’ve been trading in Harrogate since December 2023 and have no intention of stopping. The more people who choose to support small local businesses and specifically sustainable, locally grown seasonal flowers, the better.

## Add an Educational Twist to Your Weekend

For those looking to map out a full itinerary, the weekend has been paired with a series of free, expert-led heritage walks around the spa town.

Free guided walking tours will depart from the **Harrogate War Memorial**—directly opposite Bettys Tea Rooms—at** 10:30 AM and 12:00 Noon on Sunday**. These educational routes explore Harrogate’s rich spa heritage before concluding near the entrance of the Valley Gardens market, making for a seamless autumn day out.

*Independent makers looking to join the curated collective or apply for a stall ahead of the upcoming festive winter season can find the digital application process on the official Little Bird Made platform.*
