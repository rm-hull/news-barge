Thornton Dale Under-14s are proudly sporting new training and matchday tops thanks to generous sponsorship from S Taylor & Sons Ltd, a bespoke joinery business based in Pickering.

The new kit ensures the young team look the part both on and off the pitch as they compete in Division 4 of the York Junior League.

Coach Jamie Dennison said: “We are extremely grateful to S Taylor & Sons for their generous sponsorship and continued support of local grassroots sport. Backing from local businesses is hugely appreciated and helps our club grow and thrive.”

The club currently have one senior side and three junior sides and look to grow their junior section.
