York will be transformed into a sparkling winter wonderland for one of the city's most popular events next year.

The York Ice Trail will return on February 6 and 7, 2027, bringing a spectacular collection of ice sculptures to the city for a weekend of family fun.

Themed 'Legends Unleashed', the sculptures will be inspired by history and folklore.




![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21521440.jpg?type=mds-article-620)"The York Ice Trail is always a special moment in the city’s winter calendar" *(Image: Make It York)*

Sarah Loftus, managing director of Make It York, said: "The York Ice Trail is always a special moment in the city’s winter calendar.

"It’s a brilliant opportunity for families, residents and visitors to explore York, discover incredible ice sculptures and enjoy a little winter magic along the way.

"This year’s legends theme and the new added experiences are set to make it extra special."

![Themed 'Legends Unleashed', the sculptures will be inspired by history and folklore](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21521441.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Themed 'Legends Unleashed', the sculptures will be inspired by history and folklore *(Image: Make It York)*

The event is free and family-friendly, with visitors able to explore York in a unique way - admiring the artwork carved by experts.

Sculptures will be on display from 10.30am to 4pm on both days.

The trail is curated by Make It York and partners and will be created by Icebox, the UK and Europe’s leading ice sculpture specialist.

A full map of the trail will be available from February 1, 2027 online and at the Visit York Visitor Information Centre at 28 Back Swinegate.

Updates and further details will be shared on visityork.org/ice-trail and on @VisitYork social media channels.
