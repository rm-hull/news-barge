Police have released an image of a third man they would like to identify following a report of grievous bodily harm outside a Wetherspoons.

The incident happened outside the Crossed Shuttle pub on Manor House Street in Pudsey at around 1.20am on Saturday, September 5.

According to West Yorkshire Police, the victim, a male in his teens, suffered serious head injuries.

Officers have now updated their appeal and are seeking to identify three men in connection with the investigation.

Anyone who can identify the pictured men, or who has information that could assist the investigation, is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police online via the force's website using Live Chat.

Please quote reference 13260505419 and enquiries into the incident remain ongoing.
