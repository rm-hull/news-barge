A second round of funding will help businesses in York and North Yorkshire build healthier, more supportive workplaces.

Pro-Development, a York-based employee engagement and people development firm, has secured £200,000 to continue its work under the Thriving Workplaces and People programme.

The funding will support employers to improve workplace wellbeing, engagement, retention and inclusion.

Michelle Mook, managing director at Pro-Development, said: "The first round of funding demonstrated just how much demand there is from businesses for practical support around their people.

"Employers rarely have just one challenge – issues around wellbeing, retention, management and inclusion are often closely connected."

The programme forms part of the Department for Work and Pensions-funded Get Britain Working Economic Inactivity Trailblazer and is led locally by the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority.

It aims to help organisations create environments where people can enter work, stay in work and thrive.

To deliver the scheme, Pro-Development is working with McMillan and Associates HR, Hälsa Wellbeing and Spectrum First to offer support covering employee engagement, leadership and management, HR, workplace wellbeing, and neurodiversity.

Businesses across the region can access fully funded workshops, masterclasses, coaching, consultancy and tailored advice.

The first round of funding supported more than 70 businesses and delivered £283,650 of support across York and North Yorkshire in 2025.

The Mayor of York and North Yorkshire, David Skaith, said: "Our trailblazing £10 million programme offers extra support to those who need it most, helping them overcome barriers, return to work, and transform their lives."

"We've supported thousands of disabled people and those living with long-term health conditions or other complex needs.

"By supporting Pro-Development's initiative, we are creating inclusive workplaces where staff are supported to stay and thrive."

Businesses interested in the programme are encouraged to contact Pro-Development to discuss eligibility and support options.
