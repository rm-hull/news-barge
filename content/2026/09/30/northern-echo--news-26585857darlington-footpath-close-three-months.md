A public footpath near Darlington is set to close for up to three months while drainage infrastructure is installed.

The closure impacts Low Coniscliffe Footpath 9, from a point roughly 305 metres east of its junction with Low Coniscliffe Bridleway 4, stretching eastwards for around 180 metres.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

An alternative route will be signed on site to accommodate pedestrians.

The work, authorised by Darlington Council, aims to construct an outfall from a Sustainable Urban Drainage Systems (SUDS) pond.

According to the council, the footpath closed from September 24, with an expected reopening on December 24.
