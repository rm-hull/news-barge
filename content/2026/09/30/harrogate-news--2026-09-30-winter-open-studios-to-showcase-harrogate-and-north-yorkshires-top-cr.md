Taking place across the county on **Saturday 7 and Sunday 8 November 2026 (11am – 4pm)**, the weekend gives visitors a rare chance to explore the hidden spaces where art comes to life, meet local creators, and discover one-of-a-kind Christmas gifts.

Organised by the artist-run collective North Yorkshire Open Studios (NYOS), the event showcases a massive variety of styles, media, and subjects. Venues range from a former sawmill in the Dales to stunning courtyard gardens across the district.

## Local Highlights

Among the dozens of regional creators opening their doors are notable figures based right here in the Harrogate area:

* **Frances Tabbernor (Harrogate):** Operating from her studio on Tewit Well Road, Frances paints landscapes alive with memory, water, and sky. Her distinct work has previously featured on the BBC’s*The Great British Sewing Bee*.
* **Charcoal North (Harrogate):** The painting and drawing specialist will be welcoming the public to their studio space located at 79 Otley Road.

> **Garth Bayley, Project Manager at NYOS, said:**
>
> Winter Open Studios is about connecting people with the people behind the art. There’s something incredibly special about buying a Christmas gift directly from the person who made it, right in the space where it was created.

Entry to all studio venues across the weekend is completely free.
