A Darlington care service joined 320 people from across the country at a colourful festival celebrating inclusion, wellbeing and fun for adults with learning disabilities.

Hollyhurst, a residential care service in Woodland Road, attended Cygnet Social Care’s third annual Sensory Festival on September 17.

The festival was held at Saffron Lane Athletics Stadium in Leicester.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21513552.jpg?type=mds-article-620)At Cygnet Social Care’s third annual Sensory Festival at Leicester’s Saffron Lane Athletics Stadium *(Image: Supplied)*

Cate Ball, regional director for Cygnet Social Care – East Midlands, said: "The day was another fabulous event for the people we support and the teams that care for them.

"It’s wonderful to see the people we support meet new friends, try different activities and enjoy that festival atmosphere in a way that works for them.

"Thank you to everyone who helped organise the day, run stalls and support people to attend and take part.

"We would also like to thank the event’s sponsors, GPR Construction and Lightwire Electrical, for their generous support in helping to make the festival possible."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21513553.jpg?type=mds-article-620)At Cygnet Social Care’s third annual Sensory Festival at Leicester’s Saffron Lane Athletics Stadium *(Image: Supplied)*

The festival attracted around 320 people, including 140 residents and 180 staff from Cygnet care services across the country.

Other services at the event included Broughton Lodge, Cherry Tree House, Elston House, Fairways, Hansa Lodge, Langdale House, Pines, Shear Meadow, Supported Living in Birmingham, The Fields, Toller Road and Willow House.

Activities included sensory stations, a silent disco, inflatables and a human fruit machine.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21513555.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Supplied)*

There was also face painting, a photo booth and a beach bar serving soft drinks.

Entertainment included an Elvis impersonator and two residents who took to the stage.

Residents and staff danced and enjoyed live music throughout the day.

Gareth Williams, CEO of Cygnet Social Care, said: "Events like this are highlights of the year for the people we support and our colleagues.

"At Cygnet, we are passionate about going the extra mile to deliver the best experiences possible to our residents and service users.

"We ensure that our events are inclusive, accessible and engaging, providing a safe and supportive space for everyone to enjoy the type of activities that many of us take for granted."
