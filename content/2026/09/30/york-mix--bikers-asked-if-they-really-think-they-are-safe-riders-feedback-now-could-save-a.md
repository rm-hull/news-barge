Here’s a message from North Yorkshire Police to all motorcyclists.

Be honest. When was the last time someone took a proper look at your riding? Not your mate. Not the person you ride out with every Sunday.

Someone who really knows their stuff.

There are some last-minute places on the **BikeSafe** course this weekend, giving you the chance to head out with an experienced police motorcyclist and get some expert feedback on your riding.

It’s **not a test** and it’s**not about catching you out**.

It’s a chance to sharpen your skills, improve your observations, pick up some useful advice and hopefully become an even better, safer rider.

And the best bit? You get to spend a good part of the day doing what you enjoy best, riding your bike!

Police have only got a limited number of spaces available, so if **BikeSafe** has been sitting on your “I’ll do that one day” list, this weekend could be the opportunity.

**Book your place before the spaces go. Head to [https://orlo.uk/HXdTE**](https://orlo.uk/HXdTE**)
