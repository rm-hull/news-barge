Some of the biggest names in sport descended on a local golf club this past week.

Hosted by golfing legend Colin Montgomerie OBE, the two-day event brought together professional golfers, celebrities and sporting stars while raising money to support children and young people with life-shortening conditions and their families at Ilkley Golf Club.

Among those taking part were Sir Gareth Southgate, Jason Robinson, Zinzan Brooke, Steve Harmison, Lee Dixon, Andy Cole and Dame Laura Davies, alongside a host of professional golfers.

The event raised £23,361.70 for Martin House, which provides specialist palliative care and support to children, young people and families across West, North and East Yorkshire.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21519196.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Yorkshire Invitational at Ilkley Golf Club which raised funds for Martin House Children's Hospice *(Image: submitted)*

Sir Gareth, a long-term Martin House ambassador, said: “I'd say my golf went from average to reasonably good! It was certainly nerve-wracking teeing off in front of a crowd at the start.

“It's been a brilliant event, and the fact that the money is going to Martin House is very special. It costs a lot to keep the hospice running each year, so events like today are great for raising both the charity's profile and valuable funds.”

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21519185.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Colin Montgomerie OBE taking part in the Yorkshire Invitational at Ilkley Golf Club *(Image: submitted)*

More than 1,600 spectators had registered ahead of the event, with visitors getting the chance to watch some of sport’s most recognisable names compete up close.

The Invitational culminated in a professional tournament, with Chris Hanson taking victory on two-under-par, one shot ahead of runner-up Oliver Fisher.

There was also a memorable moment when Howard Rishton, from Clitheroe Golf Club, recorded a hole-in-one at the 15th, hitting a six iron into a strong wind.

Michelle Ford, Regional and Events Team Manager at Martin House, said: “We’re incredibly grateful to everyone involved in the Yorkshire Invitational for choosing Martin House as its charity partner and helping to raise such a fantastic amount.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21519197.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Yorkshire Invitational at Ilkley Golf Club which raised funds for Martin House Children's Hospice *(Image: submitted)*

“Every pound raised will help us continue providing specialist care and support to children and young people with life-shortening conditions, and their families, across Yorkshire.”

The Yorkshire Invitational was organised by SGH Sporting Events and saw amateur golfers given the opportunity to play alongside leading names from the worlds of professional golf and sport.

Each year, Martin House supports more than 550 children and young people with life-shortening conditions, and their families. Its specialist teams provide care at the hospice in Boston Spa, in hospitals and in families’ own homes.

Providing this care costs around £13 million every year, with the majority coming from voluntary donations and fundraising.
