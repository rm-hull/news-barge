A woman has been charged with supplying crack cocaine.

Julie Walker, 33, of Rowanwood Gardens in Ravenscliffe, has been charged with drug offences concerning the supply of crack cocaine.

She is due to appear at Bradford Magistrates' Court this morning (Wednesday).

A spokesperson from West Yorkshire Police said: "A woman from Bradford is due to appear in court this morning after being charged with drug offences.

"Julie Walker, aged 33, of Rowanwood Gardens in Ravenscliffe, has been charged with being concerned in the supply of crack cocaine.

"She was remanded to appear at Bradford Magistrates’ Court later.

"This is part of the work of Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing team to tackle drug supply in the local area."
