A historic North Yorkshire hotel dating back to 1890 has been put on the market for £6 million.

The Cairn Hotel, located in Harrogate town centre, is part of an eight-hotel portfolio being marketed by business property adviser Christie & Co on behalf of the joint administrators of Strathmore Hotels Limited.

Offering 135 bedrooms, a 350-seat restaurant, and extensive function space, the Cairn Hotel has been described as having "strong year-round trading" and "significant redevelopment potential."

Alistair McAlinden, joint administrator and head of Interpath in Scotland, said: "We are pleased to be able to bring this very well-established portfolio of hotels to the market as a going concern – and we are heartened by the significant level of interest expressed in the portfolio already.

"Upon the sales process being launched, interested parties should contact Christie & Co as quickly as possible to discuss and progress their interest."

The hotels, located across Scotland and the north of England, are available for purchase individually, as part of a group, or as a full portfolio.

Other properties in the sale include the Alexandra Hotel in Fort William, Ben Nevis Hotel & Leisure Club, Cumbria Grand Hotel, Nethybridge Hotel, Royal Hotel in Oban, Salutation Hotel in Perth, and Ben Wyvis Hotel in Strathpeffer.

Brian Sheldon, regional director – hospitality at Christie & Co, said: "We are delighted to be acting on behalf of the Joint Administrators from Interpath, to market the eight hotels which form part of Strathmore Hotels Limited.

"We look forward to officially launching the sales process very soon and gauging from the initial enquiries we have received to date, we anticipate being very busy."

The Cairn Hotel is being offered leasehold with an asking price of £6 million.

Interested parties are encouraged to contact Christie & Co hotel specialists Brian Sheldon, Jeremy Jones, or George Ranachan.
