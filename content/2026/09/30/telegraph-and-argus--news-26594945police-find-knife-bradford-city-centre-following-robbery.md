A KNIFE was found following a reported robbery near Bradford College.

A police cordon was in place on Randall Well Street by the college in the city centre.

The force said the suspect fled the scene in a vehicle before officers arrived.

Both the victim and the suspect were not present when police attended.

There have been no reports of injuries, the force said.

## The scene

Pictures on social media show a cordon in place with several police vans at the scene.

When the T&A went to the scene at around 1.30pm, the scene had been removed.

## What have police said?

A spokesperson for the force said: "At about 10.45am today (Wednesday), police received a report of a robbery in Randall Well Street, Bradford, where the suspect had left the scene in a vehicle.

"Officers were deployed to the incident and found that both the victim and the suspect were no longer present.

"Enquiries are ongoing and a knife has been recovered for further tests.

"There are no reports of any injuries at this stage.

"Anyone who witnessed the robbery is asked to contact police via the Live Chat options quoting log 473 of 30 September."
