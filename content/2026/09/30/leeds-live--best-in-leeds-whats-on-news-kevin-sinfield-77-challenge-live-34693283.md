Leeds Rhinos rugby legend Sir Kevin Sinfield has cheers lots of support in Yorkshire today.

Sir Kevin Sinfield has raised more than £11m for charity over the past seven years after his friend and former rugby league teammate Rob Burrow was diagnosed with motor-neurone diseases (MND) in 2019, before passing away in 2024. Sir Kevin's last 7 in 7: Together challenge took place in December, 2025, from East Anglia to Yorkshire, raising more than £1.2 million.

The six charities set to benefit once again from this year's fundraising include MND Association, Leeds Hospitals Charity, My Name5 Doddie Foundation, Darby Rimmer Foundation, MND Scotland and the Irish MND Association.

He started his latest 7/7 challenge on Sunday, September 27, in Hull, and will be finishing the following Sunday in Manchester, going all over Yorkshire in-between. He says this will be his seventh and final annual challenge, called 7 in 7: The Grand Finale. Today, we have seen Sir Kevin visit Leeds and Huddersfield.

### Sir Kevin Sinfield on 'really nice moment' he pulled over to learn he'd been knighted

Sir Kevin Sinfield has spoken about the “really nice moment” he learned he was being given a knighthood.

The Leeds Rhinos legend, 45, was named in the King's birthday honours list due to his fundraising efforts for Motor Neurone Disease (MND). He has raised more than £11m over the past seven years after his friend and former rugby league teammate Rob Burrow was diagnosed with MND in 2019, before passing away in 2024.

In a video released by the UK Government, Sir Kevin said: “We were just heading out for an evening meal. We set off driving and my said, I think you need to pull over, there’s a letter here for you, that she’d opened and started to read. And yeah, it was a really nice moment.”
