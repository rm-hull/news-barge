The North East’s first Crocs outlet opens in days with deals for new shoppers.

The new store will launch at Dalton Park on Thursday, October 9, offering Crocs’ much-loved range of clogs, sandals, slippers and accessories for adults and children, including classic styles, cosy fleece-lined options and colourful Jibbitz charms.

To celebrate the opening, Dalton Park is hosting in-store, opening-day family-friendly balloon entertainment and exclusive weekend offers.

Shoppers will enjoy 20 per cent off all weekend, and those who spend £60 or more will receive a free pack of five Jibbitz charms, while stocks last.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21378492.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Dalton Park)*

**Read More:**

Richard Kaye, Centre Manager at Dalton Park Outlet & Outdoors, said: "We’re looking forward to welcoming Crocs to Dalton Park on 9th October and celebrating the opening with a fun, family-friendly in-store experience.

"As the North East’s first Crocs outlet, the new store gives visitors even more choice when shopping for everyday favourites, seasonal styles and gifts ahead of Christmas – all at great outlet prices."

The Crocs outlet will stock a variety of classic clogs, cosy fleece-lined options and colourful Jibbitz charms for personalisation.
