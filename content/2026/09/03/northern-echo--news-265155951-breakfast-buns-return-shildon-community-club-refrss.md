A community space in a County Durham town is offering breakfasts for as little as £1.

Jubilee Fields Community Centre in Shildon has reopened its popular Wednesday breakfast drop-in, serving cheap cooked breakfasts, hot drinks and conversation.

The weekly session runs from 10am to 11:30am and offers breakfast buns from £1, a small breakfast for £3 and a large breakfast for £4.50.

  A look art Jubilee Fields Community Centre's Wednesday morning menu, including the £1 breakfast bun. 

*(Image: Jubilee Fields Community Centre on Facebook)*

Organisers announced the return of the club on social media, telling followers: "After six long weeks without us...we're finally back.

"Join Jade every Wednesday, 10:00 - 11:30 for breakfast, brews & a bit of banter because let's be honest...breakfast tastes better when someone else makes it."

One person took to the post to share their excitement, writing "Yippee."

With food prices continuing to squeeze household budgets, the breakfast club offers an affordable place to eat, socialise and meet local people.

The drop-in takes place every Wednesday morning at Jubilee Fields Community Centre, a community hub that has served the town since opening in 2001.

  Freshly made breakfast buns from just £1 at Jubilee Fields Community Centre's weekly breakfast drop-in in Shildon. 

*(Image: Jubilee Fields Community Centre)*

The project was made possible through support from The National Lottery and the Church Urban Fund, alongside years of campaigning and fundraising by local residents determined to bring a community centre to Shildon.

Today, the not-for-profit organisation is led by a volunteer board of trustees and provides affordable recreational, learning, wellbeing and social activities for people of all ages

From youth clubs and social groups to training, education, and community support services, the centre aims to improve quality of life for residents across Shildon and the surrounding area.

Jubilee Fields is also recognised as a Wellbeing Hub and says it remains committed to providing a warm and supportive environment where residents feel valued and included.
