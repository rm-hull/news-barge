Drivers have been warned to expect delays on a major North East route as thousands of festivalgoers head to Lindisfarne Festival.

National Highways North East warned of delays on the A1 northbound near Elwick as festival traffic builds.

A spokesperson for National Highways North East said: "Road users are advised to expect delays on the A1 northbound near Elwick due to increased traffic heading to Lindisfarne Festival.

"Please allow extra time for your journey and follow any signs or directions from traffic management on approach."

  Lindisfarne Festival in 2025 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The festival starts today, Thursday, September 3, at Beal Farm on the Northumberland coast and runs until Saturday, September 5.

Organisers say the event, which has an "electric line-up", is on course for a complete sell-out, as on August 25 only 500 tickets remained.

More than 250 artists will perform across nine stages at the festival’s fastest-selling edition yet.

The 2026 headliners are The Proclaimers, Faithless and Razorlight.

The bill also includes Soul II Soul, Elvana, Professor Green, Heather Small, Utah Saints, Ferocious Dog and The Amy Winehouse Band.

Sunderland band Swindled lead a strong North East line-up after a breakthrough year backed by BBC Radio 1 and a Radio 1 Big Weekend appearance.

Rising artists Leazes, Bear Park and Finn Forster will also perform.

  Lindisfarne Festival in 2025 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Festival director Conleth Maenpaa said: "After everything this festival has been through, to be standing here with more than 250 artists, nine stages, a brand-new Disgraceland area and our biggest wellbeing offering ever is pretty special.

"We've got The Proclaimers, Faithless, and Razorlight at the top of an incredible bill, but Lindisfarne has always been about much more than three names on a poster.

"It's about discovering something brilliant in a tent you weren't planning to walk into, staying up far later than you intended and spending a weekend in one of the most beautiful festival locations in the country."

  Lindisfarne Festival in 2025 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Music runs from 3pm to 1am today, then from mid-morning until 4am on Friday and Saturday.

The festival site overlooks Holy Island, around 50 miles north of Newcastle and eight miles south of Berwick-upon-Tweed.

Drivers should follow the festival signs from the A1 to reach the correct entrance. The postcode for the festival site is TD15 2PB.

A shuttle bus will run between Berwick station and the festival site, with organisers urging visitors to use public transport or car-share.

Weekend tickets include camping, while glamping and campervan options are available too.

Extra fire safety measures are in place after recent wildfires in the area.

Barbecues, liquid- or solid-fuel stoves and camping candles are banned across the site, but gas stoves with canisters under 600g are allowed.

The festival is strictly for people aged 18 and over.

Parking is chargeable and should be booked online in advance for the best rate.
