A police cordon has been lifted in a York street where a car burst into flames.

Firefighters extinguished the BMW car that was on fire in Navigation Road, off Walmgate, shortly before 5.30pm on Wednesday (September 2).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said its crews used hose reel jets to extinguish the blaze and left the incident with police.

  The BMW car on fire in Navigation Road, off Walmgate in York, on Wednesday (September 2) 

*(Image: Supplied)*

In a statement, the fire service said: “Both York and Acomb’s crews responded to reports of a car fire.

“Crews extinguished the fire using three hose reel jets, a breathing apparatus for comfort wear and a thermal imaging camera to check for any hotspots. The incident was left in the hands of police.”

North Yorkshire Police has been approached for comment.

Police officers were seen guarding the cordon on Wednesday night but had left the area by Thursday morning.

The burned-out BMW car, parked at the junction of Navigation Road and Bretgate, had also been removed by Thursday morning.


Images and video shared with The Press showed the car engulfed in flames as residents stood outside their homes behind the cordon on Wednesday evening.

No other damaged vehicles could be seen behind the 150-metre police cordon on Wednesday, and there did not appear to be any damage to buildings nearby.

  Emergency services in Navigation Road, off Walmgate in York, on Wednesday (September 2) 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

A passerby who witnessed the car exploding said they saw the driver of the car pull up on the pavement while the vehicle was smoking, before it caught fire and exploded.

"It was clear there was something wrong,” the eyewitness said. “The driver had pulled up on the pavement and was trying to get things out of the car while it was smoking.

"He was clear before it suddenly caught fire and then started to explode – it was popping.

"The police arrived before the fire service and closed the road. They have been doing a great job keeping everyone well back."

  Police in Navigation Road, off Walmgate in York, on Wednesday night (September 2) after a car fire hours earlier 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

A woman living in flats opposite where the car burst into flames said she heard a "bang like a shot" before the emergency services swooped following the fire.

"[I] ran outside and saw the smoke," she said. "My neighbour in Hope Street said there were already some police there when the car went up."

The incident came at a time when traffic had been building in Walmgate due to drivers being diverted from the York inner ring road which was closed at Fishergate due to storm damage. The inner ring road has since fully reopened.
