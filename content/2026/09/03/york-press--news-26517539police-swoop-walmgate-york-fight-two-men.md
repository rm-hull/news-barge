Police have swooped in York after a reported fight involving two men.

Two police vans were on the scene in Walmgate at 7am on Thursday (September 3) and had left by 7.20am.

One man was seen being detained by police in Hurst's Yard, near The Press office, at around 7am.


A man working at a hotel in Walmgate said he had gone outside for a smoke shortly before 7am and "one guy was on top of the other", before police arrived.

Another eyewitness saw a man appearing to flee down Walmgate to Hurst's Yard followed by the other man.

They said police then detained one man in Hurst's Yard and spoke to the other in Walmgate.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "At around 6.55am on 3 September, police were called to a disturbance in Walmgate in York. Two men were involved in a verbal argument followed by a physical altercation. No injuries were reported.

"Officers spoke to witnesses at the scene and examined CCTV footage, and both men were given words of advice. No arrests were made."
