The cause of a car fire in a York street was found to be “undetermined” after an investigation, the fire service has said.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said its investigation could not find evidence to support the blaze in Navigation Road, off Walmgate, on Wednesday evening (September 2) being accidental or deliberate.

A service spokesperson said no injuries have been reported after the BMW car caught fire, resulting in the area being cordoned off.

They said firefighters extinguished the blaze at about 5.30pm on Wednesday and protected nearby properties from fire and smoke.

   

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The BMW sustained “90 per cent fire damage and there was some heat damage to guttering on one of the nearby properties”, the fire service spokesperson said.

North Yorkshire Police said its investigation into the incident remains ongoing.

Police remained on the scene next to the burned-out car in Navigation Road into Wednesday night but had left the scene on Thursday with the area fully reopened.

The destroyed BMW car, parked at the junction of Navigation Road and Bretgate, had also been removed by Thursday morning.


Images and video shared with The Press showed the car engulfed in flames as residents stood outside their homes behind the cordon on Wednesday evening.

No other damaged vehicles could be seen behind the 150-metre police cordon on Wednesday.

A passerby who witnessed the BMW exploding said they saw the driver of the car pull up on the pavement while the vehicle was smoking, before it caught fire.

"It was clear there was something wrong,” the eyewitness said. “The driver had pulled up on the pavement and was trying to get things out of the car while it was smoking.

"He was clear before it suddenly caught fire and then started to explode – it was popping.

"The police arrived before the fire service and closed the road. They have been doing a great job keeping everyone well back."

A woman living in flats opposite where the car burst into flames said she heard a "bang like a shot" before the emergency services swooped following the fire.

"[I] ran outside and saw the smoke," she said. "My neighbour in Hope Street said there were already some police there when the car went up."

The incident came at a time when traffic had been building in Walmgate due to drivers being diverted from the York inner ring road which was closed at Fishergate due to storm damage. The inner ring road has since fully reopened.
