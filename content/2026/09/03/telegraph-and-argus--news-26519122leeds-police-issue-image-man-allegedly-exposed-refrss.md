Police have released an image as they investigate an incident in which a man allegedly exposing himself to a woman and child in a car.

Officers were called at 7.25pm on Sunday, August 30, after the incident on York Road near Selby Road in Leeds.

The man allegedly punched and kicked another man who intervened.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Safeguarding detectives are appealing for information to help identify a man who is being sought over an indecent exposure and assault in Leeds.

"The incident occurred at about 7.25pm on Sunday, August 30, in York Road, near to the junction with Selby Road, where a man exposed himself to a woman and child in a car and then punched and kicked a man who intervened.

"Specialist officers from Leeds Safeguarding are carrying out enquiries into the incident and are continuing to support the victims.

"Anyone who recognises the man in the images or who has any information that could assist the investigation is asked to contact Leeds Safeguarding online via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting crime reference 13260495041.

"Information can be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111."
