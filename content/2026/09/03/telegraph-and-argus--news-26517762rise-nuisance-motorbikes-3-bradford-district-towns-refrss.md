POLICE are appealing for information after a rise in nuisance and illegal motorbike activity in three Bradford district towns.

Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team (NPT) has reported a recent increase in incidents involving nuisance and illegal motorbikes causing disruption in Keighley, Silsden and Oxenhope.

Officers are asking the public to report sightings so they can target enforcement action.

A spokesperson for the Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team said: "Officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team are keen to increase our growing collection of seized nuisance and/or illegal motorbikes.

"Despite taking a number off of our roads over the last 12 months, we are aware there seems to have been a recent rise in the number of these bikes causing chaos in our communities, with recent reports being made in the areas of Keighley, Silsden and Oxenhope, following three off-road bikes being seized and riders reported for offences.

"However, this is where we need you.

"We are urging the public to report any sightings of these bikes.

"This helps us to be in the right areas at the right time, and where necessary, deploy specialist resources to assist us in removing them from our communities."

Reports can be made by calling police on 101 or using the LiveChat function at westyorkshire.police.uk/contact-us
