*York artist Ric Liptrot honours Japanese Print Shop in new artwork, reports MAXINE GORDON*

A MUCH-LOVED York shop which closed its doors after half a century in business has been brought back to life in a new artwork by local artist Ric Liptrot.

Ric, known for his detailed collage-style illustrations of York's independent shops, pubs and landmarks, has completed a new piece celebrating The Japanese Print Shop in High Petergate, which closed in May this year after 50 years in the city centre.

The artwork is particularly personal for Ric, who worked part-time at the shop during its final months in business before helping owner Percy Barkes clear the premises after it closed.

  The Japanese Print Shop in High Petergate. Photo - York Press


The illustration may feature in an exhibition at York's Pyramid Gallery later this year and will also be included in Ric's 2027 York calendar.

Speaking to The Press, Ric said the artwork was inspired by both the shop's significance to York and his own connection to it.

"Having worked at the shop for the last few months before closing and getting to know Percy and Delia, I wanted to create an illustration of the shop," he said.

"I wanted to keep a memory of it as there were so many customers who were sad to see it close.

"There is a feeling that York is losing some of its all-time great shops and this is my way of preserving one."

Ric worked at The Japanese Print Shop from January until its closure at the end of May, helping out on Saturdays and Tuesday mornings. He later assisted Mr Barkes in emptying the shop during June.

  Ric Liptrot with his newest art work - photo supplied


The artist is well known for his highly detailed collages, and said recreating the shop window provided an opportunity to showcase miniature versions of the Japanese prints that were once on display.

"I created miniature Japanese prints as collages which was really fun as it meant I could create my own display in the window," he said.

"I have created tiny collaged replicas of the prints Percy had on display, including some of his favourites. I’ve tried to create a charming piece of art in commemoration to the style and feel of the shop."

He added: "I enjoyed working on the detail of the prints. It is something that I have done before. For example, in my Heima shop window I cut and stuck on all of the products which feature in the shop.

"I like to fill the windows to attract the eye and give the feeling you could step into the picture."

  One of the mini prints Ric Liptrot made for his new work of art featuring York's former Japanese print shop in Petergate. Photo supplied


Signed limited-edition giclée prints of the artwork will be available through Ric's website at www.ricliptrot.bigcartel.com.

The piece may also feature in a forthcoming exhibition at York's Pyramid Gallery on Stonegate.

Ric said: "I will be exhibiting alongside York artists at The Pyramid Gallery on Stonegate in York from October 2 until November 15.

"I will have a selection of my original illustrations of York on display alongside other local artists including Lesley Birch, Emily Stubbs, Jo Walton and Lesley Shaw."

The Japanese Print Shop artwork will also take pride of place in Ric's 2027 York calendar, which highlights some of the city's best-loved independent businesses and landmarks.

"My 2027 York calendar will feature new illustrations of York including The Japanese Print Shop, The Golden Ball pub and Il Paradiso del Cibo restaurant," he said.

"I'm highlighting all the great things York has to offer. It will also include Holgate Windmill."

The calendar is available for pre-order online now and will also be on sale at Christmas events Ric is attending later this year.

  Japanese Print Shop in HIgh Petergate which is to close in May. Photo supplied


The closure of The Japanese Print Shop marked the end of an era for York. Owner Percy Barkes opened the business at 38 High Petergate in 1976 and built an international reputation as a specialist dealer in Japanese woodblock prints.

Announcing the closure earlier this year, Mr Barkes said: "I opened the shop in 1976 and I am closing the shop after 50 years. The time is right."

  Percy Barkes, owner of the Japanese Print Shop in Petergate York which closed earlier this year. York Press photo


Over five decades, the shop became a hidden gem for collectors and visitors, attracting customers interested in Japanese art from across the UK and beyond. Mr Barkes specialised in Japanese woodblock prints, making regular sourcing trips to Japan and building a loyal following through his expert knowledge and fair pricing.

Collectors interested in the remaining stock of Japanese prints can find more information through the business website at www.russianpaintingsandjapaneseprints.co.uk.
