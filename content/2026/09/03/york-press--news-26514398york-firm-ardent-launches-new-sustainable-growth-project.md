A second innovation project is set to drive smarter, more sustainable manufacturing practices in the UK.

York-based Ardent Limited, a specialist in fire protection and electronic safety systems, has partnered with Leeds Beckett University in a 30-month Knowledge Transfer Partnership (KTP) to develop an intelligent business system aimed at improving operational efficiency, supporting growth, and enabling data-driven decision-making.

The project, part-funded by UK Research and Innovation through Innovate UK, will see business and data science experts from Leeds Beckett work alongside Ardent to embed the new system.

Matt Lewindon, managing director of Ardent Limited, said: "Following the success of our first KTP, we're delighted to be continuing our partnership with Leeds Beckett University.

"This project supports an important part of Ardent's growth strategy, helping us explore new ways to improve efficiency and build greater scalability into our operations.

"Knowledge Transfer Partnerships provide a unique opportunity to combine academic expertise with real-world business challenges."

This is the second KTP between the two organisations.

The first, completed in 2025, embedded a new international marketing strategy and corporate culture into Ardent, and was awarded the highest possible grading of Outstanding from Innovate UK.

Dr Alfred Chinta, head of degree apprenticeships at Leeds Business School and project leader, said: "Through the new KTP, we will support Ardent’s growth ambitions by developing and embedding an intelligent system to improve business operations, and enable data-driven decisions, greater efficiency, and more sustainable performance.

"The system will optimise how Ardent delivers its services and manages its existing portfolio while lowering operational emissions."

An experienced graduate will be recruited to manage the project as a KTP Associate full-time at Ardent, with full support of the LBU academics.

Interested applicants can find more information and apply at leedsbeckett.ac.uk/jobs.

The closing date for applications is Wednesday 23 September 2026.
