A NEW series of drop-in advice sessions is offering free business support to the community.

The Let's Talk Business sessions, held at Shipley Library’s Enterprise Hub and delivered by Invest in Bradford, are aimed at entrepreneurs, start-ups, and established businesses.

They offer one-to-one conversations with experienced business advisers.

The sessions are designed to help attendees explore ideas, tackle challenges, and connect with resources across the Bradford district.

No booking is required to attend.

Sessions will take place from 10.30am to 2.30pm on September 24, October 22, and November 26.
