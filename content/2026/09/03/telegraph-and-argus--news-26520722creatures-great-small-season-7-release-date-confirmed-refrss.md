All Creatures Great and Small will return to screens this September.

Channel 5 has confirmed the Yorkshire-set period drama will return for a seventh season, premiering on Thursday, September 17 at 9pm.

A behind-the-scenes spin-off, All Creatures Great and Small: Behind the Magic, will air the following night at 9pm.

The new series, once again filmed in Grassington, near Skipton, will be set in 1946, with World War II behind the characters and Skeldale House once again bustling with life.

*(Image: Freelance)*

Channel 5 has described it as "once again full, and livelier than ever, as familiar faces reunite under one roof and look ahead to a hopeful new chapter."

The broadcaster said: "James is excited about new developments in veterinary medicine, but a new vet in the area threatens the status quo.

"The world at large may be changing, but inside this slice of Yorkshire, the Dales will always offer the same perfect escapism.

"Packed with warmth, humour, and community spirit, the series brings more heartwarming tales of people and animals alike."

The main cast will return for the new season, including Nicholas Ralph as James Herriot, Samuel West as Siegfried Farnon, Anna Madeley as Mrs Hall, Rachel Shenton as Helen Herriot, and Callum Woodhouse as Tristan Farnon.

  All your favourites will be back! 

*(Image: C5)*

Patricia Hodge will also return as Mrs Pumphrey, along with her beloved Pekingese Tricki.

Other returning cast members include Tony Pitts as Helen’s father Richard, Imogen Clawson as her sister Jenny, and Mollie Winnard as barmaid Maggie.

Maimie McCoy will reprise her role as Dorothy, a friend of Mrs Hall and Siegfried’s love interest.

Ms McCoy first appeared in the show’s debut season and returned in the 2026 Christmas special, where she shared a kiss with Siegfried.

She told Radio Times: "There's lots that connects them, which I think is really lovely."

Gaia Wise also returns as Charlotte, Tristan’s girlfriend.

The pair professed their love for each other in last year's festive episode, but actor Callum Woodhouse hinted that their relationship may not be smooth sailing in the new season.

Mr Woodhouse told Radio Times: "It's not really a massive spoiler to say I'm still with Charlotte Beauvoir, who is really good for him and helps him with his mental health.

"She keeps him happy and I think he's a little bit more content.

"But there's only so long that that can last.

"So, things may be coming to change, but we don't know."

A new character will join the cast in season seven.

Granville Bennett, a small animal specialist played by Caolan McCarthy, is set to shake things up at Skeldale House.

Mr McCarthy’s previous credits include Belfast and the RSC’s Cyrano de Bergerac.

The show will also feature a variety of new guest stars across its six-episode run.

Channel 5 previously confirmed that All Creatures Great and Small has been renewed for both a seventh and eighth season, along with additional Christmas specials.

The broadcaster described the show as "the jewel in 5’s drama crown."

The series remains the channel’s most successful drama to date, having been watched by more than 14 million people across its first five seasons.

All Creatures Great and Small will return to Channel 5 on Thursday, September 17 at 9pm.
