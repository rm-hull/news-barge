Changes have been proposed to a development in Stamford Bridge's conservation area.

The application, concerning a former petrol station in The Square, involves altering condition 15 of planning permission to include design changes to the roof, dormer windows, shopfront, and a raised external seating area.

The application, reference number 26/01989/VAR, can be viewed and commented on via East Riding of Yorkshire Council's planning website or by emailing [planning@eastriding.gov.uk.](mailto:planning@eastriding.gov.uk.)

The deadline for comments is September 23, 2026.

Applications can also be viewed electronically at the council’s libraries.
