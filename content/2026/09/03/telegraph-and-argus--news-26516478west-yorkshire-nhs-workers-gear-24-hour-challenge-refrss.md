MORE than 200 NHS staff are gearing up for a 24-hour challenge of running, rowing, and cycling for charity.

The '24-Hour Therapy Dash' will take place on September 18, across West Yorkshire, with participants aiming to raise vital funds for hospital charities.

Staff from Bradford Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust, Calderdale and Huddersfield NHS Foundation Trust, Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust, and Mid Yorkshire Teaching NHS Trust will take part, raising money for their respective hospital charities.

Lisa Capewell, physiotherapy spinal team leader at Bradford Teaching Hospitals, said: "Last year's Therapy Dash showed what can be achieved when NHS colleagues come together for a good cause.

"We raised an incredible amount for Bradford Hospitals Charity while promoting the importance of keeping active.

"This year, I wanted the 24-Hour Therapy Dash to be bigger than ever, and I'm delighted that so many dedicated colleagues from across West Yorkshire have chosen to get involved.

"This is just the start; I aim to get this nation-wide next year."

  The 24-Hour Therapy Dash began at Bradford Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust in 2025 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The event began in 2025 at Bradford Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust and has since grown into a region-wide effort.

This year’s participants hope to cover even greater distances.

Hospital charities use the funds to support projects beyond standard NHS funding, including specialist equipment, research, patient environment improvements, and additional support for patients and families.

Last year, Ms Capewell and 60 colleagues raised more than £4,000 for Bradford Hospitals Charity during the event.

The Bradford team covered more than 1,030 kilometres (640 miles), the equivalent of travelling from Bradford to Berlin.

With more than three times as many participants taking part this year, the teams are hoping to cover an even greater distance.

Laura Riach, director of Bradford Hospitals Charity, said: "I'm blown away by Lisa, and everyone involved in organising and participating in something of this scale.

"For these therapists and their colleagues, caring doesn't end when they clock off.

"They are committed to improving health and wellbeing both inside and outside the workplace.

"The money raised will help hospital charities across West Yorkshire fund projects that make a real difference for patients, families and staff."

The event is sponsored by My Fitness Junction, which is providing equipment free of charge for the Bradford participants.
