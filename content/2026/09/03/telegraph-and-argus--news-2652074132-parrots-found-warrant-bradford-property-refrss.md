More than 30 parrots suspected of being illegally imported into the UK were discovered during a multi-agency operation at a Bradford property.

In a highly unusual case, officers executed a warrant as part of an investigation into the suspected illegal trading of protected species.

The operation was carried out on August 27 by West Yorkshire Police's City Neighbourhood Policing Team alongside Bradford Council, the National Wildlife Crime Unit, Keighley neighbourhood officers, patrol officers and the Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA).

## Parrots discovered during warrant

West Yorkshire Police said officers found 32 parrots at the address.

The birds are suspected to have been illegally imported into the UK and enquiries are continuing to establish the full circumstances surrounding their arrival and ownership.

Police have not released details of the exact location of the property or whether any arrests have been made.

## Multi-agency investigation

The warrant formed part of an investigation into suspected wildlife crime and the illegal trading of protected species.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "During the warrant, officers discovered 32 parrots at the property which are suspected to have been illegally imported into the UK.

"Enquiries remain ongoing to establish the full circumstances surrounding the importation and ownership of the birds."

## Protecting vulnerable species

The force said the operation demonstrated the importance of agencies working together to tackle wildlife crime.

Police worked alongside Bradford Council's Animal Health Officer, the National Wildlife Crime Unit and the Animal and Plant Health Agency during the warrant.

A spokesperson added: "This operation highlights the importance of partnership working in tackling wildlife crime and protecting vulnerable species."

## Enquiries ongoing

Investigations into the suspected illegal importation of the parrots remain ongoing.

No further details have been released at this stage.
