A popular café at one of Redcar’s best known visitor attractions is to reopen under new management.

The Garden Café at Kirkleatham Walled Garden, on Plantation Road, has announced it is preparing to open its doors again following a change of management.

The café is currently closed while the new team carries out preparations and gives the venue “a little spruce up”.

An opening date has not yet been confirmed, but the business said it would share updates and sneak peeks on its social media page before welcoming customers back.

The new management took over on September 1 and said visitors could expect fresh food, seasonal specials, events and “beautiful surroundings” when the café reopens.

In a post announcing the change, the team said: “A new chapter is beginning at The Kirkleatham Walled Gardens Café and we’re so happy to have you here.

“Expect delicious food, beautiful gardens, seasonal specials, events and plenty of little sneak peeks along the way.

“We hope you’re all as excited as we are to see these doors open again.

“But first, a little spruce up. We’ll be busy getting everything ready behind closed doors and will keep you updated with little sneak peeks along the way.”

The new team also paid tribute to those who had previously run the café and said it hoped to build on the welcoming atmosphere already created there.

They said: “We also want to say a huge thank you to the team who have created such a warm and welcoming café over the years.

“We’re really looking forward to carrying that on, while adding a few little touches of our own.

“Here’s to a fresh new chapter at The Garden Café. We can’t wait to welcome you all soon.”

Kirkleatham Walled Garden is located off the A174 on the outskirts of Redcar and is part of the historic Kirkleatham estate.

The Grade II listed garden reopened to the public in 2021 following a major restoration project worth around £10m.

It now includes formal gardens, a glasshouse, an art gallery, a splash area and an events space.

The café is based at Kirkleatham Walled Garden, Plantation Road, Redcar, TS10 4AS.
