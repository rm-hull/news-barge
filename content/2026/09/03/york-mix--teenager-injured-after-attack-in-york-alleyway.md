A teenager was hurt after being assaulted in a York alleyway.

It happened at around 8.55pm on Tuesday (1 September) in the alleyway between Constantine Avenue and Hewley Avenue in Tang Hall.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A teenage boy assaulted another teenage boy, causing injuries.
