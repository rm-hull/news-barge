A drug dealer has admitted causing a crash that left a man seriously injured and blocked a major road in North Yorkshire.

James Johnson, 31, was driving an Audi dangerously on the A659 Leeds Road at Tadcaster, York Magistrates' Court heard.

He was uninsured, didn't have a valid driving licence and police found cocaine in his possession.

  


Johnson, of Cherry Tree Court, Kiwi Street, Salford, appeared in custody before York Magistrates' Court on Bank Holiday Monday.

He pleaded guilty to causing serious injury by dangerous driving of an Audi on Friday, August 28 and driving the car without insurance and without a valid driving licence.

He also admitted possessing cocaine with intent to supply it to others.

Magistrates decided his offences merited a sentence greater than their maximum powers of 12 months’ imprisonment and sent him to York Crown Court to be sentenced by a judge.

They remanded him in custody until he appears at the higher court on September 22.
