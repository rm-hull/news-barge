A truck has become "wedged" under the trailer of an HGV in North Yorkshire village.

  



The county's fire and rescue service said a crew from Harrogate was called to Summerbridge at 8.50am today (Thursday, September 3).

A service spokesperson said: "There were no injuries.

"Crews released the vehicle from under the trailer using hydraulic tools and a reciprocator saw.

"The incident was left with police and the responsible person."
