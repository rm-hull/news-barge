A New Year’s Eve 5K event is set to return to Newcastle with ambitions to double in size.

The HogmanHOWAY 5K, after a successful debut last year, will once again take over the city centre on December 31, giving people of all ages and abilities a chance to see out the year on the move.

Organisers hope to double the number of participants for this year’s edition and build on the success of the inaugural race.

Steve Cram, race founder, said: "Last year, we proved that Newcastle was a great venue to join the list of iconic locations that host New Year’s Eve running events.

"Our aim is to grow the HogmanHOWAY 5K into a spectacular showcase for the city and the North East.

"It’s exciting that Develop North, Mayor Kim McGuinness and the North East Mayoral Strategic Authority, and Newcastle City Council, share that vision, and we look forward to welcoming even more running revellers on 31 December."

Develop North PLC is sponsoring the event, which is organised by Events of the North in partnership with the North East Mayoral Strategic Authority and Newcastle City Council.

The race will follow a flat, closed-road route through central Newcastle, starting and finishing at the Civic Centre.

The event offers a prize fund for elite athletes and passes by city landmarks, including sites being developed by sponsors and partners.

Michelle Percy, chief executive of Develop North PLC, said: "Develop North is proud to support the return of the HogmanHOWAY 5K, an event that brings people together while showcasing the region at its very best.

"We share the ambition to see it grow and become an established part of the city’s New Year celebrations."

More than 1,500 runners took part in last year’s race, many of whom raised money for good causes.

The official charity is the Sir Bobby Robson Foundation, part of Newcastle Hospitals Chairty.

The foundation is once again an event partner for 2026 and is campaigning for the proposed Sir Bobby Robson Institute, a new cancer research and treatment centre for the region.

The race is open to everyone, regardless of age or experience.

Entry details are available at eventsofthenorth.com/event/hogmanhoway-5k.
