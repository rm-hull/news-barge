A vegan festival is making a festive return this December.

The York Vegan Christmas Festival will take place at York RI Sports Centre on Sunday, December 13, 2026, from 10.30am to 4.30pm.

The event promises a wide range of plant-based products and experiences, including talks, cookery demonstrations, and ethical Christmas shopping.

Victoria Bryceson, founder of Vegan Events UK, said: "I'm really looking forward to bringing our vegan festival back to York this December.

"Discover a wonderful vegan community spirit at the York Vegan Christmas Festival with 80 fantastic stalls and lots of new things to try plus new experiences."

Visitors can explore stalls offering everything from vegan food and beauty products to homeware and festive treats.

Among the confirmed food vendors are Greek Vegan Deli, offering vegan versions of traditional Greek dishes, and The Plant Based Chippy, which serves a plant-based take on the classic fish fillet.

The festival will also feature talks and demonstrations throughout the day.

A cookery session with Jessica Goldthorpe will show visitors how to create a vegan dinner party menu.

Entry is £5 for adults, with free admission for under-16s and VIP tickets available for £15.

The venue is located next to the train station NCP car park.
