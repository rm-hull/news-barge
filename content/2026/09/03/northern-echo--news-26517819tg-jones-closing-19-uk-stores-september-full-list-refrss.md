TG Jones is set to close 19 stores across the UK during September as part of a major restructure.

All WH Smith stores were renamed TG Jones last year (except for travel stores in airports and train stations), after being bought by Modella.

There are currently around 450 TG Jones stores, mostly in the UK, with 4,700 staff.

This is set to be cut drastically after the High Court approved a major restructuring plan back in July.


The plan will see 150 TG Jones stores closed, which could result in the loss of hundreds of jobs.

## TG Jones launches closing down sale with £1 items

Closing-down sales have begun in several TG Jones shops across the UK, with customers also able to access discounts online.

The TG Jones website is giving customers the chance to buy items for as little as £1, with everything from gift bags to books and toys included in the closing-down sale.

The retailer warned online customers: “Don't miss your last chance to buy and grab your favourites for less today - once items in our Sale are gone, they really are gone forever!”
