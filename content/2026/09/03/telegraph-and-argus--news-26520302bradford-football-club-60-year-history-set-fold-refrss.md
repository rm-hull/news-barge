PEOPLE have been sharing their memories after a Bradford football club with a proud 60-year history announced that it is folding.

West Bowling AFC has played its last season in the Bradford Sunday Leagues after six decades of competition.

A final farewell match will be held at Emsleys Rec on September 27.

People took to Facebook to speak of their sadness.

## 'All good things must come to an end'

Laidback Fawcett said: "Sad news. Thank you for giving me the chance to manage this great team. Was a great two years I had coaching this great club and leading a team towards a potential 4 trophies. One been the county cup but for covid to beat us and not give us the chance. Thanks again and well done to all who supported us along the way."

Kyle Dyche added: "Best run club Bradford Sunday football has ever seen. Best team I've played for and the supporters were 2nd to none always there cheering the lads on in any weather. Such a shame but with everything great there always has to be an end at some point and you've had a brilliant run be proud of everything that was achieved in your 60 years."

Gaz Stokes said: "Sad news. Played in some great games against them over the years and also played with some of those lads. It's a sign of the times, of what is becoming, the sad demise of grassroots football. Each and everyone of those who put a shirt on should be proud of their contribution to the history of the club."

Kevin Doherty added: "A great shame but all good things must come to an end. I thoroughly enjoyed my 25 years battling on Sunday mornings with great team mates against many local teams. No doubt the legacy of West Bowling AFC will live on in the many memories by so many players, officials and supporters. Thank you to all who have toiled over the years to keep the club going through both great and tough times."

## What has the club said?

A spokesperson for the club said: "It is with great regret that West Bowling AFC have taken the decision to fold the club.

"After 60 years in the Bradford Sunday Leagues, it is no longer viable to run the team.

"We are having one final game for past players to take part in, it will be on Emsleys Rec our traditional ground, on Sunday, September 27 (2pm KO).

"Then afterwards refreshments and drinks at the Bridge Tavern to say a fond farewell to the club.

"Anybody who played for West Bowling AFC please come along and play, whether its for half a game or even five minutes, if you played for the club in the past we look forward to seeing you.

"Supporters more then welcome. We have had many over the years."
