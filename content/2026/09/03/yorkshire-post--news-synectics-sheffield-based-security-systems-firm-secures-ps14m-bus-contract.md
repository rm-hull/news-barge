# Synectics: Sheffield-based security systems firm secures £1.4m bus contract with regional authority

## Yorkshire-based security systems firm, Synectics, has announced that it has secured a £1.4m contract to provide on-vehicle surveillance technology for 220 new buses being introduced by a UK regional authority.

The Sheffield-based firm said the contracts cover the supply of a combination of advanced camera technology, digital mirror systems and on-vehicle video surveillance across the new fleet.

Synectics did not name the regional authority which it had secured the contract with.

The deal comes through Synectic’s systems integration business, Ocular.

Amanda Larnder, chief executive officer of Synectics, said: "This award demonstrates the growing demand for Ocular's on-vehicle solutions and represents an important win as we continue to grow our position in the on-vehicle transport market.”

The new fleet is due to go into service this autumn.
