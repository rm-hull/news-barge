FIREFIGHTERS tackled a huge landfill-waste fire inside a building last night.

Four crews were called to Lucy Street, Halifax, shortly after 10pm on Wednesday to reports of a blaze involving a building and waste.

Firefighters used four large jets to put out the blaze, which involved a large amount of landfill waste inside a steel building.

A spokesperson for the West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called at 10.09pm last night to Lucy Street, Halifax, to a building/waste fire.

"This was a large amount of landfill waste involved inside a steel building.

"Four crews used large jets, a ground monitor, and a water curtain to put it out."
