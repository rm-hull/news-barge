Free period products are being made available throughout Middlesbrough, ensuring residents can access essential items when they need them.

Free pads and tampons are now available for residents to use in the toilets at every staffed community hub and library across Middlesbrough.

Middlesbrough Council said the scheme is designed to help residents access essential period products when they need them, regardless of their financial circumstances.

Residents often turn to community hubs and libraries for advice and support. The council said it hopes the introduction of free products will make those spaces even more inclusive and welcoming.

The council said the scheme forms part of its ongoing efforts to support residents and tackle period poverty, and thanked Tonia Nixon with TEES Community for donating the products needed to get it up and running.

The TEES Community charity are a Redcar-based organisation that have been supporting Teesside residents since 2019.

The charity previously helped women and girls in the region experiencing period poverty during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020.
