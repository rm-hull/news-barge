The first rides have been announced ahead of the return of Harrogate Indoor Funfair.

The popular attraction will take over the Yorkshire Event Centre during the October half-term holiday.

It will run from Saturday 24th October until Sunday 1st November.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a996e2eca11c83ab84.jpg)


Organisers have confirmed that the Waltzer, Frogs, Tornado Twist and Miami will be among the rides available.

More attractions are expected to be revealed in the run-up to the event.

Organisers said:

“We can’t wait to welcome you all back to the Harrogate Indoor Funfair.”


*Tickets are now available through the Indoor Funfair**website**.*
