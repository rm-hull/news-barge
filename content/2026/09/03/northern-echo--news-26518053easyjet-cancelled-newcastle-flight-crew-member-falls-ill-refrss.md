More than 100 passengers were stranded when an EasyJet flight to the North East was cancelled at the last minute after a crew member fell ill.

Flight EZY5620 from Lisbon to Newcastle was cancelled shortly before departure on Friday, August 28.

It was due to land at Newcastle International Airport at 10.45pm.

Passengers had to fly to Bristol instead, then make their own way back to the North East, with some facing an Uber journey of around four hours.

One source told The Northern Echo: "EasyJet cancelled the last flight from Lisbon to Newcastle - leaving over 100 travellers stranded.

"The only option was to fly into Bristol and Uber from Bristol to Newcastle."

EasyJet confirmed the flight was cancelled after a crew member fell ill before departure.

A spokesperson for EasyJet said: "Flight EZY5620 from Lisbon to Newcastle on 28 August was cancelled due to a member of crew feeling unwell ahead of departure.

"Cabin crew and pilots have an obligation under safety regulations to report if they feel unable to operate and the safety and wellbeing of our customers and crew is always EasyJet’s highest priority.

"We did all possible to support customers, providing options including transferring onto the next available flight or requesting a full refund as well as providing hotel accommodation and meals for those who required them."

The airline confirmed that stranded customers will get money back, adding: "We will also reimburse customers for expenses including onward travel home that they may have incurred as a result of the cancellation."

Passengers affected by the cancellation should be reimbursed for expenses, including their journey home.

The spokesperson added: "The safety and wellbeing of customers and crew is our highest priority and we are sorry for the inconvenience this may have caused."

The Lisbon service was one of two new easyJet routes from Newcastle announced in January, with flights beginning on June 22.

Passengers were advised to contact easyJet directly for the latest information about alternative travel arrangements.
