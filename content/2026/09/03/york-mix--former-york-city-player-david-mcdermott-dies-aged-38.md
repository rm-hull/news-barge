York City has paid tribute to former player David McDermott who’s died aged 38.

David played for City between 2010 and 2012, and made 21 appearances for the club, scoring once.

The club said today: “Everyone at York City Football Club is saddened to learn of the passing of former player David McDermott.

“We send all our love and support to David’s friends and family at this immensely difficult time.”

Fans also paid their tributes. One said: “Cannot believe this. Went to every game with my grandad. He was class, one of our standout players that season.”

McDermott went through Walsall’s youth system before becoming one of their youngest ever players aged 16 in 2004.

He joined Kidderminster Harriers in 2008, staying for two seasons before his move to York.

He later had spells at AFC Telford United, Stourbridge, Rushall Olympic and Worcester City.
