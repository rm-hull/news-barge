Amateur crime solver Miss Marple is taking to the stage in Chester-le-Street for a local drama company’s take on an Agatha Christie classic.

Dryburn Theatrical Workshop players’ forthcoming production is the crime mystery ‘A Murder is Announced’.

An announcement in the local paper states the time and place when a murder is to occur in Miss Blacklock's Victorian house.

The victim is not to be one of the house's occupants, however, but an unexpected and unknown visitor.

In the drama that follows it provides a classic Christie crime conundrum as to mixed motives, concealed identities, a second death and a determined inspector grimly following the case's twists and turns.

  A Murder is Announced, Dryburn Theatrical Workshop's forthcoming whodunnit taking to the stage at the Park View Theatre, Chester-le-Street, next week 

*(Image: Dryburn Theatrical Workshop)*

The famous sleuth Miss Marple is on hand to provide the solution, however, at some risk to herself, in a dramatic final confrontation.

Lee Passmoor, workshop chairman, said, “We’re delighted to be able to perform ‘A Murder Is Announced’, based on Agatha Christie’s classic novel.

“It’s a fun whodunit with an ingenious plot, suspense and a mixture of fascinating characters, offering a great evening’s entertainment.”

Performances at the Park View Theatre, in Chester-le-Street, run from Thursday to Saturday, September 10 -12, with curtain up at 7.15pm each night.

All tickets are priced £12, with reductions available on block bookings.

They can be booked at [https://www.ticketsource.co.uk/dryburntheatricalworkshop,](https://www.ticketsource.co.uk/dryburntheatricalworkshop,) on the company’s website.

Dryburn Theatrical Workshop has a long history of successful shows, bringing together what is described as a talented local cast, impressive productions and loyal audiences from across Durham, Chester-le-Street and the wider North East.

Formed in 1978 by an enthusiastic group of staff at the former Dryburn Hospital in Durham, it now has more than 60 members, aged from 12 years and upwards.

Previous shows have ranged from traditional and well-known pantomimes such as ‘Peter Pan’, ‘Alice in Wonderland’, ‘Beauty and the Beast, ‘Cinderella’ and ‘Aladdin’, to popular modern musicals, such as ‘Shrek’, ‘Legally Blonde’, ‘Grease’, ‘The Addams Family’, Disney’s ‘The Little Mermaid’ and ‘We Will Rock You'.

It’s next production following the Agatha Christie murder mystery will be its annual pantomime production, ‘Jack and the Beanstalk’, from January 13 to 17, 2027.
