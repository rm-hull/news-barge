A York charity has received a £1,000 donation to support its vital work in the community.

Survive, which provides trauma therapy, was nominated by Sarah Wilkins at the York Pavers' store as part of the Pavers Foundation’s 'Customer Causes' initiative.

Ms Wilkins, a counsellor at Survive, said: "I am a counsellor at the charity and we provide trauma therapy to clients in and around York who for some is life saving."

The scheme invites Pavers customers to nominate a charity or community group through their nearest store.

Each store then selects one cause for consideration by the Pavers Foundation Committee, with the top 18 causes receiving £1,000 each.

The Pavers Foundation has donated more than £2.5 million to charities since it launched in 2018.

While the foundation’s main programme is driven by colleague and family nominations, the Customer Causes scheme was introduced last year to involve customers directly.
