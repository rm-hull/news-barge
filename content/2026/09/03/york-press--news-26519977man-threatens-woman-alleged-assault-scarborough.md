A MAN has threated to punch a woman and then shoved her in an alleged assault in a North Yorkshire town, police say.

The county's police force said officers are investigating an alleged assault in Grange Avenue, Scarborough, which was said to have happened at 5.25am on Friday, August 21.

  



A force spokesperson said: "There was a verbal confrontation in the street.

"A couple - a man and a woman - argued with another woman, the man threatened to punch the woman and then shoved her."

North Yorkshire Police are appealing for information from anyone who may have witnessed the incident or has relevant CCTV or dash-cam footage.

Anyone who can assist is asked to contact Constable 1222 David Fulford at [david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting reference 12260163436.
