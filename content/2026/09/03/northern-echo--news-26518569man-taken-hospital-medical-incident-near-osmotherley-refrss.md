A man was stretchered to safety after suffering a medical emergency in the North Yorkshire countryside.

Cleveland Mountain Rescue volunteers were called to help a male to the east of Square Corner, near Osmotherley.

At the site , the casualty was assessed by the team medics and once treated, he was loaded onto the stretcher and wheeled to Land Rovers at Dale Head.

A Cleveland Mountain Rescue Team spokesperson said: "North Yorkshire Police contacted the team to provide assistance to a male who had a medical incident to the east of Square Corner.

"The team deployed both Land Rovers and personnel in their own vehicles to Square Corner. From there, personnel were transferred in four by four vehicles to Dale Head approximately 2 km away. They then walked the 0.4km with the required medical kit and equipment to the casualty location.

"He was taken out of the stretcher and loaded ,sitting, into one of the Land Rovers for the transfer of approximately 1.4km to the waiting ambulance at the end of the track.
