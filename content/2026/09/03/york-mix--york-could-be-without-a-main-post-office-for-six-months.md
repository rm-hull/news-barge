York could spend six months without a main Post Office.

The news comes a day after ***YorkMix*** revealed that shop TG Jones, along with the city’s flagship Post Office inside, are to close next month.

It means that the city could be without the full range of services through the busy Christmas season and beyond.

And it will put extra pressure on the remaining, smaller Post Office branches on Colliergate and Market Street.

TG Jones and the Post Office on Coney Street are set to shut Wednesday 14 October.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/tg-jones-post-office-coney-street-york-closing-2.webp)

A Post Office spokesperson said: “We fully recognise the challenging trading conditions that TG Jones and many other High Street retailers are facing at the moment.

“TG Jones had its restructuring plan approved by the High Court on 1 July, which includes seeking new commercial terms with landlords for the majority of their estate.

“Consequently, TG Jones is looking to close their York store. Regrettably, York Post Office is due to close on Wednesday 14 October.

“We know how important our products and services are to the local community.

“The vacancy has recently been advertised on www.runapostoffice.co.uk and we are interested to hear from retailers about this opportunity. We want to open a replacement branch as soon as possible.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/tg-jones-post-office-coney-street-york-closing-5.webp)

“In the interim alternative branches include Market Place and Micklegate.”

The spokesperson told ***YorkMix***: “It can take around six months to open a replacement branch.”

Asked about where York people could find the full range of Post Office services, they said: “Colliergate and Micklegate offer most of the services at York.

“For specific services customers can visit: www.postoffice.co.uk/branch-finder to check the products and services available at branches and opening times.”

TG Jones employs both the shop and Post Office staff. We have been in touch with them to ask why it’s closing and what will happen to the team who work there, but haven’t yet heard back.
