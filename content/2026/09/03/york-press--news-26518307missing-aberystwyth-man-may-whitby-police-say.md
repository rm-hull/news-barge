A missing Welsh man may be in North Yorkshire, police said.

Officers are searching for the missing man, named Anthony, from Aberystwyth, who is believed to have travelled to the Whitby area.

Anthony was reported missing on Wednesday, August 26, and is believed to have travelled to Whitby the following day, North Yorkshire Police said.

The force urged anyone with information about Anthony’s whereabouts to get in touch.

Aberystwyth and Machynlleth Police said Anthony could be using a dark blue Nissan car, and it is believed he may have also travelled towards the Norfolk or Suffolk areas.

Police said Anthony is described as being 5ft 9 tall and of a stocky build. He has short grey hair which is brushed back and has tattoos on both arms.

He was last seen wearing a light blue polo shirt, black trousers and black shoes with a gold necklace, and walks with a slight limp.
