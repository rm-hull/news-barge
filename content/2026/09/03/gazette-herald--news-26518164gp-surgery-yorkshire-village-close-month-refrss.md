A GP surgery in a rural area of North Yorkshire has said it will be closed for a month due to “unforeseen circumstances”.

The Helperby Branch Surgery in the village of Helperby, near Boroughbridge, will be closed throughout September, according to a notice on the practice’s website and social media page.

  The Helperby Branch Surgery in the village of Helperby, near Boroughbridge 

*(Image: Google)*

A spokesperson for the surgery said patients can continue to access services and appointments at its Boroughbridge site, just under six miles away, during the closure.

“Patients with existing appointments will be contacted where necessary, and our team will be available to assist with any queries or concerns,” they said.

“We sincerely apologise for any inconvenience this temporary closure may cause and thank you for your patience and understanding.”

Local resident Jil Coles, who has a visual impairment and uses a mobility aid, told the BBC that "even a short-term disruption" could pose "a big problem for those of us that cannot drive" during the closure.

"If I need to go to Boroughbridge, I've got to rely on asking my partner to take the day off work," she told the broadcaster.

"Other people will also be relying on friends and family to do exactly the same thing.

"Not everybody has friends and family to be able to get that support."
