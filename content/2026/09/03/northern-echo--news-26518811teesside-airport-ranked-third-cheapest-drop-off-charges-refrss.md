A North East airport has been named one of the cheapest in the UK for dropping off passengers in a new study.

Teesside Airport has the UK’s third-cheapest drop-off charge, with drivers paying £2.50 for up to 10 minutes at the terminal, a study found.

Only Birmingham and Inverness ranked higher, with both offering free designated drop-off areas.

The study compared airports’ standard drop-off charges, time limits, payment rules and nearby free alternatives.

Teesside's £2.50 charge was found to be significantly lower than many larger airports, with the tariff rising to £5 for stays of up to an hour.

Charges vary widely across the country, with some airports, many of them in London, charging up to £10 for a short stop outside the terminal.

Leeds Bradford Airport drop-off charges were named the third most expensive, charging £8 for up to 10 minutes.

Alex Dyer, managing director of Lapland Famille, urged travellers to look beyond the headline drop-off fee when comparing airports.

He said: "Families should compare the whole handover, not just the headline fee.

"A free car park can be better value when it gives extra time to unload, but the walk or shuttle needs to fit the luggage and the airport's check-in time.

"Before leaving, check whether the airport takes payment at the barrier or expects an online payment after the number plate is recorded."

The study used data from 26 UK airports, checked against access information published between June and August this year.

Previous airport guidance said drivers should use the terminal drop-off and pick-up car parks, as stopping elsewhere on the private estate could result in a £100 charge.
