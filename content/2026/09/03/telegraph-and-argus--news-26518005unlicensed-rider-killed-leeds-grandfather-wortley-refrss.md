AN unlicensed motorcyclist has been jailed after causing the death of a much-loved grandfather.

Reece Manley, 26, was sentenced to 14 months in prison after admitting to causing death by careless driving.

The incident led to the death of 75-year-old Alan Lloyd, who was killed in Wortley, in March 2022.

Manley, of Welford Road, Filey, had been riding an unlicensed and uninsured Kawasaki EX motorcycle when he struck Mr Lloyd on the westbound A6110 ring road.

Mr Lloyd was crossing the road at the time of the collision.

In a statement read to the court, Mr Lloyd’s family said: "Our Dad wasn’t ready to go and we as a family were not prepared to lose him in such a way.

"We didn’t get to hug Dad, tell him we loved him and say goodbye."

His family said: "Dad was also a grandad who has been deprived of quality time watching Ben and his wife, Nicola's children, Jacob and Isabelle, grow up.

"Dad was a loving and brilliant Grandad to his grandkids.

"Dad was a big part of their lives with many family get togethers.

"We are a close family, and Dad is sadly missed and will continue to be.

"From this day our lives have been shattered, the stress and anxiety that the loss of Dad has caused, has been immense on all the family."

Detective Sergeant Nina Roper, of the Major Collision Enquiry Team, said: "Mr Lloyd’s death has clearly devastated his family as their victim statement has made clear, and our thoughts remain with them today.

"The actions of Reece Manley robbed them of making more precious memories with a much-loved father and grandfather who was enjoying his retirement after a lifetime of hard work.

"Manley’s riding on that night was clearly unsafe and has resulted in Mr Lloyd and his family paying the ultimate price for his recklessness."
