Several schools are to close early due to a funeral linked to the fatal A66 crash that is expected to draw in large crowds to the area.

Jakub Matusiak’s funeral will take place on Friday, September 4, at St John the Evangelist Church on Normanby Road, South Bank.

Matusiak was one of five occupants of a Volkswagen Passat who died in the head-on collision with a marked Cleveland Police armed response vehicle on the A66 near South Bank on August 22.

The crash claimed seven lives in total, including serving officers PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough.

  Pc Matthew Blades, 37, and Pc Tom Clough, 38

Several schools have agreed with the local authority to stagger closing times because large crowds are expected to cause congestion and transport disruption.

### The planned closures are:

- Archway Academy Teesville: 11.45am
- St Peter’s: 12pm
- South Bank Primary School: 12pm
- Outwood Academy Normanby: 12pm
- St Margaret Clitherow’s Catholic Primary School: 12.15pm
- Mackenzie Thorpe School will be closed all day.
- Nunthorpe is due to remain open as usual.

At South Bank Primary, pupils using Local Authority transport will be collected from school at 11.30am.

Parents and carers have been advised to collect children from their usual collection points when their school closes.

Children who usually walk home alone can leave at the earlier time as normal.

The schools worked with the local authority and other headteachers to arrange the staggered closures.

A previous memorial for Matusiak near the crash site in South Bank, featuring orange balloons, fireworks and masked bikers, sparked mixed reactions within the local community over the tone of the event.

Earlier this week, a fundraiser launched for the families of the two Cleveland Police officers reached its target, surpassing £1.2 million.

44,000 people donated to the campaign, created by Cleveland Police Federation which closed today (September 1), at 9am, raising £1,203,932.

Organisers said money raised would be split equally between the two officers’ families, while the federation would continue to provide support.
