A BRADFORD soldier has been at the biggest gig of his life, piping in this year's Royal Edinburgh Military Tattoo.

Lance Corporal Alex Kelly, from Allerton, performed as a piper during the military spectacle held at Edinburgh Castle.

The event brings together military bands and performers from across the globe.

Lance Corporal Kelly serves with the Royal Dragoon Guards and has already built a varied career.

He said: "The Edinburgh Tattoo was the first major ceremonial event.

"I've done a few local ones here and there but nothing as internationally acclaimed."

He left school at 16 to join the Army, and since then, his journey has taken him from training grounds to combat zones, and now to one of the world’s biggest military stages.

His service has already taken him overseas, including a NATO exercise in Estonia designed to deter Russian aggression on its eastern flank. An operation in America during the summer proved even more eventful.

He said: "The best memory of that is getting a Humvee beached on an anti-tank berm in the night and having to get towed off it."

Alongside these experiences, Lance Corporal Kelly has gained a wide range of skills, including driving armoured vehicles and lorries, leadership training, kayaking, survival skills, and learning to read and write music for the bagpipes.

Surprisingly, he says one of his biggest challenges had nothing to do with the battlefield.

He said: "Not tanks, not tours, but learning to drive an ordinary car in double-quick time, having never sat behind the wheel before."

He plans to remain in the Army for many years, aiming to earn as many qualifications as possible.

But for him, it’s the friendships that matter most.

He said: "I've got decent mates in the Army from all over the country that I wouldn't have met if I'd got a job around my hometown."

Lance Corporal Kelly’s story is a reminder of the opportunities available through military service.

The British Army is currently recruiting for 10,000 roles across the UK.

More than 200 positions are available, including frontline combat, cyber security, helicopter pilots, chefs, and a variety of support roles.

Those interested in learning more about a career in the Army are encouraged to visit the official Army Jobs website.
