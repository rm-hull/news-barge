A cake bought from a County Durham restaurant has been pulled from sale across the chain after a customer reported they they found what appeared to be mould spores on top.

A social media post showing the cake has circulated online after the customer opened the tin and raised concerns about its condition.

The customer first contacted the Manjaros Peterlee branch on August 30.

The company, at 68 York Road, said it had received photographs from the customer and launched an investigation.

  The Facebook post on the mould found in one of Manjaros cakes in a tin 

*(Image: FACEBOOK)*

A spokesperson for Manjaros Peterlee said: "We take all matters concerning food safety and product quality extremely seriously.

"The customer initially contacted us on August 30 regarding their concerns. We subsequently received photographs directly from the customer and, while the matter remains under investigation, a £20 goodwill voucher has been issued to them.

"Our Health and Safety Officer is currently conducting a thorough investigation into the matter. This includes reviewing all relevant internal records, food safety documentation, handling procedures and logs to establish whether there are any identifiable issues that may have contributed to the concern raised.

"We have also contacted the manufacturer responsible for producing and supplying these cakes. Our Health and Safety Officer is liaising directly with the manufacturer as part of the investigation, including reviewing the relevant production and quality assurance processes."

The cake has been pulled from sale at every Manjaros location as a precaution.

The spokesperson added: "As a precautionary measure, we have suspended the sale of this particular cake across all Manjaros locations while the investigation is ongoing. The product will not be returned to sale until the matter has been thoroughly investigated and we are satisfied that it is appropriate to do so.

"At this stage, while the investigation remains ongoing, it would not be appropriate for us to speculate as to the cause. Once the investigation has concluded, we will carefully review the findings and take any further action considered necessary.

"The health and safety of our customers is of the utmost importance to us, and we are treating this matter with the seriousness it warrants."

The Peterlee restaurant is the chain's 12th venue in the North East and 19th nationally.

The Northern Echo has made attempt to contact the customer after the social media post.
