The Shadow Education Secretary said children should not be kept out of classrooms after schools announced they would shut early due to a funeral linked to the fatal A66 crash.

  The funeral of Jakub Matusiak - one of the __five people in the Volkswagen Passat who died__ in the South Bank crash on August 22 - is set to take place tomorrow (September 4). 

The 23-year-old died following a head-on collision between the Passat, which was travelling the wrong way, and a marked Cleveland Police armed response vehicle.

  The horror crash __claimed seven lives in total__, including serving officers PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough.

  Pc Matthew Blades, 37, and Pc Tom Clough, 38 

*(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

Several schools have agreed with Redcar and Cleveland Council to stagger closing times because large crowds are expected to cause congestion and transport disruption.

The news of the disruption due to the funeral, which is taking place at St John the Evangelist Church on Normanby Road, has sparked a response from Shadow Education Secretary Laura Trott.

  Jakub Matusiak, 23. 

*(Image: SOCIAL MEDIA)*

Ms Trott said the Education Secretary Lucy Powell has a duty to “ensure that this does not impact on the honest and decent pupils, parents and teachers in the area”.

She said: “I have been informed that numerous schools in the area will close early - citing 'transport disruption' - due to commemorations for those who were not serving in the line of duty on 22 August. I do not think we should support this.”

  MP Laura Trott 

*(Image: DAVID WOOLFALL)*

She cited how the Covid pandemic showed how damaging absence from the classroom can be and added that intimidation “must never be tolerated”.

  **Planned closures include:** 

- Archway Academy Teesville: 11.45am
- St Peter’s: 12pm
- South Bank Primary School: 12pm
- Outwood Academy Normanby: 12pm
- St Margaret Clitherow’s Catholic Primary School: 12.15pm
- Mackenzie Thorpe School will be closed all day.
- Nunthorpe is due to remain open as usual.

At South Bank Primary, pupils using local authority transport will be collected from school at 11.30am.

Parents and carers have been advised to collect children from their usual collection points when their school closes.

  Two police officers are among seven people who have died following a devastating crash on the A66 in Teesside 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

Children who usually walk home alone can leave at an earlier time to normal.

The schools have worked with the local authority and other headteachers to arrange the staggered closures.

A previous memorial for Matusiak near the crash site, featuring orange balloons, fireworks and masked bikers, sparked mixed reactions within the local community over the tone of the event.

Earlier this week, a fundraiser launched for the families of the two Cleveland Police officers reached its target, surpassing £1.2 million.

44,000 people donated to the campaign, created by Cleveland Police Federation which closed today (September 1), at 9am, raising £1,203,932.

Organisers said money raised would be split equally between the two officers’ families, while the federation would continue to provide support.
