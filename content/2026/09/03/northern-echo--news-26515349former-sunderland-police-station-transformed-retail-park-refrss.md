A FORMER police station has been transformed into a bustling retail park.

The site on Durham Road, Sunderland, had stood empty for more than a decade, and has now been redeveloped into a 32,450 sq ft retail park complete with a 110-space car park and Fastned electric vehicle charging facilities.

The project was led by Almscliffe Dhesi Group with funding support from Develop North.

Brendan O’Grady, fund manager at Tier One Capital, investment adviser to Develop North, said: "One of the strengths of Develop North's investment strategy is identifying experienced developers with well-considered schemes capable of delivering strong commercial outcomes alongside wider economic benefits.

"The regeneration of the former Farringdon Police Station is an excellent example of that approach in action.

"A prominent brownfield site has been transformed into a high-quality retail destination that has attracted major national occupiers, created new employment opportunities, and delivered lasting value for the local community."

The retail park is now fully occupied and home to B&M, Costa Drive-Thru, Greggs, Dicksons Butchers, The Tanning Shop, and Westway Vets.

The project is believed to have created more than 100 new jobs.

Bal Singh, director at Almscliffe Dhesi Group, said: "Seeing this development fully completed and occupied is enormously rewarding.

"After working on the project for several years, we've transformed what had become a long-derelict site into a vibrant retail destination that is creating jobs, attracting investment, and providing new amenities for the local community."
