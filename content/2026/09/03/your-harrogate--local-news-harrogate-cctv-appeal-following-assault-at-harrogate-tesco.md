Police have released a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to following an alleged assault in Harrogate.

The incident reportedly happened at the Tesco Express store on Cambridge Road on 22nd July.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for anyone who recognises the person pictured to come forward.

*Information can be emailed to [Fiona.Marshall@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Fiona.Marshall@northyorkshire.police.uk) or submitted through the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting ‘Tell us about’ and then ‘An existing case or report’.*

*Information can also be provided anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*

*People are asked to quote reference 12260140205.*
