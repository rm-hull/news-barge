A Bradford headteacher has apologised and explained why the school had to close on the first day of term yesterday.

Bingley Grammar School, on Keighley Road, Bingley, was shut yesterday (Wednesday) but is back open as normal today (Thursday).

In a post on social media, Luke Weston, headteacher at Bingley Grammar School, said: "Firstly, I would like to offer our sincere apologies once again that we were unable to open the school.

He confirmed that all systems were back online by the end of Wednesday and the school would reopen as normal on Thursday.

He explained why the school had closed on Wednesday.

## What the headteacher said

Mr Weston said: "Over the summer holidays, extensive work was completed across the school site, including ongoing roofing repairs and construction work for our new building on the school field.

"The most significant project was transitioning to a new electricity sub-station.

"This work was led by the National Grid which began on Wednesday, August 26, continuing over the bank holiday weekend with support from our IT and site teams.

"Unfortunately, we had no ability to alter the time given to do this.

"As a result, it became clear on Tuesday that we would not be able to get all essential systems (electrics, phone lines, and IT) back online in time to open safely on Wednesday.

"Our teams spent Wednesday ensuring all systems were operational, allowing the school to reopen on Thursday. "Thank you for your understanding and continued support."
