A HISTORIC pub dating back more than 400 years is set to go under the hammer.

The Pack Horse Inn in Widdop, near Hebden Bridge, will feature in a live stream auction by BTG Eddisons Property Auctions on October 1, with a guide price of £350,000.

Will Thompson, director at BTG Eddisons Property Auctions, said: "The Pack Horse Inn is a property with a remarkable history and a very distinctive setting."

Dating back to around 1610, the inn was built from millstone grit and served as a rest stop for packhorse drivers transporting textiles between Yorkshire and Lancashire.

Over the centuries, it also housed navvies working on reservoir projects and later became a popular destination for rock climbers.

  The inside of the Pack Horse Inn in Widdop, near Hebden Bridge 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The pub, which closed in March 2025, sits on the Pennine Way and operated for generations as a destination pub and restaurant.

Mr Thompson said: "It has been part of the life of the Upper Calder Valley for centuries and its position on the Pennine Way means it has continued to attract walkers, climbers and cyclists.

"The pub has been closed for more than a year, so it will be interesting to see whether a new owner takes on the challenge."
