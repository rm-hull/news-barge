CINEWORLD is offering £4 tickets for National Cinema Weekend, giving film fans the chance to enjoy the big screen for less.

The offer is valid at all Cineworld cinemas nationwide, including the Bradford branch, on Saturday and Sunday, September 5 and 6, covering all films and formats except IMAX, which will cost just £6.

A spokesperson said: "This coming weekend the perfect chance to catch the films you missed the first time around, or experience your favourite releases of the summer all over again – for just £4."

The promotion includes premium experiences such as 4DX, ScreenX, Superscreen, and recliner seating.

Fans spending £15 or more at the snacks counter will also receive a free scratchcard with a guaranteed reward.

The weekend line-up features some of the year’s biggest releases, including Spider-Man: Brand New Day, The Odyssey, Insidious: Out of the Further, and The Death of Robin Hood.

Family-friendly titles like Minions & Monsters, Toy Story 5, PAW Patrol: The Dino Movie, and Moana (Live Action) will also be available.

Cineworld is also running a merchandise sale, with selected items priced at £1 each or three for £2.50.
