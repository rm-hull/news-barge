## Gloria Steinem, the feminist trailblazer, writer and activist who helped define the modern women’s movement in the US, has died at 92. Here are some of her most memorable quotes.

Gloria Steinem will be remembered as a hugely influential icon of America's feminist movement.

The internationally recognised journalist, author and feminist activist was one of the most famous voices of the US’ women’s movement. She dedicated her life to causes such as reproductive freedom, the legalisation of abortion, domestic violence prevention, equal rights and pay for women, as well as the fight for political gender equality.
