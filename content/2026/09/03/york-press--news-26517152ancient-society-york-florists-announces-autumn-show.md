The world's oldest horticultural society will hold its autumn show later this month.

The Ancient Society of York Florists, established 258 years ago, will host the event on Saturday, 12 September at Wigginton Recreation Hall.

The show will feature various classes, including Roses & Fuchsias, Chrysanthemums, Dahlias, Pelargoniums and Geraniums.

There will also be Pot Plants, Cut Flowers, Gladioli, Fruit classes, Vegetables, Floral Art, Baking, Handicrafts and Photography.

New classes for Art and Children have also been included.

The show opens at 11am and closes at 3pm, with an admission fee of £1, and parking is free.

The full schedule is available at: www.ancientsocietyofyorkflorists.weebly.com
