A racist drug dealer caught with £21,000 worth of cocaine told a court he had no memory of his arrest or the abuse he inflicted on police officers.

Mark Freer launched a volley of racist and homophobic abuse at officers before shouting 'I will nut you, bite you, anything I can' as he wrestled with them.

The 25-year-old was arrested following reports of a disturbance at a block of flats near Cleveland Police headquarters.

When officers searched him, they recovered to knives and a bag containing the Class A drug, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Rachel Butt, prosecuting, said Freer was first spotted by police when he became aggressive while inhaling nitrous oxide – laughing gas.

She said the defendant spat in the face of one of the officers as he attempted to place a spit-guard over his head.

Miss Butt said Freer shouted at one officer ‘I will kill that Asian , expect a shotgun to your head, you P*** - I will skin you alive’.

The court heard how he had been recalled on licence following his arrest after he was sentenced to 71 months in prison in September 2024 for selling cocaine.

Freer, of no fixed abode but formerly of Middlesbrough, pleaded guilty to possession with intent to supply cocaine, possession of a knife, assaulting an emergency worker and numerous public order offences.

His lawyer, Albany Kidd, said he pleaded guilty to at the earliest opportunity.

“In terms of the offences themselves, there is little I can help the court with,” she said.

The defendant said: “I know my behaviour was disgusting and I do regret it but I can’t remember a thing.

“I have never done that before on previous arrests and I would like apologise to the police officers for doing that.”
