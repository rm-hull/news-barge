Leeds is among the top destinations where tickets have been snapped up in Northern's flash sale.

The sale, launched on Wednesday, September 2, and running until Friday, September 4, has seen hundreds of thousands of cut-price tickets bought by passengers across the North.

Northern, the UK's second-largest train operator, has put four million tickets up for grabs at £2 for adults and £1 for children.

Early figures reveal Leeds as one of the ten most popular destinations for these bargain tickets, which are valid for travel between September 9 and October 23, 2026.

The Leeds to Bradford, Ilkley, and Skipton routes are among the areas with the highest number of remaining sale tickets.

Despite the surge in sales, there are still plenty of deals, with tickets available for £2 on popular routes such as the Settle & Carlisle line.

The tickets can be bought on Northern's app, online, and at ticket offices and machines at stations managed by Northern.

A full list of available routes can be found using Northern's 'cheap fare finder' tool.

Northern operates 2,650 services a day to more than 500 stations across the North of England.
