The Pigeon Detectives are set to return to Bridlington Spa for a major live concert.

After nearly two decades, the Leeds-formed indie rock band will be back at the venue on Saturday 5 December 2026, marking their first performance at Bridlington Spa since 2008.

The group, known for their energetic performances, rose to fame with their 2007 debut album "Wait for Me," featuring popular tracks like "I Found Out," "Take Her Back," "Romantic Type," and "I’m Not Sorry".

Their comeback is anticipated to be a nostalgic experience for fans and an introduction for newer audiences.

Joining The Pigeon Detectives will be The Lilacs, known for their anthemic sound and high-energy performances.

The Lilacs, who recently headlined This Feeling By The Sea in April, are expected to deliver a performance filled with feel-good indie favourites.

With Christmas approaching, this concert provides an alternative to festive tunes, offering a chance to kick off the holiday season with indie anthems.

Tickets for the concert, priced at £29.50 plus a booking fee, are available for purchase now.
