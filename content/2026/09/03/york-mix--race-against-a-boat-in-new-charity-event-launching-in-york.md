Can you outrun a boat?

That’s the unique challenge heading to York’s River Ouse with the new **50+ Beat the Boat**, a fun-filled fundraising event supporting Age UK York, with City Cruises on** Sunday 4 October**.

Taking place as part of this year’s 50+ Festival, Beat the Boat will see individuals, age 50 and over, take on the light-hearted challenge.

The event, which is designed to celebrate the city’s over-50s community, encourages people to sign up to compete and race against the City Cruises boat as it sails along the river Ouse and back to its starting position.

The 2.7km distance along the riverside path will be started by the sound of the horn on the City Cruises boat sailing from Kings Staith, travelling to the iconic Millennium Bridge and, as the boat turns, returning along the opposite side of the river and finishing at Terry Avenue.

Carl Wain, deputy CEO at Age UK York said: “We couldn’t resist the opportunity to celebrate this year’s 50+ Festival with an original York event that brings people together in a fun and memorable way.

“At Age UK York, we champion the national Act Now, Age Better campaign, which encourages people aged 50 and over to stay active and take positive steps to support their health and wellbeing in later life. This event is a fantastic opportunity to put that message into action in a light-hearted and entertaining way.

We’re thrilled to be partnering with City Cruises and can’t wait to see runners taking part and spectators of all ages getting behind them, cheering them on as they sail along the river.”

Christopher Pegg, head of regional commercial, sales and marketing at City Cruises said: “We’re delighted to support Age UK York with such a brilliant event.

“The River Ouse is York’s oldest landmark, and it’s great to see it put to use in a completely new way, pitting one of our passenger boats full of cheering supporters against runners on the riverside!

“We hope it raises a fantastic amount for such a great cause.”

Whether you’re looking for a new challenge, want to enjoy a day out with friends, or simply want to support Age UK York, Beat the Boat offers a chance to get involved and help make a difference.
