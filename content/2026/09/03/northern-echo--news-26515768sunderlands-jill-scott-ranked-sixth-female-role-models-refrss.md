Two of the North East's most celebrated footballers have been named among Britain's greatest female role models in a new nationwide poll.

Former England stars Jill Scott and Beth Mead, who both grew up in the North East, were included in a list of the UK's top 30 female role models.

  Jill Scott.


Commissioned by British Gas, the survey of 2,000 people ranked Sunderland-born Jill Scott sixth, while Whitby-born Beth Mead made the top 30.

Jill helped England win the Euros in 2022, alongside Beth, before going on to win I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! and going viral for collaborations with other football legends including Roy Keane, Gary Neville and Ian Wright.

  Jill Scott.


The former Manchester City midfielder has teamed up with Women's Super League Football and British Gas to create a children's book aimed at inspiring the next generation of football fans.

Jill said: "Growing up, I didn't always see footballers like me in the books I read. I know how important it is for young people to see themselves in the stories they read."

The research found that 92 per cent of people believe seeing female footballers in books, on television and across the media would encourage more girls and young women to take up sport.

  Beth Mead.

Whitby-raised Beth Mead also made the list after campaigning to grow the women's game in recent years.

Around 65 per cent of adults said women deserve greater recognition in sport, while two-thirds of parents said children's books, television programmes and films do not show enough ambitious girls.

Jill is also a trustee of Sunderland AFC's Foundation of Light, where she supports the charity's women's football provision and its work with up to 20,000 young people and families each year.
