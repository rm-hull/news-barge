A brass band with more than 150 years of history is calling on community support to help it reach the national stage.

Felling Band will represent the town at the National Brass Band Championships in September 2026, a major event in the brass band calendar.

The band is raising £4,000 to cover the cost of competing and has launched a crowdfunder to help reach its goal.

David Snaith, chairman of Felling Band, said: "This is a huge moment for the band and for Felling.

"We have been part of this community for over 150 years.

"Competing at the National Championships this year gives us the chance to represent Felling on a national stage, and every bit of support from our community means the world to us."

The band rehearses twice a week at Felling Community Centre and has around 30 members of all ages.

To thank supporters, Felling Band will host a free community concert at Felling Community Centre on November 28, 2026, at 3pm.
