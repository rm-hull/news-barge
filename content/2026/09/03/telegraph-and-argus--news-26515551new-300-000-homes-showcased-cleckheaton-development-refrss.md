A HOUSEBUILDER has opened two new showhomes at a West Yorkshire development.

Harron Homes launched The Oakham and The Faversham at its Merchant Fields site in Cleckheaton during a recent grand opening weekend, giving potential buyers the chance to tour the properties and speak with sales executives.

Lucinda Dickens, sales and marketing director for Harron Homes Yorkshire, said: "It was fantastic to welcome visitors to Merchant Fields and give them their first look inside The Oakham and The Faversham.

"Showhomes are such an important part of helping buyers picture their future, and these two homes really demonstrate the space, flexibility and quality available at Merchant Fields.

"Whether visitors were looking for their first home, more space for a growing family or a fresh start in a well-connected location, the launch gave them a chance to explore what life here could look like."

The event marked the latest milestone for the Hunsworth Lane development, which will eventually deliver 291 homes, including 58 affordable properties.

It also features plans for new footpaths, open spaces, a fully equipped play area, and a trim trail.

Sustainability is a key focus at Merchant Fields, with homes being built to include air source heat pumps and electric vehicle charging points.

The development also aims to support local biodiversity through the creation of a pond and orchard.

Both showhomes on display are four-bedroom detached properties.

The Oakham features an integral garage, a light-filled lounge with a bay window, and an open-plan kitchen and dining area that opens onto the rear garden.

Upstairs, the main bedroom includes an ensuite, and there are three additional bedrooms and a family bathroom.

The Faversham offers a similar layout with a bright, front-facing lounge, a spacious kitchen and dining area, and an adjoining utility room.

Visitors to the grand opening were entered into a prize draw to win a spa experience for two at nearby HUB26, and those who reserved a home during the event received 50 per cent off HUB26 gym memberships for six months.

Homes at Merchant Fields currently start at £300,000.

A variety of incentives are available on selected plots, including deposit contributions, Part Exchange, Assisted Move, and support with stamp duty and legal fees.

Cleckheaton’s location offers easy access to Leeds, Bradford, and other regional centres via strong bus connections and nearby motorway links.

The town is also home to a range of independent shops, cafés, restaurants, schools, and leisure facilities, including Spen Valley Leisure Centre.
