## Can Democrats flip Kentucky’s 6th? Party hopes to mirror Andy Beshear’s local success

![Rep. Andy Barr is stepping aside to run for retiring Sen. Mitch McConnell’s seat, opening up Kentucky’s 6th District](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/16/22/image002.png)

With Rep. Andy Barr stepping aside to run for retiring Sen. Mitch McConnell’s seat, Kentucky’s 6th District has opened up. Barr is among 36 Republican House members relinquishing their seats this cycle, relative to 23 Democrats.

Navy veteran and former federal prosecutor Zach Dembo secured the Democratic nomination. He competes against Republican Ralph Alvarado, a physician and ex-state senator backed by Trump in the primary.

The central Kentucky seat has been reliably Republican since Barr unseated incumbent Democratic Rep. Ben Chandler in 2012, with the National Republican Congressional Committee noting an edge in voter registration.

However, Democrats draw optimism from 2023, when Democratic Gov. Andy Beshear decisively carried the district in an off-year contest.

Fayette County, the district’s largest locality including Lexington, backed Democrats by over 40 percentage points then. Yet Barr secured a comfortable reelection a year later as Trump won Kentucky.

## Trump-backed David Flippo faces Teresa Benitez-Thompson for open Nevada House seat

Republican Rep. Mark Amodei is stepping down after 15 years representing northern Nevada’s high desert 2nd District. However, rancher and former state Sen. James Settelmeyer, who was the retiring lawmaker’s preferred candidate, failed to secure the party nomination to succeed him.

Primary voters instead selected retired Air Force Lt. Col. and small business owner David Flippo, who won Trump's endorsement even after Amodei described the president’s selection as "a mistake."

Flippo now faces Teresa Benitez-Thompson, a social worker, former majority leader of the Nevada legislature, and former Miss America contestant who stepped down from her seat in 2024 because of term limits.

The district, which includes Reno and the state capital, Carson City, has never voted for a Democrat. Trump increased his vote share in every presidential contest between 2016 and 2024, and Republicans maintain a strong lead in voter registration.

Benitez-Thompson contends she will resist federal overreach, whereas Flippo promises to reinforce the district’s role in Trump’s "Make America Great Again" movement.

## Fired by Hegseth in Pentagon purge, retired Navy admiral runs for Nancy Mace’s open seat

![South Carolina Representative Nancy Mace showed off a tattooed look in a new YouTube video](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/17/12/07/Screenshot-2026-08-17-at-13-14-42.png)

Rep. Nancy Mace is leaving her South Carolina 1st District seat after placing a distant fifth in the Republican primary for governor. Her tenure saw on-and-off feuds with Trump, who opposed her campaign over her push to release records on disgraced financier Jeffrey Epstein.

Republicans nominated attorney and Charleston County councilwoman Jenny Costa Honeycutt against Democrat Nancy Lacore. A retired Navy vice admiral, Lacore won a hard-fought primary after being fired by Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth during a broader Pentagon purge last year.

Trump carried the district easily in 2024, but the open seat could alter the contest. Democrat Joe Cunningham flipped it during Trump’s first term before Mace won it back in 2020, and subsequent redistricting likely made the district redder.

## Ex-sheriff deputy and Air Force veteran clash in transformed Texas 35th District

Major redistricting by the state’s Republican-controlled Legislature has fundamentally transformed Texas’ 35th congressional district, shifting what was once a dependable Democratic corridor stretching from San Antonio to Austin.

The newly drawn boundaries feature a majority-Hispanic electorate that backed Trump by double digits in 2024. Aiming to win over disillusioned voters, Democrat Johnny Garcia, a former sheriff of Bexar County, which includes San Antonio, is positioning himself as a "law-and-order" candidate while voicing opposition to the president’s sweeping tariffs.

Garcia faces Republican candidate Carlos De La Cruz, brother of Republican Rep. Monica De La Cruz, who could potentially face an even tighter reelection fight in South Texas’ District 15.

A retired Air Force veteran running in a district with a strong military presence, Carlos de la Cruz earned Trump’s endorsement in a crowded primary. That field included Republican state Rep. John Lujan, who helped pass the redrawn district boundaries.

Republican House Speaker Mike Johnson recently campaigned in the district, bringing a national party presence that could prove necessary if Garcia keeps the contest closer than expected.
