Sunderland Restaurant Week is celebrating its 10th anniversary with a city-wide celebration of food and drink.

The event will run from September 5 to 13 and marks a decade since it was first launched.

More than 50 restaurants, cafes, pubs, and bars are taking part this year, offering special set menu deals at £10, £15, £20, and £25.

Roberta Redecke, head of business services at Sunderland’s Business Improvement Districts (BIDs), said: "It’s incredible to think that we have now notched up ten years.

"Not only has Sunderland Restaurant Week brought a real boost to the local economy, but it has also showcased to the region and beyond how many amazing places to eat and drink the city has to offer.

"It has made a huge difference to so many city businesses, bringing in customers at times when otherwise they might be quiet.

"And it has also created a great legacy – introducing diners to new experiences or reminding them about old favourites – which they have then continued to return to across the year."

The event has attracted more than 193,000 diners since it began and has generated more than £6 million for the local economy.

Seven venues—Angelo’s, Asiana, Gatsby, Port of Call, House of Zen, Marina Vista, and Signatures—were part of the very first Restaurant Week and are all signed up for this September’s event.

Vouchers can be downloaded from www.sunderlandbid.co.uk/restaurant-week.
