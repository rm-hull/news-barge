Passengers travelling through Bradford are facing delays and cancellations this evening due to a signalling fault between Bradford Forster Square and Shipley.

Northern said some lines have been blocked, causing disruption to services running between Bradford, Leeds, Skipton and Ilkley.

The disruption is expected to continue until at least 6.30pm on Thursday (September 3).

## 
  **Services cancelled and delayed**

According to Northern, train services running to and from Bradford Forster Square and Shipley are being cancelled or delayed while Network Rail staff work to fix the problem.

Routes affected include services between Bradford Forster Square and Leeds, Bradford Forster Square and Skipton, and Bradford Forster Square and Ilkley.

Northern said services between Leeds and Skipton are not affected by the disruption.

## 
  **Network Rail staff on site**

The rail operator said the disruption is being caused by a fault with the signalling system near Shipley.

In a travel update, Northern said services between Bradford Forster Square and Shipley were being disrupted because of a signalling fault and that Network Rail staff were on site.

Train services are currently unable to operate on some lines between Bradford Forster Square and Shipley while repairs are carried out.

## 
  **Ilkley and Skipton routes affected**

Passengers travelling between Bradford and Ilkley have been warned of cancellations and delays throughout the evening.

The 5.21pm service from Ilkley to Bradford Forster Square, due to arrive at 5.53pm, was cancelled because of the signalling fault.

Stations affected on the Ilkley line include Ilkley, Ben Rhydding, Burley-in-Wharfedale, Menston, Guiseley, Baildon, Shipley, Frizinghall and Bradford Forster Square.

The disruption is also affecting services between Skipton and Bradford Forster Square, including passengers travelling through Bradford Forster Square, Frizinghall, Shipley, Saltaire, Bingley, Crossflatts, Keighley, Steeton & Silsden, Cononley and Skipton.

## 
  **Ticket restrictions lifted**

Northern has temporarily lifted ticket restrictions in the affected area while the disruption continues.

The operator said Advance and Peak ticket restrictions will not apply until services return to normal.

Passengers have been advised to travel on the next available Northern service where possible and to check their journey before travelling.

## 
  **Compensation available for delayed passengers**

Northern said passengers delayed by 15 minutes or more may be entitled to compensation through its Delay Repay scheme.

Customers can check live travel information and service updates through National Rail and Northern's website while repairs continue.
