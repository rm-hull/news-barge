A pervert has been locked up after he tried to incite a child to engage in sexual activity.

Anthony Hancock was arrested in the summer when he was caught by a decoy posing as a teenage girl, Teesside Crown Court heard.

The 36-year-old was also failing to comply with his sex offender notification requirements before his arrest.

He had contacted the 'teenager' online and attempted to incite a girl aged 13 to 15 to engage in sexual activity.

Hancock, of Shelley Road, Middlesbrough, pleaded guilty to attempting to engage in sexual activity with a child, attempting to engage in sexual communication with a child and two counts of breaching the terms of his sexual notification requirement between May 30 and June 9.

He appeared at Teesside Crown Court on Tuesday, September 1, where he was given a five-year and four-month sentence, and an indefinite Sexual Harm Prevention Order.

Speaking after the hearing, Detective Constable David Philipson, from Cleveland Police’s Paedophile Online Investigation Team (POLIT), said: “Hancock is a repeat offender whose first offence occurred more than a decade ago.

“I welcome the sentence imposed by the courts, which demonstrates the level of harm that Hancock poses, and I am pleased that he is now behind bars where he cannot be a further risk.

“Cleveland POLIT are committed to robust and swift action against individuals like Hancock who offend against children online.”
