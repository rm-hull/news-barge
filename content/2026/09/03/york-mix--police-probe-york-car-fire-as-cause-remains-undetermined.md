Police say they are making enquiries into the car fire in York.

The BMW went up in flames on Navigation Road at about 5.30pm.

The incident was caught on video.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “An fire investigation was conducted yesterday evening, and the cause was found to be ‘undetermined’.

“That means the investigator couldn’t find evidence to support an accidental or deliberate cause.

“As for the fire itself, the vehicle was well alight when crews from Acomb arrived, it was outside two three-storey buildings.

“As well as extinguishing the fire, crews protected nearby properties from fire and smoke”

No injuries were reported. The car sustained 90% fire damage and there was some heat damage to guttering on one of the nearby properties.
