A man is taking on a 30-day rowing challenge to raise funds and awareness for a local cause close to his heart.

Ollie Varey is attempting to row 350 miles (563,270 metres) on a rowing machine throughout September, the equivalent distance of crossing the English Channel, in support of Acorn Community Care.

The charity is based on an 8-acre farm in Norton, Malton.

He said: "In the month of September, I will be using the Rowing machine in the gym to row the equivalent of the English Channel.

"This is 350 miles (563,270 metres) in 30 days.

"I am doing this challenge for Acorn Community Care, a wonderful charity that provides care and support for people living with learning and physical disabilities through a wide range of services that they provide."

The charity provides care and support for people living with learning and physical disabilities through a wide range of services that we provide.

Mr Varey said: "I am doing this challenge to raise awareness and funds for an amazing charity.

"The service users will be coming down to help me out and contribute to the challenge, which is a great way for them to help the charity and also keep fit."
