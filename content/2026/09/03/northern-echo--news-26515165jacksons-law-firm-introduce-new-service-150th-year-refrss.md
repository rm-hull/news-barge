A LAW firm has launched a new specialist intellectual property (IP) service.

Jacksons, which has offices on Teesside and in Newcastle, has introduced the new service in its 150th year, following CILEX lawyer and associate Charlotte Barugh completing a Postgraduate Certificate in Intellectual Property from Bournemouth University.

The new service covers trademarks, copyright, design and patents.

Ms Barugh, who works in the dispute resolution team, said: "I’m delighted to have achieved a Postgraduate Certificate in Intellectual Property with Merit from Bournemouth University.

"Completing a postgraduate qualification in just five months alongside practice was a challenge, but one that was incredibly rewarding.

"IP is a growing and exciting area of law, and I’m looking forward to putting what I have learnt into practice and spending more time working in this area."

The launch comes as 2026 marks both the firm's 150th anniversary and the anniversary of the UK’s first trademark registration.

Jacksons has long handled IP-related matters, but the new service expands its expertise, supported by Ms Barugh’s academic training from the Centre for Intellectual Property Policy and Management (CIPPM).

The course, accredited by the Intellectual Property Regulation Board, equips Ms Barugh and the firm with advanced, practical knowledge of IP law in both commercial and dispute scenarios.

Her qualification allows Jacksons to advise on both contentious and non-contentious IP matters, and will help the firm provide strategic, commercially-focused guidance on protecting value and avoiding disputes.

Richard Flounders, dispute resolution partner at Jacksons, said: "As a firm, we support our team members to identify new opportunities and achieve both personal and professional development goals – and Charlotte is a great example of this.

"Charlotte is passionate about helping our clients through any IP challenges or preventing future issues relating to IP law, including copyright, trademarks, patents, and designs.

"Through her work in practice at the firm, Charlotte is running with her qualification, and we are very proud to see her succeed."

Ms Barugh’s IP work will include trademark services and registration renewals, and she will work alongside Samantha Higgins, associate solicitor in the Corporate and Commercial team at Jacksons.
