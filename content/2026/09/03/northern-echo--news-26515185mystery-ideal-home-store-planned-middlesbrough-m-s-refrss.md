The future of a former Marks and Spencer store remains unclear after a new department store and café failed to open.

Ideal Home Store was set to open within the vacant Linthorpe Road shop in Middlesbrough on Friday (August 28) but, despite advertising the date since June, the huge store remains bare.

The shop was meant to feature local businesses selling fashion, cosmetics and homeware, with more than 200 brands and 50 local partnerships signed up.

But photos taken this week show how the former M&S store’s ground floor is almost completely empty, days after it was due to open.

All that can be seen inside the huge space is three tables, several chairs, a carboard box, and other miscellaneous items.

Ideal Home Store is yet to comment on the situation.

The project was first announced in spring, with an open day being advertised for businesses in May ahead of the "grand opening" on August 28.

Bosses said the site would offer perks including no staffing costs, TikTok live-selling booths and computerised tills that could be operated from a phone.

They hoped the venture would “revitalise the high street experience” with a “carefully curated selection of UK and European fashion, beauty, and homeware brands”.

The firm also promised to reopen the former M&S café, selling an “array of freshly brewed coffees, artisan teas, and delectable sandwiches and pastries”.

Ideal Home Store posters still cover the unit’s shutters and doors.

M&S had been a staple of Middlesbrough town centre for 122 years when it closed its doors in April 2023, much to the disappointment of shoppers.

A petition was even launched in a bid to save the retail giant’s site in the town - but despite being signed by more than 1,000 people, it wasn’t enough.

The closure followed the likes of House of Fraser, Debenhams, Miss Selfridge, TK Maxx and Topshop in the long list of brands to pull out of the town centre.

In April 2024, it was revealed that the store was snapped up by Linthorpe 54 Limited, a firm owned by local businessman Nasser Din and his business partner Zahid Iqbal.

The unit has largely stayed closed since then, apart from hosting the immersive arts experience Happy Rooms in summer 2024.

Ideal Home Store and the Hillstreet Centre have been contacted for comment.
