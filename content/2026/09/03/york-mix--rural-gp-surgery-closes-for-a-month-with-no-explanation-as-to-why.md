The GP surgery on Main Street in Helperby, near Easingwold, has closed for a month.

Villagers have been told that it will be shut during September due to “unforeseen circumstances”.

Nigel Denison, chair of Brafferton and Helperby Parish Council, told the BBC that the temporary closure had come as “a bit of a surprise”.

He said he felt the community could cope in the short term but if it was closed for a longer period then it would be a problem.

The Boroughbridge branch of ***Church Lane Surgery*** is at least 6 miles away. 

**In a statement on their website, Church Lane Surgery says:**

“During this period, patients can continue to access services and appointments at our Boroughbridge site.

“Patients with existing appointments will be contacted where necessary, and our team will be available to assist with any queries or concerns.

“We sincerely apologise for any inconvenience this temporary closure may cause and thank you for your patience and understanding.

“In the event of a medical emergency please dial 999. For all other medical queries please call NHS 111. Thank you for your support.”

No further explanation has been given.
