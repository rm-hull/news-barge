Fresh images have revealed the latest progress on a major transformation of a Teesside town centre, with retail units beginning to take shape.

Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council is showcasing progress at Eston Precinct, where work continues on the redevelopment by Esh Construction for a "vibrant and welcoming" destination.

The development includes Unit 1 - a large space at the heart of the scheme which the council say could be transformed into a large café or restaurant.

The regeneration project aims to create opportunities for businesses and revitalise the town centre for residents and visitors.

  The Eston Precinct redevelopment is taking shape. 

*(Image: Redcar & Cleveland Borough Council)*

Councillor Lynn Pallister MBE, the council's Cabinet Member for Growth and Enterprise, said: "It's fantastic to see the continued progress at Eston Precinct.

"We're creating a place that residents can be proud of and that businesses will want to be part of."

The council said potential occupiers have already shown strong interest in the development.

Businesses interested in space at Eston Precinct can contact commercial property agent Portland Dodds Brown about availability.

Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council will continue to share updates as work progresses at Eston Precinct.

The scheme will create seven new retail units, 14,600 square feet of retail space and an outdoor leisure area, with the first phase expected to finish by the end of autumn.

Additional work will include new paving, trees, benches and upgraded lighting and power infrastructure to support events and markets.
