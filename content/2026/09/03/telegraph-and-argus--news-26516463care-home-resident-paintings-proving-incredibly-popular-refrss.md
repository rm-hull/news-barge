A care home resident has raised more than £100 by selling his artwork for the first time.

David Hardman, 75, who lives at Herncliffe Care Home in Keighley and was diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease a decade ago, has long been passionate about painting, a skill he began developing at the age of four.

As his condition progressed, he found it difficult to hold a paintbrush steady and stopped painting for two years.

Mr Hardman gradually retrained himself to paint, adapting his technique to work around the condition.

*(Image: Supplied)*

## Artwork sold at the care home fair

Mr Hardman sold his paintings for the first time at the care home’s annual Summer Fayre on August 22.

His artwork raised £110 towards the £1,000 collected on the day, with proceeds going to the residents’ activity fund and Dementia Friendly Keighley.

He said: "I have never sold my work before, so I’m thrilled to have raised so much money for two very worthy causes."

  David with members of his family at the Summer Fayre

## Weekly art classes for residents

Mr Hardman began leading weekly art classes for fellow residents earlier this year.

He said: "Both teaching others and creating my own artwork gives me a great sense of achievement, and it’s a pleasure to share my passion with my fellow residents and the team.

"My favourite pieces are those I create for others, and I have sketched and painted many different pieces for everyone at Herncliffe.

  A table of David's art at the Summer Fayre 

*(Image: Herncliffe Care Home)*

"My wife Janet has been a constant source of support and encouragement in helping me to keep going, and with lots of practice I am now able to continue painting."

Each session explores a different topic and residents are encouraged to share memories and stories inspired by their artwork.

## 'We are immensely proud of David'

Helen McAskill, manager at Herncliffe Nursing Home, said: "David is doing a wonderful job leading his art classes, and it has been lovely to see the difference they are making to our residents.

"With support from our activities team, the sessions bring everyone together to learn new skills, relax and enjoy each other’s company.

"It has also been fantastic to see David sharing his passion with others and growing in confidence each week.

"We all had a wonderful time at our Summer Fayre, and it was brilliant to see so many people from the local community come along to support our home and David.

"His paintings proved incredibly popular, and we feel honoured to have helped him sell his artwork for the very first time.

"We are immensely proud of David and hope he continues sharing his wonderful talent with others for many years to come."
