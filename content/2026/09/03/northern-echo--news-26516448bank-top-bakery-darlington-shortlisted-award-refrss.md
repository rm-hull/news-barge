A Darlington bakery says it cannot keep up with demand for its school cake and custard slices as customers flock in for traditional favourites.

Bank Top Bakery on Victoria Road has been shortlisted in The Northern Echo’s Best Bakery category, but owner William Lees, 64, from Middlesbrough, says its growing reputation has largely been built through word of mouth.

The family-run business is run by Mr Lees alongside his partner Tracey, daughter and a close friend.

The team makes its products on site each day, with fresh cakes, pastries and savoury bakes prepared for customers.

Mr Lees said the bakery had become increasingly busy over the years.

He said: “It has been going seven years now and it is a family-run business.

“Everything is made on site, and everything is fresh every day. We are really busy. In the last three years, it has got so busy we can hardly handle it.

“I think the product is good, the prices are good and you get a lot for your money. That’s why people seem to love it.”

While pastries, croissants and other baked goods remain popular, Mr Lees said the bakery’s most in-demand products are its traybakes and traditional sweet treats.

He said: “The millionaire brownies, tiffins and things like that are really popular.

“But the traditional stuff is probably the biggest seller. The school cake and custard slices just fly. We cannot make enough of them.”

Bank Top Bakery is among businesses shortlisted in the Best Bakery category in The Northern Echo’s awards.

Mr Lees said he was “made up” with the nomination.

He said: “I’m made up to be nominated again for this award.

“We clearly are doing something right and it seems to be so popular despite little advertising. We don’t promote on social media, people just come through word of mouth.

“There is nothing like it in the area.”

Mr Lees took over the bakery from former owners Colin Jepson and Paul Hope in March 2024, after deciding to continue the established business when they stepped down.

A builder by trade, he said he had wanted to maintain the bakery’s place in the community and preserve the recipes which had made it a local favourite.

The bakery sells a selection of cakes, traybakes, pastries, pies, quiches, pasties and scones.
