**About Corner Grill House**

Corner Grill House is an independent steak restaurant in the heart of York, where great food, quality ingredients and genuine Yorkshire hospitality come together.

We’re passionate about serving the best ingredients we can find, with a particular focus on quality Yorkshire meat, carefully prepared and cooked to let the ingredients speak for themselves.

But for us, it’s not just about the food. We want every guest who walks through our doors to feel welcome, enjoy themselves and leave with a reason to come back.

By dining with us, you’re supporting an independent York restaurant and becoming part of our story.

**Sunday Roast at Corner Grill House**

Our Sunday Sharing Roast is served every Sunday from 12pm–6pm, subject to availability.

Expect quality Yorkshire beef and pork, crispy roast potatoes, fluffy Yorkshire puddings, pigs in blankets, seasonal vegetables and, of course, plenty of homemade gravy with unlimited refills.

It’s a proper Sunday roast, made for sharing and best enjoyed with good company — and a couple of glasses of Prosecco.
