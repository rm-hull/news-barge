A new beauty training centre and upgraded lecture theatre have been completed following a significant refurbishment at York College.

The projects were completed ahead of the new academic year and were delivered by York-based Ben Johnson Interiors in collaboration with architecture firm Bowman Riley.

The improvements aim to enhance learning environments and support student success through modern, purpose-built facilities.

Ken Merry, principal and chief executive of York College, said: "Our hair, beauty and make-up courses are hugely popular and this development now gives us one of the best teaching facilities in the region, so it will make a massive difference to our students and staff.

"Modern lecture theatres are integral to the success of both our students and York College as a whole.

"This work has given us a best-in-class facility, and there’s no doubt it’s going to be a valuable asset for the hundreds of people who will use it every day."

The first project involved converting a disused office space into a professional beauty training centre.

The refurbishment included new flooring, ceilings, partition walls, kitchen units, and full redecoration.

Specialist beauty equipment and furnishings were also installed, and was completed in just three weeks.

The second project focused on the first-floor lecture theatre, which had not been refurbished since the campus opened in 2007.

The five-week refurbishment included removing and reupholstering 90 seats, installing new lighting, and fitting acoustic wall panels.

The theatre also received new flooring, a black ceiling, a modern lectern, and contemporary furniture.

Emma Dodsley of Ben Johnson Interiors said: "We have a wealth of experience in the education sector, so it’s been a genuine privilege to support York College on these two projects in our home city.

"They also demonstrate our ability to work in live environments, while managing the programme challenges that inevitably arise on projects of this nature, without compromising on quality.

"Both completed spaces now offer inspiring and visually appealing learning environments that will support and enhance the high standard of teaching and training that York College is renowned for."

Antony Wright of Bowman Riley praised the teamwork that made the upgrades possible.

He said: "These projects were completed within very tight timeframes, with multiple contractors working simultaneously across various locations, which required careful planning and coordination.

"After working on several other successful projects with York College in recent years, as well as numerous other schools and colleges, we’ve become adept at meeting the education sector’s tight summer deadlines.

"Both projects have completely refreshed and transformed two tired spaces and will provide York College with improved facilities for many years to come, so we’re very pleased to have played a leading role in their delivery."
