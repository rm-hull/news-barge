A care home has taken residents on a round-the-world adventure without leaving their doorstep.

Appletree Grange Care Home in Chester-le-Street, Birtley, hosted a "Holiday in the Home" themed event, transforming rooms into vibrant destinations including Mexico, Tunisia, Greece, Italy, France, Australia, and Cornwall.

Residents, relatives, and staff dressed in themed outfits and explored decorated stations featuring various local food from the countries.

The day ended with a performance from Alex Hall Music inspired by residents reminiscing about holidays they have been on.

The event proved a hit with everyone who took part, with one resident of the home saying: "I loved visiting and learning about so many countries today in the home.

"I loved the food from other countries also, and the musician that came to perform was absolutely wonderful."

The event was designed to spark conversation and reminiscence, something staff say it achieved.

The general manager at Appletree Grange said: "This has been a huge amount of fun and sparked a lot of memories for residents to talk about."

Appletree Grange Care Home is part of Barchester Healthcare, which provides residential and dementia care for 32 residents, from respite care to long-term stays.
