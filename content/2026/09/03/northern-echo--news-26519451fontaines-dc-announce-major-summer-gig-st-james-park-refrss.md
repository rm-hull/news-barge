Irish rock band Fontaines DC are set to play a huge summer gig at St James’ Park.

The BRIT award winners have announced that they will be taking to the stage at the Newcastle stadium on Thursday, July 1, next year.

It marks the band’s first-ever stadium performance in the North East - and follows their headline set at Exhibition Park last summer.

Speculation had been rife last month that the band were due a trip to Newcastle after a music ‘insider’ leaked they would be coming to St James’ Park.

And that leak turned out to be correct as tickets are set to go on sale in a matter of days.

The group, which were shortlisted for the Mercury Prize in Newcastle last year, have built a reputation as one of the most compelling live acts of their generation.

They have headlined a growing list of iconic venues across Europe and North America and will now return to Tyneside as part of a five-date run of outdoor shows.

  Fontaines D.C. headlining The LCR, UEA in Norwich on 24th February 2020. Picture: Steve Hunt 

*(Image: Steve Hunt)*

Fontaines has already sold out their entire UK arena tour, with outdoor shows kicking off in Cardiff next summer before dates in London, Manchester, and Glasgow.

Brad Miller, chief operating officer at Newcastle United said: “St. James’ Park has a proud history of hosting world-class events - from major music acts to international sporting occasions, such as the upcoming EUROs in 2028.

“Welcoming one of the world’s biggest rock bands next summer reinforces our status as a premier destination for major events in the UK.

“Fontaines D.C.’s first stadium performance in the North East promises to be an unforgettable night, uniting the city’s passion for live music with the famed atmosphere of St. James’ Park.”

Newcastle United has secured an exclusive early access allocation of tickets for Mags members and season ticket holders, available via pre-sale from 10am on Tuesday (September 8).

All remaining tickets will go on general sale from 10am on Thursday (September 10) via Fontaines' official website.

You can also register your interest for hospitality packages with Newcastle United, which go on sale from next Thursday.
