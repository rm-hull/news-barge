A WELL-KNOWN fish and chip shop in Bradford city centre has been sold.

Sunbridge Friary has been snapped up after its leasehold went on the market.

Ernest Wilson estate agents confirmed the chippy had been bought and that the new owner is currently getting the business ready to open.

## What will it be called?

Signs in the window say "City Fisheries - Opening soon".

At this stage, no reopening date has been set.

## Chippy was put off for sale

Laura Hollingsworth and Kieran Osborne previously owned the chippy, opening it last November after extensively refurbishing the former In Plaice site.

The Sunbridge Friary had been put up for sale and its listed guide price was £29,950.

When the shop was listed on Rightmove, it was described as being in a "bustling location" with a "strong footfall" throughout the year.

The Rightmove page said that the shop was up for sale due to the owners' commitments to other business interests in Sheffield, rather than any issues with performance.

The Rightmove sale page said the shop had a weekly turnover of around £6,000.

It reported that the summer months saw a higher turnover due to its location, with an expected increase to between £7,000 and £8,000 per week.

## Owners had it for less than a year

When Ms Hollingsworth and Mr Osborne bought the shop, they told the T&A about their plans for the business.

It was their “first dive into the fish and chip market”.

At the time of opening, Ms Hollingsworth told the T&A: “We’re very excited. We’ve had a full renovation.

“We run two cafes and an artisan pizza shop in Sheffield.

“When we considered this fish and chip restaurant, we thought ‘Why not’?"

“The location is just perfect.

“It’s all very traditional.

“And there’s always something going on in the city centre.

“There’s a lot of history to this building."

A former manager of Sunbridge Friary also spoke about the building and its history as a chippy.

He said: “The exterior of this building is beautiful.

“It’s quite a historic building, and there’s been a fish and chip shop here for a long time."
