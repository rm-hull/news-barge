POLICE have launched an appeal to find a wanted man who has links to Bradford.

Blake Turner, 29, was convicted of grievous bodily harm (GBH) and is wanted for breaching the terms of his licence.

Leamington Police said he has connections to Bradford, as well as Nuneaton and Leamington.

A spokesperson for Leamington Police said: "The 29-year-old has breached the terms of his licence and is wanted on recall to prison having been convicted of GBH.

"He is 5ft 6in tall and has connections to Nuneaton and Leamington as well as Bradford in West Yorkshire.

"If you see him, please do not approach him and instead call 999 immediately."
