Police are appealing for help to identify a man in connection with an assault in Bradford.

West Yorkshire Police have released a picture of an individual following an incident in the Bradford South area on August 22.

The man is believed to be either a potential suspect or a witness.

A police spokesperson said: "Members of the public should not approach anyone who they believe to be displayed in the image but should contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111."

Anyone with information is asked to quote the reference number BD9088 when contacting police.

Information can be submitted via an online form at westyorkshire.police.uk/do-you-recognise/recognise-someone
