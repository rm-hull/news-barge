A HISTORIC Yorkshire golf club has been acquired by the UK’s largest owner and operator of country clubs.

Moor Allerton Golf Club now joins The Club Company’s growing portfolio of venues across England, marking a significant step in the company’s expansion plans.

Founded in 1923 and set within 220 acres of countryside, Moor Allerton features a 27-hole course designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr and has long been regarded as one of Yorkshire’s leading golf destinations.

Richard Calvert, chief executive officer of The Club Company, said: "Moor Allerton has a proud heritage, a loyal membership and a reputation that has been built over generations.

"We see ourselves not simply as owners, but as custodians, with a responsibility to protect the heritage, traditions and values that have shaped the club for more than a century.

"Our approach has always been simple: respect a club's heritage, plan for the long term and continually enhance the experience for members and visitors alike.

"Great clubs never stand still, and our ambition is to build on the exceptional foundations that already exist at Moor Allerton, drawing on our experience of operating leading country clubs and an unwavering focus on delivering an outstanding member experience, whilst remaining true to the character and values that define the club today."

The Club Company will work closely with Moor Allerton’s members, employees and the wider golfing community as it begins the next chapter for the club.

Ian Land, a spokesperson for Moor Allerton Golf Club, said: “Throughout this process, it became clear that The Club Company shares our belief that great clubs should continue to evolve whilst remaining true to the heritage and values that make them unique.

"Their proven track record of investing in well-established country clubs, enhancing the customer and member experience, and operating successful country clubs gave us complete confidence that they are the right organisation to build on everything that has made Moor Allerton so special."

The Club Company currently operates 18 country clubs across England and aims to be the UK's premier collection of country clubs.

The business focuses on acquiring venues with heritage and long-term growth potential, investing in facilities, and enhancing experiences for both members and visitors.

Moor Allerton Golf Club moved to its current location in 1970 and has built a reputation for high-quality golf, a welcoming community, and a rich sporting legacy.
