A car burst into flames on a York street last night (Wednesday).

The blue BMW was captured on fire on **Navigation Road**.

Fire crews were called to the scene at **5.27pm**.

**Video**

The area around the car was cordoned off by police while firefighters tackled the blaze and investigations took place.

Residents reported hearing a bang before seeing the fire.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Both York and Acomb’s crews responded to reports of a car fire.

“Crews extinguished the fire using three hose reel jets, a breathing apparatus for comfort wear and a thermal imaging camera to check for any hotspots.

“The incident was left in the hands of police.”
