A magical day trip to Lapland from a North East airport has proved so popular that an extra festive-season flight has been added.

Travel company Transun has added a direct flight from Teesside Airport to Swedish Lapland on Sunday, December 13, after huge demand.

Passengers will fly from Teesside Airport to the Arctic for a Christmas-themed day trip before returning home that evening.

Visitors can enjoy a reindeer sleigh ride, husky sledding and a snowmobile trip through the snowy Lapland landscape.

The trip also includes a private meeting with Santa, giving families the opportunity to meet Father Christmas in his Lapland home ahead of the festive season.

Transun said it added the date after incredible demand from locals looking for a memorable Christmas experience closer to home.

The direct route means travellers can swap long journeys to larger airports for a convenient departure from Teesside.

Seats are now on sale for the new flight, but organisers are urging families to book quickly, as places are limited.

The additional flight is scheduled to depart Teesside Airport at 7am on Sunday, December 13, before returning at around 8.30pm the same evening.

The Saturday, December 12 departure from Teesside Airport has already sold out, although places are still available for the trip to Finnish Lapland on Thursday, December 17.
