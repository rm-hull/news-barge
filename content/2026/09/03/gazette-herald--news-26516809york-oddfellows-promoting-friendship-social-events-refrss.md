An organisation with historical ties to pub culture is promoting friendship this month.

The Oddfellows, active since 1810, is known for its wide range of social events and welfare services.

These include film clubs, nature walks, coffee mornings, fitness classes, and craft groups.

The York group, led by the Social Organiser for the York area, is focusing on fostering connections throughout September.

The organisation's origins relate to pubs named after it, as founding members used to meet in such public spaces.

The Oddfellows' reach extends beyond social activities, providing support to the Royal National Lifeboat Institution (RNLI), donating to those in need, and assist various charities.

Members are regularly contacted by the welfare team, ensuring a network of care and communication.

Alongside the York group, social organisers in Scarborough and Goole offer a variety of activities for members.

A song about the Oddfellows is available on YouTube under Nette-Jay.
