A lifelong cycling advocate in Driffield has been honoured with a national lifetime achievement award.

Julie Turner, 67, received the David Dansky Lifetime Achievement Award from the Bikeability Trust for her 28 years of service promoting road safety and cycling in the East Riding.

The award recognises her work with East Riding of Yorkshire Council’s road safety team, where she has helped more than 15,000 children and adults learn to cycle safely.

Ms Turner said: "Cycling has shaped such a huge part of my life, and I feel incredibly fortunate that it has become both my career and my main form of volunteering.

"What I’m proudest of is helping people who never thought cycling was 'for them.'

"Watching confidence grow, friendships form, and people discover what they’re capable of is incredibly rewarding."

For the past 25 years, she has delivered Bikeability—the modern version of cycling proficiency training—in schools across the East Riding.

Councillor Paul West, the council’s cabinet member for environment and transport, said: "She is an extremely worthy winner of this award, and we are lucky to have her on our road safety team.

"Julie has undoubtedly increased the number of riders in the East Riding and couldn't have done more to promote the positives of cycling."
