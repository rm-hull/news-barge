THE date for Bradford's Christmas lights switch-on has been revealed.

For years, people have flocked to The Broadway to see the festivities get underway - six weeks before the big day arrives.

The likes of Eternal, B*witched and Liberty X have all performed before pressing that magic button.

## When are Bradford's Christmas lights being switched on this year?

This year, Bradford’s Christmas lights will be turned on at The Broadway on Saturday, November 14.

The date was confirmed by organisers, who said the shopping centre’s annual Festive Launch would take place that day.

Further details, including this year’s headline performers and entertainment schedule, will be announced in the coming weeks.

The event is expected to attract thousands of visitors to Bradford city centre as the countdown to Christmas begins.

It traditionally includes live entertainment, family activities and seasonal attractions before the city’s Christmas lights are illuminated.

*(Image: Newsquest)*

The launch also marks the beginning of the Christmas shopping season at The Broadway.

Retailers and restaurants will be preparing for one of the busiest periods of the year.


## A look at previous years

Last year, 90s R&B group Eternal headlined the event.

The London group formed in 1992, with the original line-up consisting of sisters Easther and Vernie Bennett, Kéllé Bryan and Louise Redknapp.

The group were an international success with hits such as "Stay" and "I Wanna Be the Only One".

Visitors also saw performances from Robbie Williams tribute act Rob-B-Williams, saxophonist Ellie Sax and bhangra group Punjabi Roots.

  Eternal performed at last year's Festival Launch 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

## B*witched switched the lights on in 2023

In 2023, Irish girl group B*witched headlined the festive celebrations, performing some of their biggest hits.

The group, which rose to fame in the 1990s, consists of twin sisters Edele and Keavy Lynch, Lindsay Armaou and Sinéad O’Carroll.

## What the general manager had to say

The switch-on has become one of Bradford’s most popular festive events, attracting crowds from across the district and beyond.

The Broadway’s General Manager Ian Ward said the Christmas launch had brought the community together for the past several years.

Further announcements are expected in the coming weeks.
