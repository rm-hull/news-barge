New York hard rock band Spread Eagle will perform in Harrogate as part of its latest UK tour.

The group will bring The Brutal Divine Tour to the den on Saturday 3rd October.

Spread Eagle formed in New York City more than three decades ago and made its name with its self-titled debut album and the song ‘Switchblade Serenade’.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a9971aa84aab7b4d2b.jpg)


Known for its raw “streetmetal” sound, the band went on to release Open to the Public and Subway to the Stars.

Its fourth full-length album, The Brutal Divine, has now inspired its latest tour.

Spread Eagle will be joined in Harrogate by female-fronted London band Star Circus, which will bring its glam-influenced hard rock to the venue.

Doors will open at 7pm, with the den remaining open until 1am.
