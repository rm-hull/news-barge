A Washington-based sales and lettings business is giving aspiring investors an opportunity to tour properties across the North East and learn directly from experienced professionals.

Nicky Middleton, based in Washington, is the owner and director of Oasis Sales and Lettings and The One Stop Property Shop North East. She has almost 40 years of experience in the housing sector helping people navigate the North East property market.

She said: “We’re passionate about the North East and what it offers investors.”

She began her career as a Youth Training Scheme (YTS) trainee with the local council and has since worked across the public and private sectors.

Ms Middleton said: “I’ve seen just about everything the housing sector can throw at you.

“From helping vulnerable families and dealing with anti-social behaviour to supporting people through some of the most difficult times in their lives.

“Those experiences taught me that housing is about people first and property second.”

Her business also offers property sourcing, investment advice, refurbishment project management, and long-term property management.

She said: “If I’m going to manage a property, I need confidence in it from day one.”

That approach has helped the business develop a loyal client base, with many investors returning to build larger property portfolios across the region.

Based in Washington, Nicky continues to personally oversee much of the day-to-day operation of the business.
