A MAIN road connecting two North Yorkshire villages is closed whilst a cattle grid is replaced.

North Yorkshire Council said Heygate Bank between Rosedale Abbey and Egton Bridge is shut until Monday, September 21.

  



Pedestrian access will still be possible, with a signed diversion route in place, the council said.

A North Yorkshire Council spokesperson said: "Our on-site teams will manage access to properties and businesses within the closure.

"However, due to the nature of the works and physical constraints of the site, there may be periods when vehicle access is not possible."

The council thanked the public for their patience during the essential works.
