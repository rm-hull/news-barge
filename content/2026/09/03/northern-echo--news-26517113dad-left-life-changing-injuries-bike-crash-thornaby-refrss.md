A father of seven remains in hospital with "life changing injuries" seven months after he was involved in a crash with two teenage riders on off road bikes.

The collision happened on Thorntree Road in Thornaby on January 31.

The 46-year-old, who has a wife and seven children, can no longer live at home with his family and remains in hospital, where he requires around the clock care.

Two 16-year-old boys, who cannot be identified for legal reasons, were both 15 years old when they drove the illegal bikes in the Thornaby town centre area.

As they made their way towards Thorntree Road, the victim was on a pedal bike when he was hit by one of them and was left with multiple serious injuries.

The rider of the illegal bike was arrested and later charged with causing serious injury by dangerous driving and driving without a licence or insurance.

The second rider removed the illegal bikes from the scene, and was charged with perverting the course of justice, possession of a class B drug, driving without a licence or insurance and driving dangerously.

Both pleaded guilty to the offences and were sentenced on Wednesday (September 2).

Both were disqualified from driving and received a Youth Offending Team referral. One must also pay £495 in compensation.

  Officer in the case, Detective Constable Nichola Lamport, from Cleveland Police’s Collision Investigation Unit, said: “The actions of these two young boys have left a father-of-seven hospitalised, unable to be at home with his family, and in need of around-the-clock care.

  

  “We presented a thorough and robust investigation to the courts, working closely with the family of the victim, and we are all disappointed with today’s sentence."
