Good news for York drivers – the inner ring road is fully open.

Work to fix the Fishergate gyratory has been completed and the roadworks are finished.

It comes more than three days after a section of Tower Street was closed due to storm damage on Sunday.

Water forced its way up through the drains during a torrential downpour, buckling the road at the pedestrian crossing near the junction with Fishergate and Paragon Street.

On Monday, City of York Council said the road would have to be closed for a few days “while we work with Yorkshire Water to understand the cause, then get it fixed”.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/york-storm-damage-tower-street-diversion-31-aug-2026-ben-thorpe-5.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/traffic-queuing-walmgate-york-roadworks-1-sept-2026.webp)

It added yesterday (Wednesday): “Once the new surface has set and the surface is safe to drive on, the road will re-open to all traffic.

“We’re hoping that this will be by late tonight (Wednesday). We know it’s been a tough few days on the network with delays across the city, so thank you to everyone for your patience while a solution was put in place.”

The closure caused long delays as road users were diverted along Piccadilly and through Walmgate.

So the reopening will be a major relief to all.
