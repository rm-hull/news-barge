Emergency services have been called to a house fire in a North Yorkshire village.

Two fire crews were on the scene in Appleton le Street, near Malton, shortly after 11.30pm on Wednesday (September 2).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the fire was out when its crews arrived but there was “minor smoke logging the ground floor”.

“Crews naturally ventilated the property by opening doors and windows,” a service spokesperson said.

They added that “advice was given to the occupier”.
