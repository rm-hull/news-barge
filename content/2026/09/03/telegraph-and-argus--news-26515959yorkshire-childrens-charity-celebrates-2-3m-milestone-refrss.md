A CHILDREN'S charity has reached a major milestone thanks to years of community support.

The Principle Trust Children’s Charity has now raised £2.3 million and supported more than 6,300 children over 15 years, helping young people across Yorkshire through respite breaks.

Founded in 2011 by Mike Davies MBE, the charity provides time away for children facing serious challenges, including mental health difficulties, trauma, abuse, poverty, illness, and disability.

Mike Davies MBE, founder of The Principle Trust Children’s Charity, said: "We have such a strong community around us and their support has been instrumental in helping us grow and deliver our mission over the past 15 years.

"Every act of support, no matter how big or small, helps us make a real difference."

The Principle Trust marked International Day of Charity on September 5 by celebrating the supporters who have helped make these memories possible.

Mr Davies said: "Our 15th anniversary is a celebration not just of what The Principle Trust has achieved, but of every person, business, and organisation that has stood behind us.

"What we have learnt since starting is that to make lasting memories it really does take a community and we are incredibly grateful for everyone who has helped make that possible."
