A major indie rock band is heading to Teesside next month, selling out both shows in a matter of hours.

The Courteeners will play two shows at Middlesbrough Empire on Thursday, October 29, to promote their new album, God Bless The Band.

The Manchester group, fronted by Liam Fray, originally announced a single show at 5pm, but soon added an additional performance at 8pm due to high demand.

Both shows sold out within hours of going on sale at 10am on Thursday, September 3.

The shows are being staged in partnership with Jacaranda Records, following the release of their latest album on August 28.

Formed in 2006, The Courteeners have built a huge following with hits including Not Nineteen Forever, What Took You So Long? and Are You In Love With A Notion?

The Middlesbrough shows are among the Empire's biggest bookings in recent months.
