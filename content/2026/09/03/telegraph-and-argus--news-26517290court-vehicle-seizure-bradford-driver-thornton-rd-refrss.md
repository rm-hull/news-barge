A driver has been reported to court and had their vehicle seized after officers stopped a car on a Bradford road and discovered it was being driven without insurance.

The incident involved a Toyota that was brought to a halt on Thornton Road by officers from the Bradford West neighbourhood policing team.

## 
  **Driver Tried to Shake Off Officers**

Police said the motorist attempted to throw officers off their trail by making a series of turns during the journey.

However, officers were able to keep track of the vehicle before eventually stopping it on Thornton Road.

## 
  **Car Found to Have No Insurance**

Checks carried out by officers revealed the vehicle was not insured.

Driving without insurance is an offence under UK law and can result in a vehicle being seized, penalty points and a fine.

The Toyota was subsequently seized by police.

## 
  **Court Report Issued**

The driver was reported to court in connection with the offence.

West Yorkshire Police highlighted the incident as part of ongoing work to improve road safety across the district and tackle uninsured driving.

The force regularly carries out proactive patrols aimed at identifying motorists who are driving illegally and reducing the risks posed to other road users.
