A popular bonfire night event has added a second date after selling out in previous years.

Big Blaze, held at Lintz Hall Farm in Burnopfield, will take place on Thursday, November 5, and due to high demand, an extra date has been added for Saturday, November 7.

Organisers have promised a packed evening of entertainment, including a spectacular fireworks display by Reaction Fireworks, the team behind Newcastle’s New Year’s Eve displays.

Christiano Crawford, co-director of the event, said: "We’ve really turned up the heat this year and added another date to make sure everyone has the opportunity to come along.

"We know how popular Big Blaze is for families to mark Bonfire Night in real style and we can’t wait for them to come along and enjoy all of the attractions and entertainment we have this time round."

Visitors can enjoy indoor stage shows, fairground rides, a photo booth, and dedicated Instagram photo areas.

There will also be an array of street food, including Greek dishes, pizzas, burgers, and doughnuts, alongside multiple bars.

The main event will be the lighting of a large bonfire, followed by a fire and angle grinding show by award-winning performers La Flamo.

Tickets start from £8 and are available at www.psycho-path.co.uk/big-blaze.
