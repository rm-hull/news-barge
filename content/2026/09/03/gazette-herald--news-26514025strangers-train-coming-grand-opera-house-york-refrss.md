A chilling tale of crime, obsession, and psychological suspense will take centre stage in York next year.

The national tour of Strangers on a Train, Craig Warner’s stage adaptation of Patricia Highsmith’s classic novel, will arrive at the Grand Opera House in York from February 2 to 6, 2027.

David Gilbery, CEO of Bill Kenwright Ltd, said: "We’re delighted to be working with the brilliant Roxana Silbert to bring Craig Warner’s gripping adaptation of Patricia Highsmith’s celebrated novel to audiences across the UK.

"Few thrillers have stood the test of time quite like Strangers on a Train."

The play tells the story of two strangers who meet on a train and become entangled in a dark plot involving two murders, manipulation, and mounting fear.

Mr Gilbery said: "We dedicate this production of Strangers on a Train to the memory of Craig Warner, who sadly passed away earlier this year.

"I had been looking forward to sharing this journey with him and greatly treasured the conversations we had about the production and his exceptional adaptation of Patricia Highsmith's novel.

"Craig's writing is elegant, gripping, and profoundly theatrical, and it has been a privilege to revisit his work in preparing this production."
