## Mike Dean admits to playing secret games during matches

Mike Dean  (Adam Davy/PA) *(Image: PA)*

Former Premier League referee Mike Dean has revealed he occasionally set himself unofficial challenges during top-flight matches, including seeing how long he could go without blowing the whistle or leaving the centre circle.

The comments have drawn criticism from some supporters, who questioned the professionalism of the approach.

Dean retired from on-field refereeing in 2022 before stepping down from his role as a video assistant referee the following year, bringing to an end a 22-year career in which he officiated 553 Premier League matches.

## What has he said?

His latest remarks, however, have reignited debate about refereeing standards.

One of the most recognisable officials in English football, Dean was often a divisive figure, attracting both praise and criticism during his time in the game.

  Speaking on former FC Halifax Town striker *Jamie Vardy's Having a Party* podcast, he recalled how referees would jokingly challenge themselves to see how long they could allow play to continue without blowing the whistle.

  Mike Dean on the Jamie Vardy's Having A Party podcast 

*(Image: Jamie Vardy's Having A Party YouTube)*

Dean claimed there were occasions when he went between 20 and 25 minutes without stopping play.


He also described a personal challenge of remaining within the centre circle for as long as possible during a match, saying he once managed around 15 minutes without stepping outside it.

The comments prompted a strong reaction on social media, with some supporters arguing that referees should focus solely on officiating matches.

Critics described the revelations as unprofessional and said they risk undermining confidence in match officials, while others questioned whether such an approach was appropriate at the highest level of English football.
