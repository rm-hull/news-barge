THE Prime Minister of Uganda will visit Bradford this September for a major international event.

Robinah Nabbanja will headline the inaugural Uganda–UK Business and Community Partnerships for Humanity Dialogue Meeting at the Midland Hotel on September 9.

The event will run from 10am to 5pm.

Organised by the Uganda High Commission in partnership with the Uganda Europe United Kingdom Association (UEUKA) and the Uganda Red Cross Society, the event will focus on the theme "Strengthening Partnerships for Shared Value and Community Impact."

It aims to bring together leaders from government, business, healthcare, academia, and the Ugandan diaspora to explore collaboration and investment opportunities.

Manoj Joshi, president of UEUKA, said: "Together, Bradford and Uganda can build partnerships that create shared prosperity, strengthen communities and save lives."

Other distinguished guests will include Nimisha Madhvani, Uganda's high commissioner to the UK, Bradford’s lord mayor Chris Herd, and Stephen Place, leader of Bradford Council.

The event builds on the Uganda–Bradford Business Investment Promotional Event held in April 2025 and aims to deepen ties between the two regions.

Uganda Airlines is set to present on the role of air connectivity in boosting trade, tourism, and investment between Uganda and the UK.

A fundraising initiative will also support the Uganda Red Cross Society’s humanitarian programmes.
