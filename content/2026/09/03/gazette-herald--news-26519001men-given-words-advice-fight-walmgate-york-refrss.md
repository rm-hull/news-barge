Two men have been given “words of advice” after a fight in York, police said.

Officers swooped in Walmgate at about 6.55am on Thursday morning (September 3) after the altercation between the two men.

North Yorkshire Police said no one was injured after the fight.

Two police vans were on the scene in Walmgate and had left by 7.20am on Thursday.

One man was seen being detained by police in Hurst's Yard, near The Press office, at around 7am.


A man working at a hotel in Walmgate said he had gone outside for a smoke shortly before 7am and "one guy was on top of the other", before police arrived.

Another eyewitness saw a man appearing to flee down Walmgate to Hurst's Yard followed by the other man.

They said police then detained one man in Hurst's Yard and spoke to the other in Walmgate.

A police spokesperson said its officers were called to the “disturbance” in Walmgate, adding: “Two men were involved in a verbal argument followed by a physical altercation. No injuries were reported.
