HUNDREDS of pounds worth of clothing have been stolen from a shop in York city centre.

North Yorkshire Police has released a CCTV image of a man it wants to speak to following a theft from a popular clothing shop in York.

The theft took place at Zara on Spurriergate on July 31, with £434.82 worth of stock reported stolen.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured on CCTV as he may have information that will assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to email [Ben.Hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Ben.Hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk), call North Yorkshire Police on 101, or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Please quote reference 12260151763 when passing on information.
