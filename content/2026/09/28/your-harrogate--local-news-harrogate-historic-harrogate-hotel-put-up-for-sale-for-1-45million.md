A 19-bedroom hotel in Harrogate has been put on the market for £1.45million.

The freehold of Ascot House Hotel on King’s Road is being marketed by specialist property agent Christie & Co.

The hotel, which is currently not trading, includes 19 en-suite bedrooms, two function rooms and a restaurant with space for 35 diners.

The stone building also has a private car park and an outdoor terrace with seating.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6abab8308dd32babf2e.jpg)

Guest accommodation is spread across three floors, with reception, dining and function areas on the ground floor.

The sales listing states:

> “Ascot House Hotel historically had an excellent reputation as a hospitality business.”

Christie & Co says the restaurant and function rooms offer opportunities for additional income, while a buyer could also consider operating the property as an aparthotel or accommodation requiring fewer staff.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6abab8308e5b8dd028d.jpg)

Alternative uses, including residential conversion, could be explored subject to planning permission.

The property is close to Harrogate Convention Centre, with Harrogate Civic Centre immediately behind it.
