# Trinity Leeds sees big name shut at shopping centre

## It isn't all bad news though

Trinity Leeds has seen a big name shut at the shopping centre.

At Trinity Kitchen, the centre’s food court, we have seen Pho shut. It is not all bad news though for those who were a fan of their food.

Pho has opened a new Pho restaurant in Boar Lane. The new Pho has opened in the former unit of bar Tapped.

The chain specialises in Vietnamese street food. Pho first opened in Leeds in Trinity Kitchen back in October 2013 - it was a kiosk-style format rather than its standard sit-down restaurant offer.

Pho, in Boar Lane, launched on Friday, September 18. As always, we’ll keep you posted on the latest openings and closures in Leeds city centre.
