Miriam Margolyes is returning to Teesside for a no-holds-barred evening of stories, laughter and audience interaction.

The outspoken actress, known for her roles in Harry Potter, Romeo and Juliet, and Blackadder, will bring her trademark humour and candour to the stage as part of her latest UK tour.

Stockton Globe said: "We can't wait for Miriam Margolyes to return to Stockton this December.

"Join her in conversation for stories from the new book, naughty confessions, and a no-holds-barred Q&A where no question is off-limits.

"Following three bestselling memoirs and sold-out tours that left audiences in stitches, Britain's most outspoken national treasure is back on the road – and she's not holding back."

Promoters said audiences can expect memories and mammaries, Arsenal, politics, filth, and "absolutely everything in between."

The event promises to be an evening of unfiltered entertainment, with organisers describing it as engaging, unpredictable and thoroughly entertaining.

They said conversations could cover anything from her colourful life and career to her sharp take on modern life, but one thing is certain – "you never really know what you are going to get with her."

**Read More:**

The show is part of a wider UK tour running from September to December, including a stop at Sunderland Empire on Wednesday, September 30.

The show will take place at Stockton Globe on Tuesday, December 1, at 7.30 pm.

The event is recommended for ages 14 and above, subject to parental discretion.
