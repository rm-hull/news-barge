Bradford residents are being urged to keep an eye out for an "extra-special" rare 50p coin in their change.

The London 2012 Olympic swimming coin, described by the Royal Mint as "extra special," features a swimmer in action.

A limited number of the coins were released before a design change, and collectors believe there could be more in circulation.

A version of the 50p coin fetched £4,000 on eBay, 8,000 times its face value, when it sold last year, inspiring a mini bidding war which saw 28 bids placed.

![]() *(Image: Royal Mint)*

Describing the coin, the Royal Mint said: "The design was created by Jonathan Olliffe, and it depicts a swimmer in action with fluid lines struck into the metal representing the waves.

"In the rare first design, these lines covered the swimmer’s face. Soon after its release, the coin was tweaked to make the face more visible, but not before the first design made it out into the world.

"One of the most exciting aspects of this special 50p is that no one knows exactly how many entered circulation before the changes were made, only that numbers are extremely limited."

The coin attracted 28 bids before selling for £4,000, with collectors across the country now being encouraged to check their spare change.

## What makes a coin valuable?

The 50 pence piece has become the most valued and collected coin in the UK, with many collectable designs appearing on its heptagonal canvas.

Its 27.5mm diameter makes it the largest of any British coin, and allows space for decorative pictures. It has often been used to celebrate big events over the past 50 years of British history.

The rarest coins tend to be of the greatest value, with the mintage (number of coins with each design made) being the fundamental attraction for collectors.

Along with the design, other aspects of the coin which increase value are the condition of the coin and whether it has an error in its design.

The way in which it is sold can also determine the coin’s value - while some coin collectors will bid vast amounts of money on eBay or at auction, others opt for more robust valuations by selling via a coin dealer.
