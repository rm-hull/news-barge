# Watch dramatic moment police chase young driver through Leeds streets

## West Yorkshire Police warning after 23,000 killed and injured on roads

A young driver was arrested by police in Leeds following a lengthy pursuit.

The young driver failed to stop in East Leeds on Thursday night, September 24. Police shared footage of the pursuit and issued a stark warning.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesman said: "What started with an attempt to avoid being dealt with by police quickly escalated into dangerous and reckless driving on public roads.

"Thankfully, nobody was injured and a 20-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of driving a vehicle dangerously and document offences. He was bailed pending further enquiries.

"We’re highlighting the risks faced by young drivers and the consequences that dangerous decisions behind the wheel can have.

"The NPCC reports that more than 23,000 people have been killed or injured in collisions involving young drivers aged 17-24 in Great Britain from 2021 to 2025. If the police ask you to stop, stop."
