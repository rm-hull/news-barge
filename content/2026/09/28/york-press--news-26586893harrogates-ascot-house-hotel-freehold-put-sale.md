## Historic hotel goes to market in North Yorkshire town for £1.45m

The exterior of the Ascot House Hotel in Harrogate. *(Image: Christie & Co)>*

A highly regarded hotel has gone up for sale in a sought-after North Yorkshire town.

Ascot House Hotel in Harrogate, in King's Road, is being marketed as a rare freehold opportunity by property agents Christie & Co for £1,450,000.

The property features 19 en suite letting rooms, two function rooms, and a 35-cover restaurant.

The hotel, a former Victorian townhouse, is approximately 0.3 miles from Harrogate Convention Centre and 0.6 miles from the town’s railway and bus stations.

A spokesperson for Christies & Co said: "The opportunity to purchase a freehold hotel/property in the heart of Harrogate is a rare one and therefore we expect significant interest.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21509112.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of the Ascot House Hotel in Harrogate. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

"Ascot House Hotel historically had an excellent reputation as a hospitality business.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21509150.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The exterior of The Ascot Arms Hotel in Harrogate *(Image: Christie & Co)*

"The function areas and restaurant offer an opportunity to maximise additional income streams.

"The hotel has 19 bedrooms, a restaurant, function/meeting space and offers a lot more property/accommodation than the traditional B&Bs in the town."

The property is set over two levels on the ground floor, with a grand entrance hall leading to the reception area, breakfast room, and restaurant.

A hallway leads to the office and the function rooms.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21509123.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of the Ascot House Hotel in Harrogate. *(Image: Christie & Co)*

Guest accommodation is spread across the ground, first, and second floors, with rooms varying in size and style.

The hotel also benefits from a private car park at the front and side of the property, permit access parking on the road outside of the hotel, and a terrace area with seating.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21509124.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of the Ascot House Hotel in Harrogate. *(Image: Christie & Co)*

There are further options for the future of the site.

The spokesperson for Christies added: "There is also the opportunity for a new owner to convert the hotel into a more hands-off accommodation business, such as an aparthotel or limited staff offering.

"Subject to planning permission the property has alternative uses including residential."

Images used in marketing materials are for illustration purposes only, as the hotel is not currently operating, the agents added.
