TWO people arrested after a 28-year-old man's "sudden and unexplained" death in York have been released on bail.

North Yorkshire Police said paramedics found the man collapsed at an address in Wood Steet, Heworth at around 11pm on Friday, September 25.

He died in hospital during the early hours of September 26.




His family have been informed and are being supported, police said.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "On Sunday morning (September 27), officers arrested a 54-year-old man and a 36-year-old woman in connection with the investigation.

"They have now been released on bail.

"Witnesses or anyone with information about the incident are asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101.

"If you would prefer to remain anonymous, call Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or make an online report.

"Please quote reference number 12260189777 when providing details."
