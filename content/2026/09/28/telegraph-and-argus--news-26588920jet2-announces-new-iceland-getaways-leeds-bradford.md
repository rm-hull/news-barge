Jet2 has announced new flights to a "once-in-a-lifetime" destination.

Departing from Leeds Bradford Airport, the city breaks offer the chance to explore Reykjavik and Iceland’s dramatic natural wonders during key school holiday periods.

Flights will run on Mondays and Fridays from October 22 to November 8, 2027, February 18 to February 28, 2028, and April 7, 2027 to April 24, 2028.

Customers can choose between a flight-only option with Jet2.com or a full Jet2CityBreaks package, which includes flights, 22kg of hold luggage, and a selection of hotels in central Reykjavik.

Packages start with a low deposit of just £60 per person.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21512201.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Northern Lights in Reykjavik *(Image: submitted)*

Steve Heapy, CEO of Jet2, said: "Iceland continues to prove a huge hit with customers, thanks to its abundance of natural wonders, unique culture and incredible once-in-a-lifetime experiences.

"From chasing the Northern Lights and bathing in geothermal waters to exploring Reykjavik’s food, culture and striking scenery, it is easy to see why holidaymakers continue to seek out this unforgettable destination.

"Our Winter 27/28 programme to Iceland from Leeds Bradford Airport gives customers and independent travel agents fantastic choice and flexibility when it comes to experiencing everything Iceland has to offer.

"With services timed around key school holiday periods, coupled with the value and convenience of a Jet2CityBreaks package, we are confident this will be another hugely successful programme from Leeds Bradford Airport."

Travellers can add excursions such as the South Shore, Golden Circle, and the Blue Lagoon to their itinerary, offering a tailored Icelandic experience.

Reykjavik, Iceland’s capital, offers visitors a blend of culture, architecture, and nightlife, making it an ideal base for both city exploration and day trips into the surrounding wilderness.

Full details of the Iceland programme are available on the Jet2.com and Jet2CityBreaks.com websites.

Independent travel agents can find more information and booking options at trade.jet2holidays.com.
