A CCTV image has been released of a man police want to speak to following the theft of a bicycle in York city centre.

The incident happened almost a month ago, on August 31, at 8.20pm when a bike was stolen from Walmgate.

**Recommended reads:**

Police say they believe the person in the newly-released CCTV image "will have information that could help our investigation".

Anyone who can help with information, is asked to email [steven.holloway@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:steven.holloway@northyorkshire.police.uk) referencing 12260171219.
