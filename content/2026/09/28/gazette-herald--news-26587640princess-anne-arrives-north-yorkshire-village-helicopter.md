A MEMBER of the Royal family has arrived in a North Yorkshire village.

Princess Anne, the Princess Royal, landed by helicopter in Snainton.

It is understood the Princess is attending an event to present an award.

Her helicopter landed in Snainton's playing field at around 1.30pm.
