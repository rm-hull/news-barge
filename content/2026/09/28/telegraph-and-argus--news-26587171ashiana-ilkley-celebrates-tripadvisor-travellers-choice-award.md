AN Ilkley takeaway is celebrating after being named a TripAdvisor Travellers’ Choice Award 2026 winner - placing the business among the top 10 per cent worldwide.

The award is based on reviews and opinions from travellers around the world.

For owner and manager of Ashiana, located on Wells Road, Forhad Miah, the recognition is a proud moment for a small independent business that has built its reputation by putting customers first.

“To be recognised in the top 10 per cent of businesses worldwide by Tripadvisor is something we are incredibly proud of,” said Forhad.

“We are a small family-run business in Ilkley, so to receive recognition on a global scale is genuinely very special to us.

"Every single customer who has visited us, ordered from us, left a review or recommended us to friends and family has played a part in this achievement.”

![]()Ashiana owner Forhad Miah *(Image: submitted)*

Ashiana’s roots remain firmly in Ilkley, with local customers playing an important role in the business’s journey since it opened on Wells Road in May 2023.

Many have supported the takeaway, recommended it to friends and family and introduced visitors to the area to Ashiana.

For a local independent business, that word of mouth can make a huge difference. Customer reviews have also become an important part of Ashiana’s development, with the team using feedback, both positive and constructive, to continually improve.

The Tripadvisor award puts the Ilkley business on an international stage, with its certificate stating that reviews from millions of Tripadvisor travellers place Ashiana Indian Takeaway Ilkley in the top 10 per cent worldwide.

It is the latest achievement for Ashiana, which offers authentic and contemporary Bangladeshi cuisine.

The takeaway was named Takeaway of the Year 2024 at the national Curry Life Awards and Yorkshire Takeaway of the Year at the Yorkshire and North East Curry Awards 2025.

Forhad said the latest award reflects the hard work of the whole team, as well as the support Ashiana has received from its customers.

“This award belongs to our customers as much as it belongs to us. Without the people of Ilkley and everyone who has supported us over the years, we simply wouldn’t be here.

“Every review, every recommendation and every order means something to a small family business like ours. We genuinely cannot thank everyone enough.”

The team is now looking forward to building on the achievement and continuing to develop Ashiana Ilkley while maintaining the food, service and welcoming experience that customers have come to expect.
