An instance of suspected arson led crews to a long-derelict Bradford mill.

Crews were called to the Midlands Mills, on Valley Road, just after 4.30pm yesterday (Sunday).

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: “Two crews attended the small fire on the ground floor of the derelict mill.

"One hose reel jet was used to extinguish this.

"It's been recorded as suspected deliberate ignition."

### History of Midlands Mills

The site is a grade II-listed and was originally built in 1871.

It was home to the British Mohair Suppliers until 2001.
