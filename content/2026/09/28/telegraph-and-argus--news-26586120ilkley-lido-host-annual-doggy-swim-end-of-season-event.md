Dogs will take a dip at a popular outdoor pool during a special end-of-season event.

Ilkley Lido will host its annual Doggy Swim on October 10 and 11, giving four-legged friends the chance to splash around before the pool is drained for winter.

The event has become a firm favourite with pet owners and marks the close of the summer season at the historic lido, which first opened in 1935 and remains one of West Yorkshire’s best-loved outdoor attractions.

![]()The Lido is hugely popular in summer *(Image: Freelance)*

Dogs will be able to swim alongside their owners during the dedicated sessions.

Tickets are priced at £8.20 per dog, which includes entry for one owner.

![]()Details of the doggy day *(Image: Freelance)*

Additional adult tickets are £4.90, while junior tickets cost £3.30.

Sessions will run from 9am to 10.30am, 11am to 12.30pm, and 1pm to 2.30pm on both days.

All bookings must be made online in advance.
