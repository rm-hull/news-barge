# Exciting new Leeds city centre opening following big name retailer closure

## The new opening has come to Leeds following success in Leeds

An exciting new store has opened in Leeds city centre replacing a big name retailer.

We have seen Clintons in Commercial Street shut. The shop sold cards and gifts.

It has now been replaced by something rather unique. After being successful in York, we have seen Ghosted launch in Leeds city centre.

On the new shop’s TikTok account, the blurb states: “A shop full of glow in the dark ghosts. Designed by artist Xander Platz.”

Inside, you will find a selection of ghost-themed products. As always, we’ll bring you details of the latest openings and closures in Leeds.
