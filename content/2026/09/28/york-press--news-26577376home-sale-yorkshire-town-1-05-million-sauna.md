## Look inside £1m home for sale in Yorkshire town (with its own home cinema and sauna)

The five-bedroom house for sale in Algarth Road, Pocklington, for £1.05 million *(Image: RM English)*

A home has hit the market for £1.05 million in an East Yorkshire town – and it has its own home cinema and a sauna.

The five-bedroom house is for sale in Algarth Road, Pocklington.

RM English, which is marketing the property, said it is an "exceptional" home in the "most prestigious and sought-after address in Pocklington".

At the centre of the home is a modern kitchen with a large central island.

Four double bedrooms, two of which have en-suite facilities, are all located on the first floor.

![]()Inside the five-bedroom house for sale in Algarth Road, Pocklington, for £1.05 million *(Image: RM English)*

Standout features at the property include its sauna, included with the home’s gym.

There is also the cinema room with a 120-inch screen, 4k projector and audio system.

Outside, the home has a triple garage and is accessed via electric gates.

"Set well back from the road behind a generous front garden, the property enjoys an unusually peaceful setting with minimal passing traffic, creating a calm and private environment," a RM English spokesperson said.

"Yet, within just five minutes on foot, you can reach the heart of Pocklington, with its shops, cafés, and everyday amenities."
