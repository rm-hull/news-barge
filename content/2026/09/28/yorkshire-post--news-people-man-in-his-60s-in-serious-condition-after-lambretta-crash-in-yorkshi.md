# Man in his 60s in critical condition after Lambretta crash in Yorkshire

## A man in his 60s is in a critical condition in hospital after a crash involving two cars and a Lambretta scooter.

The 61-year-old man was riding the Lambretta scooter on Canal Road in Bradford - near to the ACW Garden Centre - when it was involved in a crash with a black Volvo and a VW Golf.

Police said the incident happened at around 9.40am on Saturday (Sep 26).

A statement from West Yorkshire Police said: “Officers were called to the collision involving a grey Volkswagen Golf, a Lambretta scooter and a black Volvo on Canal Road, near to the ACW Garden Centre.

“The rider of the scooter, a 61-year-old man, received serious injuries and was taken to hospital for treatment. He remains in a critical but stable condition.

“The driver of the Golf, a 24-year-old man, was interviewed under caution by officers, and enquiries are continuing.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact police on 101, quoting 13260548392.
