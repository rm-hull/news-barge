A NATIONAL treasure is set to bring his comedy show to a small East Yorkshire venue next month.

Alan Carr is set to perform a work-in-progress comedy show at Pocklington Arts Centre.

The award-winning comedian will appear next Sunday (October 11), offering fans a rare chance to see him test new material.

Kelly Munro-Fawcett, venue director, said: "We’re absolutely thrilled to welcome Alan Carr to Pocklington Arts Centre. He’s such a well-loved comedian, and it’s fantastic to have him performing here in Pocklington.

"It’s a brilliant opportunity for audiences to see him up close as he tries out his new material, and we’re really looking forward to what promises to be a very special evening of comedy."

Alan is best known for hosting the popular chat show Chatty Man, as well as winning popular BBC reality show Celebrity Traitors last year.

Tickets are priced at £18 and will go on sale at 10am on September 29 on pocklingtonartscentre.co.uk
