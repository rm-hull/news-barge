AN arrest was made after a man was injured during a street fight in Bradford city centre.

Police were called to Sackville Street at 7.02am on Sunday following reports of an ongoing altercation.

A man suffered non life threatening injuries.

A 24-year-old man was arrested and has since been bailed pending enquiries.

## Police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers were called at 7.02am on Sunday 27 September to reports of an ongoing fight.

"On arrival, officers located a man with non life-threatening injuries."

"A 24-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of affray and has since been bailed pending further enquiries.

"Enquiries remain ongoing by Bradford CID."

Anyone who witnessed the fight is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting log 339 of September 27.
