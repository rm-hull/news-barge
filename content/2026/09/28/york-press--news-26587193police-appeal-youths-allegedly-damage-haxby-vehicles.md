YOUTHS deliberately damaged a number of vehicles in a York suburb, police say.

Police are investigation the incident which occurred on Lowfield Drive, Haxby, on September 19, at about 9.30pm.

A spokesperson for the force said: "It’s reported that a group of youths passed through the street kicking out at vehicles at about 9.30pm, causing damage."

The authorities are now appealing for any witnesses or anyone with dashcam or CCTV footage of the incident to come forward.

The trouble follows recent reports of anti-social behaviour in Haxby, including reports that the windscreen of a blue Skoda was criminally damaged while it was parked in North Lane, Haxby, between Saturday (September 19) and the early hours of Sunday (September 20).

**Recommended reads:**

Earlier this month, Haxby Town Council urged locals residents to report incidents of anti-social behaviour directly to the police rather than posting or commenting on social media.

Information about the latest spate of incidents should initially be submitted online.

You can email PC Ben Robinson-Brockhill at [ben.robinson-brockhill@northyorkshirepolice.uk](mailto:ben.robinson-brockhill@northyorkshirepolice.uk), or use the 'Tell us about' and 'An existing case or report' options on the North Yorkshire Police website.

Live chat is also available on the website from Monday to Friday between 9am and 5pm.

Alternatively, information can be shared by calling 101.

For those wishing to remain anonymous, Crimestoppers can be contacted on 0800 555 111 or via their website.
