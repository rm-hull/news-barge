CIRCUS Mondao is returning to Silsden with a brand new show.

The traditional big top circus, based at Jacksons Event Field on Keighley Road, will run from September 30 to October 4.

A spokesperson for Circus Mondao said: "This year we are so pleased to return to Silsden with another great show.

"It's so nice to come back to a town where it's tradition for the families to come and see the circus every year, and we get to see those families grow up and even bring their own kids."

This year's show promises a packed line-up of international performers and classic circus acts.

Highlights include Russian swing, Icarian, the wheel of death, and the Russian bar.

Other acts feature glass balancing, chair balancing, fast-paced juggling from Brazil, and the return of Chile’s award-winning clown, Kikin.

There will also be showgirls, Miss Madalanes ponies, and Vader the black stallion.

The spokesperson added: "We keep to circus traditions, but also move with the times of amazing technical lighting effects, music, and production.

"This is a show not to be missed and not to be confused with others."
