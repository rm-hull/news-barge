Emmerdale and Coronation Street will be disrupted this week due to a football match airing on ITV.

The UEFA Nations League coverage will affect the broadcaster’s usual Tuesday evening (September 29) schedule.

Here's what we know about the TV schedule for soaps this week.

## Emmerdale and Coronation Street soap schedules to change this week

Emmerdale and Coronation Street will mostly follow their usual schedules this week, with one exception.

Scotland will face Switzerland in the UEFA Nations League from 7.30pm, with kick-off at 7.45pm.

These broadcast times mean the football match will cross over both slots for Coronation Street and Emmerdale.

There is currently no Sunday episode of either soap, meaning fans will miss one episode this week, at the time of writing.

## Emmerdale star Lisa Riley issues scam warning on GMB

Lisa Riley, known for playing Mandy Dingle in Emmerdale, has warned others about scams that can appear on websites, even when they look "flawless" and are believed to be real.

On Friday, September 25, Riley appeared on the ITV show, where she told viewers that she has been scammed three times despite being vigilant around scams and fraud.

She was scammed while renewing her ESTA and buying Stevie Wonder concert tickets.

When buying the tickets from a site she thought was real, £9,600 nearly left her bank account, but her bank asked for authorisation so she could stop the payment.

She also revealed her heartbreak after fake AI images showing her pregnant were circulated online alongside fake news stories, following four failed IVF attempts.

“Petrified” that her details are still out there, Riley hopes to help others avoid scams.

She said people should “automatically presume that it is going to be a scam” to stay alert.
