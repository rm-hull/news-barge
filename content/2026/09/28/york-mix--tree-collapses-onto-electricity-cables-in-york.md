Emergency services were called after a tree fell on electricity cables in York.

It happened on **Heslington Lane** at**6.45pm last night** (Sunday).

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The Tadcaster crew attended a tree which had collapsed onto electricity cables.

#### Heslington Lane, York

“Crews cordoned off the area and the electricity board was requested to attend.”
