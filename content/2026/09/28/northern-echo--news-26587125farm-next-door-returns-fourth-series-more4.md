Fans have been reacting to the announcement that the popular show Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids is returning for a fourth series.

The show that follows the Yorkshire Shepherdess and her nine children as they renovate their new family home, Anty John’s, will be coming back to More4.

Fans do not have to wait long to see more from Amanda, as the first episode will be airing on Monday, October 5.

## Fans react to the new series of Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids

Wise Owl Films made the announcement about the show returning via a post on Instagram featuring screenshots from the new series.

The caption read: “NEW SERIES KLAXON ALERT... Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids is back for a fourth series starting at 9pm on Monday October 5th on More4.

“Filmed across Winter 2025 and Spring 2026 the series continues to follow the Owens as the ambitious renovation of their new family home, Anty John’s, enters its third year.

“Meanwhile at Ravenseat we see the family welcome some unexpected new faces at Christmas; we’re there to see all hands on deck for a busy Easter lambing season; as Amanda continues to uncover the fascinating and moving history behind Anty John’s and the Clarkson family who once lived there; and we’re with Edith and her sisters as they prepare the cream tea van for its grand Summer opening.”

Fans took to the comments of the post to share their excitement about the “fantastic news”.

One person said: “Yesssssss, can't wait, love this programme.”

Another person said: “Great news, looking forward to it!”

A third person commented: “Fantastic news love them all such hard working family.”

Many fans “can’t wait” to catch up with the Owen family again and see how the work on Anty John’s progresses.
