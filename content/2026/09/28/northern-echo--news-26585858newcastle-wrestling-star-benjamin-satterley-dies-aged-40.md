Tributes have been paid to Newcastle-born wrestler Benjamin Satterley following his sudden death on Sunday, September 27, at the age of 40.

Satterley - known to wrestling fans around the world as Neville during his time in WWE and PAC in All Elite Wrestling (AEW) - was a familiar figure on the North East wrestling scene and earned a reputation as one of the region's most talented performers before going on to find international success.

AEW confirmed his death in a statement on Monday (September 28), offering its condolences to Satterley's family, friends and fans.

![]()Benjamin Satterley, also known as "Pac," passed away suddenly on Sunday, at the age of 40. *(Image: AEW)*

Born in Newcastle, Satterley began his professional wrestling career in 2004 and quickly established himself as a standout talent on the North East independent wrestling circuit.

He was particularly well known on the Teesside wrestling scene, where he became a familiar face to local fans long before his rise to international stardom.

One Teesside wrestling fan remembered Satterley on Facebook, writing: "For wrestling fans in Teesside and the wider North East, PAC may already have been a familiar face long before his international success.

"He was a regular with promotions such as Three Count Wrestling between 2005 and 2008, so if you attended local shows during that period, there's a good chance you saw him in action."

His high-flying style, athleticism and in-ring ability helped make him one of the UK's leading wrestling stars before he went on to compete on the global stage.

Satterley joined WWE in 2012, initially wrestling in its developmental system before making his name as Neville.

During his time with WWE, he won championship gold and became renowned for his spectacular aerial offence.

He continued wrestling after leaving the organisation in 2018, competing internationally before signing with AEW in 2019 as Pac.

There, Satterley became a multiple-time champion and competed in some of AEW's most memorable matches, earning the admiration of fans and fellow wrestlers alike for his exciting style, incredible athleticism and lasting impact on the sport.

Fans from across the wrestling world have since paid tribute to Satterley, remembering him as one of the North East's most gifted and successful wrestlers.

No cause of death has been announced.
