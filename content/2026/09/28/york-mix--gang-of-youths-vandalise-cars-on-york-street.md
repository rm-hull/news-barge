A gang of youths vandalised cars parked on a York street.

It happened on Lowfield Drive, Haxby, at about 9.30pm on Saturday, 19 September.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It’s reported that a group of youths passed through the street kicking out at vehicles causing damage.

#### Lowfield Drive, Haxby

“If anyone was in the area and saw anything suspicious or has evidential dashcam footage or CCTV, please contact us online in the first instance.”
