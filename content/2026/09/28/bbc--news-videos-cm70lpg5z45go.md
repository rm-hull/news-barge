# Women protest in the streets of Delhi after string of sexual assaults

Hundreds of women have come together in Delhi to protest against the alleged gang-rape of a 17-year-old near Delhi university's campus.

This is one of multiple protests that have been taking place in the capital over the past week, after a string of sexual assaults and rape cases have made headlines.

BBC correspondent Azadeh Moshiri has been speaking to women at one of the protests who have told her they simply haven't felt safe for years.
