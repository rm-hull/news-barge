A self-built six-bedroom home with its own gym and spa facilities has hit the market for £1.2 million.

Located on Conroy Close, Hartlepool, the detached property spans more than 4,600 square feet across three floors and features a gym, cinema room, sauna, and steam room.

It also includes an outdoor kitchen, swim spa, and a south-facing garden.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21512479.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Outdoor kitchen area *(Image: Collier Estates)*

Built on a 0.25-acre corner plot, the home also comes with a triple garage and a multi-car driveway.

The property features modern design elements, including a white rendered façade, Cedar panelling, and anthracite windows.

Inside, smart lighting and an integrated sound system are installed throughout the home.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21512472.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Collier Estates)*

The statement entrance includes a glazed doorway leading into a double-height hallway.

The space is illuminated by natural light and a cluster pendant light, with oversized pale grey tiled flooring and a bespoke floating oak staircase.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21512477.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Collier Estates)*

To the right of the hallway is a family living room with a media wall, recessed fire, and LED ceiling features.

On the left, a home office offers a quiet workspace with a full height portrait window.

A guest washroom is located at the rear of the staircase.

A Manhattan-style glazed wall separates the hallway from the open-plan family space at the rear.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21512471.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Collier Estates)*

The open-plan area includes a dining room, snug, and kitchen, all with bifold doors leading to the landscaped garden.

The kitchen is finished in a pale grey polished concrete style and features dual central islands, a breakfast bar, and integrated appliances.

A hidden utility room is accessed through a secret door within the kitchen cabinetry.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21512470.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Utility room *(Image: Collier Estates)*

Beyond the utility room is a second hallway, which leads to the triple garage, service entrance, and a staircase up to the cinema, bar, and games room.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21512468.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A triple garage fit for the family fleet *(Image: Collier Estates)*

The entertainment area, located above the garage, features a home bar, cinema screen, starlight ceiling, and LED lighting.

This space could also serve as guest accommodation or a sixth bedroom.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21512478.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Games room *(Image: Collier Estates)*

The first floor includes four large double bedrooms, two with en-suite bathrooms.

A central staircase leads to the top floor master suite.

The master suite includes a walk-in dressing room, en-suite bathroom, and Juliet balcony.

The en-suite features a freestanding slipper bath, wet-room shower, steam room, and sauna.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21512476.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The home's sauna *(Image: Collier Estates)*

The master bedroom is fully air-conditioned, and French doors open to a glazed Juliet balcony.

The landscaped rear garden includes a swim spa, lounge area, pond, and a Cedar-clad outbuilding housing a gym and sauna.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21512475.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A garden room currently functions as a family gym *(Image: Collier Estates)*

The triple garage is fitted with electric doors and hexagon LED lighting.
