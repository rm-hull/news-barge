One of the BBC's best-known political is returning to County Durham for the first time in four years.

Question Time is heading to Peterlee , giving residents the chance to put questions directly to politicians and commentators in front of a national television audience.

Fiona Bruce will host the show from the East Durham town on Thursday, October 8, with audience applications now open.

The visit marks the programme's first appearance in County Durham since a broadcast from Bishop Auckland in December 2022.

The panel included then Hexham MP Guy Opperman, Labour's Lucy Powell, SNP politician Stewart Hosie, journalist Isabel Oakeshott and fashion designer Patrick Grant.

![]()Question Time has recorded shows in Bishop Auckland in 2022 and 2018. *(Image: The Northern Echo)*

Panellists for the October programme are yet to be announced, but the show will be broadcast on BBC One at 9pm on October 8.

The long-running BBC One show tours the UK each week, inviting members of the public to raise issues and challenge leading figures from politics and public life.

Those wishing to be part of the audience can apply online through the BBC's application process.

Successful applicants will be notified on the Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday before the programme, and are selected to reflect a range of ages, backgrounds and political opinion, the BBC says.
