Police have confirmed that a missing Bradford teenager is believed to have travelled to London.

Mustafa, 16, from Manningham and Lower Grange, was last seen on Pancras Road at 12.05pm on Wednesday, September 23, after leaving King’s Cross station.

He had been spotted at Leeds train station earlier that morning.

He is described as a Middle Eastern male, around 5ft 6in tall, with curly brown hair and brown eyes.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "We’re continuing to appeal for information to locate a missing teenager from Bradford, who is now believed to have travelled to London.

![Mustafa has been reported missing](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21492006.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: WYP)*

"Officers are concerned for his welfare, and we would urge anyone who has seen him, not to approach him but report it to police immediately calling 999 quoting reference 241 of 23 September."

Mustafa was last seen wearing a light grey and black tracksuit, which is black below the knees and from the arms upwards, and grey in the middle.

He was also wearing black trainers and carrying a black Foot asylum sports bag.

![Mustafa has been reported missing](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21492005.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: WYP)*

He also has links to Birmingham.

Anyone with information should contact police on 101, quoting log 241 from September 23.

Information can also be provided online via the West Yorkshire Police live chat at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat.
