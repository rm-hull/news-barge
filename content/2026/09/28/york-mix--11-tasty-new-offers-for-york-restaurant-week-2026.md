York Restaurant Week is back! Here’s how to get some delicious deals.

York Restaurant Week returns for its autumn edition from Monday 5 to Sunday** 18 October**. Whether you fancy a quick bite, a long lazy lunch or a full-on feast, you’ll find tempting offers priced between £5 and £30.

We’ve put together a guide to the 11 venues taking part in the event for the first time – which includes Sloppy’s Bar & Kitchen, Giggling Squid, and Northern Monk Refectory.

All the offers are now live, priced at either £5, £10, £15, £20, £25 or £30. There is no limit to the amount of offers diners can access, and vouchers are completely free to download.

York Restaurant Week is organised by the York Business Improvement District (York BID) and is a celebration of the wealth of incredible hospitality businesses in the city.

For more information, the full list of offers and to download your free vouchers, visit the York Restaurant Week website.

Please check each individual voucher for the full terms and conditions.
