A COLLAPSED tree has fallen onto broadband cables in a suburb of York.




North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Tadcaster was called to Heslington Lane at 6.45pm yesterday (Sunday, September 27).

A fire service spokesperson said its crews cordoned off the area.

*An earlier statement from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the tree had collapsed onto electricity cables.*
