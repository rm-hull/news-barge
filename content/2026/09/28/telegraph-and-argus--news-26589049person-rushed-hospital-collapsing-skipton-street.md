A PERSON was rushed to hospital after collapsing in the street in Skipton town centre at the weekend.

Emergency services were scrambled to the scene at midday on Sunday to reports that a person had collapsed on Newmarket Street/High Street in Skipton.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 12.08pm on Sunday to report a person who had collapsed on Newmarket Street/High Street, Skipton.

"An ambulance was dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to Airedale Hospital."

The fire service reported that they were called at 12.42pm to the scene outside the Caroline Square building.

A spokesperson for the North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "A crew from Skipton assisted a person who had fallen in the street, into the ambulance, alongside paramedics."
