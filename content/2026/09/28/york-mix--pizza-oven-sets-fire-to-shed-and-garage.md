Six firefighters tackled a blaze in Pocklington which began in a pizza oven early this morning (Monday).

They were called to the incident at **Amos Drive, Pocklington**, at** 2.04am**.

The fire began with the pizza oven in a shed which then spread to a garage and the “exterior of property including bi-fold doors and fascias of property,” Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said.

#### Amos Drive, Pocklington

“Extinguished by crews using six breathing apparatus, three hose reels, one main line and portable lighting.”
