# Mobile speed camera locations in Leeds this week

## These cameras are set up in known casualty zones

In a bid to reduce accidents across Leeds, mobile speed cameras have been put in place..

Mobile speed cameras have been set up by the West Yorkshire Police Camera Enforcement Unit on the behalf of the West Yorkshire Camera Partnership. These cameras are then set up in known casualty zones, which are areas identified by the police as being at risk of accidents

The cameras shine a laser at passing vehicles, which allows them to determine their speeds. They are designed to keep speeds down on roads to reduce accidents.
