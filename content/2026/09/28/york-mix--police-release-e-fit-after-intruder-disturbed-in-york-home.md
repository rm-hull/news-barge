This is the face of a man who was disturbed while burgling a York home.

As ***YorkMix*** reported earlier this month, residents at a property on Stockton Lane disturbed an intruder at around 2.49am on Tuesday 15 September.

The man immediately made off after being discovered. It is believed he gained entry through an insecure door at the rear of the house.

#### Stockton Lane, York

The suspect is described as

* a white man
* aged in his thirties or forties
* with a slim, athletic build
* pale skin
* bald with some short, dark hair at the back of his head
* and dark eyes.

He was described as having an overall clean and tidy appearance and was wearing a dark top and trousers.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are asking anyone who recognises the man from the e-fit, or who has any information that could assist our investigation, to get in touch.”
