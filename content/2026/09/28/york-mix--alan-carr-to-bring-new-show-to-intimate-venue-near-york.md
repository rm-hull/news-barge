Award-winning comedian Alan Carr is heading to our neck of the woods for a special show next month.

Best known for hosting hit chat show*Chatty Man*, as well as appearances on * 8 Out of 10 Cats Does Countdown*, * RuPaul’s Drag Race UK*, and more, Alan is to play**Pocklington Arts Centre**.

The winner of *Celebrity Traitors* is bringing his work-in-progress show to the venue at 7.30pm on Sunday 11 October.

It’s a rare chance to see one of the UK’s best-loved comedians up close as he tests new jokes and shapes his next show.

Venue director Kelly Munro-Fawcett said: “We’re absolutely thrilled to welcome Alan Carr to Pocklington Arts Centre!

“He’s such a well-loved comedian, and it’s fantastic to have him performing here in Pocklington.

“It’s a brilliant opportunity for audiences to see him up close as he tries out his new material, and we’re really looking forward to what promises to be a very special evening of comedy.”

Tickets, priced at £18, will go on sale at 10am tomorrow, Tuesday 29 September. Visit the Pocklington Arts Centre website to book.
