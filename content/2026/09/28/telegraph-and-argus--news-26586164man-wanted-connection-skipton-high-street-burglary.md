Police are searching a man with Bradford links over a high street burglary in a North Yorkshire town.

Stewart Walton, 45, is wanted over a break-in on Skipton High Street.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Officers are searching for wanted man Stewart Walton, 45.

"He is being sought in connection with a burglary on Skipton High Street.

"He could be in the Keighley, Skipton or Bradford areas."

Anyone with information about his whereabouts is urged to contact North Yorkshire Police by calling 101.

Information can also be given anonymously through Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Anyone who spots Mr Walton should call 999 immediately.
