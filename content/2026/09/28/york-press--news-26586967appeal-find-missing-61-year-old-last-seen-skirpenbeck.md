POLICE have issued an appeal to find a woman who was last seen in an East Yorkshire village yesterday (Sunday, September 27).

Humberside Police said missing 61-year-old Alison was last seen in the area of Dolegate, near Skirpenbeck, a village close to the A166.

The force described her as white, with short dark hair and around 5ft 5in tall.

The force said anybody with information which can help to locate Alison, or have seen her, should call 101 quoting the log 466 of Sunday, September 27.
