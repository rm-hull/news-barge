# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image of person following theft at housing estate in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police has issued a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to following a theft in Harrogate.

The incident happened at 2am on July 7 at a property on the Dene Park housing estate.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Please contact us if you recognise the person pictured in the CCTV image as they may have information that will assist our investigation.”

![The police have released a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to following a theft at a property at a housing estate on Dene Park in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/crime/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260133566 when providing any details regarding the incident.
