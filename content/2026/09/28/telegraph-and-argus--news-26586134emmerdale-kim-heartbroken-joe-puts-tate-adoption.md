From a devastating adoption decision to growing concerns over Gabby's health, there's no shortage of drama in Emmerdale this week.

The biggest storyline of the week centres on Joe Tate's decision to put baby Tate up for adoption.

Despite pleas from Kim and Graham, Joe remains determined to go through with the plan.

Graham goes as far as issuing an ultimatum, warning Joe he will leave his life forever if the adoption proceeds.

Although Kim is briefly given hope when baby Tate returns to Home Farm, it soon becomes clear Joe has only brought him back so she can say goodbye before the adoption goes ahead.

The fallout leaves Kim devastated, while Graham's departure creates a new sense of emptiness at Home Farm.

## **Manpreet fears Gabby's healthy lifestyle has gone too far**

Meanwhile, concerns are mounting for Gabby after a persistent cough refuses to go away.

After initially dismissing medical advice, Gabby's fixation on organic foods and "clean eating" begins to ring alarm bells for Manpreet.

The situation worsens when Gabby coughs up blood, prompting further tests.

As Manpreet learns more about Gabby's social media content and attitudes towards medicine, she begins to suspect she could be suffering from orthorexia, an unhealthy obsession with healthy eating.

But convincing Gabby she has a problem may prove easier said than done.

## **Sadie and Robert continue desperate cash search**

Elsewhere, Sadie and Robert remain convinced Jimmy's missing money is hidden somewhere at the depot.

Sadie attempts to get closer to Ruby by feigning an interest in her wine business, but her search nearly unravels when Caleb catches her snooping around.

The following day she is forced into damage limitation after almost letting slip too much information while bonding with a drunken Ruby.

Despite several setbacks, Sadie remains determined to uncover the cash, even as Robert begins to question whether the search is worth the risk.

## **Trouble ahead for Sarah and Thea**

With Charity's trial date drawing closer, Sarah finds herself struggling under the weight of her guilt.

At the same time, Thea's feelings towards Kim continue to shift.

After initially softening following a conversation at Will's graveside, she quickly reverts to her original plan when she overhears what she perceives to be a callous comment about baby Tate's situation.

The developments leave Thea more determined than ever to seek revenge.

## **Nicola struggles while Vinny reaches breaking point**

Nicola continues to process the revelations surrounding Jimmy and the impact they have had on her family.

Vinny, meanwhile, seeks advice from Aaron before later breaking down while returning Jimmy's belongings to Nicola.

There is also tension elsewhere as Moses asks questions about Leyla, putting Sarah in an awkward position and sparking fury from Mack when he learns the conversation has taken place.

With shock decisions, emotional confrontations and life-changing consequences, it's another busy week in the Dales.
