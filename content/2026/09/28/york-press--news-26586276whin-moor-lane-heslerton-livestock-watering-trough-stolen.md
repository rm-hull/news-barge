Police are investigating the theft of a livestock watering trough from land on Whin Moor Lane in West Heslerton.

The incident took place between September 12 and 14.

Officers are appealing for information from anyone who was in the area and may have seen anything suspicious.

They are also asking for any relevant dashcam footage that could assist with the investigation.

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC Ben Robinson-Brockhill by email at [ben.robinson-brockhill@northyorkshirepolice.uk.](mailto:ben.robinson-brockhill@northyorkshirepolice.uk.)

The public can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260180772.
