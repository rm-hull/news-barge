Police are searching for a missing 14-year-old girl who disappeared in the early hours of this morning.

Izata Bah left a house in the Ayresome ward area of Middlesbrough at around 4am on Monday (September 28).

It is unknown what clothing Izata was wearing however she has a school uniform with her and could have changed into this, or she may be wearing gym clothes and a sports jacket.

![]()Izata Bah, 4, was last seen in Middlesbrough's Ayresome ward at around 4am on Monday (September 28). *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Izata is around 5’5” tall, of slim build, with light brown wavy/curly hair with blond streaks.

The teenager has a scar next to her right eye.

If you have seen Izata or know her current whereabouts please contact Cleveland Police on the 101 number quoting 194363.
