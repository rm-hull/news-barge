A crash has closed a North Yorkshire road tonight (Monday).

The A163 is shut at Barlby.

North Yorkshire Police issued a traffic warning at 8.18pm.

#### Crash location

It said: “The A163 at Barlby is currently closed while emergency services deal with a serious road traffic collision.  
“The closure is expected to remain in place for some time.  
“Please avoid the area and use an alternative route.”
