A MAN has been left with serious injuries after three-vehicle crash on a major Bradford road.

The incident, which took place on Canal Road, involved two cars and a motor scooter, with officers called at 9.42am on Saturday.

Emergency services attended the scene near the ACW Garden Centre.

West Yorkshire Police confirmed the vehicles involved were a grey Volkswagen Golf, a black Volvo, and a Lambretta scooter.

A 61-year-old man riding the scooter was taken to hospital with serious injuries and remains in a critical but stable condition.

![]()Police cars at the scene on Canal Road, Bradford *(Image: T&A)*

## What have police said?

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for witnesses following a road traffic collision in Bradford in which the rider of a motor scooter was seriously injured.

"At 9.42am on Saturday, officers were called to the collision involving a grey Volkswagen Golf, a Lambretta scooter and a black Volvo on Canal Road, near to the ACW Garden Centre.

"The rider of the scooter, a 61-year-old man, received serious injuries and was taken to hospital for treatment.

"He remains in a critical but stable condition.

"The driver of the Golf, a 24-year-old man, was interviewed under caution by officers, and enquiries are continuing."

Police are appealing for witnesses or anyone with dashcam footage of the collision or events leading up to it.

Information can be submitted via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat, quoting reference 13260548392.
