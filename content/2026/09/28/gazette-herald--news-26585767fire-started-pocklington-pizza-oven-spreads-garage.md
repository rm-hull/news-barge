FIREFIGHTERS have tackled a blaze in an East Yorkshire town which began in a shed early this morning (Monday, September 28).

Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said a crew was called to Amos Drive in Pocklington at 2.04am.

A service spokesperson said the fire involved a pizza oven in a shed which then spread to the garage and exterior of a property including its bi-fold doors and fascias.

The spokesperson added: "The fire was extinguished by crews using six breathing apparatus, three hose reels, one main line and portable lighting."
