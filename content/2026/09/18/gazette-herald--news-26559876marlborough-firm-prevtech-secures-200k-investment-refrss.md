A precision engineering firm in Wiltshire has secured a £200,000 investment for the next stage of its growth.

PrevTech, based in Marlborough, received the investment from the British Business Bank's South West Investment Fund delivered by The FSE Group.

The company manufactures complex components to exact customer specifications, serving clients in the motorsport sector, including Formula 1 supply chains.

The company is also building opportunities in other advanced engineering industries.

PrevTech's reputation for delivering high-quality, quick-turnaround machining work has led to increasing demand from customers seeking flexible and responsive manufacturing partners.

The funding will enable the company to expand its facilities, recruit additional skilled staff, and invest in quality and cybersecurity accreditations.

These upgrades are expected to help increase production capacity and enable the business to access new opportunities in aerospace and defence.

PrevTech's managing director and co-founder, Martyn Roberts, said: "We've built the business on the back of our experience, technical expertise, and a commitment to delivering quality work for customers.

"Demand has continued to grow, and this investment gives us the confidence and resources to take the next step.

"Expanding into larger premises, bringing in additional skilled people, and achieving the accreditations needed for new sectors will help us unlock exciting opportunities while continuing to support our existing customers."

The funding is expected to support the creation of up to five new jobs over the next three years.

These roles include CNC machining and quality-focused positions, which will bolster advanced manufacturing skills within the region.

Amanda Sheppard, Investment Manager at The FSE Group, said: "PrevTech is a great example of a South West manufacturing business with strong technical capability and clear growth potential.

"Martyn and Chris have built a solid reputation in a relatively short period and have identified a well-defined route for growth.

"This funding will help the business increase capacity, create skilled jobs, and open up new opportunities in aerospace and defence, supporting the long-term development of the company."

The South West Investment Fund aims to drive sustainable economic growth by supporting innovation and creating local opportunity for new and growing businesses across the South West.

It offers a range of commercial finance options with smaller loans from £25k to£100k, debt finance from £100k to £2m and equity investment up to £5 million.
