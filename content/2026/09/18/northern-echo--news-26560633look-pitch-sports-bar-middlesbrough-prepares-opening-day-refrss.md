The finishing touches are being added to Middlesbrough's newest sports bar.

The new spot with mega sports-watching screens and interactive games is preparing to open to the public in four days.

Pitch! Sports Bar will hold soft launches on Thursday, September 17 and Friday, September 18, before officially opening on Monday, September 21, in the former Bar Fresco building in Middlesbrough town centre.

On Thursday morning, dust sheets still covered the floors of the bar, but by lunchtime, staff had cleared them away.

Everyone involved is helping to finish the lighting, flooring and decor before the first guests arrive.

  PITCH Sports Bar opens in Middlesbrough on Monday. 

*(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

More than two years in the making, the highly anticipated sports bar is now just days away from welcoming its first customers.

A spokesperson for Pneuma Group - the hospitality investor behind Pitch! - said: "The concept has always been there. It's been in the making for two years now and it wasn't always going to be a sports bar.

"But choosing Middlesbrough was intentional.

"We have over 300 employees at Pneuma who live in Middlesbrough, which was ideal for market research, and this was what the area needed."

  PITCH Sports Bar opens in Middlesbrough on Monday. 

*(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

The project was initially planned for the former Doctors Browns site on Corporation Road before being moved to Exchange Walk in May.

Pitch! has transformed the prominent town-centre site into a two-floor sports and entertainment venue, centred on what operators call the country's largest bar screen, measuring 23ft.

  PITCH Sports Bar opens in Middlesbrough on Monday. 

*(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

The giant LED screen is joined by 27 others, allowing up to six sporting events to be shown at once, including football, Formula One, boxing, rugby and darts.

The venue has six viewing zones with individual audio, while the first floor features interactive darts, two sports simulators and a mezzanine overlooking the main screen.

The simulators will offer football, rugby, cricket and golf for groups of up to six people.

  PITCH Sports Bar opens in Middlesbrough on Monday. 

*(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

"It will be immersive and interactive," the spokesperson said.

"The idea is that it will be a venue for everyone, even in the off-season or for people who just want to come and enjoy some food with friends."

The menu takes inspiration from traditional American sports bars, with wings, chicken tenders, smash burgers, loaded nachos and sharing dishes.

  PITCH Sports Bar opens in Middlesbrough on Monday. 

*(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

A dedicated app launching on Monday will let customers order food and drinks, view fixtures, book activities and access special offers.

VIP booths with private table service will also be available to book, particularly for major events where groups are looking for some extra privacy and luxury while watching the game.

  PITCH Sports Bar opens in Middlesbrough on Monday. 

*(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

For peak events, including international tournaments, customers will also need to book tickets in advance to help manage venue capacity and ensure everyone has enough space to enjoy themselves safely.

The venue also worked with several local businesses during its development, including Middlesbrough graffiti artist Robert Hutchinson, who created murals inside the building representing sports including football, golf and F1.

After two years of planning and months of work, the team is excited to just open the doors and get started, welcoming customers from Teesside and beyond.

The Pneuma Group said: "We've taken the time to get it right and create something positive with a vision we've had for a long time."
