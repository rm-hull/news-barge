# Woman found asleep in front of vehicle arrested on suspicion of drink driving

## A woman has been arrested after officers were called to reports of a suspected drink driver on the Yorkshire coast.

At around 10am on Thursday Sep 17, officers attended an address on Wayside Crescent in Bridlington and spotted a woman asleep in the front of her vehicle.

It was reported she had been witnessed driving the vehicle to the address before falling asleep, police said.

She was requested to take a roadside breathalyser to which she failed, blowing 123.

A 55-year-old woman was arrested on suspicion of drink driving.

She remains in police custody at this time.
