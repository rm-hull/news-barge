# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after Range Rover deliberately damaged in Knaresborough

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after a car was deliberately damaged while parked on a road in Knaresborough.

The black Range Rover had been parked legally and without causing an obstruction on Tentergate Road when it was targeted.

The damage was discovered on Thursday, September 10, but officers believe the incident may have happened the previous day.

Officers are now urging anyone who witnessed the damage being caused, or who has relevant CCTV, doorbell or dash cam footage, to come forward.
