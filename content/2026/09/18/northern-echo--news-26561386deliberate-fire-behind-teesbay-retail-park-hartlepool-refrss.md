Firefighters have attended a bin fire on wasteland behind a North East retail park, which the fire service is treating as 'deliberate'.

The incident happened on land behind Teesbay Retail Park on Brenda Road, Hartlepool, on Thursday (September 17) at around 9.30pm, following reports of a bin fire.

One fire engine, from Hartlepool, was required at the scene.

Cleveland Fire Brigade say they were at the incident for around 20 minutes before the fire was brought under control.

A spokesperson for the fire service said: “Cleveland Fire Brigade were called on 17/09/2026 at 21:30 to an incident on the wasteland behind Teesbay Retail Park, Hartlepool.

One fire engine was in attendance from Hartlepool. Refuse fire. The stop call was received at 9.50. The cause is believed to be deliberate."
