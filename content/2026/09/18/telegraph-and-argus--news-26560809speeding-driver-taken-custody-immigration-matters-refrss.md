A DRIVER stopped for speeding is now in custody due to suspected outstanding immigration matters.

Police stopped a BMW travelling at 57mph in Bradford and found the driver had no licence, no insurance, and was not wearing a seatbelt.

They also discovered outstanding immigration matters, the force said.

Officers from West Yorkshire Police Bradford East said: "Speeding turned out to be just the beginning of the driver's problems.

"We also identified offences of no licence, no insurance and no seatbelt."

Police seized the vehicle and took the driver to custody.
