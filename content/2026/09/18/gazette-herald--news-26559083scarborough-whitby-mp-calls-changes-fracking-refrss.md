AN MP is pressing for changes to energy legislation.

Alison Hume, MP for Scarborough and Whitby, wants to prevent certain gas extraction methods she believes are not fully covered by the current fracking moratorium.

Her campaign follows Europa Oil & Gas’s proposal for a “proppant squeeze” operation near Burniston, which was rejected by North Yorkshire Council's Area Planning Committee amid strong public opposition.

Ms Hume said: "I’m continuing the fight to close what I regard as a loophole in the fracking moratorium on behalf of my constituents, particularly those in and around Burniston who have made their opposition to the proposed proppant squeeze there clear."

She argues that the technique still amounts to fracking and should be covered by the ban, regardless of the volume of fluid used.

The Government maintains that “proppant squeezing” does not carry the same environmental and safety concerns associated with large-scale shale gas extraction and, as such, does not undermine its manifesto commitment.

However, Ms Hume has highlighted expert advice to the contrary and remains committed to pushing for legislative clarity.

She said: "My view is simple: if it looks like fracking and sounds like fracking, then no matter what volume of fluid is being used, it is fracking."
