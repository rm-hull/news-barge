FILM fans in Bradford have plenty to choose from this Saturday, with a packed programme across The Light Bradford and ODEON Bradford.

From the terrifying return of Resident Evil and the long-awaited Practical Magic 2 to family favourites including Shaun the Sheep, Toy Story 5 and Paw Patrol, there is something for horror fans, families, animation lovers and blockbuster audiences alike.

There are also special screenings and big-screen returns, including the 40th anniversary presentation of The Transformers: The Movie, while Spider-Man: Brand New Day, Pressure and Runner continue their runs.
