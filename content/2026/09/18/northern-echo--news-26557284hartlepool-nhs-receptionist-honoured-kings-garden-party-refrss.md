A hospital receptionist whose warm smile and friendly guidance have helped generations of North East patients has received royal recognition for more than 35 years’ NHS service.

Laurence Herdman, known as 'the man behind the desk' at University Hospital of Hartlepool, was invited to the King's Garden Party at Holyrood Palace earlier this year for his decades of dedication to patients.

Based in the outpatient department, Mr Herdman has spent decades guiding and comforting vulnerable patients at University Hospital of Hartlepool, University Hospital of North Tees and in the wider community.

  Laurence Herdman, the long-serving receptionist at University Hospital of Hartlepool, was honoured with an invitation to the King’s Garden Party at Holyrood Palace in recognition of more than 35 years of dedicated NHS service to patients across the North East 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Mr Herdman said: "I feel humbled and honoured to have been invited to attend the King's Garden Party.

"I've always believed I was simply doing my job, so to receive this recognition is very special.

"The greatest reward has always been helping patients and working alongside fantastic colleagues throughout my career."

His nomination began with a letter from a member of the public to Sir James Mackey, chief executive of NHS England.

The letter praised Laurence’s warmth and kindness, and his commitment to making every patient feel valued, including those with complex illnesses, mental health conditions, learning difficulties or physical disabilities.

The letter also sparked a wave of social-media support, as patients and residents shared stories of his compassion.

One patient called him "an absolute gentleman, kind, caring and very helpful," while another said: "A wonderful man!

"He makes everyone feel special."

Others called him "one in a million", "a fantastic asset" and "worthy of a British Empire Medal".

Tracey Anyinsah, centralised outpatient reception team lead at University Hospitals Tees, said: "Over his 35-year career, Laurence has become a respected member of the hospital team, known for supporting anxious and vulnerable patients during difficult periods in their lives.

"His invitation to the King's Garden Party is a fitting tribute to a career devoted to caring for others and improving the lives of thousands of patients."

Mr Herdman’s presence at the front desk has made a lasting impression in Stockton and Hartlepool, where he is known for his professionalism and kindness to everyone who walks through the door.
