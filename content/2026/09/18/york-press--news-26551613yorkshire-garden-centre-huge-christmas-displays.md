## Yorkshire garden centre with huge Christmas displays and 'locally sourced' plants

You can find a huge range of plants to decorate your garden with. *(Image: Getty)*

Even though summer is over, it does not mean you have to leave your garden looking boring.

A trip to a garden centre is an easy and fun way to pick up plants and flowers to decorate your garden.

Luckily, in Yorkshire, there are many amazing garden centres to visit with plenty of seeds, plants, and gardening equipment to help you.

Hardwick’s Garden Centre can be found on the edge of Boroughbridge and has everything you could need, from garden furniture to a huge Christmas department.

  The centre has seeds, flowers, trees, and more. 

*(Image: Getty)*

## What is Hardwick’s Garden Centre like?

Hardwick’s Garden Centre is a family-run business that has been going for over 25 years, with a team that is on hand to help you with any questions you might have.

The garden centre has an “extensive range of locally sourced plants and flowers” from colourful bedding plants that will last long, as well as climbing plants to cover bare walls and fences, and hanging baskets.

There are also lots of conifers, sculpted trees, shrubs and ornamental trees to style your garden.

If you want to grow your own fruits and vegetables, the garden centre has apple, cherry, plum, and pear trees as well as bushes for berries and a whole variety of seeds and herbs that are easy to manage at home.

Hardwick’s also offers a range of garden furniture from tables and chairs to relax on to statues and barbecues and pizza ovens to be able to host the perfect garden party.

Alongside all of its outdoor products, the garden centre has its own gift department filled with “unique gifts and treasures” including tableware and wall art.


You can also treat yourself by picking up some home décor for your own house to add a bit of extra colour.

During the festive season, the garden centre has its huge Christmas display with a “massive range of stylish baubles and Christmas decorations to suit every home”.

Whether you like the traditional red and green decorations or prefer something a little more subdued, Hardwick’s will have the perfect tinsel, bauble or gonk for you.
