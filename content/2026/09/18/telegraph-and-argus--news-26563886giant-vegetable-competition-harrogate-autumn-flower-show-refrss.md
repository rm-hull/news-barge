The region's biggest vegetables have been revealed at the Harrogate Autumn Flower Show.

The show opened today (September 18) and runs until September 20 at Newby Hall & Gardens near Ripon.

The annual NEHS Giant Vegetable Competition has once again produced some impressive entries, with growers competing across a range of categories.

· Graham Haswell – giant marrow – 36.43kg

· Chris Marriott – giant beetroot – 21.88kg

· Paul Proud – longest cucumber – 1,022mm

· Stephen Purvis – giant leek – 8.4kg

The show is also home to the National Onion Championship, attracting some of the country’s finest giant onion growers.

This year’s champion was Stephen Purvis of Seaham, County Durham, whose onion weighed in at 7.972kg.

The National Onion Championship has been a highlight of the Harrogate Autumn Flower Show since its introduction in 1983.

In 2023, the competition made horticultural history when an onion, clocking in at 8.97kg, was grown and weighed at the show, setting a world record.

Now celebrating its 50th anniversary, the Harrogate Autumn Flower Show brings together competitive flowers, fruit and vegetables, specialist nurseries, gardening displays, plants, shopping and family activities for three days of autumn gardening inspiration.

Show tickets also grant access to the historic house and gardens.
