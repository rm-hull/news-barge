THE MINISTRY of Justice has confirmed an investigation is underway after a Bradford man died while in prison in June this year.

On Tuesday, June 16, Liam Makin, 31, from Bradford, died whilst imprisoned at HMP Hindley, a Category C adult male prison in Wigan in Greater Manchester.

A spokesperson for the MoJ said: "This prisoner has passed away.

"An investigation into his cause of death is ongoing and we will provide an update when this investigation has concluded."

## Why was Makin in prison?

The T&A understands Makin was serving a custodial sentence for aggravated burglary.

According to reports, he was jailed for more than six years following a terrifying break-in at a home in Harehills, Leeds, in September 2023.

Makin was armed with a hammer, while another male carried a knife.

During the case, the judge spoke of Makin's "significant record" and stated that he had been involved in crime since being a youngster.

## Where is HMP Hindley?

HMP Hindley is in Bickershaw, a village near Wigan in Greater Manchester.

The site is often referred to as being “in Hindley” locally, but technically it’s in the neighbouring village of Bickershaw.

It’s a Category C adult male prison that also operates as a Young Offender Institution (YOI).
