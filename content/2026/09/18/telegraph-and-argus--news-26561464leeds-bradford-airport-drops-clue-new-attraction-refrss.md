LEEDS-BRADFORD Airport has teased a forthcoming attraction - and users won't have long to wait.

Bosses posted a cryptic message to Facebook with the words: "Something new is coming and we can't wait to tell you about it..."

The post was accompanied with several photos showing a new American themed restaurant.

Leeds Bradford Airport has significantly expanded its food and drink offering in recent years, giving travellers a much wider choice before they fly.

## What's on offer

The airport's modernised terminal includes a range of cafés, restaurants and bars designed to cater for everything from a quick snack to a relaxed sit-down meal. According to the airport, the redevelopment has created a broader food and drink selection for passengers.

Popular outlets include Greggs for pastries and sandwiches, Starbucks for coffee and light bites, and Burger King for fast food favourites.

After security, passengers can visit Cabin Bar, which serves meals alongside wines, beers and cocktails, or Tap + Brew, a Yorkshire-themed venue offering local craft beers from Salt Beer Factory as well as breakfasts, burgers, fish and chips and other pub classics.

The aim is to ensure passengers can enjoy a meal, drink or snack regardless of their flight time, whether they are catching an early morning departure or relaxing before an evening flight.

## A long history of travel

Leeds Bradford Airport traces its origins to 1931 when it opened as Yeadon Aerodrome, serving the cities of Leeds and Bradford.


Scheduled passenger flights began in 1935, linking Yorkshire with destinations including London, Newcastle and Edinburgh.

During the Second World War, the site became an important aircraft manufacturing and testing centre, with thousands of military aircraft built there.

Civil aviation resumed in 1947 and the airport expanded steadily in the following decades.

A new runway opened in 1965, while major improvements in the 1980s established it as Yorkshire's principal regional airport.

Today, it serves millions of passengers each year
