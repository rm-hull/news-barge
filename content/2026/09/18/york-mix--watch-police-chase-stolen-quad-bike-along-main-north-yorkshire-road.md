Footage of an extraordinary police chase of a suspect on a stolen quad bike has been released by police.

Taken at 4.15am on Thursday morning, it shows officers pursuing the vehicle along the A171.

It came shortly after someone had dialled 999 to report their red quad bike stolen.

Video

Officers travelling towards Whitby on the A171 spot the suspected stolen quad bike heading in the opposite direction towards Scaling Dam.

The driver didn’t stop for police and is seen weaving along the road, crossing to the wrong side of the carriageway, to prevent officers overtaking.

At one point the suspect discards a phone from his pocket. It was later recovered by officers.

At 4.39am, just 24 minutes after the initial report, a man in his 30s from the Cleveland area was arrested on suspicion of:

* Theft of a motor vehicle
* Dangerous driving
* Failing to stop for police
* Driving without insurance
* Driving otherwise than in accordance with a licence.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/north-yorkshire-police-quad-bike-stolen-a171-police-video-2.webp)

The suspected stolen quad bike was recovered, the man remains in custody and enquiries are ongoing.

Chief Inspector Alex Langley from the Specialist Operations team at North Yorkshire Police said: “This incident highlights the dedication of our officers who work around the clock to protect our communities.

“Thanks to a swift response and coordinated policing tactics, a suspected stolen vehicle was recovered, a suspect was arrested and a potentially dangerous situation was brought to a safe conclusion.

“While most people are asleep, our officers are out on North Yorkshire’s roads preventing crime, pursuing offenders and keeping the public safe.

“This footage provides a glimpse into the reality of policing and the work carried out by our Specialist Operations officers every day.”
