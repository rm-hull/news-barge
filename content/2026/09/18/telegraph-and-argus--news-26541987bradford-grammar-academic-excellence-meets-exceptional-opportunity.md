  **Are you looking for an independent school where academic ambition, sporting success and creative excellence thrive side by side?**

  At **Bradford Grammar School (BGS)**, pupils benefit from an outstanding education that extends far beyond the classroom. For children aged** 4 to 18**, BGS combines academic excellence with exceptional opportunities in sport, music, drama and co-curricular life, creating an environment where young people are encouraged to discover their passions, fulfil their potential and develop the confidence to succeed.

## 
  **Nurturing talent, building confidence**

  Blending academic rigour with a wide range of experiences 

*(Image: Bradford Grammar)*

At BGS, every pupil is recognised as an individual. Dedicated staff provide the support, encouragement and opportunities needed for students to grow academically, socially and personally.

The school blends academic rigour with a wide range of experiences that develop curiosity, creativity and resilience. Throughout their educational journey, pupils are challenged to achieve their best while being supported to flourish as confident and well-rounded individuals.

Families can experience the vibrant BGS community firsthand by attending one of the school's open events, where visitors can explore the facilities, meet staff and pupils, and discover the opportunities available.

## 
  **A commitment to the arts**

While many schools have reduced investment in creative subjects, the arts remain central to life at BGS. Through an extensive programme of music, drama and performance opportunities, pupils develop creativity, confidence and essential life skills.

## 
  **Music: The heartbeat of BGS**

Music plays a vibrant role throughout the school. From ensembles and recitals to major concerts and productions, pupils of all abilities are encouraged to develop their talents and perform regularly.

Students frequently showcase their skills at prestigious venues across Yorkshire, while the school's Music Scholarship programme supports talented young musicians within a thriving creative community.

## 
  **Outstanding sporting opportunities**

Sport is an integral part of life at BGS, promoting teamwork, leadership, resilience and wellbeing alongside competitive success.

Pupils benefit from exceptional facilities, including a flagship sports barn, all-weather tennis and netball courts, AstroTurf pitches, outdoor cricket lanes, a modern fitness suite and the 25-metre Adrian Moorhouse swimming pool.

Whether competing at a high level or simply enjoying an active lifestyle, every pupil can participate in the school's sporting programme. Sports Scholarships help talented athletes balance their sporting ambitions with academic achievement.

## 
  **Excellence beyond the classroom**

  At BGS, every pupil is recognised as an individual 

*(Image: Bradford Grammar)*

A defining feature of BGS is its extensive co-curricular programme. With a broad range of clubs, societies, competitions, volunteering opportunities and leadership activities, pupils are encouraged to pursue their interests, discover new talents and develop valuable life skills.

The school takes pride in celebrating achievement in all its forms, recognising commitment, personal growth and success both inside and outside the classroom.

## 
  **Scholarships, bursaries and opportunity**

BGS is committed to widening access through its means-tested Assisted Places Award bursary programme, helping talented young people benefit from a BGS education regardless of financial circumstances.

  The school also offers **Academic, Music and Sports Scholarships**, recognising and rewarding talent, ambition and potential.

## 
  **Discover Bradford Grammar School**

  Everything at BGS is underpinned by three core principles: **excellence, opportunity and happiness**.

  As Headmaster **Simon Hinchliffe** explains: "We believe in providing every opportunity for our pupils to embrace academic, sporting and creative excellence within an aspirational and caring environment. An environment in which happiness is the key to an individual's success."

Families are warmly invited to attend an upcoming open event and experience what makes Bradford Grammar School such a distinctive and successful school community.
