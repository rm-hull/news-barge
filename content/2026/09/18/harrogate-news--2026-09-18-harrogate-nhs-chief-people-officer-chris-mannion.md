**HARROGATE**— Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust (HDFT) has formally expanded its executive leadership team with the appointment of Chris Mannion as its new Chief People Officer.Mannion joined the local healthcare provider earlier this month, transitioning directly from his previous post as Deputy Director of Workforce and Organisational Development at Mid Yorkshire Teaching NHS Trust, a position he had maintained since 2019.

In his new capacity as workforce head, Mannion will oversee the implementation of the trust’s overarching employment strategy, directing regional recruitment campaigns, retention initiatives, and cultural development protocols across the hospital and its community networks. He succeeds Angela Wilkinson, who retired in May 2026 following an eight-year tenure as Director of People and Culture.

As an executive director sitting directly on the Executive Board, Mannion will report directly to Jonathan Coulter, the Chief Executive of the Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust. The board-level position completely bypasses standard, time-stepped NHS “Agenda for Change” pay bands, operating instead under the dedicated Very Senior Manager (VSM) pay scheme.

While exact individual packages are finalised by the board’s internal Remuneration Committee depending on executive experience, equivalent VSM director roles at the trust command a baseline salary typically sitting between £115,000 and £135,000 per annum, alongside statutory senior director pension contributions.


**Jonathan Coulter, Chief Executive of Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust, stated:**

Chris joins us with a wealth of NHS experience and an impressive track record of leading workforce and organisational development initiatives that have made a real difference for colleagues and patients. Throughout the recruitment process, Chris demonstrated a genuine passion for people, leadership and culture, alongside a clear understanding of the challenges and opportunities facing the NHS workforce.



Beginning his career within the NHS Graduate Management Training Scheme in 2007, Mannion has worked extensively across workforce restructuring and personnel transformation sectors. His previous management contributions were recognised nationally when he was named Deputy People Leader of the Year at the Healthcare People Management Association (HPMA) Awards in 2024.

Academic and professional credentials linked to the new executive include a BSc in Psychology, an MA in Human Resource Management, and a Chartered Fellowship with the Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (CIPD). He is also an ACAS-trained workplace mediator and a graduate of the NHS Leadership Academy’s Nye Bevan Programme.


**Chris Mannion, incoming Chief People Officer, added:**

What attracted me to HDFT was its clear commitment to its values and to creating an environment where staff feel supported and are able to deliver exceptional care and experiences for patients. I am looking forward to meeting colleagues across our services and communities, listening to their experiences and working with them to build on the Trust’s many strengths.



The management shift follows the appointment of Julia Nixon as Chief Operating Officer in July, completing the trust’s latest executive alignment strategy. Further structural updates, board reports, and organisational vacancies can be reviewed directly via the verified public portal of the Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust.
