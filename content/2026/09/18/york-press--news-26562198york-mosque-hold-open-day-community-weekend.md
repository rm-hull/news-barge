York Mosque is to hold an open day welcoming people from across the city to learn about Islam.

The mosque, in Bull Lane, will open its doors from 2pm to 4pm on Sunday (September 20).

Faizal Mohamudbuccus, from York Mosque, encouraged people to come along to the event.

“We welcome the community to come look around the mosque and learn about Islam,” he said.

“We will have stalls exploring different areas of our religion as well as other stalls from various organisations. These include the police, the health team from the council and the fire and rescue team.

“There will also be photos showing the history of the mosque too.”

York Mosque started in 1983 and officially opened its mosque in Bull Lane in 2018.

The purpose-built mosque cost more than £1 million, funded by donations and events including sponsored walks, food festivals and jumble sales.
