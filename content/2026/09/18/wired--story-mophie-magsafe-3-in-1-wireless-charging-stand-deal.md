Charging all your gadgets is an unavoidable daily task, but wireless charging makes it easy. Mophie’s uniquely extendable stand is one of the best Apple 3-in-1 wireless chargers I have tested. And it’s available at a deep discount right now at half off (technically it’s 49 percent off). While this 3-in-1 stand has been discounted before, I’ve never seen it this low.

A 3-in-1 MagSafe charging stand is the best way to keep your desk or nightstand clutter-free but still charge your gadgets every day. This Mophie MagSafe 3-in-1 Charging Stand has a pad for regular AirPods or wireless earbuds on the weighted base and an Apple Watch charger branching off. But this stand also features a clever trick: You can adjust the height of the iPhone charging pad.

Whether you have a low nightstand or work at a standing desk, this clever, compact stand enables you to keep your iPhone in your eye line. You can also turn your phone to landscape orientation for StandBy mode to use your iPhone as a bedside alarm clock. The 15-watt iPhone charger sits atop a telescoping pole that can extend from 7.5 to 16 inches. The pad can also tilt to your preferred angle. For content creators or folks making video calls, it can also serve as a tripod to help you film a quick video or take video calls.

The Mophie MagSafe 3-in-1 Charging Stand is both MFi and Qi-certified to provide up to 15 watts to your iPhone. It can also deliver 5 watts to your Apple Watch and 5 watts to your wireless charging AirPods case or other wireless earbuds. You get a 36-watt power supply and a 5-foot USB-C cable in the box.

If you have issues with the height of your iPhone, this is the 3-in-1 stand you need. Height adjustment is not a feature I’ve ever seen in another charger, but you can find plenty of fixed-height alternatives in my best MagSafe chargers guide.
