A FIRE and rescue crew has been called to a car that became stuck at the entrance to a York city centre car park.

Firefighters from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service helped free a car that had become wedged under the entrance sign to Coppergate Centre Car Park at around 10am on Friday (September 18).

  


  A fire crew was called out to the area to free the car from the entrance 

*(Image: John Firth)*

An eyewitness said the jammed car had caused long queues for those trying to park and along Parliament Street.

At around 10.30am, the car had been removed and traffic had returned to normal.
