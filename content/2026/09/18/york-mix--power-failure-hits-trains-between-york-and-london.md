There’s disruption to rail services on the East Coast Main Line today (Friday).

A power failure between Stevenage and Finsbury Park means that fewer trains are able to run.

As a result, services between Stevenage and London may be cancelled, altered or delayed by up to 20 minutes.

National Rail says: “Network Rail and local electricity suppliers are onsite working to make sure the power is restored.

“Until this is done, we expect there will be delays and changes to services in the area.”

Disruption is expected until at least 12pm.

## LNER customer advice

Rail replacement buses have been requested to run between Newark Northgate and Lincoln in place of cancelled services.

You can use your ticket on other train companies at no extra cost.

## Hull Trains advice

You should continue to travel as planned, however your journey may be delayed by 20 minutes.

There’s more information on the National Rail website.
