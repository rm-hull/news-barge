PEOPLE taking four or more medications are being encouraged to attend drop-in events during Falls Awareness Week.

Bradford District Care NHS Foundation Trust’s Falls Prevention team will be on hand to offer advice and identify whether individuals may be at increased risk of falling.

Sophie Myers, falls prevention lead pharmacy technician, said: "Falls can be caused by many different things from a trip or a slip to a sudden drop in blood pressure.

"Some medications, especially in combination, can cause slow reactions, impaired balance, dizziness or fainting so we’re encouraging local people to come for a quick meds check to understand their risk and get top tips to prevent a fall."

The team will be available at venues in Bradford, Bingley, Keighley, and Ilkley throughout the week.

Events include Canalside Health Care Centre in Bingley on Monday, September 21 from 10am to 1pm, and Keighley Healthy Living on Tuesday, September 22 during the same hours.

Most falls are not serious, but Department of Health and Social Care statistics show that during 2024-25, there were 221,600 emergency hospital admissions in England for people aged 65 and over following a fall.

The team will also offer advice on falls prevention and home safety.
