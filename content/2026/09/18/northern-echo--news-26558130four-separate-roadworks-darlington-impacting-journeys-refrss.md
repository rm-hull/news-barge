Four Darlington roads are affected by works, with restrictions continuing into October.

### Sadberge Road

Temporary traffic signals were introduced on Sadberge Road for water main work between Flat Hill Close and the A67.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 20 at 11pm.


### Auckland Oval

Construction is affecting Auckland Oval in both directions at A68 West Auckland Road.

Restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 22 at 11pm.

### Auckland Avenue

One lane traffic is in operation on Auckland Avenue in both directions between Brinkburn Road and Auckland Oval.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 22 at 11pm because of construction.

### Winchester Way

One lane traffic is in operation on Winchester Way in both directions at Rochester Way.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until October 15 at 11pm because of electricity work.
