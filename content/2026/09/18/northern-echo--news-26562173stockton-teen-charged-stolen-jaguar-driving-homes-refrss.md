A teenager has been charged after a stolen Jaguar was used to deliberately damage properties in Teesside.

Between 4.35am and 6am on Tuesday (September 15), two properties in Roseworth and Tilery in Stockton were deliberately rammed by a black Jaguar reportedly stolen the previous evening.

A 48-hour dispersal order was put in place in the Tilery and Portrack areas which ran from 7pm on Tuesday, September 15, to 7pm on Thursday, September 17.

After an investigation, an 18-year-old man was arrested and charged with aggravated vehicle taking, making off without payment and two counts of criminal damage.

Cleveland Police have confirmed he was remanded in custody before appearing at Teesside Magistrates' Court on Friday (September 18).

The charges follow another early-morning crash involving a stolen car in Billingham that same day.

Emergency services were called to Braemar Road at about 2.49am on Tuesday after a crash was reported.

Residents said a speeding car clipped a kerb, overturned, hit two parked vehicles and then crashed into a house.

A spokesperson for Cleveland Fire Brigade confirmed that two appliances from Stockton Fire Station attended the scene after a vehicle reportedly crashed into a property.

Cleveland Police said officers received reports that a car in Billingham had rolled, hit another car and been driven away at around 2am.

Officers later found the car on The Causeway, and discovered it was a car stolen earlier that night from a property in Norton.

Witnesses said one of the parked cars was pushed into a gas box, causing a small gas leak and prompting an emergency response from the fire service.

No injuries were reported in connection with any of the incidents involving the stolen vehicle.
