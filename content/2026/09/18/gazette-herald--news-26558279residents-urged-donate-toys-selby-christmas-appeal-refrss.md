A CHRISTMAS toy appeal is returning to help brighten the festive season for children in need.

Yorkshire Building Society’s Selby branch, on Gowthorpe, is once again collecting new toys, games and children’s gifts for the Selby Hands of Hope Christmas Toy Appeal, which supports families who may struggle to afford presents.

Vicky Wood, customer consultant at Yorkshire Building Society, said: "We're delighted to be supporting Selby Hands of Hope's Christmas Toy Appeal for a sixth year.

"The appeal provides invaluable support to local families each festive season, and we're proud to continue playing a small part in helping make Christmas special for children in our community."

The collection will run from October 1 until December 12, and donations can be dropped off during branch opening hours.

Richard Singh, chairman of Selby Hands of Hope, said: "We are incredibly grateful to Yorkshire Building Society in Selby for continuing to support our Christmas Toy Appeal."
