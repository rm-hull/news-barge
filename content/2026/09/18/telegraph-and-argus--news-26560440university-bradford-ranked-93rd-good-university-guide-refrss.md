THE University of Bradford has dropped in ranking in a prestigious guide.

The Times and The Sunday Times Good University Guide 2026 has been released today.

The University of Bradford ranks 93rd out of 133 – a fall of seven places from last year's list.

It also fell out of the top 10 in the North and North East rankings.

## Where did the other local universities come?

The news comes in a year where the university has climbed into the global top 500 in the QS World Rankings, entered the UK Top 30 in the Daily Mail Guide and came 51st in the Guardian Guide.

Looking at other universities in the area, Leeds placed 22nd (up four places), Leeds Beckett 73rd (up 10 places), Huddersfield 95th (up seven places), Leeds Arts 124th (down 21 places) and Leeds Trinity 125th (down 35 places).

Although the latter did win University of the Year for Student Experience.

## What has the Uni of Bradford said about the ranking?

A University of Bradford spokesperson said: "The University of Bradford continues to perform strongly across a range of national and international rankings.

"In the past year alone, we have climbed into the global top 500 in the QS World University Rankings, entered the UK Top 30 in the Daily Mail University Guide and are ranked 51st in the Guardian University Guide.

"We are particularly proud that, for the fifth consecutive year, the University has been ranked first in England on the Higher Education Policy Institute's Social Mobility Index, reflecting our long-standing commitment to widening opportunity and helping students from all backgrounds realise their potential.

"While league tables measure different things in different ways, together they demonstrate a university moving in the right direction, delivering excellent outcomes for students and continuing to make a significant contribution through education, research and innovation."

## Who topped the list?

Imperial College London was named The Times and The Sunday Times University of the Year 2027.

In the main academic rankings, it has risen three places from sixth to third this year.

St Andrews is the number one university in the UK in the main rankings, while for the second time in the guide's 33-year history both Cambridge and Oxford dropped out of the top three, ranking fourth and fifth respectively.

## How do the rankings work?

The Times and The Sunday Times Good University Guide 2027 provides the "definitive rankings for UK universities and the most comprehensive overview of higher education in Britain, making use of the latest data".

The guide provides prospective students and their families with the information to make an informed choice about their higher education, evaluating everything from satisfaction with teaching quality and the student experience to degree continuation rates and graduate employment prospects.

A 96-page supplement will be published with The Sunday Times on September 20.
