A BRADFORD salon has been crowned Hair Team of the Year at the 2026 Nation’s Salon Awards.

NewU Professional Hairdressing, which has served the community for 21 years, took home the top prize at the national event held in Birmingham on Sunday, September 6.

The awards celebrate excellence across the hair and beauty industry, recognising outstanding talent from salons across the UK.

Carmen Demeca, of NewU Professional Hairdressing, said: "On Sunday, September 6, our team was absolutely thrilled to win Hair Team of the Year at the Nation's Salon Awards, competing against top salons from all over the UK.

"To be recognised on a national level once again means the world to our entire team. It is an incredible achievement for our independent salon, especially as we have proudly served our local community for 21 years."

The salon was previously featured by the Telegraph & Argus after winning Hairdressers of the Year in 2025.

The ceremony took place at the Leonardo Royal Hotel Birmingham and was attended by leading salons, hair professionals, and beauty specialists from across the country.
