A BRADFORD man who used social media to engage in sexual conversations with children has been jailed for more than five years.

Aaron Grimshaw, 23, of no fixed abode, admitted communicating with three children online through social media platforms, with conversations of a sexual nature.

Following his arrest, officers seized Grimshaw's mobile phone and downloaded evidence of the communications, which formed part of the investigation.

At Bradford Crown Court on Thursday, Grimshaw was sentenced to five-and-a-half years in prison.

He was also ordered to sign the sex offenders register and given a sexual harm prevention order.

The court heard Grimshaw admitted multiple offences, including sexual assault of a female child, sexual activity with a child, engaging in sexual communication with a child and making indecent photographs of a child.

Grimshaw was also sentenced for separate offending in Gloucestershire.

## Statement from police

Following the sentencing, DC Emma Moist, of Bradford District's Safeguarding Unit, said: “Grimshaw’s sentence reflects the seriousness of his actions.

“Sexual communication with a child can cause significant harm to young people and their families.

“We remain committed to protecting children, supporting victims and bringing offenders to justice.

“We take all reports of this nature seriously and will investigate thoroughly."
