A speeding motorist has kept his driving licence after he told magistrates he could become an alcoholic again if he was banned.

Mark Ready, 61, of Pasture Lane, Hovingham, was clocked breaking a 30mph limit in Flaxton, between York and Malton, on December 23 last year, Harrogate magistrates heard.

He pleaded guilty to speeding.

Under the totting up procedure he faced at least six months off the road.

After hearing from defence barrister Halima Kamran, magistrates decided there was a "risk to the defendant’s mental health and potential for relapsing into alcoholism should he be unable to drive".

They also heard that he could be isolated if banned from the roads and would be unable to provide support for other AA members and a family member.

They decided this amounted to "exceptional hardship" and let him keep his licence, but gave him three penalty points.

They also fined him £122 and ordered him to pay £120 prosecution costs and a £49 statutory surcharge.
