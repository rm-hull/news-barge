Hundreds of former BrewDog staff are set to miss out on wages and holiday pay following the collapse of its retail arm.

An administrators’ report from AlixPartners revealed that there are “insufficient funds” to pay certain creditors, including nearly 500 affected workers.

The shortfall comes despite the sale of the BrewDog brand, intellectual property, UK breweries and 11 bars to US drinks giant Tilray in a rescue deal for about £33 million earlier this year.

The administration process saw the closure of 36 bars, and the takeover deal left around 200,000 crowdfunding investors with worthless shares.

According to the report, around £489,000 is owed in retail employee wage arrears and accrued holiday pay.

A further £2.4 million is owed to HMRC for unpaid VAT.

AlixPartners said: "There are now insufficient funds available to allow for a distribution to these preferential creditors.

"This is due to a reduced level of realisations for the retail business and increased costs during the administration period."

The report also noted that those made redundant were provided with information on government support.

BrewDog PLC owes around £190 million to unsecured creditors, who are expected to receive less than a penny in the pound.
