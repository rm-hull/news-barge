Grassroots footballers in Yorkshire will receive new kit and training equipment through a sponsorship from father-and-son McDonald’s franchisees investing in their community.

John and Thomas Atherton, owners of 13 McDonald’s restaurants across Yorkshire, are sponsoring Haxby FC’s Under-12s and Under-14s through their Monks Cross and Clifton Moor restaurants.

Thomas Atherton said: "It's great to be working alongside my dad to support such a fantastic local club.

  Haxby U14's 

*(Image: Supplied)*

"We hope the new kit and equipment give the players a real boost, and we wish both teams every success this season."

John Atherton said: "Supporting our local community has always been at the heart of what we do.

"Thomas and I are incredibly proud to be working together to support Haxby FC and invest in opportunities for young people."

The sponsorship is part of McDonald’s wider commitment to grassroots football.
