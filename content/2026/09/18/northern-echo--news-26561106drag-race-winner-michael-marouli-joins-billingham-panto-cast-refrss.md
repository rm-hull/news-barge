A North East-born drag star fresh from a history-making international TV win is set to swap the runway for the stage in Teesside this Christmas.

After becoming the first champion of RuPaul's Drag Race Down Under Vs The World, Michael Marouli will join Billingham Forum's 2026 Christmas pantomime, starring as the villainous Carabosse in this year's festive production of Sleeping Beauty.

  RuPaul's Drag Race star Michael Marouli will play Carabosse in Billingham's Christmas panto, Sleeping Beauty, this December. 

*(Image: Tees Active)*

The international drag star defeated Australian favourite Art Simone in the grand final of the global Drag Race competition on September 11, adding another major achievement to a career that has already seen them reach the final of RuPaul's Drag Race UK season five.

Off the back of global triumph, Marouli is now preparing to entertain audiences across Teesside throughout the festive season.

  Drag Race star Michael Marouli. 

*(Image: Tees Active)*

Marouli said: "Winning Drag Race Down Under vs The World has been an absolute dream come true.

"To celebrate that achievement and then return home to perform for audiences in the North East this Christmas makes it even more special.

"I can't wait to bring Carabosse to life in Sleeping Beauty.

"She's fabulous, fierce and just a little bit wicked, which sounds right up my street!"

The production will run at the Forum Theatre from December 2, 2026, until January 3, 2027.

  RuPaul's Drag Race star Michael Marouli will play Carabosse in Billingham's Christmas panto, Sleeping Beauty, this December. 

*(Image: Tees Active)*

The theatre said Marouli's international success has fuelled excitement around the annual pantomime, which promises spectacular costumes, magical effects, comedy and family entertainment.
