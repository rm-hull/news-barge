MORE than 100 content creators have registered their interest in a new Creator Studio opening in Bradford.

The studio, launching on September 19 at The Broadway as part of Bodycare’s latest Studio Store, is designed to give influencers, community groups, and customers a dedicated space to produce and share beauty content.

Charles Denton, CEO of Bodycare, said: "The proof of concept has been exceptional.

"More than 100 creators have already shown an interest in getting involved in Bradford, and the number continues to grow every day."

The Creator Studio will officially open at 1.20pm, with Bradford-born TV presenter Anita Rani cutting the ribbon.

Ms Rani will also co-host a South Asian Party Glam Masterclass alongside celebrity make-up artist Min Sandhu.

The session will be followed by a live audience Q&A.

The studio forms part of Bodycare’s wider Studio Store format, which combines beauty retail with experiences, technology, and community engagement.

Bradford is the fourth location to adopt the format, following openings in Sheffield, Derby, and Leicester.

Other opening day highlights include goody bags, a DJ, dancing, and beauty masterclasses.

Bodycare is inviting Bradford’s creators to get involved with the new Creator Studio.

Creators can register their interest by emailing [creators@bodycare.com.](mailto:creators@bodycare.com.)
