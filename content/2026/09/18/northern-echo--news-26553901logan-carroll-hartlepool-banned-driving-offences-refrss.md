A North East driver has been banned for three years and given a suspended prison sentence after admitting dangerous driving and three other motoring offences.

Logan Carroll, 21, from Kesteven Road, Hartlepool, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on September 11.

He admitted dangerous driving, driving without a licence or insurance, and failing to provide a specimen.

The offences happened on May 5 in Station Town and Spennymoor, County Durham.

Carroll drove an Audi A4 on the B1280 without a valid licence or insurance, then failed to provide police with a required blood sample.

Carroll was given a 24-week prison sentence, suspended for 24 months, and ordered to complete 300 hours of unpaid work for dangerous driving.

He was banned from driving for three years and must pass an extended test before getting back behind the wheel. Carroll must also pay £85 in prosecution costs.
