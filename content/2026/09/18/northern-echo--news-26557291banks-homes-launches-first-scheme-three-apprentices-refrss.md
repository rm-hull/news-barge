A County Durham housebuilder has launched its first apprenticeship scheme to train new talent and create careers in the property industry.

Meadowfield-based Banks Homes has taken on three apprentices: Amy Ward, James Rylance and Kaci Lonsdale.

Russ Hall, managing director at Banks Property and Banks Homes, said: “Investing in talent identification is a core part of the Banks Group’s longstanding ‘development with care’ approach, and we have trained many apprentices through our 50-year history who have gone on to enjoy very successful careers both with us and elsewhere.”

“Our first apprenticeship programme is the latest stage of this process and combines comprehensive on-the-job training with support of both the highly experienced Banks Homes team and outside institutions who’ve been specially chosen for the specialist expertise they can provide for each role.”

“We had an overwhelming response to all three positions, with Amy, James, and Kaci showing themselves to be outstanding candidates for the roles they’ve now taken up.”

Ms Ward is a commercial trainee at the company’s headquarters, Mr Rylance is a trainee assistant site manager and Ms Lonsdale is a trainee sales executive.

Mr Rylance and Ms Lonsdale will gain experience at Banks Homes developments across the North East and Yorkshire.

Banks Homes has partnered with Northumbria University, New College Durham and Sunderland College to provide tailored training.

The apprentices can also gain formal qualifications, including potentially achieving degree-level accreditation.

Founded in 2023, Banks Homes is expanding its residential portfolio across County Durham, the Tees Valley and North Yorkshire as part of the Banks Group.

More developments are planned later this year.

The apprenticeship scheme adds to the Banks Group’s graduate programme, which builds vocational, technical and professional skills through practical experience on live projects.

Mr Hall said: “We have an ambitious, long-term growth strategy in place and our new apprentices will play a hands-on role in its successful delivery while also developing themselves as both professionals and people.”
