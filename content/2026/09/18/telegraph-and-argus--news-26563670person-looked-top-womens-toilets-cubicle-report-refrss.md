POLICE have released an image of a man they would like to speak to following an alleged sexual offence at a business premises in West Yorkshire.

Detectives from Kirklees Safeguarding are appealing for help from the public to identify the man after a report that someone looked over the top of a cubicle in the women's toilets in Batley.

The incident is alleged to have taken place on June 25.

Officers believe the man pictured may be able to assist with their enquiries and are urging anyone who recognises him to come forward.

## Police statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Do you know this man?

"Detectives from Kirklees Safeguarding are appealing for the public's help to identify him following an incident where a man is said to have looked over the top of a cubicle in the women's toilets in business premises in Batley on June 25.

"If you can assist with identifying this man then please contact Kirklees Police via Live Chat | West Yorkshire Police, or on 101, quoting reference 13260360495.

"Information can also be reported anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555111 or online."
