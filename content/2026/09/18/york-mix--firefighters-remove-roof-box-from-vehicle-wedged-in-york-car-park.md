Firefighters stepped in after a car became wedged in a York car park.

It happened at the entrance to the Coppergate Centre Car Park on **Piccadilly** at**9.52am today** (Friday).

York firefighters removed a roof box from the vehicle to free it.

The crew used a reciprocating saw and hand tools. No injuries were reported at the incident.
