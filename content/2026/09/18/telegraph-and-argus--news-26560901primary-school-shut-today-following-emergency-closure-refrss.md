A BRADFORD primary school has been forced to shut today.

Baildon Church of England Primary School, on Coverdale Way, has closed this morning.

The closure was listed on Bradford Council’s emergency school closures page.

## Why is the school shut?

This is after the school lost its water supply.

The water has been cut off and is not expected to be restored for four to six hours.

A spokesperson for the school said: "As we currently have no water in school and Yorkshire Water don't anticipate the issue being resolved for 4-6 hours, school is closed today.

"Further details about work children can complete will be sent out shortly."

It comes after Newby Primary School, on Ryan Street, West Bowling, was shut on Thursday,

This was down to planned water supply works by Yorkshire Water.

It is understood Newby Primary has reopened today.

### Yorkshire Water provide statement

It is understood that the Baildon area is currently experiencing issues with the water supply.

Yorkshire Water said: "You may have no water or low water pressure at the moment.

"It may be cloudy or discoloured for a while when it comes back, but you can clear this by running your tap for a few minutes.

"We're aware of the issue and would like to apologise for the inconvenience caused.

"We aim to get to site and would expect supplies to be restored within 4 to 6 hours."
