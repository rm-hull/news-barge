Organisers of Bishop Auckland's Christmas Town event have cut the price of passes after confirming the event will go ahead.

It comes days after Christmas Town issued a statement confirming this year's event would return following the temporary suspension of bookings and pass sales.

The event is free to enter, but visitors can buy passes to access its rides, curing and tipis.

Organisers say the have cut the prize of the passes to between £5 and £10, having listened to concerns that last year's prices were too high.

Bookings reopened at 5pm on Thursday (September 17) and anyone who had bought a pass at higher prices previously is set to be automatically refunded the difference, organisers said.

The 'Golden Passes' will cost £5 on Thursday, £7.50 on Friday and £10 on both Saturday and Sunday.

Organisers said Saturday and Sunday passes are now 60 per cent cheaper than they were last year.

The pass includes unlimited rides on the event's helter skelter, twister, fun house and ferris wheel, access to the curling lane, entry to the tipis featuring live music and a festive bar, and a ten per cent discount at all bars.

Santa's Grotto will continue to be booked separately and will cost £5 per child and £2 for each accompanying adult.

Christmas Town co-founder and Baccanalia managing director Thomas Wales said the changes had been made in response to feedback from visitors.

He said: "People bought passes in their thousands last year, but some told us the price felt too high.

  Bishop Auckland’s Christmas Town


"We've listened, and we've been working on how to make this year more affordable.

"Christmas Town is a not-for-profit community event, but it costs close to £200,000 to put on.

"That covers the attractions and entertainment visitors enjoy, as well as less exciting things like power, toilets and staffing.

"We need to cover that cost while keeping passes affordable and making sure people can still come into town, browse the market and enjoy the atmosphere for free."

Mr Wales said strong sales last year and early interest in this year's event had helped give organisers confidence to reduce prices.

He added: "Last year's sales showed us the demand was there, and this year's early bookings have given us more confidence.

"We've been working through the numbers for some time so we could bring prices down and allow more people to take part.

"The support from the event's principal partner, Bishop Auckland Town Council, has made this possible, and we're looking forward to welcoming people back."

  Bishop Auckland’s Christmas Town


Organisers said visitors who had already purchased passes at the previous prices have automatically been refunded the difference.

Mr Wales added: "Every pass sold helps pay for this community event.

"The more people who buy one, the more scope we have to keep prices affordable and develop the event in future."

Christmas Town returns to Bishop Auckland Market Place from December 3 to 6.

The free-to-enter event attracts around 30,000 to 35,000 visitors each year and features Christmas markets, street entertainment, food, bars and festive attractions.

Organisers have warned that, with prices reduced significantly, daily allocations are expected to sell out and visitors are being encouraged to book early.
