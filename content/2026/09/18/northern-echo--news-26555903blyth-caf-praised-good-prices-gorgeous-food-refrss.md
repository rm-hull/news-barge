A trip to the seaside is a great idea even throughout the autumn and winter time.

You can spend the day walking along the beaches or enjoying some fresh fish and chips while watching the sea.

To warm yourself up after an autumnal walk, it might be a good idea to head to a local café for a hot coffee and slice of cake.

If you are taking a trip to Blyth any time soon, it could be worth stopping off at the highly rated Italian café, Caffe Ginevra.


## What is Caffe Ginevra in Blyth like?

Caffe Ginevra has four coffee shops around the north east in Blyth, Newcastle’s city centre, and in Prudhoe, where the brand opened its first two stores.

The Blyth location can be found just a few steps away from South Beach, making it a great option to grab a coffee for a beach walk or to relax after.

Caffe Ginevra blends its own coffee that you can either enjoy at the coffee shop or buy from the café’s website.

You can have a range of coffees including an espresso, flat white, cappuccino, and caffe latte.

The café also offers a few different hot chocolates and teas for those who are not a fan of coffee.

In terms of food, the café has lots of options for light lunches from focaccia toasties with different fillings to pizzas.


For those wanting something sweet, there are cakes, muffins, and even ice cream available to try.

Caffe Ginevra has been roasting its coffee since 1923 in the city of Caltanissetta in Central Sicily.

There are three special blends that are based on the three different locations of the café’s coffee shops.
