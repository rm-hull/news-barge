Rail services serving Bradford Interchange are beginning to return to normal this afternoon after a vehicle crashed into a railway bridge in Bramley.

Northern Rail said a road vehicle collided with a railway bridge on Friday afternoon, leading to disruption on routes linking Bradford with Leeds, Halifax, Manchester and Chester.

Network Rail engineers have now inspected the bridge and all lines between Bramley and Leeds have reopened, but Northern have warned that knock-on delays may continue until around 4pm today (September 18).

Passengers are being advised that some services may still be delayed, cancelled or altered at short notice while the timetable recovers.

## Which services are affected?

The disruption has affected trains calling at Bradford Interchange as well as stations including Low Moor, Halifax, and Leeds.

The operator has temporarily lifted ticket restrictions in affected areas, meaning passengers can use the next available Northern service to complete their journey where possible.

Advance and peak-time restrictions will remain suspended until the disruption officially ends.

Travellers are being advised to check before they travel as other train operators may also have been affected by the incident.

Anyone delayed by 15 minutes or more may be eligible for compensation through Northern's Delay Repay scheme.
