Three policing orders have been issued to people relating to their behaviour on a County Durham Street.

On Thursday (September 17), Durham Police issued Community Protection Warnings (CPWs) in relation to three people and their behaviour on Church Street in Seaham.

A CPW is a formal warning issued to an individual whose behaviour is having a persistent, unreasonable, or detrimental impact on the quality of life of people within the community.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "The warning outlines the specific actions the individual must take, or cease, and explains the potential consequences of failing to comply with these requirements.

"We will continue to listen to concerns from our local community and take appropriate action where individuals continue to engage in behaviour that negatively impacts others."
