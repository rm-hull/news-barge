A TEMPORARY closure will affect Station Road in Denholme due to gas works.

The road will be shut between its junction with Stradmore Road and outside No.12 Station Road.

The closure begins on September 21, at 8am and is expected to last until October 9, at 5pm.

However, the closure could last for up to 18 months if required.

This closure does not apply to vehicles involved with the gas works.

Pedestrians will still have access.

Alternative routes for vehicles include the unaffected sections of Station Road, Main Road, Knowles Street, and Stradmore Road.

A spokesperson for the council said: "NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council by reason of gas works to be executed on or near Station Road, Denholme has made the above-named Order."

The notice was issued by the Director of Legal and Governance for the council.
