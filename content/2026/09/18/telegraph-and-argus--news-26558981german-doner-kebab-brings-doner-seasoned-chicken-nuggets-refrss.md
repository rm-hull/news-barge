GERMAN Doner Kebab (GDK) has launched its first-ever chicken nuggets, now available in all UK restaurants nationwide including the Bradford branch.

The new Doner Seasoned Chicken Nuggets, made from 100 per cent chicken breast, are now on menus across the UK and bring GDK’s signature kebab flavour to a classic snack.

Chris Waters, chief marketing officer at GDK, said: "Chicken nuggets are a firm favourite, so we wanted to give them the GDK treatment and bring the bold, unmistakable flavour of our doner to a much-loved classic.

"Our new Doner Seasoned Chicken Nuggets are all about big flavour, and for huge value as part of GDK’s Fiver menu, reinforcing our commitment to delivering quality and distinctive flavour at an accessible price point."

The nuggets are available in three sizes: four for £2.99 with one dip, eight for £5 with two dips, and 20 for £11.99 with four dips.

Customers can also add four nuggets as a side to any order.

The launch is part of GDK’s Fiver menu, aimed at offering value meals for just £5.

Timed to coincide with Freshers Week, the new menu item is targeted at students seeking a quick snack between lectures, after studying, or to share with friends.
