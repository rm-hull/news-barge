In 2024, the Senate Department for Mobility, Transport, Climate Protection and the Environment began installing the green signposts. The signage was finally completed a few weeks ago. Since then, a white Berlin hiking bear, complete with rucksack and walking stick, has been showing tourists, locals and newcomers the city’s hiking trails.
