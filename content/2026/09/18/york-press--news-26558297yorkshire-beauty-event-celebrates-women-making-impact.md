YORKSHIRE women are leading the way in health, beauty, and wellbeing.

A standout event will take place in the region as part of British Beauty Week, returning for its third year.

This is Yorkshire Beauty will be held at Grantley Hall on Sunday, October 25, 2026, celebrating female founders, CEOs, and community leaders who are shaping the future of the sector.

The event brings a taste of the London-based British Beauty Week to Yorkshire and has quickly become a key date in the region’s beauty and wellbeing calendar.

Victoria Clapham, co-founder of This is Yorkshire Beauty, said: "We wanted to create an event that celebrates the incredible women, businesses and ideas we have here in Yorkshire, while also giving our audience the opportunity to learn from people who are genuinely making an impact.

"There is so much innovation and expertise happening outside London, and we wanted British Beauty Week to reflect that.

"Yorkshire has an incredible beauty, health and wellbeing community, and we’re incredibly proud to be waving the flag for the region once again."

This year’s theme is ‘IMPACT,’ focusing on the positive influence of women and businesses in the industry.

The speaker programme will feature some of Yorkshire’s most inspiring female voices, including CEOs, entrepreneurs, and health and fitness experts.

Jo Goodall-Thorne, co-founder of the women’s health app Luna, will discuss the importance of health education and body literacy for women and girls.

Nicola Glennon, CEO of Wellness & Aesthetics Technology, will join Dr Lubna Khan-Salim to explore the evolving role of technology in aesthetics and wellbeing.

The event will also address the power of community in supporting physical and mental health, and challenge traditional views on ageing and fitness.

Leeds-based trainer Caroline Asquith will offer practical advice for women on staying strong at every stage of life.

The event is designed as a full day of self-care and connection.

Guests will enjoy a two-course lunch at Grantley Hall, followed by workshops and masterclasses, including sessions on body confidence and clean makeup.

There will also be opportunities to try out the latest facial and body sculpting technology.

Lexus Leeds will offer complimentary test drives of its newest electric vehicles.

A beauty hall will showcase independent brands, most of them Yorkshire-founded.

Dr Lubna Khan-Salim, co-founder of This is Yorkshire Beauty, said: "For us, impact isn’t just about the conversations we have on stage.

"It’s about what people take away from the day, and how we can use beauty and wellbeing to create genuine connection in our communities."
