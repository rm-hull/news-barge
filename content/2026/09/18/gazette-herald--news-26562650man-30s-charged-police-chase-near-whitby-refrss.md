A man in his 30s has been charged with a string of motoring offences after his arrest following a police chase along a main North Yorkshire road.

The man was charged with theft of a motor vehicle, driving without insurance, failing to stop for police and driving whilst disqualified and is due to appear before court on Friday (September 18).

  


  A man in his 30s was arrested after the police chase along the A171 to Whitby 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

It comes after he was involved in the police chase, which begun on the A171 close to Whitby, following police reports relating to a “stolen” quad bike on Thursday (September 17).

As reported by The Press, a video of the incident was released by North Yorkshire Police of the pursuit.

A force spokesperson said: “Thanks for all the engagement with yesterday’s footage, which has now been viewed more than one million times.”
