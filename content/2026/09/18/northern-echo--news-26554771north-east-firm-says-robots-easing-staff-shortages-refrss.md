A North East technology firm says demand for its robots is helping businesses ease staff shortages, work more efficiently and free employees for other tasks.

Newcastle-based Spark Robotics says its machines are tackling repetitive, labour-intensive jobs across several industries.

The company says the machines free workers for other jobs while making daily operations more efficient and consistent.

Eric Guo, founder of Spark Robotics, said: "The aim is not to put people out of a job, but to empower them through technology."

"They are a perfect solution at sites like hotels, hospitality venues, logistics sites, shopping centres and large buildings where they manage repetitive work, have staff pressure, consistency requirements and high-volume routines.

"Spark Robotics helps organisations identify where robots can support those tasks in real working environments."

The robots deliver room service in hotels and carry out cleaning work in care homes, shopping centres and other large facilities.

Businesses including amusement arcades, garden centres, casinos, warehouses and logistics sites are also adopting them.

Spark Robotics grew out of New Apex Ltd, a company that supplies electronic point-of-sale equipment to the hospitality and leisure sectors.

Mr Guo said industries facing staff shortages and rising costs were turning to automation.

He said: "The robots have so many practical applications."

"In hospitality, robotics can support service teams with room delivery or repetitive item movement, while in cleaning, robotics can support teams responsible for large or high-footfall areas."

"The same applies in logistics and large-site operations, where robotics can support repeated movement, cleaning and other routines."

The company says its robots’ customisable designs are a major selling point.

Businesses can brand or “dress” the robots to match their image, making them both useful tools and talking points for customers.

Spark Robotics says it is growing rapidly and plans to move to larger premises in the autumn.

Mr Guo said: "We are proud of the rate of growth of the company and the fact that we continue to be able to offer so many solutions across a wide range of industries."
