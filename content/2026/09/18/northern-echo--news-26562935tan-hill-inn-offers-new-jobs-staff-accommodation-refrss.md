Britain's highest pub is on the hunt for new staff, offering the chance to live and work amid North Yorkshire's most spectacular countryside.

The Tan Hill Inn, near Swaledale, has launched a recruitment drive, and staff able to work surrounded by some of the Yorkshire Dales' most spectacular scenery.

From uninterrupted dark skies and sweeping Yorkshire Dales views to being surrounded by centuries of history, the team behind one of the country's most famous inns believes it can offer something a little different to people searching for a new job.

  The Tan Hill Inn


Known as the highest pub in the UK, the remote North Yorkshire inn welcomes visitors from around the world each year and has become a destination in its own right.

Now its owner, Andrew Hields, is looking for new staff to join the business, which also includes The Station Inn at Ribblehead and The Green Dragon Inn at Hardraw.

Around eight positions are currently available, including roles for chefs, housekeepers, bar assistants and front-of-house managers.

While some jobs are aimed at experienced hospitality workers, training will also be offered to suitable applicants who have not previously worked in the industry.

  The Tan Hill Inn


Mr Hields hopes the unique setting will help attract people looking for a change from more traditional hospitality roles.

He said: “The Tan Hill Inn is internationally renowned and we are constantly welcoming visitors from around the world.

“It's a picture-perfect location with no light pollution, stunning countryside and a really special place where we offer a fantastic experience.

“We're hoping that backdrop, and the strong packages we can offer staff, will appeal to people either currently working in the industry or looking for a start.”

The isolated location, which helps make the inn such a popular attraction, can also present recruitment challenges.

To help overcome that, the business offers accommodation for staff while they are working, alongside shift patterns designed to reduce travel demands.

Mr Hields said: “We recognise that for a lot of people travelling to and from the pubs each day is not easy, which is why we have specific shifts such as four days on followed by three days off.

  The view from Tan Hill


“We give accommodation for the days they are working and then that gives them three days when they can return home, which we also believe offers a great work-life balance.”

The Tan Hill Inn has become one of the most recognisable pubs in Britain thanks to its dramatic setting on the high Pennine moors.
