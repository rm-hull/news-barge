A SKIPTON family came together for a special birthday and raised much-needed funds for the Yorkshire Air Ambulance.

Juli Boocock turned 70 years old in May and celebrated in style with two parties over a fun-packed weekend.

Instead of presents, Juli asked friends and family, including her three daughters, Sally, Claire and Emma, that they donate money instead to the Yorkshire Air Ambulance.

And, together they raised a very impressive £1,000.

Juli said: “I have all I need and thought it would be a great opportunity to give something back to such a worthy cause and much needed service within our community.

“I am so grateful to everyone for their generosity.”

The air ambulance helicopters fly seven days a week, 365 days a year; each crew includes a pilot, a technical crew member, a consultant doctor with A&E experience and two specialist critical care paramedics.

They are funded by the generosity of the public through donations, grant funding, and the sale of previous helicopters.
