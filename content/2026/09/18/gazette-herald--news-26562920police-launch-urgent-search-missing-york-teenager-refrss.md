POLICE have launched an urgent search for a missing York teenager.

Rahim, 13, from the Holgate area, was last seen in the Cornlands Road area of York at 4.30pm on Thursday (September 17).

He is described as a black male, with short black hair, of medium build and roughly 5ft 2 inches tall.

  


A force spokesperson said: “He may be wearing a grey tracksuit top with black shorts.

“If you see Rahim, please call 999.

“If you have any other information that could help us find him, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101, and speak to the Force Control Room.

“Please quote 12260182984 when sharing information.”
