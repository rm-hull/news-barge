A bigger and better Home Bargains store will open its doors in the North East this weekend following months of expansion work.

Located in Spennymoor, just off Oxford Road, the expanded store will officially open on Saturday (September 19), and now includes an in-store café and nearly 1,000 square metres of additional retail space.

The discount retailer secured approval to extend into the former Iceland supermarket unit next door, transforming the site into a much larger shopping destination.

   

*(Image: Google Maps)*

Councillor Dean Ranyard said: "Good to see some new and revamped businesses opening in Spennymoor.

"The new bigger Home Bargains store which now includes a café opens this Saturday."

Builders knocked through the wall between the units and closed the former Iceland entrance.

The revamped store will now cover 1,705 square metres, making it almost 1,000 square metres larger than before.

The project was first unveiled last year when Home Bargains owner TJ Morris Limited submitted plans to take over the neighbouring unit.

The proposal was initially met with concern, with Iceland warning it could be forced to leave the area.

However, the supermarket chain relocated to new premises on Festival Walk in March, clearing the way for construction to begin.

Councillor Dean Ranyard said the expansion was part of wider improvements in the town centre.

  Iceland opened its new store on Festival Walk in March, clearing the way for the expanded Home Bargains site. 

*(Image: Steve Coulthard)*

He said: "The Post Office has recently relocated to the former Lloyds Bank premises, whilst Siesta Coffee brings a new offering to the town in the former John Willetts Fireplace building.

"After years of campaigning to have the dilapidated Festival Walk Shopping Precinct redeveloped, it's pleasing to see we now have a vibrant town centre offering catering for the everyday needs of local people.

"With a range of national chain stores and independent businesses, there are pleasingly few vacant retail units in the town centre."

The opening comes just days after independent café Siesta Coffee began trading nearby on Monday (September 14), in the former John Willetts Fireplace building.

Developers reassured locals at the start of the campaign that the nearby Post Office and Morrisons Daily, which share the site, would not be affected by the expansion.

The expanded Home Bargains store in Spennymoor will open to the public at 8am on Saturday.
