FRANKLIN Pele left Bradford Bulls under a huge cloud less than 18 months ago, but now he has been handed a remarkable second chance to star in the NRL.

Pele had been playing in the New South Wales Cup for South Sydney Rabbitohs in 2026, but he has impressed enough to be awarded a train and trial contract with the NRL side over pre-season.

If that goes to plan, he looks set to feature for Souths in 2027 at the highest level of the club game.

The big prop had previously featured for Cronulla Sharks and Canterbury Bulldogs in the NRL, but he made an ill-fated move to Hull FC in 2024, struggling badly in Super League.

A move to Bulls that summer seemed to reinvigorate him, but while he was out with an injury in May 2025.

The big prop negotiated a deal with a French third-tier rugby union side, while still under contract at Bradford.

Bulls released a statement at the time saying: “No Bradford Bulls player, nor individual, ever has been or ever will be bigger than the club itself.

“Any contracted player must fully be committed to representing the club, its badge and its supporters.

“Franklin Pele has, therefore, played his last match for our club."

“The club is considering its legal position and will be making no further comment at this stage.”

But Pele never returned to Bradford and now, he has a chance he must try and grab out in Australia.
