A MARQUEE company is celebrating 25 years of growth, innovation, and success.

Coopers Marquees, based at Willow Farm in Wilberfoss, York, has expanded from a regional operation into a national leader in temporary venue solutions since its founding in 1998 by managing director Jonathan Cooper.

The company is now marking its 25th anniversary with a look back at the achievements and partnerships that have defined its journey.

Jonathan Cooper, founder and managing director of Coopers Marquees, said: "We’re delighted to be marking a quarter-century of design ingenuity, major event partnerships, and continued investment.

"As Yorkshire’s premier marquee hire company, we’ve grown considerably, a far cry from the humble beginnings of our first push up traditional tent that was delivered in a borrowed cattle truck."

Coopers Marquees now delivers temporary venues for high-profile sporting fixtures, corporate galas, trade exhibitions, and luxury private events across the UK.

The company has earned a reputation for design excellence and reliability, supported by a dedicated team and long-standing client relationships.

Mr Cooper said: "Reaching 25 years is a tribute to the dedicated team in our yards, office, and installation crews who work in all weather conditions to bring our clients' visions to life.

"From our earliest days to building large-scale venues across the country, our goal has remained simple: deliver absolute reliability, design excellence, and an unforgettable experience for every client."

The company’s offering has expanded to include in-house luxury toilet hire, fencing, and event furniture through its integrated brands Mobaloo, Fence Hire, and Yorkshire Furniture & Events—making it a one-stop shop for large-scale event delivery.

Coopers Marquees is also fully accredited by MUTA, the UK trade association for marquee hire, and Mr Cooper has served on its executive committee to help raise industry standards.

As part of its anniversary celebrations, the company has launched a new Glass House Marquee called ‘The Orangery’ and has invested in customisable shipping containers to serve the growing demand for long-term commercial and temporary structure hires.

Mr Cooper said: "Our core isn’t going to change.

"Quality, reliability, and an absolute commitment to customer service will always be the foundation of everything we do.

"We are still a family business.

"I have had no greater pleasure than welcoming my eldest son into the business.

"Sam has the same work ethic and respect for what we do, couple this with fresh ideas and clear vision, we are set to evolve brightly into the future."
