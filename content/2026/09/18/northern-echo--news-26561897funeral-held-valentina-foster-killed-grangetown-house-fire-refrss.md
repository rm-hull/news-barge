Mourners dressed in pink lined the streets to say a final farewell to a seven-year-old killed alongside her aunt in a devastating house fire.

Valentina Foster’s funeral took place at St John's Church, Middlesbrough, on Friday (September 18), three weeks after the blaze which claimed her life and that of her aunt, Natalie McDonald.

White horses donned in pink drapes and plumes pulled a carriage carrying Valentina's pink coffin to the church, where crowds waited to pay their respects.

  The funeral of seven year old house fire victim Valentina Foster takes place at St John's Church in South Bank, Middlesbrough this morning


Many mourners wore pink T-shirts bearing a photograph of Valentina's face, while floral tributes reflected the personality of a little girl who was deeply loved by her family.

Among the arrangements were white flowers spelling out the word "granddaughter", pink butterfly wing displays, tiaras and Disney princess-themed tributes.

  The funeral of seven year old house fire victim Valentina Foster takes place at St John's Church in South Bank, Middlesbrough this morning


Valentina's coffin was carefully carried into the church by pallbearers as grieving relatives, friends and members of the community looked on.

The funeral comes weeks after Valentina, seven, and her aunt Natalie McDonald, 34, died following a fire at a house on Birchington Avenue in Grangetown on August 26.

Natalie was pronounced dead at the scene, while Valentina died later at James Cook University Hospital.

Pathologist Dr Hoggard concluded both died from the effects of the fire, inquests opened into both of their deaths heard previously.
