A new cultural venue combining a city library with digital experiences has opened in the North East, promising to inspire residents and visitors for decades.

Culture House, located in Sunderland’s Keel Square, officially opened its doors on September 12.

Billed as the National Centre for Creative Smart Cities, the venue houses Sunderland’s new library, an immersive digital gallery and interactive storytelling experiences.


Stuart Mckenna, managing director of Seeper, the London-based spatial storytelling studio behind the venue’s experience design, said: "When we were first asked to help with the audio-visual hardware for this building, it was obvious within minutes that hardware wasn't the answer.

"Culture House needed an experience, not a set of boxes on a wall.

"Five years on, we've designed the DNA of a building that will outlive all of us, one that will inspire, educate and entertain generations of people who haven't been born yet.

"This is exactly the kind of project we set the studio up to do."

Seeper spent five years working with Sunderland City Council to blend technology, storytelling and civic purpose in the building.

Seeper put visitors at the heart of the design, shaping the building’s architecture, systems and content from the start.

  Inside the new Culture House in Sunderland’s Keel Square, combining a city library with immersive digital experiences to create a landmark venue 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Chris Brown, creative director at Seeper, said: "We felt our job, through Experience Design, was to give Culture House a soul.

"A layer that's as much about sound, light and interaction as it is about what you see on screens.

"The Atrium holds one of Europe's largest indoor LED installations, and with the Immersion Gallery's spatial sound, we had the chance to do what we love most: dig into a place's origin stories and turn them into vivid worlds people can walk into.

  Inside the new Culture House in Sunderland’s Keel Square, combining a city library with immersive digital experiences to create a landmark venue designed to inspire residents and visitors for generations. 

*(Image: Supplied)*

"We didn't just fill a House with content.

"We helped turn it into a Home."

FaulknerBrowns Architects designed the building, with Seeper working alongside the team from concept to completion.

Seeper’s work ranged from 3D visualisation and virtual-reality walkthroughs to integrating audio-visual systems into the building.

  Inside the new Culture House in Sunderland’s Keel Square, combining a city library with immersive digital experiences to create a landmark venue 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Russell Hall, technical director (software) at Seeper, said: "We started with people, not technology.

"Everything in Culture House was designed around two human questions: what will move the visitor standing in the atrium, and what will let a small in-house team run this building with confidence for years to come?

"The technology came second – as the tool to meet those goals."

The result is a connected, interactive venue where every digital element—from the atrium’s giant screens to the 360-degree Immersion Gallery—works as part of one experience.

  Inside the new Culture House in Sunderland’s Keel Square, combining a city library with immersive digital experiences to create a landmark venue 

*(Image: Supplied)*

It also features creative technology labs, podcast and media studios, a teen zone, an Inventors Lab and a rooftop sky garden.

Digital features showcase Sunderland’s industrial heritage and community stories, including the Sunderland Stories touchscreen in City Eye.

Visitors can explore changing digital environments inspired by Sunderland’s coastline, glassmaking heritage and natural surroundings.

  Inside the new Culture House in Sunderland’s Keel Square, combining a city library with immersive digital experiences to create a landmark venue 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The atrium’s LED system runs on DigiLED’s energy-efficient ZEUS platform.

James Hensman, UK sales manager for DigiLED, said: "Culture House Sunderland is a superb blend of analogue and digital.

"Seeper have delivered a seamless AV experience across the learning spaces, art areas and immersive rooms, and it is a real testament to their creativity."

Culture House reimagines the library as a cultural space reflecting Sunderland’s industrial past and creative future.
