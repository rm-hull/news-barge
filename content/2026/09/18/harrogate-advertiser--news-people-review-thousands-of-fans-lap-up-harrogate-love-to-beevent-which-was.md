# Review: Thousands of fans lap up Harrogate Love to Be..event which was 'our best festival to date'

## TV celebrity Gok Wan has called a major Harrogate music event on the Stray his “favourite festival to play”

The Harrogate Stray welcomed a host of big name, talented DJs to the Love to Be event on a sunny Saturday, September 4 when festival- goers in their masses celebrated an end of summer with a night and day of House music.

Image consultant, author and DJ Gok Wan told me afterwards: “Of all of the festivals I’ve played, I call this one ‘the smiler’ everyone always smiles so much.

"It’s my favourite festival to play.”

![The crowds on the Harrogate Stray welcomed a host of big named talented DJs to the Love to Be...Festival. (Picture Nick Brooker)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjAxYjk0ZDZhLTM3NzQtNDlkNC1iMzYwLTQyNmJiNzdhYmI3NzpmMjBiMDE5Yy1jMGJhLTRjODQtODRhNy0wMmQ1YjEyZDA4ZGQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Artists performing included Trimtone, Cash Only, Gok Wan, KKlass, Sam Devine, Claptone, Grant Nelson to name but a few.

Not only was the weather on point but the music was exceptional across all three stages which included: a Main Stage, Music Factory Stage and a VIP arena.

Speaking to me afterwards, Love to Be co-organiser and DJ, Tony Walker said: “This was without a doubt our best festival to date.

"The weather gave us some extremely tough challenges during the build but our team pulled off an amazing feat to overcome these.

"The music throughout the day was superb showing how strong our brand of house music really is.

"But year on year its the vibe and our love to be family who really make this such a special event.

"The generations of happy faces united on our dancefloors by their love of house music really is the magic that is Love to be...

"The Stray in Harrogate is the perfect home for our flagship event and we cant wait for you all to see what we have in store for you in 2027 with early release and sign up discounted tickets now available on Skiddle.”

The event started from midday and I watched the venue quickly fill up with festival goers in the most amazing outfits.

The event finished around 11pm and energy did not deter.

The high energy day and night was well received by all.

I spoke to many festival-goers who had travelled from all over North and South Yorkshire, Chesterfield, and Manchester to attend this amazing annual musical event.

I was seriously impressed with the ticket prices.

The VIP tickets were exceptional value with added extras of glitter tattoos, a private bar and copious private toilet facilities – it was sheer luxury. The festival also catered for all with multiple choices of food vendors selling chips, burgers, hot dogs to Greek food.

I was also fortunate to speak to one of my favourite DJ’s – Mousse T ahead of the event.

“I love my Love To Be boys cos they really know their house music and they put on amazing event,” he said

I also managed to obtain multiple lovely comments about the event and the organiser Marc Dennis and Tony Walker.

Sam Devine said:”The crew and the crowd really know their house music .

"We are squeezing the last bit of summer out of the day, so Its going to be an absolute banger of a set today’.

K Klass said: “ ‘Love To Be is a brand we have been associated with for years.

"They have an amazing music policy.

"We’ve watched it grow and grow over the years.

"It’s incredible to see them go from strength to strength. It going to be an incredible day!

The event was a complete success from start to finish.

I, for one ,cannot wait to the next Love to Be event on Saturday, October 7 in Sheffield at the Hangr Warehouse.
