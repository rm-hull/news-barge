One vehicle ended up on its roof after a crash this afternoon (Friday).

It happened at **2.54pm** at**Thornton-le-Street** north of Thirsk.

Fire crews from Northallerton and Thirsk went to the two-vehicle collision.

#### **Thornton-le-Street**

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The occupant of the vehicle on its roof had self extricated with the support of fire crews.

“All drivers were checked over by paramedics and taken to hospital via road ambulance.

“Crews made the vehicles safe before it was left in the hands of police.”
