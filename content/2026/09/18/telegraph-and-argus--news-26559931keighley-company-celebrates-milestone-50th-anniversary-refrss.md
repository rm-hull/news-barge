A COMPANY is celebrating 50 years of business.

Marrose Abrasives, based in Keighley since 1976, has grown from a small local operation into an international supplier of abrasive products serving customers across the UK and abroad.

Sarah Beaumont, a spokeswoman for Marrose Abrasives, said: "We've been based in Keighley since 1976 and, over the last 50 years, have grown from a local business into an international company, supplying abrasives to customers across the UK and around the world.

"We are proud of our Keighley roots and thought it would be lovely to share our 50-year story with the local community.

"It’s quite something to think that a business which started here in Keighley has spent the last five decades building connections all over the world – putting Keighley on the map along the way.

"It would be great to celebrate this milestone locally and give a bit of recognition to the people who have helped make Marrose what it is today."

The company will mark the occasion at glasstec in Düsseldorf, one of the biggest events in the glass industry.
