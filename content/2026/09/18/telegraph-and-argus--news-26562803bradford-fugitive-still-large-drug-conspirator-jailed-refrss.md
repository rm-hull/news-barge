Police are still hunting a Bradford drug trafficker after another key figure in a Covid lockdown heroin conspiracy was jailed.

West Yorkshire Police renewed their appeal for information about Shafiqul Islam Ali after co-conspirator Yakub Miah was sentenced at Leeds Crown Court on Friday, September 18.

Miah, 53, also known as Mohammed Kazi, was jailed for 14 years after being convicted of several Class A drug offences linked to a major heroin trafficking operation.

Following sentencing, Detective Inspector Chris Rukin, who led the investigation, said:

“Yakub Miah was a key player in this very significant criminal enterprise.”

He continued: “He tried to cheat justice by fleeing abroad but the tenacity of the investigation team and support from our colleagues in Ireland saw him detained and brought back to the UK to face justice.”

The case involved 20kg of heroin moved from Birmingham to Leeds between April and May 2020.

## Arrest warrant remains in force

While Miah is now behind bars, police say Ali, who has strong links to Bradford, remains on the run after being sentenced in his absence.

Ali was not present when he was convicted at a 2024 trial of conspiring to supply 2kg of heroin, possessing cocaine with intent to supply and possessing criminal property.

He failed to attend Leeds Crown Court for sentencing in January 2025 and was given a 14-year prison sentence in his absence.

A warrant remains in force for his arrest.

Police describe Ali as an Asian man, around 5ft 7in tall, of slim build, with short black hair and brown eyes.

DI Rukin said: “We are determined to see that everyone who was involved answers for their actions, and we continue to appeal for anyone who has any information that could assist us in locating Shafiqul Islam Ali who remains wanted after being sentenced in his absence.”

## Jailed man traced to Ireland

The investigation uncovered evidence linking Miah to the wider drugs operation.

Detectives found encrypted messages, including a photograph showing packages of heroin stacked inside his flat in Newton Park Court, Leeds.

Miah was also linked to a drugs exchange in Leeds in May 2020 involving a taxi from Leicester and a Mercedes.

After watching the vehicles, officers stopped the taxi and recovered 2kg of heroin.

Officers later seized more heroin, cocaine and crack cocaine during a search of his home in May 2021.

Miah was arrested in July 2020 but fled the country while under investigation.

Officers traced him to Ireland, where he was arrested in January before being extradited to the UK the following month.

Anyone with information can contact West Yorkshire Police via Live Chat, quoting reference 13200327588, or anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
