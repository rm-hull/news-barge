The Guy Fawkes Arms in Scotton has won a Tripadvisor Travellers' Choice award for the seventh consecutive year.

The award places the pub among the top 10% of venues worldwide on Tripadvisor.

A spokesperson said:

> “We’ve done it again, 7 years in a row now.
>
> “Huge thanks to our team and every single customer who visits us and takes the time to leave us a kind review.”

The Guy Fawkes Arms is a country pub on Main Street in Scotton, near Knaresborough.

It is owned and run by husband and wife Adam and Danielle Vear, who took over in June 2019.

The building dates from the 17th century and takes its name from Guy Fawkes, who lived in the village during his teenage years.
