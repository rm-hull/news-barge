A man has been arrested after an assault in Stockton.

Police were called to Bishopton Road around 9.15pm on Thursday (September 17).

A 50-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of GBH with intent.

He remains in police custody.

A spokesperson for Cleveland Police said: "Anyone with information is urged to contact us on 101, quoting reference number 187356."
