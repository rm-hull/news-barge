A SHOPPING centre is set to launch a new eco-friendly fashion event.

The Broadway, a retail hub in the heart of Bradford, will host the sustainability-focused showcase on Saturday, October 3, from 11am to 5pm.

The event encourages visitors to refresh their style while making environmentally conscious choices.

Ian Ward, general manager at The Broadway, said: "With sustainability becoming an increasingly important part of everyday life, our 2026 fashion event aims to empower visitors to make small changes that collectively have a big impact.

"The centre hopes to show that sustainable fashion doesn’t mean compromising on style, and that thoughtful wardrobe habits can sit comfortably alongside the brilliant retail offer available at The Broadway."

Instead of traditional catwalks, the event will offer hands-on experiences across two main zones.

The ‘style garden’ will provide one-to-one styling sessions, while the ‘repair and personalise’ area will offer opportunities to embroider, patch, and customise clothing.

Both zones are designed to help shoppers extend the life of their wardrobes in creative and sustainable ways.

No booking is required to attend the event.

The Broadway, which opened in 2015, is home to more than 90 shops, cafes, and restaurants, and has played a key role in Bradford’s ongoing regeneration.
