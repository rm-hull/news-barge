A BRADFORD councillor has urged young people to take care on electric scooters and bikes after a teenager was left fighting for his life following a serious crash.

Councillor Talat Sajawal (Little Horton, Your Bradford Independent Group) said he was "deeply saddened" to learn of the incident, which happened on Canterbury Avenue in Bradford on Thursday evening.

An 18-year-old man was taken to Leeds General Infirmary with life-threatening injuries after the single-vehicle collision involving an e-scooter at around 7.35pm.

Cllr Sajawal said his thoughts were with the injured teenager and his family as he remains in hospital.

  Councillor Talat Sajawal

He said: "I am deeply saddened to hear about the serious e-scooter accident involving a young person from our community who is now fighting for his life in hospital.

"My thoughts and prayers are with him, his parents, and his entire family during this incredibly difficult time. I cannot imagine what they are going through."

No further update on the teenager's condition has been provided.

## 'Think about your safety and the safety of others'

The councillor also used the incident to remind young people of the importance of staying safe when using the vehicles.

He added: "I also want to gently remind all our young people - please be safe on e-scooters and e-bikes. These machines are powerful and accidents can happen in a second.

"Please wear a helmet and think about your safety and the safety of others."

## What police said

West Yorkshire Police are continuing to investigate the collision and have appealed for witnesses to come forward.

In a statement, a spokesperson for the force said: "We are appealing for witnesses following a single vehicle collision on Canterbury Avenue, Bradford yesterday (17 September) at around 7.35pm involving an E-scooter.

"An 18-year-old man has been taken to Leeds General Infirmary to be treated for life-threatening injuries."

Police said enquiries remain ongoing and are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the collision or saw the e-scooter before the incident took place.

Officers are also appealing for any dashcam, CCTV or mobile phone footage that may assist their investigation.

Cllr Sajawal urged anyone with information to contact police.

Anyone who can help is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police on 101 or via the force's online reporting service, quoting reference 1565 of September 17.
