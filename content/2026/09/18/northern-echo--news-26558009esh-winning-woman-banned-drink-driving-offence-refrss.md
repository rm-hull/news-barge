A County Durham woman caught drink-driving at more than one-and-a-half times the legal limit has been banned from driving.

Kelly Wilson, 47, of Esh Winning, admitted drink-driving a BMW on Front Street, Quebec, County Durham, on May 17.

A breath test found 54 microgrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres of her breath, well above the 35-microgramme limit.

Wilson was banned from driving for 14 months.

Her ban can be cut by 14 weeks if she completes a drink-drive rehabilitation course by June 4, 2027.

She was fined £576 and ordered to pay £85 in costs, plus a £230 victim surcharge.
