POLICE have issued an update after a reported serious sexual assault in a York city centre alleyway.

The alleged incident is said to have happened in the early hours of Thursday (September 17) in the alleyway linking Spurriergate with Church Lane.

Police said the alleged victim was being supported by specialist officers.

North Yorkshire Police arrested a 22-year-old man who was being held in police custody as part of investigations.

A force spokesperson said now said a suspect has been released on conditional bail while its enquiries continue.

  


The area was cordoned off on Thursday with police officers at the scene asking people to avoid the area.

A worker at a local bakery said she first noticed the police presence when she arrived for work at 6.30am.

North Yorkshire Police said anyone with information is asked to call them on 101 and quote reference 12260182374.
