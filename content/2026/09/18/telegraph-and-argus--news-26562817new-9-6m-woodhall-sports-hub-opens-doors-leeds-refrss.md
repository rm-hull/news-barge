A NEW multi-million pound sports hub has opened near Bradford.

The £9.6 million Woodhall Sports Hub, backed by Leeds City Council and the Football Foundation, opened to the public this past Saturday.

Located off Bradford Road, near Pudsey, the 8.3-hectare facility features three full-sized floodlit 3G pitches, two grass pitches, outdoor play areas, and a pavilion with a café, meeting space, and changing rooms.

## 'Fantastic asset'

Councillor Salma Arif, deputy leader of Leeds City Council and executive member for economy, said: "The Woodhall Sports Hub is a fantastic asset for Leeds and I’m absolutely delighted that it is now open.

"We’re determined, as a council, to do everything we can to improve the health and well-being of people across the city, with schemes like Woodhall having a key part to play in helping us reach that goal.

"I’m sure the new pitches will be a big hit with all those using them, while the site’s other facilities – such as its play areas and the pavilion café and meeting space – mean this is a resource that will also benefit the wider community."

## What the Football Foundation has said

The sports hub was funded in partnership with the Premier League, the FA, and the Government’s Football Foundation, which contributed £5.4 million through its Hubs programme.

Aimed at tackling health and well-being inequalities by promoting sport and physical activity, the facility is part of the council’s wider Leeds Ambitions plan.

Robert Sullivan, chief executive of the Football Foundation, said: "We’re delighted that the local community across west Leeds will now be able to better access the many benefits of grassroots sports thanks to the new facilities at the Woodhall Sports Hub.

"Our Hubs programme provides safe and accessible facilities that have a transformative impact on physical and mental health and bring people together, through football and other sports and activities, to strengthen local communities."

## 'Much-needed, high-quality pitches'

The hub is managed by the National Football Trust and operated by the Leisure United charity.

Surplus income from the hub will be reinvested into outdoor sports provision across the city.

Mark Coulson, chief executive officer of Leisure United, said: "This fantastic new £9.6 million facility provides much-needed, high-quality pitches.

"It’s rewarding to help provide greater capacity for grassroots sport in Calverley and the surrounding communities.

"As a not-for-profit operator, we’re also proud that surplus income from the Hub will be reinvested into outdoor sports provision across Leeds, helping more people and groups benefit in the years ahead."
