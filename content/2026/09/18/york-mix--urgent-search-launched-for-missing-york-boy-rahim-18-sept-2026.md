An urgent search is underway for a boy missing from his York home.

Rahim, 13, is from the Holgate area of the city.

He was last seen in the Cornlands Road area of York at 4.30pm yesterday (Thursday.

He is described as

- a black male
- with short black hair
- of medium build
- and roughly 5ft 2ins tall.

He may be wearing a grey tracksuit top with black shorts.

If you see Rahim, please call 999.

If you have any other information that could help police find him, call them on 101 and speak to the Force Control Room.

Quote 12260182984 when sharing information.
