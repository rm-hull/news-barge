Charities in Chippenham can now apply for up to £20,000 in funding to improve community spaces.

The support comes from the new Connecting Communities fund, launched by the Morrisons Foundation to help organisations upgrade facilities that bring people together.

The funding is aimed at charities operating spaces such as village halls, youth clubs and community centres.

It also supports charities that provide place-based support in communities, including warm hubs for the elderly, school PTAs and hospices.

Mel Cook, community champion at the Morrisons store in Chippenham, said: "It’s wonderful that we’re launching this new fund to help charities in our local community.

"Thanks to the Connecting Communities fund, charities will have the opportunity to improve their facilities, making a direct, positive impact right here on our doorstep.

"I’m looking forward to working together with the Morrisons Foundation and local charities to support the local causes that matter most to our community."

The grants are intended for capital improvements, including new equipment, essential maintenance, or refurbishments.

David Scott, Chair of the Morrisons Foundation, said: "Thriving communities are built on the strength of their shared spaces but funding to sustain them is in short supply.

"Our new Connecting Communities grants will breathe new life into community facilities that bring people together and provide important local services."

Since launching in 2015, the Morrisons Foundation has donated almost £50 million through community grants and matched the fundraising of Morrisons colleagues, supporting thousands of charities across England, Scotland and Wales.
