Raby Castle is looking to recruit festive elves and event support staff for its Christmas experience.

The seasonal roles are part of a wider recruitment drive at the County Durham attraction, set in 200 acres of historic deer parkland near Staindrop.

Dating back more than 600 years, the medieval castle draws visitors from across the region throughout the year, with the festive season proving especially popular thanks to its Christmas events and family activities.

   

*(Image: Sarah Caldercott)*

Seasonal staff will work from November through to Christmas Eve, welcoming thousands of visitors during one of the estate’s busiest periods.

Weekend work is required.

Raby Castle wants enthusiastic, energetic staff to create a memorable experience for families.

Seasonal actors will play festive elves and bring the attraction’s stories to life, while event staff deliver activities and keep visitors’ experience running smoothly.

The recruitment advert says candidates need “boundless energy”, enjoy meeting visitors and want to make Christmas magical for guests of all ages.

   

*(Image: Chris Booth)*

Successful applicants will receive barista and event training, free parking, employee assistance and ongoing development.

Staff will also get discounts at Raby Estate’s shops and food outlets, reduced rates at High Force Hotel and free entry to attractions at Raby Castle and High Force Waterfall.

Other perks include a behind-the-scenes castle tour and invitations to estate social events throughout the year.

Raby wants people who work well in a team, thrive in customer-facing roles and can help deliver a memorable Christmas experience.

The seasonal jobs offer the chance to work at one of the North East’s best-known heritage sites and support its popular Christmas programme.

More information and application details are available on the Raby Estate careers website.
