# Where and when Spitfire is scheduled to fly over Yorkshire this weekend - location and time window details

## A Battle of Britain Memorial Flight aircraft is scheduled to fly over Yorkshire this weekend.

A Spitfire from the Battle of Britain Memorial Flight (BBMF) is scheduled to fly over East Yorkshire this weekend.

The Flight operates five Spitfires, two Hurricanes, a Lancaster, a C47 Dakota, and two Chipmunk aircraft (primarily for training).

The Spitfire is expected to fly over Fort Paull between 1.40pm and 2.10pm on Sunday 20 September for the second day of their Multi Period Living History Weekend.

The flights can be altered or cancelled due to poor weather conditions or other requirements.

![A Spitfire during a flying display at the Biggin Hill Festival of Flight on August 19, 2017 in Biggin Hill, England.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/18/15/33/GettyImages-835537982.jpg?trim=0,59,0,59&width=640&quality=65)



The Battle of Britain Memorial Flight (BBMF) operates from RAF Coningsby in Lincolnshire. The Royal Air Force write: “The mission of the RAF BBMF is to maintain the priceless artefacts of our national heritage in airworthy condition in order to commemorate those who have fallen in the service of this country. The RAF BBMF also serves to promote the modern day Air Force and to inspire the future generations.”

The Battle of Britain Memorial Flight has displayed at a number airshows and events this summer. In addition to the full displays, the BBMF also conducts up to 900 flypasts each year to make the most of the flying hours and transits to and from display locations.

The display season runs from May to September each year, with pre-season work-up flying for the crews taking place during April, culminating in Public Display Approval being sought for each pilot from the Air Officer Commanding No 1 Group.
