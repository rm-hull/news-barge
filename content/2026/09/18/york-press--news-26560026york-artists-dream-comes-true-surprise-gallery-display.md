An aspiring artist's lifelong dream of seeing his work displayed in a professional gallery has come true thanks to a surprise collaboration between a renowned artist and a popular social media storyteller.

Alan Hawkins, from York, was featured on the Instagram channel of Evan BNormal, who has more than 329,000 followers and shares inspiring real-life stories.

Mr Hawkins, who spent many years caring for others - including his late mother - returned to painting later in life.

Evan BNormal teamed up with Lucy Pittaway, an award-winning artist, to help make Mr Hawkins’ dream come true.

Ms Pittaway said: "When we heard Alan's story, we knew we wanted to be involved.

"So many people put their own passions on hold while caring for others, and Alan's dedication to his family is truly inspiring.

"To showcase his work was something we were delighted to do.

"We hope it inspires others to keep pursuing the things they love, no matter their age or circumstances.

"Alan himself says he likes to paint subjects that make people smile.

"That’s a sentiment very close to my heart and myself and the team are thrilled to share his work."

Ms Pittaway and her team selected one of Mr Hawkins’ paintings, had it professionally framed through their in-house service, and created a biography display to share his journey with gallery visitors.

He was invited to the gallery without knowing the real reason for his visit.

On arrival, he was surprised to see his artwork hanging on the wall.

His emotional reaction was captured on film and has since been shared online with tens of thousands of viewers.

Mr Hawkins said: "It was quite a surprise to see my work on display in the gallery.

"It was incredible really, unbelievable.

"It felt like a dream and it was overwhelming.

"I’d like to thank people for their interest in me any my story."

His painting will remain on display at the Lucy Pittaway gallery in York until the end of the week.

While it is not for sale, the exhibition gives Mr Hawkins the opportunity to see his art professionally showcased as a tribute to his talent and perseverance.

Moved by Alan's story, Evan wanted to show both Alan and others that it is never too late to pursue what you truly love.
