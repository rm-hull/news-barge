A TOP comedian is heading to York – for one night only.

Legendary comic Jack Dee is set to perform in York next year.

The show, Jack Dee: Jack’s Joke Show, will take place at York Theatre Royal on Friday, May 28, 2027.

It forms part of the theatre’s latest programme for the coming year, which also includes the return of two previously sold-out shows.

A spokesperson for York Theatre Royal said: "Celebrating 40 years in stand-up comedy, Jack takes us on a memoir of all his favourite old stories.

"Jokes and funny anecdotes have intrigued Jack for as long as he can remember.

"Now he has assembled the best of them to form a new show that offers a glimpse of the influences that led him into and helped shape his comedy career."

  Jack Dee's Joke Show is coming to York Theatre Royal next year 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The show is recommended for audiences aged 14 and above due to swearing and adult content.

The latest season also marks the return of Ronnie Scott’s Jazz Club: The Ronnie Scott’s Story, which takes to the stage on Friday, January 22, 2027.

The show, performed by The Ronnie Scott’s All Stars, celebrates the legacy of the world-famous London jazz club through live music, narration, rare archive photos, and video footage.

Set in the dive bars and jook joints of London’s Soho, the show explores the club’s early financial struggles, frequent police raids, and interactions with gangsters, including the notorious Kray twins.

  Ronnie Scott's Jazz Club: The Ronnie Scott's Story - at York Theatre Royal in January 

*(Image: Shawn Pearce)*

A York Theatre Royal spokesperson said: "We hear how Ronnie's became neutral ground within rife gang territory, as well their scrapes with gangsters including the Krays – who were rumoured to have taken Ronnie Scott ‘for a little drive’."

The show will feature music from legendary jazz artists who performed at Ronnie Scott’s, along with stories of old Soho, celebrity visitors, and the colourful characters who shaped the club’s history.

Returning the following night is Darrell Smith & the Soul Brothers Syndicate, who will perform on Saturday, January 23, 2027, with their Northern Soul Orchestra.

  Darrell Smith - heading to York Theatre Royal 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Their narrated timeline-style show traces the origins of Northern Soul from its early days at the Twisted Wheel in Manchester and the Golden Torch in Tunstall, through to the Blackpool Mecca and the iconic Wigan Casino.

The band will perform classic Northern Soul tracks including The Drifter, The Night, I’m On My Way, There’s A Ghost in My House, and Out on The Floor.

Darrell Smith & the Soul Brothers Syndicate recently topped the R&B iTunes downloads chart with their debut single The Drifter, and their latest release, The Night, reached number one in the R&B downloads and number 25 on the Official Singles Chart within 36 hours.

To book tickets or for more information, visit www.yorktheatreroyal.co.uk.

The box office is also available by phone at 01904 623568.

Tickets for all three shows are available via general booking from 1pm today (September 18).
