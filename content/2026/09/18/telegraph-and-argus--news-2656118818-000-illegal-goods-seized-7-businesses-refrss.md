Illegal goods worth around £18,000 have been seized in a crackdown on crime.

Seven businesses in Keighley were searched during a joint operation on Thursday, September 10, involving West Yorkshire Trading Standards and Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team.

A large quantity of illicit products was discovered and confiscated.

### Full police statement

  Some of the illegal good seized in Keighley 

*(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "On Thursday, officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team assisted West Yorkshire Trading Standards with a joint operation targeting the sale of illegal and illicit products in Keighley.

"Seven different businesses were subjected to a search, during which approximately £18,000 worth of goods were located and seized.

"This will now be followed up by Trading Standards with the potential for closure orders to be sought."
