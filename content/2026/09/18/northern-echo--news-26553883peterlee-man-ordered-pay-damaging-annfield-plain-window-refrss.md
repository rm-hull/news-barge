A County Durham man must pay compensation after admitting he damaged a housing provider’s property.

Alan Metcalfe, 27, formerly of Paradise Lane, Peterlee, pleaded guilty at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court to smashing a Dash Housing double-glazed window in Annfield Plain on November 28, 2025.
