Police cordoned off a County Durham high street on Friday afternoon with officers seen sweeping the road as part of an "ongoing investigation".

Around 4.30pm today (September 18), at least eight Durham Police officers were spotted on the Front Street in Consett, near a Subway sandwich shop.

A large police cordon was put up, blocking off several businesses.

  A crime scene cordon was put up at the scene 

*(Image: Joshua Nichol)*

  The incident caused traffic and transport delays. 

*(Image: Joshua Nichol)*

Front Street was closed in both directions between the junctions with Albert Road and Medomsley Road.

The incident left bus routes diverted and queuing traffic during rush hour.

Durham Police issued a statement, warning people to avoid the area.

  Queuing traffic as police rushed to the scene in Front Street 

*(Image: Joshua Nichol)*

A spokesperson for the force confirmed: "Front Street, in Consett, has been closed as part of an ongoing police investigation.

"The road is currently closed in both directions between the junctions with Albert Road and Medomsley Road.

"We'd advise people to avoid the area and find an alternative route if they are travelling in the area."

The incident left residents and business owners baffled.

At approximately 4.38pm, a local business owner said: “They’ve been here about an hour.

“There was a line of officers sweeping the street. I’m not sure what they are looking for.

“We’ve had cannabis raids before but this is different to that.”

A resident, who wishes to remain unnamed, added: "I don’t know what’s happened, but there were five or so officers sweeping the path.

"It looked like they were looking for evidence."

The nature of the incident is not known at this time.

Cordons remain in place.

Durham Police has been contacted for further information.
