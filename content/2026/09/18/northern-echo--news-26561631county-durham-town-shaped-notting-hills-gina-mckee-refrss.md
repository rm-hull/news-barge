Before she became one of Britain's most respected actors, Gina McKee grew up in one of the newest towns in County Durham.

Best known for her award-winning role as Mary Cox in Our Friends in the North, McKee spent her formative years in Peterlee, a town built on ambition, community spirit and the heritage of east Durham's mining villages.

## A town built for a new future

Peterlee is unlike most towns in County Durham.

Established as a new town after the Second World War and named after miners' leader Peter Lee, it was designed to provide modern homes and opportunities for families from the surrounding coalfield communities.

Today, Peterlee is home to more than 20,000 people and is known for landmarks including the Apollo Pavilion, nearby stretches of the Durham Heritage Coast and its rich mining history.

While many visitors know Peterlee for its distinctive architecture and location between Sunderland and Hartlepool, it has also produced one of the North East's most acclaimed acting talents.


## Gina McKee's Peterlee roots

Born in 1964, McKee was raised in Peterlee and nearby Easington after being born into a mining family.

Her father worked in the coal industry, giving her first-hand experience of the communities that would later inspire some of her most celebrated performances.

She attended school locally and discovered acting while still a teenager.

According to published biographies, McKee joined a local drama group after spotting a poster in a shop window.

What began as a hobby quickly developed into a passion and eventually led to her first professional screen role in the Tyne Tees children's series Quest of Eagles.


## From East Durham to television success

McKee spent several summers with the National Youth Theatre before pursuing a professional acting career.

Despite being rejected by a number of prestigious drama schools, she continued to pursue acting and gradually established herself on television and in film.

Her breakthrough came in 1996 when she starred in the BBC drama Our Friends in the North, earning a BAFTA Television Award for Best Actress.

Since then she has appeared in productions including Notting Hill, The Forsyte Saga, Bodyguard, Line of Duty, Phantom Thread and My Policeman, building a reputation as one of Britain's finest screen performers.

## Why Peterlee remains proud of its star

Like many towns across County Durham, Peterlee has undergone significant change since its mining heyday.

Yet McKee's success remains a source of pride for many local people and serves as a reminder of the talent that has emerged from East Durham communities.

For a town created to offer new opportunities to mining families, the story of Gina McKee is one of its most notable success stories.

From a local drama group in Peterlee to BAFTA-winning performances watched by millions, her journey remains closely connected to the County Durham town where she grew up.
