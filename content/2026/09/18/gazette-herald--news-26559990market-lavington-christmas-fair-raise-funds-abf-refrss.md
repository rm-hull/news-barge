A Christmas fair is returning to raise funds for the Army Benevolent Fund.

Market Lavington Community Hall will host the 15th annual Christmas Country Fair on Saturday, November 14, from 10am to 2pm.

The fair offers a variety of stalls featuring handmade crafts and produce, including jewellery, textiles, home decor, glass, wood, greeting cards, preserves, as well as Christmas cards and decorations.

Visitors can also enjoy a tombola and refreshments.

All profits from the fair will support the ABF to help soldiers, ex-soldiers, and their families.

The event has raised nearly £16,000 so far. Entry to the fair is free, but attendees are encouraged to donate a £1 coin or more upon entry.
