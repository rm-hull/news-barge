A CUSTOMER or group of customers have allegedly made off without payment from a restaurant in York.

North Yorkshire Police said it happened around 6.45pm on Friday, September 4.

A force spokesperson said it is appealing to the public for information and wants to hear from anyone who witnessed what happened.

  


North Yorkshire Police issued two images of people the force said may have information which could support its investigation.

It asked anybody who recognises them to get in touch.

The force spokesperson said: "Please email the officer in charge at [nabiel.galab@northyourkshire.police.uk.](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyourkshire.police.uk.)

Information could also be provided by calling North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Information can be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through its website.

Please quote reference 12260173837 when providing information to police.
