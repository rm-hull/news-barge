Everyone’s favourite kooky family are being brought to the stage in this devilishly funny musical comedy.

Following their productions of A Little Night Music and Little Women, Wharfemede Productions return to Theatre@41 with a show full of heart, humour, and Halloween spirit.

The Addams Family will be performed at the venue from Tuesday 27 to Saturday 31 October.

Performed during Halloween week itself, audiences can expect the perfect seasonal treat – packed with gothic glamour, mischievous fun, and spectacular entertainment.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Wharfemede-Productions-the-addams-family-3.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Wharfemede-Productions-the-addams-family-2.webp)

When Wednesday Addams falls in love with a ‘normal’ young man and invites his respectable parents to dinner, chaos, secrets, and hilariously macabre mayhem unfold in true Addams style.

Packed with sharp comedy, dazzling dance numbers, and a brilliantly witty score, this spooky celebration of family, love, and embracing the wonderfully weird promises an unforgettable night at the theatre.

“At its heart, this is a brilliantly funny show, packed with wit, warmth and wonderfully eccentric characters, and I feel incredibly fortunate to be working with such an exceptionally talented cast,” said director Dr Helen ‘Bells’ Spencer.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Wharfemede-Productions-the-addams-family-4.webp)

“I cannot wait for audiences to meet this company and experience the energy, humour and heart they are bringing to the production.

“I am also particularly excited to bring elements of commedia dell’arte into our interpretation  
of the show,” Bells added.

“Having had the privilege of working with commedia companies in Italy, I have long admired the genre’s physical storytelling, heightened characters and joyful theatricality.

“Those influences feel perfectly suited to the world of The Addams Family, and I look forward to blending them with the musical’s unique humour to create something that is both fresh and deeply entertaining.”
