A County Durham motorcyclist who rode while disqualified, failed to stop for police and used a false number plate has received a suspended sentence.

John Bentley, 20, of Fountain Square, St Helen Auckland, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on September 11 after the incidents on the A689 in Rushyford on May 17.

Bentley admitted riding a Sinnis Apache 125 while disqualified, dangerously and without insurance, using a false number plate and failing to stop for police.

The court suspended Bentley’s 24-week young offender institution sentence for 24 months because of his “flagrant disregard for court orders".

He must complete 300 hours of unpaid work and remains disqualified from driving for two years unless he passes an extended test.

Bentley must also pay £85 in prosecution costs, with his guilty pleas taken into account when he was sentenced.
