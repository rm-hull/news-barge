Madness will headline Music Showcase Friday at York Racecourse.

The band, known for their ska and pop hits, will perform on the evening of Friday, July 23, 2027.

They have enjoyed a long career in the British music scene, with 15 UK top 10 singles and a string of classic tracks including One Step Beyond, Baggy Trousers, and It Must Be Love.

The band also had a UK number one with House of Fun, and their song Our House became their biggest hit in the United States.

Over the years, Madness have played major festivals including Glastonbury and Camp Bestival, received the Ivor Novello Award, and helped open the 2012 Olympics.

Tickets for the concert and racing go on sale at 10am on Friday, September 25.

A price freeze until the end of the year means entry to the Grandstand and Paddock enclosure starts at £42 per person for a group of six, with a special rate of £15 for racegoers aged 5-17, subject to limited availability.

There are no booking fees and car parking is free.

The European Breeders Fund Lyric Stakes, a £70,000 Listed race, will be the highlight of a six-race card before the concert.

A second headline act for the weekend is still to be announced and will perform on Saturday, July 24.
