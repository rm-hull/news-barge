# Talented designer's journey from quality goods handmade at home to her own physical store in Harrogate town centre

## The opening of a new shop will bring a high quality addition to Harrogate’s high street sector.

Located just along from Oliveta Harrogate restaurant at 4 Station Parade, Belindas Boutique will offer unique and luxurious ladies bags and accessories for all occasions.

It’s not a new business, it’s named after its talented owner Belinda who has been designing and creating quality handmade bags for all occasions from her home in Harrogate successfully for some time now.

All her bags are handmade by her.

(Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjJhZTQzYWVkLTIxZjMtNGE4Ny05MTNiLTk0MTQ5NDU3ZmQ0MDoyZjEzYWRiYy05N2QxLTQ4YWItYjUzMS01ODNjNWM3NWJkNDE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Fans will already have seen Belinda’s wonderful handbags on sale at the Little Bird Artisan Marketa in Valley Gardens in Harrogate, at Harewood House and Ripon Christmas Market.

Belinda said: "A couple of years ago I decided to make my own unique make-up bag rather than buying one on the high street.

"After receiving compliments from family and friends I decided to create some more bags to give away as gifts whilst experimenting with various designs and fabrics.

"Since then I have been selling my unique creations at artisan markets across North Yorkshire and through social media and my website.

"All my bags are fully handmade by myself from start to finish using the highest quality fabrics including velvet, silk and cotton in a range of unique patterns which I source from around the UK.”

Fans will already have seen Belinda and her wonderful handbags at the Little Bird Artisan Marketa in Valley Gardens in Harrogate or at Harewood House or Ripon Christmas Market.
