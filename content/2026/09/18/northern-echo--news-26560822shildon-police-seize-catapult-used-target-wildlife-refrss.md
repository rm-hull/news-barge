A catapult has been seized in Shildon after police said it had been used to target wildlife and cause criminal damage, as officers warn of an increase in youth-related anti-social behaviour.

Officers from Shildon's neighbourhood policing team seized the item on Thursday (September 17) because of 'concerns it was being used as an offensive weapon'.

  The catapult that was seized by police 

*(Image: POLICE)*

Sharing an image of the catapult on its social media page, the policing team said it would "continue to take action to tackle ASB."

A full statement from Shildon's neighbourhood policing team said: "Officers have seized a catapult following reports of it being used to target wildlife and cause criminal damage.
