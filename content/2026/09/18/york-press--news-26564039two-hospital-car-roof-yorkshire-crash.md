Two people have been taken to hospital after a car ended up on its roof following a crash in North Yorkshire.

Police, paramedics and firefighters were called to the two-vehicle collision in Thornton-le-Street, between Thirsk and Northallerton, shortly before 2.55pm on Friday (September 18).

The driver of the vehicle that landed on its roof managed to get free from the wreckage with support from firefighters, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

Both drivers were checked by paramedics and taken to hospital by ambulance, the fire service said.

A service spokesperson added: “Crews made the vehicles safe before it was left in the hands of police.”
