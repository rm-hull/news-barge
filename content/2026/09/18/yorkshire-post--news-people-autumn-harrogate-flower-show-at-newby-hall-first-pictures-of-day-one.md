The show opened on Friday for three days of horticultural excellence, autumn colour and inspiration, as the show celebrates its 50th anniversary.

The Grand Floral Pavilion will be filled with more than 5,000 blooms, alongside displays from specialist nurseries and horticultural societies, showcasing the colour and variety of the season.

The Yorkshire Post photographer Tony Johnson captured the best of the action in these amazing first photographs:
