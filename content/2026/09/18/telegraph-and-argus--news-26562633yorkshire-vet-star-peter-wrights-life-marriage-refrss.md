If you have ever watched The Yorkshire Vet, you will be familiar with Peter Wright, the star of the Channel 5 show.

Peter has worked as a veterinary surgeon for nearly 40 years and grew up in a small village a few miles south of Thirsk.

Peter spent many years of his childhood playing outside with animals and spending time in the countryside, which has shaped his veterinary career.


## Peter Wright’s life – from his work on The Yorkshire Vet to marriage to wife Lin

Early in his career, Peter worked for Alf Wright, who is also known by his pen name James Herriot, the author of All Creatures Great and Small, which has been adapted into a TV show.

He worked at Skeldale Veterinary Centre from 1982 to 2021.

In 2014, Peter was asked by a TV production company in Leeds if he wanted to be involved in a documentary series about the vet practice.

Originally, Peter had rejected the idea and ignored several emails from the production company.

Eventually, Peter agreed to a face-to-face meeting with the managing director but was still not convinced people would watch the show.

The Yorkshire Vet ended up being commissioned and is now a favourite with Channel 5 viewers, with the show starting out by following Peter and Julian Norton, as they helped animals around North Yorkshire.


The show now has 23 seasons, with the latest series currently airing on Channel 5.

The Yorkshire Vet was expanded to West Yorkshire with vets from Donaldson’s Hospital joining the show.

The Huddersfield location of the vets was opened by Peter at a ribbon-cutting ceremony in 2019.

Matt Smith, David Melleney, Shone Searson, and Rohin Aojula all featured in the show over the years.

Peter left Skeldale Veterinary Centre because it was being taken over by Medivet, meaning he could no longer do what he wanted.

In an interview with The Express, he said: “I was there from being the new boy and then I gradually became (a) partner. I then became senior partner.

“I ran the practice for almost 20 years, and I have lots of happy memories of working there.

"But, at the same time, I am what is called a mixed practitioner.

“In other words, I deal with all creatures, all shapes and sizes.

“From cows and horses down to hamsters.


"And to take the farm side away from me, you know, it had to stop because it wasn’t economically viable anymore and I couldn’t do that work anymore."

He currently works at his practice, Grace Lane Vets in Kirkbymoorside.

Peter is currently married to his wife Lin, who he has been with for decades.

The pair met at a joint work party with the local dentist’s office and vet practice.

The couple had a strange first date at an abattoir but ended up moving in together after a few weeks.

Peter and Lin have two children together called Emily and Andrew.

Before she retired, Lin had been working at Skeldale as a receptionist and took emergency calls.
