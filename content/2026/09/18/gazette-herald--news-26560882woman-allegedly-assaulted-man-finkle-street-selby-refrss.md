A WOMAN was taken to hospital after an alleged assault in a North Yorkshire town centre.

The incident occurred close to Zuzu Lounge in Finkle Street, Selby between 3.30am and 4am on Sunday (September 13).

The woman is reported to have been assaulted by an unknown man who is alleged to have caused her a head injury, requiring hospital treatment.

  


A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The victim also injured her fingers and arm due to the assault.

“The suspect is described as white, in his mid to late 20s, with black hair.

“He was wearing jeans and a black t-shirt at the time.

“Prior to the incident, the man was being verbally abusive towards people.

“The victim states that members of the public picked up her mobile phone, which had fallen out of her pocket when the suspect walked away.

“The suspect then continued to be aggressive towards other members of the public on leaving the location.

“The victim is still having physical issues after the assault and requires further hospital attention.”

Police are now appealing for more information to locate the suspect.

If you believe you can help officers in their investigation, please call 101, quoting reference number 12260179746.
