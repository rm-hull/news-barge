Excitement surrounding Darlington’s festive season has begun with the launch of its latest pantomime.

The cast of Beauty and the Beast gathered at Walworth Castle to launch the Christmas show on Thursday afternoon (September 17).

The show will run at Darlington Hippodrome from December 11 to January 3.

The launch event brought together cast members, producers, and theatre representatives to offer a first look at the upcoming show.


Among those in attendance was County Durham-born TV presenter Matt Baker, who will play Jack in the production.

The former Blue Peter presenter, from Easington, said: "This is the purpose of doing this panto here on the home turf.

"I've been all over the world, I've seen some incredible stuff, and here is where I'm happiest.

"I've done a lot of this stuff around the country, but never with the love and connection of that North East audience."

Producer Michael Harrison said Matt's "charisma, charm and North East roots" made him a perfect fit for the cast.

He will share the stage with Liv Newcomb as Belle, Ollie Llewelyn-Williams as Prince Sebastian, Neisha Yen-Jones as the Enchantress, Jamie Jones as Mrs Potty and Josh Benson as Silly Billy.

The pantomime is produced by Crossroads Pantomimes and tells the story of Belle, who finds herself trapped in a castle under a mysterious curse.

Jamie Jones, who plays Mrs Potty, said: "Audiences are guaranteed comedy, laughs, dancing, singing, messing around."
