A Durham county councillor was left with cut under his eye after being attacked.

Cllr Jack McGlenen, who represents Dalton and Dawdon, said he was attacked on Wednesday (September 17) and taken to hospital.

Police were called to Colling Avenue in Seaham at 1.55pm.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: “The man sustained bruising and a cut under his eye as a result of the incident.

"He was taken to hospital to be checked out.

"A suspect has been identified, and arrest attempts are ongoing.”

  Cllr Jack McGlenen said he was recovering at home after the attack.


Cllr McGlenen said he was recovering at home but would be ‘out of action for a while’.

He said: “I’m home now and resting, but unfortunately I’ll be out of action for a while due to a long-term injury to my eye.

"It may also take some time before it properly returns to normal.

"The overwhelming support I’ve received has truly meant the world to me.

"I’m incredibly grateful to everyone who has reached out, checked in on me and shown me so much kindness.”

Cllr McGlenen was elected as a Reform UK councillor in May 2025 with 876 votes but was expelled from the party for drunken behaviour at an Armed Forces Day event.

He joined the Social Democratic Party last month.

The councillor said he missed a Police and Communities Together (PACT) meeting after the assault, but thanked everyone who contacted him.

“Thank you to everyone who has shown me support and concern this evening.

Cllr McGlenen said the attack would not stop him carrying on as a councillor.
