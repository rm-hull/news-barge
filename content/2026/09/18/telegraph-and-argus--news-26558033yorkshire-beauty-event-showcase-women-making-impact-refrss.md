FEMALE founders, CEOs and community leaders will be in the spotlight this October for a major health and beauty event.

Taking place at Grantley Hall on October 25, the British Beauty Week event will showcase women from across the region who are driving change in the beauty, health and wellbeing sectors.

Now in its third year, the event is organised by This is Yorkshire Beauty and aims to bring some of the energy of the London-based British Beauty Week to a northern audience.

Victoria Clapham, co-founder of This is Yorkshire Beauty, said: "We wanted to create an event that celebrates the incredible women, businesses and ideas we have here in Yorkshire, while also giving our audience the opportunity to learn from people who are genuinely making an impact.

"There is so much innovation and expertise happening outside London, and we wanted British Beauty Week to reflect that."

This year’s theme is 'IMPACT' and the speaker line-up includes CEOs, entrepreneurs, health and fitness experts and community leaders.

The event will feature a two-course lunch, workshops and masterclasses, including a body confidence session and a clean-makeup masterclass.

Guests will also have the chance to experience the latest facial and body sculpting technology and explore a beauty hall showcasing a collection of independent brands, with the majority founded in Yorkshire.
