# Harrogate’s Masonic Hall is to opens its doors to the public for one day only this Sunday

## There’s rare chance to step inside one of Harrogate's most intriguing historic landmarks this weekend as the Masonic Hall opens its doors for one day only.

As part of the Harrogate Heritage Open day, the venue will be open on Sunday, September 20, from 10am until 4pm.

During this time, the public will be able to enter not only the hall, but the masonic temple, where there will be short demonstrations to demystify what goes on behind the closed doors.

Located on Station Avenue, the Grand Georgian-style building will offer visitors a rare opportunity to explore its rich history, grand architectural features, and unique ceremonial spaces that have largely remained behind closed doors.

"We are delighted to welcome the local community and visitors to enjoy our historic Masonic building," said a spokesperson for the venue.

"Whether you are curious about the heritage of Freemasonry, interested in local history, or looking for a stunning unique venue for a wedding or private event, there is something for everyone to discover.”

A distinguished venue steeped in history and tradition, Harrogate’s Masonic Hall was built in 1930 for the Right Honourable, The Earl of Harewood KC, Provincial Grand Master of Yorkshire West Riding.

Entry to the event this Sunday is completely free of charge.

Event highlights Include:

Historic Lodge Room & Mini-Ceremonies:

Visitors can view the unique, beautifully preserved main Lodge Room.

Throughout the day, regular mini-ceremonies will be conducted to give the public an authentic insight into the traditions, history, and practice of Freemasonry.

The Magnificent Ballroom:

The building’s spectacular ballroom will be set up with a few tables in a full wedding layout, showcasing its potential as a premier local event space.

Visitors can watch a rolling video presentation detailing the hall’s history and explore an exhibition dedicated to the history of Freemasonry in Harrogate.

Venue Hire & Event Consultation:

Representatives will be on hand throughout the day to answer questions and discuss hiring options for private events, function spaces, and wedding ceremonies.

Complimentary Refreshments:

Hot tea and coffee will be available for all guests as they take in the historic surroundings.
