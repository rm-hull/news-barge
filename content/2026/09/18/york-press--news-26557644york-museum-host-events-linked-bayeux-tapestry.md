## Yorkshire Museum to explore York's links to 1066 with Bayeux Tapestry events

Yorkshire Museum in York will host a special weekend of 1066-themed events, celebrating links to the Norman Conquest ahead of the Bayeux Tapestry display at the British Museum *(Image: York Museums Trust)*

A special weekend of events in York will explore the county's links to the events of 1066 as excitement builds around the Bayeux Tapestry’s display at the British Museum.

Yorkshire Museum will host activities on September 19 and 20 in celebration of the tapestry’s display at the British Museum.

York and Yorkshire were also battlegrounds in 1066, with the battles of Fulford and Stamford Bridge fought in the same year, just before the famous Battle of Hastings that became immortalised on the Bayeux Tapestry.

  Part of the Bayeux Tapestry 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Museum said: "Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 September will showcase objects and stories from the Yorkshire Museum’s collection that have links to the Tapestry and explore life in York during the 11th century in a series of intriguing events and hands-on activities designed for all ages."

Events will include a special curatorial talk, family-friendly storytelling, and tapestry-themed crafts.

Visitors will have the rare chance to handle an artefact not usually on public display.

Storytelling with Eden from Stories Alive will bring the tapestry’s characters to life, while guided craft sessions on Sunday will encourage visitors to learn through art.

Additional activities include daily talks from the museum’s visitor experience team and a self-led weaving workshop on Saturday.

All activities are included in the price of admission.
