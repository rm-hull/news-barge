A woman was left in hospital after an attack in Selby.

It happened near to the Zuzu Lounge on **Finkle Street** between**3.30am and 4am on Sunday** (13 September).

The woman was attacked by an unknown man.

#### Map location

She suffered a head injury as well as injuries to her fingers and arm and was treated in hospital.

The male attacker is described as:

- white
- in his mid to late twenties
- with black hair
- wearing jeans and a black T-shirt.

Before the assault, he was being verbally abusive towards people.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The victim says members of the public assisted after she was assaulted and some even ran after the suspect when he picked up her mobile phone which had fallen out of her pocket when he walked away.

“The suspect then continued to be aggressive towards other members of the public on leaving the location.

“The victim is still having physical issues after the assault and requires further hospital attention.”
