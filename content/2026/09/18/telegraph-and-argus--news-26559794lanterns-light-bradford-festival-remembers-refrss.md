A LANTERN parade will help light up a season of remembrance and celebration.

Thousands are expected to take part in Bradford’s Festivals of the Dead, a community project exploring themes of loss, remembrance, and celebration through creative events and workshops.

The festival is a collaboration between Cecil Green Arts, the University of Bradford, and Bradford Death Cafés.

It will culminate in two major lantern events this autumn, intended to bring together communities from across the district to remember and honour loved ones who have died.

Professor Karina Croucher, professor of archaeology at the University of Bradford, said: "Death is something that touches every one of us, yet it remains one of the subjects we find hardest to talk about.

"These events create an opportunity for people to come together through creativity, reflection, and conversation, helping us explore remembrance and loss in a supportive and meaningful way.

"We hope participants will find space not only to remember those they have lost, but also to celebrate lives lived and the connections that continue beyond death."

*(Image: Supplied)*

The main remembrance event will take place at Northcliffe Park on Saturday, November 14.

It will offer a quieter, reflective evening centred around memorial lanterns, creativity, and community connection.

The event will include a small parade, performances, and a calling of names, ending with music and a celebration of life.

Workshops will be held ahead of the event at Bradford City Library and Shipley Library as part of a five-week programme.

  Images from last year's festival 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Members of the public are invited to create their own memorial willow lanterns while exploring themes of death and remembrance in a supportive setting.

The festival also includes the return of the popular Cecil Green Arts Lantern Parade at Lister Park on Friday, October 30.

This year’s parade will follow the theme 'Celebration of Life,' inviting people to reflect on loved ones, community, and memory through art and creativity.

Both events are supported by Bradford Culture Company (City of Culture) volunteers.

Residents who wish to take part in the memorial lantern workshops can register through Eventbrite via Bradford City Library and Shipley Library.
