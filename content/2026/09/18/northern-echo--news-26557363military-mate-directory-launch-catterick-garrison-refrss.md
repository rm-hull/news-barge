A digital scheme supporting the armed forces community is set to launch in North Yorkshire.

Military Mate, an online directory for serving personnel, veterans and their families, will launch on September 23 at Catterick Leisure Centre.

The scheme uses a Military Mate business card with a QR code linking to the digital platform when scanned with a smartphone or other smart device.

The directory offers jobs, training advice, discounts and contacts for mental health and legal support.

Mal Robinson, executive director at the National Armed Forces and Emergency Services Events (NAFESE), said: "We are delighted to hold the first face-to-face event for the Military Mate project in Catterick, which, of course, is home to the British Army in the North.

"Anyone connected to the forces community is welcome to attend, including serving personnel, families and veterans, as the Military Mate and Directory will have features on it designed for them."

The event runs from 9.30am to 11.30am and is open to everyone in the armed forces community.

The UK Armed Forces Engagement Initiative, part of the National Armed Forces & Emergency Services Events (NAFESE) team, created Military Mate.

Visitors will receive a Military Mate card and can browse stands offering advice and opportunities.

Exhibitors include Darlington College, North East Jobs, Spire Healthcare, Oakwood Solicitors, and Sunderland Council.

Cardholders can keep the card and access it at any time.

Military Mate had a soft launch on Armed Forces Day in June, but Catterick Garrison will host the first stop on its Launch Tour.

People can register for the launch at militarymate.co.uk or simply turn up on the day.
