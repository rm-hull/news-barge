One of the leading voices at the Darlington Economic Campus is to be the keynote speaker at the next live Northern Echo event.

Guests and panellists at the breakfast event on September 25 at Darlington College - the latest in the Impact series of discussions being run by BUSINESSiQ, the business arm of The Northern Echo - will talk about how major projects and a local focus on investment can build stronger and prouder communities.

With a new Government firmly in place, the spotlight on Andy Burnham’s regions is brighter than ever, with his pledge to deliver power ‘into every postcode', so Ellie Fotheringham, Deputy Director for the Strategy, Planning and Budget Group of HM Treasury, will help us understand how our own regional powerhouse is working in partnership with Government to build for the future.

Ellie leads the Strategy Unit which includes working to establish and build upon the success of the Darlington Economic Campus, and has previously worked in a variety of policy and delivery roles across government including in Cabinet Office, MHCLG, DEFRA and DCMS. Prior to joining the Civil Service, Ellie worked in event planning, charity fundraising, and local authority museums in the North East.

She told us: "It has always been a key part of DEC's role in the region to help people understand what we do and the impact our work has locally and for the Government, particularly in the build up to major events like the Budget in a few weeks.

"So we welcome any chance to put ourselves in front of an audience and talk about what goes on at the campus, from helping draw up key policies to offering career opportunities to people who may not have thought we were an option.

"The building of the new Brunswick campus is another of the major projects the region will benefit from and this Impact event and the audience it attracts seems like the perfect combination for a very positive conversation about the whole region."

The panellists who will be joining Ellie are Rose Rouse, CEO of Darlington Borough Council; David Gartland, Principal of our hosts Darlington College; Rachel Anderson, Assistant Director of Policy at the North East Chamber of Commerce; Mark Stamper, Head of Inclusive Growth and Partnerships, Durham County Council; Huw Lewis, Assistant Director of Communications at Kim McGuinness’s North East Mayoral Strategic Authority; David Hodgson, UK Bus Territory Manager at Darlington engines giant Cummins, and Sophie Walton, Director of Strategic Partnerships at CPI.
