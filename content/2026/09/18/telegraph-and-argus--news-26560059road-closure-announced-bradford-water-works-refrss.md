ST Margarets Road will close temporarily on Monday for water works.

The closure will take place between numbers 78 and 80.

City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council has announced the section of the road will remain closed from 7am to 5pm on Monday.

The closure could potentially last longer if necessary.

During this period, no alternative routes for vehicular traffic will be available, although vehicles involved in the works will be permitted access.

Pedestrian access, however, will be maintained at all times.

The closure is part of an order made under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 - Section 14.

The order allows for the temporary prohibition of traffic in areas where necessary works, such as water works, are being carried out.

The closure notice was published on September 17 by the council's Director of Legal and Governance.
