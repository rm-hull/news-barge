A specialist school has been named one of just eight Beacon schools in the country by the National Autistic Society.

The Springfields Academy in Calne, part of Reach South Academy Trust, received the prestigious recognition for its outstanding support for autistic pupils and for sharing its expertise with other schools and organisations.

The Beacon Award is given to schools and services that have already achieved Advanced status in the NAS Autism Specialist Award and have shown exceptional commitment to educating, guiding, and inspiring others beyond their own setting.

Nicola Whitcombe, headteacher at The Springfields Academy, said: “We are incredibly proud to have been recognised as one of just eight Beacon schools nationally.

“This is a real reflection of the expertise and commitment of our staff, and of the work we do every day to understand and respond to the individual needs of autistic young people."

The academy currently supports 275 autistic learners aged four to 19, all of whom have an Education, Health and Care Plan.

The school’s approach focuses on personalised learning, communication support, vocational preparation, and building independence and emotional resilience.

Ms Whitcombe said: “What makes this recognition particularly special is that it acknowledges not only the practice within our own school but also our commitment to sharing what we have learned with others.

“We want to play our part in improving understanding and practice more widely, so that more autistic children and young people can have the right support to thrive.”

Earlier this year, an Ofsted inspection rated the school “exceptional” in Inclusion, Leadership and Governance, and Personal Development and Wellbeing.

Lisa Myers, head of National Autistic Society: Learn, said: “We are delighted that The Springfields Academy has achieved the Beacon Award.

“The award reflects areas of excellence beyond our specialist award criteria, demonstrating significant impact on professionals, the wider community and the generation of new knowledge.”

Tom Leverage, CEO of Reach South Academy Trust, said: “This is an exceptional achievement for The Springfields Academy and a fitting recognition of the expertise and dedication of its staff.

“We are immensely proud of the team and the difference they are making.”
