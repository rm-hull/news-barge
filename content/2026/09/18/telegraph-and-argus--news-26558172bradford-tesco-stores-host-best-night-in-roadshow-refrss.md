TESCO is bringing its Best Night In roadshow to the city.

The event will arrive at the Bradford Buttershaw Superstore on Thursday, September 24, and the Bradford Extra store on Friday, September 25.

It offers shoppers the chance to sample snacks and drinks, take part in an interactive game, and win themed prizes.

Murray Bisschop, Tesco UK marketing director, said: "As we head into autumn, more and more of our customers will be looking forward to a cosy night in and we want to help shoppers across Great Britain with everything you need to make the most of those autumn evenings indoors."

The roadshow will feature a living room-style set-up in the car park, offering a taste of the ideal night in.

As part of the campaign, millions of Clubcard holders have already received free coupons for Tesco Finest pizzas through the retailer’s Freebie Thursdays scheme.

Customers also have the chance to win one of 50 tech bundles worth £10,000 each by spending £25 in a qualifying transaction at Tesco in-store or online.

Each bundle includes an up to 98" 4K TCL television, a Sonos Arc Ultra soundbar, a PlayStation 5, a Nintendo Switch 2, a Sony Bluetooth turntable, a £2,500 furniture voucher, and £1,000 to spend at Tesco.
