A York academic will help lead a global drive to tackle major health problems facing boys and young men.

Professor Paul Galdas, of the University of York’s Department of Health Sciences, is one of four experts leading Movember’s global men’s health research programme as a Movember Institute Professorial Fellow.

He will lead research in York and mentor four postdoctoral researchers across the UK and Ireland through the five-year Discovery Research Programme, which focuses on emotional health, social connection and suicide prevention.

Professor Galdas said: "This is an opportunity to build something much bigger than an individual research project.

"Men’s health research is still a relatively small field.

"We need more people, more capacity and better evidence about what actually improves men’s lives."

The programme will study how social connections, coping strategies and early fatherhood affect the health of boys and men aged 12 to 35.

The Movember Institute of Men’s Health funds the programme, which involves researchers in Australia, the US and Canada.

Dr Rebecca Jenkinson, director of the Movember Institute’s Discovery Research Programme, said: "What set Paul apart was his breadth of experience.

"He is an internationally recognised researcher, a leader in men's health and an outstanding mentor to early career researchers."
