The late Queen offered to restore Diana’s HRH title on the day of her funeral, Earl Spencer has claimed.

Writing in his bombshell memoir, he claimed that just hours after his eulogy at Diana’s funeral, the late Queen’s private secretary Robert Fellowes beckoned him over and offered the late Princess of Wales’ HRH honorifics back.

While the Earl later wondered if he was right to reject the award, he felt “the title should never have been removed during her life, in which case it would not have been necessary to offer it to her in death.”

It is the latest allegation revealed in his memoir, *Swan Song: Diary, My Sister,* serialised in the*Daily Mail*. 

While Buckingham Palace said that the King is mindful that “grief can cloud reason” in response to the latest allegations, members of the royal family have remained silent about claims made in Earl Spencer’s book.

The King, Prince William and Princess Catherine made no remarks of the allegations as they carried out royal engagements on Thursday. The Duke of Sussex also said nothing of his uncle’s book, and its reflections on his father’s attitude around his mother’s death, when asked by reporters as he attended the inaugural Invictus Spirit Awards in London.

## Diana’s ‘revenge dress’ showed her ‘taking back the narrative’ – fashion expert

The symbolic “revenge dress” worn by Diana, Princess of Wales told the world that she was “taking back the narrative”, a fashion expert has said.

The black silk gown was unveiled in Mayfair, central London, before it goes on sale at auction, where it is expected to fetch upwards of £220,000.

It comes as Diana has been back in the headlines amid a war of words between Buckingham Palace and Diana’s brother, Earl Spencer, whose book serialised in the *Daily Mail* has levelled accusations at the King.

Morgane Halimi, global head of handbags and fashion at Sotheby’s, the auction house where the dress will go under the hammer, said: “What makes it so particular is the moment she chose to wear this dress, it was a moment in her life where she was exposed and she could have seemed fragile.

“But instead, she chose to wear this dress to express a form of self-confidence and to express to the world that she was taking back the narrative.”

## Recap: The Queen offered to restore Diana's honorifics posthumously

Queen Elizabeth II offered to restore Diana’s HRH title at her funeral, Earl Spencer has claimed.

The Earl wrote he rejected the late Queen’s “well-meaning” gesture to restore Diana’s HRH title posthumously as it felt like a “consolation award”. He claimed his sister was “dreadfully hurt” by the “petty and vindictive” decision to remove her title, and just hours after her funeral she offered to restore it.

“I felt that I knew in my bones how she would view this posthumous award,” he writes. “That's very kind of the Queen, of course. But what's the point, Robert? Now? I mean – Diana's dead.”

He said that just hours after his eulogy at Diana’s funeral, the late Queen’s private secretary Robert Fellowes beckoned him into a quiet corner of the carriage.

“I have just received a call from Her Majesty… and she would like to offer Diana her HRH title back,” he wrote Mr Fellowes as saying.

While the Earl later wondered if he was right to reject the award, he felt “the title should never have been removed during her life, in which case it would not have been necessary to offer it to her in death.”
