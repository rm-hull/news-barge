A TEENAGER is fighting for his life following an e-scooter crash.

The incident occurred on Canterbury Avenue in Bradford at around 7.35pm on Thursday evening.

The 18-year-old male was taken to Leeds General Infirmary for treatment.

### Full police statement

On Friday morning, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "We are appealing for witnesses following a single vehicle collision on Canterbury Avenue, Bradford yesterday (17 September) at around 7.35pm involving an E-scooter.

"An 18-year-old man has been taken to Leeds General Infirmary to be treated for life-threatening injuries.

"Enquiries remain ongoing.

"Any witnesses including anyone who may have footage of the incident or saw the E-scooter prior to the collision is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or reporting it online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 1565 of 17/09."
