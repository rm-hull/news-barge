A transport company has renewed its support for County Durham’s only visually impaired cricket team for a fourth year, helping players with sight loss compete nationally.

Go North East is again the Durham Visually Impaired (VI) Cricket Team’s front-of-shirt sponsor. Its players have partial or total sight loss and compete in ECB competitions.

The sponsorship helps pay for travel to away fixtures across the country, allowing players to represent Durham without the added hurdle of transport costs.

  Go North East have sponsored the team for the past four years 

*(Image: Mark Savage Photograpy)*

Ben Gilligan, managing director at Go North East, said: "We’re excited to continue supporting Durham Cricket Foundation’s VI Team this season.

"We’re committed to making our services accessible to everyone.

"It was great to have the team join us at the launch event for our new electric buses earlier this year.

"Our new EVs feature a range of accessibility upgrades, including audio-visual next-stop announcements, hearing loops and external sounders to alert pedestrians when a bus is approaching."

The VI team uses adapted equipment and modified rules to make cricket accessible.

The equipment includes larger stumps and a ball that makes a sound when it is hit or bounced.

Players from across the region take part in matches around the country.

Go North East’s Elite Bus Driver Training Academy also works with VI player Jack Moffat, an engagement manager at sight loss charity Thomas Pocklington Trust.

He runs training sessions to help bus drivers understand the needs and experiences of visually impaired passengers.

The team is part of the Durham Cricket Foundation’s disability cricket programme.

Graeme Weeks, CEO of Durham Cricket Foundation, said: "We’re delighted to continue our partnership with Go North East for a fourth consecutive year.

"Their support makes a real difference to Durham Cricket Foundation and our ability to provide opportunities for visually impaired players to enjoy the game, compete and proudly represent Durham.
