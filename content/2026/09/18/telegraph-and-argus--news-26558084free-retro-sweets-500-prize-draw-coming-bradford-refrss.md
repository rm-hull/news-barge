THOUSANDS of free sweets and a chance to win £500 are up for grabs at webuyanycar branches across West Yorkshire.

The giveaway is part of a national promotion by webuyanycar to mark welcoming its five millionth customer and 20 years in business.

The company is handing out more than 10,000 packs of watermelon-flavoured retro Millions sweets at 17 participating branches across the region, including Brighouse and three in Bradford.

A webuyanycar spokesperson said: "Anyone who picks up a pack – whether they're a webuyanycar customer or simply passing by – can enter a prize draw by sharing a photo of their Millions on social media and tagging webuyanycar, with five people set to win £500 each."

The giveaway begins on Monday, September 21, and is open to everyone while stocks last.

Branches taking part include Bradford Five Lane Ends (Morrison's car park), Bradford Lidget Green (Asda Cemetery Road Superstore), Thornbury Road B&Q (Mini Warehouse, 99 Leeds Old Road), and Brighouse (Park View House).

Other locations include Dewsbury, Halifax, Huddersfield, Keighley, and multiple sites across Leeds.
