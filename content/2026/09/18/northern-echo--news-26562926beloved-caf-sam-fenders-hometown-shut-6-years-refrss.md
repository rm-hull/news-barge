A North East coffee shop in the hometown of chart-topping singer Sam Fender has announced it will close its doors after six years in the town and almost a decade in business.

R Place will close on October 17 after three years serving customers in North Shields and earning a loyal following for its homemade doughnuts and other sweet treats.

  R Place was renowned for homemade sweet treats, including specially crafted doughnuts. 

*(Image: Too Good To Go)*

The closure of the Nile Street business was announced on social media on Wednesday (September 16) after owner Rachel chose not to renew the café's lease.

Announcing the closure, she wrote: "After a lot of thought, plenty of conversations with myself and probably far too many coffees, I've made the decision that it's time to close our doors."

She said renewing the lease did not feel right because “my heart and head are telling me it's time to move on”.

The owner added: "The truth is, running a small independent business in today's world is hard. Really hard.

"The costs, the pressures, the insanely saturated market, the uncertainty and the constant feeling of having to keep pushing just to stand still can take its toll on everything - financially, personal life, physical and mental health."

The closure will end almost a decade of serving customers across the North East, including six years in North Shields.

North Shields, the hometown of singer-songwriter Sam Fender, has developed a thriving independent food and drink scene, with R Place building a loyal following in the area.

  North Shields is well-known as the hometown of award-winning musician, Sam Fender. 

*(Image: MERCURY PRIZE)*

Thanking customers for their support, Rachel said: "To every single person who has ever bought a coffee, eaten a doughnut, brought a friend, attended a workshop, supported an event, shared a post, recommended us, checked in on me or simply walked through that door - thank you.

"Thank you for making R Place what it was. Thank you for supporting a little independent coffee shop and more importantly for supporting me.

"I genuinely couldn't have done it without you."

Customers quickly shared messages of support, thanking the café for serving the community over the last nine years.

One customer said: "Sorry to hear this Rachel, you do the best coffee, doughnuts and most of all the best chats."

Another wrote: "You should be so proud, your food and doughnuts were out of this world! I'll remember them forever with happy memories."

Signing off the message, Rachel said: "For now, I'm just incredibly grateful that I got to do this and so incredibly grateful for you all. Here's to R Place, what an adventure it's been."
