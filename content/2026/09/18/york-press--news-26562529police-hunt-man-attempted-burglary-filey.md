POLICE are investigating a suspected attempted burglary in a quiet North Yorkshire street.

The incident happened in Wooldale Drive, Filey, at around 1.20pm on Friday (September 11).

North Yorkshire Police is searching for a man in connection with the incident, who is alleged to have fled the area after being disturbed by the victim.

  


  A force spokesperson said: “We are appealing for information about the identification of the man, who is described as white, about 50 years old, white/grey hair, around 5ft 9in tall, medium build with a slight belly, wearing black framed glasses, a dark blue polo shirt and shorts.

  

  “He was carrying a flattened cardboard box with ‘DPD’ on the side.

  

  “If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance by emailing PC Amy Rogers on [__Amy.Rogers@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:__Amy.Rogers@northyorkshire.police.uk)__, or via live chat.

  

  “If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and ask for Amy Rogers.

  

  “If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

  

  “Please quote reference 12260179136 when providing information.”
