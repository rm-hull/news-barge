A popular beer festival is returning this autumn, promising a packed weekend of drinks, food and live entertainment.

Addingham Beer Festival runs on Friday, October 2 and Saturday, October 3, with sessions across both days.

Friday starts with a sponsors’ session from 4pm to 6pm, followed by a public evening running from 6pm until late.

Friday night will feature a wide selection of ales, ciders, gin, wine and prosecco, along with live music and food from The LA.

The festival continues on Saturday from 1pm, with the return of the popular quiz and a classic serving of pie and peas.

The evening session begins at 7pm and runs into the night, featuring live music from Charlie Aky and food from The Swan Inn.

Ales are sourced from local breweries including Ilkley and Craven, with many from farther afield.

Since 2011, the festival has raised nearly £100,000 for local causes.

Last year’s festival supported 17 groups and organisations.

Local organisations can apply for funding by requesting a form from [beer@addinghambeerfest.co.uk.](mailto:beer@addinghambeerfest.co.uk.)

Further details will be available in the festival programme.

The festival’s 2022 event featured more than 40 beers in cask and keg, ranging from traditional bitters and IPAs to blondes, porters, stouts and lagers.
