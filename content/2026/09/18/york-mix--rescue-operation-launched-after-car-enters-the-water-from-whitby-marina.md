A rescue operation was launched today (Friday) after a car went into the water at Whitby.

Whitby Coastguard Rescue Team, North Yorkshire Police, Yorkshire Ambulance Service, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service and Whitby RNLI were all involved.

It happened shortly before midday.

#### Map location

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/whitby-marina-car-park-gsv.webp)

The car hurtled out of the Marina car park over the edge into the River Esk.

Tyre marks were left in the grass at the riverside. The car was fully submerged.

Before emergency services arrived, a member of the public acted quickly and helped the driver from the water.

A Whitby Coastguard spokesperson said: “We would like to thank them for their actions and for stepping in during what must have been a frightening situation.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/whitby-marina-car-park-river-rescue-18-sept-2026-coastguard-tyre.webp)

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We were called with a report from Whitby that a car at the Marina car park had entered the water, shortly before midday today, and was fully submerged.

“Our officers attended immediately, alongside other emergency services.

“It’s believed that the driver had suffered a medical episode. He was taken to hospital with non-life threatening injuries.”

Whitby Coastguard said: “From the moment we arrived, everyone worked side by side to manage the scene, support the casualty and make sure they received the care they needed.

“One casualty was transferred to a waiting ambulance and, thankfully, they are safe and well.

“The tyre marks left behind are a reminder of just how quickly things can go wrong around the water. Harbours and marinas are busy working areas, with vehicles, people, equipment and unprotected edges all close together.

“Please take care when driving or working near the harbour, remain aware of your surroundings and keep a safe distance from the water’s edge wherever possible.”

The car is due to be recovered at low tide.
