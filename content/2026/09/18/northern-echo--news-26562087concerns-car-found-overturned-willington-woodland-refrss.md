A car found overturned in County Durham woodland with its doors and boot open has been removed following concerns.

Durham County Council said the vehicle found in Willington was “safely removed” after an investigation by its neighbourhood wardens.

Photographs shared by the council show the abandoned car overturned, with its doors and boot open.

A spokesperson said: "SW13 has been investigating an abandoned vehicle following concerns from local people.

“The vehicle has now been safely removed."

The authority is continuing to encourage members of the public to report abandoned vehicles so they can be investigated and dealt with appropriately.

Anyone wishing to report an abandoned vehicle, or who has information that could assist investigations, is asked to contact Durham County Council's customer services team on 03000 260000 or via the council's online reporting service.
