An unlicensed driver stopped for speeding on a Bradford road was found to be committing a string of offences before being arrested over "outstanding immigration matters."

Officers from West Yorkshire Police's Bradford East neighbourhood policing team stopped a BMW on Wakefield Road after clocking it travelling at 57mph.

Further checks revealed the motorist was allegedly driving without a seatbelt, licence or insurance.

The vehicle was subsequently seized by officers.

The driver was then arrested in connection with outstanding immigration matters and taken into custody.

No further details about the arrest have been released.

## Police statement in full

In a post on social media, West Yorkshire Police said: "We stopped this BMW on Wakefield Road West Yorkshire Police - Bradford East after clocking it travelling at 57 mph.

"Speeding turned out to be just the beginning of the driver's problems as we also identified offences of, no seatbelt, no licence, no insurance.

"After seizing the vehicle, rather than leaving the driver alone at the roadside we gave him a lift.

"But not a lift home, rather we took him down to custody for a much longer journey home due to outstanding immigration matters."
