Work on the East Yorkshire leisure centre is entering its final phase ahead of its planned opening later this year.

East Riding Leisure Goole is nearing completion, with work continuing inside and outside.

Councillor Nick Coultish, cabinet member for culture, leisure and tourism at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "This is a huge project and we are pleased to see the progress that Willmott Dixon have made on East Riding Leisure Goole.

"The building works are in their final stages and it’s exciting to see finishing touches coming together across the site.

"It’s an impressive building, offering fantastic facilities and we are sure it will be worth the wait for residents and visitors alike."

The Salt and Pepper Playport soft play area and Ninja Tag obstacle course are now complete.

Workers have fitted handrails and grills in the learner pool.

New flumes for the main pool have arrived from the United States and are nearly in place.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council is funding the project with support from the Goole Town Deal.

The redevelopment will also include a refurbished café, gym and spin studio.
