A County Durham motorist has been given a further driving ban after driving while disqualified and uninsured.

Anthony Beamson, 30, of Shotton Colliery, admitted driving a Volkswagen on the A182 at Easington Lane on December 19, 2024, while disqualified.

The court also heard the vehicle was being used without third-party insurance.

Beamson was banned from driving for a further three months, fined £200 and ordered to pay £85 in costs and an £80 surcharge.

He received no separate penalty for the insurance offence after admitting both charges and was sentenced on September 11.
