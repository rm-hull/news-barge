FIRE crews have been called to a vehicle on fire along a major road in North Yorkshire.

The incident took place at 8.26pm on Thursday (September 17) on the A168 in Boroughbridge.

  


Emergency services closed the road as firefighters worked to extinguish the blaze.

A service spokesperson said: “The vehicle was 100% damaged and the cause was believed to have been an electrical fault.

“Incident was left with police for recovery.”
