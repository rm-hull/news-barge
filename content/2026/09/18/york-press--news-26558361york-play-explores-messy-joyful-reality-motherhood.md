A SHOW exploring the messier side of parenthood will take to the stage this November.

Motherhood, performed by professional actress Elli Mackenzie of Oddsocks Productions, will be performed at the Joseph Rowntree Theatre in York on November 8.

The autobiographical play dives into the joys, challenges and absurdities of raising children while running a family business—complete with appearances from an orangutan and a giant woodlouse.

Ms Mackenzie said: "We premiered the piece at Jersey Arts Centre in January and found that members of the audience chose to stay behind at the end and chat about their experiences and we are hoping that people will do the same when we bring it York.

"There’s a great pub down the road, so we’ll see you there after the show."

Described by audiences as “A sucker punch to the feels”, “bloody brilliant” and “breathtakingly vivid,” Motherhood runs for 75 minutes with no interval.
