Council leaders are seeking proposals to bring Beverley Friary, a centuries-old East Yorkshire landmark, back into long-term use.

Parts of Beverley Friary date back to the 14th century, and the building is Grade II* listed in the heart of Beverley, near the Minster.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council wants a viable, sustainable occupier for the site, with expressions of interest open until Wednesday, October 28.

  Beverley Friary 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Councillor Michael Lee, the council's cabinet member for housing and infrastructure, said: "This is a great site at the heart of Beverley, and a great opportunity for local businesses.

"We welcome business proposals that take account of Beverley Friary’s historic character and importance to Beverley and its residents."

The Youth Hostel Association previously leased the building but left the site in 2025.

  Beverley Friary 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Proposals can be for a leasehold or freehold deal and must include an outline business plan showing respect for the building’s heritage and long-term sustainability.

The council will also require public access to the building’s most important historic areas at agreed times.

More information is available at eastriding.gov.uk.
