An actor who starred in the hit 2000’s Disney sitcom *That’s So Raven* has been sentenced to 30 months behind bars at a federal jail for a firearm charge.

Bobb’e J. Thompson, a 30-year-old actor from Kansas City who also appeared in NBC’s *30 Rock*  and the movie Role Models, was caught driving at 104 mph in a 70 mph zone by law enforcement back in September 2025.

According to federal prosecutors, officers conducting a traffic stop in Cerro Gordo County, Iowa noticed Thompson and a passenger swap seats, and the smell of marijuana from the vehicle.

During their search of the car, authorities discovered several bags containing marijuana as well as a fully loaded .40 caliber handgun - complete with a 15-round magazine and a round in the chamber.

![Bobb'e J. Thompson has been jailed after being found in his vehicle with firearm that had been reported as stolen](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/09/18/12/04/Screenshot-2026-09-18-at-13-04-35.png)

The firearm had been reported as stolen from a vehicle in Kansas City, prosecutors state. Thompson entered a guilty plea to the charge of possession of a firearm by a felon in April.

He was sentenced to 30 months behind bars at a federal prison by U.S. District Court Judge Leonard T. Strand in Sioux City on September 16. Under the federal system, there is no offer of parole. Thompson will also serve a 3-year term of supervised release after he leaves prison.

The actor was known for playing neighborhood trickster Stanley in Disney’s *That’s So Raven*, as well as appearing on Tina Fey’s* 30 Rock* as Tracy Jr, the son of Tracy Morgan’s character.

Thompson has appeared in several major film productions, including 2008’s *Role Models*, for which he was nominated for the Best Breakthrough Performance award at the MTV Movie Awards. He also appeared in the 2007 Christmas classic* Fred Claus*.

![Thompson previously starred on several hit shows, including That's So Raven and 30 Rock](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/09/18/12/45/Screenshot-2026-09-18-at-13-06-12.png)

Prosecutors say that Thompson was convicted of battery with serious bodily injury back in 2020, as well as assault with force. In 2022, he was convicted of prohibited possession of a firearm.

The actor opened up about his previous convictions on the *Eating While Broke* podcast in 2024.

“I caught a gun case," Thompson said on the podcast. "And then before that, I caught a case. I got in a fistfight and broke the boy (sic) jaw. And because of the jaw being broken, it was a great bodily injury, and it became a felony."

The star is currently being held by the U.S. Marshals Service and will be transported to a federal prison.
