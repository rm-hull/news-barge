A North Yorkshire charity is closing its Northallerton community venue later this year and is appealing for help to find a new home.

Hambleton Community Action (HCA) said on September 18 that its UpFront venue at the corner of Northallerton High Street and Romanby Road will close permanently in early December, after three years in the town.

The charity says the venue has become a vital part of community life, offering people connection, belonging and practical help.

  Hambleton Community Action's Northallerton base 

*(Image: HCA)*

Although the venue is closing, HCA chief officer Liz Lockey said the charity will continue supporting people and co-ordinating its work from offices at the rear of the building, which it has occupied since 2019.

Those services include community car and shopping schemes, support for unpaid carers and military families, help for people facing financial hardship, digital inclusion, social support, employment advice and volunteering opportunities.

Liz said: "When we opened UpFront in 2023, we could never have imagined just how important the space would become to so many people.

"We are incredibly proud of everything that has been achieved here and deeply grateful to every volunteer, supporter, partner and visitor who has helped make it such a success.

"While we are saddened to be closing the doors of our current community space, Hambleton Community Action is not closing.

"Our wider work across the community remains strong and continues to grow.

  Liz Lockey, chief officer at Hambleton Community Action (right) with colleagues from Northallerton's Yorkshire Building Society Charitable Foundation branch nominated Hambleton Community Action for a donation 

*(Image: HCA)*

"Every day, our staff and volunteers support people facing isolation, financial hardship, caring responsibilities and other challenges, and those services will continue."

She said the closure reflects the wider pressures facing charities across the UK.

Liz added: "This announcement highlights a wider issue facing charities across the country. Communities rely on the services provided by local charities more than ever, yet securing long-term funding for community spaces and essential infrastructure has become increasingly difficult.

"If we want places where people can connect, access support and build stronger communities, we need collective investment in them.

"Our immediate priority is supporting everyone who currently uses UpFront and finding a temporary home for our social supermarket, Pantry and Produce, while we work towards a longer-term solution.

"We remain optimistic about the future and are actively exploring opportunities for a new home for UpFront. We would encourage anyone who can support us, whether through a donation, partnership or by helping us identify suitable premises, to get in touch."

HCA is appealing to residents, businesses, funders and community partners to help it find a new base in Northallerton.
