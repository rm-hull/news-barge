A cycle thief described by police as ‘prolific’ is back in jail.

Officers arrested the thief after two bikes were stolen, with a combined value of £3,500.

One was taken from Castlegate, the other from St Andrewgate, both in August.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “He was also charged with a additional theft. As a result he was recalled to prison.”
