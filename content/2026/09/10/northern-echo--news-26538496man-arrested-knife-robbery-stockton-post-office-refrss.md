A man has been arrested on suspicion of robbery after allegedly entering a post office armed with a knife.

Police were called to the post office on Stockton High Street at around 2.20pm on Wednesday (September 9).

A 24-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of robbery and remains in police custody.

No injuries were reported.

A spokesperson for Cleveland Police said: “At 2.20pm on Wednesday, September 9, police were called to Stockton High Street following reports of a robbery involving a man with a knife.

“There were no injuries reported.

“A 24-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of robbery and is currently in police custody.”
