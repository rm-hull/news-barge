Exploding balloons, vortex smoke rings, and daredevil science experiments are set to take over the stage in a high-energy live show in York.

Brainiac Live, the Olivier Award-winning family science show inspired by the Sky One TV hit, will arrive at the Grand Opera House on Sunday, September 13, 2026, as part of its UK tour.

The show promises a "breathless ride" through the wild and wonderful world of science, packed with experiments "you’re far too scared (and definitely not allowed) to do at home".

Dan Colman, producer of Brainiac Live, said: "After winning the Olivier Award for Best Family Show last year, which was a huge moment for us, 2026 promises to be our busiest year yet.

"We are thrilled to see Brainiac Live as popular as ever and to be taking the show on tour across the UK and to be returning to the West End and, of course, playing to family audiences across Butlins Holiday resorts.

"There’s nothing quite like the look on kids’ faces when they see our daredevil experiments live on stage – but remember, don't try this at home."

Brainiac Live is recommended for ages five and up, but organisers say it offers fun for the whole family.
