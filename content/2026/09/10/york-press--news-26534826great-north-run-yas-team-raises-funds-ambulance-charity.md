## Yorkshire ambulance staff take on Great North Run for service charity

Lesley Butterworth *(Image: YAS)*

Yorkshire Ambulance Service staff are lacing up their trainers for this year’s Great North Run to raise funds for their official charity.

Seven runners from the service will take on the 13.1-mile challenge this Sunday, September 13, from Newcastle to South Shields, to support the Yorkshire Ambulance Service Charity (YAS Charity).

The YAS Charity funds health and wellbeing initiatives for staff and volunteers, and supports community and school programmes that equip people to respond in medical emergencies.

Lesley Butterworth, associate director of patient experience and nursing at YAS and based at the trust’s Wakefield headquarters, said: "I am proud to be fundraising for the Yorkshire Ambulance Service Charity.

"I have seen first-hand the difference the charity makes in supporting patients, volunteers and staff across Yorkshire Ambulance Service.

"Over the past few months, I have completed a couple of 10km races and a nine-mile trail race to help increase my mileage and confidence ahead of race day.”

Other runners include Julie Armitage, an ambulance practitioner, and Kimberley Swart, operational service manager for remote patient care.

Ms Armitage, who lives in Keighley, said: "I’ve chosen to raise money for YAS Charity because it’s a cause that is very close to my heart.

"Knowing that every mile I run helps support Yorkshire Ambulance Service staff, volunteers and communities across Yorkshire makes the challenge even more worthwhile.

"I’ve never considered myself a runner.

"Two and a half years ago, I started Couch to 5K, completed it, and my then crewmate and very good friend suggested we sign up for the London Landmarks Half Marathon the following April.

"That gave me the motivation to keep going and see how far I could take my running.

"Since then, the Great North Run will be our third half marathon together."

Carey Taylor, head of YAS Charity, added: "We are incredibly grateful to all of the runners taking on the Great North Run for the YAS Charity.

"The YAS Charity goes above and beyond NHS-funded work and relies entirely on donations and fundraising to keep delivering its vital work."

Supporters can donate to the runners via the YAS Charity JustGiving page.
