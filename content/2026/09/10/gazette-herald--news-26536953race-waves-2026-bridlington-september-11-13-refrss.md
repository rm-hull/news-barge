A beach racing event is returning to East Yorkshire this weekend with a three-day event.

Race the Waves is set to take over Bridlington South Beach from Friday, September 11, to Sunday, September 13, featuring vintage cars, motorcycles, and seaside charm.

With its 200-yard course on the sand, the event offers a nostalgic nod to the early days of speed trials, with vehicles competing in drag races against the backdrop of the North Sea.

  The festival draws inspiration from the beach’s historic role in early UK land speed record attempts. 

*(Image: East Riding of Yorkshire Council)*

The races, featuring pairs of cars and groups of four to five motorcycles, will be flagged off in traditional style.

This year, the event is introducing the Ken Fox Wall of Death, a live motorcycle stunt show making its debut at Race the Waves.

Visitors can catch this performance and enjoy refreshments at the Yorkshire Bus Bar, a vintage bus converted into a social hub.

*(Image: (Image: East Riding of Yorkshire Council))*

Both attractions will be located on the Old Boating Lake site, next to Bridlington Spa.

For a different kind of seaside adventure, the event is offering Beach Tank Rides from SWB Motorsport.

The weekend starts in Bridlington Old Town on Friday, September 11, from 10am to 10pm, with a display of race vehicles and motorcycles on Church Green.

Visitors can meet competitors, take photos, and see the machines up close before they hit the beach.

Racing enthusiasts can look forward to beach races starting from about 11am on Saturday and noon on Sunday, until about 4pm and 4.30pm respectively, depending on beach conditions.

The event also coincides with the Great Bridlington Steampunk Weekend, adding a family-friendly festival to the mix. Held at Bridlington Spa's Royal Hall, this festival features live entertainment, workshops, a marketplace, and more.

Visitors can also explore a hot rod display at Bridlington Lifeboat Station, open from 10am to 4pm on both Saturday and Sunday.

Bridlington Lions are hosting a car and motorbike display on South Marina Drive, open from 10am to 5pm on both days, featuring food and drink vendors.

South Marine Drive will be closed from 7am to 7pm throughout the weekend to accommodate the event.

Main event parking is available at South Cliff Park & Ride (YO15 3QN) and Strawberry Fields (YO15 3NG).

Ken Fox Wall of Death tickets can be purchased on the day for £5 per person.

For more information, visit the Race the Waves Facebook page or Instagram @racethewaves.
