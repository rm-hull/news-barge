Police are investigating after fake banknotes were used in a doorstep sale.

It happened in York on Friday (4 September).

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A member of public sold a mobile phone and after the transaction it was identified that the currency used was counterfeit notes.

“Provided is an image of a male in the York area who we believe can assist us with our enquires.”
