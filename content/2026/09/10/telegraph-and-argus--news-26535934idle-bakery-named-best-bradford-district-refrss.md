A BRADFORD cafe that blends British and Polish baking traditions is up for an award.

The Idle Bakery is one of the 10 finalists nominated for the T&A's Best Bakery 2026 competition.

Owner and bread baker Gosia Liwanowska says she was inspired by her transition of moving to the UK around a decade ago.

Ms Liwanowska said: "The Idle Bakery & Café combines traditional baking with British and Polish food heritage, creating a bakery where quality is measured not only by ingredients, but also by the time and care that goes into every bake."

  The Idle Bakery & Café 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

It specialises in traditional and sourdough breads, teacakes, baguettes, scones, sausage rolls, pies, and a changing selection of cakes.

Its adjoining café serves cooked meals, sandwiches, afternoon teas, and carefully prepared coffee.

But what really sets the bakery apart from its counterparts is its commitment to slow baking, Ms Liwanowska tells the T&A.

  The Idle Bakery & Café 

*(Image: The Idle Bakery & Café)*

Some sourdough loaves take approximately 73 hours to make, a process that Ms Liwanowska believes should never be rushed.

She said: "73 hours. One loaf. No shortcuts."

  The Idle Bakery & Café 

*(Image: The Idle Bakery & Café)*

## A tale of two cultures

Customers can find Polish cheesecakes and sweet yeast bakes beside British scones and pies, while rye and sourdough breads with a Polish twist sit alongside more familiar British loaves.

For those with a sweet tooth, the bakery is known for its rich brownies, but it’s the vanilla slice - layers of crisp pastry and smooth custard cream - that has become a customer favourite.

The cafe's owner said the team also enjoy experimenting, regularly introducing limited-edition products - which can sell out very quickly!

Speciality sourdoughs change regularly, and scone and sausage roll creations change monthly, and some flavours prove so popular that customers request their return as soon as they’re gone.

  The Idle Bakery & Café 

*(Image: The Idle Bakery & Café)*

## 'The loveliest compliments'

But perhaps the most important part of the village bakery is its relationship with the local community. Many customers visit every week so that staff get to know familiar faces and favourite orders.

Ms Liwanowska said: "Some of the loveliest compliments have been about the bread, with customers describing it as ‘the best bread in the world’ and praising the bakery for making ‘good wholesome proper sourdough bread’."

  The Idle Bakery & Café 

*(Image: The Idle Bakery & Café)*

For Ms Liwanowska , comments like these make the long hours worthwhile.
