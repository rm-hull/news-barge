Lightwater Valley Family Adventure Park has won a national award for one of its newest rides.

The park near Ripon received gold in the Best New Attraction category at the 2026 UK Theme Park Awards.

Its Spinning Racer rollercoaster was recognised following a public vote and assessment by an expert judging panel.

The ride was among several new attractions introduced at Lightwater Valley for the 2026 season.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/69d4a84738490.jpg)


It sees riders seated in freely spinning cars as they travel around the rollercoaster track.

A spokesperson for park operator Mellors Group said:

“A huge well done to the whole team who made this possible, and a massive thank you to everyone who voted and supported us.

"What an achievement!”
