**Harrogate-based independent travel agency There Travel is set to open its first high street shop** at the end of September, strengthening the company’s existing roots in the town and marking a major milestone in its growth. Founded in 2024 by owners Colin Ord and Jake Todhunter, There Travel has been based in Harrogate for some time, while building a growing business through its team of homeworkers.

The opening of its first shop represents the next step in that journey, giving There Travel a stronger physical presence in its home town and creating a welcoming space where customers can meet the team face-to-face and plan their travels. At the heart of the business is a simple philosophy: “being there” – for its customers, its people and the communities it serves.

## From the Company Founders

**Colin Ord, co-owner of There Travel, said:**

Harrogate is already home for There Travel, so opening our first shop here feels like a natural next step for us. Since Jake and I founded the business in 2024, we’ve wanted to build something that is genuinely about being there for people. We’ve established our base here in Harrogate and now we want to strengthen it, become even more visible within the town and give customers a place where they can come in, meet us and start planning their next adventure. This is a really exciting milestone for There Travel and one we’re incredibly proud of.



The new shop will complement There Travel’s homeworking operation, bringing together a physical high street presence with a team able to provide personal travel advice to customers across the country.


**Jake Todhunter, co-owner of There Travel, said:**

Our homeworkers are a huge part of what makes There Travel what it is, and the shop adds another dimension to that. We want customers to be able to connect with There Travel in the way that suits them – whether that’s sitting down with us in Harrogate or speaking to one of our homeworkers.

Our name sums up what we want the business to stand for. It’s about being there – when someone is dreaming about their next holiday, when they’re planning it, when they’re away and when they’re ready to do it all over again.


## Expanding Services Through Advantage Travel Partnership

There Travel is a member of the Advantage Travel Partnership, combining the personal service and flexibility of an independent agency with access to a wide range of travel partners and products. The new Harrogate high street presence will help customers plan everything from family holidays and beach escapes to cruises, luxury travel, and bespoke, tailor-made adventures worldwide.

For Ord and Todhunter, the launch represents more than just a secondary branch. It creates an active physical headquarters for the brand alongside its expanding remote workforce. The retail premises are slated to open to the public late this month, with further details regarding the official opening dates and community events to be announced soon.
