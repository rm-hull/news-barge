Halloween superfans can now *smell* like their favorite spooky staple. 

Candy Corn maker Brach’s is introducing its first-ever fragrance: Ode to Candy Corn, a perfume scented like the iconic yellow, orange and white Halloween candy.

Brach’s is launching its candy corn-inspired eau de parfum on the first day of fall, September 22, the company said in a press release.

“Ode to Candy Corn captures the coziness of the season in every spray and features notes of creamy vanilla, spun sugar and honey,” Brach’s said in a press release.

The perfume, described as a “must-have for candy corn superfans,” will be available for a limited time only.

![Candy Corn maker Brach’s is releasing a limited-time only perfume inspired by its iconic Halloween candy](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/09/10/20/54/Ferrara-Brach-s-Ode-to-Candy-Corn.jpeg)

The company noted in the release that the fragrance is “perfectly paired with a bag of Brach’s Classic Candy Corn.”

“Candy Corn is the true marker of fall and Team Candy Corn is passionate about their fandom, so this year, Brach's is giving them a new way to express their love for the iconic treat," said Kelly Peyser, Director of Brach's and Seasonal Marketing at Ferrara Candy Company.

"With Ode to Candy Corn, we've transformed the unmistakable flavor of Brach's Classic Candy Corn into a wearable fragrance that truly captures the sweetness, warmth and nostalgia that makes this time of year so special."

![Brach’s said the new fragrance pairs well with a bag of their Candy Corn](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/09/10/20/32/Ferrara-Brach-s-Ode-to-Candy-Corn-Flatlay.jpeg)

In addition to the spooky scented perfume, Brach’s is releasing a new Mellowcreme Spooky Graveyard candy, featuring ghosts, bats, tombstones and skulls in four festive flavors, including Caramel Corn, Cotton Candy, Marshmallow and Green Apple.

The new fragrance is available for purchase at CandyCornSeason.com while supplies last.

There will be two drops: One on September 22 and one on October 1. Each 1.7 oz bottle costs $22.
