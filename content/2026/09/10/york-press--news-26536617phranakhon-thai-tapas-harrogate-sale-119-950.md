An innovative Thai restaurant in North Yorkshire, known for its tapas, is on the market for £119,950 leasehold.

Phranakhon Thai Tapas, located in Cheltenham Crescent, Harrogate, is described by property agents Ernest Wilson & Co as an“immaculately presented restaurant and bar occupying one of the most prominent trading positions within this affluent North Yorkshire spa town which attracts thousands of visitors".

The restaurant reportedly achieves a weekly turnover of £9,000.

A spokesperson for Ernest Wilson & Co said: "Our clients have a number of other business interests meaning that this site is run entirely by staff – wonderful potential for enthusiastic owner operator to take the helm here and build on the solid foundations."

The venue is arranged over two floors and features a ground-floor bar and restaurant area with seating for 30 diners, along with a well-equipped kitchen.

The first floor offers additional seating for 24 guests, as well as ladies’ and gents’ toilets and a storage room.

The restaurant combines European influences with authentic Thai cuisine and operates from a leased premises with roadside parking.

The venue is also highly rated on the reviews websites.

TripAdvisor awards it 4.5 stars out of five based on 147 reviews,m ranking it 69th out of 296 restaurants in Harrogate.

Google awards an even better 4.7 stars out of five, based on 343 Google reviews.
