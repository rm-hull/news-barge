EMERGENCY services scrambled to The Broadway, Bradford after a person in a mobility scooter fell while on an escalator.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service (WYFRS) and the Yorkshire Ambulance Service (YAS) were called to the shopping centre just after 11am this morning.

Firefighters freed the person who was stuck under the mobility scooter.

The person was checked over by paramedics at the scene, but did not need to be taken to hospital.

At around 11.30am, one Broadway customer said: "River Island seems to be shut.

"There is a screen blocking views of the entrance."

## What the emergency services said

A spokesperson for the WYFRS said: “We were called at 11.03am today to reports of a person stuck under a mobility scooter.

"One crew attended to free them.”

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "Ambulance resources were called to The Broadway in Bradford just after 11am on Thursday morning where someone had fallen from a mobility scooter on an escalator.

"The patient was checked over on scene, but did not need to be conveyed to hospital."

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted West Yorkshire Police for more information.
