The company behind a well-known York restaurant has entered liquidation owing £90,000 in fines to the Home Office.

Sky Blue York Ltd, which previously ran the Sky Blue Chinese Restaurant at 16 Barbican Road, owes more than £100,000 to creditors, according to a liquidator’s report.

Reports posted on the Companies House says the business was incorporated in May 2021 and entered liquidation on September 1.

Anderson Brookes of Chorley, Lancashire, were appointed liquidators.

Companies House records Kheng Chooi Koay, 50, as the only director.

A further report, known as a statement of affairs, outlined the financial situation.

It said Sky Blue York Ltd has assets of £3,600 cash in hand available for distribution to creditors.

The business had no preferential creditors- those who are paid first.

However, it did have unsecured creditors- those who are paid last- who were owed £102,412.

These consisted of the Home Office, which was owed £90,000; Regency Online Shopping of Leeds, which was owed £10,000; HMRC, which was owed £1,5000; and Companies House, which was owed £912.

This meant considering such creditors, Sky Blue York Ltd had a deficit of £98,812. With issued and called up capital of £1, this gave an overall deficit of £98,813 outstanding.

The Home Office told the press that its enforcement offices have visited the restaurant twice in recent years.

First of all, on 17 July 2025, immigration enforcements officers conducted an illegal working enforcement visit to the premises.

The business was served with a Civil Penalty Referral Notice. The case is currently with the Home Office Civil Penalty Compliance Team and is still ongoing. The Home Office says it cannot provide further details at this stage.

Following a visit from immigration enforcement on December 10 2025, Sky Blue York Ltd. was issued with a civil penalty notice on January 22 2026 for £45,000 for employing one individual without the right to work. The penalty remains unpaid.

  The Sky Blue Chinese Restaurant is still trading under a new company.

Civil Penalty Notices include a fine of up to £45,000 per illegal worker for a first breach and up to a maximum of £60,000 for repeat offending, the Home Office added.

The Sky Blue Chinese Restaurant is well regarded, receiving 4.2 stars on TripAdvisor, based on 39 reviews, ranking it 392nd out of 950 York restaurants.

Google awards four stars out of five, based on 750 reviews.

However, in November 2025, the Sky Blue Chinese Restaurant gained a one-star rating for food hygiene, meaning ‘major improvement necessary.’

The restaurant told the Press in January it had taken measures following the ‘unexpected’ result, adding customers were not at risk.

However, the restaurant received a further one-star rating following a further visit in April this year from City of York Council food hygiene inspectors.

The Sky Blue Chinese Restaurant remains open as a restaurant and takeaway but under new management.
