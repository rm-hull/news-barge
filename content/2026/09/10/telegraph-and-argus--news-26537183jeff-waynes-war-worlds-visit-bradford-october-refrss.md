Jeff Wayne’s War of the Worlds concert tour will visit Bradford Live next month.

For the very first time, audiences will experience a concert rendition of the iconic score, presented by Kilimanjaro.

Audiences will hear the score performed by West End performers, a live band and orchestra, with animation included.

War of the Worlds, based on HG Wells' book, tells the story of a Martian invasion of England in the final years of the 19th Century.

The book was turned into a wildly popular double album by Jeff Wayne with vocals by Richard Burton and Julie Covington.

Now the invasion has taken on a new life as a live concert experience.

Jeff Wayne said: “It’s a great thrill to announce that a special concert rendition of my original double album is to tour the UK in October next year - a first of its kind on our home shores.”

Wayne will step back from the podium for the tour. The production will feature artists from the West End and other major performing mediums, alongside concert elements.

You can see the War of the Worlds at Bradford Live on Thursday October 15 at 7.30pm, with tickets from £31.50.
