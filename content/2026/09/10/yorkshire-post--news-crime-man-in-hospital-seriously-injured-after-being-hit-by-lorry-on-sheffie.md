# Man in hospital seriously injured after being hit by lorry on Sheffield Parkway

## A 49-year-old man has been seriously injured after being hit by a lorry on the Sheffield Parkway, police have appealed for witnesses.

At 10:08pm on Wednesday (Sept 9), police responded to reports of a crash on the Sheffield Parkway, going out of the city centre.

It was reported a Scania lorry was involved in a crash with a pedestrian.

![Sheffield Parkway](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/10/11/26/Sheffield-Parkway.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The pedestrian, a 49-year-old man, received serious injuries and was taken to hospital, where he remains.

The driver of the lorry remained at the scene and assisted officers with their enquiries.

Police have launched an appeal for any witnesses or anyone with information to come forward.

A spokesperson for South Yorkshire Police said: “We are appealing for any witnesses who have not yet spoken to officers to get in touch online or by calling 101, quoting incident number 1050 of 9 September 2026.
