A country house near Malton has had it price reduced after being on the market for two years.

Welham Hall, first listed at £2.75 million in 2024 with Savills, is currently available with Blenkin and Co and Hewetson & Johnson, York, for £2million.

The price reduction for the Norton property was made in August.

The house is set in 11.7 acres of grounds and has seven principal bedrooms and bathrooms, along with reception rooms for entertaining and family life.

The current owner said: "I just fell in love with the setting as soon as I came down the drive, it was the combination off the ancient yews and water that first grabbed me.

"The house itself was so close to what I was looking for, and I could see how I could adapt it and extend it to fit our needs.

"I really don’t want to leave, but the time is right, and I am sure the next owner will love it as I have."

The property has been redesigned and extended by the current owner, creating a home with flexible accommodation over two floors.

The kitchen, serving as the heart of the house, comprises three distinct areas, including a conservatory dining area with French doors opening onto a terrace overlooking the lake.

The principal reception rooms are designed for entertaining, with additional rooms suitable for family life.

For those who work from home, the property includes a study and a separate office.

An attached annex with its own kitchenette, WC, and office, with two first-floor rooms used as an overflow for children's sleepovers, could serve multiple purposes.

The first floor houses a master suite with a walk-in wardrobe, bathroom, dressing room, and French doors opening onto a balcony with garden views.

Three more first-floor bedrooms come with en suites, one with a dressing room or additional bedroom.

A guest wing on the ground floor offers three more bedrooms, all en suite.

The owner has created a guest wing on the ground floor with three bedrooms, all en suite, and there could be up to ten bedrooms in total if needed.

A converted barn across a courtyard provides a games room, kitchenette, gym, and shower room.

The property is approached along a sweeping driveway through Malton and Norton Golf Course, leading to the house's stone-pillared gateway.

The grounds feature a terraced garden, lawns, orchard, arena, tennis court, and paddocks.

The owner also built a timber stable block with four stables, a feed room, tack room, rug store, and small hay barn.
