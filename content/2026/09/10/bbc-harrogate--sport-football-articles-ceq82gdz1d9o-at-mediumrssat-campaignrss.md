**Harrogate Town have signed Leyton Orient midfielder Michael Craig on a three-month loan deal.**

The 23-year-old made 32 appearances for the O's last season but has not featured this campaign.

He could make his debut for Town against Tamworth at home in the National League on Saturday.

"Having that authority in central midfield can help us deliver more controlled performances going forward," boss Simon Weaver told the club website., external
