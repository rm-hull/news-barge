## York Museum Gardens to host first-ever city centre dog show

York Museum Gardens is gearing up to host the city's first-ever city centre dog show *(Image: York Dog Park)*

York Museum Gardens is gearing up to host the city's first-ever city centre dog show.

The event is set for Sunday, September 20, and will feature a range of activities and competitions for canine enthusiasts.

From technical agility classes and scent work workshops to interactive obedience training sessions with True Pawtential, there will be plenty to keep dogs and their owners engaged.

Amanda Owen, the Yorkshire Shepherdess, will be a special guest host and judge for the Dog Show Competition.

The competition will have six categories, including 'Handsomest Dog,' 'Fanciest Female,' 'Cutest Puppy,' and 'Best Trick'.

Pet owners can register their dogs on the day of the event on a first-come, first-served basis.

Registration is £2 per dog, per category.

The schedule also includes a 'Have a Go' Dog Park with all-day agility equipment and a bale racing competition between 12.30pm and 1.30pm.

A pop-up shopping village will offer a variety of treats, toys, and accessories, while food vendors will provide snacks for both pets and people.

The event, hosted in partnership with York Dog Park, will run from 10am to 4pm.

Tickets can be purchased online or from York Art Gallery and the Yorkshire Museum on the day of the event.
