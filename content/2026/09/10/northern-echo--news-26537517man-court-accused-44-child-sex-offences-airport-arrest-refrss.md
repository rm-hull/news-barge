A man accused of 44 sexual offences involving children has appeared before magistrates.

Robert Esterbrook, 79, of no fixed abode, was arrested at Leeds Bradford Airport on Monday evening (September 7) on his return to the UK following his departure in 2021.

An arrest warrant was subsequently issued.

Esterbrook was taken into custody and charged with:

• Two counts of rape

• One count of attempted rape

• Two counts of sexual assault

• One count of assault by penetration

• 28 counts of indecent assault

• Two counts of gross indecency

• Two counts of inciting a girl to commit an act of gross indecency

• Two counts of inciting a boy to commit an act of gross indecency

• Four counts of buggery

The historic offences are alleged to have taken place between the late 1970s and late 2000s.

The 79-year-old appeared at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court yesterday (September 9).

A spokesperson from Durham Police said: “No pleas were entered and he was remanded into custody.”

Esterbrook is next expected to appear at Durham Crown Court on October 7.
