A BON Jovi tribute act is set to perform in Ilkley this weekend.

The Bon Jovi Experience, the only show endorsed by Jon Bon Jovi himself, will be at King's Hall this Sunday, September 13.

Proceedings start at 7.30pm.

The Bon Jovi Experience will feature hits such as Livin’ On a Prayer, Always, It’s My Life, You Give Love a Bad Name, and Bad Medicine.

The show will be led by frontman Tony Pearce, who will be joined by a group of musicians.

A spokesperson said: "Get ready to rock your way through an unforgettable journey with the only show to be endorsed by Jon Bon Jovi himself.

"Immerse yourself in a phenomenal celebration of Bon Jovi’s timeless classics as The Bon Jovi Experience recreates the magic that defined an era with electrifying energy – transporting you back to the heyday of stadium rock.

"Whether you’re a lifelong Bon Jovi devotee or a new fan seeking an unforgettable night out, this show promises an experience like no other."

Tickets are £31 and can be purchased online HERE.

A maximum of 10 tickets can be booked online. All discounts are subject to availability.
