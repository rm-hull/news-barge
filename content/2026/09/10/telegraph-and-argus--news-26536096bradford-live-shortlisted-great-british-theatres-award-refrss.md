BRADFORD Live has been announced as a finalist for the Great Renovation award at the Great British Theatres Awards.

The awards, organised by Theatres Trust as part of its 50th anniversary celebrations, honour theatres and individuals making significant contributions to the UK’s cultural landscape.

Bradford Live reopened following a £50 million restoration led by Bradford Council in partnership with venue operator Trafalgar Entertainment.

The award category recognises theatre retrofit or refurbishment projects that successfully preserve a venue’s character while improving accessibility, comfort, technical facilities, and sustainability.

Dame Rosemary Squire and Sir Howard Panter, joint CEOs of Trafalgar Entertainment, said: "Bradford Live is a much-loved and iconic venue, rich in history and cherished by countless Bradfordians, so to receive this Great British Theatre Award nomination is a great honour.

"We thank the visionary project teams who brought Bradford Live roaring back to life—Lee Craven, Kirsten Branston, Chris Morrell, the team at Bradford Live and Bradford Council—who have transformed this glorious landmark venue into a modern and truly world-class destination, whilst honouring and preserving its stunning Art Deco heritage.

"Since reopening the doors one year ago, we’ve welcomed thousands of visitors through our doors and established ourselves at the heart of the city's cultural and live entertainment scene.

"From our sell-out opening shows with The Prat Pack and Bill Bailey, to unforgettable nights with Kaiser Chiefs, Gorillaz and Radio 1 Anthems, we've been proud to bring world-class entertainment to Bradford, creating lasting memories for thousands of visitors, and we're incredibly proud of the role we've played in bringing people together, contributing to Bradford's cultural renaissance."

Bradford Live is one of four finalists in the Great Renovation category, alongside the Arts Theatre in Cambridge, Citizens Theatre in Glasgow, and the Granville Theatre in Ramsgate.

Joshua McTaggart, CEO of Theatres Trust, said: "From rescuing buildings that have been empty for many years through to carefully reinvigorating much-loved venues, the Great Renovation finalists show how theatres can be equipped for new generations without losing what makes them special.

"Theatres Trust knew there was a need to celebrate theatres, but our judges had an incredibly hard time selecting the finalists.

"I want to extend my congratulations to every one of them.

"We can’t wait to celebrate our Great British Theatres in style this November."

The winners will be revealed at the Great British Theatres Awards ceremony at Central Hall Westminster on Monday, November 2, 2026.
