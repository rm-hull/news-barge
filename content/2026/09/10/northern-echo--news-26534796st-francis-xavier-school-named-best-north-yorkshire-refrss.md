A Richmond school has topped a new North Yorkshire ranking of state secondary schools based on inspections, exam results, pupil progress and attendance.

St Francis Xavier School on Darlington Road came first among North Yorkshire’s 41 state secondary schools in the FindGreatSchools ranking.

The joint Catholic and Church of England voluntary academy scored 81 out of 100, putting it in FindGreatSchools’ “Strong” category.

FindGreatSchools says it uses public Department for Education and Ofsted data to assess schools on inspections, attainment, progress and engagement.

  St Francis Xavier School on Darlington Road, Richmond 

*(Image: GOOGLE)*

St Francis Xavier recorded an average Attainment 8 score of 54.6 in 2024/25, against a national average of 46.

Some 61.1 per cent of pupils achieved grade 5 or above in English and maths GCSEs, compared with 45.4 per cent nationally; 87.6 per cent achieved grade 4 or above.

Overall absence was 4.2 per cent, below the national rate of 6.2 per cent, while persistent absence was 7.5 per cent against 15.8 per cent nationally.

An Ofsted inspection in November 2024 found safeguarding standards were met, while it rated the quality of education, behaviour and attitudes, personal development, and leadership and management Outstanding.

Alastair Hodgson, founder of FindGreatSchools, said: "School choice runs on playground rumour and one neighbour's opinion.

"We built the index so parents in Richmondshire can see what the numbers actually say for every school on the same basis.

"St Francis Xavier comes out top in North Yorkshire, and they earned it in the data: we don't take payment from schools, and there is no way to buy a place on the list."

The website weights inspections at 25 per cent, attainment and progress at 30 per cent each, and engagement, including attendance and destinations after leaving school, at 15 per cent.

Exam and progress figures generally use a three-year weighted average, with the latest results carrying the most weight.
