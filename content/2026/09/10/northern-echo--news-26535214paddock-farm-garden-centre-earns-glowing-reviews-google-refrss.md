A Darlington garden centre, home to nine themed gardens, has received glowing reviews from customers.

Paddock Farm Nursery and Water Gardens has an almost perfect rating of 4.5 stars from nearly 1,000 Google reviews.

The centre, on West Lane in Dalton-on-Tees, offers a diverse selection of homeware and garden ware goods, plants and birdfeed.

One review said: "Love this place.

"Quality of products is outstanding and not too badly priced with each section of the garden centre well laid out and labelled.

"The main shop holds the aquatics, again, well presented and reasonable and the staff are lovely, always happy to help or just chat and give advice.

"Water garden is a nice extra that most would charge for.

"A lovely bit of tranquillity in a hectic world and a great café to finish off a visit."

The centre is also home to the AquaPaddock, a tropical fish house where visitors will find a selection of popular and unique tropical fish.

Paddock Farm boasts nine themed gardens - Woodmans Lodge, Mediterranean Garden, The Stream, The Wildlife Garden, Gothic Garden, Japanese Garden, Bamboo Garden, Joe Bloggs Garden, and the Waterfall Garden.

The Paddock Farm Tea Room receives consistent praise from customers.

One person left a five-star review and said: "The café was absolutely amazing.

"All freshly cooked food with daily specials, and even sausages for the sausage dogs which was a brilliant touch.

"We’ll definitely be back just for the café alone."

The café offers a selection of food including breakfast sandwiches, fish and chips, afternoon tea, Sunday roasts and more.
