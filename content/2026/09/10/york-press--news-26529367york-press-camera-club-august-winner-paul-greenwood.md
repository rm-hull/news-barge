## This adorable waving seal stole the show in The Press Camera Club competition

This photo by Paul Greenwood of a 'waving' seal on the beach won over everyone at The Press when choosing the winning August photo. *(Image: Paul Greenwood)*

WE asked Camera Club members to focus on the theme of #outandabout26 for our latest competition.

It was a fitting choice as people took advantage of the August weather to explore places on their doorstep and further afield, with nature and historic landmarks both providing inspiration.

  This photo by Paul Greenwood of a 'waving' seal on the beach won over everyone at The Press when choosing the winning August photo. 

*(Image: Paul Greenwood)*

We've been treated to a variety of fantastic images - thank you to everyone for sharing your outings.

Spoilt for choice, there was one photo that made us all smile - congratulations to Paul Greenwood for your cheery photo of a waving seal which was our winning photo for August.

  Isaac Savage took this stunning bird's eye view of the city's skyline featuring York Minster, Bootham Bar and part of the city walls.


Runners up included Isaac Savage, who took a brilliant stunning bird's eye view of the city's skyline featuring York Minster, Bootham Bar and part of the city walls.

We also loved the once-in-a-lifetime solar eclipse photo by York photographer Paul 'Mortz' Mortimer who was in Majorca at the time.

  Paul 'Mortz' Mortimer was in Majorca for this once-in-a-lifetime solar eclipse photo.


Sandra Michelle Hillyer captured a peaceful moment in time, with her lovely and serene scene at Knaresborough Viaduct while Paul Anthony White ventured to Towton for his striking photo of moody skies over fields.

  A lovely, peace scene at Knaresborough Viaduct by Sandra Michelle Davies.


September’s theme is #Storytime - as every picture tells a story. Happy snapping and good luck.

  Paul Anthony White was out and about to take this gorgeous scene of moody skies at Towton.

The Press Camera Club brings together amateur photographers to share their work, swap tips and take part in monthly prize competitions.

Membership is open to everyone regardless of their age, experience and equipment. Thanks to the ubiquity of mobile phones nowadays everyone is a photographer!

To join the club, visit The Press Camera Club on Facebook - we’d love to see what York and the surrounding area looks like through your lens.
