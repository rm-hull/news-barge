A Bishop Auckland bakery has been shortlisted for a North East award.

The Hedgehog Bakery, on Newgate Street is in the running for The Northern Echo’s Best Bakery award.

The bakery offers a wide selection of products, including cookie pizzas, cookie pies, sausage rolls, brownies and more.

   

*(Image: THE HEDGEHOG BAKERY)*

Customers can also taste-test a variety of products by creating their very own eight item treat collection.

   

*(Image: THE HEDGEHOG BAKERY)*

In a Facebook post, the bakery said they feel "blessed" to be nominated.

The post read: "Hello lovely followers, we are in the top 10 of County Durham's Best Bakeries again.

"This is purely down to being nominated so a colossal thank you to everyone that has nominated us.

"We feel very blessed to have been in the top 10 for three or four years now."

The bakery receives glowing reviews online.

One customer left a five-star review and said: "Really great sausage rolls in a good choice of different flavours, and the sweet treats are out of this world.

"Products well presented, well made and reasonably priced.

"Absolutely recommend grabbing something from this bakery if you are in Bishop Auckland.

The store also offers delivery for those who can't make it into the shop and gift cards.

Readers can vote with coupons printed in the newspaper, choosing their favourite from the 10 finalists.

Post coupons to The Northern Echo Best Bakery 2026, LOCALLY House, 1 Dalston Road, Carlisle, CA2 5NP.
