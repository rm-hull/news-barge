TWO men have been arrested after a Bradford jewellers was "attacked".

Police were called to reports of a burglary at Royal Jewellers on Manchester Road just before 1.30pm today.

Two men were arrested at the scene on suspicion of aggravated burglary.

When the T&A visited the scene, a police cordon was still in place and officers were in attendance.

Forensics were being conducted inside the shop.

Broken glass could be seen on the floor where police forced their way in.

  Royal Jewellers on Manchester Road was the subject of a burglary earlier today 

*(Image: Newsquest)*


## Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At about 1.21pm today (Thursday), police received a report of business premises being attacked in Manchester Road, Bradford.

"Officers attended and arrested two men on suspicion of aggravated burglary.

"Enquiries are continuing."
