A County Durham great-grandmother who once worked in munitions has celebrated her 103rd birthday with family and care home staff.

Clara Harrison marked her birthday at Brancepeth Court, Willington Care Village, on September 3, including a trip to Lanchester Garden Centre.

Clara said: “I have had a lovely birthday; it’s been lovely. Thank you all. It will take me a week to recover from all the excitement.”

Her advice to live a long life is: “Always do what makes you happy”

  Clara Harrison celebrating her 103rd birthday at Brancepeth Court, Willington Care Village 

*(Image: SUBMITTED)*

Clara was born in September 1923 and grew up on Willington Colliery Row before moving to School Row in Oakenshaw, near Crook.

Clara worked at the munitions factory in Aycliffe before marrying miner Richard, known as Dick, in 1942.

She left work to raise their children and run the family home.

Richard died 33 years ago. Clara remembers him as “the kindest man alive”.

  Clara Harrison

Clara has three children—John, Anne and Richard—five grandchildren and four great-grandchildren.

After a lifetime of work and caring for her family, Clara moved into Brancepeth Court in Willington in March 2026.

Natalie Henderson, care manager at Brancepeth Court, said: “Clara is the life and soul with a fabulous sense of humour.”

  Clara Harrison 

*(Image: SUBMITTED)*

“She’s always making the care assistants laugh and offering life advice to younger generations.”

For her birthday, Clara tried a Colin the Caterpillar cake for the first time, after saying she had “never tried one but would like to”.

  Clara Harrison celebrating her 103rd birthday at Brancepeth Court, Willington Care Village

Clara’s birthday was packed with surprises, visitors, family, friends and staff.

The care home’s activities team accompanied her to Lanchester Garden Centre.

She had another party on September 5, with family, friends, fellow residents and staff enjoying plenty of laughter and a buffet from the kitchen team.

Natalie Henderson, care manager at Brancepeth Court, added: “It was an absolute honour and pleasure to be part of Clara’s 103rd birthday; it’s so lovely to see everyone from all generations come together to celebrate this achievement.”

“I am also incredibly proud of my team here at Brancepeth Court for their hard work and contribution to making Clara’s birthday as special as her.”
