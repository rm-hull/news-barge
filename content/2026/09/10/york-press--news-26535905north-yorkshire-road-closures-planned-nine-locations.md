Nine North Yorkshire roads are due to face temporary vehicle restrictions from September 14.

North Yorkshire Council has made an Order covering Market Place, Pickering, where vehicles will be prohibited, subject to access to premises, during footway repairs and resurfacing.

The restriction is listed between September 14 and November 30, although the Order’s maximum duration is October 9.

The council intends to make Orders for eight further locations - with all closures scheduled from October 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027.

Each closure will be subject to access to premises, shown by traffic signs and supported by a signed local alternative route where appropriate.

Bondgate, Helmsley, is due to be closed for seven nights for carriageway surfacing works.

Gray Lane and Yan Brow to Gray Lane, Keldholme, will be restricted for five days for carriageway surfacing works.

Staxton Hill to Binnington Ness, Staxton, will be closed for three days for the same type of work.

Wass to Jerry Carr Bank, Wass, and Jerry Carr Bank, Ampleforth, are due to face restrictions for seven nights for carriageway surfacing works.

Whitby Road, Kington, is listed for a three-night closure because of works.

The notice does not provide further details about the nature of those works.

Langton Road, Norton, is due to be restricted for five days for carriageway surfacing works.

Wilton Road to Weasdale, Wilton, and Weasdale to Partings Farm, Allerston, are listed for seven nights of restrictions for carriageway surfacing works.

North Lodge to Potticar Bank, Hovingham, is due to face a five-day closure because of works.

The notice does not provide further details about those works.

The dates are windows in which the restrictions may operate, rather than confirmation that each closure will run continuously throughout the period.

The specific closure periods will be indicated by signs at the locations.

The notices are Orders 2026 Nos. 522, 541, 542, 543, 544, 546, 550, 551 and 552.

Residents and motorists can contact the council’s Customer Service Centre on 0300 131 2 131. Information is also available through the council’s roadworks map at northyorks.gov.uk/roadworks-map.
