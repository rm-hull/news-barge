A full-size Metro train replica is now helping teach thousands of children about railway safety.

The mock-up cab, part of the Tyne and Wear Metro’s new fleet, has been installed at the SafetyWorks! education centre in Newcastle, where it is part of a hands-on learning environment for school groups, youth groups, and community organisations.

Donated by Nexus and Stadler, the replica was originally used to familiarise drivers ahead of the new fleet’s arrival, but it has now been repurposed as a key part of immersive safety sessions at the centre.


Deborah Rodenby, youth liaison officer at Nexus, said: "The replica of the new Metro train is a vital new tool for engaging young people when they visit SafetyWorks.

"It’s going to really grab their attention and that will make it easier for us to get the key railway safety messages across.

"It’s fantastic to give the cab mock-up a permanent new home.

"It looks amazing.

"It has supported our Metro drivers with adapting to the new trains, and now it’s got a new lease of life as a teaching aid, helping thousands of children learn about the dangers of trespassing on the lines."

The cab forms part of a realistic Metro environment at the centre, which includes tracks, platforms, ticket machines, and a level crossing.

The immersive setting helps children understand the risks associated with trespassing and the dangers of high-voltage overhead lines, while also encouraging respect for public transport.

Michael Richardson, head of fleet and depot replacement at Nexus, said: "It’s heartwarming to see that it’s now getting used to educate young people in railway safety and accident prevention.

"As a Metro operator we are committed to youth liaison work to ensure children understand the dangers of trespassing on our lines."

SafetyWorks! is a multi-agency facility used by the Tyne and Wear Fire and Rescue Service, Northumbria Police, Nexus, and local authorities.

It delivers safety education to around 5,000 school children each year.

Steve Thomas, head of prevention and education at Tyne and Wear Fire and Rescue Service, said: "We are very grateful to Nexus and Stadler for their generous donation of the replica Metro train."

"It will help ensure that the school groups visiting SafetyWorks! learn invaluable lessons about the dangers of playing on or near railway lines, alongside wider safety advice that could ultimately help save lives."

The replica was supplied by Swiss train manufacturer Stadler in 2021 and was used to help familiarise drivers with the new trains before they entered service.

It was built in Germany, shipped to the UK, and assembled at the Gosforth Metro depot.
