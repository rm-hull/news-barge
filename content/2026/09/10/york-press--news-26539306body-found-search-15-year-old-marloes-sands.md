## Body found in search for teenager from Yorkshire swept out to sea

Coastguard crews helped in the search. *(Image: Newsquest)*

A body has been found in the search for a missing teenager who got into difficulty in the sea in Pembrokeshire whilst on a family holiday from Yorkshire.

The 15-year-old boy got into difficulty in the sea at Marloes Sands on Sunday, August 30, prompting a large-scale emergency response.

A force spokesperson said: "Sadly, we can confirm that a body has been found during the search for a 15-year-old boy who was reported to have gotten into difficulty at Marloes Sands, Milford Haven on Sunday, August 30.

"Next of kin have been informed, and our thoughts are with them at this time."

The death is not being treated as suspicious.

  A body has been found more than a week after a search for a missing 15-year-old boy at Marloes Sands was stood down 

*(Image: Mandy Johns)*

Formal identification will take place in due course.

Support has been offered to the boy's family, police say.

Teams from the police, HM Coastguard and the RNLI launched a search for the teenager who was visiting the area on holiday from Yorkshire.

Searches continued for several days as teams combed the coastline and surrounding waters.

The Press has contacted the force to clarify the location of where in Yorkshire the teenager is from, and for further details.
