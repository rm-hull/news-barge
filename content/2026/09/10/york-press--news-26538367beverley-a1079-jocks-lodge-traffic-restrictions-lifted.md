TRAFFIC restrictions on a major road which links York to Beverley and Hull are about to be lifted.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council said traffic restrictions on the A1079 near Beverley will be removed on the evening of Friday, September 11, allowing full access from all the approaches.

Currently, one lane is closed of the dual carriageway both ways with speed restrictions of 30mph as the road layout is changed.

The works are part of the £87million A164/Jock's Lodge junction scheme which aimed to reduce congestion on one of the East Riding's busiest routes.

  


A council spokesperson said the rest of the traffic restrictions on the A164 - which connects Beverley with the Humber Bridge and the A63 - will be removed "in the coming weeks."

However, restrictions on the A1079 from Kingswood in Hull will remain due to ongoing National Grid works, unrelated to the Jock's Lodge scheme, the spokesperson added.

The council thanked motorists for their patience during the construction period.
