An uninsured BMW driver has been jailed for 42 months after admitting he caused the death of a seven-year-old boy in West Yorkshire.

Nathan Shaw, 35, was sentenced at Leeds Crown Court over the death of Jack Rooke.

Jack was killed in a collision on August 30, 2023, with emergency services called at about 6pm.

Shaw had been driving a BMW X4 when it collided with the boy on Longfield Avenue, Golcar - a residential street on the Sycamore estate.

Jack received emergency treatment but died a short time later.

Shaw, of Tudor Street, Linthwaite, Huddersfield, pleaded guilty to causing death while driving uninsured and two counts of attempting to pervert the course of justice.

He was also banned from driving for 57 months.

Shaw was arrested at the scene and later charged after an investigation by the Major Collision Enquiry Team (MCET).

Jack’s mother described the death’s impact on the family in a victim personal statement.

She said every day was a struggle without her “beautiful and loving boy” and that her life had been wrecked.

She described the impact on Jack’s sisters, including Mya, who had lost her best friend.

Jack’s mother said: “I can’t accept that Jack has gone and I never will.”

“I replay his funeral, me having to carry my little boy in a coffin.”

“Looking at him in the chapel of rest wanting and wishing him to open his eyes.”

“Travelling in the ambulance with Jack, seeing the paramedics trying to save his life, then being told that he had gone, is a parent’s worst nightmare that no-one should have to go through.”

“Leaving the hospital without them having to tell your other children.”

“How will I navigate that and deal with it for the rest of my life?”

“However, I have to for the sake of my other children, but it is hard. It’s hard to parent when you have been killed inside, I will never be who I used to be.”

Detective Sergeant Nina Roper, who led the investigation, said: “The death of Jack has been an absolute tragedy for his family as his mother’s victim personal statement so movingly illustrates.”

She said Jack’s death had devastated his family and that the scale of their loss was difficult to understand.

“Our thoughts remain with them following the sentencing of Nathan Shaw.”
