MEMBERS of the public are being invited to help shape the future of NHS services by standing as governors.

Bradford District Care NHS Foundation Trust is calling on people in Bradford, Airedale, Wharfedale, and Craven to nominate themselves in its latest Council of Governors election.

Sarah Jones, from Trust in Common, said: "Our governors play an important and influential role.

"They represent the voice of their communities and make a valuable contribution to our Trust’s work.

"Nominations are now open for our latest governor elections and I would welcome anyone that has been thinking about making a difference for local people, or who may be looking for opportunities to develop their skills, to come forward."

This year, six seats are available across five constituencies: two in Shipley and one each in Bradford South, Bradford West, Craven, and Keighley.

Interested individuals must be Trust members to stand for election.

Nominations close at 5pm on Friday, October 2, 2026.
