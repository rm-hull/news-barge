Tadcaster-based Sam Smith’s Brewery has reopened 18 of its pubs in the past three months, according to a Malton man.

Tom Taverner has updated his national list of Sam Smith’s pubs, which are open and which are closed.

Tom posted on Facebook: “The list now shows 313 pubs with 167 identified as open, 137 closed and 9 status unknown.”

  As previously reported, Tom carries out the research by trawling the Facebook groups concerned with beer and the Sam Smith’s brewery as well as seeking out job vacancies the brewery posts on GumTree as well as the brewery’s own **runapub.co.uk** website.

Tom began doing this in 2024, bringing together several former listings of the Sam Smith’s openings and closings, which he regularly updates to keep relevant.

The Sam Smith’s pubs that have reopened since the start of June, he says, include the Golden Ball, Scarborough; Angel & White Horse, Tadcaster; Ebor Inn, Bishopthorpe; Wellington Inn, York; Tankard Inn, Rufforth; and the Red Lion at Bramham, near Wetherby.

Tom says there are rumours of other pubs reopening in Leeds and elsewhere, though none of the pubs he mentions are in our region.

He continued: “We have lost two pubs from the list, both sold at auction, the Corporation Taps at Doncaster went for around £230,000 and the Brown Cow at Bradford, £262,000.

  “The brewery is now focussed on advertising pub management opportunities through *runapub.co.uk**.”*

Sam Smith’s Brewery appears to have stepped up its efforts to find managers for its pubs.

The Press understands a recruitment manager has been taken on to help the process. An advertisement for such a role was placed online in May.

However, the brewery did not respond to our requests for comment on this and how many other pubs we can expect to re-open soon.
