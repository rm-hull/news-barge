McDonald’s has confirmed the opening date of its new Darlington drive-thru

The fast food giant has announced that it will be opening the doors to its Faverdale restaurant, on Faverdale Industrial Estate, on Wednesday, September 23.

The venue has been under construction since March 2025 and has faced a series of setbacks, having originally been scheduled to open last December.

McDonald’s teased customers last week that the site would be opening soon - and in a new timelapse construction video confirmed the launch date.

  Opening dates for the site have been pushed back several times, __with hopes to open in early 2026 and then again in late summer.__

The restaurant will mark the international chain’s third branch in the town, the others being on North Road Industrial Estate and at Morton Park.

The wider Faverdale development has been in the pipeline for several years, having initially been delayed over the potential impact on existing businesses and jobs.

Earlier plans for the site included McDonald’s, Costa Coffee and Taco Bell, as well as additional retail units and an industrial unit.

Costa Coffee is still due to open a drive-thru there - while Taco Bell later pulled out and has instead opened on North Road in the former KFC.
