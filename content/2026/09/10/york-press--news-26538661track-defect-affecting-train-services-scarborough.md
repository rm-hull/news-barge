A TRACK defect has caused a speed restriction to trains running from a North Yorkshire town and some services may be cancelled.

York-based train operator Northern said services between Scarborough and Bridlington are being disrupted due to a safety inspection of the lines in the area.

A company spokesperson said Network Rail is at the scene.

  


Services between Bridlington and Hull may be cancelled or delayed by up to 30 minutes as a result, Northern said.

Train services can run between Filey and Scarborough, the train operator said, but may be subject to delays of up to 20 minutes and may be revised.

The company said Northern services between York and Bridlington are not affected.

Northern said it has lifted all ticket restrictions in the affected areas, including Advance and Peak restrictions, for the duration of the disruption.
