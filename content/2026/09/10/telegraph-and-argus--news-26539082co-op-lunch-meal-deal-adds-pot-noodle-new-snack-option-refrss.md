Pot Noodle fans can now grab their favourite noodles as the snack in a Co-op meal deal, in a new shake-up giving shoppers more lunchtime choices.

Selected Pot Noodle flavours have been added to both the supermarket's Core and Premium Meal Deals, meaning customers can pick one as their snack alongside a qualifying main and drink.

The new option is available in stores and online from September 9.

The Pot Noodle flavours included are:

- Pot Noodle Sweet & Sour 90g
- Pot Noodle Original Curry 90g
- Pot Noodle Flame Grilled Steak 90g
- Pot Noodle Chicken & Mushroom 90g
- Pot Noodle Beef & Tomato 90g

The Core Meal Deal costs £3.85 for Co-op members and £4.25 for non-members.

The Premium Meal Deal costs £5.50 for members and £6 for non-members.

The change comes alongside a string of new Co-op offers - here are some of the best offers to look out for.

The five side options include Bombay Potatoes, Lentil Dahl, Indian Spiced Potato Wedges, Tandoori Spiced Vegetables and Pilau Rice.

The range is available in stores and online from September 9 to October 27.

Non-members pay £9.
