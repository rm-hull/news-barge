A CHARITY shop is calling on the community to donate unwanted items to support older people through the winter.

The Age UK shop in Pudsey is taking part in The Great Winter Bag Drop and is encouraging residents to donate good-quality preloved items to help raise funds for essential services supporting older people.

Kirsty Woods, shop manager at Age UK Pudsey, said: "We're asking everyone in the local area to get involved in The Great Winter Bag Drop, by having a clear out and donating any good-quality items you no longer want or need to the Age UK Pudsey shop at 11 Church Lane, Pudsey, Leeds LS28 7LD.

"Whether it's clothing, shoes, accessories, books, homeware or other pre-loved treasures – every donation can make a difference to older people this winter."

Funds raised through donations help support services like Age UK’s Telephone Friendship Service, free and confidential Advice Line and The Silver Line Helpline.

These services offer companionship, support, and advice to vulnerable older people, especially during the colder months when isolation can be more acute.

Donors can also boost the value of their gifts by signing up for Gift Aid, allowing Age UK to claim an extra 25p for every £1 raised.
