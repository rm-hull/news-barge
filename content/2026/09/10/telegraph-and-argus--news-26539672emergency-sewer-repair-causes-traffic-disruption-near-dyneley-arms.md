MOTORISTS are being warned of delays as Yorkshire Water carries out an emergency sewer repair on Pool Bank New Road.

A contraflow system is currently in operation at the junction of Pool Bank New Road and Leeds Road (A660), near the Dyneley Arms, while the work is completed.

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson said: “We’re working as quickly as we can to complete the emergency repair and subsequent reinstatement. The contraflow system is in place for the safety of our colleagues and road users.

“As soon as we can get traffic flowing as normal, we will, and we thank everyone for their patience in the meantime.”

Additional traffic management measures are also in place further down Pool Bank New Road. Two-way temporary traffic lights have been installed around 300 metres from the junction to allow tankers access to the site. The tankers are supporting the sewer repair operation and helping to prevent any impact on the environment.

The lights will be manually controlled between 7am and 7pm.

Yorkshire Water has confirmed the emergency sewer repair is unrelated to its ongoing mains replacement project in the Pool area, where clean water pipework is currently being replaced on Arthington Lane.
