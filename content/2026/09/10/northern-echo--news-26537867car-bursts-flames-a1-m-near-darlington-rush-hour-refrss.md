A car burst into flames on the A1(M) near Darlington during the evening rush hour.

Emergency services were called at around 5.39pm on Wednesday (September 9) to a car fire on the southbound carriageway of the A1(M) near Darlington.

A spokesperson from Durham Police said: “Police received a call just after 5.50pm yesterday (September 9) following reports of a car on fire on the southbound carriageway of the A1(M) between junction 59 and 60.”

It is understood no injuries were reported.

A spokesperson for County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service said: “At 5.39pm yesterday (Wednesday), we received a report of a vehicle fire on the A1 southbound between Junction 59 and Junction 60, Bradbury Interchange.

“Two crews, from Spennymoor and Newton Aycliffe, attended to put out the fire and left at 6.20pm.”
