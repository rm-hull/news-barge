COUNTERFEIT notes have been passed in an "over the doorstep" sale in York.

North Yorkshire Police said it happened on Friday, September 4 and involved a member of the public selling a mobile phone.

  


A force spokesperson said after the transaction it was identified that counterfeit notes were used.

North Yorkshire Police have released an image of a man who the force said it believed may have information which could help with its investigation.

Anyone who can provide information to help with police enquiries is asked to contact PC Colin Irvine at [colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk), quoting reference 12260173768.
