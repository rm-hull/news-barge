AN Olympic medallist cut the ribbon to open a new gymnastics centre in Bradford.

Harry Hepworth, who took home bronze in the vault in Paris two years ago, officially opened TK Gymnastics on Barkerend Road on Tuesday.

The opening was made possible by a £41,000 Better Youth Spaces Grant, which funded essential equipment and improvements for the club.

Mr Hepworth, who attended Prince Henry's Grammar School in Otley, said: "It’s been an amazing opening.

"I’ve said a lot today about the importance of gymnastics at a grassroots level and this is a great example of it.

"Gymnastics takes a lot of discipline but it’s also lots of fun."


## Olympic medallist spoke to youngsters

He spoke with young gymnasts and showcased some of his skills during the opening event.

He said: "I’ve got the World Championships coming up in five weeks so I’ve mainly been training for that.

He also stressed the importance of enjoyment in sport from a young age.

"The main thing is that they have fun, it is serious when you get to a higher level, but you’ll never keep at it without enjoying it."

## What is in the new centre?

The new facility, a converted construction training centre, now houses two-fully equipped gymnastics spaces, a ballet studio, which also hosts taekwondo lessons.

TK Gymnastics Bradford says it relocated to the larger premises to better serve the community and increase its reach.

Kirsty Thornton, head coach and director, said: "It’s great because we’re so central, so many people can reach us."

She spoke about the success of the open day, adding: “We’ve had some great organisations dropping by, Ian and Jack Ormondroyd came at 12 to see us open.”

## How many people use it?

Established as a Community Interest Company in 2022, the club now supports more than 350 participants each week and hopes to grow further.

The £41,000 grant funded a new gymnastics floor, equipment for a SEN sensory room, and kitchen facilities to support healthy eating and holiday camps.

The new floor alone cost £30,000.

Ms Thornton said: "It’s well worth it, we have 350 children on this every week.

She said the investment will help the club welcome even more young people from across Bradford.

## 'The kids can’t get enough'

Ms Thornton said: "We are incredibly proud that this investment will enable us to reach even more young people in Bradford.

"It will help us create a welcoming, high-quality space where young people can be active, try new things and spend time in a positive environment with people who genuinely care."

Lorna Brickman has been bringing her 9 year old daughter for three years to the organisation.

She said “It’s brilliant, we come three times a week for a total of 12 hours.

"The kids can’t get enough, it’s non-stop they’re even practising when they get home.”
