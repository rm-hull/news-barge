A man has appeared before York Magistrates Court accused of blackmail, vandalising one house and threatening to drive a vehicle into a second house.

Connor Shannon, 20, is alleged to have committed all three offences in York this month against four people.

He is accused of demanding £1,000 with menaces from the first alleged victim on Saturday, September 5.

On the same day, he is alleged to have threatened a second alleged victim that he would “put a Jeep through the window” of a house belonging to a third person.

He is also accused of causing criminal damage worth more than £5,000 to a second house occupied by a fourth person on September 7.

Shannon, of no fixed address, was not asked to enter a plea to any of the charges after York magistrates heard the blackmail charge could only be heard by a judge and jury.

They sent the case to York Crown Court where Shannon will appear for a plea and trial preparation hearing on October 12.

He will then be expected to enter his plea to the three charges.

Shannon was remanded in custody at the end of the hearing.
