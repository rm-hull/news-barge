Thousands of runners taking part in this weekend’s Great North Run will be greeted with messages of encouragement from friends, family and supporters as they make their way from Newcastle to South Shields.

Rail operator Lumo has teamed up with charity Daft as a Brush Cancer Patient Care to display messages of support on digital screens positioned along the 13.1-mile route of the world-famous half marathon on Sunday.

The screens will show good-luck messages from the public to give runners an extra boost around the course.

Messages will appear at key points including the Tyne Bridge, Whitemare Pool and South Shields.

Jen Clare, Lumo’s service delivery director, said the Great North Run was one of the biggest events in the North East calendar.

She said: “This run is one of the biggest moments in the North East calendar, bringing together thousands of people in support of incredible causes.

“Whether someone is running for a personal challenge, in memory of a loved one or to raise money for charity, we know how much a message of support can mean when the miles start to feel tough.

“We can't wait to see the messages appear along the route and help create an unforgettable day for participants.”


The campaign will also raise the profile of Daft as a Brush Cancer Patient Care, a North East charity providing free transport to cancer patients travelling to hospital appointments.

Founded in Newcastle, the charity helps thousands of people reach treatment each year, easing the travel burden on patients and their families.

Over the past year, Lumo customers and staff have raised thousands of pounds for the organisation through donations and fundraising events.

Daft as a Brush chief executive Andrea Glover welcomed the backing.

She said: “We’ve been genuinely blown away by the support we’ve received from Lumo over the past year.

“This advertising space is incredibly valuable to us. It will put us in the spotlight on one of the biggest days of the year for our region and spread the message about what we do even further.

“Good luck to all of the runners and thank you to our friends at Lumo and Smart Outdoor.”
