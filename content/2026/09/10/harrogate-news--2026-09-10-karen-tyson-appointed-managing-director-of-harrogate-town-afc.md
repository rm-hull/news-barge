Harrogate Town AFC have appoined Karen Tyson as Managing Director, subject to regulatory approval, bringing more than 20 years of senior leadership experience across hospitality, sport and business to the club.

Karen previously spent 18 years at Rudding Park in Harrogate, where she held senior leadership positions with responsibility across commercial strategy, sales, marketing, revenue management, pricing, membership and operations.

During her time at Rudding she played a pivotal role in the development of the business while leading and developing high-performing teams and delivering strong commercial performance and operational excellence.

Most recently Karen spent a year as Chief Commercial Officer at York RLFC, with responsibility across commercial strategy, partnerships, sponsorship, hospitality, revenue generation and wider business development.


**Club Chairman Irving Weaver said:**

We are delighted to welcome Karen to Harrogate Town. She brings a wealth of experience, a strong connection to Harrogate and, most importantly, shares our belief in the importance of people, community and building the club in the right way.

Karen joins us at an exciting time for the football club and I know she will bring enormous energy and experience to the role.



**Karen Tyson said:**

I am delighted to be joining Harrogate Town. To be back in Harrogate and have the opportunity to play a part at the heart of the club is something I am very excited about and is absolutely the right next step for me.

Sport has a unique ability to bring people together and create a real sense of belonging. I’m looking forward to getting to know the people who make Harrogate Town what it is – our supporters, staff, players, volunteers, partners and the wider community. The relationship between a club and its supporters is incredibly important, and I am privileged to become part of that.

Harrogate Town is a club with real ambition and potential, both on and off the pitch. There is a lot to build on, and I am really looking forward to getting started, meeting as many people as possible and working together to make the connection between the club, our supporters and the wider community even stronger.



Harrogate Town AFC has confirmed that CEO Sarah Barry will leave the club following five successful years.

Sarah departs following a period of significant progress off the pitch. Last season the club set a record average home attendance, exceeding 3,000 for the first time – more than double the average of the final season with crowds prior to her appointment.

Crucial to this growth has been the continued improvement of the matchday experience, culminating in the club finishing third across the entire EFL in the Family Excellence Scheme for the 2025/26 season.


**Sarah Barry said:**

Representing Harrogate Town AFC as CEO has been an absolute honour and a privilege.

It takes a lot of dedicated people to run a football club. My heartfelt thanks and appreciation go to each and every one of our fabulous team of staff, suppliers, commercial partners and volunteers for everything you do and for making my time here so special.

And to our fantastically loyal supporters. Your positivity throughout the last year in particular has been outstanding. It cannot be underestimated how much this has helped the team on and off the pitch as we adapt and get ready to go again.

The last five years have been incredible. There is too much to list, but I’m immensely proud of how far we have come in that time.

I’d like to express my sincerest gratitude to Irving, Dorothy and Simon Weaver. Firstly for giving me the opportunity and for their kindness and support to me personally.

And more importantly, for everything they have done and accomplished for this football club in the last 17 years. What Harrogate Town has achieved is simply extraordinary and it would not have been possible to get anywhere close without their time, ambition and relentless commitment year in, year out. They deserve so much recognition for their invaluable contribution.

The journey continues and we’ve had a brilliant start to the season. There’s still a long way to go but it’s a great position to be in and whatever happens, I hope we can deliver an exciting and enjoyable season for everyone involved.

There are really exciting times ahead and I’m delighted to be staying closely connected as a trustee on the Harrogate Town Community Foundation board.

For now, I wish everyone connected with Harrogate Town the very best for the year ahead and beyond. Thank you for all the wonderful memories.



**Irving Weaver said:**

Sarah has played a hugely important role in the development of Harrogate Town over the past five years and, on behalf of Dorothy, Simon and everyone at the club, I want to thank her personally for everything she has given to us.

She has led the club with enormous energy and commitment and leaves having made a real and lasting contribution. The growth in our attendances, the improvements to the matchday experience and, just as importantly, the relationships she has built with people throughout the club and our community are testament to that. I’m delighted that Sarah will remain part of the Harrogate Town family through her role with the Community Foundation.

On a personal level, it has been a pleasure to work alongside Sarah. We are enormously grateful for everything she has done for Harrogate Town and she leaves with our friendship, our sincere thanks and our very best wishes for the future.
