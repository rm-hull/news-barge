Hull Trains, which runs from Selby and Howden to London, says it will not be able to run a service on Christmas Eve this year.

Engineering work is taking place at Doncaster station.

Hull Trains has published full details of its holiday services over Christmas and the New Year.

*24th, 25th and 26th December:**No services**
27th December: Slightly reduced service, 07:18 from London to Selby will not run
28th to 30th December: Normal service
31st December: Slightly reduced service, 19:09 from Hull will not run and the 20:18 from London King’s Cross will not run
1st January: Slightly reduced service, 07:18 from London to Selby and the 05:53**from Beverley via Selby to London will not run* either.

**A Network Rail spokesperson said:** “Engineers will use the Christmas period to replace railway points at Doncaster, renewing key pieces of infrastructure that help trains safely switch between routes to improve reliability and reduce the likelihood of delays. 

“Nearly 650 metres of track will also be renewed for smoother, more reliable journeys.

“The work begins on Thursday 24 December, with normal service resuming on Sunday 27 December.

“As a result of the work, no trains will be able to call at Doncaster station. This means that some journeys on the East Coast Main Line will be amended and rail replacement services will be in operation.*

“We’re sorry for any inconvenience caused. Passengers travelling for Christmas are urged to make journeys before 24 December where possible. Check before you travel with your train operator or National Rail.”

The work at Doncaster will also affect trains run by LNER, Grand Central and CrossCountry with further details to follow.
