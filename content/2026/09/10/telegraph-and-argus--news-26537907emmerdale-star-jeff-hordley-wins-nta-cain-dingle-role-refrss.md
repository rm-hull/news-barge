Emmerdale star Jeff Hordley has shared a heartfelt message with his fans after winning a National Television Award (NTA) for his performance as Cain Dingle.

The actor, who grew up in Oldham, took home the Best Serial Drama Performance prize at the ceremony on Tuesday, September 8, for his portrayal of Cain’s prostate cancer diagnosis on the long-running ITV soap.

Mr Hordley, who has played Cain since 2000, said: "Thank you to everyone who voted for me, it means an awful lot.

"I was in some really strong company, this really does take my breath away."

He was up against EastEnders actors Aaron Thiara and Paul Bradley, as well as Coronation Street’s Sue Devaney and Gareth Pierce.

Hordley praised the show’s writers and the educational value of the storyline.

He said: "It’s been an honour and a huge educational journey for me with this prostate cancer storyline.

"I’d like to thank Emmerdale for writing this really sensitively and highlighting all the side effects, problems and difficulties that men and families all around the country face.

"I’d like to make a special thanks to Errol, to Dennis and my father-in-law Graham for their bravery that’s inspired me through this storyline."

He also thanked his on-screen and off-screen family, including his wife Zoe Henry, who plays Rhona Goskirk in Emmerdale.

Mr Hordley said: "A massive thanks to this lady, my wingwoman Natalie J Robb.

"She makes acting a breeze.

"You’re just joyful Nat, every day is a joy working with you.

"Thank you to my real wife Zoe Henry, my rock and my soulmate.

"I am so lucky.

"To my two children, Stanley and Violet, you are my world and I am so thankful and grateful for you having to put up with me being Cain Dingle every day."

The notoriously shy actor is very different from his on-screen character.

His emotional speech brought his wife to tears as he expressed his gratitude to those closest to him.
