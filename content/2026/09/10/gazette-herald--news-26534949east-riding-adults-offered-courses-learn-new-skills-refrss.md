Adults are being encouraged to brush up on their skills and take steps towards new opportunities.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council’s Adult Learning and Employability Service is promoting its courses for learners aged 19 and over this September, as children and young people return to school.

Courses are offered at learning centres in Beverley, Bridlington, Cottingham and Goole, with online options also available.

Councillor Anne Handley, leader of East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "September is often a time when families are thinking about education and new routines, but learning is not just for children and young people.

"Our Adult Learning and Employability service helps residents build confidence, improve skills and take positive next steps at any stage of life."

The service covers a range of topics including English, maths, digital skills, and wellbeing.

Support is also available for everyday tasks such as completing forms, writing emails, and using digital devices.

Rebecca Gorman, who hopes to qualify as a teaching assistant, has progressed from Entry Level 3 to Level 2 English through the programme.

She said: "Adult Learning has helped me in many ways, including confidence, preparation and my ability to do the work."

People can find out more about East Riding Adult Learning courses by visiting www.eastriding.gov.uk/learning/employment-education-and-skills
