A popular street food event is set to return to the North East this month after two successful runs in August.

The Street Food Carousel will be back at Church Square, Hartlepool, on September 19 from 4pm to 8pm.

Due to ongoing regeneration work, the upcoming event will be limited to Church Square and will not extend into Scarborough Street as it did previously.

Organisers hope to expand the event in future as works progress.

Councillor Rob Darby, Chair of the Council's Housing, Growth and Communities Committee, said: "The first two Street Food Carousel events have been a tremendous success, bringing hundreds of people into the town centre and creating a fantastic atmosphere.

"They have shown exactly what can be achieved when the Council works closely with partners to deliver high-quality events for residents and visitors.

"I'd also encourage any traders who want to be part of the event to get in touch.

"These events are helping to showcase Hartlepool as a vibrant destination with plenty to offer, and we want as many people and businesses as possible to be involved."

The event is part of a four-weekend programme delivered by Hartlepool Borough Council in partnership with Baccanalia.

Baccanalia has invited food vendors, retailers, and other businesses to apply for stalls at the events on September 19 and October 31.

Thomas Wales, managing director of Baccanalia, said: "Street Food Carousel has already proved hugely popular across the North-East, and we're delighted with the response we have had in Hartlepool.

"We're looking forward to bringing another great event to Church Square."

Interested traders can contact [info@baccanalia.co.uk](mailto:info@baccanalia.co.uk) for more information or to apply for a stall.
