A TEENAGE boy, who was riding a motorbike, is fighting for his life following a crash with a van on a major road.

The 15-year-old was seriously injured in a collision on Ovenden Way, Halifax, at around 6pm yesterday.

The teen was sent to hospital after the incident between a white Vauxhall Combo van and a black White Knuckle 125 Trail motorbike.

Police have confirmed two other people on the motorbike, who left the scene prior to the attendance of emergency services, were not seriously injured.

A 28-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by dangerous driving and is currently in custody. Police have confirmed the man has now been released on bail.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police are appealing for witnesses and any dashcam footage after a teenage motorcyclist was seriously injured in a collision in Halifax yesterday evening (Wednesday 9 September).

"It was reported at 6.01pm that a collision had occurred on Ovenden Way, Halifax, involving a white Vauxhall Combo van and a black White Knuckle 125 Trail motorbike.

"A 15-year-old boy who was riding the motorbike was seriously injured and taken to hospital. His injuries are described as life threatening.

"There were two other people on the motorbike who left the scene prior to the attendance of emergency services. They have since been identified and confirmed to not be seriously injured.

"A 28-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by dangerous driving and has since been released on bail.

"Officers investigating this collision are keen to speak to anyone who witnessed the events leading up to it or the collision or the collision itself.

"They are also asking anyone who was in the area who has a dashcam to check whether they have captured any relevant footage.

Anyone with information is asked to contact the Roads Policing Unit via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat or by calling 101, quoting log 1338 of 9 September."
