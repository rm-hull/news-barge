A stolen vehicle from the Bradford area with false registration plates has been located.

Police located the vehicle in Batley after receiving information from local residents.

They were able to confirm that the vehicle was displaying false licence plates and had been reported stolen from the Bradford area.

## What did police say?

The force adds that it has seen a recent increase in Ford vehicle thefts.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Following information received from residents, officers located the pictured vehicle in Batley.

"Enquiries confirmed it was displaying false registration plates and had been reported as stolen from the Bradford area.

"We have seen a recent increase in Ford vehicle thefts.

"If you notice a suspicious vehicle in your area, please report it to the police so that checks can be completed.

"Thank you for helping us keep our communities safe."
