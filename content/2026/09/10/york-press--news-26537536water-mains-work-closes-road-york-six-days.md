WATER mains works have closed a road in York just off one of the main routes into the city this morning (Thursday, June 10).

City of York Council said Landsdowne Terrace is closed until 5pm on Wednesday, September 16.

  



The local authority said this is in order to ensure the works can be carried out safely.

Resident and emergency vehicle access is expected to remain available throughout the works period.

Signs and barriers will show the extent of the closure.

Drivers using the road will be diverted via Granville Terrace, with the alternative route signed.
