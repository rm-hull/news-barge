**Plans for the multi-purpose Community and Enterprise Hub in Catterick Garrison have taken a significant step forward**, with North Yorkshire Council seeking approval to manage the facility in-house ahead of its scheduled opening in February 2027. An operational review found that managing the enterprise hub internally will cost approximately £120,000 less each year than outsourcing to alternative providers, securing maximum value for local taxpayers while optimizing communal benefits.

The upcoming building forms the central anchor of a wider £21 million Catterick Garrison town centre regeneration project. The initiative was funded via a successful joint bid submitted by North Yorkshire Council and the Ministry of Defence (MoD) in 2022, securing £19 million from the central government’s Levelling Up Fund (now known as the Local Regeneration Fund), with the remaining £2 million provided as match-funding split between the council and the Defence Infrastructure Organisation (DIO).

## Addressing Business and Military Family Needs Under One Roof

Comprehensive local assessments previously identified severe regional shortages of high-quality shared community assets, flexible event spaces, and modern business facilities. The newly constructed multi-purpose hub is specifically configured to remedy these shortages by integrating flexible incubation workspaces, starter offices, retail test units, modern training facilities, an open event space, and a functional community kitchen.

The facility will also drive civilian-military integration by co-locating local civic services alongside the British Army’s specialized HIVE information service, streamlining accessible public resources for both resident military personnel and civilian families. Beyond the physical footprint of the hub, the capital scheme funds extensive structural enhancements to public open realms, nearby cycling and walking pathways, and connectivity upgrades through Coronation Park.

## Statements From Council Leadership


**Cllr Mark Crane, North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for open to business, said:**

This exciting new facility has the potential to become a focal point for the community and play an important role in the future of Catterick Garrison. It will provide opportunities for local businesses to grow, help people develop new skills, host community events and activities, and create a welcoming space for residents and visitors alike.

We want this building to be a place that brings people together and supports the continued growth and development of the town. The proposals for us as a council to run the centre will be considered carefully by the executive to ensure that there is the very best value for our taxpayers.

This building has been designed to meet those needs while creating new opportunities for learning, enterprise and community activity. Alongside the other improvements being delivered through the regeneration programme, it will help create a vibrant town centre that people can be proud of.


## Next Steps and Executive Approvals

Members of North Yorkshire Council’s executive committee are scheduled to formally evaluate and vote on the in-house management plans during their upcoming administrative assembly on Tuesday 15 September 2026. Local commercial entities, charity groups, or independent start-ups interested in securing tenancy opportunities or hosting events within the complex are encouraged to contact the authority’s dedicated property team at [estatesteam@northyorks.gov.uk](mailto:estatesteam@northyorks.gov.uk) to discuss spacing configurations.
