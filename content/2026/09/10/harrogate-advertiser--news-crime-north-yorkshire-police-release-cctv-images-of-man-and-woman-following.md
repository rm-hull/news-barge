# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV images of man and woman following theft at garden centre in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police has issued two CCTV images of a man and a woman following a theft from a garden centre in Harrogate.

The incident happened on the afternoon of Tuesday 18 August at Crimple Valley Garden Centre on Leeds Road in Pannal.

It involved the theft of hundreds of pounds worth of items including reed diffusers and blankets.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Please contact us if you recognise either person from the images.”
