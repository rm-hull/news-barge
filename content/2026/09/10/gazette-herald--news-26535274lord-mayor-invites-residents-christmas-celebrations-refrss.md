Hundreds of residents are set to enjoy a day of festive entertainment at the Lord Mayor of York’s annual Christmas Cheer Receptions.

The Lord Mayor and Sheriff of York are inviting 400 residents aged 65 and over to the annual Christmas Cheer Receptions, which will take place at the historic Assembly Rooms on December 2, 2025.

Paper forms are available from October 6 at any York library, the council’s Customer Centre at West Offices, or St Sampson’s Centre.

Completed applications should be posted or delivered to: The Christmas Cheer Draw, c/o Civic Services, West Offices, Station Rise, York, YO1 6GA.

Entries must arrive no later than November 16.

Martin Rowley BEM, the Right Honourable the Lord Mayor of York, said: "What better way to start your festivities than with good company in a spectacular setting?

"And I’m honoured to be hosting the annual Christmas Cheer Receptions on 2 December."

Each session will host 200 guests, with a morning event at 10am and an afternoon event at 2pm.

The morning programme will feature performances from Osbaldwick Primary Academy, the Shepherd Brass Band, and soloist Alex Mather.

In the afternoon, entertainment will be provided by Ralph Butterfield Primary School and the York Philharmonic Male Voice Choir.

Cllr Rowley said: "The receptions offer our older residents top class entertainment, food and fun.

"It’s a chance to meet new friends and bring in the festive season in a truly memorable way.

"If you’re eligible to apply and are free to join us on 2 December, please send off your application to enjoy this wonderful occasion."

Residents can indicate their preferred session when applying, and tickets will be distributed via a random draw.

A fundraising concert featuring the York Philharmonic Male Voice Choir will help support the event.

Applications can also be emailed to [civic@york.gov.uk](mailto:civic@york.gov.uk) by the same deadline.
