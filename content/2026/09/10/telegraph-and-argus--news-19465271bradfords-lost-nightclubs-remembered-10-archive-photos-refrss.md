How many of you remember these Bradford nightclubs from years gone by?

We’re taking a trip back to 10 of Bradford’s lost and much-missed clubs.

1. The Plush Silk and Textile Club on Lilycroft Road in Manningham was better known as The Pile Bar, where clubbers could watch live acts and dance. The club is pictured in 1988.

  


2. Flares was the last club left in Bradford’s West End. The Morley Street club closed in August 2019. The picture shows happier times at the venue’s opening.

  


3. Remember Doctor Livingstone’s in Bradford’s West End? The West End was a magnet for clubbers.

  


4. The Palace on Manningham Lane closed in 1990 after failing fire-safety tests. Its popular Sixties nights attracted acts including the Drifters and the Tremeloes.

  


5. Lingards was at Westgate, on the site of a former dress shop, and was popular in the 1980s and 1990s. Here it is in 1991.

  


6. Who remembers Mickeys Backstage Bar? Blending dance music with live entertainment, Mickeys was on the ground floor of the Castle Hotel; it is pictured in 1987.

  


7. Manningham club Dollars and Dimes appeared in the films Billy Liar and Rita, Sue and Bob Too when it was known as the Mecca.

  


8. Plants? Tick. Chrome? Tick. Dietrich’s had Eighties nightclub splendour, as this 1983 picture shows.

  


9. Remember Cavernes’ subterranean vibe? The Great Horton Road club is pictured in 1977.

  


10. Eighties clubbers will remember Dukes and Silks, from the era of Cloud 9 and JBs. Dukes and Silks is pictured in 1981.

  




Bradford's West End was once packed with clubs and nightlife spots including Che Bar, Revolution, Love Apple, Walkabout, Livingstone's, Tokyo, Brewhaus and Bierkeller.
