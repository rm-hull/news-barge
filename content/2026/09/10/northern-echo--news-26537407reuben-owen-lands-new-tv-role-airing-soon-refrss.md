Fans of the popular TV series Our Yorkshire Farm will be excited to hear that Reuben Owen will be back on their screens soon.

Reuben Owen is one of Clive and Amanda Owen’s children, who has created his own business running a heavy-plant machinery and groundwork company.

The 22-year-old even had his own Channel 5 series called Reuben Owen: Life in the Dales.

Now Reuben has landed himself a new role and will be appearing on the documentary series Our Great Yorkshire Life.


## Reuben Owen lands new TV role away from Our Yorkshire Farm

Our Great Yorkshire Life first aired in 2022 and celebrates the region’s heritage, communities, and the lives of the people who live and work there.

The series is returning for its third season and is set to focus on “four moving and visually stunning stories from across England’s picturesque county”.

The show is narrated by Dean Andrews, who is best known for playing Will Taylor in Emmerdale, which is set in the Yorkshire Dales.

The first episode of the new series will air on Channel 5 at 8pm on Friday, September 11.

Reuben is set to star in the second episode of the series that is airing at 8pm on Friday, September 18.

The show will feature Reuben taking on hovercraft racing, a slightly different task to his usual day job.


The news about the new series was posted by Daisy Beck Studios on Instagram with a picture featuring Reuben on a hovercraft.

The caption read: “NEW SERIES ANNOUNCEMENT.

“Our Great Yorkshire Life returns to your screens next Friday 11th September on Channel 5.

“This brand new series was filmed last year, following the lives of some well-known Yorkshire faces, alongside ordinary people who live and work in some of the most picturesque and breathtaking parts of the county.”

Many people took to the comments of the post to share their excitement over the announcement.

One person said: “We can’t wait to watch!”

Another person said: “Can’t wait, we saw them filming whilst on holiday in Pickering last year my husband got a selfie with them such gents.”
