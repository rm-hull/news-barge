A York flat which was a magnet for trouble has been targeted by police

Magistrates agreed to issue a Premises Closure Order for 10 Cornetto Court, Regent Street.

A police warrant was executed at the address on 14 August, and this led to the application for the order, which was granted yesterday (Wednesday).

#### Cornetto Court, York

It follows persistent anti-social behaviour and nuisance associated with 10 Cornetto Court.

The temporary order means that no one other than the tenant can enter the flat for the next three months.

The only exceptions are representatives from gas, electricity and water providers, the landlord and their agents, care professionals, and the emergency services.

Anyone who breaks the order is liable to a fine or imprisonment, or both.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/york-magistrates-court-march-2026.webp)

Chief Inspector Ryan Chapman, operational commander for neighbourhood policing in York and Selby, said: “This flat has generated a catalogue of anti-social behaviour and nuisance which have blighted the lives of the nearby residents and the local community.

“The temporary premises closure order will help to put a stop to the problem in the short term. We will then apply for the full closure order should these issues continue.

“I thank our officers, community partners, and residents for their greatly appreciated tenacity and support.”
