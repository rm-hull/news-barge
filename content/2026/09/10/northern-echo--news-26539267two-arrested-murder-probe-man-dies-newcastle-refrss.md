A murder probe has been launched after a man died in a late night 'altercation' in the North East.

The incident happened on Drysdale Crescent in Brunswick Village around 10.50pm on Wednesday, September 9.

Police were called to reports of an altercation involving several people.

A man in his 50s was found with life-threatening injuries and was pronounced dead at the scene despite the efforts of paramedics, while a woman in her 50s was also injured and taken to hospital but has since been discharged.

A 43-year-old man and a 40-year-old woman have been arrested on suspicion of murder and 38-year-old man and another 40-year-old woman have been arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender. All four remain in police custody.

Detective Inspector Barry McAtominey, of Northumbria Police’s Major Investigation Team, said: "Our thoughts are with the family of the man who has died.

"Enquiries into the incident are ongoing and we have four people in custody for questioning around what took place yesterday evening.
