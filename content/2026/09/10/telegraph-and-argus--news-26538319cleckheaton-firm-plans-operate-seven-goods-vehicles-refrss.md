A Cleckheaton-based business is applying for a goods vehicle operator's licence.

Barry Garth, trading as Garth Group Ltd, intends to use Unit 1E, Morley Carr Business Centre, Low Moor, Bradford, as an operating centre for seven goods vehicles.

The business has announced that there will be no trailers involved in the operations.

Those who own or occupy land near the proposed centre, and feel that their use or enjoyment of the land might be affected, are invited to submit written representations to the Traffic Commissioner.

These letters should include reasons for the objections and must be sent within 21 days of the notice's publication.

A copy of the representation should also be sent to Garth Group Ltd at Cavendish House, West 26 Industrial Estate, Little Wood Drive, Cleckheaton, BD19 4TE.

Further guidance on how to make representations can be obtained from the Traffic Commissioner’s Office at Hillcrest House, 386 Harehills Lane, Leeds LS9 6NF.
