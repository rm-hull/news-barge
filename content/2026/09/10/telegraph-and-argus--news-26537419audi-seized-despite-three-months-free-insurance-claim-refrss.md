A driver stopped by police in Bradford told officers a friend had included "three months' free insurance" when selling him the car.

The claim emerged after officers pulled over an Audi on Duckworth Lane and discovered the driver's licence had been revoked.

According to a post from West Yorkshire Police's Bradford West neighbourhood policing team, officers initially stopped the vehicle and found the motorist was driving without a valid licence.

## Insurance explanation 'made no material difference'

Police said the driver's suggestion that an appeal was ongoing did not prevent them from taking action, with the vehicle already being seized before officers began investigating insurance arrangements.

A spokesperson said further enquiries revealed issues regarding insurance cover, prompting a series of explanations from the driver.

Among them was a claim that he had bought the Audi from a friend and had been given three months of free insurance as part of the deal.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Various tales, including a spurious claim of buying the car from a friend with three month's free insurance included, only prolonged the inevitable."
