Police are appealing for information following a "vicious and unprovoked" alleged assault in North Yorkshire.

The incident took place on Westborough in Scarborough at around 3.15am on Sunday (September 6).

North Yorkshire Police said the alleged assault was reported as "vicious and unprovoked".

The victim was taken to hospital for treatment.

  


A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We’re appealing for help and information to identify the man in the image.

"Please quote reference number 12260174749 when providing details."

Information can be reported to police by emailing [john.axcell@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:john.axcell@northyorkshire.police.uk.)
