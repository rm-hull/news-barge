A COMMUNITY group that aims to unite people against "the increase of hatred" in the UK says it has been forced to cancel an event due to a "credible threat of a far-right protest".

Keighley Together was planning on holding a public meeting on September 10 at the Civic Centre on North Street - but on the advice of an anti-racist organisation, it has been forced to postpone.

## 'Very concerned to see black-shirted, far-right thugs'

Ian Nichol, a spokesperson for the group, said that Keighley Together was "very concerned to see that over the weekend black-shirted, far-right thugs appeared on the streets of Dover and Portsmouth spouting racial hatred and trying to create division between communities.

"This demonstrates how emboldened the far right have become and why the Keighley Together campaign that promotes love, hope and unity between communities will continue to grow in importance.

"Unfortunately we have had to take the difficult decision to cancel the meeting this Thursday because there is a credible threat of a far-right protest, by agitators not from the Keighley area, taking place outside the meeting."

## 'It is with great regret'

An official statement made by the group, and signed off by Lisa Foley Chair, Keighley Together Campaign Committee and Dave Towers Secretary, KTUC explained their decision making.

They explained that "escalation to [far-right] groups outside of Keighley" took them "by surprise".

They promise that "it's a lesson learnt," and have hopes of holding the public meeting later in the year.

## What is Keighley Together?

Keighley Together was formed by Keighley Trades Union Council in 2004 and was a component in the defeat of Nick Griffin, the leader of the British National Party, when he tried to sow division in Keighley but was soundly beaten by Anne Cryer in the 2005 General Election.

Find them on Facebook or email them on [keighleytogether@outlook.com](mailto:keighleytogether@outlook.com)

## 'Alternative to the politics of hate, division, racism and anger'

It reads: "It is with great regret that Keighley Together is cancelling the public meeting that was scheduled for September 10 at the Civic Centre.

"Keighley Together has received information from an anti-racist organisation that there is a credible threat of a far-right protest, agitators not from the Keighley area, taking place outside the meeting.

"Keighley Together is all about spreading hope, love and unity in this community and the thought of outside protesters interfering in our community is worrying and upsetting.

"However, Keighley Together has a duty of care to people who would have attended the open meeting and the possibility of conflict outside the venue means that we cannot go ahead.

"The public meeting was to inform Keighley people of the progress we have made since re-launching earlier this year and to have an open discussion about the way forward for the next year.

"We have had great support from the people and community organisations of Keighley since the re-launch.

"Keighley Together is a non-party-political, community-based organisation set up to offer an alternative to the politics of hate, division, racism and anger used by the far right.

"Keighley Together will hold a further public meeting later this year, where we will be better prepared; this escalation to groups outside of Keighley took us by surprise and it's a lesson learnt.

"We will continue with more vigour and determination to reach out to the people of Keighley.

"When you see us at local events in our community, come and say hello, find out more about Keighley Together, show your support for our message of Hope, Love and Unity."
