A body has been found after a week-long search for a missing 20-year-old man from Gateshead.

John was reported missing on September 2 after last being seen in Newcastle.

Northumbria Police carried out extensive enquiries to locate him, including reviewing CCTV, releasing footage, and issuing public appeals.

Sadly, a body matching his description was found in the River Tyne near Newcastle Quayside shortly after 11.10am on Wednesday, September 9.

Detective Inspector Nick Went, of the force’s Missing Investigation Team, said: "This is an extremely sad development, and our thoughts are with John’s family and friends.
