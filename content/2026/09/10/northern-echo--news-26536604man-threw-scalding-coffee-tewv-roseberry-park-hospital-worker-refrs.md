A man threw scalding coffee over a mental health worker leaving him screaming in pain.

Jack Cunningham was winding up another patient when a staff member intervened, a court heard.

As the worker moved Cunningham away from the 30-year-old, he accidentally knocked into him and spilt coffee on himself.

Prosecutor Saba Shan said Cunningham reacted angrily by throwing coffee over the worker, burning his torso.

She said: “He felt burning on the left side of his neck and immediately noticed a smell of coffee.

“He recalls screaming in pain and moving to the floor. The defendant was screaming – ‘I am sorry’.”

  Jack Cunningham 

*(Image: Cleveland Police)*

The court heard how the assault was captured on the CCTV inside Roseberry Park mental health hospital in Middlesbrough.

Miss Shan said Cunningham then smashed a CCTV monitor in the hospital unit when he was challenged about what happened.

The coffee caused severe blistering and permanent scarring to the victim’s skin.

Cunningham, of no fixed address, admitted causing grievous bodily harm and criminal damage after his arrest on June 23.

  Roseberry Park Hospital in Middlesbrough. 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Michele Turner, defending, said Cunningham had done well in custody and was taking his medication regularly.

She added: “When the door opened, it knocked him, and he was bare-chested, and some of the hot liquid touched him and his reaction to that was the devastating impact on the victim.”

Judge Richard Bennett sentenced the defendant to 13 months in prison after he deliberately threw the coffee over the hospital worker.

He said: “You were behaving erratically and causing a bit of a nuisance in the hospital.
