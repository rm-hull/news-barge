A RECENTLY-opened pub has sent some 'bangers' to Benidorm to raise money for a local hospice.

The Station, a small country pub set in the small hamlet of Harecroft near Wilsden, has been busy fundraising since it re-opened on June 12.

Landlord and landlady Danny and Lousie Helstrip, alongside pub manager Helen Lawn have put their heads together to come up with an innovative way of raising money for Sue Ryder Manorlands Hopsice in Keighley.

  The Station 

*(Image: T&A)*

## The challenge is Bangers to Benidorm

Currently, they have a number of banged-out cars en-route from Bradford to Benidorm.

The cars set off from The Station in Harecroft at 5.30am on Tuesday morning (September 8) and began the first leg of the journey down and across to France.

  Inside the pub 

*(Image: T&A)*

From France they will drive their way across Europe before, hopefully, all arriving in Benidorm before Friday, September 11.

A spokesperson for the pub said: "We have a great team and feel we are a great hub in the community, with fantastic support from them all.

  The team at the Station 

*(Image: T&A)*

"On Saturday, September 5 we held a charity race night to support Sue Ryder Manorlands and raised over £1,000.

"This in turn supported the next major exciting fundraiser organised by Danny and his team."

  The team at the Station 

*(Image: T&A)*

## 'The idea is simple'

The pub manager explained the concept of the Bangers to Benidorm fundraiser.

Mrs Lawn said: "Each team has to purchase a banger for a maximum budget of £500 then take on the challenge of driving all the way from Bradford to Benidorm.

  Have you visited yet? 

*(Image: T&A)*

"Most of the teams will eventually scrap their bangers in Spain putting the contributions into the charity pot but Danny plans to bring his mini back home to continue to fundraise for Manorlands Hospice."

The trip has not been without its adventures thus far, with breakdowns on day one and punctures en-route, "but the team are pulling together and laughing all the way", Mrs Lawn assures us.

  What a cosy little spot! 

*(Image: T&A)*

## 'Much more than driving a banger to Spain'

For the pub, the event is about "much more than driving a banger to Spain".

Mrs Lawn said: "Manorlands provides vital care and support to local people and their families and we want to do everything we can to help raise funds for the incredible charity.

"Anyone wishing to support us can donate through our Just Giving page.

The team has already raised nearly 10k towards their 30k goal.
