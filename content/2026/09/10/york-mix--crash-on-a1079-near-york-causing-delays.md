There was a crash on the A1079 near York this morning (Thursday).

It was close to Kexby on the inbound carriageway.

Drivers reported standstill traffic.

#### Crash location

It was is close to the roadworks which have been already causing delays on the A1079 near Four Lane Ends on the inbound carriageway to York.

The crash has now cleared and traffic is back to normal.
