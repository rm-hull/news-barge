Recycling officers will be out in force during Recycle Week to help residents cut down on food waste.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council’s waste and recycling officers will visit venues across the region from September 14 to 20, to offer advice and promote food waste recycling.

They will be at Pocklington market on September 15 from 9am to 3pm, Bridlington Hinge Centre on September 16 from 1.30pm to 3pm, Driffield Library and Customer Service Centre on September 17 from 9.30am to 3pm, and Hornsea Hub on September 18 from 10am to 3pm.

Councillor Paul West, the council’s cabinet member for environment and transport, said: "Please go along and visit our waste and recycling officers during the week because they can offer valuable advice about simple ways to recycle your food and all rubbish.

"East Riding residents do a fantastic job of recycling their waste; this area is still one of the best in the country for it, but we still need people to empty their food packaging and recycle that food."

Since weekly brown bin collections began for two-thirds of the East Riding in February, the council has delivered almost 9,000 kitchen caddies for people wanting to start recycling their food.
