WELL this is a curiosity - a pink grasshopper, and it was spotted in the Yorkshire Dales.

John Fletcher tells us it was his wife that saw it as they were walking on Barden Moor.

"Later, we discovered that pink grasshoppers are extremely rare. They are a mutation of normal green grasshoppers," he says.

The couple were very lucky to see the grasshopper - it is estimated only one in 500,000 are born with the pink coloration. And of course, they stand out easily against green grass, meaning they are frequently spotted and eaten by birds.
