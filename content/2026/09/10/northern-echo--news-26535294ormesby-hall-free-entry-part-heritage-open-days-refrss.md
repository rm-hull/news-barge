Ormesby Hall will open its doors to the public for free this month as part of Heritage Open Days.

The National Trust property in Middlesbrough will offer complimentary entry across two weekends in September, inviting visitors to explore its wider estate, gardens, model railway, and the historic home of the Pennyman family.

The free days are intended to encourage first-time visitors and mark England’s largest festival of history and culture.

Leanne Allan, senior visitor experience officer at Ormesby Hall, said: "Ormesby Hall has been part of Middlesbrough’s story for generations, but we know there are still many local people who have never stepped through the gates.

"Heritage Open Days gives us a wonderful opportunity to change that.

"By removing the admission cost, we hope people who may not have thought of visiting before will come along, explore the house and gardens, and discover just how much there is here."

Guests can visit the property free of charge on September 11, 12, and 13, and again on September 18, 19, and 20, between 10.30am and 4pm.

Heritage Open Days runs nationally from September 11 to 20 and brings together thousands of organisations and volunteers to celebrate local history, heritage and community through free events and experiences.

Ms Allan said: "We want Ormesby Hall to feel like a place that belongs to the community around it, and these weekends are about welcoming people in, sharing the stories of the Hall, and giving everyone the chance to enjoy this special part of our local heritage."

The event will include a range of activities.

On Friday, September 11, and Friday, September 18, the collections team will host Conservation in Action sessions from 10.30am to 1.30pm, giving visitors a behind-the-scenes look at how the hall’s collections are preserved.

On Saturday, September 12, More Than A Run will lead a Park to Park Heritage Walk from Ormesby Hall to Stewart Park and back.

Advance booking is required for this event.

A volunteer recruitment day will take place on Friday, September 18, offering information for those interested in joining the Ormesby Hall volunteer team.

No general admission booking is required for the open days.
