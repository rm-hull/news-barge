A winning North East lottery ticket worth £1 million has gone unclaimed, with the owner now having missed the deadline.

The EuroMillions ticket, bought in Hartlepool for the March 13, 2026 draw, had a winning Millionaire Maker code of JLZG97793.

The deadline to claim the prize was Wednesday, September 9, but no valid claim was made, despite an extensive appeal to find the winner.

Andy Carter, senior winners’ advisor at Allwyn, the operator of The National Lottery, said: "Despite a widespread appeal encouraging players to check their tickets, the EuroMillions ticket-holder in Hartlepool did not come forward to claim their prize.

"While we are disappointed the winner never came forward, it’s fantastic that this money will now go on to support National Lottery-funded projects that help strengthen communities and improve lives across the UK."

Under National Lottery rules, unclaimed prizes are redirected to support good causes, adding to the more than £33 million raised each week for community projects.

The funding supports hundreds of projects across the UK, including several in and around Hartlepool.

Sidequest Was Taken CIC, which delivers artist-led community projects to build connections and confidence, was awarded £51,025 in 2026 to expand its work in the area.

Other local causes include the Eden Education Cafe CIC, an alternative education provider and community café, received £18,616 this year for its Never Eat Alone project, which encourages social connection through shared meals.

Hartlepool Rugby Football Club, a long-standing community hub, has been granted £39,687 in total National Lottery funding, including a recent award to help develop a sustainable community energy scheme.

The Make and Mend Company, which promotes wellbeing through creativity and crafts, has received £173,056 to deliver workshops, family events, and community activities aimed at building confidence and reducing isolation.
