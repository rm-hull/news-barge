## Put a fence around it

  The Clifford's Tower mound is an accident waiting to happen.

  How soon before a child runs down it, steps in one of the many, many divots and snaps their leg?

  Then, look out City of York Council, you'll be sued.

  Why not just put a fence all the way round as it's pretty obvious the "Keep off" signs don't work.

## Taylor is a true legend

  People in sport are frequently described as 'legends' and 'role models'.

  However, there can be no doubt that Katie Taylor deserves to be labelled as both.

  

  Her achievements in boxing, and the way she has almost single-handedly propelled women's boxing into the mainstream, are extraordinary.

  

  From the age of 10, when she had to disguise herself as a boy before she was even allowed to box, to becoming an Olympic gold medallist and a multi-weight undisputed professional world champion, her journey is one that may never be repeated.

  

  Her final fight at Croke Park was a fitting way for one of the greatest athletes of all time to bring the curtain down on a remarkable career.

  

  For me, she stands alongside the late, great Muhammad Ali in terms of her impact on the sport and the inspiration she has provided to millions.

  

  Thank you, Katie, for your grit, determination, excellence, and utter humility.

  We have been privileged to witness your greatness.

  **John Jones Russet House
  Birch Close
  York**

## Stop covering the land

  If it is true we are facing a crisis of food supply then wouldn’t it be a good idea to stop covering acres of good agricultural land with unsightly imported Chinese solar panels?

  

  There are large warehouse/factory roofs and non-productive derelict sites where those items can be fitted.

  **Matthew Laverack Lord Mayors Walk
  York**

## Reform's honeymoon is fading fast

  After decades of dull, boring partisan politics by the two main parties in Britain, a refreshing breath of fresh air blew in, when Reform UK entered the political arena.

  

  However, their honeymoon with the electorate appears to be fading fast, suggestions of financial irregularities within their ranks is commandeering the headlines.

  

  Just at the time when Russia is issuing veiled threats to the west, our government order the army to stay in barracks and twiddle their thumbs all day instead of undergoing training exercises to save the meagre sum of £30m, showing Burnham and Healey's commitments to defence spending, like many other promises, to be totally false.

## No leadership whatsoever

  At the moment we have a ruling Party that has lost control of our borders, legal system, international affairs, defence and they still come up with madcap ideas that are always detrimental to the country.
