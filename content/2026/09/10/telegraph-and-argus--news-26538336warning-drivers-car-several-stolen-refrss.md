POLICE have issued a warning to drivers who own a certain model of car after several were stolen in recent days.

Over the last few evenings, four Ford Kugas have been taken from Batley, Cleckheaton, Liversedge, and Mirfield, despite increased police patrols.

Officers are urging Ford Puma, Kuga, and Focus owners to take protective measures.

These include storing keys away from doors and windows, using a Faraday pouch to prevent relay thefts, and considering an AirTag or vehicle tracker to help locate the vehicle if stolen.

Motorists are also advised to ensure any CCTV, doorbell cameras, or security systems are working and fully charged, park in a garage or well-lit area when possible, and use additional security devices such as steering wheel locks.

Police have asked residents to stay vigilant and report any suspicious activity immediately.

People are encouraged to share the information with Ford owners in the area.
