A Newton Aycliffe man was ordered to pay more than £900 after leaving his garden strewn with rubbish.

Durham County Council said a man from Whinlatter Place was taken to court after failing to comply with requirements to clear the waste.

Pictures show rubbish strewn across the garden area with bins overflowing, planks of wood propped up against the house and the garden filled with debris.

He was orderd to pay £917.

The prosecution is part of an ongoing crackdown on environmental offences across County Durham.

Durham County Council is urging residents to report waste problems and share information that could help investigations.

A spokesperson said: “If you would like to report an issue with waste to the council or have any information which may help us with our enquiries, please contact customer services on 03000 260000 or at www.durham.gov.uk/doitonline.”

The council has said that residents who fail to comply with Community Protection Notices relating to waste can face prosecution, with recent cases resulting in hefty fines.
