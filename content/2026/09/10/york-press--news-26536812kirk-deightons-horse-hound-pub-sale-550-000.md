## Popular North Yorkshire village pub for sale as a £550,000 investment

The Horse and Hound at Kirk Deighton is on the market for £560,000 as an investment. *(Image: Christie & Co)*

A popular North Yorkshire village pub is on the market for £550,000 freehold.

The Horse & Hound at Kirk Deighton, just north of Wetherby, is being sold by Christie & Co as an investment.

It has recently been rebranded from The Bay Horse and is described as an independent free house.

The pub is popular for serving "locally sourced produce, cask ales, and an extensive wine selection," attracting both locals and customers from Wetherby and Harrogate.

The pub is also known for its regular community events like a bi-weekly quiz night.

Well-regarded, the Horse and Hound receives 4.5 starts out of five, based on 436 Google Reviews.

TripAdvisor awards 4.2 stars out of five, based on 411 reviews, ranking it 15th out of 74 Wetherby restaurants.

The agents say the substantial detached public house has been well maintained, combining traditional pub features with a high-quality food-led environment.

The business offers a variety of customer trading spaces, including a main bar area, restaurant dining areas, informal drinking and eating spaces, a commercial catering kitchen, customer WC facilities, ancillary stores and service areas, and three-bedroomed living accommodation.

The trading style is firmly food-led, with a strong focus on quality dining, Sunday lunch trade, and an extensive drinks offer.

  The exterior of the Horse and Hound in Kirk Deighton. 

*(Image: Pic supplied)*

External customer trading areas provide additional seating capacity and seasonal trading opportunities.

The pub's village location and proximity to popular countryside walks make it well suited to outdoor food and beverage trade.

The property is an income-producing freehold investment, let to an experienced multi-site hospitality operator who has been occupying the premises since 2017.

The tenant entered into a new 10-year lease starting on February 26, 2023, providing income security through to February 2033.

The lease produces a passing rent of £46,371 per annum, Christie & Co added.
