A plant hire and waste collection company is raising awareness of prostate cancer through a month-long charity campaign.

Church Fenton-based RJC Plant Services Ltd is supporting Prostate Cancer UK by donating £2 from every road sweeper hire throughout September.

The campaign coincides with Male Cancer Awareness Week, which runs from September 15 to 21.

Chris Jackson, co-director of RJC Plant Services, said: "Working in our industry, we’re surrounded by men every day, whether that’s our own team, customers, drivers or people working out on sites.

"We wanted to use that reach to get people talking about something that can be very easy to put off or avoid."

Prostate cancer affects one in eight men in the UK.

The company will also use its social media channels to promote awareness and encourage men to seek medical advice if they have concerns.

RJC will host a talk with Dr Lubna Khan-Salim, focusing on the signs and symptoms of prostate cancer, risk factors, and when to seek help.

A JustGiving page has been set up for additional donations.

The campaign builds on RJC’s record of supporting community and national causes, including previous fundraising for Melanoma UK and donating a defibrillator to Church Fenton.

For more information, visit: www.rjcplantservices.com
