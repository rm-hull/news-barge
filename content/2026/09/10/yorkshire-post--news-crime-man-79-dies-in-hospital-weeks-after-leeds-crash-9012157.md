# Man, 79, dies in hospital weeks after Leeds crash

## A 79-year-old man has died in hospital after suffering life-threatening injuries in a crash with a car in Leeds last month.

At around 9am on Tuesday (Aug 11), emergency services were called to Bellbrooke Avenue in Harehills following reports of a crash involving a car and a pedestrian.

The crash, which happened close to Foundry Approach, involved a Toyota Auris and a 79-year-old man.

![Bellbrooke Avenue in Harehills](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/10/15/50/Bellbrooke-Avenue-in-Harehills.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The driver, a 59-year-old man, was arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by dangerous driving.

He was later released on bail pending further enquiries.

Police have now confirmed that the 79-year-old pedestrian died in hospital on Wednesday (Sep 9).

The crash is now being investigated by West Yorkshire Police's Major Collision Enquiry Team, which has issued a further appeal for witnesses.

A spokesperson for the force said: “If you witnessed it or have footage which may assist then please contact the team on our livechat facility or by calling 101, quoting reference 13260456415.”
