A FARM plans to add two more vehicles to its fleet.

Graves at Chairbarrow Farm, Scholes, has applied to change its goods vehicle operator’s licence.

This is to accommodate two additional vehicles at the operating centre on Whitechapel Road.

The farm has requested to maintain this increase at its current location.

Those who own or occupy land near the operating centre and believe the change will affect their use or enjoyment of the land can make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner.

These must be sent within 21 days from the notice's publication on September 4.

Representations should also be copied to the applicant at the specified address.
