Darlington marketing firm MBM has landed a major international deal.

Myk Baxter Marketing has struck a landmark partnership with PALI, a non-profit representing more than 120 independent Irish leisure businesses.

MBM will roll out custom-built, commission-free booking software across the network, opening the door to international growth and new jobs.

MBM won the PALI contract after Jungle Den, one of Ireland’s favourite indoor play centres, asked the agency to redesign its website and improve online bookings.

“Like most people, I assumed the biggest challenge would be improving the website,” Myk said.

“It wasn’t until I sat down with the Jungle Den team and really understood how the business operated that I discovered how much they were paying every year for their booking software. It genuinely surprised me, and that was the moment everything changed. It became about building a better solution.”

MBM replaced the expensive third-party platform with a custom, commission-free booking system that lets parents book parties and school visits while giving Jungle Den full control of its data and revenue.

The system is expected to save Jungle Den at least €9,600 a year.

Jungle Den owner Becky praised the transformation, saying: “Working with MBM wasn’t simply about replacing one booking system with another.

“They took the time to understand how our business operates and the new platform has simplified the booking journey, improved efficiency across the business and removed significant ongoing software costs. It provides a platform we can continue to grow with for many years to come.”

PALI was set up to unite independent play, activity and leisure businesses and now represents more than 120 firms. It has secured group insurance schemes for more than 70 per cent of its members.

MBM’s hiring will depend on demand as PALI’s 120 members begin audits and onboarding, but the potential for growth is clear.

“If member demand supports it, that work will allow us to invest in people in Ireland and expand our UK team,” Myk said.

“Soft play centres play such an important role within local communities. They’re where children celebrate birthdays, make friends and create memories. Most are independent businesses run by passionate owners. If we can help those businesses save thousands of pounds every year, improve the way they operate, and give parents a booking experience that actually feels effortless, then we’ve achieved something genuinely worthwhile.”

Myk had worked in digital marketing for 22 years when a Dubai publishing company headhunted him, just before the 2008 financial crisis hit his industry.

Seeking a fresh start, Myk moved to Thailand in 2009. But everything changed again the following year.

“I got the dreaded call that my mum had cancer, and she wasn’t going to live long,” Myk said.

“I returned home to help support her and, that year, I set up Myk Baxter Marketing in Aycliffe Business Park with a small team.”

After his mother died in 2011, Myk re-evaluated his life and work and began developing a remote-first agency model long before the pandemic.

Their footprint in fitness and leisure was strengthened by successfully delivering extensive digital projects for One Gym. MBM had supported OneGym's SEO for 12 months before being appointed to redesign its website, as the fitness chain expanded across County Durham, Teesside, Sunderland, Newcastle and surrounding areas.

The agency has also built a strong hospitality foothold, providing SEO consultancy and digital work for restaurant chains including Rio Steakhouse and Tomahawk Steakhouse, as well as Babuls restaurant.
