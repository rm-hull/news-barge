A man has been seriously injured in a shocking robbery in which an electric bike was stolen after it was hit by a car.

The 63-year-old man was riding the electric bike on Fair Bank, Shipley, at about 10.30pm last night when he was struck by a dark blue car.

The occupants of the vehicle, which is believed to be a Volkswagen, reportedly assaulted the man before stealing his bike and riding off towards Owlet Road.

*(Image: Gazz Hall)*

The victim was hospitalised with a serious leg injury.

### Full police statement

West Yorkshire Police said: "We are appealing for witnesses after a man suffered serious injuries in a robbery in Shipley.

"The 63-year-old was riding an electric bike along Fair Bank at about 10.30pm yesterday (Wednesday) when he was struck by a dark blue car.

  The scene of a robbery in Shipley 

*(Image: Gazz Hall)*

"The occupants of the car, believed to be a Volkswagen, are said to have got out of the car and assaulted the man before riding off on his electric bike towards Owlet Road, followed by the car.

"The man was taken to hospital where he was treated for a serious injury.

"Anyone who witnessed the incident, or who has footage which may assist this investigation is asked to contact Bradford CID via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat or by calling 101, quoting crime reference 13260515079.

"Information can also be reported anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555111 or online."
