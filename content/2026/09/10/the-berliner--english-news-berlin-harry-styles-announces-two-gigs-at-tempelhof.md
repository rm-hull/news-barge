Harry Styles is returning to Berlin in style with two concerts announced for 2027. The open-air gigs will take place at Tempelhof Airport with a capacity for 60,000. They’ll be part of his Together, Together Tour.

With 30 shows are scheduled in New York’s Madison Square Garden, the British is taking the approach of playing multiple shows in a handful of venues. At first, Germany was missing from the tour schedule. But now it’s official: he’s retuning to the city he lived in and played a key influence in his album *Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally,* parts of which were recorded in Hansa studios.

The first round of presale tickets starts on September 14. General presale starts on September 16. Put the dates in your diary (and start saving up) because you know they’re going to sell out fast.
