A BRADFORD firm of more than 30 years has been rated ‘exceptional’ by its customers.

Jet Plant Hire, which operates out of Bradford, recorded a Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 96—far exceeding the construction industry’s typical benchmark of 20 to 50.

The company described the result as "exceptional."

Ian Mitchell, commercial director at Jet Plant Hire, said: "We're proud of the many achievements and accreditations we've earned, but this recognition is especially meaningful because it comes directly from our customers.

"Their feedback tells us we're delivering the high standards of service, reliability, and support that they expect from Jet every day."

The NPS is a global measure of customer loyalty and satisfaction, gathered by asking customers to rate their experiences on a scale of one to ten.

Of those surveyed, 96 per cent rated Jet as "exceptional," and no detractors were recorded.

This marks an eight-point improvement on Jet’s already strong score of 88 in 2025.

With more than 30 years in the industry, Jet Plant Hire supports the UK’s road network through its nationwide fleet of road planers and specialist resurfacing equipment.

The company was recently named a 2025 ESG Innovator by Avetta, in recognition of its commitment to environmental, social, and governance standards.
