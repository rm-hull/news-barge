BRADFORD has been named one of the cheapest cities in the UK to buy a burger.

According to new research by Buzz Bingo, the city ranked seventh in a list comparing average burger prices across 30 of the UK’s largest cities.

In Bradford, beef burgers average £5.50 and veggie burgers £5.49, making it one of the most affordable places in the country for burger lovers.

Mark Fletcher, head of brand at Buzz Bingo, said: "We all love a good burger, but our research shows that the price can look very different depending on where you live.

"Hull comes out as the best-value city, while those grabbing a burger in Edinburgh could be paying almost £4 more.

"We also found that going veggie doesn’t always mean saving money, with some cities actually charging more for a veggie burger than a beef one.

"With Brits loving a takeaway burger, it’s worth knowing what you’re paying for.

"Hopefully our findings will help people find a tasty burger without breaking the bank."

The research used a popular food delivery app to compare burger prices in the UK’s most populated cities.

Hull was named the cheapest city overall, with an average burger price of £4.68.
