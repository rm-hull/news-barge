York artists are coming together for a fundraising day of street art, workshops and family fun – and to continue the legacy of Acombfest!

York artists who have helped raise thousands of pounds for youth homelessness charity SASH (Safe and Sound Homes) are bringing the creative community together once again with *The Board Meeting*, a fundraising event celebrating the legacy of Acomb Fest.

Acomb Fest, York’s first street art festival, took place this summer and was a huge success – and it’s not stopping there.

Organised by Sharon McDonagh and SOLA of RARE Collective in partnership with Art of Protest, *The Board Meeting* will take place at The Carlton Tavern, York, on**Saturday 12 September**, featuring a day of street art, workshops, live DJ sets, children’s art competitions, food and drink.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Acomb-Fest-3.webp)

The event will bring together artists, families, local businesses and the wider creative community to celebrate the spirit of Acomb Fest, which transformed Acomb into an open-air gallery through large-scale murals, live painting and community participation.

As part of the fundraising activity, the mural boards created during Acomb Fest’s Paint Jam are being offered through an online silent auction. The original boards are available to view and bid on here.

The online auction features original works from leading street artists including Lady Mkei, BOXXHEAD, HazardOne, Lincoln Lightfoot, Liskbot, Nicolas Dixon, Sola Art of Street, spAm and Victoria Hill, alongside additional donated artworks, with a percentage of each sale going to the charity SASH.

*The Board Meeting* also sees the return of Art of Protest’s spray paint workshops, with a ticketed session at 12pm followed by a drop-in session at 1pm, available on a first-come, first-served basis.

Running alongside *The Board Meeting* and continuing the fundraising campaign, RARE Collective will also launch a new exhibition in support of SASH at City Screen York. 

The exhibition opened on Sunday 6 September and will run until Saturday 10 October, providing a further opportunity for audiences to experience work from the creative community while supporting SASH.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/SASH-Team-at-Acomb-Fest.webp)

“SASH has always been close to my heart,” said Sharon McDonagh, founder of RARE Collective and long-standing supporter of the charity.

“Over the years I’ve seen first-hand the difference they make to young people’s lives, and I’ve always believed that creativity has the power to bring people together and create positive change.

“The Board Meeting is a celebration of everything that made Acomb Fest special – the artists, the community, the local businesses and the shared belief that we can make a difference when we work together.

“To see these incredible artworks continue their journey by raising money for SASH feels like the perfect next chapter. The new RARE exhibition at City Screen gives us another opportunity to bring great art to the public and support SASH through creativity.”
