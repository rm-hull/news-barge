**Two popular Harrogate district pubs are backing a national initiative** encouraging isolated residents to take a simple, life-changing step: head to their local inn, sit in a designated “empty chair,” and signal that they are open to a friendly chat.

Malvern Events, the team behind 

**The Square & Compass** in North Rigton and**The Castle** in Spofforth, has joined forces with the charity**Empty Chairs**. The initiative aims to combat the UK’s growing isolation epidemic by turning traditional pub spaces into active community hubs.
The local launch is scheduled for 

**Monday 14 September 2026**. Under the scheme, anyone feeling lonely or looking to make new connections can visit either pub, head to the designated community seating area, and wear a splash of** orange**to show they are happy to strike up a conversation.
**Alastair Scott of Malvern Inns highlighted that the rise of automation makes physical community spaces more important than ever. He said:** Having someone to talk to and share with is really important for many people, but starting the journey of making new friends can be daunting. Sometimes simply knowing that there is someone else there who is happy to have a conversation can make all the difference.We often talk about what pubs and restaurants provide in terms of food, drink and entertainment, but our role in bringing people together is arguably even more important. Empty Chairs feels like a very simple first step – and that’s what makes it so powerful.

Taking the concept a step further, both the Spofforth and North Rigton venues have announced they will introduce 

**dedicated community tables on Christmas Day**. This will offer a welcoming environment and festive company for local residents who would otherwise be spending the holiday entirely alone.
The organizers are now calling on other cafés, bars, and restaurants across Harrogate and the wider North Yorkshire footprint to adopt the scheme and help rebuild genuine human connections.
