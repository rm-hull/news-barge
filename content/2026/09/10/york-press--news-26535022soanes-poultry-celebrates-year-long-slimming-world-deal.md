A poultry company in East Riding is celebrating a successful business partnership a year after securing a major supply deal.

Soanes Poultry, based in Middleton on the Wolds, has been supplying Slimming World Kitchen with its Yorkshire-reared chicken for the past 12 months.

Ben Lee, managing director of Soanes Poultry, said: "A year ago, we were invited to supply branded chicken fillets for Slimming World Kitchen's healthy recipe boxes.

"We're now proud to supply 100 per cent of the chicken used in Slimming World Kitchen's delicious, nutritious recipe boxes."

Allison Brentnall, director of food at Slimming World Kitchen, said: "We pride ourselves on using high-quality, fresh ingredients in our Slimming World Kitchen boxes – which is why we have happily worked with Soanes for the past 12 months.

"We receive great reviews on the quality of the poultry in our boxes, so our customers are happy too, which is the most important thing to us."
