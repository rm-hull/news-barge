At last, we can reveal the venue and date for the 2027 BUSINESSiQ Awards - and nominations are now open.

We started planning the day after this year's event, searching for a venue worthy of an unforgettable night celebrating the North East’s great businesses.

  **The 2027 awards will take place atRaby Castleon Thursday, April 29.**

The historic venue will provide a spectacular backdrop for our fifth awards, with its 14th-century castle, 200-acre deer park and newly remodelled Walled Gardens. Enjoy a drink, explore the grounds and soak up the historic splendour.

There will be food and entertainment in and around The Dutch Barn before and after the awards, which will be held in The Riding School—an 18th-century architectural gem reimagined for the 21st century.


More details will follow in the coming weeks at www.businessiqawards.co.uk, where you can already nominate a business that has helped make the region a success.

  We are ready to receive your nominations. Are you ready for a night at the castle? Nominations are now open - just **CLICK HERE** and give us a few details.

Last year the BUSINESSiQ Awards at the Hilton Hotel alongside the Tyne Bridge named PD Ports as its Business of the Year, with Paul Foreman - now CEO - telling us: "Winning Business of the Year is recognition of the ambition we have for the business and the confidence we have in what we’re building for the future, working closely with partners across the North East.”

Michelle Robson, Chief People and Compliance Officer, said: “This recognition means a huge amount because it reflects the people behind the business, whether they work on the dock, in engineering, logistics or professional services. This award is testament to that collective effort and the direction we’re taking together.”

BUSINESSiQ Editor Mike Hughes said: "Each year we try to outdo ourselves, and that is getting more challenging as our events become more popular and our guests expect the best.

"But I'm so excited that our 2027 event at Raby Castle will once again reset the benchmark for regional business awards - the backdrop of the castle itself and the splendid and historic buildings we will be in for the event and the entertainment are simply spectacular. I can't wait!"
