A North East charity which provides arts and crafts activities to children in hospital has received an early Christmas present from Santa.

Darlington Round Table has donated £500 from the proceeds of its annual Santa's Sleigh to Forever Eight, a charity founded in loving memory of a brave eight-year-old girl.

Forever Eight was established in 2023 by the family of Amelia Davies, who tragically died from complications arising from treatment for neuroblastoma.

Led by Amelia's grandma, Heather Hill, the charity raises awareness of the disease, while also helping make children's hospital stays a little brighter by providing them with bags filled with crafts, toys, and activities.

Heather said: “All of us at Forever Eight are very grateful for the donation from Darlington Round Table.

“Our motto is ‘inflate creativity, create a smile one child at a time’, and this donation is going to help lots of children smile. Thank you.”

The £500 donation will help Forever Eight support more than 150 children during stays at Darlington Memorial Hospital.

Alan Britton, chairman of Darlington Round Table, said: "We're really pleased that some of the money raised through last year's Santa's Sleigh – donated by local people – will help support this fantastic Darlington charity.

“It's incredibly inspiring how they've turned such a tragic loss into a way of helping other families during very difficult times. Thanks to Amelia's family and friends, children from across our town are going to get a little bit of joy, comfort, and entertainment.”
