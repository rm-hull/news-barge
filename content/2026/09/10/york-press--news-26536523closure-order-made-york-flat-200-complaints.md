Magistrates have made a closure order on a York flat at the centre of hundreds of incidents of anti-social behaviour.

The frequency and nature of actions by people visiting the flat distressed nearby residents so much one armed themselves with a knife and was preparing to go and confront some of the visitors before changing their mind.

Another had moved out temporarily until the area quietened down, magistrates were told.

York Magistrates Court heard that from January onwards this year, the flat’s landlord Yorkshire Housing Association and North Yorkshire Police have received more than 200 complaints relating to the property.

They include constant arrivals at night, residents in the same block of flat being unable to sleep because the intercom buzzer was being continually sounded by people wanting admission, door banging, kicking of doors, swearing and loud music.

After reading statements and other evidence presented by the police, magistrates made a three-month closure order on the flat in Cornetto Court off Lawrence Street, banning anyone from going to the flat apart from the tenant.
