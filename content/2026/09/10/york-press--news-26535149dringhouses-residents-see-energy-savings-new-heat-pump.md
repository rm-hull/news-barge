## York residents cut energy use by nearly a third after housing upgrade

An upgrade at Honeysuckle House, an independent living scheme in Dringhouses, has helped residents cut energy use by nearly one third *(Image: City of York Council)*

A housing upgrade in York has helped residents cut energy use by nearly one third.

Honeysuckle House, an independent living scheme in Dringhouses, achieved the savings after each of its 27 flats was fitted with air source heat pumps.

The work was funded by a £1,894,399 grant from the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority’s Net Zero Fund, which in turn came from the UK Government.

George and Di Harris, who live in one of the one-bedroom flats, said they have already noticed the benefits.

Mr Harris said: "We used to use about £100 of electricity a month with the old system.

"Last winter we used about £65 a month.

"That’s a good saving."

The heat pumps have reduced energy use by an average of 32 per cent across the scheme.

Cllr Dave Merrett, executive member for housing at City of York Council, said: "This upgrade has made a huge difference to the tenants of the 62 homes that have benefited.

"Achieving average energy savings of around one third of what they previously used is fantastic for tenants and great for the environment too."

Similar improvements were made at Alex Lyon House in Tang Hall.
