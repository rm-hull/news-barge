Our Yorkshire Farm’s Amanda Owen said her ex-husband was “unbearable” after his operation.

Clive Owen opened up about being a “horrible patient” after having to undergo a procedure.

In a repeat of Our Farm Next Door, the pair opened up about how difficult life was when Clive was recovering.


## Amanda Owen calls ex-husband Clive “unbearable”

The pair reflected on life following Clive’s operation, with Amanda saying Clive had been a “frustrating and horrible thing”.

Amanda said: “It has been very frustrating for him.

“That is me being polite.

“You have been unbearable.”

Clive burst into laughter and responded: “I have not.

“I have not, I have kept to myself.

“I’ve minded my own business, I don’t think I have shouted at anybody or anything.”

Amanda revealed: “One day, you were sat in your underpants staring at the floor in the kitchen.”

Clive added: “It’s a really frustrating and horrible thing.”

Amanda came back with: “You really are a frustrating and horrible thing.”


In a past episode of Our Farm Next Door, Clive had opened up about taking a step back from the farm and helping his children to take over the family business.

Talking about stepping away from the farm, Clive said: “It's time to pass on the baton and let them do more and more, and that is how it is. “I am here to watch over them and annoy them, and we will get there in the end."

Clive mentioned how his sons had taken over his responsibilities while he was recovering so well that he might become “redundant” on the farm.

He added: “It means from now on they needn't hand the reins back to me, they can just carry on.

"I could find myself redundant, what do you think, Miley?

“That is what family farms are all about, everybody has to help."
