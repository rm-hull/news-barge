A PIONEERING women-only outdoor wellbeing weekend at Nell Bank has been hailed a major success after attracting 100 attendees from across the north of England and raising funds to support the charity's work with children.

  One Wild Night at Nell Bank in Ilkley 

*(Image: Heidi Marfitt)*

The inaugural One Wild Night event took place on September 5 and 6 at the outdoor education centre in Ilkley, offering participants 24 hours of activities designed to promote wellbeing, connection and time in nature.

  One Wild Night at Nell Bank in Ilkley 

*(Image: Heidi Marfitt)*

Women taking part enjoyed a wide range of experiences including bushcraft, forest bathing, foraging, nature photography, orienteering, Reiki and a drumming circle. The weekend also featured a feast of Himalayan curries prepared by Nabin Maharjan, a friend of Nell Bank originally from Nepal, followed by a sound bath beneath the trees.

  One Wild Night at Nell Bank in Ilkley 

*(Image: Heidi Marfitt)*

After spending the night under canvas, attendees began Sunday morning with sunrise Pilates before taking a woodland walk to Ilkley Lido for an outdoor swim.

  One Wild Night at Nell Bank in Ilkley 

*(Image: Heidi Marfitt)*

Proceeds from the event will support Nell Bank's core mission of providing children with opportunities to experience and learn from the natural world. Since 1977, the charity has introduced generations of young people to the outdoors and now welcomes around 24,000 children each year, many from communities where access to nature is limited.

  One Wild Night at Nell Bank in Ilkley 

*(Image: Heidi Marfitt)*

As the charity prepares to celebrate its 50th anniversary next year, organisers hope events such as One Wild Night will help sustain its work and further its ambition of ensuring every child enjoys a first experience of the outdoors.

  One Wild Night at Nell Bank in Ilkley 

*(Image: Heidi Marfitt)*

Nicola Holmes, event organiser at Nell Bank, said: "One Wild Night was created to give women the chance to pause and reconnect with themselves, with nature and, most importantly, with each other.

"Seeing 100 women come together, laugh, learn, share experiences and support one another was incredibly special. It felt like the beginning of something bigger."

  One Wild Night at Nell Bank in Ilkley 

*(Image: Heidi Marfitt)*

Dan Goodey, chief executive of The Nell Bank Charitable Trust, said it was "wonderful" to see the site transformed for the event.

He said: "The women who joined us experienced exactly what we try to give the children who visit Nell Bank, the chance to enjoy themselves while learning, and to feel the wellbeing benefits that come from spending time outdoors."

  One Wild Night at Nell Bank in Ilkley 

*(Image: Heidi Marfitt)*

Workshops were delivered by members of the Nell Bank team alongside partners Get Out Moor, Synergy Body & Mind, Rooted & Wild, Rich Bunce Photography and Loud Minority Samba Band. Sunday morning fitness sessions were led by event partner Unity, a female-owned gym based in Embsay.

  One Wild Night at Nell Bank in Ilkley 

*(Image: Heidi Marfitt)*

Participant Nicola Akers described the weekend as a unique experience.

She said: "There was something really special about being surrounded by so many women who were all there for different reasons but shared the same wish to connect, have fun and spend time outdoors.

"I left feeling energised, inspired and part of a community. I'm already looking forward to the next one."

  One Wild Night at Nell Bank in Ilkley 

*(Image: Heidi Marfitt)*

A date for the next One Wild Night is yet to be announced. Women who want to hear first can register their interest at own.nellbank.org.
