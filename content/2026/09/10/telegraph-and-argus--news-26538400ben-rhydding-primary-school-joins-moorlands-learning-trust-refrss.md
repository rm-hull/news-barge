A PRIMARY school has joined an expanding multi-academy trust.

Ben Rhydding Primary School has officially joined Moorlands Learning Trust (MLT).

The Ilkley school, which is known for its commitment to inspiring, challenging and nurturing children, has become the latest addition to the Trust's growing family of schools across West and North Yorkshire.

School leaders said the move will allow Ben Rhydding Primary School to maintain its distinctive identity while benefitting from enhanced educational opportunities, professional development and collaboration with other schools within the trust.

  Ben Rhydding Primary School 

*(Image: submitted)*

The school's learning values, which encourage pupils to consider what they can learn, how they can contribute and who they can become, closely align with Moorlands Learning Trust's vision of helping every student succeed, excel and make a positive difference in their communities.

Headteacher Peter Timms welcomed the partnership, describing it as an opportunity to build on the school's existing strengths.

He said: “We are excited to join Moorlands Learning Trust, which shares our values and aspirations for young people.

“This partnership allows us to build on our strengths while collaborating with colleagues across the Trust, sharing expertise, learning from one another and creating even greater opportunities for our pupils, staff and wider community.”

  Ben Rhydding Primary School Headteacher Peter Timms 

*(Image: submitted)*

Moorlands Learning Trust Chief Executive Officer Helen Williams said the Trust was delighted to welcome the school.

She said: “We are thrilled to welcome Ben Rhydding Primary School to our Trust family.

“The school has an excellent reputation, a strong commitment to its community and a clear set of values that closely align with our own.

“We look forward to working together to share best practice, broaden opportunities and provide an exceptional education for every child across the Trust.”

Trust leaders said the addition of Ben Rhydding Primary School marks an important milestone for MLT as it continues its commitment to enriching opportunities for young people through integrity, collaboration and empowerment.

The trust was formed on July 1, 2017, when Ilkley Grammar School converted its single academy trust to multi-academy trust status. The Skipton Academy joined in 2019 as the first partner academy, and during 2023/24 the trust welcomed Ashlands Primary School, Burley Oaks Primary School, Eastburn Primary School, Menston Primary School and Nidderdale High School.

In 2025, Benton Park School became the first secondary school in Leeds to join the Trust and, in the same year, Talbot Primary School joined as the Trust's first Leeds primary school.
