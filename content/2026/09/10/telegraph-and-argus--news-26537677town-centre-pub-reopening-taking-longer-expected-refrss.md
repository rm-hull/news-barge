THE owners of a Bradford district pub have said efforts to reopen the business are taking "much longer than we ever expected" as customers continue to wait for its return.

Reconnection opened in the former Hullabaloo premises on Westgate, Shipley, last October.

At the time, owners Beth and Nathan High - who also run The Junction pub in Baildon - told the Telegraph & Argus: "We're thrilled to be taking on the former Hullabaloo.

"Beer has always been a passion, and we can't wait to bring that love to Reconnection Shipley - a place where everyone can enjoy a proper pint in a warm, welcoming atmosphere.

"We honestly can't wait to welcome everyone in and start this new chapter."

## Pub temporarily shuts

However, on August 18, the pub announced on Facebook that it had "hit a few electrical issues" meaning it would "need to close temporarily while we get everything made safe and put right".

They added: "We’re absolutely gutted — especially with our new food menu due to launch in just 2 days! The timing really couldn’t be worse."

## No reopening date

Reconnection Shipley issued a latest update on its Facebook page yesterday (Wednesday, September 9).

It read: "We know we've been quiet for the last few weeks, but we're still here and working hard behind the scenes to get everything resolved and the doors back open.

"It's taking much longer than we ever expected, and while we don't have a reopening date just yet, we'd rather wait until we have something definite before giving one."

The Telegraph & Argus approached Reconnection Shipley for a comment.

## Flood of support from customers

Despite the ongoing uncertainty, Reconnection said it had received an overwhelming response from customers keen to hear how progress was going.

The business thanked supporters who had been in touch during the closure.

It said: "We've been inundated with lovely messages asking how things are going and checking in on us.

"We're sorry we haven't managed to reply to everyone, but please know we really do appreciate every single message."

## Hope for good news soon

While no firm timeline has been announced, the team said it hopes to share more positive news in the near future.

Thanking customers for their patience, the post concluded: "Thank you so much for sticking with us.

"Hopefully we'll have some good news to share very soon and can finally get back to doing what we love."
