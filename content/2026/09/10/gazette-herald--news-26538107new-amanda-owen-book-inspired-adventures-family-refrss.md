THE Yorkshire Shepherdess Amanda Owen has released a new children’s book.

Cosy Bedtime Stories from the Farm is a collection of 10 bedtime stories for readers aged six and above, inspired by her family’s real-life adventures on Ravenseat Farm in North Yorkshire.

RECOMMENDED READING:

The tales offer a glimpse into life on a working farm, from busy days to quiet moments in nature, and are illustrated by Becca Hall.

  Amanda Owen 

*(Image: SUBMIT)*

Amanda is a shepherdess, mother of nine, TV personality, and Sunday Times bestselling author born in Huddersfield.

She is a first-generation farmer and rose to fame through the hit TV series Our Yorkshire Farm, which attracted millions of viewers across the UK.

Her most recent series, Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, has become the highest-rated programme on the channel in the past two years.

The new book has been published in hardback, e-book and audio by Puffin.
