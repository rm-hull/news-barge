Harrogate Town AFC have completed the signing of Leyton Orient midfielder **Michael Craig** on a three-month loan contract. The 23-year-old arrive at the EnviroVent Stadium with extensive League One experience, boasting over 80 appearances in the third tier for both Orient and Reading. The Scotland youth international joins just in time for Town’s home double-header against Tamworth and AFC Fylde.

Craig, who will wear the number 19 shirt, brings a high-calibre pedigree to Simon Weaver’s squad. He began his footballing journey at Watford before joining the Tottenham Hotspur academy at age 12. Alongside his twin brother, Craig was named in Spurs’ UEFA Conference League squad as an 18-year-old in 2021. He later moved to Reading, making his professional debut for the Royals, before joining Leyton Orient ahead of the 2025/26 campaign.

Harrogate Town Manager Simon Weaver expressed his delight at securing the midfielder’s signature to inject control into the centre of the pitch.

**Weaver said:**

He’s an experienced head at just 23-years-old and a solid League One performer, so we’re delighted to have him here.

He’s up for the challenge of coming and playing for us for three months, he’s got expertise on the ball and a real presence about him. Having that authority in central midfield can help us deliver more controlled performances going forward.



Subject to standard FA and National League approval, Craig will be immediately available for selection for this weekend’s home clash against Tamworth.
