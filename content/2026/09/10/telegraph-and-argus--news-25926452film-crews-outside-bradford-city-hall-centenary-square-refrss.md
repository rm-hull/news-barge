BRADFORD is set to play a starring role in the new season of Channel 5's hit All Creatures Great and Small.

Filming for the show took place in Bradford city centre in March, with streets, including Centenary Square, rolled back in time to the 1950s.

Actors and actresses in period costumes could be seen walking outside City Hall in Centenary Square.

Vans containing props were also just behind the cameras.

One headline on a newspaper bill 'King and queen to see cup final' suggests the time period may be 1950.

King George VI and Queen Elizabeth attended Wembley to see Arsenal defeat Liverpool 2–0 in the April 1950 final

It's known that the new season of the nostalgic Yorkshire-set drama takes place after the end of World War II.

## What is All Creatures Great and Small?

All Creatures Great and Small is a British television series set in 1930s and 1940s Northern England.

  Filming in Bradford city centre 

*(Image: T&A)*

It is based on a series of books about a Yorkshire veterinary surgeon written by Alf Wight under the pen name of James Herriot.

The series, produced by Playground Entertainment for Channel 5, offers a new adaptation of Wight's books.

It has previously been filmed in the Yorkshire Dales, and received funding from Screen Yorkshire.

The new series starts on September 17.
