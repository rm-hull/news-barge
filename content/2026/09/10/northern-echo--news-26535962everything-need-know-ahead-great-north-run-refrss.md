Everything you need to know ahead of this weekend’s Great North Run.

Thousands of runners and spectators will descend on Newcastle's city centre as the Great North Run kicks off on Sunday (September 13).

### Start and finish line

Start line: Central Motorway, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE2 4PZ.

Finish line: The Leas, South Shields, NE33 3HT.

### When will the Red Arrows flypast?

The Red Arrows will be flying over the start at 10.52am.

### How can I watch?

BBC One will broadcast the run live from 10am to 2pm.

Runners can catch the highlights on BBC Three at 9pm.

The Great Run app lets runners and spectators track the race live, view results and donate to runners’ chosen charities.

The finish will also be streamed live on GreatRunTV’s YouTube channel.

### Travel

Public transport is highly recommended due to the number of road closures and limited parking options.

Plan your journey here.

Alternatively, you can use the Great North Run travel assistant.

Day Rover tickets are also available in advance or on the day via the Pop app for just £6.50 and can be used throughout the day across different methods of public transport.

### Toilets

There will be a number of toilets and urinals available at the start line, along the course, and the finish line.

### What do I get at the end?

Finishers will receive a medal and a finisher’s pack.

The pack includes a bottle of water, a finisher’s T-shirt if selected and an edible treat.
