A REGISTERED nurse is set to "take the plunge" this month by opening a new clinic in a town centre.

Amy Wilson, founder of AW Aesthetics, is launching the clinic in Ilkley later this month after outgrowing her previous space.

Ms Wilson, an Ilkley resident and mother, said: "As the business continued to grow, I found myself adding more equipment, treatments and services.

"I knew I needed a larger space and wanted the opportunity to build a team of skincare specialists around me.

"It was time to take the plunge and open my own clinic."

  The new AW Aesthetics Clinic in Ilkley 

*(Image: submitted)*

Ms Wilson has 16 years of nursing experience and has spent eight years in medical aesthetics, holding a master’s degree in advanced clinical practice and qualifications as an independent prescriber.

Since founding AW Aesthetics in 2018, she has built a reputation for safe, evidence-based care and exceptional patient care.

For the past three years, her clinic operated from Beauty in Bloom on Cowpasture Road, where she steadily expanded her services and client base.

When the former pharmacy next door became available, Ms Wilson saw the ideal opportunity to grow her business.

  The interior of the new AW Aesthetics Clinic in Ilkley 

*(Image: submitted)*

The new clinic will feature three dedicated treatment rooms and a wider range of medical-grade facial and body treatments.

Ms Wilson said: "My latest treatment for the new clinic is NeoGen Plasma.

"It's a revolutionary treatment that delivers remarkable skin rejuvenation results without surgery."

AW Aesthetics will be the only clinic in Ilkley—and within a 10-mile radius—to offer NeoGen Plasma, a procedure often described as a "surgical-free facelift."

High-profile case studies have included Strictly Come Dancing judge Shirley Ballas, broadcaster Debbie Magee, and actress Tamzin Outhwaite.

Amy's approach to aesthetics focuses on enhancing natural beauty rather than creating obvious change.

She said: "I specialise in skin health and subtle treatments that make my patients look and feel better without anyone knowing they have had any obvious procedures."

  Amy Wilson with the Neo Gen machine 

*(Image: submitted)*

The expanded clinic will also welcome Sophie Walker, a specialist in advanced skincare who brings additional expertise and services to the team.

The official opening is scheduled for mid-September, with launch celebrations to be announced soon.

Clients and followers are encouraged to stay tuned online or visit the new Cowpasture Road location once it opens later this month.
