A North Yorkshire facilities support company has gone into voluntary liquidation, with debts approaching £30,000.

Selby-based S&D Facilities Managed Ltd entered into a voluntary liquidation in August 7.

The business, which was incorporated in 2022, and based at The Old Maltings, Ousegate, appointed Wakefield-based DS Insolvency as administrator.

A financial report known as a statement of affairs says the business had no assets and no preferential creditors- those who are paid first.

However, there were trade and expense creditors owed £9800.00, a director owed £4,000, and HMRC, which was owed £15,000 in Corporation Tax.

A list of all the creditors showed BT was owed £3,647.27, director Simon Hewitt was owed £4,000, HMRC the £15,000, Positive Energy was owed £5,040 and Yu Energy was owed £1,086.72.

This left a deficiency of £28,800.99. With the former business having issued and called up capital of £1, this gave an overall deficiency or shortfall of £28,801.99.
