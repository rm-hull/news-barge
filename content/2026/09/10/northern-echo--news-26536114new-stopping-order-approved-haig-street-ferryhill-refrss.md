Part of Haig Street in Ferryhill is to be stopped up under a new Department for Transport order.

The Secretary of State has made the “Stopping up of Highway (North East) (No.12) Order 2026” under Section 247 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990.

The order covers part of the width of Haig Street and will enable development permitted by Durham County Council under planning law.

Copies are available free from the Transport Infrastructure Planning Casework Unit, PO Box 1393, Newcastle Upon Tyne, NE99 5FQ, or by email at [nationalcasework@dft.gov.uk](mailto:nationalcasework@dft.gov.uk), quoting SUO3627238.

The order can also be inspected during normal opening hours at Ferryhill Library, 12 North Street, Ferryhill, Durham, DL17 8HX.

Feedback is open from September 7, 2026 to October 19, 2026.

Anyone wishing to challenge the decision must apply to the High Court within six weeks of September 7, 2026.
