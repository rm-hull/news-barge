Several fire crews are in attendance at a large blaze near to a former power station tonight.

It’s in a field in Eggborough, near Selby.

North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service says it is close to the former power station site but not affecting it.

**Video**

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/fire-eggborough-15-sept-2026-2.webp)

The blaze has sent flames and smoke rising into the air that can be seen for miles.

A number of fire vehicles are in the area, including the Tadcaster water bowser.

They are parked in a country lane round the back of the former power station site.

Eggborough Power Station closed in 2018 and demolition began in 2020. But there is still a substation and power lines on the site.
