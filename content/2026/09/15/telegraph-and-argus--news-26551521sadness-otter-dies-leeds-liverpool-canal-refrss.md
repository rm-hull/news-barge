AN otter has died in the Bradford district, West Yorkshire Police has confirmed.

The incident happened over the weekend on the Leeds Liverpool Canal at Silsden, close to Bridge 192.

The animal drowned after going into a crayfish trap, the force said.

The crime has been recorded as unnecessary suffering.

  Leeds Liverpool Canal, Silsden 

*(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Over the weekend, unknown suspects have set a Crayfish trap on the canal close to Bridge 192 on the Leeds Liverpool Canal at Silsden.

"Unfortunately an otter became trapped and drowned.

"A crime for Unnecessary Suffering has been recorded.

"Crime number 13260525008.

"If anyone has any info please contact 101 and quote the above number."
