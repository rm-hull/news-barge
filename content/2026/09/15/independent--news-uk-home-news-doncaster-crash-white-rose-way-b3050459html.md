The ’multiple-vehicle collision’ occurred about 11am on Sunday (PA)

Two people died, and four others were seriously injured in a “multiple-vehicle collision” on Sunday.

The fatal incident happened on White Rose Way in Doncaster at 11am.

South Yorkshire Police said a black Mitsubishi L200 reportedly hit four pedestrians on the carriageway before colliding with a black Audi and a silver BMW moving in the other direction.

Two pedestrians died at the scene, while four other people were taken to hospital with serious but not life-threatening injuries.

The driver of the Mitsubishi was arrested on suspicion of two counts of causing death by dangerous driving, two counts of causing serious injury by dangerous driving, and driving while unfit.

The 30-year-old man has been bailed pending further inquiries.

The force is appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage.
