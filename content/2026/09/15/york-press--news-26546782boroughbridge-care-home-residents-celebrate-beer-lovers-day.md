## North Yorkshire care home hosts pub-style beer celebration for residents and guests

Residents at Boroughbridge Manor care home in Boroughbridge, North Yorkshire, marked National Beer Lovers’ Day with a pub-style celebration featuring UK and international ales, a beer-themed quiz and a look at the history of brewing. *(Image: Supplied)*

A North Yorkshire care home has raised a glass in celebration of National Beer Lovers’ Day.

Residents at Boroughbridge Manor in Boroughbridge enjoyed a pub-style experience with a selection of craft ales, bitters, and lagers from both the UK and abroad.

The event included a beer-themed quiz and learning about the history of beer.

  A residents at Boroughbridge Manor care home marks National Beer Lovers’ Day with a pub-style celebration 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Boroughbridge Manor general manager Lynnette Sellers said: “We love socialising and celebrating all kind of events, it is wonderful to be able to invite family and friends to come and join us for occasions like this. National Beer Lovers’ Day is a day that the residents really enjoy, who doesn’t love a cold beer on a hot summer’s day!”

Residents and their guests were invited to relax in the lounge while sampling different brews.

One resident, Andy, said: “I didn’t realise we had so many lovely local ales, it was fantastic to be able to enjoy a cold beer in the lounge with my friends and family – you can’t really beat that now can you?”

Boroughbridge Manor is part of the Barchester Healthcare group, one of the UK’s largest care providers.

The home offers dementia care, residential care, and respite care.
