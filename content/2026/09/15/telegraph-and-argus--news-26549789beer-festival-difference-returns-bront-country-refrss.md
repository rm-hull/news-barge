A steam-powered pub crawl through Brontë Country is set to return as part of a unique beer and music festival.

The Keighley & Worth Valley Railway Beer & Music Festival will run from October 15 to 18, promising four days of heritage trains, live music, and hundreds of beers.

Visitors can travel between festival venues aboard steam and diesel trains, earning the event its nickname as "Yorkshire’s biggest steam powered pub crawl."

Noel Hartley, business and operations manager at the Keighley & Worth Valley Railway, said: "There really isn't anything else quite like it.

"For four days the whole railway becomes part of the festival.

"People aren't just arriving at a venue and staying there.

"They are travelling by train between different bars, music and food throughout the Worth Valley."

  The Keighley & Worth Valley Railway Beer & Music Festival will run from October 15 to 18 

*(Image: Tom Marshall)*

Festival sites at Keighley, Ingrow, and Oxenhope will each feature their own selection of drinks and entertainment, with unlimited train travel included in rover tickets.

Hundreds of beers and ales will be on offer, alongside cider, gin, wine, street food and live music.

Mr Hartley said: "You can have a pint, listen to a band, climb aboard a steam train and head off to see what is happening at the next station."

Tickets and further information are available from beerandmusic.co.uk.
