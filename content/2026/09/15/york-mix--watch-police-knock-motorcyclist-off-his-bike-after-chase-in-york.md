This is the moment a motorbike rider who failed to stop for police came into contact with the full force of the law in York.

The dashcam video from a North Yorkshire Police car shows part of a pursuit between officers and the motorcyclist.

It ends with a “tactical contact” on Water End, where the police car bumps the rider off his bike.

**Video**

He was then arrested.

It happened at about 3.45pm on Sunday when officers attempted to stop a Honda 400 motorcycle which was being ridden without a registration plate on Lindsey Avenue.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The rider failed to stop for officers and a pursuit was initiated.

“In line with their training and national guidance, officers used tactical contact on Water End to bring the motorcycle to a safe stop.

“The rider, a man in his 20s, was not injured during the incident. Minor damage was caused to a police vehicle.

“Following the stop, the rider provided a positive roadside drugs test for cannabis and was arrested on suspicion of failing to stop, driving offences and drug-driving. He later failed to provide in custody.”

He’s been charged with:

- Using a motor vehicle without insurance
- Driving otherwise than in accordance with a licence
- Careless driving
- Failing to stop for police
- Failing to provide a specimen for analysis

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The man has been released on bail and is due to appear before magistrates at a later date.

“We continue to target those who use the North Yorkshire’s roads illegally and put other road users at risk.

“Officers are trained to use a range of tactics, where lawful, proportionate and necessary, to safely bring incidents to a conclusion and protect the public.”
