A pervert has been convicted of grooming and sexually abusing a teenage girl.

Mark Faulds allegedly used alcohol, cigarettes and cannabis to make the girl more compliant, the court heard.

The 41-year-old faced 14 charges but was cleared of seven after the judge ruled there was no evidence to support them.

Faulds was cleared of a further charge before being convicted of six counts of engaging in sexual activity with a child following a trial at Teesside Crown Court.

Throughout the trial, Faulds repeatedly denied having sex with the teenager or grooming her with alcohol or drugs.

Faulds’ DNA was found in semen on a condom in woodland, alongside traces of the teenager’s DNA, but it did not prove they had had sex.

Judge Nathan Adams adjourned sentencing until November for a pre-sentence report.

He said: “You have been found guilty of serious sexual offences involving a teenager, clearly that will result in a lengthy prison sentence.”

Faulds, of Oxford Street, Stanley, County Durham, was remanded in custody until sentencing at Durham Crown Court on November 12.

The court heard the teenager’s aunt secretly recorded her account after she said she would deny it if anyone else was told.
