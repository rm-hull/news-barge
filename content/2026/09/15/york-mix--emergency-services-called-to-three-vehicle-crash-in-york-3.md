Emergency services were called to a three-vehicle crash in York last night (Monday).

It happened in **Acomb** at**6.18pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb crews responded to a three vehicle road traffic collision.

“All persons were out of the vehicles on arrival of the crews.

“Crews relocated the vehicles to a safe space at the side of the road and left the incident in the hands of the police.”
