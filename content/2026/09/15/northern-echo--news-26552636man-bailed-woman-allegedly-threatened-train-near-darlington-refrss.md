A man in his 60s has been released on bail after allegedly threatening a woman on a train near Darlington, prompting passengers to intervene.

At about 9.45pm on Monday, August 31, a man was reported to have behaved aggressively towards a woman on a train, travelling between Darlington and Middlesbrough.

The man was then challenged by a number of other passengers on the train.

A 60-year-old man from Middlesbrough was arrested in connection with the incident and has been released on conditional bail.

A spokesperson from the British Transport Police said: “Detectives are appealing for information after a woman was threatened by another passenger whilst travelling on a train to Middlesbrough.

“We are appealing for those who intervened or witnessed the incident to get in touch with police.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact British Transport Police by texting 61016 or by calling 0800 40 50 40, quoting reference 769 of August 31.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
