An Emmerdale star has been confirmed as the latest big name to join this year's pantomime at Middlesbrough Theatre.

Olivia Bromley, best known as Emmerdale's Dawn Taylor, will play The Fairy of the Ring in this year's Aladdin.

Olivia spent eight years on Emmerdale and became the centre of some of the soap's biggest storylines.

From her affair with Joe Tate to her past crimes, Dawn cemented herself as a village favourite. However, she was reportedly 'axed' from the show, with her final episode airing on August 30, which showed Dawn's tragic death.

Now, she is swapping TV for stage as she joins a star-studded cast for the festive performance returning to Middlesbrough Theatre this December.

The actress trained at the Oxford School of Drama and has appeared in a variety of shows including A Midsummer Night's Dream at the National Theatre Riverside, Romeo & Juliet at the Royal Festival Hall, Spent at Soho Theatre and No Borders at the Bush Theatre.

Olivia said: "I can't wait to join the brilliant company of Aladdin and have a really fun Christmas.

"It'll be quite the contrast from the explosive and traumatic soap storyline I've just played.

"I'm so looking forward to it."

  Robert Laughlin as Widow Twanky, Steven Yallop as the Emperor Of China, Morgan Hughes as Balroubadour, Sol Nicholson as Aladdin, Paul Hawkyard as Abanazar and Philip Green as Wishee Washee. 

*(Image: Middlesbrough Council)*

The announcement completes the line-up for the show, which follows Aladdin as he tries to win Princess Balroubadour's heart while battling the scheming Abanazar.

Kesia Bruce, Middlesbrough Council's strategic venues manager, said: "We're delighted to be bringing another fantastic pantomime to audiences across Teesside this year.

"Aladdin is a much-loved family favourite, and with a talented cast, spectacular production values and plenty of festive magic, we know it will be a truly memorable experience.

"We're especially thrilled to welcome Olivia Bromley to the production, and we look forward to seeing audiences of all ages enjoy what will be a wonderful show."

Olivia will also be joined on stage by a host of familiar panto faces and stage stars.

Philip Green returns as Wishee Washee for his eighth consecutive year at Middlesbrough Theatre, while Paul Hawkyard - known for roles in Coronation Street and All Creatures Great and Small - takes the part of Abanazar.

Robert Laughlin also returns as Widow Twanky, with Sol Nicholson as Aladdin, Morgan Hughes as Princess Balroubadour and Steven Yallop as the Emperor of China.

The production promises dazzling costumes, special effects, musical numbers and plenty of audience participation.

Aladdin runs at Middlesbrough Theatre from Saturday, December 5 to Thursday, December 31.

A signed performance is scheduled for Thursday, December 17 at 7pm, followed by a relaxed performance on Tuesday, December 22 at 2pm.

Tickets are on sale now, costing from £25 for children and £26 for adults. Family tickets are also available.

Middlesbrough Theatre has entertained audiences for more than 65 years, with pantomime among its biggest annual draws.
