A York-based company which started with just five children at its first holiday camp is celebrating 20 years of growth, with around 500 youngsters now attending its holiday clubs across Yorkshire on a typical day during the school holidays.

Total Sports, founded by Ollie Holliday, provides holiday activities, primary school PE, PPA, CPD, wraparound care and school club solutions to children, families and schools across Yorkshire.

Mr Holliday, founder and managing director of Total Sports, said: "The idea was always to create somewhere children genuinely wanted to come during the school holidays.

  The Total Sports team leads activities at one of its holiday club venues, which now operate across Yorkshire from East Riding and York to Leeds and Wakefield 

*(Image: Supplied)*

"That means sport, of course, but it is also about fun games with friends, arts and crafts, adventure and, importantly, the sheer variety of things children can do.

"Where else would you find a 40-foot inflatable sausage dog, bubble machines, silent discos and a Nerf arena alongside football, swimming, tennis, arts and crafts and all the traditional games children love?

"We want children to have the freedom to try something different every hour, to find activities they love and, quite simply, have a brilliant day with their friends.

  Total Sports founder Ollie Holliday celebrates 20 years of the York-based business, which has grown from five children at its first holiday camp to around 500 youngsters attending each day during school holidays 

*(Image: Supplied)*

"That variety is a huge part of what makes Total Sports different."

Mr Holliday launched the company after returning to York following a teaching career in Surrey.

The business began with just five children at its first holiday club, but quickly grew through word of mouth and a focus on fun, engaging activities.

Today, Total Sports runs holiday clubs across Yorkshire, including in York, Pocklington, Knaresborough, Harrogate, Wakefield and Leeds.

  Children enjoy a range of activities at a Total Sports holiday club, where youngsters can choose from more than 50 sports, creative and adventure-based sessions each week 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Each week, children can choose from more than 50 activities, from football, swimming and tennis to arts and crafts, treasure hunts and inflatable obstacle courses.

A spokesperson for Total Sports said the company also offers primary school PE, wraparound care and extracurricular services during term time, working with more than 35 schools across the region.

The clubs are aimed at children aged five to 13 and are designed to offer a dependable, active childcare option for families during school holidays.

  Youngsters take part in games and activities at a Total Sports holiday club as the company marks two decades of providing active childcare and school holiday fun 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Mr Holliday said: "Children are different.

"Some love football and want to be running around all day, while others would much rather make something, get creative or take part in an adventure activity.

"Some want a bit of everything.

"That is why we give them the choice.

  Ollie and Kerry Holliday 

*(Image: Supplied)*

"We want children to have a really varied day and leave at the end of it thinking, 'I had a brilliant time.'

"Arty, crafty, sporty, adventure and fun – that's what Total Sports is all about."

He said that while 20 years is a milestone, the company is looking ahead to future growth and reaching more children and schools across the region.
