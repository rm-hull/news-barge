Police are investigating an incident of criminal damage after a window was smashed.

Emergency services were called to Oak Gardens in Crook shortly after 5am on Tuesday, September 15.

No one was injured during the incident and enquiries are ongoing.

A Durham Police spokesperson said: “We were called to reports of criminal damage after a window was smashed in Oak Gardens, Crook, just after 5am this morning (September 15).

“No one was injured and an investigation is ongoing.”
