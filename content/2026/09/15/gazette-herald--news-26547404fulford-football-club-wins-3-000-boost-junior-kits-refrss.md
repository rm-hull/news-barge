A York grass-roots football club is celebrating after receiving a funding boost that will help provide hundreds of young players with new kits over the next two seasons.

Fulford Football Club, based in York, was awarded £3,000 through Persimmon’s Community Champions scheme.

Graham Souter, secretary of Fulford Football Club, said: "This £3,000 will help pay for the junior team shirts for the next two seasons, allowing the club to provide around 350 shirts to its junior membership.

"Persimmon’s continued support of the local football club is very much welcomed and appreciated."

Luke Charters, MP for York Outer, was on hand to formally present a cheque to the club’s secretary alongside representatives from Persimmon.

Mr Charters said: "It was brilliant to meet Graham and hear more about everything Fulford Football Club does for the local community.

"Football is about far more than just the 90 minutes on the pitch.

"Clubs like Fulford bring people together, give players of all ages somewhere to belong and inspire the next generation."

The funding will go towards new football kits for the club’s junior teams.

Persimmon’s Community Champions scheme provides funding to charities, schools, sports clubs and community groups.
