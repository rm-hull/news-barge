York Minster is preparing to welcome a record number of worshippers this festive season with an expanded programme of Advent and Christmas services, concerts and events as it begins celebrations marking its forthcoming 1,400th anniversary.

This year’s schedule includes a second Advent Procession to meet growing demand and will see services available online through the Minster’s YouTube channel.

Other seasonal favourites will return, including the Festival of Nine Lessons and Carols and York Minster’s Christmas Carol Concerts, which will be held on two dates.

The Christmas Tree Festival will also return, featuring 100 hand-decorated trees placed throughout the cathedral.

The Reverend Canon Maggie McClean, Acting Dean of York, said: "In a time of unceasing busyness, we invite you to share in a special moment of worship, quiet reflection, or simply to enjoy the beauties of our space at a special time of year.

"We’re so delighted to be able to welcome more people, not just for Advent and Christmas 2026, but also looking ahead to our 1,400th anniversary celebrations in 2027."

Families are invited to the lighting of the 100 Christmas trees on November 28 and can also enjoy a performance of Tchaikovsky’s The Nutcracker on the Minster’s Grand Organ.
