A NEW project is bringing life-saving skills to Yorkshire communities.

The Yorkshire Ambulance Service Charity (YAS Charity) has launched a scheme to improve health outcomes in Manningham, Bradford, and Goldthorpe, Barnsley, through first aid, CPR and defibrillator training, supported by funding from NHS Charities Together.

Ripaljeet Kaur, the project officer delivering the programme, said: "I’m really excited to be working with communities in Manningham and Goldthorpe.

"These areas have been chosen because of population health needs, significant health inequalities and high ambulance demand."

The project aims to build community capacity, increase awareness of Yorkshire Ambulance Service (YAS), and promote employment and volunteer opportunities within the service.

It also seeks to educate residents about YAS services and encourage greater engagement.

Carey Taylor, head of YAS Charity, said: "At YAS Charity, we are delighted to be able to support this project and enable close working with communities in Manningham and Goldthorpe.

"The Charity’s aim is to engage with communities and to save lives, which this project helps us to achieve."

The scheme will also help develop community resources such as defibrillators.

Community groups in Manningham and Goldthorpe are encouraged to contact YAS at [yas.communityengagement@nhs.net](mailto:yas.communityengagement@nhs.net) to get involved.
