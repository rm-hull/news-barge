The family of a man found dead in the Ripon Canal on Sunday morning has now been informed, North Yorkshire Police has confirmed.

Officers were called to Boroughbridge Road at 8:41am on 13th September after a member of the public discovered the body of a man in his 60s in the water.

Emergency services attended and Boroughbridge Road was closed for much of the morning while enquiries were carried out. It has since reopened.

Police said the death is not believed to involve any suspicious circumstances. A report will be prepared for the coroner.

The force thanked those who shared its original appeal and said its thoughts were with the man’s loved ones.
