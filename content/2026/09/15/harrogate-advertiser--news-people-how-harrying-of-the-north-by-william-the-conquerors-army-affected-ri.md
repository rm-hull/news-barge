# How 'Harrying of the North’ by William the Conqueror’s army affected Ripon area's greatest historic site

## A village near Ripon is to host an event revealing how the North bounced back from the brutal consequences of William the Conqueror’s invasion.

National Trust expert Peter Lewis will give a talk in the Methodist Schoolroom on Bishop Monkton on Wednesday, September 16 at 7pm.

Peter will explain how the monks of Yorkshire’s eight Cistercian abbeys, including UNESCO-listed Fountains Abbey, rebuilt the county in the years that followed the devastation, known as the ‘Harrying of the North’.

This was a ruthless military campaign, described by some historians as a genocide, which was meted out in the North of England by William the Conqueror’s army in 1069 and 1070 in response to rebellions against Norman rule.
