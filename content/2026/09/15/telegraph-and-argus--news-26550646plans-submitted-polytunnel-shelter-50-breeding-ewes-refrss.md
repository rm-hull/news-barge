A PLAN to erect a polytunnel to house sheep on farmland in Low Bradley, near Skipton, has been submitted to North Yorkshire Council.

The 42ft by 24ft purpose built, agricultural polytunnel would be used to provide housing and shelter for around 50 breeding ewes during the lambing period.

Documents supporting the application say the structure is directly connected to the existing agricultural use of land at College End House, College Road.

In addition to providing housing for sheep during the lambing season, it will also provide protection from 'prolonged periods of rain, cold temperatures, strong winds and other adverse weather conditions.'

It is stated: "This is particularly important for newly born lambs, which are more vulnerable to exposure. Housing the flock when required will enable the livestock to be managed in a safer and more controlled environment and will contribute positively to animal welfare."
