THE chief constable of West Yorkshire Police will lead the force until 2029.

Sir John Robins QPM DL has had his tenure extended by the Mayor of West Yorkshire, Tracy Brabin, making him one of the longest-serving chief constables in the UK.

Sir John said: "I am both honoured and delighted to continue serving as Chief Constable of West Yorkshire Police.

"I look forward to working with our leaders, our people, and our partners as we continue to protect and serve the communities in West Yorkshire and the wider region.

"I am incredibly proud to be a police officer and work alongside so many colleagues who are so committed to public service.

"While policing faces significant challenges in the years ahead, I am confident that we can build on the progress we have made and continue to put the public at the heart of everything we do."

Ms Brabin said: "Sir John has dedicated more than three decades of his life to keeping the people of West Yorkshire safe and has earned a national reputation as an outstanding policing leader.

"His commitment to our communities, his focus on supporting victims and bringing offenders to justice, and his determination to drive continuous improvement have helped make West Yorkshire Police one of the country's leading forces.

"Extending his appointment provides stability, experience and strong leadership as we continue our work to make West Yorkshire a safer, fairer place for everyone."

  Sir John Robins 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Sir John has served with West Yorkshire Police since 1990, rising through the ranks to become chief constable in July 2019.

He was awarded a knighthood in the King's New Year Honours 2026 for services to policing.

Baroness Alison Lowe OBE, deputy mayor for policing and crime, said: "Sir John's leadership has been defined by a relentless commitment to public service, professionalism and partnership working.

"He has overseen significant improvements in policing performance while maintaining a clear focus on supporting victims and protecting vulnerable people.

"As one of the longest-serving Chief Constables in the country, his experience is invaluable, and I look forward to continuing to work closely with him to deliver safer communities across West Yorkshire."

During his tenure, crime rates have fallen, more offenders have been brought to justice, and high standards of service have been maintained for victims and witnesses.

Sir John has worked with the mayor and partners to address serious violence, improve neighbourhood safety, and strengthen collaboration across policing and the wider criminal justice system.
