*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

TWO men are reported to have been assaulted while trying to help door staff outside a bar in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police wish to speak to a man in connection with the incident, which happened outside Osbornes Bar in Wellington Road, Whitby, on Saturday (July 4).

One of the men suffered a split lip and a graze to the head, police said.

  


Authorities are appealing for help identifying the man in the CCTV image.

Anyone with information should email [Dan.Hardey@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Dan.Hardey@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101, quoting reference 12260125981.

Crimestoppers can also be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111.
