A FISH and chip shop has opened in a prominent spot in Bradford city centre.

City Fisheries is now operating out of the former In Plaice unit on Sunbridge Road.

Earlier this month, signs appeared in the window saying it was "opening soon".

When the T&A passed the chippy, it was open for business - with a "mega" buy one get one free offer available.

## Chippy quickly snapped up

Previously owned by Laura Hollingsworth and Kieran Osborne, Sunbridge Friary opened last November after extensive refurbishment of the site.

However, it emerged this summer that the leasehold was put on the market.

It was then quickly snapped up.

Ernest Wilson estate agents confirmed the sale and said the new owner was in the process of getting the business ready to open.

## Turnover projected to be between £7,000 and £8,000 per week

The business was listed for sale at £29,950 due to the owners’ other commitments in Sheffield, with no issues reported about its performance.

On Rightmove, the shop is described as being in a "bustling location" with a "strong footfall" throughout the year and is said to have built a "solid reputation" locally for its quality food.

It adds that there is an eat-in area with seating for up to 22 customers, a "spacious" serving counter, and a large basement for food preparation and storage.

The business was reported to have a weekly turnover of around £6,000, with higher earnings expected during the summer months.

Turnover was projected to rise to between £7,000 and £8,000 per week during busier periods.
