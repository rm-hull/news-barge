A CHARITY concert will help raise funds for a cause supporting people with sight loss.

The sixth Grand Charity Show, organised by the Bradford Accordion Band, will take place at Bingley Arts Centre on Saturday, October 3.

This year’s event is themed All Creatures Great and Smallish and aims to raise funds for Sight Airedale, a charity supporting visually impaired people across the Aire Valley.

A spokesperson for the Bradford Accordion Band said: "We would be incredibly grateful for any coverage you can provide to help us pack out the theatre for this wonderful local cause."

The band has raised more than £40,000 for charity through past events and hopes to sell all 300 tickets this year to help refurbish Sight Airedale’s meeting room.

The refurbishment will allow the charity to host new events and workshops for the visually impaired across the Aire Valley.

Tickets cost £15 and can be booked online or by calling 01274 567983.
