A man accused of arson has been acquitted after prosecutors offered no evidence against him.

Malcolm Stewart was due to stand trial at Teesside Crown Court before prosecutors dropped the charge.

The 37-year-old was accused of arson with intent to endanger life after a fire at a storage unit in Stockton on March 30 this year.

Judge Nathan Adams said: “The prosecution has offered no evidence in this case and not guilty will be entered against the charge.”

Mr Stewart, of Eddison Way, Middlesbrough, is still facing unrelated charges, the court heard.
