The Northern Echo is once again on the hunt for the region’s finest beauty businesses as it launches its Best Beauty Salon Award 2026 competition.

From luxury facials and expert skincare treatments to nail artistry, beauty enhancements and pampering experiences, salons across County Durham and Darlington play a vital role in helping people look and feel their best.

Now, The Northern Echo is inviting readers to nominate the beauty salon they believe deserves the title of Best Beauty Salon 2026.

Whether it is a long-established business with a loyal client base or a rising star making a name for itself, readers are being encouraged to put forward the salons that consistently deliver outstanding service and results.

Nominations are open now and will remain open until Sunday, September 20.

Once all nominations have been received, the ten salons with the strongest support will be selected as finalists on Monday, September 21.

  The Best Beauty Salon award is back. Vote for your favourite. 

*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

The competition will then move into the voting stage, with readers able to back their favourite finalist using voting slips published in The Northern Echo between Monday, September 28 and Saturday, October 10.

Last year's winner, Becki C's Nails, Beauty and Aesthetics, based in Darlington, said staff had "a real boost of confidence" after picking up the gong.


Northern Echo editor Gavin Foster said: “We know we have some fantastic salons out there across Darlington and County Durham.

“That is why we want to recognise and celebrate the fabulous work of beauty salons across our cities, towns and villages, and the people behind them who often work their hardest to make people look incredible.

“We are delighted to launch our search for the Best Beauty Salon, with nominations now open.

“The Northern Echo will be bringing you updates on those shortlisted in our competition, so keep an eye out to see if your favourite is featured and for more information on how to vote.”
