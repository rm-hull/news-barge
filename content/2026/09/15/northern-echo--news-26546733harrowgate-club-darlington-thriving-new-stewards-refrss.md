A Darlington social club is bucking the trend at a time when many community venues face an uncertain future, according to its stewards.

Harrowgate Club, on Salters Lane and first opened in 1907, has been under the stewardship of Paul and Michelle Gill since spring 2025.

Mr Gill said: "The club is going from strength to strength.

  Harrowgate Club, Darlington 

*(Image: Supplied by Goffy Media)*

"There’s a great team spirit around the place and everyone is pulling together.

"We’ve introduced new ideas and people have really got behind them."

He has introduced a range of new activities including car boot sales, expanded indoor sports, big-screen TVs, high-profile events, and regular entertainment featuring top talent from the clubland circuit.

Earlier this year, the club’s future was called into question following rumours that the building could be demolished and replaced with a smaller development.

  Gary Ryan 

*(Image: Supplied by Goffy Media)*

Mr Gill said: "They wanted the club to stay exactly as it is.

"This club has survived two world wars, Covid, an ever-changing world and it’s packed with heritage and lifelong emotional attachments.

"There are generations of memories here and people wanted to see that protected."

With the building’s future now secure, the focus has shifted to further growth.

  Legendary comedian Mick Miller at the club last year with his friend - Durham born comic Waler Gee 

*(Image: Supplied by Goffy Media)*

One of the biggest upcoming events is a pair of Neil Diamond Celebration Christmas Party Nights on December 12 and 19, headlined by acclaimed entertainer Gary Ryan.

Mr Gill said: "People couldn’t believe we’d got Gary.

"Some of our members and regulars have paid top theatre prices to see him perform, so to bring him here for two big Christmas celebration nights is a real coup for the club.

"The difference is that people won’t be paying theatre prices at Harrowgate.

"They’ll get a fantastic night out, Gary’s brilliant Neil Diamond Celebration show, a hot supper and a disco as well.

"They’re already proving hugely popular and there’s a real buzz around them."

Gary Ryan is currently packing theatres across the UK with his Neil Diamond Celebration production and has even sold out performances at London’s famous Adelphi Theatre in the West End.

Mr Gill said: "When you consider Gary is currently packing theatres across the country and has performed to huge audiences in London’s West End, it shows just what a coup this is for our club."

Last year, the club attracted comedian Mick Miller, who performed just weeks before appearing in the Royal Variety Performance in front of the Royal Family.

Mr Gill said: "We were able to attract the legendary comedian Mick Miller to perform on our stage here last year, just weeks before he appeared in front of the Royal Family in the televised Royal Variety Performance and it made a real statement for us – Mick loved it too."

He believes the club’s blend of community spirit, heritage, and high-quality entertainment is central to its ongoing success.

Mr Gill said: "We’re proud of the club’s history, but we’re also looking to the future.

"And with the support we’re getting, there’s every reason to be optimistic.

"Next year marks an incredible 120 year anniversary with so much to celebrate.
