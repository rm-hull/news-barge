### Hallowscream

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/hallowscream-york-returns.jpg)

Winner of Best UK Scream Park, Hallowscream at York Maze is a terrifyingly entertaining night of fear and fun with 5 separate live-action haunted house attractions and over 100 actors. But be warned the frights aren’t limited to the haunted houses, roving characters and live stage shows add to the atmosphere. The central entertainment zone is all undercover where you can get a drink from the bar while you watch the stage performances, browse the amazing range of merchandise in the Halloween MERCH Stand including Hallowscream branded souvenirs. The Fodder Barn offers a delicious range of street food including burgers, loaded fries, pizza & sweet treats.

- Elvington Lane, York, YO19 5LT
- Fri 9 Oct – Sun 1 Nov
- 6:30-11:30pm
- More info

### Mad Alice’s Ghost Train

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/mad-alice-2-of-6.webp)

Come and join Mad Alice, if you dare. Step aboard the North Yorkshire Moors Railway after dark for an immersive Halloween experience like no other.

Named after York’s most infamous ghost, the Mad Alice Ghost Train carries you on a live theatrical journey across the moonlit night, with costumed storytellers, atmospheric entertainment and a complimentary York Gin drink to steady your nerves.

But hurry, Mad Alice’s presence is for three evenings only, 18 October, 25 October and 1 November, with two services departing each night.

- North Yorkshire Moors Railway, Pickering Station, Pickering, YO18 7AJ
- Sun 18, Sun 25 Oct & Sun 1 Nov
- 6:15pm & 8pm
- More info
