SMASH hit show Operation Mincemeat is returning to Bradford, due to "phenomenal demand".

Following 290,000 tickets sold and more than 20 weeks of sold-out shows so far, the Tony and double Olivier Award-winning musical has announced an extension to the UK leg of its world tour.

Along the way, the tour has welcomed Joseph Kavanaugh, who has now seen the production more than 100 times after winning one of five Golden Tickets issued to celebrate Operation Mincemeat selling its one millionth ticket earlier this year, giving him free access to the musical anytime, anywhere in the world, for life.

The musical is set in 1943 and Britain is losing the war. Luckily, we’re about to gamble all our futures on a stolen corpse. Operation Mincemeat is the fast-paced, hilarious and true story of the twisted secret mission that won the Allies World War II. The question is, how did a well-dressed corpse wrong-foot Hitler?

Operation Mincemeat began as a tiny production in London in 2019. It quickly gained a devoted following and premiered in the West End in 2023.

Written by Triple Tony nominees SpitLip (David Cumming, Felix Hagan, Natasha Hodgson and Zoë Roberts), the show has been nominated for 68 awards and won 24, including four for Best Musical.
