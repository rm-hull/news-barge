A MAN has been rushed to hospital following a crash involving a motorbike and a car.

Police were called to the scene of the collision on Highmoor Lane in Brighouse just after 8pm last night (Monday, September 14).

The motorbike rider was taken to hospital with non life-threatening injuries, the force has confirmed.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At 8.02pm last night officers were called to a report of a motorbike and car in collision on Highmoor Lane, Brighouse.

"The rider of the motorbike. a man was taken to hospital with non life threatening injuries.

"Enquiries are ongoing by the Roads Policing Unit.

"The collision happened at 8.02pm last night on Highmoor Lane in Brighouse.
