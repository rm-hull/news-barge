A NEW doodle map mural has brought Bradford to life on the walls of a primary school.

The hand-drawn mural, created by freelance illustrator and mural artist Dave Draws, now decorates the Key Stage 2 corridor at Margaret McMillan Primary School and celebrates Bradford and its surrounding areas.

It is Mr Draws’ first time creating a map of Bradford and features notable landmarks in Bradford, Shipley, and Saltaire.

Sarah Hodge, co-headteacher at Margaret McMillan Primary School, said: "The project has captured the imagination of pupils, who were invited to suggest famous landmarks and locations they would like to see included.

"Saltaire was a particular favourite, as one of our school's houses is named after the UNESCO World Heritage Site.

"The mural forms part of our commitment to inspiring creativity through our curriculum.

"Pupils in Key Stage 2 study street art and contemporary art, and we wanted to bring these subjects to life through an inspiring and engaging piece of artwork."

Created freehand over three days, the mural serves as both a source of artistic inspiration and a reminder of Bradford’s rich history, culture, and heritage.

Dave Draws will continue working with the Priestley Academy Trust, creating murals at Atlas Community Primary School and Green Lane Primary School.
