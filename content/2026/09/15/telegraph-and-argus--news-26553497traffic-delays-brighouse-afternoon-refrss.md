A crash on a busy Brighouse roundabout prompted an emergency response on Tuesday afternoon, with traffic delays reported nearby.

Emergency services were called to the Halifax Road roundabout, near the junction with the A6025, at 1.49pm on Tuesday, September 15.

An ambulance and a team leader attended the scene, where one person was assessed. No serious injuries were reported, and the patient did not need to go to hospital.

Photos shared on social media appeared to show a vehicle had hit railings near the roundabout.

West Yorkshire Police confirmed the incident was a damage-only road traffic collision.

## Statement in full

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 1.49pm on Tuesday afternoon to report a collision on the Halifax Road roundabout close to the junction of the A6025 in Brighouse.

"An ambulance and a team leader were dispatched to the scene and one patient was checked over but did not need to be conveyed to hospital."
