A County Durham dealer was caught out when police found 31kg of cannabis in a holdall and two shopping bags.

Corbyn Farn, 32, received a 27-month sentence after admitting two charges relating to the supply of cannabis in Seaham, in December 2022.

As he was serving a suspended sentence for similar offending at the time, nine months were activated, taking his total prison term to 36 months.

Farn admitted possessing cannabis with intent to supply between December 1 and 31, 2022, at a plea hearing at the court on August 5 this year.

He also admitted being concerned in the supply of cannabis, on December 31, 2022.

Prosecutor Cainan Lonsdale said Farn was not charged until May this year as police were seeking and awaiting charging advice from the Crown Prosecution Service.

Mr Lonsdale said when Farn was arrested over an unrelated matter in December 2022 he was found with more than 31kg of cannabis in a holdall and two shopping bags.

Police also recovered scales, snap bags, a mobile phone and cannabis growing equipment.

The cannabis was estimated to be worth between £10,000 and £12,500 on the street.

Smaller amounts of cannabis were also found at a second address linked to Farn.

Messages on his phone suggested he was selling cannabis directly to “customers”.

Tony Davis, in mitigation, said Farn was under post-release licence supervision and wearing “a tag”, which was due to run until October, but he was recalled to prison after his guilty pleas last month.

Judge Amanda Rippon said Farn had been selling cannabis directly to users when he received the suspended sentence in February 2022.

Imposing the total 36-month sentence, Judge Rippon said Farn soon returned to similar drug activity despite the chance offered by the February 2022 suspended sentence, breaching it well within the two-year period.

The judge also ordered the forfeiture of the mobile phone, cannabis and other equipment seized when Farn was arrested in December 2022.

His earlier case, in February 2022, followed admissions to possessing cannabis with intent to supply and possessing £6,500 in criminal property, for which he received a ten-month sentence, suspended for two years.
