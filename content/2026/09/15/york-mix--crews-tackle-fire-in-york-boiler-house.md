Two crews were called to a fire in York last night (Monday).

Firefighters from York and Acomb responded to smoke issuing out of a boiler house on **James Street** at**11.18pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews deployed two breathing apparatus, a hose reel jet and thermal imaging camera to investigate.

#### James Street, York

“Once the fire was located crews used a main jet to extinguish and a positive pressure ventilation fan to clear the smoke.”
