Two people have been arrested as part of an ongoing investigation into reports of historic child sexual abuse at a former Bradford children's home.

A 63-year-old man and a 60-year-old woman were arrested at addresses in Bradford and Manchester on September 10.

Both have since been released on bail while enquiries continue.

The investigation follows reports made by five adults, two men and three women, who allege they were sexually abused while living at a Bradford Council-run care home during the 1980s and 1990s.

The alleged victims were aged between seven and 16 at the time of the reported offences.

The home, formerly known as Saxton Avenue Children's Home on Saxton Avenue, was renamed The Acres in 2014.

The facility has since closed and is no longer in operation.

## Arrests part of ongoing historic investigation efforts

Temporary Detective Chief Inspector Chantel Markham said the arrests were part of an ongoing effort to investigate allegations of child sexual abuse, regardless of when they are reported to have taken place.

She said: “These arrests form part of our ongoing commitment to investigate both current and non-recent sexual offences against children.

“Safeguarding and protecting children is a top priority for us in West Yorkshire.

“We would urge anyone who has been a victim of sexual abuse, no matter where or when it took place, to report it to the police. You will be listened to and supported throughout the investigation.”

Police are appealing for anyone with information about incidents alleged to have taken place at the home during the 1980s and 1990s to come forward.

Anyone who can assist the investigation is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police via 101 or online, quoting reference 13260513294.

Support is available for people affected by sexual abuse through specialist services and trained officers.
