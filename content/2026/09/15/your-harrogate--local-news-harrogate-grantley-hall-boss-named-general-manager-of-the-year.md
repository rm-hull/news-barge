Grantley Hall’s General Manager has won a national hospitality award, three years after taking charge of the Ripon hotel.

Nuno César de Sá was named General Manager of the Year at the Boutique Hotelier Awards 2026, held in London on yesterday (Monday).

He was one of ten finalists for the award, which recognises leaders in the hotel industry.

It was a double win for Grantley Hall, with its Norton Bar also named Best Hotel Bar.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aa91ad2aa3ed54280a.jpg)


The bar is one of several places to eat and drink at the hotel, which opened in 2019 following the restoration of the historic building.

A spokesperson for Grantley Hall said:

“Thank you to everyone who voted – it means so much to us.”
