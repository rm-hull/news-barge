Morrisons has launched a new chocolate snack that shoppers are comparing to a Cadbury favourite discontinued 16 years ago.

The Bradford-based supermarket giant has launched Chocolate Curves (100g), available in both Milk Chocolate and Orange flavours.

The new Morrisons snack is available now, in-store and online, for £2.


## Morrisons' new Chocolate Curves likened to discontinued Cadbury favourite

Morrisons' new Chocolate Curves have already drawn comparisons to Cadbury Snaps, which were discontinued in 2010 after launching in 2004.

## Rare Cadbury chocolate bar returns to UK supermarkets

Meanwhile, a rare seasonal Cadbury chocolate bar has returned to supermarkets across the UK.

The confectionery company confirmed in 2024 that it had discontinued its Dairy Milk Mint Crisp chocolate bar, with "no plans" to bring it back.
