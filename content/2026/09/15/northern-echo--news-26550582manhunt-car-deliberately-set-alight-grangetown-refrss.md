Police have launched a manhunt after an upside-down vehicle was set alight in Grangetown and the occupants of the vehicle fled the scene.

Emergency services were called to the A1053 on Greystones Road just after midnight on Monday (September 14), following reports of an overturned vehicle.

Cleveland Police say the vehicle was alight, with fire crews confirming that it had been done deliberately.

Officers from the police force searched the area for the occupants of the vehicle using police drone, but they had fled the scene.

An investigation into the incident is now ongoing.

T/Superintendent Daryll Tomlinson, Redcar and Cleveland’s District Commander, said: “Shortly after midnight on Monday, September 14, police received a report of a vehicle upside down on A1053 on Greystone Road.

"The vehicle was set alight. Officers conducted a search to locate the occupants using the police drone; however, they are believed to have fled the area. Enquiries are ongoing.”

In the incident, the car involved was destroyed.

One fire engine from Grangetown attended the incident.

Firefighters used a hose to tackle the blaze.

The brigade said the vehicle was “well alight” and suffered 100 per cent damage.

A Cleveland Fire Brigade spokesperson said: “Cleveland Fire Brigade were called to an incident at Greystones Road, Grangetown.

“One fire appliance in attendance from Grangetown. Breathing apparatus and one hose reel jet used. One car well alight, 100 per cent damaged.

“The cause is believed to be deliberate.

"The stop call was received at 2.36am."
