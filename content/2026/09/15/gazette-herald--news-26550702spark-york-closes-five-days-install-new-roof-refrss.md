AN independent venue in York has closed for five days for a new roof to be installed ready for autumn and winter.

Spark York shut on Monday and will remain closed until Friday, September 18 while a watertight canopy cover is put up to protect its central courtyard from the elements.

  File picture when Spark York previously installed its roof for autumn and winter. 

*(Image: SPARK)*

The community venue, on Piccadilly, is due to reopen at 5pm on Friday with a Raise the Roof Party, with further details yet to be revealed.

  Spark York's central space is open to the elements in the warmer weather. 

*(Image: Nadia Jefferson-Brown)*

Spark traditionally removes its canopy during the summer, opening up its central space for the warmer weather. The roof is then replaced with a weather-proof covering to create a snug retreat ahead of the colder autumn and winter months.

The venue is home to independent food traders and bars, and hosts events and activities such as art workshops, fitness classes and exhibitions.

  Spark York in Piccadilly, York. 

*(Image: Gabriel Macarthur)*

  One of the first events after the reopening will be the *Growing Together: Mother Daughter Wellness Workshop* on Friday, September 18.

The event is billed as an empowering evening for teenage girls and their mothers, focusing on body awareness, confidence and support while growing up.

The programme includes hormone and menstrual-cycle education, a discussion about hormones and contraception, and a session on exercise and the body, including a mini-workout.

There will also be advice on hormone-friendly nutrition from Abby of AF Nutrition, breathwork and nervous-system regulation with Beth from Be So Therapy, and a motivational talk with Sarah, from Creating Balance with Sarah Pagan.

The evening will finish with a journal-crafting activity designed to help participants reflect, set goals and develop healthy habits. Attendees will also receive a free goody bag.

  Spark York venue closes for winter roof to be installed - pictured earlier this year without its canopy cover. 

*(Image: Nadia Jefferson-Brown)*

Also on the programme is a shell-painting watercolour workshop by Inky Print Designs on Sunday, September 20.

The relaxed, adult-friendly session will guide participants through observational drawing, colour mixing and layering, with support from qualified art teacher Sarah.

Suitable for beginners and experienced painters, the workshop includes all materials and a frame, allowing attendees to take home a finished shell painting ready to display.
