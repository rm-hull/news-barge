A investigation has been launched after a cyclist suffered arm and foot injuries after being knocked off his bike in a crash.

The incident happened at about 9.25pm on Saturday, September 12, on Whitby Close near the junction with Byland Road in Skelton, East Cleveland.

A 41-year-old man was riding a bicycle when the crash happened involving a dark-coloured vehicle.

Officers believe the vehicle travelled along Jervaulx Road twice before heading towards Byland Road.

The cyclist suffered injuries to his arm and foot.

Police are now appealing for witnesses or anyone with footage that could assist their investigation.

Police officer Webster, from Redcar and Cleveland's neighbourhood policing team, said: “I am keen to speak to anyone who may have witnessed the collision or have dash cam or doorbell footage that could help the investigation.

“Please call 101 or visit the website quoting reference number 183747.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact police, quoting reference number 183747.
