A Yorkshire market town has been named one of the friendliest towns to retire to in England.

World Atlas has released its list of the friendliest places to retire in England, with "busy community centres, volunteer-run museums, and fun festivals hungry for helpers".

The list features towns that "make it easy for retiree residents to fit right in" from coastal places to areas packed with history.

Beverley in East Yorkshire made it onto the list and might be worth checking out if you are looking for somewhere peaceful to enjoy your extra time off.


## What is Beverley like?

On the town, World Atlas said: "The two towers visible for miles across the East Riding belong to Beverley Minster, a parish church larger than some English cathedrals.

"Volunteers lead guided tours of the church, built between 1220 and 1425, and along the way provide a deep dive into its fascinating history.

"While regular tours are free, if you're up for it, book the 113-step roof tour, too.

"If live theatrical productions are your thing, head to the East Riding Theatre (ERT) near the Beverley Guildhall museum.

"Set in an old red brick Baptist chapel from 1910, the 192-seat ERT offers volunteer opportunities and a great café.

"In addition to its drama and comedy performances, live blues, jazz, and other music genres are also featured."

The town has a long and rich history, with the streets lined with Georgian buildings and plenty of things to do.

The site mentioned how people love Beverley Memorial Hall and described it as a "friendly place" for retirees to go "when looking for stuff to do".

The hall offers lots of activities from bowls and tai chi to line dancing, coffee mornings, and its own art group.

The town also has a market with stalls lining the market square each week and a small market on Wednesday.


The market has spots to get a great coffee and a pastry, with World Atlas recommending Gemini Chocolate for its organic vegan brownies.

The town is also lucky to have plenty of restaurants and cafés to try out, from Italian options to Thai food.

The Kings Head could be worth a visit with expertly crafted cask ales and world lagers on offer as well as burgers and pub classics on its food menu.

If you are looking to buy a property in Beverley, the average price of a house over the last year was £266,024, according to Rightmove.
