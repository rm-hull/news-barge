**HARROGATE** — Regional cultural leads and international publishing houses have descended on the district following the official program launch for the UK’s premier independent graphic art celebration.

The award-winning **Thought Bubble Comic Art Festival** has formally shared its full statutory itinerary for its landmark 20th anniversary edition. The week-long regional celebration will commence across select multi-agency venues on**Monday 9 November 2026**, leading into the main central Thought Bubble Comic Convention hosted at the Harrogate Convention Centre on** Saturday 14 November and Sunday 15 November 2026**.

Originally established in 2007 as a grassroots one-day exhibition in a Leeds basement by independent advocates Lisa Wood (Tula Lotay) and Nabil Homsi, the non-profit venture has evolved into a cornerstone of the global publishing calendar. Operating in direct partnership with children’s charity Barnardo’s, the 2026 anniversary infrastructure is projected to host over **600 independent comic-creating exhibitors** alongside thousands of international graphic arts enthusiasts.

## Global Roster Additions and Exclusive Industry Launches

The anniversary convention roster has been expanded to include several prominent industry figures. Headline additions include veteran comic artist **Charlie Adlard**, the illustrative force behind the global multi-media phenomenon * The Walking Dead*, alongside New York Times bestselling cartoonist**Jeffrey Brown**, known for his prominent lucasfilm-licensed properties including* Star Wars: Darth Vader and Son*.

The 2026 scheduling also features major commercial publishing launches and specialized community tracks distributed across the district:

- **Cosmic Lighthouse Launch (Friday 13 November):** An exclusive first-look showcase at Everyman Harrogate debuting forthcoming graphic novels, featuring book signings with prominent science-fiction author Adrian Tchaikovsky.
- **Yorkshire Creator Showcase (Wednesday 11 November):** A dedicated public presentation led by regional non-fiction graphic novel pioneers Kristyna Baczynski and Darryl Cunningham focusing on biographical storytelling.
- **Free Comic Day (Friday 13 November):** A coordinated literacy outreach operation in partnership with Diamond Comics UK to distribute thousands of free, all-ages graphic novels to young readers through regional library networks.
- **The Comics Forum (Thursday 12 & Friday 13 November):** An internationally recognized academic convention bringing expert panel speakers together to debate this year’s core structural focus: welfare.

**Martha Julian, Thought Bubble Festival Director:**

For our twentieth festival, we wanted to nod to the past with some classic festival features that people know and love, while keeping things fresh with exciting launch events and new guest talks. Similarly with this year’s convention guest list, you can expect to see fan favourites sitting alongside the emerging creators who we are confident will be the fan favourites of the future!



## Ticket Infrastructure and Central Access Gateways

Organisers have confirmed that the vast majority of weekday festival events remain completely free to the public. Admission passes for the main weekend convention terminal in Harrogate are actively on sale, with municipal coordinators maintaining strict free-access quotas for all children under the age of 12, registered carers, and senior citizens over the age of 65.

Local independent artists looking to review portfolio submission spaces, residents seeking volunteer orientation logs, or families looking to pre-register for free admissions passes can access the unified booking grid directly at Thought Bubble Festival.
