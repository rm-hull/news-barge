# Watch: Helicopter hovers feet from cliff face during rescue

The recently released footage shows a helicopter hovering mere feet from a cliff face in the Sierra Nevada mountains in the US state of California while a rescue team secures an injured climber. The team successfully lifted the hiker off the cliff and transported them to an awaiting ambulance.
