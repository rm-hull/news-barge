**HARROGATE** — Regional transit chiefs have initiated a coordinated security expansion across the town center, linking municipal transport grids with local police networks to combat escalating anti-social behaviour and urban retail crime.

The **Harrogate Bus Company** has officially integrated its frontline fleet and terminal management operations into the Harrogate Business Improvement District’s (BID) secure digital infrastructure network. Formally launched on**Tuesday 15 September 2026**, the partnership deploys the specialized** ShopSafe Alert app** across localized transit systems, creating a unified communication bridge connecting public transport workers directly with over 30 retail hubs and law enforcement teams operating across the commercial core.

The strategic expansion targets the business community’s long-term objective of securing official **Purple Flag accreditation**. The internationally recognized standard mandates strict coordination benchmarks between local authorities, transit operators, private security groups, and police units to prove a night-time economy is safe, diverse, and tightly managed. The town centre transit infrastructure will now feed real-time crime logging data back to central business crime officers to intercept repeat offenders and track emerging vandalism trends.

## Advanced Surveillance and Body-Camera Deployment

The tech integration supplements the transport operator’s existing multi-layered security grid. The Harrogate Bus Company remains an active stakeholder within localized Shop Watch and Pub Watch radio systems, while directly interfacing with North Yorkshire Council’s centralized town CCTV and security control rooms.

To further secure vehicles and transit terminals, the operator has deployed a comprehensive surveillance upgrade across its regional assets:

- **Fleet Security:** High-definition closed-circuit television (CCTV) arrays pre-installed inside all active single- and double-decker vehicles, tracking all routes including the flagship 24, 21, and 36 lines.
- **Terminal Monitoring:** Vetted camera grids covering the main central Harrogate Bus Station terminals.
- **Frontline Enforcement:** The immediate rollout of secure body-worn video cameras to active Duty Managers patrolling regional passenger loading zones.

**Kel Pizzuti, Transdev Head of Operations:** “The safety and security of our customers and driver colleagues will always remain a top priority for us here at the Harrogate Bus Company – so the decision to join a network that drives down criminal and anti-social behaviour was an easy one. ShopSafe will help keep participating businesses informed with what’s happening in the local area and around the district.”


## Strategic Business Crime Partnerships

The operational rollout is directed by the regional Business Crime Reduction Partnership (BCRP), which seeks to bridge private sector corporate intelligence directly into statutory policing resources to lower incident resolution windows.


**Paul Evans, Harrogate BID Business Crime Officer:**

Keeping Harrogate safe is a shared responsibility, and effective partnership working is an important part of that. The ShopSafe app gives businesses a straightforward way to share information about incidents and concerns, helping us better understand what is happening across the town. Working with transport operators is particularly valuable because they have an important role to play in the experience of people travelling into, around and out of Harrogate.
