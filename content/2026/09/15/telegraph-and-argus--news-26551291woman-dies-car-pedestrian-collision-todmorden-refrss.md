A WOMAN has died following a collision involving a car and a pedestrian.

The incident happened on Kebs Road in Todmorden today at around 5.40am.

West Yorkshire Police confirmed that the 52-year-old woman was pronounced dead at the scene.

A 68-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

A police spokesperson said: "Anyone who witnessed the collision, including anyone who may have dashcam or video footage of the incident, is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or online HERE quoting reference 232 15/09."

A scene remains in place while enquiries are ongoing.
