Two local tennis players have raised £3,035 for Sue Ryder Manorlands Hospice after a 12-hour tennis marathon.

Adrian Firth and Matti Dent completed the dawn-to-dusk challenge at Riddlesden Tennis Club on Saturday, September 12. It was Mr Firth’s third consecutive fundraising marathon.

### Supporters turn out despite wet weather

Players and supporters attended throughout the day, despite wet weather. The event grew from the club’s earlier fundraising work for Manorlands.

When asked how he felt after finishing Mr Firth said: “The simple answer is exhausted and aching.”

  Adrian Firth and Matti Dent after finishing the event 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

He said the weather had raised concerns about turnout.

Mr Firth said: “Given the weather I didn't think there would be many people who would come and play, but thankfully they did and their support was amazing.”

The pair found the middle of the afternoon hardest.

  Adrian Firth and Matti Dent at the start of the day 

*(Image: Newsquest)*

### Community rallies behind fundraiser

Mr Firth said: “The support of fellow members kept us going throughout the day.”

Mr Dent said everyone involved had helped the marathon succeed.

Mr Dent said: “First and foremost we would like to thank everyone who participated on the day, whether it be playing or just helping out along the way. They all definitely kept us both going, however, it has taken its toll on our legs.”

The money will go towards Manorlands’ work supporting people in need. Mr Dent said the hospice was close to his heart after his brother died from cancer three years ago.
