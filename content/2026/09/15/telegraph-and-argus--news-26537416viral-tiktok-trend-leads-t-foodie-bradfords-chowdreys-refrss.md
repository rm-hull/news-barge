A viral TikTok trend led me to a Bradford restaurant I'd never visited before, and I wasn't disappointed.

After searching "Lunch in My City Bradford", the first result that appeared was Chowdreys on Great Horton Road.

With a menu packed with curries, grills, pakoras and chaat, it seemed like the perfect place to put the trend to the test.

## 
  **Discovering somewhere new**

As a self-confessed foodie, I'm always on the lookout for somewhere different to try.

What made this challenge even more exciting was that I'd never heard of Chowdreys before. The menu looked promising too, with everything from curries and chaat to pakoras, wings and mixed grills.

There were some surprisingly affordable meal deals on offer as well, including one featuring a kebab, fries and a drink for £8.95.

  The naanwich and fries served at Chowdreys on Great Horton Road as part of the viral "Lunch in My City" TikTok trend. 

*(Image: T&A)*

After eventually finding somewhere to park, we made our way inside, and I must say the first thing we noticed wasn't the menu or the décor.

It was the smell, the sweet flavour of rich delicate spices and grilled meet that filled the restaurant from the moment we stepped through the door.

## A naanwich worth talking about

Eventually, we settled on the naanwich, which was their big viral food. In a wrap, it's essentially a mixed grill wrapped in naan bread, it comes with the choice of a plain or garlic naan and is served alongside fries and sauces.

When the food arrived, I was genuinely surprised by the portion size. For less than £10, the plate was packed, with two naanwiches each.

The naanwich itself was loaded with meat, while the accompanying fries and selection of aiolis and sauces made it feel like exceptional value for money.

At a time when prices seem to be rising everywhere, it was refreshing to find a meal that felt generous without breaking the bank.

  My dining companion trying the viral "Lunch in My City" trend at Chowdreys on Great Horton Road. 

*(Image: T&A)*

The meat was packed with flavour and the spices came through in every bite. What really brought the dish together, though, was the naan.

Had it simply been a pile of grilled meat, the meal might have felt quite heavy. Instead, the light, fluffy naan balanced everything perfectly.

## Final verdict

One of the best things about the "Lunch in My City" trend is that it encourages people to try places they might otherwise overlook.

Without it, I probably wouldn't have discovered Chowdreys, and I'll definitely be back.

If you're looking for a filling lunch, generous portions and plenty of flavour, this Great Horton Road restaurant is well worth a visit.
