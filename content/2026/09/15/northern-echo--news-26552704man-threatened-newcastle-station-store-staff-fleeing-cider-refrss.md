A man has been jailed for threatening railway station shop workers with a shard of glass to enable him to steal four cans of cider.

Liam Ellison received a prison sentence totalling three years and 10 months at Newcastle Crown Court, having previously pleaded guilty to robbery and possessing an offensive weapon.

British Transport Police (BTP) launched an immediate response after the incident was reported at Sainsbury's in Newcastle Central Station on Monday, June 29 this year.

The court heard Ellison entered the store and made his way to the alcohol aisle.

Shop security recognised him and watched as he browsed the aisle before picking up a pack of beer.

Staff confronted him, and he dropped the pack, smashing it and scattering alcohol and glass across the floor.

Ellison grew aggressive and waved a sharp metal tent peg at staff.

He then picked up a shard of glass and threatened to stab the security workers unless they moved.

Fearing he would seriously harm them, staff let him leave with four cans of cider and immediately contacted BTP.

Officers found Ellison in the city centre within 20 minutes and detained him, recovering the metal tent peg and stolen cider.

The 36-year-old offender, of Ladykirk Road, Elswick, Newcastle, was arrested and taken into custody.

Despite being shown CCTV footage of him threatening staff and stealing the alcohol, Ellison denied the offences and showed no remorse.

BTP Constable Arron Peacock said: "Liam Ellison acted disgracefully, threatening staff simply doing their job just to steal some cider.

"When challenged, he upped the ante by grabbing a shard of broken glass and threatening to stab them.

"We take all reports of robbery and violence extremely seriously, including threats made against staff working across the rail network.

"Thanks to the staff’s body worn cameras and the quick actions of our officers, we were able to quickly arrest Ellison and secure a conviction.

"I urge anyone who witnesses or falls victim to a robbery or violent threats on the railway network to report it by texting 61016.

"As in this case, our officers can quickly deploy and make arrests to keep the railway, and all those who work on it, safe."

In 2022, Ellison was reported to have 167 arrests and 50 convictions, and was given a three-year Criminal Behaviour Order banning him from parts of Sunderland.
