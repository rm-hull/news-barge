Two thugs who forced a man off his £6,000 e-bike have been jailed for robbery.

Callum Briggs and Joshua Peters blocked the victim’s path as he rode through Middlesbrough, then asked him if he sold drugs.

The pair then forced the man off his bike on a bridge over the A66 before fleeing, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Shaun Dryden said Briggs grabbed the bike’s brake, sending the victim over the handlebars and leaving him injured.

He said the man activated the bike’s security devices, setting off an alarm and deflating a tyre.

“At this point, Briggs said – ‘a flat tyre isn’t going to bother me’ – while Peters had his hand clenched in a fist behind his back and the victim thought he was going to be assaulted,” he said.

“He walked past the defendants to the other side of the bridge and called the police.”

Mr Dryden said police arrested both men and recovered the bike.

Briggs, 20, of Aislaby Court, Guisborough, and Peters, 25, of Bolckow Road, Grangetown, both admitted the June 13 robbery.

  Joshua Peters


Briggs’ lawyer, Lee Goodchild, said his behaviour changed after he moved in with his father and resumed offending to fund his crack cocaine addiction.

  Callum Briggs


Tom Bennett, representing Peters, said his client had a history of drug addiction which drove his offending that day.

Judge Nathan Adams jailed Briggs for two years in a young offenders’ institute and Peters for 30 months.

“You both took the opportunity to rob him and steal the bike which you could see was of some value.

“You blocked his path and grabbed hold of his handlebars with enough force to throw him off his bike causing him minor injuries,” he said.

“This must have been quite a scary incident for him, with the two of you ganging up on him out of public sight.”

Briggs had also received a 51-week sentence for 11 further shop thefts after breaching a community order.
