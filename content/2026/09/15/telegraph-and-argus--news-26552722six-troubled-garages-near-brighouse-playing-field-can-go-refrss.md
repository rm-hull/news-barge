A SOCIAL housing provider can knock down a row of troubled garages near a playing field on an estate.

Calderdale Council planners ruled Together Housing dies not need prior approval to demolish the six garages, which are next to the Thornton Road Recreation Ground at Rastrick.

Planning officers heard the garages were empty and had become something of a trouble spot.

A supporting statement with the application says: “The garages are currently unused and subject to anti-social behaviour.

“Their removal will improve natural surveillance of Thornton Recreation Ground and contribute positively to the surrounding residential environment.”

In their report, planning officers said the garages are positioned within an established residential area and are surrounded by housing, public open space and estate roads.

  “The garages are currently unused and have become susceptible to anti-social

  behaviour.

“Together Housing proposes the demolition of all six garages together with associated slabs and foundations where necessary.

“Following demolition, the land would be cleared, levelled, resurfaced and left in a safe and tidy condition,” they noted.

The applicant’s supporting statement said a method statement set out the proposed demolition sequence, site establishment, environmental controls, working hours, dust suppression measures, waste management, asbestos procedures and site restoration proposals.

The works will be undertaken in accordance with all relevant health and safety legislation and current best practice.

“Any asbestos identified through the pre-demolition survey will be managed and removed in accordance with the contractor’s separate asbestos risk assessment and method statement (RAMS) prior to demolition.

“Following demolition, the land will be cleared, levelled, resurfaced and left in a safe and tidy condition.

“The removal of the garages will improve visibility and reduce opportunities for anti-social behaviour within the estate, contributing positively to the residential environment,” according to the statement.
