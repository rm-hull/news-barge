The Telegraph & Argus is once again searching for the Bradford district’s finest beauty businesses as it launches its Best Beauty Salon Award 2026 competition.

From luxury facials and specialist skincare treatments to nail artistry, beauty enhancements and relaxing pampering experiences, salons across Bradford and the surrounding areas help customers look and feel their best.

Now, Telegraph & Argus readers are being invited to nominate the beauty salon they believe deserves to be named Best Beauty Salon 2026.

Whether it is a well-established business with a loyal customer base or a newer salon making its mark locally, readers are encouraged to put forward the businesses that consistently provide excellent service and impressive results.

  Last year's winners celebrating. Mum Donna Perkins (centre) with her daughters Emma (left) and Chloe 

*(Image: Telegraph & Argus)*

Last year’s award showed just how much the competition can mean to a local business.

The Perks of Beauty, a family-run salon on Commercial Street in Shipley, had been open for only two months when it was named the Telegraph & Argus Best Beauty Salon 2025.

The salon was founded by mum Donna Perkins and her daughters, Emma and Chloe, after what they described as a “once-in-a-lifetime opportunity”.

The family were stunned when readers chose them from a final shortlist of ten salons.

Emma said the result left the family “overwhelmed” and described the win as “surreal”.

She added that the award had given the business a valuable boost as it continued to establish itself.

The recognition also helped build the family’s confidence in their decision to launch the salon together, with Emma saying it had helped overcome feelings of “imposter syndrome” and reassured them that they had made the right choice.

Nominations for this year’s competition are open now and will close on Sunday, September 20.

After nominations have been counted, the ten salons receiving the strongest support will be selected as finalists.

The competition will then enter its voting stage, with readers able to support their favourite finalist using voting slips published in the Telegraph & Argus from Monday, September 28, until Saturday, October 10, depending on the edition.

Readers can nominate their favourite beauty salon online at www.surveymonkey.co.uk/r/TABEST, or by scanning the QR code in the nomination advert.

Make sure your preferred salon gets the recognition it deserves before nominations close on Sunday, September 20.
