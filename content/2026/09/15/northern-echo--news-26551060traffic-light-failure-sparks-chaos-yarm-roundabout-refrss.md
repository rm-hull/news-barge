Traffic chaos was sparked on a busy Yarm roundabout after traffic lights got stuck on red.

Green Lane, the A67, and Thirsk Road were brought to a standstill on Sunday (September 13) after temporary lights failed at the major junction.

The roadwork lights were initially letting only three or four cars through before changing from green to red before facing total failure when all lights turned red in all directions.

It resulted in traffic gridlock - with AA at the time showing severe traffic queuing in every direction towards the roundabout at the time.

Yarm ward councillor John Coulson said furious drivers then started taking actions that were “not safe or legal”, including one who reportedly drove around the roundabout the wrong way.

“I was contacted via social media, messenger, and telephone calls regarding almost total traffic gridlock on Green Lane, Yarm due to the traffic light failure,” he said.

“I alerted the Stockton Borough Council control room who were aware and had contacted the third party who were responsible for the lights.
