A soft toy brand that could rival Jellycat has launched in the UK.

Premium soft toy company Mon Ami will debut on John Lewis' online website and in stores across the country this month.

It offers a collection of plush toys, dolls, and keepsakes built around themes of friendship and belonging.

More than 60 character-led designs will be available.

  Mon Ami soft toys are available in all John Lewis stores across the UK 

*(Image: Getty Images)*

## Soft toy brand Mon Ami launches in John Lewis stores in the UK

Each Mon Ami character is designed with a unique personality and story, such as Pia Peacock’s tropical elegance, Daphne Dragon’s golden good luck wings, and Da Pinchy Lobster’s soft-hearted charm.

The collection is priced between £22 and £50.

Colette Duck has already been unveiled as one of John Lewis’ top ten toys for playtime this Christmas.

Mon Ami characters will be available in all John Lewis stores and online from September 13.

Sara Fulmer, co-founder and CEO of Mon Ami, said: "Mon Ami is all about friendship.

"It doesn’t matter who you are or where you’re from, with Mon Ami there’s always a place to belong.


"From a child’s very first companion to a nostalgic keepsake that stirs memories years later, our creations celebrate the joy of friendship in all its forms.

"Whether they’re given to a newborn or kept by the young at heart, each piece is a gentle reminder of the wonder, comfort and imagination that comes from having a true friend by your side.

"We’re extremely excited to be launching in John Lewis – one of the biggest, most well-respected retail brands in the UK – and to reach even more customers just in time for the busy Christmas period."
