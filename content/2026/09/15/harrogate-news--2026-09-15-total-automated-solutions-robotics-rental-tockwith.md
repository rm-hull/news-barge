**TOCKWITH** — Regional automation specialists have launched a new industrial leasing framework designed to accelerate robotic adoption across Yorkshire’s manufacturing corridors by eliminating upfront capital investment risks.

Privately owned instrumentation and control systems firm **Total Automated Solutions (TAS)** has officially rolled out a specialized robotics Proof-of-Concept Rental Service. Formally launched on**Tuesday 15 September 2026** from their central technical hub in Tockwith, the service allows small and medium-sized manufacturers to integrate fully programmed robotic units into their live factory environments for flexible trial windows typically spanning**two to six months**.

The strategic deployment addresses a major bottleneck in domestic industrial modernization. While many regional manufacturers recognize the long-term benefits of automated production lines, the steep capital expenditure required for permanent industrial machinery often clashes with corporate risk tolerances. The rental framework allows engineering teams to generate empirical performance metrics regarding throughput, product quality shifts, and baseline operating costs before signing off on permanent procurement contracts.

## Custom Systems Configuration and Engineering Integration

Under the new operational service roadmap, Total Automated Solutions handles the complete technological lifecycle of the trial deployment. The firm’s dedicated systems integration engineers evaluate the client’s existing production layout, personally program the automation software, and outfit the machinery with applications tailored to specific localized bottlenecks.

The technical deployment includes the configuration of all physical infrastructure required to meet statutory workplace safety rules:

- **Mechanical End-Effectors:** Integrating application-specific robotic grippers engineered to handle the client’s actual raw materials or finished products.
- **Safety Infrastructure:** Installing mandatory surrounding perimeter guarding, light curtains, and integrated emergency-stop control loops.
- **Targeted Applications:** Optimizing units for repetitive product handling, high-speed pick-and-place operations, machine tool loading, and end-of-line packing or palletising.


**Kevin Chapman, Owner and Managing Director at Total Automated Solutions:**

Many manufacturers understand the potential benefits of robotics but need greater certainty before committing significant capital. A concept may appear viable on paper, but the real test is how it performs within the production environment. Our Proof-of-Concept Rental Service gives manufacturers the opportunity to test the technology, measure the results and build a robust business case. It turns automation from a theoretical proposal into a practical, evidence-based investment decision.



## Strengthening Yorkshire’s Advanced Engineering Grid

Established in 2007, the privately owned systems integration specialist operates an extensive industrial footprint encompassing control panel and Motor Control Centre (MCC) manufacture, programmable logic controller (PLC) engineering, SCADA software design, and industrial data communications systems.

The launch of the short-term rental option aims to help regional firms combat persistent manufacturing skills shortages and modernise aging assembly lines without absorbing crippling operational debt. At the conclusion of the trial cycle, clients hold the absolute contractual flexibility to purchase the configured robotic cell in full, scale up to a larger engineered production line, or return the machinery with no lingering capital liability.

Regional factory managers, industrial operations directors, or manufacturing business owners looking to evaluate robotic capabilities or coordinate an on-site process assessment can access the technical service directory directly at Total Automated Solutions.
