THE University of Bradford has climbed 18 places in the Guardian University Guide 2027, now ranking joint 51st out of 123 UK institutions.

Several subject areas have earned national recognition, including Paramedic Science, ranked second in the country, and Chemical Engineering, ranked sixth.

Other high-performing subjects include Midwifery (14th), Health Professions (19th), and Mechanical Engineering (20th).

Professor Gillian Murray, vice-chancellor of the University of Bradford, said: "Our rise in the Guardian University Guide 2027 rankings, alongside the outstanding performance of a number of our subject areas, is a tremendous achievement and reflects the dedication and hard work of colleagues across the University.

"To be ranked second nationally for Paramedic Science and sixth for Chemical Engineering, while also seeing several other subjects recognised among the UK's top 20, demonstrates the quality of our teaching, student experience and the real-world relevance of our programmes.

"These results highlight our commitment to providing an excellent education that enables students from all backgrounds to achieve their ambitions, develop the skills employers need and make a positive impact in their professions and communities.

"We are proud of this recognition and will continue working to deliver the best possible outcomes for our students."

The Guardian University Guide ranks UK universities using eight categories, including results from the National Student Survey, student satisfaction, career prospects, and student-to-staff ratio.

The University of Bradford received strong scores across the board, with a 91.1 per cent continuation rate—reflecting the proportion of first-year students progressing to their second year.

Student satisfaction was also high, with 84.5 per cent satisfied with teaching and 80.2 per cent satisfied with assessment.

The university's average teaching score was 56.5 out of 100.

It also ranked 12th nationally for "Value Added," a measure comparing students’ degree outcomes with their entry qualifications.

Several subject areas saw significant year-on-year improvements, including Computer Science and Information Systems (up 48 places), Law (up 37), Psychology (up 29), Mechanical Engineering (up 27), and Economics (up 21).

This marks the latest in a series of ranking successes for the university, which was recently placed joint 29th out of 128 institutions in the Daily Mail University Guide—its highest ever position in that ranking.

Clearing for 2026 remains open until October 9, giving prospective students—including mature learners—a chance to secure a place at the university.

The University of Bradford’s clearing phone line is open on 0808 196 9129, and applications can also be submitted online.
