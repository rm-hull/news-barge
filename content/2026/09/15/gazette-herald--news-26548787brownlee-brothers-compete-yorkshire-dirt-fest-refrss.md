Two of Yorkshire's most famous athletes are set to line up alongside amateur athletes at an off-road sports festival in North Yorkshire this autumn.

Jonny and Alistair Brownlee, double Olympic triathlon champions, will take part in Dirt Fest at Bramham Park on Saturday, October 3.

The event is organised by TBF Events, the events arm of the Brownlee Foundation, and will see the brothers join the gravel sportive.

A spokesman said: "They'll be riding the event's flagship gravel sportive, mixing it with age-groupers, local club riders and first-timers across the estate's private gravel tracks."

The festival, which promotes grassroots sport through the Brownlee Foundation, features a range of off-road activities.

Events on Saturday include 5k and 10k trail runs, a half marathon, and junior duathlons for children aged eight and up.

Sunday will see sprint and standard distance gravel duathlons.

The entire festival is set within Bramham Park’s private off-road trails.

The park is a Grade I listed 18th-century estate known for hosting Leeds Festival and the Bramham Horse Trials.

Dirt Fest is run by TBF Events, the events arm of the Brownlee Foundation.

The Brownlee brothers, who grew up and trained in West Yorkshire, continue to inspire young athletes through their charity and sporting achievements.
