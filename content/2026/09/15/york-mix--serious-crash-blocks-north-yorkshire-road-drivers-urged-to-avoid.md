A serious crash has closed a North Yorkshire road tonight (Tuesday).

The A171 near Helwath Beck between Whitby and Scarborough is shut.

Witnesses say the crash involves a motorbike. They have been told the road will be closed for hours.

#### Crash location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Emergency services are currently dealing with a serious collision on the A171 near Helwath Beck between Whitby and Scarborough.

“Motorists are urged to avoid the area until further notice.

“Please follow the local diversions.”

Witnesses or anyone with relevant dashcam footage of the incident are asked to call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quote reference NYP-15092026-0449.
