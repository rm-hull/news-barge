A BRADFORD football club says it has reminded players and team staff of their responsibilities after concerns were raised about litter allegedly left behind following a match.

Clayton Albion AFC issued a statement after a local resident complained about rubbish reportedly being left at its Primrose Lane ground on Sunday.

Responding to the concerns, a spokesperson for the club said it had been made aware of the allegations and had taken the matter seriously.

The spokesperson said: "As a club, we absolutely do not condone leaving litter or behaving in a way that negatively impacts the facilities we use or the surrounding community."

The club said it had addressed the issue directly with the manager responsible for the squad involved.

He added: "We have made it clear that this type of behaviour is not acceptable and must not form part of our normal club culture."

## Commitment to the community

Clayton Albion said it is working hard to build a football club that has a positive impact on both players and the wider community.

The spokesperson said the club's aims extend beyond results on the pitch, with an emphasis on maintaining high standards of behaviour and respecting local facilities.

They said: "Clayton is working extremely hard to develop a football setup that is socially responsible and has a positive impact on both its players and the wider community."

"That includes maintaining the correct standards of behaviour both on and off the pitch, respecting opposition clubs and officials, and looking after the facilities and communities that allow us to play football."

## Players reminded of responsibilities

While the club did not comment on the circumstances surrounding Sunday's fixture, it said the concerns raised had prompted a reminder to players and management about their responsibilities after matches.

The spokesperson said: "Regardless of the circumstances surrounding Sunday, we take the concerns raised by the resident seriously.

"We have reminded the team management and players of their responsibility to ensure that the area they use is left clean and tidy after fixtures, and we will reinforce this across our wider football setup."

## 'Not the standard we expect'

The club also expressed disappointment that a resident felt the need to raise concerns.

The spokesperson said: "We are disappointed to hear that a local resident felt the need to raise this concern, as this is not the standard we expect from anyone representing Clayton.

"We appreciate it being brought to our attention and will use it to make sure the correct standards are maintained going forward."
