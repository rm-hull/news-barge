APPLICATIONS are now open for secondary school places starting in September 2027.

Parents and carers in the Bradford district have until October 31, 2026, to submit their applications through the council website.

Councillor Sally Birch, executive member for children and families at Bradford Council, said: "Applying for a secondary school place is an important step for families and marks the beginning of an exciting new chapter for young people as they prepare for the next stage of their education.

"We want every child in Bradford district to feel happy, supported and able to thrive at school.

"Applying by the 31 October deadline gives your child the best chance of being offered a place at one of your preferred schools.

"The online application process is quick and easy to complete and, if you have any questions or need support along the way, our admissions team is on hand and ready to help."

Families are encouraged to attend school open evenings and consider all options before applying.

Up to five schools can be listed on the application, and it is recommended to use all preferences to improve the chances of securing a place at a preferred school.

Some schools may also require a supplementary information form.
