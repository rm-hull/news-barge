A patient was taken to hospital after a medical emergency at a County Durham school.

Emergency services were called to an address off Manor Way in Peterlee at 2.25pm on Monday, September 14.

The incident is understood to have happened at Dene Academy.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) dispatched a rapid response paramedic, an ambulance crew and a specialist critical care paramedic to the scene.

One person was taken to hospital for further treatment.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We were called to a person unwell at an address off Manor Way in Peterlee at 2.25pm on September 14.

"We dispatched a rapid response paramedic, one ambulance crew and a specialist critical care paramedic.

"One patient was taken to hospital for further treatment."
