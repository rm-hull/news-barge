Grants and expert advice are helping businesses grow and revitalise the high street in Pocklington.

Through a targeted support scheme, 14 businesses have received funding to improve shop fronts, while other improvements include upgraded signage and window renovations.

A pedestrian-friendly zone and free public Wi-Fi have also been introduced to make the town centre more inviting for visitors and residents.

  Beth Davies of Boffy's Play Cafe 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Councillor Anne Handley, leader of East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "High streets and local businesses are at the heart of our communities and it’s great to see Pocklington benefit from our support.

"We encourage interested businesses to find out more about how they can receive help and guidance from our business support team."

Businesses have also received one-to-one consultations and access to specialist training, with nine receiving a total of £73,000 in grants.

  Boffy's Play Cafe 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Boffy’s Play Café, one of the businesses supported, received a start-up capital grant and guidance from the council’s business support team.

Beth Davies, owner of the play café, said: "Starting my business at the beginning of the year was the biggest and scariest thing I have ever done.

"I would highly recommend getting in touch with the business team.

"They have massively helped us and continue to do so."
