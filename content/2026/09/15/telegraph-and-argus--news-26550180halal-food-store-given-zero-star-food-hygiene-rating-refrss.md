A STORE in the Bradford district has been given a zero-star food hygiene rating.

Ahmeds Food Store & Halal Meats, located on Drewry Road in Keighley, received the lowest possible rating following an inspection in August.

Hygiene officers said major improvement was needed in food handling, storage, and preparation.

Concerns were also raised about the cleanliness and condition of the premises, including layout, ventilation, hand washing facilities and pest control.

The store’s management of food safety, including staff knowledge and confidence in maintaining future standards, was also found to be lacking during the inspection.

The T&A have approached Ahmeds Food Store & Halal Meats for comment.

## How are ratings calculated?

Food hygiene ratings are calculated over three categories - hygienic food handling, cleanliness and condition of facilities and building, and management of food safety.

Businesses are rated on a scale from 0 (urgent improvement necessary) to 5 (very good).
