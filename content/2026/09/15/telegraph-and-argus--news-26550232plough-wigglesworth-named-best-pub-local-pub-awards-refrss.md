THE manager of a pub is "absolutely delighted" after it was named best in the area.

The Plough, in the centre of Wigglesworth which at the last census had just 379 residents, was not only named best pub, but also 'Best Rural Pub'.

This came in Skipton MP Sir Julian Smith's Local Pub Awards 2026.

Jonathan Booth, manager of The Plough, said: "I am absolutely delighted to have been awarded two awards for the Plough, to get two awards in one year is just amazing and I’d like to thank all those who voted for us.

"I would also like to thank and congratulate the full team here at the Plough for working so hard over the past year."

Joint honours for Best High Street Pub went to Skipton pubs, The Woolly Sheep Inn, and The Castle Inn.

There was also success for Tracy of the Helwith Bridge Inn, Horton-in-Ribblesdale, and Roz and Calvin, of The Castle Inn, Skipton, who were joint winners of the Staff Member of the Year category.

Over the summer, people across Sir Julian's Skipton and Ripon constituency were invited to nominate and vote for their favourite local pubs and members of pub staff, helping to recognise the important role that local hospitality businesses play in communities.

Nominations were received across four categories: Best Pub, Best High Street Pub, Best Rural Pub and Staff Member of the Year.

Sir Julian said: "The response to this year's awards has once again demonstrated just how highly local people value their pubs and the people who work in them.

"Pubs are far more than places to enjoy food and drink.

"They are employers, community hubs, meeting places and an important part of local life.

"Across Skipton and Ripon, pubs play a vital role in bringing people together and supporting our local economy.

"I would like to congratulate all of this year's winners and thank every pub and member of staff who was nominated.

"The high standard of nominations received shows the strength of our local hospitality sector and the important contribution it makes to communities across our constituency."

Certificates of recognition have been sent to all category winners, and Sir Julian will visit The Plough in Wigglesworth to present their awards in person.

It is the second year running The Plough has been named 'Best Rural Pub'.
