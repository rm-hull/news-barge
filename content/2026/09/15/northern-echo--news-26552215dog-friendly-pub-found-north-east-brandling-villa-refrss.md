A pub in the North East has been crowned the most dog-friendly pub in Britain.

The Brandling Villa in South Gosforth, Newcastle, won the title in a nationwide competition run by pet care marketplace Rover, which reviewed 110 pubs across the UK to find the best destinations for canine companions.

Charlie Cook, pub manager at The Brandling Villa, said: "We've always believed a great pub should welcome the whole family, and that absolutely includes the dog.

"Over the years, we've welcomed plenty of four-legged regulars through our doors, so we've had a lot of fun creating little touches that make a visit special for them too.

"To be recognised as Britain's most dog-friendly pub is a fantastic honour and a real testament to the Brandling Villa team, who always go the extra mile to give you and your pup a proper Geordie welcome."

  The Brandling Villa manager, Charlie Cook, and his dog Vinnie 

*(Image: Rover)*

The venue topped the list thanks to its dog-first approach and a range of features designed for especially for canine guests.

Dogs are welcome throughout the pub, offering fresh water, treats, dog-themed artwork and a 'Jimmy Kibble' treat machine.

The Brandling Villa also dominated the North East rankings, ahead of The Star Inn in Harbottle, The Owl & Otter in Dipton, The Middle Inn in Bamburgh and the Raby Arms in Hart Village.

Rover’s 'Tavern Testers' judged each pub on access, atmosphere, staff interaction, safety, facilities and the experience for dogs.

  The Brandling Villa, Newcastle. 

*(Image: Rover)*

The study was launched after new research found that suggests dogs are becoming regular pub-goers in their own right.

A survey of 2,000 dog owners found 47 per cent said their pet was on first-name terms with pub staff.

Another 43 per cent knew the names of other dogs at their local pub, but only half knew their owners’ names.

More than a quarter of owners said their dog would choose the pub over the park.

Adem Fehmi, canine behaviour expert at Rover, said: "For dogs who are comfortable in social settings, a trip to the pub can be a really positive experience, offering plenty of mental stimulation through new sights, sounds, and smells, as well as valuable quality time with their owners."

"The best dog-friendly venues understand that a warm welcome goes beyond simply allowing dogs through the door.

"Having access to fresh water, enough space to settle, and a calm, comfortable environment can all help dogs feel at ease.

"The Brandling Villa has clearly thought about the experience from a dog's perspective, while adding plenty of fun touches for owners too, making it a worthy winner this year."

Rover launched the search for up to 11 paid Tavern Testers in May, with successful applicants spending six weeks assessing pubs across the region alongside their four-legged friends.
