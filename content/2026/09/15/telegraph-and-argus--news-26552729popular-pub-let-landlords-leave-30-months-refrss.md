A POPULAR pub with an annual turnover of £450,000 is up to let after its landlords shut its doors.

The Old Glen House, located on Prod Lane in Baildon, has closed following two-and-a-half years under its current operators.

Star Pubs says it is planning a £280,000 refurbishment as the search for new occupants begin.

## Landlords move on

The pub announced its closure on social media, with operators Katie and Phil thanking customers for their support over recent years.

In a post shared online, Katie wrote: "Unfortunately after 2.5yrs we have decided to close the doors to The Old Glen House as of yesterday.

"Phil and I would like to thank everyone who supported us during our time there."

The pair also encouraged customers to visit them at The Glen Pub in nearby Gilstead.

## Customers pay tribute

The announcement prompted an outpouring of support from customers, many of whom praised the efforts made by the operators during their time at the venue.

One customer wrote: "You put your heart and soul into that place."

Another commented: "Nobody can say that you didn't try with that place."

Others wished the pair well for the future and said they would continue to support them at their new venture.

## Major investment planned

The closure comes as Star Pubs advertises The Old Glen House as available to let.

According to a listing on the company's website, a £280,000 investment is planned for the pub as part of a major refurbishment programme.

The proposed works include a full interior refurbishment, seating for around 70 diners, a fully-developed kitchen and improved outdoor space.

Star Pubs describes the venue as having "untapped potential" due to its location close to Shipley Glen and surrounding residential areas.

## Looking for a new operator

The brewery is seeking an experienced operator to take on the venue and lead its next chapter.

In its marketing materials, Star Pubs says it is looking for someone with experience running a destination food-led hospitality business, as well as a clear vision for the pub's food, drink and entertainment offering.

The listing estimates an annual turnover of £451,397 and an annual rent of £42,464.

Star Pubs has been approached for comment.
