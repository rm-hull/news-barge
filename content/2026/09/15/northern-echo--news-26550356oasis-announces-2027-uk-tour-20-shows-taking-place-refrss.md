Oasis has announced a new tour with several UK dates, with the band set to play in their hometown of Manchester as well as the iconic Knebworth Park, in Hertfordshire.

It will be the second tour in recent years after the brothers, Liam and Noel Gallagher, reunited for the Live ‘25 tour last year.

The announcement was posted on Instagram with the band saying: “START THE CELEBRATIONS!!!!

"After a period of reflection “The Most Damaging Pop Cultural Force In Recent British History” has declared itself fit for purpose and will once again return to lift the spirits of the people.

"Come witness the fitness. It shall be a sight to behold.”


## Oasis announces UK tour for 2027

The iconic rock band will play 11 nights at the Etihad over four weeks in June, with the first date on 4 and the last on 26.

It is then a return to the iconic Knebworth for four shows in September on the 11, 12, 18 and 19.

Oasis will also be perfomring at Glasgow's Cletic Park for five nights in May.

The Etihad is notably the home of Manchester City, which both the Gallagher brothers support.

Meanwhile, Oasis’ planned gigs at Knebworth will break the record for the most times an act has played there.


It has been reported that extra dates could be added due to demand, as they were for last year’s tour.

Whether you saw them at their last shows or you’re new to the Oasis fanbase, tickets will be on sale soon.

## How to get tickets to Oasis 2027?

There will be no general onsale of tickets, instead Oasis fans can register for tickets via their website.


## When did Oasis last go on tour?

The last Oasis tour was highly anticipated by fans as they scrambled to get tickets, which sold out quickly.

It took place in 2025 with shows across the UK, including Cardiff, Manchester, London and Edinburgh.

A total of 41 shows were played from July to November, with the brothers also performing in Mexico City, Tokyo, Melbourne and Sao Paolo.
