A YOUNG Bradford woman who was given a chance under new sentencing powers aimed at reducing the prison population has been locked for more than three years after she committed another burglary just 14 days later.

Last month Viktoria Keselova, 19, appeared at Bradford Crown Court and was sentenced to 30 months in custody, suspended for two years, for a series of sneak-in house burglaries and shop thefts.

## Woman stole passports, bank cards and phones

But the court heard on Tuesday that just a fortnight later Keselova forced a lock on a multi-occupancy flat in Southfield Lane, Canterbury, and stole property worth hundreds of pounds belonging to the three residents.

The court heard that one of the men woke up to find the flat was in a mess and items including passports, bank cards, mobile phones and a laptop had been stolen.

Keselova, of Brooksbank Avenue, Clayton, was caught on CCTV breaking into the flat and a few days after the burglary one of the men spotted her hiding behind some nearby bins and alerted the police.

Keselova was arrested and admitted the burglary charge when she made her first appearance before the magistrates court last month.

Recorder Tom Little KC activated 24 months from the suspended sentence and added a further 14 months in custody for the latest burglary.

## Crystal meth user

Barrister Oliver Norman, for Keselova, said his client had been using crystal meth as a coping mechanism and before she could get the help and support of the probation service she had relapsed into offending to fund her habit.

He said Keselova, who appeared via a video link to HMP New Hall, wanted to change and was sorry for her offending.

“She wants to ensure that she doesn’t return to further offending on her release,” he added.

Recorder Little said Keselova had been the beneficiary last month when her sentence of more than two years was suspended by the court.

“I have no doubt that the judge would have told you in the clearest terms that you had only just avoided an immediate custodial sentence,” he added.
