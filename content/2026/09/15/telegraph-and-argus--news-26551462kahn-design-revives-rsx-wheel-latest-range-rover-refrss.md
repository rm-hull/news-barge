Afzal Kahn, the Bradford-based car styling designer, has reintroduced one of his classic alloy wheel designs with a modern twist.

Mr Kahn, founder and chief designer of Kahn Design, has announced the return of the RSX wheel—originally launched in 2005 as one of the company’s most iconic products.

The Bradford-based automotive design firm has re-engineered the wheel for 2026 to fit the latest Range Rover, Range Rover Sport, and Defender models.

Mr Kahn said: "It was one of the wheels that helped establish what Kahn Design stands for.

"Bringing it back as a forged wheel, built properly around where our design language sits today, felt like the right way to mark its return.

"I see this as the most complete modernisation of the wire wheel concept we’ve produced."

  Automotive designer and businessman Afzal Kahn 

*(Image: PA)*

The RSX wheel, part of the KAHN Forged collection, has been completely redesigned rather than simply reissued.

Unlike the original cast version, the new wheel uses forged construction for improved strength, reduced weight, and sharper detailing.

The original RSX, introduced more than 20 years ago, became a defining product for Kahn Design.

Its ten-spoke cross-spoke design was inspired by the wire wheels of classic coachbuilt cars but with a modernised look.

The wheel was fitted to a range of vehicles including Range Rovers and BMW X models and remains one of the most requested designs in the company’s history.

The 2026 version features a more sculpted profile and a new centre section with a star-shaped surround.

Kahn Design said these updates give the wheel a more layered appearance while preserving the character of the original.

The wheel is available in 22-inch and 23-inch sizes and comes in a satin black finish.

Kahn Design, headquartered on Canal Road, has built a global reputation for bespoke vehicle styling and design.

Despite its international reach, the company has maintained strong ties to Bradford.

The return of the RSX highlights the ongoing influence of the firm in the automotive world.

It also serves as a reminder of its origins and continued growth in the city.

Kahn Design’s products continue to carry the city’s name into international motoring circles.
