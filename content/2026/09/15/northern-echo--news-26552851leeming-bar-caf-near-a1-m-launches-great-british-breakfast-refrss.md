A North Yorkshire café has created a new unique breakfast bringing together food from across Britain after its boss misheard a customer's order.

Simply Café, at Leeming Bar, has launched its Great British Breakfast featuring ingredients associated with England, Scotland, Wales and Ireland.

The unusual dish was dreamed up after Simply Dutch managing director Jason Hadlow thought he heard a customer ordering a “British breakfast” at the café counter.

When he discovered they had actually ordered something else, the misunderstanding gave him an idea.

  Simply Dutch managing director Jason Hadlow prepares to sample the new Great British Breakfast at Simply Café in Leeming Bar 

*(Image: SIMPLY CAFE)*

Jason said: “When I asked what they had ordered, it turned out they hadn’t said ‘British breakfast’ at all.

“But it made me wonder why we have a full English, a full Scottish and an Ulster fry, but nobody seems to bring the best-known ingredients from all four nations together on one plate.

“That was the beginning of the Great British Breakfast.”

The resulting plate includes traditional Yorkshire sausage and bacon to represent England, Scottish Lorne sausage, Welsh rarebit and Irish white pudding.


They are served alongside more familiar breakfast favourites including an egg, mushrooms, grilled tomato and baked beans.

The café already had most of the ingredients in its kitchen, but sourced Lorne sausage and white pudding specially to complete the dish.

Jason said the meal had been designed as a substantial café breakfast rather than simply a novelty.

  The new Great British Breakfast at Simply Café in Leeming Bar 

*(Image: SIMPLY CAFE)*

He added: “It is intended to be a proper, generous café breakfast rather than a gimmick.

“Every special ingredient has a genuine connection with the place it represents, and, importantly, it all tastes extremely good together.”

The café has also decorated its windows to mark the launch.

Jason said: “We’ve celebrated the idea throughout the café windows, so people driving past will certainly know that something British is happening inside.”

Simply Café is part of Simply Dutch at Leeming Bar and serves breakfasts, lunches and cakes. Independent listings also highlight breakfast as one of the café's established offerings.

The Great British Breakfast is being served for a limited period.
