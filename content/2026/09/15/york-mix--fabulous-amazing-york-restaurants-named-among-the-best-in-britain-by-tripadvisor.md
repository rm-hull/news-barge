The team at a York restaurant say they are ‘incredibly grateful and very excited’ after being named number one in the country – for the second year in a row.

**Casa Brazilian Rodizio** has been named the**No.1 Best of the Best Casual Dining Restaurant** in the UK by Tripadvisor for the second consecutive year.

It has also been ranked ranked number eight in the world.

And it’s not the only York restaurant to be named among the ‘best of the best’ by Tripadvisor – see below!

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/casa-brazilian-rodizio-york-restaurant-owner-eduardo.webp)

Casa Brazilian Rodizio offers an authentic Brazilian-style dining experience, with a continuous selection of freshly grilled meats carved directly at guests’ tables.

Formerly Vaquieors, it has been running in York for ten years. Its Tripadvisor reviews regularly include the words ‘fabulous’, ‘amazing’ and just plain ‘wow’.

Owner of Casa, on Micklegate, Eduardo, said: “What makes this award so special is that it comes from the experiences and reviews of our guests.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/casa-brazilian-rodizio-york-picanha-steak-carving.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/casa-brazilian-rodizio-york-restaurant-exterior-micklegate.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/casa-brazilian-rodizio-restaurant-salad-bar-york.webp)

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/casa-brazilian-rodizio-brazilian-cocktails-york.webp)

“We have an amazing team who put so much passion and hard work into Casa every single day, but none of this would be possible without the people who choose to dine with us.

“We want to say a huge thank you to everyone who has visited, returned, celebrated a special occasion with us, recommended us to friends or taken the time to leave a review. The support we receive from York, as well as visitors from around the world, is incredible.

“To be an independent restaurant here in York and be recognised as number one in the UK for two years running and number eight in the world is difﬁcult to put into words. We’re incredibly grateful and very excited about what comes next.”

## Fine dining and date night winners

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/meltons-restaurant-york-rarebit-fb.webp)

In Tripadvisor’s **Best of the Best Fine Dining Restaurants**, long-time York favourite** Melton’s**, on Scarcroft Road, comes in at number 7 in the UK.

Tripadvisor says: “The food earns rave reviews for inventive flavours and beautiful presentation, while the service is noted for being friendly and attentive.

“The cosy and inviting atmosphere contributes to a memorable dining experience, with advance reservations often recommended due to its popularity.”

In the **the Best of the Best Hidden Gems Restaurants**,** Kapadokya 50** on George Hudson Street, York, comes in at number eight in the UK.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Kapadokya_edit-1000x563.jpg)

Tripadvisor says its reviewers praised the “warm and inviting atmosphere” and the food which is “authentically Turkish and flavourful, with generous portions”.

And another North Yorkshire restaurant is on this list. The **The Saddle Room Restaurant** in**Leyburn** is at number six in Britain’s hidden gems. Its “delicious food – highlighted by Sunday roasts and local ingredients – often earns rave reviews”.

Meanwhile, **Legacy At The Grand** in York is number seven in the**Best of the Best Date Night Restaurants** in Britain.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/legacy-restaurant-grand-hotel-york-visuals-6.jpg)

The dining room at The Grand Hotel on Station Rise is clearly the place to go to dish up some romance.

“Legacy At The Grand, York is a dining gem that receives rave reviews for its warm and knowledgeable service,” Tripadvisor says.

“The staff’s ability to make guests feel genuinely cared for is frequently highlighted.”

The Travellers’ Choice Best of the Best Awards are determined based on the quality and quantity of traveller reviews and ratings specific to each subcategory posted on Tripadvisor over a 12-month period, backed by an editorial review process that places winners among the top one per cent of listings on the platform.
