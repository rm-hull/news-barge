A NORTH Yorkshire shop which stocks Pokémon is gearing up for a surge of fans as it marks the franchise’s 30th anniversary.

Hoppers in Malton has announced that all available anniversary stock—except Elite Trainer Boxes (ETBs) and binders—will be available in person only tomorrow.

A spokesperson for the shop said: "Excitement is building for the 30th anniversary tomorrow and the new products that will be on sale.

"Whilst we are pleased to have been allocated all of the available products, unfortunately our ETBs and binders are yet to arrive."

Other merchandise will be limited to one of each type per customer, with character variations also restricted.

The shop does not usually take reservations, but will be allowing those in the queue or in-store first thing tomorrow to reserve their ETBs and binders for collection when they arrive.
