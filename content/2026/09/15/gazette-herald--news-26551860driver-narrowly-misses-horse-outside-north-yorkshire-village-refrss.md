A reckless car driver narrowly missed one of five horses crossing a country road in North Yorkshire.

The incident took place last week as horses and riders returned from the gallops at Mick and David Easterby's racing stables in Sheriff Hutton.

A 10-second video dated Friday, September 11 sent to The Press shows the horses crossing in formation, with a small gap between the third and fourth.

  


  David Easterby (standing) holds a joint training license with this father Michael. 

*(Image: Michael and David Easterby)*

The rider of the third horse appears to raise their right arm towards the car before it comes into view.


As the car comes into shot, the third horse is near the centre of the road and kicks out and jumps as it passes close by.

David Easterby said the yard had been in the same place for years and the stables had put up warning signs.

  Mr Easterby said there were warning signs on the approach to the yard on the Bulmer to Sheriff Hutton road (image dated April 2024) 

*(Image: Google Street View)*

He said: "There's one bad apple which upsets the whole bunch, and this action has endangered both the staff and the horses."

A person familiar with the country road said it can be "like a racetrack with cars driving at ludicrous speeds" and that "the horses can be seen crossing from a long way away".

Mr Easterby said he hoped police would act over the reported incident.

The Press has contacted North Yorkshire Police.
