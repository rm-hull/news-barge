Last week, 39,374 children and young people participated in the first (symbolic) U16 election in Berlin. Yesterday, the results came in. Die Linke came in first with 32.8% of the vote, ahead of Die Grünen with 20.9%. They were followed by the SPD (11.9%), CDU (8.3%), Die Tierschutzpartei (Animal Welfare Party) (7.7%) and AfD (5.8%). The FDP, Volt and BSW all received less than 5% of the vote.

Despite their popularity with the under 16s, it is considered impossible for the Die Tierschutzpartei to surpass the 5% threshold in the actual election on September 20th.

In the symbolic election, children and young people under 16 can express their political preferences. The result has no influence on the regular election of the House of Representatives. The organisers stated, “The fact that so many young people cast their votes underscores their interest in participation, especially at a time when young voices are rarely heard.”

Lowering the regular voting age to 16 transformed the former U18 election for the House of Representatives into a U16 election for the first time. In the last regular election for the House of Representatives in 2021, 16,763 children and young people voted. In the last U18 election (federal election 2025), 32,497 young people participated in Berlin.

Around 340 polling stations in youth centres, schools and libraries were open last week. All young people below the voting age were eligible to vote. Citizenship was not a factor. The organisers emphasise that, due to the voluntary nature of participation, the election results are not representative of all children and young people in Berlin.
