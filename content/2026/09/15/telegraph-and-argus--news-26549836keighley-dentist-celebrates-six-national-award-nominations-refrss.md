A DENTAL practice has been named a finalist in six national award categories, including patient care and community impact.

Taylored Dental Care, based in Keighley, will represent the region at the Private Dentistry Awards with nominations for Team of the Year (North), Patient Care (North), Charity or Community Project (North), Treatment of Nervous Patients (North), Practice of the Year (North East), and Most Improved (North).

A spokesperson for the practice said: "There were more than 1,000 entries, so we're over the moon to be Finalists in 6 categories.

"We are incredibly proud to have won Treatment of Nervous Patients – North at the Private Dentistry Awards for two consecutive years running (2024 and 2025) and are hopeful for this year."

The practice relocated in November 2025 to a new site designed to support anxious patients.

The spokesperson added: "2025 was a big year for us - we completely renovated the former Italian restaurant in Keighley (Santoni) to create a practice that was fully designed to cater and care for phobic patients, and in November 2025 we moved in.

"Relocating from our previous home on Cavendish Street, Keighley where we had been based since 2009."

Taylored Dental Care also operates practices in Idle and Wibsey.
