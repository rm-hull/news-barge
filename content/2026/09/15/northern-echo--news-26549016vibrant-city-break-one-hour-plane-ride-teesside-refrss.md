Just over an hour from Teesside sits one of Europe's most popular city break destinations, where visitors can find stunning canal views, independent cafés and renowned museums.

Amsterdam's canals, historic architecture and lively atmosphere make it an easy city break, with flights from Teesside taking little over an hour.

Visitors can spend days exploring the Rijksmuseum, Van Gogh Museum and Anne Frank House, or wandering canal-lined streets and independent shops.

Amsterdam has more than 160 canals and 44 museums, giving visitors plenty of options beyond the city’s best-known attractions.

The city's vibrant food scene offers something for every taste, from cosy waterside cafés and Dutch bakeries to trendy coffee spots and international dining.

Beyond its cultural attractions, Amsterdam offers a relaxed pace, compact centre and neighbourhoods that are easy to explore.

Cycling is one of the most popular and iconic ways to get around the Dutch capital, with dedicated bike lanes stretching across the city giving visitors a chance to experience Amsterdam like a local.

Amsterdam remains a short-break favourite, combining culture, nightlife, shopping and history within easy walking distance.

For North East travellers, Amsterdam is one of the quickest European getaways from Teesside Airport, with flights taking little more than an hour.

Teesside Airport announced in July that it would reintroduce a third daily flight to Amsterdam, offering even greater flexibility for those planning a trip to the city.

That means travellers can swap Teesside for Amsterdam's famous canals, world-class museums and bustling café culture in less time than it takes to drive to some of the country's larger airports.

Whether visitors want sightseeing, museums or coffee beside the water, Amsterdam remains one of Europe's top city breaks.
