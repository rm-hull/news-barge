Footage of the arrest of a former North East church leader who preyed on vulnerable young men has been release after he was jailed.

John Grant, 77, who previously held senior roles at churches including Billingham Baptist Church, has been jailed for eight and a half years after being convicted of a string of sexual offences spanning decades.

Video released by the Metropolitan Police shows officers arresting Grant while he is in a police vehicle.

In the footage, Grant, of Colney Hatch Lane, Hornsey in north London, is seen getting into the back of a police car as officers take him into custody.

A second video released by investigators includes excerpts from his police interview, in which Grant repeatedly insisted that anything he had done was consensual and frequently answered officers' questions with "no comment".


During questioning, detectives challenged Grant over allegations involving vulnerable men and asked whether he had used his position within the church to gain sexual gratification.

When asked if he used his role in the church to help him obtain sexual gratification from victims, Grant replied: "No comment."

He also refused to answer questions about whether being in a position of power aroused him and whether there could be other victims.

At one stage, officers asked him: "How many more victims are there, John?"

Grant again responded: "No comment."

The footage has been released after Grant was sentenced at Wood Green Crown Court on Monday, September 14.

He was found guilty of three counts of sexual assault, six counts of indecent assault, and two counts of causing another person to engage in non-penetrative sexual activity.

Police said Grant had been a senior figure in churches and religious communities across the country between 1970 and 2012, including Billingham Baptist Church.

The Metropolitan Police investigation began in 2023 after one victim reported being repeatedly sexually assaulted over a period of around 30 years.

   

*(Image: PA/MET POLICE)*

According to detectives, the abuse began after the victim moved into a shared house with Grant in 1994.

Police said Grant started offering massages and, despite those advances being rejected, the victim felt pressured into situations involving sexual touching.

As officers expanded their inquiries, they identified further complainants.

Detectives said a common pattern emerged across the accounts they received, alleging that Grant groomed vulnerable young men through coercive and controlling behaviour before sexually assaulting them.

Following sentencing, Detective Chief Inspector Elsa Mak said the case was built on the courage of victim-survivors who came forward to report what had happened.

She said: "Today's sentencing follows an extensive investigation by the Met into Grant's offending and shows our commitment to bringing offenders to justice.

"We would like to pay tribute to the bravery of the victim-survivors who have already come forward.

"Their experiences and accounts directly shaped our investigation while it was in its early stages, and we thank them for putting their trust in us to help identify the scale of Grant's prolific offending across four decades."

Police believe there may be additional victims who have not yet come forward because of Grant's long-standing involvement in churches and youth groups.

Grant, of Colney Hatch Lane, Hornsey, was also handed an indefinite Sexual Harm Prevention Order and will remain on the sex offenders register for life.

Anyone with information relating to Grant has been urged to contact police on 101 quoting CAD3277/08JUL26 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously.
