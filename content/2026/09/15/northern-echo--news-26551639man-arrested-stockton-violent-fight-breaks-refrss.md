One man has been taken to hospital and another arrested after a violent fight broke out in Teesside.

It follows Cleveland Police officers hearing a disturbance while patrolling at around 8.40pm last night (September 14).

Officers found a group fighting near Yarm Lane in an area of Stockton known as the 'Chuggy'.

Several men fled the scene as the officers approached the group.

Police immediately searched the area and found a 43-year-old man, who was later taken to North Tees Hospital.

He received treatment for injuries to his arm, shoulder and head.

Police say the man's injuries are not believed to be life-threatening.

A 24-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of Section 18 assault, which involves causing grievous bodily harm with intent.

The suspect was also taken to James Cook University Hospital for treatment, though the nature of his injuries has not been disclosed.

Detectives from Stockton CID are continuing to investigate the incident and are appealing for anyone with information or footage to come forward.

A Cleveland Police spokesperson said: "At approximately 8.40pm last night, Monday 14 September, Stockton Proactive Team officers on patrols heard an ongoing violent altercation and came across a group of people fighting on the Yarm Lane side of the area known as the 'Chuggy'; several men fled the scene as officers approached.

"The officers immediately carried out area searches, and one man (43) was taken to the University Hospital of North Tees for treatment to injuries to his arm, shoulder and head – his injuries are not currently believed to be life-threatening."
