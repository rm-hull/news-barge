A woman has changed pleas and admitted cocaine supply charges she previously denied.

But Samantha Caile must wait ten months to be sentenced as her co-accused, Jonathan McKenzie, still faces trial for the same offences.

Both denied possessing cocaine with intent to supply on May 10, 2024, at a plea hearing at Durham Crown Court on June 29.

They each also denied being concerned in the supply of cocaine, between April 16 and May 5, 2024.

The trial, expected to last up to three days, was fixed with a start date on Monday, July 12, 2027.

  Woman has changed plea and admitted cocaine supply charges but she will have to wait to be sentenced until after the trial of her co-accused, at Durham Crown Court, next July


But 38-year-old Caile returned to court for a further plea hearing on Friday, September 11.

Her lawyer, Jordan Parkinson, asked for the charges to be put to her again.

Caile this time pleaded guilty, and Miss Parkinson offered no basis for the pleas.

But Judge Amanda Rippon said sentence could not be passed until after her co-accused’s trial.

The judge told Caile: “You have now pleaded guilty to two very serious offences, but you will have to wait until after the trial of your co-defendant.

“In his case, it remains a trial next July.”

The judge asked for preparation a Probation Service report on the defendant prior to sentence being passed.

She told Caile the report should consider all sentencing options, including custody.

Caile, 38, of Denewood Close, Willington, was bailed to return for sentencing after the trial of 39-year-old McKenzie, of the same address.

McKenzie remains on bail ahead of his trial on July 12, 2027.
