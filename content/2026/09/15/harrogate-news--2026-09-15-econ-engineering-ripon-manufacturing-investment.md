**RIPON, North Yorkshire**— Econ Engineering has strengthened its position as a leading UK manufacturing company following a significant £1.1m investment into its production capabilities.

The UK’s leading manufacturer of winter maintenance and specialist highways vehicles has purchased a state-of-the-art Bystronic ByStar 6225 laser cutting system. The industry-leading machine features an upgraded 12kW laser head, helping to increase cutting performance and making manufacturing quicker and more efficient compared to existing equipment.

The new laser cutter also introduces advanced mixed gas cutting technology, enabling Econ to process an even wider range of materials and thicknesses in-house. Following installation at Econ’s manufacturing headquarters in Ripon, North Yorkshire, specialist engineers have overseen final commissioning with the machine now fully operational.


**Thomas Lupton, Quality & Compliance Director at Econ Engineering, said:**

At Econ, continued investment in our people, facilities and technology is central to delivering the highest quality products and service for our customers. This significant £1.1m investment into state-of-the-art laser cutting machinery further enhances our manufacturing capacity and flexibility, supports more complex fabrication requirements and strengthens our ability to respond quickly to evolving customer needs.



The investment comes as Econ launches *Built in the UK | Built for the UK*, a campaign exploring the people, skills, partnerships, innovation and resilience behind UK manufacturing, and the value they create for communities and the economy.


**Thomas Lupton added:**

For more than five decades, Econ has been designing, engineering and manufacturing winter maintenance and highways vehicles here in the UK, supporting local authorities and contractors responsible for keeping roads safe and communities connected. UK manufacturing does far more than build products. It creates skilled jobs, strengthens communities, supports innovation and helps keep essential public services running.



Econ is the country’s leading manufacturer of gritters and snow ploughs, with 85% of the winter maintenance vehicles supplied to local councils and private contractors coming from its North Yorkshire-based factory. The firm has been established for more than 55 years.
