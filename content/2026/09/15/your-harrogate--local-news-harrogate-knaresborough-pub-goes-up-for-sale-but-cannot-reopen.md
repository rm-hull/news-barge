A former Knaresborough pub has gone up for sale - with a condition that it cannot reopen as a pub.

The Wellington Inn on Briggate is being marketed by Fleurets for £325,000, however its listing states that the freehold property is for “non-pub use only”.

The former Samuel Smith’s pub has been closed since May 2022.

Although it still has a bar, lounge and kitchen, a buyer would need to find another use for the building.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aa9052b4e365c56f6c.jpg)

The property also includes accommodation upstairs, a rear yard, small garden and large detached outbuilding.

Fleurets says a car park across the road has room for around six to eight vehicles.

No plans for the building’s future have been announced.
