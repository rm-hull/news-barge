A car travelling at speed down a Teesside residential street overturned and ploughed into parked vehicles and a house this morning.

Emergency services were called to Braemar Road in Billingham at around 2.49am on Tuesday, (September 15) following reports of a collision.

A spokesperson for the service said: "Cleveland Fire Brigade was called at 2.49am today (September 15) to Braemar Road, Billingham.

"Two appliances from Stockton Fire Station attended.

"The incident involved a vehicle which had crashed into a house.

"Fire crews made the area safe. The stop was received at 7.47am."

Residents said the vehicle was travelling at speed along Braemar Road when it clipped a kerb, overturned and hit two parked cars.

A spokesperson for Cleveland Police said: "In the early hours of today (around 2am on Tuesday 15th September) police received a report of a vehicle in the Billingham area which had rolled, hit another vehicle and then been driven away.

"On officers’ arrival, they located the vehicle (which was discovered to have been stolen earlier that night from a property in Norton), on The Causeway."

One of the parked cars was reportedly pushed into a gas box outside a nearby property, causing a small gas leak and prompting the emergency response from Cleveland Fire Brigade.

Witnesses claimed the car then came back onto its wheels before leaving the scene.

The spokesperson for Cleveland Police added: "Enquiries to trace those responsible are underway and we would appeal for anyone with information to contact Cleveland Police online via the website or by phoning 101 quoting ref 185255."
