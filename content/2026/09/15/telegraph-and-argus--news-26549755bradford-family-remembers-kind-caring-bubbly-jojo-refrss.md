A WOMAN who died too soon is being remembered through a fundraiser for her final journey.

Jojo, from Leeds, died suddenly at the age of 26 after suffering a brain aneurysm.

A fundraiser has been launched by her cousin, Kylie Pybus, to help cover funeral costs and give her "the best send-off."

Ms Pybus said: "Jojo was sadly take too soon at the age of 26 from an unexpected aneurism on the brain.

"Jojo was a kind, caring, loving and bubbly person who would do anything for anyone.

"She had a heart of gold."

Donations can be made via this LINK.

Jojo had recently found love with her fiancée Danielle and had taken on a fatherly role for Danielle’s three children, fulfilling her dream of having a family.

Although she lived in Bradford, she spent much of her life in Leeds with family and friends.

The fundraiser, titled Jojo’s Final Journey, describes her as a daughter, sister, auntie, dad, wife-to-be, and "and a above all that she was a best friend to us all."

Ms Pybus said: "Please help us give Jojo the best send-off we all know that she deserves."

Jojo’s funeral service is due to take place on September 30.
