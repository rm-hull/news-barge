A MAIN road in North Yorkshire is currently closed following a serious incident.

Emergency services are currently dealing with a serious collision on the A171 near Helwath Beck between Whitby and Scarborough.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said motorists are urged to avoid the area until further notice.

They added: "Please follow the local diversions.

"Witnesses or anyone with relevant dashcam footage of the incident should call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quote reference NYP-15092026-0449."

A number of people have commented on social media that the incident involved a motorcyclist.

They also say they have been told the road is expected to be closed for several hours.
