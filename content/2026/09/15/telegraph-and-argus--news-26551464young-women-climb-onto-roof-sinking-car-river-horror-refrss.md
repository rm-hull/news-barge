Two young women were reportedly forced to climb onto the roof of their vehicle after it left a bridge in the Yorkshire Dales and ended up in the River Dee, with the water level continuing to rise around them.

This morning, a white car could be seen lying partially submerged beneath Barth Bridge in Dent, just a short distance from the well-known Dales Way. The Grade II-listed structure spans the river and dates back to the later 18th century.

The horror is believed to have unfolded on the evening of Monday, September 14.

The vehicle appears to have broken through a series of wooden posts before plunging into the water.

A spokesperson from Cumbria Police confirmed: "Our log read, caller saying they - two young women - were on top of their car in the river and the river was rising. Police and fire attended. They were rescued by fire at around 9.15pm."

It has not been confirmed whether the driver suffered any injuries.

The incident prompted concern among local residents, with people using Facebook to ask about the occupants of the car.
