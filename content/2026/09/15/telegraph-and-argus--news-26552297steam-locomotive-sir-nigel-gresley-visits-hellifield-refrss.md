Iconic steam locomotive 60007 Sir Nigel Gresley passed through the Bradford district today.

The historic loco, which was being pulled by a Class 37 heritage diesel locomotive 37667, passed Keighley at about 1.45pm before making its way through Bingley, Shipley and Apperley Bridge.

There was a good turnout at Hellifield Station, where the loco spent an hour, ensuring a good trade at the popular Shed24H cafe.

*(Image: Lesley Tate)*

The LNER Pacific locomotive was built by the Great Northern Railway/London and North Eastern Railway and first used in 1937, and is now owned by the Sir Nigel Gresley Locomotive Trust.

She was mainly based at London King's Cross and holds the official post-war speed record for steam traction of 112mph, in May 1959, as proudly displayed on a plaque on its side.

Sir Nigel Gresley, a sister to Mallard, was on her way to the annual Grosmont Steam Gala at the North Yorkshire Moors Railway, which takes place from September 24 to September 26.

She will be one of three visiting locomotives at the event also including T3 Class No. 563 and No. 4079 Pendennis Castle.
