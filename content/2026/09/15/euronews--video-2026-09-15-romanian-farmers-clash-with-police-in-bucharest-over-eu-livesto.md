Footage shows protesters **pushing and scuffling** with security forces,**throwing objects** and**knocking down barriers** before gendarmes deployed**tear gas**.

The demonstration began peacefully in **Victory Square (Piața Victoriei)**, where farmers gathered to protest problems affecting the** livestock and farming sectors**. Tensions later escalated when some protesters tried to** block roads**around the square and confronted gendarmes, who responded with** tear gas**.

The protest was organised by **Romanian sheep farmers** and other agricultural producers, with several hundred to more than**1,000 people** taking part. Organisers had called on protesters to bring**sheep, tractors and hay bales** to the square, but restrictions prevented**live animals and heavy machinery** from entering the demonstration area.

Farmers were protesting an **EU-wide ban on exports of live sheep and goats** from Romania, extended**until 31 December 2026** following outbreaks of**peste des petits ruminants** and**sheep pox.** They also called for**better compensation for animals culled** because of the outbreaks and for crops affected by**drought**.

Protesters further demanded the **resignation of Alexandru Bociu**, head of Romania’s National Sanitary Veterinary and Food Safety Authority (ANSVSA), over his handling of the** epidemiological situation**.

At least **three protesters** required medical attention after being exposed to**tear gas** and were treated at the scene. No law enforcement officers were reported injured, while**two participants** were taken to a police station for questioning.

Traffic around the square, including the **Victoria Underpass and Kiseleff Boulevard**, was** blocked for several hours** before being reopened.
