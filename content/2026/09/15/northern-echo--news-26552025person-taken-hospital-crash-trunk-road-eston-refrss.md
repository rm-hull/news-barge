One person has been taken to hospital after a crash on a busy Middlesbrough road.

Emergency services were called to Trunk Road in Eston after a crash at around 4.53pm on Monday (September 14).

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) sent two crews to the scene.

A spokesperson for NEAS said: "We were called to reports of a road traffic collision on Trunk Road in Middlesbrough at 4.53pm on 14 September.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews and took one patient to James Cook Hospital for further treatment."

The extent of the person's injuries has not been disclosed.

A spokesperson for Cleveland Police said: "Officers attended a two-vehicle collision just before 5pm yesterday in the Church Lane / A66 area of Eston.

"Our Fire Brigade and ambulance service colleagues attended and both vehicles were recovered by 6.20pm."

Anyone with any information related to the incident should contact Cleveland Police using the online reporting and services portal.
