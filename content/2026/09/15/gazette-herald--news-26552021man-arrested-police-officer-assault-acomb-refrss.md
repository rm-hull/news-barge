A MAN has been arrested after allegedly assaulting a police officer in a busy York high street.

North Yorkshire Police have arrested a 44-year-old man on suspicion of criminal damage, a public order offence and assaulting a police officer in Green Lane, Acomb.

The incident happened at 1.18pm on Tuesday (September 15).

  


A police spokesperson said: " “We received a report of a man behaving aggressively on Green Lane, Acomb, this afternoon.

"Our officers went to the scene, and arrested a 44-year-old man on suspicion of criminal damage, a public order offence and assaulting a police officer.”

An eye-witness at the scene said: "I could see at least three police officers wearing blue gloves seeing to someone in the back of a police van shortly before 1.20pm.

"The van had its blue lights on, but it wasn't clear what the officers were responding to.

"The police presence stopped several shoppers in their tracks."
