A man has been arrested following a drugs raid at a property in Bradford.

West Yorkshire Police carried out a search warrant at an address on Daisy Street in Great Horton yesterday (Monday, September 14).

A 36-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of producing a class B drug.

The force says he has been released under investigation whilst enquiries are carried out.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "A 36 year old man has been arrested after a warrant was executed at an address on Daisy Street, Bradford yesterday.

"The man was arrested on suspicion of producing a class B drug.

"He's been released under investigation whilst enquiries are carried out."
