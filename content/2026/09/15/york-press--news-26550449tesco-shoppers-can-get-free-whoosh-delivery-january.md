Tesco shoppers can get a £25 grocery order delivered for free under a new limited-time offer - and there is a simple way to qualify.

The supermarket is offering free Whoosh delivery on orders over £25 to customers who sign up to an Anytime Delivery Saver plan by October 18, 2026.

The deal launches today, September 14, and runs until January 11, 2027, meaning shoppers can use the offer through Christmas and into the New Year.

Importantly, the £25 is the minimum order value – Tesco is not giving away £25 worth of groceries. Instead, shoppers who spend at least £25 on a qualifying Whoosh order will pay no delivery charge.


## How to get free Tesco Whoosh delivery

The offer is available to existing Anytime Delivery Saver members automatically, while new customers need to sign up to an Anytime Delivery Saver plan before October 18.

Once signed up, customers can get free Whoosh delivery on orders over £25 until January 11.

Anytime Delivery Saver plans cost:

- £7.99 a month for six months
- £6.99 a month for 12 months

Tesco says the 12-month option saves 13% compared with buying two six-month plans.

The subscription also includes free delivery and collection slots for regular online grocery shopping, priority access to Christmas and Easter slots, and the ability to book weeks in advance.

Tesco says Anytime plans provide average savings of £176 on delivery and collection charges, based on one weekly £5 slot compared with a 12-month Anytime plan.

There is also a Delivery Saver guarantee, under which shoppers receive coupons for the difference if they do not save on delivery charges.


## Free Whoosh deliveries through Christmas

The deal could be particularly useful for shoppers who find themselves short of an ingredient or need a last-minute supermarket top-up.

Tesco's Whoosh service promises delivery from store to door in 20 to 60 minutes, although it is only available in selected areas.

Francesca Jones, Head of Whoosh at Tesco, said: “We’re always looking for ways to make shopping easier for our customers. By bringing these offers together, customers can enjoy the benefits of one membership across both their weekly grocery shop and those last-minute essentials delivered through Whoosh.

"With Whoosh now covering three-quarters of the UK, more customers than ever can get what they need delivered fast and with no added delivery charge.”

Customers should check that Whoosh is available in their area before relying on the service.

The offer excludes Marketplace and F&F orders, and Tesco's fair usage policy applies.

The key deadline for new customers is October 18, 2026 – miss it and they will not qualify for this particular free Whoosh delivery promotion.
