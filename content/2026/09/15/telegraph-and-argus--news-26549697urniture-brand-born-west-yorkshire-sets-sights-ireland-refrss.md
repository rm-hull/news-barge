A UK firm has expanded into a new market, marking a milestone in its growth plans.

Bedkingdom, an e-commerce retailer based in Liversedge, West Yorkshire, has launched its brand in Ireland, where customers can now access 90 per cent of its own-brand bedroom and home furniture products through a dedicated website.

Levon Hall, chief operating officer at Bedkingdom, said: "When we started looking at where to launch the business next, Ireland was the obvious choice because of its close proximity to the UK.

"We’ve done a lot of research, and Irish people tend to take a lot of pride in their homes, with a big culture of hospitality and bringing family together, which made it the perfect fit."

The expansion follows six months of planning and preparation, with the Irish website launching in early September.

Customers in Ireland can shop without incurring additional duties or taxes, and orders over €250 qualify for free delivery.

Products will initially be shipped from the UK, but Bedkingdom plans to establish a warehouse in Ireland in the future to support faster delivery and expand its operations.

Mr Hall said: "There’s been a lot of hoops to jump through with preparing to enter a completely new market, especially post Brexit which has added a layer of complication for retailers that want to ship products outside the UK.

"As well as our owned brands, we stock several ranges from other furniture manufacturers and suppliers, and hope to establish some partnerships with new suppliers that want to access the Irish market."

The move comes as the company continues to grow, with 54 per cent year-on-year organic traffic growth and ongoing commercial success.

Bedkingdom was founded by Ashley Hainsworth, who began selling mattresses online while studying at Huddersfield University with an initial investment of £400.

The business now operates three warehouses in West Yorkshire and generates more than £28 million in revenue across the Flair Furniture Group.

The company was featured in the Sunday Times Top 100 in 2023 and placed 18th in the Ward Hadaway Yorkshire Fastest 50 in 2025.
