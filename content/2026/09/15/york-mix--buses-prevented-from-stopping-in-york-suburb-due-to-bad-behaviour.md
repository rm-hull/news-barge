Buses were prevented from stopping in a York suburb last night (Monday) due to youths causing trouble.

Services didn’t go to Chapelfields due to the anti-social behaviour.

A message was sent to all bus drivers not to stop at Chapelfields until further notice due to the problems.

Now ward councillors have issued an update on the situation

Last night’s decision caused anger among bus users in the area. One posted on Facebook: “This is absolutely disgusting.

“You kids need to stop doing this. You know who you are. The elderly people and disabled people are suffering because of you all – hope your parents are proud of you.”

Another wrote: “There are a lot of people to blame not just the kids and their parents or the terrible way its being policed.”

Westfield councillors have been investigating the issue.

They posted last night: “We can confirm that there were concerns regarding a large group of youths causing a nuisance at the Chapelfields terminus.”

For anti social incidents “drivers and supervisors are now following the following procedures”.

“First bus driver to contact or speak to a manager to report the anti-social behaviour incident and discuss any intended diversion, regardless of the time of day.

“Report the ASB incident to the police via 101 (telephone or online at Contact us | Police.uk), or 999 in an emergency. This is essential in helping the police build an accurate picture of ASB across the city.

“Contact Customer Services to ensure the diversion is published on the website. It is important that customers are informed when services are being diverted.”

The councillors added: “We appreciate how frustrating this is for residents and how important the Chapelfields terminus is and will continue to work as constructively as possible with both North Yorkshire Police and First Bus.”
