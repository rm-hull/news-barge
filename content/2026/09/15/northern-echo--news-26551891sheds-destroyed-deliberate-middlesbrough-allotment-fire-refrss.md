A fire thought to have been started deliberately destroyed two sheds and fencing at allotments in Middlesbrough last night.

Firefighters were called to the Lime Road allotments at 8.13pm on Monday, September 14.

Three fire engines attended the incident, including one from Middlesbrough and two from Grangetown.

Firefighters battled the blaze for more than three hours before bringing it under control.

A Cleveland Fire Brigade spokesperson said: "Cleveland Fire Brigade was called at 11.13pm yesterday (14.09.2026) to allotments at Lime Road, Middlesbrough.

"Three appliances attended, one from Middlesbrough and two from Grangetown.

"The fire destroyed two metal sheds and fencing.

"The stop was received at 11.41pm. The cause is believed to be deliberate."

The full extent of the damage is not known, but firefighters confirmed that two sheds were destroyed.
