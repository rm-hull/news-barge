NHS leaders are set to give updates on neighbourhood health.

The NHS West Yorkshire Integrated Care Board (ICB) will meet in public on Tuesday, September 22 at West Yorkshire Manufacturing Services, Kirkdale House, Armytage Road, Brighouse.

NHS West Yorkshire ICB will also hold its fourth Annual General Meeting (AGM) on the same day, beginning with refreshments at 12.15pm before the meeting starts at 1pm.

The AGM will be followed by the public board meeting, which includes updates from the board’s independent chair Mark Chamberlain and chief executive Jonathan Webb.

A spokesperson said: "The meeting in public will include an update report from Mark and Jonathan, as well as a focus on risk management and the Board Assurance Framework, on finance and performance, including the West Yorkshire Joint Capital Resource Plan for 2026/27, and on neighbourhood health."

The board is part of the West Yorkshire Health and Care Partnership.

To attend the AGM, contact [westyorkshireics.governance@nhs.net](mailto:westyorkshireics.governance@nhs.net) by 5pm on Monday, September 21.

Agenda questions can be submitted by 9am the same day.
