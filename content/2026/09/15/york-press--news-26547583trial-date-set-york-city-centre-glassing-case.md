A woman charged in connection with an alleged daytime “glassing” in York city centre did not attend court when her case had a hearing.

Sarah Ogden, 50, of James Street, York, is accused of wounding another woman with intent to cause her grievous bodily harm and affray, both allegedly committed in the afternoon of August 17 in St Saviourgate.

She was remanded in custody by York magistrates last month who also sent her to the crown court because the first charge can only be heard by a judge and jury.

When her case was called on for a plea and trial preparation hearing at York Crown Court, Recorder Paul Reid was told that she was too ill to attend.

He discussed the case with prosecution and defence barristers and set a trial date for February 22.

He also ordered that Ogden attend a hearing on November 5. He remanded her in custody until then.

Ogden was charged following an incident early in the afternoon of August 17 which resulted in a woman being taken to hospital with alleged head injuries.
