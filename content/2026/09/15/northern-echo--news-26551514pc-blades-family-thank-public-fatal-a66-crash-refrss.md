The family of a police officer tragically killed in the horror A66 crash has issued a heartfelt message thanking the public and emergency services for their support.

In a statement released following his funeral, Mr Blades' family said the kindness shown to them by the community, friends, colleagues and emergency services had helped them through one of the most difficult periods of their lives.

  PC Matthew Blades


The family said: "As a family, we would like to say a heartfelt thank you to every single person who has supported us following the devasting loss of Matt.

"The kindness love and support we have received from our community, friends, the emergency services, the police family, colleagues and so many people we may never even met has meant more to us than words can express.

"The day of Matt's funeral was incredibly difficult, but seeing the overwhelming respect and support shown for him, and for all of us a family, will stay with us forever.

"The powerful presence of so many people, the tributes, the silence, the claps, the uniforms, the blue lights and every gesture of respect shows just how much Matt meant to so many.

  The funeral of PC Matthew Blades was held at St Joseph's Roman Catholic Church in Hartlepool.


"To everyone who stood along the route, attended the funeral, sent messages, brought flowers, checked in on us, or simply kept us in your thoughts -thank you.

"To our police family and all the emergency services who stood behind us, both professionally and personally, we will never forget the strength and compassion you have shown.

"We are taking each day as it comes, but knowing we are surrounded by so much love and support has helped carry us through the darkest of days.

"Matt will always be loved, always be remembered, and will never be forgotten.

"From the bottom of our hearts, thank you."

The tribute comes after hundreds of people lined the streets to pay their respects to PC Blades as his funeral cortege passed through the area, with colleagues from across the emergency services joining family and friends in honouring his memory.

  The funeral of PC Matthew Blades was held at St Joseph's Roman Catholic Church in Hartlepool.


A white floral tribute spelling “Dad” rested on the hearse, while his white police cap sat above the Union Jack-draped coffin.

Officers, wearing black armbands embroidered with the names of PC Blades and Clough, embraced as they reflected on their colleague's life.

A fundraiser for the families of PCs Blades and Clough raised more than £1.2m after the crash, while officers from forces across the country were drafted in to help across Cleveland.

44,000 people donated to the fundraiser, created by Cleveland Police Federation which closed on (September 1), at 9am, raising £1,203,932.

Organisers said money raised would be split equally between the two officers’ families, while the federation would continue to provide support.

PC Blade's funeral took place on Emergency Services Day, a national day of remembrance for those who have died in the line of duty.
