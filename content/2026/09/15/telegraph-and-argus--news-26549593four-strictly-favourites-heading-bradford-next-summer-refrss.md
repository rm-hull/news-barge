A HIGH-ENERGY dance show featuring four stars from Strictly Come Dancing is heading to Bradford next summer.

Here Come The Boys will arrive at Bradford Live on June 9 as part of a 24-date UK tour, starring Aljaž Škorjanec, Carlos Gu, Gorka Marquez, and Julian Caillon.

The show promises an evening of world-class choreography, music, and entertainment, with ballroom, Latin, and contemporary routines performed by the "self-proclaimed rock stars of dance."

Aljaž Škorjanec said: "I genuinely think Here Come The Boys is going to be one of the must-see shows of 2027.

"We’re putting everything into making this our biggest, most exciting show yet, and I can’t wait for audiences to experience what we’ve got planned."

Gorka Marquez said: "It’s brilliant to be sharing the stage with three of my Strictly favourites.

"We all bring something different to the show, and that mix of personalities, styles, and a little friendly competition is going to make it incredibly exciting—for us and for the audience."

The Bradford date is one of several across the north of England, including stops in Manchester, Sheffield, Carlisle, and Gateshead.

Tickets go on general sale from 10am on September 18 via herecometheboys.com.
