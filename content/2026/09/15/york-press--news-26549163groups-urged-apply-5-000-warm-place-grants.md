Community organisations across York are being encouraged to apply for grants of up to £5,000 to help provide warm, welcoming spaces for residents during the winter months.

The Good Place, Warm Place Fund, run by City of York Council, offers a total of £40,000 to support organisations delivering or planning to deliver Warm Places this winter.

Councillor Katie Lomas said: "Warm Places provide much more than somewhere warm to sit during the winter months.

"They offer friendship, support and a sense of belonging, helping people stay connected with their local community at a time when many may be feeling isolated or facing financial pressures.

"By working together with our local voluntary and community sector, we can ensure residents have access to welcoming spaces where they can feel supported, make connections and get help when they need it most."

Warm Places must be open for at least two hours each week and are designed to offer not just warmth but also social connection and practical support.

Many venues also provide advice on managing household costs, maximising income and accessing support services.

Applications are now open and close at midnight on Sunday October 4.

Those interested can email [shapingneighbourhoods@york.gov.uk](mailto:shapingneighbourhoods@york.gov.uk) to request an application form.
