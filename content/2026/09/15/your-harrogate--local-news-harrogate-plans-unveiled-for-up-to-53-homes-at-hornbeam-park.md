Plans for up to 53 homes on land beside the Hornbeam Park Estate have been opened for public consultation.

Hornbeam Park Developments Ltd is preparing an outline planning application for the site west of Hornbeam Park Avenue, between the hospice and the railway.

The proposals include a mix of one to four-bedroom homes.

Up to 21 would be affordable under a Section 106 agreement, with a further 32 potentially available as affordable homes, subject to agreement with North Yorkshire Council.

The plans also show access from Hornbeam Park Avenue, public open space, a woodland walk and improvements to an existing public footpath.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aa9374cf0e72d2a7ac.jpg)

The developer says every home could have solar panels or an air-source heat pump.

The layout is illustrative. An outline application would establish whether housing is acceptable on the site, with details such as access, layout and appearance considered later.

Residents and businesses can comment through the **consultation page** or email [consultation@johnsonmowat.co.uk](mailto:consultation@johnsonmowat.co.uk) by 29th September 2026.

The developer says it will review responses before submitting an application to North Yorkshire Council.
