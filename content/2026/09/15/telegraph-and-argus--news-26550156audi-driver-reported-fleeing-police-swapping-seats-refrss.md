A driver was reported after fleeing police before swapping seats with a passenger, West Yorkshire Police has said.

Officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team were patrolling Halifax Road when they saw the Audi S3.

Police said the driver reacted to officers’ blue lights and sirens by driving away at speed.

About 15 minutes later, colleagues found the car travelling through Bingley towards Bradford.

It stopped when officers activated their blue lights again.

Police said a conversation with the occupants showed the driver had moved into the passenger seat.

He was later reported for driving without insurance and failing to stop for police.

The Audi was recovered because it had been driven without insurance.
