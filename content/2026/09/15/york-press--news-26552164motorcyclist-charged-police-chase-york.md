A motorcyclist has been charged following a dramatic police chase through the streets of York after failing to stop for officers.

Officers began pursuing a Honda 400 motorcycle without a registration plate on Lindsey Avenue at about 3.45pm on Sunday.

The rider, in his 20s, did not stop, prompting a police chase.


Following national guidelines, a spokesman for North Yorkshire Police said officers "used tactical contact on Water End to bring the motorcycle to a safe stop".

The motorcyclist was unharmed during the incident which caused minor damage to a police vehicle.

The rider tested positive for cannabis in a drugs test at the roadside, and was arrested on suspicion of failing to stop, driving offences and drug-driving.

The man failed to provide a specimen for further analysis while in custody.

He has since been charged with using a motor vehicle without insurance, driving otherwise than in accordance with a licence, careless driving, failing to stop for police, and failing to provide a specimen for analysis.

He has been released on bail and will appear before magistrates at a later date.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We continue to target those who use the North Yorkshire's roads illegally and put other road users at risk.

"Officers are trained to use a range of tactics, where lawful, proportionate and necessary, to safely bring incidents to a conclusion and protect the public."
