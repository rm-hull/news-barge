A NIGHTCLUB believed to be the oldest still running in the UK has been honoured with a blue plaque.

The Acapulco in Halifax, affectionately known as The Acca, has received the tribute from Halifax Civic Trust to mark 65 years of continuous operation.

The plaque was unveiled on Monday, 14 September 2026, by the Mayor of Calderdale, Councillor Andrew Tagg.

Cllr Tagg said: "Happy 65th birthday to the Acapulco! The Blue Plaque is a fitting celebration for this milestone anniversary, and for the part that the legendary nightclub plays in Halifax’s history and identity.

"For decades, the Acca has brought generations of people together and created countless memories, including my own.

"It has been, and continues to be, far more than just a place to enjoy a night out – it has become somewhat of an institution adding to our local heritage.

"Preserving and recognising places like this helps us tell the story of Halifax."

  The Blue Plaque 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Located at 3 Waterhouse Street, Halifax, the Acapulco first opened its doors in 1961.

It was founded by Ted Turczak and his wife Stephanie, who later passed the business to their children Chez and Chris Turczak in the late 1980s.

Simon Jackson, who began working at the club as a DJ in the 1980s, took over the business in 1995.

The club is now widely recognised as the oldest continuously operating nightclub in the United Kingdom.

Halifax Civic Trust awarded the blue plaque in recognition of the club’s contribution to the town’s social and cultural heritage.

Alan Goodrum, chair of Halifax Civic Trust, said: "It was so good to see so many at the unveiling of the Blue Plaque from all generations – the nightclub means such a lot to the cultural scene in Halifax and Ted Turcazk deserves to be commemorated in his adopted town.

"Thanks too, to the long-time owner Simon Jackson and Halifax BID for making this happen. Halifax has so much to offer young and those not so young."

Halifax BID nominated the Acapulco for the honour.

Manager Natalie Shaw said: "Halifax BID is incredibly proud to have nominated the Acapulco for a Halifax Civic Trust Blue Plaque.

"The Acapulco is so much more than a nightclub – it is part of Halifax’s history and has been at the heart of the town’s nightlife for generations.

"At a time when the night-time economy is facing real challenges nationally, we think it is particularly important to celebrate and share positive stories like this."
