Amanda Owen is set to give fans a first look at the latest series of her family’s hit TV show.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess will host a special behind-the-scenes event in Northallerton later this month, offering an exclusive preview of series four of Our Farm Next Door.

Taking place at Everyman Northallerton on Wednesday, September 30, the event will celebrate the More4 show and give fans a glimpse of what’s to come.

Ms Owen will be joined by special guests to discuss how the programme began and how it has followed the Owen family’s restoration of Anty John’s farmhouse.

The evening will also include a look back at key moments from the first three series and explore how the show is produced in one of the most remote parts of the Yorkshire Dales.

Our Farm Next Door follows Ms Owen, her former husband Clive, and their nine children as they farm in Swaledale and transform the derelict Anty John’s property.

Channel 4 confirmed in August 2025 that two more series had been commissioned following the show’s success on More4.

The broadcaster said the upcoming episodes would continue to follow the family’s lives as work on the property moves indoors, covering decisions about rooms, plumbing, and running water.

Cameras will also keep capturing daily life on the farm.

Series three aired earlier this year and featured Clive’s 70th birthday and other major family moments.

Lime Pictures reported that the second episode of series three grew its audience and ranked third among the week’s top 30 multichannel programmes.

The Northallerton event will give fans a chance to see how the show is made before the new series airs.

Leeds-based Wise Owl Films has documented the family’s journey through the renovation project, capturing life at both Ravenseat and Anty John’s across changing seasons and all weather conditions.

Organisers say the show reflects a time of significant change for the Owen children as they grow up, explore new interests, and start careers of their own.

The nine children—Raven, Reuben, Miles, Edith, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine, and Nancy—have each taken on different roles and responsibilities as the series has progressed.

Reuben has pursued a career in heavy machinery and groundwork, while Raven has taken a different path away from the family farm.

At the heart of the show is Anty John’s, the historic farmhouse near Ravenseat that the Owens have been restoring.

The project began with a derelict building and has involved painstaking work to turn it into a family home.

Channel 4 has described the purchase as part of the family’s long-term plans.

Ms Owen has also investigated the property’s historical links to Anthony Clarkson, a historic Swaledale map-maker.

Executive producer Mark Robinson said the project tells "the story of a family rooted in the Yorkshire Dales bringing a house back to life".

That story will continue in series four.

The Northallerton event, titled Our Farm Next Door: A Behind-the-Scenes Celebration, will take place at Everyman Northallerton at the Treadmills development on East Road on September 30.

Tickets are priced at £12.50 and are available through the Royal Television Society.

Attendees will hear from Ms Owen and her guests, see exclusive footage from series four, and learn more about how the programme is made.

The event comes after the Owen family rose to fame on Channel 5’s Our Yorkshire Farm, which ran until 2022.

They later returned to screens with Our Farm Next Door, which once again brought Ravenseat, Swaledale, and the transformation of Anty John’s into the spotlight.

Despite their separation in June 2022, Ms Owen and Mr Owen have continued to work together on the farm and co-parent their nine children.
