**HARROGATE** — Regional tourism leads and environmental specialists have detailed a new public event series on the edge of the town, unlocking backstage access to one of the North of England’s most prominent botanical archives.

The Royal Horticultural Society has officially announced a specialized “Meet the Experts” presentation program to celebrate the 25th anniversary of **RHS Garden Harlow Carr**. Running daily at 3pm from** Monday 21 September 2026**through to its conclusion on** Sunday 4 October 2026**, the initiative features a rolling calendar of informal 25-minute public lectures directed entirely by the estate’s internal team of professional horticulturists, arborists, and conservationists.

The anniversary project marks exactly a quarter of a century since the central RHS infrastructure assumed administrative stewardship of the historic Harrogate estate in 2001. Spanning over 60 acres of cultivated valley landscapes, the seasonal talks are designed to provide visitors with empirical supply chain insights, maintenance logs, and raw operational advice calibrated for the challenging northern growing climate.

## From Sub-Tropical Borders to Woodland Canopy Management

The 14-day scheduling introduces a diverse mix of soil science, plant trials, and landscape design breakdowns. Key items across the celebratory presentation schedule include:

- **Teaching Garden Developments (Mondays 21 & 28 September):** Specialized vocational training overviews and community education insights managed by Andrew Willocks.
- **Main Border Management (22, 26, 27 & 30 September):** Four distinct technical showcases directed by Emily Marston, tracking the upkeep and design transitions of the garden’s central visual axes.
- **New Streamside Project (Friday 25 September):** A dedicated project brief tracking the restoration of the nation’s longest planted streamside garden, led by Ben Rushworth at the Main Bridge.
- **Botanical Genus Trials (Thursday 24 September):** A scientific research review tracking the development of the active Disporum, Disporopsis, and Prosartes trials, directed by Molly Turgoose.
- **Arboretum and Woodlands (29 September & 3 October):** Forestry preservation logs and canopy ecosystem analyses presented by Katy Trohear and Steph Carr.


**Liz Thwaite, Head of Site at RHS Harlow Carr:**

As we celebrate our 25th anniversary, we’re delighted to offer visitors the chance to hear directly from the people who care for the garden every day. These talks will provide a fascinating glimpse behind the scenes, while sharing practical horticultural advice and seasonal inspiration that gardeners of all levels can enjoy.


## Access Logistics and Registry Protocols

Coordinators have confirmed that booking is not required for the specific anniversary talks, which are included as standard within normal garden admission tickets. Entry remains completely free for all active RHS members.

The wider estate grounds continue to operate on permanent autumn timings, opening daily from 9.30am to 6pm, with last public admission logged strictly at 5pm. Local families, landscape contractors, or regional tourism partners looking to review ticket concession slots, evaluate accessibility profiles, or access the master event index can connect directly via the central portal at RHS Garden Harlow Carr.
