NOMINATIONS are now open for a prestigious award ceremony.

The Markel 3rd Sector Care Awards recognise outstanding contributions in the not-for-profit health and care sector, including individuals and organisations in Bradford and across the UK.

Simon Johnston, UK marketing director at headline sponsor Markel, said: "Behind every thriving community are people and organisations working tirelessly to make a difference.

"From supporting those who need it most to bringing people together and creating lasting change, the not-for-profit sector plays a vital role across the UK."

The awards are open to anyone working in the sector, whether as part of a small community group or a larger organisation such as a hospice or care provider.

There are 13 categories, including a new category dedicated to mental health support.

Entries and nominations are free.

Lisa Werthmann, director at Care Management Matters (CMM), which produces the awards, said: "It is a special occasion that brings the sector together to recognise the dedication, compassion and achievements of those who make such a difference every day."

The ceremony will take place at Birmingham Town Hall on Friday, April 30, 2026.

Nominations close on November 13.

Details on how to enter are available on the Care Management Matters website.
