RITA, Sue and Bob Too is heading for Bradford, starring TV favourite Jake Quickenden.

The cult comedy began as a play, written by Buttershaw’s Andrea Dunbar at the age of 18.

Andrea wrote her first play, The Arbor, as a school assignment - it went on to premiere at London’s Royal Court Theatre in 1980 and was later staged in New York.

Commissioned for a second play, Andrea wrote Rita, Sue and Bob Too.

Another Royal Court hit, it led to the 1987 film, shot on the Buttershaw estate and other areas of Bradford.

Singer, actor and presenter Jake Quickenden returns to the role of Bob in the Regal Entertainments production of Rita, Sue and Bob Too.

Jake rose to fame as a contestant on The X Factor and was a runner-up on I’m a Celebrity...Get Me Out of Here.

He has since appeared in shows such as Celebrity MasterChef, The Real Full Monty: On Ice, Celebrity SAS Who Dares Wins and Dancing on Ice - lifting the trophy in 2018.

He co-hosted popular podcast Untapped and made his screen acting debut as Woody in Hollyoaks. His stage credits include Hair the Musical, Footloose and The Full Monty.

  Jake Quickenden is reprising the role of lothario Bob 

*(Image: David Munn)*

Said Jake: “I had such a great time when we toured with Rita, Sue & Bob Too! last year - audiences up and down the country were so enthusiastic.

“So when I was asked if I would like to return for a second UK tour, I jumped at the chance.

"Andrea Dunbar’s writing is brilliant, and the story is very funny but with a darker underside which dramatically really appeals to me.”

Further casting is yet to be announced.

The bawdy, gritty comedy is about two working-class girls from a council estate who are about to finish their final year at school. They earn cash babysitting for married couple Bob and Michelle, and both girls embark on a fling with lothario Bob.

When Bob later develops a preference for Rita, the two girls fall out but when Sue ends up in an abusive relationship the trio are reunited.

  The comedy is about two teenage girls who have an affair with a married man 

*(Image: David Munn)*

The production, which features 80s music, toured last year, receiving praise from critics and standing ovations from audiences.

Director Chantelle Joseph said: “We’ve had a fabulous reaction to news that Rita, Sue and Bob Too! is going back out on the road for a second UK tour.

“And I’m really delighted that Jake Quickenden has agreed to return in the role of Bob.

“He was a huge hit with audiences up and down the country and really understands the role, so I know this latest outing for Andrea Dunbar’s classic comedy is going to be absolutely brilliant. Roll on 2027!”

- Rita, Sue and Bob Too is at St George’s Hall from June 2-5, 2027. Visit bradford-theatres.co.uk or call (01274) 432000.
