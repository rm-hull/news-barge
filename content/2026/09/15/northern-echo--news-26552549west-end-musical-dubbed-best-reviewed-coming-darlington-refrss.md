One of the West End's biggest recent hits is coming to Darlington next year.

Award-winning musical Operation Mincemeat, based on an extraordinary Second World War spy mission, comes to Darlington Hippodrome from April 20 to 24, 2027.

In an announcement on social media, the Darlington theatre said: "Attention agents! Declassified intel just in.

"Operation Mincemeat - the best-reviewed show in West End history - is coming."

The musical tells how a secret mission helped change the course of the war by tricking Nazi Germany with a corpse carrying fake military plans.

It mixes comedy, espionage and history with original songs and a cast who play multiple roles.

Set in 1943, the show follows intelligence officers plotting to deceive Adolf Hitler and his commanders.

The production is one of London's most talked-about West End shows, described by its creators as "Singin' in the Rain meets Strangers on a Train, Noël Coward meets Noël Fielding."

  Operation Mincemeat heads to Darlington in April 2027. 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The musical has won widespread critical acclaim in the West End and has been billed as the best-reviewed show in West End history.

The show began as a fringe production at the Edinburgh Festival before transferring to London's West End, where it won the Olivier Award for Best New Musical.

The Darlington dates form part of a wider UK tour following the production's success in London.

Darlington Hippodrome members can buy tickets from Wednesday, September 23, with general sale opening on Friday, September 25.

Members also get 10 per cent off tickets and early access to preferred seats.

Operation Mincemeat will run at Darlington Hippodrome from April 20 to 24, 2027.
