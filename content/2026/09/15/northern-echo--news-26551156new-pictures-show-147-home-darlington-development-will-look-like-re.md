New images have revealed what a major new housing development on the edge of Darlington is expected to look like when completed.

Housebuilder Ashberry Homes has released a series of computer-generated images showing the design of properties at Serenity at Baydale Village, a 147-home development off Staindrop Road.

The scheme forms the first phase of a much larger project which could eventually create a new neighbourhood of up to 985 homes on the western edge of the town with the first residents expected to move in for spring 2027.

Images show a mix of traditional-style properties designed to reflect the character of the surrounding countryside.

Homes will feature country-style brickwork, red and grey roofs, rendered elevations on prominent plots and decorative brick detailing around windows.

  Solar panels, seen here on the three-bedroom Wisteria house type, will generate renewable energy for residents. 

*(Image: ASHBERRY HOMES)*

In total, the first phase will include 114 homes for private sale, ranging from two-bedroom bungalows to five-bedroom family houses.

Twenty-seven properties have been designed to be more accessible and adaptable for people with disabilities, while a further 33 homes will be provided as affordable housing.

Ashberry Homes says the development has been planned with green space and sustainability in mind.

Electric vehicle charging points will be installed and an orchard will be created within the site to encourage biodiversity and provide a community space for residents.

Emma Chesterton, sales director for Ashberry Homes, said: "We have now released the first homes for sale and are already seeing a considerable amount of interest from people looking for a new energy-efficient home on the edge of the picturesque Durham countryside, yet within easy reach of Darlington's many amenities."

Three and four-bedroom homes are currently available to reserve, with prices starting from £259,995.
