TWO teenagers were arrested after hiding in a bush during a police chase involving a reported stolen motorcycle.

Officers in Halifax pursued a 1,000cc Kawasaki after it reportedly failed to stop.

The rider lost control of the vehicle on Shroggs Road, abandoned the bike and ran away with the passenger, police say.

The force adds that the men, both 18, then hid in a nearby bush, before officers arrested them.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "The motorcycle was found to have been stolen from the Pudsey area last week and was displaying registration plates linked to another stolen motorcycle also from the Leeds area.

"Thanks to the swift actions of officers, the stolen motorcycle was recovered and the incident was brought to a safe conclusion with no injuries and no damage to the motorcycle.

The men have been released on bail while enquiries continue.
