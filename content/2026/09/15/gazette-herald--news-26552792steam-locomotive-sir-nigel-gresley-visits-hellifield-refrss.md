Iconic steam locomotive 60007 Sir Nigel Gresley visited a North Yorkshire village today.

The locomotive, which was being pulled by a diesel, spent an hour at the station, ensuring a good trade at the popular Shed24H cafe.

60007 Sir Nigel Gresley is a renowned member of the LNER A4 Pacific class, designed by the celebrated railway engineer Sir Nigel Gresley. Information about Sir Nigel and his Mallard can be found in the National Railway Museum in York.

  Sir Nigel Gresley was pulled by a diesel 

*(Image: Lesley Tate)*

The LNER Pacific locomotive was built by the Great Northern Railway/London and North Eastern Railway and first used in 1937, and is now owned by the Sir Nigel Gresley Locomotive Trust.

She was mainly based at London King's Cross and holds the official post-war speed record for steam traction of 112mph, in May 1959, as proudly displayed on a plaque on its side.

Sir Nigel Gresley was on her way to the annual Grosmont Steam Gala at the North Yorkshire Moors Railway, which takes place from September 24 to September 26.

She will be one of three visiting locomotives at the event also including T3 Class No. 563 and No. 4079 Pendennis Castle.
