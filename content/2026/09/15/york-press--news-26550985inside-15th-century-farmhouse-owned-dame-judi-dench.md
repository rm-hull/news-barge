Dame Judi Dench has returned with another instalment of her heartfelt series, Tea with Judi Dench, reuniting with Sir Ian McKellen.

In the episode, the pair discussed their careers, shared theatrical experiences, and notable film roles.

Dame Judi also broached more sensitive subjects, including her own health concerns.

Born in Heworth, Dame Judi attended The Mount School in York, a Quaker boarding and day school.

A Dame Judi Dench Walk can be found near Lendal Bride in York next to the River Ouse.

## 
  **Where does Judi Dench live now?** 

Dame Judi resides in an historic 15th-century farmhouse in the tranquil village of Outwood, Surrey.

Much of the property's original character remains intact.

Her six-acre property is also the setting for something deeply meaningful she has cultivated - a woodland dedicated to honouring individuals she has cherished and admired throughout her lifetime.

Through the years, she has planted trees as living memorials to her departed loved ones, transforming the grounds into a tranquil forest of remembrance.

The estate also features a separate cottage, swimming pool, workshops, barns, and a pond, and is thought to span approximately 7,000 square feet.

Her home, widely reported to be valued at £8 million, sits within an area renowned for its tranquil, mature woodland setting.

Nearby attractions include beauty spots such as Frensham Pond, Newland Corner, and Crooksbury Commons.
