A Teesside brewery known for charity beer festivals is heading to a new location this autumn to raise money for a lifesaving North East cause.

Three Brothers Brewing Company will hold its first Hutton Rudby beer festival from October 2 to 4, raising money for the Great North Air Ambulance Service.

The family-run Stockton microbrewery will hold the festival at Hutton Rudby Village Hall, with beer, cider, spirits, wine, prosecco and cocktails.

  Three Brothers Brewing Co. beer festival is coming to Hutton Rudby.


Live music will be played each evening, alongside a selection of street food to enjoy alongside a drink, and a charity quiz on the Sunday afternoon.

Three Brothers said its beer festivals have raised more than £28,000 for local charities since the brewery was founded in 2016.

Beth Wright from Three Brothers Brewing Company said: "Since we started in 2016, community has been a core part of our mission.

"We truly value how beer and beer festivals create connections and bring people together in a friendly, welcoming atmosphere.

"We are really excited to launch our debut Hutton Rudby Beer Festival."

  Three Brothers Brewing Co. beer festival is coming to Hutton Rudby.


Three Brothers recently collaborated on Glastonbeery, a three-day festival at Stockton's Georgian Theatre in June, which featured more than 20 cask and keg beers.

The brewery also celebrated a milestone last month as its Eaglescliffe Beer Festival returned for its 10th anniversary over the August bank holiday weekend.

  Three Brothers Brewing Co. beer festival is coming to Hutton Rudby.


Tickets for Hutton Rudby include a festival cup, event guide, £5 bar credit and a donation to the Great North Air Ambulance Service.

Visitors are urged to buy tickets in advance because entry on the day is not guaranteed.

The festival runs from noon until 11pm on Friday, October 2.

On Saturday, October 3, the festival will run two sessions, with the first running from 12pm to 5pm and the second from 6pm to 11pm.

Anyone attending both Saturday sessions must buy a separate ticket for each.

The festival wraps up on Sunday, October 4, running from 1pm to 8pm with the quiz starting at 3pm.

Beth said: "Cheers to a weekend of great company, amazing food and local brews for a life-saving cause."
