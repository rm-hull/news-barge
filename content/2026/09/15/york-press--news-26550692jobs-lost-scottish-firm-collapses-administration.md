A pioneering company has been placed into administration.

Smart Data Foundry Limited was dedicated to using financial data for community benefit employed 30 staff.

The company “experienced increasing financial pressure during 2026 as a result of a funding shortfall and ongoing cash flow constraints”.

Michelle Elliot and Callum Carmichael of FRP Advisory were appointed as joint administrators to the not-for-profit firm last month.

The company was established in 2022 “with the aim of unlocking the value of private sector financial data for public benefit through secure data sharing, research and analytics services”.

Its work sought to support organisations across the public, private and third sectors in addressing a range of societal challenges through the responsible use of data.

A spokesperson for the administrators said: “The company experienced increasing financial pressure during 2026 as a result of a funding shortfall and ongoing cash flow constraints.

“Despite exploring a range of alternative options, the company was ultimately unable to meet its financial obligations and the directors took steps to place the company into administration.

“Following the appointment, Smart Data Foundry Limited ceased trading and 25 employees were sadly made redundant.

The joint administrators have retained five employees for a short period to assist in preserving and safeguarding the company's assets, principally intellectual property.”

## 
  **Redundancy payments**

The joint administrators are “supporting affected employees in submitting claims to the Redundancy Payments Service”.

Since their appointment, the joint administrators have been working closely with key stakeholders to maximise value from the company's assets for the benefit of creditors, the spokesperson added.

The company's intellectual property is currently being assessed by specialist agents, and any interested parties are encouraged to contact FRP Advisory.

Michelle Elliot, partner at FRP and joint administrator, said: “The directors and management team worked hard over an extended period to secure a sustainable solution for the business, exploring a range of options before the company entered administration.

“Smart Data Foundry has made significant efforts to demonstrate how financial data can be used to support economic wellbeing objectives, including helping to address poverty and inequality.

“Our immediate focus is on supporting affected employees and preserving the value of the company's intellectual property. We are now exploring opportunities to realise value from the assets for the benefit of creditors and encourage any interested parties to contact us.”

One of the projects the company was working on was a prototype “festival planner” dashboard to support organisers of events such as the Edinburgh festivals, large concerts, and sporting fixtures like the Six Nations, which addresses transport and accommodation challenges.
