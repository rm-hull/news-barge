POLICE are appealing for help to find a man wanted over shop thefts and outstanding court warrants.

West Yorkshire Police said officers have carried out enquiries to locate Richard Thompson, 41.

He has connections to Shipley, Otley, Yeadon and Guiseley.

Police said anyone who can help find Thompson should call 101 or contact the force through LiveChat on its website.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for information to help locate Richard Thompson, aged 41, who is wanted for multiple counts of shop theft and outstanding court warrants.

The spokesperson added: "Anyone with information which may assist in locating Richard Thompson is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online via LiveChat on our website quoting reference 13260400226.

"Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111."
