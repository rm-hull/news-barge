Richard Hammond has spoken about the "uncomfortable" moment his daughter crashed a race car.

Izzy, 25, collided with a wall in a Formula E car earlier this year but escaped without injury.

Her father, who survived a nearly 300mph jet car crash in 2006, attended Ripon Grammar School and Harrogate College for his education.

He told the Metro: "It’s a little uncomfortable because I know from personal experience that things can and do go wrong.

"I’m a father, like any other father. I don’t want to see my little girl hurt."

Mr Hammond has appeared on Top Gear on the BBC and The Grand Tour on Amazon Prime Video alongside fellow presenters Jeremy Clarkson and James May.
