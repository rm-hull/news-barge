A RESTAURANT where celebrity chef Marco Pierre White once worked has reopened after a £500,000 refurbishment.

The Box Tree, located on Church Street in Ilkley, announced the reopening on social media as "a new chapter."

The post said: "We opened the doors to a new chapter of The Box Tree.

"Inspired by the landscape that surrounds us, our new bar and restaurant brings a little of the Yorkshire moors indoors: earthy, elegant and full of character, with thoughtful details waiting to be discovered.

"A new setting for the next chapter of our story, where Chef Patron Brayden Davies and the team are ready to welcome you.

"The doors are open. The tables are set. See you soon."

The restaurant welcomed diners back on September 9 after the works temporarily closed the venue over the summer.

## What are the works?

The refurbishment was carried out in partnership with Studio Two, an award-winning designers, and aimed to reimagine the restaurant’s interiors while preserving its heritage.

The Box Tree said the project marked "an exciting new chapter" while remaining "faithful to the heritage and character" that have defined it since 1962.

Ahead of the temporary closure, chef patron Brayden Davies created a special tasting menu titled "The Box Tree: Eras," featuring iconic dishes from the restaurant’s past.

The menu was served on July 11, 18, 25, and August 1, giving guests a final opportunity to dine at the restaurant before refurbishment work began.

## Box Tree history

The Box Tree occupies one of Ilkley’s oldest buildings, an 18th-century farmhouse dating to 1720.

It became known as Box Tree House in the late 19th century after businessman Septimus Wray planted the box trees that still give it its name.

The building has also been an antiques shop and tearoom.

Malcolm Reid and Colin Long opened The Box Tree as a restaurant in 1962, and it quickly became one of the North’s most celebrated dining destinations.

Chefs including Michael Truelove, Edward Denny and Marco Pierre White have helped shape its legacy.

Chef patron Brayden Davies remains at the helm.

The restaurant became the first Michelin-starred venue outside London and was awarded two Michelin stars.

Before taking charge at The Box Tree, Davies had spent six years working in four Michelin-starred restaurants, including Grantley Hall and Raby Hunt.
