A dispersal order has been enforced after a "stolen" black Jaguar was rammed into multiple homes.

The incidents are believed to have taken place in Stockton between 4.35am and 6am in the early hours of Tuesday morning (September 15).

Criminal damage was reported at two addresses in Roseworth and Tilery.

Several properties were found deliberately struck by a black Jaguar vehicle, which was reported stolen the evening before.

No injuries were reported.

An investigation has been launched, and the incidents are currently believed to be linked.

A 48-hour dispersal order has been put in place in the Tilery and Portrack areas which runs from 7pm tonight, Tuesday, September 15, to 7pm on Thursday, September 17.

A spokesperson from Cleveland Police said: “It gives officers the powers to ask people to leave the area for the specified time period.

"If they return, they could be arrested for breaching the order.

“The order is not to target those going about legitimate and lawful business.

"Anyone suspected of being involved in antisocial behaviour will be asked to leave the area.”

A 24-hour section 60 is also in place, which grants officers with extra powers to stop and search any individual when appropriate.

Officers can also remove any item from a person that they reasonably believe is being used to conceal their identity within the designated area under section 60AA.

Acting Superintendent Jamie Bell said: “I understand these incidents may cause some concern within the community, but I’d like to reassure residents that we are working at speed to identify those responsible and are using all the tools at our disposal.

“We are currently treating these incidents overnight as targeted and linked with no wider risk."
