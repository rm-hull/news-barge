A free comedy poetry competition launched by a North Yorkshire arts festival is helping to give humorous verse a platform, attracting hundreds of entries and inspiring the publication of a new book celebrating some of its best work.

The Wetherby Festival comedy poem competition has drawn more than 300 entries in its second year.

The competition began in 2024, with 164 entries in its first year, and has since grown to more than 300 submissions in its second year.

A spokesman for the Wetherby Festival said: "The quality blew me away.

"I was so pleased that between us, it felt like we had created an outlet that didn’t exist before.

"This book is a selection of our favourites from 2024 and 2025, but there were loads more we couldn’t fit in.

"Our poets surely are a talented bunch."

The competition was launched in partnership with Wetherby Festival after the organiser identified a lack of dedicated outlets for comedic poetry in the UK.

The spokesperson added: "Loads of poetry contests exist across the country, but I couldn’t find a single one specific to comedy."

The competition entry deadline has been extended to Wednesday, September 30 to give readers more time to submit.

  A range of other Wetherby Festival events are happening from October 2-10. For more information on the festival and tickets and for more on the comedy poem competition visit wetherbyfestival.co.uk.
