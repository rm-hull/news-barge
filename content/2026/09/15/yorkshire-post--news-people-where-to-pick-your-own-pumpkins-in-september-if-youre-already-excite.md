# Where to pick your own pumpkins in September – if you're already excited for Halloween

## Halloween is on the horizon, and pumpkin-carving is a rite of passage.

As of October, Yorkshire will be overflowing with pick-your-own pumpkin fields, from Horsforth to Harrogate.

But for those who can’t wait, there are a few spots opening early.

Here are the Yorkshire PYO pumpkin fields opening in September.

**Spilmans, Sessay, near Thirsk**

Spilmans Pumpkin Festival is launching on September 26.

Each autumn, the farm transforms into a family-friendly festival, with spooky talent shows, carving workshops, and walkabout characters.

![Spilman's Pumpkin Patch](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmE5MzI0ZDM2LTU1MTUtNGQzMy05Mzg0LWY5ZTBiZDlhMjIzMDpmYjljYzhmMC00NDdhLTQ5YTEtODU2MS02M2JkY2RmOTI1OGU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



**Balloon Tree Farm Shop, Gate Helmsley**

Balloon Tree Farm Shop is reopening at the end of September, with exact dates announced shortly.

Armed with wheelbarrows and wellies, visitors pay £2.50 in entrance fees and pick between tens of thousands of pumpkins.

![Balloon Tree Farm Shop & Cafe, Gate Helmsley](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmM1YjA3NDMyLTEzOWItNGQxNC1iNTI2LTEyYzhkYWQ3YWVkMDphOTEyMTZiOS1mNTAwLTQwMGYtYTliZi01YWYzMGYzZDg2ZWU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Balloon Tree has previously reported that special colours, like “Mellow Yellows,” are the quickest to sell out.

**Farmer Copleys, Pontefract** 

From September 26, visitors can pick between 40+ pumpkin varieties at Farmer Copleys, including bright-white “polar bears.”

![Farmer Copleys’ Pumpkin Festival returns this month.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjA3NmI0NzkwLWIwMzMtNDZiOS05NWE0LWNjMzIwY2UwOTgzOTplNGNiN2Y4MS02NTI1LTQ5ZDQtYjNlNy0wNjA4MjJmYTI3N2I=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Aside from 300,000 pumpkins to choose from, the festival will have tractor rides, live shows, and fancy dress competitions. Online tickets start at £7.89.

**Kemps Farm, Horsforth**

Kemps Farm is launching PYO pumpkins at its Horsforth location from September 26.

With 85,000 pumpkins of various shapes, sizes, and colours, visitors have plenty of options.

Tickets start at £4 per guest, including a tractor and trailer ride.
