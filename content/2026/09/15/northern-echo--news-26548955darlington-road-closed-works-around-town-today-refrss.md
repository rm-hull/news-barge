Drivers in Darlington face six roadworks schemes on Tuesday, September 15, including a full closure on Stanhope Road South.

Here's what you need to know:

### Stanhope Road South

Stanhope Road South is closed in both directions from Duke Street to Coniscliffe Road because of water main work.

Restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 16 at 11pm.


### Kendal Close

Kendal Close has water main work in both directions between A167 Grange Road and Blackwell Lane.

Restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 16 at 11pm.

### Auckland Oval

Construction is affecting both directions on Auckland Oval at A68 West Auckland Road.

Restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 22 at 11pm.

### Auckland Avenue

One lane traffic is in operation on Auckland Avenue in both directions between Brinkburn Road and Auckland Oval because of construction.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 22 at 11pm.

### Winchester Way

One lane traffic is in operation on Winchester Way in both directions at Rochester Way because of electricity work.

Restrictions are expected to remain in place until October 15 at 11pm.

### A68 Woodland Road

Temporary traffic signals are in place on A68 Woodland Road in both directions between Hollyhurst Road and Bracken Road because of telecoms work.

The restrictions are expected to remain in place until September 15 at 11.59pm.

The schemes were listed as having minimal impact, apart from Winchester Way, where the impact was listed as low.

Drivers are advised to allow extra time when travelling through the affected areas.
