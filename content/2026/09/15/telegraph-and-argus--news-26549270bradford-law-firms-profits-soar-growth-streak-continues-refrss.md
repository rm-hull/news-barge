A LAW firm has recorded its sixth consecutive year of financial growth.

Gordons, which has an office in Bradford, increased net profit by 12 per cent to £14.6 million for the year ending March 31, 2026, and saw annual turnover rise by 11 per cent to £26.3 million.

Victoria Davey, managing partner at Gordons, said: "Our continued growth is due to the lasting relationships that we establish with clients.

"Trust is earned and our success is built upon that premise.

"When this is combined with our entrepreneurial culture, it allows us to think and act decisively for our clients, providing them with advice that delivers the right outcomes.

"We keep things simple and do the basics well.

"These are traits we see in our best clients.

"We have had a positive start to our latest financial year and aim to achieve further controlled growth for the remainder of it."

Since 2020, the firm has seen net profit rise by 77 per cent and turnover grow by 46 per cent.

Growth was driven by the company's commercial teams, which delivered a 13 per cent increase in turnover year-on-year.

In recognition of the firm's performance, all staff will receive a bonus equivalent to five per cent of their annual salary.

Gordons has continued to expand its client base while strengthening relationships with existing clients such as AO, Iceland Foods, Morrisons, Wren Kitchens, Molson Coors, Evri, and Houseful, the parent company of Zoopla.

The firm also maintained its commitment to social mobility through its legal apprenticeship scheme, now in its fifteenth year.

It partnered with Harehills-based charity CATCH, which supports young people in reaching their potential.

More than 25 paralegals were recruited on various projects throughout the year.

Ms Davey said: "We remain committed to continuing to play our part in creating a variety of opportunities for those of that age.

"We know from experience that there are determined and highly capable individuals within this cohort with great attitudes who just need a chance to prove themselves."

Gordons employs 184 people and also has an office in Leeds.
