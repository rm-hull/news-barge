A WOMAN in her late 50s has been found dead in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police are appealing to find the next of kin of Roksana Matwijiszyn, who lived in Harrogate, on behalf of the Coroner’s Office.

She passed away on Sunday (September 13).

  


There are suspicious circumstances, police said.

A force spokesperson said: “If you knew her or know of any relatives that we can contact, please email [coroner@northyorkshirepolice.uk](mailto:coroner@northyorkshirepolice.uk) quoting reference number 12260179712.”
