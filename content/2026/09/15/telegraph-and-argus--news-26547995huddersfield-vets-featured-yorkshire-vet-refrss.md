If you are a fan of the Channel 5 TV series The Yorkshire Vet, you will probably know the show followed the work of Peter Wright and Julian Norton at the Skeldale Veterinary Centre in Thirsk.

The long-running series has won numerous RTS Awards and was even nominated for a National Television Award.

However, as the show progressed, The Yorkshire Vet expanded to introduce new vets with one vet practice that is not too far from Bradford in Huddersfield.

Donaldson’s Hospital was announced to be joining the show in 2020, allowing viewers to see what goes on inside the veterinary practice.


## The Huddersfield vets that featured in The Yorkshire Vet

Matt Smith, David Melleney, and Shona Searson from Donaldson’s Hospital all featured in the show.

It followed the three vets as they cared for animals in rural communities in West Yorkshire.

In one of the episodes, Matt performs eye surgery on a reindeer at Cannon Hall Farm in Barnsley, offering viewers a look into how rural vet services work.

Since the vet practice was opened by Peter Wright in 2019, Donaldson’s Hospital has opened multiple clinics, including in Sowerby Bridge and Penistone.

On the show, Paul Stead, the co-Executive Producer of The Yorkshire Vet, said: “This show captures a style of veterinary services in decline.

“Most vets in the UK specialise in either small animals or livestock.

“James Herriot’s former practice is one of the few that really do still cater for all creatures great and small!


“So to go with all the fluffy pets we’ve some hard core coverage of raw, rural care provided by Julian and his team for farmers and their cattle and we’re making a no holds barred honest account of the work that they do.”

If you would like to go back and watch the episodes featuring Donaldson’s Hospital and its vets, all of the series are currently available on Channel 5’s streaming service, 5.

The new series of The Yorkshire Vet is also currently airing on Channel 5, with the next episode being on Thursday, September 17, at 9pm.
