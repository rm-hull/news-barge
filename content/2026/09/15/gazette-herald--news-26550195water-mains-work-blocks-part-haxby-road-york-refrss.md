PART of a key residential road in York is blocked this morning as works to fix a burst water main continue.

As reported by The Press yesterday, parts of Haxby Road close to the junction with Fountayne Street were flooded after the burst which called out North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service in the early hours of yesterday (Monday, September 14).


  



This morning, works being carried out on behalf of Yorkshire Water are ongoing in Haxby Road at the junction with Walpole Street and there is a hole in about half of the York-bound side of the road.

Temporary traffic lights were in operation in Haxby Road at 7am and a stop for buses coming into York is temporarily suspended in the vicinity.

Haxby Road is a busy route into and out of York for road traffic, particularly for New Earswick and beyond to the A1237 Outer Ring Road.

The Press has contacted Yorkshire Water.
