Roadworks are continuing on Haxby Road in York this morning (Tuesday).

It follows the water mains burst which flooded part of the street in the early hours yesterday.

Firefighters were called out to divert the floodwater from properties close to the junction with Fountayne Street.

**Video**

#### Roadworks location

Works to remedy the situation by Yorkshire Water began yesterday and are continuing today.

Temporary traffic lights are operating near the junction with Walpole Street. It is causing traffic delays in both directions.

Yesterday a Yorkshire Water spokesperson told us: “We’re currently repairing a burst main on Haxby Road in York and doing so as quickly as we can.

“We apologise for the inconvenience and thank everyone in the area for their patience.”
