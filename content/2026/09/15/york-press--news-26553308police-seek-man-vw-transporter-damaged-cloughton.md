Police are trying to identify a man after a VW Transporter was damaged in North Yorkshire.

The brown van was scratched when it was parked in Newlands Road in Cloughton on August 9.

  


North Yorkshire Police said: "Fortunately the victim's car has a dashcam, which he had forgot to deactivate and it has captured footage and audio evidence.

"If you can help our appeal, please email [peter.henderson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:peter.henderson@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101 and ask for PC1668 Henderson.

"If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

"Please quote reference 12269160439 when passing on information."
