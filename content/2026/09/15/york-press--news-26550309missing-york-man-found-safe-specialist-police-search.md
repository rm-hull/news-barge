A 26-year-old man from York who was the subject of a missing person appeal issued last week has been found "safe and well".

  


Yesterday evening (Monday, September 14) officers from North Yorkshire Police located the man first reported missing from the Heslington area on Sunday, following enquiries involving specialist police resources, including the police helicopter (NPAS) and specialist search teams.

Information provided by members of the public also helped officers find him, a force spokesperson confirmed.

Police thanked everyone who shared the appeal, provided information or assisted with enquiries.

The force said public support played an important role in bringing the incident to a positive conclusion.
