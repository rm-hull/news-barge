A seventh person has been arrested in connection with a 2019 Hartlepool murder.

Two men are due to stand trial after denying the murder of Hemewand Ali Hussain earlier this year.

The 32-year-old was found dead after being shot at point-blank range inside a house in Charterhouse Street, Hartlepool, on September 14, 2019.

Daniel Nerim Kadiu is expected to stand trial at Teesside Crown Court alongside Armando Marku, 26, and Sajmir Dodoveci, 40.

The 35-year-old was charged with murder and remanded in custody until his appearance at Teesside Magistrates’ Court on Friday, September 11.

He first appeared at Teesside Crown Court on September 15 and was remanded in custody.

Detective Inspector Lyndsey Dale, from Cleveland Police, said: “I hope this development, and the fact that these complex enquiries are and have been continuing apace since 2019, reassures Mr Hussain’s family as well as the wider public that Cleveland Police will continue to do everything possible to bring to justice anyone suspected of being involved in his death.”

Four people have already been jailed following the gangland-style execution of 32-year-old father-of-two over two separate trials.

In 2022, jurors found Eugert Merizaj played a key role in persuading Mr Hussain to go to the address. He was jailed for at least 32 years.

Noza Saffari, 39, of Park Lane, Middlesbrough, was jailed for 15 years after being convicted of manslaughter but cleared of murder.

Qazim Marku, 25, of Maxwell Road, West Drayton, London, and Dorian Pirija, 33, of Trillo Avenue, Bolton, were each jailed for 19 years after being convicted of manslaughter but cleared of murder.

A trial date is yet to be confirmed.
