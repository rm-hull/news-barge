An attraction is set to bring its season to a close with a day of apple-themed entertainment, competitions and family activities expected to draw visitors from across the region.

Newby Hall will host its popular Apple Day event on Sunday, September 27, featuring the crowd favourite Apple Throwing Championships, cider tasting and the World Jam Festival and Awards.

The championships, now in their fifth year, will run from 1pm to 3pm and challenge competitors to throw apples as far as possible across the River Ure.

The finalist with the longest throw will compete in a 3.30pm grand finale for the title and trophy.

Last year’s champion, Lucas Robinson from Workington, secured victory with a throw of more than 80 metres.

Lawrence Wright, head gardener at Newby Hall, said: "Newby’s orchards are filled with heritage varieties of apples – some dating back to the 1880s.

"As well as managing the historic varieties, we work in partnership with the Northern Fruit Group to plant new, more manageable trees.

"The resulting fruit is used to make apple juice and cider, and bags of apples are sold in our shop.

"Smaller windfall apples help to feed the estate’s diverse wildlife, with visiting field fares and other migratory birds enjoying the harvest, along with insects, birds and mammals living along the river."

Visitors can also enjoy pressing demonstrations, cider tasting and a display of heritage apple varieties from the estate’s orchards.

The World Jam Festival and Awards returns this year, with entries from as far away as Australia and Brazil.

Judging is led by Michelin-starred chef Andrew Pern, and categories include jams, preserves, chutneys, relishes, pickles, curds and fruit butters.

The winner of the ‘Best in Show’ trophy will also have the chance to have their entry stocked in Newby’s shop.

Stuart Gill, commercial director at Newby Hall, said: "We’re proud to champion British apples and with more than 50 heritage varieties growing in our orchards, Apple Day is a brilliant way to celebrate the end of another fantastic season."

Entertainment will include a performance by the Harrogate Spa Town Ukes and a dance display by Beijing-based performer Yulin Luo.
