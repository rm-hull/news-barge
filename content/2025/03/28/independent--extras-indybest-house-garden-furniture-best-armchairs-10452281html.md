From finding stylish sofas to sorting which shelving units hold up better than the rest, here at IndyBest, we love a renovation, no matter how small, so be sure to take a look at our furniture section, which houses some key fail-safe finds.

Dunelm impresses us time after time with its expensive-looking furniture and reasonable prices. Case in point: its boucle accent chair, which is inspired by mid century modern silhouettes. The chair boasts a wooden-arm frame and industrial-style black metal legs, while the soft boucle upholstery gives it a contemporary feel.

Adding some retro flair into your home, the neutral colour palette slots into almost every interior setting, too.
