# Bungalow destroyed in explosion captured on camera

A suspected gas explosion that destroyed a bungalow and left a man injured has been caught on camera.

It happened on Cobb Hall Road, Newton Longville, near Milton Keynes on Sunday morning.

Additional footage showed flames and smoke rising from the property afterwards.
