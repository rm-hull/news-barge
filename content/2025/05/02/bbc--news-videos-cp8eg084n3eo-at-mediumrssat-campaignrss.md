# Why are Harry and Meghan returning to the UK?

The Duke and Duchess of Sussex are planning to relocate from the US and move back to the UK later this month.

Prince Harry and Meghan visited the King at Highgrove in July, with their children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet, and spent time with the family of Harry's late mother, Diana, Princess of Wales.

It's thought this visit was significant in driving their decision to move back.

BBC culture correspondent Noor Nanji looks at why the move raises questions over security detail, royal duties and Harry's relationship with King Charles and Prince William.
