  ANYROCKS is a fun and inclusive fitness event for all abilities, designed to challenge, motivate and inspire. Whether you are taking part on your own or teaming up with a friend, there is a category for everyone, with both single and doubles events available.

Everyone is welcome and participation is free.

The main ANYROCKS challenge will then take place at Ripon Leisure and Wellbeing Hub - The Jack Laugher Centre on Saturday 10 October but, to get everyone ready, you can take part in a test run at your local site.

## Sign up for your test run!

This is your chance to come along, have a go and get an idea of your race time ahead of the main event.

The test runs take place from Saturday 12 to Sunday 20 September at our Active North Yorkshire venues and you can book your test run in person at our sites from Saturday 15 August.

If you are booking for the doubles event, only one person needs to make a booking for both people.

## Full details of the test run workout

- 2 minutes bike (max distance)
- 1 minute rest
- 2 minutes row (max distance)
- 1 minute rest
- 2 minutes slam ball (max reps)
- 1 minute rest
- 2 minutes burpees (max reps)
- 1 minute rest
- 2 minutes run (max distance)

Sign-up for the main ANYROCKS event will be available from Monday 14 September.
